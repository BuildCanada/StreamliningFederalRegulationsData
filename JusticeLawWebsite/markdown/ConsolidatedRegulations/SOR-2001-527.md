## Canada Small Business Financing (Establishment and Operation of Capital Leasing Pilot Project) Regulations {#canada-small-business-financing-establishment-and-operation-of-capital-leasing-pilot-project-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2001-527
:::

[CANADA SMALL BUSINESS FINANCING ACT](/eng/acts/C-10.2)

Registration 2001-12-06

Canada Small Business Financing (Establishment and Operation of Capital
Leasing Pilot Project) Regulations

P.C. 2001-2237  2001-12-06
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 13(5) of the [Canada Small Business
Financing Act](/eng/acts/C-10.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBBA}, the Minister
of Industry caused a copy of the proposed [Canada Small Business
Financing (Establishment and Operation of Capital Leasing Pilot Project)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-527), substantially in the form
set out in the annexed regulations, to be laid before the House of
Commons and before the Senate on October 3, 2001;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBBA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1998, c.
  36
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister for the purposes of the [Atlantic Canada
Opportunities Agency Act](/eng/acts/A-13.7), the Minister of Western
Economic Diversification, the Minister of Industry, the Minister of
Finance and the Treasury Board, pursuant to sections 13 and 14 of the
[Canada Small Business Financing Act](/eng/acts/C-10.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDAAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Canada Small Business Financing (Establishment and
Operation of Capital Leasing Pilot Project)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-527).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678429 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Small Business
      Financing Act](/eng/acts/C-10.2). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[aggregate outstanding balance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[aggregate outstanding balance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      aggregate, calculated on the day on which a capital lease is
      entered into or is amended as a result of improvements to the
      equipment, of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total financing amount of the capital
        lease,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outstanding balance of any other capital
        lease registered with the Minister in accordance with these
        Regulations, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the outstanding loan amount referred to in
        subsection 4(3) of the Act. ([somme des soldes
        impayés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[capital lease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[capital lease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a lease or leasing
      under which a lessor provides equipment to a lessee for payment
      and that meets one of the following conditions:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lease or leasing includes a bargain
        purchase option or provides for a transfer of ownership of the
        leased equipment to the lessee at the end of its term;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the term of the lease or leasing is greater
        than 75% of the economic life of the equipment; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the net present value of the scheduled payments
        made under the lease or leasing, calculated in accordance with
        the annual imputed rate of interest used in the calculation of
        the scheduled payments, is 90% or more of the cost of the
        equipment on the day the lease or leasing is entered into.
        ([contrat de location-acquisition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[cost of the equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cost of the equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of
      equipment that is the subject of a capital lease,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of new equipment, the purchase price
        of the equipment provided to the lessee,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of used equipment, the lesser of

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the purchase price of the used equipment, and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the fair market value of the used equipment
          on the day on which the capital lease is entered into,

      and may include, in respect of either new or used equipment,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the additional costs necessary for the
        operation of the equipment, other than maintenance and training
        costs,

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the cost of software related to the operation
        of the equipment, and

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} non-refundable taxes and customs duties, if
        applicable. ([coût du matériel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[lease funder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[lease funder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who purchases
      or accepts an assignment of a capital lease.
      ([investisseur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[lessee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[lessee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who carries on or is
      about to carry on a small business in Canada and who is a party to
      a capital lease. It does not include Her Majesty or an agent of
      Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province, a municipality or a
      municipal or other public body that performs a function of
      government. ([locataire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[lessor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[lessor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Canadian Payments Association
        established by subsection 3(1) of the [Canadian Payments
        Act](/eng/acts/C-21)

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} as set out in paragraph 4(1)(b) or (c) or
          paragraph 4(2)(a) or (c) of that Act, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} as set out in any of paragraphs 4(2)(d)
          to (h) of that Act, if the member provides, with their
          application to be a lessor under these Regulations, their
          Canadian Payments Association transit number and a certificate
          issued by the member's external auditor stating that the
          member has been a commercial lessor in Canada for the past
          five years;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a local cooperative credit society, within the
        meaning of subsection 2(1) of the [Canadian Payments
        Act](/eng/acts/C-21), that is a member of a central cooperative
        credit society, within the meaning of that subsection, if the
        central cooperative credit society is a member of the Canadian
        Payments Association;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a leasing company incorporated in Canada or a
        lease funder carrying on business in Canada, with a place of
        business in Canada, that

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} maintains a rating of "BBB" or better issued
          by a Canadian bond rating agency, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} participates in a securitization program
          approved by a Canadian bond rating agency; or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other organization designated by the
        Minister as a lessor for the purposes of these Regulations.
        ([locateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outstanding balance of a capital lease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outstanding balance of a capital lease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
      means the aggregate of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any overdue payments,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the net present value of all future payments
        due under the capital lease, discounted at the annual imputed
        rate of interest used in the calculation of the scheduled
        payments, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the present value, discounted at the annual
        imputed rate of interest used in the calculation of the
        scheduled payments of the lesser of

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the residual value of the equipment set out
          in the registration form referred to in subsection 12(1), and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the bargain purchase option price of the
          equipment set out in the capital lease. ([solde impayé du
          contrat de location-acquisition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[rate of interest on Government of Canada bonds]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[rate of interest on Government of Canada
      bonds]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a capital lease with a term of less than
        three years, the one to three year Government of Canada
        marketable bonds average yield as published by the Bank of
        Canada on the last Wednesday of the month before the day on
        which the capital lease is entered into;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a capital lease with a term of three to
        five years, the three to five year Government of Canada
        marketable bonds average yield as published by the Bank of
        Canada on the last Wednesday of the month before the day on
        which the capital lease is entered into; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a capital lease with a term exceeding five
        years but not exceeding ten years, the five to ten year
        Government of Canada marketable bonds average yield as published
        by the Bank of Canada on the last Wednesday of the month before
        the day on which the capital lease is entered into. ([taux de
        rendement des obligations du gouvernement du
        Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[small business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[small business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a business carried on
      or about to be carried on in Canada for gain or profit with an
      estimated gross annual revenue

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a business being carried on, not
        exceeding \$5 million for the fiscal year of the business during
        which a capital lease is approved by a lessor, or a loan is
        approved by a lender, in respect of the business; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a business about to be carried
        on, that is not expected, at the time a capital lease is
        approved by a lessor or a loan is approved by a lender in
        respect of the business, to exceed \$5 million for its first
        fiscal year that is at least 52 weeks in duration.

      It does not include the business of farming or a business having
      as its principal object the furtherance of a charitable or
      religious purpose. ([petite entreprise]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[total financing amount of a capital lease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[total financing amount of a capital lease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
      means the aggregate of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the financed cost of the equipment, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the registration fee referred to in subsection
        14(1), if the fee has been financed and is set out in the
        registration form referred to in subsection 12(1). ([montant
        total du financement du contrat de
        location-acquisition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the provisions
  of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) apply to the determination of
  whether a person is dealing at arm's length with another person.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a lessee is
  related to another lessee or borrower if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} either lessee or borrower directly or indirectly
    controls the other in any manner;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} both lessees or both the lessee and the borrower
    are directly or indirectly controlled in any manner by the same
    person or group of persons;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the lessee carries on their small business in
    partnership with the other lessee or borrower who carries on another
    small business; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the lessee shares management services,
    administrative services, equipment, facilities or overhead expenses
    of the business with the other lessee or borrower, but is not in
    partnership with that lessee or borrower.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (3),
  [[control]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the holding of shares of a
  corporation to which are attached more than 50% of the votes necessary
  to elect a majority of their directors.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (3), a lessee is not related to
  another lessee or borrower for the purposes of these Regulations if
  their businesses are located at different premises and neither derives
  more than 25% of their actual or projected gross revenues from the
  other.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a capital
  lease is considered to have been entered into on the day on which it
  is signed by the lessee and the lessor.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678489 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to capital leases that are entered into after the coming into force
of these Regulations and that are registered with the Minister in
accordance with these Regulations.

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following conditions shall be satisfied in respect of a capital lease:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the capital lease shall meet the criteria set out
    in subsection (2);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the capital lease is to provide
    equipment necessary for the operation of the lessee's small
    business, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} new equipment, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} used equipment that has an economic life greater
      than the term of the capital lease.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations apply to a capital lease if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cost of the equipment does not exceed
    \$250,000;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total financing amount of the capital lease
    does not exceed \$250,000; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate outstanding balance in respect of a
    lessee that is a party to the capital lease or a related lessee or
    borrower does not exceed \$250,000.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to a capital lease if the capital lease is to provide

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} equipment the cost of which exceeds \$250,000;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} equipment for which the financed cost is greater than
  100% of the cost of the equipment;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} real property or immovables;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} equipment that is the subject of a conditional sale;
  or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} equipment that is the subject of a sale-lease back.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to subsection
21(2), the maximum term of a capital lease is 10 years beginning on the
day on which the capital lease is entered into.

## [Liability of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678508 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Minister is liable to pay a lessor any eligible
  loss, calculated in accordance with subsection 44(7), sustained as a
  result of a lessee's default on a capital lease.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may give notice in writing to a lessor
  of the termination of the Minister's liability in respect of a capital
  lease entered into by the lessor on or after the date set out in the
  notice.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice under subsection (2) is not effective unless
  the date set out in the notice is at least 24 hours after receipt of
  the notice at the place of business of the lessor.

## [Limitation to Minister's Liability]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678513 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The liability of the
Minister to make any payment to a lessor in respect of losses, sustained
as a result of all lessees' defaults on all capital leases entered into
by the lessor is limited to the aggregate of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 90% of that part of the aggregate total financing
  amount of all capital leases entered into by the lessor that does not
  exceed \$250,000,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 50% of that part of the aggregate total financing
  amount of all capital leases entered into by the lessor that exceeds
  \$250,000 but does not exceed \$500,000,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} 10% of that part of the aggregate total financing
  amount of all capital leases entered into by the lessor that exceeds
  \$500,000, and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} 10% of the aggregate total financing amount of all
  capital leases transferred to the lessor as a result of lease funding
  in accordance with section 36.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is not liable
to make any payment to a lessor in respect of any loss sustained as a
result of a lessee's default on a capital lease, if the aggregate
outstanding balance in respect of the lessee or a related lessee or
borrower exceeds \$250,000, when that amount was disclosed to the lessor
by the lessee or the lessor had actual knowledge of it on or before the
day on which the capital lease is entered into.

## [Loss-sharing Ratio]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678520 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The liability of the
Minister in respect of losses sustained as a result of a lessee's
default on a capital lease is limited to 85% of the lessor's eligible
loss, calculated in accordance with subsection 44(7).

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is not
liable to make any payment to a lessor unless the lessor has

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} paid to the Minister the registration fee referred to
  in subsection 14(1);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} paid to the Minister the administration fee referred
  to in subsection 14(2); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} complied with the other requirements of these
  Regulations.

## [Capital Lease Registration with the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678526 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a lessor
  shall submit a capital lease to the Minister for registration within
  four months after the day on which the capital lease is entered into.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If non-compliance with subsection (1) is inadvertent,
  the Minister shall extend the period by two months.

<!-- -->

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  registration of a capital lease shall be done on a form that shall be
  signed by the lessor and the lessee and contain the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lessor's name, address and an identification
    number assigned by the Minister;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lessee's name and the address and telephone
    number of the small business;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the capital lease is entered into;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement setting out

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the total financing amount of the capital lease,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the registration fee referred to in subsection
      14(1), if it is financed,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the cost of the equipment, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the value of the bargain purchase option or the
      residual value of the equipment;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any charges and insurance premiums referred to in
    section 16, that the lessee is required to pay;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the lessor's acknowledgement that the lessee was
    not required to pay any fees or charges other than those authorized
    by these Regulations;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the lessee's consent to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the audit, by the Minister, of the capital lease
      approval and administration file held by the lessor,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the release, by the Minister, of information in
      respect of the lessee's outstanding capital leases registered with
      the Minister in accordance with these Regulations to another
      lessor to whom the lessee applied for a capital lease,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the release by the Minister, of information in
      respect of the lessee's outstanding loans made under the Act and
      the lessee's outstanding guaranteed business improvement loans
      made under the Small Business Loans Act, to another lessor to whom
      the lessee applied for a capital lease, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the release by the Minister, of information in
      respect of the lessee's outstanding capital leases registered with
      the Minister in accordance with these Regulations to a lender to
      whom the lessee applied for a loan;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the lessor's acknowledgement that, before entering
    into the capital lease, the lessor verified within their
    organization, that the aggregate outstanding balance in respect of
    the lessee or a related lessee or borrower did not exceed \$250,000;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the lessee's acknowledgement that the aggregate
    outstanding balance in respect of the lessee or a related lessee or
    borrower does not exceed \$250,000;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the lessor's acknowledgement that the capital lease
    does not provide for any matter referred to in paragraphs 4(a) to
    (e); and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the lessor's acknowledgement that, before approving
    the capital lease, the lessor acted in accordance with the due
    diligence requirements referred to in section 19.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The registration form referred to in subsection (1)
  shall be accompanied by a capital lease cost form signed by the lessor
  which contains the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the annual imputed rate of interest used in the
    calculation of the scheduled payments;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a complete breakdown of the calculation of the
    scheduled payments; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of used equipment, the fair market
    value of the equipment on the day on which the capital lease is
    entered into.

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678555 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Every capital lease is
subject to the condition that no fee or charge is payable by a lessee
other than

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the registration fee referred to in subsection 14(1);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the administration fee referred to in subsection
  14(2); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other fee or charge authorized by these
  Regulations.

<!-- -->

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  registration fee in respect of a capital lease shall be paid by the
  lessor to the Minister when the capital lease is submitted for
  registration. This fee, which is 2% of the financed cost of the
  equipment, may be included in the total financing amount of the
  capital lease.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An administration fee in respect of a capital lease
  shall be paid by the lessor to the Minister. It is calculated at the
  end of each month at the annual rate of 1.25% applied to the
  outstanding balance of the capital lease minus the present value of
  the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the residual value of the equipment set out in the
    registration form referred to in subsection 12(1), and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the bargain purchase option price of the equipment
    set out in the capital lease.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The administration fee is payable quarterly, within
  two months after the end of each quarter.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} With each payment under subsection (3), the lessor
  shall submit to the Minister a statement that substantiates the basis
  on which the payment was calculated.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} When the lessor is unable to provide one or more of
  the statements required by subsection (4) in respect of a year, the
  Minister shall notify the lessor

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that for that year, the lessor may make the
    payments referred to in subsection (3), except the payment for the
    last quarter, on the basis of estimates of the amounts payable; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that for that year, the lessor shall submit the
    statement required by subsection (6) rather than the statements
    required by subsection (4).

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} On or before June 1 following a year in respect of
  which a lessor makes the payments referred to in subsection (5), the
  lessor shall pay any amount owing for the year, or claim any
  overpayment for the year, and submit to the Minister a statement that
  substantiates the basis on which the amount of the administration fee
  was calculated.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} On application by a lessor within one year after a
  capital lease registered with the Minister in accordance with these
  Regulations is entered into, the Minister shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the lessor has not provided to the lessee all of
    the equipment that is the subject of the capital lease, refund to
    the lessor that portion of the registration fee paid that is
    attributable to the portion of the equipment and subtract the value
    of that portion of the equipment from the total financing amount of
    the capital lease; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the lessor determines that the capital lease is
    not in compliance with the requirements of these Regulations, refund
    to the lessor the registration fee and the administration fee and
    delete the total financing amount of the capital lease from the
    registry, which terminates the Minister's liability in respect of
    the capital lease.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The lessor shall not charge the lessee for the
  administration fee except through the imposition of an annual imputed
  rate of interest.

## [Maximum Annual Imputed Interest Rate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678575 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The maximum annual
imputed rate of interest used to calculate the scheduled payments on a
capital lease shall not exceed the aggregate of 13.25% and the rate of
interest on Government of Canada bonds.

## [Additional Amounts Payable by Lessee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678577 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A lessor may require a
lessee to pay, in addition to the registration fee referred to in
subsection 14(1) and the administration fee referred to in subsection
14(2),

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any charge to register the lessor's security interest
  or to publish their rights;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any premium paid by the lessor in respect of a
  capital lease under a life or disability insurance policy that
  provides that a benefit is or may become payable to the lessor;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any charge by the lessor for the handling of a cheque
  presented or issued by a lessee that is subsequently returned because
  there are not sufficient funds, if stipulated in the capital lease;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any cost related to the inspection of the equipment,
  if stipulated in the capital lease;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any fee for changes to the capital lease requested by
  the lessee;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any expense incurred by the lessor to preserve their
  security in the event of default;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} any fee or charge related to maintaining the
  equipment in good condition, if stipulated in the capital lease; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} any premiums for equipment liability, physical damage
  and replacement insurance, if required under the capital lease.

## [Designation of Lessors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678587 .Part}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is
authorized to designate organizations as lessors.

## [Maintaining Status as Lessor]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678589 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A lessor that is a
leasing company or a lease funder shall, in order to maintain their
status as a lessor, provide proof to the Minister on an annual basis
that they

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain a rating of "BBB" or better issued by a
  Canadian bond rating agency; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} participate in a securitization program approved by a
  Canadian bond rating agency.

## [Due Diligence Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678593 .Part}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Before entering into and
administering a capital lease, the lessor shall apply the same due
diligence and business procedures as those applicable to a capital lease
of the same amount that is not subject to these Regulations, including

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtaining credit references or conducting a credit
  check on the lessee;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} completing an assessment of the ability of the lessee
  to pay, taking into account all other financial obligations of the
  lessee;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} providing for a term similar to the term of a capital
  lease of the same amount that is not subject to these Regulations; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of used equipment, substantiating in
  writing the fair market value and economic life of the equipment on
  the day on which the capital lease is entered into.

## [Payment Terms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678599 .Part}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  capital lease shall set out the total financing amount, the payment
  terms, the frequency of the scheduled payments and the date on which
  the capital lease is entered into. It shall set out either the bargain
  purchase option price or the residual value of the equipment, as the
  case may be.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the term of a capital lease in good standing is
  less than the maximum period specified in section 5, the lessor may
  renew it for one or more additional terms, at an annual imputed rate
  of interest that does not exceed the maximum annual imputed rate
  referred to in section 15 as of the renewal date, to an aggregate
  maximum term of 10 years, including all renewal terms, beginning on
  the day on which the capital lease is entered into.

## [Revision of Payment Terms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678605 .Part}

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  lessor and a lessee may, at any time, agree to revise the payment

## [Improvements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678609 .Part}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  lessor and a lessee may, at any time, agree to improve the original
  equipment that is the subject of a capital lease. The improvements do
  not include the replacement of the original equipment in its entirety.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a lessor and a lessee agree to improve the
  original equipment that is the subject of a capital lease and to amend
  the capital lease, the Minister's liability under these Regulations
  continues if the capital lease is amended accordingly and if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the capital lease as amended has a term that does
    not extend beyond the maximum period specified in section 5;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the annual imputed rate of interest used in the
    calculation of the scheduled payments is the same as the annual
    imputed rate of interest used in the original capital lease;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the lessor notifies the Minister in writing of the
    improvements made to the equipment within three months of the day on
    which the amendment to the capital lease was made, and provides the
    Minister with the following information, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the detailed cost of the improvements,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a detailed calculation of the revised payments,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the additional cost of the equipment that has
      been financed, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the new outstanding balance of the capital
      lease; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the lessor sends to the Minister, with the notice
    referred to in paragraph (c), an additional registration fee that is
    calculated on the basis of the additional cost of the equipment that
    has been financed.

## [Guarantees and Suretyships]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678627 .Part}

  suretyship, they shall state that the aggregate liability of the
  sureties may not exceed the aggregate amount referred to in subsection
  (1).

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** A lessor may, in addition
to any security or guarantee or suretyship referred to in sections 23,
24 and 26, take one or more secured or unsecured corporate guarantees or
suretyships.

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** A lessor may release a

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** A lessee may, at any time

suretyship is equal to or greater than the value of the original
guarantee or suretyship but does not exceed the maximum provided for in
section 26.

## [Amalgamation of Lessors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678687 .Part}

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When

  shall notify the Minister in writing of their intention to amalgamate
  and of the day on which the amalgamation is proposed to take effect.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On amalgamation, the Minister is liable to pay to the
  new lessor the amount of any eligible loss, calculated in accordance
  with subsection 44(7), sustained as a result of lessees' defaults on
  all capital leases entered into by the lessor and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any capital leases entered into by the amalgamating

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} claims for loss sustained in respect of capital

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if, as a result of the amalgamation, the amount
    already paid by the Minister to the amalgamating lessors as a result
    of the Minister's liability under section 7 is greater than the
    Minister's liability in respect of the new lessor, the Minister's
    liability is deemed to be equal to the amount already paid.

## [Default]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678721 .Part}

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these

## [Procedure on Default]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678723 .Part}

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  lessee is in default under section 42, and the default has not been
  corrected to the satisfaction of the lessor, the lessor shall give the
  lessee notice of the default in writing and demand that the lessee
  comply with the condition of the capital lease within the period
  specified in the notice.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the lessee fails to comply with the condition
  within the period specified, the lessor shall formally demand payment
  of the outstanding balance of a capital lease within the period
  specified in the demand.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the outstanding balance of a capital lease is not
  paid within the period specified, the lessor shall take any of the
  following measures to minimize the loss sustained as a result of a
  lessee's default on the capital lease or that will maximize the amount
  recovered:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} collect the outstanding balance of the capital
    lease;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fully realize any security, guarantee or
    suretyship;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} realize on any insurance policy under which the
    lessor is the beneficiary;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} fully implement a compromise settlement with the
    lessee or with a guarantor or surety or any other person on behalf
    of the lessee, guarantor or surety; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (4), take legal proceedings,
    including the enforcement of any resulting judgment, if the
    estimated cost of the proceedings does not exceed the estimated
    amount that may be recovered.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the lessee is a sole proprietor or a partnership,
  the lessor may execute a judgment by realizing on the assets of the
  sole proprietor or partners, other than the assets of the small
  business in respect of which the capital lease is entered into, in an
  amount no greater than the aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 25% of the total financing amount of the capital
    lease,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} interest on the judgment,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} taxed costs for, or incidental to, the legal
    proceedings against the lessee, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} legal fees and disbursements, other than costs
    referred to in paragraph (c), and other costs incurred by the lessor
    for services rendered by persons other than the lessor's employees
    for the purpose of legal proceedings against the lessee.

## [Claims Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678740 .Part}

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  lessor shall take all of the measures described in subsection 43(3)
  that are applicable before submitting a claim to the Minister for loss
  sustained as a result of a lessee's default on a capital lease.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), a lessor shall submit a
  claim for loss within 36 months after the expiry of the period
  specified in the notice referred to in subsection 43(1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is authorized to extend the period
  referred to in subsection (2) if the lessor so requests before the
  expiry of the period.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A claim for loss shall be certified by the lessor and
  shall be accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the capital lease, any amendments to the
    capital lease as a result of improvements to the equipment and other
    documentation substantiating the total financing amount of the
    capital lease;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any document in respect of the net amount
    received by the lessor from any disposition of the equipment under
    subparagraph (7)(b)(i);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of a statement of account in respect of the
    capital lease; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the capital lease approval and
    administration files held by the lessor, if requested by the
    Minister.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A claim for loss shall include the lessor's
  acknowledgement that the lessor has acted in accordance with the due
  diligence requirements referred to in section 19 when entering into
  and administering the capital lease and has taken the measures against
  the lessee required by subsection (1).

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A claim for loss shall include all documents that
  evidence the registration or publication of any rights or interests in
  respect of the capital lease.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} An eligible loss sustained by a lessor shall be
  calculated as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by determining the aggregate of the following
    amounts, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the outstanding balance of the capital lease on
      the expiry of the period specified in the notice referred to in
      subsection 43(1),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of interest calculated in accordance
      with subsection (10),

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} uncollected taxed costs for, or incidental to,
      any legal proceedings in respect of the capital lease, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} legal fees and disbursements, other than costs
      referred to in subparagraph (iii), and other costs incurred by the
      lessor for services rendered by persons other than the lessor's
      employees, for the purpose of collecting the outstanding balance
      of the capital lease from the lessee or the guarantor or surety;
      and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by deducting from the aggregate amount the
    aggregate of the following amounts, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the greater of the residual value of the
      equipment set out in the registration form referred to in
      subsection 12(1), or the net amount received by the lessor on any
      sale or other disposition or on any capital lease of the
      equipment,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any other proceeds realized from the taking of
      any measures described in subsection 43(3), and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any amount paid by the lessor to a secured
      creditor, if the secured creditor has any security interest or
      rights in the equipment financed by the capital lease, and the
      security interest or rights rank ahead of the lessor's security
      interest or rights.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} If a lessor enters into a new capital lease with
  another lessee, of equipment that was already the subject of a capital
  lease, the net amount received by the lessor referred to in
  subparagraph (7)(b)(i) shall be calculated by determining the
  aggregate of the following amounts, namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any down payment made by the lessee, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the present value of the outstanding balance of the
    new capital lease, calculated in accordance with subsection (9).

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} The present value of the outstanding balance of the
  new capital lease referred to in paragraph 8(b) shall be calculated at
  an annual imputed rate of interest and be determined by the formula

  ::: {#678766 .Subsection}
  (A - B) - C + D

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is equal to the annual imputed rate of interest used in the
      calculation of the scheduled payments in respect of the original
      capital lease;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is equal to the rate of interest on Government of Canada bonds in
      effect on the day the original capital lease was entered into;

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is equal to 1.25%, being the rate of the administration fee
      referred to in subsection 14(2); and

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is equal to the rate of interest on Government of Canada bonds in
      effect on the day the new capital lease is entered into.
  :::

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} The amount of interest referred to in subparagraph
  (7)(a)(ii) shall be calculated on the outstanding balance, taking into
  consideration any proceeds realized from the taking of any measures
  described in subsection 43(3), as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the period beginning on the day of
    default and ending on the expiry of the period specified in the
    notice referred to in subsection 43(1), at the annual imputed rate
    of interest used to calculate the scheduled payments in respect of a
    capital lease;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the six-month period immediately
    after the period referred to in paragraph (a), at the annual imputed
    rate of interest used to calculate the scheduled payments in respect
    of a capital lease on the first day of the six-month period;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the six-month period immediately
    after the period referred to in paragraph (b), at a rate of interest
    equal to one half of the rate of interest referred to in that
    paragraph; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the 24-month period immediately after
    the period referred to in paragraph (c), at a rate of interest of
    0%.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} If the total financing amount of a capital lease
  referred to in subparagraph 12(1)(d)(i) is not equal to the total
  financing amount set out in the capital lease, the eligible loss
  calculated in accordance with subsection (7) shall be calculated on
  the portion of the capital lease that was registered with the Minister
  in accordance with these Regulations.

## [Interim Claims Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678780 .Part}

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  lessor may make an interim claim to the Minister for loss, calculated
  in accordance with subsection 44(7), sustained as a result of a
  lessee's default on a capital lease, if the lessor has taken all of
  the measures described in subsection 43(3) that are applicable and
  either

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 43(3)(b) applies but the guarantee or
    suretyship has not been fully realized; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 43(3)(d) applies but the compromise
    settlement has not been fully implemented.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall pay the interim claim as if the
  lessor had fully realized the guarantee or suretyship at the time the
  interim claim was made or fully implemented the compromise settlement.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 44(2) to (11) apply, with such
  modifications as the circumstances require, to an interim claim.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, after the interim claim is paid, the lessor, by
  realizing the guarantee or suretyship or fully implementing the
  compromise settlement, recovers 100% of the guarantee or suretyship or
  compromise settlement, the lessor shall so notify the Minister and the
  interim claim is deemed to be a final claim.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If, after the interim claim is paid, the lessor, by
  realizing the guarantee or suretyship or fully implementing the
  compromise settlement, recovers less than 100% of the guarantee or
  suretyship or compromise settlement, the lessor may make a final claim
  under section 44 for the difference.

## [Subrogation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678789 .Part}

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** If the Minister pays a
lessor for loss sustained as a result of a lessee's default on a capital
lease, Her Majesty is subrogated, from the payment of the final claim,
to the rights of the lessor, up to the amount paid by the Minister.

## [Audit or Examination]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678791 .Part}

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, after giving at least 21 days notice in writing to a
  lessor, conduct an audit or examination of the lessor's documents,
  records and books of account to verify that these Regulations are
  being complied with in respect of a capital lease, including that the
  lessor has acted in accordance with the due diligence requirements
  referred to in section 19 in respect of the approval and
  administration of the capital lease and that the documents submitted
  to the Minister in respect of the lessor are accurate and complete.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is authorized to designate persons to
  carry out audits or examinations under these Regulations. A person
  designated by the Minister may, at any reasonable time, consult the
  lessor's documents, records and books of account in respect of a
  capital lease.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every lessor shall, for the purpose of an audit or
  examination under these Regulations, give all reasonable assistance to
  any person designated by the Minister to carry out the audit or
  examination, provide access to all relevant sites, answer, orally or
  in writing, all questions relating to the subject-matter of the audit
  or examination and provide all information and documents in their
  possession and all copies required for the purpose of the audit or
  examination. The designated person shall not remove the documents or
  copies from the relevant sites without the permission of the lessor.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall provide the lessor with a copy of
  the report of the audit or examination within 21 days after the report
  is completed.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a lessor refuses or intentionally fails to comply
  with any requirement of this section, the Minister may notify the
  lessor in writing that the Minister is not liable under these
  Regulations to make any payment to the lessor for any loss sustained
  as a result of a lessee's default on any capital lease entered into by
  the lessor.

- SOR/2006-272, s. 9(E)

## [Evaluation of Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678800 .Part}

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may request
the lessor or the lessee to provide any information or documentation
related to the capital lease for the purpose of evaluating these
Regulations.

cease to have effect on April 1, 2007.
