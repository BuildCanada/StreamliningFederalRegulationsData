## Faro Airport Zoning Regulations {#faro-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-508
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1995-10-31

Regulations Respecting the Zoning of Faro Airport

P.C. 1995-1815 1995-10-31
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 5.5[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EHBBA} of the
[Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed Regulations
respecting the zoning at Faro Airport, substantially in the form annexed
hereto, was published in two successive issues of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I, on August 12 and 19, 1995,
and in two successive issues of The Whitehorse Star on July 12 and 13,
1995, and a reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested
persons to make representations to the Minister of Transport with
respect thereto;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EHBBA){#footnote1_e}R.S., c. 33
  (1st Supp.), s. 1
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to
section 5.4[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to make the annexed
Regulations respecting the zoning at Faro Airport.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e-ID0EFBAA){#footnote2_e}S.C.
  1992, c. 4, s. 10
  :::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976349 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Faro Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-508).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976351 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Faro Airport, in the
      vicinity of Faro, in the Yukon Territory;
      ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de référence de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the imaginary
      inclined planes that extend upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which planes are more particularly described in Part II of
      the schedule; ([surfaces d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, that is
      prepared for the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular
      direction, which rectangular portion is more particularly
      described in Part III of the schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the imaginary
      inclined planes that extend upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and the approach surfaces, which planes are more
      particularly described in Part IV of the schedule. ([surfaces de
      transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the airport reference point is 711.4 m above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976360 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all land, including public road allowances, that lies directly beneath
the approach surfaces or transitional surfaces, which surfaces are more
particularly described in Parts II and IV of the schedule.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-437, s. 13
:::

## [Building Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976364 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct, on land to which these Regulations apply, a building,
structure or object or an addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976368 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth that is on land to which these Regulations apply grows to a
height that exceeds in elevation at the location of the object any of
the surfaces referred to in section 4, the Minister may require that the
owner or lessee of the land remove the excess growth.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Sections 2 and 3)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-976371 .scheduleLabel}

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point, shown on Faro Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.3043, dated March 9, l995, is a point located on the centre line
of runway 08-26 at a distance of 609.5 m from the threshold of
runway 08.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Faro Airport Zoning Plan No. E.3043,
dated March 9, l995, are planes abutting each end of the strip
associated with runway 08-26 and are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an inclined plane abutting the end of the strip
  associated with the approach to runway 08 having a ratio of l m
  measured vertically to 30 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 2 500 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 295 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 83.3 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an inclined plane abutting the end of the strip
  associated with the approach to runway 26 having a ratio of l m
  measured vertically to 30 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 2 500 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 295 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 83.3 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-strip .SchedHeadL1}

The strip associated with runway 08-26, shown on Faro Airport Zoning
Plan No. E.3043, dated March 9, l995, is 90 m in width, 45 m being on
each side of the centre line of the runway, and 1 339 m in length.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Transitional Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-transitional-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Faro Airport Zoning Plan No. E.3043,
dated March 9, 1995, is an inclined plane rising at a ratio of 1 m
measured vertically to 7 m measured horizontally at right angles to the
centre line and projected centre line of the strip, extending upward and
outward from the lateral limits of the strip and its approach surfaces
to a height of 45 m above the elevation of the airport reference point.
