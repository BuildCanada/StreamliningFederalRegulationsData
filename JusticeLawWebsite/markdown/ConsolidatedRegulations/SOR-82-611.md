## St. Anthony Fisheries Limited Regulations {#st.-anthony-fisheries-limited-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/82-611
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 1, 1980-81

Registration 1982-06-14

Regulations Respecting the Insurance of Loans Made to St. Anthony
Fisheries Limited

P.C. 1982-1773 1982-06-11
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Industry, Trade and Commerce and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to Industry, Trade and Commerce Vote 1a of Appropriation Act
No. 1, 1980-81, is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations
respecting the insurance of loans made to St. Anthony Fisheries
Limited](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-611).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874312 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [St. Anthony Fisheries Limited
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-611).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874314 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Industry,
    Trade and Commerce; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any lender approved by
    the Minister other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Government of Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the government of any province of Canada,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an agency of any government referred to in
      paragraph (a) or (b) or any company that, in the opinion of the
      Minister, is effectively controlled by any such government or any
      agency thereof, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any municipal corporation; ([prêteur
      privé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[St. Anthony Fisheries Limited]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[St. Anthony Fisheries Limited]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    company that is incorporated under the laws of the Province of
    Newfoundland and has its head office in the City of St. John's in
    that Province. ([St. Anthony Fisheries Limited]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874323 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 6, the
Minister may, under Industry, Trade and Commerce Vote 1a of
Appropriation Act No. 1, 1980-81, provide insurance for the full amount
of any loss that may result in respect of any loan made by a private
lender to St. Anthony Fisheries Limited.

## [Application for Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874325 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A private lender who wishes
the Minister to provide insurance referred to in section 3 shall make
application therefor to the Minister and shall provide the Minister with
such information and documents relating to the application as he may
require.

## [Payment of Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874327 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where a private lender
demands repayment of a loan referred to in section 3 in respect of which
insurance has been provided by the Minister pursuant to that section,
the amount of such insurance payable to the private lender shall not
exceed the amount of insurance in effect on the date of such demand.

## [Maximum Amount of Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874329 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The aggregate amount of
insurance that may be provided by the Minister pursuant to section 3 in
respect of any loan made by a private lender to St. Anthony Fisheries
Limited shall not exceed \$10 million at any time without the approval
of the Treasury Board.
