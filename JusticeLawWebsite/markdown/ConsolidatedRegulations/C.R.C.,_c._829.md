## Potato Processing Plant Liquid Effluent Regulations {#potato-processing-plant-liquid-effluent-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 829
:::

[FISHERIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-14)

Regulations Respecting Deleterious Substances in Liquid Effluents from
Potato Processing Plants
::::

:::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-566045 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Potato Processing Plant Liquid Effluent
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._829).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-566047 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Fisheries
      Act](/eng/acts/F-14); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[biochemical oxygen demanding matter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[biochemical oxygen demanding matter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      the substance contained in the effluent from a plant that results
      from the operation of a plant and that will exert a biochemical
      oxygen demand; ([matière ayant une demande biochimique en
      oxygène]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[canned potato products plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[canned potato products plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any
      plant where raw potatoes are peeled and shaped for distribution in
      a canned and preserved condition; ([conserverie de pommes de
      terre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[composite sample]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[composite sample]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a sample obtained
      in accordance with section 7; ([échantillon
      composite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[dehydrated potato products plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[dehydrated potato products plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes
      any plant where raw potatoes are processed to remove moisture by
      heat, vacuum or air drying, with the finished product distributed
      as granules, flakes or slices; ([fabrique de pommes de terre
      déshydratées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[deposit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[deposit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to deposit or to permit the
      deposit into water frequented by fish; ([rejeter]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[effluent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[effluent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all wastewaters deposited
      by a plant and includes process water, wash water, tank drainage,
      storm water and wastes from water and wastewater treatment
      facilities, but does not include

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} storm water that is protected from
        contamination by a deleterious substance prescribed by section 4
        originating from the plant, where the average concentration of
        biochemical oxygen demanding matter in at least 3 samples of the
        storm water, taken at intervals of 15 minutes, does not exceed
        50 mg/L, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} wastewater from the plant that, prior to being
        deposited, is treated at a site outside a plant for the purpose
        of controlling the pH of the wastewater and removing from the
        wastewater the deleterious substances prescribed by section 4,
        in such a manner that

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the pH of the wastewater, determined in
          accordance with subsection 9(3), is between 6.0 and 9.0, and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of deleterious substances
          remaining in that wastewater after the treatment, when added
          to the quantity of deleterious substances deposited directly,
          does not exceed the deposit of deleterious substances
          authorized under section 5; ([effluent]{.DefinedTermLink
          lang="fr"})

  [[existing plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[existing plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a plant that
      commenced commercial production before June 27, 1977;
      ([établissement existant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[expanded plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[expanded plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plant in which
      the total quantity of raw potatoes processed during any year
      exceeds 2.5 times the total quantity of raw potatoes processed in
      that plant during the 1976 calendar year; ([établissement à
      capacité accrue]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[frozen potato products plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[frozen potato products plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any
      plant where raw potatoes are processed and the final potato
      product is distributed in a frozen condition; ([fabrique de pommes
      de terre congelées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/95-426, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[new plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[new plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a plant that did not
      commence commercial production before June 27, 1977 and that
      commences commercial production on or after that date; ([nouvel
      établissement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of a plant means the owner or
      operator or his authorized representative;
      ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes facilities intended
      primarily for the conversion of raw potatoes and potato
      derivatives into products such as frozen potato products, potato
      chips, dehydrated potato products, canned potato products and
      potato starch and includes storage, processing, shipping and
      packaging facilities on the plant site and all properties used for
      the operation of those facilities;
      ([établissement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[potato chip plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[potato chip plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any plant where
      raw potatoes are processed and the final potato product is
      distributed as a snack food in a ready-to-eat condition;
      ([fabrique de croustilles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[process water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[process water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means water that comes into
      contact with potatoes at any stage in the processing and includes
      water used for can washing and clean-up; ([eau de
      traitement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[processing day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[processing day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a period of 24
      consecutive hours or any part thereof during which the plant is in
      operation; ([journée d'exploitation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[raw potatoes processed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[raw potatoes processed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the net
      quantity, in tonnes, of raw potatoes that are received at the
      beginning of the processing line after the raw potato washing
      operation; ([volume d'activités]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[starch plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[starch plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any plant processing
      raw potatoes into potato starch; ([féculerie]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[storm water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[storm water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means water run off that
      results from precipitation of any kind that falls on a plant or
      that passes over or through the plant; ([eaux
      pluviales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[total suspended matter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[total suspended matter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      non-filterable residue that results from the operation of a plant,
      that is contained in the effluent from that plant. ([matières
      totales en suspension]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  - SOR/95-426, s. 1

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-566078 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), these Regulations apply to every new plant and
  every expanded plant.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a plant becomes an expanded plant, these
  Regulations apply to the expanded plant on the first day of the month
  following the month in which the plant becomes an expanded plant.

## [Substances Prescribed as Deleterious Substances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-566082 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the
definition [[deleterious substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
subsection 34(1) of the Act, the following substances resulting from the
operations of a plant to which these Regulations apply are hereby
prescribed as deleterious substances:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} biochemical oxygen demanding matter; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} total suspended matter.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-426, s. 2
:::

## [Authorized Deposit of Deleterious Substances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-566088 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to these
Regulations, the owner of a plant of a class set out in Column I of
Schedule I may deposit a deleterious substance prescribed by section 4
if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the actual daily deposit of each deleterious
  substance, determined in accordance with subsection 11(1), does not
  exceed the authorized daily deposit of that substance for that class
  of plant as set out in Column III of that Schedule;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the average daily deposit of each deleterious
  substance during a month, determined in accordance with
  subsection 11(2), does not exceed the authorized average daily deposit
  of that substance for that class of plant as set out in Column IV of
  that Schedule; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the pH of each composite sample of effluent,
  determined in accordance with subsection 9(3), is between 6.0 and 9.0.

## [Additional Conditions of Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-566093 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-566094 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a plant shall,
for each type of effluent deposited by the plant,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} install and maintain facilities, including sampling
  connections and flow measuring devices, of such type as the Minister
  may in writing approve for sampling and analysing effluents for the
  purpose of enabling the Minister to determine whether the owner is
  complying with the limits of authorized deposits prescribed by
  section 5;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} take a composite sample on the regular basis
  prescribed by section 8;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} analyse the sample referred to in paragraph (b) in
  accordance with section 9;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} measure the flow in accordance with section 10; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} determine the actual and average daily deposits of
  each deleterious substance in accordance with section 11.

### [Method of Collecting Composite Samples]{.HTitleText2} {#h-566101 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A composite sample shall be
obtained by collecting effluent discharged from a plant during a
processing day

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} continually during a sampling period of 24 hours at a
  rate in proportion to the flow rate of the effluent discharged; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in such a manner that equal volumes of effluent are
  delivered into a receptacle at equal intervals not longer than one
  hour during a sampling period of 24 hours.

### [Frequency of Sampling and Analysis]{.HTitleText2} {#h-566105 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The sampling referred to in
paragraph 6(b) shall be made

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a plant where the weekly quantity of
  raw potatoes processed is less than 400 tonnes, on one processing day
  each week; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a plant where the weekly quantity of
  raw potatoes processed is 400 tonnes or more, on two processing days
  each week.

### [Analytical and Other Test Methods]{.HTitleText2} {#h-566109 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 6(c), the concentration in milligrams per litre
  of a substance described in an item of Schedule II, in each composite
  sample, shall be determined using

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the test method set out in Column I and modified in
    Column II of that item; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other method, approved in writing by the
    Minister, the results of which can be confirmed by the method
    referred to in paragraph (a).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph 6(c), procedures
  pertaining to sampling, preservation and storage of samples and
  prevention of interference relating to the test methods referred to in
  paragraph (1)(a), as outlined in the general sections of the
  publication Standard Methods for the Examination of Water and Waste
  Water, 14th edition (1975), published jointly by the American Public
  Health Association, American Water Works Association and the Water
  Pollution Control Federation, shall be adhered to.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph 6(c), the pH of a
  composite sample shall be determined using

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the test method prescribed by section 424 of the
    publication referred to in subsection (2); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other method, approved in writing by the
    Minister, the results of which can be confirmed by the method
    referred to in paragraph (a).

### [Flow Measurement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-566118 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 6(d), the flow of each type of effluent deposited by a plant
shall be measured continuously and recorded.

### [Calculation of Actual Deposit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-566120 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of paragraph 6(e), the actual daily deposit per unit of
  production of each deleterious substance prescribed by section 4 shall
  be determined using the data obtained under subsection 9(1) and
  section 10 and shall be expressed in terms of kilograms per tonne of
  raw potatoes processed per day.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph 6(e), the average daily
  deposit per unit of production during a month of each deleterious
  substance prescribed by section 4 shall be determined by calculating
  the average of the results obtained under subsection (1) and shall be
  expressed in the terms set out in that subsection.

### [Reporting and Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-566124 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  owner of a plant shall, within 30 days after the end of each month,
  sign, date and forward to the Minister a report, in such form as the
  Minister may in writing approve, showing for that month

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the actual daily deposit of deleterious substances
    deposited by the plant on each day that samples were taken,
    determined and expressed in accordance with subsection 11(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the average daily deposit of deleterious substances
    deposited by the plant, determined and expressed in accordance with
    subsection 11(2);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the pH of composite samples, determined in
    accordance with subsection 9(3);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the daily production of the plant for each day that
    samples were taken expressed in terms of tonnes of raw potatoes
    processed;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the weekly production of the plant for each week
    during the month, expressed in terms of tonnes of raw potatoes
    processed;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number of operating days; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the total daily flow, in litres, of each type of
    effluent discharged on each day that samples were taken.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner of a new plant shall, before he deposits
  any deleterious substance prescribed by section 4, and the owner of an
  expanded plant shall, within 30 days of becoming subject to these
  Regulations, sign, date and forward to the Minister a declaration, in
  such form as the Minister may in writing approve,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} showing the projected weekly production of the
    plant expressed in terms of tonnes of raw potatoes processed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} showing the projected annual production of the
    plant expressed in terms of tonnes of raw potatoes processed; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} listing the major products produced.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The owner of a plant shall, within 30 days after the
  end of each calendar year, sign, date and forward to the Minister a
  declaration, in such form as the Minister may in writing approve,
  showing the actual production of the plant for the previous year in
  terms of tonnes of raw potatoes processed.

### [Permitted Variation in Additional Conditions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-566139 .Subheading}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Where the owner of a
plant establishes to the satisfaction of the Minister that for
scientific and technical reasons a scheme of sampling and analysis,
measurement or reporting referred to in sections 7, 9, 10 and 12 other
than at the regular time interval frequencies required by section 8 is
sufficient to enable the Minister to determine whether the owner is
complying with the limits of authorized deposits prescribed by
section 5, the Minister may, in writing, permit the owner to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} take and analyse samples of each effluent in
  accordance with the scheme on a regular basis specified in the letter
  of permission,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} measure the volume of each effluent in accordance
  with the scheme on a regular basis specified in the letter of
  permission, or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} report to the Minister in accordance with the scheme
  on a regular basis specified in the letter of permission

and sections 7, 8, 9, 10 and 12 do not apply to the owner if he complies
with the scheme on the regular basis specified in the letter of
permission.
