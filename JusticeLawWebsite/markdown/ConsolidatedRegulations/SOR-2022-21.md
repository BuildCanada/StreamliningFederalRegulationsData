## Emergency Measures Regulations {#emergency-measures-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2022-21
:::

[EMERGENCIES ACT](/eng/acts/E-4.5)

Registration 2022-02-15

Emergency Measures Regulations

P.C. 2022-107 2022-02-15
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council has, by a proclamation made pursuant to
subsection 17(1) of the [Emergencies Act](/eng/acts/E-4.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2311-3_hq_30533){#fn_81000-3-2311-3_hq_30533-ID0EBBCA},
declared that a public order emergency exists;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2311-3_hq_30533-ID0EBBCA){#fn_81000-3-2311-3_hq_30533}R.S., c.
  22 (4th Supp.)
  :::

And whereas the Governor in Council believes on reasonable grounds, that
the regulation or prohibition of public assemblies in the areas referred
to in these Regulations are necessary;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
Preparedness, pursuant to subsection 19(1) of the [Emergencies
Act](/eng/acts/E-4.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2311-3_hq_30533){#fn_81000-3-2311-3_hq_30533-ID0EDAAA},
makes the annexed [Emergency Measures
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-21).
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply to these Regulations

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Emergencies
    Act](/eng/acts/E-4.5) ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[critical infrastructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[critical infrastructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the following
    places, including any land on which they are located:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} airports, aerodromes, heliports, harbours, ports,
      piers, lighthouses, canals, railway stations, railways, tramway
      lines, bus stations, bus depots and truck depots;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} infrastructure for the supply of utilities such
      as water, gas, sanitation and telecommunications;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} international and interprovincial bridges and
      crossings;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} power generation and transmission facilities;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} hospitals and locations where COVID-19 vaccines
      are administered;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} trade corridors and international border
      crossings, including ports of entry, ferry terminals, customs
      offices, bonded warehouses, and sufferance
      warehouses. ([infrastructures essentielles]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[foreign national]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foreign national]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2‍(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) ([étranger]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a police officer, police
    constable, constable, or other person employed for the preservation
    and maintenance of the public peace ([agent de la
    paix]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[protected person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[protected person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 95‍(2) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) ([personne protégée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- public assembly

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not participate in a public assembly that may reasonably be
  expected to lead to a breach of the peace by:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the serious disruption of the movement of persons
    or goods or the serious interference with trade;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the interference with the functioning of critical
    infrastructure; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the support of the threat or use of acts of serious
    violence against persons or property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person must not cause a person under the age of
  eighteen years to participate in an assembly referred to in subsection
  (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- entry to Canada ---
foreign national

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  foreign national must not enter Canada with the intent to participate
  in or facilitate an assembly referred to in subsection 2(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person registered as an Indian under the [Indian
    Act](/eng/acts/I-5);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who has been recognized as a Convention
    refugee or a person in similar circumstances to those of a
    Convention refugee within the meaning of subsection 146‍(1) of the
    [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227) who is issued a
    permanent resident visa under subsection 139‍(1) of those regulations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who has been issued a temporary resident
    permit within the meaning of subsection 24‍(1) of the [Immigration and
    Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) and who seeks to enter
    Canada as a protected temporary resident under subsection 151.1‍(2) of
    the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a person who seeks to enter Canada for the purpose
    of making a claim for refugee protection;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a protected person;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a person or any person in a class of persons whose
    presence in Canada, as determined by the Minister of Citizenship and
    Immigration or the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
    Preparedness, is in the national interest.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Travel

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not travel to or within an area where an assembly referred to in
  subsection 2(1) is taking place.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minor --- travel near public assembly

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person must not cause a person under the age of
  eighteen years to travel to or within 500 metres of an area where an
  assembly referred to in subsection 2(1) is taking place.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemptions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person is not in contravention of subsections (1)
  and (2) if they are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who, within of the assembly area, resides,
    works or is moving through that area for reasons other than to
    participate in or facilitate the assembly;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who, within the assembly area, is acting
    with the permission of a peace officer or the Minister of Public
    Safety and Emergency Preparedness;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a peace officer; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an employee or agent of the government of Canada or
    a province who is acting in the execution of their duties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of property --- prohibited assembly

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A person must not, directly
or indirectly, use, collect, provide make available or invite a person
to provide property to facilitate or participate in any assembly
referred to in subsection 2(1) or for the purpose of benefiting any
person who is facilitating or participating in such an activity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of protected places

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The following places are
designated as protected and may be secured:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} critical infrastructures;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [[Parliament Hill]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and the
  [[parliamentary precinct ]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as they are defined
  in section 79.51 of the [Parliament of Canada Act](/eng/acts/P-1);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} official residences;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} government buildings and defence buildings

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any property that is a building, structure or part
  thereof that primarily serves as a monument to honour persons who were
  killed or died as a consequence of a war, including a war memorial or
  cenotaph, or an object associated with honouring or remembering those
  persons that is located in or on the grounds of such a building or
  structure, or a cemetery;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other place as designated by the Minister of
  Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direction to render essential goods and
services

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person must make available and render the essential goods and services
  requested by the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness,
  the Commissioner of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police or a person
  acting on their behalf for the removal, towing and storage of any
  vehicle, equipment, structure or other object that is part of a
  blockade.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Method of request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any request made under subsection (1) may be made in
  writing or given verbally by a person acting on their behalf.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Verbal request

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any verbal request must be confirmed in writing as
  soon as possible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of request

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A person who, in accordance
with these Regulations, is subject to a request under section 7 to
render essential goods and services must comply immediately with that
request until the earlier of any of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day referred to in the request;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the declaration of the public order
  emergency expires or is revoked; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which these Regulations are repealed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation for essential goods and
services

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Her
  Majesty in right of Canada is to provide reasonable compensation to a
  person for any goods or services that they have rendered at their
  request under section 7, which amount must be equal to the current
  market price for those goods or services of that same type, in the
  area in which the goods or services are rendered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person who suffers loss, injury or damage as a
  result of anything done or purported to be done under these
  Regulations may make an application for compensation in accordance
  with Part V of the [Emergencies Act](/eng/acts/E-4.5) and any
  regulations made under that Part, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance --- peace officer

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  case of a failure to comply with these Regulations, any peace officer
  may take the necessary measures to ensure the compliance with these
  Regulations and with any provincial or municipal laws and allow for
  the prosecution for that failure to comply.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a failure to comply with these
  Regulations, any peace officer may take the necessary measures to
  ensure the compliance and allow for the prosecution for that failure
  to comply

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding five
    hundred dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
    months or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on indictment, to a fine not exceeding five
    thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding five
    years or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into
force on the day on which it is registered.
