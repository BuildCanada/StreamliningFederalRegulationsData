## Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Kam Lake, N.W.T.) {#reservation-to-the-crown-waiver-order-kam-lake-n.w.t. .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-27
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 1993-03-10

Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Kam Lake, N.W.T.)

P.C. 1993-340  1993-02-23
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 13(b) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), is
pleased hereby to order that any portion of the lands described in the
schedule hereto lying within 30.48 metres (100 feet) of the Ordinary
High Water Mark of Kam Lake in the Northwest Territories be included in
the sale of the said lands.
:::

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-647164 .scheduleLabel}
:::

Kam Lake Road in the City of Yellowknife in the Northwest Territories,
as said road right-of-way is shown on a plan of survey of record number
73214 in the Canada Lands Surveys Records at Ottawa, a copy of which is
filed in the Land Titles Office for the Northwest Territories Land
Registration District at Yellowknife under number 2144.
