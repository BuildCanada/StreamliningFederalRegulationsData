## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702755 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Act,
    2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a spirits licensee or a
    licensed user. ([titulaire de licence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Loss of Bulk Spirits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702759 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 109(f) of the Act, bulk spirits are lost when they are lost in
any of the following circumstances, on condition that the person
responsible for the spirits at the time of the loss keeps records, in
accordance with section 206 of the Act, that substantiate the loss:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a fire;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} shrinkage by evaporation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} processes relating to manufacturing, including aging,
  blending, racking, re-distilling, reducing and vatting;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} processes relating to handling, including stock
  operations and packaging; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the physical transfer of the spirits between
  licensees or between a licensee and an alcohol registrant.

## [Loss of Packaged Alcohol]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702766 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraphs 129(1)(c) and 138(1)(c) of the Act, packaged alcohol is lost
when it is lost through breakage that occurs

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} while the alcohol is in its original unopened
  container in an excise warehouse or in the specified premises of a
  licensed user, on condition that the excise warehouse licensee or the
  licensed user in possession of the alcohol at the time of the breakage
  keeps records, in accordance with section 206 of the Act, that
  substantiate the breakage; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} during the physical transfer of the alcohol between
  excise warehouses or between an excise warehouse and the specified
  premises of a licensed user.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702770 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations take
effect on July 1, 2003.
