## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1179698 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the owner or
    operator of a commercial conveyance. ([transporteur
    commercial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any conveyance
    that is used for the commercial transportation of persons or goods
    by air. ([moyen de transport commercial]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[crew member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[crew member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person assigned to duty
    on board a commercial conveyance. ([membre
    d'équipage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[time of departure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[time of departure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the time of take-off
    from the last point of embarkation of persons before the conveyance
    leaves Canada. ([moment du départ]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Information Collected by Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1179706 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of sections 3
to 5 is to prescribe the source, circumstances and time and manner for
the purposes of subsection 92(1) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed source

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The prescribed source from
which the Agency may collect information is the United States Customs
and Border Protection Agency or its successor.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed circumstances

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The prescribed
circumstances in which the Agency may collect information are those in
which a person is leaving or has left Canada by land.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed time and manner

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** When collecting the
information, the Agency must collect it by electronic means as soon as
it is available from the United States Customs and Border Protection
Agency or its successor.

## [Information Given to Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1179715 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of sections 7
to 12 is to prescribe the information, conveyances, classes of persons,
circumstances and time and manner for the purposes of subsection 93(1)
of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed information

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The prescribed information
that is to be given to the Agency under paragraph 93(1)(a) of the Act is
the flight code that identifies the commercial carrier and the flight
number of the conveyance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed conveyances

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The prescribed conveyances
in relation to which information must be given to the Agency are all
commercial conveyances except those commercial conveyances that are
operated by or on behalf of the Department of National Defence, the
Canadian Armed Forces or a visiting force or those used for diplomatic
missions to Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed classes of persons

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The prescribed classes of
persons who must give the information to the Agency are commercial
carriers and persons who charter a commercial conveyance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed circumstances

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The prescribed
circumstances in which information must be given to the Agency are those
in which a conveyance departs or is expected to depart from a place
inside Canada with a final destination outside of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed time --- 72 hours before
departure

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  following information becomes known to a person of a prescribed class
  of persons, as provided for in section 9, during the period beginning
  72 hours before the conveyance's scheduled time of departure and
  ending at check-in, that person must provide the information to the
  Agency as soon as possible:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the surname, first name and middle names, the date
    of birth and the sex of each person expected to be on board the
    conveyance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the unique passenger reference of each person,
    other than a crew member, expected to be on board the conveyance;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the flight code that identifies the commercial
    carrier and the flight number of the conveyance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed time --- before departure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in subsection 93(1) of
  the Act must be given to the Agency not later than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} check-in, if the information relates to a person,
    other than a crew member, expected to be on board the conveyance; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one hour before the conveyance's scheduled time of
    departure, if the information relates to a crew member expected to
    be on board the conveyance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed time --- after departure

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The following information must be given to the Agency
  not later than 30 minutes after the time of departure of the
  conveyance:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the unique passenger reference of each person,
    other than a crew member, on board the conveyance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the flight code that identifies the commercial
    carrier and the flight number of the conveyance; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the last place inside Canada from which the
    conveyance departed, regardless of whether persons boarded it at
    that place, and the date and time of that departure.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed manner

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The information referred
to in subsection 93(1) of the Act must be given to the Agency by
electronic means in accordance with the technical requirements,
specifications and procedures for electronic data interchange set out in
the document entitled CBSA Carrier Messaging Requirements established by
the Agency, as amended from time to time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information --- incomplete or inaccurate

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Any person who becomes
aware that they have given incomplete or inaccurate information to the
Agency under subsection 93(1) of the Act must, without delay and in the
manner referred to in section 12, give the missing or accurate
information to the Agency.
