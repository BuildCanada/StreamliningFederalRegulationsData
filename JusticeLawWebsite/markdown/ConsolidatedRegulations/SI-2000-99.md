## Tr'ondëk Hwëch'in (GST) Remission Order {#trondëk-hwëchin-gst-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2000-99
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2000-11-08

Tr'ondëk Hwëch'in (GST) Remission Order

P.C. 2000-1662  2000-10-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of Finance, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed
[Tr'ondëk Hwëch'in (GST) Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2000-99).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605020 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Tr'ondëk Hwëch'in
    Self-Government Agreement signed on July 16, 1998, as it read on
    November 2, 2000. ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    123(1) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15).
    ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605024 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to a person, to the extent and in the manner that a refund of tax paid
by the person is provided for in sections 15.7 to 15.11 of the
agreement, of tax under Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15)
that was paid by the person during the period beginning on September 15,
1998 and ending on October 31, 2000, on condition that no refund of that
tax is payable under section 18.1 of the [Yukon First Nations
Self-Government Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.6).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605026 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on November 2, 2000.
