[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[large diameter line
pipe]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Order, [[large diameter line pipe]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
[[subject goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in paragraph 4 of the
statement of reasons of the Canadian International Trade Tribunal issued
on November 4, 2016 in Inquiry Number NQ-2016-001.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), remission is granted of the anti-dumping duties
  that were paid under the [Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15)
  in respect of large diameter line pipe that was imported for the
  purpose of use by or on behalf of a company set out in column 1 of the
  schedule and recorded under a transaction number (B3-3 Form) set out
  in column 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted on the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
    Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years after the
    day on which this Order comes into force; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on request of the Canada Border Services Agency,
    the claimant files all evidence that is required by the Agency to
    determine eligibility for remission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
