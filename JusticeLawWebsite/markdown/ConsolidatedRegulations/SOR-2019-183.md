## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1136792 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Preclearance Act,
2016](/eng/acts/P-19.32).

## [Preclearance Area]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1136795 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized persons

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 17(d) of the Act, the following persons may
  enter a preclearance area:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder of a pass --- issued by or under the
    authority of the operator of a facility that entitles the holder to
    have access during a specified period to the preclearance area ---
    who must enter the preclearance area in order to perform the
    functions of their employment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who is authorized by the preclearance
    officer to enter the preclearance area;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who is authorized to administer or enforce
    Canadian law who must enter the preclearance area for that purpose;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a provider of emergency services who must enter the
    preclearance area in the performance of their duties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification and surveillance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph 17(e) of the Act, the
  operator of a facility must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person that they have authorized
    to enter the preclearance area in order to perform urgent repair,
    notify a preclearance officer of that entry and ensure the constant
    surveillance of the person while they are in the area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other person that they have
    authorized to enter the preclearance area, give a preclearance
    officer reasonable notice of the entry and ensure the constant
    surveillance of the person while they are in the area.

## [Identification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1136807 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Citizen under the age of 16

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 18(1)(a) of the Act, a traveller bound for the United States
by any mode of transportation other than air does not require
identification that contains their photograph if they are a Canadian
citizen under the age of 16.

## [Obligations in Preclearance Area]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1136810 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations under subsection 18(3) of the
Act

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 18(3) of the Act, a person must, if directed by a
preclearance officer,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} report to a preclearance officer, identify themselves
  and state their reason for being in a preclearance area; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} leave a preclearance area if they are not authorized
  to be there.

## [Obligations of the Operator of a Facility]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1136815 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Armed police officer

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of a facility must, if preclearance officers are not
  permitted to be armed in a preclearance area or in a preclearance
  perimeter, ensure the continuous presence of at least one armed police
  officer in the facility during the hours in which preclearance
  activities are ongoing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Patrols and response

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator of the facility must ensure that the
  armed police officer makes regular patrols within the preclearance
  areas and preclearance perimeters in which preclearance officers are
  not permitted to be armed and responds rapidly and in person to any
  emergency calls from, or alarms activated by, a preclearance officer.

## [Preclearance Consultative Group]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1136821 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inform in writing

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A traveller who elects
under section 26.1 of the Act to inform the Canadian senior officials of
the Preclearance Consultative Group of any situation referred to in
sections 22, 23 and 24, subsection 31(2) and section 32 of the Act must
do so in writing.

## [Seizure and Detention]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1136824 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of certain seized goods

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  preclearance officer must, as soon as feasible after seizing any of
  the following goods under subsection 34(1) or (3) of the Act, transfer
  them for disposal to a peace officer or a person who is authorized to
  enforce a relevant Act of Parliament:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods that are subject to the public health, food
    inspection or plant and animal health laws of either Canada or the
    United States;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods that are, or contain, [[nuclear
    substances]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 2 of the
    [Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} goods that are [[dangerous
    goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 2 of the
    [Transportation of Dangerous Goods Act, 1992](/eng/acts/T-19.01).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to traveller

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The preclearance officer must immediately give to the
  traveller a written notice advising them of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the goods are seized under subsection 34(1)
    or (3) of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for the seizure; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that the goods will be transferred for disposal as
    soon as feasible to a person who is authorized to administer or
    enforce a relevant Act of Parliament.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to traveller --- other seized
goods

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A preclearance officer who,
under subsection 34(1) or (3) of the Act, seizes goods other than goods
described in paragraphs 7(1)(a) to (c) of these Regulations must
immediately give to the traveller a written notice advising them of the
following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the goods are seized under subsection 34(1)
  or (3) of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for the seizure;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the requirements under the laws of the
  United States that must be complied with before the goods may be
  imported into the United States; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the steps that must be taken in order
  to avoid forfeiture of the goods to the Government of the United
  States.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of detained goods

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A preclearance officer
must, as soon as feasible after detaining, under subsection 34(2) of the
Act, any of the goods described in paragraphs 7(1)(a) to (c) of these
Regulations, transfer the goods to a peace officer or a person who is
authorized to enforce a relevant Act of Parliament.
