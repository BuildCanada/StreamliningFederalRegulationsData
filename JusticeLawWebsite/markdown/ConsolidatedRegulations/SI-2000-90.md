## Order Directing that Certain Documents be Discontinued {#order-directing-that-certain-documents-be-discontinued .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2000-90
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2000-10-25

Order Directing that Certain Documents be Discontinued

P.C. 2000-1547  2000-10-04
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas it appears to the Governor in Council that the documents set out
in the annexed schedule, each required by an Act of Parliament to be
laid before one or both Houses of Parliament, contain the same
information as or less information than is contained in the Public
Accounts or in any estimates of expenditures submitted to Parliament;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to section 157[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-2000-90_e_hq_5129){#fn_SI-2000-90_e_hq_5129-ID0EDBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby directs
that the documents set out in the annexed schedule be discontinued and
hereafter they need not be prepared or laid before either House of
Parliament.
