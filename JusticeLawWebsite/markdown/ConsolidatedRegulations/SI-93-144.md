## Order Transferring to the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources the Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister of Forestry and Amalgamating and Combining the Department of Energy, Mines and Resources and the Department of Forestry under the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources {#order-transferring-to-the-minister-of-energy-mines-and-resources-the-powers-duties-and-functions-of-the-minister-of-forestry-and-amalgamating-and-combining-the-department-of-energy-mines-and-resources-and-the-department-of-forestry-under-the-minister-of-energy-mines-and-resources .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-144
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1993-07-14

Order Transferring to the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources the
Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister of Forestry and
Amalgamating and Combining the Department of Energy, Mines and Resources
and the Department of Forestry under the Minister of Energy, Mines and
Resources

P.C. 1993-1490  1993-06-25
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
  Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), to transfer
  to the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources all the powers, duties
  and functions of the Minister of Forestry under any Act of Parliament;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(b) of that Act, to amalgamate
  and combine the Department of Energy, Mines and Resources and the
  Department of Forestry under the Minister of Energy, Mines and
  Resources and under the Deputy Minister of Energy, Mines and
  Resources.
