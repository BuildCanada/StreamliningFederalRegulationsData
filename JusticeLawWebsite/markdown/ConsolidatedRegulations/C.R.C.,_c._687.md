## Defence Floating Equipment Rental Order {#defence-floating-equipment-rental-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 687
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Authorizing the Minister of National Defence to Rent Floating
Equipment
::::

:::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560479 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Defence Floating Equipment Rental
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._687).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560481 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order, [[floating
equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means large, medium and small tugs,
derricks and flat scows owned by, or under charter agreement to, the
Department of National Defence.

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560483 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of National
Defence is authorized to rent on request, when commercial facilities are
not available, floating equipment at the prevailing commercial rates for
similar equipment in the area.

## [Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560485 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An agreement in the form
set out in the schedule shall be signed by the firm or individual
wishing to rent floating equipment.

In consideration of the equipment known as ![blank

- ::::: listItemBlock1

  [(1)]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText2
  To pay to Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada the rental set
  forth above for the period during which the equipment is used by, or
  is held available for the use of, the Charterer, which period includes
  the time during which the equipment is moving or being moved to or
  from the place where it is intended to be used.

- ::::: listItemBlock1
  ::: listItemLabel
  [(2)]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText2
  To indemnify Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada for any damage
  to or loss of the equipment or any part thereof caused by the
  negligence of the Charterer, his or its servants, agents or employees.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock1
  ::: listItemLabel
  [(3)]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText2
  To indemnify and save harmless Her Majesty the Queen in right of
  Canada from and against all claims for loss, costs, damages or
  expenses which may be incurred by reason of the use of said equipment,
  except where such claims occur through the negligence or wrongful act
  of Her Majesty's agents or servants.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock1
  ::: listItemLabel
  [(4)]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText2
  That the hiring of the equipment to the Charterer may be terminated at
  any time by Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada should it be
  deemed necessary or expedient by the Naval Officer in Charge of the
  equipment.
  :::

- ::::: listItemBlock1
  ::: listItemLabel

  :::

  ::: listItemText2
