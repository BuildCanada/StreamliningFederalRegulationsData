## Special Force Superannuation Regulations {#special-force-superannuation-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1586
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE SUPERANNUATION ACT](/eng/acts/P-36)

[VETERANS BENEFIT ACT](/eng/acts/V-1.3)

Regulations Respecting the Inclusion under the Public Service
Superannuation Act of Members of the Special Force
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530314 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Special Force Superannuation
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1586).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530316 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contributor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contributor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is a
    contributor under the Act; ([contributeur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[special force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[special force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
    section 2 of the [Veterans Benefit Act](/eng/acts/V-1.3).
    ([contingent spécial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Election to Count Pensionable Service]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530321 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to subsection 4(1),
every contributor may elect, under the Act, to count any period of his
service on the strength of the special force as pensionable service for
the purposes of the Act as though that service were active service in
the forces during World War II within the meaning of the Act.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  contributor to whom the Defence Services Pension Act applied was
  entitled under that Act to count as pensionable service for the
  purposes of that Act the period of his service on the strength of the
  special force, he may elect only under section 27 of the Act to count
  that period of service as pensionable service for the purposes of the
  Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a contributor on the
  strength of the special force who was absent from the Public Service
  having been granted leave of absence to enlist in the special force.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A contributor on the
strength of the special force who was absent from the Public Service
having been granted leave of absence to enlist in the special force
shall be deemed, for the purposes of the Act, to have been a contributor
under Part I of the Civil Service Superannuation Act who was absent from
the Public Service on active service in the forces during World War II,
having been granted leave of absence to enlist.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every election made by a
contributor under these Regulations shall be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} made by him while he is employed in the Public
  Service;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} evidenced in writing in the form prescribed by the
  President of the Treasury Board; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} made by him within one year of his becoming a
  contributor under the Act.
