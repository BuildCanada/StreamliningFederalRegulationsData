## [Expressions Defined]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031413 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of section
21.1 of the [Foreign Publishers Advertising Services
Act](/eng/acts/F-29.6), the following expressions are defined.

[[revenues generated by the supply of advertising services directed at the Canadian market]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[revenues generated by the supply of advertising services directed
    at the Canadian market]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an issue
    of a periodical and any material accompanying the periodical whether
    it is attached or not, means the total amount of space on a page or
    any part of a page or any lines on a page dedicated to paid
    advertisements that are placed by a Canadian advertiser, or a person
    acting on their behalf, and that are directed at the Canadian
    market. ([revenus qui proviennent des services publicitaires
    destinés au marché canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[revenues generated by the total supply of advertising services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[revenues generated by the total supply of advertising
    services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an issue of a
    periodical and any material accompanying the periodical whether it
    is attached or not, means the total amount of space on a page or any
    part of a page or any lines on a page dedicated to paid
    advertisements. ([revenus publicitaires totaux]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031417 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on July 1, 1999.
