## [Rules of Origin]{.HTitleText1} {#h-856279 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following provisions of the Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement
  for Trans-Pacific Partnership have the force of law in Canada:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Articles 3.1 to 3.18;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Annex 3-C;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Annex 3-D, including Appendix 1;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} paragraphs 1 to 9 of Article 4.2; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Annex 4-A, including Appendix 1.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(c), between Canada and
  Australia and between Canada and Malaysia, for the purposes of
  determining whether a good of heading 87.03 qualifies as originating,
  the applicable product-specific rule of origin is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a change to a good of subheading 87.03 from any
    other heading; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no change in the tariff classification required for
    a good of heading 87.03, provided there is a regional value content
    of not less than

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 40%, under the net cost method, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 50%, under the build-down method.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-856292 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Ind1B83_hq_23530){#fn_Ind1B83_hq_23530-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 43 of the [Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for
Trans-Pacific Partnership Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-36.45),
chapter 23 of the Statutes of Canada 2018 comes into force, but if they
are registered after that day, they come into force on the day on which
they are registered.
