## [Designation of Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-694626 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** A provision of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) or the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) or a
regulation made under any of those Acts that is set out in column 1 of
Schedules 1 or 2 is designated for the purpose of subsection 109.1(1) of
the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

## [Short-form Descriptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-694628 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  short-form descriptions of a designated provision that is set out in
  column 1 of an item in Schedules 1 or 2 are the descriptions set out
  in column 2 of that item.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 109.3(4) of the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), the applicable short-form description of a
  designated provision shall be used on a notice of assessment referred
  to in subsection 109.3(1) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) to
  describe a contravention of the provision.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-694632 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
