the Minister of Health, pursuant to section 37 of the [Canada Consumer

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832030 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[ignition compound]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ignition compound]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the chemical
    compound used to cover the end of the splint of the match and to
    make the match head. ([corps inflammable]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[lot]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lot]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a stock, batch, shipment or
    delivery lot of matches. ([lot]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[striking surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[striking surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that part of a book,
    box or other container of matches that is for the purpose of
    igniting matches. ([frottoir]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[wood matches]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wood matches]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} do not include wood matches
    that are attached to a common base. ([allumette en
    bois]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Specifications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832037 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matches

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Matches
  must meet the following requirements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the match must not contain any white phosphorus;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the ignition compound must be uniformly applied;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the match head must not be chipped, cracked or
    crumbling and must not adhere to the head of another match.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional requirement --- wood matches

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The splint of a wood match must be made of good grade
  and long-fibre wood and must not be broken, split or crushed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matchbooks

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A matchbook must meet the
following requirements:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} its cover must, when closed, cover all the match
  heads;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any fastening wire used to secure its cover to the
  common base of the matches must not pierce or loosen the striking
  surface;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any friction paint on the inside of the cover must
  not be present within 2.54 cm of the match heads; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} its striking surface must be on the side that is
  opposite to the side that opens.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Spontaneous ignition

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Matches must not ignite
spontaneously when tested in accordance with Schedule 1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-disintegration of ignition compound

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The ignition compound must
not, when ignited, crack, explode or drop as glowing particles from the
match.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Afterglow

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The splint of a match must
not glow for more than three seconds after the flame has been
extinguished.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Breaking resistance of splints

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** When tested in accordance
with Schedule 2, the splint of a match must withstand the following
moments without breaking:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a wood match, 700 gf∙cm;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a round match other than a wood match,
  100 gf∙cm; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in any other case,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 300 gf∙cm, when the match is placed on its edge,
    and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 100 gf∙cm, when the match is placed flat.

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832065 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Name and mailing address

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Every matchbook, box or
other container that immediately covers or encloses the matches must
carry on it the name and mailing address of the following, in a clear
and legible manner:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer, if the matches are manufactured in
  Canada; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the importer or distributor, if the matches are
  imported.

## [Compliance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832070 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matches

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** In order to determine
whether a lot of matches complies with the requirements of paragraphs
2(1)(b) and (c), subsection 2(2) and sections 5 to 7, the following
procedures are to be used:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} select at random, based on the number of matches in a
  lot set out in Column 1 of Table 1 of Schedule 3, a sample that
  consists of the number of matches set out in Column 2;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a match fails to meet any requirement set out in
  sections 5 to 7, determine that the match has a major defect;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if a match fails to meet a requirement set out in
  paragraphs 2(1)(b) or (c) or subsection 2(2), determine that the match
  has a minor defect; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the number of matches having a major defect is
  equal to or greater than the number of matches set out in Column 3 of
  the same table, or if the number of matches having a minor defect is
  equal to or greater than the number of matches set out in Column 4 of
  that table, determine that the lot does not comply with the
  requirements set out in paragraph 2(1)(b) or (c), subsection 2(2) or
  any of sections 5 to 7, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matchbooks

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** In order to determine
whether a lot of matchbooks complies with the requirements of paragraphs
3(a) to (c), the following procedures are to be used:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} select at random, based on the number of matchbooks
  in a lot set out in Column 1 of Table 2 of Schedule 3, a sample that
  consists of the number of matchbooks set out in Column 2;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a matchbook fails to meet any requirement in
  paragraphs 3(a) to (c), determine that the matchbook has a defect; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the number of matchbooks having a defect is equal
  to or greater than the number of matchbooks set out in Column 3 of the
  same table, determine that the lot does not comply with one or more of
  the requirements set out in paragraphs 3(a) to (c).

## [SCHEDULE 1]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 4)]{.OriginatingRef}[Spontaneous Ignition Test]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-832088 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The following method is to be used for testing the spontaneous
  ignition of matches:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} place, on a flat surface, a wooden block that is 22
    mm thick and 50 mm^2^ that has a hole that is 25 mm in diameter;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fill the hole in the block with the matches to be
    tested, so that the base of each match is resting on the flat
    surface, with the match heads pointing up;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} place the block in the centre of a convection oven
    at a temperature of 90 ± 1°C;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} place a temperature sensing device having a range
    of  65 to 120°C at a minimum distance of 6.5 mm and a maximum
    distance of 12 mm from, and at the same level as, the match heads;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} leave the matches in the oven for a minimum period
    of two hours.

## [SCHEDULE 2]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 7)]{.OriginatingRef}[Test for Breaking Resistance of Splints]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-832097 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The following method is to be used for testing the breaking
  resistance of splints:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} insert the match in the holding clamp of the
    apparatus as illustrated in the figure, while ensuring that the
    moment arm is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 2 cm in length, in the case of wood matches; or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 1.5 cm in length, in the case of matches other
      than wood matches;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} gradually lower the holding clamp until the match
    breaks; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} record the maximum force registered on the scale
    required to break the splint.
