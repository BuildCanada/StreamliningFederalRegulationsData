His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Employment and Immigration and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to section 15 of the [Canada Student Financial Assistance
Act](/eng/acts/S-22.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed Regulations respecting the making of loans
and the provision of other forms of financial assistance to students,
effective August 1, 1995.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973808 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  Act and these Regulations,

  [[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person to whom a loan is
      made pursuant to the Act; ([emprunteur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[consolidated student loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[consolidated student loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      a consolidated risk-shared loan agreement or a consolidated direct
      loan agreement, except in section 5 of the Act where it means a
      consolidated risk-shared loan agreement only; ([contrat de prêt
      consolidé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[course]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[course]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means formal instruction or
      training that constitutes, or is determined by a designated
      educational institution to be equivalent to, an essential element
      of a program of studies at a post-secondary school level at that
      institution, but does not include any formal instruction or
      practical training required for acceptance in a professional
      corporation or for the practice of any trade or profession unless
      that formal instruction or practical training is necessary to
      obtain a degree, certificate or diploma from that designated
      educational institution; ([cours]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[family income]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[family income]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the aggregate income
      --- including income from employment, social programs, investments
      and monetary gifts --- of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the borrower or student and their spouse or
        common-law partner, in the case of a borrower or student who is
        married or has a common-law partner,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the parents of the student, in the case of a
        full-time student who

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has never been married or in a common-law
          relationship,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has never had children,

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is pursuing education at a post-secondary
          level within four years of leaving secondary school,

        - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} has not been in the labour force for more
          than two periods of 12 consecutive months since leaving
          secondary school, and

        - [(v)]{.lawlabel} is not applying for assistance under Part V
          or VII; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the borrower or student, in any other
        case; ([revenu familial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[family physician]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[family physician]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is
      entitled under the laws of a province to practise family medicine
      and who is so practising or who is in a family medicine residency
      program accredited by the College of Family Physicians of
      Canada; ([médecin de famille]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[financial assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[financial assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any form of
      financial aid provided under the Act, including student
      loans; ([aide financière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a bank or authorized foreign bank within the
        meaning of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01),

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a credit union, caisse populaire or other
        cooperative credit society,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a company within the meaning of the Trust and
        Loans Company Act, or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Canada Post Corporation; ([institution
        financière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[full-time student]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[full-time student]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} who

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} during a confirmed period within a period of
          studies, is enrolled in courses that constitute at least 60
          per cent of a course load recognized by the designated
          educational institution as constituting a full course load,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has as their primary occupation during that
          confirmed period the pursuit of studies in those courses, and

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} meets the requirements of subsection 5(1)
          or 7(1) or section 7.01 or 33, as the case may be; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} who elects to be considered as a full-time
        student under section 2.1; ([étudiant à temps
        plein]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[loan year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[loan year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period commencing on
      August 1 in any year and ending on July 31 in the following
      year; ([année de prêt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[nurse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[nurse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is entitled under
      the laws of a province to practise as a nurse and who is so
      practising; ([infirmier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[nurse practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[nurse practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is
      entitled under the laws of a province to practise as a nurse
      practitioner and who is so practising; ([infirmier
      practicien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[part-time student]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[part-time student]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} who, during a confirmed period within a period
        of studies, is enrolled in courses that constitute at least 20
        per cent but less than 60 per cent of a course load recognized
        by the designated educational institution as constituting a full
        course load, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} who meets the requirements of subsection 12(1),
        12.1(1) or 12.2(1) or section 33, as the case may be; ([étudiant
        à temps partiel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[period of studies]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[period of studies]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the length of time
      that a designated educational institution considers to be a normal
      school year for the program of studies in which the qualifying
      student or the borrower is enrolled and that, where the period
      between the day on which that person ceased to be a full-time
      student pursuant to section 8 or a part-time student pursuant to
      section 12.3, as the case may be, and the first day of the first
      confirmed period of the current school year is less than six
      months, includes that period; ([période d'études]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[permanent disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[permanent disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any impairment,
      including a physical, mental, intellectual, cognitive, learning,
      communication or sensory impairment --- or a functional limitation
      --- that restricts the ability of a person to perform the daily
      activities necessary to pursue studies at a post-secondary school
      level or to participate in the labour force and that is expected
      to remain with the person for the person's expected
      life; ([invalidité permanente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[persistent or prolonged disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[persistent or prolonged disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      any impairment, including a physical, mental, intellectual,
      cognitive, learning, communication or sensory impairment --- or a
      functional limitation --- that restricts the ability of a person
      to perform the daily activities necessary to pursue studies at a
      post-secondary school level or to participate in the labour force
      and has lasted, or is expected to last, for a period of at least
      12 months but is not expected to remain with the person for the
      person's expected life; ([invalidité persistante ou
      prolongée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[post-secondary school level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[post-secondary school level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      education at a university or college level, including education of
      a technical or vocational nature; ([niveau
      postsecondaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[program of studies]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[program of studies]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the series of
      periods of studies

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is considered by the designated
        educational institution to be necessary to obtain a degree,
        certificate or diploma,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is recognized by the appropriate authority
        which designated that institution under the Act or the [Canada
        Student Loans Act](/eng/acts/S-23), or any successor to that
        authority, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of which is at least 12 weeks
        within a period of 15 consecutive weeks; ([programme
        d'études]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[severe permanent disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[severe permanent disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
      impairment, including a physical, mental, intellectual, cognitive,
      learning, communication or sensory impairment --- or a functional
      limitation --- that prevents a person from performing the daily
      activities necessary to participate in the labour force in a
      manner that is [[substantially gainful]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
      defined in section 68.1 of the [Canada Pension Plan
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._385), and is expected to
      remain with the person for their expected life; ([invalidité grave
      et permanente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[student loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[student loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a risk-shared loan or a
      direct loan except

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in sections 5, 10 and 11 and paragraph 15(l) of
        the Act, where it means a risk-shared loan only,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in subsection 14(2) of the Act, where it means
        a guaranteed student loan; ([prêt d'études]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2012-41, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[student loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[student loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a contract
      entered into before August 1, 2000 between a qualifying student
      and a lender that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is in the prescribed form, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} includes the student's social insurance
        number; ([contrat de prêt simple]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[under-served rural or remote community]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[under-served rural or remote
      community]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a population centre with a
      population of no more than 30,000 based on data published in the
      most recent census of population or a rural area, based on data
      published in that census. ([collectivité rurale ou éloignée mal
      desservie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations,

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Student Financial

  [[apprentice loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

      least one year; ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[confirmation of enrolment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[confirmation of enrolment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      prescribed form, which may or may not form part of a certificate
      of eligibility, and where it does not, includes the qualifying
      student's or borrower's social insurance number; ([confirmation
      d'inscription]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[confirmed period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[confirmed period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a period of
      studies, or part thereof, which is at least six consecutive weeks
      and which

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a confirmation of enrolment
        forming part of a certificate of eligibility, begins on the
        first day of the month specified by the designated educational
        institution and ends on the last day of the last month of the
        period of studies specified by the appropriate authority, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a confirmation of enrolment not
        forming part of a certificate of eligibility, begins on the
        first day of the month specified by, and ends on the last day of
        the other month specified by, the designated educational
        institution; ([période confirmée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[consolidated direct loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[consolidated direct loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a

      any full-time direct loan agreement and that includes the
      borrower's social insurance number; ([contrat de prêt direct
      consolidé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[consolidated guaranteed student loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[consolidated guaranteed student loan
      agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as subsection
      2(2) of the [Canada Student Loans
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-392); ([contrat de prêt
      garanti consolidé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[consolidated risk-shared loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[consolidated risk-shared loan
      agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a contract that is entered
      into between a borrower who has ceased to be a full-time student
      pursuant to section 8 and the lender to whom the borrower is
      indebted under any full-time risk-shared loan agreement and

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is in the prescribed form,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that includes the borrower's social insurance
        number,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in which the principal amount is the aggregate
        of the outstanding principal amounts under those agreements,

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that replaces those agreements, and

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} that includes the terms and conditions of
        repayment of the principal amount of the loan and the interest
        payable thereunder; ([contrat de prêt à risque partagé
        consolidé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[direct loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[direct loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a debt obligation

  :   [[direct loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a full-time

  [[direct student loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[direct student loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a

## [Election]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1373355 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person with a disability

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** A person who has either
a permanent disability or a persistent or prolonged disability and who,
during a confirmed period within a period of studies, is enrolled in
courses that constitute at least 40 per cent but less than 60 per cent
of a course load recognized by the designated educational institution as
constituting a full course load, may elect to be considered as a
full-time student.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Student Loans Made to Full-Time Students]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973908 .Part}

### [Obtaining a Direct Loan]{.HTitleText2} {#h-973909 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 15, the Minister may make a direct loan to a qualifying
  student who is enrolled as a full-time student at a designated
  educational institution and who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is issued, or in respect of whom is issued, a
    certificate of eligibility;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 30 days after obtaining confirmation of
    their enrolment from an officer of the designated educational
    institution but no later than the last day of the confirmed period,
    submits the confirmation of enrolment

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the Minister unless they are notified in
      writing by the appropriate authority that the confirmation of
      enrolment is to be submitted to the Minister by the designated

  :   [[period of postponement of repayment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      the period during which, by reason of a medical leave or parental

      to be paid by the borrower in respect of a student loan. ([différé
      de remboursement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  and manner specified by the Minister in the six months after the end
  of the borrower's most recent period of studies, but no later than 12
  months after

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a medical leave, the date specified
    by the medical professional; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a parental leave, the date of the
    birth or adoption of a child or the date the borrower becomes the
    guardian or tutor of a child.

  the borrower is deemed to continue to be a full-time student despite
  section 8 for a six-month period beginning on the first day of the

  a full-time student.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the borrower's medical leave or parental leave

  a new parental leave, the borrower may, no earlier than 30 days before
  the end of the six-month period referred to in subsection (3) and no
  later than 30 days after that period, submit a request to the Minister

  months.

  must be further extended or the borrower is entitled to a new medical
  leave or a new parental leave, the borrower may, no earlier than 30
  days before the end of the 12-month period referred to in

### [Medical Leave and Parental Leave]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1261855 .Subheading}

- **[[12.21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, on application,

  medical leave or parental leave, if the borrower submits the

  studies, but no later than 12 months after

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a medical leave, the date specified

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a parental leave, the date of the

  a part-time student.

  must be extended or the borrower is entitled to a new medical leave or
  a new parental leave, the borrower may, no earlier than 30 days before
  the end of the six-month period referred to in subsection (2) and no
  later than 30 days after that period, submit a request to the Minister
  to extend the length of the period of postponement of repayment to 12
  months.

  must be further extended or the borrower is entitled to a new medical
  leave or a new parental leave, the borrower may, no earlier than 30
  days before the end of the 12-month period referred to in
  subsection (3) and no later than 30 days after that period, submit a
  request to the Minister to extend the length of the period of
  postponement of repayment to 18 months.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A borrower who has been granted a period of
  postponement of repayment cannot submit a new application for
  postponement of repayment by reason of a new medical leave or a new
  parental leave for 30 days after the first day of the current
  confirmed period.

  subrogated in and to all the rights of the lender in respect of the
  risk-shared loan and, without limiting the generality of the
  foregoing, all rights and powers of the lender in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the risk-shared loan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any judgment obtained by the lender in respect of
    the risk-shared loan; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any security held by the lender for the repayment
    of the risk-shared loan.

  exercise all the rights, powers and privileges that the lender had or
  may exercise in respect of the loan, judgment or security, including
  the right to commence or continue any action or proceeding, to execute
  any release, transfer, sale or assignment, or in any way collect,
  realize or enforce the loan, judgment or security.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- SOR/2009-143, s. 23

  - [23.]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of a
    confirmed period that begins before August 1, 2009.

- --- SOR/2012-254, s. 9

  - [9.]{.lawlabel} The loan forgiveness provided for in these
    Regulations applies in respect of a year beginning on or after April
    1, 2012.

- --- SOR/2023-227, s. 3

  - [3]{.lawlabel} An application for loan forgiveness that is made in
    respect of a year that ends before the day on which these
    Regulations come into force is to be governed by the [Canada Student
    Loans Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-392) and the [Canada
    Student Financial Assistance
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-329) as they read immediately
    before that day.

- --- SOR/2024-210, s. 8

  - [8]{.lawlabel} An [[under-served rural or remote
    community]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of
    the [Canada Student Financial Assistance
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-329) immediately before the day
    on which these Regulations come into force continues to be an
    under-served rural or remote community for the purposes of section
    29 of those Regulations until November 1, 2027 or the day that is 6
    months after the day on which a census is published by Statistics
    Canada, whichever is later.
