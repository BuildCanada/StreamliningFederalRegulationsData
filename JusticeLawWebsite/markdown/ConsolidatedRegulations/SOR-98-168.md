## Farm Debt Mediation Regulations {#farm-debt-mediation-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-168

of the [Farm Debt Mediation Act](/eng/acts/F-2.27)[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018373 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Farm Debt Mediation
      Act](/eng/acts/F-2.27). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day that is not a
      Saturday or a holiday.  ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to a
      farmer or a creditor, a person who is cohabiting with a farmer or
      creditor in a conjugal relationship, having so cohabitated for a
      period of at least one year. ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Farm Debt Mediation
      Service of the Department of Agriculture and Agri-Food. ([Service
      de médiation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 20 and subsection 22(2)
  of the Act, a person is related to a farmer or a creditor if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a farmer or a creditor who is an
    individual, the person is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the spouse or common-law partner of the farmer or
      the creditor,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a child of the farmer or the creditor or of the
      farmer's or the creditor's spouse or common-law partner,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a brother, sister, mother, father, grandmother
      or grandfather of the farmer or the creditor or of the farmer's or
      the creditor's spouse or common-law partner,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an employee or employer of the farmer or the
      creditor at the time the original application for mediation was
      filed with the Service, or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any other person who acquires property of the
      farmer from the farmer under terms and conditions that are more
      favourable than those that would apply to a similar transaction in
      an open market between parties who are at arm's length; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a farmer or a creditor that is a
    corporation, cooperative, partnership or other association, the
    person

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} was an officer, director, partner or employee of
      the farmer or the creditor at the time the original application
      for mediation was filed with the Service, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} directly owns or indirectly owns, controls or
      holds 25 per cent or more of the shares of the corporation or
      cooperative.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-289, s. 1

## [Appointment of Mediators]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018392 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** An administrator may
appoint a person as mediator pursuant to paragraph 10(1)(a) of the Act
if the person is knowledgeable about or has experience with the
mediation of disputes.

## [Extension of Stay of Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018394 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An administrator who
considers it essential to the formulation of an arrangement between a
farmer and the farmer's creditors may extend the period of a stay of
proceedings referred to in paragraph 7(1)(b) or subsection 13(1) of the
Act if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the value of the farmer's assets will not
  significantly diminish during the period of the extension;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the majority of the farmer's creditors will not be
  unduly prejudiced by the extension; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} there is no indication of bad faith by the farmer.

## [Method of Notification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018399 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  administrator shall, if required by the Act or these Regulations to
  notify or inform a farmer, a farmer's creditor or the Minister of
  anything, notify or inform them in person or by means of telephone,
  priority post, courier service or, subject to subsection (3),
  facsimile or electronic mail.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A farmer, a farmer's creditor or the Minister is
  deemed to be notified or informed by an administrator

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the day they are so notified or informed in
    person or personally by telephone by the administrator;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the next business day after the day on which the
    notice or information is sent by facsimile or electronic mail; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the second business day after the day on which
    the notice or information is sent by priority post or a courier
    service to their residence or place of business.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A farmer may be notified or informed by an
  administrator by means of facsimile or electronic mail only if they
  have indicated in writing to the administrator that they will accept
  information in this manner.

- SOR/2016-289, s. 2

## [Appeal Boards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018409 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of dealing
with appeals pursuant to subsection 15(2) of the Act, an Appeal Board is
constituted in respect of each of the following regions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the West, consisting of Manitoba, British Columbia,
  Saskatchewan, Alberta, Yukon, the Northwest Territories and Nunavut;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the East, consisting of Ontario, Quebec, Nova Scotia,
  New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island and Newfoundland and Labrador.

- [(c) to (e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2016-289, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-289, s. 3
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-5-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 5"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  Appeal Board is composed of a Chairperson and up to four other members
  designated by the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each member of an Appeal Board shall provide services
  in accordance with the terms of an agreement referred to in subsection
  15(1) of the Act.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each agreement referred to in subsection 15(1) of the
  Act shall provide that the member is entitled to, in accordance with
  any directives of the Treasury Board,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} remuneration for services; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} travel and living expenses incurred by the member
    in the performance of the member's duties while absent from the
    member's ordinary place of residence.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A member shall not accept or hold any office or
  employment inconsistent with the member's duties, including performing
  any of the duties of an expert, mediator or administrator under the
  Act.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} When it is necessary for the proper conduct of
  business, the Chairperson of the Appeal Board of one region may
  appoint a member of that Appeal Board, including himself or herself,
  to serve as a temporary member of the Appeal Board of the other
  region.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-289, s. 4
:::

[Previous
Version](section-6-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 6"){.PITLink}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson of each Appeal Board shall preside at all meetings of the
  Appeal Board or a panel referred to in subsection 12(1) and has the
  supervision over, and direction of the work of, that Appeal Board
  including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the establishment of panels of members to deal with
    appeals;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the allocation of work equally among the members;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the temporary appointment of members to the Appeal
    Board of the other region; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} generally, the conduct of the work of the Appeal
    Board and its internal affairs.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson of each Appeal Board shall designate
  one of the other members as an interim Chairperson.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  Chairperson, the interim Chairperson may exercise all the powers,
  duties and functions of the Chairperson.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of an
  interim Chairperson or if the interim Chairperson is unwilling to act
  as Chairperson, the remaining members of the Appeal Board shall by a
  majority vote elect a member to act as temporary Chairperson.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A temporary Chairperson elected in accordance with
  subsection (4) may exercise all the powers, duties and functions of
  the Chairperson.

## [Appeal Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018438 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A farmer
  or a farmer's creditor may appeal a decision referred to in subsection
  15(2) of the Act by filing, within the time prescribed by section 11,
  a Notice of Appeal in the form established by the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Notice of Appeal shall be filed with the same
  office of the Service where the original Application for Mediation was
  submitted.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A Notice of Appeal or the statements referred to in
  paragraph 9(2)(b) and subsection 9(4) may be filed with the
  administrator in the form of an original document, a facsimile copy or
  an electronic version.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-289, s. 6
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-8-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 8"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  filing of a Notice of Appeal, the administrator shall inform the
  farmer or the farmer's creditors, as the case may be, and the Minister
  if the Minister is the guarantor of a debt of the farmer that is owed
  to one of the creditors, that an appeal has been filed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A farmer, a farmer's creditor or the Minister may,
  within one business day after the day on which they are informed of an
  appeal that relates to a termination of a stay of proceedings under
  paragraph 14(2)(c) or (d) of the Act or within three business days
  after the day on which they are informed of any other appeal

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} request the administrator to send them a copy of
    the Notice of Appeal; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} file with the administrator a statement in writing
    that clearly sets out their objections to the appeal together with
    any other relevant information.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The administrator shall inform the farmer or farmer's
  creditor who filed the Notice of Appeal that a statement referred to
  in paragraph (2)(b) has been filed by sending them a copy of the
  statement.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The farmer or farmer's creditor may, within one
  business day after the day on which they receive the copy of the
  statement referred to in paragraph (2)(b), file with the administrator
  a written statement in response.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-289, s. 7
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** After the time has
elapsed for filing statements under paragraph 9(2)(b) and subsection
9(4), the administrator shall send to the Chairperson of the applicable
Appeal Board

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all the information upon which the decision of the
  administrator under appeal was based; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any statements in writing and other relevant
  information that have been filed by a farmer, a farmer's creditor or
  the Minister.

## [Time for Filing of Appeals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018459 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appeal relating to the eligibility of a farmer to make an application
  under paragraph 5(1)(a) of the Act or the granting of an extension of
  a stay of proceedings under subsection 13(1) of the Act shall be made
  within four business days after the day on which the farmer or the
  farmer's creditor bringing the appeal is given notice under paragraph
  7(1)(b) of the Act of the initial stay of proceedings or under
  subsection 13(3) of the Act of the granting of the extension.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An appeal relating to the termination of a stay of
  proceedings shall be made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the termination is directed by the
    administrator pursuant to paragraph 14(2)(c) or (d) of the Act,
    within one business day after the day on which the farmer or
    farmer's creditor was informed of the termination; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, within four business days after
    the day on which the farmer or farmer's creditor was informed of the
    termination.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-289, s. 9
:::

## [Conduct of Appeals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018467 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  appeal shall be heard by a panel of the Appeal Board that consists of
  the Chairperson and two other members selected by the Chairperson.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each panel shall decide each appeal expeditiously and
  with a minimum of formality on the basis of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information sent to it pursuant to section 10;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} such other information as it considers relevant to
    deal with the appeal, if a farmer or a farmer's creditor, as the
    case may be, has been informed of, and given a reasonable
    opportunity to respond to that other information.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Every appeal shall be
decided by a majority of the members of the panel.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A member of the panel may
participate in an appeal by means of telephone or other means of
effective communication.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A panel may dismiss an
appeal or reverse the decision of an administrator.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The administrator shall
inform the farmer, the farmer's creditors and the Minister, if the
Minister is the guarantor of a debt of the farmer that is owed to one of
the creditors, of the decision of the Appeal Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-289, s. 10

## [Notice by Secured Creditors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018479 .Part}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  notice required to be given by a secured creditor to a farmer and an
  administrator under section 21 of the Act may be given

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a farmer who is an individual

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by leaving a copy of the notice with them,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by leaving a copy of the notice in a sealed
      envelope addressed to the farmer with anyone who appears to be an
      adult and who resides at the farmer's place of residence, and on
      the same day or the following day mailing another copy of the
      notice to the farmer at that place of residence, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of the notice by priority
      post, courier or registered mail addressed to the farmer;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a farmer who is a corporation,
    cooperative, partnership or other association

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by leaving a copy of the notice with an officer,
      director, partner or agent of the corporation, cooperative,
      partnership or other association,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by leaving a copy of the notice with a person at
      any place of business of the farmer who appears to be in control
      or management of the place of business, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of the notice by priority
      post, courier or registered mail addressed to the last known
      address of the head office or principal place of business of the
      farmer; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an administrator

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by leaving a copy of the notice at their office,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of the notice by electronic
      mail,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of the notice by facsimile,
      or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of the notice by regular mail,
      priority post, courier or registered mail.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notice given in the manner described in subparagraph
  (1)(a)(ii) or (iii), (b)(iii) or (c)(iv) is deemed to be given seven
  business days after the day on which the notice is sent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person who gives the notice need not produce an
  original document or have it in their possession.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-289, s. 11

## [Proof by Affidavit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018499 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The giving of any notice
or information required by the Act or these Regulations may be proved by
the affidavit of the person who gave it.
