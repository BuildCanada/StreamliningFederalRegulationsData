## Order Transferring from the Treasury Board to the Department of Public Works and Government Services the Control and Supervision of the Government Travel Modernization Office {#order-transferring-from-the-treasury-board-to-the-department-of-public-works-and-government-services-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-government-travel-modernization-office .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2004-6
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2004-01-28

Order Transferring from the Treasury Board to the Department of Public
Works and Government Services the Control and Supervision of the
Government Travel Modernization Office

P.C. 2004-5  2004-01-09
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers from the Treasury Board to the Department of Public Works and
Government Services the control and supervision of that portion of the
public service in the Treasury Board Secretariat known as the Government
Travel Modernization Office.
