[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-220

[DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORT ACT](/eng/acts/T-18)

Registration 1993-05-04

Regulations Respecting the Management, Maintenance, Proper Use and
Protection of the Historic Canals Administered by the Parks Canada
Agency

P.C. 1993-891 1993-05-04
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to sections 16 and 17 of the
[Department of Transport Act](/eng/acts/T-18), is pleased hereby to
revoke the Heritage Canals Regulations, made by Order in Council P.C.
1984-196 of January 19, 1984[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-93-220_e_hq_3838){#fn_SOR-93-220_e_hq_3838-ID0EDBAA},
and to make the annexed Regulations respecting the management,
maintenance, proper use and protection of the historic canals
administered by the Canadian Parks Service, in substitution therefor.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951289 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Historic Canals Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-220).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951291 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[approach wharf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approach wharf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that section of a wharf
    or tie-up wall in a historic canal designated by means of a sign or
    symbol as, or painted with a blue stripe indicating, a mooring space
    for vessels waiting to enter a lock; ([quai
    d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[boater campground]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[boater campground]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2015-134, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[campground]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[campground]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area in a historic canal
    where the public is permitted to camp; ([terrain de
    camping]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial craft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial craft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that carries
    persons or goods for hire or reward or that is used to provide
    another service for hire or reward, and includes a vessel chartered
    or hired for the purpose of pleasure by or on behalf of the persons
    carried on the vessel; ([embarcation commerciale]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[cultural resource]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cultural resource]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any work of nature
    or of human hand that is primarily of interest for its
    palaeontological, archeological, historic, cultural, scientific or
    aesthetic value, and includes, but is not limited to, a
    palaeontological, archeological, historic or natural site, structure
    or object or any remains, restoration or reconstruction thereof;
    ([ressource culturelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dredge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dredge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to remove, by digging,
    gathering, pulling out or otherwise removing, any material from a
    historic canal, particularly from water or wetlands, for the purpose
    of creating new channels or boat slips, making land, constructing or
    placing any in-water structures such as boat-houses, wharves or
    retaining walls, or maintaining previously dredged areas or openings
    to any upland mooring basins; ([draguer]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person appointed under the
    [Parks Canada Agency Act](/eng/acts/P-0.4) to work at a historic
    canal; ([employé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fill]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fill]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to place any material in water or
    wetlands for the purpose of making land, constructing wharves,
    stabilizing the shoreline, revetting or constructing works, building
    beaches or constructing foundations for boat-houses or other
    structures; ([remblayer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[historic canal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[historic canal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a canal set out in
    column I of Schedule I and includes the waters and any works or
    lands that belong to Canada and that are appertaining or incidental
    to the canal; ([canal historique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a registered vessel, the length
      shown on the Certificate of Registry, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other vessel, the length from
      the fore part of the head of the stem to the after part of the
      head of the stern post; ([longueur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[lock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a liftlock and a marine
    railway; ([écluse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[lying up]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lying up]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the continuous occupation by
    a vessel during the navigation season of an area in a historic canal
    designated for that purpose; ([séjour]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[moor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[moor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to secure a vessel to a wharf,
    buoy or canal wall, or to another vessel that is so secured, and
    includes to beach, store or anchor a vessel;
    ([amarrer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[natural resource]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[natural resource]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any soil, sand,
    gravel, rock, mineral, fossil or other natural material, and
    includes flora; ([ressource naturelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[navigation channel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[navigation channel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a navigable passage
    of water in a historic canal that is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an artificial channel, delimited
      by the shoreline, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} delimited by aids to navigation, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where not delimited by aids to navigation, 15
        m in width along each side of a line that indicates a navigation
        channel on Canadian Hydrographic Service charts; ([chenal de
        navigation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-profit charitable organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-profit charitable organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    organization that is operated for purposes other than profit, no
    part of the income of which is payable to or otherwise available for
    the personal benefit of any proprietor, member or shareholder of the
    organization; ([organisme de bienfaisance sans but
    lucratif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a vessel or vehicle,
    includes the authorized agent of the owner;
    ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a peace officer as
    defined in section 2 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46); ([agent
    de la paix]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person in charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person in charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a vessel,
    means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the owner of the vessel is on board, the
      owner and the operator of the vessel, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the owner of the vessel is not on board,
      the operator of the vessel; ([responsable]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[pleasure craft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pleasure craft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel other than a
    commercial craft; ([embarcation de plaisance]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[possession]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[possession]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as that term
    is given in subsection 4(3) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);
    ([possession]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person appointed
    under the [Parks Canada Agency Act](/eng/acts/P-0.4) who holds the
    office of superintendent and who has the responsibility for a
    historic canal, and includes any other person appointed under that
    Act who is authorized by that person to act on their behalf;
    ([directeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[traffic control device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[traffic control device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a sign, signal,
    marker or other device that regulates, warns or guides vessels,
    vehicles or pedestrians; ([dispositif de réglementation de la
    circulation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an amphibious craft, boat,
    canoe, air cushion vehicle, raft, ship, or other type of floating
    craft, and includes an aircraft while on the water, except when
    landing or taking off; ([bâtiment]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an organic or inorganic waste
    material, but does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} liquid that is free of solids and is derived from
      water used on a pleasure craft for household purposes, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} engine-cooling water and bilge water;
      ([déchets]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[wintering]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wintering]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the continuous occupation by
    a vessel during the non-navigation season of an area in a historic
    canal designated for that purpose. ([hivernage]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951331 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-951332 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided in Part V, these Regulations apply in respect of all historic
canals and any person, goods, vehicle or vessel in the historic canals.

### [Duties of the superintendent]{.HTitleText2} {#h-951334 .Subheading}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2002-191, s. 5(E)

\]
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent shall, before issuing a permit under these Regulations
  authorizing the applicant to engage in an activity, and in order to
  determine the terms and conditions specified in a permit, take into
  account the effects of the activity on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cultural resources, natural resources, structures,
    equipment and objects in the historic canal;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the safe navigation of vessels and the safe
    operation of locks, dams and bridges in the historic canal;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the safety of persons in the historic canal;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} wildlife and the eggs and habitat of wildlife in
    the historic canal; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the historic character of the historic canal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} post, in a historic canal, signs that are necessary
    to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} protect cultural resources, natural resources,
      structures, equipment and objects,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} ensure the safe navigation of vessels and the
      safe operation of locks, dams and bridges,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} ensure the safety of persons, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} protect wildlife or the eggs or habitat of
      wildlife;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} close, restrict or open to public use any area in a
    historic canal for the purposes of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} ensuring the safe navigation of vessels and the
      safe operation of locks, dams and bridges,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} maintaining the historic canal,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} holding events or activities referred to in
      subsection 16(1),

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} protecting cultural resources, natural
      resources, structures, equipment and objects,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} ensuring the safety of persons, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} protecting wildlife or the eggs or habitat of
      wildlife;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} restrict or schedule any activity in a historic
    canal for the purposes of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} ensuring the safe navigation of vessels and the
      safe operation of locks, dams and bridges,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} protecting cultural resources, natural
      resources, structures, equipment and objects,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} ensuring the safety of persons, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} protecting wildlife or the eggs or habitat of
      wildlife;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} give notice of the closing, restricting, opening or
    scheduling referred to in paragraph (b) or (c) by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} posting signs in the historic canal, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} using such other means as are likely to bring
      notice of the closing, restricting, opening or scheduling to the
      attention of persons affected by it; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} place, maintain, alter or remove traffic control
    devices in a historic canal.

### [Powers of a Peace Officer]{.HTitleText2} {#h-951368 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A peace officer may arrest
without warrant any person found committing an act in contravention of
these Regulations.

### [Permits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-951370 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person to whom a permit is issued under these Regulations shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the permit is issued authorizing the person
    or a vessel in the charge of the person to engage in an activity,
    ensure that the permit is carried

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on the person while the person is engaged in the
      activity, where the permit is issued under subsection 11(2),
      14(2), 15(2), 16(2), 19(2), 22(2), 44(2) or 45(2), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on board while the person or the vessel is
      engaged in the activity, where the permit is issued under
      subsection 13(2), 17(2), 39(2), 40(5), 42(2) or 43(2);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} produce the permit at the request of the
    superintendent or an employee;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} comply with the terms and conditions specified in
    the permit; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} notify the superintendent of any corrections to
    information provided in an application to obtain the permit as soon
    as possible after learning of them.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No permit is transferable to any other person or
  vessel.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may cancel a permit where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the purpose for which the permit was issued no
    longer applies; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person to whom the permit is issued

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} contravenes or fails to comply with the terms and
      conditions of the permit or these Regulations, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} provides incorrect, false or misleading
      information to the superintendent in an application to obtain the
      permit.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may amend a permit at no cost to
  the applicant.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-191, s. 5(E)
:::

- **[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the superintendent cancels a permit, the superintendent shall, as soon
  as feasible after making the decision, provide written notice of the
  cancellation, including reasons, to the permit holder.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person whose permit has been cancelled may submit
  a written request for a review of that decision to the Chief Executive
  Officer of the Parks Canada Agency within 30 days after the date of
  the notice.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Executive Officer shall, within 30 days
  after the day on which the request is received, complete the review
  and provide written notice of the decision, including reasons, to the
  person who requested it.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-134, s. 3
:::

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Controlled Activities and Areas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951394 .Part}

### [Prohibited Conduct]{.HTitleText2} {#h-951395 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} do anything that unreasonably disturbs or interferes
  with any person's use or enjoyment of a historic canal;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} behave in a manner that endangers the safety of any
  person in a historic canal;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} cause excessive noise in a historic canal between
  11:00 p.m. and 6:00 a.m.;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} operate a lock, lift or swing bridge, dam or
  wasteweir in a historic canal, or the gates or sluices of those
  structures, unless authorized to do so; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fish within 10 m of a lock or approach wharf or from
  a bridge over a navigation channel.

- SOR/2015-134, s. 4(F)

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Every person shall comply
with any sign posted or traffic control device placed in a historic
canal pursuant to subsection 4(2).

### [Restricted Activities and Areas]{.HTitleText2} {#h-951405 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No person, other than a
peace officer or an employee acting in the course of employment, shall,
in a historic canal, engage in an activity that is restricted or enter
into an area that is closed, where notice of the restriction or closure
has been given in accordance with paragraph 4(2)(d).

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Except at times and in
areas designated by means of a sign or notice, no person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a navigation channel or within 100 m of a
  structure in a historic canal, water-ski, ride a tube or kneeboard,
  engage in any activity that involves being towed behind a vessel or
  operate a vessel towing a water-skier or person riding a tube or
  kneeboard;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} dive, jump, scuba-dive, swim or bathe in a navigation
  channel or within 40 m of a lock gate or a dam in a historic canal;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} dive or jump from a bridge over a historic canal, or
  from the abutments of such a bridge; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} operate a vessel within 40 m of a dam in a historic
  canal, unless the vessel is in the navigation channel.

### [Resource Protection]{.HTitleText2} {#h-951412 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deposit in a historic canal, snow that has been
    collected during snow removal;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} discard waste in a historic canal, except in a
    receptacle designated for that purpose;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} discharge the contents of a holding tank in a
    historic canal, except at a pumpout station designated for that
    purpose; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} abandon or discard any other thing in a historic
    canal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall, except in accordance with a permit
  issued under subsection (3), remove, alter or destroy any cultural
  resource, natural resource, structure, equipment or object in a
  historic canal.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may, on receipt of an application,
  issue a permit authorizing the applicant to remove, alter or destroy a
  cultural resource, natural resource, structure, equipment or object,
  where the removal, alteration or destruction is necessary for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} scientific purposes; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the management of water levels or flows.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A permit issued under subsection (3) shall specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cultural resource, natural resource, structure,
    equipment or object, or the amount thereof, that may be removed,
    altered or destroyed and the location from which it may be removed
    or at which it may be altered or destroyed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the period for which the permit is valid.

occupies a property or facility in a historic canal shall, on vacating
the property or facility, leave it in a clean and orderly condition.

### [Transportation of Dangerous Goods]{.HTitleText2} {#h-951429 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person in charge of a vessel shall allow the vessel to transport any
  dangerous goods as defined in section 2 of the [Transportation of
  Dangerous Goods Act, 1992](/eng/acts/T-19.01) in a historic canal,
  except in accordance with a permit issued under subsection (2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may, on receipt of an application,
  issue a permit authorizing the applicant to transport dangerous goods
  in a historic canal.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A permit issued under subsection (2) shall specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the type of dangerous goods to be transported;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the dates and times when, and the route along
    which, the goods may be transported; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the period for which the permit is valid.

### [Dredge and Fill Operations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-951439 .Subheading}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall dredge, fill or dredge and fill in a historic canal,
  except in accordance with a permit issued under subsection (2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may, on receipt of an application,
  issue a permit authorizing the applicant to dredge, fill or dredge and
  fill in a historic canal.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A permit issued under subsection (2) shall specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dates and times when, the location at which,
    and the extent to which the dredging, filling or dredging and
    filling may be carried out; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the period for which the permit is valid.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may issue a stop work order, a
  restore order or both to a person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contravenes or fails to comply with the terms and
    conditions specified in a permit issued under subsection (2); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} dredges, fills or dredges and fills in a historic
    canal without a permit.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the superintendent issues a stop work order to
  a person, the person shall comply with the stop work order until such
  time as the superintendent issues a restore order.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where the superintendent issues a restore order to a
  person, the person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person to whom a permit was
    issued, restore at the person's risk and expense that part of the
    historic canal that was dredged, filled or dredged and filled,
    contrary to the terms and conditions of the permit, to the condition
    that it was in prior to the dredging, filling or dredging and
    filling or, where applicable, to the condition that was specified in
    the permit; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person without a permit, restore
    at the person's risk and expense the historic canal to the condition
    that it was in prior to the dredging, filling or dredging and
    filling.

### [Signs and Structures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-951455 .Subheading}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall erect a sign, structure or object over or in a historic
  canal, except in accordance with a permit issued under subsection (2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may, on receipt of an application,
  issue a permit authorizing the applicant to erect a sign, structure or
  object over or in a historic canal.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A permit issued under subsection (2) shall specify

### [Meeting and Overtaking of Vessels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-951550 .Subheading}

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  two vessels are approaching a bridge in a historic canal from opposite
  directions and passage by the bridge can only be made by one vessel at
  a time, the person in charge of the upbound vessel shall hold back and
  give way to the downbound vessel and shall ensure that the vessels
  pass each other at least 100 m downstream of the bridge.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where two vessels are approaching a bend in a narrow
  navigation channel from opposite directions, the person in charge of
  the upbound vessel shall hold back and give way to the downbound
  vessel and shall ensure that the vessels pass each other downstream of
  the bend.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person in charge of a vessel in a historic canal
  shall attempt to overtake another vessel while within 300 m of a lock,
  guard gate or bridge, unless directed to do so by the superintendent
  or a peace officer.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The person in charge of a vessel shall decrease the
  speed of the vessel to dead slow when the vessel is passing employees
  working on historic canal waters.

### [Seizure and Detention]{.HTitleText2} {#h-951676 .Subheading}

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent shall seize and detain, at the owner's risk, any vessel
  or goods if the superintendent has reasonable grounds to believe that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dues that have accrued on the vessel or goods have
    not been paid;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} damage has occurred to the historic canal and the
    cost of repairing the damage has not been paid or the security for
    payment of the cost has not been provided;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a fine that has been imposed in respect of the
    vessel or goods has not been paid; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} these Regulations have been contravened in respect
    of the vessel or goods.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall, as soon as feasible after
  seizing the vessel or goods, inform, in writing, the owner and the
  person in whose possession they were at the time of the seizure of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reason for the seizure;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an estimate of the cost of repairing any damage
    that has been caused to the historic canal; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if they are not detained in the place where they
    were seized, the location at which they are detained.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall release the seized and
  detained vessel or goods as soon as feasible after

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dues have been paid;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cost of repairing the damage has been paid or
    security for payment of that cost has been provided; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fine has been paid.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall, at the request of the
  person from whom the vessel or goods were seized and detained, allow
  that person or any person authorized by that person to examine, at any
  reasonable time, the vessel or goods.

::: HistoricalNote

### [Sale of Seized Vessel or Goods]{.HTitleText2} {#h-951694 .Subheading}

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  superintendent shall sell any vessel or goods seized and detained
  under subsection 49(1) if, within 30 days after the day on which the
  vessel or goods are seized,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dues have not been paid;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cost of repairing the damage has not been paid
    or the security for payment of that cost has not been provided; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fine has not been paid.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner of the seized and detained vessel or goods
  may, before they are sold, apply in writing to the Chief Executive
  Officer of the Parks Canada Agency for a review of the decision to
  seize and detain.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Executive Officer shall, within 90 days
  after the day on which the application is received, make a decision
  and provide written notice of it, including reasons, to the owner.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-191, s. 5(E)
- SOR/2015-134, s. 20

## [PART VII]{.HLabel1}[Fines]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951704 .Part}

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes any provision of these Regulations is liable to a fine not
exceeding \$400.
