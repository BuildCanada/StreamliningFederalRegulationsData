## Response Organizations Regulations {#response-organizations-regulations .Title-of-Act}

Response Organizations Regulations

P.C. 1995-1337  1995-08-15

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to paragraph 657(1)(f) and
subsection 660.9(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnotestar_e){#footnotestar_e-ID0EFABA} of the
[Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting the procedures, equipment and resources
of response organizations and oil handling facilities for use in respect
of an oil pollution incident](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-405), effective
August 15, 1995.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975593 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Shipping
    Act](/eng/acts/S-9); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[area of environmental sensitivities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[area of environmental sensitivities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    area containing threatened, vulnerable or endangered species or
    locations of cultural or high socio-economic significance; ([milieux
    sensibles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated port]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated port]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a designated port set
    out in section 1 of Schedule I to the Response Organizations
    Standards; ([port désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[geographical area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[geographical area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area that
    includes a designated port and is identified by a response
    organization as the area in which it intends to offer its services;
    ([zone géographique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    660.2(1) of the Act; ([hydrocarbures]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Oil Handling Facilities Standards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Oil Handling Facilities
    Standards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2019-252, s.
    16\]]{.Repealed}

[[on scene]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[on scene]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2019-252, s.
    16\]]{.Repealed}

[[operating environment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operating environment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means sheltered
    waters, unsheltered waters or a shoreline; ([milieux
    d'utilisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Response Organizations Standards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Response Organizations Standards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    standards set out in the Transport Canada document TP 12401,
    published in August, 1995, as amended from time to time; ([Normes
    sur les organismes d'intervention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sheltered waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sheltered waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means waters where on-water
    recovery operations for spilled oil can be carried out effectively
    without significant disruption by environmental conditions; ([eaux
    abritées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shoreline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shoreline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area between extreme low
    tide and the back shore area that may be affected by storm
    conditions; ([rivage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[treat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[treat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to carry out an activity, in a
    manner that has the least detrimental impact possible on the
    environment, for the purpose of restoring an operating environment
    in which an oil pollution incident has occurred to its condition
    before the incident; ([traiter]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unsheltered waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unsheltered waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means waters where
    on-water recovery operations for spilled oil may be significantly
    disrupted by environmental conditions. ([eaux
    ouvertes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Response Plan]{.HTitleText2} {#h-975609 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  response organization shall demonstrate in its response plan that the
  response organization has the capability to comply with the
  requirements relating to the procedures, equipment and resources
  prescribed in section 4 in respect of its geographical area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A response organization's response plan shall include
  the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the response organization
    and its geographical area;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total quantity of oil in respect of which the
    response organization is to be certified in accordance with the
    tiered response capabilities set out in section 2 of the Response
    Organizations Standards;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of each person included in the personnel
    who is available for a response to an oil spill;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of each person to be notified in the event
    of an oil spill;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name of each person included in the personnel
    who has received basic oil spill response training or any other
    training in relation to an oil spill;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the training that the response
    organization provides to its personnel in preparation for the
    responsibilities that they might be requested to undertake in
    response to an oil spill;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of the training that the response
    organization provides to its employees and to volunteers whom it
    might use to respond at short notice to an oil spill;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a description of the oil spill exercise programme
    established to evaluate the effectiveness of all aspects of the
    procedures, equipment and resources that are identified in the plan,
    including exercises to be coordinated with ships, oil handling
    facilities or the Canadian Coast Guard, as the case may be;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a list of the types and quantity of equipment for
    use at each designated port in its geographical area in respect of a
    tier 1 response capability referred to in the Response Organizations
    Standards;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a list of the types and quantity of equipment for
    use during a simultaneous response in all operating environments;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a list of the equipment for scaring off birds from
    an oil spill location and of the measures available in support of
    the wildlife rehabilitation activities of other parties;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures that the response
    organization will take, in response to an oil spill, to protect and
    treat areas of environmental sensitivities within the affected
    operating environment;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} a description of the treatment and recovery
    procedures that will be implemented in response to an oil spill;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} a description of the procedures that will be
    implemented for notifying the persons referred to in paragraph (d)
    in the event of an oil spill;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures that the response
    organization will take, in conformity with federal and provincial
    regulations relating to health and safety, to protect the health and
    safety of its personnel, of volunteers and of other individuals who
    are involved, at the request of the response organization, in a
    response to an oil spill; and

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} a description of procedures for the updating of the
    response plan.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A response organization's response plan must take
  into account any contingency plan for its geographical area that is
  issued by the Canadian Coast Guard.

### [Oil Spill Procedures, Equipment and Resources]{.HTitleText2} {#h-975632 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  procedures to be implemented with respect to a spill of a specified
  quantity of oil in a geographical area include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the response to the oil spill is within the time
    set out in section 3 of the Response Organizations Standards;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of metres of shoreline that are treated
    in a day is at least that set out in section 4 of the Response
    Organizations Standards;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the maximum number of days required to complete the
    on-water recovery operations is that set out in section 5 of the
    Response Organizations Standards;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the response operation is managed in coordination
    with the Canadian Coast Guard and federal, provincial and other
    bodies responsible for, or involved in, the protection of the
    environment;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} strategies appropriate for a simultaneous response
    are employed in all affected operating environments;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an on-water recovery operation in
    unsheltered waters present in its geographical area, the equipment
    can be operated in Beaufort Force 4 conditions;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the response capability in each applicable
    operating environment in primary areas of response and enhanced
    response areas is to be apportioned in accordance with the
    percentage of tiered response capability that is set out in Schedule
    II to the Response Organizations Standards; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the equipment and resources are provided to the
    persons managing the response operation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The equipment and resources for use with respect to a
  spill of a specified quantity of oil in a geographical area include
  the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} equipment that is appropriate for responding to the
    oil spill; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the primary and secondary temporary storage
    capacities that are referred to in section 6 of the Response
    Organizations Standards.

### [Oil Spill Exercise Programme]{.HTitleText2} {#h-975646 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The oil spill exercise
programme referred to in  paragraph 3(2)(h) shall be carried out over a
three-year period that begins on the day on which the certificate of
designation is issued.

### [Equipment at Designated Port]{.HTitleText2} {#h-975648 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The equipment that is for a
tier 1 response capability described in section 2 of the Response
Organizations Standards and is to be deployed on the shoreline and in
sheltered waters at a designated port may not be removed unless the
Commissioner permits the removal for the purpose of protecting the
environment.

### [Submission]{.HTitleText2} {#h-975650 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A response organization
shall submit to the Minister four copies of its response plan.

### [Review]{.HTitleText2} {#h-975652 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall review a
response plan within 90 days after the day on which it is submitted.

### [Plan Update]{.HTitleText2} {#h-975654 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A response organization to
which a certificate of designation has been issued pursuant to
subsection 660.4(1) of the Act shall notify the Minister of all
substantive changes to its response plan immediately after they are made
and shall, at least annually, update its response plan.

### [Declaration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-975656 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A declaration submitted
by a person or body for the purpose of subsection 660.4(1) of the Act
shall be in the form set out in the schedule.

### [Certificate of Designation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-975658 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  certificate of designation issued pursuant to subsection 660.4(1) of
  the Act to a response organization expires three years after the day
  on which it is issued or the day that the response organization

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} becomes insolvent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} commits an act of bankruptcy;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is dissolved; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} abandons or transfers its business.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a response organization applies to the Minister
  for a certificate of designation that is in respect of a period that
  begins on the day after the current certificate of designation
  expires, it shall make the application at least 90 days before that
  day.

### [Response Organization Declaration]{.HTitleText1} {#response-organization-declaration .SchedHeadL1}

Act](/eng/acts/S-9), I,

[(Name)]{.FormBlank}, for and on behalf of

[(Name of the response organization)]{.FormBlank} declare that

are available to the response organization in conformity with the
