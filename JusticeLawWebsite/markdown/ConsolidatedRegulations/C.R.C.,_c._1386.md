## Regional Development Incentives Regulations {#regional-development-incentives-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1386
:::

[REGIONAL DEVELOPMENT INCENTIVES ACT](/eng/acts/R-4.9)

Regulations Respecting Regional Development Incentives
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508291 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Regional Development Incentives
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1386).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508293 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Regional Development
      Incentives Act](/eng/acts/R-4.9); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[eligible assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[eligible assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the assets approved
      by the Minister as forming the whole or a part of a facility or
      commercial facility but does not include

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} land,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} automobiles and other means of transportation
        other than the material-handling devices that are used
        exclusively on the premises of the facility or commercial
        facility,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} assets that, in the opinion of the Minister,
        are normally considered to be a charge against income in the
        year in which they are acquired, and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} patents, franchises, copyrights and goodwill;
        ([actif admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[equity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[equity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the aggregate of the share
      capital, earned surplus, contributed surplus, capital surplus and
      shareholder's loans that are subordinated to all other types of
      debt financing that the applicant provides for the financing of an
      operation. ([avoir des actionnaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the Act,

  [[commercial operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[commercial operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any commercial
      undertaking, other than

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an initial processing operation or any
        operation described in paragraphs (a) to (d) of the definition
        [[initial processing operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a manufacturing or processing operation or any
        operation described in paragraphs (a) to (n) of the definition
        [[manufacturing or processing operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
        or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an operation in a resource-based industry;
        ([entreprise commerciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[initial processing operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[initial processing operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      operation the product of which is a fuel or a material mainly used
      for further processing or manufacturing, but does not include

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the processing by roasting, leaching or
        smelting of mineral concentrates to produce metals,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the processing of wood by the sulphite process
        into bleached pulp in a pulp mill that prior to January 1, 1972
        produced dissolving and high alpha cellulose pulp on a regular
        basis,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the converting of wood into paperboard or paper
        other than newsprint, or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the processing, other than oil refining, of a
        product resulting in a significant chemical change in the
        principal material used; ([entreprise de transformation
        initiale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[manufacturing or processing operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[manufacturing or processing operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
      means an operation whereby any goods, products, commodities or
      wares are created, fabricated, refined or made more marketable but
      does not include

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the transporting or merchandizing of any goods,
        products, commodities or wares,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the growing, catching or harvesting of any
        natural or cultivated product of nature,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the extracting of minerals by any method,

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the production of energy except as an integral
        part of and solely for use in an operation whereby any goods,
        products, commodities or wares are created, fabricated, refined
        or made more marketable,

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the grinding, mixing or blending of fertilizers
        or feedstuffs,

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the mixing of concrete or asphalt,

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the drying of hides,

      - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the cleaning or drying of peat or Irish moss,

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} salt or potash extraction,

      - [(j)]{.lawlabel} any mobile manufacturing or processing
        operation,

      - [(k)]{.lawlabel} construction work,

        - [(l)]{.lawlabel} repairing as distinct from rebuilding,

      - [(m)]{.lawlabel} the rendering of consumer services, and

      - [(n)]{.lawlabel} publishing other than printing; ([entreprise de
        fabrication ou de transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[resource-based industry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[resource-based industry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an industry
      that uses as a principal material a material

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the original location of which is not the
        consequence of human design, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is in or close to its natural state.
        ([industrie basée sur une ressource naturelle]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508331 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
any application for a development incentive received by the Minister

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the date of the registration under section 6
  of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) or the [Regional
  Development Incentives Regulations,
  1974](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1388);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on or after the date referred to in paragraph (a) and
  before April 1, 1974, where the applicant has not opted pursuant to
  subsection 4(1) for the application to be dealt with under the
  [Regional Development Incentives Regulations,
  1974](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1388); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} on or after April 1, 1974 and within two months after
  the date referred to in paragraph (a), where the applicant has opted
  pursuant to subsection 4(2) for the application to be dealt with under
  these Regulations.

## [Option]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508336 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant whose application for a development incentive is received by
  the Minister after the date referred to in paragraph 3(a) and before
  April 1, 1974, may opt in writing for the application to be dealt with
  under the [Regional Development Incentives Regulations,
  1974](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1388).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An applicant whose application for a development
  incentive is received by the Minister on or after April 1, 1974 and
  within two months after the date referred to in paragraph 3(a) may opt
  in writing for the application to be dealt with under these
  Regulations.

## [Product Not Previously Manufactured or Processed in Operation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508340 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No product shall be
determined to be a product not previously manufactured or processed in
an operation unless, on the date of application for a development
incentive for the expansion of a facility to enable the manufacturing or
processing of the product,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the product is a product that is significantly
  different from any product that on or within the three years
  immediately prior to that date is or was being manufactured or
  processed in the operation of which the facility constitutes the
  necessary components; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the product could not economically be manufactured or
  processed in that operation unless that facility is expanded and
  additional assets acquired.

## [Approved Capital Costs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508344 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  approved capital costs of a facility or commercial facility shall be
  determined by aggregating the approved capital costs of the eligible
  assets of the facility or commercial facility.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The approved capital cost of an eligible asset shall
  be determined by aggregating

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cost to the applicant of the asset to the
    extent that the cost does not, in the opinion of the Minister,
    exceed the fair market value of the asset; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} such capital expenditures as, in the opinion of the
    Minister, have been or are to be reasonably incurred and paid by the
    applicant as direct costs of designing, acquiring, constructing,
    transporting and installing the asset and of insuring the asset
    during the construction period.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a development incentive has been authorized in
  respect of a facility, the Minister shall not approve any capital
  costs of that facility that are in excess of 115 per cent of the
  capital costs that were forecast by the applicant at the time the
  Minister authorized the development incentive unless the applicant,
  before incurring such excess capital costs, obtained the agreement of
  the Minister to include such excess capital costs in determining the
  amount of the approved capital costs.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The approved capital costs of a facility in respect
  of which the Minister has authorized a development incentive shall not
  include the capital costs of any assets that, in the opinion of the
  Minister, create a significant change in the facility, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is notified of the additional assets
    and approves the change in the facility before any commitments in
    respect of those assets are made by the applicant; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the assets are acquired and put to use within 24
    months of the bringing into commercial production of the facility.

## [Jobs Created Directly in an Operation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508354 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  number of jobs created directly in an operation shall be determined to
  be the number of man days, or fractions thereof, paid by the applicant
  with respect to the employment of employees in or based upon the
  facility during the second and third years after the date on which the
  facility is brought into commercial production divided by the number
  of days that the facility is in operation during those years, and
  adjusted to take into account such circumstances as the Minister may
  determine to be appropriate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), in determining
  the number of employees of an applicant in or based upon a facility,
  there shall not be included

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} employees engaged in work that is not part of the
    operation in respect of which the development incentive was
    authorized; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the expansion of a facility,
    employees to the extent of the number normally in or based upon the
    facility as it was constituted at the date of application for the
    development incentive.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (1) and (2), the
  employees of an applicant may include the employees of a person who is
  managing and operating the facility under a management agreement with
  the applicant.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of determining the amount of any
  development incentive, the number of jobs created directly in an
  operation shall not, unless the Minister otherwise determines, exceed
  by more than 15 per cent the number of jobs specified and approved in
  the authorization by the Minister.

## [Working Capital and Capitalized Expenses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508362 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of working capital to be included in the capital to be employed
  in an operation of any class shall be such amount of the excess of
  current assets over current liabilities other than loans as, in the
  opinion of the Minister, will be required for an operation of that
  class when the operation is in production at its intended full
  capacity.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of capitalized expenses that may be
  included in the total capital costs of a facility or commercial
  facility shall be such expenditures, other than the costs of fixed
  assets, as in the opinion of the Minister are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} necessarily incurred in bringing the facility or
    commercial facility into commercial production or operation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} capable, in accordance with normal accounting
    practice, of being treated as capital expenditures and not as
    charges against income.

## [Conditions and Limitations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508368 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  offer of a development incentive under the Act shall remain open for
  acceptance by the applicant for a period of 90 days from the day on
  which the offer is made by the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any offer of a development incentive under the Act
  shall specify a date by which the construction or installation of the
  fixed assets of the facility shall begin.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the construction or installation of the fixed
  assets of a facility has not been commenced before the date specified
  pursuant to subsection (2) in respect of the facility, the offer
  referred to in that subsection shall be deemed to be withdrawn on that
  date.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A development incentive
shall not be authorized in respect of a facility unless the approved
capital costs of the facility as forecast for the purpose of the
authorization will, in the opinion of the Minister, exceed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$30,000, in the case of a facility in respect of
  which the development incentive is based only on the approved capital
  costs; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$60,000, in the case of a facility in respect of
  which a development incentive is based in part on the number of jobs
  created in the operation.

<!-- -->

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  authorizing a development incentive in respect of a facility, the
  Minister shall specify the minimum amount of the equity of the
  applicant in the operation, which amount shall be not less than 20 per
  cent of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approved capital costs of the facility as
    forecast for the purpose of the authorization, in the case of the
    establishment of a facility; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the approved capital costs of the facility as
    forecast for the purpose of the authorization plus the book value of
    the assets of the facility at the time of application, in the case
    of expansion or modernization of the facility.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The applicant shall provide the amount of equity
  specified by the Minister pursuant to subsection (1) by the day
  production starts.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** It is a condition of a
development incentive in respect of a facility that the applicant shall
undertake to train and employ to the maximum extent practicable persons
resident at the time of application for the development incentive in the
designated region in which the facility is located.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** It is a condition of a
development incentive in respect of a facility that the applicant shall
provide reasonable opportunities to Canadian manufacturers to supply the
machinery and equipment included in the eligible assets of the facility,
where such machinery and equipment are competitive in performance, price
and delivery.

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  a condition of a development incentive in respect of a facility that,
  if the amount or present value of assistance referred to in paragraph
  6(c) of the Act is changed from the amount or present value that was
  taken into consideration by the Minister when authorizing the
  development incentive, the amount of the development incentive may be
  varied to the extent of the change in that assistance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Assistance taken into consideration in accordance
  with paragraph 6(c) of the Act shall be deemed to include the present
  value of any difference between prevailing market terms for loans or
  leases and any more favourable terms on which loans or leases are made
  to the applicant.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** It is a condition of a
development incentive in respect of a facility that if,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the 30 months immediately following the day on
  which the facility is brought into commercial production, in the case
  of a facility in respect of which the development incentive is based
  only on the approved capital costs, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} during the 42 months immediately following the day on
  which the facility is brought into commercial production, in the case
  of a facility in respect of which the development incentive is based
  in part on the number of jobs created in the operation,

the facility or part of the facility is destroyed by fire, flood or
other act beyond the control of the applicant, the applicant shall pay
to Her Majesty such amount of the insurance proceeds as may be
determined by the Minister to be the same proportion of the whole of the
insurance proceeds as the development incentive paid in respect of the
destroyed assets is of the aggregate of the approved capital costs of
the destroyed eligible assets and the book value of other destroyed
assets in respect of which the insurance is paid.

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  otherwise specified in the authorization by the Minister, it is a
  condition of a development incentive in respect of a new facility that
  the applicant for the development incentive shall, for a period of at
  least 36 months after the day the facility was brought into commercial
  production, continue to carry on, at substantially the same rate as at
  the time of his application in respect of the new facility, every
  other operation utilizing a facility in any designated region whereby
  a product is manufactured or processed that is the same as or similar
  to a product manufactured or processed in the operation of which the
  new facility constitutes the necessary components.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an applicant who is subject to the condition
  referred to in subsection (1) fails to comply with that subsection, he
  thereupon ceases to be eligible to be paid the amount of the incentive
  that was based on the number of jobs created directly in the
  operation, and shall repay to Her Majesty any amount paid on account
  thereof.

<!-- -->

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  a condition of a development incentive in respect of a facility that,
  if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the 24 months immediately following the date
    on which the facility is brought into commercial production, in the
    case of a facility in respect of which the development incentive is
    based only on the approved capital costs, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during the 36 months immediately following the date
    on which the facility is brought into commercial production, in the
    case of a facility in respect of which the development incentive is
    based in part on the number of jobs created in the operation,

  eligible assets comprised in the approved capital costs on which the
  amount of development incentive is based cease to be used in the
  facility, the applicant shall, unless the Minister determines that the
  cessation of use was unavoidable, repay to Her Majesty such amount of
  the development incentive as may be determined by the Minister to be
  the same proportion of the total development incentive as the approved
  capital costs of the eligible assets that have ceased to be used in
  the facility are of the total approved capital costs.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An applicant shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the Minister forthwith upon ceasing to use
    eligible assets under the circumstances referred to in subsection
    (1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} repay any amount required to be repaid by him
    pursuant to subsection (1) not later than four months from the date
    on which he ceased to use the eligible assets.

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** It is a condition of any
development incentive that is based in part on the number of jobs
created in the operation that, if during the second and third years
immediately following the date on which the facility is brought into
commercial production, the number of jobs created directly in the
operation is less than the estimated number of jobs on which payments,
on account of the development incentive are based, the applicant shall
repay to Her Majesty the amount paid on account of the development
incentive that was related to the number of jobs that were not so
created.

## [Payment of Development Incentives]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508402 .Part}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
determine that a new, expanded or modernized facility has been brought
into commercial production when he is satisfied that the facility has
been utilized in the continuous production of quantities of goods for a
period of not less than 30 days and that most, by value, of the eligible
assets approved by the Minister in respect of the facility are being
utilized in the manufacturing or processing of those goods and will
continue to be so utilized.

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount that the Minister shall, pursuant to section 10 of the Act, pay
  on account of a development incentive at the time he is satisfied that
  the facility has been brought into commercial production shall not
  exceed an amount equal to 80 per cent of the total development
  incentive calculated without taking into account the approved capital
  costs of any eligible assets not then in use in the operation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the 30 months following the date on which a
    facility is brought into commercial production, in the case of a
    facility in respect of which a development incentive is based only
    on the approved capital costs, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during the 42 months following the date on which
    the facility is brought into commercial production, in the case of a
    facility in respect of which a development incentive is based in
    part on the number of jobs created in the operation,

  the Minister is satisfied that 80 per cent of the development
  incentive will be greater by at least 25 per cent than the amount of
  the payment made in accordance with subsection (1), he may make to the
  applicant a further payment or payments on account of the development
  incentive.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The total of all payments made on account of a
  development incentive in respect of a facility prior to the expiration
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 24 months from the date on which the facility is
    brought into commercial production, in the case of a facility in
    respect of which the development incentive is based only on the
    approved capital costs, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 36 months from the date on which the facility is
    brought into commercial production, in the case of a facility in
    respect of which the development incentive is based in part on the
    number of jobs created in the operation,

  shall not exceed 80 per cent of the development incentive that the
  Minister estimates that he will pay after the expiration of such
  period.

## [Regions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508414 .Part}

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall not authorize a primary development incentive, a
  secondary development incentive or a special development incentive
  with respect to a facility located in the region designated in the
  Designated Region Order 1970-1[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBABA}.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not authorize a special
  development incentive with respect to a facility located in any region
  designated in the Regional Development Incentives Designated Region
  Order, 1974 that is within the Province of Quebec, Ontario, Manitoba,
  Saskatchewan, Alberta or British Columbia.

## [Loan Guarantees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508418 .Part}

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** A loan guarantee may be
authorized by the Minister in respect of the establishment in the areas
comprised by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the regions designated as designated regions for the
  purposes of the Act by the Regional Development Incentives Designated
  Region Order, 1974, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the region designated as a designated region for the
  purposes of the Act by the Designated Region Order 1970-1[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0EBAHA},

of a commercial facility that provides commercial services of one or
more of the following classes:

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} business offices;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} warehousing and freight-handling facilities;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} shopping centres;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} convention facilities;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} hotel accommodation; or

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} recreational facilities.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote2_e-ID0EBAHA){#footnote2_e}SOR/71-9
    :::

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** A loan guarantee shall
not be authorized in respect of a commercial facility unless the total
capital costs of the commercial facility will, in the opinion of the
Minister, exceed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$2,500,000 if the commercial facility is located in
  the metropolitan area of Montreal;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$500,000 if the commercial facility is located in
  the metropolitan area of Winnipeg or Quebec City; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$250,000 if the commercial facility is located in
  any other place within the areas prescribed by section 22.

<!-- -->

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A loan
  guarantee shall not exceed 90 per cent of the total amount of the
  advances made by the lender to the borrower plus the interest thereon.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not authorize a loan guarantee
  unless he is satisfied that the terms and conditions of the loan are
  in accordance with reasonable commercial practice with respect to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the security provided for the loan; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the circumstances in which the borrower shall be in
    default.

<!-- -->

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  a condition of any loan guarantee that the lender shall, semi-annually
  or at such shorter intervals as may be specified in the loan guarantee
  agreement, provide the Minister with a report showing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount and date of any advances made under the
    loan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount and date of any payment received on
    account of principal or interest on the loan;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any amount due on the last day of each month of the
    period covered by the report; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount and type of insurance carried on the
    facility or commercial facility.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is a condition of any incremental loan guarantee
  that the lender pay to the Receiver General, at the time he provides
  the Minister with a report pursuant to subsection (1), a guarantee fee
  of two per cent per annum, calculated on the guaranteed portion of the
  loan outstanding as of the last day of each month of the period
  covered by the report.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} It is a condition of any shared-risk loan guarantee
  that the lender pay to the Receiver General, at the time he provides
  the Minister with a report pursuant to subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the percentage guaranteed does not exceed 50
    per cent of the loan, a guarantee fee of one per cent per annum
    calculated on the guaranteed portion of the loan outstanding as of
    the last day of each month of the period covered by the report; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the percentage guaranteed exceeds 50 per cent
    of the loan, a guarantee fee of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} one per cent per annum, calculated on the first
      50 per cent of the guaranteed portion of the loan outstanding as
      of the last day of each month of the period covered by the report,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} two per cent of the percentage guaranteed that
      exceeds 50 per cent but does not exceed 70 per cent of the
      guaranteed portion of the loan outstanding as of the last day of
      each month of the period covered by the report, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} three per cent of the percentage guaranteed
      that exceeds 70 per cent of the guaranteed portion of the loan
      outstanding as of the last day of each month of the period covered
      by the report.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section,

  [[incremental loan guarantee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[incremental loan guarantee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      guarantee under which, after default by the borrower, the lender
      is entitled to apply any proceeds obtained from the realization of
      the security firstly in satisfaction of that portion of the unpaid
      balance of the loan that is not guaranteed by Her Majesty;
      ([garantie croissante d'un prêt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[shared-risk loan guarantee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[shared-risk loan guarantee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      guarantee under which, after default by the borrower and before
      Her Majesty's liability is determined, the lender is required to
      apply any proceeds obtained from realization of the security in
      satisfaction of the loan. ([garantie d'un prêt à risques
      partagés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** A loan guarantee
agreement shall contain conditions respecting

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the notification to be given to the Minister where
  the borrower is in default;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the terms and conditions under which the loan
  guarantee may be invoked;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which any assets of the borrower may be
  taken or seized by the lender;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the disposition or management of assets taken or
  seized by the lender;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the payments to the Receiver General to be made by
  the lender out of the disposition or management of assets taken or
  seized by the lender; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} such other terms and conditions as the Minister
  considers appropriate.
