[ELECTORAL BOUNDARIES READJUSTMENT SUSPENSION ACT,
1994](/eng/acts/E-3.1)

Registration 2025-03-26

Order Designating the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons
as the Minister for the Purposes of the Electoral Boundaries
Readjustment Suspension Act, 1994

P.C. 2025-435 2025-03-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2025-84 of February 12,
  2025[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2860_hq_1741752760){#fn_81000-3-2860_hq_1741752760-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote

    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2860_hq_1741752760-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_81000-3-2860_hq_1741752760}SI/2025-14
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designates the Leader of the Government in the House
  of Commons, a member of the King's Privy Council for Canada, as the
  Minister for the purposes of the [Electoral Boundaries Readjustment
  Suspension Act, 1994](/eng/acts/E-3.1)[[Footnote
