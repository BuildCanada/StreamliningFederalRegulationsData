## [Designated Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1135666 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The provisions set out in
the schedule are designated for the purposes of subsection 177(2) of the
[Canada Transportation Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1135668 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on December 15, 2019.
