## General Import Permit No. 8 --- Eggs for Personal Use {#general-import-permit-no.-8-eggs-for-personal-use .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-42
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

Registration 1994-12-30

General Import Permit No. 8 --- Eggs for Personal Use
::::

:::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
Whereas eggs have been included on the [Import Control
List](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._604);

And Whereas the Secretary of State for External Affairs has determined
an import access quantity for eggs pursuant to subsection
6.2(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-95-42_e_hq_5271){#fn_SOR-95-42_e_hq_5271-ID0EDBDA}
of the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19);

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-95-42_e_hq_5271-ID0EDBDA){#fn_SOR-95-42_e_hq_5271}S.C.
  1994, c. 47, s. 106
  :::

Therefore, the Secretary of State for External Affairs, pursuant to
section 10[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-95-42_e_hq_5272){#fn_SOR-95-42_e_hq_5272-ID0ENCCA}
of the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), hereby cancels
the [Import of Eggs Permit](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._625), C.R.C., c.
625, and, pursuant to subsection 8.3(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*^](#fn_SOR-95-42_e_hq_5273){#fn_SOR-95-42_e_hq_5273-ID0EHCCA}
of the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), hereby issues
the annexed General permit authorizing the importation of eggs for
personal use, effective on the latest of the days on which sections 74,
106, 109 and 111 of [An Act to implement the Agreement Establishing the
World Trade Organization](/eng/acts/W-11.8), being chapter 47 of the
Statutes of Canada, 1994, come into force.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975857 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Permit,
[[eggs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means eggs of fowls of the species [Gallus
domesticus]{.otherLang lang="la"}, in the shell, fresh, preserved or
cooked (other than hatching eggs for broilers), that are classified
under tariff item No. 0407.11.91, 0407.21.10 or 0407.90.11 or heading
No. 98.04 or 98.26 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the
schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011). (*oeufs*)

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975861 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Any resident of Canada may,
under the authority of this Permit, import into Canada for the personal
use of the importer and the importer's household eggs not exceeding two
dozen for each importation.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where any goods imported
under the authority of this Permit are required to be reported in the
prescribed form under the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), that form
shall contain the statement "Imported under the authority of [General
Import Permit No. 8 --- Eggs for Personal
Use](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-42)" or "Importé en vertu de la [Licence
générale d'importation n^o^ 8 --- Oeufs pour usage
personnel](/eng/regulations/DORS-95-42)".
