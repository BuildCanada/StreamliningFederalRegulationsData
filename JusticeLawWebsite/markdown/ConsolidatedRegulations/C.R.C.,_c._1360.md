## [Participants Receiving Less than Normal Salary]{.HTitleText1} {#h-505897 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of Part II of the Act, when a participant engaged otherwise
  than on a full-time basis during an educational leave or a period of
  seasonal lay-off receives, during the educational leave or period of
  lay-off, salary that is less than what they would have received had
  they not been on leave or lay-off, they are deemed to have been, while
  so engaged, employed in the public service and the salary that they
  are deemed to have received during that leave or period of lay-off is
  the salary that they would have received had they not been on leave or
  lay-off.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The contributions required to be paid by a
  participant described in subsection (1) must be paid at the time and
  in the manner that contributions would be payable under section 6 as
  if the participant were, during their educational leave or period of
  lay-off, a participant described in that section.

## [Employment Substantially Without Interruption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-505953 .Part}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of Part
II of the Act, a person's service in the public service is deemed to be
substantially without interruption if, during a relevant period,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person ceased to be employed in the public
  service and has again become employed in it within three months after
  the day on which they ceased to be employed; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} their duties or conditions of employment in the
  public service were altered.

## [Salaries in Cases of Doubt]{.HTitleText1} {#h-505960 .Part}

Part II of the Act and these Regulations, the salary of a participant

  includes any bonus or allowance of determinate or indeterminate
  amount, is, in any case of doubt, deemed to be the annual remuneration
  received for the performance of the regular duties of a position or
  office, including the value of that bonus or allowance; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2024-85, s. 16\]]{.Repealed}

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a participant who is in receipt of
  more than one salary in respect of employment in the public service,
  is deemed to be

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in respect of salary received before August 9,
    1994, the salary that was first authorized to be paid to the
    contributor; and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of salary received after August 8,
    1994, the total of all salaries received by the contributor.

## [Persons Not in Receipt of Stated Annual Salary]{.HTitleText1} {#h-505969 .Part}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of Part II of the Act, the salary that is or was
  authorized to be paid to a person employed in the public service at a
  rate other than an annual rate is to be computed in terms of an annual
  rate by multiplying the rate of pay that they are or were paid

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an hourly rate, by the aggregate of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of hours in a standard work week
      multiplied by 52, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the number of hours in a standard work week
      divided by the number of days in a standard work week;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a daily rate, by the aggregate of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of days in a standard work week
      multiplied by 52, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} one day;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a weekly rate, by 52; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a monthly rate, by 12.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the number of hours
  or days in a person's standard work week is the number of hours or
  days that the person is or was, as the case may be, ordinarily
  required to work during such a work week.

  leave of absence without pay, on the earlier of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the day after the day on which their deputy head
    advises the Minister in writing that the participant has ceased to

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the day on which, under any other Act of

  ceased to be employed in the public service;

  employed in the public service as certified by their deputy head; or

  required to contribute under Part I of the Act by reason of becoming a
  person described in paragraph 5(1)(g) or (h) or subsection 5.1(1) of
  the Act, on the day after the last day on which they are required to
  contribute.

## [Naming and Revoking of Beneficiaries]{.HTitleText1} {#h-506026 .Part}

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  participant may, for the purposes of Part II of the Act, name up to
  five beneficiaries.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of Part II of the Act, a beneficiary
  may be any of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the estate or succession of the participant;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an individual;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a [[registered charity]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
    defined in subsection 248(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In order to name a beneficiary, the following
  information must be submitted in writing to the Minister and be
  accompanied by an attestation that is dated and signed by the
  participant and that confirms that the information is complete and
  accurate:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the full name of the participant, their date of
    birth and either their personal record identifier or their pension
    number;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the participant is naming their estate or
    succession, a reference to that effect;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the participant is naming an individual, the
    full name of the individual and their date of birth; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the participant is naming a registered charity,
    its name and registration number with the Canada Revenue Agency.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The naming is effective on the day on which the
  participant signs the attestation if it is received by the Minister
  before the death of the participant.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} In cases where the attestation is not received by the
  Minister before the death of the participant, the naming is effective
  on the day on which the participant signs the attestation if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the receipt of the attestation by the Minister was
    delayed for reasons beyond the control of the participant; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the attestation is received before the payment of
    any benefit under Part II of the Act.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a participant has named more than one beneficiary
  and the information submitted with respect to one of those
  beneficiaries is illegible or incomplete, or does not refer to the
  estate or succession of the participant, an individual or a
  [[registered charity]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection
  248(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), the validity of the
  naming with respect to the other beneficiaries will not be affected.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Any naming will revoke any previous naming.
