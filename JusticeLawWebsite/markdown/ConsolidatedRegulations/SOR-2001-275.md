## Disposal at Sea Regulations {#disposal-at-sea-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2001-275
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 2001-08-01

Disposal at Sea Regulations

P.C. 2001-1353 2001-08-01
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 332(1) of the [Canadian Environmental
Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBBA}, the Minister
of the Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on February 17, 2001, a
copy of the proposed [Disposal at Sea
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-275), substantially in the
annexed form, and persons were given an opportunity to file comments
with respect to the Regulations or to file a notice of objection
requesting that a board of review be established and stating the reasons
for the objection;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFBBA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1999, c.
  33
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to
subsection 135(1) of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)^a^, hereby makes the annexed [Disposal at Sea
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-275).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-671422 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Environmental
    Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[log-book]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[log-book]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the official log referred to
    in section 261 of the [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9).
    ([journal de bord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Contents of Report Under Subsection 130(4) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-671426 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A report
  under subsection 130(4) of the Act with respect to emergencies
  described in subsection 130(1) shall contain the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the disposal is from a ship or a platform,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the ship or platform, the name of the
      master of the ship or the person in charge of the platform and the
      name and address of its owner, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the port of registry, official number, if any,
      overall length, extreme breadth, overall height and deadweight
      tonnage of the ship or the platform;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the disposal is from an aircraft,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the pilot-in-command and the name and
      address of the aircraft's owner, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the type, model, serial number, nationality and
      registration marks of the aircraft and its maximum certificated
      take-off weight specified by its Certificate of Airworthiness;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the last point of departure and the immediate
    destination of the ship or aircraft at the time at which the
    disposal occurred;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the latitude and longitude of the disposal site and
    the depth of the sea at that place;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an extract of all portions of the log-book and
    manifests of the ship, platform or aircraft related to the
    emergency;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the circumstances under
    which the substance was disposed of and the date and time of the
    disposal;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the actions taken to
    minimize danger to human life and to the marine environment; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a description of the substance disposed of,
    including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} information on the composition and nature of the
      substance that is sufficient to permit the identification of the
      substance,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity that was disposed of,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the form of the substance, namely, solid,
      liquid or gas, and the methods used to package and contain the
      substance, if any,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the trade name of the substance, if any, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} an estimate of the time required for the
      substance to disappear below the surface of the water.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report shall be delivered to the enforcement
  officer designated for the area where the disposal occurs or to any
  other person designated by the Governor in Council and shall be
  submitted in written form or in an electronic format that is
  compatible with the one used by the addressee.

## [National Action List]{.HTitleText1} {#h-671447 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 9 and 10 of Schedule 6 to the Act, the National Action List
mechanisms for screening waste or other matter shall consist of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the Lower Level, an assessment of the
  concentration of specified substances in the waste or other matter;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the Upper Level, an assessment of
  biological responses.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Waste or
  other matter referred to in section 4 that contains any of the
  substances set out in column 1 of the table to that section at a
  concentration above the concentration set out in column 2, shall be
  assessed using three marine or estuarine biological tests for sediment
  assessment, namely, an acute lethality test and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} two sub-lethal tests; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one sub-lethal and one bioaccumulation test.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The acute lethality test shall be conducted using the
  test methodology entitled Biological Test Method: Reference Method for
  Determining Acute Lethality of Sediment to Marine or Estuarine
  Amphipods (Reference Method EPS 1/RM/35), December 1998, published by
  the Department of the Environment, as amended from time to time.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The sub-lethal tests shall be conducted using any of
  the test methodologies set out in Biological Test Method: Sublethal
  Toxicity Tests to Assess Sediments Intended for Disposal at Sea
  (Report; EPS 1/RM/40), May 2001, published by the Department of the
  Environment, as amended from time to time.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The bioaccumulation test shall be conducted using the
  test methodology entitled Guidance Manual: Bedded Sediment
  Bioaccumulation Tests (EPA/600/R-93/183), September 1993, published by
  the United States Environmental Protection Agency, as amended from
  time to time.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The tests shall be performed, and their results
  interpreted, in accordance with generally accepted standards of good
  scientific practice at the time the tests are performed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-295, s. 2
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** If the results of the three
marine or estuarine biological tests set out in subsection 5(1) meet the
criteria set out for those tests, the waste or other matter shall
subsequently be considered to be below the Lower Level of the National
Action List.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If the results of either
the acute lethality test set out in subsection 5(1) or the two other
tests set out in paragraph 5(1)(a) or (b) fail to meet the criteria set
out for those tests, the waste or other matter shall be considered to be
above the Upper Level of the National Action List.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** If the results of the acute
lethality test set out in subsection 5(1) and one of the tests set out
in paragraph 5(1)(a) or (b) meet the criteria set out for those tests,
the waste or other matter shall be considered to be below the Upper
Level but above the Lower Level of the National Action List.

## [Areas of the Sea]{.HTitleText1} {#h-671474 .Part}

**[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 122(2)(e) of the Act, the specified areas of the sea adjacent
to the territorial sea of Canada consist of the following waters:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the waters of the North Arm of Fraser River bounded
  on the east by a line at the eastern tip of Mitchell Island from the
  south shore at the intersection of 123°04′00′′ west longitude and
  49°12′10′′ north latitude to the north shore at the intersection of
  123°04′00′′ west longitude and 49°12′23′′ north latitude;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the waters of the main channel of the Fraser River
  downstream of the Alex Fraser Bridge through a line from the south
  shore of the channel at the intersection of 122°56′33′′ west longitude
  and 49°09′28′′ north latitude to the north shore of the channel at the
  intersection of 122°57′29′′ west longitude and 49°10′33′′ north
  latitude;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the waters of the Northwest Miramichi River east of a
  line from the south shore at the intersection of 65°49′12′′ west
  longitude and 46°56′18′′ north latitude to the north shore at the
  intersection of 65°49′02′′ west longitude and 46°56′29′′ north
  latitude;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the waters of the Southwest Miramichi River northeast
  of a line from the south shore at the intersection of 65°40′22′′ west
  longitude and 46°51′55′′ north latitude to the north shore at the
  intersection of 65°40′30′′ west longitude and 46°52′00′′ north
  latitude;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the waters of the east channel of the Mackenzie River
  northeast of a line from the south shore at the intersection of
  134°08′28′′ west longitude and 69°14′33′′ north latitude to the north
  shore at the intersection of 134°09′47′′ west longitude and 69°16′37′′
  north latitude;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the waters of the Mackenzie River and Beaufort Sea
  seaward of a line from the intersection of 134°48′18′′ west longitude
  and 69°28′03′′ north latitude, thence westward to the intersection of
  135°24′14′′ west longitude and 69°23′36′′ north latitude, thence
  southwestward to the intersection of 135°50′17′′ west longitude and
  69°05′32′′ north latitude, thence southeastward to the intersection of
  135°13′55′′ west longitude and 68°43′39′′ north latitude, thence
  southwestward to the intersection of 135°24′04′′ west longitude and
  68°40′34′′ north latitude; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the waters of Bras d'Or Lake, St. Peters Inlet, Great
  Bras d'Or, St. Patricks Channel, Whycocomagh Bay and St. Andrews
  Channel, in Nova Scotia.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-256, s. 1
:::

## [Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-671485 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice --- application

- **[[8.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  an application meets the requirements of paragraph 127(2)(b) of the
  Act, the Minister shall notify the applicant in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall either issue or notify the
  applicant of the refusal to issue a permit under section 127 of the
  Act within the 90-day period after the date of the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The period referred to in subsection (2) does not
  include any period

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} required for additional analysis that is necessary
    to allow the Minister to meet the requirements of subsection 127(3)
    of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during which consultations that are necessary for
    the Minister to meet the requirements of subsection 127(3) of the
    Act are carried out;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} during which a decision made under an Act of
    Parliament or an Aboriginal land claim agreement, that could affect
    the Minister's decision to issue or refuse to issue a permit under
    section 127 of the Act is pending;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} during which consultations are carried out by the
    Minister under section 77 of the [Species at Risk
    Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} during which the Minister is awaiting the
    submission by the applicant of any report required under a permit
    previously issued under section 127 of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The period referred to in subsection (2) does not
  apply

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if an applicant requests or agrees that it does not
    apply; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if an applicant makes changes to the information
    provided under paragraph 127(2)(b) of the Act that could affect the
    Minister's decision to issue or refuse to issue a permit.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-12, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice --- eligibility for renewal

**[[8.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.3 .sectionLabel}** If the Minister issues
a permit under section 127 of the Act, the Minister shall notify the
applicant whether the permit is eligible to be renewed and of the number
of times it may be renewed which may be no more than, subject to the
number of renewals permitted under subsection 127(1) of the Act, the
number of renewals requested in the application.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-12, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

**[[8.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.4 .sectionLabel}** A permit is eligible to
be renewed if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the descriptions of the load and disposal sites in
  the permit are consistent with the descriptions of the load and
  disposal sites in a permit that has been issued in the last five
  years;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is in compliance with all permit
  conditions for valid permits issued under section 127 of the Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the load site in respect of which the application
  for a permit is being made

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any waste or other matter referred to in item 1 or
    4 of Schedule 5 to the Act that has been assessed in any previous
    permit applications has been considered to be below the Lower Level
    of the National Action List under section 4 for a minimum of three
    sets of test results over a period of at least five years ending on
    the day on which the most recent permit for the load site was
    issued;

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} there have been no test results for waste or other
    matter taken from the load site for the period referred to in
    subparagraph (i) above the Lower Level of the National Action List
    under section 4, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any description of the waste or other matter
    referred to in item 2, 5 or 6 of Schedule 5 to the Act is consistent
    with those descriptions in applications in respect of permits issued
    in the period of five years preceding the date of the application;
    and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the applicant requested when applying for the permit
  that the permit be eligible for renewal and indicated the number of
  renewals desired.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-12, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit --- application for renewal

- **[[8.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder of a renewable permit shall apply for the renewal of the permit
  at least 90 days before it expires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice --- application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When an application for renewal meets the
  requirements of paragraph 127(2)(b) of the Act, the Minister shall
  notify the applicant in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall either renew or notify the
  applicant of their decision to refuse to renew a permit within the
  45-day period after the date of the notice.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-12, s. 1
:::

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-671528 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The fee that is to
accompany an application for a permit referred to in section 127 or 128
of the Act is \$2,500.
