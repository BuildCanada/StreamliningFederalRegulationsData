## GM Loan Regulations {#gm-loan-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 2, 1983-84

Registration 1987-04-01

Regulations Respecting the Provision of a Loan to General Motors of
Canada Limited

P.C. 1987-662 1987-04-01
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Regional Industrial Expansion and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to Industry, Trade and Commerce Vote L35 of Appropriation Act
No. 2, 1983-84[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EDBAA}, is
pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the provision
of a loan to General Motors of Canada
Limited](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-210).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904735 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [GM Loan Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-210).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904737 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[GM]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[GM]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means General Motors of Canada Limited, a
    corporation incorporated under the laws of Canada;
    ([GM]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Regional
    Industrial Expansion; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Renaissance Project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Renaissance Project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the modernization
    and expansion of the GM facility at Sainte-Thérèse, Quebec. ([Projet
    Renaissance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Loan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904742 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 4 and
5, the Minister may make a loan to GM in an amount not exceeding
\$110,000,000.

## [Terms and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904744 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A loan made under section 3
shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be a term loan and be due and payable on April 1,
  2017;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be interest-free unless it is in default, in which
  case the loan shall bear a rate of interest equal to the lowest rate
  of interest charged by the Federal Business Development Bank on its
  term loans; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be subject to the condition that there be a partial
  early repayment of the loan in the amount of \$37,500,000 on April 1,
  1991 in the event that GM does not give assurances to the Minister by
  December 31, 1990 that the GM facility at Sainte-Thérèse will continue
  to operate.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where a loan is made under
section 3, GM shall provide the Minister with quarterly progress reports
of expenditures made by GM in respect of the Renaissance Project.
