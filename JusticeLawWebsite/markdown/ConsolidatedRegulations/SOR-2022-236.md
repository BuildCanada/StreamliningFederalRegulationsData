## Critical Habitat of the Pugnose Minnow (Opsopoeodus emiliae) Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-pugnose-minnow-opsopoeodus-emiliae-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2022-236
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2022-11-08

Critical Habitat of the Pugnose Minnow (Opsopoeodus emiliae) Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Pugnose Minnow ([Opsopoeodus emiliae]{.otherLang lang="la"})
is a wildlife species that is listed as a threatened species in Part 3
of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4899_hq_1658956443){#fn_81000-2-4899_hq_1658956443-ID0EBBEA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4899_hq_1658956443-ID0EBBEA){#fn_81000-2-4899_hq_1658956443}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

And whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is
specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection
58(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4899_hq_1658956730){#fn_81000-2-4899_hq_1658956730-ID0EBBCA}
of that Act;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4899_hq_1658956730-ID0EBBCA){#fn_81000-2-4899_hq_1658956730}S.C.
  2015, c. 10, s. 60
  :::

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans makes the annexed
[Critical Habitat of the Pugnose Minnow (Opsopoeodus emiliae)
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-236) under subsections 58(4) and (5) of
the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4899_hq_1658956443){#fn_81000-2-4899_hq_1658956443-ID0EBABA}.

Ottawa, November 8, 2022

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Pugnose Minnow ([Opsopoeodus emiliae]{.otherLang lang="la"}),
which is identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is
included in the Species at Risk Public Registry.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
