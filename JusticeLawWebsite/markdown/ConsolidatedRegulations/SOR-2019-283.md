## [Information and Time Limit Management]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1193927 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Impact Assessment
Act](/eng/acts/I-2.75).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of time limits

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of any of
subsections 9(5), 18(5), 28(9), 36(3) and 37(6) of the Act, a time limit
may be suspended in respect of the following activities and in the
following circumstances:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any activity related to a request to designate a
  physical activity or to a designated project, if the proponent submits
  a written request to the Agency in that regard;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the undertaking of studies or the collection of
  information by the proponent related to a change in the design,
  construction or operation plans for a designated project and the
  resulting effects of the change, if the Agency or the review panel, as
  the case may be, is of the opinion that the change would alter the
  potential effects of the project and there is not sufficient
  information available to it for the purpose of conducting the impact
  assessment or preparing the impact assessment report; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the collection, by the Agency or the review panel, of
  any fees, charges or levies, costs or amounts referred to in section
  76 of the Act if they are not paid by the proponent within the time
  limit set out in section 80 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information for initial description of
project

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 10(1) of the Act, the information that is to be provided in
the initial description of a designated project is set out in Schedule 1
and must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be representative of the project at the time the
  information is provided; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} include the information related to any option that
  the proponent is considering in respect of any item in the description
  of the project.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information for detailed description of
project

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 15(1.1) of the Act, the information that is to be provided in
a detailed description of a designated project is set out in Schedule 2
and must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be representative of the project at the time the
  information is provided; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} include the information related to any option that
  the proponent is considering in respect of any item in the description
  of the project.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of commencement --- documents

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 18(1)(b) of the Act, the Agency must provide the proponent
with the following documents:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} tailored guidelines regarding the information or
  studies referred to in paragraph 18(1)(a) of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a plan for cooperation with other jurisdictions;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a plan for engagement and partnership with the
  Indigenous peoples of Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a plan for public participation; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a plan for the issuance of permits.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information format and accessibility

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Any information that is
required to be submitted by a proponent under the Act must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be in a machine-readable format; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} include a plain-language summary of the information
  in English and in French.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Physical activities

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 75(1)(a) of the Act, the designated physical activities are
those that are set out in the schedule to the [Physical Activities
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-285) or that are designated by
the Minister under section 9 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit --- response to request for
assessment

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 97(1) of the Act, the Minister must respond within 90 days
after the day on which a request is received.
