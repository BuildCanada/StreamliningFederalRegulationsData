## Order Issuing a Direction to the CRTC on a Renewed Approach to Telecommunications Policy {#order-issuing-a-direction-to-the-crtc-on-a-renewed-approach-to-telecommunications-policy .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2023-23
:::

[TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/T-3.4)

Registration 2023-02-10

Order Issuing a Direction to the CRTC on a Renewed Approach to
Telecommunications Policy

P.C. 2023-110 2023-02-09
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council, in 2006, issued to the Canadian
Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission an order entitled
[Order Issuing a Direction to the CRTC on Implementing the Canadian
Telecommunications Policy
Objectives](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-355)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2325_hq_30671){#fn_81000-3-2325_hq_30671-ID0EBBHA}
(the "2006 Direction");

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2325_hq_30671-ID0EBBHA){#fn_81000-3-2325_hq_30671}SOR/2006-355
  :::

Whereas the Governor in Council, in 2019, issued to that Commission an
order entitled [Order Issuing a Direction to the CRTC on Implementing
the Canadian Telecommunications Policy Objectives to Promote
Competition, Affordability, Consumer Interests and
Innovation](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-227)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2325_hq_30672){#fn_81000-3-2325_hq_30672-ID0EBBGA},
one of the purposes of which was to guide the Commission on how the 2006
Direction is to be implemented;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2325_hq_30672-ID0EBBGA){#fn_81000-3-2325_hq_30672}SOR/2019-227
  :::

Whereas the telecommunications market and its regulation have changed
since 2019 and the Governor in Council is of the opinion that new
directions should be issued to the Commission as a result of those
changes;

Whereas, under subsection 10(1) of the [Telecommunications
Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2325_hq_30675){#fn_81000-3-2325_hq_30675-ID0EFBEA},
the Minister of Industry had a copy of the proposed [Order Issuing a
Direction to the CRTC on a Renewed Approach to Telecommunications
Policy](/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-23), published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on June 4, 2022,
substantially in the annexed form, and a reasonable opportunity was
given to interested persons to make representations to the Minister with
respect to the proposed Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2325_hq_30675-ID0EFBEA){#fn_81000-3-2325_hq_30675}S.C.
  1993, c. 38
  :::

Whereas, under subsection 10(1) of that Act, the Minister laid the
proposed Order before each House of Parliament and 40 sitting days of
Parliament have elapsed since the proposed Order was tabled in both
Houses;

Whereas, under subsection 10(2) of that Act, the Minister consulted the
Commission with respect to the proposed Order before it was published
and laid and consulted the Commission again with respect to the proposed
order in its definitive form;

And whereas, under section 13 of that Act, the Minister, before making a
recommendation to the Governor in Council for the purposes of this
Order, notified the minister designated by the government of each
province of the Minister's intention to make the recommendation and
provided an opportunity for each of them to consult with the Minister;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Industry makes the annexed [Order
Issuing a Direction to the CRTC on a Renewed Approach to
Telecommunications Policy](/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-23) under section 8
of the [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2325_hq_30675){#fn_81000-3-2325_hq_30675-ID0EBAAA}.
:::

## [Direction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1391756 .Part}

### [Direction and Key Objectives]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391757 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direction

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In exercising its powers
and performing its duties under the [Telecommunications
Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4), the Commission must implement the Canadian
telecommunications policy objectives set out in section 7 of that Act in
accordance with this Order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Key objectives

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Commission should
consider how its decisions would promote competition, affordability,
consumer interests and innovation, in particular the extent to which
they would

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} encourage all forms of competition and investment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} foster affordability and lower prices, particularly
  when telecommunications service providers exercise market power;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} ensure that affordable access to high-quality,
  reliable and resilient telecommunications services is available in all
  regions of Canada, including rural areas, remote areas and Indigenous
  communities;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} enhance and protect the rights of consumers in their
  relationships with telecommunications service providers, including
  rights related to accessibility;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} reduce barriers to entry into the market and to
  competition for telecommunications service providers that are new,
  regional or smaller than the incumbent national service providers;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} enable innovation in telecommunications services,
  including new technologies and differentiated service offerings; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} stimulate investment in research and development and
  in other intangible assets that support the offer and provision of
  telecommunications services.

### [Principles of Effective Regulation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391769 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transparency, predictability and
coherence

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commission should
ensure that its proceedings and decisions are transparent, predictable
and coherent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Efficiency and proportionality

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commission should
ensure that the measures that it imposes through its decisions are
efficient and proportionate to their purpose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Market monitoring, research and strategic
foresight

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Commission should
further develop strong and timely market monitoring, research and
strategic foresight skills and use the results that it obtains from
these activities in the exercise of its powers and the performance of
its duties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decisions based on sound and recent
evidence

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Commission should base
its decisions on sound and recent evidence and should exercise its
powers to obtain necessary evidence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timely proceedings and decisions

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Commission should
conduct proceedings and issue decisions in a timely manner, in
recognition of the need for market clarity. The Commission should
consider whether adopting new processes or engaging external experts
would help reach this objective.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decisions of an economic nature

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** In making decisions of an
economic nature, the Commission should balance, in addition to any other
objectives that the Commission considers relevant in the circumstances,
the objectives of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fostering competition;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} promoting investment in high-quality networks;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} improving consumer choice;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} supporting the provision of innovative services; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} encouraging the provision of services at reasonable
  prices for consumers.

### [Considerations for Fixed Internet Competition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391787 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulatory framework

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** In order to foster fixed
Internet competition that is sufficient to protect the interests of
users, the Commission must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain a regulatory framework mandating the
  provision --- at just and reasonable rates --- of wholesale services
  for fixed Internet;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} monitor the effectiveness of the framework; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} adjust the framework as necessary and in a timely
  manner, including by making proactive adjustments.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aggregated wholesale high-speed access
service

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Commission must
mandate the provision of an aggregated wholesale high-speed access
service --- that is additional to any other types of wholesale
high-speed access services that are mandated --- until it determines
that broad, sustainable and meaningful competition will persist even if
the provision of an aggregated service is no longer mandated.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Variety of access speeds and costs

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Commission must
mandate the provision of wholesale high-speed access services with a
variety of speeds, including low-cost options, for the purpose of
ensuring affordable options for consumers while allowing for the
modernization of networks.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tariff setting

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Commission should set
interim and final tariffs expediently, including by reforming the
tariff-setting process and considering external expertise or
international best practices.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equitable application of regulatory
framework

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Commission should
ensure that its regulatory framework mandating the provision of
wholesale services for fixed Internet applies equitably to carriers that
are subject to the framework.

### [Considerations for Mobile Wireless Competition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391801 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mobile wireless competition

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** In order to foster mobile
wireless competition that is sufficient to protect the interests of
users, the Commission must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain a regulatory framework mandating access ---
  at just and reasonable rates --- to wholesale roaming services;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} monitor and assess the effectiveness of its approach
  to a mandated wholesale facilities-based mobile virtual network
  operator access service, considering factors such as the specific
  characteristics of lower-density or remote regions and how these
  characteristics affect the time needed to deploy wireless networks;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} adjust the facilities-based approach referred to in
  paragraph (b), including by extending the duration of the mandate to
  provide the service, if the Commission determines that it is
  necessary.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revision to approach

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Commission should
revise its approach to encourage broader service-based competition if
the effectiveness of the approach in fostering mobile wireless
competition is lessened due to changes in the mobile wireless market
structure or circumstances of competition.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Periodic review and adjustments

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Commission should

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} periodically review the effectiveness of its mobile
  wireless services regulatory framework in meeting its objectives and,
  in doing so, consider factors that could harm competition, such as
  coordinated conduct between carriers; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} make any necessary adjustments to the framework.

### [Approach to Consumer Matters]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391813 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consumer rights

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Commission must
enhance and protect the rights of consumers in telecommunications
markets by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} strengthening the ability of the Commission for
  Complaints for Telecom-television Services to better fulfill its
  mandate, including by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} increasing its operational capacity,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} ensuring that the perspectives of consumer and
    civil-society groups are better reflected in its governance,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} improving compliance with its rules, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} increasing public awareness of its
    complaint-resolution process;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} strengthening the position of consumers in their
  relationships with service providers, including by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} taking additional measures to protect consumers
    from unacceptable sales practices, such as the measures identified
    in the Commission's Report on Misleading or Aggressive
    Communications Retail Sales Practices,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} adjusting its consumer codes by harmonizing the
    provisions of its codes if doing so would be advantageous to
    consumers and providing for consumer protection measures in the
    event of a service outage or disruption,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} taking measures to promote clarity and
    transparency of pricing information and service plan characteristics
    in service providers' marketing materials, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} taking measures to ensure that consumers can
    promptly, affordably and easily cancel, downgrade, transfer or
    otherwise change their services;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} proactively identifying, removing and preventing
  barriers relating to telecommunications services, in particular for
  persons with disabilities; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} regularly collecting, reporting publicly and making
  available to consumers information relating to mobile wireless
  coverage and fixed Internet services, including service quality
  metrics during peak periods and any other information that the
  Commission considers to be in the public interest, by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} requiring that service providers participate in the
    testing of the performance of their fixed Internet services,
    including services based on commonly used technologies in rural
    areas, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} developing and implementing a standardized and
    robust approach for reporting mobile wireless coverage.

### [Measures Supporting Deployment and Universal Access]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391830 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Universal access

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Commission should
continue to take measures, in concert with other government measures, to
support the objective of universal access to high-quality, reliable and
resilient fixed Internet and mobile wireless services, including the
following measures:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} continuing to administer a funding mechanism, making
  any adjustments that the Commission determines are necessary; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} mandating improved access to support structures, such
  as telephone poles and conduits, as well as identifying and addressing
  other barriers to timely deployment of telecommunications networks,
  such as exclusionary practices and unreasonable administrative
  practices.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Funding mechanism

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Commission must, when
it reviews its funding mechanism, consider whether to prioritize funding
for mobile wireless services and operating costs of telecommunications
networks in order to foster

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} improved access to, and more affordable prices for,
  retail telecommunications services in underserved areas; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} better coordination of public funding.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Considerations

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 18 and 19, the Commission must take into account evolving

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} technologies;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} service-performance needs and user needs; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} gaps in telecommunications network services.

## [Effect of Order]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1391844 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** This Order is binding on
the Commission beginning on the day on which it comes into force and
applies in respect of matters pending before the Commission on that day.
