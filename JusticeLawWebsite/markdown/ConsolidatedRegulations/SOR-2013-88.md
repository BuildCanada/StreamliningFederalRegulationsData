## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-804111 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of these
Regulations is to prohibit the export of substances specified in the
Export Control List, or to establish regulatory conditions applicable to
the export of those substances, and to implement the Stockholm
Convention, Rotterdam Convention and Minamata Convention in relation to
the export of those substances.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-11, s. 2
- SOR/2018-196, s. 29

## [Background]{.HTitleText1} {#h-804116 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations set out the content of the notice of proposed export that
  is required under subsection 101(1) of the Act for substances
  specified in the Export Control List and the period within which and
  manner in which the notice must be provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of export

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations also set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsections 101(2) and (3) of
    the Act, the conditions applicable to the export of a substance that
    is specified in the Export Control List to a Rotterdam Party;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsection 101(3) of the Act,
    the conditions applicable to the export of a substance that is
    specified in Part 2 or 3 of the Export Control List; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsection 101(4) of the Act,
    the relevant prohibitions of the export of a substance that is
    specified in the Export Control List.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-11, s. 3
- SOR/2018-196, s. 30

## [Notice of Proposed Export]{.HTitleText1} {#h-804130 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  notice of proposed export that is required under subsection 101(1) of
  the Act must be provided to the Minister by the person proposing the
  export of a substance at least

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the person holds a permit to export the
    substance issued under paragraph 185(1)(b) of the Act, seven days
    before the export; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, 15 days before the export.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice is considered to be provided to the
  Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the day on which it is delivered, if it is
    delivered personally;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the day on which it is postmarked, if it is sent
    by mail; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the date that is indicated by the sending
    apparatus, if it is sent by electronic mail or by facsimile.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice of proposed export must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide the information set out in Schedule 1; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by a certification, dated and signed
    by the person proposing the export, or by their duly authorized
    representative, stating that the information provided in the notice
    is accurate and complete.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic or paper submission

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The notice of proposed export and the certification
  may be submitted either in writing or in an electronic format that is
  compatible with the one that is used by the Minister and the documents
  must bear the signature of the person proposing the export or their
  duly authorized representative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of changes

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The exporter must notify the Minister in writing of
  any change to the information provided in a notice of proposed export
  within 30 days after learning of it.

## [Export of Asbestos]{.HTitleText1} {#h-804151 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 5.2
and 5.3, a person must not export asbestos, whether or not it is
contained in a product.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-196, s. 32
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

**[[5.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.2 .sectionLabel}** A person that has
provided a notice of proposed export under subsection 101(1) of the Act
may export asbestos in the following circumstances:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the asbestos is, or is contained in, hazardous waste
  or hazardous recyclable material the export of which is regulated by
  the [Export and Import of Hazardous Waste and Hazardous Recyclable
  Material Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-149);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the asbestos is contained in a product that is a
  personal or household effect intended for the person's personal use;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the asbestos is contained in [[military
  equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 1 of the
  [Prohibition of Asbestos and Products Containing Asbestos
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-196).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-196, s. 32
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulated export

- **[[5.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a person
  that has provided a notice of proposed export under subsection 101(1)
  of the Act may export asbestos in the following circumstances if the
  conditions set out in subsection (4) are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the asbestos is exported for disposal of the
    asbestos or of the product containing it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the asbestos is contained in a product that was
    used before the day on which this paragraph comes into force;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the asbestos is contained in a product that is
    exported in order to service [[military
    equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 1 of the
    [Prohibition of Asbestos and Products Containing Asbestos
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-196), while that equipment is
    outside Canada for the purposes of a [[military
    operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 9(3) of
    those Regulations, if there is no technically or economically
    feasible asbestos-free alternative available;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the asbestos is contained in a product in not
    greater than trace amounts;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the asbestos is contained in raw material that is
    extracted from the ground and that is exported

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to manufacture a consumer product that contains
      asbestos in not greater than trace amounts,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to manufacture a product that is not a consumer
      product, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for a purpose other than manufacturing a
      product, if the raw material will not be sold as a consumer
      product;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the asbestos, whether or not it is contained in a
    product, is exported for use in a laboratory for analysis, in
    scientific research or as an analytical standard; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the asbestos, whether or not it is contained in a
    product, is exported for display in a museum.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to asbestos referred to
  in section 5.2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for export

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person exporting asbestos in accordance with
  subsection (1) must comply with the permit requirements set out in
  subsection (4), unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of asbestos in the exported
    product, including a raw material referred to in paragraph (1)(e),
    is less than 0.1% by weight; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the asbestos exported is referred to in paragraph
    (1)(f) and the total quantity of asbestos exported for those
    purposes by the person during the calendar year in question does not
    exceed 10 kg.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Export with permit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person who exports asbestos that is not referred to
  in paragraph (3)(a) or (b) must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the export is to a country other than a
    Rotterdam Party, hold an export permit referred to in section 5.4;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the export is to a Rotterdam Party, hold a
    permit issued under subsection 12(1) or section 14 and respect the
    conditions described in subsection 7(3);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be a resident of Canada or, in the case of a
    corporation, have a place of business in Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements of sections 20 to 22; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} include a copy of the permit with each shipment.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-196, s. 32
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for permit --- non-party
country

- **[[5.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for an export permit to export to a country that is not a
  Rotterdam Party must comply with section 11.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the Minister must issue an
  export permit on receipt of a permit application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasonable grounds

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must refuse to issue an export permit if
  the Minister believes on reasonable grounds that one or more of the
  circumstances referred to in paragraphs 16(a) to (c) apply.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other conditions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Sections 15 and 18 to 22 set out additional
  conditions applicable to the export under the authority of a permit
  issued under subsection (2).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-196, s. 32
:::

## [Conditions Relative to the Stockholm Convention]{.HTitleText1} {#h-804202 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persistent organic pollutant

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  section, [[persistent organic pollutant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
  substance listed in Annex A or B of the Stockholm Convention, other
  than one added to that Convention by an amendment that is not in force
  for Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}POP specified in Part 2 or 3 of
  Schedule 3 to Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person that has provided a notice of proposed
  export under subsection 101(1) of the Act may export a persistent
  organic pollutant that is specified in Part 2 or 3 of the Export
  Control List under the following conditions, unless the export of that
  persistent organic pollutant is prohibited by any other regulation
  made under the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a specific exemption or acceptable purpose is
    listed in Annex A or B of the Stockholm Convention for that
    persistent organic pollutant and the export is to a Stockholm Party,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that Party has registered a specific exemption or
      acceptable purpose in the relevant Register established under the
      Stockholm Convention and the export complies with the terms of
      that specific exemption or acceptable purpose,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a persistent organic pollutant
      added to the Stockholm Convention by an amendment that is not in
      force for that Party, an annual certification for the year in
      question regarding that Party that is in accordance with paragraph
      2 (b) (iii) of Article 3 of the Stockholm Convention has been
      transmitted by Canada to the Secretariat established under that
      Convention, and the export complies with the terms of the specific
      exemption or acceptable purpose listed in those Annexes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a specific exemption or acceptable purpose is
    listed in Annex A or B of the Stockholm Convention for that
    persistent organic pollutant and the export is to a State or
    regional economic integration organization that is not a party to
    the Stockholm Convention, an annual certification for the year in
    question regarding that State or organization that is in accordance
    with paragraph 2 (b) (iii) of Article 3 of the Stockholm Convention
    has been transmitted by Canada to the Secretariat established under
    that Convention, and the export complies with the terms of the
    specific exemption or acceptable purpose listed in those Annexes;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the persistent organic pollutant is exported in
    accordance with paragraph 1 (d) of Article 6 of the Stockholm
    Convention;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the persistent organic pollutant is exported for
    use in a laboratory for analysis, in scientific research or as a
    laboratory analytical standard and the total quantity exported for
    those purposes by the person during the calendar year in question
    does not exceed 10 kg;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the persistent organic pollutant is incidentally
    present in trace amounts in the product that is being exported; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the persistent organic pollutant is contained in a
    product

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that was manufactured on or before the entry into
      force for Canada of a provision of the Stockholm Convention
      prohibiting, under Annex A, or restricting, under Annex B, the
      production or use of that persistent organic pollutant, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which a notification has been
      provided by Canada in accordance with note (ii) to Annex A or B of
      the Stockholm Convention and the notification has been made
      publicly available in accordance with that note by the Secretariat
      established under the terms of that Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply to a persistent organic
  pollutant that is, or is contained in, a hazardous waste or hazardous
  recyclable material regulated by the Cross-border Movement of
  Hazardous Waste and Hazardous Recyclable Material Regulations.

## [Conditions Relative to the Rotterdam Convention]{.HTitleText1} {#h-804222 .Part}

### [General Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-804223 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 23, sections 8 to 22 set out the additional conditions
  applicable to the export of a substance specified in the Export
  Control List if that export is to a Rotterdam Party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 8 to 22 do not apply to a substance that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is contained in a manufactured item that during
    manufacture is formed into a specific physical shape or design and
    has for its final use a function or functions wholly or partly
    dependent on its shape or design;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is, or is contained in, a hazardous waste or
    hazardous recyclable material regulated by the Cross-border Movement
    of Hazardous Waste and Hazardous Recyclable Material Regulations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is, or is contained in, a controlled substance as
    defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Controlled Drugs and Substances
    Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is, or is contained in, a nuclear substance as
    defined in section 2 of the [Nuclear Safety and Control
    Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} is, or is contained in, a chemical weapon as
    defined by the Convention on the Prohibition of the Development,
    Production, Stockpiling and Use of Chemical Weapons and on Their
    Destruction, signed at Paris, France, on January 13, 1993, as
    amended from time to time;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} is, or is contained in, a food or drug as defined
    in section 2 of the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27) or a food
    additive as defined in Part B of the [Food and Drug
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} is contained in a product at a concentration of
    less than 0.1% by weight;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} is exported for the personal use of the individual
    who imports it, if the total quantity exported for that purpose by
    the exporter during the calendar year in question does not exceed
    10 kg; or

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is exported for use in a laboratory for analysis,
    in scientific research or as a laboratory analytical standard, if
    the total quantity exported for those purposes by the exporter
    during the calendar year in question does not exceed 10 kg.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Export of asbestos

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (1) and (2), in the case of
  asbestos exported to a Rotterdam Party, other than asbestos referred
  to in section 5.2 or in paragraph 5.3(3)(a) or (b), sections 11, 12
  and 14 to 22 set out additional conditions applicable to the export of
  the asbestos.

### [Exports Not Requiring a Permit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-804242 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Substances not subject to prior informed
consent procedure

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A person that has provided
a notice of proposed export under subsection 101(1) of the Act may
export a substance --- other than a substance specified in Part 1 of the
Export Control List --- that is specified in Annex III of the Rotterdam
Convention, but that is destined for use in a category other than the
category specified for that substance in that Annex, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that person is a resident of Canada or, in the case
  of a corporation, has a place of business in Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that person meets the requirements of sections 20 to
  22.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-11, s. 7

#### [Cancellation, Amendment or Suspension of Permit]{.HTitleText3} {#h-804311 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent for import not provided

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Rotterdam Secretariat advises the Rotterdam Parties through the PIC
  Circular that a country of destination does not consent to the import
  of a substance for which an export permit has been issued, the
  Minister must cancel the permit and the cancellation takes effect on
  the day that is six months after the date of publication of that PIC
  Circular.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of consent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a designated national authority advises the
  Minister that it revokes its written consent to the import of a
  substance for which an export permit has been issued, the Minister
  must cancel the permit and the cancellation takes effect on the day
  that is 30 days after the day on which the Minister is advised of the
  revocation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to transmit response

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Rotterdam Secretariat advises the Rotterdam
  Parties for the first time through the PIC Circular that a country of
  destination has failed to transmit a response with respect to the
  import of a substance for which an export permit has been issued under
  paragraph 12(1)(a) or subparagraph 12(1)(c)(i), the Minister must
  cancel the permit and the cancellation takes effect on the day that is
  six months after the date of publication of that PIC Circular.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Modification of conditions of import
  --- PIC Circular

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Rotterdam Secretariat advises the Rotterdam
  Parties through the PIC Circular that a country of destination has
  modified the conditions of import of a substance for which an export
  permit has been issued, the Minister must amend the permit in
  accordance with the new conditions and the amendment takes effect on
  the day that is six months after the date of publication of that PIC
  Circular.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Modification of conditions of import
  --- designated national authority

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a designated national authority advises the
  Minister that it has modified the conditions of import of a substance
  for which an export permit has been issued, the Minister must amend
  the permit in accordance with the new conditions and the amendment
  takes effect 30 days after the day on which the Minister is advised of
  the modifications.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of Minister

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may cancel or amend an export permit
  under this section only if a notice giving reasons for the amendment
  or cancellation has been sent to the exporter by mail, electronic mail
  or facsimile.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasonable grounds

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must cancel an export permit if the Minister believes on
  reasonable grounds that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the exporter is not able to export the substance in
    compliance with the Act, these Regulations or the conditions set out
    in the export permit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the export contravenes the Act, any regulations
    made under the Act or any other measure taken under the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the exporter failed to comply with a condition set
    out in the export permit;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the exporter failed to comply with the undertaking
    provided under paragraph 11(1)(a); or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the exporter provided false or misleading
    information in the notice of proposed export, in the permit
    application or in any document provided under paragraph 11(1)(b) or
    (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of proposed cancellation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must, before cancelling an export permit
  under this section, notify the exporter of the proposed cancellation
  by mail, electronic mail or facsimile.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice of proposed cancellation must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicate the reasons for the proposed cancellation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inform the exporter of the interim suspension of
    the permit; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} inform the exporter that it may make written
    representations concerning the proposed cancellation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interim suspension of permit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The export permit is suspended for the period
  beginning on the day on which the notice of the proposed cancellation
  is received and ending on the day on which the Minister makes a
  decision respecting the proposed cancellation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written representations

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The exporter may, within 15 days after the day on
  which the notice is received, make written representations concerning
  the proposed cancellation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of receipt

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The notice of proposed cancellation is considered to
  have been received by the exporter

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the day on which it is delivered, if it is
    delivered personally;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the 10th day after the day on which it is
    postmarked, if it is sent by mail; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the date that is indicated by the sending
    apparatus, if it is sent by electronic mail or by facsimile.

  Commodity Description and Coding System prepared by the World Customs
  Organization, as amended from time to time, if applicable.

- **12** If the substance is contained in a product,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the product; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of the substance in the product.

- **13** The following information respecting the export:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the customs office through which it is expected to
    be exported from Canada, if known;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the countries through which the substance will
    transit, if known; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proposed number of exports for the calendar
    year, if known.

- **14** The safety data sheet for the substance or, if applicable, for
  the product that contains the substance.
