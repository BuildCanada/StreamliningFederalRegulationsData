## Withdrawal of Certain Lands (North Baffin Island, N.W.T.) from Disposal Order {#withdrawal-of-certain-lands-north-baffin-island-n.w.t.-from-disposal-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/92-41
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 1992-03-11

Withdrawal of Certain Lands (North Baffin Island, N.W.T.) from Disposal
Order

P.C. 1992-345  1992-02-27
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 23(a) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), is
pleased hereby to order the withdrawal from disposal of the lands
described in the schedule hereto, for the reason that the lands are
required to facilitate the establishment of a National Park, including
all mines and minerals, whether solid, liquid or gaseous, construction
material that may be disposed of pursuant to the [Territorial Quarrying
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1527), and timber that may be
disposed of pursuant to the Forest Management Act (Northwest
Territories), without prejudice to the following interests, including
any renewals, replacements, extensions or transfers thereof:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} located or recorded mineral claims or prospecting
  permits in good standing under the Canada Mining Regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the right to obtain a surface lease on located or
  recorded mineral claims pursuant to the [Territorial Lands
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1525);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} rights in good standing created pursuant to section 8
  of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7) or the [Territorial
  Lands Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1525);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} permits, special renewal permits and leases in good
  standing under the [Canada Oil and Gas Land
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1518);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} permits in good standing under the [Territorial
  Quarrying Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1527);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} existing rights and interests granted pursuant to the
  [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} existing rights issued pursuant to the [Territorial
  Coal Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1522) and the
  [Territorial Dredging Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1523);
  and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} interests in lands described in "Exhibit 2" to the
  Acquisition Agreement dates May 5, 1988, among Her Majesty in right of
  Canada, the Northern Canada Power Commission, the Government of the
  Northwest Territories and the Northwest Territories Power Corporation.
:::

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-645452 .scheduleLabel}
:::

In the Northwest Territories;

North Baffin Island;

all those parcels of land shown as "*TYPE 2 LANDS*" on the following
maps which were signed and approved by Jim Umpherson, Regional Manager,
Land Resources, Northern Affairs Program, Yellowknife, Northwest
Territories and Gordon M. Hamre, Northern Parks Advisor, National Parks
System Planning, Canadian Parks Service, Environment Canada,
Yellowknife, Northwest Territories, which are on file with the Land
Manager, Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, Regional
Office, at Yellowknife, in the Northwest Territories, copies of which
have been deposited with the Land Management Division, Department of
Indian Affairs and Northern Development, Northern Affairs Program, in
Ottawa:

+-----------+-----------+-----------+-----------+-----------+-----------+
| *National Topographic Series Maps*                                    |
+-----------+-----------+-----------+-----------+-----------+-----------+
| 37G,      | 38B,      | 38C,      | 48A,      | 48C and   | 48D.      |
+-----------+-----------+-----------+-----------+-----------+-----------+

SAVING, EXCEPTING AND RESERVING thereout and therefrom any tract of
territorial lands described in the schedule which is subject to a lease
or agreement for sale made pursuant to the [Territorial Lands
Act](/eng/acts/T-7) and the [Territorial Lands
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1525).
