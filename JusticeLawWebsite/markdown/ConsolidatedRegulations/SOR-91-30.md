## Value of Imported Goods (GST/HST) Regulations {#value-of-imported-goods-gsthst-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-30
:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Registration 1990-12-18

Value of Imported Goods (GST/HST) Regulations

P.C. 1990-2739 1990-12-18
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 277(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA} of the
[Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), is pleased hereby to make the annexed
Regulations prescribing the manner of determining the value of goods
imported in certain circumstances.

  1990, c. 45, s. 12
  :::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934125 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/E-15); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian customs waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian customs waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Canadian
      customs waters within the meaning of the [Vessel Duties Reduction
      or Removal Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-304); ([eaux
      douanières canadiennes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[carrier media]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[carrier media]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods capable of
      storing software; ([support de transmission]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[month]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[month]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/99-321, s.
      2.\]]{.Repealed}

  [[process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of any goods, includes
      the adjustment, alteration, assembly, maintenance, manufacture,
      production, modification, overhaul, packaging, repackaging, repair
      or testing of the goods; ([traitement]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[qualifying vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[qualifying vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in section 2 of the Non-Taxable Imported Goods (GST/HST)
      Regulations; ([véhicule admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[remaining duties payable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[remaining duties payable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of
      any goods, means the total of all customs duties, excise duties
      and excise taxes (other than tax under Part IX of the Act) in
      respect of the goods to the extent that they have not been
      remitted, removed, relieved or reduced; ([droits à
      payer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[software]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[software]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means instructions or data to be
      processed by data processing equipment;
      ([logiciel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[value for duty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[value for duty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means value for duty within
      the meaning of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); ([valeur en
      douane]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel within the meaning
      of the [Vessel Duties Reduction or Removal
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-304).
      ([navire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the number of
  months or weeks in a period is the number of months or weeks, as the
  case may be, included, in whole or in part, in the period, the first
  day of the first such month or week, as the case may be, being the
  first day of the period.

## [Prescribed Manner]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934141 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 215(2) of the Act, the value of goods described in item 19,
22, 25, 28, 29, 34, 37, 50, 51, 55 or 56 of the schedule to the
[Temporary Importation (Excise Levies and Additional Duties)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-427), or, in the case of goods
imported by a non-resident person, in item 4, 10, 13, 45 or 48 of that
schedule, and imported in circumstances in which the terms and
conditions of those Regulations are met or, if those Regulations do not
apply, in which those terms and conditions (other than any respecting
security) would be met if those Regulations applied, shall be determined
by the formula

::: {#934143 .Section}
(1/60 × A × B) + C

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the value for duty of the goods;

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the number of months during which the goods remain in Canada; and

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the remaining duties payable in respect of the goods.
:::

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-321, s. 3
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 215(2) of the Act, the value of a vessel that is imported
temporarily in circumstances where section 3 of the [Vessel Duties
Reduction or Removal Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-304) applies
shall be determined by the formula

::: {#934151 .Section}
(1/120 × A × B) + C

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the value for duty of the vessel;

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the number of months during which the vessel remains in Canadian
    customs waters; and

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the remaining duties payable in respect of the vessel.
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 215(2) of the Act, the value of a vessel that is imported
temporarily in circumstances where paragraph 11(a) of the [Vessel Duties
Reduction or Removal Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-304) applies
shall be determined by the formula

::: {#934157 .Section}
(1/50 × A × B) + C

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the value for duty of the vessel;

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the number of months during which that paragraph applies in
    respect of the vessel; and

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the remaining duties payable in respect of the vessel.
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 215(2) of the Act, the value of a vessel that is imported
temporarily in circumstances where paragraph 11(b) of the [Vessel Duties
Reduction or Removal Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-304) applies
shall be determined by the formula

::: {#934163 .Section}
(1/100 × A × B) + C

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the value for duty of the vessel;

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the number of months during which that paragraph applies in
    respect of the vessel; and

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the remaining duties payable in respect of the vessel.
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 215(2) of the Act, the value of a vessel that is imported in
circumstances where section 13 or 14 of the [Vessel Duties Reduction or
Removal Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-304) applies shall be
determined by the formula

::: {#934169 .Section}
A + B

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the value of the repairs or modifications done to the vessel and
    in respect of which section 13 or 14 of those regulations applies;
    and

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the remaining duties payable in respect of the vessel.
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 215(2) of the Act, where any carrier media on which software
is stored is imported in circumstances in which tax has or will become
payable by the importer in respect of any supply made in Canada to that
importer of the right to use that software, the value of the carrier
media shall be determined by the formula

::: {#934174 .Section}
A -- B

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the value for duty of that carrier media and software; and

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the value of that software.
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 215(2) of the Act, the value of a locomotive or miscellaneous
railway equipment imported in circumstances where customs duties are
partially remitted under Order in Council P.C. 1953-18/894 shall be
determined by the formula

::: {#934179 .Section}
(1/120 × A × B) + C

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the value for duty of the locomotive or railway equipment, as the
    case may be;

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the number of months during which the locomotive or railway
    equipment, as the case may be, remains in Canada; and

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the remaining duties payable in respect of the locomotive or
    railway equipment, as the case may be.
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 215(2) of the Act, the value of railway rolling stock
imported for use in international service in circumstances where the
Railway Rolling Stock (International Service) Remission Order No. 4
applies and the rolling stock is temporarily diverted (within the
meaning of that Order) shall be determined by the formula

::: {#934185 .Section}
A + B

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the value of the monthly rental charge for the rolling stock; and

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the remaining duties payable in respect of the rolling stock.
:::

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 215(2) of the Act, the value of railway rolling stock that is
imported in circumstances where code 2338 of Schedule II to the Customs
Tariff applies, and that becomes subject to customs duties by reason of
the temporary use of the rolling stock in Canada, shall be determined by
the formula

::: {#934190 .Section}
(A × B) + C

where      

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the average monthly rental charge for the rolling stock;

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the number of months during which the temporary use in Canada
    continues; and

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the remaining duties payable in respect of the rolling stock.
:::

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 215(2) of the Act, except where any of sections 3 to 11
apply, the value of goods imported in circumstances where customs
duties, excise duties or excise taxes (other than tax under Part IX of
the Act) are reduced, removed, relieved or remitted under an Act of
Parliament, or under a regulation or remission order made under an Act
of Parliament, shall be determined by the formula

::: {#934196 .Section}
A + B

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the value for duty of the goods; and

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the remaining duties payable in respect of the goods.
:::

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 215(2) of the Act, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods (referred to in this section as "exported
  goods") have been exported for the purpose of processing them,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods (referred to in this section as "processed
  goods") are imported for the first time after that processing and are
  accompanied by evidence satisfactory to the Minister that they are the
  exported goods in their processed state or that they incorporate the
  exported goods,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the exported goods were not last imported in
  circumstances

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in which tax, calculated on a value determined
    under these Regulations (other than this section and sections 8 and
    12), was payable,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in which the goods were prescribed goods under a
    provision of the Non-Taxable Imported Goods (GST/HST) Regulations,
    other than paragraphs 3(j) and (k),

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in which a person was entitled under section
    215.1 of the Act to claim a rebate in respect of the goods, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if the goods were last imported before 1991, in
    which either

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} tax under Part VI of the Act was not payable on
      the goods, or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} relief from the payment of, or a refund or
      remission of, that tax was provided,

    on the condition that the goods be exported within a certain period,
    and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where a supply has been made of the exported goods or
  the processed goods

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} outside Canada,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to a recipient entitled under section 252 of the
    Act to claim a rebate in respect of the supply, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in circumstances in which the supply was included
    in Part V of Schedule VI to the Act,

  the processed goods are not being imported for the first time after
  that supply was made,

the value of the processed goods shall be determined by the formula

::: {#934217 .Section}
A +B

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the value of the processing, including the value of any goods
    that were added to the exported goods, and

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the remaining duties payable in respect of the processed goods.
:::

::: HistoricalNote
-  SOR/99-321, s. 4
- SOR/2002-277, s. 10
:::

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 215(2) of the Act, where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a bus or aircraft (referred to in this section as
  "the conveyance") is, on a particular day, imported temporarily by a
  lessee of the conveyance under a lease with a non-resident lessor with
  whom the lessee is dealing at arm's length,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the conveyance is exported on or before the earlier
  of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the day that is 24 months after the particular day,
    and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the lease is terminated,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the conveyance is imported more than once, the
  cumulative number of months in the periods throughout which the
  conveyance is held in Canada by the lessee under a lease with the
  lessor does not exceed 24, and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} on request made in writing to the Minister before the
  particular day, the lessee obtained written authorization from the
  Minister to determine the value of the conveyance under this section,
  subject to any terms and conditions that may be specified in the
  authorization,

the value of the conveyance shall be determined by the formula

::: {#934231 .Section}
(1/60 × A × B) + C

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the value for duty of the conveyance,

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the number of months in the period beginning on the particular
    day and ending on the day the conveyance is first exported after the
    particular day, and

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the remaining duties payable in respect of the conveyance.
:::

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-321, s. 4
:::

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 215(2) of the Act, the value of a qualifying vehicle that is
imported temporarily by an individual resident in Canada and not
accounted for as a commercial good (as defined in subsection 212.1(1) of
the Act) under section 32 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), that
is exported within 30 days after the importation and that was last
supplied in the course of a vehicle rental business to the individual by
way of lease, licence or similar arrangement under which continuous
possession or use of the qualifying vehicle is provided for a period of
less than 180 days is determined by the formula

::: {#934239 .Section}
(A × B) + C

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is
    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the qualifying vehicle is described in any of
      subheading Nos. 8703.21 to 8703.90 and 8711.20 to 8711.90 of the
      List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
      Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011),

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a truck, sport utility vehicle,
        minivan or van, \$300,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a motorhome or similar vehicle,
        \$1,000, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, \$200, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, \$300;

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the number of weeks during which the qualifying vehicle remains
    in Canada; and

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the remaining duties payable in respect of the qualifying
    vehicle.
:::

::: HistoricalNote
- 2012, c. 19, s. 48
:::
