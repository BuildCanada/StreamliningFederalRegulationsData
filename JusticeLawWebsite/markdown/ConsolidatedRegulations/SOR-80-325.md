## Trident Aircraft Ltd. Regulations {#trident-aircraft-ltd.-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/80-325
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 2, 1978-79

Registration 1980-05-05

Regulations Respecting the Insurance of Loans Made to Trident Aircraft
Ltd.

P.C. 1980-1199 1980-05-05
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Industry, Trade and Commerce and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to Industry, Trade and Commerce Vote No. 1 of Appropriation Act
No. 2, 1978-79, is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations
respecting the insurance of loans made to Trident Aircraft Ltd.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869152 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Trident Aircraft Ltd.
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-325).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869154 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Industry,
    Trade and Commerce; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any lender approved by
    the Minister other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Government of Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the government of any province of Canada,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an agency of any government referred to in
      paragraph (a) or (b) or any company that, in the opinion of the
      Minister, is effectively controlled by any such government or any
      agency thereof, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any municipal corporation; ([prêteur
      privé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Trident Aircraft Ltd.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Trident Aircraft Ltd.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a company
    incorporated under the laws of the Province of British Columbia and
    having its head office in the City of Vancouver in that province.
    ([Trident Aircraft Ltd.]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869163 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 4 and
6, the Minister may, under Industry, Trade and Commerce Vote No. 1 of
Appropriation Act No. 2, 1978-79, provide insurance in an amount not
exceeding 90 per cent of the amount of any loans, not exceeding two
million dollars in the aggregate, made by a private lender to Trident
Aircraft Ltd.

## [Application for Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869165 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A private lender who wishes
the Minister to provide insurance on loans pursuant to section 3 shall
make application therefor to the Minister and shall furnish the Minister
with such information relating to the application as the Minister may
require.

## [Fee for Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869167 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A private lender who has
been provided insurance by the Minister pursuant to section 3 shall pay
a fee to Her Majesty of one per cent per annum of the amount of
insurance in force from time to time and shall pay such fee in advance
in semi-annual instalments.

## [Payment of Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869169 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where a private lender has
demanded repayment of a loan referred to in section 3 in respect of
which insurance has been provided by the Minister pursuant to that
section prior to the maturity of that loan, the amount of such insurance
payable to the private lender shall not exceed an amount equal to the
lesser of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of insurance in effect on the date of such
  demand; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 90 per cent of the loss incurred by the private
  lender.
