the Minister of Transport, pursuant to paragraph 35(1)(d) and sections

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in these Regulations.

  [[accessible]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[accessible]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means capable of being reached

      elements of the permanent vessel
      structure. ([accessible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Shipping Act,

  :   [[change of use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that the use of a

      d'utilisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[classification society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[classification society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      classification society that is a member of the International
      Association of Classification Societies (IACS). ([société de
      classification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[construction standards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[construction standards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      Construction Standards for Small Vessels, TP 1332, published by
      the Department of Transport. ([normes de
      construction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[engine space]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[engine space]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any space that contains
      a permanently installed propulsion engine or auxiliary engine,
      including any connected spaces. ([compartiment
      moteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[final preparation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[final preparation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
      official competition, means activities to prepare for the
      competition that take place at the competition venue during the
      days and times specified by the organizer of the
      competition. ([derniers préparatifs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[formal training]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[formal training]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means practice for an

      officiel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-769422 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a pleasure craft;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a passenger-carrying vessel of not more than 15
    gross tonnage that carries not more than 12 passengers and is not a
    human-powered vessel;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a workboat of not more than 15 gross tonnage; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a human-powered vessel other than a pleasure craft.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a fishing vessel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a rescue boat or lifeboat that is carried on board
    a vessel exclusively to meet a requirement for life-saving
    appliances set out in other regulations made under the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a vessel in respect of which the Special-purpose
    Vessels Regulations apply; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an air cushion vehicle of more than 4 500 kg all up
    weight.

## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-769434 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No person shall operate or
permit another person to operate a vessel unless the safety equipment
required by these Regulations is carried on board the vessel and the
equipment meets the requirements of these Regulations.

## [Substitute Safety Equipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-769436 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister determines that there are circumstances in which equipment
  other than the safety equipment required by these Regulations provides
  a level of safety at least equivalent to that provided by the required
  safety equipment, the other equipment may be substituted for the
  required safety equipment in those circumstances.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} To determine the level of safety provided by the
  substituted equipment in the circumstances, the Minister shall assess
  the following factors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the activity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the environmental conditions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the risks to which persons on board
    are exposed;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the specific characteristics of the equipment;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the recommended practices and standards to which
    the equipment conforms;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which the equipment will be used; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the ability of the equipment to protect a person
    from injury.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The substitute equipment shall bear a mark or label
  indicating that it conforms to the recommended practices and standards
  applicable to that type of equipment.

## [Safety Equipment --- Accessibility and Maintenance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-769448 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  safety equipment required by these Regulations shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in good working order;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} readily accessible and available for immediate use;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} except for a life raft, maintained and replaced in
    accordance with the manufacturer's instructions or recommendations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Portable fire extinguishers and fixed fire
  extinguishing systems required by these Regulations shall be kept
  fully charged.

## [Standards for Safety Equipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-769455 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  personal flotation device, lifejacket, lifebuoy, self-igniting light,
  pyrotechnic distress signal or life raft required by these Regulations
  shall meet the applicable standards and tests set out in the schedule.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A mark or label on the safety equipment required by
  these Regulations, and any manufacturer's instructions or
  recommendations, shall be in English and French.

## [Approval of Life-Saving Appliances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-769459 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  personal flotation device, lifejacket, lifebuoy, self-igniting light,
  pyrotechnic distress signal or life raft required by these Regulations
  shall be of a type that is approved by the Minister and shall bear a
  mark or label indicating the approval.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall approve a type of personal
  flotation device, lifejacket, lifebuoy, self-igniting light,
  pyrotechnic distress signal or life raft if it is shown to meet the
  applicable standards and tests referred to in subsection 6(1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a personal flotation
  device required by these Regulations if it was approved by the
  Director of Ship Safety of the Department of Transport or by the
  Department of Fisheries and Oceans and bears a mark or label
  indicating that it was approved by one of those departments or by the
  Canadian Coast Guard.

## [First Aid Kit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-769464 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A first
  aid kit required by these Regulations shall be packed in a waterproof
  case capable of being tightly closed after use and shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a marine emergency first aid kit that contains the
    following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an up-to-date first aid manual or up-to-date
      first aid instructions, in English and French,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 48 doses of analgesic medication of a
      non-narcotic type,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} six safety pins or one roll of adhesive first
      aid tape,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} one pair of bandage scissors or safety scissors,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} one resuscitation face shield,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} two pairs of examination gloves,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} 10 applications of antiseptic preparations,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} 12 applications of burn preparations,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} 20 adhesive plasters in assorted sizes,

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} 10 sterile compression bandages in assorted
      sizes,

    - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} 4 m of elastic bandage,

    - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} two sterile gauze compresses,

    - [(xiii)]{.lawlabel} two triangular bandages, and

    - [(xiv)]{.lawlabel} a waterproof list of the contents, in English
      and French; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a first aid kit that meets the requirements of the
    [Maritime Occupational Health and Safety
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-120) or of provincial
    regulations governing workers' compensation, with the addition of a
    resuscitation face shield and two pairs of examination gloves if the
    kit is not required to contain them.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Instead of a first aid kit referred to in subsection
  (1), a first aid kit that meets the requirements of the [Small Vessel
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1487) as they read
  immediately before the day on which these Regulations came into force
  may be carried on board a vessel for a period of three years after
  that day unless the kit is replaced before the end of that period.

## [Personal Life-Saving Appliances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-769486 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A personal flotation device
or lifejacket required by these Regulations shall not be altered in a
way that compromises its original structural integrity or diminishes the
integrity or readability of a marking set out in a standard related to
it.

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  personal flotation device or lifejacket that is required by these
  Regulations, if it is of an inflatable type, shall be worn by a person
  in an open vessel or, if the vessel is not open, shall be worn when
  the person is on deck or in the cockpit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a sealed-hull rowing
  shell engaged in training that is governed by safety guidelines and
  procedures established by the governing body.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A personal flotation device or lifejacket that is
  required by these Regulations to be carried on board a personal
  watercraft shall be inherently buoyant.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A personal flotation
device or lifejacket required by these Regulations does not have to be
an appropriate size for an infant who weighs less than 9 kg or a person
whose chest size is more than 140 cm.

## [Portable Fire Extinguishers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-769493 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  portable fire extinguisher that is required by these Regulations to be
  carried on board a pleasure craft shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} bear a mark indicating that it is certified by a
    product certification body; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be of a type that is approved by the United States
    Coast Guard.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every portable fire extinguisher that is required by
  these Regulations to be carried on board a vessel other than a
  pleasure craft shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} bear a mark indicating that it is certified for
    marine use by a product certification body; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be of a type that is approved by the United States
    Coast Guard.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A portable fire extinguisher that is carried on board
  a vessel imported into Canada and that does not meet the requirements
  of subsection (1) or (2) shall be certified for marine use by a
  product certification body or a classification society.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** In any reference in these
Regulations to the classification of a portable fire extinguisher, the
letters in the classification refer to the following classes of fires:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Class A fires, namely, fires in combustible materials
  such as wood, cloth, paper, rubber and plastic;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Class B fires, namely, fires in inflammable liquids,
  gases and greases;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Class C fires, namely, fires that involve energized
  electrical equipment where the electrical non-conductivity of the
  extinguishing media is of importance; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Class K fires, namely, fires in cooking appliances
  that involve combustible cooking media such as vegetable or animal
  oils or fats.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A vessel to which Part 2,
4 or 5 applies may carry on board a portable fire extinguisher that is
not marked with a classification set out in column 1 of the table to
this section if the fire extinguisher contains an extinguishing agent
set out in column 2, 3 or 4 of a net weight that corresponds to the
classification set out in column 1, and if the fire extinguisher meets
the requirements of these Regulations in all other respects.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A vessel to which Part 2,
4 or 5 applies may carry on board a portable fire extinguisher that
exceeds the classification set out under those Parts.

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  portable fire extinguisher required by these Regulations shall contain
  an extinguishing agent capable of extinguishing any potential fire in
  the vessel compartment for which the fire extinguisher is intended and
  shall not weigh more than 23 kg.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A portable fire extinguisher required by these
  Regulations may be rated for Class K fires instead of Class B fires if
  it is intended for use in an area with cooking appliances that involve
  combustible cooking media and if the Class A and Class C ratings are
  maintained.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A portable carbon dioxide fire extinguisher shall be
  fitted with an electrically non-conductive horn.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A portable fire

craft, and that meets the requirements of regulations made under the

vessel before the day on which these Regulations came into force, may be
carried on board the vessel for a period of six years after the day on
which the fire extinguisher is manufactured.

## [Fire Buckets and Bailers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-769522 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A fire bucket required by
these Regulations shall have a capacity of 10 L or more, be made of
metal with a round bottom and a hole in the centre, be painted red and
be fitted with a lanyard of sufficient length to reach the water from
the location in which it is stored.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A bailer required by
these Regulations shall be made of plastic or metal, have an opening of
at least 65 cm^2^ and have a capacity of at least 750 mL.

## [Pyrotechnic Distress Signals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-769525 .Part}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  pyrotechnic distress signal required by these Regulations expires four
  years after its date of manufacture.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Type A, B, C or D flares may be carried on board a
  vessel instead of rocket parachute flares, multi-star flares, hand
  flares or smoke signals respectively, if they were approved by the
  Minister before the day on which these Regulations came into force.

## [Life Rafts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-769529 .Part}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The owner and the
operator of a vessel to which Part 4 or 5 applies shall ensure that
every life raft that is carried on board the vessel is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} marked with the date and place of last service;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} serviced at the intervals set out in section 119 of
  the [Life Saving Equipment
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1436) at a service station
  that is accredited by the manufacturer of the life raft; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} except for a coastal life raft packed in a
  valise-type container, stored in a manner that allows it to
  automatically float free if the vessel sinks.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Licensing of Pleasure Craft]{.HTitleText1} {#h-769536 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769537 .Subheading}

- **[[100]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-100 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Part applies in respect of a pleasure craft principally maintained or
  operated in Canada that is equipped, even temporarily, with one or
  more primary propulsion engines whose aggregate power is at least 7.5
  kW.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, only section 110 applies in respect of a
  pleasure craft that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} registered under the Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} registered in another country as having the right
    to fly the flag of that country.

### [Prohibition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769543 .Subheading}

**[[101]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-101 .sectionLabel}** No person shall operate
or permit another person to operate a pleasure craft unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is licensed by the Minister;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the licence is carried on board; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the owner's name and address on the licence are
  accurate.

### [Exceptions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769548 .Subheading}

#### [Licencing]{.HTitleText3} {#h-769549 .Subheading}

**[[102]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-102 .sectionLabel}** A pleasure craft may be
operated without a licence until the day on which the owner of the
pleasure craft receives the licence, up to a maximum of 90 days from the
day of the initial transfer of ownership to an end user, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the acknowledgement of receipt of the application for
  the licence by the Minister is carried on board; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the file number of the acknowledgement is marked on
  the pleasure craft in the form and manner specified by the Minister.

#### [Transfer of Licence]{.HTitleText3} {#h-769553 .Subheading}

**[[103]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-103 .sectionLabel}** A pleasure craft that
is the subject of a transfer of ownership may be operated until the day
on which the new owner of the pleasure craft receives a transferred
licence, up to a maximum of 90 days from the day of the transfer of
ownership, if documents are carried on board confirming the name and
address of the new owner and the date of the transfer of ownership.

#### [Change of Name or Address]{.HTitleText3} {#h-769555 .Subheading}

**[[104]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-104 .sectionLabel}** A pleasure craft may be
operated without an accurate owner name or address on the licence until
the day on which the owner of the pleasure craft receives an updated
licence, up to a maximum of 90 days from the day of the change of name
or address, if, in addition to the licence, documents are carried on
board confirming the new name or address and the date of the change.

**[[105]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-105 .sectionLabel}** A pleasure craft that
was licenced before the day on which these Regulations came into force
may be operated without an accurate owner name or address on the licence
until the day on which the owner of the pleasure craft receives an
updated licence, up to a maximum of one year from the day on which these
Regulations come into force.

### [Period of Validity of Licences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769558 .Subheading}

**[[106]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-106 .sectionLabel}** A pleasure craft
licence is valid for a period of 10 years beginning on the day on which
it is issued, transferred or renewed.

### [Cancellation of Licences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769562 .Subheading}

**[[107]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-107 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may cancel
a pleasure craft licence if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the owner of the pleasure craft intends to register,
  obtain a licence for, or principally maintain and operate the pleasure
  craft in another country;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the owner of the pleasure craft intends to register
  or list the pleasure craft under Part 2 of the Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the owner of the pleasure craft has been issued a
  licence under section 108 and wishes to cancel the licence;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the licence is a demonstration licence and the holder
  is no longer a vendor who sells pleasure craft in the course of a
  commercial enterprise;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the licence was issued in error; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the Minister believes on reasonable and probable
  grounds that the applicant has provided false or misleading
  information to obtain the licence.

### [Voluntary Licensing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769570 .Subheading}

**[[108]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-108 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a pleasure
craft that is not required to be licensed by these Regulations may
obtain a licence for the pleasure craft.

### [Transfer of Ownership of a Pleasure Craft]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769572 .Subheading}

**[[109]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-109 .sectionLabel}** Immediately on the
transfer of ownership of a licensed pleasure craft, the new owner of the
pleasure craft shall apply to the Minister for transfer of the licence.

### [Misleading Marking]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769574 .Subheading}

**[[110]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-110 .sectionLabel}** Subject to paragraph
102(b), the owner of a pleasure craft shall not operate or permit
another person to operate the pleasure craft if it is marked with a
number that is not a licence number or a registration number issued
under Part 2 of the Act and if that number could be confused with a
licence number or a registration number.

### [Demonstration Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769576 .Subheading}

- **[[111]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-111 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  vendor who sells pleasure craft in the course of a commercial
  enterprise may apply to the Minister for a demonstration licence for
  use on any of the enterprise's pleasure craft that are operated for
  the purpose of demonstration.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The holder of a demonstration licence shall ensure
  that it is used on only one pleasure craft at a time.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The demonstration licence shall not be transferred to
  anyone except another vendor.

**[[112]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-112 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may cancel
a demonstration licence if the licence is used for a purpose other than
the demonstration of a pleasure craft.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Safety Equipment for Pleasure Craft]{.HTitleText1} {#h-769582 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769583 .Subheading}

- **[[200]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-200
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), this Part
  applies in respect of a pleasure craft that is operated in Canada.

  craft that meets the safety equipment requirements of another country
  and that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} registered in that country as having the right to

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} licensed in that country and not principally
    maintained and operated in Canada.

### [Safety Obligation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769589 .Subheading}

**[[201]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-201 .sectionLabel}** The operator of a
pleasure craft shall take all reasonable steps to ensure the safety of
the craft and of every person on board.

### [Personal Flotation Devices and Lifejackets]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769591 .Subheading}

**[[202]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-202 .sectionLabel}** If a personal flotation
device or lifejacket that must be carried on board a pleasure craft is
to be worn by a person less than 16 years of age, it shall be inherently
buoyant.

  carried on board a pleasure craft that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is operating on a river, canal or lake in which it

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has no sleeping arrangements and is engaged in an
    official competition or in final preparation for an official
    competition.

  signals in those tables, if the pleasure craft is equipped with

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a 406 MHz personal locator beacon that is the
    subject of a technical acceptance certificate issued under
    subparagraph 5(1)(a)(iv) of the [Radiocommunication
    Act](/eng/acts/R-2), worn by the pleasure craft operator; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a 406 MHz emergency position-indicating radio
    beacon.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Human-Powered Vessels Other than Pleasure Craft]{.HTitleText1} {#h-769729 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769730 .Subheading}

**[[300]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-300 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in this Part.

[[class 3 or above waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[class 3 or above waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means waters that
    have

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} rapids with moderate and irregular waves; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} rapids that are stronger, have more obstructions
      or are otherwise more difficult to navigate than rapids with
      moderate and irregular waves. ([eaux de classe 3 ou
      plus]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[guided excursion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[guided excursion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a non-competitive
    outdoor recreational activity or excursion led by a person in charge
    of the activity or excursion during which the participants use a
    human-powered vessel. ([excursion guidée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[helmet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[helmet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a helmet that has a fastening
    system and that is designed to protect a person's head from injury
    from the mid-line of the forehead to the back of the crown of the
    head. ([casque protecteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769737 .Subheading}

**[[301]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-301 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies in
respect of a human-powered vessel other than a pleasure craft.

### [Personal Flotation Devices and Lifejackets]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769739 .Subheading}

**[[302]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-302 .sectionLabel}** If a personal flotation
device or lifejacket that must be carried on board a human-powered
vessel is to be worn by a person less than 16 years of age, it shall be
inherently buoyant.

### [Guided Excursions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769741 .Subheading}

- **[[303]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-303 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person responsible for an enterprise that conducts guided excursions
  and the leader of a guided excursion shall ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} every participant in the excursion wears the
    following safety equipment:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a personal flotation device or lifejacket of an
      appropriate size, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} when on class 3 or above waters, a helmet of an
      appropriate size; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any equipment or material that is carried on board
    the vessel and that is not being used is secured in place when the
    vessel is moving.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the water temperature is less than 15ºC, a person
  responsible for an enterprise that conducts guided excursions and the
  leader of a guided excursion shall ensure that equipment is
  immediately available or that procedures are established to protect
  the participants from the effects of hypothermia or cold shock
  resulting from swamping, capsizing or falling overboard.

**[[304]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-304 .sectionLabel}** A person responsible
for an enterprise that conducts guided excursions and the leader of a

according to their needs, on the safety and emergency procedures
relevant to the guided excursion.

  excursion, report the number of participants in the excursion to a
  person on shore who has been designated by the leader to be
  responsible for communicating with search and rescue authorities in
  case of an emergency.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the guided excursion takes place in a remote area
  and it is not possible to report the number of participants to a
  person on shore, the leader of the excursion shall leave a record of
  the number of participants and the area of operation in a known
  location on shore that is accessible to search and rescue authorities.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the guided excursion departs from a support
  vessel, the leader of the excursion may designate a person on board
  the support vessel to be responsible for communicating with search and
  rescue authorities in case of an emergency.

### [Human-Powered Passenger-Carrying Vessels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769754 .Subheading}

#### [Racing Canoes and Racing Kayaks]{.HTitleText3} {#h-769791 .Subheading}

  competition that is governed by safety guidelines and procedures
  established by the governing body is not required to carry on board
  the safety equipment required by this Part if it is attended by a
  safety craft that is carrying on board a personal flotation device or
  lifejacket of an appropriate size

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each person on board the canoe or kayak with

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a sound-signalling device; and

    visibility.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Passenger-Carrying Vessels of Not More than 15 Gross Tonnage that Carry Not More than 12 Passengers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-769812 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769813 .Subheading}

- **[[400]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-400 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Part applies in respect of a passenger-carrying vessel of not more
  than 15 gross tonnage that carries not more than 12 passengers and is
  not a human-powered vessel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph 404(3)(b) and sections 405 to 420 do not
  apply in respect of a passenger-carrying vessel that meets the safety
  equipment requirements of another country and that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} registered in that country as having the right to
    fly the flag of that country; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} licensed in that country and not principally
    maintained and operated in Canada.

### [General Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769819 .Subheading}

- **[[401]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-401 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of a passenger-carrying vessel shall, before the vessel
  leaves a place where passengers embark, ensure that all passengers are
  briefed in either or both official languages, according to their
  needs, on the safety and emergency procedures relevant to the type of
  vessel and its length, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the location of lifejackets and specifically the
    location of lifejackets for children;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the location of survival craft;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for passengers in each area of the vessel, the
    location of the lifejackets and survival craft that are closest to
    them;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the location and use of personal life-saving
    appliances, visual signals and vessel safety equipment;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the safety measures to be taken, including those
    relating to the protection of limbs, the avoidance of ropes and
    docking lines, and the effect of the movement and grouping of
    passengers on the stability of the vessel; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the prevention of fire and explosions.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} During the briefing, the operator of the vessel shall
  ensure that a demonstration is provided on the manner of donning each
  type of lifejacket.

<!-- -->

- **[[402]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-402 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of a passenger-carrying vessel shall, before departure,
  report the number of persons on board to a person on shore who has
  been designated by the operator to be responsible for communicating
  with search and rescue authorities in case of an emergency.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the passenger-carrying vessel is operated in a
  remote area and it is not possible to report the number of persons on
  board to a person on shore, the operator of the vessel shall leave the
  information respecting each voyage in a location on shore that is
  known and readily available to search and rescue authorities.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the passenger-carrying vessel is operated from a
  support vessel, or if it is used to transfer persons from another
  vessel to shore, the operator of the passenger-carrying vessel may
  designate a person on board the support vessel or the other vessel to
  be responsible for communicating with search and rescue authorities in
  case of an emergency.

**[[403]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-403 .sectionLabel}** If the water
temperature is less than 15°C, a person who operates or permits another
person to operate a passenger-carrying vessel that does not carry on
board a life raft shall ensure that equipment is carried on board the
vessel or that procedures are established to protect all persons on
board from the effects of hypothermia or cold shock resulting from
swamping, capsizing or falling overboard.

- **[[404]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-404 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  passenger-carrying vessel shall be designed and equipped to operate
  safely in its area of operation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall operate a passenger-carrying vessel
  under circumstances that exceed its design limitations, if any.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Except in an emergency, the operator of a
  passenger-carrying vessel engaged in towing shall ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there are no passengers on board the vessel or the
    tow; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vessel meets the requirements of section 521.

**[[405]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-405 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a
passenger-carrying vessel shall not operate or permit another person to
operate the vessel unless, before it is first put in service, the owner
has informed the Minister, in a form determined by the Minister, of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the intention to operate the vessel or permit its
  operation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the physical characteristics of the vessel; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the nature of its operation.

**[[406]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-406 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a
passenger-carrying vessel shall submit to the Minister, on request,
information respecting the physical characteristics of the vessel and
the nature of its operation.

##### [Life Rafts]{.HTitleText4} {#h-769871 .Subheading}

- **[[411]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-411 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection (2), "river" does not include

## [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Workboats of Not More than 15 Gross Tonnage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-769943 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769944 .Subheading}

- **[[500]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-500 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Part applies in respect of a workboat of not more than 15 gross
  tonnage.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 502 to 521 do not apply in respect of a
  workboat that meets the safety equipment requirements of another
  country and that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} registered in that country as having the right to
    fly the flag of that country; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} licensed in that country and not principally
    maintained and operated in Canada.

- **[[501]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-501 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  workboat shall be designed and equipped to operate safely in its area
  of operation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall operate a workboat under
  circumstances that exceed its design limitations, if any.

**[[502]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-502 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a workboat
shall not operate or permit another person to operate the workboat
unless, before it is first put into service, the owner has informed the
Minister, in a form determined by the Minister, of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the intention to operate the workboat or permit its
  operation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the physical characteristics of the workboat; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the nature of its operation.

**[[503]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-503 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a workboat
shall submit to the Minister, on request, information respecting the
physical characteristics of the workboat and the nature of its
operation.

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} contain 1.2 kg of carbon dioxide per cubic metre of

  dioxide, contain a sufficient quantity of a clean agent to provide the
  same protection as carbon dioxide;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it contains carbon dioxide, be able to be
  completely discharged in not more than 60 seconds, and if it contains
  a clean agent, in not more than 10 seconds; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be in addition to the portable fire extinguishers
  required by section 512.

#### [Exception for Personal Watercraft]{.HTitleText3} {#h-770051 .Subheading}

**[[517]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-517 .sectionLabel}** If every person on
board a personal watercraft is wearing a personal flotation device or a
lifejacket of an appropriate size, the personal watercraft is required
to carry on board only the following safety equipment:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a sound-signalling device;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a watertight flashlight or three pyrotechnic distress
  signals other than smoke signals;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a magnetic compass, if the personal watercraft is
  navigated out of sight of seamarks;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} navigation lights that meet the requirements of the
  [Collision Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1416), if the
  personal watercraft is operated after sunset or before sunrise or in
  periods of restricted visibility; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a first aid kit.

#### [Alternative Safety Equipment for Racing]{.HTitleText3} {#h-770058 .Subheading}

**[[518]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-518 .sectionLabel}** A racing workboat that
is engaged in formal training, in an official competition or in final
preparation for an official competition, and that is operated under
conditions of clear visibility and is attended by a safety craft, may
carry on board the safety equipment that is required by the rules of the
governing body instead of the safety equipment required by this Part.

#### [Equipment Stowage]{.HTitleText3} {#h-770060 .Subheading}

**[[519]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-519 .sectionLabel}** The equipment required
by this Part shall be protected from damage and securely stowed and, if
stowed in a locker or container, the outside of the locker or container
shall be clearly marked to indicate its contents.

#### [Emergency Procedures]{.HTitleText3} {#h-770062 .Subheading}

**[[520]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-520 .sectionLabel}** The owner and the
operator of a workboat shall ensure that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} procedures are established for the use of the
  workboat's life-saving appliances and fire extinguishing equipment in
  case of an emergency; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the crew practises the procedures so as to be at all
  times proficient in carrying them out.

#### [Towing Operations --- Additional Requirements]{.HTitleText3} {#h-770066 .Subheading}

**[[521]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-521 .sectionLabel}** A workboat that engages
in towing operations shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep two lifejackets in the wheelhouse and two others
  in the engine space if that space is normally occupied;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have means readily available for immediately
  releasing or cutting the tow line in case of an emergency;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have two means of escape from the wheelhouse directly
  to the outside, located so that one means of escape is available in
  the event of a heel;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} be clear of obstructions aft of the towing point to
  allow the tow line to swing freely;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} if it is a decked vessel,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} be watertight aft of the towing point or the engine
    space, whichever is further forward, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} be capable of draining any accumulated water
    rapidly overboard; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} if it is not a decked vessel, have positive buoyancy
  when swamped, or a gunwale of adequate height to resist swamping as a
  result of an emergency with the tow.

## [PART 6]{.HLabel1}[Critical Safety Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-770076 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770077 .Subheading}

- **[[600]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-600 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Part applies in respect of a vessel that is operated, repaired or
  maintained in Canada and that is not a vessel to which Part 7 applies.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Part does not apply in respect of an antique
  wooden pleasure craft that is repaired and maintained to conserve its
  original state.

### [General Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770081 .Subheading}

- **[[601]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-601 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who operates or permits another person to operate a vessel, or
  who repairs or maintains a vessel, shall ensure that the vessel meets
  the requirements of this Part.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner and the operator of a vessel other than a
  pleasure craft shall ensure that the vessel has adequate stability and
  structural strength to safely carry out its intended operations.

### [Watertight Closures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770085 .Subheading}

- **[[602]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-602
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every vessel shall have the means
  for positively shutting off underwater penetrations, with the
  exception of wet exhaust systems, and, if the means of shut-off are
  located in an area where there is a risk of fire, the means shall be
  made of material that is not susceptible to fire damage.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a vessel
  that meets the requirements for level flotation set out in the
  construction standards and that is provided with other means for
  stopping the admission of water in the event of failure of pipes,
  tubing or hose lines penetrating the hull below the waterline.

### [Ventilation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770089 .Subheading}

**[[603]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-603 .sectionLabel}** In order to remove any
combustible vapours from an enclosed gasoline engine space on a vessel
other than a personal watercraft, the ventilation system for the space
shall be supplemented by powered ventilation.

### [Fuel Systems]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770091 .Subheading}

- **[[604]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-604 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall install a fuel-burning appliance or system on a vessel
  unless the appliance or system and its installation conform to the
  recommended practices and standards.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall install on a passenger-carrying
  vessel a fuel-burning appliance or system that uses gaseous fuel,
  liquefied petroleum gas, compressed natural gas or naphtha.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall install a fuel-burning appliance or
  system that uses gaseous fuel, liquefied petroleum gas, compressed
  natural gas or naphtha on a vessel in a manner that permits or is
  likely to permit ingress or trapping of the fuel or vapours below
  deck.

**[[605]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-605 .sectionLabel}** No person shall install
or maintain a fuel tank or a fuel system on a vessel in a manner that
permits or is likely to permit leakage of fuel or spillage of fuel.

**[[606]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-606 .sectionLabel}** A fixed fuel tank on a
vessel shall have a means for the overboard ventilation of combustible
vapours.

**[[607]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-607 .sectionLabel}** No person shall install
below deck or enclose by boxing, on a vessel, an inboard engine that
uses gasoline as a fuel unless the carburetor or throttle body fuel
injector, if any, is fitted with a flame arrestor.

### [Electrical Systems]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770099 .Subheading}

- **[[608]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-608 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  electrical component shall be certified by a product certification
  body or a testing laboratory as being ignition-protected in accordance
  with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Society of Automotive Engineers Recommended
    Practice SAE J1171, External Ignition Protection of Marine
    Electrical Devices; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Underwriters Laboratories, Inc. Standard UL 1500,
    Ignition-Protection Test for Marine Products.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vessel uses diesel as its only fuel source;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the electrical component is isolated, in accordance
    with the specifications set out in the construction standards, from
    fuel sources such as

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} engines and cooking appliances,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} valves, connections or other fittings on vent
      lines, fill lines or distribution lines, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} fuel tanks; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the electrical component is located in a
    compartment where the only source of flammable vapour is from
    liquified-petroleum-gas or compressed-natural-gas appliances,
    cylinders, fittings, valves or regulators, and the compartment

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has, for every cubic metre of net internal
      volume, at least 0.34 m^2^ of open area exposed to the atmosphere
      outside the vessel, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is an accommodation space.

### [Pumping and Bailing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770113 .Subheading}

**[[609]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-609 .sectionLabel}** A watertight
compartment on a vessel shall be provided with a means of pumping or
with access for bailing when the vessel is in any operating condition,
unless the vessel cannot retain a sufficient quantity of water to make
it capsize or the compartment is sealed and is not readily accessible.

## [PART 7]{.HLabel1}[Construction Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-770115 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770116 .Subheading}

- **[[700]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-700 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Part applies in respect of a vessel that is constructed, manufactured
  or rebuilt in, or imported into, Canada in order to be sold or
  operated in Canada and that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} propelled or designed to be propelled by an engine;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} permanently fitted with an auxiliary engine; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fitted with a fuel-burning appliance or system that
    uses gaseous fuel, liquefied petroleum gas, compressed natural gas
    or naphtha.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Only sections 701, 703 and 704 apply in respect of a
  pleasure craft that is 24 m or more in length.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a personal watercraft is constructed, manufactured
  or rebuilt in accordance with ISO 13590, then only sections 701, 702,
  704 to 709 and 711 apply in respect of the personal watercraft.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} This Part does not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a vessel that is registered in another country as
    having the right to fly the flag of that country;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vessel that is principally maintained and
    operated in another country and that is not licensed or registered
    in Canada; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a tug.

### [General Requirement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770128 .Subheading}

**[[701]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-701 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise
indicated in these Regulations, the builder, manufacturer, rebuilder,
importer and owner of a vessel shall ensure that the vessel meets the
requirements of this Part.

### [Pleasure Craft]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770130 .Subheading}

**[[702]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-702 .sectionLabel}** A pleasure craft shall
meet

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the construction requirements of this Part; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if its date of construction, manufacture or
  rebuilding or its date of importation is before the day on which these
  Regulations come into force, the construction requirements in force on
  that date.

**[[703]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-703 .sectionLabel}** A pleasure craft that
is 24 m or more in length shall be constructed, manufactured or rebuilt
in accordance with the applicable recommended practices and standards in
force on its date of construction, manufacture or rebuilding.

### [Safety Notice]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770135 .Subheading}

**[[704]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-704 .sectionLabel}** Every safety notice
required under this Part shall

### [Structural Strength and Seaworthiness]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770201 .Subheading}

- **[[713]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-713 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  vessel's structural strength shall conform to the construction
  standards.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A vessel's structural strength and watertight
  integrity shall be adequate for its intended use, taking into account
  the maximum anticipated loads. The vessel's strength and integrity are
  adequate if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is constructed, manufactured or rebuilt
    in accordance with the recommended practices and standards for the
    type of vessel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vessel's design has been used for a vessel of
    the same type that was operated for at least five years without a
    marine occurrence or other event related to a deficiency in its
    construction or maintenance in an area where the wind and wave
    conditions are no less severe than those likely to be encountered in
    the vessel's intended area of operation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the vessel's design is supported by calculations or
    test documents proving that the design achieves the required
    structural strength; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an open vessel, the structural
    strength and watertight integrity are achieved by following
    traditional construction methods that have proven to be effective
    and reliable over time.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The materials and equipment used in the construction,
  manufacture or rebuilding of a vessel shall be suited to the operating
  and environmental conditions that the vessel may encounter.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The owner of a vessel shall ensure that the
  structural strength and watertight integrity of the vessel continue to
  be adequate for its intended use.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The hull of a vessel other than a pleasure craft
  shall be strengthened if the vessel is intended for operation in
  waters where the presence of ice requires the vessel to make
  extraordinary manoeuvres in order to avoid hull damage.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} On the request of the Minister, the builder,
  manufacturer, rebuilder, importer or owner of a vessel shall
  demonstrate that the vessel meets the requirements of this section.

### [Ventilation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770229 .Subheading}

### [Fuel Systems]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770239 .Subheading}

those standards.

permits or is likely to permit leakage of fuel or spillage of fuel.

  be accessible.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A component of a fuel system shall be liquid-tight
  and vapour-tight to the hull interior in accordance with the
  construction standards.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} On a vessel other than a personal watercraft, a fuel
  tank, fuel filter or fuel-line fitting shall not be installed over a
  source of ignition.

  shall be provided with a minimum of two means of escape in each
  accommodation, service and engine space, in accordance with the
  construction standards.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Only one means of escape is required in an
  accommodation, service or engine space if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the space is not normally occupied;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the dimensions of the space do not permit more than
    one means of escape; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the deck area is not more than 28 m^2^.

## [PART 8]{.HLabel1}[Compliance Notices]{.HTitleText1} {#h-770340 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770341 .Subheading}

- **[[800]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-800 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Part applies in respect of a vessel that is propelled or designed to
  be propelled by an engine and that is constructed, manufactured or
  rebuilt in, or imported into, Canada to be sold or operated in Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Part does not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a vessel that is registered in another country as
    having the right to fly the flag of that country;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vessel that is principally maintained and
    operated in another country and that is not licensed or registered
    in Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a pleasure craft that is 24 m or more in length;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a tug; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a high-powered, low-volume vessel that is used
    exclusively for racing.

### [Builder, Manufacturer, Rebuilder and Importer]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770350 .Subheading}

#### [Compliance Notice]{.HTitleText3} {#h-770351 .Subheading}

- **[[801]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-801 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  builder, manufacturer, rebuilder or importer of a vessel shall ensure
  that, before the initial transfer of ownership of the vessel to the
  reseller or end user, the vessel is fitted with a compliance notice
  permanently attached to the inside of the vessel, in a conspicuous
  location plainly visible from the operating position.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a vessel

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is constructed, rebuilt or imported by an
    individual for their personal use; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that meets the following criteria:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is of open construction,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it is not mass-produced,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} it is not propelled or designed to be propelled
      by an inboard engine or stern-drive, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} it has been constructed following traditional
      methods that have proven to be effective and reliable over time
      using wood or other traditional materials or, in the case of a
      canoe, using glass-reinforced plastic.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a personal watercraft that is
  constructed, manufactured or rebuilt in accordance with ISO 13590, the
  builder, manufacturer, rebuilder or importer shall ensure that the
  personal watercraft is fitted not only with a compliance notice, but
  also with the builder's plate set out in that standard.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a vessel that has been the subject of
  an initial transfer of ownership,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel has not been fitted with a compliance
    notice, the builder, manufacturer, rebuilder or importer shall
    provide the owner with a compliance notice; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel has been fitted with a compliance
    notice that is inaccurate, the builder, manufacturer, rebuilder or
    importer shall first inform the Minister and then provide the owner
    with an accurate compliance notice.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (4) does not apply in respect of a
  pleasure craft that was the subject of an initial transfer of
  ownership in Canada before the day on which these Regulations came
  into force.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A compliance notice shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be in English and French;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contain the information set out in section 802;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be in the format set out in the examples provided
    in the construction standards;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be in the form of a plate or label;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be capable of withstanding --- without loss of
    legibility --- wear, environmental conditions (including salt water
    spray), and hydrocarbons and all other chemicals to which the vessel
    may be exposed during normal operation and maintenance; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} be made in such a manner that any attempt to remove
    it or to alter its content will result in the destruction of the
    notice or in a clearly visible sign of the attempt to remove or
    alter it.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) to (5) do not apply in respect of a
  vessel, other than a pleasure craft, whose date of construction,
  manufacture, rebuilding or importation is on or before the day on
  which these Regulations come into force or within one year after that
  day.

<!-- -->

- **[[802]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-802 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  compliance notice shall contain at least the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the model of the vessel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the builder, manufacturer, rebuilder or
    importer and the manufacturer's identification code;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel that is not more than 6 m
    in length, a statement declaring that the vessel met the
    construction requirements as they read on the date of construction,
    manufacture, rebuilding or importation of the vessel;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel that is more than 6 m in
    length, a statement declaring that the vessel met the construction
    requirements for pleasure craft as they read on the date of
    construction, manufacture, rebuilding or importation of the vessel;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel that is more than 6 m in
    length and that meets the construction requirements for a vessel
    other than a pleasure craft, instead of the statement set out in
    paragraph (d), a statement declaring that the vessel met the
    construction requirements for vessels other than pleasure craft as
    they read on the date of construction, manufacture, rebuilding or
    importation of the vessel;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the design limitations of the vessel, if any;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel that is not more than 6 m
    in length, other than a personal watercraft that is constructed,
    manufactured or rebuilt in accordance with ISO 13590, the following
    recommended maximum safe limits and the circumstances in which the
    recommendations do not apply:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the maximum gross load capacity for the vessel
      and the details of the capacity that are set out in the
      construction standards,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the maximum number of persons that the vessel
      may carry, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel is designed to be fitted with an
      outboard engine, the maximum power of the engine.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The recommended maximum safe limits of the vessel
  shall be calculated in accordance with the applicable methods set out
  in the construction standards. However, alternative methods may be
  used if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the alternative methods are more accurate; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the alternative methods are more suited to the
    vessel, owing to its unique nature, and the methods set out in the
    construction standards would result in recommended maximum safe
    limits that are less safe or less suitable for the vessel.

## [PART 9]{.HLabel1}[Hull Serial Numbers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-770445 .Part}

- **[[900]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-900 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Part applies in respect of a vessel that is constructed, manufactured,
  rebuilt or imported in order to be sold or operated in Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Section 902 applies in respect of all vessels in
  Canada.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} This Part, except section 902, does not apply in
  respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a vessel that is registered in another country as
    having the right to fly the flag of that country;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vessel that is registered under the Act, other
    than a vessel registered in the small vessel register;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a vessel that is not licensed or registered under
    the Act and that is principally maintained and operated in another
    country;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a tug; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a floating object that is less than 2 m in length
    and that is not designed to be propelled by an engine.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Section 903 does not apply in respect of a vessel,
  other than a pleasure craft, whose date of construction, manufacture,
  rebuilding or importation is on or before the day on which these
  Regulations come into force or within one year after that day.

**[[901]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-901 .sectionLabel}** A person who operates
or permits another person to operate a vessel shall ensure that the
vessel is marked with a hull serial number in accordance with the
requirements of this Part.

- **[[902]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-902 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall alter, deface or remove a hull serial number.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The builder, manufacturer or rebuilder of a vessel
  may alter a hull serial number in order to correct any error or
  omission that may have occurred when the number was first marked on
  the hull of the vessel.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A hull serial number may be temporarily removed from
  a vessel if it is necessary to do so in order to repair or rebuild the
  vessel and if the number is replaced before the vessel is operated.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A hull serial number may be removed by a rebuilder if
  it is replaced with a new hull serial number.

<!-- -->

- **[[903]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-903 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  builder, manufacturer, rebuilder or importer of a vessel shall obtain
  a manufacturer's identification code from the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The builder, manufacturer or rebuilder of a vessel
  shall permanently mark a hull serial number on the hull of the vessel
  before its first sale to a reseller or an end user.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The importer of a vessel shall ensure that a hull
  serial number is permanently marked on the hull of the vessel before
  its first sale to a reseller or an end user.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The builder, manufacturer or rebuilder of a vessel
  shall permanently mark the hull serial number in a second location on
  the hull, that is either beneath a fitting or an item of hardware or
  that is on the interior of the vessel and unexposed, or, in the case
  of an imported vessel, the importer of the vessel shall ensure that
  the hull serial number is permanently marked in such a location.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The builder, manufacturer, rebuilder or importer of a
  vessel shall keep a record of the second location of the hull serial
  number and shall, on request, provide the information to any person or
  organization authorized under the Act to carry out inspections.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a vessel is imported from a country with which
  Canada does not have an agreement regarding the sharing of information
  respecting a manufacturer's identification code, the importer shall
  ensure that the country's alpha-2 code published by the Maintenance
  Agency for ISO 3166 is added to the hull serial number.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} A person who is engaged in the business of assembling
  kit vessels that have a hull serial number shall, before the initial
  transfer of ownership of the vessel to a reseller or an end user, add
  a suffix provided by the Minister to the hull serial number in a
  manner specified by the Minister.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} If the rebuilder of a vessel does not replace the
  hull serial number, the rebuilder shall, before the initial transfer
  of ownership of the vessel to a reseller or an end user, add a suffix
  provided by the Minister to the hull serial number in a manner
  specified by the Minister.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} The hull serial number shall be in the format set out
  in the construction standards and be located where it is clearly
  visible when the vessel is in the water, namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the upper starboard quarter of the outside
    surface of the transom; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel has no transom, on the uppermost
    starboard side at the aft end of the hull.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} If a vessel is not marked with a hull serial number,
  the owner of the vessel shall make a request for such a number to the
  builder, manufacturer, rebuilder or importer of the vessel.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a request for a hull serial number,
  the builder, manufacturer, rebuilder or importer of the vessel shall
  provide the applicant with a hull serial number on a plate or label,
  or shall permanently mark the hull serial number on the vessel if the
  applicant brings the vessel to them.

- [(12)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a plate or label, the applicant shall
  permanently attach it to the vessel.

- [(13)]{.lawlabel} The builder, manufacturer or rebuilder of a vessel
  shall not use the same hull serial number on more than one vessel.

**[[904]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-904 .sectionLabel}** A vessel is not
required to be marked with a hull serial number if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} despite reasonable efforts, the owner of the vessel
  is unable to obtain a hull serial number from the builder,
  manufacturer, rebuilder or importer of the vessel; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is constructed, manufactured, rebuilt or
  imported by an individual for personal use.

## [PART 10]{.HLabel1}[Safety Precautions and Operational Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-770481 .Part}

### [Mufflers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770482 .Subheading}

- **[[1000]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1000 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall operate or permit another person to operate a
  power-driven vessel unless it is equipped with a muffler that is in
  good working order.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall operate or permit another person to
  operate a vessel equipped with a muffler cut-out or by-pass unless the
  muffler cut-out or by-pass is visibly disconnected in a manner that
  ensures it cannot be easily reconnected while the vessel is in
  operation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply in respect of a
  vessel that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} was constructed or manufactured before January 1,
    1960;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is engaged in formal training, in an official
    competition or in final preparation for an official competition;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is propelled by an outboard engine or a
    stern-drive, if the exhaust gases are directed under water through
    the propeller hub or below the cavitation plate;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is operated at five or more nautical miles from
    shore; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} is propelled by gas turbines or by an aircraft-type
    propeller operating in air.

### [Engine Start-up]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770492 .Subheading}

**[[1001]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1001 .sectionLabel}** No person shall start
a gasoline-powered vessel before the engine space blower has been
operated for a period of not less than four minutes immediately before
the engine is started.

### [Fuel]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770494 .Subheading}

- **[[1002]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1002 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall permit leakage of fuel within or from a vessel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall permit fuel or oil to be discharged
  from a vessel except in accordance with the provisions relating to
  discharges of oil or oily mixtures in the [Vessel Pollution and
  Dangerous Chemicals Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-69).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall fuel a vessel that is at dockside or
  beached unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel is equipped with a portable fuel
    tank, the tank is first removed from it; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel is equipped with a fixed fuel tank,
    the person fuelling it is the only person on board.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall fuel a vessel that is equipped with a
  fixed fuel tank unless all electrical equipment is switched off, all
  doors, windows and ports are closed, all engines are shut off and all
  open flames, including pilot lights, are extinguished.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No person shall carry gaseous fuel, naphtha,
  liquefied petroleum gas or compressed natural gas on board a vessel
  that is carrying passengers. However, liquefied petroleum gas may be
  carried on board the vessel if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of liquefied petroleum gas does not
    exceed 30 kg;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the gas cylinders are well secured and are
    protected from damage and from the effects of excessive variations
    in temperature;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the gas cylinders are stored in an open space or in
    a well-ventilated location; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in a decked vessel, the gas cylinders are stored on
    an open deck in a manner that will not permit the ingress or
    accumulation of the gas below deck.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} No person shall carry fuel on board a vessel in a
  portable container that has not been designed to carry fuel.

### [Water Sports]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770515 .Subheading}

- **[[1005]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1005 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall operate or permit another person to operate a vessel for
  the purposes of towing a person on the water or in the air

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} unless a person on board other than the operator is
    keeping watch on every person being towed and is communicating with
    the operator of the vessel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} unless there is seating space on the vessel to
    accommodate every person being towed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} unless every person being towed is wearing a
    personal flotation device or lifejacket or the vessel carries on
    board the personal flotation device or lifejacket that would be
    required under Parts 2, 4 or 5 if the person was on board; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} during periods of restricted visibility or in the
    period beginning one hour after sunset and ending at sunrise.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a vessel
  that is operated during formal training, in an official competition or
  in a skill demonstration if the vessel meets the safety requirements
  of a governing body respecting that training, competition or
  demonstration.

### [Remote-controlled Vessels and Propeller-driven Surfboards]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770523 .Subheading}

**[[1006]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1006 .sectionLabel}** No person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} tow themselves using a vessel that is operated by
  remote control; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} operate a propeller-driven surfboard-type vessel.

### [Prohibition Against Careless Operation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770527 .Subheading}

**[[1007]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1007 .sectionLabel}** No person shall
operate a vessel in a careless manner, without due care and attention or
without reasonable consideration for other persons.

## [PART 11]{.HLabel1}[Pleasure Craft Accident Reporting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-770529 .Part}

- **[[1100]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1100
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This section applies in a province
  whose government has reached an agreement with the Minister in respect
  of pleasure craft accident reporting procedures, if a notice
  confirming that agreement has been published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a pleasure craft is involved in an accident that
  results in injury to a person who requires medical treatment beyond
  first aid but not admittance to a hospital or that causes property
  damage estimated at more than \$2,500, the operator of the pleasure
  craft shall complete an accident report and submit it to the Minister
  within 14 days after the day of the accident.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a pleasure craft is involved in an accident that
  results in a fatality, injury to a person who requires admittance to a
  hospital, or property damage estimated at more than \$5,000, following
  a fire, an explosion or a collision with another vessel or other
  floating or fixed structure, the operator of the pleasure craft shall
  report the accident to the local police as soon as possible.

### [Life Rafts]{.HTitleText1} {#life-rafts .SchedHeadL1}

- **1** The standards and tests for a SOLAS life raft are those for a
  life raft that are set out in sections 1.2, 4.1, 4.2 and 4.3 of the
  LSA Code, in section 5 of Part 1 of IMO Resolution MSC.81(70), and in
  the Canadian modifications set out in TP 14475.

- **2** The standards and tests for a reduced capacity life raft are
  those for a SOLAS life raft set out in sections 1.2, 4.1 (except
  paragraph 4.1.2.1), 4.2 (except paragraph 4.2.6.3.4) and 4.3 of the
  LSA Code, in section 5 of Part 1 of IMO Resolution MSC.81(70), and in
  the Canadian modifications set out in TP 14475.

- **3** The standards and tests for a coastal life raft are those set
  out in TP 14475.

### [Lifejackets]{.HTitleText1} {#lifejackets .SchedHeadL1}

- **4** The standards and tests for a small vessel lifejacket are those
  set out in Canadian General Standards Board Standard
  CAN/CGSB-65.7-M88, Lifejackets, Inherently Buoyant Type.

- **5** The standards and tests for a standard lifejacket are those set
  out in Canadian General Standards Board Standard CAN/CGSB-65-GP-14M,
  Life Jackets, Inherently Buoyant, Standard Type.

- **6** The standards and tests for a Class 1 or Class 2 lifejacket are
  those set out in Canadian General Standards Board Standard
  CAN/CGSB-65.7-2007, Life Jackets.

- **7** The standards and tests for a SOLAS lifejacket are those set out
  in sections 1.2 and 2.2 of the LSA Code, in section 2 of Part 1 of IMO
  Resolution MSC.81(70), and in the Canadian modifications set out in TP
  14475.

### [Personal Flotation Devices]{.HTitleText1} {#personal-flotation-devices .SchedHeadL1}

- **8** The standards and tests for a personal flotation device are
  those set out in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Canadian General Standards Board Standard
    CAN/CGSB-65.11-M88, Personal Flotation Devices; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Underwriters Laboratories, Inc. Standard UL 1180,
    Fully Inflatable Recreational Personal Flotation Devices, and in the
    Canadian modifications set out in TP 14475.

- **9** The standards and tests for a personal flotation device intended
  for use by children are those set out in Canadian General Standards
  Board Standard CAN/CGSB-65.15-M88, Personal Flotation Devices for
  Children.

### [Lifebuoys and Self-Igniting Lights]{.HTitleText1} {#lifebuoys-and-self-igniting-lights .SchedHeadL1}

- **10** A SOLAS lifebuoy has a nominal outside diameter of 800 mm, and
  the standards and tests for it are those set out in sections 1.2 and
  2.1 of the LSA Code, in section 1 of Part 1 of IMO Resolution
  MSC.81(70), and in the Canadian modifications set out in TP 14475.

- **11** The standards and tests for a self-igniting light are those set
  out in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} section 1.2 and paragraph 2.1.2, without reference
    to "required by regulation III/7.1.3", of the LSA Code;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 10 of Part 1 of IMO Resolution MSC.81(70);
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian modifications set out in TP 14475.

- **12** A small vessel lifebuoy has a nominal outside diameter of 610
  mm, and the standards and tests for it are those set out in sections
  1.2 and 2.1 ( except paragraphs 2.1.1.1, 2.1.1.3, 2.1.1.4 and 2.1.1.7)
  of the LSA Code, in section 1 of Part 1 of IMO Resolution MSC.81(70),
  and in the Canadian modifications set out in TP 14475.

### [Pyrotechnic Distress Signals]{.HTitleText1} {#pyrotechnic-distress-signals .SchedHeadL1}

- **13** The standards and tests for a rocket parachute flare are those
  set out in sections 1.2 and 3.1 of the LSA Code, in section 4 of Part
  1 of IMO Resolution MSC.81(70), and in the Canadian modifications set
  out in TP 14475.

- **14** The standards and tests for a hand flare are those set out in
  sections 1.2 and 3.2 of the LSA Code, in section 4 of Part 1 of IMO
  Resolution MSC.81(70), and in the Canadian modifications set out in TP
  14475.

- **15** The standards and tests for a buoyant smoke signal are those
  set out in sections 1.2 and 3.3 of the LSA Code, in section 4 of Part
  1 of IMO Resolution MSC.81(70), and in the Canadian modifications set
  out in TP 14475.

- **16** The standards and tests for a hand smoke signal are those for a
  hand orange smoke distress signal that are set out in subpart 160.037
  (except paragraphs 160.037--5 and 160.037--7) of Title 46, chapter I
  of the Code of Federal Regulations of the United States, and in the
  Canadian modifications set out in TP 14475.

- **17** The standards and tests for a multi-star flare are those set
  out in TP 14475.
