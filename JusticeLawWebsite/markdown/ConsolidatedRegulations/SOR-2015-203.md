## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-822207 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these By-laws.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Judges
    Act](/eng/acts/J-1). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[senior member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[senior member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to a
    complaint or allegation, the longest-serving eligible member of the
    Judicial Conduct Committee who has not previously considered the
    matter or, if no Judicial Conduct Committee member is available, the
    longest-serving member of the Council who has not previously
    considered the matter. ([doyen]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Establishment and Powers of a Judicial Conduct Review Panel]{.HTitleText1} {#h-822212 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of Judicial Conduct Review
Panel

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson or Vice-Chairperson of the Judicial Conduct Committee,
  established by the Council in order to consider complaints or
  allegations made in respect of a judge of a superior court may, if
  they determine that a complaint or allegation on its face might be
  serious enough to warrant the removal of the judge, establish a
  Judicial Conduct Review Panel to decide whether an Inquiry Committee
  should be constituted in accordance with subsection 63(3) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The senior member designates the members of the
  Judicial Conduct Review Panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition of Judicial Conduct Review
  Panel

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Judicial Conduct Review Panel is to be composed
  of five persons of which three are members of the Council, one is a
  puisne judge and one is a person who is neither a judge nor a member
  of the bar of a province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Serious matter

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Judicial Conduct Review Panel may decide that an
  Inquiry Committee is to be constituted only if it determines that the
  matter might be serious enough to warrant the removal of the judge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matter sent back to Chairperson or
  Vice-Chairperson

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the Judicial Conduct Review Panel decides that no
  Inquiry Committee is to be constituted, it must send the matter back
  to the Chairperson or Vice-Chairperson of the Judicial Conduct
  Committee for them to make a decision on the most appropriate way to
  resolve it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complainant informed

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the Judicial Conduct Review Panel decides that an
  Inquiry Committee is to be constituted, the Council's Executive
  Director must inform the complainant, if any, by letter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision, reasons and statement of
  issues

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Judicial Conduct Review Panel must prepare
  written reasons and a statement of issues to be considered by the
  Inquiry Committee. The Council's Executive Director must send a copy
  of the Judicial Conduct Review Panel's decision, reasons and statement
  of issues to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the judge and their Chief Justice;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Inquiry Committee, once it is constituted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice inviting Minister to designate
  members

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The Council's Executive Director must also send a
  notice to the Minister inviting that Minister to designate members of
  the bar of a province to the Inquiry Committee in accordance with
  subsection 63(3) of the Act.

## [Designating Members to Inquiry Committee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-822233 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of members

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  Inquiry Committee constituted in accordance with subsection 63(3) of
  the Act is composed of an uneven number of members designated by the
  senior member, the majority of whom are from the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister does not designate any members within
  60 days after the day on which the notice is received under paragraph
  2(8), the senior member may designate additional Council members to
  the Inquiry Committee to complete its composition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Senior member chooses chair

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The senior member also designates one of the members
  of the Inquiry Committee to chair the Committee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons not eligible to be members

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The following persons are not eligible to be members
  of the Inquiry Committee:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Chairperson or Vice-Chairperson of the Judicial
    Conduct Committee who referred the matter to the Judicial Conduct
    Review Panel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a member of the same court as that of the judge who
    is the subject of the inquiry or investigation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Judicial Conduct Review Panel who
    participated in the deliberations to decide whether an Inquiry
    Committee must be constituted.

## [Legal Counsel and Advisors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-822246 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons to advise and assist

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Inquiry Committee may
engage legal counsel and other persons to provide advice and to assist
in the conduct of the inquiry.

## [Inquiry Committee Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-822249 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaint or allegation

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Inquiry Committee may consider any complaint or allegation pertaining
  to the judge that is brought to its attention. In so doing, it must
  take into account the Judicial Conduct Review Panel's written reasons
  and statement of issues.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sufficient notice to respond

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Inquiry Committee must inform the judge of all
  complaints or allegations pertaining to the judge and must give them
  sufficient time to respond fully to them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Comments from judge

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Inquiry Committee may set a time limit to receive
  comments from the judge that is reasonable in the circumstances, it
  must notify the judge of that time limit, and, if any comments are
  received within that time limit, it must consider them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public or private hearing

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection 63(6) of the Act, hearings of the Inquiry Committee must
  be conducted in public unless, the Inquiry Committee determines that
  the public interest and the due administration of justice require that
  all or any part of a hearing be conducted in private.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition of publication if not in
  public interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Inquiry Committee may prohibit the publication of
  any information or documents placed before it if it determines that
  publication is not in the public interest and may take any measures
  that it considers necessary to protect the identity of persons,
  including persons who have received assurances of confidentiality as
  part of the consideration of a complaint or allegation made in respect
  of the judge.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principle of fairness

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Inquiry Committee must
conduct its inquiry or investigation in accordance with the principle of
fairness.

## [Inquiry Committee Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-822264 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report of findings and conclusions

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Inquiry Committee must submit a report to the Council setting out its
  findings and its conclusions about whether to recommend the removal of
  the judge from office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of report and notice to
  complainant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} After the report has been submitted to the Council,
  its Executive Director must provide a copy to the judge and to any
  other persons or bodies who had standing in the hearing. He or she
  must also notify the complainant, if any, when the Inquiry Committee
  has made the report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing conducted in public

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the hearing was conducted in public, the report
  must be made available to the public and a copy provided to the
  complainant, if any.

## [Judge's Response to Inquiry Committee Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-822272 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written submission by judge

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  30 days after the day on which the Inquiry Committee's report is
  received, the judge may make a written submission to the Council
  regarding the report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the judge's request, the Council must grant an
  extension of time for making the submission if it considers that the
  extension is in the public interest.

## [Deliberations of Council Concerning Removal of Judges from office]{.HTitleText1} {#h-822278 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Senior member chairs meetings

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  senior member who is available to participate in deliberations
  concerning the removal from office of a judge is to chair any meetings
  of Council related to those deliberations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A quorum of 17 members of the Council is required
  when it meets to deliberate the removal from office of a judge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum --- death, incapacity,
  resignation or retirement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the death, incapacity, resignation or
  retirement of a member during the deliberations, the remaining members
  constitute a quorum.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vote in event of tie

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} During the deliberations of the Council concerning
  the removal from office of a judge, the member chairing the meeting
  may vote in respect of a report of the Council's conclusions on the
  matter only in the event of a tie.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deliberations

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Deliberations of the Council concerning the removal
  from office of a judge may also be held by audio-conference or by
  video conference.

## [Consideration of Inquiry Committee Report by Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-822290 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration of report and written
submissions

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Council must consider the Inquiry Committee's report and any written
  submission made by the judge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Who must not participate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Persons referred to in subsection 3(4) and members of
  the Inquiry Committee must not participate in the Council's
  consideration of the report or in any other deliberations of the
  Council related to the matter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If the Council is of the
opinion that the Inquiry Committee's report requires a clarification or
that a supplementary inquiry or investigation is necessary, it may refer
all or part of the matter back to the Inquiry Committee with directions.

## [Council Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-822298 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report of conclusions to Minister

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Council's Executive
Director must provide the judge with a copy of the report of its
conclusions that the Council presented to the Minister in accordance
with section 65 of the Act.

## [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-822301 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Despite these By-laws,
the Canadian Judicial Council Inquiries and Investigations By-laws, as
they read immediately before the day on which these By-laws come into
force, continue to apply in respect of any inquiries or investigations
being conducted by a Review Panel or an Inquiry Committee or the Council
acting under section 11 or 12, that were commenced under those By-laws.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-822303 .Part}

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-822305 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** These By-laws come into
