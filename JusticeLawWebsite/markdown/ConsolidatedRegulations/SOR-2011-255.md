[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2011-11-15

Communications Security Establishment Regulations

P.C. 2011-1304 2011-11-15
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to subsection 123(1) of the [Public Service
Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-320-e_hq_10884){#fn_81000-3-320-e_hq_10884-ID0EDBAA},

Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-255).

## [Block Transfer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-774995 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 132(1) of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) applies to the
Communications Security Establishment.

## [Cession of Effect]{.HTitleText1} {#h-774997 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations cease to
have effect at 00:00:03 on November 16, 2011.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-774999 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force at 00:00:01 on November 16, 2011.
