## Canada Post Systems Management Limited Incorporation Authorization Order {#canada-post-systems-management-limited-incorporation-authorization-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-358
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1990-06-15

Order Authorizing the Canada Post Corporation to Procure the
Incorporation of Canada Post Systems Management Limited

P.C. 1990-1170  1990-06-15
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council, pursuant to subsection 91(6) of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is satisfied that the
Canada Post Corporation is empowered under section 16 of the [Canada
Post Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-10) to undertake the transaction
described in the annexed Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Honourable Harvie Andre and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to paragraph 91(1)(a) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order
authorizing the Canada Post Corporation to procure the incorporation of
Canada Post Systems Management Limited](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-358).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-926175 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Canada Post Systems Management Limited Incorporation Authorization
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-358).

## [Authorized Transaction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-926177 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Canada Post Corporation
is hereby authorized to procure the incorporation, under the [Canada
Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44), of a corporation named
Canada Post Systems Management Limited the shares of which will be, on
incorporation, held by the Canada Post Corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1994, c. 24, s. 34(F)
