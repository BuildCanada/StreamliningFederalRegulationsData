## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-727502 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Veterans Well-being
    Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Class A Reserve Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class A Reserve Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in subarticle 9.06(1) of the Queen's Regulations and Orders for
    the Canadian Forces and includes proceeding to and returning from
    the place where the training or duty is performed. ([service de
    réserve de classe A]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Class B Reserve Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class B Reserve Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in article 9.07 of the Queen's Regulations and Orders for the
    Canadian Forces. ([service de réserve de classe B]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Class C Reserve Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class C Reserve Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in article 9.08 of the Queen's Regulations and Orders for the
    Canadian Forces. ([service de réserve de classe C]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5). ([état
    d'urgence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[regular force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regular force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5). ([force régulière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

:   [[reserve force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5). ([force de réserve]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
Parts 2 and 3 of the Act, [[barrier to re-establishment in civilian
life]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the presence of a disability or a
temporary or permanent physical or mental health problem that limits or
prevents an individual's reasonable performance in civilian life of

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Rehabilitation Services, Vocational Assistance and Financial Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-727608 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-727609 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this section apply for the purpose of Part 2 of the
  Act.

  [[barrier to re-establishment in civilian life]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[barrier to re-establishment in civilian
      life]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2018-177, s.
      2\]]{.Repealed}

  [[diminished earning capacity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[diminished earning capacity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in
      relation to a veteran, that the veteran is incapacitated by a
      permanent physical or mental health problem that prevents them
      from performing any occupation that would be considered to be
      suitable gainful employment. ([diminution de la capacité de
      gain]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[suitable gainful employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[suitable gainful employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2016-240, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[totally and permanently incapacitated]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[totally and permanently
      incapacitated]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2016-240, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[suitable gainful
  employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to a veteran,
  employment for which the veteran is reasonably qualified by reason of
  education, training and experience and that provides a monthly rate of
  pay equal to at least 66 2/3% of the imputed income of the veteran as
  referred to in subsection 19(1) of the Act.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 10(4) and paragraph 13(4)(a) of the Act, the Minister shall
have regard to the following principles:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the provision of services be focused on
  addressing the needs of the applicant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the provision of services will involve family
  members to the extent required to facilitate the rehabilitation;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} that the services provided be focused on building the
  applicant's education, skills, training and experience; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} that the services provided not be focused solely on
  the applicant's military occupation.

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-727981 .Subheading}

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions

[[obvious]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[obvious]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, when used with reference to a
    disability or disabling condition of a member or veteran at the time
    they became a member, means that the disability or disabling
    condition was apparent at that time or would have been apparent to
    an unskilled observer on examination of the member or veteran at
    that time. ([évident]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[recorded on medical examination prior to enrolment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[recorded on medical examination prior to
    enrolment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a disability or
    disabling condition of a member or veteran, means a written record,
    X-ray film or photograph of the disability or disabling condition
    that was placed in

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a medical report made on the enrolment of the
      member or veteran;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} official documentation covering a former period
      of service of the member or veteran;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the files of the Department of Veterans Affairs
      relating to the member or veteran;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the records of a compensation board or insurance
      company relating to the member or veteran; or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the records of a medical practitioner or a
      clinic, hospital or other medical institution relating to the
      member or veteran. ([consigné lors d'un examen médical avant
      l'enrôlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

**[[48.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48.1 .sectionLabel}** An application for a

**[[48.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48.3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 44.1(2) of the Act, the Minister shall consider whether the
member or the veteran

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sustained an amputation at or above the wrist or
  ankle;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sustained legal blindness in both eyes --- meaning
  that their best corrected visual acuity is less than or equal to 6/60
  or they have less than 20 degrees of visual field remaining --- for a
  minimum of 84 consecutive days;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} sustained quadriplegia, paraplegia, hemiplegia or
  complete paralysis of a limb for a minimum of 84 consecutive days;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} sustained total loss of urinary or bowel function for
  a minimum of 84 consecutive days;

  perform at least three activities of daily living for a minimum of 112
  consecutive days;

  of five consecutive days;

  rehabilitative inpatient care for a minimum of 84 consecutive days; or

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of

  duties that exposed the member or veteran to an environmental hazard
  that might reasonably have caused the injury or disease or its
  aggravation.

- **[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For

  the rates set out in the Treasury Board Travel Directive, as amended

    severely impede the person's ability to access the medical
    examination or assessment; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the means of transportation is an automobile
    other than a taxi, the costs of transportation are payable at the

    have requested the use of their own automobile plus 2 cents per
    kilometre and shall include the costs of parking.

  assessment in a country other than Canada, the payment of the expenses
  shall be made at the same rate and subject to the same conditions as
  the rates and conditions that are established for former members of
  the armed forces of that country for similar costs, or, if no such
  rates are established, at the rates that would be payable if the
  person were resident in Canada.
