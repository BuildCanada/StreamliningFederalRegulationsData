## Quebec Beef Cattle Order {#quebec-beef-cattle-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-293
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1992-05-21

Order Granting Authority to the Fédération des Producteurs de Bovins du
Québec to Regulate the Marketing of Beef Cattle Produced in the Province
of Quebec in Interprovincial and Export Trade

P.C. 1992-1067 1992-05-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to section 2 of the [Agricultural
Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Order granting authority to the Fédération des producteurs de
bovins du Québec to regulate the marketing of beef cattle produced in
the Province of Quebec in interprovincial and export
trade](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-293).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-943592 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Quebec Beef Cattle Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-293).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-943594 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[beef cattle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[beef cattle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any beef cattle produced
    in the Province of Quebec; ([bovin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Fédération des
    producteurs de bovins du Québec. ([Fédération]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-943598 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized to regulate the marketing of beef cattle in interprovincial
and export trade and for those purposes may, with respect to persons and
property situated within the Province of Quebec, exercise all or any
powers like the powers exercisable by the Commodity Board under the
Marketing of Agricultural, Food and Fish Products Act of Quebec in
relation to the marketing of beef cattle locally within the Province of
Quebec.

## [Levies or Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-943600 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board may, in
relation to the powers granted to it under section 3,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix and impose, by order, and collect levies or
  charges from persons referred to in section 3 who are engaged in the
  production or marketing of beef cattle and for those purposes may
  classify those persons into groups and fix, by order, the levies or
  charges payable by the members of the different groups in different
  amounts; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the levies or charges for the purposes of the
  Commodity Board, including the creation of reserves, the payment of
  expenses and losses resulting from the sale or disposal of beef cattle
  and the equalization or adjustment among producers of beef cattle of
  moneys realized from the sale thereof during such period or periods of
  time as the Commodity Board may determine.
