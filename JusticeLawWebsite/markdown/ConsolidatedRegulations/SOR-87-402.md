Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 34(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EHBAA} of the

revoke the [Government Contracts
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-402), C.R.C., c. 701, and to make
the annexed Regulations respecting government contracts in substitution
therefor.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905804 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[advance payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[advance payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a payment made by or
    on behalf of His Majesty under the terms of a contract before the
    performance of that part of the contract in respect of which the
    payment is made; ([paiement anticipé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[approved financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approved financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any corporation or institution that is a member
      of the Canadian Payments Association,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that accepts deposits that are
      insured by the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation or the Régie
      de l'assurance-dépôts du Québec to the maximum permitted by law,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a credit union as defined in paragraph 137(6)(b)
      of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3),

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that accepts deposits from the
      public, if repayment of the deposit is guaranteed by His Majesty
      in right of a province, or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Canada Post Corporation; ([institution
      financière agréée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bid]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bid]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/91-651, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[bid bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bid bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bond given by a person to
    guarantee entry into a contract if the contract is awarded to that
    person; ([cautionnement de soumission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bid security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bid security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bid bond or a security
    deposit given by a person to His Majesty to guarantee entry into a
    contract if the contract is awarded to that person; ([garantie de
    soumission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[construction contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[construction contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a contract
    entered into for the construction, repair, renovation or restoration
    of any work except a vessel and includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a contract for the supply and erection of a
      prefabricated structure,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a contract for dredging,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a contract for demolition, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a contract for the hire of equipment to be used
      in or incidentally to the execution of any contract referred to in
      this definition; ([marché de travaux publics]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2011-197, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[contract security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contract security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a payment bond or a performance bond given on
      behalf of a person to His Majesty to make good on any default by
      that person under the contract by

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} compensating His Majesty therefor, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} completing the performance of the contract to
        the extent required by the terms and conditions of the payment
        bond or performance bond, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a security deposit given by the person to His
      Majesty to secure the performance of the contract to the extent
      required by the terms and conditions of the contract; ([garantie
      contractuelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contracting authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contracting authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the appropriate Minister, as defined in paragraph
      (a), (a.1) or (b) of the definition [appropriate
      Minister]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} in section 2 of the
      [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a department within the meaning of paragraph
      (a.1) of the definition of [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
      section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)
      that has the legal authority to enter into a contract;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a departmental corporation named in Schedule II
      to the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any individual --- other than a commissioner
      appointed under the [Inquiries Act](/eng/acts/I-11) and any
      individual authorized under the [Parliament of Canada
      Act](/eng/acts/P-1) to enter into a contract --- who is authorized
      by or under an Act of Parliament to enter into a contract.
      ([autorité contractante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[goods contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a contract for the
    purchase of articles, commodities, equipment, goods, materials or
    supplies and includes a contract for the construction or repair of a
    vessel; ([marché de fournitures]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[government guaranteed bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[government guaranteed bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bond of
    the Government of Canada or a bond unconditionally guaranteed as to
    principal and interest by the Government of Canada that is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} payable to the bearer,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} accompanied by a duly executed instrument of
      transfer of the bond to the Receiver General in the form
      prescribed by the [Domestic Bonds of Canada
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._698), or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} registered in the name of the Receiver General;
      ([obligation garantie par le gouvernement]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[lease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/92-503, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[payment bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payment bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bond given by a person
    to guarantee the payment for labour to be given or materials to be
    supplied in connection with a contract awarded to that person;
    ([cautionnement de paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[performance bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[performance bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bond given by a
    person to guarantee the performance of a contract awarded to that
    person; ([cautionnement d'exécution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[progress payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[progress payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2023-82, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[security deposit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[security deposit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a bill of exchange

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that is payable to the Receiver General, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is certified by an approved financial
        institution or drawn by an approved financial institution on
        itself,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a government guaranteed bond, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} such other security as may be deemed appropriate
      by the contracting authority and approved by the Treasury Board;
      ([dépôt de garantie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[service contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[service contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a contract for the
    provision of services, including printing services, but does not
    include an agreement under which a person is employed as an officer,
    clerk or employee of His Majesty; ([marché de
    services]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[suppliers' list]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[suppliers' list]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a list that is
    maintained by the contracting authority setting out the names and
    addresses of suppliers from whom the contracting authority may
    solicit bids. ([liste de fournisseurs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905853 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to all goods, service and construction contracts
  that are entered into by a contracting authority and that provide for
  the payment of any money by His Majesty, except

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a contract entered into by the National Film Board;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a contract for the construction of buildings
    entered into under the [Veterans' Land Act](/eng/acts/V-1.5);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a contract entered into under the [Indian
    Act](/eng/acts/I-5) that involves Indian moneys as defined in that
    Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a contract for the performance of legal services;

  - [(d.1)]{.lawlabel} a service contract for expert advice or testimony
    for the purpose of developing or advancing His Majesty's legal
    position in a litigation, a potential litigation or a dispute
    resolution process;

  - [(d.2)]{.lawlabel} a contract for the implementation of a settlement
    agreement in a class proceeding to which His Majesty is a party, or
    of a court order or judgment in such a proceeding;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a contract for the fit-up of an office or a
    residential accommodation if the contract for fit-up is part of a
    transaction authorized pursuant to the [Federal Real Property and
    Federal Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4) or the regulations made
    under it;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} arrangements entered into under an Interchange
    Canada Agreement;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a contract whose purpose is, for operational
    reasons, to fulfil an interim requirement for defence supplies or
    services or to ensure defence logistical capabilities on an interim
    basis, and any related contract;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a contract entered into with a government entity;
    or

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a contract in respect of which a national security
    exception is properly invoked in accordance with subsection 10(3) of
    the [Canadian International Trade Tribunal Procurement Inquiry
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-602), or a contract
    indispensable to national security in respect of which no trade
    agreement referred to in subsection 10(2) of those Regulations
    applies, if the requirements set out in subsection 10(3) of those
    Regulations are met.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(d), section 4 applies to a
  contract referred to in that section.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), section 18 applies to any
  contract referred to in that subsection.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[government
  entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a government of a province, foreign state, a
    political subdivision of a foreign state, or a municipality;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a Crown corporation or a corporation or agency
    wholly owned by a government referred to in paragraph (a); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an intergovernmental organization composed of two
    or more states, or an agency of such an organization.

### [Bids]{.HTitleText2} {#h-905874 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Before any contract is
entered into, the contracting authority shall solicit bids therefor in
the manner prescribed by section 7.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding section 5,
a contracting authority may enter into a contract without soliciting
bids where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the need is one of pressing emergency in which delay
  would be injurious to the public interest;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the estimated expenditure does not exceed

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a goods contract, \$25,000,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a contract to be entered into by
    the Minister for International Development for the acquisition of
    architectural, engineering or other services required in respect of
    the planning, design, preparation or supervision of an international
    development assistance program or project, \$100,000,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a contract for the acquisition of
    architectural, engineering or other services required in respect of
    the planning, design, preparation or supervision of the
    construction, repair, renovation or restoration of a work,
    \$100,000, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other contract to which these
    Regulations apply, \$40,000;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the work to be contracted for is such
  that it would not be in the public interest to solicit bids; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} only one person is capable of performing the
  contract.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905898 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Where a contracting
authority requires a person to give to His Majesty bid security or
contract security, that security shall be dealt with in the manner set
out in this Part.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** No security deposit in
the form of government guaranteed bonds with coupons attached thereto
shall be accepted by a contracting authority unless all coupons that are
unmatured at the time that the bonds are submitted are attached to the
bonds.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A contracting authority
shall hold any bid bond, payment bond, performance bond or
non-negotiable security deposit that is received by the authority until
the terms of the security are fulfilled.

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a negotiable security deposit is received by a contracting authority
  as bid security, the contracting authority shall hold that security
  deposit until it can be disposed of in accordance with subsection (2)
  or (3).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a bid is accepted and contract security is
  required to be provided,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the contracting authority shall continue to hold
    the security deposit referred to in subsection (1) until the
    contract security is provided; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the contract security is provided, the
    contracting authority shall return the security deposit referred to
    in subsection (1) to the person from whom it was received unless it
    forms part of the contract security, in which case it shall be
    retained as contract security.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a bid is rejected, or is accepted but contract
  security is not required, the contracting authority shall return the
  security deposit referred to in subsection (1) to the person from whom
  it was received.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Where a bill of exchange
is received by a contracting authority as contract security or is
retained by a contracting authority pursuant to paragraph 13(2)(b), the
contracting authority shall immediately deposit the amount of the bill
of exchange in the Consolidated Revenue Fund.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Where a negotiable
security deposit other than a bill of exchange is received by a
contracting authority as contract security or is retained by a
contracting authority pursuant to paragraph 13(2)(b), the contracting
authority shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} hold the contract security in a place adequate for
  the safekeeping of the security; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} immediately forward the contract security to the
  Receiver General for safekeeping.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A contracting authority
that has received a negotiable security deposit in respect of a contract
shall return that security deposit to the contractor if His Majesty has
no claim against the contractor arising out of the contract or relating
in any manner to the contract and if the contract has been

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} satisfactorily performed; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} terminated for a reason that is not attributable to
  any fault of the contractor.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Where a security deposit

attached to it at the time it is submitted, the contracting authority or
the Receiver General, as the case may be, shall return any matured
coupons in excess of the security deposit requirement to the person from
whom the security deposit was received, if that person so requests.

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Deemed Terms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905917 .Part}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following terms are deemed to be expressly set out in every
  construction, goods or service contract that provides for the payment
  of any money by His Majesty:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the contractor declares that the contractor has
    not, directly or indirectly, paid or agreed to pay, and will not,
    directly or indirectly, pay a contingency fee to any individual for
    the solicitation, negotiation or obtaining of the contract if the
    payment of the fee would require the individual to file a return
    under section 5 of the [Lobbying Act](/eng/acts/L-12.4);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all accounts and records relating to any payment by
    the contractor of fees or other compensation for the solicitation,
    negotiation or obtaining of the contract shall be subject to any
    accounting and auditing provisions of the contract;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the contractor declares that the contractor has not
    been convicted of an offence under any of the provisions referred to
    in subsection 750(3) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or that,
    if the contractor has been convicted of any of those offences, it is
    one for which

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a pardon was granted under the [Criminal Records
      Act](/eng/acts/C-47) --- as it read immediately before the coming
      into force of section 109 of the Safe Streets and Communities Act
      --- that has not been revoked or ceased to have effect,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a record suspension has been ordered under the
      [Criminal Records Act](/eng/acts/C-47) and that has not been
      revoked or ceased to have effect,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an order of restoration was made under
      subsection 750(5) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) that
      restores the contractor's capacity to enter into the contract or
      to receive any benefit under the contract as the case may be, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the conviction was set aside by a competent
      authority;

  - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} the contractor declares that the contractor has
    not, during the solicitation, negotiation or obtaining of the
    contract, committed an act or engaged in an activity that would
    constitute an offence under

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} section 121 (frauds on the government), 124
      (selling or purchasing office), 380 (fraud) or 418 (selling
      defective stores to His Majesty) of the [Criminal
      Code](/eng/acts/C-46),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 80(1)(d) or subsection 80(2) or
      154.01(1) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11),

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} section 3 (bribing a foreign public official)
      or 4 (accounting) of the [Corruption of Foreign Public Officials
      Act](/eng/acts/C-45.2), or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} section 45 (conspiracies, agreements or
      arrangements between competitors), 47 (bid-rigging) or 49
      (agreements or arrangements of federal financial institutions) of
      the [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the contractor consents, in the case of a contract
    that has a value in excess of \$10,000, to the public disclosure of
    basic information --- other than information described in any of
    paragraphs 20(1)(a) to (d) of the [Access to Information
    Act](/eng/acts/A-1) --- relating to the contract; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a contractor who makes a false declaration under
    paragraph (a), (c) or (c.1) or who fails to comply with the terms
    set out in paragraph (b) or (d) is in default under the contract and
    agrees, in addition to any other remedies that may be available
    against them, that they will immediately return any advance payments
    and that the contracting authority may terminate the contract.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following terms are deemed to be expressly set
  out in every bid solicitation for a construction, goods or service
  contract that provides for the payment of any money by His Majesty:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the bidder declares that the bidder has not,
    directly or indirectly, paid or agreed to pay, and will not,
    directly or indirectly, pay, a contingency fee to any individual for
    the solicitation, negotiation or obtaining of the contract if the
    payment of the fee would require the individual to file a return
    under section 5 of the [Lobbying Act](/eng/acts/L-12.4);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the bidder declares that the bidder has not been
    convicted of an offence under any of the provisions referred to in
    subsection 750(3) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or that, if
    the bidder has been convicted of any of those offences, it is one
    for which

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a pardon was granted under the [Criminal Records
      Act](/eng/acts/C-47) --- as it read immediately before the coming
      into force of section 109 of the Safe Streets and Communities Act
      --- that has not been revoked or ceased to have effect,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a record suspension has been ordered under the
      [Criminal Records Act](/eng/acts/C-47) and that has not been
      revoked or ceased to have effect,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an order of restoration was made under
      subsection 750(5) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) that
      restores the bidder's capacity to enter into the contract or to
      receive any benefit under the contract as the case may be, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the conviction was set aside by a competent
      authority; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the bidder declares that the bidder has not, during
    the bid solicitation process, committed an act or engaged in an
    activity that would constitute an offence under

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} section 121 (frauds on the government), 124
      (selling or purchasing office), 380 (fraud) or 418 (selling
      defective stores to His Majesty) of the [Criminal
      Code](/eng/acts/C-46),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 80(1)(d) or subsection 80(2) or
      154.01(1) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11),

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} section 3 (bribing a foreign public official)
      or 4 (accounting) of the [Corruption of Foreign Public Officials
      Act](/eng/acts/C-45.2), or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} section 45 (conspiracies, agreements or
      arrangements between competitors), 47 (bid-rigging) or 49
      (agreements or arrangements of federal financial institutions) of
      the [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34).
