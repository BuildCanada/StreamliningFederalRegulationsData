## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1009414 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Copyright
    Act](/eng/acts/C-42). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commissioner of
    Patents. ([commissaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Correspondence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1009418 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  correspondence intended for the Commissioner shall be addressed to the
  Copyright Office.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Correspondence addressed to the Copyright Office may
  be physically delivered to the Office during ordinary business hours
  of the Office and shall be considered to be received by the Office on
  the day of the delivery.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), where
  correspondence addressed to the Copyright Office is physically
  delivered to the Office outside of its ordinary business hours, it
  shall be considered to have been delivered to the Office during
  ordinary business hours on the day when the Office is next open for
  business.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Correspondence addressed to the Copyright Office may
  be physically delivered to an establishment that is designated by the
  Commissioner in the Canadian Patent Office Record as an establishment
  to which correspondence addressed to the Office may be delivered,
  during ordinary business hours of that establishment, and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the delivery is made to the establishment on
    a day that the Office is open for business, the correspondence shall
    be considered to be received by the Office on that day; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the delivery is made to the establishment on
    a day that the Office is closed for business, the correspondence
    shall be considered to be received by the Office on the day when the
    Office is next open for business.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (4), where
  correspondence addressed to the Copyright Office is physically
  delivered to an establishment outside of ordinary business hours of
  the establishment, it shall be considered to have been delivered to
  that establishment during ordinary business hours on the day when the
  establishment is next open for business.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Correspondence addressed to the Copyright Office may
  be sent at any time by electronic or other means of transmission
  specified in the Canadian Patent Office Record.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (6), where, according
  to the local time of the place where the Copyright Office is located,
  the correspondence is delivered on a day when the Office is open for
  business, it shall be considered to be received by the Office on that
  day.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (6), where, according
  to the local time of the place where the Copyright Office is located,
  the correspondence is delivered on a day when the Office is closed for
  business, it shall be considered to be received by the Office on the
  day when the Office is next open for business.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-211, s. 1
:::

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as otherwise provided by the Act or these Regulations, communication
  in respect of a copyright shall be in writing, but the Commissioner
  may also accept oral communications.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may request that an oral
  communication be confirmed in writing.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-211, s. 2
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  address required to be furnished pursuant to the Act or these
  Regulations shall be a complete mailing address and shall include the
  street name and number, where one exists, and the postal code.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Commissioner has not been notified of a
  change of address, the Commissioner is not responsible for any
  correspondence not received by an author, legal representative, any
  person purporting to be the agent of an author or their legal
  representative, or by an assignor, assignee, licensor or licensee.

## [Application for Registration of Copyright]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1009440 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for the registration of a copyright

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a work, shall be made in accordance with section
    55 of the Act, and deal with the registration of only one work; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a performer's performance, sound recording or
    communication signal, shall be made in accordance with section 56 of
    the Act, and deal with the registration of only one performer's
    performance, sound recording or communication signal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for the registration of a copyright
  referred to in subsection (1) shall be accompanied by the fee set out
  in column 2 of item 1 of the schedule.

## [Request for Registration of Assignment or Licence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1009446 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  request for the registration of an assignment of copyright, or a
  licence granting an interest in a copyright, shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be in writing; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contain the following information:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the names and addresses of the assignor and
      assignee or the licensor and licensee,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the interest being granted by
      assignment or licence, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the title of the work, performer's performance,
      sound recording or communication signal, and, if available, the
      registration number of that work, performer's performance, sound
      recording or communication signal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request for registration referred to in
  subsection (1) shall be accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the evidence required by paragraph 57(1)(a) of the
    Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fee set out in column 2 of item 2 of the
    schedule.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-211, s. 3
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1009459 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where the Commissioner
determines that an application for registration of copyright, or a
request for registration of an assignment of copyright, or a licence
granting an interest in a copyright, is defective because it lacks any
information or other item, the Commissioner shall notify the person
applying for or requesting registration and that person shall have sixty
days from the date of that notice to cure the defect. If the defect is
not cured within that sixty day period, the Commissioner shall notify
that person that the application or request has been rejected, in which
case no further action may be taken for registration unless a fresh
application or request is made and the applicable fee set out in the
schedule for that fresh application or request is paid.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** All applications for
registration of copyright, requests for registration of an assignment of
copyright or of a licence granting an interest in a copyright, and any
correspondence to the Commissioner shall be legible and clear and, if in
paper form, on white paper that measures at least 21 cm by 28 cm but not
more than 22 cm by 35 cm, on one side only, with left and upper margins
of at least 2.5 cm.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-211, s. 4
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The fee to be paid by a
user of a service of the Copyright Office set out in column 1 of any of
items 3 to 8 of the schedule is the fee set out in column 2 of that
item.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-211, s. 5
:::
