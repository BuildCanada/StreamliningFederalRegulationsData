## Accredited ICAO Missions Remission Order (Part IX of the Excise Tax Act) {#accredited-icao-missions-remission-order-part-ix-of-the-excise-tax-act .Title-of-Act}

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Accredited ICAO Missions Remission Order (Part IX of the Excise Tax Act)

P.C. 2003-1495  2003-10-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to remit certain tax, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EFBAA} of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed
[Accredited ICAO Missions Remission Order (Part IX of the Excise Tax
Act)](/eng/regulations/SI-2003-164).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-608965 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[accredited ICAO mission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[accredited ICAO mission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permanent
    mission of a foreign state that was accredited to the International
    Civil Aviation Organization in Canada during the eligible period.
    ([mission accréditée auprès de l'OACI]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning
    on April 1, 2002 and ending on the day immediately before the day on
    which the Order Amending the ICAO Privileges and Immunities Order
    (Accredited Missions) is registered. ([période
    admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the tax imposed under Part IX of
    the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15). ([taxe]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-608970 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 and
4, remission is hereby granted to each accredited ICAO mission of tax
that became payable by the mission equal to the amount, if any, by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax that became payable by the mission during the
  eligible period

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the tax that would have become payable by the mission
  during the eligible period if, for that period, the mission had been
  accorded the privileges provided under section 5 of the [ICAO
  Privileges and Immunities Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-563).

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-608975 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Each grant of remission to
an accredited ICAO mission is conditional on the mission having paid the
tax to which the claim for remission pertains and on the mission
submitting a written claim for that remission to the Minister of
National Revenue before the first day of the third calendar month
following the calendar month in which this Order is made, which claim
shall be accompanied by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} original receipts verifying applicable tax paid by
  the mission; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} written evidence verifying that, for the period to
  which the receipts pertain, the mission constituted an accredited ICAO
  mission.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** There shall not be
included, in determining the amount of a remission under section 2, any
amount of tax that was otherwise refunded, rebated, credited or
remitted.
