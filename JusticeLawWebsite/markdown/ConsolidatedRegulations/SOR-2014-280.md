## [Oath of Secrecy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-808291 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following oath of
secrecy is prescribed for the purposes of paragraph 45.45(1)(a) of the
[Royal Canadian Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10):

"I, ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-solid}, do swear (or
solemnly affirm) that I will not, without due authority, disclose or
make known to any person not legally entitled to it any knowledge or
information obtained by me in the course of my duties under the [Royal
Canadian Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10). (*In the case of an oath
other than a solemn affirmation, add*: So help me (*Name of Deity*)).
