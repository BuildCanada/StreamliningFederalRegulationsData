## Manitoba Turkey Marketing Levies Order {#manitoba-turkey-marketing-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/84-835
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1984-10-29

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Levies From
Certain Turkey Producers in the Province of Manitoba
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Manitoba Turkey Producers' Marketing Board, pursuant to section 4 of
the [Manitoba Turkey Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._160), C.R.C.,
c. 160, hereby makes the annexed [Order providing for the fixing,
imposing and collecting of levies from certain turkey producers in the
province of Manitoba](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-835).

Winnipeg, Manitoba, October 23, 1984
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884712 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Manitoba Turkey Marketing Levies
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-835).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884714 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Natural Products Marketing Act
    (Manitoba); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Manitoba Turkey Producers'
    Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    raising or keeping of turkeys in the Province of Manitoba;
    ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a turkey of any variety, grade
    or class raised or kept for slaughter in the Province of Manitoba.
    ([dindon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884720 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay
levies to the Board at the rate of one and one-half cents (\$.015) per
kilogram live weight on all turkeys marketed by him in interprovincial
or export trade.

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884722 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), every producer shall pay the levies referred to in
  section 3 to the Board at its office in Winnipeg, Manitoba not later
  than seven days after the last day of the week in which the turkeys
  were sold.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The levies referred to in section 3 may be deducted
  from the amount owed to the producer by the person who purchases the
  turkey and paid by the purchaser to the Board at its office in
  Winnipeg, Manitoba not later than seven days after the last day of the
  week in which the turkeys were sold.

## [Exclusion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884726 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Order does not apply
to the sale of turkeys less than 10 days old.
