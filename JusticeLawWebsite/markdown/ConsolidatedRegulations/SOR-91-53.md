## [Prescribed Amount]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939081 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
definition [[estimated federal sales tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
subsection 121(1) of the Act, the prescribed amount in respect of a
residential complex is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where, in an application filed for a rebate under
  section 121 of the Act in respect of the complex, the amount applied
  for is not based on the fair market value of the complex, the
  consideration for the supply of the complex or the subscription price
  (within the meaning assigned by subsection 336(6) of the Act) for an
  interest in a limited partnership that relates to that complex, the
  amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#939084 .Paragraph}
  A × \$50

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is
      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the complex is not a condominium unit,
        the number of square metres of the interior floor space of the
        complex, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the complex is a condominium unit, the
        total of

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the number of square metres of the interior
          floor space of the unit, and

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the amount equal to the total number of
          square metres of interior floor space of the common areas of
          the condominium complex in which the unit is situated
          multiplied by the fraction obtained by dividing the number of
          square metres of interior floor space of the unit by the total
          number of square metres of interior floor space of all
          condominium units in the condominium complex; and
  :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the amount determined by the
  formula

  ::: {#939092 .Paragraph}
  A × 4.25%

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is
      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where a rebate under subsection 121(2) of the
        Act is payable to an individual in respect of the complex, the
        consideration for the supply by way of sale of the complex to
        the individual,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where a rebate under subsection 121(3) of the
        Act is payable to a builder in respect of the complex, the
        consideration for the supply by way of sale of the complex by
        the builder,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where a builder of the complex was deemed
        under section 191 of the Act to have collected, at any time, tax
        in respect of the complex and a rebate under section 121 of the
        Act is payable to the builder in respect of the complex,

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the builder is a limited partnership, the
          complex is a residential condominium unit and subsection
          336(5) of the Act applies to the builder in respect of the
          complex, 80% of the subscription price (within the meaning
          assigned by subsection 336(6) of the Act) for the interest in
          the limited partnership that relates to that complex, and

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the fair market value of
          the complex at that time,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} where tax was deemed under subsection 336(3)
        of the Act to have been collected in respect of a supply of the
        complex and a rebate under subsection 121(3) of the Act is
        payable to the builder of the complex, the consideration for the
        supply, and

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a complex that is a condominium
        unit, where tax was deemed under subsection 336(4) of the Act to
        have been collected in respect of a supply of the condominium
        complex in which the unit is situated and a rebate under
        subsection 121(3) of the Act is payable to the builder of the
        complex, the portion of the consideration for the supply that is
        attributable to that unit.
  :::

## [Interior Floor Space]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939104 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/96-551, s. 2(E)

\]
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), in these Regulations, the interior floor space of a
  complex or unit includes the width of its enclosing walls that are not
  adjacent to any other complex or unit and half of the width of its
  enclosing walls that are adjacent to another complex or unit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The interior floor space of a complex and of the
  common areas of a condominium complex does not include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} storage rooms, attics and basements, unless they
    are finished to a standard comparable to the living areas of the
    complex

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the complex is a specified single unit
      residential complex, by the builder who supplies the complex to
      the person who is entitled to a rebate under section 121 in
      respect of the complex, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, by a builder of the complex;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} parking areas; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} areas set aside for the placement of equipment for
    the heating of, or the supply of water, gas or electricity to, the
    complex or the condominium complex.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/96-551, ss. 3, 4(T)\]]{.Repealed}

- SOR/96-551, s. 3
