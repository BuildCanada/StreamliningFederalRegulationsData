## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614031 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to any beneficiary of an overpayment in respect of a Canada Education
Savings grant paid under the [Canada Education Savings
Act](/eng/acts/C-3.6) and the [Canada Education Savings
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-151) during the period beginning
on January 1, 1998 and ending on June 30, 2005.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614033 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
the condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the beneficiary is a person so designated in respect
  of a registered education savings plan within the meaning of section
  146.1 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the grant was paid to or for the benefit of the
  beneficiary during the period specified in section 1;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the grant has not been repaid; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the minimum contributions under subparagraph
  4(1)(c)(i) or (ii) of the [Canada Education Savings
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-151) were not made to the
  registered education savings plan in respect of the beneficiary.
