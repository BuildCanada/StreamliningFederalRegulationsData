## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806502 .Part}

Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Railway Safety
Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2).

## [Designated Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806505 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated provisions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  provision of the Act or its regulations that is set out in column 1 of
  a Part of Schedule 1 is designated as a provision the contravention of
  which may be proceeded with as a violation in accordance with sections
  40.13 to 40.22 of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amounts --- individuals

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount set out in column 2 of a Part of Schedule
  1 is the maximum amount payable by an individual in respect of a
  violation of the designated provision set out in column 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amounts --- corporations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amount set out in column 3 of a Part of Schedule
  1 is the maximum amount payable by a corporation in respect of a
  violation of the designated provision set out in column 1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designations --- orders, rules and
emergency directives

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following instruments are designated as provisions the contravention
  of which may be proceeded with as a violation in accordance with
  sections 40.13 to 40.22 of the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an order made under subsection 7(2) or 19(1) of the
    Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a rule in force under section 19 or 20 of the Act;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} an order made under section 32.01 of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an emergency directive made under section 33 of the
    Act; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an order made under section 36 of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amounts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The maximum amount payable for a violation referred
  to in paragraph 1(a) or (b) is \$25,000 in the case of an individual
  and \$125,000 in the case of a corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amounts

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The maximum amount payable for a violation referred
  to in paragraph (1)(b.1), (c) or (d) is \$50,000 in the case of an
  individual and \$250,000 in the case of a corporation.
