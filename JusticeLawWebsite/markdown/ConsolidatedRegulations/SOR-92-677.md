## Specific Agreement Confirmation Regulations {#specific-agreement-confirmation-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-677
:::

[INDIAN LANDS AGREEMENT (1986) ACT](/eng/acts/I-6.7)

Registration 1992-11-23

Regulations Respecting the Confirmation of Specific Agreements by
Referendum

P.C. 1992-2318  1992-11-19
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
section 5 of the [Indian Lands Agreement (1986)
Act](/eng/acts/I-6.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations respecting the confirmation of specific
agreements by referendum](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-677).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1988, c. 39
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949559 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Specific Agreement Confirmation
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-677).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949561 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Assistant Deputy Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Assistant Deputy Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Assistant Deputy Minister, Lands, Revenues and Trusts, of the
    Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development;
    ([sous-ministre adjoint]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a band in respect of which a
    referendum is to be held; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[deputy presiding officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deputy presiding officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
    appointed by the presiding officer pursuant to paragraph 9(1)(a);
    ([président du scrutin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Indian
    Affairs and Northern Development; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a sheriff, deputy
    sheriff, sheriff's officer, police officer, police constable or any
    other person who is employed for the preservation and maintenance of
    the public peace; ([agent de la paix]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[presiding officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[presiding officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a presiding officer
    appointed pursuant to subsection 4(1); ([président
    d'élection]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[referendum]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[referendum]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a referendum held under
    section 10 of the schedule to the [Indian Lands Agreement (1986)
    Act](/eng/acts/I-6.7) to confirm a specific agreement.
    ([référendum]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Secret Ballot]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949570 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A referendum shall be
conducted by secret ballot.

## [Appointment of Presiding Officer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949572 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  band proposes to confirm a specific agreement by referendum, the
  council of the band shall, within 15 days after the band enters into
  the specific agreement, appoint a presiding officer and inform the
  Minister of the date on which the referendum is to be held and the
  name of the presiding officer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of the information referred to in
  subsection (1), the Minister may designate an official of the
  Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development to be an
  observer at the referendum and shall inform the council of the band of
  the name of any official so designated.

## [Eligible Voters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949576 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every person is an eligible
voter and entitled to vote at a referendum who

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a member of the band;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is at least 18 years of age; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is not disqualified from voting at band elections.

## [Notice of Referendum]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949581 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  presiding officer shall post a notice of the referendum, at least 30
  days before the date of the referendum, in at least two public places
  where the notice is likely to come to the attention of the members of
  the band.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice referred to in subsection (1) shall set
  out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the time and date of the referendum;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the question to be submitted to the voters; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the location of the polling stations.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the specific agreement that is the subject
  of the referendum shall be attached to the notice referred to in
  subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-232, s. 3
:::

## [Voters' List]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949591 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** At least 15 days before the
date of the referendum, the presiding officer shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prepare a list, in alphabetical order, of every
  eligible voter that indicates the polling station where each person is
  to vote; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} post a copy of the list of eligible voters in at
  least two public places where the list is likely to come to the
  attention of the members of the band.

<!-- -->

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  eligible voter may apply to the presiding officer within 10 days after
  the posting of the list of voters to have the list revised on the
  ground that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of an eligible voter has been omitted;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of an eligible voter has been incorrectly
    set out; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of a person not entitled to vote has been
    included.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the presiding officer is satisfied that a
  revision is necessary to the list of eligible voters, the presiding
  officer shall revise the list accordingly.

## [Duties of Presiding Officer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949601 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  the referendum, the presiding officer shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint a deputy presiding officer for each polling
    station to exercise the powers and perform the duties of the
    presiding officer under sections 10 to 18, subsection 19(1), and
    sections 21 and 23;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prepare a sufficient number of ballots that
    indicate the question to be submitted to the voters;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} procure a ballot box and an adequate supply of lead
    pencils for marking the ballots for each polling station;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} before a polling station opens, deliver a ballot
    box, ballots and pencils to the deputy presiding officer at that
    polling station; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide at least one polling booth for each polling
    station where a voter can mark a ballot free from observation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appointment of deputy presiding officers pursuant
  to paragraph (1)(a) shall be approved by the council of the band.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Immediately before a
polling station is opened, the presiding officer shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} open the ballot box in the presence of witnesses and
  ask every person who is present to witness that it is empty;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} lock and seal the ballot box; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} place the ballot box in public view for the reception
  of ballots.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Where a person arrives at
a polling station for the purpose of voting and the presiding officer is
satisfied that the person's name is included on the list of eligible
voters, the presiding officer shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} initial the back of a ballot in such a manner that,
  when the ballot is folded, the initials can be seen without unfolding
  the ballot;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} give the ballot to that person; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} place a mark opposite the name of that person on the
  list of voters.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** At the request of a voter
at a polling station, the presiding officer shall explain to the voter
how to vote.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 16,
the presiding officer shall not allow more than one voter at a time in a
polling booth.

## [Use of Interpreters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949620 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Where the presiding
officer does not understand the language spoken by a voter, the
presiding officer shall appoint an interpreter to communicate to the
voter all matters necessary to enable the voter to vote.

## [Responsibilities of Voter]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949622 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A person who receives a
ballot pursuant to section 11 shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} go immediately to a polling booth;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} place a mark on the ballot beside the word "YES" or
  "NO" opposite the question stated on the ballot;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} fold the ballot in a manner that conceals the
  person's mark but exposes the initials on the back of the ballot; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} immediately deliver the ballot to the presiding
  officer for deposit in the ballot box.

## [Assistance to Special Voters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949628 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  request of a voter who is not able to read or who is incapacitated by
  blindness or other physical cause, the presiding officer shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assist the voter by marking the ballot in the
    manner directed by the voter and placing the ballot in the ballot
    box; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is practicable and helpful to do so, provide
    a template for the use of the voter.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, pursuant to subsection (1), the presiding
  officer marks a ballot, the officer shall indicate on the list of
  voters, opposite the name of the voter, that the ballot was marked by
  the officer at the request of the voter, and the reasons therefor.

## [Soiled or Spoiled Ballots]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949634 .Part}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Where a voter receives a
soiled or improperly printed ballot or inadvertently spoils a ballot in
marking it, the voter is entitled to another ballot if the voter returns
the original ballot to the presiding officer.

## [Forfeit of Right to Vote]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949636 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Where a voter who
receives a ballot refuses to vote or leaves the polling booth without
returning the ballot to the presiding officer, the voter forfeits the
right to vote and the presiding officer shall indicate on the list of
voters opposite the name of that voter that the voter did not return the
ballot or refused to vote, as the case may be.

## [Hours for Voting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949638 .Part}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  presiding officer shall keep each polling station open from 9:00 a.m.
  until 6:00 p.m. on the day set for voting.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the presiding officer is satisfied, after
  consulting with the chief or a member of the council of the band, that
  it would be inconvenient to the voters to close the polling stations
  at 6:00 p.m., the presiding officer or deputy presiding officer may
  keep them open for up to two additional hours.

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** A voter who is inside a
polling station at the time fixed for closing the polling station is
entitled to vote before the polling station is closed.

## [Examination and Counting of Ballots]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949643 .Part}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Immediately after the
closing of a polling station, the presiding officer shall examine the
ballots in the presence of any members of the council of the band who
are present and shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} reject any ballot that was not supplied by the
  presiding officer and any ballot that does not clearly indicate the
  decision of the voter; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} of the ballots that have not been rejected, count the
  number of votes that were in favour of and the number of votes that
  were against the specific agreement.

<!-- -->

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  presiding officer shall collect the ballots used in the voting, place
  them in a sealed envelope and keep them for 60 days.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} After the 60 days referred to in subsection (1), the
  presiding officer shall destroy the ballots in the presence of two
  witnesses unless the Assistant Deputy Minister directs the officer not
  to destroy them.

## [Maintaining Peace and Order]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949650 .Part}

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The presiding officer
shall maintain peace and good order during the voting and for that
purpose may enlist the assistance of peace officers.

## [Report on Referendum]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949652 .Part}

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  the presiding officer knows the results of the voting at all of the
  polling stations, the presiding officer shall immediately prepare a
  report that sets out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the referendum was held;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of eligible voters;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number of eligible voters who voted;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number of votes in favour of the specific
    agreement;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the number of votes against the specific agreement;
    and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number of rejected ballots.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report shall be prepared in triplicate and be
  signed by the presiding officer and by the chief or a member of the
  council of the band.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The presiding officer shall submit a copy of the
  report to the chief or a member of the council of the band and to the
  Assistant Deputy Minister.

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Within seven days after a
referendum is held, an observer designated under subsection 4(2) shall
submit a written report to the Minister that sets out the information
referred to in paragraphs 24(1)(a) to (f).

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The Assistant Deputy
Minister shall inform the Attorney General of Ontario of the results of
the vote within 15 days after receipt of the report referred to in
section 24.

## [Approval Requirement for Confirmation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949665 .Part}

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Where a majority of all
eligible voters of a band vote in a referendum, a specific agreement is
confirmed if a majority of those who vote assent to it.

## [Subsequent Referendum]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949667 .Part}

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a majority of all eligible voters of a band did not vote in a
  referendum, the council of the band may, if the proposed specific
  agreement was assented to by a majority of those who voted, call
  another referendum to be held in accordance with these Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where under a referendum that is called under
  subsection (1) the proposed specific agreement is assented to by a
  majority of those who vote, the specific agreement is confirmed.

## [Appeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949671 .Part}

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who voted at a referendum may file an appeal if that person
  believes on reasonable grounds that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there was a contravention of these Regulations that
    may have affected the results of the referendum; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there occurred a corrupt practice in connection
    with the referendum.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An appeal under subsection (1) shall be filed by
  sending by registered mail to the Assistant Deputy Minister, within
  seven days after the date of the referendum, a notice of appeal
  stating the grounds and particulars of the appeal.

<!-- -->

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Not
  later than 21 days after receipt of a notice of appeal, the Assistant
  Deputy Minister shall send a copy of the notice of appeal by
  registered mail to the presiding officer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Not later than 10 days after receipt of a copy of a
  notice of appeal, the presiding officer shall send to the Assistant
  Deputy Minister by registered mail a statutory declaration that
  contains an answer to the particulars stated in the notice of appeal.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of the declaration provided by the
  presiding officer pursuant to subsection (2), the Assistant Deputy
  Minister shall forward the notice of appeal and the declaration to the
  Minister.

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, if the
material filed under subsections 29(2) and 30(2) is not sufficient to
decide the validity of the grounds of appeal, conduct a further
investigation.

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Minister is satisfied that the grounds for an appeal are
  established and that the result of the referendum was thereby
  affected, the Minister shall allow the appeal, direct that another
  referendum be conducted, and notify the council of the band of the
  decision.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister is of the opinion that the grounds
  for an appeal are not established or did not affect the results of the
  referendum, the Minister shall dismiss the appeal and notify the
  council of the band of the decision.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-232, s. 4(F)
:::

## [Forms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949687 .Part}

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
prescribe such forms as are required for the purposes of these
Regulations.
