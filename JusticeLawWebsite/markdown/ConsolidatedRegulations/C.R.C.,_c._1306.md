## Caribbean Development Bank Privileges and Immunities Order {#caribbean-development-bank-privileges-and-immunities-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1306
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Order Respecting the Privileges and Immunities of the Caribbean
Development Bank in Canada
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503173 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Caribbean Development Bank Privileges and Immunities
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1306).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503175 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations;
    ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Caribbean Development
    Bank. ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503179 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Organization shall have in Canada the legal capacities of a body
  corporate and shall, to such extent as it may require, have the
  privileges and immunities set forth in Articles II and III of the
  Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Representatives of states and governments that are
  members of the Organization shall, to such extent as may be required
  for the performance of their functions, have the privileges and
  immunities set forth in Article IV of the Convention for
  representatives of Members.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} All officials of the Organization in Canada shall, to
  such extent as may be required for the performance of their functions,
  have the privileges and immunities set forth in Article V of the
  Convention for officials of the United Nations.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} All experts performing missions for the Organization
  in Canada shall, to such extent as may be required for the performance
  of their functions, have the privileges and immunities set forth in
  Article VI of the Convention for experts on missions for the United
  Nations.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Order
exempts a person who is a Canadian citizen or a permanent resident of
Canada from liability for any taxes or duties imposed by any law in
Canada.
