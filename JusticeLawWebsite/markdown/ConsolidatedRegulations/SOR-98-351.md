## Federal Book Rebate (GST/HST) Regulations {#federal-book-rebate-gsthst-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-351
:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Registration 1998-06-18

Federal Book Rebate (GST/HST) Regulations

P.C. 1998-1141 1998-06-18
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph (f)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EJCAA} of the
definition [[specified person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection
259.1(1) and section 277[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EFCAA} of the [Excise
Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), hereby makes the annexed [Federal Book Rebate
(GST/HST) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-351).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EJCAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1997, c.
  10, s. 69.1(1)
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e-ID0EFCAA){#footnoteb_e}S.C. 1993, c.
  27, s. 125(1)
  :::
:::

## [Specified Persons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022676 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Any charity or qualifying
non-profit organization listed in the schedule is prescribed for the
purposes of paragraph (f) of the definition [[specified
person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 259.1(1) of the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022678 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
deemed to have come into force on October 24, 1996.
