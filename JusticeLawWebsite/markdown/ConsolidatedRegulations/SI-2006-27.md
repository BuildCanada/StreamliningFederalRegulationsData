## Order Transferring from the Minister of National Revenue to the Minister of Transport the Control and Supervision of the Royal Canadian Mint {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-national-revenue-to-the-minister-of-transport-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-royal-canadian-mint .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-27
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2006-02-22

Order Transferring from the Minister of National Revenue to the Minister
of Transport the Control and Supervision of the Royal Canadian Mint

P.C. 2006-66 2006-02-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDEAA} of the [Public
Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34),
hereby transfers from the Minister of National Revenue to the Minister
of Transport

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDEAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2003, c.
  22, s. 207
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the control and supervision of the Royal Canadian
  Mint, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of
  National Revenue under the [Royal Canadian Mint Act](/eng/acts/R-9),

effective February 6, 2006.
