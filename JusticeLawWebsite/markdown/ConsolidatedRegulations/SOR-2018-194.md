## Order Declaring that the Wastewater Systems Effluent Regulations Do Not Apply in Quebec {#order-declaring-that-the-wastewater-systems-effluent-regulations-do-not-apply-in-quebec .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2018-194
:::

[FISHERIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-14)

Registration 2018-10-01

Order Declaring that the Wastewater Systems Effluent Regulations Do Not
Apply in Quebec

P.C. 2018-1208 2018-09-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Minister of the Environment has caused to be published in
the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on March 21,
2015, a copy of the proposed [Order Declaring that the Wastewater
Systems Effluent Regulations Do Not Apply in
Quebec](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-194), substantially in the annexed
form, and persons were given an opportunity to file comments with
respect to the proposed Order;

And whereas on August 23, 2018, the Minister of the Environment and the
Government of Quebec entered into an agreement under section
4.1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-1960-E_hq_13359){#fn_81000-2-1960-E_hq_13359-ID0EHDBA}

to the effect that there are in force provisions under the laws of
Quebec that are equivalent in effect to provisions of the [Wastewater
Systems Effluent Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-139)[[Footnote

## [Declaration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-853023 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 2 and 3, [[effluent]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[final discharge
point]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[wastewater
system]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} have the same meanings as in section 1 of
the [Wastewater Systems Effluent
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-139).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- Regulations

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The [Wastewater Systems
Effluent Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-139) do not apply in
Quebec in respect of a wastewater system that would otherwise have been
subject to those regulations and that is subject to the Environment
Quality Act, CQLR c. Q-2, and

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Regulation respecting municipal wastewater
  treatment works, CQLR c. Q-2, r. 34.1; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a wastewater system that is owned or
  operated by the Government of Quebec, an authorization that is issued
  or amended under that Act on or after the day on which the agreement
  entitled [Accord Canada-Québec relatif aux lois et règlements
  applicables aux ouvrages municipaux et provinciaux d'assainissement
  des eaux usées au Québec]{.otherLang lang="fr"} comes into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- subsection 36(3) of
the Act

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 36(3) of the
[Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) does not apply in respect of any deposit
of effluent from the final discharge point of a wastewater system
referred to in section 2 if the effluent would otherwise have been
regulated under the [Wastewater Systems Effluent
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-139).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-853032 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
