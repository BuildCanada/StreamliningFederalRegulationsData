## Front End Wheel Loader Remission Order {#front-end-wheel-loader-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/80-176
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1980-11-12

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties and Sales Tax on
Certain Front End Wheel Loaders and Their Parts

P.C. 1980-2751 1980-10-16
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, the Minister of Industry, Trade and Commerce
and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 17 of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed Order respecting the remission of customs duty and sales tax on
certain front end wheel loaders and their parts.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-635892 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Front End Wheel Loader Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-80-176).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-635894 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Canadian value added]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian value added]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of front end wheel loaders produced
      in Canada, the aggregate of the following costs to the
      manufacturer of producing front end wheel loaders and the
      following allowances:

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the cost of parts produced in Canada and the
        cost of materials, to the extent that such materials are of
        Canadian origin, where the parts and materials are incorporated
        in the front end wheel loaders at the factory of the
        manufacturer in Canada, other than the cost of parts produced in
        Canada or the cost of materials, to the extent that such
        materials are of Canadian origin, where the parts and materials
        have been exported from Canada and subsequently imported into
        Canada as parts or materials,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} transportation costs, including insurance
        charges, incurred in transporting parts and materials from a
        Canadian supplier or frontier port of entry to the factory of
        the manufacturer in Canada for incorporation into the front end
        wheel loaders, to the extent that such costs are not included
        under subparagraph (i),

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that part of the following costs that are
        reasonably attributable to the production of the front end wheel
        loaders:

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} wages paid for direct production labour in
          Canada,

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} wages paid for indirect production and
          non-production labour in Canada,

        - [(C)]{.lawlabel} materials used in the production operation
          but not incorporated in the final product,

        - [(D)]{.lawlabel} light, heat, power and water,

        - [(E)]{.lawlabel} workmen's compensation, unemployment
          insurance and group insurance premiums, pension contributions
          and similar expenses incurred in respect of labour referred to
          in clauses (A) and (B),

        - [(F)]{.lawlabel} taxes on land and buildings in Canada,

        - [(G)]{.lawlabel} fire and other insurance premiums in respect
          of production inventories and the production plant and its
          equipment that are paid to a company authorized by law to
          carry on a business in Canada,

        - [(H)]{.lawlabel} rent for factory premises paid to a
          beneficial owner in Canada,

        - [(I)]{.lawlabel} maintenance and repairs carried out in Canada
          to buildings, machinery and equipment used for production
          purposes,

        - [(J)]{.lawlabel} tools, dies, jigs, fixtures and other similar
          plant equipment items of a non-permanent character that have
          been manufactured in Canada,

        - [(K)]{.lawlabel} engineering services, experimental work and
          product development work executed in Canada, and

        - [(L)]{.lawlabel} miscellaneous factory expenses,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} administrative and general expenses incurred
        in Canada that are reasonably attributable to the production of
        the front end wheel loaders,

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} depreciation in respect of machinery and
        permanent plant equipment, to the extent that such depreciation
        is reasonably attributable to the production of the front end
        wheel loaders, and

      - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a capital cost allowance of five per cent of
        the total capital outlay incurred by the manufacturer for land
        and buildings in Canada owned by the manufacturer and used by
        the manufacturer in the production of front end wheel loaders,
        to the extent that such allowance is reasonably attributable to
        the production of the front end wheel loaders, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of parts produced in Canada, the
      aggregate of those costs of producing the parts and those
      allowances that would be included in the calculation of Canadian
      value added if the parts were front end wheel loaders; ([valeur
      canadienne ajoutée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cost of materials]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cost of materials]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where they are acquired by a manufacturer from a
      related person, the cost to that person of the materials to the
      extent that they are of Canadian origin, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where they are acquired by a manufacturer from
      any person other than one described in paragraph (a), the price
      paid by the manufacturer for the materials minus the duty paid
      value of imported goods used in the production thereof and foreign
      charges applicable thereto; ([coûts des matières]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[cost of parts produced in Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cost of parts produced in Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where they are acquired by a manufacturer from a
      related person, the Canadian value added of the parts, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where they are acquired by a manufacturer from
      any person other than one described in paragraph (a), the price
      paid by the manufacturer for the parts minus the duty paid value
      of imported goods used in the production thereof and foreign
      charges applicable thereto; ([coût des pièces produites au
      Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cost of sales of a front end wheel loader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cost of sales of a front end wheel loader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where it is imported by a manufacturer, the price
      paid by the manufacturer for the front end wheel loader, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where it is manufactured in Canada by a
      manufacturer, the factory cost of production of the front end
      wheel loader; ([coût d'un chargeur à benne frontale sur pneus
      vendu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[depreciation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[depreciation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an allowance of ten per
    cent of the capital cost to the manufacturer of machinery and
    permanent plant equipment acquired within the term of one hundred
    and twenty months ending on the last day of a period referred to in
    subsection 4(1) less that part of the capital cost to the
    manufacturer in respect of machinery and permanent plant equipment
    that was disposed of before the beginning of that period;
    ([amortissement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[front end wheel loader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[front end wheel loader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, also variously known
    as [[front end loader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[wheel
    loader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[tractor loader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    or [[tractor shovel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, means a new self
    propelled vehicle, fitted or designed to be fitted with a front
    mounted bucket or shovel having a cubic capacity for general purpose
    use of up to and including 14 cu. yd. (10.70 m^3^), mounted on
    wheels, all wheels being driven, with steering being effected either
    by articulation of the frame of an articulated loader or by the
    movement of the steered wheels of a rigid frame loader, but does not
    include skid steer loaders, crawler mounted loaders, loaders mounted
    on industrial or agricultural two-wheel drive tractors and
    combination loader-backhoes; ([chargeur à benne frontale sur
    pneus]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a manufacturer of front
    end wheel loaders; ([fabricant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[net sales value of a front end wheel loader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[net sales value of a front end wheel loader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means an amount equal to

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the price received by the manufacturer for the
        front end wheel loader, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the costs of transporting the front end wheel
        loader within Canada but not including any other costs of
        transportation or delivery charges,

    less

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} federal sales and excise taxes and any
        applicable provincial taxes paid in respect of the front end
        wheel loader, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} rebates, commissions, discounts and other
        allowances granted by the manufacturer subsequent to the sale in
        respect of the front end wheel loader; ([valeur nette de vente
        d'un chargeur à benne frontale sur pneus]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

[[parts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[parts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means parts, accessories and parts
    thereof for use as original equipment in front end wheel loaders;
    ([pièces]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[related person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[related person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned to
    that expression by section 251 of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([personne liée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/85-59, s. 1(F)
:::

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of this Order,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} parts acquired by a manufacturer shall be deemed to
    be produced outside Canada, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} materials acquired by a manufacturer shall be
    deemed to be of non-Canadian origin

  unless for any such parts or materials acquired from a supplier in
  Canada, the manufacturer has obtained from the supplier a certificate
  in the form prescribed by the Minister of National Revenue, stating

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of parts, their cost determined
    pursuant to the definition [[cost of parts produced in
    Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of materials, their cost determined
    pursuant to the definition [[cost of materials]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    in section 2.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), iron, steel or
  aluminum that has been poured in Canada is deemed to be of Canadian
  origin.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-635949 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Order applies to a manufacturer who, in any twelve month period ending
  on July 31 after 1981, imports front end wheel loaders or parts and
  produces front end wheel loaders in Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Remission may be granted to a manufacturer referred
  to in subsection (1) in respect of a period described therein only
  where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ratio of the net sales value of front end wheel
    loaders produced by the manufacturer in Canada at any time and sold
    by him anywhere in that period to the net sales value of all front
    end wheel loaders sold by the manufacturer in that period for
    consumption in Canada is not less than 75 to 100; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (3), the sum of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian value added of front end wheel
      loaders produced in Canada by the manufacturer in that period,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian value added of parts produced in
      Canada at any time by the manufacturer and sold by him in that
      period, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian value added of parts produced in
      Canada at any time and acquired by or on behalf of the
      manufacturer in that period,

    is not less than 40 per cent of the cost of sales of all front end
    wheel loaders that the manufacturer sold for consumption in Canada
    in that period.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For any twelve month period ending on July 31 after
  1987, where a manufacturer fails to meet the 40 per cent requirement
  described in paragraph (2)(b) in order to be eligible for remission,
  the required percentage of Canadian value added is reduced by two
  percentage points for each 100 per cent increase of the dollar amount
  of Canadian value added in the production of front end wheel loaders
  by the manufacturer over and above the dollar amount of Canadian value
  added in the twelve month period ending July 31, 1983.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, in computing the
  net sales value of front end wheel loaders that were sold for
  consumption in Canada by a manufacturer in any twelve month period
  ending on July 31 after 1981, the manufacturer shall deduct an amount
  equal to the net sales value of front end wheel loaders that were
  imported and sold by the manufacturer in any such period

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that are exempt from customs duties under the
    [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that are subsequently exported from Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for which no claim for remission is made pursuant
    to section 7; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which the total customs duties under
    the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) are paid and for which
    there has been no remission.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, in computing the
  cost of sales of front end wheel loaders that were sold for
  consumption in Canada by a manufacturer in any twelve month period
  ending on July 31 after 1981, the manufacturer shall deduct an amount
  equal to the cost of sales of front end wheel loaders that were
  imported and sold by the manufacturer in any such period

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that are exempt from customs duties under the
    [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that are subsequently exported from Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for which no claim for remission is made pursuant
    to section 7; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which the total customs duties under
    the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) are paid and for which
    there has been no remission.

- SI/88-17, s. 2
- SI/89-5, s. 1
- SI/95-82, s. 1
:::

## [Remission of Customs Duties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-635972 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SI/88-17, s. 2(E)

\]
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 7, remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid
  or payable under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on the
  following goods imported or taken out of warehouse prior to January 1,
  1998 by the manufacturer or on the manufacturer's behalf:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} front end wheel loaders, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} parts, except pneumatic tires, tubes, buckets,
    digging teeth, cutting edges, cabs and roll-over protection
    structures

  that were or are imported or taken out of warehouse by a manufacturer
  for consumption or released after July 31, 1981.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, where a
  manufacturer, in any twelve month period ending on July 31 after 1987,
  fails to achieve the percentage of Canadian value added required under
  subsection 4(2) or (3), the remission granted under subsection (1)
  shall be reduced by two per cent for each percentage point that the
  manufacturer's Canadian value added is less than the Canadian value
  added required under subsection 4(3).

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-17, s. 2
- SI/89-5, s. 2
- SI/98-6, s. 12
:::

## [Remission of Sales Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-635983 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the sales tax paid or payable under the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on each front end wheel loader for which customs
duties are remitted by this Order in an amount equal to the difference
between the sales tax calculated on the duty-paid value of the front end
wheel loader and the sales tax calculated on the value for duty of the
front end wheel loader.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-17, s. 2(E)
:::

## [Claim for Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-635987 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No remission shall be
granted under this Order unless a claim therefor is made to the Minister
of National Revenue within three years of the date of importation.

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-635989 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a manufacturer has, by notice in writing to the
  Minister of National Revenue accompanied by the consent in writing of
  any related person, designated that person as a person associated with
  the manufacturer in the production of front end wheel loaders in
  Canada in a period ending on July 31 of the year specified in the
  notice, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the designation referred to in paragraph (a) has been
  approved by the Minister of Regional Industrial Expansion on or before
  a day not later than the 30th day after the commencement of the period
  referred to in that paragraph,

that person so designated shall, with respect to the importation of
front end wheel loaders and sale of front end wheel loaders, be deemed
for the purposes of this Order in that period not to be a separate
person but to be one and the same person as the manufacturer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/89-5, s. 3
:::

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-635996 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A manufacturer shall submit
to the Minister of National Revenue, as a condition of any remission
granted under section 5 or 6, such reports as may be required by that
Minister respecting the production and sales by the manufacturer of
front end wheel loaders.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/89-5, s. 4
:::
