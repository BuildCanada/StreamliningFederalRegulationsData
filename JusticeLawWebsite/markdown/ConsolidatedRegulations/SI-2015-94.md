## Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure, Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs as the Minister for the purposes of the Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-infrastructure-communities-and-intergovernmental-affairs-as-the-minister-for-the-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2015-94
:::

[CANADA STRATEGIC INFRASTRUCTURE FUND ACT](/eng/acts/C-10.3)

Registration 2015-11-18

Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure, Communities and
Intergovernmental Affairs as the Minister for the purposes of the Act

P.C. 2015-1235 2015-11-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Canada Strategic
Infrastructure Fund Act](/eng/acts/C-10.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_320_hq_10667){#fn_320_hq_10667-ID0EBDAA},
effective November 4, 2015,

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_320_hq_10667-ID0EBDAA){#fn_320_hq_10667}S.C.
  2002, c. 9, s. 47
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2014-376 of April 7,
  2014[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-829-e_hq_13569){#fn_81000-3-829-e_hq_13569-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-829-e_hq_13569-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_81000-3-829-e_hq_13569}SI/2014-41
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of Infrastructure,
  Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs, a member of the Queen's
  Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of the
  [Canada Strategic Infrastructure Fund Act](/eng/acts/C-10.3)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_320_hq_10667){#fn_320_hq_10667-ID0EBAAAA}.
