March 10, 1999 and laid before the Senate on March 11, 1999;

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027741 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Small Business
      Financing Act](/eng/acts/C-10.2). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who carries on or
      is about to carry on a small business to whom a loan has been made
      under the Act. It does not include Her Majesty or an agent of Her
      Majesty in right of Canada or a province, a municipality or a
      municipal or other public body that performs a function of
      government. ([emprunteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[conventional loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[conventional loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a loan that is not
      subject to the Act. ([prêt ordinaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means equipment that is used or
      to be used in the course of carrying on a small business, and
      includes computer software, any ship, boat or other vessel used or
      to be used in navigation and water supply systems. It does not
      include inventory of the small business except inventory that is
      leased by the borrower to the borrower's customers.
      ([matériel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[going concern]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[going concern]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a small business that
      has carried on operations at any time within 60 days prior to
      purchase or, in the case of a small business that operates on a
      seasonal basis, during the season prior to purchase. ([entreprise
      en exploitation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[health care industry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[health care industry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} refers to a small
      business classified under the heading Major Group 86 -- Health and
      Social Service Industries of the Standard Industrial
      Classification - Establishments (SIC-E) 1980 published by
      Statistics Canada. ([industrie des soins
      médicaux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[hospitality industry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[hospitality industry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} refers to a small
      business classified under the heading Major Group 91 --
      Accommodation Service Industries of the Standard Industrial
      Classification - Establishments (SIC-E) 1980 published by
      Statistics Canada.  ([industrie hôtelière]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[improvement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[improvement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes construction,
      renovation and modernization and, with respect to equipment,
      installation. ([amélioration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[intangible asset]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[intangible asset]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a non-monetary
      asset without physical substance that can be sold, transferred,
      licensed, rented or exchanged or that arises from a contractual or
      other legal right. ([bien incorporel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any document
      described in section 10. ([contrat de prêt]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[loan term]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[loan term]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period set out in a
      loan agreement for repayment of the total amount of the loan.
      ([durée du prêt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[mini-storage industry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mini-storage industry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} refers to a small
      business classified under the heading 479 -- Other Storage and
      Warehousing Industries of the Standard Industrial Classification -
      Establishments (SIC-E) 1980 published by Statistics
      Canada. ([industrie du mini-entreposage]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[responsible officer of the lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[responsible officer of the
      lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2009-102, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[working capital costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[working capital costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means costs to fund
      the day-to-day operating expenses of a business. ([frais liés au
      fonds de roulement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Whether persons are at arm's length from each other
  must, for the purposes of these Regulations, be determined in
  accordance with the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a loan is
  considered to have been made on the day on which the first
  disbursement of funds is made by the lender.

- **[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsections 4(3) and 7(2) of the Act, borrowers are
  related when one borrower

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} controls, directly or indirectly in any manner, the
    other borrower;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is controlled, directly or indirectly in any
    manner, by the same person or group of persons as the other
    borrower;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} carries on their small business in partnership with
    the other borrower, which carries on another small business; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} shares management services, administrative
    services, equipment, facilities or overhead expenses of the business
    with the other borrower, but is not in partnership with that
    borrower.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2022-157, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1),
  [[control]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to hold shares of a corporation
  to which are attached more than 50% of the votes that are necessary to
  elect a majority of its directors.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), borrowers whose businesses
  are located at different premises are not related if neither borrower
  derives more than 25% of their actual or projected gross revenues from
  the other.

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027805 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  registration fee is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a loan referred to in any of
    paragraphs 5(1)(a) to (d), 2% of the amount of the loan; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a loan referred to in paragraph
    5(1)(e), 2% of the authorized amount of the line of credit.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} If the borrower and the lender renew a loan
  referred to in paragraph 5(1)(e) within five years after the day on
  which the line of credit is opened, the lender must pay an additional
  registration fee of 2% of the renewed authorized amount.

- [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} If the borrower and the lender agree to an increase
  in the authorized amount of a loan referred to in paragraph 5(1)(e),
  the lender must pay a registration fee of 2% of the increase in the
  authorized amount.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The annual administration fee is payable quarterly
  within two months after the end of each quarter and is calculated at
  the annual rate of 1.25% applied

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a loan referred to in any of
    paragraphs 5(1)(a) to (d), to the end-of-month balances of the loan
    during the year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a loan referred to in paragraph
    5(1)(e), to the daily outstanding amount for the line of credit for
    each month during the year.

- [(3) to (6)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2009-102, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} With each payment made under subsection (2), the
  lender must submit a statement that substantiates the basis on which
  the payment was calculated.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (7), if the lender is
  unable to provide the statements required by that subsection in
  respect of a year, the Minister must notify the lender

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that for that year, the lender may make the
    payments under subsection (2), except the payment for the last
    quarter of the year, on the basis of estimates of the amounts
    payable; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the lender must submit for that year a
    statement under subsection (9) rather than the statements required
    by subsection (7).

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} On or before June 1 following a year in respect of
  which a lender makes payments under subsection (8), the lender must
  pay any deficiency for the year and provide a statement that indicates
  the basis on which the amount of the annual administration fee for the
  year was calculated.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} On application by a lender, made within one year
  after the day on which a loan referred to in any of paragraphs 5(1)(a)
  to (d) is made, the Minister must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the lender has disbursed less than the full
    amount of the loan registered, refund to the lender that portion of
    the registration fee that is attributable to the portion of the loan
    that was not disbursed and subtract the amount of the undisbursed
    portion from the amount of the loan registered; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the lender determines that the loan is not in
    compliance with the requirements of the Act and these Regulations,
    refund to the lender the registration fee and the annual
    administration fee and delete the entire amount of the loan
    registered.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} On application by a lender, made within one year
  after the day on which a loan referred to in paragraph 5(1)(e) is
  opened, the Minister must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the lender has made available less than the
    amount of the loan registered, refund to the lender that portion of
    the registration fee that is attributable to the portion of the loan
    that was not made available and subtract the amount of the loan that
    was not made available from the amount of the loan registered; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the lender determines that the loan is not in
    compliance with the requirements of the Act and these Regulations,
    refund to the lender the registration fee and the annual
    administration fee and delete the entire authorized amount of the
    loan registered.

## [Loan Classes and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027820 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A loan
  must fall within one of the following prescribed classes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} loans to finance the purchase or improvement of
    real property or immovables of which the borrower is or will become
    the owner, if the purchase or improvement is necessary for the
    operation of the borrower's small business;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} loans to finance the purchase of leasehold
    improvements to real property or immovables of which the borrower is
    or will become the tenant or the improvement of such real property
    or immovables, if the purchase or improvement is necessary for the
    operation of the borrower's small business;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} loans to finance the purchase or improvement of
    equipment necessary for the operation of the borrower's small
    business;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} loans to finance the purchase of intangible assets
    and working capital costs;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} lines of credit for working capital costs; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} loans to finance the payment by the borrower of
    registration fees payable in respect of a loan referred to in any of
    paragraphs (a) to (e).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A loan referred to in paragraph (1)(a) may not be
  made for the purchase of real property or immovables unless, at the
  time the loan is approved by the lender,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least 50% of the area of the real property or
    immovables is used for the operation of the small business or is
    intended to be so used within 90 days after the final disbursement
    under the loan agreement; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that portion of the area is not intended to be used
    within three years after the day on which the loan is made for

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} resale, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} leasing or subleasing, except in the case of a
      small business in the health care industry, hospitality industry
      or mini-storage industry.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A loan referred to in paragraph (1)(a) for the
  purchase or improvement of real property or immovables may include the
  cost of decontamination of real property or immovables if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the decontamination is required under a federal or
    provincial law, and the decontamination plan is disclosed to the
    lender on or before the day on which the loan is made; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2024-64, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A loan referred to in paragraph (1)(b) may not be
  made if the real property or immovables are intended to be used within
  three years after the day on which the loan is made for subleasing
  except in the case of a small business in the health care industry,
  hospitality industry or mini-storage industry.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The cost of purchasing or improving the equipment,
  real property, immovables or leasehold improvements financed by a loan
  referred to in any of paragraphs (1)(a) to (c) must not include the
  cost of labour provided by the borrower or the borrower's employees
  but may include the cost of labour provided by any subcontractor.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A loan referred to in any of paragraphs (1)(a) to (d)
  may not be used to finance the payment of any refundable taxes.

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} arose more than 365 days before

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a loan referred to in paragraph

    borrower of registration fees payable in respect of a loan referred
    to in paragraph 5(1)(e), five years beginning on the day on which
    the line of credit is opened.

## [Criteria for Eligibility]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1370916 .Part}

**[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 4(2)(e) of the Act, a borrower is eligible for a loan on
application to a lender if

## [Terms of the Loan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027872 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On or
  before the day on which a loan referred to in any of paragraphs
  5(1)(a) to (d) is made, the lender and the borrower must sign a
  document that sets out the principal amount of the loan, the rate of
  interest payable, the repayment terms, the frequency of payments of
  principal and interest and the day on which the first payment of
  principal and interest is due.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} On or before the day on which a loan referred to in
  paragraph 5(1)(e) is opened, the lender and the borrower must sign a
  document that sets out the authorized amount of the line of credit,
  the rate of interest payable, the repayment terms and, if applicable,
  the frequency of payments and the day on which the first payment is
  due.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The lender and the borrower may, at any time, agree
  to amend the terms of the loan or, at the end of a loan term, to renew
  the loan, to an aggregate maximum term of 15 years for a loan referred
  to in any of paragraphs 5(1)(a) to (d), beginning on the day on which
  the first payment of principal and interest is due.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} On or before the day on which a loan is renewed or
  its terms are amended, the lender and borrower must sign a document
  that sets out the terms of the renewal or amendment.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the terms described in
  subsections (1) and (3) may be set out in more than one document, as
  long as each document is signed by the lender and the borrower.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The repayment terms must provide that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the loan is payable by instalments;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at least one instalment of principal and interest
    is payable annually; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the first instalment of principal and interest is
    payable no later than one year after the day on which the loan is
    made.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Before the end of the five year period that begins
  the day after a loan referred to in paragraph 5(1)(e) is opened, the
  lender and the borrower may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} submit a new registration under section 2 for an
    additional five year term, along with the registration fee under
    subsection 4(1.1), if the additional five year term begins within
    five years after the day on which the line of credit is opened;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} convert the line of credit to a loan that meets the
    requirements of subsections (1) and (3) to (5), with a maximum loan
    term of 10 years; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} enter into an agreement to repay the balance of the
    line of credit with a conventional loan.

- [(6.1)]{.lawlabel} Before the end of the additional five year term
  referred to in paragraph (6)(a), the lender and the borrower may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} convert the line of credit to a loan that meets the
    requirements of subsections (1) and (3) to (5), with a maximum loan
    term of 10 years; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into an agreement to repay the balance of the
    line of credit with a conventional loan.

## [Interest Rate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027885 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

## [Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027904 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  lender must, when making a loan referred to in paragraph 5(1)(a) or
  (c), take valid and enforceable first-ranking security in the assets
  of the small business whose purchase or improvement is to be financed
  by the loan.

  small business is to be financed by a loan and another source of
  financing, the security taken by the lender in those assets must be
  equal in rank to that taken in those assets in relation to the other
  source of financing.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a loan referred to in paragraph
  5(1)(b), (d) or (e), or a loan referred to in paragraph 5(1)(c) for
  the financing of computer software, the lender must take security in
  any assets of the small business in respect of which the loan is made.

  loan is made or opened, the lender makes or opens one or more
  conventional loans to the same borrower to finance a purchase or
  improvement that would be eligible for a loan, the lender

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} must, in addition to any security otherwise
    required by this section to be taken, take security in the same
    assets and equal in rank to that taken to secure the conventional
    loan or loans; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may take security to secure the conventional loan
    or loans on the same assets and equal in rank to that taken to
    secure the loan.

  rank, but if the existing security is the result of the application of
  a provision respecting subsequently acquired property, the lender must
  obtain all necessary postponements to ensure that the security in the

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  undertaking any of the following actions, the lender must notify the

  the day on which it is to take effect:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a lender amalgamates with another lender;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a lender acquires the lending business of another
    lender;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a lender discontinues its commercial lending
    business and sells all of its outstanding loans to another lender;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a lender closes a branch and sells that branch's
    outstanding loans to another lender.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When an action set out in paragraph (1)(a) takes
  effect, the Minister's liability under the Act in respect of losses
  sustained by the amalgamating lenders as a result of loans made by

  result of those loans and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the loans made by the amalgamating lenders are
    considered to have been made by the new lender;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount already paid by the Minister in respect
    of those loans to the amalgamating lenders as a result of the
    Minister's liability under subsection 6(2) of the Act is considered
    to have been paid to the new lender; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if, as a result of the amalgamation, the amount
    already paid by the Minister to the amalgamating lenders as a result
    of the Minister's liability under subsection 6(2) of the Act is
    greater than the Minister's liability with respect to the new
    lender, the Minister's liability is considered to be equal to the
    amount already paid.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When an action set out in any of paragraphs (1)(b)
  to (d) takes effect, the Minister's liability under the Act continues
  in respect of losses sustained by the transferee lender as a result of
  those loans and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister's liability under the Act in respect
    of losses sustained by the transferor as a result of loans made by
    them continues in respect of losses sustained by the transferee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the loans made by the transferor are considered to
    have been made by the transferee;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount already paid by the Minister in respect
    of those loans to the transferor as a result of the Minister's
    liability under subsection 6(2) of the Act is considered to have
    been paid to the transferee; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if, as a result of the transfer, the amount already

    the Minister's liability under subsection 6(2) of the Act is greater
    than the Minister's liability with respect to the transferee, the
    Minister's liability is considered to be equal to the amount already

## [Claims Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028116 .Part}

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  lender must take all of the measures described in subsection 37(3)
  that are applicable before submitting a claim to the Minister for loss
  sustained as a result of a loan.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Regardless of the nature of the default, a lender
  must submit a claim for loss within 60 months after the day on which
  the last payment on the loan is received or, if the borrower has made
  no payment on the loan, 60 months after the day on which the first
  payment is scheduled.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is authorized to extend the 60-month
  period if the lender requests the extension before that period
  expires.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A claim for loss must be certified by the lender and
  be accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a loan referred to in any of
    paragraphs 5(1)(a) to (d), documentation that substantiates

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the cost and proof of payment of the purchase or
      improvement that was financed by the loan in an amount equal to or
      greater than 75% of the principal outstanding on the loan, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount disbursed by the lender under the
      loan agreement;

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a loan referred to in paragraph
    5(1)(e),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an attestation signed by the borrower stating
      that

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the line of credit will only be used to pay for
        working capital costs, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the working capital costs paid through the line
        of credit were not incurred more than 365 days before the line
        of credit was authorized, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} documentation that substantiates the amount
      provided by the lender under the loan agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the loan record; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the loan approval and administration file, if
    requested by the Minister.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A claim for loss must include the lender's
  acknowledgement that it has acted with due diligence in applying the
  procedures referred to in section 8 and has taken the measures
  described in subsection 37(3).

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A claim for loss must include all documents that
  evidence the security taken by the lender in respect of the loan and
  all guarantees and suretyships taken by the lender in respect of the
  loan.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} A loss sustained by a lender in respect of a loan
  must be calculated by determining the aggregate of the following
  amounts and deducting from that aggregate amount the proceeds realized
  from the taking of any measures described in subsection 37(3) and any
  overcharges referred to in paragraph 27(2)(b) that have not been
  reimbursed to the borrower:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of principal outstanding on the loan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of interest due and not paid pursuant to
    the loan agreement, calculated in accordance with subsection (8);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} uncollected taxed costs for, or incidental to, any
    legal proceedings in respect of the loan; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} legal fees and disbursements, other than the costs
    referred to in paragraph (c), and other costs incurred by the lender
    for services rendered to it by persons other than its employees, for
    the purpose of collecting, or attempting to collect, the loan from
    the borrower or the guarantor or surety.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The amount of interest referred to in paragraph
  (7)(b) is calculated as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the period beginning on the day after
    the last day on which interest is current and ending on the day of
    the first scheduled payment date after that day, at the rate of
    interest in effect under the loan agreement on the last day on which
    interest is current,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the 12-month period immediately
    following the period referred to in paragraph (a), at the rate of
    interest in effect under the loan agreement on the first day of the
    12-month period,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the 12-month period immediately
    following the period referred to in paragraph (b), at a rate of
    interest equal to one half of the rate of interest referred to in
    that paragraph, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any period following the period
    referred to in paragraph (c), at a rate of interest of 0%.

## [Additional Claims Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028141 .Part}

- **[[38.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the Minister has paid the lender the amount of any loss, calculated in
  accordance with subsection 38(7), the lender may, within the
  applicable period set out below, submit an additional claim for part
  of the previously unclaimed loss if the failure to respect the
  deadline for claiming that part of the loss was inadvertent:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the lender has notified the
    Minister of recovery of 100% of the compromise settlement, guarantee
    or suretyship under subsection 39(4), within 12 months after the day
    of notification;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the lender has submitted a final
    claim under subsection 39(5) after an interim claim, within
    12 months after the day on which the claim was made final; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, within 12 months after the date
    of expiry of the period specified in subsection 38(2) or (3), as the
    case may be.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An additional claim for part of the loss arising from
  any amount paid as a result of a claim submitted under a deemed trust
  by the Canada Revenue Agency or by any provincial department of
  revenue may be submitted after the period specified in subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An additional claim for part of the loss must be
  certified by the lender and be accompanied

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an additional claim submitted under
    subsection (1) in respect of a loan referred to in any of paragraphs
    5(1)(a) to (d), by documentation that substantiates

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the cost and proof of payment of the purchase or
      improvement that was financed by the loan in an amount equal to or
      greater than 75% of the principal outstanding on the loan, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if appropriate, the costs referred to in
      paragraphs 38(7)(c) and (d) that were not previously claimed;

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an additional claim submitted
    under subsection (1) in respect of a loan referred to in paragraph
    5(1)(e),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by an attestation signed by the borrower stating
      that

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the line of credit will only be used to pay for
        working capital costs, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the working capital costs paid through the line
        of credit were not incurred more than 365 days before the line
        of credit was authorized, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if appropriate, by documentation that
      substantiates the costs referred to in paragraphs 38(7)(c) and (d)
      that were not previously claimed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an additional claim submitted under
    subsection (2), by documentation that substantiates

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount paid as a result of a claim submitted
      under a deemed trust by the Canada Revenue Agency or by any
      provincial department of revenue, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if appropriate, the costs referred to in
      paragraphs 38(7)(c) and (d) that were not previously claimed.

## [Interim Claims Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028157 .Part}

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  lender may make an interim claim to the Minister in accordance with
  this section for loss sustained as a result of a loan where the lender
  has taken all of the measures described in subsection 37(3) that are
  applicable and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 37(3)(b) applies but the guarantee or
    suretyship has not been fully realized; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 37(3)(d) applies but the compromise
    settlement has not been fully implemented.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must pay the interim claim as if the
  lender had fully implemented the compromise settlement or fully
  realized the guarantee or suretyship at the time the interim claim is
  submitted.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 38(2) to (8) apply, with any
  modifications that the circumstances require, in respect of the
  submission of an interim claim.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, after the interim claim is paid, the lender, by
  fully implementing the compromise settlement or fully realizing the
  guarantee or suretyship, recovers 100% of the compromise settlement,
  guarantee or suretyship, the lender must so notify the Minister and
  the interim claim is deemed to be a final claim.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If, after the interim claim is paid, the lender, by
  fully implementing the compromise settlement or fully realizing the
  guarantee or suretyship, recovers less than 100% of the compromise
  settlement, guarantee or suretyship, the lender may submit a final
  claim under section 38 for the difference.

## [Subrogation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028168 .Part}

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  the Minister pays a lender for a loss sustained by it as a result of a
  loan, Her Majesty is subrogated from the payment of the final claim
  for the loss to the rights of the lender up to the amount paid by the
  Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, after the Minister makes the payment, additional
  proceeds are realized from the taking of any measures by the lender
  described in subsection 37(3), the Minister must be paid an amount
  equal to 85% of the proceeds and the lender must be paid an amount
  equal to 15%.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The payment made to the Minister under subsection (2)
  is to be taken into account in determining the losses sustained by the
  lender on loans made after March 31, 2009 when calculating the
  Minister's limit of liability with respect to the lender under
  subsection 6(2) of the Act.
