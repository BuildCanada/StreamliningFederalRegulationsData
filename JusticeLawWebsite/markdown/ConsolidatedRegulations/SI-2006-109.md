## Order Respecting Ex-Gratia Payments to Chinese Head Tax Payers {#order-respecting-ex-gratia-payments-to-chinese-head-tax-payers .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-109
:::

Registration 2006-08-23

Order Respecting Ex-Gratia Payments to Chinese Head Tax Payers

P.C. 2006-767 2006-08-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Canadian Heritage and the Treasury Board, hereby makes
the annexed Order Respecting Ex-Gratia Payments to Chinese Head Tax
Payers.
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612197 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Canadian
Heritage.

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612199 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is hereby
authorized, on application under subsection 3(1), to make an
[ex-gratia]{.otherLang lang="la"} payment of \$20,000 to any person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} who paid the head tax or on whose behalf the head tax
  was paid under An Act to Restrict and Regulate Chinese Immigration
  into Canada, S.C. 1885, c. 71, and any subsequent amendment to that
  Act or under An Act Respecting the Immigration of Chinese Persons,
  S.N. 1906 (6 Ed. VII), c. 2 of Newfoundland, and any subsequent
  amendment to that Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} who is a Canadian citizen or a permanent resident of
  Canada or, in the opinion of the Minister, has a long-standing
  connection to Canada; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} who was alive on February 6, 2006.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612204 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), an application for an [ex-gratia]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} payment referred to in this Order shall be made to the
  Minister on or before March 31, 2008, shall be in the form approved by
  the Minister and shall be supported by such evidence as he or she
  deems necessary.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an application is submitted after March 31,
  2008, the application may be accepted by the Minister if he or she is
  satisfied that the applicant was unable to submit the application
  before that date because of facts or circumstances beyond the control
  of the applicant.

## [Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612208 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Each payment shall be a
one-time lump sum payment.

## [No Crown Liability]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612210 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Payments made under this
Order shall not be construed as an admission of liability on the part of
the Crown.
