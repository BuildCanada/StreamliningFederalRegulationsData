## Order Transferring to the Department of Employment and Immigration the Control and Supervision of Certain Portions of the Public Service and Transferring to the Minister of Employment and Immigration the Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister of Labour and Amalgamating and Combining the Department of Employment and Immigration and the Department of Labour Under the Minister of Employment and Immigration {#order-transferring-to-the-department-of-employment-and-immigration-the-control-and-supervision-of-certain-portions-of-the-public-service-and-transferring-to-the-minister-of-employment-and-immigration-the-powers-duties-and-functions-of-the-minister-of-labour-and-amalgamating-and-combining-the-department-of-employment-and-immigration-and-the-department-of-labour-under-the-minister-of-employment-and-immigration .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-142
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1993-07-14

Order Transferring to the Department of Employment and Immigration the
Control and Supervision of Certain Portions of the Public Service and
Transferring to the Minister of Employment and Immigration the Powers,
Duties and Functions of the Minister of Labour and Amalgamating and
Combining the Department of Employment and Immigration and the
Department of Labour Under the Minister of Employment and Immigration

P.C. 1993-1488  1993-06-25
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
  Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), to transfer

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the Department of Employment and Immigration the
    control and supervision of

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} those portions of the public service in the
      Department of the Secretary of State of Canada known as the
      Student Assistance Branch, the Office of the Assistant
      Under-Secretary of State (Social Development and Regional
      Operations) other than the portion thereof relating to translation
      and related services and other than the Native Citizens
      Directorate, and the Education Support Branch other than the
      Canadian Studies and Special Projects Directorate and other than
      the Youth Participation Directorate, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} those portions of the public service in the
      Department of National Health and Welfare known as the Income
      Security Programs Branch and the Social Service Programs Branch
      other than New Horizons/Seniors Independence Programs, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to the Minister of Employment and Immigration all
    the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Labour under any
    Act of Parliament; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(b) of that Act, to amalgamate
  and combine the Department of Employment and Immigration and the
  Department of Labour under the Minister of Employment and Immigration
  and under the Deputy Minister of Employment and Immigration.
