## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-674673 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a company,
    means an entity other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a joint venture;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a financial institution;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a bank holding company;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an insurance holding company;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary of a financial institution, other
      than where the subsidiary is a subsidiary of a financial
      institution only because it is a subsidiary of the company; or

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary of a bank holding company or
      insurance holding company. ([entité désignée]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an insurance company
    incorporated by or under the laws of Canada or a province. ([société
    d'assurances]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[joint venture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[joint venture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a real property entity

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that was formed by a company, or by a designated
      entity controlled by a company, and one or more other persons for
      the purpose of a specific business undertaking;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in which the company or designated entity has a
      substantial investment; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which the persons who formed it
      have agreed on joint control, regardless of the distribution of
      their equity. ([coentre­prise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[minority interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minority interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an equity interest,
    in an entity that is controlled by a company, that is held by a
    person other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity controlled by the
      company. ([participation minoritaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[real property entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[real property entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity that is
    primarily engaged in holding, managing or otherwise dealing with

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} real property;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shares of a body corporate that is primarily
      engaged in holding, managing or otherwise dealing with real
      property, including shares of a body corporate that is another
      real property entity; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ownership interests in an unincorporated entity
      that is primarily engaged in holding, managing or otherwise
      dealing with real property, including ownership interests in an
      unincorporated entity that is another real property
      entity. ([entité immobilière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[related real property entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[related real property entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of
    a company, means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity, other than a designated
      entity controlled by the company, in which the company or a
      designated entity controlled by the company beneficially owns
      sufficient shares or ownership interests to cause the company or
      designated entity to have a substantial investment in the real
      property entity; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity that is controlled by a
      real property entity described in paragraph (a). ([entité
      immobilière apparentée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[securities dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[securities dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity described
    in paragraph (g) of the definition [[financial
    institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the
    Act. ([négociant en valeurs mobilières]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[third party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[third party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a company, means
    a person other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a designated entity controlled by the company; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the
      company. ([tierce partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[total assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[total assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a company, at a
    particular time, means the amount determined by the formula

    ::: {#674705 .Definition}
    A - B

    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is the total of the assets that would be reported on the balance
        sheet of the company prepared as at that time in accordance with
        the accounting principles and specifications of the
        Superintendent referred to in subsection 331(4) of the Act; and

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is the total of all amounts each of which is an amount included
        in the value of A that is in respect of an asset of a subsidiary
        of the company, if that subsidiary is
        - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a financial institution,

        - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a bank holding company,

        - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an insurance holding company,

        - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary of a financial institution,
          other than where the subsidiary is a subsidiary of a financial
          institution only because it is a subsidiary of the company, or

        - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary of a bank holding company or an
          insurance holding company. ([actif total]{.DefinedTermLink
          lang="fr"})
    :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Book value of interest in real property

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 8 to 11, the book value of an asset that is an interest of a
company in real property, at a particular time, is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the asset is real property, the gross book value
  of the real property, less any accumulated depreciation on the real
  property, that would be reported on a balance sheet of the company
  prepared as at that time in accordance with the accounting principles
  and specifications of the Superintendent referred to in
  subsection 331(4) of the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the asset is a security or loan, the book value of
  the security or loan that would be reported on a balance sheet of the
  company prepared as at that time in accordance with the accounting
  principles and specifications of the Superintendent referred to in
  subsection 331(4) of the Act.

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-674718 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exempt companies

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Sections 506 to 508 of the
Act do not apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a mutual company having an aggregate of surplus and
  minority interests of five billion dollars or more; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a company, other than a mutual company, if it

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is widely held and has equity of five billion
    dollars or more, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has equity of five billion dollars or more and is
    controlled by

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a widely held bank holding company,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a widely held insurance holding company,

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} an eligible financial institution, as defined in
      section 370 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), other than a
      foreign bank as defined in section 2 of that Act, or

    - [(D)]{.lawlabel} a foreign bank, as defined in section 2 of the
      [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), that is widely held as defined in
      section 2.3 of that Act.

## [Prescribed Subsidiary]{.HTitleText1} {#h-674729 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed subsidiary

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 506 to 508 of the Act, a prescribed subsidiary of a company is
any subsidiary of the company, except where that subsidiary is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a financial institution;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a bank holding company;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an insurance holding company;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary of a financial institution, other than
  where the subsidiary is a subsidiary of a financial institution only
  because it is a subsidiary of the company; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary of a bank holding company or insurance
  holding company.

## [Amount Determined]{.HTitleText1} {#h-674737 .Part}

## [Valuation of Equity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-674804 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equity owned by company

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 507 and 508 of the Act, the value of the participating shares
and ownership interests referred to in those sections that are
beneficially owned by a company and its subsidiaries prescribed by
subsection 4 is equal to their book value as reported on the
consolidated balance sheet of the company.

## [Interests of a Company in Real Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-674807 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-674808 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition and value of interest in real
property

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of Part IX of the Act, other than subsection 502(3) of the
  Act, sections 8 to 12 define the interests of a company in real
  property and set out how those interests are to be valued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection 502(3) of the Act, the
  interests of a company in real property means real property referred
  to in paragraph 11(1)(a) and shares and ownership interests referred
  to in paragraph 11(1)(b) that become beneficially owned by the
  company, or by a designated entity controlled by the company, as a
  result of the realization of a security interest on a loan or debt
  obligation referred to in subsection 11(1).

### [Direct Interests --- Real Property and Debt Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-674814 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direct interests

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following are interests of a company in real property:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} real property beneficially owned by the company or
    by a designated entity controlled by the company; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a debt obligation, for which the company or a
    designated entity controlled by the company is liable, that was
    issued for the purpose of acquiring or improving real property
    referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of direct interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of a company in real
  property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of real property referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a), the book value of the real property; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a debt obligation referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b), any amount by which the book value of the debt
    obligation exceeds the book value of the real property referred to
    in that paragraph.

### [Indirect Interests --- Real Property, Shares and Ownership Interests]{.HTitleText2} {#h-674824 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain indirect interests

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following are interests of a company in real property:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at a particular time, real property

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that is beneficially owned by

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the company
        that is a joint venture, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} an entity in which an entity described in
        clause (A) has a substantial investment, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that would be reported on a balance sheet of the
      company prepared as at that time in accordance with the accounting
      principles and specifications of the Superintendent referred to in
      subsection 331(4) of the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shares and ownership interests beneficially owned
    by the company, or by a designated entity controlled by the company,
    in any related real property entity of the company other than a
    related real property entity referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of those interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of a company in real
  property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of real property referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a), the book value of the real property; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of shares and ownership interests
    referred to in paragraph (1)(b), the book value of the shares and
    ownership interests.

### [Indirect Interests --- Loans and Debt Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-674838 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain indirect interests

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following are interests of a company in real property:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a debt obligation issued by a related real property
    entity of the company and beneficially owned by the company or by a
    designated entity controlled by the company;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a loan made by the company, or by a designated
    entity controlled by the company, to a related real property entity
    of the company;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a loan made by the company, or by a designated
    entity controlled by the company, to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity in which a financial
      institution, bank holding company or insurance holding company
      controlled by the company has a substantial investment, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity that is controlled by a
      real property entity described in subparagraph (i);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a debt obligation issued by a real property entity
    described in subparagraph (c)(i) or (ii) and beneficially owned by
    the company or by a designated entity controlled by the company;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a debt obligation that is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} issued by a related real property entity of the
      company,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} beneficially owned by a third party, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} guaranteed by the company or by a designated
      entity controlled by the company; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a loan made by a third party to a related real
    property entity of the company and guaranteed by the company or by a
    designated entity controlled by the company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of those interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of a company in real
  property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a debt obligation referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a) or (d), the book value of the debt obligation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a loan referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b) or (c), the book value of the loan; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a guaranteed debt obligation referred
    to in paragraph (1)(e) or a guaranteed loan referred to in
    paragraph (1)(f),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligation is issued by, or the loan
      is made to, an entity that is a related real property entity of
      the company and the entity beneficially owns real property that is
      an interest of the company in real property under
      paragraph 9(1)(a), any amount by which the face value of the
      guarantee exceeds the value of the real property determined in
      accordance with paragraph 9(2)(a), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the face value of the
      guarantee.

### [Indirect Interests --- Secured Loans and Debt Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-674860 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain indirect interests

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  company or a designated entity controlled by a company makes a loan
  to, or beneficially owns or guarantees the debt obligation of, a third
  party, the loan or debt obligation is an interest of the company in
  real property if it is secured by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} real property beneficially owned by a third party
    in conjunction with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the company,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the designated entity,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the company,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a financial institution, bank holding company or
      insurance holding company controlled by the company,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} an entity controlled by a financial institution,
      bank holding company or insurance holding company referred to in
      subparagraph (iv), or

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity described in
      subparagraph 10(1)(c)(i) or (ii); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shares or ownership interests beneficially owned by
    a third party in

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an entity that beneficially owns real property in
      conjunction with the company, a related real property entity of
      the company or a designated entity controlled by the company, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of those assets

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of a company in real
  property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a loan or debt obligation that is
    secured by real property referred to in paragraph (1)(a), the lesser
    of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the net realizable value of the third party's
      interest in the real property at the time that the security
      interest was given, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

      ::: {#674879 .Subparagraph}
      A - B

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is
          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the loan was made by the company or a
            designated entity controlled by the company or the debt
            obligation is beneficially owned by the company or a
            designated entity controlled by the company, the book value
            of the loan or debt obligation, or

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligation is guaranteed by the
            company or a designated entity controlled by the company,
            the face value of the guarantee, and

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the total net realizable value of any other security
          interests that were given for the loan or debt obligation;
      :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a loan or debt obligation that is
    secured by shares or ownership interests beneficially owned by a
    third party in an entity referred to in subparagraph (1)(b)(i), the
    lesser of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the net realizable value of the third party's
      interest in those shares or ownership interests at the time that
      the security interest was given, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

      ::: {#674888 .Subparagraph}
      A - (B - (C × D/E))

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is
          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the loan was made by the company or a
            designated entity controlled by the company or the debt
            obligation is beneficially owned by the company or a
            designated entity controlled by the company, the book value
            of the loan or debt obligation, or

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligation is guaranteed by the
            company or a designated entity controlled by the company,
            the face value of the guarantee,

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the total net realizable value of all security interests
          that were given for the loan or debt obligation,

      [C]{.dfn}
      :   is the net realizable value of the entity's interest in the
          real property referred to in subparagraph (1)(b)(i),

      [D]{.dfn}
      :   is the value of the shares or ownership interests that are
          given as the security interest, and

      [E]{.dfn}
      :   is the total value of the outstanding shares or ownership
          interests in the entity; and
      :::

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a loan or debt obligation that is
    secured by shares or ownership interests beneficially owned by a
    third party in a related real property entity referred to in
    subparagraph (1)(b)(ii), the lesser of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the net realizable value of the third party's
      interest in those shares or ownership interests at the time that
      the security interest was given, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

      ::: {#674900 .Subparagraph}
      A - B

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is
          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the loan was made by the company or a
            designated entity controlled by the company or the debt
            obligation is beneficially owned by the company or a
            designated entity controlled by the company, the book value
            of the loan or debt obligation, or

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligation is guaranteed by the
            company or a designated entity controlled by the company,
            the face value of the guarantee, and

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the total net realizable value of any other security
          interests that were given for the loan or debt obligation.
      :::

### [Other Interests]{.HTitleText2} {#h-674906 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other interests

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following are interests of a company in real property:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a guarantee given by the company, or by a
    designated entity controlled by the company, to an entity other than
    the company or designated entity for the purpose of completing the
    development of real property that is beneficially owned by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the company,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a designated entity controlled by the company,
      or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the company;
      and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an agreement made by the company, or by a
    designated entity controlled by the company, to support a third
    party's cost of operating or financing real property that is
    beneficially owned by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the company,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a designated entity controlled by the company,
      or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of those interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of a company in real
  property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a guarantee referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a), the estimated cost of completing the development
    of the real property; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an agreement referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b), the amount of any funds advanced by the company,
    or by a designated entity controlled by the company, under the
    agreement.
