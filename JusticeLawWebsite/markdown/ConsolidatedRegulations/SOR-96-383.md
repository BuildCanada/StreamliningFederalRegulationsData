## Discontinuance and Continuance of Proceedings Order, 1996 {#discontinuance-and-continuance-of-proceedings-order-1996 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/96-383
:::

[CANADA TRANSPORTATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10.4)

Registration 1996-07-10

Discontinuance and Continuance of Proceedings Order, 1996

P.C. 1996-1137 1996-07-10
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to subsection 195(3) of the [Canada
Transportation Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Discontinuance and Continuance of Proceedings Order,
1996](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-383).

  :::
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985789 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies to
proceedings in respect of classes of matters in respect of which no
decision or order has been made by the National Transportation Agency
before July 1, 1996.

## [Discontinuance of Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985791 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Proceedings in respect of
the following classes of matters shall be discontinued:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reconsideration under section 171 of the National
  Transportation Act, 1987 of any branch line abandonment application;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an application under section 232 of the National
  Transportation Act, 1987 for a permit to construct and operate a
  commodity pipeline; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the review under section 256 of the National
  Transportation Act, 1987 of a proposed acquisition in a transportation
  undertaking.

## [Continuance of Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985796 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Proceedings in respect of the
  following classes of matters shall be continued in accordance with the
  provisions of the [Canada Transportation Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a complaint under section 35 of the National
    Transportation Act, 1987 in respect of a farm crossing referred to
    in section 215 of the Railway Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an exemption under section 70 of the National
    Transportation Act, 1987;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an application referred to in section 72 of the
    National Transportation Act, 1987 for a domestic licence;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the suspension or cancellation under section 75 of
    the National Transportation Act, 1987 of a domestic licence;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a complaint referred to in section 77 of the
    National Transportation Act, 1987 with respect to the failure of a
    licensee to comply with section 76 of that Act;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a complaint referred to in section 80 of the
    National Transportation Act, 1987 with respect to the imposition by
    a licensee of an unreasonable basic fare level or an unreasonable
    basic fare increase;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} an application under section 88 of the National
    Transportation Act, 1987 for a scheduled international licence;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} terms and conditions referred to in section 91 of
    the National Transportation Act, 1987;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the suspension or cancellation under section 92 of
    the National Transportation Act, 1987 of a scheduled international
    licence;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} an application under section 94 of the National
    Transportation Act, 1987 for a non-scheduled international licence;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} terms and conditions referred to in section 96 of
    the National Transportation Act, 1987;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the suspension or cancellation under section 97 of
    the National Transportation Act, 1987 of a non-scheduled
    international licence;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} liability insurance referred to in section 98 of
    the National Transportation Act, 1987;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} an application in accordance with sections 111 to
    132 of the Railway Act for the construction of a railway line,
    branch line or industrial spur;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} a proceeding referred to in sections 201 to 204 of
    the Railway Act; and

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} an application under section 326 of the Railway Act
    for leave to construct or maintain lines, wires, other conductors or
    other structures or appliances for telegraphic or telephonic
    purposes, or for the conveyance of power or electricity for other
    purposes.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Proceedings in respect of the following classes of
  matters shall be continued in accordance with the provisions of the
  Railway Act as it read immediately before July 1, 1996:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application or complaint under section 212 of
    the Railway Act in respect of drainage;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an approval under subsection 213(4) of the Railway
    Act of the character of works, specifications or plans in respect of
    drainage works;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an application for a farm crossing under
    section 216 of the Railway Act; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a proceeding under section 230 of the Railway Act.
