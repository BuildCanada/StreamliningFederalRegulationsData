## [PART 1]{.HLabel1} {#h-1395696 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1395697 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these By-laws.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [College of Patent Agents and
    Trademark Agents Act](/eng/acts/C-33.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[CEO]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CEO]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the individual appointed by the
    Board under section 23 of the Act. ([premier
    dirigeant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[CIPO]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CIPO]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Intellectual
    Property Office and includes the Patent Office and the Office of the
    Registrar of Trademarks. ([OPIC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[class 1 licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[class 1 licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a patent agent licence
    issued under subsection 26(1) of the Act or a trademark agent
    licence issued under subsection 29(1) of the Act that is not a class
    2 or class 4 licence. ([permis de catégorie 1]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[class 2 licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[class 2 licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a patent agent licence
    issued under subsection 26(1) of the Act or a trademark agent
    licence issued under subsection 29(1) of the Act that is subject to
    the restriction set out in section 47. ([permis de catégorie
    2]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[class 3 licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[class 3 licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a patent agent in
    training licence issued under subsection 26(2) of the Act or a
    trademark agent in training licence issued under subsection 29(2) of
    the Act. ([permis de catégorie 3]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[class 4 licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[class 4 licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a patent agent licence
    issued under subsection 26(1) of the Act or a trademark agent
    licence issued under subsection 29(1) of the Act, to an individual
    who is not practising as a patent agent or a trademark agent and who
    is not providing any services in that capacity to the public or in
    connection with their employment. ([permis de catégorie
    4]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Code of Professional Conduct
    for Licensees established under subsection 33(1) of the
    Act. ([Code]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[foreign practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foreign practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who
    is described in subsection 19(1) or 20(1) of the
    Regulations. ([praticien étranger]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[professional regulatory body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[professional regulatory body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a body
    that has a statutory duty to regulate a profession, other than under
    the Act. ([organisme de réglementation
    professionnelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Register]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Register]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Register of Patent
    Agents, referred to in section 28 of the Act, or the Register of
    Trademark Agents, referred to in section 31 of the
    Act. ([registre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [College of Patent
    Agents and Trademark Agents
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-129). ([Règlement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[supervisor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[supervisor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who is
    authorized under section 64 to act as the supervisor of a class 3
    licensee. ([superviseur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[incompetence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of the Act, [[incompetence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
  failure of a licensee to apply relevant knowledge, skills or judgment
  in a manner consistent with the standards of competence set out in the
  Code while practising as a patent agent or trademark agent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[professional
  misconduct]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the Act, [[professional
  misconduct]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means conduct by a licensee that is
  inconsistent with the standards of professional conduct set out in the
  Code and that would reasonably be regarded as unprofessional or that
  may discredit the profession and includes any of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contravening the Act, the Regulations, these
    By-laws or the Code;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} exhibiting behaviour while practising as a patent
    agent or trademark agent that tends to bring discredit to the
    profession or undermines public confidence in it;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} being convicted of or pleading guilty to an offence
    described in paragraph 73(d); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} being found by a professional regulatory body to
    have committed professional misconduct or to be incompetent,
    incapacitated or unfit to practise within the meaning of the statute
    under which the body made its finding.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1} {#h-1395722 .Part}

### [Organization of the College]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1395723 .Subheading}

#### [Fiscal Year]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1395724 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fiscal year

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The fiscal year of the
College begins on January 1 and ends on December 31 of the same calendar
year.

#### [Annual General Meeting]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1395727 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of meeting

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** At least 60 days before the
day on which the annual general meeting is to take place, the CEO must
give public notice of the meeting that sets out the date of the meeting,
the means of attendance and, if applicable, the location.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agenda

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The agenda for an annual
general meeting must include the following items:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the CEO's presentation of the College's annual report
  and audited financial statements;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Chairperson's address to the meeting;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} questions from the licensees to the Board; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any additional matter that the Board adds to the
  agenda.

#### [Board of Directors]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1395736 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of Board

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board has the following duties on behalf of the College:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on an annual basis, appoint an independent auditor
    to audit the accounts of the College and approve the audited
    financial statements;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} monitor the College's financial performance;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} designate any bank listed in Schedule I to the
    [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) as a bank for the College;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} ensure multi-year financial planning by approving
    the College's budgets in advance of each fiscal year;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} establish and publish an investment policy and
    appoint an investment dealer, on the recommendation of the CEO;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} approve terms of reference and a skills matrix for
    committees of the College;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with the Board's policies, consult
    with any working groups that the Board establishes to assist with
    its duties and functions; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the College's and CEO's actions are
    compliant with all legal requirements.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of Board --- CEO

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board has the following duties with respect to
  the CEO:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide direction to the work of the CEO by
    establishing performance expectations, providing regular performance
    reviews and undertaking succession planning;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} develop standards, principles and regulatory
    objectives;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} establish working groups to assist the CEO in
    carrying out the CEO's work;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} monitor the performance of the CEO and of the
    working groups; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} oversee the CEO's effectiveness with respect to
    engagement with members and with the public.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  College must pay to each director the remuneration set out in Schedule
  2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reimbursement of expenses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every director is entitled to the reimbursement of
  reasonable expenses that they incur in performing their duties and
  functions as directors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office of elected directors

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The term of office of each
elected director begins at the conclusion of the first annual general
meeting following their election and ends on the earlier of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day after the third anniversary of the day on
  which the term began, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the conclusion of the fourth annual general meeting
  following their election.

#### [Election of Directors]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1395764 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election process to be published

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Board must publish the
process for the election of directors on the College's website.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution of elected directors

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The number of directors
elected to the Board is to be divided evenly between patent agents and
trademark agents.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancies

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Each year, the CEO
determines if any elected director positions will be vacant at the
conclusion of the next annual general meeting. If any positions will be
vacant, an election must be held before that meeting to elect the
appropriate number of patent agents and trademark agents to satisfy the
requirements of section 10.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election Commissioner

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board must, on the recommendation of the CEO, appoint an Election
  Commissioner to address any issues that arise concerning the election
  process, including to rule on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any dispute concerning the ineligibility of a
    nominee; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other dispute that arises during the election
    period or regarding the election results.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No review by Board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A ruling of the Election Commissioner is not
  reviewable by the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligibility criteria

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraphs 14(f)(ii) and 17(h)(iii) of the Act, an individual is not
eligible to be an elected director if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the five years immediately before the day of the
  election, they have been found to have committed professional
  misconduct or to be incompetent by the Discipline Committee;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the five years immediately before the day of the
  election, they have been found by a professional regulatory body to
  have committed professional misconduct or to be incompetent within the
  meaning of the statute under which the body made its finding;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} they are the subject of an action taken by the
  Investigations Committee under subsection 37.1(1) of the Act;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} they are the subject of an application to the
  Discipline Committee by the Investigations Committee under subsection
  49(1) of the Act;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} they have already served as a director for a period
  of six years and, on the date of the election, less than two years
  have elapsed since the end of that period;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} they are employed by a
  [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} they have not provided the CEO with a declaration
  regarding conflicts of interest; or

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} they have not attended an orientation for prospective
  candidates for election.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** An individual must not be
appointed or elected or continue as a director if doing so would place
them in a conflict of interest.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confirmation of nominations

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  CEO must confirm the eligibility of every nominee to become a
  candidate for election as a director in accordance with the criteria
  set out in section 14 of the Act and sections 13 and 14 of these
  By-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disputes about eligibility

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The CEO must refer any dispute about the eligibility
  of a nominee to the Election Commissioner, who will make a decision
  regarding the nominee's eligibility on the basis of the CEO's reasons
  and a submission from the nominee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equal number of eligible nominees and
  positions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, at the end of the nominations period, the number
  of eligible nominees for election is less than or equal to the number
  of vacant director positions, the CEO is to declare those nominees
  elected and notify the nominees and the members accordingly.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of withdrawal by candidate

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A candidate who withdraws
from an election must give notice to the CEO in writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voting

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee whose licence is not suspended is eligible to vote in an
  election for directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic voting

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Voting during an election of directors is to be done
  by electronic means.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disputes concerning election results

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  candidate asserts that there are reasonable grounds to dispute the
  validity of the election results, the candidate may file a notice of
  dispute with the Election Commissioner that must include the
  procedural defect being alleged.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for filing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of dispute must be filed within five days
  after the day on which the election results are published on the
  College's website.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inquiry by Election Commissioner

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Election Commissioner must hold an inquiry if
  they find that the notice of dispute demonstrates reasonable grounds
  to doubt the validity of the election process.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report and recommendations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Election Commissioner must prepare a written
  report of their findings and recommendations and submit it to the
  Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration by Board

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may, after reviewing the Election Commissioner's report and
  recommendations, take either of the following actions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} declare the election result to be valid; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} declare the election result to be invalid and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} declare another candidate to have been elected,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} direct that another election be held.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minor procedural defect

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An election result is not invalid based solely on a
  minor procedural defect.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal --- elected directors

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  directors may, by resolution, remove an elected director from office
  for cause if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a motion is made to remove the director and at
    least two-thirds of the directors in attendance at a meeting vote in
    favour of the removal; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notice of the intention to make the motion is
    provided to the Board and to the director at least two weeks in
    advance of the vote on the motion.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal --- appointed directors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The directors may, by resolution, request that the
  Minister remove an appointed director from office for cause if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a motion is made to remove the director and at
    least two-thirds of the directors in attendance at a meeting vote in
    favour of the removal; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notice of the intention to make the motion is
    provided to the Board and to the director at least two weeks in
    advance of the vote on the motion.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligibility due to absences

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** An elected director who,
without a valid reason, is absent from two consecutive Board meetings
has met the ineligibility criteria for the purpose of subparagraph
17(h)(iii) of the Act.

#### [Board Meetings]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1395833 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Frequency of meetings

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board is to meet at least four times each year. The Board sets the
  location of the meeting or the electronic means to be used in order to
  hold the meeting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic means

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Board meeting held by electronic means must be
  conducted by any means that permits all persons who are participating
  in the meeting to communicate with each other simultaneously.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The quorum for a Board
meeting is five directors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules of order

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The Board must adopt
rules of order for the conduct of its meetings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of meeting

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At
  least seven days before a Board meeting, the CEO must publish on the
  College's website

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the agenda and any meeting materials that are not
    confidential; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any information that enables the public to
    participate in the meeting, including how to access the meeting if
    it is to be held by electronic means.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to publish notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Failure of the CEO to publish the notice does not
  invalidate the meeting.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agenda

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The agenda for a Board
meeting must relate only to the Board's work.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Simple majority

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 20
and 34, a motion or resolution tabled at a Board meeting is decided by a
majority vote of the directors who are in attendance at the meeting.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjournments

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The Chairperson may, with
the consent of a majority of directors who are in attendance at a
meeting, adjourn the meeting to a fixed time and place.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[In camera]{.otherLang lang="la"}
meetings

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may exclude the public from a Board meeting to discuss any
  matter if the Board is of the opinion that the harm of public
  disclosure of the matter under consideration outweighs the benefits of
  open discussion and attendance by the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[In camera]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  meetings --- exclusion of CEO

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may exclude the CEO from an [in
  camera]{.otherLang lang="la"} meeting

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to consider the CEO's appointment, reappointment,
    dismissal, performance or terms of office; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to provide an opportunity for the directors to
    discuss matters relevant only to directors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special Board meetings --- notice

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson, or any three directors, may call a special Board meeting
  to be held by electronic means by providing at least 24 hours' written
  notice to the CEO.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} As soon as feasible after receiving the notice, the
  CEO must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} advise the directors and the public of the reason
    for the meeting, the date and time when it will be held, and the
    electronic means for participating in the meeting; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} publish on the College's website the agenda and any
    meeting materials that are not confidential.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to publish

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Failure of the CEO to publish the notice does not
  invalidate the meeting.

#### [Officers]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1395872 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson and CEO

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson and the CEO are the officers of the College.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}CEO

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The CEO serves [ex officio]{.otherLang lang="la"} and
  does not have a vote at Board meetings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of Chairperson

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The Chairperson has the
following duties:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} carry out the College's business;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} act as the spokesperson for the Board; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} participate in the Board's decision-making.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election of Chairperson

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1} {#h-1395949 .Part}

### [Licensing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1395950 .Subheading}

#### [Applications]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1395951 .Subheading}

##### [Class 1 Licence]{.HTitleText4} {#h-1395952 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant for a class 1 licence must submit an application to the
  Registrar that contains the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their name, address, telephone number and email
    address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} proof that they are resident in Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} their complete professional employment history;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} proof that they successfully completed the
    applicable agent training program and the qualifying examinations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} proof that, as of the effective date of the
    licence, they will be insured against professional liability, as
    required by subsection 34(1) of the Act, or proof that they are
    exempt from that requirement and the reason for the exemption;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any information necessary to assist the Registrar
    in determining whether the applicant is of good character and fit to
    practise;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a declaration that the applicant will, when
    licensed, practise with integrity, uphold the independence of the
    profession and comply with the Code; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any other information that the Registrar requires
    to determine whether the applicant meets the requirements for the
    licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing and application fee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be submitted to the Registrar
  within six months after the day on which the College advises the
  applicant of their successful completion of the qualifying
  examinations and be accompanied by the fee set out in item 1 of
  Schedule 1, payable to the College.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence fee

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before the Registrar issues the licence, the licensee
  must pay to the College the fee set out in item 10 of Schedule 1,
  prorated to the number of months remaining in the year following the
  month in which the licence is issued.

##### [Class 2 Licence]{.HTitleText4} {#h-1395968 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  class 1 licensee who wishes to obtain a class 2 licence must submit an
  application to the Registrar that contains the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their name, address, telephone number and email
    address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} their complete professional employment history;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} proof that, as of the effective date of the
    licence, they will be insured against professional liability, as
    required by subsection 34(1) of the Act, or proof that they are
    exempt from that requirement and the reason for the exemption; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} confirmation of the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that they have completed all client matters or
      made arrangements to the satisfaction of their clients to have the
      clients' files returned to them or transferred to one or more
      licensees whose class of licence permits them to proceed with the
      file or files being transferred,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that they have assigned any matters in progress
      at CIPO to one or more licensees described in subparagraph (i) and
      advised the appropriate Office of CIPO in writing of the name of
      the successor licensee or licensees, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the location of any of their files that are not
      returned to the clients or transferred as described in
      subparagraph (i).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application fee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be accompanied by the fee set
  out in item 2 of Schedule 1, payable to the College.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Verification of information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar may seek independent verification from
  a successor licensee of the information submitted by a licensee under
  subparagraph (1)(d)(ii).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence fee

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Before the Registrar issues the licence, the licensee
  must pay to the College the fee set out in item 12 of Schedule 1,
  prorated to the number of months remaining in the year following the
  month in which the licence is issued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on class 2 licences

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** A class 2 licensee is not
entitled to represent persons in the presentation and prosecution of
applications for patents or for the registration of trademarks or in
other business before CIPO.

##### [Class 3 Licence]{.HTitleText4} {#h-1395987 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant for a class 3 licence must submit an application to the
  Registrar that contains the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their name, address, telephone number and email
    address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} proof that they are resident in Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} their complete professional employment history;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} proof that, as of the effective date of the
    licence, they will be insured against professional liability, as
    required by subsection 34(1) of the Act, or proof that they are
    exempt from that requirement and the reason for the exemption;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a criminal record check;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} records of any disciplinary sanction by an
    employer, academic institution, professional regulatory body, or any
    other body or association of which the applicant is or was a member;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} records of any bankruptcy or insolvency;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a declaration that the applicant

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has not been served with an application for
      bankruptcy, made an assignment of property for the benefit of
      creditors or presented a proposal in bankruptcy to creditors under
      the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has not had a judgment entered against them by a
      court,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is not personally subject to an order for
      costs,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} has not been charged with, pleaded guilty to or
      been found guilty of any offence under the [Criminal
      Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the [Controlled Drugs and Substances
      Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8), the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), the
      [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), a provincial act respecting
      securities, employment standards or official languages or any
      other provincial act that creates an offence that implicates an
      individual's integrity, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} has not had their professional licence restricted
      or suspended by a professional regulatory body, or been found by a
      professional regulatory body to have committed professional
      misconduct or to be incompetent within the meaning of the statute
      under which the body would make its finding;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a list of any degrees, diplomas and certificates
    that they have been awarded in or outside Canada and the names of
    the educational institutions that awarded them;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the name of every professional regulatory body
    under which the applicant is licensed to practise in or outside
    Canada;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} evidence that the applicant is proficient in one or
    both of the official languages;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a specimen signature, a current photograph and any
    other information necessary to confirm their identity;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} a copy of a training agreement executed by the
    applicant and their supervisor; and

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} any other information that the Registrar requires
    to determine whether the applicant meets the requirements for the
    licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application fee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be accompanied by the fee set
  out in item 3 of Schedule 1, payable to the College.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence fee

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before the Registrar issues the licence, the licensee
  must pay to the College the fee set out in item 14 of Schedule 1,
  prorated to the number of months remaining in the year following the
  month in which the licence is issued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions on licence

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** The following conditions
apply to a class 3 licence:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensee must comply with the terms of the
  training agreement; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licensee must not, without the supervision of a
  class 1 licensee and without a training agreement in effect, represent
  any person in the presentation or prosecution of an application for a
  patent or for the registration of a trademark or in any other business
  before CIPO.

### [Qualifying Examinations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1396052 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Process

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar must
publish on the College's website information about the qualifying
examinations that includes

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dates of the examinations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the registration process;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the examination fee set out in item 8 of Schedule 1;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the process for administering examinations;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the method of communication of the examination
  results; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statement that it is possible to request a
  re-marking of the examination.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination advisory group

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar may appoint
an advisory group composed of any combination of class 1 licensees,
class 2 licensees and representatives of CIPO to assist the Registrar in
preparing, administering and marking the qualifying examinations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Training program

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** A class 3 licensee may
write the qualifying examinations after they have completed a training
program approved by the Registrar.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifying examinations

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a class 3
  licensee must write the qualifying examinations at the first sitting
  of the examinations that follows the completion of their training
  program.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for delayed examinations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a licensee is not able to write the examinations
  as required by subsection (1) for a reason beyond their control, the
  Registrar may, on request of the licensee, allow the licensee to write
  the examinations at the next following sitting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination fees

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The licensee must pay to the College the examination
  fee set out in item 8 of Schedule 1 to write the qualifying
  examinations or any part of the examinations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-marking examinations

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee who receives less than a passing mark on the qualifying
  examinations may, on payment to the College of the fee set out in item
  9 of Schedule 1, request that their examinations or any part of their
  examinations be re-marked.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if the re-marking will
  not materially affect the outcome and result in a passing mark.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reimbursement of fee

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the re-marking of a licensee's examinations
  results in their receiving a passing mark, the fee for re-marking paid
  under subsection (1) must be reimbursed to the licensee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No review

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Re-marking results are not reviewable by the
  Registration Committee under section 66.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure in examinations

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee who does not initially receive a passing mark on the
  qualifying examinations may write them only one additional time, and
  must do so at the first sitting of the examinations that takes place
  after they receive their examination results or the results of any
  re-marking under subsection 56(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for delayed examinations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a licensee is not able to write the examinations
  as required by subsection (1) for a reason beyond their control, the
  Registrar may, on request of the licensee, allow the licensee to write
  the examinations at the next following sitting.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Undertaking

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** A licensee referred to in
subsection 57(1) must give an undertaking in writing to the Registrar
that they agree not to practise until they have entered into a new
training agreement with the same or another supervisor.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remedial training

- **[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  class 3 licensee does not receive a passing mark on the qualifying
  examinations within two years after the day on which they complete the
  training program, they must provide the Registrar with a remedial
  training and education plan prepared in conjunction with their
  supervisor or, if they have no supervisor, in accordance with any
  conditions that the Registrar may impose on their licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-examination

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On completion of the remedial training and education
  plan, the licensee may, despite subsection 57(1), request to rewrite
  the qualifying examinations or any part of the examinations, on
  payment to the College of the fee set out in item 8 of Schedule 1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waiver or modification of requirements

**[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** If an applicant for a
licence or a licensee is unable to meet any requirement for licensing or
training for reasons beyond their control, the Registrar may waive or
modify that requirement on request by the applicant or licensee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registrar may impose conditions

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar may impose
either or both of the following conditions on a licence at the time of
its issuance or reinstatement if it is in the public interest to do so:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the licensee complete any remedial training and
  examinations specified by the Registrar; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the licensee work under the supervision of or in
  conjunction with a class 1 licensee approved by the Registrar, for a
  specified period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar must set
the effective date of a licence when it is issued or reinstated.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accommodation for applicants or licensees

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar must
reasonably accommodate an applicant for a licence or a licensee if they
provide the Registrar with satisfactory information that demonstrates
the need for an accommodation in order to provide a fair opportunity for
the applicant or licensee to successfully complete any of the applicable
training, qualifying examinations or other licensing requirements.

#### [Supervisors]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1396103 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

- **[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following individuals are eligible to act as the supervisor of a class
  3 licensee:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a class 1 licensee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (3), a class 2 licensee;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a representative of CIPO.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar must authorize an eligible individual
  set out in subsection (1) to act as the supervisor of a class 3
  licensee if they apply to the Registrar and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have completed the supervisor training program;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} demonstrate in their application how they will
    provide training that focuses on ethical and competent practices;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} are not the subject of an investigation under
    section 37 of the Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} are not the subject of an application to the
    Discipline Committee by the Investigations Committee under
    subsection 49(1) of the Act; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} do not have a suspended licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional requirement --- class 2
  licensees

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In addition to meeting the requirements of subsection
  (2), a class 2 licensee must provide the Registrar with a training
  plan that demonstrates how the class 3 licensee under their
  supervision will receive training in the areas in which the class 2
  licensee's practice is restricted under section 47.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remedial supervisor training

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A supervisor must retake any part or all of the
  supervisor training program, as specified by the Registrar, if there
  are reasonable grounds to believe that the supervisor requires
  remedial training in some or all aspects of being a supervisor.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of authorization

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar must revoke
a supervisor's authorization if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the supervisor no longer meets any of the criteria
  set out in paragraphs 64(2)(b) to (e);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the supervisor does not comply with subsection 64(4)
  when required; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the supervisor's licence is suspended or revoked.

#### [Review of Registrar's Decisions with Respect to Licensing]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1396126 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registrar's decisions reviewable

**[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** An applicant for a
licence or a licensee may request that the Registration Committee review
any licensing decision of the Registrar with respect to their
application or licence, as applicable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration Committee

- **[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of a request made under section 66, the Registration Committee
  must consider the request and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} affirm or vary the Registrar's decision or dismiss
    the request; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} hold a hearing on the merits of the request and, at
    the conclusion of the hearing, affirm or vary the Registrar's
    decision or dismiss the request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registration Committee may request that the
  Registrar obtain additional information from the applicant or licensee
  and provide it to the Committee, at any time during the Committee's
  consideration of the request, including during any hearing of the
  request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written reasons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Registration Committee must provide reasons to
  the applicant or licensee in writing for each action it takes under
  subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When Registrar's decision varied

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Registration Committee varies the Registrar's
  decision, the Registrar must give effect to the varied decision
  without delay and notify the applicant or licensee and, if applicable,
  their supervisor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision final

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Registration Committee's decision is final and
  not reviewable by the Board.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1} {#h-1396142 .Part}

### [Obligations of Licensees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1396143 .Subheading}

#### [Obligations]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1396144 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** A licensee must meet the
following requirements in order to maintain the validity of their
licence:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have the necessary skills and competencies to
  practise the profession; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be of good character and remain fit to practise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Professional liability insurance

- **[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  professional liability insurance required by subsection 34(1) of the
  Act must meet the following requirements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be issued by a company licensed in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cover claims made in and outside Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} indemnify the licensee for any civil liability that
    arises from their acting as either a patent agent or a trademark
    agent; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} have coverage limits of no less than \$1 million
    per claim and \$2 million aggregate per year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following licensees are exempt from the
  requirement in subsection 34(1) of the Act to be insured against
  professional liability:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a class 3 licensee who has neither a supervisor nor
    a training agreement in effect and who has given an undertaking
    under section 58;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a class 4 licensee;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any licensee who is employed by an employer that
    carries on business in Canada and who provides patent agent or
    trademark agent services solely to their employer and not to the
    public.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual requirements

**[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** On or before March 31 of
each year, every licensee must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pay to the College the applicable fee set out in
  items 10 to 16 of Schedule 1;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} except for a class 4 licensee, file the annual
  licensee report with the Registrar;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if they are a class 3 licensee, provide the Registrar
  with the name of their supervisor; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if they are a class 4 licensee, provide the Registrar
  with their contact information and a declaration that they

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} continue to be resident in Canada,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} have complied with the requirements of section 73,
    and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} are not practising as a patent agent or trademark
    agent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

**[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** The annual licensee
report must contain all the following information in respect of the
licensee:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} confirmation of the mailing and civic addresses of
  each location of their business or practice, their email address and
  telephone number, and, if applicable, the name of their firm;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the areas of their practice and the approximate
  percentage by time or billings associated with each area;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of any professional regulatory body under
  which they are licensed;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} confirmation of their professional liability
  insurance and the name of the insurer, or confirmation that they are
  exempt from the requirement to be so insured and the reason for the
  exemption;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the official language or languages in which they are
  able to practise as a patent agent or trademark agent;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} their continuing professional development plan for
  the next year and a declaration that they have completed the previous
  year's plan;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a declaration that they continue to be resident in
  Canada;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a declaration that they have complied with the
  requirements of section 73; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} any other information or documents that the Registrar
  determines are necessary to carry on the activities of the College.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of deadlines

**[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar may, having
regard to the specific circumstances of the licensee, extend the
deadline for payment of the licence renewal fees by a licensee and for
filing the annual licensee report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licensees to advise Registrar

**[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** A licensee must advise
the Registrar in writing as soon as feasible after the occurrence of any
of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are served with an application for bankruptcy,
  make an assignment of property for the benefit of creditors or present
  a proposal in bankruptcy to creditors under the [Bankruptcy and
  Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} they have a judgment entered against them by a court;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} they are personally subject to an order for costs;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} they are charged with, plead guilty to or are found
  guilty of any offence under the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the
  [Controlled Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8), the [Income
  Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), a
  provincial act respecting securities, employment standards or official
  languages or any other provincial act that creates an offence that
  implicates an individual's integrity; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} they have their professional licence restricted or
  suspended by a professional regulatory body, or are found by a
  professional regulatory body to have committed professional misconduct
  or to be incompetent within the meaning of the statute under which the
  body made its finding.

### [Licence Suspension]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1396191 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of suspension

**[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar must give
the notice under subsection 35(2) of the Act to the licensee at least
seven days before the licence suspension is to take effect and must
include in the notice the reasons for the suspension.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lifting of suspension

- **[[75]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-75 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Registrar must reinstate a licence suspended under section 35 of the
  Act if the licensee, within three years after the date of the
  suspension, corrects the failure that resulted in the suspension and
  pays to the College the reinstatement fee set out in item 17 of
  Schedule 1, together with any outstanding fees owed by the licensee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lifting of suspension after three years

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar must reinstate a licence that has been
  suspended under section 35 of the Act for more than three years if the
  Registrar determines that the licensee is of good character, is fit to
  practise and has

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} corrected the failure that resulted in the
    suspension;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} completed any continuing professional development
    requirements that the Registrar may specify;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} completed any further training and examinations
    that the Registrar may specify to address the failure that resulted
    in the suspension; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} paid to the College the fee set out in item 17 of
    Schedule 1, together with any outstanding fees owed by the licensee.

### [Revocation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1396203 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation after five-year suspension ---
notice

**[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 35(4) of the Act, the Registrar may revoke a licence that has
been suspended for at least five years by giving notice to the licensee
at their last known email address at least 30 days before the day on
which the revocation is to take effect.

### [Surrender of Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1396206 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to surrender --- requirements

- **[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee who wishes to surrender their licence under section 36 of the
  Act must submit an application to the Registrar that contains the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their current and, if available, future contact
    information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reason for the surrender of the licence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} their complete professional employment history;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} confirmation of the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that they have completed all client matters or
      made arrangements to the satisfaction of their clients to have the
      clients' files returned to them or transferred to one or more
      licensees whose class of licence permits them to proceed with the
      file or files being transferred,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that they have assigned to one or more licensees
      described in subparagraph (i) any matters in progress at CIPO and
      have advised the appropriate Office of CIPO in writing of the name
      of the successor licensee or licensees, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the location of any of their files that are not
      returned to the clients or transferred as described in
      subparagraph (i); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the applicable fees set out in items 18 and 19 of
    Schedule 1, together with any outstanding fees owed by the licensee,
    payable to the College.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Verification of information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar may seek independent verification from
  a successor licensee of the information provided by a licensee under
  subparagraph (1)(d)(ii).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surrender --- approval and effective date

**[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar may approve
the surrender of a licence and set the effective date of the surrender
if the Registrar is satisfied that the application to surrender the
licence is complete and that the licensee is not the subject of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an investigation under section 37 of the Act; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an application by the Investigations Committee under
  subsection 49(1) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed surrender

**[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** A licence is deemed to
have been surrendered on the appointment of the licensee to the
judiciary or on the death of the licensee.

## [PART 5]{.HLabel1} {#h-1396226 .Part}

### [Registers and Certificates]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1396227 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal of personal information by
Registrar

**[[80]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar must not
make public in the Registers any of a licensee's [[personal
information]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 3 of the
[Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21), that was added in accordance with section
28 or 31 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registrar's certificate

**[[81]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-81 .sectionLabel}** On the request of a
licensee and on payment to the College of the fee set out in item 20 of
Schedule 1, the Registrar must produce a certificate that sets out the
information in respect of the licensee that is contained in the
Register.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foreign practitioners --- requirements

- **[[82]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-82 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  foreign practitioner who makes a request under paragraph 19(1)(b) or
  20(1)(b) of the Regulations must pay to the College the fee set out in
  item 21 of Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foreign practitioners --- continued
  inclusion in Register

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In order to continue to be included in the Register,
  a foreign practitioner must provide the statement required by
  subsection 19(2) or 20(2) of the Regulations each year during the
  period beginning on May 1 and ending on June 30.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional requirements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The statement must be accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} proof, furnished by the relevant competent
    authority in the foreign practitioner's country of residence, that
    they are authorized to act as a patent agent or as a trademark agent
    in that country; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fee set out in item 22 of Schedule 1, payable
    to the College.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of deadlines

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar may, having regard to the specific
  circumstances of a foreign practitioner, extend the deadline for
  payment of the fee referred to in paragraph (3)(b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of status

**[[83]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-83 .sectionLabel}** A foreign practitioner
must notify the Registrar in writing without delay of the occurrence of
any of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} they become a resident of Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} their country of residence changes, in which case
  they must provide the name of their new country of residence;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the country where they are authorized as a patent
  agent or trademark agent changes, in which case they must provide
  proof of the new authorization, furnished by the relevant competent
  authority; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} they cease to be authorized to act as a patent agent
  or trademark agent in any country.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal from Registers

**[[84]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-84 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar must remove
from the Registers the name and contact information of any individual
who no longer meets the requirements set out in paragraph 19(1)(a) or
20(1)(a) or subsection 19(2) or 20(2) of the Regulations.

## [PART 6]{.HLabel1} {#h-1396251 .Part}

### [Investigations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1396252 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation

**[[85]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-85 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar must not
dismiss a complaint when there are reasonable grounds to believe that
the licensee who is the subject of the complaint has committed
professional misconduct or was incompetent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for appeal

**[[86]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-86 .sectionLabel}** A complainant's request
under subsection 38.1(4) of the Act for an appeal of the Registrar's
decision must be made in writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licensee's application for review of
provisional action

- **[[87]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-87 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application to the Discipline Committee under subsection 37.2(1) of
  the Act must be submitted to the Registrar and include a copy of the
  decision of the Investigations Committee made under subsection 37.1(1)
  of the Act and a description of the reasons for the appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registrar to forward application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar must forward the application for review
  to the Discipline Committee together with a copy of the decision to be
  reviewed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supplementary information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Discipline Committee may request

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the licensee provide it with any additional
    information the Committee considers necessary to make its decision
    under subsection 37.2(2) of the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the Investigations Committee provide it with
    any of the information and documents that the Investigations
    Committee considered in making its decision under subsection 37.1(1)
    of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of investigation

**[[88]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-88 .sectionLabel}** The notice under section
38 of the Act must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be made in writing;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} request that the licensee provide any further
  representations in writing within 30 days after the day of the notice;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} inform the licensee that there is to be no oral
  hearing in connection with the complaint.

## [PART 7]{.HLabel1} {#h-1396271 .Part}

### [Fees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1396272 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual adjustment

**[[89]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-89 .sectionLabel}** Every fee set out in
Schedule 1 is to be adjusted in each fiscal year on January 1 by the
percentage change in the October All-items Consumer Price Index for
Canada --- as published by Statistics Canada under the [Statistics
Act](/eng/acts/S-19) --- between the month of October in the preceding
year and the month of October in the year before that year, and rounded
to the next highest dollar.
