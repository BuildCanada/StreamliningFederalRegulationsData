## Manitoba Milk Marketing Levies Order, 1991 {#manitoba-milk-marketing-levies-order-1991 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-315
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1991-05-10

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Levies
Payable for the Marketing, in Interprovincial and Export Trade, of Milk
and Cream Produced in Manitoba
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Manitoba Milk Producers' Marketing Board, pursuant to section
4[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EFBBA}
of the [Manitoba Milk Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._155), C.R.C.,
c. 155, made pursuant to section 2 of the Agricultural Products
Marketing Act, hereby revokes the Manitoba Milk Marketing Levies Order,
C.R.C., c. 156 and makes the annexed Order providing for the fixing,
imposing and collecting of levies payable for the marketing, in
interprovincial and export trade, of milk and cream produced in
Manitoba, in substitution therefor.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EFBBA){#footnote1_e}SOR/90-271,
  1990 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 1998
  :::

Dated at Winnipeg, Manitoba, May 3, 1991
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934371 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Manitoba Milk Marketing Levies Order,
1991](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-315).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934373 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Natural Products Marketing Act
    of Manitoba; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Dairy
    Commission; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Manitoba Milk
    Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act; ([Office
    de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cream]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cream]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means milk, as defined in the
    [Manitoba Milk Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._155), that
    contains more than six per cent butterfat; ([crème]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[cream annual permitted quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cream annual permitted quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    quantity of cream, expressed in terms of kilograms of butterfat,
    determined by subtracting the amount of cream marketed by a cream
    producer in intra-provincial trade in Manitoba in a dairy year from
    the cream quota of the cream producer for that year; ([quantité
    annuelle autorisée de crème]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Cream Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Cream Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Cream Marketing Plan
    Regulation established and amended from time to time pursuant to the
    Act; ([programme de la crème]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cream producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cream producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person to whom a
    cream quota is allotted by the Commodity Board; ([producteur de
    crème]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cream quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cream quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a quota, expressed in
    kilograms of butterfat, allotted by the Commodity Board to a cream
    producer pursuant to the Cream Plan; ([contingent de
    crème]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dairy year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dairy year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the 12 month period
    beginning on August 1 of any year and ending on July 31 of the
    following year; ([année laitière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[deemed number of production days]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deemed number of production days]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    number assigned by the Commodity Board to a milk producer in respect
    of a calendar month representing the number of days of milk
    production during that calendar month on which the milk producer is
    deemed by the Commodity Board to have marketed milk; ([nombre
    présumé de jours de production]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[milk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[milk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, milk as defined in the [Manitoba
    Milk Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._155), that contains six per
    cent or less of butterfat; ([lait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[milk monthly permitted quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[milk monthly permitted quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    quantity of milk, expressed in litres, determined by multiplying the
    milk quota of a milk producer firstly by the deemed number of
    production days of that milk producer for a month, and secondly by
    the monthly eligible percentage for that month, and then subtracting
    from the result the amount of milk marketed by that milk producer
    during that month in intra-provincial trade in Manitoba; ([quantité
    mensuelle autorisée de lait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Milk Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Milk Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Manitoba Milk Producers'
    Marketing Plan Regulation established and amended from time to time
    pursuant to the Act; ([programme du lait]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[milk producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[milk producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person to whom a milk
    quota is allotted by the Commodity Board; ([producteur de
    lait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[milk quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[milk quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a quota, expressed in
    litres, that is allotted by the Commodity Board to a milk producer
    pursuant to the Milk Plan; ([contingent de lait]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[monthly eligible percentage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[monthly eligible percentage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    percentage of milk marketed by the Commodity Board during a calendar
    month that the Commodity Board estimates has been used for
    manufacturing purposes during that calendar month and that is
    eligible for a subsidy payment under programs operated by the
    Commission. ([pourcentage mensuel admissible]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934391 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies in
respect of milk and cream marketed in interprovincial and export trade.

## [Milk Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934393 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A levy is imposed on each
person who markets milk of \$1.50 per hectolitre of milk marketed by the
person.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A levy is imposed on each
milk producer of \$3.44 per hectolitre of milk marketed by the milk
producer in any calendar month within the milk monthly permitted
quantity of the milk producer for that calendar month.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A levy is imposed on each
milk producer of \$40 per hectolitre of milk marketed by the milk
producer in any calendar month in excess of the milk monthly permitted
quantity of the milk producer for that calendar month.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A levy is imposed on each
person who markets milk and who has not been allotted a milk quota of
\$40 per hectolitre of milk marketed by the person.

## [Cream Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934398 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A levy is imposed on each
person who markets cream of \$0.085 per kilogram of butterfat in respect
of all cream marketed by the person.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A levy is imposed on each
cream producer of \$4.00 per kilogram of butterfat in respect of all
cream marketed by the cream producer during a dairy year in excess of
the cream annual permitted quantity of that cream producer for that
dairy year.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A levy is imposed on each
person who markets cream and who has not been allotted a cream quota of
\$4.00 per kilogram of butterfat in respect of all cream marketed by the
person.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934402 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A levy imposed by this
Order is due at the time of the marketing of the milk or cream in
respect of which the levy is imposed.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Any person on whom levies
are imposed by this Order shall pay the levies to the Commodity Board at
its office at 36 Scurfield Boulevard, Winnipeg, Manitoba R3T 3N5 within
seven days after the marketing of the milk or cream by that person.
