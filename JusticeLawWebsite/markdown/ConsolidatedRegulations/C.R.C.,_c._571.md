## Excise Act Licence Fees Regulations {#excise-act-licence-fees-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 571
:::

[EXCISE ACT](/eng/acts/E-14)

Regulations Prescribing Fees for Licences Issued Under the Excise Act
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558148 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Excise Act Licence Fees
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._571).

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558150 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Every person to whom a
licence is granted under the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14) for a purpose
set out in Column I of an item of the schedule shall pay the annual fee
set out in Column II of that item.
