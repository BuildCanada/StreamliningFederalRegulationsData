## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003122 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 2, any
person may, under the authority of this Permit, export from Canada any
goods of United States origin as described in item 5400 of Group 5 of
the Schedule to the [Export Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Permit does not
authorize the exportation of goods described in section 1 to any country
listed in the [Area Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-543) or to any
of the following countries:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Cuba;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Democratic People's Republic of Korea;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Iran; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Syria.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-203, s. 1
- SOR/2005-223, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Where any goods exported
under the authority of this Permit are required to be reported in the
prescribed form under the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), the statement
"GEP-12" or "LGE-12" shall be inserted in the appropriate field of the
report.
