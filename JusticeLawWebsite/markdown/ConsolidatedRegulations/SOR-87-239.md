## St. Marys Paper Inc. Regulations {#st.-marys-paper-inc.-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

Registration 1987-04-14

Regulations Respecting Insurance of a Loan Made to St. Marys Paper Inc.

P.C. 1987-759 1987-04-13

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Regional Industrial Expansion and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to Industry, Trade and Commerce Vote 1a of Appropriation Act
No. 1, 1980-81[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EGCAA}, as
extended by Industry, Trade and Commerce Vote 1e of Appropriation Act
No. 4, 1981-82[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2star_e){#footnote2star_e-ID0EDCAA}, is
pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations respecting insurance of a
loan made to St. Marys Paper Inc.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904897 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [St. Marys Paper Inc.
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-239).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904899 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Regional
    Industrial Expansion; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any lender, or its
    assignee, other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Government of Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the government of any province of Canada,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an agency of any government referred to in
      paragraph (a) or (b) or any company that is effectively controlled
      by any such government or an agency thereof, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a municipal corporation; ([prêteur
      privé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[St. Marys Paper Inc.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[St. Marys Paper Inc.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a company
    incorporated with that name under the laws of Canada and having its
    head office in Sault Ste. Marie, Ontario. ([St. Marys Paper
    Inc.]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904908 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Insurance provided by the
Minister in respect of a loan made by a private lender to St. Marys
Paper Inc. to permit St. Marys Paper Inc. to modernize its facility to
manufacture supercalendered paper at Sault Ste. Marie shall be subject
to the terms and conditions set out in sections 4 to 7.

## [Terms and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904910 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The insurance referred to
in section 3 shall be for an amount up to 90 per cent of any net loss up
to a maximum of 15 million dollars that may result in respect of the
loan referred to in that section.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A private lender shall pay
to the Minister by semi-annual payments in advance a fee of at least one
per cent per annum of the amount of insurance in force.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where a private lender has
demanded repayment of a loan insured under these Regulations, the amount
payable by the Minister shall not exceed an amount equal to the lesser
of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of insurance in force on the date of
  demand; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 90 per cent of the net loss incurred by the private
  lender up to a maximum of 15 million dollars.
