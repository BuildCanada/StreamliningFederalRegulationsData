::::

::::::::::: section

The Quebec Region Wood Producers' Syndicate, pursuant to sections
3[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EHCBA}

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021306 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who acquires or
    receives wood that is subject to the Plan from a producer.
    ([acheteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Syndicat des
    propriétaires forestiers de la région de Québec]{.otherLang
    lang="fr"}. ([Syndicat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Plan conjoint des
    producteurs de bois de la région de Québec]{.otherLang lang="fr"},
    RLRQ, c. M-35.1, r. 124. ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a producer who is subject to
    the Plan and engaged in the production or marketing of wood.
    ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[wood]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wood]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means wood that is produced in the
    Quebec Region and that is subject to the Plan.
    ([bois]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021315 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay to
the Commodity Board, for all wood marketed by the producer in
interprovincial and export trade, the applicable levies fixed or imposed
in the following regulations, as amended from time to time:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [Règlement sur les contributions des producteurs de
  bois de la région de Québec]{.otherLang lang="fr"}, RLRQ, c. M-35.1,
  r. 117.1; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [Règlement sur les fonds du Syndicat des
  propriétaires forestiers de la région de Québec]{.otherLang
  lang="fr"}, RLRQ, c. M-35.1, r. 121.1.

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2014-245, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

## [Method of Collection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021322 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** All levies fixed and
imposed under the Regulations set out in section 2 are payable by the
producer or the buyer to the Commodity Board at its head office located
in Quebec City, Quebec in compliance with the terms and conditions set
out in those Regulations, including, as the case may be, by direct
payment from the producer to the Commodity Board, by a deduction made by
the Commodity Board from the sale price payable to the producer or by a
deduction effected by the buyer and remitted directly to the Commodity
Board.
