## Off-road Compression-Ignition (Mobile and Stationary) and Large Spark-Ignition Engine Emission Regulations {#off-road-compression-ignition-mobile-and-stationary-and-large-spark-ignition-engine-emission-regulations .Title-of-Act}

:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 2020-12-04

Off-road Compression-Ignition (Mobile and Stationary) and Large
Spark-Ignition Engine Emission Regulations

P.C. 2020-975 2020-12-04
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 332(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a2){#fn_a2-ID0EICBA} of the [Canadian
Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b2){#fn_b2-ID0EFCBA}, the Minister of the
Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on March 9, 2019, a copy of
the proposed Off-road Compression-Ignition (Mobile and Stationary) and
Large Spark-Ignition Engine Emission Regulations, substantially in the
annexed form, and persons were given an opportunity to file comments
with respect to the proposed Regulations or to file a notice of
objection requesting that a board of review be established and stating
the reasons for the objection;

recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to sections
160[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_c2){#fn_c2-ID0EGBAA} and 319 of

1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote

Off-road Compression-Ignition (Mobile and Stationary) and Large

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Concurrent sale

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, an engine that is sold in Canada is considered to be sold
concurrently in Canada and in the United States if an engine that
belongs to the same emission family --- or to the same emission families
in the case of an engine that is subject to evaporative emission
standards and belongs to more than one emission family --- is offered
for sale in the United States during the 365 days preceding

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an engine that is imported into
  Canada, the day on which it is imported; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an engine that is manufactured in
  Canada,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the national emissions mark is
    applied to the engine, if known, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the day referred to in subparagraph (i) is not
    known, the day on which the manufacture of the engine was completed.

## [Model Year]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1277211 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Model year

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A year
  that is used by a manufacturer of an engine as a model year must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the period of production of a model of engine
    does not include January 1 of a calendar year, correspond to the
    calendar year during which the period of production falls; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the period of production of a model of engine
    includes January 1 of a calendar year, correspond to that calendar
    year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The period of production must not include more than
  one January 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Imported engines

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an engine that is imported does not bear the
  engine's date of manufacture, its model year corresponds to the
  calendar year during which it is imported.

## [Prescribed Engines]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1277221 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Section 149 of Act --- definition
[[engine]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Mobile
  or stationary compression-ignition engines or mobile large
  spark-ignition engines --- including those that have a complete fuel
  system --- are prescribed for the purposes of the definition
  [[engine]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 149 of the Act unless they

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are regulated under the On-Road Vehicle and Engine
    Emission Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are or will be installed in a vehicle that is
    regulated under the On-Road Vehicle and Engine Emission Regulations
    and are intended to propel such a vehicle;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} are or will be installed in an [[auxiliary power
    unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 1(1) of the
    On-Road Vehicle and Engine Emission Regulations, that is or will be
    installed in a tractor that is regulated under those Regulations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} are regulated under the Marine Spark-ignition
    Engine, Vessel and Off-road Recreational Vehicle Emission
    Regulations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} are or will be installed in a vehicle that is
    regulated under the Marine Spark-ignition Engine, Vessel and
    Off-road Recreational Vehicle Emission Regulations and are intended
    to propel such a vehicle;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} are designed exclusively for competition and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} have performance characteristics that are
      substantially superior to non-competitive engines,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are not displayed for sale in any public
      dealership or otherwise offered for sale to the general public,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} bear a label that meets the requirements set
      out in section 34 and

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} sets out the statement "COMPETITION ENGINE /
        [MOTEUR DE COMPÉTITION]{.otherLang lang="fr"}", or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} meets the requirements set out in section 620,
        subpart G, of CFR 1039 or, in accordance with section 630,
        subpart G, of CFR 1048, section 620, subpart G, of CFR 1054, as
        the case may be;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} are designed to be used exclusively in a military
    machine that is designed for use in combat or combat support during
    military activities only, including reconnaissance missions, rescue
    missions and training missions, and bear

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a label to that effect that meets the
      requirements set out in section 34 and sets out the statement
      "THIS ENGINE IS DESIGNED TO BE USED EXCLUSIVELY IN MILITARY

## [Engine Standards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1277263 .Part}

### [General Standards]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1277264 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evaporative emission standards ---
application

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The evaporative emission
standards referred to in these Regulations apply in respect of engines
that have a complete fuel system with non-metallic fuel lines or fuel
tanks and that are fuelled with volatile liquid fuels.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emission control system --- requirements

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  emission control system that is installed on an engine to enable it to
  conform to the standards set out in these Regulations must not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in its operation, release a substance that causes
    air pollution and that would not have been released if the system
    had not been installed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in its operation or malfunction, make the engine or
    the machine in which the engine is installed unsafe, or endanger
    persons or property near the engine or machine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defeat device

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An engine must not be equipped with an auxiliary
  emission control device that reduces the effectiveness of the emission
  control system under conditions that may reasonably be expected to be
  encountered under normal operation of the engine, unless a description
  of the auxiliary emission control device is included in the evidence
  of conformity under section 40 or 41 and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conditions under which the auxiliary emission
    control device operates are substantially included in the test
    procedures referred to in subsection 1(4);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the auxiliary emission control device is needed to
    protect the engine against damage or accident;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the auxiliary emission control device is only used
    to start the engine;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the auxiliary emission control device is installed
    in a compression-ignition engine that is designed to power an
    emergency machine and is activated during emergency response
    operations to maintain speed, torque or power in either of the
    following circumstances:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the emission control system is in an abnormal
      state, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the device acts to maintain the emission control
      system in a normal state; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the auxiliary emission control device is installed
    in a compression-ignition engine other than one referred to in
    paragraph (d) and the following criteria are met:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the auxiliary emission control device

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} is designed to prevent the operation of the
        emission control system only to the extent necessary to allow
        the engine to meet the demands of an emergency situation,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} is accompanied by a notice in writing
        discouraging improper use of the auxiliary emission control
        device,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} is designed so that it can only operate for a
        maximum of 120 hours in total before it has to be reset,

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} is designed so that it can only be reset to
        reestablish the time limit referred to in clause (C) by the
        input of a temporary code, the reconfiguration of the engine's
        electronic control module or the use of another secure feature
        that is unique to each engine, and

      - [(E)]{.lawlabel} is designed so that its operation can be
        manually stopped, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the engine on which the auxiliary emission
      control device is installed is designed to electronically record
      the number of times the auxiliary emission control device is used.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standards

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 11(2), if a
  compression-ignition engine is equipped with an auxiliary emission
  control device referred to in subsection (2) that meets the
  requirements set out in paragraph (2)(d) or (e), the engine and the
  diagnostic system with which it is equipped are not required to
  conform to the following standards when the auxiliary emission control
  device is operating:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the engine, the standards referred
    to in sections 10 to 16; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the diagnostic system, the standards
    referred to in section 17.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustable parameters --- requirements

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Engines
  equipped with adjustable parameters must conform to the applicable
  standards under these Regulations for any specification within the
  physically adjustable range.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Engines using non-commercial fuel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to an engine that is
  designed to operate using fuel that is not generally offered for sale
  in Canada if the engine is adjusted in accordance with the
  manufacturer's instructions referred to in subsection 36(2) and
  conforms to the applicable standards under these Regulations for those
  specifications.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative test procedures

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite
  subsection 1(4), a company may, instead of using the test procedures
  set out in CFR 60 in respect of their stationary compression-ignition
  engines, replace one or more of those test procedures with alternative
  test procedures that have parameters and specifications that are
  equivalent to or more stringent than the parameters and specifications
  of the test procedures set out in CFR 60.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Published test procedures

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The alternative test procedures referred to in
  subsection (1) must be included in a test procedure published by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the government of a state or of a political
    subdivision of a state, or an institution of a state or of a
    political subdivision of a state;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an international organization of states or an
    international organization that is established by the governments of
    states, or an institution of any of those international
    organizations; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an organization that develops test procedures based
    on consensus and that is internationally recognized as being
    competent to establish those test procedures.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equivalence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company that uses alternative test procedures under
  subsection (1) must, as part of the evidence of conformity under
  section 40 or 41, as the case may be, include evidence demonstrating
  that the alternative test procedures have parameters and
  specifications that are equivalent to or more stringent than the
  parameters and specifications of the test procedures set out in CFR
  60.

  authorized representative of the company and contain the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company's name, street address and, if
    different, mailing address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, email address and telephone number of an
    authorized representative of the company;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the business number assigned to the company by the
    Minister of National Revenue;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number of engines that the company intends to
    import in reliance on subsection 153(2) of the Act, the name of the
    manufacturer and the unique identification number, make, model and
    model year of the engines, as well the applicable emission families;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any engines that are installed in a
    machine, the name of the machine manufacturer and the make, model
    and type of machine;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} for each engine, a statement, as applicable, that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the engine's manufacture will be completed in
      Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the company is authorized to apply the national
      emissions mark to the engine and will apply it at the location
      specified in their application under section 26, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the engine is a replacement engine;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the company makes the statement referred to in
    subparagraph (f)(i),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a statement from the manufacturer of the engine
      that the engine will, when completed in accordance with
      instructions provided by the manufacturer, conform to the
      requirements prescribed under these Regulations, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a statement from the company that the engine
      will be completed in accordance with the instructions referred to
      in subparagraph (i);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the company makes the statement referred to in
    subparagraph (f)(ii),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the authorization number assigned to it by the
      Minister,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the street address of the location where the
      engine will be kept until the national emissions mark is applied
      to it, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures that will be
      taken to ensure that the engine does not leave the company's
      possession or control before the national emissions mark is
      applied to it; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the company makes the statement referred to in
    subparagraph (f)(iii),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the street address of the location where the
      engine will be kept until the evidence of conformity referred to
      in subsection 41(1) has been submitted, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures that will be taken
      to ensure that the engine does not leave the company's possession
      or control before the evidence of conformity is submitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternate declaration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an engine that is covered by an EPA
  certificate and meets either of the following conditions, the company
  may make a declaration that contains the information set out in
  paragraphs (4)(d) and 44(1)(a) to (d) instead of the information set
  out in paragraphs (2)(a) to (i):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the engine will have its manufacture completed in
    Canada by the addition of an emission control system for exhaust
    emissions, or part of such a system, in a manner that conforms to
    the certificate and the certificate holder's installation
    instructions; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the engine will have its manufacture completed in
    Canada by the addition of a complete fuel system, or part of such a
    system, in a manner that conforms to the certificate and the
    certificate holder's installation instructions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}50 engines or more

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A company that imports 50 engines or more during a
  calendar year may submit the declaration referred to in subsection (3)
  after importation --- but no later than March 31 of the calendar year
  following the calendar year during which the engine is imported ---
  if, before importing the engine, the company submits a notice to the
  Minister that includes the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company's name, street address and, if
    different, mailing address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, email address and telephone number of an
    authorized representative of the company;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the business number assigned to the company by the
    Minister of National Revenue; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the engine is covered by an EPA
    certificate and will, as applicable, have its manufacture completed
    in Canada by the addition of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an emission control system for exhaust emissions,
      or part of such a system, in a manner that conforms to the
      certificate and the certificate holder's installation
      instructions; or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a complete fuel system, or part of such a
      system, in a manner that conforms to the certificate and the
      certificate holder's installation instructions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paragraph 155(1)(a) of Act

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person's authorized representative,
    if any, and their email address and telephone number;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the business number assigned to the person by the
    Minister of National Revenue, if any;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement of whether the person intends to import
    compression-ignition engines, large spark-ignition engines or both;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a written statement that the engine will be used in
    Canada solely for purposes of exhibition, demonstration, evaluation
    or testing;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the engine will be imported;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the date by which the engine will be removed from
    Canada or destroyed;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the engine's unique identification number, make,
    model and model year and the name of the engine manufacturer; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the engine is installed in a machine, the type
    of machine, the name of the machine manufacturer and the machine's
    make and model.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission

  their authorized representative and submitted to the Minister before

  submitted quarterly.

## [Maintenance, Retention and Submission of Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1277766 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records to be maintained

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company must maintain records, in writing or in a readily readable
  electronic or optical form, that contain the following information in
  relation to an engine:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the evidence of conformity referred to in section
    40 or 41, as the case may be;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any declaration made under subsection
    44(1) or paragraph 46(1)(b) and any notice submitted under
    subsection 44(3);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the engine's unique identification number
    includes characters that provide information in respect of the
    engine's manufacture, the means by which to interpret those
    characters to obtain that information; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any written instructions required under
    sections 36 to 39.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of retention

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The records must be retained for a period of eight
  years beginning on any one of the following days, as applicable:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the information referred to in
    paragraphs (1)(a), (c) and (d),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the engine is manufactured in Canada, the last
      day of the calendar year that corresponds to the model year of the
      engine, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the engine is imported, the day on which it
      is imported; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the information referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b), the day on which the declaration is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 155(6) of Act

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 155(6) of the Act, the
  record maintained by a person who makes a declaration referred to in
  paragraph 155(1)(a) of the Act must include a copy of the declaration
  and documents evidencing the use of the engine in Canada and its
  disposition and must be kept as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the declaration,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a copy must be kept in writing with the engine
      until it is disposed of, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a copy must be kept in writing or in a readily
      readable electronic or optical form at the person's place of
      business for a period of eight years beginning on the day on which
      the engine was disposed of; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the documents evidencing the use of
    the engine in Canada and its disposition, a copy must be kept in
    writing or in a readily readable electronic or optical form at the
    person's place of business for a period of eight years beginning on
    the day on which the engine was disposed of.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Location of retention

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the records referred to in subsection (1),
  subparagraph (3)(a)(ii) or paragraph (3)(b) are retained at a location
  other than a place of business of the company or person, the company
  or person must keep a record of the name and telephone number of a
  contact person at that location and the street address and, if
  different, the mailing address of that location.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission to Minister

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister makes a written request for a record
  referred to in subsection (1), the record must be submitted to the
  Minister in either official language

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 40 days after the day on which the request
    is made; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the record must be translated from a language
    other than English or French, within 60 days after the day on which
    the request is made.

## [Registration System]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1277792 .Part}

  system maintained by a company in respect of the engines that it
  manufactures or imports must include the following information:

    with an auxiliary emission control device referred to in subsection
    7(2) that meets the requirements set out in paragraph 7(2)(e); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each engine referred to in paragraph (a), if
    available,

      on the company's behalf for the reset of the auxiliary emission
      control device and, for each request, a summary of the emergency
      situation in which the auxiliary emission control device was
      activated, based on the information provided in the request for
      reset, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the number of times the auxiliary emission
      control device has been reset.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Imported engines

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an imported engine, the registration
  system in respect of the engine may be maintained by the manufacturer
  of the engine on the company's behalf.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of retention

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The information included in the registration system
  in respect of an engine must be retained for a period of eight years

  manufacture is completed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Location of retention

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the registration system is retained at a location
  other than a place of business of the company, the company must keep a
  record of the name and telephone number of a contact person at that
  location as well as the street address and, if different, the mailing
  address of that location.

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** A company applying under

standard prescribed under these Regulations must, before manufacturing
or importing the engine, submit the following information in writing to
the Minister:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company's name and street address and, if
  different, mailing address;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, email address and telephone number of an
  authorized representative of the company;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the province or country under the laws of which the
  company is established;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the standards from which an exemption is sought,
  including a reference to the provisions of these Regulations that
  prescribe the standards;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the duration requested for the exemption;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the estimated number of engines for which the
  exemption is sought and an estimate of the changes in emissions if the
  exemption is granted;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the reason for requesting the exemption, including
  technical and financial information that demonstrates in detail why
  conformity to the standards referred to in paragraph (d) would

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} create substantial financial hardship for the
    company,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} impede the development of new features for
    emission monitoring or emission control that are equivalent or
    superior to those that conform to prescribed standards, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} impede the development of new kinds of engines or
    engine systems or components;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the basis of the application is substantial
  financial hardship,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the world production of engines manufactured by the
    company or by the manufacturer that is the subject of the
    application in the 12-month period beginning two years before the
    start of the exemption period being sought, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the total number of engines manufactured for, or
    imported into, the Canadian market in the 12-month period beginning
    two years before the start of the exemption period being sought; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the company is requesting that information
  submitted be treated as confidential under section 313 of the Act, the
  reasons for the request.

## [Defect Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1277826 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  notice of defect referred to in subsections 157(1) and (4) of the Act
  must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the company giving the notice and its
    street address and, if different, mailing address, and the name,
    email address, telephone number and, if any, facsimile number of the
    appropriate contact person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each engine in respect of which the notice is
    given, its make, model and model year, the name of its manufacturer,
    the period during which it was manufactured and any applicable
    emission families;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the range or ranges of unique identification
    numbers for the engines in respect of which the notice is given, if
    known;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the machine or type of machine in
    or on which the engine is installed or is likely to be installed;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the total number of engines in respect of which the
    notice is given or, if the total number is not known, the estimated
    number;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the estimated percentage of the potentially
    affected engines that contain the defect;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of the defect;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} an evaluation of the pollution risk arising from
    the defect;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the measures to be taken to correct
    the defect;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a chronology of the principal events that led to
    the determination of the existence of the defect, if known; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a description of the means available to the company
    to contact the current owner of each defective engine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Language of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of defect must be given in writing and,
  when given to a person other than the Minister, must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in both official languages; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the person's official language of choice, if it
    is known.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company must, within 60 days after giving a notice
  of defect, submit to the Minister the initial report referred to in
  subsection 157(7) of the Act containing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an update to the information referred to in
    subsection (1), if there have been any changes to that information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if not already provided in the notice,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the range or ranges of unique identification
      numbers for the engines in respect of which the notice is given,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the total number of engines in relation to which
      the notice of defect has been given, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a chronology of the principal events that led
      to the determination of the existence of the defect; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} copies of all notices, bulletins and other
    circulars issued by the company in respect of the defect, including
    a detailed description of the nature and physical location of the
    defect with diagrams and other illustrations as necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quarterly report

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a company submits an initial report under
  subsection (3), it must submit, within 45 days after the end of each
  quarter, a quarterly report to the Minister respecting the defect and
  its correction that contains the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number, title or other identification assigned
    by the company to the notice of defect;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the revised number of engines in
    respect of which the notice of defect was given;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the notice of defect was given to
    the current owners of the affected engines and the dates of any
    follow-up communications; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total number or percentage of engines repaired
    by or on behalf of the company, including engines requiring
    inspection only.
