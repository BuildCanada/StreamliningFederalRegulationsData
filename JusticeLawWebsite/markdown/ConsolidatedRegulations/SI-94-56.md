## Order Authorizing the Minister of Transport to Prescribe Fees (Ship/Shore Communications Services) {#order-authorizing-the-minister-of-transport-to-prescribe-fees-shipshore-communications-services .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/94-56
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1994-05-04

Order Authorizing the Minister of Transport to Prescribe Fees
(Ship/Shore Communications Services)

P.C. 1994-641 1994-04-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph
19(1)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI_94-56_EN_hq_20089){#fn_SI_94-56_EN_hq_20089-ID0EDBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to authorize the Minister of Transport to prescribe, by order, the
charges to be paid by users of ship/shore communications services
provided by Coast Guard Radio Stations.
