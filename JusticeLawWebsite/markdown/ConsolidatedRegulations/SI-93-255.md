## Designation of Countries (Standards Council of Canada) Order {#designation-of-countries-standards-council-of-canada-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-255
:::

[STANDARDS COUNCIL OF CANADA ACT](/eng/acts/S-16)

Registration 1993-12-29

Order Designating Countries for the Purposes of Paragraph 4(2)(d) of the
Standards Council of Canada Act

P.C. 1993-2112  1993-12-15
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology, pursuant to
subsection 4(4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-255_e_hq_6554){#fn_SI-93-255_e_hq_6554-ID0EHBAA}
of the [Standards Council of Canada Act](/eng/acts/S-16), is pleased
hereby to make the annexed Order designating countries for the purposes
of paragraph 4(2)(d) of the Standards Council of Canada Act, effective
on the day on which section 224 of [An Act to implement the North
American Free Trade Agreement](/eng/acts/N-23.8), being chapter 44 of
the Statutes of Canada, 1993, comes into force.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-647144 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Designation of Countries (Standards Council of Canada)
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-93-255).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-647146 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following countries are
designated for the purposes of paragraph 4(2)(d) of the [Standards
Council of Canada Act](/eng/acts/S-16):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any country that is a member economy of the
  Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any country that is a member state of the European
  Free Trade Association;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any country that is a member state of the European
  Union;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any country that is a party to the
  [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the
  [Canada--United States--Mexico Agreement Implementation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-10.65);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any country that is a member state of the
  Organization of American States, including Cuba; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any country that is a member of the World Trade
  Organization.
