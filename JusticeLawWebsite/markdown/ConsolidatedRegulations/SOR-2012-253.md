## [Payment of Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785650 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of fees

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 3, every person who requests that a service set out in
  column 1 of the schedule be performed must pay the fee set out in
  column 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacement of lost or stolen passport

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a person requests that a service set out in any
  of items 1 to 6, 9 and 10 of the schedule be performed to replace a
  lost or stolen passport or other travel document,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is deemed to have made a request for the
    service set out in item 14 of the schedule; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fee set out in item 14 of the schedule applies
    in addition to whichever of the fees set out in items 1 to 6, 9 and
    10 of the schedule is applicable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee for accelerated service

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a government body exercising any of the functions
  listed in subsection 12(1) of the [Canadian Passport
  Order](/eng/regulations/SI-81-86) is required to open one of its
  offices outside of that office's normal business hours to perform one
  or more services set out in item 1 or 2 of the schedule within the
  time required by the person for whom the services are performed, the
  person must pay, in addition to any applicable fees, the fee set out
  in item 8 of the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee for retention of non-expired
  passport

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} When a person requests that a service set out in item
  1 or 2 of the schedule be performed and also requests to retain the
  valid passport that was previously issued to them during the
  processing of the request, the applicable fee is increased by \$45.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-309, s. 2

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General exceptions

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No fee
  is payable in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a service set out in item 1 or 2 of the schedule
    that is performed for

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a destitute person, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a child less than 16 years of age or mentally
      incompetent person living in an institution in another country; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the issuance of an emergency travel document for
    the return of a Canadian citizen who is deported to Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Humanitarian service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fees set out in paragraphs 7(a) and (b) and item
  8 of the schedule do not apply in respect of a person who requests any
  of the services referred to in those provisions in order to travel
  outside Canada in support of any humanitarian operation conducted in
  response to a natural disaster or human conflict, including rescue,
  relief and reconstruction operations, if the person provides an
  official document to the Minister from an appropriate authority
  attesting to the person's participation in the humanitarian operation.

## [Fee Adjustments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785675 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustment factors --- subparagraph
1(a)(i) of schedule

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (4), the fee set out in subparagraph 1(a)(i) of the
  schedule is to be adjusted in accordance with this section in respect
  of each fiscal year to take into account

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any variation in the external cost incurred by the
    Minister to send passports and other travel documents in Canada by
    mail or courier;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any variation in the amounts incurred by the
    Minister that are payable to a government body, including a Crown
    corporation, for exercising any of the powers listed in
    subsection 12(1) of the [Canadian Passport
    Order](/eng/regulations/SI-81-86), as it read on November 30, 2012.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustment calculation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (3) and (4), the amount of the
  adjustment in respect of a given fiscal year is calculated during the
  preceding fiscal year and is the positive or negative amount, as the
  case may be, determined by the formula

  ::: {#785683 .Subsection}
  \[(A/B - C/D) x B/E\] + \[(F/G - H/I) x G/E\] + J

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the total external cost incurred by the Minister to send
      passports and other travel documents in Canada by mail or courier
      in the reference year, in constant dollars;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of passports and other travel documents sent
      by the Minister by mail or courier in Canada in the reference
      year;

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the total external cost incurred by the Minister to send
      passports and other travel documents by mail or courier in Canada
      in the fiscal year preceding the reference year, in constant
      dollars;

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of passports and other travel documents sent
      by the Minister by mail or courier in Canada in the fiscal year
      preceding the reference year;

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of passports and other travel documents issued
      by the Minister in Canada in the reference year;

  [F]{.dfn}
  :   is the total external cost incurred by the Minister for the
      exercise of his or her functions by other government bodies in the
      reference year, in constant dollars;

  [G]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of passports issued by the Minister in Canada
      in the reference year as a result of passport applications
      received by other government bodies;

  [H]{.dfn}
  :   is the total external cost incurred by the Minister for the
      exercise of his or her functions by other government bodies in the
      fiscal year preceding the reference year, in constant dollars;

  [I]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of passports issued by the Minister in Canada
      in the fiscal year preceding the reference year as a result of
      passport applications received by other government bodies; and

  [J]{.dfn}
  :   is the total amount of all adjustments that would have been made
      since the base year, but for the application of subsection (4).
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rounding up

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the adjustment calculation results in a fraction
  of a dollar, the amount of the adjustment is to be rounded up to the
  nearest dollar.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} There is to be no fee adjustment in respect of a
  given fiscal year if the adjustment calculation for that year, prior
  to rounding up, results in an amount greater than -\$1 but less than
  \$1.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-309, ss. 3, 7

    adjusted by the same amount as the fee set out in subparagraph
    1(a)(i) of the schedule; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the fee set out in item 4 of the schedule is
    adjusted to an amount representing 60% of the fee set out in item 3
    of the schedule, as adjusted in accordance with paragraph (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rounding up

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an adjustment calculation results in a fee that
  includes a fraction of a dollar, the resulting fee is to be rounded up
  to the nearest dollar.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustment factors --- subparagraph
1(b)(i) of schedule

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any variation in the external cost incurred by the

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any variation in the amounts incurred by the
    Minister for the delivery of the passport program outside Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustment calculation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (3) and (4), the amount of the
  adjustment in respect of a given fiscal year is calculated during the
  preceding fiscal year and is the positive or negative amount, as the
  case may be, determined by the formula

  ::: {#785717 .Subsection}
  \[(A/B - C/D) x B/E\] + \[(F/G - H/I) x G/E\] + J

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the total external cost incurred by the Minister to send
      passports by mail or courier outside Canada in the reference year,
      in constant dollars;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of passports sent by the Minister by mail or

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the total external cost incurred by the Minister to send

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of passports sent by the Minister by mail or

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of passports issued or sent by the Minister

  [F]{.dfn}
  :   is the total external cost incurred by the Minister for the
      Department of Foreign Affairs and International Trade to deliver
      the passport program outside Canada in the reference year, in
      constant dollars;

  [G]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of passports issued by the Minister outside

  [H]{.dfn}
  :   is the total external cost incurred by the Minister for the

  [I]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of passports issued by the Minister outside

      of passport applications received by the Department of Foreign

  :   is the total amount of all adjustments that would have been made

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of sections

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Constant Dollars

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 4(2) and 6(2), the amount of the external cost, in constant
dollars, for a given fiscal year, is determined by the following
formula:

::: {#785746 .Section}
A × 1.02^--B^

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the amount of the external cost, in current dollars; and

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the difference in the number of years between
:::

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reference year and the base year, in the case of
  elements A and F of the formulas set out in subsections 4(2) and 6(2);
  or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the year preceding the reference year and the base
  year, in the case of elements C and H of the formulas set out in those
  subsections.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Same fees

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, if
a fee adjustment is made in accordance with these Regulations, the fees
for a subsequent fiscal year in respect of which no adjustment is made
remain the fees resulting from the last adjustment.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785754 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a fee
  paid by a person for a service set out in paragraph 7(a) or (b) or
  item 8 of the schedule is to be remitted by the Minister to the person
  if the reason for travelling is the serious illness of the person or
  another individual, or the death of the other individual.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The remission is to be given if the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} makes a written request to the Minister for the
    remission within 180 days after the service is performed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provides a written statement to the Minister to the
    effect that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the reason for the trip is their own serious
      illness or the serious illness or death of another individual, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the reason for the trip is the serious
      illness or death of another individual, the person has or has had
      a relationship with that individual; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provides an official document to the Minister from
    an appropriate authority attesting to the serious illness or death.

- SOR/2014-309, ss. 6, 7
