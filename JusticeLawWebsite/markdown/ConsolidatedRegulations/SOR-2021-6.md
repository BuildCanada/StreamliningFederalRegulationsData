## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1284301 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Pension
    Plan](/eng/acts/C-8). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[additional contribution rate ratio]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[additional contribution rate ratio]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    ratio --- rounded to the nearest whole number or, if equidistant
    from two whole numbers, to the higher whole number --- of the
    percentage specified in paragraph 46(1)(c) of the Act to the
    percentage specified in paragraph 46(1)(b) of the Act. ([rapport du
    taux de cotisation supplémentaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the portions of benefits
    under the Act in respect of the additional Canada Pension
    Plan. ([prestations]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[legislated]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[legislated]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of benefits, as determined without
      regard to subsection 113.1(11.142) of the Act; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a first additional contribution
      rate or second additional contribution rate, as set out in
      Schedule 2 to the Act, without regard to any deemed amendment
      under subsection 113.1(11.141) of the
      Act. ([législatif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[review period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[review period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any three-year period
    for which the Chief Actuary prepares a report for the purpose of
    subsection 115(1) of the Act. ([période d'examen]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application of Subsections 113.1(11.141) and (11.142) of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1284311 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Range --- paragraph 113.1(11.141)(a)

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraph 113.1(11.141)(a) of the Act, the range consists
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each year starting with 2024 and ending with
    2038, every percentage point that is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} greater than or equal to 0.41, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each year starting with 2039, every percentage
    point that is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} greater than or equal to 0.31, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} less than or equal to -0.21.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Range --- paragraph 113.1(11.141)(b)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph 113.1(11.141)(b) of the
  Act, the range consists of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each year starting with 2024 and ending with
    2038, every percentage point that is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} greater than 0.30 and less than 0.41, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} greater than -0.31 and less than -0.20; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each year starting with 2039, every percentage
    point that is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} greater than 0.20 and less than 0.31, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} greater than -0.21 and less than -0.10.

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} greater than or equal to 0.31 multiplied by the

### [Actions When Rates in Surplus Position]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1284367 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No previous rate increase or reduction in
benefits

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** If the first additional
contribution rates and second additional contribution rates are equal to
the corresponding legislated first additional contribution rates and
second additional contribution rates and benefits are equal to or
greater than the corresponding legislated benefits, a value for S2 and
the period to which S2 is to apply are to be determined, for the
purposes of sections 8 to 10, such that if the Chief Actuary were to
calculate the first additional contribution rates under paragraph
115(1.1)(d) of the Act assuming the following, the rate calculated for
the first year after the review period would be as close as possible to
and no greater than the legislated first additional contribution rate
for self-employed persons for that year minus 0.1:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} S2 is to apply to the shortest period that is a
  multiple of three years and no fewer than six years, starting with the
  year after the review period;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} benefits that become payable after the review period
  are to be increased for the year in which they become payable in
  accordance with section 9, using the assumptions set out in the most
  recent report prepared for the purpose of section 115 of the Act; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for each year of the period to which S2 applies,
  benefits that became payable before that year are to be adjusted by
  multiplying them not by the ratio referred to in paragraph 45(2)(b)
  and subparagraphs 56(2)(c)(ii), 58(1.1)(b)(ii) and 59(c)(ii) of the
  Act, but by the value determined by the formula

  ::: {#1284373 .Paragraph}
  (1 + S2) × (PI~t~ / PI~t--1~) -- S2

  where

  [S2]{.dfn}
  :   is a multiple of 0.01 between 0 and 1,

  [PI~t~]{.dfn}
  :   is the Pension Index for that year, based on the assumption of
      future inflation set out in the most recent report prepared for
      the purpose of section 115 of the Act, and

  [PI~t--1~]{.dfn}
  :   is the Pension Index for the year before that year, based on the
      assumption of future inflation set out in the most recent report
      prepared for the purpose of section 115 of the Act.
  :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Target benefit multipliers

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 9 and 10, the interim target benefit multiplier is the value
determined by the following formula for the last year of whichever of
the applicable periods determined under section 5, paragraph 6(b) or
subsection 7(1) or (3) is the shortest and the final target benefit
multiplier is the value determined by that formula for the last year of
whichever of those periods is the longest:

::: {#1284421 .Section}
BM~t--1~ × \[(1 + S1 + S2) -- (S1 + S2) × (PI~t--1~ / PI~t~)\]

where

[BM~t--1~]{.dfn}
:   is the value of the benefit multiplier for the year before that
    year;

[S1]{.dfn}
:   is the value for S1 determined under subsection 7(1), if applicable
    to that year;

[S2]{.dfn}
:   is the value for S2 determined under section 5, paragraph 6(b) or
    subsection 7(3), if applicable to that year;

[PI~t--1~]{.dfn}
:   is the Pension Index for the year before that year, based on the
    assumption of future inflation set out in the most recent report
    prepared for the purpose of section 115 of the Act; and

[PI~t~]{.dfn}
:   is the Pension Index for that year, based on the assumption of
    future inflation set out in the most recent report prepared for the
    purpose of section 115 of the Act.
:::

  are equal, the benefit multiplier is equal to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the value determined by the following formula, for
    each year for which that value is less than the interim and final
    target benefit multipliers:

    ::: {#1284434 .Paragraph}
    BM~t--1~ × \[(1 + S1 + S2) -- (S1 + S2) × (PI~t--1~ / PI~t~)\]

    where

    [BM~t--1~]{.dfn}
    :   is the value of the benefit multiplier for the year before that
        year,

    [S1]{.dfn}
    :   is the value for S1, if any, determined under subsection 7(1),

    [S2]{.dfn}
    :   is the value for S2, if any, determined under section 5,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the value determined by the following formula, for

    BM~t--1~ × \[(1 + S) -- S × (PI~t--1~ / PI~t~)\]

    where

### [Actions When Rates in Deficit Position]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1284477 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No previous increase in benefits

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  benefits are less than or equal to the corresponding legislated
  benefits, a value for S4 is to be determined, for the purposes of
  sections 13 to 15, such that if the Chief Actuary were to calculate
  the first additional contribution rates under paragraph 115(1.1)(d) of
  the Act assuming the following, the rates calculated would be as close
  as possible to the corresponding first additional contribution rates
  for self-employed persons, excluding any past temporary increases in
  those rates resulting from the operation of these Regulations:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} benefits accompanied by temporary increases in the
    first additional contribution rates and second additional
    contribution rates calculated under subparagraphs 115(1.1)(d)(ii)
    and (e)(ii) of the Act, respectively, are to be excluded;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} S4 is to apply to a period of six years, starting
    with the year after the review period;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} benefits that become payable after the review
    period are to be decreased for the year in which they become payable
    in accordance with section 14, using the assumptions set out in the
    most recent report prepared for the purpose of section 115 of the
    Act; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for each year of the period to which S4 applies,
    benefits that became payable before that year are to be adjusted by
    multiplying them not by the ratio referred to in paragraph 45(2)(b)
    and subparagraphs 56(2)(c)(ii), 58(1.1)(b)(ii) and 59(c)(ii) of the
    Act, but by the value determined by the formula

    ::: {#1284485 .Paragraph}
    (1 + S4) × (PI~t~ / PI~t--1~) -- S4

    where

    [S4]{.dfn}
    :   is a multiple of 0.01 between -0.4 and 0,

    [PI~t~]{.dfn}
    :   is the Pension Index for that year, based on the assumption of
        future inflation set out in the most recent report prepared for
        the purpose of section 115 of the Act, and

    [PI~t--1~]{.dfn}
    :   is the Pension Index for the year before that year, based on the
        assumption of future inflation set out in the most recent report
        prepared for the purpose of section 115 of the Act.
    :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed change to contribution rates

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the rates calculated under subsection (1) exceed
  the corresponding first additional contribution rates for
  self-employed persons, excluding any past temporary increases in those
  rates resulting from the operation of these Regulations, by more than
  0.0001,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the first additional contribution rates for
    self-employed persons are deemed to equal the corresponding rates
    calculated under subsection (1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the first additional contribution rates for
    employees and employers are deemed to equal the corresponding rates
    referred to in paragraph (a), divided by two; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the second additional contribution rates are deemed
    to equal the new corresponding first additional contribution rates
    multiplied by the additional contribution rate ratio.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed temporary change to contribution
  rates

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For each year for which the first additional
  contribution rate calculated under subparagraph 115(1.1)(d)(ii) of the
  Act would include a temporary increase if benefits were determined in
  accordance with sections 14 and 15 and subsection 5(4) of the
  [Calculation of Contribution Rates Regulations,
  2021](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-5) did not apply,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the first additional contribution rate for
    self-employed persons is deemed to equal the sum of the first
    additional contribution rate for self-employed persons for that
    year, as adjusted in accordance with subsection (2), if applicable,
    and the amount of any temporary increase, rounded to the nearest
    multiple of 0.01, that would apply to that year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the first additional contribution rate for
    employees and employers is deemed to equal the rate calculated under
    paragraph (a), divided by two; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the second additional contribution rates are deemed
    to equal the new corresponding first additional contribution rates
    multiplied by the additional contribution rate ratio.
