## [Prescription]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1011484 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The territory where the
customs laws of Israel are applied in accordance with Article III of the
Protocol on Economic Relations set out in Annex V to the
Israeli-Palestinian Interim Agreement on the West Bank and the Gaza
Strip, dated September 28, 1995, as that Protocol is amended from time
to time, is a prescribed territory for the purposes of the definition
[[country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1011486 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_IndB9FF_hq_16387){#fn_IndB9FF_hq_16387-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 41 of the [Canada-Israel Free Trade Agreement Implementation
Act](/eng/acts/C-6.4), being chapter 33 of the Statutes of Canada, 1996,
comes into force.
