## [Remissions and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1375573 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fingerprinting fees

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
fingerprinting fees paid or payable under section 2 of the Royal
Canadian Mounted Police, Fingerprinting for Visa, Licensing or Security
Clearance Purposes Fee Regulations if the following conditions are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person for whom the fingerprinting request is
  made is an Indigenous person who, as a result of the residential
  school system, underwent an imposed name change in Canada or is a
  descendant of such a person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} they are seeking to change their name from the
  imposed name to a name that they wish to reclaim; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fingerprinting request

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is made in writing during the period beginning on
    the day on which this Order comes into force and ending on the fifth
    anniversary of that day, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} sets out the imposed name and the name that is to
    be reclaimed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criminal record verification fees

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
criminal record verification fees paid or payable under section 2 of the
[Royal Canadian Mounted Police, Criminal Record Verification for Civil
Purposes Fee Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-485) if the following
conditions are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person for whom the criminal record verification
  request is made is an Indigenous person who, as a result of the
  residential school system, underwent an imposed name change in Canada
  or is a descendant of such a person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} they are seeking to change their name from the
  imposed name to a name that they wish to reclaim; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the criminal record verification request

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is made in writing during the period beginning on
    the day on which this Order comes into force and ending on the fifth
    anniversary of that day, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} sets out the imposed name and the name that is to
    be reclaimed.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1375588 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
