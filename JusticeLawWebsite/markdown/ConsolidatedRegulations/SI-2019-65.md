## Order Designating the Minister of Natural Resources, a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of the two Acts {#order-designating-the-minister-of-natural-resources-a-member-of-the-queens-privy-council-for-canada-as-the-minister-for-the-purposes-of-the-two-acts .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2019-65
:::

[CANADIAN ENERGY REGULATOR ACT](/eng/acts/C-15.1)

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2019-07-24

Order Designating the Minister of Natural Resources, a member of the
Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of
the two Acts

P.C. 2019-1054 2019-07-09
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 8 of the [Canadian Energy
Regulator Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1861_hq_25291){#fn_81000-3-1861_hq_25291-ID0EICDA}
and pursuant to paragraph (c.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-1861_hq_25292){#fn_81000-3-1861_hq_25292-ID0EGCDA}
of the definition [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
section 2 of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-1861_hq_25293){#fn_81000-3-1861_hq_25293-ID0EBCDA},

- [a)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of Natural Resources, a member
  of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the
  purposes of the [Canadian Energy Regulator
  Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1861_hq_25291){#fn_81000-3-1861_hq_25291-ID0EBABDA};
  and

- [b)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of Natural Resources as the
  appropriate Minister with respect to the Canadian Energy Regulator for
  the purposes of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-1861_hq_25293){#fn_81000-3-1861_hq_25293-ID0EBAADA}.
