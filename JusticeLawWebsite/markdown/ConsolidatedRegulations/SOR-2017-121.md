## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-839374 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[active fleet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[active fleet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all of the locomotives
      being used by a railway company for its railway operations in
      Canada. ([parc de locomotives actives]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[CFR]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[CFR]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Part 1033, or any other part
      that is specified, of Title 40 of the Code of Federal Regulations
      of the United States, as amended from time to
      time. ([CFR]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[crankcase emissions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[crankcase emissions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means substances
      emitted into the atmosphere from any portion of the ventilation or
      lubrication system of a locomotive crankcase. The crankcase is the
      housing for the crankshaft and other related internal
      parts. ([émissions du carter]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[EPA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[EPA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the United States Environmental
      Protection Agency. ([EPA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[EPA certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[EPA certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a certificate of
      conformity to United States federal standards that is issued by
      the EPA under the CFR. ([certificat de l'EPA]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[exhaust emissions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[exhaust emissions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means substances emitted
      into the atmosphere from any opening that is downstream from the
      exhaust port or exhaust valve of a locomotive engine. ([émissions
      de gaz d'échappement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[family emission limit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[family emission limit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the maximum
      level of nitrogen oxides or particulate matter, or the maximum
      combined level of nitrogen oxides and hydrocarbons, that a
      locomotive covered by an EPA certificate issued under Part 1033 or
      Part 92 of the CFR, as applicable, and belonging to an engine
      family may emit under paragraph 4(5)(b). The family emission limit
      is determined by the manufacturer or remanufacturer of the
      locomotive in accordance with Subpart H of the CFR or Subpart D of
      Part 92 of the CFR, as applicable, and is set out in the EPA
      certificate issued under Part 1033 or Part 92 of the CFR, as
      applicable. ([limite d'émissions de la famille de
      moteurs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[freshly manufactured locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[freshly manufactured locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      locomotive that contains less than 25 percent previously used
      parts. ([locomotive nouvellement construite]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[incidental operations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[incidental operations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the operation
      of locomotives by a railway company in the case where

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the locomotives cross the border between Canada
        and the United States;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the company's locomotives that are operated in
        Canada operate for a distance of not more than 40.23 km (25
        miles) in one direction on a line of railway inside Canada; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the calendar year preceding any day on which
        the conditions set out in paragraphs (a) and (b) are met, the
        operating time of the company's locomotives in Canada
        represented an average of less than five percent of the total
        operating time of all of its locomotives. ([exploitation
        accessoire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[line-haul locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[line-haul locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a locomotive
      that has a total rated power of more than 1 715 kW (2,300
      horsepower). ([locomotive de ligne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a self-propelled piece of
      on-track railway equipment that is designed for moving or
      propelling railway cars that are designed to carry freight, other
      equipment, or passengers, but that itself is not designed to carry
      freight, other equipment, or passengers other than those operating
      the locomotive. It does not include railway equipment that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is constructed for movement both on and off
        lines of railway;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is powered by a steam engine;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is powered only by an external source of
        electricity;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is designed primarily for use in maintenance,
        construction, the post-accident recovery of equipment, repairs
        or other similar uses;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} has a total rated power of less than 750 kW
        (1,006 horsepower); or

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} is operated solely for the purposes of
        exhibition, demonstration, evaluation or
        testing. ([locomotive]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[locomotive engine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[locomotive engine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an engine that
      propels a locomotive. ([moteur de locomotive]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[new locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[new locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a freshly manufactured locomotive;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a remanufactured locomotive;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an upgraded locomotive; or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an imported locomotive. ([locomotive
        nouvelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[particulate matter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[particulate matter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means respirable
      particulate matter with a diameter of less than or equal to 10
      microns. ([particules]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[percent previously used parts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[percent previously used parts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      percentage, calculated in accordance with paragraph 1033.640(c) of
      the CFR, that represents the total dollar value of the previously
      used parts of the locomotive relative to the total dollar value of
      the unused parts and previously used parts of the locomotive. ([%
      de pièces usagées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[permanently removed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[permanently removed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
      locomotive, means removed from the total fleet as a result of
      having been scrapped, sold or destroyed. ([retirée de façon
      permanente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[place into service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[place into service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to place a new
      locomotive into initial operation or to permit the placement of a
      new locomotive into initial operation. ([mettre en
      service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[power assembly]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[power assembly]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
      locomotive engine, a module in which the combustion of fuel
      occurs, which consists of the cylinder, the piston and piston
      rings, the valves and ports for the admission of charge air and
      the discharge of exhaust gases, the fuel injection components and
      controls, and the cylinder head and its associated
      components. ([ensemble de puissance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[refurbished locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[refurbished locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a locomotive
      that contains less than 50 percent previously used
      parts. ([locomotive remise à neuf]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[remanufactured locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[remanufactured locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      locomotive that was originally manufactured after December 31,
      1972, that contains more than 25 percent previously used parts and
      that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has had, whether during a single maintenance
        event or cumulatively within a five-year period, each power
        assembly

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} replaced, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} inspected and qualified for use;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has been converted to enable it to operate
        using a fuel other than a fuel it was originally manufactured to
        use;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is a repowered locomotive; or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is a previously used locomotive into which a
        remanufactured locomotive engine is installed. ([locomotive
        reconstruite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[remanufactured locomotive engine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[remanufactured locomotive engine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      locomotive engine that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has had, whether during a single maintenance
        event or cumulatively within a five-year period, each power
        assembly

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} replaced, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} inspected and qualified for use;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has been converted to enable it to operate
        using a fuel other than a fuel it was originally manufactured to
        use; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a locomotive engine that does
        not contain power assemblies, has been repaired to a condition
        that is equivalent to or better than its original condition with
        respect to reliability and fuel consumption. ([moteur de
        locomotive reconstruit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[repowered locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[repowered locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a previously
      used locomotive whose engine has been replaced with a locomotive
      engine that has never been remanufactured and that has never been
      installed in a locomotive. ([locomotive
      réalimentée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[service life]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[service life]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the total life of a
      locomotive. Service life begins when the locomotive is originally
      manufactured and ends when the locomotive is permanently removed
      from service. ([durée de service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[switch locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[switch locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a locomotive that
      has a total rated power of 1 715 kW (2,300 horsepower) or
      less. ([locomotive de manoeuvres]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[tier of standards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[tier of standards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the tier of
      standards set out in Tables 1 to 3 to section 1033.101 of the CFR
      that applies in respect of a locomotive according to its year of
      original manufacture. ([niveau des normes]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[total fleet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[total fleet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the active fleet of a
      railway company plus all of the locomotives that it has put in
      storage in Canada. ([parc total de locomotives]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[upgraded locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[upgraded locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a locomotive
      that was originally manufactured before January 1, 1973 and that
      is

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a repowered locomotive; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a refurbished locomotive that contains more
        than 25 percent previously used parts. ([locomotive mise à
        niveau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[useful life]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[useful life]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period during which
      an emission standard applies in respect of a locomotive or
      locomotive engine, as determined in accordance with paragraph
      1033.101(g) of the CFR. ([durée de vie utile]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[year of original manufacture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[year of original manufacture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      calendar year of the date of original manufacture of a locomotive,
      as determined in accordance with subsection (3). ([année de
      construction initiale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Total rated power

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The total rated power of a locomotive is equal

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a locomotive propelled by only one
    engine, to its rated power, which is determined in accordance with
    the provisions of section 1033.140 of the CFR; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a locomotive propelled by more than
    one engine, to the sum of the rated power of each of its engines,
    including any permanently installed auxiliary engine that can be
    operated while the main propulsion engine is operating but not
    including an auxiliary engine that operates only to reduce the
    idling time of the main propulsion engine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of original manufacture

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The date of original manufacture of a locomotive is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a locomotive that has been assembled
    only once, the date on which the assembly was completed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a switch locomotive that has been
    assembled more than once, the date of completion of the most recent
    assembly of the locomotive that resulted in the locomotive
    containing less than 25 percent previously used parts;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a line-haul locomotive that has been
    assembled more than once, the date of completion of the most recent
    assembly of the locomotive that resulted in the locomotive
    containing less than 50 percent previously used parts; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an upgraded locomotive, January 1,
    1973.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-839443 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection of environment

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of these
Regulations is to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} reduce emissions of nitrogen oxides, particulate
  matter, hydrocarbons, carbon monoxide and smoke resulting from the
  operation of locomotives; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish emission standards and test procedures for
  locomotives.

## [Emission Standards, Testing and Labelling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-839448 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-839449 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application --- sections 4 to 9

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) to (5), sections 4 to 9 apply in respect of a new
  locomotive for the duration of its useful life if the locomotive is
  placed into service on or after the day on which these Regulations
  come into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- locomotive covered by EPA
  certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a new locomotive placed into service on or after
  the day on which these Regulations come into force is covered by an
  EPA certificate that sets out family emission limits, section 4
  applies in respect of the locomotive for the duration of its service
  life.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- remanufactured locomotive

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 4 to 9 do not apply in respect of a new
  locomotive placed into service on or after the day on which these
  Regulations come into force if the locomotive

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a remanufactured locomotive;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is operated by a railway company that has realized
    gross revenues of \$30 million or less for the provision of rail
    transportation services in Canada in each of the two calendar years
    before the year in which the locomotive is placed into service;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is not covered by an EPA certificate; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is operated for the provision of freight rail
    transportation services or tourist and excursion rail transportation
    services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- nonroad engines

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Sections 4 to 9 do not apply in respect of a new
  locomotive that is a switch locomotive placed into service on or after
  the day on which these Regulations come into force if the locomotive

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is equipped with a nonroad engine within the
    meaning of the definition of [[nonroad]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
    section 1033.901 of the CFR;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is covered by an EPA certificate issued under Part
    89 or Part 1039 of the CFR, as applicable; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is affixed with the EPA locomotive label set out in
    paragraph 1033.150(e)(4) of the CFR and the EPA engine label set out
    in section 1039.135 of Part 1039 of the CFR or section 89.110 of
    Part 89 of the CFR, as applicable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- incidental operations

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Sections 4 to 9 do not apply in respect of a new
  locomotive placed into service on or after the day on which these
  Regulations come into force if the locomotive is operated by a railway
  company as part of incidental operations.

### [Emission Standards]{.HTitleText2} {#h-839468 .Subheading}

#### [Exhaust Emissions]{.HTitleText3} {#h-839469 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- placing into service

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company must not place into service a locomotive unless, when
  tested in accordance with section 8, the locomotive conforms to the
  applicable exhaust emission standards set out in Tables 1 and 2 to
  section 1033.101 of the CFR and in paragraph 1033.101(f)(1) of the
  CFR.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternate standards --- carbon monoxide
  and particulate matter

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), when tested in accordance
  with section 8, the locomotive placed into service by the railway
  company may conform to the alternate carbon monoxide and particulate
  matter emissions standards set out in paragraph 1033.101(i) of the CFR
  instead of the applicable carbon monoxide and particulate matter
  emission standards set out in Tables 1 and 2 to section 1033.101 of
  the CFR.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- EPA certificate and
  labels

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply in respect of a
  locomotive that is covered by an EPA certificate and that is affixed
  with the EPA locomotive label and EPA locomotive engine label that are
  set out in section 1033.135 of the CFR.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- operating without EPA
  certificate or with EPA certificate that does not set out family
  emission limits

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a locomotive is not covered by an EPA certificate
  or is covered by an EPA certificate but one that does not set out
  family emission limits, a railway company must not operate the
  locomotive after the locomotive has been placed into service unless,
  when tested in accordance with section 8, the locomotive conforms to
  the applicable exhaust emission standards set out in Tables 1 and 2 to
  section 1033.101 of the CFR and in paragraph 1033.101(f)(1) of the
  CFR.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- operating with EPA
  certificate that sets out family emission limits

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a locomotive is covered by an EPA certificate that
  sets out family emission limits, a railway company must not operate
  the locomotive after the locomotive has been placed into service
  unless, when tested in accordance with section 8, the locomotive
  conforms to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicable exhaust emission standards set out
    in Tables 1 and 2 to section 1033.101 of the CFR and in paragraph
    1033.101(f)(1) of the CFR for which there are no family emission
    limits; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the family emission limits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternate standards --- carbon monoxide
  and particulate matter

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (4) or (5), when tested in
  accordance with section 8, a locomotive referred to in subsection (4)
  or (5) may conform to the alternate carbon monoxide and particulate
  matter emissions standards set out in paragraph 1033.101(i) of the CFR
  instead of the applicable carbon monoxide and particulate matter
  emission standards set out in Tables 1 and 2 to section 1033.101 of
  the CFR.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Footnotes

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, footnotes d and e
  to Table 1 to section 1033.101 of the CFR and footnotes b and c to
  Table 2 to that section do not apply.

#### [Smoke Emissions]{.HTitleText3} {#h-839487 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- placing into service

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company must not place into service a locomotive unless, when
  tested in accordance with section 8, the locomotive conforms to the
  smoke standards set out in Table 3 to section 1033.101 of the CFR that
  correspond to the locomotive's tier of standards.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions to subsection (1)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a
  locomotive that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is covered by an EPA certificate and is affixed
    with the EPA locomotive label and EPA locomotive engine label that
    are set out in section 1033.135 of the CFR; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} emits 0.05 grams per brake horsepower-hour of
    particulate matter or less.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- operating

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A railway company must not operate a locomotive after
  the locomotive has been placed into service unless, when tested in
  accordance with section 8, the locomotive conforms to the applicable
  smoke standards set out in Table 3 to section 1033.101 of the CFR.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception to subsection (3)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (3) does not apply in respect of a
  locomotive that emits 0.05 grams per brake horsepower-hour of
  particulate matter or less.

#### [Crankcase Emissions]{.HTitleText3} {#h-839499 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- crankcase emissions

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company must not operate a locomotive that releases crankcase
  emissions directly into the atmosphere.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- testing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a
  locomotive in the case where crankcase emissions are added physically
  or mathematically to the exhaust emissions when tested in accordance
  with section 8 and with paragraph 1033.115(a)(1) of the CFR.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- treated crankcase
  emissions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When crankcase emissions are routed to the exhaust
  upstream of the exhaust aftertreatment, the release is deemed not to
  be a direct release into the atmosphere.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- EPA certificate and
  labels

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) to (3) do not apply in respect of a
  locomotive that is covered by an EPA certificate and that is affixed
  with the EPA locomotive label and EPA locomotive engine label that are
  set out in section 1033.135 of the CFR.

### [Testing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-839509 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial testing

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company must test a locomotive in accordance with section 8
  before placing the locomotive into service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a
  locomotive that is covered by an EPA certificate and is affixed with
  the EPA locomotive label and EPA locomotive engine label that are set
  out in section 1033.135 of the CFR.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}In-use testing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In every calendar year beginning with the 2019
  calendar year, a railway company that has realized gross revenues of
  \$250 million or more for the provision of rail transportation
  services in Canada in each of the two preceding calendar years must
  test, in accordance with section 8, a sample of locomotives that is
  determined in accordance with subsections (4) to (6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}In-use test sample characteristics

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Each locomotive in the sample referred to in
  subsection (3) must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be operated in the provision of freight rail
    transportation services;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} not have been tested in the previous calendar year;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be at more than 50 percent of its useful life at
    the time of testing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}In-use test sample size

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The number of locomotives that the railway company
  must test under subsection (3) is the number determined by multiplying
  0.00075 by the number of locomotives that meet all of the following
  conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they were part of the active fleet of the railway
    company on December 31 of the previous calendar year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they are subject to the exhaust emissions standards
    referred to in section 4;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} they have the characteristics set out in paragraphs
    (4)(a) to (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rounding up

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the multiplication set out in subsection (5)
  results in a product that is a fraction, the product is to be rounded
  up to the next whole number.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Testing required by Minister

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may require a railway company to test
  any locomotive that it operates, if the Minister determines that it is
  necessary to do so in order to ascertain whether the locomotive
  conforms to section 4 or 5.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Test procedures

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A locomotive tested for
conformity to section 4 or 5 must be tested in accordance with the test
procedures set out in Subpart F of the CFR.

### [Labelling]{.HTitleText2} {#h-839533 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Locomotive engine label and locomotive
label

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) to (4), a railway company must not operate a
  locomotive unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the locomotive engine or locomotive engines are
    affixed with a label containing the following information:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the heading "Engine Emission Control
      Information", the heading "[Renseignements sur le contrôle des
      émissions du moteur]{.otherLang lang="fr"}" or a bilingual heading
      consisting of both headings,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the full corporate name of the locomotive engine
      manufacturer or remanufacturer, as the case may be,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a statement that identifies the tier of
      standards to which the locomotive must conform and that indicates
      whether the locomotive is a line-haul locomotive or a switch
      locomotive,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the exhaust emission standards to which the
      locomotive engine must conform under section 4, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the useful life of the locomotive; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the locomotive is affixed with a label containing
    the following information:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the heading "Original Locomotive Emission Control
      Information", the heading "[Renseignements sur le contrôle des
      émissions de la locomotive initiale]{.otherLang lang="fr"}" or a
      bilingual heading consisting of both headings,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the full corporate name of the locomotive
      manufacturer or remanufacturer, as the case may be,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a statement that identifies the tier of
      standards to which the locomotive must conform and that indicates
      whether the locomotive is a line-haul locomotive or a switch
      locomotive,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the exhaust emission standards to which the
      locomotive must conform under section 4, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} date of original manufacture of the locomotive.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception to subsection (1)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a
  locomotive covered by an EPA certificate and affixed with the EPA
  locomotive label and EPA locomotive engine label that are set out in
  section 1033.135 of the CFR.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional label for refurbished
  locomotives

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the labels required by subsection (1),
  a refurbished locomotive must be affixed with a label containing the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the heading "Refurbished Locomotive Emission
    Control Information", the heading "[Renseignements sur le contrôle
    des émissions de la locomotive remise à neuf]{.otherLang lang="fr"}"
    or a bilingual heading consisting of both headings;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement that identifies the tier of standards
    to which the locomotive must conform and that indicates whether the
    locomotive is a line-haul locomotive or a switch locomotive; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the exhaust emission standards to which the
    locomotive must conform under section 4.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception to subsection (3)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (3) does not apply in respect of a
  refurbished locomotive covered by an EPA certificate and affixed with
  the EPA locomotive label set out in paragraph 1033.640(f)(3) of the
  CFR.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Labelling

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A label referred to in subsection (1) or (3) must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a label referred to in paragraph
    (1)(a),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} be durable throughout the useful life of the
      engine,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} be legible and affixed to the engine in a
      position in which it will remain readily visible after the engine
      is installed in the locomotive,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} be affixed to an engine part that is necessary
      for the normal operation of the engine and that does not normally
      require replacement during the useful life of the locomotive,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} not be affixed to any equipment that is easily
      detached from the engine,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} be affixed so that it cannot be removed without
      being destroyed or defaced,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} be made up of one piece or, if all the pieces
      are permanently affixed to the same engine part, more than one
      piece, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} be lettered in a colour that contrasts with the
      background colour of the label; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a label referred to in paragraph
    (1)(b) or subsection (3),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} be permanently affixed to the locomotive,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} be legible and affixed to the locomotive in a
      position in which it will remain readily visible,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} be affixed to a locomotive chassis part that is
      necessary for the normal operation of the locomotive and that does
      not normally require replacement during the service life of the
      locomotive,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} not be affixed to the engine or to any equipment
      that is easily detached from the locomotive,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} be affixed so that it cannot be removed without
      being destroyed or defaced,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} be made up of one piece except in the case of a
      locomotive whose tier of standards is 0, in which case it may be
      made up of more than one piece, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} be lettered in a colour that contrasts with the
      background colour of the label.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Language

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A label referred to in subsection (1) or (3) must be
  worded in English, French or both.

## [Idling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-839578 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- idling

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a railway
  company must ensure that the locomotives in its active fleet do not
  idle for more than 30 minutes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A locomotive may idle for more than 30 minutes to the
  extent that is necessary to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prevent locomotive engine damage, such as damage
    resulting from the freezing of the engine coolant;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintain air pressure for the brakes or the starter
    system;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} recharge the locomotive battery;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} heat or cool the cab, if the heating or cooling is
    necessary for reasons of health and safety;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide head end power, if necessary for reasons of
    passenger health and safety;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} perform diagnostic testing and necessary
    maintenance; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} respond to an emergency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Anti-idling policy

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A railway company must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a written anti-idling policy that reflects the
    railway company's commitment to reducing locomotive idling;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} submit the policy to the Minister together with, as
    the case may be, the initial report filed under section 13 or the
    incidental operations report filed under section 16;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if an amendment is made to the policy, submit a
    copy of the amended version to the Minister

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} together with the annual report filed under
      section 14, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} within 45 days after the end of each calendar
      year, if the company operates its locomotives in Canada as part of
      incidental operations; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} keep a copy of the policy that was submitted in
    accordance with paragraph (b) and, if it was amended, a copy of the
    amended version.

## [Records and Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-839599 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application --- exception

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Sections 12 to 15 do not
apply to a railway company that operates its locomotives in Canada as
part of incidental operations.

### [Contents of Record]{.HTitleText2} {#h-839602 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of record --- total fleet

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company must keep, for each locomotive in its total fleet, a
  record containing the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating whether the locomotive is a
    line-haul locomotive or a switch locomotive;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating the type of rail
    transportation service for which the locomotive is primarily
    operated;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date of original manufacture;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the locomotive's tier of standards;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the locomotive identification number;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the name of the locomotive manufacturer or
    remanufacturer, as the case may be;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the model of the locomotive;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the name of the locomotive engine manufacturer or
    remanufacturer, as the case may be; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the model of the locomotive engine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information --- active fleet

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the information set out in subsection
  (1), for each of the locomotives in its active fleet, a railway
  company must include in the locomotive's record the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the locomotive engine or
    locomotive engines, including the total rated power, the number of
    cylinders and the type of fuel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the locomotive's annual fuel consumption by type of
    fuel or, in the absence of that information, the annual fuel
    consumption of the active fleet by type of rail transportation
    service and type of fuel; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the useful life of the locomotive.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information --- new
  locomotives covered by EPA certificates

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the information set out in
  subsections (1) and (2), for each new locomotive in its active fleet
  that is covered by an EPA certificate and to which the exhaust
  emission standards referred to in section 4 apply, a railway company
  must include in the locomotive's record the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the EPA engine family number applicable to the
    locomotive;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement as to whether the locomotive is an
    imported locomotive, a freshly manufactured locomotive, a
    refurbished locomotive, a remanufactured locomotive or an upgraded
    locomotive, or any combination of these;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the locomotive was placed into
    service; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any maintenance performed on the locomotive that
    substantially affects its emissions or that is likely to
    substantially affect its emissions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information --- new
  locomotives not covered by EPA certificates

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the information set out in
  subsections (1) and (2), for each new locomotive in its active fleet
  that is not covered by an EPA certificate and to which the exhaust
  emission standards referred to in section 4 apply, a railway company
  must include in the locomotive's record the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the locomotive's anti-idling
    technology;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the locomotive's crankcase
    emissions handling;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the basic engine design;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the emission control equipment and
    related components and an explanation of the operation of the
    emission control system;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the results of all emissions testing;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of each emissions test;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the locomotive's hours of operation at the time of
    each test;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating whether the locomotive is an
    imported locomotive, a freshly manufactured locomotive, a
    refurbished locomotive, a remanufactured locomotive or an upgraded
    locomotive, or any combination of these;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the locomotive was placed into
    service; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} any maintenance performed on the locomotive that
    substantially affects its emissions or that is likely to
    substantially affect its emissions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information --- locomotives
  permanently removed from total fleet

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the information set out in
  subsections (1) to (4), as applicable, for each locomotive that is
  permanently removed from its total fleet, a railway company must
  include in the locomotive's record the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of its removal; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for its removal.

### [Initial Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-839642 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of initial report

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company must file with the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a company created on or before the
    day on which these Regulations come into force, a report with
    respect to all of the locomotives in its total fleet, as the fleet
    existed on that day, within 90 days after that day; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a company created after the day on
    which these Regulations come into force, a report with respect to
    each locomotive in its total fleet, as the fleet existed on the day
    on which the company was created, within 90 days after the day of
    its creation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of initial report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the railway company, the address of its
    head office and, if different, its mailing address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the address of the department within the railway
    company responsible for maintaining the records required under
    section 12;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name, telephone number and email address of the
    contact person for the railway company; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for each locomotive in its total fleet, the
    information set out in subsection 12(1).

### [Annual Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-839654 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of annual report

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company must file with the Minister, within 45 days after the
  end of each calendar year beginning after the end of 2018, a report
  with respect to each locomotive in its active fleet, as the fleet
  existed on December 31 of the calendar year, and each locomotive
  permanently removed from its total fleet in the calendar year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of annual report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the railway company, the address of its
    head office and, if different, its mailing address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the address of the department within the railway
    company responsible for maintaining the records required under
    section 12;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name, telephone number and email address of the
    contact person for the railway company;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for each locomotive in its active fleet, the
    information set out in subsections 12(1) and (2);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a new locomotive in its active fleet
    to which the exhaust emission standards set out in section 4 apply,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the information set out in paragraphs 12(3)(a) to
      (c), if the locomotive is covered by an EPA certificate, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the information set out in paragraphs 12(4)(a)
      to (i), if the locomotive is not covered by an EPA certificate;
      and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a locomotive permanently removed
    from the total fleet during the calendar year, the information set
    out in subsections 12(1) and (5).

### [In-use Test Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-839668 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of in-use test report

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company must file with the Minister, within 90 days after the
  end of each calendar year, a report with respect to each locomotive
  tested under subsection 7(3) in that calendar year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of in-use test report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the railway company, the address of its
    head office and, if different, its mailing address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the address of the department within the railway
    company responsible for maintaining the records required under
    section 12;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name, telephone number and email address of the
    contact person for the railway company; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the information set out in subsection 12(1) and
    paragraphs 12(4)(e) to (g).

### [Incidental Operations Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-839678 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of incidental operations report

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company that operates its locomotives in Canada as part of
  incidental operations must file with the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a company created on or before the
    day on which these Regulations come into force, a report confirming
    the incidental operations existing on that day, within 90 days after
    that day;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a company created after the day on
    which these Regulations come into force, a report confirming the
    incidental operations existing on the day on which the company is
    created, within 90 days after the day of its creation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a company, other than a company
    referred to in paragraph (b), that begins incidental operations
    after the day on which these Regulations come into force, a report
    confirming the incidental operations and the day on which those
    operations began, within 90 days after that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of incidental operations
  report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the railway company, the address of its
    head office and, if different, its mailing address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the address of the department within the railway
    company responsible for keeping the copies of the anti-idling policy
    referred to in subsection 10(3);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name, telephone number and email address of the
    contact person for the railway company; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement from the railway company that it
    operates its locomotives in Canada as part of incidental operations.

### [Report Certification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-839691 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certification

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Each report referred to
in sections 13 to 16 must include a certification, by the person
responsible for the oversight of the railway company's locomotive
emissions program or by a person authorized to act on that person's
behalf, that all the information contained in the report is accurate.

### [Keeping of Records and Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-839694 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records --- duration

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company must keep every record referred to in section 12 for
  at least four years after the day on which the locomotive is
  permanently removed from its total fleet.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports --- duration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A railway company must keep every report referred to
  in sections 13 to 16 for at least eight years after the day on which
  it is filed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records and reports --- form

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A railway company must keep every record and report
  in written form or in a readily readable electronic or optical form.
