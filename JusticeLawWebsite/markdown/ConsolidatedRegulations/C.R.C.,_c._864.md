Regulations Made Under the Fisheries Improvement Loans Act
::::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567151 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Fisheries Improvement Loans
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._864).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567153 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Fisheries Improvement Loans
    Act](/eng/acts/F-22); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[averaging period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[averaging period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/78-139, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[insured fishing vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insured fishing vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a fishing
    vessel that is insured under or pursuant to the Fishing Vessel
    Insurance Regulations or that is otherwise insured so that the
    indemnity, in the event of total or partial loss, will be
    approximately equal to the indemnity that would be paid in similar
    circumstances if the fishing vessel had been insured under or
    pursuant to those Regulations; ([bâtiment de pêche
    assuré]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[interest period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[interest period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/78-139, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a motor vehicle other than a
    passenger car. ([véhicule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-139, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the Act
and these Regulations,

[[addition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[addition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the addition to a building
    and includes the

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} addition of one or more rooms or storeys,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} construction of an attached garage, ice house or
      outbuilding, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} installation and construction of foundations and
      basements for a building and the purchase of materials for any
      such purpose; ([rajout]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[alteration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[alteration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the structural alterations
    to the exterior or interior of any building or structure that are
    designed to improve, modernize or increase the usefulness of any
    building or structure and includes the

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} purchase of material for any such purpose,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} relocation of any equipment, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} alteration of plumbing, heating, ventilating,
      water supply or air conditioning systems or parts thereof;
      ([modification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hull]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hull]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the body of the vessel;
    ([coque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[major]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[major]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to repair and
    overhaul, a cost of not less than \$400 as estimated by the
    borrower; ([majeurs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[principal occupation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[principal occupation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the activity in
    which a person engages or intends to engage during a fishing season
    and as a result of which he gains or intends to gain all or part of
    his livelihood; ([occupation principale]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[repair]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[repair]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the repair and the material
    required for the repair of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any type of water supply system,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a shore installation,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fishing equipment,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a fishing vessel, or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any building or structure

    for use in connection with a primary fishing enterprise and includes
    the painting of any such building or structure;
    ([réparation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[responsible officer of the lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[responsible officer of the lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manager or assistant manager of the lender or
      a branch thereof,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person for the time being acting as the
      manager or assistant manager of the lender or a branch thereof,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the credit committee of the lender or a branch
      thereof, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any person duly authorized by the lender to
      supervise the making of loans; ([fonctionnaire responsable du
      prêteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[superstructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[superstructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the equipment affixed
    to a hull including masts, rigging and rails, but does not include
    electronic equipment or the engine of a vessel.
    ([superstructure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph (a) of the definition [[fisherman]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
section 2 of the Act, a person has or intends to acquire a prescribed
interest in a fishing vessel or a proprietary share in a fishing vessel
if he has or intends to acquire an equitable or legal interest therein.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph (b) of the definition [[fisherman]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
section 2 of the Act, a person has a prescribed interest in a weir or
similar fish catching or trapping device that is affixed to the ground,
or a proprietary share in such a weir or similar fish catching or
trapping device, if he has

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an equitable or legal interest in the weir or device;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a subsisting licence, where required, issued to him
  by a competent authority to use the weir or device in its present
  location or in a proposed location.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph b) of the definition [[primary fishing
enterprise]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the Act, the
processing of fish that may be carried on in any area is the preparation
of fish or the making of fishery products to the extent that the
preparation or making is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} normally carried out by fishermen in that particular
  area; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} carried out for the purpose of making fish into a
  saleable product to meet a particular marketing situation.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the
definition [[shore installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of
the Act, the class or kind of shore installations are structures,
appliances or machinery, including piers, skidways, stages or wharves of
any class or kind that are affixed to the ground and used in connection
with a primary fishing enterprise.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567195 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
a guaranteed loan authorized under the Act.

## [Classes or Kinds of Fishing Equipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567197 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The following are the
classes or kinds of fishing equipment prescribed for the purpose of the
Act and these Regulations:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} tools, appliances and machines of any class or kind
  that are not normally affixed to real or immovable property;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} skiffs, dories, lifeboats, marine and other engines,
  hoists, winches, gurdies, anchors and electronic equipment for use on
  fishing vessels;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} vehicles, skids and toboggans for use in connection
  with a primary fishing enterprise or for carrying products of the sea;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} weirs, nets, seines, trawls, lines, traps and pots.

## [Development or Improvement of a Primary Fishing Enterprise]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567203 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subparagraph 3(a)(vi) of the Act, the development or improvement of a
primary fishing enterprise is the

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sinking, construction, installation, repair,
  alteration or improvement of any type of water supply system;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} repair, alteration or improvement of a structure,
  other than a building, if made in connection with a primary fishing
  enterprise; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} moving of a shore installation from a site to a new
  site and the installation of the shore installation on the new site.

## [Application for Loan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567208 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every applicant for a
loan shall submit to the lender a signed application, in the form set
out in Schedule I, containing the required information.

## [Purchase or Construction of Fishing Vessel Loans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567210 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the Act and
these Regulations, a purchase or construction of a fishing vessel loan
is a guaranteed loan if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fishing vessel in respect of which the loan is
  made is an insured fishing vessel;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the loan is,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a purchase of a fishing vessel, not
    greater than 90 per cent of the purchase price of the vessel in the
    relevant sales contract or in other written evidence of the sale,
    and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the construction of a fishing
    vessel

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} not greater than 90 per cent of the estimated
      cost of construction thereof as set out in the application
      referred to in section 11, or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} not greater than the cash expenditure incurred in
      the construction of the vessel where the vessel is constructed in
      whole or in part by the borrower; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the lender has required, in accordance with normal
  lending practices, and the borrower has provided evidence that the
  funds advanced in respect of the loan were expended for the purpose
  set out in the application for the loan.

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/78-139, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

## [Loan Terms and Revision of Loan Terms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567263 .Part}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Repayment of a loan shall be made in
  instalments that are payable at least annually but at the option of
  the lender repayments may be scheduled more frequently than annually.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to paragraph 3(e) of the Act, the terms for
  repayment of the loan shall be set out in the agreement in connection
  with the loan and the repayment period and the amount and frequency of
  instalment payments shall conform to the probable ability of the
  borrower to pay, having regard to the type of fishing enterprise
  carried on by him, the relevant marketing conditions, the repayment of
  other obligations and to any other relevant circumstances.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a borrower is in default in respect of the
  repayment of a loan or advises the lender that some of the terms of
  the agreement in connection with the loan are such that he may have to
  default, or where a borrower wishes to take out additional loans, and
  the lender is of the opinion that it would be appropriate in the light
  of the borrower's total repayment obligations to alter or revise the
  terms of the loan or any agreement in connection therewith, the lender
  may, with the approval of the borrower alter or revise the terms of
  the loan or agreement by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} extending the term for repayment of the loan within
    the maximum terms specified in the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} changing the amount of the periodic instalments; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} changing the periods between instalments, but in no
    case shall instalments be due less frequently than annually.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/78-139, s. 7\]]{.Repealed}

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The terms of a loan or any agreement in connection
  therewith may not be altered or revised in a manner that would result
  in longer terms for repayment of the loan than the terms prescribed by
  paragraph 3(e) of the Act or paragraph 13(d) of the Regulations unless
  the Minister approves the alteration or revision.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister approves the alteration or
  revision of the terms of a loan or any agreement in connection
  therewith in accordance with subsection (5), the alteration or
  revision shall not discharge the liability of the Minister to the
  lender under the Act.

::: HistoricalNote

## [Rate of Interest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567276 .Part}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), the
  maximum rate of interest per annum payable to a bank incorporated by
  or under the provisions of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) in respect
  of a loan is the aggregate of one per cent per annum and the prime
  lending rate in effect at that bank on the date the agreement in
  connection with the loan was signed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), the maximum rate of
  interest per annum payable to a bank other than a bank incorporated by
  or under the provisions of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) in respect
  of a loan shall be determined by the Minister for each month prior to
  the first day of that month and shall be the aggregate of one per cent
  per annum and the average of the prime lending rates in effect at the
  following chartered banks on the third Wednesday of the month
  preceding the month for which the rate is determined:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Bank of Montreal,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Bank of Nova Scotia,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Royal Bank of Canada,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Toronto-Dominion Bank,

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Banque Canadienne Nationale,

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the Banque Provinciale, and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Imperial Bank of Commerce

  rounded to the nearest one-eighth of one per cent or, if the result
  would be equidistant from two multiples of one-eighth of one per cent,
  to that multiple thereof that is the lower.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When the rate of interest determined by the Minister
  for a month pursuant to subsection (2) is a different rate from the
  rate in effect for the previous month, the Minister shall forthwith
  advise the banks to which subsection (2) applies of the new rate by
  such means as he sees fit.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The rate of interest payable to a bank in respect of
  a loan shall be revised

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a bank incorporated by or under the
    provisions of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), whenever the prime
    lending rate of that bank changes; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a bank other than a bank
    incorporated by or under the provisions of the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), on the first day of each month.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-139, s. 8
- SOR/79-507, s. 1

## [Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567294 .Part}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  lender shall, at the time of making a loan, take security for the
  repayment thereof

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} under section 88 of the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by way of a chattel mortgage or commercial pledge;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by way of a mortgage or hypothec on real property;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by way of an assignment of any rights and interest
    of the borrower under an agreement for sale; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} by way of a written undertaking by the borrower to
    give security as required under paragraph (a), (b), (c) or (d) as
    the case may be.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A responsible officer of a lender may, whenever he
  deems it appropriate, release any security taken pursuant to
  subsection (1), but every lender shall, throughout the term of a loan,
  maintain adequate security in accordance with normal banking practice
  in a manner prescribed by subsection (1) for the repayment of the
  balance of the loan outstanding at any time.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where, in the opinion of a responsible officer of a
  lender, security additional to the security taken pursuant to
  subsection (1) or (2) is deemed necessary, the lender may take such
  additional security for the repayment of a loan as the responsible
  officer considers appropriate.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every lender shall, at the time of making a loan,
  require the borrower to give to the lender, in addition to any
  security taken pursuant to subsection (1) or (2), a written promise to
  repay the loan signed by the borrower and setting forth the principal
  amount of the loan, the basis for establishing the rate of interest
  and the repayment terms.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-139, s. 8
- SOR/79-150, s. 1
:::

## [When Entire Amount Becomes Due and Payable]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567307 .Part}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a borrower is in default in respect of any payment on a loan, the
  entire amount of the balance outstanding on the loan shall, at the
  option of the lender, become due and payable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a borrower is convicted of an offence under the
  Act, the entire amount of the balance outstanding on a loan shall
  become due and payable.

## [Misrepresentation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567311 .Part}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Where a lender discovers
that any statement in an application for a loan is false in any material
respect or that a borrower has used or is using the proceeds of a loan
otherwise than for a purpose specified in the application for the loan,
the lender may take any action it considers proper in the circumstances
and shall immediately make a full report thereon to the Minister who may
request the lender to take such action or further action as the Minister
may require.

## [Procedure on Default]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567313 .Part}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a borrower is in default in respect of any payment on a loan and the
  entire amount of the balance outstanding on the loan becomes due and
  payable in accordance with section 20, the lender may take such
  action, whether by legal proceedings or otherwise, as it considers
  advisable in the circumstances to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} effect collection of the loan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} obtain additional security;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} realize upon any or all of the security it has
    taken; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} effect any compromise with or grant any concession
    to any person other than the borrower.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any action taken by the lender pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall not discharge the liability of the Minister to
  the lender under the Act.

## [Procedure for Claims]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567321 .Part}

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  claim for loss by a lender in respect of a loan shall not be made to
  the Minister until 90 days have elapsed after the entire amount of the
  loan becomes due and payable.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Where a loan has been in default for a period of 18
  months, a lender shall forthwith submit a claim for loss unless the
  lender has corresponded with the Minister in respect of the loan prior
  to the expiration of that period.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A claim for loss submitted by a lender in respect of
  a loan shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the unpaid principal amount of the loan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the uncollected earned interest outstanding at the
    time the claim is approved for payment by the Minister at the full
    rate of interest specified in the written promise to repay the loan
    for a maximum period of 180 days unless a longer period is justified
    for reasons beyond the control of the lender, and thereafter at
    one-half the rate of interest specified in the written promise to
    repay the loan;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any uncollected charge for insurance prescribed by
    these Regulations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any uncollected taxed costs for or incidental to
    legal proceedings in respect of the loan;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} legal fees, legal costs and legal disbursements,
    whether taxable or not, actually incurred by the lender, with or
    without litigation, in collecting or endeavouring to collect the
    loan or in protecting the interests of the Minister but only to the
    extent that the Deputy Minister of Justice taxes or allows; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other disbursement actually incurred by the
    lender in collecting or endeavouring to collect the loan or in
    protecting the interests of the Minister but only to the extent that
    the Minister allows.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A claim for loss by a lender in respect of a loan
  shall be submitted to the Minister by the lender together with a copy
  of the borrower's application form.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A claim for loss by a lender in respect of a loan
  shall be approved for payments by the Minister within 60 days after
  receipt by the Minister of the claim and shall thereupon be paid
  forthwith.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a claim for loss by a lender has been paid
  pursuant to subsection (4), the lender shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} execute a receipt in the form set out in Schedule
    II;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} send the receipt and the written promise to repay
    the loan by post to the Minister; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} deal with any security held by it for the loan in
    such manner as the Minister may direct.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Any expense incurred by the lender in dealing with
  any security pursuant to paragraph (5)(c) shall be paid by the
  Minister to the lender.

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  acting on behalf of the Minister, a lender shall, notwithstanding that
  its claim for loss in respect of a loan has been paid, take such
  reasonable steps as the Minister considers necessary to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} collect payments of principal and interest due by
    the borrower under the terms of the loan; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} realize upon any security taken under these
    Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any amount that is collected or realized pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall be remitted forthwith to the Minister.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any actual expenses of the lender incurred under
  subsection (1) shall be paid by the Minister to the lender.

## [Reports to Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567350 .Part}

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Any lender that has made
a loan under these Regulations shall furnish to the Minister such
reports or information as the Minister may from time to time require.

## [Registry]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567352 .Part}

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The registry established
by the Minister pursuant to the [Fisheries Improvement Loans
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._864), made by Order in Council
P.C. 1969-1266 June 25, 1969, for the purpose of recording loans under
the Act is hereby continued.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567354 .Part}

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  provision of these Regulations requiring security to be taken under
  section 88 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) shall be deemed to have
  been satisfied if a document purporting to give such security is
  signed and delivered, notwithstanding that the security is not
  effective in respect of some or all of the property to which the
  document relates by reason of any statute exempting property from
  seizure under writs of execution that was in force on September 1,
  1944.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a borrower

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has made a false material statement in any
    application that has been scrutinized and checked by a responsible
    officer of the lender with the care required of him by the lender in
    the conduct of its ordinary business, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has used the proceeds of any loan otherwise than
    for the purpose specified in the application for the loan,

  the liability of the Minister to the lender under the Act shall not
  for such reason be discharged to any extent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A lender may require a borrower to insure the fishing
  equipment referred to in paragraph 13(c) or the personal or immovable
  property referred to in paragraph 15(c) or 16(c) and assign the
  insurance to the lender.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a lender, under the terms of a loan or any
  agreement in connection therewith, pays the premiums under a policy of
  insurance on property under which any amount is or may become payable
  to the lender, the lender may charge the amount of the premiums to the
  borrower.
