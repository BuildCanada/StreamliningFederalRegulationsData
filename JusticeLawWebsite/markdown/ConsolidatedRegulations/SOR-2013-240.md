## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-802519 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Agri-Food Act, 2004, S.S.
    2004, c. A-15.21. ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan
    Forage Seed Development Commission constituted in accordance with
    the Act. ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[forage seed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[forage seed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all forage grasses and
    forage legumes that are produced in Saskatchewan, except alfalfa
    seed, turf and amenity, and reclamation species, and includes all
    grades of forage seed and all potential mixtures as determined in
    accordance with the Forage Seed Development Plan Regulations, R.R.S.
    c. A-15.21, Reg. 3. ([semences fourragères]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Supervisory Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Supervisory Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agri-Food
    Council continued by the Act. ([Organisme de
    surveillance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-802525 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board and the
Supervisory Board are authorized to regulate the marketing of forage
seed in interprovincial and export trade and, for that purpose, with
respect to persons and property situated within Saskatchewan, to
exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable by them in
relation to the marketing of forage seed locally within the province
under the Act.

## [Levies and Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-802527 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board and the
Supervisory Board, in relation to the powers granted to them by
section 2 with respect to interprovincial and export trade, are
authorized to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix, impose and collect levies or charges from
  persons within Saskatchewan who are engaged in the production or
  marketing of forage seed and, for those purposes, to classify those
  persons into groups and fix the levies or charges payable by the
  members of the different groups in different amounts; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the levies or charges for their purposes,
  including the creation of reserves, the payment of expenses and losses
  resulting from the sale or disposal of any forage seed and the
  equalization or adjustment among forage seed producers of money
  realized from the sale of forage seed during any period that they may
  determine.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-802531 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
