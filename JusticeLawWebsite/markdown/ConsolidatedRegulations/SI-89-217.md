## Minister of National Revenue Authority to Prescribe Fees Order {#minister-of-national-revenue-authority-to-prescribe-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/89-217
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1989-10-25

Minister of National Revenue Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

P.C. 1989-1949 1989-09-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that the cost of
providing a service in respect of a request for an advance income tax
ruling should be borne by the person to whom it is provided.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue and the Treasury
Board, pursuant to paragraph 19(b) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1983-653 of March 3,
  1983[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI_89-217_EN_hq_20107){#fn_SI_89-217_EN_hq_20107-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI_89-217_EN_hq_20107-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_SI_89-217_EN_hq_20107}SI/83-62,
    1983 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 1168
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to authorize the Minister of National Revenue to
  prescribe, by order, the fee to be paid by a person to whom a service
  is provided in respect of a request for an advance income tax ruling.
:::
