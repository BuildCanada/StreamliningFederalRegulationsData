## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-677999 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-678000 .Subheading}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[affidavit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[affidavit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a written statement
    confirmed by oath or a solemn declaration.
    ([affidavit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any application,
    complaint, question or dispute as defined in section 3 of the Code
    made or referred to the Board in writing under the Code.
    ([demande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Labour
    Code](/eng/acts/L-2). ([Code]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[complaint]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[complaint]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any complaint filed in
    writing with the Board under subsection 97(1), 110(3) or 133(1) of
    the Code. ([plainte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a calendar day.
    ([jour]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[intervenor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[intervenor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person whose request to
    intervene made under section 12.1 has been granted.
    ([intervenant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any applicant, respondent and
    intervenor. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an employer, an employers'
    organization, a trade union, a council of trade unions, an employee
    or a group of employees. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee of the
    Administrative Tribunals Support Service of Canada whom the Board
    has authorized in writing to act on its behalf.
    ([greffier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reply]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reply]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the document by which the
    applicant replies in writing to a response and that is the final
    step in the application process. ([réplique]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[response]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[response]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the document by which a
    respondent responds in writing to an application.
    ([réponse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Returning Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Returning Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual
    appointed by the Board to conduct a representation vote. ([directeur
    du scrutin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Registrar]{.HTitleText2} {#h-678022 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In addition to processing
any matters on behalf of the Board, a Registrar may make binding
decisions on uncontested applications on behalf of the Board in respect
of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} amendments to certification orders made pursuant to
  section 18 of the Code, incidental to a change of a party's name;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} applications for certification pursuant to section 24
  of the Code;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} successor rights, privileges and duties under section
  43 of the Code;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} change of the name incidental to successor employer
  applications pursuant to sections 44 to 46 of the Code; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} withdrawal of any complaint or application prior to
  its referral by the Chairperson to a panel.

### [Signatures and Authorizations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-678037 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application, response, reply or request to intervene filed with the
  Board shall be signed as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it is filed by a trade union, a council of trade
    unions or an employers' organization, it shall be signed by the
    president or secretary or two other officers or by any individual
    authorized by the trade union, the council of trade unions or
    employers' organization;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is filed by an employer, it shall be signed
    by the employer or by the general manager or chief executive officer
    or by any individual authorized by the employer; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if it is filed by an employee, it shall be signed
    by the employee or by any individual authorized by the employee.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), the Board may
  require that an authorization be given in writing and filed with the
  Board.

### [Filing and Service of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-678044 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  application, response, reply, request to intervene or other document
  is required to be filed with the Board or served on any person, it
  must be filed or served on the person, the person's legal counsel or
  the person's representative

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by delivery by hand;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by mail at the address for service, as defined in
    subsection (2);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by fax that provides a proof of receipt of the
    document; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by any other means authorized by the Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), address for
  service means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the Board, the address of an office
    of the Administrative Tribunals Support Service of Canada that is
    identified as a Board office; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other person, the person's
    address as it appears in any notice issued by the Board in that
    proceeding, or if no address appears, the latest known address of
    the person.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A document that is transmitted by fax must include
  the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone and fax numbers of the
    person transmitting the document;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone and fax numbers of the
    person to whom the document is being transmitted;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of transmission; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total number of pages being transmitted.

### [Notices of Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-678081 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the Board
  shall, on receipt of an application and to the extent possible, give
  notice of the application in writing to a person whose rights may be
  directly affected by the application.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the rights of employees could be affected by an
  application, the Board may, in writing, require an employer or a trade
  union to do one or both of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immediately post any notices of the application
    that are provided by the Board, for the reasonable period that it
    prescribes, in places where those notices are most likely to come to
    the attention of the employees who may be affected by the
    application; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify the employees who may be affected by the
    application by any other means set by the Board that ensures that
    they receive effective notice of the application.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An employer or a trade union, as the case may be,
  must provide written confirmation to the Board that it has complied
  with any requirement prescribed in subsection (2).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The date on which the employees are deemed to have
  received notice of the application is the earliest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the employees are given notice by
    the Board of the application under subsection (1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the notice is posted under
    paragraph (2)(a); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the employees are notified of the
    application under paragraph (2)(b).

### [Responses and Replies]{.HTitleText2} {#h-678092 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who makes a response or reply must include the following
  information in the response or reply:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
    and fax numbers of the respondent and of their legal counsel or
    representative, if applicable;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Board's file number for the relevant
    application;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} full particulars of the facts, relevant dates and
    grounds for the response or reply;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a copy of supporting documents for the response or
    reply;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the person's position relating to the order or
    decision sought by the applicant or respondent, as the case may be;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an indication as to whether a hearing is being
    requested and, if so, the reasons for the request; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of the order or decision sought.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A response must be filed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for certification,
    within 10 days of the receipt of notice of the application; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other application, within 15
    days of the receipt of notice of the application.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A reply must be filed within 10 days of the filing of
  the response.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A request for an extension of time to file a response
  or reply must be made in writing to the Board and set out the grounds
  for the request.

### [Request to Intervene]{.HTitleText2} {#h-678109 .Subheading}

- **[[12.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any person who wishes to apply for
  intervenor status must make a request to intervene in writing that
  includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
    and fax numbers of the person and of their legal counsel or
    representative;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the person's interest in the
    matter including, an explanation of any prejudice that the person
    would suffer if the intervention were denied and an explanation as
    to whether their interest is different from that of any other party;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an indication as to how the intervention will
    assist the Board in furthering the objectives of the Code.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request to intervene must be filed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for certification,
    within 10 days of the receipt of notice of the application; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other application, within 15
    days of the receipt of notice of the application.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A response to a request to intervene must be filed
  within 10 days of the receipt of the request.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A reply must be filed within 5 days of the filing of
  the response.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the request to intervene is granted, the
  intervenor must file with the Board written submissions on the merits
  of the case within 10 days of the receipt of notice that the request
  to intervene has been granted, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Board's file number for the relevant
    application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} full particulars of the facts, relevant dates and
    grounds for the submissions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of supporting documents for the submissions;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the intervenor's position relating to any order or
    decision sought;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an indication as to whether a hearing is being
    requested and, if so, the reasons for the request; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the order or decision sought.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A response to the intervenor's submissions on the
  merits of the case must be filed within 10 days of the filing of the
  submissions.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2014-243, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} A request for any extension of time for filing a
  document under this section must be made in writing to the Board and
  set out the grounds for the requested extension.

### [Expedited Process]{.HTitleText2} {#h-678135 .Subheading}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** An expedited process
applies to the following matters:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} applications for interim orders made under section
  19.1 of the Code;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} applications to file a decision or order of the Board
  in the Federal Court or in the superior court of a province, made
  under sections 23 and 23.1 of the Code;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} referrals to the Board directed by the Minister under
  section 80, subsection 87.4(5) or section 107 of the Code;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} applications for declaration of an invalid strike or
  lockout vote made under subsections 87.3(4) and (5) of the Code;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} applications for declaration of unlawful strike or
  lockout made under sections 91 and 92 of the Code;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} unfair labour practice complaints respecting
  dismissals for union activities referred to in subsection 94(3) of the
  Code and prohibited uses of services referred to in subsections 94(4)
  and (6) of the Code; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} complaints respecting a dismissal made under section
  133 of the Code.

### [Confidentiality of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-678173 .Subheading}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the Board
  must place a document on the public record if the document is relevant
  to the proceeding.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board, on its own initiative or at the request of
  a party, may declare that a document is confidential.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In determining whether a document is confidential,
  the Board must consider whether disclosure would cause specific direct
  harm to a person and whether the specific direct harm would outweigh
  the public interest in disclosure.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Board declares that a document is
  confidential, the Board may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} order that the document or any part of it not be
    placed on the public record;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} order that a version or any part of the document
    from which the confidential information has been removed be placed
    on the public record;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} order that any portion of a hearing, including
    argument, examination or cross-examination, which deals with the
    confidential document, be conducted in private;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} order that the document or any part of it be
    provided to the parties, or only to their legal counsel or
    representative, and that the document not be placed on the public
    record; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} make any other order that it considers appropriate.

- SOR/2012-305, s. 11

    application, if the non-complying party is the applicant; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} decide the application without further notice, if
    the non-complying party is the respondent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a party does not attend a pre-hearing proceeding
  or a hearing after having been given notice, the Board may decide the
  matter in the party's absence.

**[[47.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47.1 .sectionLabel}** If the Board
determines that an oral hearing is necessary, it may give notice of the
hearing by any available means, including telephone, fax, publication in
a daily newspaper or the posting of notices.

  are pending on the day these Regulations come into force.

  1992 before the coming into force of these Regulations is not
