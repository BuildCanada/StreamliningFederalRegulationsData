  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

      Act](/eng/acts/O-2.7); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Assessment to Be Paid]{.HTitleText1} {#h-775255 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 23(5) of the Act, the amount of the assessment
  is determined by multiplying the pension plan assessment base by the
  basic rate that is in effect for the fiscal year in which the
  assessment is due to be paid.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2019-53, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the assessment to be paid is
  equal to zero if,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a pension plan that has been
    registered under section 12 of the [Pooled Registered Pension Plans
    Act](/eng/acts/P-15.3), the plan is terminated and wound up no later
    than four months after the end of the pension plan year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a pension plan that has been
    registered or is filed for registration under section 10 of the
    [Pension Benefits Standards Act, 1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the plan is terminated and wound up no later than
      six months after the end of the pension plan year,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the plan has been terminated for five or more
      pension plan years, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the plan is terminated with a [[solvency
      deficit]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 24.1(1)
      of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
      1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), and the plan is a [[negotiated
      contribution plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Pension Benefits Standards Act,
      1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01), or the employer who is required to pay
      into the plan under subsection 9(1.1) of that Act is

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a [[bankrupt]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
        defined in section 2 of the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency
        Act](/eng/acts/B-3),

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} an insolvent person in respect of whom a
        partial or complete stay of proceedings applies under subsection
        69(1) or 69.1(1) of that Act, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} a debtor company in respect of which an order
        under subsection 11.02(1) or (2) of the Companies' Creditors
        Arrangements Act has been made and in respect of which any stay
        of proceedings under that order remains in effect.

## [Determination of Pension Plan Assessment Base]{.HTitleText1} {#h-775267 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2016-275, s. 6

\]
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The pension plan assessment
base is determined in accordance with the following formula:

::: {#775271 .Section}
A + B + 50

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the lesser of
    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of beneficiaries in excess of 50, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 950; and

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the lesser of
    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 75% of the number of beneficiaries in excess of
      1,000, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 19,000.
:::

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-275, s. 6

## [Determination of the Basic Rate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-775281 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The basic rate for a fiscal
year is the rate determined in accordance with the following formula:

::: {#775283 .Section}
(A + B)/C

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the estimated total amount of expenses to be incurred by the
    Office during the fiscal year for or in connection with the
    administration of the [Pension Benefits Standards Act,
    1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01) and the [Pooled Registered Pension Plans
    Act](/eng/acts/P-15.3);

[B]{.dfn}
:   is 20% of the amount determined in accordance with the following
    formula:
    ::: {#775287 .NestedFormula}
    D -- E

    where

    [D]{.dfn}
    :   is the total amount of expenses incurred by the Office for or in
        connection with the administration of the [Pension Benefits
        Standards Act, 1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01) and the [Pooled
        Registered Pension Plans Act](/eng/acts/P-15.3) and by the
        Superintendent for the registration of pension plans and their
        supervision under those Acts, including inspection, in the
        second to sixth preceding fiscal years, and

    [E]{.dfn}
    :   is the total amount of assessments received by the Office as
        well as the fees collected under the [Pension Benefits Standards
        Act, 1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01) and the [Pooled Registered Pension
        Plans Act](/eng/acts/P-15.3) in the second to sixth preceding
        fiscal years if, for those years, B had been equal to zero; and
    :::

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the estimated total amount of the pension plan assessment bases
    of all plans.
:::

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-275, ss. 4, 6

## [Publication of Basic Rate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-775294 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Superintendent shall publish in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, no later than 180 days
  before the beginning of each fiscal year, a notice setting out the
  basic rate that is in effect for that fiscal year.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the notice setting out the
  basic rates that are in effect for the fiscal years beginning on April
  1, 2016 and April 1, 2017 in respect of pooled registered pension
  plans shall be published no later than January 31, 2017.

send each administrator of a pension plan notice in writing of the
assessment against it.
