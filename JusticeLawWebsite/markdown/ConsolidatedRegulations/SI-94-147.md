## Order Authorizing the Minister of Agriculture to Prescribe Fees {#order-authorizing-the-minister-of-agriculture-to-prescribe-fees .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/94-147
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1994-12-28

Order Authorizing the Minister of Agriculture to Prescribe Fees

P.C. 1994-2098  1994-12-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 19(1)(b) of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to authorize the
Minister of Agriculture to prescribe by order the fees to be paid by any
user to whom the Research Branch of the Department of Agriculture
provides scientific or technical assistance.
