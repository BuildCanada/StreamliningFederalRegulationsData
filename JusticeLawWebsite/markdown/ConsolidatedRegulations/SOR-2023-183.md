## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1420387 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[CCASCOE]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CCASCOE]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the NATO Climate Change and
    Security Centre of Excellence. ([CECCS]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[international personnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international personnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the personnel
    assigned to the CCASCOE by a participating NATO member state, other
    than by Canada. ([personnel international]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Rights, Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1420392 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal capacity

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The CCASCOE has the legal
capacity of a corporation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privileges and immunities

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  CCASCOE has, to the extent required to perform its functions, the
  privileges and immunities set out in articles 8 and 12 of the Paris
  Protocol.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inviolability

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In accordance with article 13 of the Paris Protocol,
  the archives and other official documents of the CCASCOE are
  inviolable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}International personnel

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} International personnel have, to the
  extent required to perform their functions, the following privileges
  and immunities:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} those set out in paragraph 1 of article 7 of the
    Paris Protocol; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the extent set out in article 8 of the Paris
    Protocol, those set out in paragraphs 5 to 10 of article XI of the
    Agreement between the Parties to the North Atlantic Treaty regarding
    the Status of their Forces, done at London on 19 June, 1951.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other individuals

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Personnel of [[Supreme
  Headquarters]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or of [[Allied
  Headquarters]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in paragraphs (b)
  and (c) of article 1 of the Paris Protocol respectively, and of other
  international military Headquarters or organizations established under
  the North Atlantic Treaty also benefit from the privileges and
  immunities set out in subsection (1), to the extent required to
  perform their functions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waiver

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Article
  6 of the Paris Protocol applies to all indemnity claims in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Civil liability --- international
  personnel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 15, 17 and 19 of the [Visiting Forces
  Act](/eng/acts/V-2) apply to the CCASCOE with any necessary
  adaptations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No tax exemption to Canadians residing in
Canada

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Order
exempts a Canadian citizen, residing or ordinarily resident in Canada,
from liability for any taxes or duties imposed by any law in Canada.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1420414 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
