## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1254215 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[casual
goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[casual goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods other than goods
imported for sale or for an industrial, occupational, commercial or
institutional or other like use.

## [Casual Goods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1254218 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}United States Tariff

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Casual goods that are
acquired in the United States

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} are considered to originate in the United States and
  are entitled to the benefit of the United States Tariff if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the marking of the goods is in accordance with the
    marking laws of the United States and indicates that the goods are
    the product of the United States or Canada, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the goods do not bear a mark and there is no
    indication that the goods are not the product of the United States
    or Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} are considered to originate in Mexico and are
  entitled to the benefit of the Mexico Tariff if the marking of the
  goods is in accordance with the marking laws of the United States and
  indicates that the goods are the product of Mexico.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mexico Tariff

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Casual goods that are
acquired in Mexico

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} are considered to originate in Mexico and are
  entitled to the benefit of the Mexico Tariff if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the marking of the goods is in accordance with the
    marking laws of Mexico and indicates that the goods are the product
    of Mexico or Canada, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the goods do not bear a mark and there is no
    indication that the goods are not the product of Mexico or Canada;
    and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} are considered to originate in the United States and
  are entitled to the benefit of the United States Tariff if the marking
  of the goods is in accordance with the marking laws of Mexico and
  indicates that the goods are the product of the United States.
