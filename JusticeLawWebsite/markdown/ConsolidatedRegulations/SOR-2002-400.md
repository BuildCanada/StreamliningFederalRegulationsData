## CCRFTA Sugar Aggregate Quantity Limit Order {#ccrfta-sugar-aggregate-quantity-limit-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2002-400
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 2002-10-31

CCRFTA Sugar Aggregate Quantity Limit Order

P.C. 2002-1864 2002-10-31
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 49.3(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EGCAA} of the
[Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EDCAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [CCRFTA Sugar Aggregate Quantity Limit
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-400).

## [Tariff Rate Quota]{.HTitleText1} {#h-697576 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The aggregate quantity
limit of goods of tariff item Nos. 1701.91.90, 1701.99.90, 1702.90.11,
1702.90.12, 1702.90.13, 1702.90.14, 1702.90.15, 1702.90.16, 1702.90.17,
1702.90.18 and 1702.90.89 that are entitled to the Costa Rica Tariff,
for the period set out in column 1 of the schedule, is the applicable
aggregate quantity limit set out in column 2.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-228, s. 1

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-697580 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force on the day on which section
37 of the [Canada-Costa Rica Free Trade Agreement Implementation
Act](/eng/acts/C-1.9), chapter 28 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, comes
into force.
