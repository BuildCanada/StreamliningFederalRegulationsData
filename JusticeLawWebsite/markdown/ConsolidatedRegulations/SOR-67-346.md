## Canadian Vickers Dry Dock Regulations {#canadian-vickers-dry-dock-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/67-346
:::

[DRY DOCKS SUBSIDIES ACT](/eng/acts/D-4)

Registration 1967-06-29

Regulations Respecting the Dry Docks of Canadian Vickers Limited

P.C. 1967-1317 1967-06-29
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Public Works, pursuant to section 18 of the Dry Dock
Subsidies Act, is pleased hereby to revoke the Canadian Vickers Limited
Dry Dock Rules and Regulations approved by Order in Council P.C.
1956-760 of 17th May, 1956,[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^(1)^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA} and to
approve the annexed [Regulations Respecting the Dry Docks of Canadian
Vickers Limited](/eng/regulations/SOR-67-346), in substitution therefor.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^(1)^](#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnote1_e}SOR/56-175,
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part II, Vol. 90, No. 11
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860517 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Canadian Vickers Dry Dock
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-67-346).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860519 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Canadian Vickers
    Limited; ([Compagnie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dock-master]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dock-master]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a dock-master, manager or
    other person in charge of a Dry Dock; ([maître de
    bassin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Dry Dock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Dry Dock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Duke of Connaught Dry
    Dock or The General Georges P. Vanier Dry Dock of the Company, as
    the case may be. ([cale sèche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Tariff of Fees and Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860524 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to these Regulations, the fees for use of a Dry Dock by a vessel are
  as set out in Schedule A, and are payable to the Company by the owner
  of the vessel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Dry Dock fees and the charges for the work done are,
  at the option of the Company, payable by the owner of a vessel before
  the vessel leaves the Dry Dock.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The owner of a vessel shall pay the cost of all
  shores, staging, stage poles, trestles, keel blocks and other
  equipment cut, broken, or rendered useless by the vessel in entering
  or leaving the Dry Dock.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} When two or more vessels are docked together, each
  vessel shall be charged individually in accordance with these
  Regulations, except that if one vessel is ready to leave the Dry Dock
  before the other vessel, the Company may float the other vessel, if it
  is in fit condition, and re-dock it, but no charge shall be made
  therefor or for the time taken.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (6), in calculating the fees
  payable under these Regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fees payable at a rate "per day" shall be
    calculated on each period of twenty-four hours or part thereof; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fees payable at a rate "per ton" shall be
    calculated on the gross tonnage ascertained from the current edition
    of the Lloyds Register of Shipping or the certificate of the vessel
    except that in the case of naval vessels the fees shall be
    calculated by displacement.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The fees "per day" set out in Schedule A

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are payable for any time required for cooling a
    vessel's engines while it is being docked and for warming its
    engines in preparation for its being moved out of a Dry Dock; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are not payable in respect of any portion of a
    docking period that is a Sunday or holiday of the Company if no work
    is done on the vessel on that day.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The fees payable under these Regulations do not
  include pilotage charges and charges for preparing or shifting keel
  and bilge blocks or shores for painting or repairs.

## [Admission to Dry Docks]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860537 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  vessel shall be admitted into a Dry Dock until

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} application for use of a Dry Dock has been made to
    the Company in the form set out in Schedule B;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the time and manner of its entry and the purpose
    and duration of its stay in the Dry Dock have been

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} agreed upon with the Company, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} entered in an entry book to be kept by the
      Company for that purpose; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the entry fee set out in Schedule A has been paid
    to the Company.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The entry book referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  open to public inspection.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a vessel is not ready to be admitted into a Dry
  Dock at the time arranged, the vessel shall, if required by the
  dock-master, stand aside for any other vessel.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), the entry fee set out in
  Schedule A is payable as a deposit towards the full fees payable for
  use of the Dry Dock by a vessel.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a vessel is not ready to be admitted to a Dry
  Dock at the time arranged,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the entry fee shall be forfeited to the Company;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the owner or master of the vessel shall pay to the
    Company

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the full dockage fees for one day,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on demand, in respect of any period beyond the
      first day that a Dry Dock is vacant by reason of the non-arrival
      of the vessel, fees equal to the fees that would have been payable
      by him if the vessel had occupied the Dry Dock during that period,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} on demand, any other costs and damages incurred
      by the Company as a result of the non-arrival of the vessel.

## [Docking]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860554 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  vessel shall have sufficient men on board to manoeuvre the vessel
  while it is entering or leaving a Dry Dock.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} All orders of the dock-master regarding the moving of
  a vessel into or out of a Dry Dock shall be obeyed by the captain and
  crew of the vessel.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The number of tugs required for towing a vessel from
  berth to a Dry Dock and back, shall be fixed by the harbour pilot or
  the persons responsible for the vessel's navigation.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} While a vessel is in a Dry Dock, its captain and crew
  shall obey all reasonable orders issued by the dock-master.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The orders referred to in subsection (4) shall be in
  writing if the captain of the vessel so requires.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A vessel shall not enter a Dry Dock until requested
  to do so by signal from the dock-master.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} No vessel shall enter a Dry Dock under her own steam
  unless specifically requested to do so by the dock-master but shall be
  warped in under the control of the dock-master or moved by the use of
  tugs, as the dock-master sees fit.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The propellers or side wheels of a vessel shall not
  be moved without the permission of the dockmaster while the vessel is
  in a Dry Dock.

## [Use of a Dry Dock]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860564 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A vessel
  using a Dry Dock shall be trimmed and upright to the reasonable
  satisfaction of the dock-master.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the permission of the dock-master is first
  obtained,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no anchors or cables shall be lowered to the
    pontoon deck of a floating dock or the Dry Dock floor of a graving
    dock; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} while the vessel is in a Dry Dock

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} no steam shall be blown off,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} no water shall be removed from the boilers or
      tanks, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} no appreciable change shall be made in the
      distribution of the weight of the vessel or her contents.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person other than the dock-master shall remove or
  alter any position of any of the supports on which a vessel rests.

## [Abnormal Risks]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860574 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where abnormal risks are
involved in docking a vessel,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the owner of the vessel shall bear the risks and
  shall pay for any damage thereby ensuing to a Dry Dock or the
  Company's property;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Company may exact a bond or other guarantee to
  protect it from all loss caused as a result of the use of the Dry Dock
  by the vessel; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the owner of the vessel shall pay the extra cost of
  preparing a Dry Dock for the reception of the vessel and, in addition
  to the applicable fees set out in Schedule A shall pay a surcharge of
  twenty per cent of those fees.

## [Manifest of Cargo]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860579 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The owner or agent of a
vessel in a Dry Dock shall, when required by the Company, produce the
manifest of the cargo of the vessel docked as evidence of the quantity
liable for dockage fees.

## [Damage to Vessel]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860581 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Company is not liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for any damage that may be caused to a vessel while
    in a Dry Dock or while entering or leaving a Dry Dock; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for any damage arising from any delay to the vessel
    unless the damage was caused by the Company.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a vessel is about to leave a Dry Dock the owner
  of the vessel shall ensure that the inlet and outlet valves and all
  the openings in the vessel's bottom below the water are closed.

## [Order of Docking]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860587 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), vessels
  shall dock in the order in which they have been booked, but the
  Company may alter the sequence in unusual cases.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty's ships of war and vessels in distress
  involving risk of foundering shall at all times have priority of
  docking over all vessels entered in the entry book.

## [Repairs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860591 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it appears that the repairs to a vessel in a Dry
    Dock will require longer use of a Dry Dock than was at first
    anticipated, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Dry Dock has been engaged by another vessel for
    a definite time,

  the repairs to the vessel in that Dry Dock shall be completed to the
  extent necessary to enable it to leave the Dry Dock, and the vessel
  shall, if required by the dock-master, then leave the Dry Dock.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A vessel that leaves a Dry Dock pursuant to
  subsection (1) may re-enter a Dry Dock as soon as one is available.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A vessel that leaves and re-enters a Dry Dock
  pursuant to this section is not, in respect of its second entry,
  required to pay any entry fee or any fees for pumping out and
  submerging the dock.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** All repairs to vessels
using a Dry Dock shall be carried out only by men employed by the
Company unless otherwise specially arranged.

## [Refuse]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860600 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Ashes and refuse shall be
disposed of as directed by the dock-master and any expenses incurred in
removing them shall be borne by the owner of the vessel.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** No discharge shall be
made from the soil pipes or scuppers of a vessel while in a Dry Dock,
except where connected to a Dry Dock sewage system.

## [Foreign Vessels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860603 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Where any person wishes
to engage a Dry Dock for a foreign vessel for which there is no owner or
responsible agent resident in Canada, the Company may, before the vessel
enters the Dry Dock, require either a bond with two solvent sureties or
a cash deposit, in an amount equal to the estimated amount of dockage
fees and cost of work to be done on the vessel.

## [Refusal to Obey Orders]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860605 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Where the captain,
officers or any of the crew of a vessel refuse to obey any of the
provisions of these Regulations, the Company may float the vessel and
remove it from a Dry Dock, and any cost incurred in so doing shall be
paid by the owner of the vessel.

## [Emergencies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860607 .Part}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  in the opinion of the Company, it is advisable to remove a vessel from
  a Dry Dock due to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an emergency that is liable to endanger a vessel or
    any property of the Company, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a fire occurring on a vessel or in or about a Dry
    Dock,

  the Company may float the vessel and cause it to be removed from the
  Dry Dock.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a fire occurring on board a vessel is caused by
  the vessel's officers or crew, the cost of any work done to enable the
  vessel to be removed from a Dry Dock and any damage done to the
  property of the Company shall be paid by the owner of the vessel.

## [Accidents to Dock Workmen]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860614 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a vessel is
liable for any injuries caused to dock workmen due to any fault or
carelessness on the part of his employees or persons under his control.

## [Damages to Dry Dock]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860616 .Part}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Where any damage is
caused to a Dry Dock or other property of the Company due to any fault
or carelessness of the employees of the owner of a vessel or persons
under his control, the owner of the vessel shall pay the cost of making
good such damage, and shall compensate the Company for any loss of
dockage fees and charges and other consequential damages that may be
sustained by the Company during the time the damage is being repaired.

## [Floating Dry Docks]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860618 .Part}

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** No vessel shall lie
alongside or shall be attached to a Floating Dry Dock without special
permission of the Company.

## [Explosives]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860620 .Part}

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), no vessel
  shall enter a Dry Dock with any explosive material on board, or
  carrying or having carried on its current voyage oil with a flashing
  point below twenty-three degrees Celsius, unless the owner of the
  vessel furnishes a certificate given by a competent analyst that all
  tanks and holds of the vessel are free from inflammable vapour.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to any vessel that is
  owned and operated by the Government of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/77-347, s. 1
:::

## [Oil]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860626 .Part}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No oil
  burning or oil carrying vessel shall enter a Dry Dock until such
  precautions to prevent the leaking of oil from the vessel as may be
  required by the dock-master have been taken.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a dock-master considers it necessary, he may
  require the cargo or oil tanks of a vessel referred to in
  subsection (1) to be cleared before the vessel enters a Dry Dock.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Cargo and oil tanks required by the dock-master
  pursuant to subsection (1) to be cleared shall be thoroughly steamed
  out and ventilated in such a manner as to expel therefrom all gases
  and other dangerous or explosive fumes, and the owner or agent of a
  vessel shall, before repairs are undertaken therein, furnish a
  certificate given by a competent analyst that all tanks and holds of
  the vessel are free of inflammable vapour.

## [Fire Hydrants]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860631 .Part}

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Every vessel in a Dry
Dock shall be connected to a shore fire hydrant by the Company.
