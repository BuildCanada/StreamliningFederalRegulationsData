## Exported Dairy Products Assistance Payments Order {#exported-dairy-products-assistance-payments-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/80-11
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 3, 1978-79

Registration 1980-01-09

Order Prescribing Terms and Conditions Respecting Payments to Exporters
of Dairy Products

P.C. 1979-3564 1979-12-20
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture and the Treasury Board, pursuant to Canadian
Dairy Commission Vote 50a of Appropriation Act No. 3, 1978-79, is
pleased hereby to revoke the [Exported Dairy Products Assistance
Payments Order](/eng/regulations/SI-80-11) made by Order in Council P.C.
1979-741 of 15th March, 1979[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EDBAA} and to make the
annexed [Order prescribing terms and conditions respecting payments to
exporters of dairy products](/eng/regulations/SI-80-11) in substitution
therefor.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634719 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Exported Dairy Products Assistance Payments
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-80-11).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634721 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Dairy
    Commission; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[export assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[export assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a payment, pursuant
    to this Order, out of the Canadian Dairy Commission Account to an
    exporter; ([aide à l'exportation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[exporter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exporter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who exports out of
    Canada a dairy product. ([exportateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application for Export Assistance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634726 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An exporter may apply for
export assistance by forwarding an application to the Commission setting
out the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the common name of the dairy product that is to be
  exported;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the common name of the dairy products contained in
  the product that is to be exported;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the destination of the dairy product that is to be
  exported;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of dairy product that is to be exported;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the composition of the dairy product that is to be
  exported; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the latest date on which the dairy product will be
  exported.

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634734 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of the application referred to in section 3, the Commission
  shall inform the applicant in writing of the amount of export
  assistance, if any, authorized pursuant to this Order to be paid to
  him.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where export assistance has been authorized to be
  paid to an applicant and that applicant has been informed pursuant to
  subsection (1) of the amount of the assistance so authorized, the
  applicant shall inform the Commission of his acceptance, or otherwise,
  of the amount of the assistance.

## [Terms and Conditions of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634738 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may
authorize export assistance to an exporter

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a dairy product that is butter or skim
  milk powder, in an amount not exceeding the difference between

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian support price for the butter or skim
    milk powder payable by the Commission at the time the export
    assistance is authorized by the Commission, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the world market price for the butter or skim milk
    powder at that time;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of cheese, other than cheese referred to
  in paragraphs (c) or (d), or any other dairy product other than butter
  or skim milk powder, in an amount not exceeding the difference between

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the price for the cheese or other dairy product, as
    the case may be, calculated on the basis of the market price for
    milk as determined by the Canadian support prices for butter and
    skim milk powder payable by the Commission at the time the export
    assistance is authorized by the Commission, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the world market price for the cheese or other
    dairy product at that time;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of aged cheddar cheese that is to be
  exported to a member state of the European Economic Community, in an
  amount not exceeding an amount that would reduce the price of aged
  cheddar cheese to a level below the free-at-frontier values as agreed
  between Canada and the European Economic Community under the European
  Economic Community Common Customs Tariff heading number 04.04E as that
  heading relates to aged cheddar cheese; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in respect of cheese to be exported to the United
  States, in an amount not exceeding the difference between

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of the F.O.B. price of the cheese
    manufactured in Canada, the freight costs in respect of that cheese
    to its destination in the United States and the United States'
    customs duty charges in respect of the cheese, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the United States' wholesale price of similar
    cheese produced in the United States.

## [Payment of Export Assistance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634750 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** After the exportation of a
dairy product in respect of which export assistance has been authorized
to be paid to an applicant referred to in section 4, the exporter of the
product shall submit to the Commission the following information
supported by documentation satisfactory to the Commission, namely:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the common name of the dairy product exported;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of dairy product exported;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the destination of the dairy product exported;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the port of export;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date of export shipments; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} such further information as the Commission may
  require to show that the exportation of the dairy product was carried
  out in accordance with the conditions on which the authorization was
  based.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** On receipt of the
information referred to in section 6 from an exporter, the Commission
shall pay to the exporter export assistance in the amount authorized
pursuant to subsection 4(1).
