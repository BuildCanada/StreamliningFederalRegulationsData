## Indian Band Council Borrowing Regulations {#indian-band-council-borrowing-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 949
:::

[INDIAN ACT](/eng/acts/I-5)

Regulations Respecting the Borrowing of Money by Councils of Bands and
the Disposition of Such Moneys
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-598126 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Indian Band Council Borrowing
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._949).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-598128 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The council of a band may
borrow money for band projects or housing purposes and may make loans
out of moneys so borrowed to members of the band for housing purposes,
on such terms and conditions as may be determined by the council.
