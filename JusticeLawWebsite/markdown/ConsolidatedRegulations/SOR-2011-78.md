## Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials (Tunisia) Regulations {#freezing-assets-of-corrupt-foreign-officials-tunisia-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2011-78
:::

[FREEZING ASSETS OF CORRUPT FOREIGN OFFICIALS ACT](/eng/acts/F-31.6)

Registration 2011-03-23

Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials (Tunisia) Regulations

P.C. 2011-427 2011-03-23
::::

:::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas Tunisia and Egypt have asserted to the Government of Canada, in
writing, that each of the persons listed in the annexed Freezing Assets
of Corrupt Foreign Officials (Tunisia and Egypt) Regulations have
misappropriated property of Tunisia or of Egypt, as the case may be, or
have acquired property inappropriately by virtue of their office or a
personal or business relationship and have asked the Government of
Canada to freeze the property of those persons;

Whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that each of those persons
are politically exposed foreign persons in relation to Tunisia or Egypt,
as the case may be;

Whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that there is internal
turmoil or an uncertain political situation in Tunisia and Egypt;

And whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that the making of the
annexed Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials (Tunisia and Egypt)
Regulations is in the interest of international relations;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to
subsections 4(1) to (3) of the [Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign
Officials Act](/eng/acts/F-31.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-870-E_hq_10504){#fn_81000-2-870-E_hq_10504-ID0EDBAA},
hereby makes the annexed Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials
(Tunisia and Egypt) Regulations.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-780088 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Section 3 applies in
respect of the property of the politically exposed foreign persons
listed in Schedule 1.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-780093 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Asset freeze

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A person in Canada must not

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deal, directly or indirectly, in any property,
  wherever situated, of any politically exposed foreign person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into or facilitate, directly or indirectly, any
  financial transaction related to a dealing referred to in
  paragraph (a); or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide financial services or other related services
  in respect of any property of any politically exposed foreign person.

## [Application Before Publication]{.HTitleText1} {#h-780099 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application before publication

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 11(2)(a) of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22),
these Regulations apply before they are published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-780102 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
