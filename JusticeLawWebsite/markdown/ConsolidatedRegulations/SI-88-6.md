## Goodyear Remission Order {#goodyear-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/88-6
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1988-01-06

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duty and a Portion of the
Federal Sales Tax on Tires

P.C. 1987-2672  1987-12-22
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest, is pleased hereby, on the recommendation of the
Minister of Finance, the Minister of Regional Industrial Expansion and
the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 17[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-88-6_e_hq_6455){#fn_SI-88-6_e_hq_6455-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the
annexed [Order respecting the remission of customs duty and a portion of
the federal sales tax on tires](/eng/regulations/SI-88-6).

- ::: Footnote

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641909 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Goodyear Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-88-6).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641911 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[tires]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all tires with the exception of
solid press-on tires; solid pneumatic-shaped tires; pneumatic tires for
forklift trucks and off-the-road bias ply tires in sizes greater than
11.00 × 24.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/92-203, s. 2(F)
:::

## [Remission of Customs Duty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641915 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on tires imported by Goodyear
Canada Inc., during the period commencing on October 1, 1987 and ending
on December 31, 1997.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-109, s. 1, SI/92-117, s. 1
:::

## [Remission of Sales Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641919 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5,
remission is hereby granted of the sales tax paid or payable under the
[Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on the goods for which customs duty is
remitted by this Order, in an amount equal to the difference between

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of sales tax paid or payable on the goods,
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of sales tax that would be payable in
  respect of the goods if the duty paid value used to calculate the
  sales tax on the goods were reduced by the amount of the remission of
  customs duty granted under this Order.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/92-203, s. 2(F)
:::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641925 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The remission referred to
in section 3 is granted on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Goodyear Canada Inc. continues to be a manufacturer
  of tires in Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} remission of customs duty shall not exceed \$12.6
  million for each of the five twelve month periods commencing on
  October 1, 1987; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within two years after the date of importation of the
  goods for which remission is claimed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-109, s. 2
- SI/92-117, s. 2
:::
