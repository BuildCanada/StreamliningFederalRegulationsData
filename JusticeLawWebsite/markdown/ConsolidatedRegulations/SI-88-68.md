## Order Authorizing the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs to Prescribe Fees {#order-authorizing-the-minister-of-consumer-and-corporate-affairs-to-prescribe-fees .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/88-68
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1988-04-27

Order Authorizing the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs to
Prescribe Fees

P.C. 1988-672 1988-04-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to authorize the Minister of
Consumer and Corporate Affairs to prescribe, by order, the fees to be
paid by persons for services in respect of the issuance of documents by
the Office of the Registrar General of Canada.
