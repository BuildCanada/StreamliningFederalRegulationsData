## Adjustment of Certain Fees in Respect of Publication in the Canada Gazette for the 2019--2020 and 2020--2021 Fiscal Years Remission Order {#adjustment-of-certain-fees-in-respect-of-publication-in-the-canada-gazette-for-the-20192020-and-20202021-fiscal-years-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2024-14
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2024-03-13

Adjustment of Certain Fees in Respect of Publication in the Canada
Gazette for the 2019--2020 and 2020--2021 Fiscal Years Remission Order

P.C. 2024-196 2024-03-01
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that the
collection of the amounts is unreasonable, on the recommendation of the
Treasury Board and the Minister of Public Works and Government Services,
under subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_15952_hq_1702910130){#fn_15952_hq_1702910130-ID0EMGAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_15952_hq_1702910221){#fn_15952_hq_1702910221-ID0EJGAA},
remits the following amounts representing annual adjustments --- that
should have been made under subsection 17(1) of the [Service Fees
Act](/eng/acts/S-8.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_15952_hq_1702912375){#fn_15952_hq_1702912375-ID0EGGAA}
--- to the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) insertion fees
charged under section 17 of the [Department of Public Works and
Government Services Act](/eng/acts/P-38.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-2-5354-EN_V2_hq_1703194252){#fn_81000-2-5354-EN_V2_hq_1703194252-ID0EBGAA}:
