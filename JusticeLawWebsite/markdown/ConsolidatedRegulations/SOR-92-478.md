## Food Research and Development Centre Fees Order {#food-research-and-development-centre-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-478
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1992-07-30

Order Prescribing the Fees to be Paid by Any User to Whom Technical
Assistance Services or the Use of Research and Development Facilities
Are Provided by the Food Research and Development Centre of the
Department of Agriculture at Saint-Hyacinthe, Québec
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1992-1740
of July 30, 1992[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBDA} and paragraph
19(1)(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby
makes the annexed Order prescribing the fees to be paid by any user to
whom technical assistance services or the use of research and
development facilities are provided by the Food Research and Development
Centre of the Department of Agriculture at Saint-Hyacinthe, Québec,
effective September 1, 1992.

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFBDA){#footnotea_e}SI/92-147,
  1992 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3504
  :::

Ottawa, July 30, 1992

BILL McKNIGHT

*Minister of Agriculture*
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-946172 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Food Research and Development Centre Fees
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-478).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-946174 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Centre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Centre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Food Research and
    Development Centre of the Department of Agriculture at
    Saint-Hyacinthe, Québec; ([Centre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Centre personnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Centre personnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means development
    assistants, research assistants, scientific professionals and
    scientific researchers; ([personnel du Centre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[cycle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cycle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a permeability test,
    means the maximum number of samples that can be tested
    simultaneously on the instrument; ([cycle]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[first day of rest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[first day of rest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a member of
    the Centre personnel, means the day that begins immediately after
    midnight of the day on which the member worked the member's last
    normal work day; ([premier jour de repos]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[normal work day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[normal work day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a work day of 7.5
    hours, not including Saturdays and holidays; ([jour
    ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[overtime work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[overtime work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means work performed by Centre
    personnel, other than scientific researchers, outside a normal work
    day; ([travail supplémentaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[permeability test]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permeability test]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a test made to test
    the permeability of materials used for packaging food; ([essai de
    perméabilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[second day of rest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[second day of rest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the day that begins
    immediately after midnight of the first day of rest or immediately
    after midnight of a paid holiday where the first day of rest is
    immediately followed by a paid holiday; ([deuxième jour de
    repos]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[user]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[user]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person to whom technical
    assistance services or the use of research facilities is provided by
    the Centre. ([usager]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-946185 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and section 5, a user shall pay the following fee
  for the use of the Centre's research and development facilities
  calculated on a per hour or part thereof basis:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the period beginning on September 1, 1992 and
    ending on August 31, 1993, \$10.50;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the period beginning on September 1, 1993 and
    ending on August 31, 1994, \$21.00;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the period beginning on September 1, 1994 and
    ending on August 31, 1995, \$31.50;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} after August 31, 1995, \$42.00.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A user shall be subject to a minimum fee for the use
  of the Centre's research and development facilities calculated on the
  basis of a period of 4 hours.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5, a
user shall pay for the services of a member of the Centre personnel set
out in column II of an item of Schedule I during the period of time set
out in column I of that item the fee calculated on a per hour or part
thereof basis, in accordance with the rate set out in column III of that
item.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A user shall pay for each
hour of overtime work, the following fee:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on a day that is a normal work day,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the first 7.5 hours of overtime, 1.5 times the
    applicable rate set out in column III of an item of Schedule I,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for the next 9 hours of overtime, 2 times the
    applicable rate set out in column III of an item of Schedule I;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on a first day of rest,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the first 7.5 hours of overtime, 1.5 times the
    applicable rate set out in column III of an item of Schedule I, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for the next 16.5 hours of overtime, 2 times the
    applicable rate set out in column III of an item of Schedule I; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a second day of rest and any subsequent day of
  rest, 2 times the applicable rate set out in column III of an item of
  Schedule I.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A user shall pay for a
permeability test the fee calculated in accordance with Schedule II.

## [SCHEDULE II]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 6)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-946211 .scheduleLabel}
:::

The fee payable by a user for a permeability test shall be calculated in
accordance with the following formula:

::: {#946213 .Provision}
Fee = \[AxB\] + \[CxD\] + E

Where

["A"]{.dfn}
:   is the number of cycles required to test all the samples.

["B"]{.dfn}
:   is the basic rate for a cycle, which rate is
    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$398, in the case of a film, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$1,013, in the case of a rigid container,

["C"]{.dfn}
:   is the number of samples,

["D"]{.dfn}
:   is the basic rate for a sample, which rate is
    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$43, in the case of a film, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$103, in the case of a rigid container,

["E"]{.dfn}
:   is a base amount of \$60 for data analysis and preparation of a
    report.
:::
