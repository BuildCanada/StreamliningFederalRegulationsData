## Basin Head Marine Protected Area Regulations {#basin-head-marine-protected-area-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2005-293

Basin Head Marine Protected Area Regulations

P.C. 2005-1657 2005-09-26
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to subsection 35(3) of
the [Oceans Act](/eng/acts/O-2.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Basin Head Marine Protected Area
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-293).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719078 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Basin Head Marine Protected
      Area designated under section 2. ([zone]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
      2 of the [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9).
      ([bâtiment]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in addition to the waters,
      the seabed and subsoil below the waters to a depth of two metres.
      ([eaux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, all geographical coordinates
  (latitude and longitude) are expressed in the North America Datum 1983
  (NAD 83) geodetic reference system.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the schedule, the lines connecting the points are
  rhumb lines.

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719086 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The areas of the sea at
Basin Head comprised of the management zones described below --- and
depicted in the schedule --- are together designated as the Basin Head
Marine Protected Area:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Zone 1, consisting of waters lying generally
  northeast of a rhumb line connecting points at 46°23′20″ N, 62°06′10″
  W and 46°23′18″ N, 62°06′08″ W, that are within an area of the sea
  bounded by the low-water line of the harbour and by the rhumb line to
  its points of intersection with the low-water line;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Zone 2, consisting of waters that are within an area
  of the sea bounded by the low-water line of the harbour and by the
  following rhumb lines to their respective points of intersection with
  the low-water line, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a line connecting points at 46°23′20″ N, 62°06′10″
    W and 46°23′18″ N, 62°06′08″ W, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a line connecting points at 46°22′39″ N, 62°06′29″
    W and 46°22′40″ N, 62°06′29″ W; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Zone 3, consisting of waters that are within an area
  of the sea bounded by the low-water line of the Northumberland
  Straight and by the following rhumb lines, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to its points of intersection with the low-water
    line, a line connecting points at 46°22′39″ N, 62°06′29″ W and
    46°22′40″ N, 62°06′29″ W,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to its point of intersection with the low-water
    line, a line connecting points at 46°23′51″ N, 62°04′30″ W and
    46°22′55″ N, 62°04′02″ W,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a line connecting points at 46°22′55″ N,
    62°04′02″ W and 46°21′07″ N, 62°07′36″ W, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} to its point of intersection with the low-water
    line, a line connecting points at 46°21′07″ N, 62°07′36″ W and
    46°22′04″ N, 62°08′04″ W.

## [Prohibited Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719097 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  Area, no person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} disturb, damage or destroy, or remove from the
    Area, any living marine organism or any part of its habitat; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} carry out any activity --- including depositing,
    discharging or dumping any substance, or causing any substance to be
    deposited, discharged or dumped --- that is likely to result in the
    disturbance, damage, destruction or removal of a living marine
    organism or any part of its habitat.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a person may carry out any
  activity excepted under section 4 or any scientific or educational
  activity for which a plan is approved under section 6.

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719103 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The following activities
may be carried out in the Area:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the following fishing activities, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} fishing that is carried out in accordance with the
    [Aboriginal Communal Fishing Licences
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-332),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in Zone 2 or 3, any recreational fishing activity
    that is carried out in accordance with the [Maritime Provinces
    Fishery Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-55) or the Wildlife
    Conservation Act of Prince Edward Island, R.S.P.E.I. 1988, c. W-4.1,
    and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in Zone 2 or 3, any commercial fishing activity
    that is carried out in accordance with the Atlantic Fishery
    Regulations 1985,or the [Maritime Provinces Fishery
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-55);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in Zone 2, the operation of a motorized vessel south
  of a rhumb line connecting points at 46°22′56″ N, 62°06′39″ W and
  46°22′56″ N and 62°06′34″ W solely for the purpose of transiting that
  area in order to launch the vessel from, or land it at, a boat launch;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any of the following activities --- for which
  approval or authorization is not required under the [Canadian
  Navigable Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-22) or the [Fisheries
  Act](/eng/acts/F-14), as the case may be, or that is carried out in
  accordance with an approval or authorization required under either of
  those Acts --- namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in Zone 2, the maintenance, repair or removal of a
    bridge, wharf or boat launch, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in Zone 3, the maintenance, repair or removal of a
    bridge or wharf; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any activity that is carried out for the purpose of
  public safety, national defence, national security or law enforcement
  or in response to an emergency.

## [Activity Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719116 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every person who proposes
to carry out a scientific or an educational activity in the Area shall
submit to the Minister for approval, not less than 60 days before the
day on which the activity is proposed to begin, a plan that contains the
following information and documents:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number and, if
  applicable, the facsimile number and electronic mail address of a
  person who can be contacted in respect of the plan;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the proposed activity that
  sets out

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its purpose,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the period or periods during which it is to be
    carried out,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a map on which its location is identified,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the types of data that are to be collected, if
    any, and the sampling protocols or other techniques to be used to
    collect the data,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the types of equipment, if any, that are to be used
    during the proposed activity, including those for gathering data
    and, if any of the equipment is to be anchored or moored in the
    Area, the methods by which the anchoring or mooring is to be
    conducted,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the type and identity of every vessel that is to
    be used to carry out the proposed activity, and

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} every substance, if any, that is to be deposited,
    discharged or dumped within the Area during the proposed activity;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an assessment of the environmental effects that are
  likely to occur within the Area as a result of the proposed activity;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a list of every licence, permit, authorization or
  consent obtained or applied for in respect of the proposed activity.

<!-- -->

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall, within 30 days after the day on which a plan that is
  submitted in accordance with section 5 is received, approve the plan
  if the proposed activity is not likely to damage or destroy the
  habitat of a living marine organism in the Area and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a scientific activity that is
    proposed to be carried out in Zone 1, the activity is for the
    purpose of managing the Area or monitoring the effectiveness of
    conservation measures implemented in the Area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an educational activity that is
    proposed to be carried out in Zone 1, the activity is for the
    purpose of increasing public awareness of the Area or providing
    information in respect of the conservation measures implemented in
    the Area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Minister shall not
  approve a plan if the cumulative environmental effects of the proposed
  activity, in combination with any other past and current activities
  carried out within the Area, are likely to damage or destroy the
  habitat of living marine organisms in the Area.

## [Reporting of Accidents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719136 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every person involved in an
accident that is likely to result in any disturbance, damage,
destruction or removal prohibited under subsection 3(1) shall, within
two hours after its occurrence, report the accident to the Canadian
Coast Guard.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719138 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
