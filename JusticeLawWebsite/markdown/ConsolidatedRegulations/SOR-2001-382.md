## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673596 .Part}

Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).

## [International Agencies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673599 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed international agencies

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subparagraphs 482(2)(b)(ii) and 944(2)(b)(ii) of the Act, the following
are prescribed international agencies:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Asian Development Bank;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Inter-American Development Bank;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the International Bank for Reconstruction and
  Development;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the International Finance Corporation;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the African Development Bank;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the European Investment Bank;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Caribbean Development Bank;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the Nordic Investment Bank;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development;
  and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the Council of Europe Development Bank.

## [Definition of Widely Distributed]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673612 .Part}

[[widely distributed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in respect of a bank

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraphs 482(2)(d) and 944(2)(d) of the Act, [[widely
  distributed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of debt obligations the distribution of
    which is exempt from the requirement to file a prospectus under the
    laws of Canada, a province or a jurisdiction outside Canada, that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at least 90% of the maximum authorized principal,
      of the debt obligations, is held by one or more persons other than
      the bank or the bank holding company, as the case may be, and its
      subsidiaries, and

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the debt obligations were issued to at least
        25 persons other than the bank or the bank holding company, as
        the case may be, and its subsidiaries within six months after
        the day on which the first of the debt obligations was issued,
        or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligations are issued on a
        continuous basis, there are on average at least 25 holders other
        than the bank or the bank holding company, as the case may be,
        and its subsidiaries, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at the time of their initial distribution, the
      debt obligations met at least three of the following criteria,
      namely,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} their initial term was less than one year,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} they were rated by a rating agency,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} they were distributed through a person
        authorized to trade in securities, and

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} they were distributed in accordance with an
        offering circular or memorandum, or a similar document relating
        to the distribution of securities; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any other debt obligations, that a
    prospectus relating to the issuance of the debt obligations was
    filed under the laws of Canada, a province or a jurisdiction outside
    Canada.

- Definition of [[maximum authorized principal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In subparagraph (1)(a)(i) [[maximum authorized
  principal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to any debt
  obligation, the maximum amount of debt that may be issued under the
  terms of the debt obligation or any agreement relating to it.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673631 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EBEA}[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a Bank, on the date that section 482
  of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) comes into force, as enacted by
  section 127 of the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1), chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a bank holding company, on the date
  that section 944 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) comes into force
  as enacted by section 183 of the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1), chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001.
