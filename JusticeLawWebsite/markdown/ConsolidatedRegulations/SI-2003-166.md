## Coin-operated Devices (Streamlined Accounting Users) Remission Order {#coin-operated-devices-streamlined-accounting-users-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

Coin-operated Devices (Streamlined Accounting Users) Remission Order

P.C. 2003-1620  2003-10-23

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-608988 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible claimant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible claimant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a reporting
    period, means a registrant who has made an election, under section
    227 of the Act, which applies to that reporting period. ([demandeur
    admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning
    on January 1, 1991 and ending on April 23, 1996. ([période
    admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible supply]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible supply]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a supply in respect of
    which the tax payable under Division II of Part IX of the Act would
    be equal to zero because of subsection 165.1(2) of the Act if that
    subsection were in effect at the time the supply was made.
    ([fourniture admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[net tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[net tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in Division
    V of Part IX of the Act. ([taxe nette]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
    subsection 123(1) of the Act. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[registrant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registrant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who, at any time
    during the eligible period, was a registrant within the meaning
    assigned by subsection 123(1) of the Act.
    ([inscrit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reporting period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reporting period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of a registrant has the
    meaning assigned by subsection 123(1) of the Act. ([période de
    déclaration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission of the Goods and Services Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-608998 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 and
5, remission is hereby granted to a registrant of any amount collected
or collectible by the registrant as or on account of tax under Division
II of Part IX of the Act in respect of eligible supplies made by the
registrant in a reporting period of the registrant beginning in the
eligible period and during which the registrant was an eligible
claimant.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The amount of the remission
granted under section 2 with respect to a reporting period of a
registrant is reduced by the total of all amounts that were collected or
collectible by the registrant as or on account of tax under Division II
of Part IX of the Act in respect of the eligible supplies and that are
included in the net tax for the reporting period, or portion of that net
tax, that remains unpaid at the time the registrant files an application
for remission under section 5, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that net tax is a positive amount;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an assessment of that net tax has not been made under
  section 296 of the Act before the time at which the registrant files
  the application; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} such assessment cannot, because of section 298 of the
  Act, be made at or after that time.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission of interest and
penalties paid by a registrant in respect of any amount for which a
remission under section 2 is granted is hereby granted to the
registrant.

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-609005 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A remission shall be
granted if an eligible claimant files an application in writing for the
remission with the Minister of National Revenue not later than two years
after the day on which this Order is made, except to the extent to which
the amount has otherwise been rebated, credited or remitted to the
registrant under the Act or the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11).
