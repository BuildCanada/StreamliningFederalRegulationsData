[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of this
Order, [[fabricated industrial steel components]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
means [[subject goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in paragraph 9
of the Statement of Reasons that the Canadian International Trade
Tribunal issued on June 9, 2017 in Inquiry Number NQ-2016-004.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), remission is granted of the anti-dumping and
  countervailing duties paid or payable under the [Special Import
  Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15) in respect of fabricated industrial
  steel components contained in modules imported into Canada for use in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the LNG Canada Development Inc. project located
    near Kitimat, British Columbia; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Woodfibre LNG Limited project located near
    Squamish, British Columbia.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The remission is granted if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
    Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years after the
    date of importation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the claimant, on request, provides the Canada
    Border Services Agency with evidence demonstrating eligibility for
    remission .

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
