## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-680063 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[community development]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[community development]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the social,
    cultural, economic or environmental enrichment of a
    community. ([développement communautaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[declarant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[declarant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-181, s. 100\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a company referred to in subsection 489.1(1) of
      the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8); or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a company referred to in section 444.2(1) of the
      Trust and Loan Company Act. ([déclarant]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to a declarant,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the first public accountability statement,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all full quarters of the financial year of the
        declarant that begin after the date of coming into force of
        these Regulations, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the financial year of the declarant; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for all subsequent public accountability
      statements, the financial year of the
      declarant. ([période]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[public accountability statement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public accountability statement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    statement that is required to be published

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-181, s. 100\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} under subsection 489.1(1) of the [Insurance
      Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8); or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} under subsection 444.2(1) of the [Trust and Loan
      Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8). ([déclaration
      annuelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Affiliates of Declarant]{.HTitleText1} {#h-680080 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affiliates of declarant

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 489.1(1) of the [Insurance Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) and subsection 444.2(1) of the [Trust and Loan
  Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), the affiliates of a declarant in
  respect of which a public accountability statement is required to be
  published are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the affiliates of the declarant that are finance
    entities as defined in the [Finance Entity
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-388) except for an affiliate
    that is a finance entity that makes or refinances loans to, or
    enters into any other similar arrangements for advancing funds or
    credit with, only entities that are its affiliates; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the affiliates of the declarant that are financial
    institutions with equity of less than one billion dollars, other
    than foreign institutions that carry on business exclusively outside
    of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 489.1(1) of the
  [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) and subsection 444.2(1) of
  the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), the affiliates
  of a declarant in respect of which a public accountability statement
  is not required to be published in a given period are the entities
  referred to in paragraphs (1)(a) and (b) in respect of which a public
  accountability statement is published by another declarant for that
  period.

## [Content of Public Accountability Statement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-680088 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}What statement must contain

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) to (4), the public accountability statement must
  contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a list of affiliates of the declarant in respect of
    which the statement is published;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a list of affiliates of the declarant in respect of
    which the public accountability statement of another declarant is
    published for the period and the name of the other declarant;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} detailed examples, in relation to the declarant and
    the affiliates in respect of which the statement is published,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} of their goals in the area of community
      development and of their participation during the period in
      activities for the purpose of community development, including the
      making of financial contributions for that purpose,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} of activities undertaken on their behalf during
      the period by their employees on a voluntary basis for the purpose
      of community development,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} of charitable donations that they made during
      the period,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} of their philanthropic activities, other than
      charitable donations, during the period, including their total
      value in money to the extent that the value of those activities
      can be expressed in money, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} of any new initiatives or technical assistance
      programs that they undertook during the period in relation to

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} financing for small businesses, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} investments or partnerships in micro-credit
        programs;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total value in money of either

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the charitable donations made during the period
      by the declarant and the affiliates in respect of which the
      statement is published, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the charitable donations made during the period
      by the financial group of which the declarant is a member;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a report, in respect of the declarant and the
    affiliates in respect of which the statement is published, setting
    out the total amount of money that they authorized to be made
    available during the period by way of debt financing to firms in
    Canada, and then setting out that amount broken down, by province,
    in accordance with the amount of debt financing that was authorized,
    and the number of firms to which it was authorized, within each of
    the following ranges, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} \$0 to \$24,999,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} \$25,000 to \$99,999,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} \$100,000 to \$249,999,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} \$250,000 to \$499,999,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} \$500,000 to \$999,999,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} \$1,000,000 to \$4,999,999, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} \$5,000,000 and greater;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an overview of initiatives undertaken during the
    period, by the declarant and by the affiliates in respect of which
    the statement is published, to improve access to financial services
    for low-income individuals, senior citizens and disabled persons;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a declarant that is a bank referred
    to in subsection 459.3(1) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) or a
    company referred to in subsection 444.2(1) of the [Trust and Loan
    Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), a listing of the street addresses
    --- including, if the address is shared, the precise location --- of
    the facilities that were opened or closed by the declarant in each
    province during the period, if those facilities are or were

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} branches that are open to the public and at which
      a product or service is offered, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} facilities, other than branches, at which the
      declarant has accepted deposits from customers or disbursed cash
      to customers;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the number of individuals employed in each province
    at financial year-end by the declarant and by the affiliates in
    respect of which the statement is published, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of full-time positions held by those
      individuals, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the number of part-time positions held by those
      individuals; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the total amount of income and capital taxes paid
    or payable by the financial group of which the declarant is a member
    in respect of its financial year, broken down by total amounts paid
    or payable to federal and provincial governments.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subparagraph (1)(c)(v) and paragraph 1(e) do not
  apply to insurance companies if they did not participate in the
  activities described in those provisions over the course of the
  period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A declarant may refuse to report the information
  required under paragraph (1)(e) for the ranges set out in
  subparagraphs (vi) and (vii) if, by providing that information, a firm
  could be identified.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a firm could be identified by the declarant
  reporting, for a province, the information required under paragraph
  (1)(e) for the ranges set out in subparagraphs (1)(e)(i) to (v), the
  declarant may report that information together with the information
  reported for another province, provided that the declarant indicates
  that fact in the report and identifies the provinces concerned.

## [Time and Manner Requirements for Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-680129 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time requirement

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A declarant must prepare a
public accountability statement and file a written copy of it with the
Commissioner within 135 days after the end of each period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of disclosure

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The declarant must disclose
the public accountability statements to its customers and to the public
by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} informing its customers and the public within 135
  days after the end of each period, through advertising, making public
  announcements, posting notices, mailing information or otherwise,
  about any means by which the statement is available to the customers
  and the public;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} making copies of the statement available on request
  to customers and the public at no charge; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} posting the statement on its websites through which
  products or services are offered in Canada.
