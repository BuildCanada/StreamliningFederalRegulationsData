## Solvent Degreasing Regulations {#solvent-degreasing-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-283
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 2003-07-24

Solvent Degreasing Regulations

P.C. 2003-1145 2003-07-24
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 332(1) of the [Canadian Environmental
Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EFBDA}, the Minister of the
Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on December 7, 2002, a copy
of the proposed [Solvent Degreasing
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-283), substantially in the form
set out in the annexed Regulations, and persons were given an
opportunity to file comments with respect to the proposed Regulations or
to file a notice of objection requesting that a board of review be
established and stating the reasons for the objection;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e-ID0EFBDA){#fna_e}S.C.
  1999, c. 33
  :::

And whereas, pursuant to subsection 93(3) of that Act, the National
Advisory Committee has been given an opportunity to provide its advice
under section 6 of that Act;

And whereas, in the opinion of the Governor in Council, pursuant to
subsection 93(4) of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EBABA}, the proposed Regulations
do not regulate an aspect of a substance that is regulated by or under
any other Act of Parliament in a manner that provides, in the opinion of
the Governor in Council, sufficient protection to the environment and
human health;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment and the Minister of
Health, pursuant to subsection 93(1) of the [Canadian Environmental
Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EDAAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Solvent Degreasing Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-283).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703426 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[cold degreaser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cold degreaser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means equipment designed for
    using a solvent in its liquid form to remove soils from a substrate
    by spraying, brushing, flushing, agitating or
    immersing. ([dégraisseur à froid]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[consumption unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[consumption unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to a solvent,
    means each kilogram of the solvent that may be used in a degreasing
    process in excess of the threshold set out in column 3 of Schedule 1
    and that has been issued by the Minister under section 4. ([unité de
    consommation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[degreasing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[degreasing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a physical process that
    uses solvents to remove soils from a substrate by dissolving or
    dispersing the soils. ([dégraissage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[degreasing process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[degreasing process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a process set out
    in column 2 of Schedule 1. ([procédé de
    dégraissage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reclaimed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reclaimed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to a solvent, means
    recovered, reprocessed and upgraded through processes such as
    filtering, drying, distillation and chemical treatment to restore
    the solvent to a state that renders it reusable in a degreasing
    process. ([régénéré]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[recovered]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[recovered]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to a solvent, means
    collected after the solvent has been used, or collected from
    machinery, equipment or containers during servicing or before
    disposal of the machinery, equipment or
    container. ([récupéré]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[recycled]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[recycled]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to a solvent, means
    recovered and cleaned by a process such as filtering or drying for
    its reuse in a degreasing process. ([recyclé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[residue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[residue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any solid, liquid or sludge
    waste that is generated by a degreasing
    process. ([résidus]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[soils]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[soils]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes uncured coatings, adhesives,
    inks and contaminants such as dirt, oil and
    grease. ([salissures]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[solvent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[solvent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance set out in column
    1 of Schedule 1. ([solvant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vapour degreaser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vapour degreaser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means equipment designed
    for using a solvent in its vapour form to remove soils from a
    substrate by condensation of the vapour on the
    substrate. ([dégraisseur à la vapeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[waste management facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[waste management facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a facility
    that is authorized under applicable laws to handle, recycle or
    dispose of solvents and residues. ([installation de gestion des
    déchets]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a calendar
    year. ([année]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703441 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dry cleaning of textile and apparel goods, rugs,
  furs, leathers and other similar articles, except in the course of
  their manufacture; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the use of a continuous scouring machine in a textile
  mill.

## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703445 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall, in a given year, use a solvent set out in column 1 of
  Schedule 1 in a degreasing process set out in column 2 in a quantity
  that exceeds the threshold set out in column 3 on or after the date
  set out in column 4 unless, for each kilogram of solvent that exceeds
  the threshold, the person has a consumption unit issued by the
  Minister under subsection 4(1) or transferred under section 6.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), in a determination of the
  quantity of a solvent used in a degreaser, only the amount of solvent
  that is added to the equipment shall be considered used.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In a determination of the quantity of a solvent used
  in a degreasing process, the amount of the solvent that has been
  reclaimed or recycled on site and reused at that site by the person in
  the same degreasing process shall not be counted.

## [Issuance of Consumption Units]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703450 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall, on request, issue consumption units to a person for a
  given solvent and a specific degreasing process for the year beginning
  on the date set out in column 4 of Schedule 1 or for any subsequent
  year, if the following conditions are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person has used the solvent in that process in
    the year immediately preceding that date;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the request was submitted to the Minister before
    October 1 of the year preceding that date;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the request is made on a form provided by the
    Minister and contains the information specified in Schedule 2 for
    each of the years relied on to establish the person's average annual
    consumption under subsection (4);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} at the time of submitting the request, the person
    had documentary evidence to support the information referred to in
    paragraph (c); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the person has been informed in writing by the
    Minister of their average annual consumption.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person's consumption units for a year are equal
  to the person's average annual consumption determined in accordance
  with subsection (4), multiplied by the percentage set out in column 4
  of Schedule 3, less the threshold set out in column 3 of Schedule 1,
  less any consumption unit retired under section 5.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the calculation in subsection (2) results in a
  number that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is zero or less than zero, no consumption units are
    issued to the person; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not a whole number, the units are rounded to the
    nearest whole number or, if the result is equidistant from two whole
    numbers, to the higher of them.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person's average annual consumption in respect of a
  solvent used in a specific degreasing process is established as
  follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the person used the solvent in that process in
    the last year of the period set out column 5 of Schedule 1 but not
    in the preceding year, the average annual consumption is the
    quantity of the solvent used by the person during that year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person used the solvent in that process in
    the last two years of the period set out in column 5 of Schedule 1
    but not in the year prior to those two years, the annual consumption
    is the average quantity of the solvent used by the person during
    each of those two years;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the person used the solvent in that process in
    the last three years of the period set out in column 5 of Schedule 1
    but not in the year prior to those three years, the annual
    consumption is the average quantity of the solvent used by the
    person during each of those three years; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the person used the solvent in that process in
    earlier years of the period set out in column 5 of Schedule 1, in
    addition to the last three years of that period, the annual
    consumption is the average quantity of the solvent used by the
    person during each of three consecutive years that the person
    chooses from that period and identifies in the request.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Following a person's request, the Minister shall
  inform the person in writing, of their average annual consumption.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall inform the person in writing of
  the number of consumption units issued to that person before the
  beginning of the year for which the Minister issues the units.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Consumption units are valid only for the year for
  which they are issued.

## [Retirement of Consumption Units]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703472 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person wishes to retire a consumption unit, the person shall submit to
  the Minister the information specified in Schedule 4 on a form
  provided by the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A consumption unit that is retired shall not be
  reissued.

## [Transfer of Consumption Units]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703476 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  may transfer unexpended consumption units to another person for use in
  the same year and in the same degreasing process for which the
  consumption units were issued.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person shall not transfer consumption units until
  they have been informed in writing that the Minister allows the
  transfer under subsection (4).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The transferor and transferee shall jointly apply for
  the transfer of consumption units and shall submit to the Minister the
  information specified in Schedule 5 on a form provided by the
  Minister.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall allow a transfer, and shall so
  inform the transferor and transferee in writing, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the transferor has an unexpended consumption unit
    for each consumption unit of the proposed transfer; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the transferee undertakes to use each transferred
    consumption unit in the same year and in the same degreasing process
    for which the transferor's consumption unit was issued.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister discovers that the transferor did not
  have the unexpended consumption unit referred to in paragraph (4)(a)
  or that the transferee breached the undertaking referred to in
  paragraph (4)(b), the Minister shall cancel the transfer and transfer
  back to the transferor the consumption units unused by the transferee.

## [Reporting and Record Keeping --- Consumption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703485 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every person who has a
consumption unit shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} submit a report to the Minister containing the
  information specified in Schedule 6 on a form provided by the
  Minister, no later than 30 days after the end of the year to which the
  consumption unit applies; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} keep a copy of the report and any documents,
  including shipping records, supporting the information provided to the
  Minister or showing quantities of solvent purchased, returned or
  transferred, or sent to a waste management facility, at the location
  where the solvent is being used, for a period of five years after the
  end of the year in respect of which the report is made.

## [Reporting and Record Keeping --- Sales of Solvent]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703489 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Every person who sells a
solvent for use in degreasing shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} submit a report containing the information specified
  in Schedule 7 to the Minister on a form provided by the Minister, no
  later than 30 days after the end of the year in which the sale occurs;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} keep a copy of the report and any documents,
  including shipping records, supporting the information provided to the
  Minister or showing quantities of solvent purchased, returned or
  transferred, or sent to a waste management facility, at the seller's
  principal place of business in Canada, for a period of five years
  after the year of the sale.

## [Signature of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703495 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Any application, notice or
report submitted to the Minister under these Regulations shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, be signed and dated by
  a person authorized to do so; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, be signed and dated by the person
  submitting the document, or by a person authorized to act on behalf of
  that person.

## [Refusal or Cancellation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703499 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may refuse to issue a consumption unit or may cancel a
  consumption unit if the person requesting it or the user, as the case
  may be, has submitted any false or misleading information.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not refuse to issue, or shall not
  cancel, a consumption unit unless the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has provided the person requesting the issuance or
    the user, as the case may be, with written reasons for refusing to
    issue the consumption unit or for cancelling it; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has allowed that person a period of at least 30
    days after sending the notice in which to make oral or written
    representations in respect of the refusal or cancellation.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703505 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.

## [SCHEDULE 2]{.scheduleLabel}[(Paragraph 4(1)(c))]{.OriginatingRef}[Information for a Consumption Unit Request]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-703516 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- [1]{.lawlabel} Applicant Information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name, civic and postal addresses, e-mail address,
    if any, telephone number and fax number, if any

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} number of facilities and civic address for each
    facility

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} name, civic and postal addresses, e-mail address,
    if any, telephone number and fax number, if any, of the person
    authorized to submit the request on behalf of the applicant

- [2]{.lawlabel} Confidentiality

  Indicate if a request for confidentiality is being made under section
  313 of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
  1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) and the reason for the request.

- [3]{.lawlabel} Solvent and Degreasing Process Information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name of solvent (see column 1 of Schedule 1)

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} name of degreasing process (see column 2 of
    Schedule 1)

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} date of commencement of the use of the solvent in
    the degreasing process

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} years to be used for the average annual consumption

- [4]{.lawlabel} Information concerning average annual consumption

  For each year relied on to establish the annual consumption, provide
  the following information with quantities in kilograms with respect to
  the solvent:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} opening inventory;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} quantity supplied by sellers (identify each seller
    by name and civic address);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} quantity supplied by others (identify each supplier
    by name and civic address);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} quantity sold or transferred to others (identify
    each purchaser or transferee by name and civic address); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} closing inventory.

## [SCHEDULE 5]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsection 6(3))]{.OriginatingRef}[Information for a Consumption Unit Transfer]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-703558 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- [1]{.lawlabel} Transferor Information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name, civic and postal addresses, e-mail address,
    if any, telephone number and fax number, if any

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} number of facilities and civic address for each
    facility

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} name, civic and postal addresses, e-mail address,
    if any, telephone number and fax number, if any, of the person
    authorized to submit the information on behalf of the transferor

- [2]{.lawlabel} Transferee Information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name, civic and postal addresses, e-mail address,
    if any, telephone number and fax number, if any

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} number of facilities and civic address for each
    facility

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} name, civic and postal addresses, e-mail address,
    if any, telephone number and fax number, if any, of the person
    authorized to submit the information on behalf of the transferee

- [3]{.lawlabel} Confidentiality

  Indicate if a request for confidentiality is being made under section
  313 of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
  1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) and the reason for the request.

- [4]{.lawlabel} Solvent and Degreasing Process Information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name of solvent for which consumption units were
    issued (see column 1 of Schedule 1)

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} name of degreasing process for which consumption
    units were issued (see column 2 of Schedule 1)

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} year to which the issued consumption units apply

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} effective date of consumption unit transfer

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} consumption units, in kilograms, for the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} issued to the transferor before transfer

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} transferor's unexpended units before transfer

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} unexpended consumption units to be transferred

## [SCHEDULE 6]{.scheduleLabel}[(Paragraph 7(a))]{.OriginatingRef}[Information for Annual Solvent Consumption Report]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-703579 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- [1]{.lawlabel} Person Using Solvent

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name, civic and postal addresses, e-mail address,
    if any, telephone number and fax number, if any

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} number of facilities and civic address for each
    facility

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} name, civic and postal addresses, e-mail address,
    if any, telephone number and fax number, if any, of the person
    authorized to submit the report on behalf of the person using
    solvent

- [2]{.lawlabel} Confidentiality

  Indicate if a request for confidentiality is being made under section
  313 of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
  1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) and the reason for the request.

- [3]{.lawlabel} Solvent and Degreasing Process Information

  The following information must be given for each solvent and
  degreasing process:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} applicable year of report;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} name of solvent and degreasing process;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} average annual consumption, in kilograms;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} consumption units issued by the Minister, in
    kilograms; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} total quantity of solvent, in kilograms,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in opening inventory,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} supplied by sellers (identify each seller by
      name and civic address),

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} supplied by others (identify each other
      supplier by name and civic address),

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} transferred to use in a degreasing process from
      other on-site uses (identify each other on-site use),

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} transferred to use in a degreasing process from
      other off-site uses (identify each other off-site use),

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} reclaimed or recycled on-site,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} transferred on-site for other uses (identify
      each other use),

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} transferred off-site for other uses (identify
      each other use),

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} sold or transferred to others (identify each
      other purchaser or transferee by name and civic address), and

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} in closing inventory.

## [SCHEDULE 7]{.scheduleLabel}[(Paragraph 8(a))]{.OriginatingRef}[Information for Annual Solvent Sales Report]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-703604 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- [1]{.lawlabel} Seller Information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name, civic and postal addresses, e-mail address,
    if any, telephone number and fax number, if any

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} number of facilities and civic address for each
    facility

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} name, civic and postal addresses, e-mail address,
    if any, telephone number and fax number, if any, of the person
    authorized to submit the report on behalf of the seller

- [2]{.lawlabel} Confidentiality

  Indicate if a request for confidentiality is being made under section
  313 of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
  1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) and the reason for the request.

- [3]{.lawlabel} Solvent Information

  Total quantity, in kilograms, of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} solvent sold (identify each purchaser by name and
    civic address, and specify date of sale);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} solvent returned by purchasers (identify each
    purchaser by name and civic address, and specify date of return);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} recycled solvent sold (identify each purchaser by
    name and civic address, and specify date of sale);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} recycled solvent returned by purchasers (identify
    each purchaser by name and civic address, date of return);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} reclaimed solvent sold (identify each purchaser by
    name and civic address, and specify date of sale); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} reclaimed solvent returned by purchasers (identify
    each purchaser by name and civic address, and specify date of
    return).
