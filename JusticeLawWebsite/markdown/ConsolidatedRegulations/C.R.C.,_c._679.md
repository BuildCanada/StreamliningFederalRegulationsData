## Canadian Forces Laundries Order {#canadian-forces-laundries-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 679
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Use of the Laundries Operated by the Canadian Armed
Forces
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560381 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Canadian Forces Laundries Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._679).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560383 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of National
    Defence; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[laundry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[laundry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a facility operated by the
    Department for laundering, dry cleaning or pressing public property;
    ([service de blanchissage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Defence. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Personal Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560388 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Laundries may be utilized
with respect to the personal property of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Canadian Forces personnel and their dependents,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} civilians employed by the Department, and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} civilian employees of contractors performing work for
  the Department,

when civilian laundry facilities are not available in the area, when the
capacity of the laundries will permit the laundering, dry cleaning or
pressing of such personal property, when it does not interfere with the
laundering and dry cleaning of public property, and providing such
service does not entail an increase to establishment or equipment.

## [Rates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560394 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The personal property of
the persons described in section 3 may be accepted for laundering, dry
cleaning and pressing by laundries in accordance with rates and
conditions prescribed from time to time by the Minister.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The personal property of
persons described in section 3 may be accepted for pressing at the rate
of one-half the price (to the closest five cents upwards) for the
complete dry cleaning service of similar items authorized by the
Canadian Forces.

## [Claims]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560397 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Claims in respect of loss
or damage to personal property accepted for laundering, dry cleaning or
pressing shall be settled as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} individual claims not in excess of \$15 may be
  settled as prescribed by the Minister;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} individual claims in excess of \$15 involving the
  negligence of an officer or servant while acting within the scope of
  his duties or employment, may be settled pursuant to the provisions of
  the National Defence Claims Order, 1970;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} individual claims in excess of \$15 but not in excess
  of \$200 not involving negligence of an officer or servant acting
  within the scope of his duties or employment, may be settled as
  prescribed by the Minister; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} individual claims in excess of \$200 not involving
  negligence of an officer or servant acting within the scope of his
  duties or employment, are to be settled in each case as authorized by
  the Treasury Board.
