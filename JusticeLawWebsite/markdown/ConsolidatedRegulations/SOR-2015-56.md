[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to a person who satisfies the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person was employed by Atomic Energy of Canada
    Limited and became employed by Candu Energy Inc. on October 2, 2011
    as a result of an agreement between Atomic Energy of Canada Limited,
    Candu Energy Inc., and SNC Lavalin Group Inc., signed on June 29,
    2011, and has continued to be so employed without interruption until
    the coming into force of these Regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person ceased to be deemed to be employed in
    the public service for the purposes of the [Public Service
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) (the "Act") as a result of Candu
    Energy Inc. ceasing on October 2, 2014 to be deemed to be part of
    the public service as a result of that agreement and a direction of
    the Treasury Board dated June 23, 2011 made under subsection 40.1(2)
    of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- person re-employed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to a person who is
  re-employed by Candu Energy Inc.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions --- survivors and children

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 2, 3 and 7 do not apply to the survivor and
  children of a person who has received a return of contributions or has
  exercised an option under subsection 6(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 10(5) of Act

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 10(5) of the Act, the one-year period referred to in
paragraph (a) of that subsection begins on the day on which the person
ceases to be employed by Candu Energy Inc.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Survivor and children benefits

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The survivor and children
of a person who dies while employed by Candu Energy Inc. are entitled to
one of the following benefits to which they would have been entitled if
the person had been employed in the public service:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the death benefit referred to in subsection 12(8) of
  the Act; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the allowances referred to in subsection 13(3) of the
  Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sections 12 and 13 of Act

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 and 13 of the Act, the age of a person when they cease to be
employed in the public service is their age on the day on which they
cease to be employed by Candu Energy Inc.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pensionable service

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12, 13 and 13.01 of the Act, pensionable service includes the
period of service that begins on October 2, 2014 and ends on the day on
which a person ceases to be employed by Candu Energy Inc.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When certain provisions are applicable

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections
  12, 13 and 13.01 of the Act only apply to a person as of the day on
  which they cease to be employed by Candu Energy Inc.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, if on October 2, 2014, were it not for these
  Regulations, the person would be entitled to a return of contributions
  under subsection 12(3) of the Act, they may request, in writing, the
  return of contributions no later than October 1, 2015, and if, in the
  same circumstances, they would be entitled to exercise an option under
  section 13.01 of the Act, they may exercise the option within the same
  period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 26(2) of Act

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 26(2) of the Act, a person is deemed to cease to be employed
in the public service on the day on which they cease to be employed by
Candu Energy Inc.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Group 1 contributor

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A person to whom these
Regulations apply is considered to be a Group 1 contributor.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
deemed to have come into force on October 2, 2014.
