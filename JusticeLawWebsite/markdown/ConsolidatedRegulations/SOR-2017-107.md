## Designation of Regulatory Provisions for Purposes of Enforcement (Canada Wildlife Act) Regulations {#designation-of-regulatory-provisions-for-purposes-of-enforcement-canada-wildlife-act-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2017-107
:::

[CANADA WILDLIFE ACT](/eng/acts/W-9)

Registration 2017-06-02

Designation of Regulatory Provisions for Purposes of Enforcement (Canada
Wildlife Act) Regulations

P.C. 2017-566 2017-06-02
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to paragraph 12(k)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2017-107_e_hq_20317){#fn_SOR-2017-107_e_hq_20317-ID0EGCAA}
of the [Canada Wildlife Act](/eng/acts/W-9)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SOR-2017-107_e_hq_20318){#fn_SOR-2017-107_e_hq_20318-ID0EDCAA},
makes the annexed [Designation of Regulatory Provisions for Purposes of
Enforcement (Canada Wildlife Act)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2017-107).

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2017-107_e_hq_20317-ID0EGCAA){#fn_SOR-2017-107_e_hq_20317}S.C.
  2009, c. 14, s. 47(2)
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SOR-2017-107_e_hq_20318-ID0EDCAA){#fn_SOR-2017-107_e_hq_20318}R.S., c.

  :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated provisions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The provisions set out in
the schedule are designated for the purposes of paragraph 13(1)(b) of
the [Canada Wildlife Act](/eng/acts/W-9).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Ind435B_hq_20325){#fn_Ind435B_hq_20325-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
subsection 47(2) of the Environmental Enforcement Act, chapter 14 of the
Statutes of Canada, 2009, comes into force, but if they are registered
after that day, they come into force on the day on which they are
registered.
