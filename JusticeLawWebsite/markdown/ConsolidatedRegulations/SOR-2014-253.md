[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[major event]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[major event]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an event of national or
    international significance that is held within or outside Canada and
    in which the Royal Canadian Mounted Police is involved, including an
    official visit to Canada by Her Majesty, members of the Royal
    Family, a head of state or a representative of a foreign government,
    or a sporting event, conference, summit or exhibition. ([évènement
    majeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[major investigation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[major investigation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an
    investigation of national or international interest or of a complex
    case in which the Royal Canadian Mounted Police is
    involved. ([enquête majeure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an activity in which the
    Royal Canadian Mounted Police is involved that is necessary and that
    relates to the protection of the
    public. ([opération]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Circumstances for appointment

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 50.2(1) of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), a person may be appointed as a casual worker to
the Royal Canadian Mounted Police for a period of more than 90 working
days in one calendar year for a major investigation, a major event or an
operation if the services of the person are required as a result of
unforeseen circumstances such as an unknown duration or the unexpected
need for a particular skill.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}S.C. 2013, c. 18
