## Bait Services Fee Order {#bait-services-fee-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/96-180
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1996-03-29

Order Prescribing the Fee to Be Paid for Bait Services Provided by the
Department of Fisheries and Oceans
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to Order in Council P.C.
1988-440 of March 10, 1988[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EDBDA},
hereby makes the annexed [Order prescribing the fee to be paid for bait
services provided by the Department of Fisheries and
Oceans](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-180).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e-ID0EDBDA){#footnote1star_e}SI/88-41,
  1988 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2010
  :::

Ottawa, March 29, 1996

FRED MIFFLIN

*Minister of Fisheries and Oceans*
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979763 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Bait Services Fee Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-180).

## [Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979765 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The fee to be paid by a
person to whom bait services are provided by the Department of Fisheries
and Oceans is \$ 0.60 per kilogram of bait.
