## Authorizations to Transport Restricted Firearms and Prohibited Firearms Regulations {#authorizations-to-transport-restricted-firearms-and-prohibited-firearms-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-206
:::

[FIREARMS ACT](/eng/acts/F-11.6)

Registration 1998-03-24

Authorizations to Transport Restricted Firearms and Prohibited Firearms
Regulations

P.C. 1998-481 1998-03-24
::::

:::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 118 of the [Firearms
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA}, the Minister
of Justice had a copy of the proposed [Authorizations to Transport
Restricted Firearms and Prohibited Firearms
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-206), substantially in the annexed
form, laid before each House of Parliament on November 27, 1996, which
date is at least 30 sitting days before the date of this Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to section 117 of
the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)^a^, hereby makes the annexed
[Authorizations to Transport Restricted Firearms and Prohibited Firearms
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-206).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1995, c.
  39
  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019667 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-269, s. 2
:::

## [Threat to Safety]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019671 .Part}

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** A chief firearms
officer may issue to an individual an authorization to transport if the
chief firearms officer determines that the transportation of a
restricted firearm or prohibited firearm, as the case may be, between
two or more specified places will not pose a threat to the safety of the
individual or any other individual.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-269, s. 1
:::

## [Handguns]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1378331 .Part}

**[[1.11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.11 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 1.1,
the chief firearms officer may issue to an individual an authorization
to transport a handgun from a port of entry only if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the individual holds a registration certificate for
  the handgun that was issued on the basis of an application that,
  before the day on which this provision comes into force, was made in
  accordance with section 54 of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the individual possesses an authorization to carry
  the handgun under section 20 of the Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the individual requires the handgun to train, compete
  or coach in a handgun shooting discipline that is on the programme of
  the International Olympic Committee or the International Paralympic
  Committee, and the individual provides a letter to a chief firearms
  officer from a provincial or national sport shooting governing body
  indicating

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the individual trains, competes or coaches in
    such a discipline,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the specific discipline in which the individual
    trains, competes or coaches, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that the handgun that the individual seeks to
    transport is necessary for training, competing or coaching in that
    specific discipline; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a non-resident individual, a customs
  officer confirms, under section 35 of the Act, the declaration and
  authorization to transport referred to in that section in respect of
  the handgun.

::: HistoricalNote
- [SOR/2022-219, s. 3](#1377978-1378341){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1377978-1378341.html"
  role="button"}
:::

## [Manner of Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019675 .Part}

**[[1.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 54(1) of the Act, an application for an authorization to
transport a restricted firearm or prohibited firearm made by an
individual, other than a non-resident, who holds a licence and a
registration certificate for the firearm or by a non-resident may, in
addition to being made in the prescribed form, be made by telephone by
either speaking to a person or to an interactive voice response system.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-269, s. 3
:::

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019679 .Part}

**[[1.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 54(1) of the Act, an application that is made by telephone
under section 1.2 shall include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant\'s name;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reason for transporting the firearm;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the frequency, dates and times that the firearm will
  be transported; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the place of departure and the destination of the
  transported firearm.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-269, s. 3
:::

**[[1.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.4 .sectionLabel}** In addition to the
information referred to in section 1.3, an application that is made by
telephone under section 1.2 by an individual who holds a licence and a
registration certificate for the firearm shall include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant\'s licence number;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of the registration certificate for the
  firearm;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the firearm identification number for the firearm;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is an individual other than a
  non-resident and is not the owner of the firearm, the owner\'s licence
  number.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-269, s. 3
:::

**[[1.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.5 .sectionLabel}** In addition to the
information referred to in section 1.3, an application that is made by
telephone under section 1.2 by a non-resident who does not hold a
licence, or who holds a licence but not a registration certificate for
the firearm, shall include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the confirmation number of the applicant\'s
  declaration provided by a customs officer under section 3 of the
  [Importation and Exportation of Firearms Regulations
  (Individuals)](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-215);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a non-resident who does not hold a
  licence, the applicant\'s residence address and mailing address;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a non-resident who holds a licence,
  their licence number;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the make, model, type and action of the firearm and
  its serial number, if any; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of ammunition that the cartridge
  magazine of the firearm can contain.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-269, s. 3
:::

## [Renewal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019702 .Part}

**[[1.6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 67(1) of the Act, the manner in which an authorization to
transport is renewed is any of the manners in which it can be issued.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-269, s. 3
:::

## [Number of Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019706 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** An authorization to
transport may authorize the transportation of one or more restricted
firearms or prohibited firearms.

## [Contents of Authorizations to Transport that Take the Form of Licence Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019708 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An authorization to
transport that takes the form of a condition attached to a licence must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} identify all of the firearms to which the
  authorization to transport applies; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} specify

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the period for which the authorization to transport
    is issued,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the places between which the firearms to which it
    applies may be transported, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for which the firearms may be
    transported between the specified places.

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019715 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A chief firearms officer
who issues an authorization to transport shall attach to it the
condition that the firearm be transported by a route that, in all the
circumstances, is reasonably direct.

## [Revocation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019717 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A chief firearms officer
who issues an individual\'s authorization to transport shall revoke it
if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the individual\'s licence to possess any of the
  firearms referred to in the authorization is revoked or reaches its
  expiry date; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the chief firearms officer becomes aware that the
  individual\'s physical or mental state has deteriorated to an extent
  that may affect the safety of the individual or of any other person.

## [Notice of Refusal or Revocation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019721 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A notice
  of a decision to refuse to issue an authorization to transport or to
  revoke an authorization to transport is sufficiently given if the
  notice is addressed to the applicant for or holder of the
  authorization at their address that is set out in the application for
  the authorization or, if the individual has advised the chief firearms
  officer of a change of that address, at the new address, and the
  notice is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} delivered personally, at any time that is
    reasonable in the circumstances;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sent by registered mail or by courier; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} transmitted by electronic means that can produce a
    paper record.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice is deemed to be received

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the day of delivery, if delivered personally;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the fifth working day, excluding Saturdays and
    holidays, after

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the postmark date, if it is sent by mail, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date of shipment on the waybill, if it is
      sent by courier; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the day of transmission, if sent by electronic
    means.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-269, s. 4
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019735 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on December 1, 1998.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-471, s. 16
:::
