## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726959 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Trust and Loan Companies
Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8).

## [Record Date]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726961 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 140(5) of the Act, except paragraphs 140(5)(c)
  and (d), the prescribed period for the record date is not more than 60
  days before the day on which the particular action is to be taken.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraphs 140(5)(c) and (d) of
  the Act, the prescribed period for the record date is not less than 21
  days and not more than 60 days before the day of the meeting.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection 140(7) of the Act, the
  prescribed period for the notice of the record date to be given is not
  less than seven days before the date fixed.

## [Notice of Meeting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726966 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 141(1) of the Act, the prescribed period for the notice to be
sent is not less than 21 days and not more than 60 days before the day
of the meeting.

## [Shareholder Proposals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726968 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of subsection 146(1.1) of the Act, the prescribed number of
  the company\'s outstanding shares is the number of voting shares

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is equal to 1% of the total number of the
    company\'s outstanding voting shares as of the day on which the
    proposal is submitted, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whose fair market value, as determined at the close
    of business on the day before the day on which the proposal is
    submitted, is at least \$2,000;

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 146(1.1) of the Act,
  the prescribed period for the person to have been the registered
  holder or beneficial owner is the six-month period immediately before
  the day on which the proposal is submitted.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 146(1.4) of the Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company may request, within 14 days after it
  receives the person\'s proposal, that the person provide the proof
  that they meet the requirements; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person shall provide the proof that they meet the
  requirements within 21 days after receiving the company\'s request.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 146(3) of the Act, the proposal and the statement in support
of it together are not to exceed 500 words.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 146(5)(a) of the Act, the prescribed number of days is 90
days.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 146(5)(c) of the Act, the prescribed period is two years.

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraph 146(5)(d) of the Act, the prescribed minimum
  amount of support for a proposal is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 3% of the total number of shares voted if the
    proposal was introduced at an annual meeting;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 6% of the total number of shares voted at its last
    submission if the proposal was introduced at two annual meetings;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 10% of the total number of shares voted at its last
    submission if the proposal was introduced at three or more annual
    meetings.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph 146(5)(d) of the Act,
  the prescribed period is five years.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 146(5.1) of the Act, the prescribed period within which the
company is not required to set out a proposal in or attach it to a
management proxy circular is two years.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 147(1) of the Act, the prescribed period for the person to be
notified is 21 days after the day on which the company receives the
proposal or the proof under subsection 146(1.4) of the Act if it has
requested that proof.

## [Electronic Voting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726990 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 154(5) of the Act, when a vote is held at a meeting, the
voting may be carried out by means of a telephonic, electronic or other
communication facility, if the facility

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} enables the votes to be gathered in a manner that
  permits their subsequent verification; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} permits the tallied votes to be presented to the
  company without it being possible for the company to identify how each
  shareholder or group of shareholders voted.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726994 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
