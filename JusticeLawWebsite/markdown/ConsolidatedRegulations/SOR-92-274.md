Investment Limits (Foreign Companies) Regulations

P.C. 1992-1029  1992-05-14

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to sections 616, 617 and 703 of the
[Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed Regulations respecting investments in Canada by foreign
companies, effective June 1, 1992.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-943308 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Insurance Companies
Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8). (*Loi*)

## [Limits on Investment in Real Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-943310 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
618 of the Act, the prescribed percentage of the value of the assets in
Canada is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 15 per cent in the case of investment by a foreign
  life company for the classes of insurance referred to in subsection
  618(1);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 10 per cent in the case of investment by a foreign
  life company for the classes of insurance referred to in subsection
  618(2); or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} 10 per cent in the case of investment by a foreign
  property and casualty company or a foreign marine company.

## [Limits on Investment in Equities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-943317 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
619 of the Act, the prescribed percentage of the value of the assets in
Canada is 25 per cent.
