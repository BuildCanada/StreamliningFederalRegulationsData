## Order Acknowledging Receipt of the Assessments Done Pursuant to Subsection 23(1) of the Act {#order-acknowledging-receipt-of-the-assessments-done-pursuant-to-subsection-231-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2005-71
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2005-07-27

Order Acknowledging Receipt of the Assessments Done Pursuant to
Subsection 23(1) of the Act

P.C. 2005-1341 2005-07-14
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, hereby acknowledges receipt of the
assessments done pursuant to subsection 23(1) of the [Species at Risk
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBBAA} by the
Committee on the Status of Endangered Wildlife in Canada (COSEWIC) with
respect to the species enumerated in the Annex to this Order.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EBBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2002, c.
  29
  :::
:::
