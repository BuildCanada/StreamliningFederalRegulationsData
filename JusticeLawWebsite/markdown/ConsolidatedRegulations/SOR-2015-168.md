## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-818266 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[conversion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[conversion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the conversion of a mutual
    property and casualty insurance company into a company with common
    shares. ([transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[converted company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[converted company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a property and
    casualty insurance company that was a mutual company and has been
    converted into a company with common shares and, except for the
    purpose of paragraphs 13(1)(g), 14(2)(n) and 16(2)(i), includes a
    holding corporation of that company. ([société
    transformée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[converting company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[converting company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mutual property
    and casualty insurance company whose directors have passed a
    resolution under section 3 recommending conversion of the
    company. ([société en transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligibility date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligibility date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the date on which the
    directors of a mutual property and casualty insurance company pass a
    resolution under section 3 recommending conversion of the
    company. ([date d'admissibilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible mutual policyholder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible mutual policyholder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
    who holds a mutual policy if

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they held it on the eligibility date;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they applied for it before the date specified in
      the resolution passed under section 3 and it was issued after the
      eligibility date but before the day on which the special
      resolution referred to in section 5 is passed; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} they held it before the eligibility date but it
      lapsed before that date and was reinstated after that date but
      before the day on which the special resolution referred to in
      section 5 is passed. ([souscripteur admissible d'une police
      mutuelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible non-mutual policyholder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible non-mutual policyholder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    person who

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} holds a non-mutual policy and has done so for the
      12-month period ending on the eligibility date; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} belongs to any other group of policyholders
      specified in the resolution passed under section 3. ([souscripteur
      admissible d'une police non mutuelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible policyholder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible policyholder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an eligible mutual policyholder; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an eligible non-mutual
      policyholder. ([souscripteur admissible]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[holding corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[holding corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a body corporate
    that is incorporated as a company under the Act and that holds all
    of the voting shares of a converted company. ([société
    mère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[independent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[independent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of an actuary,
    financial market expert or valuation expert, that the actuary,
    financial market expert or valuation expert

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} does not have a conflict of interest with a
      converting company, any of its eligible policyholders or any
      persons or classes of persons who are to be provided with benefits
      as a result of a conversion;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not a related party of the converting company;
      and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is appointed on mutual agreement of the
      converting company and the two policyholder
      committees. ([indépendant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mutual policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mutual policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a policy the holding of
    which entitles its holder to vote at all policyholder meetings of a
    converting company, but does not include a policy issued or assumed
    by a company with common shares that amalgamated with a mutual
    company after the eligibility date. ([police
    mutuelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mutual property and casualty insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mutual property and casualty insurance
    company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mutual company that is also a
    property and casualty company. ([société mutuelle d'assurances
    multirisques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-mutual policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-mutual policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a policy, other than
    a mutual policy, that is issued by a mutual property and casualty
    insurance company. ([police non mutuelle]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[related party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[related party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
    section 518 of the Act. ([personne apparentée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[value of the converting company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[value of the converting company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    estimated market value or range of market values of a converting
    company, excluding

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the value of capital contributions made at the
      time of incorporation as a mutual property and casualty insurance
      company;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} amounts recorded in any account maintained under
      section 70 or 83.04 of the Act; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any expenses expected to be incurred to effect
      the conversion. ([valeur de la société en
      transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-818297 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Companies with non-mutual policyholders

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
mutual property and casualty insurance companies in which some of the
policyholders hold non-mutual policies.

## [Initiation of Conversion Process]{.HTitleText1} {#h-818300 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resolution of directors

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** If the directors of a
mutual property and casualty insurance company wish to pursue its
conversion, they must pass a resolution recommending conversion and
setting out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a date before the eligibility date by which any
  mutual policy that will be issued after the eligibility date and
  before the day on which the special resolution referred to in section
  5 is passed must have been applied for so that its holder qualifies as
  an eligible mutual policyholder; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any group of policyholders who hold non-mutual
  policies --- other than those who have held their policies for the
  12-month period ending on the eligibility date --- who are to qualify
  as eligible non-mutual policyholders.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Before special meeting of eligible
policyholders

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Before seeking
authorization from the Superintendent to send a notice referred to in
paragraph 237(1.2)(a) of the Act, a converting company must ensure that
a conversion proposal has been developed in accordance with sections 5
to 13 and that the right to vote on that conversion proposal and on the
authorization referred to in paragraph 237(1.1)(c) of the Act has been
extended to all eligible non-mutual policyholders at a special meeting
referred to in section 14.

## [Negotiations Between Classes of Policyholders]{.HTitleText1} {#h-818307 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vote to negotiate

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Not less than 30 days after
the eligibility date, the eligible mutual policyholders must vote by
special resolution on whether to negotiate, with the eligible non-mutual
policyholders, the elements referred to in section 12.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization to send notice of intent to
negotiate

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  30 days of an affirmative vote under section 5, a converting company
  must, for the purpose of obtaining the Superintendent's authorization
  to send to its eligible policyholders a notice of intent to negotiate,
  submit to the Superintendent the notice, which must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicate that the eligible mutual policyholders
    have passed a special resolution authorizing the negotiations
    referred to in section 12 with the eligible non-mutual policyholders
    and set out the criteria for determining who qualifies as an
    eligible mutual policyholder or eligible non-mutual policyholder;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} indicate when and how all necessary information
    relevant to the conversion process will be published on the
    converting company's website and how the policyholders may request
    to receive that information by mail; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} contain a summary of the conversion process.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sending and publication of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of the Superintendent's authorization, the
  converting company must send the notice to all of its eligible
  policyholders and publish it on its website.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for initial court order

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Not less
  than 30 days and not more than 45 days after the day on which a notice
  is sent under subsection 6(2), the converting company must file with
  the court an application for an initial order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of initial order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within 60 days after the filing of the application,
  the court must make an initial order setting out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any information and documents that the converting
    company must publish on its website in addition to those required by
    these Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the procedures for making applications or filing
    submissions for the purpose of subsections 8(1) and (5) and
    9(1), (3) and (5);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manner of effecting service for the purpose of
    subsections 8(3) and 9(3);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date, time and location of the hearings
    referred to in subsections 8(6) and 9(6); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the procedures for submitting costs and expenses to
    the court for the purpose of section 11.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent orders

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The court may make any subsequent order that it
  considers necessary to ensure the efficiency, transparency and
  fairness of the negotiations referred to in section 12.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication on website

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A converting company must publish on its website all
  orders made by the court for the purpose of these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application by counsel

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member
  in good standing of the bar --- or of the Chambre des notaires du
  Québec --- of the province in which the application for the initial
  order is made may apply to the court, in the time and manner set out
  in the initial order, to be appointed as counsel for either the
  eligible mutual policyholders or the eligible non-mutual policyholders
  of a converting company in respect of the negotiations referred to in
  section 12.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrictions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Counsel must not have any interest that is in
  conflict with that of the class of policyholders they seek to
  represent and must not be an officer, employee or related party of the
  converting company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Counsel who submit an application under
  subsection (1) must serve a copy of it on the converting company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The converting company must publish on its website
  the names of all counsel who have submitted applications under
  subsection (1) for each class of policyholders, as well as the content
  of their applications or a summary of their experience.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objections

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Eligible policyholders may file, in the time and
  manner set out by the court in the initial order, an objection to the
  candidacy of any counsel who has submitted an application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The court must appoint counsel for each of the two
  classes of policyholders and must notify the converting company of its
  decision following a hearing at which the court assesses the ability
  of each applicant to fairly and adequately represent the interests of
  the class of policyholders in question, including by taking into
  account their experience in financial law, corporate law, securities
  law or mediation and arbitration and by having regard to any
  objections filed under subsection (5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The converting company must publish on its website
  the name and contact information of the selected counsel for each
  class of policyholders.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} All communication with the policyholder committees is
  to be made through counsel for the class of policyholders that the
  committee represents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of appointment

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} Counsel's appointment ends on the day on which the
  notice referred to in subsection 14(1) is sent or the day on which the
  conversion process is terminated, whichever is earlier.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committee membership

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  eligible policyholder may submit to counsel for the class of
  policyholders to which they belong, in the time and manner set out in
  the initial order, an application for membership on the policyholder
  committee for that class.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrictions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Applicants must not have any interest that is in
  conflict with that of the eligible policyholders they seek to
  represent and must not be an officer, employee or related party of the
  converting company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing and service

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Counsel must file with the court all applications
  received under subsection (1) and must serve on counsel for the other
  class of policyholders and on the converting company the names of the
  applicants from the class of policyholders they represent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The converting company must publish on its website
  the names of all applicants for membership on each policyholder
  committee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objections

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any eligible policyholder or counsel for a class of
  policyholders may file, in the time and manner set out by the court in
  the initial order, an objection to the candidacy of a policyholder who
  has submitted an application for membership on a policyholder
  committee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The court must appoint at least three and not more
  than nine eligible policyholders to be members of each policyholder
  committee and must notify the converting company and counsel for the
  class of policyholders represented by that committee of its decision
  following a hearing at which the court assesses the ability of each
  applicant to fairly and adequately represent the interests of the
  class of policyholders in question, including by taking into account
  their experience in negotiations and business and financial affairs
  and by having regard to any objections filed under subsection (5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The converting company must publish on its website
  the names of the members of each policyholder committee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of company in respect of
  policyholder committee members

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} In any legal proceeding, the converting company is
  bound to take up the interest for and assume the defence of any member
  of the policyholder committees for anything done or omitted to be done
  in good faith in the performance of their functions and, if
  applicable, indemnify that member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of representation

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The policyholder committees are disbanded on the day
  on which the notice referred to in subsection 14(1) is sent or the day
  on which the conversion process is terminated, whichever is earlier.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  converting company must, subject to the [Supervisory Information
  (Insurance Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-56) and
  the [Supervisory Information (Insurance Holding Companies)
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-484), provide to counsel for
  each class of policyholders whatever information or documents they
  reasonably require to facilitate the negotiations referred to in
  section 12.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to information and documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any information or document that the converting
  company provides to counsel for one class of policyholders must be
  provided to counsel for the other class.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The court may make orders respecting the
  confidentiality of information provided by the converting company and
  determine under what circumstances the information can be used or
  disclosed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs payable by converting company

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  court must determine, having regard to their reasonableness, the
  amounts payable by a converting company in relation to the
  negotiations referred to in section 12, including the costs of counsel
  and any experts consulted by the policyholder committees, remuneration
  for policyholder committee members and reimbursement of their expenses
  for travel, accommodation and meals in respect of attendance at
  policyholder committee meetings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All amounts determined by the court must be paid by
  the converting company no later than the day on which it seeks the
  Superintendent's authorization to send the notice referred to in
  paragraph 237(1.2)(a) of the Act or the day on which the conversion
  process is terminated under section 21, whichever comes first, or on
  any other day that the court may set.

## [Method and Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-818382 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of negotiations

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  policyholder committees, with the assistance of the counsel appointed
  by the court, must enter into negotiations to establish the method of
  allocating the value of the converting company and to determine
  whether any benefits will be provided to any persons or classes of
  persons --- other than eligible policyholders --- as a result of the
  conversion.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Method of allocating value of
  converting company

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The method of allocating the value of the converting
  company among eligible policyholders and, as the case may be, any
  persons or classes of persons --- other than eligible policyholders
  --- must be described in detail and set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the basis on which any variable amount of benefits
    will be calculated;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any fixed, minimum or maximum amount of benefits to
    be provided to each of them;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the rationale for choosing the method of
    determining and allocating the benefits; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate value of the benefits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of variable amount

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The variable amount of benefits referred to in
  paragraph (2)(a) must, in respect of each eligible policyholder, be
  calculated having regard to at least the following factors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their obligations, rights and benefits;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the premiums paid by them;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the length of time they have held a policy with the
    company; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the historical growth of the company's surplus
    account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons or classes of persons other
  than eligible policyholders

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If benefits will be provided to any persons or
  classes of persons --- other than eligible policyholders --- as a
  result of the conversion, the policyholder committees must establish a
  list of those persons or classes of persons to whom benefits will be
  provided including, any persons or classes of persons suggested by the
  converting company and approved by the policyholder committees.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval by policyholder committees

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The method of allocating the value of the converting
  company and, if any, the list of persons or classes of persons ---
  other than eligible policyholders --- are considered approved by the
  two policyholder committees once at least two thirds of the members of
  each committee approve them.

## [Conversion Proposal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-818402 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of conversion proposal

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  converting company must develop a conversion proposal that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a report setting out the value of the converting
    company as estimated by the company and a description of the method
    used and any assumptions made in estimating that value;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the eligibility date;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the list of persons or classes of persons --- other
    than eligible policyholders --- approved under subsection 12(5), if
    any;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the benefits to be
    provided to eligible policyholders and the persons or classes of
    persons referred to in paragraph (c);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the detailed description of the method approved
    under subsection 12(5);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the mechanisms proposed to effect
    an initial issuance of common shares or any other class of shares,
    including a copy of the proposed by-law authorizing the issuance of
    those shares;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if shares in the converted company are to be issued
    to a holding corporation, a description of the proposed activities
    of the holding corporation;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if shares have been issued and remain outstanding
    immediately prior to the effective date of the conversion, a
    statement describing how those shares will be converted into common
    shares following conversion;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the benefits referred to in paragraph (d)
    include shares, a description of the measures to be taken by the
    converted company, in the two years following the effective date of
    the conversion, to assist the eligible policyholders and the persons
    or classes of persons referred to in paragraph (c) who receive the
    shares to sell those shares on a public market and to address any
    potential imbalances that may arise between the volume of shares
    offered for sale by them and the volume of shares sought for
    purchase by public market participants;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the measures referred to in
    paragraph (i) would be affected if the converted company were to
    issue additional shares in the two years following the effective
    date of the conversion; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the directors of the converting
    company may terminate the conversion process at any time before
    letters patent of conversion are issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Valuation day

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent is authorized to specify the day
  at which the value of a converting company must be estimated by the
  converting company.

## [Special Meeting of Eligible Mutual Policyholders]{.HTitleText1} {#h-818419 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Superintendent's authorization

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Once
  the conversion proposal is completed, a converting company must obtain
  the Superintendent's authorization to send to the eligible mutual
  policyholders a notice of a special meeting at which the eligible
  mutual policyholders may vote by special resolution on whether to
  amend the company's by-laws to permit all eligible non-mutual
  policyholders to vote on the conversion proposal and on the
  authorization referred to in paragraph 237(1.1)(c) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information and documents to
  Superintendent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} To obtain the Superintendent's authorization, the
  converting company must submit to the Superintendent

## [Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-818572 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation to directors, officers or
employees

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  converting company or converted company must not pay any fee,
  compensation or other consideration in relation to the conversion of
  the company to any director, officer or employee of the company, other
  than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the regular compensation provided to the person in
    that person's capacity as a director, officer or employee of the
    company; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any benefits provided to eligible policyholders or
    the persons or classes of persons referred to in paragraph 13(1)(c)
    as a result of a conversion.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contracts for services

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A converting company or converted company must not
  pay any fee, compensation or other consideration under a contract for
  services in relation to the conversion of the company to an entity
  with which a director, an officer or an employee of the company is
  associated in any way unless the terms and conditions of that contract
  are at least as favourable to the company as market terms and
  conditions, as defined in subsection 534(2) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of shares

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A converted company must
not, prior to the listing of its shares on a recognized stock exchange
in Canada and for a period of one year after that listing, issue or
provide shares, share options or rights to acquire shares to the
following persons, other than shares issued to eligible policyholders or
the persons or classes of persons referred to in paragraph 13(1)(c) as a
result of a conversion:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any director, officer or employee of the company; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person who was a director, officer or employee of
  the converted company during the year preceding the day on which the
  conversion takes effect.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acquisition

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During
  a company's first four years as a converted company, the Minister may
  only give an approval under subsection 407(1) of the Act in respect of
  the company if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proposed acquisition would not result in the
    converted company having a major shareholder; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is of the opinion that the converted
    company is, or is about to be, in financial difficulty and that the
    proposed acquisition would facilitate an improvement in its
    financial condition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A body corporate that holds all of the voting shares
  of a converted company and makes an application under paragraph
  39(1)(b) of the Act must, for the duration of the four-year period,
  include in that body corporate's incorporating instrument a provision
  that restricts the issue, transfer or ownership of that body
  corporate's shares, of any class or series, so as to prevent that body
  corporate from having a major shareholder.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal of provision

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), the body corporate may amend
  its incorporating instrument to remove the provision included in it
  under that subsection, if the Minister has given an approval under
  paragraph (1)(b).
