## Coffin or Casket Remission Order {#coffin-or-casket-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/83-88
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1983-05-11

Order Respecting the Remission of Taxes Imposed Under Division III of
Part IX and Under any other Part of the Excise Tax Act on Imported
Coffins or Caskets

P.C. 1983-1250 1983-04-28
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
section 17 of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is
pleased hereby to make the annexed Order respecting the remission of
customs duty and sales and excise taxes on imported coffins or caskets.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639348 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Coffin or Casket Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-83-88).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639350 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted of the taxes paid or payable under Division
III of Part IX and under any other Part of the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on any imported coffin or casket that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is imported by a funeral director who does not
  normally reside in Canada or carry on business in Canada in order to
  transport the remains of a non-resident person who died in Canada,
  where the funeral service and burial or cremation take place outside
  Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is imported into Canada containing the remains of a
  person who died outside Canada, where the funeral service is held in
  Canada and the burial or cremation takes place outside Canada; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is imported into Canada containing the remains of a
  person who died outside Canada, where the burial or cremation takes
  place in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
- SI/91-8, s. 2
- SI/98-9, s. 2
:::

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639357 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted under
this Order on condition that a claim for remission is made in writing to
the Minister of National Revenue, in a form satisfactory to the
Minister, within two years after the date of importation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
:::
