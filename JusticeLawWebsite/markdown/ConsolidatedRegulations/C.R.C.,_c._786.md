## Samples of Negligible Value Remission Order {#samples-of-negligible-value-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 786
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Taxes Paid or Payable Under Division
III of Part IX and Under any Other Part of the Excise Tax Act and
Customs Duties Paid or Payable Under Section 21 of the Customs Tariff on
Samples of Negligible Value
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-562034 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Samples of Negligible Value Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._786).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-562036 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to an
    area or place, means the manager of the customs office or customs
    offices that serve that area or place; ([agent en chef des
    douanes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sample]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sample]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an article that is
    representative of a particular category of goods that have been
    produced or an article for which production is contemplated, and
    includes any package, case, binder or other article attached to or
    imported as an integral part of the article.
    ([échantillon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
:::

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-562042 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission is hereby granted of taxes
  paid or payable under Division III of Part IX and under any other Part
  of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) and customs duties paid or
  payable under section 21 of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)
  in respect of consignments of imported samples of goods of all kinds
  where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the samples are of negligible value;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the samples will be used only in soliciting orders
    for goods of the kind represented by the sample;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 4, there is not more than one
    sample of each kind or quality in a consignment; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the goods referred to in paragraph (b) will be
    supplied direct from abroad.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(a), a sample in a
  consignment shall be deemed to be of negligible value where the taxes
  imposed under Division III of Part IX and under any other Part of the
  [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) and the customs duties imposed under
  section 21 of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) payable on the
  sample would not exceed two dollars if this Order did not apply.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
- SI/91-8, s. 2
- SI/98-18, s. 2
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Consignments of foodstuffs,
non-alcoholic beverages, perfumes and chemical products that will be
consumed or destroyed during demonstration may consist of more than one
sample of each kind or quality when the quantity and the manner in which
they are packaged preclude their being used otherwise than as samples.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A chief officer of customs
may require that a sample be made useless as merchandise by marking,
tearing, perforating, gluing or otherwise altering it, but not in such a
manner as to destroy its usefulness as a sample.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
:::
