## Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order, 1984, No. 1 {#reservation-to-the-crown-waiver-order-1984-no.-1 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/84-962
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 1984-12-07

Order Waiving the Reservation to the Crown with Respect to Certain
Territorial Lands in the Northwest Territories

P.C. 1984-3911 1984-12-06
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 9(b) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Order waiving the reservation to the Crown
with respect to certain territorial lands in the Northwest
Territories](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-856).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-885777 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order, 1984, No.
1](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-962).

## [Waiver]{.HTitleText1} {#h-885779 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The reservation to the
Crown set out in paragraph 9(b) of the [Territorial Lands
Act](/eng/acts/T-7) is hereby waived with respect to a grant of the
territorial lands described in the schedule.

::::: section
:::: {#885781 .Schedule}
::: {}
## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-885782 .scheduleLabel}
:::

All that portion of Boffa Drive in the City of Yellowknife in the
Northwest Territories, as said right-of-way is shown on a plan of survey
of record number 40350 in the Canada Lands Surveys Records at Ottawa, a
copy of which is filed in the Land Titles Office for the Northwest
Territories Land Registration District at Yellowknife under number 70,
as said portion is shown outlined in green on a sketch plan of record on
file 18-1-61 in the Land Management Division of the Department of Indian
Affairs and Northern Development at Ottawa, said portion containing
approximately 0.02 hectares (0.05 acres), more or less.
