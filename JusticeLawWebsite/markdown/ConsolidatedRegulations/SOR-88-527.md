## Manitoba Turkey Marketing (Interprovincial and Export Trade) Order {#manitoba-turkey-marketing-interprovincial-and-export-trade-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-527
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1988-10-17

Order Respecting the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade of
Turkey Produced in Manitoba
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Commodity Board, pursuant to section 3[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFBBA} of the [Manitoba Turkey
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._160), C.R.C., c. 160, hereby makes
the annexed [Order respecting the marketing in interprovincial and
export trade of turkey produced in
Manitoba](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-527).

  :::

Winnipeg, Manitoba, October 12, 1988
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913949 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Manitoba Turkey Marketing (Interprovincial and Export Trade)
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-527).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913951 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Natural Products Marketing Act
    of Manitoba; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[clearance certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[clearance certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a certificate
    issued by order of the Commodity Board authorizing the movement or
    transportation of turkey; ([autorisation de
    transport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Manitoba Turkey
    Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act; ([Office
    de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an inspector appointed under
    the Act; ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person engaged in raising, keeping or preparing
      for slaughter turkeys and includes an employee of the person,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person entitled under a lease or agreement to a
      share of turkey production by a person referred to in
      paragraph (a), or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who, as security for a debt, takes
      possession of turkeys from a person referred to in paragraph (a);
      ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to convey, ship or transfer
    turkeys in interprovincial or export trade by any means;
    ([transporter]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a turkey of any variety, grade
    or class raised or kept for slaughter within the Province of
    Manitoba. ([dindon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913963 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No producer shall market
turkey in interprovincial or export trade unless an order of the
Commodity Board authorizes the producer to do so.

## [Transport]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913965 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall transport
or deliver for transport turkeys without having obtained a clearance
certificate in respect of the turkeys.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every person who transports
turkeys shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the transport of the turkeys, have the
  clearance certificate issued in respect of those turkeys in the
  vehicle used to transport the turkeys and produce the clearance
  certificate for examination by an inspector on the inspector's
  request; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible after the transport of the
  turkeys, report the date of transport, the quantity, origin and
  destination of the turkeys, and the clearance certificate number to
  the Commodity Board.
