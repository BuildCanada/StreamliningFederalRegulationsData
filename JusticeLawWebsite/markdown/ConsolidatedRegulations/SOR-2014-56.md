## Order Declaring an Amnesty Period (2014) {#order-declaring-an-amnesty-period-2014 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2014-56
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 2014-03-13

Order Declaring an Amnesty Period (2014)

P.C. 2014-277 2014-03-13
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 117.14(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_AmnestyOrder2014-E_hq_13472){#fn_AmnestyOrder2014-E_hq_13472-ID0EGCAA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_AmnestyOrder2014-E_hq_13473){#fn_AmnestyOrder2014-E_hq_13473-ID0EDCAA},
makes the annexed [Order Declaring an Amnesty Period
(2014)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-56).

Definition of [[firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following prohibited
firearms:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Ceská Zbrojovka (CZ) Model CZ858 Tactical-2P rifle;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a Ceská Zbrojovka (CZ) Model CZ858 Tactical-2V rifle;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a Ceská Zbrojovka (CZ) Model CZ858 Tactical-4P rifle;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a Ceská Zbrojovka (CZ) Model CZ858 Tactical-4V rifle;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a SAN Swiss Arms Model Classic Green rifle;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a SAN Swiss Arms Model Classic Green carbine;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a SAN Swiss Arms Model Classic Green CQB rifle;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a SAN Swiss Arms Model Black Special rifle;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a SAN Swiss Arms Model Black Special carbine;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} a SAN Swiss Arms Model Black Special CQB rifle;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} a SAN Swiss Arms Model Black Special Target rifle;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} a SAN Swiss Arms Model Blue Star rifle;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} a SAN Swiss Arms Model Heavy Metal rifle;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} a SAN Swiss Arms Model Red Devil rifle; or

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} a SAN Swiss Arms Model Swiss Arms Edition rifle.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amnesty period set out in subsection (3) is declared under section
  117.14 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) for a person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the day before this Order is registered,
    possessed a firearm and held a licence that was issued under the
    [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during the amnesty period, continues to hold the
    licence while in possession of the firearm.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of the amnesty period is to permit the
  person to do any of the following during that period:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} possess the firearm;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} deliver the firearm to a peace officer, firearms
    officer or chief firearms officer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sell or give the firearm to a business -- including
    a museum -- authorized to acquire and possess prohibited firearms;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} transport the firearm for the purposes of
    paragraph (b) or (c);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} use the firearm in target practice or at a target
    shooting competition, under the auspices of a shooting club or
    shooting range that is approved under section 29 of the [Firearms
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6), and, for that purpose, transport the firearm
    in accordance with,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a firearm referred to in any of
      paragraphs 1(a), (b), (e), (h) and (k) to (o), the requirements
      set out in section 10 of the [Storage, Display, Transportation and
      Handling of Firearms by Individuals
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-209) or section 11 of the
      [Storage, Display and Transportation of Firearms and Other Weapons
      by Businesses Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-210), as
      applicable, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a firearm referred to in any of
      paragraphs 1(c), (d), (f), (g), (i) and (j), the requirements set
      out in section 11 of the [Storage, Display, Transportation and
      Handling of Firearms by Individuals
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-209) or section 12 of the
      [Storage, Display and Transportation of Firearms and Other Weapons
      by Businesses Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-210), as
      applicable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amnesty period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amnesty period begins on the day on which this
  Order is registered and ends on March 14, 2016.
