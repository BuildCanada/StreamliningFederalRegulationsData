## Canadian Livestock Feed Board Headquarters Regulations {#canadian-livestock-feed-board-headquarters-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1026
:::

[LIVESTOCK FEED ASSISTANCE ACT](/eng/acts/L-10)

Regulations Designating the Headquarters of the Canadian Livestock Feed
Board
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-476479 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Canadian Livestock Feed Board Headquarters
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1026).

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-476481 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The City of Montreal in the
Province of Quebec is designated as the City in which the headquarters
of the Canadian Livestock Feed Board shall be situated.
