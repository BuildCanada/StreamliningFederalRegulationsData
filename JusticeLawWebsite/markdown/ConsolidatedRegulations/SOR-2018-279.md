## Critical Habitat of the Killer Whale (Orcinus orca) Northeast Pacific Northern Resident Population Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-killer-whale-orcinus-orca-northeast-pacific-northern-resident-population-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2018-279
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2018-12-13

Critical Habitat of the Killer Whale (Orcinus orca) Northeast Pacific
Northern Resident Population Order
::::

:::::::: section
::::::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Killer Whale ([Orcinus orca]{.otherLang lang="la"})
Northeast Pacific northern resident population is a wildlife species
that is listed as a threatened species in Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBAJA};

Whereas the Minister responsible for the Parks Canada Agency, namely the
Minister of the Environment, and the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans
have together prepared a recovery strategy for that species;

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

Whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is
specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection
58(2)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBAGA} of that Act;

And whereas the Minister of the Environment and the Minister of
Fisheries and Oceans are of the opinion that the annexed Order would
affect areas in respect of which wildlife management boards are
authorized by land claims agreements to perform functions in respect of
wildlife species and, pursuant to subsection 58(8) of that Act, have
consulted the wildlife management boards in question with respect to the
Order;

Therefore, the Minister of the Environment and the Minister of Fisheries
and Oceans, pursuant to subsections 58(4) and (5) of the [Species at
Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDAEA}, make the annexed [Critical
Habitat of the Killer Whale (Orcinus orca) Northeast Pacific Northern
Resident Population Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-279).

Gatineau, December 11, 2018

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Killer Whale ([Orcinus orca]{.otherLang lang="la"}) Northeast
Pacific northern resident population, which is identified in the
recovery strategy for that species that is included in the Species at
Risk Public Registry.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_IndA995_hq_23954){#fn_IndA995_hq_23954-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force on the day on which the
[Critical Habitat of the Killer Whale (Orcinus orca) Northeast Pacific
Southern Resident Population Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-278) comes
into force, but if it is registered after that day, it comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
