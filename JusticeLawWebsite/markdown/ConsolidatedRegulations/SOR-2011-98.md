## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781723 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day other than Saturday
    or a holiday. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[deposit-type instrument]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deposit-type instrument]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in subsection 627.01(1) of the Bank Act. ([instrument de type
    dépôt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-181, s. 110\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-181, s. 110\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a retail association, as defined in section 2 of
      the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01); or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a company, as defined in section 2 of the [Trust
      and Loan Companies
      Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8). ([institution]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a deposit-type
    instrument, includes any return payable under the instrument by an
    institution in respect of the deposit. ([intérêt]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[interest rate benchmark]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[interest rate benchmark]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in subsection 627.01(1) of the Bank Act. ([taux d'intérêt de
    référence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Manner of Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781736 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clear and simple language

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any disclosure that is
required to be made by an institution under these Regulations must be
made in language, and presented in a manner, that is clear, simple and
not misleading.

## [Disclosure in Respect of the Issuance of a Deposit-Type Instrument]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781739 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2024-212, s. 7(E)

\]
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be disclosed

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At or
  before the time an institution enters into an agreement with a person
  for the issuance of a deposit-type instrument, the institution must
  disclose the following information to the person, orally and in
  writing:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the annual rate of interest in respect of the
    instrument, if the rate of interest is fixed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the rate of interest is variable,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} how the rate of interest is determined,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the prime lending rate or the interest rate
      benchmark, as the case may be, that is used for the calculation of
      the rate of interest,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the prime lending rate or the interest rate
      benchmark in effect when the information is disclosed, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} how the person may obtain the rate of interest
      from the institution during the investment period;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any charges in respect of the instrument;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} when interest is calculated and paid under the
    instrument;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the dates on which the investment period specified
    in the instrument begins and ends;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} whether the instrument may be redeemed prior to
    maturity and, if so, the effect of early redemption on the interest
    payable;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the agreement provides that the issuance of the
    instrument may be cancelled within a specified period, the duration
    of the period;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the agreement provides that after the maturity
    of the instrument a new instrument may be issued to the person
    without a further agreement being entered into, the fact that a new
    instrument may be issued without a further agreement, the conditions
    under which a new instrument may be issued without a further
    agreement and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} whether its rate of interest is fixed or
      variable, and the rate or method for determining the rate,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} its investment period, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any charges related to its issuance or the
      cancellation of its issuance; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the instrument relates to a deposit that is not
    eligible for deposit insurance coverage by the Canada Deposit
    Insurance Corporation, the fact that it is not eligible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception: agreements entered into by
  telephone

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an agreement for the issuance of a
  deposit-type instrument that is entered into by telephone, the
  institution is not required to provide the disclosure referred to in
  subsection (1) in writing on or before entering into the agreement.
  However, the institution must provide the written disclosure after
  entering into the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception: agreements entered into by
  electronic means or by mail

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an agreement for the issuance of a
  deposit-type instrument that is entered into by electronic means or by
  mail, the institution is not required to provide the disclosure
  referred to in subsection (1) orally. However, before entering into
  the agreement the institution must disclose, in addition to the
  written disclosure referred to in subsection (1), the telephone number
  of a person who is knowledgeable about the terms and conditions of the
  instrument.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New instruments issued without further
  agreement

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a new instrument is issued to a person pursuant to
  an agreement referred to in paragraph (1)(h), the institution must
  disclose in writing the information concerning the instrument referred
  to in subsection (1) to the person without delay after the instrument
  is issued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of time --- disclosure by
mail

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An institution that
provides the written disclosure referred to in section 3 by mail is
considered to have provided the disclosure five business days after the
postmark date.

## [Subsequent Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781769 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information --- amendments

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Before making an amendment
to any terms or conditions of a deposit-type instrument, the institution
must disclose the amendment, and its potential impact on the interest
payable, in writing to the person to whom the instrument was issued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information --- current value

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An institution that issues
a deposit-type instrument must, if requested by the person to whom it is
issued, disclose to the person without delay the amount of the principal
and accrued interest on the day the request was made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information --- redemption before
maturity

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An institution that redeems
a deposit-type instrument before the end of the investment period must,
before redeeming the instrument, disclose to the person to whom the
instrument was issued the amount of the principal and accrued interest,
any penalty or charge for the redemption and the net amount payable by
the institution on redemption.

## [Advertisements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781776 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required content --- all advertisements

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In each
  of its advertisements for deposit-type instruments, an institution
  must disclose how the public may obtain information about the
  instruments.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required content --- advertisements
  referring to an instrument's features or interest payable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In each of its advertisements for deposit-type
  instruments that refer to features of deposit-type instruments or the
  interest payable under them, an institution must also disclose

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which interest is to be accrued and
    any limitations in respect of the interest payable; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the instruments relate to deposits that are not
    eligible for deposit insurance coverage by the Canada Deposit
    Insurance Corporation, the fact that they are not eligible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (2)(b) does not apply to an institution to
  which subsection 378.2(2) of the [Cooperative Credit Associations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01) or subsection 413.1(2) of the [Trust and Loan
  Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) applies.

## [Cancellation Periods for Certain Instruments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781786 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New instruments issued without further
agreement

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An institution must allow a
person to whom a new instrument is issued pursuant to an agreement
referred to in paragraph 3(1)(h) to cancel the issuance of the
instrument within at least 10 business days after the day of its
issuance.
