## Critical Habitat of the Salamander Mussel (Simpsonaias ambigua) Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-salamander-mussel-simpsonaias-ambigua-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2019-331
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2019-09-11

Critical Habitat of the Salamander Mussel (Simpsonaias ambigua) Order
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Salamander Mussel ([Simpsonaias ambigua]{.otherLang
lang="la"}) is a wildlife species that is listed as an endangered
species in Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBBEA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EBBEA){#fn_a}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

And whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is
specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection
58(2)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBBCA} of that Act;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b-ID0EBBCA){#fn_b}S.C.
  2015, c. 10, s. 60
  :::

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to subsections
58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDABA}, makes the annexed [Critical
Habitat of the Salamander Mussel ([Simpsonaias ambigua]{.otherLang
lang="la"}) Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-331).

Ottawa, September 6, 2019

::: FlushLeft
Jonathan Wilkinson
:::

::: FlushLeft
Minister of Fisheries and Oceans
:::
:::::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Salamander Mussel ([Simpsonaias ambigua]{.otherLang lang="la"}),
which is identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is
included in the Species at Risk Public Registry.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
