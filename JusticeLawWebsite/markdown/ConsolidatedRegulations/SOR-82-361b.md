## Non-Public Funds Staff Regulations {#non-public-funds-staff-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/82-361b
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33)

Registration 1982-03-26

Regulations Respecting the Appointment of Persons to the Staff of the
Non-Public Funds, Canadian Forces

P.C. 1982-942 1982-03-25
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Public Service Commission has decided that it is not
practicable nor in the best interests of the Public Service to apply the
provisions of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) to
positions and employees of the Staff of the Non-Public Funds, Canadian
Forces;

And Whereas the Public Service Commission has further decided that it is
desirable to make the annexed Regulations prescribing how such positions
and employees shall be dealt with.

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Public Service Commission, is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to section 39 of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), to make the annexed Order
  approving the exclusion by the Public Service Commission of all
  positions and employees of the Staff of the Non-Public Funds, Canadian
  Forces from the operation of the Public Service Employment Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to subsection 35(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) to make the annexed Regulations
  respecting the appointment of persons to the Staff of the Non-Public
  Funds, Canadian Forces.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873504 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Non-Public Funds Staff
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-361b).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873506 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only in respect of persons to whom the [Non-Public Funds Staff Exclusion
Approval Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-361a) applies.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873508 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of National
Defence may appoint such persons as he deems proper to the Staff of the
Non-Public Funds, Canadian Forces.
