## Sandy Bay Band Council Method of Election Regulations {#sandy-bay-band-council-method-of-election-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-125
:::

[INDIAN ACT](/eng/acts/I-5)

Registration 2003-03-27

Sandy Bay Band Council Method of Election Regulations

P.C. 2003-417  2003-03-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
subsection 74(3) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), hereby makes the
annexed [Sandy Bay Band Council Method of Election
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-125).
:::

## [Method of Election]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699831 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The chief and the
councillors of the Sandy Bay Band shall be elected by a majority of the
votes of the electors of the Band.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699833 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
