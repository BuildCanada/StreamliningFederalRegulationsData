## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-957729 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[beluga calf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[beluga calf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a beluga that is dark in
      colour and less than 2 m in length, measured from the point of the
      upper jaw to the notch between the tail flukes; ([veau de
      béluga]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[beneficiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[beneficiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is a
      beneficiary under

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Agreement approved, given effect and
        declared valid by the [Western Arctic (Inuvialuit) Claims
        Settlement Act](/eng/acts/W-6.7),

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Agreement approved, given effect and
        declared valid by the [James Bay and Northern Quebec Native
        Claims Settlement Act](/eng/acts/J-0.3), or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Agreement approved, given effect and
        declared valid by the [Labrador Inuit Land Claims Agreement
        Act](/eng/acts/L-4.3); ([bénéficiaire]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[blinking reflex test]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[blinking reflex test]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2009-66, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[blueback]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[blueback]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a hooded seal that has not
      moulted its blue coat; ([jeune à dos bleu]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[bowhead calf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[bowhead calf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bowhead whale that is
      mottled in appearance and less than 7.5 m in length, measured from
      the point of the upper jaw to the notch between the tail
      flukes; ([veau de baleine boréale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[collector vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[collector vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel greater
      than 19.8 m in length that is used to collect, transport or
      process seals or seal parts fished from vessels 19.8 m in length
      or less; ([bateau de récupération]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[crushed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[crushed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to the cranium of a
      seal, means that both halves of the cranium have been broken so
      that neither half presents a solid structure when
      palpated; ([écrasée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of
      Fisheries and Oceans; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/93-336, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[marine mammal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[marine mammal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/94-52, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[narwhal calf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[narwhal calf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a narwhal that is light
      in colour and less than 1.8 m in length, measured from the point
      of the upper jaw to the notch between the tail
      flukes; ([baleineau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[nuisance seal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[nuisance seal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a seal that represents
      a danger

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to fishing equipment despite deterrence
        efforts, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} based on a scientific recommendation, to the
        conservation of anadromous or catadromous fish stocks because it
        inflicts great damage to them along estuaries and in rivers and
        lakes during the migration of those species; ([phoque
        nuisible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[palpate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[palpate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to examine the right and
      left halves of the cranium by pressing it by hand from the
      top; ([palpation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Sealing Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Sealing Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Sealing Area
      illustrated and enumerated in Schedule III; ([zone de pêche du
      phoque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strike]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strike]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2003-103, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[Subarea]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Subarea]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
      1 of the [Pacific Fishery Management Area Regulations,
      2007](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-77); ([sous-secteur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[whitecoat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[whitecoat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a harp seal that has not

      lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-957759 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the management and control of fishing for marine
  mammals and related activities in Canada and in Canadian fisheries
  waters;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the management and control of fishing for marine
  mammals from Canadian fishing vessels in the Antarctic; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the conservation and protection of marine mammals in
  Canada and in Canadian fisheries waters.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-126, s. 4
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** Despite paragraph 3(a),
these Regulations do not apply to fishing for marine mammals that is
authorized by an aquaculture licence issued under the [Pacific
Aquaculture Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-270).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-270, s. 13
:::

**[[3.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.2 .sectionLabel}** In the event of any
inconsistency between these Regulations and any regulations respecting
marine mammals made under the Oceans Act, the [Saguenay-St. Lawrence
Marine Park Act](/eng/acts/S-1.3) or the [Canada National Marine
Conservation Areas Act](/eng/acts/C-7.3), the regulations made under
those Acts shall prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-126, s. 5
:::

**[[3.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.3 .sectionLabel}** In the event of any
inconsistency between these Regulations and the [Canadian Aviation
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433), the latter shall prevail to
the extent of the inconsistency.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-126, s. 5
:::

### [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-957801 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall disturb a marine mammal except

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when carrying on a work, undertaking or activity
    that is authorized, otherwise permitted or required under the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when fishing for marine mammals under the authority
    of these Regulations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the manner set out in a licence issued under the
    [Fishery (General) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-53)
    authorizing them to fish for marine mammals for experimental,
    scientific, educational or public display purposes; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the manner authorized under the [Species at Risk
    Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1),
  [[disturb]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} includes to approach the marine
  mammal to, or to attempt to,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} feed it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} swim with it or interact with it;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} move it or entice or cause it to move from the
    immediate vicinity in which it is found;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} separate it from members of its group or go between
    it and a calf;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} trap it or its group between a vessel and the shore
    or between a vessel and one or more other vessels; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} tag or mark it.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), in the case of a
  marine mammal that is of a species set out in column 1 of Schedule VI,
  [[disturb]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} also includes to approach the marine
  mammal with a vehicle of the type set out in column 2 within the
  approach distance set out in column 3 in the area set out in column 4
  during the period set out in column 5.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), in the case of a
  whale, dolphin or porpoise in resting position or with its calf,
  [[disturb]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} also includes to approach the whale,
  dolphin or porpoise with a vehicle of the type set out in column 2 of
  Schedule VI, at an approach distance of less than 200 m in all
  Canadian fisheries waters from January 1 to December 31.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The requirement to respect the approach distances set
  out in Schedule VI and in subsection (4) does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a vessel that is in transit; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder of a seal fishery observation licence
    who is observing the seal fishery.

- SOR/2018-126, s. 6

**[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** The prohibition set out
in section 7 does not apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} employees of the Department who are performing their
  duties or functions or persons who are assisting them or who are
  otherwise present at the request of the Department; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} employees of the Parks Canada Agency or the
  Department of National Defence, members of the Canadian Forces and
  peace officers, while they are performing their duties or functions.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-126, s. 6

- **[[7.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  an aircraft is being operated at an altitude of less than 304.8 m
  (1,000 ft.) within a radius of one-half nautical mile from a marine
  mammal, no person shall perform a flight manoeuvre --- including
  taking off, landing or altering the course or altitude of the aircraft
  --- for the purpose of bringing the aircraft closer to the marine
  mammal or otherwise disturbing it.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the holder of a licence that is issued under the
    [Fishery (General) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-53) and that
    authorizes fishing for marine mammals for experimental, scientific,
    educational or public display purposes, when the holder is
    conducting activities authorized by the licence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to employees of the Department who are performing
    their duties or functions or person who are assisting them or who
    are otherwise present at the request of the Department;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to employees of the Parks Canada Agency or the
    Department of National Defence, members of the Canadian Forces and
    peace officers, who are performing their duties or functions;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to a commercial flight that is operating on a
    scheduled flight plan; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} to a helicopter that is being used for seal pup
    observation on the ice of the waters of the Gulf of St. Lawrence, if
    it maintains a distance of at least two metres from the seal pups.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the holder of a seal fishery
  observation licence is permitted to take off or land an aircraft
  within a radius of one-half nautical mile from a marine mammal for the
  purpose of conducting activities authorized by the licence.

- SOR/2018-229, s. 1

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No person shall attempt to
kill a marine mammal except in a manner that is designed to kill it
quickly.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No person shall fish for a
marine mammal without having on hand the equipment that is necessary to
retrieve it.

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person who kills or wounds a marine mammal shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fail to make a reasonable effort to retrieve it

  it has been crushed.

  as possible after it is shot to confirm that the cranium has been
  crushed.

  determines that the cranium is not crushed shall immediately strike
  the seal with a club or hakapik on the top of its cranium until the

## [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Marine Mammal Disturbance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958013 .Part}

### [Authorized Disturbance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-958014 .Subheading}

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite sections 7 and 7.2, the
  Minister may authorize the disturbance of marine mammals if it is
  established that the activity causing the disturbance

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} could benefit marine mammals without jeopardizing
    the survival of the species in the wild;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} could improve a marine mammal's immediate chance of
    survival;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} would contribute to the conservation and protection
    of marine mammals;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} could ease the pain and suffering of a marine
    mammal that is in distress;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} would contribute to marine scientific research; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} would permit the production of audiovisual records
    of activities of marine mammals, which could facilitate a better
    understanding of marine mammals and thereby contribute to their
    conservation and protection.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The authorization may be subject to any condition
  respecting

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the waters in which marine mammals may be
    disturbed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the marine mammals that may be disturbed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the period during which marine mammals may be
    disturbed;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the type, size, number and identification of
    vehicles that are permitted to be used and the persons who are
    permitted to operate them;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which those vehicles are to be
    operated, including the distance they are required to maintain from
    marine mammals, their speed and direction and the requirement to
    avoid impeding the path of marine mammals;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which marine mammals may be disturbed
    and the measures that are required to mitigate or minimize the
    negative effects of disturbing them;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the diagnostic assessment or any other assessments
    that are to be carried out in respect of marine mammals before,
    during and after the disturbance;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the information that must be reported to the
    Minister, as well as the method by which, the times at which and the
    person for whom the report is to be made; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the records that must be maintained with respect to
    the activity causing the disturbance as well as the manner and form
    in which the records are to be maintained, the times at which and
    the person for whom the records are to be produced and the period
    for which the records are to be retained.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-126, s. 9
:::

### [Accidental Contact with Marine Mammals]{.HTitleText2} {#h-958035 .Subheading}

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** Immediately after any
accidental contact between a vehicle or fishing gear and a marine
mammal, the operator of the vehicle or the fishing gear, as the case may
be, shall --- unless the contact is reported as a bycatch in a log book
--- notify the Minister of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date, time and location of the incident;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the species of marine mammal involved in the
  incident;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the circumstances of the incident;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the size and type of vehicle and, if applicable, the
  type of fishing gear involved in the incident;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the weather and sea conditions at the time of the
  incident;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the observed state of the marine mammal after the
  incident; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the direction of travel of the marine mammal after
  the incident, to the extent that it can be determined.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-126, s. 9
