## Critical Habitat of the White Sturgeon (Acipenser transmontanus) Kootenay River Population Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-white-sturgeon-acipenser-transmontanus-kootenay-river-population-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2016-86
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2016-05-02

Critical Habitat of the White Sturgeon (Acipenser transmontanus)
Kootenay River Population Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the White Sturgeon ([Acipenser transmontanus]{.otherLang
lang="la"}) Kootenay River population is a wildlife species that is
listed as an endangered species in Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the [Species
at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBAGA};

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

And whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is
specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection
58(2) of that Act;

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to subsections
58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDADA}, makes the annexed [Critical
Habitat of the White Sturgeon (Acipenser transmontanus) Kootenay River
Population Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-86).

Ottawa, April 21, 2016

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838445 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the White Sturgeon ([Acipenser transmontanus]{.otherLang lang="la"})
Kootenay River population, which is identified in the recovery strategy
for that species that is included in the Species at Risk Public
Registry.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838447 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
