## Prince Edward Island Milk Order {#prince-edward-island-milk-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-628
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1994-10-06

Prince Edward Island Milk Order

P.C. 1994-1654 1994-10-06
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to subsections 2(1) and (2) of the
[Agricultural Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), is pleased hereby
to repeal the Prince Edward Island Milk Order, 1988, made by Order in
Council P.C. 1988-1607 of August 11, 1988[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EBBAA}, and
to make the annexed Order granting authority to the Prince Edward Island
Milk Marketing Board to regulate the marketing in interprovincial and
export trade of milk produced in Prince Edward Island, in substitution
therefor.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e-ID0EBBAA){#footnote1star_e}SOR/88-422,
  1988 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3625
  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-965125 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Natural Products Marketing Act
    of Prince Edward Island; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Prince Edward Island Milk
    Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[milk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[milk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means milk or cream from cows, produced
    in the Province of Prince Edward Island for consumer use in liquid
    form in Canada; ([lait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    milk, established and amended from time to time pursuant to the Act.
    ([plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-16, s. 30
:::

## [Interprovincial Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-965133 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Board is authorized to regulate the marketing
  of milk in interprovincial trade and for that purpose may, with
  respect to persons and property situated within the Province of Prince
  Edward Island, exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable
  by the Board in relation to the marketing of milk locally within the
  province under the Act and a Plan.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The authority to regulate the marketing of milk in
  interprovincial trade does not include any power that is exercised by
  the Canadian Dairy Commission under the [Canadian Dairy Commission
  Act](/eng/acts/C-15) in relation to the marketing of milk in
  interprovincial trade.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-16, s. 31
:::

## [Levies or Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-965139 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Board may, in relation
to the powers granted to it under section 3,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix and impose levies or charges on milk or any
  component of milk and collect those levies or charges from persons
  described in subsection 3(1) who are engaged in the production or
  marketing of milk and for that purpose may classify those persons into
  groups and fix the levies or charges payable by the members of the
  different groups in different amounts; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the levies or charges for the purposes of the
  Board, including the creation of reserves, the payment of expenses and
  losses resulting from the sale or disposal of milk and the
  equalization or adjustment among producers of milk of moneys realized
  from the sale thereof during such period or periods of time as the
  Board may determine.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-16, s. 32
