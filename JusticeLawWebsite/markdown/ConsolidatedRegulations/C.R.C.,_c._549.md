## Used Mattress Materials Regulations {#used-mattress-materials-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 549
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Regulations Respecting the Importation of Materials from Used or
Second-Hand Mattresses
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557762 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Used Mattress Materials
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._549).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557764 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Materials from used or
second-hand mattresses may be imported only if they

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have been cleaned and fumigated; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} are accompanied by a certificate that is signed by
  the exporter or other person having knowledge of such cleaning and
  fumigation and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} gives complete details of the procedure used to
    clean and fumigate the materials, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} contains a statement certifying that that
    procedure was carried out.
