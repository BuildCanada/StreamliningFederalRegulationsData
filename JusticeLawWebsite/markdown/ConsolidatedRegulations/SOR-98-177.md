## National Parks of Canada Domestic Animals Regulations {#national-parks-of-canada-domestic-animals-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-177
:::

[CANADA NATIONAL PARKS ACT](/eng/acts/N-14.01)

Registration 1998-03-19

National Parks of Canada Domestic Animals Regulations

P.C. 1998-401 1998-03-19
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Canadian Heritage, pursuant to subsection 7(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
National Parks Act, hereby makes the annexed National Parks Domestic
Animals Regulations, 1998.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1996, c.
  10, s. 246
  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018537 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2005-350, s.
    2\]]{.Repealed}

[[domestic animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[domestic animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an animal of a species
    of vertebrates that has been domesticated by humans so as to live
    and breed in a tame condition and depend on humankind for survival.
    ([animal domestique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[keep]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[keep]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2005-350, s.
    2\]]{.Repealed}

[[keeper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[keeper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2005-350, s.
    2\]]{.Repealed}

[[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit issued under
    section 4. ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[physical control]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[physical control]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a horse, donkey, mule or llama,
      restraint by a bridle, tether or hobble, or by such means as
      circumstances require;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a domestic animal to which
      paragraph (a) does not apply, restraint by a leash that does not
      exceed 3 metres in length; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in all cases, confinement in a container,
      enclosure or motor vehicle suitable for keeping the animal.
      ([moyen de retenue]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-350, s. 2
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018550 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), these Regulations apply to parks and,
  subject to sections 40 and 41 of the [Canada National Parks
  Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01), to park reserves as if they were parks.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The provisions of these Regulations that are
  applicable to dogs are subject to the [Wood Buffalo National Park Game
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-830).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in the town of Banff.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The prohibition in paragraph 3(2)(b) and the
  requirement in section 5 that a domestic animal be kept under physical
  control do not apply

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in any park, in respect of a dog that is involved
    in a search and rescue operation or training for a search and rescue
    operation that is being conducted under the authorization of the
    superintendent; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in Wapusk National Park of Canada, in respect of a
    dog trained in the detection of, or protection from, polar bears
    that is being used in the park in that capacity.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The provisions of paragraphs 5(a) and (b) that
  require a person to ensure that a domestic animal does not chase,
  molest, bite or injure wildlife or become a nuisance or cause
  unreasonable disturbance to wildlife does not apply in respect of a
  dog trained in the detection of, or protection from, polar bears that
  is being used in that capacity in Wapusk National Park of Canada if
  the wildlife in question is a polar bear.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Section 3, subsections 4(1) to (4) and (6) to (8),
  paragraphs 5(a) to (c), sections 6 to 8 and subsections 9(1) to (4)
  and section 10 do not apply in the Town of Jasper.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-350, s. 3
- SOR/2010-23, s. 12
- SOR/2010-67, s. 26
- SOR/2010-140, s. 15
:::

## [Prohibitions and Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018562 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} bring into or keep in a park any cattle, sheep,
    pigs, goats or live poultry;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} bring into or keep in a park any horses, donkeys or
    mules except for their own recreational use or in accordance with a
    licence issued to them under the [National Parks of Canada
    Businesses Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-455); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} bring into or keep in a park any llamas except in
    accordance with a licence issued to them under the [National Parks
    of Canada Businesses Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-455).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall, except as authorized by a permit,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep a dog or cat in a park for more than 30 days
    in any year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} bring a domestic animal into an area of a park that
    the superintendent has designated, by a sign or other clearly posted
    notice, as an area in which domestic animals are not permitted; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} graze a horse, donkey, mule or llama in a park.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (1) and (2), a person may
  transport a domestic animal by vehicle directly through a park if the
  vehicle does not stop in the park other than for service to the
  vehicle or for emergency purposes.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-350, ss. 4, 13(F)
:::

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** No person shall bring
into or keep in a park a domestic animal that has an infectious disease
or a disease that is dangerous to humans, other domestic animals or
wildlife in the park.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-350, s. 5
:::

## [Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018578 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent shall, on application, issue a permit authorizing the
  permit holder to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep a dog or cat in a park for more than 30 days
    in any year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} bring a dog, cat, horse, donkey, mule or llama into
    an area referred to in paragraph 3(2)(b); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} graze a horse, donkey, mule or llama in a park.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 3(1), the superintendent of
  Grasslands National Park of Canada may, to facilitate grazing for
  ecological purposes in the park, on application, issue a permit
  authorizing the permit holder to graze any domestic animal that is a
  herbivore in the park.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The permits referred to in subsections (1) and (1.1)
  expire on the earlier of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of cancellation of the permit, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} March 31 of the fiscal year for which the permit
    was issued.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a tag is issued with a permit, the permit
  holder shall ensure that the tag is securely fixed on the means of
  physical control of the domestic animal.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The permit shall contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the permit holder;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} standard conditions that shall be specified by the
    superintendent in respect of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the area and period of authorized grazing, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description sufficient to identify the
      domestic animal; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any supplementary conditions related to grazing
    that may be specified by the superintendent for the purposes of the
    preservation, control and management of the park, including the
    maintenance or restoration of its ecological integrity, the
    protection of its cultural, historical or archaeological resources,
    public safety and the preservation of public health in the park and
    the enjoyment of persons visiting the park.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything in these Regulations, the
  superintendent shall, where it is necessary to do so for the
  protection of the natural resources of the park or for the safety of
  the public, prohibit the entrance of a species of domestic animal into
  the park or into a portion of it.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall refuse to issue and shall by
  written notification cancel a permit if the presence of the domestic
  animal or the activity in question would be detrimental or is found to
  be detrimental to the natural resources of the park, to park property,
  or to the health or safety of the public.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} A permit applies only in respect of the domestic
  animal for which it is issued.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} A permit holder shall produce the permit at the
  request of the superintendent, a park warden or an enforcement
  officer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-350, ss. 6, 13(F)
:::

## [Control of Domestic Animals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018601 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every person who brings a
domestic animal into a park or who keeps a domestic animal in a park
shall have the animal under physical control at all times and shall
ensure that the animal does not

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} chase, molest, bite or injure any person, other
  domestic animal or wildlife;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} become a nuisance or cause unreasonable disturbance
  to persons, wildlife, property or facilities; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} gain access to an area where the animal is
  prohibited.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-350, s. 7
:::

**[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** In the Town of Jasper,
every person who keeps a domestic animal shall ensure that the animal
does not

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} chase, molest, bite or injure wildlife;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} become a nuisance or cause disturbance to wildlife;
  or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} gain access to an area where the animal is
  prohibited.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-23, s. 13
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every person who brings a
cat or dog into a park or who keeps a cat or dog in a park shall
immediately pick up any fecal matter deposited by the animal and shall
either remove the matter to a refuse container or from the park.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-350, s. 7
:::

**[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** Section 6 does not
apply, in Wapusk National Park of Canada, in respect of a sled dog or a
dog trained in the detection of, or protection from, polar bears that is
being used in the park in that capacity.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-67, s. 27
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where a domestic animal
that a person brings into, or keeps or grazes in, a park shows symptoms
of disease, the superintendent may require the person to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any domestic animal, obtain a
  veterinarian's statement confirming that the animal is not contagious;
  or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a cat or dog, produce a veterinarian's
  certificate or other evidence that the animal has been vaccinated
  against rabies, distemper or parvo virus.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-350, s. 8
:::

**[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** Before entering Wapusk
National Park of Canada with a sled dog team, or with a dog trained in
the detection of, or protection from, polar bears, the person who is
bringing the dogs into the park shall submit to the superintendent a
veterinarian's certificate or other evidence that establishes that the
dogs

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have been vaccinated against rabies, distemper, parvo
  virus and Bordetella, and that those vaccinations are not out of date;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have been dewormed not more than six months before
  entering the park.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-67, s. 28
:::

## [Removal of Domestic Animals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018630 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent, a park warden or an enforcement officer may order a
  person who brings a domestic animal into a park or who keeps or grazes
  a domestic animal in a park to remove the animal from the park and may
  cancel any permit issued in respect of the animal if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the animal has bitten or injured, or attempted to
    bite or injure, any person or other domestic animal or wildlife in
    the park;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person does not provide the evidence referred
    to in section 7 within 48 hours after the superintendent's
    requirement; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person has violated another provision of these
    Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within 24 hours after receiving an order pursuant to
  subsection (1), the person who brought the domestic animal, in respect
  of which the order was made, into the park or who keeps or grazes it
  in the park shall remove it from the park.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), no person shall bring back
  into a park any domestic animal removed from the park in compliance
  with an order issued pursuant to subsection (1) unless a new permit is
  issued for that domestic animal.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall not issue a new permit for
  an animal removed under paragraph (1)(b) until the superintendent is
  provided with a veterinarian's certificate of the renewed good health
  of the domestic animal or confirming that it has been vaccinated.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-350, ss. 9, 13(F)
:::

## [Impounding and Destruction of Domestic Animals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018641 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A park
  warden or an enforcement officer may impound a domestic animal that
  is, in a park,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at large;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} found chasing or molesting persons, other domestic
    animals or wildlife;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} creating a nuisance or hazard or causing an
    unreasonable disturbance to park residents, visitors, wildlife,
    property or facilities.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent, a park warden or an enforcement
  officer may destroy any domestic animal that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is at large in the park and that the superintendent
    or park warden has attempted in vain to capture; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that poses a danger to persons, other domestic
    animals or natural resources of the park.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent, a park warden or an enforcement
  officer shall, as soon as possible after a domestic animal has been
  impounded or destroyed, notify its owner if the owner can be readily
  identified.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent, a park warden or an enforcement
  officer may destroy or dispose of any domestic animal that was found
  at large in the park if it has been abandoned.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} In the Town of Jasper, the superintendent or park
  warden may impound a domestic animal that has

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} chased, molested, bitten or injured wildlife;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} become a nuisance or caused a disturbance to
    wildlife; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} gained access to an area where the animal is
    prohibited.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} In the Town of Jasper, the superintendent or park
  warden may destroy any domestic animal that poses a danger to the
  natural resources of the park.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} In the Town of Jasper, the superintendent or park
  warden shall, as soon as possible after a domestic animal has been
  impounded or destroyed under subsection (5) or (6), notify the person
  who keeps the animal, if the person can be readily identified.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-350, s. 10
- SOR/2010-23, s. 14
:::

domestic animal from an impoundment facility in a park unless authorized
to do so by the superintendent, a park warden or an enforcement officer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-350, s. 11
- SOR/2010-140, s. 16

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A person who brings a
domestic animal into a park or who keeps or grazes a domestic animal in
a park shall assist in capturing the animal when requested to do so by
the superintendent, a park warden or an enforcement officer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-350, s. 11

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018666 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding anything
in these Regulations, the superintendent may keep domestic animals in a
park for use for purposes of park management.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-350, s. 13(F)
