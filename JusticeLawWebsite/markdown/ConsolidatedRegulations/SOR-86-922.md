## Service Equipment Cars Regulations {#service-equipment-cars-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-922
:::

[RAILWAY SAFETY ACT](/eng/acts/R-4.2)

Registration 1986-08-27

Regulations Respecting the Movement in Trains of Service Equipment Cars
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
CTC 1986-9 RAIL

The Canadian Transport Commission, pursuant to section 46 of the
National Transportation Act and section 227 of the Railway Act, hereby
makes the annexed [Regulations respecting the movement in trains of
service equipment cars](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-922).

Hull, Quebec, in the National Capital Region, August 26, 1986
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899109 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Service Equipment Cars
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-922).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899111 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[marshal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marshal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to assign a car to a specific
    location in a train, relative to the locomotive or caboose;
    ([triage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[service equipment car]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[service equipment car]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means rolling stock
    used to house employees at work sites, a material car used for
    transporting railway maintenance-of-way equipment or for railway
    company purposes if not used in revenue service and wrecking
    auxiliaries. ([wagon de matériel de service]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Marshalling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899115 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 4 and
5, a railway company shall marshal occupied service equipment cars other
than flangers, plows, spreaders, test cars and business cars

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a freight train, at the rear end of
  the train immediately ahead of the caboose; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a mixed freight and passenger train,
  immediately ahead of any passenger cars.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  railway company shall marshal

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an open top trailer being used in piggyback
    service, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an open top car other than a car that has permanent
    bulkheads and that is loaded lengthwise with timber, poles, rail,
    rods or other similar commodities,

  nearer than second from an occupied service equipment car and at least
  one of the intervening cars shall be a full size steel boxcar or
  bulkhead type car.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No railway company shall marshal a car containing
  dangerous goods

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where a train consists of seven cars or more,
    nearer than sixth from an occupied service equipment car; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where a train consists of less than seven cars,
    other than near the middle of the train but not nearer than second
    from an occupied service equipment car.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where it
  is necessary to set off service equipment cars at a point where track
  configuration may require the use of additional measures to ensure the
  safety of the set-off movement, a railway company may marshall
  occupied service equipment cars to the front end of a train,
  immediately behind the locomotive.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the cars of a train are marshalled in
  accordance with subsection (1), the train

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall not be moved a distance exceeding 32.18 km
    (20 miles); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall not exceed a speed of 32.18 km (20 miles) per
    hour at any time.

## [Lengths]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899132 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A railway company shall
restrict a train having 30 or less occupied service equipment cars to
not more than 60 cars in total.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where a train has more than
30 occupied service equipment cars, a railway company shall restrict the
train to service equipment cars.

## [Train Speeds]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899135 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No railway company shall
permit a train having occupied service equipment cars to exceed a speed
of 56.31 km (35 miles) per hour at any time.

## [Securement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899137 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No railway company shall,
move an occupied service equipment car, unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all heavy furniture, appliances and other heavy
  objects are securely fastened to the structure of the car; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all tools and equipment are secured in a place
  isolated from living quarters in the car.

## [Communication]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899141 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A railway company shall,
within two years after the date of the coming into force and these
Regulations, provide each occupied service equipment car with a means of
voice communication with the train crew in other parts of the train or
with an emergency brake valve.

## [Protection in Sidings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899143 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company shall protect occupied service equipment cars placed
  in sidings, back tracks or other tracks against other train movements
  by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} issuing a train order in accordance with the second
    sentence of Rule 203 of the Regulations No. 0-8, Uniform Code of
    Operating Rules or a Manual Block System Bulletin pursuant to
    Rule 323 of the said Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} spiking the switches of back tracks and other
    tracks; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where practicable, spiking the switches of sidings.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No railway company shall permit the coupling to or
  the moving of occupied service equipment cars until

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the locomotive or train involved in the coupling or
    moving has come to a stop not more than 12 feet or not less than six
    feet from the cars to be moved or coupled; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} employees in or about the cars are notified by a
    member of the train crew that a coupling or moving is to take place.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No railway company shall cut off occupied service
  equipment cars while they are in motion or cut off other cars while
  the other cars are in motion toward occupied service equipment cars.

<!-- -->

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  railway company shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} refill propane cylinders used in occupied service
    equipment cars on the right-of-way; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} carry spare propane cylinders in occupied service
    equipment cars.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where propane cylinders are installed for use in
  service equipment cars, a railway company shall install

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an automatic shut-off valve for the supply of fuel
    from each propane cylinder; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a pilot flame monitoring device to automatically
    shut off the supply of fuel in the event of pilot flame failure.
