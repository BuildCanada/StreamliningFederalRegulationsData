## Manitoba Pullet Order {#manitoba-pullet-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/80-352
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1980-05-15

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing of Pullets in
Manitoba

P.C. 1980-1276 1980-05-15
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to section 2 of the [Agricultural
Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Order granting authority to regulate the marketing of pullets
in Manitoba](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-352).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869271 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Manitoba Pullet Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-352).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869273 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Natural Products Marketing Act
    of Manitoba;

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Manitoba Egg
    Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act;

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Manitoba Egg Producers'
    Marketing Plan, established and amended from time to time pursuant
    to the Act, and any regulations made pursuant to the Act to give
    effect to the Plan;

[[pullet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pullet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the female of any class of
    domestic chicken belonging to the species [Gallus
    domesticus]{.otherLang lang="la"} that is less than 20 weeks of age
    and produced for the purpose of laying eggs for human consumption.

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869279 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized to regulate the marketing of pullets in interprovincial and
export trade and for such purposes may, by order or regulation, with
respect to persons and property situated within the Province of
Manitoba, exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable by it
in relation to the marketing of pullets locally within that province
under the Act and the Plan.

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869281 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized, in relation to the powers granted to it by section 3 with
respect to the marketing of pullets in interprovincial and export trade,
to make orders fixing, imposing and collecting levies or charges from
persons situated in the Province of Manitoba who are engaged in the
production or marketing of pullets and for such purposes to classify
such persons into groups and fix the levies or charges payable by the
members of the different groups in different amounts and the Commodity
Board may use such levies or charges for its purposes including the
creation of reserves and the payment of expenses and losses resulting
from the sale or disposal of pullets and the equalization or adjustment
among producers of pullets of moneys realized from the sale thereof
during such period or periods of time as it may determine.
