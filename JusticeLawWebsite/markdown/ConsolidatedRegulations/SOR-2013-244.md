## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-802607 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Air Canada pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Air Canada pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
      [[plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a defined benefit plan
      administered by Air Canada that was established before May 2,
      2013, excluding a multi-employer pension plan. ([régime de pension
      d'Air Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou
      [régime]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[solvency deficiency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[solvency deficiency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount by
      which the solvency liabilities of a plan that are determined by
      means of a solvency valuation of the plan, exceed the aggregate of
      the market value of the assets of the plan related to the defined
      benefit provisions that is determined by means of the solvency
      valuation of the plan. ([déficit de solvabilité]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided in these Regulations,
  words and expressions used in these Regulations have the same meaning
  as in the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-802615 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Air Canada pension plans

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of all Air Canada pension plans.

## [Application of the Pension Benefits Standards Regulations, 1985]{.HTitleText1} {#h-802618 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of application

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  otherwise specified, these Regulations do not have the effect of
  rendering the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) inapplicable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, section 9 of the [Pension Benefits Standards
  Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), with the exception of
  subsection 9(14), does not apply in respect of Air Canada pension
  plans.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special payment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 9(14) of the [Pension
  Benefits Standards Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), a
  reference to [[special payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a
  reference to the amount required to be paid in accordance with
  paragraph 5(1)(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other payment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the amounts required to be
  paid in accordance with paragraph 5(1)(b) are "other payments" for the
  purposes of paragraph 10(2)(d) of the [Pension Benefits Standards
  Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19).

## [Funding]{.HTitleText1} {#h-802628 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Solvency standards

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The funding of any plan is
considered to meet the standards for solvency if the funding is in
accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Funding during each plan year

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  plan must be funded in each plan year by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contributions that are equal to the normal cost of
    the plan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount that is determined under subsection (2);
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount that is required to be paid by an
    employer under a defined contribution provision in that plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), the amount
  required to be paid to each plan for a plan year is equal to the
  amount determined

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the aggregate amount of the
    solvency deficiencies of all plans is equal to or greater than
    \$150,000,000, by the formula

    ::: {#802640 .Paragraph}
    A x B/C

    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is \$150,000,000,

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount of the solvency deficiency of the plan, and

    [C]{.dfn}
    :   is the aggregate amount of the solvency deficiencies of all
        plans; or
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the aggregate amount of the
    solvency deficiencies of all plans is less than \$150,000,000, by
    the formula

    ::: {#802646 .Paragraph}
    D + (\$150,000,000 -- E) x F/G

    where

    [D]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount of the solvency deficiency of the plan,

    [E]{.dfn}
    :   is the aggregate amount of the solvency deficiencies of all
        plans,

    [F]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount of the solvency liabilities of the plan, and

    [G]{.dfn}
    :   is the aggregate amount of the solvency liabilities of all
        plans.
    :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Funding for 2020 plan year

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless the whole of the pension plan is terminated or
  unless Air Canada provides notice in accordance with subsection 6(1),
  the plan must also be funded, for the 2020 plan year, by the amount
  that is determined under subsection (4), and that amount must be paid
  by December 31, 2020.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special payment for 2020 plan year

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (3), the amount
  required to be paid to each plan for the plan year is equal to the
  amount determined

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the aggregate amount of the
    solvency deficiencies of all plans is equal to or greater than the
    amount determined by the formula set out in A, by the formula

    ::: {#802657 .Paragraph}
    A x (B/C)

    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount obtained by the formula
        ::: {#802660 .NestedFormula}
        (\$200,000,000 × D) -- \[(\$150,000,000 x D) + E\]

        where

        [D]{.dfn}
        :   is the number of plan years that are after the 2013 plan
            year, and

        [E]{.dfn}
        :   are the amounts paid for those plan years that are in
            addition to the amounts required to be paid in accordance
            with subsection (1),
        :::

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount of the solvency deficiency of the plan, and

    [C]{.dfn}
    :   is the aggregate amount of the solvency deficiencies of all
        plans; or
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the aggregate amount of the
    solvency deficiencies of all plans is less than the amount
    determined by the formula set out in G, by the formula

    ::: {#802667 .Paragraph}
    F + (G -- H) x I/J

    where

    [F]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount of the solvency deficiency of the plan,

    [G]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount obtained by the formula
        ::: {#802671 .NestedFormula}
        (\$200,000,000 × K) -- \[(\$150,000,000 x K) + L\]

        where

        [K]{.dfn}
        :   is the number of plan years that are after the 2013 plan
            year, and

        [L]{.dfn}
        :   are the amounts paid for those plan years that are in
            addition to the amounts required to be paid in accordance
            with subsection (1),
        :::

    [H]{.dfn}
    :   is the aggregate amount of the solvency deficiencies of all
        plans,

    [I]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount of the solvency liabilities of the plan, and

    [J]{.dfn}
    :   is the aggregate amount of the solvency liabilities of all
        plans.
    :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest rate

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If an employer fails to make the payment to the plan
  that is referred to in subsection (3) within the period that is set
  out in that subsection, the amount that must be paid in accordance
  with that subsection is to bear interest at the rate that was used to
  determine the solvency liabilities of the plan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discontinuance of funding --- notice to
the Superintendent

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, on
  or before the earlier of the day on which the actuarial report is
  filed with the Superintendent for a plan year and June 30 of that plan
  year, Air Canada provides written notice to the Superintendent
  indicating its choice to discontinue funding of all the Air Canada
  pension plans under these Regulations for that plan year, these
  Regulations, except subsections 1(1), 3(3) and (4), this section and
  section 12, cease to apply to the plans on December 31 of the previous
  plan year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discontinuance of funding --- notice to
  members and beneficiaries

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within 30 days after notice is provided in accordance
  with subsection (1), Air Canada must notify all plan members and
  beneficiaries in writing indicating that it has chosen to discontinue
  funding of all the Air Canada pension plans under these Regulations
  and indicating the plan year for which its choice will begin to apply.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special payment --- notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each plan must be funded for the plan year in which
  the notice was provided in accordance with subsection (1) by an amount
  that must be paid within 30 days after that notice has been provided
  and is equal to the amount determined

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the aggregate amount of the
    solvency deficiencies of all plans is equal to or greater than the
    amount determined by the formula set out in A, by the formula

    ::: {#802688 .Paragraph}
    A × (B/C)

    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount obtained by the formula
        ::: {#802691 .NestedFormula}
        (\$200,000,000 × D) -- \[(\$150,000,000 x D) + E\]

        where

        [D]{.dfn}
        :   is the number of plan years that are after the 2013 plan
            year, excluding the year in which the notice is provided,
            and

        [E]{.dfn}
        :   are the amounts paid for those plan years that are in
            addition to the amounts required to be paid in accordance
            with subsection 5(1),
        :::

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount of the solvency deficiency of the plan, and

    [C]{.dfn}
    :   is the aggregate amount of the solvency deficiencies of all
        plans; or
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the aggregate amount of the
    solvency deficiencies of all plans is less than the amount
    determined by the formula set out in G, by the formula

    ::: {#802698 .Paragraph}
    F + (G -- H) x I/J

    where

    [F]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount of the solvency deficiency of the plan,

    [G]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount obtained by the formula
        ::: {#802702 .NestedFormula}
        (\$200,000,000 × K) -- \[(\$150,000,000 x K) + L\]

        where

        [K]{.dfn}
        :   is the number of plan years that are after the 2013 plan
            year, excluding the year in which the notice is provided,
            and

        [L]{.dfn}
        :   are the amounts paid for those plan years that are in
            addition to the amounts required to be paid in accordance
            with subsection 5(1),
        :::

    [H]{.dfn}
    :   is the aggregate amount of the solvency deficiencies of all
        plans,

    [I]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount of the solvency liabilities of the plan, and

    [J]{.dfn}
    :   is the aggregate amount of the solvency liabilities of all
        plans.
    :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest rate

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If an employer fails to make the payment to the plan
  that is referred to in subsection (3) within the period that is set
  out in that subsection, the amount that must be paid in accordance
  with that subsection is to bear interest at the rate that was used to
  determine the solvency liabilities of the plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Average solvency ratio

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In respect of the plan year in which the notice under
  subsection (1) has been provided, the average solvency ratio for each
  plan must be adjusted by increasing the solvency assets by the amount
  determined under subsection (3).

## [Solvency Ratio Level]{.HTitleText1} {#h-802713 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Section 9.3 of the [Pension
Benefits Standards Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), with
the exception of subsection 9.3(2), does not apply in respect of an Air
Canada pension plan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Void amendment

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraphs 10.1(2)(c) and (d) of the Act, the prescribed solvency ratio
level is 1.0.

## [Plan Termination]{.HTitleText1} {#h-802718 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special payment --- plan termination

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  whole of an Air Canada pension plan is terminated, the special payment
  referred to in paragraph 29(6)(b) of the Act that is due on
  termination is equal to the sum of, in the absence of termination, the
  amount that would have been required to be paid to the plan in
  accordance with paragraph 5(1)(b) for the period beginning on the day
  on which the plan is terminated and ending on the last day of the plan
  year in which it is terminated and the amount that would have been
  determined in accordance with subsection 5(4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Solvency deficiency and solvency
  liabilities to be used

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the solvency deficiency and solvency
  liabilities of the plan and the aggregate amount of the solvency
  deficiencies and solvency liabilities of all plans that are to be used
  to determine the amount referred to in subsection (1) are the last
  amounts determined before the termination.

## [Rights to Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-802724 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information --- subparagraph 28(1)(b)(iv)
of Act

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The following information
is prescribed for the purposes of subparagraph 28(1)(b)(iv) of the Act,
in addition to the information referred to in section 23 of the [Pension
Benefits Standards Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the plan's solvency deficiency as shown
  in the last actuarial report filed with the Superintendent;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fact that the plan is being funded in accordance
  with these Regulations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount of payments, other than the normal cost,
  that were required to be paid to the plan during the plan year covered
  by the statement; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount of special payments that would have been
  paid to the plan for the plan year covered by the statement if the
  plan had been funded in accordance with section 9 of the [Pension
  Benefits Standards Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19)
  during that plan year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recipients

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  statement referred to in paragraph 28(1)(b.1) of the Act must be
  mailed to the former member of the plan and either to that former
  member's spouse, or if the former member is cohabiting with a
  common-law partner, to that common-law partner rather than the former
  member's spouse, whose names and addresses are shown on the records of
  the administrator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information --- subparagraph
  28(1)(b.1)(ii) of Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in paragraphs 10(a)
  to (d) is prescribed for the purposes of subparagraph 28(1)(b.1)(ii)
  of the Act.

## [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-802736 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Outstanding amounts

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
any amounts that were required to be paid under the [Air Canada Pension
Plan Funding Regulations, 2009](/eng/regulations/SOR-2009-211) and have
not yet been paid continue to be required until they are paid and
subsections 9(14) and 10(2) of the [Pension Benefits Standards
Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) continue to apply in
respect of those amounts.
