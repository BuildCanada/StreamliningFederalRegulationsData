## Toronto/Buttonville Airport Zoning Regulations {#torontobuttonville-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-148
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1988-02-25

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Toronto/Buttonville Airport

P.C. 1988-304 1988-02-25
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 4.5[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EJBBA} of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed Zoning Regulations
respecting Toronto/Buttonville Airport, substantially in the form set
out in the schedule hereto, was published in two successive issues of
the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on June 13th
and 20th, 1987, and in two successive issues of the Richmond Hill
Liberal and the Markham Economist on June 17th and 24th, 1987, and in
two successive issues of [L'Express de Toronto]{.otherLang lang="fr"} on
June 16th and 23rd, 1987, and a reasonable opportunity was thereby
afforded to interested persons to make representations to the Minister
of Transport with respect thereto.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EJBBA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1985, c. 28, s. 1
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to
section 4.4[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFAAA} of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting zoning at Toronto/Buttonville
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-148).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911138 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Toronto/Buttonville Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-148).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911140 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Toronto/Buttonville
      Airport at Markham, in the Regional Municipality of York, in the
      Province of Ontario; ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part II of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, prepared for
      the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
      which strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the assigned
  elevation of the airport reference point is 193 m above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911151 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, which lands are more particularly described in
Part VI of the Schedule.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-168, s. 1(F)
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911155 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911160 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in paragraphs 4(a) to (c), the Minister
may require that the owner or occupier of the land on which that object
is growing remove the excessive growth.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-168, s. 2(E)
:::

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911164 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit that land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste that is edible by or
attractive to birds.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-911167 .scheduleLabel}

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[(s. 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-is.-2description-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point, shown on Toronto/Buttonville Airport Zoning
Plan No. 19-036-84-05, sheet 5, dated May 30, 1986, is a point distant
45.0 m measured northeasterly at right angles to the centre line of
runway 15-33 from a point distant 485.0 m measured southeasterly along
the said centre line from the northwesterly end of the said runway.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[(s. 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iis.-2description-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Toronto/Buttonville Airport Zoning Plan
No. 19-036-84-05, Sheets 2, 5, 6, 8 and 9, dated May 30, 1986, are
surfaces abutting each end to the strips associated with runways
designated 03-21 and 15-33 and are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 03 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 30 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 1 446 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 174.6 m from the projected centre line; the said imaginary
  horizontal line being 48.2 m measured vertically above the elevation
  at the end of the strip;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 15 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 1 724 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 333.6 m from the projected centre line; the said imaginary
  horizontal line being 43.1 m above the end of the strip;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 21 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 30 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 1 203 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 150.3 m from the projected centre line; the said imaginary
  horizontal line being 40.1 m measured vertically above the elevation
  at the end of the strip; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 33 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 1 768 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 340.2 m from the projected centre line; the said imaginary
  horizontal line being 44.2 m measured vertically above the elevation
  at the end of the strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[(s. 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiis.-2description-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Toronto/Buttonville Airport Zoning Plan
No. 19-036-84-05, Sheets 1 to 9 inclusive, dated May 30, 1986, is an
imaginary surface located at a common plane established at a constant
elevation of 45 m above the assigned elevation of the airport reference
point, except where that common plane is less than 9 m above the surface
of the ground, the outer surface is an imaginary surface located at 9 m
above the surface of the ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[(s. 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Description of the Strips]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivs.-2description-of-the-strips .SchedHeadL1}

The strips, as shown on Toronto/Buttonville Airport Zoning Plan
No. 19-036-84-05, Sheet 5, dated May 30, 1986 are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 03-21 being 60 m in
  width, 30 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway, and
  837.93 m in length; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 15-33, being 150 m
  in width, 75 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway,
  and 1 176.19 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[(s. 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Description of the Transitional Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vs.-2description-of-the-transitional-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface shown on Toronto/Buttonville Airport Zoning
Plan No. 19-036-84-05, Sheets 2, 5, 6 and 8, dated May 30, 1986 is a
surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at a ratio of 1 m
measured vertically to 7 m measured horizontally at right angles to the
centre line and the projected centre line of the strip extending upward
and outward from the lateral limits of the strip and its approach
surfaces to an intersection with the outer surface or another
transitional surface of an adjoining strip.
