## Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Treasury Board the Control and Supervision of the Regional Offices in the Communications and Consultations Secretariat {#order-transferring-from-the-privy-council-office-to-the-treasury-board-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-regional-offices-in-the-communications-and-consultations-secretariat .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-76
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2006-05-31

Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Treasury Board
the Control and Supervision of the Regional Offices in the
Communications and Consultations Secretariat

P.C. 2006-363 2006-05-10
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA} of the [Public
Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34),
hereby transfers from the Privy Council Office to the Treasury Board the
control and supervision of those portions of the federal public
administration within the Privy Council Office known as the Regional
Offices in the Communications and Consultations Secretariat, effective
July 1, 2006.
