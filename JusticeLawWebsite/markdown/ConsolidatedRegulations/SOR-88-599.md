## Alberta Rules of Practice Respecting Reduction in the Number of Years of Imprisonment without Eligibility for Parole {#alberta-rules-of-practice-respecting-reduction-in-the-number-of-years-of-imprisonment-without-eligibility-for-parole .Title-of-Act}

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-916477 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Rules may be cited as
the [Alberta Rules of Practice Respecting Reduction in the Number of
Years of Imprisonment Without Eligibility for
Parole](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-599).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-916479 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Rules,

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who makes an
    application and includes, according to the context, counsel acting
    for that person; ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an application made by an
    applicant pursuant to subsection 672(1) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46); ([demande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Attorney General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Attorney General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Attorney General
    of Alberta and includes counsel acting for the Attorney General;
    ([procureur général]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Chief Justice]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chief Justice]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chief Justice of the
    Court of Queen's Bench of Alberta; ([juge en chef]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[clerk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[clerk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of an application,
    the clerk of the Court of Queen's Bench of Alberta for the judicial
    district in which the sentence that is the subject of the
    application was imposed; ([greffier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the judge of the Court of
    Queen's Bench of Alberta designated by the Chief Justice to empanel
    a jury pursuant to subsection 672(2) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46) in respect of an application.
    ([juge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-916487 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An application shall be in
writing and shall contain

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's given names, surname and any other
  names the applicant may have used and the applicant's date of birth;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and place of the institution in which the
  applicant is detained;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the offence that is the subject of the application,
  the date of conviction and the sentence imposed;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the length of time the applicant has been imprisoned
  for the offence that is the subject of the application;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the grounds to be relied on in support of the
  application;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the relief sought;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's address for service; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} an outline of any evidence that the applicant intends
  to present at the hearing of the application.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application shall be
filed with the clerk.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant shall cause the applicant's application to be served on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Attorney General;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Solicitor General of Canada; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the officer in charge of the institution in which
    the applicant is detained.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Service of an application may be effected by
  registered mail, in which case it shall be deemed to have been
  effected on the tenth day after the day on which it was mailed.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Proof of service of an application may be established
  by filing with the clerk an affidavit of the person who effected the
  service or by any other means satisfactory to the clerk.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Solicitor General of Canada and the officer in
  charge of the institution in which an applicant is detained shall not
  be parties to the application.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where a clerk receives
proof of service of an application in accordance with section 5, the
clerk shall deliver the application and the proof of service of the
application to the Chief Justice who shall forward the application and
the proof of service of the application to the judge.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** On receipt of an
application, the judge may, on the judge's own initiative or on the
request of the Attorney General, dismiss the application where the judge
determines that subsection 672(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)
does not apply to the applicant.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** In respect of an
application, the judge shall make all necessary arrangements with the
applicant and the Attorney General for the conduct of the hearing,
including the fixing of the date for the empanelling of a jury and the
hearing of the application.

## [Orders]{.HTitleText1} {#h-916509 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  addition to any other order that a judge may make, the judge may make
  an order

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} requiring the Attorney General to file a general
    outline of the evidence that the Attorney General intends to present
    at the hearing of an application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} permitting the proof of facts by affidavit; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} requiring that an applicant be brought before the
    court.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a judge makes an order pursuant to
  paragraph (1)(b), the judge may, on application, require the
  attendance of the deponent at or prior to the hearing of the
  application for the purpose of cross-examination on the affidavit.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a judge makes an order pursuant to
  paragraph (1)(c), section 460 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)
  applies with such modifications as the circumstances require.

## [Hearing of Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-916517 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A jury referred to in
subsection 672(2) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) shall be
empanelled in accordance with Part XVII of that Code with such
modifications as the circumstances require.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** No person other than the
applicant and the Attorney General may present evidence at the hearing
of an application.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** At the hearing of an
application, the applicant shall present evidence first and may, if the
judge so permits, present rebuttal evidence after the evidence of the
Attorney General is presented.

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A duly
  certified transcript of the proceedings at the trial and sentencing of
  the applicant shall be admissible in evidence at the hearing of an
  application.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the hearing of an application, the judge shall
  rule on the admissibility of any evidence.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Where, at any time before
or after the commencement of the hearing of an application, the judge
determines that subsection 672(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)
does not apply to the applicant, the judge shall dismiss the application
and discharge the jury.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** After the evidence is
presented at the hearing of an application, the applicant, followed by
the Attorney General, shall address the jury.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The judge, at the hearing
of an application, shall address the jury after the address to the jury,
if any, by the applicant and the Attorney General.

## [Orders and Directions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-916527 .Part}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A judge may make any
orders or give any directions that the judge considers necessary for the
due hearing and disposition of an application, including, without
restricting the generality of the foregoing, orders or directions with
respect to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the extension or the abridgement of a time period;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sufficiency of an application or any affidavit
  relating to an application;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the service and the proof of service of any document
  in relation to an application; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the adjournment of the hearing of an application.
