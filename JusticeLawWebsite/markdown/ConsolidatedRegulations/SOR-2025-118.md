[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[motor
vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order, [[motor
vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a good that is classified under any of the tariff
  items set out in Schedule 1; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a good that is classified under any of the tariff
  items set out in Schedule 2 that is otherwise classifiable under a
  tariff item set out in Schedule 1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surtax

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Motor
  vehicles that originate in the United States that are not entitled to
  the United States Tariff are subject to a surtax in the amount of 25%
  of the value for duty determined in accordance with sections 47 to 55
  of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motor vehicles entitled to United
  States Tariff

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Motor vehicles that originate in the United States
  that are entitled to the United States Tariff are subject to a surtax
  in the amount of 25% of the value determined by the formula

  ::: {#1526208 .Subsection}
  A − B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the value for duty of the motor vehicle determined in
      accordance with sections 47 to 55 of the [Customs
      Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the value, determined in accordance with the
        [CUSMA Rules of Origin
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-155), of all goods that
        originate in Canada and Mexico used in the production of the
        motor vehicle, if the importer claims that the value of those
        goods is greater than 15% of the value determined for A, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 15% of the value determined for A, in any other
        case.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the surtax is determined in accordance with
  paragraph (a) of the description of B in the formula set out in
  subsection (2), the importer must provide, when requested by an
  officer, documentary evidence to determine the value of goods that
  originate in Canada and Mexico used in the production of the motor
  vehicle.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Country of origin

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the determination
  of whether goods originate in Canada, the United States or Mexico is
  to be made in accordance with sections 2, 4 to 8 and 10 to 13 of the
  [Determination of Country of Origin for the Purpose of Marking Goods
  (CUSMA Countries) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-23).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsections 2(1)
and (2), the following motor vehicles are not subject to the surtax:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} motor vehicles that are classified under a tariff
  item of Chapter 98 or 99 of the List of Tariff Provisions that is not
  set out in Schedule 2;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} motor vehicles that are classified under tariff
  item No. 8704.60.00 that have a gross vehicle weight exceeding five
  tonnes; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} motor vehicles that are in transit to Canada on the
  day on which this Order comes into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on April 9, 2025, but if it is registered after that day, it comes into
force on the day on which it is registered.
