## Designation of Place of Inspection and Sale {#designation-of-place-of-inspection-and-sale .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/82-221
:::

[STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS ACT](/eng/acts/S-22)

Registration 1982-11-10

Designation of Place of Inspection and Sale
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
I, P. M. Pitfield, Clerk of the Privy Council, pursuant to paragraphs
24(a) and 25(a) of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22), do
hereby revoke the [Designation of Place of Inspection and
Sale](/eng/regulations/SI-82-221), SI/80-179 of 12 November,
1980[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-82-221_e_hq_11257){#fn_SI-82-221_e_hq_11257-ID0EBBCA}
and designate, effective November 1, 1982, Room 418 in the Blackburn
Building, 85 Sparks Street, Ottawa, as the place at which any statutory
instrument that has been registered pursuant to section 6 or 32 of the
said Act and that is not precluded from inspection or the obtaining of
copies thereof by any regulations made under that Act or by any other
Act of Parliament may be inspected or copies thereof obtained.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-82-221_e_hq_11257-ID0EBBCA){#fn_SI-82-221_e_hq_11257}SI/80-179,
  1980 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3472
  :::

Ottawa, October 26, 1982

::: FlushRight
P. M. PITFIELD
:::

::: FlushRight
*Clerk of the Privy Council*
