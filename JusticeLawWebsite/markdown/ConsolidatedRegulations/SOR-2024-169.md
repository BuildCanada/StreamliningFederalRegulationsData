## Supplementary Rules Respecting Nicotine Replacement Therapies Order {#supplementary-rules-respecting-nicotine-replacement-therapies-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2024-169
:::

[FOOD AND DRUGS ACT](/eng/acts/F-27)

Registration 2024-08-15

Supplementary Rules Respecting Nicotine Replacement Therapies Order
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Minister of Health believes on reasonable grounds that the
use of a therapeutic product, other than the intended use, may present a
risk of injury to health;

Therefore, the Minister of Health makes the annexed [Supplementary Rules
Respecting Nicotine Replacement Therapies
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-169) under section 30.01[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1486245 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Order.

  [[brand element]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[brand element]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a brand name,
      trademark, trade name, logo, distinguishing guise, graphic
      arrangement, design or slogan that is reasonably associated with,
      or that evokes, a product, a service or a brand of product or
      service. ([élément de marque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[List]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[List]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the document entitled List of
      Nicotine Replacement Therapy Dosage Forms that may be Accessible
      for Self-selection by Purchasers or Consumers, as amended from
      time to time and published by the Government of Canada on its
      website. ([Liste]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[nicotine replacement therapy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[nicotine replacement therapy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      natural health product, other than a homeopathic medicine, that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contains nicotine or its salts; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is for administration in the oral
        cavity. ([thérapie de remplacement de la
        nicotine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[pharmacist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[pharmacist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection C.01.001(1) of the [Food and Drug
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870). ([pharmacien]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Natural Health
      Products
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196). ([Règlement]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[young person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[young person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who is
      under 18 years of age. ([jeune]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Words and expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context otherwise requires, words and
  expressions used in this Order have the same meaning as in the
  Regulations.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1486259 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Regulations

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  greater certainty, unless otherwise provided by this Order, the
  Regulations apply to a nicotine replacement therapy referred to in
  this Order, with the following modifications:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in paragraphs 18(1)(a) and 39(1)(a), section 92 and
    subsection 103.4(1) of the Regulations, the reference to "these
    Regulations" is to be read as including a reference to this Order;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in paragraphs 17(1)(e) and (2)(d) of the
    Regulations, the reference to "Part 5" is to be read as including a
    reference to this Order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- clinical trials

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Order does not apply to the sale or importation
  of a nicotine replacement therapy for the purposes of a clinical trial
  regulated by Part 4 of the Regulations.

## [Product Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1486267 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for product licence

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
product licence, in respect of a nicotine replacement therapy, that is
submitted under section 5 of the Regulations must contain, in addition
to the information and documents required under that section, mock-ups
of every label to be used in connection with the nicotine replacement
therapy --- including any leaflet, any package insert and any
information that appears on a website --- and mock-ups of its packages.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for amendment

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application to amend a
product licence, in respect of a nicotine replacement therapy, that is
submitted under subsection 11(2) of the Regulations must contain, in
addition to the information and documents required under that
subsection, mock-ups of every label to be used in connection with the
nicotine replacement therapy --- including any leaflet, any package
insert and any information that appears on a website --- and mock-ups of
its packages, to be used in conjunction with it after the change, if the
change is any of those described in paragraphs 11(1)(a) to (h) of the
Regulations or in section 5.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changes --- brand name or non-medicinal
ingredient

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 12 of the
Regulations, if a licensee makes any of the following changes in respect
of a nicotine replacement therapy, the licensee must not sell any lot or
batch of the nicotine replacement therapy affected by the change unless
an application to amend the product licence is submitted in accordance
with subsection 11(2) of the Regulations and the licence is amended
accordingly:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a change to a brand name that was submitted under
  paragraph 5(e) of the Regulations; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an addition or substitution of a non-medicinal
  ingredient that affects the flavour of the nicotine replacement
  therapy but that does not affect its safety or efficacy.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance and amendment

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 7 and 10 of the Regulations, in respect of a nicotine
replacement therapy, the application for a product licence or the
application to amend a product licence must also be in accordance with
section 3 or 4, as the case may be.

## [Sale]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1486278 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale on retail basis

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not sell a nicotine replacement therapy on a retail basis unless
  it is in a dosage form set out in the List.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a pharmacist or a
  person working under their supervision who sells a nicotine
  replacement therapy on a retail basis if the nicotine replacement
  therapy is not otherwise accessible to the public for self-selection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale for further sale

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A person must not sell a
nicotine replacement therapy that is in a dosage form not set out in the
List to another person, other than a pharmacist, for further sale on a
retail basis by that other person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Brand name

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A person must not sell a
nicotine replacement therapy under a brand name for which there are
reasonable grounds to believe may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} mislead a purchaser or consumer in respect of the
  intended use of the nicotine replacement therapy;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be appealing to young persons;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be associated with young persons; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} be mistaken for [[cannabis]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as
  defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) or
  for a food.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appealing to young persons

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A person must not sell a
nicotine replacement therapy if its label or package displays statements
or graphic design elements, including brand elements, for which there
are reasonable grounds to believe that they could be appealing to young
persons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flavours

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A person must not
manufacture or sell a nicotine replacement therapy that is in a dosage
form set out in the List if it contains the flavour of a confectionery,
dessert, soft drink or energy drink.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flavours --- mint or menthol

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A person must not
manufacture or sell a nicotine replacement therapy that is in a dosage
form not set out in the List if it contains a flavour other than mint,
menthol or a combination of mint and menthol.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Labelling

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person must not sell a nicotine replacement therapy unless it is
  labelled in accordance with this Order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a person may sell a nicotine
  replacement therapy that is not labelled in accordance with this Order
  if the sale is to a manufacturer or distributor.

## [Labelling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1486303 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A statement, in both
official languages, indicating an intended use by individuals 18 years
of age or older must be shown on the outer label of a nicotine
replacement therapy or, if there is no outer label, on the inner label.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warnings

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The warnings
"**WARNING**: Nicotine is highly addictive." and "[**AVERTISSEMENT** :
La nicotine crée une forte dépendance.]{.otherLang lang="fr"}" must be
shown on the principal display panel of the inner label of a nicotine
replacement therapy and, if there is an outer label, on the principal
display panel of the outer label.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presentation --- statements and warnings

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  statements and warnings required under sections 14 and 15 must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} clearly and prominently displayed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} readily discernible to a purchaser or consumer
    under the customary conditions of purchase and use, without having
    to manipulate the label.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presentation --- warnings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The warnings required under section 15 must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in characters of a single colour of type that is a
    visual equivalent of 100% solid black type on a white background or
    a uniform neutral background with maximum 5% tint of colour;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in characters of a standard sans serif font that is
    not decorative; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in characters of a type size of at least 6 points.

## [Advertising]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1486318 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- advertisement

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 18, 19, 24 and 25, [[advertisement]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as
defined in section 2 of the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27), does
not include advertising by means of the label or package of the nicotine
replacement therapy.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Smoking cessation

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A person must not
advertise or otherwise promote a nicotine replacement therapy for a use
that is other than smoking cessation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appealing to young persons

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A person must not
advertise or otherwise promote a nicotine replacement therapy if there
are reasonable grounds to believe that the advertisement or promotion
could be appealing to young persons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flavours

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** A person must not
advertise a nicotine replacement therapy, including by means of its
label or package, in a manner that could cause a purchaser or consumer
to believe that it contains

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the nicotine replacement therapy is in a dosage
  form set out in the List, the flavour of a confectionery, dessert,
  soft drink or energy drink; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the nicotine replacement therapy is in a dosage
  form not set out in the List, a flavour other than mint, menthol or a
  combination of mint and menthol.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flavour name --- descriptive or
qualifying words

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A person must not
advertise a nicotine replacement therapy, including by means of its
label or package, by displaying a flavour name that is preceded or
followed by any descriptive or qualifying words.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flavour name --- reasonably conveyed

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** A person must not
advertise a nicotine replacement therapy that is in a dosage form set
out in the List, including by means of its label or package, by
displaying a flavour name that does not reasonably convey its flavour.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flavour name --- mint or menthol

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** A person must not
advertise a nicotine replacement therapy that is in a dosage form not
set out in the List, including by means of its label or package, by
displaying a flavour name that is other than "mint", "menthol" or a
combination of "mint" and "menthol".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statements

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Any advertisement for a
nicotine replacement therapy must contain

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an advertisement in English, the
  statement

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} "This product is intended for smoking cessation
    only. Do not use if you are under 18 years of age.", or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} "Only to be used by adults who are trying to quit
    smoking.";

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an advertisement in French, the
  statement

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} "[Ce produit est uniquement destiné à vous aider à
    cesser de fumer. Ne pas utiliser si vous avez moins de 18
    ans.]{.otherLang lang="fr"}", or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} "[À utiliser uniquement par des adultes qui
    désirent cesser de fumer.]{.otherLang lang="fr"}";

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an advertisement in both official
  languages, the statements set out in

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} subparagraphs (a)(i) and (b)(i), or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} subparagraphs (a)(ii) and (b)(ii); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the statement set out in
  subparagraph (a)(i) or (ii) or (b)(i) or (ii).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warnings

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Any advertisement for a
nicotine replacement therapy must contain

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an advertisement in English, the
  warning "**WARNING**: This product contains nicotine. Nicotine is
  highly addictive.";

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an advertisement in French, the
  warning "[**AVERTISSEMENT** : Ce produit contient de la nicotine. La
  nicotine crée une forte dépendance]{.otherLang lang="fr"}.";

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an advertisement in both official
  languages, the warnings set out in paragraphs (a) and (b); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the warning set out in
  paragraph (a) or (b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audio advertising

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The statements and
warnings required under sections 24 and 25 that are contained in an
audio advertisement for a nicotine replacement therapy must be
communicated

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in their entirety at the same speed, volume and tone
  as the main message, without any word being emphasized more than any
  other; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} without any music or background sound.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Visual advertising

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** The statements and
warnings required under sections 24 and 25 that are contained in a
visual advertisement for a nicotine replacement therapy must be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} clearly and prominently displayed; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} readily discernible to a purchaser or consumer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audio-visual advertising

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Sections 26 and 27 apply
to an advertisement for a nicotine replacement therapy that consists of
both audio and visual components.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1486363 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Six month transition --- appealing to
young persons

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may sell a nicotine replacement therapy that is not in
  accordance with section 10 during the period of six months after the
  day on which this Order comes into force.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2025-9, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Six month transition --- advertising

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person may advertise a nicotine replacement therapy
  in a manner that is not in accordance with sections 18 to 28 during
  the period of six months after the day on which this Order comes into
  force.
