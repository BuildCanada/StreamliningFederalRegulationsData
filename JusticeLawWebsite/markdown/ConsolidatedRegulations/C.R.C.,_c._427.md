## Minister Designation Order (Canada Business Corporations Act) {#minister-designation-order-canada-business-corporations-act .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 427
:::

[CANADA BUSINESS CORPORATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/C-44)

Order Designating a Member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada to
Act as the Minister for the Purposes of the Canada Business Corporations
Act
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-556656 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the Minister Designation Order (Canada Business Corporations Act).

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-556658 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Consumer
and Corporate Affairs is hereby designated as the member of the Queen\'s
Privy Council for Canada to act as the Minister for the purposes of the
[Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44).
