## Foreign Claims (People's Republic of China) Settlement Regulations {#foreign-claims-peoples-republic-of-china-settlement-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/82-381
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 9, 1966

Registration 1982-04-01

Regulations Respecting the Determination and Payment out of the Foreign
Claims Fund of Certain claims against the Government of the People's
Republic of China

P.C. 1982-995 1982-04-01
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Secretary of State for External Affairs and the Minister of Finance,
pursuant to Vote 22a of Schedule B to Appropriation Act No. 9, 1966, is
pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the
determination and payment out of the Foreign Claims Fund of Certain
claims against the Government of the People's Republic of
China](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-381).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873519 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Foreign Claims (People's Republic of China) Settlement
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-381).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873521 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement between the
    Government of Canada and the Government of the People's Republic of
    China concerning the settlement of private property claims, signed
    at Beijing, August 20, 1981; ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canadian citizen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian citizen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who is a Canadian citizen within the
      meaning of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29) and who is not a
      citizen of any other country,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who is a Canadian citizen within the
      meaning of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29) and who is also a
      citizen of another country but whose dominant nationality is
      Canadian, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that is incorporated under the laws
      of Canada and that is substantially owned by persons described in
      paragraphs (a) or (b); ([citoyen canadien]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[certain bank accounts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certain bank accounts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the bank
    accounts in the People's Republic of China of Daniel and Nina
    Gnarowski, Alexei I. Zaionchkovsky, Archibald H. Carter and G.T.
    Fulford Co. Ltd.; ([certains comptes bancaires]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Chief Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chief Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chief
    Commissioner of the Commission; ([commissaire en
    chef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[claim]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[claim]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a claim by a Canadian citizen
    against the Government of the People's Republic of China in respect
    of property in the People's Republic of China, including certain
    bank accounts, affected by measures of intervention, expropriation,
    state disposition and administration or other similar measures taken
    by the Government of the People's Republic of China on or after
    October 1, 1949 and on or before August 20, 1981;
    ([réclamation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[claimant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[claimant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canadian citizen who has
    submitted a notice of claim to the Government of Canada;
    ([réclamant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Foreign Claims
    Commission established by Order in Council P.C. 1981-1447 of May 28,
    1981; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the special account known as the
    Foreign Claims Fund established in the Consolidated Revenue Fund by
    the Minister of Finance pursuant to the Vote;
    ([caisse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Secretary of State for
    External Affairs; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[notice of claim]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[notice of claim]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a written
    communication that, in the opinion of the Commission, clearly
    defines the subject-matter of a claim; ([avis de
    réclamation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Vote]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Vote]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Vote 22a of Schedule B to
    Appropriation Act No. 9, 1966. ([crédit]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Notice of Claim]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873537 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  notice of claim received by the Government of Canada on or before
  October 20, 1981 shall be referred to the Commission.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Only claims in respect of which a notice of claim has
  been received by the Government of Canada on or before October 20,
  1981 shall be considered by the Commission.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} All documentary evidence on which a claimant intends
  to rely in establishing his claim shall be submitted to the Commission
  on or before June 1, 1982.

## [Eligibility of Claimant to Receive an Award]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873542 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In order
  to be eligible to receive an award in respect of a claim, a claimant
  must have been a Canadian citizen as of October 20, 1981 and the
  rights pertaining to the claim must have been held continuously by a
  Canadian citizen during the period commencing on the date the claim
  arose and ending on October 20, 1981.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a claim is deemed
  to have arisen when the owner of the property constituting the
  subject-matter of the claim was effectively deprived of the incidents
  of ownership and control thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/83-690, s. 1
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where a claimant dies after
October 20, 1981, an award in respect of his claim may be made to any
lawful successor in interest thereto, irrespective of his nationality,
if the deceased claimant

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} was eligible within the meaning of subsection 4(1);
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} submitted a notice of claim on or before October 20,
  1981.

## [Recommendation for Award and Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873551 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Commissioner shall submit a report to the Minister and the
  Minister of Finance on each claim considered by the Commission
  containing the recommendations of the Commission concerning

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the eligibility of the claimant to receive an
    award, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the award, if any, that should be
    paid to the claimant out of the Fund,

  having regard to all applicable laws, including the laws of Canada,
  the laws of the People's Republic of China, international law and
  these Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Commissioner may submit a report to the
  Minister and the Minister of Finance on any claim on the basis of the
  information then available to the Commission where, in his opinion,
  postponement of his report in respect of the claim would result in
  undue delay.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the report referred to in subsection (1)
  shall, when approved by the Minister and the Minister of Finance, be
  sent to the claimant in respect of whom the report was made.

<!-- -->

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of an award that may be recommended by the Commission for a
  corporation that is a Canadian citizen is limited to that proportion
  of the award it would have otherwise recommended be paid to the
  corporation out of the Fund that is equivalent to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proportion of the total issued share capital of
    the corporation that was beneficially held by Canadian citizens on
    the date the claim arose, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proportion of the total issued share capital of
    the corporation that was beneficially held by Canadian citizens on
    October 20, 1981,

  whichever is the lesser.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, in the opinion of the Commission, a claimant
  has been paid or is likely to be paid any compensation in respect of
  his claim from a source other than the Fund, the Commission shall
  deduct the amount of that compensation from the amount of the award it
  would have otherwise recommended be paid to the claimant out of the
  Fund.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a claimant by reason of his own neglect or
  default has failed to receive, or has forfeited his right to receive,
  any compensation in respect of his claim from a source other than the
  Fund, subsection (2) shall apply as if he had been paid that
  compensation.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall not recommend in respect of any
  one person or any one claim an award in excess of \$100,000.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Chief Commissioner
shall deliver to the Minister of Finance all bonds, shares and other
documents on the basis of which the Commission has recommended an award.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No right to an award is
conferred by any recommendation of the Commission contained in a report
made by the Chief Commissioner to the Minister or the Minister of
Finance.

## [Payment from the Fund]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873569 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of a report referred to in subsection 6(1), the Minister and
  the Minister of Finance shall determine

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the portion of any award recommended by the
    Commission that is to be paid out of the Fund in respect of a claim;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the claimant is dead, the person, if any, to
    whom payment of the award is to be made.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In computing the amount to be paid out of the Fund in
  respect of a claim, the Minister and the Minister of Finance shall
  deduct the amount of any compensation that has been paid or is likely
  to be paid to the claimant from a source other than the Fund that the
  Commission has not already deducted pursuant to section 7 in
  recommending an award.

<!-- -->

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  making payment of an award or any part thereof, the Minister of
  Finance shall obtain a release in such form as he considers
  satisfactory in respect of the claim for which payment is to be made.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may recommend or the Minister of
  Finance may require that, as a condition to the granting of an award,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a claimant assign to Her Majesty in right of Canada
    his entitlement to compensation in respect of his claim from a
    source other than the Fund; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if such entitlement cannot be validly assigned, the
    claimant give a written undertaking to do all things necessary to
    vest in Her Majesty the amount of such compensation and his rights
    therein.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Awards in respect of
claims shall be paid out of that part of the Fund consisting of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} money received from the Government of the People's
  Republic of China under Articles I and III of the Agreement and
  credited to the Fund pursuant to paragraph (b) of the Vote;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} money transferred to the Fund pursuant to section 13;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any interest credited to the Fund in respect of the
  money referred to in paragraphs (a) and (b).

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Finance
shall transfer to the Fund from the War Claims Fund established pursuant
to Vote 696, Appropriation Act No. 4, 1952, the balance remaining of the
portion of the money and of the proceeds and earnings of property
credited to the War Claims Fund pursuant to paragraph (b) of Order in
Council P.C. 1976-3098 of December 16, 1976, which related to property
of residents of China that was vested in the Custodian of Enemy Property
in respect of World War II.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Where the money in the
part of the Fund described in section 12 is insufficient to pay in full
all awards that the Minister and the Minister of Finance determine may
be paid out of that part of the Fund,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a payment shall be made in respect of each award
  equal to the full amount thereof or \$5,000, whichever is the lesser;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the balance of any awards that are not paid in full
  shall be paid on a [pro rata]{.otherLang lang="la"} basis from any
  money remaining in that part of the Fund.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Awards may be made in one
or more instalments at such times as may, in the opinion of the Minister
of Finance, be warranted having regard to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amounts available in the part of the Fund out of
  which awards may be paid; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the possible amounts that may be awarded in respect
  of unpaid claims including those still before the Commission.

## [Deputy Chief Commissioner]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873591 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** In the event of the
absence or incapacity of the Chief Commissioner or if the office of the
Chief Commissioner becomes vacant, the Deputy Chief Commissioner may
perform all the duties and functions of the Chief Commissioner under
these Regulations.
