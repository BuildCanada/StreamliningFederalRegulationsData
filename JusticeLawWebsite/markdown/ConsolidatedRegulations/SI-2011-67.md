## Order Transfering to Shared Services Canada the Control and Supervision of Certain Portions of the Federal Public Administration in the Department of Public Works and Government Services {#order-transfering-to-shared-services-canada-the-control-and-supervision-of-certain-portions-of-the-federal-public-administration-in-the-department-of-public-works-and-government-services .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2011-67
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2011-08-17

Order Transfering to Shared Services Canada the Control and Supervision
of Certain Portions of the Federal Public Administration in the
Department of Public Works and Government Services

P.C. 2011-877 2011-08-03
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EEGCCA} of the [Public Service
  Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBGCCA}, transfers to Shared
  Services Canada the control and supervision of the following portions
  of the federal public administration in the Department of Public Works
  and Government Services known as:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Services Transition and Major Projects Unit,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Chief Technology Officer Unit,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Enterprise Services Unit,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the Service Management and Delivery Unit,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the Email, Data Centre and Network Services Unit,
    and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the Email, Data Centre and Network Services
    Support Unit, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} orders that the Minister of Public Works and
  Government Services shall preside over Shared Services Canada,

effective August 4, 2011.

  2003, c. 22, s. 207
