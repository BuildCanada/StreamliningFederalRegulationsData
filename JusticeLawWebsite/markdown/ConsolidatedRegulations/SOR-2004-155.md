## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-711691 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Chemical Weapons Convention
      Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-27.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[CAS registry number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[CAS registry number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      identification number assigned to a chemical substance by the
      Chemical Abstracts Service Division of the American Chemical
      Society. ([numéro d'enregistrement CAS]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[IUPAC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[IUPAC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Union of
      Pure and Applied Chemistry. ([UICPA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Schedule 1 chemical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Schedule 1 chemical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a toxic chemical
      or precursor listed in Schedule 1 of the Schedules of Chemicals
      set out in the Annex on Chemicals in the Convention. ([produit du
      tableau 1]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of these Regulations,
  [[facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the plant site, plant or unit
  where the activities authorized under these Regulations are carried
  out.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic transmission

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any notice or application that is required to be
  given in writing under these Regulations may be given by electronic
  means.

## [National Limit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-711703 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National limit

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The maximum aggregate
quantity of Schedule 1 chemicals authorized in Canada at any given time
shall not exceed one tonne.

## [Authorizations by Licence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-711706 .Part}

### [Authorization]{.HTitleText2} {#h-711707 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to these Regulations, the National Authority shall authorize, by the
  issuance, amendment or renewal of a licence, an individual who
  ordinarily resides in Canada to produce, use, acquire or possess a
  Schedule 1 chemical if the following conditions are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} public health and safety, the environment,
    international relations and national defence and security are
    unlikely to be compromised;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the activities are for research, medical,
    pharmaceutical or protective purposes;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate quantity of Schedule 1 chemicals that
    may be produced does not exceed 10 kg per year per facility; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the quantities authorized are consistent with the
    intended purpose referred to in paragraph (b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Production for protective purposes

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} There shall be only one licensed facility in Canada
  for the production of Schedule 1 chemicals for protective purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of licence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The term of a licence shall not exceed three years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The National Authority shall assign a number and a
  date of issue to each licence.

### [Applications]{.HTitleText2} {#h-711723 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence application

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  written application for a licence in respect of each facility shall be
  submitted to the National Authority by the individual who will be
  responsible for the activities requested in the application. It shall
  be signed by that applicant and by the individual responsible for the
  facility, if they are not the same individual, and shall contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the following information in respect of the
    applicant:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their full name and any aliases,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} their addresses for the last five years,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} their citizenship,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} their date and place of birth and a copy of
      their birth certificate or other document that establishes their
      place and date of birth,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a summary of their relevant education and work
      experience,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of any entity for which
      they work and their position, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} their business telephone number, facsimile
      number and email address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the following information in respect of the
    individual responsible for the facility:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their full name and any aliases,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} their addresses for the last five years,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} their citizenship,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} their date and place of birth and a copy of
      their birth certificate or other document that establishes their
      place and date of birth,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of any entity for which they
      work and their position, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} their business telephone number, facsimile
      number and email address;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the following information in respect of each
    individual who will have access to a Schedule 1 chemical in carrying
    out the activities requested in the application:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their full name and any aliases,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} their addresses for the last five years,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} their citizenship,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} their date and place of birth and a copy of
      their birth certificate or other document that establishes their
      place and date of birth,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of any entity for which they
      work and their position, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} their business telephone number, facsimile
      number and email address;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the facility name and address;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate quantity of Schedule 1 chemicals that
    is to be authorized by the licence in respect of each calendar year;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the activities to be carried out
    and the purpose of each activity;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} for each Schedule 1 chemical that is to be
    authorized by the licence,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its IUPAC name and CAS registry number (or
      structural formula if no CAS registry number has been assigned),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity to be authorized in respect of each
      calendar year,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity to be produced in respect of each
      calendar year and the production method to be employed, if
      applicable, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the IUPAC name, CAS registry number (or
      structural formula if no CAS registry number has been assigned)
      and quantity of each precursor listed in Schedule 1, 2 or 3 of the
      Schedules of Chemicals set out in the Annex on Chemicals in the
      Convention to be used for the production of the Schedule 1
      chemical in respect of each calendar year, if applicable;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a licence that would authorize the
    production of an aggregate quantity of more than 100 g of Schedule 1
    chemicals during each calendar year, a detailed technical
    description of the facility; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the proposed term of the licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information communicated

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application shall include the consent of the
  applicant and of the individuals referred to in paragraphs (1)(b)
  and (c) to share the information required under paragraphs (1)(a), (b)
  and (c) in order to verify it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time to submit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application for a licence shall be submitted to
  the National Authority

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to activities being carried out at an
    existing facility as of the date of coming into force of these
    Regulations, not later than 60 days after that date; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to activities proposed to be carried
    out at a facility,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the proposed aggregate quantity of Schedule 1
      chemicals to be produced per calendar year exceeds 100 g, at least
      200 days before the day on which those activities are proposed to
      start, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, at least 60 days before the
      day on which those activities are proposed to start.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any applicable change referred to in subsection
    (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time to submit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application to amend a licence shall be submitted

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an amendment to the detailed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the licensee;

    made; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proposed term of the licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time to submit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application shall be submitted to the National
  Authority at least 60 days before the licence expires.

### [Conditions Applicable to Licensees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-711803 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of licensee

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The licensee

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall ensure that the activities authorized by the
  licence are carried out safely;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2018-202, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} shall not transfer, assign or otherwise dispose of a
  licence;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} shall notify the National Authority in writing, no
  later than seven days before any change, of the change

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} of name of the licensee, of the individual
    responsible for the facility, of the facility or of the entity, if
    any for whom they work, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the list of individuals referred to in
    paragraph 4(1)(c);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} shall control access to the Schedule 1 chemicals set
  out in the licence;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} shall inform the National Authority as soon as
  practicable of any loss or unintended release of a Schedule 1
  chemical; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} shall immediately inform the National Authority of
  any theft of a Schedule 1 chemical or any attempt by an unauthorized
  individual to obtain a Schedule 1 chemical.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of activities

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** In the case of a cessation
of the activities authorized by the licence, the licensee shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the National Authority in writing of the
  cessation of activities, at least seven days before the activities
  cease;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} take measures to destroy or transfer the Schedule 1
  chemicals immediately after the cessation of activities;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if it is intended to destroy the Schedule 1
  chemicals,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} notify the National Authority of that intent and of
    the proposed method of destruction at least seven days before the
    day on which destruction is proposed to take place,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2018-202, s. 5\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} provide the National Authority with
    documentation, no later than 30 days after the cessation of
    activities, that confirms the destruction, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} bear the costs of the destruction;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if it is intended to transfer the Schedule 1
  chemical,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} notify the National Authority in writing of the
    intent to transfer and of the name, address, telephone number and
    fax number of the recipient of the transfer at least seven days
    before the day on which the transfer is proposed to take place,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} provide the National Authority with documentation,
    no later than 30 days after the cessation, that confirms the
    transfer, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} bear the costs of the transfer;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} return the licence to the National Authority no later
  than 30 days after the cessation of activities;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the record referred to in section 10 is
  retained for five years after the cessation of activities; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} if a facility ceases to exist within five years after
  the cessation of activities, provide the National Authority with a
  copy of the record referred to in section 10 within 30 days after the
  day on which the facility ceases to exist.

- SOR/2018-202, s. 5

### [Domestic Transfers, Exports and Imports]{.HTitleText2} {#h-711839 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Domestic transfers

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** If a Schedule 1 chemical
is transferred within Canada, it shall only be transferred to the holder
of a licence issued under these Regulations, to the single small-scale
facility referred to in section 19 or to an individual referred to in
paragraph 18(c).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exports and imports

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), if a
  licensee intends to export or import a Schedule 1 chemical, the
  licensee shall, at least 45 days before the export or import, provide
  a written notice to the National Authority that contains

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the
    licensee, as well as the number and date of issuance of the licence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date of the licensee's application under the
    [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), if applicable;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for each Schedule 1 chemical, its IUPAC name, its
    CAS registry number (or its structural formula if no CAS registry
    number has been assigned) and its quantity;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the export or import;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated date of shipment;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an export, the name and licence
    particulars of the exporter, if different from the licensee, and the
    name and address, including the country, of the ultimate recipient
    and of the individual or entity for which that recipient acts, as
    well as their telephone and fax numbers; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an import, the name and licence
    particulars of the importer, if different from the licensee, and the
    name and address, including the country, of the originating sender
    and of the individual or entity for which that sender acts, as well
    as their telephone and fax numbers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saxitoxin

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a licensee intends to export or import 5 mg or
  less of the Schedule 1 chemical saxitoxin for medical or diagnostic
  purposes, the licensee may provide the notice required under
  subsection (1) at any time before the export or import.

### [Public Health and Safety, International Relations, Environmental and National Defence or Security Risks]{.HTitleText2} {#h-711854 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National Authority may issue or amend

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  immediate measures involving a Schedule 1 chemical are required in
  order to prevent the compromising of public health and safety, the
  environment, international relations or national defence and security,
  the National Authority shall issue or amend a licence to authorize
  those measures.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licence issued or an amendment made under
  subsection (1) is valid for no more than 30 days.

### [Refusal, Suspension or Cancellation of a Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-711862 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of licence

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  National Authority shall suspend a licence if public health and
  safety, the environment, international relations or national defence
  and security is likely to be compromised by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensed activity or the manner in which the
    licensed activity is carried out; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any of the following situations, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the licensee or any individual acting on the
      licensee's behalf fails to comply with the Act or the regulations
      made under it, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the licensee or any individual acting on the
      licensee's behalf is convicted of an offence under the Act.

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2018-202, s. 8\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of suspension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a licence is suspended, the licensee shall
  comply with the obligations established by the licence, but shall
  cease the activities authorized by the licence for the duration of the
  suspension.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} comply with paragraphs 9(b) to (e); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} no later than 30 days after the cancellation,
    provide to the National Authority a copy of the record referred to
    in section 10.

## [Authorizations Without a Licence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-711902 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorizations

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The following activities
are authorized without a licence:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the acquisition and possession of the Schedule 1
  chemical saxitoxin occurring naturally in organisms such as shellfish
  and algae, if the chemical is not recovered;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the acquisition and possession of the Schedule 1
  chemical ricin occurring naturally in the castor bean plant ([Ricinus
  communis]{.otherLang lang="la"}) or its beans, if the chemical is not
  recovered; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the acquisition, possession and use of a Schedule 1
  chemical by a peace officer to the extent necessary for the
  administration or enforcement of the law.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-202, s. 11

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Single small-scale facility

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  production, use, acquisition and possession of Schedule 1 chemicals
  for research, medical, pharmaceutical or protective purposes are
  authorized, without a licence, subject to the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all activities are carried out at the single
    small-scale facility located at Defence Research and Development
    Canada --- Suffield, in Alberta;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the production is carried out in reaction vessels
    in production lines not configured for continuous operation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the volume of each reaction vessel does not exceed
    100 L;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total volume of all reaction vessels with a
    volume exceeding 5 L is not more than 500 L;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the following information is provided to the
    National Authority by the individual responsible for the single
    small-scale facility, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} his or her name,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a list of all individuals who will have access
      to a Schedule 1 chemical in carrying out the activities, including
      their name, citizenship, date and place of birth, position,
      business address, telephone number, facsimile number and
      electronic mail address,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the maximum aggregate quantity of all Schedule
      1 chemicals stored at any one time during the calendar year,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a description of the activities carried out at
      the single small-scale facility and the purpose of each activity,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures to control access
      to the single small-scale facility and to the Schedule 1
      chemicals,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a description of the storage arrangements for
      Schedule 1 chemicals and precursors listed in Schedule 1, 2 or 3
      of the Schedules of Chemicals set out in the Annex on Chemicals in
      the Convention used for the production of Schedule 1 chemicals,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} a detailed technical description of the single
      small-scale facility, including detailed diagrams and an inventory
      of equipment,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} information on any anticipated change at the
      facility compared to any information previously submitted under
      subparagraph (vii),

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} for each Schedule 1 chemical produced, acquired,
      possessed, used, imported, exported or stored during the previous
      calendar year,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} its IUPAC name and CAS registry number (or
        structural formula if no CAS registry number has been assigned),

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the quantity produced and a description of the
        production methods used,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the IUPAC name, CAS registry number (or
        structural formula if no CAS registry number has been assigned)
        and quantity of each precursor listed in Schedule 1, 2 or 3 of
        the Schedules of Chemicals set out in the Annex on Chemicals in
        the Convention used in the production of the Schedule 1
        chemical,

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} the quantity used and the purpose of its use,

      - [(E)]{.lawlabel} the quantities received from and supplied to
        other facilities in Canada, including the quantity of each
        shipment, its purpose and the name of the other facility,

      - [(F)]{.lawlabel} the amounts imported and exported, including
        the name of the source or destination country, purpose and date
        of transfer,

      - [(G)]{.lawlabel} the maximum quantity stored at any time during
        the calendar year, and

      - [(H)]{.lawlabel} the quantity stored at the end of the calendar
        year, and

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} for each Schedule 1 chemical that is anticipated
      to be produced, used or stored,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} its IUPAC name and CAS registry number (or
        structural formula if no CAS registry number has been assigned),
        and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the quantity that is anticipated to be produced
        and the purpose of the production;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if it is intended to transfer a Schedule 1 chemical
    within Canada, the Schedule 1 chemical is transferred only to the
    holder of a licence issued in accordance with these Regulations or
    to an individual referred to in paragraph 18(c);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if it is intended to export or import a Schedule 1
    chemical, the notice required by section 12 is provided;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2018-202, s. 12\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} access to the Schedule 1 chemicals is controlled;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the National Authority is informed as soon as
    practicable of any loss or unintended release of a Schedule 1
    chemical; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the National Authority is informed immediately of
    any theft of a Schedule 1 chemical or any attempt by an unauthorized
    individual to obtain a Schedule 1 chemical.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time to submit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The individual responsible for the single small-scale
  facility shall provide to the National Authority

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information required under subparagraphs
    (1)(e)(iii) and (ix) by March 1 of each calendar year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information required under subparagraph
    (1)(e)(x) by September 15 of each calendar year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice respecting certain changes

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The individual responsible for the single small-scale
  facility shall notify the National Authority in writing of any change
  to the information required

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} under subparagraphs (1)(e)(i) and (ii) no later
    than seven days before the change; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} under subparagraph (1)(e)(viii) at least 200 days
    before the change.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retain documents

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  individual responsible for the activities at the single small-scale
  facility shall ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any document is retained that supports the
    information required under paragraph 19(1)(e); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a record is retained showing the IUPAC name, CAS
    registry number (or structural formula if no CAS registry number has
    been assigned) and quantity of each Schedule 1 chemical produced,
    used, acquired, possessed, transferred, exported, imported or
    destroyed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The documents, records and reports shall be retained
  for five years after the authorized activities cease.
