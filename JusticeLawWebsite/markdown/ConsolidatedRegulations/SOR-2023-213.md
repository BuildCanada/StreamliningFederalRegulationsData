## Order Exempting Certain Navigable Waters Located in Quebec from the Application of Section 23 of the Canadian Navigable Waters Act {#order-exempting-certain-navigable-waters-located-in-quebec-from-the-application-of-section-23-of-the-canadian-navigable-waters-act .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2023-213
:::

[CANADIAN NAVIGABLE WATERS ACT](/eng/acts/N-22)

Registration 2023-10-06

Order Exempting Certain Navigable Waters Located in Quebec from the
Application of Section 23 of the Canadian Navigable Waters Act

P.C. 2023-1019 2023-10-06
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Minister of Transport has received an application to exempt
the navigable waters listed in the schedule to the annexed Order from
the application of section 23[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81100-2-386_hq_1676562923){#fn_81100-2-386_hq_1676562923-ID0EECCA}
of the [Canadian Navigable Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-22)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81100-2-386_hq_1676553558){#fn_81100-2-386_hq_1676553558-ID0EBCCA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81100-2-386_hq_1676562923-ID0EECCA){#fn_81100-2-386_hq_1676562923}S.C.
  2019, c. 28, s. 57
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81100-2-386_hq_1676553558-ID0EBCCA){#fn_81100-2-386_hq_1676553558}R.S., c.
  N-22; S.C. 2012, c. 31, s. 316; S.C. 2019, c. 28, s. 46
  :::

And whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that this exemption
would be in the public interest;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, makes the annexed [Order
Exempting Certain Navigable Waters Located in Quebec from the
Application of Section 23 of the Canadian Navigable Waters
Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-213) under subsection 24(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81100-2-386_hq_1676562923){#fn_81100-2-386_hq_1676562923-ID0EEAAA}
of the [Canadian Navigable Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-22)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81100-2-386_hq_1676553558){#fn_81100-2-386_hq_1676553558-ID0EBAAA}.
:::

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1424431 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Navigable waters

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The navigable waters listed
in the schedule to this Order are exempt from the application of section
23 of the [Canadian Navigable Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-22).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1424434 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2023-213_e_transform_hq_1698848549){#fn_SOR-2023-213_e_transform_hq_1698848549-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is
published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part II.
