## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000611 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Ontario Pork
    Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Farm Products
    Marketing Act of Ontario. ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hogs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hogs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means hogs produced in Ontario.
    ([porcs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production of hogs. ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[weanling hogs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[weanling hogs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means hogs that have a weight
    of less than 56 kilograms. ([porcelets sevrés]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

- SOR/2013-86, s. 1

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000619 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3, this
Order applies to the marketing, in interprovincial and export trade by
persons in Ontario, of weanling hogs that are not marketed for the
purpose of slaughter and all other hogs that are marketed for the
purpose of slaughter.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2013-86, s. 2

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000623 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order does not apply
to producers in the County of Haliburton and the Territorial Districts
of Ontario except for the Territorial Districts of Cochrane and
Timiskaming.

## [Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000626 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Each producer shall pay to
the Commodity Board the following charge for each hog marketed in
interprovincial or export trade by or on behalf of the producer:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$0.20 for a weanling hog; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$0.95 for any other hog.

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000632 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  hogs are marketed through the Commodity Board, the Commodity Board
  shall deduct the charge imposed pursuant to section 5 from the moneys
  payable by the Commodity Board to the producer for the sale of the
  hogs.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the charge payable in respect of hogs marketed
  by or on behalf of a producer has not been deducted in the manner
  described in subsection (1), the producer shall remit the charge to
  the Commodity Board at its office located at 15 Waulron Street,
  Etobicoke, Ontario, no later than the 15th day of the month following
  the month in which the hogs were marketed.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000636 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on September 12, 1996.
