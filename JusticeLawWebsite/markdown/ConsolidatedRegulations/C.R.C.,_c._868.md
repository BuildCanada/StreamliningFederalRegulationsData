## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567380 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Fitness and Amateur Sport
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._868).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567382 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
[Fitness and Amateur Sport Act](/eng/acts/F-25),

[[amateur sport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[amateur sport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any athletic activity
    when engaged in solely for recreation, fitness or pleasure and not
    as a means of livelihood; ([sport amateur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[fitness]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fitness]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the state in which a person is
    able to function at his physical and mental optimum.
    ([santé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})
