## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1261206 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Belarus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Belarus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Republic of Belarus and
    includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any of its political subdivisions;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its government and any of its departments or any
      government or department of its political subdivisions; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any of its agencies or any agency of its
      political subdivisions. ([Bélarus]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[consumer communication device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[consumer communication device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of
    the following items:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} computers;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} disk drives, solid-state storage equipment and
      other memory devices;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} input/output control units, other than industrial
      controllers designed for chemical processing;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} graphics accelerators and graphics coprocessors;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} monitors;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} printers;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} modems, network access controllers and
      communications channel controllers;

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} keyboards, mice and similar devices;

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} mobile phones, including cellular and satellite
      telephones, personal digital assistants, subscriber identity
      module (SIM) cards and similar devices;

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} information security equipment and peripherals;

    - [(k)]{.lawlabel} digital cameras and memory cards;

    - [(l)]{.lawlabel} television and radio receivers;

    - [(m)]{.lawlabel} recording devices;

    - [(n)]{.lawlabel} batteries, chargers, carrying cases and
      accessories for a good referred to in paragraphs (a) to (m); and

    - [(o)]{.lawlabel} software, other than encryption source code, for
      use with a good referred to in paragraphs (a) to (n). ([dispositif
      de communication]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Foreign
    Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Restricted Goods and Technologies List]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Restricted Goods and Technologies List]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    the Restricted Goods and Technologies List, prepared by the
    Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development and published
    on the Department's website, as amended from time to time. ([Liste
    des marchandises et technologies réglementées]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[technology]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[technology]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any form of technical data
    or technical assistance, such as providing instruction, training,
    consulting or technical advice services or transferring know-how or
    technical data. ([technologie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [List]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1261214 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Listed person

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A person whose name is
listed in Schedule 1 is a person who is in Belarus, or is or was a
national of Belarus who does not ordinarily reside in Canada, and in
respect of whom the Governor in Council, on the recommendation of the
Minister, is satisfied that there are reasonable grounds to believe is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who has participated in gross and systematic
  human rights violations in Belarus;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} a person engaged in activities that directly or
  indirectly facilitate, support, provide funding for or contribute to a
  violation or attempted violation of the sovereignty or territorial
  integrity of Ukraine or that obstruct the work of international
  organizations in Ukraine;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a current or former senior official of the Government
  of Belarus;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an associate of a person referred to in any of
  paragraphs (a) to (b);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a family member of a person referred to in any of
  paragraphs (a) to (c) or (g);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an entity owned, held or controlled, directly or
  indirectly, by a person referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to (d) or
  acting on behalf of or at the direction of such a person;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} an entity owned, held or controlled, directly or
  indirectly, by Belarus or acting on behalf of or at the direction of
  Belarus; or

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a senior official of an entity referred to in
  paragraph (e) or (f).

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1261222 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited dealings and activities

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deal in any property, wherever situated, that is
  owned, held or controlled by a listed person or by a person acting on
  behalf of a listed person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into or facilitate any transaction related to a
  dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial or related services in respect
  of a dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} make available any goods, wherever situated, to a
  listed person or to a person acting on behalf of a listed person; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} make available any property or provide any financial
  or related services to or for the benefit of a listed person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restricted goods

- **[[3.6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It
  is prohibited for any person in Canada and any Canadian outside Canada
  to export, sell, supply or ship any good, wherever situated, to
  Belarus or to any person in Belarus if the good is referred to in the
  Restricted Goods and Technologies List or in Schedule 3.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restricted technologies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for any person in Canada and any
  Canadian outside Canada to provide to Belarus or to any person in
  Belarus any technology that is referred to in the Restricted Goods and
  Technologies List or in Schedule 3.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- goods

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods temporarily exported for use by a
    representative of the media from Canada or from a partner country
    that is listed in Annex 1 to the Restricted Goods and Technologies
    List;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods for use in support of international nuclear
    safeguards verifications;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} goods for use by a department or agency of the
    Government of Canada or of a partner country that is listed in Annex
    1 to the Restricted Goods and Technologies List;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} goods for use in inspections under the Convention
    on the Prohibition of the Development, Production, Stockpiling and
    Use of Chemical Weapons and on their Destruction, signed at Paris,
    France on January 13, 1993, as amended from time to time;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} goods for use in relation to the activities of the
    International Space Station;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} software updates for end-users that are civilian
    entities that are owned, held or controlled by a Canadian or a
    national of a partner country that is listed in Annex 1 to the
    Restricted Goods and Technologies List, or subsidiaries of those
    entities;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} civil aircraft registered in a foreign state that
    are departing from Canada after a temporary sojourn in Canada or
    civil aircraft registered in Canada departing for a temporary
    sojourn abroad;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the following goods, if stored on board an aircraft
    or ship:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} equipment and spare parts that are necessary for
      the proper operation of the aircraft or ship, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} usual and reasonable quantities of supplies
      intended for consumption on board the aircraft or ship during the
      outgoing and return flight or voyage;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} goods exported for use or consumption on an
    aircraft or ship that is registered in Canada or the United States;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} goods exported by an air carrier that is owned by a
    Canadian or a national of the United States for use in the
    maintenance, repair or operation of an aircraft registered in Canada
    or the United States;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} consumer communication devices that are generally
    available to the public and designed to be installed by the user
    without further substantial support; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} personal effects exported by an individual that are
    solely for the use of the individual or the individual's immediate
    family and are not intended for sale or to remain in Belarus unless
    consumed there.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- technologies

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply to technology provided
  in relation to a good if the export, sale, supply or shipment of that
  good is authorized by subsection (3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Section 3 does not apply in
respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any payment made by or on behalf of a listed person
  that is due under a contract that the listed person entered into
  before they became a listed person, provided that the payment is not
  made to a listed person or to a person acting on behalf of a listed
  person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any transactions necessary for a Canadian to transfer
  to a non-listed person any accounts, funds or investments of a
  Canadian held by a listed person on the day on which that person
  became a listed person;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any dealings with a listed person required with
  respect to loan repayments made to any person in Canada, or any
  Canadian outside Canada, for loans entered into with any person other
  than a listed person, and for enforcement and realization of security
  in respect of those loans, or payments by guarantors guaranteeing
  those loans;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any dealings with a listed person required with
  respect to loan repayments made to any person in Canada, or any
  Canadian outside Canada, for loans entered into with that listed
  person before they became a listed person, and for enforcement and
  realization of security in respect of those loans, or payments by
  guarantors guaranteeing those loans;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any benefit paid under the [Old Age Security
  Act](/eng/acts/O-9), the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or an
  Act respecting the Québec Pension Plan, CQLR, c. R-9, any
  superannuation, pension or benefit paid under or in respect of any
  retirement savings plan or under any retirement plan, any amount paid
  under or in respect of the [Garnishment, Attachment and Pension
  Diversion Act](/eng/acts/G-2) or the [Pension Benefits Division
  Act](/eng/acts/P-6.7) and any other payment made in respect of
  disability to any person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} financial services required in order for a listed
  person to obtain legal services in Canada with respect to the
  application of any of the prohibitions set out in these Regulations;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} any transaction in respect of any account at a
  financial institution held by a diplomatic mission, if the transaction
  is required in order for the mission to fulfill its diplomatic
  functions as set out in Article 3 of the Vienna Convention on
  Diplomatic Relations or, if the diplomatic mission has been
  temporarily or permanently recalled, when the transaction is required
  in order to maintain the mission premises;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} any transaction with any international organization
  with diplomatic status, with any United Nations agency, with the
  International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement or with any entity
  that has entered into a grant or contribution agreement with the
  Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a transaction by the Government of Canada that is
  provided for in any agreement or arrangement between Canada and
  Belarus.

  defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies

  Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[provincial
  companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
  [[societies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms are defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fraternal benefit societies regulated by a provincial
  Act in respect of their insurance activities and insurance companies
  and other entities regulated by a provincial Act that are engaged in
  the business of insuring risks;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} companies regulated by the [Trust and Loan Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} trust companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} loan companies regulated by a provincial Act;

  paragraph 5(h) of the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and
  Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501) if the business involves
  the opening of an account for a client; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} entities authorized under provincial legislation to
  engage in the business of dealing in securities or to provide
  portfolio management or investment counselling services.

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every

  out in section 6 must disclose without delay to the Commissioner of

  Security Intelligence Service

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the existence of property in their possession or
    control that they have reason to believe is owned, held or

  Act](/eng/acts/S-14.5) and no civil proceedings lie against a person

  from Schedule 1.

  decide whether there are reasonable grounds to recommend to the
  Governor in Council that the applicant's name be removed from Schedule
  1.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is not so established, provide notice to the
    applicant of his or her determination.
