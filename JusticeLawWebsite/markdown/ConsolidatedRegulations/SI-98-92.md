## Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Seepage Lake, N.W.T.) {#reservation-to-the-crown-waiver-order-seepage-lake-n.w.t. .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/98-92
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 1998-09-16

Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Seepage Lake, N.W.T.)

P.C. 1998-1525  1998-08-26
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 13(b) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), is
pleased hereby to order that any portion of the lands described in the
schedule hereto lying within 30.48 metres (100 feet) of the ordinary
high water mark of Seepage Lake within Lot 1047 of the Town of Norman
Wells in the Northwest Territories be included in the grant of the said
lands.
:::

::::: section
:::: {#653580 .Schedule}
::: {}
## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-653581 .scheduleLabel}
:::

All that parcel of land, within Lot 1047 in Quad 96 E/07 in the Town of
Norman Wells in the Northwest Territories, lying within 30.48 metres
(100 feet) of the ordinary high water mark around Seepage Lake as said
parcel is shown in pink on a sketch plan on file 33-1-10 in the Land
Management Division of the Department of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development at Ottawa.
