## Ontario Fresh Potato Order {#ontario-fresh-potato-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-553
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1987-09-10

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Fresh Potatoes Produced in Ontario

P.C. 1987-1875 1987-09-10
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to section 2[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Agricultural Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed [Order granting authority to regulate the marketing
in interprovincial and export trade of fresh potatoes produced in
Ontario](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-553).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-907613 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Fresh Potato Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-553).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-907615 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Farm Products Marketing Act of
    the Province of Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing
    Board of Ontario, established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Fresh
    Potato Growers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fresh potatoes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fresh potatoes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means potatoes produced in
    Ontario, other than potatoes that are processed or potatoes that are
    sold as certified seed; ([pommes de terre fraîches]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    fresh potatoes, established and amended from time to time pursuant
    to the Act, and any regulations made under the Act to give effect to
    the plan; ([plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} canning, dehydrating, chipping, drying, freezing
      or processing with any chemical or by heat and combining or mixing
      potatoes with one or more other vegetables, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entering into a contract for the purchase of
      potatoes for the purpose of performing on the potatoes any of the
      operations mentioned in paragraph (a).
      ([transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-907625 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Board and the Commodity
Board are each authorized to regulate the marketing of fresh potatoes in
interprovincial and export trade and for that purpose may, with respect
to persons and property situated within the Province of Ontario,
exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable by each of them
respectively in relation to the marketing of fresh potatoes locally
within the Province under the Act and the Plan.

## [Levies or Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-907627 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board may, in
relation to the powers granted to it by section 3 with respect to the
marketing of fresh potatoes in interprovincial and export trade,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix and impose, by order, and collect levies or
  charges from persons situated in the Province of Ontario who are
  engaged in the production or marketing of fresh potatoes and for that
  purpose, classify those persons into groups and fix, by order, the
  levies or charges payable by the members of the different groups in
  different amounts; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the levies or charges collected pursuant to
  paragraph (a) for the purposes of the Commodity Board, including the
  creation of reserves, the payment of expenses and losses resulting
  from the sale or disposal of fresh potatoes and the equalization or
  adjustment among producers of fresh potatoes of moneys realized from
  the sale of fresh potatoes during such period or periods of time as
  the Commodity Board may determine.
