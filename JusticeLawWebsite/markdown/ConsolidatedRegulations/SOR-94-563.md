## ICAO Privileges and Immunities Order {#icao-privileges-and-immunities-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-563
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Registration 1994-08-16

Order Respecting the Privileges and Immunities in Canada of the
International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO)

P.C. 1994-1364  1994-08-16
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Secretary of State for External Affairs and the Minister of Finance,
pursuant to section 5 of the [Foreign Missions and International
Organizations Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-94-563_e_hq_6301){#fn_SOR-94-563_e_hq_6301-ID0EFBAA},
is pleased hereby to repeal the International Civil Aviation
Organization Privileges and Immunities Order, C.R.C., c. 1313, and to
make the annexed [Order respecting the privileges and immunities in
Canada of the International Civil Aviation Organization
(ICAO)](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-563), in substitution therefor.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-94-563_e_hq_6301-ID0EFBAA){#fn_SOR-94-563_e_hq_6301}S.C.
  1991, c. 41
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964545 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [ICAO Privileges and Immunities Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-563).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964547 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[accredited ICAO mission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[accredited ICAO mission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permanent
    mission of a foreign state that is accredited to ICAO in Canada.
    ([mission accréditée auprès de l'OACI]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Headquarters Agreement
    between the Government of Canada and the International Civil
    Aviation Organization, signed at Calgary and Montreal on October 4
    and 9, 1990 and in force since February 20, 1992;
    ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations;
    ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[ICAO]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ICAO]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Civil Aviation
    Organization; ([OACI]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Vienna Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Vienna Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Vienna
    Convention on Diplomatic Relations. ([Convention de
    Vienne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-326, s. 1
:::

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964556 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} ICAO
  shall have in Canada the legal capacities of a body corporate and, to
  the extent specified in Articles 2, 3, 4(1) to (3), 5, 6, 8 and 9 of
  the Agreement, the privileges and immunities set out in Articles II
  and III of the Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Permanent representatives of foreign states that are
  members of ICAO and members of their families forming part of their
  households shall have in Canada, to the extent specified in Articles
  12, 15 and 23 of the Agreement, privileges and immunities comparable
  to the privileges and immunities accorded in Canada to diplomatic
  representatives, and members of their families forming part of their
  households, under the Vienna Convention.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Other representatives of foreign states that are
  members of ICAO shall have in Canada, to the extent specified in
  Articles 13(a) to (f), 16 and 23 of the Agreement, the privileges and
  immunities accorded in Canada to diplomatic representatives under the
  Vienna Convention.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Members of the administrative and technical staff,
  and members of their families forming part of their households, and
  the service staff of the mission of a member state of ICAO, other than
  persons who are Canadian citizens or permanent residents of Canada,
  shall have in Canada, to the extent specified in Articles 14 and 17 of
  the Agreement, privileges and immunities comparable to the privileges
  and immunities accorded in Canada to such persons under the Vienna
  Convention.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The President of the Council and the Secretary
  General, the Deputy Secretary General and the Assistant Secretaries
  General of ICAO, officers of equivalent ranks, and officials belonging
  to senior categories occupying posts at the P-4 level or higher, and
  members of their families forming part of their households, shall have
  in Canada, to the extent specified in Articles 19, 21, 24 and 25 of
  the Agreement, privileges and immunities accorded in Canada to
  diplomatic agents, and members of their families forming part of their
  households, under the Vienna Convention.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Officials of ICAO other than those referred to in
  subsection (5) shall have in Canada, to the extent specified in
  Articles 20, 21, 24 and 25 of the Agreement, the privileges and
  immunities set out in section 18 of Article V of the Convention.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-110, s. 5
:::

## [Privileges and Immunities --- Accredited ICAO Missions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964566 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Accredited ICAO missions
shall have, in Canada, the full extent of privileges and immunities
accorded, under the Vienna Convention, to diplomatic missions of foreign
states in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-326, s. 2
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Accredited ICAO missions
shall have, in Canada, with respect to the period beginning on January
1, 1991 and ending on December 31, 2000, those tax relief privileges in
relation to Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) that are
accorded, under the Vienna Convention, to diplomatic missions of foreign
states in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-326, s. 2
:::
