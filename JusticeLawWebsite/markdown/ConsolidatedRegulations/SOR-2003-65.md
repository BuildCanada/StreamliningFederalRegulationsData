## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707811 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank
      Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[balance sheet value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[balance sheet value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of the
      shares and ownership interests held by an entity, means the value
      reported on the entity's balance sheet on an unconsolidated
      basis. ([valeur au bilan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[book value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[book value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2008-161, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[minority interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[minority interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an equity
      interest, in an entity that is controlled by a foreign bank, that
      is held by a person other than

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the foreign bank; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity controlled by the foreign
        bank. ([participation minoritaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulatory capital

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, subject to subsection (3), the
  regulatory capital of a foreign bank, at any time, is the amount
  determined by the formula

  ::: {#707823 .Subsection}
  A - B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the total of the amounts of the shareholders' equity, minority
      interests and subordinated indebtedness that are reported in the
      financial statements of the foreign bank; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of goodwill that is included in the financial
      statements of the foreign bank.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction relating to security

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In calculating the amount of regulatory capital under
  subsection (2), an amount may be included in respect of a security
  only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the security is, by its terms, subordinate in right
    of payment to all liabilities of the entity that issued the security
    other than liabilities that, by their terms, rank equally with, or
    are subordinate to, that security;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the security is issued and fully paid up; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of subordinated indebtedness or a
    preferred share, the security

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has an initial minimum term of five years or more
      or has no term, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} cannot be redeemed or purchased for cancellation
      in the first five years after it is issued.

## [Investments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707836 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed activity

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 522.08(1)(f) of the Act and subject to
  subsections (2) to (4), a prescribed activity in relation to a
  Canadian entity is developing, designing, holding, managing,
  manufacturing, selling or otherwise dealing with any data transmission
  system, information site, communication device or information platform
  or portal that is used to provide information services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit on size of investment --- foreign
  bank

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A foreign bank may not acquire or hold control of, or
  a substantial investment in, a Canadian entity engaging in an activity
  described in subsection (1) if the sum of the following exceeds the
  lesser of 5% of the foreign bank's regulatory capital and \$1 billion:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate balance sheet value of the shares and
    ownership interests that the foreign bank would acquire in the
    Canadian entity under paragraph 522.08(1)(f) of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate balance sheet value of the shares and
    ownership interests held, whether individually or jointly, by the
    foreign bank and the entities associated with the foreign bank in
    Canadian entities engaging in an activity described in
    subsection (1) that the foreign bank or any entity associated with
    the foreign bank holds control of, or a substantial investment in,
    under paragraph 522.08(1)(f) of the Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate value of outstanding loans made by
    the foreign bank and the entities associated with the foreign bank,
    whether individually or jointly, to Canadian entities engaging in an
    activity described in subsection (1) that the foreign bank or any
    entity associated with the foreign bank holds control of, or a
    substantial investment in, under paragraph 522.08(1)(f) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit on size of investment --- entity
  associated with a foreign bank

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An entity associated with a foreign bank may not
  acquire or hold control of, or a substantial investment in, a Canadian
  entity engaging in an activity described in subsection (1) if the sum
  of the following exceeds the lesser of 5% of the foreign bank's
  regulatory capital and \$1 billion:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate balance sheet value of the shares and
    ownership interests that the entity associated with the foreign bank
    would acquire in the Canadian entity under paragraph 522.08(1)(f) of
    the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate balance sheet value of the shares and
    ownership interests held, whether individually or jointly, by the
    entity associated with the foreign bank, the foreign bank and any
    other entity associated with the foreign bank in Canadian entities
    engaging in an activity described in subsection (1) that the entity
    associated with the foreign bank, the foreign bank or any other
    entity associated with the foreign bank holds control of, or a
    substantial investment in, under paragraph 522.08(1)(f) of the Act;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate value of outstanding loans made by
    the entity associated with the foreign bank, the foreign bank and
    any other entity associated with the foreign bank, whether
    individually or jointly, to Canadian entities engaging in an
    activity described in subsection (1) that the entity associated with
    the foreign bank, the foreign bank or any other entity associated
    with the foreign bank holds control of, or a substantial investment
    in, under paragraph 522.08(1)(f) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restricted activities

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A foreign bank or an entity associated with a foreign
  bank may not acquire or hold control of, or a substantial investment
  in, a Canadian entity engaging in an activity described in
  subsection (1) if the activities of the Canadian entity include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} activities that a bank is not permitted to engage
    in under any of sections 412, 417 and 418 of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} dealing in securities, except as may be permitted
    under paragraph 522.08(1)(e) of the Act or as may be permitted to a
    bank under paragraph 409(2)(c) of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} dealing in goods, wares or merchandise that a bank
    is not permitted to deal in under subsection 410(2) of the Act,
    other than as permitted under subsection (1);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} activities that a bank is not permitted to engage
    in under section 416 of the Act if the Canadian entity engages in
    the activities of a finance entity or of any other entity prescribed
    under paragraph 522.08(2)(c) of the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} acquiring or holding control of, or a substantial
    investment in, another Canadian entity unless

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a Canadian entity that is
      controlled by the foreign bank or the entity associated with a
      foreign bank, the foreign bank or the entity associated with a
      foreign bank itself would be permitted to acquire or hold control
      of, or a substantial investment in, the other Canadian entity
      under section 522.07, 522.08 or 522.1 or Division 8 of Part XII of
      the Act, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a Canadian entity that is not
      controlled by the foreign bank or the entity associated with a
      foreign bank, the foreign bank or the entity associated with a
      foreign bank itself would be permitted to acquire or hold control
      of, or a substantial investment in, the other Canadian entity
      under section 522.07 or 522.08, any of paragraphs 522.1(a) or (c)
      to (e) or Division 8 of Part XII of the Act; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any activity prescribed under paragraph
    522.08(2)(e) of the Act.

## [Exemption from Restrictions on Investments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707862 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from restrictions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraph 2(4)(e)(ii), subsection 522.22(1) of the Act does not apply
in determining whether a foreign bank or an entity associated with a
foreign bank would be permitted to acquire or hold control of, or a
substantial investment in, a Canadian entity under section 522.07 or
522.08, any of paragraphs 522.1(a) or (c) to (e) or Division 8 of Part
XII of the Act.

## [Non-application of Subsection 522.22(1) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707867 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 522.22(1) of the
Act does not apply where, under paragraph 522.08(1)(f) of the Act, a
foreign bank acquires or holds control of, or a substantial investment
in, a Canadian entity whose business is limited to activities described
in subsection 2(1). Nothing in this section limits the operation of
subsections 2(2) and (4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 522.22(1) of the
Act does not apply where, under paragraph 522.08(1)(f) of the Act, an
entity associated with a foreign bank acquires or holds control of, or a
substantial investment in, a Canadian entity whose business is limited
to activities described in subsection 2(1). Nothing in this section
limits the operation of subsections 2(3) and (4).
