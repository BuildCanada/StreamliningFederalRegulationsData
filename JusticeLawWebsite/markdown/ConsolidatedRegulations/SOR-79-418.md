## Ontario Pork Producers' Marketing Order {#ontario-pork-producers-marketing-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/79-418
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1979-05-22

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing of Hogs in Ontario

P.C. 1979-1476 1979-05-17
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to section 2 of the [Agricultural
Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), is pleased hereby to revoke the
Ontario Hog Producers' Marketing Order made by Order in Council
P.C. 1967-1033 of 25th May, 1967[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_SOR-79-418_e_hq_10330){#fn_SOR-79-418_e_hq_10330-ID0EDBAA},
and to make the annexed [Order granting authority to regulate the
marketing of hogs in Ontario](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-418) in
substitution thereof.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865839 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Pork Producers' Marketing
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-418).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing
    Board of Ontario, established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Régie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Pork
    Producers' Marketing Board, established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hogs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hogs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means hogs produced in Ontario;
    ([porcs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    hogs, established and amended from time to time pursuant to the Act,
    and any regulations made pursuant to the Act to give effect to the
    Plan. ([plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865848 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board and the
Board are each authorized to regulate the marketing of hogs in
interprovincial and export trade and for such purposes may, with respect
to persons and property situated in the Province of Ontario, exercise
all or any powers like the powers exercisable by each of them in
relation to the marketing of hogs locally in that Province under the Act
and the Plan.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-240, s. 1
:::

## [Levies or Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865852 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board may, in
relation to the powers granted to it by section 3 with respect to the
marketing of hogs in interprovincial and export trade,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix and impose by order and collect levies or charges
  from persons situated in the Province of Ontario who are engaged in
  the production or marketing of hogs and for that purpose classify
  those persons into groups and, by order, fix the levies or charges
  payable by the members of the different groups in different amounts;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the levies or charges collected pursuant to
  paragraph (a) for the purposes of the Commodity Board, including the
  creation of reserves, and the payment of expenses and losses resulting
  from the sale or disposal of hogs, and the equalization or adjustment
  among producers of hogs of moneys realized from the sale of hogs
  during such period or periods of time as the Commodity Board may
  determine.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-240, s. 2
