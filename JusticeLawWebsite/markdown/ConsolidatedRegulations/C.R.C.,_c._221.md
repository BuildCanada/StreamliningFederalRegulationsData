## Ontario Turkey Marketing Levies Order {#ontario-turkey-marketing-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 221
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Levies from
Certain Turkey Producers in Ontario
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536895 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Turkey Marketing Levies
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._221).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536897 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Turkey
    Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act; ([Office
    de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[market allotment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[market allotment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the total quantity of
    turkey meat that a producer is entitled to market in
    intraprovincial, interprovincial and export trade pursuant to the
    Canadian Turkey Marketing Quota Regulations and the Orders made by
    the Commodity Board pursuant to the Plan; ([allocation de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Turkey Producers'
    Marketing Plan established and amended from time to time pursuant to
    the Act and any regulations made thereunder to give effect to the
    Plan; ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person engaged in the
    production of turkeys in the Province of Ontario;
    ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a turkey or any class or part
    thereof produced or grown in the Province of Ontario.
    ([dindon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536905 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay to
the Commodity Board, in addition to the licence fees set out under the
Plan, levies at the rate of \$0.05 per pound live weight on every turkey
sold by him that is in excess of his market allotment.

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536907 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who buys turkeys from a producer shall deduct from the moneys
  payable to the producer for the turkeys all levies payable by the
  producer to the Commodity Board under section 3 and shall pay such
  levies to the Commodity Board at its office, 29 Wellington Street
  South, St. Marys, Ontario, not later than the 15th day of the month
  next following the month in which the levies were deducted.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every producer shall pay to the Commodity Board at
  its office, 29 Wellington Street South, St. Marys, Ontario, all levies
  payable by him under section 3 that were not deducted and paid to the
  Commodity Board in the manner prescribed in subsection (1), not later
  than the 15th day of the month next following the month in which the
  turkeys in respect of which the levies are payable were sold.
