## Public Lands Oil and Gas Regulations {#public-lands-oil-and-gas-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1326
:::

[FEDERAL REAL PROPERTY AND FEDERAL IMMOVABLES ACT](/eng/acts/F-8.4)

Regulations Respecting the Leasing of Oil and Gas Rights Located on
Certain Public Lands
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503472 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Public Lands Oil and Gas
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1326).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503474 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Director, Resource
    Management and Conservation Branch, Department of Energy, Mines and
    Resources; ([directeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[gas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all natural gas including
    casinghead gas and all hydrocarbons not defined as oil herein;
    ([gaz]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[lease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an oil and gas lease described
    in section 3; ([bail]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Energy, Mines
    and Resources; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means crude petroleum oil and all other
    hydrocarbons, regardless of gravity, that are produced at a well in
    liquid form by customary production methods;
    ([pétrole]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance recovered through
    or by the customary means of production of oil and gas;
    ([produit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[spacing unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[spacing unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area designated by the
    appropriate provincial authority for drilling or production purposes
    for oil or gas; ([unité d'espacement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unit operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unit operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means exploration and
    development on areas included within a unit agreement entered into
    pursuant to section 6; ([exploitation unitaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any stratum or sequence of strata
    that is identified by the Director as a zone.
    ([zone]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Leases]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503485 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to these
Regulations, the Minister may, by way of lease, grant to any person the
exclusive right to search for and take oil and gas, the property of Her
Majesty in right of Canada, within and under lands in the Provinces of
British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Ontario and Quebec.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), no lease shall be granted unless the Minister has
  called for tenders and the grantee is the person making the highest
  tender.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, without calling for tenders, grant
  a lease of natural gas rights for zones in lands that are required

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to complete a spacing unit; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a unit operation.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A lease granted under these
Regulations shall be in such form as the Minister may approve.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** In order to conserve oil
and gas resources and to secure the orderly and regular development and
the most efficient, economic recovery thereof, the Minister may from
time to time enter into an agreement with persons entitled to search for
and take oil and gas for the consolidation, merger or combination of
their interests, whether such purpose is accomplished by unit
operations, cooperative development or joint participation.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The fee for a service
described in Column I of an item of the schedule shall be that set out
in Column II of that item.
