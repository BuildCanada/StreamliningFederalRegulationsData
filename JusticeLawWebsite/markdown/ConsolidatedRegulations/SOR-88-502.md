## Nordion International Inc./Theratronics International Limited Shares Order, 1988 {#nordion-international-inc.theratronics-international-limited-shares-order-1988 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-502
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1988-09-23

Order Respecting the Sale or other Disposition of the Shares of Nordion
International Inc. and of Theratronics International Limited by Atomic
Energy of Canada Limited and the Acquisition of those Shares by Canada
Development Investment Corporation

P.C. 1988-2232 1988-09-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council, pursuant to subsection 101(6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBBA} of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is satisfied that Atomic Energy of
Canada Limited and Canada Development Investment Corporation are
empowered to undertake the annexed transactions;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBBA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1984, c. 31, s. 11
  :::

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, the Minister of
Energy, Mines and Resources and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
paragraphs 101(1)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EJAAA} and (d)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EHAAA} and 108(2)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFAAA} of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Order respecting the sale or other disposition of the shares of
Nordion International Inc. and of Theratronics International Limited by
Atomic Energy of Canada Limited and the acquisition of those shares by
Canada Development Investment Corporation](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-502).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913646 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Nordion International Inc./Theratronics International Limited
Shares Order, 1988](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-502).

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913648 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Atomic Energy of Canada
Limited is hereby authorized to sell or otherwise dispose of all of the
common shares of Nordion International Inc. and of Theratronics
International Limited to Canada Development Investment Corporation.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Canada Development
Investment Corporation is hereby authorized to acquire from Atomic
Energy of Canada Limited all of the common shares of Nordion
International Inc. and of Theratronics International Limited.
