## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003460 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [National Energy Board Electricity
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-130).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003462 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [National Energy Board
    Act](/eng/acts/N-7); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[adjustment transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[adjustment transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a transfer of
    power or energy to adjust energy account balances or to compensate
    for services rendered; ([transfert en vue d'un
    redressement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[border accommodation transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[border accommodation transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    transfer of power or energy for the purpose of providing electricity
    to

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person in the United States who lacks ready
      access to services from a power system in that country,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a work that is located in part in Canada and in
      part in the United States, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person in the United States who has lost
      service from a power system in that country as a result of an
      emergency; ([transfert en vue d'un service
      frontalier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[carrier transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[carrier transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a transfer of power
    or energy wheeled from one power system, through the circuits of
    another power system that acts as a carrier, for delivery to a third
    party or to the original power system; ([transfert relatif au
    transport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[electricity transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[electricity transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a transfer of any
    of the following classes, namely

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a sale transfer,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an equichange transfer,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an adjustment transfer,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a carrier transfer, or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} storage transfer; ([transfert
      d'électricité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[energy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[energy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the total quantity of energy in
    the form of electricity transmitted over a period of time, expressed
    in units of watt hours or multiples or sub-multiples of watt hours;
    ([énergie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[environmental effect]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[environmental effect]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
    project,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any change that the project may cause in the
      environment, including any effect of any such change on health and
      socio-economic conditions, on physical and cultural heritage, on
      the current use of lands and resources for traditional purposes by
      Aboriginal persons, or on any structure, site or thing that is of
      historical, archaeological, paleontological or architectural
      significance,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} repercussions on the environment of malfunctions
      or accidents that may occur and any cumulative repercussions on
      the environment that are likely to result from the project in
      combination with other projects or activities that have been or
      will be carried out, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any change to the project that may be caused by
      the environment; ([effets environnementaux]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[equichange transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[equichange transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an interchange of
    equal quantities of power or energy within a stated period;
    ([transfert d'équivalents]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[firm energy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[firm energy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means energy that is intended to
    be available at all specified times during a period covered by an
    agreement respecting the sale thereof; ([énergie
    garantie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[firm power]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[firm power]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means power or power-production
    capacity that is intended to be available at all specified times
    during a period covered by an agreement respecting the sale thereof;
    ([puissance garantie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[interruptible energy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[interruptible energy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means energy that is
    made available under an agreement that permits curtailment,
    interruption or cessation of delivery at the option of the supplier;
    ([énergie interruptible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[interruptible power]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[interruptible power]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means power that is made
    available under an agreement that permits curtailment, interruption
    or cessation of delivery at the option of the supplier; ([puissance
    interruptible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[notice]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[notice]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a notice of the application for
    a permit, published by the applicant in accordance with section
    58.12 or 119.04 of the Act; ([avis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an authorization for

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the construction and operation of an
      international power line issued under Part III.1 of the Act, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exportation of electricity issued under Part
      VI of the Act; ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[power]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[power]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rate of transferring energy,
    expressed in units of watts or multiples or sub-multiples of watts;
    ([puissance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[power line outside Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[power line outside Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that part of
    a power line in the United States that is between its connection to
    the international power line at the border and the first switching
    station in the United States; ([ligne située à
    l'étranger]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[power system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[power system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the generating
    stations, transformers, switching stations, transmission lines,
    substations, distribution lines and circuits necessary for the
    production, transmission and distribution of electricity; ([réseau
    d'électricité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[power transfer capability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[power transfer capability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount
    of power that can be transferred from one power system to another
    without impairing the reliability criteria of the interconnected
    systems; ([capacité de transfert de puissance]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[sale transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sale transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a transfer of power or
    energy under a contract of sale; ([transfert relatif à la
    vente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[storage transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[storage transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a transfer of energy
    that is banked for the time being in the form of water in a
    reservoir of another power system, in the expectation that
    equivalent energy will be returned at a later time. ([transfert en
    vue du stockage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- SOR/99-338, s. 1(F)

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Inspection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003499 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member
  of the Board or any person who has the relevant training or experience
  and who is authorized by the Board in writing for the purpose may,
  with respect to any licence or permit issued under Division II of Part
  VI of the Act, inspect any instruments, devices, plant, equipment,
  books, records or accounts or any other thing used for or in
  connection with the exportation of electricity, and conduct any tests
  that are necessary to conduct the inspection.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person authorized by the Board to exercise any of
  the powers referred to in subsection (1) shall produce the
  authorization when requested to do so during the exercise of those
  powers.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is the operator or is in charge of
  any of the premises or things referred to in subsection (1) shall
  permit a member of the Board or a person authorized by the Board to
  exercise the powers referred to in that subsection and shall assist
  the member or person in exercising those powers.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Information to Be Furnished by Applicants for Permits for the Construction and Operation of International Power Lines]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003504 .Part}

### [International Power Lines of Less than 50 kV]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1003505 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application for a permit
for the construction and operation of an international power line that
does not exceed an operating voltage of 50 kV shall contain the
following information, unless the Board advises the applicant that the
information is already in the possession of the Board or that the
information is not relevant to the application:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the applicant and any authorized
  representative of the applicant and their mailing address, address for
  personal service, telephone number and any other telecommunications
  numbers of the applicant or the authorized representative of the
  applicant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the owner and of the operator
  of the international power line in Canada, if the identity of the
  owner or operator is different from that of the applicant;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a proof of publication of the notice;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of any early public notification
  process implemented by the applicant;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a map, on a scale sufficient to locate and identify
  all essential features, showing

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all terminal points, the general route, the
    international boundary crossover point and the distance in
    kilometres from the international boundary crossover point to each
    terminal point of the international power line in and outside
    Canada,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the provinces, cities, towns, villages, park
    boundaries, rivers, major roads, railways and navigable waters
    through, under or across which the international power line is to
    pass, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the power line outside Canada;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a plan of survey from which the international
  boundary crossover point can be accurately determined on the ground;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the owner and the operator of
  the power line outside Canada;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a brief engineering description of the international
  power line, including:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the voltage level,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the number and size of conductors,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the maximum power transfer capability, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a single-line diagram identifying all the
    facilities that constitute the international power line;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any agreement between the applicant and the
  owner or the operator of the power line outside Canada dealing with
  the construction and operation of the international power line and the
  power line outside Canada;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} in respect of environmental concerns,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} evidence to demonstrate that a screening is not
    required under the [Canadian Environmental Assessment
    Act](/eng/acts/C-15.2) because the international power line is of a
    project or class that is listed in the [Exclusion List
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-639), or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the environmental effects,
    including a consideration of the following factors, namely,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the significance of the environmental effects,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} comments received from the public, and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} measures that are technically and economically
      feasible and that would mitigate any significant adverse
      environmental effects;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} a description of the approvals that are required to
  be obtained from all of the provinces through which the international
  power line will pass and a statement respecting the current status of
  the approvals; and

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} a description of any approvals that are required for
  the construction and operation of the power line outside Canada and a
  statement respecting the current status of the approvals.

### [International Power Lines Greater than 50 kV]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1003531 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An application for a permit
for the construction and operation of an international power line that
exceeds an operating voltage of 50 kV shall contain the following
information, unless the Board advises the applicant that the information
is already in the possession of the Board or that the information is not
relevant to the application:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the applicant and of any authorized
  representative of the applicant and their mailing address, address for
  personal service, telephone number and any other telecommunications
  numbers of the applicant or the authorized representative of the
  applicant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the owner and the operator of the
  international power line,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their names and addresses, where they are not the
    applicant,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the power systems that each owns
    or operates, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a copy of their most recent annual report;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a proof of publication of the notice;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of any early public notification
  process implemented by the applicant;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a map on a scale sufficient to locate and identify

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the general route and facility sites being
    considered,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the alternative route and facility sites under
    consideration,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the areas subject to physical and environmental
    constraints, including biophysical and land use or natural resource
    use constraints, that limit the general route or facility sites, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the approximate sites of all proposed ancillary
    facilities;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the environmental, land-use and
  other criteria used for the identification of the proposed, and any
  alternative, general route and major facility sites;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} all terminal points and the international boundary
  crossover point;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the distance in kilometres from the international
  boundary crossover point to each terminal point;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a map, on a scale sufficient to locate and identify
  all essential features, showing

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all terminal points, the international boundary
    crossover point and the distance in kilometres from the
    international boundary crossover point to each terminal point of the
    international power line in and outside Canada,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the provinces, cities, towns, villages, park
    boundaries, rivers, major roads, railways and navigable waters
    through, under or across which the international power line is to
    pass, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the power line outside Canada;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} a plan of survey from which the international
  boundary crossover point can be accurately determined on the ground;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the owner and of the operator
  of the power line outside Canada;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the most recent annual report of the owner
  and the operator of the power line outside Canada;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} a brief engineering description of the proposed
  international power line, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the voltage level,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the number and size of conductors,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the tower or other structures
    that will provide physical support for the international power line,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a single-line diagram identifying all the
    facilities that constitute the international power line,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the power transfer capability for sustained
    transmission of power under winter and summer conditions, and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the criteria for the stated power transfer
    capability;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} the total export and import power transfer
  capabilities, with and without the proposed international power line,
  of the applicant's power system and of the power system to which it
  will be interconnected by the international power line, stating the
  criteria for those capabilities;

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} a copy of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} each interconnection agreement that relates to the
    construction of the international power line, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any other agreement between the applicant and the
    owner or the operator of the power line outside Canada that relates
    to the construction and operation of the international power line
    and the power line outside Canada;

- [(p)]{.lawlabel} a description of the provincial requirements and
  associated review process that must be satisfied, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the review process applicable to
    each approval that is required,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of any public consultation process
    provided for under the review process, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a schedule for the review process;

- [(q)]{.lawlabel} a description of the approvals that are required to
  be obtained, including a statement respecting the current status of
  the approvals,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} from all the provinces through which the
    international power line will pass, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} from the appropriate authorities for the
    construction or operation of the power line outside Canada;

- [(r)]{.lawlabel} a schedule showing the projected dates for

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} each approval referred to in subparagraph (q)(i),
    and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the start and completion of construction of the
    international power line and the power line outside Canada;

- [(s)]{.lawlabel} an environmental assessment report, which may consist
  of a screening report or a comprehensive study report done pursuant to
  the [Canadian Environmental Assessment Act](/eng/acts/C-15.2) or a
  report done pursuant to provincial legislation, for the construction
  and operation of the international power line and of any associated
  temporary or permanent roads;

- [(t)]{.lawlabel} unless otherwise detailed in the report referred to
  in paragraph (s),

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a map showing the proposed general route and
    covering a width of at least one kilometre on each side of the
    international power line, on a scale sufficient to clearly show the
    existing environment, including the surface geology, the habitats of
    wildlife of ecological, economic or human importance, rare and
    endangered plant species, spawning beds, public recreational areas,
    parks, historic and archaeological sites, conservation areas, Indian
    reserves and existing land use, and a description of the
    environmental components shown on the map,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the width of the right-of-way proposed and the
    reasons why that width was selected,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the environmental effects,
    including a consideration of the following factors, namely,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the significance of the environmental effects,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} comments received from the public, and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} measures that are technically and economically
      feasible and that would mitigate any significant adverse
      environmental effects,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the measures that would be taken to minimize any
    adverse visual effects of the international power line,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the levels of the radio and television interference
    expected at the edge of the right-of-way under fair and foul weather
    conditions at maximum loading of the international power line, and
    the measures to be taken to minimize the potential interference,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} for operating voltages above 240 kV, the levels of
    noise, ozone concentration, electric field gradient and magnetic
    field strength expected at the edge of the right-of-way at maximum
    loading of the international power line, and the measures to be
    taken to protect people and animals, from electric shock on
    contacting vehicles or metallic structures,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} for any substation that forms part of the
    international power line, the audible noise in decibels that would
    be caused by the operation of the facilities, a description of the
    public exposure to the noise and any measures to be taken to
    minimize the noise,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the pesticides and herbicides to be used in the
    construction and maintenance of the right-of-way, including

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} quantities,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} methods of application,

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} potential adverse environmental effects, and

    - [(D)]{.lawlabel} measures to be taken to mitigate any potential
      harmful effects,

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the plans for surface restoration after
    construction and for the disposal of excavation and construction
    debris and wastes, and

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the applicant's intentions with
    respect to environmental inspection of the project during
    construction and operation of the international power line;

- [(u)]{.lawlabel} for every comprehensive study report referred to in
  paragraph (s), in addition to the factors referred to in paragraph
  (t), a consideration of the following factors, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the project,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} alternative means of carrying out the project that
    are technically and economically feasible and the environmental
    effects of any such alternative means,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the need for, and the requirements of, any
    follow-up programme in respect of the project,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the capacity of the renewable resources that are
    likely to be significantly affected by the project to meet the needs
    of the present and those of the future;

- [(v)]{.lawlabel} a description of any adverse effects on other
  provinces that operation of the proposed international power line at
  the power transfer capabilities referred to in paragraph (n) may have,
  including adverse effects on the operation of power systems in other
  provinces and the measures to be taken to mitigate or minimize those
  effects; and

- [(w)]{.lawlabel} a description of any safety and environmental
  standards, practices and procedures to be used in the design,
  construction and operation of the international power line, including
  the date of issue of any documents respecting those matters.

### [Terms and Conditions of Permits for International Power Lines]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1003604 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The following are matters
in respect of which terms and conditions may be included in any permit
for the construction and operation of an international power line:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} notice of any change in the identity of the owner or
  operator of the facilities;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the location of the facilities;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the location of the international boundary crossover
  point;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the electrical and physical characteristics,
  including the power transfer capability, of the facilities;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} practices and procedures related to the protection
  and restoration of the environment affected by the facilities;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} requirements respecting monitoring of the
  construction, operation and environmental effects of the facilities;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} requirements respecting approval by the Board of any
  change that may be made to the facilities;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} requirements relating to the mitigation of any
  adverse effects that the operation of the facilities may have on the
  reliability of any power systems to which the facilities are
  interconnected; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} requirements relating to the obligation of persons
  who provide other persons with the necessary facilities for the export
  of power from Canada to verify whether persons who wish to export
  electricity have the required permits or licences for that export.

the holder of a permit or certificate shall be in the form set out in
the schedule.

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Information to Be Furnished by Applicants for Permits for the Exportation of Electricity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003617 .Part}

### [Border Accommodation Transfers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1003618 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An application for a permit
for the exportation of electricity for the purposes of a border
accommodation transfer shall contain the following information, unless
the Board advises the applicant that the information is already in the
possession of the Board or that the information is not relevant to the
application:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the names of the applicant and any authorized
  representative of the applicant and their mailing address, address for
  personal service, telephone number and any other telecommunications
  numbers of the applicant or the authorized representative of the
  applicant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a proof of publication of the notice;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the period for which the permit is sought and, for
  each year in that period, an estimate of the following quantities,
  namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the maximum quantity of firm power export,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the maximum quantity of interruptible power
    export,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the maximum monthly and annual quantities of firm
    energy exports, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the maximum monthly and annual quantities of
    interruptible energy exports;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any electricity transfer agreement that
  covers the proposed exportation of electricity;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the international power line over
  which the applicant proposes to export electricity, setting forth

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of the certificate or permit issued by
    the Board,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the holder of the certificate or
    permit,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the names of the owner and the operator of the
    power line outside Canada, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the voltage level of the international power line;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the approvals that are required to
  be obtained, including a statement respecting the current status of
  the approvals,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} from the provinces, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} from the appropriate authorities for the
    importation of electricity into the United States; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and nature of the business of each
  person or agency outside Canada to be supplied with electricity,
  together with a statement of the power to be supplied to each.

### [Other than Border Accommodation Transfers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1003637 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An application for a permit
for the exportation of electricity, other than for a border
accommodation transfer, shall contain the following information, unless
the Board advises the applicant that the information is already in the
possession of the Board or that the information is not relevant to the
application:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the names of the applicant and any authorized
  representative of the applicant and their mailing address, address for
  personal service, telephone number and any other telecommunications
  numbers of the applicant or the authorized representative of the
  applicant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the applicant's power system, a copy
  of the applicant's latest annual report and, if applicable, the
  applicant's most recent publicly available generation or development
  plan;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a proof of publication of the notice;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of each person or agency outside Canada to
  be supplied with electricity and the nature of the business carried on
  by the person or agency or, if that information is unknown at the time
  of the application, a brief description of the markets to be served;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a sale transfer, the period for which
  the permit is sought and, for each year in that period, an estimate of
  the following quantities, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the maximum quantity of firm power export and
    import,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the maximum quantity of combined firm power and
    interruptible power export and import,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the maximum monthly and annual quantities of firm
    energy exports and imports, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the maximum monthly and annual quantities of
    interruptible energy exports and imports;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an equichange transfer, storage
  transfer, adjustment transfer or carrier transfer, a statement of the
  annual quantities of energy for exportation and for importation for
  each class of transfer for the period for which the permit is sought;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any electricity transfer agreement that
  covers the proposed exportation of electricity;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} where no agreement exists, a statement of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the estimated maximum duration of specific exports
    and the basis for that estimation, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the period of time for which the permit is sought
    and the basis for the selection of that period of time;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a list of the international power lines over which
  the applicant proposes to export or import electricity, setting forth
  in respect of each line

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of the certificate or permit issued by
    the Board,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the holder of the certificate or
    permit,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the owner of the power line outside
    Canada,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the voltage level and operating designation of
    each circuit, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the maximum power transfer capability of each
    circuit and the basis for that limit;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the total simultaneous power transfer capability
  under normal operating conditions for all of the international power
  lines listed in accordance with paragraph (i) and the basis for that
  limit;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} a description of the approvals required for the
  importation of electricity into the United States, and a statement
  respecting the current status of the approvals;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} a description of the provincial approvals that are
  required to be obtained by the applicant, and a statement respecting
  the current status of the approvals;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} a description of the review process applicable to
  each provincial approval that must be obtained, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of any public consultation provided
    for under the review process, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a schedule for the review process;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} whether new or modified facilities will be required
  to effect the proposed exportation of electricity and, if applicable,
  a detailed description of those facilities;

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} the adverse environmental effects resulting from the
  proposed exportation of electricity, and the measures to be taken to
  mitigate any of those environmental effects;

- [(p)]{.lawlabel} a description of any adverse effects that the
  proposed exportation of electricity could have on the operation of any
  power system in neighbouring provinces;

- [(q)]{.lawlabel} where the application specifies the terms and
  conditions of the proposed exportation of electricity, a description
  detailing the manner in which the applicant

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has informed those persons who have declared an
    interest in buying electricity for consumption in Canada of the
    quantities and classes of service available for sale, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has given those persons who have demonstrated an
    intention to buy electricity for consumption in Canada after having
    been so informed, an opportunity to purchase electricity on terms
    and conditions, including price, as favourable as the terms and
    conditions specified in the application; and

- [(r)]{.lawlabel} where the application does not specify the terms and
  conditions of the proposed exportation of electricity a description,
  including supporting documentation, detailing the manner in which the
  applicant

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} will inform those persons who declare an interest
    in buying electricity for consumption in Canada of the quantities
    and classes available for sale, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} will give those persons who demonstrate an
    intention to buy electricity for consumption in Canada after having
    been so informed, an opportunity to purchase electricity on terms
    and conditions, including price, as favourable as the terms and
    conditions of the export.

### [Terms and Conditions of Permits for the Exportation of Electricity]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1003676 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The following are matters
in respect of which terms and conditions may be included in any permit
for the exportation of electricity:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the duration of the permit;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the maximum quantities of power and energy
  authorized;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the classes of electricity transfers authorized;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} requirements relating to the maximum duration of
  export contracts;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} requirements relating to the filing with the Board
  and prior approval by the Board of electricity transfer agreements, or
  any amendments to those agreements, that are entered into pursuant to
  the permit;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the qualification of each class of electricity
  transfer as firm power or interruptible power;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} conditions under which the permit holder must curtail
  or interrupt the exportation;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the international power lines over which electricity
  transfers are authorized;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} requirements relating to the measurement of power and
  energy for the purposes of the permit;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} any changes in circumstances about which the permit
  holder is required to inform the Board;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} requirements relating to the protection and
  restoration of the environment;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} requirements relating to the mitigation of adverse
  effects of the export on the reliability of the power systems; and

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} requirements relating to the opportunities for
  Canadians to purchase the electricity proposed to be exported from
  Canada.
