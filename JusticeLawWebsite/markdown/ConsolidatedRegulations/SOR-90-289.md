## ACOA Loan Insurance Regulations {#acoa-loan-insurance-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-289
:::

[ATLANTIC CANADA OPPORTUNITIES AGENCY ACT](/eng/acts/A-13.7)

Registration 1990-05-14

Regulations Respecting the Provision of Loan Insurance Under the
Atlantic Canada Opportunities Agency (Acoa) Loan Insurance Program
::::

::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Minister for the purposes of the Atlantic Canada Opportunities
Agency Act, pursuant to paragraphs 20(1)(b) and (2)(a) of the [Atlantic
Canada Opportunities Agency Act](/eng/acts/A-13.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBBFA} and with the
approval of the Minister of Finance, hereby makes the annexed
Regulations respecting the provision of loan insurance under the Action
Program in Atlantic Canada.

ELMER MACKAY

*Minister for the purposes of the Atlantic Canada Opportunities Agency
Act*

MICHAEL WILSON

*Minister of Finance*
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925174 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [ACOA Loan Insurance
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-289).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-283, s. 2
- SOR/95-470, s. 2
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925178 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual, partnership,
    cooperative or body corporate, or any trustee or legal
    representative thereof, that makes an application for loan insurance
    pursuant to section 3, but does not include the federal government,
    a provincial or municipal government or an agency thereof or a legal
    entity owned or controlled by that government or agency;
    ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[capital costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[capital costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
    project, the capital costs, not exceeding \$20,000,000, as
    determined in accordance with generally accepted accounting
    principles, that are necessary to carry out the project, other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the value of goodwill,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cost of motor vehicles that are not used
      exclusively on a project site or between two or more project
      sites, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the portion of the cost of any asset that exceeds
      its fair market value; ([coûts des
      immobilisations]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[claim]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[claim]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a claim by a lender to the
    Agency for a payment pursuant to a loan insurance agreement;
    ([réclamation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Fisheries Alternatives Program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Fisheries Alternatives Program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/95-470, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

[[formal demand]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[formal demand]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a demand made by a
    lender to an applicant for the repayment of the outstanding amount
    under a loan agreement; ([demande officielle]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bank, a credit union, a
    caisse populaire or any other cooperative credit society, a trust
    company, a loan company, an insurance company, a pension fund or a
    non-bank affiliate of a foreign bank as defined in subsection 303(1)
    of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), but does not include the
    federal government, a provincial or municipal government or any
    agency thereof; ([prêteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement entered
    into by a lender and an applicant whereby the lender makes a loan to
    the applicant to finance the applicant's project; ([convention de
    prêt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[loan insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[loan insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means loan insurance provided
    under these Regulations; ([assurance-prêt]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[loan insurance agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[loan insurance agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement
    entered into by the Agency and a lender whereby the Agency provides
    insurance to the lender under these Regulations; ([convention
    d'assurance-prêt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commercial activity in
    Atlantic Canada described in subsection 4(1).
    ([opérations]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-283, s. 3
- SOR/93-18, s. 1
- SOR/95-470, s. 3
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925195 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An applicant may make an
application, which shall be in writing, for loan insurance to the Agency
and shall provide therein a detailed description of the project and any
other information that the Agency considers necessary to evaluate the
application effectively.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-470, s. 4
:::

## [Projects]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925199 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No loan
  insurance shall be provided by the Agency in respect of a project
  unless that project consists of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the establishment, expansion or modernization of a
    commercial enterprise that carries out activities described in the
    schedule;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the establishment, expansion or modernization of a
    commercial enterprise that carries out activities that are not
    described in the schedule but represent a strategic investment for
    the economic benefit of Atlantic Canada; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the purchase of the assets of an existing
    commercial enterprise, where at the time of the application

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the existing commercial enterprise has ceased or
      is about to cease operating as a result of circumstances beyond
      the control of its present owner, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the purchase is an arm's length transaction and
      is by an applicant that is unrelated to the present owner or to
      the owner's immediate predecessor and has not been contrived
      solely for the purpose of making an application for loan insurance
      pursuant to section 3.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No loan insurance shall be provided in respect of
  projects relating to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the wholesale or retail trade industries, the real
    estate industry, government service industries and services of a
    personal or social nature; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} mergers, refinancing or acquisitions, except in
    respect of a project referred to in paragraph (1)(c).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-283, s. 4
- SOR/93-18, s. 2(F)
- SOR/95-470, s. 5
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agency may approve an application for loan insurance referred to in
  section 3 where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's project is located in Atlantic
    Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the loan is not less than \$100,000
    and its term not more than 15 years;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/95-470, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total amount of the loan is to be used to
    finance the project;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the loan is to be applied only to capital costs;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the lender acquires enforceable security for the
    repayment of the loan by way of a security interest in a form that
    is consistent with standard banking practice;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is unable to obtain a loan, or a loan
    on reasonable terms, without the loan insurance;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the project would not likely be carried out within
    the proposed time or scope or in the proposed location without the
    loan insurance;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the project or, where the project will form a part
    of the applicant's existing commercial enterprise, the project
    together with the existing enterprise is or will become commercially
    viable within reasonable bounds of risk; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the project would constitute a significant
    contribution to the economic benefit of Atlantic Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When an application for loan insurance is approved by
  the Agency, the Agency may enter into a loan insurance agreement with
  the lender.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-283, s. 5
- SOR/95-470, s. 6
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/95-470, s.
7\]]{.Repealed}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where a loan is made in
respect of the establishment, expansion or modernization of a commercial
enterprise designed to serve food or drink, no loan insurance shall be
provided unless that commercial enterprise carries out activities that
are described in the schedule under the heading "SECTOR 3: TOURISM".

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-470, s. 8
:::

## [Insurance Coverage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925231 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/95-470, s. 9

\]
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The amount of loan
insurance provided to a lender shall not exceed, on the date of a formal
demand, 90 per cent of the principal amount outstanding on the loan that
is owed by the applicant pursuant to a loan agreement.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-283, s. 6
- SOR/95-470, s. 10
:::

## [Insurance Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925237 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Every lender shall pay to
the Agency in advance an insurance fee that is calculated in accordance
with the following formulas:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fee payable on the date of the first
  disbursement,

  ::: {#925240 .Paragraph}
  A × B × 0.01
  :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fee payable on the date of each subsequent
  disbursement,

  ::: {#925243 .Paragraph}
  A × B × C/365 × 0.01, and
  :::

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fee payable on each anniversary date of the first
  disbursement until the earlier of the date of the formal demand and
  the date that the loan is repaid

  ::: {#925246 .Paragraph}
  A × D × 0.01

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the percentage of the loan that the Agency agrees to insure
      under the loan insurance agreement,

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the disbursement,

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of days remaining between the date of the
      disbursement and the next anniversary of the first disbursement
      under the loan agreement, and

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the loan that is outstanding under the loan
      agreement on the anniversary date of the first disbursement.
  :::

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-283, s. 6
- SOR/95-470, s. 11
:::

## [Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925254 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** No claim made under a
loan insurance agreement shall be paid until a formal demand has been
made by the lender to the applicant and the lender has provided evidence
to the Agency that the lender has taken all reasonable steps to protect
the lender's rights and to realize on the security for the loan made
under the loan agreement.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-283, s. 6
- SOR/93-18, s. 3
- SOR/95-470, s. 12
:::

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A claim by a lender shall
contain all of the information that is required by the Agency in order
to determine the amount that is payable.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-283, s. 6
- SOR/93-18, s. 3
- SOR/95-470, s. 12
:::

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  maximum amount payable by the Agency to a lender in respect of a claim
  shall be the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the percentage stipulated in the loan insurance
    agreement, of the loss incurred by the lender, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the percentage, stipulated in the loan insurance
    agreement, of the principal amount outstanding on the loan on the
    date of the formal demand.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(a),
  [[loss]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the total of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the principal amount of the loan that is
    outstanding and the amount of interest that is unpaid on the date of
    the formal demand,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reasonable legal fees, disbursements and other
    expenses paid by the lender to collect or to endeavour to collect
    the amounts referred to in paragraph (a) or any portion thereof,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} disbursements made by the lender to protect any
    security provided for the loan, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} interest on the amounts referred to in
    paragraphs (a) to (c) calculated beginning on the date of the formal
    demand until the date the Agency pays the lender's claim,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at the rate stipulated in the loan agreement,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} for a period not exceeding 180 days beginning
        on the date of the formal demand, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} for an additional period not exceeding
        180 days, where the Agency and the lender have agreed to wait
        for the expiration of that additional period before realizing on
        the security or any portion thereof, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at one-half the rate referred to in
      subparagraph (i) for any period after the period referred to in
      clause (i)(A) and, where applicable, the additional period
      referred to in clause (i)(B),

  less

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the total of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the net amount realized on the security provided
      for the loan, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any other amount recovered by the lender after
      the date of the formal demand that is in excess of all other
      indebtedness of the applicant to the lender.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-283, s. 6
- SOR/93-18, s. 4(E)
- SOR/95-470, s. 13
:::

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Agency shall not
consider any application for loan insurance that is received after
September 30, 1996.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-283, s. 6
- SOR/93-18, s. 5
- SOR/95-470, s. 14
:::

s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Sections 4 and 7)]{.OriginatingRef}[Commercial Activities]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-925285 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- [SECTOR 1:]{.lawlabel} PRIMARY INDUSTRIES

  - [1.1]{.lawlabel} Agriculture

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} specialized services to agriculture

  - [1.2]{.lawlabel} Fishing

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} aquaculture

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specialized services for fishing

  - [1.3]{.lawlabel} Logging and Forestry

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} harvesting operations

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specialized services to logging and forestry

  - [1.4]{.lawlabel} Mining

- [SECTOR 2:]{.lawlabel} MANUFACTURING INDUSTRIES

  - [2.1]{.lawlabel} Manufacturing

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all manufacturing operations in which goods,
      products, commodities or wares are created, fabricated, refined or
      made more marketable

  - [2.2]{.lawlabel} Processing

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} processing and packaging operations

- [SECTOR 3:]{.lawlabel} TOURISM

  - [3.1]{.lawlabel} Accommodation

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} hotels, motels, cabins, cottages and related
      services

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} campgrounds, hunting and fishing camps

  - [3.2]{.lawlabel} Attractions

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} tourism-related attractions and services, theme
      parks and similar operations

- [SECTOR 4:]{.lawlabel} OTHER SERVICE INDUSTRIES

  - [4.1]{.lawlabel} Services to business including repair and
    maintenance and services in support of local or regional development

  - [4.2]{.lawlabel} Commercial research and development

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-470, ss. 15, 16
