## [Prescribed Supervisory Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678917 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 435.1 of the [Cooperative Credit Associations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01), the following information or any component of
  that information is prescribed supervisory information in relation to
  an association:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any rating assigned by the Superintendent to the
    association to assess its financial condition and any other such
    rating that is substantially based on information obtained from the
    Superintendent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any stage of intervention assigned to the
    association on the basis of the principles set out in the Guide to
    Intervention for Federal Financial Institutions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any order made in respect of the association under
    subsection 409(3) of the [Cooperative Credit Associations
    Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01), any prudential agreement entered into by
    the association under section 438.1 of that Act or any direction
    issued to it under section 439 of that Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any report prepared by or at the request of the
    Superintendent or any recommendation made by the Superintendent as a
    result of an annual or special examination or other supervisory
    review of the association, including any related correspondence to
    or from the directors or officers of the association.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 435.1 of the [Cooperative
  Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01), the information
  prescribed by the following regulations is prescribed supervisory
  information in relation to an affiliate of an association:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Banks)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-59), if the affiliate is a
    bank to which the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) applies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Authorized Foreign
    Banks) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-58), if the affiliate
    is an authorized foreign bank to which the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) applies;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Insurance Companies)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-56), if the affiliate is a
    company, society, foreign company or provincial company to which the
    [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Trust and Loan
    Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-55), if the
    affiliate is a company to which the [Trust and Loan Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) applies;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Bank Holding
    Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-480), if the
    affiliate is a bank holding company to which the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) applies; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Insurance Holding
    Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-484), if the
    affiliate is an insurance holding company to which the [Insurance
    Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies.

## [Prohibited Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678933 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 and
4, an association shall not, directly or indirectly, disclose
information referred to in section 1.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-196, s. 13
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 2"){.PITLink}
:::

## [Limited Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678937 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An association may disclose
information referred to in section 1 to its affiliates or to its
directors, officers, employees, auditors, securities underwriters or
legal advisors, or to those of its affiliates, if the association
ensures that the information remains confidential.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-196, s. 13
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An association or any of
its affiliates may disclose information referred to in paragraph 1(1)(c)
if the association or affiliate considers the information to contain a
material fact or material change that is required by the securities laws
of the relevant jurisdiction to be disclosed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-196, s. 14(E)
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678944 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
