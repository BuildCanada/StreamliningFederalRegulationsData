## Order Transferring Certain Portions of the Public Service Commission to the Canada School of Public Service {#order-transferring-certain-portions-of-the-public-service-commission-to-the-canada-school-of-public-service .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2004-43
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2004-04-21

Order Transferring Certain Portions of the Public Service Commission to
the Canada School of Public Service

P.C. 2004-367  2004-03-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers from the Public Service Commission to the Canada School of
Public Service, effective April 1, 2004, the control and supervision of
those portions of the public service in the Public Service Commission
known as Language Training Canada, and Corporate Services (commonly
known as Integrated Services) within the Learning and Development
Programs Branch, except for those portions carring out the powers,
duties and functions of the Public Service Commission under paragraph
5(a) and sections 6[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EJEAA}, 8, 35[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_e){#fnb_e-ID0EHEAA}, 37[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fnc_e){#fnc_e-ID0EFEAA} and 41[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fnd_e){#fnd_e-ID0EDEAA} of the [Public Service
Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).
