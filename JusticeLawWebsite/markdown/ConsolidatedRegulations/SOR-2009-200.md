## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749660 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these regulations.

[[agricultural product originating in Iceland]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agricultural product originating in Iceland]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means an originating agricultural product that is entitled to the
    benefit of the Iceland Tariff, but is excluded from the benefit of
    the Norway Tariff and the Switzerland -- Liechtenstein Tariff.
    ([produit agricole originaire d'Islande]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[agricultural product originating in Norway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agricultural product originating in Norway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means an originating agricultural product that is entitled to the
    benefit of the Norway Tariff, but is excluded from the benefit of
    the Iceland Tariff and the Switzerland -- Liechtenstein Tariff.
    ([produit agricole originaire de Norvège]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

:   agricultural product originating in Switzerland or Liechtenstein
    means an originating agricultural product that is entitled to the
    benefit of the Switzerland -- Liechtenstein Tariff, but is excluded
    from the benefit of the Iceland Tariff and the Norway Tariff.
    ([produit agricole originaire de Suisse ou du
    Liechtenstein]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[originating product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[originating product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in the Canada -- EFTA Free Trade Agreement, signed January 26, 2008.
    ([produit originaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749666 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 24(1)(b) of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), and
subject to section 3, originating products exported from Iceland,
Norway, Switzerland or Liechtenstein are entitled to the benefit of the
Iceland Tariff, the Norway Tariff or the Switzerland -- Liechtenstein
Tariff if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the products are shipped to Canada without shipment
  through another country either

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on a through bill of lading, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} without a through bill of lading and the importer
    provides, when requested by an officer, documentary evidence that
    indicates the shipping route and all points of shipment and
    transhipment prior to the importation of the products; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the products are shipped to Canada through another
  country and the importer provides, when requested by an officer,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} documentary evidence that indicates the shipping
    route and all points of shipment and transhipment prior to the
    importation of the products, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the customs control documents that
    establish that the products remained under customs control while in
    that other country.
