## Approved Breath Analysis Instruments Order {#approved-breath-analysis-instruments-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/85-201
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 1985-11-27

Order Approving Certain Breath Analysis Instruments as Suitable for the
Purposes of Section 258 of the Criminal Code
::::

:::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Attorney General of Canada, pursuant to the definition [[approved
instrument]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 238(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EHBCA} of the
[Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), hereby revokes the Approved Instruments
Order, C.R.C., c. 428, and makes the annexed [Order approving certain
breath analysis instruments as suitable for the purposes of section 258
of the](/eng/regulations/SI-85-201) [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), in
substitution therefor, effective December 4, 1985.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e-ID0EHBCA){#footnote_e}S.C. 1985, c.
  19, s. 36
  :::

Ottawa, November 7, 1985

::: FlushRight
JOHN C. CROSBIE
:::

::: FlushRight
*Attorney General of Canada*
:::
:::::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639899 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Approved Breath Analysis Instruments
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-85-201).

## [Approved Instruments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639901 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following instruments,
each being an instrument of a kind that is designed to receive and make
an analysis of a sample of the breath of a person in order to measure
the concentration of alcohol in the blood of that person, are hereby
approved as suitable for the purposes of section 258 of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46):

- [(a) to (c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2012-237, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(d) to (g)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2013-107, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} Intoxilyzer ® 5000 C;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2012-237, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2013-107, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} BAC Datamaster C;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} Alco-Sensor IV-RBT IV;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2013-107, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} Alco-Sensor IV/RBT IV-K;

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} Alcotest 7110 MKIII Dual C;

- [(p)]{.lawlabel} Intoxilyzer® 8000 C;

- [(q)]{.lawlabel} DataMaster DMT-C;

- [(r)]{.lawlabel} Intox EC/IR II; and

- [(s)]{.lawlabel} Intoxilyzer® 9000.
