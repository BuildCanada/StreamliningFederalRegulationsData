## Gros Morne National Park of Canada Snowshoe Hare Regulations {#gros-morne-national-park-of-canada-snowshoe-hare-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2005-205
:::

[CANADA NATIONAL PARKS ACT](/eng/acts/N-14.01)

Registration 2005-06-28

Gros Morne National Park of Canada Snowshoe Hare Regulations

P.C. 2005-1271 2005-06-28
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to section 17 of the [Canada
National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Gros Morne National Park of Canada Snowshoe Hare
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-205).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717351 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada National Parks Act.
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Chief Executive Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chief Executive Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in subsection 2(1) of the [Parks Canada Agency
    Act](/eng/acts/P-0.4). ([directeur général]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a son or daughter of a person or
    an individual adopted in fact by a person.
    ([enfant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[community]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[community]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following
    communities in the Province of Newfoundland and Labrador, namely,
    Birchy Head, Cow Head, Curzon Village, Glenburnie, Neddy Harbour,
    Norris Point, Rocky Harbour, St. Paul's, Sally's Cove, Shoal Brook,
    Trout River, Winter House Brook and Woody Point.
    ([collectivité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who, at the
    time of their application for and receipt of a snare permit, is a
    permanent resident of a community and who

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} was, on August 13, 1973, at least 19 years of age
      and a permanent resident of the park or a community; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is the child of a person described in paragraph
      (a). ([admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Gros Morne National Park of
    Canada. ([parc]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[possession]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[possession]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 4(3) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).
    ([possession]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[season]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[season]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period for which snare
    permits are issued. ([saison]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[snare]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[snare]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the act of capturing or
    attempting to capture an animal using a snare. ([chasse au
    collet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[snare permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[snare permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit issued under
    section 8. ([permis de chasse au collet]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[snowshoe hare]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[snowshoe hare]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of the species
    [Lepus americanus]{.otherLang lang="la"} and includes any part of
    such an animal. ([lièvre d'Amérique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the superintendent of
    the park. ([directeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717367 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to Gros Morne National Park of Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of an inconsistency between these
  Regulations and any other regulation made under the Act, these
  Regulations prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Sections 5 and 6 do not
apply to the superintendent, a park warden, an enforcement officer or a
peace officer while they are performing functions related to the
management of the harvesting of snowshoe hare in the park or to any
other person authorized by the superintendent while they are conducting
scientific research in the park in respect of the snowshoe hare or the
harvesting activity.

## [Traditional Renewable Resource Harvesting Activity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717372 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The snaring of snowshoe
hare in the park is hereby specified to be a traditional renewable
resource harvesting activity.

## [General Prohibitions --- Snaring and Possession]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717374 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall snare
snowshoe hare in the park unless they are the holder of a snare permit.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No person shall possess
snowshoe hare in the park unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is the holder of a snare permit and the
  snowshoe hare was snared in the park; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person is the holder of a permit issued by the
  Province of Newfoundland and Labrador that entitles them to snare
  snowshoe hare outside the park, and the snowshoe hare was snared
  outside the park.

## [Snare Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717379 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717380 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An application for a snare
permit shall be submitted to the superintendent on the form provided by
the superintendent and must include the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and date of birth of the applicant and the
  address of their permanent residence; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} written evidence that the applicant is an eligible
  person.

### [Issuance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717384 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to any other provisions of these Regulations and any determinations
  made under sections 15 to 17, the superintendent shall issue a permit
  to snare snowshoe hare in the park to an eligible person on
  application by that person in accordance with section 7.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person is entitled to obtain a snare permit for a
  given season if they are already the holder of a valid or suspended
  snare permit for that season or if, during the 12 months preceding the
  submission of the application, a snare permit previously issued to
  them was revoked.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The permit holder shall sign the snare permit on
  receipt.

### [Contents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717391 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  snare permit shall contain the name and date of birth of the permit
  holder, the address of their permanent residence and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the transfer or assignment of the
    permit is prohibited;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the duties of the permit holder as
    set out in sections 12 to 14;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement that contravention of a condition of
    the permit constitutes an offence under subsection 24(3) of the Act;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any standard conditions that are specified by the
    superintendent, in accordance with sections 15 to 17, in respect of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the duration of the permit,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the areas in the park in which the permit holder
      may snare snowshoe hare,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} all or part of any of those areas, identified
      on the permit as areas in which the permit holder may snare
      snowshoe hare, in which the snaring of snowshoe hare is restricted
      for part of the duration of the permit,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the maximum number of snowshoe hare that may be
      snared and possessed by the permit holder in the season, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the method to be used by the permit holder to
      indicate their snares, snaring lines and trails.

    - [(vi) and (vii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2009-322, s.
      27\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may, when necessary to ensure the
  protection of snowshoe hare in the park and the preservation, control
  and management of the park, including the maintenance or restoration
  of its ecological integrity, include in all snare permits to be issued
  for a given season any supplementary conditions that may be required.

### [Expiry]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717407 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A snare permit expires on
the earliest of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the expiry date stated on the permit,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the permit is revoked, and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the permit holder is no longer a
  permanent resident of the park or a community.

## [Prohibitions --- Holders of Snare Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717412 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** No holder of a snare
permit shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} transfer or assign their snare permit;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use a snare other than one that is constructed of
  metallic wire;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} set a snare in a manner capable of capturing an
  animal other than a snowshoe hare;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph 12(e), set more snares than the
  maximum number of snowshoe hare that may be snared as set out in their
  snare permit;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} bait a snare with any bait other then naturally
  occurring flora;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} cut any flora with a trunk diameter that is greater
  than 7.5 cm measured at a height of 15 cm above the ground, for use in
  snaring snowshoe hare;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} cut or damage white pine ([Pinus strobus]{.otherLang
  lang="la"}), yellow birch [(Betula alleghaniensis)]{.otherLang
  lang="la"}, black ash ([Fraxinus nigra]{.otherLang lang="la"}) or red
  maple ([Acer rubrum]{.otherLang lang="la"});

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} leave a snare unattended for more than 48 hours;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} snare snowshoe hare in an area of the park that has
  been closed to the snaring of snowshoe hare by the superintendent
  under section 16; or

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} use snowshoe hare snared under their snare permit for
  any purpose other than for their own personal use --- including as
  gifts --- or the use of their household.

## [Duties of Holders of Snare Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717424 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The holder of a snare
permit shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} carry their snare permit when they are snaring or in
  possession of snowshoe hare in the park;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on request by the superintendent, a park warden, an
  enforcement officer or a peace officer, immediately produce for
  examination, as applicable, their snare permit, snares and any
  snowshoe hare in their possession;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} notify the superintendent, in writing as soon as
  possible, of any change in the address of their permanent residence;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} report, without delay, to a park warden, any
  accidental capture by the permit holder in any of their snares of an
  animal other than a snowshoe hare if that animal is wounded or dead;
  and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} after they snare one half of the limit of snowshoe
  hare specified in their permit, reduce the number of their snares so
  that the number of their snares does not at any subsequent time exceed
  the remainder of that limit.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-322, s. 28(F)
:::

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder of a snare permit shall remove their snares, snaring line
  indicators and trail indicators when the limit of snowshoe hare
  specified in the snare permit has been reached or, if that limit is
  not reached, before the expiry of the permit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the permit holder fails to comply with subsection
  (1), the superintendent shall remove and dispose of the snares,
  snaring line indicators and trail indicators.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The permit holder shall be responsible for all
  expenses incurred by the superintendent under subsection (2).

<!-- -->

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  30 days after the expiry of a snare permit, the permit holder shall
  return the permit to a park warden.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The permit holder shall state the following
  information on the back of their snare permit before returning it:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of snares set by the permit holder;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of snowshoe hare snared by the permit
    holder;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the areas in which the snowshoe hare were snared;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number of days during which the permit holder
    carried on the snaring activity under the permit; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other information that the permit holder was
    required to record under a condition of the permit.

## [Powers of Superintendent]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717445 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent shall,
for the purposes of the conservation of snowshoe hare and the
maintenance of the ecological integrity of the park, in respect of each
season, determine those areas in the park in which the snaring of
snowshoe hare will be permitted or restricted.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent may,
at any time, for purposes of management of the park, public safety or
the conservation of natural resources, close areas of the park to the
snaring of snowshoe hare for any period that the superintendent
determines necessary.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Based on the
determinations made under sections 15 and 16, the superintendent shall,
in respect of each season, determine

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the limit of snowshoe hare that may be snared under a
  snare permit; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the maximum number of snare permits to be issued for
  each area where the snaring of snowshoe hare will be permitted.

## [Suspension and Revocation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717451 .Part}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may suspend a snare permit if the permit holder
  contravenes any provision of these Regulations or any condition of
  their permit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall reinstate a suspended snare
  permit

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the contravention that led to the suspension has
    been corrected;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} after a period of 30 days from the date of the
    suspension, unless proceedings have been instituted before the end
    of that period in respect of the alleged contravention; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if proceedings are instituted, after the permit
    holder has been found not guilty of an offence in respect of the
    alleged contravention or after the proceedings against them have
    been discontinued.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall revoke the snare permit if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the permit holder is convicted of an offence in
    respect of a contravention of any provision of these Regulations or
    any condition of the permit; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} except when the permit was reinstated under
    paragraph (2)(b) or (c), the permit has been suspended twice during
    the period for which it was issued.

## [Decisions of the Superintendent]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717461 .Part}

### [Notice]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717462 .Subheading}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** If the superintendent
refuses to issue a snare permit or suspends or revokes a snare permit,
the superintendent shall, as soon as possible after making their
decision, provide written notice of their decision, including reasons,
to the applicant or permit holder, as applicable.

### [Review]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717464 .Subheading}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person to whom the superintendent has refused to issue a snare permit,
  or whose snare permit has been suspended or revoked by the
  superintendent, may request a review of the superintendent's decision
  by submitting a request in writing to the Chief Executive Officer
  within 30 days after receipt by that person of the notice referred to
  in section 19.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of the written request for review, the
  Chief Executive Officer shall require that the superintendent issue or
  reinstate the snare permit if the Chief Executive Officer reaches a
  decision that differs from the superintendent's decision with respect

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a refusal, to the requirements and
    matters to be considered under sections 7 and 8; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a suspension or revocation, to the
    reasons set out in section 18.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Executive Officer shall provide written
  notice of their decision, including reasons, to the person who
  requested the review.
