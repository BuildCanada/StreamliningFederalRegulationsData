## Northern Transportation Company Limited Exemption and Transfer Order {#northern-transportation-company-limited-exemption-and-transfer-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/85-658
:::

[NORTHERN TRANSPORTATION COMPANY LIMITED DISPOSAL AUTHORIZATION
ACT](/eng/acts/N-26.3)

Registration 1985-07-13

Order Exempting Property, Rights or Interests from the Application of
Subsection 3(2) of the Northern Transportation Company Limited Disposal
Authorization Act and Transferring the Control, Management and
Administration Thereof

P.C. 1985-2208 1985-07-13
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport pursuant to subsection 3(3) of the [Northern
Transportation Company Limited Disposal Authorization
Act](/eng/acts/N-26.3), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order
exempting property, rights or interests from the application of
subsection 3(2) of the](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-658) [Northern
Transportation Company Limited Disposal Authorization
Act](/eng/acts/N-26.3) and transferring the control, management and
administration thereof.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887778 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Northern Transportation Company Limited Exemption and Transfer
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-658).

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887780 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The property, rights and
interests of Her Majesty set out in the schedule are exempt from the
application of subsection 3(2) of the [Northern Transportation Company
Limited Disposal Authorization Act](/eng/acts/N-26.3).

## [Transfer of Control, Management and Administration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887782 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  control, management and administration of the property, rights and
  interests set out in items 1 to 4 of Part I of the schedule are
  transferred to the Minister of Public Works.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The control, management and administration of the
  property, rights and interests set out in items 5 to 8 of Part I of
  the schedule are transferred to the Canadian Commercial Corporation.
