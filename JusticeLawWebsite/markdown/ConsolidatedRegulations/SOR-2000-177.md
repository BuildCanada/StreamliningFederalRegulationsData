## Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Notice Regulations (Compensation in Respect of the Restructuring of Federal Member Institutions) {#canada-deposit-insurance-corporation-notice-regulations-compensation-in-respect-of-the-restructuring-of-federal-member-institutions .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2000-177
:::

[CANADA DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-3)

Registration 2000-05-04

Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Notice Regulations (Compensation in
Respect of the Restructuring of Federal Member Institutions)

P.C. 2000-630 2000-05-04
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 39.37[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the [Canada
Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3), hereby makes the
annexed [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Notice Regulations
(Compensation in Respect of the Restructuring of Federal Member
Institutions)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-177).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656137 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Deposit Insurance
    Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[receivership order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[receivership order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an order made under
    paragraph 39.13(1)(b) of the Act. ([Version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[vesting order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vesting order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an order made under
    paragraph 39.13(1)(a) of the Act. ([Version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

## [Notice Relating to Offer of Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656142 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  addition to the information set out in subsection 39.25(1) of the Act,
  the following information must accompany a notice given by the
  Corporation under subsection 39.24(1) of the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of the vesting order;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of the effect of the vesting order
    as described in subsection 39.13(2), paragraph 39.13(4)(a),
    subsections 39.14(1) and (3) and section 39.15 of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the basis for

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the offer of compensation by the Corporation, in
      the case of a notice containing such an offer, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} no offer of compensation by the Corporation, in
      the case of a notice stating that no such offer is being made; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which a person must respond to the
    Corporation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice given by the Corporation under subsection
  39.24(1) of the Act to a holder or their assignee or successor, as
  described in that subsection, must be sent or delivered to the holder,
  assignee or receiver at the address or number shown in the records of
  the federal member institution or its transfer agent on the day the
  vesting order is made,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by prepaid mail;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by messenger service; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by facsimile or other electronic means.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-101, s. 1
:::

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  addition to the information set out in subsection 39.25(2) of the Act,
  the following information must accompany a notice given by the
  Corporation under subsection 39.24(2) of the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of the receivership order;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of the effect of the receivership
    order as described in subsections 39.13(3), (4) and (5) and 39.14(2)
    and section 39.15 of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the basis for

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the offer of compensation by the Corporation, in
      the case of a notice containing such an offer, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} no offer of compensation by the Corporation, in
      the case of a notice stating that no such offer is being made; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which a federal member institution
    must respond to the Corporation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice given by the Corporation to a federal member
  institution under subsection 39.24(2) of the Act must be sent or
  delivered to the institution at the address or number shown in the
  records of the Corporation on the day the receivership order is made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by prepaid mail;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by messenger service; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by facsimile or other electronic means.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-101, s. 2
:::

## [Response to Notice Relating to Offer of Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656173 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A notice of a person's or a
federal member institution's acceptance of or objection to an offer of
compensation made by the Corporation, or their acceptance of or
objection to the fact that no offer of compensation was made by the
Corporation, must be sent or delivered to the Corporation

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by prepaid mail;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} by messenger service; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} by facsimile or other electronic means.

## [Notice of Appointment of Assessor]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656179 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A notice sent or delivered
by the Corporation under section 39.3 of the Act must be sent or
delivered by a method set out in subsection 2(2) or 3(2) to the
following address:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a notice to a dissenting offeree, to
  the most recent address or number shown in the records of the federal
  member institution or its transfer agent on the day the vesting order
  is made or indicated by the dissenting offeree in the notice of
  objection, if any; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a notice to a federal member
  institution, to the address or number shown in the records of the
  Corporation on the day the receivership order is made.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656183 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
