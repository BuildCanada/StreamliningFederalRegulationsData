## Order Binding Certain Agents of Her Majesty for the Purposes of Part 1 of the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act {#order-binding-certain-agents-of-her-majesty-for-the-purposes-of-part-1-of-the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2001-8
:::

[PERSONAL INFORMATION PROTECTION AND ELECTRONIC DOCUMENTS
ACT](/eng/acts/P-8.6)

Registration 2000-12-13

Order Binding Certain Agents of Her Majesty for the Purposes of Part 1
of the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act

P.C. 2000-1778 2000-12-13
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Industry, pursuant to paragraph 26(2)(a) of the
[Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents
Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Order Binding Certain Agents of Her Majesty for the
Purposes of Part 1 of the Personal Information Protection and Electronic
Documents Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-8).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2000, c.
  5 
  :::
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679243 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Part 1 of the [Personal
Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6) is
binding on the following agents of Her Majesty in right of Canada to
which the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21) does not apply:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Atomic Energy of Canada Limited; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Broadcasting Corporation.

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2005-250, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-250, s. 1
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679250 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on January 1, 2001.
