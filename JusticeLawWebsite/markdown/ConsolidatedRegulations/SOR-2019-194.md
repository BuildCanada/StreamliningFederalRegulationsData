## Administration and Enforcement (Assisted Human Reproduction Act) Regulations {#administration-and-enforcement-assisted-human-reproduction-act-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2019-194

Administration and Enforcement (Assisted Human Reproduction Act)
Regulations

P.C. 2019-752 2019-06-09

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 66(1) of the [Assisted Human
Reproduction Act](/eng/acts/A-13.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81200-2-414_hq_24785){#fn_81200-2-414_hq_24785-ID0EDADA},

Parliament, substantially in the annexed form;

recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to subsection
65(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81200-2-401_E_hq_20863){#fn_81200-2-401_E_hq_20863-ID0EGACA}
of the [Assisted Human Reproduction Act](/eng/acts/A-13.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81200-2-414_hq_24785){#fn_81200-2-414_hq_24785-ID0EDACA},
makes the annexed [Administration and Enforcement (Assisted Human
Reproduction Act) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-194).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1143180 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Assisted Human Reproduction
      Act](/eng/acts/A-13.4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an
      individual, means a person who is cohabiting with the individual
      in a conjugal relationship at the relevant time, having so
      cohabited for a period of at least one year. ([conjoint de
      fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[donor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[donor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an [in
      vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo, means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an embryo that is created for
        reproductive use

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the individual for whom the embryo is created
          and who has no spouse or common-law partner at the time the
          embryo is created, regardless of the source of the human
          reproductive material used to create the embryo, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (3), the couple for
          whom the embryo is created and who are spouses or common-law
          partners at the time the embryo is created, regardless of the
          source of the human reproductive material used to create the
          embryo; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an embryo that is created for
        the purpose of improving or providing instruction in assisted
        reproduction procedures, the individuals whose human
        reproductive material is used to create the
        embryo. ([donneur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, the term
  [[spouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include a person who, at the
  relevant time, lives separate and apart from the person to whom they
  are married because of the breakdown of their marriage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Donor of [in vitro]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} embryo --- specific case

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the donor of an [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  embryo is a couple at the time the embryo is created and if the embryo
  is created using human reproductive material from only one of the
  individuals in the couple, that individual becomes the donor of the
  embryo under paragraph (a) of the definition
  [[donor]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection (1) for the purposes of
  these Regulations and section 54 of the Act if, before any further
  measure with respect to the administration or enforcement of the Act
  is taken, that individual is no longer the spouse or common-law
  partner of the other individual.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[designated
  officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations and subsection
  52(3) and section 54 of the Act, [[designated
  officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Director General responsible
  for overseeing compliance with and enforcement of the Act.

## [Application for Restoration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1143197 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of notice

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A notice
  referred to in subsection 51(1) of the Act must be served by any
  method that provides proof of delivery on the Minister at least 15
  clear days before the day on which the application for an order of
  restoration is to be made to the provincial court judge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the provincial court judge to whom the application
    is to be made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and time when and the place where the
    application is to be heard;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the seized material or information in respect of
    which the application is to be made; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the evidence on which the applicant intends to rely
    to establish that the applicant is entitled to possession of the
    seized material or information in respect of which the application
    is to be made.

## [Section 54 of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1143207 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent of donor --- [in
vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo created for reproductive use

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 54 of the Act, if an [in vitro]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} embryo is created for a couple's reproductive use, in order
  for a measure that may be taken with respect to the embryo to be
  consistent with the consent of the donor, the measure must be
  consistent with the consent of each spouse or common-law partner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsection (1) does not apply
  to a donor of an [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo referred to
  in subsection 1(3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent of donor --- [in
  vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo created for improving or providing
  instruction in assisted reproduction procedures

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 54 of the Act, if an [in
  vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo is created for the purpose of
  improving or providing instruction in assisted reproduction
  procedures, in order for a measure that may be taken with respect to
  the embryo to be consistent with the consent of the donor, the measure
  must be consistent with the consent of each individual whose human
  reproductive material is used to create the embryo.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written consent

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 54 of the Act and this
  section, the donor's consent must be in writing, signed by the donor
  and attested to by a witness or, if the seized sperm, ova or [in
  vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo was donated under the condition of
  anonymity for the purpose of assisted reproduction, the consent must
  be attested to in a document, signed by the person who originally
  obtained consent from the donor.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal --- impossibility of obtaining
consent

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If it is impossible to
obtain the consent of the donor in relation to any sperm, ovum or [in
vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo that was forfeited, the designated
officer may direct an inspector to dispose of them only after a period
of 180 days after their forfeiture.
