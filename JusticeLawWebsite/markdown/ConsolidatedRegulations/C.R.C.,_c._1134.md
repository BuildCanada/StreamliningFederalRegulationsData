## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-495387 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[assessed value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assessed value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a lot in a
    park, means the value of the lot, including improvements, determined
    according to a provincial assessment completed for the park or,
    where no provincial assessment has been completed, determined on the
    basis of an expert appraisal of the lot or of an equivalent lot;
    ([évaluation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[base factor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[base factor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of water services or
    sewer services, means the numerical value assigned to a unit each
    year by a council or committee of residents of a park and
    representatives of Parks Canada for purposes of determining the
    owner's share of recoverable capital costs incurred in respect of
    the park to deliver water services or sewer services, as the case
    may be, to lots in the park; ([facteur de base]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any trade, calling,
    industry, employment or occupation carried on for gain or profit in
    a park; ([commerce]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dwelling unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dwelling unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means two or more rooms used
    or intended for use as a single domestic housekeeping unit with
    cooking, living, sleeping and sanitary facilities;
    ([logement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[lot]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lot]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/96-171, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[lot volume value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lot volume value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the result obtained
    by multiplying the aggregate of the volume values for the units on a
    lot by the number of months of occupancy permitted under the lease,
    permit or licence of occupation for that lot divided by 12; ([valeur
    volumétrique du lot]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/96-171, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual, syndicate,
    association, corporation, company, club or organization in charge
    of, or responsible for, a residence or for the operation of any
    accommodation, activity, business, entertainment, church, hospital,
    school, university or other facility within a park, and includes an
    employee, manager, lessee, transferee or partner of such an
    individual, syndicate, association, corporation, company, club or
    organization; ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/96-171, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[street]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[street]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a lane;
    ([rue]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/93-165, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[total capital costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[total capital costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a water system in a park, the
      capital cost incurred to deliver water services to lots in the
      park, calculated on an annual basis, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a sewer system in a park, the
      capital cost incurred to deliver sewer services to lots in the
      park, calculated on an annual basis; ([coût total en
      capital]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[total operating and maintenance costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[total operating and maintenance costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a water system in a park, the
      operating and maintenance costs incurred to deliver water services
      to lots in the park, calculated on an annual basis, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a sewer system in a park, the
      operating and maintenance costs incurred to deliver sewer services
      to lots in the park, calculated on an annual basis; ([coût total
      de fonctionnement et d'entretien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a building, or a portion of a
    building, in a park designated as a unit by a council or committee
    of residents of the park and representatives of Parks Canada;
    ([unité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[volume value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[volume value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of water services
    or sewer services, means the numerical value assigned to a unit,
    determined by comparing the estimated annual volume of water that
    the unit will use or the sewage that it will generate annually, as
    the case may be, to the volume of water used or sewage generated by
    a building that has been established as the benchmark of one volume
    value for the park in which the unit is located. ([valeur
    volumétrique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-495423 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall, in any park,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make any connection with a water or sewer main,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make any connection with a water pipe connected to
    a water main, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} make any connection with a sewer pipe connected to
    a sewer main,

  without the permission in writing of the superintendent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent is authorized to grant the
  permission after taking into consideration

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the capacity of the park's sewer and water systems;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any technical or material constraints;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the preservation of the park's natural resources
    and the protection of its cultural, historical and archaeological
    resources;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the safety, health and enjoyment of persons who are
    visiting or residing in the park; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the preservation, control and management of the
    park.

## [Connections to Water Main and Sewer Main]{.HTitleText1} {#h-495474 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Each application for the
making of a connection to a water main or sewer main in a park shall be
made to the superintendent on a prescribed form obtainable from the
superintendent and shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be signed by the owner of the lot in respect of which
  the application is made or his agent authorized in writing for the
  purpose;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} state the type of premises in respect of which the
  water is to be used or the sewer connected; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} state the number of rooms or the floor area of any
  building in respect of which the water is to be used or the sewer
  connected, where these are factors in establishing the water or sewer
  rate payable.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-38, s. 7(F)
- SOR/93-165, s. 3
- SOR/2001-320, s. 20(F)
:::

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  owner of a building who is required pursuant to subsection 3(1) to
  connect his building to a water main or sewer main shall pay

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all the costs for the construction of that part of
    the connection that is within his lot line; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the extra costs for the construction of that part
    of the connection that is outside his lot line that arise because of
    the presence of rock, frost or other obstacles or because of the
    removal and replacement during the construction of any portion of a
    street, sidewalk or boulevard.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receiving an application made in accordance with
  section 10 and receiving a security deposit from the applicant in the
  form of a certified cheque or cash deposit in an amount equal to the
  portion of the estimated cost of construction for which the owner is
  responsible under subsection (1), the superintendent shall cause the
  connection to be made and any soil or portion of a street, sidewalk or
  boulevard that was removed for the making of the connection to be
  replaced.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} On completion of a connection to a water main or
  sewer main, the superintendent shall prepare a statement setting out
  the portion of the actual costs for which the owner is responsible and
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the amount of the security deposit referred
    to in subsection (2) exceeds the portion of the actual costs for
    which the owner is responsible, deposit that portion to the credit
    of the Receiver General and refund the balance to the owner; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the amount of the security deposit referred
    to in subsection (2) is equal to or less than the portion of the
    actual costs for which the owner is responsible, deposit that
    portion to the credit of the Receiver General and notify the owner
    of the amount of the balance, if any, of the portion payable by him.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-38, s. 3
- SOR/89-449, s. 2(F)
- SOR/93-165, s. 3
- SOR/2001-320, ss. 6, 20(F)
:::

- **[[11.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an additional building is
  erected on a lot on which there is another building already connected
  to a water main or sewer main or where an existing building on a lot
  is altered, the owner of the building shall apply to the
  superintendent for permission to make an additional connection or to
  alter an existing connection.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On approving an application made pursuant to
  subsection (1) and receiving from the applicant a security deposit in
  the form of a certified cheque or cash deposit in an amount equal to
  the estimated cost of the construction for which the owner of the
  building is responsible under section 11.2, the superintendent may
  prescribe terms respecting

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the location of the connection to a previously
    installed connection or to a water main or sewer main;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which the connection is to be
    constructed, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the grade, type and size of pipe;

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quality and extent of pipe bedding; and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the quality and extent of pipe trench backfill
      material.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the owner of a building does not comply with
  any terms prescribed by the superintendent pursuant to subsection (2),
  the superintendent may cause the connection to be made in accordance
  with the terms.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The owner of the building may enter into an agreement
  with the superintendent permitting the superintendent to cause the
  connection to a water main or sewer main to be made.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} On completion of a connection to a water main or
  sewer main made pursuant to subsection (3) or (4), the superintendent
  shall prepare a statement setting out the costs for which the owner is
  responsible under section 11.2 and shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the amount of the security deposit referred
    to in subsection (2) exceeds the costs for which the owner is
    responsible, deposit that amount to the credit of the Receiver
    General and refund the balance to the owner; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the amount of the security deposit referred
    to in subsection (2) is equal to or less than the costs for which
    the owner is responsible, deposit that amount to the credit of the
    Receiver General and notify the owner of the amount of the balance,
    if any, of the amount payable by him.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-38, s. 3
- SOR/89-449, s. 3(F)
- SOR/93-165, s. 3
- SOR/2001-320, s. 20(F)
:::

- **[[11.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a connection is made pursuant

  costs

  obstacles or because of the removal and replacement during the

## [Discontinuance of Water Supply]{.HTitleText1} {#h-495521 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may shut off the water supply in respect of any lot or
  building where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any fixture or appliance is causing a wastage of
    water;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a water fixture has been installed or connection
    made without his approval; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} water or sewer charges have not been paid within 30
    days after the due date for payment thereof.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where any water is shut off pursuant to
  subsection (1) it shall not be turned on again until the fixture,
  appliance or connection has been removed, repaired or replaced to the
  satisfaction of the superintendent or the charges have been paid, as
  the case may be.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-38, s. 7(F)
- SOR/93-165, s. 3
- SOR/2001-320, s. 20(F)
:::

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent may
shut off or interrupt the supply of water in respect of any or all
buildings or lots in the park for such periods as are required where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is expedient to repair, replace or install any
  water main or other work; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is a conflagration or other circumstance that,
  in the opinion of the superintendent, requires such action.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-38, s. 7(F)
- SOR/93-165, s. 3
- SOR/2001-320, s. 20(F)
:::

## [Applicable Charges for Water and Sewerage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-495535 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  owner of a lot located in Kootenay National Park of Canada or Fundy
  National Park of Canada shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} inform the superintendent of that park of the
    number of rooms --- excluding any halls, vestibules, bathrooms,
    pantries or closets --- the floor area of those rooms and the use of
    any buildings on the lot for the purposes of determining the annual
    quantity charge for water or sewerage payable by the owner; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the lot is connected to the park water system,
    pay an annual quantity charge for water at the rate set out in
    Schedule IV or VIII, as the case may be.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2010-140, s. 8\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every owner of a lot located in Kootenay National
  Park of Canada shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the lot is connected to the park water or sewer
    system, pay an annual service connection charge at the rate set out
    in Schedule IV;

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} if the lot is connected to the park sewer system,
    pay an annual quantity charge for the sewer at the rate set out in
    Schedule IV; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the lot is connected to the park water or sewer
    system and the lot fronts or abuts on a street along which a water
    main or sewer main has been laid, pay

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an annual general assessment charge, based on the
      assessed value of the lot, at the rate set out in Schedule IV, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an annual special assessment charge for water
      and sewerage, based on the frontage and the area of the lot, at
      the rate set out in Schedule IV.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (1) and (2), in
  determining the frontage of a lot,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the frontage of a lot fronting or abutting on only
    one street is the length of the side of the lot that fronts or abuts
    on that street;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the frontage of a lot fronting or abutting on two
    or more streets along which water mains have been laid and from one
    of which a connection has been made is the length of the side of the
    lot that fronts or abuts on the street from which the connection was
    made;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} notwithstanding paragraph (b), the frontage of a
    corner lot at an intersection of water mains is the length of the
    longest side of the lot plus 25 feet, except that where the shortest
    side of the lot is less than 25 feet in length, the frontage is the
    aggregate of the longest and shortest sides;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} notwithstanding paragraph (c), the frontage of a
    lot fronting or abutting on two or more streets along which water
    mains have been laid and from more than one of which connections
    have been made is twice the length of the longest side of the lot;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of paragraphs (a) to (d), the
    frontage of a lot the sides of which are not parallel is that number
    of feet that bears the same ratio to the frontage in feet of the
    nearest rectangular lot in the same block as the square root of the
    area of the irregular lot is to the square root of the area of the
    rectangular lot; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} notwithstanding paragraphs (a) to (e), where a lot
    has no buildings, or where each building on the lot has a floor area
    of less than 300 square feet, and the lot forms part of a group of
    lots, having a combined frontage, calculated under those paragraphs,
    of more than 200 feet, each of which is being used in connection
    with a single enterprise, the frontage of the lot is one-half the
    amount determined under any of paragraphs (a) to (e).
