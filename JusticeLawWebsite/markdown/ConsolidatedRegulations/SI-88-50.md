## Revised Statutes of Canada, 1985 Distribution Direction {#revised-statutes-of-canada-1985-distribution-direction .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/88-50
:::

[REVISED STATUTES OF CANADA, 1985 ACT](/eng/acts/R-7.7)

Registration 1988-03-30

Direction Respecting the Distribution of the Revised Statutes of Canada,
1985

P.C. 1988-508  1988-03-17
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 8(2) of the [Revised
Statutes of Canada, 1985 Act](/eng/acts/R-7.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDABA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Direction respecting the distribution of the
Revised Statutes of Canada, 1985](/eng/regulations/SI-88-50).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EDABA){#footnote1_e}S.C.
  1987, c. 48
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641890 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Direction may be cited
as the [Revised Statutes of Canada, 1985 Distribution
Direction](/eng/regulations/SI-88-50).

## [Distribution]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641892 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Revised Statutes of
Canada, 1985 shall, as soon as practicable after they become available,
be distributed without charge to the persons and classes of persons
listed in the schedule.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-641895 .scheduleLabel}
:::

  ------ ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    1.   Governor General
    2.   Lieutenant Governors of the Provinces
    3.   Speaker of the Senate
    4.   Speaker of the House of Commons
    5.   Prime Minister
    6.   Leader of the Opposition in the Senate
    7.   Leader of the Opposition in the House of Commons
    8.   Ministers of the Government of Canada
    9.   Members of the Parliament of Canada not otherwise included in this Schedule
  10     Members of the judiciary of Canada, including provincial court judges
  11     Attorneys General, Ministers of Justice and Solicitors General of the Governments of the Provinces
  12     Chief or senior legislative counsel of each Province
  13     Chief librarians of court houses in Canada
  14     Chief librarians of law schools in Canada
