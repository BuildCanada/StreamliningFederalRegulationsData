## Withdrawal of Certain Lands (Peel River Basin, Y.T.) from Disposal Order {#withdrawal-of-certain-lands-peel-river-basin-y.t.-from-disposal-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/92-73
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 1992-05-06

Withdrawal of Certain Lands (Peel River Basin, Y.T.) from Disposal Order

P.C. 1992-708  1992-04-09
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, for the reason
that the lands are required to facilitate the settlement of native land
claims, is pleased hereby, pursuant to paragraph 23(a) of the
[Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), to order the withdrawal from
disposal of the lands describes in the schedule hereto, including all
mines and minerals, whether solid, liquid or gaseous, including sand and
gravel that may be disposed of pursuant to the [Territorial Quarrying
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1527), and also including the
forest resources which may be disposed of pursuant to the Yukon Timber
Regulations, for a period terminating on December 31, 1993, without
prejudice to the holders of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} recorded mineral claims in good standing under the
  [Yukon Quartz Mining Act](/eng/acts/Y-4) and the [Yukon Placer Mining
  Act](/eng/acts/Y-3);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} existing oil and gas rights, interests and privileges
  under the [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} surface rights in good standing granted under section
  8 of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7).
