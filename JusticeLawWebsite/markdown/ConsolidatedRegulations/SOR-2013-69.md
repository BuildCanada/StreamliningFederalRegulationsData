## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803581 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Nunavut Waters and Nunavut
    Surface Rights Tribunal Act](/eng/acts/N-28.8).
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an appurtenant
    undertaking, or an undertaking in relation to a use of waters or
    deposit of waste for which a licence is not required, of a type set
    out in Schedule 1. ([entreprise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Nunavut Land Claims Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803585 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the issuance by the Board of a type A or B licence,
  or the approval by the Board of an application for a use of waters or
  deposit of waste without a licence, constitutes an approval by the
  Board for the purposes of section 13.7.1 of the Agreement; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a use of waters or a deposit of waste without a
  licence that is described in section 4 or 5 is not authorized by these
  Regulations unless the Board has approved the use or deposit.

## [Application for Approval of Use of Waters or Deposit of Waste Without a Licence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803589 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An application for the
approval of a use of waters or deposit of waste without a licence must
be submitted to the Board and contain the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name, postal address and telephone
  number and their fax number and electronic mail address, if any;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the owner of any land that will be used
  in relation to the waters to be used or the waste to be deposited;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the type of undertaking in respect of which waters
  will be used or waste will be deposited;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the equipment that will be used in using the waters
  or depositing the waste;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the location of the undertaking, including its
  geographical coordinates, if known, and the water management area in
  which the undertaking is located;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for a use of waters,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the purpose for which the waters will be used,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of water, in cubic metres, that will
    be used per day,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} whether the use of the waters will involve
    watercourse crossing or training, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the period or periods during which the waters will
    be used; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for a deposit of waste,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the type of waste to be deposited,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of waste, in cubic metres, that will
    be deposited per day,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where the waste will be deposited,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any measures that will be taken to avoid or
    mitigate any adverse impacts, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the period or periods during which the waste will
    be deposited.

## [Use of Waters Without a Licence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803607 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 11(2)(a) of the Act, a licence is not required
  for a use of waters that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is of a type set out in column 2 of Schedule 2 that
    satisfies a criterion set out in column 3 in respect of an
    undertaking set out in column 1;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} would not substantially affect the quality,
    quantity or flow of the waters;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} would not substantially affect the quality,
    quantity or flow of waters flowing through Inuit-owned land; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} would not adversely affect the use of waters by a
    person who would be entitled to compensation under section 58 or 60
    of the Act if their use of those waters were to be adversely
    affected by an applicant for a licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), no use of waters without a
  licence is authorized if a licence is required for another use of
  waters, or a deposit of waste, in respect of the same undertaking.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No use of waters without a licence is authorized
  unless the following conditions are complied with:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} measures must be taken prior to using waters to
    minimize any alteration to the bed or banks of a watercourse whose
    waters are to be used, and the measures must be maintained during
    the operation of the undertaking;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prior to the closure or abandonment of the
    undertaking or the end of the period authorized for the use of
    waters, whichever occurs first, the site must be restored --- to the
    extent practicable --- to the state in which it was before the
    waters were used; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person who has a mineral right and
    who uses waters in relation to that right, the person must respect
    the priority conferred on Inuit by section 62 of the Act as if that
    person had a licence for the use.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (3)(b), a site need not be restored
  prior to the end of the period authorized for the use of waters
  without a licence if the Board issues a licence for a use of waters on
  that site prior to the end of that period.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A use of waters without a licence is authorized for a
  period of one year after the day on which the Board approves the
  application for the approval of the use.

## [Waste Deposit Without a Licence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803621 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 12(2)(a) of the Act, a licence is not required
  for a deposit of waste that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} satisfies a criterion set out in column 3 of
    Schedule 3 in respect of an undertaking set out in column 1 that is
    engaged in an activity set out in column 2;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} would not substantially affect the quality,
    quantity or flow of the waters in which it is deposited;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} would not substantially affect the quality,
    quantity or flow of waters flowing through Inuit-owned land; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} would not adversely affect the use of waters by a
    person who would be entitled to compensation under section 58 or 60
    of the Act if their use of those waters were to be adversely
    affected by an applicant for a licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a deposit of waste that is discharged
  from a vessel within the meaning of section 2 of the [Canada Shipping
  Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15), a licence is not required if the
  deposit is not prohibited under Part 9 of that Act and satisfies
  paragraphs (1)(c) and (d).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), no deposit of waste without a
  licence is authorized if a licence is required for another deposit of
  waste, or a use of waters, in respect of the same undertaking.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No deposit of waste without a licence is authorized
  unless the following conditions are complied with:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the waste must not be deposited to surface waters
    or within 31 m of the ordinary high water mark of any body of water;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the waste must not contain more than 15 mg/L of
    petroleum or petroleum product and must not have a visible
    hydrocarbon sheen;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prior to the closure or abandonment of the
    undertaking or the end of the period authorized for the deposit,
    whichever occurs first, the site must be restored --- to the extent
    practicable --- to the state in which it was before the waste was
    deposited; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person who has a mineral right and
    who deposits waste in relation to that right, the person must
    respect the priority conferred on Inuit by section 62 of the Act as
    if that person had a licence for the deposit.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (4)(c), a site need not be restored
  prior to the end of the period authorized for the deposit without a
  licence if the Board issues a licence for a deposit of waste on that
  site prior to the end of that period.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A deposit of waste without a licence is authorized
  for a period of one year after the day on which the Board approves the
  application for the approval of the deposit.

## [Books and Records: Use of Waters or Deposit of Waste Without a Licence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803637 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who is authorized under these Regulations to use waters or deposit
  waste without a licence must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain accurate and detailed books and records of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of water, in cubic metres, used each
      day,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of waste, in cubic metres,
      deposited each day,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the type of waste deposited each day,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} where the waste is deposited,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of the substance, or
      substances, in the deposited solid or liquid that has the effect
      of making the deposit waste,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the methodology used to calculate or determine
      the information referred to in subparagraphs (i) to (iv), and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the measures that were taken to avoid or
      mitigate any adverse impacts of the deposit of waste;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} keep the books and records on the site of the
    undertaking during the period of its operation and make them
    available during that period to an inspector on request;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} submit to the Board a report containing a summary
    description and supporting photographs of the restoration of the
    site of the undertaking within 30 days after the earlier of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the undertaking is closed or
      abandoned, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the last day of the period authorized for the
      use or deposit without a licence; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} keep the books and records for two years after the
    day on which they submit the report describing the restoration of
    the site of the undertaking.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subparagraph (1)(c)(ii), a person need not
  submit a report to the Board if the person obtains the Board's
  approval for a use of waters or deposit of waste without a licence, or
  a licence for a use of waters or a deposit of waste, on the same site
  within the period referred to in subparagraph (1)(c)(ii).

## [Licensing Criteria for Use of Waters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803654 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 42(1) of the Act, the appropriate licence for a
  use of waters is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a type B licence if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the use is of a type set out in column 2 of
      Schedule 2 and satisfies a criterion set out in column 4 in
      respect of an undertaking set out in column 1, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the use satisfies the criterion set out in
      paragraph 4(1)(a) but does not satisfy one or more criterion set
      out in paragraphs 4(1)(b) to (d); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a type A licence if the use is of a type set out in
    column 2 of Schedule 2 and satisfies a criterion set out in column 5
    in respect of an undertaking set out in column 1.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph 1(a), a type A licence is the
  appropriate licence for a use of waters if a type A licence is
  required for another use of waters, or a deposit of waste, in respect
  of the same undertaking.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections 42(1) and (3) of the
  Act, a type B licence is the licence that the Board may issue for a
  use of waters described in subsection 4(1).

## [Licensing Criteria for Deposit of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803663 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 42(1) of the Act, the appropriate licence for a
  deposit of waste is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a type B licence if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the deposit satisfies a criterion set out in
      column 4 of Schedule 3 in respect of an undertaking set out in
      column 1 that is engaged in an activity set out in column 2, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the deposit satisfies the criterion set out in
      paragraph 5(1)(a) but does not satisfy one or more criterion set
      out in paragraph 5(1)(b) or (c); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a type A licence if the deposit meets a criterion
    set out in column 5 of Schedule 3 in respect of an undertaking set
    out in column 1 that is engaged in an activity set out in column 2.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph 1(a), a type A licence is the
  appropriate licence for a deposit of waste if a type A licence is
  required for another deposit of waste, or a use of waters, in respect
  of the same undertaking.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections 42(1) and (3) of the
  Act, a type B licence is the licence that the Board may issue for a
  deposit of waste described in subsection 5(1).

## [Public Hearings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803672 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 13.7.3 of the Agreement and subsection 52(1) of
  the Act, no public hearing is required in respect of an application
  for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an amendment to a type A licence that does not
    affect the use, flow or quality of waters or alter the term of the
    licence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one or several renewals of a type A licence if the
    total duration of the renewal or renewals does not exceed 180 days;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the assignment of a type A licence; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the issuance, amendment, renewal, assignment or
    cancellation of a type B licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 13.7.3 of the Agreement,
  no public hearing is required in respect of an application for a use
  of waters or deposit of waste without a licence.

## [Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803680 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 76(1) of the Act, the Board may fix the
  amount of security required to be furnished by an applicant for a
  licence, a licensee or a prospective assignee in an amount not
  exceeding the aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the costs of the abandonment of the undertaking;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the costs of the restoration of the site of the
    undertaking;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the costs of any ongoing measures that may remain
    to be taken after the abandonment of the undertaking; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the compensation that a person, including the
    designated Inuit organization, who is adversely affected by the use
    of waters or deposit of waste may be entitled to under section 13 of
    the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In fixing an amount of security, the Board may have
  regard to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ability of the applicant, licensee or
    prospective assignee to pay the costs referred to in subsection (1);
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the past performance by the applicant, licensee or
    prospective assignee in respect of any other licence.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Security must be in the form of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a promissory note guaranteed by a bank listed in
    Schedule I or II to the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) and made
    payable to the Receiver General;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a certified cheque drawn on a bank listed in
    Schedule I or II to the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) and made
    payable to the Receiver General;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a performance bond approved by the Treasury Board
    for the purposes of paragraph (c) of the definition [[security
    deposit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Government
    Contracts Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-402);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an irrevocable letter of credit from a bank listed
    in Schedule I or II to the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01); or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a cash payment.

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803696 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A fee of \$30 is payable
on the submission of an application for a licence, an application for
the amendment, renewal, cancellation or assignment of a licence or an
application under section 77 of the Act.

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (4) to (6),
  the fee payable by a licensee for the right to use waters, calculated
  on an annual basis, is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an agricultural undertaking, the
    greater of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} \$30, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} \$0.15 for each 1 000 m^3^ that is authorized by
      the licence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an industrial or mining undertaking,
    or the undertaking set out in item 8, column 1 of Schedule 1, the
    greater of \$30 and the aggregate of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the first 2 000 m^3^ per day that is
      authorized by the licence, \$1 for each 100 m^3^ per day,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for any quantity greater than 2 000 m^3^ per day
      but less than or equal to 4 000 m^3^ per day that is authorized by
      the licence, \$1.50 for each 100 m^3^ per day, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for any quantity greater than 4 000 m^3^ per
      day that is authorized by the licence, \$2 for each 100 m^3^ per
      day; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a power undertaking,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a Class 0 power undertaking, nil,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a Class 1 power undertaking, \$1,500,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for a Class 2 power undertaking, \$4,000,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} for a Class 3 power undertaking, \$10,000,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} for a Class 4 power undertaking, \$30,000,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} for a Class 5 power undertaking, \$80,000, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} for a Class 6 power undertaking, \$90,000 for
      the first 100 000 kW of authorized production and \$1,000 for each
      1 000 kW of authorized production in excess of 100 000 kW.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), if a licence
  authorizes a use of waters on a basis other than a daily basis, the
  licence fee payable must be calculated by converting the rate of
  authorized use to an equivalent daily rate.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the volume of water is specified in a licence to
  be total watercourse flow, the licence fee must be calculated using
  the mean daily flow of the watercourse, calculated on an annual basis.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Licence fees are payable only for the portion of the
  year during which the licence is in effect.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No licence fees are payable in respect of a diversion
  of waters where the waters are not otherwise used.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} No licence fees are payable for the right to the use
  of waters on, in or flowing through Inuit-owned lands.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Licence fees shall be paid or, in the case of an
  initial payment, deducted from the deposit

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a licence for a term of one year or
    less, at the time the licence is issued; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a licence for a term of more than one
    year,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the first year of the licence, at the time
      the licence is issued, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for each subsequent year of the licence, and for
      any portion of the final year of the licence, on the anniversary
      of the date of issuance of the licence.

## [Books and Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803725 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A licensee must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain accurate and detailed books and records of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of water, in cubic metres, used each
    day,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of waste, in cubic metres, deposited
    each day,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the type of waste deposited each day,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of the substance, or substances,
    in the deposited solid or liquid that has the effect of making the
    deposit waste, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the methodology used to calculate or determine the
    information referred to in subparagraphs (i) to (iv);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} keep the books and records on the site of the
  appurtenant undertaking during the period of its operation or until
  the expiry or cancellation of the licence; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} keep the books and records for a period of at least
  five years after the expiry or cancellation of the licence.

## [Annual Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803735 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee must submit an annual report to the Board, in a form
  acceptable to the Board, by March 31 of each year. The report must
  contain the following information in respect of the previous calendar
  year:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensee's name and licence number;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of water, in cubic metres, used by the
    licensee, the source of the water and the purpose of its use;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the quantity, in cubic metres, and type of waste
    deposited by the licensee and the location of the deposit, including
    its geographical coordinates;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of the substance, or substances,
    in the deposited solid or liquid that has the effect of making the
    deposit waste;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the measures that were taken to avoid or mitigate
    any adverse impacts of the deposit of waste;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a summary of any maintenance, modification or
    construction of a work that forms part of the appurtenant
    undertaking;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the appurtenant undertaking was abandoned, a
    summary description and supporting photographs of the site of the
    undertaking at the time of its abandonment;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the site of the appurtenant undertaking was
    restored, a summary description and supporting photographs of the
    restoration;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a summary of any study or monitoring program
    undertaken, any data collected under the study or program, any work
    constructed and any plan submitted pursuant to paragraph 70(1)(c) of
    the Act;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a summary of any changes made to operation and
    maintenance plans in respect of any work that forms part of the
    appurtenant undertaking; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a summary of any measures taken in response to a
    direction given by an inspector under section 87 of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must be signed and dated by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensee, if the licensee is an individual; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an authorized agent of the licensee, if the
    licensee is not an individual.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} On application in writing by a licensee, the Board
  may extend the period for submitting an annual report for a period of
  up to 60 days if the Board is satisfied that an extension is justified
  in the circumstances.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Within 90 days after the day of the expiry or
  cancellation of a licence, the licensee must submit a report to the
  Board --- in a form acceptable to the Board and signed and dated by
  the person referred to in subsection (2) --- that contains the
  information set out in subsection (1) in respect of the current
  calendar year.

## [Public Register]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803754 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  register referred to in section 78 of the Act must be in printed or
  electronic form and contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of each application and all documents
    received or provided by the Board in respect of the application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all records in respect of any public hearing held
    in connection with the application;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all documents received or provided by the Board in
    respect of compliance with the conditions of a licence or the
    conditions imposed on the use of waters or deposit of waste without
    a licence; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all documents received or provided by the Board in
    respect of the cancellation of a licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(a), a document received by the
  Board in respect of an application does not need to be kept in the
  register if an Act of Parliament requires that the document be kept in
  a registry maintained by the Nunavut Planning Commission or the
  Nunavut Impact Review Board.

## [Reporting of Unauthorized Deposit of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803762 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 12(3) of the Act, a person who reports a
  deposit of waste that is not authorized by a licence or these
  Regulations must report the location, date and nature of the deposit

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to an inspector, in person or by telephone or
    electronic mail; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to any emergency spill notification service of the
    Nunavut government, by telephone, fax or electronic mail.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Information that is reported in person or by
  telephone must also be reported in writing to an inspector without
  delay.

## [Water Management Areas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803768 .Part}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  watershed shown on the map set out in Schedule 4 is established as a
  water management area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The boundaries of each watershed are described in the
  document entitled Nunavut Watersheds Descriptions dated December 1,
  2010, and deposited with the Board by the Assistant Deputy Minister,
  Northern Affairs Organization, Department of Indian Affairs and
  Northern Development.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any part of a watershed described in the document
  referred to in subsection (2) that is a marine area does not form part
  of a water management area.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board must make the document referred to in
  subsection (2) available to the public.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803774 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
