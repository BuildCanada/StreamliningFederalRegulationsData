## Oaths of Allegiance and Office Order (N.W.T.) {#oaths-of-allegiance-and-office-order-n.w.t. .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1239
:::

[NORTHWEST TERRITORIES ACT](/eng/acts/N-27)

Order prescribing oaths of allegiance and office
::::

:::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499795 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Oaths of Allegiance and Office Order
(N.W.T.)](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1239).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 59
:::

::: PITLink

Version](section-1-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 1"){.PITLink}
:::

## [Oath of Allegiance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499799 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The oath of allegiance
prescribed by the Oaths of Allegiance Act is the oath of allegiance to
be taken and subscribed by the Commissioner of the Northwest
Territories.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 60
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 2"){.PITLink}
:::

## [Oath of Office]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499803 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The oath of office to be
taken and subscribed by the Commissioner of the Northwest Territories is
in the form set out in Schedule I.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, 2014, c. 2, s.
61\]]{.Repealed}

[Previous

## [Swearing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499806 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The oath of allegiance and
oath of office of the Commissioner of the Northwest Territories shall be
sworn and subscribed before the Clerk or Assistant Clerk of the Privy
Council.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, 2014, c. 2, s.
62\]]{.Repealed}

## [SCHEDULE I]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 3)]{.OriginatingRef}[Oath of Office for Commissioner of the Northwest Territories]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-499810 .scheduleLabel}
:::

I, ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot} do solemnly and
sincerely promise and swear that I will duly and faithfully and to the
best of my skill and knowledge execute the powers and trust reposed in
me as the Commissioner of the Northwest Territories. So help me God.
