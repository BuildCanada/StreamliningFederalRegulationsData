## Transfer of Duties Order {#transfer-of-duties-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2015-97
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2015-11-18

Transfer of Duties Order

P.C. 2015-1238 2015-11-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-2015-97_e_hq_16321){#fn_SI-2015-97_e_hq_16321-ID0EHDAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SI-2015-97_e_hq_16322){#fn_SI-2015-97_e_hq_16322-ID0EEDAA},
transfers from the Minister of Transport to the Minister of
Infrastructure, Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs the powers,
duties and functions under the [Bridge To Strengthen Trade
Act](/eng/acts/B-8.05)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_SI-2015-97_e_hq_16323){#fn_SI-2015-97_e_hq_16323-ID0EBDAA},
effective November 4, 2015.
