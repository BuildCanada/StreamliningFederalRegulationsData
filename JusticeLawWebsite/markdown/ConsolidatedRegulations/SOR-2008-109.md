## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-742848 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Sections 426 to 436 of the
[Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) do not apply to aircraft objects that were
not registered under those sections before the day on which these
Regulations come into force.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Five years after the day on
which these Regulations come into force, sections 426 to 436 of the
[Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) cease to apply to aircraft objects that
were registered under those sections before that day.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-742851 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Ind11FA_hq_4381){#fn_Ind11FA_hq_4381-ID0EBCA}[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
the Aircraft Protocol, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
[International Interests in Mobile Equipment (aircraft equipment)
Act](/eng/acts/I-19.6), chapter 3 of the Statutes of Canada, 2005, comes
into force for Canada.
