## Insurance Business (Banks and Bank Holding Companies) Regulations {#insurance-business-banks-and-bank-holding-companies-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-330
:::

[BANK ACT](/eng/acts/B-1.01)

Registration 1992-05-21

Insurance Business (Banks and Bank Holding Companies) Regulations

P.C. 1992-1106 1992-05-21
::::

::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 416 of the [Bank
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EDBAA}, is
pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations respecting the extent to
which a bank may undertake the business of insurance and relations
between banks and entities that undertake the business of insurance,
insurance agents and insurance brokers, effective June 1, 1992.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944558 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[authorized type of insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorized type of insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} credit or charge card-related insurance,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} creditors' disability insurance,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} creditors' life insurance,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} creditors' loss of employment insurance,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} creditors' vehicle inventory insurance,

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} export credit insurance,

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} mortgage insurance, or

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} travel insurance; ([assurance
      autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bank web page]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bank web page]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a web page that a bank
    uses in relation to its business in Canada, including any
    information provided by the bank that is accessible on a
    telecommunications device. It does not include a web page that is
    only accessible by employees or agents of the bank; ([page Web de
    banque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[credit or charge card-related insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[credit or charge card-related insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in
    respect of a bank, means a policy of an insurance company that
    provides insurance to the holder of a credit or charge card issued
    by the bank as a feature of the card, without request and without an
    individual assessment of risk,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} against loss of, or damage to, goods purchased
      with the card,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} under which the insurance company undertakes to
      extend a warranty provided by the manufacturer of goods purchased
      with the card, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} against any loss arising from a contractual
      liability assumed by the holder when renting a vehicle, when the
      rental is paid for with the card; ([assurance carte de crédit ou
      de paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[creditors' disability insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[creditors' disability insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect
    of a bank, means a group insurance policy that will pay all or part
    of the amount of a debt of a debtor to the bank, or to a loan
    company that is an affiliate of the bank, in the event of bodily
    injury to, or an illness or disability of,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a natural person, the debtor
      or the spouse or common-law partner of the debtor,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who is a guarantor of all or
      part of the debt,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a body corporate, any
      director or officer of the body corporate, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is an entity, any natural person
      who is essential to the ability of the debtor to meet the debtor's
      financial obligations to the bank or to the loan company;
      ([assurance-invalidité de crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[creditors' life insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[creditors' life insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
    bank, means a group insurance policy that will pay to the bank, or
    to a loan company that is an affiliate of the bank, all or part of
    the amount of a debt of a debtor or, where a debt is in respect of a
    small business or a farm, fishery or ranch, all or part of the
    amount of the credit limit of a line of credit, in the event of the
    death of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a natural person, the debtor
      or the spouse or common-law partner of the debtor,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who is a guarantor of all or
      part of the debt,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a body corporate, a director
      or officer of the body corporate, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is an entity, any natural person
      who is essential to the ability of the debtor to meet the debtor's
      financial obligations to the bank or to the loan company;
      ([assurance-vie de crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[creditors' loss of employment insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[creditors' loss of employment insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in
    respect of a bank, means a policy of an insurance company that will
    pay, without any individual assessment of risk, all or part of the
    amount of a debt of a debtor to the bank, or to a loan company that
    is an affiliate of the bank, in the event that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the debtor, if the debtor is a natural person,
      becomes involuntarily unemployed, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who is a guarantor of all or
      part of the debt becomes involuntarily unemployed; ([assurance
      crédit en cas de perte d'emploi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[creditors' vehicle inventory insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[creditors' vehicle inventory insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in
    respect of a bank, means a policy of an insurance company that
    provides insurance against direct and accidental loss or damage to
    vehicles that are held in stock for display and sale purposes by a
    debtor of the bank, some or all of which have been financed by the
    bank; ([assurance crédit pour stocks de véhicules]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[export credit insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[export credit insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a policy of an
    insurance company that provides insurance to an exporter of goods or
    services against a loss incurred by the exporter due to a
    non-payment for exported goods or services; ([assurance crédit des
    exportateurs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[group insurance policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[group insurance policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
    bank, means a contract of insurance between an insurance company and
    the bank, or between an insurance company and a loan company that is
    an affiliate of the bank, that provides insurance severally in
    respect of a group of identifiable persons who individually hold
    certificates of insurance; ([police d'assurance
    collective]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity that is
    approved, registered or otherwise authorized to insure risks under
    an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province; ([société
    d'assurances]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[line of credit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[line of credit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commitment on the
    part of a bank to lend to a debtor, without a predetermined
    repayment schedule, one or more amounts, where the aggregate amount
    outstanding does not exceed a predetermined credit limit, which
    limit does not exceed the reasonable credit needs of the debtor;
    ([marge de crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[loan company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[loan company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a company incorporated
    under the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) or a loan
    or trust corporation incorporated by or under an Act of the
    legislature of a province; ([société de prêt]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[mortgage insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mortgage insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a bank,
    means an insurance policy that provides insurance to the bank, or to
    a loan company that is an affiliate of the bank, against loss caused
    by a default on the part of a debtor who is a natural person under a
    loan from the bank or from the loan company that is secured by a
    mortgage on real property or on an interest in real property;
    ([assurance hypothèque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[personal accident insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[personal accident insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a group
    insurance policy that provides insurance to a natural person

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whereby the insurance company undertakes to pay
      one or more sums of money in the event of bodily injury to, or the
      death of, the person that is caused by an accident, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whereby the insurance company undertakes to pay a
      certain sum for each day that the person is hospitalized in the
      event of bodily injury to the person that is caused by an accident
      or in the event of an illness or disability of the person;
      ([assurance accidents corporels]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[small business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[small business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a business that is or,
    if it were incorporated, would be a small business corporation
    within the meaning of subsection 248(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); ([petite entreprise]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[travel insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[travel insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a policy of an insurance company that provides
      insurance to a natural person in respect of a trip by the person
      away from the place where the person ordinarily resides, without
      any individual assessment of risk, against

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} loss that results from the cancellation or
        interruption of the trip,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} loss of, or damage to, personal property that
        occurs while on the trip, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} loss that is caused by the delayed arrival of
        personal baggage while on the trip, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a group insurance policy that provides insurance
      to a natural person in respect of a trip by the person away from
      the province in which the person ordinarily resides

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} against expenses incurred while on the trip
        that result from an illness or the disability of the person that
        occurs on the trip,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} against expenses incurred while on the trip
        that result from bodily injury to, or the death of, the person
        caused by an accident while on the trip,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} whereby the insurance company undertakes to
        pay one or more sums of money in the event of an illness or the
        disability of the person that occurs on the trip, or of bodily
        injury to, or the death of, the person that is caused by an
        accident while on the trip,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} against expenses incurred by the person for
        dental care necessitated by an accident while on the trip, or

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in the event that the person dies while on the
        trip, against expenses incurred for the return of that person's
        remains to the place where the person was ordinarily resident
        before death, or for travel expenses incurred by a relative of
        that person who must travel to identify that person's remains.
        ([assurance voyage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-190, s. 1
- SOR/2002-269, s. 3
- SOR/2011-183, s. 1

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
these Regulations, a web page is not a bank web page by reason only that
the web page provides access to a bank web page or promotes the business
of a bank in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-183, s. 2
:::

## [Permitted Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944615 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A bank may carry on any
aspect of the business of insurance, other than the underwriting of
insurance, outside Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-183, s. 3
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A bank
  may administer an authorized type of insurance and personal accident
  insurance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A bank may administer a group insurance policy for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its employees or the employees of an entity that
    the bank holds control of, or a substantial investment in, under
    section 468 of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the employees of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} another bank or a bank holding company, that
      controls the bank, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an entity that the other bank or the bank
      holding company, referred to in subparagraph (i), holds control
      of, or a substantial investment in, under section 468 or 930 of
      the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the employees of an entity that an affiliate of the
    bank holds control of, or a substantial investment in, in accordance
    with section 522.07 or 522.08 or subsection 522.32(1) or (3) of the
    Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the employees in Canada of an authorized foreign
    bank that is an affiliate of the bank; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the bank is controlled by a foreign bank, the
    employees in Canada of an affiliate of the foreign bank that is an
    entity referred to in section 522.17 or 522.18 of the Act and that
    carries on a business described in one of those sections.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-269, s. 4
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A bank
  may provide advice regarding an authorized type of insurance or a
  service in respect of an authorized type of insurance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A bank may provide advice in respect of an insurance
  policy that is not of an authorized type of insurance, or services in
  respect thereof, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the advice is general in nature;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the advice is not in respect of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a specific risk, proposal in respect of life
      insurance, insurance policy or service, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a particular insurance company, agent or broker;
      and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the bank does not thereby refer any person to a
    particular insurance company, agent or broker.

## [Promotion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944639 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A bank shall not, in
relation to its business in Canada, promote an insurance company, agent
or broker unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company, agent or broker deals only in authorized
  types of insurance; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the promotion takes place outside a branch of the
  bank and is directed to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all the holders of credit or charge cards issued by
    the bank who regularly receive statements of account or notices of
    those statements,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all the bank's customers who are natural persons
    and who regularly receive statements of account or notices of those
    statements, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the general public.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-183, s. 4
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-6-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 6"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A bank
  shall not, in relation to its business in Canada, promote an insurance
  policy of an insurance company, agent or broker, or a service in
  respect of such a policy, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the policy is of an authorized type of insurance or
    the service is in respect of such a policy;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the policy is to be provided by a corporation
    without share capital, other than a mutual insurance company or a
    fraternal benefit society, that carries on business without
    pecuniary gain to its members and the policy provides insurance to a
    natural person in respect of the risks covered by travel insurance;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the policy is a personal accident insurance policy
    and the promotion takes place outside a branch of the bank;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the service is in respect of a policy referred to
    in paragraph (b) or of a policy referred to in paragraph (c) that is
    promoted as described in that paragraph; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the promotion takes place outside a branch of the
    bank and is directed to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all the holders of credit or charge cards issued
      by the bank who regularly receive statements of account or notices
      of those statements,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all the bank's customers who are natural persons
      and who regularly receive statements of account or notices of
      those statements, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the general public.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1) and section 6, a bank
  may exclude from a promotion referred to in paragraph (1)(e) or 6(b)
  persons

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of whom the promotion would contravene
    an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} who have notified the bank in writing that they do
    not wish to receive promotional material from the bank; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} who are holders of a credit or charge card that was
    issued by the bank and in respect of which the account is not in
    good standing.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-171, s. 3(E)
- SOR/2011-183, s. 5
:::

## [Web Promotion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944664 .Part}

- **[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  promotion referred to in paragraph 6(b) may take place on a bank web
  page if it relates to an insurance company, agent or broker that deals
  only in authorized types of insurance and the promotion referred to in
  paragraph 7(1)(c) or (e) may take place on a bank web page if it
  relates to only an authorized type of insurance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, a bank shall not, on a bank web page,
  provide access to a web page --- directly or through another web page
  --- through which there is promotion of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an insurance company, agent or broker that does not
    deal only in authorized types of insurance; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an insurance policy of an insurance company, agent
    or broker, or a service in respect of such a policy, that is not of
    only an authorized type of insurance.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-183, s. 6
:::

## [Prohibited Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944672 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No bank
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide, directly or indirectly, an insurance
    company, agent or broker with any information respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a customer of the bank in Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an employee of a customer of the bank in Canada,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the bank is an entity with
      members in Canada, any such member, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the bank is a partnership
      with partners in Canada, any such partner;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} permit any of its subsidiaries to provide, directly
    or indirectly, an insurance company, agent or broker with any
    information that it receives from the bank respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a customer of the bank in Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an employee of a customer of the bank in Canada,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the bank is an entity with
      members in Canada, any such member, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the bank is a partnership
      with partners in Canada, any such partner;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} provide an affiliate of the bank that is not a
    subsidiary of the bank with information unless the bank ensures that
    the affiliate does not directly or indirectly provide the
    information to an insurance company, agent or broker if the
    information is in respect of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a customer of the bank in Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an employee of a customer of the bank in Canada,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the bank is an entity with
      members in Canada, any such member,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the bank is a partnership
      with partners in Canada, any such partner; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} permit a subsidiary of the bank that is a trust or
    loan corporation incorporated by or under an Act of the legislature
    of a province to provide, directly or indirectly, an insurance
    company, agent or broker with any information respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a customer of the subsidiary in Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an employee of a customer of the subsidiary in
      Canada,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the subsidiary is an entity
      with members in Canada, any such member, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the subsidiary is a
      partnership with partners in Canada, any such partner.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a bank or
  a subsidiary of a bank that is a trust or loan corporation
  incorporated by or under an Act of the legislature of a province where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the bank or the subsidiary has established
    procedures to ensure that the information referred to in that
    subsection will not be used by an insurance company, agent or broker
    to promote in Canada the insurance company, agent or broker or to
    promote in Canada an insurance policy, or a service in respect
    thereof; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the insurance company, agent or broker, as the case
    may be, has given an undertaking to the bank or to the subsidiary,
    in a form acceptable to the Superintendent, that that information
    will not be used to promote in Canada the insurance company, agent
    or broker or to promote in Canada an insurance policy, or a service
    in respect thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-269, s. 5
:::

- **[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  bank holding company shall control a trust or loan corporation
  incorporated by or under an Act of the legislature of a province if
  the trust or loan corporation directly or indirectly provides an
  insurance company, agent or broker with any information in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a customer of the trust or loan corporation in
    Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an employee in Canada of a customer of the trust or
    loan corporation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the trust or loan corporation
    is an entity with members in Canada, any such member; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the trust or loan corporation
    is a partnership with partners in Canada, any such partner.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a bank
  holding company or a trust or loan corporation if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the bank holding company or the trust or loan
    corporation has established procedures to ensure that the
    information referred to in that subsection will not be used by an
    insurance company, agent or broker to promote in Canada

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the insurance company, agent or broker, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an insurance policy or a service in respect of
      an insurance policy; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the insurance company, agent or broker, as the case
    may be, has given an undertaking to the bank holding company or to
    the trust or loan corporation, in a form acceptable to the
    Superintendent, that the information will not be used to promote in
    Canada

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the insurance company, agent or broker, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an insurance policy or a service in respect of
      an insurance policy.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-269, s. 6
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No bank shall provide a
telecommunications device that is primarily for the use of customers in
Canada and that links a customer with an insurance company, agent or
broker.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A bank shall not carry on
business in Canada on premises that are adjacent to the premises of an
insurance company, agent or broker unless the bank clearly indicates to
its customers that its business and the premises on which its business
is carried on are separate and distinct from those of the insurance
company, agent or broker.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-183, s. 7
:::

## [Transitional]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944719 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Where a bank administered
a policy on May 6, 1992 the administration of which is not permitted
under section 4, the bank may continue to administer the policy in
respect of any person in respect of whom coverage was provided under the
policy on that date.
