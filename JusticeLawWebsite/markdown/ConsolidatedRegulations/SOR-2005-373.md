Political Activities Regulations
::::

Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote

Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-373).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721147 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Candidacy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721149 .Part}

### [Content of a Request]{.HTitleText2} {#h-721150 .Subheading}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  request for permission, referred to in subsection 114(1) or (2) or
  115(1) of the Act, and a request for leave of absence without pay,
  referred to in subsection 114(3) of the Act, must be in writing and
  must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the duties that the employee
    performs on a permanent and temporary basis,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the nature of their duties, including their
      position title,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of their duties, approved
      by the relevant organization,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the location of their place of work, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the level and visibility of their position and
      duties; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the election and, if applicable, the
    electoral district.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may, in addition to the information
  referred to in subsection (1), request from the employee or relevant
  organization any other information that is necessary to determine
  whether seeking nomination or being a candidate impairs or is
  perceived as impairing the employee's ability to perform their duties
  in a politically impartial manner.

### [Decision]{.HTitleText2} {#h-721163 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** In making a decision in
accordance with sections 114 and 115 of the Act, the Commission must
review and analyse any information that is requested from the employee
and the relevant organization.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Investigations of Allegations of Improper Political Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721170 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-721171 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies in
respect of investigations under sections 118 and 119 of the Act.

### [Format of Allegation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-721173 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  allegation that an employee has failed to comply with any of
  subsections 113(1), 114(1) to (3) and 115(1) of the Act or that a
  deputy head has contravened section 117 of the Act may be made by
  submitting the allegation to the Commission in writing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Failure to submit an allegation in writing does not
  prevent the Commission from investigating the allegation if,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an allegation against an employee,
    the political activity alleged to be improper could impair or be
    perceived as impairing the ability of that employee to perform their
    duties in a politically impartial manner; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an allegation against a deputy head,
    there is reason to believe that the deputy head has contravened
    section 117 of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2012-239, s. 3

### [Time Frame for Submitting Allegations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-721181 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  allegation must be submitted to the Commission within the following
  time frame:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an alleged contravention that began
    during an election period, within 30 days after the day on which the
    election period ended; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an alleged contravention that began
    outside an election period, within 30 days after the day on which
    the person making the allegation became aware of the alleged
    contravention but no later than one year after the day on which the
    contravention began.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Failure to make an allegation within the time frame
  prescribed in subsection (1) does not prevent the Commission from
  investigating the allegation if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an allegation against an employee,
    the political activity alleged to be improper could impair or be
    perceived as impairing the ability of that employee to perform their
    duties in a politically impartial manner; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an allegation against a deputy head,
    there is reason to believe that the deputy head has contravened
    section 117 of the Act.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), an allegation is
  deemed to have been submitted on the day that is,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the allegation is sent by mail, six days after

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date of the postmark or the date of the
      postage meter impression authorized by Canada Post Corporation, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if both the postmark and the postage meter
      impression appear on the envelope, the date of the later of them;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the allegation is delivered by hand or by
    courier service, the day on which it is delivered; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the allegation is transmitted by electronic
    means, the day on which it is transmitted.

- SOR/2012-239, s. 4

### [Investigation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-721197 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If the Commission decides
to investigate an allegation, it shall inform the person who made the
allegation and

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an allegation against an employee, the
  employee and the deputy head of the organization that employs the
  employee; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an allegation against a deputy head,
  the deputy head and the Clerk of the Privy Council.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** If the Commission decides
not to investigate an allegation, it shall inform the person who made
the allegation of its decision and the reasons for it.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** If the Commission decides
to investigate an allegation, the Commission shall inform both the
person who made the allegation and the employee or deputy head against
whom the allegation is made that they may be heard and shall inform them
as to whether submissions are to be made orally or in writing.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The withdrawal of an
allegation by the person making it does not prevent the Commission from
continuing its investigation if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an allegation against an employee, the
  political activity alleged to be improper could impair or be perceived
  as impairing the ability of that employee to perform their duties in a
  politically impartial manner; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an allegation against a deputy head,
  there is reason to believe that the deputy head has contravened
  section 117 of the Act.

<!-- -->

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  allegation is made against an employee, the Commission shall, in
  writing with reasons for its decision, inform the person who made the
  allegation, the employee and the deputy head of the organization that
  employs that employee as to whether the allegation is substantiated
  and of any corrective action that the Commission considers appropriate
  to be taken or of its decision to dismiss the employee.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an allegation is made against a deputy head, the
  Commission shall, in writing with reasons for its decision, inform the
  person who made the allegation, that deputy head and the Clerk of the
  Privy Council as to whether the allegation is substantiated.

### [Disclosure of Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-721209 .Subheading}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may disclose personal information obtained in the conduct
  of an investigation under section 118 or 119 of the Act if the
  disclosure would

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} promote political impartiality in the public
    service;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} promote accountability;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ensure that action is taken to correct improper
    political activity on the part of employees or deputy heads or
    prevent the recurrence of such activity; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} encourage the adoption or continuance of proper
    practices regarding political activity on the part of employees or
    deputy heads.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Prior to a disclosure under subsection (1) that could
  infringe privacy interests, the Commission shall consider whether the
  public interest in disclosure outweighs those privacy interests.
