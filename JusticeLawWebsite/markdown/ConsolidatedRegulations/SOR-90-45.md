## Order Fixing the Boundaries of the Town of Banff in Banff National Park and Adding a Description of the Boundaries as Schedule IV to the Act {#order-fixing-the-boundaries-of-the-town-of-banff-in-banff-national-park-and-adding-a-description-of-the-boundaries-as-schedule-iv-to-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-45
:::

[CANADA NATIONAL PARKS ACT](/eng/acts/N-14.01)

Registration 1989-12-27

Order Fixing the Boundaries of the Town of Banff in Banff National Park
and Adding a Description of the Boundaries as Schedule IV to the Act

P.C. 1989-2522  1989-12-21
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas a public hearing in the town of Banff in relation to the
boundaries thereof was held on August 31, 1989;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to
section 8.1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-90-45_e_hq_6117){#fn_SOR-90-45_e_hq_6117-ID0EDDAA}
of the National Parks Act, is pleased hereby

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-90-45_e_hq_6117-ID0EDDAA){#fn_SOR-90-45_e_hq_6117}R.S., c.
  39 (4th Supp.), s. 7
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to fix the boundaries of the town of Banff in
  accordance with the schedule hereto, marked Schedule IV; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to add that schedule as Schedule IV to the National
  Parks Act.
:::

::::: section
:::: {#926375 .Schedule}
::: {}
## [SCHEDULE IV]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 8.1)]{.OriginatingRef}[The Town of Banff]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-926376 .scheduleLabel}
:::

#### [Banff National Park]{.HTitleText2} {#banff-national-park .SchedHeadL2}

All that parcel of land in Banff National Park in the Province of
Alberta, shown as the town of Banff on Plan Number 72468 in the Canada
Lands Survey Records at Ottawa, a copy of which is filed in the Land
Titles Office in Calgary under Number LTO 8911788, said parcel
containing 487 hectares, more or less.
