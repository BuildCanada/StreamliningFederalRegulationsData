## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-799904 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods for which preferential
    tariff treatment under the CEFTA is claimed.
    ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Origin Determination]{.HTitleText1} {#h-799908 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** On receipt of an opinion as
to whether the goods are originating within the meaning of Annex C of
CEFTA, an officer shall review the opinion, including the rationale for
it and any relevant supporting documents, to determine if the goods are
originating.

## [Method of Sending Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-799910 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The request to the customs
administration of the EFTA state of export referred to in section
42.1(1.1) of the Act shall be sent by any method that produces
confirmation of receipt.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-799912 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
deemed to have come into force on July 1, 2009.
