## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1534029 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Origin of goods

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Order, the origin of goods is determined in accordance with the rules of
origin set out in the [Determination of Country of Origin for the
Purpose of Marking Goods (CUSMA Countries)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-23) or the Determination of Country
of Origin for the Purpose of Marking Goods (Non-CUSMA Countries)
Regulations, as the case may be.

## [Surtax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1534032 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surtax --- countries with no trade
agreement

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 3, goods that are classified under a tariff item set out in
  column 4 of Schedule 1 and do not originate in a country listed in
  Schedule 3, as well as those that are otherwise classifiable under one
  of those tariff items but are classified under a tariff item of
  Chapter 99 of the List of Tariff Provisions, are subject to a surtax
  in the amount of 50% of their value for duty determined in accordance
  with sections 47 to 55 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of goods of the same class imported
    during the same quarterly period exceeds the total set out in column
    2 for that class; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of goods of the same class that
    originate in the same country and are imported during the same
    quarterly period exceeds the quantity determined by multiplying the
    percentage set out in column 3 by the total set out in column 2 for
    that class.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transitional measures

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} For the period beginning on August 1, 2025 and
  ending on September 25, 2025, the surtax referred to in subsection (1)
  applies if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of goods of the same class imported
    during that period exceeds the quantity determined by the formula

    ::: {#1535184 .Paragraph}
    A − B

    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is the quarterly total set out in column 2 of Schedule 1 for
        goods of the same class, and

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is the quantity of goods of the same class that are imported
        during the period beginning on June 27, 2025 and ending on July
        31, 2025 under a permit that is issued under subsection 8(1) of
        the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19) in respect
        of steel goods referred to in item 82 of the [Import Control
        List](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._604); or
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of goods of the same class that
    originate in the same country and are imported during the period
    beginning on June 27, 2025 and ending on September 25, 2025 under a
    permit that is issued under subsection 8(1) of the [Export and
    Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19) in respect of steel goods
    referred to in item 82 of the [Import Control
    List](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._604) exceeds the quantity
    determined by multiplying the percentage set out in column 3 of
    Schedule 1 by the quarterly total set out in column 2 of that
    Schedule for that class.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quarterly period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a quarterly
  period is a three-month period, the first of which begins on the day
  on which this Order comes into force.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2025-155, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Import permit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Goods are considered to be imported in excess of the
  limits set out in subsection (1) if they are not imported under a
  permit that is issued under subsection 8(1) of the [Export and Import
  Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19) in respect of steel goods referred to in
  item 82 of the Import Control List and that is valid at the time at
  which the goods are accounted for under subsection 32(1), (3) or (5)
  of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surtax --- countries with trade agreement

- **[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 3, goods that are
  classified under a tariff item set out in column 4 of Schedule 2 and
  originate in a country listed in Schedule 3, as well as those that are
  otherwise classifiable under one of those tariff items but are
  classified under a tariff item of Chapter 99 of the List of Tariff
  Provisions, are subject to a surtax in the amount of 50% of their
  value for duty determined in accordance with sections 47 to 55 of the
  [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of goods of the same class that are
    imported during the same quarterly period exceeds the quarterly
    total set out in column 2 of Schedule 2 for that class; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of goods of the same class that
    originate in the same country and are imported during the same
    quarterly period exceeds the quantity determined by multiplying the
    percentage set out in column 3 of Schedule 2 by the quarterly total
    set out in column 2 of that Schedule for that class.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transitional measures

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the period beginning on August 1, 2025 and ending
  on September 25, 2025, the surtax referred to in subsection (1)
  applies if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of goods of the same class imported
    during that period exceeds the total set out in column 2.1 of
    Schedule 2 for that class; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of goods of the same class that
    originate in the same country and are imported during that period
    exceeds the quantity determined by multiplying the percentage set
    out in column 3 of Schedule 2 by the total set out in column 2.1 of
    that Schedule for that class.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quarterly period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a quarterly
  period is a three-month period, the first of which begins on September
  26, 2025.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Import permit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Goods are considered to be imported in excess of the
  limits set out in subsection (1) if they are not imported under a
  permit that is issued under subsection 8(1) of the [Export and Import
  Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19) in respect of steel goods referred to in
  item 82 of the Import Control List and that is valid at the time at
  which the goods are accounted for under subsection 32(1), (3) or (5)
  of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following goods are not
subject to the surtax and are excluded from the determination of the
quantity of goods imported:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods referred to in subsections 2(1) and (1.1) that
  are in transit to Canada on or before June 27, 2025;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} goods referred to in subsections 2.1(1) and (2)
  that are in transit to Canada on or before August 1, 2025;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods originating in Canada, the United States or
  Mexico;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[casual goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
  section 2 of the [Persons Authorized to Account for Casual Goods
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-418); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} goods that are classified under a tariff item of
  Chapter 98 of the List of Tariff Provisions, even if the goods are
  otherwise classifiable under a tariff item set out in column 4 of
  Schedule 1 or in column 4 of Schedule 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other surtaxes

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** Despite any other order
made under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), if a good that is
subject to a surtax under this Order is also subject to any other surtax
under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), only the surtax under
this Order is applicable to the good.
