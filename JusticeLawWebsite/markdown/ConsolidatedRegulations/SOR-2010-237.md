## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765660 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cooperative Credit Associations
Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01).

## [Exemption Circumstances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765662 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
section 487.14 of the Act, the prescribed circumstances are that the
exemption does not prejudice any of the members or the shareholders or
the public interest.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765664 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on June 1, 2011.
