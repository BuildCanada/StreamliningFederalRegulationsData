## Government Property Traffic Regulations {#government-property-traffic-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 887
:::

[GOVERNMENT PROPERTY TRAFFIC ACT](/eng/acts/G-6)

Regulations Respecting Government Property Traffic
::::

:::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581438 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Government Property Traffic
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._887).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581440 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commissioner of the
    Royal Canadian Mounted Police; ([commissaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Confederation Bridge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Confederation Bridge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the bridge and
    other real property situate, lying and being upon the lands and
    lands covered by water described in Schedule B, as amended from time
    to time, to a lease document registered on October 5, 1993 as
    document 3562 in the Prince County Registry Office in the province
    of Prince Edward Island. ([pont de la
    Confédération]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[constable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[constable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a member of a provincial or municipal police
      force, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person authorized to enforce the Regulations
      by a Minister or the Minister's Deputy, Assistant Deputy or Acting
      Deputy, or by the Commissioner or any officer referred to in
      subsection 6(1) of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Act; ([agent
      de sûreté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any of the departments named in Schedule A to the
      [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11),

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any Crown corporation as defined in section 83 of
      the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other division or branch of the public
      service of Canada; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[driver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[driver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is driving or is
    in actual physical control of a vehicle;
    ([conducteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[government property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[government property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means property owned or
    occupied by Her Majesty in right of Canada; ([terrain du
    gouvernement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[highway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[highway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any highway, road, street,
    avenue, parkway, driveway, square, place, bridge, viaduct or trestle
    designed and intended for, or used for the passage of vehicles;
    ([route]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a department mentioned in
      Schedule A to the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11),
      the Minister presiding over the department,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a Crown corporation, the
      president or other chief executive officer thereof, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to any other division or branch of
      the public service of Canada, the person who under the [Financial
      Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) is the appropriate Minister;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the standing of a vehicle,
    whether occupied or not, except for the purpose of and while
    actually engaged in loading or unloading passengers or goods;
    ([mettre en stationnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[South Klondike Highway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[South Klondike Highway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that portion of
    the Klondike Highway that is situated in the Province of British
    Columbia between 60° North Latitude 134°40' West Longitude on the
    British Columbia and Yukon boundary and 59°39' North Latitude 135°9'
    West Longitude on the British Columbia and Alaska boundary; ([route
    Klondike sud]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device in, upon, or by which
    a person or property is or may be transported or drawn upon a
    highway, except a device used exclusively upon stationary rails or
    tracks. ([véhicule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-93, s. 1
- SOR/89-341, s. 1
- SOR/90-860, s. 1
- SOR/95-174, s. 1
- SOR/97-277, s. 1
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581464 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
government property and the highways thereon except

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the National Parks;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} the Jacques Cartier and Champlain bridges that
  cross the St. Lawrence River, together with all their approaches,
  including the portion of the Bonaventure Autoroute owned by Her
  Majesty in right of Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/84-238, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} territorial lands as defined in the [Territorial
  Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Indian reserves;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} property under the control of the National Capital
  Commission; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} property to which the [Airport Traffic
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._886) or the [Traffic on the
  Land Side of Airports Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-102)
  apply.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/84-238, s. 1
- SOR/89-341, s. 2
- SOR/2006-102, s. 33
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to a member of the Canadian Forces when operating with due
authority a vehicle owned by Her Majesty in right of Canada.

## [Registration and Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581476 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as provided in subsections (2) and (3), no person shall operate a
  vehicle on a highway unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} he holds all licences and permits that he is, by
    the laws of the province and the municipality in which the highway
    is situated, required to hold in order to operate the vehicle in
    that province and municipality; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle is registered and equipped as required
    by the laws of the province and the municipality in which the
    highway is situated.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall operate a vehicle on the South
  Klondike Highway unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person holds all licences and permits that he
    is, by the laws of the Yukon Territory, required to hold in order to
    operate the vehicle in the Yukon Territory; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle is registered and equipped as required
    by the laws of the Yukon Territory.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall operate a vehicle on the
  Confederation Bridge unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person holds all licences and permits that the
    person is, by the laws of the province of Prince Edward Island,
    required to hold in order to operate the vehicle in the province of
    Prince Edward Island; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle is registered and equipped as required
    by the laws of the province of Prince Edward Island.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-93, s. 2
- SOR/97-277, s. 2
:::

## [Compliance with Provincial and Municipal Laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581489 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as provided in subsections (2) and (3), no person shall operate a
  vehicle on a highway otherwise than in accordance with the laws of the
  province and the municipality in which the highway is situated.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall operate a vehicle on the South
  Klondike Highway otherwise than in accordance with the laws of the
  Yukon Territory.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall operate a vehicle on the
  Confederation Bridge otherwise than in accordance with the laws of the
  province of Prince Edward Island.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), in the event of
  any inconsistency between the provisions of the [Government Property
  Traffic Act](/eng/acts/G-6) and these Regulations and the provisions
  of the laws of the province and the municipality in which the highway
  is situated, the provisions of the [Government Property Traffic
  Act](/eng/acts/G-6) and these Regulations prevail to the extent of the
  inconsistency.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), in the event of
  any inconsistency between the provisions of the [Government Property
  Traffic Act](/eng/acts/G-6) and these Regulations and the provisions
  of the laws of the Yukon Territory, the provisions of the [Government
  Property Traffic Act](/eng/acts/G-6) and these Regulations prevail to
  the extent of the inconsistency.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (3), in the event of
  any inconsistency between the provisions of the [Government Property
  Traffic Act](/eng/acts/G-6) and these Regulations and the provisions
  of the laws of the province of Prince Edward Island, the provisions of
  the [Government Property Traffic Act](/eng/acts/G-6) and these
  Regulations prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-93, s. 3
- SOR/97-277, s. 3
:::

## [Traffic Signs and Devices]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581499 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Public Works, the Minister of any department having the
  control or management of any government property, or the Commissioner
  may mark or erect or cause to be marked or erected on any government
  property, traffic signs or devices

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing rate of speed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} regulating or prohibiting parking and designating
    parking areas;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing load limits for any vehicle or class of
    vehicles;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prohibiting or regulating the use of any highway by
    any vehicle or class of vehicles;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} designating any highway as a one-way highway;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} for stopping vehicles;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} for regulating pedestrian traffic; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} for directing or controlling in any other manner
    traffic on government property.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as authorized by subsection (1), no person
  shall mark or erect any traffic sign or device on government property.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person, other than the Minister of Public Works,
  the Minister of any department having the control or management of
  government property or the Commissioner, shall, without the authority
  of such Minister or the Commissioner, remove or deface any traffic
  sign or device on such property.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Any traffic sign or device
on government property bearing the words "Government of Canada" or an
abbreviation thereof, or purporting to have been erected by or under the
authority of the Minister of Public Works, any other Minister or the
Commissioner, shall [prima facie]{.otherLang lang="la"} be deemed to
have been erected pursuant to these Regulations.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The driver of a vehicle on
a highway shall obey the instructions of any traffic sign or device
applicable to that driver, vehicle or highway.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Any traffic sign or
device on government property marked or erected prior to September 17,
1952 under An Act to Provide for the Regulation of Vehicular Traffic on
Dominion Property or the regulations made thereunder or under other
lawful authority shall be deemed to have been marked or erected pursuant
to these Regulations.

## [Traffic Direction and Control]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581515 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The driver of a vehicle
on a highway shall comply with any traffic directions given to him by a
constable.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Every person on
government property shall produce to a constable upon demand

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any permit issued to him under these Regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any licence or permit he holds authorizing him to
  drive a vehicle; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any certificate of registration of a vehicle held by
  him.

<!-- -->

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as provided in subsections (2) and (2.1), every driver of a vehicle
  who is directly or indirectly involved in an accident on government
  property shall forthwith report the accident as required by the laws
  of the province in which the accident occurred.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every driver of a vehicle who is directly or
  indirectly involved in an accident on the South Klondike Highway shall
  forthwith report the accident as required by the laws of the Yukon
  Territory.

- [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} Every driver of a vehicle who is directly or
  indirectly involved in an accident on the Confederation Bridge shall
  forthwith report the accident as required by the laws of the province
  of Prince Edward Island.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the driver of a vehicle is involved in an
  accident referred to in any of subsections (1) to (2.1) and any
  property of Her Majesty is damaged by the accident, the driver shall
  forthwith report the accident to a member of the Royal Canadian
  Mounted Police or to the person in charge, control or occupation of
  the property so damaged.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-93, s. 4
- SOR/97-277, s. 4
:::

## [Parking]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581528 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** No person shall park a
vehicle in any area designated by a sign as an area in which parking is
prohibited.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Where an area is by sign
designated as an area where parking is reserved for persons holding
permits or is designated as an area where parking is prohibited except
under a permit, no person shall park a vehicle in the area unless there
is attached to and exposed on that vehicle a label furnished with the
permit and displayed in accordance with the terms of the permit
authorizing that vehicle to be parked in that area.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Where an area is by sign
designated as an area where parking is permitted for a period of time,
no person shall park a vehicle in the area for any greater period of
time than that indicated on the sign.

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an area is by sign designated as an area where parking is reserved for
  a class of persons, other than disabled persons, no person shall park
  a vehicle in the area unless the person is a member of that class.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an area is by sign designated as an area where
  parking is reserved for disabled persons, no person shall park a
  vehicle in the area unless there is displayed on the vehicle a valid
  parking permit for disabled persons issued by a provincial or
  municipal authority.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-398, s. 1
:::

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  Minister, or a person authorized by a Minister, may issue permits and
  furnish labels, for the purposes of section 15, in respect of lands
  under the Minister's administration.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister or person who issues a permit under
  subsection (1) may revoke that permit.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless sooner revoked, a permit issued under these
  Regulations is valid for the period stated thereon, and a label
  furnished with the permit is valid only during the period that the
  permit is valid.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-517, s. 1
:::

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A constable who finds a
vehicle parked in contravention of these Regulations may, in addition or
as an alternative to any other action that may be taken under these
Regulations, remove the vehicle and, if the constable deems it necessary
to protect the vehicle or the interests of the owner, store the vehicle
in a suitable place.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-517, s. 2
- SOR/95-174, s. 2(F)
- SOR/2011-43, s. 1
:::

## [Speed]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581546 .Part}

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** No person shall drive a
vehicle on a highway at a rate of speed in excess of the speed limit
indicated for the highway by a sign.

## [Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581548 .Part}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes any provision of these Regulations, or operates a vehicle on
a highway in contravention of a provision of any applicable provincial
or municipal law referred to in section 5 or 6, is liable on summary
conviction to a fine not exceeding \$500 or to a term of imprisonment
not exceeding six months, or to both.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-860, s. 2
- SOR/94-517, s. 3
- SOR/97-277, s. 5
:::

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding section 21, any
  person who is alleged to have contravened a provision of these
  Regulations governing the parking of a vehicle may, at any time within
  15 days, excluding holidays, after the day on which the contravention
  is alleged to have occurred, register a plea of guilty to the alleged
  offence by paying into court, either personally or by mail, the amount
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$25 in the case of a contravention of section 14,
    15 or 16 or subsection 17(1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$50 in the case of a contravention of subsection
    17(2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any amount paid pursuant to subsection (1) shall be
  paid into the court designated by the Minister for such payment.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a payment referred to in subsection (1) is sent
  to the court by mail, the payment into court shall be deemed to have
  been made on the day on which the payment was mailed.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, where,
  pursuant to subsection (1), a payment into court is made by or on
  behalf of any person who is alleged to have violated these
  Regulations, such payment shall be deemed to be in full satisfaction
  of any penalty that may be imposed upon the summary conviction of that
  person for that alleged violation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/80-619, s. 1
- SOR/90-860, s. 3
- SOR/96-398, s. 2
:::

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in these
Regulations shall be construed to authorize a member of a provincial or
municipal police force to enter any defence establishment except in the
manner set out in the [Defence Controlled Access Area
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-957).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-517, s. 4
:::
