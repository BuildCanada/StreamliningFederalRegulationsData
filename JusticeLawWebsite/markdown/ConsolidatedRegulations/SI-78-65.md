## Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development Authority to Prescribe Fees Order {#minister-of-indian-affairs-and-northern-development-authority-to-prescribe-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/78-65
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1978-04-26

Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development Authority to
Prescribe Fees Order

P.C. 1978-1008 1978-04-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to authorize the
Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, by order, to
prescribe fees or charges to recover the costs of providing maps,
navigation charts and interpretive publications required by national
parks users.
