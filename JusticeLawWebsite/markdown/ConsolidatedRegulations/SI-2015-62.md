## Order Designating the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to be the Minister referred to in the Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-public-safety-and-emergency-preparedness-to-be-the-minister-referred-to-in-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2015-62
:::

[PREVENTION OF TERRORIST TRAVEL ACT](/eng/acts/P-19.45)

Registration 2015-07-15

Order Designating the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
Preparedness to be the Minister referred to in the Act

P.C. 2015-1048 2015-06-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the [Prevention of
Terrorist Travel Act](/eng/acts/P-19.45)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-859-E_hq_13947){#fn_81000-3-859-E_hq_13947-ID0EBBAA},
designates the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness, who
is a minister of the Crown, to be the Minister referred to in that Act.
