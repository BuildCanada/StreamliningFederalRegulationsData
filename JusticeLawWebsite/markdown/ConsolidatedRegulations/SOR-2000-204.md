## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656998 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656999 .Subheading}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Nuclear Safety and Control
    Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a document issued by the
    Commission or by a designated officer authorized under paragraph
    37(2)(b) of the Act, indicating that a person is certified.
    ([attestation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[certified]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certified]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means certified by the Commission
    under paragraph 21(1)(i) of the Act or by a designated officer
    authorized under paragraph 37(2)(b) of the Act. ([version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Class I nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class I nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Class IA
    nuclear facility and a Class IB nuclear facility. ([installation
    nucléaire de catégorie I]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Class IA nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class IA nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the
    following nuclear facilities:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a nuclear fission or fusion reactor or
      subcritical nuclear assembly; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vehicle that is equipped with a nuclear
      reactor. ([installation nucléaire de catégorie
      IA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Class IB nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class IB nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the
    following nuclear facilities:

    :   - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a facility that includes a particle
          accelerator, other than a particle accelerator described in
          paragraphs (d) and (e) of the definition [[Class II prescribed
          equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 1 of the [Class II
          Nuclear Facilities and Prescribed Equipment
          Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-205);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a plant for the processing, reprocessing or
      separation of an isotope of uranium, thorium or plutonium;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a plant for the manufacture of a product from
      uranium, thorium or plutonium;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a plant, other than a Class II nuclear facility
      as defined in section 1 of the [Class II Nuclear Facilities and
      Prescribed Equipment Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-205),
      for the processing or use, in a quantity greater than 10^15^ Bq
      per calendar year, of nuclear substances other than uranium,
      thorium or plutonium;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a facility for the disposal of a nuclear
      substance generated at another nuclear facility; and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a facility prescribed by paragraph 19(a) or (b)
      of the [General Nuclear Safety and Control
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202). ([installation
      nucléaire de catégorie IB]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[effective dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[effective dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 1(1) of the [Radiation Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-203). ([dose
    efficace]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[equivalent dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[equivalent dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 1(1) of the [Radiation Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-203). ([dose
    équivalente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[exclusion zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exclusion zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a parcel of land within
    or surrounding a nuclear facility on which there is no permanent
    dwelling and over which a licensee has the legal authority to
    exercise control. ([zone d\'exclusion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[federal authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Minister of the Crown in right of Canada;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an agency of the Government of Canada or a parent
      Crown corporation, as defined in subsection 83(1) of the
      [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), or any other body
      established under an Act of Parliament that is ultimately
      accountable through a Minister of the Crown in right of Canada to
      Parliament for the conduct of its affairs;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any department or departmental corporation that
      is set out in Schedule I or II to the [Financial Administration
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other body that is set out in Schedule 1 to
      the [Canadian Environmental Assessment Act,
      2012](/eng/acts/C-15.21); and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Executive Council of --- or a minister,
      department, agency or body of the government of --- Yukon, the
      Northwest Territories or Nunavut.

      It does not include a council of the band as defined in subsection
      2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), Export Development Canada
      or the Canada Pension Plan Investment Board. It also does not
      include a Crown corporation that is a wholly-owned subsidiary, as
      defined in subsection 83(1) of the [Financial Administration
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11), or a not-for-profit corporation that enters
      into an agreement under subsection 80(5) of the [Canada Marine
      Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7), that is not set out in Schedule 1 to the
      [Canadian Environmental Assessment Act, 2012](/eng/acts/C-15.21).
      ([autorité fédérale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hazardous substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hazardous substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[hazardous
    waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance or waste, other than a
    nuclear substance, that is used or produced in the course of
    carrying on a licensed activity and that may pose a risk to the
    environment or the health and safety of persons. ([substance
    dangereuse ou déchet dangereux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[IAEA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[IAEA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Atomic Energy
    Agency. ([AIEA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[IAEA Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[IAEA Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement between
    the Government of Canada and the International Atomic Energy Agency
    for the Application of Safeguards in Connection with the Treaty on
    the Non-proliferation of Nuclear Weapons, effective on February 21,
    1972; INFCIRC/164; UNTS vol. 814, R. No. 11596. ([Accord avec
    l\'AIEA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[jurisdiction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[jurisdiction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a federal authority;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any agency or body that is established under an
      Act of Parliament and that has powers, duties or functions in
      relation to an assessment of the environmental effects of the
      preparation of a site for a Class I nuclear facility or its
      construction, operation, decommissioning or abandonment;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the government of a province;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any agency or body that is established under an
      Act of the legislature of a province, and that has powers, duties
      or functions in relation to an assessment of the environmental
      effects of the preparation of a site for a Class I nuclear
      facility or its construction, operation, decommissioning or
      abandonment;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any body that is established under a land claims
      agreement referred to in section 35 of the [Constitution Act,
      1982](/eng/Const/), and that has powers, duties or functions in
      relation to an assessment of the environmental effects of the
      preparation of a site for a Class I nuclear facility or its
      construction, operation, decommissioning or abandonment;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a governing body that is established under
      legislation that relates to the self-government of Indians and
      that has powers, duties or functions in relation to an assessment
      of the environmental effects of the preparation of a site for a
      Class I nuclear facility or its construction, operation,
      decommissioning or abandonment;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a government of a foreign state or of a
      subdivision of a foreign state, or any institution of such a
      government; and

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} an international organization of states or any
      institution of such an organization. ([instance]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[licensed activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensed activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an activity
    described in paragraph 26(e) of the Act that a licence authorizes
    the licensee to carry on in relation to a Class I nuclear facility.
    ([activité autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is licensed to
    carry on an activity described in paragraph 26(e) of the Act in
    relation to a Class I nuclear facility. ([titulaire de
    permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the equipment
    prescribed by section 20 of the [General Nuclear Safety and Control
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202). ([équipement
    réglementé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the information
    prescribed by section 21 of the [General Nuclear Safety and Control
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202). ([renseignements
    réglementés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[safeguards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[safeguards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a verification system that
    is established in accordance with a safeguards agreement.
    ([garanties]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[safeguards agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[safeguards agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the IAEA Agreement and any arrangement between
      Canada and the IAEA made under that agreement; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any agreement to which Canada is a party for the
      establishment in Canada of a verification system in respect of
      nuclear substances, prescribed equipment or prescribed
      information, and any arrangements made under such an agreement.
      ([accord relatif aux garanties]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sealed source]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sealed source]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a radioactive nuclear
    substance in a sealed capsule or in a cover to which the substance
    is bonded, where the capsule or cover is strong enough to prevent
    contact with or the dispersion of the substance under the conditions
    for which the capsule or cover is designed. ([source
    scellée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who performs work that
    is referred to in a licence. ([travailleur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657050 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of Class I nuclear facilities.

## [Licence Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-657052 .Part}

### [General Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657053 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence in respect of a Class I nuclear facility, other than a licence
to abandon, shall contain the following information in addition to the
information required by section 3 of the [General Nuclear Safety and
Control Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the site of the activity to be
  licensed, including the location of any exclusion zone and any
  structures within that zone;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} plans showing the location, perimeter, areas,
  structures and systems of the nuclear facility;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} evidence that the applicant is the owner of the site
  or has authority from the owner of the site to carry on the activity
  to be licensed;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed management system for the activity to be
  licensed, including measures to promote and support safety culture;

- [(d.1)]{.lawlabel} the proposed human performance program for the
  activity to be licensed, including measures to ensure workers' fitness
  for duty.

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name, form, characteristics and quantity of any
  hazardous substances that may be on the site while the activity to be
  licensed is carried on;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the proposed worker health and safety policies and
  procedures;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the proposed environmental protection policies and
  procedures;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the proposed effluent and environmental monitoring
  programs;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the application is in respect of a nuclear
  facility referred to in paragraph 2(b) of the [Nuclear Security
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-209), the information required
  by section 3 of those Regulations;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the proposed program to inform persons living in the
  vicinity of the site of the general nature and characteristics of the
  anticipated effects on the environment and the health and safety of
  persons that may result from the activity to be licensed; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the proposed plan for the decommissioning of the
  nuclear facility or of the site.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-199, s. 4

### [Licence to Prepare Site]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657069 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence to prepare a site for a Class I nuclear facility shall contain
the following information in addition to the information required by
section 3:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the site evaluation process and of
  the investigations and preparatory work that have been and will be
  done on the site and in the surrounding area;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the site\'s susceptibility to human
  activity and natural phenomena, including seismic events, tornadoes
  and floods;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proposed program to determine the environmental
  baseline characteristics of the site and the surrounding area;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed quality assurance program for the design
  of the nuclear facility; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the effects on the environment and the health and
  safety of persons that may result from the activity to be licensed,
  and the measures that will be taken to prevent or mitigate those
  effects.

### [Licence to Construct]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657076 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence to construct a Class I nuclear facility shall contain the
following information in addition to the information required by section
3:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed design of the nuclear
  facility, including the manner in which the physical and environmental
  characteristics of the site are taken into account in the design;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the environmental baseline
  characteristics of the site and the surrounding area;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proposed construction program, including its
  schedule;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the structures proposed to be built
  as part of the nuclear facility, including their design and their
  design characteristics;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the systems and equipment proposed
  to be installed at the nuclear facility, including their design and
  their design operating conditions;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a preliminary safety analysis report demonstrating
  the adequacy of the design of the nuclear facility;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the proposed quality assurance program for the design
  of the nuclear facility;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures to facilitate Canada\'s
  compliance with any applicable safeguards agreement;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the effects on the environment and the health and
  safety of persons that may result from the construction, operation and
  decommissioning of the nuclear facility, and the measures that will be
  taken to prevent or mitigate those effects;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the proposed location of points of release, the
  proposed maximum quantities and concentrations, and the anticipated
  volume and flow rate of releases of nuclear substances and hazardous
  substances into the environment, including their physical, chemical
  and radiological characteristics;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures to control releases of nuclear
  substances and hazardous substances into the environment;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} the proposed program and schedule for recruiting,
  training and qualifying workers in respect of the operation and
  maintenance of the nuclear facility; and

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} a description of any proposed full-scope training
  simulator for the nuclear facility.

### [Licence to Operate]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657091 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence to operate a Class I nuclear facility shall contain the
following information in addition to the information required by section
3:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the structures at the nuclear
  facility, including their design and their design operating
  conditions;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the systems and equipment at the
  nuclear facility, including their design and their design operating
  conditions;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a final safety analysis report demonstrating the
  adequacy of the design of the nuclear facility;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures, policies, methods and
  procedures for operating and maintaining the nuclear facility;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the proposed procedures for handling, storing,
  loading and transporting nuclear substances and hazardous substances;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures to facilitate Canada\'s
  compliance with any applicable safeguards agreement;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the proposed commissioning program for the systems
  and equipment that will be used at the nuclear facility;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the effects on the environment and the health and
  safety of persons that may result from the operation and
  decommissioning of the nuclear facility, and the measures that will be
  taken to prevent or mitigate those effects;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the proposed location of points of release, the
  proposed maximum quantities and concentrations, and the anticipated
  volume and flow rate of releases of nuclear substances and hazardous
  substances into the environment, including their physical, chemical
  and radiological characteristics;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures to control releases of nuclear
  substances and hazardous substances into the environment;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures to prevent or mitigate the
  effects of accidental releases of nuclear substances and hazardous
  substances on the environment, the health and safety of persons and
  the maintenance of national security, including measures to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} assist off-site authorities in planning and
    preparing to limit the effects of an accidental release,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} notify off-site authorities of an accidental
    release or the imminence of an accidental release,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} report information to off-site authorities during
    and after an accidental release,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} assist off-site authorities in dealing with the
    effects of an accidental release, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} test the implementation of the measures to prevent
    or mitigate the effects of an accidental release;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures to prevent acts of sabotage or
  attempted sabotage at the nuclear facility, including measures to
  alert the licensee to such acts;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} the proposed responsibilities of and qualification
  requirements and training program for workers, including the
  procedures for the requalification of workers; and

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} the results that have been achieved in implementing
  the program for recruiting, training and qualifying workers in respect
  of the operation and maintenance of the nuclear facility.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-208, s. 11

### [Licence to Decommission]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657114 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence to decommission a Class I nuclear facility shall contain the
following information in addition to the information required by section
3:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of and the proposed schedule for the
  decommissioning, including the proposed starting date and the expected
  completion date of the decommissioning and the rationale for the
  schedule;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the nuclear substances, hazardous substances, land,
  buildings, structures, systems and equipment that will be affected by
  the decommissioning;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures, methods and procedures for
  carrying on the decommissioning;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures to facilitate Canada\'s
  compliance with any applicable safeguards agreement;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the nature and extent of any radioactive
  contamination at the nuclear facility;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the effects on the environment and the health and
  safety of persons that may result from the decommissioning, and the
  measures that will be taken to prevent or mitigate those effects;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the proposed location of points of release, the
  proposed maximum quantities and concentrations, and the anticipated
  volume and flow rate of releases of nuclear substances and hazardous
  substances into the environment, including their physical, chemical
  and radiological characteristics;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures to control releases of nuclear
  substances and hazardous substances into the environment;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures to prevent or mitigate the
  effects of accidental releases of nuclear substances and hazardous
  substances on the environment, the health and safety of persons and
  the maintenance of national security, including an emergency response
  plan;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the proposed qualification requirements and training
  program for workers; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} a description of the planned state of the site on
  completion of the decommissioning.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-208, s. 12

### [Licence to Abandon]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657129 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence to abandon a Class I nuclear facility shall contain the
following information in addition to the information required by
sections 3 and 4 of the [General Nuclear Safety and Control
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the results of the decommissioning; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the results of the environmental monitoring programs.

### [Periodic Safety Reviews]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657133 .Subheading}

- **[[8.01]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.01
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee who is licensed to
  operate a nuclear power plant must conduct a periodic safety review of
  the nuclear power plant at an interval specified in the licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), [[nuclear power
  plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a nuclear facility consisting of any
  fission-reactor installation that has been constructed to generate
  electricity on a commercial scale.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-199, s. 5
:::

## [Time Lines: Application for Licence to Prepare Site]{.HTitleText1} {#h-657139 .Part}

### [Compliance Verification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657140 .Subheading}

**[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** The Commission shall,
within 60 days after the day on which an application for a licence to
prepare a site for a Class I nuclear facility is received, determine
whether the application contains sufficient detailed information for the
Commission to commence its review.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2012-288, s. 2
:::

### [Time Periods --- Review of Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657144 .Subheading}

**[[8.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.2 .sectionLabel}** The Commission shall,
within five days after the day on which it determines that an
application contains sufficient detailed information for it to commence
its review, give notice of the commencement of its review

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by providing notice in writing to this effect by mail
  or email to the applicant; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by posting notice to this effect on its Internet
  site.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2012-288, s. 2
:::

- **[[8.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission shall render its decision in respect of an application
  within a time period of 24 months from the day on which the notice is
  posted in accordance with paragraph 8.2(b).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following are excluded from the 24-month time
  period:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any period granted by the Commission for the
    preparation and submission of information requested by the
    Commission, which in the opinion of the Commission is necessary to
    complete the review;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any period, not to exceed 30 days following the
    Commission's receipt of a response to the request for information
    referred to in paragraph (a), that the Commission requires to
    determine whether the information requested has been provided and is
    adequate;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any period that is required by any jurisdiction to
    respond to an offer to consult and cooperate made by the Commission
    under section 18 of the [Canadian Environmental Assessment Act,
    2012](/eng/acts/C-15.21) with respect to the Class I nuclear
    facility and, if the offer is accepted by any jurisdiction, any
    period that is required for consultation and cooperation with that
    jurisdiction;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any period that is required to conduct, and render
    a decision on, an environmental assessment of the proposed
    preparation of the site for the Class I nuclear facility, or its
    construction, operation, decommissioning or abandonment, by any
    jurisdiction that is obligated by law to conduct that assessment and
    render a decision; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any period during which the licence application
    review was adjourned under section 14 of the [Canadian Nuclear
    Safety Commission Rules of
    Procedure](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-211).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall give notice of the beginning and
  end of any period that is excluded from the 24 months

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by providing notice in writing to this effect by
    mail or email to the applicant; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by posting notice to this effect on its Internet
    site.

## [Certification of Persons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-657163 .Part}

### [Application for Certification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657164 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  section and sections 10 to 13 do not apply in respect of Class IB
  nuclear facilities.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission or a designated officer authorized
  under paragraph 37(2)(b) of the Act may certify a person referred to
  in paragraph 44(1)(k) of the Act for a position referred to in a
  licence after receiving from the licensee an application stating that
  the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} meets the applicable qualification requirements
    referred to in the licence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has successfully completed the applicable training
    program and examination referred to in the licence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is capable, in the opinion of the licensee, of
    performing the duties of the position.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission or a designated officer authorized
  under paragraph 37(2)(b) of the Act may renew a certification after
  receiving from a licensee an application stating that the certified
  person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has safely and competently performed the duties of
    the position for which the person was certified;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} continues to receive the applicable training
    referred to in the licence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has successfully completed the applicable
    requalification tests referred to in the licence for renewing the
    certification; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is capable, in the opinion of the licensee, of
    performing the duties of the position.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A certification expires five years after the date of
  its issuance or renewal.

### [Application for Examination]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657177 .Subheading}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  licence requires a person to successfully complete an examination
  administered by the Commission in order to be certified, the person
  may take the examination after the Commission receives from the
  licensee an application that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the applicable examination; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the person has successfully
    completed the applicable training program referred to in the
    licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall notify the licensee and the
  person of the examination results.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice of examination results shall include a
  description of the licensee\'s and the person\'s right to be provided
  with an opportunity to be heard in accordance with the procedure
  referred to in section 13.

### [Refusal to Certify]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657185 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission or a designated officer authorized under paragraph 37(2)(b)
  of the Act shall notify a licensee who has applied for the
  certification of a person and the person in respect of whom
  certification is being sought of a proposed decision not to certify
  the person, as well as the basis for the proposed decision, at least
  30 days before refusing to certify the person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice shall include a description of the
  licensee\'s and the person\'s right to be provided with an opportunity
  to be heard in accordance with the procedure referred to in section
  13.

### [Decertification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657189 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission or a designated officer authorized under paragraph 37(2)(b)
  of the Act shall notify a person in respect of whom a certificate has
  been issued and the licensee concerned of a proposed decision to
  decertify the person, as well as the basis for the proposed decision,
  at least 30 days before decertifying the person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice shall include a description of the
  licensee\'s and the person\'s right to be provided with an opportunity
  to be heard in accordance with the procedure referred to in section
  13.

### [Opportunity to Be Heard]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657193 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  licensee or a person referred to in section 10, 11 or 12 has received
  a notice and has requested, within 30 days after the date of receipt
  of the notice, an opportunity to be heard either orally or in writing,
  the licensee or the person shall be provided with such an opportunity
  in accordance with the request.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On completion of a hearing held in accordance with
  subsection (1), the licensee and the person shall be notified of the
  decision and the reasons for it.

## [Records to Be Kept and Retained]{.HTitleText1} {#h-657197 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee shall keep a record of the results of the effluent and
  environmental monitoring programs referred to in the licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee who operates a Class I nuclear
  facility shall keep a record of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} operating and maintenance procedures;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the results of the commissioning program referred
    to in the licence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the results of the inspection and maintenance
    programs referred to in the licence;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the nature and amount of radiation, nuclear
    substances and hazardous substances within the nuclear facility; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the status of each worker\'s qualifications,
    requalification and training, including the results of all tests and
    examinations completed in accordance with the licence.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee who decommissions a Class I nuclear
  facility shall keep a record of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the progress achieved in meeting the schedule for
    the decommissioning;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the implementation and results of the
    decommissioning;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which and the location at which any
    nuclear or hazardous waste is managed, stored, disposed of or
    transferred;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and quantity of any radioactive nuclear
    substances, hazardous substances and radiation that remain at the
    nuclear facility after completion of the decommissioning; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the status of each worker\'s qualifications,
    requalification and training, including the results of all tests and
    examinations completed in accordance with the licence.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is required by this section to keep
  a record referred to in paragraph (2)(a) to (d) or (3)(a) to (d) shall
  retain the record for 10 years after the expiry date of the licence to
  abandon issued in respect of the Class I nuclear facility.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is required by this section to keep
  a record referred to in paragraph (2)(e) or (3)(e) shall retain the
  record for the period that the worker is employed by the licensee and
  for five years after the worker ceases to be so employed.

into force on the day on which they are approved by the Governor in
