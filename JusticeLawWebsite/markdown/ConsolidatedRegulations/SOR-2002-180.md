## CATSA Aerodrome Designation Regulations {#catsa-aerodrome-designation-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2002-180
:::

[CANADIAN AIR TRANSPORT SECURITY AUTHORITY ACT](/eng/acts/C-11.2)

Registration 2002-05-02

CATSA Aerodrome Designation Regulations

P.C. 2002-737 2002-05-02
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to paragraph 34(a) of the [Canadian
Air Transport Security Authority Act](/eng/acts/C-11.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [CATSA Aerodrome Designation
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-180).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2002, c.
  9
  :::
:::

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-682434 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The aerodromes set out in
the schedule are designated for the purposes of the [Canadian Air
Transport Security Authority Act](/eng/acts/C-11.2).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-682436 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
