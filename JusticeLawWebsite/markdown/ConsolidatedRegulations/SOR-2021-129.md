## College of Patent Agents and Trademark Agents Regulations {#college-of-patent-agents-and-trademark-agents-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2021-129
:::

[COLLEGE OF PATENT AGENTS AND TRADEMARK AGENTS ACT](/eng/acts/C-33.7)

Registration 2021-06-10

College of Patent Agents and Trademark Agents Regulations

P.C. 2021-526 2021-06-10
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Administrator of the Government of Canada in Council,
on the recommendation of the Minister of Industry, pursuant to paragraph
46(1)(d) and sections 72, 76 and 86 of the [College of Patent Agents and
Trademark Agents Act](/eng/acts/C-33.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4316_hq_27927){#fn_81000-2-4316_hq_27927-ID0EDBAA},
makes the annexed [College of Patent Agents and Trademark Agents
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-129).

## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1299837 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [College of Patent Agents and
Trademark Agents Act](/eng/acts/C-33.7).

## [Committees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1299840 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for Investigations Committee
and Discipline Committee members

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The majority of the members
of the Investigations Committee and of the Discipline Committee,
established under subsection 21(1) of the Act, must be individuals who

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} are not licensees; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} are not employed by a
  [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committee administering licensing
requirements

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The committee of the
College that is established to administer the requirements, including a
qualifying exam, that are to be met in order to obtain a licence under
section 26 or 29 of the Act must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be composed of individuals who may be removed at
  pleasure by the Board;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} not include any individual who is a member of an
  association the primary purpose of which is to represent the interests
  of persons who provide advice on patents or trademarks;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} include a representative from the Patent Office and
  the Office of the Registrar of Trademarks; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} be subject to a conflict of interest policy
  established by the Board.

## [Patent Agent Licence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1299851 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicant for patent agent licence ---
requirements

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 26(1) of the Act, an individual who makes an application for
a patent agent licence must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be resident in Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements for an applicant for such a
  licence, as set out in the by-laws made by the Board or the College,
  including requirements with respect to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} training,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} qualifying examinations,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} good character and fitness to practise, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} fees.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Patent agent licence --- conditions

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A patent agent licence is
subject to the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensee must be resident in Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licensee must meet the requirements for a holder
  of a patent agent licence, as set out in the by-laws made by the Board
  or the College, including requirements with respect to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} training,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} good character and fitness to practise, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} fees.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicant for patent agent in training
licence --- requirements

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 26(2) of the Act, an individual who makes an application for
a patent agent in training licence must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be resident in Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements for an applicant for such a
  licence, as set out in the by-laws made by the Board or the College,
  including requirements with respect to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} education,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} good character and fitness to practise,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} fees, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the provision of administrative forms.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Patent agent in training licence ---
conditions

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A patent agent in training
licence is subject to the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensee must be resident in Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licensee must meet the requirements for a holder
  of a patent agent in training licence, as set out in the by-laws made
  by the Board or the College, including requirements with respect to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} training,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} good character and fitness to practise, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} fees.

## [Investigations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1299913 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to Federal Court

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 44 to 48 of the Act, an application may be made to the Federal
Court by notice of application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed period

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 46(1)(d) of the Act, the prescribed period is the period of 10
days after the day on which the investigator removes the sealed package
containing the document or thing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Federal Court orders

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 46(5) of the Act, the Federal Court may
  make orders respecting the retention, opening or return of sealed
  packages.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An investigator or any interested person may apply to
  the Federal Court for an order referred to in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for opening sealed package

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any order made by the Federal Court under
  subsection (1), a sealed package may be opened if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} consent is given by the holder of a privilege with
    respect to that document or thing; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sealed package was returned to the owner of the
    document or thing in that sealed package.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicable period

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 47(1) of the Act, the applicable period is the one that ends
the latest among the following periods:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the period of 30 days after the day on which the
  investigator took possession of the document or thing;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the period of 30 days after the day on which the
  sealed package containing that document or thing was, or could have
  been, opened, if applicable; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the period agreed to by the owner of the document or
  thing in the sealed package and, if applicable, the holder of a
  privilege with respect to that document of thing.

## [Unauthorized Representation --- Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1299932 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation before Patent Office

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 70(1) of the
Act does not apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who is permitted to represent another person
  before the Patent Office under the [Patent
  Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-423) or under the [Patent
  Act](/eng/acts/P-4) or any other Act of Parliament or any Act of the
  legislature of a province;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an employee who is representing their employer,
  unless their employer is representing another person; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who is performing an administrative
  activity, if they have the permission of a patent agent, whose licence
  is not suspended, to perform that activity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation before Office of Registrar
of Trademarks

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 71(1) of the
Act does not apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who is permitted to represent another person
  before the Office of the Registrar of Trademarks under the [Trademarks
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-227) or under the [Trademarks
  Act](/eng/acts/T-13) or any other Act of Parliament or any Act of the
  legislature of a province;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an employee who is representing their employer,
  unless their employer is representing another person; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who is performing an administrative
  activity, if they have the permission of a trademark agent, whose
  licence is not suspended, to perform that activity.

## [Authorization to Make By-laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1299943 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority for College to make by-laws

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The College is authorized
to make by-laws with respect to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conditions that are to be imposed on licences or
  classes of licences, including a condition that requires a licensee to
  be of good character and fit to practise;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the requirements that individuals or classes of
  individuals must meet under section 26 or 29 of the Act, including
  requirements in relation to qualifying examinations, to fees with
  respect to qualifying examinations and to being of good character and
  fit to practise;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the determination as to what constitutes good
  character and fitness to practise for the purposes of the conditions
  and requirements referred to in paragraphs (a) and (b), respectively;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} restrictions on the entitlement of licensees or
  classes of licensees to provide representation under section 27 or 30
  of the Act.

## [Registers --- Additional Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1299950 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Register of Patent Agents --- additional
information

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of paragraph 28(1)(f) of the Act, the Registrar must
  include in the Register of Patent Agents the name and contact
  information of an individual who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a resident of a country other than Canada and is
    authorized to act as a patent agent under the law of that country;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has made a request in writing to the Registrar to
    be included in the Register.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Register of Patent Agents --- annual
  requirement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In order to maintain their name in the Register, an
  individual referred to in subsection (1) must continue to meet the
  requirements of paragraph of (1)(a) and must in each year, within the
  period established by by-laws made by the Board, provide to the
  Registrar a statement signed by the individual setting out their
  country of residence and declaring that they are authorized to act as
  a patent agent under the law of that country.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Register of Trademark Agents ---
additional information

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of paragraph 31(1)(f) of the Act, the Registrar must
  include in the Register of Trademark Agents the name and contact
  information of an individual who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a resident of a country other than Canada and is
    authorized to act as a trademark agent under the law of that
    country; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has made a request in writing to the Registrar to
    be included in the Register.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Register of Trademark Agents --- annual
  requirement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In order to maintain their name in the Register, an
  individual referred to in subsection (1) must continue to meet the
  requirements of paragraph (1)(a) and must in each year, within the
  period established by by-laws made by the Board, provide to the
  Registrar a statement signed by the individual setting out their
  country of residence and declaring that they are authorized to act as
  a trademark agent under the law of that country.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1299965 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed holder of patent agent in training
licence

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual is deemed to hold a patent agent in training licence during
  the period described in subsection (2) if that individual

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is working in the area of Canadian patent law and
    practice, including the preparation and prosecution of applications
    for a patent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is supervised, in respect of that work,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by an individual who holds a patent agent licence
      or who, before the day on which these Regulations come into force,
      is a patent agent, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by an individual who is responsible for a legal
      clinic associated with a Canadian faculty of law;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is resident in Canada; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provides notice to the College that they meet the
    requirements of paragraphs (a) to (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period for purposes of subsection (1)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), the applicable
  period begins on the later of the day on which these Regulations come
  into force and the day on which the notice referred to in paragraph
  (1)(d) is received and ends on the earliest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the individual is issued a patent
    agent licence or a patent agent in training licence under section 26
    of the Act,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the applicable licence referred to
    in subsection (1) is surrendered,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the applicable licence referred to
    in subsection (1) is revoked, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the day that is one year after the day on which
    these Regulations come into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed holder of trademark agent in
training licence

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual is deemed to hold a trademark agent in training during the
  period described in subsection (2) if that individual

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is working in the area of Canadian trademark law
    and practice, including the preparation and prosecution of
    applications for the registration of trademarks;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is supervised, in respect of that work,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by an individual who holds a trademark agent
      licence or who, before the day on which these Regulations come
      into force, is a trademark agent, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by an individual who is responsible for a legal
      clinic associated with a Canadian faculty of law;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is resident in Canada; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provides notice to the College that they meet the
    requirements of paragraphs (a) to (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period for purposes of subsection (1)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), the applicable
  period begins on the later of the day on which these Regulations come
  into force and the day on which the notice referred to in paragraph
  (1)(d) is received and ends on the earliest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the individual is issued a
    trademark agent licence or a trademark agent in training licence
    under section 29 of the Act,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the applicable licence referred to
    in subsection (1) is surrendered,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the applicable licence referred to
    in subsection (1) is revoked, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the day that is one year after the day on which
    these Regulations come into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifying examination for patent agents

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** If, before the day on
which these Regulations come into force, an individual sat for a paper
of the qualifying examination for patent agents and, on that day, the
final mark for that paper was not yet determined,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Examining Board established under section 20 of
  the [Patent Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-423), as it read
  immediately before the day on which these Regulations come into force,
  must mark that paper; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the individual may meet the requirements of
  subsection 26(1) of the Act by passing that qualifying examination and
  paying the fee, if any, established for that purpose by by-laws made
  by the Board or the College.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifying examination for trademark
agents

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** If, before the day on
which these Regulations come into force, an individual sat for a
qualifying examination for trademark agents and, on that day, the final
mark for that examination was not yet determined,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the examining board established under section 17 of
  the [Trademarks Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-227), as it
  read immediately before the day on which these Regulations come into
  force, must mark that examination; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the individual may meet the requirements of
  subsection 29(1) of the Act by passing that examination and paying the
  fee, if any, established for that purpose by by-laws made by the Board
  or the College.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Patent agent licence --- requirements for
former patent agent

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** An individual who is
resident in Canada, who has passed the qualifying examination for patent
agents and who was a former patent agent whose name was removed under
subsection 23(2) of the [Patent Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-423) from
the register of patent agents kept under section 15 of the [Patent
Act](/eng/acts/P-4), as those provisions read immediately before the day
on which these Regulations come in to force, is considered to meet the
requirements for a patent agent licence if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} they apply in writing to the Registrar for
  reinstatement not later than one year after the day on which their
  name was removed from that register; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} they pay the fee, if any, established for that
  purpose by by-laws made by the Board or the College.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Trademark agent licence --- requirements
for former trademark agent

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** An individual who is
resident in Canada, who has passed the qualifying examination for
trademark agents and who was a former trademark agent whose name was
removed under subsection 20(2) of the [Trademarks
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-227) from the list of trademark
agents kept under section 28 of the [Trademarks Act](/eng/acts/T-13), as
those provisions read immediately before the day on which these
Regulations come into force, is considered to meet the requirements for
a trademark agent licence if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} they apply in writing to the Registrar for
  reinstatement not later than one year after the day on which their
  name was removed from that list; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} they pay the fee, if any, established for that
  purpose by by-laws made by the Board or the College.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request deemed to be made --- patent
agents

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** If, on the day before the
day on which these Regulations come into force, the name of an
individual was on the register of patent agents in accordance with
paragraph 22(b) of the [Patent Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-423), as
it read immediately before the day on which these Regulations come into
force, the individual is deemed to have made the request referred to in
paragraph 19(1)(b) of these Regulations on the day on which these
Regulations come into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request deemed to be made --- trademark
agents

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** If, on the day before the
day on which these Regulations come into force, the name of an
individual was on the list of trademark agents in accordance with
paragraph 19(b) of the [Trademarks
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-227), as it read immediately
before the day on which these Regulations come into force, the
individual is deemed to have made the request referred to in paragraph
20(1)(b) of these Regulations on the day on which these Regulations come
into force.
