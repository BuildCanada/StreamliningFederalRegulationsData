## Order Transferring to the Minister of Communications and to the Minister of Multiculturalism and Citizenship Certain Powers, Duties and Functions of the Secretary of State of Canada and De-amalgamating the Departments of the Secretary of State of Canada, Multiculturalism and Citizenship and Communications {#order-transferring-to-the-minister-of-communications-and-to-the-minister-of-multiculturalism-and-citizenship-certain-powers-duties-and-functions-of-the-secretary-of-state-of-canada-and-de-amalgamating-the-departments-of-the-secretary-of-state-of-canada-multiculturalism-and-citizenship-and-communications .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-200
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1993-11-17

Order Transferring to the Minister of Communications and to the Minister
of Multiculturalism and Citizenship Certain Powers, Duties and Functions
of the Secretary of State of Canada and De-amalgamating the Departments
of the Secretary of State of Canada, Multiculturalism and Citizenship
and Communications

P.C. 1993-1900  1993-11-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(b) of the [Public Service
  Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), to revoke
  paragraph (b) of Order in Council P.C. 1993-1489 of June 25,
  1993[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-200_e_hq_6525){#fn_SI-93-200_e_hq_6525-ID0EBBBAA}
  and to de-amalgamate the Department of the Secretary of State of
  Canada, the Department of Multiculturalism and Citizenship and the
  Department of Communications that were amalgamated by that Order in
  Council; and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-200_e_hq_6525-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_SI-93-200_e_hq_6525}SI/93-143,
    1993 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3235
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of that Act, to transfer
  from the Secretary of State of Canada

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the Minister of Communications the powers,
    duties and functions referred to in clauses (a)(ii)(A) and (C) of
    Order in Council P.C. 1993-1489 of June 25, 1993[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-200_e_hq_6525){#fn_SI-93-200_e_hq_6525-ID0EBABAAA},
    and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to the Minister of Multiculturalism and
    Citizenship the powers, duties and functions referred to in
    clause (a)(ii)(B) of Order in Council P.C. 1993-1489 of June 25,
    1993[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-200_e_hq_6525){#fn_SI-93-200_e_hq_6525-ID0EBAAAAA}.
