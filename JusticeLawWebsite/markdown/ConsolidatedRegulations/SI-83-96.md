## Meat Inspection Overtime Inspection Fees Remission Order {#meat-inspection-overtime-inspection-fees-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/83-96
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1983-05-25

Order Respecting the Remission of a Portion of Certain Fees Paid or
Payable for Overtime Inspection Services Respecting Meat Inspection

P.C. 1983-1431 1983-05-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section
17 of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of a portion
of certain fees paid or payable for overtime inspection services
respecting meat inspection](/eng/regulations/SI-83-96).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639370 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Meat Inspection Overtime Inspection Fees Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-83-96).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639372 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of a portion of the fees paid or payable under subsections 80(1) and
81(1) of the Meat Inspection Regulations, as amended by Order in Council
P.C. 1982-1710 of 10th June, 1982[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_SI_83-96_EN_hq_10776){#fn_SI_83-96_EN_hq_10776-ID0EFCA},
in an amount equal to the difference between the amount of the fees
prescribed by that Order in Council and the amount of the fees
prescribed by the Meat Inspection Regulations, as amended by Order in
Council P.C. 1982-3169 of 21 October, 1982[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^2^](#fn_SI_83-96_EN_hq_10777){#fn_SI_83-96_EN_hq_10777-ID0EBCA}.
