## Railway Rolling Stock (Canadian Manufactured) Remission Order, No. 1 {#railway-rolling-stock-canadian-manufactured-remission-order-no.-1 .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 780
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duty Paid on Imported Articles
and Materials Used or Consumed in the Manufacture of Railway Rolling
Stock Purchased by Canadian Companies for Use in International Service
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561972 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Railway Rolling Stock (Canadian Manufactured) Remission Order, No.
1](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._780).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561974 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Revenue; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[railway rolling stock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[railway rolling stock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means railway
    passenger, baggage and freight cars manufactured in Canada and
    purchased during the period January 1, 1969 to December 31, 1985 by
    Canadian companies for use in international service. ([matériel
    roulant de chemin de fer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/81-70, s. 2
:::

## [Grant of Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561980 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Order,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duty paid in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} imported articles and materials used in the
  manufacture of, wrought into or attached to railway rolling stock;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} imported materials, other than fuel or plant
  equipment, directly consumed in the manufacture or production of
  railway rolling stock; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} imported articles and materials, other than fuel or
  plant equipment, in quantity sufficient to produce railway rolling
  stock, where

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} domestic articles and materials of the same class
    are used or directly consumed in the manufacture of, wrought into or
    attached to the railway rolling stock, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} those imported articles and materials were used in
    the plant producing the railway rolling stock during the 12-month
    period immediately preceding the production of the railway rolling
    stock.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/81-70, s. 3
:::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561989 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission granted under section 3
  shall be paid to a manufacturer of railway rolling stock if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister
    subsequent to the date the railway rolling stock entered
    international service;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the customs duty on the articles and materials in
    respect of which the claim referred to in paragraph (a) is made was
    paid within the four year period immediately preceding the date on
    which the claim for remission was filed; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer provides to the Minister such
    information in such form and manner as may be required by the
    Minister for the administration of this Order.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(b) applies to all customs duty paid on
  or after April 1, 1981.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/85-145, s. 1
:::

## [Reduction of Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561998 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where imported articles or
materials enter into a process of manufacture that results in the
production of a by-product, the remission payable under this Order in
respect of the articles or materials shall be reduced by the same
proportion that the value of the by-product bears to the total value of
all production from the articles or materials.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where imported articles or
materials referred to in section 3 enter into a process of manufacture
that results in the production of merchantable scrap or waste, the
remission payable under this Order in respect of the articles or
materials shall be reduced by a sum to be arrived at by applying to the
Canadian sales value of the merchantable scrap or waste the prevailing
rates of customs duty on merchantable scrap or waste of the same kind
when imported as such.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding section 6,
no reduction referred to therein resulting from the production of
merchantable scrap or waste shall be calculated on higher rates of
customs duty than were paid on the imported articles or materials from
which the scrap or waste was derived.

## [Repayment of Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-562002 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where, at any time within
10 years of the date that any railway rolling stock in respect of which
remission is granted under this Order enters international service, that
rolling stock is removed from that service and placed in domestic
service in Canada for a period of more than 90 days in any calendar
year, the manufacturer of the rolling stock shall repay to the Crown the
entire amount of the remission so granted.
