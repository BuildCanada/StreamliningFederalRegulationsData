## Assistance Fund (W.V.A. and C.W.A.) Regulations {#assistance-fund-w.v.a.-and-c.w.a.-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1578
:::

[DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS ACT](/eng/acts/V-1)

Regulations Respecting Assistance Fund (War Veterans Allowances and
Civilian War Allowances)
::::

::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-529958 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Assistance Fund (W.V.A. and C.W.A.)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1578).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-529960 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [War Veterans Allowance
    Act](/eng/acts/W-3); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Assistance Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Assistance Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the moneys
    appropriated by Parliament for the provision of assistance in
    accordance with these Regulations; ([fonds de
    secours]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Assistance Fund
    Committee. ([Comité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Assistance Fund Committee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-529965 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be at the head office of the Department of Veterans Affairs an
  Assistance Fund Committee composed of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Director, Program Operations, Veterans
    Services, Department of Veterans Affairs, who shall be chairman, or
    in his absence the Acting Director;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Assistant Director, Program Operations, Benefit
    Administration, or in his absence the Acting Assistant Director; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Assistant Director, Program Operations, Client
    Services, or in his absence the Acting Assistant Director.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief, Economic Support Benefits, Veterans
  Services Program of the Department of Veterans Affairs shall be the
  Secretary of the Committee.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/80-567, s. 1
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The District Authority
appointed under the provisions of the Act in each district of the
Department of Veterans Affairs shall be responsible to the Committee for
the administration of the Assistance Fund.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Committee shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} coordinate the work of District Authorities with
  respect to the administration of the Assistance Fund;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} allocate moneys from the Assistance Fund to District
  Authorities as required;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} issue instructions governing the administration of
  the Assistance Fund for the guidance of District Authorities;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} report to the Minister of Veterans Affairs on the
  operation of District Authorities with respect to the administration
  of the Assistance Fund; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} recommend to the Minister desirable changes in, or
  additions to, these Regulations.

## [District Authority]{.HTitleText1} {#h-529981 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Secretary of a District
Authority shall keep such record of its meetings with respect to the
administration of the Assistance Fund as the Committee shall direct.

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Minister of Veterans Affairs may grant
  financial assistance from the Assistance Fund to a recipient under the
  Act or under Part XI of the Civilian Warrelated Benefits Act if that
  person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a resident of Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} makes an application to the Minister demonstrating
    that the assistance is required to meet an emergency or an
    unexpected financial contingency or to relieve financial distress.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The assistance shall not exceed the sum of one
  thousand dollars during any calendar year.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/80-567, s. 2
- .SOR/2005-282, s. 1
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where in any case the
District Authority is of the opinion that the recipient would be likely
to apply the amount of any assistance otherwise than to the best
advantage, it may direct that the payments be made to, and administered
by, such person or persons as it selects.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The District Authority
shall refer applicants for assistance from the Assistance Fund to the
Welfare Services Branch of the Department of Veterans Affairs for
counselling on assistance that may be available from any source.
