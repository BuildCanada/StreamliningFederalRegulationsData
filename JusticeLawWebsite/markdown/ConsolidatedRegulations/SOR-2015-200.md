## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-822079 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Permit.

  [[eligible destination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[eligible destination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Australia,
      Austria, Belgium, the Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland,
      France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Japan,
      Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, New Zealand,
      Norway, Poland, Portugal, the Republic of Korea, Slovakia,
      Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey, the United Kingdom
      or the United States. ([destination admissible]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Export Controls Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Export Controls Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Export
      Controls Division of the Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and
      Development. ([Direction des contrôles à
      l'exportation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 1
      of the [Export Control
      List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202). ([Guide]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Permit, [[composite]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
  [[development]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[matrix]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
  [[production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[software]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  and [[user-accessible programmability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have the
  same meaning as in the Guide under the heading "Definitions of Terms
  Used in Groups 1 and 2".

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-822088 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 to 5,
any resident of Canada may, under this Permit, export or transfer from
Canada any good or technology referred to in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any item in Group 1 of the Guide; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} items 5504.2.a. to 5504.2.g. of the Guide.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unauthorized destinations

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Permit does not authorize the export or transfer of goods or
  technology to a country

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is not an eligible destination;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is listed in the [Area Control
    List](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-543); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which an order or regulation has been
    made under section 4 of the [Special Economic Measures
    Act](/eng/acts/S-14.5) or section 2 of the [United Nations
    Act](/eng/acts/U-2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unauthorized goods or technology

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Permit does not authorize the export or transfer
  of goods or technology that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are intended to be used in a country that is not an
    eligible destination;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are referred to in the schedule to this Permit;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} are referred to in any item of the Guide other than
    those referred to in section 2, unless the export or transfer is
    also authorized by another permit issued under subsection 7(1.1) of
    the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} are referred to in any of subparagraphs 3(3)(c)(i)
    to (iii) of the [Export Permits
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-204); or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} are intended for the development, production or use
    of rocket systems or unmanned aerial vehicles with a range of 300 km
    or greater.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other unauthorized goods or technology

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} This Permit does not authorize the export or transfer
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} software that is specially designed or modified for
    the development or use of the goods or technology referred to in
    item 5504.2.h. or 5504.2.i. of the Guide; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} technology that is specially designed or modified
    for the development or production of the goods or technology
    referred to in item 5504.2.h. or 5504.2.i. of the Guide.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A resident of Canada who
exports or transfers goods or technology under this Permit must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide in writing to the Export Controls Division,
  before making their first export or transfer under this Permit in a
  calendar year, the following information:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their name, address, telephone number, facsimile
    number and email address, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the resident of Canada is a corporation, the
    business number assigned to the corporation by the Minister of
    National Revenue, the name of a contact person and their address,
    telephone number, facsimile number and email address;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide to the Export Controls Division, within 30
  days after each six-month period ending on January 31 or July 31, a
  report containing the following information in respect of any export
  or transfer made under this Permit during that period:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number, facsimile
    number and email address of each consignee,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the consignee is a corporation, the name and
    title of a contact person who has knowledge of the export or
    transfer and their telephone number, facsimile number and email
    address,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a description of each good or technology exported
    or transferred and the item number of the Guide in which it is
    referred to, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the quantity and value of each good or technology
    exported or transferred by country of destination;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} within 15 days after receipt of a request from the
  Export Controls Division, provide to the Export Controls Division the
  records referred to in section 5 in respect of any export or transfer
  made during the period specified in the request;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} insert the term "GEP-41" or "LGE-41" in the
  appropriate field of the form prescribed under the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) if the good exported is required to be reported
  under that Act; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} obtain, before the export or transfer, a written
  statement from each consignee that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} identifies the country of end use of the good or
    technology, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} indicates whether the consignee is an end-user or
    a distributor or, if the consignee is neither, describes the
    consignee's role in relation to the export or transfer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A resident of Canada who
exports or transfers a good or technology under this Permit must retain,
for a period of six years after the year in which the export or transfer
is made, the following records in respect of that export or transfer:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of the export or transfer;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of each consignee;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the quantity and value of the export or transfer;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and any part number or unique identifier of
  the good or technology, as well as a description of the good or
  technology and its technical specifications;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the item number of the Guide in which the good or
  technology is referred to, and a comparison of the technical
  specifications set out in the item with the technical specifications
  of the good or technology;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the written statement obtained from each consignee
  under paragraph 4(e); and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a copy, if available, of any contract between the
  resident of Canada and each consignee and any invoice or export or
  shipping document relating to the export or transfer.
