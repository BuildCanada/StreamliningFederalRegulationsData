## Order transferring to the Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development the control and supervision of the portion of the federal public administration in the Department of Canadian Heritage known as the Urban Aboriginal Youth and Community Programs Unit {#order-transferring-to-the-department-of-indian-affairs-and-northern-development-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-portion-of-the-federal-public-administration-in-the-department-of-canadian-heritage-known-as-the-urban-aboriginal-youth-and-community-programs-unit .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2012-12
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2012-03-28

Order transferring to the Department of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development the control and supervision of the portion of the federal
public administration in the Department of Canadian Heritage known as
the Urban Aboriginal Youth and Community Programs Unit

P.C. 2012-286 2012-03-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBCAA},
hereby transfers to the Department of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development the control and supervision of the portion of the federal
public administration in the Department of Canadian Heritage known as
the Urban Aboriginal Youth and Community Programs Unit, effective April
1, 2012.
