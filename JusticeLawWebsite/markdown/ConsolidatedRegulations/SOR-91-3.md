## Export Inspection and Certification Exemption Regulations {#export-inspection-and-certification-exemption-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-3
:::

[HEALTH OF ANIMALS ACT](/eng/acts/H-3.3)

Registration 1990-12-13

Regulations Exempting Animals from the Application of Section 19 of the
Health of Animals Act
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to subsection 19(6) of the [Health
of Animals Act](/eng/acts/H-3.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-91-3_e_hq_5222){#fn_SOR-91-3_e_hq_5222-ID0EDBCA},
hereby makes the annexed [Regulations exempting animals from the
application of section 19 of the Health of Animals
Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-3).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934112 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Export Inspection and Certification Exemption
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-3).

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934114 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** All animals are exempt from
the application of section 19 of the [Health of Animals
Act](/eng/acts/H-3.3) except for horses, swine, foxes, mink, members of
the suborder [rumenatiae]{.otherLang lang="la"} and members of the
family [camelidae]{.otherLang lang="la"}.
