## Alberta Chicken Order {#alberta-chicken-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/99-145
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1999-03-25

Alberta Chicken Order

P.C. 1999-524 1999-03-25
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food, pursuant to
section 2[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Agricultural Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), hereby makes the

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028668 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Marketing of Agricultural
    Products Act of Alberta. ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[chicken]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chicken]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any category of chicken under
    six months of age and not raised or used for egg production, and
    includes broilers, roasters, and Rock Cornish hen.
    ([poulet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Alberta Chicken
    Producers Board established under the Act and the Plan. ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Alberta Chicken Producers
    Plan Regulations made under the Act. ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028674 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized to regulate the marketing of chickens in interprovincial and
export trade, and for that purpose may, with respect to persons and
property situated within the Province of Alberta, exercise all or any
powers like the powers exercisable by it in relation to the marketing of
chickens locally within that Province under the Act and the Plan.

## [Levies or Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028676 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board may in
relation to the powers granted to it by section 2,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix and impose levies or charges on chicken or any
  component of chicken and collect those levies or charges from persons
  who, within the Province of Alberta, are engaged in the production or
  marketing of chicken and for that purpose classify those persons into
  groups and fix the levies or charges payable by the members of the
  different groups in different amounts; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the levies or charges for the purposes of the
  Commodity Board, including the creation of reserves, the payment of
  expenses and losses resulting from the sale or disposal of chicken,
  and the equalization or adjustment among producers of chicken of money
  realized from the sale of chicken during any period that the Board may
  determine.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028680 .Part}

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028682 .Part}
