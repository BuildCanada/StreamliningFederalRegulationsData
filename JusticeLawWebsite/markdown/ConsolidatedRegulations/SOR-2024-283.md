## [Grounds of Inadmissibility]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1501461 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grounds of inadmissibility

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 48(4) of the [Preclearance Act, 2016](/eng/acts/P-19.32),
grounds of inadmissibility are prescribed as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a permanent resident, the grounds set
  out in sections 34 and 35, subsection 36(1) and section 37 of the
  [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a [[protected
  person]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} within the meaning of the [Immigration
  and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), the grounds set out in
  sections 34 to 35.1, subsection 36(1) and section 37 of that Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a foreign national who holds a
  permanent resident visa, the grounds set out in sections 34 to 35.1,
  subsection 36(1) and section 37 of the [Immigration and Refugee
  Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a foreign national who holds a
  permanent resident visa accompanied by a family member who is
  inadmissible or has been refused entry to Canada through a
  preclearance area or preclearance perimeter on the grounds set out in
  any of sections 34 to 35.1, subsection 36(1) or section 37 of the
  [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), the grounds
  set out in sections 34 to 35.1, subsection 36(1) and sections 37 and
  42 of that Act; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a foreign national, the grounds set
  out in sections 34 to 42 of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5).

## [Adaptations and Exclusions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1501469 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptations --- [Immigration and Refugee
Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5)

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In respect of a traveller
entering Canada through a preclearance area or preclearance perimeter,
the [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) is adapted
as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} subsection 11(1) is to be read as follows:

  > ::: ReadAsText
  > - **11** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A foreign national must, before entering
  >   Canada or before entering a preclearance area or preclearance
  >   perimeter, apply to an officer for a visa or for any other
  >   document required by the regulations.
  > :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} subsection 11(1.01) is to be read as follows:

  > ::: ReadAsText
  > - **11** [(1.01)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a foreign
  >   national must, before entering Canada or before entering a
  >   preclearance area or preclearance perimeter, apply for an
  >   electronic travel authorization required by the regulations by
  >   means of an electronic system, unless the regulations provide that
  >   the application may be made by other means.
  > :::

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a reference to "on their entry" in section 19 is to
  be read as a reference to "in a preclearance area or preclearance
  perimeter";

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "have come to Canada" in paragraph
  20(1)(a) of the English version is to be read as a reference to "are
  coming to Canada through a preclearance area or preclearance
  perimeter";

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "upon arrival in Canada" in
  subsection 24(2) is to be read as a reference to "upon arrival in a
  preclearance area or preclearance perimeter"; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "outside Canada" in the portion of
  subsection 31(3) before paragraph (a) is to be read as a reference to
  "outside a preclearance area or preclearance perimeter".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptations --- [Immigration and Refugee
Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227)

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In respect of a traveller
entering Canada through a preclearance area or preclearance perimeter,
the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227) are adapted as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the following provisions, a reference to
  "subsection 44(2) of the Act" is to be read as a reference to
  "subsection 48(5) of the [Preclearance Act, 2016](/eng/acts/P-19.32)":

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 12.06(d),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 37(1)(d), and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 41(b);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the following provisions, a reference to "port of
  entry" is to be read as a reference to "preclearance area or
  preclearance perimeter":

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} subsection 27(1),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} paragraphs 37(1)(a) and (d),

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the portion of section 38 before paragraph (a)
    and paragraph 38(a),

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} subsections 40(1) and (3),

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} subsection 71.1(1), and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 240(1)(a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "departure from Canada" in paragraph
  37(1)(c) is to be read as a reference to "departure from the
  preclearance area or preclearance perimeter";

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "leave Canada" in subsection 40(1)
  is to be read as a reference to "leave the preclearance area or
  preclearance perimeter";

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the following provisions, a reference to a time
  period that begins on the day on which a traveller enters Canada is to
  be read as a time period that begins on the day on which the traveller
  is authorized in a preclearance area or preclearance perimeter to
  enter Canada:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} subparagraph 132(1)(a)(i),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} paragraphs 183(3)(a) and (b), and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 184(2)(b);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the following provisions, a reference to "enters
  Canada" or "entering Canada" is to be read as a reference to "enters
  Canada through a preclearance area or preclearance perimeter" or
  "entering Canada through a preclearance area or preclearance
  perimeter" respectively:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the portion of subsection 184(2) before paragraph
    (b),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} subsection 198(1) and the portion of subsection
    198(2) before paragraph (a), and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the portion of section 214 before paragraph (a);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the following provisions, a reference to "before
  entering Canada" is to be read as a reference to "before entering a
  preclearance area or preclearance perimeter":

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} section 197, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} section 213;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "entry into Canada" in paragraph
  180(b) of the English version is to be read as "entry into Canada
  through a preclearance area or preclearance perimeter"; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "[contrôle d'arrivée]{.otherLang
  lang="fr"}" in paragraph 180(b) of the French version is to be read as
  a reference to "[contrôle d'arrivée dans une zone de précontrôle ou un
  périmètre de précontrôle]{.otherLang lang="fr"}".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusions --- [Immigration and Refugee
Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227)

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The following provisions of
the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227) do not apply in a
preclearance area or preclearance perimeter:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} paragraphs 38(b) to (h);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 41(c); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} subsection 188(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptations --- enactments under the
[Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In respect of a traveller
entering Canada through a preclearance area or preclearance perimeter,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a reference to "the province where the customs office
  through which the alcoholic beverages are imported is located" in
  paragraph 3(2)(a) of the [Returning Persons Exemption
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-61) is to be read as "the
  province where the person enters Canada";

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a reference to "the province in which the customs
  office where the alcoholic beverages are imported is situated" in
  paragraph 3(a) of the Tariff Item No. 9805.00.00 Exemption Order is to
  be read as "the province where the person enters Canada"; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a reference to "the province in which the customs
  office where the alcoholic beverages are imported is situated" in
  paragraph 2(a) of the [Tariff Item No. 9807.00.00 Exemption
  Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-225) is to be read as "the province
  where the settler enters Canada".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptations --- [Plant Protection
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-212)

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
[Preclearance Act, 2016](/eng/acts/P-19.32), a reference to "place of
entry" in the [Plant Protection
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-212) includes a reference to a
preclearance area or preclearance perimeter.
