## Testing of Unregistered US Military Herbicides, including Agent Orange, at CFB Gagetown Ex Gratia Payments Order {#testing-of-unregistered-us-military-herbicides-including-agent-orange-at-cfb-gagetown-ex-gratia-payments-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2007-87
:::

Registration 2007-10-03

Testing of Unregistered US Military Herbicides, including Agent Orange,
at CFB Gagetown Ex Gratia Payments Order

P.C. 2007-1326 2007-09-10
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Veterans Affairs, hereby makes the annexed [Testing of
Unregistered US Military Herbicides, including Agent Orange, at CFB
Gagetown Ex Gratia Payments Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2007-87).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613493 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Veterans
    Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[primary caregiver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[primary caregiver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in relation to an
    individual, means the adult person who, immediately before the
    individual died,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} was primarily responsible, without remuneration,
      for ensuring that care was provided to the individual; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a continuous period of at least one year,
      resided in the principal residence of the individual and
      maintained the individual or was maintained by the individual.
      ([principal donneur de soins]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613499 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is authorized,
on application, to make an [ex gratia]{.otherLang lang="la"} lump sum
payment of \$20,000 to any individual who meets the following
conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} between June 1, 1966 and June 30, 2011, the
  individual is diagnosed with any one or more of the following medical
  conditions:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} soft tissue sarcoma,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} non-Hodgkin's lymphoma,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} Hodgkin's disease,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} chloracne,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} respiratory cancers (of the lung/bronchus, larynx
    or trachea)

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} prostate cancer,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} multiple myeloma,

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} acute and subacute (or early onset) transient
    peripheral neuropathy,

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} porphyria cutanea tarda,

  - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} type 2 diabetes (mellitus), and

  - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} spina bifida; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} at any time from June to September of either 1966 or
  1967 the individual or, in the case of an individual diagnosed with
  spina bifida, a biological parent of the individual

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} worked or lived at CFB Gagetown,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} was posted to or trained at CFB Gagetown, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} resided in a community, as determined by the
    Minister, any portion of which lay within 5 km of the perimeter of
    CFB Gagetown.

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  individual dies before receiving payment, the payment shall be made to
  the primary caregiver of the individual, if any.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an individual dies before making an application,
  the primary caregiver may apply for the payment on behalf of the
  individual.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the primary caregiver dies before receiving
  payment, no payment shall be made.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613524 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application for an [ex
gratia]{.otherLang lang="la"} payment must be made in the manner
approved by the Minister no later than June 30, 2011, unless
circumstances beyond the control of the applicant necessitate a longer
period. The application must be supported by any evidence that the
Minister considers necessary.

## [Cessation of Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613528 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall cease to
make payments under this Order on December 30, 2011.

- SI/2010-96, s. 3

## [Review]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613532 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant who is dissatisfied with a decision may request, in writing,
  a review by the Minister within 60 days after receipt of the decision,
  unless circumstances beyond the control of the applicant necessitate a
  longer period.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An applicant who is dissatisfied with a decision made
  on review may request, in writing, a further review of the decision
  within 60 days after receipt of the first level review decision,
  unless circumstances beyond the control of the applicant necessitate a
  longer period.

## [No Crown Liability]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613536 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A payment made under this
Order does not constitute an admission of liability on the part of the
Crown.

## [Exclusion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613538 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** This Order does not apply
to members of foreign military units.
