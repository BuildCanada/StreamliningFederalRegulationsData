## Ontario Fresh Grape Service Charge (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#ontario-fresh-grape-service-charge-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 198
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Regulations Respecting the Service Charge Imposed on Certain Persons
Engaged in the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade of Fresh
Grapes Produced in Ontario
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536425 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Ontario Fresh Grape Service Charge (Interprovincial and
Export) Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._198).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536427 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[appointed dealer-shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appointed dealer-shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    dealer-shipper in possession of a valid certificate of appointment
    issued by the Commodity Board pursuant to the Ontario Fresh Grape
    Shippers and Dealer-Shippers (Interprovincial and Export)
    Regulations; ([négociant-expéditeur désigné]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[appointed shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appointed shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a shipper in
    possession of a valid certificate of appointment issued by the
    Commodity Board pursuant to the Ontario Fresh Grape Shippers and
    Dealer-Shippers (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations;
    ([expéditeur désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Fresh
    Grape Growers' Marketing Board; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dealer-shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dealer-shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who sells or
    offers to sell, receives, assembles, packs, ships or transports
    fresh grapes but does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a servant employed by and driving a vehicle owned
      by a producer, an appointed shipper or an appointed
      dealer-shipper,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a railway company, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who transports fresh grapes by motor
      vehicle as the agent of a producer;
      ([négociant-expéditeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fresh grapes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fresh grapes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means grapes produced in
    Ontario other than grapes that are used by a processor for
    processing; ([raisin frais]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the manufacture of grape
    products or juice, beverage, spirits or wine from grapes and
    includes bottling, distilling or fermenting with sugar or sulphur
    dioxide or any other chemical; ([transformation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    business of processing grapes; ([transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production of fresh grapes; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who receives,
    assembles, packs, ships or transports fresh grapes but does not
    include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a servant employed by and driving a vehicle owned
      by a producer, an appointed shipper or an appointed
      dealer-shipper,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a railway company, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who transports fresh grapes by motor
      vehicle as the agent of a producer. ([expéditeur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536444 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of fresh grapes in interprovincial and export
trade and to persons and property situated within the Province of
Ontario.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A producer who sells a
product directly to a consumer is, in respect of the product so sold,
exempt from these Regulations.

## [Imposition and Rate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536447 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay to
the Commodity Board, in addition to the remuneration of the appointed
shipper as determined by the Commodity Board from time to time, a
service charge for the marketing of fresh grapes in the amount of 3/8 of
one cent per pound of fresh grapes.

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536449 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  fresh grapes are sold through the Commodity Board, the Board shall
  deduct the service charge imposed by section 5 from the moneys payable
  by the Commodity Board to the producer for the sale of the fresh
  grapes.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where fresh grapes are sold through a dealer-shipper,
  the dealer-shipper shall deduct the service charge imposed by section
  5 from the moneys payable by him to the producer and remit the amount
  deducted to the Commodity Board within 21 days after the receipt by
  him of the fresh grapes.
