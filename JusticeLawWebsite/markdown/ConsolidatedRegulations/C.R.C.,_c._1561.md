## Translation Bureau Regulations {#translation-bureau-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1561
:::

[TRANSLATION BUREAU ACT](/eng/acts/T-16)

Regulations Respecting the Provision of Translation Services
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-528902 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Translation Bureau
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1561).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-528904 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a department of the Public
    Service and includes both Houses of the Parliament of Canada and all
    bureaus, branches, commissions and agencies created or appointed by
    Act of Parliament or by order of the Governor in Council;
    ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} or
    [département]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee of a
    department designated in accordance with the provisions of paragraph
    7(1)(b); ([fonctionnaire désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Interdepartmental Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Interdepartmental Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Interdepartmental Advisory Committee on Government Translations
    established by section 14; ([comité
    interministériel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[interpretation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[interpretation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means oral translation;
    ([interprétation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[proofreading]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[proofreading]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the examination and
    correction of typographical details or of illustrations on sheets
    printed from type or plates and includes the verification of numbers
    contained in tables and documents; ([correction
    d'épreuves]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[revision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[revision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the examination and
    correction of the meaning and form of a translation and includes the
    insertion into a translation of changes made in the original text
    after it has been submitted for translation to the Bureau;
    ([révision]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[translation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[translation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the written transposition
    of words and numbers from one language to another and includes an
    adaptation that accurately conveys the meaning thereof.
    ([traduction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Responsibilities of the Bureau]{.HTitleText1} {#h-528913 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Bureau shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to these Regulations, make all translations
  requested by departments;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cooperate with departments in revising documents
  prepared by departments;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} deliver translations or revisions in legible form to
  the department requesting the translations or revisions;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} ensure that translations or revisions made by it
  conform, in so far as Canadian usage permits, to "le français
  universel" or Standard English, whichever is applicable;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} subject to these Regulations, provide interpretation
  services requested by departments; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} give technical advice on questions relating to
  translation, terminology and style to employees of departments.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  in cases of particular urgency, the Bureau shall make translations in
  the English or French language in accordance with the following order
  of priority:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} documents that are required to be published in both
    the English and French languages including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all Parliamentary documents,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all official departmental documents and
      publications, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} speeches and press releases;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} handbooks and administrative directions intended
    for the use of departmental employees;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} documents, including correspondence, that are
    required to be translated solely for the assistance of an individual
    who has not a working knowledge of both the English and French
    languages; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} staff magazines.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Bureau, upon receiving a request for translation
  from a department, shall forthwith estimate the amount of time
  required to make the translation and determine whether or not its
  normal facilities for serving the department will permit the making of
  the translation within the maximum time available for that purpose.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Bureau is of the opinion that its normal
  facilities for serving a department will not permit the making of a
  translation within the maximum time available for that purpose, the
  Bureau shall communicate with the designated official of the
  department in order

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to obtain an extension of the time available for
    making the translation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to obtain a change in the department's translation
    priorities; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to make any other arrangement with the department
    for making the translation.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Bureau shall make a translation of a document from or into a language
  other than English or French if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the International Translations Index contained in
    the National Science Library of the National Research Council has
    been consulted by the department requesting the translation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where a translation of the document is available
    elsewhere, a reasonable effort has been made by the department to
    obtain it.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a department does not have the facilities to do
  so, the Bureau shall prepare the final presentation of a translation
  of a document from or into a language other than English or French,
  including the typing, page-setting, printing and proofreading of such
  a translation.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** When, in the opinion of the
Superintendent of the Bureau, the Bureau is unable to deal adequately
with requests for translations or interpretations made by departments,
the Bureau may, in accordance with the provisions of the [Government
Contracts Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-402) and in compliance
with any security requirements, engage the services of professional
translators or interpreters as independent contractors.

## [Responsibilities of Departments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-528941 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The head
  of every department that uses the services of the Bureau shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} eliminate the need for translation requests to the
    Bureau based solely on the inability of an employee to read or
    prepare texts in the English or French language;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} designate an employee in his department who shall
    communicate with and receive communications from the Bureau in
    respect of translations and revisions requested from the Bureau by
    the department;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide the Bureau annually with a five-year
    forecast of its translation requirements, including, in the case of
    translation requirements in respect of languages other than English
    or French, a listing of the languages from or into which
    translations are required;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} include in his production schedules for
    publications, the time required for translation, such time to be
    determined in consultation with the Bureau; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} supply to the Bureau's Terminology Centre two
    copies in each language of any document that is published by the
    department in both the English and French languages, upon the
    publication thereof.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A department shall, in the manner determined by its
  designated official and the Bureau, send all documents for translation
  or revision to the Chief of the Translation Division attached to the
  department or, if no Translation Division has been attached to the
  department, to the Director of Production of the Bureau, together with
  two copies of a request for translation in a form approved by the
  Superintendent of the Bureau.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a department makes a request for translation,
  it shall, at the time of making the request, advise the Bureau of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the maximum time available for translation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any security measures that may be necessary in
    respect of the document, in respect of which the translation is
    requested.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A department shall attach to all documents, in
  respect of which translation is requested, any documentation that may
  be of assistance to the Bureau including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the original and translated versions of similar
    previous texts;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any document where the text prepared in consequence
    thereof is submitted for translation into the language used in that
    document;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} previously prepared scientific or technical
    vocabularies; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the source of quotations, information or texts used
    as reference.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where a department intends
to request translation of a scientific, technical or specialized
document that is being prepared in the department, it shall, upon the
completion of the first draft thereof, forward a copy of the draft to
the Bureau in order that terminological research may be commenced by the
Bureau.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Subject to subsection 5(2),
a department shall be responsible for the final presentation of
translations delivered to it by the Bureau, including the typing,
printing, page-setting and proofreading of such translations.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A department shall take
full responsibility for any changes it makes in translations provided by
the Bureau.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Where the Bureau has
attached a Translation Division to a department, the department shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide suitable office space for the Translation
  Division;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} acquire for the library of the Translation Division
  such reference material as the Chief of the Translation Division
  considers necessary for the performance of his duties and, at the
  request of the Chief of the Translation Division, place such reference
  material in the office space occupied by the Translation Division; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} make available to the Translation Division the
  stenographic and typing services required by it to meet emergencies.

## [Interpretation Services]{.HTitleText1} {#h-528965 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  requirements of both Houses of the Parliament of Canada and of the
  committees thereof in respect of interpretation services shall be
  given first priority by the Bureau.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (1), the Bureau shall provide
  interpretation services to departments.

<!-- -->

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  department commences preparation for any conference in respect of
  which it intends to request the Bureau to provide interpretation
  services, it shall notify the Bureau in writing and provide the Bureau
  with any information and documentation that may be useful to the
  Bureau including agenda, working papers, reports, speeches and
  specialized vocabularies.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, in the opinion of the Superintendent of the
  Bureau, a department has not provided sufficient information and
  documentation to allow reasonable preparation to be made by the Bureau
  for provision of interpretation services, the Superintendent may limit
  the services of the Bureau to interpretation that can be made without
  special preparation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A department that has requested interpretation
  services for a conference shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where possible, organize a meeting for the day
    preceding the conference between the employees of the Bureau who are
    to provide the interpretation services and specialists attending the
    conference for the purpose of familiarizing the employees of the
    Bureau with the material to be discussed at the conference;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide and install at the conference the equipment
    deemed necessary by the Bureau to enable the Bureau to provide the
    interpretation services requested; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} make all travel and accommodation arrangements and
    pay all travel expenses in respect of employees of the Bureau who
    are to provide interpretation services at conferences held outside
    of Ottawa.

## [Interdepartmental Committee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-528976 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  Interdepartmental Advisory Committee on Government Translations,
  consisting of eight members, shall consider questions of priority
  resulting from requests made for translations and the provision of
  interpretation services.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Interdepartmental Committee shall consist of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} two members appointed by the Under Secretary of
    State, one of whom shall act as Secretary of the Interdepartmental
    Committee and shall have no vote;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one member appointed by the Secretary of the
    Treasury Board;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Superintendent of the Bureau or his
    representative;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Queen's Printer or his representative;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} one member appointed from the Special Secretariat
    on Bilingualism of the Privy Council; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} two designated officials representing departments
    not otherwise represented, selected according to an annual rotation
    system.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Interdepartmental Committee shall meet once at
  the beginning of each calendar year to choose its chairman and
  thereafter at the call of the chairman.

<!-- -->

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a conflict in priorities arises because of requests made by more than
  one department for translations or the provision of interpretation
  services, the Bureau shall refer the conflict to the Interdepartmental
  Committee for a decision.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The decision of the Interdepartmental Committee shall
  be binding upon all departments.

## [Terminology Centre]{.HTitleText1} {#h-528990 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Bureau shall maintain a Terminology Centre that shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} carry out terminological research and prepare and
    distribute terminological instructions and bulletins;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make its services available to departments; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} cooperate with universities and other institutions
    and agencies involved in terminological research.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Departments shall cooperate with the Terminology
  Centre and supply it with copies of documents containing specialized
  glossaries and vocabularies that come into their possession.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Departments shall seek the assistance of and
  cooperate with the Terminology Centre in the selection of a title for
  any new body under the jurisdiction of the Parliament of Canada in
  respect of which a title in both the English and French languages is
  required.
