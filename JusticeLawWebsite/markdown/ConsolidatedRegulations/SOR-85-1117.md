## Alberta Potato Marketing Levies (Interprovincial and Export) Order {#alberta-potato-marketing-levies-interprovincial-and-export-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/85-1117
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1985-11-29

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Levies From
Potato Producers in the Province of Alberta
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Alberta Potato Commission, pursuant to section 4[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-85-1117_e_hq_6391){#fn_SOR-85-1117_e_hq_6391-ID0EFBBA}
of the [Alberta Potato Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._133), C.R.C.,
c. 133, hereby makes the annexed [Order providing for the fixing,
imposing and collecting of levies from potato producers in the Province
of Alberta](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-1117).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886050 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Alberta Potato Marketing Levies (Interprovincial and Export)
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-1117).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886052 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    business of buying, receiving or selling potatoes in the Province of
    Alberta; ([négociant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[grower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[grower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is a producer of
    potatoes in the Province of Alberta; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[ungraded potatoes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ungraded potatoes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means potatoes that are
    delivered by a grower to any other person without first being graded
    or sorted except to remove potatoes that are so damaged or diseased
    as to be unfit for human consumption. ([pommes de terre non
    classées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886057 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every grower shall pay to
the Commission in respect of potatoes described in an item in Column I
of the schedule that are sold in interprovincial or export trade a levy
in the amount set out in Column II of that item.

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886059 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  grower who sells potatoes in interprovincial or export trade, other
  than to a dealer or a dealer's agent, shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the end of every calendar month, calculate the
    total sales of potatoes made by him or on his behalf in
    interprovincial or export trade; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on or before the 15th day of the following calendar
    month, pay to the Commission at its office at Lethbridge, Alberta,
    the levy referred to in section 3 in respect of the sales referred
    to in paragraph (a).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every dealer who buys or receives potatoes from a
  grower for the purpose of sale in interprovincial or export trade
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deduct from the moneys payable to the grower, the
    levy described in section 3; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pay that levy to the Commission at its office at
    Lethbridge, Alberta, on or before the 15th day of the month
    following the month of sale.
