## C.N.R. Company Exemption Order {#c.n.r.-company-exemption-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1244
:::

[OFFICIAL LANGUAGES ACT](/eng/acts/O-3.01)

Order Exempting the C.N.R. Company from Subsection 10(2) of the Official
Languages Act
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499864 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited
as the [C.N.R. Company Exemption
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1244).

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499866 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Canadian National
Railway Company is hereby exempted from the application of subsection
10(2) of the [Official Languages Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01) in respect of
services provided or made available by it to the public, other than to
the travelling public, at places elsewhere than in Canada.
