## Withdrawal of Entities Regulations {#withdrawal-of-entities-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2000-143
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

[PUBLIC SERVICE SUPERANNUATION ACT](/eng/acts/P-36)

Registration 2000-04-07

Withdrawal of Entities Regulations

T.B. 828066 2000-04-06
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Treasury Board, on the recommendation of the President of the
Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 42.1(1)(v.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EHBAA} of the [Public
Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and paragraph 7(2)(a) of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed
[Withdrawal of Entities Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-143).

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EHBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1999, c.
  34, s. 92(6)
  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656095 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[withdrawing employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[withdrawing employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity or
    portion of an entity which, by virtue of an order made under
    subsection 42(4) of the Act is deleted from Part I or II of Schedule
    I of the Act. ([employeur radié]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656099 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to a person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by virtue of an order made under subsection 42(4)
    of the Act, ceases to be employed in the public service for the
    purposes of the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is employed by the withdrawing employer on the day
    on which the order is made.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to a person who
  subsequently becomes re-employed by the withdrawing employer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 78(E)

## [Applicable Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656107 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Sections 12 to 13.01 of the
Act apply to a person only on and after the day on which the person
ceases to be employed by the withdrawing employer.

## [Survivor and Children]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656109 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 12(8) of the Act, the survivor and children of a person who
dies while employed by the withdrawing employer are entitled to a death
benefit equal to a return of contributions.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 13(3) of the Act, the survivor and children of a person who
dies while employed by the withdrawing employer are entitled to the
allowances prescribed in paragraphs 12(4)(a) and (b) of the Act, subject
to the limitations set out in subsections 12(4) and (5) of the Act.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 26(2) of the Act, a child who was born to or adopted by a
person or became the stepchild of the person, during the period that
begins on the day on which the person ceases to be employed in the
public service for the purposes of the Act and that ends on the day on
which that person ceases to be employed by the withdrawing employer, is
entitled to an allowance under Part I of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 78(E)

## [Adaptation of Subsection 10(5) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656115 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 10(5) of the Act, the one-year period referred to in
paragraph 10(5)(a) of the Act begins on the day on which the person
ceases to be employed by the withdrawing employer.

## [Adaptation of Sections 12 and 13 of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656117 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 and 13 of the Act, pensionable service includes the period
of service with the withdrawing employer that begins on the day on which
the person ceases to be employed in the public service for the purposes
of the Act and that ends on the day on which the person ceases to be
employed by the withdrawing employer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 78(E)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-8-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 8"){.PITLink}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 and 13 of the Act, the age of a person when the person
ceases to be employed in the public service for the purposes of the Act
is the age of the person on the day on which that person ceases to be
employed by the withdrawing employer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 78(E)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous

**[[9.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.1 .sectionLabel}** A contributor to whom
these Regulations apply is considered to be a Group 1 contributor.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 77
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656127 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
