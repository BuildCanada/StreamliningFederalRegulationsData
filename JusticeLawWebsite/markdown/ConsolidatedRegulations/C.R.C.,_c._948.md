## Disposal of Forfeited Goods and Chattels Regulations {#disposal-of-forfeited-goods-and-chattels-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 948
:::

[INDIAN ACT](/eng/acts/I-5)

Regulations Respecting the Disposal of Goods and Chattels Ordered
Forfeited Pursuant to Subsection 103(3) of the Indian Act
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-598107 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Disposal of Forfeited Goods and Chattels
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._948).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-598109 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-598111 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Goods and chattels
forfeited to Her Majesty pursuant to the provisions of subsection 103(3)
of the Act shall be sold at public auction following advertisement
published in such local papers as the Minister may designate.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where any goods and
chattels have been forfeited pursuant to subsection 103(3) of the Act,
anyone (other than the person accused of an offence resulting in such
forfeiture, or the person in possession of such goods and chattels when
the offence was committed) who claims an interest in such goods and
chattels as owner, mortgagee, lien-holder or holder of any like
interest, may, within 30 days after such forfeiture, apply to the
Minister for a determination of his interest.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where, following such
application, it appears to the satisfaction of the Minister,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the claimant is innocent of any complicity in
  the offence resulting in such forfeiture, or of any collusion with the
  offender in relation thereto, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that he exercised all reasonable care in respect of
  the person permitted to obtain the possession of such goods and
  chattels to satisfy himself that they were not likely to be used
  contrary to the provisions of the Act, or, if a mortgagee or
  lien-holder, that before becoming such mortgagee or lien-holder
  exercised such care with respect to the mortgagor or lien-giver,

the Minister may order that the interest of the claimant be not affected
by such forfeiture.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where the circumstances
make it appear to the Minister that the goods and chattels forfeited
pursuant to subsection 103(3) of the Act should in the public interest
be disposed of otherwise than by public auction, the Minister may direct
that they be otherwise disposed of, in which case the direction shall
prescribe such conditions and restrictions as the Minister may deem
necessary or advisable.
