## Order Designating the Commission on the Future of Health Care in Canada as a Department and the Prime Minister as Appropriate Minister {#order-designating-the-commission-on-the-future-of-health-care-in-canada-as-a-department-and-the-prime-minister-as-appropriate-minister .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2001-60
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2001-05-09

Order Designating the Commission on the Future of Health Care in Canada
as a Department and the Prime Minister as Appropriate Minister

P.C. 2001-742 2001-04-26
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFABAA} of the
  definition [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), designates the
  Commission on the Future of Health Care in Canada as a department for
  the purposes of that Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EDCAAA} of the
  definition [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2
  of that Act, designates the Prime Minister as the appropriate Minister
  with respect to the Commission on the Future of Health Care in Canada.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFABAA){#footnotea_e}S.C.
    1992, c. 1, s. 69(3)
    :::
