## Small Manufacturers Production Equipment Exemption Regulations {#small-manufacturers-production-equipment-exemption-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/82-572
:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Registration 1982-06-03

Regulations Exempting Small Manufacturers from the Application of
Paragraph 1(r) of Part XIII of Schedule III to the Excise Tax Act in
Respect of Production Equipment

P.C. 1982-1678 1982-06-03
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue and the Minister of Finance, pursuant
to paragraph 1(r) of Part XIII of Schedule III to the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations
exempting small manufacturers from the application of paragraph 1(r) of
Part XIII of Schedule III to the Excise Tax Act in respect of production
equipment](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-572).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874241 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Small Manufacturers Production Equipment Exemption
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-572).

## [Prescription]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874243 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 1(r) of Part XIII of Schedule III to the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the persons prescribed are manufacturers who

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sell goods of their own manufacture, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} manufacture goods for their own use

that are otherwise subject to the consumption or sales tax, if the value
of the goods manufactured and sold or manufactured for their own use
does not exceed in the aggregate \$50,000 in any calendar year.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Section 2 is effective as
of November 17, 1978.
