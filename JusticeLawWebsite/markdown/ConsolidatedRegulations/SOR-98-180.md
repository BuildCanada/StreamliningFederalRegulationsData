## Ontario Hydro Nuclear Facilities Exclusion from Part II of the Canada Labour Code Regulations (Occupational Health and Safety) {#ontario-hydro-nuclear-facilities-exclusion-from-part-ii-of-the-canada-labour-code-regulations-occupational-health-and-safety .Title-of-Act}

Ontario Hydro Nuclear Facilities Exclusion from Part II of the Canada
Labour Code Regulations (Occupational Health and Safety)

P.C. 1998-408 1998-03-19
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Labour and after consultation with the Atomic Energy
Control Board, pursuant to section 159[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the [Canada
Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2), hereby makes the annexed [Ontario Hydro
Nuclear Facilities Exclusion from Part II of the Canada Labour Code
Regulations (Occupational Health and
Safety)](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-180).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018717 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Occupational Health and Safety
    Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. O.1, except subsections 16(6) and (7) and
    sections 17 to 19. ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means employment with Ontario
    Hydro or a person referred to in paragraph (b) of the definition
    [[nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}. ([emploi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a nuclear facility in
    Ontario that is subject to the [Nuclear Safety and Control
    Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3) or any regulations made under that Act and
    that is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} owned and operated by Ontario Hydro; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} owned or operated by a person other than Ontario
      Hydro if, on the day on which these Regulations come into force or
      on a day after that day, it is owned and operated by Ontario
      Hydro. ([installation nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-13, s. 1
:::

## [Exclusion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018726 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Employment on or in
connection with a nuclear facility is hereby excluded from the
application of Part II of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2),
except sections 158 to 160.

## [Application of Ontario Act and Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018728 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4, the
Act and any regulations made under it, as amended from time to time,
apply, to the extent that they are relevant, in relation to employment
on or in connection with a nuclear facility.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
3,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reference to \"section 158 of the Provincial
  Offences Act\" in subsection 54(2) of the Act and any regulations made
  under the Act shall be read as a reference to \"section 487 of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)\";

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reference to \"sections 159 and 160 of the
  Provincial Offences Act\" in subsection 56(5) of the Act and any
  regulations made under the Act shall be read as a reference to
  \"sections 488.1, 489.1 and 490 of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46)\"; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "a person employed in, or a member
  of, a police force to which the Police Services Act applies" in
  paragraph 43(2)(a) of the Act shall be read as "a person employed in,
  or a member of, a police force to which the Police Services Act
  applies or a member of an on-site nuclear response force, as defined
  in section 1 of the [Nuclear Security
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-209)".

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-186, s. 1
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In case of an inconsistency
between any of the provisions of the [Nuclear Safety and Control
Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3) and any regulations made under that Act relating
to occupational safety and health in relation to employment referred to
in section 2 and the provisions of these Regulations, the Act and the
regulations made under the Act, the provisions of the [Nuclear Safety
and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3) and its regulations prevail to the
extent of the inconsistency.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-13, s. 2
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018739 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on April 1, 1998.
