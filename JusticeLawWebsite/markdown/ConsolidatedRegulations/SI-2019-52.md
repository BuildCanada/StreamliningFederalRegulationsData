## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1178706 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fiscal stabilization overpayments

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted to a
province set out in column 1 of the schedule of the amount set out in
column 2 in respect of the fiscal stabilization overpayments made by the
Minister of Finance under the [Federal-Provincial Fiscal Arrangements
Act](/eng/acts/F-7.8), for the fiscal year beginning April 1, 2015 and
ending March 31, 2016, to avoid any negative impact on the province's
fiscal situation.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1178709 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
