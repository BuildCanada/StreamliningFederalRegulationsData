## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858467 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Personal Information Protection
and Electronic Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6).

## [Report to Commissioner]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858470 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report --- content, form and manner

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A report
  of a breach of security safeguards referred to in subsection 10.1(2)
  of the Act must be in writing and must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the circumstances of the breach
    and, if known, the cause;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which, or the period during which, the
    breach occurred or, if neither is known, the approximate period;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the personal information that is
    the subject of the breach to the extent that the information is
    known;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number of individuals affected by the breach
    or, if unknown, the approximate number;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the steps that the organization
    has taken to reduce the risk of harm to affected individuals that
    could result from the breach or to mitigate that harm;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the steps that the organization
    has taken or intends to take to notify affected individuals of the
    breach in accordance with subsection 10.1(3) of the Act; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of a person who
    can answer, on behalf of the organization, the Commissioner's
    questions about the breach.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An organization may submit to the Commissioner any
  new information referred to in subsection (1) that the organization
  becomes aware of after having made the report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of communication

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The report may be sent to the Commissioner by any
  secure means of communication.

## [Notification to Affected Individual]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858485 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notification

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A notification provided by
an organization, in accordance with subsection 10.1(3) of the Act, to an
affected individual with respect to a breach of security safeguards must
contain

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the circumstances of the breach;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which, or period during which, the breach
  occurred or, if neither is known, the approximate period;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the personal information that is the
  subject of the breach to the extent that the information is known;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the steps that the organization has
  taken to reduce the risk of harm that could result from the breach;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the steps that affected individuals
  could take to reduce the risk of harm that could result from the
  breach or to mitigate that harm; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} contact information that the affected individual can
  use to obtain further information about the breach.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direct notification --- form and manner

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 10.1(5) of the Act, direct notification must be given to the
affected individual in person, by telephone, mail, email or any other
form of communication that a reasonable person would consider
appropriate in the circumstances.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indirect notification --- circumstances

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 10.1(5) of the Act, indirect notification must
  be given by an organization in any of the following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} direct notification would be likely to cause
    further harm to the affected individual;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} direct notification would be likely to cause undue
    hardship for the organization; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the organization does not have contact information
    for the affected individual.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indirect notification --- form and
  manner

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 10.1(5) of the Act,
  indirect notification must be given by public communication or similar
  measure that could reasonably be expected to reach the affected
  individuals.

## [Record-keeping]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858504 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record-keeping requirements

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 10.3(1) of the Act, an organization must
  maintain a record of every breach of security safeguards for 24 months
  after the day on which the organization determines that the breach has
  occurred.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The record referred to in subsection 10.3(1) of the
  Act must contain any information that enables the Commissioner to
  verify compliance with subsections 10.1(1) and (3) of the Act.
