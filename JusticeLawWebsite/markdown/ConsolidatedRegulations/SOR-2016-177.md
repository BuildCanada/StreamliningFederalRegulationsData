## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831807 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[cellulose fibre insulation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cellulose fibre insulation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means cellulose
    fibre for use as loose-fill thermal insulation in the interior of
    new and existing buildings. ([isolant en fibres
    cellulosiques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insulation standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insulation standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Underwriters
    Laboratories of Canada standard CAN/ULC-S703, entitled Standard for
    Cellulose Fibre Insulation for Buildings, as amended from time to
    time. ([norme sur l'isolant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Specifications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831812 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum critical radiant flux

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  cellulose fibre insulation is tested in accordance with section 6.3.9
  of the insulation standard, all three test specimens must have a
  critical radiant flux greater than or equal to 0.12 W/cm^2^.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional specimens

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), if only one of the three test
  specimens has a critical radiant flux less than 0.12 W/cm^2^, three
  more specimens must be tested, all of which must have a critical
  radiant flux greater than or equal to 0.12 W/cm^2^.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Combustion and loss of mass

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Cellulose fibre insulation
must not exhibit any flaming combustion or any loss of mass greater than
15% when tested in accordance with sections 6.3.3 and 6.3.12 of the
insulation standard.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Separation of non-cellulosic components

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Cellulose fibre insulation
must not exhibit any separation of non-cellulosic components greater
than 1.5% by mass when tested in accordance with section 6.3.11 of the
insulation standard.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corrosiveness --- metal

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A test specimen that is
made of aluminum, copper or steel must not exhibit any perforation when
cellulose fibre insulation is tested in accordance with section 6.3.2 of
the insulation standard.

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831824 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), cellulose fibre insulation that is sold to a
  consumer must have the following information marked on its container
  legibly and in a prominent location:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and principal place of business in Canada
    of either of the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer, if the insulation is
      manufactured in Canada, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the importer, if the insulation is imported; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day, month and year of its manufacture.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- no container

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the cellulose fibre insulation has no container,
  the information must be marked on the packaging or, if there is no
  packaging, on the bill of sale or other document given to the consumer
  at the time of sale.
