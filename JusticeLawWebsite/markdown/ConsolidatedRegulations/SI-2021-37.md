## Order Designating the Minister of Justice as the Minister for the Purposes of that Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-justice-as-the-minister-for-the-purposes-of-that-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2021-37
:::

[UNITED NATIONS DECLARATION ON THE RIGHTS OF INDIGENOUS PEOPLES
ACT](/eng/acts/U-2.2)

Registration 2021-07-07

Order Designating the Minister of Justice as the Minister for the
Purposes of that Act

P.C. 2021-635 2021-06-24
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Administrator of the Government of Canada in Council,
on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of
the [United Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples
Act](/eng/acts/U-2.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_320_hq_10667){#fn_320_hq_10667-ID0EBBAA},
designates the Minister of Justice, who is a federal minister, to be the
Minister for the purposes of that Act.
