## Order Designating the Minister of Industry as the Minister for the Purposes of the Civil Lunar Gateway Agreement Implementation Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-industry-as-the-minister-for-the-purposes-of-the-civil-lunar-gateway-agreement-implementation-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2022-52
:::

[CIVIL LUNAR GATEWAY AGREEMENT IMPLEMENTATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-31.4)

Registration 2022-10-26

Order Designating the Minister of Industry as the Minister for the
Purposes of the Civil Lunar Gateway Agreement Implementation Act

P.C. 2022-1090 2022-10-07
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under section 5 of the [Civil Lunar Gateway
Agreement Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-31.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4922-E_hq_1660845335){#fn_81000-2-4922-E_hq_1660845335-ID0EBBAA},
designates the Minister of Industry, a member of the King's Privy
Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act,
effective October 26, 2022.
