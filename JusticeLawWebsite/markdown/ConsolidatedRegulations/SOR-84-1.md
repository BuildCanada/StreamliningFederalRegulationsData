## New Brunswick Hog Marketing Levies (Interprovincial and Export Trade) Order {#new-brunswick-hog-marketing-levies-interprovincial-and-export-trade-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/84-1
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1983-12-19

Order Respecting Levies on Hogs Produced in New Brunswick and Marketed
in Interprovincial and Export Trade
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The New Brunswick Hog Marketing Board pursuant to section 4 of the [New
Brunswick Hog Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-42), made by Order in
Council P.C. 1981-3629 of 17th December 1981[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBBA}, hereby makes the annexed
Order respecting levies on hogs produced in New Brunswick and marketed
in interprovincial and export trade.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBBA){#fn_1e}SOR/82-42, 1982 [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 183
  :::

Moncton, New Brunswick, December 2, 1983
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-881901 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [New Brunswick Hog Marketing Levies (Interprovincial and Export
Trade) Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-1).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-881903 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person appointed by the Local
    Board to conduct business on its behalf and includes a person
    designated by the Local Board to receive levies payable to it;
    ([agent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an animal of the porcine species
    produced in New Brunswick; ([porc]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Local Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Local Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Porc NB Pork;
    ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production of hogs. ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-181, s. 1
:::

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-881911 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  producer shall pay to the Local Board or to its agent a levy of \$1.45
  plus HST per hog marketed outside the province of New Brunswick.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The levies payable by a producer to the Local Board
  under this Order shall be deducted from the moneys payable to the
  producer by the Local Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/84-464, s. 1(F)
- SOR/2004-181, s. 2
- SOR/2005-229, s. 1
:::
