## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1023925 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an officer designated by the
    Minister under subsection 58(1) or 59(1) of the Act.
    ([agent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Time Period]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1023929 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The time period within
which an officer may further re-determine the origin, tariff
classification or value for duty under paragraph 59(1)(b) of the Act is
five years from the date of the determination under section 58 of the
Act, where the granting of the refund or the making of a correction
referred to in paragraph 59(1)(b) of the Act occurs within the period of
time beginning on the first day of the 37th month and ending on the last
day of the 48th month after the date on which the determination was made
under section 58.

## [Notice]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1023931 .Part}

### [Determination, Re-determination and Further Re-determination of Origin, Tariff Classification and Value for Duty by a Designated Officer under Sections 58 and 59 of the Act]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1023932 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an
  officer makes a determination of origin of imported goods pursuant to
  subsection 58(1) of the Act, or a re-determination or further
  re-determination of origin of imported goods pursuant to
  subsection 59(1) of the Act, the officer shall give notice of the
  determination, re-determination or further re-determination, as the
  case may be,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the importer of the goods,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the owner of the goods at the time of release,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any person liable for payment of duties on the
      goods at the time of release, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the person who accounted for the goods under
      subsection 32(1), (3) or (5) of the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where preferential tariff treatment under a free
    trade agreement was claimed in respect of those goods, to the person
    who has completed and signed a Certificate of Origin for the goods.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an officer makes a determination of tariff
  classification or value for duty of imported goods pursuant to
  subsection 58(1) of the Act, or a re-determination or further
  re-determination pursuant to subsection 59(1) of the Act, the officer
  shall give notice of the determination, re-determination or further
  re-determination, as the case may be, to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the importer of the goods;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the owner of the goods at the time of release;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person liable for payment of duties on the
    goods at the time of release, or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person who accounted for the goods under
    subsection 32(1), (3) or (5) of the Act.

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** Despite
subsection 3(1), in the case of a determination that is treated under
subsection 97.201(5) of the Act as if it were a re-determination under
paragraph 59(1)(a) of the Act, the officer shall give notice of
re-determination, referred to in subsection 59(2) of the Act, to the
exporter or producer of the goods subject to the determination.

## [Payment of Duties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1023967 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The persons to whom
paragraphs 59(3)(a) and 65(1)(a) of the Act apply are those persons who
were given notice of the determination, re-determination or further
re-determination, as the case may be, under section 3 or 4, other than a
person referred to in paragraph 3(1)(b) or 4(1)(b).

## [Refund of Duties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1023969 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The persons to whom
paragraphs 59(3)(b) and 65(1)(b) of the Act apply are those persons who
were given notice of the determination, re-determination or further
re-determination, as the case may be, under section 3 or 4 --- other
than a person referred to in paragraph 3(1)(b) or 4(1)(b) or a person
authorized under paragraph 32(6)(a) or subsection 32(7) of the Act to
account for goods.
