## Oil and Gas Land Order No. 2-1961 {#oil-and-gas-land-order-no.-2-1961 .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1520
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Order Respecting the Granting of Oil and Gas Leases upon Tender for
Canada Lands Returned to Her Majesty
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526392 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
[Oil and Gas Land Order No. 2-1961](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1520).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526394 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Oil and Gas Land
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1518) and all other words and
phrases used in this Order have the same meaning as in those
Regulations.

## [Oil and Gas Leases]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526396 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Tenders may be called for
oil and gas leases for the Canada lands referred to in subsection 57(1)
of the Regulations that have not been disposed of under Oil and Gas Land
Order No. 1-1961.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Tenders for an oil and gas
lease will, pursuant to section 57 of the Regulations, be called upon in
one of the following sets of terms and conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a cash bonus whereby the lessee will, in addition to
  complying with all provisions of the Regulations, pay to Her Majesty a
  fixed sum of money;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an exploratory work bonus whereby the lessee will, in
  addition to complying with all the provisions of the Regulations,
  deposit with the Chief money, bonds or an approved note of a value
  equal to the amount that the lessee undertakes to expend for
  exploratory work on the lease area within one or more fixed periods;
  or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a cash bonus plus a drilling commitment whereby the
  lessee will, in addition to complying with all the provisions of the
  Regulations, pay to Her Majesty a fixed sum of money and undertake to
  drill a well within the lease area to a fixed depth or horizon within
  one or more fixed periods.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where a lease is granted on
the condition of an exploratory work bonus,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of expenditure made by the lessee under
  the condition shall be determined in the same way as the allowable
  expenditure referred to in section 43 of the Regulations is
  determined;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the portion of the deposit equal to the expenditure
  made during the period set out in the condition shall be returned to
  the permittee;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the portion of the deposit not returned to the
  permittee is forfeited to Her Majesty; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} expenditure incurred by the lessee in order to obtain
  a return of the deposit cannot be used to effect a reduction of rental
  pursuant to section 80 of the Regulations.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where an oil and gas lease
is granted on the condition of a cash bonus plus a drilling commitment

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the size of the oil and gas lease area will vary with
  the location of the area and the depth of the potential reservoir
  horizons;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the oil and gas lease will be cancelled if the lessee
  does not, within the period set out in the condition, drill to
  commercial production, to a precambrian horizon or to the depth set
  out in the condition;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the period within which the well is to be drilled may
  be extended by the Chief, where in the opinion of the Chief,
  operations for the drilling of the well have been commenced; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} expenditure incurred by the lessee in drilling the
  well referred to in the condition cannot be used to effect a reduction
  of rental pursuant to section 80 of the Regulations.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Any person may request that
an oil and gas lease be offered for tender under any one of the sets of
terms and conditions set out in this Order.

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), every oil and gas lease granted under this Order is
  granted upon the condition that the lessee must be a person or
  corporation that meets the requirements of subsection 54(2) of the
  Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to an oil and gas lease
  granted under this Order for Canada lands that have been held under an
  earlier permit where the oil and gas lease is issued to the person or
  corporation who held that earlier permit.
