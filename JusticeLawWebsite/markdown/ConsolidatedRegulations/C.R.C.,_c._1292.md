## Reproduction of Postage Stamps Regulations {#reproduction-of-postage-stamps-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1292
:::

[CANADA POST CORPORATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10)

Regulations Respecting the Reproduction of Postage Stamps
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502346 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Reproduction of Postage Stamps
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1292).

## [Reproductions Authorized]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502348 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 and
4, reproductions of postage stamps

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} may be made and published to illustrate or accompany
  a news item or article in a newspaper, magazine or periodical; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} may be made and published

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in a philatelic publication or catalogue, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on the stationery of a philatelic club or society,
    other than on envelopes or other like containers or articles.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Any reproduction made and
published pursuant to section 2 shall be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least 50 per cent larger or 50 per cent smaller,
  by area measurement, than the postage stamp of which it is a
  reproduction; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} clearly defaced by means of a line that is embodied
  in the plate or die from which the reproduction is made.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Plates or dies used or
employed in the reproduction of postage stamps pursuant to section 2
shall be made entirely of babbit or other soft metal or material.

## [Reproduction with Consent of the Canada Post Corporation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502358 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In addition to
reproductions of postage stamps authorized by section 2, reproduction of
postage stamps may, where the Canada Post Corporation considers that it
will be in the public interest, be made or published with the written
consent of the Canada Post Corporation, which consent may be sought by
an application in writing addressed to the following address:
