## National Parks Cemetery Regulations {#national-parks-cemetery-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/83-677
:::

[CANADA NATIONAL PARKS ACT](/eng/acts/N-14.01)

Registration 1983-08-24

Regulations Respecting the Control and Management of Cemeteries in the
National Parks of Canada

P.C. 1983-2584 1983-08-24
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to subsection 7(1) of the
National Parks Act, is pleased hereby to revoke the [National Parks
Cemetery Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-677), C.R.C., c. 1117, and
to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the control and management
of cemeteries in the National Parks of
Canada](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-677), in substitution therefor.
:::

## [Reserved Areas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-880741 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent may
divide a cemetery into areas and may reserve

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an area for the burial of the remains of persons not
  more than one year of age;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an area for the burial of ashes; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an area for the burial of the remains of persons who
  at anytime served with or were attached to the naval, army or air
  forces of Her Majesty or a power allied or associated with Her
  Majesty.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-32, s. 9(F)
- SOR/92-675, s. 2(E)
:::

## [Cemetery Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-880748 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent shall
maintain a register for a cemetery in which shall be entered,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of each grave in the cemetery for which a
  certificate has been issued, the name and address of the person to
  whom the certificate was issued, the date of issue of the certificate
  and the amount paid therefor, the number of the certificate and such
  other particulars as the superintendent considers necessary; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of each person whose remains or ashes are
  buried in a grave in the cemetery,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} his name, address prior to death, nationality,
    religion, age and sex,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the next of kin of the
    person or the nearest relative of the person,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date and cause of death,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the name of the attending physician,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the date of burial, and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} such other particulars as the superintendent
    considers necessary.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-32, ss. 3, 9(F)
- SOR/92-675, s. 2(E)
:::

## [Use of Plot]{.HTitleText1} {#h-880760 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No person shall use a plot
or grave for any purpose other than for the burial of human remains or
ashes.

## [Certificates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-880762 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
certificate shall be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in Form A set out in Schedule II; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} accompanied by the applicable fee.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-477, s. 2
:::

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the superintendent receives an application that complies with the
  requirements of section 7 for a certificate for a grave and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is applying for the certificate in
    respect of the burial of the remains or ashes of a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} before his death was a permanent resident of one
      or more parks for a period of at least 25 years in the aggregate
      and at sometime lived in the park where the burial is to take
      place, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at the time of his death was a permanent
      resident of the park where the burial is to take place,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is applying for a certificate in
    respect of the burial of the remains or ashes of a person who is a
    next of kin of a person for whose remains or ashes a certificate has
    been issued under paragraph (a), and the person in respect of whose
    burial the application is made

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} lived in one or more parks for a period of at
      least 25 years in the aggregate, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} was, at the time of the death of the person
      described in paragraph (a), a permanent resident of the park where
      the burial is to take place,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/90-32, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

  the superintendent shall, if there are any available graves in the
  cemetery, select such a grave and issue a certificate to the applicant
  in respect of that grave in Form B set out in Schedule II.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A certificate issued pursuant to subsection (1) is
  valid for 10 years and may be renewed once by the certificate holder
  at no additional charge for an additional period of 10 years.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-32, ss. 4, 9(F)
- SOR/92-675, s. 2(E)
:::

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  certificate is issued on or after the date of the coming into force of
  these Regulations and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a certificate that has not been
    renewed, no burial takes place in the grave before the expiration of
    a period of 10 years from the date of issue of the certificate, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a certificate that has been renewed
    in accordance with subsection 8(2), no burial takes place in the
    grave and a new application is not received before the expiration of
    the renewal period,

  the certificate becomes void on the day following the expiration of
  the initial period of 10 years or the renewal period, as the case may
  be, and the interest in that grave of any person named in the
  certificate is thereby terminated without compensation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a certificate was issued before the date of the
  coming into force of these Regulations and no burial takes place in
  the grave before the expiration of a period of 49 years from the date
  of issue of the certificate, the certificate becomes void on the day
  following the expiration of that period and the interest of any person
  in that grave is thereby terminated without compensation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The holder of a certificate issued pursuant to
  subsection 8(1) that has expired and may no longer be renewed may
  reacquire an interest in the grave for which the certificate was
  issued by submitting a new application therefor in accordance with
  section 7.

## [Burials]{.HTitleText1} {#h-880792 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall bury or cause to be buried any human remains or ashes in
  a grave unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} he complies with the laws of the province in which
    the cemetery is located respecting cremation and the burial of human
    remains or ashes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} he applies to the superintendent for authorization
    to bury the human remains or ashes; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the superintendent has authorized in writing the
    burial of the human remains or ashes and the opening of a grave
    therefor.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding paragraph (1)(b), where the human
  remains or ashes are those of a person whose identity is unknown or
  who is an indigent, no application to the superintendent for the
  authorization referred to in paragraph (b) is required.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-32, s. 9(F)
- SOR/92-675, s. 2(E)
:::

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** An application referred
to in paragraph 11(1)(b) shall be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} endorsed by the applicant on his own behalf or on
  behalf of the estate of the person whose remains or ashes are to be
  buried; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} accompanied by the applicable fee and the
  authorization for burial or cremation that is required by the laws of
  the province in which the cemetery is located.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-576, s. 1
- SOR/96-477, s. 3
:::

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent shall,
on receipt of an application referred to in section 12, authorize in
writing the burial of human remains or ashes in a grave and the opening
of the grave for that purpose where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is the holder of a certificate for that
  grave; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person whose remains or ashes are to be buried
  was, at the time of his death, the holder of a certificate for that
  grave.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-32, s. 9(F)
- SOR/92-675, s. 2(E)
:::

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent shall,
on receipt of any authorization for burial or cremation required by the
laws of the province in which the cemetery is located, authorize in
writing the opening of a grave and burial of human remains or ashes
where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is an available grave; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person whose remains or ashes are to be buried is
  an indigent or his identity is unknown.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-32, s. 9(F)
- SOR/92-675, s. 2(E)
- SOR/96-477, s. 4
:::

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** No person shall bury or
cause to be buried

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ashes of a person in a grave that is less than 1
  m below the surface of the ground surrounding the grave;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the remains of a child one week old or less in a
  grave that is less than 1 m below the surface of the ground
  surrounding the grave; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other human remains in a grave that is less than
  2 m below the surface of the ground surrounding the grave.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** No person shall bury in
one grave more than one person's remains or ashes, except where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the remains of a mother and her child one week old or
  less are buried at the same time;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the ashes of not more than three persons are buried;
  or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the remains of one person and the ashes of not more
  than two persons are buried.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-675, s. 1
:::

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/85-576, s.
  2\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Burial of ashes shall not be permitted during the
  period commencing on November 1 in any year and ending on April 30 in
  the following year.

## [Planting, Construction and other Work]{.HTitleText1} {#h-880832 .Part}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall, in a cemetery, plant any trees, shrubs, hedges, flowers,
  grass or other vegetation or construct, erect or place any foundation,
  fence, railing, curbing, monument, vault, mausoleum, tablet or other
  structure or do any other construction or any alteration work

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} without written authorization from the
    superintendent to do so;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on a Sunday or other holiday;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} between the hours of eight o'clock in the evening
    and eight o'clock in the morning; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} within 30 m of any grave in which a burial is
    taking place.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(a) does not apply to any person who,
  with the permission of the superintendent, does work in a cemetery
  that is necessary for the routine operation, maintenance,
  administration or use of the cemetery.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-32, s. 9(F)
- SOR/92-675, s. 2(E)
:::

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-880842 .Part}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the superintendent receives an application from the holder of a
  certificate for authorization to construct, erect or place a
  foundation, monument or tablet on the grave to which the certificate
  relates and the superintendent is satisfied that, based on the plans
  and specifications submitted to him with the application, the proposed
  foundation, monument or tablet complies with the requirements of
  section 22, he shall give written authorization to the holder of the
  certificate to do so.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the planting of vegetation or the construction,
  erection or placing of a structure or any other construction or any
  alteration work is necessary for the operation, maintenance,
  administration or use of a cemetery, the superintendent may give
  written authorization to any person to do so.

## [Foundations, Monuments and Tablets]{.HTitleText1} {#h-880852 .Part}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  monument or tablet authorized to be constructed, erected or placed
  pursuant to subsection 20(1) shall be confined within the limits of
  the grave in respect of which it is constructed, erected or placed and
  shall, where possible, be aligned with other monuments or tablets on
  adjoining graves.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A monument or tablet shall be placed on a concrete
  foundation not less than 10 cm thick and not less than 10 cm wider or
  10 cm longer than the base of the monument or tablet it supports.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The foundation of a tablet shall be so placed that
  the top of the tablet is level with the surrounding ground.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The area of a tablet shall not exceed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a grave in which human remains are
    buried, .20 m^2^; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a grave in which ashes are buried,
    .10 m^2^.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A monument shall not exceed 1 m in height above the
  level of the surrounding ground and shall be of such other dimensions
  and design as not to be, in the opinion of the superintendent,
  offensive to the majority of people visiting the cemetery.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} No person shall place an inscription on any marker,
  monument or tablet without the written approval of the superintendent.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall not give his written
  approval for an inscription referred to in subsection (6) if, in his
  opinion, the inscription would be offensive to the majority of the
  people visiting the cemetery.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-32, s. 9(F)
- SOR/92-675, s. 2(E)
:::

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  any person constructs, erects or places any monument or tablet in a
  cemetery in a manner that fails to comply with section 19, 20 or 22,
  the superintendent may order the person, by notice in writing mailed
  to him at his last known address, to remove the monument or tablet.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the monument or tablet referred to in
  subsection (1) has not been removed within 30 days from the date of
  receipt of the notice referred to in that subsection, the
  superintendent may remove it.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the whereabouts of the person referred to in
  subsection (1) is unknown, the superintendent may, after taking
  reasonable steps to notify the person, remove the monument or tablet
  referred to in that subsection.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-32, ss. 6, 9(F)
- SOR/92-675, s. 2(E)
:::

## [Exhumations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-880871 .Part}

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall exhume human remains or ashes from a cemetery without the
  written authorization of the superintendent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for the exhumation of human remains or
  ashes from a cemetery shall be made in writing to the superintendent
  and shall be accompanied by the applicable fee and any authorization
  for exhumation that is required by the laws of the province in which
  the cemetery is located.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the superintendent receives an application that
  complies with the requirements of subsection (2) for the exhumation of
  human remains or ashes from a grave, he shall give written
  authorization to the applicant for that exhumation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-576, s. 3
- SOR/90-32, s. 9(F)
- SOR/92-675, s. 2(E)
- SOR/96-477, s. 6
:::

## [General Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-880878 .Part}

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall place in a cemetery

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any stand, holder, vase or other receptacle for
    flowers or plants, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any ornament

  that is in a state of disrepair or is no longer suitable for the
  purpose for which it is intended to be used.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may remove from a cemetery any
  article referred to in subsection (1) and, if he does so, shall notify
  the holder of the certificate of the grave in respect of which the
  article was placed in the cemetery.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-32, s. 9(F)
- SOR/92-675, s. 2(E)
:::

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** No person shall take an
animal into a cemetery.

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** No person shall drive a
vehicle over any area in a cemetery other than a driveway unless
authorized by the superintendent.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-32, s. 9(F)
- SOR/92-675, s. 2(E)
:::

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** No person shall cover a
grave with a slab of cement, crushed rock or other similar material.
