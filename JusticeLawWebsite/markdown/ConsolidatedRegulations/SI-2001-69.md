## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605305 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this section apply in this Order.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/E-15). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).
      ([Indien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a reserve within the meaning of subsection 2(1)
        of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5);

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Category IA or IA-N lands as defined in
        subsection 2(1) of the Cree-Naskapi (of Quebec) Act; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an Indian settlement as defined in either the
        [Indians and Bands on certain Indian Settlements Remission
        Order](/eng/regulations/SI-92-102) or the [Indians and Bands on
        Certain Indian Settlements Remission Order
        (1997)](/eng/regulations/SI-97-127). ([réserve]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[road vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[road vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a motor vehicle than
      can be driven on a highway, other than a vehicle that runs only on
      rails or an electrically propelled wheelchair, and includes a
      trailer, semi-trailer or a detachable axle. ([véhicule
      routier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the goods and services tax
      imposed under Division II of Part IX of the Act.
      ([taxe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} All other words and expressions have the same meaning
  as in subsection 123(1) of the Act.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605317 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted to an Indian of tax paid on the supply of a
road vehicle acquired in Quebec during the period beginning on December
1, 1998 and ending on January 31, 2000.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605319 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on
condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the road vehicle was acquired by an Indian outside a
  reserve for the personal use and consumption of the Indian, and,
  except where the Indian is a small supplier, not for use in a
  commercial activity of the Indian;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the road vehicle was not delivered to a reserve;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the tax paid has not been otherwise rebated, refunded
  or remitted under the Act or the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an application for remission is submitted in writing
  to the Minister of National Revenue within one year after the coming
  into force of this Order, together with the following documentation:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the Certificate of Indian Status card
    issued to the Indian by the Department of Indian Affairs and
    Northern Development,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the invoice or agreement relating to the
    supply of the road vehicle, stating the name and address of the
    supplier, the date of the supply, the name of the Indian, the value
    of the consideration paid for the supply and a sufficiently detailed
    description of the road vehicle, including its vehicle
    identification number, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} proof that the tax payable on the supply of the
    road vehicle has been paid.
