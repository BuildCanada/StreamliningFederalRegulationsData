## Weather Modification Information Regulations {#weather-modification-information-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1604
:::

[WEATHER MODIFICATION INFORMATION ACT](/eng/acts/W-5)

Regulations Prescribing the Registration and Provision of Information
Respecting Weather Modification Activities within Canada and Canadian
Waters
::::

:::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-532386 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Weather Modification Information
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1604).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-532388 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Weather Modification
    Information Act](/eng/acts/W-5); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[weather modifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[weather modifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who
    engages in any weather modification activity. ([agent de la
    modification du temps]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Designation of Administrator]{.HTitleText1} {#h-532392 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Assistant Deputy
Minister, Atmospheric Environment Service, Department of the Environment
is designated Administrator for the purposes of the Act and these
Regulations.

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-532394 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  information specified in Schedule I is hereby prescribed for the
  purposes of subsection 3(1) of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information specified in Schedule I shall be sent
  to the Administrator by registered mail at least 10 days before the
  commencement of the weather modification activity to which the
  information relates.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a person who has provided the Administrator
  with information in accordance with subsection (2) proposes to make a
  change in the weather modification activity to which the information
  relates, that person shall forthwith by registered mail provide the
  Administrator with appropriate changes in that information.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a person who has provided the Administrator
  with information in accordance with subsection (2) decides to abandon
  the proposed weather modification activity to which the information
  relates, that person shall forthwith by registered mail notify the
  Administrator of his decision to abandon the proposed activity.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (2), where in the opinion
  of the Administrator there is any reasonable cause why the information
  required by that subsection cannot be provided at least 10 days before
  the commencement of a weather modification activity, he may at his
  discretion waive the 10 days notice period.

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-532401 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A daily record of a weather
modification activity maintained pursuant to paragraph 4(1)(a) of the
Act shall be maintained in the form and manner set out in Schedule II.

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-532403 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A report
  to be submitted to the Administrator pursuant to paragraph 4(1)(b) of
  the Act shall be submitted in the form and manner set out in Schedule
  III subject to such modifications as may be necessary in order for the
  form to contain any information or observations specified by the
  Administrator.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where more than one report is submitted to the
  Administrator pursuant to paragraph 4(1)(b) of the Act in respect of
  the same weather modification activity, any subsequent reports may be
  abbreviated by clear reference to any preceding reports in respect of
  any factual details that remain unchanged.

## [SCHEDULE I]{.scheduleLabel}[(s. 4)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-532408 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** *General Identification*

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} date of notice;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} province and general area of proposed activity or
    operations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} date operations expected to begin;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} expected total duration of operating period;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} primary atmospheric elements to be affected;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} primary results expected, i.e. increase, suppress,
    disperse, etc.;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} class of operation, e.g. research, operational,
    experimental, etc.;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} whether mainly ground operation or airborne, or
    both;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} class of beneficiary, e.g. agriculture, forestry,
    etc.

- **2** *General Information Concerning Weather Modifier*

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name of organization;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} address of organization;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} telephone number of organization;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} name of chief officer of organization;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} title of chief officer of organization;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} permanent address of chief officer of organization;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} telephone number of chief of organization;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} experience and qualifications of chief officer of
    organization or of a designated technical or operations officer, as
    related to the proposed weather modification activity;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if subsidiary or affiliate, name and address of
    parent organization;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} other activities of organization;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} public liability insurance carried applicable to
    the proposed weather modification activity and to any consequential
    effects;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} list of up to five similar weather modification
    activities, clearly identified, undertaken by organization

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} elsewhere;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} other representative weather modification
    activities previously undertaken by organization, stated briefly
    with references;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} type of any of the operations under this proposal
    to be subcontracted to other persons, and to whom.

- **3** *General Information Concerning Organization for Whom Activity
  is to be Conducted*

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name of organization;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} address of organization;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} telephone number of organization;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} name of chief officer of organization;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} title of chief officer of organization;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} permanent address of chief officer of organization;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} telephone number of chief officer of organization;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} nature of organization.

- **4** *General Information Concerning all Field Bases of Activity*

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} primary field base of organization for the
    operation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} address and location of primary field base;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} telephone number of primary field base;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} specific locations of any secondary field bases and
    their functions.

- **5** *General Information Concerning Operating Field Personnel*

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name of field officer in charge;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} title of field officer in charge;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} permanent address of field officer in charge;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} permanent telephone number of field officer in
    charge;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} brief statement of experience and qualifications of
    field officer in charge;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} field address of field officer in charge;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} field telephone number of field officer in charge;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} total numbers of field personnel to be employed
    full and part time in operations;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} names, addresses and telephone numbers of persons
    having custody of daily record of activities as specified in section
    5 of these Regulations.

- **6** *General Information Concerning Proposed Activity*

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} reasons of organization for whom activity is to be
    conducted for seeking weather modification;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specific modifications sought;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} quantitative estimate of modification effect
    expected;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any secondary effects that may be anticipated;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} geographic area to be affected by the operation (a
    detailed map, of a scale equal to or greater than 1:500,000, of the
    area to be affected must be enclosed with the boundaries clearly
    marked);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} estimate of any geographic area that might be
    affected outside the area indicated in paragraph (e);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} approximate total cost of activity;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} approximate total funds to be expended in Canada in
    the operation;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} general periods of operation (e.g. --- days with
    warm fog only).

- **7** *General Information Concerning Operations and Techniques*

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} general

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} method(s) to be used,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} general principle(s) of technique(s) to effect
      modification, i.e. outline any modification models or hypothesis
      on which activity is based,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} brief description of specific technique used in
      operation,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} brief description of typical operation,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} if the weather modifier considers himself to hold
      proprietary rights or things patentable concerning any aspect of
      the operation, these should be specified,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} any patents held or applied for concerning any
      aspects of the operation should be specified, indicating by whom
      and where;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} equipment to effect modification

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} types of equipment to be used, including for each
      type

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} common nomenclature of equipment,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} numbers,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} general principle of operation,

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} where applicable a brief description of the
        functional aspects in terms applicable to its usually intended
        purpose and principle of operation, e.g. for a silver iodide
        smoke generator --- ice nucleating smoke output in numbers of
        ice nuclei per unit time active at a specified temperature,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} proposed location or operating locale of any
      equipment used, clearly indicated on a map referred to in
      paragraph 6(e) of this Schedule or equivalent,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any patents held or applied for, for any
      equipment used in the operation, indicating by whom and where
      applied for, and the patent number;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} material(s) to be emitted or dispersed into the
    atmosphere, including for each where applicable:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} general class,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} generic name,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} common name,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} chemical composition and nature,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} physical state,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} size(s) of particles,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} dispersal rate(s) per hour,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} dispersal rate(s) per period of operation,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} maximum rate of emission,

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} total quantities to be dispersed,

    - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} expected hours of operation of any equipment or
      of operations emitting or dispersing materials into the
      atmosphere, with expected daily totals of hours of operation and
      total for the complete activity, and

    - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} toxic, corrosive or other harmful effects of
      any of the materials, however minor, where specifically known
      indicating any known references or any tests performed; otherwise,
      an assessment of any possible harmful effects. (The modifier
      should assure himself that there are no violations of the Clean
      Air Act or other legislation and provision of information under
      this Schedule does not signify approval or compliance with any
      other legislation.)

- **8** *General Information Concerning the Use of any Aircraft or
  Airborne Equipment*

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether aircraft or any airborne equipment is to be
    used indicating number of vehicles and equipment of each category;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the valid "Operating Certificate" issued
    under the authority of the Minister of Transport pursuant to the
    [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), appertaining to any airborne
    operations of the activity. (The aircraft operator or modifier
    should assure himself that there will be no violations of the
    Canadian [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), Air Regulations, Air
    Navigation Orders or other appertaining legislation and provision of
    information under this Schedule does not constitute approval or
    compliance with any Act other than the [Weather Modification
    Information Act](/eng/acts/W-5).)

- **9** *General Information Concerning Use of any Ground Vehicles*

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} numbers and types of self-powered vehicles to be
    used in the activity or operation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} numbers, types and function of any other mobile
    vehicles to be used in the activity or operation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} general areas of operation or location of any
    vehicles.

- **10** *General Information Concerning any Measuring or Observing
  Instrumentation*

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a brief description of any special atmospheric or
    other observations to be made by the weather modifier, or for the
    weather modifier by arrangement with other persons, as a result of
    the activity or operation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} itemization of any measuring or observing
    instrumentation that will be installed or used particularly for this
    activity, indicating the general nature of the instrumentation and
    location of usage or installation (a summary map may be used).

- **11** *Certification by Organization for Whom Activity is to be
  Conducted*

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} statement identifying activity, certifying that the
    activity is to be conducted for the organization and outlining the
    working arrangement with person conducting the activity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} name of the organization;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} name of certifying officer in full;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} title of certifying officer;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} signature of certifying officer and date.

- **12** *Certification by Person Proposing to Conduct the Activity*

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} statement certifying truth of all information
    provided to the Administrator pursuant to subsection 3(1) of the
    Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} name of the organization (or person);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} name of certifying officer in full;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} title of certifying officer;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} signature of certifying officer and date.

## [SCHEDULE II]{.scheduleLabel}[(s. 5)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-532524 .scheduleLabel}
:::

###### [Form]{.HTitleText}

- **1** Daily records shall be maintained in a form that will allow
  ready recognition of the detailed information required by paragraph
  4(1)(a) of the Act under the following categories:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name of the field officer in charge;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} names of personnel engaged in any field operations,
    together with their employment function and area of operation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} names of locations of all custodians and authorized
    holders of records;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} locations and functions of all field bases;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} location of each piece of equipment and
    instrumentation of any sort, identification of the piece, function,
    hours and times of operation, names of operators and custodians;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} materials emitted or dispersed into the atmosphere
    including class, physical state, size, generic and common name,
    chemical composition and quantities, rates, hours and times of
    emission or dispersion;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} aircraft log data and flight plans including
    identification as to purpose of flights;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} general tracks and areas of operation of any
    aircraft, the aircraft speed, flight altitudes and temperature at
    flight altitude, all with accuracy consistent with the
    instrumentation normally available in the aircraft and with the
    attendant ground facilities, and the time of special operation of
    the aircraft and facilities while actually engaged in weather
    modification procedures to within ±5 minutes;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} location and operations of any self-powered ground
    vehicles;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} locations and operations of any other mobile
    vehicles;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} any special atmospheric or other observations made
    appertaining to the weather modification activity;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} exact location of area for which modification of
    weather is being sought on each occasion.

###### [Manner]{.HTitleText}

- **2** Daily records shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintained in an orderly manner;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} clearly itemized as to their content, origin and
    recording officer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} clearly understandable to any person knowledgeable
    in the subject matter but not directly associated with the weather
    modification activities; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} readily available for inspection.

## [SCHEDULE III]{.scheduleLabel}[(s. 6)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-532528 .scheduleLabel}
:::

###### [Form]{.HTitleText}

- **1** Reports shall be in a form that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} clearly describes the weather modification activity
    to which they relate; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provides the information described in subparagraphs
    4(1)(b)(i) to (iii) of the Act and specified by the Administrator
    pursuant to subparagraph 4(1)(b)(iv) of the Act.

- **2** The last report submitted in respect of the weather modification
  activity that is not completed within one month shall, in addition to
  containing the information required for the last month, contain a
  summary of the information provided for all months during which the
  activity was carried on.

###### [Manner]{.HTitleText}

- **3** Reports shall be submitted in duplicate.

- **4** A certificate shall be endorsed on each report signed by the
  weather modifier or by an authorized official of the weather modifier
  where the weather modifier is not an individual.

- **5** The certificate referred to in section 4 shall certify that the
  report contains a true account of all weather modification activities
  undertaken for the period covered by the report and that the
  information is complete and correct to the best of the knowledge of
  the person signing the certificate.
