## Advance Payments for Crops Guarantee Assignments Regulations {#advance-payments-for-crops-guarantee-assignments-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 672
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Regulations Respecting the Assignment of the Right of Organizations
under Subsection 12(1) of the Advance Payments for Crops Act
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560275 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Advance Payments for Crops Guarantee Assignments
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._672).

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560277 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any person occupying the
position of Assistant Deputy Minister, Food Production and Marketing
Branch, Department of Agriculture, is hereby designated as the paying
officer for the purposes of Part IX of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11) in respect of an assignment of the rights of an
organization under subsection 12(1) of the [Advance Payments for Crops
Act](/eng/acts/C-49).

## [Prescription]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560279 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 81(1)(b) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the rights of an organization under subsection
12(1) of the [Advance Payments for Crops Act](/eng/acts/C-49) is a
prescribed class of Crown debts.
