## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-740077 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Forces
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Reserve Force Pension Fund
    established under section 84. ([caisse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of the reserve
    force. ([membre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member or former member
    who is entitled to an annuity or annual allowance under Division 1
    of Part 2. ([pensionné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Earnings

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} earnings means pay earned by a member of the Canadian
  Forces at the rates prescribed or established under the [National
  Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) for their rank, together with earned
  premiums in lieu of leave;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a member who is exempted from training and duty under
  article 9.09 or 9.10 or was granted leave for maternity or parental
  purposes under article 16.26 or 16.27 of the Queen's Regulations and
  Orders for the Canadian Forces is deemed to be in receipt of earnings,
  during each week for which the member is exempted or granted leave,
  equal to the weekly rate of pay calculated in accordance with
  instruction 205.461 of the Compensation and Benefits Instructions for
  the Canadian Forces established under section 35 of the [National
  Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a period in the reserve force before
  April 1, 1999, where records of the Canadian Forces or the Department
  of National Defence permit the verification of the rank of a member
  and the duration of the period but not the ascertainment of the
  member's earnings, the member is deemed, for each day of the period,
  to have been in receipt of earnings equal to ¼ of the standard basic
  daily rate of pay, applicable to their rank for that day, prescribed
  or established under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of Reserve Force Pension

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Reserve Force Pension
Plan is established.

#### [Participation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-740094 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of this section and section 5,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[Monthly Earnings Threshold]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
    in respect of any month, means 1/12 of the Year's Maximum
    Pensionable Earnings, within the meaning of subsection 2(1) of the
    [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), for the calendar year in which
    the month falls, the result rounded to the nearest cent; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [[Annual Earnings Threshold]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
    in respect of any period of 12 months, means the total of the
    Monthly Earnings Thresholds for that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial participation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member becomes a participant in the Reserve Force
  Pension Plan

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on March 1, 2007 if, during each of any two
    consecutive periods of 12 months beginning on or after April 1, 1999
    and ending no later than March 1, 2007, the earnings that they were
    entitled to receive were equal to or greater than 10 per cent of the
    Annual Earnings Threshold, if the member already was or became a
    member of the Canadian Forces during the first month of the first
    period and remained a member of the Canadian Forces, without any
    interruption of more than 60 days, until March 1, 2007; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, on the first day of the month
    following two consecutive periods of 12 months --- the second of
    which shall end after March 1, 2007 --- during each of which the
    earnings that they were entitled to receive were equal to or greater
    than 10 per cent of the Annual Earnings Threshold, if the member
    already was or became a member of the Canadian Forces during the
    first month of the first period and remained a member of the
    Canadian Forces without any interruption of more than 60 days
    throughout those two periods.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent participation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A pensioner or the former participant referred to in
  section 55 becomes a participant on the day in respect of which they
  are again entitled to receive earnings as a member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A member shall not become a participant if they were
  considered to be a member of the regular force on March 1, 2007 under
  paragraph 8.1(1)(a) of the [Canadian Forces Superannuation
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._396) or if, under Part I of
  the Act, they, as a contributor,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are in receipt of an annuity or an annual
    allowance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are entitled to a deferred annuity or an annual
    allowance; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} are a person in respect of whom the payment of a
    transfer value has been effected.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Member ceasing to be a participant

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member
  ceases to be a participant:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the day on which the member ceases to be a
    member;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the day before the day on which the member is
    required to contribute to the Canadian Forces Pension Fund; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the last day of a period of 12 months, as a
    participant, in respect of which they were not entitled to receive
    earnings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Member shall not cease to be a
  participant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member who has to their credit less than two years
  of pensionable service is deemed not to have ceased to be a
  participant if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the member ceased to be a participant by ceasing to
    be a member and the member has again become a member within 60 days,
    in which case, for the purpose of these Regulations, the member is
    deemed not to have ceased to be a member; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the member is entitled to receive earnings equal to
    or greater than 10 per cent of the Annual Earnings Threshold in
    respect of each of the two consecutive periods of 12 months ending
    at the end of the first month following the month during which the
    member would have ceased to be a participant, if at the end of that
    month the member reached that minimum entitlement of earnings, or,
    if not, the end of the second month following that month.

### [DIVISION 2]{.HLabel2}[Contributions, Pensionable Earnings and Pensionable Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-740123 .Subheading}

#### [Contributions]{.HTitleText3} {#h-740124 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rate of contributions

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  contribution of a participant to the Fund shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the following percentage of the participant's
    earnings:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 4.3 per cent in respect of 2007,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 4.6 per cent in respect of 2008,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 4.9 per cent in respect of 2009, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} 5.2 per cent in respect of 2010 and each
      subsequent year; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 1 per cent of the participant's earnings, if the
    participant has to their credit 35 years of pensionable service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A participant shall not contribute to the Fund in
  respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any portion of the participant's rate of pay in a
    calendar year that is in excess of the product of 66 2/3 times the
    defined benefit limit determined for that year in accordance with
    the definition [[defined benefit limit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
    subsection 8500(1) of the [Income Tax
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._945); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any of the participant's earnings in a calendar
    year after the calendar year in which the participant reaches
    71 years of age.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contribution on deemed earnings

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Reservations on account of
  contributions in respect of earnings that the participant is deemed to
  have received under paragraph 2(b) shall be equal to ½ of the amount
  of the participant's contributions in respect of earnings after the
  period of exemption or leave.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reservations for subsequent periods

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Reservations on account of contributions in respect
  of earnings that the participant is deemed to have received in a
  subsequent period of exemption or leave shall commence after the
  contributions in respect of earnings for previous periods have been
  paid.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opting out

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The participant may opt not
to contribute in respect of earnings that the participant is deemed to
have received under paragraph 2(b) during a period of exemption or
leave, before all of the contributions have been paid, and the
contributions already paid shall be refunded.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of unpaid contributions

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Any contribution that
remains unpaid at the time that a pensioner commences to receive an
annuity or annual allowance under Division 1 of Part 2 shall be
recovered by reservation from the monthly benefits payable to the
pensioner under Divisions 1 and 3 of that Part in amounts equal to 10
per cent of those benefits.

#### [Pensionable Earnings]{.HTitleText3} {#h-740149 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pensionable earnings

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be counted as pensionable earnings

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the earnings in respect of which the participant is
    required to make the contribution to the Fund set out in
    paragraph 6(1)(a); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsections 11(3), 26(1) and 32(1), the
    earnings in respect of which the participant makes an election to
    count as pensionable earnings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not pensionable earnings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} There shall not be counted as pensionable earnings
  those earnings in respect of which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the participant has opted, under section 8, not to
    contribute;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the member was entitled to a return of
    contributions, within the meaning of section 38;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the payment of a transfer value has been effected
    in accordance with section 61; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an amount is charged to the Fund and credited to
    the Canadian Forces Pension Fund under section 83.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  participant is entitled to make an election, once only in respect of
  each type of earnings in each period during which the participant is a
  participant, to count past earnings and transfer value earnings as
  pensionable earnings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Past earnings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Past earnings are the earnings, up to the product
  calculated under paragraph 6(2)(a), in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any period in the reserve force, including any
    period before March 1, 2007, during which the participant was not a
    participant except any period

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the participant is counting as pensionable
      service for the purposes of, or in respect of which the payment of
      a transfer value or a commuted value has been effected under, the
      [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) or the [Royal
      Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which the payment of a transfer
      value has been effected in accordance with section 61;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any period in the reserve force in respect of which
    the participant was entitled to a return of contributions within the
    meaning of section 38; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any period in the regular force in respect of which
    the participant was entitled to a cash termination allowance or a
    return of contributions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Most recent earnings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A past earnings election is for all of the past
  earnings. However, there shall be counted as pensionable earnings,
  starting with the most recent, only those that would result in a
  maximum of 35 years of pensionable service to the credit of the
  participant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer value earnings

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Transfer value earnings are those earnings in respect
  of which the payment of the last transfer value was effected in
  accordance with section 61.

##### [Past Earnings Elections]{.HTitleText4} {#h-740175 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When entitled to elect

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  participant may make a past earnings election no earlier than two
  years after the day on which the participant became a participant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Former participant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the participant had formerly been a participant,
  they may make the election as soon as they again become a participant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participant since March 1, 2007

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A participant, who becomes a participant on March 1,
  2007 and remains a participant without interruption, may make the
  election beginning on the day after the first day, on or after March
  1, 2007, in respect of which they are entitled to receive earnings but
  no earlier than the first day of the 25th month following the two
  periods referred to in paragraph 4(2)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A participant who has ceased to contribute to the
  Fund by virtue of paragraph 6(2)(b) is not entitled to make a past
  earnings election.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount to be paid

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  participant who makes an election to count past earnings as
  pensionable earnings shall pay the full amount to count all of those
  past earnings that can be counted as pensionable earnings or a lesser
  amount for which the participant opts at the time of making the
  election.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of full amount

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The full amount shall be the total of all amounts ---
  plus interest at seven per cent compounded annually and calculated
  from the middle of the calendar year until the day of the election ---
  determined for each calendar year by the following formula applied to
  those past earnings that can be counted as pensionable earnings that
  relate to any period in that year:

  ::: {#740200 .Subsection}
  A × B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the contribution rate set out in paragraph 6(1)(a) on the date
      of the election or, if the participant has remained a participant
      since March 1, 2007, 4.3 per cent; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount that would be calculated as the updated pensionable
      earnings under subsection 37(2), for the calendar year if
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the participant's past earnings that can be
        counted as pensionable earnings for that calendar year were
        their pensionable earnings for that calendar year, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the year in which the election is made or, if
        the participant has remained a participant since March 1, 2007,
        the year 2007, is the year that the participant ceased to be a
        participant.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retroactive changes not included in
  calculations

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} The calculation referred to in the description of B
  of the formula in subsection (2) shall not include any retroactive
  adjustments to the participant's earnings made after the day on which
  they make the election referred to in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The participant cannot modify the amount for which
  the participant has opted.

- SOR/2016-64, s. 61

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If amount is not more than \$500

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  amount to be paid is not more than \$500, the participant shall pay it
  in a lump sum no later than 30 days after

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day of the election, if the participant opted
    for an amount of not more than \$500; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the date of the notice advising
    the participant of the amount to be paid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Partial payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the participant pays less than that amount, the
  participant is deemed to have opted, at the time of making the
  election, for the amount received by the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If amount is more than \$500

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** If the amount to be paid
is more than \$500, the participant shall pay it by way of monthly
instalments.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Instalments

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  instalments shall be payable in equal amounts that may not be less
  than \$5.00, except the last one, and be calculated using the
  mortality rates set out in the Complete life table, Canada, 1995-97,
  published by Statistics Canada, and interest at four per cent
  compounded annually.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When payable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The instalments shall be payable on the first day of
  each month following the month of the election until the earlier of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the end of the period chosen by the participant
    ending before the later of 20 years and the participant's
    65th birthday, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the death of the participant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change in amount to pay

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When the amount shown in the notice advising the
  participant of the amount to be paid is larger than the amount on
  which the participant's instalments are based,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the participant is deemed to have chosen the period
    consistent with paragraph (2)(a) that would result in the lowest
    possible instalments not lower than the current instalments; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any arrears shall be paid by instalments on the
    first day of each month following the month of the notice,
    calculated in the manner referred to in subsection (1), except that
    the instalments may be for less than \$5.00, until the end of the
    remainder of the period deemed to have been chosen or, if earlier,
    the death of the participant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amending plan of instalments

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The participant may
decrease the period of instalments by increasing the amount of the
remaining instalments.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Default

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  participant or pensioner who has been sent a notice of payment default
  shall pay the arrears by way of monthly instalments.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time and amount of payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The instalments

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall be calculated in the manner referred to in
    subsection 18(1), except that they may be for amounts of less than
    \$5.00;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall be payable on the first day of each month
    following the date of the notice, until the earlier of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the end of the remainder of the period referred
      to in paragraph 18(2)(a) or (3)(a), or, if that remainder is less
      than five years, the end of a period, up to a maximum of five
      years, chosen by the participant or pensioner, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the death of the participant or pensioner; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} after the period referred to in paragraph 18(2)(a)
    or (3)(a), shall be adjusted to be not less than the amount of the
    instalment before the last of those referred to in section 17 and
    the period shall be adjusted accordingly.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-payment

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may, at any time, pre-pay any amount that remains unpaid in
  respect of a past earnings election.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount paid shall be applied to the payment of
  any amount referred to in the notice referred to in subsection 20(1)
  and the person may direct

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the remainder be applied towards future
    instalments; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the remainder be applied to reduce the balance
    of the amount to be paid and that the period of instalments remain
    the same or be decreased.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No direction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless the person has given the direction no later
  than 30 days after the date of the payment, the remainder shall be
  applied to reduce the balance of the amount to be paid and the period

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the reserve force,

  the deferred annuity or annual allowance and the bridge benefit to
  which the pensioner was previously entitled.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction calculation

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  event that a pensioner ceases to be entitled to an annual allowance
  under section 49 or subsection 50(1), an amount, subject to
  subsections (2) and (3), determined by the following formula shall be
  deducted from any annuity or annual allowance payments that the
  pensioner receives on again ceasing to be a participant or on becoming
  entitled to an annuity under subsection 50(1):

  ::: {#740480 .Subsection}
  A/12 × B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is five per cent of the sum of the annual allowance and the bridge
      benefit that the pensioner was receiving before again becoming a
      participant or before becoming entitled to a disability pension
      under the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or a provincial
      pension plan; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the lesser of
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of years, rounded to the nearest
        1/10 of a year, during which the pensioner received the annual
        allowance; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the value determined for B of either
        subsection 46(1) or paragraph 46(2)(b), which value was used in
        determining the amount of the annual allowance.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum amount

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The deduction from the amount of those payments shall
  not result in the pensioner receiving less than the payments that the
  pensioner was receiving --- before the pensioner again became a
  participant or the pensioner became entitled to the disability pension
  --- minus the amount of the bridge benefit, if the pensioner is no
  longer entitled to it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The total of the amounts to be deducted shall not
  exceed the total amount that was received as an annual allowance and a
  bridge benefit by the pensioner before the pensioner again became a
  participant or the pensioner became entitled to the disability
  pension.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms of payment

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  annuity, annual allowance and bridge benefit shall be paid in equal
  monthly payments in arrears,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the annuity or annual allowance,
    until the end of the month during which the pensioner dies; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the bridge benefit, until the
    earliest of the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the day preceding the day on which the pensioner
      becomes entitled to a disability pension under the [Canada Pension
      Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or a provincial pension plan,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the last day of the month in which the pensioner
      reaches 65 years of age, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the day that the annuity or annual allowance
      ceases to be paid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arrears

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any amount in arrears after the pensioner's death
  shall be paid to the survivor who is entitled to an annual allowance
  under Division 2. If there is no survivor, it shall be paid to the
  pensioner's estate or succession.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Apportionment if two survivors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If there are two survivors who are entitled to an
  annual allowance under Division 2, each survivor's portion shall be
  determined in accordance with section 64 as if the reference to "death
  benefit" in that section were a reference to "amount in arrears".

##### [Transfer Value]{.HTitleText4} {#h-740504 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** A pensioner who is
entitled to a deferred annuity and has not reached 50 years of age may
opt for the payment of a transfer value, and the making of the option
extinguishes the entitlement to the deferred annuity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for opting

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** A pensioner shall make an
option for the payment of a transfer value no later than one year after
the day on which they cease to be a participant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Option not made

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** The option is deemed not
to have been made if, before the transfer value has been paid, the
former participant again is entitled to receive earnings as a member or
is required to contribute to the Canadian Forces Pension Fund.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of transfer value

**[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** The transfer value is an
amount, together with interest calculated in accordance with section 62,
equal to the greater of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the actuarial present value, on the date of the
  option, of the accrued pension benefits that would be payable to or in
  respect of the pensioner, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a return of contributions, calculated as of the date
  of the option as if the pensioner had been entitled to a return of
  contributions on that date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pensionable earnings to pensioner's
credit

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** The calculation of the
accrued pension benefits shall be based on the pensionable earnings to
the pensioner's credit on the day after the day on which they cease to
be a participant and for which they have paid or ought to have paid
before the date of the option.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation rules

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** The calculation of the
actuarial present value of the accrued pension benefits is subject to
the following rules:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the indexing benefits referred to in Division 3 are
  to be increased to take into account the period beginning on the later
  of January 1 of the year in which the option was made and the day on
  which the pensioner ceased to be a participant and ending on the date
  of the option; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the possibility that the pensioner could receive an
  annual allowance is to be excluded.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Actuarial valuation report

- **[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of subsection (2), the actuarial valuation report is the
  actuarial valuation report most recently laid before Parliament, under
  section 56 of the Act, before the date of the option or, if that
  report was laid before Parliament in the month in which the option was
  made or in the preceding month, the preceding report that was laid
  before Parliament, in each case with any terminological modifications
  that the circumstances require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Actuarial assumptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In determining the actuarial present value of the
  accrued pension benefits, the following actuarial assumptions are to
  be used:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the mortality rates for pensioners and survivors
    are the mortality rates, including annual percentages of mortality
    reduction, in respect of contributors and survivors used in the
    preparation of the actuarial valuation report;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the interest rates are the interest rates for fully
    indexed pensions --- adjusted by the interest rates for unindexed
    pensions to take into account Division 3 --- determined in
    accordance with the section entitled "Pension Commuted Values" of
    the Standards of Practice --- Practice-Specific Standards for
    Pension Plans, published by the Canadian Institute of Actuaries, as
    amended from time to time;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the probability that a pensioner will be survived
    by children is based on the rates regarding the average number,
    average age and eligibility status of children at the death of a
    pensioner, used in the preparation of the actuarial valuation
    report;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the probability that a pensioner will become
    entitled to an annuity under subsection 50(1) is based on the rates
    of termination owing to disability (any occupation), in respect of
    contributors used in the preparation of the actuarial valuation
    report, taking into account the probability --- as set out in that
    report --- that there would be immediate eligibility for a
    disability pension under the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or
    a provincial pension plan; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the probability that a pensioner will have a
    survivor at death is based on the probability that a contributor
    will have a survivor at death and on the age difference between the
    contributor and the survivor that was used in the preparation of the
    actuarial valuation report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deductions from payment

**[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** There shall be deducted
from the amount of the transfer value

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} contributions remaining unpaid in respect of earnings
  that the pensioner is deemed to have received under paragraph 2(b);
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} arrears in respect of a past earnings election.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment effected by transfer

- **[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  payment of a transfer value is effected

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by transferring any portion that may, in accordance
    with section 8517 of the [Income Tax
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._945), be transferred, at
    the direction of the former participant, to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a pension plan that is registered under the
      [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), if that pension plan so
      permits,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a retirement savings plan or fund for the former
      participant that is of the kind prescribed for the purposes of
      section 26 of the [Pension Benefits Standards Act,
      1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01), or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a financial institution authorized to sell
      immediate or deferred life annuities, as defined in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
      1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), for the purchase from that
      financial institution of such an annuity for the former
      participant; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by paying any excess to the former participant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment if former participant deceased

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, after the option for the payment of the transfer
  value described in section 53 has been exercised but before that
  payment has been effected, the former participant dies, the following
  rules apply:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount that may be transferred shall be paid
    accordingly and any excess shall be paid

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the person who would have been entitled to an
      annual allowance as a survivor under Division 2 had the option for
      the payment of a transfer value not been exercised, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to the former participant's estate or succession
      if there is no person who would have been entitled to the annual
      allowance as a survivor; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if no amount may be transferred, all of the
    transfer value shall be paid in accordance with subparagraphs (a)(i)
    and (ii)

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Apportionment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If there are two persons who would have been entitled
  to an annual allowance under Division 2 as survivors had the transfer
  value option not been exercised, each of them shall be entitled to a
  portion determined in accordance with section 64 as if the reference
  to "death benefit" in that section were a reference to "excess" or
  "transfer value", as the case may be.

### [DIVISION 1.1]{.HLabel2}[Debit Balances in Pay Accounts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-740560 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of debit balances

**[[62.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} from any return of contributions or a transfer value

    indirectly, to the debit balance.

### [DIVISION 2]{.HLabel2}[Benefits to Survivors, Children and Other Beneficiaries]{.HTitleText2} {#h-740569 .Subheading}

#### [Participant Who Has Less Than Two Years of Pensionable Service]{.HTitleText3} {#h-740570 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Death benefits

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** The survivor of a
participant who has to their credit less than two years of pensionable
service is entitled to a death benefit equal to the greater of a return
of contributions and an amount determined by the formula

::: {#740573 .Section}
A × B/C

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the participant's pensionable earnings in the 12 months prior to
    the month of death;

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the number of years of pensionable service to the participant's
    credit; and

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the lesser of 12 and the number of months of pensionable service
    to the participant's credit .
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Apportionment if two survivors

- **[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If two
  survivors are entitled to the death benefit, each survivor's portion
  shall be determined by the formula

  ::: {#740581 .Subsection}
  A × B/C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the death benefit;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of years that the survivor cohabited with the
      participant while married to or in a relationship of a conjugal
      nature with the participant; and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of years that the two survivors cohabited with
      the participant while married to or in a relationship of a
      conjugal nature with the participant.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rounding Years

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In determining the number of years, a part of a year
  shall be counted as a full year if the part is six or more months and
  shall not be counted if it is less.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum benefit --- no survivor

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** If a participant, who has
to their credit less than two years of pensionable service, dies leaving
no survivor entitled to the death benefit, a death benefit equal to a
return of contributions shall be paid to the estate or succession of the
participant.

#### [Participant Who Has Not Less Than Two Years of Pensionable Service]{.HTitleText3} {#h-740592 .Subheading}

Definition of [[basic allowance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
sections 67 and 68, [[basic allowance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to one per cent of the greater of the
  pensioner's total pensionable earnings and total updated pensionable
  earnings; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the pensioner was in receipt of an annual
  allowance at the time of death, an amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#740597 .Paragraph}
  A × B/C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount calculated under paragraph (a),

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the annual allowance, and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the deferred annuity to which the pensioner was
      entitled.
  :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefits to survivor of a pensioner

- **[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  survivor of a pensioner is entitled to an annual allowance equal to
  the basic allowance or, if there are two survivors, to an amount
  determined by the formula

  ::: {#740605 .Subsection}
  A × B/C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the basic allowance;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of years that the survivor cohabited with the
      pensioner while married to or in a relationship of a conjugal
      nature with the pensioner; and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of years that the two survivors cohabited with
      the pensioner while married to or in a relationship of a conjugal
      nature with the pensioner.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rounding Years

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In determining the number of years, a part of a year
  shall be counted as a full year if the part is six or more months and
  shall not be counted if it is less.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Death of one of two survivors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If one of the survivors dies, the remaining
  survivor's annual allowance shall be equal to the basic allowance
  commencing the first day of the month following the death of the
  survivor.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Course of study

- **[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of this section and sections 69 and 72, [[course of
  study]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a course of training or instruction
  given by a school, college, university or other institution of an
  educational, professional or technical nature.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefits to child of a pensioner

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each child of a pensioner who, at the date of the
  pensioner's death, is under 18 years of age or is 18 or more years of
  age but less than 25 years of age and enrolled in and pursuing a
  full-time course of study is entitled

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the pensioner died leaving a survivor entitled
    to an allowance under this Division, to an annual allowance equal to
    ¼ of the basic allowance or, if there are more than two children, to
    an annual allowance equal to ½ of the basic allowance divided by the
    number of children; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the pensioner died without leaving a survivor
    entitled to receive an allowance under this Division, and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} there are fewer than four children, to an annual
      allowance equal to ½ of the basic allowance, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} there are more than three children, to an annual
      allowance equal to 1 ½ times the basic allowance divided by the
      number of children.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recalculation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The proportion of the basic allowance that
  constitutes the annual allowance shall be revised when the number of
  children who are entitled changes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing entitlement

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The child of a pensioner who, at the date of the
  pensioner's death, was entitled to an annual allowance shall be
  entitled to the annual allowance for any period during which the child
  is enrolled in and pursuing a full-time course of study until the
  child reaches 25 years of age.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A child, at the date of the pensioner's death, is
  deemed to have been enrolled in and pursuing a full-time course of
  study if the child

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is again enrolled in and pursuing a full-time
    course of study, or dies, no later than the last day of the fifth
    month after the month in which the child ceased to be enrolled in
    and pursuing the full-time course of study; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not again enrolled in and pursuing a full-time
    course of study within that period due to illness and is again
    enrolled in and pursuing a full-time course of study, or dies, no
    later than the last day of the twelfth month after the month in
    which the child ceased to be enrolled in and pursuing the full-time
    course of study.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scholastic break

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A child is deemed not to have ceased to be enrolled
  in and pursuing a full-time course of study if the child fulfils the
  conditions set out in paragraph (5)(a) or (b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

**[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** The entitlement to an
annual allowance of a child who is 18 or more years of age but less than
25 years of age and enrolled in and pursuing a full-time course of study
is subject to the child submitting to the Minister, in respect of each
period of enrolment,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a declaration by a responsible officer of the
  relevant institution that the child is or was enrolled in a full-time
  course of study; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a declaration by the child that the child is or was
  pursuing a full-time course of study.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefits to survivor and child of a
participant

**[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** The survivor and child of
a participant, where the participant has to their credit not less than
two years of pensionable service, are entitled to the annual allowances
to which they would have been entitled under section 67 or 68 had the
participant, immediately before death, become a pensioner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement of annual allowance

- **[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  commencement date of the annual allowance payable to the survivor or
  child is the day after the death.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommencing studies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a child who has ceased to be entitled
  and who again becomes so entitled, the commencement date is the first
  day of the month in which the child enrols in and pursues a full-time
  course of study.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms of payment

- **[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  annual allowance shall be paid in equal monthly payments in arrears,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a survivor, until the end of the
    month during which the survivor dies; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a child, until the end of the month
    during which the earlier of the following occurs:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the child reaches 18 years of age or dies, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the child is enrolled in and pursuing a
      full-time course of study, the child ceases to be enrolled in and
      pursuing a full-time course of study or reaches 25 years of age.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arrears

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any amount in arrears after the death of the survivor
  or child who has reached 18 years of age shall be paid to the estate
  or succession.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marriage or cohabitation after pensioner

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waiver by survivor

  survivor may waive entitlement to an annual allowance if it results in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an increase in the allowance payable to a child
    under section 68 or 70; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a payment under section 78.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time for waiver

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The survivor shall waive the entitlement no later
  than three months after the survivor is notified of their entitlement
  to the allowance. That waiver is to take effect as of the day after
  the death of the participant or pensioner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Child after pensioner reaches 60 years

**[[75]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-75 .sectionLabel}** A person who becomes the
child of a pensioner at a time when the pensioner was over 60 years of
age is not entitled to an annual allowance, unless,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} after that time the pensioner again became a
  participant; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the child was born to the pensioner following a
  gestation period that commenced prior to the day when the pensioner
  reached 60 years of age or ceased to be a participant, whichever is

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Death within one year after marriage

**[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76 .sectionLabel}** If a participant or
pensioner dies within one year after marriage, no annual allowance is
payable to the survivor or a person who becomes a child of the
participant or pensioner after the marriage unless it is established
that the participant or pensioner was at the time of the marriage in
such a condition of health as to justify the participant or pensioner in
having an expectation of surviving for at least one year after the
marriage.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving provision

**[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in sections 75
and 76 shall be held to prejudice any right that a child of an earlier
marriage of the participant or pensioner has to an allowance under
section 68 or 70.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum benefit --- no survivor or child

**[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78 .sectionLabel}** If, on the death of a
participant or pensioner who has to their credit not less than two years
of pensionable service, there is no survivor or child to whom an annual
allowance may be paid, or if the survivor or child to whom an annual
allowance may be paid dies or ceases to be entitled to it and no other
amount may be paid to them under this Division, an amount equal to the
amount determined by the following formula shall be paid as a death
benefit to the estate or succession of the participant or pensioner:

::: {#740674 .Section}
A -- B

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the greater of a return of contributions and an amount equal to
    five times the total of the annuity determined in accordance with
    section 41 plus the bridge benefit determined in accordance with
    section 48; and

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the aggregate of all amounts paid to a survivor or child under
    this Division and to the participant or pensioner under Division 1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Survivor not to receive benefits

**[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** A survivor is not
entitled to receive any benefit under this Division in respect of the
participant or pensioner if the survivor could not be found within one
year after the Minister has been notified of the death of the
participant or pensioner or the survivor is found criminally responsible
for the death of the participant or pensioner.

### [DIVISION 3]{.HLabel2}[Indexing Benefits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-740682 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indexing benefit

**[[80]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
entitled to an indexing benefit:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a pensioner who is in receipt of an annuity or annual
  allowance and who

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is receiving it under paragraph 43(1)(d) or
    subsection 50(1),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has reached at least 55 years of age and whose
    combination of years of age and full years of pensionable service
    equals at least 85, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has reached 60 years of age; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a survivor or child who is in receipt of an annual
  allowance under Division 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retirement month or year

- **[[81]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-81 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of this section, [[retirement month]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  or [[retirement year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a pensioner, the month or year during
    which the pensioner most recently became entitled to an annuity or
    annual allowance under Division I of Part 2; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a survivor or child, the retirement
    month or retirement year of the pensioner, or the month or year of
    death of the participant, in respect of whom the annual allowance is
    payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of indexing benefit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The indexing benefit for a month in any year shall be
  calculated with reference to the retirement year and shall be equal to
  the amount of the supplementary retirement benefit that would be
  payable for that month in respect of the annuity or annual allowance
  and the bridge benefit of the pensioner, survivor or child under
  subsections 4(1) and (2) of the [Supplementary Retirement Benefits
  Act](/eng/acts/S-24) if that Act applied to that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of indexing benefit ---
  year following retirement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In respect of a month in the year following the
  retirement year, the indexing benefit is equal to the amount
  determined by the formula

  ::: {#740699 .Subsection}
  A × B/12

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the indexing benefit that would, but for this
      subsection, be payable to the pensioner, survivor or child for
      that month; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of complete months that remained in the retirement
      year after the retirement month.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum amount

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The aggregate of the amount of the annuity or annual
  allowance and the bridge benefit, and of the indexing benefit that may
  be payable in respect of that amount, in any month of any year, shall
  not be less than the aggregate of the following amounts that were or
  may be payable for any month in the year before that year:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the annuity or annual allowance and that portion of
    the indexing benefit that related to it; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the pensioner continues to be entitled to the
    bridge benefit, that benefit and the portion of the indexing benefit
    that related to it.

- SOR/2008-307, s. 14

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of payment

**[[82]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-82 .sectionLabel}** The indexing benefit
shall be paid at the same time, in the same manner and in respect of the
same period as the annuity, annual allowance and bridge benefit.

### [DIVISION 4]{.HLabel2}[Contributors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-740711 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participants who become contributors

- **[[83]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-83 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  former participant is required to contribute to the Canadian Forces
  Pension Fund and has pensionable earnings to their credit at that
  time, there shall be charged to the Fund and credited to the Canadian
  Forces Pension Fund an amount equal to the transfer value that would
  have been determined under section 56, excluding interest, if the
  former participant had had the right to opt for the payment of a
  transfer value and had done so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and obligations extinguished

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The former participant's rights and obligations, and
  those of any person to whom a benefit might have become payable in
  respect of that former participant, under these Regulations shall be
  extinguished on the day before the day on which the former participant
  is required to contribute to the Canadian Forces Pension Fund.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Establishment and Administration of the Reserve Force Pension Fund]{.HTitleText1} {#h-740717 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fund established

**[[84]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-84 .sectionLabel}** The Reserve Force Pension
Fund is established.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts to be deposited

**[[85]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-85 .sectionLabel}** There shall be deposited
into the Fund

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amounts that participants are required to
  contribute under the Act and the amounts that they are required to
  pay, under these Regulations, in respect of elections made to count
  past earnings or transfer value earnings as pensionable earnings; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the income from the investment of any contributions
  and from amounts deposited into the Fund plus profits minus losses on
  the sale of the investments.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When amounts to be deposited

- **[[86]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-86 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  time within which the amounts referred to in paragraph 59.3(a) of the
  Act are to be deposited into the Fund is no later than 30 days after
  the end of the month in which the contributions to which they relate
  are deposited.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} except for the period before the laying before
    Parliament of the first actuarial valuation report referred to in
    subsection 59.6(1) of the Act, a deposit may only be made, in
    respect of the benefits that have accrued in relation to the
    pensionable earnings referred to in paragraph 10(1)(a), during the
    period beginning with the laying before Parliament of an actuarial
    valuation report that does not show a non-permitted surplus and
    ending with laying of any actuarial report that shows a
    non-permitted surplus; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the time within which the arrears resulting from
    paragraph (a) are to be deposited into the Fund is no later than 30
    days after the laying of the most recent actuarial report that no
    longer shows a non-permitted surplus.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-permitted surplus

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A non-permitted surplus exists when the amount by
  which assets exceed liabilities in the Fund, as determined by the
  actuarial valuation report, is greater than the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 20% of the amount of liabilities in respect of
    participants and pensioners as determined in that report, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the greater of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} twice the estimated amount, for the calendar year
      following the date of that report, of the total of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the contributions that will be required of
        participants in respect of their earnings during that year, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the part of the amounts to be deposited under
        section 85 that is in respect of the benefits that have accrued
        in relation to the pensionable earnings referred to in paragraph
        10(1)(a), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 10% of the amount of liabilities in respect of
      participants and pensioners as determined in that report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deposit of estimated amounts based on
actuarial report

- **[[87]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-87 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If,
  following the laying before Parliament of any actuarial valuation
  report under subsection 59.6(1) of the Act, an amount not previously
  subject to an estimate under paragraph 59.3(a) of the Act is estimated
  under that paragraph to be required to meet the cost of benefits
  payable under these Regulations, the time within which this amount is
  deposited to the Fund is 15 years after the laying of the report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equal annual instalments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} That amount shall be deposited in equal annual
  instalments, with the first instalment to be paid in the fiscal year
  in which the report is laid before Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, after the laying of a subsequent report, the
  amount that is estimated is less than the amount that was estimated
  following the immediately preceding report, the amount of each
  remaining instalment shall be reduced accordingly.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of amounts

**[[88]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-88 .sectionLabel}** The amounts deposited to
the Fund shall be transferred to the Public Sector Pension Investment
Board within the meaning of the [Public Sector Pension Investment Board
Act](/eng/acts/P-31.7) to be dealt with in accordance with that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of benefits

**[[89]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-89 .sectionLabel}** All amounts required for
the payment of benefits payable under these Regulations and all
necessary and reasonable costs for the administration of the Reserve
Force Pension Plan shall be charged to the Fund and paid out of the
assets of the Public Sector Pension Investment Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review date

**[[90]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-90 .sectionLabel}** The review date referred
to in subsection 59.6(2) of the Act is March 31, 2008.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

**[[91]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91 .sectionLabel}** The annual report
referred to in section 59.7 of the Act shall include:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement showing the amounts paid into and out of
  the Fund during the fiscal year, by appropriate classifications, and
  the number of participants and the number of persons receiving
  benefits; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} beginning with the fiscal year ending March 31, 2008,
  the financial statements of the Reserve Force Pension Plan for the
  fiscal year.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-740756 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and transmission of documents

- **[[92]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-92 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  any person makes an election, option or waiver, or gives a direction,
  it is valid only if it is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} made or given in writing, and signed and dated by
    the person making or giving it; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sent to the Minister within one week after the date
    that it bears.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When made

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The election, option or waiver is made, and the
  direction is given, on the date that it bears, except that an option
  for an annual allowance or the payment of a transfer value made before
  or on the day on which the participant ceases to be a participant is
  made on the day after that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of sending

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The date of the sending of the election, option,
  waiver or direction is the day on which it is delivered or, if it is
  sent by mail, the day on which it is mailed and the postmark is
  evidence of that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Irrevocable

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The election, option or waiver is irrevocable.
