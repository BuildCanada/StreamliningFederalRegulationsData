## National Energy Board Cost Recovery Regulations {#national-energy-board-cost-recovery-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-7
:::

[NATIONAL ENERGY BOARD ACT](/eng/acts/N-7)

Registration 1990-12-13

Regulations Respecting the Recovery of Certain Costs of the National
Energy Board
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the National Energy Board has determined that certain costs are
attributable to its responsibilities under the [National Energy Board
Act](/eng/acts/N-7)or any other Act of Parliament;

Therefore, the National Energy Board, pursuant to section 24.1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EFBBA} of
the [National Energy Board Act](/eng/acts/N-7)and with the approval of
the Treasury Board, hereby makes the annexed [Regulations respecting the
recovery of certain costs of the National Energy
Board](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-7), effective January 1, 1991.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  large oil pipeline company shall, every year, pay to the Board a cost
  recovery charge calculated in the manner set out in subsection 14(1).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each large gas pipeline company shall, every year,
  pay to the Board a cost recovery charge calculated in the manner set
  out in subsection 14(2).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each large power line company shall, every year, pay
  to the Board a cost recovery charge calculated in the manner set out
  in subsection 14(3).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Each large commodity pipeline company shall, every
  year, pay to the Board an administration levy of \$50,000.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} However, a company is not required to pay, for a year
  or any portion of a year, a cost recovery charge or administration
  levy payable under this section if the company pays a levy under
  section 5.2 or 5.3 in that year.

- **[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  large oil pipeline company, large gas pipeline company or large
  commodity pipeline company is not required to pay the portion of a
  cost recovery charge or administration levy payable under section 4
  that exceeds 2% of the estimate of the cost of service in respect of
  the company for the year in question if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a large oil pipeline company or a
    large gas pipeline company, the company files a request for relief
    with the Board within 30 days after the day on which the Board
    notifies the company of the cost recovery charge payable by the
    company in that year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a large commodity pipeline company,
    the company files a request for relief with the Board within 30 days
    after the date of the Board's invoice to the company for the
    administration levy payable in that year; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the request for relief includes the company's
    estimated cost of service for that year.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} After consultation with the company that filed the
  request, the Board may, by December 15 in the year in which the
  request is filed, adjust the company's estimated cost of service if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the actual circumstances of the company are
    different in a material respect from the circumstances reported by
    the company in its request; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is an error or omission in the calculation of
    the estimate.

- [(3) to (5)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2009-307, s. 5\]]{.Repealed}

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Any company that obtains relief under this section
  shall provide to the Board, on or before August 31 of the following
  year, its actual cost of service for the year in respect of which the
  relief is obtained.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} If, in respect of any year, relief is obtained under
  this section by one or more large oil pipeline companies, the Board
  shall, on or before September 30 of the following year, determine the
  revised amount of relief for each of those companies, which amount
  shall be the portion of the revised and adjusted cost recovery charge
  calculated in accordance with section 17 that exceeds 2% of the actual
  cost of service for the company for the year in respect of which the
  relief is obtained.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} If, in respect of any year, relief is obtained under
  this section by one or more large gas pipeline companies, the Board
  shall, on or before September 30 of the following year, determine the
  revised amount of relief for each of those companies, which amount
  shall be the portion of the revised and adjusted cost recovery charge
  calculated in accordance with section 17 that exceeds 2% of the actual
  cost of service for the company for the year in respect of which the
  relief is obtained.

## [Determination of Board Costs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939657 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
calculating cost recovery charges in accordance with these Regulations,
the total costs attributable for a year to the responsibilities of the
Board under the Act or any other Act of Parliament are 95 per cent of
the aggregate of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} one fourth of the estimated program costs of the
  Board including the costs of goods and services provided to the Board
  by other federal departments or agencies, as set out in the
  Expenditure Plan published in the Estimates of the Government of
  Canada for the Board's fiscal year ending in that year, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} three fourths of the forecasted program costs of the
  Board including the costs of goods and services provided to the Board
  by other federal departments or agencies, as prepared for the
  Expenditure Plan to be published in the Estimates of the Government of
  Canada for the Board's fiscal year beginning during that year.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-267, ss. 4, 9
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall, on or
before September 30 in every year, calculate

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount, if any, by which the total program costs
  determined in accordance with section 6 for the preceding year exceed
  the actual expenditures of the Board during that preceding year; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount, if any, by which the actual expenditures
  of the Board during the preceding year exceed the total determined in
  accordance with section 6 for that preceding year.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-267, s. 9
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall, in every
year, adjust the total costs determined in accordance with section 6 by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deducting therefrom the amount calculated under
  paragraph 7(a), if any, or by adding thereto the amount calculated
  under paragraph 7(b), if any;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} deducting therefrom any costs that are recovered
  under any other Act of Parliament; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} deducting from those total costs the total of all
  administration levies received under subsection 4(4) from large
  commodity pipeline companies, under subsection 5.1(1) from
  intermediate commodity pipeline companies, under subsection 5.1(2)
  from small commodity pipeline companies and in respect of border
  accommodation commodity pipelines under subsection 5.1(4).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-267, s. 5
- SOR/2001-89, s. 5
- SOR/2009-307, s. 9

## [Deliveries, Transmissions and Cost of Service]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939675 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On or
  before August 31 in every year, each large oil pipeline company and
  large gas pipeline company shall provide the Board with the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a forecast of its deliveries, in cubic metres, for
    the following year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its actual deliveries, in cubic metres, for the
    previous year.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On or before August 31 in every year, each large
  power line company and small power line company shall provide the
  Board with the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a forecast of its electricity transmissions, in
    megawatt hours, for the following year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its actual electricity transmissions, in megawatt
    hours, for the previous year.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} On or before August 31 in every year, each
  intermediate oil pipeline company, intermediate gas pipeline company,
  small oil pipeline company and small gas pipeline company shall
  provide the Board with the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an estimate of the cost of service of its
    operations for the current year that are subject to the jurisdiction
    of the Board; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the actual cost of service for the preceding year
    of its operations that are subject to the jurisdiction of the Board.

## [Notification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939688 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On or
  before September 30 in every year, the Board shall notify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each large power line company of the cost recovery
    charge that will be payable by that company during the following
    year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} each large oil pipeline company and large gas
    pipeline company of the cost recovery charge that will, subject to
    subsection (2), be payable by each of those companies during the
    following year.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On or before December 31 in every year, the Board
  shall notify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each large oil pipeline company that obtained
    relief under section 4.1 in respect of the following year of the
    cost recovery charge reduction determined under paragraph
    14.1(1)(a);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} each large oil pipeline company that did not obtain
    relief under section 4.1 in respect of the following year of the
    cost recovery charge increase determined under paragraph 14.1(1)(b);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} each large gas pipeline company that obtained
    relief under section 4.1 in respect of the following year of the
    cost recovery charge reduction determined under paragraph
    14.1(2)(a); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} each large gas pipeline company that did not obtain
    relief under section 4.1 in respect of the following year of the
    cost recovery charge increase determined under paragraph 14.1(2)(b).

## [Allocation of Board Time]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939700 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On or
  before September 30 in every year, the Board shall determine for the
  immediately preceding fiscal year of the Board, on the basis of
  information provided by its management information systems, the
  percentage that each of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the time spent by officers and employees of the
    Board on activities directly related to the responsibilities of the
    Board in respect of oil, oil pipelines, oil products, natural gas
    liquids and liquefied petroleum gas,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the time spent by officers and employees of the
    Board on activities directly related to the responsibilities of the
    Board in respect of gas pipelines and natural gas, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the time spent by officers and employees of the
    Board on activities directly related to the responsibilities of the
    Board in respect of electricity and international and
    interprovincial power lines

  is to the total time spent by officers and employees of the Board
  during that immediately preceding fiscal year on activities related to
  the responsibilities of the Board under the Act or any other Act of
  Parliament in respect of which costs are attributed in accordance with
  section 6.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), the time spent by
  officers and employees of the Board on activities directly related to
  the administration of the Board and on activities indirectly related
  to the responsibilities of the Board referred to in paragraphs
  (1)(a), (b) and (c) shall be apportioned among each of the amounts of
  time referred to in those paragraphs, respectively, in the same
  proportion as each amount of time is to the total time spent by
  officers and employees of the Board on activities directly related to
  those responsibilities.

## [Allocation of Costs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939710 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall, each year, determine the total amount of costs to be
  recovered for the following year from large oil pipeline companies by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} multiplying the percentage determined under
    paragraph 12(1)(a) by the total costs determined under section 6 and
    adjusted under section 8; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} deducting from the product determined under
    paragraph (a)

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the total of the administration levies to be paid
      during that year under section 5.1 by intermediate oil pipeline
      companies and small oil pipeline companies, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the total of the levies to be paid during that
      year under section 5.2 by large oil pipeline companies,
      intermediate oil pipeline companies and small oil pipeline
      companies.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall, each year, determine the total
  amount of costs to be recovered for the following year from large gas
  pipeline companies by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} multiplying the percentage determined under
    paragraph 12(1)(b) by the total costs determined under section 6 and
    adjusted under section 8; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} deducting from the product determined under
    paragraph (a)

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the total of the administration levies to be paid
      during that year under section 5.1 by intermediate gas pipeline
      companies, small gas pipeline companies and persons or companies
      operating border accommodation pipelines, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the total of the levies to be paid during that
      year under section 5.2 by large gas pipeline companies,
      intermediate gas pipeline companies and small gas pipeline
      companies.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall, each year, determine the total
  amount of costs to be recovered for the following year from large
  power line companies by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} multiplying the percentage determined under
    paragraph 12(1)(c) by the total costs determined under section 6 and
    adjusted under section 8; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} deducting from the product determined under
    paragraph (a)

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the total of the administration levies to be paid
      during that year under subsection 5.1(2) by small power line
      companies, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the total of the levies to be paid during that
      year under section 5.3.

## [Invoicing and Interest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939839 .Part}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  June 30 in every year, the Board shall issue to each company that is
  required to pay an administration levy under subsection 4(4) or
  section 5.1 an invoice for the amount of the levy.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On March 31, June 30, September 30 and December 31 in
  every year, the Board shall issue to each large oil pipeline company,
  large gas pipeline company and large power line company an invoice for
  25% of the cost recovery charge payable by that company for that year,
  as adjusted under section 19.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Except for a levy that is required to be paid under
  subsection 5.2(1) or 5.3(1), the amount of any levy or charge that is
  payable to the Board shall be paid within 30 days after the date of
  the Board's invoice for the amount.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a company fails to pay any amount invoiced by the
  Board within the period required by these Regulations, the company
  shall pay interest on the outstanding amount at a rate of 1.5% per
  month, compounded monthly, beginning

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of amounts payable under subsection
    (3), on the 31st day after the date of the Board's invoice; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of levies payable under subsections
    5.2(2) and 5.3(2), on the 91st day after the date of the Board's
    invoice.

  - [29.]{.lawlabel} The cost recovery charge payable by a large power
    line company for 2010 is equal to the amount determined under
    subsection 14(3) of the Regulations except that

    - [(*a*)]{.lawlabel} the forecast of electricity transmissions
      referred to in the description of B in that subsection shall be
      that provided by the company for 2010 under section 26 of these
      Regulations; and

    - [(*b*)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of the forecast of electricity
      transmissions referred to in the description of C in that
      subsection shall be the aggregate of those provided by all large
      power line companies for 2010 under section 26 of these
      Regulations.

- --- SOR/2009-307, s. 30

  - - [30.]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} On or before March 31, 2010, the
      Board shall issue to each large power line company an invoice for
      the cost recovery charge that is payable by that company for 2010.

    - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the cost recovery charge for 2010
      shall be paid by the company by May 15, 2010.

    - [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the company fails to pay the amount of the
      cost recovery charge by May 15, 2010, the company shall pay
      interest on the outstanding amount at a rate of 1.5% per month,
      compounded monthly, beginning on May 16, 2010.
