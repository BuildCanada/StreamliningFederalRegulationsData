## Compensation for Certain Birds Destroyed in British Columbia (Avian Influenza) Regulations {#compensation-for-certain-birds-destroyed-in-british-columbia-avian-influenza-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-150
:::

[HEALTH OF ANIMALS ACT](/eng/acts/H-3.3)

Registration 2004-06-04

Compensation for Certain Birds Destroyed in British Columbia (Avian
Influenza) Regulations
::::

:::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food considers that there
is no readily available market for birds referred to in the annexed
Regulations;

Therefore, the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food, pursuant to
section 55[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea){#footnotea-ID0EGCDA} of the [Health of
Animals Act](/eng/acts/H-3.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb){#footnoteb-ID0EDCDA}, hereby makes the
annexed [Compensation for Certain Birds Destroyed in British Columbia
(Avian Influenza) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-150).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-711617 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Health of Animals
    Act](/eng/acts/H-3.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bird]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bird]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2005-113, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[chicken]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chicken]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a female chicken broiler
    breeder or a chicken table egg layer. ([poulet]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[control area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[control area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area in British
    Columbia that is declared to be a control area under subsection
    27(1) of the Act and in respect of which the Minister\'s declaration
    dated March 11, 2004 remains in effect. ([région
    contrôlée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[duck]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[duck]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a female duck breeder or a duck
    table egg layer. ([canard]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[goose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a female goose breeder.
    ([oie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-113, s. 1
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-711627 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of chickens, ducks and geese destroyed in the control area under
subsection 48(1) of the Act because of the outbreak of avian influenza.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-113, s. 2
:::

## [Method of Calculation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-711631 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraph 55(a) of the Act, the market value of a chicken
  is determined as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a female chicken broiler breeder
    that is 25 weeks of age or younger or a chicken table egg layer that
    is 19 weeks of age or younger, in accordance with the formula

    ::: {#711635 .Paragraph}
    B + \[(A - B)/D\]E
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a female chicken broiler breeder
    that is older than 25 weeks of age or a chicken table egg layer that
    is older than 19 weeks of age, in accordance with the formula

    ::: {#711638 .Paragraph}
    A + \[(C - A)/(F - D)\](E - D)

    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is the maximum amount with respect to a chicken under section 2
        of the [Compensation for Destroyed Animals and Things
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-233);

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is the value of a day-old female chicken broiler breeder or a
        day-old chicken table egg layer, as the case may be;

    [C]{.dfn}
    :   is the salvage value of the chicken;

    [D]{.dfn}
    :   is 25 weeks in the case of a female chicken broiler breeder and
        19 weeks in the case of a chicken table egg layer;

    [E]{.dfn}
    :   is the age of the chicken, in weeks, when it was destroyed; and

    [F]{.dfn}
    :   is the length, in weeks, of the average production cycle of a
        female chicken broiler breeder or a chicken table egg layer, as
        the case may be.
    :::

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The market value of a chicken shall not exceed the
  maximum amount with respect to a chicken under section 2 of the
  [Compensation for Destroyed Animals and Things
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-233).

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraph 55(a) of the Act, the market value of a duck is
  determined as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a duck that is 22 weeks of age or
    younger, in accordance with the formula

    ::: {#711652 .Paragraph}
    B + \[(A - B)/D\]E
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a duck that is older than 22 weeks
    of age, in accordance with the formula

    ::: {#711655 .Paragraph}
    A + \[(C - A)/(F - D)\](E - D)

    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is \$73.34 in the case of a female duck breeder and \$37.51 in
        the case of a duck table egg layer;

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is the value of a day-old female duck breeder or a day-old duck
        table egg layer, as the case may be;

    [C]{.dfn}
    :   is the salvage value of the duck;

    [D]{.dfn}
    :   is 22 weeks;

    [E]{.dfn}
    :   is the age of the duck, in weeks, when it was destroyed; and

    [F]{.dfn}
    :   is the length, in weeks, of the average production cycle of a
        female duck breeder or a duck table egg layer, as the case may
        be.
    :::

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite the [Compensation for Destroyed Animals and
  Things Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-233), the amount that is
  established for the purpose of subsection 51(3) of the Act as the
  maximum amount with respect to a duck is \$73.34.
