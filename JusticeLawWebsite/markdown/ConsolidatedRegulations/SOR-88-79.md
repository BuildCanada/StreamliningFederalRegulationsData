## Temporary Storage Period Regulations {#temporary-storage-period-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-79
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1987-12-31

Regulations Prescribing the Period within which Goods May Remain in
Temporary Storage in an Intermediate Country before They Are Deemed Not
To Have Been Shipped Directly to Canada

P.C. 1987-2743 1987-12-31
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 18(2) of the
[Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-88-79_e_hq_10331){#fn_SOR-88-79_e_hq_10331-ID0EDBAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations prescribing the
period within which goods may remain in temporary storage in an
intermediate country before they are deemed not to have been shipped
directly to Canada](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-79), effective January 1,
1988.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-918447 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Temporary Storage Period
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-79).

## [Temporary Storage Period]{.HTitleText1} {#h-918449 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 18(1)(d) of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), where
goods are exported to Canada from any country but are transhipped in any
intermediate country, the goods shall be deemed not to have been shipped
directly to Canada from the first mentioned country if the goods remain
in temporary storage in the intermediate country for a period exceeding
six months.
