## Nordion International Incorporation Authorization Order (Incorporation of Corporations) {#nordion-international-incorporation-authorization-order-incorporation-of-corporations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-163
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1990-03-08

Order Authorizing Nordion International Inc. to Incorporate Five
Corporations under the Laws of the Netherlands, France, the United
Kingdom, Italy and the Federal Republic of Germany

P.C. 1990-434  1990-03-08
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council, pursuant to subsection 91(6) of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is satisfied that
Nordion International Inc., as a corporation incorporated under the
[Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44), is empowered to
undertake the transaction described in the annexed Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of State (Privatization and Regulatory
Affairs) and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 91(1)(a) of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Order authorizing Nordion International Inc. to
incorporate five corporations under the laws of the Netherlands, France,
the United Kingdom, Italy and the Federal Republic of
Germany](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-163).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921708 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the Nordion International Incorporation Authorization Order
(Incorporation of Corporations).

## [Authorized Transaction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921710 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Nordion International Inc.
is hereby authorized, for the purpose of sales and distribution of its
products in the countries referred to in paragraphs (a) to (e), to
incorporate the following corporations, all of the shares of which, on
incorporation, will be held by, on behalf of or in trust for Nordion
International Inc.:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a corporation incorporated under the laws of the
  Netherlands;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation incorporated under the laws of France;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a corporation incorporated under the laws of the
  United Kingdom;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a corporation incorporated under the laws of Italy;
  and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a corporation incorporated under the laws of the
  Federal Republic of Germany.
