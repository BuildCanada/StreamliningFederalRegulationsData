## Regulations Defining "official development assistance" {#regulations-defining-official-development-assistance .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2021-128
:::

[OFFICIAL DEVELOPMENT ASSISTANCE ACCOUNTABILITY ACT](/eng/acts/O-2.8)

Registration 2021-06-10

Regulations Defining "official development assistance"

P.C. 2021-524 2021-06-10
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Administrator in Council has taken into account, among other
things, the most recent definition of "official development assistance"
formulated by the Development Assistance Committee of the Organisation
for Economic Co-operation and Development;

Therefore, His Excellency the Administrator of the Government of Canada
in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister for International
Development, pursuant to section 3.1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-3784_hq_27292){#fn_81000-2-3784_hq_27292-ID0EGCAA}
of the [Official Development Assistance Accountability
Act](/eng/acts/O-2.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-3784_hq_27293){#fn_81000-2-3784_hq_27293-ID0EDCAA},
makes the annexed [Regulations Defining "official development
assistance"](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-128).

## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1299222 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[official development
assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of the [Official Development Assistance Accountability
  Act](/eng/acts/O-2.8), [[official development
  assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means international assistance that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} meets the criteria set out in the definition
    "official development assistance" or "ODA" published on the website
    of the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development, as
    amended from time to time; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} meets the criteria set out in subsection 4(1.1) of
    that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation of amendments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(a), any amendment
  to the definition "official development assistance" on the website of
  the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development is
  incorporated only when the amendment is available in both official
  languages.
