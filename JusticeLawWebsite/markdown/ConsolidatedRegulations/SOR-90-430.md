## Dundee Estates Limited Sale Authorization Order {#dundee-estates-limited-sale-authorization-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-430
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1990-07-27

Order Authorizing Dundee Estates Limited to Sell all of the Assets of
Dundee Estates Limited to Odyssey Cape Breton Tourist Attractions
Limited

P.C. 1990-1519 1990-07-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council, pursuant to subsection 91(6) of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is satisfied that Dundee
Estates Limited, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Enterprise Cape Breton
Corporation, is otherwise empowered to sell all of the assets of Dundee
Estates Limited;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister for the purposes of the [Atlantic Canada
Opportunities Agency Act](/eng/acts/A-13.7) and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to subsection 91(3) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to make the annexed Order
authorizing Dundee Estates Limited to sell all of the assets of Dundee
Estates Limited to Odyssey Cape Breton Tourist Attractions Limited.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-926361 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Dundee Estates Limited Sale Authorization
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-430).

## [Authorized Sale]{.HTitleText1} {#h-926363 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Dundee Estates Limited is
hereby authorized to sell all of the assets of Dundee Estates Limited to
Odyssey Cape Breton Tourist Attractions Limited.
