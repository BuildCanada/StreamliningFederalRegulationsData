## [Rules of Origin]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1451146 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Force of law

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following provisions of
the Canada--Ukraine Free Trade Agreement have the force of law in
Canada:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Articles 3.1 to 3.14; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Annexes 3-A and 3-C.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1451153 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}S.C. 2024, c. 3

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2024-68_e_transform_hq_1715189715){#fn_SOR-2024-68_e_transform_hq_1715189715-ID0EBCA}[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 9 of the [Canada--Ukraine Free Trade Agreement Implementation
Act, 2023](/eng/acts/C-10.541) comes into force, but if they are
registered after that day, they come into force on the day on which they
are registered.
