## Alberta Milk Order {#alberta-milk-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-719
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1994-11-15

Alberta Milk Order

P.C. 1994-1876  1994-11-15
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to section 2[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EHCAA} of the
Agricultural Products Marketing Act, is pleased hereby to repeal the
[Alberta Milk Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-719), made by Order in
Council P.C. 1984-1305 of April 18, 1984[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0EBCAA}, and to
make the annexed Order granting authority to the Alberta Milk Control
Board to regulate the marketing in interprovincial and export trade of
milk produced in Alberta, in substitution therefor.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^ \*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EHCAA){#footnote1_e}S.C.
  1991, c. 34, s. 2
  :::

  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-966180 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Dairy Board Act of Alberta;
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Alberta Milk
    Control Board established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[milk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[milk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means milk or cream from cows, produced
    for consumer use in liquid form in Canada; ([lait]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a plan for the marketing of milk,
    established and amended from time to time pursuant to the Act.
    ([plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-16, s. 35
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-966188 .Part}

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies in
respect of milk produced in the Province of Alberta and milk referred to
in paragraph 3(1)(b) produced in the Province of British Columbia.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-16, s. 36
:::

## [Interprovincial Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-966192 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2001-16, s. 37

\]
:::

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Commodity Board is authorized to regulate the
  marketing of milk in interprovincial trade and for that purpose may
  exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable by the
  Commodity Board in relation to the marketing of milk locally within
  the province under the Act and a Plan, with respect to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} persons and property situated within the Province
    of Alberta, except in respect of milk produced in that Province and
    marketed in the Province of Saskatchewan pursuant to a milk quota
    granted by The Milk Control Board of Saskatchewan in accordance with
    the [Saskatchewan Milk Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-721); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} persons situated within the Province of British
    Columbia who ship milk produced in that Province to the Province of
    Alberta, and who have been given milk quota pursuant to this Order.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The authority to regulate the marketing of milk in
  interprovincial trade does not include any power that is exercised by
  the Canadian Dairy Commission under the [Canadian Dairy Commission
  Act](/eng/acts/C-15) in relation to the marketing of milk in
  interprovincial trade.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-16, s. 38
:::

## [Levies or Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-966202 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board may, in
relation to the powers granted to it under section 3,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix and impose levies or charges on milk or any
  component of milk and collect those levies or charges from persons
  described in subsection 3(1) who are engaged in the production or
  marketing of milk and for that purpose may classify those persons into
  groups and fix the levies or charges payable by the members of the
  different groups in different amounts; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the levies or charges for the purposes of the
  Commodity Board, including the creation of reserves, the payment of
  expenses and losses resulting from the sale or disposal of milk and
  the equalization or adjustment among producers of milk of moneys
  realized from the sale of milk during such period or periods of time
  as the Commodity Board may determine.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-16, s. 39
