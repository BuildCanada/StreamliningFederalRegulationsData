## Delegation of Powers (Customs) Regulations {#delegation-of-powers-customs-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-359
:::

[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

Registration 1986-03-25

Regulations Delegating Powers Conferred by Section 163 of the Customs
Act Upon the Minister of National Revenue
::::

:::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 163(2) of the
[Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), hereby revokes the [Delegation of
Powers (Customs) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-359) made on the
12th of October, 1984[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnotea){#footnotea-ID0EDBCA} and, in
substitution therefor, makes the annexed [Regulations delegating powers
conferred by section 163 of the](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-359) [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) upon the Minister of National Revenue.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnotea-ID0EDBCA){#footnotea}SOR/84-807, 1984
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3965
  :::

Ottawa, March 25, 1986

::: FlushRight
ELMER MacKAY
:::

::: FlushRight
*Minister of National Revenue*
:::
