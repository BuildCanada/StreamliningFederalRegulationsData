## Canadian Pork Promotion-Research Levies Order {#canadian-pork-promotion-research-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2021-217
:::

[FARM PRODUCTS AGENCIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-4)

Registration 2021-10-08

Canadian Pork Promotion-Research Levies Order
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council has, by the Canadian Pork
Promotion-Research Agency Proclamation[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12576){#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12576-ID0EGAKA},
established the Canadian Pork Promotion-Research Agency pursuant to
subsection 39(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12577){#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12577-ID0EEAKA}
of the [Farm Products Agencies Act](/eng/acts/F-4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12578){#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12578-ID0EBAKA};

Whereas that Agency has been empowered to implement a promotion and
research plan pursuant to that Proclamation;

Whereas the proposed Canadian Pork Promotion-Research Levies Order is an
order of a class to which paragraph 7(1)(d)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12579){#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12579-ID0EGAIA}
of that Act applies by reason of section 2 of the [Agencies' Orders and
Regulations Approval Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._648)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12580){#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12580-ID0EDAIA},
and has been submitted to the National Farm Products Council pursuant to
paragraph 42(1)(d)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12577){#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12577-ID0EBAIA}
of that Act;

And whereas, pursuant to paragraph 7(1)(d)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12579){#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12579-ID0EBAHA}
of that Act, the National Farm Products Council has approved the
proposed Order after being satisfied that it is necessary for the
implementation of the promotion and research plan that that Agency is
authorized to implement;

Therefore, the Canadian Pork Promotion-Research Agency, pursuant to
paragraphs 42(1)(d)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12577){#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12577-ID0ELAGA}
and (e)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12577){#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12577-ID0EJAGA}
of the [Farm Products Agencies Act](/eng/acts/F-4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12578){#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12578-ID0EGAGA}
and section 9 of the schedule to the Canadian Pork Promotion-Research
Agency Proclamation[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12576){#fn_81000-2-1829-E_hq_12576-ID0EDAGA},
makes the annexed Canadian Pork Promotion-Research Levies Order.

Ottawa, October 8, 2021

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1327512 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who acts as an agent
    in the buying or selling of hogs. ([négociant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[imported hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[imported hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a hog that is imported
    into Canada and classified under one of the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) tariff item numbers set out in Schedule
    1.1. ([porc importé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[imported pork product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[imported pork product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pork product
    that is imported into Canada and classified under one or more of the
    [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) tariff item numbers set out in
    Schedule 2. ([produit du porc importé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[importer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[importer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who imports hogs
    or pork products into Canada. ([importateur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who processes
    hogs. ([transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who produces
    hogs. ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[purchaser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[purchaser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who purchases hogs
    and includes a producer, a dealer and a
    processor. ([acheteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[seller]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[seller]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who sells hogs and
    include a dealer and a producer. ([vendeur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[collector]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), in this Order, [[collector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in Ontario, the Ontario Pork Producers' Marketing
    Board;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in Quebec,[Les Éleveurs de porcs du
    Québec]{.otherLang lang="fr"};

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in Nova Scotia, Pork Nova Scotia;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in New Brunswick, Porc NB Pork;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in Manitoba, the Manitoba Pork Council;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in British Columbia, the British Columbia Hog
    Marketing Commission;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in Prince Edward Island, the Prince Edward Island
    Hog Commodity Marketing Board;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in Saskatchewan, the Saskatchewan Pork Development
    Board; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in Alberta, the Alberta Pork Producers' Development
    Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other collector

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Agency appoints a person under subsection 9(5)
  of the schedule to the Canadian Pork Promotion-Research Agency
  Proclamation, other than a collector referred to in subsection (1), to
  collect on the Agency's behalf the levies and statements imposed by
  this Order for a province, that person is, for the purposes of this
  Order, one of the collectors for that province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dealer

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** If more than one dealer is
involved in the purchase or sale of hogs, the reference to "purchaser"
is to the dealer who first receives payment of the purchase price.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Levy amount --- seller (Quebec)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A seller who sells hogs in interprovincial trade that
  are produced in Quebec must pay to the Agency, for each hog sold, a
  levy in the amount of \$1.274 per hundred kilogram dressed weight.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Levy amount --- producer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A producer who produces hogs in a province set out in
  column 1 of the table to subsection (1), but processes them in another
  province, must pay to the Agency, for each hog processed, a levy in
  the amount set out in column 2 for the province in which the hog was
  produced.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Levy amount --- producer (Quebec)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A producer who produces hogs in Quebec, but processes
  them in another province, must pay to the Agency, for each hog
  processed, a levy of \$1.274 per hundred kilogram dressed weight.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of levies --- collector

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  levies referred to in section 4 must be paid or remitted, as the case
  may be, to the Agency through a collector in the province where the
  hogs were produced.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statements --- collector

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All statements referred to in section 8 and
  subsection 10(4) must be submitted to the Agency through a collector
  for the province where the hogs were produced.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction of levy by purchaser

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  purchaser must deduct from the purchase price the levy payable under
  subsection 4(1) or (2), as the case may be, and remit it to the Agency
  on behalf of the seller.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment by processor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The processor must pay the levy payable under
  subsection 4(3) or (4) as the case may be, to the Agency on behalf of
  the producer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Document --- purchaser

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  time of the sale, the purchaser referred to in subsection 6(1), must
  provide the seller with a document setting out the purchase price and
  the amount of the levy deducted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Document --- processor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the time the hogs are processed, the processor
  referred to in subsection 6(2), must provide the producer with a
  document setting out the amount of the levy payable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit and information

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The purchaser or the
processor referred to in subsection 6(1) or (2) respectively, must remit
or pay, as the case may be, the levy to the Agency within the period
established by the laws of the collector's province for remitting levies
payable on the sale or processing of hogs in that province, together
with a statement that sets out:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, mailing address, telephone number and, if
  available, email address of the purchaser or processor, as the case
  may be;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of hogs produced in a province set out in
  column 1 of the table to subsection 4(1),

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of hogs purchased or processed, as the
    case may be, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the levies remitted or paid, as the
    case may be;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of hogs produced in Quebec,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the total number of kilograms purchased or
    processed, as the case may be, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the levies remitted or paid, as the
    case may be; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, mailing address, telephone number and, if
  available, email address of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the seller, if a levy was deducted from the
    purchase price under subsection 6(1), or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the producer, if a levy was paid under subsection
    6(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Every person referred to in
sections 4 and 6 must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep all records relating to the sale or processing
  of hogs, including the records necessary to verify any of the
  statements provided under section 8 and the amounts of levies paid,
  for a period of seven years after the day on which the hogs were sold
  or processed; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the request of the Agency, make those records
  available for examination by the Agency or the collector for the
  province in which the hogs were produced.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  request of the Agency, every person referred to in section 4 or 6 must
  prove that a levy payable under this Order has been

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deducted and remitted under subsection 6(1); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} paid under subsection 6(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to deduct levy

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a purchaser does not deduct the levy as required
  by subsection 6(1), the seller must pay the levy payable in respect of
  the sale to the Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to pay levy

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a processor does not pay the levy as required by
  subsection 6(2), the producer must pay the levy payable in respect of
  the processed hogs to the Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment and information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} When paying the levy in accordance with
  subsection (2) or (3), the seller or the producer, as the case may be,
  must provide a statement to the Agency that sets out:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, mailing address, telephone number and, if
    available, email address of the seller or producer, as the case may
    be;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of hogs produced in a province set out
    in column 1 of the table to subsection 4(1),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of hogs purchased or processed, as the
      case may be, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the levies paid; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of hogs produced in Quebec,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the total number of kilograms purchased or
      processed, as the case may be, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the levies paid.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability --- purchaser and seller

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  purchaser does not deduct the levy in accordance with subsection 6(1)
  and the seller does not pay the levy in accordance with subsection
  10(2), the purchaser and the seller are jointly and severally, or
  solidarily liable for the levy that is payable to the Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability --- processor and producer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the processor does not pay the levy in accordance
  with subsection 6(2) and the producer does not pay the levy in
  accordance with subsection 10(3), the producer and the processor are
  jointly and severally, or solidarily liable for the levy payable to
  the Agency.

    if applicable, broken down by the applicable [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) tariff item number, as set out in
    Schedule 2; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the levy to be paid to the Agency.
