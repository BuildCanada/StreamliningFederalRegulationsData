## [Prescribed Health Care Services]{.HTitleText1} {#h-933344 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
10 of Part II of Schedule V to the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the
following services are prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} laboratory, radiological or other diagnostic services
  generally available in a health care facility; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the administration of drugs, biologicals or related
  preparations in conjunction with the provision of services included in
  paragraph (a).
