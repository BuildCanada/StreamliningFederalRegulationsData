## [Individual Agreements in Effect]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1407441 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Individual agreements

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
[First Nations Jurisdiction over Education in British Columbia
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.75), the following individual agreements are in
effect:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Canada-First Nation Education Jurisdiction
  Agreement between Canada and Canim Lake Band, entered into on March
  10, 2023;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Canada-First Nation Education Jurisdiction
  Agreement between Canada and Ditidaht First Nation, entered into on
  March 30, 2023; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Canada-First Nation Education Jurisdiction
  Agreement between Canada and Sḵwx̱wú7mesh Úxwumixw (Squamish Nation),
  entered into on March 30, 2023.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on July 1, 2023, but if it is registered after that day, it comes into
force on the day on which it is registered.
