## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032176 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited activities

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada to knowingly

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deal in any property that is owned, held or
  controlled, directly or indirectly, by one of the following or by a
  person acting on behalf of or at the direction of that person:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a person associated with the Taliban, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a person associated with ISIL (Da'esh) or
    Al-Qaida;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into or facilitate any transaction related to a
  dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide or acquire any financial or related services
  in respect of a dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} make available any property other than the property
  referred to in section 3 to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a person associated with the Taliban or a person
    acting on behalf of or at the direction of that person or an entity
    that is owned, held or controlled by that person, directly or
    indirectly, or to or for the benefit of a person associated with the
    Taliban, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a person associated with ISIL (Da'esh) or Al-Qaida
    or a person acting on behalf of or at the direction of that person
    or an entity that is owned, held or controlled by that person,
    directly or indirectly, or to or for the benefit of a person
    associated with ISIL (Da'esh) or Al-Qaida; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial or related services to or for
  the benefit of any person or any entity referred to in paragraph (d)
  or acquire any such services from or for the benefit of any such
  person or entity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- humanitarian assistance in
Afghanistan

- **[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Section 2 does not prohibit the
  provision, processing or payment of funds, other financial assets or
  economic resources or the provision of goods and services that are
  necessary to ensure the timely delivery of humanitarian assistance or
  to support other activities that support basic human needs in
  Afghanistan by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the United Nations, including its programs, funds
    and other entities and bodies, as well as its specialized agencies
    and related organizations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} international organizations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} humanitarian organizations that have been granted
    observer status by the United Nations General Assembly and members
    of those humanitarian organizations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} bilaterally or multilaterally funded
    non-governmental organizations that are participating in any United
    Nations humanitarian response plan, refugee response plan or other
    United Nations appeals for assistance or in groups of humanitarian
    organizations coordinated by the United Nations Office for the
    Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs, including the employees,
    grantees, subsidiaries and implementing partners of those
    non-governmental organizations while acting in those capacities;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other person authorized for that purpose by a
    United Nations committee established by the Security Council acting
    within its mandate; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other international organization that provides
    humanitarian assistance or leads other activities that support basic
    human needs in Afghanistan and are so recognized by Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- humanitarian assistance
  in other states

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Section 2 does not prohibit the provision, processing
  or payment of funds, other financial assets or economic resources or
  the provision of goods and services that are necessary to ensure the

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the United Nations, including its programs, funds
    and other entities and bodies, as well as its specialized agencies
    and related organizations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} international organizations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} humanitarian organizations that have been granted
    observer status by the United Nations General Assembly and members
    of those humanitarian organizations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} bilaterally or multilaterally funded
    non-governmental organizations that are participating in any United
    Nations humanitarian response plan, refugee response plan or other
    United Nations appeals for assistance or in groups of humanitarian

  9, 2024 and the day on which paragraph 1 of Resolution 2664 (2022),
  adopted by the Security Council on December 9, 2022, or any resolution
  that replaces that paragraph, ceases to have effect.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Embargo --- arms and related material

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada to knowingly

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide or collect funds by any means, directly or
  indirectly, with the intention that the funds be used, or in the
  knowledge that the funds are to be used, by a person associated with
  the Taliban or a person associated with ISIL (Da'esh) or Al-Qaida;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sell, supply or transfer, directly or indirectly,
  arms and related material or explosives, wherever situated, to a
  person associated with the Taliban or to a person associated with ISIL
  (Da'esh) or Al-Qaida; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide, directly or indirectly, technical assistance
  or financial assistance related to the sale, supply, transfer,
  manufacture, maintenance or use of arms and related material or
  explosives to a person associated with the Taliban or to a person
  associated with ISIL (Da'esh) or Al-Qaida.

## [Obligations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1249964 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to determine

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each of
  the entities set out in subsection (2) must determine on a continuing
  basis whether they are in possession or control of property owned,
  held or controlled by or on behalf of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person associated with the Taliban; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person associated with ISIL (Da'esh) or Al-Qaida.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The entities are:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[authorized foreign banks]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
    as defined in section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), in
    respect of their business in Canada, and banks regulated by that
    Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a cooperative credit societies, savings and credit
    unions and caisses populaires regulated by a provincial Act and
    associations regulated by the [Cooperative Credit Associations
    Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[foreign companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
    defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), in respect of their insurance activities in
    Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[provincial
    companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
    [[societies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms are defined in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} fraternal benefit societies regulated by a
    provincial Act in respect of their insurance activities and
    insurance companies and other entities regulated by a provincial Act
    that are engaged in the business of insuring risks;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} companies regulated by the [Trust and Loan
    Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} trust companies regulated by a provincial Act;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} loan companies regulated by a provincial Act;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} entities that engage in any activity referred to in
    paragraph 5(h) of the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and
    Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501) if the activity
    involves the opening of an account for a client; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} entities authorized under provincial legislation to
    engage in the business of dealing in securities or to provide
    portfolio management or investment counselling services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to disclose --- RCMP or CSIS

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person in Canada, every Canadian outside Canada and every entity set
  out in subsection 7(2) must disclose without delay to the Commissioner
  of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police or to the Director of the
  Canadian Security Intelligence Service

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the existence of property in their possession or
    control that they have reason to believe is owned, held or
    controlled by or on behalf of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a person associated with the Taliban, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a person associated with ISIL (Da'esh) or
      Al-Qaida; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any information about a transaction or proposed
    transaction in respect of property referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No proceedings under the [United Nations
  Act](/eng/acts/U-2) and no civil proceedings lie against a person for
  a disclosure made in good faith under subsection (1).

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1249999 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  that wishes to engage in any activity that is prohibited under these
  Regulations must, before doing so, apply to the Minister in writing
  for a certificate to exempt the activity from the application of the
  Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must issue the certificate if the
  Security Council did not intend that the activity be prohibited or if
  the Security Council, the 1988 Sanctions Committee or the ISIL
  (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions Committee, as the case may be, has
  approved the activity in advance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Basic or extraordinary expenses

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person whose property is affected by the application of section 2 may
  apply to the Minister in writing for a certificate to exempt the
  property from the application of that section if the property is
  necessary for basic or extraordinary expenses.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If it is established in accordance with Resolution
  1452 (2002) of December 20, 2002, adopted by the Security Council that
  the property is necessary for basic or extraordinary expenses, the
  Minister must issue a certificate within the following time periods:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 15 working days after receiving the
    application, in the case of property necessary for basic expenses,
    if the 1988 Sanctions Committee or the ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida
    Sanctions Committee, as applicable, does not oppose the application;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 30 working days after receiving the
    application, in the case of property necessary for extraordinary
    expenses, if the 1988 Sanctions Committee or the ISIL (Da'esh) and
    Al-Qaida Sanctions Committee, as applicable, approves the
    application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mistaken identity

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person whose name is the same as or similar to the name of a person
  associated with the Taliban or to the name of a person associated with
  ISIL (Da'esh) or Al-Qaida and who claims not to be that person may
  apply to the Minister in writing for a certificate stating that they
  are not that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination by Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within 45 working days after receiving the
  application, the Minister must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} issue the certificate, if it is established that
    the applicant is not the person associated with the Taliban or the
    person associated with ISIL (Da'esh) or Al-Qaida, as the case may
    be; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide notice to the applicant of his or her
    determination, if it is not so established.

## [Personal Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1250060 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication by Minister

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, for the purpose of enforcing these Regulations or
  fulfilling any obligation under a resolution of the Security Council
  with respect to the Taliban, ISIL (Da'esh) or Al-Qaida or to respond
  to a request from the Security Council or any of its subsidiary
  organs, disclose any personal information to an official or the
  Security Council or any of its subsidiary organs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt of information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An official may receive any personal information
  disclosed to them under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication by official

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An official may, for the purpose of enforcing these
  Regulations or fulfilling any obligation under a resolution of the
  Security Council with respect to the Taliban, ISIL (Da'esh) or
  Al-Qaida or to respond to a request from the Security Council or any
  of its subsidiary organs, disclose any personal information to the
  Minister.
