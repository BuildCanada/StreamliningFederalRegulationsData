## [Rules of Origin]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1194661 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following provisions of
the Canada-Israel Free Trade Agreement have the force of law in Canada:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Article 3.1;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 1 of Article 3.2;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Articles 3.3 to 3.10;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 1 of Article 3.11;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} Articles 3.12 to 3.14; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} Annexes 3.4 and 3.12.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1194671 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2019-276_e_transform_hq_25912){#fn_SOR-2019-276_e_transform_hq_25912-ID0EBCA}[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
the Act to amend the Canada-Israel Free Trade Agreement Implementation
Act and to make related amendments to other Acts, chapter 6 of the
Statutes of Canada, 2019, comes into force, but if they are registered
after that day, they come into force on the day on which they are
registered.
