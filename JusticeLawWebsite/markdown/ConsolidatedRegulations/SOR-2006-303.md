## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726503 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).

## [Distributing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726505 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of the Act and subject to subsection (2), any one of the
  following constitutes a distributing bank or a distributing bank
  holding company:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a bank or a bank holding company that is a
    reporting issuer under any legislation that is set out in column 2
    of the schedule; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a bank or a bank holding company
    that is not a reporting issuer referred to in paragraph (a), a bank
    or a bank holding company

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that has filed a prospectus or registration
      statement under provincial or foreign legislation,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any of the securities of which are listed and
      posted for trading on a stock exchange in or outside Canada, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that is involved in, is formed for, results
      from or is continued after an amalgamation, a reorganization, an
      arrangement or a statutory procedure, if one of the participating
      bodies corporate is an entity referred to in subparagraph (i) or
      (ii).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A bank or a bank holding company that is subject to
  an exemption under provincial securities legislation, or to an order
  of the relevant provincial securities regulator that provides that the
  bank or bank holding company is not a reporting issuer for the
  purposes of the applicable legislation, is not a distributing bank or
  a distributing bank holding company for the purpose of subsection (1).
