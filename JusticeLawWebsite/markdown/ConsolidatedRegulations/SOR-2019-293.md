[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of goods classified under tariff
item No. 4011.90.90 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the
schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) for which the CPTPT
treatment is claimed and that were imported during the period commencing
on December 30, 2018 and ending on the coming into force of this Order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
the condition that the importer makes a claim for remission to the
Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years
after the date of importation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
