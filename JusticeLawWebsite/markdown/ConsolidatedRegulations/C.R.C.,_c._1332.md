[PUBLIC SERVANTS INVENTIONS ACT](/eng/acts/P-32)

Regulations Respecting Public Servants Inventions Made Pursuant to the
Public Servants Inventions Act

:::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503633 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Public Servants Inventions
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1332).

## [Report of Invention to Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503635 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Every public servant who
invents an invention shall furnish to the appropriate Minister, in
addition to any information required by the appropriate Minister under
paragraph 4(1)(a) of the [Public Servants Inventions
Act](/eng/acts/P-32), hereinafter referred to as the Act, a statement,
in Form 1 of the schedule,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} giving information in respect of the matters set out
  in that form; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} setting out such information as may be useful in
  determining whether the invention is vested in Her Majesty by the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-822, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A question as to whether an
invention is vested in Her Majesty by the Act is, for the purposes of
subsection 5(1) of the Act, deemed to have arisen and to have been
referred to the appropriate Minister on the date that the public servant
who invented the invention furnishes to that Minister, or an officer
designated by him, a statement in Form 1 of the schedule completed as
required by section 2.

## [Determination by Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503642 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  determination by the appropriate Minister under section 5 of the Act
  in respect of an invention is, for the purposes of that section,
  deemed to be made on the day on which a certificate in Form 2 of the
  schedule is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} delivered personally to the inventor; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} received by the inventor by registered mail.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A certificate described in subsection (1) that is
  sent by registered mail to an inventor at his latest known address is
  deemed to have been received by him,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the latest known address of the inventor is in
    Canada, three days after the day on which the certificate was so
    sent to him; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the latest known address of the inventor is not
    in Canada, seven days after the day on which the certificate was so
    sent to him.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an
  application for a patent for an invention has been filed with the
  Patent Office and any question arises as to whether or not the
  invention is vested in Her Majesty by the Act, the appropriate
  Minister shall, on being informed by the Patent Office of the filing
  of the application, file with the Patent Office a certificate in Form
  2 of the schedule of any determination made by him under section 5 of
  the Act in respect of the invention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A certificate in Form 2 of the schedule that is filed
  with the Patent Office forms part of the application for a patent for
  the invention to which the certificate relates.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A certificate in Form 2 of
the schedule purporting to be signed by the appropriate Minister or
person authorized by him is admissible in evidence without any proof
that the person so signing is the appropriate Minister or person or of
the signature of the Minister or person.

## [Appeals and Applications to Federal Court]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503654 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an appeal under subsection 5(2) of the Act or an
    application under subsection 5(3) of the Act shall be instituted in
    the manner provided by section 48 of the [Federal Courts
    Act](/eng/acts/F-7) for instituting a proceeding against the Crown;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 48 of the [Federal Courts
    Act](/eng/acts/F-7) and the Federal Court Rules are applicable to
    such an appeal or application as though it were a proceeding
    instituted under section 48 of the [Federal Courts
    Act](/eng/acts/F-7).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In a proceeding instituted under this section, the
  words "NOTICE OF APPEAL" or "APPLICATION", as the case may be, shall
  be substituted for the words "STATEMENT OF CLAIM" or "DECLARATION"
  where they appear in Schedule A of the [Federal Courts
  Act](/eng/acts/F-7).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2002, c. 8, s. 182
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where a public servant
files an application for a patent and

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the appropriate Minister or the Federal Court of
  Canada has determined that the invention is vested in Her Majesty, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is stated in the application that the invention is
  vested in Her Majesty,

the patent issued pursuant to the application shall be issued in the
name of Her Majesty unless the appropriate Minister, pursuant to section
8 of the Act, waives, abandons or transfers the Canadian or all rights
in respect of the invention vested in Her Majesty.

## [Waiver, Abandonment or Transfer of Rights]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503666 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an
  application for a patent has been filed in Canada for an invention
  vested in Her Majesty by the Act and the appropriate Minister,
  pursuant to section 8 of the Act, waives, abandons or transfers any of
  the Canadian ownership rights in respect of the invention, the
  appropriate Minister shall execute an instrument accordingly and
  register a copy thereof with the Patent Office.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An instrument referred to in subsection (1) shall,
  where applicable, be in Form 3 of the schedule.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every instrument executed
pursuant to section 8 of the Act that affects the rights of an inventor
shall be delivered personally to the inventor or sent to him by
registered mail at his latest known address.

## [Forms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503671 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
patent for an invention made by a public servant alone or in conjunction
with any other person shall be in Form 4, 5, 6 or 7 of the schedule
wherever and to the extent that such form is applicable.

## [Notification of Signing Authority]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503673 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The appropriate Minister
shall keep the Patent Office informed at all times of the name and
position of every person who, for the purposes of these Regulations, may
sign documents on his behalf.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [\[Revoked, SOR/93-296,
s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

## [Public Servants Inventions Committee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503676 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be a committee called the Public Servants Inventions Committee
  consisting of a representative from each of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Department of Consumer and Corporate Affairs;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Department of Energy, Mines and Resources;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Department of Justice;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} National Research Council of Canada;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Canadian Patents and Development Limited;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} Atomic Energy Control Board;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} Atomic Energy of Canada Limited;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} Treasury Board;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Department of National Defence;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} Department of Communications;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} Department of the Environment;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} Department of Supply and Services; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} Department of Industry, Trade and Commerce.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Public Servants Inventions Committee shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} act in an advisory capacity in respect of the
    administration of the Act and these Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on request, assist any Minister in determining an
    amount of an award; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on request, act in an advisory capacity to
    departments on matters pertaining to general patent policy.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The representative of the Department of Consumer and
  Corporate Affairs shall act as the chairman of the Public Servants
  Inventions Committee and the representative of the Treasury Board
  shall act as the secretary of the Committee.
