## Order Transferring from the Department of Justice to the Department of the Solicitor General the Control and Supervision of the National Crime Prevention Centre {#order-transferring-from-the-department-of-justice-to-the-department-of-the-solicitor-general-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-national-crime-prevention-centre .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2003-230
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2003-12-31

Order Transferring from the Department of Justice to the Department of
the Solicitor General the Control and Supervision of the National Crime
Prevention Centre

P.C. 2003-2087  2003-12-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers from the Department of Justice to the Department of the
Solicitor General the control and supervision of the portion of the
public service in the Department of Justice known as the National Crime
Prevention Centre, effective December 12, 2003.
