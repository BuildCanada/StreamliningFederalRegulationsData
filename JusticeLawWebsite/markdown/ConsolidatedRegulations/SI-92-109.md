## Order Giving Notice that a Tax Information Exchange Convention Between Canada and the United Mexican States Came into Force April 27, 1992 {#order-giving-notice-that-a-tax-information-exchange-convention-between-canada-and-the-united-mexican-states-came-into-force-april-27-1992 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/92-109
:::

[AGREEMENTS AND CONVENTIONS](/eng/acts/Z-02)

CANADA-MEXICO TAX INFORMATION EXCHANGE CONVENTION ACT, 1991

Registration 1992-06-17

Order Giving Notice that a Tax Information Exchange Convention Between
Canada and the United Mexican States Came into Force April 27, 1992
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
Notice is hereby given, pursuant to section 24 of the [Canada-Mexico Tax
Information Exchange Convention Act, 1991](/eng/acts/C-6.87)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-92-109_e_hq_6215){#fn_SI-92-109_e_hq_6215-ID0EBBCA},
that the Convention between the Government of Canada and the Government
of the United Mexican States for the Exchange of Information with
Respect to Taxes came into force on April 27, 1992.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-92-109_e_hq_6215-ID0EBBCA){#fn_SI-92-109_e_hq_6215}S.C.
  1992, c. 3, Part IV
  :::

Ottawa, June 3, 1992

::: FlushRight
DON MAZANKOWSKI
:::

::: FlushRight
*Minister of Finance*
