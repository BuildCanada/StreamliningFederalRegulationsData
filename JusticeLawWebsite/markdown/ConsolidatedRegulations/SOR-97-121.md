## Order Restricting Certain Immunity In Relation to the United States {#order-restricting-certain-immunity-in-relation-to-the-united-states .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-121
:::

[STATE IMMUNITY ACT](/eng/acts/S-18)

Registration 1997-02-18

Order Restricting Certain Immunity In Relation to the United States

P.C. 1997-242  1997-02-18
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Foreign Sovereign Immunities Act of 1976 of the United
States, of October 21, 1976 (28 U.S.C.) does not accord immunity to an
agency or instrumentality of a foreign state that is a separate legal
person, unless a majority of its shares or other ownership interest is
owned by the foreign state or a political subdivision of the foreign
state;

And whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that the immunity
under the [State Immunity Act](/eng/acts/S-18), in relation to the
United States, exceeds the immunity accorded by the Foreign Sovereign
Immunities Act of 1976 of the United States, of October 21, 1976 (28
U.S.C);

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to section
15[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-97-121_e_hq_5659){#fn_SOR-97-121_e_hq_5659-ID0EFBAA}
of the [State Immunity Act](/eng/acts/S-18), hereby makes the annexed
[Order Restricting Certain Immunity In Relation to the United
States](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-121).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003397 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The immunity accorded under
the [State Immunity Act](/eng/acts/S-18), in relation to the United
States, shall not extend to a legal entity, whether or not it is a
corporate entity, wherever registered, that is acting on behalf of, on
instructions from or at the request of the United States, unless a
majority of the shares or other ownership interest of the legal entity
is owned by the United States or a political subdivision of the United
States.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003399 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on February 18, 1997.
