## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1337714 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1337715 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Rules.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission counsel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission counsel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means counsel designated
    by the Chairperson of the Complaints Commission for the purposes of
    a hearing. ([avocat de la Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[counsel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[counsel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of the bar of a
    province. ([avocat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a calendar
    day. ([jour]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a submission, affidavit or
    any other documentary material, regardless of physical form or
    medium, including any correspondence, memorandum, book, plan, map,
    drawing, diagram, pictorial or graphic work, photograph, film,
    microform, sound recording, videotape and machine readable record,
    and any copy, in whole or in part, of that
    material. ([document]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[electronic transmission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[electronic transmission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes
    transmission by email or by means of the Complaints Commission's
    website, but does not include transmission by fax. ([transmission
    électronique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[file]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[file]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to file with the
    Registrar. ([déposer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[intervenor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[intervenor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is granted
    leave by the Complaints Commission to intervene at a hearing in
    accordance with section 44. ([intervenant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a complainant or a person who is
    the subject of a complaint and any other person who satisfies the
    Complaints Commission that they have a substantial and direct
    interest in the hearing under section 250.44 of the
    Act. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an individual, a
    corporation, a partnership and an unincorporated
    association. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Registrar of the
    Complaints Commission. ([greffier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1337729 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Rules apply to all
hearings conducted under subsection 250.38(1) of the Act.

### [Purpose]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1337732 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of these
Rules is to enable the Complaints Commission to act fairly, effectively
and expeditiously and to conduct its proceedings in a manner
proportional to the significance of the issues and the complexity of the
complaint before it and the public interest, including through the
efficient use of public resources.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matter not provided for in Rules

**[[2.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.2 .sectionLabel}** If a matter is not
provided for in these Rules, the practice it is to be determined by
reference to the purpose of these Rules and any practice directions
issued under section 29.

### [Non-compliance with Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1337737 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of non-compliance

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  failure to comply with these Rules is an irregularity and does not
  render a proceeding or a step, document or order in a proceeding a
  nullity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission may grant relief

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If there is a failure to comply with a rule, the
  Complaints Commission may grant any relief that it considers necessary
  to achieve the purpose of these rules.

### [Varying Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1337743 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Varying or supplementing rules

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Complaints Commission may vary or supplement a rule, or dispense with
  compliance with a rule in whole or in part, in order to deal with all
  matters as informally and expeditiously as the circumstances and
  considerations of fairness permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Complaints Commission varies or supplements
  these Rules or dispenses with compliance with all or part of them, it
  must inform the parties and intervenors.

### [Computation of Time]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1337749 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Interpretation Act](/eng/acts/I-21)

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The computation of time
under these Rules or under an order of the Complaints Commission or a
decision of the Registrar is governed by sections 26 to 29 of the
[Interpretation Act](/eng/acts/I-21).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension or abridgement

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Complaints Commission may, on motion, extend or abridge a period
  provided for by these Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affidavit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The affidavit in support of a motion for an extension
  or abridgement of time must set out the reason for the delay or
  urgency, as the case may be.

### [Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1337757 .Subheading}

#### [Memorandum of Fact and Law]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1337758 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A memorandum of fact and
law must contain a table of contents and, in consecutively numbered
paragraphs,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of fact;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the points in issue;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the submissions;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the order sought;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a list of the authorities, statutes and regulations
  to be referred to; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in an appendix and, if necessary as a separate
  document, a copy of the authorities or relevant excerpts and any
  statutory provisions cited or relied on that have not been reproduced
  in the memorandum of another party or intervenor.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission to Commission

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** At the end of the hearing,
the parties must submit their memorandum of fact and law to the
Complaints Commission.

#### [Service]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1337769 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of service

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Service
  of all documents is effected

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, by leaving a copy of
    the document with that individual;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a partnership, corporation or
    unincorporated association, by leaving a copy of the document with a
    partner, officer or director; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person referred to in
    paragraphs (a) or (b) who is represented by counsel, by leaving a
    copy of the document with counsel with the proof of service referred
    to in section 11.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other means of service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Service of a document is also effected by sending a
  copy of the document by electronic transmission in accordance with
  subsection (3), by fax transmission in accordance with subsection (4),
  by registered mail, or by any other means the Complaints Commission
  orders.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service by electronic transmission

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A document that is served by electronic transmission
  must be accompanied by an electronic message setting out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sender's name, telephone number, email address
    and mailing address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person being served;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the transmission;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the title or description of the document being
    transmitted; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name, telephone number and email address of the
    person to be contacted if transmission problems occur.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service by fax

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A document that is served by fax must be accompanied
  by a cover page setting out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sender's name, telephone number, fax number,
    email address and mailing address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person being served;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the transmission;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total number of pages being transmitted,
    including the cover page;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the title or description of the document being
    transmitted; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the name, telephone number and email address of the
    person to contacted if transmission problems occur.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format --- electronic transmission

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A document that is served by electronic transmission
  must be in PDF (Portable Document Format) or any other format allowed
  by the Complaints Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service after 5 p.m.

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A document that is served by electronic transmission
  or by fax after 5 p.m. at the recipient's local time is deemed to have
  been served on the next day that is not a Saturday or a holiday.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed served

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** If a document has been
served in a manner not authorized by these Rules or by an order of the
Complaints Commission, the Complaints Commission may, on motion, order
that a document be deemed validly served if it is satisfied that the
document came to the notice of the recipient or that it would have come
to that person's notice were it not for the person's avoidance of
service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Service of a document is proved by:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgement of service signed by or on
    behalf of the recipient; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an affidavit of service stating the name of the
    person who served the document and the date, place and manner of
    service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delivery receipt

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The affidavit of service for a document that was
  served by electronic transmission, fax or registered mail must include
  a receipt confirming that the document was successfully delivered to
  the recipient.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retaining proof

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person serving documents under these Rules must
  maintain the proof referred to in subsections (1) and (2) that the
  document was served in accordance with section 9 and must produce that
  proof to the Complaints Commission on request.

#### [Filing of Documents]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1337808 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service before filing

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document that is required to be filed under these Rules may be filed
  only if a copy of that document has been served on each party and any
  intervenors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a document referred to in subsection (1) is
  filed, it must be accompanied by proof of service that meets the
  requirements of section 11.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic filing

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  parties and intervenors must file their documents by electronic
  transmission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative means of filing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Complaints Commission may, if it considers that
  it is justified in the circumstances, allow paper filing or fax
  filing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format -- electronic transmission

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document that is filed by electronic transmission must be in either
  PDF (Portable Document Format) or any other format allowed by the
  Complaints Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing after 5 p.m.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document that is filed by electronic transmission
  or fax transmission after 5 p.m. standard time is deemed to have been
  filed on the next day that is not Saturday or a holiday.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format --- paper filing

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), only
  those documents that are printed on letter size paper and that have
  numbered pages may be filed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format --- non-standard

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a document is not printed on letter size paper and
  cannot be reasonably converted to that format by the person filing it,
  it may be filed in its existing format.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Original

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of any oral or written hearing, the electronic version of
  a document filed by electronic transmission is deemed to be the
  original.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Production of original

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Complaints Commission requests to examine the
  original of any document filed, the party or intervenor who filed it
  must produce it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Irregularity or defect

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** At any time before the
Complaints Commission sends its report under section 250.48 of the Act,
it may draw the attention of a party to any irregularity or defect of a
document and permit the party to remedy it on any conditions that the
Complaints Commission considers fair.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement under oath or solemn
affirmation

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  statement made under oath or solemn affirmation may be filed by
  electronic transmission if a scanned version of the statement is filed
  along with a document attesting to the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the document sent by electronic transmission
    is an electronic version of a paper document that has been signed by
    the person making the oath or solemn affirmation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the signed paper document is available and
    will be produced on the Complaints Commission's request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retaining document in paper format

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The statement made under oath or solemn affirmation
  must be retained in paper format by the party or intervenor filing it
  for one year after the final report is issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Production of the original

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Complaints Commission requests to examine the
  original of the statement made under oath or solemn affirmation, the
  party or intervenor who filed it must produce the original signed
  document for review.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certified electronic copy

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** If a document is filed by
electronic transmission and a copy certified by the Registrar or a
person designated by the Registrar is requested from the Complaints
Commission, the Registrar may provide a paper copy of the electronic
document stamped "certified copy".

#### [Official Record of Hearings]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1337847 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Official record of hearing

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The Complaints Commission
must keep, in the original and official record of a hearing, all
documents in the format in which they were filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public consultation

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any confidentiality order
  made under section 55, the public may consult, in a format determined
  by the Registrar, documents that have been received in evidence in the
  public record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Private hearings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Documents filed with the Complaints Commission in
  respect of a private hearing do not form part of the public record.

### [Composition of Panel for Hearings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1337855 .Subheading}

#### [Composition and Chairperson of Panel]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1337856 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition of panel

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 250.4 of the Act, the member or members of the Complaints
Commission who are assigned by the Chairperson of the Complaints
Commission make up the panel that is to hold hearings under subsection
250.38(1) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of Chairperson

**[[22.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.1 .sectionLabel}** The Chairperson of
the Complaints Commission must designate, from among the members of the
panel, a person who will act as Chairperson of the panel and who will be
responsible for the management of day-to-day business, including
communications between the panel and the Complaints Commission and
between the panel, parties and intervenors.

#### [Absence, Incapacity or Vacancy]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1337861 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence, incapacity or vacancy

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  event of the absence or incapacity of a member of the panel or if a
  position on the panel is vacant, the hearing may continue with the
  remaining members of the panel or the Chairperson of the Complaints
  Commission may assign one or more additional members to the panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Single member

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  Chairperson of a panel composed of only one member, the Chairperson of
  the Complaints Commission may assign one or more members of the
  Complaints Commission to the panel to conduct the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Panel not void

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The absence or incapacity of a member of the panel or
  the vacancy of a position on the panel does not invalidate the
  proceeding.

#### [New Member]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1337869 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assignment of new member

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** If the Chairperson of the
Complaints Commission assigns a new member under subsection 23(1) or
(2), the following rules apply:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the parties and intervenors must make submissions to
  the Chairperson of the Complaints Commission as to how the hearing
  should proceed; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Chairperson of the Complaints Commission may,
  after considering the submissions and taking into account the
  interests of justice and the purpose of these Rules, establish the
  procedure for the hearing, including the rehearing of evidence or the
  use of transcripts of evidence given to date.

#### [Decision Making]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1337874 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Majority of votes

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** A matter before a panel
is decided by a majority of the members, but in the case of a tie, the
Chairperson of the panel has the deciding vote.

#### [Interim or Preliminary Questions]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1337877 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson of panel or delegate

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The Chairperson of the
panel or their delegate may, at any time during a proceeding, determine
any interim or preliminary question, including a motion, and may
exercise any powers of the Complaints Commission necessary to determine
the question.

### [Questions To Be Determined]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1337880 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Question of law, jurisdiction, practice
or procedure

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** At any time during a
proceeding, the Complaints Commission may determine any question of law,
jurisdiction, practice or procedure and may stay a proceeding in whole
or in part until the question is determined.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to Federal Court

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Complaints Commission may, in accordance with section 18.3 of the
  [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7), refer any question of law, of
  jurisdiction or of practice or procedure to the Federal Court for
  hearing and determination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Commission

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of a reference to the Federal Court, the
  Complaints Commission may, subject to the direction of the Court,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} proceed to hear evidence relevant to all questions
    except those that have been referred to the Court; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} stay the hearing as it relates to the questions
    that have been referred to the Court and reserve its decision
    pending the decision of the Court.

### [Practice Directions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1337890 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Complaints Commission may issue practice directions that are
  consistent with these Rules and their purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Technology

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Complaints Commission may issue directions
  requiring the use of any electronic or digital means of communication,
  storage or retrieval of information or any technology that it
  considers appropriate to facilitate the conduct of a hearing or a case
  management conference.

### [Proceedings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1337896 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transcription or recording ---
proceedings

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Complaints Commission may transcribe or record its proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record --- proceedings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any transcript or recording is a part of the record
  of the proceedings.

### [Accommodation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1337902 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Registrar

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** Any person requiring
reasonable accommodation at a proceeding must, no later than 30 days
before the day on which the proceeding is to begin, provide notice to
the Registrar.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Pre-hearing Procedures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1337905 .Part}

### [Hearings Respecting Similar Questions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1337906 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers regarding similar questions

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If two
  or more hearings before the Complaints Commission concern the same or
  similar questions, the Complaints Commission may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} combine the hearings or any part of them;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} hear the questions at the same time;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} hear the questions one immediately after the other;
    or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} stay one or more of the questions pending
    determination of one of them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders regarding procedure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Complaints Commission may make orders respecting
  the procedure to be followed with respect to hearings.

### [Case Management]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1337916 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Case management conferences

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** The Complaints Commission
may conduct one or more case management conferences, which will be
presided over by a member of the panel that will conduct the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions regarding scheduling

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The Complaints Commission
may issue directions with respect to the scheduling of case management
conferences.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions regarding matters for
consideration

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Complaints Commission may include in the directions referred to in
  section 34 a list of the matters to be considered at the case
  management conference and may require the filing of memoranda
  regarding any of those matters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matters to be considered

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Matters to be considered include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dates, duration and location of the hearing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the hearing will be oral or written, or
    partly oral and partly written, and whether documents will be
    presented electronically or in paper format in the course of the
    hearing;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any pending or anticipated motions and a deadline
    date for the hearing of motions;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any issues of confidentiality;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the identification, clarification, simplification
    and elimination of issues;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the possibility of obtaining admissions of
    particular facts or documents, including an agreed statement of
    facts;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the official language to be used for the hearing,
    as well as the official language in which each witness will testify,
    and the need for interpreters, if any;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a timetable for the exchange or serving and filing
    of the various documents related to the hearing, including
    affidavits of documents, statements or summaries of anticipated
    evidence, joint briefs of authorities and agreed books of documents;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any matter relating to pre-hearing disclosure;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a timetable for the intervenors;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} all matters related to expert witnesses;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the advisability of a pre-hearing reference or
    determination of a question or issue of law, of jurisdiction or of
    practice and procedure;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} any requirement for a notice of a constitutional
    question;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} a timetable for any subsequent case management
    conferences; and

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} any other matters that may facilitate the conduct
    of the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** After each case
management conference, the Complaints Commission may issue an order in
writing setting out any rulings it has made relating to the matters
considered at the conference.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Firm requirements

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  dates set and other requirements established by case management orders
  are firm.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for variation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request for a variation must be made by motion
  showing that compelling reasons exist for the variation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Commission

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Complaints Commission may vary any order made
  under subsection (2) if it is satisfied that compelling reasons exist
  for the variation.

### [Motions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1337950 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motions

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** The procedure set out in
sections 39 to 43 applies only to the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a motion relating to the jurisdiction of the
  Complaints Commission;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a motion to stay or dismiss a proceeding;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a motion raising any constitutional questions,
  including questions raised under the Canadian Charter of Rights and
  Freedoms;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a motion for a confidentiality order;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a motion that a hearing or part of a hearing in a
  proceeding be held in private;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a motion to prohibit a person from disclosing
  information disclosed in a hearing; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} any other question that the Complaints Commission
  orders resolved by way of motion under sections 39 to 43.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of motion

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  motion is commenced by a written notice of motion setting out the
  grounds for the motion and the order sought.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of motion must be accompanied by any
  supporting affidavits.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of response

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** Any Commission counsel,
party or intervenor who has been served with a notice of motion may, no
later than 14 days after being served, serve a response on the other
parties and any intervenors, stating the grounds on which the motion is
opposed and, if applicable, any supporting affidavits.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence and memorandum

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** No later than 14 days
after the service of the response, the parties participating in the
motion must serve on the other parties

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any supplementary evidence to be relied on; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a memorandum of fact and law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Testimony by affidavit

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), testimony
  on a motion must be given by affidavit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral testimony

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Complaints Commission may, before or during the
  hearing, grant leave for oral testimony in relation to an issue raised
  in the notice of motion and may, on application of any party with
  standing to cross-examine, permit cross-examination on affidavits.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Commission

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** After hearing a motion,
the Complaints Commission may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} make the order sought or any other suitable order;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} dismiss the motion, in whole or in part;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} adjourn the hearing of the motion, in whole or in
  part; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the motion is heard prior to the commencement of
  the hearing on the merits of the proceeding to which the motion
  relates, adjourn the hearing of the motion so that the panel presiding
  at the hearing on the merits may determine it.

### [Intervention]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1337982 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Valuable contribution

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** The Complaints Commission
may allow a person to intervene if the Complaints Commission is
satisfied that the person can make a significant contribution or bring a
significant perspective to the hearing, and the potential benefits of
the intervention outweigh any prejudice to the parties that might be
caused by the intervention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motion

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  motion for leave to intervene must be served on the parties and the
  intervenors along with an affidavit setting out the facts on which the
  motion is based.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A motion for leave to intervene must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the title of the proceedings in which the person
    making the motion wishes to intervene;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of that person;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the valuable contribution or
    perspective that the person will bring to the proceeding in light of
    the matters in issue in the hearing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of the party, if any, whose position that
    person intends to support;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the official language to be used by that person at
    the hearing of the motion and, if leave is granted, at the hearing
    on the merits; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of how that person proposes to
    participate in the proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** Commission counsel, a
party or an intervenor served with a motion for leave to intervene may,
within 14 days after that service, serve a response to the motion on the
person making the motion and on the parties and intervenors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** A person making a motion
for leave to intervene may, within seven days after the service of the
response referred to in section 46, serve a reply on the Commission
Counsel, the parties and intervenors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint submissions

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** If two or more persons
making a motion for leave to intervene have the same or substantially
similar expertise or perspectives, the Complaints Commission may require
them to file joint submissions, or otherwise participate jointly.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Commission

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** The Complaints Commission
may limit the participation of an intervenor to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cross-examination of witnesses;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the right to lead evidence;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the obligation to produce or disclose documents;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} one or more issues raised in the hearing;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a portion of the hearing only;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} written submissions; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} time-limited oral submissions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intervention allowed

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** If a motion for leave to
intervene is allowed,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Registrar must send to the intervenor a list of
  all documents filed in the proceedings before or on the day on which
  the motion for leave to intervene is allowed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on request, the intervenor may obtain copies of the
  documents on the list from the Registrar;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Commission counsel, each party and each other
  intervenor must serve on the intervenor any document that is filed by
  them after the day on which the motion for leave to intervene is
  allowed; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} access by an intervenor to a document filed or
  received in evidence is subject to any relevant confidentiality order
  of the Complaints Commission.

### [Production and Disclosure of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1338017 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation of parties and intervenors

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Parties and intervenors must provide
  to the Complaints Commission all documents that are in their
  possession, power or control and that are relevant to the subject
  matter of the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a party or intervenor claims that a document is
  confidential or contains confidential information, or is subject to
  privilege or protection under sections 37 to 39 of the [Canada
  Evidence Act](/eng/acts/C-5), that party or intervenor must present
  evidence in support of each claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality ruling

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Complaints Commission may make a ruling on the
  confidentiality claim with respect to each document or piece of
  information, except claims for protection under sections 37 to 39 of
  the [Canada Evidence Act](/eng/acts/C-5), and may make such orders as
  it considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to provide

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 77, no document or other piece of
  evidence that a party or intervenor seeks to use for the hearing will
  be received in evidence unless the party or intervenor provides copies
  to the Complaints Commission at least 30 days before the hearing
  commences.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing obligation to inform

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** Any party or intervenor
who has filed documents with or produced documents to the Complaints
Commission and then comes into possession or control of or obtains power
over a relevant document, or who becomes aware that the disclosure is
incomplete or that the information contained in those documents is
inaccurate, must as soon as possible notify the Complaints Commission of
the new documents, the incompleteness of the disclosure, or the
inaccuracy of the information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality of documents

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** Documents produced by a
party, an intervenor or any other person must be treated as confidential
by the Complaints Commission unless they are made part of the public
record or the Complaints Commission determines otherwise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Undertakings

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** Before a party, an
intervenor or a person who may be called as a witness is provided with a
document that has not yet been introduced into evidence, the Complaints
Commission may require that the party, intervenor, person and their
counsel provide to the Complaints Commission the form set out in
Schedule 1.

### [Confidentiality Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1338050 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motion

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** The Complaints Commission
may, on its own motion or on the motion of Commission counsel or a party
or intervenor who has filed or will file a document, order that the
document or information in the document be treated as confidential and
may make any order that it considers appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of motion

**[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** Commission counsel, a
party or an intervenor making a motion for an order of confidentiality
must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} include in the grounds for the motion details of the
  specific, direct harm that would allegedly result from unrestricted
  disclosure of the document or information;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} include in the motion a draft confidentiality order
  that contains the following elements

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the document, the information or
    the category of documents or information for which the order is
    sought,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the names of the persons or categories of person
    who are entitled to have access to the confidential document or
    information,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the document, information or category of document
    or information available to the persons or categories of persons
    referred to in subparagraph (ii),

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any proposed confidentiality agreement that the
    persons referred to in subparagraph (ii) intend to sign and the
    provisions of that agreement,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the number of copies of confidential documents to
    be provided to the persons referred to in subparagraph (ii) and any
    limitations on subsequent reproduction of that document by those
    persons, and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the procedures to be followed with respect to
    confidential documents once the hearing is completed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing without confidentiality order

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** A party, an intervenor or
Commission counsel claiming confidentiality in a document to be filed
that is not covered by a confidentiality order must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} file a public version of the document that does not
  include the confidential information;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the Registrar with a version of the document
  marked "confidential" that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} includes and identifies the confidential
    information referred to in paragraph (a) that has been deleted from
    the public version,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} identifies the nature of the claim to
    confidentiality, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} does not include information for which protection
    is claimed under the[Canada Evidence Act](/eng/acts/C-5); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} bring a motion under section 55 for an order allowing
  them to file the confidential version.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing with confidentiality order

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** A party, an intervenor or
Commission counsel who wishes to file a document containing information
that is subject to a confidentiality order under section 55 must file a
public version that does not include the confidential information and a
confidential version with each page clearly marked "confidential"
together with a reference to the date of the confidentiality order. The
confidential material that has been deleted from the public version must
be highlighted in the confidential version.

### [Witness Interviews]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1338092 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interview by Commission representative

- **[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

  subject matter of the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presence of counsel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Persons who are interviewed are entitled to have
  counsel present at their own expense.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement or summary of anticipated
evidence

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  Commission counsel determines that a person will be called as a
  witness following an interview, Commission counsel will provide the
  witness' statement if one exists or prepare a summary of the witness'
  anticipated evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline anticipated evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Commission counsel must provide a statement or

  confidentiality orders, at least three days before the witness
  testifies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of summary

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The summary of the witness's anticipated evidence
  must be in writing and contain the substance of the testimony of the
  witness, as well as a list of documents to which the witness may
  refer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of documents

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a witness refuses to be interviewed by Commission
  counsel before testifying, if no statement is given or if, for any
  reason, no summary of anticipated evidence can be prepared, Commission
  counsel must, at least three days before that witness is to testify,
  provide the parties and intervenors with the list of documents that
  may be presented to the witness and must highlight the portions of the
  documents that relate to the witness's anticipated evidence.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Conduct of Hearings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1338110 .Part}

### [Interpreter]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1338111 .Subheading}

### [Private Hearings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1338117 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** If the Complaints
Commission orders that a hearing or any part of a hearing be held in
private under section 250.42 of the Act, it may make any of the
following orders:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that certain persons be excluded;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that certain persons be admitted on terms and
  conditions;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} that restrictions be placed on the disclosure and
  publication of evidence, including the use of non-identifying initials
  and the redaction of transcripts and exhibits;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} that restrictions be placed on public access to the
  Complaints Commission's record, or that the Registrar maintain
  separate public and confidential records; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} that any other order that the Complaints Commission
  considers appropriate be made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Private hearings

- **[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person believes that any information described in paragraph
  250.42(a), (b) or (c) of the Act is likely to be disclosed, they may
  request that the Complaints Commission hold all or part of the hearing
  in private.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Burden of proof

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person making the request bears the burden of
  establishing why it is necessary for the hearing to be private.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Private consideration of request

- **[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Complaints Commission may convene in private to consider a request
  under section 64.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} After convening in private to consider the request
  under section 64, the Complaints Commission must make its decision
  public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of public decision

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The public decision must set out the following in
  writing:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the criteria and principles applied in determining
    whether the information will be heard in private;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the general nature, to the extent possible, of any
    evidence that will be heard in private; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for accepting or denying the request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidential reasons

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Complaints Commission may, in addition to its
  reasons referred to in paragraph (3)(c), issue confidential reasons,
  not available to the public, referring to specific evidence that it
  decided should be heard in private. If the Complaints Commission
  decides to issue confidential reasons, it must give reasons for its
  decision.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-disclosure of information

- **[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), if a
  hearing or a part of a hearing is ordered to be held in private, no
  person may disclose, except to their representative or to another
  person who participates in or attends the hearing or the part of that
  hearing that was ordered to be held in private,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any information disclosed in the hearing or the
    part of the hearing that is held in private; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the confidential reasons, if any, issued by the
    Complaints Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order for disclosure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the motion of a person, the Complaints Commission
  may make an order permitting a person to disclose any information
  referred to in subsection (1).

### [Examination of Witnesses]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1338149 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Testimony

**[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** All testimony before the
Complaints Commission must be given under oath or solemn affirmation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence by affidavit

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** The evidence of a witness
or proof of a particular fact or document may be given by affidavit,
unless the Complaints Commission orders otherwise, and the deponent must
be made available for cross-examination, if required.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of witnesses

- **[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Complaints Commission may, on request or on its own motion, order that
  witnesses be excluded from the hearing until they are called to
  testify.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No communication by excluded witnesses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No witness who has been excluded from a hearing may
  communicate with any person about any evidence or testimony that is
  given during the course of a hearing until after the witness has
  testified, except with the leave and on the direction of the
  Complaints Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witness counsel

**[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** Witnesses may be
represented by counsel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sequence of examination

**[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** The sequence of
examination in the ordinary course of a hearing is as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Commission counsel leads the examination in chief of
  the witnesses, and may ask both leading and non-leading questions;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the parties may then cross-examine the witness in the
  order determined by the parties or, failing their agreement, by the
  Complaints Commission;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to the leave of the Commission, intervenors
  and counsel may cross-examine a witness; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Commission counsel may re-examine the witness.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to lead examination in chief

- **[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party, an intervenor, a witness or their counsel may apply to the
  Complaints Commission for leave to lead the examination in chief of a
  particular witness.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicable rules

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If leave to lead the examination in chief is granted,
  the examination must be governed by the normal rules for the
  examination of one's own witness in court proceedings, unless the
  Complaints Commission directs otherwise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limits

**[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** The Complaints Commission
may reasonably limit further examination or cross-examination of a
witness if it is satisfied that the examination or cross-examination has
been sufficient to disclose fully and fairly all matters relevant to the
issues in the proceeding.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission questions

**[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** The Complaints Commission
may question any witness who gives oral evidence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication with witnesses

**[[75]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-75 .sectionLabel}** Unless authorized by the
Complaints Commission, no party, intervenor, witness or their counsel,
may speak to a witness, after that witness has been sworn, affirmed or
otherwise accepted as a witness, about the evidence that they have given
or are expected to give until the evidence of that witness is complete.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intention to call witness

- **[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party must provide the Complaints Commission with 15 days' notice of
  its intention to call a witness.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to call witness

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An intervenor or counsel for a witness may by motion,
  on 15 days' notice, apply to the Complaints Commission for leave to
  call a witness whom the intervenor or counsel for a witness believes
  has evidence relevant to an issue, if Commission counsel has refused
  to call the witness.

### [Use of Documents During Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1338183 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement

- **[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  party, intervenor or counsel for a witness calls a witness, they must
  provide Commission counsel, the parties and any intervenors who have
  an interest in the subject matter of the proposed evidence with the
  statement of that witness, at least five days before the witness is to
  testify.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written summary

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the absence of such a statement, the party,
  intervenor or counsel for the witness must, subject to any
  confidentiality orders, prepare and provide Commission counsel with a
  written summary of that witness' anticipated evidence and a list of
  documents that may be put to the witness at least five days before the
  witness is to testify.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cross-examination --- previous statement

- **[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person uses a document for the purpose of contradicting a witness on
  cross-examination on previous statements, section 10 of the [Canada
  Evidence Act](/eng/acts/C-5) applies, with any necessary
  modifications.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List and copy of documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Counsel or the person conducting the
  cross-examination must provide to the parties, Commission counsel, the
  witnesses and any intervenors who have an interest in the subject
  matter of the proposed evidence, not later than five days before the
  testimony of that witness begins, a list of documents that may be put
  to the witness and copies of those documents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to produce document

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If counsel or the person conducting the
  cross-examination fails to produce a document in accordance with
  subsection (2), the Complaints Commission may order that the document
  be produced to all parties, adjourn the proceedings if necessary or
  make any other suitable order to ensure fairness in the proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of document for other purposes

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If counsel or the person conducting the
  cross-examination wishes to use a document in cross-examination for
  any purpose other than to contradict the witness, that document must
  be produced in accordance with section 51.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of summary of anticipated evidence

**[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** A summary of anticipated
evidence is not testimony and may not be referred to in the examination
of the witness.

### [Witness Panels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1338200 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witness panels

**[[80]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80 .sectionLabel}** The Complaints Commission
may require that some or all of the witnesses testify as a panel at a
time and in a manner determined by the Complaints Commission.

### [Expert Witnesses]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1338203 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expert report

- **[[81]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-81 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  Commission counsel, a party or an intervenor intends to introduce the
  report evidence of an expert witness at the hearing, they must serve
  it on all parties and intervenors at least 60 days before the expert
  is scheduled to testify.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report in response

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Commission counsel, a party or an intervenor may
  serve the report of an expert in response on all parties and
  intervenors, provided that it is served at least 30 days before the
  responding expert is scheduled to testify.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report in reply

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Commission counsel, the party or the intervenor
  calling the expert may, at least 15 days before the expert is
  scheduled to testify, serve an expert report in reply on all parties
  and intervenors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of report

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A report referred to in any of subsections (1) to (3)
  must include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a full statement of the evidence of the expert;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the expert's qualifications and areas in respect of
    which it is proposed that he or she be qualified as an expert;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the sources and documents relied on in the report;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the form set out in Schedule 3, duly completed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expert report provided to the Registrar

- **[[82]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-82 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  otherwise ordered under section 36, a report referred to in section 81
  must be filed at least 10 days before the expert is scheduled to
  testify.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reading by the Commission

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Complaints Commission may read the expert report
  in advance of the testimony of the expert.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The report of an expert does not form part of the
  record unless it is received in evidence at the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission-appointed experts

- **[[83]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-83 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Complaints Commission may, at any time, by order, appoint one or more
  independent experts to inquire into and report on any question of fact
  or opinion relevant to an issue in a proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The order must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the expert being appointed, and their
    title and qualifications;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the instructions given to the expert with respect
    to the preparation of the report;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the questions to be posed to the expert;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the report of the expert is to be
    provided to the Complaints Commission; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the nature and extent of the expert's participation
    in the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar must serve a copy of the report on
  every party and any intervenor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The report does not form part of the record until it
  is received in evidence at the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any party or intervenor may file a written response
  to the expert's report and may examine the expert. The order and
  nature of such examinations are to be determined by the Complaints
  Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further or supplementary report

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Complaints Commission may order the expert to
  make a further or supplementary report and subsections (3) to (5)
  apply to that report.

### [Adjournment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1338242 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of Commission

- **[[84]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-84 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Complaints Commission may, of its own motion or on the request of
  Commission counsel, a party or an intervenor, adjourn a hearing if it
  is necessary to permit a fair hearing to be held.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors for consideration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In considering whether to grant an adjournment, the
  Complaints Commission may consider the following factors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any prejudice that might be caused to a person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the timing of the request or motion for the
    adjournment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number of prior requests for an adjournment;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number of adjournments already granted;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} prior directions or orders with respect to the
    scheduling of future hearings;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the public interest;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the costs of an adjournment;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the availability of witnesses;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the efforts made to find alternatives to the
    adjournment request;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the principle of a fair hearing; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} any other relevant factor.

### [Maintenance of Order at Hearings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1338259 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders or directions

- **[[85]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-85 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Complaints Commission may make orders or give directions that it
  considers necessary for the maintenance of order at the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attendance at a hearing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the Complaints
  Commission, by order, may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} impose restrictions on a person's presence at a
    hearing; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} exclude a person from further participation in or
    attendance at a hearing until the Complaints Commission orders
    otherwise.

I undertake to the Military Police Complaints Commission (Commission)
that documents or information that are produced to me in connection with
the Commission's proceedings will not be used by me for any purpose
other than for those proceedings. I undertake that I will not disclose
any such documents or information to anyone other than the following
persons provided that they have also given this written undertaking

FOR COUNSEL

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the parties, intervenors or witnesses I represent in
  this proceeding;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} counsel and staff assisting me in the proceeding; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any experts retained in the proceeding.

FOR NON-COUNSEL

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} my counsel;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any persons retained to assist me in the proceeding;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any experts retained by me in the proceeding.

I understand that this undertaking ceases to apply once any such
document or information has been entered as a public exhibit in the
hearing, or to the extent that the Commission may release me from the
undertaking with respect to any document or information.

I also understand that this undertaking applies only with respect to
documents disclosed by the Commission and has no application with
respect to documents or information that I already possess, or that I
may obtain independently during the Commission's proceedings, even if
they are the same as some of the documents or information produced to me
in connection with the Commission's proceedings.

I further understand that the documents and information which remain
subject to this undertaking at the end of the proceeding must be
destroyed and

FOR COUNSEL, I undertake:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to destroy those documents or information (and any
  copies that I may have made) and to provide a certificate of
  destruction to the Commission or to return those documents to the
  Commission for destruction; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to collect and destroy such documents or information
  (and any copies that I may have made) from anyone to whom I have
  disclosed any documents or information that were provided to me by the
  Commission in connection with the Commission's proceedings, or to
  return those documents to the Commission.

FOR NON-COUNSEL, I undertake:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to provide such documents or information to my
  counsel and understand that my counsel will destroy them; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if I am not represented by counsel, to give to the
  Commission all documents and information provided to me by the
  Commission for the purposes of the proceeding (and any copies that I
  may have made).

I undertake to safeguard the documents or information provided to me,
including notes taken from or copies made of those documents or
information, at all times.

If documents or information subject to this undertaking are lost or
stolen or that here has been unauthorized access, I undertake to advise
the Commission immediately. I agree to take whatever steps are necessary
to mitigate the risks of improper disclosure of the documents or
information.
