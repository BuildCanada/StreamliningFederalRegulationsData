P.C. 2016-744 2016-08-05

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834147 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1
.sectionLabel}** [[Cannabis]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
subsection 2(1) of the [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) and in relation
to which an activity is conducted in accordance with the [Cannabis
Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5), is exempt from the application of the [Food and
Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27), unless the cannabis

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is manufactured, sold or represented for use in the
  diagnosis, treatment, mitigation or prevention of a disease, disorder
  or abnormal physical state, or its symptoms, or in restoring or
  correcting organic functions, in human beings or animals;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is represented for use in modifying organic functions
  in human beings or animals;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is manufactured, sold or represented for use in
  disinfection in premises in which food is manufactured, prepared or
  kept;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} is an [[active pharmaceutical
  ingredient]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection
  C.01A.001(1) of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870); or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2025-43, s. 75\]]{.Repealed}

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} is sold to be used for the purpose of a [[clinical
  trial]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section C.05.001 of the
  [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), or an
  [[experimental study]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section
  C.08.013 of those Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cannabis for medical purposes

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Despite paragraph 1(a),
cannabis that is manufactured or sold in accordance with the [Cannabis
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144) is exempt from the
application of the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27) from the time it
is manufactured or sold by any of the following persons, unless it is
represented for a use referred to in that paragraph:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person to which a licence, other than a cannabis
  drug licence, was issued under subsection 62(1) of the [Cannabis
  Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a [[designated person]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as
  defined in Part 14 of the [Cannabis
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-therapeutic research on cannabis

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Despite paragraph 1(f),
cannabis is exempt from the application of the [Food and Drugs
Act](/eng/acts/F-27) if it is sold to be used for the purpose of
[[non-therapeutic research on cannabis]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
defined in subsection 1(2) of the [Cannabis
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144), that is conducted in
accordance with those Regulations.
