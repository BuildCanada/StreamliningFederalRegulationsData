## Racing Products Transfer and Loan Regulations {#racing-products-transfer-and-loan-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/85-730
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1985-08-08

Regulations Respecting the Transfer or Loan of Racing Photographic or
Video Products

P.C. 1985-2417 1985-08-07
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
section 52 of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is
pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the transfer
or loan of racing photographic or video
products](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-730).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887823 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Racing Products Transfer and Loan
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-730).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887825 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement referred to in
    subsection 3(1); ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an association as defined
    in subsection 188(9) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);
    ([association]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who has contracted
    with the Minister to produce a product;
    ([entrepreneur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Director of the Race
    Track Division of the Department of Agriculture and includes any
    person designated by him to act on his behalf for the purposes of
    these Regulations; ([directeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Agriculture;
    ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person appointed by the
    Minister pursuant to subsection 188(3) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46); ([fonctionnaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any photographic material,
    video material or video signal that relates to a horse race and that
    is produced for the Minister by a contractor by means of a
    photo-finish or video camera during the running of the race;
    ([matériel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[product user]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[product user]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a contractor or an
    association. ([utilisateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Transfer or Loan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887835 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to these Regulations, the Director is hereby authorized to transfer or
  loan products to product users and, for such purpose, to enter into an
  agreement in writing with any product user.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An agreement may provide for the transfer or loan of
  products to the product user from time to time in accordance with the
  agreement.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An agreement shall provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the Director may at any time terminate the
    agreement if any product that has been transferred or loaned
    pursuant thereto is used by the product user contrary to the
    condition set out in section 4; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that, where a product user to whom a product has
    been loaned pursuant to the agreement uses the product contrary to
    the condition set out in section 4, the product user shall deliver
    up the product to an officer forthwith after the Director requests
    the product user to do so.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-388, s. 1
:::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887844 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Director shall not
transfer or loan a product pursuant to section 3 except subject to the
condition that the product transferred or loaned will be used only for
viewing or reproduction, or for both viewing and reproduction, for one
or more of the following purposes:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} promoting the horse racing industry;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} compiling personal records for persons who
  participate in horse racing;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} assisting in informing legal telephone account
  bettors about horse races;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} informing legal telephone account bettors about horse
  races in conjunction with the distribution of pre-race or post-race
  betting information by an association; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} providing the public with general information about
  horse racing.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-388, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Director shall not loan
a product to a product user unless the product user has entered into an
agreement that complies with subsection 3(3).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-388, s. 3
:::
