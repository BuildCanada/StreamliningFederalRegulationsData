## Order Designating the Executive Director of the Public Appointments Commission Secretariat as Deputy Head {#order-designating-the-executive-director-of-the-public-appointments-commission-secretariat-as-deputy-head .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-63
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2006-05-03

Order Designating the Executive Director of the Public Appointments
Commission Secretariat as Deputy Head

P.C. 2006-229 2006-04-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 11(2)(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby designates the
Executive Director of the Public Appointments Commission Secretariat as
deputy head in respect of that Secretariat.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2003, c.
  22, s. 8
