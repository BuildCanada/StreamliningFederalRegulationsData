Regulations Prescribing the Fees To Be Paid for the Use of Royal

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955066 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police, Forensic Laboratory
Services Fees Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-480).

## [Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955068 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The fee to be paid by a
person, other than a member of a law enforcement agency, for the use of
Royal Canadian Mounted Police forensic laboratory services is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$110 for each hour of service provided, or portion
  thereof; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to the travel costs of a person
  employed by the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, determined in
  accordance with the Travel Directive, c. VI.1 of the Administration
  Manual of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, where the employee
  travels in order to provide the forensic laboratory services.
