## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725565 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Pest Control Products
      Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an invertebrate or a
      vertebrate, other than a human. ([animal]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[domestic animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[domestic animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an animal that is
      under the control of humans and dependent on them for its
      survival. ([animal domestique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an incident whose effects
      relate to the health or environmental risks or the value of a pest
      control product. It does not include an incident that results from

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an act or activity that constitutes an offence
        under the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46); or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exposure of a targeted subject of a pest
        control product that is manufactured for the purpose of
        controlling such a subject. ([incident]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[incident report]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[incident report]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a report that
      contains the information described in section 3 and that is
      received by the Minister from a registrant or an
      applicant. ([rapport d'incident]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[individual of a species at risk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[individual of a species at
      risk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2019-173, s.
      2\]]{.Repealed}

- Application of definition [[incident report]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of applying the definition [[incident
  report]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection (1), the information need
  not be proved or substantiated by the registrant or applicant in order
  to be included in the report.

## [Classification of Incidents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725576 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incident categories

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Incidents are classified,
according to the subject and effects of the exposure to the pest control
product, into the following categories:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an incident whose effect is a human death;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an incident that has a major effect on a human,
  namely, that human had or has symptoms that indicate a condition that
  could be life-threatening or result in adverse reproductive or
  developmental effects or in chronic disability;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an incident that has an effect on a human other than
  the effects set out in paragraph (a) or (b);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an incident whose effect is an animal death or, if
  the incident occurs in the United States, a domestic animal death;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an incident that has an effect on an animal other
  than death;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} an incident that has an effect on terrestrial or
  aquatic plants;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} an incident of package failure that could result in
  the exposure of a human to a pest control product or in injury to a
  human; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} an incident whose effects are identified in a
  scientific study, namely, that the effects are observed during a human
  epidemiological study or a scientific investigation, whether
  concluded, discontinued or ongoing, and the study or investigation is
  sponsored by the registrant or applicant and indicates

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any new health or environmental hazard associated
    with a pest control product,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any health or environmental risk associated with a
    pest control product that may be greater than the risk determined at
    the time of registration, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the presence of a previously undetected component
    or derivative of a pest control product.

## [Prescribed Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725603 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Section 13 of the Act

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following information that is received by a registrant or an applicant
  about an incident that is associated with their pest control product
  or with any pest control product that has the same active ingredient,
  and that is required by sections 7, 8 and 16, is prescribed
  information for the purpose of section 13 of the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contact information for the registrant or
    applicant;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the incident occurred;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the registrant or applicant
    received the information about the incident;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the city, province or state, and country where the
    incident occurred;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the identification of the pest control product;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} information about the application of the pest
    control product, including the site, method and date of its
    application;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the category of the incident, determined in
    accordance with section 2;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} information about the circumstances of the exposure
    to the pest control product, including the site, date, route of
    exposure, weather conditions, duration and subject information,
    which, in the case of a human subject, is restricted to age, gender
    and whether the subject is pregnant;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} information about the incident and its effects,
    including the symptoms, duration and outcome; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} scientific test information, including information
    about the sample, the method of analysis and the results.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Section 13 of the Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following information about an incident that is
  associated with the registrant's or applicant's pest control product
  or with any pest control product that has the same active ingredient,
  and that is required by section 9, is prescribed information for the
  purpose of section 13 of the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contact information for the registrant or
    applicant;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the title and date of the study and the name of the
    author;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the identification of the pest control product;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the category of the incident, determined in
    accordance with section 2;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the scientific study and the test data generated
    during the study; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the type of scientific study, an indication of
    which of subparagraphs 2(f)(i) to (iii) is the reason for submitting
    it, and the information the study reveals about the incident and its
    effects.

## [General Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725627 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports deemed received

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), all information about incidents and their effects
  that is required by these Regulations is deemed to have been received
  by the registrant or applicant on a certain date if it is received on
  that date by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an employee or agent of the registrant or
    applicant; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an employee or agent of a related corporation,
    within the meaning of the definition [[related
    persons]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 251(2) of the [Income
    Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), whether the related corporation is
    located in or outside Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an incident whose effects are
  identified in a scientific study, if the information is received by a
  person described in paragraph (1)(b), the information is deemed to
  have been received by the registrant or applicant one year after its
  receipt by that person.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Incident

  registrant or applicant must provide the completed translated study as
  soon as feasible after the incident report is provided.

## [Annual Summary]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725714 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of annual summary

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  registrant must provide the Minister with an annual summary in respect
  of an active ingredient if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the active ingredient is a component of a pest
    control product that is the subject of an incident about which the
    registrant received information during the year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the active ingredient is implicated in at least 10
    incidents of the type set out in paragraph (a), each of which
    relates to the same type of subject of exposure referred to in
    section 2, namely, a human, an animal or one set out in paragraphs
    2(f) to (h).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the term
  [[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a calendar year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The annual summary must contain the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of each incident referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b), the category of incident set out in section 2; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a concise critical analysis of all the data
    relating to the active ingredient, including a comparison with
    analyses of past years and commentary on any changes that arose
    during the year in the risk profile of any pest control product of
    which the active ingredient is a component.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The annual summary must be provided by March 31 of
  the year that follows the year during which the registrant receives
  information about the incidents specified in subsection (1).

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725729 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Keep and submit at Minister's request

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Every registrant and
applicant must keep a record of every incident report and any
information they have that relates to that report for six years after
the day on which the report is provided to the Minister. The record must
be provided to the Minister, on request, for the purpose of making
comparisons or a historical analysis of the incidents or to permit the
Minister to exercise powers under the Act.

## [Placement in Register]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725732 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incident reports and supplemental
information

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  Minister must place all incident reports that relate to registered
  pest control products in the Register, including any supplemental
  information in support of the reports that is volunteered by the
  registrant or applicant, such as any relevant opinion or commentary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must remove from the incident reports,
  and from any supplemental information provided in connection with
  them, all personal information as defined in section 3 of the [Privacy
  Act](/eng/acts/P-21).
