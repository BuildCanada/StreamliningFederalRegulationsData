## Minister Designation Order (Precious Metals Marking Act) {#minister-designation-order-precious-metals-marking-act .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1302
:::

[PRECIOUS METALS MARKING ACT](/eng/acts/P-19)

Order Designating the Minister for the Purposes of the Precious Metals
Marking Act
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502914 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Minister Designation Order (Precious Metals Marking
Act)](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1302).

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502916 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Consumer
and Corporate Affairs, a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada,
is hereby designated as the Minister for the purposes of the [Precious
Metals Marking Act](/eng/acts/P-19).
