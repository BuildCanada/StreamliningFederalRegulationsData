## Statistics Canada Census and Survey Related Term Employment Regulations {#statistics-canada-census-and-survey-related-term-employment-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2010-147

Statistics Canada Census and Survey Related Term Employment Regulations

P.C. 2010-772 2010-06-17

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Canadian Heritage and the Public Service Commission,
pursuant to section 21 of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-757909 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Service Employment
    Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[acting appointment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[acting appointment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the temporary
    performance of the duties of another position by a person, if the
    performance of those duties would have constituted a promotion had
    the person been appointed other than on an acting basis to the
    position. ([nomination intérimaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[deployment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deployment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the transfer under section
    6 of a person, an employee not excluded by the Order or an
    individual employed by a separate agency to a position.
    ([mutation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[internal appointment process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[internal appointment process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a process
    for making one or more appointments in which only persons may be
    considered. ([processus de nomination interne]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Statistics Canada Census
    and Survey Related Term Employment Exclusion Approval
    Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2010-81) ([décret]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person excluded by the Order
    who is appointed or deployed to a position.
    ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[position]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[position]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a position excluded by the
    Order. ([poste]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-757920 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
those positions in Statistics Canada that are excluded from the
application of the definitions [[deployment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
[[internal appointment process]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection
2(1) and of paragraphs 22(2)(a) to (c) and sections 39.1 to 41, 48, 51
to 53, 57, 59 and 62 of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) and to the persons appointed or deployed to
those positions on a specified term basis, except for those persons
mentioned in subsection 5(1) of the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19),
whose function is solely to provide administrative or management support
or to perform data processing duties for the purpose of conducting

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the population census and the agriculture census as
  described in the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19), or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the national survey, made pursuant to sections 7 and
  8 of that Act when it replaces the population census long-form
  questionnaire and is conducted in conjunction with the population
  census.

## [Dealing with Excluded Positions and Persons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-757926 .Part}

### [Appointment or Deployment on a Specified Term Basis]{.HTitleText2} {#h-757927 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Appointments or deployments
to positions may be on a specified term basis only.

### [Internal Appointment Process]{.HTitleText2} {#h-757929 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A person is not eligible
for an internal appointment process other than for an acting
appointment.

### [Acting Appointments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-757931 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission must, at the time that the following acting appointments
  are made or proposed as a result of an internal appointment process,
  inform the persons in the area of recourse, within the meaning of
  subsection 77(2) of the Act, in writing of the name of the person who
  is proposed to be, or has been, appointed and of their right and
  grounds to make a complaint:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an acting appointment of four months or more;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an acting appointment that extends the person's
    cumulative period in the acting appointment from less than four
    months to four months or more.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An acting appointment of less than four months, if it
  does not extend the cumulative period of the acting appointment of a
  person in a position to four months or more, is excluded from the
  application of sections 30 and 77 of the Act.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), the provision of paragraph
  30(2)(a) of the Act respecting official language proficiency continues
  to apply, in the case of an acting appointment of less than four
  months, to a vacant bilingual position if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Commission is able to fill the position with an
    appointment of a person or of an employee not excluded by the Order
    who meets the official language proficiency qualification; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cumulative period of the acting appointments of
    all persons and the acting appointments within the meaning of the
    [Public Service Employment
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-334) of all employees not
    excluded by the Order in that position is four months or more.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} An acting appointment of four months or more but not
  more than twelve months to an encumbered bilingual position that the
  Commission cannot fill with an acting appointment of a person or an
  acting appointment within the meaning of the [Public Service
  Employment Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-334) of an employee
  not excluded by the Order who meets the language proficiency
  qualification under paragraph 30(2)(a) of the Act is excluded from the
  application of that paragraph respecting official language
  proficiency.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (4) does not apply to an acting
  appointment to the same position if the cumulative period of the
  acting appointments of all persons and the acting appointments within
  the meaning of the [Public Service Employment
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-334) of all employees not
  excluded by the Order in that position is more than twelve months.

### [Deployment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-757942 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Statistician may deploy to a position a person, an employee not
  excluded by the Order or an individual employed by a separate agency
  from which deployments have been approved under subsection 51(2) of
  the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A deployment may be made within an occupational group
  or, unless deployment within the meaning of any regulations made under
  paragraph 26(1)(a) of the Act is excluded under those regulations,
  between occupational groups.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A deployment made to a position is to be made
  following the same rules as are established in any regulations
  regarding deployment, within the meaning of subsection 2(1) of the
  Act, made by the Treasury Board.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A deployment must not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} constitute a promotion within the meaning of any
    regulations made by the Treasury Board; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} change a period of employment from specified term
    to indeterminate.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A deployment may be made without consent of a person
  if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} agreement to being deployed is a condition of
    employment of their current position; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person has in the course of their employment
    harassed another person or an employee not excluded by the Order and
    the deployment is made to another position.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} On deployment, a person ceases to be the incumbent of
  the position to which they had previously been appointed or deployed.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} A deployment is not an appointment for the
  application of the Act.

### [Probationary Period -- Termination of Employment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-757955 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} While a
  person is on probation, the Chief Statistician may notify the person
  in writing that their employment will be terminated

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} seven days after the day on which the notice is
    received and that the person ceases to be an employee at the end of
    the notice period, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the date specified in the notice, that they will
    be paid an amount equal to the salary they would have been paid if
    they had been given notice that their employment will be terminated
    seven days after the day on which the notice is received and that
    the person ceases to be an employee on the date specified in the
    notice.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of paragraph (1)(a), the person ceases to
  be an employee at the end of the period specified in the notice.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of paragraph (1)(b), the person ceases to
  be an employee on the date specified in the notice and the person is
  paid an amount equal to the salary they would have been paid if they
  had been given notice that their employment will be terminated seven
  days after the day on which the notice is received.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-757962 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
