## Off-Road Small Spark-Ignition Engine Emission Regulations {#off-road-small-spark-ignition-engine-emission-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-355
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 2003-11-06

Off-Road Small Spark-Ignition Engine Emission Regulations

P.C. 2003- 1774 2003-11-06
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 332(1) of the [Canadian Environmental
Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBBA}, the Minister
of the Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on March 29, 2003, a copy
of the proposed Off-Road Small Spark-Ignition Engine Emission
Regulations, substantially in the annexed form, and persons were given
an opportunity to file comments with respect to the Regulations or a
notice of objection requesting that a board of review be established and
stating the reasons for the objection;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFBBA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1999, c.
  33
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to section
160 of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)^a^, hereby makes the annexed Off-Road Small
Spark-Ignition Engine Emission Regulations.
:::

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706155 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of these
Regulations is to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} reduce emissions of hydrocarbons, oxides of nitrogen
  and carbon monoxide from engines by establishing emission limits for
  those substances or combinations of those substances;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} reduce emissions of the toxic substances
  formaldehyde, 1,3 butadiene, acetaldehyde, acrolein and benzene
  through the establishment of emission limits for hydrocarbons from
  engines; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} establish emission standards and test procedures for
  engines that are aligned with those of the EPA.

## [Background]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706160 .Part}

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations set
out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribed engines for the purposes of section 149 of
  the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} requirements respecting the conformity of prescribed
  engines with emission-related standards for the purposes of sections
  153 and 154 of the Act; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} other requirements for carrying out the purposes of
  Division 5 of Part 7 of the Act.

## [Model Year]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706169 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A year
  that is used by a manufacturer of an engine as a model year shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the period of production of a model of engine
    does not include January 1 of a calendar year, correspond to the
    calendar year during which the period of production falls or the
    calendar year following that calendar year, at the manufacturer's
    choice; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the period of production of a model of engine
    includes January 1 of a calendar year, correspond to that calendar
    year.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The period of production of a model of engine shall
  include only one January 1.

## [Prescribed Engines]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706177 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Off-road
  engines, including those of the 2019 and later model years that have a
  complete fuel system, are prescribed for the purposes of the
  definition [[engine]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 149 of the Act
  if they

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} operate under characteristics significantly similar
    to the theoretical Otto combustion cycle;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} use a spark plug or other sparking device;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} develop no more than 30 kW of power measured at the
    crankshaft, or its equivalent, when equipped only with standard
    accessories --- such as oil pumps or coolant pumps --- necessary for
    their operation; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} have a displacement of 1 000 cm^3^ or less.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, an engine
  kit is considered to be an engine that has a complete fuel system.

- [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2020-258, s. 58\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The engines referred to in subsection (1) do not
  include an engine that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} designed exclusively for competition, namely one
    that has the following characteristics, and bears a label that meets
    the requirements of subsections 17.2(3) and (4) and indicates that
    the engine is a competition engine:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its performance characteristics are substantially
      superior to non-competition engines, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it is not displayed for sale in any public
      dealership or otherwise offered for sale to the general public;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} regulated by the On-Road Vehicle and Engine
    Emission Regulations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} regulated by the Marine Spark-Ignition Engine,
    Vessel and Off-Road Recreational Vehicle Emission Regulations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} designed to be used in reduced-scale models of
    vehicles that are not capable of transporting a person;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} designed to be used exclusively in emergency and
    rescue machines and that bears either a label to that effect and
    that meets the requirements set out in subsections 17.2(3) and (4)
    or the U.S. label referred to in paragraph 660(c) of subpart G of
    CFR 1054;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} designed to be used exclusively in military
    machines that are used only in combat or combat support during
    military activities, including reconnaissance missions, rescue
    missions and training missions and that bears either a label to that
    effect and that meets the requirements set out in subsections
    17.2(3) and (4) or the U.S. emission control information label
    referred to in paragraph 225(e) of subpart C of CFR 1068;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} being exported and that is accompanied by a written
    statement establishing that it will not be used or sold for use in
    Canada;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in conformity with the requirements of the Off-road
    Compression-Ignition (Mobile and Stationary) and Large
    Spark-Ignition Engine Emission Regulations and has a displacement of
    1 000 cm^3^ or less and a gross engine power of more than 19 kW but
    less than or equal to 30 kW; or

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in conformity with the requirements of the Off-road
    Compression-Ignition (Mobile and Stationary) and Large
    Spark-Ignition Engine Emission Regulations as if it were a [[large
    spark-ignition engine]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
    subsection 1(1) of those Regulations, but that has a gross engine
    power of 19 kW or less.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of section 152 of the Act, the
  prescribed engines are those prescribed under subsection (1) that are
  manufactured in Canada, except any engine that will be used in Canada
  solely for purposes of exhibition, demonstration, evaluation or
  testing.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the classes of engines that
  are prescribed for the purposes of section 154 of the Act are those
  that are prescribed under subsection (1).

## [Application for Authorization to Apply the National Emissions Mark]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706199 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  company that intends to apply a national emissions mark in relation to
  an engine shall apply to the Minister to obtain an authorization.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application shall be signed by a person who is
  authorized to act on behalf of the company and shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and street address of the head office of
    the company and, if different, its mailing address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the company is seeking to obtain
    the authorization to apply the national emissions mark under these
    Regulations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the street address of the location at which the
    national emissions mark will be applied; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} information to show that the company is capable of
    verifying compliance with the standards set out in these
    Regulations.

### [Emission Control Systems]{.HTitleText2} {#h-706213 .Subheading}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  emission control system that is installed on an engine to enable it to
  conform to the standards set out in these Regulations shall not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in its operation, release a substance that causes
    air pollution and that would not have been released if the system
    were not installed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in its operation or malfunction, make the engine or
    the machine in which the engine is installed unsafe, or endanger
    persons or property near the engine or machine.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An engine must not be equipped with an auxiliary
  emission control device that reduces the effectiveness of the emission
  control system under conditions that may reasonably be expected to be
  encountered under normal operation of the engine, unless a description
  of the auxiliary emission control device is included in the evidence
  of conformity under section 16 or 17 and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conditions under which the auxiliary emission
    control device operates are substantially included in the test
    procedures referred to in section 13.1;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the auxiliary emission control device is needed to
    protect the engine against damage or accident; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the auxiliary emission control device is only used
    to start the engine.

### [Maintenance, Retention and Submission of Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-706428 .Subheading}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company shall maintain a record in writing, or in a readily readable
  electronic or optical form, of the following information and retain
  the record for the following periods:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any declaration set out in section 19,
    for a period of eight years after the year of the importation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the evidence of conformity set out in sections 16
    or 17, as the case may be, for a period of eight years after the
    date of manufacture of the engine;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a company that imported less than 50 engines
    during a given calendar year, the number of engines imported, for a
    period of eight years after the calendar year in question; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, a copy of the declaration set out in
    section 20, and information demonstrating that the company has
    disposed of the engine in accordance with that declaration, for a
    period of eight years after the day of the disposal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the record is retained by another person on a
  company's behalf, the company shall keep a record of that other
  person's name, telephone number and civic address and, if different,
  their mailing address.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister makes a written request for a record
  referred to in subsection (1) or (2), the company shall submit it to
  the Minister in either official language

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 40 days after the day on which the request
    is made to the company; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 60 days after the day on which the request
    is made to the company, if the record is to be translated from a
    language other than French or English.

### [Information Regarding Suspension or Revocation of EPA Certificate]{.HTitleText2} {#h-706441 .Subheading}

  revoked;

    model and type of machine; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a suspended or revoked EPA certificate
  covering fuel lines, fuel tanks, or complete fuel systems, installed
  in machines, the make, model and type of each machine.

## [Rental Rate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706489 .Part}

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The annual rental rate to
be paid to a company by the Minister under subsection 159(1) of the Act,

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706491 .Part}

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A company applying under
section 156 of the Act for an exemption from conformity with any
standard prescribed under these Regulations shall, before the
importation or manufacture of an engine, submit in writing to the
Minister

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} its name and street address and, if different,
  mailing address;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the province or country under the laws of which it is
  established;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the section number, title and text of the standards
  from which an exemption is sought;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the duration requested for the exemption;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the estimated number of engines for which the
  exemption is sought and an estimate of the changes in the level of
  emissions if the exemption is granted;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the reason for requesting the exemption, including
  technical and financial information that demonstrates in detail why
  conformity to the standards referred to in paragraph (c) would

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} create substantial financial hardship for the
    company,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} impede the development of new features for
    emission monitoring or emission control that are equivalent or
    superior to those that conform to prescribed standards, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} impede the development of new kinds of engines or
    engine systems or components;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the basis of the application is substantial
  financial hardship,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the world production of engines manufactured by the
    company or by the manufacturer that is the subject of the
    application in the 12-month period beginning two years before the
    start of the exemption period being sought, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the total number of engines manufactured for, or
    imported into, the Canadian market in the 12-month period beginning
    two years before the start of the exemption period being sought; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the company is requesting that information
  submitted be treated as confidential under section 313 of the Act or
  otherwise, the reasons for the request.

## [Defect Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706514 .Part}

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  notice of defect referred to in subsections 157(1) and (4) of the Act
  shall contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the company giving the notice and its
    street address and, if different, mailing address, and the name,
    email address and phone number of the contact person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each engine in respect of which the notice is
    given, its make, model, model year and the period during which the
    engine was manufactured, any applicable emission family and the
    range or ranges of unique identification numbers, if known;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total number of engines in respect of which the
    notice is given or, if the total number is not known, the estimated
    number;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the machine or type of machine in
    or on which the engine is installed or is likely to be installed;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the estimated percentage of the potentially
    affected engines that contain the defect;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the defect;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} an evaluation of the pollution risk arising from
    the defect;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the measures to be taken to correct
    the defect;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a chronology of the principal events that led to
    the determination of the existence of the defect, if known; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a description of the means available to the company
    to contact the current owner of each affected engine.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of defect shall be given in writing and,
  in the case of notices given to a person other than the Minister,
  shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in both official languages; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the person's official language of choice, if it
    is known.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company shall, within 60 days after giving a notice
  of defect, submit to the Minister the initial report referred to in
  subsection 157(7) of the Act containing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an update of the information required by
    subsection (1) if there has been any change to the information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if not already provided in the notice,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the range or ranges of unique identification
      numbers,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the total number of engines in respect of which
      the notice of defect was given, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a chronology of the principal events that led
      to the determination of the existence of the defect; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} copies of all notices, bulletins and other
    circulars issued by the company in respect of the defect, including
    a detailed description of the nature and physical location of the
    defect with diagrams and other illustrations as necessary.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a company submits an initial report, it shall
  submit a quarterly report, within 45 days after the end of each of the
  following six quarters, to the Minister respecting the defect and its
  correction containing the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number, title or other identification assigned
    by the company to the notice of defect;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the revised number of engines in
    respect of which the notice of defect was given;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the notice of defect was given to
    the current owners of the affected engines and the dates of any
    follow-up communications with those owners in respect of the notice;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total number, or percentage, of engines
    repaired by or on behalf of the company, including engines requiring
    inspection only.
