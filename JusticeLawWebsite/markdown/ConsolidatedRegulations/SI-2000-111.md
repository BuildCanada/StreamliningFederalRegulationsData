## Order Authorizing the Minister of Foreign Affairs to Prescribe the Fee {#order-authorizing-the-minister-of-foreign-affairs-to-prescribe-the-fee .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2000-111
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2000-12-20

Order Authorizing the Minister of Foreign Affairs to Prescribe the Fee

P.C. 2000-1723 2000-11-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Foreign Affairs and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
paragraph 19(1)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_5496_hq_19986){#fn_5496_hq_19986-ID0EDBAA} of
the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby authorizes
the Minister of Foreign Affairs to prescribe, by order, the fee to be
paid by foreign nationals to participate in an international youth
exchange program in Canada.
