## Labelle Area Wood Producers' Levy (Interprovincial and Export Trade) Order {#labelle-area-wood-producers-levy-interprovincial-and-export-trade-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-663
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1987-11-20

Order Respecting the Fixing and Imposing of Levies on, and the
Collecting of Levies From, Producers of Wood in the Labelle Area in the
Province of Quebec, With Respect to the Marketing of Wood in
Interprovincial and Export Trade
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Wood Producers' Syndicate of the County of Labelle, pursuant to
section 4 of the [Quebec Wood Order, 1983](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-713),
made by Order in Council P.C. 1983-2885 of September 22, 1983[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBBA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Order respecting the fixing and imposing of levies on, and
the collecting of levies from, producers of wood in the Labelle area in
the Province of Quebec, with respect to the marketing of wood in
interprovincial and export trade](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-663).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EDBBA){#footnote1_e}SOR/83-713,
  1983 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3444
  :::

Saint-Eustache, Quebec, November 17, 1987
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910298 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Labelle Area Wood Producers' Levy (Interprovincial and Export
Trade) Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-663).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910300 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production or marketing of wood; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Syndicate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Syndicate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Wood Producers'
    Syndicate of the County of Labelle; ([Syndicat]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[wood]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wood]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means wood produced in the municipal
    county of Labelle in the Province of Quebec.
    ([bois]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910305 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A producer shall pay to the
Syndicate in respect of the marketing of wood in interprovincial and
export trade a general administration levy calculated at one of the
following rates:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in tonnes, \$0.56 per
  tonne;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in apparent cubic metres,
  \$0.35 per apparent cubic metre;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in solid cubic metres,
  \$0.53 per solid cubic metre;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in cords of 128 apparent
  cubic feet (4 ft. × 4 ft. × 8 ft.), \$1.27 per cord;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in cords of 160 apparent
  cubic feet (5 ft. × 4 ft. × 8 ft.), \$1.59 per cord;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in cords of 192 apparent
  cubic feet (6 ft. × 4 ft. × 8 ft.), \$1.90 per cord;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in cords of 224 apparent
  cubic feet (7 ft. × 4 ft. × 8 ft.), \$2.22 per cord;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in cords of 256 apparent
  cubic feet (8 ft. × 4 ft. × 8 ft.), \$2.54 per cord;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in cubic feet, \$1.50 per
  100 cubic feet;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured by board measure, \$2.54
  per 1,000 board feet;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in short tons, \$0.50 per
  short ton;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in units of 1,000 pounds,
  \$0.25 per unit;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in firewood units (4 ft. ×
  8 ft. × 16 in.), \$0.42 per unit;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is sold by the piece, 4.6 per cent of
  the sale price to the mill.

## [Method of Collection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910321 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), a producer shall pay any levy payable by
  the producer pursuant to section 3 to the Syndicate at its
  headquarters in Saint-Eustache, Quebec, within 30 days after the
  marketing of the wood in respect of which the levy is payable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Syndicate markets wood in interprovincial
  or export trade on behalf of a producer, the Syndicate may deduct from
  any money payable to the producer by the Syndicate any levy payable by
  the producer, pursuant to section 3, in respect of the marketing of
  that wood.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a producer does not pay the levy set out in
  section 3 to the Syndicate pursuant to subsection (1) and where the
  Syndicate has not deducted the levy pursuant to subsection (2), any
  person who markets that producer's wood in interprovincial or export
  trade shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deduct from any money payable to the producer by
    that person any levy payable by the producer to the Syndicate
    pursuant to section 3; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 30 days after the marketing of the wood in
    respect of which the levy is payable, pay the levy to the Syndicate
    and provide the Syndicate with an accounting in respect of the
    payment.
