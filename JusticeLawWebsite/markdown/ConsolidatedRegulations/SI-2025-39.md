## Order Transferring from the Minister of Foreign Affairs to the Minister for International Trade the Powers, Duties and Functions Under Subsection 53(2) of the Customs Tariff {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-foreign-affairs-to-the-minister-for-international-trade-the-powers-duties-and-functions-under-subsection-532-of-the-customs-tariff .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-39
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2025-03-26

Order Transferring from the Minister of Foreign Affairs to the Minister
for International Trade the Powers, Duties and Functions Under
Subsection 53(2) of the Customs Tariff

P.C. 2025-419 2025-03-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EJEAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EGEAA},
transfers from the Minister of Foreign Affairs to the Minister for
International Trade the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of
Foreign Affairs under subsection 53(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2839_hq_1741793772){#fn_81000-3-2839_hq_1741793772-ID0EEEAA}
of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-3-2839_hq_1741720736){#fn_81000-3-2839_hq_1741720736-ID0EBEAA}.
