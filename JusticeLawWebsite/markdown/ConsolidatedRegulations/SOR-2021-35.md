## Canada Recovery Benefits Regulations {#canada-recovery-benefits-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2021-35
:::

[CANADA RECOVERY BENEFITS ACT](/eng/acts/C-10.10)

Registration 2021-03-15

Canada Recovery Benefits Regulations

P.C. 2021-151 2021-03-15
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Administrator of the Government of Canada in Council,
on the recommendation of the Minister of Employment and Social
Development, pursuant to subsections 9(2), 16(2) and 23(4) of the
[Canada Recovery Benefits Act](/eng/acts/C-10.10)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4XXX_hq_28630){#fn_81000-2-4XXX_hq_28630-ID0EDBAA},
makes the annexed [Canada Recovery Benefits
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-35).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Recovery Benefits
Act](/eng/acts/C-10.10).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canada Recovery Benefit

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 9(1) of the Act, the maximum number of two-week periods in
respect of which a Canada recovery benefit is payable to a person is 27.
