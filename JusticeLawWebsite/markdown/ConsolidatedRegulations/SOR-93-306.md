## Designated Employees Pension Protection Regulations {#designated-employees-pension-protection-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-306
:::

[AIRPORT TRANSFER (MISCELLANEOUS MATTERS) ACT](/eng/acts/A-10.4)

Registration 1993-06-08

Regulations Respecting Pension Protection for Designated Employees

P.C. 1993-1216 1993-06-08
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the President of the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsections 5(3) and
(4) of the Airport Transfer (Miscellaneous Matters) Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed Regulations respecting pension protection for
designated employees.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952976 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Designated Employees Pension Protection
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-306).

## [Applicable Enactments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952978 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 and
4, the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and the
regulations made under that Act apply to a person who makes an election
under paragraph 5(1)(e) of the [Airport Transfer (Miscellaneous Matters)
Act](/eng/acts/A-10.4).

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subsections 12(4), (6) and
(7) and 13(2) and (3) of the [Public Service Superannuation
Act](/eng/acts/P-36) do not apply in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surviving spouse of a designated employee for an
  airport who became the spouse of that employee on or after the
  transfer date for that airport, unless the designated employee had
  again become a contributor under the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a child of a designated employee for an airport who
  was conceived by, adopted by, or became the stepchild of, a designated
  employee on or after the transfer date for the airport, unless the
  designated employee had again become a contributor under the [Public
  Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

## [Adapted Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952983 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
5 of the [Airport Transfer (Miscellaneous Matters)
Act](/eng/acts/A-10.4),

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in subparagraphs 10(9)(b)(i) and (ii) of the [Public
  Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the year in which a
  person ceased to be employed in the Public Service is the year in
  which the person ceased to be employed by a designated airport
  authority;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in subsections 12(1) to (3) and (6) to (8) and
  section 13 of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36),

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of years of pensionable service and the
    number of years of service of a designated employee for an airport
    includes any period of service with a designated airport authority
    during the period beginning on the transfer date for the airport and
    ending on the day preceding the day on which the person ceases to be
    employed by the designated airport authority, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the day on which a person ceases to be employed in
    the Public Service is the day on which that person ceases to be
    employed by a designated airport authority; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in subsection 69(3) of the [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the retirement year or retirement
  month of a person who is a designated employee for an airport is the
  year or month, as the case may be, in which the transfer date for the
  airport occurs.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952990 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations shall be
deemed to have come into force on July 1, 1992.
