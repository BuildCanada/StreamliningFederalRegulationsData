## Consolidated Computer Inc. Adjustment Assistance Regulations {#consolidated-computer-inc.-adjustment-assistance-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 967
:::

[DEPARTMENT OF INDUSTRY ACT](/eng/acts/I-9.2)

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

Regulations Respecting Adjustment Assistance to Consolidated Computer
Inc.
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-600115 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Consolidated Computer Inc. Adjustment Assistance
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._967).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-600117 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations, all
words and expressions have the same meaning as in the [General
Adjustment Assistance Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._971).

## [Special Loans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-600119 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection 14(8) of
  the [General Adjustment Assistance
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._971), the Board may, under
  subsection 14(4) of those Regulations, provide insurance in an amount
  not exceeding 99 per cent of the amount of loans, not exceeding \$30
  million in the aggregate, made by private lenders for the purpose of
  facilitating the leasing of computer products manufactured by
  Consolidated Computer Inc., a corporation incorporated under the laws
  of the Province of Ontario and having its head office in the City of
  Toronto in that Province.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding section 17 of the [General Adjustment
  Assistance Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._971), where a
  private lender had demanded repayment of a loan described in
  subsection (1) in respect of which insurance has been provided under
  subsection 14(8) of the said Regulations, prior to the maturity of
  that loan, the amount payable by Her Majesty to the private lender
  shall not exceed the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of insurance in effect on the date of
    such demand: and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 99 per cent of the loss incurred by the private
    lender.
