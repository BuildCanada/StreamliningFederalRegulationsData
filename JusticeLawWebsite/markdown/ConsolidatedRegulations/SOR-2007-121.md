**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Proceeds of Crime (Money
    Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501).
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[financial entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[financial entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 1(2) of the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and
    Terrorist Financing
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-184). ([entité
    financière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[foreign money services business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foreign money services business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    person or entity referred to in paragraph 5(h.1) of the
    Act. ([entreprise de services monétaires étrangère]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[money services business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[money services business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person or
    entity referred to in paragraph 5(h) of the Act. ([entreprise de
    services monétaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prescribed Entities Not Eligible for Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-732231 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  corporation is not eligible for registration with the Centre if its
  chief executive officer, its president or one of its directors or a
  person or entity that owns or controls, directly or indirectly, 20% or
  more of the shares of the corporation is referred to in any of
  paragraphs 11.11(1)(a) to (d), (e.1) and (f) of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An entity that is not a corporation is not eligible
  for registration with the Centre if its chief executive officer, its
  president or one of its directors or a person or entity that owns or
  controls, directly or indirectly, 20% or more of the entity is
  referred to in any of paragraphs 11.11(1)(a) to (d), (e.1) and (f) of
  the Act.

## [Applications, Notifications, Clarifications and Supplementary Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-732235 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An applicant or a
registered person or entity must submit the following information to the
Centre, in accordance with guidelines that are prepared by the Centre,
electronically if they have the technical capability to do so or by
telephone if they do not:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information contained in an application for
  registration referred to in section 11.12 of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a notification, referred to in section 11.13 of the
  Act, of a change to the information contained in an application or of
  newly obtained information;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2019-240, s. 85\]]{.Repealed}

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a clarification requested by the Centre under
  section 11.14 or 11.17 of the Act;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the information contained in an application for the
  renewal of registration referred to in section 11.19 of the Act; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a notification of the cessation of an activity
  referred to in section 11.2 of the Act.

## [Renewal of Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-732245 .Part}

**[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 11.19 of the Act, when a person or entity that is referred to in
paragraph 5(h) of the Act and that is engaged in the business of
providing the service referred to in subparagraph 5(h)(iv) of the Act,
or a person or entity that is referred to in paragraph 5(h.1) of the
Act, renews their registration for the first time, they must do so no
later than

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person, the last day of the month of
  the person's first birthday that occurs after the second anniversary
  of their initial registration;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, the last day of the
  month of the first anniversary of its incorporation that occurs after
  the second anniversary of its initial registration; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an entity other than a corporation,
  the day after the second anniversary of its initial registration.
