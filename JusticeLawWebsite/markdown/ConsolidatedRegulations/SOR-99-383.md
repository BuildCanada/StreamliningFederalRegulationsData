## [Determination of Interest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031949 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subparagraph 26.03(1)(d)(ii) of the [Canada Deposit Insurance
Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3), the interest payable in relation to a
deposit shall be determined in the following manner:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the deposit contract specifies that the interest
  payable is to be determined by reference to an index or reference
  point referred to in subsection 14(2.51) of the Act, in accordance
  with the Interest Payable on Certain Deposits By-law, except that the
  interest termination date referred to in that By-law is deemed to be
  the date on which payment is made under subparagraph 26.03(1)(d)(ii)
  of the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, in accordance with the provisions
  of the deposit contract, except that the maturity date of the deposit
  is deemed to be the date on which payment is made under that
  subparagraph.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031953 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This By-law comes into
force on October 15, 1999.
