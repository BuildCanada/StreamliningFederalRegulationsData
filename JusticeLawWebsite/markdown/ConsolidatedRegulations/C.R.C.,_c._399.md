## Children of Deceased Veterans Education Assistance Regulations {#children-of-deceased-veterans-education-assistance-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 399
:::

[CHILDREN OF DECEASED VETERANS EDUCATION ASSISTANCE ACT](/eng/acts/C-28)

Regulations Respecting Education Assistance to Children of Deceased
Veterans
::::

:::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-555240 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Children of Deceased Veterans Education Assistance
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._399).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-310, s. 2
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-555244 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Children of Deceased Veterans
Education Assistance Act](/eng/acts/C-28).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-171, s. 1
:::

## [Allowances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-555248 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An allowance under the Act
to or in respect of a student shall be computed and paid on a monthly
basis during the academic year of the educational institution in which
the student is taking his course of education or instruction, up to the
end of the week during which the student's final examination for the
academic year is held.

## [Annual Adjustment of Allowances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-555250 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When the
  monthly allowance specified in paragraphs 4(1)(a) and (b) of the Act
  is adjusted annually pursuant to section 9 of the Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the product obtained by multiplying the amount
    referred to in paragraph (1)(a) of that section by the ratio
    referred to in paragraph (1)(b) of that section shall be adjusted to
    the nearest cent in accordance with subsection (2) of this section;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quotient obtained from the ratio referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b) of that section shall be expressed as a decimal
    fraction in accordance with subsection (3) of this section.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the product referred to in paragraph (1)(a)
  contains a fractional part of a dollar represented by three or more
  digits,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the third and subsequent digits shall be dropped if
    the third digit is less than five; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the second digit shall be increased by one and the
    third and subsequent digits shall be dropped if the third digit is
    five or greater than five.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the quotient referred to in paragraph (1)(b)
  contains a fraction that is less than one, that fraction shall be
  expressed as a decimal fraction of four digits after the decimal point
  and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the third digit after the decimal point shall
    remain unchanged and the fourth digit after the decimal point shall
    be dropped if the fourth digit after the decimal point is less than
    five; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the third digit after the decimal point shall be
    increased by one and the fourth digit after the decimal point shall
    be dropped if the fourth digit after the decimal point is five or
    greater than five.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-310, s. 3
:::

## [Costs of Education]{.HTitleText1} {#h-555263 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  costs of education or instruction that may be paid in respect of a
  student under the Act to an educational institution in which the
  student is taking his course of education or instruction include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} tuition fees;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} instrument rental fees;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} library fees;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} registration fees;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} regular examination fees; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} student activity fees and similar fees as specified
    in educational institution calendars.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Costs that may not be paid on behalf of a student
  under the Act include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fees for supplemental examinations or examinations
    for repeated courses;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fees for registration in professional organizations
    such as law societies, medical and dental associations and
    engineering societies; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} refundable caution money or deposits.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The costs of education or instruction payable under
  the Act in respect of a student

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall not exceed \$4,198.18 for any 12-month
    period; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may be paid at such times and subject to such
    conditions in respect of the student as the Minister may arrange
    with the educational institution.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-807, s. 1
- SOR/2005-171, s. 2
:::

## [Annual Adjustment of Maximum Costs of Education and Instruction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-555281 .Part}

**[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** The amount referred to
in paragraph 5(3)(a) shall be adjusted, on the first day of each
calendar year, in the same manner as the basic monthly amount of an
allowance is adjusted under section 9 of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-171, s. 3
:::

## [Allowance May Be Terminated]{.HTitleText1} {#h-555285 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Any allowance authorized
under the Act to or in respect of a student may be terminated

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the event of the failure of the student to comply
  with the rules and regulations of the educational institution in which
  the student is taking his course of education or instruction; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the event that the progress or standard of
  achievement of the student is considered unsatisfactory by the head of
  the educational institution and the Minister.

## [Counselling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-555289 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Department shall, as
directed by the Minister, provide counselling and guidance to assist
potential students and their parents or guardians in planning and
implementing appropriate educational programs.
