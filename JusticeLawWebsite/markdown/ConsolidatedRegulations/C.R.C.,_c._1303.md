## Precious Metals Marking Regulations {#precious-metals-marking-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1303
:::

[PRECIOUS METALS MARKING ACT](/eng/acts/P-19)

Regulations Respecting the Marking of Articles Containing Precious
Metals
::::

:::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502925 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Precious Metals Marking
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1303).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502927 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Precious Metals Marking
Act](/eng/acts/P-19).

## [Exemption from Application of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502929 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every article sold to a
dealer who is outside Canada is exempt from the application of the Act.

## [Parts Exempt from Assay]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502931 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The parts of an article
that are exempt from assay for the purposes of the Act and these
Regulations are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any mechanism, movement or works, where the article
  consists of a case or cover containing or incorporating the mechanism,
  movement or works;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} brooch pins, brooch joints and brooch catches on

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} gold or silver insignia pins, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any article to which a quality mark specified in
    Column I of item 1, 2 or 4 of the table to section 7 may be applied;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the article is an article to which a quality
  mark specified in Column I of item 1 or 2 of the tables to sections 6,
  8 and 9 or in Column I of item 1, 2 or 4 of the table to section 7 may
  be applied, the following parts thereof:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} springs, winding bars, sleeves, crown cores, joint
    pins, screws, rivets, dust bands, movement rings and spring pins,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} hat pin stems and sockets,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} scarf pin, stick pin and collar tack clutchbacks,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} bracelet tongues,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} locket bezels,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} knife skeletons and blades,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} lapel button backs and the posts to which they
    are attached,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} earring setting posts, and

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} tubes and posts for attachment to drilled stones
    together with any attached collet or claw setting; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the article is a spectacle frame to which a
  quality mark specified in the table to section 10 may be applied, the
  following parts thereof:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} pad posts,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} cores and inner winding of comfort cable temples,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any part permanently encased in a non-metallic
    covering, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} hinge assemblies.

- SOR/82-251, s. 1

## [Marks]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502954 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections 10(1), 11(6) and 12(1), a quality mark may be applied
  to a precious metal article by any method.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a quality mark is stamped, branded, engraved or
  imprinted on a precious metal article, a trademark that is applied to
  that article pursuant to subsection 4(3) of the Act shall be applied
  by the same method as the quality mark.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where only part of an article is composed of a
  precious metal plating of a quality specified in section 7, 8 or 9 and
  a quality mark is applied to the article, the name of the part to
  which the quality mark is applicable shall be applied immediately
  before or after the mark.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a statement of the thickness of the precious
  metal is expressed as a whole numeral and followed immediately by the
  word "micrometres", or the symbol, "vm" the word "micron", or the
  symbol "v" may be substituted therefor.

## [Articles other than Plated Articles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502962 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  quality mark specified in Column I of an item of the table to this
  section may be applied to an article, other than a plated article, or
  part thereof, that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is composed wholly or partly of a precious metal
    specified in Column II of that item; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} meets the requirements specified in Column III of
    that item.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where only part of an article, other than a plated
  article, is composed wholly or partly of a precious metal and the
  quality mark is applied to the article, the name of the part to which
  the quality mark is applicable shall be applied immediately before or
  after the mark.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a quality mark for gold is used, the word
  "gold", "or", the letter "G" or the abbreviation "Au" may be applied
  to the article in conjunction with the quality mark.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the quality of gold in an article is
  represented

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in karats, the number shown in conjunction with
    that representation shall be in the same proportion to the number 24
    as the quality of the gold in the article is to pure gold; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in decimals, the number used shall be in the same
    proportion to the number 1 as the quality of the gold in the article
    is to pure gold.

## [Plated Articles other than Watch Cases, Spectacle Frames, Flatware and Hollow Ware]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502978 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  quality mark specified in Column I of an item of the table to this
  section may be applied to a plated article, other than a watch case,
  spectacle frame, or an article of flatware or hollow ware that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is plated with the precious metal of a kind and
    quality specified in Column II of that item; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} meets the requirements specified in Columns III and
    IV of that item.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a quality mark specified in Column I of item 1
  or 2 of the table to this section is applied to an article,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a fraction showing the ratio of the weight of the
    gold in the article to the gross weight of the article, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the quality of the gold expressed in
    karats or decimals

  may be applied immediately preceding the quality mark.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} All numerals and characters expressing fractions or
  statements of quality authorized to be applied to an article by
  subsection (2) shall be of equal size and not larger than the quality
  mark referred to in subsection (1).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a quality mark specified in Column I of item 3,
  5 or 6 of the table to this section is applied to an article plated
  with gold or silver to a thickness of at least one micrometre, a
  statement of the thickness of the gold or silver expressed as a whole
  numeral and followed immediately by the word "micrometre", or the
  symbol "vm" therefor, may be applied to the article in conjunction
  with the quality mark.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a quality mark specified in Column I of item 4

  which all numerals are of equal size and not larger than the quality

  quality mark specified in Column I of an item of the table to this

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} gold that is at least 10 karats in quality or
    silver that is at least 925 parts by weight in every 1,000 parts

## [Bracelet Watch Cases]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503017 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  quality mark specified in Column I of an item of the table to this
  section may be applied to a bracelet watch case if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a quality mark specified in Column I
    of item 1 or 2 of the table, a sheet of gold that is at least 10
    karats in quality is soldered or brazed,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a quality mark specified in Column I
    of item 3 of the table, a sheet of silver that is at least 925 parts
    by weight per 1,000 parts pure silver in quality is soldered or
    brazed,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a quality mark specified in Column I
    of item 4 of the table, gold that is at least 10 karats in quality
    is plated by any means, or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a quality mark specified in Column I
    of item 5 of the table, silver that is at least 925 parts by weight
    per 1,000 parts pure silver in quality is plated by any means

  to at least the thickness specified in Column II of that item to the
  entire outer surface of the bezel, the centre, if any, the lugs and
  the back of the watch case.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where only

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the bezel, centre and lugs, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the bezel

  of a bracelet watch case are plated as described in subsection (1), a
  quality mark specified in Column I of an item of the table to this
  section may be applied to the case if it is stamped, branded, engraved
  or imprinted in the location and accompanied by the words specified in
  Column III or IV of that item, whichever is applicable.

## [Tolerances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503094 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  as part of a quality mark or in conjunction therewith, a
  representation in karats or decimals is made as to the quality of the
  gold in an article, the actual quality of the gold in the article
  shall not be less than the represented quality by more than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} seven parts by weight in every 1,000 parts where
    solder is used, except that for articles of white gold to which a
    mark is applied indicating a quality 18 karats or greater, the
    actual quality of the gold in the article shall not be less than the
    represented quality by more than 15 parts by weight in every 1,000
    parts where solder is used; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} three parts by weight in every 1,000 parts where no
    solder is used.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a quality mark specified in Column I of item 2
  of the table to section 6 is applied to an article, the actual quality
  of the silver in the article shall not be less than the represented
  quality by more than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} two parts by weight in every 1,000 parts, where no
    solder is used; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} six parts by weight in every 1,000 parts, where
    solder is used.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply to a precious
  metal article that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is imported into Canada, or manufactured in Canada,
    prior to March 15, 1982 or that is in transit to a dealer in Canada
    on March 15, 1982, and meets the requirements of those subsections
    as they read immediately prior to March 15, 1982, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is sold by a manufacturer in Canada prior to
    January 1, 1983 or that is in transit to a dealer in Canada on
    January 1, 1983 and meets the requirements of those subsections as
    they read immediately prior to March 15, 1982,

  unless the article is marked on or after March 15, 1982.
