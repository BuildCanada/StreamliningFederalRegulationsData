## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786715 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Rules.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Immigration and Refugee
    Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[appellant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appellant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is the subject
    of an appeal, or the Minister, who makes an appeal to the Division
    from a decision of the Refugee Protection
    Division. ([appelant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contact information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contact information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, with respect to a
    person,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person's name, postal address and telephone
      number, and their fax number and email address, if any; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of counsel for a person who is the
      subject of an appeal, if the counsel is a person referred to in
      any of paragraphs 91(2)(a) to (c) of the Act, in addition to the
      information referred to in paragraph (a), the name of the body of
      which the counsel is a member and the membership identification
      number issued to the counsel. ([coordonnées]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Refugee Appeal
    Division. ([Section]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[interested person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[interested person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person whose
    application to participate in an appeal under rule 46 has been
    granted. ([personne intéressée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an appeal by a person who is the
      subject of an appeal, the person and, if the Minister intervenes
      in the appeal, the Minister; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an appeal by the Minister, the
      person who is the subject of the appeal and the
      Minister. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a conference, an
    application, or an appeal that is decided with or without a
    hearing. ([procédure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registry office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registry office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a business office of
    the Division. ([greffe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Immigration and
    Refugee Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227). ([Règlement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is the subject
    of an appeal in the case of an appeal by the
    Minister. ([intimé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[UNHCR]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[UNHCR]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the United Nations High
    Commissioner for Refugees and includes its representative or
    agent. ([HCR]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vulnerable person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vulnerable person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who has
    been identified as vulnerable under the Guideline on Procedures with
    Respect to Vulnerable Persons Appearing Before the IRB issued under
    paragraph 159(1)(h) of the Act. ([personne
    vulnérable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include Saturdays,
    Sundays or other days on which the Board offices are closed. ([jour
    ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Rules Applicable to Appeals Made by a Person Who Is the Subject of an Appeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786735 .Part}

### [Filing and Perfecting an Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-786736 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing appeal

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To file
  an appeal, the person who is the subject of the appeal must provide to
  the Division three copies of a written notice of appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy provided to Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division must provide a copy of the notice of
  appeal to the Minister without delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice of appeal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the notice of appeal, the appellant must indicate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their name and telephone number, and an address
    where documents can be provided to them;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if represented by counsel, counsel's contact
    information and any limitations on counsel's retainer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the identification number given by the Department
    of Citizenship and Immigration to them;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Refugee Protection Division file number, the
    date of the notice of decision relating to the decision being
    appealed and the date that they received the written reasons for the
    decision;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the language --- English or French --- chosen by
    them as the language of the appeal; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the representative's contact information if the
    Refugee Protection Division has designated a representative for them
    in the proceedings relating to the decision being appealed, and any
    proposed change in representative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The notice of appeal provided under this rule must be
  received by the Division within the time limit for filing an appeal
  set out in the Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Perfecting appeal

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To
  perfect an appeal, the person who is the subject of the appeal must
  provide to the Division two copies of the appellant's record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy provided to Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division must provide a copy of the appellant's
  record to the Minister without delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of appellant's record

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The appellant's record must contain the following
  documents, on consecutively numbered pages, in the following order:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the notice of decision and written reasons for the
    Refugee Protection Division's decision that the appellant is
    appealing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all or part of the transcript of the Refugee
    Protection Division hearing if the appellant wants to rely on the
    transcript in the appeal, together with a declaration, signed by the
    transcriber, that includes the transcriber's name and a statement
    that the transcript is accurate;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any documents that the Refugee Protection Division
    refused to accept as evidence, during or after the hearing, if the
    appellant wants to rely on the documents in the appeal;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a written statement indicating

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} whether the appellant is relying on any evidence
      referred to in subsection 110(4) of the Act,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} whether the appellant is requesting that a
      hearing be held under subsection 110(6) of the Act, and if they
      are requesting a hearing, whether they are making an application
      under rule 66 to change the location of the hearing, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the language and dialect, if any, to be
      interpreted, if the Division decides that a hearing is necessary
      and the appellant needs an interpreter;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any documentary evidence that the appellant wants
    to rely on in the appeal;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any law, case law or other legal authority that the
    appellant wants to rely on in the appeal; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a memorandum that includes full and detailed
    submissions regarding

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the errors that are the grounds of the appeal,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the errors are located in the written
      reasons for the Refugee Protection Division's decision that the
      appellant is appealing or in the transcript or in any audio or
      other electronic recording of the Refugee Protection Division
      hearing,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} how any documentary evidence referred to in
      paragraph (e) meets the requirements of subsection 110(4) of the
      Act and how that evidence relates to the appellant,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the decision the appellant wants the Division to
      make, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} why the Division should hold a hearing under
      subsection 110(6) of the Act if the appellant is requesting that a
      hearing be held.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Length of memorandum

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The memorandum referred to in paragraph (3)(g) must
  not be more than 30 pages long if typewritten on one side or 15 pages
  if typewritten on both sides.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The appellant's record provided under this rule must
  be received by the Division within the time limit for perfecting an
  appeal set out in the Regulations.

### [Intervention by the Minister]{.HTitleText2} {#h-786778 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of intervention

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To
  intervene in an appeal at any time before the Division makes a
  decision, the Minister must provide, first to the appellant and then
  to the Division, a written notice of intervention, together with any
  documentary evidence that the Minister wants to rely on in the appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice of intervention

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the notice of intervention, the Minister must
  indicate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} counsel's contact information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the identification number given by the Department
    of Citizenship and Immigration to the appellant;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the appellant's name, the Refugee Protection
    Division file number, the date of the notice of decision relating to
    the decision being appealed and the date that the Minister received
    the written reasons for the decision;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether the Minister is relying on any documentary
    evidence referred to in subsection 110(3) of the Act and the
    relevance of that evidence; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} whether the Minister is requesting that a hearing
    be held under subsection 110(6) of the Act, and if the Minister is
    requesting a hearing, why the Division should hold a hearing and
    whether the Minister is making an application under rule 66 to
    change the location of the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's intervention record

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the documents referred to in
  subrule (1), the Minister may provide, first to the appellant and then
  to the Division, the Minister's intervention record containing the
  following documents, on consecutively numbered pages, in the following
  order:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all or part of the transcript of the Refugee
    Protection Division hearing if the Minister wants to rely on the
    transcript in the appeal and the transcript was not provided with
    the appellant's record, together with a declaration, signed by the
    transcriber, that includes the transcriber's name and a statement
    that the transcript is accurate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any law, case law or other legal authority that the
    Minister wants to rely on in the appeal; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a memorandum that includes full and detailed
    submissions regarding

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the grounds on which the Minister is contesting
      the appeal, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the decision the Minister wants the Division to
      make.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Length of memorandum

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The memorandum referred to in paragraph (3)(c) must
  not be more than 30 pages long if typewritten on one side or 15 pages
  if typewritten on both sides.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof documents were provided

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The documents provided to the Division under this
  rule must be accompanied by proof that they were provided to the
  appellant.

### [Reply]{.HTitleText2} {#h-786800 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply to Minister's intervention

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To reply
  to a Minister's intervention, the appellant must provide, first to the
  Minister and then to the Division, a reply record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of reply record

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The reply record must contain the following
  documents, on consecutively numbered pages, in the following order:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all or part of the transcript of the Refugee
    Protection Division hearing if the appellant wants to rely on the
    transcript to support the reply and the transcript was not provided
    with the appellant's record or by the Minister, together with a
    declaration, signed by the transcriber, that includes the
    transcriber's name and a statement that the transcript is accurate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any documentary evidence that the appellant wants
    to rely on to support the reply and that was not provided with the
    appellant's record or by the Minister;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any law, case law or other legal authority that the
    appellant wants to rely on to support the reply and that was not
    provided with the appellant's record or by the Minister; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a memorandum that includes full and detailed
    submissions regarding

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} only the grounds raised by the Minister,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} how any documentary evidence referred to in
      paragraph (b) meets the requirements of subsection 110(4) or (5)
      of the Act and how that evidence relates to the appellant, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} why the Division should hold a hearing under
      subsection 110(6) of the Act if the appellant is requesting that a
      hearing be held and they did not include such a request in the
      appellant's record, and if the appellant is requesting a hearing,
      whether they are making an application under rule 66 to change the
      location of the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Length of memorandum

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The memorandum referred to in paragraph (2)(d) must
  not be more than 30 pages long if typewritten on one side or 15 pages
  if typewritten on both sides.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof document was provided

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The reply record provided to the Division must be
  accompanied by proof that it was provided to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under this rule must be received
  by the Division no later than 15 days after the day on which the
  appellant receives the Minister's notice of intervention, the
  Minister's intervention record, or any additional documents provided
  by the Minister, as the case may be.

### [Extension of Time]{.HTitleText2} {#h-786819 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for extension of time to file
or perfect

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who is the subject of an appeal who makes an application to the
  Division for an extension of the time to file or to perfect an appeal
  under the Regulations must do so in accordance with rule 37, except
  that the person must provide to the Division the original and a copy
  of the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy provided to Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division must provide a copy of an application
  under subrule (1) to the Minister without delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person who is the subject of the appeal must
  include in an application under subrule (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their name and telephone number, and an address
    where documents can be provided to them;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if represented by counsel, counsel's contact
    information and any limitations on counsel's retainer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the identification number given by the Department
    of Citizenship and Immigration to them; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Refugee Protection Division file number, the
    date of the notice of decision relating to the decision being
    appealed and the date that they received the written reasons for the
    decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accompanying documents --- filing

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An application for an extension of the time to file
  an appeal under subrule (1) must be accompanied by three copies of a
  written notice of appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accompanying documents --- perfecting

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An application for an extension of the time to
  perfect an appeal under subrule (1) must be accompanied by two copies
  of the appellant's record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for extension of time to
  reply

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A person who is the subject of an appeal may make an
  application to the Division for an extension of the time to reply to a
  Minister's intervention in accordance with rule 37.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors --- reply

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In deciding an application under subrule (6), the
  Division must consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the application was made in a timely manner
    and the justification for any delay;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether there is an arguable case;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prejudice to the Minister, if the application was
    granted; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the nature and complexity of the appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of decision on application

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The Division must without delay notify, in writing,
  both the person who is the subject of the appeal and the Minister of
  its decision with respect to an application under subrule (1) or (6).

### [Disposition of an Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-786845 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision without further notice

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Unless a hearing is held
under subsection 110(6) of the Act, the Division may, without further
notice to the appellant and to the Minister, decide an appeal on the
basis of the materials provided

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a period of 15 days has passed since the day on
  which the Minister received the appellant's record, or the time limit
  for perfecting the appeal set out in the Regulations has expired; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the reply record has been provided, or the time
  limit for providing it has expired.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Rules Applicable to Appeals Made by the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786850 .Part}

### [Filing and Perfecting an Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-786851 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing appeal

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To file
  an appeal in accordance with subsection 110(1.1) of the Act, the
  Minister must provide, first to the person who is the subject of the
  appeal, a written notice of appeal, and then to the Division, two
  copies of the written notice of appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice of appeal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the notice of appeal, the Minister must indicate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} counsel's contact information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person who is the subject of the
    appeal and the identification number given by the Department of
    Citizenship and Immigration to them; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Refugee Protection Division file number, the
    date of the notice of decision relating to the decision being
    appealed and the date that the Minister received the written reasons
    for the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof document was provided

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice of appeal provided to the Division must be
  accompanied by proof that it was provided to the person who is the
  subject of the appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The notice of appeal provided under this rule must be
  received by the Division within the time limit for filing an appeal
  set out in the Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Perfecting appeal

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To
  perfect an appeal in accordance with subsection 110(1.1) of the Act,
  the Minister must provide, first to the person who is the subject of
  the appeal and then to the Division, any supporting documents that the
  Minister wants to rely on in the appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of appellant's record

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the documents referred to in
  subrule (1), the Minister may provide, first to the person who is the
  subject of the appeal and then to the Division, the appellant's record
  containing the following documents, on consecutively numbered pages,
  in the following order:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the notice of decision and written reasons for the
    Refugee Protection Division's decision that the Minister is
    appealing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all or part of the transcript of the Refugee
    Protection Division hearing if the Minister wants to rely on the
    transcript in the appeal, together with a declaration, signed by the
    transcriber, that includes the transcriber's name and a statement
    that the transcript is accurate;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any documents that the Refugee Protection Division
    refused to accept as evidence, during or after the hearing, if the
    Minister wants to rely on the documents in the appeal;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a written statement indicating

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} whether the Minister is relying on any
      documentary evidence referred to in subsection 110(3) of the Act
      and the relevance of that evidence, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} whether the Minister is requesting that a
      hearing be held under subsection 110(6) of the Act, and if the
      Minister is requesting a hearing, why the Division should hold a
      hearing and whether the Minister is making an application under
      rule 66 to change the location of the hearing;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any law, case law or other legal authority that the
    Minister wants to rely on in the appeal; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a memorandum that includes full and detailed
    submissions regarding

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the errors that are the grounds of the appeal,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the errors are located in the written
      reasons for the Refugee Protection Division's decision that the
      Minister is appealing or in the transcript or in any audio or
      other electronic recording of the Refugee Protection Division
      hearing, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the decision the Minister wants the Division to
      make.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Length of memorandum

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The memorandum referred to in paragraph (2)(f) must
  not be more than 30 pages long if typewritten on one side or 15 pages
  if typewritten on both sides.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof documents were provided

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any supporting documents and the appellant's record,
  if any, provided to the Division must be accompanied by proof that
  they were provided to the person who is the subject of the appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under this rule must be received
  by the Division within the time limit for perfecting an appeal set out
  in the Regulations.

### [Response to an Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-786886 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response to appeal

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To
  respond to an appeal, the person who is the subject of the appeal must
  provide, first to the Minister and then to the Division, a written
  notice of intent to respond, together with the respondent's record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice of intent to respond

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the notice of intent to respond, the respondent
  must indicate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their name and telephone number, and an address
    where documents can be provided to them;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if represented by counsel, counsel's contact
    information and any limitations on counsel's retainer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the identification number given by the Department
    of Citizenship and Immigration to them;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Refugee Protection Division file number and the
    date of the notice of decision relating to the decision being
    appealed;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the language --- English or French --- chosen by
    them as the language of the appeal; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the representative's contact information if the
    Refugee Protection Division has designated a representative for them
    in the proceedings relating to the decision being appealed, and any
    proposed change in representative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of respondent's record

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The respondent's record must contain the following
  documents, on consecutively numbered pages, in the following order:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all or part of the transcript of the Refugee
    Protection Division hearing if the respondent wants to rely on the
    transcript in the appeal and the transcript was not provided with
    the appellant's record, together with a declaration, signed by the
    transcriber, that includes the transcriber's name and a statement
    that the transcript is accurate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written statement indicating

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} whether the respondent is requesting that a
      hearing be held under subsection 110(6) of the Act, and if they
      are requesting a hearing, whether they are making an application
      under rule 66 to change the location of the hearing, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the language and dialect, if any, to be
      interpreted, if the Division decides that a hearing is necessary
      and the respondent needs an interpreter;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any documentary evidence that the respondent wants
    to rely on in the appeal;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any law, case law or other legal authority that the
    respondent wants to rely on in the appeal; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a memorandum that includes full and detailed
    submissions regarding

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the grounds on which the respondent is contesting
      the appeal,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the decision the respondent wants the Division
      to make, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} why the Division should hold a hearing under
      subsection 110(6) of the Act if the respondent is requesting that
      a hearing be held.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Length of memorandum

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The memorandum referred to in paragraph (3)(e) must
  not be more than 30 pages long if typewritten on one side or 15 pages
  if typewritten on both sides.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof documents were provided

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The notice of intent to respond and the respondent's
  record provided to the Division must be accompanied by proof that they
  were provided to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under this rule must be received
  by the Division no later than 15 days after

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the respondent receives any
    supporting documents; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Division allows an application for an
    extension of time to perfect the appeal under rule 12, the day on
    which the respondent is notified of the decision to allow the
    extension of time.

### [Reply]{.HTitleText2} {#h-786918 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's reply

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To
  reply to a response by the respondent, the Minister must provide,
  first to the respondent and then to the Division, any documentary
  evidence that the Minister wants to rely on to support the reply and
  that was not provided at the time that the appeal was perfected or
  with the respondent's record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply record

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the documents referred to in
  subrule (1), the Minister may provide, first to the respondent and
  then to the Division, a reply record containing the following
  documents, on consecutively numbered pages, in the following order:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all or part of the transcript of the Refugee
    Protection Division hearing if the Minister wants to rely on the
    transcript to support the reply and the transcript was not provided
    with the appellant's record, if any, or the respondent's record,
    together with a declaration, signed by the transcriber, that
    includes the transcriber's name and a statement that the transcript
    is accurate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any law, case law or other legal authority that the
    Minister wants to rely on to support the reply and that was not
    provided with the appellant's record, if any, or the respondent's
    record; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a memorandum that includes full and detailed
    submissions regarding

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} only the grounds raised by the respondent, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} why the Division should hold a hearing under
      subsection 110(6) of the Act if the Minister is requesting that a
      hearing be held and the Minister did not include such a request in
      the appellant's record, if any, and if the Minister is requesting
      a hearing, whether the Minister is making an application under
      rule 66 to change the location of the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Length of memorandum

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The memorandum referred to in paragraph (2)(c) must
  not be more than 30 pages long if typewritten on one side or 15 pages
  if typewritten on both sides.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof documents were provided

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any documentary evidence and the reply record, if
  any, provided to the Division under this rule must be accompanied by
  proof that they were provided to the respondent.

### [Extension of Time]{.HTitleText2} {#h-786933 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for extension of time ---
Minister

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister makes an application to the Division for an extension of the
  time to file or to perfect an appeal under the Regulations, the
  Minister must do so in accordance with rule 37.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accompanying documents --- filing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for an extension of the time to file
  an appeal under subrule (1) must be accompanied by two copies of a
  written notice of appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accompanying documents --- perfecting

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application for an extension of the time to
  perfect an appeal under subrule (1) must be accompanied by any
  supporting documents, and an appellant's record, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for extension of time ---
  person

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person who is the subject of an appeal may make an
  application to the Division for an extension of the time to respond to
  an appeal in accordance with rule 37.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of application for extension of
  time to respond to appeal

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The person who is the subject of the appeal must
  include in an application under subrule (4)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their name and telephone number, and an address
    where documents can be provided to them;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if represented by counsel, counsel's contact
    information and any limitations on counsel's retainer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the identification number given by the Department
    of Citizenship and Immigration to them; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Refugee Protection Division file number, the
    date of the notice of decision relating to the decision being
    appealed and the date that they received the written reasons for the
    decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors --- respond

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} In deciding an application under subrule (4), the
  Division must consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the application was made in a timely manner
    and the justification for any delay;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether there is an arguable case;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prejudice to the Minister, if the application was
    granted; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the nature and complexity of the appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of decision on application

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Division must without delay notify, in writing,
  both the person who is the subject of the appeal and the Minister of
  its decision with respect to an application under subrule (1) or (4).

### [Disposition of an Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-786957 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision without further notice

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Unless a hearing is held
under subsection 110(6) of the Act, the Division may, without further
notice to the parties, decide an appeal on the basis of the materials
provided

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a period of 15 days has passed since the day on
  which the Minister received the respondent's record, or the time limit
  for providing it set out in subrule 10(6) has expired; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Minister's reply has been provided.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Rules Applicable to All Appeals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786962 .Part}

### [Communicating with the Division]{.HTitleText2} {#h-786963 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communicating with Division

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** All communication with
the Division must be directed to the registry office specified by the
Division.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change to contact information

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** If the contact
information of a person who is the subject of an appeal changes, the
person must without delay provide the changes in writing to the Division
and to the Minister.

### [Counsel]{.HTitleText2} {#h-786968 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retaining counsel after providing notice

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person who is the subject of an appeal retains counsel after providing
  a notice of appeal or a notice of intent to respond, as the case may
  be, the person must without delay provide the counsel's contact
  information in writing to the Division and to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change to counsel's contact information
  --- person

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the contact information of counsel for a person
  who is the subject of an appeal changes, the person must without delay
  provide the changes in writing to the Division and to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change to counsel's contact information
  --- Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the contact information of counsel for the
  Minister changes, the Minister must without delay provide the changes
  in writing to the Division and to the person who is the subject of the
  appeal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration --- counsel not representing
or advising for consideration

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** If a person who is the
subject of an appeal retains counsel who is not a person referred to in
any of paragraphs 91(2)(a) to (c) of the Act, both the person who is the
subject of the appeal and their counsel must without delay provide the
information and declarations set out in the schedule to the Division in
writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Becoming counsel of record

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (2), as soon as
  counsel for a person who is the subject of an appeal provides on
  behalf of the person a notice of appeal or a notice of intent to
  respond, as the case may be, or as soon as a person becomes counsel
  after the person provided a notice, the counsel becomes counsel of
  record for the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation on counsel's retainer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person who is the subject of an appeal has
  notified the Division of a limitation on their counsel's retainer,
  counsel is counsel of record only to the extent of the services to be
  provided within the limited retainer. Counsel ceases to be counsel of
  record as soon as those services are completed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request to be removed as counsel of
record

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To be
  removed as counsel of record, counsel for a person who is the subject
  of an appeal must first provide to the person and to the Minister a
  copy of a written request to be removed and then provide the written
  request to the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof request was provided

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request provided to the Division must be
  accompanied by proof that copies were provided to the person
  represented and to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request --- if date for proceeding
  fixed

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a date for a proceeding has been fixed and three
  working days or less remain before that date, counsel must make the
  request orally at the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Division's permission required

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Counsel remains counsel of record unless the request
  to be removed is granted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removing counsel of record

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To
  remove counsel as counsel of record, a person who is the subject of an
  appeal must first provide to counsel and to the Minister a copy of a
  written notice that counsel is no longer counsel for the person and
  then provide the written notice to the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof notice was provided

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice provided to the Division must be
  accompanied by proof that copies were provided to counsel and to the
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ceasing to be counsel of record

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Counsel ceases to be counsel of record when the
  Division receives the notice.

### [Refugee Protection Division Record]{.HTitleText2} {#h-786999 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing notice of appeal

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Division must without delay provide a copy of the notice of appeal to
  the Refugee Protection Division after the appeal is perfected under
  rule 3 or 9, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preparing and providing record

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Refugee Protection Division must prepare a record
  and provide it to the Division no later than 10 days after the day on
  which the Refugee Protection Division receives the notice of appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of record

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Refugee Protection Division record must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the notice of decision and written reasons for the
    decision that is being appealed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Basis of Claim Form as defined in the [Refugee
    Protection Division Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-256) and any
    changes or additions to it;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all documentary evidence that the Refugee
    Protection Division accepted as evidence, during or after the
    hearing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any written representations made during or after
    the hearing but before the decision being appealed was made; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any audio or other electronic recording of the
    hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing record to absent Minister

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister did not take part in the proceedings
  relating to the decision being appealed, the Division must provide a
  copy of the Refugee Protection Division record to the Minister as soon
  as the Division receives it.

### [Language of the Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787014 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Choice of language

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is the subject of an appeal must choose English or French
  as the language of the appeal. The person must indicate that choice in
  the notice of appeal if they are the appellant or in the notice of
  intent to respond if they are the respondent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Language --- Minister's appeals

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the appellant is the Minister, the language of the
  appeal is the language chosen by the person who is the subject of the
  appeal in the proceedings relating to the decision being appealed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changing language

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who is the subject of an appeal may change
  the language of the appeal that they chose under subrule (1) by
  notifying the Division and the Minister in writing without delay and,
  if a date for a proceeding has been fixed, the notice must be received
  by their recipients no later than 20 days before that date.

### [Designated Representatives]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787022 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of designation

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Refugee Protection Division designated a representative for the person
  who is the subject of the appeal in the proceedings relating to the
  decision being appealed, the representative is deemed to have been
  designated by the Division, unless the Division orders otherwise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of counsel to notify

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Refugee Protection Division did not designate
  a representative for the person who is the subject of the appeal and
  counsel for a party believes that the Division should designate a
  representative for the person because the person is under 18 years of
  age or is unable to appreciate the nature of the proceedings, counsel
  must without delay notify the Division in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subrule (2) does not apply in the case of a person
  under 18 years of age whose appeal is joined with the appeal of their
  parent or legal guardian if the parent or legal guardian is 18 years
  of age or older.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The notice must include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether counsel is aware of a person in Canada who
    meets the requirements to be designated as a representative and, if
    so, the person's contact information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any available supporting documents; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reasons why counsel believes that a
    representative should be designated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for being designated

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} To be designated as a representative, a person must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be 18 years of age or older;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} understand the nature of the proceedings;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be willing and able to act in the best interests of
    the person who is the subject of the appeal; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} not have interests that conflict with those of the
    person who is the subject of the appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} When determining whether a person who is the subject
  of an appeal is unable to appreciate the nature of the proceedings,
  the Division must consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the person can understand the reason for
    the proceeding and can instruct counsel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person's statements and behaviour at the
    proceeding;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} expert evidence, if any, on the person's
    intellectual or physical faculties, age or mental condition; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether the person has had a representative
    designated for a proceeding in a division other than the Refugee
    Protection Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation applies to all proceedings

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The designation of a representative for a person who
  is under 18 years of age or who is unable to appreciate the nature of
  the proceedings applies to all subsequent proceedings in the Division
  with respect to that person unless the Division orders otherwise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}End of designation --- person reaches
  18 years of age

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The designation of a representative for a person who
  is under 18 years of age ends when the person reaches 18 years of age
  unless that representative has also been designated because the person
  is unable to appreciate the nature of the proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of designation

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The Division may terminate a designation if the
  Division is of the opinion that the representative is no longer
  required or suitable and may designate a new representative if
  required.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation criteria

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} Before designating a person as a representative, the
  Division must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assess the person's ability to fulfil the
    responsibilities of a designated representative; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the person has been informed of the
    responsibilities of a designated representative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibilities of representative

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} The responsibilities of a designated representative
  include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deciding whether to retain counsel and, if counsel
    is retained, instructing counsel or assisting the represented person
    in instructing counsel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} making decisions regarding the appeal or assisting
    the represented person in making those decisions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} informing the represented person about the various
    stages and procedures in the processing of their case;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} assisting in gathering evidence to support the
    represented person's case and in providing evidence and, if
    necessary, being a witness at the hearing;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} protecting the interests of the represented person
    and putting forward the best possible case to the Division; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} informing and consulting the represented person to
    the extent possible when making decisions about the case.

### [Specialized Knowledge]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787065 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to parties

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  using any information or opinion that is within its specialized
  knowledge, the Division must notify the parties and give them an
  opportunity to,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a date for a hearing has not been fixed, make
    written representations on the reliability and use of the
    information or opinion and provide written evidence in support of
    their representations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a date for a hearing has been fixed, make oral
    or written representations on the reliability and use of the
    information or opinion and provide evidence in support of their
    representations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing written representations and
  evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party must provide its written representations and
  evidence first to any other party and then to the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof written representations and
  evidence were provided

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The written representations and evidence provided to
  the Division must be accompanied by proof that they were provided to
  any other party.

### [Notice of Constitutional Question]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787075 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of constitutional question

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party who wants to challenge the constitutional validity,
  applicability or operability of a legislative provision must complete
  a notice of constitutional question.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party must complete the notice as set out in
  Form 69 of the [Federal Courts Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106) or
  any other form that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the party's name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Division file number;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the specific legislative provision that is being
    challenged;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the material facts relied on to support the
    constitutional challenge; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the legal argument to be made in
    support of the constitutional challenge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The party must provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the notice to the Attorney General of
    Canada and to the attorney general of each province of Canada, in
    accordance with section 57 of the [Federal Courts
    Act](/eng/acts/F-7);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the notice to the Minister even if the
    Minister has not yet intervened in the appeal;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the notice to the UNHCR, if the UNHCR has
    provided notice of its intention to provide written submissions, and
    to any interested person; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the original notice to the Division, together with
    proof that copies were provided under paragraphs (a) to (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under this rule must be received
  by their recipients at the same time as the Division receives the
  appellant's record, respondent's record or the reply record, as the
  case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deciding of constitutional question

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Division must not make a decision on the
  constitutional question until at least 10 days after the day on which
  it receives the notice of constitutional question.

### [Conferences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787096 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to participate at conference

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Division may require the parties to participate at a conference to
  discuss issues, relevant facts and any other matter in order to make
  the appeal fairer and more efficient.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information or documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division may require the parties to give any
  information or provide any document, at or before the conference.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written record

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division must make a written record of any
  decisions and agreements made at the conference.

### [Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787104 .Subheading}

#### [Form and Language of Documents]{.HTitleText3} {#h-787105 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents prepared by party

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document prepared for use by a party in a proceeding must be
  typewritten, in a type not smaller than 12 point, on one or both sides
  of 216 mm by 279 mm (8 ½ inches x 11 inches) paper.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Photocopies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any photocopy provided by a party must be a clear
  copy of the document photocopied and be on one or both sides of 216 mm
  by 279 mm (8 ½ inches x 11 inches) paper.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of documents

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If more than one document is provided, the party must
  provide a list identifying each of the documents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consecutively numbered pages

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A party must consecutively number each page of all
  the documents provided as if they were one document.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Language of documents --- person

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  documents used by a person who is the subject of an appeal in an
  appeal must be in English or French or, if in another language, be
  provided together with an English or French translation and a
  declaration signed by the translator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Language of Minister's documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All documents used by the Minister in an appeal must
  be in the language of the appeal or be provided together with a
  translation in the language of the appeal and a declaration signed by
  the translator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Translator's declaration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A translator's declaration must include the
  translator's name, the language and dialect, if any, translated and a
  statement that the translation is accurate.

#### [Documents or Written Submissions not Previously Provided]{.HTitleText3} {#h-787122 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents or written submissions not
previously provided --- person

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is the subject of an appeal who does not provide a document
  or written submissions with the appellant's record, respondent's
  record or reply record must not use the document or provide the
  written submissions in the appeal unless allowed to do so by the
  Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person who is the subject of an appeal wants to
  use a document or provide written submissions that were not previously
  provided, the person must make an application to the Division in
  accordance with rule 37.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents --- new evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person who is the subject of the appeal must
  include in an application to use a document that was not previously
  provided an explanation of how the document meets the requirements of
  subsection 110(4) of the Act and how that evidence relates to the
  person, unless the document is being presented in response to evidence
  presented by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In deciding whether to allow an application, the
  Division must consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the document's relevance and probative value;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any new evidence the document brings to the appeal;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the person who is the subject of the
    appeal, with reasonable effort, could have provided the document or
    written submissions with the appellant's record, respondent's record
    or reply record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents or written submissions not
  previously provided --- Minister

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If, at any time before the Division makes a decision,
  the Minister, in accordance with paragraph 171(a.5) of the Act,
  submits documentary evidence or written submissions in support of the
  Minister's appeal or intervention that were not previously provided,
  the Minister must provide the documentary evidence or written
  submissions first to the person who is the subject of the appeal and
  then to the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof documents or written submissions
  provided

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The additional documents or written submissions
  provided to the Division under subrule (5) must be accompanied by
  proof that they were provided to the person who is the subject of the
  appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply to Minister's documents or
  written submissions

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The person who is the subject of the appeal may reply
  to the additional documents or written submissions in accordance with
  rule 5 with any modifications that the circumstances require.

#### [Providing a Document]{.HTitleText3} {#h-787141 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General provision

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Rules 31 to 35 apply to
any document, including a notice or request in writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing documents to Division

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document to be provided to the Division must be provided to the
  Division's registry office that is located in the same region as the
  Refugee Protection Division's registry office through which the notice
  of decision under appeal was provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing documents to Refugee
  Protection Division

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document to be provided to the Refugee Protection
  Division must be provided to the Refugee Protection Division's
  registry office through which the notice of decision under appeal was
  provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing documents to Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A document to be provided to the Minister must be
  provided to the Minister's counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing documents to person other
  than Minister

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A document to be provided to a person other than the
  Minister must be provided to the person's counsel if the person has
  counsel of record. If the person does not have counsel of record, the
  document must be provided to the person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How to provide document

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** A document may be
provided in any of the following ways:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by hand;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by regular mail or registered mail;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} by courier;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} by fax if the recipient has a fax number and the
  document is no more than 20 pages long, unless the recipient consents
  to receiving more than 20 pages; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} by email or other electronic means if the Division
  allows.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application if unable to provide document

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  party is unable to provide a document in a way required by rule 32,
  the party may make an application to the Division to be allowed to
  provide the document in another way or to be excused from providing
  the document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be made in accordance with
  rule 37.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allowing application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division must not allow the application unless
  the party has made reasonable efforts to provide the document to the
  person to whom the document must be provided.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof document was provided

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Proof
  that a document was provided must be established by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgment of receipt signed by the
    recipient or a statement of service, if the document was provided by
    hand;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a confirmation of receipt if the document was
    provided by registered mail, courier, fax or email or other
    electronic means; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement of service if the document was provided
    by regular mail.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement of service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(a) or (c), a
  statement of service consists of a written statement, signed by the
  person who provided the document, that includes the person's name and
  a statement of how and when the document was provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement -- unable to provide proof

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a party is unable to provide proof that a document
  was provided in a way required by paragraph (1)(a) to (c), the party
  must provide a written statement, signed by the party, that includes
  an explanation of why they are unable to provide proof.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When document received by division

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document provided to the Division or to the Refugee Protection
  Division is considered to be received on the day on which the document
  is date-stamped by that division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When document received by recipient
  other than division

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document provided by regular mail other than to the
  Division or to the Refugee Protection Division is considered to be
  received seven days after the day on which it was mailed. If the
  seventh day is not a working day, the document is considered to be
  received on the next working day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of time limit --- next
  working day

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When the time limit for providing a document ends on
  a day that is not a working day, the time limit is extended to the
  next working day.

### [Applications]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787184 .Subheading}

#### [General]{.HTitleText3} {#h-787185 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General provision

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** Unless these Rules
provide otherwise,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a party who wants the Division to make a decision on
  any matter in a proceeding, including the procedure to be followed,
  must make an application to the Division in accordance with rule 37;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a party who wants to respond to the application must
  respond in accordance with rule 38; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a party who wants to reply to a response must reply
  in accordance with rule 39.

#### [How to Make an Application]{.HTitleText3} {#h-787191 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of application and time limit

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  these Rules provide otherwise, an application must be made in writing
  and without delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a date for a hearing has been fixed, the Division
  must not allow a party to make an application orally at the hearing
  unless the party, with reasonable effort, could not have made a
  written application before that date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless these Rules provide otherwise, in a written
  application, the party must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state the decision the party wants the Division to
    make;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give reasons why the Division should make that
    decision; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if there is another party and the views of that
    party are known, state whether the other party agrees to the
    application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affidavit or statutory declaration

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Unless these Rules provide otherwise, any evidence
  that the party wants the Division to consider with a written
  application must be given in an affidavit or statutory declaration
  that accompanies the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing application to other party
  and Division

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A party who makes a written application must provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to any other party, a copy of the application and a
    copy of any affidavit or statutory declaration; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the Division, the original application and the
    original of any affidavit or statutory declaration, together with
    proof that a copy was provided to any other party.

#### [How to Respond to a Written Application]{.HTitleText3} {#h-787208 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responding to written application

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  response to a written application must be in writing and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state the decision the party wants the Division to
    make; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give reasons why the Division should make that
    decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence in written response

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any evidence that the party wants the Division to
  consider with the written response must be given in an affidavit or
  statutory declaration that accompanies the response. Unless the
  Division requires it, an affidavit or statutory declaration is not
  required if the party who made the application was not required to
  give evidence in an affidavit or statutory declaration, together with
  the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing response

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party who responds to a written application must
  provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the other party, a copy of the response and a
    copy of any affidavit or statutory declaration; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the Division, the original response and the
    original of any affidavit or statutory declaration, together with
    proof that a copy was provided to the other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under subrule (3) must be received
  by their recipients no later than seven days after the day on which
  the party receives the copy of the application.

#### [How to Reply to a Written Response]{.HTitleText3} {#h-787222 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replying to written response

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  reply to a written response must be in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence in reply

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any evidence that the party wants the Division to
  consider with the written reply must be given in an affidavit or
  statutory declaration that accompanies the reply. Unless the Division
  requires it, an affidavit or statutory declaration is not required if
  the party was not required to give evidence in an affidavit or
  statutory declaration, together with the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing reply

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party who replies to a written response must
  provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the other party, a copy of the reply and a copy
    of any affidavit or statutory declaration; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the Division, the original reply and the
    original of any affidavit or statutory declaration, together with
    proof that a copy was provided to the other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under subrule (3) must be received
  by their recipients no later than five days after the day on which the
  party receives the copy of the response.

### [Joining or Separating Appeals]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787234 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeals automatically joined

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** The Division must join
any appeals of decisions on claims that were joined at the time that the
Refugee Protection Division decided the claims.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to join

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may make an application to the Division to join appeals.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to separate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party may make an application to the Division to
  separate appeals that are joined.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of application and providing
  application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party who makes an application to join or separate
  appeals must do so in accordance with rule 37, but the party is not
  required to give evidence in an affidavit or statutory declaration.
  The party must also

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide a copy of the application to any person who
    will be affected by the Division's decision on the application; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide to the Division proof that the party
    provided the copy of the application to any affected person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under this rule must be received
  by their recipients,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the person who is the subject of the appeal is
    the applicant, at the same time as the Division receives the
    person's notice of appeal, notice of intent to respond or reply
    record; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Minister is the applicant, at the same time
    as the Division receives the Minister's notice of appeal, notice of
    intervention or reply.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In deciding the application, the Division must
  consider any relevant factors, including whether

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the appeals involve similar questions of fact or
    law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} allowing the application would promote the
    efficient administration of the Division's work; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} allowing the application would likely cause an
    injustice.

### [Proceedings Conducted in Public]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787255 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister considered party

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of this rule, the Minister is considered to be a party
  even if the Minister has not yet intervened in the appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who makes an application to the Division to
  have a proceeding conducted in public must do so in writing and in
  accordance with this rule rather than rule 37.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a date for a hearing has been fixed, the Division
  must not allow a person to make an application orally at the hearing
  unless the person, with reasonable effort, could not have made a
  written application before that date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the application, the person must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state the decision they want the Division to make;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give reasons why the Division should make that
    decision;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state whether they want the Division to consider
    the application in public or in the absence of the public;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} give reasons why the Division should consider the
    application in public or in the absence of the public; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} include any evidence that they want the Division to
    consider in deciding the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The person must provide the original application and
  two copies to the Division. The Division must provide a copy of the
  application to the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response to application

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A party may respond to a written application. The
  response must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state the decision they want the Division to make;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give reasons why the Division should make that
    decision;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state whether they want the Division to consider
    the application in public or in the absence of the public;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} give reasons why the Division should consider the
    application in public or in the absence of the public; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} include any evidence that they want the Division to
    consider in deciding the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's notice

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister responds to a written application,
  the response must be accompanied by a notice of intervention in
  accordance with subrule 4(2), if one was not previously provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing response

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The party must provide a copy of the response to the
  other party and provide the original response and a copy to the
  Division, together with proof that the copy was provided to the other
  party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing response to applicant

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The Division must provide to the applicant either a
  copy of the response or a summary of the response referred to in
  paragraph (13)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply to response

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} An applicant or a party may reply in writing to a
  written response or a summary of a response.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing reply

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} An applicant or a party who replies to a written
  response or a summary of a response must provide the original reply
  and two copies to the Division. The Division must provide a copy of
  the reply to the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} An application made under this rule must be received
  by the Division without delay. The Division must specify the time
  limit within which a response or reply, if any, is to be provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality

  [(13)]{.lawlabel} The Division may take any measures it considers
  necessary to ensure the confidentiality of the proceeding in respect
  of the application, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} providing a summary of the response to the
    applicant instead of a copy; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Division holds a hearing in respect of the
    appeal and the application,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} excluding the applicant or the applicant and
      their counsel from the hearing while the party responding to the
      application provides evidence and makes representations, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} allowing the presence of the applicant's counsel
      at the hearing while the party responding to the application
      provides evidence and makes representations, on receipt of a
      written undertaking by counsel not to disclose any evidence or
      information adduced until a decision is made to hold the hearing
      in public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summary of response

  [(14)]{.lawlabel} If the Division provides a summary of the response
  under paragraph (13)(a), or excludes the applicant and their counsel
  from a hearing in respect of the application under
  subparagraph (13)(b)(i), the Division must provide a summary of the
  representations and evidence, if any, that is sufficient to enable the
  applicant to reply, while ensuring the confidentiality of the
  proceeding having regard to the factors set out in paragraph 166(b) of
  the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of decision on application

  [(15)]{.lawlabel} The Division must notify the applicant and the
  parties of its decision on the application and provide reasons for the
  decision.

### [Assignment of Three-member Panel]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787301 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of order

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Chairperson of the Board orders a proceeding to be conducted by three
  Division members, the Division must without delay notify the parties
  --- including the Minister even if the Minister has not yet intervened
  in the appeal --- and the UNHCR in writing of the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing documents to UNHCR

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division must provide the UNHCR with a copy of
  the following documents at the same time that it provides notice of
  the order:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Refugee Protection Division record; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the notice of appeal, appellant's record, notice of
    intent to respond, respondent's record, reply record, Minister's
    notice of intervention, Minister's intervention record, if any,
    Minister's reply, and Minister's reply record, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}UNHCR's notice to Division

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the UNHCR receives notice of an order, the UNHCR
  may provide notice to the Division in accordance with subrule 45(1) of
  its intention to provide written submissions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Division may, without further notice to the
  parties and to the UNHCR, decide the appeal on the basis of the
  materials provided if a period of 15 days has passed since the day on
  which the Minister and the UNHCR receive notice of the order.

### [UNHCR and Interested Persons]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787313 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules applicable to UNHCR and interested
persons

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** These Rules, with the
exception of rules 25 (notice of constitutional question) and 47 to 49
(withdrawal, reinstatement, reopening), apply to the UNHCR and
interested persons with any modifications that the circumstances
require.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Division

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  UNHCR must notify the Division in writing of its intention to provide
  written submissions in an appeal conducted by a three-member panel,
  and include its contact information and that of its counsel, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to person and Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division must without delay provide a copy of the
  UNHCR's notice to the person who is the subject of the appeal and to
  the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing written submissions to
  Division

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The UNHCR's written submissions must be received by
  the Division no later than 10 days after the day on which the UNHCR
  provided the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- written submissions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The UNHCR's written submissions must not raise new
  issues.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Length of written submissions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The UNHCR's written submissions must not be more than
  30 pages long if typewritten on one side or 15 pages if typewritten on
  both sides.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing written submissions

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Division must without delay provide a copy of the
  UNHCR's written submissions to the person who is the subject of the
  appeal and to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The person who is the subject of the appeal or the
  Minister may respond to the UNHCR's submissions in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- response

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} A response must not raise new issues.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Length of response

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} A response must not be more than 30 pages long if
  typewritten on one side or 15 pages if typewritten on both sides.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing response

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} The response must first be provided to the person
  who is the subject of the appeal or to the Minister, as the case may
  be, and then to the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof response provided

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} The response provided to the Division must be
  accompanied by proof that it was provided to the person who is the
  subject of the appeal or to the Minister, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under subrules (10) and (11) must
  be received by their recipients no later than seven days after the day
  on which the person who is the subject of the appeal or the Minister,
  as the case may be, receives the UNHCR's submissions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application by person to participate

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person, other than the UNHCR, may make an application to the Division
  to be allowed to participate in an appeal conducted by a three-member
  panel. The person must make the application without delay and in
  accordance with this rule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be in writing and include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of why the applicant wants to
    participate;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the submissions the applicant wants to put forward
    and an explanation of how they are relevant to the appeal;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of the differences between the
    applicant's submissions and those of the person who is the subject
    of the appeal and the Minister;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of how the applicant's submissions
    may help the Division decide the appeal; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the contact information of the applicant and their
    counsel, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division must provide a copy of the application
  to the person who is the subject of the appeal and to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The person who is the subject of the appeal or the
  Minister may respond to the application in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- response

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A response must not raise new issues.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Length of response

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A response must not be more than 30 pages long if
  typewritten on one side or 15 pages if typewritten on both sides.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A response must be received by the Division no later
  than 10 days after the day on which the person who is the subject of
  the appeal or the Minister, as the case may be, receives the
  application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of decision on application

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The Division must without delay notify the applicant,
  the person who is the subject of the appeal and the Minister in
  writing of its decision on the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing documents

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} If the Division allows the application, it must
  without delay provide the interested person with a copy of the
  following documents as soon as they are available:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Refugee Protection Division record;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the notice of appeal, appellant's record, notice of
    intent to respond, respondent's record, reply record, Minister's
    notice of intervention, Minister's intervention record, if any,
    Minister's reply, and Minister's reply record, if any; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the written submissions of any other interested
    person and the UNHCR.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- written submissions

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} The interested person's written submissions must not
  raise new issues.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Length of written submissions

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} The interested person's written submissions must not
  be more than 30 pages long if typewritten on one side or 15 pages if
  typewritten on both sides.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing written submissions

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} The interested person's written submissions must
  first be provided to the person who is the subject of the appeal and
  to the Minister and then to the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof written submissions provided

  [(13)]{.lawlabel} The written submissions provided to the Division
  must be accompanied by proof that they were provided to the person who
  is the subject of the appeal and to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

  [(14)]{.lawlabel} The person who is the subject of the appeal or the
  Minister may respond to the written submissions in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- response

  [(15)]{.lawlabel} A response must not raise new issues.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Length of response

  [(16)]{.lawlabel} A response must not be more than 30 pages long if
  typewritten on one side or 15 pages if typewritten on both sides.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing response

  [(17)]{.lawlabel} The response must first be provided to the
  interested person, then to the person who is the subject of the appeal
  or to the Minister, as the case may be, and then to the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof response provided

  [(18)]{.lawlabel} The response provided to the Division must be
  accompanied by proof that it was provided to the interested person,
  and to the person who is the subject of the appeal or to the Minister,
  as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(19)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under subrules (17) and (18) must
  be received by their recipients no later than seven days after the day
  on which the person who is the subject of the appeal or the Minister,
  as the case may be, receives the interested person's written
  submissions.

### [Withdrawal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787389 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Abuse of process

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of subsection 168(2) of the Act, withdrawal of an appeal
  is an abuse of process if withdrawal would likely have a negative
  effect on the Division's integrity. If the requirements set out in
  rule 7 or 13, as the case may be, for deciding an appeal on the basis
  of the materials provided have not been met, withdrawal is not an
  abuse of process.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal on notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the requirements set out in rule 7 or 13, as the
  case may be, for deciding an appeal have not been met, an appellant
  may withdraw an appeal by notifying the Division in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to withdraw

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the requirements set out in rule 7 or 13, as the
  case may be, for deciding an appeal have been met, an appellant who
  wants to withdraw an appeal must make an application to the Division
  in accordance with rule 37.

### [Reinstating a Withdrawn Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787397 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to reinstate withdrawn appeal

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appellant may apply to the Division to reinstate an appeal that was
  made by the appellant and was withdrawn.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appellant must make the application in accordance
  with rule 37. If a person who is the subject of an appeal makes the
  application, they must provide to the Division the original and a copy
  of the application and include in the application their contact
  information and, if represented by counsel, their counsel's contact
  information and any limitations on counsel's retainer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents provided to Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division must provide to the Minister, without
  delay, a copy of an application made by a person who is the subject of
  an appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Division must not allow the application unless it
  is established that there was a failure to observe a principle of
  natural justice or it is otherwise in the interests of justice to
  allow the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In deciding the application, the Division must
  consider any relevant factors, including whether the application was
  made in a timely manner and the justification for any delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent application

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the appellant made a previous application to
  reinstate an appeal that was denied, the Division must consider the
  reasons for the denial and must not allow the subsequent application
  unless there are exceptional circumstances supported by new evidence.

### [Reopening an Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787411 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to reopen appeal

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At any
  time before the Federal Court has made a final determination in
  respect of an appeal that has been decided or declared abandoned, the
  appellant may make an application to the Division to reopen the
  appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be made in accordance with
  rule 37. If a person who is the subject of an appeal makes the
  application, they must provide to the Division the original and a copy
  of the application and include in the application their contact
  information and, if represented by counsel, their counsel's contact
  information and any limitations on counsel's retainer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents provided to Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division must provide to the Minister, without
  delay, a copy of an application made by a person who is the subject of
  an appeal .

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allegations against counsel

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If it is alleged in the application that the person
  who is the subject of the appeal's counsel in the proceedings that are
  the subject of the application provided inadequate representation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person must first provide a copy of the
    application to the counsel and then provide the original and a copy
    of the application to the Division, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application provided to the Division must be
    accompanied by proof that a copy was provided to the counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of pending application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The application must be accompanied by a copy of any
  pending application for leave to apply for judicial review or any
  pending application for judicial review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factor

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Division must not allow the application unless it
  is established that there was a failure to observe a principle of
  natural justice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In deciding the application, the Division must
  consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the application was made in a timely manner
    and the justification for any delay; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the appellant did not make an application for
    leave to apply for judicial review or an application for judicial
    review, the reasons why an application was not made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent application

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If the appellant made a previous application to
  reopen an appeal that was denied, the Division must consider the
  reasons for the denial and must not allow the subsequent application
  unless there are exceptional circumstances supported by new evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other remedies

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} If there is a pending application for leave to apply
  for judicial review or a pending application for judicial review on
  the same or similar grounds, the Division must, as soon as is
  practicable, allow the application to reopen if it is necessary for
  the timely and efficient processing of appeals, or dismiss the
  application.

### [Decisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787435 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  the Division makes a decision, other than an interlocutory decision,
  it must provide in writing a notice of decision to the person who is
  the subject of the appeal, to the Minister and to the Refugee
  Protection Division. The Division must also provide in writing a
  notice of decision to the UNHCR and to any interested person, if they
  provided written submissions in the appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written reasons

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division must provide written reasons for the
  decision, together with the notice of decision, if a hearing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} was not held under subsection 110(6) of the Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} was held under subsection 110(6) of the Act and the
    decision and reasons were not given orally at the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for written reasons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A request under paragraph 169(1)(e) of the Act for
  written reasons for a decision must be made in writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When decision of single member takes
effect

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  decision, other than an interlocutory decision, made by a single
  Division member takes effect

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if made in writing, when the member signs and dates
    the reasons for the decision; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if given orally at a hearing, when the member
    states the decision and gives the reasons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When decision of three-member panel
  takes effect

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A decision, other than an interlocutory decision,
  made by a panel of three Division members takes effect

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if made in writing, when all the members sign and
    date their reasons for the decision; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if given orally at a hearing, when all the members
    state their decision and give their reasons.

### [General Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787454 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No applicable rule

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** In the absence of a
provision in these Rules dealing with a matter raised during the
proceedings, the Division may do whatever is necessary to deal with the
matter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Division

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** The Division may, after
giving the parties notice and an opportunity to object,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} act on its own initiative, without a party having to
  make an application or request to the Division;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} change a requirement of a rule;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} excuse a person from a requirement of a rule; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} extend a time limit, before or after the time limit
  has expired, or shorten it if the time limit has not expired.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to follow rules

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** Unless proceedings are
declared invalid by the Division, a failure to follow any requirement of
these Rules does not make the proceedings invalid.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Rules Applicable to an Appeal for Which a Hearing Is Held]{.HTitleText1} {#h-787465 .Part}

### [Fixing a Date for a Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787466 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conference to fix date for hearing

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** The Division may require
the parties to participate in a scheduling conference or otherwise give
information to help the Division fix a date for a hearing.

### [Notice to Appear]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787469 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to appear

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When,
  in accordance with paragraph 171(a) of the Act, the Division gives
  notice to the person who is the subject of the appeal and to the
  Minister of any hearing, it must notify them in writing of the date,
  time and location fixed for the hearing and the issues that will be
  raised at the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date fixed for hearing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The date fixed for the hearing of an appeal must not
  be earlier than 10 days after the day on which the person who is the
  subject of the appeal and the Minister receive the notice referred to
  in subrule (1), unless they consent to an earlier date.

### [Conduct of a Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787475 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction of hearing

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  hearing is restricted to matters relating to the issues provided with
  the notice to appear unless the Division considers that other issues
  have been raised by statements made by the person who is the subject
  of the appeal or by a witness during the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standard order of questioning

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the Division orders otherwise, any witness,
  including the person who is the subject of the appeal, will be
  questioned first by the appellant, then by any other party, then by
  the appellant in reply, and then by the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limiting questioning of witnesses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division may limit the questioning of witnesses,
  including the person who is the subject of the appeal, taking into
  account the nature and complexity of the issues and the relevance of
  the questions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral representations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Representations must be made orally at the end of a
  hearing unless the Division orders otherwise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limits on representations

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} After all the evidence has been heard, the Division
  must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set time limits for representations, taking into
    account the complexity of the issues and the amount of relevant
    evidence heard; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} indicate what issues need to be addressed in the
    representations.

### [Person Who Is the Subject of an Appeal in Custody]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787489 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Custody

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** The Division may order a
person who holds a person who is the subject of an appeal in custody to
bring the person to a proceeding at a location specified by the
Division.

### [Interpreters]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787492 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Need for interpreter --- person

- **[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person who is the subject of an appeal needs an interpreter, the
  person must indicate the language and dialect, if any, to be
  interpreted in the appellant's record if they are the appellant or in
  the respondent's record if they are the respondent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changing language of interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who is the subject of an appeal may change
  the language and dialect, if any, that they specified under
  subrule (1), or if they had not indicated that an interpreter was
  needed, they may indicate that they need an interpreter, by notifying
  the Division in writing and indicating the language and dialect, if
  any, to be interpreted. The notice must be received by the Division no
  later than 20 days before the date fixed for the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Need for interpreter --- witness

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If any party's witness needs an interpreter for a
  hearing, the party must notify the Division in writing and specify the
  language and dialect, if any, to be interpreted. The notice must be
  received by the Division no later than 20 days before the date fixed
  for the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpreter's oath

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The interpreter must take an oath or make a solemn
  affirmation to interpret accurately.

### [Observers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787502 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Observers

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application under rule 42 is not necessary if an observer is the UNHCR
  or a member of the staff of the Board or if the person who is the
  subject of the appeal consents to or requests the presence of an
  observer other than a representative of the press or other media of
  communication at the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Observers --- factor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division must allow the attendance of an observer
  unless, in the opinion of the Division, the observer's attendance is
  likely to impede the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Observers --- confidentiality of
  proceeding

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division may take any measures it considers
  necessary to ensure the confidentiality of the proceeding despite the
  presence of an observer.

### [Witnesses]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787510 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing witness information

- **[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  party wants to call a witness, the party must provide the following
  witness information in writing to any other party and to the Division:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the witness's contact information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a brief statement of the purpose and substance of
    the witness's testimony or, in the case of an expert witness, the
    expert witness's brief signed summary of the testimony to be given;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the time needed for the witness's testimony;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the party's relationship to the witness;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an expert witness, a description of
    the expert witness's qualifications; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} whether the party wants the witness to testify by
    means of live telecommunication.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof witness information provided

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The witness information provided to the Division must
  be accompanied by proof that it was provided to any other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under this rule must be received
  by their recipients no later than 20 days before the date fixed for
  the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to provide witness information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a party does not provide the witness information,
  the witness must not testify at the hearing unless the Division allows
  them to testify.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In deciding whether to allow a witness to testify,
  the Division must consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the relevance and probative value of the proposed
    testimony; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reason why the witness information was not
    provided.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requesting summons

- **[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party who wants the Division to order a person to testify at a hearing
  must make a request to the Division for a summons, either orally at a
  proceeding or in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In deciding whether to issue a summons, the Division
  must consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the necessity of the testimony to a full and proper
    hearing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person's ability to give that testimony; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the person has agreed to be summoned as a
    witness.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Using summons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a party wants to use a summons, they must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide the summons to the person by hand;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide a copy of the summons to the Division,
    together with proof that it was provided to the person by hand; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} pay or offer to pay the person the applicable
    witness fees and travel expenses set out in Tariff A of the [Federal
    Courts Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancelling summons

- **[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person who is summoned to appear as a witness wants the summons
  cancelled, the person must make an application in writing to the
  Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person must make the application in accordance
  with rule 37, but is not required to give evidence in an affidavit or
  statutory declaration.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arrest warrant

- **[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person does not obey a summons to appear as a witness, the party who
  requested the summons may make a request to the Division orally at the
  hearing, or in writing, to issue a warrant for the person's arrest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party who makes a written request for a warrant
  must provide supporting evidence by affidavit or statutory
  declaration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for issue of arrest
  warrant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division must not issue a warrant unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person was provided the summons by hand or the
    person is avoiding being provided the summons;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person was paid or offered the applicable
    witness fees and travel expenses set out in Tariff A of the [Federal
    Courts Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person did not appear at the hearing as
    required by the summons; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person's testimony is still needed for a full
    and proper hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of warrant

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A warrant issued by the Division for the arrest of a
  person must include directions concerning detention or release.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excluded witness

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** If the Division excludes
a witness from a hearing room, no person may communicate to the witness
any evidence given while the witness was excluded unless allowed to do
so by the Division or until the witness has finished testifying.

### [Changing the Location of a Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787563 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to change location

- **[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may make an application to the Division to change the location
  of a hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party must make the application in accordance
  with rule 37, but is not required to give evidence in an affidavit or
  statutory declaration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under this rule must be received
  by their recipients no later than 20 days before the date fixed for
  the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In deciding the application, the Division must
  consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the party is residing in the location where
    the party wants the hearing to be held;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether a change of location would allow the
    hearing to be full and proper;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether a change of location would likely delay the
    hearing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} how a change of location would affect the
    Division's operation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} how a change of location would affect the parties;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} whether a change of location is necessary in order
    to accommodate a vulnerable person; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} whether a hearing may be conducted by means of live
    telecommunication with the person who is the subject of the appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to appear

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Unless a party receives a decision from the Division
  allowing the application, the party must appear for the hearing at the
  location fixed and be ready to start or continue the hearing.

### [Changing the Date or Time of a Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787582 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to change date or time

- **[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may make an application to the Division to change the date or
  time fixed for a hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make the application in accordance with rule 37,
    but is not required to give evidence in an affidavit or statutory
    declaration; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give at least six dates and times, within the
    period specified by the Division, on which the party is available to
    start or continue the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of period specified by Division

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division must provide notice of the period
  referred to in paragraph (2)(b) in a manner that will allow public
  access to it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing two working days or less away

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the party wants to make an application two working
  days or less before the date fixed for the hearing, the party must
  make the application orally on the date fixed for the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In deciding the application, the Division must
  consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a date and time that was fixed after
    the Division consulted or tried to consult the party, any
    exceptional circumstances for allowing the application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the party made the application;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the time the party has had to prepare for the
    hearing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the efforts made by the party to be ready to start
    or continue the hearing;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a party who requests more time to
    obtain information in support of their arguments, the Division's
    ability to proceed in the absence of that information without
    causing an injustice;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} whether the party has counsel;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the knowledge and experience of any counsel who
    represents the party;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any previous delays and the reasons for them;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} whether the date and time fixed were peremptory;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} whether the change is required to accommodate a
    vulnerable person;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} whether allowing the application would unreasonably
    delay the hearing or likely cause an injustice; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the nature and complexity of the matter to be
    heard.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent application

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the party made a previous application that was
  denied, the Division must consider the reasons for the denial and must
  not allow the subsequent application unless there are exceptional
  circumstances supported by new evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for medical reasons

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If a person who is the subject of an appeal makes the
  application for medical reasons, other than those related to their
  counsel, they must provide, together with the application, a legible,
  recently dated medical certificate signed by a qualified medical
  practitioner whose name and address are printed or stamped on the
  certificate. A person who has provided a copy of the certificate to
  the Division must provide the original document to the Division
  without delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of certificate

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The medical certificate must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the particulars of the medical condition, without
    specifying the diagnosis, that prevent the person from participating
    in the hearing on the date fixed for the hearing; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the person is expected to be able
    to participate in the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to provide medical certificate

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} If a person who is the subject of an appeal fails to
  provide a medical certificate in accordance with subrules (7) and (8),
  the person must include in their application

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} particulars of any efforts they made to obtain the
    required medical certificate, supported by corroborating evidence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} particulars of the medical reasons for the
    application, supported by corroborating evidence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of how the medical condition
    prevents them from participating in the hearing on the date fixed
    for the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to appear

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} Unless a party receives a decision from the Division
  allowing the application, the party must appear for the hearing at the
  date and time fixed and be ready to start or continue the hearing.

### [Abandonment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-787623 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Abandonment after hearing scheduled

- **[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  determining whether an appeal has been abandoned under
  subsection 168(1) of the Act after a date for a hearing has been
  fixed, the Division must give the appellant an opportunity to explain
  why the appeal should not be declared abandoned,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immediately, if the appellant is present at the
    hearing and the Division considers that it is fair to do so; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, by way of a special hearing,
    after notifying the appellant in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to consider

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division must consider, in deciding if the appeal
  should be declared abandoned, the explanation given by the appellant
  and any other relevant factors, including the fact that the appellant
  is ready to start or continue the proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Medical reasons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the appellant is the person who is the subject of
  the appeal and the explanation includes medical reasons, other than
  those related to their counsel, they must provide, together with the
  explanation, the original of a legible, recently dated medical
  certificate signed by a qualified medical practitioner whose name and
  address are printed or stamped on the certificate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of certificate

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The medical certificate must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the particulars of the medical condition, without
    specifying the diagnosis, that prevented the person from pursuing
    their appeal; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the person is expected to be able
    to pursue their appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to provide medical certificate

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a person who is the subject of an appeal fails to
  provide a medical certificate in accordance with subrules (3) and (4),
  the person must include in their explanation

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} particulars of any efforts they made to obtain the
    required medical certificate, supported by corroborating evidence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} particulars of the medical reasons included in the
    explanation, supported by corroborating evidence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of how the medical condition
    prevented them from pursuing their appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Start or continue proceedings

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the Division decides not to declare the appeal
  abandoned, it must start or continue the proceedings without delay.
