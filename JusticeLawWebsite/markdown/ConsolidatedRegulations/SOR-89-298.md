## Bank of Canada Notes Regulations {#bank-of-canada-notes-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-298
:::

[BANK OF CANADA ACT](/eng/acts/B-2)

Registration 1989-06-08

Regulations Respecting Notes Issued by the Bank of Canada

P.C. 1989-1086 1989-06-08
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 25(3) of the [Bank of
Canada Act](/eng/acts/B-2), is pleased hereby to revoke Regulation No. 1
made by Order in Council P.C. 2752 of October 28, 1936[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, and to make the annexed
[Regulations respecting notes issued by the Bank of
Canada](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-298), in substitution therefor,
effective July 1, 1989.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA){#fn_1e}Not
  published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919294 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Bank of Canada Notes
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-298).

## [Notes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919296 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Notes issued by the Bank of
Canada shall be in the following denominations:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/96-122, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} five dollars;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} ten dollars;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} twenty dollars;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fifty dollars; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} one hundred dollars.

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2000-174, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-122, s. 1
- SOR/2000-174, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The notes referred to in
section 2 shall bear facsimiles of the signatures of the Governor in
office at the time the notes are printed and of the Deputy Governor
appointed under subsection 6(1) of the [Bank of Canada
Act](/eng/acts/B-2) in office at the time the notes are printed, or in
the event that the Deputy Governor is absent or unable to act or the
Deputy Governor's office is vacant, the signature of an additional
Deputy Governor appointed under subsection 7(1) of that Act.
