## Kapuskasing Airport Zoning Regulations {#kapuskasing-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-100
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1987-02-19

Zoning Regulations Respecting Kapuskasing Airport

P.C. 1987-281 1987-02-19
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 4.5[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EHBBA} of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed [Zoning regulations
respecting Kapuskasing Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-100),
substantially in the form set out in the schedule hereto, was published
in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on February 8,
1986 and a reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested
persons to make representations to the Minister of Transport with
respect thereto;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EHBBA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1985, c. 28, s. 1
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to
section 4.4[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFAAA} of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Zoning Regulations respecting Kapuskasing
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-100).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900697 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Kapuskasing Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-100).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900699 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Kapuskasing Airport, in
      the Town of Kapuskasing, in the District of Cochrane, in the
      Province of Ontario; ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part II of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway prepared for the
      take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction, which
      strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the schedule;
      ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the assigned
  elevation of the airport reference point is 221.0 m above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900710 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, which lands are more particularly described in
Part VI of the schedule.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900712 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900717 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in paragraphs 4(a) to (c), the Minister
may require that the owner or occupier of the land on which that object
is growing remove the growth or the excessive portion thereof.

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900719 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit that land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste that is edible by or
attractive to birds.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point, shown on Kapuskasing Airport Zoning Plan
No. 56-090-83-26, Sheet 5, dated October 24, 1984, is a point distant
100 m measured southwesterly at right angles to the centre line of
runway 17-35, from a point distant 900 m measured northwesterly along
the said centre line from the southeasterly end of said runway.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Kapuskasing Airport Zoning Plan
No. 56-090-83-26, Sheets 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 11, 12 and 13 dated
October 24, 1984, are surfaces abutting each end of the strips
associated with the runways designated 17-35 and 10-28 and are described
as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 17 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 35 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 10 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 2 500 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 280 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 62.5 m measured vertically above the elevation
  at the end of the strip; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 28 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip distant 2 500 m measured horizontally from
  the end of the strip, the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal line
  being 280 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary horizontal
  line being 62.5 m measured vertically above the elevation at the end
  of the strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Kapuskasing Airport Zoning Plan
No. 56-090-83-26, Sheets 1 to 9, dated October 24, 1984, is an imaginary
surface located at a common plane established at a constant elevation of
45 m above the assigned elevation of the airport reference point, except
where that common plane is less than 9 m above the surface of the
ground, in which case the imaginary surface is located at 9 m above the
surface of the ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strips]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strips .SchedHeadL1}

The strips, as shown on Kapuskasing Airport Zoning Plan
No. 56-090-83-26, Sheet 5, dated October 24, 1984, are described as
follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 10-28, being 60 m in
  width, 30 m on each side of the centre line of the runway, and 1 260 m
  in length; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 17-35, being 300 m
  in width, 150 m on each side of the centre line of the runway, and
  1 800 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Kapuskasing Airport Zoning Plan
No. 56-090-83-26, Sheets 2, 4, 5, 6 and 8, dated October 24, 1984, is a
surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at a ratio of 1 m
measured vertically to 7 m measured horizontally at right angles to the
centre line and centre line produced of each strip extending upward and
outward from the lateral limits of each strip and its approach surfaces
to an intersection with the outer surface or another transitional
surface of an adjoining strip.
