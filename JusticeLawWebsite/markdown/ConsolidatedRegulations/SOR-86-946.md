## Abatement of Duties Payable Regulations {#abatement-of-duties-payable-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-946
:::

[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

Registration 1986-09-11

Regulations Respecting the Abatement of Duties on Imported Goods that
Have Suffered Damage, Deterioration or Destruction or a Loss in Volume
or Weight

P.C. 1986-2067 1986-09-11
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas a copy of proposed regulations, substantially in the form
annexed hereto, was published in Part I of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) on March 15, 1986 and a reasonable
opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to make
representations with respect thereto to the Minister of National
Revenue;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to
subsection 75(1) and section 81 of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EGBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the abatement of
duties on imported goods that have suffered damage, deterioration or
destruction or a loss in volume or weight](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-946),
effective on the coming into force of subsection 75(1) and section 81 of
the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EBBAA}.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899190 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualified appraiser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualified appraiser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who, in
    relation to any goods, is qualified by virtue of his business,
    occupation or profession to appraise those goods and to appraise the
    loss in value thereto where the goods have suffered damage,
    deterioration or destruction. ([appréciateur
    qualifié]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Determination of Amount of Abatement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899194 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of an abatement of duties that may be granted on imported goods
  that have suffered damage, deterioration or destruction from the time
  of shipment to Canada to the time of release shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of perishable goods or brittle goods
    such as crockery, china, glass and glassware, an amount equal to
    that proportion of the duties otherwise payable on the goods that 85
    per cent of the loss in value of the goods is of the value for duty
    of the goods;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of sugar or any other saccharine
    product on which duty is determined according to the polarimetric
    test and that has suffered damage or deterioration from salt water,
    an amount equal to the difference between

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the duties otherwise payable on the goods, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the duties that would be payable on the goods
      if, after the percentage of polarization of the goods is
      determined, there is deducted from that percentage an amount equal
      to five times the percentage of salt present in that portion of
      the water found in the damaged goods that is in excess of the
      water found in samples of undamaged goods, as certified by an
      officer authorized to test such samples; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other goods, an amount equal to
    that proportion of the duties otherwise payable on the goods that
    the loss in value of the goods is of the value for duty of the
    goods.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The determination of the loss in value of goods shall
  be made by a qualified appraiser.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the amount of an abatement of duties that may be
  granted on imported bulk wines and spirits that have suffered a loss
  in volume or weight arising from natural causes while in a bonded
  warehouse shall be an amount equal to that proportion of the duties
  otherwise payable on the goods that the loss in volume or weight is of
  the volume or weight of the goods when originally warehoused.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No abatement of duties shall be granted under
  subsection (1) in respect of any loss in volume or weight in excess of
  one sixth of one per cent of the original volume or weight multiplied
  by the number of months that have elapsed since the date of original
  warehousing or eight per cent of the original volume or weight,
  whichever is the lesser.

## [Abatement Not to Be Granted]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899206 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No abatement of duties
shall be granted in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods for which there is a manufacturer's or
  producer's recommended shelf life period or allowable
  storage-before-use period if the goods have suffered damage or
  deterioration by reason of the expiration of that period;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} iron or steel or any manufacture thereof that has
  suffered damage or deterioration by reason of rust; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} packages of liquids that have suffered damage or
  deterioration, where the damage or deterioration is confined to the
  package and does not extend to the liquid contents thereof.
