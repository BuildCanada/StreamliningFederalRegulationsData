## Protection of Passenger Information Regulations {#protection-of-passenger-information-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2005-346
:::

[IMMIGRATION AND REFUGEE PROTECTION ACT](/eng/acts/I-2.5)

Registration 2005-11-21

Protection of Passenger Information Regulations

P.C. 2005-2038 2005-11-21
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 5(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EGCBA} of the
[Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EDCBA}, the Minister
of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness caused a copy of the
proposed [Protection of Passenger Information
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-346), substantially in the form
set out in the annexed Regulations, to be laid before each House of
Parliament;

Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-346).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-720765 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Immigration and Refugee
    Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[advance passenger information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[advance passenger information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2016-38, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Border Services
    Agency. ([Agence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in section 2 of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227). ([véhicule
    commercial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[day of departure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[day of departure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a commercial vehicle that carries
      persons or goods by air, the day of take-off from the last point
      of embarkation of persons before the vehicle arrives in Canada;
      and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a commercial vehicle that carries
      persons or goods by water or land, the day of departure from the
      last point of embarkation of persons before the vehicle arrives in
      Canada. ([jour du départ]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[enforcement database]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[enforcement database]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2016-38, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[intelligence official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[intelligence official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2016-38, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[passenger name record information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[passenger name record information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    information that is referred to in paragraph 269(1)(e) of the
    [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227) and is provided to the
    Agency. ([renseignements sur le dossier passager]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[PAXIS system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[PAXIS system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2016-38, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[serious transnational crime]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[serious transnational crime]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an act or
    omission that constitutes an offence punishable in Canada by a
    maximum term of imprisonment of at least four years and that is
    committed

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in more than one country;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in only one country but a substantial part of its
      preparation, planning, direction or control takes place in another
      country;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in only one country but an organized criminal
      group that engages in criminal activities in more than one country
      is implicated in the act or omission;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in only one country but has substantial effects
      in another country; or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in a country other than Canada but the offender
      intends to travel to or transit through Canada. ([crime
      transnational grave]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[terrorism offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[terrorism offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an act or omission that is committed for a
      political, religious or ideological purpose, objective or cause
      with the intention of intimidating the public with regard to its
      security, including its economic security, or with the intention
      of compelling a person, government or domestic or international
      organization to do or refrain from doing any act, and that is
      committed with the intention to

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} cause death or serious bodily harm,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} endanger a person's life,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} cause a serious risk to the health or safety
        of the public,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} cause substantial property damage that is
        likely to result in the harm referred to in any of
        subparagraphs (i) to (iii), or

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} cause serious interference with or serious
        disruption of an essential service, facility or system other
        than as a result of lawful or unlawful advocacy, protest,
        dissent or stoppage of work, such as a strike, that is not
        intended to result in the harm referred to in any of
        subparagraphs (i) to (iii);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an act or omission referred to in paragraph (a)
      of the definition terrorist activity in subsection 83.01(1) of the
      [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} knowingly participating in or contributing to an
      activity for the purpose of enhancing a terrorist group's ability
      to facilitate or commit an act or omission referred to in
      paragraph (a) or (b) or instructing a person, group or
      organization to carry out an activity for that purpose;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an indictable offence if the act or omission that
      constitutes the offence is committed for the benefit of, at the
      direction of or in association with a terrorist group;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any of the following, if they are carried out for
      the purpose of committing an act or omission referred to in
      paragraph (a) or (b):

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} collecting, using or possessing property,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} providing or making available property or a
        financial or related service, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} inviting a person, group or organization to
        provide property or a financial or related service;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} attempting or threatening to commit an act or
      omission referred to in paragraph (a) or (b);

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} conspiring to commit, or facilitating,
      instructing or counselling the commission of, an act or omission
      referred to in paragraph (a) or (b);

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} being an accessory after the fact to an act or
      omission referred to in paragraph (a) or (b); or

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} harbouring or concealing for the purpose of
      enabling a terrorist group to facilitate or commit an act or
      omission referred to in paragraph (a) or (b). ([infraction de
      terrorisme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[terrorist group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[terrorist group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 83.01(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([groupe
    terroriste]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[time of departure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[time of departure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a commercial vehicle that carries
      persons or goods by air, the time of take-off from the last point
      of embarkation of persons before the vehicle arrives in Canada;
      and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a commercial vehicle that carries
      persons or goods by water or land, the time of departure from the
      last point of embarkation of persons before the vehicle arrives in
      Canada. ([moment du départ]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-38, s. 1

## [Passenger Name Record Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-720808 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agency may use passenger name record information only in accordance
  with these Regulations for the purposes of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Agency may retain passenger name record
  information only in accordance with these Regulations for the purposes
  of the Act or for the purposes of program legislation as defined in
  section 2 of the [Canada Border Services Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-1.4).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Agency may disclose passenger name record
  information only in accordance with these Regulations for the purposes
  of the Act, national security, the defence of Canada or the conduct of
  international affairs.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-38, s. 2

## [Retention, Use and Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-720815 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agency may retain the passenger name record information provided about
  a person until the day that is three years and six months after the
  day of departure of the commercial vehicle that carried or was
  expected to carry the person to Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} After the period referred to in subsection (1), the
  Agency may retain the passenger name record information provided about
  a person who was or was expected to be carried to Canada, if the
  information is required to identify persons who are reasonably
  suspected of having committed a terrorism offence or serious
  transnational crime, but only for as long as the information is
  required for that purpose and in no case longer than six years after
  the day of departure of the commercial vehicle that carried or was
  expected to carry the person to Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-38, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) to (5), an official of the Agency may, for the
  following purposes, have access to any passenger name record
  information that is retained:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to identify persons who have or may have committed
    a terrorism offence or a serious transnational crime; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to conduct a trend analysis or develop risk
    indicators for the purpose referred to in paragraph (a).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} During the period that begins 72 hours after the time
  of departure of a commercial vehicle and ends two years after its day
  of departure, an official of the Agency may have access to the name of
  any person who was or was expected to be on board only for the purpose
  referred to in paragraph (1)(a) and only if that official confirms
  that the information is necessary for that purpose.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} During the period that begins the day after the last
  day of the period referred to in subsection (2) and ends three years
  and six months after the day of departure, an official of the Agency
  may have access to the passenger name record information provided
  about a person who was or was expected to be on board a commercial
  vehicle and could serve to identify any such person only if access is
  authorized by the President of the Agency for the purpose of
  identifying a person who is reasonably suspected of having committed a
  terrorism offence or serious transnational crime.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} During the period that the passenger name record
  information provided about a person who was or was expected to be on
  board a commercial vehicle is retained under subsection 3(2), an
  official of the Agency may have access to that information only for
  the purpose referred to in paragraph (1)(a).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, an official of the
  Agency may have access to passenger name record information only if
  their functions require it.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If access referred to in subsection (3) to the
  passenger name record information is authorized by the President of
  the Agency, the following information must be recorded in a register
  kept by the Agency for that purpose and that information must be
  retained for a period of at least two years:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the official who has requested access;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for the request;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person who is the subject of the
    request; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the request was made, the date on
    which authorization was granted by the President and the date on
    which the information is accessed.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** If passenger name record
information must be retained under the [Access to Information
Act](/eng/acts/A-1) or the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21) for a period
longer than the period referred to in subsection 3(1) or (2), as the
case may be, during that longer period the information may be accessed
only for a purpose for which it is required to be retained.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-38, s. 2

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Agency may, on a
case-by-case basis, when necessary for the purposes of the Act, national
security, the defence of Canada or the conduct of international affairs,
disclose passenger name record information to a federal or provincial
government department or another federal or provincial government
authority if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} there are reasonable grounds to believe that the
  information would be relevant to the prevention, investigation or
  prosecution of a terrorism offence or serious transnational crime;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the department or authority exercises functions
  directly related to the prevention, detection, investigation or
  prosecution of terrorism offences or serious transnational crimes;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the department or authority has undertaken to apply
  standards to protect the information that are at least equivalent to
  those set out in these Regulations;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the department or authority has undertaken not to
  further disclose the information without the permission of the Agency,
  unless required by law to do so; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Agency discloses only the information that is
  necessary for the purposes for which it is disclosed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-38, s. 2
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in these Regulations prevents the Agency from disclosing
passenger name record information to comply with a subpoena, warrant or
order issued by a court, person or body with jurisdiction in Canada to
compel the disclosure of the information.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-38, s. 2

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Agency may, on a
case-by-case basis, when necessary for the purposes of the Act, national
security, the defence of Canada or the conduct of international affairs,
disclose passenger name record information to a foreign government
authority under an international agreement or arrangement if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} there are reasonable grounds to believe that the
  information would be relevant to the prevention, investigation or
  prosecution of a terrorism offence or serious transnational crime;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the authority exercises functions directly related to
  the prevention, detection, investigation or prosecution of a terrorism
  offence or serious transnational crime;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the authority has undertaken to apply standards to
  protect the information that are at least equivalent to those set out
  in these Regulations or the standards designed to protect passenger
  name record information that were negotiated by that authority with
  the European Union; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Agency discloses only the information that is
  necessary for the purposes for which it is disclosed.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If the Agency discloses
passenger name record information under sections 6 to 8, the following
information must be recorded in a register kept by the Agency for that
purpose and that information must be retained for a period of at least
two years:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person to whom the information is
  disclosed and the government department or authority where they are
  employed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for the disclosure;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person who is the subject of the
  disclosure; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the information was disclosed.
