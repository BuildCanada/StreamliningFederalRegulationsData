## Officers Authorized to Exercise Powers or Perform Duties of the Minister of National Revenue Regulations {#officers-authorized-to-exercise-powers-or-perform-duties-of-the-minister-of-national-revenue-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-1066
:::

[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

Registration 1986-11-06

Regulations Authorizing a Designated Officer or Class of Officers to
Exercise Powers or Perform Duties of the Minister of National Revenue
Under the Customs Act

P.C. 1986-2486 1986-11-06
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 164(3) of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFABA}, a copy of
proposed [Regulations authorizing a designated officer or class of
officers to exercise powers or perform duties of the Minister of
National Revenue under the Customs Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1066),
substantially in the form annexed hereto, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on June 28, 1986 and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations to the Minister of National Revenue with respect
thereto;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to
paragraph 164(1)(a) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Regulations authorizing a designated officer
or class of officers to exercise powers or perform duties of the
Minister of National Revenue under the Customs
Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1066), effective on the day that
paragraph 164(1)(a) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) comes into
force.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-889276 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to an
    area or place, means the manager of the customs office or customs
    offices that serve that area or place; ([agent en chef des
    douanes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[region]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[region]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following regions
    for which there is a regional office of the Department of National
    Revenue, namely,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Atlantic;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Quebec;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Northern Ontario;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Southern Ontario;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Prairie; and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} Pacific. ([région]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[regional collector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regional collector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the manager of the
    customs offices within a region. ([receveur
    régional]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- SOR/97-335, s. 1

## [Delegation of the Powers and Duties of the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-889290 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An officer holding the
position of Deputy Minister of National Revenue for Customs and Excise,
or a person authorized to perform the duties of that position, may
exercise all the powers and perform all the duties of the Minister under
the Act, other than those powers and duties described in
subsections 9(1) and (2), paragraphs 9(5)(h) and 24(1)(c),
subsection 51(3), sections 59 and 105, subsections 106(3) and 109(1) and
sections 134, 149 and 165 of the Act.

position of assistant deputy minister of the Department of National
Revenue, Customs and Excise, or a person authorized to perform the

duties of the Minister under the Act, other than those powers and duties
described in subsections 9(1) and (2), paragraphs 9(5)(h) and 24(1)(c),
subsection 51(3), sections 59 and 105, subsections 106(3) and 109(1) and
sections 134, 149 and 165 of the Act.

subsections 95(4), 97.2(1) and 99(4), paragraphs 142(1)(a) and (b),
section 147 and subsection 166(2) of the Act.
