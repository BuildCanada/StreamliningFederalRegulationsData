## Energy Conservation Equipment Exemption Regulations {#energy-conservation-equipment-exemption-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 590
:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Regulations Respecting the Application of Section 9 of Part XVIII of
Schedule III to the Excise Tax Act to Energy Conservation Equipment
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558166 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Energy Conservation Equipment Exemption
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._590).

## [Determination]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558168 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following equipment,
articles and materials are hereby prescribed, for the purposes of
section 9 of Part XVIII of Schedule III to the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15), to be energy conservation equipment:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} thermal insulation designed for pipes, ducts,
  boilers, tanks and wrapping materials designed exclusively for use
  with such insulation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} wood-burning stoves, wood-burning stove kits,
  wood-burning furnaces and wood-burning space heaters other than

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} fireplaces, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} stoves, furnaces or space heaters that burn any
    oil or gas;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/79-716, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} wind deflectors designed to reduce fuel consumption
  and for installation on motor vehicles and trailers;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} waterwheels for converting water power to mechanical
  or electrical energy and pumps and generators specifically designed
  for use with such waterwheels;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} loading dock door seals and shelters designed to
  conserve heated or refrigerated air during loading and unloading; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} power factor correction capacitors.
