## Exempt Personal Information Bank Order, No. 5 (ND) {#exempt-personal-information-bank-order-no.-5-nd .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/85-38
:::

[PRIVACY ACT](/eng/acts/P-21)

Registration 1984-12-21

Order Respecting the Designation of the Security and Intelligence
Information Files, No. ND-P70, as an Exempt Personal Information Bank

P.C. 1984-4088 1984-12-20
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Defence and the Treasury Board, is pleased
hereby, pursuant to subsection 18(1) of the [Privacy
Act](/eng/acts/P-21)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_5896_hq_11617){#fn_5896_hq_11617-ID0EDBAA}, to
make the annexed Order respecting the designation of the Security and
Intelligence Information Files, No. ND-P70, as an exempt personal
information bank.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_5896_hq_11617-ID0EDBAA){#fn_5896_hq_11617}S.C.
  1980-81-82-83, c. 111, (Schedule II)
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886445 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Exempt Personal Information Bank Order, No. 5
(ND)](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-38).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886447 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The personal information
bank under the control of the Department of National Defence containing
the Security and Intelligence Information files No. ND-P70 is hereby
designated as an exempt bank on the basis of section 21 of the [Privacy
Act](/eng/acts/P-21).
