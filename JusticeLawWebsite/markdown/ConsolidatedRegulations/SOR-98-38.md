## Regulations Exempting Certain Goods From the Application of Subsection 118(1) of the Customs Tariff {#regulations-exempting-certain-goods-from-the-application-of-subsection-1181-of-the-customs-tariff .Title-of-Act}

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022784 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a medical emergency, a fire,
    a flood or any disaster that threatens life, property or the
    environment. ([urgence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022788 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Imported goods that, in
response to an emergency, are temporarily diverted to a use other than
that for which they were released are exempt from the application of
subsection 118(1) of the Act for the period during which the goods are
used in response to the emergency.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Imported goods that are
consumed in response to an emergency are exempt from the application of
subsection 118(1) of the Act.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022791 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on January 1, 1998.
