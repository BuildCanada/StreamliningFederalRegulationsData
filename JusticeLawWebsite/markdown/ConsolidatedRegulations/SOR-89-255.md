## [Small Retransmission System]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919189 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) to (4) and section 4, [[small retransmission
  system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a cable retransmission system, or a
  terrestrial retransmission system utilizing Hertzian waves, that
  retransmits a signal, with or without a fee, to not more than
  2,000 premises in the same service area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), where a cable
  retransmission system is included in the same unit as one or more
  other cable retransmission systems, the number of premises to which
  the cable retransmission system retransmits a signal is deemed to be
  equal to the total number of premises to which all cable
  retransmission systems included in that unit retransmit a signal.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (2), a cable
  retransmission system is included in the same unit as one or more
  other cable retransmission systems where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are owned or directly or indirectly controlled
    by the same person or group of persons; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} their service areas are each less than 5 km
    distant, at some point, from at least one other among them, and
    those service areas would constitute a series of contiguous service
    areas, in a linear or non-linear configuration, were it not for that
    distance.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply to a cable
  retransmission system that was included in a unit on December 31,
  1993.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-754, s. 1
- SOR/2005-147, s. 4
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The definition set out in
subsection 3(1) does not include a cable retransmission system that is a
master antenna system if it is located within the service area of
another cable retransmission system that retransmits a signal, with or
without a fee, to more than 2,000 premises in that service area.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-754, s. 1
- SOR/2005-147, s. 5
:::
