## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-723426 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an aerodrome in respect of
    which an airport certificate issued under section 302.03 of the
    [Canadian Aviation Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433) is in
    force. ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[airport operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an airport set out in Schedule 1,
      the Minister; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an airport set out in Schedule 2,
      the person or organization that has been issued an airport
      certificate under section 302.03 of the [Canadian Aviation
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433) in respect of the
      airport. ([exploitant de l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[land side]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[land side]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an airport, means
    an area that is on airport property, that is not intended to be used
    for activities related to aircraft operations and to which the
    non-travelling public has access. ([côté ville]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Transport. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[parking area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[parking area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any area on airport
    property designated by a sign as an area for parking
    vehicles. ([aire de stationnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[road]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[road]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a highway, street or work
    designed and intended for or used for the circulation of vehicles,
    excluding a parking area and a sidewalk. ([route]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[sign]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sign]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a sign, device or pavement
    marking communicating instructions that is erected or placed under
    the authority of the airport operator. ([panneau de
    signalisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an automobile, over-snow vehicle, truck,
      commercial passenger vehicle or any self-propelled vehicle or
      device and includes an air cushion vehicle as defined in section 2
      of the [Air Cushion Vehicle
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._4) but does not include a
      wheelchair or other similar device or an aircraft; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a bicycle or any cycle, regardless of the number
      of wheels it may have. ([véhicule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-723440 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of the land side of an airport set out in Schedule 1 or 2.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-723442 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The parking fine category
applicable at an airport set out in column 1 of an item of Schedule 2 is
the category set out in column 3 of the item.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Operation of Vehicles --- Vehicle Traffic and Parking]{.HTitleText1} {#h-723444 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  airport operator may, in order to ensure the safety of persons and
  goods and the efficient operation of the airport, erect or have
  erected or place or have placed signs

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} regulating

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the rate of speed of vehicles or classes of
      vehicles, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the load limit or dimensions for vehicles or
      classes of vehicles;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} regulating or prohibiting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the parking or stopping of vehicles or classes of
      vehicles, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the movement of vehicles or classes of vehicles;
      and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} designating

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} areas for the parking of vehicles or classes of
      vehicles,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} areas for the parking of vehicles operated by a
      person or a class of persons,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} areas for the loading or unloading of vehicles
      or classes of vehicles, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} roads as one-way roads.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An enforcement officer authorized under section 21
  may, for the purpose of ensuring the safety of persons and goods and
  the efficient operation of the airport, give instructions for
  directing or controlling traffic.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall operate a
vehicle at a rate of speed greater than

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the speed specified by the airport operator on a
  sign; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if no speed is specified by the airport operator,
  50 km/h.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No person shall operate a
vehicle unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person holds all licences, permits and
  certificates required by the laws of the province and the municipality
  in which the airport is located for the operation of the vehicle in
  that province and municipality; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle is registered and equipped as required by
  the laws of the province and the municipality in which the airport is
  located.

<!-- -->

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), no person shall operate a vehicle elsewhere than on
  a road or in a parking area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An airport operator may authorize a person to operate
  a vehicle elsewhere than on a road or in a parking area, subject to
  any conditions that are necessary to ensure the safety of persons and
  goods and the efficient operation of the airport.

<!-- -->

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the operator of a vehicle shall obey

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any instructions displayed on a sign that are
    applicable to the vehicle; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any instructions given by an enforcement officer in
    accordance with subsection 4(2) that are applicable to the vehicle.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In case of conflict between the instructions referred
  to in paragraphs (1)(a) and (b), the operator of a vehicle shall obey
  the instructions given by an enforcement officer.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to these Regulations, the operator of a
  vehicle shall, in respect of the operation of the vehicle, obey the
  laws of the province and the municipality in which the airport is
  located.

<!-- -->

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} park a vehicle outside a parking area; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} stop a vehicle outside a parking area in order to
    load or unload persons or goods except where it is permitted by a
    sign.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall park or stop a vehicle in a manner
  that obstructs traffic.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (1) and (2), an airport operator
  may authorize a person to park or stop a vehicle in a place where
  there is no sign authorizing the parking or stopping of vehicles,
  subject to any conditions that are necessary to ensure the safety of
  persons and goods and the efficient operation of the airport.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} An airport operator may move and store in a suitable
  place a vehicle that has been parked or stopped in contravention of
  this Part or of a notice prohibiting that vehicle from entering or
  parking on airport property.

- SOR/2009-123, s. 2

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  airport operator may issue or authorize the issuance of a permit in
  respect of the parking of a vehicle in a specified parking area and
  may, in respect of the permit, specify a period of validity and any
  conditions necessary for the safety of persons and goods and the
  efficient operation of the airport.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An airport operator may revoke a permit if the permit
  holder fails to comply with a condition of the permit.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** No person shall park a
vehicle in a parking area reserved for permit holders unless the person
holds a permit authorizing them to park in that area and they park the
vehicle in accordance with the conditions of the permit.

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall park a vehicle in a parking area where parking spaces are
  marked so as to wholly or partially occupy more than one space.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall park a vehicle in a parking area

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the hours within which parking is prohibited
    in the parking area; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a period greater than that indicated on a sign
    or greater than that for which a fee has been paid.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall park a vehicle in a parking area
  reserved

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (b), for a certain person or a
    specified class of person or vehicle unless the person is the person
    or a person of the specified class or is operating a vehicle of the
    specified class; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for persons with disabilities unless the vehicle is
    identified by a sign issued by a government agency responsible for
    such signs as a vehicle used for the transport of a person with a
    disability and a person with a disability is using the vehicle at
    the time.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The operator of a vehicle
shall, at the request of an enforcement officer authorized under section
21, produce

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any permit issued by the airport operator under this
  Part;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any licence or permit authorizing them to operate the
  vehicle;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any certificate of registration of the vehicle under
  the person's control; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other certificate required in respect of the
  vehicle by the laws of the province and the municipality in which the
  airport is located.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The operator of a vehicle
that is involved in an accident shall report the accident as required by
the laws of the province and the municipality in which the accident
occurred.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Pedestrians, Animals and Littering]{.HTitleText1} {#h-723503 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  airport operator may, in order to ensure the safety of persons and
  goods and the efficient operation of the airport,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} erect or have erected or place or have placed signs

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} regulating or prohibiting the movement of
      pedestrians and persons using a wheelchair or other similar
      device,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} designating areas for the use of pedestrians and
      persons using a wheelchair or other similar device,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} prohibiting animals from being at large without
      the prior authorization of the airport operator, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} prohibiting littering; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} mark any portion of a road for the use of
    pedestrians and persons using a wheelchair or other similar device.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An enforcement officer authorized under section 21
  may, for the purpose of ensuring the safety of persons and goods and
  the efficient operation of the airport, give instructions for
  directing or controlling pedestrian traffic and that of persons using
  a wheelchair or other similar device.

### [Pedestrians]{.HTitleText2} {#h-723513 .Subheading}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2),
  pedestrians and persons using a wheelchair or other similar device
  shall obey

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any instructions displayed on a sign; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any instructions given by an enforcement officer in
    accordance with subsection 15(2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In case of conflict between the instructions referred
  to in paragraphs (1)(a) and (b), pedestrians and persons using a
  wheelchair or other similar device shall obey the instructions given
  by an enforcement officer.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Pedestrians and persons
using a wheelchair or other similar device shall, to the extent
possible,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where there is a sidewalk or a portion of a road has
  been marked or an area designated for their use, use the sidewalk or
  the marked portion of the road or the designated area; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where there is no sidewalk and no portion of a road
  has been marked or area designated for their use, use the shoulder of
  the road and face oncoming traffic.

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Where no portion of a
road has been marked and no area designated as a pedestrian crossing and
no sign prohibits the crossing of the road, pedestrians and persons
using a wheelchair or other similar device shall, when crossing that
road, yield the right-of-way to vehicles using the road.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-723525 .Part}

### [Enforcement Officers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-723526 .Subheading}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
authorized as enforcement officers for the purposes of these
Regulations:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} members of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} members of the police force of the province or the
  municipality in which the airport is located;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} by-law enforcement officers of the municipality in
  which the airport is located; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an airport operator and its agents, contractors and
  employees assigned to duties related to the enforcement of these
  Regulations.

### [Punishment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-723532 .Subheading}

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Any person who
contravenes a provision of these Regulations is liable on summary
conviction to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a fine not exceeding \$500;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a term of imprisonment not exceeding six months; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fine and the term of imprisonment referred to in
  paragraphs (a) and (b).

<!-- -->

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who is alleged to have contravened a provision of these
  Regulations, other than section 5 or 6, subsection 8(3) or section 14,
  may register a plea of guilty to the alleged offence by making payment
  in accordance with the terms and conditions set out on a ticket or
  other notification document in the amount of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$30 in the case of a contravention of subsection
    9(1), section 11, subsection 12(1) or (2) or paragraph 12(3)(a) at
    an airport set out in Schedule 1;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$40 in the case of a contravention of subsection
    9(1), section 11, subsection 12(1) or (2) or paragraph 12(3)(a) at
    an airport in parking fine category 1 set out in Schedule 2;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$40 in the case of a contravention of subsection
    9(2) at an airport set out in Schedule 1;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} \$50 in the case of a contravention of section 13,
    subsection 16(1) or section 17 or 18;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} \$50 in the case of a contravention of subsection
    9(1), section 11, subsection 12(1) or (2) or paragraph 12(3)(a) at
    an airport in parking fine category 2 set out in Schedule 2;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} \$50 in the case of a contravention of subsection
    9(2) at an airport in parking fine category 1 set out in Schedule 2;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} \$75 in the case of a contravention of subsection
    9(2) at an airport in parking fine category 2 set out in Schedule 2;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} \$75 in the case of a contravention of subsection
    7(1) or 8(1); and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} \$100 in the case of a contravention of paragraph
    12(3)(b).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person who is alleged to have contravened section
  5 or 6, subsection 8(3) or section 14 may register a plea of guilty to
  the alleged offence by making payment in accordance with the terms and
  conditions set out on a ticket or other notification document in the
  amount equal to the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fine prescribed under the highway traffic laws
    of the province or the by-laws of the municipality in which the
    offence was committed, as amended from time to time, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$500.
