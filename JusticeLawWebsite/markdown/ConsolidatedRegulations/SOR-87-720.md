## Non-residents' Temporary Importation of Baggage and Conveyances Regulations {#non-residents-temporary-importation-of-baggage-and-conveyances-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-720
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1987-12-10

Regulations Respecting the Temporary Importation of Baggage and
Conveyances by a Person Not a Resident of Canada

P.C. 1987-2478 1987-12-10
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to tariff
item 70305-1[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFBAA} of
Schedule A to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the temporary
importation of baggage and conveyances by a person not a resident of
Canada](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-720).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EFBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1980-81-82-83, c.
  67, s.15
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910658 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Non-residents' Temporary Importation of Baggage and
Conveyances Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-720).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910660 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[baggage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[baggage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the baggage referred to in
    section 4; ([bagages]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[beer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[beer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means beer or malt liquor as defined in
    section 4 of the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14);
    ([bière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cigar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cigar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 2
    of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1);
    ([cigare]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cigarette]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cigarette]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1);
    ([cigarette]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a person,
    means an individual who is the common-law partner of the per-son
    within the meaning of subsection 248(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any vehicle, aircraft,
    water-borne craft or other contrivance that is used to move persons
    or goods but does not include a mobile home trailer that is more
    than 2.6 metres in width; ([moyen de transport]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[immediate container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[immediate container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in section 2 of the [Excise Act,
    2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1); ([contenant immédiat]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[manufactured tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[manufactured tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in section 2 of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1); ([tabac
    fabriqué]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Revenue; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who, in the settled
    routine of that person's life, makes their home, resides and is
    or-dinarily present in Canada; ([résident]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[temporary resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[temporary resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who is not a resident of Canada and who
      resides temporarily in Canada for the purpose of

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} studying at an educational institution,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} employment for a period not exceeding
        36 months, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} performing preclearance activities on behalf
        of the Government of the United States under the terms of the
        Agreement on Land, Rail, Marine, and Air Transport Preclearance
        between the Government of Canada and the Government of the
        United States of America, done at Washington on March 16, 2015,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the spouse or common-law partner or any dependant
      of a person described in subparagraph (a)(i) or (ii), and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the spouse or common-law partner or any dependant
      of a person described in subparagraph (a)(iii); ([résident
      temporaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vaping device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vaping device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1); ([dispositif
    de vapotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vaping substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vaping substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1); ([substance
    de vapotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[visitor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[visitor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is not a resident
    or a temporary resident and who enters Canada for a period not
    exceeding 12 months. ([visiteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Terms and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910681 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A person who is not a
resident may import baggage or conveyances classified under tariff item
No. 9803.00.00 for the personal use of that person in Canada if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the baggage or conveyances are not used by a
  resident;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of conveyances, they are not used in
  Canada for

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} moving passengers or goods for compensation or for
    transporting goods for sale, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} soliciting sales or subscriptions on behalf of the
    Canadian office of a business or on behalf of a business based in
    Canada;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/94-784, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of consumable goods

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that are imported by a person described in
    subparagraph (a)(i) or (ii) or paragraph (b) of the definition
    [[temporary resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2, they
    accompany that person at the time when that person initially arrives
    in Canada,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that are imported by any other temporary resident,
    they accompany the temporary resident at the time that temporary
    resident initially arrives in Canada or at any time that that
    temporary resident returns to Canada after an absence abroad, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that are imported by a person other than a
    temporary resident, they accompany that person at the time the
    person arrives in Canada;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} where the baggage or conveyances are imported by a
  visitor, the visitor declares that the visitor intends

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to leave Canada on a specified date, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to make a series of visits to Canada within the
    succeeding 12 months and specifies the date on which the visitor
    intends to leave Canada at the end of the final visit of the
    visitor;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of baggage or conveyances other than
  consumable goods, the baggage or conveyances are exported from Canada
  or destroyed under the supervision of a customs officer before the
  expiration of the time limit specified in section 5; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a visitor referred to in
  subparagraph (e)(ii), the visitor produces, on each arrival in Canada
  during the period referred to in that subparagraph, the customs form
  issued to the visitor in respect of any baggage or conveyances that
  were imported on a previous visit and remained in Canada during the
  visitor's absence if the visitor is requested by a customs officer to
  do so.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-542, s. 3
- SOR/94-784, s. 2
- SOR/96-370, s. 2
- SOR/98-62, s. 4
- SOR/2005-258, s. 2
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the baggage that a person who is not a resident of
  Canada may temporarily import under tariff item No. 9803.00.00
  consists of any goods that the person imports for their personal use
  and that are appropriate for their needs and consistent with the
  purpose, nature and duration of their intended stay in Canada, but
  does not include more than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 1.5 L of wine, or 1.14 L of spirits, or 1.14 L of
    wine and spirits, or 8.5 L of beer or ale;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 200 cigarettes, 50 cigars, 200 g of manufactured
    tobacco and 200 tobacco sticks;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} 120 millilitres of vaping substance in liquid
    form, or 120 grams of vaping substance in solid form, within any
    combination of not more than twelve vaping devices and immediate
    containers; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 200 rounds of ammunition or, if they are for the
    use of that person at a meet held under the auspices of a recognized
    shooting or rifle association of Canada, 1,500 rounds of ammunition.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person described in subparagraph (a)(iii) or
  paragraph (c) of the definition [[temporary
  resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 has been absent from
  Canada for not less than 48 hours, the baggage that that person may
  import under tariff item No. 9803.00.00 does not include alcoholic
  beverages or tobacco products.

## [Time Limits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910707 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Imported
  baggage and conveyances classified under tariff item No. 9803.00.00
  may remain in Canada

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where they are imported by a visitor, until the
    earlier of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the expiration of the date that the visitor has
      declared, under subparagraph 3(e)(i) or (ii), the visitor intends
      to leave Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 12 months after the date of importation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if they are imported by a person described in
    subparagraph (a)(i) of the definition [[temporary
    resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2, or by the spouse,
    common-law partner or dependant of that person, until the day on
    which that person completes their studies at an educational
    institution;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if they are imported by a person described in
    subparagraph (a)(ii) of the definition [[temporary
    resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2, or by the spouse,
    common-law partner or dependant of that person, until the earlier of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the day on which that person completes their
      employment in Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 36 months after the day on which that person
      arrives in Canada; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if they are imported by a person described in
    subparagraph (a)(iii) or paragraph (c) of the definition [[temporary
    resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2, until the day on which
    the person described in subparagraph (a)(iii) completes their
    employment in Canada with the Government of the United States.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), where it is
  impossible or impracticable for a visitor or temporary resident to
  comply with the requirements of that subsection, the Minister may
  extend the length of time during which any imported baggage or
  conveyances that are classified under tariff item No. 9803.00.00 may
  remain in Canada

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of baggage or conveyances imported by a
    visitor, to any date up to 18 months after the date of importation;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of baggage or conveyances imported by a
    temporary resident, to any date up to six months after the
    termination of his employment or studies, as the case may be, in
    Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-542, s. 3
- SOR/96-370, s. 4
- SOR/96-526, s. 3
- SOR/98-62, s. 5
- SOR/2005-258, s. 4

## [Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910723 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may require
security, in the form of cash or a certified cheque, in respect of any
baggage or conveyances imported and classified under tariff item
No. 9803.00.00 of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in an amount
not exceeding the duties that would be payable if that tariff item did
not apply.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-542, s. 2
