## [A Proclamation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612303 .Part}

Whereas, by Order in Council P.C. 2005-1898 of November 1, 2005, the
Governor in Council declared that, in accordance with Article 27 of the
Agreement on Social Security between Canada and the Republic of Latvia,
signed at Riga on June 29, 2005, the Agreement shall enter into force on
the first day of the fourth month following the final day of the month
in which the Parties shall have exchanged written notices through the
diplomatic channel confirming that their respective legal requirements
for the entry into force of this Agreement have been completed;

Whereas that Order in Council was laid before Parliament on November 4,
2005 and, as Parliament was dissolved before the expiry of the
thirty-sitting days period required under the [Old Age Security
Act](/eng/acts/O-9), the Order was laid before Parliament again on May
2, 2006;

Whereas, before the twentieth sitting day after the Order had been laid
before Parliament, no motion for the consideration of either House to
the effect that the Order be revoked was filed with the Speaker of the
appropriate House;

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 42(2) of the [Old Age Security
Act](/eng/acts/O-9), the Order came into force on the thirtieth sitting
day after it had been laid before Parliament, being June 20, 2006;

Whereas the exchange of the written notices was completed on July 14,
2006;

Whereas the Agreement shall enter into force on the first day of the
fourth month following the final day of the month in which the Parties
exchanged written notices, being November 1, 2006;

And whereas, by Order in Council P.C. 2006-808 of August 29, 2006, the
Governor in Council directed that a proclamation do issue giving notice
that the Agreement on Social Security between Canada and the Republic of
Latvia shall enter into force on November 1, 2006;

Now know you that We, by and with the advice of Our Privy Council for
Canada, do by this Our proclamation give notice that the Agreement on
Social Security between Canada and the Republic of Latvia, signed at
Riga on June 29, 2005, a copy of which is annexed, shall enter into
force on November 1, 2006.

Of all which Our loving subjects and all others whom these presents may
concern are hereby required to take notice and to govern themselves
accordingly.

In testimony whereof, We have caused this Our proclamation to be
published and the Great Seal of Canada to be hereunto affixed. Witness:
Our Right Trusty and Well-beloved Michaëlle Jean, Chancellor and
Principal Companion of Our Order of Canada, Chancellor and Commander of
Our Order of Military Merit, Chancellor and Commander of Our Order of
Merit of the Police Forces, Governor General and Commander-in-Chief of
Canada.

At our government house, in Our City of Ottawa, this second day of
October in the year of Our Lord two thousand and six and in the
fifty-fifth year of Our Reign.

By Command,

::: FlushRight
RICHARD DICERNI
:::

::: FlushRight
*Deputy Registrar General of Canada*

#### [ARTICLE 7]{.HLabel2}[Detachments]{.HTitleText2} {#article-7detachments .SchedHeadL2}

An employed person who is subject to the legislation of a Party in
respect of employment by an employer who has a place of business in the
territory of that Party, and who is sent by his or her employer to work
in the territory of the other Party for the same or a related employer
shall, in respect of that work, be subject only to the legislation of
the first Party as though that work were performed in its territory.
This coverage may not be maintained for more than 60 months without the
prior consent of the competent authorities of both Parties.

##### [ARTICLE 12]{.HLabel3}[Minimum Period To Be Totalized]{.HTitleText3} {#article-12minimum-period-to-be-totalized .SchedHeadL3}

Notwithstanding any other provision of this Agreement, if the total
duration of the creditable periods accumulated by a person under the
legislation of a Party is less than one year and if, taking into account
only those periods, no right to a benefit exists under the legislation
of that Party, the competent institution of that Party shall not be
required to pay a benefit to that person in respect of those periods by
virtue of this Agreement.

    ::: listItemLabel
    [(a)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText2
    an Old Age Security pension shall be paid to a person who is outside
    Canada only if that person's periods of residence, when totalized as
    provided in Chapter 1, are at least equal to the minimum period of
    residence in Canada required by the [Old Age Security
    Act](/eng/acts/O-9) for the payment of a pension outside Canada;
    :::
    :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock1
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(b)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText2
    an allowance and a guaranteed income supplement under the [Old Age
    Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9) shall be paid to a person who is
    outside Canada only to the extent permitted by that Act.
    :::
    :::::

##### [ARTICLE 14]{.HLabel3}[Benefits Under the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8)]{.HTitleText3} {#article-14benefits-under-the-canada-pension-plan .SchedHeadL3}

If a person is eligible for a benefit under the [Canada Pension
Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) solely through the application of the totalizing
provisions of Chapter 1, the competent institution of Canada shall
calculate the amount of benefit payable to that person in the following
manner:

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [(a)]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText2
  the earnings-related portion of the benefit shall be determined in
  conformity with the provisions of the [Canada Pension
  Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), exclusively on the basis of the pensionable
  earnings under that Plan;
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [(b)]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText2
  the flat-rate portion of the benefit shall be determined by
  multiplying:
  :::
  :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock2
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(i)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText2
    the amount of the flat-rate portion of the benefit determined in
    conformity with the provisions of the [Canada Pension
    Plan](/eng/acts/C-8)
    :::
    :::::

  by

  - ::::: listItemBlock2
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText2
    the fraction which represents the ratio of the periods of
    contributions to the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) in
    relation to the minimum qualifying period required under that Plan
    to establish eligibility for that benefit, but in no case shall that
    fraction exceed the value of one.
    :::
    :::::

#### [CHAPTER 3]{.HLabel2}[Benefits Under the Legislation of the Republic of Latvia]{.HTitleText2} {#chapter-3benefits-under-the-legislation-of-the-republic-of-latvia .SchedHeadL2}

##### [ARTICLE 15]{.HLabel3}[Calculating the Amount of Benefit Payable]{.HTitleText3} {#article-15calculating-the-amount-of-benefit-payable .SchedHeadL3}

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  If, under the legislation of the Republic of Latvia, the conditions
  for eligibility for a benefit are met without the need to take into
  account creditable periods accumulated under the legislation of
  Canada, the competent institution of the Republic of Latvia shall
  determine the amount of that benefit exclusively on the basis of the
  creditable periods accumulated under its legislation.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  If, under the legislation of the Republic of Latvia, eligibility for a
  benefit can be established only through the application of the
  totalizing provisions of Chapter 1, the competent institution of the
  Republic of Latvia:
  :::
  :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock1
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(a)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText2
    shall calculate the theoretical amount of the benefit which would be
    paid if the totalized creditable periods had been accumulated under
    the legislation of the Republic of Latvia alone; and
    :::
    :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock1
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(b)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText2
    on the basis of the theoretical amount calculated in accordance with
    sub-paragraph (a), shall determine the amount of benefit payable by
    applying the ratio of the length of the creditable periods
    accumulated under the legislation of the Republic of Latvia to the
    totalized creditable periods.
    :::
    :::::

##### [ARTICLE 16]{.HLabel3}[Entitlement and Payment of Benefits]{.HTitleText3} {#article-16entitlement-and-payment-of-benefits .SchedHeadL3}

that their respective legal requirements for the entry into force of
this Agreement have been completed. The date of the exchange of the
written notices shall be the date of the delivery of the last notice.
