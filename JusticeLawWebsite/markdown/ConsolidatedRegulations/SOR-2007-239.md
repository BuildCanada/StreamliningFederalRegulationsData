## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736041 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [First Nations Fiscal
    Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.67). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who requests a
    review under subsection 33(1) of the
    Act. ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day other than a
    Saturday or holiday. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[intervenor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[intervenor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person or organization
    added as an intervenor under
    section 14. ([intervenant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an applicant or a First Nation
    that is the subject of a review. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[review]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[review]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in sections 8 to 36, a review conducted under
      subsection 33(1) of the Act; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in sections 37 to 42, a review conducted under
      subsection 33(2) of the Act. ([examen]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

## [Delegation to Panel]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736054 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation to panel

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of a request for review made under subsection 33(1) of the
  Act, or on initiating a review under subsection 33(2) of the Act, the
  Commission may delegate its power of review to a panel consisting of
  one or more commissioners.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of commissioners

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The commissioners of that panel shall be designated
  by the Chief Commissioner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission includes panel

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, a reference to the Commission
  includes a reference to a panel constituted under subsection (1).

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may make a
decision in any review without holding a hearing.

## [Delivery and Filing of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736066 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Methods of delivery

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Delivery
  of a document may be made personally or by sending it by registered
  mail, fax or e-mail.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal delivery

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Personal delivery of a document is made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, by leaving the
    document with that individual or with a person at least 18 years of
    age residing at that individual's place of residence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a First Nation, by leaving the
    document with the individual apparently in charge, at the time of
    delivery, of the main administrative office of the First Nation, or
    with the First Nation's legal counsel; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, by leaving the
    document with the individual apparently in charge, at the time of
    delivery, of its head office or one of its branch offices, or with
    an officer or director of the corporation or the corporation's legal
    counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of delivery

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), a document shall be
  considered to have been delivered

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if delivered personally, on the day on which
    personal delivery is made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if sent by registered mail, on the fifth day after
    it is mailed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if sent by fax, on the day indicated on the
    confirmation of transmission; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if sent by e-mail, on the day indicated in the
    electronic confirmation that the e-mail has been opened.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of delivery to Commission

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Documents filed with the Commission are considered to
  have been delivered on the date stamped by the Commission under
  subsection 5(2) or (3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delivery of copies

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** When a document is filed

parties and intervenors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Documents filed on behalf of a party
  shall be signed by a person authorized by the party and shall set out
  the nature of the person's authorization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of capacity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may at any time require a person who
  has signed a document on behalf of a party to provide proof of the
  person's authority to act in that capacity.

## [Reviews Under Subsection 33(1) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736099 .Part}

### [Procedure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-736100 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing a request for review

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  request for review shall set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number, and any
    available fax number and e-mail address, of the applicant and of any
    representative acting on behalf of the applicant;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an address for the delivery of documents, if
    different from the address of the applicant or representative;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the First Nation, and the title of any
    local revenue law, in respect of which the request for review is
    made;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the grounds for the request for review;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the facts and any law on
    which the applicant intends to rely;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the remedy sought;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any other information that the applicant believes
    the Commission requires in order to conduct the review; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the review is requested on an expedited basis,
    the reasons for the urgency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accompanying documentation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request for review shall be accompanied by two
  copies of the applicant's request for a remedy made to the council of
  the First Nation pursuant to paragraph 33(1)(b) of the Act and any
  related correspondence with the council of the First Nation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deficient request

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If a request for review
does not contain the information required under section 8, the
Commission shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} identify what information is missing; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} advise the applicant that the information must be
  provided before the Commission will proceed with the request for
  review.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  First Nation that is the subject of the review may file a reply to the
  request for review within 10 business days after the day on which the
  Commission delivers to it a copy of the request for review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of reply

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A reply to a request for review shall identify the
  request for review to which the reply relates and set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number, and any
    available fax number and e-mail address, of the First Nation and of
    any representative acting on behalf of the First Nation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an address for the delivery of documents, if
    different from the address of the First Nation or its
    representative;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an outline of the position the First Nation takes
    in response to the request for review;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an admission or denial of each allegation of fact
    contained in the request for review;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of any additional facts and any
    law on which the First Nation intends to rely;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other information that the First Nation
    believes the Commission requires in order to conduct the review; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the review is requested on an expedited basis,
    the reasons for the urgency.

the review solely on the basis of the submissions of the applicant and
any intervenors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of response

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Within 10 business days
after the filing of a request for review that meets the requirements of
section 8, the Commission shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} publish a notice of the review on an Internet site

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} deliver a copy of the request for review to the First

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adding intervenor

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person or organization may apply to the Commission to be added as an
  intervenor

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a hearing is to be held, at any time before the
    hearing begins; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, at any time before a decision on
    the review is rendered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of an application under subsection (1),
  the Commission may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} add the person or organization as an intervenor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} direct the person or organization to provide the
    Commission with additional information in support of the
    application; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} reject the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adding Board as intervenor

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the First Nations Financial Management Board makes
  an application in accordance with subsection (1), the Commission shall
  add the Board as an intervenor for the purpose of making
  representations as to the impact of any requested remedy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role of intervenors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An intervenor may make written submissions and, if a
  hearing is being held, oral argument to the Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delivery of documents

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** On adding a person or
organization as an intervenor, the Commission shall deliver to the
person or organization copies of the request for review, any reply filed
under section 10 and any response filed under section 12.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statutory declarations

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may require confirmation, by way of a statutory
  declaration, of any facts alleged in a request for review or reply
  that have not been admitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cross-examination on declaration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party who has submitted a statutory declaration
  shall make the deponent of the declaration available for
  cross-examination on the content of the declaration by any other
  party.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments to request for review

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** An applicant may file
with the Commission a request that a review already under way address
additional issues that would otherwise be the subject of a separate
review.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence and information

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** In conducting a review,
the Commission may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} accept evidence that is not given on oath or evidence
  that would not ordinarily be admissible in a court of law;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the request of a party, require another party to
  provide the Commission with any documents necessary to the conduct of
  its review; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} direct that written submissions be filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consolidation

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may hold a
single hearing in respect of more than one review or consolidate two or
more reviews.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required attendance

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may
require the parties to attend any conferences or meetings necessary to
the conduct of a review.

### [Settlement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-736173 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Settlement conferences

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  settlement conference may be scheduled by the Commission or at the
  request of a party at any stage in a review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presence of party

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a representative is acting for a party, the
  Commission may direct that the party be present at the settlement
  conference or available to instruct the representative at that time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informal resolution

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may make settlement recommendations to the parties and may
  suspend the review to enable the parties to attempt to resolve the
  subject of the request for review informally.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Referral to mediation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may recommend to the parties that the
  request for review be referred to formal mediation, in which case the
  Commission shall provide to the parties a roster of mediators who are
  independent of the Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent order

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** If the parties reach an
agreement, the Commission may make an order under paragraph 33(3)(a) of
the Act embodying that agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommencement of review

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** If six months have
elapsed since a review was referred to mediation or adjourned for other
settlement discussions without a settlement being reached, the
Commission shall recommence the review and notify the parties
accordingly.

### [Hearings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-736188 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directed hearing

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may at any time direct that a hearing be held in respect of
  a request for review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of hearing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Commission directs that a hearing be held, the
  Commission shall fix the time, date and place for the hearing and
  deliver a notice of the hearing to all parties and intervenors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct of hearings

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may direct that a brief written summary of the submissions
  of any party or intervenor be filed in advance of a hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expediting hearing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} With the agreement of the parties, the Commission may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} proceed on the basis of an agreed statement of
    facts; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} limit the number of witnesses to be called at a
    hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Teleconferencing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may hear oral evidence or oral
  submissions by teleconference.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjournments

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may adjourn a hearing for any time and
  on any terms that it considers appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to attend

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** If a party or intervenor
who has been given notice of a hearing in accordance with
subsection 25(2) fails to attend, the Commission may proceed with the
hearing and complete its review in the absence of that party or
intervenor.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subpoenas

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may, at any time before the conclusion of a hearing, request the
  Commission to apply to a justice of the peace for a subpoena
  compelling a person to appear before the Commission to give evidence
  and to bring any documents specified in the subpoena.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Travel expenses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall pay travel expenses, in the
  amount set out in Tariff A of the [Federal Courts
  Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106), to every person compelled by
  subpoena to appear before it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-hearing conferences

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  hearing is to be held, a pre-hearing conference between the parties
  shall be scheduled by the Commission to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} direct the pre-hearing production of documents by a
    party;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine whether a subpoena will be required in
    respect of any person who may be required as a witness;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} direct a party to provide further information
    regarding its position;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} develop an agreed statement of facts or obtain
    admissions that might facilitate the hearing;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} attempt to simplify the matters in dispute between
    the parties, including the resolution of some or all of them;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} determine the conduct of the hearing, including the
    order in which the parties will proceed, the number and identity of
    witnesses and the estimated time required; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} decide any other matter that may aid in securing a
    just, expeditious or inexpensive disposition of the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Require attendance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a representative is acting for an applicant, the
  Commission may direct that the applicant be present at the pre-hearing
  conference.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Single Commissioner

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A pre-hearing conference may be conducted by a single
  Commissioner or an employee of the Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination outside hearing

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  request of a party, the Commission may consent to the examination of a
  person outside a hearing, having regard to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the expected absence of the person at the time of
    the hearing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the age or any illness or infirmity of the person;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the distance the person resides from the place
    where the hearing will be held; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the expense of having the person attend the
    hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In giving its consent under subsection (1), the
  Commission shall give directions regarding the time, place and manner
  of the examination, the notice to be given to the person being
  examined and to the other parties, the attendance of any other
  witnesses and the production of any requested documents or other
  material.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cross-examination

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Other parties may cross-examine any person examined
  outside a hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies of transcript

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A party submitting transcribed evidence shall provide
  a copy of the transcript, at its own expense, to the Commission and to
  all other parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adverse witnesses

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), a party
  may call another party as a witness at a hearing by requesting that a
  subpoena be delivered and travel expenses be paid to that party in
  accordance with subsections 28(1) and (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the other party is a corporation, it shall provide
  as a witness an officer or director of the corporation who has
  knowledge of the facts in issue.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First Nation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the other party is a First Nation, it shall
  provide as a witness the person who administers the taxes of the First
  Nation or another person who has knowledge of the facts in issue.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A party shall give another party at least five
  business days notice of its intention to call that party or a person
  referred to in subsection (2) or (3) as a witness, unless the other
  party or that person is in attendance at the hearing.

### [Compliance and Costs]{.HTitleText2} {#h-736248 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-compliance

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** If an applicant fails to
comply with these Regulations, the Commission may dismiss the request
for review.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time periods variable

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may, on
the application of a party, extend or shorten any period provided for in
these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Variances

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** If it is necessary to
secure the just, expeditious or inexpensive hearing of a request for
review, or to accommodate the customs or culture of the First Nation in
respect of which a hearing is held, the Commission may vary any
procedure provided for by these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** No costs shall be awarded
in any review.

### [Decisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-736257 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decisions of the Commission

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  decisions and orders of the Commission in a review shall be issued in
  written form and shall be accompanied by written reasons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A decision or order of the Commission, and the
  accompanying reasons, shall be published in the First Nations Gazette
  or on an Internet site maintained by the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of copies

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall provide a copy of each decision,
  order and accompanying reasons to all parties and intervenors and make
  copies of them available for inspection by the public at its offices.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance period

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An order made under paragraph 33(3)(a) of the Act
  shall set out the period within which the First Nation shall implement
  the required remedy.

## [Reviews Under Subsection 33(2) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736267 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of review

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Commission decides to conduct a review under subsection 33(2) of the
  Act, it shall deliver a notice of review to the First Nation that is
  the subject of the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of review shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the Commission is of the opinion that the First
    Nation has not complied with the Act or a regulation, identify the
    provision of the Act or regulation in question and the reason that
    the Commission believes it has not been complied with; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Commission is of the opinion that the First
    Nation has unfairly or improperly applied a law, identify the law in
    question and the reason that the Commission believes it has been
    unfairly or improperly applied.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Production of documents

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice of review may require a First Nation to
  produce any documents set out in the notice relating to the subject of
  the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Within 10 business days after the delivery of a
  notice of review, the Commission shall publish a notice of the
  proposed review on an Internet site maintained by the Commission and
  deliver a copy of the notice to the First Nations Financial Management
  Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply by First Nation

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  First Nation that receives a notice of review may file a reply within
  10 business days after delivery of the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of reply

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A reply to a notice of review shall identify the
  notice to which the reply relates and set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number, and any
    available fax number and e-mail address, of the First Nation and of
    any representative acting on behalf of the First Nation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an address for delivery of documents, if different
    from the address of the First Nation or its representative;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an outline of the position that the First Nation
    takes in response to the allegations set out in the notice;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an admission or denial of each allegation of fact
    contained in the notice;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of any additional facts and any
    law on which the First Nation intends to rely; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other information that the First Nation
    believes the Commission requires in order to conduct the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to reply

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a First Nation fails to file a reply, the
  Commission may proceed with the review.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statutory declarations

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may require confirmation, by way of a statutory
  declaration, of any facts alleged in a reply that have not been
  admitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cross-examination on declaration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party who has submitted a statutory declaration
  shall make the deponent of the declaration available for
  cross-examination by the Commission on the content of the declaration.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence and information

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** In conducting a review,
the Commission may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} accept evidence that is not given on oath or evidence
  that would not ordinarily be admissible in a court of law;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the request of a party, require another party to
  provide the Commission with any documents necessary to the conduct of
  its review; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} direct that written submissions be filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adding intervenor

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person or organization may apply to the Commission to be added as an
  intervenor

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a hearing is to be held, at any time before the
    hearing begins; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, at any time before the decision
    on the review is rendered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of an application under subsection (1),
  the Commission may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} add the person or organization as an intervenor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} direct the person or organization to provide the
    Commission with additional information in support of the
    application; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} reject the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adding Board as intervenor

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the First Nations Financial Management Board makes
  an application in accordance with subsection (1), the Commission shall
  add the Board as an intervenor for the purpose of making
  representations as to the impact of any potential remedy.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may at any time direct that a review be set down for a
  hearing and, at the request of the First Nation, shall hold a hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedures for hearing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Section 20, subsection 25(2) and sections 26, 27 and
  33 to 36 apply, with any modifications that are necessary, to a
  hearing directed under subsection (1).
