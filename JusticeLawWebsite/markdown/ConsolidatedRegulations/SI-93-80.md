## Storage Charges Remission Order, 1993 {#storage-charges-remission-order-1993 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-80
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1993-06-16

Order Respecting the Remission of Certain Storage Charges Paid or
Payable under Section 4 of the Storage of Goods Regulations

P.C. 1993-1069  1993-05-25
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Treasury
Board and the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to
subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-80_e_hq_6516){#fn_SI-93-80_e_hq_6516-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of certain storage
charges paid or payable under section 4 of the Storage of Goods
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SI-93-80).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-647555 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Storage Charges Remission Order, 1993](/eng/regulations/SI-93-80).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-647557 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted of the storage charges paid or payable under
section 4 of the [Storage of Goods
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-991) in an amount equal to the
amount by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the storage charges paid or payable under section 4
  of those Regulations for the period beginning on September 18, 1986
  and ending on September 14, 1988

exceed

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the storage charges that would have been paid or
  payable for that period if the storage charges had been payable
  beginning on the fourth business day after the day on which the goods
  were left at the customs office.

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-647562 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission in respect of
storage charges that have been paid is granted on the condition that a
claim for remission is made to the Minister of National Revenue within
two years after the day on which this Order is made.
