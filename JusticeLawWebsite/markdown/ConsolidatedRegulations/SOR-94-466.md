## Dairy Products Marketing Regulations {#dairy-products-marketing-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-466
:::

[CANADIAN DAIRY COMMISSION ACT](/eng/acts/C-15)

Registration 1994-06-23

Regulations Regulating the Marketing of Dairy Products

P.C. 1994-1069 1994-06-23
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to section 12 of the [Canadian
Dairy Commission Act](/eng/acts/C-15), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations regulating the marketing of dairy
products](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-466).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964131 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Dairy Products Marketing
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-466).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964150 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of the production for marketing, or the
  marketing, in interprovincial or export trade of dairy products.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of the
  production for marketing, or the marketing, in interprovincial trade
  of milk or cream in liquid form for consumer use.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2005-290, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-14, s. 3
- SOR/2005-290, s. 3
:::

## [Canadian Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964157 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  the beginning of a dairy year, the Commission shall calculate the
  Canadian requirements for milk and cream for that dairy year for use
  as or in dairy products.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Canadian requirements for milk and cream in
  respect of a dairy year shall be calculated on the basis of estimates,
  prepared by the Commission, of the quantity of milk or cream, for use
  as or in dairy products,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that, during the previous dairy year, was

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} produced for all markets in all of the provinces
      in which there is a Board,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} used in dairy products imported into and
      exported from Canada, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} used in dairy products held by the Commission
      in inventory; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is anticipated to be in demand during the
    dairy year.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may, from time to time during a dairy
  year, adjust its calculation of the Canadian requirements, based on
  the actual levels of production, use and demand during that dairy
  year.

## [Federal Quota]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964167 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  portion of the federal quota that each Board shall have available to
  allot in a dairy year shall be determined before the beginning of the
  dairy year by the unanimous agreement of the Committee, after it has
  taken into consideration the Canadian requirements referred to in
  subsection 4(1).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any adjustment that is required in respect of the
  portion of the federal quota referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  determined by the unanimous agreement of the Committee as soon as
  possible after an adjustment to the Canadian requirements is made
  pursuant to subsection 4(3).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A Board is authorized to allot any of the portion of
  the federal quota of the province in which the Board has jurisdiction
  to any person who is entitled, or to suspend or cancel any of that
  portion that has been allotted.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a Board allots to a person a share of the portion
  of the federal quota of the province in which the Board has
  jurisdiction, it shall do so at the same time as it allots a share of
  the provincial quota to that person.

## [Powers of a Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964173 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 5(3), a Board may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} exercise all or any powers like the powers that may
  be exercised,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} apply all or any criteria like the criteria that are
  applied, and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} administer all or any rules like the rules that are
  administered

by the Board in relation to the entitlement to, and allotment,
suspension or cancellation of provincial quota.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-14, s. 4
:::

## [Licensing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964181 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission shall cause a federal licence to be issued only to a person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to whom a share of the portion of the federal quota
    has been allotted;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} who is registered as a milk producer with, or is
    licensed as a cream producer by, the Manitoba Milk Producers; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} who is licensed as a producer under the Milk Act of
    Ontario.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A federal licence is valid only so long as the person
  who holds it continues to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} hold a share of the federal quota;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be registered as a milk producer with, or licensed
    as a cream producer by, the Manitoba Milk Producers; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be licensed as a producer under the Milk Act of
    Ontario.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall engage in the marketing in
  interprovincial or export trade of a dairy product unless the dairy
  product is, or is made from, milk or cream that is produced by a
  person who holds a federal licence.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (3) applies to a dairy product that is, or
  is made from, milk or cream that is produced in Canada.

## [Designated Boards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964196 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where, pursuant to
provincial law, a dairy product is required to be marketed in
intraprovincial trade by or through a Board, or to a person designated
by a Board, that dairy product shall only be marketed in interprovincial
or export trade by or through that Board, or to the person designated by
that Board.

## [Levies and Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964198 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Commission is
authorized to fix, impose and collect levies or charges from persons
engaged in the marketing of any dairy product in interprovincial or
export trade or the production or processing of any dairy product for
marketing in interprovincial or export trade, and may classify those
persons into groups, fix the levies or charges payable by the members of
the different groups and use those levies or charges for the purpose of
carrying out its functions under the Act.

## [Books and Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964200 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every person who is
engaged in the production or processing of a dairy product for marketing
in interprovincial or export trade shall maintain complete and accurate
books and records of all matters relating to that production or
processing and shall make those books and records available to the
Commission on its request.

**[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
SOR/2005-290, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

## [Seizure and Disposal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964203 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector appointed under the Act may seize any dairy product that the
  inspector believes on reasonable grounds was marketed in
  interprovincial or export trade in contravention of these Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An inspector who seizes a perishable dairy product
  under these Regulations shall dispose of it, and any proceeds realized
  from its disposal shall be credited to the Canadian Dairy Commission
  Account.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person from whom a dairy product has been seized
  is found not to have contravened the provision in respect of which the
  dairy product was seized, the Commission shall surrender the dairy
  product or proceeds to the person.
