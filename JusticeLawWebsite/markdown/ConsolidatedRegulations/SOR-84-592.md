## Offshore Area Exclusion Order {#offshore-area-exclusion-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/84-592
:::

[CANADA-NOVA SCOTIA OFFSHORE PETROLEUM RESOURCES ACCORD IMPLEMENTATION
ACT](/eng/acts/C-7.8)

Registration 1984-07-25

Order Declaring that Part I of the Canada-Nova Scotia Oil and Gas
Agreement Act Does Not Apply in Respect of Certain Parts of The Offshore
Area

P.C. 1984-2634 1984-07-25
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources and having been satisfied
that the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources has consulted with the
Minister of Mines and Energy for the Province of Nova Scotia as required
pursuant to subsection 6(2) of An Act respecting the Canada-Nova Scotia
Agreement on Offshore Oil and Gas Resource Management and Revenue
Sharing and to make related and consequential amendments, being
chapter 29 of the Statutes of Canada 1984, and that the Minister of
Mines and Energy for the Province of Nova Scotia has given his approval
in accordance with that subsection, is pleased hereby to make the
annexed Order declaring that Part I of the Canada-Nova Scotia Oil and
Gas Agreement Act does not apply in respect of certain parts of the
offshore area.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884506 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Offshore Area Exclusion Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-592).

## [Exclusion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884508 .Part}

Scotia Oil and Gas Agreement Act does not apply in respect of the
following parts of the offshore area, as described by reference to the
grid system set out in the [Canada Oil and Gas Land
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1518), C.R.C., c. 1518:
