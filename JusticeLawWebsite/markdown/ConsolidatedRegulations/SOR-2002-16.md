## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-681741 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 2, Her
Majesty in right of Canada is exempt from the application of paragraph
9(1)(c) of the [Radiocommunication Act](/eng/acts/R-2).

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-681743 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The exemption under section
1 applies only if the encrypted subscription programming signal or
encrypted network feed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is decoded for the purposes of national defence or
  security; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not available from a lawful distributor.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-681747 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
