## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Application and Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-816190 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-816191 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency with Acts or regulations

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In the event of any
inconsistency between these Rules and an Act of Parliament or any
regulation made under such an Act, that Act or regulation prevails to
the extent of the inconsistency.

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-816194 .Subheading}

Definition of [[holiday]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Rules,
[[holiday]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Saturday, Sunday or any other
day defined as a holiday in subsection 35(1) of the [Interpretation
Act](/eng/acts/I-21).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General principle

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Rules are to be
interpreted and applied in order to permit the just, most expeditious
and least expensive conduct of proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedural matters not provided for

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal is to
determine any procedural matter not provided for in these Rules in a
manner that is consistent with these Rules.

### [Computation and Extension of Time]{.HTitleText2} {#h-816201 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of time limits

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Any time limit provided by
these Rules or fixed by an order of the Tribunal that ends on a holiday
is extended to the next day that is not a holiday.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of time limits

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may extend any
time limit fixed in these Rules before or after the end of the time
limit.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Rules Applicable to All Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-816206 .Part}

### [Official Languages]{.HTitleText2} {#h-816207 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Official languages --- Tribunal
proceedings

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** All Tribunal proceedings
are conducted in English or French, depending on the language chosen by
the applicant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communications with Tribunal

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A party
  may use English or French in any oral or written communication with
  the Tribunal. However, once the applicant has selected a language, all
  oral and written communications, including in documents and exhibits,
  must be made in that language, unless the parties consent to do
  otherwise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Default language selection

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the applicant does not indicate their choice of
  official language in their request, all oral and written
  communications, including in documents and exhibits, must be made in
  the language in which the request to the Tribunal is made. That
  language is deemed to be the language for the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral interpretation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a party requires interpretation services in order
  to participate in or have a witness testify at a hearing in the
  official language in which the proceeding is conducted, the party
  must, at least seven days before the hearing,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the Tribunal of the requirement in writing;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} indicate whether the party requires interpretation
    services from a language other than English or French.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A party who requires interpretation services from a
  language other than English or French must pay for the costs of those
  services.

### [Dispensing with Compliance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-816221 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dispensing with compliance

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If the application of any
rule would cause unfairness to a party, the Tribunal may release the
party from the obligation of complying with the rule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Gap in evidence or non-compliance

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may draw the attention of a party to any gap in the evidence
  of its case or any non-compliance with these Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remedy

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On request, the Tribunal may permit the party to
  remedy any gap in its evidence or non-compliance on any conditions
  that the Tribunal considers just, before the end of the proceedings.

### [Confidential Material]{.HTitleText2} {#h-816229 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidential material

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  request, the Tribunal may order that documents and exhibits that are
  to be filed be treated as confidential.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for the confidential treatment of the
    documents and exhibits; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the nature and extent of potential harm that could
    result from the disclosure of the documents and exhibits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Making of confidential documents and
  exhibits

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before making an order under subrule (1), the
  Tribunal must be satisfied that the documents and exhibits are to be
  treated as confidential, given the public interest in open and
  accessible proceedings.

### [Filing and Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-816239 .Subheading}

Definition of [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of rules
15 to 17, [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include a request
for review.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for review

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Any request for review
made under section 8, 9 or 11 or subsection 12(2) or 13(2) of the
[Agriculture and Agri-Food Administrative Monetary Penalties
Act](/eng/acts/A-8.8) that was transmitted by fax or by electronic means
must also be sent by registered mail to the Tribunal within 15 days
after the day on which they are transmitted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to provide current contact
information

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A party must notify the
Tribunal of their full name, civic address, mailing address if different
from their civic address and at least one of their telephone number, fax
number and email address, and of any change in their contact
information, without delay.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manners of filing with Tribunal

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document may be filed with the Tribunal in any of the following
  manners:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by hand delivery to the Tribunal's head office;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by registered mail to the Tribunal's mailing
    address;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by courier to the Tribunal's mailing address;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by fax or other electronic means to the Tribunal;
    or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} by ordinary mail to the Tribunal's mailing address.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt after 5:00 p.m.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document that is filed with the Tribunal after 5:00
  p.m., local time of the place where the sender is located, is
  considered to have been received on the next day that is not a
  holiday.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service on any party

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document may be served on a party in any of the following manners:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by hand delivery to the person that is the party;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by registered mail to the party's mailing address;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by courier to the party's mailing address;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by fax or other electronic means to the party; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} by ordinary mail to the party's mailing address.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service to legal counsel or
  representative

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document may be served on a party who is
  represented by legal counsel or another duly authorized representative
  by serving it on that party's legal counsel or duly authorized
  representative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt of service after 5:00 p.m.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A document that is served on a party after 5:00 p.m.,
  local time of the place where the sender is located, is deemed to have
  been received on the next day that is not a holiday.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registered mail or courier

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  filing or service of a document by registered mail or courier is
  effective on the day indicated on the receipt issued by the post
  office or courier service, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fax or other electronic means

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The filing or service of a document by fax or other
  electronic means is effective on the day on which it is sent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ordinary mail

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The filing or service of a document by ordinary mail
  is effective on the day indicated on the postmark stamped on the
  envelope or, if no postmark is legible, on the day of the receipt.

### [Representation of Parties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-816275 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Individuals

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual may act in person or be represented by legal counsel or by
  another duly authorized representative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporations, partnerships or
  associations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A corporation, partnership or unincorporated
  association must be represented by legal counsel or by an officer,
  partner or member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coordinates of counsel or
  representative

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party who is represented by legal counsel or
  another duly authorized representative must notify the Tribunal of the
  legal counsel or representative's contact information and of any
  changes to such information within seven days after the day on which
  the change is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of counsel or representative

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A party may change its legal counsel or
  representative by notifying the Tribunal of the change and the contact
  information of the new legal counsel or representative within seven
  days after the day on which that change is made.

### [Proceedings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-816285 .Subheading}

#### [General]{.HTitleText3} {#h-816286 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings public

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Proceedings of the Tribunal are
  public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[In camera]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  proceedings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may order that a proceeding be held [in
  camera]{.otherLang lang="la"} at the request of any party if the party
  satisfies the Tribunal that the circumstances of the case warrant the
  request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remote conference

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may order
that a hearing, or any other step in a proceeding, be conducted in whole
or in part by teleconference, videoconference or any other form of
electronic communication.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affidavit evidence

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may present evidence by affidavit by serving the affidavit on
  the other party and filing it with the Tribunal,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a proceeding by written submissions,
    within 30 days after the expiry of the time limits set out in rules
    36 or 52; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a proceeding by oral hearing, at
    least 15 days before the hearing date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Availability of deponents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party who intends to present evidence by affidavit
  must ensure that the deponent of the affidavit is available for
  cross-examination. The timing of the cross-examination is to be
  decided on the parties' mutual consent or, if the parties do not
  consent, by the Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transcript of cross-examination

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party who cross-examines the deponent of an
  affidavit must file the transcript of the cross-examination with the
  Tribunal within seven days after the day on which the
  cross-examination takes place.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taking notice

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may take
notice of any matter in order to expedite any proceeding.

#### [Hearings]{.HTitleText3} {#h-816305 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of proceeding

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal establishes
the order of proceeding at the start of the oral hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of witnesses

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may order witnesses to be excluded from the hearing while the
  other witnesses are called to give evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recording of hearings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Hearings before the Tribunal may be recorded.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oath or solemn affirmation

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person to be examined on an oral examination must take an oath or make
  a solemn affirmation before being examined.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination, cross-examination and
  re-examination

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party at a hearing is entitled to examine their own
  witnesses, cross-examine any witnesses of the other party and
  re-examine their own witnesses on matters raised in cross-examination.

### [Impartiality and Conflict of Interest]{.HTitleText2} {#h-816318 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parties raising bias or conflict of
interest

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party that believes that a member of the Tribunal is not in a position
  to act impartially in a matter or is in a conflict of interest must
  immediately give written notice to the Tribunal, stating the reason
  for the opinion.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision within seven days of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson of the Tribunal must issue a decision
  within seven days after the day on which he or she receives a notice
  under subrule (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of decision

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Chairperson determines that the member that
  was the subject of the notice is not in a position to act, the
  Chairperson must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exclude the member from the matter; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give any directions that he or she considers
    necessary for the matter to be reconvened with a differently
    constituted Tribunal or order a new hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tribunal member raising bias or conflict
of interest

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** If a member of the
Tribunal feels that he or she is not in a position to act impartially or
cannot review a matter due to a conflict of interest, the Chairperson
must direct that the matter be reconvened with a differently constituted
Tribunal or order a new hearing.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Review of Notices of Violation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-816330 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to all
proceedings before the Tribunal initiated as a result of a request made
in accordance with the Agriculture and Agri-Food Administrative Monetary
Penalties Regulations or the Agriculture and Agri-Food Administrative
Monetary Penalties Regulations Respecting the Pest Control Products Act
and Regulations under subsection 8(1), paragraph 9(2)(c) or paragraph
11(1)(b) of the Agriculture and Agri-Food Administrative Penalties Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgement of receipt

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** Within seven days after
the day on which the Tribunal receives a request referred to in rule 28,
the Tribunal must send

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgement of receipt of the request for
  review to the applicant; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgement of receipt of the request for
  review, along with a copy of the request for review, to the competent
  minister in relation to the violation or his or her delegated
  representative.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tribunal file

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Within 15 days after the
day on which the acknowledgement of receipt is sent, the Minister or his
or her delegated representative must file with the Tribunal

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} proof of service of the notice of violation that is
  the subject of the review; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement that there is no monetary penalty set out
  by the notice of violation or, if there is one, that it has not
  already been paid by the applicant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Addendum

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** Within 15 days after the
day on which a request referred in rule 28 is made, unless the following
information is already contained in the applicant's request for review,
the applicant must file with the Tribunal an addendum containing the
following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's full name, civic address, mailing
  address if different from their civic address and at least one of
  their telephone number, fax number and email address;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is represented by a representative,
  written authority from the applicant for the representative to so act,
  as well as the representative's full name, civic address, mailing
  address if different from their civic address and at least one of
  their telephone number, fax number and email address;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is represented by legal counsel,
  their full name, civic address, mailing address if different from
  their civic address and at least one of their telephone number, fax
  number and email address;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's reasons for the request, other than
  defences that are not allowed under subsection 18(1) of the
  Agriculture and Agri-Food Administrative Penalties Act, along with any
  supporting documents;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's choice of official language for the
  proceeding; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a complete copy of the notice of violation issued by
  the Canadian Food Inspection Agency, Health Canada or the Canada
  Border Services Agency that is being challenged.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order on admissibility

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal must make a decision on the admissibility of a request for
  review within 60 days after the day on which the acknowledgement of
  receipt of the request is sent to the parties, and send that decision
  to the parties in writing without delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grounds to consider

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal must, in coming to its decision on
  admissibility, consider any relevant factor, including whether

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister or his or her delegated representative
    has complied with all of the requirements of rule 30; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has complied with all of the
    requirements of rule 31.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's report

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** The Minister or his or
her delegated representative must, within 30 days after the day on which
the Tribunal decides that the request for review is admissible,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} serve on the applicant a report containing any
  information relating to the violation, along with any supporting
  documents, and, if applicable, a written confirmation of the
  Minister's refusal of the request to enter into a compliance agreement
  made under paragraph 9(2)(a) of the [Agriculture and Agri-Food
  Administrative Monetary Penalties Act](/eng/acts/A-8.8); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} file the report, any supporting documents and, if
  applicable, the written confirmation of the refusal set out in
  paragraph (a), along with proof that the report, supporting documents
  and, if applicable, the written confirmation of the refusal set out in
  paragraph (a) have been served on the applicant, with the Tribunal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgement of receipt

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** Within seven days after
the day on which the Tribunal receives the report, the Tribunal must
send an acknowledgement of receipt to the parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional submissions

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** Within 30 days after the
day on which the report is served, the applicant must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicate to the Tribunal whether they wish to proceed
  by way of oral hearing or by way of written submissions; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} file any additional submissions in relation to the
  report with the Tribunal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No further submissions

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** No further submissions
are to be filed after

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the expiry of the time limit for the filing of a
  Minister's report, if it has not been filed by the Minister or his or
  her delegated representative in accordance with rule 33;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the filing of the applicant's additional submissions
  in accordance with rule 35; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the expiry of the time limit set out in rule 35 for
  the filing of the applicant's additional submissions, if none has been
  filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision or notice of hearing

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal must, after
the day on which no further submissions are to be filed in accordance
with rule 36,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a review is proceeding by way of written
  submissions, render a decision based on the documents and exhibits
  received from the parties; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a review is proceeding by way of a hearing, send a
  notice of hearing to all parties at least 30 days before the hearing
  date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of witnesses

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** At least 20 days before
the hearing of a matter, each party must serve on the other party and
file with the Tribunal a list of the witnesses it intends to call, along
with their civic address, mailing address if different from their civic
address and telephone number.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to appear

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** If one of the parties
does not appear at the hearing, and if the Tribunal is satisfied that a
notice of the hearing was sent to the most recent address on file of
that party, the Tribunal may grant or dismiss the request for review, or
proceed with the hearing in the party's absence and dispose of the
review in any applicable manner referred to in section 14 of the
[Agriculture and Agri-Food Administrative Monetary Penalties
Act](/eng/acts/A-8.8).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Postponement and adjournment

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may postpone or adjourn a hearing on any terms that it
  considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delay

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any request for a postponement or an adjournment must
  be made at least 8 days before the hearing date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision after hearing or later

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may render a
decision orally at the end of a hearing or it may reserve its decision
until a later date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision to be sent

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal must provide
a decision in writing and send a copy of it to all parties without
delay.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Review of Minister's Decisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-816388 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to all
proceedings before the Tribunal initiated as a result of a request made
in accordance with the Agriculture and Agri-Food Administrative Monetary
Penalties Regulations or the Agriculture and Agri-Food Administrative
Monetary Penalties Regulations Respecting the Pest Control Products Act
and Regulations under subsection 12(2) or paragraph 13(2)(b) of the
Agriculture and Agri-Food Administrative Penalties Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No new evidence

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** The parties may present
new evidence only with the permission of the Tribunal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgement of receipt

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** Within seven days after
the day on which the Tribunal receives a request referred to in rule 43,
the Tribunal must send

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgement of receipt of the request for
  review to the applicant; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgement of receipt of the request, along
  with a copy of the applicant's request for review, to the competent
  minister in relation to the violation or his or her delegated
  representative.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tribunal file

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** Within 15 days after the
day on which the acknowledgement of receipt is sent, the Minister or his
or her delegated representative must file with the Tribunal proof that
the Minister's decision that is subject to the review has been served on
the applicant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Addendum

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** Within 15 days after the
day on which a request referred to in rule 43 is made, unless the
following information is already contained in the applicant's request
for review, the applicant must file with the Tribunal an addendum
containing the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's full name, civic address, mailing
  address if different from their civic address and at least one of
  their telephone number, fax number and email address;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is represented by a representative,
  written authority from the applicant for the representative to so act,
  as well as the representative's full name, civic address, mailing
  address if different from their civic address and at least one of
  their telephone number, fax number and email address;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is represented by legal counsel,
  their full name, civic address, mailing address if different from
  their civic address and at least one or more of their telephone
  number, fax number and email address;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's reasons to vary or set aside the
  Minister's decision;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's choice of official language for the
  proceeding;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the Minister's decision, including any
  reasons; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a complete copy of the notice of violation issued by
  the Canadian Food Inspection Agency, Health Canada or by the Canada
  Border Services Agency that is being challenged.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision on admissibility

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal must make a decision on the admissibility of the request for
  review within 60 days after the day on which the acknowledgement of
  receipt of a request is sent to the parties, and send that decision to
  the parties in writing without delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grounds to consider

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal must, in coming to its decision on
  admissibility, consider any relevant factor, including whether

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister or his or her delegated representative
    has complied with rule 46; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has complied with all of the
    requirements of rule 47.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents relating to the decision

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** The Minister or his or
her delegated representative must, within 30 days after the day on which
the Tribunal decides that the request for review is admissible,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} serve on the applicant a certified copy of all
  documents or exhibits relevant to the request for review that are in
  the possession of the Minister whose decision is subject to the
  review;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} file with the Tribunal the certified copy of all
  documents or exhibits relevant to the request for review that are in
  the possession of the Minister whose decision is subject to the review
  along with proof that the documents were served on the applicant and
  any written confirmation of the Minister's refusal to enter into a
  compliance agreement pursuant to a request made under paragraph
  9(2)(a) of the [Agriculture and Agri-Food Administrative Monetary
  Penalties Act](/eng/acts/A-8.8); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if there are any documents or exhibits that cannot be
  reproduced,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} serve on the applicant a notice of filing
    containing a list of the documents or exhibits that cannot be
    reproduced, along with the reasons why they cannot be reproduced;
    and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} file the original documents or exhibits with the
    Tribunal along with proof that the notice of filing and reasons were
    served on the applicant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgement of receipt

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** Within seven days after
the day on which documents or exhibits were filed in accordance with
rule 49, the Tribunal must send an acknowledgement of receipt of the
material to the parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for oral hearing and additional
reasons

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** Within 30 days after the
day on which the documents or exhibits that are in the possession of the
Minister are filed in accordance with rule 49, the applicant must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicate to the Tribunal whether they wish to proceed
  by way of oral hearing or by way of written submissions; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} file any additional reasons in response to the
  documents and exhibits filed by the Minister or his or her delegated
  representative with the Tribunal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No further reasons

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** No further reasons can be
filed after

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the expiry of the time limit for the filing of the
  Minister's documents or exhibits by the Minister or his or her
  delegated representative in accordance with rule 49, if they have not
  been filed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the filing of the applicant's additional reasons in
  accordance with rule 51; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the expiry of the time limit for the filing of the
  applicant's additional reasons in accordance with rule 51, if none has
  been filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision or notice of hearing

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal must, after
the day on which no further reasons are to be filed in accordance with
rule 52,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a review is proceeding by way of written
  submissions, render a decision based on the documents or exhibits
  received from the parties; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a review is proceeding by way of oral hearing,
  send a notice of hearing to all parties at least 30 days before the
  hearing date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to appear

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** If one of the parties
does not appear at the hearing, and if the Tribunal is satisfied that a
notice of the hearing was sent to the most recent address on file of
that party, the Tribunal may grant or dismiss the request for review, or
proceed with the hearing in the party's absence and dispose of the
review in any manner referred to in section 14 of the [Agriculture and
Agri-Food Administrative Monetary Penalties Act](/eng/acts/A-8.8).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Postponements and adjournments

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may postpone or adjourn a hearing on any terms that it
  considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delay

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any request for a postponement or an adjournment must
  be made at least 8 days before the hearing date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decisions after hearing or later

**[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may render a
decision orally at the end of a hearing or it may reserve its decision
until a later date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision to be sent without delay

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal must provide
a decision in writing and send a copy of it to all parties without
delay.
