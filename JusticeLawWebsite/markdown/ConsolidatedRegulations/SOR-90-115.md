## Royal Canadian Mounted Police Training Academy Fees Order {#royal-canadian-mounted-police-training-academy-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-115
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1990-02-02

Order Prescribing the Fees To Be Paid by Persons to whom Services or the
Use of Facilities is Provided by the Royal Canadian Mounted Police at
the R.C.M.P. Training Academy in Regina, Saskatchewan
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Solicitor General of Canada, pursuant to Order in Council P.C.
1989-1898 of September 21, 1989[[Footnote

Regina, Saskatchewan](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-115).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921482 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Training Academy Fees
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-115).

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921484 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any person to whom the
Royal Canadian Mounted Police provide one of the following services or
the use of one of the following facilities at the Royal Canadian Mounted
Police Training Academy in Regina, Saskatchewan, shall pay the following
fees:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of academic training provided in a
  classroom by an RCMP instructor, \$31 per hour;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of gas and small arms training, \$24 per
  hour;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of driver training provided by an RCMP
  instructor using an RCMP vehicle, \$31 per hour;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of accommodation \$17 per day; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the use of a classroom by a person
  other than an RCMP instructor, \$17 per hour.
