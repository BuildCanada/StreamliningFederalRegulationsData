## Rail Rate Freeze Compensatory Payments Order {#rail-rate-freeze-compensatory-payments-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/76-31
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 4, 1975

Registration 1976-03-24

Order Prescribing Terms and Conditions Respecting Compensatory Payments
for For-Hire Highway Transportation Companies, Non-Federally Regulated
Railway Companies and Water Transportation Companies

P.C. 1976-503 1976-03-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport and the Treasury Board, pursuant to Transport
Vote 50a, Appropriation Act No. 4, 1975, is pleased hereby to make the
annexed Order prescribing terms and conditions respecting compensatory
payments for for-hire highway transportation companies, non-federally
regulated railway companies and water transportation companies.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634001 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Rail Rate Freeze Compensatory Payments
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-76-31).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634003 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Transport
    Commission; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[claim]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[claim]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a claim for compensation filed
    with the Commission pursuant to subsection 3(1);
    ([réclamation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a for-hire highway transportation company, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a non-federally regulated railway company or a
      water transportation company whose tariffs are differentially
      related to railway tariffs of federally regulated companies;
      ([compagnie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[revenue foregone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[revenue foregone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the actual revenues
    lost by a company on traffic actually carried and as a direct result
    of the rail rate freeze. ([perte subie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Claims for Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634011 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a company may file with the Commission a claim for
  compensation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within thirty days of the publication of this Order
    in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), in respect of
    revenue foregone as a result of the rail rate freeze during the 1973
    calendar year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within ninety days of the publication of this Order
    in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), in respect of
    revenue foregone as a result of the rail rate freeze during the 1974
    calendar year.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every claim shall set out separately in respect of
  each movement of goods the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of shipment of the goods;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the shipper and consignee;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the origin and destination of the goods;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the goods;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the tolls, rates, charges or allowances in respect
    of the carriers' freight-bill;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the total freight charge or revenue;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the claim for compensation;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the carrier's tariff items covering the movement
    and the corresponding tariff items for federally regulated railways
    containing rates that were subject to the rail rate freeze;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the percentage or specific increases
    in highway transport rates that would have been applied but for the
    rail rate freeze and the dates on which such increases would have
    been applied; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the percentage of specific increases
    in highway transport rates and the effective dates of such increases
    for the 1971 and 1972 calendar years.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every company shall provide with its claims a written
  undertaking by the company that its records will be made available for
  inspection by the Commission at a time and place convenient to the
  company.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every company shall provide with its claim a
  declaration in the form set out in section 38 of the [Canada Evidence
  Act](/eng/acts/C-5) declaring that no compensation is being claimed in
  respect of movements of manufacturer's goods in cases where the
  company is directly integrated with the manufacturer or owner of such
  goods.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the Commission is of the opinion that a claim
  is accompanied by information that is insufficient for the
  determination of the amount that may be paid to the company under
  section 5, the Commission may require the company to submit such
  further information as the Commission deems necessary.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (7), where, within the period
  set out in paragraph (1)(a) or (b), whichever is applicable, a company
  cannot provide the information required by subsection (2), the company
  may submit that information in respect of the first full week in each
  of the months of February, May, August and November of the 1973 or
  1974 calendar year, as the case may be, in order that the total amount
  of the claim may be estimated.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may require a company that has
  submitted information pursuant to subsection (6) to submit information
  in respect of such other weeks or months of the 1973 or 1974 calendar
  year as the Commission may deem necessary.

## [Advance Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634032 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Upon receipt of a claim and
any information filed by a company pursuant to section 3, the Commission
may recommend to the Minister that advance payments of not more than
twenty per cent of the total amount claimed by the company be made to
the company subject to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} adjustment pursuant to audit as required by the
  Commission; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the posting by the company of a security bond payable
  to Her Majesty in an amount equal to the advance payments.

## [Payment of Claims]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634036 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 6, the Commission shall, on the basis of the claims and all
  information filed, determine the total amount that may be paid to a
  company in respect of its claims and recommend to the Minister that
  payment be made accordingly.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Upon receipt of a recommendation under subsection
  (1), the Minister may pay to a company the amounts determined by the
  Commission.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may, at any time, adjust the total
  amount determined pursuant to subsection (1) and any amount paid to a
  company in excess of the adjusted amount shall be repayable forthwith
  to Her Majesty.

<!-- -->

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) to (4), the audited claims of each company shall be
  calculated by the Commission as a percentage of the total audited
  claims of all companies and that percentage shall determine the total
  amount to be paid to a company.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3) or (4), the total payment
  to a company shall not exceed 115/183rds of its total audited claims.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The total payments to all for-hire highway
  transportation companies shall not exceed \$10,000,000.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The total payments to all non-federally regulated
  railway companies and water transportation companies whose tariffs are
  differentially related to railway tariffs of federally regulated
  companies shall not exceed \$3,000,000.
