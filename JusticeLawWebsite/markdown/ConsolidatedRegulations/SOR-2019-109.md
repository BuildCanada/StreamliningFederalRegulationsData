## [Low-materiality Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1124158 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of subsection 22(1) of the Act, the following fees are
  considered to be low-materiality fees:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any fee that is less than \$51;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any fee that is \$51 or more but less than \$151 if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the three most recent reports tabled in respect
      of the fee before the first day of the current fiscal year set out
      no more than \$500,000 of annual revenue received from that fee,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the fee has been adjusted since the tabling of
      the most recent report in respect of it and the three most recent
      reports tabled in respect of it set out no more than \$500,000 of
      annual revenue received from that fee,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} there have not been three reports in respect of
      the fee and the fee has, at no time since the day on which these
      Regulations came into force, been \$151 or more or referred to in
      Schedule 2, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the fee was previously considered a
      low-materiality fee in accordance with paragraph (a),
      subparagraphs (i) to (iii) or paragraph (c) and has not since
      ceased to be a low-materiality fee in accordance with section 3;
      and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fees referred to in Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraphs (1)(a) and (b) do not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any fee the amount of which is determined by a
    formula; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fees referred to in Schedule 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of low-materiality

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  low-materiality fee ceases to be a low-materiality fee on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which it is increased to \$151 or more;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the fee is \$51 or more but less than \$151, the
    first day of the fiscal year after the tabling of a third
    consecutive report setting out more than \$500,000 of annual revenue
    received from that fee;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which it is adjusted, if the adjusted
    fee is \$51 or more but less than \$151 and the three most recent
    reports tabled in respect of the fee set out more than \$500,000 of
    annual revenue received from that fee;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the day on which it is added to Schedule 2; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the day on which it is removed from Schedule 1 if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is \$151 or more, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it is \$51 or more but less than \$151 and the
      three most recent reports tabled in respect of it set out more
      than \$500,000 of annual revenue received from that fee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraphs (1)(a) to (d) do not apply to fees
  referred to in Schedule 1.

## [Power to Amend Schedules]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1485334 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to be taken into account

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** The President of the
Treasury Board must take into account the following factors when
amending the schedules in the exercise of the power referred to in
subsection 22(3) of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the fee to which the amendment relates;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the context in which the fee is charged, including
  the characteristics of the persons to whom it is charged;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the annual revenue received from the fee as set out
  in the three most recent reports tabled in respect of the fee; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any inaccuracies in the fee descriptions set out in
  the schedules, including as a result of an amendment to the instrument
  by which the fee is fixed.

## [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1124185 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees from \$51 to \$151

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Despite subparagraph
2(1)(b)(iii), a fee that is \$51 or more but less than \$151 is not,
unless it is referred to in Schedule 1, considered to be a
low-materiality fee if, during the one-year period before the day on
which these Regulations come into force, a report setting out more than
\$500,000 of revenue received from that fee was tabled or a document to
the same effect was published on the website of the federal entity in
relation to which the fee was fixed.
