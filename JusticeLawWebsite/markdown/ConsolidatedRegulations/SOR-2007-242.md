## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736472 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[assessable property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assessable property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means reserve land, an
    interest in reserve lands or a right to occupy, possess or use
    reserve lands, that is subject to assessment under a property
    assessment law. ([bien sujet à évaluation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[assessor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assessor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person designated by a
    First Nation to conduct assessments of assessable
    property. ([évaluateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[property assessment law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[property assessment law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a law made
    under subparagraph 5(1)(a)(i) of the [First Nations Fiscal
    Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.67). ([texte législatif relatif à
    l'évaluation foncière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Inspections]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736480 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedures

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Inspections of assessable
property for assessment purposes that are provided for in a property
assessment law shall be carried out in accordance with procedures
prescribed in sections 3 to 7 or procedures that are set out in the laws
in respect of assessment inspections in the province in which the
assessable property is located.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-29, s. 19
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of inspection

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  attending to inspect an assessable property, the assessor shall
  deliver a notice of assessment inspection to the person named on the
  assessment roll at the address indicated on the roll.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of assessment inspection for an assessable
  property shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} include a description of the assessable property;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out the proposed date and time when the
    inspection will take place and invite the person named on the
    assessment roll to contact the assessor at a telephone number set
    out in the notice to make arrangements for a different date and
    time, if necessary; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} describe the alternate assessment procedure
    authorized under section 6.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to assessable property

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If an assessable property
is occupied by a person other than the person named on the assessment
roll, the person named on the assessment roll shall make arrangements
with the occupant to provide access to the assessor.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Business hours

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise requested
by the person named on the assessment roll, inspections of an assessable
property shall be conducted between 09:00 and 17:00 local time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment without inspection

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** If the assessor attends at
an assessable property to inspect it and no occupant 18 years of age or
older is present or permission to inspect the property is denied, the
assessor may assess the value of the assessable property based on the
information available to him or her.

## [Delivery of Notices]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736499 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Methods of delivery

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Delivery
  of a notice may be made personally or by sending it by mail, fax or
  e-mail.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal delivery

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Personal delivery of a notice is made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of delivery to a residential dwelling,
    by leaving the notice with a person at least 18 years of age
    residing there; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of delivery to any other assessable
    property, by leaving the notice with the person apparently in
    charge, at the time of delivery, of the assessable property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of delivery

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice shall be considered to have been delivered

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if delivered personally, at the time that personal
    delivery is made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if sent by mail, five days after the day on which
    the notice is postmarked;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if sent by fax, at the time indicated on the
    confirmation of transmission; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if sent by e-mail, at the time indicated in the
    electronic confirmation that the e-mail has been opened.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736513 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
