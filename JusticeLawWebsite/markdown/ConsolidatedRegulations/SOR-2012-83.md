## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-792103 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[alternate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[alternate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an indeterminate employee
    who is appointed or deployed to the position of an opting employee.
    ([remplaçant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[opting employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[opting employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an indeterminate
    employee whose work unit is relocated and who does not wish to
    relocate or whose services are no longer required by reason of lack
    of work, the discontinuance of a function or the transfer of work or
    a function outside the public service and who has not received a
    guarantee of a reasonable job offer. ([fonctionnaire
    optant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-792107 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
an alternate who is excluded from the application of section 16,
paragraph 22(2)(a), subsection 29(3), sections 30, 31 and 39.1 to 48,
subsection 51(4) and section 77 of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

## [Alternates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-792111 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An alternate must submit an
irrevocable resignation from employment in the public service that is
accepted by the deputy head and that is to take effect no later than
five days after the day on which they are appointed or deployed to the
position of the opting employee.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An alternate must not
perform the duties of the position of the opting employee.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An alternate ceases to be
an employee in the public service on the day on which the irrevocable
resignation takes effect.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-792115 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
