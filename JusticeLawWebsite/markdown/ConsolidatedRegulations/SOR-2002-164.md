## National Capital Commission Animal Regulations {#national-capital-commission-animal-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2002-164
:::

[NATIONAL CAPITAL ACT](/eng/acts/N-4)

Registration 2002-04-25

National Capital Commission Animal Regulations

P.C. 2002-671  2002-04-25
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Canadian Heritage, pursuant to subsection 20(1) of the
[National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4), hereby makes the annexed
[National Capital Commission Animal
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-164).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-681876 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Capital Pathway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Capital Pathway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mixed use
    recreational pathway on Commission land that has a paved or stone
    dust surface and has been marked by the Commission at access points
    maintained by the Commission and at intersections with signs or
    surface markings bearing the pictogram in the form set out in
    Schedule 2. ([sentier de la capitale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means real property or
    immovables owned by the Commission or under the control and
    management of the Commission. ([terrain de la
    Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[domestic animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[domestic animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an animal of a species
    of vertebrates, other than fish, that has been domesticated by
    humans so as to live in a tame condition and depend on humans for
    survival. ([animal domestique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[keeper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[keeper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the owner of an animal or the
    person having charge of the animal, except where the owner or the
    person is a minor, in which case [[keeper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means the father or mother of the minor or another adult responsible
    for the minor. ([responsable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[leased agricultural property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[leased agricultural property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    Commission land, of which the Commission is the lessor, that is
    leased for commercial or non-commercial agricultural purposes, and
    includes any residence on the land. ([propriété agricole
    louée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[leased land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[leased land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means leased agricultural
    property or leased residential property. ([terrain
    loué]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[leased residential property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[leased residential property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Commission
    land, of which the Commission is the lessor, that is leased strictly
    for residential purposes. ([propriété résidentielle
    louée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[off-leash area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[off-leash area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area on unleased
    land referred to in section 9. ([aire pour animaux en
    liberté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[organized event]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[organized event]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a temporary activity
    or a seasonal program that is organized or authorized by the
    Commission. ([événement organisé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police who
      is a peace officer;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who is employed by the Commission as a
      conservation officer and who has been designated as a peace
      officer under paragraph 7(1)(d) of the [Royal Canadian Mounted
      Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10); or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a police officer of the municipality in which the
      Commission land is situated. ([agent de la paix]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[picnic area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[picnic area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area that contains a
    group of picnic tables that are permanently affixed or chained to
    the ground, or that is identified by signs as a picnic area.
    ([terrain de pique-nique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shoreline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shoreline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the high-water mark. ([ligne
    de rive]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[trail]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[trail]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mixed use trail, other than a
    Capital Pathway, on Commission land that has been marked by the
    Commission at entry points and intersections with signs bearing the
    trail name or number or a pictogram. ([sentier]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[unleased land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unleased land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Commission land that is
    not subject to a lease between the Commission as lessor and another
    party as lessee. ([terrain non loué]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-681895 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on all unleased land; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on all leased land that is subject to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a lease entered into on or after the day on which
    these Regulations come into force that states that the lease is
    subject to these Regulations as amended from time to time, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a lease that has been renewed or amended on or
    after the day on which these Regulations come into force that states
    that the lease, as renewed or amended, is subject to these
    Regulations as amended from time to time.

<!-- -->

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections
  4 to 8 and subsection 10(2) do not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} peace officers in the exercise of their duties;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Commission employees in the exercise of their
    duties; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} persons who are assisting a peace officer or a
    Commission employee in the exercise of the officer's or employee's
    duties.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 6(1) and (2), section 8 and paragraph
  20(1)(a) do not apply to a blind or visually impaired keeper of a
  guide dog, or to a physically disabled keeper of an assistance dog,
  while they are being accompanied by that dog.

## [Unleased Land --- Prohibitions and Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-681907 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-681908 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall have an animal other than a domestic animal on unleased
  land.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall have more than two domestic animals
  on unleased land at any one time.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The prohibition in subsection (1) shall not have the
  effect of preventing the Commission from having, on Commission lands,
  wildlife indigenous to those lands.

### [Domestic Animals Other Than Hoofed Animals]{.HTitleText2} {#h-681913 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Sections 6 to 9 apply only
to keepers of domestic animals other than hoofed animals.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), no person shall have a domestic animal on unleased
  land except in the locations set out in Schedule 1.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the locations set out in Schedule 1, no person
  shall have a domestic animal in any of the following areas:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a beach and its immediate environs, the boundaries
    of which are clearly identified by signs;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a building;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the campground located at LeBreton Flats;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a picnic area, food concession area or outdoor
    restaurant;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a play structure area;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (3), within three metres of
    the shoreline of a body of water of a permanent nature;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} an area in which an organized event is being held;
    or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any area of unleased land that is marked, in
    accordance with subsection 27(1), by signs as an area where domestic
    animals are prohibited.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The prohibition contained in paragraph (2)(f) does
  not apply to the Queen Elizabeth Driveway corridor land or the Colonel
  By Drive corridor land.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall have a domestic animal referred to in
  section 5 in any area of unleased land that is marked, in accordance
  with subsection 27(2), by signs as a location where hoofed animals are
  permitted.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite any other provision of these Regulations, the
  keeper of a domestic animal is authorized to cross, with their animal,
  any area of unleased land where such an animal is otherwise prohibited
  under these Regulations if the animal is restrained in accordance with
  subsection 7(1) and the animal is on a Capital Pathway or trail where
  the presence of that animal is permitted.

<!-- -->

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall have a domestic animal on unleased land where domestic
  animals are permitted unless the animal is restrained in one of the
  following ways:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), it is restrained on a
    leash or by the handle of a harness

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that is held by a person, or is securely attached
      to an object that cannot be displaced by the animal, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is not more than two metres (6.5 feet)
      long;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is in a container from which it cannot escape;
    or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is in a vehicle.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall, on unleased land,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ski, rollerskate or rollerblade while having a
    domestic animal on a leash; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ride a bicycle, scooter or any other non-motorized
    vehicle --- other than a wheelchair --- while having a domestic
    animal on a leash or while having a domestic animal attached in some
    manner to the vehicle.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Every keeper of a domestic
animal shall immediately pick up any fecal matter deposited by the
animal and shall remove the matter from unleased land.

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite
  any other provision of these Regulations, the keeper of a domestic
  animal may allow the animal to run free on a portion of unleased land
  that the Commission has marked by signs as an off-leash area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The keeper of a domestic animal shall have the animal
  under control within the off-leash area.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), the keeper of a
  domestic animal is considered to have the animal under control if the
  animal, following a voice command or hand signal given by the keeper,
  obeys immediately and, as the case may be,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} stops attacking or chasing another animal or a
    person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} stops any display of aggression toward another
    animal or a person;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} stops any behaviour toward a person that a
    reasonable person would find harassing or disturbing; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} comes to and stays with the keeper.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite any other provision of these Regulations, the
  keeper of a domestic animal is authorized, for the purpose of
  accessing an off-leash area, to cross, with their animal, any parking
  lot of the Commission adjacent to the area if the animal is restrained
  in accordance with subsection 7(1).

### [Hoofed Animals]{.HTitleText2} {#h-681949 .Subheading}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  section applies only to keepers of hoofed animals.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall have a hoofed animal on any area of
  unleased land except in a location that the Commission has, in
  accordance with subsection 27(2), marked by signs as a location where
  hoofed animals are permitted.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall have a hoofed animal in a location
  referred to in subsection (2) unless it is restrained in one of the
  following ways:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is restrained by reins that are held by a
    person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is restrained by a lead

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that is held by a person, or is securely attached
      to an object that cannot be displaced by the animal, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is not more than two metres (6.5 feet)
      long; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is in a container from which it cannot escape.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite any other provision of these Regulations, the
  keeper of a hoofed animal is authorized to cross, with their hoofed
  animal, any area of unleased land where a hoofed animal is otherwise
  prohibited under these Regulations if the animal is restrained in
  accordance with subsection (3) and the animal is on a trail where the
  presence of that animal is permitted.

### [Domestic Animals]{.HTitleText2} {#h-681960 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Sections 12 to 14 apply
to keepers of all domestic animals.

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall use a domestic animal in an organized event on unleased
  land without the authorization of the Commission under subsection
  28(1).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall use a domestic animal to pull a sled,
  wagon or other conveyance on unleased land except as part of an
  organized event in respect of which an authorization has been issued
  by the Commission under subsection 28(1).

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The keeper of a domestic
animal shall not allow the animal, while on unleased land, to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} chase, attack, bite or injure a person or another
  animal or fight with another animal;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} damage property of the Commission;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} make noise for more than 15 minutes between the hours
  of 10:00 p.m. and 7:00 a.m.; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} drink from, or get on or into, an ornamental or
  drinking fountain, or a body of water of a permanent nature surrounded
  by Commission land.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The keeper of a domestic
animal shall not, on unleased land, leave the animal unattended in a
vehicle or container in excessive heat or cold that could endanger the
health of the animal.

## [Leased Land --- Prohibitions and Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-681971 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-681972 .Subheading}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  lessee shall have an animal other than a domestic animal on leased
  land.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The keeper of a domestic animal shall not allow the
  animal, while on leased land, to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} chase, attack, bite or injure a person or another
    animal or fight with another animal;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} damage property of the Commission; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} enter any body of water of a permanent nature
    surrounded by Commission land or drink from that body of water.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The keeper of a domestic animal shall not, on leased
  land, leave the animal unattended in a vehicle or container in
  excessive heat or cold that could endanger the health of the animal.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The keeper of a domestic animal shall not, on leased
  land, have the animal within three metres of the shoreline of any body
  of water of a permanent nature.

### [Leased Residential Property]{.HTitleText2} {#h-681981 .Subheading}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** No person shall have a
hoofed animal on leased residential property.

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  lessee shall allow more than three domestic animals to be on the
  lessee's leased residential property at one time, except if the
  additional animals are unweaned young of any of the three permitted
  animals.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No lessee shall allow a domestic animal that is on
  the lessee's leased residential property to make noise for more than
  15 minutes between the hours of 10:00 p.m. and 7:00 a.m.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every lessee shall pick up and dispose of fecal
  matter deposited by a domestic animal on the lessee's leased
  residential property.

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** No person shall have a
domestic animal on leased residential property unless it is restrained
in one of the following ways:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is in the residence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is restrained on a leash or by the handle of a
  harness

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that is held by a person, or is securely attached
    to an object that cannot be displaced by the animal, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is not more than two metres (6.5 feet) long;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is in a container from which it cannot escape;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} it is in an enclosure or behind a fence that is of a
  construction and dimensions sufficient to keep it within the limits of
  the property; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} it is in a location on the property controlled by an
  invisible electronic fencing system.

### [Leased Agricultural Property]{.HTitleText2} {#h-681995 .Subheading}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A lessee of leased
agricultural property who is the keeper of a domestic animal shall use
effective measures to keep it within the limits of the property.

## [Powers of Peace Officers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-681997 .Part}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  peace officer shall order the keeper of an animal to remove the animal
  from Commission land if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the animal is on Commission land in a location
    where the keeper is prohibited from having the animal without the
    authorization of the Commission under subsection 28(1) and the
    keeper has not obtained that authorization; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the peace officer believes, on reasonable grounds,
    that the animal is acting in a dangerous or destructive fashion.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person to whom an order is given under
  subsection (1) shall comply with that order.

<!-- -->

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  peace officer is authorized to capture an animal that is unrestrained
  on Commission land in contravention of these Regulations if the keeper
  of the animal is not present or is unable or unwilling to capture the
  animal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When the keeper of the animal is not present, the
  peace officer is authorized to impound the animal at the expense of
  the owner.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When the keeper of the animal is present, the peace
  officer shall order the keeper

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a domestic animal, to restrain the
    animal as described in section 7 or 10, as applicable, or, if it is
    not possible to restrain the animal in such a fashion, to remove the
    animal from Commission land; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an animal other than a domestic
    animal, to remove the animal from Commission land.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every person to whom an order is given under
  subsection (3) shall comply with that order.

<!-- -->

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), a peace
  officer is authorized to take any injured animal that they find or
  capture pursuant to subsection 21(1) to a veterinarian for medical
  care at the expense of the owner.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), if the peace officer
  believes, on reasonable grounds, that the animal is so severely
  injured that the most humane action would be to destroy the animal,
  the peace officer is authorized to destroy it.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before taking any action under subsection (1) or (2),
  the peace officer shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the keeper of the animal is not present, use
    reasonable efforts to locate the keeper; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the keeper is present or is located, obtain
    their consent to the action proposed.

<!-- -->

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), if, on
  unleased land, a peace officer finds an animal unattended in a vehicle
  or container in excessive heat or cold that could endanger the health
  of the animal, the peace officer is authorized to take reasonable
  measures to remove the animal from the vehicle or container.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before taking any action under subsection (1), the
  peace officer shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} use reasonable efforts to locate the keeper of the
    animal; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the keeper is present or is located, obtain
    their consent to the action proposed.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When a peace officer removes an animal from a vehicle
  or container pursuant to subsection (1) and the keeper of the animal
  is not present, the peace officer is authorized to impound the animal
  at the expense of the owner.

<!-- -->

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  having taken all reasonable measures to stop the animal, a peace
  officer is authorized to destroy an animal that is attacking the peace
  officer or another person on Commission land if the peace officer
  believes, on reasonable grounds, that it would be too dangerous for
  the peace officer or the keeper of the animal, if present, to attempt
  to capture the animal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} After having taken all reasonable measures to stop
  the animal, a peace officer is authorized to destroy an animal that is
  attacking another animal on Commission land if the peace officer
  believes, on reasonable grounds, that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the animal being attacked may suffer serious injury
    or death; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it would be too dangerous for the peace officer or
    the keeper of the animal, if present, to attempt to capture the
    animal.

<!-- -->

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), after
  having taken all reasonable measures to stop the animal, a peace
  officer is authorized to destroy an animal that is at large on
  Commission land if the peace officer believes, on reasonable grounds,
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the continued presence of the animal will

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} damage property of the Commission, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} pose a threat to public safety; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it would be too dangerous for the peace officer to
    attempt to capture the animal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before destroying an animal under subsection (1), the
  peace officer shall use reasonable efforts to locate the keeper of the
  animal.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the keeper of the animal is present or is located,
  the peace officer shall warn the keeper that the animal will be
  destroyed unless it is controlled or captured.

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** When a peace officer
impounds an animal, takes an animal to a veterinarian or destroys an
animal pursuant to any of sections 21 to 25, the peace officer shall, as
soon as possible, inform the owner of the animal of the peace officer's
actions, if the name and the address, phone number or other indicators
of the whereabouts of the owner can be readily ascertained from

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} tags or other identification worn by the animal;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any tattoo or other marking on the animal; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other source of information readily available.

## [Powers of the Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-682039 .Part}

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  locations on unleased land where domestic animals are otherwise
  permitted under these Regulations, the Commission may, by signs, mark
  an area where domestic animals are prohibited, if such a prohibition
  is necessary for reasons of public safety or for protection of
  property of the Commission.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In areas of unleased land where hoofed animals are
  not otherwise permitted under these Regulations, the Commission may,
  by signs, mark an area where hoofed animals are permitted, if the
  presence of hoofed animals does not pose a threat to public safety or
  to property of the Commission.

<!-- -->

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  presence of an animal does not pose a threat to public safety or
  property of the Commission, the Commission shall, on application,
  issue a written authorization to a person to use the animal in an
  organized event on Commission land.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The keeper of an animal shall, while the animal is on
  Commission land pursuant to an authorization issued under subsection
  (1), control the animal at all times in accordance with the terms of
  the authorization.

<!-- -->

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  authorization issued pursuant to subsection 28(1) shall, at a minimum,
  specify the duration of the authorization, the description of
  Commission land to which the authorization applies and any special
  animal restraining measures or public health and safety requirements.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person contravenes these Regulations by virtue
  only of the fact that the person does anything permitted in an
  authorization issued pursuant to subsection 28(1) while that
  authorization remains in effect.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An authorization issued pursuant to subsection 28(1)
  is no longer in effect if the person to whom it was issued fails to
  comply with any term or condition of the authorization.

## [Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-682050 .Part}

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes these Regulations is liable on summary conviction to a fine
of not more than \$500 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than
six months, or to both.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-682052 .Part}

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.

## [SCHEDULE 1]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsections 6(1) and (2))]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-682055 .scheduleLabel}
