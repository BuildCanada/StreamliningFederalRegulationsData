## Order Transferring the Control and Supervision of Certain Portions of the Federal Public Administration in Passport Canada from the Department of Foreign Affairs and International Trade to the Department of Citizenship and Immigration and to the Department of Human Resources and Skills Development {#order-transferring-the-control-and-supervision-of-certain-portions-of-the-federal-public-administration-in-passport-canada-from-the-department-of-foreign-affairs-and-international-trade-to-the-department-of-citizenship-and-immigration-and-to-the-department-of-human-resources-and-skills-development .Title-of-Act}

:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2013-05-22

Order Transferring the Control and Supervision of Certain Portions of
the Federal Public Administration in Passport Canada from the Department
of Foreign Affairs and International Trade to the Department of
Citizenship and Immigration and to the Department of Human Resources and
Skills Development

P.C. 2013-540 2013-05-07
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EEEAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBEAA},
effective July 2, 2013,

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EEEAA){#fn_612645E_hq_7867}S.C.
  2003, c. 22, s. 207
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBEAA){#fn_612645E_hq_7868}R.S., c.
  P-34
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} transfers to the Department of Citizenship and
  Immigration from the Department of Foreign Affairs and International
  Trade the control and supervision of those portions of the federal
  public administration in Passport Canada within the Department of
  Foreign Affairs and International Trade known as

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Office of the Chief Executive Officer,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Strategic Initiatives Division of the
    Operations Bureau,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the e-Passport project team in the Operations
    Bureau,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the Policy, Research and Communications Bureau,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the Corporate Services and Human Resources Bureau,
    except for the regional Human Resources Units,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the Security Bureau,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the Business Information and Technology Bureau,
    except for the regional Information Technology Units,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the Enterprise Alignment and Innovation Bureau,

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the Administrative Support unit of the
    Departmental Legal Services Unit,

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} the Official Travel Section of the Central
    Operations Division of the Operations Bureau,

  - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} the Certificate of Identity Section of the Central
    Operations Division of the Operations Bureau, and

  - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} the Complex Case Management Units of the
    Operations Bureau; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} transfers to the Department of Human Resources and
  Skills Development from the Department of Foreign Affairs and
  International Trade the control and supervision of those portions of
  the federal public administration in Passport Canada within the
  Department of Foreign Affairs and International Trade known as

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Office of the Chief Operating Officer,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Strategic Operations Division of the
    Operations Bureau,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Functional Services Division of the
    Operations Bureau,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the Western Operations Division of the Operations
    Bureau,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the Eastern Operations Division of the Operations
    Bureau,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the Ontario Operations Division of the Operations
    Bureau,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the Central Operations Division of the Operations
    Bureau, except for the Official Travel Section and the Certificate
    of Identity Section,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the regional Human Resources Units of the
    Corporate Services and Human Resources Bureau, and

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the regional Information Technology Units of the
    Business Information and Technology Bureau.
