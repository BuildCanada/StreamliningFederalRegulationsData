## Order Transferring from the Department of Transport to the Department of Fisheries and Oceans the Control and Supervision of the Canadian Coast Guard other Than the Harbours and Ports Directorate and the Regional Harbours and Ports Branches, the Marine Regulatory Directorate, and the Ship Inspection Directorate and the Regional Ship Inspection Branches {#order-transferring-from-the-department-of-transport-to-the-department-of-fisheries-and-oceans-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-canadian-coast-guard-other-than-the-harbours-and-ports-directorate-and-the-regional-harbours-and-ports-branches-the-marine-regulatory-directorate-and-the-ship-inspection-directorate-and-the-regional-ship-inspection-branches .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/95-46
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1995-04-19

Order Transferring from the Department of Transport to the Department of
Fisheries and Oceans the Control and Supervision of the Canadian Coast
Guard other Than the Harbours and Ports Directorate and the Regional
Harbours and Ports Branches, the Marine Regulatory Directorate, and the
Ship Inspection Directorate and the Regional Ship Inspection Branches

P.C. 1995-527  1995-03-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), is pleased
hereby to transfer, effective April 1, 1995, to the Department of
Fisheries and Oceans the control and supervision of that portion of the
public service in the Department of Transport known as the Canadian
Coast Guard, other than

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Harbours and Ports Directorate and the regional
  Harbours and Ports Branches;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Marine Regulatory Directorate; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Ship Inspection Directorate and the regional Ship
  Inspection Branches.
