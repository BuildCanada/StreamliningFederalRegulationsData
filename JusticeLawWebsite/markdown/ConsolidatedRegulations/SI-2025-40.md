## Order Transferring from the Minister of State (Tourism) to the Minister of Industry the Powers, Duties and Functions Under the Canadian Tourism Commission Act {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-state-tourism-to-the-minister-of-industry-the-powers-duties-and-functions-under-the-canadian-tourism-commission-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-40
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2025-03-26

Order Transferring from the Minister of State (Tourism) to the Minister
of Industry the Powers, Duties and Functions Under the Canadian Tourism
Commission Act

P.C. 2025-420 2025-03-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EJEAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EGEAA},
transfers to the Minister of Industry the powers, duties and functions
under the [Canadian Tourism Commission Act](/eng/acts/C-23.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1741736111){#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1741736111-ID0EDEAA}
that were transferred to the Minister of State (Tourism) by Order in
Council P.C. 2023-769 of July 26, 2023[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1741736266){#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1741736266-ID0EBEAA}.
