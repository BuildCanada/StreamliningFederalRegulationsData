## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1424173 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Proceeds of Crime (Money
    Laundering) and Terrorist Financing
    Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[specified report]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[specified report]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a report made to the
    Centre under the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist
    Financing Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-184) that is
    required to contain the information set out in any of Schedules 1 to
    4 and 6 to those Regulations. ([déclaration visée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Assessment of Expenses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1424178 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed expenses

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Expenses incurred by the
Centre in relation to ensuring compliance with Parts 1 and 1.1 of the
Act and in relation to the Centre's activities under sections 51.1 to
51.3 of the Act are prescribed expenses for the purpose of subsection
51.1(1) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed persons and entities

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following persons and
entities are prescribed for the purpose of subsection 51.1(3) of the
Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} every bank to which the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01)
  applies and every [[authorized foreign bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as
  defined in section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} every life company or foreign life company to which
  the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} every company to which the [Trust and Loan Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) applies; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} every person or entity, other than an entity referred
  to in paragraphs (a) to (c), that made 500 or more specified reports
  during the fiscal year in which the expenses referred to in subsection
  51.1(1) of the Act were incurred.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed information

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** All information that is
required for performing a calculation under these Regulations is
prescribed information for the purpose of section 51.3 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment in writing

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An assessment or interim
assessment under section 51.1 of the Act must be made in writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Apportionment

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 51.1(3) of the Act, the portion of the expenses incurred in a
fiscal year that is to be assessed against a person or entity is to be
determined

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an entity referred to in paragraph
  3(a), by the formula

  ::: {#1424194 .Paragraph}
  A + B − C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the base assessment amount determined under section 7 for the
      entity in respect of the fiscal year,

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the proportional assessment amount determined under section 8
      for the entity in respect of the fiscal year, and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of any interim assessment made against the entity
      under subsection 51.1(4) of the Act during the fiscal year;
  :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an entity referred to in paragraph
  3(b) or (c), by the formula

  ::: {#1424200 .Paragraph}
  A + D − C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the base assessment amount determined under section 7 for the
      entity in respect of the fiscal year,

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the proportional assessment amount determined under section 9
      for the entity in respect of the fiscal year, and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of any interim assessment made against the entity
      under subsection 51.1(4) of the Act during the fiscal year; and
  :::

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person or entity referred to in
  paragraph 3(d), by the formula

  ::: {#1424206 .Paragraph}
  E − F

  where

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is the proportional assessment amount determined under section 9
      for the person or entity in respect of the fiscal year, and

  [F]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of any interim assessment made against the person or
      entity under subsection 51.1(4) of the Act during the fiscal year.
  :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Base amount

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The base
  assessment amount for an entity referred to in any of paragraphs 3(a)
  to (c) in respect of a fiscal year is the amount determined by the
  following formula, rounded to the nearest multiple of \$10 or, if the
  result obtained is equidistant from two multiples of \$10, to the
  higher of them:

  ::: {#1424213 .Subsection}
  G × H ÷ I

  where

  [G]{.dfn}
  :   is
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the value of the entity's assets in Canada
        as of the end of the entity's financial year that ends in the
        fiscal year is greater than or equal to \$1 trillion, \$250,000,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the value of the entity's assets in Canada
        as of the end of the entity's financial year that ends in the
        fiscal year is greater than or equal to \$500 billion but less
        than \$1 trillion, \$200,000,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the value of the entity's assets in Canada
        as of the end of the entity's financial year that ends in the
        fiscal year is greater than or equal to \$100 billion but less
        than \$500 billion, \$150,000,

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the value of the entity's assets in Canada
        as of the end of the entity's financial year that ends in the
        fiscal year is greater than or equal to \$10 billion but less
        than \$100 billion, \$100,000,

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the value of the entity's assets in Canada
        as of the end of the entity's financial year that ends in the
        fiscal year is greater than or equal to \$1 billion but less
        than \$10 billion, \$75,000,

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the value of the entity's assets in Canada
        as of the end of the entity's financial year that ends in the
        fiscal year is greater than or equal to \$500 million but less
        than \$1 billion, \$50,000,

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the value of the entity's assets in Canada
        as of the end of the entity's financial year that ends in the
        fiscal year is greater than or equal to \$100 million but less
        than \$500 million, \$25,000,

      - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the value of the entity's assets in Canada
        as of the end of the entity's financial year that ends in the
        fiscal year is greater than or equal to \$10 million but less
        than \$100 million, \$10,000,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the value of the entity's assets in Canada
        as of the end of the entity's financial year that ends in the
        fiscal year is greater than 0 but less than \$10 million,
        \$5,000, or

      - [(j)]{.lawlabel} if the value of the entity's assets in Canada
        as of the end of the entity's financial year that ends in the
        fiscal year is less than or equal to 0, nil;

  [H]{.dfn}
  :   is the highest Consumer Price Index for any calendar year
      beginning with 2024 and ending with the calendar year that ends in
      the fiscal year; and

  [I]{.dfn}
  :   is the Consumer Price Index for 2024.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consumer Price Index

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), a reference to the
  Consumer Price Index for a calendar year is a reference to the average
  of the all-items Consumer Price Index for Canada, not seasonally
  adjusted, as published by Statistics Canada under the authority of the
  [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19), for each month in that year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proportional assessment amount --- banks

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  proportional assessment amount for an entity referred to in paragraph
  3(a) in respect of a fiscal year is the amount determined by the
  formula

  ::: {#1424233 .Subsection}
  (J − K) × (L ÷ M) × (N ÷ O)

  where

  [J]{.dfn}
  :   is the Centre's expenses referred to in section 2 for the fiscal
      year;

  [K]{.dfn}
  :   is the total of all amounts determined under section 7 for the
      fiscal year;

  [L]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of specified reports that all entities referred to
      in paragraph 3(a) made during the fiscal year;

  [M]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of specified reports that all persons and entities
      made during the fiscal year;

  [N]{.dfn}
  :   is the value of the entity's assets in Canada as of the end of the
      entity's financial year that ends in the fiscal year; and

  [O]{.dfn}
  :   is the value of the assets in Canada of all entities referred to
      in paragraph 3(a) as of the end of each of those entities'
      financial years that end in the fiscal year.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of assets

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the descriptions of N and O in
  subsection (1), if the value of an entity's assets in Canada is less
  than 0, the value of that entity's assets in Canada is deemed to be
  nil.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proportional assessment amount ---
non-banks

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  proportional assessment amount in respect of a fiscal year for an
  entity referred to in paragraph 3(b) or (c) that made 500 or more
  specified reports during that year, or for a person or entity referred
  to in paragraph 3(d), is the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#1424246 .Subsection}
  (J − K) × (P ÷ M) × (Q ÷ R)

  where

  [J]{.dfn}
  :   is the Centre's expenses referred to in section 2 for the fiscal
      year;

  [K]{.dfn}
  :   is the total of all amounts determined under section 7 for the
      fiscal year;

  [P]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of specified reports that persons or entities other
      than entities referred to in paragraph 3(a) made during the fiscal
      year;

  [M]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of specified reports that all persons and entities
      made during the fiscal year;

  [Q]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of specified reports that the person or entity made
      during the fiscal year; and

  [R]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of specified reports made during the fiscal year by
      all entities referred to in paragraph 3(b) or (c) that each made
      500 or more specified reports during that year and by all persons
      and entities referred to in paragraph 3(d).
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fewer than 500 specified reports

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The proportional assessment amount for an entity
  referred to in paragraph 3(b) or (c) in respect of a fiscal year
  during which the entity made fewer than 500 specified reports is nil.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assets of subsidiaries

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 7(1) and section 8, the value of an entity's assets in Canada
excludes the value of the assets in Canada of any subsidiary of the
entity that is itself referred to in any of paragraphs 3(a) to (c).
