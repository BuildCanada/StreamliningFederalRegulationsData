## [Approved Screening Devices]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1450642 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Devices

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
definition [[approved screening device]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
section 320.11 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the following
devices, each being a device that is designed to ascertain the presence
of alcohol in a person's blood, are approved:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Alcolmeter S-L2;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Alco-Sûr;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Alcotest® 7410 PA3;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Alcotest® 7410 GLC;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} Alco-Sensor IV DWF;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} Alco-Sensor IV PWF;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} Intoxilyzer 400D;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} Alco-Sensor FST;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} Dräger Alcotest 6810;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} Dräger Alcotest® 6820;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} Intoxilyzer 800;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} Alcotest 7000; and

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} Alcotest 6000.
