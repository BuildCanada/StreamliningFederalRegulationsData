## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-747633 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
exempt from the provisions of the [Export and Import Permits
Act](/eng/acts/E-19) while acting in the course of their employment:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} employees of a foreign state who, for the purpose of
  ensuring public safety or security, carry goods in the course of their
  employment when entering or exiting Canada for which permits are
  required under the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19) and
  whose duties and functions are described in an arrangement or
  commitment between the foreign state and the Government of Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} members of the Correctional Service of Canada who are
  designated as peace officers under section 10 of the [Corrections and
  Conditional Release Act](/eng/acts/C-44.6);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} police officers or police constables who are members
  of a police force in Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} persons designated as fishery officers under the
  [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) when performing any duties or
  functions under that Act or the [Coastal Fisheries Protection
  Act](/eng/acts/C-33);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} employees of the Canadian Coast Guard;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} persons designated under section 6 of the [Migratory
  Birds Convention Act, 1994](/eng/acts/M-7.01), section 11 of the
  [Canada Wildlife Act](/eng/acts/W-9), section 12 of the [Wild Animal
  and Plant Protection and Regulation of International and
  Interprovincial Trade Act](/eng/acts/W-8.5) or section 85 of the
  [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3); and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} persons referred to in paragraph (d) or (d.1) of the
  definition [[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) and persons referred to in
  paragraph (b) or (f) of the definition [[public
  officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 117.07(2) of that Act.
