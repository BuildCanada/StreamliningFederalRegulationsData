## Order Designating the Greater Toronto Airports Authority as a Designated Airport Authority and Designating the Date for the Transfer of the Toronto-Lester B. Pearson International Airport to the Greater Toronto Airport Authority {#order-designating-the-greater-toronto-airports-authority-as-a-designated-airport-authority-and-designating-the-date-for-the-transfer-of-the-toronto-lester-b.-pearson-international-airport-to-the-greater-toronto-airport-authority .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/96-49
:::

[AIRPORT TRANSFER (MISCELLANEOUS MATTERS) ACT](/eng/acts/A-10.4)

Registration 1996-06-12

Order Designating the Greater Toronto Airports Authority as a Designated
Airport Authority and Designating the Date for the Transfer of the
Toronto-Lester B. Pearson International Airport to the Greater Toronto
Airport Authority

P.C. 1996-808 1996-05-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection
2(2) of the [Airport Transfer (Miscellaneous Matters)
Act](/eng/acts/A-10.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBBCA} hereby
designates

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EBBCA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1992, c.
  5
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Greater Toronto Airports Authority as a
  designated airport authority; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day immediately following the closing date as
  defined in the Agreement to Transfer entered into under the authority
  of Order in Council P.C. 1996-807 of May 30, 1996, as the transfer
  date for the Toronto --- Lester B. Pearson International Airport to
  the Greater Toronto Airports Authority.
