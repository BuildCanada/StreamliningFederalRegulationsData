## Order Transferring from the President of the Treasury Board to the President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada the Control and Supervision of the Public Service Human Resources Management Agency of Canada {#order-transferring-from-the-president-of-the-treasury-board-to-the-president-of-the-queens-privy-council-for-canada-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-public-service-human-resources-management-agency-of-canada .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2003-221
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2003-12-31

Order Transferring from the President of the Treasury Board to the
President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada the Control and
Supervision of the Public Service Human Resources Management Agency of
Canada

P.C. 2003-2072  2003-12-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers from the President of the Treasury Board to the President of
the Queen's Privy Council for Canada the control and supervision of that
portion of the public service known as the Public Service Human
Resources Management Agency of Canada, over which the President of the
Queen's Privy Council for Canada shall preside, effective December 12,
2003.
