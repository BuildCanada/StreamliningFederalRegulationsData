## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-842476 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [National Security and
    Intelligence Committee of Parliamentarians
    Act](/eng/acts/N-16.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of the
    Committee. ([membre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[restricted area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[restricted area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is indicated by a perimeter and monitored
      continuously;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to which access is limited to persons who hold
      the proper authorization and to escorted visitors; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for which records detailing the access to the
      area are maintained and audited. ([zone d'accès
      restreint]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sensitive information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sensitive information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means information or
    documents

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that a member obtains, creates or has access to
      in the course of exercising their powers or performing their
      duties and functions under the Act; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that a department is taking measures to
      protect. ([renseignements sensibles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Security Clearances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-842488 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Necessary security clearance

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraph 10(a) of the Act, the necessary security
  clearance is a Top Secret security clearance that is issued by the
  Clerk of the Privy Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change in personal circumstances

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member must, without delay, provide the Clerk of
  the Privy Council with a report of any change in their personal
  circumstances that may affect their security clearance, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a criminal conviction;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} being the subject of a law enforcement action;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} association with criminals; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a significant change in their personal financial
    situation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security briefing

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A member must attend a
security briefing provided by the Secretariat

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} before accessing sensitive information for the first
  time; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} before accessing sensitive information that is in a
  category for which they have not already received a security briefing.

## [Procedures and Practices for Protecting Sensitive Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-842502 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Handling of sensitive information

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A member may handle
sensitive information only when they are inside a restricted area.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discussion of sensitive information ---
location

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member
  may discuss sensitive information only when they are inside a
  restricted area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prevention of indirect disclosure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member must ensure that they do not indirectly
  disclose sensitive information when discussing their powers, duties
  and functions under the Act outside of a restricted area.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transportation --- limit

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A member may transport
sensitive information only directly from one restricted area to another.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transportation requirements

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** When a member transports
sensitive information, they must use equipment that has been provided to
them by the Secretariat for that purpose and must maintain physical
control of that equipment for the duration of the transport.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic equipment requirements

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member
  may electronically produce, store or transmit sensitive information
  only using electronic equipment that is provided to them by the
  Secretariat for that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reception of sensitive information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a member receives sensitive information using
  electronic equipment that is not provided to them by the Secretariat,
  the member must report the occurrence to the Secretariat as soon as
  the circumstances permit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sensitive information --- return to
custody

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If a member takes custody
of sensitive information from the Secretariat, the member must ensure
that the information is returned to the custody of the Secretariat.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information --- remit to custody

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A member must, as soon as
the circumstances permit, remit to the custody of the Secretariat

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all information and documents created by the member
  that are derived from sensitive information; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all information that they receive from a source other
  than a department and that is related to the exercise of their powers
  or the performance of their duties and functions under the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage of electronic devices

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Before entering a
restricted area, a member must store outside of the area all electronic
devices on their person that were not provided to them by the
Secretariat.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incident reports

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A member must, without
delay, provide the Executive Director of the Secretariat with a report
of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} every act, event or omission that they believe could
  result in

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an unauthorized access to sensitive information or
    a restricted area, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an unauthorized disclosure, destruction, removal,
    modification or use of sensitive information; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any contact with an individual that they believe may
  constitute an attempt to access sensitive information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergency situations

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** In the event of an
emergency situation at the premises in which a member is exercising
their powers or performing their duties and functions under the Act, the
member must take all measures that can be safely taken in the
circumstances to protect the sensitive information that is in their
custody.
