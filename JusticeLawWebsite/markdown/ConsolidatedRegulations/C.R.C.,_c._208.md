## Ontario Greenhouse Vegetable Marketing (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#ontario-greenhouse-vegetable-marketing-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 208
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Regulations Respecting the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade
of Greenhouse Vegetables Produced in Ontario
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536514 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Ontario Greenhouse Vegetable Marketing (Interprovincial
and Export) Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._208).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536516 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[appointed shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appointed shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a shipper in
    possession of a valid and subsisting certificate of appointment
    issued by the Commodity Board pursuant to the [Ontario Greenhouse
    Vegetable Appointed Shippers' Procedures (Interprovincial and
    Export) Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._210); ([expéditeur
    désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Greenhouse
    Vegetable Producers' Marketing Board; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[greenhouse vegetables]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[greenhouse vegetables]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means tomatoes,
    cucumbers and lettuce produced in the Province of Ontario in a
    greenhouse or any other enclosure under glass, plastic or other
    material used for the purpose of controlling temperature and
    providing protection for growing plants; ([légumes de
    serre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production of greenhouse vegetables; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any quantity of greenhouse
    vegetables; ([produit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[retailer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[retailer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who sells or
    offers to sell a product directly to the consumer and includes the
    operator of a roadside stand; ([détaillant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who offers for
    sale, sells, receives, assembles, packs, ships for sale or
    transports a product but does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a servant employed by and driving a vehicle owned
      by a producer or an appointed shipper,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a railway company, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who transports the product by motor
      vehicle as agent of the producer; ([expéditeur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[wholesaler]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wholesaler]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who buys a product
    through the Commodity Board and who offers for sale or sells the
    product to a retailer. ([grossiste]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536529 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the marketing of a product in interprovincial and
  export trade;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} persons and property situated in the Province of
  Ontario; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a product produced in the Counties of Essex, Kent and
  Lambton in the Province of Ontario.

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536534 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A producer who sells a
product directly to a consumer or a consumer who buys a product directly
from a producer is, in respect of that product, exempt from these
Regulations.

## [Sale of Product]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536536 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person other than

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a producer who sells or offers for sale a product
  through the Commodity Board,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a wholesaler who buys a product through the Commodity
  Board for resale, or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a producer authorized under a licence issued by the
  Commodity Board to sell or offer for sale a product

shall sell the product or offer the product for sale.

## [Purchase of Product]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536542 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No person other than

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a wholesaler who buys a product through the Commodity
  Board for resale in the normal course of his business, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a retailer who buys a product through the Commodity
  Board or from a wholesaler for resale in the normal course of his
  business

shall buy a product.

## [Transportation of Product]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536547 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No person other than

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an appointed shipper who ships a product for a
  producer,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a wholesaler who buys a product through the Commodity
  Board for resale in the normal course of his business,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a railway company, or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a producer authorized to transport a product under a
  licence issued by the Commodity Board

shall transport a product.

## [Shipping of Product]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536554 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No person other than

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an appointed shipper who ships a product for a
  producer,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a wholesaler who buys a product through the Commodity
  Board for resale in the normal course of his business, or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a producer authorized to ship a product under a
  licence issued by the Commodity Board

shall ship a product.

## [Licences to Producers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536560 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licence may be issued to a producer by the Commodity Board to sell,
  transport or ship a product otherwise than through the Commodity
  Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licence issued pursuant to subsection (1) shall
  expire on December 31st of the year for which the licence is issued.
