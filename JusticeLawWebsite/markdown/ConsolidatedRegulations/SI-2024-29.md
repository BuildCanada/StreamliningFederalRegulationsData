## Order Accepting the Recommendation of the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness that each listed entity set out in the annexed Schedule 1 remain a listed entity and that the entity in Schedule 2 no longer remain a listed entity {#order-accepting-the-recommendation-of-the-minister-of-public-safety-and-emergency-preparedness-that-each-listed-entity-set-out-in-the-annexed-schedule-1-remain-a-listed-entity-and-that-the-entity-in-schedule-2-no-longer-remain-a-listed-entity .Title-of-Act}

:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 2024-07-03

Order Accepting the Recommendation of the Minister of Public Safety and
Emergency Preparedness that each listed entity set out in the annexed
Schedule 1 remain a listed entity and that the entity in Schedule 2 no
longer remain a listed entity

P.C. 2024-728 2024-06-18
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, under subsection 83.05(8.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502556){#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502556-ID0ELEEA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502588){#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502588-ID0EIEEA},
the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness has reviewed
each entity set out in the annexed Schedule 1 and listed in the
[Regulations Establishing a List of
Entities](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-284)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502602){#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502602-ID0EFEEA},
made under subsection 83.05(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502614){#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502614-ID0EDEEA}
of that Act, and has determined that there are still reasonable grounds,
as set out in subsection 83.05(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502614){#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502614-ID0EBEEA}
of that Act, for each entity to remain a listed entity;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502556-ID0ELEEA){#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502556}S.C.
  2019, c. 13, s. 141(4)
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote

  C-46
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502602-ID0EFEEA){#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502602}SOR/2002-284
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502614-ID0EDEEA){#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502614}S.C.
  2019, c. 13, s. 141(1)
  :::

Whereas, under subsection 83.05(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502614){#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502614-ID0EBADA}
of that Act, the Governor in Council is satisfied, on the recommendation
of that Minister, that there are reasonable grounds to believe that each
listed entity set out in the annexed Schedule 1 has knowingly carried
out, attempted to carry out, participated in or facilitated a terrorist
activity or that has knowingly acted on behalf of, at the direction of
or in association with such an entity;

Whereas, under subsection 83.05(8.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502556){#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502556-ID0EDACA}
of that Act, that Minister has reviewed the entity set out in the
annexed Schedule 2 and listed in those Regulations, and has determined
that there are no longer reasonable grounds, as set out in subsection
83.05(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502614){#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502614-ID0EBACA}
of that Act, for the entity to remain a listed entity;

And whereas, under subsection 83.05(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502614){#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502614-ID0EBABA}
of that Act, the Governor in Council is satisfied, on the recommendation
of that Minister, that there are no longer reasonable grounds to believe
that the listed entity set out in the annexed Schedule 2 has knowingly
carried out, attempted to carry out, participated in or facilitated a
terrorist activity or that has knowingly acted on behalf of, at the
direction of or in association with such an entity;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council accepts the
recommendation of the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
Preparedness, made under subsection 83.05(8.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502556){#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502556-ID0EEAAA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502588){#fn_SI-2024-29_e_hq_1719502588-ID0EBAAA},
that each listed entity set out in the annexed Schedule 1 remain a
listed entity and that the entity in Schedule 2 no longer remain a
listed entity.
