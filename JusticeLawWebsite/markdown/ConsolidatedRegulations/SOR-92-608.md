## Saskatchewan Canola Development Commission Levies Order {#saskatchewan-canola-development-commission-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-608
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1992-10-16

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Levies from
Certain Persons who are Engaged in the Production and Marketing of
Canola in Saskatchewan
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Saskatchewan Canola Development Commission, pursuant to sections 3
and 4 of the Saskatchewan Canola Order, made by Order in Council P.C.
1992-1026 of May 14, 1992[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBBA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Order providing for the fixing, imposing and collecting of
levies from certain persons who are engaged in the production and
marketing of canola in Saskatchewan](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-608).

  [[Return to footnote

  :::

Saskatoon, Saskatchewan, October 16, 1992

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-947865 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Saskatchewan Canola Development Commission Levies
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-608).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-947867 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Agri-Food Act, Statutes of
    Saskatchewan 1990-91, c. A-15.2; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who buys canola;
    ([acheteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[canola]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[canola]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means canola produced in
    Saskatchewan; ([colza]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan Canola
    Development Commission; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who is engaged in the production or
      marketing of canola and includes the employer of such a person,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who is entitled under any lease or
      agreement

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to a share of the canola produced by a person
        mentioned in paragraph (a), or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to the proceeds from the sale of that canola,
        or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who takes possession of any canola under
      any form of security or legal proceedings for a debt.
      ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-947879 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay to
the Commission a levy of 75 cents per metric tonne of canola or portion
of one marketed by that producer in interprovincial and export trade, in
the manner set out in section 4.

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-947883 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  buyer shall deduct from every payment made to a producer a levy of 50
  cents per tonne or portion thereof on a pro rata basis of canola
  marketed by the producer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every buyer who buys canola from a producer or
  acquires canola from a producer for sale on the producer's behalf
  shall deduct the amount of the levy referred to in subsection (1) from
  any proceeds payable to or on behalf of the producer.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every buyer shall forward levies referred to in
  subsection (1) to the Commission, together with a statement on a form
  provided by the Commission that sets out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of canola obtained from the producer;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of levy being paid on behalf of the
    producer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the producer; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the producer's Canadian Wheat Board identification
    number.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every buyer who collects a levy shall, unless
  otherwise directed by the Commission, pay the amount of the levy
  collected to the Commission within 55 days after the end of the month
  in which the levy was collected.
