## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000455 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Duties Relief Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-44).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000457 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[beer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[beer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means beer or malt liquor as defined in
    section 4 of the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14);
    ([bière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}means the Minister of National
    Revenue; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[textile fabric]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[textile fabric]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means textile fibre, glass
    fibre, textile fabric, glass fabric or other textile product that is
    of a stage between textile fibre and fabric that is composed
    exclusively of fibres that fall within a class set out in
    Schedule II. ([tissus textiles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-55, s. 1
- SOR/2003-236, s. 1
:::

## [Classes of Persons Who May Apply for Relief]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000465 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An application for relief
described in subsection 89(1) of the Act may be made by any person who
is the importer or exporter of the imported or exported goods, or is the
processor, owner or producer of those goods between the time of their
direct shipment to Canada and their export or deemed export.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-55, s. 2
:::

## [No Relief to Be Granted]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000469 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Relief under
subsection 89(1) of the Act may not be granted in respect of the
imported goods described in that subsection where the exported goods
described in that subsection are damaged before being exported.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-55, s. 2
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraphs 89(1)(c) and (e) of the Act, relief may not be granted in
respect of the goods described in Schedule I.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-55, s. 2
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraphs 89(1)(d) and (e) of the Act, relief may not be granted in
respect of the following goods:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} spirits that are imported for use in the processing
  in Canada of spirits or that are directly consumed or expended in the
  processing in Canada of spirits;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods that are imported for processing in Canada or
  are directly consumed or expended in the processing in Canada of other
  goods, where they are processed or directly consumed or expended after
  the same quantity of domestic or imported goods of the same class is
  processed or directly consumed or expended; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} imported goods, other than textile fabric, where the
  imported goods are processed or directly consumed or expended in a
  plant in Canada other than the plant in which the same quantity of
  domestic or imported goods of the same class is processed or directly
  consumed or expended.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-55, s. 2
- SOR/2003-236, s. 2
:::

## [Time Limits for Exportation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000482 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the following goods must be exported within four
  years after the date on which the imported goods were released:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods described in paragraph 89(1)(a) or (b) of the
    Act on which duties are relieved; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods, in the processing of which in Canada, goods
    described in paragraph 89(1)(c) of the Act on which duties are
    relieved are directly consumed or expended.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Spirits, wine or beer that are imported goods
  described in paragraph 89(1)(b) of the Act on which duties are
  relieved must be exported within five years after the date on which
  they were released.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraphs 89(1)(d) and (e) of
  the Act, the exported goods must be exported within two years after
  the date on which the imported goods on which duties are relieved were
  released.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-55, s. 2
- SOR/2003-236, s. 3
:::

## [Portion of Duties that May Be Relieved]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000491 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where the amount of duties
otherwise payable in respect of imported textile fabric that is
considered under section 10 to be of the same class as domestic textile
fabric exceeds the amount of duties that would have been payable had the
domestic textile fabric been imported, the portion of the duties so
payable that may be relieved is the proportion that the amount that
would have been so payable is of the amount that was payable.

## [Same Condition Processes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000493 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 89(1)(a) of the Act, goods are considered to be in the same
condition in which they were imported after they

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} undergo any of the processes referred to in
  subparagraph (6)(b) of Article 2.5 of the Canada--United
  States--Mexico Agreement, if those processes do not materially alter
  the characteristics of the goods;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} are used for

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} display or demonstration, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the development or production, other than as plant
    equipment, of goods to be exported; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of reusable containers, are used in the
  international transportation of goods.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-55, s. 2
- [SOR/2020-64, s. 3](#1243579-1257005){.wb-lbx

## [Goods of the Same Class]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000502 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), for the
  purposes of paragraphs 89(1)(d) and (e) of the Act, domestic or
  imported goods shall be considered to be of the same class if the
  goods are so similar that they may be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} used interchangeably in the processing of goods in
    Canada; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} directly consumed or expended interchangeably in
    the processing in Canada of goods.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraphs 89(1)(d) and (e) of
  the Act, domestic and imported textile fabrics composed of different
  fibres shall be considered to be of the same class if the domestic and
  imported textile fabrics are composed of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fibres that fall within one class set out in
    Schedule II; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fibres that do not fall within one class set out in
    Schedule II, if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any particular class set out in that Schedule
      within which fall fibres of which the domestic textile fabric is
      composed

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} is a class within which fall fibres of which
        the imported textile fabric is composed, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} represents less than five per cent of the
        weight of the domestic textile fabric, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the difference between the following proportions
      does not exceed 33 percentage points:

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the proportion, expressed as a percentage, that
        the aggregate weight of the fibres of which the domestic textile
        fabric is composed that fall within the particular class is of
        the weight of the domestic textile fabric, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the proportion, expressed as a percentage, that
        the aggregate weight of the fibres of which the imported textile
        fabric is composed that fall within that particular class is of
        the weight of the imported textile fabric.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-55, s. 2
:::

## [Prescribed Classes of Ships, Aircraft and Telegraph Cable Ships]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000518 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 89(3)(b) of the Act, the ships and aircraft described in
Schedule III are prescribed as classes of ships and aircraft.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-55, s. 2
:::

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 89(3)(c) of the Act, the prescribed class of telegraph cable
ships includes only those ships that are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} registered in any country;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} used exclusively for the laying and repairing of
  oceanic telegraph cables outside Canada; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} proceeding on an ocean voyage outside Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-55, s. 2
:::

## [Prescribed Use]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000528 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 89(3)(g) of the Act, goods are used or destined for use where
they

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} are purchased by the government of a country that is
  party to the Canada--United States--Mexico Agreement or its authorized
  agent, by a department of the Government of Canada, or by a Crown
  corporation named in Schedule II or Schedule III to the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) acting on behalf of the Government
  of Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} are for use solely and exclusively in conjunction
  with

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a project undertaken jointly by the Government of
    Canada and the government of a country that is party to the
    Canada--United States--Mexico Agreement, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an undertaking, located in Canada, of the
    government of a country that is party to the Canada--United
    States--Mexico Agreement; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} are or will become the property of the government of
  a country that is party to the Canada--United States--Mexico
  Agreement.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-55, s. 2
- [SOR/2020-64, s. 4](#1243581-1257011){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1243581-1257011.html"

## [Amendment of Certificate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000537 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may amend a
certificate issued under subsection 90(1) of the Act where any of the
information set out in the application for relief has changed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-55, s. 2
:::

## [Cancellation or Suspension of Certificate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000541 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may cancel a
certificate issued under subsection 90(1) of the Act where the person to
whom the certificate was issued

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} requests the Minister in writing to cancel the
  certificate;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is bankrupt;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is the subject of a receivership in respect of the
  person's debts;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} ceases to operate in Canada; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fails to make representations in accordance with
  subsection 17(2).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-55, s. 2
:::

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may suspend
or cancel a certificate issued under subsection 90(1) of the Act where
the person to whom the certificate was issued

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fails to comply with any Act of Parliament, or any
  regulation made pursuant to an Act of Parliament that prohibits,
  controls or regulates the importation or exportation of goods; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} has, in the use of the certificate, acted dishonestly
  in business dealings with Her Majesty or servants of Her Majesty.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-55, s. 2
:::

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall, immediately after suspending a certificate, give the
  certificate holder notice confirming the suspension and providing all
  relevant information concerning the grounds on which the Minister
  suspended the certificate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The certificate holder may, within 30 days after the
  day on which the certificate is suspended, make representations to the
  Minister regarding why the certificate should be reinstated.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, before cancelling a certificate,
  except in the case of a cancellation under section 15 or a
  cancellation subsequent to a suspension under section 16, give the
  certificate holder

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 30 days notice of the proposed cancellation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all relevant information concerning the grounds on
    which the Minister proposes to cancel the certificate.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The certificate holder may, within 15 days after the
  day on which the notice referred to in subsection (3) is given, make
  representations to the Minister regarding why the certificate should
  not be cancelled.

## [Reinstatement of Certificate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000562 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
reinstate a certificate suspended under section 16 where the cause for
the suspension no longer exists.

## [Manner of Reporting Exports to a Country that is Party to the Canada--United States--Mexico Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000564 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2020-64, s. 5

\]
:::

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of paragraph 95(1)(a) of the Act, the person who exports
  the goods shall report to an officer at a customs office by providing
  the evidence described in section 93 of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph 98(1)(a) of the Act,
  the person who exports the goods shall report to an officer at a
  customs office by providing a written declaration of the amount of
  duties that was relieved.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-55, s. 2
:::

## [Satisfactory Proof of Exportation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000570 .Part}

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 101 of the Act, an application shall be accompanied by proof of
export in the form of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a customs document presented to an officer of the
  customs administration of the country where the repair, addition of
  equipment or work was performed respecting the importation of the
  goods into that country;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a document of a transportation company respecting the
  exportation of the goods;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a written statement made by the exporter in the
  country where the work was performed stating that the goods exported
  to Canada are the goods that were imported into that country for
  repair, the addition of equipment or work; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} other documentation that establishes that the goods
  were exported.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-55, s. 2
:::
