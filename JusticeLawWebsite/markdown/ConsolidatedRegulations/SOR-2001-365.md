## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673122 .Part}

Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Trust and Loan Companies
Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8).

## [Aggregate Financial Exposure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673125 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aggregate financial exposure

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 483.2(1) of the Act, the aggregate financial exposure of a
company is the amount determined by the formula

::: {#673128 .Section}
A + B + C - D

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the book value of all investments by the company and its
    subsidiaries in securities of the related party;

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the total of the principal amounts of all outstanding loans to
    the related party that are held by the company and its subsidiaries,
    other than
    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} loans referred to in paragraph 479(a) of the Act,
      and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} deposits made for clearing purposes with a
      financial institution that is a direct clearer or a member of a
      clearing group under the by-laws of the Canadian Payments
      Association;

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the total of all outstanding amounts in respect of endorsements,
    acceptances or guarantees by the company and its subsidiaries on
    behalf of the related party, other than guarantees referred to in
    paragraph 479(a) of the Act; and

[D ]{.dfn}
:   is the total of all amounts included in the value of A, B and C in
    respect of transactions referred to in section 478 of the Act.
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673136 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2001-365_e_hq_5974){#fn_SOR-2001-365_e_hq_5974-ID0EBCA}[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 483.2 of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8),
as enacted by section 552 of the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1), chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, comes
into force.
