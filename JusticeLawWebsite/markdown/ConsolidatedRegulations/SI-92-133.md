## Special Voting Rules Referendum Fees Tariff {#special-voting-rules-referendum-fees-tariff .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/92-133
:::

[CANADA ELECTIONS ACT](/eng/acts/E-2.01)

[REFERENDUM ACT](/eng/acts/R-4.7)

Registration 1992-07-29

Tariff of Fees, Costs, Allowances and Expenses for Special Returning
Officers and Other Persons Employed at or with Respect to a Referendum
Under the Special Voting Rules

P.C. 1992-1629 1992-07-16
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the President of the Privy Council, pursuant to subsection 11(1) of the
Special Voting Rules (Schedule II to the Canada Elections Act), as
adapted by regulations made by the Chief Electoral Officer on July 14,
1992[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EJCAA} pursuant to
section 7 of the [Referendum Act](/eng/acts/R-4.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0EGCAA}, and
subsection 12(2) of the Special Voting Rules (Schedule II to the Canada
Elections Act) as that provision applies pursuant to subsection 7(1) of
the [Referendum Act](/eng/acts/R-4.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0EDCAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Tariff of fees, costs, allowances and
expenses for special returning officers and other persons employed at or
with respect to a referendum under the Special Voting
Rules](/eng/regulations/SI-92-133).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-644783 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Tariff may be cited as
the [Special Voting Rules Referendum Fees
Tariff](/eng/regulations/SI-92-133).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-644785 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The fees, costs, allowances
and expenses set out in the schedule shall be paid to special returning
officers, chief assistants, scrutineers and clerical assistants employed
at or with respect to a referendum under the [Special Voting
Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-148).
