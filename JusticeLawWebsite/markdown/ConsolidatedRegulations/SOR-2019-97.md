## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1123778 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[cord]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cord]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a band, rope, strap, string, chain, wire or any
      other component that, when any tension is removed, is capable of
      folding in every direction; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any combination of components that are connected
      end to end that, when any tension is removed, is capable of
      folding in every direction. ([corde]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[corded window covering]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[corded window covering]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an indoor
    window covering that is equipped with at least one
    cord. ([couvre-fenêtre à cordes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[loop]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[loop]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a shape, the majority of which is
    formed by a reachable cord, that creates a completely bounded
    opening. ([boucle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reachable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reachable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} with respect to a cord, refers to
    the part of the cord that any person can touch when the corded
    window covering has been installed, whether the window covering is
    fully opened, fully closed or in any position in
    between. ([atteignable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Specifications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1123787 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Small parts

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Every part of a corded
window covering that is accessible to a child and is small enough to be
totally enclosed in a small parts cylinder as illustrated in Schedule 1
must be affixed to the corded window covering so that the part does not
become detached when it is subjected to a force of 90 N applied in any
direction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lead content

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every external component of
a corded window covering must not contain more than 90 mg/kg of lead
when tested in accordance with the principles set out in the
Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development document entitled
OECD Principles of Good Laboratory Practice, Number 1 of the OECD Series
on Principles of Good Laboratory Practice and Compliance Monitoring,
ENV/MC/CHEM(98)17, the English version of which is dated January 21,
1998 and the French version of which is dated March 6, 1998.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unreachable cords

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A cord that is not
reachable must remain so, whether the corded window covering is fully
opened, fully closed or in any position in between, throughout the
useful life of the corded window covering.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reachable cord with one free end ---
length

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A reachable cord with one
free end must not exceed 22 cm in length when it is pulled in any
direction by the gradual application of force attaining 35 N.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reachable cord between two consecutive
contact points --- length

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A reachable cord with no
free end must not exceed 22 cm in length between two consecutive contact
points when it is pulled in any direction by the gradual application of
force attaining 35 N.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loop created by a reachable cord ---
perimeter

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If a reachable cord is
pulled in any direction by the gradual application of force attaining
35 N, the perimeter of any loop, whether it is existing, created or
enlarged, must not exceed 44 cm.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Two reachable cords

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** If two reachable cords with
one free end each can be connected to one another, end to end, after
each has been pulled in any direction by the gradual application of
force attaining 35 N, the following criteria must be met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the length of the resulting cord must not exceed
  22 cm; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the perimeter of the loop that is created must not
  exceed 44 cm.

## [Information and Advertising]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1123804 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to [Canada Consumer Product
Safety Act](/eng/acts/C-1.68) or Regulations

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Information that appears on
a corded window covering, that accompanies one or that is in any
advertisement for one must not make any direct or indirect reference to
the [Canada Consumer Product Safety Act](/eng/acts/C-1.68) or these
Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presentation --- general

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The information required
by these Regulations must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} appear in both English and French;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be legible and prominently and clearly displayed and,
  in particular, the characters must be in a colour that contrasts
  sharply with the background;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} remain legible and visible throughout the useful life
  of the corded window covering under normal conditions of
  transportation, storage, sale and use; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of information that is required on the
  corded window covering, be indelibly printed on the corded window
  covering itself or on a label that is permanently affixed to it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Print

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  required information must be printed in a standard sans-serif type
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not compressed, expanded or decorative; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a large x-height relative to the ascender or
    descender of the type, as illustrated in Schedule 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Height of type

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The height of the type is determined by measuring an
  upper case letter or a lower case letter that has an ascender or
  descender, such as "b" or "p".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signal words

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  signal words "Warning" and "[Mise en garde]{.otherLang lang="fr"}"
  must be in boldfaced, upper case type not less than 5 mm in height.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other information --- height of type

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All other required information must be in type not
  less than 2.5 mm in height.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information --- general

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The following information
must appear on every corded window covering, as well as on any packaging
in which the corded window covering is displayed to the consumer:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} its model name or model number;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} its date of manufacture, consisting of the year and
  either the month or week, listed in that order;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corded window covering that is
  manufactured in Canada, the name and principal place of business of
  the manufacturer or the person for whom the corded window covering was
  manufactured; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corded window covering that is
  imported for commercial purposes, the name and principal place of
  business of the manufacturer and the importer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Instructions --- assembly, installation
and operation

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The following
instructions must accompany every corded window covering in text,
drawings or photographs, or in any combination of them:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} how to assemble the corded window covering and a
  quantitative list of its parts, if it is sold not fully assembled;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} how to install it; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} how to operate it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warning

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The following warning or
its equivalent must appear on every corded window covering, the
packaging of the corded window covering, any accompanying instructions
and all of the advertisements for the corded window covering:

**WARNING**

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [•]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  STRANGULATION HAZARD --- Young children can be strangled by cords.
  Immediately remove this product if a cord longer than 22 cm or a loop
  exceeding 44 cm around becomes accessible.
  :::
  :::::

**MISE EN GARDE**

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [•]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  RISQUE D'ÉTRANGLEMENT --- Les enfants en bas âge peuvent s'étrangler
  avec des cordes. Enlevez immédiatement ce produit si une corde
  mesurant plus de 22 cm devient accessible ou si le contour d'une
  boucle de plus de 44 cm devient accessible.
  :::
  :::::
