## Sept-Iles Airport Zoning Regulations {#sept-iles-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/78-657
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1978-08-22

Zoning Regulations Respecting Sept-Iles Airport

P.C. 1978-2587 1978-08-16
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 6 of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to approve the annexed Zoning
Regulations respecting Sept-Iles Airport, made by the Minister of
Transport.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-863352 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Sept-Iles Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-657).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-863354 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Sept-Iles Airport, in the
      Township of Letellier, in the Province of Quebec;
      ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      determined in the manner set out in Part I of the schedule;
      ([point de repère de l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane extending upward and outward from each end of a
      strip along and at right angles to the projected centre line
      thereof, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part III of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway prepared for the
      take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction, which
      strip is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule;
      ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane extending upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part VI of the schedule;
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} coordinates and azimuths are of the M.T.M. System
    in which the central meridian of the zone is 67°30′; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} distances are expressed in the metric system.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, the airport reference point is deemed to be 51.77 metres
above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-863368 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands and lands under water, including public road allowances,
adjacent to or in the vicinity of the airport, the outer limits of which
lands are described in Part II of the schedule, other than such lands as
from time to time form part of the airport.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-863370 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct, on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point any of the surfaces hereinafter set out that
project immediately over and above the surface of the land at that
location, namely,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surface.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-863375 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in section 5, the Minister may make a
direction that the owner or occupier of the land on which that object is
growing remove the excessive growth.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point is situated at the provincial geodetic point
number 120 M.O.T. within the airport limits, the coordinates of which
are E. 392 770.593 metres and N. 5 565 720.089 metres.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Limits of Lands]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-outer-limits-of-lands .SchedHeadL1}

All and singular those certain parcels or tracts of land and premises
being part of the Townships of Arnaud, Letellier and Moisie,
Registration Division of Sept-Iles, more particularly described as
follows:

Commencing at a point, the coordinates of which are
E. 387 541.941 metres and N. 5 564 058.809 metres, being the
intersection of the northwesterly limit of the approach surface
associated with the strip 10-28 with the arc of a circle, having a
radius of 5 486.224 metres, the centre of which being the reference
point of the airport; THENCE, following the last-mentioned arc of a
circle, in general northerly, easterly and southerly directions, to its
intersection with the northeasterly limit of the approach surface
associated with the strip 14-32; THENCE, following the last-mentioned
northeasterly limit having an azimuth of 99°30′48″ to a point the
coordinates of which are E. 409 084.015 metres and
N. 5 563 297.666 metres; THENCE, following a line being the
southeasterly limit of the approach surface associated with the
strip 14-32, having an azimuth of 198°02′39″, a distance of
4 876.50 metres to a point, the coordinates of which are
E. 407 573.449 metres and N. 5 558 660.791 metres; THENCE, following the
southwesterly limit of the approach surface associated with the
strip 14-32, having an azimuth of 296°34′30″, to the intersection with
the arc of a circle, having a radius of 5 486.224 metres, the centre of
which is the reference point of the airport; THENCE, following the
last-mentioned arc of a circle, in general southwesterly, westerly and
northwesterly directions, to its intersection with the southeasterly
limit of the approach surface associated with the strip 10-28; THENCE,
along the said southeasterly limit, having an azimuth of 239°25′11″, to
a point, the coordinates of which are E. 378 658.076 metres and
N. 5 556 962.838 metres; THENCE, following a line, being the
southwesterly limit of the approach surface associated with the
strip 10-28, having an azimuth of 337°57′01″, a distance of
4 876.56 metres to a point, the coordinates of which are
E. 376 827.371 metres and N. 5 561 482.724 metres; THENCE, following the
northwesterly limit of the approach surface associated with the
strip 10-28, having an azimuth of 76°28′52″ to the point of
commencement.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Approach Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-each-approach-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Being a surface abutting each end of the strips associated with
runways 06-24, 10-28 and 14-32 and more particularly described as
follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runways 10-28 and 14-32 consisting of an inclined plane having a
  ratio of one (1) metre measured vertically to fifty (50) metres
  measured horizontally and having:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the outer ends 14 and 28 rising to an imaginary
    horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected centre line
    of the strip, 60.96 metres measured vertically above the elevation
    at the end of the strip and 3 047.90 metres measured horizontally
    from the end of strip 14 and 3 047.95 metres measured horizontally
    from the end of strip 28, the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
    line being 609.6 metres from the projected centre line of the strip,
    and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the outer ends 10 and 32 rising to an imaginary
    horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected centre line
    of the strip, 304.8 metres measured vertically above the elevation
    at the end of the strip and 15 239.25 metres measured horizontally
    from the end of strip 10 and 15 239.76 metres measured horizontally
    from the end of strip 32, the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
    line being 2 438.28 metres from the projected centre line of
    strip 10 and 2 438.25 metres from the projected centre line of
    strip 32, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway 06-24 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio of
  one (1) metre measured vertically to forty (40) metres measured
  horizontally rising to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right
  angles to the projected centre line of the strip, 76.2 metres measured
  vertically above the elevation at the end of the strip and
  3 047.90 metres measured horizontally from the end of the strip, the
  outer ends of the imaginary horizontal line being 381.0 metres from
  the projected centre line of the strip,

which approach surfaces are shown on Department of Public Works Plan
No. M-77-4400, dated the 7th day of March, 1978.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Being an imaginary surface consisting of:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a common plane established at a constant elevation of
  45.72 metres above the assigned elevation of the airport reference
  point, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the common plane described in paragraph (a) is
  less than 9.144 metres above the surface of the ground, an imaginary
  surface located 9.144 metres above the surface of the ground,

which outer surface is shown on Department of Public Works Plan
No. M-77-4400, dated the 7th day of March, 1978.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-strip .SchedHeadL1}

Each strip is described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 10-28 is
  304.8 metres in width, 152.4 metres on each side of the centre line of
  the runway and 2 560.32 metres in length,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 14-32 is
  304.8 metres in width, 152.4 metres on each side of the centre line of
  the runway and 2 255.52 metres in length, and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 06-24 is
  152.4 metres in width, 76.2 metres on each side of the centre line of
  the runway and 1 919.08 metres in length,

which strips are shown on Department of Public Works Plan No. M-77-4400,
dated on the 7th day of March, 1978.

### [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-videscription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Being a surface consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio of one
(1) metre measured vertically to seven (7) metres measured horizontally
at right angles to the centre line and centre line produced of the
strip, and extending upward and outward from the lateral limits of the
strip and its approach surfaces to an intersection with the outer
surface, an approach surface or another transitional surface of an
adjoining strip, which surfaces are shown on Department of Public Works
Plan No. M-77-4400, dated the 7th day of March, 1978.
