## Theratronics International Limited Authorization Order, 1988 {#theratronics-international-limited-authorization-order-1988 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-501
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1988-09-23

Order Authorizing Theratronics International Limited to Incorporate a
Corporation and to Hold and Acquire Shares thereof

P.C. 1988-2231 1988-09-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council, pursuant to subsection 101(6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EGCBA} of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is satisfied that Theratronics
International Limited, as a corporation incorporated under the [Canada
Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_2e){#fn_2e-ID0EBCBA}, is empowered to
procure the incorporation of a corporation and to hold and acquire the
shares thereof;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EGCBA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1984, c. 31, s. 11
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_2e-ID0EBCBA){#fn_2e}S.C. 1974-75-76, c.
  33(E); S.C. 1978-79, c. 9, ss. 1(F)
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources and the
Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraphs 101(1)(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EHAAA} and (b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFAAA} of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Order authorizing Theratronics International Limited to
incorporate a corporation and to hold and acquire shares
thereof](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-501).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913630 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Theratronics International Limited Authorization Order,
1988](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-501).

## [Authorized Transactions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913632 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Theratronics International
Limited is hereby authorized

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to procure the incorporation of a corporation all the
  shares of which, on incorporation, will be held by, on behalf of or in
  trust for Theratronics International Limited; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to acquire shares of the corporation that, on
  acquisition, will be held by, on behalf of or in trust for
  Theratronics International Limited.
