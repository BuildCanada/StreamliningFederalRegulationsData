## Special Economic Measures (Iran) Regulations {#special-economic-measures-iran-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2010-165
:::

[SPECIAL ECONOMIC MEASURES ACT](/eng/acts/S-14.5)

Registration 2010-07-22

Special Economic Measures (Iran) Regulations

P.C. 2010-952 2010-07-22
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that the situation in
Iran constitutes a grave breach of international peace and security that
has resulted or is likely to result in a serious international crisis;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to
subsections 4(1) to (3) of the [Special Economic Measures
Act](/eng/acts/S-14.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-661_hq_9413){#fn_81000-2-661_hq_9413-ID0EDBAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Special Economic Measures (Iran)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-165).

## [List]{.HTitleText1} {#h-760073 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A person whose name is
listed in Schedule 1 is a person in respect of whom the Governor in
Council, on the recommendation of the Minister, is satisfied that there
are reasonable grounds to believe is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who has engaged in activities that directly
  or indirectly facilitate, support, provide funding for, contribute to,
  or could contribute to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Iran's proliferation-sensitive nuclear activities,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Iran's activities related to the development of
    chemical, biological or nuclear weapons of mass destruction or
    delivery systems for such weapons, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} activities that undermine international peace,
    security or stability in a manner that is consistent with the
    policies of Iran;

- [(a.01)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person referred to in
  paragraph (a) that is an entity, a senior official of the entity;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} a person who has participated in gross and
  systematic human rights violations in Iran;

- [(a.2)]{.lawlabel} a former or current senior official of the
  Government of Iran;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a former or current senior official in the Islamic
  Revolutionary Guard Corps;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an associate of a person referred to in any of
  paragraphs (a) to (b);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a family member of a person referred to in any of
  paragraphs (a) to (c) and (f);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an entity owned --- or held or controlled, directly
  or indirectly --- by a person referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to
  (d); or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a senior official of an entity referred to in
  paragraph (e).

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-760082 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deal in any property, wherever situated, that is
  owned --- or that is held or controlled, directly or indirectly --- by
  a listed person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into or facilitate any transaction related to a
  dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial or related service in respect
  of a dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} make any goods, wherever situated, available to a
  listed person or to a person acting on behalf of a listed person; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial or related service to a listed
  person or to a person acting for the benefit of a listed person.

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** Section 3 does not
apply with respect to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pension payments to any person in Canada or any
  Canadian outside Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any transaction in respect of any account held by a
  diplomatic mission at a financial institution, if the transaction is
  required in order for the mission to fulfil its diplomatic functions
  as set out in Article 3 of the Convention or, if the diplomatic
  mission has been temporarily or permanently recalled, if the
  transaction is required in order to maintain the mission premises;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any transaction with any international organizations
  with diplomatic status, with any United Nations agencies, with the
  International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement, or with any
  Canadian non-governmental organizations that have entered into a grant
  or contribution agreement with the Department of Foreign Affairs,
  Trade and Development;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the transfer of any accounts, funds or investments
  held on behalf of a Canadian by a listed person on the day on which
  that person's name was listed to any person who is not a listed
  person;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any dealings with respect to loan repayments made by
  a listed person on loans entered into before the day on which that
  person's name was listed to any person in Canada or any Canadian
  outside Canada who is not a listed person;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the exercise of a right by a person who is not a
  listed person with respect to a listed person's property that is
  subject to a lien, mortgage or security interest, to a hypothec or
  prior claim, to a charge or to a judicial, administrative or arbitral
  decision;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} financial services required in order for a listed
  person to obtain legal services in Canada with respect to the
  application of any of the prohibitions set out in these Regulations;
  or

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} property that is necessary for the civil nuclear
  cooperation projects described in Annex III to the Joint Comprehensive
  Plan of Action or for any activity required for the implementation of
  that Plan.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(0.1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this section.

  [[arms and related material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[arms and related material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any type
      of weapon, ammunition, military equipment --- including military
      vehicles --- or paramilitary equipment, and their spare
      parts. ([armes et matériel connexe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[technical assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[technical assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any form of
      assistance, such as providing instruction, training, consulting
      services or technical advice. ([aide technique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for any person in Canada or any
  Canadian outside Canada to export, sell, supply or ship any of the
  goods listed in Schedule 2, wherever situated, to Iran, to a person in
  Iran, or to a person for the purposes of a business carried on in or
  operated from Iran.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for any person in Canada or any
  Canadian outside Canada to transfer, provide or disclose to Iran or
  any person in Iran any technical data related to the goods listed in
  Schedule 2.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for any person in Canada and any
  Canadian outside Canada to export, sell, supply or ship any of the
  following goods, wherever situated, or to provide any of the following
  technical assistance or technical data, to Iran or to any person in
  Iran:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} those that are listed in the following provisions
    of the Guide:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} group 1, items 1-1.A.1. to 1-1.A.3.,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} group 1, item 1-1.C.,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} group 1, items 1-7.A. to 1-7.E.,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} group 1, items 1-9.A. to 1-9.E.,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} group 2, items 2-10.c. and 2-10.d.,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} group 2, item 2-21.b.1., and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} group 2, items 2-21.b.3. and 2-21.b.4.;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any battle tanks, armoured combat vehicles,
    large-calibre artillery systems, combat aircraft, attack
    helicopters, warships, missiles or missile systems as defined for
    the purposes of the Register of Conventional Arms, established by
    Resolution 46/36 L adopted by the United Nations General Assembly on
    December 9, 1991, as amended from time to time, or related material
    including their spare parts;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} those that appear in document S/2015/546 of the
    Security Council of the United Nations, entitled Missile Technology
    Control Regime: Equipment, Software and Technology Annex, which is
    an annex to the letter dated July 16, 2015 from the Permanent
    Representative of the United States of America to the United
    Nations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any technical assistance or technical data in
    respect of any activity related to ballistic missiles capable of
    delivering nuclear weapons, including launches using ballistic
    missile technology, or related to the development of nuclear weapon
    delivery systems, that is inconsistent with the Joint Comprehensive
    Plan of Action.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for any person in Canada and any
  Canadian outside Canada to provide any property, technical assistance
  or financial or related services to Iran or to any person in Iran
  related to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the exportation, sale, supply or shipment of any of
    the goods referred to in subsection (3);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the manufacture or use of such a good in Iran or on
    behalf of Iran; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the provision of technical assistance or technical
    data referred to in subsection (3).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for any person in Canada and any
  Canadian outside Canada to import, purchase or acquire arms and
  related material from Iran or from any person in Iran.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for any person in Canada, and any
  Canadian outside Canada, who is an owner or master of a ship or an
  operator of an aircraft to ship

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any of the goods referred to in paragraph (3)(c)
    from Iran; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} arms and related material, wherever situated, to
    any person in Canada who acquired them from Iran or from any person
    in Iran.

**[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** Sections 4 and 8 do not

## [Obligations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-760123 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The following entities must
determine on a continuing basis whether they are in possession or
control of property that is owned --- or that is held or controlled,
directly or indirectly --- by a listed person:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[authorized foreign banks]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
  as defined in section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), in
  respect of their business in Canada, and banks regulated by that Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cooperative credit societies, savings and credit
  unions and caisses populaires regulated by a provincial Act and
  associations regulated by the [Cooperative Credit Associations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[foreign companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
  defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), in respect of their insurance business in
  Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[provincial
  companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
  [[societies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms are defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fraternal benefit societies regulated by a provincial
  Act in respect of their insurance activities and insurance companies
  and other entities regulated by a provincial Act that are engaged in
  the business of insuring risks;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} companies regulated by the [Trust and Loan Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} trust companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} loan companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} entities that engage in any business described in
  paragraph 5(h) of the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and
  Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501) if the business involves
  the opening of an account for a client; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} entities authorized under provincial legislation to
  engage in the business of dealing in securities or to provide
  portfolio management or investment counselling services.
