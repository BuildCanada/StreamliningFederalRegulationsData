## Withdrawal from Disposal Order (Kelly Lake Area, N.W.T.) {#withdrawal-from-disposal-order-kelly-lake-area-n.w.t. .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/95-23
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 1995-03-08

Order Respecting the Withdrawal from Disposal of Certain Lands in the
Northwest Territories

P.C. 1995-228  1995-02-14
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 23(a) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), is
pleased hereby:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke the [Withdrawal from Disposal Order (Kelly
  Lake Area, N.W.T.)](/eng/regulations/SI-95-23), made by Order in
  Council P.C. 1994-73 of January 13, 1994[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-95-23_e_hq_6603){#fn_SI-95-23_e_hq_6603-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-95-23_e_hq_6603-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_SI-95-23_e_hq_6603}SI/94-11,
    1994 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 920
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to make the annexed [Order respecting the withdrawal
  from disposal of certain lands in the Northwest
  Territories](/eng/regulations/SI-93-150) in substitution therefor.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-648971 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Withdrawal from Disposal Order (Kelly Lake Area,
N.W.T.)](/eng/regulations/SI-95-23).

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-648973 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Order
is to withdraw certain lands from disposal in order to set aside and
protect them pursuant to paragraph 17.4.1(a) of the Sahtu Dene and Metis
Comprehensive Land Claim Agreement, which was approved, given effect and
declared valid by the [Sahtu Dene and Metis Land Claim Settlement
Act](/eng/acts/S-1.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_SI-95-23_e_hq_6604){#fn_SI-95-23_e_hq_6604-ID0EBBA}
on June 23, 1994.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_SI-95-23_e_hq_6604-ID0EBBA){#fn_SI-95-23_e_hq_6604}S.C.
  1994, c. 27
  :::

## [Lands Withdrawn from Disposal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-648975 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4, the
lands described in the schedule are withdrawn from disposal for the
period beginning on the date of registration of this Order and ending on
the day that some or all of those lands are designated as a territorial
park.

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-648977 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Section 3 does not apply in
respect of existing rights, interests, permits and privileges, in
respect of the lands described in the schedule, or any renewals,
replacements or extensions thereof, including

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} existing located or recorded mineral claims or
  prospecting permits in good standing acquired under the Canada Mining
  Regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} existing rights in good standing created pursuant to
  section 8 of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7) or under the
  [Territorial Lands Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1525);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} rights to obtain a surface lease on existing located
  or recorded mineral claims acquired pursuant to section 8 of the
  [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7) or under the [Territorial Lands
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1525);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} existing permits, special renewal permits and leases
  in good standing acquired under the [Canada Oil and Gas Land
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1518);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} existing permits in good standing acquired under the
  [Territorial Quarrying Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1527);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} existing rights, permits and interests granted under
  the [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} existing rights issued pursuant to the [Territorial
  Coal Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1522) or the
  [Territorial Dredging Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1523)
  or pursuant to the Forest Management Act of the Northwest Territories;
  and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} interests in lands described in Exhibit 2 to the
  Acquisition Agreement, dated May 5, 1988, among Her Majesty in right
  of Canada, the Northern Canada Power Commission, the Government of the
  Northwest Territories and the Northwest Territories Power Corporation.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Sections 3 and 4)]{.OriginatingRef}[Lands Withdrawn from Disposal]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-648990 .scheduleLabel}
:::

In the Northwest Territories, all that parcel of land shown as "Surface
Withdrawal" on the following National Topographic Series map on file at
the office of the Regional Manager, Land Resources Division, Department
of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, at Yellowknife, which map is
entitled "*LANDS TO BE WITHDRAWN FROM DISPOSAL OF SURFACE INTERESTS, PER
CLAUSE 17.4 OF THE SAHTU LAND CLAIM*" approved on August 13, 1993 by Jim
Umpherson, Regional Manager, Land Resources, Department of Indian
Affairs and Northern Development.

National Topographic Series Map

(District Of Mackenzie, N.W.T.)

96E

Including any substances or materials that may be disposed of pursuant
to the [Territorial Quarrying
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1527);

Including timber that may be disposed of pursuant to the Forest
Management Act of the Northwest Territories; and

Saving and excepting therefrom and reserving thereout all mines and
minerals, including hydrocarbons, whether solid, liquid or gaseous, and
rights to work the same.
