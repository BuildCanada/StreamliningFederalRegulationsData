## Natural Gas and Gas Liquids Tax Regulations {#natural-gas-and-gas-liquids-tax-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/82-359
:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Registration 1982-03-26

Regulations Respecting the Natural Gas and Gas Liquids Tax

P.C. 1982-923 1982-03-25
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance and the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant
to paragraph 25.19(c) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), is
pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the natural
gas and gas liquids tax](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-359).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873446 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Natural Gas and Gas Liquids Tax
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-359).

## [Prescription of Uses or Sources]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873448 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 25.19(c) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the
following uses and sources of marketable pipeline gas are prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} use by the producer thereof in

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} compressors used in a field gathering system for
    transporting gas to a gas processing plant,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} line heaters in a gas gathering system,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} pilot lights in flare stacks located within a gas
    gathering system or gas field, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} buildings or other structures that are designed
    and used to house equipment described in subparagraphs (i), (ii) and
    (iii), or equipment related thereto; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} wells

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that are not part of a business of producing,
    distributing or processing gas, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} whose production of gas does not exceed
    1,000 gigajoules in a calendar year.

## [Prescription of Rate of Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873458 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following rates of tax
in respect of natural gas and gas liquids are prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of paragraph 25.13(5)(d) of the
  [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the rate of 45 cents per gigajoule,
  in the case of marketable pipeline gas received after January 31, 1983
  but before August 1, 1983;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subparagraph 25.14(4)(a)(iv) of
  the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the rate of \$8.35 per cubic
  metre, where the ethane is received after January 31, 1983 but before
  August 1, 1983;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subparagaph 25.14(4)(b)(iv) of
  the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the rate of \$11.45 per cubic
  metre, where the propane is received after January 31, 1983 but before
  August 1, 1983;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subparagraph 25.14(4)(c)(iv) of
  the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the rate of \$12.75 per cubic
  metre, where the butanes are received after January 31, 1983 but
  before August 1, 1983;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of paragraph 25.13(5)(d) of the
  [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the rate of 15 cents per gigajoule,
  in the case of marketable pipeline gas received after July 31, 1983
  but before February 1, 1984;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subparagraph 25.14(4)(a)(iv) of
  the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the rate of \$2.80 per cubic
  metre, where the ethane is received after July 31, 1983 but before
  February 1, 1984;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subparagraph 25.14(4)(b)(iv) of
  the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the rate of \$3.80 per cubic
  metre, where the propane is received after July 31, 1983 but before
  February 1, 1984;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subparagraph 25.14(4)(c)(iv) of
  the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the rate of \$4.25 per cubic
  metre, where the butanes are received after July 31, 1983 but before
  February 1, 1984;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of paragraph 25.13(5)(d) of the
  [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the rate of 0 cents per gigajoule,
  in the case of marketable pipeline gas received after January 31,
  1984;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subparagraph 25.14(4)(a)(iv) of
  the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the rate of 0 cents per cubic
  metre, where the ethane is received after January 31, 1984;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subparagraph 25.14(4)(b)(iv) of
  the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the rate of 0 cents per cubic
  metre, where the propane is received after January 31, 1984; and

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subparagraph 25.14(4)(c)(iv) of
  the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the rate of 0 cents per cubic
  metre, where the butanes are received after January 31, 1984.
