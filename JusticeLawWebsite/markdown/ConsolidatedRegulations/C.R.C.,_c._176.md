## Ontario Bean Order {#ontario-bean-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 176
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Beans Produced in Ontario
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-535985 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Bean Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._176).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-535987 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[beans]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[beans]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means white pea beans and yellow-eye
    beans produced in Ontario; ([haricots]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing
    Board of Ontario established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Régie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Bean
    Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act; ([Office
    de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    beans established and amended from time to time pursuant to the Act
    and any regulations made pursuant to the Act to give effect to the
    Plan. ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-535994 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board and the
Board are each authorized to regulate the marketing of beans in
interprovincial and export trade and for such purposes may, with respect
to persons and property situated within the Province of Ontario,
exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable by each of them
in relation to the marketing of beans locally within that Province under
the Act and the Plan.

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-535996 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/80-90\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the powers granted to it by section 3
  with respect to the marketing of beans in interprovincial and export
  trade,

to make orders fixing, imposing and collecting levies or charges from
persons situated in the Province of Ontario who are engaged in the
production or marketing of beans and for such purpose classifying such
persons into groups and fixing the levies or charges payable by the
members of the different groups in different amounts and the Commodity
Board may use such levies or charges for its purposes, including the
creation of reserves and the payment of expenses and losses resulting
from the sale or disposal of beans and the equalization or adjustment
among producers of beans of moneys realized from the sale thereof during
such period or periods of time as it may determine.
