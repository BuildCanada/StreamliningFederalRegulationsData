## Leamy Lake Navigation Channel Regulations {#leamy-lake-navigation-channel-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-547
:::

[NATIONAL CAPITAL ACT](/eng/acts/N-4)

Registration 1998-10-29

Leamy Lake Navigation Channel Regulations

P.C. 1998-1930  1998-10-29
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Canadian Heritage, pursuant to section 20 of the
[National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4), hereby makes the annexed [Leamy
Lake Navigation Channel Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-547).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025115 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[authorized vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorized vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2007-113, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[boat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[boat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a watercraft of any description
    capable of being used for navigation regardless of how it is
    propelled. ([bateau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[channel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[channel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Leamy Lake navigation
    channel described in Schedule 1. ([chenal]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Commission employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a person acting
    on behalf of the Commission under a contract or other agreement.
    ([employé de la Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[control booth operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[control booth operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commission
    employee operating the control booth situated at the Gatineau River
    entrance to the channel. ([préposé du poste de
    contrôle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[motorboat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[motorboat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a motorized boat that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is used exclusively for pleasure; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is designed to carry fewer than 10 passengers and
      is ordinarily used to carry them for remuneration. ([bateau à
      moteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[motor fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[motor fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes gasoline, fuel oil and
    diesel fuel. ([carburant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, as
      defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
      Act](/eng/acts/R-10), designated as a peace officer under
      paragraph 7(1)(d) of that Act;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a police officer in the police department of the
      City of Gatineau, in the Province of Quebec; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a supernumerary special constable appointed under
      paragraph 7(1)(c) of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
      Act](/eng/acts/R-10) for the purposes of these Regulations and
      designated as a peace officer under paragraph 7(1)(d) of that Act.
      ([agent de la paix]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shuttle boat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shuttle boat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a motorized boat that is
    designed to carry 10 or more passengers and is ordinarily used to
    carry them for remuneration. ([bateau-mouche]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[waiting area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[waiting area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area of the channel
    described in Schedule 2. ([aire d'attente]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-113, s. 1

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025134 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to peace officers or Commission employees acting in the course of
their employment or duties, other than the control booth operator acting
under sections 10 to 12.

## [Prohibitions and Obligations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025136 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/2007-113,
s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person may, in the
channel,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} swim or bathe;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} do anything that unreasonably interferes with another
  person's use of the channel;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} behave in a manner that endangers public safety;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} stop a boat anywhere outside the waiting area, except
  in the case of an emergency;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} anchor a boat, except in the case of an emergency;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} dump sewage or other waste;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} dump motor fuel or motor oil;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} sell motor fuel or motor oil or supply a motorboat or
  a shuttle boat with motor fuel or motor oil; or

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} fuel a motorboat or a shuttle boat with motor fuel or
  add motor oil to a motorboat's engine or a shuttle boat's engine,
  except in the case of an emergency.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-113, s. 3
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-4-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person may operate a
boat in the channel if the dimensions, configuration, draught or
condition of the boat or the goods or equipment on it could

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} endanger public safety; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} delay or obstruct navigation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-113, s. 4
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-5-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 5"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person operating a boat in the channel shall comply with the
  instructions of a peace officer, the control booth operator or any
  other Commission employee acting in the course of their employment or
  duties in respect of the safe and orderly use of the channel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person operating a motorboat or a shuttle boat
  in the channel shall obey the signal lights in the channel, unless
  otherwise instructed by a peace officer, the control booth operator or
  any other Commission employee acting in the course of their employment
  or duties.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-113, s. 5, err.(E), Vol. 152, No. 4
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-6-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 6"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every person operating a
boat in the channel shall control the boat in a manner that minimizes
its wake in order to avoid endangering public safety or causing damage
to the shoreline, to any other boat or to any structure or object.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-113, s. 5

## [Permission to Enter Channel]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025163 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Sections 9 to 12 apply
during the hours of operation of the control booth set out by the
Commission on a sign posted on the booth.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No person operating a
motorboat or a shuttle boat may enter the channel from the Gatineau
River unless the control booth operator has given the person permission
to enter the channel.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-113, s. 6
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-9-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 9"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 12,
the control booth operator shall give a person operating a motorboat
permission to enter the channel if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} there are fewer than 20 motorboats in the channel;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} there are fewer than 25 motorboats in total in the
  channel and Lac de la Carrière.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 12,
the control booth operator shall give a person operating a shuttle boat
permission to enter the channel if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} there are no shuttle boats in the channel; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is not more than one shuttle boat in Lac de la
  Carrière.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The control booth
operator shall deny a person operating a boat permission to enter the
channel if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the control booth operator believes, on reasonable
  grounds, that the person is impaired by alcohol or drugs;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person is behaving in a manner that endangers
  public safety;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the channel is blocked by a disabled boat; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an emergency exists in the channel or in Lac de la
  Carrière.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-113, s. 7
:::

## [Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025181 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes any of sections 4 to 7 and 9 is liable on summary conviction
to a fine of not more than \$500 or to imprisonment for a term of not
more than six months, or to both.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-113, s. 8

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on October 29, 1998.

## [SCHEDULE 1]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 1)]{.OriginatingRef}[Leamy Lake Navigation Channel]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-1025188 .scheduleLabel}
:::

That portion of Leamy Lake, in the City of Gatineau, in the Province of
Quebec, the north passage linking Leamy Lake to the Gatineau River and
the part of the south passage linking Leamy Lake to Lac de la Carrière,
delineated by a line drawn from a point at coordinates 45°27′22″
75°43′12″ to a point at coordinates 45°27′13″ 75°43′16″, thence to a
point at coordinates 45°27′8″ 75°43′19″, thence to a point at
coordinates 45°27′9″ 75°43′23″, thence to a point at coordinates
45°27′7″ 75°43′31″, thence to a point at coordinates 45°27′2″ 75°43′33″,
thence to a point at coordinates 45°26′59″ 75°43′30″, thence to a point
at coordinates 45°26′59″ 75°43′20″, thence to a point at coordinates
45°26′51″ 75°43′22″ and thence in a southerly direction to the concrete
abutment supporting the steel arches of the bridge crossing the south
passage linking Leamy Lake to Lac de la Carrière, on the east side of
the passage, and a line drawn from a point at coordinates 45°27′21″
75°43′12″ to a point at coordinates 45°27′14″ 75°43′16″, thence to a
point at coordinates 45°27′11″ 75°43′18″, thence to a point at
coordinates 45°27′7″ 75°43′32″, thence to a point at coordinates
45°27′2″ 75°43′35″, thence to a point at coordinates 45°26′58″
75°43′30″, thence to a point at coordinates 45°26′58″ 75°43′23″, thence
to a point at coordinates 45°26′57″ 75°43′21″, thence to a point at
coordinates 45°26′52″ 75°43′22″, thence in a southerly direction to the
concrete abutment supporting the steel arches of the bridge crossing the
south passage linking Leamy Lake to Lac de la Carrière, on the west side
of the passage.
