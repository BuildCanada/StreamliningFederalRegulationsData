## Reportable Diseases Regulations {#reportable-diseases-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-2
:::

[HEALTH OF ANIMALS ACT](/eng/acts/H-3.3)

Registration 1990-12-13

Regulations Prescribing Certain Diseases as Reportable Diseases
::::

::::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to subsection 2(2) of the [Health
of Animals Act](/eng/acts/H-3.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-91-2_e_hq_5221){#fn_SOR-91-2_e_hq_5221-ID0EDBCA},
hereby makes the annexed [Regulations prescribing certain diseases as
reportable diseases](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-2).

cited as the [Reportable Diseases
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-2).

## [Reportable Diseases]{.HTitleText1} {#h-933213 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The diseases set out in the
schedule are prescribed as reportable diseases for the purpose of
section 2 of the [Health of Animals Act](/eng/acts/H-3.3).
