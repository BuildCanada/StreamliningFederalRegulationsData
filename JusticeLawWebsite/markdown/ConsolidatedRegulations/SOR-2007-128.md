## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-732615 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Shipping Act,
      2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[cargo transport unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cargo transport unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a road freight
      vehicle, a railway freight wagon, a freight container, a road tank
      vehicle, a railway tank wagon or a portable tank. ([engin de
      transport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[IMO]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[IMO]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Maritime
      Organization. ([OMI]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport.
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[short-run ferry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[short-run ferry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that
      regularly operates over the most direct water route between two
      points not more than 5 km apart and that is limited to the
      transport of unberthed passengers and of cargo transport units
      carried on an open vehicle deck. ([traversier]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[SOLAS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[SOLAS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Convention
      for the Safety of Life at Sea, 1974 and the Protocol of 1988
      relating to the Convention. ([SOLAS]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of interpreting a document
  incorporated by reference in these Regulations other than CAN/CSA
  Standard S826.1-01, Ferry Boarding Facilities, "should" shall be read
  to mean "shall".

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, every
  reference to "Administration" in a document incorporated by reference
  in these Regulations means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a Canadian vessel, the Minister; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a foreign vessel, the government of
    the state whose flag the vessel is entitled to fly.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise indicated in these Regulations, any
  reference in these Regulations to a document is a reference to the
  document as amended from time to time.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-732629 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as otherwise provided in Parts 1 to 3, these Regulations apply in
  respect of Canadian vessels everywhere and foreign vessels in Canadian
  waters.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations, other than section 102, do not
  apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pleasure craft; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} vessels used for commercially catching, harvesting
    or transporting fish or other living marine resources unless the
    vessels are 24 m or more in length and their sole participation in
    those activities is in respect of the catch or harvest of other
    vessels or of aquaculture facilities.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of vessels
  that are capable of engaging in the drilling for, or the production,
  conservation or processing of, oil or gas.

### [Exemptions and Equivalents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-732661 .Subheading}

**[[101]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-101 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
this Part, the Marine Technical Review Board established under section
26 of the Act may exercise the powers of the Administration under
regulations 4 and 5 of Chapter I of SOLAS.

### [General --- Marking Mass of One Tonne or More]{.HTitleText2} {#h-732663 .Subheading}

- **[[102]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-102 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall consign to be loaded on a vessel in Canadian waters a
  package or object of a gross mass of one tonne or more unless its
  gross mass is clearly and durably marked on the outside of the package
  or object.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The authorized representative and the master of a
  vessel in Canadian waters, and every agent charged with loading a
  vessel in Canadian waters, shall not cause or permit to be loaded on
  the vessel a package or object of a gross mass of one tonne or more
  unless its gross mass is clearly and durably marked on the outside of
  the package or object.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the exact mass of the package or object would be
  difficult to ascertain because of its nature, an approximate mass may
  be marked accompanied by the word "approximate" or "approximatif", or
  a reasonable abbreviation.

- **[[105]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-105 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is a Canadian vessel engaged or about to

### [DIVISION 2]{.HLabel2}[Solid Bulk Cargo Other Than Grain]{.HTitleText2} {#h-732682 .Subheading}

#### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-732683 .Subheading}

- **[[106]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-106 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Division.

  [[BC Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[BC Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2021-60, s.
      2\]]{.Repealed}

  [[concentrates]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[concentrates]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means materials that are
      obtained from a natural ore by a process of enrichment or
      benificiation by physical or chemical separation and removal of
      unwanted constituents. ([concentrés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[dangerous goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[dangerous goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means materials that are
      covered by the IMDG Code and are solid bulk cargo. ([marchandises
      dangereuses]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[double-side skin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[double-side skin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
      vessel, that each side of the vessel is constructed by the side
      shell and a longitudinal bulkhead that connects the double bottom
      and the deck. Hopper side tanks and top-side tanks may, if fitted,
      be integral parts. ([double muraille]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[IMSBC Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[IMSBC Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International
      Maritime Solid Bulk Cargoes Code, published by the IMO. ([Code
      IMSBC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[incompatible]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[incompatible]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of two or more
      kinds of goods, means that the goods may react dangerously when
      mixed. ([incompatible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a vessel, means 96%
      of the total length on a waterline at 85% of the least moulded
      depth measured from the top of the keel, or the length from the
      fore side of the stem to the axis of the rudder stock on that
      waterline if that is greater. In vessels designed with a rake of
      keel, the waterline on which the length is measured shall be
      parallel to the designed waterline. ([longueur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[single-side skin construction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[single-side skin construction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect
      of vessel, means that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any part of a cargo hold is bounded by the side
        shell; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one or more cargo holds are bounded by a
        double-side skin with a width measured perpendicular to the side
        shell of less than 760 mm in vessels constructed before January
        1, 2000 and less than 1 000 mm in vessels constructed on or
        after January 1, 2000 but before July 1, 2006. ([muraille
        simple]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[solid bulk cargo]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[solid bulk cargo]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any bulk cargo
      other than liquid or gas. ([cargaison solide en
      vrac]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[space]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[space]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an enclosed space in a vessel.
      ([espace]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If dangerous goods are carried on barges that are
  joined together and towed as a single unit, those barges shall be
  regarded as a single vessel for the purposes of this Division.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Division, every reference to
  "competent authority", "authority of the port of State" or
  "appropriate authority" in the IMSBC Code means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel in Canadian waters or a
    Canadian vessel in international waters, the Minister;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel in the waters of a foreign
    state, the government of that state; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a foreign vessel in international
    waters, the government of the state whose flag the vessel is
    entitled to fly.

##### [Chapter XII of SOLAS]{.HTitleText4} {#h-732719 .Subheading}

- **[[109]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-109 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  authorized representative of a Safety Convention vessel of 150 m or
  more in length that has a single-side skin construction, was
  constructed before July 1, 1999 and is carrying solid bulk cargo that
  has a density of 1 780 kg/m^3^ or more shall ensure that the vessel
  complies with regulations 6.1.1 to 6.1.3 of Chapter XII of SOLAS

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel constructed 17 years or
    more before the day on which this section comes into force, no later
    than the day on which this section comes into force; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel constructed less than 17
    years before the day on which this section comes into force, on the
    earliest of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the seventeenth anniversary of the date of its
      construction,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the day on which this section comes into force
      if on or before that day and after the fifteenth anniversary of
      the date of its construction the vessel had a periodical
      inspection required by regulation 2 of Chapter XI of SOLAS, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the day on which it has its first periodical
      inspection required by regulation 2 of Chapter XI of SOLAS after
      the fifteenth anniversary of the date of its construction if that
      day is after the day on which this section comes into force.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The master of a Safety Convention vessel shall ensure
  that the vessel complies with regulation 6.4 of Chapter XII of SOLAS.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The authorized representative of a Safety Convention
  vessel of 150 m or more in length that has a single-side skin
  construction, was constructed on or after July 1, 1996 but before July
  1, 1999 and is carrying solid bulk cargo that has a density of 1 780
  kg/m^3^ or more shall ensure that the vessel complies with regulation
  7.1 of Chapter XII of SOLAS.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The authorized representative of a Safety Convention
  vessel shall ensure that the requirement of regulation 8.3 of Chapter
  XII of SOLAS is met.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The authorized representative of a vessel of 150 m or
  more in length shall ensure that the vessel complies with regulation
  11.1 of Chapter XII of SOLAS.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (5) does not apply before January 1, 2013
  in respect of a Canadian vessel that is not a Safety Convention vessel
  if it has on board a loading guidance manual that the Minister has
  determined adapts the principles for safe loading and unloading
  procedures set out in sections 4 to 6 of the BLU Code to apply in
  respect of the vessel.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The authorized representative of a vessel of less
  than 150 m in length that was constructed on or after July 1, 2006
  shall ensure that the vessel complies with regulation 11.3 of Chapter
  XII of SOLAS.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The authorized representative of a Safety Convention
  vessel of 150 m or more in length that has a single-side skin
  construction and is carrying solid bulk cargo that has a density of 1
  780 kg/m^3^ or more shall ensure that the vessel complies with
  regulation 14 of Chapter XII of SOLAS, unless that regulation does not
  apply in respect of the vessel.

##### [General Precautions]{.HTitleText4} {#h-732743 .Subheading}

- **[[111]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-111
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every person on a vessel who enters
  either a cargo space containing dangerous goods or a space adjacent to
  that cargo space shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} comply with the requirements of the IMSBC Code
    regarding wearing self-contained breathing apparatus;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be aware of the potential dangers in entering the
    space that are described in the IMSBC Code; and

  vessel's breadth in the athwartship direction for the full width of
  the hold.

### [DIVISION 3]{.HLabel2}[Grain Cargo]{.HTitleText2} {#h-732864 .Subheading}

#### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-732865 .Subheading}

- **[[121]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-121 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Division.

  [[grain]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[grain]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means wheat, corn, oats, rye,
      barley, rice, pulses and other seeds and the processed form of
      seeds whose behaviour is similar to that of seeds in their natural
      state. ([grain]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a vessel, means 96%
      of the total length on a waterline at 85% of the least moulded
      depth measured from the top of the keel, or the length from the
      fore side of the stem to the axis of the rudder stock on that
      waterline if that is greater. In vessels designed with a rake of
      keel, the waterline on which the length is measured shall be
      parallel to the designed waterline. ([longueur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Division, the reference in
  A4 of the International Grain Code to "an equivalent accepted by the
  Administration in accordance with regulation I/5 of the International
  Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea, 1974, as amended" shall, in
  respect of Canadian vessels, be read as a reference to "a replacement
  granted by the Marine Technical Review Board under section 28 of the
  [Canada Shipping Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) or an equivalent
  accepted by that Board under section 102 of the [Cargo, Fumigation and
  Tackle Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-128)".

#### [Application]{.HTitleText3} {#h-732871 .Subheading}

**[[122]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-122 .sectionLabel}** This Division applies
in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Canadian vessels that are loading or carrying grain
  in bulk and are engaged or about to engage on a voyage other than an
  inland voyage, a sheltered waters voyage or a near coastal voyage,
  Class 2;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} vessels in Canadian waters that are loading or
  carrying grain in bulk that is for export to a place that is not
  within the limits of an inland voyage; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} foreign vessels in Canadian waters that are carrying
  grain in bulk and are engaged or about to engage on a voyage other
  than an inland voyage.

##### [Lashings and Components]{.HTitleText4} {#h-732981 .Subheading}

- **[[135]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-135 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of 4.5.1 of the Timber Code, all lashings used for the
  securing of timber shall be tested by a competent person to determine
  whether they

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements of 4.1.2 of the Code;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of chains, have a link weld capable of
    a 90° cold bend without separation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of flexible steel wire ropes, are at
    least 16 mm in diameter.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of 4.5.1 of the Timber Code, all
  components used for the securing of timber shall be tested by a
  competent person to determine whether they

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements of 4.1.2.2 and 4.1.2.3 of the
    Code; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have a breaking strength of at least 14 100 kg.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of 4.5.1 of the Timber Code, if
  lashings or components meet the requirements referred to in
  subsection (1) or (2), as the case may be, the competent person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} mark each of them in a distinctive manner that
    indicates the month and year of testing; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sign and issue a certificate that sets out

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the distinguishing mark,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the lashings or components,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date of testing,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the number of similar lashings or components
      tested on that date,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the proof load,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the original breaking strength,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} their name and

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if they are an employee, the name and address
        of their employer, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if they are not an employee, their address, and

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the qualifications that qualify them as a
      competent person.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of 4.5.1 of the Timber Code,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lashings and components in respect of which a
    certificate is issued under paragraph (3)(b) shall be re-tested by a
    competent person at least once every four years after the day on
    which the certificate is issued to determine whether or not they
    exhibit permanent deformation after having been subjected to a proof
    load of at least 40% of their original breaking strength; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the lashings or components do not exhibit
    permanent deformation in the test, the competent person may sign and
    issue a certificate that sets out the information specified in
    paragraph (3)(b).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of 4.5.1 of the Timber Code, if, when
  a visual examination under 4.5.3 of the Timber Code or section 138 is
  made, the diameter of a section of a lashing or component appears to
  be reduced by 5% or more from its original diameter, any certificate
  issued in respect of the lashing or component is invalid unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the reduction is 10% or less, the lashing or
    component is re-tested by a competent person subjecting it to a
    proof load of at least 40% of its original breaking strength and it
    does not exhibit permanent deformation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the reduction is more than 10%, a representative
    sample of the lashing or component, or of another lashing or
    component in respect of which the same certificate applies and that
    has a similar reduction in a section of its diameter, is tested by a
    competent person to destruction and has a breaking strength of at
    least 133 kN.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of 4.5.1 of the Timber Code, if, when
  a visual examination under 4.5.3 of the Timber Code or section 138 is
  made, the cross-sectional area of a section of a lashing or component
  appears to be reduced by 10% or more from its original area, any
  certificate issued in respect of the lashing or component is invalid
  unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the reduction is 20% or less, the lashing or
    component is re-tested by a competent person subjecting it to a
    proof load of at least 40% of its original breaking strength and it
    does not exhibit permanent deformation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the reduction is more than 20%, a representative
    sample of the lashing or component, or of another lashing or
    component in respect of which the same certificate applies and that
    has a similar reduction in a section of its cross-sectional area, is
    tested by a competent person to destruction and has a breaking
    strength of at least 133 kN.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of 4.5.1 of the Timber Code, any
  certificate issued in respect of a web lashing is invalid if when a
  visual examination under 4.5.3 of the Timber Code or section 138 is
  made it appears that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the length of an edge cut exceeds the lashing's
    thickness;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the depth of an abrasion is more than 15% of the
    lashing's thickness, taken as a proportion of all plies;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total depth of abrasions on both sides of the
    lashing is more than 15% of the lashing's thickness, taken as a
    proportion of all plies;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the depth of the warp thread damage is 50% or less
    of the lashing's thickness and the damage

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is within 25% of the width of the lashing from
      its edge, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is over an area that is more than 25% of the
      lashing's width;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the depth of the warp thread damage is more than
    50% of the lashing's thickness and the damage

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is within 25% of the width of the lashing from
      its edge, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is over an area that is more than 12.5% of the
      lashing's width;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} weft thread damage allows warp threads to separate
    over an area that is wider than 25% of the lashing's width and
    longer than twice its width;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any part of the lashing is melted, charred or
    damaged by chemicals;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} load-bearing stitches are broken or worn; or

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the web-lashing is damaged in such a way that the
    total effect of the damage on the lashing is approximately the same
    as the effect of any of the types of damage referred to in
    paragraphs (a) to (h).

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[competent
  person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a professional engineer or a person
  with similar qualifications.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who packs dangerous goods in a cargo

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the unit is, immediately before stowing,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the unit's doors or similar openings are closed and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} if the unit is temperature controlled, it has

#### [Precautions Respecting Wheeled Cargo Transport Units]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733141 .Subheading}

- **[[150]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-150 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  inclement weather is forecast, the master of a vessel that is carrying
  a wheeled cargo transport unit that contains dangerous goods shall
  ensure that adequate means, having regard to the kind of weather
  forecast and the planned route of the voyage, are provided to restrain
  the wheeled cargo transport unit and any wheeled cargo transport units
  stowed in its immediate vicinity. The means shall include any one or
  any combination of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} brakes that can be set;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} wheel stops;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} lashing down with securing devices that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are described in Table 1 to section 4.2 of Annex
      13 to the CSS Code,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are attached to the deck, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} are not used with a working load that exceeds
      the applicable percentages of the maximum securing load set out in
      that Table for the devices; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} devices specifically designed for securing wheeled
    cargo transport units.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If securing devices are used, the master shall ensure
  that the information required by section 2.1 or 2.2, as the case may
  be, of the Annex to Appendix 2 to the CSS Code is kept on board.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who secures a wheeled cargo transport
  unit that contains dangerous goods shall take into consideration

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} movement between the body and the wheels of the
    unit due to the springing of the unit's suspension;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the centre of gravity of the unit in relation to
    both the wheel base and the wheel-to-ground contact area; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of rail vehicles, the attachment of the
    body to the running gear.

#### [Vehicles, Motor Boats and Other Conveyances Carried on a Vessel]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733156 .Subheading}

- **[[151]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-151 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  passenger vessel that is engaged on a sheltered waters voyage, a near
  coastal voyage, Class 2 or an inland voyage may carry a motor vehicle,
  other than a recreational vehicle, that has fuel in its fuel tank or
  is carrying fuel outside the fuel tank to propel the vehicle if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle is driven on and off the vessel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fuel tank is not so full as to be likely to
    spill because of an increase in volume from temperature changes;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the ignition is switched off; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle is carrying, outside the fuel tank, no
    more than 25 L of the fuel needed to propel it and the fuel is in a
    means of containment that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is designed for the carriage of that type of
      fuel,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} meets the requirements of the [Transportation of
      Dangerous Goods Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286) for
      the means of containment,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is secured within the vehicle, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if a gaseous fuel is carried, is equipped with
      valves protected by valve caps.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A passenger vessel that is engaged on a sheltered
  waters voyage, a near coastal voyage, Class 2 or an inland voyage may
  carry a recreational vehicle or recreational trailer that is carrying
  cylinders of liquefied petroleum gas or propane if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle or trailer is driven or towed on and
    off the vessel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the vehicle or trailer carries cylinders of
    liquefied petroleum gas,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it does not carry more than two cylinders with a
      total capacity not exceeding 65 L,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the gas is for domestic use,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} each cylinder is secured to the vehicle or
      trailer, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the valves of each cylinder are securely closed
      at all times while the vehicle or trailer is on the vessel;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} when the vehicle or trailer carries a portable
    barbecue,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the barbecue is equipped with not more than one
      cylinder of propane with a capacity not exceeding 65 L, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the valves on the cylinder are securely closed
      at all times while the vehicle or trailer is on the vessel; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the vehicle, it meets the
    requirements of paragraphs (1)(b) to (d).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A passenger vessel that is engaged on a sheltered
  waters voyage, a near coastal voyage, Class 2 or an inland voyage may
  carry a trailer, including one carrying containers of gasoline, that
  is being used to carry a motorboat or a total of not more than two
  motorbikes, all-terrain vehicles, snowmobiles and personal watercraft
  that have gasoline in their fuel tanks or are carrying containers of
  gasoline outside their fuel tanks to propel them if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the trailer is towed by or attached to a motor
    vehicle that is driven on and off the vessel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fuel tanks are not so full as to be likely to
    spill because of an increase in volume from temperature changes; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} not more than two containers of gasoline are being
    carried and each of them

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has a capacity not exceeding 25 L,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} meets the requirements of the [Transportation of
      Dangerous Goods Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286) for
      the means of containment, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is secured on the trailer or in the other
      conveyance.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A passenger vessel that is engaged on a sheltered
  waters voyage, a near coastal voyage, Class 2 or an inland voyage may
  carry a service repair vehicle that meets the requirements of
  subsection (1) and is carrying cylinders of propane, compressed oxygen
  or dissolved acetylene if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} not more than four service repair vehicles that are
    carrying any of those cylinders are carried at the same time;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle is not carrying more than one cylinder
    of propane with a capacity not exceeding 65 L, one cylinder of
    compressed oxygen with a capacity not exceeding 50 L and one
    cylinder of dissolved acetylene with a capacity not exceeding 75 L;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the cylinders

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are secured to the vehicle in an upright position
      by permanently installed fittings, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} have their valves securely closed and protected
      by a screw-on metal cap;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle is stowed at either end of the vehicle
    deck and access to the place where it is stowed is restricted by
    means of barriers or signs;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} when the vessel has a mechanical ventilation system
    for the vehicle deck, it is operating while the vehicle is stowed on
    board;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the vessel's master ensures that a continuous watch
    is maintained on the vehicle deck to monitor all service vehicles;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle is stowed so that it is always
    accessible in case of fire;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} on closed-deck ferries, a minimum distance of 12 m
    is maintained athwartships between service vehicles; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on open-deck ferries, the maximum distance feasible
    but in any case at least 6 m is maintained athwartships between
    service vehicles.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A passenger vessel that is engaged on a sheltered
  waters voyage, a near coastal voyage, Class 2 or an inland voyage may
  carry a motor vehicle that meets the requirements of subsection (1)
  and is carrying a cylinder of refrigerated liquid oxygen if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the vehicle is loaded, the vessel's master
    is provided with a copy of any emergency response instructions
    contained in the dangerous goods shipping documents or prepared by
    the shipper;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cylinder

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has a capacity not exceeding 450 L, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is secured to the vehicle in an upright position
      by permanently installed fittings;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} not more than one vehicle that is carrying a
    cylinder of refrigerated liquid oxygen is carried at the same time;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle is stowed at one end of the vehicle
    deck and access to the place where it is stowed is restricted by
    means of barriers or signs;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle is not stowed within 3 m of other
    motorized conveyances that have fuel in their fuel tanks;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel has a mechanical ventilation system
    for the vehicle deck, it is operating while the vehicle is stowed on
    board;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (i), if the vehicle is stowed
    on a closed vehicle deck, or a partially closed vehicle deck not
    fitted with overhead deluge sprinklers,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the vessel has a mechanical ventilation system
      for the deck, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} no other dangerous goods are carried on the same
      deck;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (i), if the vehicle is stowed
    on a partially closed vehicle deck fitted with an overhead deluge
    sprinkler system,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the refrigerated liquid oxygen is deemed to have
      a 5.1 subsidiary risk classification for the purpose of
      determining the segregation requirements under the IMDG Code,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} only dangerous goods that require not more than
      "AWAY FROM" segregation are carried on the same deck and those
      that require "AWAY FROM" segregation are separated by a horizontal
      distance of at least 12 m, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} no inert compressed gases that have subsidiary
      risks are carried on the deck;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} when the vehicle is carrying dangerous goods solely
    for hospital supply,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle is on an open vehicle deck or on a
      partially open vehicle deck space fitted with an overhead deluge
      sprinkler system,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the accompanying dangerous goods documentation
      is clearly marked "Medical gases for hospital supply" or "[gaz
      médicaux pour fournitures d'hôpitaux]{.otherLang lang="fr"}", and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the only other compressed gases that are
      carried in the vehicle are

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} compressed oxygen, contained in up to a maximum
        of 10 cylinders of not more than 25 cm in diameter or 150 cm in
        length, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} compressed nitrous oxide, contained in up to a
        maximum of 4 cylinders of not more than 25 cm in diameter or 150
        cm in length;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} when the cylinder is attached to a live-fish tank
    system,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the tank is closed and permanently secured to the
      chassis of the vehicle,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the tank is oxygenated with not more than 5 L of
      gaseous oxygen per minute or in a manner or quantity that ensures
      that the oxygen is consumed by the fish to the maximum extent
      feasible,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the cylinder has a capacity not exceeding 17 L,
      and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the tank and the tubes connecting the cylinder
      to the tank are manufactured and installed to withstand a maximum
      pressure of 172.4 kPa (25 psi) in accordance with an engineering
      standard recognized by a provincial engineering association; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the vessel's master or an officer designated by the
    master is aware at all times of the location on board of the
    vehicle's operator.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (8), a passenger vessel that is
  engaged on a voyage other than a voyage described in subsection (1)
  may carry a motorized conveyance that has fuel in its fuel tank if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when it is stowed on deck,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its fuel tank is not so full as to be likely to
      spill because of an increase in volume from temperature changes,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} its ignition is switched off; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when it is stowed under deck,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its fuel tank is drained so that not more than 10
      L of gasoline remain in the tank,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} its ignition is switched off, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} its battery terminals are disconnected and
      taped to prevent electrical shorting or it is stowed in a hold
      designated as a special category space in the vessel's inspection
      certificate issued under the Act or, in the case of a foreign
      vessel, by or under the authority of the government of the state
      whose flag the vessel is entitled to fly.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The master of a vessel that is not a passenger vessel
  shall ensure that a motorized conveyance that has fuel in its fuel
  tank is not carried on the vessel unless the conditions set out in
  subsection (6) are met.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Before a motor vehicle that is carrying dangerous
  goods classified as Class 1 in the IMDG Code and is to be stowed under
  deck or towed on and off a closed vehicle deck, the vessel's master
  shall ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its fuel tank is drained and its engine runs until
    it stalls for want of fuel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its ignition is switched off; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its battery terminals are disconnected and taped to
    prevent electrical shorting.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} The master of a vessel shall ensure that, before a
  motor vehicle is loaded onto the vessel, it is inspected for leakage
  of fuel or any other substance and, if there are signs of such
  leakage, the master shall ensure that it is not loaded onto the vessel
  until the leakage is stopped.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} To the extent feasible, the master of a vessel shall
  ensure that every motorized conveyance on board is stowed so as to
  permit inspection.

##### [Stowing]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733285 .Subheading}

**[[158]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-158 .sectionLabel}** If explosives, other
than those that have toxic or lachrymatory properties, of different
compatibility groups cannot be segregated by the methods specified in
the IMDG Code and a vessel's authorized representative or master has so
informed the Department of Transport Marine Safety Office nearest to the
vessel, they may be stowed by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} placing them in a steel container that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is in new or like-new condition, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if they are explosive substances set out in the
    IMDG Code, is sheathed on all interior metal surfaces except the
    roof with wood or plywood that is 19 mm thick and, if the container
    has a wooden floor, is sheathed on the floor with masonite panels,
    or another material that is at least as smooth and hard as masonite,
    at least 6 mm thick;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} taping all joints in the interior sheathing or
  sealing them using waterproof materials to prevent leaking or sifting;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} securing the container against all movements of the
  vessel likely to be encountered on the voyage;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the container is carried on deck, stowing it so
  that access to its door is maintained at all times;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} except in the case of vessels carrying explosives
  exclusively, maintaining an intervening space of at least 6 m between
  the container and other containers of incompatible goods and between
  the containers and incompatible goods that are not in containers; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the container is stowed in a cargo hold that does
  not have a means of detecting fire,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} fitting a system of sniffing-pipes in or adjacent
    to the spaces containing the explosives,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} regularly monitoring the air in those spaces, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} entering the times and results of the monitoring
    in the deck log book.

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} specifying the name, registration number, port of

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} specifying the date of loading, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} certifying that the representative was present
      during the loading and that to the best of the representative's
      knowledge the loading was done in accordance with this Division.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The master shall keep the statement on board until
  the military explosives are unloaded.

##### [Explosives Used for Marine Drilling and Blasting Operations]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733308 .Subheading}

- **[[160]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-160 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a vessel that is engaged in marine drilling and blasting
  operations using explosives shall ensure that the requirements set out
  in subsections (2) to (15) are met.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Explosives other than detonators

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall not be carried on the vessel; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall be stowed on an explosives-storage craft
    specially adapted for the carriage and stowage of explosives.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The craft shall be moored securely to the vessel in a
  position that is remote from the drills and as far as possible from
  any navigable channel.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The distance between the craft and the vessel shall
  be kept to a minimum to facilitate easy handling of the explosives.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No person shall be on board the craft until the
  explosives are actually required.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The craft shall be equipped with an efficient anchor
  that has sufficient line for use in case of an emergency.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} A sign reading "EXPLOSIVES" or
  "[EXPLOSIFS]{.otherLang lang="fr"}" in letters that are at least 10 cm
  in height and displayed against a background of contrasting colour
  shall be displayed in a conspicuous position on the craft.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} If work is discontinued for more than 24 hours, all
  explosives remaining on the craft shall be moved ashore and placed in
  a magazine that is licensed under section 7 of the [Explosives
  Act](/eng/acts/E-17) and meets the requirements of the [Explosives
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._599).

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} No person shall smoke or use naked lights or
  spark-producing equipment on the craft.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} Areas on the vessel where smoking or using naked
  lights or spark-producing equipment could create a fire or explosion
  hazard shall be identified and warning notices displayed in
  conspicuous places on board the vessel prohibiting the activity in
  those areas.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} Lightning conductors shall be fitted to wooden
  masts, and to steel masts if electrical conductivity of the rigging is
  defective, on the vessel and the craft. If the vessel or craft has a
  steel mast and the hull is made of steel, the mast shall be bonded to
  the hull.

- [(12)]{.lawlabel} Detonators on the vessel shall be stowed in a locked
  box or cabinet remote from the drills.

- [(13)]{.lawlabel} The armed charges shall be made ready only a few
  minutes before use and shall be stowed in a receptacle on the vessel.

- [(14)]{.lawlabel} In each shift, one person shall be assigned to
  transfer the explosives from the craft to the vessel and another
  person shall be assigned to arm the charges.

- [(15)]{.lawlabel} The two persons shall also be responsible for the
  mooring lines between the vessel and the craft and shall pay special
  attention to the lines while other vessels are navigating in the
  vicinity.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Fumigation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-733366 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-733367 .Subheading}

**[[200]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-200 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in this Part.

[[aerate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aerate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to reduce or attempt to reduce
    the concentration of a fumigantl. ([aérer]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[alongside]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[alongside]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means alongside a wharf or quay.
    ([Version anglaise seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[clearance certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[clearance certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a certificate
    issued by a fumigator-in-charge or a marine chemist that certifies
    that a vessel or space is gas-free. ([certificat
    d'attestation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[competent person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[competent person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who has the
    knowledge and experience to safely and adequately perform the duties
    required by this Part of a fumigator-in-charge, including at least
    150 hours of experience on board vessels under the supervision of a
    marine chemist or fumigator-in-charge in the use and operation of
    equipment that detects the presence of gas in the atmosphere.
    ([personne compétente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fumigant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fumigant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pesticide that acts in a
    gaseous state to fumigate. ([fumigant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fumigation in transit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fumigation in transit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the fumigation
    on a vessel of bulk cargo, or of a space that contains bulk cargo,
    while

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is en route between two ports; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is in a Canadian port if it is the
      intention of the master to continue the fumigation after the
      vessel leaves the port. ([fumigation en cours de
      route]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fumigator-in-charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fumigator-in-charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a competent person
    responsible for carrying out a fumigation.
    ([spécialiste]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[gas-free]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gas-free]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a space or a vessel,
    means that the presence of a fumigant cannot be detected in the
    space or the vessel by a fumigator-in-charge or another competent
    person using detection methods and equipment that are appropriate to
    the fumigant. ([exempt de gaz]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[marine chemist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marine chemist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a qualified person who

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a graduate of a post-secondary educational
      institution and has

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} successfully completed courses in chemical
        engineering,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} successfully completed a general course with a
        major in chemistry, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} obtained a fellowship in the Chemical
        Institute of Canada; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has at least three years' experience in chemical
      or engineering work after meeting the requirements of paragraph
      (a), of which at least 150 working hours were spent in ship-board
      work involving the testing of tank vessels and other vessels in
      the application of gas hazard control standards under the
      supervision of a person with at least 500 hours' experience in
      that work. ([chimiste de la marine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Seaway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Seaway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    2(1) of the [Canada Marine Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7). ([voie
    maritime]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[space]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[space]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an enclosed space on a vessel.
    ([espace]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[TLV]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[TLV]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a fumigant, means the
    highest allowable concentration of the fumigant in a space to which
    a person may be exposed under this Part. ([VLE]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-733388 .Subheading}

**[[201]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-201 .sectionLabel}** This Part does not
apply in respect of a cargo transport unit carried on a short-run ferry
if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the unit is stowed at either end of the ferry and is
  separated from all other cargo transport units and all vehicles by a
  distance of at least 1 m;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} smoking and the use of naked lights or
  spark-producing equipment are prohibited in the vicinity of the unit;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any parking brakes that are fitted on the unit are
  securely set; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} no person other than the operator of the unit is
  permitted by the ferry's master to approach within 1 m of the unit.

### [DIVISION 1]{.HLabel2}[General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-733394 .Subheading}

#### [Application]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733395 .Subheading}

**[[202]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-202 .sectionLabel}** This Division applies
in respect of fumigation and aeration

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on a Canadian vessel; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on a foreign vessel that is in Canadian waters if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the fumigation begins in Canadian waters, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any cargo destined for a Canadian port is
    fumigated in transit.

#### [Use of Fumigants]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733401 .Subheading}

- **[[203]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-203 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall use a fumigant other than one set out in column 1 of
  Schedule 1 to fumigate on a vessel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, at any time other than during a fumigation of a
  space, a person has reasonable grounds to believe that the
  concentration of a fumigant set out in column 1 of Schedule 2 in the
  space exceeds the TLV for the fumigant set out in column 2 or 3, the
  person shall immediately

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} warn every person whom they know to be in the space
    that it should be evacuated; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify the vessel's master of the excessive
    concentration.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} After being notified of the excessive concentration,
  the master shall advise all persons on board the vessel of the
  excessive concentration.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every person in the space shall evacuate it after
  being advised of the excessive concentration.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No person who has been advised of the excessive
  concentration shall enter the space unless that person wears a
  self-contained breathing apparatus required by paragraph 210(3)(d).

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The master shall ensure that the space is aerated by
  crew members with experience using the equipment to be used in the
  aeration or by persons assisting the fumigator-in-charge.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (3) to (6) cease to apply when a
  competent person determines that the concentration of the fumigant
  does not exceed the applicable TLV.

#### [Fumigating When a Vessel Is Not Alongside]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733412 .Subheading}

- **[[204]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-204 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall fumigate on a Canadian vessel that is not alongside.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall fumigate a space on a foreign vessel
  that is not alongside unless the space contains bulk cargo.

#### [Fumigating the Contents of Barges or Cargo Transport Units]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733416 .Subheading}

**[[205]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-205 .sectionLabel}** No person shall begin
to fumigate the contents of a barge or cargo transport unit that is on
board a vessel.

#### [Notification and Conduct of Fumigation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733418 .Subheading}

- **[[206]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-206
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before beginning to fumigate on a
  vessel in a Canadian port, the fumigator-in-charge shall ensure that
  notice of the intention to fumigate is given in writing to the
  Department of Transport Marine Safety Office nearest to the vessel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before a vessel on which fumigation in transit has
  begun arrives at a Canadian port or the Seaway, the vessel's master
  shall give notice to the Department of Transport Marine Safety Office
  nearest to the port or entry point to the Seaway that fumigation in
  transit on the vessel has begun.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If feasible, the notice shall be given

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel referred to in subsection
    (1), at least 24 hours before fumigation begins; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel referred to in subsection
    (2), at least 24 hours before its arrival at the port or in the
    Seaway.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The notice shall specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel referred to in subsection
    (1), the name of the port where the fumigation will be carried out
    and, if applicable, the number of the berth within the port;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel referred to in subsection
    (2), the name of the port or the entry point to the Seaway; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel referred to in
    subsection (1) or (2), the name of the fumigant and method of
    application involved and whether the fumigation

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is or will be of the cargo, cargo spaces or
      accommodation spaces on board the vessel,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} will be completed before the vessel leaves the
      port or the Seaway, as the case may be,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is or will be a fumigation in transit, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} is or will be of cargo on board a vessel that
      will be unloaded at a Canadian port.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the fumigant to be used in a fumigation referred
  to in subsection (1) is, or during the fumigation is likely to become,
  a flammable gas, the vessel's master shall, before fumigation begins,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} remove all flammable materials, including refuse
    and oily waste, from any space that is to be fumigated; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} disconnect all electrical circuits that lead to any
    space that is to be fumigated.

#### [Fumigator-in-Charge]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733436 .Subheading}

- **[[207]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-207
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  master of a vessel shall ensure that every fumigation and every
  aeration are carried out under the direction of a fumigator-in-charge.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A fumigator-in-charge is not required to be present
  in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a fumigation in transit that began in a Canadian
    port if the tests required by sections 219 and 220 are conducted and
    the requirements of section 221 are met;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a fumigation in transit that began outside Canadian
    waters during the period beginning when the vessel enters Canadian
    waters and ending when it enters a Canadian port to unload cargo; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a fumigation of a cargo transport unit on board a
    vessel that began before the unit was loaded onto the vessel.

#### [Report of Danger]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733443 .Subheading}

**[[208]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-208 .sectionLabel}** If persons on board a
vessel are in serious and imminent danger as a result of a fumigation on
the vessel, its master shall immediately report the danger and the
circumstances that gave rise to it to the Department of Transport Marine
Safety Office nearest to the vessel by the quickest means available.

### [DIVISION 2]{.HLabel2}[Fumigation of Cargo, Cargo Spaces and Accommodation Spaces While a Vessel Is Alongside]{.HTitleText2} {#h-733445 .Subheading}

#### [Application]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733446 .Subheading}

**[[209]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-209 .sectionLabel}** This Division applies
in respect of the fumigation and aeration of cargo, a cargo space or an
accommodation space on a vessel while it is alongside.

#### [Fumigation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733448 .Subheading}

- **[[210]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-210 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  fumigator-in-charge shall not begin fumigating or permit it to begin
  unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all persons who are on board the vessel and are not
    engaged in the fumigation or in the care of the vessel have
    disembarked;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fumigator-in-charge has on display near the
    gangways and near the entrances that lead to a space that is to be
    fumigated a sign that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} corresponds to the sign set out in Schedule 3 and
      bears the name of the fumigant being used, the date and hour when
      the fumigation began and the signature of the fumigator-in-charge
      or the vessel's master, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is rectangular in shape, at least 250 mm wide
      and at least 200 mm high with the word "DANGER" in letters at
      least 25 mm high; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} he or she has posted a person to keep watch at each
    place where the vessel can be boarded while it is alongside.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 212(3), the persons referred to
  in paragraph (1)(a) shall not board the vessel until a clearance
  certificate has been issued in respect of the vessel.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} During fumigation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person keeping watch shall not allow anyone who
    is not engaged in the fumigation or care of the vessel to board it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fumigator-in-charge shall take all feasible
    measures to prevent the leakage of the fumigant from a space that is
    being fumigated;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fumigator-in-charge or a competent person
    acting under the direction of the fumigator-in-charge shall conduct
    any periodic tests that the fumigator-in-charge determines are
    necessary to ascertain whether a fumigant is leaking from a space
    that is being fumigated;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} each person on board the vessel shall have
    available for immediate use a self-contained breathing apparatus
    that can protect them against the fumigant; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (8), no person shall enter a
    space that is being fumigated.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall remove a sign referred to in
  paragraph (1)(b) until a clearance certificate has been issued in
  respect of the vessel or until aeration has been completed.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} When a clearance certificate has been issued in
  respect of the vessel, the vessel's master shall ensure that any signs
  warning of the fumigation are removed.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the fumigant leaks from a space that is being
  fumigated,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} every person who is taking part in the fumigation
    shall, under the direction of the fumigator-in-charge, take all
    feasible measures to stop the leakage; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fumigator-in-charge shall immediately notify
    the master of the leakage.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} If the leakage referred to in subsection (6) is
  stopped, the fumigator-in-charge shall notify the master of the
  stoppage. However, if the fumigator-in-charge determines that the
  leakage cannot be stopped, he or she shall direct the persons taking
  part in the fumigation to cease the fumigation and to aerate the
  space.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} If the fumigator-in-charge determines that entry into
  a space that is being fumigated is necessary, the fumigator-in-charge
  and one or more other persons experienced and knowledgeable in the use
  of the self-contained breathing apparatus required by paragraph (3)(d)
  may enter the space if they wear

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the apparatus; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a safety harness fitted with a lifeline that is
    tended by a person outside the space who is also wearing the
    apparatus.

#### [Fumigation of Cargo]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733472 .Subheading}

- **[[211]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-211
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph 210(1)(a), the
  fumigator-in-charge may begin fumigating cargo or permit it to begin
  when there are crew members on board the vessel who are not engaged in
  the fumigation or in the care of the vessel if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fumigator-in-charge has inspected the space in
    which the cargo is located and has advised the vessel's master in
    writing that during the fumigation no fumigant is likely to leak
    from the space containing the cargo and into a space that is
    ordinarily occupied by crew members; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the space in which the cargo is located

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is not adjacent to a space that is ordinarily
      occupied by crew members, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is separated by at least two gas-tight bulkheads
      from a space used by crew members.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} During a fumigation begun under subsection (1), the
  fumigator-in-charge or a competent person acting under the direction
  of the fumigator-in-charge shall conduct any periodic tests that the
  fumigator-in-charge determines are necessary to determine whether the
  concentration of a fumigant set out in column 1 of Schedule 2 in a
  space that is ordinarily occupied by crew members exceeds the TLV for
  the fumigant set out in column 2 or 3 of Schedule 2.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a test result shows that the concentration of a
  fumigant exceeds the applicable TLV, all persons on board the vessel
  who are not wearing the self-contained breathing apparatus required by
  paragraph 210(3)(d) shall immediately disembark.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section, "gas-tight", in relation to a
  bulkhead, means that no fumigant can pass

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} through the bulkhead; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} over the top, under the bottom or around either end
    of the bulkhead.

#### [Aeration]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733484 .Subheading}

- **[[212]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-212
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After a space is fumigated, the
  fumigator-in-charge shall ensure that it is aerated.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before the aeration begins, the fumigator-in-charge
  shall advise the vessel's master in writing of the location of the
  spaces that will be occupied by a crew member for the purpose of
  assisting in the aeration.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A crew member may, subject to the direction of the
  fumigator-in-charge, board the vessel to assist in the aeration by
  opening the hatches of the vessel and operating generating and
  ventilation machinery if the crew member is wearing the self-contained
  breathing apparatus required by paragraph 210(3)(d).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a crew member assists in the aeration of a space,
  the fumigator-in-charge shall, as frequently as he or she determines
  it is necessary, conduct tests to measure the concentration of the
  fumigant in every space occupied by the member.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a test result shows that the concentration of the
  fumigant exceeds the TLV for the fumigant set out in column 2 or 3 of
  Schedule 2, every person in the space shall wear the self-contained
  breathing apparatus required by paragraph 210(3)(d) or evacuate the
  space until a test result shows that the concentration of the fumigant
  does not exceed the applicable TLV.

#### [Clearance Certificates]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733491 .Subheading}

- **[[213]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-213 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  fumigator-in-charge shall not issue a clearance certificate in respect
  of a vessel unless the vessel is gas-free.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the vessel is gas-free, the fumigator-in-charge
  shall issue a clearance certificate.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to Division 3, until a clearance certificate
  is issued, the vessel shall remain alongside.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The vessel's master shall record in the vessel's
  logbook the issuance of a clearance certificate and the date of
  issuance.

### [DIVISION 3]{.HLabel2}[Fumigation in Transit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-733497 .Subheading}

#### [Beginning Fumigation in Canadian Waters]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733498 .Subheading}

**[[214]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-214 .sectionLabel}** No person shall begin
fumigation in transit on a foreign vessel in Canadian waters unless the
vessel is moored or at anchor in a Canadian port.

#### [SUBDIVISION 1]{.HLabel3}[Beginning Fumigation in a Canadian Port]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733500 .Subheading}

##### [Application]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733501 .Subheading}

**[[215]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-215 .sectionLabel}** This Subdivision
applies in respect of fumigation in transit on a foreign vessel that
begins when the vessel is moored or at anchor in a Canadian port.

##### [Methyl Bromide]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733503 .Subheading}

**[[216]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-216 .sectionLabel}** No person shall
fumigate with methyl bromide.

##### [General]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733505 .Subheading}

- **[[217]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-217 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall begin fumigation unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fumigator-in-charge, accompanied by the
    vessel's master or the master's agent, has inspected the space in
    which the cargo is to be fumigated before the cargo that is to be
    fumigated is loaded on board and has determined that, during the
    fumigation, no fumigant is likely to leak from the space containing
    the cargo and into an adjoining space or out of the vessel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fumigator-in-charge has given a notice in
    writing to the vessel's master that specifies

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the fumigant that will be used,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any hazards that the fumigation might present,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the precautions or other steps that the crew
      shall take in relation to the fumigation, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the results of the inspection referred to in
      paragraph (a) and specifically the determination of the
      fumigator-in-charge that, during the fumigation, no fumigant is
      likely to leak from the space containing the cargo and into an
      adjoining space or out of the vessel;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the loading of cargo onto the vessel is completed
    and all persons, other than the persons who are engaged in the
    fumigation or who will sail with the vessel, have disembarked;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the fumigator-in-charge has given notice in writing
    to the following of the location of the spaces that will be
    fumigated and of all other spaces that the fumigator-in-charge
    determines are unsafe for entry by any person during the fumigation
    and before the vessel becomes gas-free:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the vessel's master,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the harbour master at the port or, if there is
      no harbour master, the person responsible for the port, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Department of Transport Marine Safety
      Office nearest to the vessel;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} every person who will sail with the vessel has been
    informed by the fumigator-in-charge or by the vessel's master of the
    carrying out of the fumigation and of the danger of entering the
    spaces referred to in paragraph (d);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} there is on display near the gangways and near the
    entrances that lead to the space in which the cargo is to be
    fumigated a sign that meets the requirements of paragraph 210(1)(b);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the fumigator-in-charge has posted a person to keep
    watch at each place where the vessel can be boarded while it is
    moored or at anchor;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} no fewer than two crew members, one of whom is an
    officer, have knowledge of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any instructions that appear on the package
      containing the fumigant to be used in the fumigation,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any recommendations or information provided by
      the manufacturer of the fumigant in respect of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the method of detecting the fumigant when it is
        in a gaseous state,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the behaviour and properties of the fumigant,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the symptoms likely to be shown by and the
        medical treatment of a person who has been poisoned by the
        fumigant, and

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} the emergency procedures that should be taken
        to prevent fire and explosion of the fumigant, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the operation of any equipment on board that is
      used to detect the presence of a fumigant; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the vessel's master has designated at least two of
    the crew members referred to in paragraph (h) to ensure that safe
    conditions in the accommodation spaces and working spaces are
    maintained after the fumigator-in-charge leaves the vessel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall remove the signs referred to in
  paragraph (1)(f) until a clearance certificate has been issued in
  respect of the space or until aeration has been completed.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When a clearance certificate has been issued in
  respect of the space, the vessel's master shall ensure that any signs
  warning of the fumigation are removed.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The person keeping watch shall not allow a person who
  is not engaged in the fumigation or who will sail with the vessel to
  board it while it is moored or at anchor.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(a), fumigation may begin when
  the inspection referred to in that paragraph is carried out after
  cargo has been loaded if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holds adjacent to the accommodation spaces are
    not fumigated; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is alongside or, if it is not safe for
    the vessel to be alongside, it is moored elsewhere or at anchor and
    a launch service is available on short notice at all times.

##### [Equipment and Documents]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733537 .Subheading}

- **[[218]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-218 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a vessel shall ensure that it is equipped with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} four sets of self-contained breathing apparatus
    that meet the requirements of paragraph 210(3)(d) together with four
    additional filled air bottles, four safety harnesses and four
    lifelines;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} two devices that can detect the presence of a
    fumigant when the fumigant is used in its gaseous state;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the instructions provided by the manufacturer of
    the fumigant for the disposal of the fumigant;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the most recent version of the Medical First Aid
    Guide for Use in Accidents Involving Dangerous Goods (MFAG),
    published by the IMO; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the medicines and medical equipment specified in
    the Guide referred to in paragraph (d) for the fumigant used in the
    fumigation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The equipment required by paragraphs (1)(a) and (b)
  is in addition to any other equipment that is required to be carried
  on board the vessel when no fumigation takes place.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the devices referred to in paragraph (1)(b)
  require re-arming after use, the authorized representative of the
  vessel shall ensure that it is equipped with 10% more spare tubes than
  are needed to conduct the tests required by paragraph 220(3)(a) for
  the duration of the voyage.

##### [Tests Before Leaving Port]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733547 .Subheading}

- **[[219]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-219
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 220(1), the
  vessel shall not leave the port until

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 24 hours after fumigation begins or any longer
    period that the fumigator-in-charge determines is necessary to
    ascertain whether there is any leakage of the fumigant has elapsed;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fumigator-in-charge has advised the vessel's
    master in writing that he or she has determined that there are at
    least two crew members on board the vessel, including at least one
    officer, who meet the requirements of paragraph 217(1)(h).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fumigator-in-charge shall conduct any periodic
  tests that he or she determines are necessary to ascertain whether a
  fumigant is leaking from a space in which cargo is being fumigated,
  including tests to determine whether the concentration of the fumigant
  in the space is sufficiently high during the testing to detect
  leakage.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The tests shall be conducted at least three times and
  the last test shall be conducted at the end of the 24-hour period or
  the longer period, as the case may be.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the fumigator-in-charge determines that a fumigant
  is leaking, the vessel shall not leave the port until he or she

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ascertains that the leakage has stopped after
    conducting any additional tests that he or she determined were
    necessary; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} issues a clearance certificate in respect of every
    space into which the fumigant was leaking.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If, after conducting the periodic tests and any
  additional tests, the fumigator-in-charge determines that no fumigant
  is leaking from the space, he or she shall advise the vessel's master
  in writing that on completion of the testing no fumigant was detected
  in any space adjoining the space in which cargo is being fumigated or,
  in the case of a fumigation referred to in subsection 217(5), in the
  holds adjacent to the accommodation spaces.

##### [When Fumigator-in-charge Is on Board the Vessel After It Leaves Port]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733558 .Subheading}

- **[[220]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-220
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Section 219 does not apply if a
  fumigator-in-charge is on board the vessel when it leaves the port and
  remains on board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for at least 24 hours;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} until he or she determines that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of the fumigant in a space that
      is being fumigated is sufficiently high that he or she could
      detect whether any of the fumigant is leaking from the space,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all spaces in the vessel that are adjacent to
      the space in which the cargo is being fumigated are gas-free, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} there are at least two crew members on board
      the vessel, including at least one officer, who meet the
      requirements of paragraph 217(1)(h); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for any additional period that he or she determines
    is necessary in the circumstances.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fumigator-in-charge or a competent person acting
  under his or her direction shall conduct any periodic tests in the
  vessel that the fumigator-in-charge determines are reasonably
  necessary to ascertain whether any fumigant is leaking from a space
  that is being fumigated, including tests to determine whether the
  concentration of the fumigant in the space where the cargo is being
  fumigated is sufficiently high during the testing to detect leakage.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The tests shall be conducted

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least every eight hours, with the first test
    beginning when the vessel leaves the place where the fumigation
    began; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a manner that will indicate whether any fumigant
    is leaking into any space that is ordinarily or is likely to be
    occupied by a crew member.

##### [Before the Fumigator-in-charge Leaves the Vessel]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733571 .Subheading}

**[[221]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-221 .sectionLabel}** The fumigator-in-charge
shall not leave the vessel unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of the fumigant in a space that is
  being fumigated was sufficiently high that he or she could have
  detected whether any of the fumigant was leaking from the space;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all spaces in the vessel that are adjacent to the
  space in which the cargo is being fumigated are gas-free;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the equipment referred to in paragraphs 218(1)(a)
  and (b) is on board;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} there is a sufficient number of crew members on board
  who are qualified to use the devices referred to in paragraph
  218(1)(b) to detect the presence of a fumigant in a space during the
  voyage; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} he or she has notified the vessel's master in writing
  that the responsibility for carrying out the fumigation rests with the
  master.

#### [SUBDIVISION 2]{.HLabel3}[Fumigation That Begins in a Canadian Port or Outside Canadian Waters]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733578 .Subheading}

##### [Application]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733579 .Subheading}

**[[222]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-222 .sectionLabel}** This Subdivision
applies in respect of fumigation in transit on a foreign vessel and the
aeration of spaces in it if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fumigation begins while the vessel is moored or
  at anchor in a Canadian port; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is in Canadian waters and the fumigation
  began before it entered Canadian waters.

##### [Fumigant Detected in a Space That Is Likely to Be Occupied]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733583 .Subheading}

- **[[223]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-223
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every person who detects a fumigant
  in a space that is likely to be occupied by any person shall
  immediately notify the persons occupying the space and the vessel's
  master.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person in the space shall evacuate it.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The vessel shall go to the nearest Canadian port and
  remain there until the space is gas-free.

##### [Report of Danger]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733588 .Subheading}

**[[224]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-224 .sectionLabel}** If persons on board a
vessel are in serious and imminent danger as a result of the fumigation
of the vessel, its master shall immediately report the danger and the
circumstances that gave rise to it to the Department of Transport Marine
Safety Office nearest to the vessel by the quickest means available.

##### [Recording Tests]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733590 .Subheading}

**[[225]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-225 .sectionLabel}** If a person conducts a
test to determine whether a fumigant is present in a space, the vessel's
master shall record the date and results of the test in the vessel's
logbook.

##### [Aeration]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733592 .Subheading}

- **[[226]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-226 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the vessel is under way, the vessel's master or, if a
  fumigator-in-charge is on board, the fumigator-in-charge shall ensure
  that any aeration of a space that was fumigated is carried out in a
  manner that minimizes the likelihood of the fumigant entering a space
  in the vessel that is ordinarily occupied by a crew member or into a
  ventilation system.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The vessel's master shall ensure that the aeration is
  carried out only if he or she determines that it is necessary for the
  safety of the crew or to meet an emergency that could affect the crew.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} During the aeration, the master or, if a
  fumigator-in-charge is on board, the fumigator-in-charge shall conduct
  tests to determine the concentration of a fumigant in a space that is
  ordinarily occupied by a crew member and in each ventilation system.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a test result shows that the concentration of the
  fumigant exceeds the TLV for the fumigant set out in column 2 or 3 of
  Schedule 2,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the master shall direct that the aeration be
    stopped and the space that is being aerated be sealed to prevent
    leakage of the fumigant; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aeration of the space shall not resume unless
    the master or, if a fumigator-in-charge is on board, the
    fumigator-in-charge determines that its resumption will not cause
    the concentration of the fumigant to exceed the applicable TLV in
    the space or ventilation system.

### [DIVISION 4]{.HLabel2}[Arrival of Cargo That Has Been Fumigated in Transit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-733600 .Subheading}

#### [SUBDIVISION 1]{.HLabel3}[Unloading or Topping Off]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733601 .Subheading}

##### [Application]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733602 .Subheading}

**[[227]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-227 .sectionLabel}** This Subdivision
applies in respect of a foreign vessel in Canadian waters if it is
carrying bulk cargo that has been fumigated in transit and any part of
it is to be unloaded or topped off at a Canadian port.

##### [Notice]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733604 .Subheading}

**[[228]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-228 .sectionLabel}** No vessel shall enter
Canadian waters until a notice has been sent to the Department of
Transport Marine Safety nearest to the port to which the vessel is
destined that sets out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the vessel;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the port;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the expected date of the vessel's arrival in the
  port;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the cargo;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name of the fumigant that was used to fumigate
  the cargo; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the fumigation in transit began.

##### [Entering Spaces]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733612 .Subheading}

- **[[229]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-229 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall enter a space that was fumigated unless a clearance
  certificate has been issued in respect of the space.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to any person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} who is wearing a self-contained breathing apparatus
    that can protect them against any fumigant that is used for the
    fumigation and who is experienced and knowledgeable in the use of
    the apparatus; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} who is wearing a safety harness fitted with a
    lifeline that is tended by a person outside the space who is also
    wearing such an apparatus.

##### [Removal of Signs]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733618 .Subheading}

- **[[230]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-230 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  a clearance certificate has been issued in respect of a space, the
  vessel's master shall ensure that any signs warning of the fumigation
  are removed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The master shall enter the date and time of the
  issuance of the clearance certificate and of the removal of the signs
  in the vessel's logbook.

##### [Conditions for Unloading and Topping Off]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733622 .Subheading}

- **[[231]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-231 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall unload or top off the cargo unless a clearance
  certificate has been issued in respect of the space where the cargo is
  located.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who unloads or tops off the cargo shall
  use mechanical equipment that is controlled from outside the space in
  which the fumigation in transit was carried out.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who operates the mechanical equipment
  shall do so on an open deck of the vessel windward of the hatchway
  through which the cargo is unloaded or loaded and well clear of all
  ventilators.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} During the unloading or topping off of the cargo, no
  person shall enter the space from which it is being unloaded or topped
  off.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (4), a person may enter the space
  to service the mechanical equipment if they are at all times
  accompanied by a competent person who continuously measures the
  concentration of the fumigant at or near the place where the servicing
  takes place.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the concentration of a fumigant set out in column
  1 of Schedule 2 in a space exceeds one half of the TLV for the
  fumigant set out in column 2 or 3 of Schedule 2, every person in the
  space shall evacuate it or wear a self-contained breathing apparatus
  that can protect them against the fumigant.

#### [SUBDIVISION 2]{.HLabel3}[When Cargo Is Not to Be Unloaded or Topped Off]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733630 .Subheading}

##### [Application]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733631 .Subheading}

**[[232]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-232 .sectionLabel}** This Subdivision
applies in respect of a foreign vessel if it is carrying bulk cargo that
has been fumigated in transit and enters a Canadian port for a purpose
other than the unloading or topping off of any of that cargo.

##### [Duty of Master]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733633 .Subheading}

**[[233]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-233 .sectionLabel}** The master of a vessel
shall ensure that a fumigator-in-charge boards the vessel immediately
after it is moored in a Canadian port.

##### [Duties of Fumigator-in-charge]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733635 .Subheading}

**[[234]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-234 .sectionLabel}** The fumigator-in-charge
shall, as soon as feasible,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} display near the gangways and near the entrances that
  lead to a space in which cargo has been fumigated a sign that meets
  the requirements of paragraph 210(1)(b);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} post a person to keep watch at each place where the
  vessel can be boarded while it is moored; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} conduct any periodic tests that he or she determines
  are necessary to ascertain whether a fumigant is leaking from a space
  in which the cargo has been fumigated, including tests to determine
  whether the concentration of the fumigant in the space where the cargo
  has been fumigated is sufficiently high during the testing to detect
  leakage.

##### [Leakage of Fumigant]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733640 .Subheading}

- **[[235]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-235 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the fumigator-in-charge determines that a fumigant is leaking into a
  space that is likely to be occupied by any person, the vessel shall
  not leave the port until

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fumigator-in-charge ascertains that the leakage
    has stopped after conducting any additional tests that he or she
    determined were necessary; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fumigator-in-charge issues a clearance
    certificate in respect of the space into which the fumigant was
    leaking.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, after conducting the initial tests and any
  additional tests, the fumigator-in-charge determines that no fumigant
  is leaking from the space, he or she shall advise the vessel's master
  in writing that on completion of the testing no fumigant was detected
  in any space adjoining the space in which cargo was fumigated.

##### [Duties of Person Keeping Watch]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733646 .Subheading}

- **[[236]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-236 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  person keeping watch shall not allow a person who is not a crew member
  and is not on the vessel's business to board the vessel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person keeping watch shall ensure that any person
  who is not a crew member but is on the vessel's business does not
  enter a space in respect of which a sign required by paragraph 234(a)
  is displayed.

##### [If Cargo is Aerated]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733650 .Subheading}

- **[[237]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-237 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the vessel's master directs that any of the cargo that was fumigated
  in transit be aerated, the fumigator-in-charge or, if the
  fumigator-in-charge is not on board, the master shall ensure that the
  aeration is carried out in a manner that minimizes the likelihood of
  the fumigant entering a space in the vessel that is ordinarily
  occupied by a crew member or into a ventilation system.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} During the aeration, the fumigator-in-charge or, if
  the fumigator-in-charge is not on board, the master shall conduct
  tests to measure the concentration of a fumigant in a space that is
  ordinarily occupied by a crew member and in each ventilation system.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a test result shows that the concentration of the
  fumigant exceeds the TLV for the fumigant set out in column 2 or 3 of
  Schedule 2, the master shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure that every person in the space

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} wears self-contained breathing apparatus that can
      protect them against the fumigant, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} evacuates it until a test result shows that the
      concentration of the fumigant does not exceed the applicable TLV;
      or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} direct that the aeration be stopped and the space
    that is being aerated be sealed to prevent leakage of the fumigant
    until a fumigator-in-charge determines that the resumption of
    aeration will not cause the concentration of the fumigant to exceed
    the applicable TLV in the space or ventilation system.

### [DIVISION 5]{.HLabel2}[Carriage of Cargo Transport Units That Have Been Fumigated]{.HTitleText2} {#h-733659 .Subheading}

**[[238]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-238 .sectionLabel}** This Division applies
in respect of cargo transport units of which the contents have been
fumigated but not aerated before the units are loaded on board a vessel.

**[[239]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-239 .sectionLabel}** No person shall load a
cargo transport unit onto a vessel unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a competent person has determined that the
  concentration of fumigant is reasonably uniform throughout the unit;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vessel's master has been informed that the
  contents of the unit have been fumigated.

<!-- -->

- **[[240]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-240 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a vessel shall ensure that every cargo transport unit on
  board is stowed on an open deck at a distance of at least 6 m from the
  crew accommodation, the passenger accommodation, if any, work areas
  and vessel ventilation intakes.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a cargo transport unit may be
  stowed below deck in a vessel that is equipped with a mechanical
  ventilation system that operates in the space where the unit is stowed
  if the vessel carries not more than 25 passengers or 1 passenger for
  every 3 m of the vessel's overall length, whichever is greater.

**[[241]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-241 .sectionLabel}** If a cargo transport
unit is stowed on board a vessel, the vessel shall not enter a Canadian
port unless the vessel's master has notified the following of the
vessel's expected arrival at the port at least 24 hours before the
vessel enters the port:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Department of Transport Marine Safety Office
  nearest to the port; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the harbour master at the port or, if there is no
  harbour master, the person responsible for the port.

### [DIVISION 1]{.HLabel2}[Cargo Gear]{.HTitleText2} {#h-733729 .Subheading}

#### [Compliance]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733730 .Subheading}

- **[[302]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-302 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  authorized representative of a vessel shall ensure that the
  requirements of this Division are met in respect of cargo gear that is
  part of the vessel's equipment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as provided in subsection (3), the owner of
  cargo gear that is not part of a vessel's equipment shall ensure that
  the requirements of this Division are met in respect of the gear.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of unitized cargo and wire banding or
  flat steel strapping that unitizes cargo,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the cargo is loaded on a vessel in Canadian
    waters, the shipper shall ensure that the requirements of sections
    361 and 362 are met in respect of the banding or strapping; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} while the cargo is being carried on or unloaded
    from a vessel, the master of the vessel shall ensure that the
    requirements of sections 361 and 362 are met in respect of the
    banding or strapping.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a category 4 lifting appliance is used on board a
  Canadian vessel on a temporary or seasonal basis, the owner of the
  appliance and the authorized representative of the vessel shall ensure
  that the requirements of this Division are met in respect of the
  appliance.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Sections 303 to 306 do not apply in respect of cargo
  gear on board a foreign vessel if the gear was tested and examined in
  accordance with, and a certificate was signed after the last time the
  gear was tested or thoroughly examined under,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the laws of the state whose flag the vessel is
    entitled to fly if that state is a party to Convention 152; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the regulations, rules or codes of a classification
    society that the Minister has determined would result in an
    equivalent or greater level of safety to that provided for by those
    sections.

#### [Testing, Thorough Examination and Inspection]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733741 .Subheading}

##### [Lifting Appliances]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733742 .Subheading}

- **[[303]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-303
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every lifting appliance shall be
  tested by a competent person in the manner set out in Schedule 4

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the appliance is used for the first time or,
    in the case of a category 4 lifting appliance installed on a vessel
    that is not a restricted vessel, before the first time it is used on
    the vessel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} after replacement, modification or repair of any
    stress-bearing part, unless the part is mechanically detachable and
    has been tested separately from the appliance and certified in
    accordance with section 312;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at least once every five years if the appliance is
    a category 1 lifting appliance, category 2 lifting appliance, or
    category 3 lifting appliance; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} at least once every four years if the appliance is
    a category 4 lifting appliance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a lifting appliance cannot be tested in the manner
  set out in Schedule 4 because of any design feature specific to the
  appliance, the testing shall be adapted to take that feature into
  account.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A lifting appliance shall not be tested unless the
  competent person has been provided with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a certificate in respect of the appliance that was
    issued under subsection 312(1) after a previous test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the design plans of the appliance's manufacturer
    that set out the manufacturer's ratings for the appliance, including
    its safe working load; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an affidavit sworn by the appliance's manufacturer
    or a professional engineer that attests to its safe working load and
    compliance with the requirements of section 317 in respect of safety
    factors at that safe working load.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A lifting appliance passes the test if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the means provided on all winches to stop and hold
    the load in position are effective;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if electrical winches are fitted with
    electromagnetic brakes and with mechanical brakes for manual
    operation, the mechanical brakes are in good condition;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the emergency stopping devices fitted on winches
    are effective;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} no defects or signs of permanent deformation are
    detected; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a mobile crane on a vessel that is
    not a restricted vessel, the slewing capacity of the crane when
    tested secured to the vessel is adequate to control the boom under
    the heel produced by the testing.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(a) does not apply in respect of a
  category 5 lifting appliance that is installed on a Canadian vessel
  that is not a Safety Convention vessel and is operated on that vessel
  before this section comes into force.

- **[[304]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-304 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  lifting appliance shall be thoroughly examined

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by a person referred to in paragraph 300(2)(a)

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by a competent person at least once every year

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A category 5 lifting appliance installed on a

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by a competent person at least once every year

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A lifting appliance passes the examination if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} certificates have been issued under section 312 for

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the parts that align and swivel under load are

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the mechanical, electrical, gearing, hydraulic and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} parts are not affected by corrosion to the extent

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} no defects or signs of permanent deformation are

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the design plans of the gear's manufacturer that

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an affidavit sworn by the gear's manufacturer or a

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Gear passes the test if it is does not break and no

##### [Wire Ropes]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733792 .Subheading}

**[[307]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-307 .sectionLabel}** Before being used, wire
rope shall be tested by a competent person causing a sample section to
be pulled to destruction.

- **[[308]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-308 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Wire
  rope shall be thoroughly examined by a competent person at least once
  every year after it is tested or, if the rope passes over a drum or
  sheave, at least once every six months after it is tested.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Wire rope passes the examination if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is no sign of internal corrosion;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is no sign of a tendency towards separation
    of the strands or wires;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} there are no flats on individual wires;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number of broken wires in any length equal to
    eight diameters does not exceed

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 10% of the total number of wires in the length,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any lesser number of broken wires that is
      required by section 12 of Wear Standards for Cargo Gear, TP 9396,
      published by the Department of Transport; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} its diameter is not reduced by more than

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 7% when elongation of the lay has occurred or a
      strand is becoming buried, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 10% when the lay is uniform.

##### [Metal Fittings Attached to Wire Ropes]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733806 .Subheading}

- **[[309]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-309
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before being used, a metal fitting
  attached to a wire rope by swaging or socketing shall be tested by a
  competent person with a load equal to 200% of the rope's safe working
  load.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A fitting passes the test if it does not break and no
  defects or signs of permanent deformation are detected.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (1) and (2), fittings attached by
  swaging by the same machine to wire rope that is the same type and
  size and is less than 25 mm in diameter may be type tested if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least 10% of the fittings are proof tested by a
    person referred to in paragraph 300(2)(b) with a load equal to 200%
    of the rope's safe working load and the fittings tested do not break
    and no defects or signs of permanent deformation are detected;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one fitting in every one thousand or fewer is
    pulled to destruction and the fitting does not break or fail at less
    than the breaking strength of the rope;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person who performs the proof testing signs a
    certificate that certifies that the fitting passed the test and sets
    out

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the fittings and the rope,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any identifying marks on the fittings,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the manufacturer of the fittings,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the date of the test, the number of fittings
      tested and the total number of fittings being certified,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the load applied and the safe working load,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} their name and

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if they are an employee, the name and address
        of their employer, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if they are not an employee, their address, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} their technical qualifications;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the fittings are marked to identify them to the
    test certificate; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the certificate is kept on board the vessel or at
    the rope owner's premises, as the case may be.

##### [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733825 .Subheading}

- **[[310]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-310 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  lifting appliance, loose gear or main accessory gear shall be used
  unless a certificate was issued under subsection 312(1) or (2) after
  the last time it was tested or thoroughly examined under

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any of sections 303 to 306;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the laws of a state whose flag a foreign vessel is
    entitled to fly if the appliance or gear is being used on the vessel
    and the state is a party to Convention 152; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} regulations, rules or codes of a classification
    society that the Minister has determined would result in an
    equivalent or greater level of safety to that provided for by
    sections 303 to 306.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No loose gear or wire rope shall be used unless an
  expert person inspected it within the preceding three months and
  determined that it is safe to use.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No wire rope shall be used unless it passed its most
  recent thorough examination under section 308.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No wire rope in which any wire is broken shall be
  used unless an expert person inspects it on the day of its intended
  use and determines that it is safe to use.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No wire rope with a metal fitting attached to it by
  swaging or socketing shall be used unless the metal fitting passed the
  test under section 309.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Friction, electro-magnetic and vacuum connectors
  shall not be used to lift any type of material not listed on the
  certificate issued under subsection 312(2) if anyone is within range
  of any material that could fall when the lifting is started.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} No mechanical, electrical, gearing, hydraulic or
  pneumatic system or machine shall be used if it is not in good working
  order.

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if they are not an employee, their address; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the position, qualifications, certification or
    experience that qualifies them as a competent person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If loose gear or main accessory gear passes the test
  under section 305 or the thorough examination under section 306, the

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of loose gear,

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a test of a friction, an

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} their name and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if they are an employee, the name and address of

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the position, qualifications, certification or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} their name and

#### [Safe Working Loads]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733926 .Subheading}

##### [Exceeding Safe Working Loads]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733927 .Subheading}

- **[[318]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-318 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  lifting appliance shall be loaded beyond its safe working load.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The resultant load on main accessory gear or loose
  gear used with a lifting appliance shall not exceed the safe working
  load of the gear.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Single-sheave pulley blocks may be used in any
  position where the maximum resultant load on the head fitting is not
  more than twice the safe working load engraved or stamped on the
  block.

##### [Determining Safe Working Loads]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733932 .Subheading}

###### [Derricks]{.HTitleText5} {#h-733933 .Subheading}

**[[319]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-319 .sectionLabel}** In each case referred
to in subsections 323(2) and (3), the safe working load shall be

plan for the derrick.

###### [Loose Gear --- General]{.HTitleText5} {#h-733935 .Subheading}

- **[[320]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-320 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  safe working load of loose gear shall be determined by an expert
  person testing a prototype of the gear to destruction.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the safe working load of
  specially designed spreader beams, lifting frames and lifting clamps
  shall be determined by an expert person using design calculations.

###### [Slings]{.HTitleText5} {#h-733939 .Subheading}

- **[[321]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-321 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  safe working load of slings that are not used in a straight vertical
  lift configuration or as one-legged slings shall be determined in
  accordance with sections 5.3.1.2 to 5.3.1.6 of Safety and Health in
  Ports.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The safe working load of a synthetic webbing sling
  shall be determined in a manner that takes into account any potential
  loss of strength caused by the stress introduced by the interaction of
  the cargo hook intended to be used with the sling.

###### [Wire Rope]{.HTitleText5} {#h-733943 .Subheading}

**[[322]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-322 .sectionLabel}** The safe working load
of a wire rope is one fifth of the breaking strength of the sample
tested under section 307.

##### [Marking or Indicating Safe Working Loads]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733945 .Subheading}

  clearly marked on it.

  it shall be the safe working load with single purchase only. If two
  safe working loads are marked, the first shall be the safe working
  load with single purchase and the second shall be the safe working
  load with double purchase.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a certificate has been issued under subsection
  312(4) in respect of a derrick tested when operated in union purchase,

###### [Main Accessory Gear]{.HTitleText5} {#h-733951 .Subheading}

shall have its safe working load clearly marked on it.

###### [Pulley Blocks]{.HTitleText5} {#h-733953 .Subheading}

**[[325]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-325 .sectionLabel}** Every pulley block
shall have its safe working load permanently engraved or stamped on it.

###### [Slings]{.HTitleText5} {#h-733955 .Subheading}

**[[326]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-326 .sectionLabel}** The safe working load
of slings in a straight vertical lift configuration shall be indicated
as follows:

  material attached securely to the sling;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of wire-rope slings and reusable natural-
  or synthetic-fibre rope or webbing slings, as specified in
  paragraph (a) or by stating the safe working loads for the various
  sizes of slings used on a notice and exhibiting it so that it can be
  easily read by persons using the slings;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of disposable webbing slings, by marking
  the slings with a "U" or the word "DISPOSABLE" or "JETABLE"; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of disposable stranded rope slings, by
  using marker yarns or other means that identify the slings with their
  certificates issued under subsection 312(2).

#### [Derricks]{.HTitleText3} {#h-733984 .Subheading}

##### [Operation in Union Purchase]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733985 .Subheading}

- **[[329]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-329 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  derrick is marked with a safe working load for operation in union
  purchase under subsection 323(3), a stress diagram or position-setting
  data shall be provided to the operator of the derrick.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If two derricks are operated in union purchase but
  are not marked under subsection 323(3),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the derricks shall both be rigged in single
    purchase or both be rigged in double purchase;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the load lifted shall not be in excess of one-half
    the safe working load of the derricks as rigged; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the angle formed by the cargo runners shall not
    exceed 120°.

**[[330]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-330 .sectionLabel}** If opposite sets of
derricks are rigged for operation in union purchase so that both the
inner and outer derricks are served by common runners with running
pulley blocks on them and the blocks are shackled to the lifting hook,
the safe working load is twice the safe working load with single
purchase of the set of derricks with the smaller safe working load.

##### [Boom Angle]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733993 .Subheading}

**[[331]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-331 .sectionLabel}** If a derrick's boom is
tested at an angle exceeding 15° above the horizontal, the angle at
which it was tested shall be marked on it and the boom shall not be
operated at a lower angle.

##### [Guys]{.HTitleText4} {#h-733995 .Subheading}

- **[[332]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-332 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  derrick with a fixed boom shall, unless the special design of the
  derrick renders preventer guys unnecessary, be fitted with preventer
  guys that are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} secured to the head of the boom and independent of
    other fittings;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} made from wire or chains; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} strong enough to take working stresses without
    assistance from the derrick's fibre guys.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A derrick's guys shall be secured only to eye pads or
  other fittings that are sufficiently strong to withstand the working
  stresses.

##### [Preventing Accidental Lifting]{.HTitleText4} {#h-734002 .Subheading}

**[[333]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-333 .sectionLabel}** No derrick shall be
used unless measures have been taken to prevent the foot of the derrick
from being accidentally lifted out of its socket or support.

#### [Category 4 Lifting Appliances on Canadian Vessels Other Than Restricted Vessels]{.HTitleText3} {#h-734004 .Subheading}

##### [Application]{.HTitleText4} {#h-734005 .Subheading}

**[[334]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-334 .sectionLabel}** Sections 335 to 341
apply in respect of category 4 lifting appliances on Canadian vessels
that are not restricted vessels.

##### [General]{.HTitleText4} {#h-734007 .Subheading}

- **[[335]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-335 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  category 4 lifting appliance shall not be used unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its manufacturer has specified conditions for its
    operation on a vessel, such as

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the maximum heel that may occur in the direction
      of the boom,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the maximum side load, if any, to which it may
      be exposed, expressed in terms of the angle that the cable may
      make normal to the plane of the boom, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which it is to be secured to the
      vessel to ensure the appliance's stability and safety;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is operated in accordance with the specified
    conditions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} when the appliance is operated on a vessel, its
    authorized representative has the manufacturer's load radius chart
    for the safe working load of the appliance;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Department of Transport Marine Safety Office
    nearest to the vessel has been provided with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a drawing that indicates the details of the
      installation, including how and where on the vessel the appliance
      is secured,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} sufficient information to demonstrate that the
      points at which the appliance is secured and the supporting
      structure would be sufficiently strong to withstand the resulting
      loads on the structure if the appliance were used to lift the
      maximum load at the maximum radius at which it was tested under
      subsection 303(1), and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} sufficient stability data to demonstrate that
      the vessel constitutes a safe platform for the appliance when it
      is being used.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a category 4 lifting
  appliance may be used if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its design and its installation on the vessel are
    approved by a classification society in accordance with its
    regulations, rules or codes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} after it is tested under paragraph 303(1)(a), a
    certificate referred to in subsection 312(1) is signed by a surveyor
    employed by the classification society;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is operated in accordance with any conditions
    specified by the classification society in accordance with its
    regulations, rules or codes; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the classification society has provided the
    authorized representative with a load radius chart for the safe
    working load of the appliance when operated on a vessel.

##### [Load Moment Indicator Systems]{.HTitleText4} {#h-734025 .Subheading}

- **[[336]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-336 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  load moment indicator system that meets the requirements set out in
  Schedule 7 shall be installed on a category 4 lifting appliance unless
  it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} handles bulk commodities or cargo by means of a
    magnet or a clamshell bucket; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is used to handle or hold hoses in connection with
    the transfer of bulk liquids or other products handled by hoses.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A manufacturer's manual that contains the
  instructions for the operation and maintenance of the system shall be
  available to the operator of the appliance.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A certificate or document that sets out the following
  and is issued by the installer of the system shall be attached to the
  register referred to in section 311:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the model numbers and serial numbers of the
    appliance and the system; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} confirmation that the system is in good working
    order after installation and that the values indicated on the load
    radius chart for the appliance are correctly indicated by the
    system.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The operator of the appliance and the officer in
  charge of material handling shall have successfully completed training
  in the safe operation of the system and shall each hold a certificate
  or document furnished by his or her employer or the company that
  installed the system attesting to the successful completion of the
  training.

##### [Installation]{.HTitleText4} {#h-734035 .Subheading}

- **[[337]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-337 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  category 4 lifting appliance shall not be used unless it is installed
  by securing it to the vessel with anti-tipping lashings at four points
  in a manner that does not jeopardize the watertight integrity of the
  vessel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On any open sea passage, a category 4 lifting
  appliance shall be lashed and chocked as well as secured to the vessel
  with anti-tipping lashings.

##### [Safe Navigation]{.HTitleText4} {#h-734039 .Subheading}

**[[338]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-338 .sectionLabel}** A category 4 lifting
appliance shall not obstruct the visibility from the navigation bridge
or otherwise impair the safe navigation of the vessel.

##### [Use of Appliances]{.HTitleText4} {#h-734041 .Subheading}

**[[339]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-339 .sectionLabel}** A vessel's master shall
ensure that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a category 4 lifting appliance is not used to lift

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a load that exceeds the maximum load with which it
    was tested in accordance with subsection 6(1) of Schedule 4, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a load at a radius greater than the maximum radius
    at which it was tested in accordance with subsection 6(1) of
    Schedule 4;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} when a category 4 lifting appliance is used, the
  vessel does not heel more than 5° or any lower angle that is set out
  in the conditions specified by the manufacturer or the classification
  society, as the case may be; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if there are tanks containing liquids on the vessel,
  the effects of free surface in the tanks are taken into consideration
  when a category 4 lifting appliance is used and are minimized when it
  is used to lift loads that could cause the vessel to heel more than
  2°.

##### [Placards]{.HTitleText4} {#h-734048 .Subheading}

**[[340]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-340 .sectionLabel}** A durable placard that
sets out the following in clearly legible letters and figures shall be
securely fixed to a category 4 lifting appliance's cab in a location
easily visible to an operator seated at the control position:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the load radius chart for the safe working load of
  the appliance when operated on a vessel;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the appliance's model number and serial number;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the length of the appliance's boom; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any conditions specified by the manufacturer or the
  classification society, as the case may be.

##### [Inspection and Maintenance]{.HTitleText4} {#h-734054 .Subheading}

- **[[341]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-341
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Category 4 lifting appliances shall
  be inspected and maintained in accordance with the manufacturer's
  recommendations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The requirements of clauses 4.1 to 4.3, 4.5 and 4.6
  of the Safety Code on Mobile Cranes shall be met in respect of mobile
  cranes.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "owner" in clauses 4.2.1.1, 4.3.1

    reference to "the authorized representative of the vessel on which
    the crane is installed"; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the wording of clause 4.2.1.2 of the Safety Code on

  a certificate or document furnished by his or her employer or the
  installer of the system attesting to the successful completion of the
  training.

##### [Use of Lifting Appliances]{.HTitleText4} {#h-734076 .Subheading}

**[[344]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-344 .sectionLabel}** The operator of a
category 4 lifting appliance shall not use it to lift

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a load that exceeds the maximum load with which it
  was tested in accordance with subsection 6(2) of Schedule 4; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a load at a radius greater than the maximum radius at
  which it was tested in accordance with subsection 6(2) of Schedule 4.

##### [Placards]{.HTitleText4} {#h-734080 .Subheading}

**[[345]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-345 .sectionLabel}** A durable placard that
sets out the following in clearly legible letters and figures shall be
securely fixed to a category 4 lifting appliance's cab in a location
easily visible to an operator seated at the control position:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the load radius chart for the safe working load of
  the appliance;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the appliance's model number and serial number;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the length of the appliance's boom; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any conditions specified by the manufacturer.

##### [Inspection and Maintenance]{.HTitleText4} {#h-734086 .Subheading}

- **[[346]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-346
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Category 4 lifting appliances shall
  be inspected and maintained in accordance with the manufacturer's
  recommendations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The requirements of clauses 4.1 to 4.3, 4.5 and 4.6
  of the Safety Code on Mobile Cranes shall be met in respect of mobile
  cranes.

#### [Mechanical, Electrical, Gearing, Hydraulic and Pneumatic Systems]{.HTitleText3} {#h-734090 .Subheading}

**[[347]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-347 .sectionLabel}** The mechanical,
electrical, gearing, hydraulic and pneumatic systems that are part of a
lifting appliance shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet design standards at least equivalent to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the lifting appliance is operated on board a
    foreign vessel, design standards approved by the government of the
    state whose flag the vessel is entitled to fly,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the lifting appliance is operated onshore or on
    a restricted vessel, those set out in

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the regulations of the province in which the
      appliance is being operated,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the standards of the International Standards
      Organization, or

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the standards of the Deutsches Institut für
      Normung e. V. (DIN), or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the regulations, rules or
    codes of a classification society; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be suitably protected or constructed for the
  environment in which they are likely to be used.

#### [Rope]{.HTitleText3} {#h-734100 .Subheading}

- **[[348]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-348 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Rope
  shall not be used unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is free from patent defects;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is used in accordance with the manufacturer's
    recommendations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is made of one continuous length;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of wire rope, it meets the requirements
    of sections 4.4.3.1 and 4.4.3.8 of Safety and Health in Ports; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of wire rope that has a splice in it
    and is used in the raising or lowering of a load that could rotate,
    the splice prevents the lay of the wire from opening.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Wire clips shall not be used to form a terminal joint
  in a wire rope unless the rope is used in standing gear and the clips
  are fitted in accordance with their manufacturer's recommendations.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Wire rope shall not be used on a category 1 lifting
  appliance, a mobile crane on a vessel or a category 5 lifting
  appliance installed on a vessel if the rope is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} anti-rotation or torqueless left-handed laid wire
    rope of 4 × 29 construction with strands composed of one core wire,
    seven inner wires, seven filler wires and 14 outer wires, if the
    core wire or the inner wires have a greater diameter than that of
    the outer wires; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} of essentially the same design as the rope
    described in paragraph (a).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Rope made of fibre interspersed with wire strand
  shall not be used on the main hoist of a lifting appliance.

#### [Thread Diameter of Sheaves]{.HTitleText3} {#h-734113 .Subheading}

- **[[349]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-349 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  wire rope is used on a lifting appliance referred to in column 1 of
  Schedule 8, the thread diameter of a sheave that is used with the rope
  shall be not less than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the diameter set out in column 2 if the rope is
    used as a running rope; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the diameter set out in column 3 if the rope is
    used as a fixed span rope.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), if the manufacturer of the
  rope has recommended a thread diameter of a sheave that is greater
  than that required by subsection (1), the thread diameter of the
  sheave shall be not less than that recommended by the manufacturer.

#### [Pallets]{.HTitleText3} {#h-734119 .Subheading}

- **[[350]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-350
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Pallets shall be of adequate
  strength for the use for which they are proposed and free from visible
  defects that would likely affect their safe use.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Pallets that are used in conjunction with bar bridles
  shall have a lip of at least 75 mm.

#### [Mobile Elevating Work Platforms]{.HTitleText3} {#h-734123 .Subheading}

**[[351]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-351 .sectionLabel}** Mobile elevating work
platforms that are not lifting appliances shall meet the requirements of
any regulations of the province in which they are being operated.

#### [Forklift Trucks]{.HTitleText3} {#h-734125 .Subheading}

- **[[352]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-352 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  lifting capacity of a forklift truck that is specified by the
  manufacturer in terms of mass and distance from the mast

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall be marked on the truck in a readily visible
    location; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall not be increased by adding counterweights or
    by any other means.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The mass of the truck and of any counterweights, as
  well as their total mass, shall be marked on a forklift truck in a
  readily visible location.

#### [Wrought Iron Loose Gear]{.HTitleText3} {#h-734131 .Subheading}

**[[353]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-353 .sectionLabel}** Loose gear made of
wrought iron shall not be used.

#### [Repairs]{.HTitleText3} {#h-734133 .Subheading}

- **[[354]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-354
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Repairs that involve the heating,
  fairing or welding of structural stress-bearing parts of lifting
  appliances and repairs to loose gear shall be performed by an expert
  person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Webbing slings shall not be repaired.

#### [Structural and Control Safety Measures]{.HTitleText3} {#h-734137 .Subheading}

##### [Eliminating the Possibility of Dangerous Contact]{.HTitleText4} {#h-734138 .Subheading}

- **[[355]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-355
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Chain and friction drives, cog
  wheels, electric conductors, gearing, motors, shafting, sheaves and
  steam pipes that are part of cargo gear shall be covered, shielded,
  fenced, enclosed or otherwise protected by covers or casings,
  barriers, guardrails, screens, mats or platforms to eliminate the
  possibility of dangerous contact by persons or objects.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of parts
  that are, by their position or construction, as safe as if they were
  protected as required by that subsection.

##### [Reducing Risk of Accidental Descent]{.HTitleText4} {#h-734142 .Subheading}

- **[[356]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-356
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Cranes and winches shall be provided
  with means that minimize the risk of the accidental raising or
  lowering of a load.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lever that is used to raise or lower the load
    shall

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} raise the load when moved back or towards the
      operator,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} lower the load when moved forward or away from
      the operator, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} return to the neutral position when released by
      an operator;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a lever that controls the link motion reversing
    gear of a crane or winch shall be provided with a spring or other
    locking arrangement; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the motion of the slew control and the resultant
    direction of the slew shall be consistent.

##### [Use of Lifting Appliances with Limit Switches]{.HTitleText4} {#h-734152 .Subheading}

- **[[357]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-357 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  limit switches are provided on a lifting appliance, the appliance
  shall not be used unless the switches are in good working order.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Limit switches shall not be left in the by-pass
  condition when the appliance is in operation.

##### [Equipment for Lifting Appliances]{.HTitleText4} {#h-734156 .Subheading}

- **[[358]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-358
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Lifting appliances shall have an
  emergency stop button at the operator's position.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A lifting appliance's machinery shall have a means
  for promptly cutting off its power in the event of an emergency.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A category 2 lifting appliance shall have

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an adjustable automatic slow-down device on the
    lowering function; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an indicator specifying the overrun to be expected
    under normal working conditions.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If wind speed is a limiting condition for the use of
  a lifting appliance, the appliance shall have an anemometer that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is located on a part of the appliance that is
    exposed to the wind; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a read-out in the operator's cab or, in the
    case of a self-unloading vessel, on the bridge or in the control
    room of the vessel.

##### [Control of Steam]{.HTitleText4} {#h-734166 .Subheading}

**[[359]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-359 .sectionLabel}** If workers are present,
exhaust steam and, so far as is feasible, live steam from a crane or
winch shall not obscure any part of the decks, gangways, stages or
wharves.

##### [Report of Accident or Incident]{.HTitleText4} {#h-734168 .Subheading}

- **[[360]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-360 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  serious accident or incident occurs involving a lifting appliance that
  is part of a vessel's equipment, the vessel's master shall immediately
  report the accident or incident to the Department of Transport Marine
  Safety Office nearest to the accident or incident by the quickest
  means available.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a serious accident or incident occurs involving a
  lifting appliance that is not part of a vessel's equipment, the
  appliance's owner shall immediately report the accident or incident to
  the Department of Transport Marine Safety Office nearest to the
  accident or incident by the quickest means available.

#### [Unitized Cargo]{.HTitleText3} {#h-734172 .Subheading}

- **[[361]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-361
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unitized cargo shall not be lifted
  by using the material that unitizes the cargo unless the material is
  wire banding or flat steel strapping.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The mass of unitized cargo lifted by using wire
  banding or flat steel strapping that unitizes the cargo shall not
  exceed the mass determined by the formula A/B

  ::: {#734176 .Subsection}
  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the breaking strength of the banding or strapping; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the minimum safety factor required by subsection (3) or
      paragraph (4)(b).
  :::

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to paragraph (4)(b),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the banding or strapping is for a single lift,
    the minimum safety factor shall be 3.5; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the banding or strapping is for multiple lifts,
    the minimum safety factor shall be 4.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the banding or strapping is not designed to be
  vertical during lifting,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the banding or strapping shall provide for a bridle
    angle of at least 40°; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the minimum safety factor shall be obtained by the
    formula A × 1/2B

    ::: {#734185 .Paragraph}
    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is the minimum safety factor required under subsection (3); and

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is the secant of the maximum bridle angle during lifting.
    :::

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (4), [[bridle
  angle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the maximum angle between the
  vertical at one of the top corners of the unitized cargo and the
  banding or strapping during lifting.

<!-- -->

- **[[362]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-362 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  section applies in respect of wire banding or flat steel strapping
  that unitizes cargo when the unitized cargo is lifted using the
  banding or strapping.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the banding or strapping is for a single lift, it
  shall not be used to lift the unitized cargo more than once.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the banding or strapping is for multiple lifts, it
  shall not be used

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to lift the unitized cargo more than four times; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} more than four months after the first time it is
    used.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The banding or strapping shall not be used to lift
  the unitized cargo for more than four minutes at a time or any longer
  period that is necessary to complete the lift.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The banding or strapping shall be used to lift the
  unitized cargo only in accordance with the lifting method indicated on
  the certificate under paragraph (8)(i).

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The knot strength of wire banding shall be at least
  90% of the breaking strength of the wires.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The banding or strapping shall be accompanied by a
  certificate that is signed by the person responsible for unitizing the
  cargo, that certifies the breaking strength and the safety factor of
  the banding or strapping and that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of banding, the strength of joints was
    tested at least once for every 1 000 tonnes of cargo unitized and at
    least three times for every consignment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of strapping, the strength of joints
    was tested at least once for every 300 units; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of multiple-use banding or strapping,
    the banding or strapping meets the requirements of section 30 of
    Unitized Cargo Standard, TP 11232, published by the Department of
    Transport, or equivalent requirements.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The certificate shall also set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the material used to unitize the cargo is
    wire banding or flat steel strapping;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the banding or strapping is single-lift or
    multiple-lift;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the plant, warehouse or other location at which the
    banding or strapping was fitted on the cargo;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any identifying marks on the banding or strapping;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the cargo;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number of units of cargo that the certificate
    covers;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the approximate mass of the units;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the unitizing system used;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the lifting method that should be used, including
    the number and type of hooks or other lifting devices and the bridle
    angle, if any;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} in the case of banding,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of bands fitted on each unit and how
      many times each band is wrapped around a unit,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the diameter and breaking strength of the wires
      in the banding, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the type of knot used to tie the banding and
      the breaking strength of the knot;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} in the case of strapping,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of straps fitted on each unit,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the width, thickness and breaking strength of
      the straps, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the type and number of seals and their location
      and breaking strength; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} if the banding or strapping has been used to lift
    unitized cargo, the date of the lift and the name of the vessel and
    of the port, if any, involved.

### [DIVISION 2]{.HLabel2}[Access Equipment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-734221 .Subheading}

#### [Shore-based Power-operated Ramps]{.HTitleText3} {#h-734222 .Subheading}

- **[[363]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-363 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  owner of a shore-based power-operated ramp that is constructed one
  year or more after the coming into force of this section shall ensure
  that the requirements of CAN/CSA Standard S826.1-01, Ferry Boarding
  Facilities, other than Appendices A to D, that apply in respect of the
  ramp are met.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner, as defined in CAN/CSA Standards S826.3-01
  and S826.4-01, Ferry Boarding Facilities, of a shore-based
  power-operated ramp shall ensure that it is maintained and inspected
  in accordance with the requirements of those Standards.

#### [Accommodation Ladders]{.HTitleText3} {#h-734226 .Subheading}

- **[[364]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-364 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  authorized representative of a vessel shall ensure that every
  accommodation ladder fitted on the vessel and the vessel's support and
  suspension points for the ladder meet

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the requirements of ISO Standard 5488, Shipbuilding
    --- Accommodation Ladders or of a classification society; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the ladder is part of pilot transfer equipment
    referred to in regulation 23 of Chapter V of SOLAS, the requirements
    of the annex to IMO Resolution A.889(21), Pilot Transfer
    Arrangements.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every accommodation ladder fitted on the vessel and
  the vessel's support and suspension points for the ladder shall be
  thoroughly examined by a competent person at least once every year.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An accommodation ladder passes the examination if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} certificates have been issued under section 312 for
    the loose gear used with the ladder;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the parts that align and swivel under load are
    free;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the mechanical, electrical. gearing, hydraulic and
    pneumatic systems are in good working order;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} parts are not affected by corrosion to the extent
    that they cannot be opened; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} no defects or signs of permanent deformation are
    detected.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The vessel's support and suspension points for the
  ladder pass the examination if no defects or signs of permanent
  deformation are detected.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The ladder shall not be used if it and the vessel's
  support and suspension points for it did not pass the most recent
  examination.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The competent person shall provide the authorized
  representative with a record of the examination, including
  measurements taken at intervals not exceeding five years of any
  corrosion, deformation or significant wear in structural or moving
  parts.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The record shall be kept on board the vessel.

#### [Report of Accident or Incident]{.HTitleText3} {#h-734242 .Subheading}

- **[[365]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-365 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  serious accident or incident occurs involving a shore-based
  power-operated ramp, the owner, as defined in CAN/CSA Standards
  S826.3-01 and S826.4-01, Ferry Boarding Facilities, of the ramp shall
  immediately report the accident or incident to the Department of
  Transport Marine Safety Office nearest to the accident or incident by
  the quickest means available.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a serious accident or incident occurs involving an
  accommodation ladder fitted on a vessel, the vessel's master shall
  immediately report the accident or incident to the Department of
  Transport Marine Safety Office nearest to the accident or incident by
  the quickest means available.

## [SCHEDULE 4]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsections 303(1) and (2), paragraph 339(a) and section 344)]{.OriginatingRef}[Testing of Lifting Appliances]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-734289 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- - **1** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A category 1 lifting appliance, category 2
    lifting appliance or category 3 lifting appliance with an SWL of 20
    tonnes or less shall be tested with a load equal to 125% of its SWL.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} A category 1 lifting appliance, category 2 lifting
    appliance or category 3 lifting appliance with an SWL of more than
    20 tonnes but not more than 50 tonnes shall be tested with a load
    equal to its SWL plus 5 tonnes.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} A category 1 lifting appliance, category 2 lifting
    appliance or category 3 lifting appliance with an SWL of more than
    50 tonnes shall be tested with a load equal to 110% of its SWL.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} A category 5 lifting appliance shall be tested
    assembled with its accessory gear with a load equal to 110% of the
    working load of the assembled appliance.

- **2** During the testing of a category 1 lifting appliance, category 2
  lifting appliance or category 3 lifting appliance,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the load shall be lifted with the lifting appliance
    at an angle that is not more than 15° from the horizontal or at the
    lowest feasible working angle; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the load shall be swung as far as possible in both
    directions after it has been lifted.

- **3** A lifting appliance, other than a mobile crane, with a jib that
  has a variable radius shall be tested with the load at the maximum and
  minimum radii of the jib.

- **4** A lifting appliance designed to articulate, telescope and
  elevate under full-load conditions shall be tested with the load over
  its full operational range.

- - **5** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the test shall be
    carried out using a dead load.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} A spring or hydraulic balance may be used for the
    test instead of a dead load if

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the appliance is not rigged for union purchase
      and is being tested after the renewal, modification or repair of a
      stress-bearing part other than a part of the brake system;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a dead load is not available for the test;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the balance was calibrated by a competent person
      not more than 12 months before the test; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the indicator on the balance remains constant for
      at least five minutes during the test.

- - **6** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A category 4 lifting appliance that is to be
    used on a vessel that is not a restricted vessel shall be tested

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the vessel or onshore

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at its maximum and minimum radii, and at one or
        more of its intermediate radii, with a load equal to its SWL on
        a vessel at those radii, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at its maximum and minimum projected radii
        while on board the vessel with a load less than or equal to its
        SWL on a vessel at those radii; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on completion of installation on a vessel, over
      its full operational range on the vessel at at least the maximum
      radius at which it was tested under paragraph (a) with at least
      the load with which it was tested under that paragraph.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} A category 4 lifting appliance that is to be used
    onshore or on a restricted vessel shall be tested

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} over its full operational range at its maximum
      radius at base-boom length with at least 100% of the maximum
      weight permitted by the manufacturer's load rating chart at that
      radius; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} over its full operational range

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at its maximum and minimum radii, and at one or
        more of its intermediate radii, with a load equal to its SWL at
        those radii, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at its maximum and minimum projected radii
        with a load less than or equal to its SWL at those radii.

## [SCHEDULE 7]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsections 336(1) and 343(1))]{.OriginatingRef}[Load Moment Indicator Systems]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-734336 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** A load moment indicator system installed on a category 4 lifting
  appliance shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} use the same type of measuring units that the load
    radius chart uses;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} clearly indicate the measuring units used;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} give a clear and precise indication of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the lifting capacity of the appliance,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the total weight suspended,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the elevated angle of the boom, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the radius of the boom;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be clearly visible so that an operator seated at
    the control position can easily read the indications during both day
    and night conditions;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be resistant to magnetic influence, weather, shock
    and vibration;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} have a visual system that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} warns the operator when 85% of the appliance's
      SWL has been reached, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} does not create an operational hazard; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} have a visual and audible system in the interior of
    the cab and an exterior audible system that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} warns the operator when 98% of the appliance's
      SWL has been reached,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} does not create an operational hazard, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the audible signal, is
      identifiable by the operator despite ambient and machinery noise.

- **2** A load moment indicator system that is installed on a category 4
  lifting appliance on a Canadian vessel that is not a restricted vessel
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} give a clear and precise indication of the angles
    of heel and trim and of the reduced lifting capacity for those
    angles;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be clearly visible so that an operator seated at
    the control position can easily read the indication during both day
    and night conditions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} have an automatic emergency braking system that
    prevents freefall of the load without jolting in the event of
    overload; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} have a manual control to bypass the automatic
    emergency braking system that is accessible to an operator seated at
    the control position and is protected against accidental operation.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- SOR/2021-60, s. 11

  - - [11]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any document that was issued by
      the Minister of Transport to a shipper following an initial
      verification of the shipper's procedures for sampling, testing and
      controlling the moisture content of cargo before the day on which
      these Regulations come into force is deemed to be a letter of
      approval issued under paragraph 115.1(4)(a) of the [Cargo,
      Fumigation and Tackle Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-128).

    - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any document that was issued by the Minister of
      Transport to a shipper following a verification of modifications
      to the shipper's procedures for sampling, testing and controlling
      the moisture content of cargo before the day on which these
      Regulations come into force is deemed to be a letter of approval
      issued under paragraph 115.1(4)(a) of the [Cargo, Fumigation and
      Tackle Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-128).

    - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any document that was endorsed by the Minister of
      Transport following an intermediate verification of the shipper's
      procedures for sampling, testing and controlling the moisture
      content of cargo before the day on which these Regulations come
      into force is deemed to be a letter of approval endorsed under
      paragraph 115.1(4)(b) of the [Cargo, Fumigation and Tackle
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-128).

    - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any document that was issued by the Minister of
      Transport to a shipper following a renewal verification of the
      shipper's procedures for sampling, testing and controlling the
      moisture content of cargo before the day on which these
      Regulations come into force is deemed to be a letter of approval
      issued under paragraph 115.1(4)(c) of the [Cargo, Fumigation and
      Tackle Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-128).
