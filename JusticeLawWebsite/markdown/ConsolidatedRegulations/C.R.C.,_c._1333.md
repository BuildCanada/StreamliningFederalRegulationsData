## Application to Canadian Penitentiary Service Regulations {#application-to-canadian-penitentiary-service-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1333
:::

[CORRECTIONS AND CONDITIONAL RELEASE ACT](/eng/acts/C-44.6)

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33)

Regulations Applying the Public Service Employment Act to Officers of
the Canadian Penitentiary Service
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503839 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Application to Canadian Penitentiary Service
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1333).

## [Application of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503841 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding Part I of
the [Corrections and Conditional Release Act](/eng/acts/C-44.6), the
provisions of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)
apply to all members of the Correctional Service of Canada including
those known as Deputy Commissioner, Directors of Divisions, Regional
Directors and all other members of the Correctional Service of Canada
except the Commissioner of Penitentiaries.

- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (2nd Supp.), ss. 15, 18
