## Villeneuve Airport Zoning Regulations {#villeneuve-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-568
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1981-07-10

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Villeneuve Airport

P.C. 1981-1893 1981-07-09
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 6 of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to approve the annexed
[Regulations respecting Zoning at Villeneuve
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-568) made by the Minister of Transport.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871516 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Villeneuve Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-568).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871518 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Villeneuve Airport, in
      township 54, range 27, west of the Fourth Meridian, in the
      Province of Alberta; ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part III of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport including the runway prepared for the
      take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction, which
      strip is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule;
      ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part VI of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the assigned
  elevation of the airport reference point is 2,227 feet above sea
  level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871529 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, that are within the outer limits described in
Part II of the schedule.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871531 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point, any

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} approach surface;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} transitional surface.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871536 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in section 4, the Minister may make a
direction that the owner or occupier of the land on which that object is
growing remove the excessive growth.

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871538 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit that land or any Part
of it to be used for the disposal or accumulation of any waste, material
or substance edible by or attractive to birds.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point is a point commencing at the end of the
centre line of runway 26L; THENCE westerly along the said centre line of
runway 08R-26L for a distance of 1,752 feet, THENCE southerly and
perpendicular to the said centre line of runway 08R-26L a distance of
2,050 feet to a point being the airport reference point.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Department of Public Works Plan
No. E1455 dated June 21, 1978, are surfaces abutting each end of the
strips associated with the runways designated 08L-26R, 08R-26L, 16L-34R
and 16R-34L and are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 08L,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 26R,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 08R,

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting each end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 26L,

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting each end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 16L,

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting each end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 34R,

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting each end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 16R, and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting each end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 34L,

consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio of one foot measured
vertically to 50 feet measured horizontally rising to an imaginary
horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected centre line of
the strip, 200 feet measured vertically above the elevation at the end
of the strip, except at the end of runway approach 26L where 600 feet is
measured vertically above the elevation at the end of the strip and
10,000 feet measured horizontally from the end of the strip, the outer
ends of each imaginary horizontal line being 1,250 feet from the
projected centre line.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Department of Public Works Plan No. E1455
dated June 21, 1978, is an imaginary surface located at a common plane
established at a constant elevation of 150 feet above the assigned
elevation of the airport reference point, except where that common plane
is less than 30 feet above the surface of the ground, the imaginary
surface is located at 30 feet above the surface of the ground.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strips]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-the-strips .SchedHeadL1}

The strips, shown on Department of Public Works Plan No. E1455 dated
June 21, 1978, are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 08L-26R is 500 feet
  in width, 250 feet being on each side of the centre line of the runway
  and 4,400 feet in length, 200 feet extending beyond each end of the
  runway;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 08R-26L is 500 feet
  in width, 250 feet being on each side of the centre line of the runway
  and 4,300 feet in length, 200 feet extending beyond the end of runway
  approach 08R and 600 feet extending beyond the end of runway
  approach 28L;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 16L-34R is 500 feet
  in width, 250 feet being on each side of the centre line of the runway
  and 3,900 feet in length, 200 feet extending beyond each end of the
  runway; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 16R-34L is 500 feet
  in width, 250 feet being on each side of the centre line of the runway
  and 4,400 feet in length, 200 feet extending beyond each end of the
  runway.

### [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-videscription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Department of Public Works Plan
No. E1455 dated June 21, 1978, is a surface consisting of an inclined
plane having a ratio of one foot measured vertically to seven feet
measured horizontally at right angles to the centre line and centre line
produced of each strip and extending upward from the lateral limits of
each strip and its approach surface to an intersection with the outer
surface or another transitional surface of an adjoining strip.
