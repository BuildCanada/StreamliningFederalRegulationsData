## Blainville Motor Vehicle Test Centre Benefit Eligibility Protection Regulations {#blainville-motor-vehicle-test-centre-benefit-eligibility-protection-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/99-4
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

[PUBLIC SERVICE SUPERANNUATION ACT](/eng/acts/P-36)

Registration 1998-12-14

Blainville Motor Vehicle Test Centre Benefit Eligibility Protection
Regulations

T.B. 826779 1998-12-10
::::

::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Treasury Board, on the recommendation of the President of the
Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 42.1(1)(u)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-99-4_e_hq_4094){#fn_SOR-99-4_e_hq_4094-ID0EHBAA}
of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and paragraph
7(2)(a) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby
makes the annexed [Blainville Motor Vehicle Test Centre Benefit
Eligibility Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-4).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032019 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[new employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[new employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means PMG Technologies Inc. to
    whom Her Majesty in right of Canada transferred the administration
    of the Blainville Motor Vehicle Test Centre on June 15, 1996.
    ([nouvel employeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032023 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
a person who, by reason of the transfer of the Blainville Motor Vehicle
Test Centre by her Majesty in right of Canada to the new employer,
ceased to be employed in the public service and became employed by the
new employer and exercises an option under section 3.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 67(E)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 2"){.PITLink}
:::

## [Applicable Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032027 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A person may, within one
year after the date of the coming into force of these Regulations,
exercise an option to have the period that begins on the date on which
the person ceased to be employed in the public service and that ends on
the date the person ceases to be employed by the new employer counted
for determining eligibility for an annuity or other benefit under the
Act, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on June 14, 1996 the person was a contributor under
  the Act and ceased to be employed in the public service to become
  employed by the new employer; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} has not received any annuity or other benefit under
  section 12, 13 or 13.01 of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 67(E)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An option exercised
pursuant to section 3 is irrevocable and must be exercised in writing,
dated and signed by the person, and sent to the Minister.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Sections 12, 13 and 13.01
of the Act only apply to a person on and after the date on which that
person ceases to be employed by the new employer.

## [Surviving Spouse and Children]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032035 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 26(1) of the Act, a surviving spouse of a person who became
the spouse of that person during the period that begins on the date on
which the person ceased to be employed in the public service and that
ends on the date on which the person ceases to be employed by the new
employer is entitled to an allowance under Part I of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 67(E)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-6-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 6"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 26(2) of the Act, a child who was born to or adopted by a
person or became the stepchild of the person during the period that
begins on the date on which the person ceased to be employed in the
public service and that ends on the date on which the person ceases to
be employed by the new employer is entitled to an allowance under Part I
of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 67(E)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-7-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 7"){.PITLink}
:::

## [Adaptation of Subsection 10(5) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032042 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 10(5) of the Act, the one year period referred to in
paragraph 10(5)(a) of the Act shall begin on the date on which the
person ceases to be employed by the new employer.

## [Adaptation of Sections 12 and 13 of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032044 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 and 13 of the Act, pensionable service includes the period
of service with the new employer that begins on the date on which the
person ceased to be employed in the public service and that ends on the
date on which the person ceases to be employed by the new employer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 67(E)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-9-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 9"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 and 13 of the Act, the age of a person when the person
ceased to be employed in the public service is the age of the person on
the date on which the person ceases to be employed by the new employer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 67(E)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-10-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 10"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1 .sectionLabel}** A contributor to whom
these Regulations apply is considered to be a Group 1 contributor.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 66
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032054 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
