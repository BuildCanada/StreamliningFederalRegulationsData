## DHC Shares Sale Regulations {#dhc-shares-sale-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-61
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1985-12-20

Regulations Respecting the Sale of Shares of The de Havilland Aircraft
of Canada, Limited

P.C. 1985-3760  1985-12-20
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Regional Industrial Expansion and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to section 34[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-86-61_e_hq_6041){#fn_SOR-86-61_e_hq_6041-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the sale of shares of The de
Havilland Aircraft of Canada, Limited](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-61).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-86-61_e_hq_6041-ID0EFBAA){#fn_SOR-86-61_e_hq_6041}S.C.
  1984, c. 31, s. 7
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897628 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [DHC Shares Sale Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-61).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897630 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[CDIC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CDIC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Development Investment
    Corporation; ([CDIC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[DHC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[DHC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The de Havilland Aircraft of
    Canada, Limited. ([DHC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-124, s. 1
:::

## [Authority]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897636 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Regional
Industrial Expansion on behalf of Her Majesty in right of Canada may
become a party to the Acquisition Agreement with The Boeing Company
regarding the sale of 31,999 Class A shares and 10,000 Class B common
shares of DHC to The Boeing Company, including all amendments thereto,
and may assume all liability of CDIC thereunder in respect of any
obligation assumed towards The Boeing Company.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-124, s. 2
:::
