## Canadian Transportation Agency Designated Provisions Regulations {#canadian-transportation-agency-designated-provisions-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/99-244
:::

[CANADA TRANSPORTATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10.4)

Registration 1999-06-11

Canadian Transportation Agency Designated Provisions Regulations

P.C. 1999-1059  1999-06-10
::::

:::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to subsection 36(1) of the [Canada
Transportation Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR_99-244_EN_hq_5305){#fn_SOR_99-244_EN_hq_5305-ID0EDBAA},
hereby approves the [Canadian Transportation Agency Designated
Provisions Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-244), made by the
Canadian Transportation Agency.

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1029590 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The provisions,
requirements and conditions set out in column 1 of the schedule are
designated for the purposes of subsection 177(1) of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-71, s. 2
- [SOR/2019-176, s. 21](#1135823-1189535){.wb-lbx

## [Maximum Amount]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1029594 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The maximum amount payable
in respect of a contravention of a provision, requirement or condition
set out in column 1 of the schedule is the amount

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a corporation, set out in column 2; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an individual, set out in column 3.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-71, s. 3(E)

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1029600 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
