## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781802 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-181, s. 112\]]{.Repealed}

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-181, s. 112\]]{.Repealed}

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a retail association, as defined in section 2
        of the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);
        or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a company, as defined in section 2 of the
        [Trust and Loan Companies
        Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8). ([institution]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[point of service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[point of service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a physical location
      to which the public has access and at which an institution
      provides registered products through a natural person in
      Canada. ([point de service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[registered
  product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 385.131 of the
  [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01), section
  434.1 of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) and
  these Regulations, [[registered product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
  registered education savings plan, a registered retirement savings
  plan, a registered retirement income fund, a registered disability
  savings plan or any other plan, arrangement or fund to which Division
  G of Part I of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) applies that is
  provided by an institution to a natural person.

## [Manner of Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781814 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clear and simple language

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  information that is required to be provided by an institution under
  subsection 385.131(1) of the [Cooperative Credit Associations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01), subsection 434.1(1) of the [Trust and Loan
  Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) or these Regulations must be
  disclosed in language, and presented in a manner, that is clear,
  simple and not misleading.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of providing information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information must be provided in writing and
  orally, subject to the following exceptions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an account that is opened by
    telephone, or an agreement that is entered into by telephone, the
    institution need only provide the information orally before opening
    the account or entering into the agreement; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an account that is opened by
    electronic means or by mail, or an agreement that is entered into by
    electronic means or by mail, the institution need only provide the
    information in writing before opening the account or entering into
    the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accounts opened by telephone

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An institution that only provides information orally
  in accordance with paragraph (2)(a) must provide the same information
  in writing after opening the account or entering into the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accounts opened by electronic means

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An institution that only provides information in
  writing in accordance with paragraph (2)(b) must, before opening the
  account or entering into the agreement, provide the telephone number
  of a person who is knowledgeable about the terms and conditions of the
  registered product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of the disclosure

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An institution that provides the information in
  writing referred to in subsections (2) to (4) by mail is considered to
  have provided that information to the individual on the fifth business
  day after the postmark date.

## [Circumstances Under Which Information Need Not Be Provided]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781828 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  institution is not required to provide the information referred to in
  subsection 385.131(1) of the [Cooperative Credit Associations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01) or subsection 434.1(1) of the [Trust and Loan
  Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the account to be opened, or the product or service
    in respect of which an agreement is to be entered into, will form
    part of a customer's existing registered product; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the customer has been previously advised in writing
    of all charges in respect of the registered product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Trust and loan companies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company, as defined in section 2 of the [Trust and
  Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), is not required to provide the
  information referred to in subsection 434.1(1) of that Act or section
  4 if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the account to be opened is being opened by the
    company solely for the purpose of acting as trustee in respect of
    the registered product; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the agreement to be entered into is an agreement
    that the company is entering into solely for the purpose of acting
    as trustee in respect of the registered product.

## [Additional Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781838 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of amendments

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Before amending any terms
and conditions in respect of a registered product, the institution must
disclose the proposed amendment in writing to the person to whom the
product was provided.

## [List of Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781841 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Branches and websites

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  institution must maintain a list of the charges applicable to
  registered products at each of its branches and points of service
  where registered products are offered in Canada and on each of its
  websites through which registered products are offered in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Making list available

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An institution must, on request, make the list
  available to its customers and to the public for inspection during
  business hours at each of the branches and points of service referred
  to in subsection (1).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781847 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}August 1, 2011

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on November 1, 2011.
