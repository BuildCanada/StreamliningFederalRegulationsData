## Order Designating the Commission of Inquiry into the Deployment of Canadian Forces to Somalia as a Department and the Prime Minister as Appropriate Minister {#order-designating-the-commission-of-inquiry-into-the-deployment-of-canadian-forces-to-somalia-as-a-department-and-the-prime-minister-as-appropriate-minister .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/95-47
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1995-04-19

Order Designating the Commission of Inquiry into the Deployment of
Canadian Forces to Somalia as a Department and the Prime Minister as
Appropriate Minister

P.C. 1995-529  1995-03-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-95-47_e_hq_6607){#fn_SI-95-47_e_hq_6607-ID0EFBBAA}
  of the definition [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of
  the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to designate the
  Commission of Inquiry into the Deployment of Canadian Forces to
  Somalia as a department for the purposes of that Act; and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-95-47_e_hq_6607-ID0EFBBAA){#fn_SI-95-47_e_hq_6607}S.C.
    1992, c. 1, s. 69
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-95-47_e_hq_6607){#fn_SI-95-47_e_hq_6607-ID0EFAAAA}
  of the definition [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
  section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to
  designate the Prime Minister as the appropriate Minister with respect
  to the Commission of Inquiry into the deployment of Canadian Forces to
  Somalia.
:::
