## Nova BUS Corporation Exemption Order {#nova-bus-corporation-exemption-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/96-446
:::

[MOTOR VEHICLE SAFETY ACT](/eng/acts/M-10.01)

Registration 1996-09-17

Nova BUS Corporation Exemption Order

P.C. 1996-1455 1996-09-17
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to paragraph 9(1)(c) of the [Motor
Vehicle Safety Act](/eng/acts/M-10.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Nova BUS Corporation Exemption
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-446), being of the opinion that
conformity with certain prescribed standards would impede the
development of new kinds of buses.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote

  16
  :::
:::

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000955 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Six hundred and fifty units
of Nova LFS low-floor model buses, manufactured by Nova BUS Corporation,
are exempt from the requirements in subsections 217(11), (14), (17) and
(19) of Schedule IV to the [Motor Vehicle Safety
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1038) that are applicable to
push-out windows, for a period of two years beginning on the day on
which this Order is registered under the [Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000957 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on September 17, 1996.
