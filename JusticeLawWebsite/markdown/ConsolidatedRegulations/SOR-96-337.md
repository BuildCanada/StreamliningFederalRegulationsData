## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985646 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Transportation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who makes an
    application to the Agency for a certificate of fitness or for the
    variation of a certificate. ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[deductible]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deductible]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount of risk for
    which an applicant retains financial responsibility under an
    insurance contract. ([franchise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insurer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an insurance company that
    provides third party liability insurance coverage to an applicant
    and includes an insurance company that is wholly owned by the
    parties it insures. ([assureur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[named perils pollution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[named perils pollution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means risks that are
    set out in an insurance contract that are associated with seepage or
    pollution or contamination resulting from but not limited to
    collision, overturning, derailment, upset, hostile fire, lightning
    or explosion or other railway related accidents. ([risques de
    pollution désignés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[self-insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[self-insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means self-insured retention
    and deductible. ([autoassurance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[self-insured retention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[self-insured retention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount of
    risk for which an applicant takes financial responsibility, outside
    of an insurance contract. ([affectation pour
    autoassurance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[third party liability insurance coverage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[third party liability insurance coverage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means financial compensation provided for in a contract entered into
    between an applicant and an insurer, or in the case of
    self-insurance, financial compensation provided by the applicant, in
    respect of the following matters arising out of an applicant's
    proposed construction or operation of a railway, including a
    proposed temporary construction or operation of a railway resulting
    from unforeseen or exceptional circumstances:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} third party bodily injury or death, including
      injury or death to passengers,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} third party property damage, excluding damage to
      cargo, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} named perils pollution. ([assurance
      responsabilité civile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985659 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Sections 3 to 5 of these
Regulations apply only to persons proposing to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} construct a railway; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} operate a railway that relates to a passenger rail
  service.

## [Determination of Adequate Third Party Liability Insurance Coverage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985663 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Third party liability
insurance coverage is adequate if there is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sufficient insurance, including self-insurance, to
  compensate for the following matters that may arise out of an
  applicant's proposed construction or operation of a railway, including
  a proposed temporary construction or operation of a railway resulting
  from unforeseen or exceptional circumstances:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} third party bodily injury or death, including
    injury or death to passengers,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} third party property damage, excluding damage to
    cargo, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} named perils pollution;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written confirmation provided to the Agency by the
  applicant that the applicant has fully disclosed to the insurer the
  nature and extent of the proposed construction or operation of the
  railway and any associated third party liability risks; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} full disclosure to the Agency by the applicant of the
  amount of self-insured retention and of the third party liability
  risks that may arise from the proposed construction or operation of
  the railway.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** In determining whether
third party liability insurance coverage is adequate, the Agency shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine the risks associated with the proposed
  construction or operation of the railway by considering information
  that is provided by the applicant, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} passenger ridership,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} passenger and freight train miles,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} volume of railway traffic,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} class and volume of dangerous goods transported by
    rail,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} types of population areas served,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} number of level crossings,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} speed of trains,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} train crew training,

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} method of train control, and

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} overall safety record of the applicant; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of self-insurance, assess the financial
  capability of the applicant to sustain the level of self-insurance, on
  the basis of the following information provided by the applicant:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the three most recent annual financial reports that
    have been filed with the Agency pursuant to section 344 of the
    Railway Act, as that section read immediately before the coming into
    force of the Act, or with the Minister of Transport in accordance
    with any applicable regulations made pursuant to section 50 of the
    Act,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the applicant does not file the reports
    referred to in subparagraph (i), audited financial statements for
    the three most recent complete fiscal years, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where the applicant does not have the information
    referred to in subparagraph (i) or (ii), other financial information
    that establishes the applicant's financial capability to sustain the
    self-insurance.

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1186113 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant for a certificate of fitness for the proposed operation of a
  railway that relates to a passenger rail service or the proposed
  construction of a railway must provide the Agency with the information
  set out in Schedule 1.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A holder of a certificate of fitness for the
  operation of a railway that relates to a passenger rail service or the
  construction of a railway must provide the Agency with the information
  set out in Schedule 1, accompanied by a description of any changes to
  the information that was most recently provided under this section,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the expiry of any insurance coverage and as
    soon as feasible after the holder has arranged for new insurance
    coverage to replace it; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any case, at least once a year.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant for a certificate of fitness for the proposed operation of a
  railway that does not relate to a passenger rail service must provide
  the Agency with the information set out in Schedule 2.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A holder of a certificate of fitness for the
  operation of a railway that does not relate to a passenger rail
  service must provide the Agency with the information set out in
  Schedule 2, accompanied by a description of any changes to the
  information that was most recently provided under this section,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the expiry of any insurance coverage and as
    soon as feasible after the holder has arranged for new insurance
    coverage to replace it; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any case, at least once a year.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (1) and (2), the information set
  out in item 13 of Schedule 2 is not required to be provided by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an applicant for a certificate of fitness, if there
    will be the minimum liability insurance coverage set out in Item 4
    of Schedule IV to the Act for the proposed operation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a holder of a certificate of fitness, if the
    minimum liability insurance coverage set out in Item 4 of Schedule
    IV to the Act is maintained in respect of the operation.
