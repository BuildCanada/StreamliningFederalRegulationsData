## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809323 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Standing Orders.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
    Act](/eng/acts/R-10). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[conduct process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[conduct process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the
    administrative actions, decisions or processes provided for by the
    Act, the Regulations, the Commissioner's Standing Orders or the
    Force's policies relating to an alleged contravention of the Code of
    Conduct by a member. ([processus disciplinaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[parties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[parties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the parties referred to in
    subsection 45.1(1) of the Act. ([parties]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Royal Canadian
    Mounted Police Regulations,
    2014](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-281). ([Règlement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[subject member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[subject member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member who is the
    subject of a conduct process. ([membre visé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Conduct Authorities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809331 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation as conduct authorities

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following persons, subject to any requirements that may be established
  by the Commissioner under subsection (2), are designated as conduct
  authorities in respect of the members who are under their command:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} members who are in command of a detachment and
    persons who report directly to an officer or to a person who holds
    an equivalent managerial position;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} officers, or persons who hold equivalent managerial
    positions; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} officers who are in command of a Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may establish the requirements that
  a person must meet before acting as a conduct authority.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may revoke the designation of a
  person as a conduct authority by written notice. The revocation takes
  effect as soon as the notice is served on the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of conduct process

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} At the time of the revocation, any conduct process
  that is the responsibility of the conduct authority is suspended until
  another conduct authority takes responsibility for the conduct
  process.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with [Public Servants
  Disclosure Protection Act](/eng/acts/P-31.9)

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a person who is designated as a conduct authority
  is a senior officer, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Public
  Servants Disclosure Protection Act](/eng/acts/P-31.9), the person must
  administer the conduct process in a manner that complies with that
  Act.

## [Conduct Measures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809346 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remedial conduct measures

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  conduct authority who is referred to in paragraph 2(1)(a) may impose
  one or more of the following remedial conduct measures against a
  subject member:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an admonishment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a direction to work under close supervision for a
    period of not more than one year;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a direction to undergo training;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a direction to undergo medical treatment as
    specified by a Health Services Officer;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a direction to attend counselling sessions or
    complete a rehabilitative program;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a direction to complete a program or engage in an
    activity;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a removal, restriction or modification of duties as
    specified by the conduct authority for a period of not more than one
    year;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a reassignment to another position not involving a
    relocation or demotion;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a reprimand;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a financial penalty of not more than eight hours of
    the member's pay, deducted from the member's pay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct measure by agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The conduct authority and the subject member may
  agree on the imposition of any other conduct measure, other than a
  financial penalty or a corrective or serious conduct measure.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corrective conduct measures

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A conduct authority
referred to in paragraph 2(1)(b) may impose, in addition to the remedial
conduct measures set out in section 3, one or more of the following
corrective conduct measures against a subject member:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an ineligibility for promotion for a period of not
  more than one year;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a deferment of pay increment for a period of not more
  than one year;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a suspension from duty without pay for a period of
  not more than 80 hours;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a financial penalty of not more than 80 hours of the
  member's pay, deducted from the member's pay;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a forfeiture of annual leave for a period of not more
  than 80 hours;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any combination of the measures referred to in
  paragraphs (c) to (e) totalling not more than 80 hours.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Serious conduct measures

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  conduct authority referred to in paragraph 2(1)(c) may impose, in
  addition to any remedial and corrective conduct measures, one or more
  of the following serious conduct measures against a subject member:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a removal, restriction or modification of duties as
    specified by the conduct authority for a period of not more than
    three years;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an ineligibility for promotion for a period of not
    more than three years;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a deferment of pay increment for a period of not
    more than two years;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a reduction to the next lower rate of pay for a
    period of not more than two years;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a demotion for a period of not more than three
    years;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a demotion for an indefinite period;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a transfer to another work location;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a suspension from duty without pay;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a forfeiture of annual leave for a period of not
    more than 160 hours;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a financial penalty deducted from the member's pay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of demotion

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of the demotion referred to in paragraph
  (1)(e), the member's rate of pay reverts, on the expiration of the
  period of demotion, to the rate of pay that the member received before
  the demotion, subject to any adjustments applicable to that rank or
  level.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct boards and persons designated
  by Commissioner

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Conduct boards and persons who are designated as
  conduct authorities by the Commissioner under subsection 2(3) of the
  Act may impose any of the measures referred to in subsection 5(1)
  against a subject member.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligibility for promotion

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  conduct measure is imposed under any of paragraphs 4(b) and 5(1)(c) to
  (e), the subject member is ineligible for promotion for the period set
  by the conduct authority.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Demotion under paragraph 5(1)(f)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A conduct authority who imposes a demotion under
  paragraph 5(1)(f) must specify the period, up to a maximum of three
  years, during which the subject member is ineligible for promotion.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of financial penalty

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of sections 3 to 5, a financial penalty is to be calculated
  based on the subject member's substantive rank or level on the day on
  which the penalty is imposed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collection

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may determine the manner in which a
  financial penalty is to be collected, if necessary, to avoid causing
  the member undue financial hardship.

## [Decision by Conduct Authority]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809397 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written decision

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A conduct authority must
cause to be served on the subject member a copy of their decision
setting out any findings in respect of the alleged contravention of the
Code of Conduct, any conduct measure imposed in respect of that
contravention and the reasons for the decision. The decision takes
effect as soon as it is served.

## [Review Authority]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809400 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of review authority

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner may designate a person to be a review authority in
  respect of decisions made by conduct authorities and as the conduct
  authority in respect of the subject member for any decision that the
  review authority decides to review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reason for review

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A review authority may, on their own initiative,
  review a decision to determine if a finding is clearly unreasonable or
  a conduct measure is clearly disproportionate to the nature and
  circumstances of the contravention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of review authority

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the review authority makes the determination that
  a finding is clearly unreasonable or a conduct measure is clearly
  disproportionate and if it is in the public interest to do so, the
  review authority may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} rescind any finding made by the conduct authority
    that the subject member has not contravened the Code of Conduct,
    substitute for that finding a finding that the subject member has
    contravened the Code of Conduct and impose any one or more of the
    conduct measures referred to in subsection 5(1) that is
    proportionate to the nature and circumstances of the contravention;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} rescind or amend any conduct measure imposed by the
    conduct authority, or substitute any one or more of the measures
    referred to in subsection 5(1) that is proportionate to the nature
    and circumstances of the contravention; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} rescind any conduct measure imposed by the conduct
    authority and initiate a hearing in accordance with subsection 41(1)
    of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of notice

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  review authority intends to substitute another finding for one that
  was made by the conduct authority, amend any imposed conduct measure
  or substitute another measure for one that was imposed by the conduct
  authority, the review authority must cause to be served on the subject
  member a notice informing the member of this intention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submissions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The member may, within 14 days after the day on which
  they are served with the notice, provide the review authority with
  written submissions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  review authority must render a decision in writing as soon as feasible
  after considering the subject member's submissions, and cause a copy
  of it to be served on the member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the review authority renders a decision under
  paragraph 9(3)(a) or (b), the reasons for the decision must be
  included.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taking effect

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The decision takes effect as soon as it is served.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information previously provided

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If a review authority
initiates a hearing, any decisions rendered by the conduct authority and
information received by the conduct authority or review authority from
or on behalf of the subject member during the conduct process and not
forming a part of the investigation, including any admission, must not
be provided to the conduct board and the board must not consider the
information, unless the member requests that it be considered.

## [Conduct Board Rules of Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809425 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct hearing

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Proceedings before a conduct board
  must be dealt with by the board as informally and expeditiously as the
  principles of procedural fairness permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptation of rules

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The conduct board may adapt these rules of procedure
  if the principles of procedural fairness permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-compliance with rules

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The conduct board may remedy any failure to comply
  with these rules of procedure, including by setting aside a proceeding
  either wholly or in part, in accordance with the principles of
  procedural fairness.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matters not provided for

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If any matter arises in the proceedings that is not
  otherwise provided for in the Act, the Regulations or these Standing
  Orders, the conduct board may give any direction that it considers
  appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence of subject member

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The circumstances
referred to in subsection 45.1(8) of the Act are that the conduct board
is satisfied that the subject member has received reasonable notice of
the date, time and place of the hearing and has failed to attend without
reasonable cause.

Definition of [[investigation report]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[investigation report]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
  report resulting from the investigation referred to in subsection
  40(1) of the Act and includes supporting material.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents to be provided and served

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} As soon as feasible after the members of the conduct
  board have been appointed, the conduct authority must provide a copy
  of the notice referred to in subsection 43(2) of the Act and the
  investigation report to the conduct board and must cause a copy of the
  investigation report to be served on the subject member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents to be provided by member

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Within 30 days after the day on which the subject
  member is served with the notice or within another period as directed
  by the conduct board, the subject member must provide to the conduct
  authority and the conduct board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an admission or denial, in writing, of each alleged
    contravention of the Code of Conduct;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any written submissions that the member wishes to
    make; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any evidence, document or report, other than the
    investigation report, that the member intends to introduce or rely
    on at the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further investigation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A subject member may request that the conduct board
  cause a further investigation to be made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further information or documents

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A conduct board may order a person to provide any
  further information or documents that the board requires to perform
  its role under subsection 45(1) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protected information

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Parties are not required to provide any information
  or documents that are protected by privilege or any communication that
  is protected by subsection 47.1(2) of the Act or subsection 56(3) of
  the Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-hearing conference

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  conduct board may direct the parties to participate in a pre-hearing
  conference, to be held in any manner directed by the board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Results in writing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A conduct board that holds a pre-hearing conference
  must record in writing any directions, decisions, agreements or
  undertakings that arise from the pre-hearing conference and must
  provide a copy of that record to each of the parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motions

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may bring a motion before the conduct board at any time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date for hearing motion

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The board may fix a date for hearing the motion or,
  if the principles of procedural fairness permit, hear it immediately.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of motion

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the board fixes a date, the moving party must, at
  least 14 days before that date, provide the board and serve the
  responding party with a notice of motion that sets out the grounds for
  the motion and the relief sought, together with any evidence to be
  relied on.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response to motion

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The responding party must, at least seven days before
  the date fixed for the hearing, provide the board and serve the moving
  party with any evidence to be relied on, together with written
  submissions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witnesses to appear

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  30 days after the day on which the notice of hearing is served, the
  parties must submit to the conduct board a list of the witnesses that
  they want to have summoned before the board and a list of the issues
  in respect of which they may want to rely on expert testimony.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information on witnesses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The list of witnesses must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of each witness;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for requesting the appearance of each
    witness;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the anticipated evidence of each
    witness; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the appropriate means that will allow each witness
    to testify.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of witnesses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The board must establish a list of the witnesses that
  it intends to summon, including any expert in respect of whom a party
  has indicated an intention under subsection 19(3) to question, and may
  seek further submissions from the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing reasons

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The board must provide the parties with the list of
  witnesses that it will hear and its reasons for accepting or refusing
  any witness on the list submitted by the parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expert report

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  party who intends to use an expert report must, at least 30 days
  before the hearing, submit it to the conduct board and serve it on the
  other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of expert report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The expert report must contain the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the issues addressed in the report;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the expert's qualifications with
    respect to those issues;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the expert's curriculum vitae, attached as a
    schedule;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the opinions expressed in the report;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the facts and assumptions on which the opinions are
    based;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the results of any tests carried out;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the reasons supporting each opinion expressed;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the methodology on which the expert
    has relied;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the expert's findings;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a medical expert's report, the
    expert's opinions concerning the diagnosis and prognosis for the
    person who is the subject of the report; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} any literature or other documents specifically
    relied on in support of the opinions expressed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responding expert report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party who is served with an expert report must,
  within 14 days after the day on which it is served, notify the board
  and the other party of their intention, if any, to question the expert
  or to obtain a responding expert report. The board must fix a deadline
  for the submission of a responding expert report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reading of allegations

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  commencement of a hearing, the conduct board must read to the subject
  member each allegation of contravention of the Code of Conduct that is
  set out in the notice of the hearing, and the member must admit or
  deny each allegation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed denial

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a member does not admit or deny an allegation, the
  member is deemed to have denied the allegation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of position

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The board may permit a member to change their
  position in respect of an allegation at any time before the final
  decision in respect of the allegation is rendered.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjournment

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The conduct board may, if
necessary, adjourn the hearing for up to 30 days or, in exceptional
circumstances, for a longer period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recording of proceedings

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** A hearing before a
conduct board must be recorded and, at the request of a party who is
appealing a decision of the board, a transcript of the recording must be
prepared and given to them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision without further evidence

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If no
  testimony is heard in respect of an allegation, the conduct board may
  render a decision in respect of the allegation based solely on the
  record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Member guilty of offence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The conduct board may rely on a finding by a court in
  Canada that a member is guilty of an offence under an Act of
  Parliament or of the legislature of a province to decide that the
  member has contravened the Code of Conduct.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision on conduct measures

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  determining the appropriate conduct measures to impose, the conduct
  board may examine any material submitted by the parties and hear their
  oral submissions and any witness, including those referred to in
  subsection 18(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proportionality

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The conduct board must impose conduct measures that
  are proportionate to the nature and circumstances of the contravention
  of the Code of Conduct.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  conduct board must render a decision as soon as feasible after the
  hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taking effect of decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An oral decision that is rendered in the presence of
  the subject member takes effect immediately. A written decision takes
  effect as soon as a copy of it is served on the member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of decision

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The conduct board must cause a copy of the decision
  to be served on the subject member and the conduct authority.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record of conduct proceedings

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The conduct board must
compile a record after the hearing, including

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the notice of hearing referred to in subsection 43(2)
  of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the notice served on the subject member of the place,
  date and time of the hearing;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any other information provided to the
  board;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a list of any exhibits entered at the hearing;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the directions, decisions, agreements and
  undertakings, if any, referred to in subsection 16(2);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the recording and the transcript, if any, of the
  hearing; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a copy of all written decisions of the board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of exhibits

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  the conduct board decides otherwise, it must cause to be returned to a
  party anything the party tendered as an exhibit, after the end of the
  period in which an appeal may be presented, or, if an appeal has been
  presented, after the disposition of the appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Destruction or disposal of exhibits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the request of a party or if a party refuses to
  accept a returned exhibit, the conduct board must cause the exhibit to
  be destroyed or disposed of.

## [Waiver]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809540 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written waiver

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Any waiver by a subject
member of a right under these Standing Orders must be made in writing.

## [Representation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809543 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this section and in sections 30 and 31.

[[assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means legal guidance and
    information provided to a subject member who may be subject to the
    serious conduct measures referred to in paragraphs5(1)(a) to (j) or
    who has received the notice under section 10, or to the conduct
    authority in respect of the subject
    member. ([assistance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Conduct Authority Representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Conduct Authority Representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    person who is authorized by the Director of the Conduct Authority
    Representative Directorate to provide representation or assistance
    to a conduct authority. ([représentant des autorités
    disciplinaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Conduct Authority Representative Directorate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Conduct Authority Representative
    Directorate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the unit within the Force
    that provides representation or assistance to a conduct
    authority. ([Direction des représentants des autorités
    disciplinaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Member Representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Member Representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is
    authorized by the Director of the Member Representative Directorate
    to provide representation or assistance to a subject
    member. ([représentants des membres]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Member Representative Directorate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Member Representative Directorate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    unit within the Force that may provide representation or assistance
    to a subject member. ([Direction des représentants des
    membres]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[representation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[representation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the act of representing
    a subject member or conduct authority, including providing legal
    advice, litigation or advocacy for the purpose of these Standing
    Orders. ([représentation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Member representation

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  Member Representative may provide representation to a subject member
  if the member

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is subject to a stoppage of pay and allowances
    under paragraph 22(2)(b) of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has received a notice under subsection 43(2) of the
    Act; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is a respondent in an appeal instituted by a
    conduct authority under subsection 45.11(1) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Member assistance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Member Representative may provide assistance to a
  subject member if the member

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is subject to any of the conduct measures set out
    in paragraphs 5(1)(a) to (j); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has been served with a notice under subsection
    10(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Director of the Member Representative
  Directorate decides that one of the following circumstances applies, a
  Member Representative must not represent or assist a subject member:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Member Representative is involved in the
    alleged misconduct as a party, witness, participant, or interested
    person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any representation or assistance could result in a
    conflict of interest;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any representation or assistance could impair the
    efficiency, administration or good government of the Force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discontinuation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Director may discontinue providing representation
  or assistance if the subject member

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} engages in deceitful conduct towards the Member
    Representative assigned to the member;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fails to cooperate with the Member Representative;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} requests or encourages the Member Representative to
    act in a manner that is unlawful or unethical; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} acts in a manner that causes an irreparable breach
    of confidence in the Member Representative's relationship with the
    subject member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of decision

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If, under subsection (3), a subject member cannot be
  represented or assisted or if, under subsection (4), the Director
  discontinues the provision of representation or assistance, the
  Director must cause a notice to this effect to be served on the
  subject member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excluded costs

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a subject member is not represented or assisted by
  a Member Representative, the subject member is responsible for all of
  the subject member's costs in responding to an allegation of a
  contravention of the Code of Conduct.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application to unionized members

**[[30.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30.1 .sectionLabel}** Section 30 does not
apply to a subject member who is a member of a bargaining unit that is
represented by a bargaining agent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct authority representation

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  Conduct Authority Representative may represent a conduct authority in
  the following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a conduct authority intends to direct that a
    subject member's pay and allowances be stopped under paragraph
    22(2)(b) of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a conduct authority intends to initiate a conduct
    hearing under subsection 41(1) of the Act; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a conduct authority intends to appeal or is a
    respondent in an appeal of a conduct board decision under subsection
    45.11(1) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct authority assistance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Conduct Authority Representative may provide
  assistance

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to a conduct authority who intends to impose any of
    the measures set out in paragraphs 5(1)(a) to (j); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to a review authority who intends to prepare the
    notice referred to in subsection 10(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Director of the Conduct Authority
  Representative Directorate decides that one of the following
  circumstances applies, a Conduct Representative must not represent or
  assist a conduct authority:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Conduct Authority Representative is involved in
    the alleged misconduct as a party, witness, participant or
    interested person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any representation or assistance could result in a
    conflict of interest;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any representation or assistance could impair the
    efficiency, administration or good government of the Force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Only persons who are Conduct Authority
  Representatives are authorized to provide representation and
  assistance under subsections (1) and (2) to conduct authorities.

## [Appeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809594 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Redress for certain written decisions

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member who is aggrieved by one of the following written decisions may
  seek redress by means of an appeal of the decision in accordance with
  the [Commissioner's Standing Orders (Grievances and
  Appeals)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-289):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the decision by a conduct authority to temporarily
    reassign the member to other duties during a conduct process;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the decision to suspend the member under section 12
    of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the decision to direct that the member's pay and
    allowances be stopped under paragraph 22(2)(b) of the Act; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the decisions to deny or discontinue representation
    or assistance under subsection 30(3) or (4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Redress for other decisions, acts or
  omissions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member who is aggrieved by any decision, act or
  omission that leads to one of the written decisions set out in
  paragraphs (1)(a) to (d) may seek redress by means of an appeal of the
  decision in accordance with the [Commissioner's Standing Orders
  (Grievances and Appeals)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-289).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of appeal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An appeal made under this section does not stay the
  execution of the written decision being appealed or of any related
  process.
