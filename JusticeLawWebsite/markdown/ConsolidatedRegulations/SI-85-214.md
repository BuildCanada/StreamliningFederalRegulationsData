## Government and Long-Term Corporate Debt Obligations Remission Order {#government-and-long-term-corporate-debt-obligations-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/85-214
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1985-12-11

Order Respecting the Remission of Certain Income Taxes Paid or Payable
by Certain Persons in Respect of Interest From Government and Long-Term
Corporate Debt Obligations

P.C. 1985-3480 1985-11-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to make the annexed remission order, is pleased
hereby, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance and the
Treasury Board and pursuant to section 17[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the annexed
Order respecting the remission of certain income taxes paid or payable
by certain persons in respect of interest from government and long-term
corporate debt obligations.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639989 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the Government and Long-Term Corporate Debt Obligation Remission Order.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639991 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639994 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to each non-resident person who is liable for tax under Part XIII of the
Act in respect of any amount paid or credited to him as, on account or
in lieu of payment of, or in satisfaction of, interest of an amount
equal to the amount, if any, by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax that would be payable by the non-resident
  person under Part XIII of the Act in respect of the amount so paid or
  credited

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the tax that would be payable by the non-resident
  person under Part XIII of the Act in respect of the amount so paid or
  credited if the references to "1986" in subparagraphs 212(1)(b)(ii)
  and (vii) of the Act were read as references to "1987".

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where a person required to
deduct or withhold a tax payable by a non-resident person under Part
XIII of the Act is liable to pay as tax under Part XIII on behalf of the
non-resident person the whole of the amount that should have been
deducted or withheld, remission is hereby granted to that person of an
amount equal to the amount, if any, by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax payable by the person so required to deduct
  or withhold under Part XIII of the Act

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the tax that would be payable by the person so
  required to deduct or withhold under Part III of the Act if the
  references to "1986" in subparagraphs 212(1)(b)(ii) and (vii) of the
  Act were read as references to "1987".
