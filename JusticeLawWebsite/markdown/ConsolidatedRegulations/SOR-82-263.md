## National Historic Parks General Regulations {#national-historic-parks-general-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/82-263
:::

[CANADA NATIONAL PARKS ACT](/eng/acts/N-14.01)

Registration 1982-02-26

General Regulations Respecting the Control and Management of the
National Historic Parks of Canada

P.C. 1982-596 1982-02-25
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to section 7 of the National
Parks Act and section 3 of the National Historic Parks Order, C.R.C.,
c. 1112, is pleased hereby to make the annexed [General Regulations
respecting the Control and Management of the National Historic Parks of
Canada](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-263).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873155 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [National Historic Parks General
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-263).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873157 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[archaeological site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[archaeological site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any site or area
    containing historical resources buried or partially buried on land,
    resting on the surface of land or submerged or partially submerged
    beneath the surface of any watercourse or body of water in a Park;
    ([site archéologique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Director of a Region of
    Parks Canada, Department of the Environment;
    ([directeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[diver's marker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[diver's marker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a floating device that
    incorporates a marker in the form of a red square flag with a white
    diagonal stripe extending from the head of the hoist; ([indicateur
    de plongée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[explosive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[explosive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any substance or device
    other than a firearm made, manufactured or used to produce a violent
    effect by explosion or pyrotechnic effect;
    ([explosif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fauna]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fauna]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all vertebrates or
    invertebrates, living or dead, or parts thereof and includes the
    eggs or young of fauna but does not include the fossil remains of
    any fauna; ([faune]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fireplace]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fireplace]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a grill or stove or other
    such device designed for the kindling or maintenance of fire;
    ([foyer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[flora]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[flora]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plant matter, living or
    dead, and includes fungi and moulds but does not include the fossil
    remains of any flora; ([flore]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[historical object]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[historical object]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any historical
    resource of a movable nature and includes any specimen, artifact,
    document or work of art, or any reproduction thereof; ([objet
    historique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[historical resource]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[historical resource]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any work of nature
    or of man that is primarily of interest for its palaeontological,
    prehistorical, historical, cultural, natural, scientific or
    aesthetic value including, but not limited to, a palaeontological,
    prehistorical, historical or natural site, structure or object or
    any remains, restoration or reconstruction thereof; ([ressource
    historique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[kindle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[kindle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to start, or attempt to start,
    a fire; ([allumer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[maintain]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[maintain]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, with respect to a fire, to
    add fuel to enjoy the use of, or allow to burn;
    ([alimenter]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of the
    Environment; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[moor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[moor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to make fast or tie up a
    watercraft to a wharf or to another watercraft or to use a space or
    stall at a wharf and includes to dock, beach, store or anchor a
    watercraft; ([amarrer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[natural object]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[natural object]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any natural material,
    soil, sand, gravel, rock, mineral or other object or natural
    phenomenon not included within the terms
    [[flora]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[fauna]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} that
    is located within a Park, but does not include the fossil remains of
    any flora or fauna; ([objet naturel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any area set apart as a National
    Historic Park in accordance with Part II of the National Parks Act;
    ([parc]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Superintendent of a
    Park; ([directeur du parc]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any boat, canoe, raft,
    amphibious vehicle or any other structure or device, other than a
    seaplane, used or capable of being used for transportation on water;
    ([bateau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[wharf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wharf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any wharf, dock, pier, jetty,
    quay, landing, breakwater, mole or sea-wall or any floating
    structure or device that may be used to moor watercraft.
    ([quai]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Preservation of Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873177 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall disturb, remove, deface, damage or destroy any
  archaeological site or historical resource in a Park.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), a Director may issue
  a permit to any person authorizing the person to remove historical
  resources from a Park for management of the Park or scientific
  purposes or for the purpose of public display.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall remove, deface, damage or destroy flora, fauna or natural
  objects in a Park.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), a Superintendent may
  issue a permit to any person authorizing the person to remove, deface,
  damage or destroy flora, fauna or natural objects in a Park for
  management of the Park or scientific purposes.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A permit issued by the Superintendent under
  subsection (2) shall specify the kind and amount of, and the location
  from which, flora, fauna or natural objects may be removed, defaced,
  damaged or destroyed, and any other conditions applicable to the
  permit.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall remove,
deface, damage or destroy any public building, fence or other structure
in a Park or any signboard, sign or notice placed, posted or erected in
a Park.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No person shall deposit any
snow, leaves, rubbish or other matter in a Park except in such places,
at such times and under such conditions as may be specified by the
Superintendent.

## [Use of Water Resources]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873187 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No person shall pollute any
watercourse in a Park.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 9 to
11, no person shall obstruct, divert or otherwise interfere with any
watercourse.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Superintendent of a
Park may permit the taking of water from any watercourse or water supply
system in the Park to a place outside the Park in the case of drought,
fire, contamination of a water supply or other emergency.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A Director may issue a
permit to any person, for a period not exceeding 10 years, authorizing
the person to take water for domestic or business water supply purposes
within a Park from any watercourse, well or water supply system in the
Park.

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may enter into an agreement with a municipality or water
  district adjacent to a Park for the supply of water from the Park.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may enter into an agreement with persons
  residing on land adjacent to a Park for the supply of water from the
  Park for domestic purposes and for use in establishments providing
  tourist accommodation.

## [Restricted Areas and Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873195 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Superintendent of a Park shall designate any activity or area in the
  Park as restricted if it is necessary for the preservation, control or
  management of the Park.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Superintendent of a Park designates any
  activity or area as restricted pursuant to subsection (1), he or she
  shall so inform the public by the posting, at all information offices
  or centres located in the Park, of notices describing the activity and
  of maps showing the area.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent of a Park may, on application to
  him or her by any person, authorize that person to engage in an
  activity in the Park or to enter an area of the Park that has been
  designated as restricted pursuant to subsection (1) on such terms and
  conditions as the Superintendent may prescribe.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent of a Park may suspend or cancel
  any authorization issued under subsection (3) if it is necessary to do
  so for the preservation, control or management of a Park.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No person shall engage in an activity or enter an
  area that has been designated as restricted pursuant to subsection (1)
  except in accordance with the terms and conditions prescribed by the
  Superintendent.

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873204 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section,

  [[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pass, licence,
      ticket or other form of permission, issued pursuant to
      subsection (4) in respect of a designated activity, authorizing
      the holder to carry on the designated activity;
      ([autorisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any activity in
      a Park, including travel, entry into an area of the Park and the
      use of any facility in the Park, that is designated pursuant to
      subsection (2). ([activité désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent may, for the proper management of
  the Park, designate any activity, other than an activity referred to
  in section 12, that may be carried on only by the holder of an
  authorization issued in respect of that activity.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent shall post a list of designated
  activities and their description at information offices or centers
  located in the Park or at the entrances to the Park.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent shall, on request, issue an
  authorization in respect of a designated activity if the designated
  activity will not result in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any significant adverse effects on the natural and
    cultural resources of the Park;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any adverse effects on the safety and health of
    persons using the Park;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any significant adverse effects on the enjoyment of
    persons using the Park; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any significant adverse effects on the
    preservation, control and management of the Park.

- [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent shall set in the authorization
  any terms and conditions that are necessary for the preservation,
  control and management of the Park or for the safety of the public.\

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No person shall carry on a designated activity unless
  the person is the holder of an authorization issued in respect of that
  designated activity.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The holder of an authorization shall, at the request
  of the Superintendent or a warden or other officer of the Park,
  produce the authorization for inspection.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend an authorization, where the holder fails to
    comply with any prescribed term or condition of it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reinstate any authorization suspended pursuant to
    paragraph (a), where the failure referred to in that paragraph has
    been remedied; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} revoke an authorization, where the holder is
    convicted of a contravention of these Regulations.

## [Prevention of Nuisances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873225 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  nuisance exists on any premises in a Park, the Superintendent of the
  Park is authorized to order the owner, lessee, licensee or occupier of
  the premises to abate the nuisance and, if necessary, to clean up the
  premises.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the owner, lessee, licensee or occupier of any
  premises where a nuisance exists fails, after reasonable notice from
  the Superintendent, to abate the nuisance and, where necessary, clean
  up the premises, the Superintendent may take such steps as are
  necessary to abate the nuisance and clean up the premises.

## [Camping]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873231 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited, without a permit issued for this purpose, for any person
  to use, occupy, reside or camp on any land in a Park or erect or use
  any structure in a Park or park any vehicle in a Park for the purpose
  of camping.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section prevents a person from using
  the facilities provided in a public picnic ground for the preparation
  and consumption of meals.

## [Fires]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873268 .Part}

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** No person shall, in a
Park, kindle or maintain any fire except

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a fireplace provided by the Superintendent;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a portable stove burning fuel other than wood or
  charcoal; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in a charcoal or gas-fired barbecue.

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding section
24, the Superintendent may, by written authorization, permit a person to
kindle and maintain a fire and may stipulate the terms and conditions
under which the fire shall be kindled and maintained.

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** No person shall, in a
Park, be in possession of more than five gallons of gasoline or other
inflammable liquid, unless the gasoline or other inflammable liquid is
stored in the tank of a motor vehicle, a motor-driven boat or an
aircraft or unless a permit has been obtained for possession of the
gasoline or other inflammable liquid.

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** No person shall, in a
Park,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} allow a fire to spread beyond the confines of the
  fireplace, portable stove or barbecue;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} discard a lighted cigar, cigarette, match or other
  burning substance, except in a receptacle provided for that purpose by
  the Superintendent;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} discard any unused match or any article or substance
  that could cause the kindling of a fire, except in a receptacle
  provided for that purpose by the Superintendent;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} operate any machine or equipment that may cause the
  kindling of a fire, unless reasonable precautions have been taken;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} when removing or disposing of brush or any
  inflammable material, allow the brush or inflammable material to be
  accumulated, stored, handled, transported or disposed of in an
  unreasonable manner; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} leave any fire unattended.

## [Explosives]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873284 .Part}

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), no person
  shall, except in accordance with a permit issued pursuant to
  subsection (2), have in their possession, store or use any explosive
  or bring any explosive into a Park.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Superintendent may issue a permit authorizing a
  person described in subsection (3) to have in their possession, store
  or use any explosive in a Park, where that person is authorized under
  the [Explosives Act](/eng/acts/E-17) to have in their possession,
  store, use, make, manufacture or sell such an explosive in any other
  part of Canada.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A permit referred to in subsection (2) may be issued
  to the following persons:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person engaged in construction or demolition work
    who requires the explosive for their work;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person representing an organization retained by
    written agreement with the Director to present a reenactment of a
    historic event or other program for the education or entertainment
    of the public; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person in the employ of Her Majesty in right of
    Canada whose duties include the presentation of programs for the
    education or entertainment of the public.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), any person may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} transport explosives through a Park in accordance
    with the [Explosives Act](/eng/acts/E-17) or any provincial
    regulation respecting explosives; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} possess flares or fuses for use as safety equipment
    for motor vehicles, trains or boats in accordance with any federal
    or provincial requirements.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-217, s. 6(E)

## [Aircraft]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873297 .Part}

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** Except as authorized by
the Superintendent for the control or management of the Park, no person
shall, in a Park, take off or land or deposit persons or objects from
any aircraft, sail plane, glider, body kite, hang glider or other device
designed or used to carry persons or objects through the air in
powerless flight.

## [Vessels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873299 .Part}

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall operate or use, or cause to be operated or used, on or
  under any watercourse in a Park any type of motorized vessel,
  water-skiing equipment or subsurface diving equipment unless a sign or
  notice authorized by the Superintendent permitting such operation or
  use is posted on or near the watercourse.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), the Superintendent
  may operate or use, or cause to be operated or used, any vessel or
  equipment referred to in subsection (1) on or under any watercourse in
  the Park where such operation or use is necessary for the management
  of the Park or for the safety of the public.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall drain, dump or discharge any waste or
  refuse from a vessel into any watercourse in a Park.

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** No person shall moor any
vessel in the approach to or in the lee of any wharf in a Park in such a
manner as to obstruct the free flow of vessels to and from the wharf.

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall moor any vessel at any wharf or area in a Park except in
  accordance with a traffic control device erected by the Superintendent
  pursuant to subsection (2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent may designate any wharf or area in
  the Park or any portion thereof as a wharf or area where mooring is
  allowed or where mooring is allowed only

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with a permit issued by the
    Superintendent,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during specified periods of time, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for particular types of vessels

  and the Superintendent shall erect a traffic control device at or near
  the designated wharf or area to indicate such designation.

<!-- -->

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall undertake any underwater activities in a Park unless he
  or she first places a diver's marker in the vicinity of the dive site
  so that the marker is clearly visible to vessel operators.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who operates a vessel in the vicinity of
  a diver's marker referred to in subsection (1) shall proceed with due
  caution and at a slow speed.

## [Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873317 .Part}

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may enter
into an agreement with a province or any person for the development,
operation and maintenance in a Park of telephone, telegraph, natural gas
and electrical, other than hydro-electrical, services for use only in
the Park.

## [Advertisements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873319 .Part}

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** No person shall display
or distribute any advertisement or handbill in a Park except under and
in accordance with a permit issued by the Superintendent.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873321 .Part}

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall, in a Park,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cause any excessive noise;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} behave in a manner that unreasonably disturbs other
    persons in the Park or unreasonably interferes with their enjoyment
    of the Park;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} carry out any action that unreasonably interferes
    with fauna or the natural beauty of the Park;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} carry out any action that threatens the historic
    resources in the Park; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} create or cause any nuisance in the Park.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent may remove or have removed from a
  Park any person who by their disorderly conduct, behaviour or action
  is in violation of subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person who has been removed from a Park under
  subsection (2) shall enter or attempt to enter that Park for one year
  following the date of removal unless that person applies for and
  obtains permission from the Director to enter the Park.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-217, s. 6(E)
