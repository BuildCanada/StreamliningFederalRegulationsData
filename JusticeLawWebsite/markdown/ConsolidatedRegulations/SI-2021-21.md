## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1293121 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[refugee travel
document]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order, [[refugee
travel document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a travel document issued
under the United Nations Convention Relating to the Status of Refugees,
signed at Geneva on July 28, 1951 and the Protocol to that Convention,
signed at New York on January 31, 1967.

## [Remission and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1293124 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted to any
person who meets the conditions set out in section 3 of the fees paid or
payable under

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} subsection 2(1) of the [Passport and Other Travel
  Document Services Fees Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-253) and
  items 1 to 10 and item 14 of the schedule to those Regulations in
  respect of the issuance or replacement of a passport, certificate of
  identity, refugee travel document or emergency travel document;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 4 of the [Consular Services Fees
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-538) for the issuance of a travel
  document;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} subsection 31(1) of the [Citizenship
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-246) and item 6 of the schedule
  to those Regulations for an application for a certificate of
  citizenship;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} subsection 294.1(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee
  Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227) for the
  processing of an application for an electronic travel authorization;
  and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} subsections 308(1) and (2) and section 315 of the
  [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227) for the processing of an
  application for a permanent resident card or permanent resident travel
  document.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the period beginning on January 10, 2020 and
  ending on June 30, 2020, the person, or a person acting on their
  behalf, made an application for a passport, certificate of identity,
  refugee travel document, emergency travel document, certificate of
  citizenship, electronic travel authorization, permanent resident card
  or permanent resident travel document;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that person, or the person acting on their behalf,
  included with the application a declaration that the person is a
  family member of a victim of the January 8, 2020 crash of Ukraine
  International Airlines Flight PS752 and was required to travel to
  Canada or abroad to attend to the personal affairs of the victim or to
  provide support to the family of the victim; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fees in question have not been remitted under
  section 11 of the [Passport and Other Travel Document Services Fees
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-253).
