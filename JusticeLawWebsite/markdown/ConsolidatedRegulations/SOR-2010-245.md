## Canadian Press Pension Plan Solvency Deficiency Funding Regulations, 2010 {#canadian-press-pension-plan-solvency-deficiency-funding-regulations-2010 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2010-245
:::

[PENSION BENEFITS STANDARDS ACT, 1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01)

Registration 2010-10-28

Canadian Press Pension Plan Solvency Deficiency Funding Regulations,
2010

P.C. 2010-1357 2010-10-28
::::

:::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 39[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_1a){#fn_1a-ID0EGCAA} of the [Pension Benefits
Standards Act, 1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_1b){#fn_1b-ID0EDCAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Canadian Press Pension Plan Solvency Deficiency Funding Regulations,
2010](/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-245).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765932 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Pension Benefits Standards
      Act, 1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[aggregate amount of deferred special payments]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[aggregate amount of deferred special
      payments]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the sum of the amounts
      considered to be owed to the pension fund under section 8 of the
      [Canadian Press Pension Plan Solvency Deficiency Funding
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2009-183) and the portion of
      special payments that have been deferred under subsection 6(1) of
      the [Solvency Funding Relief
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-275), including interest.
      ([total des paiements spéciaux différés]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[beneficiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[beneficiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member or a former
      member of the Canadian Press pension plan or any other person who
      is entitled to pension benefits under that plan except

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a former member who has transferred all of
        their pension benefit credits under section 26 of the Act;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a former member who used their pension benefit
        credits to purchase a life annuity under section 26 of the Act;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a former member who has had their pension
        benefits transferred to another plan; and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a former member for whom the administrator has
        purchased an immediate or deferred life annuity.
        ([bénéficiaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian Press pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian Press pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      Canadian Press Pension Plan for Employees Represented by the
      Canadian Media Guild, registered under the Act as number 56945 and
      the Pension Plan of the Canadian Press, registered under the Act
      as number 56457. ([régime de retraite de la Presse
      canadienne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[subsidized portion of early retirement benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[subsidized portion of early retirement
      benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount by which an early
      retirement benefit that is payable to a former member under the
      Canadian Press pension plan with the consent of the employer or
      the administrator exceeds the amount of an early retirement
      benefit that would have been payable to that member without that
      consent. ([partie subventionnée des prestations de retraite
      anticipée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided in these Regulations,
  words and expressions used in these Regulations have the same meaning
  as in the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) and the [Solvency Funding Relief
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-275).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an inconsistency between these
  Regulations and the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), or the [Solvency Funding Relief
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-275), these Regulations
  prevail.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765946 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to the Canadian Press pension plan if, within
  30 days after the day on which these Regulations come into force

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the employer provides a written statement to the
    Superintendent confirming that the information set out in section 3
    has been provided to the beneficiaries and that less than one third
    of the members of the plan have objected and less than one third of
    the beneficiaries excluding members have objected to their plan
    being subject to these Regulations and that the plan will meet the
    solvency standards set out in these Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the administrator provides written notice to the
    Superintendent and the beneficiaries that the plan will be funded in
    accordance with these Regulations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the employer provides a declaration to the
    Superintendent stating that the employer has assumed all the
    liabilities of the plan and acknowledging that it owes the aggregate
    amount of deferred special payments; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the employer provides written confirmation to the
    Superintendent that it has received an initial investment of
    \$2.25 million.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 6(1) and sections 11 and 12 of the
  [Solvency Funding Relief Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-275)
  do not apply to the Canadian Press pension plan.

## [Provision of Information to Beneficiaries]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765956 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  administrator shall provide the beneficiaries with the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the solvency ratio of the plan as at December 31,
    2008 and December 31, 2009 and the estimated solvency ratio as at
    June 30, 2010;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the solvency liabilities exceed
    the solvency assets of the plan as at December 31, 2008 and
    December 31, 2009 and the estimated amount by which the solvency
    liabilities exceed the solvency assets as at June 30, 2010;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the extent to which the
    beneficiaries' benefits would be reduced if the plan were fully
    terminated and wound up with the estimated solvency ratio as at
    June 30, 2010;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that the application of
    these Regulations to the plan may result in a reduction of benefits
    as compared to those that the beneficiaries were entitled to receive
    as at June 30, 2010; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that the subsidized portion
    of early retirement benefits may only be paid if the aggregate
    amount of the subsidized portion does not result in the reduction of
    the solvency ratio as at December 31, 2008 by more than 10%.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the costs of compliance with subsection (1) are
  paid out of the Canadian Press pension plan, the employer shall
  reimburse the plan with an amount equal to those costs within 30 days
  of the coming into force of these Regulations.

## [Funding]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765965 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 8 of the
[Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), the Canadian Press pension plan meets
the standards for solvency if the funding of the plan is in accordance
with these Regulations.

## [Funding Rules for 2010 and Subsequent Plan Years]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765967 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  solvency deficiency as at December 31, 2009 is determined to be the
  amount by which the solvency liabilities exceed the sum of the
  solvency assets, not including the amounts deferred under section 7 of
  the Canadian Press Pension Plan Solvency Deficiency Regulations, and
  the present value of the going concern special payments payable for
  the period beginning on January 1, 2010 and ending December 31, 2023.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On December 1, 2010, the Canadian Press pension plan
  shall be funded by a payment equal to the aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a amount equal to one sixth of one of the equal
    annual solvency special payments that would be sufficient to
    liquidate the solvency deficiency determined as at December 31, 2009
    over 14 years;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to one sixth of the going concern
    special payments determined as at or before December 31, 2009
    adjusted to exclude the special payments deferred under section 7 of
    the Canadian Press Pension Plan Solvency Deficiency Regulations from
    the going concern assets; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by a contribution equal to the normal cost of the
    plan.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Beginning on January 1, 2011, the plan shall be
  funded by monthly payments equal to the aggregate of the following
  amounts:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to one twelfth of one of the equal
    annual solvency special payments that would be sufficient to
    liquidate the solvency deficiency determined on December 31, 2009
    over 14 years;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to one twelfth of the going concern
    special payments determined as at or before December 31, 2009
    adjusted to exclude the special payments deferred under section 7 of
    the Canadian Press Pension Plan Solvency Deficiency Regulations from
    the going concern assets; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by a contribution equal to the normal cost of the
    plan.

## [Funding Rules for 2011 and Subsequent Plan Years]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765978 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the 2011 plan year ,
the Canadian Press pension plan shall be funded in accordance with
section 9 of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) with the following modifications:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the unfunded liability is the amount by which the
  going concern deficit determined as at December 31, 2010 exceeds the
  sum of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the present value of going concern special payments
    referred to in paragraph 5(3)(b); and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the present value of solvency special payments
    referred to paragraph 5(3)(a);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the solvency deficiency on December 31, 2010 shall be
  determined as the amount by which the solvency liabilities exceed the
  sum of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the solvency assets,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the present value of going concern special
    payments payable for the period beginning on January 1, 2010 and
    ending on December 31, 2023, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the present value of solvency special payments
    referred to in paragraph 5(3)(a); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the solvency special payments shall be sufficient to
  liquidate the solvency deficiency by equal annual payments over
  13 years.

## [Funding Rules for 2012 and Subsequent Plan Years]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765988 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the 2012 and subsequent
plan years, the Canadian Press pension plan shall be funded in
accordance with section 9 of the [Pension Benefits Standards
Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) with the following
modifications:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the unfunded liability determined as at a valuation
  date after December 31, 2010 is the amount by which the going concern
  deficit determined as at that valuation date exceeds the sum of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the present value of going concern special payments
    determined in respect of any plan year after the valuation date, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the present value of solvency special payments
    referred to in paragraphs 5(3)(a) and 6(c) that are payable after
    the valuation date;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the solvency deficiency, determined as at a valuation
  date after December 31, 2010, is the amount by which the solvency
  liabilities exceed the sum of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the adjusted solvency asset amount, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the present value of going concern special
    payments determined before January 1, 2011 that are payable after
    the valuation date but no later than December 31, 2023, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the present value of solvency special payments
    referred to in paragraphs 5(3)(a) and 6(c) that are payable after
    the valuation date; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if there is a solvency deficiency as at a valuation
  date, the annual solvency special payments to fund this deficiency for
  the plan year following the valuation date shall be equal to the
  amount determined in accordance with the following formula:

  ::: {#765998 .Paragraph}
  A/B -- C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the solvency deficiency,

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the greater of
      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of years in the period beginning on
        the valuation date and ending on December 31, 2023,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} five, and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the going concern special payments determined after
      December 31, 2010 and payable in the plan year following the
      valuation date.
  :::

## [Reduction of Aggregate Amount of Deferred Special Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766007 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The aggregate amount of
deferred special payments shall be reduced by any special payment paid
to the pension fund.

## [Application of Gains]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766009 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  solvency gain that emerges on December 31, 2010 shall be calculated as
  the amount by which the sum of the following amounts exceeds the
  solvency liabilities:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the solvency assets;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the present value of the going concern special
    payments that are payable for the period beginning on January 1,
    2011 and ending on December 31, 2023; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the present value of the solvency special payments
    referred to in paragraph 5(3)(a) established on December 31, 2009
    and payable after December 31, 2010.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A solvency gain that emerges after December 31, 2010
  shall be calculated as the amount by which the sum of the following
  amounts exceed the solvency liabilities:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the adjusted solvency asset amount;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the present value of the going concern special
    payments determined before January 1, 2011 that are payable for the
    period beginning on January 1, 2011 and ending on December 31, 2023;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the present value of the solvency special payments
    referred to in paragraphs 5(3)(a) and 6(c) determined as at
    December 31, 2009 and December 31, 2010 that are payable after the
    valuation date.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For valuation dates after December 31, 2009, if there
  is a solvency gain, this gain may be applied to reduce the outstanding
  balance of any solvency deficiency determined before January 1, 2011
  and the special payments determined to liquidate that solvency
  deficiency, which become due at the latest dates in the payment
  schedule, may be eliminated or reduced in such a way that the present
  value of the special payments is reduced by the amount of reduction
  applied to the outstanding balance of the solvency deficiency.

## [Early Retirement Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766020 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  aggregate value of the subsidized portion of early retirement benefits
  consented to since January 1, 2009 reduces the solvency ratio of a
  Canadian Press pension plan as at December 31, 2008 by more than 10%,
  the employer shall immediately pay to the pension fund an amount that
  would restore the solvency ratio to the value as at December 31, 2008
  minus 10% and shall immediately notify the Superintendent in writing
  of the payment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), the solvency ratio
  has been reduced by more than 10% if

  ::: {#766024 .Subsection}
  (A -- B)/A \> 0.10

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the solvency ratio determined as at December 31, 2008; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the solvency ratio determined as at December 31, 2008 taking
      into consideration the aggregate value of the subsidized portion
      of early retirement benefits consented to since January 1, 2009.
  :::

- SOR/2015-60, s. 55(F)
:::

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of any secured borrowing obtained by the employer once it has
  obtained an aggregate of \$1.5 million of secured borrowing after
  January 31, 2009 must be subordinated to the aggregate amount of
  deferred special payments.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the employer does not comply with subsection (1),
  it shall immediately notify the Superintendent in writing of its
  non-compliance and immediately pay to the pension fund the aggregate
  amount of deferred special payments. These Regulations will then no
  longer apply.

## [Void Amendments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766035 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 10.1(2)(d) of the Act, the prescribed solvency ratio level is
one.

## [Statement of Investment Policies and Procedures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766037 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsection 7.2(1)
of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), the administrator shall review and
confirm or amend the statement of investment policies and procedures
referred to in subsection 7.1(1) of those Regulations at least once
every quarter.

## [Information to Be Provided]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766039 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 12(2) of the Act, the administrator shall file with the
Superintendent

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the financial statements of the employer; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the minutes of Board of Director meetings
  documenting results of reviews of the statement of investment policies
  and procedures.

<!-- -->

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  written statement to be provided under paragraph 28(1)(b) of the Act
  shall, in addition to the information referred to in subsection 23(1)
  of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate value of the subsidized portion of
    early retirement benefits consented to in the preceding plan year
    and the number of members who were granted subsidized early
    retirement in the preceding plan year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the rate of return of the plan's portfolio of
    investments and loans for the previous plan year and the asset mix
    as at the last plan year end;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount that the employer paid to the pension
    fund during the preceding plan year; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount that the employer would have paid to the
    pension fund in the absence of these Regulations based on the most
    recently filed actuarial report under subsection 12(2) of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The written statement required to be provided in
  accordance with paragraph 28(1)(b.1) of the Act shall include all
  information referred to in paragraphs (1)(b) to (d).

## [Termination of Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766050 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** On the termination of the
whole of the Canadian Press pension plan, the aggregate amount of
deferred special payments, less any reductions made under section 8,
shall be immediately paid to the pension fund.

## [Ceasing Funding]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766052 .Part}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  employer may cease to be governed by these Regulations if it gives
  written notice to the Superintendent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If notice is given on or before December 31, 2018

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} these Regulations, other than this subsection,
    cease to apply effective the first plan year following the date of
    the notice;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate amount of deferred special payments
    shall be paid into the pension fund in equal monthly instalments
    during the first plan year following the date of the notice; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the actuarial report required under
    subsection 12(2) of the Act shall not include the present value of
    the special payments determined under these Regulations.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If notice is given after December 31, 2018

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} these Regulations, other than this subsection,
    cease to apply effective the first plan year following the date of
    the notice;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate amount of deferred special payments
    shall be paid to the pension fund in equal monthly instalments
    during the first plan year following the date of the notice

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the special payments determined under section 7
    shall continue to be made.

## [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766063 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 13 of the
[Canadian Press Pension Plan Solvency Deficiency Funding
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2009-183), the obligation to pay or
remit deferred special payments to the pension fund under section 7 of
those Regulations is deferred until December 1, 2010. Special payments
and normal costs that have been deferred until that date and that have
not been paid into the pension fund shall be considered to be owed to
the pension fund under section 8 of the [Canadian Press Pension Plan
Solvency Deficiency Funding Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2009-183).

## [Cease to Be in Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766065 .Part}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations cease
to be in force on January 1, 2024.
