## Office of the Governor General's Secretary Employment Regulations {#office-of-the-governor-generals-secretary-employment-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2006-114
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2006-06-01

Office of the Governor General's Secretary Employment Regulations

P.C. 2006-435 2006-06-01
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Canadian Heritage and the Public Service Commission,
pursuant to section 21 of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Office of the Governor General's Secretary Employment
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-114).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e-ID0EDBAA){#fna_e}S.C.
  2003, c. 22, ss. 12 and 13
  :::
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-723634 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
the positions designated by position numbers 00025450 to 00025460 in the
Office of the Governor General's Secretary and the persons occupying
them that are excluded from the application of the [Public Service
Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), with the exception of the
definitions [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[public
service]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) and of sections 17 to
21, 54 and 55, 68 to 72, subsection 111(1), sections 112 to 116, section
118, sections 120 to 122, section 134 and 135 of that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-198, s. 1
- SOR/2017-81, s. 1
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-1-20100923.html "Link to previous version of section 1"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** Sections 17, 55, 72,
134 and 135 of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)
apply to the Governor General as if the Governor General was a deputy
head.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-198, s. 1
:::

**[[1.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.2 .sectionLabel}** Sections 112 to 116,
118, 120 to 122 and 135 of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) apply to a person occupying a position referred
to in section 1 as if they were an employee.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-198, s. 1
:::

## [Dealing with Excluded Positions and Persons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-723644 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Positions referred to in
section 1 may be filled by persons appointed by the Governor General or
a person authorized to do so by the Governor General.

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  occupying a position referred to in section 1 may only be dismissed or
  laid-off if the person is given at least one day's notice.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person may only be dismissed for cause.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person may only be laid off if the services of
  the person are no longer required in the Office by reason of lack of
  work or the discontinuance of a function.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A person occupying a
position referred to in section 1 ceases to be so employed 30 days after
the day on which the Governor General who held office at the time of the
person's appointment ceases to hold office.

**[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** A person who has been
employed for at least three years in one of the positions referred to in
section 1, or in one or more of those positions consecutively, may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} during a period of one year after they cease to be so
  employed, participate in an advertised appointment process for which
  the organizational criterion established under section 34 of the
  [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) entitles all
  employees to be considered, as long as they meet any other criteria
  established under that section; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} make a complaint under section 77 of that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-198, s. 2
:::
