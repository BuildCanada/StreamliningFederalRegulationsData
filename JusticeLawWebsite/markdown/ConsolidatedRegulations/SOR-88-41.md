## Railway Interswitching Regulations {#railway-interswitching-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-41
:::

[CANADA TRANSPORTATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10.4)

Registration 1987-12-17

Railway Interswitching Regulations

P.C. 1987-2640 1987-12-17
::::

::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the National Transportation Agency has taken into consideration
the reduction in costs that, in the opinion of the Agency, results from
moving a greater number of cars, or from transferring several cars, at
the same time;

And Whereas the Agency has considered the average variable costs of all
movements of traffic that are subject to the rates prescribed by the
annexed Regulations.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to
paragraph 137(1)(a) and subsection 152(4) of the National Transportation
Act, 1987[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_3792_hq_7590){#fn_3792_hq_7590-ID0EBBAA}, is
pleased hereby to approve the annexed Regulations respecting the
interswitching of rail traffic, made by the National Transportation
Agency.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-912794 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Transportation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[car block]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[car block]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means 60 or more cars that, as a
    block, are interswitched at an interchange and are destined to, or
    originate from a single shipper at a siding; ([rame de
    wagons]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[siding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[siding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a private siding that connects with a line of
      railway of a terminal carrier,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a team track of a terminal carrier,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a track where traffic may be loaded or unloaded
      directly from or into a shipper's facility abutting a terminal
      carrier's tracks,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a track for loading or unloading in a public
      stockyard, and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a point of origin or a point of destination, as
      those expressions are defined in section 87 of the Act,

    but does not include

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a track that is used by a terminal carrier for
      the transfer of traffic between cars or between a car and a
      warehouse owned by the terminal carrier, or

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a track that serves a reload or distribution
      compound, a container terminal or any other facility operated by a
      terminal carrier or its agent or for the terminal carrier's own
      purposes; ([voie d'évitement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[terminal carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[terminal carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a railway company
    that interswitches traffic at an interchange to or from a siding.
    ([transporteur de tête de ligne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-203, s. 1
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-912810 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations do not apply

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the operation of transferring
    traffic, where a terminal carrier has interswitched traffic to a
    siding for unloading in accordance with these Regulations and
    transfers the traffic to another siding at the request of a shipper
    or another railway company; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the operations and administration of
    a railway company if, in the immediately preceding three year
    period, the company derived at least 90% of its gross freight
    revenues from interswitching, according to the returns prepared by
    the company under regulations made under section 50 of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Agency determines that a railway company
  does not qualify for an exemption from the application of these
  Regulations under paragraph (1)(b) because the company derived less
  than 90 per cent of its gross freight revenues from interswitching in
  the immediately preceding three year period, these Regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} become applicable to the company 10 days after the
    day on which the Agency sends the company notification of that
    determination; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} apply to the company for a period of one year after
    the day on which they became applicable to the company under
    paragraph (a).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A railway company referred to in subsection (2) may,
  on the expiration of the period referred to in paragraph (2)(b),
  present to the Agency evidence that it qualifies for an exemption from
  the application of these Regulations under paragraph (1)(b).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Agency shall, within 60 days after receiving the
  evidence referred to in subsection (3), notify the company as to
  whether it qualifies for an exemption.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-912822 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A terminal carrier shall at
all times furnish to interswitched traffic a level of service equal to
the level of service accorded the terminal carrier's line haul traffic.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A railway company for whom
a terminal carrier interswitches a car shall deliver an empty car to the
terminal carrier at the interchange for delivery to a siding for
loading.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No charge shall be made by
a terminal carrier for the delivery of an empty car from an interchange
to a siding for loading or for the return of an empty car to an
interchange after unloading.

## [Interswitching Distance Zones]{.HTitleText1} {#h-912826 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  distances referred to in subsection (2), other than the distances
  referred to in subparagraph (2)(d)(i) and paragraph (2)(e), are
  measured along the track of the terminal carrier.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the following
  interswitching distance zones are established:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} interswitching distance zone 1, being a zone that
    includes sidings located wholly or partly within 6.4 km of an
    interchange;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} interswitching distance zone 2, being a zone that
    includes sidings located

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} wholly or partly within 10 km of an interchange,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} wholly outside interswitching distance zone 1;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} interswitching distance zone 3, being a zone that
    includes sidings located

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} wholly or partly within 20 km of an interchange,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} wholly outside interswitching distance zones l
      and 2;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} interswitching distance zone 4A, being a zone that
    includes sidings located

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} wholly or partly within a radius of 30 km of an
      interchange,

    - [(i.1)]{.lawlabel} wholly or partly within 40 km or less of an
      interchange, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} wholly outside interswitching distance zones 1,
      2 and 3;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} interswitching distance zone 4B, being a zone that
    includes sidings that are

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} wholly or partly within a radius of 30 km of an
      interchange, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} wholly outside interswitching distance zones 1,
      2, 3 and 4A.
