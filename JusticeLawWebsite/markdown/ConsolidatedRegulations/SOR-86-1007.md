## One-day Local Employment Seminars Fee or Charge Order {#one-day-local-employment-seminars-fee-or-charge-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-1007
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1986-09-26

Order Prescribing the Fees or Charges To Be Paid by Participants to
One-Day Local Employer Seminars Hosted by Canada Employment Centres
Across Canada
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Employment and Immigration, pursuant to Order in Council
P.C. 1986-2103 of September 11, 1986[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EDBCA}, hereby makes
the annexed Order prescribing the fees or charges to be paid by
participants to one-day local employer seminars hosted by Canada
Employment Centres across Canada.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e-ID0EDBCA){#footnote_e}SI/86-179,
  1986 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 4245
  :::

Ottawa, September 23, 1986

::: FlushRight
BENOIT BOUCHARD
:::

::: FlushRight
*Minister of Employment and Immigration*
:::
:::::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888242 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [One-Day Local Employment Seminars Fee or Charge
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1007).

## [Fee or Charge]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888244 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The fee or charge to be
paid by each participant to the one-day local employer seminars hosted
by Canada Employment Centres across Canada is \$25.
