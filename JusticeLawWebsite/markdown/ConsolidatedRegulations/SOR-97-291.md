## Tailored Collar Shirts Remission Order, 1997 {#tailored-collar-shirts-remission-order-1997 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-291
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1997-06-17

Tailored Collar Shirts Remission Order, 1997

P.C. 1997-830 1997-06-17
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 101 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Tailored Collar Shirts Remission Order,
1997](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-291).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnote1_e}R.S., c. 41
  (3rd Supp.)
  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005843 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order, [[tailored
collar shirt]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a man's or boy's fabric shirt
of heading No. 62.05, with a collar that consists of one or more pieces
of material that are cut and sewn or fused and that is designed with two
pointed or round ends.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 28
:::

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005847 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 to 5,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) to a tailored
collar shirt manufacturer set out in the schedule in respect of tailored
collar shirts imported into Canada by the manufacturer during the period
beginning on July 1, 1997 and ending on December 31, 2004.

- SOR/98-28, s. 29

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005875 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission granted under
section 2 shall not exceed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the remission is claimed in respect of tailored
  collar shirts imported during the period beginning on July 1, 1997 and
  ending on December 31, 1997, an amount equal to one half of the total
  amount of customs duties remitted to the manufacturer under the
  Tailored Collar Shirts Remission Order, 1988 in respect of shirts
  imported during 1995; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the remission is claimed in respect of tailored
  collar shirts imported during any calendar year beginning on or after
  January 1, 1998 and ending on or before December 31, 2012, an amount
  equal to the total amount of customs duties remitted to the
  manufacturer under the Tailored Collar Shirts Remission Order, 1988 in
  respect of shirts imported during 1995.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-22, s. 29(F)
- SOR/2005-9, s. 2
- SOR/2008-256, s. 4
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission shall not be
granted under section 2 in respect of tailored collar shirts imported
during the period beginning on July 1, 1997 and ending on December 31,
1997 where the total amount of the customs duties remitted to the
manufacturer under this Order and the Tailored Collar Shirts Remission
Order, 1988 in respect of those shirts would exceed the total amount of
customs duties remitted to that manufacturer under the Tailored Collar
Shirts Remission Order, 1988 in respect of shirts imported during 1995.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on
condition that a claim for remission is made to the Minister within five
years after the day on which the tailored collar shirts are imported
into Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-315, s. 1
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005885 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on July 1, 1997.
