## Nisga'a Final Agreement Indian Remission Order {#nisgaa-final-agreement-indian-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2000-39
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2000-05-24

Nisga'a Final Agreement Indian Remission Order

P.C. 2000-664 2000-05-05
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of Finance, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed
[Nisga'a Final Agreement Indian Remission

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604827 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[former reserve lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[former reserve lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means lands described
    in paragraph 2(b) of Chapter 3 of the Nisga'a Final Agreement that
    are within Nisga'a Lands. ([anciennes terres de
    réserve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([Indien]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Nisga'a Final Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Nisga'a Final Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in subsection 2(1) of the [Nisga'a Final Agreement
    Act](/eng/acts/N-23.3). ([Accord définitif nisga'a]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Nisga'a Lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Nisga'a Lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    Chapter 1 of the Nisga'a Final Agreement. ([Terres
    Nisga'a]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604833 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted of any tax imposed or levied in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the estate or interest of an Indian in former reserve
  lands;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the personal property of an Indian situated on former
  reserve lands; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an Indian's ownership, occupation, possession or use
  of any property referred to in paragraph (a) or (b).

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604838 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission under section 2
is granted only where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax has not otherwise been rebated, refunded or
  remitted; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the property referred to in paragraph 2(a) or (b), or
  the Indian in respect of the ownership, occupation, possession or use
  of the property referred to in paragraph 2(a) or (b), would, but for
  the Nisga'a Final Agreement, be exempt from taxation by reason of the
  applicability of section 87 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604842 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The remission is applicable
during the period beginning on the day on which the [Nisga'a Final
Agreement Act](/eng/acts/N-23.3) comes into force and ending on

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} May 31, 2008, in the case of transaction taxes; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} December 31, 2012, in the case of all other taxes.
