## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-745166 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Disability Savings
    Act](/eng/acts/C-3.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[assistance holdback amount]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assistance holdback amount]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, at a
    particular time,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an RDSP that is, at the particular
      time, a specified disability savings plan, nil; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the total amount of bonds and
      grants paid into an RDSP within the 10-year period before the
      particular time, less any amount of bond or grant paid in that
      10-year period that has been repaid to the Minister. ([montant de
      retenue]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canada Disability Savings Bond.
    ([bon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[grant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[grant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canada Disability Savings
    Grant. ([subvention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[issuer agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[issuer agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement entered
    into by the Minister and an issuer of an RDSP that relates to the
    payment of a grant or bond. ([convention
    d'émetteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[RDSP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[RDSP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registered disability savings
    plan under section 146.4 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).
    ([REEI]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Requirements for Payment of Grant]{.HTitleText1} {#h-745178 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may pay a
grant into an RDSP in respect of a contribution made to and not
withdrawn from the RDSP if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the issuer enters into an issuer agreement with the
  Minister that applies to the RDSP and includes the terms and
  conditions set out in section 4;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the issuer submits, at the request of the holder of
  the RDSP, an application for the grant to the Minister;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the beneficiary is less than 49 years of age at the
  end of the year preceding the year in which the contribution is made;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total of the contribution and all other
  contributions made to an RDSP of the beneficiary does not exceed
  \$200,000;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the beneficiary is a DTC-eligible individual in
  respect of the year in which the contribution is made, and in respect
  of the year or years to which the contribution is allocated; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the issuer complies with the requirements of these
  Regulations and the terms and conditions of the issuer agreement that
  applies to the RDSP.

## [Requirements for Payment of Bond]{.HTitleText1} {#h-745188 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may pay a bond
into an RDSP if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the issuer enters into an issuer agreement with the
  Minister that applies to the RDSP and includes the terms and
  conditions set out in section 4;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder requests, no later than December 31 of the
  year in which the beneficiary attains 49 years of age, that the issuer
  submit an application for the bond;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the issuer submits an application for the bond to the
  Minister;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the beneficiary is less than 49 years of age at the
  end of the year preceding the year for which the bond is payable;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the beneficiary is a DTC-eligible individual in
  respect of the year for which the bond is payable; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the issuer complies with the requirements of these
  Regulations and the terms and conditions of the issuer agreement that
  applies to the RDSP.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2013-131, s. 2
- SOR/2014-134, s. 2

## [Terms and Conditions of Issuer Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-745198 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every issuer agreement
shall include the following terms and conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the issuer shall provide the Minister with any
  information that the Minister requires for the purposes of the Act and
  these Regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the issuer shall maintain records and books of
  account that relate to the amounts paid under the Act in the form and
  containing any information that the Minister requires to ensure
  compliance with the Act and these Regulations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the issuer shall allow the Minister access to all
  documents and other information that the Minister requires for
  auditing amounts paid or repaid under the Act and these Regulations;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the issuer shall report to the Minister annually or
  within any other period set out in the issuer agreement with respect
  to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all contributions, payments and transfers to, and
    all payments and transfers from, an RDSP,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the assistance holdback amount, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any other information related to the RDSP that is
    specified in the issuer agreement;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the issuer shall submit all information to the
  Minister in a format and manner that is acceptable to the Minister;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the issuer shall not charge fees related to the RDSP
  against the assistance holdback amount of the RDSP;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the issuer shall, when transferring the property of
  the RDSP, provide to the issuer of the new plan all information that
  it is required to provide in accordance with paragraph 146.4(8)(c) of
  the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the issuer shall repay any amount required to be
  repaid to the Minister under these Regulations and shall do so within
  the period specified in the agreement.

## [Repayments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-745213 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 5.1, an issuer of an RDSP shall repay to the Minister,
  within the period set out in the issuer agreement, the amount referred
  to in subsection (2) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the RDSP is terminated;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the plan ceases to be an RDSP as a result of the
    application of paragraph 146.4(10)(a) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2021, c. 23, s. 95\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the beneficiary dies.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount that must be repaid as a result of the
  occurrence of an event described in subsection (1) is the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fair market value, immediately before the
    occurrence, of the property held by the RDSP, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the assistance holdback amount of the RDSP
    immediately before the occurrence.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (1) and (2), if the beneficiary
  of an RDSP that is a specified disability savings plan dies, the
  issuer of the RDSP shall repay to the Minister, within the period set
  out in the issuer agreement, any portion of an amount paid into the
  RDSP as a grant or bond within the 10-year period preceding the time
  of the death that remains in the RDSP at that time.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply if the event described in
  subsection (1) or (3) occurs after the calendar year in which the
  beneficiary attains 59 years of age.

**[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** If an event described
in paragraph 5(1)(a), (b) or (d) occurs while the beneficiary of an RDSP
is no longer a DTC-eligible individual, the issuer of the RDSP shall
repay to the Minister, within the period set out in the issuer
agreement, the lesser of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fair market value, immediately before the
  occurrence of the event, of the property held by the RDSP, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#745229 .Paragraph}
  A + B -- C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is
      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the event occurs before the calendar year in
        which the beneficiary attains 51 years of age, the total amount
        of grants and bonds paid into the RDSP within the 10-year period
        before the day on which the beneficiary ceased to be a
        DTC-eligible individual, less any portion of that amount that
        was repaid to the Minister within that period,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the event occurs after the calendar year in
        which the beneficiary attains 50 years of age but before the
        calendar year in which they attain 60 years of age and the
        beneficiary ceased to be a DTC-eligible individual before the
        calendar year in which they attained 50 years of age, the total
        amount of grants and bonds paid into the RDSP within the period
        (expressed in number of years) determined by the following
        formula that ended before the day on which the beneficiary
        ceased to be a DTC-eligible individual, less any portion of that
        amount that was repaid to the Minister within that period:

        ::: {#1309758 .NestedFormula}
        60 -- n

        where

        [n]{.dfn}
        :   is the beneficiary's age on --- or the age that they would
            have attained by --- December 31 of the calendar year in
            which the event occurs,
        :::

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the event occurs after the calendar year
        in which the beneficiary attains 50 years of age but before the
        calendar year in which they attain 60 years of age and the
        beneficiary ceased to be a DTC-eligible individual after the
        calendar year in which they attained 49 years of age, the total
        amount of grants and bonds paid into the RDSP during the period
        beginning on January 1 of the year that is 10 years before the
        year in which the event occurs and ending on the day preceding
        the day on which the beneficiary ceased to be a DTC-eligible
        individual, less any portion of that amount that was repaid to
        the Minister within that period, or

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if the event occurs after the calendar year in
        which the beneficiary attains 59 years of age, nil,

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of any grant or bond that is paid into the RDSP
      during the period beginning on the day on which the beneficiary
      ceased to be a DTC-eligible individual and ending on the day on
      which the event occurs, and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of any grant or bond that has been repaid since the
      day on which the beneficiary ceased to be a DTC-eligible
      individual.
  :::

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
calculating an amount to be repaid under these Regulations with respect
to amounts that are transferred from a prior RDSP to a new RDSP in
accordance with subsection 146.4(8) of the [Income Tax
Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), all amounts of bonds, grants and contributions
transferred are considered to have been paid into or made to the new
RDSP as of the day on which the amounts were paid into or made to the
prior RDSP.

## [Waiver --- Undue Hardship]{.HTitleText1} {#h-745272 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 11 of the Act, the Minister may waive the requirement in
subparagraph 6(2)(a)(i) or 7(2)(a)(i) or (b)(i) of the Act that the
individual be at least 18 years of age on December 31 of the year
preceding the particular year if the beneficiary is not a qualified
dependant of an eligible individual.

## [Authorized Collection of Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-745276 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 15 of the Act, the following are prescribed information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the beneficiary's name, address, date of birth,
  social insurance number and, if applicable, date of death;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the holder is an individual, the holder's name,
  address and social insurance number;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the holder is a department, agency or institution
  that maintains the beneficiary, its business name, business number and
  address;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the issuer;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in relation to any specific RDSP, the number assigned
  to the corresponding plan in respect of which written notification was
  given by the Minister of National Revenue in accordance with
  paragraph 146.4(2)(a) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number assigned to the disability savings plan
  entered into between the issuer and the holder;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the disability savings plan between
  the issuer and the holder was entered into;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the disability savings plan entered
  into between the issuer and the holder ends;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date and amount of contributions paid into the
  RDSP;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the total amount of contributions paid into the RDSP;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the amount of total earnings in the RDSP;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} if there is a transfer of amounts from a prior RDSP
  to a new RDSP,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amounts transferred as well as the book value
    and fair market value of those amounts,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the transfer date,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the new issuer,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} with respect to both the prior RDSP and new RDSP,
    the number assigned to the corresponding plan in respect of which
    written notification was given by the Minister of National Revenue
    in accordance with paragraph 146.4(2)(a) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} with respect to both the prior RDSP and new RDSP,
    the number assigned to the disability savings plan entered into
    between the issuer and the holder;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} if there is a new holder of an RDSP after the RDSP is
  entered into, the new holder's name, address and social insurance
  number or business number, as the case may be, and the day on which
  they became the new holder of the RDSP;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} the disability assistance payments made, indicating
  the taxable and non-taxable portions of the payments and the date of
  each payment made;

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} the fair market value of the RDSP;

- [(p)]{.lawlabel} if a contribution is withdrawn subsequent to a waiver
  granted by the Minister of National Revenue in accordance with
  paragraph 146.4(12)(c) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), the
  amount and date of the contribution withdrawn; and

- [(q)]{.lawlabel} whether or not a beneficiary is a DTC-eligible
  individual.
