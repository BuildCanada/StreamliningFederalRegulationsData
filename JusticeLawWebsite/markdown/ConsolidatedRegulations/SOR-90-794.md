## Baker Lake Airport Zoning Regulations {#baker-lake-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-794
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1990-11-22

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Baker Lake Airport

P.C. 1990-2476 1990-11-22
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 5.5[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EHABA} of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed Zoning
Regulations respecting Baker Lake Airport, substantially in the form set
out in the schedule hereto, was published in two successive issues of
the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on February 10th
and 17th, 1990, and in two successive issues of the News/North on
March 12th, and 19th, 1990, and a reasonable opportunity was thereby
afforded to interested persons to make representations to the Minister
of Transport with respect thereto;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to
section 5.4[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EFBAA} of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting zoning at Baker Lake
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-794).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931162 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Baker Lake Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-794).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931164 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Baker Lake Airport, in
      the vicinity of Baker Lake, in the Northwest Territories;
      ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part II of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/93-401, s.
      2\]]{.Repealed}

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, prepared for
      the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
      which strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the airport reference point is 3.0 m above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931177 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, which lands are more particularly described in
Part VI of the schedule.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931179 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point shown on Baker Lake Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.2670 dated August 24, 1988, is a point located on the centre line
of runway 161-341 distant 640.0 m from the threshold of runway 341.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Baker Lake Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.2670, dated August 24, 1988, are surfaces abutting each end of the
strip associated with the runway designated 161-341, and are described
as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 161 consisting of an inclined plane having a
  ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally rising
  to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 3 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 525 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 75.0 m measured vertically above the elevation
  at the end of the strip; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 341 consisting of an inclined plane having a
  ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally rising
  to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 3 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 525 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 75.0 m measured vertically above the elevation
  at the end of the strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Baker Lake Airport Zoning Plan No. E.2670,
dated August 24, 1988, is an imaginary surface located at a common plane
established at a constant elevation of 45 m above the elevation of the
airport reference point, except that, where that common plane is less
than 9 m above the surface of the ground, the outer surface is an
imaginary surface located at 9 m above the surface of the ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strip .SchedHeadL1}

The strip associated with runway 161-341, shown on Baker Lake Airport
Zoning Plan No. E.2670 dated August 24, 1988, is 150 m in width, 75 m
being on each side of the centre line of the runway and 1 400 m in
length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Baker Lake Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.2670 dated August 24, 1988, is a surface consisting of an inclined
plane rising at a ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 7 m measured
horizontally at right angles to the centre line and projected centre
line of the strip and extending upward and outward from the lateral
limits of the strip and its approach surfaces to an intersection with
the outer surface.

### [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Lands to Which These Regulations Apply]{.HTitleText1} {#part-videscription-of-the-lands-to-which-these-regulations-apply .SchedHeadL1}

The boundary of the outer limits of lands, shown on Baker Lake Airport
Zoning Plan No. E.2670 dated August 24, 1988, is described as follows:

COMMENCING at a point on the southeasterly boundary of Baker Lake
Settlement, Plan 567 Yellowknife Land Titles Office, where it is
intersected by the circumference of a circle having a radius of
4 000 metres and centered at the airport reference point as described in
Part I of the schedule;

THENCE southeasterly following the circumference of the said circle
clockwise to its intersection with the northeasterly boundary of Baker
Lake Settlement, Plan 567 Yellowknife Land Titles;

THENCE southeasterly and southwesterly along the northeasterly and
southeasterly limits of Plan 567 Yellowknife Land Titles Office, to its
intersection with the northeasterly limit of Lot 1 000, Quad 66 A/8,
Plan 1269 Yellowknife Land Titles;

THENCE northwesterly along the northeasterly limit of Lot 1 000,
Quad 66 A/8 to the most northerly corner of said Lot 1 000, Quad 66 A/8;

THENCE southwesterly along the northwesterly limit of Lot 1 000,
Quad 66 A/8 to the most westerly corner of said Lot 1 000, Quad 66 A/8;

THENCE southeasterly along the southwesterly limit of Lot 1 000,
Quad 66 A/8 to its intersection with the southeasterly boundary of Baker
Lake Settlement, Plan 567 Yellowknife Land Titles;

THENCE southwesterly along the southeasterly limit of Baker Lake
Settlement, Plan 567 Yellowknife Land Titles, to the point of
commencement.
