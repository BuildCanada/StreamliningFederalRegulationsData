## Organization of American States Privileges and Immunities in Canada Order {#organization-of-american-states-privileges-and-immunities-in-canada-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/99-350
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Registration 1999-08-26

Organization of American States Privileges and Immunities in Canada
Order

P.C. 1999-1491  1999-08-26
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to paragraphs 5(1)(a), (b),
(c), (g) and (h) of the [Foreign Missions and International
Organizations Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-99-350_e_hq_5622){#fn_SOR-99-350_e_hq_5622-ID0EDBAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Organization of American States Privileges and
Immunities in Canada Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-350).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031858 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in Schedule
    III to the [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Organization of
    American States. ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031862 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Organization shall have the legal capacities of a body corporate and
  the privileges and immunities set out in Articles II and III of the
  Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Representatives of foreign states that are members of
  the Organization shall have, during their journey to and from the
  place of a meeting of the Organization in Canada, as well as in
  Canada,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the following privileges and immunities set out in
    Section 11 of Article IV of the Convention, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} immunity from personal arrest or detention and
      from seizure of their personal baggage, and, in respect of words
      spoken or written and all acts done by them in their capacity as
      representatives, immunity from legal process of every kind,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} inviolability for all papers and documents,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the right to use codes and to receive papers or
      correspondence by courier or in sealed bags,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} exemption from immigration restrictions, alien
      registration and national service obligations in Canada when
      visiting or passing through Canada in the exercise of their
      functions,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the same immunities and facilities in respect of
      their personal baggage as are accorded to diplomatic envoys, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} such other privileges, immunities and facilities
      not inconsistent with the foregoing as diplomatic envoys enjoy,
      except that they shall have no right to claim exemption from
      customs duties on goods imported (otherwise than as part of their
      personal baggage) or from excise duties or sales taxes; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the privileges and immunities set out in Sections
    12, 13 and 14 of Article IV of the Convention subject to Sections 15
    and 16 of that Article.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Officials of the Organization who are not Canadian
  citizens or permanent residents shall have the following privileges
  and immunities set out in Section 18 of Article V of the Convention:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immunity from legal process in respect of words
    spoken or written and all acts performed by them in their official
    capacity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} exemption from taxation on the salaries and
    emoluments paid to them by the Organization;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} immunity from national service obligations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} immunity from immigration restrictions and alien
    registration;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the same privileges in respect of exchange
    facilities as are accorded to the officials of comparable ranks
    forming part of diplomatic missions to the Government of Canada;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the same repatriation facilities in time of
    international crisis as diplomatic envoys; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the right to import free of duty their furniture
    and effects at the time of first taking up their post in Canada.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Officials of the Organization, who are Canadian
  citizens or permanent residents, shall have the following privileges
  and immunities set out in Section 18 of Article V of the Convention:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immunity from legal process in respect of words
    spoken or written and all acts performed by them in their official
    capacity; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the same privileges in respect of exchange
    facilities as are accorded to the officials of comparable ranks
    forming part of diplomatic missions to the Government of Canada.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Experts who are not Canadian citizens or permanent
  residents and who are performing missions for the Organization shall
  have, during their journey to and from the place of their mission in
  Canada, as well as in Canada, the privileges and immunities set out in
  Article VI of the Convention.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Experts who are Canadian citizens or permanent
  residents and who are performing missions for the Organization shall
  have, during their journey to and from the place of their mission in
  Canada, as well as in Canada, the following privileges and immunities
  set out in Section 22 of Article VI of the Convention, subject to the
  restrictions set out in Section 23 of that Article:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of words spoken or written and acts done
    by them in the course of the performance of their mission, immunity
    from legal process of every kind, which immunity shall continue to
    be accorded notwithstanding that the persons concerned are no longer
    employed on missions for the Organization; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inviolability for all papers and documents.
