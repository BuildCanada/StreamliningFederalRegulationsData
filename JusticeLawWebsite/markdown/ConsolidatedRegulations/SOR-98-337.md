## Senate Sessional Allowance (Deductions for Non-attendance) Regulations {#senate-sessional-allowance-deductions-for-non-attendance-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-337
:::

[PARLIAMENT OF CANADA ACT](/eng/acts/P-1)

Registration 1998-06-10

Senate Sessional Allowance (Deductions for Non-attendance) Regulations
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Senate, pursuant to section 59 of the [Parliament of Canada
Act](/eng/acts/P-1), hereby makes the annexed Senate Sessional Allowance
(Deductions for Non-attendance) Regulations.

June 9, 1998
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022516 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The deduction to be made
from the sessional allowance of a senator under subsection 57(1) of the
[Parliament of Canada Act](/eng/acts/P-1) is increased to \$250 per day
for every sitting day beyond twenty-one on which the senator does not
attend a sitting of the Senate.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-23, s. 1
- err.(F), Vol. 136, No. 2
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022520 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on July 1, 1998.
