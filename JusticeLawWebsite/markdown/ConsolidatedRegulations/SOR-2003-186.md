## [Prescribed Holding Body Corporate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-700270 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 163(2) of the [Trust and Loan Companies
Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), a holding body corporate of a foreign
institution is prescribed if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holding body corporate is incorporated or formed
  otherwise than by or under an Act of Parliament or of the legislature
  of a province; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holding body corporate, and entities it controls
  are, when viewed as a whole, engaged primarily in the business of
  providing financial services.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-700274 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
