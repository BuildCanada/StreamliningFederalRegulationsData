## Royal Canadian Mounted Police Case Examination Fees (Bermuda) Regulations {#royal-canadian-mounted-police-case-examination-fees-bermuda-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-421
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1989-08-24

Regulations Respecting the Fees To Be Paid by the Commissioner of the
Bermuda Police Force for Services Provided by the Forensic Laboratory
Services and Identification Services of the Royal Canadian Mounted
Police

P.C. 1988-1591 1989-08-24
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that the cost of
services provided to persons by the Royal Canadian Mounted Police should
be borne by the persons using those services.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Solicitor General of Canada and the Treasury
Board, pursuant to section 19 of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to revoke Order in Council P.C.
1980-14/2323 of August 27, 1980[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA} and, pursuant to
paragraph 19(a) of the said act, to make the annexed [Regulations
respecting the fees to be paid by the Commissioner of the Bermuda Police
Force for services provided by the Forensic Laboratory Services and
Identification Services of the Royal Canadian Mounted
Police](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-421).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920254 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Case Examination Fees
(Bermuda) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-421).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920256 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[case examination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[case examination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the examination,
    photography and analysis of exhibits that have been collected during
    an investigation by the Bermuda Police Force, which investigation
    has been authorized by the Commissioner of the Bermuda Police Force
    and are presented to the Royal Canadian Mounted Police at one time
    as part of a single investigation; ([examen
    judiciaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commissioner of the Bermuda Police Force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commissioner of the Bermuda Police Force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means the person who is in charge of the operation of the police
    force for Bermuda; ([commissaire de la police des
    Bermudes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member as defined in the
    [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10).
    ([membre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920261 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner of the
Bermuda Police Force shall pay the following fees expressed in Canadian
dollars for the services specified:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each case examination performed by the Forensic
  Laboratory Services of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, one thousand
  dollars;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each case examination performed by the
  Identification Services of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, two
  hundred and fifty dollars; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the presence in Bermuda of a member for the
  purpose of testifying in court in respect of a service provided
  pursuant to paragraph (a) or (b),

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} one hundred and seventy-five dollars per day,
    including travelling time, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all transportation, lodging and meal expenses of
    the member that relate to the court appearance, at Government of
    Canada rates as set out in the Treasury Board Personnel Management
    Manual, Chapter 370, Appendix C, dated April 1989.
