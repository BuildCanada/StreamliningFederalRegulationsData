## Gross Revenue Insurance Program Account for the Province of Alberta Remission Order {#gross-revenue-insurance-program-account-for-the-province-of-alberta-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2002-61
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2002-04-10

Gross Revenue Insurance Program Account for the Province of Alberta
Remission Order

P.C. 2002-485  2002-03-22
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food and the Treasury Board,
considering that it is in the public interest to do so, hereby remits to
the province of Alberta, pursuant to subsection 23(2.1) of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), any interest accrued
until March 31, 2002 on the federal share of the Gross Revenue Insurance
Program Account for the Province of Alberta.
:::
