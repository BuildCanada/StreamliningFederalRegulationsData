## Proclamation Fixing Valuation Days {#proclamation-fixing-valuation-days .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 946
:::

[INCOME TAX ACT](/eng/acts/I-3.3)

Proclamation Fixing Valuation Days
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Know You that We, by and with the advice of Our Privy Council for
Canada, do by this Our Proclamation

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix December 22, 1971 as the day in relation to any
  property referred to in paragraph 24(a) of the Income Tax Application
  Rules, 1971 for the purposes of subdivision c of Division B of Part I
  of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} fix December 31, 1971 as the day in relation to any
  property referred to in paragraph 24(b) of those Rules for the
  purposes of that subdivision.
