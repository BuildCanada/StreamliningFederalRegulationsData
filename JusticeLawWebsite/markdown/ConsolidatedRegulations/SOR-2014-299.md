## [Rules of Origin]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809882 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following provisions of
the Canada-Korea Free Trade Agreement have the force of law in Canada:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Articles 3.1 to 3.6;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Paragraphs 1 and 2 of Article 3.7;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Articles 3.8 to 3.17;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Article 3.20; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} Annex 3-A.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809889 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Ind3F82_hq_15048){#fn_Ind3F82_hq_15048-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 47 of the [Canada--Korea Economic Growth and Prosperity
Act](/eng/acts/C-6.48), chapter 28 of the Statutes of Canada, 2014,
comes into force, but if they are registered after that day, they come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
