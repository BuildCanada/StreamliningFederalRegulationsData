## Execution of Purchase of Property Documents Regulations {#execution-of-purchase-of-property-documents-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1590
:::

[VETERANS' LAND ACT](/eng/acts/V-1.5)

Regulations Authorizing Certain Persons to Execute Documents for the
Purchase of Property on Behalf of the Director, the Veterans' Land Act
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530511 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Execution of Purchase of Property Documents
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1590).

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530513 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
acquiring property pursuant to sections 8, 13, 54 and 71 to 73 of the
Act, the power and duty of The Director, The Veterans' Land Act, to
execute the documents mentioned in the schedule relative to the purposes
aforesaid is hereby delegated to the persons named in the schedule who
shall exercise the delegated power and duty by signing their respective
names for and on behalf of the Regional Director or Acting Regional
Director.
