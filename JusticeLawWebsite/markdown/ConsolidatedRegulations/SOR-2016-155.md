## Expiry of Sections 10 to 12 and 13 to 15 of the Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006 Regulations {#expiry-of-sections-10-to-12-and-13-to-15-of-the-softwood-lumber-products-export-charge-act-2006-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2016-155
:::

[SOFTWOOD LUMBER PRODUCTS EXPORT CHARGE ACT, 2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55)

Registration 2016-06-17

Expiry of Sections 10 to 12 and 13 to 15 of the Softwood Lumber Products
Export Charge Act, 2006 Regulations

P.C. 2016-572 2016-06-17
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 101(a) and
section 102 of the [Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act,
2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDABA}, makes the annexed [Expiry
of Sections 10 to 12 and 13 to 15 of the Softwood Lumber Products Export
Charge Act, 2006 Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-155).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EDABA){#fn_a}S.C.
  2006, c. 13
  :::
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ceasing to be in Force

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Sections 10 to 12 and 13 to
15 of the [Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act,
2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55) cease to be in force on October 12, 2015.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
deemed to have come into force on October 13, 2015.
