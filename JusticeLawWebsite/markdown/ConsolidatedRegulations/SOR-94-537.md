## Royal Canadian Mounted Police Automated Fingerprint Identification System Fees Regulations {#royal-canadian-mounted-police-automated-fingerprint-identification-system-fees-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-537
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1994-08-04

Regulations Prescribing the Fees To Be Paid by the Users of the Royal
Canadian Mounted Police Automated Fingerprint Identification System

P.C. 1994-1340  1994-08-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Solicitor General of Canada and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
paragraph 19(1)(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-94-537_e_hq_6299){#fn_SOR-94-537_e_hq_6299-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed [Regulations prescribing the fees to be paid by the
users of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Automated Fingerprint
Identification System](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-537).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964328 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Automated Fingerprint
Identification System Fees Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-537).

## [Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964330 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The fee to be paid by law
enforcement agencies for the use of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police
Automated Fingerprint Identification System is \$33.79 annually per
sworn police officer.
