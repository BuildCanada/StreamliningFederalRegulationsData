## Order Extending the Application of Section 14 of the Citizenship Act {#order-extending-the-application-of-section-14-of-the-citizenship-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2023-24
:::

[CITIZENSHIP ACT](/eng/acts/C-29)

Registration 2023-07-19

Order Extending the Application of Section 14 of the Citizenship Act
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, by the [Order Extending the Application of Section 14 of that
Act for a Period of Five Years](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-82)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673371464){#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673371464-ID0EIEEA},
the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, under subsection
28.1(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673372333){#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673372333-ID0EGEEA}
of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673371523){#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673371523-ID0EDEEA},
extended the application of section 14[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1675278300){#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1675278300-ID0EBEEA}
of that Act for a period of five years, ending on July 31, 2024;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673371464-ID0EIEEA){#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673371464}SOR/2019-82
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673372333-ID0EGEEA){#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673372333}S.C.
  2014, c. 22, s. 27
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673371523-ID0EDEEA){#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673371523}R.S., c.
  C-29
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1675278300-ID0EBEEA){#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1675278300}S.C.
  2017, c. 14, s. 8
  :::

And whereas, under subsection 28.1(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673372333){#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673372333-ID0EDADA}
of that Act, the Minister may, before the expiry of each extended
period, extend the application of section 14[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1675278300){#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1675278300-ID0EBADA}
for up to five years;

Therefore, the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, under subsection
28.1(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673372333){#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673372333-ID0EGACA}
of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673371523){#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673371523-ID0EDACA},
extends the application of section 14[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1675278300){#fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1675278300-ID0EBACA}
of that Act until July 31, 2027.

Ottawa, June 27, 2023
