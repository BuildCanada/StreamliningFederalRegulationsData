**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Nuclear Safety and Control
    Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Category I nuclear material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Category I nuclear material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a nuclear
    substance listed in column 1 of Schedule 1 that is in the
    corresponding form set out in column 2 and the corresponding
    quantity set out in column 3 of Schedule 1. ([matière nucléaire de
    catégorie I]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Category II nuclear material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Category II nuclear material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a nuclear
    substance listed in column 1 of Schedule 1 that is in the
    corresponding form set out in column 2 and the corresponding
    quantity set out in column 4 of Schedule 1. ([matière nucléaire de
    catégorie II]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Category III nuclear material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Category III nuclear material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    nuclear substance listed in column 1 of Schedule 1 that is in the
    corresponding form set out in column 2 and the corresponding
    quantity set out in column 5 of Schedule 1. ([matière nucléaire de
    catégorie III]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[design basis threat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[design basis threat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    characteristics of a potential adversary in respect of which
    countermeasures are incorporated into the design and evaluation of a
    physical protection system. ([menace de référence]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[direct visual surveillance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[direct visual surveillance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means direct
    observation by a person who is physically present at the place that
    is under observation. ([surveillance visuelle
    directe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[effective intervention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[effective intervention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an intervention
    that is timely and powerful enough to prevent a person or group of
    persons, including those equipped with weapons or explosive
    substances, from committing sabotage or from removing Category I, II
    or III nuclear material otherwise than in accordance with a
    licence. ([défense efficace]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[explosive substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[explosive substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} anything intended to be used to make a substance
      capable of producing an explosion, a detonation or a pyrotechnic
      effect;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} anything, or any part of any thing, used or
      intended to be used or adapted to cause, or to aid in causing, an
      explosion in or with a substance referred to in paragraph (a); and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an incendiary grenade, firebomb, Molotov cocktail
      or other similar incendiary substance or device and a delaying
      mechanism or other thing intended for use in connection with such
      a substance or device. ([substance explosive]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[high-security site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[high-security site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a nuclear power
    plant or a nuclear facility where Category I or II nuclear material
    is processed, used or stored. ([site à sécurité
    élevée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inner area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inner area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area inside a protected
    area that is surrounded by a barrier or structure that meets the
    requirements of section 13. ([zone intérieure]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in this section and sections 2 to 7.2, a person
      who is licensed to carry on an activity described in any of
      paragraphs 26(a), (b), (e) or (f) of the Act in relation to
      Category I, II or III nuclear material or a nuclear power plant;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in sections 7.3 to 38, a person who is licensed
      to carry on an activity described in any of paragraphs 26(a),
      (b), (e) or (f) of the Act in relation to a high-security site;
      and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in Part 2, a person who is licensed to carry on
      an activity described in any of paragraphs 26(a), (b) or (e) of
      the Act in relation to a nuclear facility set out in column 2 of
      Schedule 2. ([titulaire de permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nuclear power plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear power plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a nuclear facility
    consisting of any fission-reactor installation that has been
    constructed to generate electricity on a commercial
    scale. ([centrale nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nuclear security guard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear security guard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2006-191, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

[[nuclear security officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear security officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
    whose function is to provide security at a high-security site and to
    whom an authorization referred to in subsection 18(2) has been
    issued. ([agent de sécurité nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[off-site response force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[off-site response force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a local,
    provincial or federal police service whose members are not located
    at a nuclear facility. ([force d'intervention
    externe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[on-site nuclear response force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[on-site nuclear response force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a team of nuclear security officers whose members
      are

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} trained in the use of firearms, authorized to
        carry firearms in Canada and qualified to use them, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} permanently located at a high-security site;
        or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a local, provincial or federal police service, a
      Canadian Forces unit or any other force

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} under contract to a licensee,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} whose members are trained in the use of
        firearms, authorized to carry firearms in Canada and qualified
        to use them, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} whose members are permanently located at a
        high-security site. ([force d'intervention nucléaire
        interne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Personnel Security Standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Personnel Security Standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    document entitled Chapter 2 --- 4 --- Personnel Security Standard,
    published by the Treasury Board Secretariat and dated June 9, 1994,
    as amended from time to time. ([Norme sur la sécurité du
    personnel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[physical protection measure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[physical protection measure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an element
    or a combination of elements in place at a nuclear facility for its
    protection --- or for the protection of nuclear substances at the
    facility --- against potential adversaries. ([mesure de protection
    physique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[physical protection system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[physical protection system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all of the
    physical protection measures in place at a nuclear
    facility. ([système de protection physique]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[physical protection system support person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[physical protection system support
    person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} carries out the design, implementation,
      maintenance or repair of a physical protection system at a
      high-security site or conducts training related to one or more of
      those activities; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} could have access to prescribed information in
      carrying out the activities referred to in paragraph
      (a). ([préposé au système de protection physique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[potential adversary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[potential adversary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person ---
    whether or not they have authorized access to a nuclear facility ---
    who might attempt

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the unauthorized removal of Category I, II or III
      nuclear material; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sabotage. ([agresseur potentiel]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[prescribed information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the information
    prescribed by section 21 of the [General Nuclear Safety and Control
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202). ([renseignements
    réglementés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[protected area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[protected area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area that is
    surrounded by a barrier that meets the requirements of section
    9. ([zone protégée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[response force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[response force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2006-191, s.
    2\]]{.Repealed}

[[sabotage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sabotage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any deliberate act or
    omission, directed against a nuclear facility or nuclear substances,
    that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} endangers or could endanger the health or safety
      of any person; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} results or could result in contamination of the
      environment. ([sabotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[security monitoring room]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[security monitoring room]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a security
    monitoring room referred to in section 15. ([local de
    surveillance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[threat and risk assessment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[threat and risk assessment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    evaluation of the adequacy of an existing or a proposed physical
    protection system designed to safeguard against

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} intentional acts that could pose a threat to the
      security of a high-security site; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exploitation of weaknesses in the physical
      protection measures of a high-security site. ([évaluation de la
      menace et du risque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unobstructed area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unobstructed area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2006-191, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

[[vehicle portal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vehicle portal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a structure situated on
    the perimeter of a protected area that is enclosed on the sides and
    consists of two movable gates, separated by a space sufficiently
    large to accommodate land vehicles having an operational requirement
    to enter the area. ([sas pour véhicule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vital area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vital area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area inside a protected
    area containing equipment, systems, devices or a nuclear substance,
    whose sabotage could pose an unreasonable risk to the environment or
    to the health or safety of persons arising from exposure to
    radiation. ([zone vitale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[weapon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[weapon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means anything that could be used or
    is capable of being used to jeopardize the security of a nuclear
    facility or a nuclear substance or anything, including firearms,
    that is used, designed to be used or intended for use in causing
    death or injury to any person or for the purpose of threatening or
    intimidating any person. ([arme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Licence Applications]{.HTitleText2} {#h-658458 .Subheading}

#### [Licence in Respect of Category I or II Nuclear Material or a Nuclear Facility]{.HTitleText3} {#h-658459 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence in respect of Category I or II nuclear material, other than a
licence to transport, and an application for a licence in respect of a
nuclear facility referred to in paragraph 2(b) shall contain the
following information in addition to the information required by section
3 of the [Nuclear Substances and Radiation Devices
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-207) or sections 3 to 8 of the
[Class I Nuclear Facilities Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-204),
as applicable:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the arrangements referred to in section 35;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the site plan referred to in section 16;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed security equipment,
  systems and procedures;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed on-site and off-site
  communications equipment, systems and procedures;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed structure and
  organization of the nuclear security officer service, including the
  duties, responsibilities and training of nuclear security officers;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the proposed plan and procedures to assess and
  respond to breaches of security; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the current threat and risk assessment.

#### [Licence to Transport Category I, II or III Nuclear Material]{.HTitleText3} {#h-658474 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence to transport Category I, II or III nuclear material shall
contain, in addition to any other information required by section 7 of
the [Packaging and Transport of Nuclear Substances Regulations,
2015](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-145), a written transportation security
plan that includes

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, quantity, radiation level in Gy/h, chemical
  and physical characteristics and isotopic composition of the nuclear
  material;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a threat assessment consisting of an evaluation of
  the nature, likelihood and consequences of acts or events that may
  place prescribed information or nuclear material at risk;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the conveyance;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed security measures;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the communication arrangements made among the
  licensee, the operator of the land vehicle transporting the nuclear
  material, the recipient of the material and any off-site response
  force along the route;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the arrangements made between the licensee and any
  off-site response force along the route;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the planned route; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the alternate route to be used in case of an
  emergency.

### [Exemption From Licence Requirement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-658486 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  may, without a licence to carry on that activity, transport Category
  I, II or III nuclear material within an area in which the material is
  required by section 7 to be processed, used or stored.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the exemption established in
  subsection (1) relates only to the activity specified in that
  subsection and does not derogate from the licence requirement imposed
  by section 26 of the Act in relation to other activities.

#### [Areas for Processing, Use and Storage of Category I, II or III Nuclear Material]{.HTitleText3} {#h-658493 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee shall process, use and store Category I nuclear material in
  an inner area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall process, use and store Category
  II nuclear material in a protected area.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall process, use and store Category
  III nuclear material in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a protected area;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an area that is under the direct visual
    surveillance of the licensee; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an area to which access is controlled by the
    licensee and that is designed and constructed to prevent persons
    from gaining unauthorized access to the Category III nuclear
    material by using hand-held tools.

- SOR/2006-191, s. 9

#### [Requirements Concerning Category III Nuclear Material]{.HTitleText3} {#h-658503 .Subheading}

- **[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), if a
  licensee processes, uses or stores Category III nuclear material in an
  area referred to in paragraph 7(3)(c), the licensee shall ensure that
  the area is equipped with devices that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} detect any intrusion into it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} detect any unauthorized removal of Category III
    nuclear material;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} detect any tampering that may cause any of the
    devices to malfunction or cease to function; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} when an event referred to in paragraph (a), (b)
    or (c) is detected, set off a continuous alarm signal that is both
    audible and visible to a person in the service of the licensee or of
    an alarm monitoring service under contract to the licensee.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensee need not comply with subsection (1) if it
  takes physical protection measures in respect of the area that provide
  the same level of protection as the devices referred to in that
  subsection.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2006-191, s. 10
:::

#### [Arrangements with Off-site Response Force]{.HTitleText3} {#h-658513 .Subheading}

- **[[7.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall make or cause
  to be made written arrangements with an off-site response force that
  is capable of making an effective intervention at an area where
  Category III nuclear material is processed, used or stored.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The arrangements shall include provisions for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} annual familiarization visits by members of the
    off-site response force to the area where the Category III nuclear
    material is processed, used or stored; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the joint development of a contingency plan by the
    licensee and the off-site response force to facilitate the force
    making an effective intervention.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a licensee does not have alarm monitoring
  capability, the alarm monitoring service under contract to the
  licensee shall notify the licensee and the off-site response force,
  immediately on receipt of an alarm signal from the area where the
  Category III nuclear material is processed, used or stored.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2006-191, s. 10

### [Requirements for High-security Sites]{.HTitleText2} {#h-658522 .Subheading}

##### [Design Basis Threat Analysis]{.HTitleText4} {#h-658528 .Subheading}

- **[[7.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission shall establish a design basis threat analysis and update
  it as necessary to incorporate changes to the design basis threat.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall provide the current design basis
  threat analysis to every licensee, who shall take that analysis into
  account in the design of their physical protection system and make
  modifications to that system as necessary.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2006-191, s. 10
:::

##### [Facility-specific Threat and Risk Assessment]{.HTitleText4} {#h-658534 .Subheading}

- **[[7.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall conduct, at
  least once every 12 months, a threat and risk assessment specific to a
  facility where it carries on licensed activities in order to determine
  the adequacy of its physical protection system.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall make modifications to its
  physical protection system, as necessary, to counter any credible
  threat identified as a result of the threat and risk assessment.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall keep a written record of each
  threat and risk assessment that it conducts.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall provide a copy of the written
  record, together with a statement of actions taken as a result of the
  threat and risk assessment, to the Commission within 60 days after
  completion of the assessment.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2006-191, s. 10
:::

### [Requirements Concerning Protected, Inner and Vital Areas]{.HTitleText2} {#h-658546 .Subheading}

::: HistoricalNote

#### [Barrier Enclosing Protected Area]{.HTitleText3} {#h-658549 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  protected area shall be enclosed by a barrier that is located at its
  perimeter.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the barrier shall be
  designed and constructed to inhibit any unauthorized entry into the
  protected area and must be one or a combination of the following
  structures:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a fence at least 2.4 m in height constructed of
    wire chain links made of wire not smaller than gauge number 11,
    having openings whose sides do not exceed 6 cm in length, and topped
    with at least three strands of barbed wire or barbed tape installed
    parallel to the fence on brackets angled outward;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a wall at least 2.4 m in height, including any wall
    that forms part of a building, constructed of steel, wood, concrete,
    masonry or other substantial material or composites of such
    materials and, if it does not form part of a building, topped with
    at least three strands of barbed wire or barbed tape installed
    parallel to the wall on brackets angled outward; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a structure that provides the same level of
    protection as those referred to in paragraphs (a) and (b).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For facilities in respect of which a construction
  licence is issued after the day on which section 9.1 of these
  Regulations comes into force, the barrier must be designed and
  constructed to inhibit unauthorized entry into the protected area and
  must consist of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the following elements, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an exterior fence extending at least 3 m above
      grade but otherwise constructed to the specifications set out in
      paragraph (2)(a),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an interior fence extending at least 2.4 m above
      grade but otherwise constructed to the specifications set out in
      paragraph (2)(a), and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a separation of not less than 5 m between the
      two fences that is free of obstructions, other than guard posts,
      vehicle portals and intrusion detection and assessment devices; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a structure, whether or not combined with other
    physical protection measures, that provides the same level of
    protection as the structures referred to in paragraph (a).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (3), permanent security facilities
  such as guard posts and vehicle portals may join with the exterior and
  interior fences provided that a continuous barrier is maintained.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The interior fence referred to in subparagraph
  (3)(a)(ii) is considered to be the perimeter of the protected area.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Each gate, door, window or other means of entry or
  exit in the barrier shall be constructed so that it can be closed and
  locked.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Those means of entry or exit shall be kept closed and
  locked except when persons or land vehicles are entering or exiting
  the protected area under the direct visual surveillance of a nuclear
  security officer.

### [Access Control at Nuclear Facilities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-658925 .Subheading}

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  licensee shall permit any person to enter or remain in a nuclear
  facility unless the person has a facility-access security clearance or
  is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} escorted at all times by a person who has a
    facility-access security clearance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a member of an off-site response force, a peace
    officer or a member of another external emergency response force who
    requires access for the purpose of carrying out their duties at the
    site of the nuclear facility; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an inspector who is designated under section 29 of
    the Act to carry out inspections at a nuclear facility.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before granting a facility-access security clearance
  to a person, a licensee shall verify the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a record emanating from the Canadian Police
    Information Centre or from a police service serving the locality
    where the facility is located, showing the results of a criminal
    record name check on the person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person's personal history, composed of their
    educational achievement, professional qualifications, employment
    history and character references, unless the person has been
    employed for more than 10 years at the facility; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if a person's personal history cannot be
    established for at least the last five years, information relating
    to the trustworthiness of the person including, where available, a
    criminal record name check on that person from each country in which
    the person has resided for one or more years in the last five years.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A facility-access security clearance may be granted
  for any term not exceeding five years and shall be subject to any
  terms and conditions necessary to minimize the risk to the security of
  the nuclear facility.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a licensee may
  accept a facility-access security clearance granted by another
  licensee or an authorization referred to in subsection 17(1) or
  section 18.

  security practice.
