## Royal Canadian Mounted Police, Criminal Record Verification for Certain Enterprises Fees Regulations {#royal-canadian-mounted-police-criminal-record-verification-for-certain-enterprises-fees-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-482
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1993-09-23

Regulations Prescribing the Fees To Be Paid for Criminal Record
Verification for the Purposes of Licensing or Otherwise Certifying an
Individual for Employment in an Enterprise Related to Insurance, Real
Estate or Securities

P.C. 1993-1819  1993-09-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Solicitor General of Canada and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
paragraph 19(1)(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-93-482_e_hq_6266){#fn_SOR-93-482_e_hq_6266-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed Regulations prescribing the fees to be paid for
criminal record verification for the purposes of licensing or otherwise
certifying individuals for employment in an enterprise related to
insurance, real estate or securities.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955094 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police, Criminal Record
Verification for Certain Enterprises Fees
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-482).

## [Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955096 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The fee to be paid by a
provincial agency for the verification of criminal records by the Royal
Canadian Mounted Police for the purpose of licensing or otherwise
certifying an individual for employment in an enterprise related to
insurance, real estate or securities is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$5, in respect of the verification of Canadian
  criminal records; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$60, in respect of the verification of foreign
  criminal records.
