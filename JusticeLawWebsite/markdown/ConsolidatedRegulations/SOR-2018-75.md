## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-859209 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[contract grower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contract grower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who raises
    hogs that they do not own on behalf of a
    producer. [(éleveur-contractant)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}

[[cull boar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cull boar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an uncastrated hog that is
    no longer used for breeding and is marketed for slaughter. [(verrat
    de réforme)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}

[[cull sow]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cull sow]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a female hog that is no
    longer used for breeding and is marketed for slaughter. [(truie de
    réforme)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}

[[feeder hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[feeder hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a hog that is at least 30
    days old and is marketed for hog production instead of for
    slaughter. [(porc d'engraissement)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}

[[market hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[market hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a hog other than a
    weanling, feeder hog, cull sow, stag or cull boar. [(porc de
    marché)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who owns hogs that
    are bred or raised in Nova Scotia for commercial purposes or that
    are produced or marketed in Nova Scotia but does not include a
    processor or contract grower. [(producteur)]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"}

[[stag]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[stag]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a castrated hog that has been
    used for breeding. [(verrat castré)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}

[[weanling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[weanling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a hog that is at least 10
    days old but is younger than 30 days old. [(porc
    sevré)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-859219 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay to
the Commodity Board the following levies in respect of any hogs sold by
the producer in interprovincial and export trade:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$0.25 for weanlings; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$2.00 for stags, cull boars, feeder hogs, market
  hogs and cull sows.

## [Remittance of Levies and Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-859223 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall, for
the reporting periods set out in column 1 of the schedule and by the
deadline set out in column 2 of the schedule,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} remit to the Commodity Board the levies referred to
  in section 2; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the Commodity Board with a quarterly report
  that sets out the number of weanlings, stags, cull boars, feeder hogs,
  market hogs and cull sows sold by the producer in interprovincial and
  export trade.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-859227 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
