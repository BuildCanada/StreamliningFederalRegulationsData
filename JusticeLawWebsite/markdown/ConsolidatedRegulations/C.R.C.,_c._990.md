## Motor Vehicle Operators Hours of Work Regulations {#motor-vehicle-operators-hours-of-work-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 990
:::

[CANADA LABOUR CODE](/eng/acts/L-2)

Regulations Respecting Hours of Work Standards for Employees Engaged in
the Transport of Goods, Passengers and Mail by Motor Vehicle
::::

:::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604462 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Motor Vehicle Operators Hours of Work
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._990).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604464 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Part III of the [Canada Labour
    Code](/eng/acts/L-2); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bus operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bus operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a motor vehicle operator
    who operates a bus; ([conducteur d\'autobus]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[city motor vehicle operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[city motor vehicle operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a motor
    vehicle operator who operates exclusively within a 10-mile radius of
    his home terminal and is not a bus operator and includes any motor
    vehicle operator who is classified as a city motor vehicle operator
    in a collective agreement entered into between his employer and a
    trade union acting on his behalf or who is not classified in any
    such agreement but is considered to be a city motor vehicle operator
    according to the prevailing industry practice in the geographical
    area where he is employed; ([conducteur urbain de véhicule
    automobile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who operates an
    industrial establishment described in section 3;
    ([employeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[highway motor vehicle operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[highway motor vehicle operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a motor
    vehicle operator who is not a bus operator or a city motor vehicle
    operator; ([conducteur routier de véhicule
    automobile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[motor vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[motor vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any vehicle that is
    operated by an employee and is propelled otherwise than by muscular
    power but does not include any vehicle designed for running on
    rails; ([véhicule automobile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[motor vehicle operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[motor vehicle operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
    operates a motor vehicle; ([conducteur de véhicule
    automobile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[standard hours of work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[standard hours of work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of any
    class of motor vehicle operator for which hours of work are
    described in these Regulations, means the hours of work so
    described; ([durée normale du travail]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[working hours]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[working hours]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all hours from the time
    that a motor vehicle operator begins his work shift as required by
    his employer until the time he is relieved of his job
    responsibilities but does not include any time

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} during a work shift when he is relieved of his
      job responsibilities by his employer for authorized meals and rest
      while en route,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} spent during stops en route due to illness or
      fatigue,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} resting en route as one of two operators of a
      motor vehicle that is fitted with a sleeper berth, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} resting while en route in a motel, hotel or other
      similar regular place of rest where sleeping accommodation is
      provided. ([durée du travail]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Modification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604479 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The provisions of sections
169 and 171 of the Act are modified to the extent set out in these
Regulations for the purpose of the application of Division I of the Act
to the following classes of employees:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} bus operators,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} city motor vehicle operators, and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} highway motor vehicle operators

who are employed upon or in connection with the operation of any
industrial establishment engaged in

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the transportation of goods or passengers by motor
  vehicle from any point within a province to any point outside that
  province, or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the transportation of mail anywhere in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-594, s. 2
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604489 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to these Regulations and the Commercial Vehicles Drivers Hours of
  Service Regulations, employees of the classes specified in section 3
  may be employed in excess of the standard hours of work, and the total
  hours that may be worked by any such employees may exceed 48 hours in
  a week.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Employees of the classes specified in paragraphs 3(b)
  and (c) are exempted from the application of subsection 169(2) of the
  Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-43, s. 1
- SOR/92-594, s. 2
:::

## [City Motor Vehicle Operators]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604495 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and section 8, the standard hours of work of a city
  motor vehicle operator may exceed 8 hours in a day and 40 hours in a
  week but shall not exceed 9 hours in a day and 45 hours in a week, and
  no employer shall cause or permit a city motor vehicle operator to
  work longer hours than 9 hours in a day or 45 hours in a week.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In a week in which a general holiday occurs that,
  under Division V of the Act, entitles a city motor vehicle operator to
  a holiday with pay in that week, the standard hours of work of the
  city motor vehicle operator in that week may exceed 32 hours but shall
  not exceed 36 hours, but, for the purposes of this subsection, in
  calculating the time worked by a city motor vehicle operator in any
  such week, no account shall be taken of any time worked by the
  operator on the holiday or of any time during which the operator was
  at the disposal of the employer during the holiday.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-594, s. 2
- SOR/95-533, s. 3(E)
:::

## [Highway Motor Vehicle Operator]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604501 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this section and section 8, the standard hours of work of a highway
  motor vehicle operator may exceed 40 hours in a week but shall not
  exceed 60 hours, and no employer shall cause or permit a highway motor
  vehicle operator to work longer hours than 60 hours in a week.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In a week in which a general holiday occurs that,
  under Division V of the Act, entitles a highway motor vehicle operator
  to a holiday with pay in that week, the standard hours of work of the
  highway motor vehicle operator in that week may exceed 32 hours but
  shall not exceed 50 hours, but, for the purposes of this subsection,
  in calculating the time worked by a highway motor vehicle operator in
  any such week, no account shall be taken of any time worked by the
  operator on the holiday or of any time during which the operator was
  at the disposal of the employer during the holiday.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), the hours of work of a
  highway motor vehicle operator who does not normally drive on public
  roads may, pursuant to an authorization made under the Commercial
  Vehicles Drivers Hours of Service Regulations, exceed 60 hours in a
  week.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a permit referred to in subsection (3) has been
  issued in respect of a motor vehicle operator, the hours of work set
  out in the permit shall be regarded as the standard hours of work for
  that operator.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-43, s. 2
- SOR/92-594, s. 2
- SOR/95-533, s. 3(F)
:::

## [Mixed Employment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604509 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), where an employee is employed in any day or in any
  week in not less than two of the following classes of employees,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a city motor vehicle operator,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a highway motor vehicle operator, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an employee whose hours of work are not described
    in these Regulations and are not calculated according to the [Canada
    Labour Standards Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._986),

  his standard hours of work in any such day or week, as the case may
  be, shall be deemed to be the standard hours of work for the class of
  employment in which he works the greatest number of hours in that day
  or week.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), where an employee works as
  described in subsection (1), section 174 of the Act does not apply in
  respect of any time worked by the employee in a day or week while
  employed as a highway motor vehicle operator.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the total working hours of an employee
  described in subsection (1) exceeds 60 hours in any week, all hours
  worked in excess of 60 shall be counted as overtime.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-594, s. 2
:::

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  during any period of two or more weeks in which an employee is
  employed, he works in any week in not less than two of the following
  classes of employment,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a city motor vehicle operator,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a highway motor vehicle operator, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an employee whose hours of work are not described
    in these Regulations, but are calculated according to the [Canada
    Labour Standards Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._986),

  his standard hours of work in that week shall be deemed to be the
  standard of hours of work for the class of employment in which he
  works the greatest number of hours in that week.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an employee is employed in any week during a
  period described in subsection (1) in not less than two of the classes
  of employment described therein and he works the greatest number of
  hours in that week in a class of employment described in paragraph (a)
  or (b) thereof,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} his standard hours of work as determined according
    to subsection 7(1), and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} his standard and maximum hours of work

  in the period shall be reduced by 40 for every week in which he so
  works.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), where an employee works as
  described in subsection (1), section 174 of the Act does not apply in
  respect of any time worked by the employee in a day or week while
  employed as a highway motor vehicle operator.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the total working hours of an employee
  described in subsection (1) exceeds 60 hours in any week, all hours
  worked in excess of 60 shall be counted as overtime.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where an employee described in subsection (1) works
  the greatest number of hours in a week as an employee referred to in
  paragraph (c) thereof, his standard and maximum hours of work while
  working as described in paragraphs (a) and (c) thereof shall be
  counted for the purpose of the [Canada Labour Standards
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._986) but no account shall be
  taken of any hours worked as an employee described in paragraph (b)
  thereof for that purpose.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-594, s. 2
:::

## [Bus Operators]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604535 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the
calculation of hours worked by a bus operator, account shall be taken of
all hours from the time that the work shift of the bus operator begins
until the time it ends but no account shall be taken of any time when
the bus is in a garage or parked and the operator is not required by his
employer to remain with the bus.

## [Weekly Rest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604537 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Where the nature of the
work in an industrial establishment necessitates irregular distribution
of the hours of work of motor vehicle operators with the result that the
operators

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have no regularly scheduled daily or weekly hours, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have regularly scheduled hours but the number of
  hours differs from time to time,

hours of work may be scheduled and actually worked by those operators
without regard to section 173 of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-594, s. 2
:::
