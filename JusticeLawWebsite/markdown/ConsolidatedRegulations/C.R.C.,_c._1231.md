## Navigable Waters Bridges Regulations {#navigable-waters-bridges-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1231
:::

[CANADIAN NAVIGABLE WATERS ACT](/eng/acts/N-22)

NAVIGABLE WATERS PROTECTION ACT

Regulations Respecting Bridges Over Navigable Waters
::::

::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499597 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Navigable Waters Bridges
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1231).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499599 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[bridge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bridge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a bridge under construction;
    ([pont]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
    ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[movable span]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[movable span]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, without restricting the
    generality of the term, includes a lift, draw, swing and jackknife
    span. ([travée mobile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499604 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to every bridge over a navigable water constructed
  after November 17, 1923.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), these Regulations do
  not apply to any bridge over a canal or over the waters of any public
  harbour to the extent that these Regulations conflict with any other
  regulations made or approved by the Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-970, s. 1
:::

## [Lights]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499610 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may prescribe
lights or markings to be exhibited and the time during which the lights
shall be exhibited on any bridge or on any span through or under which
any passage to navigation is approved.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-970, s. 2
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The owner of every bridge
shall cause to be exhibited, maintained and operated at the bridge the
lights and other aids to navigation prescribed by or under these
Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-970, s. 2
:::

## [Approval of Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499617 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where a plan and
description of a proposed bridge is required by the [Canadian Navigable
Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-22) or the Railway Act to be submitted to the
Minister for his approval, that plan and description shall indicate any
lights or markings prescribed by the Minister pursuant to section 4.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-970, s. 2
- [2019, c. 28, s. 187](#1158429-1199352){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1158429-1199352.html"
  role="button"}
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where a bridge consists of
more than one span, the Minister may prescribe the span or spans through
or under which any passage for navigation is approved.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-970, s. 2

## [Movable Spans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499625 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise approved
by the Minister, the owner of a movable span bridge shall maintain on
duty in charge of the span at all times a responsible person who is
trained to operate the span.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-970, s. 3
:::

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  person in charge of a vessel passing through or under a bridge where
  two passages for navigation are approved shall use the passage on the
  vessel's starboard side.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall make special provisions governing
  navigation through or under any bridge where more than two passages
  for navigation are approved.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The signal to be given by
a vessel for the purpose of having a movable span opened shall be three
long blasts of a whistle or horn.

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  the signal specified in section 12 is given by a vessel approaching a
  movable span bridge, the responsible person referred to in section 10
  shall open the span immediately, or as soon thereafter as is
  reasonably possible, to permit the passage of the vessel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), the Minister may
  prohibit the opening of any movable span during specified periods and
  the person in charge of the span shall not open it except under such
  conditions as may be prescribed by the Minister.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person in charge of a vessel shall not allow the
  vessel to enter a passage for navigation through or under a movable
  span until the span is fully open unless the vessel can safely move
  under the span while it is closed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-970, s. 4
:::
