## African Development Bank Privileges and Immunities Order {#african-development-bank-privileges-and-immunities-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/84-360
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Registration 1984-05-04

Order Respecting the Privileges and Immunities of the African
Development Bank in Canada

P.C. 1984-1432 1984-05-03
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Secretary of State for External Affairs, pursuant to section
3[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBBAA}
of the Privileges and Immunities (International Organizations) Act, is
pleased hereby to make the annexed Order respecting the privileges and
immunities of the African Development Bank in Canada.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-882705 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [African Development Bank Privileges and Immunities
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-360).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-882707 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement establishing
    the African Development Bank; ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations;
    ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the African Development
    Bank. ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-882712 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Organization shall have in Canada the legal capacities of a body
  corporate and shall, to the extent provided in sections 50 to 55 and
  subsections 57(1), (3) and (4) of the Agreement, have the privileges
  and immunities set forth in Articles II and III of the Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Representatives of states and governments that are
  members of the Organization shall have in Canada, to the extent
  provided in section 56 and subsection 57(2) of the Agreement, the
  privileges and immunities set forth in Article IV of the Convention
  for representatives of Members.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Officials of the Organization shall have in Canada,
  to the extent provided in section 56 and subsection 57(2) of the
  Agreement, the privileges and immunities set forth in Article V of the
  Convention for officials of the United Nations.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Experts performing missions for the Organization
  shall have in Canada, to the extent provided in section 56 and
  subsection 57(2) of the Agreement, the privileges and immunities set
  forth in Article VI of the Convention for experts on missions for the
  United Nations.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Order
exempts a Canadian citizen, residing or ordinarily resident in Canada,
from liability or any taxes or duties imposed by any law in Canada.
