## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1138780 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Permit.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Export and Import Permits
    Act](/eng/acts/E-19). ([Loi sur les licences d'exportation et
    d'importation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Export Controls Operations Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Export Controls Operations Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    the Export Controls Operations Division of the Department of Foreign
    Affairs, Trade and Development. ([Direction des opérations des
    contrôles à l'exportation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 1
    of the [Export Control
    List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202). ([Guide]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1138786 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 to 6,
any resident of Canada may export any of the following goods from Canada
to the United States:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any good referred to in item 2-1 of the Guide;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any good referred to in item 2-3 of the Guide;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any good referred to in Group 9 of the schedule to
  the [Export Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unauthorized goods

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Permit does not
authorize

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the export of goods to a country other than the
  United States;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the export of [[prohibited
  ammunition]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[prohibited
  devices]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[prohibited
  firearms]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or [[prohibited
  weapons]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 84(1) of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the export of any good referred to in item 2-2.a. or
  2-4.a. of the Guide.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1138797 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A resident of Canada who
exports a firearm under this Permit must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} hold a licence under the [Firearms
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6) issued by the chief firearms officer of the
  province in which they reside; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the firearm is a restricted firearm, hold a
  registration certificate for it issued under that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  resident of Canada who exports under this Permit a good referred to in
  Group 9 of the schedule to the [Export Control
  List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202) must provide in writing to the
  Export Controls Operations Division

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before exporting under this Permit in a calendar
    year, their name, address, telephone number, email address and any
    facsimile number;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 30 days after each six-month period ending
    on June 30 and December 31 of the calendar year for which the
    information in paragraph (a) was provided, a report stating

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} whether they exported a good under this Permit
      during that period, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if a good was exported,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of each consignee,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a description of the good, the provision of the
        schedule to the [Export Control
        List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202) that refers to it and the
        item number assigned to it by the Guide, and

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the quantity and value in Canadian dollars of
        the good; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} within 15 days after the day on which the resident
    of Canada receives a request from the Export Controls Operations
    Division, any information referred to in paragraph (b) that is
    requested by it in respect of exports during the period specified in
    the request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(b), a resident of Canada who
  provides the information referred to in paragraph (1)(a) during the
  six month period ending on December 31 is not obliged to provide a
  report for the six-month period ending on June 30 during the same
  calendar year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary export

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to the export of a good
  if the good is to be returned to Canada and to the resident of Canada
  who exported it within two years after the day of export.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Good not returned within two years

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a good referred to in subsection (3) is not
  returned to the resident of Canada within two years after the day of
  export, the resident of Canada must provide in writing to the Export
  Controls Operations Division the information referred to in
  subsection (1) no later than 30 days after the day that is two years
  after the day of export.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A resident of Canada who
exports a good under this Permit must retain, for a period of six years
after the year in which the good is exported, the following records:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the good was exported;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of each consignee;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the quantity and value in Canadian dollars of the
  exported good; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the exported good, the provision of
  the schedule to the [Export Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202)
  that refers to it and the item number assigned to it by the Guide.
