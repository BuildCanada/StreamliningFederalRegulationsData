[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2011-11-15

Transfer of the Communications Security Establishment in the Department
of National Defence Regulations

P.C. 2011-1303 2011-11-15
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to subsection 123(1) of the [Public Service
Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2011-254_e_hq_11581){#fn_SOR-2011-254_e_hq_11581-ID0EDBAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Transfer of the Communications Security
Establishment in the Department of National Defence
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-254).

## [Block Transfer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-774983 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 132(1) of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) applies to all
persons employed or engaged in the portion of the federal public
administration in the Department of National Defence known as the
Communications Security Establishment.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-774985 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on November 16, 2011.
