## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-859271 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[accessible part]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[accessible part]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any part of a product
    that may be touched, licked, mouthed or swallowed during the
    reasonably foreseeable use of the product. ([partie
    accessible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[consumer product containing lead]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[consumer product containing lead]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
    of the following products that contains lead:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a product that is brought into contact with the
      user's mouth during normal use, except for

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a kitchen utensil, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a product that is subject to the [Glazed
        Ceramics and Glassware
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-175);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any clothing or clothing accessory that is
      intended for use by a child under 14 years of age;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a product that is intended for use in learning or
      play by a child under 14 years of age;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a book or similar printed product that is
      intended for a child under 14 years of age, except if it is

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} printed on paper or cardboard, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} printed and bound in a conventional manner
        using conventional materials;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a product whose primary purpose is to facilitate
      the relaxation, sleep, hygiene, carrying or transportation of a
      child under four years of age. ([produit de consommation contenant
      du plomb]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[good laboratory practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[good laboratory practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means practices
    that are in accordance with the principles set out in the
    Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development document
    entitled OECD Principles of Good Laboratory Practice, Number 1 of
    the OECD Series on Principles of Good Laboratory Practice and
    Compliance Monitoring, ENV/MC/CHEM(98)17, the English version of
    which is dated January 21, 1998 and the French version of which is
    dated March 6, 1998. ([bonnes pratiques de
    laboratoire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-859286 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lead content --- accessible part

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), each accessible part of a consumer product
  containing lead must not contain more than 90 mg/kg of lead when
  tested in accordance with good laboratory practices.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each accessible part may contain more than 90 mg/kg
  of lead if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} lead is necessary to produce an essential
    characteristic of the part;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no alternative part containing less lead is
    available; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the part, when tested in accordance with good
    laboratory practices, does not release more than 90 mg/kg of lead.
