[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this By-law,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Deposit Insurance
Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed class of federal member
institutions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The class consisting of all
federal member institutions is prescribed for the purpose of subsection
39.15(7.4) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed class of eligible financial
contracts

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The class consisting of
every eligible financial contract that meets the following conditions
and to which a federal member institution is a party is prescribed in
respect of that institution for the purpose of subsection 39.15(7.4) of
the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it contains provisions permitting the taking of an
  action referred to in paragraph 39.15(7)(a), (b) or (f) of the Act by
  a party other than

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Canada,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the government of a foreign country,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a central bank,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a [[clearing house]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
    defined in subsection 39.15(9) of the Act, or

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a [[central counter-party]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
    as defined in section 2 of the [Payment Clearing and Settlement
    Act](/eng/acts/P-4.4);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is not governed by Canadian law or at least one of
  the other parties to it is neither an individual who is resident in
  Canada nor a [[Canadian entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
  section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} it meets one of the following conditions:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is entered into, amended or renewed on or after
    October 1, 2023 and at least one of the other parties to it is

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a federal member institution or an affiliate of a
      federal member institution, or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} an institution that has been identified by the
      Financial Stability Board as a global systemically important bank
      or an affiliate of an institution that has been so identified,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it is entered into, amended or renewed on or after
    October 1, 2024, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} at least one other party to it and the federal
    member institution are together parties to another contract that is
    part of a class prescribed by this section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contract provisions

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every federal member
institution must ensure that all eligible financial contracts that are
part of the class prescribed in respect of it by section 3 contain
provisions indicating the parties' agreement to the application of
subsections 39.15(7.1) to (7.104) and (7.11) of the Act in relation to
the actions that the parties, other than any referred to in
subparagraphs 3(a)(i) to (v), may take.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This By-law comes into
force on the day on which it is registered.
