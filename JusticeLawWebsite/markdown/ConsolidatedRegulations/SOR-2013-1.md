## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-792833 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Permit.

  [[controlled]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[controlled]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means controlled in any manner
      that results in control in fact, whether directly through the
      ownership of securities or indirectly through a trust, an
      agreement or an arrangement, the ownership of any corporation or
      otherwise. ([contrôlé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Argentina,
      Australia, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Czech Republic, Denmark,
      Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Japan,
      Luxembourg, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Portugal,
      Republic of Korea, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey, Ukraine,
      the United Kingdom or the United States. ([pays
      désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation, a trust, a
      partnership, a fund or an unincorporated association or
      organization. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Export Controls Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Export Controls Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Export
      Controls Division of the Department of Foreign Affairs and
      International Trade. ([Direction des contrôles à
      l'exportation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[government of a foreign country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[government of a foreign country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the government of any political subdivision of
        a foreign country;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any agent or agency of a government of a
        foreign country or its political subdivisions; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any entity controlled by a government of a
        foreign country or its political subdivisions. ([gouvernement
        étranger]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 1
      of the [Export Control
      List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202). ([Guide]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[ineligible destination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[ineligible destination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Afghanistan,
      Belarus, Burma (Myanmar), Côte d'Ivoire, Cuba, Democratic People's
      Republic of Korea, Eritrea, Guinea, Iran, Iraq, Lebanon, Liberia,
      Libya, Pakistan, Sierra Leone, Sudan, Syria and
      Zimbabwe. ([destination inadmissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Permit, [[development]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
  [[information security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
  [[production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[software]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  and [[use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have the same meaning as in the Guide
  under the heading "Definitions of Terms Used in Groups 1 and 2".

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-792849 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to sections 3 and 4, any resident of Canada may, under this Permit,
  export or transfer from Canada any good or technology described in
  subsection (2) if the export or transfer is made to a consignee that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is controlled by a resident of Canada; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is controlled by an entity that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has its head office in a designated country, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} controls the resident of Canada who exports or
      transfers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Goods and technologies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The goods and technologies referred to in
  subsection (1) are the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any good referred to in item 1-5.A.2. or 1-5.A.3.
    of the Guide, other than a good referred to in item

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 1-5.A.2.c., or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 1-5.A.3.b.;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any good referred to in item 1-5.B.2. of the Guide,
    other than a good

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} used in the development or production of any good
      referred to in any of subparagraphs (a)(i) to (iii) or (b)(ii), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} specially designed to evaluate and validate the
      information security functions of a good referred to in any of
      subparagraphs (a)(i) to (iii) or (c)(i) to (iii);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any good referred to in item 1-5.D.2. of the Guide,
    other than software

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} used in the development, production or use of any
      good referred to in any of subparagraphs (a)(i) to (iii) or
      (c)(ii) or (iii),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that has the characteristics of a good referred
      to in any of subparagraphs (a)(i) to (iii) or that performs or
      simulates the functions of any of those goods,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that certifies software referred to in
      subparagraph (ii),

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} specially designed or modified to support
      technology used in the development, production or use of a good
      referred to in any of subparagraphs (a)(i) to (iii), (b)(i)
      or (ii) or (c)(i) to (iii); or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any technology referred to in item 1-5.E.2. of the
    Guide, other than a technology used in the development, production
    or use of a good referred to in any of subparagraphs (a)(i) to
    (iii), (b)(i) or (ii) or (c)(i) to (iii).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Countries and governments to which export
or transfer is not authorized

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Permit does not authorize the export or transfer of a good or
  technology to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an ineligible destination;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a country that is listed in the [Area Control
    List](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-543);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a country in respect of which there is an order or
    regulation made under section 4 of the [Special Economic Measures
    Act](/eng/acts/S-14.5) or section 2 of the [United Nations
    Act](/eng/acts/U-2); or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the government of a foreign country.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Goods or technologies the export or
  transfer of which is not authorized

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Permit does not authorize the export or transfer
  of a good or technology that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is intended to be used by anyone other than the
    consignee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is intended to be incorporated into a product to be
    used by anyone other than the consignee;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} was developed or produced under contract with the
    Government of Canada or developed or produced for the exclusive use
    of the Government of Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} has been used in the development or production of a
    product for the exclusive use of the Government of Canada or under
    contract with the Government of Canada; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} is referred to in any item of the Guide other than
    those referred to in section 2, unless the export or transfer of the
    goods or technology is also authorized by another export permit
    issued under subsection 7(1.1) of the [Export and Import Permits
    Act](/eng/acts/E-19).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A person who exports or
transfers a good or technology under this Permit must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide the following information in writing to the
  Export Controls Division before making their first export or transfer
  in a calendar year:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their name, address, telephone number, facsimile
    number and email address, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the person is a corporation, the business
    number assigned to the corporation by the Minister of National
    Revenue and the name of a contact person and their address,
    telephone number, facsimile number and email address;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} insert the term [[GEP-46]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
  [[LGE-46]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in the appropriate field of the form
  prescribed under the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) if the good
  exported is required to be reported under that Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide to the Export Controls Division, within 15
  days after receipt of its request, the records referred to in
  paragraph (e) in respect of any export or transfer made during the
  period specified in the request;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide to the Export Controls Division, before
  January 31, the records referred to in subparagraph (e)(ii) in respect
  of any export or transfer made during the previous calendar year; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} retain the following records for a period of six
  years after the year in which any export or transfer is made:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the item number of the Guide which describes the
    good or technology,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name and any version number used to identify
    the good or technology,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the estimated value of the good or technology
    exported or transfered and, in the case of a good or technology that
    can be quantified, the quantity of the export or transfer,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the following information in respect of the good
    or technology:

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} their cryptographic algorithms and the key sizes
      employed,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the function performed by the cryptographic
      algorithms, and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the keys are generated and
      managed, if known to the person who exports or transfers,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the date of the export or transfer, and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the consignee's name, address, telephone number,
    facsimile number and email address.
