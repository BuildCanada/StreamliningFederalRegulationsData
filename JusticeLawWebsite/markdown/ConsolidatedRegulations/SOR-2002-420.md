## Order Specifying that the Canadian Forces are a Portion of the Public Sector Employing One Hundred or more Employees for the Purpose of Subsection 4(1) of that Act {#order-specifying-that-the-canadian-forces-are-a-portion-of-the-public-sector-employing-one-hundred-or-more-employees-for-the-purpose-of-subsection-41-of-that-act .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2002-420
:::

[EMPLOYMENT EQUITY ACT](/eng/acts/E-5.401)

Registration 2002-11-21

Order Specifying that the Canadian Forces are a Portion of the Public
Sector Employing One Hundred or more Employees for the Purpose of
Subsection 4(1) of that Act

P.C. 2002-1956 2002-11-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to paragraph 4(1)(d) of the [Employment Equity
Act](/eng/acts/E-5.401)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBBBA}, the Treasury
Board has consulted the Minister of Labour and the Minister of National
Defence concerning the application of that Act to the Canadian Forces;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnote1_e-ID0EBBBA){#footnote1_e}S.C. 1995, c.
  44
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Treasury Board, and the Minister of Labour,
pursuant to paragraph 4(1)(d) of the [Employment Equity
Act](/eng/acts/E-5.401)^a^, hereby specifies that the Canadian Forces
are a portion of the public sector employing one hundred or more
employees for the purpose of subsection 4(1) of that Act.
