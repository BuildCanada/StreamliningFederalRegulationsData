## Order Designating the Inquiry under the name of the Public Inquiry into Foreign Interference in Federal Electoral Processes and Democratic Institutions as a Department and the Prime Minister as the Appropriate Minister {#order-designating-the-inquiry-under-the-name-of-the-public-inquiry-into-foreign-interference-in-federal-electoral-processes-and-democratic-institutions-as-a-department-and-the-prime-minister-as-the-appropriate-minister .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2023-63
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2023-10-11

Order Designating the Inquiry under the name of the Public Inquiry into
Foreign Interference in Federal Electoral Processes and Democratic
Institutions as a Department and the Prime Minister as the Appropriate
Minister

P.C. 2023-883 2023-09-07
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} under paragraph (b)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2566_hq_1689080400){#fn_81000-3-2566_hq_1689080400-ID0EGCBAA}
  of the definition [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of
  the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2566_hq_1689080424){#fn_81000-3-2566_hq_1689080424-ID0EBCBAA},
  designates the commission that is named the Inquiry under the name of
  the Public Inquiry into Foreign Interference in Federal Electoral
  Processes and Democratic Institutions as a department for the purposes
  of that Act; and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2566_hq_1689080400-ID0EGCBAA){#fn_81000-3-2566_hq_1689080400}S.C.
    1992, c. 1, s. 69(3)
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2566_hq_1689080424-ID0EBCBAA){#fn_81000-3-2566_hq_1689080424}R.S., c.
    F-11
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} under paragraph (b)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2566_hq_1689080463){#fn_81000-3-2566_hq_1689080463-ID0EGBAAA}
  of the definition [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
  section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)^b^,
  designates the Prime Minister as the appropriate Minister with respect
  to the commission named in paragraph (a).

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2566_hq_1689080463-ID0EGBAAA){#fn_81000-3-2566_hq_1689080463}S.C.
    1992, c. 1, s. 69(1)
    :::
