## Order Directing that Certain Documents be Discontinued {#order-directing-that-certain-documents-be-discontinued .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/94-34
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1994-04-06

Order Directing that Certain Documents be Discontinued

P.C. 1994-506 1994-03-24
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas it appears to the Governor in Council that the documents set out
in the schedule hereto, each required by or under an Act of Parliament
to be laid before one or both Houses of Parliament, contain the same
information as or less information than is contained in the Public
Accounts or in any estimates of expenditures submitted to Parliament;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to section 157[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to
direct that the documents set out in the schedule hereto be
discontinued.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnote1_e}S.C.
  1991, c. 24, s. 47
  :::
:::
