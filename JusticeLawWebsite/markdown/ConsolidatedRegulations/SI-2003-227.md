## Order Transferring from the Minister of Industry to the Minister of Environment the Control and Supervision of the Office of Infrastructure of Canada {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-industry-to-the-minister-of-environment-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-office-of-infrastructure-of-canada .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2003-227
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2003-12-31

Order Transferring from the Minister of Industry to the Minister of
Environment the Control and Supervision of the Office of Infrastructure
of Canada

P.C. 2003-2082  2003-12-12
::::

::::: section
:::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers from the Minister of Industry to the Minister of the
Environment the control and supervision of that portion of the public
service known as the Office of Infrastructure of Canada, effective
December 12, 2003, over which the Minister of the Environment shall
preside.
