## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-986131 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Goods Imported and Exported Refund and Drawback
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-42).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-986133 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[beer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[beer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means beer or malt liquor as defined in
    section 4 of the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14);
    ([bière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Crown corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Crown corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation named
    in Schedule II or Schedule III to the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11); ([société d'État]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[textile fabric]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[textile fabric]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means textile fibre, glass
    fibre, textile fabric, glass fabric or other textile product that is
    of a stage between textile fibre and fabric that is composed
    exclusively of fibres that fall within a class set out in
    Schedule I. ([tissus textiles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-237, s. 1
:::

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Drawback]{.HTitleText1} {#h-986141 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-986142 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies in
respect of the grant, under subsection 113(1) of the Act, of a drawback
of the duties paid in respect of imported goods described in
subsection 89(1) of the Act, other than goods in respect of which the
[Exported Motor Vehicles Drawback
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-34) apply.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-54, s. 3
:::

### [Circumstances in Which an Application for Drawback May Be Made]{.HTitleText2} {#h-986146 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5, an
application for a drawback may be made where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods described in paragraph 89(1)(a)
  of the Act, the goods were not damaged before being exported;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of exported goods described in any of
  paragraphs 89(1)(b) to (e) of the Act, the goods

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} were not used in Canada before being exported for
    any purpose other than display or demonstration, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} were not damaged before being exported;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of imported goods described in
  paragraph 89(1)(d) of the Act, other than textile fabric, on which
  relief of duties would otherwise have been granted,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the imported goods are processed in the plant in
    Canada in which the same quantity of domestic or imported goods of
    the same class was processed and subsequently exported, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the exported goods were exported within two years
    after the date the imported goods were released;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of imported textile fabric described in
  paragraph 89(1)(d) of the Act on which relief of duties would
  otherwise have been granted,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the imported textile fabric is processed into
    textile goods in a plant in Canada by the processor of textile goods
    who processed the same quantity of domestic or imported textile
    fabric of the same class that was subsequently exported, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the exported textile goods were exported within
    two years after the date the imported textile fabric was released;
    and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of imported goods described in
  paragraph 89(1)(e) of the Act on which relief of duties would
  otherwise have been granted,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the imported goods are directly consumed or
    expended in the processing of goods in the plant in Canada in which
    the same quantity of domestic or imported goods of the same class
    was directly consumed or expended in the processing of the exported
    goods, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the exported goods were exported within two years
    after the date the imported goods were released.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-54, s. 3
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
drawback under this Part may be made where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods were exported or deemed to have been
  exported before the application for drawback is made; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant provides a waiver from all other
  persons entitled to claim a drawback, refund or remission of the
  duties, waiving their right to do so.

### [Prescribed Classes of Goods and Use]{.HTitleText2} {#h-986166 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 89(3)(b) of the Act, the ships and aircraft described in
Schedule II are prescribed as classes of ships and aircraft.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-54, s. 3
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 89(3)(c) of the Act, the prescribed class of telegraph cable
ships includes only those telegraph cable ships that are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} registered in any country;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} used exclusively for the laying and repairing of
  oceanic telegraph cables outside Canada; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} proceeding on an ocean voyage outside Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-54, s. 3
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 89(3)(g) of the Act, goods are used or destined for use where
they

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} are purchased by the government of a country that is
  party to the Canada--United States--Mexico Agreement or its authorized
  agent, by a department of the Government of Canada, or by a Crown
  corporation acting on behalf of the Government of Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} are for use solely and exclusively in conjunction
  with

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a project undertaken jointly by the Government of
    Canada and the government of a country that is party to the
    Canada--United States--Mexico Agreement, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an undertaking, located in Canada, of the
    government of a country that is party to the Canada--United
    States--Mexico Agreement; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} are or will become the property of the government of
  a country that is party to the Canada--United States--Mexico
  Agreement.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-54, s. 3
- [SOR/2020-64, s. 1](#1243574-1256998){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1243574-1256998.html"
  role="button"}
:::

### [Persons Who May Claim a Drawback]{.HTitleText2} {#h-986184 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a drawback may be claimed by any person who is the
  importer or exporter of the imported or exported goods, or is the
  processor, owner or producer of those goods between the time of their
  direct shipment to Canada and their export or deemed export.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of the goods described in section 10, a
  drawback may be claimed only by the importer of the goods.

### [Time Limits for Applying for Drawback]{.HTitleText2} {#h-986188 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** In the case of spirits,
wine, or beer that are imported goods described in paragraph 89(1)(b) of
the Act on which the relief of duties would otherwise have been granted,
an application for drawback shall be made within five years after the
date on which the imported spirits, wine or beer were released.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-54, s. 3
- SOR/2003-237, s. 2
:::

### [Goods of the Same Class]{.HTitleText2} {#h-986192 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), for the
  purposes of paragraphs 89(1)(d) and (e) of the Act, domestic or
  imported goods shall be considered to be of the same class if the
  goods are so similar that they may be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} used interchangeably in the processing of goods in
    Canada; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} directly consumed or expended interchangeably in
    the processing in Canada of goods.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraphs 89(1)(d) and (e) of
  the Act, domestic and imported textile fabrics composed of different
  fibres shall be considered to be of the same class if the domestic and
  imported textile fabrics are composed of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fibres that fall within one class set out in
    Schedule I; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fibres that do not fall within one class set out in
    Schedule I, if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any particular class set out in that Schedule
      within which fall fibres of which the domestic textile fabric is
      composed

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} is a class within which fall fibres of which
        the imported textile fabric is composed, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} represents less than five per cent of the
        weight of the domestic textile fabric, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the difference between the following proportions
      does not exceed 33 percentage points:

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the proportion, expressed as a percentage, that
        the aggregate weight of the fibres of which the domestic textile
        fabric is composed that fall within the particular class is of
        the weight of the domestic textile fabric, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the proportion, expressed as a percentage, that
        the aggregate weight of the fibres of which the imported textile
        fabric is composed that fall within that particular class is of
        the weight of the imported textile fabric.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-54, s. 3
:::

### [Same Condition Uses and Operations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-986208 .Subheading}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 89(1)(a) of the Act, imported goods will be considered to be
in the same condition after they

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} undergo any of the processes referred to in
  subparagraph (6)(b) of Article 2.5 of the Canada--United
  States--Mexico Agreement, if those processes do not materially alter
  the characteristics of the goods;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} are used for

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the development or production, other than as plant
    equipment, of goods to be exported, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} display or demonstration; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of reusable containers, are used in the
  international transportation of goods.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-54, s. 3
- [SOR/2020-64, s. 2](#1243576-1257000){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1243576-1257000.html"
  role="button"}
:::

### [Portion of Duties that May Be Granted as a Drawback]{.HTitleText2} {#h-986217 .Subheading}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Where the amount of
duties paid in respect of imported textile fabric that is considered
under section 11 to be of the same class as domestic textile fabric
exceeds the amount of duties that would have been payable had the
domestic textile fabric been imported, the portion of the duties so paid
that may be granted as a drawback is the proportion that the amount that
would have been so payable is of the amount that was so paid.

### [No Drawback to Be Granted]{.HTitleText2} {#h-986219 .Subheading}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A drawback shall not be
granted in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} imported goods described in paragraph 89(1)(d) of the
  Act on which relief of duties would otherwise have been granted, where
  the domestic goods referred to in subparagraph 4(c)(i) or (d)(i) are
  processed before those imported goods are processed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} imported goods described in paragraph 89(1)(e) of the
  Act on which relief of duties would otherwise have been granted, where
  the domestic goods referred to in subparagraph 4(e)(i) are consumed or
  expended before those imported goods are consumed or expended;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} imported goods described in paragraph 89(1)(c) or (e)
  of the Act on which relief of duties would otherwise have been
  granted, if those goods are goods described in Schedule III; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} imported goods described in paragraph 89(1)(d) of the
  Act on which relief of duties would otherwise have been granted, if
  those goods are spirits, wine or beer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-54, s. 3
- SOR/2003-237, s. 3
:::

**[[14.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14.1 .sectionLabel}** A drawback in respect
of duties levied under section 21.1 or 21.2 of the Act shall not be
granted to a person in respect of goods subject to those duties to the
extent that it can reasonably be regarded that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount has previously been refunded, remitted,
  applied or paid to that person under the Act or any other Act of
  Parliament; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person has applied for a refund, payment or
  remission of the amount under any other Act of Parliament.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-237, s. 3
:::

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Refund]{.HTitleText1} {#h-986232 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-986233 .Subheading}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies in
respect of the grant, under subsection 113(1) of the Act, of a refund of
the duties paid on imported goods described in subsection 89(1) of the
Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-54, s. 3
:::

### [Circumstances in Which an Application for a Refund May Be Made]{.HTitleText2} {#h-986237 .Subheading}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
refund under this Part may be made where the goods have not been
exported or deemed to have been exported before the application for a
refund is made.

### [Persons Who May Claim a Refund]{.HTitleText2} {#h-986239 .Subheading}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A refund may be claimed
by the person to whom the Minister of National Revenue has issued a
certificate under subsection 90(1) of the Act in respect of the goods on
which the duties were paid.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-54, s. 1
:::

### [No Refund to Be Granted]{.HTitleText2} {#h-986243 .Subheading}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A refund in respect of
duties levied under section 21.1 or 21.2 of the Act shall not be granted
to a person in respect of goods subject to those duties to the extent
that it can reasonably be regarded that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount has previously been refunded, remitted,
  applied or paid to that person under the Act or any other Act of
  Parliament; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person has applied for a refund, payment or
  remission of the amount under any other Act of Parliament.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-237, s. 4
:::
