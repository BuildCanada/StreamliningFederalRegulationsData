## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771409 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations, "Act" means the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply for the purposes of
  section 119 of the Act,

  [[action or proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[action or proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include an
      internal investigation or internal proceeding, including a
      grievance, staffing, or disciplinary procedure, unless it is
      likely to have serious financial or other personal consequences
      for the director or officer. ([enquête ou
      procédure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the president,
      vice-president, secretary, treasurer, comptroller, general
      counsel, general manager or managing director of a Crown
      corporation or any other individual appointed as an officer of a
      Crown corporation who performs functions similar to those normally
      performed by an individual holding any of those offices or any
      other individual appointed as an officer of a Crown corporation by
      or under an Act of Parliament. ([dirigeant]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an officer or director who
      participates in, or who reasonably expects to participate in, an
      action or proceeding as a witness or intervenor or in any other
      capacity. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Request]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771418 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  request for an indemnification or advance under section 119 of the Act
  shall be made in writing to the Secretary of the Treasury Board as
  soon as feasible after the director or officer becomes a party to an
  action or proceeding or incurs a cost, charge or expense.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} specify the capacity and period in which the
    director or officer has served the Crown corporation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} include a description of the nature of the action
    or proceeding;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} specify the amount requested and the amount that
    has already been approved in respect of the action or proceeding;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} include a copy of any court documents or other
    documents exchanged by the parties in relation to the action or
    proceeding that contain a statement of the material facts relied
    upon and the relief claimed;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} specify the name of the legal counsel retained or
    to be retained;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} provide a detailed accounting of the costs, charges
    and expenses that have been incurred in respect of the action or
    proceeding including any amount paid to settle the action or
    proceeding or satisfy a judgment and an estimate of any such costs,
    charges or expenses expected to be incurred in respect of the action
    or proceeding;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} include a copy of any invoices and other supporting
    documents that reflect the amounts paid or payable in respect of the
    action or proceeding;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any information that may be used to rebut a
    presumption referred to in section 3; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request related to an internal
    investigation or internal proceeding, any information that
    demonstrates that the investigation or proceeding is likely to have
    or has had serious financial or other personal consequences for the
    director or officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The individual making the request shall, on the
  request of the Secretary of the Treasury Board, provide any other
  information required to assess whether the individual is entitled to
  an indemnification or advance and the amount of the indemnification or
  advance.

## [Presumption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771435 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A director or officer is
presumed not to have fulfilled the condition set out in paragraph 119
(1)(a) of the Act if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the director or officer initiates the action or
  proceeding, unless it forms part of a legitimate defence to an action
  or proceeding in respect of which an indemnification or advance is
  paid to the director or officer under section 119 of the Act; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Crown corporation served by the director or
  officer commenced the action or proceeding against the director or
  officer.

## [Decision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771440 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requests no more than \$100,000

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Secretary of the Treasury Board shall make a decision in relation to a
  request if the sum of the amount requested and any other amounts
  already approved in respect of the same action or proceeding is no
  more than \$100,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notification of the decision shall be made in writing
  within 30 days of receipt of the request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requests more than \$100,000 and no more
than \$250,000

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President of the Treasury Board shall make a decision in relation to a
  request if the sum of the amount requested and any other amounts
  already approved in respect of the same action or proceeding exceeds
  \$100,000 but is no more than \$250,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notification of the decision shall be made in writing
  within 30 days of receipt of the request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requests more than \$250,000

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Treasury Board shall make a decision in relation to a request if the
  sum of the amount requested and any other amounts already approved in
  respect of the same action or proceeding is more than \$250,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notification of the decision shall be made in writing
  within 90 days of receipt of the request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If the request is rejected
in whole or in part, the notice shall include the reasons for that
decision.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses, costs or charges to be approved

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** In determining whether
costs, charges or expenses are reasonably incurred, the Secretary or
President of the Treasury Board, or the Treasury Board, as the case may
be, shall take into consideration, among other things,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether it is or was necessary to retain legal
  counsel before the commencement of an action or proceeding;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the skill, experience and specialized knowledge of
  the legal counsel;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the complexity and novelty of the matter;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount of money or value of any property that is
  involved;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the time expended or to be expended by the legal
  counsel;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number of documents prepared; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the place where, and the circumstances under which,
  the services are rendered.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-permitted basis for refusal

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A request for an
indemnification or an advance shall not be refused based solely on the
fact that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the director or officer, as the case may be, acted
  negligently; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the director or officer has agreed to a settlement of
  the action or proceeding.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771471 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Any approval of an
indemnification or an advance under section 4, 5 or 6 is conditional on
the individual

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} providing a written undertaking to the Secretary of
  the Treasury Board in the form required by the Treasury Board to
  reimburse any amounts to which they are not entitled;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} providing a written authorization to pay to Her
  Majesty any amounts that the director or officer is to receive or is
  awarded as costs, charges and expenses;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} notifying the Secretary of the Treasury Board in
  writing of any substantial changes to the information that is required
  under subsection 2(2) including the details of any settlement of the
  action or proceeding;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} providing, on the request of the Secretary or
  President of the Treasury Board, or the Treasury Board, as the case
  may be, information with respect to the progress of the action or
  proceeding, any changes in scope of liability or any other matter
  relevant to the indemnification or advance that has been approved;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the approval is given prior to the conclusion of
  the action or proceeding, submitting to the Secretary of the Treasury
  Board, on a monthly basis, any invoice and any other supporting
  documents that reflect the costs incurred; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} providing to the Secretary of the Treasury Board at
  the conclusion of the action or proceeding a final accounting of any
  amounts advanced and reimbursing any amounts that are not expended.

## [Obligation to Repay]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771480 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repayment

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Any amounts indemnified
or advanced shall be repaid

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the conditions set out in section 10 are not met;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the amounts are approved as a result of a
  misrepresentation or the concealment of a material fact; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the amounts are paid as the result of an
  administrative error.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771486 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
