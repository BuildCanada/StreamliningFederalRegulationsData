## Technical Data Control Regulations {#technical-data-control-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-345
:::

[DEFENCE PRODUCTION ACT](/eng/acts/D-1)

Registration 1986-03-20

Regulations Respecting the Control of Unclassified Technical Data
Disclosing Critical Technology

P.C. 1986-695 1986-03-20
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Supply and Services, pursuant to section 26 of the
[Defence Production Act](/eng/acts/D-1), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed Regulations respecting the control of unclassified technical
data disclosing critical technology.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-896776 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Technical Data Control
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-345).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-896778 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[array of design or manufacturing know-how]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[array of design or manufacturing
    know-how]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the know-how and related
    technical information required to achieve a development, production
    or utilization purpose and includes any services, processes,
    procedures, specifications, design data and criteria and testing
    techniques required to achieve such development, production or
    utilization purpose; ([savoir-faire en matière de conception ou de
    fabrication]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canadian contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who is

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian citizen, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a permanent resident,

      located in Canada, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that is incorporated under the laws
      of Canada or a province; ([entrepreneur canadien]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Canadian government agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian government agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    department, departmental corporation or Crown corporation set out in
    Schedule I; ([organisme canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[certified Canadian contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certified Canadian contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    Canadian contractor

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} who has been certified as a certified contractor
      in accordance with section 6, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whose certification has not expired or been
      revoked; ([entrepreneur canadien agréé]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[certified contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certified contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a certified Canadian contractor, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a certified United States contractor referred to
      in and governed by Directive 5230.25; ([entrepreneur
      agréé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[critical technology]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[critical technology]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means technology that
    consists of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an array of design or manufacturing know-how,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} keystone manufacturing, inspection and test
      equipment,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} keystone materials, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} goods accompanied by sophisticated know-how,

    that would make a significant contribution to the military potential
    of any one or more countries and the unauthorized disclosure of
    which may be detrimental to the security of Canada or to cooperative
    efforts for defence being carried out by Canada or an associated
    government; ([technologie cruciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Crown corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Crown corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11); ([société
    d'État]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in the
    [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);
    ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[departmental corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[departmental corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
    meaning as in the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);
    ([établissement public]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Directive 5230.25]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Directive 5230.25]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the United States
    Department of Defense Directive 5230.25 entitled Withholding of
    Unclassified Technical Data from Public Disclosure (49 Federal
    Register 40040 of 10 December, 1984); ([directive
    5230.25]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[goods accompanied by sophisticated know-how]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods accompanied by sophisticated
    know-how]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that cannot be used unless certain technical
      information or operation, application, maintenance or other
      know-how is provided or disclosed to the prospective user thereof,
      or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which embedded know-how is
      inherently derivable by reverse engineering or is revealed by use
      of the goods; ([biens exigeant un savoir-faire
      sophistiqué]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Joint Certification Office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Joint Certification Office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an office
    jointly staffed by the United States and Canada that manages and
    administers the certification process set out in section 6; ([Bureau
    mixte d'agrément]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[keystone manufacturing, inspection and test equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[keystone manufacturing, inspection and test
    equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the equipment specifically
    necessary for the effective application of technical information and
    know-how; ([matériel essentiel de fabrication, d'inspection et
    d'essai]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[keystone materials]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[keystone materials]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means materials
    specifically necessary for the effective application of technical
    information and know-how; ([matériaux essentiels]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[located in Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[located in Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a person,
    means that the person is ordinarily resident in Canada; ([se
    trouvant au Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[other legitimate business activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[other legitimate business activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} providing or seeking to provide equipment or
      technology to a foreign government with the approval of the
      Government of Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} bidding or preparing to bid on a sale of surplus
      government property,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} selling or producing products for the commercial
      domestic marketplace or, if any required export license or permit
      has been obtained, for the commercial foreign marketplace,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} engaging in scientific research in a professional
      capacity, or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} acting as a subcontractor to a concern engaged in
      any activity described in any of paragraphs (a) to (d). ([autres
      activités commerciales admissibles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/91-522, s. 1(E)\]]{.Repealed}

[[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in the Immigration Act, 1976; ([résident permanent]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[technical data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[technical data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any blueprint, drawing, plan, instruction,
      computer software or other documentation that discloses critical
      technology, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other technical information that discloses
      critical technology if the critical technology can be used or
      adapted to be used to design, engineer, produce, manufacture,
      operate, repair, overhaul or reproduce any equipment; ([données
      techniques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unclassified technical data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unclassified technical data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means technical
    data other than technical data that, in the interest of the national
    security of Canada, are subject to a system of security
    classification; ([données techniques non
    classifiées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any day other than a
    Saturday or a holiday. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-522, s. 1
- SOR/94-603, s. 1(F)
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-896826 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of unclassified technical data that are
  administered and controlled by a Canadian government agency.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations shall not be read or construed so
  as to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} apply to scientific or educational data that are in
    the public domain; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} apply to or affect the release of technical data by
    a Canadian government agency to any foreign government or
    international organization, or to their respective representatives
    or contractors, pursuant to official agreements or arrangements made
    with the Canadian government agency or the Canadian government.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-522, s. 2
- SOR/94-603, s. 2
:::

## [Joint Certification Office]{.HTitleText1} {#h-896834 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  Canadian contractor who wishes to request information or make an
  inquiry with respect to any matter to which these Regulations relate
  shall direct the request or inquiry to the Joint Certification Office
  at the following address:

  United States-Canada Joint Certification Office

  Defense Logistics Services Center

  Federal Center

  Battle Creek, Michigan, USA, 49017-3084

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may appoint one or more persons to
  represent Canada in, and participate in, the operation of the Joint
  Certification Office.

## [Eligibility to Obtain Data]{.HTitleText1} {#h-896842 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A certified contractor is,
subject to any other provision of these Regulations, eligible to obtain
unclassified technical data.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-522, s. 3
:::

## [Application for Certification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-896846 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  Canadian contractor who wishes to be certified as a certified
  contractor shall apply for certification to the Joint Certification
  Office at the address set out in subsection 4(1) on a form obtained
  from that Office and shall certify as follows on the form:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the person who will act on its behalf as
    custodian of any unclassified technical data that it obtains is a
    Canadian citizen or a permanent resident of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the unclassified technical data that it wishes
    to obtain are or will be needed

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to bid or perform on a contract with a Canadian
      government agency, the United States Department of Defense or
      another agency of the Government of Canada or of the United
      States, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for other legitimate business activities in
      which the Canadian contractor is engaged or plans to engage;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that it

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} acknowledges its responsibilities under any
      applicable export control laws of Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} agrees that any unclassified technical data that
      it obtains will not be disseminated by it in a manner that would
      contravene the export control laws of Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that it will not provide access to any unclassified
    technical data that it obtains to persons other than its employees
    or persons acting on its behalf, except

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where it obtains the data from a Canadian
      government agency, the United States Department of Defense or an
      agency of the United States Department of Defense, with the
      permission of the Canadian government agency, the United States
      Department of Defense or the agency of the United States
      Department of Defense, as the case may be, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with paragraph 9(6)(b) or
      Directive 5230.25, whichever is applicable;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} that no person employed by it or acting on its
    behalf who will have access to unclassified technical data

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is debarred or suspended from performing, or is
      otherwise ineligible to perform, on Canadian or United States
      Government contracts,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has contravened the export control laws of the
      United States,

    - [(ii.1)]{.lawlabel} has been convicted of a contravention of the
      export control laws of Canada, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has had a certification revoked under section 8
      or under Directive 5230.25; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} that it

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is not debarred or suspended from performing, and
      is not otherwise ineligible to perform, on Canadian or United
      States government contracts,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has not contravened the export control laws of
      the United States,

    - [(ii.1)]{.lawlabel} has not been convicted of a contravention of
      the export control laws of Canada, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has not had a certification revoked under
      section 8 or under Directive 5230.25.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Canadian contractor referred to in subsection (1)
  shall disclose on the form referred to in that subsection any business
  activity of the contractor in relation to which the contractor will or
  may wish to obtain unclassified technical data, described sufficiently
  so that, in the event that the contractor subsequently makes a request
  for unclassified technical data, an evaluation can be made of whether
  the unclassified technical data requested by the contractor properly
  relates to any such business activity.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a Canadian contractor referred to in
  subsection (1) cannot truthfully certify as required by paragraph
  (1)(e) or (f), the contractor may request that the application for
  certification be accepted on the basis of extenuating circumstances
  set forth by the contractor in writing and submitted to the Joint
  Certification Office with the application for certification.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a Canadian contractor applies for certification
  pursuant to subsection (1), the contractor shall attest, on the form
  on which the application is made and in the manner indicated in the
  form, that the information and attestations are true, complete and
  accurate to the best of the knowledge and belief of the contractor and
  are provided in good faith.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where, for the purpose of determining whether a
  Canadian contractor who has submitted a completed form to the Joint
  Certification Office should be certified as a certified contractor,
  the Joint Certification Office requires information additional to that
  provided on or in conjunction with the form, the contractor shall
  provide such additional information as the Joint Certification Office
  may reasonably request.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-522, s. 4
:::

## [Disposition of Application for Certification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-896875 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an
  application for certification is made under subsection 6(1) by a
  Canadian contractor, the Joint Certification Office may accept the
  application or refer it to an officer of the Department of Supply and
  Services designated by the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an application is referred to an officer
  pursuant to subsection (1), the officer shall review the application
  and either accept or reject it.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an application is rejected pursuant to
  subsection (2), the Canadian contractor may, within 20 working days
  after being notified of the rejection, request that the application be
  reviewed by the Deputy Minister of Supply and Services and, on the
  review, the Deputy Minister may accept the application or confirm its
  rejection.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where an application is accepted pursuant to this
  section, the Canadian contractor is thereby certified as a certified
  contractor for a period commencing on the date that the application is
  accepted and ending five years after that date, unlesss the
  certification is sooner revoked under section 8.

## [Suspension and Revocation of Certification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-896881 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Minister receives credible information from which he determines
  that a certified Canadian contractor

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has contravened any provision of these Regulations,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has contravened any export control laws of Canada,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has not complied with anything that the contractor
    certified in the application made by the contractor pursuant to
    subsection 6(1),

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} omitted from the application referred to in
    paragraph (c) any material fact or mis-stated any material fact
    therein, or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} certified in bad faith to anything in the
    application referred to in paragraph (c),

  the Minister may, by notice in writing to the contractor, suspend the
  contractor's eligibility to obtain unclassified technical data.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of suspension sent pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall state the reason for the suspension in sufficient
  detail to enable the contractor to make representations to the
  Minister pursuant to subsection (3).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A certified Canadian contractor to whom a notice is
  sent pursuant to subsection (1) may make representations in writing to
  the Minister in respect of the suspension and, where the contractor
  shows the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the reason for the suspension no longer
    exists, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the decision to suspend the contractor ought
    not to have been made,

  the Minister shall remove the suspension.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where, within 20 working days after the date of the
  notice sent pursuant to subsection (1), the contractor has not shown
  as described in paragraph (3)(a) or (b), the Minister may revoke the
  contractor's certification as a certified Canadian contractor.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister revokes a contractor's
  certification as a certified Canadian contractor pursuant to
  subsection (4), the Minister shall give written notice of the
  revocation to the contractor.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Within thirty days after a notice of revocation is
  given in accordance with subsection (5), a Canadian contractor whose
  certification has been revoked may submit to the Minister new or
  additional information showing why the revocation should not have been
  made and, in such a case, the Minister shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} reconsider the revocation in light of the new or
    additional information; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give written notice to the contractor as to whether
    or not the contractor's certification will be restored.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-522, s. 5
- SOR/94-603, s. 3(F)
:::

## [Access to and Control of Unclassified Technical Data]{.HTitleText1} {#h-896902 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  certified contractor may request unclassified technical data by
  submitting to the Canadian government agency that administers and
  controls the data a request in writing therefor.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request made pursuant to subsection (1) shall
  include a statement of the use that the contractor intends to make of
  the unclassified technical data.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (4) and (5), a Canadian
  government agency to which a request for unclassified technical data
  is made by a certified contractor pursuant to subsection (1) shall
  authorize release of the data to the contractor unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the certification of the contractor stands
    suspended;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the agency is of the opinion that the data
    requested are not properly related to any business activity
    disclosed by the contractor in the course of becoming a certified
    contractor; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the data are requested for a purpose other than to
    permit the contractor to bid or perform on a contract with any other
    Canadian government agency or any other agency of the Government of
    Canada, and the Canadian government agency determines that the
    purpose for which the data are being sought is one that may
    jeopardize a technological or operational military advantage of
    Canada.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a Canadian government agency to which a request
  is made by a certified contractor pursuant to subsection (1) is
  unable, on the information provided by the contractor, to determine
  whether paragraph (3)(b) applies, the agency may request the
  contractor to provide additional information with respect to the use
  that the contractor intends to make of the unclassified technical data
  or, if the agency considers it appropriate, may request the contractor
  to submit, pursuant to section 6, a new application in which the
  business activity of the contractor to which the data relates is
  disclosed by the contractor pursuant to subsection 6(2).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A Canadian government agency to which a request for
  unclassified technical data is made by a certified contractor pursuant
  to subsection (1) may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the interest of the national security of Canada,
    refuse to authorize the release of the data if the agency deems that
    the purpose for which the data are sought is such as may possibly
    jeopardize the development or maintenance of a defence industrial
    base in Canada or an important technological or operational military
    advantage of Canada or the agency may in such circumstance authorize
    the release of the data subject to such restricitons or limitations
    as the agency considers advisable;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} impose special conditions or safeguards on the use
    of the data in order to prevent the data from becoming available to
    persons who are not authorized to obtain it; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} require the contractor to enter into an agreement
    governing the use of the data by the contractor, providing for
    payment by the contractor and generally setting forth the rights and
    obligations of the contractor and the agency with respect to the
    unclassified technical data.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A certified contractor who receives unclassified
  technical data pursuant to these Regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall use the data only for purposes that are
    consistent with the matters to which it has certified pursuant to
    subsection 6(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (7), shall not disclose the
    data to any person other than another certified contractor in
    relation to a business activity disclosed by the other contractor in
    the course of becoming a certified contractor; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} shall comply with any restrictions, limitations,
    conditions or safeguards imposed pursuant to subsection (5).

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} A certified contractor who wishes to disclose
  unclassified technical data other than as permitted by subsection (6)
  may do so only if authorized by the Canadian government agency that
  administers and controls the data and only in accordance with that
  authorization.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Where a request by a certified contractor to a
  Canadian government agency

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to subsection (1) for unclassified
    technical data, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to subsection (7) for authorization to
    disclose unclassified technical data other than as permitted by
    subsection (6),

  is denied by the agency, the agency shall, in writing, give its reason
  for the denial to the contractor and inform the contractor of any
  procedures that have been established to which the contractor may have
  recourse if the contractor wishes the agency to reconsider the
  request.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-522, s. 6
- SOR/94-603, s. 4
