## [Rules of Origin]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841604 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following provisions of
the Protocol on Rules of Origin and Origin Procedures of the
Canada--European Union Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement have
the force of law in Canada:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Articles 1 and 2;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} paragraphs 1 to 3 of Article 3;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Articles 4 to 17; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Annexes 1, 4, 5, 5‑A and 7.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841610 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Ind7D41_hq_20654){#fn_Ind7D41_hq_20654-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 97 of the [Canada--European Union Comprehensive Economic and
Trade Agreement Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-4.8), chapter 6 of the
Statutes of Canada, 2017, comes into force, but if they are registered
after that day, they come into force on the day on which they are
registered.
