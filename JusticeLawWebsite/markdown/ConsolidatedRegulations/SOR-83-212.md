[GARNISHMENT, ATTACHMENT AND PENSION DIVERSION ACT](/eng/acts/G-2)

Garnishment and Attachment Regulations

P.C. 1983-658 1983-03-03
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to sections 9, 12, 14, and 19 of the
[Garnishment, Attachment and Pension Diversion
Act](/eng/acts/G-2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn1star_e){#fn1star_e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting garnishment and
attachment proceedings](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-212).

## [Exclusions from Salary]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878219 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
definition [[salary]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in sections 4 and 16 of the
Act, the following amounts are deemed to be or to have been excluded
from a person's salary:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any contribution or payment required by law to be
  made out of the moneys payable to the person, including,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Canada Pension Plan and Quebec Pension Plan
    contributions,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} contributions required to be made pursuant to any
    Act listed in the schedule to the Act,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} employment insurance contributions,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} income tax payments, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} federal and provincial statutory tax deductions;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any amount that is a compulsory premium payment
  deducted from the moneys payable to the person for purposes of
  insurance or health care including,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} payments for a provincial medical care or hospital
    insurance plan,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} payments for the Public Service Health Care Plan,
    where the person is employed outside Canada, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} payments for disability insurance under the
    Disability Insurance Plan or the Public Service Management Insurance
    Plan except where such payment is part of union membership dues or
    fees;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any amount deducted pursuant to an Act of the
  Parliament of Canada other than Part I of the Act;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any amount payable to the person as a remuneration
  supplement in respect of costs of a provincial medical care or
  hospital insurance plan;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any amount deducted as union membership dues or fees
  but not including any other amount deducted and payable to a union;
  and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any amount that is intended as a reimbursement to the
  person for special costs incurred by the person in the course of
  duties and for which the person can produce an invoice or receipt,
  including,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a payment to a Royal Canadian Mounted Police
    member, to help defray the cost of upkeep and replacement of
    articles of uniform,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a payment to a Royal Canadian Mounted Police
    member, whose duties require that member to wear civilian clothing,
    to help defray the cost of upkeep and replacement of articles of
    that clothing,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a reimbursement for the cost of boots or gloves
    required to be worn at work where the person is a supervisory letter
    carrier, a part-time letter carrier or a mail dispatcher,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a payment to furnish canoes or boats or camping
    equipment where the person is employed as a fisheries warden or
    fishery guardian,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} an allowance paid to a pasture manager or a pasture
    rider in compensation for the use of that person's horse,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a reimbursement for the loss of clothing or
    personal property in a marine disaster or shipwreck,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} a reimbursement of costs incurred by an air
    traffic controller in obtaining the annual medical examination
    required to maintain a valid air traffic control licence,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} a reimbursement of the membership fees paid to
    an association or institution, where membership in the association
    or institution is in the interests of the department on behalf of
    which or the Crown corporation, Senate, House of Commons or Library
    of Parliament on behalf of which or by which salary or remuneration
    is payable to the person,

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} a payment of a foreign service premium, a
    post-differential allowance or a salary equalization factor on being
    posted abroad or at an isolated post,

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} a payment of a living cost differential allowance
    pursuant to the Isolated Posts Directive, and

  - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} a reimbursement for the cost of court clothing
    that the person is required to wear to carry out their duties.
