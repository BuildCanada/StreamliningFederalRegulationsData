## Order Dissolving the Canadian Securities Regulation Regime Transition Office {#order-dissolving-the-canadian-securities-regulation-regime-transition-office .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2023-5
:::

[CANADIAN SECURITIES REGULATION REGIME TRANSITION OFFICE
ACT](/eng/acts/C-23.05)

Registration 2023-03-29

Order Dissolving the Canadian Securities Regulation Regime Transition
Office

P.C. 2023-191 2023-03-09
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, under subsection 17(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4752_hq_1660592899){#fn_81000-2-4752_hq_1660592899-ID0EECAA}
of the [Canadian Securities Regulation Regime Transition Office
Act](/eng/acts/C-23.05)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4752_hq_1660045707){#fn_81000-2-4752_hq_1660045707-ID0EBCAA},
dissolves the Canadian Securities Regulation Regime Transition Office
effective March 30, 2023.
