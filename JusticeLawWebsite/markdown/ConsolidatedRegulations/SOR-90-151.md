## Corporation de chauffage urbain de Montréal (CCUM) Incorporation and Sale Authorization Order {#corporation-de-chauffage-urbain-de-montréal-ccum-incorporation-and-sale-authorization-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-151
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1990-02-22

Order Authorizing the Canadian National Railway Company to Procure the
Incorporation of Corporation de Chauffage Urbain de Montréal (CCUM) and
the Subsequent Sale of 7O% of the Shares Thereof to Compagnie
Internationale Financière et Industrielle du Québec CIFIQ Inc.

P.C. 1990-324  1990-02-22
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council, pursuant to subsection 91(6) of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is satisfied that the
Canadian National Railway Company is empowered, pursuant to paragraphs
91(1)(a) and (d) of that Act, to undertake the transactions described in
the annexed Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to paragraphs 91(1)(a) and (d) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to revoke Order in Council P.C.
1988-2254 of September 29, 1988[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-90-151_e_hq_6119){#fn_SOR-90-151_e_hq_6119-ID0ECBAA}
and to make the annexed Order authorizing the Canadian National Railway
Company to procure the incorporation of Corporation de chauffage urbain
de Montréal (CCUM) and the subsequent sale of 70% of the shares thereof
to Compagnie internationale financière et industrielle du Québec CIFIQ
Inc.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921656 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Corporation de chauffage urbain de Montréal (CCUM) Incorporation
and Sale Authorization Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-151).

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921658 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Canadian National
Railway Company is hereby authorized

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to procure the incorporation, under the laws of the
  Province of Quebec, of the Corporation de chauffage urbain de Montréal
  (CCUM), all the shares of which, on incorporation, will be held by the
  Canadian National Railway Company, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to subsequently sell 70% of the issued and
  outstanding shares of the share capital of Corporation de chauffage
  urbain de Montréal (CCUM) to Compagnie internationale financière et
  industrielle du Québec CIFIQ Inc.

on or before December 31, 1990.
