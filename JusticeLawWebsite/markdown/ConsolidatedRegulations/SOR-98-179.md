## Ontario Hydro Nuclear Facilities Exclusion from Part I of the Canada Labour Code Regulations (Industrial Relations) {#ontario-hydro-nuclear-facilities-exclusion-from-part-i-of-the-canada-labour-code-regulations-industrial-relations .Title-of-Act}

[CANADA LABOUR CODE](/eng/acts/L-2)

Ontario Hydro Nuclear Facilities Exclusion from Part I of the Canada
Labour Code Regulations (Industrial Relations)

P.C. 1998-407 1998-03-19

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}

the Minister of Labour, pursuant to sections 121.2[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018683 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means employment with Ontario
    Hydro or a person referred to in paragraph (b) of the definition
    [[nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}. ([emploi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a nuclear facility in
    Ontario that is subject to the [Nuclear Safety and Control
    Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3) or any regulations made under that Act and
    that is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} owned and operated by Ontario Hydro; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} owned or operated by a person other than Ontario
      Hydro if, on the day on which these Regulations come into force or
      on a day after that day, it is owned and operated by Ontario
      Hydro. ([installation nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Labour Relations Act, 1995]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Labour Relations Act, 1995]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Schedule A
    to the Labour Relations and Employment Statute Law Amendment Act,
    1995, S.O. 1995, c. 1. ([Loi de 1995 sur les relations de
    travail]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Labour Relations and Employment Statute Law Amendment Act, 1995]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Labour Relations and Employment Statute Law Amendment Act,
    1995]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means An Act to restore balance and
    stability to labour relations and to promote economic prosperity and
    to make consequential changes to statutes concerning labour
    relations, S.O. 1995, c.1. ([Loi de 1995 modifiant des lois en ce
    qui concerne les relations de travail et l'emploi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-519, s. 1
:::

## [Exclusion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018693 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Employment on or in
connection with a nuclear facility is hereby excluded from the
application of Part I of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2), except
sections 121.2 to 121.5.

## [Application of Ontario Acts and Instruments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018695 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4, the
following Acts and instruments, as amended from time to time, apply, to
the extent that they are relevant, in relation to employment on or in
connection with a nuclear facility:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Labour Relations Act, 1995 and the regulations
  made under it;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the regulations made under the Labour Relations Act,
  R.S.O. 1990, c. L.2, in so far as they are not inconsistent with the
  Labour Relations Act, 1995, and until those regulations are repealed
  or replaced; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any rules of procedure made by the Ontario Labour
  Relations Board under section 110 of the Labour Relations Act, 1995.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
3,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 1(3)(a) of the Labour Relations Act, 1995
  shall be read without reference to the words \"architectural\" and
  \"land surveying\";

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the definition \"employee\" in subsection 1(1) of
  that Act shall include persons whose duties include the supervision of
  other employees; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} every reference in section 15, 54 or 136 of that Act
  to the \"Human Rights Code\" shall be read as a reference to the
  \"[Canadian Human Rights Act](/eng/acts/H-6)\".

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Any proceeding instituted
before the Canada Industrial Relations Board before the day on which
these Regulations come into force shall be continued as if these
Regulations had not been made.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1998, c. 26, s. 85
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018707 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on April 1, 1998.
