## Quebec Sex Offender Information Registration Regulations {#quebec-sex-offender-information-registration-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2005-6
:::

[SEX OFFENDER INFORMATION REGISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/S-8.7)

Registration 2004-12-24

Quebec Sex Offender Information Registration Regulations
::::

::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
WHEREAS the [Sex Offender Information Registration
Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7), (S.C. 2004, c. 10) provides for the creation of a
national sex offender database;

WHEREAS the Act provides that persons who are convicted of offenses of a
sexual nature will be required to report to a designated place to have
the information concerning them registered in a database available to
police services investigating crimes of a sexual nature;

WHEREAS section 18 of the Act provides that the Lieutenant Governor in
Council of a province may, for the purposes of the Act, make regulations
respecting the means by which sex offenders are required to report and
to provide notification, authorizing persons or classes of persons to
collect and to register information, and designating places as
registration centres and the area of the province served by each
registration centre;

WHEREAS it is expedient to make such regulations;

IT IS ORDERED, therefore, on the recommendation of the Minister of
Public Security:

THAT the annexed [Quebec Sex Offender Information Registration
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-6) be made.

December 21, 2004

::: FlushLeft
Lise Thibault
:::

::: FlushLeft
Lieutenant Governor of Quebec
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722030 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Sex Offender Information
Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7).

## [Report and Notification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722032 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A sex
  offender whose main residence is located in Quebec may make any report
  required under section 4.3 of the Act by telephone, facsimile or
  electronic mail to the [Centre québécois d'enregistrement des
  délinquants sexuels de la Sûreté du Québec]{.otherLang lang="fr"} in
  Montreal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A sex offender whose main residence is located in
  Quebec may provide any notification required under section 6 of the
  Act by telephone, facsimile or electronic mail to the [Centre
  québécois d'enregistrement des délinquants sexuels de la Sûreté du
  Québec]{.otherLang lang="fr"} or in person.

## [Persons Authorized to Collect Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722036 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
authorized in Quebec to collect information for the purposes of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the [Sûreté du Québec]{.otherLang
  lang="fr"};

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a member of a municipal police force; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a member of a native police force.

## [Persons Authorized to Register Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722041 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Any member, or other
employee, of the [Sûreté du Québec]{.otherLang lang="fr"} who is
assigned to the [Centre québécois d'enregistrement des délinquants
sexuels de la Sûreté du Québec]{.otherLang lang="fr"} is authorized in
Quebec to register information for the purposes of the Act.

## [Registration Centres]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722043 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A place
  set out in column 1 of the schedule is designated as a registration
  centre in Quebec, and its designated area of service is set out in
  column 2.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The [Centre québécois d'enregistrement des
  délinquants sexuels de la Sûreté du Québec]{.otherLang lang="fr"} in
  Montreal is designated as a registration centre in Quebec, and its
  designated area of service is Quebec.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722047 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
