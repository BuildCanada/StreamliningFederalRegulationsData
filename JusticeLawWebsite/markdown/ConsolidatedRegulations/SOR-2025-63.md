## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1511966 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Radiocommunication
    Act](/eng/acts/R-2). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[correctional facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[correctional facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a building or
    part of a building that is used for the detention of persons and
    established as a correctional facility by order of the Government of
    Quebec, and includes the land occupied by the
    building. ([établissement de détention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
    2 of the Act. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[penitentiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[penitentiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a facility that is
    operated permanently or temporarily by the Correctional Service of
    Canada for the care and custody of inmates, and includes the land
    occupied by the facility. ([pénitencier]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1511973 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employees of correctional services

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to any applicable conditions set out in sections 3 to 14, the
  following employees who are required to install, use, possess, import
  or distribute a jammer in the course of their duties or training are
  exempt from the application of subsection 4(4) and paragraph 9(1)(b)
  of the Act in respect of those activities:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} employees of the Correctional Service of Canada;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} employees of the [Sous-ministériat des services
    correctionnels]{.otherLang lang="fr"} of Quebec's [Ministère de la
    Sécurité publique]{.otherLang lang="fr"}.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procurement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following persons who are required to import a
  jammer on behalf of the Correctional Service of Canada or the
  [Sous-ministériat des services correctionnels]{.otherLang lang="fr"}
  of Quebec's [Ministère de la Sécurité publique]{.otherLang lang="fr"}
  in the course of their duties are exempt from the application of
  subsection 4(4) of the Act in respect of that activity:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of Public Works and Government
    Services of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} employees of the Department of Public Works and
    Government Services of Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of Public Security of Quebec; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} employees of the [Sous-ministériat des services à
    la gestion]{.otherLang lang="fr"} of Quebec's [Ministère de la
    Sécurité publique]{.otherLang lang="fr"}.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response to solicitation of bids

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person or entity is exempt from the application of
  subsection 4(4) of the Act in respect of the offering for sale of a
  jammer if the offer is in response to a solicitation of bids in
  relation to the provision of jammers to the Correctional Service of
  Canada or the [Sous-ministériat des services
  correctionnels]{.otherLang lang="fr"} of Quebec's [Ministère de la
  Sécurité publique]{.otherLang lang="fr"}.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suppliers

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any applicable conditions set out in
  sections 3 to 14, a person or entity referred to in paragraph (a), (b)
  or (c) that is required to install, use, possess, manufacture, import,
  distribute, lease, offer for sale or sell a jammer in accordance with
  the terms of a contract referred to in that paragraph is exempt from
  the application of subsection 4(4) and paragraph 9(1)(b) of the Act in
  respect of those activities:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person or entity that has entered into a contract
    with the Government of Canada for the provision of jammers, or
    services related to jammers, to the Correctional Service of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person or entity that has entered into a contract
    with the Government of Quebec for the provision of jammers, or
    services related to jammers, to the [Sous-ministériat des services
    correctionnels]{.otherLang lang="fr"} of Quebec's [Ministère de la
    Sécurité publique]{.otherLang lang="fr"}; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person or entity that performs any part of the
    work under a contract referred to in paragraph (a) or (b) or any
    related subcontract.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purposes

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The exemptions under subsections (1) to (4) are
  granted for the following purposes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} public safety, including with respect to
    penitentiaries and prisons;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the investigation or prosecution of offences in
    Canada, including the preservation of evidence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the protection of property or the prevention of
    serious harm to any person.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1511997 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  the exemptions under subsection 2(1) and (4) are invoked, the
  Correctional Service of Canada or the [Sous-ministériat des services
  correctionnels]{.otherLang lang="fr"} of Quebec's [Ministère de la
  Sécurité publique]{.otherLang lang="fr"}, as the case may be, must
  provide a written notice to the Minister that contains the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the telephone number and email address of a person
    who is responsible for responding, during and outside of business
    hours, to inquiries related to jammers made by the Minister or by
    inspectors who are appointed under paragraph 5(1)(j) of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, title, telephone number and email address
    of every contact person who is responsible for jammers and for
    ensuring compliance with this Order;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for each penitentiary or correctional facility
    where a jammer will be installed or used

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name and postal address of the penitentiary
      or correctional facility,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an indication of whether the jammer will be used
      on a sporadic or continuous basis,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the radio frequency bands in which the jammer
      will be operating, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the month and year during which use of the
      jammer is intended to begin; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an exemption under subsection 2(4),
    the name of any person or entity to which the exemption applies and
    the locations where a jammer is intended to be used.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Update information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Correctional Service of Canada or the
  [Sous-ministériat des services correctionnels]{.otherLang lang="fr"}
  of Quebec's [Ministère de la Sécurité publique]{.otherLang lang="fr"},
  as the case may be, must notify the Minister of any updates to the
  information provided under subsection (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of information provided under paragraph
    (1)(a) or (b), as soon as feasible after the information changes;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of information provided under paragraph
    (1)(c) or (d), before the intended changes are implemented.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confirm information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Correctional Service of Canada or the
  [Sous-ministériat des services correctionnels]{.otherLang lang="fr"}
  of Quebec's [Ministère de la Sécurité publique]{.otherLang lang="fr"},
  as the case may be, must confirm the accuracy of the information
  provided under subsections (1) and (2) once a year, on or before the
  anniversary of the day on which this Order comes into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to NAV CANADA

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Correctional Service of Canada or the [Sous-ministériat des services
  correctionnels]{.otherLang lang="fr"} of Quebec's [Ministère de la
  Sécurité publique]{.otherLang lang="fr"}, as the case may be, must
  provide to NAV CANADA a written notice of their intention to use a
  jammer to interfere with or obstruct any radiocommunication at a
  penitentiary or correctional facility at least 28 days before the
  jammers are used for the first time at that penitentiary or
  correctional facility. The notice must contain the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and postal address of the penitentiary or
    correctional facility;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which use of the jammer is intended to
    begin; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the power levels and antenna patterns of the jammer
    and the radio frequency bands on which the jammer will be operating.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Update information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), if any of the information
  provided under subsection (1) is expected to change, a new written
  notice must be provided to NAV CANADA at least 14 days before

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the jammer is used for the first
    time; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it has already been used for the first time, the
    day on which the change is implemented.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice referred to in subsection (2) is not
  required if the change is only in respect of information referred to
  in paragraph (1)(c) and the change does not have the potential to
  increase the interference with or obstruction of radiocommunication
  caused by the jammer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on installation and use

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  employee referred to in subsection 2(1) or a person or entity referred
  to in subsection 2(4) must not install or use a jammer in any location
  other than those notified to the Minister in accordance with section
  3.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on use by suppliers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person or entity referred to in subsection 2(4)
  must not use a jammer unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the use is carried out under the supervision of an
    employee referred to in subsection 2(1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the use is to test the functionality
    of the jammer, to perform maintenance on the jammer or to provide
    training in relation to jammers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Training

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An employee who carries out
an activity under the exemption set out in subsection 2(1) must have
received or be receiving specialized training for that activity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to directives

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Correctional Service of
Canada or the [Sous-ministériat des services correctionnels]{.otherLang
lang="fr"} of Quebec's [Ministère de la Sécurité publique]{.otherLang
lang="fr"}, as the case may be, must ensure that its directives in
respect of jammers are accessible to the employees referred to in
subsection 2(1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrict interference or obstruction

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  employee referred to in subsection 2(1) or a person or entity referred
  to in subsection 2(4) that installs or uses a jammer must make every
  reasonable effort to restrict the jammer's interference with or
  obstruction of radiocommunication to the smallest physical area, the
  fewest number of radio frequencies, the appropriate power level and
  the minimum duration required to accomplish the intended purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrict interference or obstruction
  outside facility

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the jammer is intended to be used on a continuous
  basis, an employee referred to in subsection 2(1) or a person or
  entity referred to in subsection 2(4) that installs or uses a jammer
  must also make every reasonable effort to restrict the jammer's
  interference with or obstruction of radiocommunication in the area
  above the penitentiary or correctional facility and in the areas of
  the penitentiary or correctional facility before the visitor control
  point.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimize emissions and exposure

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An employee referred to in
subsection 2(1) or a person or entity referred to in subsection 2(4)
that installs or uses a jammer must do so in a manner that minimizes
unwanted emissions and the exposure of any person to radio frequency
fields.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Analysis of jammer parameters

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Correctional Service of Canada or the [Sous-ministériat des services
  correctionnels]{.otherLang lang="fr"} of Quebec's [Ministère de la
  Sécurité publique]{.otherLang lang="fr"}, as the case may be, must
  conduct an analysis of each jammer or system of jammers before it is
  first used at a penitentiary or correctional facility and before each
  subsequent change to its parameters to determine

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its parameters; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the measures to be taken to restrict its
    interference with or obstruction of radiocommunication in accordance
    with section 8.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jammer analysis --- records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Correctional Service of Canada or the
  [Sous-ministériat des services correctionnels]{.otherLang lang="fr"}
  of Quebec's [Ministère de la Sécurité publique]{.otherLang lang="fr"},
  as the case may be, must maintain a record of each analysis conducted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jammer characteristics

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  employee referred to in subsection 2(1) or a person or entity referred
  to in subsection 2(4) must ensure that any jammer for which they are
  responsible allows for adjustments to its power levels and the radio
  frequencies that it can interfere with or obstruct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jammer technical specifications ---
  records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Correctional Service of Canada or the
  [Sous-ministériat des services correctionnels]{.otherLang lang="fr"}
  of Quebec's [Ministère de la Sécurité publique]{.otherLang lang="fr"},
  as the case may be, must maintain a record of the technical
  specifications of each jammer that is used at a penitentiary or
  correctional facility.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interference and obstruction test

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Correctional Service of Canada or the [Sous-ministériat des services
  correctionnels]{.otherLang lang="fr"} of Quebec's [Ministère de la
  Sécurité publique]{.otherLang lang="fr"}, as the case may be, intends
  to use a jammer on a continuous basis at a penitentiary or
  correctional facility, it must conduct a test --- in the areas of the
  penitentiary or correctional facility before the visitor control point
  --- of the interference with or obstruction of radiocommunication that
  continuous jammer use causes to devices that are used for wireless
  telecommunications services. The test must be conducted for each radio
  frequency band that the jammer interferes with or obstructs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When test is required

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The test must be conducted within a reasonable time
  after any of the following situations occur:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a jammer or system of jammers is used on a
    continuous basis for the first time at penitentiary or correctional
    facility;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} changes are made to the parameters of the jammer or
    system of jammers referred to in paragraph (a); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister or an inspector appointed under
    paragraph 5(1)(j) of the Act requests --- in the course of verifying
    compliance or preventing non-compliance with this Order or
    investigating interference that could be caused by the use of
    jammers at the penitentiary or correctional facility --- that the
    test be conducted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Correctional Service of Canada or the
  [Sous-ministériat des services correctionnels]{.otherLang lang="fr"}
  of Quebec's [Ministère de la Sécurité publique]{.otherLang lang="fr"},
  as the case may be, must maintain a record of each test conducted that
  contains the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of each person who conducted the test and
    any of their relevant qualifications to conduct it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the start and end dates of the test;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the methods used to test for the
    interference or obstruction;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the wireless telecommunications services for which
    interference or obstruction was detected;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the times and locations at which interference or
    obstruction was detected; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the actions, if any, taken to mitigate the
    interference or obstruction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prevention of non-authorized access and
storage

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** An employee referred to
in subsection 2(1) or a person or entity referred to in subsection 2(4)
must take reasonable measures to ensure that any jammer for which they
are responsible is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} accessible only to employees, persons or entities
  that are exempt from the application of subsection 4(4) of the Act;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} turned off and stored in a secure location or secure
  manner when not in use, including during transport.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuous use --- records

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Correctional Service of Canada or the [Sous-ministériat des services
  correctionnels]{.otherLang lang="fr"} of Quebec's [Ministère de la
  Sécurité publique]{.otherLang lang="fr"}, as the case may be, must
  maintain a record for each jammer that is intended to be used on a
  continuous basis at a penitentiary or correctional facility. The
  record must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each occasion when a jammer is turned on,
    either initially or after a period of non-operation

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the location at which, date on which and, if
      known, the time at which it was turned on,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the radio frequency bands and, if known, the
      radio frequencies that were interfered with or obstructed,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the employee who was responsible
      for the jammer when it was turned on, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the reason why it was turned on, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each occasion when a jammer stops, either by
    being turned off or becoming non-operation

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the location at which and date on which and, if
      known, time at which the jammer stopped,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the employee who was responsible for
      the jammer when the jammer stopped, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the reason the jammer stopped.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}All other use --- records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Correctional Service of Canada or the
  [Sous-ministériat des services correctionnels]{.otherLang lang="fr"}
  of Quebec's [Ministère de la Sécurité publique]{.otherLang lang="fr"},
  as the case may be, must maintain a record of each use --- by an
  employee referred to in subsection 2(1) or a person or entity referred
  to in subsection 2(4) --- of a jammer intended to be used on a
  sporadic basis. The record must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the location and date and, if known, time;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the employee, person or entity that
    used the jammer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the radio frequencies that were interfered with or
    obstructed; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the purposes referred to in subsection 2(5) for
    which the jammer was used.

effect on the fifth anniversary of the day on which it comes into force.
