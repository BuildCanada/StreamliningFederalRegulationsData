## [Violations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737864 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The contravention of any of
the following provisions is a violation that may be proceeded with under
sections 73.11 to 73.5 of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a provision of the Act set out in Column 1 of Part 1
  of the schedule;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a provision of the Act and a provision of the
  [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-184) set out in columns 1 and 2
  of Part 2 of the schedule or, in the case of an item of Part 2 of the
  schedule where no provision of the Act is set out in column 1, a
  provision of the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist
  Financing Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-184) set out in
  column 2 of Part 2 of the schedule; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a provision of the Act and a provision of the
  [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing
  Suspicious Transaction Reporting
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-317) set out in Columns 1 and 2
  of Part 3 of the schedule.

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2019-240, s. 107\]]{.Repealed}

## [Classification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737872 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  violation is classified as a minor, serious or very serious violation,
  as set out in column 2 of Part 1 of the schedule and in column 3 of
  Parts 2 and 3 of the schedule.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A series of minor violations identified on a notice
  of violation shall be considered to be a serious violation for the
  purpose of section 73.21 of the Act if the total of the penalties for
  the violations set out in the notice is equal to or greater than
  \$10,000.

## [Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737878 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to subsection
73.1(2) of the Act, the range of penalties in respect of a violation is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$1 to \$1,000 in the case of a minor violation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$1 to \$100,000 in the case of a serious violation;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$1 to \$500,000 in the case of a very serious
  violation.

## [Additional Criteria]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737883 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
73.11 of the Act, the history of compliance by the person or entity with
the Act, other than Part 2 of the Act, the [Proceeds of Crime (Money
Laundering) and Terrorist Financing
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-184), the [Proceeds of Crime
(Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Suspicious Transaction
Reporting Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-317) and the [Proceeds
of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Registration
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-121) are prescribed as criteria
that are to be taken into account in determining the amount of a
penalty.

## [Service of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737887 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Service
  of any document under Part 4.1 of the Act may be made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person, by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} personally serving the document on the person,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} leaving the document with someone who appears to
      be an adult member of the same household at the last known address
      or usual place of residence of the person, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} sending the document by registered mail,
      courier, fax or other electronic means to the person's last known
      address or usual place of residence; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an entity, by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} leaving the document at the entity's head office
      or place of business, with an officer or other person who appears
      to be in control or management of the head office or place of
      business,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} sending the document by registered mail, courier
      or fax to the head office or place of business of the entity, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} sending the document by electronic means other
      than by fax to any person referred to in subparagraph (i).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2023-193, s. 41\]]{.Repealed}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** In the absence of proof to
the contrary, a document --- other than a document that is personally
served --- is deemed to be served

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a document that is left with an adult
  referred to in subparagraph 7(1)(a)(ii), on the day the document is
  left with the adult;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a document that is sent by registered
  mail or courier, on the tenth day after the date indicated in the
  receipt issued by the postal or courier service; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a document sent by fax or other
  electronic means, on the day of transmission.

## [Interest Rate Determination]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737903 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 73.28 of the Act, the prescribed rate of interest
  at any time in a particular quarter is the total of:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the rate that is the simple arithmetic mean,
    expressed as a percentage per year and rounded to the next higher
    whole percentage where the mean is not a whole percentage, of all
    amounts each of which is the average equivalent yield, expressed as
    a percentage per year, of Government of Canada Treasury Bills that
    mature approximately three months after their date of issue and that
    are sold at auctions of Government of Canada Treasury Bills during
    the first month of the quarter preceding the particular quarter; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} four per cent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The interest referred to in subsection (1) is
  calculated and compounded monthly.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1)
  [[quarter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a period of three consecutive
  months ending on March 31, June 30, September 30 or December 31.
