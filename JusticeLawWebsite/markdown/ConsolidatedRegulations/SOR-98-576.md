## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025343 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[capacity plate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[capacity plate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a plate pertaining to
    the recommended safe limits of engine power and recommended gross
    load capacity. ([plaque de capacité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[conformity plate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[conformity plate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a notice in the form
    of a plate that pertains to compliance with construction standards.
    ([plaque de conformité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025347 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The charge to be paid for
the issue of a capacity plate is \$5.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The charge to be paid for
the issue of a conformity plate is \$5.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025350 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on April 1, 1999.
