## Outerwear Fabrics Remission Order, 1998 {#outerwear-fabrics-remission-order-1998 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-90

Outerwear Fabrics Remission Order, 1998

P.C. 1997-2058 1997-12-29

:::::::::::::::: section

the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 101 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025935 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[outerwear apparel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[outerwear apparel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means children's and
    infants' coats, jackets and snow and ski wear; women's and girls'
    jackets and snow and ski wear; men's and boys' jackets and snow and
    ski wear; women's and girls' coats; and, men's and boys' coats made
    from outerwear fabric; ([vêtements de dessus]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[outerwear fabric]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[outerwear fabric]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means broadwoven fabrics
    consisting solely of nylon or solely of other polyamide fibres or
    filaments; broadwoven fabrics consisting solely of polyester fibres
    or filaments; broadwoven fabrics consisting solely of fibres or
    filaments solely of acetate, solely of triacetate or solely of
    rayon; or broadwoven fabrics consisting solely of cotton fibres, of
    cotton fibres mixed solely with man-made fibres or of man-made
    fibres mixed solely with other man-made fibres. ([tissu pour
    vêtements de dessus]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025939 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 and
4, remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable
under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), to a manufacturer of
outerwear fabrics set out in the schedule in respect of outerwear
fabrics imported into Canada by the manufacturer during the period
beginning on January 1, 1998 and ending on December 31, 2004 for use in
the manufacture of outerwear apparel.

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3
and 4, remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable

beginning on January 1, 2005, and ending on December 31, 2006 for use in

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission granted under
this Order in respect of imports during any calendar year shall not
exceed the total amount of customs duties remitted to the manufacturer
under the Outerwear Fabrics and Outerwear Apparel Remission Order in
respect of outerwear fabrics imported during 1995.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on
condition that a claim for remission is made to the Minister of National
Revenue within three years after the day on which the outerwear fabrics
are imported into Canada.
