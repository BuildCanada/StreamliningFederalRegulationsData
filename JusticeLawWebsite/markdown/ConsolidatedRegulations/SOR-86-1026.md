## Ferry Cable Regulations {#ferry-cable-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-1026
:::

[CANADIAN NAVIGABLE WATERS ACT](/eng/acts/N-22)

NAVIGABLE WATERS PROTECTION ACT

Registration 1986-10-16

Regulations Respecting Ferry Cables in Navigable Waters

P.C. 1986-2335 1986-10-16
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to paragraphs 28(a) and (b) and
section 29 of the Navigable Waters Protection Act, is pleased hereby to
revoke the [Ferry Cable Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1026),
C.R.C., c. 1230 and to make the annexed [Regulations respecting ferry
cables in navigable waters](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1026), in
substitution therefor.
:::

## [Approval]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888435 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall lay, stretch or maintain a ferry cable across, over, in
  or under any navigable water unless his plans for the ferry cable are
  approved by the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall approve the plans referred to in
  subsection (1) if the Minister determines that the operation of the
  ferry cable will not interfere with the safety of navigation on the
  navigable water and that the vessel to be used in the operation of the
  ferry cable meets the requirements prescribed by or under the [Canada
  Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-374, s. 6
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The plans referred to in
subsection 3(1) shall contain the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the navigable water, the proposed
  location of the ferry cable and the depth of water at the proposed
  location;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the type of operations for which the ferry cable is
  to be used;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the specifications and description of the ferry
  cable;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the lighting and signals proposed for the on-shore
  terminal ends of the ferry cable;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the proposed hours of operation of the ferry cable;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the proposed schedule of crossings of the navigable
  water; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} where the ferry cable is to be operated on a seasonal
  basis, the proposed period of operation of the ferry cable.

## [Lights]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888449 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  owner of a ferry cable shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} install a red light and a green light at each of
    the on-shore terminal ends of the ferry cable in such a manner that
    the lights are clearly visible from a vessel approaching the ferry
    cable from any direction;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the ferry cable is in the raised position,
    ensure that the red lights referred to in paragraph (a) are
    illuminated;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the ferry cable is in the down position,
    ensure that the green lights referred to in paragraph (a) are
    illuminated; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the red lights or the green lights
    referred to in paragraph (a) are illuminated 24 hours a day to
    indicate the position of the ferry cable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister has approved plans for the
  operation of a ferry cable on a seasonal basis, subsection (1) does
  not apply outside the period of operation of the ferry cable.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-374, s. 6(F)
:::

## [Operation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888459 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a ferry cable
shall operate the ferry cable in accordance with the plans for the ferry
cable approved by the Minister under subsection 3(2).

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a ferry cable
shall maintain the ferry cable in the down position when it is not in
use.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No person in charge of a
vessel shall navigate the vessel across a ferry cable when the red
lights are illuminated at the on-shore terminal ends of the ferry cable.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a ferry cable
shall maintain on duty, in charge of the ferry cable, a responsible
person capable of operating the ferry cable safely.

## [Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888464 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every person who violates
any provision of these Regulations is liable on summary conviction to a
fine of \$500 or to imprisonment for a term of six months or to both.
