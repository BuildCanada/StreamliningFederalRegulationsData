[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules of origin

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following provisions of
the Canada-Chile Free Trade Agreement have the force of law in Canada:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Articles D-01 to D-03;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 1 of Article D-04;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Articles D-05 to D-13;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Articles D-16 and D-17; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} Annexes D-01, D-03.1 and D-03.2.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The [CCFTA Rules of Origin
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-340)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_81000-2-4442_hq_28601){#fn_81000-2-4442_hq_28601-ID0EBBA}
are repealed.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_81000-2-4442_hq_28601-ID0EBBA){#fn_81000-2-4442_hq_28601}SOR/97-340
  :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the 120th day after the day on which they are registered.
