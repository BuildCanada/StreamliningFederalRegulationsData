## Direct Shipment of Goods Regulations {#direct-shipment-of-goods-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-876
:::

[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

Registration 1986-08-14

Regulations Prescribing the Terms and Conditions Subject to Which Goods
Exported to Canada from any Country but that Pass in Transit Through
Another Country Shall Be Deemed to Be Shipped Directly to Canada from
the First Mentioned Country

P.C. 1986-1853 1986-08-13
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 164(3) of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), a copy of proposed Regulations, substantially in
the form annexed hereto, was published in Part I of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) on March 15, 1986 and a reasonable
opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to make
representations with respect thereto to the Minister of National
Revenue.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to
section 54 and paragraph 164(1)(i) of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Regulations prescribing the terms and
conditions subject to which goods exported to Canada from any country
but that pass in transit through another country shall be deemed to be
shipped directly to Canada from the first mentioned
country](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-876), effective on the coming into
force of section 54 and paragraph 164(1)(i) of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899049 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 54 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), where
  goods are exported to Canada from any country but pass in transit
  through another country, the goods shall be deemed to be shipped
  directly to Canada from the first mentioned country on condition that
  the journey of the goods from that country to Canada is uninterrupted
  and the importer of the goods complies with section 3.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the transfer of
  goods from one carrier to another carrier is not an interruption of
  the journey of the goods to Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-184, s. 1(F)
:::

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the importer of any goods referred to in
  subsection 2(1) shall, where an officer so requests, submit the
  original bill of lading for the goods, or a certified copy thereof, to
  the officer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the original bill of lading for the goods, or a
  certified copy thereof, is not available, the importer of the goods
  shall, where an officer so requests, provide the officer with such
  other information or documents as are available to the importer for
  the purpose of enabling the officer to determine the country of export
  of the goods.
