## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-632025 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Order.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Income Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Canada-US Tax Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canada-US Tax Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      convention, as defined in the [Canada-United States Tax Convention
      Act, 1984](/eng/acts/C-10.7). ([convention fiscale
      Canada--États-Unis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Embassy or Consulate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Embassy or Consulate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Embassy of
      Canada located in Washington, D.C., United States or any one or
      more of the offices of the Consulate General of Canada that are
      located in the United States. ([ambassade et
      consulats]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[locally engaged employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[locally engaged employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      individual who during the taxation year

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a non-resident of Canada;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is a Canadian citizen; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is paid by the government of Canada for
        services rendered by the individual in the discharge of
        functions of a governmental nature at an Embassy or
        Consulate. ([employé recruté sur place]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of meanings in Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context otherwise requires, words and
  expressions used in this Order have the same meaning as in the Act.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-632038 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission of Income Tax

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 and
4, remission is granted to each locally engaged employee with respect to
tax payable under the Act for taxation years that begin after 2016 of
the lesser of, in respect of remuneration that is taxable in Canada, and
not in the United States, because of Article XIX of the Canada-US Tax
Convention,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount added to the tax payable under Part I of
  the Act by the employee under subsection 120(1) of the Act for the
  taxation year, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount, if any, of United States state-level
  income tax paid by the employee for the taxation year.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-632043 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount not otherwise claimed

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted only
to the extent the amount remitted has not otherwise been rebated,
remitted, credited or refunded to any person under the Act, the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) or any other Act of
Parliament.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing and Documentation

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted to a
locally engaged employee in respect of a taxation year on the condition
that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the locally engaged employee applies for the
  remission in writing to the Minister on or before the day that is two
  years after their filing-due date for the taxation year; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application is accompanied by the documentation
  required to determine both the eligibility for and the amount of the
  remission.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-632050 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is made.
