## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858330 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Deposit Insurance
      Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[liquidation entitlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[liquidation entitlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount
      to which the holder of a share of a federal member institution is
      entitled to be paid, in the event that the institution is wound
      up, in priority to any amount to be paid to a holder of another
      share of the institution that ranks subordinate in right of
      payment to that share. ([part de liquidation]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a conversion or exchange privilege, issued by a
        federal member institution, that is convertible at any time into
        a share; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an option or right, issued by a federal member
        institution, to acquire a share or privilege referred to in
        paragraph (a). ([action]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[subordinated debt]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[subordinated debt]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a conversion or exchange privilege, issued by a
        federal member institution, that is convertible at any time into
        subordinated debt; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an option or right, issued by a federal member
        institution, to acquire subordinated debt or a privilege
        referred to in paragraph (a). ([dette
        subordonnée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Same class

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, shares and
  liabilities of a federal member institution are of the same class if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the event of a winding-up of the federal member
    institution, they rank equally in right of payment; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} following the making of an order under subsection
    39.13(1) of the Act, they receive treatment that is substantially
    equivalent as a result of the making of the order and any actions
    taken in furtherance of the order or in accordance with their terms,
    having regard to the manner in which their resolution value is
    estimated.

## [Persons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858346 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed persons

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 39.23(1) of the Act and subject to
  subsections (2) to (4), a prescribed person is a person who,
  immediately before the making of an order under subsection 39.13(1) of
  the Act with respect to a federal member institution, owns, directly
  or through an intermediary, any of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shares of the federal member institution;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} liabilities of the federal member institution that,
    after the order is made, are converted in whole or in part into
    common shares under subsection 39.2(2.3) of the Act or in accordance
    with their terms;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subordinated debt of the federal member institution
    that is vested in the Corporation under the order;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} liabilities of the federal member institution ---
    other than liabilities that, after the order is made, are assigned
    to or assumed by a bridge institution or third party --- if a
    winding-up order is made under the [Winding-up and Restructuring
    Act](/eng/acts/W-11) with respect to the federal member institution
    as a result of an application made under section 39.22 or 39.3717 of
    the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} liabilities of the federal member institution that,
    after the order is made, are assigned to or assumed by a corporation
    that is described in subsection 10(2) of the Act and that is then
    liquidated, during a period in which a majority of the corporation's
    voting shares are held by, on behalf of or in trust for the
    Corporation, other than liabilities that, after being assigned to or
    assumed by that corporation, are assigned to or assumed by a third
    party;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} liabilities of the federal member institution that,
    after the order is made, are assigned to or assumed by a bridge
    institution --- other than liabilities that, after being assigned to
    or assumed by the bridge institution, are assigned to or assumed by
    a third party --- when a winding-up order is made under the
    [Winding-up and Restructuring Act](/eng/acts/W-11) with respect to
    the bridge institution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion --- assignee or transferee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A prescribed person referred to in subsection (1)
  includes the person's successor in interest but does not include an
  assignee or transferee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion --- amounts paid

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person is not a prescribed person with respect to
  any liability if, after the order is made, the amounts owing under the
  liability's terms are paid in full.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion --- intermediary

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person is not a prescribed person with respect to
  any shares or liabilities if, immediately before the making of the
  order, they own the shares or liabilities solely as an intermediary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in this section.

  [[intermediary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[intermediary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a natural person,
      corporation, trust, partnership or entity that, in the ordinary
      course of its business, owns or holds shares or liabilities on
      behalf of another person and is acting in that capacity, other
      than a natural person, corporation, trust, partnership or entity
      that, in the ordinary course of its business, manages or
      administers a pension fund, a mutual fund, an investment fund or
      similar type of fund and is acting in that
      capacity. ([intermédiaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[liability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[liability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a debt of at least \$100
      owed immediately before the making of an order under subsection
      39.13(1) of the Act by the federal member institution and excludes
      any claim against the federal member institution if

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the value of the debt is too uncertain to be
        provable against the institution in a winding-up proceeding; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the debt is for a monetary loss resulting from
        the ownership, purchase or sale of an equity interest in the
        institution or from the rescission, or, in Quebec, the
        annulment, of a purchase or sale of an equity interest in the
        institution, or for contribution or indemnity in respect of the
        claim. ([élément du passif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858368 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of compensation

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of determining the amount of compensation to which a
  prescribed person is entitled, the Corporation must estimate the
  liquidation value and resolution value of the person's shares or
  liabilities that are referred to in subsection 2(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liquidation value

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The liquidation value of a share or liability is the
  estimated value that the prescribed person would have received in
  respect of the share or liability if an order under the [Winding-up
  and Restructuring Act](/eng/acts/W-11) to wind up the federal member
  institution had been made immediately before the making of an order
  under subsection 39.13(1) of the Act in respect of the institution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assumptions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The liquidation value is to be estimated

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as if no order under subsection 39.13(1) of the Act
    has been made in respect of the federal member institution; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} without taking into consideration any assistance,
    financial or other, that is or may be provided to the federal member
    institution, directly or indirectly, by the Corporation, by the Bank
    of Canada or by Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province after
    any order to wind up the institution has been made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resolution value

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The resolution value of a share or liability is the
  total of the estimated value of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the share or liability, if it is not held by the
    Corporation and it was not converted, after the making of the order
    under subsection 39.13(1) of the Act, into common shares under
    subsection 39.2(2.3) of the Act or in accordance with their terms;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} common shares that are the result of a conversion
    of the share or liability carried out, after the making of the order
    under subsection 39.13(1) of the Act, under subsection 39.2(2.3) of
    the Act or in accordance with their terms;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any dividend or interest payments made, after the
    making of the order under subsection 39.13(1) of the Act, with
    respect to the share or liability to any person other than the
    Corporation; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other cash, securities or other rights or
    interests that are received or are to be received with respect to
    the share or liability as a direct or indirect result of the making
    of an order under subsection 39.13(1) of the Act and any actions
    taken in furtherance of the order, including cash, securities or
    other rights or interests to be received from any of the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Corporation or the federal member
      institution,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the liquidator of the federal member
      institution, if the institution is wound up,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the liquidator of a corporation described in
      subsection 10(2) of the Act, if the corporation is liquidated,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the liquidator of a bridge institution, if the
      bridge institution is wound up.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of compensation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (7), the amount of compensation
  to which a prescribed person is entitled with respect to each share or
  liability is determined by the formula

  ::: {#858390 .Subsection}
  A − B − C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the estimated liquidation value;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the estimated resolution value; and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the share or liability is converted into
        common shares in accordance with its terms, an amount equal to
        an estimate of losses attributable to that conversion, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, zero.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a prescribed person is not
  entitled to compensation if the amount of compensation determined
  under subsection (5) is zero or a negative value.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Difference in time

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In determining the amount of compensation to which a
  prescribed person is entitled, the Corporation must consider the
  difference between the estimated day on which the liquidation value
  would be received and the estimated day on which the resolution value
  is, or would be, received.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Same class

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Every offer of compensation in respect of the shares
  or liabilities of a federal member institution that are of the same
  class must be calculated using the same amount of compensation, in the
  case of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} common shares, per share,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shares other than common shares, per dollar of
    liquidation entitlement, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} liabilities, per dollar of principal and accrued
    and unpaid interest.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice --- offer of compensation

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within a
  reasonable period of time after the day set out in subsection (2), the
  Corporation must give each prescribed person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a notice containing an offer of compensation in an
    amount equal to, or in a value estimated by the Corporation to be
    equal to, the compensation to which that person is entitled; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a notice stating that no offer of compensation is
    being made because that person is not entitled to compensation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Day

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A day is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a share or liability referred to in
    any of paragraphs 2(1)(a) to (c), the earlier of the date on which a
    notice referred to in subsection 39.2(3) of the Act with respect to
    the federal member institution is to take effect or the day on which
    a winding-up order is made in respect of that institution;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a liability referred to in
    paragraph 2(1)(d), the day on which the winding-up order is made in
    respect of the federal member institution;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a liability referred to in
    paragraph 2(1)(e), the day on which the order is made to liquidate
    the corporation described in subsection 10(2) of the Act; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a liability referred to in
    paragraph 2(1)(f), the day on which the winding-up order is made in
    respect of the bridge institution.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The notice referred to in
section 4 must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} state that an order has been made under subsection
  39.13(1) of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out a description of the order's effects;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} state that the prescribed person has 45 days from the
  day on which a summary of the notice is published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) to notify the Corporation of their
  decision to accept or object to the offer, or to object to the absence
  of an offer, and;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} state that the compensation to be paid will be
  determined by an assessor if the offer or the absence of an offer is
  objected to by prescribed persons who together own, in the case of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} common shares, at least 10% of the shares of the
    same class,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} shares other than common shares, at least 10% of
    the liquidation entitlement of the shares of the same class, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} liabilities, at least 10% of the principal amount
    and accrued and unpaid interest of the liabilities of the same
    class; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} state that, if the prescribed person does not notify
  the Corporation of their objection within the period set out in
  paragraph (c), the prescribed person will receive the compensation
  offered or no compensation, as the case may be, and will not be
  permitted to contest its amount or value or the fact that no
  compensation is offered.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A summary of the notice
referred to in section 4 must be published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) and on the website of the federal
member institution.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of compensation

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation must pay a
prescribed person any compensation offered under paragraph 4(1)(a) if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed person notifies the Corporation of
  their acceptance of the offer within the period set out in paragraph
  5(c);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed person does not notify the Corporation
  of their acceptance of, or objection to, the offer within the period
  set out in paragraph 5(c); or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed person notifies the Corporation of
  their objection to the offer but the condition set out in paragraph
  5(d) is not met by the end of that period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of assessor

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An assessor is to be
appointed under section 39.26 of the Act if the condition referred to in
paragraph 5(d) is met within the period referred to in paragraph 5(c).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation's notice to dissenters

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Within 45 days after the
day on which an assessor is appointed, the Corporation must provide, to
each prescribed person whose compensation is to be determined by the
assessor, a notice of the appointment of the assessor indicating that
the prescribed person is bound by the assessor's determination of the
amount of compensation to be paid which may be lower or higher than that
contained in the offer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessor's determination

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
reviewing a decision made by the Corporation and determining the amount
of compensation to be paid to a prescribed person under section 39.26 of
the Act, the assessor must consider

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the Corporation based its determination on an
  unreasonable estimate or on an erroneous finding of fact that it made
  in a perverse or capricious manner or without regard for the material
  before it; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the manner of determining compensation set out in
  section 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessor's notice

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  assessor must provide, to every prescribed person whose compensation
  is determined by the assessor, a notice

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} setting out the assessor's determination of the
    amount of compensation that the person is entitled to;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} stating that the person is bound by the assessor's
    determination as to the amount of compensation to be paid; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} stating that the Corporation must pay the
    compensation within 90 days after the date of the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy to Corporation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The assessor must provide the Corporation with a copy
  of each notice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of compensation

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation must pay
a prescribed person the compensation that they are entitled to receive
within

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a notice referred to in subsection 11(1) is
  provided to the person, 90 days after the date of that notice; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, 135 days after the day on which a
  summary of the notice referred to in section 4 is published in the
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).
