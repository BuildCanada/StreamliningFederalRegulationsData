## Order Transferring from the Minister for International Trade to the President of the King's Privy Council for Canada the Control and Supervision of the Canadian Intergovernmental Conference Secretariat {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-for-international-trade-to-the-president-of-the-kings-privy-council-for-canada-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-canadian-intergovernmental-conference-secretariat .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-79
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2025-06-04

Order Transferring from the Minister for International Trade to the
President of the King's Privy Council for Canada the Control and
Supervision of the Canadian Intergovernmental Conference Secretariat

P.C. 2025-488 2025-05-13
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBCAA},
transfers from the Minister for International Trade to the President of
the King's Privy Council for Canada the control and supervision of that
portion of the federal public administration known as the Canadian
Intergovernmental Conference Secretariat.
