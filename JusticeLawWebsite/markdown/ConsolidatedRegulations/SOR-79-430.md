## Broadcasting Industry Commission Salesmen Hours of Work Regulations {#broadcasting-industry-commission-salesmen-hours-of-work-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/79-430
:::

[CANADA LABOUR CODE](/eng/acts/L-2)

Registration 1979-05-22

Regulations Respecting Hours of Work for the Class of Employees Known as
Commission Salesmen who are Employed in Connection with the Radio and
Television Broadcasting Industry in Canada

P.C. 1979-1515 1979-05-17
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Labour, pursuant to section 31 and paragraph 32.1(1)(b)
of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2), is pleased hereby to make
the annexed [Regulations respecting hours of work for the class of
employees known as commission salesmen who are employed in connection
with the Radio and Television Broadcasting Industry in
Canada](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-430).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865867 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Broadcasting Industry Commission Salesmen Hours of Work
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-430).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865869 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Part III of the [Canada Labour
Code](/eng/acts/L-2).

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865871 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The class of employees
known as commission salesmen who are employed in connection with the
radio and television broadcasting industry in Canada and paid on the
basis of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a commission, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a salary plus commission

are exempt from the application of sections 169, 171, 173 and 174 of the
Act.
