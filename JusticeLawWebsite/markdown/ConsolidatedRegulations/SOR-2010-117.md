apply in these Regulations.

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[continuous journey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[continuous journey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as

    continu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Place of Supply]{.HTitleText1} {#h-754214 .Part}

### [DIVISION 1]{.HLabel2}[Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-754215 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Part.

[[Canadian element]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian element]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of a service means the
    portion of the service that is performed in Canada. ([élément
    canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canadian rights]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian rights]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of intangible
    personal property, means that part of the property that can be used
    in Canada. ([droits canadiens]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[computer-related service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[computer-related service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a technical support service that is provided by
      means of telecommunications and relates to the operation or use of
      computer hardware or software; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a service involving the electronic storage of
      information and computer-to-computer transfer of
      information. ([service informatique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[final recipient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[final recipient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
    computer-related service or access to the Internet, means a person
    that is the recipient of a supply of the service or access and that
    acquires it otherwise than for the purpose of supplying it to
    another person. ([dernier acquéreur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[RDSP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[RDSP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registered disability savings
    plan as defined in subsection 248(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([REEI]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[RESP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[RESP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registered education savings
    plan as defined in subsection 248(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([REEE]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[RRIF]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[RRIF]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registered retirement income
    fund as defined in subsection 248(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([FERR]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[RRSP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[RRSP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registered retirement savings
    plan as defined in subsection 248(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([REER]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[specified location]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[specified location]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of a supplier means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a permanent establishment of the supplier; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vending machine. ([emplacement
      déterminé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[TFSA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[TFSA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    248(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([CÉLI]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed delivery

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Part, property is deemed to be delivered in a particular province by a
supplier and is deemed not to be delivered in any other province by the
supplier if the supplier

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} ships the property to a destination in the particular
  province that is specified in the contract for carriage of the
  property or transfers possession of the property to a common carrier
  or consignee that the supplier has retained on behalf of the recipient
  to ship the property to such a destination; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sends the property by mail or courier to an address
  in the particular province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Part applies for the purposes of section 3 of Part IX of Schedule IX
  to the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transition --- Ontario and British
  Columbia

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of applying the provisions of Part 3
  in relation to the application of this Part between February 25, 2010
  and July 1, 2010, the following rules apply:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Ontario and British Columbia are deemed to be
    participating provinces;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the tax rate for Ontario is deemed to be 8%; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the tax rate for British Columbia is deemed to be
    7%.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transition --- Prince Edward Island

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of applying the provisions of Part
  3.1 in relation to the application of this Part between November 8,
  2012 and April 1, 2013, the following rules apply:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Prince Edward Island is deemed to be a
    participating province; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the tax rate for Prince Edward Island is deemed to
    be 9%.

### [DIVISION 2]{.HLabel2}[Intangible Personal Property]{.HTitleText2} {#h-754250 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Division does not
apply to intangible personal property to which Part VII or VIII of
Schedule IX to the Act applies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian rights primarily in
participating provinces

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A supply
  of intangible personal property (other than intangible personal
  property that relates to real property or to tangible personal
  property) in respect of which the Canadian rights can only be used
  primarily in participating provinces is made in a participating
  province if an equal or greater proportion of those Canadian rights
  cannot be used in another participating province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian rights primarily in
  participating provinces

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (1), a supply of intangible
  personal property (other than intangible personal property that
  relates to real property or to tangible personal property) in respect
  of which the Canadian rights can only be used primarily in
  participating provinces is made in a particular participating province
  if,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a supply for which the value of the
    consideration is \$300 or less that is made through a specified
    location of the supplier in the particular participating province
    and in the presence of an individual who is, or who acts on behalf
    of, the recipient, the intangible personal property can be used in
    the particular participating province;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a supply that is not determined
    under paragraph (a) to be made in a participating province, the
    following conditions are satisfied:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the ordinary course of business of the
      supplier, the supplier obtains an address (in this paragraph
      referred to as the "particular address") that is

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the supplier obtains only one address that
        is a home or a business address in Canada of the recipient, the
        home or business address in Canada obtained by the supplier,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the supplier obtains more than one address
        described in clause (A), the address described in that clause
        that is most closely connected with the supply, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the address in Canada of the
        recipient that is most closely connected with the supply,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the particular address is in the particular
      participating province, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the intangible personal property can be used in
      the particular participating province; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a supply that is not determined
    under paragraph (a) or (b) to be made in a participating province,
    the tax rate for the particular participating province is the
    highest among the tax rates for the participating provinces in which
    the intangible personal property can be used.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian rights primarily in
non-participating provinces

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A supply of intangible
personal property (other than intangible personal property that relates
to real property or to tangible personal property) in respect of which
the Canadian rights can only be used primarily in non-participating
provinces is made in a non-participating province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian rights --- no primary location
of use

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A supply of intangible
personal property (other than intangible personal property that relates
to real property or to tangible personal property) in respect of which
the Canadian rights can be used otherwise than only primarily in
participating provinces and otherwise than only primarily outside
participating provinces is made in a particular province if,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a supply for which the value of the
  consideration is \$300 or less that is made through a specified
  location of the supplier in the particular province and in the
  presence of an individual who is, or who acts on behalf of, the
  recipient, the intangible personal property can be used in the
  particular province;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a supply that is not determined under
  paragraph (a) to be made in a province, the following conditions are
  satisfied:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the ordinary course of business of the supplier,
    the supplier obtains an address (in this paragraph referred to as
    the "particular address") that is

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the supplier obtains only one address that is
      a home or a business address in Canada of the recipient, the home
      or business address in Canada obtained by the supplier,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the supplier obtains more than one address
      described in clause (A), the address described in that clause that
      is most closely connected with the supply, or

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the address in Canada of the
      recipient that is most closely connected with the supply,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the particular address is in the particular
    province, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the intangible personal property can be used in
    the particular province; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a supply that is not determined under
  paragraph (a) or (b) to be made in a province, the tax rate for the
  particular province is the highest among the tax rates for the
  provinces in which the intangible personal property can be used.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intangible personal property that relates
to real property

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A supply of intangible
personal property that relates to real property is made

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a participating province if the real property that
  is situated in Canada is situated primarily in participating provinces
  and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an equal or greater proportion of the real property
    is not situated in another participating province, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if subparagraph (i) does not apply, the tax rate
    for the participating province is the highest among the
    participating provinces for which no greater proportion of the real
    property is situated in another participating province; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a non-participating province if the real property
  that is situated in Canada is not situated primarily in participating
  provinces.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intangible personal property that relates
to tangible personal property

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A supply of intangible
personal property that relates to tangible personal property is made

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a participating province if the tangible personal
  property that is ordinarily located in Canada is ordinarily located
  primarily in participating provinces and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an equal or greater proportion of the tangible
    personal property is not ordinarily located in another participating
    province, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if subparagraph (i) does not apply, the tax rate
    for the participating province is the highest among the
    participating provinces for which no greater proportion of the
    tangible personal property is ordinarily located in another
    participating province; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a non-participating province if the tangible
  personal property that is ordinarily located in Canada is not
  ordinarily located primarily in participating provinces.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Same highest rate

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** If a supply of intangible
personal property cannot be determined under paragraph 6(2)(c) or 8(c)
or subparagraph 9(a)(ii) or 10(a)(ii) to be made in a single
participating province because the tax rates for two or more
participating provinces (each of which is referred to in this section as
a "specified province") are the same, the supply is made in the
specified province where the business address of the supplier that is
most closely connected with the supply is located or, if the business
address of the supplier that is most closely connected with the supply
is not located in one of the specified provinces, in the specified
province that is closest in proximity, determined in any reasonable
manner, to the business address of the supplier that is most closely
connected with the supply.

### [DIVISION 3]{.HLabel2}[Services]{.HTitleText2} {#h-754294 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** This Division does not
apply to a service to which any of sections 4 to 5 of Part VI or Part
VII or VIII of Schedule IX to the Act applies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General rule for services --- address
obtained

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 14 to 17, a
  supply of a service is made in a province if, in the ordinary course
  of business of the supplier, the supplier obtains an address in the
  province that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the supplier obtains only one address that is a
    home or a business address in Canada of the recipient, the home or
    business address in Canada obtained by the supplier;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the supplier obtains more than one address
    described in paragraph (a), the address described in that paragraph
    that is most closely connected with the supply; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the address in Canada of the
    recipient that is most closely connected with the supply.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General rule for services --- no
  address obtained

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (1) and sections 14 to 17, a
  supply of a service is made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a participating province if the Canadian element
    of the service is performed primarily in participating provinces and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an equal or greater proportion of the Canadian
      element of the service is not performed in another participating
      province, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if subparagraph (i) does not apply, the tax rate
      for the participating province is the highest among the
      participating provinces for which no greater proportion of the
      service is performed in another participating province; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a non-participating province if the Canadian
    element of the service is not performed primarily in participating
    provinces.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Services in relation to real property

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A supply of a service in
relation to real property is made

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a participating province if the real property that
  is situated in Canada is situated primarily in participating provinces
  and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an equal or greater proportion of the real property
    is not situated in another participating province, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if subparagraph (i) does not apply, the tax rate

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a non-participating province if the real property
  that is situated in Canada is not situated primarily in participating
  provinces.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Services in relation to tangible personal
property

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** If a person makes a
supply of a service in relation to tangible personal property that is
situated in one or more provinces at the particular time when the
Canadian element of the service begins to be performed and, at all times
when the Canadian element of the service is performed, the tangible
personal property remains in the province in which it was situated at
the particular time, the supply is made

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a participating province if the tangible personal
  property is situated primarily in participating provinces at the
  particular time and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an equal or greater proportion of the tangible
    personal property is not situated in another participating province
    at the particular time, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if subparagraph (i) does not apply, the tax rate
    for the participating province is the highest among the
    participating provinces for which no greater proportion of the
    tangible personal property is situated in another participating
    province at the particular time; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a non-participating province if the tangible
  personal property is not situated primarily in participating provinces
  at the particular time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Services in relation to tangible personal
property

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** If a person makes a
supply of a service in relation to tangible personal property that is
situated in one or more provinces at the particular time when the
Canadian element of the service begins to be performed and, at any time
during the period when the Canadian element of the service is performed,
the tangible personal property does not remain in the province in which
it was situated at the particular time, the supply is made

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a participating province if the tangible personal
  property is situated primarily in participating provinces at any time
  when the service is performed, the Canadian element of the service is
  performed primarily in participating provinces and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an equal or greater proportion of the service is
    not performed in another participating province, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if subparagraph (i) does not apply, the tax rate
    for the participating province is the highest among the
    participating provinces for which no greater proportion of the
    service is performed in another participating province; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a non-participating province if the tangible
  personal property is not situated primarily in participating provinces
  at all times when the service is performed or the Canadian element of
  the service is not performed primarily in participating provinces.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal services

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A supply of a service
(other than an advisory, consulting or professional service) all or
substantially all of which is performed in the presence of the
individual to whom it is rendered is made

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a participating province if the Canadian element
  of the service is performed primarily in participating provinces and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an equal or greater proportion of the service is
    not performed in another participating province, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if subparagraph (i) does not apply, the tax rate
    for the participating province is the highest among the
    participating provinces for which no greater proportion of the
    service is performed in another participating province; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a non-participating province if the Canadian
  element of the service is not performed primarily in participating
  provinces.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Same highest rate

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** If a supply of a service
cannot be determined under any of subparagraphs 13(2)(a)(ii), 14(a)(ii),
15(a)(ii), 16(a)(ii) and 17(a)(ii) to be made in a single participating
province because the tax rates for two or more participating provinces
(each of which is referred to in this section as a "specified province")
are the same, the supply is made in the specified province where the
business address of the supplier that is most closely connected with the
supply is located or, if the business address of the supplier that is
most closely connected with the supply is not located in one of the
specified provinces, in the specified province that is closest in
proximity, determined in any reasonable manner, to the business address
of the supplier that is most closely connected with the supply.

### [DIVISION 4]{.HLabel2}[Transportation Services]{.HTitleText2} {#h-754337 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** This Division applies
despite Divisions 2 and 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Division.

[[leg]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[leg]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of a journey on a conveyance means a
    part of the journey that begins where passengers embark or disembark
    the conveyance or where it is stopped to allow for its servicing or
    refuelling and ends where it is next stopped for any of those
    purposes. ([étape]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[origin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[origin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of a continuous journey has the same
    meaning as in subsection 1(1) of Part VII of Schedule VI to the
    Act. ([point d'origine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[stopover]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[stopover]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a continuous
    journey, has the same meaning as in subsection 1(1) of Part VII of
    Schedule VI to the Act except that it does not include, in the case
    of a continuous journey of an individual or group of individuals
    that does not include transportation by air and the origin and
    termination of which are in Canada, any place outside Canada where,
    at the time the journey begins, the individual or group is not
    scheduled to be outside Canada for an uninterrupted period of at
    least 24 hours during the course of the
    journey. ([escale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[termination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[termination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of a continuous journey has the
    same meaning as in subsection 1(1) of Part VII of Schedule VI to the
    Act. ([destination finale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Passenger transportation services

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A supply of a passenger
transportation service is made

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a participating province if the passenger
  transportation service

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is part of a continuous journey in respect of which
    there is a ticket or voucher, issued in respect of the particular
    passenger transportation service included in the continuous journey
    that is provided first, specifying the origin of the continuous
    journey and

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the origin is a place in the participating
      province, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the termination and all stopovers in respect of
      the continuous journey are in Canada,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is part of a continuous journey in respect of
    which there is no ticket or voucher, issued in respect of the
    particular passenger transportation service included in the
    continuous journey that is provided first, specifying the origin of
    the continuous journey and

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the passenger transportation service included in
      the continuous journey that is provided first cannot begin
      otherwise than in the participating province, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the termination and all stopovers in respect of
      the continuous journey are in Canada, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is not part of a continuous journey and

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the passenger transportation service begins in
      the participating province, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the passenger transportation service ends in
      Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a non-participating province if the passenger
  transportation service

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is part of a continuous journey in respect of which
    there is a ticket or voucher, issued in respect of the particular
    passenger transportation service included in the continuous journey
    that is provided first, specifying the origin of the continuous
    journey and

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the origin is a place outside the participating
      provinces, or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the termination or a stopover in respect of the
      continuous journey is outside Canada,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is part of a continuous journey in respect of
