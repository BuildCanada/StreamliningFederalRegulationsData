## Memorial Cross Order (World War II) {#memorial-cross-order-world-war-ii .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1623
:::

ORDER RESPECTING THE AWARD OF THE MEMORIAL CROSS TO MOTHERS AND WIDOWS
OF SAILORS, SOLDIERS, AIRMEN, MERCHANT SEAMEN AND FIRE FIGHTERS---WORLD
WAR II
::::

::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-535777 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the *Memorial Cross Order (World War II)*.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-535779 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Cross]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Cross]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Memorial Cross, which shall
    be a cross patonce in silver, suspended by a purple ribbon; at the
    end of the upright a crown; at the foot, and at the end of each arm,
    a maple leaf; in the centre, within a wreath of laurel, the Royal
    Cypher of the reigning monarch; engraved with the number, rank and
    name of the sailor, soldier, airman, merchant seaman or fire fighter
    commemorated; ([Croix]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fire fighter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fire fighter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who served
    during World War II as a member of the Corps of (Civilian) Canadian
    Fire Fighters for Service in the United Kingdom, who was on service
    while in receipt of pay and allowances as a fire fighter outside the
    continents of North and South America, the islands adjacent thereto
    and the territorial waters thereof, including service in Greenland,
    Iceland or the Aleutian Islands but not including service in
    Newfoundland, Bermuda or the West Indies;
    ([pompier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[merchant seaman]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[merchant seaman]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who served
    during World War II as Master, Officer or member of the crew of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a ship registered in Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a United Kingdom ship registered in Canada or
      elsewhere, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a registered ship of one of the countries allied
      with His Majesty during World War II

    if, in the case of a person who served on other than a ship
    registered in Canada, that person was born or domiciled in Canada or
    Newfoundland, or was an ordinary resident in Canada or Newfoundland
    at anytime during the period between September 9, 1929 and the date
    on which he commenced to so serve; ([marin de la marine
    marchande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Veterans
    Affairs; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mother]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mother]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the natural mother or, where
    the sailor, soldier, airman, merchant seaman or fire fighter was not
    raised from childhood by his natural mother, the woman who, in the
    opinion of the Minister, raised him from childhood;
    ([mère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sailor, soldier or airman]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sailor, soldier or airman]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person
    who served during World War II in the naval, army or air forces of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} His Majesty raised in Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} His Majesty, other than those raised in Canada,
      or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one of the countries allied with His Majesty
      during World War II

    if, in the case of a person who served in other than the naval, army
    or air forces of His Majesty raised in Canada, that person was born
    or domiciled in Canada or Newfoundland or was an ordinary resident
    in Canada or Newfoundland at any time during the period between
    September 9, 1929 and the date on which he commenced to so serve;
    ([marin, soldat, aviateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[widow]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[widow]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the legal widow or widower of
    a sailor, soldier, airman, merchant seaman or fire fighter and any
    other person who is pensionable as a widow under the [Pension
    Act](/eng/acts/P-6) or the Civilian War Pensions and Allowances Act
    by reason of death of the sailor, soldier, airman, merchant seaman
    or fire fighter or who, if the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) or the
    Civilian War Pensions and Allowances Act had applied to her, could
    have been deemed to be the widow of the sailor, soldier, airman,
    merchant seaman or fire fighter, but does not include any person who
    was at the time of the death of the sailor, soldier, airman,
    merchant seaman or fire fighter, divorced, judicially separated or
    separated pursuant to a written or other agreement from him;
    ([veuve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[World War II]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[World War II]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 3(1) of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6). ([Seconde
    Guerre mondiale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    ::: HistoricalNote
    - SI/91-69, s. 1
    :::

## [Award of the Cross]{.HTitleText1} {#h-535797 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5, a
Cross may be issued as a memento of personal loss and sacrifice on the
part of the mother and widow of a sailor, soldier, airman, merchant
seaman or fire fighter of Canada or Newfoundland who laid down his life
for his country during World War II or who dies or died from causes
attributable to service during World War II.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A Cross referred to in
section 3 shall be issued in respect of a sailor, soldier, airman,
merchant seaman or firefighter described in that section to only the
following persons, namely,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the mother, if alive,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the widow, if alive,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where there is no person referred to in paragraph (a)
  or (b), to the eldest living child,

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where there is no person referred to in
  paragraphs (a) to (c), to the father, if alive, and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} where there is no person referred to in
  paragraphs (a) to (d), to the brother or sister, if alive, whichever
  is the eldest

of the sailor, soldier, airman, merchant seaman or firefighter.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/81-31, s. 1
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A Cross or Crosses may be
issued in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} each sailor, soldier or airman who

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} was killed in action during World War II,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} died while on active service during World War II,
    or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} died or dies from causes attributable to service
    during World War II in the naval, military or air forces, whether
    his death occurred or occurs while serving on active service or
    subsequent thereto,

  except that, where death occurred or occurs subsequent to his
  discharge from the naval, military or air forces, a Cross may be
  issued to the widow or her next of kin only if she was married to the
  sailor, soldier or airman at the time of his discharge;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} each merchant seaman who lost his life through enemy
  action during World War II or who died or dies from causes
  attributable to service as a merchant seaman during World War II,
  except that the Cross may be issued to the widow or her next of kin
  only if she was married to the merchant seaman at the time he
  sustained the injury or disability which resulted in his death; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} each fire fighter whose death occurred during his
  service while in receipt of pay and allowances as a fire fighter
  during World War II or who died or dies from causes attributable to
  such service, except that the Cross may be issued to the widow or her
  next of kin only if she was married to the fire fighter at the time he
  sustained the injury or disability which resulted in his death.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A Cross shall not be issued
to a person referred to in section 4 unless that person furnishes proof
of the death and service of the sailor, soldier, airman, merchant seaman
or firefighter in respect of whom the cross may be issued and the
eligibility of the person to be issued the Cross.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/81-31, s. 2
:::
