## Home Buyer Grant Regulations {#home-buyer-grant-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/75-24
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 4, 1974

Registration 1974-12-24

Regulations Prescribing the Terms and Conditions in Respect of Home
Buyer Grants

P.C. 1974-2881 1974-12-20
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of State for Urban Affairs, pursuant to Vote 12b in the
schedule to Appropriation Act, No. 4, 1974, is pleased hereby to make
the annexed [Regulations prescribing the terms and conditions in respect
of home buyer grants](/eng/regulations/SOR-75-24).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861151 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Home Buyer Grant Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-75-24).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861153 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Central Mortgage and
    Housing Corporation; ([Société]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[grant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[grant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an amount calculated in
    accordance with section 6 of these Regulations;
    ([subvention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[housing unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[housing unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a unit providing therein living, sleeping,
      eating, food preparation and sanitary facilities for one family,
      with or without other essential facilities shared with other
      family housing units and includes a house intended for human
      habitation comprising not more than two family housing units; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a bounded space in a building designated or
      described as a separate unit on a registered condominium or strata
      lot plan or description or similar plan or description registered
      pursuant to the laws of a province, and intended for human
      habitation, and includes any interest in land appertaining to
      ownership of the unit. ([logement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application of Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861160 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
any individual who at no time prior to November 1, 1974 owned, or whose
spouse at no time prior to that date owned, whether jointly with another
person or otherwise, a housing unit in Canada that was used as a
principal residence by that individual or his spouse.

## [Grant]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861162 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5, a
grant may be made to an individual if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} he has purchased or built a housing unit; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} he is a member of a cooperative housing corporation
  and has purchased a share of the capital stock of a cooperative
  housing corporation for the purpose of occupying a housing unit.

## [Conditions of Grant]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861166 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No grant
  shall be made to an individual applying therefor unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the housing unit referred to in section 4 has not
    been previously occupied and the individual has occupied the housing
    unit for the first time subsequent to the 31st day of October, 1974;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the individual

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has a freehold interest or a right to acquire a
      freehold interest, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has a leasehold interest or a licence having a
      term of not less than five years

    in respect of the land on which the housing unit is located;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the individual produces a building permit or other
    evidence satisfactory to the Corporation showing that the housing
    unit complies with the building codes that are in force in respect
    of the area in which the housing unit is located; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the housing unit is a mobile home, the home
    conforms with the following standards prescribed by the Canadian
    Standards Association:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} CSA Standard Z240.5-1971,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} CSA Standard Z240.1-1972,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} CSA Standard Z240.0-1973,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} CSA Standard Z240.3.1-1973, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} CSA Standard Z240.5.1-1973.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Corporation makes a loan under section 15
  of the [National Housing Act](/eng/acts/N-11) to a non-profit
  corporation that is a cooperative association described in paragraph
  15.1(1)(b) of that Act toward the capital cost of a housing project of
  which the housing unit is a part, the amount of the grant shall be
  applied toward the reduction of the loan.

## [Amount of Grant]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861182 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The amount of a grant made
to an individual under these Regulations in respect of a housing unit
shall be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} five hundred dollars, where the cost or purchase
  price of the housing unit does not exceed the minimum amount of the
  cost or purchase price of a housing unit that the Corporation requires
  for the purpose of insuring a maximum loan under the [National Housing
  Act](/eng/acts/N-11); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} five hundred dollars minus one dollar for each dollar
  by which the cost or purchase price of the housing unit exceeds the
  minimum amount of the cost or purchase price of a housing unit that
  the Corporation requires for the purpose of insuring a maximum loan
  under the [National Housing Act](/eng/acts/N-11).
