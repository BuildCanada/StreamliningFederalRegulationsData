the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 277(1)[[Footnote

annexed Regulations prescribing taxable school cafeteria food and
beverages.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934094 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15).
(*Loi*)

## [Prescribed Food and Beverages]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934096 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
12 of Part III of Schedule V to the Act, the following are prescribed
food and beverages:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} carbonated beverages;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} beverages included in paragraph 1(d) of Part III of
  Schedule VI to the Act, when sold in cans, cartons or bottles; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} foods included in any of paragraphs 1(e) to (l) of
  Part III of Schedule VI to the Act, when prepackaged for sale to
  consumers.
