## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838988 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Environmental Violations
Administrative Monetary Penalties Act](/eng/acts/E-12.5), unless
otherwise indicated.

## [Designated Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838991 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions of Environmental Acts and
regulations

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  contravention of a provision of an Environmental Act or of any of its
  regulations that is set out in column 1 of Schedule 1 is designated as
  a violation that may be proceeded with in accordance with the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders or directions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The contravention of any order or direction made
  under a provision of an Environmental Act or of any of its regulations
  that is set out in column 1 of Schedule 2 is designated as a violation
  that may be proceeded with in accordance with the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of permit, licence or
  authorization

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The failure to comply with a condition of a permit,
  licence or other authorization issued under an Environmental Act or
  any of its regulations that is referred to in a provision set out in
  column 1 of Schedule 3 is designated as a violation that may be
  proceeded with in accordance with the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other provisions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The contravention of subsection 174(1) or paragraph
  178(1)(a) of the [Greenhouse Gas Pollution Pricing
  Act](/eng/acts/G-11.55) is designated as a violation that may be
  proceeded with in accordance with the Act.

## [Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-839001 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Formula --- Type A, B or C

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of the penalty for each Type A, B or C violation is to be
  determined by the formula

  ::: {#1186288 .Subsection}
  W + X + Y + Z

  where

  [W]{.dfn}
  :   is the baseline penalty amount determined under section 5;

  [X]{.dfn}
  :   is the history of non-compliance amount, if any, as determined
      under section 6;

  [Y]{.dfn}
  :   is the environmental harm amount, if any, as determined under
      section 7; and

  [Z]{.dfn}
  :   is the economic gain amount, if any, as determined under section
      8.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Formula -- types D or E

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the penalty for each Type D or E
  violation is to be determined by the formula

  ::: {#1186296 .Subsection}
  W + X + Y

  where

  [W]{.dfn}
  :   is the baseline penalty amount determined under section 5;

  [X]{.dfn}
  :   is the history of non-compliance amount, if any, as determined
      under section 6; and

  [Y]{.dfn}
  :   is the economic gain amount, if any, as determined under section
      8.1.
  :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Baseline penalty amount

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The baseline penalty amount
for a violation is the amount set out in column 3 of Schedule 4 or of
Schedule 5 that corresponds to the category of the violator and the type
of violation committed as set out in columns 1 and 2, respectively, of
the applicable schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}History of non-compliance amount

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  violator has a history of non-compliance, the history of
  non-compliance amount is the amount set out in column 4 of Schedule 4
  or of Schedule 5 that corresponds to the category of the violator and
  the type of violation committed as set out in columns 1 and 2,
  respectively, of the applicable Schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}History of non-compliance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A violator has a history of non-compliance if, in the
  five years preceding

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the commission of a violation relating to any
    Division of Part 7 of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
    1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) or any regulation made under that Division,
    they were subject to an enforcement action in relation to that
    Division or any of those regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the commission of a violation relating to Part 9 of
    the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)
    or any regulation made under that Part, they were subject to an
    enforcement action in relation to that Part or any of those
    regulations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the commission of a violation relating to the
    [Greenhouse Gas Pollution Pricing Act](/eng/acts/G-11.55) or a
    regulation made under that Act, they were subject to an enforcement
    action in relation to that Act or any of those regulations; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the commission of a violation relating to any
    Environmental Act, other than the [Canadian Environmental Protection
    Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) or the [Greenhouse Gas Pollution
    Pricing Act](/eng/acts/G-11.55), or a regulation made under one of
    those Acts, they were subject to an enforcement action in relation
    to that Act or any of that Act's regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of [[enforcement
  action]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (2), [[enforcement
  action]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the imposition of a ticket,
  penalty, conviction or injunction or the use of environmental
  protection alternative measures.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Environmental harm amount

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If the violation has
resulted in harm to the environment, the environmental harm amount is
the amount set out in column 5 of Schedule 4 that corresponds to the
category of the violator and the type of violation committed as set out
in columns 1 and 2, respectively, of that Schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Economic gain amount

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), if the violation has resulted in economic gain to
  the violator, including an avoided financial cost, the economic gain
  amount is the amount set out in column 6 of Schedule 4 that
  corresponds to the category of the violator and the type of violation
  committed as set out in columns 1 and 2, respectively, of that
  Schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Only gain avoidance of cost of
  authorization

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the only economic gain is the avoidance of the
  cost of obtaining a permit, licence or other authorization, the
  economic gain amount is the amount set out in column 7 of Schedule 4
  that corresponds to the category of the violator and the type of
  violation committed as set out in columns 1 and 2, respectively, of
  that Schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Economic advantage amount

**[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** If the violation has
resulted in economic gain to the violator, including an avoided
financial cost, the applicable economic gain amount is the amount set
out in column 5 of Schedule 5 that corresponds to the category of the
violator and the type of violation committed as set out in columns 1 and
2, respectively, of that Schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specific case

- **[[8.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  amount of the penalty for a violation referred to in subsection 2(4)
  is \$0.25.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continued violation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a violation referred to in subsection (1) is
  continued on more than one day, the penalty in respect of each of the
  separate violations that, because of section 12 of the Act, result
  from that continuation shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$0.25 for one that is committed during the
    nine-day period beginning on the day after the day of the
    contravention;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$0.50 for one that is committed during the 10-day
    period beginning on the tenth day following the day of the
    contravention; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$1.00 for one that is committed on or after the
    twenty-first day following the contravention.

## [Service]{.HTitleText1} {#h-839029 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of service

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A notice
  of violation referred to in section 10 of the Act is to be served

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by leaving a copy of it with the individual at
      any place or with someone who appears to be an adult member of the
      same household at the individual's last known address or usual
      place of residence or, in the case of an individual under the age
      of 18, with a parent or other person having custody of them or
      exercising parental authority over them, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of it by registered mail,
      courier, fax or other electronic means to the recipient's last
      known address or usual place of residence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person other than an individual,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by leaving a copy of it with their authorized
      representative or with an officer or other individual who appears
      to direct or control the person's head office or place of business
      or that of their authorized representative,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of it by registered mail,
      courier or fax to the person's authorized representative or to the
      person's head office or place of business or that of their
      authorized representative, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of it by electronic means to
      an individual referred to in subparagraph (i); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a ship or vessel,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by delivering a copy of it personally to the
      master or another individual who is, or appears to be, in charge
      of the ship or vessel,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by affixing a copy of it to the ship or vessel
      in a manner than ensures that it is prominently in view,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} by leaving a copy of it with the authorized
      representative of the owner or operator of the ship or vessel or
      with an officer or other individual who appears to direct or
      control the owner, operator or authorized representative's head
      office or place of business,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of it by registered mail,
      courier or fax to the owner or operator of the ship or vessel, or
      to the authorized representative, head office or place of business
      of that owner or operator, or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of it by electronic means to an
      individual referred to in subparagraph (i), (iii) or (iv).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Service may be proven by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgement of service signed by or on
    behalf of the person or ship or vessel served, specifying the date
    and location of service;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a certificate of service, in a form approved by the
    Minister and signed by the person who effected the service, stating
    that service was made on the person or ship or vessel named in the
    certificate and indicating the means by which and day on which
    service was effected; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a record of electronic transmission setting out the
    date and time of transmission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date service effective

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the absence of an acknowledgement of service or a
  certificate of service, service is considered effective,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service by registered mail or
    courier, on the 10th day after the day on which the notice is sent,
    as indicated on the receipt issued by the postal or courier service;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service by fax or other electronic
    means, on the day on which it is sent, as indicated on the record of
    transmission.

## [Request for Review]{.HTitleText1} {#h-839055 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}On behalf of ship or vessel

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
section 15 of the Act, a review may be requested on behalf of a ship or
vessel by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} its owner;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} its operator or master; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an authorized representative of its owner or
  operator.
