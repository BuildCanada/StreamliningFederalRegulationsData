## Payments to the Provinces Regulations {#payments-to-the-provinces-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2007-252
:::

[SOFTWOOD LUMBER PRODUCTS EXPORT CHARGE ACT, 2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55)

Registration 2007-11-15

Payments to the Provinces Regulations
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister for International Trade and the Minister of National
Revenue, pursuant to subsection 100(2) of the [Softwood Lumber Products
Export Charge Act, 2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_609530-E_hq_2746){#fn_609530-E_hq_2746-ID0EDBAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Payments to the Provinces
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-252).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Softwood Lumber Products
    Export Charge Act, 2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[non-excluded provinces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-excluded provinces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Ontario,
    Quebec, Manitoba, British Columbia, Saskatchewan and
    Alberta. ([provinces non exclues]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of the amount of the payments to be made to the provinces by the
Minister of National Revenue under section 99 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First calculation

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  first calculation of an amount of a payment is for the period
  beginning on October 12, 2006 and ending on September 30, 2007.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Later calculations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Later calculations are to be made in respect of
  calendar quarters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reconciliation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Except for the final reconciliation, a reconciliation
  of the amounts used in calculations is to be made annually.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revenue

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The amount of revenue to be
attributed to a province is the revenue derived from the charge imposed
under section 10 or 15 of the Act on softwood lumber products
originating from that province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of the costs referred to in paragraphs 99(1)(a) and (b) of the
  Act to be attributed to a non-excluded province for a period is
  determined by the formula

  ::: {#736926 .Subsection}
  A × (B/C)

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the costs that the Minister becomes aware of during the period;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the volume in board feet of softwood lumber products exported
      from the non-excluded province to the United States during the
      period under export permits;

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the volume in board feet of softwood lumber products exported
      from all of the non-excluded provinces to the United States during
      the period under export permits.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of the costs referred to in paragraph
  99(1)(b) of the Act, the formula applies unless the Minister
  determines otherwise under section 99 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
