## Order Directing that the Document Set Out in the Schedule be Discontinued {#order-directing-that-the-document-set-out-in-the-schedule-be-discontinued .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2022-18
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2022-03-16

Order Directing that the Document Set Out in the Schedule be
Discontinued

P.C. 2022-201 2022-03-03
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas it appears to the Governor in Council that the document set out
in the schedule, required by an Act of Parliament to be laid before both
Houses of Parliament, contains the same information as or less
information than is contained in the Public Accounts or in any estimates
of expenditures submitted to Parliament;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Natural Resources, pursuant to section
157[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1155-E_hq_16356){#fn_81000-3-1155-E_hq_16356-ID0EECAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-1155-E_hq_16358){#fn_81000-3-1155-E_hq_16358-ID0EBCAA},
directs that the document set out in the schedule be discontinued.
