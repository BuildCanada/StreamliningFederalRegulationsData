## Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure, Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs to be the Minister for the purposes of the Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-infrastructure-communities-and-intergovernmental-affairs-to-be-the-minister-for-the-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2015-99
:::

[NEW BRIDGE FOR THE ST. LAWRENCE ACT](/eng/acts/N-22.6)

Registration 2015-11-18

Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure, Communities and
Intergovernmental Affairs to be the Minister for the purposes of the Act

P.C. 2015-1242 2015-11-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the [New Bridge for the St.
Lawrence Act](/eng/acts/N-22.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-859-E_hq_13947){#fn_81000-3-859-E_hq_13947-ID0EBDAA},
effective November 4, 2015,

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-859-E_hq_13947-ID0EBDAA){#fn_81000-3-859-E_hq_13947}S.C.
  2014, c. 20, s. 375
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2014-857 of June 26,
  2014[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-829-e_hq_13569){#fn_81000-3-829-e_hq_13569-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-829-e_hq_13569-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_81000-3-829-e_hq_13569}SI/2014-63
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of Infrastructure,
  Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs, a member of the Queen's
  Privy Council for Canada, to be the Minister for the purposes of the
  [New Bridge for the St. Lawrence Act](/eng/acts/N-22.6)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-859-E_hq_13947){#fn_81000-3-859-E_hq_13947-ID0EBAAAA}.
