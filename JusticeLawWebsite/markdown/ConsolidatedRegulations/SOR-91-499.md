## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-937298 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Rules shall be
liberally construed to secure the fairest, least expensive and most
expeditious determination of every proceeding, in accordance with
section 35 of the Act.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Rules of General Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-937300 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-937301 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Except where the context
otherwise requires, this Part applies to all proceedings before the
Tribunal.

### [Directions on Procedure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-937303 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** If a question of procedure
arises that is not covered or not fully covered in these Rules, the
question must be disposed of in a manner that the Tribunal directs and
that is consistent with any of these Rules that are applicable and that
considerations of procedural fairness permit.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-87, s. 2

### [Dispensing with or Varying Procedure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-937307 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may dispense
with, vary or supplement any of these Rules if it is fair and equitable
to do so or to provide for a more expeditious or informal process, as
the circumstances and considerations of fairness permit.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-139, s. 2
:::

### [Combining of Proceedings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-937311 .Subheading}

**[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may, on
its own initiative or on the written request of a party, combine two or
more proceedings to provide for a more expeditious or informal process,
as the circumstances and considerations of fairness permit.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-139, s. 2
:::

### [Defect in Form and Irregularity]{.HTitleText2} {#h-937315 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No proceeding is invalid by
reason of a defect in form or a technical irregularity.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2006-161, s. 1(F)

### [Extending or Abridging Time Limits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-937319 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** If it is fair and equitable
to do so, the Tribunal may extend or abridge the time limits fixed by
these Rules or otherwise fixed by the Tribunal, either before or after
their expiry.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-139, s. 3

### [Computation of Time]{.HTitleText2} {#h-937323 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise provided,
the computation of time under these Rules or a direction of the Tribunal
is governed by sections 26 to 28 of the [Interpretation
Act](/eng/acts/I-21).

### [Participation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-937325 .Subheading}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who proposes to participate in a proceeding, other than a
  proceeding under Part II or Part X, shall file with the Tribunal a
  notice of participation on the relevant Tribunal form on or before the
  date that is specified in the notice published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) under rule 54, 65, 71, 73.1, 73.5,
  76, 78 or 85, as the case may be.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person referred to in subrule (1) sets out, in
  the notice of participation, the name of the counsel by whom they will
  be represented, the counsel shall file with the Tribunal a notice of
  representation on the relevant Tribunal form on or before the date
  that is specified in the notice published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) under rule 54, 65, 71, 73.1, 73.5,
  76, 78 or 85, as the case may be.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-139, s. 4
:::

### [Counsel of Record]{.HTitleText2} {#h-937331 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  counsel who signs a document filed pursuant to these Rules on behalf
  of any party shall be the counsel of record for the party commencing
  on the date of filing and continuing until a change, if any, is made
  in accordance with subrule (2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party in a proceeding may change the party's
  counsel of record by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} filing with the Tribunal a Notice of Change of
    counsel of record signed by the new counsel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} serving a copy of the Notice on the former counsel
    and every other party in the proceeding; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} filing with the Tribunal proof of service of the
    Notice.

### [Filing of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-937338 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  parties must file their documents by electronic transmission.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may, if it considers that it is
  justified in the circumstances, allow paper filing.

- [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} Documents filed by electronic transmission shall
  constitute the original.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party may make a written request to the Tribunal to
  file a document as a single copy exhibit.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A document that is filed by fax must be accompanied
  by a cover page setting out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sender's name, postal and email addresses, and
    telephone and fax numbers ;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total number of pages transmitted, including
    the cover page;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the title or description of the document
    transmitted; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, telephone number and email address of a
    person to contact if transmission problems occur.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A document that is filed by email must be accompanied
  by an electronic message setting out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sender's name, telephone number, postal and
    email addresses;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the title or description of the document
    transmitted; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name, telephone number and email address of a
    person to contact if transmission problems occur.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule 31(3) and rule 96, the date of
  filing of a document is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the document is filed by electronic
    transmission, the date of transmission; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the date on which the document
    is received by the Tribunal, as evidenced by the date stamped on it.

### [Statement Under Oath]{.HTitleText2} {#h-937358 .Subheading}

- **[[12.01]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.01
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a statement made under oath or
  affirmation is filed by electronic transmission, it must include a
  handwritten signature and the following statement: *"The document that
  is being submitted by electronic transmission to the Tribunal is an
  electronic version of a paper document that has been signed by the
  affiant. The signed document in paper copy is available and will be
  produced if requested by the Tribunal."*

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party filing the statement must keep the signed
  document in paper copy until one year after all time limits for
  appeals expire.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} On the Tribunal's request, the party filing the
  statement must provide the signed document in paper copy for review.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-87, s. 4
:::

### [Official and Other Languages]{.HTitleText2} {#h-937365 .Subheading}

- **[[12.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (2), all
  documents filed with the Tribunal must be in English or French.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person may file an original document in a language
  other than English or French if, at the same time, the person also
  files a translation of it in English or French and an affidavit
  attesting to the accuracy of the translation. If the document is
  required to be served, the translation and affidavit must be served at
  the same time.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-139, s. 5
:::

### [Service of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-937371 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to rule 17 and in addition
  to the provisions of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) in respect
  of appeals under Part VII of that Act, the following rules apply to
  the service of documents:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a document is required to be served personally,
    the service shall be made

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on an individual, by leaving a copy of it with
      the individual,

    - [(i.1)]{.lawlabel} on a general partnership, by leaving a copy of
      it with one of the partners,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on a corporation, by leaving a copy of it with
      an officer, director or agent of the corporation, or with a person
      at any place of business of the corporation who appears to manage
      or be in control of the place of business, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, in any manner that the
      Tribunal determines is just and equitable in the circumstances;
      and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a document is not required to be served
    personally, the service shall be made at the address for service of
    the party.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2000-139, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The address for service of a party shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where there is a counsel of record for a party, the
    business address of the counsel as shown on the most recent document
    filed by the counsel that shows the counsel's address; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where there is no counsel of record

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the Minister of National Revenue
      or the President, the Office of the Deputy Attorney General of
      Canada in Ottawa, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other party, the address of
      the party as shown on the most recent document filed by the party
      that shows the party's address.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The service of a document at an address for service
  must be made by sending it to the address for service by hand, mail,
  registered mail, courier or electronic transmission.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A document that is served by fax must be accompanied
  by a cover page setting out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sender's name, postal and email addresses and
    telephone and fax numbers;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person to be served;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total number of pages transmitted, including
    the cover page;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the title or description of the document
    transmitted; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name, telephone number and email address of a
    person to contact if transmission problems occur.

- [(5.1)]{.lawlabel} A document that is served by email must be
  accompanied by an electronic message setting out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sender's name, telephone number, postal and
    email addresses;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person to be served;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the title or description of the document
    transmitted; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, telephone number and email address of a
    person to contact if transmission problems occur.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} In the absence of proof to the contrary, the date of
  service of a document is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the document is served personally, the date of
    delivery;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the document is served by mail or registered
    mail, the date that is five days after the earliest postal date
    appearing on the envelope containing the document;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the document is served by electronic
    transmission, the date of the transmission; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the document is served by courier, the date of
    receipt indicated on the courier receipt.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} If a document is required under these Rules to be
  served by a party or by direction of the Tribunal, the Tribunal may
  direct that a proof of service be filed with the Tribunal.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} A proof of service of a document shall be made by
  showing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgement of service signed by or on
    behalf of the person served; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an affidavit of service stating the name of the
    person who served the document and the date, place and manner of
    service.

### [Brief]{.HTitleText2} {#h-937627 .Subheading}

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  appellant must, within 60 days after receipt of an acknowledgement
  referred to in section 32,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} file with the Tribunal a brief prepared in
    accordance with subrules (2) and (3); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to rule 17, serve a copy of the brief
    without delay on the other parties, and send to the Tribunal a
    confirmation that the service has been effected.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A brief referred to in subrule (1) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be dated and signed by the appellant or by the
    appellant's counsel, if any;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be numbered on each page;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be divided into paragraphs that are numbered
    consecutively and that set out

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the grounds for appeal and of the
      material facts relevant to each ground,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the goods in issue,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a statement of points in issue between the
      parties,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the statutory provisions relied on,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the history of the proceeding before the filing
      of the notice of appeal,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the Tribunal's jurisdiction to hear the appeal,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} a brief statement of argument to be made at the
      hearing, and

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the decision, order, finding or
      declaration sought;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} include a table of authorities on which the
    appellant intends to rely and a copy of those authorities that are
    reasonably necessary in the presentation of the appeal or as
    otherwise directed by the Tribunal;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} include a copy of any document that may be useful
    in explaining or supporting the appeal and any other information
    relating to the appeal that the Tribunal requires; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} contain the name, address for service, telephone
    number and email address of the appellant and of the appellant's
    counsel, if any.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An appellant who intends to rely at the hearing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on any documents or authorities that were not
    available or that could not be included in a brief filed with the
    Tribunal must, not less than 20 days before the hearing, file them
    with the Tribunal and, subject to rule 17, serve a copy of them on
    the other parties;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on any physical exhibit must, not less than 20 days
    before the hearing, file it with the Tribunal and notify the other
    parties of the filing; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on any witness testimony must, not less than 20
    days before the hearing, file with the Tribunal a list providing the
    name and occupation of any proposed witness as well as the language
    to be used at the hearing.

### [Request for Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-937874 .Subheading}

- **[[61.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this rule, a request for information includes a request for the
  production of a document.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party may direct a request for information to any
  other party.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party that makes a request for information shall
  file the request with the Tribunal and serve it on the other parties
  within any time that the considerations of fairness and efficiency
  permit and that the Tribunal may direct.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A request for information shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be in writing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out the name of the party to whom it is
    addressed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} number each request for information consecutively;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} explain how the request is relevant or necessary to
    the proceeding; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be dated.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the party to whom the request is addressed refuses
  to give some or all of the information requested, that party must,
  within any time that the considerations of fairness and efficiency
  permit and that the Tribunal may direct,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the party contends that some or all of the
    information requested is not relevant or necessary, give reasons in
    support of that contention;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the party contends that some or all of the
    information requested is not available, give reasons for its
    unavailability and provide any other available information or
    documents that are of the same nature and to the same effect as the
    requested information; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the party makes any other contention, including
    a contention based on criteria referred to in paragraphs (7)(c) to
    (f), give reasons in support of that contention.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may, on its own initiative or on the
  request of a party, refuse the request or grant all or part of it on
  the basis of criteria set out in subrule (7).

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subrule (6), the Tribunal shall
  take the following criteria into account:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the relevance and necessity of the information
    requested;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any contentions referred to in subrule (5);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the sufficiency of the information already on the
    record;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the availability of the information from other
    sources;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the ability or inability of the party to respond;
    and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other matter relevant to the request.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} If the Tribunal orders the party to whom the request
  is addressed to provide some or all of the information requested, the
  party shall, within any time that the considerations of fairness and
  efficiency permit and that the Tribunal may direct,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serve on the other party

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a full and adequate response, in writing, for
      each question in the request,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a signed declaration that the responses are
      complete and correct to the best of the information and belief of
      the party providing the response, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the information requested, or copies of it; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} file with the Tribunal copies of the responses and
    information in the number that it indicates are necessary for it and
    the interested parties.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} A party to whom a request for information is directed
  complies with the order of the Tribunal if the party indicates to the
  other party which of its records contain the relevant information and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the party specifies in sufficient detail where the
    information may be found;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the burden of obtaining the information is
    substantially the same for either party; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the party directing the request is given a
    reasonable opportunity to examine the records and make copies or
    summaries of them.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} If a party files a response or documents that
  contain confidential information, the party who provides the
  confidential information shall, within any time that the
  considerations of fairness and efficiency permit and that the Tribunal
  may direct, file a non-confidential edited version or a
  non-confidential summary of the response in accordance with paragraph
  46(1)(b) of the Act.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} If a party to whom a request for information is
  directed does not comply with this rule, the party who directed the
  request for information may request the Tribunal to order compliance.

## [PART XII]{.HLabel1}[Extension Inquiries Under Section 30.07 of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-938373 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-938374 .Subheading}

**[[109]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-109 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies in
respect of an extension inquiry under section 30.07 of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-139, s. 62
:::

### [Notice of Expiry]{.HTitleText2} {#h-938378 .Subheading}

**[[110]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-110 .sectionLabel}** If the Tribunal is
required to publish a notice of expiry under subsection 30.03(1) of the
Act in respect of an order referred to in that subsection, the notice
shall be published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/)
not later than eight months before the expiry date and shall set out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the order is scheduled to expire;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on or before which written submissions must
  be filed by interested parties requesting or opposing an extension
  inquiry;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the address of the Tribunal for the filing or service
  of documents and where information in respect of the extension inquiry
  may be obtained;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number of copies of each written submission that
  must be filed;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} instructions in respect of the filing of confidential
  information; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other relevant information that the Tribunal may
  require.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-139, s. 62
:::

### [Request for Extension]{.HTitleText2} {#h-938388 .Subheading}

**[[111]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-111 .sectionLabel}** A request for an
extension inquiry filed with the Tribunal shall be signed by the
requester or by their counsel, if any, and, in addition to providing the
information required by section 30.05 of the Act, shall be accompanied
by the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone and fax numbers, if
  any, of the requester or their counsel;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and description of the imported goods and
  the like or directly competitive goods;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the names of the domestic producers on whose behalf
  the request is being made and the proportion of total domestic
  production of like or directly competitive goods that is produced by
  them;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any information that is necessary to address the
  matters raised in subsection 4(1) and section 6 or 7 of the [Canadian
  International Trade Tribunal Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-35)
  and subsection 30.03(2) of the Act, as the case may be; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating why the order that is the
  subject of the extension inquiry continues to be necessary to prevent
  or remedy serious injury to domestic producers of like or directly
  competitive goods.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-139, s. 62
:::

**[[112]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-112 .sectionLabel}** If a notice is provided
under subsection 30.06(3) of the Act, the Tribunal shall give the other
interested parties referred to in that subsection an opportunity to make
representations to the Tribunal concerning the request referred to in
rule 111.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-139, s. 62
:::

**[[113]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-113 .sectionLabel}** If the Tribunal
commences an extension inquiry, the notice published under subsection
30.07(2) of the Act shall set out the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the statutory authority for the extension inquiry;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the subject-matter of the extension inquiry, together
  with any other relevant details of the extension inquiry that the
  Tribunal directs;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on or before which an other interested party
  must file a notice of participation;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date on or before which counsel for an interested
  party must file a notice of representation and, if appropriate, a
  declaration and undertaking referred to in subrule 16(1);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date on or before which written submissions must
  be filed;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number of copies of each written submission that
  must be filed;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} instructions with respect to the filing of
  confidential information;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the address of the Tribunal to which written
  submissions or correspondence may be sent or delivered and at which
  information in respect of the extension inquiry may be obtained;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the place and time fixed for the commencement of a
  hearing in the extension inquiry; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} any other information that the Tribunal considers
  relevant to the extension inquiry.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-139, s. 62
- SOR/2002-402, s. 9(E)
- SOR/2006-161, s. 7
- SOR/2018-87, ss. 88, 91
:::
