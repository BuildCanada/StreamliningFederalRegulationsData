## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557350 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[authorized hours]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorized hours]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of any location
    at which customs and excise services may be provided and in respect
    of any such service, means the hours of service prescribed by the
    Deputy Minister for that service at that location; ([heures
    autorisées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Deputy Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Deputy Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Deputy Minister of
    National Revenue for Customs and Excise;
    ([sous-ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Revenue; ([Ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[special services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[special services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means customs and excise
    services

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provided at any time upon request by any person
      therefor and approved by the Deputy Minister,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provided at any time by a person in a supervisory
      capacity at an establishment licensed under the [Excise
      Act](/eng/acts/E-14) or at a bonded warehouse, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provided in connection with

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the acceptance outside authorized hours of
        reports from operators of aircraft, trains, vessels or motor
        vehicles, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the examination of persons and the examination
        and release or other disposition of goods conveyed on aircraft,
        trains, vessels or motor vehicles,

    but does not include

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} services provided by an officer at a distillery,
      brewery, tobacco manufactory, cigar manufactory or a tobacco
      packer licensed under the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14) during a
      regular 8-hour period prescribed by the Deputy Minister for each
      day of the week except Saturday and Sunday and any holiday,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the examination or documentation at any time of
      military aircraft, vessels or motor vehicles, or

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the examination of the effects of a settler or a
      returning resident of Canada during authorized hours at a
      destination within a port area. ([services
      spéciaux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557365 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person for whom a special service is provided shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pay in respect of that service a charge of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the first 2 hours or portion thereof ![blank
      line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$54; and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for each hour or portion thereof in excess of
      2 hours ![blank
      line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$27; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide or pay for the transportation and living
    expenses of, and suitable accommodation for, each officer required
    to render such service.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where more than one officer is engaged in performing
  a special service, the time spent in performing that service is, for
  the purposes of this section, the aggregate of the time spent by all
  the officers so engaged.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/83-924, s. 1
- SOR/84-932, s. 1
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If the chief officer of
customs for the place or area in which a special service is to be
performed is of the opinion that security is required to guarantee the
payment of charges incurred in providing that service, the person
requesting that service must give security in accordance with the
requirements of the [Financial Security (Electronic Means)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-42) and in an amount determined
by the Minister.
