## Remission Order Concerning Certain Expenses Incurred as a Result of the Tsunami Disaster of December 26, 2004 in South and Southeast Asia {#remission-order-concerning-certain-expenses-incurred-as-a-result-of-the-tsunami-disaster-of-december-26-2004-in-south-and-southeast-asia .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-100
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2006-07-12

Remission Order Concerning Certain Expenses Incurred as a Result of the
Tsunami Disaster of December 26, 2004 in South and Southeast Asia

P.C. 2006-587 2006-06-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that the
collection of costs for certain expenses incurred as a result of the
tsunami disaster of December 26, 2004 in South and Southeast Asia is
unreasonable and unjust and that remission of that debt is in the public
interest, on the recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs and
the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed
[Remission Order Concerning Certain Expenses Incurred as a Result of the
Tsunami Disaster of December 26, 2004 in South and Southeast
Asia](/eng/regulations/SI-2006-100).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612170 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission is hereby granted of the
  following costs --- paid or payable --- and, any interest payable on
  those costs, incurred as a result of the tsunami disaster of December
  26, 2004 in South and Southeast Asia, to the person responsible for
  their payment:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} costs incurred for the repatriation to Canada or
    elsewhere of deceased Canadian citizens and their accompanying
    baggage;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} transportation costs for up to two persons
    designated by the estate or succession to accompany the remains or
    ashes of the deceased; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fees incurred for emergency hospitalization or
    emergency medical care of Canadian citizens.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to the portion of costs
  covered by insurance, public health or any other source of
  indemnification.

<!-- -->

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission is hereby granted of the
  fee --- paid or payable --- under section 4 of the [Consular Services
  Fees Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-538) and the fees --- paid
  or payable --- set out in the schedule to the [Passport Services Fees
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._719), and, any interest
  payable on those fees, to Canadian victims of the tsunami disaster of
  December 26, 2004 who applied for a passport as a result of that
  disaster.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to passport services
  fees that are reimbursable under the [Repayment of Receipts
  Regulations, 1997](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-127) to destitute persons.

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612180 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on
condition that the claimant provides, before December 26, 2006, an
official document to the Department of Foreign Affairs and International
Trade evidencing either the claimant's payment of the fees or costs or
the claimant's indebtedness for them.
