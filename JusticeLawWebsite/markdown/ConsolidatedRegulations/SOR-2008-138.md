## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743747 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[apparel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[apparel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any good referred to in tariff item No.
      3926.20.92, 3926.20.93, 3926.20.94, 3926.20.95 or 3926.20.99,
      subheading 4015.19 or 4015.90 or Chapter 61 or 62 of the List; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} those goods referred to in heading 43.04 of the
      List that are articles of apparel or clothing accessories.
      ([vêtements]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[List]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[List]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the List of Tariff Provisions set
    out in the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).
    ([liste]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[textiles]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[textiles]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any good referred to in Chapter 50, 51, 52, 53,
      54, 55, 56, 58, 59 or 60 of the List;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} those goods referred to in subheading 4008.11,
      7019.40, 7019.51, 7019.52 or 7019.59 of the List for use in the
      manufacture of apparel;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} woven, knitted or crocheted fabrics, felt or
      nonwovens, impregnated, coated, covered or laminated with plastics
      referred to in subheading 3921.12, 3921.13 or 3921.90, or yarn
      referred to in subheading 7019.19 of the List; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} artificial fur referred to in heading 43.04 of
      the List. ([textiles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[value of the textiles produced in Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[value of the textiles produced in Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means the value free on board (FOB) at the place of exit from Canada
    as declared on the Export Declaration (Form B-13A) prescribed by the
    Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness under the
    [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([valeur des textiles produits au
    Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
calculating the value for duty of imported materials under paragraph
3(2)(b), imported fibres referred to in headings 50.01 to 50.03, 51.01
to 51.05, 52.01 to 52.03 and 53.01 to 53.05 of the List are not
considered as imported materials.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743760 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 4, remission is granted of the customs duties paid or
  payable on the importation of apparel produced in a country or
  territory set out in the schedule in whole or in part from textiles
  produced in Canada and shipped directly to that country or territory
  from Canada, without undergoing further processing outside that
  country or territory, and then shipped directly to Canada from that
  country or territory.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), if the textiles
  produced in Canada contain imported materials, remission shall be
  granted if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the imported materials are sufficiently transformed
    to undergo a change in tariff classification or to be considered
    bleached for the purpose of tariff classification; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the value for duty of the imported materials,
    determined in accordance with sections 48 to 53 of the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), is less than 50% of the value of the
    textiles produced in Canada.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted in an amount equal to the lesser
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the value of the textiles produced in Canada and
    incorporated in the imported apparel, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the customs duties paid or payable on the imported
    apparel.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743771 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted under
section 3 on the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that no other claim for relief, refund or drawback
  under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) has been, or may be,
  granted in respect of any customs duties paid or payable with respect
  to the goods on which the remission is claimed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the textiles produced in Canada are exported on
  or after the day on which this Order comes into force;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} that the apparel is imported within two years after
  the day on which the textiles produced in Canada, which were
  incorporated in the apparel, are exported;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} that a claim for remission is made by the importer to
  the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two
  years after the day on which the apparel is imported into Canada; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} that the importer provides the Canada Border Services
  Agency with any evidence or information that demonstrates that the
  importer is entitled to the remission.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743778 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
