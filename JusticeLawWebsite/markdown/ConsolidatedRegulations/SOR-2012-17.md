## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1495143 .Part}

**[[0.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-0.1 .sectionLabel}** In this Permit,
[[country of melt and pour]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the country where
the raw steel contained in a carbon steel product is first produced in a
liquid state in a steel-making furnace and poured into its first solid
state which can take the form of a semi-finished product that is a slab,
billet or ingot or of a finished steel mill product.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-784137 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Any resident of Canada may,
under the authority of this Permit, import into Canada any goods
described in item 80 of the [Import Control
List](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._604).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** If any goods imported under
the authority of this Permit are required to be accounted for under
section 32 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), the resident of
Canada must, at the time of accounting or interim accounting, whichever
comes first, include the statement "GIP80" or "LGI80" in the prescribed
form.

## [Terms and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-784140 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A resident of Canada who
imports goods under the authority of this Permit must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 10 days after receipt of a request from the
  Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development, provide to the
  Department the documents and records referred to in section 4 in
  respect of any import made during the period specified in the request;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} at all reasonable times

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} make the documents and records referred to in
    section 4 available for inspection by any persons authorized by the
    Minister, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} provide to those persons all reasonable assistance
    to facilitate the inspection of those documents and records;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} within the time period specified by the Department,
  provide to the Department any documents and records necessary for
  making a determination on the country of origin, the country of melt
  and pour, the import value or the quantity of the imported carbon
  steel products; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} at the time of importation, specify the country of
  melt and pour of the imported goods in the form and manner determined
  by the Canada Border Services Agency.

**[[3.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.2 .sectionLabel}** Paragraph 3(d) does not
apply if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total value for duty of the imported goods does
  not exceed \$5,000; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the imported goods are

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} cold-formed or cold-finished angles, shapes or
    sections,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} welded angles, shapes or sections,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} switch blades, crossing frogs, point rods or
    other crossing pieces for railway or tramway track construction,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} structures, parts of structures or structural
    plates, rods, angles, shapes, sections, tubes or the like,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} stranded wire, ropes, cables or the like, not
    electrically insulated,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} barbed wires or fencing wires, or

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} wire nails, tacks, pins, staples or the like.

## [Documents and Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-784142 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A resident of Canada who
imports goods under this Permit must retain, for a period of six years
after the year in which the import is made, documents and records
containing the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the importer or consignee;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} proof of Canadian residency;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date of entry of the goods into Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the quantity, expressed in kilograms, of the goods;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the country from which the goods are imported;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the country of origin of the goods;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the shipping document with freight and other
  transportation costs indicated separately;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the tariff classification of the goods indicated in
  the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011);

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the import value in Canadian dollars;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the goods; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the country of melt and pour.
