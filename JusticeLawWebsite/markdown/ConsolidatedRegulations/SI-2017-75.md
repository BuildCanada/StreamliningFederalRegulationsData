## Statistics Canada Census and Survey Related Term Employment Exclusion Approval Order {#statistics-canada-census-and-survey-related-term-employment-exclusion-approval-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2017-75
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2017-12-13

Statistics Canada Census and Survey Related Term Employment Exclusion
Approval Order

P.C. 2017-1438 2017-11-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Public Works and Government Services, pursuant to
section 20 of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBDHA}, approves

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EBDHA){#fn_a}S.C.
  2003, c. 22, ss. 12 and 13
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the re-application by the Public Service Commission
  of the provisions of that Act to the positions excluded pursuant to
  the [Statistics Canada Census and Survey Related Term Employment
  Exclusion Approval Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2010-81)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBBBHA} and the persons occupying
  them;

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b-ID0EBBBHA){#fn_b}SI/2010-81
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exclusion by the Public Service Commission from
  the application of the definitions
  [[deployment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[internal appointment
  process]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1),
  paragraphs 22(2)(a) to (c)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_c){#fn_c-ID0EOEAHA} and sections 39.1 to
  41[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_d){#fn_d-ID0EMEAHA}, 48, 51 to
  53[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_e){#fn_e-ID0EKEAHA}, 57, 59 and
  62 of that Act to positions in Statistics Canada whose function is
  solely to provide administrative or management support or to perform
  data processing duties for the purpose of conducting the population
  census and the agriculture census as described in the [Statistics
  Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179){#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179-ID0EHEAHA},
  or the national survey made pursuant to sections 7 and 8 of the
  [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179){#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179-ID0EEEAHA}
  when the survey replaces the population census long-form questionnaire
  and is conducted in conjunction with the population census and to the
  persons appointed or deployed to those positions on a specified term
  basis, except for those persons mentioned in subsection 5(1) of the
  [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179){#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179-ID0EBEAHA}.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_c-ID0EOEAHA){#fn_c}S.C. 2015, c. 5, s. 2
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_d-ID0EMEAHA){#fn_d}S.C. 2006, c. 9, s. 103;
    S.C. 2015, c. 5, s. 7
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_e-ID0EKEAHA){#fn_e}S.C. 2015, c. 5, s. 9
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179-ID0EHEAHA){#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179}R.S., c.
    S-19
    :::

Whereas the Public Service Commission has decided that it is neither
practicable nor in the best interests of the public service to apply
certain provisions of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EKAGA} to positions in Statistics
Canada whose function is solely to provide administrative or management
support or to perform data processing duties for the purpose of
conducting the population census and the agriculture census as described
in the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179){#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179-ID0EHAGA},
or the national survey made pursuant to sections 7 and 8 of the
[Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179){#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179-ID0EEAGA}
when the survey replaces the population census long-form questionnaire
and is conducted in conjunction with the population census and to the
persons appointed or deployed to those positions on a specified term
basis, except for those persons mentioned in subsection 5(1) of the
[Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179){#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179-ID0EBAGA};

And whereas, pursuant to subsection 20(2) of the [Public Service
Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBAFA}, the Public Service
Commission has consulted with the employer;

Therefore, the Public Service Commission, pursuant to section 20 of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBCEA},

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} reapplies the provisions of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EEABEA} to the positions excluded
  pursuant to the [Statistics Canada Census and Survey Related Term
  Employment Exclusion Approval
  Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2010-81)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBABEA} and the persons occupying
  them,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} excludes from the application of the definitions
  [[deployment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[internal appointment
  process]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1),
  paragraphs 22(2)(a) to (c)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_c){#fn_c-ID0ERAAEA} and sections 39.1 to
  41[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_d){#fn_d-ID0EPAAEA}, 48, 51 to
  53[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_e){#fn_e-ID0ENAAEA}, 57, 59 and
  62 of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EKAAEA} to positions in
  Statistics Canada whose function is solely to provide administrative
  or management support or to perform data processing duties for the
  purpose of conducting the population census and the agriculture census
  as described in the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179){#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179-ID0EHAAEA},
  or the national survey made pursuant to sections 7 and 8 of the
  [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179){#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179-ID0EEAAEA}
  when the survey replaces the population census long-form questionnaire
  and is conducted in conjunction with the population census and to the
  persons appointed or deployed to those positions on a specified term
  basis, except for those persons mentioned in subsection 5(1) of the
  [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179){#fn_SI-2017-75_e_hq_21179-ID0EBAAEA}.

Ottawa, July 18, 2017
