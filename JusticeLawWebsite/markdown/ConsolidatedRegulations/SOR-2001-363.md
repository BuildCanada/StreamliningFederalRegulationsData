## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673072 .Part}

Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).

## [Aggregate Financial Exposure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673075 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aggregate financial exposure

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 495.2(1) of the Act, the aggregate financial exposure of a
bank is the amount determined by the formula

::: {#673078 .Section}
A + B + C - D

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the book value of all investments by the bank and its
    subsidiaries in securities of the related party;

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the total of the principal amounts of all outstanding loans to
    the related party that are held by the bank and its subsidiaries,
    other than
    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} loans referred to in paragraph 491(a) of the Act,
      and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} deposits made for clearing purposes with a
      financial institution that is a direct clearer or a member of a
      clearing group under the by-laws of the Canadian Payments
      Association;

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the total of all outstanding amounts in respect of endorsements,
    acceptances or guarantees by the bank and its subsidiaries on behalf
    of the related party, other than guarantees referred to in paragraph
    491(a) of the Act; and

[D]{.dfn}
:   is the total of all amounts included in the value of A, B and C in
    respect of transactions referred to in section 490 of the Act.
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673086 .Part}

.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which

section 129 of the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1), chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, comes
into force.
