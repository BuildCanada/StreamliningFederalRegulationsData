## National Historic Parks Wildlife and Domestic Animals Regulations {#national-historic-parks-wildlife-and-domestic-animals-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-613
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

[CANADA NATIONAL PARKS ACT](/eng/acts/N-14.01)

Registration 1981-07-24

Regulations Respecting Wildlife and Domestic Animals in the National
Historic Parks of Canada

P.C. 1981-2021 1981-07-23
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
section 7 of the National Parks Act, section 3 of the National Historic
Parks Order and paragraph 13(a) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations
respecting wildlife and domestic animals in the National Historic Parks
of Canada](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-613).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871632 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [National Historic Parks Wildlife and Domestic Animals
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-613).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871634 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a director of Parks Canada of
    the Department of the Environment; ([Directeur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[domestic animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[domestic animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an animal of a species
    of vertebrates that has been domesticated by man so as to live and
    breed in a tame condition and depend on man for survival; ([animal
    domestique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[exotic wildlife]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exotic wildlife]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all wildlife that is
    not indigenous to a park; ([espèce exotique]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any weapon from which any
    shot, bullet or other missile can be discharged and that is capable
    of causing injury or death to wildlife and includes anything that
    can be adapted for use as a firearm and anything that is designed
    for or capable of being used for the purpose of causing injury or
    death to wildlife; ([arme à feu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[keep]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[keep]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} with respect to a domestic animal,
    means to own, possess, harbour, maintain, or have control or custody
    of the domestic animal; ([garder]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[keeper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[keeper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} with respect to a domestic animal,
    means the person who keeps it, except that where that person is
    under the age of eighteen years, [[keeper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means the parent or other adult who is responsible for that person;
    ([gardien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any National Historic Park of
    Canada; ([parc]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[possession]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[possession]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 3(4) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);
    ([possession]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/92-587,
    s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

[[wildlife]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wildlife]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all wild mammals, amphibians,
    reptiles and wild birds and includes any part of such mammal,
    amphibian, reptile or bird, including their eggs or young. ([animal
    sauvage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-587, s. 2
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871648 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any domestic animal being used to assist or guide
  blind persons; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any domestic animal kept in the Park for purposes of
  Park management, preservation and control.

## [Wildlife]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871652 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided in these Regulations, no person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} disturb, hunt, capture or destroy any wildlife within
  a Park;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have in his possession, or in the possession of his
  servant or agent or any other person on his behalf, any wildlife
  killed or procured within a Park;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have in his possession within a Park any wildlife
  during a closed season for that wildlife, established for the Province
  in which the wildlife was captured or killed, or a closed season for
  that wildlife, established under the Migratory Birds Convention Act;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} touch or feed wildlife in a Park or entice wildlife
  to approach by holding out food-stuffs or bait of any kind.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may issue a permit authorizing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the taking or killing of wildlife within a Park for
    scientific purposes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sale or other public disposal of wildlife
    products from a Park; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the relocation or destruction of wildlife when he
    considers it necessary for Park management purposes.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person whom the superintendent has authorized to
  take or kill wildlife pursuant to paragraph (1)(a) shall remove from a
  Park any wildlife taken or killed without the permission of the
  superintendent.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-587, s. 2
:::

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall be in possession of a firearm within a Park unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the firearm is carried in a motor vehicle, is
    unloaded, dismantled or encased and is not easily accessible to any
    occupant of the motor vehicle; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} he has a permit to carry a firearm issued by the
    superintendent and he is complying with the conditions of the
    permit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a peace officer in
  the performance of his duty, to any person acting under a permit
  issued pursuant to subsection 5(1) or to any Park employee taking part
  in a historic re-enactment program or a historic animation program.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-199, s. 1
- SOR/92-587, s. 2
- SOR/94-363, s. 4(E)
:::

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), no person shall use any poison, poisonous
  substance, gas or drug for the purpose of capturing, injuring or
  destroying wildlife, or have in his possession any poison, poisonous
  substance, gas, or drug that may be used for such purpose.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a peace officer, to
  the superintendent, or to a person acting under a permit issued
  pursuant to subsection 5(1).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-587, s. 2
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No person shall, between
sunset and sunrise, shine a movable light having a voltage greater than
4.5 volts in any area of a Park frequented by wildlife.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-199, s. 2
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
authorized by the Director, no person shall release within a Park,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any wildlife;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any exotic wildlife; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any domestic animal.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every person in
possession of a firearm shall immediately, on the request of a peace
officer, allow the peace officer to inspect

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the firearm; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any permit relating to the possession or use of the
  firearm.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** No person shall obstruct,
hinder, or in any way interfere with or wilfully furnish false
information to any peace officer in the discharge of his duties in
administering or enforcing these Regulations.

## [Domestic Animals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871689 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** No keeper shall bring or
keep a domestic animal

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in any building in a Park;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on any beach in a Park where public swimming is
  allowed; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in any other area in a Park designated by the
  superintendent by means of a sign or notice as an area where domestic
  animals are prohibited.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-199, s. 3
- SOR/92-587, s. 2
:::

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  keeper shall bring or keep a domestic animal in a Park unless the
  domestic animal is under physical control at all times in the Park.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), [[physical
  control]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} restrained by a leash whose length does not exceed
    three metres; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} kept in a container or enclosure, or in a motor
    vehicle.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-199, s. 4
- SOR/92-587, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent may,
at any time and for such period of time as he deems necessary for the
management or protection of the Park, prohibit the bringing into or
keeping of domestic animals in the Park.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-587, s. 2
:::

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A keeper who brings a
domestic animal into a Park shall ensure that the animal

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} does not become a nuisance or cause an unreasonable
  disturbance to persons;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not bite or injure or attempt to bite or injure
  any person;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not have any disease that may be contagious or
  dangerous to persons, domestic animals or wildlife; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} does not harass, chase or otherwise disturb wildlife
  or other domestic animals.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-199, s. 5
- SOR/92-587, s. 2
:::

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may order a keeper to remove from the Park any domestic
  animal that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is dangerous or hazardous to persons;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a disease that is believed to be infectious or
    dangerous to persons or to other domestic animals or wildlife;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has harassed, chased or otherwise disturbed other
    domestic animals or wildlife;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} has bitten or injured, or attempted to bite or
    injure, any person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any animal in respect of which an order has been made
  pursuant to subsection (1) shall be removed within a reasonable time
  and shall not be returned or brought into any Park, except in the case
  of a diseased domestic animal, which may be returned or brought into a
  Park when a certificate from a veterinarian indicating that the animal
  is in good health is provided to the superintendent.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-199, s. 6
- SOR/92-587, s. 2
:::

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** No keeper shall keep or
allow to remain in a Park any domestic animal in respect of which an
order has been made pursuant to subsection 16(1), unless a certificate
referred to in subsection 16(2) is issued.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-199, s. 7
:::

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  peace officer or the superintendent may remove from a Park or cause to
  be impounded any domestic animal

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is brought into or kept in a Park in violation
    of section 14; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is not removed from a Park within a reasonable
    time following the making of an order pursuant to section 16.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A peace officer may destroy any domestic animal that
  is at large and that he has been unable to remove from a Park or
  impound.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a peace officer or the superintendent impounds
  a domestic animal pursuant to subsection (1) or where a peace officer
  destroys a domestic animal pursuant to subsection (2), the peace
  officer or superintendent, as the case may be, shall, if the name of
  the keeper can readily be ascertained, forthwith notify the keeper of
  the domestic animal.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-199, s. 8
- SOR/92-587, s. 2
:::

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** No keeper shall remove an
impounded domestic animal from impoundment without first paying the
applicable impounding fee set out in the schedule for each day or part
thereof during which the animal was impounded.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-199, s. 9
:::

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Where a domestic animal
is not claimed by its keeper within a period of 72 hours after the
impoundment, the superintendent may dispose of or authorize the disposal
of the animal.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-199, s. 9
- SOR/92-587, s. 2
:::

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The keeper of a domestic
animal that is at large in a Park shall, at the request of a peace
officer or the superintendent, assist in capturing the domestic animal.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-199, s. 9
- SOR/92-587, s. 2
:::
