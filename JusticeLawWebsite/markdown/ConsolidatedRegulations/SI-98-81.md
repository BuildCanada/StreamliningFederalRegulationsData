## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-653180 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this section apply in these Specifications.

  [[actual scale interval]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[actual scale interval]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
      [[d]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the value, expressed in units of
      mass

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to analogue indications, of the
        difference between the values corresponding to two consecutive
        scale marks; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to digital indications, the
        difference between two consecutively indicated values. ([échelon
        réel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}*ou*[d]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"} )

  [[automatic indicating device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[automatic indicating device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      weighing device in which the position of equilibrium is obtained
      without an operator's intervention. ([appareil de pesage à
      équilibre automatique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[auxiliary indicating element]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[auxiliary indicating element]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      rider, a vernier or, in the case of a digital indicating device,
      the last figure after the decimal sign that is clearly
      differentiated from other figures such as by size, shape or
      colour. ([dispositif indicateur auxiliaire]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[initial zero-setting mechanism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[initial zero-setting mechanism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      feature that automatically sets a weighing device to zero at the
      time the device is switched on and before it is ready for
      use. ([dispositif de mise à zéro initiale]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[maximum capacity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[maximum capacity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
      [[Max]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the maximum weighing capacity of
      a weighing device and, in the case of a multiple range device, the
      maximum weighing capacity of the relevant range. ([portée
      maximale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}*ou*[Max]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"} )

  [[metrological function]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[metrological function]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a function of
      a weighing device that is necessary for the measurement process
      --- including the sensing of the measured quantity, the
      transmission, processing, storage and correction or adjustment of
      measurement signals or values and the indication or printing of
      measurement values --- and that can impact on the final quantity
      determination or price calculation or affect the validity of
      transactions. Metrological functions include, but are not limited
      to,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} displaying or printing gross, tare, net and
        negative weight values, zero, and unit and total prices;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} setting zero, linearity, sensitivity, scale
        intervals, maximum capacity and motion detection; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} performing the computation and rounding of
        price and weight values and the storing of values. ([fonction
        métrologique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[module]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[module]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a part of a weighing device
      that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} performs a specific metrological function;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} can be examined and tested separately; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is subject to the error limits set out in these
        Specifications. ([module]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[multi-interval device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[multi-interval device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a weighing
      device

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that has a single weighing range divided into
        partial weighing ranges, each of which has a different scale
        interval; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in which the appropriate partial weighing range
        is automatically determined according to the load applied, both
        for increasing and decreasing loads. ([appareil à échelons
        multiples]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[multiple range device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[multiple range device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a weighing
      device that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has, for the same weighing and load-receiving
        element, two or more weighing ranges, each of which

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} extends from zero to the maximum capacity of
          the range, and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has a different verification scale interval;
          and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} selects automatically, or permits the operator
        to select manually, any of the weighing ranges. ([appareil à
        étendues multiples]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[non-automatic indicating device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[non-automatic indicating device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      weighing device that requires an operator's intervention to obtain
      equilibrium, such as an equal-arm scale with weights. ([appareil
      de pesage à équilibre non automatique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[non-automatic weighing device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[non-automatic weighing device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
      [[weighing device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a weighing machine
      that weighs discrete loads and that requires an operator's
      intervention during the weighing process, such as to deposit the
      load to be measured on the weighing and load-receiving element and
      to remove it therefrom or to obtain weight results. ([appareil de
      pesage à fonctionnement non automatique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"}*ou*[appareil de pesage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} )

  [[number of verification scale intervals]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[number of verification scale
      intervals]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the value obtained by using
      the following formula:

      ::: {#653209 .Definition}
      Max/e

      where

      [Max]{.dfn}
      :   represents the maximum capacity, and

      [e]{.dfn}
      :   represents the verification scale interval. ([nombre
          d'échelons de vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})
      :::

  [[on-board weighing system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[on-board weighing system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a weighing
      device designed to be an integral part of, or attached or secured
      to, the frame, chassis, lifting mechanism or bed of mobile
      equipment such as a truck, tractor, trailer or forklift. ([système
      de pesage monté sur véhicule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[verification scale interval]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[verification scale interval]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
      [[e]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a value, specified by the
      manufacturer and expressed in units of mass, that is used for the
      classification and inspection of a weighing device. ([échelon de
      vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}*ou*[e]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"} )

  [[weight classifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[weight classifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a weighing device
      with a digital indicating element that rounds a weight value up to
      the next verification scale interval. ([appareil
      classificateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Specifications, a reference to a
  verification scale interval or actual scale interval refers to that of
  the weighing range or partial weighing range, as the case may be, that
  is being tested.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/2005-85, s. 1

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-653219 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), these Specifications apply to any non-automatic
  weighing device and to any equipment or accessory that is attached to
  the device or used in conjunction with it.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A non-automatic weighing device that, before June 15,
  1998, was approved under subsection 3(1) of the [Weights and Measures
  Act](/eng/acts/W-6) or inspected under the terms of a previous
  enactment --- or whose class, type or design was so approved ---

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is subject to the performance requirements set out
    in these Specifications; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not subject to any of the provisions of these
    Specifications relating to design, composition, construction or
    marking where the non-automatic weighing device meets the
    requirements that applied when its class, type or design was
    approved or, failing which, that applied to the device on its
    approval or inspection.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/2005-85, s. 2
:::

### [Verification Scale Interval]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653241 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The verification scale
interval for Class ![Oval outline with the text I
inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef004.png) and ![Oval outline with
the text II inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef005.png) weighing
devices that are not equipped with auxiliary indicating elements, for
Class ![Oval outline with the text III
inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef006.png), ![Oval outline with the
text IIIHD inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef007.png) and ![Oval
outline with the text IIII
inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef008.png) weighing devices, for
multiple range devices and for multi-interval devices is equal to the
actual scale interval.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For multi-interval devices,
the verification scale interval of a particular partial weighing range
must be smaller than the verification scale interval of the immediately
superior partial weighing range, and each range must comply with the
requirements set out in section 3 for the class into which the device
falls.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The verification scale
interval for Class ![Oval outline with the text I
inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef004.png) and ![Oval outline with
the text II inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef005.png) weighing
devices that are equipped with auxiliary indicating elements corresponds
to the value of the scale interval preceding the actual scale interval
of the auxiliary indicating element, and is larger than the actual scale
interval.

## [Performance - General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-653245 .Part}

### [In-service Limits of Error]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653246 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  in-service limits of error set out in column I of the table to this
  section apply, in respect of each class of non-automatic weighing
  devices set out in column II of that table, according to the specified
  load.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the table to this section,
  [[m]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the weight of the load placed on the
  weighing device, and is expressed in the number of verification scale
  intervals.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The in-service limits of error that apply to a Class
  ![Oval outline with the text IIIHD
  inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef007.png) weighing device are
  ±1 e where the load is not less than zero and not more than 500
  verification scale intervals, and where the load exceeds 500
  verification scale intervals, calculated by adding ±1 e for each
  additional 800 verification scale intervals or less, to a maximum of
  ±22 e.

### [Acceptance Limits of Error]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653257 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The acceptance limits of
error applicable for each class of non-automatic weighing device are
one-half the in-service limits of error set out in section 8 for that
device for a specified load.

### [Modules]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653259 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Where a module, such as
an indicating element, or a weighing and load-receiving element, is
tested separately for approval pursuant to section 3 of the [Weights and
Measures Act](/eng/acts/W-6),

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a module that is the only part of a
  weighing device that is subject to measurement errors due to
  disturbances or influence factors, the acceptance limits of error are
  the limits of error set out in these Specifications for that device
  for a specified load; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of all other modules, the acceptance
  limits of error are 0.7 times the limits of error set out in these
  Specifications for the weighing device for a specified load.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/2008-81, s. 2

### [Differences Between Results]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653265 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Regardless of the
difference between the results permitted by these Specifications, no
weighing result shall have an error that exceeds the applicable limits
of error for the given load, with the exception of results obtained
during disturbance tests.

### [Repeatability]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653267 .Subheading}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The difference between
the results of several weighings of the same load placed in
approximately the same location on a weighing and load-receiving element
shall not exceed the absolute value of the applicable in-service limits
of error for that load.

### [Eccentric Loading]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653269 .Subheading}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The difference between
the results for different positions of a load placed on a weighing and
load-receiving element shall not exceed the absolute value of the
applicable in-service limits of error for that load.

### [Load Discrimination and Sensitivity]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653271 .Subheading}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** With respect to
non-automatic indicating devices, the addition or removal of a load
equal to the applicable in-service limits of error for the load placed
on the weighing and load-receiving element, but not exceeding two
verification scale intervals, shall cause

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a weigh-beam or beam-over-pan scale
  that is without a supplementary indicating element and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is equipped with a trig loop, a movement in the
    position of the weigh-beam from the centre position to either limit
    of the trig loop, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is not equipped with a trig loop, the position of
    rest of the weigh-beam or lever system to change from the horizontal
    position or midway between limiting stops to either limit of motion;
    and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a weigh-beam or beam-over-pan scale
  that has a supplementary indicating element that has graduations
  without specific values, the position of the indicator to change

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a Class ![Oval outline with the text
    I inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef004.png) or ![Oval outline
    with the text II inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef005.png)
    device, by at least 1 mm (0.04 in.),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a Class ![Oval outline with the
    text III inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef006.png) or ![Oval
    outline with the text IIII
    inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef008.png) device with a
    maximum capacity not exceeding 30 kg (70 lb.), by at least 2 mm
    (0.08 in.), and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a Class ![Oval outline with the
    text III inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef006.png) or ![Oval
    outline with the text IIII
    inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef008.png) device with a
    maximum capacity greater than 30 kg (70 lb.), or a Class ![Oval
    outline with the text IIIHD
    inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef007.png) device, by at least
    5 mm (0.2 in.).

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** With respect to automatic
indicating devices, the addition or removal of a load equivalent to 1.4
times the actual scale interval to or from the weighing and
load-receiving element shall cause a change of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an analogue indicating element, at
  least one actual scale interval; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a digital indicating element, two
  actual scale intervals.

### [Return-to-Zero]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653285 .Subheading}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Under
  stable environmental conditions, the deviation from zero as soon as
  the weight indication has stabilized following the removal of a load
  that has remained on a weighing and load-receiving element for up to
  30 minutes shall not exceed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a single range weighing device,
    one-half of the verification scale interval;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a multiple range device, one-half of
    the verification scale interval for the range being tested; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a multi-interval device, one-half of
    the smallest verification scale interval.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a multiple range device, following the
  removal of a load greater than the maximum capacity of the first
  range, which load has remained on the weighing and load-receiving
  element for up to 30 minutes, and immediately after switching the
  device to its lowest weighing range, the indication near zero shall
  not vary by more than one verification scale interval of the lowest
  weighing range over a period of five minutes after switching the
  device to its lowest weighing range.

### [Creep]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653292 .Subheading}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** If a load is kept on a
weighing and load-receiving element under stable environmental
conditions, the difference between the weight indication obtained
immediately after placing the load on the element and the weight
indication observed during the following 30 minutes shall not exceed the
absolute value of the applicable limits of error for that load.

### [Agreement of Indications and Recordings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653296 .Subheading}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The weight values that
are indicated or recorded by the indicating and recording elements of a
weighing device and any equipment and accessories attached to the

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} exactly, in the case of digital values produced by

  the case of analogue values produced by elements, equipment and
  accessories that have the same verification scale intervals; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} within 0.6 times the largest verification scale
  interval or the verification scale interval common to the elements,
  equipment and accessories, in all other cases.

### [Computation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653303 .Subheading}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Indicated or recorded monetary value shall equal the

  weight indication or registration by the price per unit.

## [Performance -- Influence Factors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-653311 .Part}

### [Off-level Effect]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653312 .Subheading}

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  portable or movable weighing device, other than a device of the freely
  suspended type or an on-board weighing system, shall be equipped with
  a permanent means of indicating when it is level, unless it is capable
  of performing within the applicable limits of error when tilted up to
  three degrees or five per cent in any direction from a level position.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An on-board weighing system shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} capable of performing within the applicable limits
    of error when tilted up to three degrees or five per cent in any
    direction from a level position; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} equipped with a means to blank the weight
    indications and prevent the transmission, printing and storage of
    the weight results when the registrations are no longer within the
    applicable limits of error.

### [Declared Conditions of Use]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653318 .Subheading}

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** A weighing device shall
perform within the applicable limits of error when exposed to the range
of conditions specified by the manufacturer, as well as at any
temperature, humidity, pressure, voltage, frequency or other influencing
factors that may be encountered under normal conditions of use.

### [Temperature Effect on No Load Indication]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653320 .Subheading}

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The weight indication at
or near zero shall not vary by more than

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Class ![Oval outline with the text I
  inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef004.png) weighing devices, one
  verification scale interval for each change in temperature of 1°C;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Class ![Oval outline with the text
  IIIHD inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef007.png) weighing
  devices, three verification scale intervals for each change in
  temperature of 5°C; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of all other classes of weighing device,
  one verification scale interval for each change in temperature of 5°C.

### [Temperature]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653325 .Subheading}

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  weighing device shall perform within the applicable limits of error
  when exposed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where a temperature range is marked on the device,
    within that temperature range; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where no temperature range is marked on the device,
    at any temperature of at least -10°C and no more than 40°C.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The minimum marked temperature range on a weighing
  device shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Class ![Oval outline with the text I
    inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef004.png) devices, 5°C;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Class ![Oval outline with the text
    II inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef005.png) devices, 15°C;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Class ![Oval outline with the text
    III inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef006.png), ![Oval outline
    with the text IIIHD inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef007.png)
    and ![Oval outline with the text IIII
    inside](/images/SI-98-81img/SI-98-81_ef008.png) devices, 30°C.

### [Barometric Pressure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653334 .Subheading}

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The weight indication at
or near zero on a weighing device shall not vary by more than one
verification scale interval for a change in barometric pressure of 1 kPa
in the barometric pressure range of 95 to 105 kPa.

### [Voltage and Frequency Variations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653336 .Subheading}

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** A weighing device that
operates using alternating current shall, when exposed to voltage
variations from -15 per cent to +10 per cent and to frequency variations
of ± 2 per cent of its marked nominal values,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} perform within the applicable limits of error; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} blank the weight indication and prevent the
  transmission, printing and storage of weight results.

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** A battery-operated
weighing device, when exposed to battery voltage output less than or in
excess of the nominal voltage or voltage range, shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} perform within the applicable limits of error; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} blank the weight indication and prevent the
  transmission, printing and storage of weight results.

### [Humidity]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653343 .Subheading}

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** A weighing device shall
perform within applicable limits of error when exposed to relative
humidity of up to 85 per cent

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where a temperature range is marked on the device,
  within that temperature range; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where no temperature range is marked on the device,
  at any temperature of at least -10°C and no more than 40°C.

## [Performance -- Disturbances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-653347 .Part}

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** An electronic weighing
device, when disturbed by interference such as the effects of
electromagnetic or electrostatic fields, short time power reduction,
voltage spikes, electrostatic discharges or other disturbances that may
be encountered under normal conditions of use, shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide a weight indication or recording that does
  not differ from the value of the weight indication or recording that
  would be provided without interference by more than one verification
  scale interval;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} blank the weight indication and prevent the
  transmission, printing and storage of weight results;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide an error message and prevent the
  transmission, printing and storage of weight results; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide a weight indication that is so completely
  unstable that it cannot be interpreted, stored or printed as a correct
  measurement value.

## [Design, Composition and Construction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-653353 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653354 .Subheading}

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** A weighing device shall
be of a design, composition and construction that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} under normal conditions of use, enables it to measure
  accurately and does not facilitate the perpetration of fraud; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provides it with a static operating mode for
  inspection purposes, even if the device is designed to be used in a
  dynamic operating mode.

  to permit accurate measurement.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The indications and recordings generated by a

  under normal conditions of use of the device.

become obliterated or illegible under normal conditions of use of the
weighing device.

### [Over-capacity]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653388 .Subheading}

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an electronic computing scale other

  verification scale intervals; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other scale, 105 per cent of its
  maximum capacity.

### [Zero-setting and Zero-tracking Mechanisms]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653392 .Subheading}

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** A weighing device shall
have a means to set the zero indication within

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} ± 0.25 of the verification scale interval, in the
  case of a device that does not have an auxiliary indicating element;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} ± 0.5 of the actual scale interval, in the case of a
  device with an auxiliary indicating element.

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** A weighing device
equipped with a digital indicating element, other than a weighing device
with an auxiliary indicating element, shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have an element that displays a special signal when
  the deviation from zero is not greater than 0.25 times the
  verification scale interval; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have an automatic means that maintains a zero balance
  condition to within ± 0.25 of the verification scale interval.

## [Installation and Use]{.HTitleText1} {#h-653458 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653459 .Subheading}

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  weighing device and any equipment and accessories attached to or used
  in conjunction with the device shall be installed and used in a manner
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensures accurate measurement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not facilitate the perpetration of fraud;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is in accordance with the manufacturer or
    importer's instructions; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} respects the parameters, restrictions and
    limitations set out in the notice of approval issued in respect of
    the device under section 3 of the [Weights and Measures
    Act](/eng/acts/W-6).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The equipment and accessories described in
  subsection (1) shall be installed and used in such a manner that they
  do not detrimentally affect the performance of the device.

### [Approaches for Vehicle Scales]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653504 .Subheading}

- **[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Approach and exit ramps to and from
  a permanently installed vehicle scale shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be straight for a distance of at least 3 m adjacent
    to the weighbridge;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be as wide as, and in the same plane as, the
    weighbridge;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be constructed, for a distance of at least 3 m
    adjacent to the weighbridge, of concrete or similar durable material
    to ensure that they remain smooth, level and in the same plane as
    the weighbridge; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} beyond the 3 m distance set out in paragraphs (a)
    and (c), be constructed so as to permit

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} easy access by the vehicle being weighed,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} ready access for testing purposes, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any water to drain away from the scale.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where approach and exit ramps to and from a
  permanently installed vehicle scale include grating, the grating shall
  be of sufficient strength to withstand all loads that are intended to
  be supported by the scale.

**[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** Approach and exit ramps
to and from a temporarily installed vehicle scale shall be constructed,
for a distance of not less than 3 m adjacent to the weighbridge of the
scale, in a manner that ensures that the ramps remain level and in the
same plane as the weighbridge.

### [Unattended Vehicle Scales]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653516 .Subheading}

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** An unattended vehicle
scale shall be used only if it is part of an installation that has

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an automatic means to indicate to a vehicle operator
  that the indicating element of the scale has returned to zero and that
  the operator may drive onto the weighbridge;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a printer that, on demand, prints a ticket bearing
  the information required by section 66; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an automatic means of preventing weight indications
  and printing of a ticket unless

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the scale has returned to zero prior to weighing a
    vehicle, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the load being weighed is fully supported on the
    weighbridge.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/2008-81, s. 17
:::

### [Printed Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-653525 .Subheading}

**[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** Where a ticket printed by
a weighing device shows a total price for a transaction, it shall also
show the weight and unit price for the transaction.

**[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** The cash register tape of
a system comprised of one or more weighing and load-receiving elements
that interface with one or more electronic cash registers shall show

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the net weight of the commodity weighed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the price per unit of weight;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total price; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name or identification code of the product.

**[[67.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67.1 .sectionLabel}** The following
information shall be provided for each transaction by an on-board
weighing system for the weighing of waste or materials for recycling,
whether at curbside or at the customer's premises:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an identification of the customer;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date of the transaction;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the net weight of the waste or materials to be
  recycled;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the price per unit of weight if the total price is
  indicated;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the identification number of the vehicle on which the
  on-board weighing system is installed; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the identification number of the on-board weighing
  system used if more than one system is installed on the same vehicle.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/2008-81, s. 18
:::
