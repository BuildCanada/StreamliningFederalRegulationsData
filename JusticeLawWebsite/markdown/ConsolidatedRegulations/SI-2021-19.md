## Canada Emergency Response Benefit and Employment Insurance Emergency Response Benefit Remission Order {#canada-emergency-response-benefit-and-employment-insurance-emergency-response-benefit-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2021-19
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2021-05-12

Canada Emergency Response Benefit and Employment Insurance Emergency
Response Benefit Remission Order

P.C. 2021-363 2021-04-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Administrator of the Government of Canada in Council,
considering that the collection of certain overpayments and certain
payments is unreasonable and unjust, on the recommendation of the
Minister of Employment and Social Development and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4172_hq_27176){#fn_81000-2-4172_hq_27176-ID0EGCAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4172_hq_27177){#fn_81000-2-4172_hq_27177-ID0EDCAA},
makes the annexed [Canada Emergency Response Benefit and Employment
Insurance Emergency Response Benefit Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2021-19).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission --- Canada emergency response
benefit

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission of overpayments of income
  support that are repaid or to be repaid under subsection 12(1) of the
  [Canada Emergency Response Benefit Act](/eng/acts/C-3.7) is granted to
  any person who meets the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person would have been eligible to receive the
    income support payment if their self-employment gross income had
    been taken into account for the purpose of paragraph (b) of the
    definition [[worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of that
    Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person has filed with the Minister of National
    Revenue, not later than December 31, 2022, a return of income under
    the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) in respect of the 2019 and
    2020 taxation years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claim

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the person has repaid the overpayment, remission
  is granted only if they make a claim for remission in writing to the
  Minister of Employment and Social Development.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission --- employment insurance
emergency response benefit

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission of employment insurance
  emergency response benefit payments that are repaid or to be repaid
  under section 43 of the [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6),
  or that are returned or to be returned under section 44 of that Act,
  is granted to any person who meets the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person would have been eligible for the
    employment insurance emergency response benefit if their
    self-employment gross income had been insurable earnings for the
    purpose of subparagraphs 153.9(1)(a)(iii) and (c)(iii) of that Act;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person has filed with the Minister of National
    Revenue, not later than December 31, 2022, a return of income under
    the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) in respect of the 2019 and
    2020 taxation years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claim

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the person has repaid or returned the payment,
  remission is granted only if they make a claim for remission in
  writing to the Minister of Employment and Social Development.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is made.
