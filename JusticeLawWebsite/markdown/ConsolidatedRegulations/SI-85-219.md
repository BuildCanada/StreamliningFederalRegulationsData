## Merchandise for Photographic Layouts Remission Order {#merchandise-for-photographic-layouts-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/85-219
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1985-12-25

Order Respecting the Remission of the Tax Imposed Under Division III of
Part IX of the Excise Tax Act Customs Duties Imposed Under Section 21 of
the Customs Tariff on Merchandise Temporarily Imported By a Publisher
for the Purpose of Producing a Layout

P.C. 1985-3606 1985-12-12
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, is pleased hereby, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue and the Treasury
Board and pursuant to section 17[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the annexed
Order respecting the remission of the customs duties and a portion of
the sales tax paid or payable on merchandise temporarily imported for
topical illustrations.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-640038 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Merchandise for Photographic Layouts Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-85-219).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-640040 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[advertising material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[advertising material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means catalogues, price
    lists, trade notices, brochures, folders, posters and displays;
    ([matériel publicitaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[layout]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[layout]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means photographs of merchandise
    designed solely to provide topical illustration to an accompanying
    editorial text or article in a publication; ([mise en
    page]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[merchandise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[merchandise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods to be photographed
    for inclusion in a layout in a publication but does not include
    photographic equipment or film used in the production of such a
    layout; ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[publication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[publication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a periodical magazine that
    is published in Canada no fewer than four times a year.
    ([publication]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission of Customs Duties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-640046 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
section 21 of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on merchandise
temporarily imported by a publisher.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
- SI/91-31, s. 1
- SI/98-16, s. 2
:::

## [Remission of the Tax Imposed Under Division III of Part IX of the Excise Tax Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-640050 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5,
remission is hereby granted of the tax imposed under Division III of
Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on merchandise
temporarily imported by a publisher for the purpose of producing a
layout.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/91-8, s. 2
:::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-640054 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The remission granted under
sections 3 and 4 is subject to the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the merchandise is imported on or after January 1,
  1984;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the merchandise is exported or destroyed under the
  supervision of an officer of the Department of National Revenue,
  Customs and Excise within 60 days of the date the goods were accounted
  for under the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the importer provides an officer of the Department of
  National Revenue, Customs and Excise with a signed declaration
  certifying that the merchandise will not be used to produce
  advertising material or other printed matter promoting the
  availability of such merchandise in Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the importer maintains for a period of three years
  records sufficient to establish the use in Canada of the merchandise
  and layouts produced for inspection by an officer of the Department of
  National Revenue, Customs and Excise; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue, within three years of the date the goods were
  accounted for under the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) of the
  merchandise.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
- SI/91-31, s. 2(F)
