## [Investments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707725 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed activity

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 554(2)(f) of the Act and subject to
  subsections (2) and (3), a prescribed activity in relation to an
  entity is developing, designing, holding, managing, manufacturing,
  selling or otherwise dealing with any data transmission system,
  information site, communication device or information platform or
  portal that is used to provide information services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit on size of investment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A society may not acquire control of, or hold,
  acquire or increase a substantial investment in, an entity engaging in
  an activity described in subsection (1) if the sum of the following
  exceeds 5% of the society's regulatory capital:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate balance sheet value of the shares and
    ownership interests that the society and its subsidiaries, whether
    individually or jointly, would acquire in the entity under paragraph
    554(2)(f) of the Act,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate balance sheet value of the shares and
    ownership interests held by the society and its subsidiaries,
    whether individually or jointly, in entities engaging in an activity
    described in subsection (1) that the society holds control of, or a
    substantial investment in, under paragraph 554(2)(f) of the Act, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate value of outstanding loans made by
    the society and its subsidiaries, whether individually or jointly,
    to entities engaging in an activity described in subsection (1) that
    the society holds control of, or a substantial investment in, under
    paragraph 554(2)(f) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restricted activities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A society may not acquire control of, or hold,
  acquire or increase a substantial investment in, an entity engaging in
  an activity described in subsection (1) if the entity engages in the
  business of accepting deposit liabilities or if the activities of the
  entity include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} activities that a company is not permitted to
    engage in under any of sections 466 and 469 of the Act or that a
    property and casualty company is not permitted to engage in under
    section 478 of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any financial intermediary activity that exposes
    the entity to material market or credit risk, including the
    activities of a finance entity, a factoring entity and a financial
    leasing entity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the activities of a specialized financing entity;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} dealing in securities, except as may be permitted
    under paragraph 554(2)(e) of the Act or as may be permitted to a
    company under paragraph 440(2)(b) of the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} dealing in goods, wares or merchandise that a
    company is not permitted to deal in under subsection 441(3) of the
    Act, other than as permitted under subsection (1);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} acquiring control of or acquiring or holding a
    substantial investment in another entity unless

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an entity that is controlled by
      the society, the society itself would be permitted under Part XII
      of the Act to acquire a substantial investment in the other
      entity, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an entity that is not controlled
      by the society, the society itself would be permitted to acquire a
      substantial investment in the other entity under subsection
      552(2), paragraph 552(3)(b) or (c) or subsection 554(1) or (2) of
      the Act; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any activity prescribed under paragraph 554(3)(e)
    of the Act.

## [Exemption from Restrictions on Investments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707747 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from restrictions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraph 2(3)(f)(ii), subsections 554(4) and (5) of the Act do not
apply in determining whether a society would be permitted to acquire a
substantial investment in an entity under subsection 552(2), paragraph
552(3)(b) or (c) or subsection 554(1) or (2) of the Act.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707750 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
