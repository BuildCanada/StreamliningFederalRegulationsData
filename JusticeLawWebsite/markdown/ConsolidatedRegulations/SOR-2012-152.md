## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-783185 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Alberta Barley Commission.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-783187 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
wheat and barley grown in Manitoba, Saskatchewan and Alberta and the
Peace River District of British Columbia.

## [Responsible Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-783189 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commission is the
agency to which amounts are to be remitted under section 83.1 of the
[Canada Grain Act](/eng/acts/G-10).

## [Deductions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-783191 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person required to issue a cash purchase ticket under the Act must
  collect a deduction of the following amounts:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$0.48 per tonne for wheat; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$0.56 per tonne for barley except in the case of
    barley grown in Alberta for which the amount to be collected is
    \$0.04 per tonne.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amounts that are deducted must be remitted to the
  Commission within 30 days from the end of the month in which they are
  collected along with a report that indicates the amounts deducted in
  respect of wheat and barley from each producer.

## [Refunds of Amounts Deducted]{.HTitleText1} {#h-783197 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  producer may make an application for a refund from the Commission of
  any amount that was deducted under subsection 4(1).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be in the appropriate form
  supplied by the Commission, be signed and dated and contain the
  producer's name, mailing address and telephone number.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The application may be made once every crop year and
  must be submitted by July 31 of that crop year.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission must remit the refund not later than
  90 days after the end of the crop year.

## [Annual Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-783205 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Commission must provide
an annual report to the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food not later
than 120 days after the end of the crop year that contains the following
information in respect of wheat and of barley:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total amounts of deductions;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total amounts paid for research activities into
  new and improved varieties of wheat and of barley, the recipients of
  the funds and a description of those activities;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total amounts paid for the promotion of the
  marketing and use of wheat and of barley, the recipients of the funds
  and a description of those activities;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total amounts paid for technical assistance
  relating to the use of wheat and of barley, the recipients of the
  funds and a description of the technical assistance activities;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the total amounts of refunds; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} details of the administrative costs.
