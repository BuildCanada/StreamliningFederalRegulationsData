## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919899 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations, "Act"
means the [Canadian International Trade Tribunal Act](/eng/acts/C-18.3).
(*Loi*)

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
Act,

[[domestic production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[domestic production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the value or the
    volume of like or directly competitive goods produced in
    Canada; ([production nationale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[like or directly competitive goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[like or directly competitive goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods that are identical in all respects to the
      goods that are the subject of a complaint, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the absence of any identical goods referred to
      in paragraph (a), goods the uses and other characteristics of
      which closely resemble those goods that are the subject of a
      complaint; ([marchandises similaires ou directement
      concurrentes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[other interested party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[other interested party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, for the purposes of
    subsections 19.02(2), 25(2), 26(2), (3) and (4), 28(1) and (3),
    29(2) and (4), 30(4), 30.01(5) and (8), 30.011(6) and (9), 30.012(7)
    and (10), 30.06(3), 30.07(2) and (3), 30.09(2), 30.3(2) and 30.31(4)
    and paragraph 30.32(a) of the Act, means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a domestic producer,

    - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} a trade union that represents persons employed
      in the domestic industry producing like goods or directly
      competitive goods, or an association that includes such trade
      unions,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an exporter to Canada or importer into Canada of
      like or directly competitive goods,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an association of, or that includes, domestic
      producers, exporters or importers in Canada of like or directly
      competitive goods,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an association whose purpose includes to advocate
      for consumer interests in Canada,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the government of any country, mentioned in the
      report, complaint or extension request referred to in the relevant
      subsection, or in respect of which the inquiry referred to in the
      relevant subsection is held, as the case may be, and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other person who, because that person's
      rights or pecuniary interests may be affected or for any other
      reason, is entitled to be heard by the Tribunal. ([autres
      intéressés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Quorum]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919916 .Part}

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
reviewing and reporting on developments and providing advice pursuant to
section 19.02 of the Act, the Chairman may determine that one member
constitutes a quorum of the Tribunal, which member has and may exercise
all of the Tribunal's powers and has and may perform all of the
Tribunal's duties and functions.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-27, s. 1
:::

**[[3.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.2 .sectionLabel}** In order to provide for
the efficient management of the resources and caseload of the Tribunal,
the Chairman may, taking into account the complexity and precedential
nature of the matter at issue, determine that one member constitutes a
quorum of the Tribunal, which member has and may exercise all of the
Tribunal's powers and has and may perform all of the Tribunal's duties
and functions for the purposes of:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} conducting inquiries and reporting on requests for
  textile tariff relief in respect of the Reference made by the Minister
  to the Tribunal on July 6, 1994 pursuant to section 19 of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} hearing, determining, and dealing with any appeal
  made to the Tribunal pursuant to the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)
  or the [Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} hearing, determining and dealing with any appeal made
  to the Tribunal pursuant to section 81.19 of the [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15) in respect of an application for a rebate under
  section 120 of that Act; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} making an order under any of section 75.3 and
  subsections 75.4(8) and 75.6(7) of the [Special Import Measures
  Act](/eng/acts/S-15).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-27, s. 1
- SOR/96-229, s. 1
- SOR/2018-88, s. 16

## [Factors to Be Examined]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919927 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of commencing an inquiry under subsection 26(1), 30.07(1),
  30.22(3), 30.23(3) or 30.25(7) of the Act, the Tribunal shall, to
  determine whether paragraph 26(1)(b), 30.07(1)(b), 30.22(3)(b),
  30.23(3)(b) or 30.25(7)(b) of the Act, as the case may be, has been
  satisfied, examine whether a domestic producer is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in reality, a domestic producer who produces in
    Canada like or directly competitive goods;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} related to an exporter or importer of like or
    directly competitive goods; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} also an importer or exporter of like or directly
    competitive goods.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of commencing an inquiry under
  subsection 30.01(4), 30.011(5) or 30.012(6) of the Act, the Tribunal
  shall take into account the factors referred to in paragraphs (1)(a)
  to (c).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), an importer or
  exporter and a domestic producer are related to each other if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are individuals connected by blood
    relationship, marriage or adoption within the meaning of subsection
    251(6) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one is an officer or director of the other;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} each such person is an officer or director of the
    same two corporations, associations, partnerships or other
    organizations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} they are partners;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} one is the employer of the other;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} they directly or indirectly control or are
    controlled by the same person;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} one directly or indirectly controls or is
    controlled by the other;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any other person directly or indirectly owns, holds
    or controls five per cent or more of the outstanding voting stock or
    shares of each such person; or

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} one directly or indirectly owns, holds or controls
    five per cent or more of the outstanding voting stock or shares of
    the other.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of determining, during an inquiry into a matter referred to
  the Tribunal under paragraph 20(2)(a) of the Act or an inquiry into a
  complaint referred to in subsection 27(1) or 30.28(1) of the Act,
  whether the goods that are the subject of the reference or complaint
  are being imported as set out in that provision, the Tribunal shall
  examine, among other factors,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the actual volume of the goods imported into
    Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the effect of the imported goods on prices of like
    or directly competitive goods in Canada; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the impact of the imported goods on domestic
    producers of like or directly competitive goods in Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal shall consider, with respect to the
  factor mentioned in paragraph (1)(a), whether there has been a
  significant increase in the importation into Canada of the goods and,
  where there has been, the rate and amount of such increase, either
  absolutely or relative to the production in Canada of like or directly
  competitive goods.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal shall consider, with respect to the
  factor mentioned in paragraph (1)(b),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the prices of the imported goods have
    significantly undercut the prices of like or directly competitive
    goods produced and sold in Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the effect of the importation into Canada
    of the goods has been

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to depress significantly the prices of like or
      directly competitive goods produced and sold in Canada, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to limit to a significant degree increases in
      the prices of like or directly competitive goods produced and sold
      in Canada.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal shall evaluate, with respect to the
  factor mentioned in paragraph (1)(c), all relevant economic factors
  that have a bearing on domestic producers of like or directly
  competitive goods or on workers producing such goods, including the
  actual and potential changes in the level of production, employment,
  sales, market share, profits and losses, productivity, return on
  investments, utilization of production capacity, cash flow,
  inventories, the terms and conditions of employment, growth or ability
  to raise capital or investments.

**[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
determining, during an inquiry into a matter referred to the Tribunal
pursuant to paragraph 30.21(1)(a) of the Act or an inquiry into a
complaint referred to in subsection 30.22(6) of the Act, whether the
goods originating in the People's Republic of China that are the subject
of the reference or complaint are being imported in such increased
quantities or under such conditions as to cause or threaten to cause
market disruption to domestic producers of like or directly competitive
goods, the Tribunal shall examine, among other factors,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the actual volume of the goods originating in the
  People's Republic of China that are imported into Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the effect of the imported goods on prices of like or
  directly competitive goods in Canada; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the impact of the imported goods on domestic
  producers of like or directly competitive goods in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-347, s. 3
:::

**[[5.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
determining, during an inquiry into a matter referred to the Tribunal
pursuant to paragraph 30.21(1)(b) of the Act or an inquiry into a
complaint referred to in subsection 30.23(6) of the Act, whether an
action causes or threatens to cause a significant diversion of trade
into the domestic market in Canada, the Tribunal shall examine, among
other factors and to the extent that evidence is reasonably available to
support such examination,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the actual or imminent increase in market share of
  goods originating in the People's Republic of China in Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the nature or extent of the action;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the actual or imminent increase in the importation of
  goods originating in the People's Republic of China that is due to the
  action;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the conditions of supply and demand in the domestic
  market for the like or directly competitive goods; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the volume of the goods originating in the People's
  Republic of China that are imported into Canada and into any WTO
  Member taking an action.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-347, s. 3
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
determining, during an inquiry into an extension request referred to in
subsection 30.08(1) of the Act, whether an order continues to be
necessary to prevent or remedy serious injury, the Tribunal shall review
recent developments in the domestic and world markets relating to goods
that are like or directly competitive with the goods that are the
subject of the order and evaluate the likely effects of the termination
of the existing order on the basis of, among others, the factors set out
in section 5.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-12, s. 4
:::

**[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
determining, during an inquiry referred to in subsection 30.25(10) of
the Act, whether the order referred to in subsection 30.25(1) of the Act
continues to be necessary to prevent or remedy market disruption to
domestic producers of like or directly competitive goods, the Tribunal
shall review recent developments in the domestic market and in the
market of the People's Republic of China relating to goods that are like
or directly competitive with the goods that are the subject of the order
and evaluate the likely effects of the termination of the existing order
on the basis of, among others, the factors set out in section 5.1.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-347, s. 4
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
determining, during an inquiry into an extension request referred to in
subsection 30.08(1) of the Act, whether there is evidence that domestic
producers of like or directly competitive goods are adjusting, the
Tribunal shall take into account evidence of measures undertaken or
planned by domestic producers of like or directly competitive goods,
such as measures to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} increase productivity;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} improve the product, its marketing or its servicing;
  or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} rationalize production.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-12, s. 4
