or undertaking that is a corporation --- the New Brunswick Power Nuclear
Corporation --- that is an agent of Her Majesty in right of New
Brunswick whose activities are regulated pursuant to the [Nuclear Safety
and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3)[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-748054 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Point Lepreau facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the nuclear
facility of the New Brunswick Power Nuclear Corporation located at Point
Lepreau, New Brunswick.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The laws of New Brunswick
that are incorporated by these Regulations do not apply to employment on
or in connection with the Point Lepreau facility related to
construction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporating regulations made in the
future

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, the
incorporation by reference of an Act and its regulations includes all
regulations made under that Act after the coming into force of these
Regulations, unless otherwise provided.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-748061 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Parts I, II and III of the
[Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2) and the [Non-smokers' Health
Act](/eng/acts/N-23.6) apply to employment on or in connection with the
Point Lepreau facility.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The following Acts and
instruments, as amended from time to time, are incorporated by reference
and apply to the use of tobacco in the Point Lepreau facility and, to
the extent that they are relevant to industrial relations, occupational
health and safety or labour standards, to employment on or in connection
with the Point Lepreau facility:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sections 33 to 35 of the Judicature Act (R.S.N.B.
  1973, c. J-2) and its regulations;![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Public Interest Disclosure Act (S.N.B. 2007, c.
  P-23.005) and its regulations.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Industrial Relations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-748068 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 4,
employment on or in connection with the Point Lepreau facility is
excluded from the application of Part I of the [Canada Labour
Code](/eng/acts/L-2), except sections 121.1 to 121.5.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The following Acts and
instruments, as amended from time to time, are incorporated by reference
and apply, to the extent that they are relevant to industrial relations,
to employment on or in connection with the Point Lepreau facility:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Industrial Relations Act (R.S.N.B. 1973, c. I-4) and
  its regulations, other than the definition [[construction
  industry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 1(1) and sections 51.2
  and 51.21 of that Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Public Service Labour Relations Act (R.S.N.B.
  1973, c. P-25) and its regulations.![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptations --- Act

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  respect of the application of the Industrial Relations Act (R.S.N.B.
  1973, c. I-4),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} references to "construction industry" shall be read
    as a reference to the industry that is composed of the businesses
    that are engaged in the operation and maintenance of the Point
    Lepreau facility;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} references to "construction project" in sections
    51.1, 51.3, 51.5, 51.6, 51.8 and 51.9 shall be read as a reference
    to the operation and maintenance aspects of a refurbishment project;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} references to "construction work" in the
    definitions [[off-site work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[on-site
    work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 51.11 shall be read as a
    reference to "operation and maintenance work".

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptations --- Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The reference to "refurbishment" in section 7 of the
  Major Projects Regulation - Industrial Relations Act (N.B. Reg 90-51)
  shall be read as a reference to "operation and maintenance aspects of
  the refurbishment".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of collective agreement

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Any collective agreement
entered into under the Industrial Relations Act (R.S.N.B. 1973, c. I-4)
before the day on which these Regulations come into force that contains
provisions respecting terms and conditions of employment of workers who
are responsible for the construction aspects of the refurbishment of the
Point Lepreau facility applies to workers who are responsible for the
operation and maintenance aspects of the refurbishment at the facility.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collective agreement --- PSLRA

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The collective agreement
between New Brunswick Power Holding Corporation and the International
Brotherhood of Electrical Workers, Local 37 in effect from December 31,
2007 to December 31, 2010 is deemed to be a collective agreement entered
into under the Public Service Labour Relations Act (R.S.N.B. 1973, c.
P-25) and its regulations incorporated by paragraph 7(b).

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Occupational Health and Safety]{.HTitleText1} {#h-748087 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 4,
employment on or in connection with the Point Lepreau facility is
excluded from the application of Part II of the [Canada Labour
Code](/eng/acts/L-2), except sections 158 to 160.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The following Acts and
instruments, as amended from time to time, are incorporated by reference
and apply, to the extent that they are relevant to occupational health
and safety, to employment on or in connection with the Point Lepreau
facility:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Boiler and Pressure Vessel Act, (S.N.B. 1976, c.
  B-7.1) and its regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Elevators and Lifts Act (R.S.N.B. 1973, c. E-6) and
  its regulations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Electrical Installation and Inspection Act (S.N.B.
  1976, c. E-4.1) and its regulations;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Occupational Health and Safety Act (S.N.B. 1983, c.
  O-0.2) and its regulations, other than the definition "construction"
  in section 1 of that Act;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} Plumbing Installation and Inspection Act (S.N.B.
  1976, c. P-9.1) and its regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptation

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The reference to "where
construction is carried on" in the definition [[project
site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 1 of the Occupational Health and
Safety Act (S.N.B. 1983, c. O-0.2) shall be read as a reference to
"where the operation and maintenance aspects of the refurbishment
project at the Point Lepreau facility are carried on".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The provisions of the
[Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3) and its regulations
relating to occupational health and safety in relation to employment on
or in connection with the Point Lepreau facility prevail over any
provisions of these Regulations to the extent they are inconsistent with
the provisions of the [Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3)
and its regulations.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Labour Standards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-748101 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 4,
employment on or in connection with the Point Lepreau facility is
excluded from the application of Part III of the [Canada Labour
Code](/eng/acts/L-2), except sections 265 to 267.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The following Acts and
instruments, as amended from time to time, are incorporated by reference
and apply, to the extent that they are relevant to labour standards, to
employment on or in connection with the Point Lepreau facility:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Employment Standards Act, (S.N.B. 1982, c. E-7.2) and
  its regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Days of Rest Act (S.N.B. 1985, c. D-4.2) and its
  regulations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} New Brunswick Day Act (S.N.B. 1975, c. N-4.1);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Wage-Earners Protection Act (R.S.N.B. 1973, c.
  W-1).![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Use of Tobacco]{.HTitleText1} {#h-748110 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 4,
employment on or in connection with the Point Lepreau facility is
excluded from the application of the [Non-smokers' Health
Act](/eng/acts/N-23.6), except sections 8.1 and 8.2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Smoke-free Places Act
(S.N.B. 2004, c. S-9.5) and its regulations, other than subsection 2(3)
of that Act, as amended from time to time, are incorporated by reference
and apply to the use of tobacco in the Point Lepreau facility.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-748115 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
