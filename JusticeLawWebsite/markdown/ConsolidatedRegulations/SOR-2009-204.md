[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to a person who, by reason of the agreement dated
  December 1, 2001 between the International Development Research
  Centre, established under section 3 of the [International Development
  Research Centre Act](/eng/acts/I-19), and The Micronutrient
  Initiative, incorporated by Letters Patent issued July 4, 2001 under
  the [Canada Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8), ceased to be deemed to
  be employed, on December 1, 2001, in the public service for the
  purposes of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36)
  (the "Act") and became employed by The Micronutrient Initiative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- person re-employed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to a person who
  becomes re-employed by The Micronutrient Initiative.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When provisions are applicable

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Sections 13 and 13.01 of
the Act only apply to the person on or after the day on which they cease
to be employed by The Micronutrient Initiative.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefits for survivor and children

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The survivor and children
of the person who dies while employed by The Micronutrient Initiative
are entitled to one of the following benefits to which they would have
been entitled if the person had been employed in the public service:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the death benefit under subsection 12(8) of the Act;
  or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the allowances referred to in subsection 13(3) of the
  Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 26(2) of the Act

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 26(2) of the Act, a person is deemed to cease to be employed
in the public service on the day on which the person ceases to be
employed by The Micronutrient Initiative.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 10(5) of the Act

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 10(5) of the Act, the one-year period referred to in
paragraph (a) of that subsection begins on the day on which the person
ceases to be employed by The Micronutrient Initiative.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pensionable service

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 13 and 13.01 of the Act, pensionable service includes the
period of service that begins on the day on which the person ceases to
be employed in the public service and that ends on the day on which they
cease to be employed by The Micronutrient Initiative.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Section 13 of the Act

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
13 of the Act, the age of the person when they cease to be employed in
the public service is the age of that person on the day on which they
cease to be employed by The Micronutrient Initiative.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Group 1 contributor

**[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** A contributor to whom
these Regulations apply is considered to be a Group 1 contributor.
