## Certain Fees in Respect of the Issuance of Temporary Resident Visas for Certain Extended Family (Crisis in Gaza) Remission Order {#certain-fees-in-respect-of-the-issuance-of-temporary-resident-visas-for-certain-extended-family-crisis-in-gaza-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2024-12-18

Certain Fees in Respect of the Issuance of Temporary Resident Visas for
Certain Extended Family (Crisis in Gaza) Remission Order

P.C. 2024-1306 2024-12-09
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Treasury
Board and the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, makes the annexed
[Certain Fees in Respect of the Issuance of Temporary Resident Visas for
Certain Extended Family (Crisis in Gaza) Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2024-67) under subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted of the fees
  paid or payable under subsection 296(1) or 315.1(1) of the
  [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227) in respect of an
  application for a temporary resident visa referred to in
  subsection (2) to any person who meets the condition set out in that
  subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted on the condition that, during
  the period beginning on January 9, 2024 and ending on April 22, 2025,
  the person has submitted an application for a temporary resident visa
  in accordance with either

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Temporary public policy to facilitate temporary
    resident visas for certain extended family affected by the crisis in
    Gaza; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Updated temporary public policy to facilitate
    temporary resident visas for certain extended family affected by the
    crisis in Gaza.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission dates

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission of fees
referred to in subsection 1(1) is to be made on the following dates:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fees paid before the day on which this Order comes
  into force are to be remitted on that day; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} fees paid during the period beginning on the day that
  this Order comes into force and ending on April 22, 2025 are to be
  remitted on April 23, 2025.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
