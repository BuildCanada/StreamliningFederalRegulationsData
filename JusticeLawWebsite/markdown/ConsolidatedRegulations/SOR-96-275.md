## Related Party Transactions (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations {#related-party-transactions-cooperative-credit-associations-regulations .Title-of-Act}

with a Related Party of the Association

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-981513 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Related Party Transactions (Cooperative Credit
Associations) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-275).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-981515 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cooperative Credit Associations
Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01). (*Loi*)

## [Prescribed Related Party Transactions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-981517 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
424 of the Act, an association may, directly or indirectly, enter into
any of the following transactions with a related party of the
association:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the issue by or to the association of conversion
  privileges, options or rights to acquire securities the issuance of
  which is not otherwise provided for in the terms and conditions
  pursuant to which a security is being or has been issued;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the purchase, for the purpose of cancellation, of any
  shares or debt obligations issued by or to the association the
  purchase of which is not otherwise provided for in the terms and
  conditions pursuant to which the shares or debt obligations were
  issued;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the redemption of any redeemable shares or any debt
  obligations issued by or to the association the redemption of which is
  not otherwise provided for in the terms and conditions pursuant to
  which the shares or debt obligations were issued; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the payment or delivery of money or property by or to
  the association as a consequence of a reduction of the stated capital
  of the association or related party.
