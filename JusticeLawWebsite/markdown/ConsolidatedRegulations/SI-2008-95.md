## Order Respecting Ex Gratia Payments to Veterans and Science and Technology Workers Involved in Nuclear Weapons Testing or Nuclear Decontamination {#order-respecting-ex-gratia-payments-to-veterans-and-science-and-technology-workers-involved-in-nuclear-weapons-testing-or-nuclear-decontamination .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2008-95
:::

Registration 2008-09-17

Order Respecting Ex Gratia Payments to Veterans and Science and
Technology Workers Involved in Nuclear Weapons Testing or Nuclear
Decontamination

P.C. 2008-1587 2008-09-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Defence, hereby makes the annexed [Order
Respecting Ex Gratia Payments to Veterans and Science and Technology
Workers Involved in Nuclear Weapons Testing or Nuclear
Decontamination](/eng/regulations/SI-2008-95).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614106 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Defence. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[science and technology worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[science and technology worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    civilian employee of the Government of Canada attached to elements
    of the naval, army or air forces of Canada for the purposes of
    scientific or technical support to those forces. ([travailleur en
    science et technologie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a former member of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the naval, army or air forces of Canada; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Forces. ([ancien
      combattant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614113 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is hereby
authorized, on application under section 4, to make an [ex
gratia]{.otherLang lang="la"} payment of \$24,000 to a veteran or
science and technology worker who participated in allied forces nuclear
weapons testing in the United States, Australia or the South Pacific
during the period beginning on January 1, 1946 and ending on December
31, 1963 or who assisted in the decontamination of the nuclear reactor
in Chalk River, Ontario during the period beginning on January 1, 1952
and ending on December 31, 1953 or during the period beginning on
January 1, 1958 and ending on December 31, 1958, and who is alive on the
day on which the payment is to be made.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In the case of a veteran or
science and technology worker who has died, the Minister is authorized,
on application under section 4, to make the payment set out in section 2

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the veteran or science and technology worker died
  testate and the distribution of the estate or the liquidation of the
  succession of the veteran or science and technology worker has not
  been completed, to the executor of the estate or liquidator of the
  succession;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the veteran or science and technology worker died
  testate and the distribution of the estate or the liquidation of the
  succession of the veteran or science and technology worker has been
  completed, to the natural person who is entitled under the will to the
  residue of the estate or, if there are two or more such persons, to
  those persons on a [pro rata]{.otherLang lang="la"} basis of their
  share of the residue; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the veteran or science and technology worker died
  intestate, to the adult who was ordinarily living with the veteran or
  science and technology worker at the time of the veteran's or science
  and technology worker's death and was primarily responsible for
  providing care to the veteran or science and technology worker without
  remuneration or, if there are two or more such adults, to those adults
  on a [pro rata]{.otherLang lang="la"} basis.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614119 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for an [ex gratia]{.otherLang lang="la"} payment under
  section 2 or 3 shall be submitted to the Minister on or before
  December 31, 2009 by the veteran or science and technology worker, or
  by the person or persons referred to in section 3, shall be in the
  form approved by the Minister and shall be supported by any evidence
  that the Minister considers necessary.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an application is submitted after December 31,
  2009, the application may be accepted by the Minister if he or she is
  satisfied that the applicant was unable to submit the application
  before that date because of facts or circumstances beyond the control
  of the applicant.

## [Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614123 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  payment under section 2 or 3 consists of a one-time lump sum payment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No payment shall be made by the Minister under this
  Order after December 31, 2010, unless circumstances beyond the control
  of the applicant necessitate a longer period for payment.

## [No Crown Liability]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614127 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A payment made under this
Order does not constitute an admission of liability on the part of the
Crown.
