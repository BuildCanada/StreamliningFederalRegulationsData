## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696284 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Farm Products Agencies
    Act](/eng/acts/F-4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[CFC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CFC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2015-229, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[chicken]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chicken]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2015-229, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[federal market development quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal market development quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    number of kilograms of chicken, expressed in live weight, that,
    during the period referred to in the schedule, a producer is
    entitled under these Regulations to market in interprovincial or
    export trade to any holder of a market development licence issued
    under the [Canadian Chicken Licensing
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-22). ([contingent fédéral
    d'expansion du marché]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[federal quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the number of kilograms
    of chicken, expressed in live weight, not including a federal market
    development quota or federal specialty chicken quota, that a
    producer is entitled under these Regulations to market in
    interprovincial or export trade during the period referred to in the
    schedule. ([contingent fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[federal specialty chicken quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal specialty chicken quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    number of kilograms of chicken, expressed in live weight, that a
    producer is entitled under these Regulations to market in
    interprovincial or export trade, during the period referred to in
    the schedule, to any holder of a specialty chicken licence.
    ([contingent fédéral de poulet de spécialité]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2015-229, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[primary processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[primary processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a processor who
    slaughters chicken that was produced and marketed under a federal
    market development quota or a federal specialty chicken quota.
    ([transformateur primaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2015-229, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[Provincial Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Provincial Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2015-229, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[provincial market development quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial market development quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    the number of kilograms of chicken, expressed in live weight, that a
    producer is entitled, under orders, regulations, policy directives,
    permits or any other form for making quota allotments made or issued
    by the Commodity Board of the province in which the producer\'s
    registered production facilities are located, to market in
    intraprovincial trade during the period referred to in the schedule.
    ([contingent provincial d\'expansion du marché]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[provincial quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the number of
    kilograms of chicken, expressed in live weight, not including a
    provincial market development quota or provincial specialty chicken
    quota, that a producer is entitled, under any orders, regulations,
    policy directives, permits or other form for making quota allotments
    made or issued by the Commodity Board of the province in which the
    producer's registered production facilities are located, to market
    in intraprovincial trade during the period referred to in the
    schedule. ([contingent provincial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[provincial specialty chicken quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial specialty chicken quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    number of kilograms of chicken, expressed in live weight, that a
    producer is entitled, under any orders, regulations, policy
    directives, permits or other form for making quota allotments made
    or issued by the Commodity Board of the province in which the
    producer's registered production facilities are located, to market
    in intraprovincial trade, during the period referred to in the
    schedule, to any holder of a specialty chicken licence. ([contingent
    provincial de poulet de spécialité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registered production facilities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registered production facilities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    production facilities that are registered under the laws of the
    province in which they are situated. ([installations de production
    agréées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[specialty chicken licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[specialty chicken licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a specialty
    chicken licence that is issued by CFC under the [Canadian Chicken
    Licensing Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-22). ([permis de
    poulet de spécialité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696303 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), these Regulations apply to the marketing of chicken
  in interprovincial or export trade.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to the marketing of
  chicken under quota exemptions authorized by a Commodity Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-229, s. 2(E)

## [Marketing Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696309 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No producer may market
chicken in interprovincial or export trade

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} unless a federal quota, a federal market development
  quota or a federal specialty chicken quota has been allotted to the
  producer, on behalf of CFC, by the Commodity Board of the province in
  which the producer's registered production facilities are located;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in excess of the federal quota, federal market
  development quota or federal specialty chicken quota referred to in
  paragraph (a), taking into account any adjustment made under
  subsection 6(3) of the schedule to the [Chicken Farmers of Canada
  Proclamation](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-158);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} unless the chicken is marketed during the period
  during which chicken produced in the province in which the producer\'s
  registered production facilities are located is authorized to be
  marketed; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} contrary to any rules that the Commodity Board
  referred to in paragraph (a) has been authorized by CFC, under
  subsection 22(3) of the Act, to apply in performing, on behalf of CFC,
  the function of allotting and administering federal quotas, federal
  market development quotas and federal specialty chicken quotas.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-144, s. 2
- SOR/2015-229, s. 2(E)

## [Entitlement to a Federal Quota]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696317 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A producer is entitled to
be allotted a federal quota if the producer

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is allotted a provincial quota under the rules of the
  Commodity Board of the province in which the producer\'s registered
  production facilities are located;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is in compliance with the orders, regulations and
  rules of the Commodity Board in connection with that provincial quota;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is in compliance with the rules referred to in
  paragraph 3(d); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} is in compliance with the orders and regulations of
  CFC.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-229, s. 2(E)
:::

Version](section-4-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}

## [Relationship of Federal Quota to Provincial Quota]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696325 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The quantity of chicken
that a producer is authorized to market from a province under a federal
quota for the period referred to in the schedule is equal to the quota
allotment to the producer for that period by the Commodity Board of the
province minus the quantity of chicken marketed by the producer in
intraprovincial trade in the province during that period.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-229, s. 2(E)

## [Entitlement to a Federal Market Development Quota]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696329 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A producer is entitled to
be allotted a federal market development quota if the producer

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is allotted a provincial market development quota or
  provincial quota under the rules of the Commodity Board of the
  province in which the producer\'s registered production facilities are
  located;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} has elected to participate in a market development
  quota program;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is in compliance with the orders, regulations and
  rules of the Commodity Board in connection with that program;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} has the registered production facilities required to
  raise the chicken authorized to be marketed; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} is in compliance with the rules referred to in
  paragraph 3(d).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-229, s. 2(E)

## [Marketing Under a Federal Market Development Quota]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696338 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If a producer is allotted a
federal market development quota, the chicken produced under the quota
must be marketed by the producer in accordance with the [Canadian
Chicken Licensing Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-22) during the
period referred to in the schedule to a person who holds a valid market
development licence issued under those Regulations.

## [Relationship of Federal and Provincial Market Development Quota]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696340 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The number of kilograms of
chicken that a producer is authorized to market from a province under a
federal market development quota allocation by or on behalf of CFC for
the period referred to in the schedule is equal to the provincial market
development quota allotted to the producer for that period by the
Commodity Board of the province minus the number of kilograms of chicken
marketed by the producer under the conditions of the provincial market
development quota in intraprovincial trade in that province during the
period.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-229, s. 2(E)

## [Entitlement to a Federal Specialty Chicken Quota]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696344 .Part}

**[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** A producer is entitled
to be allotted a federal specialty chicken quota if the producer

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is allotted a federal quota, on behalf of CFC, by the
  Commodity Board of the province in which the producer's registered
  production facilities are located;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is allotted a provincial specialty chicken quota or
  provincial quota under the rules of the Commodity Board of the
  province in which the producer's registered production facilities are
  located;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} has elected to participate in a specialty chicken
  quota program;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} is in compliance with the orders, regulations and
  rules of the Commodity Board in connection with that program;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} has the registered production facilities that are
  required to raise the chicken authorized to be marketed;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} is in compliance with the rules referred to in
  paragraph 3(d); and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} has made arrangements with a primary processor to
  process the volumes of specialty chicken requested or has the
  processing facilities that are required to process the chicken
  authorized to be marketed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-144, s. 3
- SOR/2015-229, s. 2(E)

## [Marketing Under a Federal Specialty Chicken Quota]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696355 .Part}

**[[8.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.2 .sectionLabel}** If a producer is
allotted a federal specialty chicken quota, the chicken produced under
the quota must be marketed by the producer in accordance with the
[Canadian Chicken Licensing Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-22)
to a person who holds a valid specialty chicken licence.

specialty chicken quota allotted to the producer for that period by the
Commodity Board of the province minus the number of kilograms of chicken
marketed by the producer under the conditions of the provincial

## [Limits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696363 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board of a
province must allot federal quotas to producers in the province in such
manner that the aggregate of the following numbers of kilograms of
chicken that is produced in the province, and authorized to be marketed,
during the period referred to in the schedule will not exceed the
applicable number of kilograms of chicken set out in column 2 of the
schedule in respect of the province for that period:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of kilograms of chicken authorized to be
  marketed by producers in interprovincial or export trade under federal
  quotas allotted on behalf of CFC by the Commodity Board;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of kilograms of chicken authorized to be
  marketed by producers in intraprovincial trade under provincial quotas
  allotted by the Commodity Board; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number of kilograms of chicken anticipated to be
  marketed by producers under quota exemptions authorized by the
  Commodity Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-268, s. 2(F)
- SOR/2015-229, s. 2(E)
