## Withdrawal of Certain Lands (North and South Baffin, Kitikmeot East and West and Keewatin, N.W.T.) from Disposal Order {#withdrawal-of-certain-lands-north-and-south-baffin-kitikmeot-east-and-west-and-keewatin-n.w.t.-from-disposal-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/92-139
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 1992-08-12

Withdrawal of Certain Lands (North and South Baffin, Kitikmeot East and
West and Keewatin, N.W.T.) from Disposal Order

P.C. 1992-1598 1992-07-16
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, and for the
reason that the lands are required to facilitate the settlement of
native land claims, pursuant to paragraph 23(a) of the [Territorial
Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), is pleased hereby:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke Orders in Council P.C. 1992-824[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-92-139_e_hq_5154){#fn_SI-92-139_e_hq_5154-ID0EFDBAA};
  P.C. 1992-825[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-92-139_e_hq_5155){#fn_SI-92-139_e_hq_5155-ID0EDDBAA}
  and P.C. 1992-826[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*^](#fn_SI-92-139_e_hq_5156){#fn_SI-92-139_e_hq_5156-ID0EBDBAA}
  of April 30, 1992;

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-92-139_e_hq_5154-ID0EFDBAA){#fn_SI-92-139_e_hq_5154}SI/92-79,
    1992 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2010
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-92-139_e_hq_5155-ID0EDDBAA){#fn_SI-92-139_e_hq_5155}SI/92-80,
    1992 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2012
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*^](#fn_SI-92-139_e_hq_5156-ID0EBDBAA){#fn_SI-92-139_e_hq_5156}SI/92-81,
    1992 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2016
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to order the withdrawal from disposal of the lands
  described in schedules "I", "II", "III", "IV" and "V" hereto, for the
  period terminating on June 30, 1995, or on the date of coming into
  force of the legislation ratifying the land claims Agreement between
  the Inuit of the Nunavut Settlement Area and Her Majesty in right of
  Canada, whichever comes first, without prejudice to the following
  interests, including any renewals, replacements, extensions or
  transfers thereof:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} located or recorded mineral claims or prospecting
    permits in good standing under the Canada Mining Regulations,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the right to obtain a surface lease on located or
    recorded mineral claims pursuant to the [Territorial Lands
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1525),

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} rights in good standing created pursuant to
    section 8 of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7) or the
    [Territorial Lands Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1525),

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} permits, special renewal permits and leases in
    good standing under the [Canada Oil and Gas Land
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1518),

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} permits in good standing under the [Territorial
    Quarrying Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1527),

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} existing rights and interests granted pursuant to
    the [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5),

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} existing rights issued pursuant to the
    [Territorial Coal Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1522) and
    the [Territorial Dredging
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1523), and

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} interests in lands described in "Exhibit 2" to
    the Acquisition Agreement dated May 5, 1988, among Her Majesty in
    right of Canada, the Northern Canada Power Commission, the
    Government of the Northwest Territories and the Northwest
    Territories Power Corporation.
