## Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Department of Human Resources and Skills Development the Control and Supervision of the Policy Research Initiative {#order-transferring-from-the-privy-council-office-to-the-department-of-human-resources-and-skills-development-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-policy-research-initiative .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-74
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2006-05-31

Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Department of
Human Resources and Skills Development the Control and Supervision of
the Policy Research Initiative

P.C. 2006-361 2006-05-10
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA} of the [Public
Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34),
hereby transfers from the Privy Council Office to the Department of
Human Resources and Skills Development the control and supervision of
the portion of the federal public administration within the Privy
Council Office known as the Policy Research Initiative, effective June
1, 2006.
