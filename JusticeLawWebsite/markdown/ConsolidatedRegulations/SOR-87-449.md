## Stephenville Airport Zoning Regulations {#stephenville-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-449
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1987-07-30

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Stephenville Airport

P.C. 1987-1455 1987-07-30
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 4.5[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EHBBA} of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed Zoning Regulations
Respecting Stephenville Airport, substantially in the form set out in
the schedule hereto, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on October 4, 1986 and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations to the Minister of Transport with respect thereto.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EHBBA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1985, c. 28, s. 1
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to
section 4.4[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFAAA} of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting zoning at Stephenville
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-449).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-906015 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Stephenville Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-449).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-906017 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Stephenville Airport in
      the electoral district of Port au Port, in the Province of
      Newfoundland; ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part II of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, prepared for
      the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
      which strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the assigned
  elevation of the airport reference point is deemed to be 15 m above
  sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-906028 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, which lands are more particularly described as
follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lands within the outer limits of lands described
  in Part VI of the schedule; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lands directly under that portion of the approach
  surfaces that extend beyond the said outer limits.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-906032 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-906037 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in paragraphs 4(a) to (c), the Minister
may require that the owner or occupier of the land on which that object
is growing remove the growth or the excessive portion thereof.

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-906039 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit that land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste edible by or attractive
to birds.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point, shown on Public Works Canada Stephenville
Airport Zoning Plans S-1670-4 and S-1670-5 dated July 31, 1985, is
determined by measuring 1 585 m northeasterly along the projected centre
line of runway 10-28 from its intersection with the westerly end of the
strip; thence measuring northwesterly at right angles 150 m from the
said centre line of runway 10-28 to the northerly edge of the strip; the
said point having co-ordinate values of N 5 378 350.07 m and
E 301 010.98 m.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Public Works Canada Stephenville Airport
Zoning Plans S-1670-0, S-1670-1, S-1670-4, S-1670-5, S-1670-8 and
S-1670-9, dated July 31, 1985, are surfaces abutting each end of the
strip associated with the runway designated 10-28 and are described as
follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 10 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 28 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Public Works Canada Stephenville Airport
Zoning Plans S-1670-0, S-1670-1, S-1670-2, S-1670-3, S-1670-4, S-1670-5,
S-1670-6, S-1670-7 and S-1670-8, dated July 31, 1985, is an imaginary
surface located at a common plane established at a constant elevation of
45 m above the assigned elevation of the airport reference point, except
that, where that common plane is less than 9 m above the surface of the
ground, the imaginary surface is located at 9 m above the surface of the
ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strip .SchedHeadL1}

The strip associated with runway 10-28, as shown on Public Works Canada
Stephenville Airport Zoning Plans S-1670-0, S-1670-4 and S-1670-5 dated
July 31, 1985, is 300 m in width, 150 m on each side of the centre line
of the runway and 3 170 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Public Works Canada Stephenville
Airport Zoning Plans S-1670-0, S-1670-1, S-1670-4, S-1670-5 and S-1670-8
dated July 31, 1985, is a surface consisting of an inclined plane rising
at a ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 7 m measured horizontally at
right angles to the centre line and centre line produced of the strip
extending upward and outward from the lateral limits of the strip and
its approach surfaces to its intersection with the outer surface.

### [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Lands to Which These Regulations Apply]{.HTitleText1} {#part-videscription-of-the-lands-to-which-these-regulations-apply .SchedHeadL1}

The boundary of the outer limits of lands, shown on Public Works Canada
Stephenville Airport Zoning Plans S-1670-0, S-1670-1, S-1670-2,
S-1670-3, S-1670-4, S-1670-5, S-1670-6, S-1670-7 and S-1670-8 dated
July 31, 1985, is a circular area having a radius of 4 000 metres from
the airport reference point.
