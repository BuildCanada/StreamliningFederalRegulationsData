## Manitoba Vegetable Marketing (Interprovincial and Export) Order {#manitoba-vegetable-marketing-interprovincial-and-export-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-19
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1988-12-26

Order Respecting the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade of
Vegetables Produced in Manitoba
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Manitoba Vegetable Producers' Marketing Board, pursuant to section 3
of the [Manitoba Vegetable Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-544), made by
Order in Council P.C. 1985-1887 of June 13, 1985[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EHBBA} pursuant to section 2 of
the [Agricultural Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), hereby revokes
the Manitoba Root Crop Marketing (Interprovincial and Export)
Regulations, C.R.C., c. 159, and makes the annexed [Order respecting the
marketing in interprovincial and export trade of vegetables produced in
Manitoba](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-19), in substitution therefor.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EHBBA){#fn_1e}SOR/85-544, 1985 [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2757
  :::

Winnipeg, Manitoba, December 19, 1988
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-918610 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Manitoba Vegetable Marketing (Interprovincial and Export)
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-19).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-918612 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Natural Products Marketing Act
    of Manitoba; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Manitoba Vegetable
    Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[manufacturing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[manufacturing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} canning, dehydrating, freezing, frying or
      pickling vegetables,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} processing vegetables with sugar, sulphur dioxide
      or any other chemical or by heat, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} combining or mixing for sale any vegetables as
      defined in this Order with one or more vegetables other than
      vegetables as defined in this Order,

    but does not include any such activity carried on in a restaurant to
    prepare vegetables for immediate consumption by a consumer;
    ([fabrication]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[marketing tag]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marketing tag]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a tag issued by the
    Commodity Board in respect of a quantity of vegetables for the
    purpose of authorizing the sale or transportation of that quantity
    of the vegetables; ([étiquette de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the plan for the marketing of
    vegetables established pursuant to the Act; ([plan]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who produces
    vegetables in the Province and includes the employer of that person,
    any person who under any lease or agreement is entitled to a share
    of any vegetables produced by either of those persons, or any person
    who takes possession of any vegetables provided as security for a
    debt by any of those persons; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Province of Manitoba;
    ([province]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vegetables]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vegetables]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any variety, class, grade
    or size of potatoes, parsnips, onions, carrots or rutabagas produced
    in the Province. ([légumes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-918626 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies in
respect of vegetables marketed in interprovincial and export trade,
other than green onions.

## [Purchase and Sale]{.HTitleText1} {#h-918628 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall buy or
purchase vegetables except from or through the Commodity Board.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No producer shall sell
vegetables except to or through the Commodity Board.

## [Payment to Producers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-918631 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commodity Board may conduct pools for the distribution of all monies
  received from the sale of vegetables.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commodity Board shall, after deducting all
  necessary and proper disbursements and expenses and after making such
  allowances for reserves as may be approved by The Manitoba Natural
  Products Marketing Council, distribute the remainder of the monies
  received from the sale of such vegetables in such manner that each
  producer receives a share thereof relative to the quantity, quality,
  variety, grade, class or size of the vegetables delivered by the
  producer.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commodity Board may make an initial payment on
  delivery of the vegetables and subsequent payments until the remainder
  of the monies received from the sale is distributed.

## [Marketing Tags]{.HTitleText1} {#h-918636 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall sell or transport or have in his possession for sale or
  transportation any vegetables unless they are packed in a container
  and a marketing tag is attached to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the container, where the vegetables are packed in a
    single container; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outermost container, where the vegetables are
    packed in two or more containers.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a
  quantity of vegetables transported in bulk by a person pursuant to a
  direction of the Commodity Board.

## [Delivery Order]{.HTitleText1} {#h-918642 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), no producer shall transport vegetables or deliver
  vegetables for transport unless the Commodity Board has issued a
  delivery order to the producer for the vegetables.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a producer who
  delivers vegetables for transport to a person retained by the
  Commodity Board to transport vegetables.

## [Exempt Manufacturer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-918646 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 28(1) and (2) of the Plan, the J.R. Simplot Company is
designated as an exempt manufacturer in respect of potatoes sold to the
J.R. Simplot Company for use in manufacturing at its plant in Grand
Forks, North Dakota, U.S.A., pursuant to the terms of a written contract
of purchase and sale entered into by the J.R. Simplot Company and the
producer of the potatoes before the planting of the seeds used to
produce the potatoes, if those potatoes are used by the J.R. Simplot
Company in manufacturing at that plant.
