## Canadian Hatching Egg Producers Quota Regulations {#canadian-hatching-egg-producers-quota-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-209
:::

[FARM PRODUCTS AGENCIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-4)

Registration 1987-03-31

Regulations Respecting the Establishment of a Quota System in Respect of
Signatory Provinces for the Marketing of Broiler Hatching Eggs in
Interprovincial and Export Trade
::::

::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council has, by the Canadian Broiler Hatching
Egg Marketing Agency Proclamation[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Order_e_hq_7569){#fn_Order_e_hq_7569-ID0EECGA},
established the Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg Marketing Agency pursuant
to subsection 17(1) of the Farm Products Marketing Agencies
Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_Order_e_hq_7572){#fn_Order_e_hq_7572-ID0EBCGA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Order_e_hq_7569-ID0EECGA){#fn_Order_e_hq_7569}SOR/87-40,
  1987 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 317
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_Order_e_hq_7572-ID0EBCGA){#fn_Order_e_hq_7572}S.C.
  1970-71-72, c. 65
  :::

Whereas the Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg Marketing Agency has been
empowered to implement a marketing plan pursuant to the Canadian Broiler
Hatching Egg Marketing Agency Proclamation[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Order_e_hq_7569){#fn_Order_e_hq_7569-ID0EBAFA};

Whereas, pursuant to section 6 of the schedule to the Canadian Broiler
Hatching Egg Marketing Agency Proclamation[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Order_e_hq_7569){#fn_Order_e_hq_7569-ID0EBAEA},
the Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg Marketing Agency has applied the
allocation system set out in Schedule "B" annexed to the Federal
Provincial Agreement for Broiler Hatching Eggs;

Whereas the proposed Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg Marketing Agency
Quota Regulations annexed hereto are regulations of a class to which
paragraph 7(1)(d) of the said Act applies by reason of section 2 of the
[Agencies' Orders and Regulations Approval
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._648), C.R.C., c. 648, and have been
submitted to the National Farm Products Marketing Council pursuant to
paragraph 23(1)(f) of the said Act;

And Whereas the National Farm Products Marketing Council is satisfied
pursuant to paragraph 7(1)(d) of the said Act that the making of the
proposed Regulations is necessary for the implementation of the
marketing plan that the Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg Marketing Agency
is authorized to implement and has approved the proposed Regulations on
February 24, 1987.

Therefore, the Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg Marketing Agency, pursuant
to paragraph 23(1)(f) of the Farm Products Marketing Agencies
Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_Order_e_hq_7572){#fn_Order_e_hq_7572-ID0EGABA}
and subsection 5(1) of the schedule to the Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg
Marketing Agency Proclamation[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Order_e_hq_7569){#fn_Order_e_hq_7569-ID0EDABA},
hereby makes the annexed [Regulations respecting the establishment of a
quota system in respect of signatory provinces for the marketing of
broiler hatching eggs in interprovincial and export
trade](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-209), effective April 1, 1987.

Ottawa, March 31, 1987
:::

## [Application for Quota]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904685 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  producer in a signatory province who has been allotted a provincial
  quota by the Commodity Board of that province and who intends to
  market in interprovincial trade broiler hatching eggs produced in that
  province shall apply to the Commodity Board for an interprovincial
  quota.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A producer in a signatory province who has been
  allotted a provincial quota by the Commodity Board of that province
  and who intends to market in export trade broiler hatching eggs
  produced in that province shall apply to the Commodity Board for an
  export quota.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904689 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  producer in a signatory province shall market in interprovincial trade
  broiler hatching eggs produced in the province

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} unless an interprovincial quota has been allotted
    to the producer, on behalf of the Agency, by the Commodity Board of
    that province;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in excess of the interprovincial quota referred to
    in paragraph (a); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} contrary to any rule of the Commodity Board of the
    province that the Commodity Board has been authorized by the Agency,
    pursuant to subsection 22(3) of the Act, to apply in performing on
    behalf of the Agency the function of allotting and administering
    interprovincial quotas.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No producer in a signatory province shall market in
  export trade broiler hatching eggs produced in the province

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} unless an export quota has been allotted to the
    producer, on behalf of the Agency, by the Commodity Board of that
    province;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in excess of the export quota referred to in
    paragraph (a); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} contrary to any rule of the Commodity Board of the
    province that the Commodity Board has been authorized by the Agency,
    pursuant to subsection 22(3) of the Act, to apply in performing on
    behalf of the Agency the function of allotting and administering
    export quotas.

## [Determination of Quota]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904701 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  number of broiler hatching eggs that a producer in a signatory
  province is entitled to market in interprovincial trade during the
  period set out in the schedule shall be equal to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total number of broiler hatching eggs that the
    producer is authorized by the Commodity Board of the province to
    market under that producer's interprovincial quota and provincial
    quota

  minus

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of broiler hatching eggs marketed by the
    producer in intraprovincial trade during that period.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The number of broiler hatching eggs that a producer
  in a signatory province is entitled to market in export trade during
  the period set out in the schedule shall be equal to the number of
  broiler hatching eggs that the producer is authorized by the Commodity
  Board of the province to market under that producer's export quota.

## [Limits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904708 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Interprovincial quotas shall be
  allotted by the Commodity Board of each signatory province to
  producers in that province in such a manner that the total number of
  broiler hatching eggs produced in the province and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} authorized to be marketed under interprovincial
    quotas allotted on behalf of the Agency by the Commodity Board of
    the province,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} authorized to be marketed under provincial quotas
    allotted by the Commodity Board of the province, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} expected to be marketed during the period set out
    in the schedule under quota exemptions granted in the province

  will not exceed the number of broiler hatching eggs set out in Column
  I of the schedule in respect of that province.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Export quotas shall be allotted by the Commodity
  Board of each signatory province to producers in that province in such
  a manner that the total number of broiler hatching eggs produced in
  the province and authorized to be marketed under export quotas will
  not exceed the number of broiler hatching eggs set out in Column II of
  the schedule in respect of that province.
