## [Prescribed Supervisory Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678991 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 637 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the
  following information or any component of that information is
  prescribed supervisory information in relation to a bank:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any rating assigned by the Superintendent to the
    bank to assess its financial condition and any other such rating
    that is substantially based on information obtained from the
    Superintendent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any stage of intervention assigned to the bank
    under the Guide to Intervention for Federal Financial Institutions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any order made in respect of the bank under
    subsection 485(3) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), any
    prudential agreement entered into by the bank under section 644.1 of
    that Act or any direction issued to it under section 645 of that
    Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any report prepared by or at the request of the
    Superintendent or any recommendation made by the Superintendent as a
    result of an annual or special examination or other supervisory
    review of the bank, including any related correspondence to or from
    the directors or officers of the bank; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2011-196, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any category in which the bank is classified under
    the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Differential Premiums
    By-law and any premium rate assigned to it and any annual premium
    determined for it under that By-law.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 637 of the [Bank
  Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the information prescribed by the following
  regulations is prescribed supervisory information in relation to an
  affiliate of a bank:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Authorized Foreign
    Banks) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-58), if the affiliate
    is an authorized foreign bank to which the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) applies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Cooperative Credit
    Associations) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-57), if the
    affiliate is an association to which the [Cooperative Credit
    Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01) applies;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Insurance Companies)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-56), if the affiliate is a
    company, society, foreign company or provincial company to which the
    [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Trust and Loan
    Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-55), if the
    affiliate is a company to which the [Trust and Loan Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) applies;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Bank Holding
    Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-480), if the
    affiliate is a bank holding company to which the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) applies; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Insurance Holding
    Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-484), if the
    affiliate is an insurance holding company to which the [Insurance
    Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies.

## [Prohibited Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679009 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 and
4, a bank shall not, directly or indirectly, disclose information
referred to in section 1.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-196, s. 5
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 2"){.PITLink}
:::

## [Limited Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679013 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A bank may disclose
information referred to in section 1 to its affiliates or to its
directors, officers, employees, auditors, securities underwriters or
legal advisors, or to those of its affiliates, if the bank ensures that
the information remains confidential.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-196, s. 5
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A bank or any of its
affiliates may disclose information referred to in paragraph 1(1)(c) if
the bank or affiliate considers the information to contain a material
fact or material change that is required by the securities laws of the
relevant jurisdiction to be disclosed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-196, s. 6(E)
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679020 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
