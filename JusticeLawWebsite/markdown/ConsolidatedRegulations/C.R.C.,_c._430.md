## Designated Areas Firearms Order {#designated-areas-firearms-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 430
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Order Designating Areas in Which Permits to Possess a Firearm or
Ammunition May Be Issued to Persons under the Age of Fourteen Years
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-556667 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Designated Areas Firearms Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._430).

## [Areas Designated]{.HTitleText1} {#h-556669 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Each area set out in the
schedule is designated as an area within which a permit to possess a
firearm or ammunition may be issued by a local registrar of firearms to
a person under the age of 14 years who resides within that area.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-556672 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- [1]{.lawlabel} *Ontario*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} *District of Cochrane* --- excluding the organized
    municipalities of:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Towns: Cochrane, Hearst, Kapuskasing, Smooth Rock
      Falls and Timmins;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Townships: Black River (Matheson), Calvert,
      Fauquier, Glackmeyer, Kendrey, Mount Joy, Playfair, Shackleton and
      Machin, Tisdale and Whitney.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} *District of Thunder Bay* --- that portion lying
    north of No. 11 Highway, bordered on the east by the
    Cochrane-Thunder Bay District Boundary and on the west by the
    Kenora-Thunder Bay boundary.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} *District of Kenora and Patricia* --- those
    portions lying north of the main Canadian National Railway line.

- [2]{.lawlabel} *Manitoba*

  The area comprising the whole of the Province of Manitoba.

- [3]{.lawlabel} *Alberta*

  That area commencing from the point on the Alberta-Saskatchewan
  boundary intersected by the north boundary of Township 68, thence west
  along the north boundary of Township 68 to its intersection with the
  5th meridian, thence north along the 5th meridian to its intersection
  with the north boundary of Township 92, then west along the north
  boundary of Township 92 to its intersection with the Alberta-British
  Columbia boundary, thence north along the British Columbia-Alberta
  boundary to the boundary of the Northwest Territories, thence east
  along this boundary to the point where it meets the boundary between
  Alberta and Saskatchewan and thence southerly along the
  Saskatchewan-Alberta boundary to the point of commencement.

- [4]{.lawlabel} *Yukon Territory*

  The area comprising the whole of the Yukon Territory.

- [5]{.lawlabel} *Northwest Territories*

  The area comprising the whole of the Northwest Territories.

- [6]{.lawlabel} *Quebec*

  All that part of the Province of Quebec situated north of the
  50th degree of latitude, between the Quebec-Ontario border eastwards
  to the 68th degree of longitude, thence along a line extending due
  north to the 51st degree of latitude, thence eastwards along the
  51st degree of latitude to the junction with the 60th degree of
  longitude, thence along the coast to the Quebec-Newfoundland border,
  following an imaginary line situated about 10 miles from the coast.

- [7]{.lawlabel} *Saskatchewan*

  All that area of the Province of Saskatchewan situated north of the
  54th parallel of north latitude.
