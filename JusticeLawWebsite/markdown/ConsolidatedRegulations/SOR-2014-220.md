## CHFTA Sugar Aggregate Quantity Limit Order {#chfta-sugar-aggregate-quantity-limit-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2014-220
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 2014-09-26

CHFTA Sugar Aggregate Quantity Limit Order

P.C. 2014-982 2014-09-25
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 49.6(9)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EGCAA} of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EDCAA}, makes the annexed [CHFTA
Sugar Aggregate Quantity Limit Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-220).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EGCAA){#fn_a}S.C.
  2014, c. 14, s. 43
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b-ID0EDCAA){#fn_b}S.C.
  1997, c. 36
  :::
:::

## [Tariff Rate Quota]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806442 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The aggregate quantity
limit of goods of tariff item Nos. 1701.91.10, 1701.99.10, 1702.90.21,
1702.90.61, 1702.90.70 and 1702.90.81 that are entitled to the Honduras
Tariff is, for the period set out in column 1 of the schedule, the
applicable aggregate quantity limit set out in column 2.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806444 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Ind4235_hq_14615){#fn_Ind4235_hq_14615-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force on the day on which section
43 of the [Canada--Honduras Economic Growth and Prosperity
Act](/eng/acts/C-6.10), chapter 14 of the Statues of Canada, 2014, comes
into force, but if these Regulations are registered after that day, they
come into force on the day on which they are registered.
