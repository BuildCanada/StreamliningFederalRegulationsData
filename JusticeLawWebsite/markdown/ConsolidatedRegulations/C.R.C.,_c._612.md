## Export of Logs Permit {#export-of-logs-permit .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 612
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

General Export Permit No. Ex. 5
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559024 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Permit may be cited as
the [Export of Logs Permit](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._612).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559026 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any person may, under the
authority of this General Export Permit, export from Canada to any
country, logs of all kinds of wood upon presentation to the collector of
customs at the Canadian port of exit of a certificate satisfactory to
the collector that the export consists of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} peeled poles, peeled piling or peeled crib timber, 11
  inches or under top diameter;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} posts or pitprops; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} boomsticks or swifters used as bindings on floating
  booms.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/80-97, Sch. I, s. 1
- SI/89-113, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** If any goods exported under
the authority of this Permit are required to be reported in the
prescribed form under the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), that form
must contain the statement "Exported under the authority of General
Export Permit No. Ex. 5" or "[Exporté en vertu de la Licence générale
d'exportation n^o^ Ex. 5]{.otherLang lang="fr"}".
