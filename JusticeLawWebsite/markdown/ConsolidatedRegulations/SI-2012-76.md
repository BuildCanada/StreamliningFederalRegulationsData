## Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Hecla and Griper Bay, N.W.T.) {#reservation-to-the-crown-waiver-order-hecla-and-griper-bay-n.w.t. .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2012-76
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 2012-10-20

Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Hecla and Griper Bay, N.W.T.)

P.C. 2012-1110 2012-09-20
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
section 13[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-1328-E_hq_12323){#fn_81000-2-1328-E_hq_12323-ID0EECAA}
of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-1328-E_hq_12324){#fn_81000-2-1328-E_hq_12324-ID0EBCAA},
orders that the lands described in the annexed schedule lying within
30.48 m (100 feet in width) of the ordinary high water mark along the
shoreline of Hecla and Griper Bay and any unnamed stream, creek or
waterbody within or adjacent to that land, in the Northwest Territories,
be included in the grant of those lands.
