## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1401735 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Tobacco and Vaping Products
    Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[assortment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assortment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a package that contains

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} two or more of the types of vaping products
      referred to in subsection 2(1); or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} two or more brands of vaping products of the same
      type, but with different characteristics, such as different
      flavours or nicotine
      concentrations. ([assortiment]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[civic address]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[civic address]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for an address in Canada, the unit number, civic
      number, street name, municipality name, province name and postal
      code; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for an address outside Canada, the unit number,
      civic number, street name, municipality name, province or state
      name, postal or ZIP code and country name. ([adresse
      municipale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[GTIN]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[GTIN]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Global Trade Item Number
    assigned by GS1 to a brand of vaping product or brand of
    assortment. ([GTIN]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include an individual
    or entity that only packages, labels or distributes vaping products
    on behalf of a manufacturer. ([fabricant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[net sales]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[net sales]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the value of a
    manufacturer's sales of a vaping product or assortment during a
    specified period minus the value of any returns of that vaping
    product or assortment to the manufacturer during the same
    period. ([ventes nettes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[net volume]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[net volume]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the volume of a vaping
    substance sold by a manufacturer during a specified period minus the
    volume of any returns of that vaping substance to the manufacturer
    during the same period. ([volume net]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unique product identifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unique product identifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a
    brand of vaping product or brand of assortment, means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the GTIN; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the alphanumeric code referred to in subsection
      3(3). ([identifiant de produit unique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[vaping device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vaping device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
    paragraphs (a) and (b) of the definition [[vaping
    product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the
    Act. ([dispositif de vapotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vaping part]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vaping part]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
    paragraph (c) of the definition [[vaping
    product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the Act. ([pièce de
    vapotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vaping substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vaping substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
    paragraph (d) of the definition [[vaping
    product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the Act. ([substance
    de vapotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1401755 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retail sale

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to the following types of vaping products that are
  intended for retail sale:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a vaping part that does not contain a vaping
    substance and that is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a vaping part that contains, or that is intended
      to contain, a source of energy,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a vaping part --- including a tank, cartridge or
      pod --- that is a reservoir for a vaping substance and that
      contains an aerosol-generating component, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a vaping part that contains a microprocessor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vaping device that does not contain a vaping
    substance;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a vaping part that contains a vaping substance;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a vaping device that contains a vaping substance;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a vaping substance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assortments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations also apply to assortments that are
  intended for retail sale.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to a vaping product or
  assortment that is the subject of an authorization, including a
  licence, issued under the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27)
  authorizing its sale or to an assortment that includes a vaping
  product that has been issued such an authorization.

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1401771 .Part}

### [General Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1401772 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations prescribe the information that manufacturers of vaping
  products must submit to the Minister, as well as the form and manner
  in which the information is to be submitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of reports

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the information required for each type
  of report set out in these Regulations, every report must contain the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the manufacturer on whose behalf the
    report is submitted, as well as the telephone number, email address
    and civic address of the manufacturer's principal place of business
    in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, telephone number and email address of the
    individual who prepared the report and, if that individual works for
    an entity other than the manufacturer, the name of the entity, as
    well as the telephone number, email address and civic address of the
    entity's principal place of business;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date of the report and an attestation by the
    individual who prepared it that the information in the report is
    true and complete to the best of their knowledge and belief and is
    provided in good faith; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a report on sales, the period
    covered by the report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unique product identifier

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For each brand of vaping product or brand of
  assortment that does not have a GTIN, the manufacturer must create an
  alphanumeric code of no more than 10 characters that will serve as the
  unique product identifier for that brand.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Language

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The information in the reports must be in English or
  French.

### [Report on Sales]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1401786 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Brands of vaping products

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  report on sales must contain the following information for each brand
  of vaping product, other than a brand of vaping product contained in
  an assortment, that a manufacturer sells during the period covered by
  the report:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the unique product identifier;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the type of vaping product;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the brand name or the brand family name;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vaping product that is contained
    in a package, the number of units of the vaping product in the
    package;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any additional brand element or any descriptive
    term that distinguishes the vaping product from other vaping
    products in the report; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a type of vaping product referred to
    in any of paragraphs 2(1)(c) to (e),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name given to the vaping substance,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} one of the following:

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} an indication of the flavour set out in the
        schedule that corresponds to the dominant flavour of the vaping
        substance,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the word "Other", if the dominant flavour of
        the vaping substance does not correspond to any of the flavours
        set out in the schedule, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the word "Unflavoured", if applicable,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of nicotine, if any,
      expressed in milligrams per millilitre, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the volume in millilitres of the vaping
      substance per unit of the vaping product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sales --- vaping products

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report on sales must contain the following
  information with respect to sales made by the manufacturer and returns
  made to the manufacturer during the period covered by the report, for
  each brand of vaping product:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vaping product that is contained
    in a package that is not an assortment,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of packages sold, or the number "0" if
      none were sold, in each province, throughout Canada and for
      export, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for each of the categories referred to in
      subparagraph (i), the number of packages that were returned to the
      manufacturer, or the number "0" if none were returned;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vaping product that is not
    contained in a package,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of units sold, or the number "0" if
      none were sold, in each province, throughout Canada and for
      export, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for each of the categories referred to in
      subparagraph (i), the number of units that were returned to the
      manufacturer, or the number "0" if none were returned;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vaping product that is sold in
    Canada,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian dollar value, excluding any taxes or
      duties, of the net sales of the vaping product in each province
      and throughout Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the total Canadian dollar value of any vaping
      duty and additional vaping duty imposed under the [Excise Act,
      2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1) on the vaping product that was sold minus
      the total Canadian dollar value of any vaping duty and additional
      vaping duty imposed on any returns of the vaping product in each
      province and throughout Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vaping product that is sold for
    export, the name of each country of destination and the Canadian
    dollar value of the net sales of the vaping product to that country;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a type of vaping product referred to
    in any of paragraphs 2(1)(c) to (e), the net volume in litres of the
    vaping substance sold in each province, throughout Canada and for
    export.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Brands of assortments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The report on sales must contain the following
  information with respect to sales of assortments made by the
  manufacturer during the period covered by the report:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each brand of assortment sold,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the unique product identifier,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the brand name or the brand family name, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any additional brand element or any descriptive
      term that distinguishes the assortment from other assortments in
      the report; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the contents of the assortment,
    including the following information for each brand of vaping product
    contained in the assortment:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the brand name, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a type of vaping product referred
      to in any of paragraphs 2(1)(c) to (e),

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the name given to the vaping substance, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the total volume in millilitres of the vaping
        substance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sales --- assortments

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The report on sales must contain the following
  information with respect to sales made by the manufacturer and returns
  made to the manufacturer during the period covered by the report, for
  each brand of assortment:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of sales made by the manufacturer, the
    number of assortments sold, or the number "0" if none were sold, in
    each province, throughout Canada and for export;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of returns to the manufacturer for each
    of the categories referred to in paragraph (a), the number of
    assortments that were returned, or the number "0" if none were
    returned;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an assortment that is sold in
    Canada,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian dollar value, excluding any taxes or
      duties, of the net sales of the assortment in each province and
      throughout Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the total Canadian dollar value of any vaping
      duty and additional vaping duty imposed under the [Excise Act,
      2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1) on the assortment that was sold minus the
      total Canadian dollar value of any vaping duty and additional
      vaping duty imposed on any returns of the assortment in each
      province and throughout Canada; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an assortment that is sold for
    export, the name of each country of destination and the Canadian
    dollar value of the net sales of the assortment to that country.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit --- initial report

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The initial report on sales must be submitted within
  the time limit set out in subsection (6) for the first complete period
  after the day on which these Regulations come into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit --- report on sales

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The report on sales must be submitted

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the period beginning on January 1 and ending on
    June 30 of a year, on or before July 31 of that year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the period beginning on July 1 and ending on
    December 31 of a year, on or before January 31 of the following
    year.

### [Report on Ingredients]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1401841 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Brand information

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  report on ingredients must contain the following information for each
  brand of vaping product that a manufacturer sells in Canada, including
  a brand of vaping product contained in an assortment, if the vaping
  product is of a type referred to in any of paragraphs 2(1)(c) to (e):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the unique product identifier;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the brand name or the brand family name;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any additional brand element or any descriptive
    term that distinguishes the vaping product from other vaping
    products in the report;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name given to the vaping substance,

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} one of the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an indication of the flavour set out in the
      schedule that corresponds to the dominant flavour of the vaping
      substance,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the word "Other", if the dominant flavour of the
      vaping substance does not correspond to any of the flavours set
      out in the schedule, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the word "Unflavoured", if applicable; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of nicotine, if any, expressed in
    milligrams per millilitre.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ingredient information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report on ingredients must contain the following
  information for each ingredient that is used in the manufacture of the
  vaping substance:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its common name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its chemical name, if applicable;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its registry number assigned by the Chemical
    Abstracts Service of the American Chemical Society, if applicable;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and civic address of its supplier;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} its concentration in the vaping substance,
    expressed in milligrams per millilitre or millilitres per
    millilitre, as the case may be; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the ingredient is nicotine, an indication of
    whether the nicotine is natural or synthetic or a mixture of both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}More than one substance in ingredient

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If more than one substance was used in the
  manufacture of any ingredient

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the report on ingredients must contain the
    information referred to in subsection (2) for each of those
    substances and for any substance used in the manufacture of those
    substances;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information referred to in paragraph (a) may be
    submitted by the ingredient supplier on behalf of the manufacturer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- export

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The report on ingredients is not required for a brand
  of vaping product if the vaping product, or an assortment that
  contains it, is manufactured solely for export.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identical vaping substance

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a vaping substance that is identical
  to that contained in another brand of vaping product for which a
  report on ingredients is submitted, the manufacturer may submit a
  report on ingredients that does not include the information referred
  to in subsections (2) and (3) if it includes the information referred
  to in subsection (1) for both brands of the vaping product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[identical]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (5),
  [[identical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a vaping substance,
  means the vaping substance contains the same ingredients in the same
  concentrations as a vaping substance contained in another brand of
  vaping product, including a brand of vaping product contained in an
  assortment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit --- report on ingredients

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The report on ingredients must be submitted on or
  before the day on which the manufacturer first sells a brand of vaping
  product, including a brand of vaping product contained in an
  assortment, that was not included in a previous report on ingredients.

### [Electronic Submission]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1401874 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reporting by manufacturer

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  manufacturer must submit the report on sales and the report on
  ingredients using the following forms established by the Minister and
  attaching the forms to an email:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the report on sales, the form entitled Sales
    Report for Vaping Products, as amended from time to time; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the report on ingredients, the form entitled
    Ingredients Report for Vaping Products, as amended from time to
    time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information submitted by supplier

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An ingredient supplier who submits information on the
  manufacturer's behalf under paragraph 5(3)(b) may do so using the form
  established by the Minister entitled Supplemental Ingredients Report
  for Vaping Products (Suppliers) -- Ingredients with More than One
  Substance, as amended from time to time, and attaching the form to an
  email.

### [Change to Ingredient Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1401882 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of change

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If there are changes to the
information referred to in subsection 5(2) after the report on
ingredients is submitted, the manufacturer must submit a notification of
change that meets the following requirements:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it includes, for each brand of vaping product to
  which the change pertains,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the unique product identifier, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the brand name or the brand family name;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it includes the name of the manufacturer, as well as
  the telephone number, email address and civic address of the
  manufacturer's principal place of business in Canada;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} it includes the name, telephone number and email
  address of the individual who prepared the notification and, if that
  individual works for an entity other than the manufacturer, the name
  of the entity, as well as the telephone number, email address and
  civic address of the entity's principal place of business;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} it includes the date of the notification and an
  attestation by the individual who prepared it that the information in
  the notification is true and complete to the best of their knowledge
  and belief and is provided in good faith;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the information in the notification is in English or
  French;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} it is submitted using the form established by the
  Minister entitled Notification of Change --- Vaping Product
  Ingredients, as amended from time to time, and attaching the form to
  an email; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} it is submitted on or before the day on which the
  manufacturer first sells a vaping product that was manufactured in
  accordance with the change.

## [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1401894 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report --- December 31, 2023

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A manufacturer must submit,
on or before December 31, 2023, a report on ingredients that contains
the information referred to in subsections 5(1) to (3) with respect to
each brand of vaping product, including a brand of vaping product
contained in an assortment, that the manufacturer sells during the
period beginning on the day on which these Regulations come into force
and ending on December 31, 2023.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1401897 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
