## Public Service Income Benefit for Survivors of Employees Slain on Duty Remission Order {#public-service-income-benefit-for-survivors-of-employees-slain-on-duty-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2023-6
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2023-03-29

Public Service Income Benefit for Survivors of Employees Slain on Duty
Remission Order

P.C. 2023-216 2023-03-10
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that the
collection of certain overpayments is unreasonable and unjust, on the
recommendation of the Treasury Board, makes the annexed [Public Service
Income Benefit for Survivors of Employees Slain on Duty Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2023-6) under subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4815_hq_30861){#fn_81000-2-4815_hq_30861-ID0EECAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission of overpayments
that were paid under the Public Service Income Benefit Plan for
Survivors of Employees Slain on Duty is granted to any beneficiaries who
received those overpayments before May 31, 2023 as a result of
administrative errors.
