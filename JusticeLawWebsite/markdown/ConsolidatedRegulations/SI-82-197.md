## Assigning to the Minister of the Environment, the Administration, Management and Control of Certain Public Lands {#assigning-to-the-minister-of-the-environment-the-administration-management-and-control-of-certain-public-lands .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/82-197
:::

[CANADA WILDLIFE ACT](/eng/acts/W-9)

Registration 1982-10-13

Assigning to the Minister of the Environment, the Administration,
Management and Control of Certain Public Lands

P.C. 1982-2855 1982-09-22
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor General in Council is satisfied that the public
lands described in the schedule hereto are required for wildlife
research, conservation or interpretation.

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to
subsection 4(1) of the [Canada Wildlife Act](/eng/acts/W-9), is pleased
hereby to assign to the Minister of the Environment, the administration,
management and control of the public lands described in the schedule
hereto.
:::
