## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832926 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[accessible part]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[accessible part]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any part of a product
    that may be touched, licked, mouthed or swallowed during the
    reasonably foreseeable use of the product. ([partie
    accessible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a container in which a
    surface coating material is sold to a
    consumer. ([contenant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[display panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[display panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any part of a container
    on which the common or generic name of a surface coating material,
    or the description of its function, is displayed. ([aire
    d'affichage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[good laboratory practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[good laboratory practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means practices
    that are in accordance with the principles set out in the
    Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development document
    entitled OECD Principles of Good Laboratory Practice, Number 1 of
    the OECD Series on Principles of Good Laboratory Practice and
    Compliance Monitoring, ENV/MC/CHEM(98)17, the English version of
    which is dated January 21, 1998 and the French version of which is
    dated March 6, 1998. ([bonnes pratiques de
    laboratoire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[surface coating material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[surface coating material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a paint or
    other similar material, with or without pigment, that forms a solid
    film after it is applied to a surface and that can be
    removed. ([revêtement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Surface Coating Materials]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832933 .Part}

### [Lead]{.HTitleText2} {#h-832934 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lead content and test method

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  surface coating material must not contain more than 90 mg/kg total
  lead when a dried sample is tested in accordance with a method that
  conforms to good laboratory practices.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a surface coating
  material that is used

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as an anti-corrosive or anti-weathering coating on
    the interior or exterior surface of a building having an
    agricultural purpose or on equipment having an agricultural purpose;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as an anti-corrosive or anti-weathering coating on
    a structure, other than a building, having an agricultural or public
    purpose;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} as a touch-up coating on metal surfaces;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the production of outdoor graphic art, including
    billboard displays; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in an art, craft or hobby activity, except if the
    surface coating material is used by children.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warning

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), if a surface coating material that is used
  for a purpose mentioned in subsection 2(2) contains more than 90 mg/kg
  total lead, the display panel on the container must contain the
  following warning, or its equivalent, with the italicized portion
  being replaced by the applicable uses:

  **DANGER**

  **CONTAINS LEAD / [CONTIENT DU PLOMB]{.otherLang lang="fr"}**

  **DO NOT APPLY TO SURFACES ACCESSIBLE TO CHILDREN OR PREGNANT WOMEN
  /**

  **[NE PAS APPLIQUER SUR UNE SURFACE ACCESSIBLE AUX ENFANTS OU AUX
  FEMMES ENCEINTES]{.otherLang lang="fr"}**

  **Must be used exclusively \[*Insert the applicable uses mentioned in
  subsection 2(2)*\]. / [Utiliser uniquement aux fins suivantes :
  \[*insérer les fins mentionnées au paragraphe 2(2) qui
  s'appliquent*\].]{.otherLang lang="fr"}**

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Small containers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), if the area of the display
  panel is less than 35 cm^2^, the display panel may contain only the
  following warning:

  **DANGER**

  **CONTAINS LEAD / [CONTIENT DU PLOMB]{.otherLang lang="fr"}**

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signal word "DANGER"

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the warning required by subsection (1) or (2) is
  displayed in separate English and French display panels, the signal
  word "**DANGER**" must be displayed in each display panel, but if the
  warning is displayed in one bilingual display panel, the signal word
  "**DANGER**" need only be displayed once.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legibility and type

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  warning required by section 3 must appear in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a permanent, clear and legible manner;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} capital letters, except for the portion dealing
    with uses in the warning under subsection 3(1);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} bold-face or medium-face sans serif type; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} type having the minimum height and size set out in
    columns 2 and 3 of the table to this subsection according to the
    display panel area set out in column 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of display panel area

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the calculation of the area of a display panel,
  any seam and any convex or concave surface at the top or bottom of the
  container are to be excluded.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Curved container --- display panel area

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a container that is curved in any way,
  other than a bag, the display panel area is 40% of the total area of
  the container, excluding the top and bottom.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of minimum height of type

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(d), the minimum
  height of type is determined by measuring an upper case letter or a
  lower case letter that has an ascender or descender, such as "b" or
  "p".

### [Mercury]{.HTitleText2} {#h-832979 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mercury content and test method

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A surface coating material
must not contain more than 10 mg/kg total mercury when a dried sample is
tested in accordance with a method that conforms to good laboratory
practices.

## [Products With Applied Stickers, Films or Surface Coating Materials]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832982 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lead content

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A sticker, film or other
similar material that can be removed, or a surface coating material,
that is applied to an accessible part of the following products must not
contain more than 90 mg/kg total lead when it is tested in accordance
with a method that conforms to good laboratory practices:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} furniture;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} products for children;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} pencils; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} artist brushes.
