Acquisition of Permanent Resident Status Fee Remission Order

P.C. 2006-366 2006-05-11

:::: section

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of Citizenship and Immigration and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613031 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the amount of \$485, being a portion of the fee of \$975 payable
under subsection 303(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227), if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fee is paid at or after 12:00 a.m. Eastern
  Daylight Saving Time on May 3, 2006 and before the coming into force
  of the Regulations Amending the Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Regulations (Section 303 Fee) ; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fee was paid before 12:00 a.m. Eastern Daylight
  Saving Time on May 3, 2006 and the person in respect of whom the fee
  was paid acquires permanent resident status at or after 12:00 a.m.
  Eastern Daylight Saving Time on May 3, 2006 and before the coming into
  force of the Regulations Amending the Immigration and Refugee
  Protection Regulations (Section 303 Fee).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In the case where the fee
of \$975 was paid, the amount remitted shall be repaid to the person who
paid the fee.
