## Ontario Asparagus Growers' Marketing-for-Processing Order {#ontario-asparagus-growers-marketing-for-processing-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/78-859
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1978-11-20

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing of Asparagus for
Processing Produced in Ontario

P.C. 1978-3451 1978-11-16
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to section 2 of the [Agricultural
Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), is pleased hereby to revoke the
[Ontario Asparagus Growers' Marketing-for-Processing
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-859) made by Order in Council
P.C. 1967-1029 of 25th May, 1967[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_SOR-78-859_e_hq_10311){#fn_SOR-78-859_e_hq_10311-ID0EDBAA}
and to make the annexed [Order granting authority to regulate the
marketing of asparagus for processing produced in
Ontario](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-859) in substitution therefor.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-864598 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Asparagus Growers' Marketing-for-Processing
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-859).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-864600 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[asparagus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[asparagus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means asparagus produced in
    Ontario; ([asperge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Farm Products Marketing
    Board of Ontario, established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Régie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Asparagus
    Growers' Marketing Board, established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    asparagus established and amended from time to time pursuant to the
    Act, and any regulations made pursuant to the Act to give effect to
    the Plan; ([plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means canning, dehydrating,
    drying, freezing or processing with sugar or sulphur dioxide or any
    other chemical; ([transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who, on the first day of May in any
      year, is the beneficial owner of a property on which asparagus is
      growing, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the tenant of property referred to in
      paragraph (a) where, on the first day of May in any year, the
      property is rented to that tenant. ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-864611 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board and the
Board are each authorized to regulate the marketing of asparagus in
interprovincial and export trade and for such purposes may, by order or
regulation, with respect to persons and property situated within the
Province of Ontario, exercise all or any powers like the powers
exercisable by each of them respectively in relation to the marketing of
asparagus locally within that Province under the Act and the Plan.

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-864613 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized in relation to the powers granted to it by section 3 with
respect to the marketing of asparagus in interprovincial and export
trade to make orders fixing, imposing and collecting levies or charges
from persons situated in the Province of Ontario who are engaged in the
production or marketing of asparagus and for such purpose classifying
such persons into groups and fixing the levies or charges payable by the
members of the different groups in different amounts, and the Commodity
Board may use such levies or charges for its purposes, including the
creation of reserves, and the payment of expenses and losses resulting
from the sale or disposal of asparagus and the equalization or
adjustment among producers of asparagus of moneys realized from the sale
thereof during such period or periods of time as it may determine.
