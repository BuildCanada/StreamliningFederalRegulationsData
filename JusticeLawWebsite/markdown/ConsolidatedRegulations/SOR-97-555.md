## Broadcasting Distribution Regulations {#broadcasting-distribution-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-555
:::

[BROADCASTING ACT](/eng/acts/B-9.01)

Registration 1997-12-08

Broadcasting Distribution Regulations
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 10(3) of the [Broadcasting
Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFACA}, a copy of the
proposed [Broadcasting Distribution
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-555), substantially in the annexed
form, was published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/),
Part I, on July 12, 1997, and a reasonable opportunity was given to
licensees and other interested persons to make representations to the
Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission with respect
thereto;

Therefore, the Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications
Commission, pursuant to subsection 10(1) of the [Broadcasting
Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Broadcasting Distribution
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-555).

Hull, Quebec, December 8, 1997

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1010376 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Broadcasting
    Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[adult programming service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[adult programming service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    programming service that provides programming devoted to depicting
    explicit sexual activity. ([service de programmation pour
    adultes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[affiliate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[affiliate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a distribution undertaking, a
      person that controls the licensee, or that is controlled by the
      licensee or by a person that controls the licensee; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a television station, one that has
      an affiliation agreement with another television
      station. ([affiliée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[affiliation agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[affiliation agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement
    between one or more television stations and another television
    station according to which programs provided by the latter station
    are broadcast by the one or more stations at a predetermined
    time. ([contrat d'affiliation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[allowable contribution to local expression]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[allowable contribution to local
    expression]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a licensed area the majority of
      the population of which resides in a metropolitan market, any
      contribution to local expression; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a licensed area the majority of
      the population of which does not reside in a metropolitan market,
      the sum of

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any contributions to local expression that are
        directed to community programming for distribution on the
        licensee's own community channel, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any other contributions to local expression up
        to the amount of the contribution referred to in subparagraph
        (i). ([contribution à l'expression locale
        admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[AM station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[AM station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a station that broadcasts
    in the AM frequency band of 525 to 1 705 kHz. It does not include a
    carrier current undertaking or a transmitter that only rebroadcasts
    the radiocommunications of another station. ([station
    AM]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[anglophone market]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[anglophone market]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a licensed
    area, means a market that is not a francophone market. ([marché
    anglophone]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Atlantic provinces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Atlantic provinces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Nova Scotia, New
    Brunswick, Prince Edward Island and Newfoundland and
    Labrador. ([provinces de l'Atlantique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[authorized non-Canadian programming service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorized non-Canadian programming
    service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a programming service that is
    authorized for distribution by the Commission and is included in the
    appendix to Broadcasting Regulatory Policy CRTC 2011-399, dated June
    30, 2011 and entitled List of non-Canadian programming services
    authorized for distribution, as amended from time to time. ([service
    de programmation non canadien approuvé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[basic service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    services that is distributed by a licensee in a licensed area for a
    single fee and that consists of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a terrestrial distribution
      undertaking that distributes programming services on a digital
      basis, the programming services that are distributed in accordance
      with section 17 or a condition of its licence;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a terrestrial distribution
      undertaking that distributes programming services on an analog
      basis, the programming services that are required to be
      distributed under section 41 or a condition of its licence, and
      any other services that are included in the package; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a DTH distribution undertaking,
      the programming services that are distributed in accordance with
      section 46 or a condition of its licence. ([service de
      base]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[broadcast week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[broadcast week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a period of seven
    consecutive days beginning on a Sunday. ([semaine de
    radiodiffusion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[broadcast year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[broadcast year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning on
    September 1 in a calendar year and ending on August 31 of the
    following calendar year. ([année de radiodiffusion]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[cable distribution undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[Canadian programming service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is provided by a licensed programming
      undertaking. ([service de programmation canadien]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Category A service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Category A service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a licence that was issued before
      September 1, 2011,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a pay television service, other than a Category
        C service or a service that was designated as a Category 2
        service by the Commission before that day, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a specialty service, other than a Category C
        service or a service that was designated as a Category 2 service
        by the Commission before that day; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a licence that is issued during the
      period beginning on September 1, 2011 and ending on August 31,
      2018, a Canadian programming service that is designated as a
      Category A service by the Commission. ([service de catégorie
      A]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[community programming]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[community programming]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to
    a licensed area, programming that is produced

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by the licensee in the licensed area or by
      members of the community served in the licensed area;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by the licensee in another licensed area or by
      the members of the community served in that other licensed area
      and that is relevant to the community referred to in paragraph
      (a);

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by another licensee in a licensed area or by the
      members of the community served in that licensed area and that is
      relevant to the community referred to in paragraph (a); or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by a person licensed to operate a network for the
      purpose of producing community programming for distribution by the
      licensee on a community channel.

    This definition includes community access television programming and
    local community television programming. ([programmation
    communautaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[community programming undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[community programming undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    television programming undertaking operated by a not-for-profit
    organization that is licensed to operate a community
    channel. ([entreprise de programmation
    communautaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[community television corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[community television corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    not-for-profit corporation that resides within a licensed area, that
    is incorporated by or under the laws of Canada or of a province and
    of which

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the primary activity is to produce local
      community television programming or to operate a community channel
      that is reflective of the community it represents;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} board members are drawn from the community; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all board members are entitled to participate and
      vote at an annual meeting. ([société de télévision
      communautaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[comparable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[comparable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of two or more
    programming services, means that not less than 95% of the video and
    audio components of those programming services, exclusive of
    commercial messages and of any part of the services carried on a
    subsidiary signal, are the same. ([comparable]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[contribution to community programming]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

      locale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1010592 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
persons licensed to carry on a distribution undertaking, other than a
person licensed to carry on

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a subscription television system;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a relay distribution undertaking; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an undertaking that only rebroadcasts the
  radiocommunications of one or more other licensed undertakings.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1010597 .Part}

### [Prohibition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1010598 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A licensee shall not
distribute programming services except as required or authorized under
its licence or these Regulations.

### [Transfer of Ownership]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1010600 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in this section.

  [[associate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[associate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, when used to indicate a
      relationship with a person, includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a partner of the person;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a trust or an estate in which the person has a
        substantial beneficial interest or in respect of which the
        person serves as a trustee or in a similar capacity;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the spouse or common-law partner of the person;

      - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} a child of the person or of their spouse or
        common-law partner, including a child adopted in fact by the
        person or by the spouse or common-law partner;

      - [(c.2)]{.lawlabel} the spouse or common-law partner of a child
        referred to in paragraph (c.1);

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a relative of the person, or of the spouse or
        common-law partner referred to in paragraph (c), if that
        relative has the same residence as the person;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a corporation of which the person alone, or the
        person together with one or more associates as described in this
        definition, has, directly or indirectly, control of 50% or more
        of the voting interests;

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a corporation of which an associate, as
        described in this definition, of the person has, directly or
        indirectly, control of 50% or more of the voting interests; and

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a person with whom the person has entered into
        an arrangement, a contract, an understanding or an agreement in
        respect of the voting of shares of a licensee corporation or of
        a corporation that has, directly or indirectly, effective
        control of a licensee corporation, except when the person
        controls less than 1% of all issued voting shares of a
        corporation whose shares are publicly traded on a stock
        exchange. ([liens]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
      person, means an individual who is cohabiting with the person in a
      conjugal relationship, having so cohabited for a period of at
      least one year. ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[common shares]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[common shares]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the shares that
      represent the residual equity in the earnings of a corporation,
      and includes securities that are convertible into such shares at
      all times at the option of the holder and the preferred shares to
      which are attached rights to participate in the earnings of the
      corporation with no upper limit; ([actions
      ordinaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an individual, a
      partnership, a joint venture, an association, a corporation, an
      estate, a trust, a trustee, an executor and an administrator or a
      legal representative of any of them. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[voting interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[voting interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a corporation with share capital, means the
        vote attached to a voting share;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation without share capital, means an
        interest that entitles the owner to voting rights similar to
        those enjoyed by the owner of a voting share;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a partnership, a trust, an association or a
        joint venture means an interest or right in the assets of it
        that entitles the owner to receive a share of its profits, to
        receive a share of its assets on dissolution and to participate
        directly in its management or to vote on the election of the
        persons to be entrusted with the power and responsibility to
        manage it; and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a not-for-profit partnership, trust,
        association or joint venture, means a right that entitles the
        owner to participate directly in its management or to vote on
        the election of the persons to be entrusted with the power and
        responsibility to manage it. ([intérêt avec droit de
        vote]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[voting share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[voting share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a share in the capital
      of a corporation, to which is attached one or more votes that are
      exercisable at meetings of shareholders of the corporation, either
      under all circumstances or under a circumstance that has occurred
      and is continuing, and includes any security that is convertible
      into such a share at all times at the option of the holder.
      ([action avec droit de vote]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, control of a voting
  interest by a person includes situations in which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is, directly or indirectly, the
    beneficial owner of the voting interest; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person, by means of an arrangement, a contract,
    an understanding or an agreement, determines the manner in which the
    interest is voted, but the solicitation of proxies or the seeking of
    instructions regarding the completion of proxies in respect of the
    exercise of voting interests is not considered to be such an
    arrangement, contract, understanding or agreement.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, effective control
  of a licensee or its undertaking includes situations in which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person controls, directly or indirectly, other
    than by way of security only, a majority of the voting interests of
    the licensee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person has the ability to cause the licensee or
    its board of directors to undertake a course of action; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Commission, following a public hearing of an
    application for a licence, or in respect of an existing licence,
    determines that a person has such effective control and sets out
    that determination in a decision or public notice.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided under a condition of its
  licence, a licensee shall obtain the prior approval of the Commission
  in respect of any act, agreement or transaction that directly or
  indirectly would result in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a change of the effective control of its
    undertaking; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who, alone, or together with an associate,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} controls less than 30% of the voting interests of
      the licensee, having control of 30% or more of those interests,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} controls less than 30% of the voting interests
      of a person who has, directly or indirectly, effective control of
      the licensee, having control of 30% or more of those interests,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} owns less than 50% of the issued common shares
      of the licensee, owning 50% or more of those shares but not
      having, directly or indirectly, effective control of the licensee,
      or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} owns less than 50% of the issued common shares
      of a person who has, directly or indirectly, effective control of
      the licensee, owning 50% or more of those shares but not having,
      directly or indirectly, effective control of the licensee.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A licensee shall notify the Commission within 30 days

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 20% of the voting interests of the licensee, having
    control of 20% or more but less than 30% of those interests;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 20% of the voting interests of a person who has,

    control of 20% or more but less than 30% of those interests;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 40% of the voting interests of the licensee, having

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} 40% of the voting interests of a person who has,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the percentage of the voting interests controlled

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy or a complete description of the act,
    agreement or transaction.

  the service being distributed; or

  responsible for that Canadian programming service and that agreement
  pertains to commercial messages directed at a target market of
  consumers.

### [Basic Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1011135 .Subheading}

**[[45.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45.1 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise

distribute as part of its basic service any programming services other
than those that are referred to in section 46.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-239, s. 20

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this section.

  [[independent ownership group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[independent ownership group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      ownership group --- other than the Corporation or a major
      ownership group --- that owns and operates an independently-owned
      television station or an ownership group designated as such by the
      Commission in paragraph 31 of Broadcasting Regulatory Policy CRTC
      2010--162 dated March 19, 2010 and entitled Distribution by
      direct-to-home services of stations from the major ownership
      groups in the Atlantic provinces and independently owned stations
      across Canada. ([groupe de propriété
      indépendante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[independently-owned television station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[independently-owned television station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
      means a licensed television station that is not owned by one of
      the major ownership groups and that provides, in one of the
      official languages, local programming to the community it is
      licensed to serve. ([station de télévision de propriété
      indépendante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided under a condition of its
  licence, a licensee shall distribute, as part of its basic service, to
  the extent that they are available, the programming services of two
  independently-owned television stations from each independent
  ownership group to subscribers whose residence or other premises are
  located within the Grade B official contour or noise-limited bounding
  official contour.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided under a condition of its
  licence, a licensee shall distribute as part of its basic service,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the educational television programming services, to
    the extent that they are available, whose operation is the
    responsibility of an educational authority designated by the
    province in which the subscriber's residence or other premises are
    located; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the programming services of a programming
    undertaking that the Commission has required, under paragraph
    9(1)(h) of the Act, to be distributed as part of the basic service.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided under a condition of its
  licence, a licensee shall distribute as part of its basic service to a
  subscriber whose residence or other premises are located in Ontario,
  Quebec, Manitoba, British Columbia, Saskatchewan or Alberta,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the programming service, to the extent that it is
    available, of one of each of the Corporation's English-language and
    French-language network television stations that originate in the
    province in which the subscriber's residence or other premises are
    located;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the programming service, to the extent that it is
    available, of one television station from each major ownership group
    that originates in the province in which the subscriber's residence
    or other premises are located; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the programming service, to the extent that it is
    available, of one independently-owned television station that
    originates in the province in which the subscriber's residence or
    other premises are located.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided under a condition of its
  licence, a licensee shall distribute as part of its basic service to
  subscribers whose residence or other premises are located in the
  Atlantic provinces

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the programming services, to the extent that they
    are available, of two of each of the Corporation's English-language
    and French-language network television stations that originate in
    the Atlantic provinces;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the programming services, to the extent that they
    are available, of two television stations that originate in the
    Atlantic provinces, from each major ownership group; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the programming service, to the extent that it is
    available, of one independently-owned television station that
    originates in the Atlantic provinces.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If two or more programming services from the
  Corporation or two or more programming services from the same
  ownership group are available to be distributed by a licensee in
  accordance with paragraph (5)(a) or (b), the licensee is required to
  distribute only one of those programming services from the Corporation
  or one of those programming services from the ownership group, as the
  case may be, if the operator of the programming undertaking that
  provides the programming services has not committed to providing local
  programming as part of its licence issuance or most recent licence
  renewal or amendment.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided under a condition of its
  licence, a licensee shall distribute as part of its basic service to a
  subscriber whose residence or other premises are located in Yukon, the
  Northwest Territories or Nunavut

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the programming services of at least one of the
    Corporation's Northern Television Services; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the programming services referred to in
    subsections (2) to (4) that are distributed in another province.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided under a condition of its
  licence, a licensee may, in addition to the programming services
  required under subsections (2) to (7), distribute to a subscriber as
  part of its basic service

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a 4 + 1 package of programming services that
    originates

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the same time zone as that in which the
      subscriber's residence or other premises is located, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if no such package originates in that time zone,
      in any other time zone; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the programming service of any radio station whose
    market, as defined in section 2 of the [Radio Regulations,
    1986](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-982), includes the location of the
    subscriber's residence or other premises.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} A licensee that distributes a programming service
  under this section may also distribute the high definition version of
  that programming service.

::: HistoricalNote

### [Contribution to Canadian Programming]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1011216 .Subheading}

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as otherwise provided under a condition of its licence, a licensee
  shall, for each broadcast year, contribute to Canadian programming an
  amount equal to 4.7% of its gross revenues derived from broadcasting
  activities in the previous broadcast year less any contribution to
  locally reflective news programming made in the current broadcast year
  to a maximum of an amount equal to 0.6% of gross revenues derived from
  broadcasting activities in the previous broadcast year.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The licensee shall make the contribution as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} not more than an amount equal to 0.5% of its gross
    revenues derived from broadcasting activities in the previous
    broadcast year is to be contributed to any number of independent
    production funds; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the remainder of the total required contribution is
    to be made to the Canadian production fund.
