## Special Authority to Possess Regulations (Firearms Act) {#special-authority-to-possess-regulations-firearms-act .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-208
:::

[FIREARMS ACT](/eng/acts/F-11.6)

Registration 1998-03-24

Special Authority to Possess Regulations (Firearms Act)

P.C. 1998-483 1998-03-24
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 118 of the [Firearms
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA}, the Minister
of Justice had a copy of the proposed [Special Authority to Possess
Regulations (Firearms Act)](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-208), substantially
in the annexed form, laid before each House of Parliament on October 30,
1997, which date is at least 30 sitting days before the date of this
Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to paragraphs
117(i), (k) and (m) of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)^a^, hereby
makes the annexed [Special Authority to Possess Regulations (Firearms
Act)](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-208).

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Manufacture and Transfer of Replica Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019830 .Part}

### [Manufacture without Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019831 .Subheading}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual or business may, without a licence, manufacture a replica
  firearm in performance of a contract to supply the holder of a licence
  issued for any of the prescribed purposes listed in paragraph 22(f) of
  the [Firearms Licences Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-199).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The individual or business may transfer the replica
  firearm only to the licence holder with whom the contract described in
  subsection (1) was entered into.

### [Obligations of Manufacturer]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019835 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The manufacturer referred
to in section 2 shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the manufacturer is a business, designate at least
  one individual at the place of manufacture who is responsible for the
  use, storage and handling of the replica firearm;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} keep a record with respect to replica firearms
  manufactured or transferred that includes

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of each replica firearm,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date of manufacture and the date of transfer
    of each replica firearm,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity transferred, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number and licence
    number of the holder of the licence referred to in subsection 2(2);
    and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} make the records available to a chief firearms
  officer on request.

### [Storage]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019844 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The individual or business
referred to in subsection 2(1) by whom a replica firearm is manufactured
shall ensure that the replica firearm is stored in the manner set out in
section 5.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Replica firearms shall be
stored in a container, receptacle, vault, safe or room that is kept
securely locked and that is constructed so that it cannot readily be
broken open or into.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Temporary Transfers of Replica Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019847 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019848 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Part.

[[transferee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transferee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person to whom a
    replica firearm is transferred under section 8.
    ([cessionnaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transferor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transferor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any individual or business who was in lawful
      possession of a replica firearm on commencement day;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder of a licence issued for any of the
      prescribed purposes listed in paragraph 22(f) of the [Firearms
      Licences Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-199); and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a chief firearms officer.
      ([cédant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019855 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to a
transfer as defined in subsection 84(1) of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46), other than by sale, bartering or giving.

### [Transfer]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019857 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A transferor may transfer a
replica firearm to a transferee, whether or not the transferee holds a
licence to acquire replica firearms, who is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} acting in the course of his or her duties or
  employment in motion picture, television, video or theatrical
  production or in publishing activities; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an instructor who is designated by a chief firearms
  officer in accordance with section 7 of the Act and who is acting in
  his or her capacity as an instructor under that section.

### [Obligations of Transferor]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019861 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A transferor shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the transferee is a business, ensure that the
  transferee designates at least one individual at the work place who is
  responsible for the use, storage and handling of the replica firearm;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the transferee is informed of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the provisions of these Regulations, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the offence provisions of the Act and the
    [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) relating to replica firearms and
    imitation firearms;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} keep a record with respect to replica firearms
  transferred that includes

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of each replica firearm,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date of the transfer,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity transferred, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the
    transferee; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} make the records available to a chief firearms
  officer on request.

### [Obligations of Transferee or Designated Person]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019873 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The transferee or, if a
person is designated under paragraph 9(a), that person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide a signed statement to the transferor
  indicating that the requirements of paragraph 9(b) have been met; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} keep a record of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the quantity and the description of replica
    firearms at the work place, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date on which each replica firearm was
    received.

### [Period of Transfer]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019879 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The period of the
transfer shall not exceed one year, but a transfer may be extended for a
further period of up to one year if the transferor updates the
information described in section 9 and verifies that the transferee is
in compliance with paragraph 9(a).

### [Storage]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019881 .Subheading}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The transferee shall
ensure that the replica firearms are stored in the manner set out in
section 5.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Possession of Certain Prohibited Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019883 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** An individual who holds a
licence authorizing the possession of a prohibited firearm, other than a
handgun referred to in subsection 12(6) of the Act, may be authorized by
a chief firearms officer to possess such a firearm in the circumstances
set out in subsection 14(1) or (2).

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  chief firearms officer of the province in which the following
  activities are to take place may, if the safety of any person will not
  be endangered, authorize the possession of a firearm referred to in
  section 13 at a shooting range and in the course of transporting the
  firearm by a route that is, in all the circumstances, reasonably
  direct between the place authorized under section 17 of the Act with
  respect to that firearm and the shooting range

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an automatic firearm, if it is being
    used for test firing or demonstration purposes on an occasional
    basis, at a shooting range maintained by the Minister of National
    Defence under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other prohibited firearm, if it
    is being used for test firing or demonstration purposes or for
    target shooting or competitive events, on an occasional basis, at a
    shooting range approved under section 29 of the Act or maintained by
    the Minister of National Defence under the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The chief firearms officer of the province in which
  the individual referred to in section 13 resides may, if the safety of
  any person will not be endangered, authorize the possession of a
  firearm referred to in that section in the course of transporting the
  firearm by a route that, in all the circumstances, is reasonably
  direct between the place authorized under section 17 of the Act with
  respect to that firearm and a customs office if the firearm is being
  used on an occasional basis at an event outside of Canada.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** An individual who holds a
licence authorizing the possession of a prohibited firearm may be
authorized by the chief firearms officer of the province in which the
individual resides to possess such a firearm in the course of
transporting it by a route that is, in all the circumstances, reasonably
direct between the place authorized under section 17 of the Act with
respect to that firearm and the location of an approved verifier for the
purpose of verification.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019891 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on December 1, 1998.
