## Payments and Settlements Requisitioning Regulations, 1997 {#payments-and-settlements-requisitioning-regulations-1997 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-130
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1998-02-23

Payments and Settlements Requisitioning Regulations, 1997

T.B. 825983-4 1998-02-19
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Treasury Board, pursuant to sections 10[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} and 33 of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed
[Payments and Settlements Requisitioning Regulations,
1997](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-130).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018139 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[digital signature]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[digital signature]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the result of the
    transformation of a message by means of a cryptosystem using keys
    such that a person having the initial message can determine

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the transformation was created using the
      key that corresponds to the signer's key, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the message has been altered since the
      transformation was made. ([signature numérique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[settlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[settlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include adjusting or
    correcting entries within a particular appropriation.
    ([règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018146 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
every requisition addressed to the Receiver General requesting a payment
to be made out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund or requesting an
interdepartmental or intradepartmental settlement.

## [Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018148 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  requisition must be made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a pre-printed form designed specifically for the
    purpose of requisitioning payments or settlements;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a printed or written form resulting from an
    operating procedure of the Receiver General or the department or
    other organization submitting the requisition; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the form of an electronic instruction that is
    either issued by on-line transfer or on a magnetic tape, disk,
    diskette or any other electronic physical storage device.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every requisition must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the design format and operational standards
    for requisitions set out in Design of Forms for Input to the Central
    Accounting System (CAS), Receiver General Directive 1986-12;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if made in the form of an electronic instruction
    issued by on-line transfer, be authorized by a digital signature;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} contain the information set out in the schedule.

## [Certification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018158 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The certification of a
requisition must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} comply with the requirements of the Treasury Board's
  Policy on Account Verification for the control of financial
  transactions;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be in such a form that it cannot easily be imitated
  or duplicated by a person other than the person authorized to certify
  the requisition; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be such that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it clearly identifies the person certifying the
    requisition,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it involves the use of information that is
    personally generated at the time of certification by the person
    authorized to certify it and does not originate from a stored
    location as part of an automated process, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} it can be authenticated by the Receiver General
    before the payment or settlement is made and can be audited after
    the payment or settlement is made.

## [Integrity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018166 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  appropriate Minister shall protect the integrity of every requisition
  made by the Minister until the requisition is received by the Receiver
  General.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Receiver General shall protect the integrity of
  every requisition from the time it is received by the Receiver General
  until it is verified in accordance with section 6.

## [Verification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018170 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Receiver General shall not make a payment or settlement in respect of
  a requisition unless the Receiver General has

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} verified that the requisition is authentic and has
    been certified by a person duly authorized under subsection 33(1) of
    the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the requisition has been made in the form of an
    electronic instruction issued by on-line transfer, acknowledged
    receipt of the requisition.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate Minister shall, for the purpose of
  subsection (1), provide the Receiver General with the information
  required to identify the person who has certified the requisition.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018176 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** \[Repeal\]

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018178 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on February 23, 1998.
