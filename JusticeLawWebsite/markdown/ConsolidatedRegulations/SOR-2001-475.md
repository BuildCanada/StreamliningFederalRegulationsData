## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676861 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[approved credit rating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approved credit rating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
      security or instrument of a type described in the heading of
      column 2 or 3 of the schedule, means a rating that has been issued
      by an approved credit rating organization set out in column 1 of
      an item of the schedule and that is at or above the rating
      category set out for that type of security or instrument in column
      2 or 3 of that item, or that is at or above a rating category that
      the approved credit rating organization has adopted to replace
      that rating category. ([note approuvée]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[approved credit rating organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approved credit rating organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Dominion Bond Rating Service Limited;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Fitch, Inc.;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Moody's Investors Service, Inc.;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Standard & Poor's Corporation; or

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a successor of an organization named in any of
        paragraphs (a) to (d). ([agence de notation
        agréée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any of the following that is authorized to
        carry on business under the laws of Canada or a province,
        namely,

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a bank,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an authorized foreign bank,

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a loan company,

        - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a trust company,

        - [(v)]{.lawlabel} an insurance company to which the [Insurance
          Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies,

        - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} an insurance corporation incorporated by or
          under an Act of the legislature of a province,

        - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} a central cooperative credit society,

        - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} a cooperative credit association, or

        - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} a local cooperative credit society;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Alberta Treasury Branches, established under
        the Alberta Treasury Branches Act of the Province of Alberta; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Fédération des caisses Desjardins du
        Québec. ([institution financière canadienne]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[cash equivalent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cash equivalent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an evidence of
      indebtedness that has a remaining term to maturity of 365 days or
      less and is issued, or fully and unconditionally guaranteed as to
      principal and interest

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by the Government of Canada or the government
        of a province;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by the government of the United States, the
        government of one of the states of the United States, the
        government of another sovereign state or a permitted
        supranational agency, if the evidence of indebtedness has an
        approved credit rating; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by a Canadian financial institution, or by a
        financial institution that is not incorporated or organized
        under the laws of Canada or a province, if the evidences of
        indebtedness of that issuer or guarantor that are rated as
        short-term debt by an approved credit rating organization have
        an approved credit rating. ([quasi-espèces]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[permitted supranational agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[permitted supranational agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the African Development Bank;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Asian Development Bank;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Caribbean Development Bank;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the European Bank for Reconstruction and
        Development;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Inter-American Development Bank;

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the International Bank for Reconstruction and
        Development;

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the International Finance Corporation; or

      - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any person that is prescribed for the purpose
        of subparagraph (g)(v) of the definition [[foreign
        property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 206(1) of the
        [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([organisme supranational
        accepté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approved credit rating

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a security or
  an instrument is considered to have an approved credit rating only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an approved credit rating organization has issued
    an approved credit rating for the security or instrument;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the approved credit rating organization has not
    made any announcement that the rating of the security or instrument
    may be downgraded to a rating category that would not be an approved
    credit rating; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} no approved credit rating organization has rated
    the security or instrument in a rating category that is not an
    approved credit rating.

## [Conditions for Money Market Mutual Funds]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676903 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A money market mutual fund
must satisfy the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all of its assets must be invested in one or more of
  the following, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} cash,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} cash equivalents,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} evidences of indebtedness, other than cash
    equivalents, that have remaining terms to maturity of 365 days or
    less, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} floating rate evidences of indebtedness not
    referred to in subparagraphs (ii) and (iii), if the principal
    amounts of the obligations will continue to have a market value of
    approximately par at the time of each change in the rate to be paid
    to the holders of the evidences of indebtedness;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} its portfolio of evidences of indebtedness must have
  a dollar-weighted average term to maturity not exceeding 90 days,
  calculated on the basis that the term of a floating rate obligation is
  the period remaining to the date of the next rate setting;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} not less than 95% of its assets must be invested in
  cash, cash equivalents or evidences of indebtedness denominated in a
  currency in which the net asset value per security of the mutual fund
  is calculated; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} not less than 95% of its assets must be invested in
  one or more of the following, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} cash,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} cash equivalents, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} evidences of indebtedness of issuers whose
    commercial paper has an approved credit rating.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676917 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on November 7, 2001.
