## Designating the Advisory Council on the Status of Women as a Portion of the Public Service and Authorizing the President to Exercise and Perform all the Powers and Functions of the Treasury Board in Relation to Personnel Management of the Council {#designating-the-advisory-council-on-the-status-of-women-as-a-portion-of-the-public-service-and-authorizing-the-president-to-exercise-and-perform-all-the-powers-and-functions-of-the-treasury-board-in-relation-to-personnel-management-of-the-council .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/82-38
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1982-02-10

Designating the Advisory Council on the Status of Women as a Portion of
the Public Service and Authorizing the President to Exercise and Perform
all the Powers and Functions of the Treasury Board in Relation to
Personnel Management of the Council

P.C. 1982-48 1982-01-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Honourable Judy Erola, is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to the definition [[public
  service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 7(9) of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to designate the Advisory Council
  on the Status of Women as a portion of the public service of Canada
  for the purposes of section 7 of that Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to subsection 7(3) of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to authorize the President of the
  Advisory Council on the Status of Women to exercise and perform, in
  such manner and subject to such terms and conditions as the Governor
  in Council directs, all of the powers and functions of the Treasury
  Board in relation to personnel management of the Advisory Council on
  the Status of Women.
