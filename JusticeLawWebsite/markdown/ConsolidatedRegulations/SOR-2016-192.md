## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832859 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[oral LD~50~ value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[oral LD~50~ value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the single dose of a
    substance that, when administered orally in an animal assay, is
    expected to cause the death of at least 50% of a defined population
    of animals. ([DL~50~ orale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[replacement chemical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[replacement chemical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a chemical
    product, as defined in the [Consumer Chemicals and Containers
    Regulations, 2001](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-269), for restocking a
    science education set. ([produit chimique de
    rechange]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[science education set]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[science education set]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a collection of
    products, materials or substances that contains a chemical product
    as defined in the [Consumer Chemicals and Containers Regulations,
    2001](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-269) and that is for the purpose of
    instructing the user about a science. ([trousse d'expérience
    scientifique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832865 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chemicals --- restrictions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  chemical that is listed in the schedule or a chemical, the oral LD~50~
  value of which is equal to or less than 50 mg/kg of the body weight of
  the test animal, must not be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} included in a science education set;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a replacement chemical; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), required in any
    experiment described in the instructions accompanying the science
    education set or the replacement chemical.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The instructions accompanying a science education set
  or a replacement chemical may require the use of methanol for the
  purpose of operating a spirit lamp.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A chemical included in a
science education set and any replacement chemical must not be sold in
an amount that is more than five times the oral LD~50~ value of that
chemical calculated for a child of 20 kg body weight.

## [Packaging]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832876 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Container

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A chemical included in a
science education set, if it is a chemical product as defined in the
[Consumer Chemicals and Containers Regulations,
2001](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-269), and any replacement chemical, must
be packaged in a container that meets the requirements of those
Regulations.

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832879 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presentation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The information that is
required by these Regulations must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} appear in English and French;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be clearly and prominently shown and appear in
  distinct contrast to other information or visual images; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be readily discernible to the consumer at the time of
  sale or during reasonably foreseeable use.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Science education set --- information

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every science education set
must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} contain or display on its container directions for
  the safe use of the chemicals that it contains; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} display, on its container and in any literature
  accompanying it, the following statement:

  WARNING. This set contains chemicals that may be harmful if misused.
  Read the information in the instruction manual. If splashed in the
  eyes, or on skin, flush thoroughly with water. Get medical attention
  immediately if splashed in eyes. Not to be used by children except
  under adult supervision. Not recommended for children under 12 years
  of age.

  [AVERTISSEMENT. Ce nécessaire contient des produits chimiques qui
  peuvent être nocifs s'ils sont mal utilisés. Lire les indications de
  la brochure d'instructions. En cas d'éclaboussures sur la peau ou dans
  les yeux, bien rincer à grande eau. Recourir immédiatement aux soins
  du médecin si les yeux ont été touchés. Ne doit pas être utilisé par
  des enfants qui ne sont pas sous la surveillance d'un adulte.
  Contre-indiqué pour les enfants de moins de 12 ans.]{.otherLang
  lang="fr"}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chemicals --- information

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A chemical included in a
science education set and any replacement chemical must be packaged in a
container that displays the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the common name of the chemical or its name as
  accepted by the International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the oral LD~50~ value of the chemical is more than
  50 mg/kg but less than 2000 mg/kg of the body weight of the test
  animal, the hazard symbol set out in item 1, column 2, of Schedule 2
  to the [Consumer Chemicals and Containers Regulations,
  2001](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-269), in accordance with the
  requirements for hazard symbols set out in paragraph 17(b) and
  sections 18, 21, 22, 25 and 26 of those Regulations; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the chemical is a chemical product as defined in
  the [Consumer Chemicals and Containers Regulations,
  2001](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-269), the hazard symbols and the
  information that are required to be displayed on its container in
  accordance with those Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- pathogenic
micro-organisms

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A science education set
must not contain or display on its container any instruction for,
description of or words that suggest a test or procedure be performed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to cultivate or demonstrate the presence of a
  pathogenic micro-organism; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to cultivate any micro-organism that is in or comes
  from a product or substance that may contain pathogenic
  micro-organisms or to demonstrate the presence of any micro-organism
  in such a product or substance.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-832906 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** Inorganic acids other than an aqueous solution of hydrochloric
  acid that contains less than 5% of the acid

- **2** Organic acids other than an aqueous solution of acetic acid that
  contains less than 5% of the acid

- **3** Ammonium hydroxide

- **4** Potassium hydroxide and sodium hydroxide other than an aqueous
  solution that contains a maximum of 1% potassium hydroxide or sodium
  hydroxide

- **5** Oxidizing compounds other than an aqueous solution that contains
  a maximum of 10% ammonium nitrate, potassium chlorate, potassium
  perchlorate, potassium nitrate, strontium nitrate or other oxidizing
  compound

- **6** Potassium permanganate other than an aqueous solution that
  contains a maximum of 10% potassium permanganate

- **7** Manganese dioxide

- **8** Hydrogen peroxide other than an aqueous solution that contains a
  maximum of 3% hydrogen peroxide

- **9** Methanol

- **10** Carbon tetrachloride
