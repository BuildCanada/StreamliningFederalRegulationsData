## Transfer of Military Films to NATO Order {#transfer-of-military-films-to-nato-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 735
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Authorizing the Minister of National Defence to Transfer Certain
Films to Other Military Forces
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561264 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Transfer of Military Films to NATO
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._735).

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561266 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of National
Defence is authorized to transfer, gratuitously, to NATO and to NATO and
Commonwealth military forces, when he considers that the public interest
would be best served, military technical, training and information films
and film material, having a value estimated not to exceed \$1,000.
