## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785118 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include a Saturday or
    a holiday. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible enterprise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible enterprise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a business with
    authorized credit of less than \$1 million, fewer than 500 employees
    and annual revenues of less than \$50 million. ([entreprise
    admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-181, s. 117\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-181, s. 117\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a retail association, as defined in section 2 of
      the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a company, as defined in section 2 of the [Trust
      and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8).
      ([institution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[point of service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[point of service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a physical location
    to which the public has access and at which an institution, an
    affiliate of the institution, or an agent or representative of the
    institution or affiliate carries on business with the public and
    opens or initiates the opening of retail deposit accounts of the
    institution or affiliate through natural persons in Canada. ([point
    de service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785128 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Sections 3 and 4 apply only
with respect to paper-based cheques or other instruments deposited in
Canada that are encoded with magnetic ink to allow for character
recognition, are not damaged or mutilated such that they are unreadable
by cheque clearing systems, are drawn on an institution's branch located
in Canada and are issued in Canadian dollars.

## [Maximum Cheque Hold Period]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785130 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An institution must make
available for withdrawal any funds deposited by cheque or other
instrument to a retail deposit account or to a deposit account held by
an eligible enterprise

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a cheque or other instrument not
  exceeding \$1,500, no more than four business days after the day of
  the deposit if it is deposited in person with an employee at one of
  the institution's branches or points of service or no more than five
  business days after the day of the deposit if it is deposited in any
  other manner; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a cheque or other instrument greater
  than \$1,500, no more than seven business days after the day of the
  deposit if it is deposited in person with an employee at one of the
  institution's branches or points of service or no more than eight
  business days after the day of the deposit if it is deposited in any
  other manner.

## [Availability of First \$100]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785134 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An institution must make
the first \$100 of all funds deposited by a cheque or other instrument
on any one day to a retail deposit account available for withdrawal

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} immediately, if it is deposited in person with an
  employee at one of the institution's branches or points of service;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the business day following the day of the deposit,
  if it is deposited in any other manner.

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785138 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Section 3 does not apply in
respect of a deposit that is made by an eligible enterprise if the
institution has reasonable grounds to believe that there is a material
increased credit risk, having regard to factors such as

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an escalating overdraft balance that is not being
  reduced by deposits received;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a negative change in the credit score or other
  behaviour scores that may impact the credit risk of the enterprise;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an unexplained change in the history of cheques or
  other instruments deposited to the account;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} high numbers of cheques or other instruments
  deposited that are returned as dishonoured items from other
  institutions that may impact the available balance in the account; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} notice of bankruptcy or of creditor action against
  the enterprise.

<!-- -->

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections 3 and 4 do not apply in
  respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a deposit that the institution has reasonable
    grounds to believe is being made for illegal or fraudulent purposes
    in relation to the depositor's account;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an account that has been open for less than
    90 days;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a cheque or other instrument that has been endorsed
    more than once; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a cheque or other instrument that is deposited at
    least six months after the date of the cheque.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An institution that relies on this section as grounds
  for refusing to comply with section 3 or 4 must provide the depositor
  in writing with notice of its refusal and a statement indicating that
  the depositor may contact the Financial Consumer Agency of Canada if
  they have a complaint, including contact information for the Agency,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immediately, if the cheque or other instrument was
    deposited in person with an employee at one of the institution's
    branches or points of service; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} upon request, if it was deposited in any other
    manner.

## [Disclosure of Policy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785154 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An institution must
disclose in writing to any person who opens a retail deposit account to
which they may deposit cheques or other instruments

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the maximum periods during which the institution may
  hold funds deposited by cheque or other instruments before making them
  available for withdrawal, as set out in section 3; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the institution's policies concerning the maximum
  period during which the institution may hold funds deposited by cheque
  or other instrument in any situation to which section 3 does not
  apply.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An institution must
disclose the information referred to in section 7 to its customers and
to the public by means of a written notice, copies of which must be
displayed and made available at each of the institution's branches where
personal deposit accounts are offered, at each of the institution's
points of service and on each of the institution's websites through
which it offers products and services in Canada.

## [Notice of Change in Policy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785159 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  institution must disclose any change to the information referred to in
  section 7

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by means of a notice that is displayed, for a
    period of at least 60 days immediately before the effective date of
    the change, at each of the institution's branches where products or
    services are offered, at each of the institution's points of service
    and on each of the institution's websites through which it offers
    products and services in Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by providing every customer in whose name a retail
    deposit account is kept and to whom a statement of account is
    provided with a notice in writing at least 30 days before the
    effective date of the change or, if the customer has instructed the
    institution in writing to provide such a notice to another person,
    by providing that other person with the notice in writing at least
    30 days before the effective date of the change.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the change results in a shortened period during
  which an institution may hold funds deposited by cheque or other
  instrument, the institution may meet the obligations set out in
  paragraph (1)(a) and (b) after the effective date of the change.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice that is sent to a customer by mail is
  considered to be provided to the customer on the fifth business day
  after the postmark date.
