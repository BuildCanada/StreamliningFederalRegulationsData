## [Mimimum Amount]{.HTitleText1} {#h-808180 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  minimum amount of grain that the Canadian National Railway Company and
  Canadian Pacific Railway Company must each move during the following
  periods is specified to be:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 345 000 t a week for the period that begins on
    November 30, 2014 and ends on December 20, 2014;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 200 000 t a week for the period that begins on
    December 21, 2014 and ends on January 3, 2015;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 325 000 t a week for the period that begins on
    January 4, 2015 and ends on February 21, 2015;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} 345 000 t a week for the period that begins on
    February 22, 2015 and ends on March 21, 2015; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} 465 000 t for the period that begins on March 22,
    2015 and ends on March 28, 2015.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a week begins on
  Sunday and ends on the following Saturday.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-808189 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
