## Official Languages (Communications with and Services to the Public) Regulations {#official-languages-communications-with-and-services-to-the-public-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-48

[OFFICIAL LANGUAGES ACT](/eng/acts/O-3.01)

Registration 1991-12-16

Regulations Respecting Communications with and Services to the Public in
Either Official Language

P.C. 1991-2541  1991-12-16
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 84 of the Official Languages Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fnstar1_e){#fnstar1_e-ID0EBBDA}, the President of
the Treasury Board has sought the views of members of the English and
French linguistic minority communities and members of the public
generally on the proposed Regulations concerning communications with and
services to the public in either official language;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fnstar1_e-ID0EBBDA){#fnstar1_e}R.S., c. 31 (4th
  Supp.)
  :::

Whereas, pursuant to section 85 of the said Act, the President of the
Treasury Board has laid a draft of the proposed Regulations before the
House of Commons on November 8, 1990, which date is at least thirty days
before a copy of the proposed Regulations was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) under section 86 of the said Act;

And Whereas, pursuant to section 86 of the said Act, the proposed
Regulations were published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) on March 23, 1991, which date is at
least thirty days before the proposed effective date thereof, and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations to the President of the Treasury Board with respect
thereto;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 32 of the
Official Languages Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fnstar1_e){#fnstar1_e-ID0EBAAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed Regulations respecting communications with
and services to the public in either official language.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-946238 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Official Languages
    Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[CMA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CMA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a census metropolitan area,
    excluding Ottawa-Gatineau, as used by Statistics Canada for the
    purposes of the most recent decennial census of population. ([région
    métropolitaine de recensement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[CSD]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CSD]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a census subdivision, excluding
    any census subdivision or any part of a census subdivision within
    the National Capital Region, as used by Statistics Canada for the
    purposes of the most recent decennial census of
    population. ([subdivision de recensement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[immigration services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[immigration services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2019-242, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[Method I]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Method I]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2019-242, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[regional economic development agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regional economic development agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    the Atlantic Canada Opportunities Agency, the Economic Development
    Agency of Canada for the Regions of Quebec, the Canadian Northern
    Economic Development Agency, the Federal Economic Development Agency
    for Southern Ontario, the Federal Economic Development Initiative
    for Northern Ontario and the Department of Western Economic
    Diversification. ([organisme de développement économique
    régional]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[route]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[route]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of paragraphs 7(4)(c) and (d), a
      route on which a federal institution provides the travelling
      public with a transportation service by aircraft or train that is
      carried out by a single conveyance, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsection 7(2) and paragraph
      7(4)(e), a route on which a federal institution provides the
      travelling public with a two-way transportation service by
      aircraft, train or ferry between the starting and finishing points
      of a flight, train run or ferry crossing that is carried out by a
      single conveyance between those two points, with or without
      intermediate stops. ([trajet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 32(2)(a) of the Act and these Regulations, [[English or French
linguistic minority population]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all persons,
in a province in which an office or facility of a federal institution is
located, for whom the first language, or one of the first languages,
learned at home in childhood and still understood, is the minority
official language and those who speak the minority official language at
home, as determined by Statistics Canada based on the published data
from the most recent decennial census of population.

### [Calculation of Population Numbers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-946257 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Part, the number of persons of the English or French linguistic minority
population in a province, CMA, CSD or service area is equal to the total
number of the persons for whom the first language, or one of the first
languages, learned at home in childhood and still understood, is the
minority official language and those who speak the minority official
language at home, as determined by Statistics Canada based on the
published data from the most recent decennial census of population.

### [Specific Circumstances]{.HTitleText2} {#h-946314 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 22(b) of the Act, there is significant demand
  for communications with and services from an office or facility of a
  federal institution in an official language where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the services provided by the office or facility are
    provided to a restricted clientele, the members of which are
    identifiable, those services are specifically intended for that
    clientele and at that office or facility over a year at least 5 per
    cent of the demand from that clientele for those services is in that
    language;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the office or facility provides ship-to-shore
    communications services, including coast radio station services and
    vessel traffic services, and at that office or facility over a year
    at least 5 per cent of the demand from the public for those services
    is in that language;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2019-242, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the office or facility is located at a point of
    entry into Canada, other than an airport or a ferry terminal, in
    Ontario, Quebec or New Brunswick, and at that office or facility
    during a year at least 5% of the demand from the public for services
    is in the minority official language;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the office or facility provides search and rescue
    services from a vessel that has long-range capabilities or from an
    aircraft, the vessel or aircraft from which the service is provided
    is distinctively marked by the Department of National Defence or the
    Canadian Coast Guard as a search and rescue vessel or aircraft or is
    crewed by the Department of National Defence with personnel
    specially trained for search and rescue operations, and at that
    office or facility over a year at least 5 per cent of the demand
    from the public for those services is in that language; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the office or facility is a Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police detachment that provides services in a province to sections
    of the Trans-Canada Highway where there is a point of entry to
    another province that is officially bilingual, and over a year at
    least 5 per cent of the demand from the public for those services is
    in that language.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph 22(b) of the Act, there
  is significant demand for communications with and services from an
  office or facility of a federal institution in both official languages
  where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the office or facility provides ship-to-shore
    communications services, including coast radio station services and
    vessel traffic services, and the service area of the office or
    facility includes all or a portion of the Bay of Fundy, the St.
    Lawrence River or the Gulf of St. Lawrence up to the innermost limit
    of Cabot Strait, but not including Cabot Strait, and up to the
    southern limit of the Strait of Belle Isle, but not including the
    Strait of Belle Isle;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the office or facility provides air traffic control
    services and related advisory services in circumstances in which
    either official language may be used in accordance with sections
    602.133 and 602.134 of the [Canadian Aviation
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the office or facility is located at a point of
    entry into Canada, other than an airport or ferry terminal, in
    Ontario, Quebec or New Brunswick, and at that point of entry at
    least 500,000 persons come into Canada during a year; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the office or facility provides search and rescue
    services from a vessel that has long-range capabilities or from an
    aircraft, the vessel or aircraft from which the service is provided
    is distinctively marked by the Department of National Defence or the
    Canadian Coast Guard as a search and rescue vessel or aircraft or is
    crewed by the Department of National Defence with personnel
    specially trained for search and rescue operations, and the office
    or facility provides those services

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in or over Ontario, Quebec or New Brunswick,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in or over Hudson Bay, Hudson Strait or James
      Bay, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in or over an area that falls within the
      boundaries of the Halifax Search and Rescue Region as set out in
      Annex 3B of the National Search and Rescue Manual, published by
      the Department of National Defence and the Canadian Coast Guard,
      as amended from time to time.

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 23(1) of the Act, there is significant demand
  for services to the travelling public, other than air traffic control
  services and related advisory services, from an office or facility of
  a federal institution in both official languages if the facility is an
  airport, railway station or ferry terminal or the office is located at
  an airport, railway station or ferry terminal and at that airport,
  railway station or ferry terminal over a year at least 5% of the
  demand from the public for services is in the minority official
  language.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 23(1) of the Act,
  there is significant demand for services to the travelling public from
  an office or facility of a federal institution in an official language
  where the office or facility provides those services on a route and on
  that route over a year at least 5 per cent of the demand from the
  travelling public for services is in that language.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 23(1) of the Act,
  there is significant demand for services to the travelling public,
  other than air traffic control services and related advisory services,
  from an office or facility of a federal institution in both official
  languages where the facility is an airport or the office is located in
  an airport and over a year the total number of emplaned and deplaned
  passengers at that airport is at least 1,000,000.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 23(1) of the Act,
  there is significant demand for services to the travelling public from
  an office or facility of a federal institution in both official
  languages where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the facility is a railway station that serves the
    travelling public and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is located in a CMA that has at least 5,000
      persons of the English or French linguistic minority population,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is located outside a CMA and within a CSD that
      has at least 500 persons of the English or French linguistic
      minority population and the number of those persons is equal to at
      least 5 per cent of the total population of the CSD;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the facility is a ferry terminal located in Canada
    and over a year the total number of arriving and departing
    passengers at that ferry terminal is at least 100,000;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the office or facility provides those services on
    board an aircraft

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on a route that starts, has an intermediate stop
      or finishes at an airport located in the National Capital Region,
      the CMA of Montreal or the City of Moncton or in such proximity to
      that Region, CMA or City that it primarily serves that Region, CMA
      or City,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on a route that starts and finishes at airports
      both of which are located in Ontario, Quebec or New Brunswick, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} on a route that starts and finishes at airports
      that are located in two of those provinces;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the office or facility provides those services on
    board a train

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on an interprovincial route that starts in,
      finishes in or passes through Ontario, Quebec or New Brunswick, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on a route that starts and finishes at railway
      stations both of which are located in Ontario, Quebec or New
      Brunswick; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the office or facility provides those services on
    board a ferry on a route on which over a year there are at least
    100,000 passengers.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 23(1) of the Act,
  there is significant demand for services to the travelling public,
  other than air traffic control services and related advisory services,
  from an office or facility of a federal institution in both official
  languages if the office or facility is a train station or airport and
  is located in a provincial or territorial capital or the office or
  facility is located in an airport that is located in a provincial or
  territorial capital.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Nature of the Office]{.HTitleText1} {#h-946350 .Part}

### [Health, Safety and Security of the Public]{.HTitleText2} {#h-946351 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 24(1)(a) of the Act, the circumstances that relate to the
health, safety or security of members of the public are the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where an office or facility of a federal institution
  provides emergency services, including first aid services, in a clinic
  or health care unit at an airport, railway station or ferry terminal;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where an office or facility of a federal institution
  uses signage that includes words or standardized public announcements
  regarding health, safety or security in respect of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} passengers on aircraft, trains or ferries,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} members of the public at airports, railway
    stations or ferry terminals, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} members of the public in or on the grounds of
    federal buildings; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where an office or facility of a federal institution
  uses written notices or signage that includes words for alerting the
  public to hazards of a radioactive, explosive, chemical, biological or
  environmental nature or to other hazards of a similar nature.

### [Location of the Office]{.HTitleText2} {#h-946359 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 24(1)(a) of the Act, the circumstances that relate to the
location of an office or facility of a federal institution are the
following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the office or facility is located in a
  [[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
  [Canada National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01) or on land set aside as
  a national historic site under section 42 of that Act and the office
  or facility does not provide the services referred to in paragraph
  (b);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the office or facility is located in a park or
  on land referred to in paragraph (a), the office or facility is one of
  two or more offices or facilities in the park or on the land that
  provide the services of a post office and those services are not
  available in both official languages at at least one of those offices
  or facilities;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the office or facility is located in such
  proximity to a park or land referred to in paragraph (a) that it
  provides specific services for visitors to the park or land and those
  services are not available in that park or on that land;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the office or facility is located in the Yukon
  Territory, serves the public generally and, of all offices or
  facilities of the institution in the Yukon Territory, is the office or
  facility at which over a year there is the greatest number of persons
  using the French language to request services;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} where the office or facility is located in the
  Northwest Territories, serves the public generally and, of all offices
  or facilities of the institution in the Northwest Territories, is the
  office or facility at which over a year there is the greatest number
  of persons using the French language to request services; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} where the office or facility is located in Nunavut,
  serves the public generally and, of all offices or facilities of the
  institution in Nunavut, is the office or facility at which during a
  year there is the greatest number of persons using the French language
  to request services.

### [National or International Mandate of the Office]{.HTitleText2} {#h-946366 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 24(1)(a) of the Act, the circumstances that relate to the
national or international mandate of an office of a federal institution
are the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the office is a diplomatic mission or consular
  post or an office of the Department of Citizenship and Immigration
  that is located in a diplomatic mission or consular post;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the office is responsible for organizing or
  hosting an exposition, fair, exhibition, competition or game of
  national or international scope that is open to the public;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the office participates in an event referred to
  in paragraph (b); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the office is located in a province at a point
  of entry into Canada, other than an airport, and which, among those
  points of entry into Canada for the same province, has the greatest
  number of persons coming into Canada during a year.

### [Other Circumstances]{.HTitleText2} {#h-946373 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 24(1)(b) of the Act, the circumstances in which it is
reasonable that communications with and services from an office or
facility of a federal institution be available in both official
languages are the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the office or facility serves one or more
  entire provinces and those services are

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} correspondence services,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} offered by means of toll-free long-distance
    telephone services, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} offered by means of local telephone services, if
    the office or facility provides the same services by means of
    toll-free long-distance telephone services;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where those communications and services are made
  available by the office or facility through an automated system
  accessible to the public and the communications and services are
  directly related to the operation of the system or consist of
  providing material or information that originated with the
  institution;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where those communications and services are the
  provision in an airport, railway station or ferry terminal of signage,
  including information display systems with respect to aircraft, train
  or ferry transportation services or baggage pick-up; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where those communications and services are offered
  by the office or facility by means of video conferencing services.

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Contract for Services to the Travelling Public]{.HTitleText1} {#h-946381 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 23(2) of the Act, services to the
  travelling public are the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} restaurant, cafeteria, car rental, travel
    insurance, ground transportation dispatch, foreign exchange, duty
    free shop and hotel services;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} self-service equipment, including automated banking
    machines and vending machines, and the provision of instructions for
    the use of public telephones and electronic games; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} passenger screening and boarding services, public
    announcements and the provision of other information to the public,
    and carrier services, including counter services for tickets and
    check-in but excluding carrier services in respect of buses provided
    at railway stations or ferry terminals.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a service referred to in subsection (1) is
  provided by means of printed or pre-recorded material, such as signs,
  notices and menus, car rental contracts and travel insurance policies
  for the travelling public, the material shall be provided in both
  official languages.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a service referred to in subsection (1) is
  provided by means other than those referred to in subsection (2), the
  service shall be offered to the travelling public by such means as
  will enable any member of that public to obtain those services in the
  official language of his or her choice.

## [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Ten-Year Review]{.HTitleText1} {#h-946389 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must ensure
that a review of these Regulations and their administration and
operation is conducted 10 years after this section comes into force and
every 10 years after that, and must cause a report on the review to be
laid before each House of Parliament on any of the first 30 days on
which that House is sitting after the report is completed.
