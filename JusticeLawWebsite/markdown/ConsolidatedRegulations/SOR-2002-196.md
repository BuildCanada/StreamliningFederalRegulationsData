## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-684188 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in Schedule
    III to the [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[experts performing missions for the Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[experts performing missions for the
    Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means governmental or
    intergovernmental experts who are invited by the Organization to
    attend the Meeting and the preparatory meetings of the Ministers of
    Foreign Affairs and Ministers of Finance. ([experts en mission pour
    l'Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Meeting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Meeting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the meeting of the
    Organization to be held in Kananaskis, Alberta from June 26 to
    27, 2002. ([réunion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the intergovernmental
    conference of the eight leading industrialized countries, also known
    as the G8. ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[preparatory meetings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[preparatory meetings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the meetings of
    the Ministers of Foreign Affairs of the eight leading industrialized
    countries to be held in Whistler, British Columbia from June 12 to
    13, 2002 and the meetings of the Ministers of Finance of those
    countries to be held in Halifax, Nova Scotia from June 14 to
    15, 2002. ([réunions préparatoires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-684195 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During
  the period beginning on June 5, 2002 and ending on July 3, 2002, the
  Organization shall have in Canada the legal capacity of a body
  corporate and the privileges and immunities set out in Article II,
  Sections 2 to 5 of the Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} During the period beginning on June 5, 2002 and
  ending on July 3, 2002, representatives of states and governments that
  are members of the Organization shall have in Canada, to such extent
  as may be required for the exercise of their functions in Canada in
  relation to the Meeting and the preparatory meetings, the privileges
  and immunities set out in Sections 11, 12 and 14 to 16 of Article IV
  of the Convention.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} During the period beginning on June 5, 2002 and
  ending on July 3, 2002, experts performing missions for the
  Organization shall have in Canada, to such extent as may be required
  for the exercise of their functions in Canada in relation to the
  Meeting and the preparatory meetings, the privileges and immunities
  set out in Article VI of the Convention.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-684200 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
