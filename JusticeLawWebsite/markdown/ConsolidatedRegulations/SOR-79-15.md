## Handling of Carloads of Explosives on Railway Trackage Regulations {#handling-of-carloads-of-explosives-on-railway-trackage-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/79-15
:::

[RAILWAY SAFETY ACT](/eng/acts/R-4.2)

Registration 1978-12-22

Regulations Governing the Handling of Carloads of Explosives on Railway
Company Team or Spur Tracks
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
C.T.C. 1978-11 RAIL

The Canadian Transport Commission, pursuant to Sections 295 and 296 of
the Railway Act and Section 46 of the National Transportation Act,
hereby makes the [Regulations Governing the Handling of Carloads of
Explosives on Railway Company Team or Spur
Tracks](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-15), in accordance with the schedule
hereto.

These regulations shall come into force six (6) months from the date
hereof.

Dated at Ottawa, this 21st day of December, 1978
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-864729 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Handling of Carloads of Explosives on Railway Trackage
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-15).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-864731 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Transport
      Commission; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Railway Transport
      Committee of the Commission; ([Comité]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[explosives]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[explosives]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any compound, mixture or
      substance the common purpose of which is to function by detonation
      as prescribed by the Committee for the purposes of these
      Regulations; ([explosifs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[handling site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[handling site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a point on a team or
      spur track where a rail car is placed for loading or unloading;
      ([lieu de manutention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[hazard area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[hazard area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area surrounding a
      handling site that may reasonably be expected to suffer serious
      damage, if the maximum quantity of explosives authorized for that
      handling site were to explode; ([zone dangereuse]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[shipper or consignee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[shipper or consignee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person in
      charge of an explosives handling operation or his agent;
      ([expéditeur ou destinataire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[team or spur track]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[team or spur track]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a track on
      railway property that is available to the general public for
      loading and unloading purposes. ([voie de débord ou embranchement
      industriel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of these Regulations,

  [[danger factors]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[danger factors]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the maximum net weight of explosives proposed
        to be present on a handling site at any one time, whether inside
        or outside of a rail car or other vehicle, or in transfer from
        one vehicle to the other,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of times per year, month or week
        that any quantity of explosives will be loaded or unloaded at a
        handling site,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the maximum number of hours any quantity of
        explosives may be present at a handling site whether in a rail
        car or other vehicle or in transfer from one vehicle to the
        other, and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the distance between a handling site and an
        occupied building or other place or facility specified in
        paragraph 6(2)(b). ([facteurs dangereux]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Handling Site]{.HTitleText1} {#h-864747 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  not more than one carload of explosives at any one time and not more
  than six carloads of explosives per year are to be handled on a
  handling site, the railway company in conjunction with the shipper or
  consignee, shall select as a handling site the most suitable site
  based on the danger factors.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the time a handling site is selected by a railway
  company pursuant to subsection (1), the railway company shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} inform the local fire authority thereof;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the shipper or consignee has obtained
    approval for the site from the Chief Inspector, Explosives Branch,
    Department of Energy, Mines and Resources; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} notify the Committee thereof.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where more than one carload
of explosives at any one time or more than six carloads of explosives
per year are to be handled on a handling site, application for authority
to handle the carloads of explosives at the site shall be made to the
Committee by the railway company on behalf of the shipper or consignee.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  desirable distance between occupied dwellings or places where people
  congregate and a handling site is dependent on the net weight of
  explosives being handled at that handling site.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where it is impossible to find a suitable handling
  site based on all the danger factors, a handling site may be selected
  under section 3 or 4 that affords an acceptable risk by virtue of the
  site's topographical features.

<!-- -->

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  application made pursuant to section 4 shall set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the following particulars in respect of the
    location of the handling site:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the railway company,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the railway subdivision,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the railway mileage point, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the name of the hamlet, village, town or city in
      which or nearest to which such site is located;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} full information concerning the danger factors; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} evidence that the appropriate local fire authority
    has been informed as to the proposed use of the handling site.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information concerning the distance described in
  paragraph 2(2)(d) provided with each application made pursuant to
  section 4 shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be provided in duplicate on a plan, drawn to a
    scale of not less than two hundred feet to one inch, covering the
    hazard area for the maximum quantity of any carload of explosives to
    be handled at any one time, and showing the railway track and
    right-of-way boundaries; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} include the distance between the handling site and
    schools, theatres, hospitals, office buildings, department stores,
    stations, single dwellings, apartment houses, open spaces and other
    places of public assembly, factories, pump houses, storage tanks,
    warehouses, electric power stations, power lines, installations
    already handling other commodities (reactive or otherwise, whether
    in transit or in storage) and the nearest fire hydrant or other
    source of water available for firefighting purposes.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** On receipt of an
application under section 4 the Committee may, by letter of authority,
authorize a shipper or consignee to handle carloads of explosives at the
handling site named in that letter.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[General Provisions when Carloads of Explosives Are on the Handling Site]{.HTitleText1} {#h-864771 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  railway company shall require that all reasonable precautions are
  taken against mishaps on a handling site and in particular shall
  ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} smoking and the use of welding or other equipment
    with an open flame is prohibited on the handling site by suitable
    signs and otherwise;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} suitable signs indicating that access to the area
    is restricted are conspicuously posted at the handling site;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} locked derails are applied to the railway track or
    that switches are locked using private padlocks to prevent the car
    from being contacted by other cars from either direction;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} when a consignee breaks the seal of a carload of
    explosives while taking delivery, or when a shipper is loading a
    carload of explosives, all car doors are securely padlocked whenever
    the car is unattended during meal periods or other interruptions of
    operations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the handling site and immediate surrounding area
    are kept free of litter and other fire hazards;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} fire extinguishers, with an effective total rating
    of at least two 10 B:C or the equivalent first aid fire protection,
    are readily available at the site;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if explosives are imminently threatened by fire,
    the hazard area is to be evacuated immediately;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} damaged packages of explosives and any material
    escaping therefrom are removed from railway property without delay;
    and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any fire, explosion or similar incident at a
    handling site is reported immediately to the railway company by the
    person in charge of the handling operation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every railway company shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immediately on receipt of a report under
    paragraph (1)(i), report to the Director, Safety and Standards,
    Railway Transport Committee, by telephone at the number specified
    from time to time by the Committee, the fire, explosion or similar
    incident reported to the railway company; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 30 days thereafter, send a detailed report
    in writing to the Director referred to in paragraph (a).
