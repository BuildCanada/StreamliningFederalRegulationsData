## Order Transferring from the Minister of Employment and Immigration to the Minister of Labour, all the Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister of Employment and Immigration under Certain Acts {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-employment-and-immigration-to-the-minister-of-labour-all-the-powers-duties-and-functions-of-the-minister-of-employment-and-immigration-under-certain-acts .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/95-36
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1995-03-22

Order Transferring from the Minister of Employment and Immigration to
the Minister of Labour, all the Powers, Duties and Functions of the
Minister of Employment and Immigration under Certain Acts

P.C. 1995-373 1995-03-07
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement
and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), all the powers, duties and
functions of the Minister of Labour under any Act of Parliament were
transferred to the Minister of Employment and Immigration by Order in
Council P.C. 1993-1488 of June 25, 1993[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-95-36_e_hq_6606){#fn_SI-95-36_e_hq_6606-ID0EBBBA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-95-36_e_hq_6606-ID0EBBBA){#fn_SI-95-36_e_hq_6606}SI/93-142,
  1993 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3234
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the
[Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34), is pleased hereby to transfer from the Minister of
Employment and Immigration to the Minister of Labour all the powers,
duties and functions of the Minister of Employment and Immigration under

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the [Canadian Centre for Occupational Health and
  Safety Act](/eng/acts/C-13);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} section 16 of the Corporations and Labour Unions
  Returns Act;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Department of Labour Act, other than sections 2
  and 5;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the [Fair Wages and Hours of Labour
  Act](/eng/acts/L-4);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the [Government Employees Compensation
  Act](/eng/acts/G-5);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} paragraphs 28(2)(d) and 43(2)(a) of the [Hazardous
  Materials Information Review Act](/eng/acts/H-2.7);

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} [An Act respecting the Hudson Bay Mining and Smelting
  Co., Limited](/eng/acts/H-5.6), chapter 62 of the Statutes of Canada,
  1947;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the [Merchant Seamen Compensation
  Act](/eng/acts/M-6);

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} section 9 of the [Non-smokers' Health
  Act](/eng/acts/N-23.6);

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} Part II of the [Status of the Artist
  Act](/eng/acts/S-19.6);

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} the [Wages Liability Act](/eng/acts/W-1); and

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} the [West Coast Ports Operations Act,
  1994](/eng/acts/W-6.4).
