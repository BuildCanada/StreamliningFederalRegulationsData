## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-523240 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), these Regulations apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any ship engaged in loading or unloading, or
    handling cargo, bunker coal, ship's stores and ship and cargo
    fittings in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any shore based derrick, crane or other loading or
    unloading equipment and any gear attached to that equipment when the
    equipment is being used in the processes; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any area on shore that is within the scope of any
    derrick, crane or other hoisting equipment used in loading or
    unloading a ship and the immediate approaches to such an area not
    including any sheds or warehouses or any part of a wharf forward or
    aft of the ship's mooring lines.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any fishing vessel; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any ship, where on account of the small quantity of
    cargo handled at any time it is not necessary to employ more than 10
    workers in the processes and where the weight hoisted at any one
    time does not exceed 455 kg.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-784, s. 1
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding anything
contained in these Regulations, the Board may, if satisfied that it can
with propriety do so, exempt any ship or owner of cargo gear from full
compliance with any of the requirements of these Regulations.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Compliance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-523252 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as provided in subsection 11(4), the owner, master or officer in
  charge of a ship shall comply with Part III.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2007-128, s. 401\]]{.Repealed}

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who by himself, his agents or workers
  carries on the processes, and all persons employed by him in the
  processes, shall comply with Part V.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The owner, master or officer in charge of a ship
  shall comply with section 42 so far as it concerns

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any hatch not taken over by the persons, agents,
    workers or persons employed for the purposes of the processes, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any hatch that, after having been taken over by the
    persons, agents, workers or persons employed, for the purposes of
    the processes,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has been reported by written notice, in the
      prescribed form, to the owner, master or officer in charge of the
      ship, by or on behalf of the persons, agents, workers or persons
      employed, as being a hatch at which the processes have been
      completed, or completed for the time being, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has been left by the said persons, agents,
      workers or persons employed, fenced and covered as required by
      section 42, or has been taken into use by or on behalf of the
      owner of the ship, and in either case has been reported by the
      written notice referred to in subparagraph (i),

  and the said owner, master or officer in charge of the ship shall give
  immediately a written acknowledgement, in the prescribed form, of the
  written notice referred to in paragraph (b).

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Life Saving Equipment Required]{.HTitleText1} {#h-523265 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  ship is loading or unloading from or to a boom, raft or crib, the
  employer of the workers engaged in the processes shall keep in the
  water as near as practicable to the working place, a life saving boat
  equipped with oars, a boat hook, and an approved lifebuoy having 27 m
  of heaving line attached thereto.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the speed of the current in the water where the
  loading or unloading from or to a boom, raft or crib is being carried
  on is three knots or more, the life saving boats referred to in
  subsection (1) shall, in addition to the equipment mentioned in that
  subsection, be manned and fitted with a motor.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-784, s. 2
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where a ship is loading or
unloading from or to an unfenced open scow or a fenced scow where the
fencing does not extend to at least 760 mm above the load, the employer
of the workers engaged in such loading or unloading

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall provide on or near the scow, at least one
  approved lifebuoy having 27 m of line attached thereto; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the speed of the current in the water is three
  knots or more, shall keep in the water, as near as practicable to the
  working place, a manned life saving boat fitted with a motor and
  equipped with oars and a boat hook.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-784, s. 3
:::

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Safe Means of Passage and Access]{.HTitleText1} {#h-523276 .Part}

### [Safe Means between Ship and Wharf]{.HTitleText2} {#h-523277 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Safe
  means shall be provided for the passage of workers to and from a ship
  and the wharf at which the ship lies, unless conditions are such that
  the workers would not be exposed to undue risk if no special
  appliances were provided.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The safe means required by subsection (1) are as
  follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where practicable, a ship's accommodation ladder,
    gangway or similar construction, which shall be

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} not less than 560 mm wide,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} properly secured, and securely fenced throughout
      to a clear height of not less than 915 mm by means of upper and
      lower rails, taut ropes or chains, or by other equally safe means,
      except that in the case of the ship's accommodation ladder such
      fencing shall be necessary on one side only, if the other side is
      protected by the ship's side,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} constructed of suitable material, in good
      condition and suitable for the purpose, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} so treated as to prevent workers slipping
      thereon; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any case other than a case referred to in
    paragraph (a), a ladder, which shall be of adequate strength and
    length and properly secured to prevent slipping.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In cases where the gangway or ladder rests on or is
  secured to the bulwark, steps or other safe means with a suitable
  handhold shall be provided from bulwark to deck.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A safety net or other satisfactory arrangement shall
  be provided, if required, so that workers accidentally falling from
  the safe means referred to in subsection (1) will not fall between the
  ship and the wharf.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Safety precautions similar to those referred to in
  subsection (4) shall be taken, where required, alongside hatches where
  the processes are being carried on, on the side of the ship next to
  the wharf.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-784, s. 4
:::

### [Safe Means between Ships or other Vessels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-523292 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  ship is loading or unloading from or to a boom, raft, crib or scow,
  safe means of access shall be provided.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the safe means referred to in subsection (1)
  consists of a rope ladder, the ladder

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall be equipped with flat wooden treads; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall be so made as to prevent twisting.

<!-- -->

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  ships or vessels are alongside one another, safe means shall be
  provided for the passage of workers between the said ships or vessels,
  unless conditions are such that the workers would not be exposed to
  undue risk if no special appliances were provided.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The safe means required by subsection (1) shall be
  provided by the ship or the vessel with the highest freeboard.

### [Safe Means from Deck to Hold]{.HTitleText2} {#h-523301 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the processes are carried on in a hold the depth of which exceeds 1.52
  m, safe means of access shall be provided from the deck to the hold.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The safe means of access referred to in
  subsection (1) shall ordinarily be by ladder, properly secured, which
  shall comply with the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it shall provide foothold of a depth, including any
    space behind the ladder, of not less than 115 mm, a width of not
    less than 250 mm, and a firm handhold;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it shall not be recessed under the deck more than
    is reasonably necessary to keep it clear of the cargo hatchway,
    unless there is means of access to the ladder otherwise than
    [via]{.otherLang lang="la"} the cargo hatchway;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (3), ladders shall, so far as
    practicable, be in a continuous vertical line from the upper deck to
    the bottom of the hold, unless a safe landing is provided at the
    lower end of any discontinued ladder to prevent a descending worker
    from stepping off the bottom rung into space; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where a section of a ladder ends at a hatchway
    coaming, arrangements for secure handhold and foothold (e.g. cleats
    or cups) shall be provided on such coaming; such foothold to have a
    depth, including any space behind the said arrangements, of not less
    than 115 mm, with a width of not less than 250 mm.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The safe means of access referred to in
  subsection (1) may be provided by a sloping ladder or stairway,
  provided the ladder or stairway meets the requirements of paragraph
  (2)(a) and subsection (4).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the processes are carried on in the hold of an
  undecked vessel, the employer of the workers shall provide the safe
  means referred to in subsection (1), which safe means shall,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it consists of a ladder, be equipped with
    adequate means for securing the ladder; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it consists of a rope ladder, also comply with
    the requirements of section 9.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} There shall be room to pass between any winch or
  other obstruction and the means of access referred to in subsection
  (1).

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Shaft tunnels shall be equipped with adequate
  handhold or foothold on each side.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-784, s. 5
:::

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  safe means required by subsection 8(1) and by sections 9 and 10, and
  all places at which workers are employed, or to which they may be
  required to proceed in the course of their employment, shall be
  efficiently lighted while the processes are being carried on.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The lighting required by subsection (1) shall not
  interfere with the navigation of other vessels.

<!-- -->

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Suitable gear shall be provided for
  lifting removable hatch beams on and off.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The suitable gear shall render it unnecessary for
  workers to go upon the beam.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Removable hatch covers
and beams shall be marked to identify the deck, hatch and hatch section
to which they belong, unless interchangeability renders all or some of
such information unnecessary.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** To ensure the safety of
the workers, hatch beams and covers shall be maintained in good
condition.

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Adequate hand grips shall be
  provided on portable hatch covers.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Non-portable, removable hatch covers shall be
  provided with safe means for lifting them off and on.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Hatch coverings of the hinged or folding type shall
  be fitted with a locking device or other means of preventing
  accidental closure.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Locking devices for portable hatch beams shall be
  kept in good order.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Working space around a
hatch shall be not less than 915 mm wide, unless provision is made to
enable workers to remove and replace hatch covers and beams in safety.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-784, s. 6
:::

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a worker is required to go outside of the rails or bulwarks to secure
  deck cargo or for any other purpose connected with the processes,
  means to ensure the safety of the worker shall be provided.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where deck cargo is stowed so close to the ship's
  side and at such a height that the rails or bulwarks do not protect
  workers from falling overboard, means to enable the attachment of the
  derrick guys without the need for a worker to go outboard of the deck
  cargo shall be provided.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Except in cases where the
special design of the lifting machinery renders provision unnecessary,
fixed derrick booms shall be fitted with suitable guy preventers which
shall be secured to the head of the derrick boom independently of other
fittings.

## [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Safety Measures During Processes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-523354 .Part}

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** Precautions shall be
taken to facilitate the escape of workers employed in cargo spaces or
bunkers in the handling of coal or bulk cargo.

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  cargo gear shall be loaded beyond the safe working load.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No load shall be left suspended from any lifting
  machinery unless a capable person is present in charge of the
  machinery while the load is so left.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Derrick guys and preventers shall be secured only to
  such eye pads or other fittings as are suitable for the intended
  loads.

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** Only capable and reliable
persons shall operate lifting machinery or transporting machinery, give
signals to a driver of such machinery, or attend to cargo falls on winch
ends or winch drums.

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** Where goods are placed on
a wharf,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a clear passage leading to the safe means of access
  required by section 8 shall be maintained; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where workers are required to go between the goods
  and the edge of the wharf, a space at least 915 mm in width, clear of
  obstructions other than fixed structures, plant, and appliances in
  use, shall be maintained.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-784, s. 9
:::

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At
  hatches at which the processes are being carried on, free passage to
  the safe means of access required by section 11 shall not be impeded
  by cargo or other equipment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Cargo shall be so stowed that the safe means of
  access required by section 11 shall not be obstructed.

<!-- -->

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Staging used in the processes shall
  be adequately constructed, supported, and where necessary, fastened.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Staging used for carrying cargo between ship and
  shore shall not be so steep as to be unsafe.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Staging that is slippery shall be sanded or otherwise
  made safe.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a forklift truck is operating on wooden hatch
  covers, means shall be taken to distribute the weight so that a single
  hatch cover is not required to bear any wheel.

<!-- -->

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} While
  workers are on a ship for the purposes of the processes, any hatchway
  of a cargo or bunker space exceeding 1.52 m in depth that is not in
  use, and the coamings of which are less than 760 mm in height shall be
  fenced to a height of 915 mm or securely covered.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply during mealtimes or
  other short interruptions of work unless it is manifest that a
  dangerous condition exists.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Measures similar to those referred to in
  subsection (1) shall be taken to protect other deck openings that are
  dangerous to workers.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-784, s. 10
:::

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Hatch
  coverings shall not be used in the construction of cargo staging or
  for other purposes that may expose them to damage.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Hatch beams and coverings shall be replaced in their
  correct positions on the hatches.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When hatches, beams and coverings are taken off, they
  shall be placed clear of the hatch coamings and cargo shall not be
  dragged over them.

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** Where the processes are
carried on at an intermediate deck, the hatch at such deck shall be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} completely covered; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} covered to the extent of one section of hatch
  coverings, in which case the opening shall be fenced, and means
  provided to prevent objects from falling into the hold below.

<!-- -->

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the working space in a hold is confined to the hatchway, cargo hooks
  shall not be hooked into bands or fastenings securing the loads.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to breaking out or
  making up slings.

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** Where the processes are
carried on on a skeleton deck, adequate staging shall be provided,
unless the space beneath such deck is filled to within 610 mm of such
deck.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-784, s. 11
:::

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** Where stacking,
unstacking, stowing or unstowing, or handling in connection with the
processes cannot be otherwise safely carried out, measures shall be
taken, by shoring or other means, to guard against accident.

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  beams of any hatch in use for the processes shall, if not removed, be
  secured to prevent their displacement.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Hatch coverings of the hinged or folding type shall
  be secured so as to prevent accidental closure.

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** Lines used for hauling
loads into place shall be protected from chafing where passing around
corners.

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** Chains shall not be
shortened by tying knots in them.

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** Shackle pins shall be
secured to prevent backing out.

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), where the
  processes involve the use of a cargo fall at a hatchway, a hatch
  tender or signaller shall be employed for each such fall.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where married falls are used at a hatchway, one hatch
  tender or signaller may be employed for each pair of such falls.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} This section shall not apply in any case where the
  inspector is satisfied that the operator of the lifting machinery has
  a clear and unrestricted view of the processes involved.

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** Where a hatch leading to
a hold more than 1.52 m in depth is not fitted with a permanent hatch
coaming of a height sufficient for the protection of a hatch tender or
signaller, a temporary structure sufficient to give the protection
required shall be erected.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-784, s. 12
:::

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** Where workers are
transported to or from a ship by water for the purposes of the
processes, the vessel used for such transport shall be manned, operated
and equipped in accordance with the regulations pertaining to such
vessel.

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Internal combustion engines shall
  not be used in bunkers or cargo spaces unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} adequate ventilation is provided;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} suitable fire extinguishing equipment is readily
    available;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} exhaust pipes, connections and mufflers are tight;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the exhaust is so directed as not to inconvenience
    the operator; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} no explosives, flammable liquids or gases, or
    similar dangerous material is present.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Operators of internal combustion engines in bunkers
  or cargo spaces shall not work alone.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Carbon monoxide gas concentration in bunkers or cargo
  spaces shall not exceed 100 parts per million.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where internal combustion engines for use in the
  processes are to be brought on board, the officer in charge shall
  first be notified.

<!-- -->

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Forklift trucks shall be fitted with
  overhead guards to protect the operator from a falling load, unless
  the nature of the processes is such as to render such guards
  impracticable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The brakes and steering mechanism of forklift trucks
  shall be maintained in good order.

## [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-523421 .Part}

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  in an emergency, no unauthorized person shall remove or interfere with
  any fencing, gangway, ladder, hatch coverings, life saving boat or
  appliance, lights, first-aid equipment, or other things required by
  these Regulations to be provided.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where anything referred to in subsection (1) is
  removed, such thing shall be restored at the end of the period during
  which its removal was necessary.

<!-- -->

- **[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  in an emergency, no worker shall be required to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} go to or from a ship other than by the safe means
    referred to in sections 8, 9 and 10;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter or leave a cargo space other than by the safe
    means of access referred to in section 11;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} go upon the hatch beams to adjust the gear for
    lifting them on or off;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} go on board a ship that has been fumigated until
    the fumigating officer has certified that it is safe to do so;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} carry on the processes in uncovered holds while
    other work is proceeding above, unless conditions are such that they
    are not exposed to injury from falling objects due to such other
    work; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} operate cargo gear that does not comply with Part
    IV.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except in an emergency, no worker shall be required
  to work with or in proximity to goods that are dangerous to life or
  health by reason of their nature or condition unless all precautions
  have been taken to safeguard the workers, and to advise them of the
  conditions existing.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) also applies to places where the
  dangerous goods referred to therein have been stowed, if the fact of
  such stowage constitutes a risk to workers.

<!-- -->

- **[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  employer shall report any case of serious accident to a worker as soon
  as possible after the occurrence thereof.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report shall be made to the inspector at the port
  where the accident happens, or if it happens at a port where there is
  no inspector, to the Chairman, and the report shall set out in detail
  all circumstances of the accident.
