## Railway Safety Appliance Standards Regulations {#railway-safety-appliance-standards-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1171
:::

[RAILWAY SAFETY ACT](/eng/acts/R-4.2)

Regulations Respecting Railway Safety Appliance Standards
::::

::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-497321 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Railway Safety Appliance Standards
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1171).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-497323 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all rolling stock constructed or reconstructed and used on railways
owned and operated by railway companies subject to the jurisdiction of
the Commission.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding section 2,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sections 1 to 112 of Schedule I apply in respect of
  box and other house cars built or rebuilt prior to October 1, 1966;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sections 112.1 to 112.44 of Schedule I shall apply in
  respect of box and other house cars built or rebuilt on and after
  October 1, 1966, or under construction prior thereto.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Sections 112.1 to 112.44 of
Schedule I apply to all box and other house cars in service after
December 31, 1982.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/80-751, s. 1
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding section 1
and sections 316 to 349 of Schedule I, sections 349.1 to 349.20 of
Schedule I apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all caboose cars with platforms built new or rebuilt
  after January 1, 1967; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all caboose cars with platforms in service after
  December 31, 1982.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/80-751, s. 2
:::

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  railway company shall submit to the secretary of the Railway Transport
  Committee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before September 1, 1974,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} information respecting the total number and types
      of cars and caboose cars with platforms that will require
      modification by virtue of section 4 and paragraph 5(b), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the time schedule fixed by the company for the
      making of any such modification; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} progress reports on the railway company's
    compliance with the time schedule referred to in subparagraph
    (a)(ii), including information respecting the total number and type
    of the cars and caboose cars with platforms that remains to be
    modified

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} before August 1, 1980, for the period commencing
      on July 1, 1979 and ending on June 30, 1980,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} before February 1, 1981, for the period
      commencing on July 1, 1980 and ending on December 31, 1980,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} before August 1, 1981, for the period
      commencing on January 1, 1981 and ending on June 30, 1981,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} before February 1, 1982 for the period
      commencing on July 1, 1981 and ending on December 31, 1981, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} before August 1, 1982 for the period commencing
      on January 1, 1982 and ending on June 30, 1982.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every railway company shall, not later than February
  1, 1983, submit to the Secretary of the Railway Transport Committee a
  final report containing information respecting

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total number of cars and caboose cars with
    platforms that have been modified as required by virtue of section 4
    and paragraph 5(b); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the action that has been taken in respect of any
    cars or caboose cars with platforms that have not been modified for
    the reason that they are not in service.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No railway company shall put in service any car or
  caboose car with platform that has not been modified as required by
  virtue of section 4 or paragraph 5(b).

- SOR/80-751, s. 3

## [SCHEDULE I]{.scheduleLabel}[(ss. 3, 4 and 5)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-497354 .scheduleLabel}

### [Box and other House Cars]{.HTitleText1} {#box-and-other-house-cars .SchedHeadL1}

#### [Hand Brakes]{.HTitleText2} {#hand-brakes .SchedHeadL2}

##### [Number]{.HTitleText3} {#number .SchedHeadL3}

- - **1** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each box or other house car shall be equipped
    with an efficient hand brake which shall operate in harmony with the
    power brake thereon.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The hand brake may be of any efficient design, but
    shall provide the same degree of safety as the design shown on Plate
    A of Schedule II.

##### [Dimensions]{.HTitleText3} {#dimensions .SchedHeadL3}

- - **2** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The brake shaft shall be not less than 1 1/4
    inches in diameter, of wrought iron or steel without weld.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The brake wheel may be flat or dished, not less
    than 15, preferably 16, inches in diameter, of malleable iron,
    wrought iron or steel.

##### [Location]{.HTitleText3} {#location .SchedHeadL3}

- - **3** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The hand brake shall be so located that it
    can be safely operated while the car is in motion.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The brake shaft shall be located on the end of the
    car to the left of and not less than 17 nor more than 22 inches from
    the centre.

##### [Manner of Application]{.HTitleText3} {#manner-of-application .SchedHeadL3}

- - **4** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There shall be not less than four inches
    clearance around rim of brake wheel.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Outside edge of brake wheel shall be not less than
    four inches from a vertical plane parallel with end of car and
    passing through the inside face of knuckle when closed with coupler
    horn against the buffer block or end sill.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Top brake shaft support shall be fastened with not
    less than 1/2-inch bolts or rivets (See Plate A of Schedule II).

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} A brakeshaft step

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall support the lower end of the brake shaft;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} which will permit the brake chain to drop under
      the brake shaft shall not be used; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in a u-shaped form is preferred (See Plate A of
      Schedule II).

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} Brake shaft shall be arranged with a square fit at
    its upper end to secure the hand brake wheel; said square fit shall
    be not less than 7/8 inch square. Square-fit taper; nominally 2 in
    12 inches (See Plate A of Schedule II).

  - [(6)]{.lawlabel} Brake chain shall be of not less than 3/8,
    preferably 7/16 inch wrought iron or steel, with a link on the brake
    rod end of not less than 7/16, preferably 1/2 inch wrought iron or
    steel, and shall be secured to brake shaft drum by not less than 1/2
    inch hexagon or square-headed bolt. Nut on said bolt shall be
    secured by riveting end of bolt over nut (See Plate A of Schedule
    II).

  - [(7)]{.lawlabel} Lower end of brake shaft shall be provided with a
    trunnion of not less than 3/4, preferably one inch in diameter
    extending through brake shaft step and held in operating position by
    a suitable cotter or ring (See Plate A of Schedule II).

  - [(8)]{.lawlabel} Brake shaft drum shall be not less than 1 1/2
    inches in diameter (See Plate A of Schedule II).

  - [(9)]{.lawlabel} Brake ratchet wheel shall be secured to brake shaft
    by a key or square fit; said square fit shall be of not less than 1
    5/16 inches square.

  - [(10)]{.lawlabel} When ratchet wheel with square fit is used
    provision shall be made to prevent ratchet wheel from rising on
    shaft to disengage brake pawl (See Plate A of Schedule II).

  - [(11)]{.lawlabel} Brake ratchet wheel shall be not less than 5 1/4,
    preferably 5 1/2 inches in diameter and shall have not less than 14,
    preferably 16 teeth (See Plate A of Schedule II).

  - [(12)]{.lawlabel} If brake ratchet wheel is more than 36 inches from
    brake wheel, a brake shaft support shall be provided to support this
    extended upper portion of brake shaft; said brake shaft support
    shall be fastened  with not less than 1/2-inch bolts or rivets.

  - [(13)]{.lawlabel} The brake pawl shall be pivoted

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} upon a bolt or rivet not less than 5/8 inch in
      diameter; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} upon a trunnion secured by not less than 1/2-inch
      bolt or rivet.

  - [(14)]{.lawlabel} Where the brake pawl is pivoted as provided under
    paragraph (a) or (b), there shall be a rigid metal connection
    between brake shaft and pivot of pawl.

  - [(15)]{.lawlabel} Brake wheel shall be held in position on brake
    shaft by a nut on a threaded extended end of brake-shaft; said
    threaded portion shall be not less than 3/4 inch in diameter; said
    nut shall be secured by riveting over or by the use of a lock nut or
    suitable cotter.

  - [(16)]{.lawlabel} Brake wheel shall be arranged with a square fit
    for brake shaft in hub of said wheel; taper of said fit, nominally 2
    in 12 inches (See Plate A of Schedule II).

#### [Brake Step]{.HTitleText2} {#brake-step .SchedHeadL2}

- - **5** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If brake step is used, it shall be not less
    than 28 inches in length.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Outside edge shall be not less than eight inches
    from face of car and not less than four inches from a vertical plane
    parallel with end of car and passing through the inside face of
    knuckle when closed with coupler horn against the bufferblock or end
    sill.

##### [Manner of Application]{.HTitleText3} {#manner-of-application-1 .SchedHeadL3}

- **6** Brake step shall be supported by not less than two metal braces
  having a minimum cross-sectional area 3/8 by 1 1/2 inches or
  equivalent, which shall be securely fastened to body of car with not
  less than 1/2-inch bolts or rivets.

#### [Running Boards]{.HTitleText2} {#running-boards .SchedHeadL2}

##### [Number]{.HTitleText3} {#number-1 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **7** [(1)]{.lawlabel} One longitudinal running board.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} On outside-metal roof cars, two latitudinal
    extensions.

##### [Dimensions]{.HTitleText3} {#dimensions-1 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **8** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Longitudinal running board shall be not less
    than 18, preferably 20, inches in width.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Latitudinal extensions shall be not less than 24
    inches in width.

##### [Location]{.HTitleText3} {#location-1 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **9** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Full length of car, centre of roof.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} On outside-metal roof cars there shall be two
    latitudinal extensions from longitudinal running board to ladder
    locations, except on refrigerator cars where such latitudinal
    extensions cannot be applied on account of ice hatches.

##### [Manner of Application]{.HTitleText3} {#manner-of-application-2 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **10** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Running boards shall be continuous from end
    to end and not cut or hinged at any point; the length and width of
    runningboards may, however, be made up of a number of pieces
    securely fastened to saddle blocks with screws or bolts.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The ends of longitudinal running boards shall be
    not less than six nor more than 10 inches from a vertical plane
    parallel with end of car and passing through the inside face of
    knuckle when closed with coupler horn against the buffer block or
    end sill; and if more than four inches from edge of roof of car,
    shall be securely supported their full width by substantial metal
    braces.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Running boards shall be made of wood and securely
    fastened to car.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (3) but subject to the
    following conditions, running boards may be of a material other than
    wood if

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the material and design is of a type accepted by
      the A.A.R. and approved by the Commission; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the construction and installation is in
      accordance with A.A.R. requirements and acceptable to the
      Commission.

#### [Sill Steps]{.HTitleText2} {#sill-steps .SchedHeadL2}

##### [Number]{.HTitleText3} {#number-2 .SchedHeadL3}

- **11** Six.

##### [Dimensions]{.HTitleText3} {#dimensions-2 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **12** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Minimum cross-sectional area 1/2 inch by 1
    1/2 inches, or equivalent, of wrought iron or steel.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Minimum length of tread, 10, preferably 12, inches.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Minimum clear depth, eight inches.

##### [Location]{.HTitleText3} {#location-2 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **13** [(1)]{.lawlabel} One near each end on each side of car, so
    that there shall be not more than 18 inches from end of car to
    centre of tread of sill step.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} One at each end of car, centre of sill step to be
    as nearly central with the ladder as possible.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Outside edge of tread of step shall be not more
    than four inches inside of face of side of car, preferably flush
    with side of car.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Tread shall be not more than 24, preferably not
    more than 22, inches above the top of rail.

##### [Manner of Application]{.HTitleText3} {#manner-of-application-3 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **14** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sill steps exceeding 21 inches in depth
    shall have an additional tread.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sill steps shall be securely fastened with not less
    than 1/2-inch bolts with nuts outside (when possible) and riveted
    over, or with not less than 1/2-inch rivets.

#### [Ladders]{.HTitleText2} {#ladders .SchedHeadL2}

##### [Number]{.HTitleText3} {#number-3 .SchedHeadL3}

- **15** Four.

##### [Dimensions]{.HTitleText3} {#dimensions-3 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **16** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Minimum clear length of tread:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} side ladders, 16 inches; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} end ladders, 14 inches.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Maximum spacing between ladder treads, 19 inches.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Top ladder tread shall be located not less than 12
    nor more than 18 inches from roof at eaves.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Spacing of side ladder treads shall be uniform
    within a limit of two inches from top ladder tread to bottom tread
    of ladder.

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} Maximum distance from bottom tread of side ladder
    to top tread of sill step, 21 inches.

  - [(6)]{.lawlabel} End ladder treads

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall, where construction of car permits, be
      spaced to coincide with treads of side ladders, a variation of two
      inches being allowed; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where construction of car will not permit the
      application of a tread of end ladder to coincide with bottom tread
      of side ladder, the bottom tread of end ladder shall coincide with
      second tread from bottom of side ladder.

  - [(7)]{.lawlabel} Hard wood treads, minimum dimensions 1 1/2 by two
    inches.

  - [(8)]{.lawlabel} Iron or steel treads, minimum diameter 5/8 inch.

  - [(9)]{.lawlabel} Minimum clearance of treads, two, preferably 2 1/2,
    inches.

##### [Location]{.HTitleText3} {#location-3 .SchedHeadL3}

- **17** Ladders shall be located

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one on each side, not more than eight inches from
    right end of car, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one on each end, not more than eight inches from
    left side of car,

  measured from inside edge of ladder-stile or clearance of ladder
  treads to corner of car.

##### [Manner of Application]{.HTitleText3} {#manner-of-application-4 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **18** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Metal ladders without stiles near corners of
    cars shall have foot guards or upward projections not less than two
    inches in height near inside end of bottom treads.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Stiles of ladders, projecting two or more inches
    from face of car, will serve as foot guards.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Ladders shall be securely fastened with not less
    1/2-inch bolts with nuts outside (when possible) and riveted over,
    or with not less than 1/2-inch rivets; 3/8-inch bolts may be used
    for wooden treads that are gained into stiles.

#### [End Ladder Clearance]{.HTitleText2} {#end-ladder-clearance .SchedHeadL2}

- **19** No part of car above end sills within 30 inches from side of
  car, except buffer block, brake shaft, brake wheel, brake step,
  running board or uncoupling lever shall extend to within 12 inches of
  a vertical plane parallel with end of car and passing through the
  inside face of knuckle when closed with coupler horn against the
  buffer block, or end sill, and no other part of end of car or fixtures
  on same above end sills, other than exceptions herein noted, shall
  extend beyond the outer face of buffer block.

#### [Roof Handholds]{.HTitleText2} {#roof-handholds .SchedHeadL2}

##### [Number]{.HTitleText3} {#number-4 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **20** [(1)]{.lawlabel} One over each ladder.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} One right-angle handhold may take the place of two
    adjacent specified roof handholds, if the dimensions and locations
    coincide, and an extra leg is securely fastened to car at point of
    angle.

##### [Dimensions]{.HTitleText3} {#dimensions-4 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **21** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Minimum diameter, 5/8 inch, wrought iron or
    steel.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Minimum clear length, 16 inches.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Minimum clearance, two, preferably 2 1/2, inches.

##### [Location]{.HTitleText3} {#location-4 .SchedHeadL3}

- **22** On roof of car: one parallel to treads of each ladder, not less
  than eight nor more than 15 inches from edge of roof, except that on
  refrigerator cars, where ice hatches prevent, the location may be
  nearer edge of roof.

##### [Manner of Application]{.HTitleText3} {#manner-of-application-5 .SchedHeadL3}

- **23** Roof handholds shall be securely fastened with not less than
  1/2-inch bolts with nuts outside (when possible) and riveted over, or
  with not less than 1/2-inch rivets.

#### [Side Handholds]{.HTitleText2} {#side-handholds .SchedHeadL2}

##### [Number]{.HTitleText3} {#number-5 .SchedHeadL3}

- **24** Four. (Tread of side ladder is a side handhold)

##### [Dimensions]{.HTitleText3} {#dimensions-5 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **25** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Minimum diameter, 5/8 inch, wrought iron or
    steel.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Minimum clear length, 16 inches, preferably 24
    inches.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Minimum clearance, two, preferably 2 1/2, inches.

##### [Location]{.HTitleText3} {#location-5 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **26** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Horizontal: one near each end on each side
    of car.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Side handholds shall be not less than 24 nor more
    than 30 inches above centre line of coupler, except as provided
    above, where tread of ladder is a handhold.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Clearance of outer end of handhold shall be not
    more than eight inches from end of car.

##### [Manner of Application]{.HTitleText3} {#manner-of-application-6 .SchedHeadL3}

- **27** Side handholds shall be securely fastened with not less than
  1/2-inch bolts with nuts outside (when possible) and riveted over or
  with not less than 1/2-inch rivets.

#### [Horizontal End Handholds]{.HTitleText2} {#horizontal-end-handholds .SchedHeadL2}

##### [Number]{.HTitleText3} {#number-6 .SchedHeadL3}

- **28** Eight or more. (Four on each end of car.) (Tread of end ladder
  is an end handhold.)

##### [Dimensions]{.HTitleText3} {#dimensions-6 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **29** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Minimum diameter, 5/8 inch, wrought iron or
    steel.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Minimum clear length, 16 inches, preferably 24
    inches.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (2), a handhold 14
    inches in length may be used where it is impossible to use one 16
    inches in length.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Minimum clearance, two, preferably 2 1/2, inches.

##### [Location]{.HTitleText3} {#location-6 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **30** [(1)]{.lawlabel} One near each side on each end of car, not
    less than 24 nor more than 30 inches above centre line of coupler,
    except as provided above, when tread of end ladder is an end
    handhold.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Clearance of outer end of handhold shall be not
    more than eight inches from side of car.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} One near each side of each end of car on face of
    end sill or sheathing over end sill, projecting outward or downward.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Clearance of outer end of handhold shall be not
    more than 16 inches from side of car.

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} On each end of cars with platform end sills six or
    more inches in width, measured from end post or siding, and
    extending entirely across end of car, there shall be one additional
    end handhold not less than 24 inches in length, located near centre
    of car, not less than 30 nor more than 60 inches above platform end
    sill.

##### [Manner of Application]{.HTitleText3} {#manner-of-application-7 .SchedHeadL3}

- **31** Horizontal end handholds shall be securely fastened with not
  less than 1/2-inch bolts with nuts outside (when possible) and riveted
  over, or with not less than 1/2-inch rivets.

#### [Vertical End Handholds]{.HTitleText2} {#vertical-end-handholds .SchedHeadL2}

##### [Number]{.HTitleText3} {#number-7 .SchedHeadL3}

- **32** Two on full-width platform end sill cars, as heretofore
  described.

##### [Dimensions]{.HTitleText3} {#dimensions-7 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **33** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Minimum diameter, 5/8 inch, wrought iron or
    steel.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Minimum clear length, 18, preferably 24, inches.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Minimum clearance, two preferably 2 1/2, inches.

##### [Location]{.HTitleText3} {#location-7 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **34** [(1)]{.lawlabel} One on each end of car opposite ladder, not
    more than eight inches from side of car.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Clearance of bottom end of handhold shall be not
    less than 24 nor more than 30 inches above centre line of coupler.

##### [Manner of Application]{.HTitleText3} {#manner-of-application-8 .SchedHeadL3}

- **35** Vertical end handholds shall be securely fastened with not less
  than 1/2-inch bolts with nuts outside (when possible) and riveted
  over, or with not less than 1/2-inch rivets.

#### [Uncoupling Levers]{.HTitleText2} {#uncoupling-levers .SchedHeadL2}

##### [Number]{.HTitleText3} {#number-8 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **36** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Two.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Uncoupling levers may be either single or double,
    and of any efficient design.

##### [Dimensions]{.HTitleText3} {#dimensions-8 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **37** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Handles of uncoupling levers, except those
    shown on Plate B of Schedule II, or of similar designs, shall be not
    more than six inches from sides of car.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Uncoupling levers of design shown on Plate B of
    Schedule II and of similar designs, shall conform to the following
    prescribed limits:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} handles shall be not more than 12, preferably
      nine, inches from sides of cars;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} centre lift-arms shall be not less than seven
      inches long;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} centre of eye at end of centre lift-arm shall be
      not more than 3 1/2 inches beyond centre of eye of uncoupling pin
      of coupler when horn of coupler is against the buffer block or end
      sill (See Plate B of Schedule II);

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} ends of handles shall extend not less than four
      inches below bottom of end sill or shall be so constructed as to
      give a minimum clearance of two inches around handle (See Plate B
      of Schedule II);

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} minimum drop of handles shall be 12 inches;
      maximum, 15 inches over all (See Plate B of Schedule II);

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} handles of uncoupling levers of the rocking or
      push-down type shall be not less than 18 inches from top of rail
      when lock block has released knuckle, and a suitable stop shall be
      provided to prevent inside arm from flying up in case of breakage.

##### [Location]{.HTitleText3} {#location-8 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **38** [(1)]{.lawlabel} One on each end of car; when single lever is
    used it shall be placed on left side of end of car.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Cars built after June 1, 1919 must be equipped with
    coupler operating lever connected directly with coupler lock of lock
    lift without the use of links, clevises or chains.

### [Box or other House Cars Having Centre Sill or Underframe Cushioning Devices with More than Twelve Inches Longitudinal Impact Absorbing Travel]{.HTitleText1} {#box-or-other-house-cars-having-centre-sill-or-underframe-cushioning-devices-with-more-than-twelve-inches-longitudinal-impact-absorbing-travel .SchedHeadL1}

#### [Hand Brakes]{.HTitleText2} {#hand-brakes-1 .SchedHeadL2}

##### [Number]{.HTitleText3} {#number-126 .SchedHeadL3}

- **496** Two double levers, operative from either side.

##### [Dimensions]{.HTitleText3} {#dimensions-121 .SchedHeadL3}

- **497** Rear end levers shall extend across end of tender with handles
  not more than 12, preferably nine, inches from side of tender with a
  guard bent on handle to give not less than two inches clearance around
  handle.

##### [Location]{.HTitleText3} {#location-130 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **498** [(1)]{.lawlabel} One on rear end of tender and one on front
    end of locomotive.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Handles of front end levers shall be not more than
    12, preferably nine, inches from ends of buffer beam, and shall be
    so constructed as to give a minimum clearance of two inches around
    handle.

##### [Manner of Application]{.HTitleText3} {#manner-of-application-121 .SchedHeadL3}

- **499** Uncoupling levers shall be securely fastened with bolts or
  rivets.

#### [Couplers]{.HTitleText2} {#couplers .SchedHeadL2}

- **500** Locomotives shall be equipped with automatic couplers at rear
  of tender and front of locomotive.

#### [Handrails and Steps for Headlights]{.HTitleText2} {#handrails-and-steps-for-headlights-1 .SchedHeadL2}

- - **501** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Locomotives having headlights which cannot
    be safely and conveniently reached from pilot beam or steam chests
    shall be equipped with secure handrails and steps suitable for the
    use of men in getting to and from such headlights and headlight
    equipment.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} A suitable metal end or side ladder shall be
    applied to all tanks more than 48 inches in height, measured from
    the top of end sill, and securely fastened with bolts or rivets.

### [Safety Appliances]{.HTitleText1}[All Locomotive Types]{.HTitleText1} {#safety-appliancesall-locomotive-types .SchedHeadL1}

#### [Pilots]{.HTitleText2} {#pilots .SchedHeadL2}

- **502** Locomotives in Road Service (normal road operation) shall be
  equipped with a pilot that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is located at the front;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a minimum height above the rail of not less
    than three inches and a maximum of not more than six inches;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is securely attached and adequately braced; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is maintained in a safe and suitable condition for
    service.

#### [Pilot Steps]{.HTitleText2} {#pilot-steps .SchedHeadL2}

##### [Number]{.HTitleText3} {#number-127 .SchedHeadL3}

- **503** Two.

##### [Location]{.HTitleText3} {#location-131 .SchedHeadL3}

- **504** One at right and left side of each pilot.

##### [Dimensions]{.HTitleText3} {#dimensions-122 .SchedHeadL3}

- **505** Pilot steps shall be of sufficient width and depth for a man
  to stand on.

##### [Manner of Application]{.HTitleText3} {#manner-of-application-122 .SchedHeadL3}

- **506** Pilot step shall be securely fastened with bolts or rivets.

- **507** Locomotives of a design capable of normal operation in either
  direction, such as Diesel electric or electric units, or any type of
  locomotive regularly assigned to such road service as to necessitate
  the running of tender first shall be equipped at the front and rear
  with a pilot or combination pilot and footboards, except that in the
  case of Diesel "A" and "B" units application is required only on the
  front end of "A" units.

#### [Footboards]{.HTitleText2} {#footboards .SchedHeadL2}

- - **508** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Locomotives in yard switching and transfer
    service shall be equipped with footboards at the front and rear.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The height from the top of rail to top of tread
    shall be not more than 12 inches, nor less than nine inches.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} On locomotives in yard switching service,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} footboards may be constructed in two sections if
      the majority of horizontal space on each side of the coupler is
      protected by the footboards;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} footboards shall be securely fastened to two
      one-inch by four-inch metal brackets, if footboard is not cut or
      notched at any point;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if footboard is cut or notched or in two sections
      not less than four one-inch by three-inch metal brackets shall be
      used, two located on each side of coupler;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} each bracket shall be secured to the buffer-beam,
      end-sill, frame or plate forming the end structure of the
      locomotive or tender by not less than two 7/8-inch bolts.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Metal of serrated anti-skid design, constructed
    with sufficient open area to prevent accumulation of snow and ice on
    the tread surface shall be used.

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} If wood, preferably two inches thick but not less
    than 1 1/2 inches in thickness, is used, the footboard shall not be
    cut or notched at any point, outer corners may be radiused or
    slightly beveled.

  - [(6)]{.lawlabel} The footboards shall be

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} of sufficient width to provide adequate foot
      space, notwithstanding projecting grab irons or other devices; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} not less than 10 inches wide and three feet in
      length.

  - [(7)]{.lawlabel} Footboards shall be located at front and rear,
    extending not less than 18 inches outside gauge of straight track.

  - [(8)]{.lawlabel} Foot stops, not less than four inches in height,
    shall be applied above the tread, at the back and each inner end.

    - ::::: listItemBlock0
      ::: listItemLabel
      [NOTE:]{.lawlabel} 
      :::

      ::: listItemText4
      It is preferable that any openings between the footboards and
      underside of buffer beam or frame be covered for the protection of
      employees.
      :::
      :::::

#### [Combination Pilot and Footboards]{.HTitleText2} {#combination-pilot-and-footboards .SchedHeadL2}

- - **509** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Locomotives operating in road and yard
    switching services shall be equipped at the front and rear with
    combination pilots and footboards.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Footboards shall be metal, serrated anti-skid
    design, constructed with sufficient open area to prevent
    accumulation of snow and ice on the tread surface.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The combination pilot, footboards and associated
    supports shall be of adequate strength and of an appropriate design
    acceptable to the Commission for the requirements of road service,
    maintained in a safe and satisfactory operating condition.

#### [Couplers]{.HTitleText2} {#couplers-1 .SchedHeadL2}

- **510** All locomotives shall be equipped with automatic couplers at
  front and rear.

#### [Uncoupling Levers]{.HTitleText2} {#uncoupling-levers-20 .SchedHeadL2}

##### [Number]{.HTitleText3} {#number-128 .SchedHeadL3}

- **511** Two double levers operative from both sides.

##### [Location]{.HTitleText3} {#location-132 .SchedHeadL3}

- **512** One on front and one on rear of each locomotive.

- **513** Uncoupling lever handles shall be not more than 12, preferably
  nine, inches from side of unit and shall be so constructed as to give
  a minimum clearance of two inches around handle.

### [Locomotives of Special Construction]{.HTitleText1} {#locomotives-of-special-construction .SchedHeadL1}

- **514** Locomotive units of untried type or locomotives not covered
  specifically in the foregoing sections, relative to sill steps, hand
  holds, ladders, running boards, and other safety appliances may be
  considered as being of special construction. All such units shall be
  equipped as near as possible with a full complement of suitable safety
  appliances as required for locomotives of approximately the same type.
