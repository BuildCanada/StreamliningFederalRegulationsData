## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765287 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[cargo vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cargo vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a freighter, container
    vessel, self-unloader, car carrier or bulk carrier classified under
    tariff item No. 8901.90.91 or 8901.90.99 in the List of Tariff
    Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), but does not include a barge or its
    pusher vessel. ([navire de charge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[imported into Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[imported into Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of tankers
    and cargo vessels, means permanently imported into Canada for the
    first time, but does not apply to tankers or cargo vessels produced
    in Canada that have been exported and then subsequently re-imported
    into Canada. ([importé au Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765293 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission of the customs
duties paid or payable under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) is
granted in respect of tankers classified under subheading No. 8901.20 in
the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and cargo vessels.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-140, s. 5
:::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765297 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tanker or cargo vessel was imported into Canada
  on or after January 1, 2010;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made by the importer to the
  Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years
  after the date of importation; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the importer files, on request, all evidence that is
  required by the Canada Border Services Agency to determine eligibility
  for remission.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-140, s. 6
