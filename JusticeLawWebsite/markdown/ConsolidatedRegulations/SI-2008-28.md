## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614003 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted to the
Maritime Harbours Society in respect of a debt owed to Her Majesty in
the amount of \$480,500 arising out of an agreement signed on October
21, 1999 in accordance with the Port Divestiture Program.

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614005 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
the condition that the Maritime Harbours Society provides Her Majesty,
represented by the Minister of Transport, with evidence of the sale of
the Digby Fisherman's Wharf to the Digby Harbour Port Association.
