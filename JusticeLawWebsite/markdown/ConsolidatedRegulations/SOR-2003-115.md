## Regulations Respecting Excise Licences and Registrations {#regulations-respecting-excise-licences-and-registrations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-115
:::

[EXCISE ACT, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1)

Registration 2003-03-27

Regulations Respecting Excise Licences and Registrations

P.C. 2003-389 2003-03-27
::::

::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 304(1) of the
[Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Regulations Respecting Excise Licences and
Registrations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-115).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699701 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Act,
    2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence issued under section
    14, 19, 20 or 22 of the Act. ([licence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registration issued
    under any of sections 15 to 18 of the Act.
    ([autorisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Issuance of Licence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699706 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In order
  to be issued a licence, a person must submit to the Minister a
  completed application, in the form authorized by the Minister,
  accompanied by a list of the premises in respect of which the
  application is being made.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (3) and (4), an applicant is
  eligible for a licence, other than a licence issued under section 22
  of the Act, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are not the subject of a receivership in
    respect of their debts;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they have not, in the five years immediately before
    the date of the application,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} failed to comply with any Act of Parliament,
      other than the Act, or of the legislature of a province respecting
      the taxation of or controls on alcohol, tobacco products, cannabis
      products or vaping products or any regulations made under it, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} acted to defraud Her Majesty;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an applicant who is an individual,
    they

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are at least eighteen years of age, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} have sufficient financial resources to conduct
      their business in a responsible manner;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an applicant that is a partnership
    or unincorporated body,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the partnership or unincorporated body is
      composed only of individuals, each of the individuals meets the
      requirement of subparagraph (c)(i) and the partnership or
      unincorporated body meets the requirement of subparagraph (c)(ii),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the partnership or unincorporated body is
      composed only of corporations, each of the corporations meets the
      requirement of subparagraph (c)(ii), and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where the partnership or unincorporated body is
      composed of both individuals and corporations, each of the
      individuals meets the requirement of subparagraph (c)(i) and the
      partnership or unincorporated body and each of the corporations
      meet the requirement of subparagraph (c)(ii); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an applicant that is a corporation,
    the corporation meets the requirement of subparagraph (c)(ii).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), an applicant for a licence
  issued under section 19 of the Act authorizing them to possess in
  their excise warehouse non-duty-paid packaged alcohol must, in
  addition to meeting the requirements of subsection (2), where the
  province in which the warehouse is located has enacted legislation
  respecting the warehousing of packaged alcohol, be authorized by that
  province or its liquor authority to warehouse the alcohol at that
  warehouse.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The additional requirement referred to in
  subsection (3) does not apply to an applicant who is an alcohol
  licensee that is producing or packaging alcohol in the province where
  the warehouse is located.

## [Issuance of Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699727 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In order
  to be issued a registration, a person must submit to the Minister a
  completed application, in the form authorized by the Minister,
  accompanied by a list of the premises in respect of which the
  application is being made.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An applicant is eligible for a registration if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are not the subject of a receivership in
    respect of their debts; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they have not, in the five years immediately before
    the date of the application,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} failed to comply with any Act of Parliament,
      other than the Act, or of the legislature of a province respecting
      the taxation of or controls on alcohol or tobacco products or any
      regulations made under it, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} acted to defraud Her Majesty.

## [Duration of Licence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699735 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A licence is valid for the
period specified in the licence, which period

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a cannabis licence issued to a person,
  shall end on or before the date of expiry of the licence or permit
  issued to the person under subsection 62(1) of the [Cannabis
  Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) and shall not exceed five years;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vaping product licence, shall not
  exceed three years; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, shall not exceed two years.

## [Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699737 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 23(3)(b) of the Act, the amount of security to
  be provided by an applicant for a spirits licence, a tobacco licence,
  a cannabis licence or a vaping product licence must be an amount of
  not less than \$5,000 and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a spirits licence, be sufficient to
    ensure payment of all amounts in respect of bulk spirits referred to
    in sections 104 to 112 of the Act for which the licensee is or will
    be responsible up to a maximum amount of \$2 million;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a tobacco licence or a vaping
    product licence, be sufficient to ensure payment of the amount of
    duty referred to in paragraph 160(b) of the Act up to a maximum
    amount of \$5 million per licence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a cannabis licence,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the licensee is authorized under subsection
      159.2(2) of the Act to have reporting periods that are calendar
      quarters, be sufficient to ensure payment of one-third of the
      amount of duty referred to in paragraph 160(b) of the Act up to a
      maximum amount of \$5 million per licence, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, be sufficient to ensure
      payment of the amount of duty referred to in paragraph 160(b) of
      the Act up to a maximum amount of \$5 million per licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The types of security that are acceptable for the
  purposes of paragraph 23(3)(b) of the Act are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a bank draft;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a certified cheque;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a Canada Post money order; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a bond issued by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an entity that is licensed or otherwise
      authorized under the laws of Canada or of a province to carry on
      the fidelity or surety class of insurance business in Canada and
      that is recommended to the Treasury Board by the Office of the
      Superintendent of Financial Institutions as an entity whose bonds
      may be accepted by the Government of Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Canadian Payments Association
      referred to in section 4 of the [Canadian Payments
      Act](/eng/acts/C-21),

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that accepts deposits insured by
      the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation or the Régie de
      l'assurance-dépôts du Québec to the maximum permitted by the
      statutes under which those institutions were established,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a credit union as defined in subsection 137(6)
      of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that accepts deposits from the
      public, if repayment of the deposits is guaranteed by Her Majesty
      in right of a province.

## [Notification of Changes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699754 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every licensee and
registrant shall, without delay, notify the Minister in writing of any
changes to any of the information provided under these Regulations.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every licensee shall,
without delay, notify the Minister in writing of any changes to the
licensee's fiscal months.

## [Amendment of Licence or Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699757 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall amend a
licence or registration if the Minister receives a notification pursuant
to section 6 that necessitates the amendment.

## [Renewal of Licence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699759 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In order
  to have a licence renewed, a licensee must submit to the Minister a
  completed renewal application, in the form authorized by the Minister,
  not less than thirty days before the day on which the licence expires.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensee is eligible to have a licence renewed if
  they have not ceased to meet the applicable requirements of section 2.

## [Suspension of Licence or Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699763 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  grounds for the suspension of a licence or registration by the
  Minister are that the licensee or registrant

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fails to meet any of the applicable requirements of
    section 2, 3, 6, 7 or 13;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fails to meet the conditions of the licence or
    registration;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is bankrupt;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} ceases to carry on the business for which the
    licence or registration was issued;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} fails to comply with any Act of Parliament, other
    than the Act, or of the legislature of a province respecting the
    taxation of or controls of alcohol, tobacco products, cannabis
    products or vaping products, or any regulations made under it; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} acts to defraud His Majesty.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, immediately after suspending a
  licence or registration, notify the licensee or registrant in writing
  of the suspension and provide all relevant information concerning the
  grounds for the suspension.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The licensee or registrant may, within 90 days after
  the day on which the licence or registration is suspended, make
  representations to the Minister respecting the reasons why the licence
  or registration should be reinstated.

## [Cancellation of Licence or Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699773 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  grounds for the cancellation of a licence or registration by the
  Minister are that the licensee or registrant

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} requests the Minister in writing to cancel the
    licence or registration;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is bankrupt;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ceases to meet the applicable requirements of
    section 2 or 3, as the case may be;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} ceases to carry on the business for which the
    licence or registration was issued;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} fails to comply with any Act of Parliament, other
    than the Act, or of the legislature of a province respecting the
    taxation of or controls on alcohol, tobacco products, cannabis
    products or vaping products, or any regulations made under it; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} acts to defraud Her Majesty.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, before cancelling a licence or
  registration on any of the grounds referred to in paragraphs (1)(b) to
  (f), give the licensee or registrant 90 days notice of the proposed
  cancellation and provide them with all relevant information concerning
  those grounds.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The licensee or registrant may, within 90 days after
  the day on which the notice referred to in subsection (2) is given,
  make representations to the Minister respecting the reasons why the
  licence or registration should not be cancelled.

## [Facilities, Equipment and Personnel]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699784 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Every licensee and
registrant shall provide, at the premises in respect of which the
licence or registration is issued,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} adequate space for the examination of goods or
  records by an officer;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the equipment and personnel necessary to ensure that
  the goods or records to be examined are made available to the officer;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the personnel necessary to furnish information, for
  audit purposes, to an officer with respect to the operations,
  inventory system and records system of the licensee or registrant.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699789 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations take
effect on April 1, 2003.
