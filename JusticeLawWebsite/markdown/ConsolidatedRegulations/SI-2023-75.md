## Order Acknowledging Receipt of the Assessments Done Under Subsection 23(1) of the Species at Risk Act (Chestnut-collared Longspur and Nine Other Wildlife Species) {#order-acknowledging-receipt-of-the-assessments-done-under-subsection-231-of-the-species-at-risk-act-chestnut-collared-longspur-and-nine-other-wildlife-species .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2023-75
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2023-12-06

Order Acknowledging Receipt of the Assessments Done Under Subsection
23(1) of the Species at Risk Act (Chestnut-collared Longspur and Nine
Other Wildlife Species)

P.C. 2023-1166 2023-11-24
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, acknowledges receipt, on the making of
this Order, of the assessments done pursuant to subsection 23(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-4-4024-E_hq_26007){#fn_81000-4-4024-E_hq_26007-ID0EBBAA}
by the Committee on the Status of Endangered Wildlife in Canada
(COSEWIC) with respect to the species set out in the annexed schedule.
