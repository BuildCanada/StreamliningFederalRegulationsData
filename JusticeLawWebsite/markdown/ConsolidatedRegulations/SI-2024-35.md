## Order Designating the Minister of Labour and the Minister of Employment and Social Development to be the Specified Ministers for the Purposes of the Canadian Sustainable Jobs Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-labour-and-the-minister-of-employment-and-social-development-to-be-the-specified-ministers-for-the-purposes-of-the-canadian-sustainable-jobs-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2024-35
:::

[CANADIAN SUSTAINABLE JOBS ACT](/eng/acts/C-23.25)

Registration 2024-07-17

Order Designating the Minister of Labour and the Minister of Employment
and Social Development to be the Specified Ministers for the Purposes of
the Canadian Sustainable Jobs Act

P.C. 2024-852 2024-06-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under section 5 of the [Canadian Sustainable Jobs
Act](/eng/acts/C-23.25)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#nbp_81000-3-2727_hq_1718835022){#nbp_81000-3-2727_hq_1718835022-ID0EBBAA},
designates the Minister of Labour and the Minister of Employment and
Social Development, members of the King's Privy Council for Canada, to
be the specified Ministers for the purposes of that Act.
