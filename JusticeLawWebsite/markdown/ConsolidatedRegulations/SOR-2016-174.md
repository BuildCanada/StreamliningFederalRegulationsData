## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831626 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[glass door or enclosure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[glass door or enclosure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the following
    products when they are for domestic use:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a bathtub or shower door or enclosure that is
      made of glass or that contains a pane of glass;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a storm door that is made of glass or that
      contains a pane of glass; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an exterior door, other than a storm door, that
      contains a pane of glass that has an area greater than 0.5 m^2^
      and whose lowest edge is less than 900 mm from the bottom edge of
      the door. ([portes et enceintes contenant du
      verre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[laminated glass]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[laminated glass]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means glass that is formed
    when two or more sheets of glass are bonded to an intervening layer
    or layers of plastic material. ([verre feuilleté]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[responsible person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[responsible person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer, in the case of a glass door or
      enclosure that is manufactured in Canada; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the importer, in the case of a glass door or
      enclosure that is imported. ([responsable]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[safety glass]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[safety glass]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means laminated, tempered or
    wired glass. ([verre de sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[safety glass standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[safety glass standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian
    General Standards Board standard CAN/CGSB‑12.1‑M90, entitled
    Tempered or Laminated Safety Glass, published in November
    1990. ([norme sur le verre de sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tempered glass]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tempered glass]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means glass that has been
    treated chemically or thermally so that, on fracture, it
    disintegrates into many small granular pieces. ([verre
    trempé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[wired glass]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wired glass]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means glass into which a wire
    mesh has been embedded. ([verre armé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[wired safety glass standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wired safety glass standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Canadian General Standards Board standard CAN/CGSB‑12.11‑M90,
    entitled Wired Safety Glass, published in November 1990. ([norme sur
    le verre de sécurité armé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Safety Glass Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831642 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The glass that is contained
in a glass door or enclosure must be a safety glass set out in column 1
of the table to this section that, when tested in accordance with the
applicable test referred to in column 2, meets the requirement for that
test set out in column 3.

## [Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831650 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  responsible person must prepare and maintain documents that show that
  a glass door or enclosure meets the requirements of these Regulations
  and must keep those documents for a period of at least four years
  after the day on which the glass door or enclosure is manufactured in
  Canada or the day on which it is imported.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspector's request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The responsible person must provide an inspector with
  any of the documents that the inspector requests in writing, within
  15 days after the day on which the request is received.
