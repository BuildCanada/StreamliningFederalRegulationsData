## Sex Offender Information Registration Regulations (Canadian Forces) {#sex-offender-information-registration-regulations-canadian-forces .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2008-247

Sex Offender Information Registration Regulations (Canadian Forces)

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746247 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[military police]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[military police]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include the
    Canadian Forces National Investigation Service. ([police
    militaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Office of the Provost Marshal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Office of the Provost Marshal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Office of the Canadian Forces Provost Marshal located at National
    Defence Headquarters in Ottawa. ([bureau du grand
    prévôt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sex offender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sex offender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 3(1) of the [Sex Offender Information Registration
    Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7). ([délinquant sexuel]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

- SOR/2014-120, ss. 1(E), 2(F)

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746254 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
a sex offender who is subject to the Code of Service Discipline or who
is an officer, or non-commissioned member, of the primary reserve.

## [Registration Centres]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746256 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of the [Sex Offender Information Registration
  Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7), the following places are designated as
  registration centres:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in Canada,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Office of the Provost Marshal, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the place located on each defence establishment
      set out in the schedule that serves as the station of the military
      police; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} outside Canada, each place that serves as the
    station of military police that carry out law enforcement
    operations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A registration centre serves any sex offender to whom
  these Regulations apply.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-120, s. 2(F)

## [Means of Reporting and Notification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746266 .Part}

### [Reporting]{.HTitleText2} {#h-746267 .Subheading}

#### [First Report]{.HTitleText3} {#h-746268 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 4(3) of the [Sex Offender Information Registration
Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7), the first report by a sex offender under that Act
shall be in person.

#### [Subsequent Report]{.HTitleText3} {#h-746270 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of subsection 4.1(2) of the [Sex Offender Information
  Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7), a report by a sex offender under
  paragraph 4.1(1)(a) or (b) of that Act shall be in person or by
  telephone, facsimile or electronic mail.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection 4.1(2) of the [Sex
  Offender Information Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7), a report by a
  sex offender under paragraph 4.1(1)(c) of that Act shall be in person.

### [Notification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-746274 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of section
6 of the [Sex Offender Information Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7),
any notification that a sex offender is required to provide under that
section shall be in person or by telephone, facsimile or electronic
mail.

## [Persons Authorized to Collect Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746276 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the [Sex
Offender Information Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7), the following
persons are authorized to collect information in relation to sex
offenders to whom these Regulations apply:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the military police; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person employed by the military police or the
  Office of the Provost Marshal, whose duties include the collection of
  information under that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-120, s. 2(F)
:::

## [Persons Authorized to Register Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746282 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the [Sex
Offender Information Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7), any person who
is authorized under section 7 to collect information is authorized to
register information in relation to sex offenders to whom these
Regulations apply.

## [Designated Class of Operations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746284 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** All operations that involve
a unit or other element of the Canadian Special Operations Forces
Command are designated for the purpose of subsection 227.16(1) of the
[National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746286 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Ind140D_hq_5341){#fn_Ind140D_hq_5341-ID0EBCA}[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
An Act to amend the National Defence Act, the Criminal Code, the Sex
Offender Information Registration Act and the Criminal Records Act,
chapter 5 of the Statutes of Canada, 2007, comes into force, but if
these Regulations are registered after that day, they come into force on
the day on which they are registered.
