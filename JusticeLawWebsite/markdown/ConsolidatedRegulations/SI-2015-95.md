## Transfer of Duties Order {#transfer-of-duties-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2015-95
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2015-11-18

Transfer of Duties Order

P.C. 2015-1236 2015-11-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612481-E_hq_7374){#fn_612481-E_hq_7374-ID0EGDAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612481-E_hq_7375){#fn_612481-E_hq_7375-ID0EDDAA},
transfers from the Minister of Transport to the Minister of
Infrastructure, Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs the powers,
duties and functions under the Letters Patent of incorporation for the
establishment of the Windsor-Detroit Bridge Authority issued by Order in
Council P.C. 2012-1350 of October 9, 2012[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-1145-E_hq_16249){#fn_81000-3-1145-E_hq_16249-ID0EBDAA},
except any power, duty or function under section 9.1 of those Letters
Patent, effective November 4, 2015.
