## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508881 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Pension Continuation
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1392).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508883 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
    Pension Continuation Act](/eng/acts/R-10.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[authorized advisor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorized advisor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of the
    Force, a person employed in the public service or a person whose
    services were retained by the Minister for the purposes of the Act.
    ([conseiller autorisé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2013-125, s. 1

## [Service in the Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508889 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  period of service of a person as a special constable in the Force or
  as a member of a provincial police force of a province with which the
  Governor in Council entered into an arrangement under section 5 of the
  Act as it was before the coming into force of the [Royal Canadian
  Mounted Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11), shall be deemed to
  be a period during which that person served in the Force and to be
  service for the purposes of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), any period of service
  of a person as a member of a provincial police force described in
  subsection (1) shall be deemed to be a period during which he served
  in the Force and to be service for the purposes of section 5 of the
  Act only if his service in the Force has been continuous since the
  date of absorption of that police force into the Force and the
  disability sustained during that period, in respect of which the
  application for compensation under section 5 of the Act is made, is a
  disability for which that person is not and was not eligible to
  receive compensation under a law of a province that provides for the
  payment of workmen's compensation.

s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

## [Allowances to Be Included as Pay]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508894 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 34(2) of the Act, the amount of allowances for
  pension purposes to be received by any constable of the Force shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the value of free meals or quarters, or of single
    or double rations, supplied or received as part of the remuneration
    of the constable, determined where applicable as provided from time
    to time for the purposes of any Act of the Parliament of Canada
    respecting income taxes; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} service pay received by him after October 1, 1966,
    as compensation for long service in the Force.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a member has not received an allowance
  specified in subsection (1) due to absence on authorized leave with or
  without pay, the rate of pay for pension purposes shall not be
  reduced.

**[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
section 11 of the Act, the amount of allowances for purposes of pension
to be received by the Commissioner and other officers of the Force shall
include service pay received by them after April 1, 1981.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/84-315, s. 1
:::

## [Royal Canadian Mounted Police (Dependants) Pension Fund]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508903 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 55(3) of the Act, the rate of interest shall be the rate
described in subsection 30(2) of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
Superannuation Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1393) calculated
in respect of each quarter in each fiscal year on the last day of June,
September, December and March on the balance to the credit of the Royal
Canadian Mounted Police (Dependants) Pension Fund on the last day of the
preceding quarter.

## [Full-time Attendance at a School or University Substantially Without Interruption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508905 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 47(8)(b) of the Act, the expression "full-time attendance at a
school or university" shall have the same meaning as in section 19 of
the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1393) and the circumstances
under which attendance at a school or university shall be determined to
be substantially without interruption shall be as specified in section
19 of those Regulations.

## [Election in Respect of Surviving Spouse Pension]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508907 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 9 to 26,

[[election]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[election]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an election under section
    20.1 of the Act; ([choix]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[level of reduction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[level of reduction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
    pension, one of the three amounts determined pursuant to section 17,
    where levels one, two and three correspond to a reduction that
    provides a surviving spouse with a pension that is equal to 30, 40
    or 50 per cent, respectively, of a pension referred to in
    subparagraph 17(b)(i), if paragraph 17(b) were read without
    reference to supplementary benefits payable under the [Supplementary
    Retirement Benefits Act](/eng/acts/S-24); ([niveau de
    réduction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an officer referred to in
    section 20.1 of the Act; ([officier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[spouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[spouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a spouse referred to in section
    20.1 of the Act. ([conjoint]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-348, s. 1
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
20.1 of the Act, an officer may make an election to reduce the amount of
the officer's pension not later than one year after the later of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which this section comes into force, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the officer and the spouse are
  married.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-348, s. 1
:::

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite section 9, an officer may
  make the election after the expiration of the period provided for in
  that section if the officer has received, from an authorized advisor
  who normally gives information about these matters, erroneous or
  misleading information regarding the period during which the officer
  could make an election or materially erroneous or misleading
  information regarding the amount of the reduction of the officer's
  pension or the amount of the pension to which the spouse would be
  entitled and, in making the election, acted on that information.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The election may be made not later than three months
  after the day on which a written notice containing the corrected
  information is sent to the officer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-348, s. 1
- SOR/2013-125, s. 3

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An election shall be made
in writing and shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicate the level of reduction in respect of the
  officer's pension;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} indicate the date of birth of the officer's spouse
  and the date of the spouse's marriage to the officer; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be signed and dated by the officer and signed by a
  witness other than the officer's spouse.

  designated by the Minister, within the period set out in section 9 or
  subsection 10(2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An election is made on the day on which it is sent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The day of the sending of the election is the day on
  which it is delivered or, if it is sent by mail, the day on which it
  is mailed and the postmark is evidence of that day.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The officer, or a person
acting on behalf of the officer, shall, within one year after the day on
which an election is made, send to the Minister or to a person
designated by the Minister

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a document that is evidence of the date of birth of
  the spouse;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a document that is evidence of the marriage of the
  officer and the spouse; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the name of the spouse as indicated on a
  document referred to in paragraph (a) is not the same as the name of
  the spouse as indicated on a document referred to in paragraph (b),
  any other document that confirms that the evidence of the birth and
  the marriage relates to the spouse, or a statutory declaration by
  which the spouse declares that the documents are in respect of the
  spouse.

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Proof
  of age of the officer's spouse is established by a birth certificate
  issued by a civil authority.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a birth certificate referred to in
  subsection (1) cannot be obtained, proof of age of the spouse is
  established by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statutory declaration of the spouse in which the
    spouse attests to the spouse's date of birth and explains the
    reasons why the birth certificate cannot be obtained; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a document that was created

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} within five years after the date of birth of the
      spouse and that indicates the name of the spouse and the date of
      birth or age of the spouse, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at least 20 years before the day on which the
      election is made and that indicates the name of the spouse and the
      date of birth of the spouse, which document is accompanied by a
      statutory declaration by which a person, other than the officer or
      the spouse, attests that the date stated in the document is the
      correct date of birth of the spouse.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a document or statutory declaration referred to
  in paragraph (2)(b) is to be submitted but cannot be obtained, the
  statutory declaration referred to in paragraph (2)(a) shall set out
  the reasons why the document or statutory declaration cannot be
  obtained.

## [Non-compliance with Requirements to Submit Evidence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508960 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** An election is considered
not to have been made where any document or statutory declaration that
is required by any of sections 13 to 15 is not submitted within one year
after the day on which the election is made.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-348, s. 1

## [Calculation of the Reduction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508964 .Part}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The amount of the
reduction of the monthly instalment of the pension of an officer who
makes an election is equal to the amount determined as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} calculate the actuarial present value of the pension
  to which the officer is entitled under Part II of the Act immediately
  before the election, and the supplementary benefits payable in respect
  thereof under the [Supplementary Retirement Benefits
  Act](/eng/acts/S-24);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} convert the amount determined in accordance with
  paragraph (a) into a joint and survivor pension benefit that consists
  of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a pension that is payable, during the life of the
    officer, by monthly instalments beginning on the first day of the
    month after the month in which the election is made, and that is
    determined taking into account the supplementary benefits payable of
    the [Supplementary Retirement Benefits Act](/eng/acts/S-24), as if
    the pension were a pension payable under Part II of the Act, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a pension that is payable during the life of the
    surviving spouse, by monthly instalments beginning on the first day
    of the month after the month in which the officer died and that is
    equal to 30, 40 or 50 per cent, in accordance with the election made
    by the officer, of the pension determined in accordance with
    subparagraph (i), and that is determined taking into account the
    supplementary benefits payable under the [Supplementary Retirement
    Benefits Act](/eng/acts/S-24), as if the pension were a pension
    payable under Part II of the Act; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} subtract the first monthly instalment of the pension
  referred to in subparagraph (b)(i) from the first monthly instalment
  of the pension referred to in paragraph (a), and adjust the amount
  obtained to take into account

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the reduction is effective during the lesser
    of

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the period of the life of the officer,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the period of the life of the spouse, and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the duration of the marriage,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that the reduction becomes effective in the month
    referred to in subsection 22(1) or (2), as the case may be, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that the increase in the reduction under section
    18 will apply to the reduction in each year referred to in that
    section.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-348, s. 1
:::

## [Indexation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508979 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The amount of the
reduction determined in accordance with section 17 is increased on
January 1 of each year after the year in which the reduction is first in
effect, by the amount that would be payable as a supplementary benefit
under the [Supplementary Retirement Benefits Act](/eng/acts/S-24) if the
amount of the reduction determined under section 17 were a pension that
became payable under Part II of the Act on January 1 of the year in
which the election was made.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-348, s. 1
:::

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of section 17, the following are the only demographic
  assumptions on which the actuarial present values are to be based:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the rate of mortality for any officer is that set
    out in the actuarial valuation report laid before Parliament in
    accordance with section 30 of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11), taking into account the
    mortality projection factors set out in the report;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the rates of mortality of surviving spouses are
    those set out for spouses in the actuarial valuation report laid
    before Parliament in accordance with section 45 of the [Public
    Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), taking into account the
    mortality projection factors set out in the report; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the rates of divorce shall be those established by
    the Superintendent of Financial Institutions in accordance with the
    most recent statistics on divorce published by Statistics Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the actuarial
  valuation reports referred to in that subsection are the most recent
  actuarial valuation reports, or, where the most recent actuarial
  valuation reports were tabled less than two months before the day on
  which the officer made an election, the previously tabled reports.

## [Revision of an Election]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508997 .Part}

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  officer who has made an election may revise the level of reduction
  where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the officer has received, from an authorized
    advisor who normally gives information about these matters,
    materially erroneous or misleading information regarding the amount
    of the reduction of the officer's pension or the amount of the
    pension to which the spouse would be entitled and, in making the
    election, acted on that information; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the pension that is payable to the
    officer is adjusted in accordance with the [Pension Benefits
    Division Act](/eng/acts/P-6.7) after the day on which the election
    was made.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The revision of the level of reduction shall be in
  writing.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The revision is valid only if it is sent to the
  Minister, or to a person designated by the Minister,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within three months after the day on which a
    written notice containing the corrected information is sent to the
    officer; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within three months after the day on which the
    pension is adjusted in accordance with the [Pension Benefits
    Division Act](/eng/acts/P-6.7).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A revision of the level of reduction is effective on
  the day on which it is sent.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The day of the sending of the revision is the day on
  which it is delivered or, if it is sent by mail, the day on which it
  is mailed and the postmark is evidence of that day.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-348, s. 1
- SOR/2013-125, s. 10

## [Effective Dates of Reduction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-509010 .Part}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  amount of the reduction of an officer's pension, calculated in
  accordance with section 17, shall be subtracted from the monthly
  instalment of the pension payable to the officer, effective on the
  first day of the second month after the month in which the election is
  made.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an officer has revised the level of reduction
  pursuant to subsection 21(1), the revised amount of the reduction
  shall be subtracted from the monthly instalments of the pension
  payable to the officer, effective on the first day of the month after
  the month in which the revision was made.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-348, s. 1
:::

## [Cessation of Effect of Election]{.HTitleText1} {#h-509016 .Part}

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** An election ceases to
have effect on the earliest of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the spouse dies,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the annulment of the marriage of the
  officer and the spouse takes effect, and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the divorce between the officer and
  the spouse takes effect.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-348, s. 1
:::

## [Revocation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-509023 .Part}

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 20.1(5) of the Act, an election made by an officer is deemed
to be revoked on the day on which the officer is required to contribute
to the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Account pursuant to
subsection 5(1) of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation
Act](/eng/acts/R-11).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-348, s. 1
:::

## [Termination of Reduction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-509027 .Part}

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Where an election ceases
to have effect pursuant to section 23 or is deemed to be revoked
pursuant to section 24, the reduction ceases on the first day of the
month in which the election is revoked or ceases to have effect.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-348, s. 1
:::

## [Surviving Spouse Allowance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-509031 .Part}

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The pension to which a
spouse is entitled on the death of the officer who made an election is
equal to an amount determined in accordance with subparagraph 17(b)(ii),
if paragraph 17(b) were read without reference to supplementary benefits
payable under the [Supplementary Retirement Benefits
Act](/eng/acts/S-24).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-348, s. 1
:::

## AMENDMENTS NOT IN FORCE {#amendments-not-in-force .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2024, c. 25, s. 112

  - ###### Terminology change --- English version {#terminology-change-english-version .MarginalNote}

    [112]{.lawlabel} Unless the context requires otherwise, every
    reference to "Force" is replaced by a reference to "RCMP" in the
    English version of every Act of Parliament and the English version
    of every order, regulation or other instrument made under an Act of
    Parliament.
