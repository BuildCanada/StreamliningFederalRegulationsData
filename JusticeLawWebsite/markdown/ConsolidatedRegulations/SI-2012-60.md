## Order Transferring to the Department of Health and to the Public Health Agency of Canada the Control and Supervision of Certain Portions of the Federal Public Administration {#order-transferring-to-the-department-of-health-and-to-the-public-health-agency-of-canada-the-control-and-supervision-of-certain-portions-of-the-federal-public-administration .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2012-60
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2012-08-01

Order Transferring to the Department of Health and to the Public Health
Agency of Canada the Control and Supervision of Certain Portions of the
Federal Public Administration

P.C. 2012-965 2012-06-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12091){#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12091-ID0EEEAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12092){#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12092-ID0EBEAA},
transfers, effective June 30, 2012,

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12091-ID0EEEAA){#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12091}S.C.
  2003, c. 22, s. 207
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12092-ID0EBEAA){#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12092}R.S., c.
  P-34
  :::

\(a\) to the Department of Health the control and supervision of the
portions of the federal public administration in the Public Health
Agency of Canada known as

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Communications Directorate,

- [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Human Resources Directorate,

- [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Information Management and Information
  Technology Directorate,

- [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the Facilities and Safety Division,

- [(v)]{.lawlabel} the Integrated Security Services Division,

- [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the Access to Information and Privacy Office,

- [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the Financial Policy, Systems and Operations
  Division, and

- [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the Assets and Material Management Division; and

\(b\) to the Public Health Agency of Canada the control and supervision
of the portions of the federal public administration in the Department
of Health known as the Audit and Accountability Bureau and the
Departmental Performance Measurement and Evaluation Directorate.
