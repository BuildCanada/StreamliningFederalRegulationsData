## Order Transferring from the Minister of Transport to the Minister of National Revenue the Control and Supervision of the Royal Canadian Mint and the Powers, Duties and Functions under the Royal Canadian Mint Act {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-transport-to-the-minister-of-national-revenue-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-royal-canadian-mint-and-the-powers-duties-and-functions-under-the-royal-canadian-mint-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2003-240
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2003-12-31

Order Transferring from the Minister of Transport to the Minister of
National Revenue the Control and Supervision of the Royal Canadian Mint
and the Powers, Duties and Functions under the Royal Canadian Mint Act

P.C. 2003-2098  2003-12-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers from the Minister of Transport to the Minister of National
Revenue

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the control and supervision of the Royal Canadian
  Mint, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the powers, duties and functions under the [Royal
  Canadian Mint Act](/eng/acts/R-9),

effective December 12, 2003.
