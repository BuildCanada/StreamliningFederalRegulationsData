## Royal Canadian Mounted Police Public Complaints Commission Rules of Practice {#royal-canadian-mounted-police-public-complaints-commission-rules-of-practice .Title-of-Act}

Rules of Practice and Procedure of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police
Public Complaints Commission

to paragraph 45.33(b)[[Footnote

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950554 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Rules may be cited as
the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Public Complaints Commission Rules of
Practice](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-17).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950568 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Rules apply in
respect of any proceedings with respect to a hearing conducted by the
Commission in accordance with section 45.45 of the Act.

## [Dispensation of Rules and Substitution of Periods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950570 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may dispense with any of these Rules where the
  circumstances and considerations of fairness require such dispensation
  so that the hearing may be conducted informally and expeditiously.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission, on its own initiative or on a motion
  that requests an extension or abridgement of a period in which an act
  is to be done, may extend or abridge the period where the
  circumstances and considerations of fairness require the Commission to
  do so.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Commission dispenses with the Rules in
  accordance with subsection (1) or extends or abridges a period in
  accordance with subsection (2), the Commission shall inform the
  parties and interested persons forthwith.

## [Filing of Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950575 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  record is to be filed with the Commission in accordance with these
  Rules or a direction of the Commission, the record may be filed with
  the Commission if the Commission is in session or with the Registrar
  by hand delivery, registered mail, courier, telex, facsimile or other
  means of communication where the necessary facilities for filing in
  such manner exist.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the day on which a record
  is filed is the day on which the record is received by the Commission
  or any person authorized by the Commission to receive records.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A record that is received by the Commission after
  five o'clock in the afternoon of a business day shall be deemed to be
  filed with the Commission on the next business day, unless the record
  is filed while the Commission is in session.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any confidential record filed with the Commission
  shall be marked "Confidential/*[Confidentiel]{.otherLang lang="fr"}*"
  and kept separate from the public record.

## [Service of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950581 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document, other than a document referred to in section 47.2 of the
  Act, may be served

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by hand delivery, mail, courier, telex, facsimile
    or other means of communication if there are facilities for
    accepting service in such manner; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where service cannot be made in accordance with
    paragraph (a), by a notice that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} sets out the contents of the document to be
      served and states that the original document is filed with the
      Commission, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is published twice within a period of seven days
      in a daily newspaper circulated in the place of the latest known
      residence of the person being served.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document may be served

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the Commission by leaving a copy of the document
    with a person who is authorized to accept service on behalf of the
    Commission;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on a person who is not a child by leaving a copy of
    the document with the person, with the duly authorized counsel
    acting for the person or with another person who appears to be at
    least 16 years of age and who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} resides or is employed at the residence of the
      person being served, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is employed at the principal office of the
      person being served as set out in any document filed during the
      proceedings; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on a person who is a child, by leaving a copy of
    the document with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the child, if the child is at least 14 years of
      age, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the father, the mother or the guardian of the
      child.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Service of any document is effected when the document
  is received by the Commission or a person referred to in subsection
  (2), or on the day following the day on which the last notice was
  published in a daily newspaper, in the case of service referred to in
  paragraph (1)(b).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a document is served on a person who may reply
  to the document within a certain period, the period commences at the
  time at which service of the document is effected in accordance with
  subsection (3).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Service of a notice of a hearing referred to in
  subsection 45.45(2) of the Act shall be effected at least 10 days
  before the day fixed for the hearing.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Any document to be served on a person shall set out
  the name or official title of the person.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Proof of service of a document served in accordance
  with this section shall be made by the person who served the document
  by filing, forthwith, a declaration under oath or a solemn affirmation
  with the Commission.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-447, s. 1
:::

## [Requests for Records and Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950603 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A party
  or interested person may, in writing, request that any other party or
  interested person produce, forthwith, any of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any pertinent record that is in the person's
    possession or control; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a full and adequate reply to the request, where the
    request is for information.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request referred to in subsection (1) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be addressed to a party or interested person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be delivered or, where the Commission requires that
    the request be served, served within the period established by the
    Commission; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be filed in accordance with subsection 5(1) and
    delivered to the other parties or interested persons.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a request for information, each item
  of information that is requested shall be numbered consecutively.

<!-- -->

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a person to whom a request that is referred to in
  section 7 is delivered or served shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deliver a copy of the record or reply to the other
    parties or interested persons and file the record or reply in
    accordance with subsection 5(1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a reply to a request for
    information, provide a full and adequate reply on a separate page
    for each item of the request.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any period that the Commission may
  establish, a person who does not comply with subsection (1) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out the grounds on which the refusal to comply
    is based;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} produce a statement that describes any other
    information or record that would be of assistance to the party or
    interested person who made the request; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} deliver a copy of the grounds referred to in
    paragraph (a)  and, if applicable, the statement referred to in
    paragraph (b) to the parties and interested persons and file those
    copies in accordance with subsection 5(1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party or interested person who has made a request
  in accordance with section 7 and who has not received the record or a
  full and adequate reply may, by motion, request that the Commission
  order the production of the record or reply.

<!-- -->

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  person does not comply with section 8, the person may not put in
  evidence at a hearing the record that the person did not deliver and
  file and that is to the person's advantage unless the Commission is
  satisfied that there existed sufficient reasons for not complying with
  the request.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a party or interested person refuses to deliver
  and file a record, the person who made the request in accordance with
  section 7 may, where authorized by the Commission, introduce secondary
  evidence of the contents of the record.

## [Stay of Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950625 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Where a party or
interested person has not complied with these Rules or any direction
issued by the Commission, the Commission may stay the proceedings until
the Rules or direction have been complied with or take such other steps
as the Commission considers fair and reasonable so that the hearing may
be conducted informally and expeditiously.

## [Issues to Be Determined]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950627 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may, at any time that there is an issue of jurisdiction,
  practice or procedure that should be decided before the proceedings
  are continued, determine the issue.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} At any stage of the proceedings, the Commission may
  refer any question or issue of law, of jurisdiction or of practice and
  procedure to the Federal Court for a hearing and determination and the
  Commission may, pending the determination of the issue, order the
  whole or part of the proceedings to be stayed.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2002, c. 8, s. 183
:::

## [Conference]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950633 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may, at
any time, direct any party, interested person or the counsel of the
party or interested person either to appear before the Commission at a
specified time, date and place for a conference or to participate in a
teleconference at a specified time and date for the purpose of
considering the following issues:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the clarification and simplification of contentious
  issues;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the admission of particular facts or of information;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the official language in which the hearing will be
  conducted;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the procedure to be followed in connection with
  requests for records or information;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the procedure to be followed during the course of the
  hearing;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the exchange among parties or interested persons of
  records; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} any other matter that may aid in the clarification
  and simplification of the evidence and the preparation of the final
  report of the Commission.

<!-- -->

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  agreements reached in respect of the issues considered in accordance
  with section 12 shall be recorded

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a conference, in the minutes signed
    by the parties and interested persons attending the session at which
    the agreements are made and countersigned by the Commission member
    presiding at that session; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a teleconference, in minutes signed
    by the Commission member presiding at the session and the Commission
    shall deliver a copy of the minutes to the participating parties and
    interested persons.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an agreement on an issue referred to in section
  12 cannot be reached, the Commission may decide the issue so that the
  hearing may be conducted informally and expeditiously.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall deliver copies of any agreement
  referred to in subsection (1) and of any decision referred to in
  subsection (2) to the parties and the interested persons.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 4, an agreement referred to in
  subsection (1), together with any decision of the Commission pursuant
  to subsection (2), shall govern the hearing.

## [Summons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950649 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party or interested person, on request, may obtain a summons in Form 1
  set out in the schedule issued by the Commission and sealed with the
  Commission's seal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A summons referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  completed by the party or interested person on behalf of whom it is
  issued or the counsel for that party or interested person and shall
  contain the information required by Form 1.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (2), any party or
  interested person requiring the attendance of a witness during the
  hearing may forward the name and address of the proposed witness to
  the Registrar at least seven days before the witness is required to
  appear at the hearing, so that the Commission may issue a summons to
  that witness.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A person that has been
summoned in accordance with section 14, may, before appearing as a
witness or at any other time, by motion request that the person receives
payment of the fees and allowances that are payable to witnesses
summoned before the Federal Court of Canada.

## [Interlocutory Motions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950655 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party or interested person may bring before the Commission, in writing
  or orally, any issue that arises during the proceedings.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A motion shall contain a clear and concise statement
  of the facts, the order sought and the grounds therefor.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A written motion shall be filed with the Registrar
  or, during the hearing, with the hearing process officer and shall be
  served on the parties and the interested persons to the proceedings.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A motion may be made orally during the hearing in
  accordance with the procedure established by the Commission.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A motion may be disposed of by the Commission in
  writing or orally.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-447, s. 2(E)
:::

## [Intervention]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950664 .Part}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who intends to satisfy the Commission that the person has a
  substantial and direct interest in a complaint shall file with the
  Commission

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a motion to intervene that is signed and dated by
    the person making the motion or by any other person on behalf of
    that person; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an affidavit as to the facts on which the motion is
    based.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A motion to intervene shall set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the style of cause of the proceedings in which the
    person filing the motion wishes to intervene;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the person filing the
    motion or the person signing the motion on behalf of that person;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the matters in the
    proceedings that affect the person filing the motion;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the facts on which the
    motion is based; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the official language that the person intends to
    use in the arguments in respect of the motion.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall serve, forthwith, a copy of a
  motion to intervene and the supporting affidavit on each of the
  parties and interested persons after the documents are filed with the
  Commission.

<!-- -->

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party or interested person who has been served with a copy of a motion
  to intervene and the supporting affidavit may, within fourteen days
  after the service or within such other period as the Commission may
  direct, file with the Commission a response to the motion dealing with
  the matters raised in the motion and stating whether the party or
  interested person intends to oppose the motion.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the response referred to in subsection (1)
  shall be served by the person who filed the response on the person
  making the motion and on all other parties and interested persons.

<!-- -->

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  motion to intervene may be heard at any time, date and place fixed by
  the Commission, including at the time fixed for a conference or
  teleconference that is held in accordance with section 12.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall serve on the person who filed the
  motion, the parties and interested persons a notice of the time, date
  and place at which the motion shall be heard.

<!-- -->

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Commission grants or denies a motion to intervene, the Registrar
  shall serve on the parties, the interested persons and the person who
  filed the motion notice thereof.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a motion to intervene is granted,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Registrar shall serve on the interested person
    who filed the motion copies of the documents filed by the parties
    and the other interested persons prior to the granting of the
    motion; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} each party and interested person shall serve on the
    interested person who has filed the motion, copies of all documents
    served on the day the motion is granted and thereafter.

## [Hearing in Private]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950687 .Part}

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Commission directs that a hearing or a portion thereof is to be
  held in private in accordance with subsection 45.45(11) of the Act,
  the hearing may be attended only by the following persons who complete
  and file Form 2 set out in the schedule:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the parties, interested persons, counsel and
    authorized representatives of the parties and interested persons;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other person that the Commission considers
    should be present.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), the required
  Commission staff, including stenographers and interpreters, may attend
  a hearing or portion thereof that is held in private.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Records with respect to the hearing or portion
  thereof that is held in private that are filed with the Commission
  shall be marked "Confidential/*[Confidentiel]{.otherLang lang="fr"}*"
  and kept separate from the public record.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may authorize persons who have filed a
  Form 2 to make copies of the records referred to in subsection (3) and
  of the transcripts of the hearing or portion thereof that is held in
  private.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where an expert witness is to testify at a hearing in
  private, the Commission may authorize, subject to any condition the
  Commission imposes, confidential information to be made available to
  that witness.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A person referred to in subsection (1) who is present
  at a hearing in private shall submit the person's transcripts, notes,
  and any other records to the Commission at the conclusion of the
  arguments.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950697 .Part}

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** During normal business
hours, the Commission shall make available to the parties and interested
persons records filed in the course of the proceedings, other than a
hearing in private, and shall permit the persons to make copies thereof.

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may, in
the interests of justice, adjourn any hearing for such time, date and to
such place and on such terms as the Commission considers advisable.

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** No portion of the
proceedings is invalid by reason of an irregularity in form or
procedure.

## [FORM 2]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 21)]{.OriginatingRef}[Style of Cause]{.scheduleTitleText}  [Declaration and Undertaking]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#form-2section-21style-of-cause-declaration-and-undertaking .scheduleLabel}

I hereby undertake:

- [1]{.lawlabel} THAT I will maintain the confidentiality of any
  information or evidence that I receive during the course of the
  hearing in private and will not disclose any information or evidence
  that I receive during the course of that hearing;

- [2]{.lawlabel} THAT I will not reproduce in any manner, without the
  prior written approval of the Commission, any records dealing with the
  evidence taken and submissions made in the hearing in private;

- [3]{.lawlabel} THAT at the end of the hearing, I will return to the
  Commission all records provided to me by the Commission during the
  hearing in private, and any notes taken by me with respect to evidence
  that I received during the course of that hearing in private.
