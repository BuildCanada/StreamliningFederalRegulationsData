## [Public Officer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719376 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the
definition [[public officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 30(1) of
the Act, a prescribed individual is a person employed in a federal
institution or whose services are required by a federal institution, on
a casual or temporary basis or under a student employment program.

## [Availability of Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719378 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 35(1) of the Act, information that is obtained, or prepared
from information that is obtained, under any program other than the
[Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or the [Old Age Security
Act](/eng/acts/O-9) may be made available to the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Canada Revenue Agency, for the administration or
  enforcement of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Statistics Canada, for the administration or
  enforcement of the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Department of Citizenship and Immigration, for
  the administration or enforcement of the [Immigration and Refugee
  Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Public Service Commission, for the administration
  or enforcement of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Security Intelligence Service, for the
  administration or enforcement of the [Canadian Security Intelligence
  Service Act](/eng/acts/C-23);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the Department of Justice, for the administration of
  activities conducted with respect to legal proceedings and activities
  related to mutual legal assistance under an agreement;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Library and Archives of Canada, for the
  administration or enforcement of the [Library and Archives of Canada
  Act](/eng/acts/L-7.7);

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, for the
  administration of the following activities, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} enforcing the laws of Canada or a province,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} carrying out a lawful investigation, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} exercising mutual legal assistance under an
    agreement;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Canada Border Services Agency, for the
  administration or enforcement of the [Immigration and Refugee
  Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5); and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Transportation Accident Investigation
  and Safety Board, for the administration or enforcement of the
  [Canadian Transportation Accident Investigation and Safety Board
  Act](/eng/acts/C-23.4).

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 35(1) of the Act, information that is obtained, or prepared
from information that is obtained, under the [Canada Pension
Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) may be made available to the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Canada Revenue Agency, for the administration or
  enforcement of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Correctional Service of Canada, for the purpose
  of the administration or enforcement of the [Corrections and
  Conditional Release Act](/eng/acts/C-44.6);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Department of Justice or the Royal Canadian
  Mounted Police, for the purpose of any investigations or prosecutions
  in relation to war crimes or crimes against humanity or any activities
  relating to extradition from Canada for such crimes;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Department of Veterans Affairs, for the purpose
  of the administration or enforcement of any of the following Acts:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} [Veterans Well-being Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} [Children of Deceased Veterans Education
    Assistance Act](/eng/acts/C-28),

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} [Civilian War-related Benefits
    Act](/eng/acts/C-31),

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} [Department of Veterans Affairs
    Act](/eng/acts/V-1),

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6), and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} [War Veterans Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Library and Archives of Canada, for the
  administration or enforcement of the [Library and Archives of Canada
  Act](/eng/acts/L-7.7);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} Statistics Canada, for the administration or
  enforcement of the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19); and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Department of Public Works and Government
  Services, for the administration or enforcement of any of the
  following Acts:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} [Canadian Forces Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-17), and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/R-11).
