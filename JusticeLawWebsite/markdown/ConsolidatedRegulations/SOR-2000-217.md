## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660199 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Controlled Drugs and
    Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[adult]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[adult]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual of 18 years of age
    or older. ([adulte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[advertisement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[advertisement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a targeted
    substance, includes any representation by any means for the purpose
    of promoting, directly or indirectly, the sale or other disposal of
    the targeted substance. ([publicité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in relation to an
    individual, means a person who is cohabiting with the individual in
    a conjugal relationship, having so cohabited for a period of at
    least one year. ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[competent authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[competent authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a public authority
    of a foreign country that is authorized under the laws of the
    country to approve the importation or exportation of targeted
    substances into or from the country. ([autorité
    compétente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2019-170, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[designated criminal offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated criminal offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an offence involving the financing of terrorism
      against any of sections 83.02 to 83.04 of the [Criminal
      Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an offence involving fraud against any of
      sections 380 to 382 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the offence of laundering proceeds of crime
      against section 462.31 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an offence involving a criminal organization
      against any of sections 467.11 to 467.13 of the [Criminal
      Code](/eng/acts/C-46); or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a conspiracy or an attempt to commit, being an
      accessory after the fact in relation to, or any counselling in
      relation to, an offence referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to
      (d). ([infraction désignée en matière criminelle]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[designated drug offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated drug offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2019-170, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[destroy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[destroy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a targeted substance,

    is rendered impossible or
    improbable. ([destruction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[emergency medical service vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[emergency medical service vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is owned or operated by the government of Canada

      services. ([hôpital]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[import permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[import permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2019-170, s.

[[licensed dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    autorisé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[midwife]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[nurse practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nurse practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as

    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-230). ([infirmier
    praticien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2019-170, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[pharmacist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pharmacist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is entitled

    lang="fr"})

[[podiatrist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[podiatrist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in

[[prescription]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[qualified person in charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[senior person in charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[senior person in charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the individual

[[specified name]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[specified name]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name specified in column 1 of an item of

      chemical name specified in column 2 of that item; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the controlled substance is not specified in
      Schedule 2, its chemical name and, if available, its common
      name. ([nom spécifié]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[targeted substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[targeted substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a controlled substance set out in Schedule 1; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a midwife, nurse practitioner or
      podiatrist, a controlled substance set out in Schedule 1 that the
      midwife, nurse practitioner or podiatrist may prescribe, possess

      [New Classes of Practitioners
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-230). ([substance
      ciblée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[test kit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[test kit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a kit

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that contains a targeted substance and a reagent
      system or buffering agent;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is used during the course of a chemical or
      analytical procedure to test for the presence or quantity of a
      targeted substance for a medical, laboratory, industrial,
      educational, law administration or enforcement, or research
      purpose; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the contents of which are not intended or likely
      to be consumed by, or administered to, a person or an
      animal. ([nécessaire d'essai]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transhipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transhipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2019-170, s.

## [Non-application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660248 .Part}

or a person acting under their direction and control who, in respect of
the conduct of the member or person, is exempt from the application of
subsection 4(2) or section 5, 6 or 7 of the Act by virtue of the
[Controlled Drugs and Substances Act (Police Enforcement)

## [Possession]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660263 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized persons

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  is authorized to possess a targeted substance set out in Part 2 of
  Schedule 1 if the person has obtained the targeted substance in
  accordance with these Regulations, in the course of activities
  conducted in connection with the administration or enforcement of an
  Act or regulation, or from a person who is exempt under section 56 of
  the Act from the application of subsection 5(1) of the Act with
  respect to that targeted substance, and the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} requires the targeted substance for their business
    or profession and is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a licensed dealer,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a pharmacist, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a practitioner who is registered and entitled
      to practise in the province in which they possess the targeted
      substance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is a practitioner who is registered and entitled to
    practise in a province other than the province in which they possess
    the targeted substance for emergency medical purposes only;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is a hospital employee or a practitioner in a
    hospital;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} has obtained the targeted substance for their own
    use or the use of another person for whom, or an animal for which,
    they are responsible

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} from a practitioner, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with a prescription that was not
      issued or obtained in contravention of these Regulations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} is employed as an inspector, a member of the Royal
    Canadian Mounted Police, a police constable, a peace officer or a
    member of the technical or scientific staff of the Government of
    Canada, the government of a province or a university in Canada and
    their possession is in connection with that employment;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} is exempt under section 56 of the Act with respect
    to the possession of that targeted substance and their possession is
    for a purpose set out in the exemption;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} has imported the targeted substance in accordance
    with section 68 for their own use or the use of another person for
    whom, or an animal for which, they are responsible;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} is a person who, in accordance with a permit for
    transit or transhipment, is responsible for the targeted substance
    while it is in transit or in transhipment in Canada; or

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agent or mandatary

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person is authorized to possess a targeted
  substance set out in Part 2 of Schedule 1 if the person is acting as
  the agent or mandatary of any person who is authorized to possess it
  in accordance with any of paragraphs (1)(a) to (d) and (f) to (h).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agent or mandatary --- person referred
  to in paragraph (1)(e)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person is authorized to possess a targeted
  substance set out in Part 2 of Schedule 1 if they

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are acting as the agent or mandatary of a person
    who they have reasonable grounds to believe is a person referred to
    in paragraph (1)(e); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} possess the targeted substance for the purpose of
    assisting that person in the administration or enforcement of an Act
    or regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Export by individual

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An individual may possess a targeted substance set
  out in Part 2 of Schedule 1 for the purpose of exporting that
  substance in accordance with section 69.

## [Test Kits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660271 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized activities

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A person may sell, possess
or otherwise deal in a test kit if the following conditions are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a registration number has been issued for the test
  kit under section 6 or subsection 10(2) and has not been cancelled
  under subsection 9(1);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the test kit is labelled with

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the registration number, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a test kit that is not subject to
    the labelling requirements of the [Medical Devices
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282),

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the manufacturer or
      assembler or, if the test kit is manufactured or assembled in
      accordance with a custom order, the name and address of the person
      for whom the test kit was manufactured or assembled, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} its brand name; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the test kit will be used for a medical, laboratory,
  industrial, educational, law administration or enforcement, or
  research purpose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for registration number

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  manufacturer or assembler of a test kit or, if the test kit is
  manufactured or assembled in accordance with a custom order, the
  person for whom the test kit was manufactured or assembled, may obtain
  a registration number for the test kit by submitting to the Minister
  an application containing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the brand name of the test kit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the design and
    construction of the test kit;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the targeted substance
    and other substances, if any, contained in the test kit, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the specified name of the targeted substance and
      the name of any other substance,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the substance is a salt, the name of the
      salt, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the substance;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed use of the test kit;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the directions for use of the test kit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signature and attestation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be signed and dated by the person authorized by the
    applicant for that purpose; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} include an attestation by that person that all of
    the information submitted in support of the application is correct
    and complete to the best of their knowledge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information and documents

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The applicant must, not later than the date specified
  in the Minister's written request to that effect, provide the Minister
  with any information or document that the Minister determines is
  necessary to complete the review of the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal of registration number

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must refuse to issue a registration number for a test kit if
  the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the test kit poses a risk to public health or
    safety, including the risk of the targeted substance in the test kit
    being diverted to an illicit market or use, because

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the total amount of the targeted substance in the
      test kit is too high, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the adulterating or denaturing agent in the test
      kit is not likely to prevent or deter the consumption of the
      targeted substance in the test kit by a person or animal or the
      administration of that substance to a person or animal; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the test kit is likely to be used for a purpose
    other than one referred to in section 6.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before refusing to issue a registration number, the
  Minister must send the applicant a notice that sets out the Minister's
  reasons and gives the applicant an opportunity to be heard.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of registration number

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must cancel the registration number of a test kit if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister receives a notice from the holder of
    the registration number stating that they have ceased to conduct all
    activities with respect to the test kit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the test kit is a medical device and is no longer
    approved for sale in Canada under the [Medical Devices
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one of the circumstances referred to in
    subparagraph 7(1)(a)(i) or (ii) exists; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the test kit is being used for a purpose other than a medical,
    laboratory, industrial, educational, law administration or
    enforcement, or research purpose and that the test kit poses a risk
    to public health or safety, including the risk of the targeted
    substance in the test kit being diverted to an illicit market or
    use.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of cancellation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When the registration number of a test kit is
  cancelled, the number

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} must not be displayed on the label of any test kit
    manufactured or assembled after the cancellation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a cancellation under paragraph
    (1)(a), must remain on the label of any existing test kit until all
    of the test kits with that registration number are disposed of.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for new number

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following persons may, after a period of 90 days following the
  cancellation of the registration number of a test kit, obtain a new
  registration number by submitting to the Minister an application that
  is supported by proof that the circumstances that gave rise to the
  cancellation have been corrected:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer or assembler of the test kit; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the test kit is manufactured or assembled in
    accordance with a custom order, the person for whom the test kit was

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualified person in charge

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Only an individual who meets the following

    licence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they

      entitled by a provincial professional licensing authority or a
      professional association in Canada and entitled to practise a
      profession that is relevant to their duties, such as pharmacist,
      practitioner, pharmacy technician or laboratory technician,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} hold a diploma, certificate or credential
      awarded by a post-secondary educational institution in Canada in a
      field or occupation that is relevant to their duties, such as
      pharmacy, medicine, dentistry, veterinary medicine, pharmacology,
      chemistry, biology, pharmacy technician, laboratory technician,
      pharmaceutical regulatory affairs or supply chain management or
      security, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} hold a diploma, certificate or credential that
      is awarded by a foreign educational institution in a field or
      occupation referred to in subparagraph (ii) and hold

        73(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection

        organization or institution that is responsible for issuing
        equivalency assessments and is recognized by a province;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} they have sufficient knowledge of and experience
    with the use and handling of the targeted substances specified in
    the dealer's licence to properly carry out their duties; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} they have sufficient knowledge of the provisions of
    the Act and these Regulations that are applicable to the activities
    specified in the dealer's licence to properly carry out their
    duties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An applicant for a dealer's licence may designate an

    requirements;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} those requirements are not necessary for the
    activities specified in the licence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the individual has sufficient knowledge ---
    acquired from a combination of education, training or work
    experience --- to properly carry out their duties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  Minister must revoke an export permit if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensed dealer requests the Minister to do so
    or informs the Minister of the loss or theft of the permit or the
    actual or potential unauthorized use of the permit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licensed dealer does not carry out the
    corrective measures specified by the Minister under paragraph
    29(2)(c) by the specified date;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the licensed dealer has contravened a term or
    condition of the permit;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the licensed dealer submitted false or misleading information or
    false or falsified documents in or in support of the application for
    the permit;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} information received from a competent authority or
    the United Nations gives the Minister reasonable grounds to believe
    that the licensed dealer has been involved in the diversion of a
    targeted substance to an illicit market or use; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the dealer's licence has been revoked.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must not revoke an export permit for a
  ground set out in paragraph (1)(d) or 24(1)(e) or (g) if the licensed
  dealer meets the following conditions, unless the Minister has
  reasonable grounds to believe that it is necessary to do so to protect
  public health or safety, including to prevent a targeted substance
  from being diverted to an illicit market or use:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensed dealer does not have a history of
    non-compliance with the Act, the [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) or
    their regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licensed dealer has carried out, or signed an
    undertaking to carry out, the necessary corrective measures to
    ensure compliance with the Act, the [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5)
    and their regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before revoking an export permit, the Minister must
  send the licensed dealer a notice that sets out the Minister's reasons
  and gives the dealer an opportunity to be heard.

### [Destruction of Targeted Substances]{.HTitleText2} {#h-660694 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Destruction at site

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** A licensed dealer that
intends to destroy a targeted substance at the site specified in their
licence must ensure that the following conditions are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensed dealer obtains the prior approval of the
  Minister;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the destruction is carried out in the presence of two
  of the following persons, at least one of whom must be a person
  referred to in subparagraph (i):

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the senior person in charge, the qualified person
    in charge or an alternate qualified person in charge, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a person who works for or provides services to the
    licensed dealer and holds a senior position;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the destruction is carried out in accordance with a
  method that complies with all federal, provincial and municipal
  environmental protection legislation applicable to the place of
  destruction; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} as soon as the destruction is completed, the person
  who carried out the destruction and each of the two persons referred
  to in paragraph (b) who were present at the destruction sign and date
  a joint declaration attesting that the targeted substance was
  completely destroyed, to which each signatory must add their name in
  printed letters.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refills

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** A pharmacist may only
refill a prescription for a targeted substance if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the practitioner who prescribed it expressly directs
  that the prescription may be refilled and specifies the number of
  refills;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the pharmacist makes a record of each refill in
  accordance with section 53;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} less than one year has elapsed since the day on which
  the prescription was issued by the practitioner;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} at least one refill remains on the prescription; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case where an interval between refills has
  been specified by the practitioner, it has expired.

  the original filling and at each refill;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number assigned to the prescription.

### [General Obligations of Pharmacists]{.HTitleText2} {#h-660977 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of location

**[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** A pharmacist who closes

than 10 days after the closure, inform the Minister of the date of
closure, the location to which the targeted substance was moved and the
quantity of the targeted substance that was moved.

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  pharmacist who does not wish to receive or have possession of targeted
  substances may make a written request to the Minister to send to
  licensed dealers and pharmacies a notice, issued under section 79,
  that states that targeted substances must not be supplied to that
  pharmacist.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited actions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A pharmacist must not, unless their pharmacy has
  received a retraction issued under section 80,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sell or provide a targeted substance to a
    pharmacist or practitioner named in a notice issued under section
    79; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fill a prescription or order for a targeted
    substance from a practitioner named in a notice issued under section
    79.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Importation --- Canadian residents

- **[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual who is a resident of Canada may, at the time of the
  individual's return to Canada from a foreign country, import a
  targeted substance that is in their possession at the time of entry if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the substance has been obtained under a
    prescription and is labelled with the information set out in
    paragraphs 51(2)(a) to (g);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the individual is importing the substance

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for their own use,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for the use of a person for whom the individual
      is responsible and who is travelling with them, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for the use of an animal for which the

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the quantity being imported does not exceed the
    lesser of

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a 90-day supply, based on the usual daily dose.

    to (d) are met; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity imported is the least of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the content of the container,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a 90-day supply, based on the usual daily dose,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the usual daily dose multiplied by the number
      of days that the individual will stay in Canada.

- **[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the

  advising them that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pharmacists practising in the notified pharmacies

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pharmacists practising in the notified pharmacies
    must not fill an order or prescription for any targeted substance

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the prohibitions in both paragraphs (a) and (b)

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Circumstances requiring a notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must be sent if the pharmacist or
  practitioner named in the notice has

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} made a request to the Minister in accordance with
    subsection 57(1) or section 62 to send the notice;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contravened a rule of conduct established by the
    provincial professional licensing authority in the province in which
    the pharmacist or practitioner is practising and the authority has
    requested the Minister in writing to send the notice; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} been convicted of a designated substance offence or
    of a contravention of these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recipients

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice must be sent to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all licensed dealers;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all pharmacies within the province in which the
    pharmacist or practitioner named in the notice is registered and
    entitled to practise and is practising;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the provincial professional licensing authority of
    the province in which the pharmacist or practitioner named in the
    notice is registered and entitled to practise;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all pharmacies in an adjacent province in which a
    prescription or order from the pharmacist or practitioner named in
    the notice may be filled; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any provincial professional licensing authority in
    another province that has requested the Minister in writing to send
    the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other circumstances

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may send the notice described in
  subsection (1) to the persons and authorities specified in
  subsection (3) if the Minister has taken the measures specified in
  subsection (5) and has reasonable grounds to believe that the
  pharmacist or practitioner named in the notice

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has contravened a provision of the Act or these
    Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has, on more than one occasion, self-administered a
    targeted substance under a self-directed prescription or order or,
    in the absence of a prescription or order, contrary to accepted
    professional practice;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has, on more than one occasion, prescribed,
    administered or provided a targeted substance to a person who is a
    spouse, common-law partner, parent or child of the pharmacist or
    practitioner, including a child adopted in fact, contrary to
    accepted professional practice; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is unable to account for a quantity of targeted
    substance for which the pharmacist or practitioner was responsible
    under these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures before sending notice

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The measures that must be taken before sending the

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} consulted with the provincial professional

    practitioner to whom the notice relates is registered and entitled
    to practise;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} given that pharmacist or practitioner an
    opportunity to be heard; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} considered

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the compliance history of the pharmacist or
      practitioner in respect of the Act and its regulations, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} whether the actions of the pharmacist or
      practitioner pose a risk to public health or safety, including the
      risk of the targeted substance being diverted to an illicit market
      or use.

## [Notification of Application for Order of Restoration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-661292 .Part}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the evidence on which the applicant intends to rely

## [Permit for Transit or Transhipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1212440 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[83]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-83 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  targeted substance is to be shipped from a country of export to a
  foreign country by a route that requires it to be in transit through
  Canada or to be transhipped in Canada, the exporter in the country of
  export or their agent or mandatary in Canada must obtain a permit for
  the transit or transhipment of the targeted substance by submitting to
  the Minister an application that contains the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, municipal address and telephone number of
    the exporter in the country of export;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, municipal address and telephone number of
    the person who will be responsible for the targeted substance while
    it is in Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the targeted substance,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its specified name and, if applicable, its brand
      name,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if it is a salt, the name of the salt,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} its quantity, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a raw material, its purity and
      its anhydrous content;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the expected date of transit or transhipment in
    Canada;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name of the customs office where the
    importation or exportation is anticipated;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} each proposed mode of transportation in Canada; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a transhipment, the municipal
    address of every place in Canada at which the targeted substance
    will be stored during the transhipment and the expected duration of
    the storage at each place.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the export permit issued by the competent
    authority in the country of export; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the import permit issued by the competent
    authority in the country of final destination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signature and attestation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The application must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be signed and dated by a person who is authorized
    for that purpose by the exporter, including an agent or mandatary in
    Canada of that exporter; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} include an attestation by that person that all of
    the information submitted in support of the application is correct
    and complete to the best of their knowledge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information and documents

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The applicant must, not later than the date specified
  in the Minister's written request to that effect, provide the Minister
  with any information or document that the Minister determines is
  necessary to complete the review of the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance

- **[[84]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-84
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), on
  completion of the review of the application for a permit for transit
  or transhipment, the Minister must issue to the applicant a permit for
  transit or transhipment that contains

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, municipal address and telephone number of
    the exporter in the country of export;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, municipal address and telephone number of
    the person who is responsible for the targeted substance while it is
    in Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the specified name of the targeted substance, its
    quantity, its purity and, if applicable, its strength per unit, the
    number of units per package and the number of packages;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the countries of export and final destination and
    the numbers and expiry dates of the export and import permits issued
    by the competent authority in each of those countries;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the expected date of the transit or transhipment in
    Canada;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the name of the customs office where the
    importation or exportation is anticipated;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} each proposed mode of transportation in Canada;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a transhipment, the municipal
    address of every place in Canada at which the targeted substance
    will be stored during the transhipment and the expected duration of
    the storage at each place; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the dates of issuance and expiry of the permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must refuse to issue a permit for
  transit or transhipment if the Minister has reasonable grounds to
  believe that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the issuance of the permit

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} would contravene an international obligation,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} would contravene the Act, the regulations or
      another Act of Parliament or a law of the country of export, of
      the country of final destination or of a country of transit or
      transhipment, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} would likely create a risk to public health or
      safety, including the risk of the targeted substance being
      diverted to an illicit market or use; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the import permit issued by the competent authority
    in the country of final destination has expired or has been
    suspended or revoked.
