## Canadian Forces Materiel Remission Order {#canadian-forces-materiel-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-617
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1994-09-29

Order Respecting the Remission of the Customs Duties Paid or Payable in
Respect of Certain Canadian Forces Materiel Imported by the Department
of National Defence

P.C. 1994-1614  1994-09-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to section 101 of the
[Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-94-617_e_hq_6305){#fn_SOR-94-617_e_hq_6305-ID0EDBAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of
the customs duties paid or payable in respect of certain Canadian Forces
materiel imported by the Department of National
Defence](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-617).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-965044 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Canadian Forces Materiel Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-617).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-965046 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[materiel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all property of Her Majesty in
right of Canada, as represented by the Minister of National Defence,
provided for the use of the Canadian Forces and includes any vessel,
vehicle, aircraft, animal, arms, ammunition, clothing, stores,
provisions or equipment necessary or used for the purposes of executing
Canada's role in the North Atlantic Treaty Organization or as a member
of the United Nations.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-965048 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted to the Department of National Defence of the
customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of materiel imported by the
Department of National Defence as a result of the closure of the
Canadian Forces Bases at Lahr and Baden-Soellingen, in the Federal
Republic of Germany, and the Canadian Forces Station Bermuda, in
Bermuda, and of the recall of the Canadian Contingent, United Nations
Force Cyprus, from the Republic of Cyprus.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-965050 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted
pursuant to section 3 on the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the Department of National Defence certifies, in
  a form approved by the Minister of National Revenue, that the materiel

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is the property of Her Majesty in right of Canada
    as represented by the Minister of National Defence, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is being imported as a result of the closure of
    the Canadian Forces Bases at Lahr and Baden-Soellingen, in the
    Federal Republic of Germany, and the Canadian Forces Station
    Bermuda, in Bermuda, and of the recall of the Canadian Contingent,
    United Nations Force Cyprus, from the Republic of Cyprus;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the materiel is imported and accounted for
  during the period beginning on April 1, 1992 and ending on December
  31, 1995; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} that a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue no later than December 31, 1997.
