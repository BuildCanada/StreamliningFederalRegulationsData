## Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development the Control and Supervision of the Aboriginal Affairs Secretariat {#order-transferring-from-the-privy-council-office-to-the-department-of-indian-affairs-and-northern-development-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-aboriginal-affairs-secretariat .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-51
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2006-03-22

Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Department of
Indian Affairs and Northern Development the Control and Supervision of
the Aboriginal Affairs Secretariat

P.C. 2006-119 2006-03-03
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA} of the [Public
Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34),
hereby transfers from the Privy Council Office to the Department of
Indian Affairs and Northern Development the control and supervision of
that portion of the federal public administration within the Plans and
Consultation Group in the Privy Council Office known as the Aboriginal
Affairs Secretariat, effective April 1, 2006.
