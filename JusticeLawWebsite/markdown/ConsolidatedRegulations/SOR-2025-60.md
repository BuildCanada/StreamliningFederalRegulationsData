## Order Designating Certain Excluded Classes of Projects {#order-designating-certain-excluded-classes-of-projects .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2025-60
:::

[IMPACT ASSESSMENT ACT](/eng/acts/I-2.75)

Registration 2025-02-28

Order Designating Certain Excluded Classes of Projects
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Minister of the Environment is of the opinion that the
carrying out of a project under one of the designated classes of
projects under the annexed Order will cause only insignificant adverse
environmental effects;

And whereas, under subsection 89(2) of the [Impact Assessment
Act](/eng/acts/I-2.75)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5248_hq_1700151884){#fn_81000-2-5248_hq_1700151884-ID0EBBCA},
that Minister has considered the comments received from the public in
deciding whether to make the designation;

  2019, c. 28, s. 1
  :::

Therefore, the Minister of the Environment makes the annexed [Order
Designating Certain Excluded Classes of
Projects](/eng/regulations/SOR-2025-60) under subsection 88(1) of the
[Impact Assessment Act](/eng/acts/I-2.75)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5248_hq_1700151884){#fn_81000-2-5248_hq_1700151884-ID0EBABA}.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1513465 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Impact Assessment
    Act](/eng/acts/I-2.75). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[allied petroleum product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[allied petroleum product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
    meaning as in section 1 of the [Storage Tank Systems for Petroleum
    Products and Allied Petroleum Products
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2008-197). ([produit
    apparenté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[building]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[building]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a roofed physical work and
    includes a moveable accommodation. ([bâtiment]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[developed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[developed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} with respect to land means that it
    is permanently altered from its natural state for human use or is
    landscaped and maintained for human use. ([aménagé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[expansion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[expansion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an increase in the exterior
    dimensions or the production capacity of a physical
    work. ([agrandissement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hook-up]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hook-up]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a structure or line that is
    used to connect a physical work to a main gas, oil, sewer, water,
    power or telecommunication line. ([raccordement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[modification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[modification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an alteration to a
    physical work that does not alter the purpose or function of the
    work but does not include an expansion or
    relocation. ([modification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[petroleum product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[petroleum product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 1 of the [Storage Tank Systems for Petroleum Products and
    Allied Petroleum Products
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2008-197). ([produit
    pétrolier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[water body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[water body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a lake, canal,
    reservoir, ocean, river and its tributaries, and wetland, up to the
    annual high-water mark, but does not include a ditch that does not
    contain [[fish habitat]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14), a sewage or
    waste treatment lagoon, a mine tailings pond, an artificial
    irrigation pond or a dugout. ([plan d'eau]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[wetland]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wetland]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an estuary, tidal flat, marsh,
    swamp, bog, fen or other land where the presence of water has caused
    the formation of hydric soils and favoured the dominance of
    hydrophytic or water-tolerant plants. ([terres
    humides]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any reference to a physical
work in this Order includes the systems and equipment required for the
operation of a physical work including communication, electrical,
heating, fire-prevention, plumbing or security systems and equipment but
not including systems and equipment that produce goods or energy
primarily for any purpose other than the operation of the physical work.

## [Designated Classes of Projects]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1513480 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Federal lands or lands outside of Canada

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), the classes of projects that are set out
  in Schedule 1 and carried out on federal lands or lands outside Canada
  are designated under section 88 of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lands administered by Parks Canada
  Agency

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The classes of projects that are set out in Schedule
  2 and carried out on federal lands administered by the Parks Canada
  Agency are designated under section 88 of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Wildlife Area
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1609)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The classes of projects that are set out in Schedule
  3 and carried out in areas referred to in Schedule I of the [Wildlife
  Area Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1609) are designated
  under section 88 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The classes of projects set
out in Schedules 1 to 3 do not include projects that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} cause a change to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the water level of a water body,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the alignment of a watercourse,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any characteristic of a wetland,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} [[wildlife species]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, listed
    in Schedule 1 of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3), their
    [[residences]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or [[critical
    habitats]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as these terms are defined in
    subsection 2(1) of that Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} include any activity set out in subsection 5(1) of
  the [Migratory Birds Regulations,
  2022](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-105), unless the activity is described
  in subsection 5(2) of those Regulations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} involve the release of a [[deleterious
  substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 34(1) of
  the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) into a water body;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} involve any activity referred to in subsection 5(1)
  of the Canadian Navigable Waters Act, subsection 34.4(1), 35(1) or
  36(3) of the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) or subsection 2(1) of the
  [Scott Islands Protected Marine Area
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-119);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} involve any prohibited activities in areas referred
  to in subsection 35(1) of the [Oceans Act](/eng/acts/O-2.4);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} involve the removal of any structure or resource that
  is of historical, archaeological, paleontological or architectural
  significance; or

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} cause damage to any structure, resource or site that
  is of historical, archaeological, paleontological or architectural
  significance.

## [SCHEDULE 1]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsection 3(1) and section 4)]{.OriginatingRef}[Classes of Projects Carried out on Federal Lands or Lands Outside Canada]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-1513510 .scheduleLabel}
:::

### [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Non-specific Classes]{.HTitleText1} {#part-1non-specific-classes .SchedHeadL1}

- **1** The operation, maintenance or repair of any physical work,
  whether or not the physical work is referred to in this Schedule

- **2** Any physical activity that is carried out entirely within the
  interior of a building

- - **3** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The construction of any well used to conduct
    geotechnical, environmental or scientific investigations but not
    including projects that involve the placement of temporary or
    permanent fill in a water body

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The decommissioning of any well referred to in
    subsection (1)

- **4** The construction, installation, expansion, modification,
  decommissioning, removal, replacement or moving of a physical work
  that has a footprint of no more than 25 m^2^ and that is not otherwise
  referred to in this Schedule, not including any project that involves

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any work in a water body;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the placement of temporary or permanent fill in a
    water body; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the disturbance of known or suspected subsurface
    contamination, unless the project is located within a site that is
    classified as closed in the Federal Contaminated Sites Inventory but
    not including sites closed that require risk management measures

- **5** The construction, installation, expansion, modification,
  decommissioning, removal, replacement or moving of an in-water
  structure that has a footprint of no more than 10 m^2^ and that is not
  otherwise referred to in this Schedule, not including any project that
  involves

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the placement of temporary or permanent fill in a
    water body;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the disturbance of known or suspected subsurface
    contamination, unless the project is located within a site that is
    classified as closed in the Federal Contaminated Sites Inventory but
    not including sites closed that require risk management measures;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the use of vehicles or heavy machinery on the
    substrate of a water body;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the placing or affixing legs or piles in the
    substrate or bed of a water body; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the use of explosives

### [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Building Classes]{.HTitleText1} {#part-2building-classes .SchedHeadL1}

#### [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText2} {#interpretation-and-application .SchedHeadL2}

- **6** In this Part, [[special purpose building]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means a residential accommodation building, a hospital, a health
  centre, a fire, paramedic or police station, an educational
  institution, a recreational, artistic, cultural or sporting facility
  or a community or religious centre.

- **7** The classes of projects described in sections 9 to 11 do not
  include any project that involves

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the placement of temporary or permanent fill in a
    water body;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the disturbance of known or suspected subsurface
    contamination, unless the project is located within a site that is
    classified as closed in the Federal Contaminated Sites Inventory but
    not including sites closed that require risk management measures; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the demolition of a building or special purpose
    building that is less than 30 m from a school, hospital or
    residential building.

- **8** This Part applies to any building, special purpose building,
  prefabricated structure or tent pad.

#### [Physical Works --- Developed Land]{.HTitleText2} {#physical-works-developed-land .SchedHeadL2}

- - **9** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On developed land, the construction,
    installation, decommissioning, removal, replacement, moving or
    demolition of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any building that has a footprint of no more than
      1000 m^2^; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any special purpose building that has a footprint
      of no more than 1500 m^2^

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} On developed land, the construction, installation,
    decommissioning, removal, replacement or moving of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any prefabricated structure that has a footprint
      of no more than 1000 m^2^; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any tent pad that has a footprint of no more than
      1000 m^2^

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} On developed land, the expansion of any building,
    special purpose building, prefabricated structure or tent pad,
    provided that the total footprint of all expansions of that physical
    work is no more than 1000 m^2^

#### [Physical Works --- Land That is not Developed]{.HTitleText2} {#physical-works-land-that-is-not-developed .SchedHeadL2}

- - **10** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On land that is not developed, the
    construction, installation, decommissioning, removal, replacement or
    moving of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any building that has a footprint of no more than
      100 m^2^;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any prefabricated structure that has a footprint
      of no more than 100 m^2^;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any tent pad that has a footprint of no more than
      100 m^2^; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any special purpose building that has a footprint
      of no more than 500 m^2^

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} On land that is not developed, the expansion of any
    building, special purpose building, prefabricated structure or tent
    pad, provided that the total footprint of all expansions of that
    physical work is no more than 100 m^2^

- **11** The modification of any building, special purpose building,
  prefabricated structure or tent pad

### [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Physical Works or Related Physical Works]{.HTitleText1} {#part-3physical-works-or-related-physical-works .SchedHeadL1}

#### [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText2} {#interpretation-and-application-1 .SchedHeadL2}

- **12** In this Part, [[physical work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a lighting system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} signage;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fire suppression infrastructure;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a parking lot;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a paved area;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an access road;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a charging station for electric vehicles;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} infrastructure that generates solar or wind power;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a gravel or stone dust area;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} an accessibility structure for active
    transportation;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} an awning;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a bollard;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} a security system;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} a heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC)
    system; and

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} an air pollution control system.

- **13** The classes of projects described in sections 15 to 17 do not
  include any project that involves

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the placement of temporary or permanent fill in a
    water body; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the disturbance of known or suspected subsurface
    contamination, unless the project is located within a site that is
    classified as closed in the Federal Contaminated Sites Inventory but
    not including sites closed that require risk management measures.

- **14** A physical work is a related physical work when it refers to a
  physical work that is related to an existing building or other
  structure.

#### [Physical Works --- Developed Land]{.HTitleText2} {#physical-works-developed-land-1 .SchedHeadL2}

- - **15** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On developed land, the construction,
    installation or moving of any related physical work that has a
    footprint of no more than 1000 m^2^

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} On developed land, the expansion of any related
    physical work provided that the total footprint of all expansions of
    that physical work is no more than 1000 m^2^

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} On developed land, the decommissioning, removal or
    replacement of any physical work that has a footprint of no more
    than 1000 m^2^

#### [Physical Works --- Land That Is Not Developed]{.HTitleText2} {#physical-works-land-that-is-not-developed-1 .SchedHeadL2}

- - **16** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On land that is not developed, the
    construction, installation or moving of any related physical work
    for which the footprint is no more than 100 m^2^

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} On land that is not developed, the expansion of any
    related physical work, provided that the total footprint of all
    expansions of that physical work is no more than 100 m^2^

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} On land that is not developed, the decommissioning,
    removal or replacement of any physical work that has a footprint of
    no more than 100 m^2^

- **17** The modification of any physical work or related physical work

### [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Utility Infrastructure]{.HTitleText1} {#part-4utility-infrastructure .SchedHeadL1}

#### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#application .SchedHeadL2}

- **18** This Part applies to any hydrant, hook-up, water-related
  utility infrastructure --- other than a water pipeline or water
  treatment facility --- septic system, water treatment facility,
  utility infrastructure set out in section 23 or electrical substation.

- - **19** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The classes of projects described in
    sections 20 to 22 do not include any project that involves

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the placement of temporary or permanent fill in a
      water body;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the crossing of a water body, other than an
      overhead crossing by a telecommunication line or an electrical
      transmission or distribution line of 130 kV or less; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the disturbance of known or suspected subsurface
      contamination, unless the project is located within a site that is
      classified as closed in the Federal Contaminated Sites Inventory
      but not including sites closed that require risk management
      measures.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The class of projects described in section 21 does
    not include any project that involves the use of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} vehicles or heavy machinery on the substrate of a
      water body; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} explosives.

#### [Hydrants and Hook-ups]{.HTitleText2} {#hydrants-and-hook-ups .SchedHeadL2}

- **20** The installation, modification, decommissioning, abandonment,
  removal or replacement of a hydrant or hook-up that is part of a
  utility distribution system

#### [Water-related Utility Infrastructure]{.HTitleText2} {#water-related-utility-infrastructure .SchedHeadL2}

- - **21** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The construction or installation of any
    water-related utility infrastructure that has a footprint of no more
    than 100 m^2^

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The expansion of any water-related utility
    infrastructure referred to in subsection (1), provided that
    expansion does not increase the total footprint of that
    water-related infrastructure to more than the limit set out in that
    subsection

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The decommissioning, removal or replacement of any
    water-related utility infrastructure that has a footprint of no more
    than 1000 m^2^

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} The modification of any water-related utility
    infrastructure that has a footprint of no more than 1000 m^2^

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} The modification of a water treatment facility

- - **22** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On developed land, the installation,
    expansion, removal or replacement of a septic system that has a
    footprint of no more than 1000 m^2^ and that is located more than
    30 m from a water body

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The modification of any septic system

#### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#interpretation .SchedHeadL2}

- **23** For the purposes of sections 25 to 29, [[utility
  infrastructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a water pipeline;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a sewer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a drain;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a steam line;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a service tunnel;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an overhead or underground telecommunication line;
    and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} an overhead or underground electrical transmission
    or distribution line of 130 kV or less, other than an
    interprovincial or international line.

- - **24** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The classes of projects described in
    sections 25 to 29 do not include any project that involves

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the placement of temporary or permanent fill in a
      water body;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the crossing of a water body, other than an
      overhead crossing by a telecommunication line or an electrical
      transmission or distribution line of 130 kV or less; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the disturbance of known or suspected subsurface
      contamination, unless the project is located within a site that is
      classified as closed in the Federal Contaminated Sites Inventory
      but not including sites closed that require risk management
      measures.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The classes of projects described in sections 27 to
    29 do not include any project for which the electrical transmission
    or distribution line referred to in paragraph 23(g) is located

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a protected marine area established under
      subsection 4.1(1) of the [Canada Wildlife Act](/eng/acts/W-9); or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in an area set out in the schedule to the
      [Migratory Bird Sanctuary
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1036), prescribed as a
      migratory bird sanctuary.

#### [Utility Infrastructure]{.HTitleText2} {#utility-infrastructure .SchedHeadL2}

- - **25** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The construction or installation of any
    utility infrastructure referred to in paragraph 23(a), (f) or (g)
    that is no more than 100 m in length

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The lengthening of any utility infrastructure
    referred to in subsection (1) if the length of the utility
    infrastructure and the lengthening is no more than 100 m

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The modification, decommissioning, removal or
    replacement of any utility infrastructure referred to in paragraph
    23(a), (f) or (g) that is no more than 1000 m

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} The construction, installation, lengthening,
    modification, decommissioning, removal or replacement of any utility
    infrastructure referred to in paragraph 23(a), (f) or (g), of any
    length, that is located

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} underneath a railway, road or airport pavement;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} alongside and contiguous to the infrastructure
      referred to in paragraph (a), if on developed land; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} within the right of way of a telecommunication or
      electrical line

- - **26** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The modification, removal or replacement,
    more than 30 m from a water body, of any utility infrastructure
    referred to in any of paragraphs 23(b) to (e) that is no more than
    1000 m in length

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The construction, installation, lengthening,
    modification, abandonment, removal or replacement, more than 30 m
    from a water body, of any utility infrastructure referred to in any
    of paragraphs 23(b) to (e), of any length, that is located

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} underneath a railway, road or airport pavement;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} alongside or contiguous to the infrastructure
      referred to in paragraph (a), if it is located on developed land;
      or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} within the right of way of a telecommunication or
      electrical line

#### [Electrical Substations]{.HTitleText2} {#electrical-substations .SchedHeadL2}

- - **27** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On developed land, the construction,
    installation, decommissioning, removal or replacement of an
    electrical substation that has a footprint of no more than 1000 m^2^
    and that is linked to an electrical transmission or distribution
    line referred to in paragraph 23(g)

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} On developed land, the expansion of an electrical
    substation referred to in subsection (1), provided that expansion

- **33** The decommissioning of any road, parking lot or pull-off
  located within the Town of Banff

- **34** The modification, repair, decommissioning or removal of any
  recreational ground located within the Town of Banff

## [SCHEDULE 3]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsection 3(3) and section 4)]{.OriginatingRef}[Classes of Projects Within an Area Referred to in Schedule I of the [Wildlife Area Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1609)]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-1513868 .scheduleLabel}
:::

### [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Non-specific Classes]{.HTitleText1} {#part-1non-specific-classes-2 .SchedHeadL1}

- **1** The operation, maintenance or repair of any physical work,
  including overhead or underground electrical transmission or
  distribution lines, whether or not the physical work is referred to in
  this Schedule

- **2** Any physical activity that is carried out entirely within the
  interior of a building

- - **3** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The construction of any well used to conduct
    geotechnical, environmental or scientific investigations but not
    including any project that involves the placement of temporary or
    permanent fill in a water body

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The decommissioning of any well referred to in
    subsection (1)

### [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Building Classes]{.HTitleText1} {#part-2building-classes-1 .SchedHeadL1}

#### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#application-4 .SchedHeadL2}

- **4** The classes of projects described in sections 6 to 9 do not
  include any project that involves

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the placement of temporary or permanent fill in a
    water body;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the disturbance of known or suspected subsurface
    contamination, unless the project is located within a site that is
    classified as closed in the Federal Contaminated Sites Inventory but
    not including sites closed that require risk management measures; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} demolition that is to be carried out less than 30 m
    from a building.

- **5** This Part applies to any building located within an area
  referred to in Schedule I of the [Wildlife Area
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1609).

#### [Physical Works --- Developed Land]{.HTitleText2} {#physical-works-developed-land-3 .SchedHeadL2}

- **6** On developed land, the construction, installation,
  decommissioning, removal, replacement, moving or demolition of any
  building that has a footprint of no more than 1000 m^2^

- **7** On developed land, the expansion of any building, provided that
  the total footprint of all expansions of that physical work is no more
  than 1000 m^2^

#### [Physical Works --- Land That Is Not Developed]{.HTitleText2} {#physical-works-land-that-is-not-developed-2 .SchedHeadL2}

- - **8** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On land that is not developed, the
    construction or installation of any building that has a footprint of
    no more than 100 m^2^

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} On land that is not developed, the expansion of any
    building, provided that the total footprint of all expansions of
    that physical work is no more than 100 m^2^

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} On land that is not developed, the decommissioning,
    removal, replacement or moving of any physical work referred to in
    subsection (1), if the footprint is no more than 100 m^2^

- **9** The modification of any building

### [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Other Physical Works]{.HTitleText1} {#part-3other-physical-works .SchedHeadL1}

#### [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText2} {#interpretation-and-application-3 .SchedHeadL2}

- **10** In this Part, [[other physical work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a parking lot;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a paved area; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a gravel or stone dust area.

- **11** The classes of projects described in sections 13 and 14 do not
  include any project that involves

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the placement of temporary or permanent fill in a
    water body; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the disturbance of known or suspected subsurface
    contamination, unless the project is located within a site that is
    classified as closed in the Federal Contaminated Sites Inventory but
    not including sites closed that require risk management measures.

- **12** This Part applies to any other physical work.

#### [Physical Works --- Developed Land]{.HTitleText2} {#physical-works-developed-land-4 .SchedHeadL2}

- **13** On developed land, the decommissioning, removal or replacement
  of any other physical work that has a footprint of no more than
  1000 m^2^

#### [Physical Works --- Land that is not Developed]{.HTitleText2} {#physical-works-land-that-is-not-developed-3 .SchedHeadL2}

- **14** On land that is not developed, the decommissioning, removal or
  replacement of any other physical work that has a footprint of no more
  than 100 m^2^

### [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Utility Infrastructure]{.HTitleText1} {#part-4utility-infrastructure-1 .SchedHeadL1}

#### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#application-5 .SchedHeadL2}

- - **15** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The classes of projects described in
    sections 17 to 19 do not include any project that involves

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the placement of temporary or permanent fill in a
      water body; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the disturbance of known or suspected subsurface
      contamination, unless the project is located within a site that is
      classified as closed in the Federal Contaminated Sites Inventory
      but not including sites closed that require risk management
      measures.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The classes of projects described in section 17 do
    not include any project that involves the use of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} vehicles or heavy machinery on the substrate of a
      water body; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} explosives.

- **16** This Part applies to any water-related utility infrastructure,
  other than a water pipeline or water treatment facility, as well as
  any water pipeline.

#### [Water-related Utility Infrastructure]{.HTitleText2} {#water-related-utility-infrastructure-1 .SchedHeadL2}

- **17** The decommissioning, removal or replacement of any
  water-related utility infrastructure that has a footprint of no more
  than 1000 m^2^

#### [Water Pipelines]{.HTitleText2} {#water-pipelines .SchedHeadL2}

- **18** The decommissioning, removal or replacement of any water
  pipeline that is no more than 1000 m in length

- **19** The decommissioning, removal or replacement of any water
  pipeline, of any length, that is located

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} underneath a railway, a road or airport pavement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} alongside and contiguous to the infrastructure
    referred to in paragraph (a), if on developed land; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} within the right of way of a telecommunication or
    electrical line

### [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Linear Infrastructures]{.HTitleText1} {#part-5linear-infrastructures .SchedHeadL1}

#### [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText2} {#interpretation-and-application-4 .SchedHeadL2}

- **20** In this Part, [[linear infrastructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a road; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a guardrail, handrail, curb, fence or gate.

- **21** The classes of projects described in sections 23 and 24 do not
  include any project that involves

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the placement of temporary or permanent fill in a
    water body; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the disturbance of known or suspected subsurface
    contamination, unless the project is located within a site that is
    classified as closed in the Federal Contaminated Sites Inventory but
    not including sites closed that require risk management measures.

- **22** This Part applies to any linear infrastructure.

#### [Linear Infrastructures]{.HTitleText2} {#linear-infrastructures .SchedHeadL2}

- **23** The decommissioning, removal or replacement of any linear
  infrastructure referred to in paragraph 20(a) that is no more than
  100 m in length

- - **24** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The construction or installation of any
    linear infrastructure referred to in paragraph 20(b) that is related
    to a building or other structure and that is no more than 100 m in
    length

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The lengthening of any linear infrastructure
    referred to in paragraph 20(b), if the lengthening is no more than
    100 m in length

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The modification, decommissioning, removal or
    replacement of any linear infrastructure referred to in paragraph
    20(b)

### [PART 6]{.HLabel1}[Mobility Projects]{.HTitleText1} {#part-6mobility-projects .SchedHeadL1}

#### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#application-6 .SchedHeadL2}

- **25** The classes of projects described in sections 27 to 29 do not
  include any project that involves

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the placement of temporary or permanent fill in a
    water body; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the disturbance of known or suspected subsurface
    contamination, unless the project is located within a site that is
    classified as closed in the Federal Contaminated Sites Inventory but
    not including sites closed that require risk management measures.

- **26** This part applies to any sidewalk, boardwalk, path or trail.

#### [Mobility --- Developed Land]{.HTitleText2} {#mobility-developed-land-1 .SchedHeadL2}

- - **27** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On developed land, the construction or
    installation of any sidewalk, boardwalk, path or trail that is
    related to a building or other structure and that has a footprint of
    no more than 1000 m^2^

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} On developed land, the expansion of any sidewalk,
    boardwalk, path or trail that is related to a building or other
    structure, provided that the total footprint of all expansions of
    that physical work is no more than 1000 m^2^

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} On developed land, the decommissioning, removal or
    replacement of any sidewalk, boardwalk, path or trail that has a
    footprint of no more than 1000 m^2^

#### [Mobility --- Land That Is Not Developed]{.HTitleText2} {#mobility-land-that-is-not-developed-1 .SchedHeadL2}

- - **28** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On land that is not developed, the
    construction or installation of any sidewalk, boardwalk, path or
    trail that is related to a building or other structure and that has
    a footprint of no more than 100 m^2^

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} On land that is not developed, the expansion of any
    sidewalk, boardwalk, path or trail that is related to a building or
    other structure, provided that the total footprint of all expansions
    of that physical work is no more than 100 m^2^

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} On land that is not developed, the decommissioning,
    removal or replacement of any sidewalk, boardwalk, path or trail
    that has a footprint of no more than 100 m^2^

- **29** The modification of any sidewalk, boardwalk, path or trail

### [PART 7]{.HLabel1}[Structures in or Near Water]{.HTitleText1} {#part-7structures-in-or-near-water .SchedHeadL1}

- **30** The modification or removal of any marine navigation aid
  structure or structure used for berthing or mooring that has a
  footprint of no more than 1000 m^2^ but not including any project that
  involves

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the placement of temporary or permanent fill in a
    water body;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the disturbance of known or suspected subsurface
    contamination, unless the project is located within a site that is
    classified as closed in the Federal Contaminated Sites Inventory but
    not including sites closed that require risk management measures;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the use of vehicles or heavy machinery on the
    substrate of a water body; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the use of explosives
