## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-812839 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Territorial Lands
      Act](/eng/acts/T-7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[boundary post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[boundary post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2020-209, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day other than a
      Saturday or a holiday. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Chief]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Chief]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chief of Financial
      Analysis and Royalties Administration Section of the Department of
      Indian Affairs and Northern Development. ([chef]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[contiguous]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[contiguous]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means units, claims or lands
      that, partly or totally, share a common boundary and not merely a
      point. ([contigu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[corner post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[corner post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2020-209, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[cost of work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cost of work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means expenses incurred in
      performing work, but excludes all of the following:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} transportation costs outside Canada for persons
        who performed work or for equipment used in work;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} taxes and fees paid to any level of government
        or any institution of public government;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} costs of staking and recording a claim;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} legal fees;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} administrative costs; and

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} expenses incurred in performing work referred
        to in paragraph (c) of the definition of
        [[work]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} that exceed 10% of the total
        expenses incurred in performing the undertakings referred to in
        paragraph (a) and subparagraph (b)(ii) of that
        definition. ([coût des travaux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[depreciable assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[depreciable assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means buildings, plant,
      machinery and equipment. ([actif amortissable]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[environmental baseline studies]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[environmental baseline studies]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      description of selected environmental attributes that existed
      before mineral exploration or mining development and that are used
      to establish a benchmark from which to measure changes to the
      environment. Selected environmental attributes include
      meteorologic, hydrologic and hydro-geologic attributes, surface
      water and groundwater quality, aquatic resources, soil profiling,
      ecosystems, wildlife and wildlife habitat, cultural heritage and
      archaeology.  ([études environnementales de base]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[exploration cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[exploration cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an expense incurred
      for the purpose of determining the existence, location, extent,
      quality or economic potential of a mineral deposit in Nunavut, but
      does not include an expense incurred for the purpose of bringing a
      mine into production. ([frais d'exploration]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a mine, means
      the fiscal period of the mine's operator as that period is defined
      in section 249.1 of the [Income Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([exercice]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[holder of the surface rights]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[holder of the surface rights]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      lessee or registered holder of the surface rights to the
      land. ([titulaire des droits de surface]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[legal post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[legal post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2020-209, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence to prospect
      referred to in section 3. ([licence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[mine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an undertaking that produces or
      has produced minerals or processed minerals from lands within the
      Nunavut Mining District referred to in section 2, and includes the
      depreciable assets that are located in Nunavut and used in
      connection with the undertaking. ([mine]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[mineral]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mineral]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means amber and any naturally
      occurring inorganic substance, including frac sand, found in the
      Nunavut Mining District referred to in section 2, excluding
      material the taking of which is regulated under the [Territorial
      Quarrying
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1527). ([minéral]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[mining property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mining property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a recorded claim or a leased claim within the
        boundaries of which a mine or part of a mine is situated; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a group of contiguous recorded or leased claims
        within the boundaries of which a mine or part of a mine is
        situated and

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that belong to the same owner, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the mine is operated as a joint venture,
          that are owned exclusively by the members of the joint venture
          or parties related to the members of the joint venture,
          regardless of the degree of ownership of each recorded claim
          or leased claim. ([propriété minière]{.DefinedTermLink
          lang="fr"})

  [[mining reclamation trust]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mining reclamation trust]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a trust
      that is established for a mine and that is created

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsection 76(1) of the
        [Nunavut Waters and Nunavut Surface Rights Tribunal
        Act](/eng/acts/N-28.8); or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as a condition of

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a lease issued under the [Territorial Lands
          Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1525),

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a contract with the Minister relating to the
          reclamation or environmental management of a mining property,
          or

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a permit issued under the [Territorial Land
          Use Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1524). ([fiducie
          de restauration minière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Mining Recorder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Mining Recorder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person
      designated by the Minister as the Mining Recorder. ([registraire
      minier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[mining royalty valuer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mining royalty valuer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
      acting on the Minister's behalf for the purpose of ascertaining
      the value of minerals or processed minerals produced from a
      mine. ([évaluateur des redevances minières]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a recorded claim,
      leased claim, mine or mining property, means any person with a
      legal or beneficial interest in the recorded claim, leased claim,
      mine or mining property. ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[precious stone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[precious stone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a diamond, a
      sapphire, an emerald or a ruby. ([pierre
      précieuse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[prescribed form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prescribed form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a form prescribed by
      the Minister under section 28 of the Act.  ([formule
      prescrite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means crushing, grinding,
      flotation, beneficiation, concentrating, milling, roasting,
      smelting, leaching, recrystallization or refining performed on
      minerals, and if the output of a mine is precious stones, cleaning
      and sorting that output. ([traitement]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[processing assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[processing assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means tailings disposal
      facilities and depreciable assets that are located in Nunavut and
      that are used directly and exclusively in processing. ([biens
      utilisés pour le traitement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[prospecting permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prospecting permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prospecting
      permit issued under the [Nunavut Mining
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-69) as they read
      immediately before November 1, 2020. ([permis de
      prospection]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[related]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[related]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of two or more
      persons, means that the persons are

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} related persons within the meaning of section
        251 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), read without
        reference to paragraph 251(5)(b) of that Act;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} associated corporations within the meaning of
        section 256 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), read
        without reference to subsection 256(1.4) of that Act;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} affiliated persons within the meaning of
        section 251.1 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} other than for the purpose of subsection 74(1),
        owners or operators of the same mine. ([liées]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[Supervising Mining Recorder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Supervising Mining Recorder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      person designated by the Minister as the Supervising Mining
      Recorder. ([registraire minier en chef]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[undeducted balance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[undeducted balance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a depreciation allowance, the
        original cost of the depreciable assets in respect of which the
        depreciation allowance is claimed, less any depreciation
        allowances previously claimed in respect of those assets;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a development allowance, the
        undeducted balance of costs eligible for a development allowance
        under paragraph 70(1)(i); and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a mining reclamation trust
        contribution allowance, the total of all contributions made to
        the mining reclamation trust, less any allowances previously
        claimed. ([fraction non amortie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a unit described in section 4

  [[witness post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[witness post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2020-209, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any of the following undertakings that are
        performed in respect of a recorded claim --- or, for the purpose
        of subsection 42(3), in respect of a claim prior to its
        recording --- for the purpose of assessing its mineral
        potential:

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} examination of outcrops and surficial
          deposits,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} excavation,

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} sampling,

        - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} geochemical study or analysis,

        - [(v)]{.lawlabel} drilling,

        - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} geological mapping,

        - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} geophysical study or analysis,

        - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} remote sensing, if one or more
          undertakings set out in subparagraphs (i) to (vii) have been
          performed to evaluate the results of the remote sensing and
          are reported on, together with the remote sensing, in
          accordance with subsection 42(1),

        - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the placing of grid lines in the field for
          the purpose of performing any of the undertakings referred to
          in subparagraphs (i) to (vii),

        - [(x)]{.lawlabel} petrography,

        - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} data analysis, map generation and
          preparation of reports that are submitted under these
          Regulations in respect of undertakings referred to in
          subparagraphs (i) to (viii) and (x);

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any of the following undertakings that are
        performed in respect of a recorded claim:

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the preparation of a plan of survey under
          paragraph 57(1)(a);

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the building of roads, airstrips or docks
          for the purpose of performing any of the undertakings referred
          to in paragraph (a); and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} environmental baseline studies that are
        conducted in conjunction with undertakings referred to in
        subparagraphs (a)(i) to (vii) and (ix) or subparagraph (b)(ii),
        as well as analysis of the data resulting from the studies, map
        generation and the preparation of an appendix as required in
        paragraph 4(t) of Schedule 2. ([travaux]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-812912 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nunavut Mining District

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of the Nunavut Mining District, the area of which is described
in Schedule 2 to the [Northwest Territories Mining District and Nunavut
Mining District Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2000-53).

## [Licence to Prospect]{.HTitleText1} {#h-812915 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of licence

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Mining Recorder must issue a licence to prospect to a person who has
  applied for one and paid the applicable fee set out in Schedule 1 if
  the person is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an individual who is 18 years of age or older; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a company that is incorporated or registered under
    the Business Corporations Act (Nunavut), S.N.W.T. 1996, c. 19, or
    that is incorporated under the [Canada Business Corporations
    Act](/eng/acts/C-44).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for licence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for a licence must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name, address, telephone number and
    email address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number and email
    address of every person who is authorized to act on behalf of the
    applicant;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an applicant who is an individual,
    proof that they are 18 years of age or older; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an applicant that is a corporation,
    the title of the Act referred to in paragraph (1)(b) under which the
    corporation is incorporated or registered under.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of licence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A licence is valid from the date of its issuance or
  renewal until March 31 of the following calendar year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for renewal

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A licence may be renewed by making an application to
  the Mining Recorder containing the information set out in paragraphs
  (2)(a) and (b) and, in the case of an applicant that is a corporation,
  the information set out in paragraph 2(d). The application must be
  made not earlier than January 1 preceding the end of the period of
  validity of the licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change in information

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If, after the issuance or renewal of the licence,
  there is any change in information referred to in paragraph (2)(a)
  or (b) or, in the case of an applicant that is a corporation, any
  change in the information referred to in paragraph 2(d), the licensee
  must provide the new information to the Mining Recorder within 10
  business days after the day on which the information was changed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence not transferable

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A licence is not transferable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence authorization --- licensee or
authorized person

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Only a licensee or a person
authorized to act on behalf of a licensee may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prospect for the purpose of recording a claim;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} make an application for the recording of a claim, for
  a lease of a recorded claim or for the renewal of that lease;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} acquire, by themselves or with another licensee, a
  recorded claim, a lease of a recorded claim or an interest in either
  of them; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} be issued a certificate of work under section 47 or a
  certificate of extension under subsection 49.2(1).

## [Prohibitions Respecting Prospecting and Mining]{.HTitleText1} {#h-812939 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lands not open for prospecting

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited to prospect on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} lands covered by a prospecting permit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} lands covered by a recorded claim or a leased
    claim;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} lands mentioned in any of subsections 52(5) and
    (6), 56(1) and (2), 62.1(5) and (6) and 67(2), section 85 and
    subsections 93(4) to (6) that are not open for prospecting;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} lands that have been withdrawn from disposal under
    paragraph 23(a) of the Act or set apart and appropriated under any
    of paragraphs 23(b) to (e) of the Act by the Governor in Council;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} lands for which the minerals have been granted by
    the Crown;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} lands subject to a prohibition on prospecting under
    a land use plan that has been approved under federal legislation or
    under a land claims agreement; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} lands used as a cemetery.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The prohibition under paragraph (1)(a) does not apply
  to the permittee and the prohibition under paragraph (1)(b) does not
  apply to the claim holder or the lessee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition on removing minerals

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited to remove minerals or processed

  mine building, or to create any tailings or waste disposal area for
  the purpose of the commencement of production from a mine, unless the
  claim holder has been issued a lease of the surface rights to, or a
  grant of, the land covered by the claim.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a unit contains, partly or totally, only one
  recorded claim and a land referred to in subsection 5(1) or a land of
  Nunavut to which these Regulations do not apply and that land is no
  longer referred to in subsection 5(1) or becomes subject to these
  Regulations, that land subsequently becomes part of that recorded
  claim.

  claim

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a unit contains, partly or totally, more than one
  recorded claim and a land referred to in subsection 5(1) or a land of
  Nunavut to which these Regulations do not apply and that land is no
  longer referred to in subsection 5(1) or becomes subject to these
  Regulations, that land subsequently becomes part of one of those
  recorded claims, in accordance with the following order of priority:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if at least one recorded claim is contiguous to
    that land,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the claim that is contiguous to it and that was
      recorded under subsection 96(2) or, if two or more are contiguous
      to it, the claim that encompasses the claim that was staked first,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the claim that is contiguous to it and that was
      recorded under subsection 13(1) or, if two or more are contiguous
      to it, the claim that was recorded first; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if there is no recorded claim that is contiguous to
    that land,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the claim recorded under subsection 96(2) that
      encompasses the claim that was staked first, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the claim that was recorded first under
      subsection 13(1).

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The report must be prepared and signed

    subsection 1(1) of the Engineers and Geoscientists Act, S Nu

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The report must be accompanied by the following
  documents:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement of work in the prescribed form;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a table setting out the cost of work by type of
    work, together with, for each type of work, details of the costs
    sufficient to enable evaluation of the report in accordance with
    subsection 43(1); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a table setting out the cost of work that is
    allocated to each claim.

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a holder of a recorded claim uses their own
  equipment to do work or personally does work,

    be more than the cost of engaging another person to do that work.

  supporting documents used to justify the cost of work and make them
  available on request to the Mining Recorder until the holder has

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Work reported once

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Work reported must not be reported in any other
  report.

### [Suspension]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1283580 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for suspension

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  holder of a recorded claim is unable to do the work as required under
  subsection 39(1) because the claim holder is, for reasons beyond the
  claim holder's control, waiting for a public authority to give an
  authorization or decision without which the work cannot proceed, the
  claim holder may make an application for a suspension of one year with
  respect to the claim --- beginning on the anniversary date of the
  recording of the claim --- of the work requirements set out in
  subsection 39(1) and the charges under subsection 40(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be made to the Supervising
  Mining Recorder not later than 120 days after the end of the year for
  which the suspension is applied for and must be accompanied by
  documents showing that the claim holder is waiting for the
  authorization or decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Companies' Creditors Arrangement
  Act](/eng/acts/C-36)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an order under section 11.02 of the [Companies'
  Creditors Arrangement Act](/eng/acts/C-36) has been made with respect
  to a claim holder, the holder of a recorded claim may make an
  application for a suspension, with respect to that claim, of the work
  requirements set out in subsection 39(1) and the charges under
  subsection 40(1) until the first anniversary date of the recording of
  the claim that is at least 12 months after the day on which the order
  has ceased to have effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The application must be made to the Supervising
  Mining Recorder not later than 120 days after the day on which the
  order was made and must be accompanied by a copy of the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recording of suspension

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the requirements set out in subsections (1)
  and (2) or (3) and (4) are met, the Supervising Mining Recorder must
  record the suspension.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of suspension

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} When a suspension of the work requirements set out in
  subsection 39(1) and the charges under subsection 40(1) is recorded,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the duration of the claim is extended by the
    duration of the suspension; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of determining a year referred to
    in subsections 39(1) and 40(1), any year included in a suspension is
    to be excluded.

### [Reduction of Area of a Recorded Claim]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1283607 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder of a recorded claim (in this section referred to as the
  "original claim") may make an application to the Mining Recorder to
  reduce the number of units included in the claim if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a certificate of work in respect of the claim sets
    out a cost of work of at least \$135 per unit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} each unit included in the reduced-area claim is
    contiguous to another unit in that claim; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the units included in the reduced-area claim do not
    enclose a unit that is not included in that claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}One application per year

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Not more than one application for a reduced-area
  claim may be made per year beginning on the date of the recording of
  the original claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recording of reduced-area claim

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Mining Recorder must record the reduced-area
  claim if the conditions set out in subsections (1) and (2) are met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of recording

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} When a reduced-area claim is recorded,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the recording date of the original claim is
    considered to be its recording date;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information recorded, including the
    applications and the documents filed with respect to the original
    claim, is considered to have been recorded or presented with respect
    to the reduced-area claim; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the recording of the original claim is cancelled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opening of lands for prospecting

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (6) and section 14, the lands
  in the original claim that are not within the reduced-area claim are
  open for prospecting, and the units containing those lands are
  available for recording as a claim, beginning on the 31st day after
  the day on which the recording of the original claim is cancelled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delay in opening lands ---
  environmental damage

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe
  that there is unremedied environmental damage to the lands referred to
  in subsection (5), the Minister may delay opening the lands for
  prospecting and making available the units containing those lands for
  recording as a claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} For one year after the recording of the original
  claim is cancelled under paragraph (4)(c), the former claim holder and
  any person related to the former claim holder are not permitted to
  apply to record a claim that includes any unit that was included in
  the original claim but does not form part of the reduced-area claim or
  to acquire a legal or beneficial interest in respect of that claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reduction --- subsections 39(1) and
  40(1)

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} For the application of subsections 39(1) and 40(1),
  the number of units included in the claim is considered to be reduced
  on the first anniversary date of the recording of the claim following
  the reduction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

- **[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the transfer is made to a licensee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the transfer of a recorded claim or

  to which the lease applies.

  property

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a recorded claim or a lease of a recorded claim or
  an interest in either of them is part of a mining property, its
  transfer may be recorded only if security in the amount of any unpaid
  royalties in relation to the mining property has been deposited with
  the Minister.

## [Royalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-813547 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement of production of mine

- **[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of these Regulations, the date on which a mine commences
  production is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the mine includes a mill or concentrator, the
    first day of the first 90-day period during which the mill or
    concentrator operates at an average of at least 60% of its rated
    capacity; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} otherwise, the day the mine begins to produce
    minerals in reasonable commercial quantities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumptions respecting mineral or
  processed mineral

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a mineral or
  processed mineral is considered to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be produced and be part of the output of a mine if
    the mineral or processed mineral is in a saleable form or has been
    removed from the mine; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} form part of the output of the mine if it is
    produced from the reprocessing of tailings from a mine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumptions respecting related persons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if minerals or processed minerals that have been
    sold by an operator to a person not related to the operator are
    later sold to a person related to the operator, those minerals or
    processed minerals are considered to have been sold by the operator
    to a related person; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if minerals or processed minerals that have been
    sold by an operator to a person related to the operator are later
    sold to a person not related to the operator and proof of that sale
    is provided, those minerals or processed minerals are considered to
    have been sold by the operator to a person not related to the
    operator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Royalties payable to Receiver General

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The royalties payable to the Receiver General of
  Canada under subsection (1) in respect of a mine accrue during a
  fiscal year as the output of a mine is produced and must be remitted
  to the Chief not later than the last day of the fourth month after the
  end of that fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint and several liability

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to paragraph 74(1)(b), any person who was an
  owner or operator of a mine during the fiscal year in respect of which
  the royalties were payable is jointly and severally liable for the
  entire amount of the royalties payable in respect of the period during
  which that person was an owner or operator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of value of output

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the value of the
  output of a mine for a fiscal year must be calculated in accordance
  with the formula

  ::: {#813578 .Subsection}
  A + B -- C + D + E + F + G + H -- I + J

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the total of
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proceeds from sales, during the fiscal
        year, of minerals or processed minerals produced from the mine
        to persons not related to the operator, if proof of those sales
        is provided,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the market value of any minerals or processed
        minerals produced from the mine that were sold or transferred to
        a person related to the operator, or to any other person if the
        proof of that disposition is not provided, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the minerals or processed minerals produced
        from the mine are precious stones that have been cut or polished
        before their sale or transfer, the market value of those
        precious stones before they were cut or polished;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the market value of any inventories of minerals and processed
      minerals produced from the mine, as at the end of the fiscal year,
      determined under subsection (9);

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the market value of any inventories of minerals and processed
      minerals produced from the mine, as at the beginning of the fiscal
      year, determined under subsection (9);

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the lesser of
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any payment received during the
        fiscal year that is related to a cost that has been claimed as a
        deduction or allowance under this section, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that cost;

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is any excess amount referred to in paragraph 70(5)(b);

  [F]{.dfn}
  :   is any amount withdrawn, during the fiscal year, from a mining
      reclamation trust established in respect of lands to which these
      Regulations apply, up to the maximum of the total of the amounts
      contributed to the trust;

  [G]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of any proceeds received, during the fiscal year,
      from insurance on minerals or processed minerals produced from the
      mine;

  [H]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of any grants in respect of the mine that were made
      to the operator, or of any loans to the operator in respect of the
      mine that were forgiven, by the federal government during the
      fiscal year;

  [I]{.dfn}
  :   is the total of the deductions and allowances claimed under
      subsection 70(1); and

  [J]{.dfn}
  :   is the total of
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the sum of the amounts
        referred to in paragraphs 70(8)(d) and (9)(e) exceeds the
        undeducted balance of the depreciable assets eligible for a
        depreciation allowance at the end of the fiscal year, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the sum of the amounts
        referred to in paragraphs 70(9)(c) and (d) exceeds the
        undeducted balance of the development allowance at the end of
        the fiscal year.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint venture --- determination of
  value of A

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of determining the value of A in
  subsection (4), if a mine is operated as a joint venture whose members
  deliver separate mining royalty returns under subsection 74(1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a diversion of any or all of the production of the
    mine from one member of the joint venture to another does not
    constitute a sale or transfer for the purposes of subsection 77(2),
    even if consideration is paid for the diversion; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any consideration paid to the member from whom the
    production was diverted must be included by that member as proceeds
    of sale of minerals or processed minerals produced from the mine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain costs and values excluded

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Costs related to the production of or value for
  minerals or processed minerals from lands other than lands to which
  these Regulations apply must not be taken into account for the
  purposes of determining the values of A to D, G and I in subsection
  (4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Last year of production --- option for
  calculation

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a mining royalty return for the last
  fiscal year of production of a mine, the operator may, for the purpose
  of determining the value of B in subsection (4), elect to use the
  actual proceeds from the sale to a party not related to the operator
  of minerals or processed minerals in inventory at the end of the
  fiscal year, if proof of that sale is provided, rather than the market
  value of the inventory of minerals or processed minerals at the end of
  that fiscal year as required under subsection (4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election is irrevocable

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} An election made under subsection (7) is irrevocable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Market value of precious stones

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} If the minerals or processed minerals referred to in
  paragraphs (b) and (c) of the description of A, and in the
  descriptions of B and C, in subsection (4) are precious stones, the
  market value of the precious stones is as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the mining royalty valuer and the operator agree
    on a value for the stones, that value; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the mining royalty valuer and the operator
    cannot agree on a value for the stones, the maximum amount that
    could be realized from the sale of the stones on the open market
    after they are sorted into market assortments.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing of market value of precious
  stones

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (9), the market value
  must be determined

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when the value is calculated for inventory
    purposes, at the beginning or end of the fiscal year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the value is calculated for any other purpose,
    as of the last time the precious stones were valued by the mining
    royalty valuer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Market value of other minerals

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} If the minerals or processed minerals referred to in
  paragraphs (b) and (c) of the description of A, and in the
  descriptions of B and C, in subsection (4) are not precious stones,
  their market value is the price that could be obtained from their sale
  to a person who is not related to the operator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing of market value of other
  minerals

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (11), the market value
  must be determined

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when the value is calculated for inventory
    purposes, at the beginning or end of the fiscal year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the value is calculated for any other purpose,
    at the time the minerals or processed minerals are shipped from the
    mine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion respecting hedging
  transactions

  [(13)]{.lawlabel} Gains and losses from hedging transactions must not
  be included in calculating the value of the mine's output.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exchange rate

  [(14)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of these Regulations, the Bank of
  Canada's exchange rate must be used to convert foreign currencies into
  Canadian dollars

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as of the date of that transaction if a transaction
    is carried out in a foreign currency; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as of the last day of the fiscal year if
    inventories have been valued in a foreign currency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operating costs for operations outside
  Canada

  [(15)]{.lawlabel} When operating costs are incurred for operations
  outside of Canada, the operator may convert foreign currency
  transactions for those costs into Canadian dollars using the Bank of
  Canada's average exchange rate for the month in which those costs were
  incurred.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deductions

- **[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  calculating the value of the output of a mine for a fiscal year, only
  the following deductions and allowances may be claimed:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the costs, incurred during the fiscal year, of
    sorting, valuing, marketing and selling the minerals or processed
    minerals produced from the mine;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the costs, incurred during the fiscal year, of
    insurance, storage, handling and transportation to the processing
    plant or market, in respect of the minerals or processed minerals
    produced from the mine;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the costs, incurred during the fiscal year, of
    mining and processing minerals or processed minerals from the mine;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the costs, incurred during the fiscal year, of
    repair, maintenance or reclamation at the mine;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the consideration paid by a member of a joint
    venture for minerals or processed minerals diverted from another
    member of the joint venture, when each member is delivering a
    separate mining royalty return in accordance with section 74;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} general and administrative costs incurred during
    the fiscal year for property, employees or operations at the mine
    that are not otherwise allocated to operating costs;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} exploration costs incurred during the fiscal year
    by an owner of the mine, other than on the mining property, if those
    costs have not been otherwise claimed as an allowance or deduction
    under these Regulations, in an amount not greater than 10% of the
    value of the output of the mine multiplied by the owner's share of
    that output, calculated

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} after deduction of the costs referred to in
      paragraphs (a) to (f), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} before the deduction of any depreciation
      allowance, mining reclamation trust contribution allowance,
      development allowance or processing allowance;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (5), paragraphs (8)(d) and
    (9)(e) and subsection (10), a depreciation allowance for the
    depreciable assets of the mine, and for the depreciable assets of
    any facilities located outside Nunavut that are used for the
    processing of minerals or processed minerals produced from the mine
    in an amount not exceeding the undeducted balance of the cost of
    those depreciable assets at the end of the fiscal year of the mine;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a development allowance, not exceeding the
    undeducted balance at the end of the fiscal year of the mine of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} exploration costs incurred, before the date of
      commencement of production, on the mining property as constituted
      on the date of commencement of production and not deducted under
      paragraph (g) in respect of any other mine,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all costs incurred before the date of the
      commencement of production for the purposes of bringing the mine
      into production less the total of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the value of any minerals or processed minerals
        produced from the mining property that were sold or transferred
        before the date of commencement of production, calculated in
        accordance with section 69, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the market value of any minerals or processed
        minerals produced from the mining property that are in inventory
        on the date of commencement of production, calculated in
        accordance with section 69,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} exploration costs incurred on the mining
      property after the date of commencement of production,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} costs incurred after the date of commencement of
      production for workings designed for continuing use, including the
      clearing, removing or stripping of overburden from a new deposit
      at the mine, the sinking, excavation or extension of a mine shaft,
      main haulage way or similar underground workings, the construction
      of an adit or other underground entry and the construction of a
      road or of tailings disposal structures at the mine, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} if minerals or processed minerals are being
      produced in commercial quantities from a recorded claim or leased
      claim that was incorporated into the mining property after the
      date of the commencement of production of the mine, or from
      another mining property that was incorporated into the mining
      property on which the mine is located after the date of the
      commencement of production,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the claim or lease was purchased, the
        purchase price of the claim or lease or the amount referred to
        in clause (B), whichever is the lesser, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the costs referred to in
        subparagraphs (i) and (ii) that were incurred on the
        incorporated claim or lease and that have not been previously
        claimed as a deduction or allowance under these Regulations;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a mining reclamation trust contribution allowance,
    determined by the operator, not exceeding the undeducted balance at
    the end of the fiscal year of amounts contributed to the mining
    reclamation trust with respect to any environmental impact resulting
    from the mining of minerals from lands to which these Regulations
    apply;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} if minerals or processed minerals are processed by
    the operator of the mine before their sale or transfer, an annual
    processing allowance equal to the lesser of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), 8% of the original
      cost of the processing assets used by the operator in the
      processing of the output of the mine during the fiscal year, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 65% of the value of the output of the mine,
      after deduction of the amounts referred to in paragraphs (a) to
      (j); and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} if minerals or processed minerals from the mine are
    processed at another mine, or at any facilities located outside
    Nunavut that are used for the processing of minerals or processed
    minerals produced from another mine that is owned by the operator or
    by a person related to the operator, the total of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the costs of the other mine that
      are not deductible under paragraph (8)(b),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the processing allowance for
      the other mine is reduced under paragraph (8)(c), and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the undeducted balance of

      adjusted under paragraph (8)(d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction respecting related person

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When the operator of a mine claims a deduction for
  costs incurred in a transaction with a related person, the costs
  allowed as a deduction under this section must be the amount of the
  actual costs incurred by the related person, exclusive of any profit,
  gain or commission to the related person or to any other related
  person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Depreciation allowance

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A depreciation allowance may be claimed in respect of
  a depreciable asset in the fiscal year in which it is first used in
  the operations of the mine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reduction in depreciation allowance

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} When an operator disposes of, or receives insurance
  proceeds in respect of, assets for which a depreciation allowance has
  been claimed,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the undeducted balance of depreciable assets must
    be reduced by the lesser of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the proceeds of disposition or insurance

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the lesser of the amounts referred to in
    subparagraphs (a)(i) and (ii) exceeds the undeducted balance of
    depreciable assets in the fiscal year in which the assets were

    of the mine for that fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Depreciation allowance --- proceeds of
  disposition

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (5), when the operator
  of a mine sells to a related person an asset for which a depreciation
  allowance has been claimed or removes the asset from the mine, the
  proceeds of disposition of the asset are the amount that could be
  expected to be realized from the sale of the asset to a person not
  related to the operator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Depreciation allowance --- purchase
  cost

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} When the operator of a mine purchases from a related
  person an asset that is eligible for a depreciation allowance or
  transfers to the mine an asset from another mine owned by the
  operator, the cost of the asset for the purposes of calculating a
  depreciation allowance is the amount that the operator could be
  expected to pay to purchase that asset from a person not related to
  the operator.

    surveying a claim for the purpose of taking it to lease;

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} rent paid for the lease of a recorded claim under
    these Regulations;

  - [(w)]{.lawlabel} the cost of preparing any financial information not
    required for the calculation of mining royalties;

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} any cost incurred after any precious stones have
    been last valued by the mining royalty valuer, if those stones were
    sold or transferred to a related party, or to any other person if
    proof of the disposition is not provided, or if the stones were cut
    and polished before their sale or transfer;

  - [(y)]{.lawlabel} any costs related to public, community or
    government relations unless those costs were incurred for
    environmental assessments or other regulatory processes; and

  - [(z)]{.lawlabel} any fines, penalties or bribes.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of owner or operator has no effect

- **[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  change in the ownership or of the operator of a mine does not affect

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the undeducted balance of the depreciable assets
    eligible for a depreciation allowance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the undeducted balance of the costs eligible for a
    development allowance;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the undeducted balance of contributions to a mining
    reclamation trust; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the original cost of the assets used for
    calculating a processing allowance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs not eligible for development
  allowance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to paragraph 70(1)(i), if the recording of a
  claim is cancelled, or a lease expires or is cancelled, any costs
  incurred in respect of that claim or lease that would otherwise be
  eligible for a development allowance are no longer eligible for a
  development allowance in respect of any mine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Combining operations on two mining
  properties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a mining property is acquired by the operator of
  another mine and the operations on the two mining properties are
  combined to become a single operation, the undeducted balance of the
  costs eligible for the development allowance, the undeducted balance
  of the cost of the depreciable assets eligible for the depreciation
  allowance, the undeducted balance of the contributions to a mining
  reclamation trust and the original cost of the processing assets
  eligible for the processing allowance for each mine must be combined.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Valuations when mining property
  purchased from Crown

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (3), if an operator
  purchases a mining property from the Crown, the value of the
  undeducted balance of the costs eligible for the development
  allowance, of the undeducted balance of the cost of the depreciable
  assets eligible for the depreciation allowance and of the original
  cost of the processing assets eligible for the processing allowance of
  the purchased mine is the lesser of the value established at the time
  the Crown acquired the mining property and the value established in
  the agreement of purchase and sale for that property.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of royalty return

- **[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On or
  before the last day of the fourth month after the end of each fiscal
  year of a mine, including the fiscal year during which the mine

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the weight and value of the minerals or processed

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} sold or transferred during the fiscal year of the

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} sold or transferred during the fiscal year of

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if exploration costs are claimed as a deduction

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in respect of depreciable assets,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the proceeds from the disposition during the

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the original cost of depreciable assets disposed

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents to accompany royalty return

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every mining royalty return must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} accompanied by the financial statements for the

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} signed by the operator of the mine and include a

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Royalty return --- election under

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the operator must deliver the initial mining

    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the operator must deliver a mining royalty return
    for the mine's subsequent fiscal years if there are any amounts that
    qualify for determining the values of D to H in subsection 69(4), or
    if the mine recommences production.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Royalty returns for certain joint
ventures

- **[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  mine is operated as a joint venture and each member of the joint
  venture takes its share of the output of the mine in kind and sells
  that share separately and independently from other members of the
  joint venture to purchasers who are not related to any of the members
  of the joint venture,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each member may deliver to the Chief a separate
    mining royalty return for the royalty payable under subsection 69(1)
    on the value of its share of the output of the mine, in lieu of
    including that information in a mining royalty return delivered
    under subsection 73(1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} each member, and any person related to that member,
    is liable to pay only those royalties attributable to that member's
    share of the output of the mine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint venture respecting single mine

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When, under subsection (1), more than one member of a
  joint venture delivers a mining royalty return to the Chief for a
  single mine,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each member of the joint venture is considered to
    be a separate operator for the purposes of these Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} each member must indicate on the mining royalty
    return the percentage of the output of the mine represented by that
    mining royalty return;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the value of the output on the mining royalty
    return for each member must be calculated in accordance with section
    69 using

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in respect of costs eligible for deductions under
      paragraphs 70(1)(a) to (f),

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a percentage of costs that have been jointly
        incurred, equal to the percentage of the output of the mine
        received by that member, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the costs that have been incurred by that
        member alone,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in determining a deduction for exploration costs
      under paragraph 70(1)(g), the exploration costs incurred by that
      member alone,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a depreciation allowance based on

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a percentage of the depreciable assets of the
        mine that are jointly held, equal to the percentage of the
        output of the mine received by that member, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the depreciable assets of the mine that are
        held by the member alone,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a development allowance based on

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a percentage of the costs referred to in
        subparagraphs 70(1)(i)(i) to (v) that have been jointly
        incurred, equal to the percentage of the output of the mine
        received by that member, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the costs referred to in subparagraphs
        70(1)(i)(i) to (v) that have been incurred by that member alone,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a mining reclamation trust contribution allowance
      equal to the amount contributed to a mining reclamation trust in
      respect of lands to which these Regulations apply by that member,
      and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a processing allowance based on

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a percentage of the processing assets of the
        mine that are jointly held equal to the percentage of the output
        of the mine received by that member, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the processing assets of the mine that are held
        by that member alone;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amounts in column 1 of the table to subsection
    69(1) must be adjusted by multiplying each amount by a percentage
    equal to the percentage of the output of the mine received by that
    member; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} each mining royalty return must be based on the
    same fiscal year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of assessment of royalties

- **[[75]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-75 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  six years after the end of a particular fiscal year of a mine, the
  Chief must send to the operator of the mine a notice of assessment for
  the amount of the royalty payable for that fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of reassessment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, during or after the period referred to in
  subsection (1), there are reasonable grounds to believe that the
  operator of a mine or any other person who delivers a mining royalty
  return has made a fraudulent or negligent misrepresentation in
  completing the mining royalty return or in supplying any other
  information under section 73 or 74, the Chief may send a notice of
  reassessment for the amount of the royalty payable for a fiscal year
  in respect of the mine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When royalties are considered payable

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Chief sends an operator a notice of assessment
  or reassessment for the amount of royalty payable for a fiscal year,
  the amount of royalty assessed or reassessed for the fiscal year is
  considered to be payable on the last day of the fourth month after the
  end of that fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Royalty returns when ownership of mine
  changes

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the ownership of a mine changes, the operator may
  file a separate mining royalty return for the portion of the fiscal
  year before the change of ownership and the portion of the fiscal year
  after the change of ownership, and each such portion is considered to
  be a fiscal year of less than 12 months for the purposes of subsection
  70(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping

- **[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  operator of a mine must keep at an office in Canada and make available
  to the Chief, to substantiate information required on mining royalty
  returns,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} records, books of account and other documents
    evidencing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the weight of all minerals extracted from the
      mine and of all minerals or processed minerals processed at the
      mine, whether or not they are produced from the mine,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the weight and value of all minerals or
      processed minerals produced from the mine, sold, transferred or
      removed from the mine by the mine's operator,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any amounts received from a processing plant
      and any other amounts received from the sale of minerals or
      processed minerals, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the costs, payments, allowances and other
      deductions referred to in section 70;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the financial statements of the mine and the
    operator;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a reconciliation between the documents referred to
    in paragraphs (a) and (b) and the mining royalty return;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the financial statements of an owner or the
    operator of the mine are audited by an external auditor,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the audited financial statements and the
      accompanying signed audit opinion of the external auditor, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any working papers and documentation prepared by
      the external auditor that are in the possession of an owner or the
      operator;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any documents filed by an owner or the operator
    with a stock exchange or securities commission;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any documents related to any internal audits of a
    company that is an owner or the operator; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any other documents that contain information
    necessary for ascertaining the amount of royalty payable under
    section 69.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-disclosure of confidential
  information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited to disclose information of a
  confidential nature acquired for the purposes of sections 69 to 77,
  except

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the extent necessary to determine the amount of
    royalties payable under section 69;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when required under a land claims agreement

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} under an agreement entered into by the Minister for
    the purpose of the administration of section 69 with the government
    of a country, province or state, or with an Aboriginal organization
    owning mineral rights, under which the officers of that government
    or Aboriginal organization are provided with the information and the
    Chief is provided with information from the government or Aboriginal
    organization.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition on removal of minerals

- **[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), it is
  prohibited to remove minerals or processed minerals produced from a
  mine, other than for the purposes of assay and testing to determine
  the existence, location, extent, quality or economic potential of a
  mineral deposit in the lands constituting the mining property, until
  the weight and any other information necessary to establish the value
  of those minerals or processed minerals has been ascertained and
  entered in the books of account referred to in subsection 76(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition on removal of precious stones

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Precious stones must not be removed from a mine ---
  other than in a bulk sample or in a concentrate for the purposes of
  establishing the grade and the value of the stones in a mineral
  deposit --- or cut, polished, sold or transferred, until they have
  been valued by a mining royalty valuer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Facilities required for valuation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The operator of a mine must provide, in Nunavut, any
  facilities and equipment, other than computer equipment, necessary for
  a mining royalty valuer to value any precious stones produced from the
  mine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Facilities considered part of mine

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, facilities
  referred to in subsection (3) are considered to be part of the mine
  and any transfer of the precious stones from one part of the mine to
  another is not considered to be a removal of the stones from the mine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cleaning of precious stones

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Precious stones must not be presented to the mining
  royalty valuer until the operator of the mine has cleaned the stones
  so as to remove all substances from the stones that are not part of
  them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presentation of precious stones to
  royalty valuer

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} As soon as any precious stones have been processed
  into a saleable form, they must be presented to a mining royalty
  valuer for valuation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Separate valuation of precious stones

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} An operator who produces precious stones and sells or
  transfers them to persons who are related to the operator must present
  to a mining royalty valuer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all stones that are to be sold or transferred to a
    person related to the operator, for separate valuation before their
    sale or transfer; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all stones that are to be cut or polished by the
    operator or any related party, for separate valuation before their
    being cut or polished.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presentation of diamonds to royalty
  valuer

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (6) and (7), unless
  otherwise agreed on by the operator and the mining royalty valuer, an
  operator must present to the mining royalty valuer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} diamonds with a weight of 10.8 carats or more,
    separately, together with the weight of each diamond;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} diamonds with a weight from 2.8 carats to 10.79
    carats, in lots separated according to weight in carats, together
    with the number of diamonds per lot;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} diamonds with a weight from 0.66 carats to 2.79
    carats, in lots separated according to weight in grainers, from
    which randomly selected samples, accurately representing the
    composition of each lot, have been separated; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} diamonds with a weight of less than 0.66 carats, in
    lots separated according to industry standard DTC sieve sizes, from
    which randomly selected samples, accurately representing the
    composition of each lot, have been separated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Estimate of market value of diamonds

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} Before diamonds are presented to the mining royalty
  valuer under subsection (8), the operator must provide an estimate of
  the market value of each diamond or lot, as the case may be, to the
  Chief.

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-813882 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recorded claim and lease subject to
public works

**[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78 .sectionLabel}** Every recorded claim,
whether leased or not, is subject to the right of the Crown and of the
Commissioner of Nunavut to construct and maintain roads or other public
works on or over the lands covered by the claim.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension --- death or incompetency of
claim holder

**[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** If the holder of a
recorded claim for which no lease has been issued dies or is declared by
a court of competent jurisdiction to be incapable of managing their
affairs and notice of the death or declaration is filed with the Mining
Recorder within 180 days after the date of the death or declaration, and
if the recording of the claim was not cancelled before the filing of the
notice, the running of a time period within which anything is required
to be done by a claim holder with respect to that claim under these
Regulations is suspended until the anniversary date of the recording of
the claim that is at least 12 months after the day on which the notice
was filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension on account of strike

**[[80]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80 .sectionLabel}** If, as a result of a
[[strike]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
[Federal Public Sector Labour Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-33.3), a holder
of a recorded claim or lease is unable, through no fault on their part,
to do a thing within the time required by these Regulations, the
deadline for doing that thing is extended for a period ending 15 days
after the last day of the strike.

## [Review by the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-813914 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for ministerial review

- **[[84]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-84 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person with a legal or beneficial interest in the subject matter of a
  decision made or an action taken or omitted to be taken under these
  Regulations may request that the Minister review any issue the person
  has with respect to the decision, action or omission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request for review must be made in writing within
  30 days after the day on which the decision is made or the action is
  taken or, in the case of an omission to take action, within 30 days
  after the day on which action should have been taken and must specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the requester and their contact
    information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the issue the requester wishes the Minister to
    review;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the decision, act or omission
    took place; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the corrective relief requested.

  original claim that, but for the cancellation of its recording under
  paragraph (5)(b), would have followed the transitional period, the
  Mining Recorder must amend the date of the recording of the converted
  claim to that date.

      the work was done, setting out for each one its identification
      number and the name associated with it, if any;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number of each National Topographic System of
      Canada 1:50 000 map sheet that shows the lands covered by the
      recorded claim;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the four values representing either

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the maximum and minimum latitude and longitude
        of the lands covered by the recorded claim, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the maximum and minimum Northings and Eastings
        UTM coordinates of the lands covered by the recorded claim;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the name of the individual who prepared and
      signed the report;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} for each period that work was done in the field,
      the first date and last date that work in the field was done;

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the date of the report;

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a report submitted in paper format in more
      than one volume, the volume number and total number of volumes in
      the report; and

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} for a report submitted on more than one
      electronic medium, the number of media submitted and the
      identifiers for each.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where information is provided

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in subsection (1) must
    appear

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the parts of a report submitted in paper
      format, on the front cover of each volume of the report; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the parts of a report submitted in electronic
      format,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on a label affixed to, or on a sheet of paper
        inserted into, each container submitted, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in a file named "Readme -
        [Lisez-moi]{.otherLang lang="fr"}" stored on each electronic
        medium submitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Details of contents

  **4** A report must include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a table of contents;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a list of the electronic media submitted and their
    identifiers;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a list of the tables, charts, diagrams, maps,
    sections and images presented in the report and the location of each
    in the report;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a list of the recorded claims in respect of which
    the work was done, setting out for each one

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its identification number and recording date and
      the name, if any, associated with it,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the number of units included in it,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the area covered by it in hectares,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the number of each National Topographic System
      of Canada 1:50 000 map sheet that shows its location, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the name of the claim holder;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a bibliography setting out the sources and
    references used in the report;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an alphabetized list of definitions for all
    abbreviations used in the report;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a brief description of the regional geology of the
    area in which the work was done;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the purpose of the work being
    reported on;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the previous work that was done on the
    lands covered by the recorded claim, and on the lands adjacent to
    those lands, that is relevant to the work being reported on;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a summary and a detailed description of the work
    being reported on;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} if sampling was performed, a description of the
    sampling methodology and a statement of the number of samples
    analyzed;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} identification of quality control samples submitted
    to laboratories, along with the elemental concentration of each
    sample;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} a description of the methods of preparation,
    processing and analysis applied to samples and the calculations used
    to arrive at the results of geochemical analyses;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} specification of the cutoff grades used for the
    results of geochemical analyses shown on maps and sections;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} a description of any geophysical or remote sensing
    anomalies and any anomalous results of geochemical analysis, with an
    explanation of the criteria used to define them;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} interpretation of the data collected and of
    observations made during the work;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} conclusions and recommendations resulting from the
    interpretation of the data and of observations made during the work;

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} if geochemical analyses were performed, copies of
    all resulting analytical certificates and laboratory reports;

  - [(s)]{.lawlabel} an electronic copy of the raw data that was
    acquired in electronic format during the work;

  - [(t)]{.lawlabel} if environmental baseline studies were conducted,
    an appendix that sets out the results of the studies; and

  - [(u)]{.lawlabel} an appendix containing a copy of every report made
    by a contractor, together with all maps and sections produced and
    data collected in connection with the report made by the contractor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maps or sections

  **5** A report must include the following maps or sections:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a regional geological map that is marked to
    indicate the area in which the work was done and that is published
    by the Geological Survey of Canada, the Canada-Nunavut Geoscience
    Office, the Northwest Territories Geoscience Office or other source
    of geoscientific publications;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a map that shows the area in which the work was
    done, the regional infrastructure (including roads, airfields, ports
    and mine sites) and, if the scale of the map permits, communities
    and boundaries of Nunavut;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one or more maps on which the following elements
    are indicated:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the boundaries of the recorded claim where the
      work was done and,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the type of work being reported on,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the location of the work with respect to the
        boundaries of the recorded claim, and

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the identification number of each recorded
        claim and the name, if any, associated with it,

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2020-209, s. 40\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the location and type of the previous work that
      was done on the lands covered by the claim, and on the lands
      adjacent to those lands, that is related to the work being
      reported on,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} all surface and underground workings that are
      not part of the work being reported on,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the boundaries of each prospecting permit zone
      and each recorded claim, or leased claim, that is adjacent to the
      boundaries of the recorded claim on which the work was done,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the infrastructure, hydrography and natural
      landmarks within the boundaries referred to in subparagraphs (i)
      and (iv);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} one or more maps or sections with a symbol
    indicating every site at which samples or data were collected, and a
    sample identifier for each sample; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} one or more maps or sections indicating any
    geochemical or geophysical anomalies.

#### [Geographic Location and Coordinates]{.HTitleText2} {#geographic-location-and-coordinates .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sample sites --- coordinate systems

  - **6** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All coordinates used in a report to locate
    the sites where samples or data were collected --- including those
    in sections, maps, tables and lists --- must be geographic
    coordinates or UTM coordinates.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coordinate system specified on map

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} The geodetic reference system --- and, for UTM
    coordinates, the zone number --- must be indicated for each map,
    section and table in which that coordinate system is used.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Geodetic coordinates

    [(3)]{.lawlabel} The geodetic reference system must be one of the
    following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} North American Datum 1927 (NAD 27);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} North American Datum 1983 (NAD 83);

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Canadian Spatial Reference System (NAD 83
      (CSRS));

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} World Geodetic System 1984 (WGS 84).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Method for determination of location

  **7** For each location where work was done, a report must specify how
  the location was determined for each type of work, using one or more
  of the following methods:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a satellite positioning system, indicating the
    geodetic reference system used and, if UTM coordinates are used, the
    zone number;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a map, indicating the scale, the geodetic reference
    system used and, if UTM coordinates are used, the zone number;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} surveying, together with a description of the
    surveying equipment and method used.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elevation coordinates

  **8** If elevation coordinates are used with respect to the work,
  those coordinates and their zero reference level must be specified in
  the report.

#### [Maps, Sections and Samples]{.HTitleText2} {#maps-sections-and-samples .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coordinates for geographic locations

  - **9** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each map and section submitted with a report

    specification of those coordinates that is sufficient to allow the
    location of any point on the map.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements respecting grid lines

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} If grid lines were used to perform the work, at
    least one map of each grid must be submitted showing the name of the
    grid, its geographic location and the coordinates established to
    identify each of the grid lines.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for maps and sections

    [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each map and section must

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be legible;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have its scale set out on it using a bar scale in
      metric units;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} have a geographic north indicator;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} have a legend; and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} indicate the units of measure used on it.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elevation coordinates

    [(4)]{.lawlabel} Each map and section must show the relevant
    elevation coordinates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Location of data collection sites

  **10** Every table or list that contains data with respect to a site
  (including sample data) must set out the location of the data
  collection site specified in geographic or UTM coordinates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cross-referencing of sample identifiers

  - **11** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an identifier used in a report to
    identify a sample, such as in an analytical certificate, is not the
    same as the corresponding sample identifier shown on the sample
    location maps or sections required under paragraph 5(d), a table
    that makes a cross-reference between the two identifiers must be
    provided.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sample identifiers

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} Data about samples must set out one of the
    following identifiers for each sample:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sample identifier shown on a map or section
      as required under paragraph 5(d);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the identifier cross-referenced, in accordance
      with subsection (1), to the sample identifier mentioned in
      paragraph (a).

#### [Information About Types of Work]{.HTitleText2} {#information-about-types-of-work .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excavation

  **12** Reporting on excavation work must include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} maps and sections showing the location, dimensions
    and orientation of each of the workings, including stripped areas,
    trenches, open pits, shafts, adits, drifts and ramps;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maps or sections showing any anomalous results of
    geochemical analyses;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the excavation methods and the equipment used,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the samples or groups of samples collected from
      each of the workings,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the geological observations and any
      geotechnical observations,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the results of any geophysical surveys, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the results of geoscientific investigations; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any log made respecting excavation of
    overburden or extraction of bedrock.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Drilling

  **13** Reporting on drilling work must include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} maps showing the locations of drill hole collars
    and traces;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sections showing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the overburden thickness,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the geological units intersected,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the locations where samples were taken, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} all anomalous results of geochemical analyses;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the drilling methods and the equipment used,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the samples and their down-hole intervals,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the geological observations and any
      geotechnical observations,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the results of any geophysical surveys, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the results of geoscientific investigations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} drill logs setting out for each drill hole

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the hole number,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the hole or core diameter,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date of drilling, the date logged, the
      geographic or UTM coordinates of the collar, initial azimuth and
      inclination, final hole length and the results of any down-hole
      tests, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the overburden thickness; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the location where the cores and chip samples
    obtained from the drilling are stored at the time the report is
    submitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Geological mapping

  **14** Reporting on geological mapping work must include the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a table setting out the geological formations of
    the area mapped, if the information on those formations is published
    by the Geological Survey of Canada, the Canada-Nunavut Geoscience
    Office, the Northwest Territories Geoscience Office or other source
    of geoscientific publications;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maps or sections showing geological units,
    structural data, areas of mineralization, locations of rock outcrops
    and mineralized erratic blocks, anomalous results of geochemical
    analyses, locations of any trenches, stripped areas, drill holes and
    other workings referred to in the report, and, if applicable,
    indicators of the direction of ice movement;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the features of geological
    interest, such as overburden, rock types, structures, veins, areas
    of mineralization, mineralized erratic blocks, altered zones,
    anomalous results of geochemical analyses, and, if applicable,
    indicators of the direction of ice movement; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the results of geoscientific investigations,
    including the results of any petrographic studies, and any
    interpretation that has been made of the glacial history based on
    the geological mapping work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sampling and geochemistry

  **15** Reporting on sampling and geochemical analysis must include the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} complete results of geochemical analyses;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a geological description of the samples or group of
    samples;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the sampling methodologies,
    including any pattern of sample collection;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the dates the samples or group of samples were
    collected and the dates that any preparation or processing of the
    samples was done in the field or in a laboratory;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of any preparation or processing of
    samples or group of samples that was done in the field or in a
    laboratory;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the methods of geochemical
    analysis used on each sample or group of samples, including a list
    of the elements and compounds for which the analysis was done and
    the limits for their detection;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of any statistical methods used and
    the results obtained;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the results of the geochemistry survey, represented
    on maps or sections; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if any geoscientific investigation of surficial
    deposits was performed, an interpretation of the sources from which
    the indicator minerals came, a description of the methods used to
    determine the direction of movement of ice, water or other transport
    medium for those minerals and any interpretation of the glacial
    history revealed by the investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Geophysics or remote sensing work

  - **16** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Reporting on geophysical analysis or remote
    sensing work must be accompanied by the following information and
    documents:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the type of survey performed;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the dates that the survey started and ended;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the work was done using a grid, the distance
      between the lines, the azimuth and the length of the lines
      surveyed and the total distance surveyed;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2020-209, s. 44\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the survey methods, including

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the characteristics of the equipment used;

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the characteristics of the instruments and
        sensors used, including their precision,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the positioning system used to record the
        location of data collection points, and

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a description of any methods used to make
        corrections to the data;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the methods of analysis applied
      to the data;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a database or spreadsheet containing the
      geographic coordinates of each ground data collection site, the
      raw measurements, the measurements used to correct the raw
      measurements, the corrected measurements and calculated results
      used for interpretations; and

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the results of the geophysical or remote sensing
      survey, represented on maps, sections, pseudo-sections or
      profiles.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Down-hole geophysical survey

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a down-hole geophysical survey was performed,
    the section required under paragraph (1)(h) must show

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the hole number;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date the hole was drilled;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the location of the drill hole collar and trace;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the geographic or UTM collar coordinates;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the initial azimuth and inclination of the hole;
      and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the length of the hole.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electromagnetic survey

    [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an electromagnetic survey was performed, the map
    required under paragraph (1)(h) must show the projection of the
    surveyed drill hole to the surface and the location and
    configuration of the transmitter and receiver.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Geophysical survey grids

    [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a ground-based, waterborne or airborne
    geophysical survey was performed, it must be presented in the report
    accompanied by a map showing the surveyed lines.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Underground geophysical survey

    [(5)]{.lawlabel} If an underground geophysical survey was performed,
    it must be presented in the report accompanied by a map showing the
    location of the geophysical survey, the surveyed lines and the
    location, dimensions and orientation of the underground workings.

## [SCHEDULE 3]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsection 1(1) and paragraph 8(1)(c))]{.OriginatingRef}[Nunavut Lands Division]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-1283846 .scheduleLabel}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}North American Datum of 1927

  **1** All latitudes and longitudes used in this Schedule refer to the
  North American Datum of 1927 (NAD27).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}South of latitude 70°

  - **2** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Grid areas, the whole or greater part of
    which lies south of latitude 70°, are bounded on the east and west
    sides by successive meridians of longitude of the series 50°00′00″,
    50°15′00″, 50°30′00″, which series may be extended as required, and
    on the north and south sides by geodesics joining the points of
    intersection of the east and west boundaries with successive
    parallels of latitude of the series 40°00′00″, 40°10′00″, 40°20′00″,
    which series may be extended as required.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}North of latitude 70°

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} Grid areas, the whole of which lies north of
    latitude 70°, are bounded on the east and west sides by successive
    meridians of longitude of the series 50°00′00″, 50°30′00″,
    51°00′00″, which series may be extended as required, and on the
    north and south sides by geodesics joining the points of
    intersection of the east and west boundaries with successive
    parallels of latitude of the series 70°00′00″, 70°10′00″, 70°20′00″,
    which series may be extended as required.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}South boundary of grid area north of
    latitude 70°

    [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), each grid area the
    northeast corner of which has a latitude of 70°10′00′′ is bounded on
    its south side by the north boundaries of the two grid areas to its
    immediate south.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Latitude and longitude

    [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every grid area is referred to by the latitude and
    longitude of the northeast corner of that grid area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sections

  - **3** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every grid area is divided into sections.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meridians

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each section is bounded on the east and west sides
    by meridians spaced

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a section within a grid area, the
      whole or greater part of which lies south of latitude 60° or
      between latitudes 70° and 75°, at intervals of one-tenth of the
      interval between the east and west boundaries of the grid area;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a section within a grid area, the
      whole or greater part of which lies between latitudes 60° and 68°
      or between latitudes 75° and 78°, at intervals of one-eighth of
      the interval between the east and west boundaries of the grid
      area; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a section within a grid area, the
      whole or greater part of which lies between latitudes 68° and 70°
      or between latitudes 78° and 85°, at intervals of one-sixth of the
      interval between the east and west boundaries of the grid area.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Section boundaries

    [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each section is bounded on the north and south
    sides by the geodesics joining the points of intersection of the
    east and west boundaries of the grid area and spaced at intervals of
    one-tenth of the length of those boundaries.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}South boundary --- southernmost
    sections

    [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (3), each section in the
    southernmost row of sections in each grid the northeast corner of
    which has a latitude of 70°10′00′′ is bounded on its south side by
    the south boundary of the grid area.

    corresponds

      paragraph (2)(a)

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Units

  - **4** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each section is divided into units.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}East and west boundaries

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every unit is bounded on the east and west sides by
    meridians spaced at intervals of one-quarter of the interval between
    the east and west boundaries of the section.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}North and south boundaries

    [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each unit is bounded on the north and south sides
    by geodesics joining the points of intersection of the east and west
    boundaries of the grid area and spaced at intervals of one-fortieth
    of the length of those boundaries.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}South boundary --- southernmost units

    [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (3), each unit in the
    southernmost row of units within a section referred to in subsection
    3(4) is bounded on its south side by the south boundary of the
    section.
