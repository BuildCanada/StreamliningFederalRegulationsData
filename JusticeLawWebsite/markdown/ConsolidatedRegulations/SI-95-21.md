## Income Tax Remission Order (Canada Pension Plan) {#income-tax-remission-order-canada-pension-plan .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/95-21
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1995-02-22

Income Tax Remission Order (Canada Pension Plan)

P.C. 1995-201  1995-02-07
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that the
collection of the tax is unjust, on the recommendation of the Minister
of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-95-21_e_hq_6602){#fn_SI-95-21_e_hq_6602-ID0EHBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to remit the net total of the taxes payable under Parts I and I.1 of the
[Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) for the 1987 to 1995 taxation years
that, without regard to sections 122.2 and 122.4 to 122.64 thereof as
they read at any time, would not be payable by a taxpayer if the part of
any amount received by the taxpayer after 1987 and before 1996 by reason
of section 63.1 of the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) that was
payable for a month in a year preceding the year in which it was
received had been received in that preceding year, and all relevant
penalties and interest.
