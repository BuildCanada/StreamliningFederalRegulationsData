## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-892998 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Baie Verte Mines Inc. Regulations,
1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-262).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-893000 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Baie Verte Mines Inc.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Baie Verte Mines Inc.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a company
    incorporated with that name under the laws of the Province of
    Ontario and having its principal place of business at Baie Verte,
    Newfoundland; ([Baie Verte Mines Inc.]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Regional
    Industrial Expansion; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any lender, or its
    assignee, other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Government of Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the government of any province of Canada,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an agency of any government referred to in
      paragraph (a) or (b) or any company that is effectively controlled
      by any such government or any agency thereof, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any municipal corporation. ([prêteur
      privé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-893009 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 5 to 9,
the Minister may provide insurance for the full amount of any loss up to
one million dollars in the aggregate that may result in respect of loans
made by a private lender to Baie Verte Mines Inc.

## [Application for Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-893011 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application for
insurance referred to in section 3 shall be made to the Minister and
shall contain such information and documents relating to the application
as the Minister may require.

## [Terms and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-893013 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No loans made by a private
lender to Baie Verte Mines Inc. may be insured under these Regulations
unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the loan is for revolving credit advances for working
  capital purposes not exceeding four million dollars; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the loan is secured by the inventory or accounts
  receivable or both the inventory and accounts receivable of Baie Verte
  Mines Inc.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The insurance provided by
the Minister to a private lender shall cease on March 1, 1987.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The amount of insurance
provided by the Minister to a private lender may be reduced at the
request of the private lender.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A private lender shall pay
to the Minister by semi-annual payments in advance a premium of one per
cent of the amount of insurance in force.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Where a private lender has
demanded repayment of a loan insured under these Regulations the amount
payable by the Minister shall not exceed the lesser of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of insurance in force on the date of
  demand; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 100 per cent of the loss incurred by the private
  lender.
