## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1012637 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this permit.

[[imported from Israel or another CIFTA beneficiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[imported from Israel or another CIFTA
    beneficiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by the
    [Regulations Defining Certain Expressions for the Purposes of the
    Customs Tariff](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-62). ([importé d'Israël ou
    d'un autre bénéficiaire de l'ALÉCI]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[roses]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[roses]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means cut roses and rose buds, fresh,
    suitable for bouquets or ornamental purposes that are classified
    under tariff item No. 0603.10.11 or under heading 98.04 or 98.26 in
    the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the
    [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011). ([roses]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-81, s. 1
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1012643 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any resident of Canada may,
under the authority of this permit, import from Israel or another CIFTA
beneficiary for their personal use or the use of their household, roses
not exceeding two dozen in respect of each importation.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Where roses imported under
the authority of this Permit are required to be reported in the
prescribed form under the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), that form
shall contain the statement "Imported under the authority of [General
Import Permit No. 6 --- Roses for Personal
Use](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-80)" or "[Importé en vertu de la [Licence
générale d'importation n^o^ 6 --- Roses pour usage
personnel](/eng/regulations/DORS-97-80)]{.otherLang lang="fr"}".
