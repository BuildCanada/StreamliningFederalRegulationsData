## [Non-application --- Business or Commercial Purposes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1456107 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 347.01(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), section 347
of that Act does not apply in respect of an agreement or arrangement if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the borrower is not a natural person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the borrowing is for a business or commercial
  purpose; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} either

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the credit advanced is more than
    \$10,000 but less than or equal to \$500,000 and the annual
    percentage rate of interest --- calculated in accordance with
    generally accepted actuarial practices and principles --- does not
    exceed 48% on the credit advanced, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the credit advanced is more than
    \$500,000.

## [Non-Application --- Pawnbroking Loans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1456115 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 347.01(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), section 347
of that Act does not apply in respect of an agreement or arrangement if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person who receives interest carries on a
  business related to pawnbroking;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the credit is advanced in exchange for the pawning of
  tangible personal property or corporeal movable property, other than a
  vehicle;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the event of the borrower's default, the only
  recourse of the person referred to in paragraph (a) is the seizure of
  the pawned property; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the credit advanced is less than
  \$1,000 and the annual percentage rate of interest --- calculated in
  accordance with generally accepted actuarial practices and principles
  --- does not exceed 48% on the credit advanced.

## [Limit --- Payday Loans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1456122 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit on total cost of borrowing

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 347.1(2)(a.1) of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the limit on the total cost of borrowing under
  a payday loan agreement is 14% of the amount of money advanced to the
  borrower under the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In determining whether a payday loan agreement
  complies with the limit fixed under subsection (1), the total cost of
  borrowing does not include a fee, fine, penalty or other charge that
  is specifically authorized under the applicable provincial law and
  imposed on the borrower

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for defaulting in any payment; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for providing a dishonoured cheque or other
    dishonoured instrument, if the amount of the fee, fine, penalty or
    other charge is \$20 or less.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[applicable provincial
  law]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (2), [[applicable provincial
  law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the legislative measures referred to
  in subsection 347.1(3) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) that
  apply in the province in which the payday loan agreement is entered
  into.
