## Order Transferring Certain Portions of the Department of Foreign Affairs to the Department of International Trade {#order-transferring-certain-portions-of-the-department-of-foreign-affairs-to-the-department-of-international-trade .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2003-209
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2003-12-31

Order Transferring Certain Portions of the Department of Foreign Affairs
to the Department of International Trade

P.C. 2003-2049  2003-12-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} transfers to the Department of International Trade,
  effective December 12, 2003, the control and supervision of the
  following portions of the public service in the Department of Foreign
  Affairs and International Trade:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the International Business Development Branch,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Trade, Economic, and Environmental Policy
    Branch, including the Chief Air Negotiator, except for those
    portions relating to environmental policy, summitry, the Asia
    Pacific Economic Cooperation, Organization for Economic Cooperation
    and Development and the International Assistance Envelope,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} those portions of the Communications Bureau and
    the Executive Service Bureau that support the international trade
    policy and international business development including the Trade
    Communications Division,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} those portions of the International Academic
    Relations Division relating to international business development,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} those portions of the Arts and Cultural Industries
    Promotion Division relating to international business development,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} those portions of the Policy Planning Secretariat
    relating to trade policy and international business development,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} those portions of the geographic branches of the
    Department that relate exclusively to trade policy and international
    business development,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} those portions of Canadian missions abroad that
    relate exclusively to trade policy and international business
    development, and

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the Trade Law Division; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} transfers from the Minister of Foreign Affairs, to
  the Minister for International Trade to be styled Minister of
  International Trade, effective December 12, 2003, the powers, duties
  and functions of the Minister of Foreign Affairs under the [Department
  of Foreign Affairs and International Trade Act](/eng/acts/E-22)
  related to international trade and commerce.
