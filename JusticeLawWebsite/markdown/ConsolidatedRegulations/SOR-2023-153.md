## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1412259 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).  ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[competent authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[competent authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Secretaría de
    Estado en los Despachos de Industria y Comercio (Secretariat of
    State of Industry and Commerce), or a successor that Honduras
    provides written notice of to Canada. ([autorité
    compétente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[customs administration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[customs administration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the authority
    that is responsible under the law of Canada for the administration
    of customs laws and regulations. ([administration
    douanière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods for which preferential
    tariff treatment under CHFTA is
    claimed. ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a good that is used in the
    production of another good and includes a part or ingredient of a
    good.  ([matière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification letter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification letter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a letter that
    requests information regarding the origin of goods that are the
    subject of a verification of origin. ([lettre de
    vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification questionnaire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification questionnaire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    questionnaire that requests information regarding the origin of
    goods that are the subject of a verification of
    origin. ([questionnaire de vérification]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[verification visit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification visit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the entry into any
    premises for the purpose of conducting a verification of origin of
    goods under paragraph 42.1(1)(a) of the Act. ([visite de
    vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Methods of Conducting a Verification of Origin]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1412270 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Methods in addition to verification visit

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In addition to a
verification visit, a verification of origin of goods may be conducted
by reviewing any of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a verification questionnaire completed by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the exporter or producer of the goods, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a producer or supplier of a material;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written response to a verification letter received
  from a person referred to in paragraph (a); or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other information received from a person referred
  to in paragraph (a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Verification letter and verification
questionnaire

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A verification letter or
verification questionnaire must specify

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the customs administration as well as
  the name and title of the officer who is sending the letter or
  questionnaire;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the goods or materials that are the
  subject of the verification of origin; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the period within which the letter must be answered
  or the questionnaire must be completed and returned, which must be a
  minimum of 30 days beginning on the day on which the letter or
  questionnaire is received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of time

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A person who receives a
verification letter or verification questionnaire may, on a single
occasion and within the time specified in the letter or questionnaire,
make a written request to the customs administration for an extension of
that time. The extension granted must not exceed 30 days unless the
person is an exporter or producer of the goods that are the subject of
the verification of origin and one of the following circumstances
applies:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} owing to a bankruptcy of the exporter or producer or
  other financial distress situation or business reorganization, the
  exporter or producer or a related person has lost control of the
  records containing the information that substantiates the origin of
  the goods; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for any other reason, that the exporter or producer
  could not reasonably have been expected to foresee and that results in
  a partial or complete loss of the records of the exporter or producer,
  including loss due to fire, flooding or other natural cause.

## [Verification Visit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1412287 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed premises

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The premises in Honduras of
the following persons are prescribed premises for the purposes of a
verification visit:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the exporter or producer of the goods; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a producer or supplier of a material.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  officer may conduct a verification visit only if:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an officer sends

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a written notice of the intention to conduct the
      visit to the person whose premises are to be the subject of the
      visit,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a written notice of the intention to conduct the
      visit to the competent authority, at least five business days
      before the notice is sent to the person referred to in
      subparagraph (i), and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if requested by Honduras, a written notice to
      the Embassy of Honduras in Canada of the intention to conduct the
      visit; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person whose premises is to be the subject of
    the visit consents to it in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The written notice of the verification visit must
  specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the customs administration on whose
    behalf the notice is sent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and title of the officer who is to conduct
    the visit;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person whose premises are to be the
    subject of the visit;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date and place of the visit;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the object and scope of the visit, including a
    description of the goods or materials that are the subject of the
    verification of origin; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the legal authority for the visit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Postponement

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who receives a notice referred to in subparagraph 6(1)(a)(i) may, on a
  single occasion and within 15 days after the day on which the notice
  is received, request a postponement of the verification visit by
  sending a written request for postponement to the officer who sent the
  notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The postponement referred to in subsection (1) may be
  for a period not exceeding 60 days beginning on the day on which the
  notice is received or for any longer period that is agreed to by the
  customs administration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Postponement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The verification visit is postponed if, within 15
  days after the day on which the competent authority receives the
  notice referred to in subparagraph 6(1)(a)(ii), Honduras sends a
  written request for the postponement to the officer who sent the
  notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum period

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The postponement referred to in subsection (3) may be
  for a period not exceeding 60 days beginning on the day on which the
  notice is received or for any longer period that is agreed to by
  Honduras and the customs administration.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Observers

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who receives a notice referred to in subparagraph 6(1)(a)(i) may
  designate two observers to be present during the verification visit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Observers' role

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The observers' participation is limited to
  observation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identification of observers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who designates observers must identify them
  to the officer who is conducting the verification visit.

## [Denial or Withdrawal of Preferential Tariff Treatment Under CHFTA]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1412324 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements --- exporter and producer

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 42.1(2) of the Act, the exporter and the
  producer of goods are, in respect of a verification of origin under
  paragraph 42.1(1)(a) of the Act, required to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain in Honduras for five years after the date
    on which the Certificate of Origin was signed, or for a longer
    period specified by Honduras, in accordance with Article 5.6 of the
    CHFTA, records in relation to the origin of the goods;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the officer who is conducting the
    verification of origin with access to the records; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, reply to the verification letter or
    complete and return the verification questionnaire to the officer
    within the period specified under paragraph 3(c) or, in the case of
    an extension, under section 4.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent to verification visit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 42.1(2) of the Act,
  consent to the verification visit must be provided in writing by the
  exporter or producer within 30 days after the day on which the notice
  referred to in subparagraph 6(1)(a)(i) is received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of intention to re-determine
origin

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  officer must, with any statement that they provide to an exporter or
  producer under subsection 42.2(1) of the Act indicating that goods
  that are the subject of a verification of origin are not eligible for
  preferential tariff treatment under CHFTA, attach a written notice of
  the intention to re-determine the origin of those goods under
  subsection 59(1) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The written notice must specify the date as of which
  preferential tariff treatment under CHFTA may be denied or withdrawn
  in respect of the goods that are the subject of the verification of
  origin and the period during which the exporter or producer of the
  goods may provide written comments or additional information regarding
  the origin of those goods.

## [Means of Transmission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1412338 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confirmation of receipt

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A verification letter, a
verification questionnaire and the notices referred to in subparagraph
6(1)(a)(i) and section 10 must be sent by a means that can produce
confirmation of receipt.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1412341 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}October 1, 2014

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
deemed to have come into force on October 1, 2014.
