## Crown Waiver Order (Northwest Territories) {#crown-waiver-order-northwest-territories .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/90-11
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 1990-02-14

Crown Waiver Order (Northwest Territories)

P.C. 1990-122  1990-01-25
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 13(a) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), is
pleased hereby to order that all that portion of the lands described in
the schedule hereto lying within 30.48 metres (100′) of the Ordinary
High Water Mark of the Beaufort Sea in the Northwest Territories be
included in the sale of the said lands.
:::

::::: section

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-642393 .scheduleLabel}
:::

The whole of Lot numbered 11 in Block numbered 12B in the Hamlet of
Tuktoyaktuk in the Northwest Territories, as said lot is shown on a plan
of survey of record number 56088 in the Canada Lands Surveys Records at
Ottawa, a copy of which is filed in the Land Titles Office for the
Northwest Territories Land Registration District at Yellowknife under
number 607.
