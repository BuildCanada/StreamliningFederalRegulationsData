## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785055 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [National Housing
    Act](/eng/acts/N-11). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[housing loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[housing loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 7 of the Act. ([prêt à l'habitation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Designation of Approved Lenders]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785060 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** To be designated as an
approved lender for the purposes of Part I of the Act, a person must
meet the criteria set out in paragraph 3(1)(a) or (b) and, as
applicable, subsections 3(2) and (3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General criteria

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  person must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a corporation whose articles do not restrict its
    powers to lend in the jurisdictions in which it operates and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a financially sound institution with at least
      \$3,000,000 of unencumbered paid-up capital that is incorporated
      by or under an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a
      province,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a federal financial institution or an authorized
      foreign bank within the meaning of section 2 of the [Bank
      Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01),

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a trust, loan or insurance corporation that is
      incorporated and regulated by or under an Act of the legislature
      of a province,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a cooperative credit society that is
      incorporated and regulated by or under an Act of the legislature
      of a province,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a federal or provincial department, agency or
      Crown corporation, or

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} any other entity as long as the housing loans
      that it insures with the Corporation are guaranteed by Her Majesty
      in right of Canada or a province; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a federally or provincially regulated pension fund,
    or its subsidiary, that was designated, before the coming into force
    of these Regulations, as an approved lender under section 5 of the
    Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria for underwriting

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} To underwrite housing loans, the person must, in
  addition to meeting the criteria set out in paragraph (1)(a) or (b),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have at least three years' experience underwriting
    residential mortgage or hypothecary loans in Canada and the
    capability and resources to underwrite housing loans and make loan
    commitments;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be a subsidiary of a parent corporation that is an
    approved lender for the purposes of Part I of the Act and that meets
    the criteria set out in paragraph (a), if the parent corporation
    undertakes to fulfil the task of underwriting housing loans in
    Canada for the subsidiary and to be accountable to the Corporation
    for the subsidiary's performance in relation to those loans; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} have paid-up capital of at least \$5,000,000 or be
    an entity referred to in subparagraph (1)(a)(v) or (vi) and, in
    either case, employ at least two mortgage officers who each have a
    minimum of ten years' residential mortgage or hypothecary
    underwriting experience and who are responsible for underwriting the
    person's housing loans in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria for administering

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} To administer housing loans, the person must, in
  addition to meeting the criteria set out in paragraph (1)(a) or (b),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have at least three years' experience administering
    residential mortgage or hypothecary loans in Canada and the
    capability and resources to administer housing loans and meet all
    insurance conditions;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be a subsidiary of a parent corporation that is an
    approved lender for the purposes of Part I of the Act and that meets
    the criteria set out in paragraph (a), if the parent corporation
    undertakes to fulfil the task of administering housing loans in
    Canada for the subsidiary and to be accountable to the Corporation
    for the subsidiary's performance in relation to those loans; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} have paid-up capital of at least \$5,000,000 or be
    an entity referred to in subparagraph (1)(a)(v) or (vi) and, in
    either case, employ at least two mortgage officers who each have a
    minimum of ten years' residential mortgage or hypothecary
    administration experience and who are responsible for administering
    the person's housing loans in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- designated cooperative
  credit societies

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A cooperative credit society referred to in
  subparagraph (1)(a)(iv) need not meet the criteria set out in
  subsection (2) to underwrite housing loans or in subsection (3) to
  administer them if, before the coming into force of these Regulations,
  it was designated as an approved lender under section 5 of the Act.

## [Conditions on the Corporation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785086 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction --- substitution

- **[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation must not replace an insured loan with another housing loan
  under the same insurance coverage unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} both loans are made to the same borrower and the
    purpose of the new loan is to discharge the outstanding balance of
    the loan being replaced; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the new loan is made in relation to a loan workout
    whose purpose is to reduce or avoid losses on a real or potential
    housing loan insurance claim in respect of the outstanding loan
    being replaced.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction --- portfolio insurance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Corporation makes a commitment to insure a
  portfolio of housing loans up to a specified total value, it must not
  insure a housing loan in accordance with that commitment later than
  one year after the day on which the commitment is made.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-109, s. 1
:::

## [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785096 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reduced capital requirement

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For a period of one year
beginning on the day on which these Regulations come into force, the
amount of unencumbered paid-up capital required under
subparagraph 3(1)(a)(i) is at least \$1,000,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing portfolio commitment

- **[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 3.1(1) does not apply in
  respect of insurance coverage resulting from a commitment to insure a
  portfolio of housing loans up to a specified total value that is made
  before that subsection comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing portfolio commitment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 3.1(2) does not apply in respect of a
  commitment that is made before that subsection comes into force.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-109, s. 2
:::
