## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939453 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Pension and Allowance Adjustment
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-620).

## [Annual Adjustment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939455 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The basic pension shall be
adjusted annually, pursuant to subsection 75(1) of the [Pension
Act](/eng/acts/P-6), in the following manner:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount referred to in paragraph 75(1)(a) or (b)
  of that Act shall be adjusted to the nearest cent in accordance with
  the following rules, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the amount contains a fractional part of a
    dollar, the fraction shall be expressed as a decimal fraction of
    three or more digits after the decimal point,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the third and subsequent digits after the decimal
    point shall be dropped if the third digit after the decimal point is
    less than five, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the second digit after the decimal point shall be
    increased by one and the third and subsequent digits after the
    decimal point shall be dropped if the third digit after the decimal
    point is five or greater than five; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quotient obtained from the ratio referred to in
  subparagraph 75(1)(a)(ii) of that Act shall be expressed as a decimal
  fraction in accordance with the following rules, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the quotient contains a fraction that is less
    than one, that fraction shall be expressed as a decimal fraction of
    four or more digits after the decimal point,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the fourth and subsequent digits after the decimal
    point shall be dropped if the fourth digit after the decimal point
    is less than five, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the third digit after the decimal point shall be
    increased by one and the fourth and subsequent digits after the
    decimal point shall be dropped if the fourth digit after the decimal
    point is five or greater than five.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-601, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The percentage referred to
in subsection 75(3) of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) shall be the
quotient expressed as a decimal fraction in accordance with paragraph
2(b), converted to a percentage in the following manner:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quotient shall be multiplied by 100; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 100 shall be subtracted from the product obtained
  pursuant to paragraph (a).
