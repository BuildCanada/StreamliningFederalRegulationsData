## Indian Statute-Barred Income Tax Assessment Remission Order {#indian-statute-barred-income-tax-assessment-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/89-128
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1989-05-10

Order Respecting the Remission of Statute-Barred Income Tax Assessed as
Payable by Indians

P.C. 1989-740 1989-04-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to make the annexed remission order, is pleased
hereby, on the recommendation of the Minister of Indian Affairs and
Northern Development, the Minister of National Revenue and the Treasury
Board, pursuant to section 23 of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the annexed Order respecting the remission
of statute-barred income tax assessed as payable by Indians.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642052 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Indian Statute-Barred Income Tax Assessment Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-89-128).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642054 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned to that term
    by subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).
    ([Indien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642057 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to any Indian of income tax equal to the amount payable by that Indian
pursuant to an assessment under the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3),
including any assessed penalty relating thereto and all interest payable
thereon, where the amount assessed, the penalty and the interest have
not been paid and the statutory limitation period in respect of the
assessment expired before 1988.
