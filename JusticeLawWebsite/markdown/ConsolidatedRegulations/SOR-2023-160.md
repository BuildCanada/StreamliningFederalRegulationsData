## [Remission and Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1418606 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of goods classified under tariff
item No. 2844.43.00 that originate in Russia and that are imported
during the period beginning on the day after the day on which the
Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff Withdrawal Order (2022-2) ceases to have
effect and ending five years after the day on which this Order comes
into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on
condition that a claim for remission is made to the Minister of Public
Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years after the day on
which the goods are imported.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1418611 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order is repealed on
the seventh anniversary of the day on which it comes into force.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1418613 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
