## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1453753 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Radiocommunication
Act](/eng/acts/R-2).

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1453756 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employees of Royal Canadian Mounted
Police

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to sections 3 to 10, employees of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police
  Technical Investigation Services and other employees of the Royal
  Canadian Mounted Police who are required to install, use, possess,
  manufacture, import or distribute jammers in the course of their
  duties or training are exempt from the application of subsection 4(4)
  and paragraph 9(1)(b) of the Act in respect of those activities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}His Majesty in right of Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} His Majesty in right of Canada, as represented by any
  employee referred to in subsection (1), is exempt from the application
  of subsection 4(4) and paragraph 9(1)(b) of the Act in respect of the
  activities referred to in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public Works and Government Services

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Public Works and Government Services
  and employees of the Department of Public Works and Government
  Services who are required to import jammers on behalf of the Royal
  Canadian Mounted Police in the course of their duties are exempt from
  the application of subsection 4(4) of the Act in respect of that
  importation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}His Majesty in right of Canada

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} His Majesty in right of Canada, as represented by the
  Minister of Public Works and Government Services and any employee
  referred to in subsection (3), is exempt from the application of
  subsection 4(4) of the Act in respect of the importation referred to
  in subsection (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response to solicitation of bids

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person or entity is exempt from the application of
  subsection 4(4) of the Act in respect of the offering for sale of a
  jammer if the offer is in response to a solicitation of bids in
  relation to the provision of jammers to the Royal Canadian Mounted
  Police.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suppliers --- Royal Canadian Mounted
  Police

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 6 to 11, any person or entity
  that has entered into a contract with the Government of Canada for the
  provision of jammers, or services related to jammers, to the Royal
  Canadian Mounted Police or that performs any part of the work under
  that contract or any related subcontract --- and that is required to
  install, use, possess, manufacture, import, distribute, offer for sale
  or sell a jammer in accordance with the terms of that contract --- is
  exempt from the application of subsection 4(4) and paragraph 9(1)(b)
  of the Act in respect of those activities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purposes

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The exemptions under subsections (1) to (6) are
  granted for the following purposes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} national security;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} public safety, including with respect to
    penitentiaries and prisons;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} international relations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the investigation or prosecution of offences in
    Canada, including the preservation of evidence; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the protection of property or the prevention of
    serious harm to any person.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1453777 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  the exemption under subsection 2(1) is invoked, the Royal Canadian
  Mounted Police must provide a written notice to the Minister that
  contains the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the postal and email addresses and telephone
    numbers of the headquarters or centre of operations where
    responsibility for jammers is principally exercised;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the names, titles, postal and email addresses and
    telephone numbers of contact persons at that headquarters or centre
    of operations; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the names, titles, postal and email addresses and
    telephone numbers of the resource persons who are responsible for
    jammers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information --- update and confirm

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Royal Canadian Mounted Police must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide updated information to the Minister as soon
    as feasible after any of the information provided under
    subsection (1) changes; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} confirm the accuracy of the information provided
    under paragraph (a) and subsection (1) once a year, on or before the
    anniversary of the day on which this Order comes into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Training

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An employee who carries out
an activity under the exemption set out in subsection 2(1) must have
received or be receiving specialized training in relation to that
activity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to directives

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Royal Canadian Mounted
Police must ensure that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the employees of the Technical Investigation Services
  referred to in subsection 2(1) have access to the Technical
  Investigation Services directives that apply in respect of jammers;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the other employees referred to in subsection 2(1)
  have access to the directives of their respective operational unit
  that apply in respect of jammers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction of interference or
obstruction

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An employee referred to in
subsection 2(1) or a person or entity referred to in subsection 2(6)
must make every reasonable effort to restrict a jammer's interference
with or obstruction of radiocommunication to the smallest physical area,
the fewest number of radio frequencies, the appropriate power level and
the minimum duration required to accomplish the intended purpose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimize emissions and exposure

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An employee referred to in
subsection 2(1) or a person or entity referred to in subsection 2(6)
that installs or uses a jammer must do so in a manner that minimizes
unwanted emissions and the exposure of any person to radiofrequency
fields.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jammer characteristics

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Any jammer that is used for
the purposes of the exemption under subsection 2(1) or (6) must allow
for adjustments to power levels and to the radiofrequencies that it can
interfere with or obstruct.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prevention of unauthorized access and
storage

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An employee referred to in
subsection 2(1) or a person or entity referred to in subsection 2(6)
must ensure that any jammer for which they are responsible

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is accessible only to employees, persons or entities
  that are exempted from the application of subsection 4(4) of the Act;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} when not in use, is turned off and stored in a secure
  location or in a secure manner, including when it is being
  transported.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records relating to use

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Royal Canadian
Mounted Police must maintain records indicating, for each use of a
jammer by one of its employees or by a person or entity referred to in
subsection 2(6),

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the employee, person or entity, as the
  case may be, that used the jammer and the place at which, date on
  which and, if possible, time at which the jammer was used;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the radiofrequencies that were interfered with or
  obstructed; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the purposes listed in subsection 2(7) for which the
  jammer was used.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suppliers --- Royal Canadian Mounted
Police

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A person or entity
referred to in subsection 2(6) must not use a jammer except in the
following circumstances:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the use is carried out under the supervision of an
  employee referred to in subsection 2(1); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the use is to test the functionality
  of the jammer, to perform maintenance on the jammer or to provide
  training in relation to jammers.
