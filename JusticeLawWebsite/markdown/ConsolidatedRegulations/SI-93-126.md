## Order Amalgamating and Combining the Privy Council Office with the Federal-Provincial Relations Office under the Prime Minister {#order-amalgamating-and-combining-the-privy-council-office-with-the-federal-provincial-relations-office-under-the-prime-minister .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-126
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1993-07-14

Order Amalgamating and Combining the Privy Council Office with the
Federal-Provincial Relations Office under the Prime Minister

P.C. 1993-1493  1993-06-25
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby, pursuant to paragraph 2(b) of the
[Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34), to amalgamate and combine the Privy Council Office
with the Federal-Provincial Relations Office under the Prime Minister
and under the Clerk of the Privy Council and Secretary to the Cabinet.
