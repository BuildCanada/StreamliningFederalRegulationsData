## British Columbia Tree Fruit Pooling Regulations {#british-columbia-tree-fruit-pooling-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 147
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Regulations Respecting the Pooling of Tree Fruits Produced in British
Columbia
::::

::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521548 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [British Columbia Tree Fruit Pooling
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._147).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521550 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
unless the context otherwise requires,

[[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means B.C. Tree Fruits Limited, a
    company incorporated under the laws of the Province of British
    Columbia and having its chief place of business at the City of
    Kelowna, in the said Province; ([Agence]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[appropriate pooling committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appropriate pooling committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of matters relating to apples and
      crabapples, the committee established by subsection 6(1),

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of matters relating to tree fruits,
      other than apples and crabapples, the committee established by
      subsection 6(2), and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of matters relating to both apples or
      crabapples and other tree fruits, the committee established by
      subsection 6(3); ([comité de mise en commun]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that part of British Columbia
    lying east of the 121st meridian of west longitude and south of the
    51st parallel of north latitude; ([région]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[British Columbia Fruit Growers' Association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[British Columbia Fruit Growers' Association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means the association incorporated under that name under the laws of
    the Province of British Columbia and having its head office at the
    City of Kelowna in that Province; ([British Columbia Fruit Growers'
    Association]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the British Columbia
    Fruit Board; ([Office de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Okanagan Federated Shippers' Association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Okanagan Federated Shippers' Association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means the association incorporated under that name under the laws of
    the Province of British Columbia and having its head office at the
    City of Kelowna in that Province; ([Okanagan Federated Shippers'
    Association]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [British Columbia Tree Fruit
    Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-818), made under the [Agricultural
    Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6); ([décret]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a body corporate;
    ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[regulated product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regulated product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any tree fruit grown
    within the area in the year 1964 and subsequent years; ([produit

[[Scheme]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Scheme]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the British Columbia Tree Fruit
    Marketing Scheme, 1960, established pursuant to the Natural Products

## [Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521567 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Agency, being the
marketing agency appointed by the Commodity Board under the Scheme and
the Order, is hereby authorized to conduct a pool or pools for the
distribution of all proceeds received from the sale of the regulated
product.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Agency is hereby
required to distribute the proceeds of the sale referred to in section
3, after deducting all necessary and proper disbursements, expenses and
charges, in such manner that each person receives a share of the total
proceeds in relation to the amount, variety, size, grade and class of
the regulated product delivered by him, and to make payments in respect
thereof until total net proceeds are distributed.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the requirements
of section 4, the pool or pools conducted by the Agency shall be
constituted and operated in a manner similar to that in which like pools
heretofore operated by the Agency pursuant to contracts with persons
engaged in the production, packing and/or storing of the regulated
product or any part thereof have been conducted, and otherwise in such
manner as may from time to time be approved by the appropriate pooling
committee as provided for in section 6.

## [Pooling Committees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521571 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be a pooling committee that shall deal with matters relating to
  apples and crabapples consisting of seven members, five of whom shall
  be appointed by the British Columbia Fruit Growers' Association, one
  of whom shall be appointed by the Okanagan Federated Shippers'
  Association and one of whom shall be appointed by the Agency.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} There shall be a pooling committee that shall deal
  with matters relating to tree fruits, other than apples and
  crabapples, consisting of seven members, five of whom shall be
  appointed by the British Columbia Fruit Growers' Association, one of
  whom shall be appointed by the Okanagan Federated Shippers'
  Association and one of whom shall be appointed by the Agency.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} There shall be a pooling committee that shall deal
  with matters relating to both apples or crabapples and other tree
  fruits consisting of the members of the committees established by
  subsections (1) and (2).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person appointed as a member of one of the
  committees established by subsection (1) or (2) may also be appointed
  as a member of the other committee established by those subsections.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/83-895, s. 2
:::

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  pooling committee established by section 6 may make its own rules as
  to the convening and procedure of its meetings and the election of a
  chairman of such meetings.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The decision of the majority of members present at a
  meeting of a pooling committee at which a quorum is present shall be
  deemed to be the decision of the committee.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Five members constitute a quorum in respect of the
  committees established by subsections 6(1) and (2).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Ten members constitute a quorum in respect of the
  committee established by subsection 6(3).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} In the event of a tie vote at a meeting of a pooling
  committee, the chairman of the meeting shall have a second or casting
  vote.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/83-895, s. 2
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The British Columbia Fruit
Growers' Association, the Okanagan Federated Shippers' Association and
the Agency may at any time revoke any appointment to a pooling committee
made by it and may appoint another member to the committee in the place
of the person whose membership was revoked.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/83-895, s. 2
:::
