## Gore Bay Airport Zoning Regulations {#gore-bay-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-74
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1993-02-11

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Gore Bay Airport

P.C. 1993-262 1993-02-11
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 5.5 of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed Regulations respecting Gore
Bay Airport, substantially in the form set out in the schedule hereto,
was published in two successive issues of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on March 7th and 14th, 1992,
and in two successive issues of the Manitoulin Expositor on April 22nd
and 29th, 1992, and in two successive issues of the Manitoulin Recorder
on March 25th and April 1st, 1992, and a reasonable opportunity was
thereby afforded to interested persons to make representations to the
Minister of Transport with respect thereto;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 5.4 of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting zoning at Gore Bay
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-74).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958462 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Gore Bay Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-74).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958464 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Gore Bay Airport, in the
      Townships of Gordon and Barrie in the District of Manitoulin, in
      the Province of Ontario; ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the imaginary
      inclined planes that extend upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which planes are more particularly described in Part II of
      the schedule; ([surfaces d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the imaginary plane
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      plane is more particularly described in Part III of the schedule;
      ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, prepared for
      the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
      which portion is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the imaginary
      inclined planes that extend upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which planes are more
      particularly described in Part V of the schedule. ([surfaces de
      transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the airport reference point is 183.5 m above sea level.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-375, s. 2
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958476 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all land, including public road allowances, that is adjacent to or in
the vicinity of the airport, which land is more particularly described
in Part VI of the schedule.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958478 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct, on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [Aeronautical Facilities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958483 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No owner or lessee of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit any part of that land
to be used or developed in a manner that causes interference with any
signals or communications to and from any aircraft or to and from any
facilities used to provide services relating to aeronautics.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-375, s. 2(F)
:::

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958487 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth that is on any land to which these Regulations apply grows to a
height that exceeds in elevation at the location of the object any of
the surfaces referred to in section 4, the Minister may require that the
owner or lessee of the land remove the excessive growth.

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958489 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No owner or lessee of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit any part of that land
to be used for the disposal of any waste that is edible by or attractive
to birds.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point, shown on Gore Bay Airport Zoning Plan
No. 52-024 86-288, Sheet 5, dated September 4, 1990, is a point which
may be located by:

COMMENCING at Button 29 being the easterly end of runway 11-29 at Gore
Bay Airport, having Universal Transverse Mercator coordinates of
5 081 764.25 N and 379 127.00 E;

THENCE northwesterly along the centre line of said runway 11-29,
855.17 m to a point;

THENCE northeasterly and perpendicular to the centre line of said
runway 11-29, 428.26 m to the airport reference point, having Universal
Mercator coordinates of 5 082 304.86 N and 378 338.33 E.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Gore Bay Airport Zoning Plan
No. 52-024 86-228, Sheets 4, 5 and 6, dated September 4, 1990, are
planes abutting each end of the strip associated with runway 11-29 and
are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an inclined plane abutting the end of the strip
  associated with runway approach 11 having a ratio of 1 m measured
  vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an imaginary
  horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected centre line of
  the strip and distant 2 240 m measured horizontally from the end of
  the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal line being 486 m
  from the projected centre line; said imaginary horizontal line being
  44.8 m above the elevation at the end of the strip; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an inclined plane abutting the end of the strip
  associated with runway approach 29 having a ratio of 1 m measured
  vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an imaginary
  horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected centre line of
  the strip and distant 2 095 m measured horizontally from the end of
  the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal line being
  464.25 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary horizontal
  line being 41.9 m above the elevation at the end of the strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Gore Bay Airport Zoning Plan No. 52-024
86-228, Sheets 1 to 9 inclusive, dated September 4, 1990, is an
imaginary plane established at a constant elevation of 45 m above the
elevation of the airport reference point, except that, where that plane
is less than 9 m above the surface of the ground, the outer surface is
located at 9 m above the surface of the ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strip .SchedHeadL1}

The strip associated with runway 11-29, shown on Gore Bay Airport Zoning
Plan No. 52-024 86-228, Sheet 5, dated September 4, 1990, is 300 m in
width, 150 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway and
1 619.57 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Transitional Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-the-transitional-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Gore Bay Airport Zoning Plan
No. 52-024 86-228, Sheets 4, 5 and 6, dated September 4, 1990, is an
inclined plane rising at a ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 7 m
measured horizontally at right angles to the centre line and projected
centre line of the strip, extending upward and outward from the lateral
limits of the strip and its approach surfaces to an intersection with
the outer surface.
