## Critical Habitat of the Barrens Willow (Salix jejuna) Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-barrens-willow-salix-jejuna-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2019-236
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2019-06-24

Critical Habitat of the Barrens Willow (Salix jejuna) Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Barrens Willow ([Salix jejuna]{.otherLang lang="la"}) is a
wildlife species that is listed as an endangered species in Part 2 of
Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a1){#fn_a1-ID0EBBGA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a1-ID0EBBGA){#fn_a1}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

Whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is
specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection
58(2)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b2){#fn_b2-ID0EBBEA} of that
Act;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b2-ID0EBBEA){#fn_b2}S.C.
  2015, c. 10, s. 60
  :::

And whereas the Minister of the Environment is of the opinion that the
annexed Order would affect land that is under the authority of the
Minister of Fisheries and Oceans and, pursuant to subsection 58(9) of
that Act, has consulted with that Minister with respect to the Order;

Therefore, the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to subsections
58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a1){#fn_a1-ID0EDACA}, makes the annexed
[Critical Habitat of the Barrens Willow ([Salix jejuna]{.otherLang
lang="la"}) Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-236).

Gatineau, June 19, 2019

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1179669 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Barrens Willow ([Salix jejuna]{.otherLang lang="la"}), which is
identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is included in
the Species at Risk Public Registry.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1179671 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
