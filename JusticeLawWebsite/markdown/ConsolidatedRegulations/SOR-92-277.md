## Life Companies Borrowing Regulations {#life-companies-borrowing-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-277
:::

[INSURANCE COMPANIES ACT](/eng/acts/I-11.8)

Registration 1992-05-14

Regulations Respecting the Debt Obligations of Life Companies

P.C. 1992-1032  1992-05-14
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to the definition [[total
assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1), subsection 473(1) and
section 703 of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations respecting the debt obligations of life
companies](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-277), effective June 1, 1992.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1991, c. 47
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-943347 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Life Companies Borrowing
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-277).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-943349 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Insurance Companies
Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8). (*Loi*)

## [Prescribed Subsidiaries]{.HTitleText1} {#h-943351 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 473(1) of the Act, a prescribed subsidiary of a life company
is a subsidiary of the life company other than a subsidiary that is a
financial institution that is not engaged in the insuring of risks.

## [Debt Obligation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-943353 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 473(1) of the Act, [[debt obligation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
has the meaning assigned to that expression by subsection 2(1) of the
Act.

## [Manner of Determining Total Debt Obligations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-943355 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 473(1) of the Act and subject to subsection 473(2) of the
Act, the aggregate of the total debt obligations of a life company is
the aggregate of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the debt obligations of the life company; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any debt obligations of the prescribed subsidiaries
  of the life company that are not held by the life company.

## [Total Assets]{.HTitleText1} {#h-943359 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 473(1) of the Act, [[total assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in
respect of a life company, at a particular time, means the total of the
assets of the life company that would be reported on the balance sheet
of the life company prepared as at that time in accordance with the
accounting principles and specifications of the Superintendent referred
to in subsection 331(4) of the Act, less the total of the assets,
determined in the same manner, of any subsidiary of the life company
that is not a prescribed subsidiary.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-67, s. 5
:::

## [Prescribed Percentage of Total Assets]{.HTitleText1} {#h-943363 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 473(1) of the Act and subject to subsection 473(2) of the
Act, the prescribed percentage of the total assets of a life company is
twenty per cent.
