  the Minister considers that a person is unable for a sufficient reason
  to make a request for a reconsideration or to make an appeal, an
  application, a statement or a notice, the request for reconsideration,
  appeal, application, statement or notice may be made on that person's
  behalf by a responsible person or agency.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a person is or has been in receipt of
  assistance pursuant to the [Unemployment Assistance
  Act](/eng/acts/U-0.6) or the Canada Assistance Plan, an application
  for a pension may be made on behalf of that person by the appropriate
  provincial authority.

### [Approval of an Application for a Pension]{.HTitleText2} {#h-499927 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), where the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is satisfied that an applicant is qualified for a
    pension in accordance with sections 3 to 5 of the Act, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} approves the application after the last day of the
    month in which it was received,

  the Minister's approval shall be effective on the latest of

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the application was received,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the applicant became qualified for
    a pension in accordance with sections 3 to 5 of the Act, and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date specified in writing by the applicant.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister is satisfied that an applicant
  mentioned in subsection (1) attained the age of 65 years before the
  day on which the application was received, the Minister's approval of
  the application shall be effective as of the latest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day that is one year before the day on which
    the application was received,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the applicant attained the age of
    65 years;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the applicant became qualified for
    a pension in accordance with sections 3 to 5 of the Act; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the month immediately before the date specified in
    writing by the applicant.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister, under subsection 5(2) or 11(3) of
  the Act, deems an application to have been made and approved, the
  Minister's approval shall be effective on the day on which the person
  attained the age of 65 years.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister waives the requirement for an
  application in respect of a person under subsection 5(4) of the Act,
  the Minister's approval is effective on the day on which the person
  attains the age of 65 years.

### [Manner of Rounding Off Amount of Partial Monthly Pension]{.HTitleText2} {#h-499949 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where the amount of a
partial monthly pension referred to in subsection 3(3) of the Act, as
increased under subsection 7.1(2) of the Act if applicable, contains a
fraction of a dollar that is represented by three or more digits,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the third digit is less than 5, the third and
  subsequent digits shall be dropped; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the third digit is 5 or greater than 5, the second
  digit shall be increased by 1 and the third and subsequent digits
  shall be dropped.

### [Manner of Quarterly Adjustment of Benefit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-499955 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the amount of a full monthly pension or supplement is adjusted
  quarterly pursuant to subsection 7(2) or 12(2) of the Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there shall be an adjustment to the nearest cent,
    in accordance with subsection (2), of the product obtained

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by multiplying the amount referred to in
      paragraph 7(2)(a) of the Act by the ratio referred to in paragraph
      7(2)(b) of the Act, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by multiplying the amount referred to in
      paragraph 12(2)(a) of the Act by the ratio referred to in
      paragraph 12(2)(b) of the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the ratios referred to in paragraph (a) shall be
    expressed as decimal fractions in accordance with subsection (3).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the product referred to in subparagraph
  (1)(a)(i), as increased under subsection 7.1(1) of the Act if
  applicable, or the product referred to in subparagraph (1)(a)(ii),
  contains a fraction of a dollar that is represented by three or more
  digits,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the third digit is less than 5, the third and
    subsequent digits shall be dropped; and,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the third digit is 5 or greater than 5, the
    second digit shall be increased by 1 and the third and subsequent
    digits shall be dropped.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the ratios referred to in paragraph (1)(a)
  contain a fraction that is less than 1, that fraction shall be
  expressed as a decimal fraction of four digits after the decimal
  point, and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the fourth digit after the decimal point is
    less than 5, the fourth digit after the decimal point shall be
    dropped; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the fourth digit after the decimal point is 5
    or greater than 5, the third digit after the decimal point shall be
    increased by 1 and the fourth digit after the decimal point shall be
    dropped.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-813, s. 5
- SOR/2013-23, s. 4

### [Determination of Average Consumer Price Index for Period of Months]{.HTitleText2} {#h-499970 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  average of the Consumer Price Index for Canada for a period of months
  shall be determined by dividing the aggregate of the Consumer Price
  Index for Canada, as published by Statistics Canada under the
  authority of the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19), for each month in
  that period by the number of months in that period.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the quotient obtained pursuant to
  subsection (1) contains a fraction that is less than 1, such fraction
  shall be expressed as a decimal fraction of two digits after the
  decimal point and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the second digit after the decimal point shall be
    dropped if that digit is less than 5; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the first digit after the decimal point shall be
    increased by one and the second digit shall be dropped if the second
    digit is 5 or greater than 5.

### [Month in which Loss of Income Occurs]{.HTitleText2} {#h-500002 .Subheading}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 14 of the Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the month in which an applicant or an applicant's
  spouse or common-law partner

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} ceases to hold an office or employment shall be the
    month in which the last day in respect of which he receives income
    from that office or employment falls, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} ceases to carry on a business shall be the month
    in which the last day on which he actively carries on that business
    falls; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the month in which an applicant or an applicant's
  spouse or common-law partner suffers a loss of income due to
  termination or reduction of pension income shall be the month in which
  that termination or reduction actually occurs.

::: HistoricalNote

### [Definition of Pension Income]{.HTitleText2} {#h-500010 .Subheading}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 14 of the Act, [[pension income]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
aggregate of amounts received as

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} annuity payments;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} alimony and maintenance payments;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} employment insurance benefits;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} disability benefits deriving from a private insurance
  plan;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any benefit, other than a death benefit, under the
  [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or a provincial pension plan as
  defined in the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} superannuation or pension payments, other than a
  benefit received pursuant to the Act or any similar payment received
  pursuant to a law of a provincial legislature;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} compensation under a federal or provincial employee's
  or worker's compensation law in respect of an injury, disability or
  death;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} income assistance benefits under an agreement
  referred to in subsection 33(1) of the [Department of Human Resources
  Development Act](/eng/acts/H-5.8) by reason of a permanent reduction
  in the work force as described in that subsection; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} income assistance benefits under the Plant Workers'
  Adjustment Program, the Fisheries Early Retirement Program or the
  Northern Cod Adjustment and Recovery Program by reason of a permanent
  reduction in the work force.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/84-656, s. 3
- SOR/86-956, s. 1(E)
- SOR/90-813, s. 9
- SOR/2001-148, s. 1
:::

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 19, the Minister
  shall determine the age and identity of an applicant for the purposes
  of the Act in accordance with whichever of subsections (2) to (2.2) is
  applicable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall determine the age and identity of
  an applicant on the basis of any information provided to the Minister
  by the Canada Employment Insurance Commission under subsection 28.2(5)
  of the [Department of Employment and Social Development
  Act](/eng/acts/H-5.7).

- [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall determine the age and identity
  of an applicant on the basis of a birth certificate or a certified
  copy of one.

- [(2.2)]{.lawlabel} If there is sufficient reason to believe that a
  birth certificate is not available, the Minister shall determine the
  age and identity of an applicant on the basis of any other evidence
  and information with respect to the age and identity of the applicant
  that is available from any source.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is unable to determine the age and
  identity of an applicant under any of subsections (2) to (2.2), the
  Minister shall, subject to the following conditions, request that
  Statistics Canada search the census records for information as to the
  age and identity of the applicant:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any such request shall be made in the form
    prescribed by the Chief Statistician of Canada and shall bear the
    signed consent of the person concerning whom the information is
    sought and shall provide such specific information as may be
    necessary for the purpose of making a search of the census records;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any information supplied by Statistics Canada shall
    be kept confidential and shall not be used for any purpose other
    than that of establishing the age of the applicant as required under
    the Act, the Canada Assistance Plan or the [Canada Pension
    Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), as the case may be.

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of the Act and these Regulations,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person resides in Canada if he makes his home and
    ordinarily lives in any part of Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person is present in Canada when he is physically
    present in any part of Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who lives on a ship outside the territorial
  waters of Canada shall, while so living, be deemed not to be living in
  any part of Canada.

- [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), a person who is not
  a Canadian citizen or a permanent resident of Canada does not reside
  in Canada for the purposes of the Act and these Regulations during any
  period in which he is present in Canada

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of carrying out his duties as a
    properly accredited diplomat, consular officer, representative or
    official of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a country other than Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the United Nations or any of its agencies, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any intergovernmental organization in which
      Canada participates;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as a member of a military force present in Canada
    for training or for any other purpose in connection with the defence
    or security interests of Canada or under any treaty or agreement
    between Canada and another country;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} as the spouse, common-law partner or dependant of a
    person referred to in paragraph (a) or (b) or the dependant of that
    person's spouse or common-law partner; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} as a member of the staff of or as a person
    otherwise accompanying a person referred to in paragraph (a), (b) or
    (c).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the Act and these Regulations,
  where a person becomes the spouse or common-law partner of a person
  residing in Canada while the person residing in Canada is absent from
  Canada in any of the circumstances specified in paragraph (5)(a) or
  (b), the period outside Canada of the spouse after their marriage or
  of the common-law partner after becoming such a partner is considered
  a period of residence and presence in Canada, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the spouse or common-law partner returns to Canada
    either before or within six months after the return of the person
    residing in Canada or within six months after that person's death if
    that person dies while so absent from Canada; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the spouse or common-law partner attains, during
    that period outside Canada, an age at which the spouse or common-law
    partner is eligible to be paid a pension under the Act.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any interval of absence from Canada of a person
  resident in Canada that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} of a temporary nature and does not exceed one year,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of attending a school or
    university, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} specified in subsection (5)

  shall be deemed not to have interrupted that person's residence or
  presence in Canada.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The absences from Canada referred to in paragraph
  (4)(c) of a person residing in Canada are absences under the following
  circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} while that person was employed out of Canada

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by the United Nations or one of its specialized
      agencies,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by the North Atlantic Treaty Organization,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} by the Commonwealth Secretariat,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} by the Organization of Economic Cooperation and
      Development,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} by l'Agence de coopération culturelle et
      technique, or

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} by a Canadian firm or corporation as a
      representative or member thereof,

    if during his employment out of Canada he

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} had in Canada a permanent place of abode to
      which he intended to return, or

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} maintained in Canada a self-contained domestic
      establishment,

    and he returned to Canada within six months after the end of his
    employment out of Canada or he attained, while employed out of
    Canada, an age at which he was eligible to be paid a pension under
    the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} while that person was employed or engaged out of
    Canada

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by the Government of Canada or of the government
      or a municipal corporation of any province,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the performance of services in another
      country under a development or assistance program that is
      sponsored or operated in that country by the Government of Canada
      or of a province or by a non-profit Canadian agency,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} as a member of the Canadian Forces, pursuant to
      and in connection with the requirements of his duties,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in work for Canada connected with the
      prosecution of any war,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} as a member of the armed forces of any ally of
      Canada during any war,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} as a missionary with any religious group or
      organization,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} as a worker in lumbering, harvesting, fishing
      or other seasonal employment,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} as a transport worker on trains, aircraft,
      ships or buses running between Canada and points outside Canada or
      other similar employment, or

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} as an employee, a member or an officer of an
      international charitable organization,

    if he returned to Canada within six months of the end of his
    employment or engagement out of Canada or he attained, while
    employed or engaged out of Canada, an age at which he was eligible
    to be paid a pension under the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} while that person was accompanying their spouse or
    common-law partner who was absent from Canada in any of the
    circumstances specified in paragraph (a) or (b) or for the purpose
    of attending school or university, if that person

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} returned to Canada either before or within six
      months after the return of their spouse or common-law partner or
      within six months after the death of their spouse or common-law
      partner, if their spouse or common-law partner died while so
      absent from Canada, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} while so absent from Canada, attained an age at
      which he was eligible to be paid a pension under the Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} while that person was awaiting transportation to
    Canada during or immediately after World War II, if he

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} was unable to return to Canada owing to the
      dislocation of transportation facilities, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} returned to Canada when transportation became
      available;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} while that person was accompanying his spouse who
    was resident in Canada and awaiting transportation to Canada during
    or immediately after World War II, if that person

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} was unable to return to Canada owing to the
      dislocation of transportation facilities, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} returned to Canada when transportation became
      available; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} while that person was a dependent person and was
    accompanying and residing outside Canada with the person on whom he
    was dependent, if the person on whom he was dependent resided in
    Canada and was absent from Canada in any of the circumstances
    specified in paragraph (a) or (b) and if the dependent person

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} returned to Canada before or within six months
      after the return of the person on whom he was dependent or within
      six months after that person's death, if that person died while so
      absent from Canada, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} while so absent from Canada, attained an age at
      which he was eligible to be paid a pension under the Act.

- [(5.1)]{.lawlabel} Where, by virtue of an agreement entered into under
  subsection 40(1) of the Act, a person is subject to the Act while
  residing in a country other than Canada, the absence from Canada of
  that person, the person's spouse or common-law partner and the
  dependants of the person or of the person's spouse or common-law
  partner, if the spouse, common-law partner or dependants, as the case
  may be, reside with the person in that country, shall, for the
  purposes of an allowance, not be considered to have interrupted the
  residence or presence in Canada of the person, spouse, common-law
  partner or dependants.

- [(5.2)]{.lawlabel} Where a person who is resident in Canada is subject
  to the legislation of a country other than Canada by virtue of an
  agreement entered into under subsection 40(1) of the Act, and the
  person's spouse or common-law partner or the dependant of the person
  or of the person's spouse or common-law partner engages in pensionable
  employment as defined in the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or
  in the plan of a province providing a comprehensive pension plan, any
  period in such pensionable employment shall, for the purposes of the
  Act and these Regulations, be considered to be a period of residence
  in Canada.

- [(5.3)]{.lawlabel} Where, by virtue of an agreement entered into under
  subsection 40(1) of the Act, a person is subject to the legislation of
  a country other than Canada, that person shall, for the purposes of
  the Act and these Regulations, be deemed not to be resident in Canada.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (5)(f), [[dependent
  person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, with respect to a person who was
  absent from Canada in any of the circumstances specified in paragraph
  (5)(a) or (b), that person's father, mother, brother, sister, child or
  foster child or the father, mother, child or foster child of the
  person's spouse or common-law partner.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} [[Dependant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a person who does not reside in
    Canada within the meaning of subsection (2.1),

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a person who is deemed to be
    resident in Canada within the meaning of subsection (5.1), or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a person referred to in subsection
    (5.2) who, while resident in Canada is subject to the legislation of
    a country other than Canada,

  that person's father, mother, brother, sister, child or foster child
  or the father, mother, child or foster child of the person's spouse or
  common-law partner.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 4.1 of the Act as it
  relates to the requirement of subparagraph 3(1)(c)(iii) of the Act,
  the prescribed information is,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person who is in receipt of or has
    been approved for a retirement, disability or survivor's pension
    under the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or a retirement,
    disability or surviving spouse's pension under An Act respecting the
    Québec Pension Plan, CQLR, c. R-9, information indicating that the
    person, for at least 40 years, for all or part of each of those
    years,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} had unadjusted pensionable earnings under the
      [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) that were above the person's
      basic exemption for that year, or had unadjusted pensionable
      earnings under An Act respecting the Québec Pension Plan that were
      above the person's personal exemption for that year,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} received a retirement pension or disability
      pension under either the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or
      An Act respecting the Québec Pension Plan, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} had time excluded from a contributory period
      under the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) because the person
      was a family allowance recipient, or had time not included in a
      contributory period under An Act respecting the Québec Pension
      Plan because the person was a recipient of family benefits; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person who is not in receipt of
    and has not been approved for a retirement, disability or survivor's
    pension under the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or a
    retirement, disability or surviving spouse's pension under An Act
    respecting the Québec Pension Plan, CQLR, c. R-9, information
    indicating that the person,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for at least 40 years, for all or part of each of
      those years, met the requirements set out in subparagraph
      (a)(i), (ii) or (iii), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for the most recent calendar year for which the
      filing due date has passed and for at least 40 of the years
      referred to in subparagraph (i), other than for 1969, 1970 and
      1971, filed an income tax return as a resident of Canada with the
      Minister of National Revenue.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (8),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the [Canada Pension
    Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), [[basic exemption]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
    [[contributory period]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[unadjusted
    pensionable earnings]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} have the same meaning
    as in subsection 2(1) of that Act and [[family allowance
    recipient]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 42(1) of that Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to An Act respecting the Québec Pension
    Plan, R.S.Q., c. R-9, [[recipient of family
    benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[personal
    exemption]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[unadjusted pensionable
    earnings]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[contributory
    period]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} have the same meaning as in paragraph
    1(v) and sections 43, 98 and 101 of that Act respectively.

### [Legal Residence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-500144 .Subheading}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsections 4(1), 19(2) and 21(2) of the Act, [[legal
  residence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, with respect to a person described
  in any of those subsections, means that, on the applicable day
  specified in paragraph (a) or (b) of those subsections, that person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is or was lawfully in Canada pursuant to the
    immigration laws of Canada in force on that day;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is or was a resident of Canada and is or was absent
    from Canada, but

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is deemed, pursuant to subsection 21(4) or (5) or
      under the terms of an agreement entered into under subsection
      40(1) of the Act, not to have interrupted the person's residence
      in Canada during that absence, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} was lawfully in Canada pursuant to the
      immigration laws of Canada immediately prior to the commencement
      of the absence; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is not or was not resident of Canada but is deemed,
    pursuant to subsection 21(3) or under the terms of an agreement
    entered into under subsection 40(1) of the Act, to be or to have
    been resident in Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 4.1 of the Act as it
  relates to the requirement of paragraph 4(1)(a) of the Act, the
  prescribed information is both

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a current address in Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information that is referred to in

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 21(8)(a), in the case of a person who
      is in receipt of or has been approved for a retirement, disability
      or survivor's pension under the [Canada Pension
      Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or a retirement, disability or surviving
      spouse's pension under An Act respecting the Québec Pension Plan,
      CQLR, c. R-9, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 21(8)(b), in the case of a person who
      is not in receipt of and has not been approved for a retirement,
      disability or survivor's pension under the [Canada Pension
      Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or a retirement, disability or surviving
      spouse's pension under An Act respecting the Québec Pension Plan,
      CQLR, c. R-9.

### [Sponsorship Immigrants]{.HTitleText2} {#h-500163 .Subheading}

- **[[22.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections
  11(8), 19(6.2) and 21(9.1) of the Act, the following are events in
  relation to a sponsor referred to in those subsections:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the death of the sponsor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sponsor's conviction of an offence under the
    [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) relating to the sponsored

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a determination that the sponsor is a bankrupt as
    defined in section 2 of the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency
    Act](/eng/acts/B-3); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the sentencing of the sponsor to a term of
    imprisonment of more than six months.

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whose undertaking in respect of a pensioner, the
    spouse or common-law partner of a pensioner, or a survivor, as the
    case may be, was in effect on or after March 7, 1996; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to whom an event referred to in any of paragraphs
    (1)(a) to (d) occurred on or after March 7, 1996.

### [Payment to Person other than Beneficiary]{.HTitleText2} {#h-500183 .Subheading}

::: HistoricalNote

- SOR/96-521, s. 28(F)

\]

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a person or agency provides evidence satisfactory to the Minister that
  a beneficiary, by reason of infirmity, illness, insanity or other
  cause, is incapable of managing his own affairs, the Minister may
  direct the benefit to be paid on behalf of such beneficiary

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to any person or agency that the Minister is
    satisfied is authorized by or pursuant to any law of Canada or of a
    province to manage the affairs of that beneficiary; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the Minister is not satisfied that any person
    or agency is so authorized, to any person or agency that the
    Minister may appoint for such purpose and that has entered into an
    agreement with the Minister to administer and expend the benefit on
    behalf of that beneficiary in accordance with the terms of the
    agreement.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall furnish to a person or agency
  appointed under subsection (1) to receive a benefit such directions
  regarding the administration and expenditure of the benefit as the
  Minister considers in the best interests of the beneficiary.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any person or agency appointed under subsection (1)
  shall account for the benefit payments received and the disbursements
  made, the account to be in such form and to be made at such times as
  the Minister may require.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a benefit is paid to any person or agency to be
  administered and expended for and on behalf of a beneficiary, the
  notices required by section 25 shall be given by such person or
  agency.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A benefit payment may be made to the estate of a
  deceased beneficiary or, if there is no estate, to a person or agency
  designated by the Minister, where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an amount is payable as a benefit to the deceased
    beneficiary; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a benefit payment made to the beneficiary or made
    on behalf of the beneficiary by cheque or otherwise is returned to
    the Minister after the beneficiary's death.

### [Suspension of Payments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-500209 .Subheading}

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall suspend the payment of a benefit in respect of any
  beneficiary where it appears to him that the beneficiary is ineligible
  for payment of the benefit and may suspend the payment where it
  appears to him that further inquiry into the eligibility of the
  beneficiary is necessary, and such suspension shall continue until
  evidence satisfactory to the Minister is given that the beneficiary is
  eligible for the benefit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When payment of any benefit that has been suspended
  under subsection (1) is resumed, the Minister shall cause payment of
  the benefit to be made for any portion of the period of suspension
  during which the beneficiary was eligible for benefit.

succession, under this Act or any other Act or program administered by

### [Deductions from Benefits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-500229 .Subheading}

- **[[28.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 36(2)
  of the Act, the Minister may make a deduction from a benefit payable
  under the Act if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the provincial authority or the municipal authority
    that paid the advance, assistance or welfare payments submits a
    record of the payment to the Minister, together with an irrevocable
    consent to the deduction signed by the person receiving the payment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the record and consent referred to in paragraph (a)
    are received by the Minister prior to the expiration of one year
    after the day on which the consent is signed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister and the provincial authority or the
    municipal authority that paid the advance, assistance or welfare
    payment referred to in paragraph (a) have concluded an agreement in
    respect of the procedure to be followed in submitting records and
    consents; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount deducted exceeds \$50.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection 36(2) of the Act, if,
  for any reason, no deduction has been made or a deduction and payment
  have been made by the Minister for an amount that is less than the
  amount that might have been paid under that subsection, the Minister
  shall not authorize any other deduction and payment.

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** A request for a
reconsideration under section 27.1 of the Act shall be made in writing
and be conveyed to the Minister and shall set out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and Social Insurance Number or
  Account Number of the person; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the grounds for the request for a reconsideration and
  a statement of the facts that form the basis of that request.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/83-84, s. 5
- SOR/96-521, s. 17
- SOR/2000-133, s. 10

- **[[29.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection
  27.1(1) and (1.1) of the Act and subject to subsection (2), the
  Minister may allow a longer period to make a request for
  reconsideration of a decision or determination if the Minister is
  satisfied that there is a reasonable explanation for requesting a
  longer period and the person has demonstrated a continuing intention
  to request a reconsideration.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must also be satisfied that the request
  for reconsideration has a reasonable chance of success, and that no
  prejudice would be caused to the Minister or a party by allowing a
  longer period to make the request, if the request for reconsideration

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is made after the 365-day period after the day on
    which the person is notified in writing of the decision or
    determination; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is made by a person who has applied again for the
    same benefit.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may take any necessary steps to obtain
  information that the Minister may require to decide whether to allow a
  longer period to make a request for reconsideration.

### [Interest on Amounts Owing to Her Majesty]{.HTitleText2} {#h-500306 .Subheading}

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this section.

  [[average bank rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[average bank rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the simple
      arithmetic mean of the bank rates that are established during the
      month before the month in respect of which interest is being
      calculated. ([taux d'escompte moyen]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[bank rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[bank rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rate of interest
      established weekly by the Bank of Canada as the minimum rate at
      which the Bank of Canada makes short-term advances to members of
      the Canadian Payments Association. ([taux
      d'escompte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[debt]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[debt]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a debt due under subsection 37(2) of the Act in
        respect of which a penalty has been imposed under section 44.1
        of the Act; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a debt due under subsection 37(2.02) of the
        Act. ([créance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[demand for payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[demand for payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a demand for
      payment in writing and includes a notification of a decision to
      impose a penalty under section 44.1 of the Act or of the amount of
      a penalty imposed under that section. ([demande de
      paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[due date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[due date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a debt,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where a payment schedule has been established,
        any day on which a scheduled payment is to be made; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where no payment schedule has been established,
        the day that is 120 days after the day on which a demand for
        payment was issued. ([date d'exigibilité]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Interest is payable on all debts that are recoverable
  on or after the day on which this section comes into force.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The accrual of interest on a debt, at the rate set
  out in subsection (4), begins on the due date.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Interest accrues on a debt at a rate that is
  calculated daily and compounded monthly at the average bank rate plus
  three per cent.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Interest does not accrue on a debt during the period
  in which a reconsideration under subsection 27.1(2) of the Act, an
  appeal under subsection 28(1) of the Act or section 55 of the
  [Department of Employment and Social Development Act](/eng/acts/H-5.7)
  or a judicial review under the [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7) is
  pending in respect of that debt.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The accrual of interest on a debt or on a portion of
  the debt, as applicable, ceases on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day before the day on which a scheduled payment
    in respect of the debt or a payment of the debt in full is received
    by Her Majesty;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the debt or the portion of the
    debt is remitted under subsection 37(4) of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the penalty that constitutes the
    debt is reduced or the decision imposing that penalty is rescinded
    under

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} subsection 44.1(4) of the Act, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the latest of the following decisions in respect
      of a decision or determination of the Minister under section 44.1
      of the Act:

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a decision of the Minister under subsection
        27.1(2) of the Act, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a decision on an appeal under subsection 28(1)
        of the Act or section 55 of the [Department of Employment and
        Social Development Act](/eng/acts/H-5.7) or a judicial review
        under the [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the accrued interest is remitted
    under subsection (7); or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the debtor dies.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The conditions under which the Minister may remit in
  whole or in part the interest payable under this section are that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the interest has ceased to accrue in accordance
    with paragraph (6)(c);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the interest cannot be collected within the
    reasonably foreseeable future;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the administrative costs of collecting the interest
    would exceed the amount of that interest; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the payment of the interest would result in undue
    hardship to the debtor.
