## Public Works Nuisances Regulations {#public-works-nuisances-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1365
:::

[DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND GOVERNMENT SERVICES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-38.2)

Regulations Respecting Loitering and Nuisances on Public Works
::::

:::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-506132 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Public Works Nuisances
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1365).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-506134 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** No person shall loiter or
commit any nuisance in, on or about any public work.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No unauthorized person
shall, except in cases of emergency, open or use a door designated as an
emergency exit in or on any building under the control of the Minister.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall, without
the prior authorization of the Minister, enter on or into any public
work or portion thereof, to which the public is not permitted entry.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-370, s. 1
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect,
construct or post any thing, material or object in or upon any public
work other than in such places as are specifically designated for such
purposes.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-370, s. 1
:::

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall occupy, reside, camp or sleep in or upon any public work
  or use any vehicle for the purpose of occupying a public work or
  residing, camping or sleeping thereon.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall erect, use, occupy or maintain any
  structure in or upon any public work except by or under the authority
  of the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-370, s. 1
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect,
remove, alter or deface any information sign, regulatory sign, or
traffic sign or device on any public work, except by or under the
authority of the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-370, s. 1
:::

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person found contravening section 6 of these Regulations shall
  forthwith, on receiving notice from the Minister or a peace officer,
  either orally or in writing, requiring him to cease such activity and
  to quit the public work, remove his personal property from and quit
  the public work and shall not thereafter resume the activity to which
  the notice applies.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/85-370, s. 1
  - SOR/90-155, s. 1
  - SOR/92-737, s. 1
  :::

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/92-737, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A peace officer may remove
from a public work any person who refuses to obey a notice under section
8 and any personal property apparently in the possession of that person.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-370, s. 1
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Any information, sign,
regulatory sign, or traffic sign or device erected in or upon any public
work shall, unless the contrary is established, be presumed to have been
erected by or under the authority of the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-370, s. 1
:::

**[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1 .sectionLabel}** [\[Revoked,
SOR/92-737, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-506163 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who contravenes any provision of sections 2, 3, 4, 5, or 7 is
  guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not
  exceeding \$400.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person who fails to forthwith remove his personal
  property from and quit a public work after receiving a notice referred
  to in section 8 or who resumes an activity in respect of which the
  person has received such a notice is guilty of an offence and liable
  on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding \$400.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-370, s. 1
- SOR/90-155, s. 3
- SOR/92-737, s. 3
:::
