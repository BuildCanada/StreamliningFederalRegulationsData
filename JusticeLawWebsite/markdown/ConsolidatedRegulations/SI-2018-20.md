## Application for Citizenship --- Minors Remission Order {#application-for-citizenship-minors-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2018-20
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2018-02-21

Application for Citizenship --- Minors Remission Order

P.C. 2018-132 2018-02-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}

collection of certain fees is unreasonable, on the recommendation of the
Treasury Board and the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, pursuant
to subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote

makes the annexed [Application for Citizenship --- Minors Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2018-20).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[minor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection 2(1)
of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted, to
any person who paid for a minor who is the subject of an application for
grant of citizenship under subsection 5(1) of the [Citizenship
Act](/eng/acts/C-29), during the period beginning on June 19, 2017 and
ending on the day before the day on which the Regulations Amending the
Citizenship Regulations (Fees for Minor Applicants) come into force, of
the amount of \$430, being a portion of the fee of \$530 paid for that
application under subsection 31(1) of the [Citizenship
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-246).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-2018-20_e_hq_21431){#fn_SI-2018-20_e_hq_21431-ID0EBCA}[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
.sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force on the day on which the
Regulations Amending the Citizenship Regulations (Fees for Minor
Applicants) come into force, but if it is made after that day, it comes
into force on the day on which it is made.
