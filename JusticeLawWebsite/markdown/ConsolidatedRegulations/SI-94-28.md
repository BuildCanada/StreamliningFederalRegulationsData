## Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Great Slave Lake, N.T.) {#reservation-to-the-crown-waiver-order-great-slave-lake-n.t. .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/94-28
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 1994-03-23

Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Great Slave Lake, N.T.)

P.C. 1994-390  1994-03-10
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, is pleased
hereby,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Territorial Lands
  Act](/eng/acts/T-7), to order that any portion of the lands described
  in the schedule hereto lying within 30.48 metres (100 feet) of the
  Ordinary High Water Mark of Great Slave Lake in the Northwest
  Territories be included in the sale of the said lands; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to section 14 of the [Territorial Lands
  Act](/eng/acts/T-7), to order that any portion of the lands described
  in the schedule hereto lying below the Ordinary High Water Mark of
  Great Slave Lake in the Northwest Territories be included in the sale
  of the said lands.
:::

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-647678 .scheduleLabel}

The whole of Lot numbered 11 in Block lettered E in the City of

of survey of record number 73520 in the Canada Lands Surveys Records at
Ottawa, a copy of which is filed in the Land Titles Office for the
