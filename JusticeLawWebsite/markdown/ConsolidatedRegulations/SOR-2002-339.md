Definition of [[member institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  section, [[member institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
  meaning as in section 2 of the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shared premises

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A bank to which paragraph 413(1)(b) or (c) of the
  [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) applies may carry on business in Canada
  on premises that are shared with those of a member institution that is
  affiliated with the bank if, on those premises, whenever a request is
  made in person to open a deposit account with the bank, the bank opens
  the account at a work space that is separate and distinct from every
  work space at which the member institution carries on business.
