## [Drug Screening Equipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-852436 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approved equipment

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the
definition [[approved drug screening equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
in section 320.11 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the following
equipment that is designed to ascertain the presence of a drug in a
person's body is approved:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Dräger DrugTest® 5000 and a Dräger DrugTest® 5000
  STK-CA, when used together; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a SoToxa™, an Abbott SoToxa™ Test Cartridge and an
  Abbott SoToxa™ Oral Fluid Collection Device, when used together.
