## British Columbia Hog Order {#british-columbia-hog-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-158
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1990-03-08

Order Granting Authority to the British Columbia Hog Marketing
Commission to Regulate the Marketing of Hogs Produced in British
Columbia in Interprovincial and Export Trade

P.C. 1990-429 1990-03-08
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to section 2 of the [Agricultural
Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed Order granting authority to the British Columbia Hog Marketing
Commission to regulate the marketing of hogs produced in British
Columbia in interprovincial and export trade.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921672 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [British Columbia Hog Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-158).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921674 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the British Columbia Hog
    Marketing Commission; ([office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any hog produced in British
    Columbia. ([porcin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921678 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Board is authorized to
regulate the marketing of hogs in interprovincial and export trade and
for those purposes may, with respect to persons and property situated in
British Columbia, exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable
by the Board under the Natural Products Marketing (BC) Act of British
Columbia in relation to the marketing of hogs locally within British
Columbia.

## [Levies or Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921680 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Board may, in relation
to the powers granted to it by section 3,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix and impose, by order, and collect levies or
  charges from persons referred to in that section who are engaged in
  the production or marketing of hogs and for those purposes may
  classify those persons into groups and fix, by order, the levies or
  charges payable by the members of the different groups in different
  amounts; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the levies or charges for the purposes of the
  Board, including the creation of reserves, the payment of expenses and
  losses resulting from the sale or disposal of hogs and the
  equalization or adjustment among producers of hogs of moneys realized
  from the sale thereof during such period or periods of time as the
  Board may determine.
