## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-857346 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Marine Liability
Act](/eng/acts/M-0.7).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-857349 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the carriage by water, under a contract of
    carriage, of passengers from one place in Canada to the same or
    another place in Canada, either directly or by way of a place
    outside Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the carriage by water, otherwise than under a
    contract of carriage, of persons from one place in Canada to the
    same or another place in Canada, either directly or by way of a
    place outside Canada, excluding

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the master of a ship, a member of a ship's crew
      or any other person employed or engaged in any capacity on board a
      ship on the business of the ship,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a person carried on board a ship other than a
      ship operated for a commercial or public purpose,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a person carried on board a ship in pursuance
      of the obligation on the master to carry shipwrecked, distressed
      or other persons or by reason of any circumstances that neither
      the master nor the owner could have prevented, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a stowaway, a trespasser or any other person who
      boards a ship without the consent or knowledge of the master or
      the owner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an adventure tourism activity that meets the
    conditions set out in subsection 37.1(1) of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the carriage of a sail trainee or a person who is a
    member of a class of persons prescribed under paragraph 39(d) of the
    Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} search and rescue operations that are carried out
    by the Canadian Coast Guard Auxiliary;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a carriage performed by the Government of Canada or
    the government of a province, or by an entity that is entitled to
    indemnification by that government for liability under Part 4 of the
    Act; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a carriage by a [[pleasure
    craft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} as defined in section 2 of the [Canada
    Shipping Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15).

## [Liability Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-857366 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coverage for damages for death or
personal injury

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who performs the whole or part of a carriage by ship must maintain
  liability insurance coverage for damages for death or personal injury
  caused by an incident that occurs in the course of the carriage and is
  due to the fault or neglect of the person or of the person's servants
  or agents or mandataries acting within the scope of their employment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum amount

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The coverage must be for an amount that is not less
  than \$250,000 multiplied by the passenger capacity of the ship.

## [Certificate of Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-857372 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to carry

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  person referred to in subsection 3(1) must ensure that proof of
  insurance, signed by the insurer or its authorized representative, is
  carried on board the ship as proof that the insurer has issued a
  liability insurance policy to the person for the liability insurance
  coverage required by section 3.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of proof of insurance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The proof of insurance referred to in subsection (1)
  may be in paper or electronic form and must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the ship is covered by a protection and
    indemnity club that is a member of the International Group of
    Protection and Indemnity Associations, the certificate of entry
    issued by the club in respect of the ship; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, a certificate of insurance in
    the form set out in the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fleets

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the liability insurance policy provides coverage
  in respect of a fleet of ships to which paragraph (2)(b) applies, the
  person referred to in subsection 3(1) must ensure that a certificate
  of insurance in the form set out in the schedule is carried on board
  one ship and a copy of it is carried on board every other ship in the
  fleet.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a person is not required to
  carry proof of insurance on board a ship if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is not feasible to do so because the ship does
    not have any space where the proof of insurance can be protected
    from the elements; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of proof of insurance kept on an
    electronic device, it is not possible to display the certificate due
    to technical reasons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Production within 24 hours

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a designated officer boards the ship under
  subsection 128(1) of the Act and the proof of insurance is not carried
  on board the ship due to a reason set out in subsection (4), the
  person referred to in subsection 3(1) must produce the proof of
  insurance to the designated officer within 24 hours after the
  designated officer boarded the ship.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fleets --- amount of insurance

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a liability insurance policy that
  provides coverage in respect of a fleet of ships to which paragraph
  (2)(b) applies, the certificate of insurance must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount of insurance applicable to each ship
      in the fleet, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of insurance applicable to the ship
      in the fleet with the highest passenger capacity and the number of
      ships in the fleet to which that amount applies; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} state that the policy provides the same coverage as
    the coverage that would be provided if a separate policy had been
    issued in respect of each ship listed on the certificate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions to form of certificate

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a liability insurance policy that
  provides coverage in respect of a fleet of ships to which paragraph
  (2)(b) applies,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a ship is exempted under the regulations made
    under the [Canada Shipping Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) from the
    registration requirement set out in subsection 46(1) of that Act,
    the name of the ship and its official number are not required to be
    set out on the certificate of insurance; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if proof of insurance is in paper form and the
    fleet has more ships than can be listed on the front of the
    certificate of insurance, the information regarding the ships that
    cannot be listed on the front of the certificate must be set out on
    the back.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Sections 3 and 4 do not
apply until

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person referred to in subsection
  3(1) who, at the end of the day before the day on which these
  Regulations come into force, holds a liability insurance policy that
  provides coverage for death or personal injury caused by an incident
  that occurs in the course of a carriage by ship, the moment at which
  the first of the following events occurs:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the policy expires,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the cancellation of the policy takes effect, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the modification of the policy takes effect; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the 60th day after the day on
  which these Regulations come into force.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-857405 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}30th day after publication

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the 30th day after the day on which they are published in the
[Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part II.

Pursuant to the [Regulations Respecting Compulsory Insurance for Ships
Carrying Passengers](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-245), made under Part 4
of the [Marine Liability Act](/eng/acts/M-0.7), and subject to the terms
and conditions of the above-mentioned liability insurance policy, this
is to certify that the above-named insured is the holder of a liability
insurance policy, that is in force, for the carriage of persons on board
the ships listed in this certificate.

If the liability insurance policy provides coverage in respect of a
fleet of ships, the policy provides the same coverage as the coverage
that would be provided if a separate policy had been issued in respect
of each ship listed on this certificate.
