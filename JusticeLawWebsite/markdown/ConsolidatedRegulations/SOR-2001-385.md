## [Exemption from Restrictions on Investments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673677 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed circumstances --- life
companies and insurance holding companies

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraph 495(3)(d)(ii) and 971(3)(d)(ii) of the [Insurance Companies
Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), subsections 495(6) to (8) of the Act do not
apply in determining whether a company would be permitted to acquire a
substantial investment in an entity under subsection 493(2), paragraph
493(3)(b) or (c) or subsection 493(4) or 495(1) or (2) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed circumstances --- property and
casualty companies and marine companies

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subparagraph 495(5)(d)(ii) of the [Insurance Companies
Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), subsections 495(6) to (8) of the Act do not
apply in determining whether a company would be permitted to acquire a
substantial investment in an entity under subsection 493(2), paragraph
493(3)(b) or (c) or subsection 495(1) or (4) of the Act.

- SOR/2009-296, s. 29

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed circumstances --- societies

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subparagraph 554(3)(d)(ii) of the [Insurance Companies
Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), subsections 554(4) and (5) of the Act do not
apply in determining whether a society would be permitted to acquire a
substantial investment in an entity under subsection 552(2), paragraph
552(3)(b) or (c) or subsection 554(1) or (2) of the Act.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673686 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2001-385_e_hq_6012){#fn_SOR-2001-385_e_hq_6012-ID0EBCA}[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
paragraphs 501(a) and (b) and 554(9)(a) and (b) of the [Insurance
Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), as enacted by sections 426 and 437 of
the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1), chapter
9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, come into force.
