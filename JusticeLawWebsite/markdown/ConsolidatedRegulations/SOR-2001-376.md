## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673444 .Part}

Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673447 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from associated status

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the
application of any provision of the Act that would otherwise apply to an
entity associated with a foreign bank, the following classes of entities
associated with a foreign bank are exempted from the status of being
associated with a foreign bank:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} entities each of which is the government of a foreign
  country, or of any political subdivision of a foreign country, that
  does not in Canada carry on a business that includes any of the
  activities referred to in paragraphs (a) to (g) of the definition
  "financial services entity" in subsection 507(1) of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} entities each of which is controlled by the
  government of a foreign country, or of any political subdivision of a
  foreign country, and that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is not a foreign bank,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is not controlled by a foreign bank,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} does not engage in Canada in any of the
    activities referred to in paragraphs (a) to (h) of the definition
    "financial services entity" in subsection 507(1) of the Act, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} has not been deemed under subsection 507(3) of the
    Act to be associated with a foreign bank;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} entities each of which is an entity that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is associated with a foreign bank only because the
    entity controls the foreign bank, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is an entity described in paragraphs 468(1)(a)
    to (f) of the Act;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} entities each of which is an entity that is
  controlled by an entity exempted under paragraph (c) and that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is not a foreign bank described in paragraph
    508(1)(a) of the Act, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is not controlled by a foreign bank described in
    paragraph 508(1)(a) of the Act;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} entities each of which is a Canadian entity that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is not described in paragraph (c) or (d),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is an entity associated with a foreign bank only
    because it controls the foreign bank, except where the foreign bank
    is described in paragraph 508(1)(a) of the Act and either the
    foreign bank or an entity controlled by it

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} engages in or carries on business in Canada,
      otherwise than by holding, managing or otherwise dealing with real
      property,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} maintains a branch in Canada, other than an
      office referred to in section 522 of the Act,

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} establishes, maintains or acquires for use in
      Canada an automated banking machine, a remote service unit or a
      similar automated service, or, in Canada, accepts data from such a
      machine, unit or service other than in circumstances described in
      section 511 or 512 of the Act,

    - [(D)]{.lawlabel} acquires or holds control of, or a substantial
      investment in, a Canadian entity, or

    - [(E)]{.lawlabel} acquires or holds any share or ownership interest
      in a Canadian entity that

      - [(I)]{.lawlabel} an entity associated with the foreign bank
        holds control of, or a substantial investment in, or

      - [(II)]{.lawlabel} the foreign bank, an entity associated with
        the foreign bank and one or more other entities associated with
        the foreign bank would, if they were one person, hold control
        of, or a substantial investment in, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has not been deemed under subsection 507(3) of
    the Act to be associated with a foreign bank; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} entities each of which is an entity that is
  controlled by a Canadian entity exempted under paragraph (e) and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is not a foreign bank described in paragraph
    508(1)(a) of the Act, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is not controlled by a foreign bank described in
    paragraph 508(1)(a) of the Act.

## [Ministerial Deeming]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673478 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister authorized to make order

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, by order,
and subject to any terms and conditions that the Minister considers
appropriate, deem, for the purposes of any provision of the Act, any
entity not to be an entity associated with a foreign bank.

subsection 507(17) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), as enacted by
section 132 of the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1), chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, comes
into force.
