## Tax Rebate Discounting Regulations {#tax-rebate-discounting-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-108
:::

[TAX REBATE DISCOUNTING ACT](/eng/acts/T-3)

Registration 1986-01-10

Regulations Concerning the Statement Describing the Discounting
Transaction and the Notice of the Actual Amount of the Refund of Tax
Received by the Discounter

P.C. 1986-103 1986-01-10
::::

:::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs, pursuant to section
10[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-86-108_e_hq_3778){#fn_SOR-86-108_e_hq_3778-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Tax Rebate Discounting Act](/eng/acts/T-3), is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations concerning the statement describing the
discounting transaction and the notice of the actual amount of the
refund of tax received by the discounter](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-108).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-890102 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Tax Rebate Discounting
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-108).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-890104 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The statement describing
the discounting transaction that is required by subparagraph 4(1)(b)(i)
of the [Tax Rebate Discounting Act](/eng/acts/T-3) shall be as
prescribed in Schedule I.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-509, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The notice of the actual
amount of the refund of tax received by the discounter that is required
by paragraph 5(b) of the [Tax Rebate Discounting Act](/eng/acts/T-3)
shall be as prescribed in Schedule II.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-509, s. 1
:::
