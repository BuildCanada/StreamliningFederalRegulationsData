## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726911 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Insurance Companies
Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8).

## [Record Date]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726913 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraphs 142(1)(a), (b) and (e) and subparagraph
  142(1)(c)(iii) of the Act, the prescribed period for the record date
  is not more than 60 days before the day on which the particular action
  is to be taken.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 766(1) of the Act,
  except paragraphs 766(1)(c) and 766(1)(d), the prescribed period for
  the record date is not more than 60 days before the day on which the
  particular action to be taken.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subparagraphs 142(1)(c)(i)
  and (ii) and paragraphs 766(1)(c) and (d) of the Act, the prescribed
  period for the record date is not less than 21 days and not more than
  60 days before the day of the meeting.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subparagraph 142(1)(d)(i) of the
  Act, the prescribed period for the record date is not less than 21
  days and not more than 90 days before the day of the meeting.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subparagraph 142(1)(d)(ii) of the
  Act, the prescribed period for the record date is not less than 10
  days before the day of the meeting.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections 142(4) and 766(3) of
  the Act, the prescribed period for the notice of the record date to be
  given is not less than seven days before the date fixed.

## [Notice of Meeting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726921 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 143(1) and 767(1) of the Act, the prescribed period for the
notice to be sent is not less than 21 days and not more than 60 days
before the day of the meeting.

## [Shareholder and Policyholder Proposals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726923 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsections 147(1.1) and 770(1.1) of the Act, the
  prescribed number of the company's or the insurance holding company's
  outstanding shares is the number of voting shares

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is equal to 1% of the total number of the
    company's or the insurance holding company's outstanding voting
    shares as of the day on which the proposal is submitted, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whose fair market value, as determined at the close
    of business on the day before the day on which the proposal is
    submitted, is at least \$2,000.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of 147(1.1) and 770(1.1) of the Act,
  the prescribed period for the person to have been the registered
  holder or beneficial owner is the six-month period immediately before
  the day on which the proposal is submitted.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 147(1.4) and 770(1.4) of the Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company or the insurance holding company may
  request, within 14 days after it receives the person's proposal, that
  the person provide the proof that they meet the requirements; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person shall provide the proof that they meet the
  requirements within 21 days after receiving the company's or the
  insurance holding company's request.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 147(3) and 770(3) of the Act, the proposal and the statement
in support of it together are not to exceed 500 words.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraphs 147(5)(a) and 770(5)(a) of the Act, the prescribed number of
days is 90 days.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraphs 147(5)(c) and 770(5)(c) of the Act, the prescribed period is
two years.

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraphs 147(5)(d) and 770(5)(d) of the Act, the
  prescribed minimum amount of support for a proposal is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 3% of the total number of shares voted and
    policyholder votes cast if the proposal was introduced at an annual
    meeting;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 6% of the total number of shares voted and
    policyholder votes cast at its last submission, if the proposal was
    introduced at two annual meetings; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 10% of the total number of shares voted and
    policyholder votes cast at its last submission, if the proposal was
    introduced at three or more annual meetings.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraphs 147(5)(d) and
  770(5)(d) of the Act, the prescribed period is five years.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 147(5.1) and 770(5.1) of the Act, the prescribed period
within which the company or the insurance holding company is not
required to attach any proposal to a notice of meeting is two years.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 148(1) and 771(1) of the Act, the prescribed period for the
person to be notified is 21 days after the day on which the company or
the insurance holding company receives the proposal or the proof under
subsection 147(1.4) or 770(1.4) of the Act if it has requested that
proof.

## [Electronic Voting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726945 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 157(5) and 778(5) of the Act, when a vote is held at a
meeting, the voting may be carried out by means of a telephonic,
electronic or other communication facility if the facility

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} enables the votes to be gathered in a manner that
  permits their subsequent verification; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} permits the tallied votes to be presented to the
  company or the insurance holding company without it being possible for
  the company or the insurance holding company to identify how each
  shareholder or policyholder or group of shareholders or group of
  policyholders voted.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726949 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
