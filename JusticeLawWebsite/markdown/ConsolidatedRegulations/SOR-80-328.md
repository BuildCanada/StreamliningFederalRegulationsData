## Ontario Soya-Bean Marketing Levies Order {#ontario-soya-bean-marketing-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/80-328
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1980-05-09

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Levies from
Certain Soya-Bean Producers in Respect of the Marketing in
Interprovincial and Export Trade of Soya-Beans Produced In Ontario
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Ontario Soya-Bean Growers' Marketing Board, pursuant to section 4 of
the [Ontario Soya-Bean Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-183)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EDBBA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed Order providing for the fixing, imposing and
collecting of levies from certain soya-bean producers in respect of the
marketing in interprovincial and export trade of soya-beans produced in
Ontario.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e-ID0EDBBA){#footnote_e}SOR/80-183,
  1980 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 645
  :::

Dated at Chatham, Ontario, this 3rd day of May, 1980
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869183 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Soya-Bean Marketing Levies
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-328).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869185 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Ontario Soya-Bean
    Growers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    soya-beans established and amended from time to time pursuant to the
    Act and any regulations made pursuant to the Act to give effect to
    the plan; ([plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production of soya-beans in Ontario; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[soya-beans]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[soya-beans]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means soya-beans produced in
    Ontario. ([soja]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869192 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies only to
the marketing of soya-beans in interprovincial and export trade and to
persons and property situated within the Province of Ontario.

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869194 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay to
the Commodity Board, in addition to the licence fees set out under the
Plan, levies at the rate of one dollar per tonne of soya-beans sold by
the producer or on behalf of that producer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-81, s. 1
:::

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869198 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay to
the Commodity Board at its office, 175 Keil Drive South, Chatham,
Ontario, all levies payable by him pursuant to section 4 not later than
the 15th day of the month following the month in which the soya-beans in
respect of which levies are payable were sold.
