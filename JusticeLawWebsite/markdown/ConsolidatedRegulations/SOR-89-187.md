## Order Authorizing the Minister of Finance to Exercise the Powers of the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans Referred to in the Act in Respect of National Sea Products Limited {#order-authorizing-the-minister-of-finance-to-exercise-the-powers-of-the-minister-of-fisheries-and-oceans-referred-to-in-the-act-in-respect-of-national-sea-products-limited .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-187
:::

[ATLANTIC FISHERIES RESTRUCTURING ACT](/eng/acts/A-14)

Registration 1989-03-23

Order Authorizing the Minister of Finance to Exercise the Powers of the
Minister of Fisheries and Oceans Referred to in the Act in Respect of
National Sea Products Limited
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 7 of the [Atlantic Fisheries
Restructuring Act](/eng/acts/A-14), is pleased hereby:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1988-734 of April 21,
  1988; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to authorize the Minister of Finance to exercise the
  powers of the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans referred to in that Act
  in respect of National Sea Products Limited.
