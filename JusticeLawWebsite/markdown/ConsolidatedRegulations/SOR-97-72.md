## Free Trade Agreement Advance Rulings Regulations {#free-trade-agreement-advance-rulings-regulations .Title-of-Act}

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1012422 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[advance ruling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[advance ruling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an advance ruling given
    under paragraph 43.1(1)(a) or (b) of the Act. ([décision
    anticipée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a good that is used in the
    production of another good, and includes a part or an ingredient.
    ([matière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-256, s. 17
:::

## [Classes of Persons Eligible to Apply for an Advance Ruling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1012429 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** An application for an
advance ruling in respect of goods proposed to be imported from a free
trade partner may be made by any member of the following classes of
persons:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} importers of those goods in Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} persons who are authorized to account for those goods
  under paragraph 32(6)(a) or subsection 32(7) of the Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} exporters and producers of those goods in a free
  trade partner other than Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} If the goods are produced in a NAFTA country other
  than Canada or in Chile, Costa Rica, Peru, Colombia, Jordan, Panama or
  in Honduras, producers in a NAFTA country other than Canada or in
  Chile, Costa Rica, Peru, Colombia, Jordan, Panama or in Honduras of a
  material that is used in the production of those goods; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} where the goods are produced in Israel or another
  CIFTA beneficiary, producers in Israel or another CIFTA beneficiary or
  in the United States of a material that is used in the production of
  those goods.

## [Application for an Advance Ruling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1012439 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application for an
advance ruling in respect of goods shall be made not less than 120 days
before the date of importation of the goods.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An application for an
advance ruling shall be made in English or French.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where, during the course of
an evaluation of an application for an advance ruling, an officer
determines that the information contained in the application is
insufficient to make a ruling, the officer may request supplemental
information from the applicant and shall specify a period of not less
than 30 days within which the information shall be provided.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/97-331, s. 5(E)
:::

## [Advance Ruling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1012445 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1012446 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An officer shall give
consistent advance rulings with respect to applications for advance
rulings that are based on facts and circumstances that are identical in
all material respects.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An officer shall provide
the applicant with a full explanation of the reasons for an advance
ruling.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-124, s. 2
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An advance ruling shall be
made in writing, in the same language as the language of the application
for the advance ruling, and given to the applicant.

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1012452 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** An advance ruling in
respect of goods shall be effective

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the date on which it is given; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on a later date specified in the advance ruling,
  which is not later than the date of importation of the goods.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An advance ruling shall
apply to goods that are the subject of the advance ruling and are
imported on or after the effective date of the advance ruling.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** An advance ruling shall
remain in effect and shall be honoured by the Minister if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is no change in the material facts or
  circumstances on which it is based;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all of its terms and conditions are complied with;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} it has not been revoked.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-108, s. 3(F)
:::

### [Declining and Postponing the Issuance of an Advance Ruling]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1012463 .Subheading}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/97-331, s. 6

\]
:::

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  officer may postpone the issuance of an advance ruling in respect of
  goods where goods other than those that are the subject of the advance
  ruling are the subject of one of the following processes the result of
  which is likely to affect the advance ruling:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a verification of origin under section 42.1 of the
    Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a re-determination or further re-determination of
    origin under section 59, 60 or 61 of the Act; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a hearing before the Canadian International Trade
    Tribunal or any court.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An officer may decline or postpone the issuance of an
  advance ruling where the applicant fails to provide supplemental
  information requested under section 6 within the period specified.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/97-331, s. 7
- SOR/98-53, s. 11
- SOR/2001-108, s. 4
:::

## [Modification or Revocation of an Advance Ruling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1012474 .Part}

### [Grounds for Modification or Revocation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1012475 .Subheading}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/97-331, s. 8

\]
:::

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** An officer may modify or
revoke an advance ruling given in respect of goods that are the subject
of the advance ruling

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the advance ruling is based on an error

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} of fact,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the tariff classification of the goods or of
    the materials used in the production of the goods,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods exported from a NAFTA
    country, Chile, Costa Rica or Honduras in the application of a
    regional value content requirement under Chapter Four of NAFTA,
    Chapter D of CCFTA, Chapter IV of CCRFTA or Chapter Four of CHFTA,
    as the case may be,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods exported from a NAFTA
    country, in the application of the rules for determining whether
    goods qualify as goods of a NAFTA country under Annex 300-B or 302.2
    or Chapter Seven of NAFTA,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods exported from a NAFTA country,
    in the application of the rules for determining whether the goods
    are qualifying goods under Chapter Seven of NAFTA, or

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods exported from a NAFTA
    country, Chile, Costa Rica, Peru, Colombia, Jordan, Panama or
    Honduras in the application of the rules for determining whether the
    goods that re-enter Canada after being exported from Canada to
    another NAFTA country, Chile, Costa Rica, Peru, Colombia, Jordan,
    Panama or Honduras for repair or alteration qualify for treatment
    that is free of customs duty under Article 307 of NAFTA, Article
    C-06 of CCFTA, Article III.6 of CCRFTA, Article 205 of CPFTA,
    Article 205 of CCOFTA, Article 2-4 of CJFTA, Article 2.07 of CPAFTA
    or Article 3.8 of CHFTA, as the case may be;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods exported from a NAFTA country,
  Chile, Costa Rica, an EFTA state, Peru, Colombia, Jordan, Panama or
  Honduras, if the advance ruling is not in accordance with an
  interpretation agreed to by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the NAFTA countries regarding Chapter Three or Four
    of NAFTA,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Canada and Chile regarding Chapter C or D of
    CCFTA,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} Canada and Costa Rica regarding Chapter III or IV
    of CCRFTA,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} Canada and the EFTA states regarding Annex C to
    CEFTA,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} Canada and Peru regarding Chapter Two or Three of
    CPFTA,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} Canada and Colombia regarding Chapter Two or Three
    of CCOFTA,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} Canada and Jordan regarding Chapter 2 or 4 of
    CJFTA,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} Canada and Panama regarding Chapter Two or Three
    of CPAFTA, or

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} Canada and Honduras regarding Chapter Three or
    Four of CHFTA;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if there is a change in the material facts or
  circumstances on which the advance ruling is based;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if preferential tariff treatment under a free trade
  agreement has been denied or withdrawn from the goods under
  subsection 42.1(2) or (3) of the Act;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods exported from a NAFTA country,
  Chile, Costa Rica or Honduras, if the exporter or producer of the
  goods has not complied with the terms and conditions of the advance
  ruling with regard to the regional value content of the goods or any
  other terms or conditions of the advance ruling;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods exported from a NAFTA country,
  Chile, Costa Rica or Honduras, if the operations of the exporter or
  producer of the goods are not consistent with the material facts and
  circumstances on which the advance ruling is based with regard to the
  regional value content of the goods;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods exported from a NAFTA country,
  Chile, Costa Rica or Honduras, if for the purposes of the application
  for the advance ruling the supporting data and computations used in
  applying the basis or method for calculating value or allocating cost
  were incorrect in any material respect with regard to the regional
  value content of the goods;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} to conform with a modification of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Chapter Three, Four, Five or Seven of NAFTA,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Chapter C, D or E of CCFTA,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} Chapter Three of CIFTA,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} Chapters III, IV and V of CCRFTA,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} Chapter II or Annex C to CEFTA,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} Chapter Two, Three or Four of CPFTA,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} Chapter Two, Three or Four of CCOFTA,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} Chapters 2, 4 and 5 of CJFTA,

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} Chapter Two, Three or Four of CPAFTA, or

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} Chapter Three, Four or Five of CHFTA; or

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} to conform with a decision of a Canadian court or
  tribunal or a change in Canadian law.

### [Notice of Modification or Revocation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1012514 .Subheading}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** An officer shall give
notice in writing of any modification or revocation of an advance ruling
and of the effective date of the modification or revocation to the
person to whom the advance ruling was given.

### [Effective Date of Modification or Revocation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1012516 .Subheading}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A modification or
revocation of an advance ruling shall be effective

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the date on which the modification or revocation
  is issued; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on such later date as may be specified in the notice
  given under section 16.

### [Application of Modification or Revocation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1012520 .Subheading}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a
  modification or revocation of an advance ruling shall apply to goods
  that are the subject of the advance ruling and are imported on or
  after the effective date of the modification or revocation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A modification or revocation of an advance ruling
  shall apply to goods that are the subject of the advance ruling and
  are imported before the effective date of the modification or
  revocation where the modification or revocation is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the detriment of the person to whom the advance
    ruling was given and that person has not acted in accordance with
    the terms and conditions of the advance ruling; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the benefit of the person to whom the advance
    ruling was given.

### [Postponement of Effective Date]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1012526 .Subheading}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  officer shall postpone the effective date of a modification or
  revocation of an advance ruling for a period not exceeding 90 days
  where the person to whom the advance ruling was given demonstrates
  that the person has relied in good faith to the person\'s detriment on
  that advance ruling.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An officer shall give, in writing, to the person to
  whom the advance ruling was given, notice of any postponement made
  under subsection (1).
