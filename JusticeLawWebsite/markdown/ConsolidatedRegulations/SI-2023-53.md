## Order Transferring from the Department of Canadian Heritage to the Department of Employment and Social Development the Control and Supervision of the Anti-Racism Secretariat {#order-transferring-from-the-department-of-canadian-heritage-to-the-department-of-employment-and-social-development-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-anti-racism-secretariat .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2023-53
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2023-08-16

Order Transferring from the Department of Canadian Heritage to the
Department of Employment and Social Development the Control and
Supervision of the Anti-Racism Secretariat

P.C. 2023-783 2023-07-26
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2604_hq_1690307956){#fn_81000-3-2604_hq_1690307956-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2604_hq_1690307970){#fn_81000-3-2604_hq_1690307970-ID0EBCAA}
transfers from the Department of Canadian Heritage to the Department of
Employment and Social Development the control and supervision of the
portion of the federal public administration in the Department of
Canadian Heritage known as the Anti-Racism Secretariat.
