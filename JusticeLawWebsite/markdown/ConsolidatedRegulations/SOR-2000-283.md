## Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg and Chick Orderly Marketing Regulations {#canadian-broiler-hatching-egg-and-chick-orderly-marketing-regulations .Title-of-Act}

Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg and Chick Orderly Marketing Regulations

## [Marketing Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663056 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** No producer, dealer or
hatchery operator shall engage in the marketing of broiler hatching eggs
or chicks produced in a non-signatory province in interprovincial trade
into a signatory province unless that producer, dealer or hatchery
operator

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} holds an orderly marketing quota for that signatory
  province; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} markets a number of broiler hatching eggs or chicks
  that is not in excess of the orderly marketing quota referred to in
  paragraph (a).

<!-- -->

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Section
  2 does not apply to a producer, dealer or hatchery operator where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} broiler hatching eggs produced in a non-signatory
    province are marketed by the producer, dealer or hatchery operator
    in a non-signatory province into a signatory province;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a number of chicks, equivalent to 96% of the number
    of broiler hatching eggs referred to in paragraph (a), will be
    returned by the hatchery operator in the signatory province to the
    producer in the province in which the eggs were produced or to the
    dealer or hatchery operator from whom the eggs were received;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the producer, dealer or hatchery operator in a
    non-signatory province applies for, and receives from the Agency,
    authorization to market a designated quantity of broiler hatching
    eggs;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the producer, dealer or hatchery operator in a non-
    signatory province markets broiler hatching eggs only to the extent
    of the Agency authorization granted; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the broiler hatching eggs are marketed pursuant to
    a contract that is in the form provided by the Agency and that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} specifies the number of broiler hatching eggs to
      be marketed and the number of chicks to be returned to the
      province in which the eggs were produced, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} provides that the parties shall comply with any
      applicable Order, Regulation or requirement of the Agency.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application referred to in paragraph (1)(c) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be in the form provided by the Agency;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be completed and signed by the applicant;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} contain information relevant to the marketing of
    the broiler hatching eggs and chicks, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of eggs to be marketed and the number
      of chicks to be returned to the province in which the eggs were
      produced, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the price at which any such eggs shall be
      marketed;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by invoices, sales receipts or other
    documents evidencing the number of broiler hatching eggs marketed by
    the applicant in the year prior to the year for which the
    application is being made; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be filed with the Agency

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the year 2000, on or before September 1,
      2000, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for subsequent years, on or before October 1 of
      the year preceding the year for which the authorization is sought.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Agency shall grant an authorization if the
  applicant meets the requirements set out in this section.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} An authorization issued under this section expires on
  December 31 of the year for which it is issued.

## [Orderly Marketing Quotas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663081 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  producer, dealer or hatchery operator who, during the qualifying
  period, marketed into a signatory province broiler hatching eggs or
  chicks produced in a non-signatory province and who wishes to obtain
  an orderly marketing quota shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} apply to the Agency for a licence, pursuant to the
    [Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg and Chick Licensing
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-516), for the year to which the
    quota applies; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} apply to the Agency for a quota no later than
    September 30 of the year preceding the year in respect of which the
    quota applies.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for an orderly marketing quota
  referred to in subsection (1) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be in the form provided by the Agency;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be completed and signed by the applicant; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application by an applicant to
    whom an orderly marketing quota has not previously been allotted, be
    accompanied by invoices, sales receipts or other evidence of the
    number of broiler hatching eggs or chicks produced in a
    non-signatory province and marketed by the applicant in
    interprovincial trade into the signatory province during the
    qualifying period.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An applicant referred to in paragraph (2)(c) shall
  make the applicant's books and records available for examination in
  order that the Agency may verify the accuracy of the information given
  pursuant to that paragraph.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agency shall allot an orderly marketing quota to any applicant who
  meets the requirements of section 4 and is a holder of a licence under
  the [Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg and Chick Licensing
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-516) for the year in respect of
  which the orderly marketing quota applies.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The orderly marketing quota in respect of a signatory
  province allotted to an applicant under subsection (1) for a year
  shall be equal to the number of broiler hatching eggs and chicks,
  adjusted to take into account the principle of comparative advantage
  of production, determined by the formula

  ::: {#663094 .Subsection}
  X x Y

  where

  [X]{.dfn}
  :   represents the percentage change, from the year the signatory
      province was first referred to in the definition [[signatory
      provinces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 1 of the schedule to
      the [Canadian Hatching Egg Producers
      Proclamation](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-40) to the year in respect
      of which the orderly marketing quota is to apply, in the amount
      that is the total number of broiler hatching eggs and chicks
      subject to limits for that signatory province as set out in the
      schedule to the [Canadian Hatching Egg Producers Quota
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-209), and

  [Y]{.dfn}
  :   represents the number of broiler hatching eggs and chicks that
      were marketed during the qualifying period by the applicant into
      that signatory province.
  :::

- SOR/2008-9, s. 2

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  orderly marketing quota shall be expressed in a number of broiler
  hatching eggs.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of marketing chicks, the number of
  broiler hatching eggs in the orderly marketing quota shall be
  converted into the number of chicks by multiplying the number of
  broiler hatching eggs in the quota by the hatchability for the
  province of production during the year preceding the year in which the
  chicks are marketed.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An orderly marketing quota
expires on December 31 of the year for which it is allotted.

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  producer, dealer or hatchery operator shall sell, give as security,
  lease or assign an orderly marketing quota.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a producer, dealer or hatchery operator ceases
  to carry on business, the Agency may, on application, re-allot any
  orderly marketing quota held by that producer, dealer or hatchery
  operator on such terms and conditions as are necessary for the orderly
  marketing of broiler hatching eggs or chicks.

## [Reduction of Orderly Marketing Quotas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663107 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Where a producer, dealer or
hatchery operator markets broiler hatching eggs or chicks produced in a
non-signatory province in interprovincial trade into a signatory
province in excess of that producer's, dealer's or hatchery operator's
orderly marketing quota for that signatory province for a year, the
Agency shall reduce, in such manner and to such extent as are necessary
for the orderly marketing of broiler hatching eggs and chicks, that
producer's, dealer's or hatchery operator's orderly marketing quota for
that signatory province for the following year.

## [Suspension and Cancellation of Orderly Marketing Quotas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663109 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** If a licence issued by
the Agency to a producer, dealer or hatchery operator pursuant to the
[Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg and Chick Licensing
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-516) is suspended or cancelled, the
orderly marketing quota for that producer, dealer or hatchery operator
shall be suspended or cancelled, as the case may be.

## [New Entrants]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663111 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Agency shall, on
application, on such terms and conditions as are necessary for the
orderly marketing of broiler hatching eggs and chicks, allot orderly
marketing quotas for a signatory province to persons in a non-signatory
province who did not market broiler hatching eggs or chicks produced in
the non-signatory province in interprovincial trade into the signatory
province during the qualifying period, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the market for broiler hatching eggs or chicks in the
  signatory province has increased; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Agency has assigned a number of broiler hatching
  eggs or chicks for the allotment of orderly marketing quotas.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663115 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
