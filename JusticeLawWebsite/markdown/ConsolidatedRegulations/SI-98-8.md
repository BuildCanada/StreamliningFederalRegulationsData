## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-653165 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of all the taxes paid or payable under Division III of Part IX and under
any other Part of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), and of the
customs duties paid or payable under section 21 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), on goods donated by a non-resident of
Canada to religious, charitable or educational institutions in Canada.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** \[Repeal\]

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-653169 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on January 1, 1998.
