## [Total Assets]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676594 .Part}

Definition of [[total assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the
definition [[total assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of
the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), as it applies in
sections 59.1, 412 and 939 of that Act, [[total
assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a company or an insurance
holding company, at a particular time, means the total value of the
assets that would be reported on its balance sheet, prepared as at that
time in accordance with the accounting principles and specifications of
the Superintendent referred to in subsection 331(4) or 887(4), as the
case may be, of that Act.
