## Sulphur in Gasoline Regulations {#sulphur-in-gasoline-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/99-236
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 1999-06-04

Sulphur in Gasoline Regulations

P.C. 1999-1023 1999-06-04
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 48(1) of the Canadian Environmental
Protection Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFACA}, the Minister
of the Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on October 31, 1998, a copy
of the proposed [Sulphur in Gasoline
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-236), substantially in the form set
out in the annexed regulations, and persons were given an opportunity to
file a notice of objection requesting that a board of review be
established and stating the reasons for the objection;

And whereas, in the opinion of the Governor in Council, the
concentration of sulphur in gasoline prescribed in the annexed
regulations, if exceeded, would, on the combustion of the gasoline in
ordinary circumstances, result in a significant contribution to air
pollution;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to sections
47[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EGCAA}
and 87 of the Canadian Environmental Protection Act^a^, hereby makes the
annexed [Sulphur in Gasoline Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-236).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1029229 .Part}

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do
not apply to gasoline if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the gasoline is in transit through Canada, from a
  place outside Canada to another place outside Canada, and is
  accompanied by written evidence establishing that the gasoline is in
  transit;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the gasoline is produced or sold for export and is
  accompanied by written evidence establishing that the gasoline will be
  exported; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2015-187, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the gasoline is being imported in a fuel tank that
  supplies the engine of a conveyance that is used for transportation by
  water, land or air.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Requirements Pertaining to Sulphur in Gasoline]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1029237 .Part}

### [Prescribed Levels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1029238 .Subheading}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of section 139 of the Act, the concentration of sulphur in
  each batch of gasoline produced or imported by a primary supplier
  shall not exceed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the primary supplier has elected
    under section 9 to calculate the concentration of sulphur in that
    gasoline on the basis of a pool average, 80 mg/kg; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, 12 mg/kg.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of section 139 of the Act and subject
  to subsection (2.1), the pool average of a pool in respect of which a
  primary supplier has made an election under section 9 shall not exceed
  10 mg/kg.

- [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection 139 of the Act, if a
  primary supplier uses sulphur compliance units to adjust a pool
  average in accordance with section 15, the adjusted pool average shall
  not exceed 10 mg/kg.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of section 139 of the Act, the
  concentration of sulphur in gasoline that is sold shall not exceed 80
  mg/kg.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) to (3) do not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} gasoline for use in aircraft, if the gasoline has
    an octane number of at least 99.5 or contains at least 5 mg/L of
    lead;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} gasoline for use in competition vehicles, if the
    gasoline has an antiknock index of at least 100;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} gasoline for use in scientific research in Canada;
    or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2000-104, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} gasoline-like blendstock.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2015-187, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

### [Methods for Sampling and Analysis]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1029260 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of these Regulations, when a primary supplier takes samples
  they shall use one of the following methods:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the primary supplier is using an
    alternative sampling method in accordance with subsection 6(1) of
    the [Benzene in Gasoline Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-493),
    that sampling method;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the sampling method described in
    the National Standard of Canada CAN/CGSB-3.5-2016, entitled
    Automotive Gasoline.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of sulphur in gasoline and the
    concentration of sulphur in an oxygenate shall be measured in
    accordance with the ASTM International test method D5453-19a,
    entitled Standard Test Method for Determination of Total Sulfur in
    Light Hydrocarbons, Spark Ignition Engine Fuel, Diesel Engine Fuel,
    and Engine Oil by Ultraviolet Fluorescence; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of sulphur in butane shall be
    measured in accordance with the ASTM International test method
    D6667-14(2019), entitled Standard Test Method for Determination of
    Total Volatile Sulfur in Gaseous Hydrocarbons and Liquefied
    Petroleum Gases by Ultraviolet Fluorescence.

- [(3) to (5)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2003-319, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of sections 12, 24 and 25, if a
  primary supplier is using an alternative method in accordance with
  subsection 6(2) of the [Benzene in Gasoline
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-493), they may use that method to
  analyze samples of gasoline.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (6), compliance with a requirement
  set out in section 2 shall be determined in accordance with the test
  methods set out in subsection (2).

### [Records of Type of Gasoline]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1029274 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  primary supplier may, before importing a batch of gasoline or
  dispatching one from a refinery or blending facility, identify the
  gasoline by recording it as one of the following types:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} low-sulphur gasoline;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} gasoline for use in aircraft;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} gasoline for use in competition vehicles;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} gasoline for use in scientific research;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} gasoline for export;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} gasoline in transit through Canada; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2015-187, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} gasoline-like blendstock.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any batch of gasoline dispatched by a primary
  supplier from a refinery or blending facility, or imported by a
  primary supplier, that has not been identified and recorded under
  subsection (1) is considered, for the purposes of these Regulations,
  to have been identified as low-sulphur gasoline.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A primary supplier shall, for each batch of gasoline
  that is identified as a type referred to in any of paragraphs (1)(b)
  to (f), keep a record of its type and the use for which it was sold or
  delivered.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Requirements Pertaining to a Pool Average]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1029313 .Part}

### [Pool Average Election]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1029314 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  primary supplier may, in respect of a pool, elect to calculate the
  concentration of sulphur in gasoline that is in the pool on the basis
  of a pool average by submitting to the Minister a notice of the
  election within the period specified in subsection (2.1) or (2.2).

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} A pool shall be composed of one of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} gasoline that is produced at a particular refinery;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} gasoline that is produced at a particular blending
    facility;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} gasoline that is imported.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notice of an election under subsection (1) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} specify the pool to which the election applies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} describe the techniques that the primary supplier
    will use to construct and evidence the pool average, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the location at its facilities where, the manner
      in which, and the frequency with which, the primary supplier will
      collect samples,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the location at which the primary supplier will
      determine the sulphur concentration of batches,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the method by which the primary supplier will
      determine the volume of batches,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the method by which the primary supplier will
      make and maintain the records,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the location in Canada where the primary supplier
      will keep the samples and records, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} how subparagraphs (i) to (v) will be applied to
      any batch referred to in any of subsections 10(3) to (5); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the primary supplier intends to adjust the
    recorded concentration of sulphur of a batch of gasoline in
    accordance with subsection 10(6), describe the techniques that the
    primary supplier will use to construct and evidence that adjustment
    and the sulphur concentration and volume of the sulphur-limited
    oxygenate or sulphur-limited butane added to the batch, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name and civic address of each facility at
      which the sulphur-limited oxygenate or sulphur-limited butane will
      be added,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the method by which, and the location at which,
      the primary supplier will determine the sulphur concentration of
      the sulphur-limited oxygenate or sulphur-limited butane,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the method by which the primary supplier will
      determine the volume of the sulphur-limited oxygenate or
      sulphur-limited butane,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the method by which the primary supplier will
      adjust the sulphur concentration of each batch,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the method by which the primary supplier will
      keep the record required under paragraph 12(d), and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the location in Canada where the primary
      supplier will keep the records.

- [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} The notice shall be submitted to the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the primary supplier is, for the
    first time, producing or importing gasoline, no later than 60 days

### [Calculation of Pool Average]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1029345 .Subheading}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) to (6), a
  primary supplier shall, for each pool in respect of which they make an
  election under section 9, calculate the pool average of all gasoline
  in the pool that was identified as low-sulphur gasoline under section
  5.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In calculating a pool average, a primary supplier
  shall exclude all batches of low-sulphur gasoline that were exported
  by the primary supplier or an affiliate of the primary supplier.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a primary supplier imports and delivers a batch of
  low-sulphur gasoline to a refinery or a blending facility, the primary
  supplier may include that batch in the pool average for the refinery
  or blending facility if the batch is excluded from the pool average
  for gasoline that is imported.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a primary supplier dispatches from a refinery and
  delivers, or imports and delivers, gasoline-like blendstock to a
  blending facility where it is blended to produce a batch of
  low-sulphur gasoline, the primary supplier may include the volume of
  that gasoline-like blendstock and its concentration of sulphur in the
  pool average for the refinery or for gasoline that is imported, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the primary supplier owns the gasoline-like
    blendstock;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the gasoline-like blendstock is, prior to being
    blended, segregated from all other batches of gasoline stored at the
    blending facility; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the batch of low-sulphur gasoline is excluded from
    the pool average for that blending facility.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a primary supplier dispatches from a refinery and
  delivers, or imports and delivers, gasoline-like blendstock to a
  blending facility where it is blended with low-sulphur gasoline to
  produce a batch of low-sulphur gasoline, the primary supplier shall
  include the volume of that gasoline-like blendstock and its
  concentration of sulphur in the pool average for the refinery or for
  gasoline that is imported.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a primary supplier dispatches from a refinery and
  delivers, or imports and delivers, a batch of low-sulphur gasoline to
  a facility where sulphur-limited oxygenate or sulphur-limited butane
  is added to the batch, the primary supplier may, if they own the
  batch, adjust the recorded concentration of sulphur of the batch to
  reflect the addition of the oxygenate or butane and may use the
  adjusted recorded concentration of the batch, instead of its measured
  concentration, to calculate the pool average for the refinery or for
  gasoline that is imported.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (6), if the
  sulphur-limited oxygenate that is added to the batch is denatured fuel
  ethanol, the primary supplier may, in adjusting the recorded
  concentration of the batch, assume that the sulphur concentration of
  the oxygenate is 5 mg/kg.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total volume of each type of sulphur-limited
    oxygenate or sulphur-limited butane that was added to low-sulphur
    gasoline, in m^3^; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the volume-weighted average sulphur concentration,
    in mg/kg, of each type of sulphur-limited oxygenate or
    sulphur-limited butane that was added to low-sulphur gasoline.
