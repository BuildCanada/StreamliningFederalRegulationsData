## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-727353 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Musquash Estuary Marine
      Protected Area designated under section 2.
      ([zone]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
      2 of the [Canada Shipping Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15).
      ([bâtiment]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in addition to the waters,
      the seabed and subsoil below the waters to a depth of two metres.
      ([eaux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, all geographical coordinates
  (latitude and longitude) are expressed in the North America Datum 1983
  (NAD 83) geodetic reference system.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the schedule, the lines connecting the points are
  rhumb lines.

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-727363 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The areas of the sea in the
Musquash Estuary comprised of the management zones described below --
and depicted in the schedule -- are together designated as the Musquash
Estuary Marine Protected Area:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Zone 1, consisting of waters lying generally
  northwest of a rhumb line connecting points at 45°11′19.63″ N,
  66°15′37.35″ W and 45°11′23.96″ N, 66°15′33.38″ W, that are within an
  area bounded by the low-water line of the estuary and by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the rhumb line to its points of intersection with
    the low-water line,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the northeasterly limit of Highway 790, where it
    crosses the southwestern arm of the West Branch Musquash River,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the southeasterly limit of the abandoned Canadian
    Pacific Railway right-of-way, where it crosses the northeastern arm
    of the West Branch Musquash River, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the southerly limit of Route 1, where it crosses
    the East Branch Musquash River;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Zone 2A, consisting of waters that are within an area
  bounded by the low-water line of the estuary and by the following
  rhumb lines to their respective points of intersection with the
  low-water line, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a line connecting points at 45°11′19.63″ N,
    66°15′37.35″ W and 45°11′23.96″ N, 66°15′33.38″ W, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a line connecting points at 45°08′47.00″ N,
    66°15′11.00″ W and 45°09′08.91″ N, 66°13′55.87″ W;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Zone 2B, consisting of waters that are within an area
  known as Gooseberry Cove and bounded by the low-water line of the cove
  and by a rhumb line, to its points of intersection with the low-water
  line, connecting points at 45°08′28.46″ N, 66°15′12.23″ W and
  45°08′14.39″ N, 66°15′35.50″ W; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Zone 3, consisting of waters that are within an area
  bounded by the low-water line of the estuary and by the following
  rhumb lines to their respective points of intersection with the
  low-water line, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a line connecting points at 45°08′47.00″ N,
    66°15′11.00″ W and 45°09′08.91″ N, 66°13′55.87″ W,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a line connecting points at 45°08′28.46″ N,
    66°15′12.23″ W and 45°08′14.39″ N, 66°15′35.50″ W, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a line connecting points at 45°08′14.39″ N,
    66°15′35.50″ W and 45°08′35.60″ N, 66°14′16.77″ W.

## [Prohibited Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-727378 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  Area, no person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} disturb, damage or destroy, or remove from the
    Area, any living marine organism or any part of its habitat; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} carry out any activity --- including depositing,
    discharging or dumping any substance, or causing any substance to be
    deposited, discharged or dumped --- that is likely to result in the
    disturbance, damage, destruction or removal of a living marine
    organism or any part of its habitat.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a person may carry out any
  activity excepted under section 4 or any scientific, educational,
  archaeological, commercial tourism or habitat restoration activity for
  which a plan is approved under section 6.

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-727384 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The following activities
may be carried out in the Area:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the following fishing activities, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} fishing that is carried out in accordance with the
    [Aboriginal Communal Fishing Licences
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-332),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any of the following recreational fishing
    activities carried out in accordance with the [Atlantic Fishery
    Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-21) or the [Maritime
    Provinces Fishery Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-55), namely,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} manually fishing for scallops or clams, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} fishing for any other species by means of angling
      or a dip net, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any of the following commercial fishing
    activities carried out in accordance with the [Atlantic Fishery
    Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-21) or the [Maritime
    Provinces Fishery Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-55), namely,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} in Zone 1, fishing for elvers or eels by means of
      a hand-deployed fyke net or dip net,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} in Zone 2A, 2B or 3, fishing for lobster by means
      of individual traps and for herring by means of a weir, beach
      seine, bar seine or drag seine,

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} in Zone 3, fishing for scallops, and

    - [(D)]{.lawlabel} in any Zone, manually fishing for clams;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in Zone 2A, 2B or 3, the recreational or commercial
  harvesting of dulse manually;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the operation of a vessel in Zone 2A or 2B at a speed
  no greater than five knots or in Zone 3 at a speed no greater than
  eight knots;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in Zone 2A, the construction of a boat launch, the
  maintenance, repair or removal of a wharf or boat launch or the
  maintenance of a navigation channel, for which approval or
  authorization is not required under the New Brunswick Clean Water Act,
  S.N.B. 1989, c. C-6.1, the [Canadian Navigable Waters
  Act](/eng/acts/N-22) or the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14), as the
  case may be, or that is carried out in accordance with an approval or
  authorization required under any of those Acts; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any activity that is carried out for the purpose of
  public safety, national defence, national security or law enforcement
  or in response to an emergency.

## [Activity Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-727402 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every person who proposes
to carry out a scientific, educational, archaeological, commercial
tourism or habitat restoration activity in the Area shall submit to the
Minister for approval, not less than 60 days before the day on which the
activity is proposed to begin, a plan that contains the following
information and documents:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number and, if
  applicable, the facsimile number and electronic mail address of a
  person who can be contacted in respect of the plan;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the proposed activity that
  sets out

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its purpose,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the period or periods during which it is to be
    carried out,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a map on which its location is identified,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the types of data that are to be collected, if
    any, and the sampling protocols or other techniques to be used to
    collect the data,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the types of equipment, if any, that are to be used
    during the proposed activity, including those for gathering data
    and, if any of the equipment is to be anchored or moored in the
    Area, the methods by which the anchoring or mooring is to be
    conducted,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the type and identity of every vessel that is to
    be used to carry out the proposed activity, and

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} every substance, if any, that is to be deposited,
    discharged or dumped within the Area during the proposed activity;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an assessment of the environmental effects that are
  likely to occur within the Area as a result of the proposed activity;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a list of every licence, permit, authorization or
  consent obtained or applied for in respect of the proposed activity.

<!-- -->

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall, within 30 days after the day on which a plan that is
  submitted in accordance with section 5 is received, approve the plan
  if the proposed activity is not likely to damage or destroy the
  habitat of a living marine organism in the Area and, in the case of a
  habitat restoration activity, the proposed activity is to be carried
  out for the purpose of managing the Area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Minister shall not
  approve a plan if the cumulative environmental effects of the proposed
  activity, in combination with any other past and current activities
  carried out within the Area, are likely to damage or destroy the
  habitat of living marine organisms in the Area.

## [Reporting of Accidents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-727418 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every person involved in an
accident that is likely to result in any prohibited activity shall,
within two hours after its occurrence, report the accident to the
Canadian Coast Guard.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-727420 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
