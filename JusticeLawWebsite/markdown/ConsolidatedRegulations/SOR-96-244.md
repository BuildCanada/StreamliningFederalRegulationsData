## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-980444 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [National Energy Board Act Part VI (Oil and Gas)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-244).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-980446 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [National Energy Board
    Act](/eng/acts/N-7); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[heavy crude oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[heavy crude oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance that has a
    density greater than 875.7 kg/m^3^ and is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} oil, other than refined petroleum products,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a blend of oils, other than refined petroleum
      products, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a blend of oils, other than refined petroleum
      products, with refined petroleum products; ([pétrole brut
      lourd]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence for the exportation
    or importation of oil or gas issued under Part VI of the Act;
    ([licence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[light crude oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[light crude oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance that has a
    density equal to or less than 875.7 kg/m^3^ and is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} oil, other than refined petroleum products,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a blend of oils, other than refined petroleum
      products, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a blend of oils, other than refined petroleum
      products, with refined petroleum products; ([pétrole brut
      léger]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an order authorizing the
    exportation, importation, exportation for subsequent importation or
    importation for subsequent exportation of gas or authorizing the
    exportation of oil that is issued by the Board under these
    Regulations; ([ordonnance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[refined petroleum products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[refined petroleum products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} oil recovered by the processing of oil sands,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} gasoline-type fuels for use in internal
      combustion engines,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} oil for use as a component in the blending of
      gasoline-type fuels referred to in paragraph (b),

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} middle distillates, including the products
      commercially known as kerosene, stove oil, diesel fuel, furnace
      oil, diesel oil, gas oil, distillate heating oil, engine
      distillates and Nos. 1, 2 and 3 fuel oils,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} heavy fuel oils, including Nos. 4, 5 and 6 fuel
      oils, bunker "C" oil, "C" grade oil, residual fuel oil, heavy
      bunker oil, intermediate and thin bunker fuels and any blend of
      heavy fuel oils, and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} partially processed oil, whether commingled with
      crude oil or equivalent hydrocarbons or not. ([produits pétroliers
      raffinés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-980468 .Part}

### [Procedures for Applying For and Issuing Licences and Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-980469 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In addition to the
requirements of these Regulations, Part I of the National Energy Board
Rules of Practice and Procedure, 1995 applies in respect of the
procedures to be followed in applying for and issuing a licence or an
order.

### [Approval of Licences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-980471 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The approval of the
Governor in Council is required prior to the issuance of a licence for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the exportation of gas;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the importation of gas;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the exportation of heavy crude oil; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the exportation of oil other than heavy crude oil.

### [Conditions of Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-980477 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/2000-256,
s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

### [Suspension and Revocation of Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-980479 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Board may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend an order if any term or condition of the
    order has not been complied with or has been contravened; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} revoke an order if the holder of the order refuses
    to comply with any term or condition of the order, or does not
    comply with any conditions imposed for the lifting of a suspension.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before suspending or revoking an order, the Board
  shall send a notice to the holder of the order setting out the term or
  condition of the order that it is alleged the holder has not complied
  with, has contravened or has refused to comply with, and shall afford
  the person an opportunity to be heard.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsections (1) and (2), the Board
  may suspend or revoke an order on the application or with the consent
  of the holder of the order.

### [Inspections]{.HTitleText2} {#h-980486 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member
  of the Board or any person authorized by the Board in writing for the
  purpose may, in order to carry out an inspection in connection with
  the exportation or importation of oil or gas, at any reasonable time

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter any premises in which oil or gas is produced
    or recovered for export from Canada, is exported from Canada or is
    imported into Canada, or any place of business related to such
    production or recovery;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inspect any instruments, devices, plant, equipment,
    books, records or accounts or any other thing used for or in
    connection with the exportation or importation of oil or gas; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} conduct any tests that are necessary in order to
    carry out the inspection.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person authorized by the Board to exercise the
  powers referred to in subsection (1) shall produce the authorization,
  when requested to do so during the exercise of those powers.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is the operator of or in charge of
  any premises or any thing referred to in subsection (1) shall permit a
  member of the Board or a person authorized by the Board to exercise
  the powers referred to in that subsection and shall assist the member
  or person in exercising those powers.

### [Units of Measurement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-980494 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of these Regulations, all gas shall be measured in units of
  measurement that meet the requirements of the [Electricity and Gas
  Inspection Act](/eng/acts/E-4), and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of volume measurement, shall be
    expressed as the number of cubic metres the gas would occupy at
    standard conditions, namely, at a temperature of 15°C and at an
    absolute pressure of 101.325 kPa; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of thermal measurement, shall be
    computed as the number of joules on a dry basis where dry gas has a
    moisture content of less than 110 mg/m^3^.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where volume is measured under conditions of
  temperature and pressure other than the standard conditions described
  in paragraph (1)(a), the volume shall be converted to the equivalent
  under the standard conditions, in accordance with the Ideal Gas Laws,
  and shall be corrected for deviations from the Ideal Gas Laws in
  accordance with subsection (3), where the amount of the deviation
  exceeds one per cent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Correction for deviation from the Ideal Gas Laws
  shall be based on the tables published in American Gas Association
  (AGA) Report No. 3, Orifice Metering of Natural Gas and Other Related
  Hydrocarbon Fluids, as amended from time to time.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsections (1) to (3), propane,
  butanes and ethane may be measured in liquid form, in which case the
  volume measurement shall be computed in cubic metres.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, the units of measurement of liquids, other than liquids
determined by the Board to be cryogenic liquids, shall be computed at a
temperature of 15°C.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Gas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-980503 .Part}

### [DIVISION I]{.HLabel2}[GAS OTHER THAN PROPANE, BUTANES AND ETHANE]{.HTitleText2} {#h-980504 .Subheading}

#### [Application]{.HTitleText3} {#h-980505 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** This Division applies to
gas other than propane, butanes and ethane.

**[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 119.01(1.1) of the Act, [[natural gas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
means a mixture of gas that is composed of at least 85% methane and that
may also contain other hydrocarbons that at a temperature of 15°C and an
absolute pressure of 101.325 kPa are in a gaseous state, as well as
minor amounts of non-hydrocarbon gas and impurities.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-212, s. 1
:::

#### [Exemption]{.HTitleText3} {#h-980510 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Ethylene and propylene
are exempt from the operation of Part VI of the Act.

#### [Information to be Furnished by Applicants for Licences for Exportation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-980512 .Subheading}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** An applicant for a
licence for the exportation of gas shall furnish to the Board all the
information that is necessary for the Board to dispose of the
application, including, unless otherwise authorized by the Board,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the terms that the applicant is requesting for the
  licence, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the duration of the licence,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the maximum daily, annual and term quantities of
    gas proposed to be exported,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the tolerance levels that are
    necessary to accommodate temporary operating conditions, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the points of exportation of the gas from Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} information respecting the applicant's gas supply
  supporting the proposed exportation, whether contractually dedicated
  or undedicated, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the quantities of gas under contract
    to or owned by the applicant, including daily and annual volumes,
    reserves and the termination date of every such contract, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a copy of each pro forma contract for each type of
    gas purchase contract;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} information respecting the applicant's gas market,
  including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} details of the applicant's gas export sale,
    including

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a copy of every gas export sales contract for the
      proposed exportation,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a detailed summary of the terms and conditions of
      every such contract, including the details of the matters referred
      to in Schedule I, substantially in the form set out therein, and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the name of a person to whom questions respecting
      the details of every such contract may be directed, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the export market to be served by
    the proposed exportation;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the gas proposed to be exported is from a gas
  supply other than a contractually dedicated pool, field or area, a gas
  supply and demand balance for the reserves supporting the application,
  on both an aggregate and an annual basis for the duration of the
  proposed exportation, identifying all firm contractual commitments
  supported by those reserves;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} details of the transportation arrangements pertaining
  to the proposed exportation, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the details and status of all contractual
    arrangements for the movement of the gas in and outside Canada,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a copy of every transportation contract for the
    movement of the gas in Canada, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a description of any existing or proposed
    gathering, storage or transmission facility, and of any new facility
    other than a gathering, storage or transmission facility, that is
    required to move the gas to market, whether the facility is, or will
    be, in or outside Canada,

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} information respecting the potential environmental
  effects of the proposed exportation and any social effects that would
  be directly related to those environmental effects;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} an assessment of the impact of the proposed
  exportation on Canadian energy and natural gas markets to determine
  whether Canadians are likely to have difficulty in meeting their
  energy requirements at fair market prices;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a copy of, or details of the status of, each approval
  or authorization of a federal, provincial or state government
  pertaining to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the removal of gas from a province,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the importation of gas into the country of
    destination,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} transportation services,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} tariffs and tolls,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} facilities,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} environmental reviews, and

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} contractual arrangements necessary for the
    exportation of gas; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a status sheet summarizing the contractual
  arrangements and regulatory approvals and authorizations,
  substantially in the form set out in Schedule II.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-256, s. 2
:::

#### [Orders for Exportation or Importation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-980587 .Subheading}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Where the Board
determines that an application for an order for the exportation or
importation of gas contains all the information necessary to dispose of
the application, the Board may issue an order authorizing a person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to export gas

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a period not exceeding two years, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a period exceeding two years but not exceeding
    20 years, in quantities of not more than 30,000 m^3^ per day;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to import gas

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a period not exceeding two years, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a period exceeding two years but not exceeding
    20 years, in quantities of not more than 30,000 m^3^ per day; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to export gas for subsequent import or to import gas
  for subsequent export for a period not exceeding 25 years.

#### [Amendments to Gas Export Sales Contracts and Gas Import Purchase Contracts]{.HTitleText3} {#h-980638 .Subheading}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section,

  [[gas export sales contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[gas export sales contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      contract, other than a third party contract, for the sale of gas
      between

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder of a licence and the importer,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder of a licence and the vendor, where
        the holder or an affiliate or subsidiary of the holder is also
        the importer, or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the holder of a licence and the importer and
        between the holder and the vendor, where the holder is an
        affiliate or subsidiary of the importer and of the vendor;
        ([contrat de vente de gaz à l'exportation]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[holder of a licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[holder of a licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person who
      holds a licence for the exportation of gas; ([titulaire d'une
      licence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[importer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[importer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the importer of gas in the
      country of destination of the gas; ([importateur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[third party contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[third party contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a contract for
      the sale of gas contracted, under a gas export sales contract to a
      third party, by the holder of a licence or the importer of the
      gas, where

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sale to the third party is for a term of
        less than two years,
