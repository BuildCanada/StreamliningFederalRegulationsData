## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-697414 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[casual goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods other than goods
imported for sale or for an industrial, occupational, commercial or
institutional or other like use.

## [Casual Goods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-697416 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Casual goods that are
acquired in Costa Rica are deemed to originate in Costa Rica and are
entitled to the benefit of the Costa Rica Tariff if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the marking of the goods is in accordance with the
  marking laws of Costa Rica and indicates that the goods are the
  product of Costa Rica or Canada; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods do not bear a mark and there is no evidence
  to indicate that the goods are not the product of Costa Rica or
  Canada.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-697420 .Part}

**[[Footnote

subsection 34(1) of the [Canada-Costa Rica Free Trade Agreement
Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-1.9), chapter 28 of the Statutes of
Canada, 2001, comes into force.
