## Proclamation Giving Notice that the Agreement on Social Security Between Canada and the Republic of Austria is in Force as of July 1, 2023 {#proclamation-giving-notice-that-the-agreement-on-social-security-between-canada-and-the-republic-of-austria-is-in-force-as-of-july-1-2023 .Title-of-Act}

:::

[OLD AGE SECURITY ACT](/eng/acts/O-9)

Registration 2023-06-21

Proclamation Giving Notice that the Agreement on Social Security Between
Canada and the Republic of Austria is in Force as of July 1, 2023
::::

::::: {.section .order}
Mary May Simon

\[L.S.\]

Canada

CHARLES THE THIRD, by the Grace of God of the United Kingdom, Canada and
His other Realms and Territories KING, Head of the Commonwealth,
Defender of the Faith.

Shalene Curtis-Micallef

Deputy Attorney General

Great Seal of Canada

TO ALL TO WHOM these presents shall come or whom the same may in any way
concern,

GREETING:

#### [**A Proclamation**]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1401946 .Subheading}

Whereas by Order in Council P.C. 2022-658 of June 10, 2022, the Governor
in Council declared that, in accordance with Article 32 of the Agreement
on Social Security between Canada and the Republic of Austria, signed at
Vienna on July 5, 2021, the Agreement, after each party notified the
other by diplomatic note that it has completed the internal legal
procedures necessary for the entry into force of the Agreement, enters
into force on the first day of the fourth month following the month in
which the second diplomatic note is received;

Whereas, in accordance with subsection 42(1) of the [Old Age Security
Act](/eng/acts/O-9), the Order in Council was laid before the House of
Commons on June 14, 2022 and the Senate on June 15, 2022;

Whereas, before the twentieth sitting day after the Order had been laid
before both Houses of Parliament, no motion for the consideration of
either House to the effect that the Order be revoked was filed with the
Speaker of the appropriate House;

Whereas, under subsection 42(2) of that Act, the Order came into force
on the thirtieth sitting day after it had been laid before Parliament,
being October 27, 2022;

Whereas the exchange of diplomatic notes, through diplomatic channels,
was completed in March 2023;

Whereas the Agreement, after each party has notified the other by
diplomatic note that it has completed the internal legal procedures
necessary for the entry into force of the Agreement, is to enter into
force on the first day of the fourth month following the month in which
the second diplomatic note is received, being July 1, 2023;

And whereas, by Order in Council P.C. 2023-457 of May 18, 2023, the
Governor in Council directed, under subsection 41(2) of the [Old Age
Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9), that a proclamation be issued giving
notice that the Agreement is in force as of July 1, 2023;

Now Know You that We, by and with the advice of Our Privy Council for
Canada, do by this Our Proclamation give notice that the annexed
Agreement on Social Security between Canada and the Republic of Austria
is in force as of July 1, 2023.

Of all which Our loving subjects and all others whom these presents may
concern are required to take notice and to govern themselves
accordingly.

In testimony whereof, We have caused this Our Proclamation to be
published and the Great Seal of Canada to be affixed to it.

WITNESS:

Our Right Trusty and Well-beloved Mary May Simon, Chancellor and
Principal Companion of Our Order of Canada, Chancellor and Commander of
Our Order of Military Merit, Chancellor and Commander of Our Order of
Merit of the Police Forces, Governor General and Commander-in-Chief of
Canada.

At the City of Ottawa, this fifth day of June, two thousand and
twenty-three and in the first year of Our reign.

BY COMMAND,

::: FlushLeft
Simon Kennedy

### [AGREEMENT ON SOCIAL SECURITY BETWEEN CANADA AND THE REPUBLIC OF AUSTRIA]{.HTitleText1} {#agreement-on-social-security-between-canada-and-the-republic-of-austria .SchedHeadL1}

**CANADA AND THE REPUBLIC OF AUSTRIA ("AUSTRIA")** (hereinafter referred
to as the "Parties")

**RESOLVED** to further strengthen the relations between them in the
field of social security,

**NOTING** the Agreement on Social Security between Canada and the
Republic of Austria done at Vienna on 24 February 1987 (the "1987
Agreement") and the Supplementary Agreement to the Agreement on Social
Security Between Canada and the Republic of Austria done at Vienna on 12
September 1995 (the "1995 Supplementary Agreement"), and

**TAKING INTO ACCOUNT** the changes in their respective legislation
since the 1987 Agreement and the 1995 Supplementary Agreement were
signed,

**HAVE AGREED** as follows:

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#part-igeneral-provisions .SchedHeadL1}

#### [ARTICLE 1]{.HLabel2}[Definitions]{.HTitleText2} {#article-1definitions .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Agreement:

  [[benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, for a Party, any cash
      benefit that is payable under the legislation of that Party and
      includes any supplements or increases to that cash benefit;

  [[competent agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[competent agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means:

      \- for Canada, the competent authority, and

      \- for Austria, the agency, the institution, the organization, or
      the body responsible for all or part of the implementation of the
      legislation of Austria, and;

  [[competent authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[competent authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means:

      \- for Canada, the Minister or Ministers responsible for the
      implementation of the legislation of Canada, and

      \- for Austria, the Federal Minister or Ministers responsible for
      the implementation of the legislation of Austria;

  [[legislation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[legislation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, for a Party, the laws
      referred to in Article 2;

  [[national]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[national]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, for Canada, a Canadian
      citizen; and, for Austria, an Austrian citizen;

  [[period of coverage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[period of coverage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means:

      for Canada,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a period of contribution under the [Canada
        Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a period of residence under the [Old Age
        Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9), and

      \- for Austria, a period of contribution or equivalent period that
      is defined or recognized as a period of coverage under the
      legislation referred to in Article 2(1)(a);

- [2]{.lawlabel} A term that is used in this Agreement that is not
  defined in this Article has the meaning assigned to it in the Parties'
  respective laws.

#### [ARTICLE 2]{.HLabel2}[Legislative Scope]{.HTitleText2} {#article-2legislative-scope .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} This Agreement applies to the following legislation:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for Austria:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the legislation concerning pension insurance,
      including regulations and statutory instruments, with the
      exception of the legislation concerning the insurance for
      notaries, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} with regard to Part II only, to the legislation
      concerning sickness insurance and accident insurance, including
      regulations and statutory instruments;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for Canada:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9) and its
      regulations, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) and its
      regulations.

- [2]{.lawlabel} This Agreement applies to any laws, regulations, and
  statutory instruments that amend, supplement, consolidate, or
  supersede the legislation referred to in paragraph 1.

- [3]{.lawlabel} This Agreement also applies to any laws and regulations
  that extend the legislation of a Party to include a new category of
  persons or to include a new benefit unless the Party that implements
  the changes advises the other Party, within six months of the entry
  into force of those laws and regulations, that this Agreement does not
  apply to that new category of persons or to that new benefit.

#### [ARTICLE 3]{.HLabel2}[Personal Scope]{.HTitleText2} {#article-3personal-scope .SchedHeadL2}

This Agreement applies to any person who is or who has been subject to
the legislation of Canada or Austria, or both Parties, and to any other
person who derives eligibility for a benefit from that first person
under the legislation of a Party.

#### [ARTICLE 4]{.HLabel2}[Equality of Treatment]{.HTitleText2} {#article-4equality-of-treatment .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} When determining eligibility for and payment of a
  benefit, a Party shall apply the same conditions to a person who is or
  who has been subject to the legislation of the other Party, and to any
  other person who derives eligibility for a benefit from that first
  person, as the conditions that apply to a national of the first Party.

- [2]{.lawlabel} A Party shall apply paragraph 1 even if a person
  resides in or is present in the territory of a third State.

- [3]{.lawlabel} Paragraph 1 does not apply to the legislation of
  Austria concerning the apportionment of insurance burdens that result
  from an agreement with a third party.

- [4]{.lawlabel} For the legislation of Austria concerning credit for a
  period of war service or a period considered as equivalent to war
  service, Austria shall apply equal treatment to an Austrian national
  and to a Canadian national who was an Austrian national immediately
  before 13 March 1938.

- [5]{.lawlabel} Austria shall apply to a Canadian national that is
  subject to the legislation of Austria in accordance with Article 9 a
  treatment equal to the treatment applied to an Austrian national.

#### [ARTICLE 5]{.HLabel2}[Export of Benefits]{.HTitleText2} {#article-5export-of-benefits .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise provided in this Agreement, a Party
  shall not reduce, modify, suspend, or cancel a benefit payable to a
  person described in Article 3 based only on the fact that the person
  who is eligible for that benefit resides in or is present in the
  territory of the other Party. A Party shall pay that benefit when that
  person resides in or is present in the territory of the other Party.

- [2]{.lawlabel} For Canada, an allowance or a guaranteed income
  supplement is payable to a person who is outside Canada only to the
  extent permitted by the [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9).

- [3]{.lawlabel} For Austria, paragraph 1 does not apply to a
  compensatory supplement or a single payment to maintain purchasing
  power.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Provisions Concerning the Applicable Legislation]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiprovisions-concerning-the-applicable-legislation .SchedHeadL1}

#### [ARTICLE 6]{.HLabel2}[General Rules for Employed Persons]{.HTitleText2} {#article-6general-rules-for-employed-persons .SchedHeadL2}

Subject to Articles 7 to 10, an employed person who works in the
territory of a Party is, in respect of that work, subject only to the
legislation of that Party. This applies even if the employer's place of
business is in the territory of the other Party.

#### [ARTICLE 7]{.HLabel2}[Self-Employed Persons]{.HTitleText2} {#article-7self-employed-persons .SchedHeadL2}

A person who meets the criteria for compulsory selfemployment coverage
under the legislation of both Parties and who resides in the territory
of one Party is subject only to the legislation of that Party.

#### [ARTICLE 8]{.HLabel2}[Detachments]{.HTitleText2} {#article-8detachments .SchedHeadL2}

If an employed person who is subject to the legislation of a Party is
detached by that person's employer to work in the territory of the other
Party, that person is, in respect of that work, subject only to the
legislation of the first Party for the first sixty months, as though
that work were performed in the first Party's territory.

#### [ARTICLE 9]{.HLabel2}[Government Employment]{.HTitleText2} {#article-9government-employment .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding this Agreement, the provisions
  regarding social security of the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic
  Relations of 18 April 1961 and the Vienna Convention on Consular
  Relations of 24 April 1963 continue to apply to persons covered by
  those conventions.

- [2]{.lawlabel} A person who is employed by the government or another
  public employer of a Party and who is sent by that Party to perform
  services in the territory of the other Party is subject only to the
  legislation of the first Party in respect of those services.

- [3]{.lawlabel} Except as provided in paragraphs 1 and 2, a person who
  resides in the territory of a Party and who is employed in that
  territory by the government of the other Party is subject only to the
  legislation of the first Party in respect of that employment.

#### [ARTICLE 10]{.HLabel2}[Exceptions]{.HTitleText2} {#article-10exceptions .SchedHeadL2}

At the request of an employed person and that person's employer or of a
self-employed person, the Parties may, through their competent
authorities, jointly consent to modify the application of Articles 6 to
9, taking into account the nature and circumstances of the work.

#### [ARTICLE 11]{.HLabel2}[Coverage and Residence under the Legislation of Canada]{.HTitleText2} {#article-11coverage-and-residence-under-the-legislation-of-canada .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of calculating a benefit under the
  [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if, during any period of presence or residence in
    Austria, a person is subject to the [Canada Pension
    Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), or to a comprehensive pension plan of a
    province of Canada, Canada shall consider that period to be a period
    of residence in Canada for that person; Canada shall also consider
    that period to be a period of residence in Canada for that person's
    spouse or common-law partner and for dependants who accompany that
    person during a period of presence or residence in Austria and who
    are not subject to the legislation of Austria by reason of
    employment or self-employment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a person is subject to the legislation of
    Austria during any period of presence or residence in Canada, Canada
    shall determine if that period for that person, and for that
    person's spouse or common-law partner and dependants who accompany
    that person during a period of presence or residence in Canada, is
    considered a period of residence in Canada in accordance with the
    provisions of the [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9) and its
    regulations.

- [2]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph 1:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Canada shall consider that a person is subject to
    the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or to the comprehensive
    pension plan of a province of Canada during a period of presence or
    residence in Austria only if, during that period, that person makes
    a contribution pursuant to that plan by reason of employment or
    self-employment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Canada shall consider that a person is subject to
    the legislation of Austria during a period of presence or residence
    in Canada only if during that period that person or that person's
    employer makes a compulsory contribution pursuant to that
    legislation by reason of employment or self-employment.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Provisions Concerning Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiiprovisions-concerning-benefits .SchedHeadL1}

#### [CHAPTER 1]{.HLabel2}[Totalizing]{.HTitleText2} {#chapter-1totalizing .SchedHeadL2}

##### [ARTICLE 12]{.HLabel3}[Periods under the legislation of Canada and Austria]{.HTitleText3} {#article-12periods-under-the-legislation-of-canada-and-austria .SchedHeadL3}

- [1]{.lawlabel} If a person is not eligible for a benefit because that
  person does not have sufficient periods of coverage under the
  legislation of a Party, the Party, through its competent agency, shall
  determine the eligibility of that person for that benefit by
  totalizing the periods of coverage and the periods specified in
  paragraphs 2 and 3, provided that those periods do not overlap. That
  Party, through its competent agency, shall consider the periods
  specified in paragraphs 2 and 3 as if they were completed under its
  legislation.

- [2]{.lawlabel} To determine eligibility for a benefit under the
  legislation of Canada, Canada shall consider:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a period of coverage under the legislation of
    Austria as a period of residence in Canada under the [Old Age
    Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a period of residence in Austria that occurs when a
    person has reached the age at which periods of residence in Canada
    are creditable for the purposes of the [Old Age Security
    Act](/eng/acts/O-9) as a period of residence in Canada under the
    [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9), provided that this period of
    residence in Austria does not overlap with a period of coverage
    under the legislation of Austria;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a calendar year that includes at least three months
    that are periods of coverage under the legislation of Austria as a
    calendar year that is a period of coverage under the [Canada Pension
    Plan](/eng/acts/C-8).

- [3]{.lawlabel} To determine eligibility for a benefit under the
  legislation of Austria, Austria shall consider:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a calendar year that is a period of coverage under
    the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) during which a person is
    employed or self-employed as 12 months of contributions of
    compulsory insurance due to gainful activity under the legislation
    of Austria;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a month that contains at least fifteen days of
    residence under the [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9) as a month
    that is a period of coverage under the legislation of Austria
    provided that the period of coverage under the [Old Age Security
    Act](/eng/acts/O-9) does not overlap with a period of coverage under
    the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8).

##### [ARTICLE 13]{.HLabel3}[Periods completed under the System of a third State]{.HTitleText3} {#article-13periods-completed-under-the-system-of-a-third-state .SchedHeadL3}

If a person is not eligible for a benefit based on the periods of
coverage under the respective legislation of both Parties, totalized in
accordance with Article 12, a Party, through its competent agency, shall
determine the eligibility of that person for that benefit by totalizing
these periods and periods of coverage completed under the legislation of
a third State to which it is bound by a social security instrument that
provides for the totalizing of periods.

##### [ARTICLE 14]{.HLabel3}[Minimum Period to be Totalized]{.HTitleText3} {#article-14minimum-period-to-be-totalized .SchedHeadL3}

If the total of the periods of coverage completed by a person under the
legislation of a Party is less than one year and if that person is not
eligible for a benefit under the legislation of that Party based only on
those periods of coverage, then that Party, through its competent
agency, is not required to pay a benefit to that person for those
periods. The other Party, through its competent agency, shall, however,
take into account those periods of coverage to determine whether that
person is eligible for a benefit under its legislation in accordance
with Articles 12 and 13.

#### [CHAPTER 2]{.HLabel2}[Benefits Under the Legislation of Austria]{.HTitleText2} {#chapter-2benefits-under-the-legislation-of-austria .SchedHeadL2}

##### [ARTICLE 15]{.HLabel3}[Special Rules for Totalization]{.HTitleText3} {#article-15special-rules-for-totalization .SchedHeadL3}

For the application of Chapter 1, the following applies:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the legislation of Austria makes a benefit
  conditional on the completion of a period of coverage in an occupation
  that is covered by a special scheme or a period of coverage in a
  specified occupation or a specified employment, only a period of
  coverage that is covered by a corresponding special scheme under the
  legislation of Canada or a period of coverage in the same specified
  occupation or the same specified employment under the legislation of
  Canada is taken into account for that benefit;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the legislation of Austria provides that a period
  of payment of a pension prolongs the reference period during which to
  complete periods of coverage, a period of payment of a benefit under
  the legislation of Canada corresponding to that pension also prolongs
  that reference period.

##### [ARTICLE 16]{.HLabel3}[Calculation of the Benefits]{.HTitleText3} {#article-16calculation-of-the-benefits .SchedHeadL3}

- [1]{.lawlabel} If a person is eligible for a benefit under the
  legislation of Austria without the application of Chapter 1, Austria,
  through its competent agency, shall determine the amount of the
  benefit in accordance with the legislation of Austria based only on
  the periods of coverage that are completed under that legislation.

- [2]{.lawlabel} If a person is eligible for a benefit under the
  legislation of Austria based only on the totalizing provisions of
  Chapter 1, Austria, through its competent agency, shall determine the
  amount of the benefit in accordance with the legislation of Austria
  concerning the calculation of a benefit under bilateral agreements.

#### [CHAPTER 3]{.HLabel2}[Benefits Under the Legislation of Canada]{.HTitleText2} {#chapter-3benefits-under-the-legislation-of-canada .SchedHeadL2}

##### [ARTICLE 17]{.HLabel3}[Benefits without Totalization]{.HTitleText3} {#article-17benefits-without-totalization .SchedHeadL3}

If a person is eligible for a benefit under the legislation of Canada
without the application of Chapter 1, Canada, through its competent
agency, shall determine the benefit in accordance with the legislation
that Canada applies, based only on periods of coverage completed under
that legislation.

##### [ARTICLE 18]{.HLabel3}[Benefits under the [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9)]{.HTitleText3} {#article-18benefits-under-the-old-age-security-act .SchedHeadL3}

- [1]{.lawlabel} If a person is eligible for a pension or allowance
  under the [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9) based only on the
  totalizing provisions of Chapter 1, Canada shall calculate the pension
  or allowance payable to that person based only on the periods of
  residence in Canada that are identified in accordance with that Act.

- [2]{.lawlabel} Paragraph 1 also applies to a person outside Canada who
  is eligible for a full pension in Canada even if that person has not
  resided in Canada for the minimum period required by the [Old Age
  Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9) for the payment of a pension outside
  Canada.

- [3]{.lawlabel} Canada shall pay an Old Age Security pension to a
  person who is outside Canada only if that person's periods of
  residence, totalized in accordance with Chapter 1, are at least equal
  to the minimum period of residence in Canada required by the [Old Age
  Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9) for the payment of a pension outside
  Canada.

##### [ARTICLE 19]{.HLabel3}[Benefits under the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8)]{.HTitleText3} {#article-19benefits-under-the-canada-pension-plan .SchedHeadL3}

If a person is eligible for a benefit based only on the totalizing
provisions of Chapter 1, Canada shall calculate the benefit payable to
that person in the following manner:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the earnings-related portion of the benefit is
  determined in accordance with the provisions of the [Canada Pension
  Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), based only on the pensionable earnings under
  that Plan;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the flat-rate portion of the benefit is pro-rated by
  multiplying:

  the flat-rate portion of the benefit determined in accordance with the
  [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8)

  by

  the fraction representing the ratio of the periods of contribution to
  the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) in relation to the minimum
  qualifying period required under that Plan to establish eligibility
  for that benefit. That fraction must not exceed the value of one.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Administrative and Miscellaneous Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivadministrative-and-miscellaneous-provisions .SchedHeadL1}

#### [ARTICLE 20]{.HLabel2}[Administrative Arrangement]{.HTitleText2} {#article-20administrative-arrangement .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} The Parties, through their competent authorities, shall
  conclude an administrative arrangement that establishes the terms for
  the implementation of this Agreement.

- [2]{.lawlabel} The Parties, through their competent authorities, shall
  designate their liaison agencies in the administrative arrangement.

#### [ARTICLE 21]{.HLabel2}[Communication of Information, Mutual Assistance and Medical Examination]{.HTitleText2} {#article-21communication-of-information-mutual-assistance-and-medical-examination .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} A Party, through its competent authority, shall inform
  the other Party of a change in legislation that affects the
  implementation of this Agreement.

- [2]{.lawlabel} A Party, through its competent agency, shall assist the
  other Party in implementing this Agreement as if it were implementing
  its own legislation. This assistance is provided free of charge,
  subject to any provision for the reimbursement of certain types of
  expenses in an administrative arrangement concluded pursuant to
  Article 20.

- [3]{.lawlabel} If a Party, through its competent agency, requires a
  person who resides or is present in the territory of the other Party
  to undergo a medical examination and makes a request to that other
  Party, the examination is arranged or carried out by that other Party,
  through its competent agency. The examination is at the expense of the
  competent agency of the first Party and takes place in accordance with
  the procedures of the competent agency of the other Party.

#### [ARTICLE 22]{.HLabel2}[Protection of personal information]{.HTitleText2} {#article-22protection-of-personal-information .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} Subject to the Parties' respective laws, personal
  information means information about an identifiable person that is
  recorded in any form.

- [2]{.lawlabel} For the implementation of this Agreement and the
  legislation, the Parties, through their competent agencies, shall, in
  accordance with their domestic laws:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} collect and disclose to each other personal
    information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the personal information received from each
    other;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} not disclose the personal information received from
    each other to any person or body unless:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure is for the sole purpose of
      implementing this Agreement and the legislation, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure is required or permitted under
      the Parties' domestic laws;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} maintain and protect the confidentiality of the
    personal information received from each other;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} protect the personal information received from each
    other from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, and
    disposal;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} take reasonable measures to retain and store
    complete, accurate, and up-to-date personal information in a secure
    location;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} inform each other without delay when they realize
    that personal information disclosed to each other is erroneous, has
    changed, or should not have been disclosed to each other, and carry
    out the necessary deletion or correction of the personal information
    received without delay;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} investigate cases where they have reasonable
    grounds to believe that incidents may compromise the protection of
    personal information, take necessary measures to address such
    incidents, and inform each other without delay of such incidents;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} upon request, provide the person to whom the
    personal information relates, access to their personal information,
    including details regarding its use and disclosure;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} upon request of a person to whom personal
    information relates, correct their personal information when there
    is an error or omission;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} register the date, subject, and purpose of all
    disclosure of personal information to each other, and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} dispose of the personal information collected from
    each other.

- [3]{.lawlabel} The Parties shall, in accordance with their domestic
  laws, provide the person to whom the personal information relates a
  right of review before a court of law if that person is refused access
  to his or her personal information or if that person's correction of
  personal information request is refused.

#### [ARTICLE 23]{.HLabel2}[Exemption or Reduction of Fees and Authentication]{.HTitleText2} {#article-23exemption-or-reduction-of-fees-and-authentication .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} If a Party's legislation provides that a person is
  exempt from all or part of a legal, consular, or administrative fee
  for a certificate or document that is required to apply that
  legislation, the exemption applies to any legal, consular, or
  administrative fee for a certificate or document of the other Party.

- [2]{.lawlabel} An official document that is required to apply this
  Agreement does not have to be authenticated by diplomatic or consular
  authorities.

#### [ARTICLE 24]{.HLabel2}[Language of Communication]{.HTitleText2} {#article-24language-of-communication .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} The Parties may, through their competent agencies,
  competent authorities and liaison agencies, designated in an
  administrative arrangement concluded pursuant to Article 20,
  communicate directly with each other in any official language of
  either Party.

- [2]{.lawlabel} A Party, through its competent agency, shall not reject
  a claim or other document submitted to it based only on the fact that
  that claim or other document is written in an official language of the
  other Party.

#### [ARTICLE 25]{.HLabel2}[Submitting a Claim, Notice or Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#article-25submitting-a-claim-notice-or-appeal .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} If a claim, notice, or appeal that should be submitted
  within a prescribed period to the competent agency of a Party is
  submitted within the same prescribed period to the competent agency of
  the other Party, that claim, notice, or appeal is considered to be
  submitted to the competent agency of the first Party within the
  prescribed period. The date of submission of the claim, notice, or
  appeal to the competent agency of the other Party is deemed to be the
  date of submission to the competent agency of the first Party.

- [2]{.lawlabel} A Party, through its competent agency, shall consider a
  claim for a benefit under the legislation of the other Party to be a
  claim for the corresponding benefit under its own legislation if a
  person provides information at the time of application that indicates
  that periods of coverage have been completed under the legislation of
  that first Party. This does not apply if a person requests that the
  processing of a claim for a benefit under the legislation of that
  first Party be deferred.

- [3]{.lawlabel} The competent agency of a Party to which a claim,
  notice, or appeal is submitted in accordance with paragraphs 1 and 2
  transmits it without delay to the competent agency of the other Party.

#### [ARTICLE 26]{.HLabel2}[Payment of Benefit]{.HTitleText2} {#article-26payment-of-benefit .SchedHeadL2}

- 1
  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Austria, through its competent agency, shall pay a
    benefit in accordance with the legislation that it applies to a
    person who resides outside its territory or to a representative
    authorized under its legislation. It shall pay that benefit in its
    national currency or in another freely convertible currency.

  <!-- -->

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Canada shall pay a benefit in a freely convertible
    currency to any person who resides outside its territory.

- [2]{.lawlabel} The competent agency of a Party does not deduct any
  amount for administrative expenses from any benefit paid.

#### [ARTICLE 27]{.HLabel2}[Resolution of Disputes]{.HTitleText2} {#article-27resolution-of-disputes .SchedHeadL2}

The Parties shall resolve a dispute that arises in the interpretation or
application of this Agreement in accordance with the spirit and
fundamental principles of this Agreement.

#### [ARTICLE 28]{.HLabel2}[Understandings with a Province of Canada]{.HTitleText2} {#article-28understandings-with-a-province-of-canada .SchedHeadL2}

Austria may conclude an understanding with any province of Canada on any
social security matter that is within provincial jurisdiction, provided
that the understanding is not contrary with this Agreement.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Transitional and Final Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vtransitional-and-final-provisions .SchedHeadL1}

#### [ARTICLE 29]{.HLabel2}[Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#article-29transitional-provisions .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} Subject to paragraph 2, this Agreement, on entry into
  force, replaces the 1987 Agreement and the 1995 Supplementary
  Agreement.

- 2
  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} A benefit entitlement acquired by a person in
    accordance with the provisions of the 1987 Agreement and the 1995
    Supplementary Agreement is maintained.

  <!-- -->

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} A claim to a benefit made but not finally
    adjudicated at the date on which this Agreement comes into force is
    adjudicated according to the provisions of the 1987 Agreement and
    the 1995 Supplementary Agreement.

- [3]{.lawlabel} A period of coverage that is completed before the date
  of entry into force of this Agreement is taken into account to
  determine eligibility for a benefit under this Agreement.

- [4]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise provided by the legislation of a
  Party, this Agreement does not make a person eligible to receive
  payment of a benefit for a period before the date of entry into force
  of this Agreement.

- [5]{.lawlabel} A benefit is payable under this Agreement in respect of
  an event that takes place before the date of entry into force of this
  Agreement.

- [6]{.lawlabel} If, at the date on which this Agreement comes into
  force, a person is covered under the legislation of Austria or under
  the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) in accordance with the
  provisions of the 1987 Agreement and the 1995 Supplementary Agreement,
  and if that person would not be covered in accordance with the
  provisions of this Agreement, that person remains covered as long as
  they would continue to be covered in accordance with the provisions of
  the 1987 Agreement and the 1995 Supplementary Agreement.

- [7]{.lawlabel} For a person who is detached by that person's employer
  to work in the territory of the other Party on the date of entry into
  force of this Agreement, the detachment period completed before that
  date is taken into account when calculating the period of sixty months
  referred to in Article 8.

#### [ARTICLE 30]{.HLabel2}[Protection of Rights]{.HTitleText2} {#article-30protection-of-rights .SchedHeadL2}

This Agreement does not affect the rights under the legislation of
Austria of any person who has suffered disadvantages in the field of
social security because of political or religious reasons or by reason
of descent.

#### [ARTICLE 31]{.HLabel2}[Duration and Termination]{.HTitleText2} {#article-31duration-and-termination .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} This Agreement remains in force indefinitely. A Party
  may terminate it at any time with 12 months' notice, in writing,
  through diplomatic channels to the other Party.

- [2]{.lawlabel} If a Party terminates this Agreement, a person is
  entitled to a benefit that is already acquired in accordance with this
  Agreement. This Agreement continues to have effect in relation to any
  person who applies for a benefit before its termination, if that
  person would have acquired a benefit had this Agreement not been
  terminated.

- [3]{.lawlabel} The Parties shall continue to apply Part II of this
  Agreement to a detachment that commences prior to the termination of
  this Agreement.

#### [ARTICLE 32]{.HLabel2}[Entry into Force]{.HTitleText2} {#article-32entry-into-force .SchedHeadL2}

Each Party shall notify the other by diplomatic note that it has
completed the internal legal procedures necessary for the entry into
force of this Agreement. This Agreement enters into force on the first
day of the fourth month following the month in which the second note is
received.

respective Governments, have signed this Agreement.
