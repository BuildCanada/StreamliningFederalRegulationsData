## Declaring the United States to be a Reciprocating Country for Purposes of the Act {#declaring-the-united-states-to-be-a-reciprocating-country-for-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/78-179
:::

[NUCLEAR LIABILITY ACT](/eng/acts/N-28)

Registration 1978-12-13

Declaring the United States to be a Reciprocating Country for Purposes
of the Act

P.C. 1975-896 1975-04-24
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor General in Council is of opinion that satisfactory
arrangements exist in the United States of America for compensation for
injury or damage resulting from the production, processing, carriage,
storage, use or disposition of nuclear material in that country,
including any such injury or damage occasioned in Canada.

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources, pursuant
to subsection 34(2) of the [Nuclear Liability Act](/eng/acts/N-28), is
pleased hereby to declare the United States of America to be a
reciprocating country for purposes of that Act.
