## Canada Shipping Act Gold Franc Conversion Regulations {#canada-shipping-act-gold-franc-conversion-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/78-73
:::

[CURRENCY ACT](/eng/acts/C-52)

Registration 1978-01-24

Regulations Respecting Conversion of Values Expressed in Gold Francs
into Canadian Dollar Equivalents for Purposes of the Canada Shipping Act

P.C. 1978-104 1978-01-19
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance and the Minister of Transport, is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke, pursuant to subsection 651(1) of the
  [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9), the Gold Franc Equivalent Order
  made by Order in Council P.C. 1975-2579 of 28th October,
  1975[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDADAA}, as
  amended[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^2^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0EBADAA}; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to make, pursuant to section 2 of An Act to amend the
  Currency and Exchange Act and other Acts in consequence thereof, being
  chapter 38 of the Statutes of Canada, 1976-77, the annexed
  [Regulations respecting Conversion of Values expressed in Gold Francs
  into Canadian Dollar Equivalents for purposes of the Canada Shipping
  Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-73).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-863942 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Canada Shipping Act Gold Franc Conversion
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-73).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-863944 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[S.D.R.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the special drawing rights issued
by the International Monetary Fund.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-863946 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
[Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9), the equivalent dollar value of
gold francs shall be determined as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} gold francs shall be converted into S.D.R.'s at the
  exchange rate of 15.075 gold francs per S.D.R.; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} S.D.R.'s shall be converted into Canadian dollars at
  the exchange rate established by the International Monetary Fund for
  S.D.R.'s and Canadian dollars.
