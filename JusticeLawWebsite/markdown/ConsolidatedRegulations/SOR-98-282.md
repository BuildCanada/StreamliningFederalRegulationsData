## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021337 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Food and Drugs
    Act](/eng/acts/F-27). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[active device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[active device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a medical device that
    depends for its operation on a source of energy other than energy
    generated by the human body or gravity. A medical device that
    transmits or withdraws energy or a substance to or from a patient
    without substantially altering the energy or the substance is not an
    active device. ([instrument actif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[active diagnostic device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[active diagnostic device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an active
    device that, whether used alone or in combination with another
    medical device, is intended to supply information for the purpose of
    detecting, monitoring or treating a physiological condition, state
    of health, illness or congenital deformity. ([instrument
    diagnostique actif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[active therapeutic device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[active therapeutic device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an active
    device that, whether used alone or in combination with another
    medical device, is intended to support, modify, replace or restore a
    biological function or structure for the purpose of treating or
    mitigating an illness or injury or a symptom of an illness or
    injury. ([instrument thérapeutique actif]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[applicable requirements of sections 10 to 20]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicable requirements of sections 10 to
    20]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a decorative contact lens, the
      requirements set out in section 10, subsections 11(2) and 12(2)
      and sections 13 to 17; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any other medical device, the
      requirements set out in section 10, subsections 11(1) and 12(1)
      and sections 13 to 20. ([exigences applicables prévues aux
      articles 10 à 20]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bar code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bar code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a unique bar code in the
    symbology of the Universal Product Code (UPC), the Health Industry
    Business Communications Council (HIBCC) or the European Article
    Number (EAN), assigned to a medical device by the
    manufacturer. ([code à barres]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[body orifice]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[body orifice]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a natural opening or a
    permanent artificial opening in the body, such as a stoma. ([orifice
    du corps]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Saturday; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a Sunday or other holiday. ([jour
      ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[central cardiovascular system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[central cardiovascular system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    heart, pericardium, pulmonary veins, pulmonary arteries, cardiac
    veins, coronary arteries, common carotid arteries, cerebral
    arteries, brachiocephalic artery, aorta, inferior and superior vena
    cava, renal arteries, iliac arteries and femoral arteries. ([système
    cardiovasculaire central]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[central nervous system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[central nervous system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the brain,
    meninges, spinal cord and cerebrospinal fluid. ([système nerveux
    central]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[closed-loop system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[closed-loop system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a medical
    device, means a system that enables the device to sense, interpret
    and treat a medical condition without human intervention. ([système
    à boucle fermée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commissioner of Patents]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commissioner of Patents]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Commissioner of Patents appointed under subsection 4(1) of the
    Patent Act. ([commissaire aux brevets]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[control number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[control number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a unique series of
    letters, numbers or symbols, or any combination of these, that is
    assigned to a medical device by the manufacturer and from which a
    history of the manufacture, packaging, labelling and distribution of
    a unit, lot or batch of the device can be determined. ([numéro de
    contrôle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[custom-made device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[custom-made device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a medical device,
    other than a mass-produced medical device, that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is manufactured in accordance with a health care
      professional's written direction giving its design
      characteristics;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} differs from medical devices generally available
      for sale or from a dispenser; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the sole use of a particular patient of
        that professional, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for use by that professional to meet special
        needs arising in the course of his or her practice. ([instrument
        fait sur mesure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[decorative contact lens]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[decorative contact lens]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device
    referred to in section 2.1 of the Act. ([lentilles cornéennes à but
    esthétique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dental material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dental material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2002-190, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[directions for use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[directions for use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a medical
    device, means full information as to the procedures recommended for
    achieving the optimum performance of the device, and includes
    cautions, warnings, contra-indications and possible adverse
    effects. ([mode d'emploi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dispenser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dispenser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is a member of
    a professional governing body and who is entitled, by virtue of
    their membership in that body, to manufacture or adapt a medical
    device in accordance with a health care professional's written
    directions in order to meet the specific requirements of a
    patient. ([préparateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[General Council Decision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[General Council Decision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
    assigned by subsection 30(6) of the Act. ([décision du Conseil
    général]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[genetic testing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[genetic testing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the analysis of DNA,
    RNA or chromosomes for purposes such as the prediction of disease or
    vertical transmission risks, or monitoring, diagnosis or
    prognosis. ([test génétique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[health care facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[health care facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a facility that
    provides diagnostic or therapeutic services to patients. It includes
    a group of such facilities that report to one common management that
    has responsibility for the activities carried out in those
    facilities. ([établissement de santé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[health care professional]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[health care professional]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
    is entitled under the laws of a province to provide health services
    in the province. ([professionnel de la santé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[identifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[identifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a unique series of letters
    or numbers or any combination of these or a bar code that is
    assigned to a medical device by the manufacturer and that identifies
    it and distinguishes it from similar
    devices. ([identificateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[implant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[implant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a medical device that is
    listed in Schedule 2. ([implant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[invasive device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[invasive device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a medical device that
    is intended to come into contact with the surface of the eye or
    penetrate the body, either through a body orifice or through the
    body surface. ([instrument effractif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[[in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} diagnostic device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[[in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} diagnostic
    device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[IVDD]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    medical device that is intended to be used [in vitro]{.otherLang
    lang="la"} for the examination of specimens taken from the
    body. ([instrument diagnostique [in vitro]{.otherLang
    lang="la"}]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou [IDIV]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who sells a
    medical device under their own name, or under a trademark, design,
    trade name or other name or mark owned or controlled by the person,
    and who is responsible for designing, manufacturing, assembling,
    processing, labelling, packaging, refurbishing or modifying the
    device, or for assigning to it a purpose, whether those tasks are
    performed by that person or on their
    behalf. ([fabricant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[medical device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[medical device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device within the
    meaning of the Act, but does not include any device that is intended
    for use in relation to animals. ([instrument
    médical]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[medical device family]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[medical device family]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a group of
    medical devices that are made by the same manufacturer, that differ
    only in shape, colour, flavour or size, that have the same design
    and manufacturing process and that have the same intended
    use. ([famille d'instruments]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[medical device group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[medical device group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a medical device
    comprising a collection of medical devices, such as a procedure pack
    or tray, that is sold under a single name. ([ensemble
    d'instruments]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[medical device group family]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[medical device group family]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    collection of medical device groups that are made by the same
    manufacturer, that have the same generic name specifying their
    intended use, and that differ only in the number and combination of
    products that comprise each group. ([famille d'ensembles
    d'instruments]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[name of the device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[name of the device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a medical
    device, includes any information necessary for the user to identify
    the device and to distinguish it from similar devices. ([nom de
    l'instrument]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[near patient [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} diagnostic device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[near patient [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} diagnostic
    device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[near patient
    IVDD]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"}
    diagnostic device that is intended for use outside a laboratory, for
    testing at home or at the point of care, such as a pharmacy, a
    health care professional's office or the bedside. ([instrument
    diagnostique clinique [in vitro]{.otherLang
    lang="la"}]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[objective evidence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[objective evidence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means information that
    can be proved true, based on facts obtained through observation,
    measurement, testing or other means, as set out in the definition

    International Organization for Standardization standard ISO

    amended from time to time. ([preuve tangible]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a partnership and an

[[qualified investigator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualified investigator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is

    province and who is designated, by the ethics committee of the
    health care facility at which investigational testing is to be
    conducted, as the person to conduct the testing. ([chercheur
    compétent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[quality management system certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[quality management system certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    32(2)(f), 3(j) or (4)(p), as applicable, that is issued by a

    32.1. ([certificat de système de gestion de la
    qualité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[quality system certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[recall]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[recall]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a recall ordered by the Minister under section
      21.3 of the Act; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any action taken by a manufacturer, importer or
      distributor of a medical device, after the device has been sold,
      to recall or correct the device, or to notify its owners and users
      of its defectiveness or potential defectiveness, after becoming
      aware that the device

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} may present a risk of injury to health,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} may fail to conform to any claim made by the
        manufacturer or importer relating to its effectiveness,
        benefits, performance characteristics or safety, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} may not meet the requirements of the Act or

[[regulatory agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the intended use of the device, including any new

      lang="fr"})

[[surgical or dental instrument]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[test kit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021401 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sale and advertising for sale of a medical
  device; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the importation of a medical device for sale or for
  use on individuals, other than importation for personal use.

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
these Regulations apply to a medical device that is a drug referred to
in paragraph (b) or (c) of the definition
[[biocide]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 1(1) of the [Biocides
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-110).

  ajax-after="1457115-1531296.html"}

  product were an [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} diagnostic device.

## [Classification of Medical Devices]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021414 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Medical devices are
classified into one of Classes I to IV by means of the classification
rules set out in Schedule 1, where Class I represents the lowest risk
and Class IV represents the highest risk.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If a medical device can be
classified into more than one class, the class representing the higher
risk applies.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021417 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1021418 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to
medical devices that are not subject to Part 2 or 3.

### [Manufacturer's Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1021420 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  manufacturer shall ensure that the medical device meets the applicable
  requirements of sections 10 to 20.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A manufacturer shall keep objective evidence to
  establish that the medical device meets those requirements.

### [Safety and Effectiveness Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1021426 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A medical device shall be
designed and manufactured to be safe, and to this end the manufacturer
shall, in particular, take reasonable measures to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} identify the risks inherent in the device;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the risks can be eliminated, eliminate them;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the risks cannot be eliminated,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} reduce the risks to the extent possible,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} provide for protection appropriate to those risks,
    including the provision of alarms, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} provide, with the device, information relative to
    the risks that remain; and

  is that in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} adverse effects;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} problems referred to in paragraph 57(1)(a);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} incidents referred to in subsection 59(1); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} serious risks of injury to human health that are
    relevant to the safety of the medical device and are referred to in
    subsection 61.2(2).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The summary report shall contain a concise critical
  analysis of the information referred to in subsection (2).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} In preparing the summary report, the holder shall
  determine, on the basis of the critical analysis, whether what is
  known about the benefits and risks associated with the medical device
  has changed in any of the following ways:

    of the device may be less;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of each of the risks,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the harm associated with the risk occurs, the

      persons could be more serious; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a new risk has been identified.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The holder shall include the conclusions they reach
  under subsection (4) in the summary report.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If, in preparing the summary report, the holder
  concludes that what is known about the benefits and risks associated
  with the medical device has changed in any of the ways referred to in
  paragraphs (4)(a) to (c), they shall notify the Minister, in writing,
  within 72 hours after having reached the conclusion, unless that has
  already been done.

#### [Cancellation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1391540 .Subheading}

- **[[68.21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68.21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may cancel an
  authorization for a medical device by written notice to the holder,
  giving reasons, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the holder has contravened these Regulations or any provision of the
    Act relating to medical devices;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the risks associated with the device outweigh the benefits
    associated with it, having regard to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the uncertainties relating to those benefits and
      risks, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the urgent public health need for the device or
      the absence of any such need;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the holder does not have a quality management system in place that
    is adequate to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} control the quality and, if applicable, the
      purity and sterility of the device and the materials used in the
      manufacture of the device, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the device meets the specifications
      of the device;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the health or safety of patients, users or other persons may be
    unduly affected by the device;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the holder has failed to comply with the terms and conditions of the
    authorization;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an authorization issued in respect
    of an application submitted on the basis of subsection 68.11(4), the
    Minister becomes aware that the authorization or licence for sale of
    the device that had been issued by the regulatory agency is
    suspended or revoked;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the holder has not submitted to the Minister all
    information, documents or material that was requested under section
    68.23 to enable the Minister to determine whether to cancel the
    authorization, within the time limit specified in the request;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a Class I device that is not a UPHN
    medical device,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the holder does not, within 120 days after the
      day on which the device ceases to be a UPHN medical device, submit
      an application under section 45 for an establishment licence that
      would authorize the holder to sell or import the device,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Minister refuses to issue an establishment
      licence to the holder under section 47, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the holder withdraws the application referred
      to in subparagraph (i);

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a Class II device that is not a UPHN
    medical device,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the holder does not, within 120 days after the
      day on which the device ceases to be a UPHN medical device,
      provide the Minister with a copy of a contract, signed by both the
      holder and a registrar recognized by the Minister under section
      32.1, that meets the following conditions:

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the contract indicates that the holder has
        begun the certification process to determine whether they have a
        quality management system that satisfies the standard referred
        to in paragraph 32(2)(f), and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the contract is in effect when that period
        expires, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the holder does not, within two years after the
      day on which the device ceases to be a UPHN medical device,
      provide the Minister with a copy of the quality management system
      certificate referred to in paragraph 32(2)(f); or

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a Class III or IV device that is not
    a UPHN medical device,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the holder does not, within 120 days after the
      day on which the device ceases to be a UPHN medical device,
      provide the Minister with a copy of a contract, signed by both the
      holder and a registrar recognized by the Minister under section
      32.1, that meets the following conditions:

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the contract indicates that the holder has
        begun the certification process to determine whether they have a
        quality management system that satisfies the standard referred
        to in paragraph 32(3)(j) or (4)(p), as the case may be, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the contract is in effect when that period
        expires, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the holder does not, within two years after the
      day on which the device ceases to be a UPHN medical device,
      provide the Minister with a copy of the quality management system
      certificate referred to in paragraph 32(3)(j) or (4)(p), as the
      case may be.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} If a medical device ceases to be a UPHN medical
  device after an application for an authorization is submitted under
  section 68.11 but before the Minister issues the authorization under
  section 68.12, the reference to "the device ceases to be a UPHN
  medical device" in subparagraph (1)(h)(i), (i)(i) or (j)(i), as the
  case may be, shall be read as "the Minister issued the authorization".

- [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1.1) ceases to apply in respect of the
  medical device if the device is authorized in relation to a medical
  condition that qualifies it as a UPHN medical device.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A ground for cancellation specified in subparagraph
  (1)(i)(i) or (j)(i) does not apply if the holder has already provided
  the Minister with a copy of the quality management system certificate
  referred to in paragraph 32(2)(f), (3)(j) or (4)(p), as the case may
  be, or a copy of the contract referred to in the applicable
  subparagraph.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A ground for cancellation specified in subparagraph
  (1)(i)(ii) or (j)(ii) does not apply if the holder has already
  provided the Minister with a copy of the quality management system
  certificate referred to in paragraph 32(2)(f), (3)(j) or (4)(p), as
  the case may be.

- **[[68.31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68.31
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The holder of an authorization for a

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} adverse effects;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} problems referred to in paragraph 57(1)(a);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} incidents referred to in subsection 68.27(1); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} serious risks of injury to human health that are
    relevant to the safety of the medical device and are referred to in
    subsection 68.3(1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The summary report shall contain a concise critical
  analysis of the information referred to in subsection (2).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} In preparing the summary report, the holder shall
  determine, on the basis of the critical analysis, whether what is
  known about the benefits and risks associated with the medical device
  has changed in any of the following ways:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the potential benefits for patients through the use
    of the device may be less;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of each of the risks,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the harm associated with the risk is more likely
      to occur, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the harm associated with the risk occurs, the
      consequences for the health or safety of patients, users or other
      persons could be more serious; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a new risk has been identified.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The holder shall include the conclusions they reach
  under subsection (4) in the summary report.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If, in preparing the summary report, the holder
  concludes that what is known about the benefits and risks associated
  with the medical device has changed in any of the ways referred to in
  paragraphs (4)(a) to (c), they shall notify the Minister, in writing,
  within 72 hours after having reached the conclusion, unless that has
  already been done.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Custom-Made Devices and Medical Devices to Be Imported or Sold for Special Access]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021921 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1021922 .Subheading}

- **[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Part applies to custom-made devices and medical devices that are to be
  imported or sold for special access.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Part, [[special access]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means access to a medical device for emergency use or if conventional
  therapies have failed, are unavailable or are unsuitable.

### [Authorization]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1021928 .Subheading}

- **[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  health care professional wishes to obtain a medical device referred to
  in section 70, the professional shall apply to the Minister for an
  authorization that would permit the manufacturer or importer of the
  device to sell, or to import and sell, the device to that
  professional.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application shall contain the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the device, its class and its
    identifier, including the identifier of any medical device that is
    part of a system, test kit, medical device group, medical device
    family or medical device group family;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of units required;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the manufacturer or
    importer;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, title and telephone number of the
    representative of the manufacturer or importer to contact for any
    information concerning the device;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the diagnosis, treatment or prevention for which
    the device is required;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statement that sets out

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the reasons the device was chosen for the
      diagnosis, treatment or prevention,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the risks and benefits that are associated with
      its use, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the reasons the diagnosis, treatment or
      prevention could not be accomplished using

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a licensed medical device that is available for
        sale in Canada, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a medical device for which the manufacturer of
        that device holds an authorization issued under section 68.12
        and that is available for sale in Canada;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of each health care facility
    at which the device is to be used by that professional;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the known safety and effectiveness information in
    respect of the device;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a written undertaking by the health care
    professional that the professional will inform the patient for whom
    the device is intended of the risks and benefits associated with its
    use;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the directions for use, unless directions are not
    required for the device to be used safely and effectively; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a custom-made device, a copy of the
    health care professional's written direction to the manufacturer
    giving the design characteristics of the device.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Medical Devices for Investigational Testing Involving Human Subjects]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021976 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1021977 .Subheading}

**[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to
medical devices that are to be imported or sold for investigational
testing involving human subjects.

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1021979 .Subheading}

- **[[80]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  no person shall import or sell a medical device for investigational
  testing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A manufacturer or importer of a Class II, III or IV
  medical device may sell the device to a qualified investigator for the
  purpose of conducting investigational testing if the manufacturer or
  importer holds an authorization issued under subsection 83(1) and
  possesses records that contain all the information and documents
  required by section 81.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A manufacturer or importer of a Class I medical
  device may sell the device to a qualified investigator for the purpose
  of conducting investigational testing if the manufacturer or importer
  possesses records that contain all the information and documents
  required by section 81.

### [Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1021984 .Subheading}

**[[81]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-81 .sectionLabel}** The records referred to
in section 80 shall contain the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the
  manufacturer and the importer of the device;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the device, its class and its identifier,
  including the identifier of any medical device that is part of a
  system, test kit, medical device group, medical device family or
  medical device group family;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the device and of the materials used
  in its manufacture and packaging;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the features of the device that
  permit it to be used for the medical conditions, purposes and uses for
  which it is manufactured, sold or represented;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a list of the countries other than Canada where the
  device has been sold, the total number of units sold in those
  countries, and a summary of any reported problems with the device and
  any recalls of the device in those countries;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a risk assessment comprising an analysis and
  evaluation of the risks, and the risk reduction measures adopted for
  the purposes of conducting investigational testing of the device,
  including, as appropriate,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the results of any previous research, testing and
    studies conducted with respect to the device,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the methods currently used to
    diagnose or treat the medical condition in respect of which the
    investigational testing is being proposed, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} information respecting any cautions, warnings,
    contra-indications and possible adverse effects associated with the
    use of the device;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the names of all the qualified investigators to whom
  the device is proposed to be sold and their qualifications, including
  their training and experience;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of each institution at which the
  investigational testing is proposed to be conducted and, in the case
  of a Class III or IV device, written approval from the institution
  indicating that the investigational testing may be carried out there;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a protocol of the proposed investigational testing,
  including the number of units of the device proposed to be used for
  the testing, the hypothesis for and objective of the testing, the
  period of time during which the testing will be carried out and a copy
  of the patient consent form;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the device label; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} a written undertaking from each qualified
  investigator to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} conduct the investigational testing in accordance
    with the protocol provided by the manufacturer,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} inform a patient who is to be diagnosed or treated
    using the device of any risks and benefits associated with its use,
    and obtain the patient's written consent for its use,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} not use the device or permit it to be used for
    any purpose other than the investigational testing specified in the
    protocol,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} not permit the device to be used by any other
    person except under the direction of the qualified investigator, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in the event of an incident involving the device
    and that meets the following conditions, report the incident and the
    circumstances surrounding it to the Minister and to the manufacturer
    or importer of the device within 72 hours after the qualified
    investigator becomes aware of the incident:

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the incident is related to a failure of the
      device or a deterioration in its effectiveness or any inadequacy
      in its labelling or in its directions for use, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the incident has led to the death or a serious
      deterioration in the state of health of a patient, user or other
      person or could do so were the incident to recur.

### [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Medical Devices other than in Vitro Diagnostic Devices]{.HTitleText1} {#part-1medical-devices-other-than-in-vitro-diagnostic-devices .SchedHeadL1}

#### [Invasive Devices]{.HTitleText2} {#invasive-devices .SchedHeadL2}

*Rule 1*:

- [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrules (2) and (3), all surgically
  invasive devices are classified as Class II.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A surgically invasive device that is intended to
  diagnose, monitor, control or correct a defect of the central
  cardiovascular system or the central nervous system or of a fetus in
  utero is classified as Class IV.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A surgically invasive device that is intended to be
  absorbed by the body, or that is normally intended to remain in the
  body for at least 30 consecutive days, is classified as Class III.

*Rule 2*:

- [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrules (2) to (4), all invasive devices
  that penetrate the body through a body orifice or that come into
  contact with the surface of the eye are classified as Class II.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A device described in subrule (1) that is intended to
  be placed in the oral or nasal cavities as far as the pharynx or in
  the ear canal up to the ear drum is classified as Class I.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A device described in subrule (1) that is normally
  intended to remain in the body or in contact with the surface of the
  eye for at least 30 consecutive days is classified as Class III.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A device described in subrule (1) that is intended to
  be represented as preventing the transmission of infectious agents
  during sexual activities or reducing the risk thereof is classified as
  Class III.

*Rule 3*:

Despite rules 1 and 2

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all denture materials and orthodontic appliances, and
  their accessories, are classified as Class II;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all surgical or dental instruments are classified as
  Class I; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} all latex condoms are classified as Class II.

#### [Non-invasive Devices]{.HTitleText2} {#non-invasive-devices .SchedHeadL2}

*Rule 4*:

- [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (2), all non-invasive devices that
  are intended to come into contact with injured skin are classified as
  Class II.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A device described in subrule (1) that is intended to
  be used as a mechanical barrier, for compression or for absorption of
  exudations, is classified as Class I.

*Rule 5*:

A non-invasive device intended for channelling or storing gases,
liquids, tissues or body fluids for the purpose of introduction into the
body by means of infusion or other means of administration is classified
as Class II.

*Rule 6*:

- [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrules (2) and (3), a non-invasive
  device intended for modifying the biological or chemical composition
  of blood or other body fluids, or liquids, for the purpose of
  introduction into the body by means of infusion or other means of
  administration is classified as Class III.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A device described in subrule (1) whose
  characteristics are such that the modification process may introduce a
  foreign substance into the body that is potentially hazardous, taking
  into account the nature and quantity of the substance, is classified
  as Class IV.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A device described in subrule (1) that accomplishes
  the modification by centrifugation, gravity filtration or the exchange
  of gas or heat is classified as Class II.

*Rule 7*:

- [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (2), all other non-invasive
  devices are classified as Class I.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A device described in subrule (1) is classified as
  Class II if it is intended

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to act as a calibrator, tester or quality control
    support to another medical device; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to be connected to an active device that is
    classified as Class II, III or IV.

#### [Active Devices]{.HTitleText2} {#active-devices .SchedHeadL2}

*Rule 8*:

- [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrules (2) and (3), an active device
  intended to emit ionizing radiation, including any device or software
  intended to control or monitor such a device or directly influence its
  performance, is classified as Class III.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A device described in subrule (1) that is intended to
  be used in radiographic mode is classified as Class II.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subrule (2), an active device that is
  intended to be used for mammographies is classified as Class III.

*Rule 9*:

- [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrules (2) and (3), an active
  therapeutic device, including any dedicated software, intended to be
  used to administer or withdraw energy to or from the body is
  classified as Class II.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the administration or withdrawal by a device
  described in subrule (1) is potentially hazardous, taking into account
  the nature of the administration or withdrawal, the intensity of the
  energy and the part of the body concerned, the device is classified as
  Class III.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A device described in subrule (2) that is intended to
  control the treatment of a patient's condition through a closed loop
  system is classified as Class IV.

*Rule 10*:

- [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (2), an active diagnostic device,
  including any dedicated software, that supplies energy for the purpose
  of imaging or monitoring physiological processes is classified as
  Class II.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A device described in subrule (1) that is intended to
  be used to monitor, assess or diagnose a disease, a disorder, an
  abnormal physical state or a pregnancy, if erroneous readings could
  result in immediate danger, is classified as Class III.

*Rule 11*:

- [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrules (2) and (3), an active device,
  including any dedicated software, intended to administer drugs, body
  fluids or other substances to the body or withdraw them from the body
  is classified as Class II.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the administration or withdrawal by a device
  described in subrule (1) is potentially hazardous, taking into account
  the nature of the administration or withdrawal, the nature of the
  substance involved and the part of the body concerned, the device is
  classified as Class III.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A device described in subrule (2) that is intended to
  control the treatment of a patient's condition through a closed loop
  system is classified as Class IV.

*Rule 12*:

Any other active device is classified as Class I.

#### [Special Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#special-rules .SchedHeadL2}

*Rule 13*:

A medical device that is intended to be used for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} disinfecting or sterilizing blood, tissues or organs
  that are intended for transfusion or transplantation is classified as
  Class IV; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} disinfecting or sterilizing a medical device is
  classified as Class II.

*Rule 14*:

- [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (2), the following medical devices
  are classified as Class IV:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a medical device that is manufactured from or that
    incorporates human or animal cells or tissues or their derivatives;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a medical device that is manufactured from or that
    incorporates a product produced through the use of recombinant DNA
    technology.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A device described in subrule (1) that is intended to
  come into contact with intact skin only is classified as Class I.

*Rule 15*:

Any medical device that is a material intended to be sold to a health
care professional or dispenser for the specific purpose of configuration
or arrangement into a mould or shape to meet the needs of an individual
is classified in the class that applies to the finished medical device.

*Rule 16*:

Despite rules 1 to 15, a medical device set out in column 1 of an item
of the table to this rule is classified as the class set out in column 2
of that item.

### [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[In Vitro Diagnostic Devices]{.HTitleText1} {#part-2in-vitro-diagnostic-devices .SchedHeadL1}

#### [Use with respect to Transmissible Agents]{.HTitleText2} {#use-with-respect-to-transmissible-agents .SchedHeadL2}

*Rule 1*:

An IVDD that is intended to be used to detect the presence of, or
exposure to, a transmissible agent in blood, blood components, blood
derivatives, tissues or organs to assess their suitability for
transfusion or transplantation is classified as Class IV.

*Rule 2*:

An IVDD that is intended to be used to detect the presence of, or
exposure to, a transmissible agent is classified as Class II, unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is intended to be used to detect the presence of,
  or exposure to, a transmissible agent that causes a life-threatening
  disease if there is a risk of propagation in the Canadian population,
  in which case it is classified as Class IV; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it falls into one of the following categories, in
  which case it is classified as Class III:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is intended to be used to detect the presence
    of, or exposure to, a transmissible agent that causes a serious
    disease where there is a risk of propagation in the Canadian
    population,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it is intended to be used to detect the presence
    of, or exposure to, a sexually transmitted agent,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} it is intended to be used to detect the presence
    of an infectious agent in cerebrospinal fluid or blood, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} there is a risk that an erroneous result would
    cause death or severe disability to the individual being tested, or
    to the individual's offspring.

*Rule 3*:

An IVDD that is intended to be used for patient management is classified
as Class II, unless it falls into one of the following categories, in
which case it is classified as Class III:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is intended to be used for the management of
  patients suffering from a life-threatening disease; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is a risk that an erroneous result will lead to
  a patient management decision resulting in an imminent
  life-threatening situation for the patient.

#### [Other Uses]{.HTitleText2} {#other-uses .SchedHeadL2}

*Rule 4*:

An IVDD that is not subject to rules 1 to 3 and that is intended to be
used in diagnosis or patient management is classified as Class II,
unless it falls into one of the following categories, in which case it
is classified as Class III:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is intended to be used in screening for or in the
  diagnosis of cancer;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is intended to be used for genetic testing;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is intended to be used in screening for congenital
  disorders in the fetus;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} there is a risk that an erroneous diagnostic result
  would cause death or severe disability to the patient being tested or
  to that patient's offspring;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} it is intended to be used for disease staging; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} it is intended to be used to monitor levels of drugs,
  substances or biological components, if there is a risk that an
  erroneous result will lead to a patient management decision resulting
  in an imminent life-threatening situation for the patient.

*Rule 5*:

An IVDD that is intended to be used for blood grouping or tissue typing
to ensure the immunological compatibility of blood, blood components,
tissue or organs that are intended for transfusion or transplantation is
classified as Class III.

#### [Special Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#special-rules-1 .SchedHeadL2}

*Rule 6*:

A near patient IVDD is classified as Class III.

*Rule 7*:

In cases where an IVDD, including its analyzers, reagents and software,
is intended to be used with another IVDD, the class of both IVDDs will
be that of the IVDD in the class representing the higher risk.

*Rule 8*:

If rules 1 to 7 do not apply, all other IVDDs are classified as Class I.

*Rule 9*:

Despite rules 1 to 8, an IVDD set out in column 1 of an item of the
table to this rule is classified as the class set out in column 2 of
that item.
