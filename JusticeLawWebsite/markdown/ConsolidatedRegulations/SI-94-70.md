## Indians and Webequie Band on the Webequie Indian Settlement Remission Order {#indians-and-webequie-band-on-the-webequie-indian-settlement-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/94-70
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1994-06-01

Order Respecting the Remission of Certain Income Taxes Paid or Payable
by Indians and the Goods and Services Tax Paid or Payable by Indians or
by the Webequie Band on the Webequie Indian Settlement

P.C. 1994-800  1994-05-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, is pleased hereby, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to
subsection 23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-94-70_e_hq_6560){#fn_SI-94-70_e_hq_6560-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the
annexed [Order respecting the remission of certain income taxes paid or
payable by Indians and the goods and services tax paid or payable by
Indians or by the Webequie band on the Webequie Indian
Settlement](/eng/regulations/SI-94-70).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-648476 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Indians and Webequie Band on the Webequie Indian Settlement
Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-94-70).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-648478 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5); ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5); ([Indien]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5);
    ([réserve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Webequie Indian Settlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Webequie Indian Settlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    settlement that is situated on Winisk Lake, in the District of
    Kenora, Patricia Portion, in the Province of Ontario, at 52°, 59′
    latitude and 88°, 11′ longitude, that has an area of approximately
    27,195 hectares, including the airport lands at the Webequie
    Airport, and that is not a reserve. ([établissement indien de
    Webequie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Income Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-648484 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-648485 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of this Part, [[tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means tax under
  Parts I, I.1 and I.2 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 2, all other words and expressions
  used in this Part have the same meaning as in the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

### [Remission of Income Tax]{.HTitleText2} {#h-648489 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to a taxpayer who is an Indian in respect of the 1992 taxation year and
each taxation year following that year of the amount, if any, by which
the taxes, interest and penalties paid or payable by the taxpayer for
the taxation year under the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) exceed the
taxes, interest and penalties that would have been payable by the
taxpayer for the year under the Act if the Webequie Indian Settlement
were a reserve throughout the year.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Goods and Services Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-648491 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-648492 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of this Part, [[tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the goods and
  services tax imposed under Division II of Part IX of the [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 2, all other words and expressions
  used in this Part have the same meaning as in Part IX of the [Excise
  Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15).

### [Remission of the Goods and Services Tax]{.HTitleText2} {#h-648496 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 8,
remission is hereby granted to an individual who is an Indian and who is
the recipient of a taxable supply made on or after the day on which this
Order comes into force, of the tax paid or payable in an amount equal to
the amount, if any, by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax paid or payable by the individual

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the tax that would have been payable by the
  individual, if the Webequie Indian Settlement were a reserve.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 8,
remission is hereby granted to the Webequie band of the tax paid or
payable, where the band is the recipient of a taxable supply made on or
after January 1, 1992, in an amount equal to the amount, if any, by
which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax paid or payable by the band

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the tax that would have been payable by the band if
  the Webequie Indian Settlement were a reserve.

### [Condition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-648505 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Remission under sections 6
and 7 in respect of tax paid is granted on condition that an application
in writing for the remission be submitted to the Minister of National
Revenue within four years after the day on which the tax was paid.
