## Exposed and Processed Film and Recorded Video Tape Remission Order {#exposed-and-processed-film-and-recorded-video-tape-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 763
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of the Tax Imposed under Division III of
Part IX of the Excise Tax Act, Paid or Payable on Exposed and Processed
Film and Recorded Video Tape
::::

::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561658 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Exposed and Processed Film and Recorded Video Tape Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._763).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561660 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Canadian equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means equipment
    manufactured in Canada or imported into Canada and released and
    accounted for under the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); ([matériel
    canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canadian film]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian film]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means film manufactured in
    Canada or imported into Canada and released and accounted for under
    the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); ([film
    canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canadian video tape]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian video tape]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means video tape
    manufactured in Canada or imported into Canada and released and
    accounted for under the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); ([bande
    magnétoscopique canadienne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to an
    area or place, means the manager of the customs office or customs
    offices that serve that area or place; ([agent en chef des
    douanes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[customs duties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[customs duties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SI/88-18, s.
    2\]]{.Repealed}

[[Deputy Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Deputy Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Deputy Minister of
    National Revenue. ([sous-ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[duties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[duties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SI/98-12, s.
    2\]]{.Repealed}

[[regional collector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regional collector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SI/78-138, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who makes his home
    and is ordinarily resident in Canada. ([résident]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561673 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 7,
remission is hereby granted of the tax imposed under Division III of
Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), paid or payable on

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} non-commercial film that is exposed or processed
  while a resident is abroad, and one set of prints therefrom, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} non-commercial video tape that is recorded while a
  resident is abroad,

that are imported into Canada by the resident solely for personal use
and not for any business or occupational purpose and that, on the
resident's return to Canada, are in the actual possession of the
resident or form part of the baggage of the resident.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/86-198, s. 2
- SI/88-18, s. 2
- SI/89-249, s. 1
- SI/91-8, s. 2
- SI/98-12, s. 3
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 6 and
7, remission is hereby granted of the tax imposed under Division III of
Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), paid or payable on
importation into Canada of commercial Canadian film or commercial
Canadian video tape if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the film was exposed but not developed or processed
  abroad except to the extent necessary for testing the quality of the
  exposed film;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 5, the exposing of the film
  referred to in paragraph (a) or the recording of the tape was carried
  out solely by a resident or residents using only Canadian equipment;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the export from Canada of the film or video tape is
  verified by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the Canada Customs Export Report form
    completed in respect of the film or video tape certified by the
    chief officer of customs at the customs office where the goods were
    exported from Canada and bearing the notation thereon "not subject
    to drawback" or "non admissible au drawback",

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} customs accounting documents authenticated by an
    officer employed in the administration or enforcement of customs in
    the country to which the film or video tape was exported, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the shipping documents confirming the
    exportation of the film or video tape.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/78-138, s. 2
- SI/88-18, s. 2
- SI/91-8, s. 2
- SI/98-12, s. 4
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who is not a resident may be employed during the exposing of the film
  referred to in paragraph 4(a) or the recording of Canadian video tape
  if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is impracticable to employ a resident and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the person employed is a technical specialist, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the employment is of a casual nature; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person is required to be employed in order to
    comply with the law of the country in which the exposing of the film
    or the recording of the video tape is carried out.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Equipment, other than Canadian equipment, may be used
  during the exposing of the film referred to in paragraph 4(a) or the
  recording of Canadian video tape if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is impracticable to obtain Canadian equipment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the equipment used is a replacement for Canadian
    equipment that has broken during the exposing of the film or the
    recording of the video tape; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the equipment used is an aircraft, vessel or a
    vehicle rented in the country in which the exposing of the film or
    the recording of the video tape is carried out.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/78-138, s. 3
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No remission shall be
granted in respect of any goods described in section 4 where, due to
exportation, a refund or drawback or exemption of tax imposed under
Division III of Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) is
claimed or has been granted, except where the tax equal to the refund,
drawback or exemption have been paid.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
- SI/89-249, s. 2
- SI/98-12, s. 5
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No remission shall be
granted of tax imposed under Division III of Part IX of the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15), paid on any goods described in sections 3 and 4 at
the time of importation unless application therefor is made to the
Minister of National Revenue within two years after the date of
importation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
- SI/91-8, s. 2
- SI/98-12, s. 6
- SI/99-125, s. 1
:::
