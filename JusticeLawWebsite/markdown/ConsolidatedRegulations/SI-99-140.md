## Certain Commercial Salmon Fishing Licences Fees Remission Order {#certain-commercial-salmon-fishing-licences-fees-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/99-140
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1999-12-22

Certain Commercial Salmon Fishing Licences Fees Remission Order

P.C. 1999-2192  1999-12-10
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-99-140_e_hq_6454){#fn_SI-99-140_e_hq_6454-ID0EFDAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), considering that
it is in the public interest to do so, hereby remits to the holders of
the commercial salmon fishing licences set out below the fees that they
paid for those licences for 1999 under section 19 of the [Pacific
Fishery Regulations, 1993](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-54):

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-99-140_e_hq_6454-ID0EFDAA){#fn_SI-99-140_e_hq_6454}S.C.
  1991, c. 24, s. 7(2)
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Category A licence referred to in subitem 3(1)
  or (2) of Part I of Schedule II to the [Pacific Fishery Regulations,
  1993](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-54) that permits salmon fishing by
  trolling in Salmon Areas F, G or H, as those areas are defined in
  subsection 2(1) of those Regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a Category A licence referred to in those subitems
  that is subject to conditions specified under paragraph 22(1)(c) of
  the [Fishery (General) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-53) that
  permit salmon fishing by seine in Salmon Area B (consisting of Areas
  11 to 29 and Area 121, as described in Schedule II to the [Pacific
  Fishery Management Area Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-215)) or
  by gillnet in Salmon Area D (consisting of Areas 11 to 15 and Areas 23
  to 27, as described in that schedule) or Salmon Area E (consisting of
  Areas 16 to 22, 28, 29 and 121, as described in that schedule).
:::
