## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1451090 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Act,
2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1).

## [Additional Vaping Duty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1451093 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specified vaping provinces

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
definition [[specified vaping province]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
section 2 of the Act, the following provinces are prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Ontario;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Quebec;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} New Brunswick;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Manitoba;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} Prince Edward Island;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} Alberta;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} Yukon;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the Northwest Territories; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} Nunavut.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Section 158.58 of Act --- prescribed
circumstances

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
158.58 of the Act, a duty in respect of a specified vaping province is
imposed under that section on vaping products manufactured in Canada, or
imported, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of vaping products that are imported by
  an individual for their personal use, the individual is resident in
  the specified vaping province; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the vaping products are for
  consumption, use or sale to consumers in the specified vaping
  province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 158.6(2) of Act --- prescribed
circumstances

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 158.6(2) of the Act, a duty in respect of a specified vaping
province is imposed under that subsection on vaping products if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vaping products are not stamped; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the location of the vaping products at the particular
  time referred to in that subsection is in the specified vaping
  province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 158.61(2) of Act ---
prescribed circumstances

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 158.61(2) of the Act, a duty in respect of a specified vaping
province is imposed under that subsection on vaping products that cannot
be accounted for if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vaping products are not stamped;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the last known location of the vaping products before
  the particular time referred to in that subsection is in the specified
  vaping province; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the particular person referred to in that subsection
  is not convicted of an offence under section 218.2 of the Act in
  respect of the vaping products.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of additional vaping duty

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
158.58 and subsections 158.6(2) and 158.61(2) of the Act, the amount of
duty imposed under those provisions in respect of vaping products and a
specified vaping province is equal to the amount determined in respect
of the vaping products under Schedule 8 to the Act.

## [Transition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1451115 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}July 1 to September 30, 2024

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
facilitating the implementation of the [[coordinated vaping duty
system]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} (as defined in subsection 304.3(1) of the
Act), subparagraph 158.42(1)(a)(ii), subsection 158.44(2) and paragraphs
158.45(1)(c) and 158.46(d) of the Act do not apply before October 2024
in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} vaping products that are manufactured in Canada and
  that are stamped before July 2024;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} packaged vaping products that are imported by a
  vaping product licensee for stamping by the vaping product licensee
  and that are stamped before July 2024; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other imported vaping products that are imported
  or [[released]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} (as defined in subsection 2(1)
  of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)) before July 2024.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[qualifying vaping
product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection (2), [[qualifying vaping
  product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vaping product that is not
  stamped to indicate that additional vaping duty in respect of a
  specified vaping province has been paid and that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is manufactured in Canada and is stamped before
    January 2025;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is imported by a vaping product licensee for
    stamping by the vaping product licensee and is stamped before
    January 2025; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is imported otherwise than by a vaping product
    licensee for stamping by the vaping product licensee and is imported
    or [[released]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} (as defined in subsection 2(1)
    of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)) before January 2025.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}January 1 to March 31, 2025

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of facilitating the joining of New
  Brunswick, Manitoba, Prince Edward Island, Alberta and Yukon to the
  [[coordinated vaping duty system]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} (as defined
  in subsection 304.3(1) of the Act), the following rules apply:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subsection 158.44(2) of the Act does not apply
    before April 2025 in respect of qualifying vaping products that are
    disposed of, sold, offered for sale, purchased or possessed, as the
    case may be, in any of those specified vaping provinces;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 158.46(d) of the Act does not apply
    before April 2025 in respect of qualifying vaping products that are
    to be entered in the duty-paid market of any of those specified
    vaping provinces;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} section 158.58 of the Act does not apply in respect
    of vaping products if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of vaping products that are imported
      by an individual for their personal use, the vaping products are
      imported before January 2025 and the individual is resident in any
      of those specified vaping provinces, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the vaping products are
      qualifying vaping products that are for consumption, use or sale
      to consumers in any of those specified vaping provinces;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} subsection 158.6(2) of the Act does not apply in
    respect of vaping products if the particular time referred to in
    that subsection is before January 2025 and the vaping products are
    located in any of those specified vaping provinces at the particular
    time; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} subsection 158.61(2) of the Act does not apply in
    respect of vaping products if the particular time referred to in
    that subsection is before January 2025 and the last known location
    of the vaping products before the particular time is in any of those
    specified vaping provinces.
