## Order Transferring to the Department of National Revenue the Control and Supervision of that Portion of the Public Service in the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions known as the Pension Advice Section {#order-transferring-to-the-department-of-national-revenue-the-control-and-supervision-of-that-portion-of-the-public-service-in-the-office-of-the-superintendent-of-financial-institutions-known-as-the-pension-advice-section .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/96-31
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1996-04-17

Order Transferring to the Department of National Revenue the Control and
Supervision of that Portion of the Public Service in the Office of the
Superintendent of Financial Institutions known as the Pension Advice
Section

P.C. 1996-467 1996-03-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), is pleased
hereby to transfer to the Department of National Revenue the control and
supervision of that portion of the public service in the Office of the
Superintendent of Financial Institutions known as the Pension Advice
Section, effective April 1, 1996.
