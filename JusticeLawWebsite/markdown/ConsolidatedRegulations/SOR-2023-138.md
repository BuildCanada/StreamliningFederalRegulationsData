## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1406923 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Marine
Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7).

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1406926 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions of the Act and its regulations

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A provision of the Act or
its regulations that is set out in column 1 of a Part of Schedule 1 is
designated as a provision whose contravention may be proceeded with as a
violation in accordance with the Act.

## [Classification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1406929 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seriousness of violation

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A violation of a provision
set out in column 1 of a Part of Schedule 1 is classified as minor,
serious or very serious as set out in column 2 of that Part.

## [Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1406932 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amount --- individual

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  maximum amount of a penalty payable by an individual with respect to a
  violation of a provision set out in column 1 of a Part of Schedule 1
  is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$1,250 in the case of a minor violation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$2,500 in the case of a serious violation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$5,000 in the case of a very serious violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amount --- corporation or ship

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The maximum amount of a penalty payable by a
  corporation or ship with respect to a violation of a provision set out
  in column 1 of a Part of Schedule 1 is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$6,250 in the case of a minor violation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$12,500 in the case of a serious violation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$25,000 in the case of a very serious violation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation --- penalty

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of the penalty for each violation is to be determined by the
  formula

  ::: {#1406947 .Subsection}
  A + B + C + D -- E

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the baseline penalty amount as determined under section 6;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the history of non-compliance amount, if any, as determined
      under section 7;

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the harm amount, if any, as determined under section 8;

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the economic gain amount, if any, as determined under section
      9; and

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is the mitigating factor amount, if any, as determined under
      subsection (2).
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of mitigating factor
  amount

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The mitigating factor amount referred to in element E
  of the formula is obtained by multiplying the percentage determined
  under section 10 by the sum of elements A, B, C and D.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Baseline penalty amount

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The baseline penalty amount
applicable to a violation is the amount set out in column 2 of Part 1 of
Schedule 2, in the case of an individual, or Part 2 of that Schedule, in
the case of a corporation or ship, that corresponds to the seriousness
of the violation set out in column 1 of the applicable Part.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}History of non-compliance amount

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  violator has a history of non-compliance, the history of
  non-compliance amount applicable to a violation is the amount set out
  --- for the number of incidents of non-compliance --- in column 3 of
  Part 1 of Schedule 2, in the case of an individual, or Part 2 of that
  Schedule, in the case of a corporation or ship, that corresponds to
  the seriousness of the violation set out in column 1 of the applicable
  Part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}History of non-compliance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A violator has a history of non-compliance if, in the
  five years before the violation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a notice of violation was issued to them for a
    violation of the same provision or a similar provision of the Act or
    its regulations, and they

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} have paid the penalty for the violation under
      section 129.06 of the Act,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are deemed, under section 129.09 of the Act, to
      have committed the violation, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} were subject to a determination made under
      subsection 129.1(3) or 129.11(3) of the Act that they committed
      the violation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the violator was found guilty of an offence under
    the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Harm amount

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  violation results or may result in harm, the harm amount is the amount
  set out in column 4 of Part 1 of Schedule 2, in the case of an
  individual, or Part 2 of that Schedule, in the case of a corporation
  or ship, that corresponds to the seriousness of the violation set out
  in column 1 of the applicable Part, and that corresponds to the
  category of real or potential impact on the safety of persons and
  property and on the environment, as determined under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Category of impact

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The category of real or potential impact is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} weak, if the real or potential impact of the
    violation is trivial;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} moderate, if the real or potential impact of the
    violation is not trivial but there is no substantial or widespread
    impact on property or the environment and no injury to anyone; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} high, if the real or potential impact of the
    violation on property or the environment is substantial or
    widespread or an individual is injured.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Economic gain amount

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If, as a result of the
violation, the violator benefits from an economic gain or competitive
advantage, including by obtaining revenue, profit or income or avoiding
or delaying any cost, the economic gain amount is the amount set out in
column 5 of Part 1 of Schedule 2, in the case of an individual, or Part
2 of that Schedule, in the case of a corporation or ship, that
corresponds to the seriousness of the violation set out in column 1 of
the applicable Part.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mitigating factors amount

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The percentage that is to
be used to calculate the mitigating factor amount is the percentage set
out in column 6 of Part 1 of Schedule 2, in the case of an individual,
or Part 2 of that Schedule, in the case of a corporation or ship, that
corresponds to the seriousness of the violation set out in column 1 of
the applicable Part and is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 20%, if the violator voluntarily discloses the
  violation to an enforcement officer; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 10%, if the violator cooperates with the enforcement
  officer or has taken voluntary measures to mitigate the harm caused or
  the harm that could have been caused by the violation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing violation

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** If an "X" is set out in
column 3 of a Part of Schedule 1, a violation of a provision set out in
column 1 constitutes a separate violation for each day on which it is
continued.

## [Service]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1406984 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Methods of service

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** An enforcement officer
may serve a notice of violation

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by leaving a copy of it with the individual or, if
    the individual cannot conveniently be found, with someone who
    appears to be an adult member of the same household at the last
    known address or usual place of residence of the individual, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of it by registered mail,
    courier or fax or other electronic means to the individual's last
    known address or usual place of residence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of it by registered mail, courier
    or fax or other electronic means to the head office or place of
    business of the corporation, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by leaving a copy of it with an officer or other
    individual who appears to direct or control the head office or place
    of business of the corporation or with the corporation's agent or
    mandatary; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a ship,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by leaving a copy of it with the owner, master or
    any individual who is, or appears to be, in charge of the ship, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by fixing a copy of it to a prominent part of the
    ship.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Service may be proven by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service in person, a certificate of
  service, in a form approved by the Minister and signed by the person
  who serves the notice of violation, and that indicates the name of the
  person receiving the notice and the means by which and day on which
  service was effected;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service by fax or other electronic
  means, a record of electronic transmission that sets out the date and
  time of transmission; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, an acknowledgment of service
  signed by or on behalf of the person served, that specifies the date,
  place and means of service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date of service

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Service is effective

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a certificate of service, on the day
  indicated on the certificate;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service by fax or other electronic
  means, on the day indicated on the record of transmission; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service by registered mail or courier,
  on the fourth day after the day on which a copy of the notice of
  violation is sent, as indicated on the receipt issued by the postal or
  courier service.

## [Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1407006 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment options

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  payment of a penalty required under these Regulations must be made
  within 30 days after the day on which the notice of violation is
  served.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Method of payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The payment must be made by means of credit card, or
  a certified cheque or money order made payable to the Receiver General
  for Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed date of payment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The payment is deemed to have been made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the date indicated on the receipt, if the
    payment is made in person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the date indicated on the postmark stamped on
    the envelope, if the amount is sent by regular mail;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the date indicated on the receipt issued by the
    postal or courier service, if the payment is sent by registered mail
    or courier; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} on the date the transaction was processed, if the
    payment was made by credit card.
