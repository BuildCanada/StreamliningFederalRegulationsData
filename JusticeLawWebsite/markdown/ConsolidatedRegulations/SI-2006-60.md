## Order Respecting the Withdrawal from Disposal of Certain Lands in Nunavut (The Nunavik Marine Region, Nunavut) {#order-respecting-the-withdrawal-from-disposal-of-certain-lands-in-nunavut-the-nunavik-marine-region-nunavut .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-60
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 2006-04-19

Order Respecting the Withdrawal from Disposal of Certain Lands in
Nunavut (The Nunavik Marine Region, Nunavut)

P.C. 2006-190 2006-04-06
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 23(a) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), hereby
makes the annexed [Order Respecting the Withdrawal from Disposal of
Certain Lands in Nunavut (The Nunavik Marine Region,
Nunavut)](/eng/regulations/SI-2006-60).
:::

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612856 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Order
is to withdraw certain lands in Nunavut from disposal to facilitate the
settlement of the Nunavik Inuit Marine Region Agreement-in-Principle,
Nunavut.

## [Lands Withdrawn from Disposal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612858 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The tracts of territorial
lands set out in the schedule, including the surface and subsurface
rights to the islands that are within the Nunavik Marine Region, are
withdrawn from disposal for the period beginning on the day on which
this Order is made and ending on September 30, 2008.

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612860 .Part}

### [Disposition of Substances or Materials]{.HTitleText2} {#h-612861 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Section 2 does not apply to
the disposition of substances or materials under the [Territorial
Quarrying Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1527).

### [Existing Rights and Interests]{.HTitleText2} {#h-612863 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
section 2 does not apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the locating of a mineral claim by the holder of a
  prospecting permit granted before the day on which this Order is made;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the recording of a mineral claim that is referred to
  in paragraph (a) or that was located before the day on which this
  Order is made;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the granting of a lease under the Canada Mining
  Regulations to a person with a recorded claim if the lease covers an
  area within the recorded claim;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the issuance of a significant discovery licence under
  the [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5) to a holder of
  an exploration licence that was issued before the day on which this
  Order is made if the significant discovery licence covers an area
  subject to the exploration licence;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the issuance of a production licence under the
  [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5) to a holder of a
  significant discovery licence that is referred to in paragraph (d) if
  the production licence covers an area subject to the significant
  discovery licence;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the issuance of a production licence under the
  [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5) to a holder of an
  exploration licence or a significant discovery licence that was issued
  before the day on which this Order is made if the production licence
  covers an area subject to the exploration licence or the significant
  discovery licence;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the granting of a surface lease under the
  [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7) to a holder of a recorded claim
  under the Canada Mining Regulations or of an interest under the
  [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5) if the surface lease
  is required to allow the holder to exercise rights under the claim or
  interest; or

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the renewal of an interest.
