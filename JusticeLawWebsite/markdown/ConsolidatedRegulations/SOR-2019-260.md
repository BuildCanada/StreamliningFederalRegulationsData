[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Physical injuries

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
definition [[serious injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection
45.79(1) of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10), the
following physical injuries --- that are not transient or trifling in
nature and for which medical attention was received from a person who is
entitled to practice medicine or practice as a nurse under the laws of a
province --- are prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any physical injury that results in

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a substantial loss or impairment of mobility of the
    body,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a loss of function, in whole or in part, of any
    limb, organ or other body member,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a loss of vision or hearing in whole or in part,
    or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a substantial disfigurement;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fracture of a limb, a rib, a vertebra or the
  skull; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any burns, cuts or lacerations to a major portion of
  the body.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
