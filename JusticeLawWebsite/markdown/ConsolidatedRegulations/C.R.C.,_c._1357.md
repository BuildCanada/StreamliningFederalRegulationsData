## Counting of Service by Former Members of the Senate or House of Commons Regulations {#counting-of-service-by-former-members-of-the-senate-or-house-of-commons-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1357
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE SUPERANNUATION ACT](/eng/acts/P-36)

Regulations Respecting the Counting of Service by Former Members of the
Senate or House of Commons
::::

::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-504109 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Counting of Service by Former Members of the Senate or
House of Commons Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1357).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-504111 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contributor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contributor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is a
    contributor under the Act; ([contributeur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the President of the Treasury
    Board. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Election]{.HTitleText1} {#h-504116 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A contributor may elect to
count a period of service in respect of which he contributed under the
[Members of Parliament Retiring Allowances Act](/eng/acts/M-5) as
pensionable service for the purposes of Part I of the Act if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} he elects to do so in a form prescribed by the
  Minister; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a contributor who is entitled to an
  allowance under the [Members of Parliament Retiring Allowances
  Act](/eng/acts/M-5) for service in respect of which he made
  contributions, he surrenders his rights to that allowance.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A contributor who makes an
election pursuant to section 3 shall, for the purposes of Part I of the
Act, be credited with a period of pensionable service equal to the
aggregate of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the periods of pensionable service to his credit
  pursuant to subsection 24(2) of the [Members of Parliament Retiring
  Allowances Act](/eng/acts/M-5) at the time he ceased to be a member of
  the Senate or House of Commons; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the period of pensionable service in respect of which
  he made contributions under Part I or VI of the [Members of Parliament
  Retiring Allowances Act](/eng/acts/M-5) that has not been counted for
  the purpose of establishing the periods of pensionable service
  referred to in paragraph (a).

## [Medical Examination]{.HTitleText1} {#h-504123 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  contributor who elects within one year of becoming a contributor to
  count pensionable service pursuant to section 3 shall be medically
  examined unless he has, during the five-year period immediately prior
  to the election, been employed without interruption in the Public
  Service or as a member of the regular force, the Force, the Senate or
  House of Commons or in any combination of those employments.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A contributor who elects after one year of becoming a
  contributor to count pensionable service pursuant to section 3 shall
  be medically examined.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), a contributor who
  elects to count pensionable service pursuant to section 3 and to whom
  paragraph 3(b) applies shall be medically examined.

<!-- -->

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  medical examination referred to in section 5 shall be conducted by a
  physician licensed or otherwise authorized to practise medicine in the
  jurisdiction in which the examination is performed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A medical examination referred to in section 5 shall
  provide the information required by, and be reported in accordance
  with, Department of National Health and Welfare Form NHW 365 or
  NHW-SNBS 366.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The form referred to in subsection (2) shall, on
  completion, be submitted to the Medical Services Branch of the
  Department of National Health and Welfare.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-624, s. 1
:::

- **[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a medical
  examination required by section 5 shall be performed not more than
  three months before, nor more than six months after, the making of the
  election.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a person without fault fails to undergo a
  medical examination required by section 5 within the time set out in
  subsection (1), the person may undergo the medical examination within
  30 days after being notified by the Minister that an examination is
  required.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person shall be determined to have passed a medical
  examination required by section 5 where the Minister receives a
  report, prepared by the Assistant Deputy Minister of the Medical
  Services Branch of the Department of National Health and Welfare from
  the information contained in the form submitted pursuant to section 6,
  stating that the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is capable of five years of continuous employment
    performing the duties that the contributor was performing at the
    time of the medical examination; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a normal life expectancy.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Assistant Deputy Minister of the Medical
  Services Branch determines that the information contained in a form
  submitted pursuant to section 6 is insufficient to enable a report in
  respect of a person to be prepared under subsection (3), the Assistant
  Deputy Minister may require the person to undergo a further medical
  examination in accordance with section 6, to obtain the necessary
  information.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person who, in the circumstances referred to in
  subsection (4), does not undergo a further medical examination
  required by that subsection shall be determined not to have been
  medically examined in accordance with section 5.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-624, s. 1
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The election of a
contributor who fails to pass a medical examination required by
subsection 5(2) or (3) is void.

## [Payments to the Superannuation Account]{.HTitleText1} {#h-504145 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A contributor who makes an
election pursuant to section 3 and to whom paragraph 3(b) applies shall
pay

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the Superannuation Account, on the same basis had
  he continued to pay in accordance with the payment plan he elected
  under the [Members of Parliament Retiring Allowances
  Act](/eng/acts/M-5), an amount equal to the amount he had elected to
  contribute

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to section 7 of that Act minus the amount
    of contributions he paid pursuant to section 9 of that Act, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to section 19 of that Act minus the
    amount of contributions he paid pursuant to section 20 of that Act,

  as the case may be;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the Members of Parliament Retiring Allowances
  Account an amount equal to the amount of any allowance paid to him
  pursuant to the [Members of Parliament Retiring Allowances
  Act](/eng/acts/M-5) for any period commencing in any month commencing
  after he has been a contributor under the Act for one year together
  with simple interest at four per cent per annum; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to the Supplementary Retirement Benefits Account an
  amount equal to the amount of any supplementary retirement benefit
  paid to him pursuant to the [Supplementary Retirement Benefits
  Act](/eng/acts/S-24) in respect of an allowance paid to him pursuant
  to the [Members of Parliament Retiring Allowances Act](/eng/acts/M-5)
  for any period commencing in any month commencing after he has been a
  contributor under the Act for one year together with simple interest
  at four per cent per annum.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A contributor who makes an
election pursuant to section 3 and to whom paragraph 3(b) does not apply
shall pay to the Superannuation Account an amount calculated

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with paragraph 6(1)(e) of the Act, if
  he elects within one year of becoming a contributor, or from December
  23, 1976, whichever is later; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with paragraph 6(1)(j) of the Act, if
  he elects at a later date.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Where a contributor makes
an election pursuant to section 3, there shall be transferred to the
Superannuation Account

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all amounts paid by the contributor to the Members of
  Parliament Retiring Allowances Account less any amount paid to him as
  a withdrawal allowance under the [Members of Parliament Retiring
  Allowances Act](/eng/acts/M-5); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all amounts credited to that Account pursuant to
  section 4 of that Act in respect of the contributor.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Any amount required to be
paid by a contributor pursuant to these Regulations other than paragraph
8(a) shall be paid by him

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a lump sum at the time of making the election; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in instalments computed on such terms as are
  prescribed by section 5 of the [Public Service Superannuation
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1358).
