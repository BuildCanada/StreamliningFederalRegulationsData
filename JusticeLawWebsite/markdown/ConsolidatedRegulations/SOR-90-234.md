## Advance Income Tax Ruling Fees Order {#advance-income-tax-ruling-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-234
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1990-04-19

Order Prescribing the Fee To Be Paid by a Person to whom a Service is
Provided in Respect of a Request for an Advance Income Tax Ruling from
the Department of National Revenue
::::

:::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 19(b) of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) and Order in Council P.C.
1989-1949 of September 28, 1989[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-90-234_e_hq_5206){#fn_SOR-90-234_e_hq_5206-ID0EHCCA},
hereby revokes the Department of National Revenue (Taxation) Advance
Rulings Fees Order, made on May 25, 1983[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-90-234_e_hq_5207){#fn_SOR-90-234_e_hq_5207-ID0EDCCA},
and makes the annexed [Order prescribing the fee to be paid by a person
to whom a service is provided in respect of a request for an advance
income tax ruling from the Department of National
Revenue](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-234) in substitution therefor.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-922613 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Advance Income Tax Ruling Fees Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-234).

## [Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-922615 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The fee payable by a person
who requests the Minister of National Revenue to provide an advance
income tax ruling, whether or not the request is withdrawn, is \$221.24
during the period beginning on April 1, 2022 and ending on March 31,
2023 and \$281.22 as of April 1, 2023, for each hour or part of an hour
that is spent in preparing the ruling.
