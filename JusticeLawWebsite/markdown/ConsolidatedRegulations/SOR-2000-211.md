Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission Rules of Procedure

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Natural Resources, pursuant to section 44 of the
[Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3)[[Footnote

Procedure](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-211), made by the Canadian Nuclear

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-659768 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subrule apply in these Rules.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Nuclear Safety and Control
      Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[intervenor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[intervenor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is permitted
      to intervene at a public hearing under rule 19.
      ([intervenant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a party or intervenor.
      ([participant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a licence application, the
        applicant;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a licence renewal, suspension,
        amendment, revocation or replacement, the licensee;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a matter being heard by the
        Commission in the public interest under the Act, and if these
        Rules do not otherwise set out who the parties are, any person
        whom the Commission names as party in the proceeding;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the review by the Commission of
        an order of an inspector or a designated officer, a person who
        is named in or subject to the order;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in relation to an appeal made to the Commission
        under subsection 43(1) of the Act, the appellant;

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a rehearing and redetermination
        under subsection 43(2) of the Act, any of the persons referred
        to in that subsection; and

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a redetermination on the
        Commission\'s own initiative under subsection 43(3) of the Act,
        any of the persons referred to in subsection 43(2) of the Act.
        ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Secretary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Secretary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Secretary of the
      Commission. ([secrétaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, \"document\" includes
  information stored by electronic means.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-659784 .Part}

### [Application, Officers and Employees of the Commission, Quorum]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659785 .Subheading}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Part applies in respect of the following proceedings:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an opportunity to be heard provided by a designated
    officer under subsection 39(1) of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an opportunity to be heard provided by the
    Commission under subsection 40(1) of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} proceedings conducted by the Commission on its own
    initiative under subsection 40(3) of the Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a public hearing in relation to a licensing matter
    that is held by the Commission under paragraph 40(5)(a) of the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a public hearing in relation to any matter within
    the Commission\'s jurisdiction that is held by the Commission under
    paragraph 40(5)(b) of the Act;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an appeal made to the Commission pursuant to
    subsection 43(1) of the Act;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a rehearing by the Commission under subsection
    43(2) of the Act;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a redetermination by the Commission under
    subsection 43(3) of the Act; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a public hearing conducted by the Commission under
    section 46 of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission or a designated officer, as the case
  may be, may permit or require officers and employees of the Commission
  to participate in a proceeding under these Rules in such manner,
  including presenting information and submissions orally or in writing,
  questioning participants and responding to questions and submissions,
  as will enable the Commission or designated officer to determine the
  matter in a fair, informal and expeditious manner.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of proceedings before the Commission
  or a panel of the Commission under these Rules, the number of members
  of the Commission or a panel that constitutes a quorum shall be:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} three members for a proceeding before the
    Commission;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one panel member for a proceeding before a panel of
    one member or two members;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} two panel members for a proceeding before a panel
    of three or four members; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} three panel members for a proceeding before a panel
    of more than four members.

### [Varying Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659803 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission or, where applicable, a designated officer may vary or
  supplement any of these Rules, in order to ensure that a proceeding be
  dealt with as informally and expeditiously as the circumstances and
  the considerations of fairness permit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any procedural matter that arises in the course of a
  proceeding that is not provided for in these Rules shall be dealt with
  by the Commission or, where applicable, a designated officer as
  informally and expeditiously as the circumstances and the
  considerations of fairness permit.

### [Defect in Form or Procedure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659807 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No proceeding is invalid,
in whole or part, by reason only of a defect in form or failure to
follow a procedure that is set out in these Rules, where the defect or
failure does not result in any material harm to a relevant interest.

### [Waiver of Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659811 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  participant may waive any procedural right that is granted to the
  participant under these Rules.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the absence of proof to the contrary, where any
  participant fails to take advantage of a procedural right granted
  under these Rules, the participant is deemed to have waived that
  right.

### [Time Limits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659815 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where these Rules indicate
that a time limit is to be specified in an order of the Commission or a
designated officer, or a notice of the Commission, the time limit
specified shall provide for as informal and expeditious a proceeding as
the circumstances and the considerations of fairness permit.

### [Transmitting Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659817 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  otherwise provided by these Rules, any giving, sending, notifying,
  serving or filing that is required or permitted under these Rules may
  be done

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by hand, ordinary mail, registered mail or courier;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by facsimile, if the recipient is able to receive
    the information in that manner; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by any other method that is agreed to by the
    recipient.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document that is sent by facsimile must include the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone and facsimile
    numbers of the sender;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the transmission;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total number of pages transmitted;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone and facsimile
    numbers of the recipient; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone and facsimile
    numbers of a person to contact if transmission problems occur.

### [Filing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659829 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subrule (2), a document that is required to be filed under these
  Rules shall be filed at the office of the Secretary.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} During a proceeding, a document may be filed with the
  presiding member of the Commission or the designated officer who is
  hearing the matter.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the absence of proof to the contrary, the date of
  filing is the date on which the document is received by the Commission
  or designated officer, as indicated

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by the date stamped on the document by an employee
    of the Commission, in the case of a document that is filed in
    accordance with paragraph 7(1)(a); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by the date that appears on the covering page of
    the facsimile as processed by the receiving facsimile machine of the
    Commission, in the case of a document that is filed by facsimile.

### [Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659836 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  document is required under these Rules to be served personally, the
  service shall be made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on an individual, by leaving a copy of the document
    with the individual or the individual's authorized representative
    or, if there is no such representative, with any other person who
    appears to be an adult occupant of the place of service and who
    agrees to accept service;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a partnership or other
    non-incorporated association of persons, by leaving a copy of the
    document with a person whom the partnership or association has
    designated to the Commission as its representative for the matter in
    question or, if there is no such person, with any other person who
    appears to manage or be in control of a place of business operated
    by the partnership or association and who agrees to accept service;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on a corporation, by leaving a copy of the document
    with an officer, director or agent of the corporation, any employee
    of the corporation who is at a place of business of the corporation
    and who is responsible for the management of the place of business
    or, if there is no such person, with any other person at a place of
    business of the corporation who appears to manage or be in control
    of the place of business and who agrees to accept service.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document that is served by ordinary or registered
  mail shall be served at the address where mail is ordinarily received,
  as found on the most recent document filed with the Commission.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The date of service of a document is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the document is served personally, the date
    of delivery;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the document is served by ordinary mail, the
    date that is 10 days after mailing unless there is information that
    shows that the date is other than 10 days after mailing, in which
    case the date of service is the date so shown; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the document is served by electronic
    transmission, the date of receipt that appears on the facsimile or
    electronic version of the document as processed by the recipient\'s
    facsimile or computer.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where these Rules require a document to be served,
  the Commission or designated officer, as the case may be, may, in the
  interests of a fair, informal and expeditious consideration of the
  matter, require proof of service to be filed with the Commission or
  designated officer.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Proof of service of any document may be made by
  showing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgment of service signed by or on behalf
    of the person served; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an affidavit of service stating the name of the
    person who served the document and the date, place and manner of
    service.

### [Notice]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659853 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Information that is not
given in accordance with these Rules is considered to have been properly
given where it is shown that the information or its substance came,
within the required time limit, to the attention of the person to whom
it was to be given.

### [Official Languages]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659855 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (2), all
  documents given to the Commission or a designated officer must be in
  at least one official language.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person may give a document in a language other than
  an official language if, at the same time, the person gives a
  translation of the document in at least one official language, and an
  affidavit of the translator attesting to the accuracy of the
  translation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the original of a document referred to in
  subrule (2) is required to be served, the translation and affidavit
  must also be served, and at the same time as the service of the
  original document.

### [Confidentiality]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659860 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (2), in any
  proceeding, the Commission or a designated officer, as the case may
  be, may take measures referred to in subrule (3) to protect
  information if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information involves national or nuclear
    security;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information is confidential information of a
    financial, commercial, scientific, technical, personal or other
    nature that is treated consistently as confidential and the person
    affected has not consented to the disclosure; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} disclosure of the information is likely to endanger
    the life, liberty or security of a person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission or the designated officer may take the
  measures referred to in subrule (3) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} protection of the information outweighs in
    importance the public interest in public hearings and disclosure of
    evidence; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the measures are designed so as not to affect the
    public nature of the proceeding except to the extent necessary to
    adequately protect the information.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The measures that the Commission or designated
  officer may take include requiring that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proceeding or part of the proceeding take place
    in private, to the exclusion of members of the public, other than
    the parties and their counsel or agent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the publication of information given to the
    Commission or designated officer be restricted or prohibited; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure of information given or received by
    the Commission or designated officer be prohibited or restricted to
    some or all of the parties and intervenors, or their counsel or
    agent, if any.

### [Summonses]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659875 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  case of a public hearing that is conducted by the Commission, a
  participant may request the Commission to issue a summons by
  submitting a request in writing before the start of the hearing or an
  oral request at the hearing, but in any case as soon as the
  circumstances giving rise to the request become known to the
  participant.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request for a summons shall set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for the summons;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} how any information, record or thing to be produced
    by the person to be summoned is relevant to the matter; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} why the person to be summoned is the appropriate
    person to produce the information, record or thing.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A summons shall be served directly on the person to
  be summoned at least seven days before the day on which the person is
  required to attend the public hearing.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person who has been summoned to a public hearing
  shall attend at the time and day specified and every day of the
  hearing, unless the Commission directs that the person must only
  attend on certain days, or that the presence of the person is no
  longer required.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a public hearing is adjourned and the day for
  its reconvening is not disclosed at the time of the adjournment, the
  Commission must notify the person who is under summons of the date of
  the reconvening

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least five days before the person summoned is to
    re-attend; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the Commission has given less than five days
    notice of the reconvening, within such notice period as is fair and
    reasonable in the circumstances.

### [Adjournments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659889 .Subheading}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Commission or a
designated officer, as the case may be, on the request of a participant
or on their own initiative, may adjourn a proceeding, on such terms and
conditions as the Commission or the designated officer considers
necessary in the interests of a fair, informal and expeditious
consideration of the matter.

### [Record]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659891 .Subheading}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission shall keep a record of the following in respect of every
  proceeding:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any document that initiated the proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any notice given by the Commission or a designated
    officer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any documentary evidence, written submission or
    other material filed with the Commission or a designated officer;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any final decision of the Commission or a
    designated officer, including reasons in any case where reasons were
    given;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any transcript of the proceeding that is made by
    the Commission or a designated officer; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other relevant information that the Commission
    or a designated officer directs to be placed on the record.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any measures taken under rule 12, the
  record kept under subrule (1) shall be open to the participants and
  the public.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Public Hearings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-659903 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659904 .Subheading}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies in
respect of a public hearing that the Commission holds

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} under paragraph 40(5)(a) of the Act, in relation to
  an application for the issuance, renewal, suspension, amendment,
  revocation or replacement of a licence; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} under paragraph 40(5)(b) of the Act, in relation to
  any other matter within the Commission\'s jurisdiction.

### [Notice of Public Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659908 .Subheading}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Commission holds a public hearing on any matter under this Part,
  it may hold the hearing on one or more days and in one or more places.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall give notice of a public hearing
  to the parties at least 60 days before the start of the hearing.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall also give a notice to the public
  at least 60 days before the start of the hearing in any manner that
  the Commission considers will most likely result in the notice coming
  to the attention of persons interested in the matter being considered.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The notices referred to in subrules (2) and (3) shall
  include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the matter being heard;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the Commission proposes to hold the public
    hearing on one day, the date, time and place of the hearing;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the Commission proposes to hold the public
    hearing on more than one day, the dates, times and places, if known,
    of the public hearing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the requirement that parties who intend to
    participate in the hearing must so notify the Commission in
    accordance with rule 18;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which persons may request to
    intervene, as set out in rule 19; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the names and addresses of persons to whom copies
    of any information and submissions filed or to be filed with the
    Commission, for consideration at the hearing, must be sent.

### [Participation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659922 .Subheading}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party who intends to appear at a public hearing shall file with the
  Commission, at least 30 days before the start of the hearing, a notice
  that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the party\'s intention to appear
    and, where the party will be represented by counsel or an agent, the
    name, address and telephone and facsimile numbers of the counsel or
    agent; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the documentary information and written
    submission that the party will present to the Commission at the
    hearing, and a list of any witnesses proposed by the party.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} There shall be filed with the Commission, at least 30
  days before the start of a public hearing, a copy of the documentary
  information and written submission that the officers and employees of
  the Commission will present to the Commission at the hearing.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a public hearing comprises two hearing days,
  any documentary information and written submission filed under
  subrule (1) or (2) shall be considered by the Commission on each of
  those hearing days, and the parties and their witnesses shall attend
  on each of those hearing days unless the Commission directs otherwise
  in the interests of a fair, informal and expeditious consideration of
  the matter.

### [Intervention]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659929 .Subheading}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a request to intervene at a public hearing is filed with the
  Commission in accordance with subrules (2) and (3), the Commission,
  subject to these Rules, may permit the following persons to
  participate as intervenors at the hearing in the manner and to the
  extent that the Commission considers will enable it to determine the
  matter before it in a fair, informal and expeditious manner:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who has an interest in the matter being
    heard; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who has expertise in the matter or
    information that may be useful to the Commission in coming to a
    decision.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the notice of public hearing given under this
  Part indicates one hearing day, the request to intervene must be filed
  with the Commission by the filing date specified in the notice.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the notice of public hearing given under this
  Part indicates two hearing days, the request to intervene must be
  filed with the Commission at least 30 days before the second hearing
  day.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The request to intervene shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone and facsimile
    numbers of the requester;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the requester intends to be represented by
    counsel or an agent, the name, address and telephone and facsimile
    numbers of the counsel or agent;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the requester meets at least
    one of the conditions for intervening set out in subrule (1) and;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement setting out whether the requester
    wishes to intervene by way of written submission only or by way of
    written submission and oral presentation, and a copy of the
    information and submission.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a public hearing comprises two hearing days,
  any written submission and oral presentation of a person permitted to
  intervene at the public hearing shall be considered by the Commission
  on the second hearing day.

### [Requests for Rulings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659942 .Subheading}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At any
  time before the start of a public hearing, a participant may file a
  request with the Commission for a ruling on a particular issue by
  setting out the issue and the reasons for the ruling that is sought.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the same time that a request for a ruling is filed
  with the Commission, the participant making the request must serve a
  copy of it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the other participants, if any; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the participant is opposing a request to
    intervene, on the person requesting permission to intervene.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} At any time during the public hearing, a participant
  may make an oral request to the Commission for a ruling on a
  particular issue by explaining the issue and the reasons for the
  ruling that is sought.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall give its decision in relation to
  the request for a ruling after the Commission has provided the
  relevant persons an opportunity to present their views on the request.

### [Presentation of Information and Submissions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659950 .Subheading}

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During
  a public hearing, the Commission, subject to these Rules, may permit
  each participant to present information and submissions on the
  subject-matter orally or in writing, and may permit participants to
  question one another and any witnesses, and to respond to any
  submissions, in any manner and sequence that will enable the
  Commission to determine the matter before it in a fair, informal and
  expeditious manner.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who makes an oral presentation at a public
  hearing must have complied with the documentary filing requirements of
  rule 18 or 19, as the case may be.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who has filed documentary material with the
  Commission under rule 18 may, no later than seven days before the
  start of the public hearing, file documentary material to the extent
  necessary to supplement or amend the material originally filed.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Opportunity to Be Heard by Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-659959 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659960 .Subheading}

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies in
respect of an opportunity to be heard that is given by the Commission
under subsection 40(1) of the Act, where Part 2 does not apply.

### [Notice]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659962 .Subheading}

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission shall notify each party of the party\'s opportunity to be
  heard.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out the subject-matter of the opportunity to be
    heard;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out the time limits for filing submissions and
    information with the Commission; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set out the name and address of any person to whom
    copies of any information and written submissions must be sent, and
    the time limits for sending them.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall conduct the opportunity to be
  heard orally, by written submissions or in any other manner that will
  enable the Commission to determine the questions before it in a fair,
  informal and expeditious manner.

### [Decision of Commission]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659970 .Subheading}

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The Commission shall give
notice of its decision in respect of the matter that has been the
subject of an opportunity to be heard under this Part by sending a copy
of the decision to the parties and any other persons who intervened in
the proceeding.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Opportunity to Be Heard by Designated Officer --- Licensing Matters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-659972 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659973 .Subheading}

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies in
respect of an opportunity to be heard referred to in subsection 39(1) of
the Act that is given by a designated officer

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in considering an application for a licence under
  paragraph 37(2)(c) of the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} before renewing, suspending, amending, revoking or
  replacing a licence, or refusing to renew, suspend, amend, revoke or
  replace a licence under paragraph 37(2)(d) of the Act.

### [Notice]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659977 .Subheading}

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  designated officer shall notify each party of their opportunity to be
  heard in respect of a licensing matter referred to in rule 26.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice shall set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the subject-matter of the opportunity to be heard;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the time limits for filing submissions and
    information with the designated officer; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of any person to whom copies
    of any information and written submissions must be sent, and the
    time limits for sending them.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The designated officer shall conduct the opportunity
  to be heard orally, by written submissions or in any other manner that
  will enable the designated officer to determine the questions before
  him or her in a fair, informal and expeditious manner.

### [Decision of Designated Officer]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659985 .Subheading}

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The designated officer
shall give notice of the decision in respect of the matter that has been
heard under this Part by sending a copy of the decision to the parties
and any other persons who intervened in the proceeding.

## [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Licence Appeal or Redetermination]{.HTitleText1} {#h-659987 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659988 .Subheading}

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** Rule 30 applies in
respect of the following licensing matters that may be the subject of an
appeal or rehearing and redetermination by the Commission under
subsection 43(1) or (2) of the Act, as the case may be:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a refusal of a designated officer to issue, renew,
  suspend, amend, revoke or replace a licence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a term or condition of a licence issued by a
  designated officer;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a renewal, suspension, amendment, revocation or
  replacement, by a designated officer, of a licence;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a decision of the Commission not to issue a licence;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a decision of the Commission not to renew, suspend,
  amend, revoke or replace a licence;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any term or condition of a licence issued, renewed,
  suspended or amended by the Commission; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a suspension, amendment, revocation or replacement,
  by the Commission, of a licence.

### [Notice of Appeal or Redetermination]{.HTitleText2} {#h-659997 .Subheading}

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appeal or an application for a rehearing and redetermination may be
  made by sending to the Commission a notice that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a reference to the paragraph of subsection 43(1)
    or (2) of the Act under which the appeal or application is being
    made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a reference to the licence that is the subject of
    the appeal or application;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the grounds for the appeal or application,
    including, in the case of an appeal, a statement of how the
    appellant is directly affected by the decision being appealed;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the action that the appellant or
    applicant submits that the Commission should take under subsection
    43(4) of the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating whether or not the appellant
    or applicant intends to present new evidence;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the manner in which the appellant
    or applicant proposes to participate in the proceeding;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone and facsimile
    numbers of the appellant or applicant; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating whether the appellant or
    applicant intends to be represented by counsel or an agent and, if
    so, the name, address and telephone and facsimile numbers of the
    counsel or agent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} After receiving a notice of appeal or rehearing and
  redetermination, the Commission shall determine whether its
  consideration will be by way of public hearing under paragraph
  40(5)(b) of the Act or written submissions or by another manner that
  will enable the Commission to determine the matter before it in a
  fair, informal and expeditious manner.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} After determining the manner in which the appeal or
  rehearing and redetermination will be considered, the Commission shall
  send the appellant or applicant and any other participant a notice
  that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which the appeal or rehearing and
    redetermination will be considered and a description of the manner
    in which the appellant or applicant and any other participant may
    present information, evidence and submissions to the Commission;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date, place and time limit for giving or
    presenting information and submissions and requesting information;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the names and addresses of the persons to whom
    copies of the information and written submissions must be sent.

### [Redetermination on Commission\'s Own Initiative]{.HTitleText2} {#h-660013 .Subheading}

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  rule and rule 32 apply in respect of a redetermination by the
  Commission, on its own initiative, of any decision or order or term or
  condition of a licence, under subsection 43(3) of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Commission proposes to make a
  redetermination referred to in subrule (1), the Commission shall send
  a notice of redetermination to those persons who were participants in
  the matter being redetermined.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice of redetermination shall include the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the basis for the proposed redetermination;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the powers that the Commission might
    exercise under subsection 43(4) of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement of whether the Commission intends to
    redetermine the matter by way of public hearing or written
    submissions or by another manner that will enable the Commission to
    determine the matter before it in a fair, informal and expeditious
    manner;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the manner in which a party and
    any other person participating may present information, evidence and
    submissions to the Commission;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date, place and time limits for giving or
    presenting information and written submissions; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the names and addresses of the persons to whom
    copies of the information and written submissions must be sent.

### [Decision]{.HTitleText2} {#h-660024 .Subheading}

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The Commission shall give
notice of its decision in respect of the matter that has been the
subject of an appeal or rehearing and redetermination by sending a copy
of the decision to the parties and any other person who intervened in
the proceedings.

## [PART 6]{.HLabel1}[Orders of Inspectors and Designated Officers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660026 .Part}

### [Making of Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-660027 .Subheading}

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  making an order under section 35 or paragraph 37(2)(f) of the Act, an
  inspector or a designated officer, as the case may be, shall inform,
  either orally or in writing, the person to whom the order is given of
  its nature and the basis for it, as well as any proposed time limit
  for compliance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The inspector or designated officer shall give the
  order referred to in subrule (1) in writing.

### [Proceedings to Confirm, Amend, Revoke or Replace an Order]{.HTitleText2} {#h-660031 .Subheading}

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As
  soon as practicable and, in any event, not later than 10 days after an
  inspector or a designated officer, as the case may be, gives an order
  under subrule 33(2), the inspector or designated officer shall refer
  it to the Commission for confirmation, amendment, revocation or
  replacement in accordance with subsection 35(3) or 37(6) of the Act,
  as the case may be.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Commission acts under subsection 35(3) of
  the Act or a designated officer is authorized by the Commission to act
  under paragraph 37(2)(g) of the Act, the Commission or designated
  officer, as the case may be, shall notify the persons named in or
  subject to the order of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their opportunity to be heard under paragraph
    39(1)(c) or 40(1)(c) or (d) of the Act, as the case may be; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the requirement that those persons who intend to
    present information and submissions in respect of the order so
    notify the Commission or designated officer within 10 days after
    receipt of the notice.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} As soon as practicable after receiving a notice
  referred to in paragraph (2)(b), the Commission or designated officer
  shall notify the person giving the notice

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} of the manner in which and time when the person may
    be heard; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} of the format in which the information and
    submissions are required to be filed with the Commission or
    designated officer and sent to other parties and the time limits for
    filing and sending the information and submissions, as well as the
    names and addresses of the other parties.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A decision by the Commission or designated officer,
  as the case may be, to confirm, amend, revoke or replace an order
  shall be in writing and must be sent, within 10 days after being made,
  to the persons who are named in or subject to the decision, and to any
  other persons who intervened in the proceeding.

### [Appeal or Redetermination of an Order]{.HTitleText2} {#h-660043 .Subheading}

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  rule and rule 36 apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an appeal, under subsection 43(1) of the Act, of a
    confirmation, amendment, revocation or replacement, by a designated
    officer, of an order of an inspector; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a rehearing and redetermination, by the Commission,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} under paragraph 43(2)(e) of the Act, of an order
      of the Commission, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} under paragraph 43(2)(f) of the Act, of a
      confirmation, amendment, revocation or replacement by the
      Commission, of an order of an inspector or a designated officer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An appeal or an application for a redetermination
  referred to in subrule (1) may be made by sending to the Commission a
  notice that includes the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a reference to the paragraph of subsection 43(1)
    or (2) of the Act under which the appeal or application is being
    made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a reference to the order that is the subject of the
    appeal or application;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the grounds for the appeal or application,
    including, in the case of an appeal, a statement showing how the
    appellant is directly affected by the order being appealed;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the action that the appellant or
    applicant submits that the Commission should take under subsection
    43(4) of the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating whether or not the appellant
    or applicant wishes to present new evidence;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the manner in which the appellant
    or applicant proposes to participate in the proceeding;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone and facsimile
    numbers of the appellant or applicant; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating whether the appellant or
    applicant intends to be represented by counsel or an agent and, if
    so, the name, address and telephone and facsimile numbers of the
    counsel or agent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} After receiving the information referred to in
  subrule (2), the Commission shall decide whether the appeal or
  redetermination will be by way of public hearing under paragraph
  40(5)(b) of the Act or written submissions or by another manner that
  will enable the Commission to determine the matter before it in a
  fair, informal and expeditious manner.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} After determining the manner in which the appeal or
  redetermination will be considered, the Commission shall send to the
  appellant or applicant and to those persons who were participants in
  the proceeding related to the order being appealed or redetermined, a
  notice that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the manner in which the
    redetermination will be considered and a description of the manner
    in which the appellant or applicant and any other participant may
    present information, evidence and submissions to the Commission;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date, place and time limits for giving or
    presenting information and submissions; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the names and addresses of the persons to whom
    copies of the information and written submissions must be sent.

### [Decision]{.HTitleText2} {#h-660064 .Subheading}

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** The Commission shall give
notice of its decision in respect of the matter that has been the
subject of appeal or rehearing and redetermination by sending a copy of
the decision to the parties and to any other persons who intervened in
the proceeding.

## [PART 7]{.HLabel1}[Hearing for Contaminated Places]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660066 .Part}

### [Notice of Public Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-660067 .Subheading}

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Commission proposes, under subsection 46(1) of the Act, to conduct
  a public hearing in respect of contamination by a radioactive nuclear
  substance at any place, the Commission shall send a notice of public
  hearing, at least 60 days before the beginning of the hearing, to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the owner and the occupant of the place; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person who has a right to, or interest in, the
    place, where the right or interest is set out in the registry of the
    land registry office or other office where title to the land in
    which the place is located is recorded.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall also give a notice to the public
  at least 60 days before the start of the public hearing in any manner
  that the Commission considers will most likely result in the notice
  coming to the attention of persons interested in the matter being
  considered.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notices referred to in subrules (1) and (2) shall
  include, where applicable, the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Commission\'s reasons for believing that there
    is contamination in excess of the prescribed limit by a radioactive
    nuclear substance at the place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date, time and place of the public hearing;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the requirement that persons referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a) or (b) who intend to participate in the hearing
    must so notify the Commission in accordance with rule 18;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which persons may request to
    intervene, as set out in rule 19;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the actions the Commission may take, following the
    hearing, under subsections 46(2) and (3) of the Act; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the names and addresses of persons to whom copies
    of any information and written submissions filed or to be filed with
    the Commission, for consideration at the hearing, must be sent.

### [ Determination]{.HTitleText2} {#h-660082 .Subheading}

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission shall send a written notice of its determination to the
  persons referred to in subrule 37(1) in respect of the matter that was
  the subject of the public hearing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Commission determines that there is
  contamination in excess of the prescribed limit, the notice shall
  include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description and the amount of the contamination
    that is present at the place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement that a notice of contamination will be
    filed in accordance with subsection 46(2) of the Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the Commission intends to issue an order to
    reduce the level of contamination in accordance with subsection
    46(3) of the Act, a statement to that effect, or, where an order has
    already been issued, a copy of the order.

### [Notice of Contamination]{.HTitleText2} {#h-660089 .Subheading}

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** A notice of contamination
referred to in subsection 46(2) of the Act shall include a description
of the place that is the subject of the notice and a description of the
contamination that is present at the place.

### [Notice of Order]{.HTitleText2} {#h-660091 .Subheading}

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** An order in relation to
the contamination of a place that is made by the Commission under
subsection 46(3) of the Act shall be sent to those persons to whom a
notice of contamination was sent and shall include the following
information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the place that is the subject of the
  order and of the contamination that is present at that place;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the particular prescribed measures
  that are required to be taken to reduce the level of contamination;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any time limit for complying with the order.

## [PART 8]{.HLabel1}[Hearing where Place No Longer Contaminated]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660096 .Part}

### [Notice of Public Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-660097 .Subheading}

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Commission intends, under subsection 46(4) of the Act, to conduct
  a public hearing to determine whether contamination by a radioactive
  nuclear substance at any place is no longer present, the Commission
  shall send a notice of public hearing, at least 60 days before the
  beginning of the hearing, to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the owner and the occupant of the place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person who has a right to, or interest in, the
    place, where the right or interest is set out in the registry of the
    land registry office or other office where title to the land in
    which the place is located is recorded; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other person to whom a notice of public hearing
    was sent under subrule 37(1).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice shall include, where applicable, the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Commission\'s reasons for believing that there
    no longer is contamination in excess of the prescribed limit by a
    radioactive nuclear substance at the place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the time limits for filing information and written
    submissions with the Commission;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date, time and place of the public hearing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the action the Commission may take, following the
    hearing, pursuant to subsection 46(5) of the Act; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the names and addresses of persons to whom copies
    of any information and written submissions filed or to be filed with
    the Commission, for consideration at the hearing, must be sent.

### [Determination]{.HTitleText2} {#h-660111 .Subheading}

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  matter that was the subject of the public hearing to the persons

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice shall include, where the Commission

## [PART 9]{.HLabel1}[Notice of Emergency Order]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660115 .Part}

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** A notice of an emergency
order given under subsection 47(2) of the Act shall be sent in
accordance with rule 7 to every person named in or subject to it, and
shall include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the order; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any time limit for complying with the order.

## [PART 10]{.HLabel1}[Court Orders]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660119 .Part}

### [Publication of Facts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-660120 .Subheading}

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** Where a court, pursuant
to paragraph 60(1)(c) of the Act, orders an offender to publish the
facts relating to a conviction, the offender shall publish them in two
editions of any newspaper specified in the order, at an interval of not
less than one week and not more than two months between publications.

### [Notifying Affected Persons]{.HTitleText2} {#h-660122 .Subheading}

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** Where a court, pursuant
to paragraph 60(1)(d) of the Act, orders an offender to notify any
person who is affected by the offender\'s conduct of the facts relating
to a conviction, the offender shall give notice to the person in
accordance with rule 44 or by one of the methods referred to in rule 7,
as ordered by the court.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660124 .Part}

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** These Rules come into
force on the day on which they are approved by the Governor in Council.
