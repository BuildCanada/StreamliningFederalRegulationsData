beneficiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841656 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
[Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), [[EU country or other CETA
beneficiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a country or territory set out
in the schedule to these Regulations.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841658 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Ind8411_hq_20657){#fn_Ind8411_hq_20657-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 97 of the [Canada--European Union Comprehensive Economic and
Trade Agreement Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-4.8), chapter 6 of the
Statutes of Canada, 2017, comes into force, but if they are registered
after that day, they come into force on the day on which they are
registered.
