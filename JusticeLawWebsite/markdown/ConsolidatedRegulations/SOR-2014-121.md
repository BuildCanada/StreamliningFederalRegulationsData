## Expiry of Section 12.2 of the Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006 Regulations {#expiry-of-section-12.2-of-the-softwood-lumber-products-export-charge-act-2006-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2014-121
:::

[SOFTWOOD LUMBER PRODUCTS EXPORT CHARGE ACT, 2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55)

Registration 2014-05-16

Expiry of Section 12.2 of the Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge
Act, 2006 Regulations

P.C. 2014-577 2014-05-15
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 101(a) and
section 102 of the [Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act,
2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-2043-E_hq_13697){#fn_81000-2-2043-E_hq_13697-ID0EDBAA},
makes the annexed [Expiry of Section 12.2 of the Softwood Lumber
Products Export Charge Act, 2006
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-121).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-2043-E_hq_13697-ID0EDBAA){#fn_81000-2-2043-E_hq_13697}S.C.
  2006, c. 13
  :::
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ceasing to be in force

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Section 12.2 of the
[Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55)
ceases to be in force on October 12, 2013.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
deemed to have come into force on October 13, 2013.
