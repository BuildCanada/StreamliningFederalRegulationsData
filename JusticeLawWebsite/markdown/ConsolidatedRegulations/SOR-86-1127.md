## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-890287 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Marine Industries Limited
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1127).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-890289 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Marine Industries Limited]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Marine Industries Limited]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a company
    incorporated with that name under the laws of Canada and having its
    head office in the city of Montreal, Quebec; ([Marine Industrie
    Limitée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Regional
    Industrial Expansion; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any lender, or its
    assignee, other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Government of Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the government of any province of Canada,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an agency of any government referred to in
      paragraph (a) or (b) or any company that is effectively controlled
      by any such government or any agency thereof, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any municipal corporation. ([prêteur
      privé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-890298 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Insurance provided by the
Minister in respect of loans made by a private lender to Marine
Industries Limited to promote the growth of Marine Industries Limited
and to foster the expansion of Canadian trade shall be subject to the
terms and conditions set out in sections 4 to 7.

## [Terms and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-890300 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The insurance referred to
in section 3 shall be for an amount up to 100 per cent of any loss up to
40 million dollars in the aggregate that may result in respect of loans
referred to in that section.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A private lender shall pay
to the Minister by semi-annual payments in advance a fee of one half of
one per cent per annum of the amount of insurance in force.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where a private lender has
demanded repayment of a loan insured pursuant to the terms and
conditions set out in these Regulations, the amount payable by the
Minister shall not exceed an amount equal to the lesser of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of insurance in force on the date of the
  demand, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 100 per cent of the loss incurred by the private
  lender.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The amount of insurance
provided by the Minister to a private lender may be reduced at the
request of the private lender.
