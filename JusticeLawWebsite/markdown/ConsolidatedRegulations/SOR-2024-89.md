## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1453551 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Same meaning

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Words and expressions used
in this Marketing Authorization have the same meaning as in the [Food
and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870).

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1453554 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cheese, yogurt, kefir and buttermilk

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Prepackaged products that are cheese
  or yogurt, including drinkable yogurt, and that are made from dairy
  products, kefir or buttermilk are exempt from the application of
  subsection B.01.350(12) of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), in respect of saturated
  fat and sugars, if the condition set out in subsection (2) is met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection B.01.350(9) of the
  [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), the
  prepackaged products must contain 5% or more of the daily value for
  calcium per serving of stated size or per reference amount, whichever
  is greater.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cheese

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Prepackaged products that are cheese
  made from dairy products are exempt from the application of subsection
  B.01.350(12) of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), in respect of sodium,
  if the condition set out in subsection (2) is met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection B.01.350(11) of the
  [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), the
  prepackaged products must contain 5% or more of the daily value for
  calcium per serving of stated size or per reference amount, whichever
  is greater.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1453565 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Marketing
Authorization comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
