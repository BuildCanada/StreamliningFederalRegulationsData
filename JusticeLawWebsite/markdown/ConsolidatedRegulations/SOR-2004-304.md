## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713407 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Sex Offender Information
    Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[police service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[police service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    paragraph 2(q) of The Police Act, 1990, Statutes of Saskatchewan,
    1990-91, c. P-15.01, as amended from time to time. ([service de
    police]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police. ([GRC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Report and Notification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713412 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A sex
  offender whose main residence is located in the Province of
  Saskatchewan may make any report required under paragraph 4.1(1)(a)
  or (b) or section 4.3 of the Act by telephone.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The sex offender may provide any notification
  required under section 6 of the Act in person or by telephone.

## [Persons Authorized to Collect Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713418 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
authorized in the Province of Saskatchewan to collect information for
the purposes of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the RCMP who serves in Saskatchewan or a
  member of a police service; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person employed by the RCMP, or by a police
  service, whose duties include the collection of information under the
  Act.

## [Persons Authorized to Register Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713422 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Any person who is
authorized under section 3 to collect information is authorized in the
Province of Saskatchewan to register information for the purposes of the
Act.

## [Registration Centres]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713424 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following places are designated as registration centres in the
  Province of Saskatchewan:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Saskatchewan Sex Offender Central Registration
    Centre, administered by RCMP "F" Division and located at 6101
    Dewdney Avenue, Regina;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} each RCMP detachment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the municipal police service office in

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Estevan,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Moose Jaw,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} Prince Albert,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} Regina,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} Saskatoon, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} Weyburn; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} each office of the File Hills First Nations Police
    Service.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designated area of service of each registration
  centre is the entire Province of Saskatchewan.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-231, s. 2

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713440 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EBCA}[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the later of the
day on which the [Sex Offender Information Registration
Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7), chapter 10 of the Statutes of Canada, 2004, comes
into force and the day on which these Regulations are registered.
