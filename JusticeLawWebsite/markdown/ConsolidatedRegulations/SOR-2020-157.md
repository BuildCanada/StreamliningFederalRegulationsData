## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1254241 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[CUSMA country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CUSMA country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a party to the
    Canada--United States--Mexico Agreement. ([pays
    ACEUM]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[minimal operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minimal operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the
    following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an operation to ensure the preservation of a good
      in good condition for the purposes of transport and storage;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} packaging, re-packaging, breaking up of
      consignments or putting up a good for retail sale, including
      placing a good in bottles, cans, flasks, bags, cases or boxes;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} mere dilution with water or another substance
      that does not materially alter the characteristics of the good;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the collection of goods intended to form sets,
      assortments, kits or composite goods; and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any combination of operations referred to in
      paragraphs (a) to (d). ([activité minimale]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[originating good]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[originating good]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 1(1) of the [CUSMA Rules of Origin
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-155). ([marchandise
    originaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[originating material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[originating material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in subsection 1(1) of the [CUSMA Rules of Origin
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-155). ([matière
    originaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Shipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1254254 .Part}

### [United States Tariff]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1254255 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Goods produced in the United States

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 24(1)(b) of the Act, originating goods exported
  from a CUSMA country are entitled to the benefit of the United States
  Tariff if the United States was the country in which the last
  production process, other than a minimal operation, occurred and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods are shipped to Canada from a CUSMA
    country without transhipment through a country that is not a CUSMA
    country, either

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on a through bill of lading, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} without a through bill of lading and the
      importer provides, when requested by an officer, documentary
      evidence that indicates the shipping route and all points of
      shipment and transhipment prior to the importation of the goods;
      or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods are shipped to Canada through a country
    that is not a CUSMA country and the importer provides, when
    requested by an officer,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} documentary evidence that indicates the shipping
      route and all points of shipment and transhipment before the
      importation of the goods, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the customs control documents that
      establish that the goods remained under customs control while in
      that other country.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional condition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), originating goods that are
  classified under the subheadings set out in the schedule and that were
  produced in the United States with originating materials of Mexico are
  entitled to the benefit of the tariff referred to in subsection (1)
  only if they satisfy, in the United States or Canada, the change in
  tariff classification that is identified opposite their subheading set
  out in the schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Goods produced in Canada

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 24(1)(b) of the Act, originating goods exported
  from the United States are entitled to the benefit of the United
  States Tariff if Canada was the country in which the last production
  process, other than a minimal operation, occurred and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods are shipped to Canada from the United
    States without transhipment through a country other than Mexico,
    either

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on a through bill of lading, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} without a through bill of lading and the
      importer provides, when requested by an officer, documentary
      evidence that indicates the shipping route and all points of
      shipment and transhipment before the importation of the goods; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods are shipped to Canada through a country
    other than Mexico and the importer provides, when requested by an
    officer,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} documentary evidence that indicates the shipping
      route and all points of shipment and transhipment before the
      importation of the goods, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the customs control documents that
      establish that the goods remained under customs control while in
      that other country.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional condition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), originating goods that are
  classified under the subheadings set out in the schedule and that were
  produced in Canada with originating materials of the United States are
  entitled to the benefit of the tariff referred to in subsection (1)
  only if they satisfy, in Canada, the change in tariff classification
  that is identified opposite their subheading set out in the schedule.
