## Order Designating the Staff of the Non-Public Funds, Canadian Forces, a separate employer, for the purposes of paragraph 62(1)(a) of the Act {#order-designating-the-staff-of-the-non-public-funds-canadian-forces-a-separate-employer-for-the-purposes-of-paragraph-621a-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2000-131
:::

[FEDERAL PUBLIC SECTOR LABOUR RELATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.3)

PUBLIC SERVICE LABOUR RELATIONS ACT

Registration 2000-03-30

Order Designating the Staff of the Non-Public Funds, Canadian Forces, a
separate employer, for the purposes of paragraph 62(1)(a) of the Act

P.C. 2000-442 2000-03-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Defence, pursuant to subsection 62(4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EFBAA} of the [Public
Service Staff Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-35), hereby designates the
Staff of the Non-Public Funds, Canadian Forces, a separate employer, for
the purposes of paragraph 62(1)(a) of the [Public Service Staff
Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-35).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote

  26, s. 19(2)
