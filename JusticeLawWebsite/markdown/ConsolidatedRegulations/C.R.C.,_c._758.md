## Dipropylacetic Acid (also known as 2-Propylpentanoic Acid) Remission Order {#dipropylacetic-acid-also-known-as-2-propylpentanoic-acid-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 758
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties nn Dipropylacetic Acid
also known as 2-Propylpentanoic Acid or its Sodium Salt
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561619 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Dipropylacetic Acid (also known as 2-Propylpentanoic Acid)
Remission Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._758).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561621 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on dipropylacetic acid, also known as
2-propylpentanoic acid, or its sodium salt, whether or not with
admixture, imported on or after January 1, 1976, for use in the
treatment of epilepsy.

- SI/88-17, s. 2(E)
