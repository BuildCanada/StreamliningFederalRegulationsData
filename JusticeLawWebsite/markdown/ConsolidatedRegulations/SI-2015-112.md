## Order Designating Ministers under Certain Federal Acts {#order-designating-ministers-under-certain-federal-acts .Title-of-Act}

:::

[AGREEMENT ON INTERNAL TRADE IMPLEMENTATION ACT](/eng/acts/A-2.4)

[CANADA TRAVELLING EXHIBITIONS INDEMNIFICATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10.5)

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

[INDUSTRIAL DESIGN ACT](/eng/acts/I-9)

[MACKENZIE GAS PROJECT IMPACTS FUND ACT](/eng/acts/M-0.15)

[MUSEUMS ACT](/eng/acts/M-13.4)

[NUCLEAR LIABILITY AND COMPENSATION ACT](/eng/acts/N-28.1)

Registration 2015-11-18

Order Designating Ministers under Certain Federal Acts

P.C. 2015-1260 2015-11-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to section 8 of the [Agreement on Internal
  Trade Implementation Act](/eng/acts/A-2.4)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_16174){#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_16174-ID0EBBGBA},
  designates the Minister of Industry, a member of the Queen's Privy
  Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of any provision
  of that Act;

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_16174-ID0EBBGBA){#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_16174}S.C.
    1996, c. 17
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to the definition
  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Canada
  Travelling Exhibitions Indemnification
  Act](/eng/acts/C-10.5)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14826){#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14826-ID0EBBFBA},
  designates the Minister of Canadian Heritage, a member of the Queen's
  Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that
  Act;

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14826-ID0EBBFBA){#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14826}S.C.
    1999, c. 29
    :::

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (d) of the definition
  [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14822){#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14822-ID0EDDEBA}
  and subparagraph (a)(ii) of the definition [[appropriate
  Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 83(1) of that Act,

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14822-ID0EDDEBA){#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14822}R.S., c.
    F-11
    :::

  <!-- -->

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} repeals paragraph (b) of Order in Council P.C.
    1993-1450 of June 25, 1993[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14823){#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14823-ID0EBBBEBA},
    and

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14823-ID0EBBBEBA){#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14823}SI/93-104
      :::

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} repeals paragraph (b) of Order in Council P.C.
    1993-1982 of December 2, 1993[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14824){#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14824-ID0EBBAEBA};

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14824-ID0EBBAEBA){#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14824}SOR/93-536
      :::

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to the definition
  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#footnotef_e){#footnotef_e-ID0EECDBA} in section
  2 of the [Industrial Design Act](/eng/acts/I-9)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^g^](#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_16175){#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_16175-ID0EBCDBA},
  designates the Minister of Industry, a member of the Queen's Privy
  Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act;

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#footnotef_e-ID0EECDBA){#footnotef_e}R.S., c.
    10 (4th Supp.), s. 20(2)
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^g^](#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_16175-ID0EBCDBA){#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_16175}R.S., c.
    I-9
    :::

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to section 3 of the [Mackenzie Gas Project
  Impacts Fund Act](/eng/acts/M-0.15)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^h^](#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_16176){#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_16176-ID0EBBCBA},
  designates the Minister of the Canadian Northern Economic Development
  Agency, a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, to be the
  Minister for the purposes of that Act;

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^h^](#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_16176-ID0EBBCBA){#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_16176}S.C.
    2013, c. 40, s. 282
    :::

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to the definition
  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^i^](#footnotei_e){#footnotei_e-ID0EECBBA} in section
  2 of the [Museums Act](/eng/acts/M-13.4)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^j^](#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14825){#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14825-ID0EBCBBA},
  designates the Minister of Canadian Heritage, a member of the Queen's
  Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister responsible for the National
  Gallery of Canada, the Canadian Museum of History, the Canadian Museum
  of Nature, the National Museum of Science and Technology, the Canadian
  Museum for Human Rights and the Canadian Museum of Immigration at Pier
  21 for the purposes of that Act; and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^i^](#footnotei_e-ID0EECBBA){#footnotei_e}S.C.
    2014, c. 20, s. 194
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^j^](#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14825-ID0EBCBBA){#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_14825}S.C.
    1990, c. 3
    :::

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to section 4 of the [Nuclear Liability and
  Compensation Act](/eng/acts/N-28.1)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^k^](#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_16177){#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_16177-ID0EBBABA},
  designates the Minister of Natural Resources, a minister of the Crown,
  to be the Minister referred to in that Act.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^k^](#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_16177-ID0EBBABA){#fn_81000-3-761-E_hq_16177}S.C.
    2015, c. 4, s. 120
    :::

This Order is effective November 4, 2015.
