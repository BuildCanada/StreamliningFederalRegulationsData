## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Off Grades of Grain]{.HTitleText1} {#h-582644 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/93-363, s. 2(F)

\]
:::

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Grain of
  all kinds and varieties for which grades are established by the
  [Canada Grain Act](/eng/acts/G-10) or the [Canada Grain
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._889), but that cannot have
  any of those grades assigned to it unless it is treated or specially
  cleaned, shall be graded according to the specifications for the off
  grades of grain that are established under the grade names set out in
  this Part.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Grades established under this Part may be referred to
  as "Off Grades" or "Class III grades".

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-363, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Grain that contains
excessive moisture shall be graded according to the specifications for
the grade that would be assigned to that grain if it did not contain
excessive moisture, except that there shall be added to and made part of
the grade name the word "Tough", "Damp", "Moist" or "Wet" according to
the percentage of moisture set out in respect of that grain and word in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Schedule I, in the case of Canada grain grades;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Schedule II, in the case of Canada Western grain
  grades; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Schedule III, in the case of Canada Eastern grain
  grades.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/80-513, s. 1
- SOR/84-615, s. 1
- SOR/93-363, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[4 to 9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4_to_9 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
SOR/84-615, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Western grain that
contains excessive stones or similar material, but not in excess of 2.5
per cent, shall be graded according to the specifications for the grade
that would otherwise apply to it except that there shall be added to and
made part of the grade name the words "Rejected --- Account Stones".

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-363, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[11 to 13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11_to_13 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
SOR/84-615, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Grain that contains
stones in excess of 2.5 per cent or contains gravel, cinders or similar
material that is normally contained in grain salvaged from a wreck in
transit shall be graded "Sample Salvage".

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Grain that does not
qualify for any other grade established by or under the [Canada Grain
Act](/eng/acts/G-10) shall be graded "Sample" and there shall be added
to and made part of the grade name words to indicate the damage,
condition or admixtures.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/84-615, s. 4
- SOR/93-363, s. 2(F)
:::

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Grades of Screenings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-582667 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/93-363, s. 2(F)

\]
:::

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  grades for screenings, including wild oats and mixtures of whole and
  broken grain that are removed from other grain as a result of cleaning
  and do not qualify for any other established grade, and the
  specifications for those grades of screenings, as set out in this Part
  are hereby established.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Grades of screenings established under this Part may
  be referred to as "Grades of Screenings" or "Class IV Grades".

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No. 1 Feed Screenings

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall be grain screenings;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall be cool and sweet;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} shall contain

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} not less than 35 per cent in the aggregate of
      broken or shrunken grain,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} not more than two per cent hare's ear mustard,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} not more than one per cent hulls,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} not more than three per cent in the aggregate of
      small weed seeds that can pass through a 4½/64 inch round-hole
      sieve, chaff, hulls and dust,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} not more than six per cent in the aggregate of
      small weed seeds that can pass through a 4½/64 inch round-hole
      sieve, chaff, hulls, dust, wild and domestic mustard seed, ball
      mustard, canola and rapeseed,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} not more than eight per cent wild oats, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} not more than one per cent of the seeds
      designated as weed seeds in the [Feeds Regulations,
      2024](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-132); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} may contain wild buckwheat and not more than 10 per
    cent of other large seeds not named in this section.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No. 2 Feed Screenings

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall be grain screenings;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall be cool and sweet;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} shall contain

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} not more than two per cent hare's ear mustard or
      wild mustard seed,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} not more than one per cent hulls,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} not more than three per cent in the aggregate
      of small weed seeds that can pass through a 4½/64 inch round-hole
      sieve, chaff, hulls, and dust,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} not more than 10 per cent in the aggregate of
      small weed seeds that can pass through a 4½/64 inch round-hole
      sieve, chaff, hulls, dust, wild and domestic mustard seed, ball
      mustard, canola and rapeseed,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} not more than 49 per cent wild oats, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} not more than one per cent of the seeds
      designated as weed seeds in the [Feeds Regulations,
      2024](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-132) except that there may be a
      total of two per cent of such seeds where the excess over one per
      cent is hare's ear mustard or wild mustard; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} may contain wheat scourings.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Uncleaned Screenings shall be grain screenings that
  do not meet the specifications for No. 1 or No. 2 Feed Screenings
  because of the content of weed seeds, hulls, chaff or dust but that
  contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least 35 per cent of material that if separated
    would meet the grade requirements for No. 1 Feed Screenings; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} not more than one per cent of excessive wild oat
    hulls.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Refuse Screenings shall be grain screenings that do
  not meet the grade requirements for No. 1 or No. 2 Feed Screenings or
  Uncleaned Screenings because of the content of weed seeds, chaff or
  dust.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} An inspector may include in the grade name of any
  screenings

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if he considers the screenings to be typical of
    screenings obtained from the cleaning of a particular kind of grain,
    the name of that grain; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if deterioration or an objectionable condition or
    odour occurs in the screenings to such a degree as materially to
    affect the quality of the screenings, words to indicate the type of
    deterioration, condition or odour.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-370, s. 1
- SOR/93-363, s. 2(F)
- [SOR/2025-153, s. 1](#1534694-1534869){.wb-lbx
  ajax-after="1534694-1534869.html"}

### [Mixed Feed Oats]{.HTitleText2} {#h-582705 .Subheading}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  mixture of western wild oats and western cereal grain because of being
  so mixed shall, if it has the qualities set out in subsection (2), be
  graded "Mixed Feed Oats".

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Mixed Feed Oats shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contain not less than 50 per cent wild oats;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contain not more than one per cent of material that
    passes through a 4½/64 inch round-hole sieve; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} contain in whole, broken, hulled or hulless form
    not more than

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} five per cent heated kernels,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} five per cent wild buckwheat,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} five per cent wheat heads,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} four per cent knuckles, straw and chaff,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} five per cent in the aggregate of wild buckwheat,
      wheat heads, knuckles, straw and chaff,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} five per cent flaxseed,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} 0.1 per cent stones, or

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} 0.25 per cent ergot.

### [No. 2 Mixed Feed Oats]{.HTitleText2} {#h-582720 .Subheading}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  mixture of western wild oats and other materials that does not qualify
  for the established grade Mixed Feed Oats because of being so mixed
  shall, if it has the qualities set out in subsection (2), be graded
  "No. 2 Mixed Feed Oats".

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No. 2 Mixed Feed Oats shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contain not less than 50 per cent wild oats;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contain not more than one per cent of material that
    passes through a 4½/64 inch round-hole sieve; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} contain in whole, broken, hulled or hulless form
    not more than

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 10 per cent heated kernels,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} five per cent wild buckwheat,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 10 per cent in the aggregate of wheat heads,
      knuckles, straw and chaff,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} five per cent flaxseed,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} 0.2 per cent stones, or

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} 1/3 of one per cent ergot.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-363, s. 2(F)
- SOR/2013-146, s. 1

### [Sample Feed Grain]{.HTitleText2} {#h-582735 .Subheading}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  mixture of western grain that contains cereal grains and other
  materials, and that, because of being so mixed, does not qualify for
  any established grade or off grade shall, if it has the qualities set
  out in subsection (2), be graded "Sample Feed Grain".

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sample Feed Grain shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have as its predominating component any class of
    cereal grain in whole, broken, hulled or hulless form;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be practically free of all material that passes
    through a 4½/64 inch round-hole sieve; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} contain not more than

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 50 per cent broken grain,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 1/3 of one per cent ergot,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} five per cent heated kernels,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} 49 per cent wild oats,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} three per cent large weed seeds,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} 10 per cent unthreshed wheat heads,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} four per cent knuckles and straw,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} 10 per cent in the aggregate of unthreshed
      wheat heads, large weed seeds, knuckles and straw,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} 50 per cent in the aggregate of wild oats, large
      weed seeds, unthreshed wheat heads, knuckles and straw,

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} 0.1 per cent stones, or

    - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} five per cent flaxseed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-363, s. 2(F)
