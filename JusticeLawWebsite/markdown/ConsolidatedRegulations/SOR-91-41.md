## Social Insurance Number Disclosure Regulations {#social-insurance-number-disclosure-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-41
:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Registration 1990-12-18

Regulations Respecting the Disclosure of Social Insurance Numbers for
the Purpose of Registration under Section 240 of the Excise Tax Act

P.C. 1990-2751  1990-12-18
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph
277(1)(d)[[Footnote

of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), is pleased hereby to make the

Numbers for the purpose of registration under section 240 of the Excise
Tax Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-41).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936612 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Social Insurance Number Disclosure
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-41).

## [Disclosure Requirement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936614 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Every individual who
applies to the Minister to be registered pursuant to section 240 of the
[Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) in the form prescribed by the Minister
for that purpose shall disclose therein the individual's Social
Insurance Number.
