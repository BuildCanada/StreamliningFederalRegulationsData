## Canada Production Insurance Regulations {#canada-production-insurance-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2005-62
:::

[FARM INCOME PROTECTION ACT](/eng/acts/F-3.3)

Registration 2005-03-22

Canada Production Insurance Regulations

P.C. 2005-382 2005-03-22
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 18(2) of the [Farm Income Protection
Act](/eng/acts/F-3.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDADA}, the Minister
of Agriculture and Agri-Food has consulted with the provinces that are
parties to the agreements entered into pursuant to subsections 4(1) and
12(5) of that Act concerning the annexed [Canada Production Insurance
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-62);

Whereas the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food has been of the
opinion that exceptional circumstances exist that require that action be
taken outside the scope of the crop insurance programs established under
the agreements entered into under subsection 4(1) of that Act, in order
to compensate producers for damage to agricultural products that is
caused by wildlife, and the Minister has consulted with those provinces
under subsection 12(1) of that Act to determine the appropriate action
to be taken to remedy those circumstances;

And whereas the Governor in Council, following those consultations, has
authorized the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food under subsection
12(5) of that Act to enter into agreements with one or more of those
provinces for the purpose of assisting producers of agricultural
products with respect to damage to agricultural products that is caused
by wildlife;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food, pursuant to
subsection 18(1) of the [Farm Income Protection
Act](/eng/acts/F-3.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Canada Production Insurance
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-62).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722118 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Farm Income Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/F-3.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[actuarially sound manner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[actuarially sound manner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of
    premium rates under an insurance plan, means that the premium rates
    provide for all costs associated with the transfer of risk and are
    an estimate of the expected value of future losses under the
    insurance plan. ([saines pratiques d'actuariat]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[actuary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[actuary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Fellow of the Canadian
    Institute of Actuaries. ([actuaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[coverage level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[coverage level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the percentage of the
    value of production of an agricultural product that is insured under
    an insurance plan. ([niveau de protection]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[exposure unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exposure unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a unit of measurement of
    an agricultural product that is used to determine the amount of
    insurance coverage and related premiums under an insurance
    contract. ([unité d'exposition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[farm enterprise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[farm enterprise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all exposure units of
    agricultural products that are produced by a producer or by two or
    more producers who have a common interest in the agricultural
    products. ([exploitation agricole]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insurance plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurance plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a set of insurance
    features under an insurance program for an agricultural
    product. ([régime d'assurance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insurance program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurance program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a crop insurance
    program that provides insurance coverage that is based or not based
    on yield and that may provide for wildlife compensation. ([programme
    d'assurance production]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insured producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insured producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a holder of a
    contract of insurance who has an insurable interest in one or more
    agricultural products covered by the contract. ([producteur
    assuré]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[national certification guidelines]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[national certification guidelines]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    guidelines, developed by Canada in consultation with the provinces,
    as amended from time to time, that describe the work and
    documentation needed in preparing the certifications required by
    these Regulations and that are available on request from the
    Minister. ([lignes directrices nationales sur les
    certifications]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[new agricultural product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[new agricultural product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    agricultural product for which protection is offered under an
    insurance plan at a coverage level that does not exceed 70% of the
    value of production of the agricultural product and for which
    insufficient data exists to meet the national certification
    guidelines. ([nouveau produit agricole]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[production guarantee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[production guarantee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount of
    insurance protection provided under an insurance contract for an
    agricultural product, based on the probable yield of the
    agricultural product, the exposure units insured and the coverage
    level established under the contract. ([garantie de
    production]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[production insurance agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[production insurance agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    agreement entered into by Canada and a province to allow
    contributions to be made by the parties to an insurance
    program. ([accord sur l'assurance production]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[responsible officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[responsible officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of any
    province, the person or persons designated by the province to be
    responsible for the administration of the province's insurance
    program. ([agent compétent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[risk area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[risk area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2018-118, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[risk-splitting benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[risk-splitting benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means insurance
    coverage benefits that are deemed to be risk-splitting benefits in
    accordance with subsection 4(4). ([garantie de fractionnement du
    risque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[wildlife compensation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wildlife compensation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an amount that
    is paid to producers to compensate them for damage that is specified
    in a production insurance agreement and that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is caused by wildlife specified in the agreement;
      and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not included as part of an insurance
      plan. ([indemnités pour les dommages causés par la
      faune]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-118, s. 1

## [General Provisions for Programs and Plans Based or Not Based on Yield]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722142 .Part}

### [New Agricultural Products and Changes to Insurance Plans]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722143 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligible agricultural products

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  production insurance agreement shall provide for the agricultural
  products eligible for coverage under the insurance plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial submission

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The agreement shall provide that any proposal by the
  province, that is intended to take effect in the following year, for
  the introduction of an insurance plan for a new agricultural product,
  or for a change to an insurance program or insurance plan, that has a
  financial impact shall be submitted by the responsible officer of the
  province to the Minister, in writing, with complete operational
  details and an estimate of the financial impact at least 60 days
  before the proposed date of implementation, unless otherwise agreed to
  by Canada and the province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility of provincial proposal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The agreement shall provide that Canada shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} inform the responsible officer of the province in
    writing, as soon as possible, whether the proposal is eligible for
    contributions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} outline any elements of the proposal that are not
    in compliance with the Act or these Regulations.

- SOR/2018-118, s. 20(E)

### [Coverage Levels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722155 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum coverage levels

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A production insurance
agreement shall provide that the manner of determining the percentage of
the value of production of an agricultural product that may be insured,
as set out in the agreement, shall be subject to the following maximum
level:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 90% of the probable yield for insurance plans based
  on yield;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 90% of the value of production for insurance plans
  not based on yield if production of the agricultural product is
  reduced by weather or other agricultural perils; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for all other insurance plans not based on yield, as
  covered under section 15, 100% of the value of production minus the
  long-term average loss percentage for the agricultural product or 90%
  of the value of production if the long-term average loss percentage
  cannot be determined.

### [Determination of Losses]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722161 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insured perils

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  production insurance agreement shall provide that all insurance
  contracts to which the agreement applies, except those covering losses
  specified in paragraph 15(c), shall provide insurance against more
  than one of the perils specified in the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of losses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The agreement shall provide that losses of a farm
  enterprise, with the exception of the losses referred to in section
  15, shall be determined by subtracting the total production of an
  agricultural product, adjusted for any quality losses, from the total
  production guarantee for the agricultural product on all exposure
  units of the farm enterprise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agricultural products to be
  distinguishable

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The agreement shall provide that, for the losses in
  respect of an agricultural product to be determined separately under
  an insurance plan, the agricultural product shall meet the following
  criteria:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the agricultural product can be distinguished from
    other like agricultural products;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the agricultural product has a separate market
    price from other like agricultural products;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the agricultural product has different productive
    capabilities or production risks from other like agricultural
    products; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} there is sufficient volume of production and
    availability of data to ensure the financial viability of an
    insurance plan related to the agricultural product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The agreement shall provide that any insurance
  coverage benefit under an insurance contract that is an exception to
  subsections (1) to (3) is deemed to be a risk-splitting benefit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Level of premium support

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The agreement shall provide that the risk-splitting
  benefit shall be subject to the level of premium support from Canada
  that is associated with high-cost production coverage, as set out in
  the agreement, and shall be supported as being feasible by
  documentation that demonstrates that the following conditions are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} standards and procedures for adjusting losses are
    in place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} administrative resources agreed to as adequate by
    the parties to the agreement are available;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} administrative costs associated with the
    risk-splitting benefit are reported separately from the other
    administrative costs associated with insurance plans; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} there is sufficient volume of production and
    availability of data to ensure the financial viability of the
    risk-splitting benefits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No double indemnity

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The agreement shall provide that if there is a
  risk-splitting benefit under an insurance contract, there shall be
  loss adjustment and payment processes to ensure that damage is paid
  for only once or that any indemnity payable under a risk-splitting
  benefit is deducted from any other indemnities payable under an
  insurance contract.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-118, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Risk-splitting benefit

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A production insurance
agreement shall provide that a risk-splitting benefit may be subject to
the level of premium support from Canada that is associated with
comprehensive production coverage, as set out in the agreement, if one
of the following conditions is met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the province can prove that the risk-splitting
  benefits arise from third-party intervention;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the province demonstrates, by a statistical analysis,
  that operating a risk-splitting benefit results in indemnity payments
  that are less than would otherwise be paid under the insurance plan;
  or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total costs of the risk-splitting benefit are
  less than the total costs for the same perils and coverage level under
  an insurance plan that does not provide risk-splitting benefits.

- SOR/2018-118, s. 20(E)

### [Manner of Determining Premium Rates]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722192 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Premium rate methodologies

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  production insurance agreement shall provide for premium rate
  methodologies and shall provide that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} premium rates shall be determined in accordance
    with those methodologies; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} those methodologies shall be subject to the
    national certification guidelines.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Those premium rate methodologies shall reflect the
  following requirements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} premium rates shall be established in an
    actuarially sound manner;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} premium rates shall provide for an amount to be
    applied to the repayment of a deficit under the insurance program;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} premium rates for risk-splitting benefits shall be
    determined separately from the premium rates for other benefits in
    the insurance plan;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all elements of an insurance plan or program that
    have cost implications shall be included in the determination of
    premiums; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} premium rates shall include a margin for building
    reserves.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Actuary's opinions

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  production insurance agreement shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} require the submission of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an opinion, signed by an actuary, certifying that
      premium rate methodologies have been established in an actuarially
      sound manner, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an opinion, signed by an actuary, certifying
      that the insurance program is self-sustaining; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide for the date for meeting the requirements
    of paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to submit opinions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Until the opinions required by paragraph (1)(a) are
  submitted, Canada shall limit its payments toward premium and
  reinsurance payments under the agreement in respect of the relevant
  fiscal year and subsequent fiscal years to the following amounts:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 90% of the amount otherwise payable under the
    agreement if one of the requirements of paragraph (1)(a) is met; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 80% of the amount otherwise payable under the
    agreement if neither of the requirements of paragraph (1)(a) is met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualified opinions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the requirements of paragraph 1(a) have not been
  met, premium receipts used for calculating a payment from the Crop
  Reinsurance Fund shall be determined by the Minister using the
  estimated premium receipts that would have been collected if the
  opinions had indicated that the requirements of that paragraph had
  been met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New agricultural products

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The opinion referred to in subparagraph (1)(a)(i) is
  not required with respect to the manner of establishing premium rates
  for new agricultural products.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-118, ss. 3, 20(E)

## [Coverage Based on Yields]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722222 .Part}

### [Probable Yields]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722223 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Probable yield methodologies

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  production insurance agreement shall provide for probable yield
  methodologies and shall provide that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} probable yields shall be determined in accordance
    with those methodologies; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} those methodologies shall be subject to the
    national certification guidelines.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statistical measure of yield

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In establishing those methodologies, a statistical
  measure of yield that is based on actual yields per seeded area shall
  be used. That measure may be adjusted to reflect the demonstrated
  productive capability of producers and may take into account the
  average level of quality produced related to the levels of quality of
  the agricultural product provided for in an insurance plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Data sources

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If insurance data is unavailable or unrepresentative
  as a data source for determining the probable yield of an agricultural
  product, other data sources may be used, as agreed to by the parties
  to the agreement.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-118, s. 4(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Actuary's opinion

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  production insurance agreement shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} require the submission of an opinion, signed by an
    actuary, certifying that the probable yield methodologies result in
    probable yields that accurately reflect the producers' production
    capability for the agricultural product;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if that opinion is qualified, require the
    disclosure by the actuary of the extent and sources of any bias in
    the methodology; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide for the date for meeting the requirements
    of paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to submit opinion

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Until the opinion required by paragraph (1)(a) is
  submitted, Canada shall limit its payments toward premium and
  reinsurance payments under the agreement in respect of the relevant
  fiscal year and subsequent fiscal years to 75% of the amount otherwise
  payable under the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualified opinion

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the opinion of the actuary indicates that there is
  a bias in the probable yield methodology, payments by Canada shall be
  limited to the amount that would otherwise be provided for under the
  agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this section.

  [[provincial actual yield to provincial probable yield ratio]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[provincial actual yield to provincial probable yield
      ratio]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the weighted average of all
      actual or reported yields for a year, adjusted to reflect the
      level of quality that is provided for in an insurance plan,
      divided by the provincial probable yield for that year. ([rapport
      entre le rendement réel provincial et le rendement probable
      provincial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[provincial moving average yield]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[provincial moving average yield]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      mean of the annual weighted averages of actual or reported yields,
      adjusted to reflect the level of quality that is provided for in
      an insurance plan. ([moyenne mobile du rendement
      provincial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[provincial probable yield]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[provincial probable yield]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      weighted average of probable yields, determined in accordance with
      section 8, of all producers insured under an insurance plan for a
      year. ([rendement probable provincial]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[provincial probable yield to provincial moving average yield ratio]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[provincial probable yield to provincial moving average yield
      ratio]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the provincial probable yield
      for a year, divided by the provincial moving average yield for
      that year. ([rapport entre le rendement probable provincial et la
      moyenne mobile du rendement provincial]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tests for probable yield

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A production insurance agreement shall require the
  province to submit documents to the Minister to demonstrate, using
  insurance data of previous years, that the provincial probable yield
  of an agricultural product in a year meets either of the following
  tests, which shall be subject to the national certification
  guidelines:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the provincial probable yield to provincial moving
    average yield ratio for the year does not exceed a value of 1.015;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the mean of the provincial actual yield to
    provincial probable yield ratio, based on ten years or more, is a
    value not less than 0.985.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-118, s. 6

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time for compliance

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  production insurance agreement shall include a schedule, in accordance
  with the national certification guidelines, that sets out the deadline
  for the province to submit the documents referred to in subsection
  10(2) for each insurance plan that is based on yield.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to submit documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Until the province submits the documents required by
  subsection 10(2), Canada shall limit its payments toward premium and
  reinsurance payments under the agreement in respect of the relevant
  fiscal year and subsequent fiscal years to 75% of the amount otherwise
  payable under the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents not demonstrating compliance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the documents submitted by the province do not
  demonstrate, using insurance data of previous years, that the
  provincial probable yield for an agricultural product in a given year
  meets the test set out in either paragraph 10(2)(a) or (b), payments
  by Canada shall be limited to the amount that would otherwise have
  been payable under the agreement.

### [Unit Value of an Agricultural Product]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722269 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unit value methodologies

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  production insurance agreement shall provide for methodologies for use
  in determining the value of eligible agricultural products and shall
  provide that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the unit values shall be determined in accordance
    with those methodologies; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} those methodologies shall be subject to the
    national certification guidelines.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional requirement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Those unit value methodologies shall be based on one
  of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a market price method that reflects estimated or
    actual farmgate or replacement values of the production;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a cost of production method using standard
    accounting procedures and recommended provincial agronomic
    practices; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an alternative method provided for in the
    agreement.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-118, s. 8(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-12-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 12"){.PITLink}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum unit value

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A production insurance
agreement shall provide that the province shall submit documents to the
Minister to demonstrate, using historical market prices or replacement
values, that the maximum unit value of an agricultural product meets
either of the following tests, which shall be subject to the national
certification guidelines:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ratio of the average of the maximum unit values
  to the moving average of the farmgate or replacement values, based on
  three or more years, does not exceed a value of 1.0; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the average of the ratios of the maximum unit value
  to the actual farmgate or replacement value, based on three or more
  years, does not exceed a value of 1.0.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Until the province submits the documents required by

  fiscal year and subsequent fiscal years to 90% of the amount otherwise

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the province submits the documents required by

  federal premium support provided for high-cost production coverage as
  set out in the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No administrative procedures to prevent
  moral hazard

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the values determined under paragraphs 13(a)
  and (b) exceed 1.0 and the province cannot demonstrate that adequate
  administrative procedures are in place to prevent moral hazard,
  payments by Canada shall be based on the lower of the values
  determined under those paragraphs, which is deemed to be 1.0.

  can demonstrate that adequate administrative procedures are in place
  to prevent moral hazard, payments by Canada shall be based on the
  lower of the values determined under those paragraphs, which is deemed
  to be 1.2 if it exceeds 1.2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Methodology without bias

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (3), if the province can
  demonstrate that the methodology used to determine the unit value
  contains no bias, only payments by Canada related to the extent that
  the lower of the values determined under paragraphs 13(a) and (b)
  exceeds 1.0 shall be limited to the level of federal premium support
  provided for high-cost production coverage as set out in the
  agreement.

## [Coverage Not Based on Yield]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722303 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insurable losses

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** If a probable yield is
not used as the basis of determining coverage under an insurance plan,
the following losses, in whole or in part, may be covered in an
insurance plan:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} losses in respect of stands of fruit trees or other
  perennial plants;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} losses in respect of livestock; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} losses in respect of agricultural products caused by
  production being prevented or reduced by weather or another
  agricultural peril.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of production methodologies

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  production insurance agreement shall provide for methodologies for
  determining the value of production and shall provide that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} values of production shall be determined in
    accordance with those methodologies; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} those methodologies shall be subject to the
    national certification guidelines.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional requirement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Those methodologies are also subject to the following
  maximum limits:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for losses referred to in paragraphs 15(a) and (b),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the average cost to re-establish the interrupted
      production resulting from the loss of the fruit trees, perennial
      plants or livestock,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 80% of the present value of the expected net
      income for the period required to re-establish production, where
      net income is the difference between the value of production of an
      agricultural product and the average costs of production,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the average costs of production incurred with
      an insured agricultural product, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the expected or actual value of an insured
      agricultural product; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for losses referred to in paragraph 15(c),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the average costs of operations in preparation
      for seeding and planting, namely, summer-fallowing, cultivating,
      fertilizing the land, purchasing plants for transplanting and
      other related activities, and the costs incurred with respect to
      land rental,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 80% of the expected net income for an
      agricultural product, where net income is the value of production
      of the agricultural product minus the average costs of production
      incurred for the agricultural product,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the average costs of production incurred with
      an insured agricultural product, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the expected or actual value of an insured
      agricultural product.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tests for value of production

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A production insurance
agreement shall provide that the province shall submit documents to the
Minister to demonstrate that the value of production of an agricultural
product in the province meets either of the following tests, which shall
be subject to the national certification guidelines:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ratio of the average of the maximum values of
  production to the moving average of the farmgate or replacement
  values, based on five or more years of historical data, does not
  exceed a value of 1.0; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the average of the ratios of the maximum value of
  production to the actual farmgate or replacement value, based on five
  or more years of historical data, does not exceed a value of 1.0.

- SOR/2018-118, s. 11

## [Wildlife Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722347 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Federal contribution

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  production insurance agreement shall provide that Canada may
  contribute to wildlife compensation for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} damage or injury to an eligible agricultural
    product caused by wildlife, which may include eligible costs related
    to repair, replacement or medical treatment of the agricultural
    product; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the repair or replacement of eligible structures
    that are vital to the production of the agricultural product that
    have been damaged by wildlife.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Details to be in the agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), all eligible
  agricultural products, structures that are vital to the production of
  the agricultural products, costs and other conditions for payment
  shall be provided for in the agreement.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-118, s. 13(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-19-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 19"){.PITLink}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of damage or injury

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of paragraph 19(1)(a), a production insurance agreement
  shall provide that the value of damage or injury to an eligible
  agricultural product caused by wildlife shall be determined by
  subtracting the value of the agricultural product that is produced
  after the damage or injury occurred from the value of the agricultural
  product that, based on an inspection of the damage or injury, it is
  estimated would have been produced before the occurrence of the damage
  or injury.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Damage to structure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph 19(1)(b), a production
  insurance agreement shall provide that the value of the damage to an
  eligible structure caused by wildlife shall be determined as being the
  cost to have the structure repaired or replaced.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit on payments

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  production insurance agreement shall provide that the amount of
  wildlife compensation paid to a producer for damage or injury to an
  eligible agricultural product or structure may not exceed 80% of the
  value of the damage or injury.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of value

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), the agreement
  shall provide for the manner of determining the value of the eligible
  agricultural product.

## [Payments by Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722375 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Negligence in program administration

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** A production insurance
agreement shall provide that no contribution shall be made by the
federal government to premiums, wildlife compensation costs for eligible
agricultural products, and related administrative expenses that are a
consequence of negligence, including wrongful dismissal of personnel, in
the administration of the insurance program.

- SOR/2018-118, s. 16

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Breach of agreement

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A production insurance
agreement shall provide that, if there is a failure by the province to
comply with a requirement of these Regulations, other than a requirement
to which subsection 7(3), 9(3), 11(3), 14(3) or 18(3) applies, the
Minister may withhold payment of that portion of the contributions under
the agreement that relates to the non-compliance until the situation is
rectified.

the limitation of payments provided for in subsections 7(2), 9(2),
11(2), 14(2) and 18(2) and section 24 shall not exceed 25% of the amount
otherwise payable by Canada toward premium payments under an agreement
for each year.

## [Records and Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722386 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documentation

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** A production insurance
agreement shall provide that the province shall maintain, and shall
provide to the Minister when requested to do so, any documentation
related to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the establishment of the amounts of the protection
  for producers;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the procedures and measures used to determine
  production losses, quality losses and losses that are covered by
  insurance plans that are not based on yield, including those losses
  associated with risk-splitting and wildlife compensation; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the administrative procedures required to carry out
  the administration of an insurance plan or wildlife compensation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insurance plan records

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** A production insurance
agreement shall provide that the province shall keep records in relation
to the following items in respect of an insurance plan:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} producer identification, agricultural product
  identification, risk-splitting benefits and elements of insurance plan
  identification, year, distinguishable groups of producers, risk area,
  area insured or exposure unit, probable yield per area, actual yield

  loss percentage and level of protection; and

  indicating the nature and extent of loss and the number and amount of
  indemnity payments paid, according to agricultural products or

  provide the Minister with the following reports and information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an annual report on the administration of the
    insurance program, including wildlife compensation, if applicable,
    that sets out the audited financial statements, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} information on the status of the provincial

      funds, if applicable, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} current information by agricultural product,

      insurance in force, the number of exposure units or the area
      insured, the number of producers insured, the amount of premiums
      collected and the number and total amount of indemnities paid for
      that year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a quarterly report estimating, by agricultural
    product, the number of insurance contracts written, the amount of
    insurance in force, the amount of premiums paid, the number of
    exposure units or the area insured, the number of claims paid and
    the amount of indemnities paid;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a quarterly report estimating, by agricultural
    product, the number of claims for indemnity and the amount of
    indemnities paid for wildlife compensation; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} reports and information for the insurance plans,
    and wildlife compensation, if applicable, requested by Canada for
    the purpose of auditing, evaluating and forecasting Canada's future
    financial commitments and to ensure adequate linkages between
    insurance programs and other business risk management programs.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(d), the agreement
  shall provide for the specific data to be provided.

  subsection (1) is subject to the province's privacy legislation.
