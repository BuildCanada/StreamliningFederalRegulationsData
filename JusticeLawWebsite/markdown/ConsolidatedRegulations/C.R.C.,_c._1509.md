## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-524536 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Statutory Instruments
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1509).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-524538 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Statutory Instruments
    Act](/eng/acts/S-22); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[exempt from examination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exempt from examination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means exempt from
    the application of subsection 3(1) of the Act; ([soustrait à
    l'examen]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[exempt from publication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exempt from publication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means exempt from
    the application of subsection 11(1) of the Act; ([soustrait à la
    publication]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[exempt from registration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exempt from registration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means exempt from
    the application of subsection 5(1) of the Act. ([soustrait à
    l'enregistrement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Exemption from Examination]{.HTitleText1} {#h-524544 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following proposed
regulations and classes of proposed regulations are exempt from
examination:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} each proposed regulation that would, if it were made,
  be a regulation that would be exempt from registration; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} each proposed regulation described in subsection
  15(2), other than a proposed regulation described in paragraph (d)
  thereof, that would, if it were made, be a regulation that would be
  exempt from publication.

## [Transmission and Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-524548 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), copies of every regulation transmitted to the Clerk
  of the Privy Council pursuant to subsection 5(1) of the Act shall be
  transmitted by personal service.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Copies of a regulation may be transmitted to the
  Clerk of the Privy Council pursuant to subsection 5(1) of the Act by
  ordinary mail if they are mailed not later than the day next following
  the day on which the regulation is made.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where a regulation is
transmitted to the Clerk of the Privy Council pursuant to subsection
5(1) of the Act, the Clerk of the Privy Council shall register the
regulation

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by recording

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its name,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the regulation-making authority,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the statutory or other authority pursuant to
    which it was made,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the date upon which it was made, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the date upon which it is registered; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by assigning to it the designation "S.O.R." followed
  by an appropriate number to distinguish it from any other regulation.

- 2015, c. 33, s. 4(F)

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Clerk of the Privy
Council shall register every statutory instrument and other document,
other than a regulation, that is published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/)

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by recording

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its name or the nature of its subject matter,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the authority that issued, made or
    established it,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the statutory or other authority pursuant to
    which it was issued, made or established, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the date upon which it was published; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by assigning to it an appropriate number to
  distinguish it from any other document that has been published in the
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

## [Exemption from Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-524569 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The following classes of
regulations, the registration of which, in the opinion of the Governor
in Council, is not reasonably practicable due to the number of
regulations of those classes, are hereby exempt from registration:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} regulations made under the authority of section 12 of
  the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/97-381, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} "instructions to the trade" issued by the Canadian
  Wheat Board under the authority of the [Canadian Wheat Board
  Act](/eng/acts/C-24);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} regulations issued or made by the Canadian Grain
  Commission under the [Canada Grain Act](/eng/acts/G-10) that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are directed to a single person or body, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} apply to licensees for a period of time
    terminating not later than the end of the crop year with respect to
    which they were issued or made;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} orders made by the Minister of Labour under
  subsection 135(3) of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2);

- [(e.1)]{.lawlabel} orders made by a safety officer under subsection
  135(4) of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2);

- [(e.2)]{.lawlabel} approvals and directions given by a safety officer
  under section 137 of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2);

- [(e.3)]{.lawlabel} directions issued by a safety officer under
  subsection 145(1) or (2) of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} orders and regulations issued or made by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Transport Commission established by
    the National Transportation Act, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Radio-television and
    Telecommunications Commission established by the [Canadian
    Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission
    Act](/eng/acts/C-22) in relation to telegraphs or telephones

  that are directed to a single person or body;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} by-laws, rules and regulations made under section 233
  of the Railway Act, other than by-laws, rules and regulations
  respecting

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the smoking of tobacco, expectorating, and the
    commission of any nuisance in or upon trains, stations or other
    premises occupied by a railway company, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the travelling upon or using of a railway by
    members of the public;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} orders and directions issued by the designated
  officer under subsection 22(1) of the [Northern Pipeline
  Act](/eng/acts/N-26);

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} orders made by the Energy Supplies Allocation Board
  under the [Energy Supplies Emergency Act](/eng/acts/E-9) that are
  directed to a single person or body;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} orders made by a person engaged or employed in the
  administration or enforcement of the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14)
  whereby a close time, fishing quota, type of fishing gear or equipment
  or limit on the size or weight of fish that has been fixed in respect
  of an area under regulations made under that Act is varied in respect
  of that area or any portion of that area;

- [(j.1)]{.lawlabel} orders made under subsection 3(1) of the
  [Management of Contaminated Fisheries
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-351);

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} permits issued by the Minister of Transport, or by a
  person designated by the Minister of Transport, under paragraph
  3(3)(c) or subsection 27(1) of the Transportation of Dangerous Goods
  Act;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} membership rules established by a band under
  paragraph 10(2)(a) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) and by-laws made
  by the council of a band under section 81 or subsection 83(1) or
  85.1(1) of that Act; and

- [m)]{.lawlabel} licences, permits and other authorizing instruments
  issued pursuant to regulations made under the [Canada National Parks
  Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01).

## [Publication]{.HTitleText1} {#h-524603 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) shall be published in three parts,
namely, Part I, Part II and Part III.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The typography, style and
format of Part I, Part II and Part III of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) shall be substantially similar to
the typography, style and format of the public general Acts of the
Parliament of Canada.

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3) and
  section 14, every statutory instrument, other than a regulation, and
  every other document that is required or authorized to be published in
  the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) by or under the
  authority of the Act or any other Act of Parliament shall be published
  in Part I of the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every regulation, other than a regulation that is
  exempt from publication, shall be published in Part II of the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The following classes of statutory instruments or
  other documents shall be published in Part II of the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} orders made by the Governor in Council under the
    [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
    Act](/eng/acts/P-34);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} orders made by the Governor in Council whereby any
    member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada is designated to act
    as the Minister for the purposes of any Act of Parliament or to act
    as the appropriate Minister for the purposes of the [Financial
    Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} proclamations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} orders made under section 23 of the [Financial
    Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} rules governing the practice and procedure in
    proceedings before a judicial body that is established under an
    enactment of a province that are required to be published in the
    [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) by an Act of Parliament;
    and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/93-245, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} orders fixing the day or days on which an Act or
    any provision thereof shall come into force.

## [Publication Directed or Authorized]{.HTitleText1} {#h-524623 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Clerk of the Privy
Council may direct or authorize the publication in Part I or Part II of
the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) of any statutory
instrument or other document the publication of which, in the opinion of
the Clerk of the Privy Council, is in the public interest, unless, in
the case of a statutory instrument, the inspection and obtaining of
copies of the statutory instrument have been precluded by section 22 or,
in the case of any other document, the document contains information the
disclosure of which is otherwise prohibited by law.

- SOR/93-245, s. 5
- SOR/94-479, s. 2(E)

## [Exemption from Publication]{.HTitleText1} {#h-524627 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  classes of regulations that are exempt from registration are hereby
  exempt from publication.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following regulations and classes of regulations,
  being regulations or classes of regulations that the Governor in
  Council is satisfied affect or are likely to affect only a limited
  number of persons and with respect to which the Governor in Council is
  satisfied that reasonable steps have been or will be taken for the
  purpose of bringing their purport to the notice of those persons
  affected or likely to be affected by them, are hereby exempt from
  publication:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/91-430, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(b) and (c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/78-814, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} orders or directions made by the Minister of
    Transport or the Deputy Minister of Transport with respect to the
    matters referred to in paragraph 6(1)(f) of the [Aeronautics
    Act](/eng/acts/A-2);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/93-245, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} notices served or sent by the Minister of Labour
    under subsection 155(1) or 253(1) of the [Canada Labour
    Code](/eng/acts/L-2);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Regulations for the Transportation of Dangerous
    Commodities by Rail made by the Canadian Transport Commission under
    section 296 of the Railway Act;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} regulations made by the Canadian Transport
    Commission under paragraph 14(1)(c) of the [Aeronautics
    Act](/eng/acts/A-2) or under paragraph 42(e) of the National
    Transportation Act;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} orders made by the Coal Mining Safety Commission
    pursuant to subsection 137.2(3) of the [Canada Labour
    Code](/eng/acts/L-2);

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} regulations and other instruments made by a
    provincial authority pursuant to an agreement entered into under
    subsection 5.81(1) of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2); and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} remission orders made under section 115 of the
    [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of the remission of
    anti-dumping or countervailing duties levied under the [Special
    Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15) on any dumped or subsidized
    goods that are the subject of a report to the Minister of Finance
    under section 45 of the [Special Import Measures
    Act](/eng/acts/S-15) where those orders contain information that is
    derived from information that is treated as confidential under
    section 49 of the [Canadian International Trade Tribunal
    Act](/eng/acts/C-18.3).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The following regulations or classes of regulations,
  being regulations or classes of regulations the publication of which
  the Governor in Council is satisfied could reasonably be expected to
  be injurious to the conduct by the Government of Canada of
  federal-provincial affairs or the conduct of international affairs,
  the defence of Canada or any state allied or associated with Canada,
  as defined in subsection 15(2) of the [Access to Information
  Act](/eng/acts/A-1), or the detection, prevention or suppression of
  subversive or hostile activities, as defined in subsection 15(2) of
  the [Access to Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1), are hereby exempt from
  publication:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} regulations that bear a security classification and
    contain information in respect of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the location or movement of military or civilian
      personnel of the Department of National Defence,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the administration or training of the Canadian
      Forces,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} tactical or strategic operations or operational
      plans of the Canadian Forces,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the function of any unit or other element of the
      Canadian Forces, or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} materiel as defined in the [National Defence
      Act](/eng/acts/N-5) including any article or object being
      designed, developed or produced with the intention that it will
      become materiel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} regulations that bear a national or international
    security classification and relate to Canada's role in the North
    Atlantic Treaty Organization or to any international agreement, one
    of the purposes of which is to provide for the defence of Canada or
    the detection, prevention or suppression of subversive or hostile
    activities, as defined in subsection 15(2) of the [Access to
    Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} regulations relating to the operation and
    maintenance of materiel as defined in the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5) that contain information provided by the
    government of a country other than Canada, or by a person having a
    proprietary interest therein, on the understanding that such
    information would not be disclosed except to the extent authorized
    by that government or person;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} regulations made by the Governor in Council that
    apply to specific permits or leases issued or granted under the
    [Canada Oil and Gas Land
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1518) in areas off the
    coast of Canada declared by the Governor in Council to be areas
    concerning which negotiations with another country may be necessary
    or are in progress for the purpose of delimiting jurisdiction; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} orders made by the Minister of Transport under
    section 3 of the [Canadian Aviation Security
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-111).

    Act](/eng/acts/C-29); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2001, c. 41, s. 42\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} statutory instruments, other than regulations, the
    disclosure of which would reveal the location or movement of any
    explosive or the location of any manufacturer of explosives.

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/94-479, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The inspection of and the obtaining of copies of the
  following statutory instruments and classes of statutory instruments,
  being statutory instruments or classes of statutory instruments the
  inspection of which or the making of copies of which is not otherwise
  provided for by law and in respect of which the Governor in Council is
  satisfied that the inspection or the making of copies thereof as
  provided for by the Act would, if it were not precluded by these
  Regulations, result or be likely to result in injustice or undue
  hardship to any person or body affected thereby or in serious and
  unwarranted detriment to any such person or body in the matter or
  conduct of that person's or that body's affairs, are hereby precluded:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deportation orders, conditional deportation orders,
    departure notices, conditional departure notices, exclusion orders,
    conditional exclusion orders, rejection orders, detention orders and
    warrants for arrest issued or made under the [Immigration and
    Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) or under the authority of
    any immigration laws that were in force in Canada prior to April 10,
    1978;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} parole certificates and mandatory supervision
    certificates issued under section 12 of the Parole Act and warrants
    issued under subsection 10(2), 16(1) or (2) or section 18 of that
    Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} warrants made or issued under Part I of the
    [Corrections and Conditional Release Act](/eng/acts/C-44.6) whereby
    a person who has been sentenced or committed to a penitentiary is
    committed or transferred to any penitentiary in Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} pardons granted by the Governor in Council under
    subsection 4(5) of the [Criminal Records Act](/eng/acts/C-47) and
    any statutory instrument relating thereto;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} statutory instruments by which the salary or other
    remuneration of any person is fixed or approved by the Governor in
    Council except to the extent to which they provide for the fixing or
    approval thereof within a specified range;

  - [(f) and (g)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/78-814, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} interim prohibitory orders made under section 41 of
    the [Canada Post Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-10) if those orders
    have not been declared final;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} warrants and permits granted under subsection 22(1)
    of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) and permits or certificates
    given under section 104 of that Act;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} statutory instruments issued, made or established
    in the course of an inquiry under the Combines Investigation Act or
    an investigation ordered under section 114 of the [Canada
    Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8);

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} directions issued or made by the Governor in
    Council following a recommendation made by the Employment Support
    Board under subsection 15(1) of the [Employment Support
    Act](/eng/acts/E-5.8); and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} remission orders made under section 115 of the
    [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of the remission of
    anti-dumping or countervailing duties levied under the [Special
    Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15) on any dumped or subsidized
    goods that are the subject of a report to the Minister of Finance
    under section 45 of the [Special Import Measures
    Act](/eng/acts/S-15) where those orders contain information that is
    derived from information that is treated as confidential under
    section 49 of the [Canadian International Trade Tribunal
    Act](/eng/acts/C-18.3).
