## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1292980 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[refugee travel
document]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order, [[refugee
travel document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a travel document issued
under the United Nations Convention Relating to the Status of Refugees,
signed at Geneva on July 28, 1951, and the Protocol to that Convention,
signed at New York on January 31, 1967.

## [Remission and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1292983 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of identity or refugee travel
document

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
fees paid or payable under subsection 2(1) of the [Passport and Other
Travel Document Services Fees
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-253) for the issuance of a
certificate of identity or refugee travel document if the following
conditions are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application is made in Canada, during the period
  beginning on May 31, 2021 and ending on May 30, 2026, for the issuance
  of a certificate of identity or refugee travel document;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person for whom the application is made is an
  Indigenous person who underwent an imposed name change in Canada or is
  a descendant of such a person;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person for whom the application is made holds a
  certificate of identity or refugee travel document that is valid for a
  period of at least six months beginning on the day on which the
  application is made;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the application is made for the purpose of replacing
  the name that appears on the certificate of identity or refugee travel
  document referred to in paragraph (c) with the reclaimed name of the
  person for whom the application is made;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the application is accompanied by a birth certificate
  or other documentary evidence establishing that the name of the person
  for whom the application is made has been changed to their reclaimed
  name; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the certificate of identity or refugee travel
  document issued as a result of the application has the same period of
  validity as that of the certificate of identity or refugee travel
  document it replaces.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Travel document --- consular services

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
fees paid or payable under section 4 of the [Consular Services Fees
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-538) for the issuance of a travel
document if the following conditions are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application is made in Canada or in a mission
  abroad, during the period beginning on May 31, 2021 and ending on May
  30, 2026, for the issuance of a travel document;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person for whom the application is made is an
  Indigenous person who underwent an imposed name change in Canada or is
  a descendant of such a person;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person for whom the application is made holds a
  travel document that is valid for a period of at least six months
  beginning on the day on which the application is made;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the application is made for the purpose of replacing
  the name that appears on the travel document referred to in
  paragraph (c) with the reclaimed name of the person for whom the
  application is made;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the application is accompanied by a birth certificate
  or other documentary evidence establishing that the name of the person
  for whom the application is made has been changed to their reclaimed
  name; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the travel document issued as a result of the
  application has the same period of validity as that of the travel
  document it replaces.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permanent resident card

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
fees paid or payable under subsections 308(1) and (2) of the
[Immigration and Refugee Protection
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227) for the processing of an
application for a permanent resident card if the following conditions
are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application is made in Canada, during the period
  beginning on May 31, 2021 and ending on May 30, 2026, for the issuance
  of a permanent resident card;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person for whom the application is made is an
  Indigenous person who underwent an imposed name change in Canada or is
  a descendant of such a person;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person for whom the application is made holds a
  permanent resident card that is valid for a period of at least six
  months beginning on the day on which the application is made;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the application is made for the purpose of replacing
  the name that appears on the permanent resident card referred to in
  paragraph (c) with the reclaimed name of the person for whom the
  application is made; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the application is accompanied by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a birth certificate or other documentary evidence
    establishing that the name of the person for whom the application is
    made has been changed to their reclaimed name, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a statutory declaration that the person for whom
    the application is made is a person described in paragraph (b), is
    reclaiming their name and is unable to obtain the certificate or
    evidence referred to in subparagraph (i).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of citizenship

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
fees paid or payable under subsection 31(1) of the [Citizenship
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-246) for an application for a
certificate of citizenship if the following conditions are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application is made, during the period beginning
  on May 31, 2021 and ending on May 30, 2026, for the issuance of a
  certificate of citizenship;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person for whom the application is made is an
  Indigenous person who underwent an imposed name change in Canada or is
  a descendant of such a person;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the day on which the application is made, the
  person for whom it is made holds a valid certificate of citizenship;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the application is made for the purpose of replacing
  the name that appears on the certificate of citizenship referred to in
  paragraph (c) with the reclaimed name of the person for whom the
  application is made; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the application is accompanied by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a birth certificate or other documentary evidence
    establishing that the name of the person for whom the application is
    made has been changed to their reclaimed name, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a statutory declaration that the person for whom
    the application is made is a person described in paragraph (b), is
    reclaiming their name and is unable to obtain the certificate or
    evidence referred to in subparagraph (i).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1293018 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
