## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-788964 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Pooled Registered Pension
    Plans Act](/eng/acts/P-15.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a person, means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an individual of whom the person is the legal
      parent;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an individual who is wholly dependent on the
      person for support and of whom the person has, or immediately
      before the individual attained the age of 19 years had, in law or
      in fact, the custody and control;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a child of the person's spouse or common-law
      partner; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a spouse or common-law partner of a child of the
      person. ([enfant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all fees, levies and other
    charges that reduce a member's return on investment other than those
    that are triggered by the member's
    actions. ([coûts]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[debt obligation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[debt obligation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bond, debenture,
    note or other evidence of indebtedness of an entity. ([titre de
    créance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[deferred life annuity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deferred life annuity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in subsection 2(1) of the [Pension Benefits Standards
    Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19). ([prestation viagère
    différée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a corporation, trust, partnership or fund or an
      unincorporated association or organization; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Canada or of a province
      or the government of a foreign country or of a political
      subdivision of a foreign country, or an agency of any of
      them. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[immediate life annuity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[immediate life annuity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in subsection 2(1) of the [Pension Benefits Standards
    Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19). ([prestation viagère
    immédiate]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[life income fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[life income fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registered
    retirement income fund that meets the conditions set out in section
    41. ([fonds de revenu viager]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a deposit, financial lease,
    conditional sales contract, repurchase agreement and any other
    similar arrangement for obtaining money or credit, but does not
    include investments in securities or the making of an acceptance,
    endorsement or other guarantee mechanism. ([prêt]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[locked-in RRSP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[locked-in RRSP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registered retirement
    savings plan that meets the conditions set out in section 38. ([REÉR
    immobilisé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[marketplace]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marketplace]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an exchange;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a quotation and trade-reporting system;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other entity not included in paragraph (a)
      or (b) that

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} constitutes, maintains or provides a market or
        facility for bringing together buyers and sellers of securities
        or derivatives,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} brings together the orders for securities or
        derivatives of multiple buyers and sellers, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} uses established, non-discretionary methods
        under which the orders interact and with which the buyers and
        sellers entering the orders agree to the terms of a
        trade. ([marché]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[market value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[market value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an asset, means
    the price that would be obtained for the purchase or sale of the
    asset in an open market under conditions requisite to a fair
    transaction between parties who are at arm's length and acting
    prudently, knowledgeably and willingly. ([valeur
    marchande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an
    entity. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[PRPP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[PRPP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pooled registered pension
    plan. ([RPAC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registered retirement income fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registered retirement income fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the
    same meaning as in subsection 146.3(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([fonds enregistré de revenu de
    retraite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registered retirement savings plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registered retirement savings plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the
    same meaning as in subsection 146(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([régime enregistré
    d'épargne-retraite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[related party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[related party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a PRPP, means a
    person --- other than Her Majesty in right of Canada or of a
    province, or an agency of either one of them, or a bank, trust
    company or other financial institution that holds the assets of the
    PRPP but that is not the administrator of the PRPP --- who is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an officer, an employee or a member of the board
      of directors of the administrator of the PRPP;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} responsible for holding or investing the assets
      of the PRPP, or an officer, director or employee of the person who
      is responsible for holding or investing those assets;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a member of the PRPP;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the spouse or common-law partner or a child of
      any person referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to (c);

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that is directly or indirectly
      controlled by a person referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to
      (d);

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an entity in which a person referred to in
      paragraph (a) or (b), or the spouse or common-law partner or a
      child of such a person, has a substantial investment; or

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} an entity that has a substantial investment in
      the administrator of the PRPP. ([apparenté]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[restricted life income fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[restricted life income fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    registered retirement income fund that meets the conditions set out
    in section 40. ([fonds de revenu viager restreint]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[restricted locked-in savings plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[restricted locked-in savings plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    registered retirement savings plan that meets the conditions set out
    in section 39. ([régime d'épargne immobilisé
    restreint]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a corporation, a share of any class
      of shares of the corporation or a debt obligation of the
      corporation, and includes a warrant of the corporation, but does
      not include a deposit with a financial institution or a document
      evidencing such a deposit; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any other entity, any ownership
      interest in or debt obligation of the
      entity. ([titre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou [valeur
      mobilière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[voting share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[voting share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a share of any class of
    shares of a corporation that carries voting rights under all
    circumstances or by reason of an event that has occurred and is
    continuing or by reason of a condition that has been
    fulfilled. ([action avec droit de vote]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-789009 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indirect investments

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, the making, holding or acquiring of an investment
indirectly by an administrator on behalf of a PRPP, the holding,
acquiring or owning of property indirectly by an administrator on behalf
of a PRPP or the lending of money indirectly by an administrator on
behalf of a PRPP includes the making, holding, acquiring, owning or
lending of an investment, a property or money, as the case may be, by a
mutual fund, a pooled fund, a segregated fund or a trust fund in which
the funds of a member's account have been invested.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of these Regulations,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person or an administrator of a PRPP controls a
    corporation if securities of the corporation to which are attached
    more than 50% of the votes that may be cast to elect the directors
    of the corporation are beneficially owned by the person or the
    administrator and the votes attached to those securities are
    sufficient, if exercised, to elect a majority of the directors of
    the corporation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person or an administrator of a PRPP controls an
    unincorporated entity, other than a limited partnership, if more
    than 50% of the ownership interests into which the unincorporated
    entity is divided are beneficially owned by the person or the
    administrator and the person or the administrator is able to direct
    the business and affairs of the unincorporated entity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the general partner of a limited partnership
    controls the limited partnership; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a trustee of a trust controls the trust.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumed control

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a person or an
  administrator of a PRPP who controls an entity controls any other
  entity that is controlled by the entity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affiliates

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, an entity is affiliated with another entity if the entity
is controlled by the other entity or if both entities are controlled by
the same person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Substantial investment

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, a person or an administrator of a PRPP has a substantial
investment in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an unincorporated entity if the person or the
  administrator, or an entity controlled by the person or the
  administrator, beneficially owns more than 25% of the ownership
  interests in the unincorporated entity; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the voting rights attached to voting shares of the
    corporation that are beneficially owned by the person or the
    administrator, or by an entity controlled by the person or the
    administrator, exceed 10% of the voting rights attached to all of
    the outstanding voting shares of the corporation, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the shares of the corporation that are
    beneficially owned by the person or the administrator, or by an
    entity controlled by the person or the administrator, represent
    ownership of more than 25% of the shareholders' equity of the
    corporation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Associated

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, a person or an administrator of a PRPP is associated with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that the person or the administrator
  controls and every affiliate of every such corporation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who controls the person or the
  administrator;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a partner who has a substantial investment in a
  partnership in which the person or the administrator has a substantial
  investment;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a trust or estate or succession in which the person
  or the administrator has a substantial investment or for which the
  person or the administrator serves as a trustee or in a similar
  capacity to a trustee;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the spouse or common-law partner of the person; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a brother, sister or child or other descendant of the
  person, or the spouse or common-law partner of any of them.

## [Licensing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-789063 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed conditions

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 11(1) of the Act, the Superintendent may, on application,
issue a licence authorizing a corporation to be an administrator if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the corporation submits to the Superintendent a
  five-year business plan that includes

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the reasons why the corporation believes that the
    PRPPs that it intends to administer will be sustainable over the
    course of the business plan,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the number of plans that the corporation intends
    to have registered as PRPPs,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the corporation intends to
    meet the requirement to provide the PRPPs to its members at low
    cost, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an estimate of the costs and of the fees, levies
    and other charges that would be triggered by the actions of a
    member;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the corporation has the financial resources required
  for the administration of a PRPP;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the corporation has procedures in place that are
  sufficient to identify, manage and control the risks associated with
  the PRPPs;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the corporation has the operational capability to
  administer a PRPP;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the officers and directors of the corporation are of
  good character, having demonstrated honesty, integrity and ethical
  behaviour in all of their professional activities; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the corporation provides, on the request of the
  Superintendent, any document or information required to assess whether
  the corporation meets the conditions set out in paragraphs (b) to (e).

## [Permitted Investments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-789076 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permitted investments

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  PRPP must provide that the funds in a member's account are to be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} invested in accordance with sections 9 to 14; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} invested

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in a name that clearly indicates that the
      investment is held in trust for the PRPP and, if the investment is
      capable of being registered, registered in that name,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the name of a financial institution, or a
      nominee of it, in accordance with a custodial agreement or trust
      agreement that is entered into on behalf of the PRPP with the
      financial institution and that clearly indicates that the
      investment is held for the PRPP, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the name of CDS Clearing and Depository
      Services Inc., or a nominee of it, in accordance with a custodial
      agreement or trust agreement that is entered into on behalf of the
      PRPP with a financial institution and that clearly indicates that
      the investment is held for the PRPP.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Custodial agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a custodial
  agreement must provide that an investment made or held on behalf of a
  PRPP under the agreement does not constitute an asset of the custodian
  or nominee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}10% limit

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  administrator of a PRPP must not, directly or indirectly, invest more
  than 10% of the total market value of the funds in a member's account
  in, or lend more than 10% of the total market value of the funds in a
  member's account to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any one person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} associated persons; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} affiliated corporations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of the funds
  in a member's account that are held by a bank, trust company or other
  financial institution to the extent that the funds are fully insured
  by the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation, by Assuris or by any
  similar provincial body established for the purpose of providing
  insurance against loss of deposits with trust companies or other
  financial institutions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to investments

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a mutual fund, a pooled fund or a segregated
    fund that meets the requirements applicable to a PRPP that are set
    out in section 10;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in an unallocated general fund of a person that is
    authorized to carry on a life insurance business in Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that are made in accordance with sections 12 to 14
    of Schedule III to the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
    1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in securities issued or fully guaranteed by the
    Government of Canada, the government of a province, or an agency of
    either one of them;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in a fund composed of mortgage-backed securities
    that are fully guaranteed by the Government of Canada, the
    government of a province, or an agency of either one of them;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in a fund that replicates the composition of a
    widely recognized index of a broad class of securities that are
    traded at a marketplace; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} that involve the purchase of a contract or
    agreement in respect of which the return is based on the performance
    of a widely recognized index of a broad class of securities traded
    at a marketplace.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voting shares

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  administrator of a PRPP must not, directly or indirectly, invest the
  assets of the PRPP in the securities of a corporation to which are
  attached more than 30% of the votes that may be cast to elect the
  directors of the corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to investments that are
  made in accordance with sections 12 to 14 of Schedule III to the
  [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transaction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of sections 11 to 13, "transaction"
  includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the making of an investment in securities;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the taking of an assignment of, or otherwise
    acquiring, a loan made by a third party;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the taking of a security interest in securities or
    a hypothec on securities; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any modification, renewal or extension of a prior
    transaction.

    It does not include the making of a variable payment, the transfer
    of funds into a member's account or the withdrawal of funds from a
    member's account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Related party transactions

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 and 13,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a transaction is entered into by, or on behalf of,
  a PRPP with a person who the administrator of the PRPP, or any person
  acting on the administrator's behalf, knows will become a related
  party to the PRPP, the person is considered to be a related party of
  the PRPP in respect of the transaction; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fulfilment of an obligation under the terms of
  any transaction, including the payment of interest on a loan or
  deposit, is part of the transaction and not a separate transaction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- related party

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 13 and 14, the
  administrator of a PRPP must not, directly or indirectly,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} lend funds in a member's account to a related party
    or invest those funds in the securities of a related party; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into a transaction with a related party on
    behalf of the PRPP.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}12-month period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 13 and 14, during the period of
  12 months after the day on which a person ceases to be a related party
  of a PRPP, the administrator of the PRPP must not, directly or
  indirectly,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} lend funds in a member's account to that person or
    invest those funds in the securities of that person; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into a transaction with that person on behalf
    of the PRPP.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- services of a related party

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  administrator of a PRPP may engage the services of any related party
  for the operation or administration of the PRPP under terms and
  conditions that are not less favourable than those, including those
  relating to price, rent or interest rate, that would apply to a
  similar transaction in an open market under conditions requisite to a
  fair transaction between parties who are at arm's length and acting
  prudently, knowledgeably and willingly.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- securities of a related
  party

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The administrator of a PRPP may invest in the
  securities of a related party that are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} held by a mutual fund, a pooled fund or a
    segregated fund

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that meets the requirements applicable to a PRPP
      that are set out in section 10, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in which investors other than the administrator
      and its affiliates may invest and are invested;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} held by a fund that replicates the composition of a
    widely recognized index of a broad class of securities traded at a
    marketplace; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} issued or fully guaranteed by the Government of
    Canada, the government of a province or an agency of either one of
    them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Sections 9 to 13 do not
apply in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} investments in a corporation that are held by, or on
  behalf of, a PRPP as a result of an arrangement, within the meaning of
  subsection 192(1) of the [Canada Business Corporations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-44), for the reorganization or liquidation of the
  corporation or for the amalgamation of the corporation with another
  corporation, if the investments are to be exchanged for shares or debt
  obligations; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} assets that are acquired by, or on behalf of, a PRPP
  through the realization of a security interest or a hypothec held by,
  or on behalf of, the PRPP and that are held for a period not exceeding
  two years after the day on which the assets were acquired.

## [Investment Choices]{.HTitleText1} {#h-789143 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Default option --- prescribed period

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 23(3) of the Act, if a member does not make an investment
choice referred to in subsection 23(1) of the Act within 60 days after
the day on which the notice referred to in paragraph 41(2)(a) of the Act
is received, the investment option chosen by the administrator as the
default option will apply to the member's account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Default option

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  administrator of a PRPP must offer the same default option for all of
  the PRPPs that it administers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Default option --- investments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The default option must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a balanced fund; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a portfolio of investments that takes into account
    a member's age.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investment options

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The administrator of a
PRPP must provide

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} no more than six investment options including the
  default option in the PRPP; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the same investment options to all members of the
  PRPP.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice --- option no longer available

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  administrator of a PRPP must notify a member in writing as soon as
  feasible after the administrator becomes aware that the member's
  investment option will no longer be available.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Default options

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the member does not choose another investment
  option within 60 days after the day on which the notice is received,
  the administrator must invest the member's funds in an option that is
  similar to the previous option or in the default option.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No fee, charge or levy

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} There must be no fee, charge or other levy associated
  with the transfer of the funds in a member's account into a new
  investment option under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The notice must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each investment option that is still available,
    include the description required under paragraph 23(c);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} indicate that the member has 60 days after the day
    on which the notice is received to choose another option; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} indicate the administrator's obligations under
    subsection (2) if the member does not choose another option within
    that period.

## [Permitted Inducements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-789169 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permitted inducements

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** An administrator may
give, offer or agree to give or offer to an employer and an employer may
demand, accept or offer or agree to accept from an administrator, as an
inducement to enter into a contract with the administrator in respect of
a PRPP

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a product or a service on more favourable terms or
  conditions than the administrator would otherwise offer if the
  inducement is for the equal benefit of the employees of that employer
  who are eligible to be members of the PRPP; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a transfer of assets into the PRPP

  employer's costs associated with the transfer of assets into that
  PRPP.

## [Low-Cost Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-789174 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The following criteria
must be used to determine whether a PRPP is being provided to its
members at low cost:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} costs are to be at or below those incurred by members
  of defined contribution plans that provide investment options to
  groups of 500 or more members; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} costs are to be the same for all members of a PRPP.

## [0% Contribution Rate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-789179 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member may set a contribution rate of 0% if 12 months have elapsed
  since the member's contributions to the PRPP began.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The rate may be set at 0% for a period of 3 to 60
  months. There is no limit on the number of times that the rate may be
  set at 0%.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice to be provided to the administrator under
  subsection 45(2) of the Act must be in writing and include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the member's name and contact information and the

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the period for which the contribution rate is to be
    set at 0%.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of administrator

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The administrator must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no more than 60 days after the day on which the
    administrator is notified under subsection 45(2) of the Act

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} provide the member with written confirmation of
      when the contribution rate will be set at 0% and when
      contributions will resume, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the member's contribution rate is
      set at 0%; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no fewer than 90 days before the day on which
    contributions are to resume, provide the member with written notice
    of the day of resumption and the contribution rate on resumption.

## [Variable Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-789302 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed age

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** The age of 55 years is
prescribed for the purposes of section 48 of the Act as the age at which
a member may elect to receive variable payments from the funds in their
account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment amount

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member who has elected to receive variable payments may decide the
  amount that they are to receive as a variable payment for any calendar
  year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parameters

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The payment shall be not less than the minimum amount
  determined under subsection 8506(5) of the [Income Tax
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._945) and, for any calendar
  year before the year in which the member reaches 90 years of age, not
  more than the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#789310 .Subsection}
  C/F

  where

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the balance in the member's account
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the beginning of the calendar year, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the balance at the beginning of the calendar
        year is zero, on the day on which the election was made; and

  [F]{.dfn}
  :   is the value, at the beginning of the calendar year, of an annual
      \$1 payment, payable on January 1 of each year between the
      beginning of that calendar year and December 31 of the year in
      which the member reaches 90 years of age, established using an
      interest rate that is
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each of the first 15 years, not more than
        the monthly average yield on Government of Canada marketable
        bonds of maturity over 10 years, as published by the Bank of
        Canada, for the month of November before the beginning of the
        calendar year; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for any subsequent year, not more than 6%.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Default amount

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The minimum amount determined under subsection
  8506(5) of the [Income Tax
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._945) is to be paid as a
  variable payment for a calendar year if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member has not notified the administrator of the
    amount to be paid as a variable payment for the calendar year within
    90 days after the day on which the statement required under
    paragraph 57(1)(b) of the Act is received; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula set out in
    subsection (2) for that year is less than that minimum amount.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount deemed to be zero

  [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} If, for the calendar year in which the variable
  payment is established, part of the account was composed of funds that
  had been held in a life income fund of the holder earlier in the
  calendar year in which the variable payment was established, the
  amount determined by the formula set out in subsection (2) is deemed
  to be zero in respect of that part of the account for that calendar
  year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial year

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the calendar year in which the variable payment
  is established, the amount to be paid is multiplied by the number of
  months remaining in that year and then divided by 12, with any part of
  an incomplete month counting as one month.

## [Electronic Communications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-789509 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of paragraph 64(1)(a) of the Act, the addressee must
  consent in writing, in paper or electronic form, or orally.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements --- administrator

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before an addressee consents, the administrator must
  notify the addressee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} of the addressee's right to revoke their consent at
    any time;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} of the addressee's responsibility to inform the
    administrator of any changes the addressee makes to the designated
    information system including any changes made to the contact
    information for the designated information system; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} of the date when the consent takes effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any revocation of consent must be done in writing, in
  paper or electronic form, or orally.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** If an electronic document
is provided on a generally accessible information system, such as a
website, the administrator must provide to the member written notice, in
paper or electronic form, of the electronic document's availability and
location.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Document considered provided

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** An electronic document is
considered to have been provided to an addressee when it is entered into
or made available on the information system designated by the addressee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Believed not to be received

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  administrator has reason to believe that an addressee has not received
  an electronic document or the notice required under section 44, the
  administrator must mail a paper copy of the document to the addressee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No effect --- electronic document
  reception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The mailing of a paper copy does not affect when the
  electronic document is considered to have been provided under section
  45.
