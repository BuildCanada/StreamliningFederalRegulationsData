## Office of the Governor-General's Secretary Exclusion Approval Order {#office-of-the-governor-generals-secretary-exclusion-approval-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-89
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2006-06-14

Office of the Governor-General's Secretary Exclusion Approval Order

P.C. 2006-434 2006-06-01
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 20(3) of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EECCA}, the Public Service
Commission has re-applied, subject to the approval of the Governor in
Council, the provisions of that Act to the positions excluded under the
Office of the Governor-General's Secretary Exclusion Approval
Order[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_f){#fnb_f-ID0EBCCA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e-ID0EECCA){#fna_e}S.C.
  2003, c. 22, ss. 12 and 13
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_f-ID0EBCCA){#fnb_f}SOR/84-181
  :::

And whereas the Public Service Commission having decided that it is
neither practicable nor in the best interests of the public service to
apply certain provisions of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EBABA} to positions designated by
the position numbers GGS-S-910 to 920 in the Office of the Governor
General's Secretary and the persons occupying those positions and has
decided to exclude, having consulted with the employer in accordance
with subsection 20(2) of that Act and subject to the approval of the
Governor in Council, pursuant to subsection 20(1) of that Act, those
positions and persons from the application of the Act, with the
exception of sections 17, 18, 19, 54, 68, 69 and 111 to 122 of that Act;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Canadian Heritage, and pursuant to
section 20 of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EBCAA}, hereby approves

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the re-application of the provisions of the [Public
  Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EEABAA} to the positions
  excluded under the Office of the Governor-General's Secretary
  Exclusion Approval Order[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_f){#fnb_f-ID0EBABAA}; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exclusion from the application of the [Public
  Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EBAAAA}, with the exception of
  sections 17, 18, 19, 54, 68, 69 and 111 to 122 of that Act to
  positions designated by the position numbers GGS-S-910 to 920 in the
  Office of the Governor General's Secretary and the persons occupying
  those positions.
