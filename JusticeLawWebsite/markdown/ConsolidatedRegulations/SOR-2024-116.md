## Order Grouping Ministers' Offices for the Purpose of a Pay Equity Plan {#order-grouping-ministers-offices-for-the-purpose-of-a-pay-equity-plan .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2024-116
:::

[PAY EQUITY ACT](/eng/acts/P-4.2)

Registration 2024-05-31

Order Grouping Ministers' Offices for the Purpose of a Pay Equity Plan

P.C. 2024-627 2024-05-31
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Labour, makes the annexed [Order Grouping Ministers'
Offices for the Purpose of a Pay Equity
Plan](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-116) under subsection 173(1) of the [Pay
Equity Act](/eng/acts/P-4.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5191_hq_1688151422){#fn_81000-2-5191_hq_1688151422-ID0EBBAA}.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5191_hq_1688151422-ID0EBBAA){#fn_81000-2-5191_hq_1688151422}S.C.
  2018, c. 27, s. 416
  :::
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grouping of ministers' offices

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** All ministers' offices are
grouped for the purpose of establishing and updating a single pay equity
plan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
