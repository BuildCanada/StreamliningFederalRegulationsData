## Minister of Agriculture Authority to Prescribe Fees Order {#minister-of-agriculture-authority-to-prescribe-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/86-139
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1986-08-06

Minister of Agriculture Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

P.C. 1986-1648  1986-07-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11),
is pleased hereby to revoke the [Minister of Agriculture Authority to
Prescribe Fees Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._712) made by Order in
Council P.C. 1985-2271 of 24th July, 1985 and to authorize the Minister
of Agriculture to prescribe by order the fee to be paid by the owner of
a pet to have the pet vaccinated against rabies by an employee of the
Department of Agriculture.
