    or pilotage certificate. ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    travelling together that are joined by lines or other
    means. ([ensemble de navires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} knowledgeable about pilotage duties; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} designated by a Minister or who practises
      medicine in an occupational medicine clinic that is designated by
      a Minister. ([médecin désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    physicians and surgeons. ([médecin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Health Qualifications]{.HTitleText2} {#h-655658 .Subheading}

#### [Physical and Mental Fitness for Pilotage Duties]{.HTitleText3} {#h-655659 .Subheading}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  applicant or holder shall undergo a medical examination, at the time
  or within the interval prescribed in this subsection, conducted by a
  designated physician to determine their physical and mental fitness
  for pilotage duties

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an applicant, before the licence or
    certificate is issued; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a holder, at least once every two
    years.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(a) does not apply to an applicant who
  intends to perform pilotage duties in an area in a pilotage region and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} who is the holder of a valid licence or pilotage
    certificate for another area in the same pilotage region; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whose medical report is valid.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An applicant or holder is physically and mentally fit
  for pilotage duties if the applicant or holder

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} does not suffer from any of the disabilities
    referred to in subsection 3(1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} meets the medical standards referred to in
    subsection 3(2).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), an applicant or holder shall
  undergo additional medical examinations if the designated physician
  requires the additional examinations in order to monitor the physical
  or mental fitness of the applicant or holder.

#### [Medical Examinations]{.HTitleText3} {#h-655673 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  designated physician conducting a medical examination shall determine
  whether the applicant or holder has any of the following disabilities:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an impairment that could cause an unpredictable
    loss of consciousness and is not controlled through medication or
    otherwise;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a disorder that could prevent the applicant or
    holder from reacting effectively while performing pilotage duties;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a disorder that could prevent the applicant or
    holder from having the strength, agility or ability necessary for
    performing pilotage duties in all weather and sea conditions likely
    to be encountered while performing pilotage duties;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a disorder that could prevent the applicant or
    holder from speaking in a clear and prompt manner while performing
    pilotage duties;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a condition that could endanger others, taking into
    account the duration of pilotage duties and the conditions on board
    ship;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a condition that is likely to require emergency
    medical care and is not controlled through medication or otherwise;
    or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} an active psychiatric disorder, including drug or
    alcohol dependence or abuse.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designated physician conducting a medical
  examination shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} take into account, when assessing an applicant or
    holder, the medical fitness standards referred to and set out in
    Division 8 of Part 2 of the [Marine Personnel
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-115); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine if the applicant or holder has depth
    perception.

#### [Medical Reports]{.HTitleText3} {#h-655688 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  designated physician shall, after completing the medical examination
  of an applicant or holder, issue a medical report to the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designated physician shall set out in the medical
  report an assessment of the applicant or holder as

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} unfit for pilotage duties;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fit for pilotage duties with limitations; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fit for pilotage duties without limitations.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A designated physician who assesses an applicant or
  holder as fit for pilotage duties with limitations shall state those
  limitations in the medical report.

#### [Validity of Medical Reports]{.HTitleText3} {#h-655696 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless a
  re-examination is required under section 6, and subject to
  subsection (2), a medical report remains valid for a period of not
  more than two years beginning on the day of its issuance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A physician may, taking into account the state of
  health of the applicant or holder examined in relation to pilotage
  duties, issue a medical report that specifies a shorter period of
  validity than the period prescribed in subsection (1).

#### [Re-examination]{.HTitleText3} {#h-655702 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may at any time require a medical re-examination if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the state of health of the applicant or holder may constitute a risk
    to the safety of the ship or of persons on board the ship; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant or holder requests it.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An applicant or holder who has reasonable grounds to
  believe that they are no longer physically or mentally fit for
  pilotage duties in accordance with subsection 2(3) shall immediately
  inform the Minister and request a medical re-examination.

#### [Disagreement with a Medical Report]{.HTitleText3} {#h-655710 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant or holder who disagrees with the medical report may, within
  15 days after being informed of its content, make a request in writing
  to the Minister to submit to a medical examination by a physician
  chosen by the applicant or holder.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The physician shall conduct the medical examination
  and make a medical report in accordance with sections 3 to 5.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the physician disagrees with the assessment of the
  designated physician, the Minister shall, on the recommendation of
  both physicians, appoint a third physician to conduct a medical
  examination of the applicant or holder.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The physician appointed by the Minister shall conduct
  the medical examination and make a medical report in accordance with
  sections 3 to 5.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The report of the appointed physician is final and
  binding on the Minister and the applicant or holder.

#### [Aids to Vision and Hearing]{.HTitleText3} {#h-655717 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A holder who is required to
use an aid to vision or hearing in order to meet the medical fitness
standards referred to and set out in Division 8 of Part 2 of the [Marine
Personnel Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-115) shall, while
performing pilotage duties,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} use the aid;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an aid to vision, possess at least two
  of them; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an aid to hearing, possess replacement
  batteries for it.

- SOR/2012-80, s. 6

  applicant shall have accumulated the following sea service:

  that region.

  charge of the deck watch of a ship if the person has the immediate
  charge of the navigation, communications and safety of the ship and

**[[22.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.1 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in this Division.

[[Board of Examiners]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board of Examiners]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the persons
    appointed under subsection 22.28(4) to conduct examinations for any
    class of licence or pilotage certificate. ([jury
    d'examen]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[movage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[movage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the moving of a ship within a
    pilotage area, whether the ship is moved from one berth to another
    or is returned to the same berth, but does not include the warping
    of a ship from one berth to another solely by means of mooring lines
    attached to a wharf, to the shore or to a mooring buoy, unless a
    pilot is employed. ([déplacement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[offshore supply vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[offshore supply vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a highly
    manoeuvrable ship that is designed for the supply of offshore oil
    and gas installations. ([navire ravitailleur au
    large]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person in charge of the deck watch]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person in charge of the deck watch]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    person who has the immediate charge of the navigation,
    communications and safety of a ship and who holds a certificate of
    competency that authorizes them to do so. ([personne chargée du
    quart à la passerelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pilot dispatch office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pilot dispatch office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Atlantic
    Pilotage Authority Dispatch Office as set out in the most recent
    annual edition of the Notices to Mariners, published by the Canadian
    Coast Guard. ([bureau d'affectation des pilotes]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

#### [Ships Subject to Compulsory Pilotage]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1349126 .Subheading}

- **[[22.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following ships and classes of
  ships are subject to compulsory pilotage within the areas referred to
  in section 22.2:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ships registered in Canada of more than 1,500 gross
    tonnage;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ships not registered in Canada, including floating
    cranes;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} oil rigs;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any combination of tug and tow in which

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the combined gross tonnage exceeds 1,500, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} more than one unit is being towed and the
      combined gross tonnage exceeds 500;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} pleasure craft of more than 500 gross tonnage; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} ferries that are entering or leaving a port that is
    not one of their regularly scheduled terminals.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the following ships and
  classes of ships are not subject to compulsory pilotage within the
  areas referred to in section 22.2:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Canadian-government ships;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ships registered in Canada that are employed in
    catching or processing fish or other living resources of the sea;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Canadian-registered offshore supply vessels of
    5,000 gross tonnage or less that have an operations base in a port
    located within one of the areas;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} ferries that are operating on a regular schedule
    between two terminals and that are crewed by masters and persons in
    charge of the deck watch who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are regular members of their ferry's complement,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} hold certificates of competency under the
      [Marine Personnel Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-115);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} pleasure craft of 500 gross tonnage or less not
    registered in Canada; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} tugs of 500 gross tonnage or less that are not
    registered in Canada and that are crewed by masters and persons in
    charge of the deck watch who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are regular members of their tug's complement,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} hold certificates of competency under the
      [Marine Personnel Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-115).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a ship that is 225.5 m
  (739.83 ft.) or less in length is not subject to compulsory pilotage
  within the portion of the Cape Breton compulsory pilotage area
  described in paragraph 1(d) of Part 3 of Schedule 2 (Zone D, Strait of
  Canso) unless the ship is conducting operations that require it to
  move alongside or depart from alongside another ship.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a ship of war or vessel of
  war is not subject to compulsory pilotage within the Halifax
  compulsory pilotage area described in section 2 of Part 3 of Schedule
  2 if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ship or vessel, while within that compulsory
    pilotage area, is under the operational command of the Commander,
    Maritime Command, and has been under their operational command for
    the 30 days before the ship or vessel entered the compulsory
    pilotage area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Commander, Maritime Command, has notified the
    Atlantic Pilotage Authority in writing that the person who is, while
    the ship or vessel is within that compulsory pilotage area, the
    commanding officer of the ship or vessel has completed a program of

- **[[23.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in
  this Division.

  [[berth]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[berth]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a wharf, pier, anchorage or
      mooring buoy, and includes a berthed ship or a ship at
      anchor. ([poste]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Board of Examiners]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Board of Examiners]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the persons
      appointed under section 23.34 to conduct examinations for any
      class of licence or pilotage certificate. ([jury
      d'examen]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[deadweight tonnage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[deadweight tonnage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the weight in
      metric tons of cargo, ship's fuel, passengers and crew carried by
      a ship when loaded to its maximum Summer Load Line. ([port en
      lourd]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[movage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[movage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the moving of a ship within a
      harbour, whether the ship is moved from one berth to another or is
      returned to the same berth, but unless a pilot is employed does
      not include the warping of a ship from one berth to another solely
      by means of mooring lines attached to a wharf, the shore or a
      mooring buoy. ([déplacement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Division, the length of a ship refers to the
  distance in metres and centimetres between the fore and aft
  extremities of the ship.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this Division, a part of a district refers to
  either of the following, as the case may be:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a licence, all navigable waters of a
    district lying between two pilot boarding stations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a pilotage certificate, the
    navigable waters of a district lying between and in the vicinity of
    two berths, as indicated on the pilotage certificate.

#### [Ships Subject to Compulsory Pilotage]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1349521 .Subheading}

- **[[23.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23.3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the
  following ships are subject to compulsory pilotage:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any ship registered in Canada that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is operated in District No. 1 or District No. 1-1
      and is more than 70 m in length and more than 2,400 gross tonnage,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is operated in District No. 2 and is more than
      80 m in length and more than 3,300 gross tonnage; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any ship that is not registered in Canada and is
    more than 35 m in length.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every scow and barge that is registered in Canada and
  carrying as cargo a [[pollutant]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
  section 165 of the [Canada Shipping Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15), is
  subject to compulsory pilotage.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The following ships or classes of ships, if
  registered in Canada, are not subject to compulsory pilotage:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any ship owned by the Government of Canada and not
    engaged in commercial trade;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any ferry operating in the passenger carrying trade
    on a regular schedule between two or more terminals;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any ship designed for and engaged in fishing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any tug, floating crane or dredge; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any self-propelled barge trading regularly between
    two or more terminals in Quebec in or east of District No. 2 other
    than a barge described in subsection (2).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (3), a ship referred to in any of
  paragraphs (3)(b) to (e) is subject to compulsory pilotage if its
  operation would likely constitute a risk to the safety of navigation
  because of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the condition of the ship;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} exceptional conditions on board the ship; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} conditions related to weather, tides, currents or
    ice.

#### [Classes of Licences and Pilotage Certificates]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1349650 .Subheading}

**[[23.15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23.15 .sectionLabel}** The classes of
licences and pilotage certificates that may be issued by the Minister
are the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Class A or Class B Montreal Harbour licence or
  pilotage certificate;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Class A, Class B or Class C licence or pilotage
  certificate;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Class D apprentice pilot permit.

- **[[23.16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23.16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A Class A Montreal Harbour licence
  or pilotage certificate authorizes its holder to perform pilotage
  duties on any ship, regardless of size, in District No. 1-1.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Class B Montreal Harbour licence or pilotage
  certificate authorizes its holder to perform pilotage duties on any
  ship not exceeding 210 m in length in District No. 1-1.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A Class A licence or pilotage certificate authorizes
  its holder to perform pilotage duties on any ship in District No. 1 or
  District No. 2 or any part of those districts.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A Class B licence or pilotage certificate authorizes
  its holder to perform pilotage duties

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in District No. 1 or any part of that district,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the first year after the date on which the
      holder obtained the licence or pilotage certificate, on any ship
      not exceeding 195 m in length, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the second and any subsequent year after the
      date on which the holder obtained the licence or pilotage
      certificate, on any ship not exceeding 215 m in length; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in District No. 2 or any part of that district, on
    any ship not exceeding a deadweight tonnage of 50,000.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A Class C licence or pilotage certificate authorizes
  its holder to perform pilotage duties

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in District No. 1 or any part of that district,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the first six months after the date on which
      the holder obtained the licence or pilotage certificate, on any
      ship not exceeding 165 m in length,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the six months after the period referred to
      in subparagraph (i), on any tanker not exceeding 165 m in length
      and on any other ship not exceeding 175 m in length, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the second and any subsequent year after the
      date on which the holder obtained the licence or pilotage
      certificate, on any tanker not exceeding 165 m in length and on
      any other ship not exceeding 185 m in length; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in District No. 2 or any part of that district, on
    any ship not exceeding a deadweight tonnage of 30,000.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A Class D apprentice pilot permit authorizes its
  holder to undertake pilotage training in the presence of a licensed
  pilot on any ship, in District Nos. 1, 1-1 or 2.

#### [General Requirements for Applicants and Holders]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1349810 .Subheading}

**[[23.25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23.25 .sectionLabel}** No person shall
become the holder of a licence or pilotage certificate if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} within one year before the date of their application,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} they were convicted of an offence under the Act,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any licence or pilotage certificate previously
    held by them was suspended under the Act, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} they were convicted of an offence under section
    320.13 or 320.14 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} they have an adverse record in ship handling and
  performance of pilotage duties.

    of a licensed pilot or pilotage certificate holder effectively and

- **[[24.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in
  this Division.

  [[Board of Examiners]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Board of Examiners]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Board of
      Examiners established under section 24.21. ([jury
      d'examen]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[deck watch officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[deck watch officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who has
      the immediate charge of the navigation and security of a ship, but
      does not include a pilot. ([officier de quart à la
      passerelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Division, the length of a ship refers to the
  distance in metres between the fore and aft extremities of the ship.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this Division, the breadth of a ship refers to the
  maximum breadth distance in metres between the outside of the shell
  plating of the ship.

##### [Ships Under the Conduct of Masters or Deck Watch Officers Licensed in the United States]{.HTitleText4} {#h-1350046 .Subheading}

**[[24.7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24.7 .sectionLabel}** A ship that is
subject to compulsory pilotage under section 24.3 need not be under the
conduct of a licensed pilot or pilotage certificate holder if the ship

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} navigates only on the Great Lakes or the inland
  waters of Canada, except for occasional near coastal voyages; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is under the conduct of a master or deck watch
  officer who holds a certificate of competency or similar document
  issued pursuant to the laws of the United States that authorizes that
  person to have the conduct of the ship within the compulsory pilotage
  area in which the ship is navigating.

#### [Waiver of Compulsory Pilotage]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1350052 .Subheading}

- **[[24.8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24.8
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Great Lakes Pilotage Authority
  may waive compulsory pilotage in respect of a ship in any of the
  following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ship is in distress;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the ship is proceeding to or assisting a ship in
    difficulty or in a position of danger;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the ship is engaged in rescue or salvage
    operations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the ship is entering a compulsory pilotage area for
    the purpose of seeking refuge;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the ship is in any compulsory pilotage area
    described in paragraph 1(e) of Schedule 4 and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a licensed pilot or pilotage certificate holder
      for that area is on board and available on call, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the master or deck watch officer of the ship is
      the holder of a pilotage certificate or of a similar document
      issued by an appropriate authority of the United States, and the
      certificate or document authorizes the master or deck watch
      officer to have the conduct of a ship within the area;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a licensed pilot is not available to perform the
    functions of a pilot and the following conditions have been
    satisfied:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the owner, agent or master of the ship has
      complied with section 24.11,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the owner, agent or master of the ship has
      provided notice to that Authority of the pilotage services to be
      performed, the limits of the area for which the waiver is
      requested, the immediate and ultimate destinations of the ship
      within the compulsory pilotage area, the name, nationality,
      length, breadth, deepest draft and gross tonnage of the ship and
      the nature of the cargo on board the ship,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the master of the ship is familiar with the
      route and any marine traffic control system in the compulsory
      pilotage area and is prepared to proceed without the services of a
      pilot, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a person referred to in subparagraph (i) has
      provided to that Authority any other information required by it to
      ensure safe navigation;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} one or more licensed pilots refuse to perform the
    functions of a pilot for any reason other than the safety of the
    ship; or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the ship is necessary for carrying out the
    following work or for any related operations and that Authority
    determines that the waiver will not impede safe navigation:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} dredging work, in which case the waiver applies
      only for the site specified in the waiver and for travelling to
      and from the port and spoil grounds within the distance specified
      in the waiver, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} underwater engineering work such as the
      construction, laying or maintenance of underwater pipelines or
      cables or other similar facilities.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A waiver under paragraph (1)(h) is valid only if it
  is provided by the Great Lakes Pilotage Authority in writing and may
  be made subject to the condition that a licensed pilot be engaged for
  the overall supervision of the ships specified in the waiver, in which
  case the waiver does not apply unless a licensed pilot is so engaged.

authorized by an appropriate authority of the United States to have the
conduct of a ship in the United States waters of the Great Lakes, that
individual may also pilot in the Canadian waters of the Great Lakes,
their connecting and tributary waters and the St. Lawrence River as far

#### [Navigation in the Compulsory Pilotage Areas]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1350078 .Subheading}

**[[24.10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24.10 .sectionLabel}** The Great Lakes
Pilotage Authority may deny or withdraw pilotage service in respect of a
ship if, without reasonable cause,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person in charge of the ship fails to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} provide safe boarding and disembarking facilities
    for a pilot,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} provide adequate accommodation and meals to a
    pilot in any case when the pilot is required to be on board the ship
    for a period of more than three hours, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} sign the pilotage card supplied by that Authority
    to the pilot; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the owner, agent or master of the ship has not
  complied with section 24.11.

- **[[24.12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24.12

  a licensed pilot and is transiting International District 1 shall
  exchange pilots at Iroquois Lock if,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an upbound voyage, the ship takes
    longer than 1 hour and 15 minutes to transit from Valleyfield Bridge

#### [Qualifications]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1350115 .Subheading}

##### [Applicant for a Licence]{.HTitleText4} {#h-1350116 .Subheading}

- **[[24.15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24.15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An applicant for a licence shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have the experience at sea qualifications set out
    in section 12;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pass an examination conducted by a Board of
    Examiners by showing a knowledge of the topics set out in section
    24.19 that is compatible with the safe performance of pilotage
    duties;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} hold a Restricted Operator's Certificate (ROC-MC)
    or General Operator Certificate (GOC) issued under the
    [Radiocommunication Act](/eng/acts/R-2);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} hold training certificates showing that they have
    successfully completed

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} SEN Level II, as referred to in the [Marine
      Personnel Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-115), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a bridge resource management training course
      that has been approved by the Minister under section 114 of the
      [Marine Personnel Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-115);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} demonstrate professional conduct;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} have been declared fit to perform pilotage duties
    in accordance with the medical requirements of Part 1; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} have a degree of proficiency in the English
    language sufficient to carry out their pilotage duties.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An applicant for a licence for a compulsory pilotage
  area for which an apprenticeship system has been established shall
  have completed the full apprenticeship required by the Great Lakes
  Pilotage Authority before taking the examination referred to in
  paragraph (1)(b).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An applicant for a licence for a compulsory pilotage
  area, other than the Port of Churchill, in Manitoba, shall have
  completed at least 50 training trips in that area before taking the
  examination referred to in paragraph (1)(b).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} An applicant for a licence for the Cornwall District
  shall have a degree of proficiency in the French language sufficient
  to carry out their pilotage duties in that area.

##### [Applicant for a Pilotage Certificate]{.HTitleText4} {#h-1350133 .Subheading}

**[[24.16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24.16 .sectionLabel}** An applicant for a
pilotage certificate shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have the experience at sea qualifications set out in
  section 12;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have completed, within the three years immediately
  preceding the date of the application, at least 15 trips in each
  compulsory pilotage area in which the applicant intends to perform
  pilotage duties;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} either

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} pass an examination conducted by a Board of
    Examiners by showing a knowledge of the topics set out in section
    24.19 that is compatible with the safe performance of pilotage
    duties, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} have successfully completed the Great Lakes Marine
    Pilotage Certificate Training Program;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} hold a Restricted Operator's Certificate (ROC-MC) or
  General Operator Certificate (GOC) issued under the
  [Radiocommunication Act](/eng/acts/R-2);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} hold training certificates showing that the applicant
  has successfully completed

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} SEN Level II, as referred to in the [Marine
    Personnel Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-115), and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a bridge resource management training course that
    has been approved by the Minister under section 114 of the [Marine
    Personnel Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-115);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} demonstrate professional conduct;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} have been declared fit to perform pilotage duties in
  accordance with the medical requirements of Part 1; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} have a degree of proficiency in the English language
  sufficient to carry out their pilotage duties.

#### [Examinations]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1350162 .Subheading}

- **[[24.19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24.19
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An examination for a licence or
  pilotage certificate shall relate to the applicant's knowledge of the
  following topics:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the pilotage and navigational requirements of each
    area in which the applicant intends to perform pilotage duties,
    including knowledge of the currents, depths of water, anchorage
    areas, aids to navigation and, as applicable, tides;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the marine traffic control system, if any, in each
    area in which the applicant intends to perform pilotage duties;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the relevant parts of the Act and the regulations
    made under it;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} ship handling, including the related
    characteristics of a ship and the principles of hydrodynamics;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the practical use of all shipboard navigational
    instruments;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the duties, responsibilities and obligations of a
    pilot; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the relevant customs, port, immigration and
    pollution regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an applicant intends to perform pilotage duties in
  an area other than the Port of Churchill in Manitoba, the examination
  shall also relate to the applicant's knowledge of the [Seaway Property
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-105).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an applicant intends to perform pilotage duties in
  the Port of Churchill in Manitoba, the examination shall also relate
  to the applicant's knowledge of the [Collision
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1416) and any regulations
  respecting the Port of Churchill.

#### [Board of Examiners]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1350179 .Subheading}

- **[[24.21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24.21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A Board of Examiners shall consist
  of an officer of the Great Lakes Pilotage Authority, who shall be the
  Chairperson of the Board, and of the Board members appointed by that
  Authority under subsection (2) or (3).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an applicant for a licence for a
  compulsory pilotage area, the Great Lakes Pilotage Authority shall
  appoint the following Board members:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} two persons, each of whom holds a licence for the
    area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who does not hold a licence for the area
    but is knowledgeable about the area and holds

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a certificate not lower than master, local
      voyage, issued under the [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a certificate not lower than Master, Near
      Coastal, issued under the [Canada Shipping Act,
      2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an applicant for a pilotage
  certificate for a compulsory pilotage area, the Great Lakes Pilotage
  Authority shall appoint the following Board members:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} two persons, each of whom holds a licence or a
    pilotage certificate for the area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who does not hold a licence or pilotage
    certificate for the area but is knowledgeable about the area and
    holds

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a certificate not lower than master, local
      voyage, issued under the [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a certificate not lower than Master, Near
      Coastal, issued under the [Canada Shipping Act,
      2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson of the Board of Examiners shall
  report to the Minister the results of every examination, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of each person who passed the examination;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the class of licence or pilotage certificate to
    which each person who passed the examination is entitled.

#### [Maintaining Qualifications]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1350197 .Subheading}

- **[[24.22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24.22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The holder of a licence must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} remain fit to perform pilotage duties in accordance
    with the medical requirements set out in Part 1;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintain and, if possible, improve their competence
    with respect to pilotage duties;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} hold valid certificates of competency and valid
    certificates issued under the [Radiocommunication
    Act](/eng/acts/R-2), if they were required for the issuance of the
    licence;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} maintains valid training certificates, if they were
    required for the issuance of the licence; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} complete, every year, at least five one-way trips
    in each compulsory pilotage area for which the licence was issued.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(d) does not apply in respect of a
  licence for the Port of Churchill, Manitoba.

pilotage certificate must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} remain fit to perform pilotage duties in accordance
  with the medical requirements set out in Part 1;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintain and, if possible, improve their competence

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} hold valid certificates of competency and valid

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} maintain valid training certificates, if they were
  required for the issuance of the pilotage certificate;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} complete, in the three-year period after the date of
  issuance of the certificate, in the capacity of master or deck watch
  officer, at least 10 one-way trips in the compulsory pilotage area for
  which the pilotage certificate was issued; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} provide, at the request of the Minister, satisfactory
  evidence that the holder has complied with the requirements of
  paragraph (e).

- **[[24.25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24.25

  pilotage certificate who was on board the ship and any other person
  who had the conduct of the ship at the time the incident occurred
  shall immediately report to the Minister by the fastest means
  available all known details of the incident including any pollution or
  threat of pollution in the case where the ship

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} causes the loss of or damage to any other ship or
    property located in or adjacent to the waters in that area, whether
    or not loss or damage results to the ship; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is damaged, stranded, lost or abandoned or is in
    any manner involved in an incident that may directly or indirectly
    cause damage to or pollution of the surrounding environment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every report referred to in subsection (1) shall set
  out all information in respect of the incident that is known to the
  person making the report, including any pollution or threat of
  pollution.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a report referred to in subsection (1) cannot
  be made directly to the Minister, it shall be made to the nearest
  maritime traffic control centre.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every person who makes a report referred to in
  subsection (1) shall, as soon as possible after making the report,
  attend before an officer of the Great Lakes Pilotage Authority and
  make a written report of the incident on a form provided by the
  Minister.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every report made under subsection (1) or (4) shall
  be confidential and shall not be released by the Minister to any
  person without the prior consent of the person who made the report.

#### [Waiver of Compulsory Pilotage]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1350326 .Subheading}

- **[[25.10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25.10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Pacific Pilotage Authority may,
  on application, waive compulsory pilotage in respect of a ship in any
  of the following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ship is in distress;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person on board the ship requires medical
    evacuation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the ship is engaged in rescue or salvage
    operations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the ship is seeking refuge; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a licensed pilot is not available to perform the
    functions of a pilot and the following conditions have been met:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the owner, master or agent of the ship has
      complied with sections 25.12 and 25.13, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all persons in charge of the deck watch are
      familiar with the route and the marine traffic control system in
      the compulsory pilotage area that the ship is entering.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Pacific Pilotage Authority may waive compulsory
  pilotage in respect of a ship that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is warping and is not using its engines or a tug
    except as a line boat for the handling of the ship's lines;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is proceeding directly --- and, if applicable, in
    accordance with an established traffic separation scheme --- to a
    pilot boarding station located within a compulsory pilotage area for
    the purpose of embarking a licensed pilot; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is proceeding directly --- and, if applicable, in
    accordance with an established traffic separation scheme --- out of
    a compulsory pilotage area after disembarking a licensed pilot at a
    pilot boarding station located within that area.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Pacific Pilotage Authority may, on application,
  waive compulsory pilotage in respect of a ship of less than 10,000
  gross tonnage if, as of the day on which the application is made,
  every person in charge of the deck watch

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} holds the certificates that are required by Part 2
    of the [Marine Personnel Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-115)
    or, if the ship is not Canadian, equivalent certificates;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has completed, as a person in charge of the deck
    watch on voyages in that Authority's region or engaged in the
    coastal trade, at least

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 150 days of service in the preceding 18 months,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 365 days of service in the preceding 60 months,
      including at least 60 days in the preceding 24 months; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has served as a person in charge of the deck watch
    in the compulsory pilotage area for which the waiver is sought on at
    least one occasion within the preceding 24 months.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Compulsory pilotage may be waived in respect of a
  ship referred to in subsection (3) that is travelling in the portion
  of Area 1 west of the New Westminster railway bridge if every person
  in charge of the deck watch has, within the 24 months before the day
  on which the application is made, completed five return voyages, of
  which the Pacific Pilotage Authority was given prior notice, through
  that portion of Area 1

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with a licensed pilot; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with a person in charge of the deck watch who has
    previously completed five return voyages, of which that Authority
    was given prior notice, through that portion of Area 1 with a
    licensed pilot.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Compulsory pilotage may be waived in respect of a
  ship referred to in subsection (3) that is travelling in the portion
  of Area 1 east of the New Westminster railway bridge if every person
  in charge of the deck watch has, within the 24 months before the day
  on which the application is made, completed 10 return voyages, of
  which the Pacific Pilotage Authority was given prior notice, through
  that portion of Area 1

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with a licensed pilot; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with a person in charge of the deck watch who has
    previously completed 10 return voyages, of which that Authority was
    given prior notice, through that portion of Area 1 with a licensed
    pilot.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Compulsory pilotage may be waived in respect of a
  ship referred to in subsection (3) that is carrying dangerous goods
  and travelling in the Second Narrows Traffic Control Zone if every
  person in charge of the deck watch has, as of the day on which the
  application is made, completed six return voyages, at least one of
  which was completed within the preceding 24 months, of which the
  Pacific Pilotage Authority was given prior notice, through that Zone

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with a licensed pilot; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with a person in charge of the deck watch who has
    previously completed six return voyages, of which that Authority was
    given prior notice, through that Zone with a licensed pilot.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} If a ship is part of an arrangement of ships then the
  combined gross tonnage of all the ships in the arrangement of ships is
  taken into consideration in determining whether the ship qualifies for
  a waiver of compulsory pilotage under subsection (3).

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Pacific Pilotage Authority
  may not waive compulsory pilotage in respect of any period before the
  embarkation or after the disembarkation of the persons who meet the
  conditions set out in subsections (3) to (6), as the case may be.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (3) to (6), a ship is subject to
  compulsory pilotage if there is a risk to navigational safety because
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ship safety orders resulting from an environmental
    risk;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} exceptional circumstances on board the ship; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} extreme conditions related to weather, tides or
    currents or freshet conditions.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} An application for a waiver of compulsory pilotage
  other than an application made under a circumstance described in
  subsection (1) or (2) shall be made in writing.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} At the request of the Pacific Pilotage Authority,

#### [Examinations]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1350430 .Subheading}

- **[[25.20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25.20
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To determine whether an applicant
  for or the holder of a licence or pilotage certificate or an applicant
  who wishes to become an apprentice pilot meets the qualifications for
  applicants and holders required by the Act and these Regulations, the
  Minister may refer the qualifications of the applicant or holder to a
  committee of examiners.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} There shall be two committees of examiners consisting
  of five members each.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} One of the committees of examiners shall examine the
  qualifications of applicants and holders with respect to Area 1 and
  the other committee shall examine the qualifications of applicants and
  holders with respect to Areas 2, 3, 4 and 5.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Each committee of examiners shall consist of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} two persons appointed by the Pacific Pilotage
    Authority, one of whom shall be Chairperson of the committee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a licensed pilot appointed by the representative
    body of licensed pilots;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a licensed pilot appointed by that Authority; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a master mariner appointed by that Authority.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A committee of examiners shall be appointed for a
  term of one year beginning on July 1 of each year.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Any retiring member of a committee of examiners shall
  be eligible for re-appointment to that committee.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} A quorum of a committee of examiners shall be four
  members.

- **[[25.22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25.22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Examinations conducted by a
  committee of examiners shall be in any form that the Pacific Pilotage
  Authority determines and shall include questions relating to the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} local knowledge of every area to which the
    application, licence or pilotage certificate relates including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} tides and currents,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} dredged channel widths and depths,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} depth of water,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} important and essential cable areas and
      prohibited anchorage areas,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} anchorage areas and depths,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} aides to navigation, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} bridge signals and clearances;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} knowledge of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the [Collision
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1416),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} regulations under the [Canada Marine
      Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7) respecting harbours and ports,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Act and the regulations made under it, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the [Quarantine
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1368);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} seamanship and ship handling;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} docking problems and the use of tugs and anchors;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} general chart work, including the correction of
    courses and compass errors;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} communications;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} International Code of Signals, including the use of
    one and two flag signals;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} duties of a pilot;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} use of navigational and bridge instruments;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} practical knowledge of the interpretation and
    operation of radar; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} any other subjects that the committee of examiners
    may consider necessary.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A candidate may attempt the examinations referred to
  in subsection (1) up to six times.

#### [Eligibility for Apprenticeship System]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1350484 .Subheading}

- **[[25.24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25.24
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The name of an applicant who wishes
  to become an apprentice pilot and meets the qualifications for an
  applicant for or a holder of a licence set out in the Act and these
  Regulations shall be placed by a committee of examiners on an
  eligibility list prepared by them under paragraph 25.21(a).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The name of an applicant who meets the qualifications
  referred to in subsection (1) shall be placed on the eligibility list
  after the last name on the list.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If several applicants meet the qualifications
  referred to in subsection (1) at the same time, their names shall be
  placed on the eligibility list after the last name on the list in an
  order that ranks them, from highest to lowest, in accordance with
  their results in the examinations referred to in section 25.22.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The name of an applicant referred to in
  subsection (1) shall remain on the eligibility list for a period of
  two years.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Upon the expiration of the two-year period referred
  to in subsection (4), the name of the applicant shall be removed from
  the eligibility list unless they establish to the satisfaction of the
  committee of examiners that they

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have, during the two-year period, maintained sea
    service in each area in respect of which they intend to become an
    apprentice pilot; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} meet the qualifications referred to in subsection
    (1).

#### [Duration of Apprenticeship]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1350499 .Subheading}

- **[[25.26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25.26
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An apprentice pilot shall,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to qualify for a licence for Area 1, serve as an
    apprentice pilot for a period of not less than three months in that
    Area and complete during that period not fewer than 50 assignments
    in the Area with a licensed pilot; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to qualify for a licence for Areas 2 to 5,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} serve as an apprentice pilot for a period of not
      less than six months and not more than 24 months in those Areas,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} during the period served as required by
      subparagraph (i), complete not fewer than 90 assignments with a
      licensed pilot.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person shall cease to be an apprentice pilot when
  they

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are granted a licence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} withdraw from the apprenticeship system; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} are discharged from the apprenticeship system by
    the Pacific Pilotage Authority.

#### [Apprenticeship System Training]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1350512 .Subheading}

- **[[25.27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25.27
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An apprentice pilot shall,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when directed to do so by the Pacific Pilotage
    Authority, take examinations both written and oral before the
    committee of examiners;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make the voyages at the direction of the Pacific
    Pilotage Authority that will give them the most extensive experience
    in the waters and ports of the compulsory pilotage area in respect
    of which they wish to obtain a licence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} keep a log of all voyages made by them pursuant to

  command of the licensed pilot of the ship to which they are assigned.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Pacific Pilotage Authority may assign one
  apprentice pilot to a ship and may assign a second apprentice pilot to
  a ship after consultation with the ship's master, owner or agent.

#### [Marine Occurrence]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1350522 .Subheading}

- **[[25.28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25.28
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a ship that is subject to
  compulsory pilotage or a ship in respect of which a waiver of
  compulsory pilotage has been granted is involved in a marine
  occurrence in a compulsory pilotage area, the person who has the
  conduct of the ship at the time of the marine occurrence shall submit
  a full report on the marine occurrence to the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the person who has the conduct of the ship at the
  time of the marine occurrence is not the master, the master shall also
  submit a full report on the marine occurrence to the Minister.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who is required to submit a full report on
  the marine occurrence shall do so

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 72 hours after the marine occurrence; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within an additional period of time granted to the
    person by the Minister under subsection (4).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall grant an additional period of time
  if the Minister is notified within 72 hours after the marine
  occurrence that the person is unable to submit the report within those
  72 hours because the person was injured in the marine occurrence or
  because the person is in a location that does not have a scheduled
  transportation service or a communication system that can be used to
  submit the report.
