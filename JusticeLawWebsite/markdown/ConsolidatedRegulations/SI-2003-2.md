## Controlled Access Zone Order (Halifax, Esquimalt and Nanoose Harbours) {#controlled-access-zone-order-halifax-esquimalt-and-nanoose-harbours .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2003-2
:::

Registration 2003-01-01

Controlled Access Zone Order (Halifax, Esquimalt and Nanoose Harbours)

P.C. 2002-2190  2002-12-12
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Defence, hereby makes the annexed [Controlled
Access Zone Order (Halifax, Esquimalt and Nanoose
Harbours)](/eng/regulations/SI-2003-2).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-609085 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in this Order.

  [[controlled access zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[controlled access zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a zone,
      designated by the Minister under section 2, that includes all
      corresponding airspace above, and water and land below, the zone.
      ([zone d'accès contrôlé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[security guard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[security guard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an officer or a non-commissioned member who is
        employed on duties relating to the enforcement of this Order; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person authorized by the Chief of the Defence
        Staff to enforce this Order. ([garde de
        sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Order, [[defence
  establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[materiel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[non-commissioned
  member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have
  the same meaning as in subsection 2(1) of the [National Defence
  Act](/eng/acts/N-5).

## [Controlled Access Zones]{.HTitleText1} {#h-609093 .Part}

### [Designation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-609094 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3, the
Minister may personally, on the recommendation of the Chief of the
Defence Staff, designate as a controlled access zone an area or part of
an area of water described in column 2 of the schedule at a location
referred to in column 1.

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate a controlled access zone only if it is
  reasonably necessary to ensure the safety or security of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a defence establishment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} materiel or other property that is provided for the
    Canadian Forces or the Department of National Defence and is
    situated outside a defence establishment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a vessel, aircraft or other property under the
    control of a visiting force that is legally in Canada by virtue of
    the [Visiting Forces Act](/eng/acts/V-2) or otherwise; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any person in, on or about anything referred to in
    paragraphs (a) to (c).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The dimensions of a controlled access zone may not be
  greater than is reasonably necessary to ensure the safety or security
  of anything referred to in paragraphs (1)(a) to (c) or a person
  referred to in paragraph (1)(d).

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A controlled access zone
may be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a fixed zone; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a zone surrounding anything referred to in paragraph
  3(1)(b) or (c), whether it is moving or stationary, including a ship
  that is at anchor.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A designation may be made
or renewed for a determinate or indeterminate period.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may at any
time amend, vary or cancel a designation.

### [Notice of Designation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-609108 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall, by any
means that the Minister considers appropriate in the circumstances, give
notice of a designation and of the renewal, amendment, variation or
cancellation of a designation as soon as possible to all persons who in
the Minister's opinion may be affected by it, unless the Minister is of
the opinion that it is inadvisable to do so for reasons of international
relations or national defence or security.

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Minister shall publish a notice of a
  designation and of the renewal, amendment, variation or cancellation
  of a designation in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/)
  within 23 days after the designation, renewal, amendment, variation or
  cancellation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is of the opinion that it is
    inadvisable to publish a notice for reasons of international
    relations or national defence or security;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the designation, renewal, amendment or variation is
    for a period of less than 30 days or the cancellation takes place
    less than 30 days after the designation is made; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the designation, renewal, amendment or variation is
    in respect of a controlled access zone referred to in paragraph
    4(b).

### [Access to Controlled Access Zones]{.HTitleText2} {#h-609116 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Chief of the Defence
Staff may, having regard to the safety or security of anything referred
to in paragraphs 3(1)(a) to (c) or a person referred to in paragraph
3(1)(d),

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} permit persons or classes of persons to have access
  to a controlled access zone without conditions;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} permit persons or classes of persons to have access
  to a controlled access zone on such conditions as the Chief of the
  Defence Staff considers appropriate in the circumstances; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prohibit persons or classes of persons from having
  access to a controlled access zone.

### [Notice Relating to Access]{.HTitleText2} {#h-609121 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Chief of the Defence
Staff shall, by any means that the Chief of the Defence Staff considers
appropriate in the circumstances, give notice as soon as possible that
access to a controlled access zone is permitted or prohibited and of the
conditions of access to the zone, and of any changes to that permission
or prohibition or to those conditions, to all persons who, in the
opinion of the Chief of the Defence Staff, may be affected by them,
unless the Chief of the Defence Staff is of the opinion that it is
inadvisable to do so for reasons of international relations or national
defence or security.

## [Compliance and Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-609123 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every person on entering
or exiting a controlled access zone shall, on the demand of a security
guard, submit to a search of their person or any property or thing under
their control.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Where a person refuses to
submit to a search when required to do so under section 11,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the person is seeking entry to the controlled
  access zone, they may be refused entry; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person is exiting the zone, the person or any
  property or thing under their control may be searched by a security
  guard, which search shall be carried out with only such force as is
  necessary for that purpose.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Except where there are
reasonable grounds to believe that an immediate search is necessary to
ensure the safety or security of anything referred to in paragraphs
3(1)(a) to (c) or a person referred to in paragraph 3(1)(d), a search of
a person shall be carried out only by a security guard of the same sex
as the person.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A security guard may
without a warrant search any property or thing in a controlled access
zone if the security guard has reasonable grounds to believe that the
property or thing is, or may contain anything that is, likely to
endanger the safety or security of anything referred to in paragraphs
3(1)(a) to (c) or a person referred to in paragraph 3(1)(d).

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who is in a controlled access zone with permission shall comply
  with every condition of access established for the zone and every
  direction given under this Order by a security guard and the person,
  or any property or thing under the person's control, may be removed
  from the zone by a security guard if the person fails to comply with
  any of those conditions or directions.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is in a controlled access zone
  without permission shall comply with every direction given under this
  Order by a security guard and the person, or any property or thing
  under the person's control, may be removed from the zone by a security
  guard if the person fails to comply with any of those directions.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A security guard may use only such force as is
  necessary when removing a person, or any property or thing under the
  control of the person, from a controlled access zone under
  subsection (1) or (2).

## [Authorization by Chief of the Defence Staff]{.HTitleText1} {#h-609134 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Chief of the Defence
Staff may authorize an officer to act on behalf of the Chief of the
Defence Staff for the purposes of this Order, other than section 2.
