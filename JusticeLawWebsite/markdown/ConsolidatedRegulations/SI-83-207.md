## Statutes of Canada Distribution Direction {#statutes-of-canada-distribution-direction .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/83-207
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

[PUBLICATION OF STATUTES ACT](/eng/acts/S-21)

Registration 1983-12-14

Direction Regarding the Distribution of the Acts of Parliament

P.C. 1983-3632 1983-11-17
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Supply and Services and the President of the Treasury
Board, pursuant to subsection 10(2) of the [Publication of Statutes
Act](/eng/acts/S-21) and section 52 of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to revoke Order in Council
P.C. 1953-609 of 22nd April, 1953[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_e){#fn_e-ID0EDBAA} and to make the annexed
[Direction regarding the Distribution of the Acts of
Parliament](/eng/regulations/SI-83-207).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_e-ID0EDBAA){#fn_e}Not
  published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639252 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Direction may be cited
as the [Statutes of Canada Distribution
Direction](/eng/regulations/SI-83-207).

## [Delivery]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639254 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Acts of the Parliament
of Canada shall, as soon after the close of each session as practicable,
be delivered without charge to the persons listed in the schedule.

:::::: section
::::: {#639256 .Schedule}
::: {}
## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-639257 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** Governor General

- **2** Lieutenant Governors of the Provinces

- **3** Speaker of the Senate

- **4** Speaker of the House of Commons

- **5** Prime Minister

- **6** Leader of the Opposition in the Senate

- **7** Leader of the Opposition in the House of Commons

- **8** Ministers of the Government of Canada

- **9** Members of the Parliament of Canada not otherwise included on
  this list

- **10** Members of the judiciary of Canada, including provincial court
  judges and magistrates

- **11** Attorneys General, Ministers of Justice and Solicitors General
  of the Governments of the Provinces

- **12** Chief or senior legislative counsel of each Province

- **13** Chief librarians of court houses in Canada

- **14** Chief librarians of law schools in Canada

- **15** Members and officials of the Government of any foreign country
  and persons holding positions in universities and other institutions
  in that country if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} reciprocal arrangements exist for similar
    distribution without charge in Canada; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of Justice, the Secretary of State for
    External Affairs, the Librarian and Archivist of Canada or the
    Parliamentary Librarian recommend the free distribution.
