## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000968 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definition in this
section applies in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Alternative Fuels
    Act](/eng/acts/A-10.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000971 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
definition [[alternative fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1)
of the Act, the following, when used as the sole source of direct
propulsion energy of a motor vehicle, are prescribed to be alternative
fuels:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} ethanol;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} methanol;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} propane gas;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} natural gas;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} hydrogen;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} electricity;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsections 4(1) and 5(1) of the
  Act, any blended fuel that contains at least 50 per cent of one of the
  fuels referred to in paragraphs (a) to (e); and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsections 4(2) and 5(2) of the
  Act, any blended fuel that contains one of the fuels mentioned in
  paragraphs (a) to (e).

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
definition [[motor vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of
the Act, the following classes of vehicles are prescribed to be motor
vehicles:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} automobiles;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} passenger vans;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} buses;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} light duty trucks; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} medium duty trucks.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1000987 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on September 24, 1996.
