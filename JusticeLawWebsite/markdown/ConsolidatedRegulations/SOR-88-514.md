## DOMCO Remission Order No. 2 {#domco-remission-order-no.-2 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-514
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1988-09-29

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duty on Rotogravure Printing
Rolls Imported by Domco Industries Limited, Farnham, Quebec

P.C. 1988-2255 1988-09-29
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 101 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of customs duty on
rotogravure printing rolls imported by DOMCO Industries Limited,
Farnham, Quebec](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-514).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1987, c. 49
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913885 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the DOMCO Remission Order No. 2.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913887 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the customs duty paid or payable under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on rotogravure printing rolls imported by
DOMCO Industries Limited, Farnham, Quebec, other than 20 per cent of the
duty payable under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on the value
of the work done outside Canada to increase their value or alter their
condition by any process of manufacture or other means, on condition
that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are imported on or after July 1, 1988;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} they are used in the manufacture of resilient floor
  coverings by DOMCO Industries Limited;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} they are not less than 144 inches face width; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue, within three years of the date of their importation.
