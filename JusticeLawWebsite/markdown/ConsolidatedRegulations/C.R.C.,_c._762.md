## Exported Vessels Remission Order {#exported-vessels-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 762
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties on Imported Equipment
and Material Used in the Construction of Exported Vessels
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561643 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Exported Vessels Remission Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._762).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561645 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the customs duties paid or payable on goods imported on or after
November 27, 1970, to be wrought into or attached to vessels that are
declared by the Minister of Industry, Trade and Commerce to be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} eligible ships under the [Shipbuilding Temporary
  Assistance Program Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._349); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} eligible ships for export under the [Shipbuilding
  Industry Assistance Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._348).

- SI/88-17, s. 2(E)
