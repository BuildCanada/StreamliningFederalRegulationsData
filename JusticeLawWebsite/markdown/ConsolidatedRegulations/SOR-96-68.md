## Prescribed Persons and Organizations Regulations {#prescribed-persons-and-organizations-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/96-68
:::

[VETERANS REVIEW AND APPEAL BOARD ACT](/eng/acts/V-1.6)

Registration 1996-01-04

Regulations Prescribing Persons and Organizations

P.C. 1996-18 1996-01-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Veterans Affairs, pursuant to paragraph 45(e) of the
[Veterans Review and Appeal Board Act](/eng/acts/V-1.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-96-68_e_hq_5907){#fn_SOR-96-68_e_hq_5907-ID0EDBAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations prescribing persons
and organizations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-68).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002843 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Prescribed Persons and Organizations
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-68).

## [Prescription]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002845 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following persons and
organizations are prescribed for the purposes of sections 30 and 37 of
the [Veterans Review and Appeal Board Act](/eng/acts/V-1.6):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Army, Navy and Air Force Veterans in Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Bureau of Pensions Advocates;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Merchant Navy Coalition for Equality;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of Veterans Affairs;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the National Council of Veteran Associations in
  Canada; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the Royal Canadian Legion.
