## Form of Deeds Relating to Certain Successions of Cree and Naskapi Beneficiaries Regulations {#form-of-deeds-relating-to-certain-successions-of-cree-and-naskapi-beneficiaries-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-547
:::

[NASKAPI AND THE CREE-NASKAPI COMMISSION ACT](/eng/acts/C-45.7)

CREE-NASKAPI (OF QUEBEC) ACT

Registration 1989-11-23

Regulations Prescribing the Form of Deeds Relating to the Acceptance,
Renunciation or Settlement of a Succession of Certain Cree or Naskapi
Beneficiaries that is Composed Wholly or Partly of Movable, Immovable of
Traditional Property Located on Category IA or IA-N Land or in Which
Persons Suffering Legal Incapacity Are Interested

P.C. 1989-2302 1989-11-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
sections 10 and 177 of the Cree-Naskapi (of Quebec) Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed Regulations prescribing the form of deeds
relating to the acceptance, renunciation or settlement of a succession
of certain Cree or Naskapi beneficiaries that is composed wholly or
partly of movable, immovable or traditional property located on Category
IA or IA-N land or in which persons suffering legal incapacity are
interested.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920696 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Form of Deeds Relating to Certain Successions of Cree and
Naskapi Beneficiaries Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-547).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920698 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[deed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a deed that relates to the
acceptance, renunciation or settlement of a succession referred to in
section 177 of the Cree-Naskapi (of Quebec) Act. (*acte*)

## [Form of Deeds]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920700 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A deed shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be dated;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} indicate the place at which it was made;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} name and adequately identify each of the parties; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} precisely describe the property affected.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A deed may be made in
authentic form.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a deed that is not made in authentic form shall be
  signed by each of the parties.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party who is unable to sign the party's name to a
  deed that is not made in authentic form shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make the party's mark on the deed and include on
    the deed a declaration setting out that the party is unable to sign
    the party's name; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have the deed signed, on the party's behalf, by
    another person and include on the deed a declaration setting out
    that the party is unable to sign the party's name and that the
    person who signed on the party's behalf did so in the presence of
    and according to the instructions of the party.

<!-- -->

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A deed
  that is not made in authentic form shall, in respect of each party, be
  signed by two witnesses who know the party and, where a person signed
  on a party's behalf, that person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each witness for a party to a deed that is not made
  in authentic form shall attach to the deed an affidavit

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} declaring that the witness knows the party and the
    person, if any, who signed on the party's behalf; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} certifying the signature or mark of the party or
    the signature of the person, if any, who signed on the party's
    behalf.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (1) and (2), a person
  may be a witness for a party only if the person has reached the age of
  majority, is not mentally incapacitated, has no interest in the deed
  and is not the spouse, or relative of the first degree, of the party.

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920718 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in these
Regulations has the effect of exempting a deed from the provisions of
the [Cree-Naskapi Land Registry
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1070).
