## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841369 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Conference]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Conference]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the intergovernmental
    conference known as the United Nations Peacekeeping Defence
    Ministerial Conference, to be held in Vancouver, British Columbia on
    November 14 and 15, 2017. ([Conférence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in Schedule
    III to the [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[official delegate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[official delegate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person
    designated by a foreign state, foreign government or international
    organization who are invited to attend the Conference. ([délégué
    officiel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[relevant period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[relevant period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning
    on November 12, 2017 and ending on November 17, 2017. ([période
    visée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841375 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministers of Defence

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During
  the relevant period, the official delegates of a foreign state or a
  foreign government, including Ministers of Defence and their
  representatives, and Chiefs of Defence Staff and their
  representatives, shall have in Canada to the extent required for the
  exercise of their functions in Canada in relation to the Conference,
  the privileges and immunities set out in Sections 11, 12, 14, and 15
  of Article IV of the Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Heads, senior officials and other
  officials of international organizations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} During the relevant period, the official delegates of
  international organizations shall have in Canada to the extent
  required for the exercise of their functions in Canada in relation to
  the Conference, the following privileges and immunities:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of heads of international organizations
    and their representatives and senior officials, the privileges and
    immunities comparable to the privileges and immunities accorded to
    diplomatic agents under the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic
    Relations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of other officials, the privileges and
    immunities set out in Section 18 of Article IV of the Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Experts

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} During the relevant period, experts who are
  performing missions for international organizations shall have in
  Canada to the extent required for the exercise of their functions in
  Canada in relation to the Conference, the privileges and immunities
  set out in Article VI of the Convention.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841385 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
