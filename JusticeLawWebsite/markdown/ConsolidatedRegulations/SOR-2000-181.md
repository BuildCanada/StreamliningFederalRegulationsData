## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656193 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
"Advisory Committee" means the DNA Data Bank Advisory Committee
established by section 2.

## [DNA Data Bank Advisory Committee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656195 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** An advisory committee is
hereby established, to be known as the DNA Data Bank Advisory Committee,
consisting of a Chairperson, a Vice-Chairperson, a representative of the
Office of the Privacy Commissioner and up to six other members who may
include representatives of the police, legal, scientific and academic
communities.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-269, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The members of the Advisory
Committee shall be appointed by the Solicitor General of Canada for a
term of not more than five years.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A member of the Advisory
Committee is eligible for reappointment on the expiry of their term.

## [Duties of the Advisory Committee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656201 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Advisory Committee
shall, on its own motion when the Advisory Committee considers it
necessary or on the request of the Commissioner, advise the Commissioner
on any matter related to the establishment and operation of the national
DNA data bank that is referred to in section 3 of the [DNA
Identification Act](/eng/acts/D-3.8).

## [Annual Report to the Commissioner]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656203 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Advisory Committee
shall report annually to the Commissioner, within three months after the
end of each fiscal year, on its activities during that year.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656205 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which sections 2, 3 and 12 of the [DNA
Identification Act](/eng/acts/D-3.8), chapter 37 of the Statutes of
Canada, 1998, come into force.
