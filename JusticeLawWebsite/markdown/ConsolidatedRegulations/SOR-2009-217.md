## CPFTA Sugar Aggregate Quantity Limit Order {#cpfta-sugar-aggregate-quantity-limit-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2009-217
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 2009-07-30

CPFTA Sugar Aggregate Quantity Limit Order

P.C. 2009-1206 2009-07-30
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 49.5(9)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_1752_hq_7422){#fn_1752_hq_7422-ID0EGCAA} of the
[Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_1752_hq_7423){#fn_1752_hq_7423-ID0EDCAA},
hereby makes the annexed [CPFTA Sugar Aggregate Quantity Limit
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2009-217).

## [Tariff Rate Quota]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749850 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The aggregate quantity
limit of goods referred to in tariff item Nos. 1701.91.10, 1701.99.10,
1702.90.21, 1702.90.61, 1702.90.70 and 1702.90.81 that are entitled to
the Peru Tariff, for the period set out in column 1 of the schedule, is
the applicable aggregate quantity limit set out in column 2.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749852 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_IndFD6_hq_7847){#fn_IndFD6_hq_7847-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force on the day on which
section 42 of the [Canada-Peru Free Trade Agreement Implementation
Act](/eng/acts/C-8.4), chapter 16 of the Statutes of Canada 2009, comes
into force.
