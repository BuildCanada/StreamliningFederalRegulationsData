## Saskatchewan Flax Order {#saskatchewan-flax-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2001-344
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 2001-09-20

Saskatchewan Flax Order

P.C. 2001-1640 2001-09-20
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food, pursuant to section
2[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA}of
the [Agricultural Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), hereby makes
the annexed [Saskatchewan Flax Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-344).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672861 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Agri-Food Act, Statutes of
    Saskatchewan, S.S. 1990-91, c. A-15.2. ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan Flax
    Development Commission established pursuant to the Act.
    ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[flax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[flax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the seeds, straw, fibre, shives,
    or any part of the plant [linum usitatissimum]{.otherLang
    lang="la"}. ([lin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Saskatchewan Flax Development
    Plan, established pursuant to section 3 of The Saskatchewan Flax
    Development Plan Regulations, R.R.S. c. A-15.2 Reg 5, as amended
    from time to time, made pursuant to the Act.
    ([plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672867 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Commission is
authorized to regulate the marketing of flax in interprovincial and
export trade and for those purposes may, with respect to persons and
property situated in Saskatchewan, exercise all or any powers like the
powers exercisable by the Commission under the Act and the Plan in
relation to the marketing of flax locally, within Saskatchewan.

## [Levies or Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672869 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may, in
relation to the powers granted to it by section 2,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix, impose and collect levies or charges from
  persons referred to in that section who are engaged in the production
  or marketing of flax and for those purposes may classify those persons
  into groups and fix the levies or charges payable by the members of
  the different groups in different amounts; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the levies or charges for the purposes of the
  Commission, including the creation of reserves.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672873 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
