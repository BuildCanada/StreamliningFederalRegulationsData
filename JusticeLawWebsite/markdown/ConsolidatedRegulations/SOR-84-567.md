## Muskoka Airport Zoning Regulations {#muskoka-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/84-567
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1984-07-19

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Muskoka Airport

P.C. 1984-2610 1984-07-18
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 6 of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to approve the annexed
[Regulations respecting zoning at Muskoka
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-567) made by the Minister of Transport.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884394 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Muskoka Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-567).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884396 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Muskoka Airport at
      Gravenhurst in the District of Muskoka, in the Province of
      Ontario; ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part II of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, prepared for
      the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
      which strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the assigned
  elevation of the airport reference point is deemed to be 278 m above
  sea level.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-1047, s. 1(E)
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884409 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, which lands are more particularly described in
Part VI of the schedule.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884411 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any approach surface;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any transitional surface.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-1047, s. 2(E)
:::

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884418 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in paragraphs 4(a) to (c), the Minister
may make a direction that the owner or occupier of the land on which
that object is growing remove the excessive growth.

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884420 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit such land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste edible by or attractive
to birds.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Description of Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-isection-2description-of-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point is a point marked by a tablet described as
DHO Precise BM 278-67 and set horizontally in the north face of the
concrete foundation, 1.37 m west of the northeast (front) corner and
24 cm below the siding, of a one storey (metal clad) equipment garage on
the property of Muskoka Airways, east side of Highway 11, 6.0 km south
of the junction of Highway 11 and the entrance street to the Town of
Bracebridge, 2.9 km north of the junction of Highway 11 and Reays Road,
175.3 m south of Muskoka Airways Office and 11.9 m west of the gravel
entrance drive.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iisection-2description-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Muskoka Airport Zoning Plan
No. 44-022-82-48, Sheets 2, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9, dated August 24, 1983, are
surfaces abutting each end of the strips associated with runways
designated 09-27 and 18-36 and are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 09 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 2 500 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 310 m from the projected centre line; the said imaginary
  horizontal line being 62.50 m measured vertically above the elevation
  at the end of the strip;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 18 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 3 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 375 m from the projected centre line; the said imaginary
  horizontal line being 60 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 27 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 2 500 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 310 m from the projected centre line; the said imaginary
  horizontal line being 62.50 m measured vertically above the elevation
  at the end of the strip; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 36 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 3 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 375 m from the projected centre line; the said imaginary
  horizontal line being 60 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiisection-2description-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Muskoka Airport Zoning Plan
No. 44-002-82-48, Sheets 1 to 11 inclusive, dated August 24, 1983, is an
imaginary surface located at a common plane established at a constant
elevation of 45 m above the assigned elevation of the airport reference
point, except where that common plane is less than 9 m above the surface
of the ground, the outer surface is an imaginary surface located at 9 m
above the surface of the ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Description of the Strips]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivsection-2description-of-the-strips .SchedHeadL1}

The strips, shown on Muskoka Airport Zoning Plan No. 44-002-82-48,
Sheets 5 and 8, dated August 24, 1983, are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 09-27 being 120 m in
  width, 60 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway, and
  655 m in length; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 18-36, being 150 m
  in width, 75 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway,
  and 1643 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vsection-2description-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Muskoka Airport Zoning Plan
No. 44-002-82-48, Sheets 2, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9, dated August 24, 1983, is
a surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at a ratio of 1 m
measured vertically to 7 m measured horizontally at right angles to the
centre line and centre line produced of each strip extending upward and
outward from the lateral limits of each strip and its approach surfaces
to an intersection with the outer surface or another transitional
surface of an adjoining strip.
