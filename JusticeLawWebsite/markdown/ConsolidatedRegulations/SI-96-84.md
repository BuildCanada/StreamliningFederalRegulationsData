## Order Designating the Winnipeg Airports Authority Inc. as a Designated Airport Authority and Designating the Date for the Transfer of the Winnipeg International Airport to the Winnipeg Airports Authority Inc. {#order-designating-the-winnipeg-airports-authority-inc.-as-a-designated-airport-authority-and-designating-the-date-for-the-transfer-of-the-winnipeg-international-airport-to-the-winnipeg-airports-authority-inc. .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/96-84
:::

[AIRPORT TRANSFER (MISCELLANEOUS MATTERS) ACT](/eng/acts/A-10.4)

Registration 1996-09-18

Order Designating the Winnipeg Airports Authority Inc. as a Designated
Airport Authority and Designating the Date for the Transfer of the
Winnipeg International Airport to the Winnipeg Airports Authority Inc.

P.C. 1996-1388 1996-08-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection
2(2) of the [Airport Transfer (Miscellaneous Matters)
Act](/eng/acts/A-10.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBBCA}, hereby
designates

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EBBCA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1992, c.
  5
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Winnipeg Airports Authority Inc. as a designated
  airport authority; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day immediately following the closing date as
  defined in the Agreement to Transfer entered into under the authority
  of Order in Council P.C. 1996-1387 of August 28, 1996, as the transfer
  date for the Winnipeg International Airport to the Winnipeg Airports
  Authority Inc.
