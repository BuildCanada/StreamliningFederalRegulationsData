## [Finance Entity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673725 .Part}

Definition of [[finance entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), for the purposes of the definition [[finance
  entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsections 464(1) and 507(1) of the
  [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), 386(1) of the [Cooperative Credit
  Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01), 490(1) of the [Insurance
  Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) and 449(1) of the [Trust and Loan
  Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), [[finance
  entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity that engages in a
  business that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} issuing payment, credit or charge cards and, in
    cooperation with others including financial institutions, operating
    a payment, credit or charge card plan; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} making or refinancing loans or entering into any
    other similar arrangements for advancing funds or credit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The definition in subsection (1) does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a financial institution;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a factoring entity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a financial leasing entity; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a specialized financing entity, if it makes or
    refinances loans to, or enters into any other similar arrangements
    for advancing funds or credit with, only entities that it controls
    or in which it holds a substantial investment.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673737 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
sections 464 and 507 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), 386 of the
[Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01), 490 of the
[Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) and 449 of the [Trust and
Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), as enacted by sections 127, 132,
314, 426 and 550, respectively, of the [Financial Consumer Agency of
Canada Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1), chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada,
2001, come into force.
