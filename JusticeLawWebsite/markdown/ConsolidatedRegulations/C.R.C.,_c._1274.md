## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502137 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
mail sent to or by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any member of the Canadian Forces outside Canada who
  is served through a Canadian Forces Post Office or Fleet Mail Office
  address; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any civilian who normally receives his mail through a
  Canadian Forces Post Office.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following words shall be printed on a Forces Air Letter:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the face thereof, "Forces Air
    Letter/Lettre-avion pour militaire";

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the back thereof, "No enclosures permitted/Ne
    rien insérer" and "Authorized by the Canada Post Corporation for
    mailing in Canada/Autorisé par la Société canadienne des postes pour
    mise à la poste au Canada"; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the face or back thereof, the folding and
    opening instructions in English and in French.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Forces Air Letter shall not exceed 22 cm in length
  and 11 cm in width, and be not less than 14 cm in length and 9 cm in
  width after it is folded in accordance with the folding instructions
  printed thereon.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The minimum basis weight of a Forces Air Letter shall
  be 60 g/m^2^.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every Forces Air Letter shall be rectangular in
  shape.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No Forces Air Letter shall contain an enclosure.

## [Customs Documentation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502178 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An item
  of letter mail or a letter-post item that, by reason of its volume,
  nature or value, may be subject to customs duty in the country of
  destination shall have affixed on the address side in the upper left
  corner under the sender's name and address, where given,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a completed green label referred to in paragraph
    7.1(1)(a) of the [International Letter-post Items
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-807); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a completed green printed facsimile of the label
    referred to in paragraph (a) that is 74 mm in length and 52 mm in
    width.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the value of an item referred to in
  subsection (1) exceeds \$500, the sender shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} complete the Customs Declaration Dispatch Note set
    out in Schedule III to the [International Letter-post Items
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-807);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} securely affix the declaration and note to the
    item, on the address side thereof where possible; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} hand the item to a postal employee at a post
    office.
