## Pari-Mutuel Betting Supervision Regulations {#pari-mutuel-betting-supervision-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-365
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 1991-05-31

Pari-Mutuel Betting Supervision Regulations
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to section 204[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1){#footnote1-ID0EHBCA} of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46), hereby revokes the Race Track Supervision
Regulations, C.R.C., c. 441, and makes the annexed Regulations
respecting the supervision and operation of pari-mutuel betting at
race-courses and the prohibition, restriction and regulation of the
possession of drugs and equipment used in the administering of drugs at
race-courses, in substitution therefor.

### [Race information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-934860 .Subheading}

#### [General]{.HTitleText3} {#h-934861 .Subheading}

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the association conducting the
    pari-mutuel betting;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the pari-mutuel betting is
    supervised by the Minister and a statement that all pools are
    calculated and distributed in accordance with these Regulations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Canadian Pari-Mutuel Agency contact information;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the terms and conditions of a bet, including the
    information that a winning ticket is valid indefinitely;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of how bets are cashed and cancelled,
    including any limits to the conditions, in accordance with
    subsection 57(6) and section 117;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statement that bets may be refunded under certain
    circumstances and a description of how the public will be informed
    of any refunds;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} with respect to odds,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the ratios that are used by an
      association to display odds,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the statement that approximate odds represent
      only the probable pay-out price of the win pool at the time they
      are posted and have no bearing on the pay-out price for any other
      pool,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a statement that if approximate odds for any
      horse are posted as "1:9", the pay-out price based on a \$2 bet on
      the win pool may be as low as \$2.10, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a statement that if approximate odds for any
      horse are posted as "99:1", the pay-out price based on a \$2 bet
      on the win pool may be greater than \$200; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the value of outstanding tickets

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an association that is authorized
      to conduct betting under subsection 90(4) or section 95, for each
      of the preceding quarters ending on March 31, June 30, September
      30 and December 31, as calculated not later than three months
      after the end of each quarter, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, for the preceding year, as
      calculated not later than three months after the end of that year.

  in writing with any information set out in sections 25 to 27.

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} with respect to odds,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the approximate odds and final odds on each

### [Requirements Respecting Pari-mutuel Betting Operations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-934979 .Subheading}

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  association shall conduct pari-mutuel betting on races that are not
  held in accordance with the applicable rules of racing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No association shall offer a type of bet that is not
  expressly authorized under its permit.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Executive Director may set out terms and
  conditions in the association's permit governing each type of bet.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a bet is made in foreign currency, the value of
  the bet for the purposes of calculating the applicable deductions and
  pay-out prices shall be determined by converting the foreign currency
  into Canadian currency at one of the following rates, as applicable:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the foreign currency is one for which the Bank
    of Canada quotes a rate of exchange with Canadian currency, the rate
    of exchange quoted by the Bank on the day before the day on which
    the post time of the first race of the racing card is scheduled or,
    if there is no rate quoted for that day, the most recent rate of
    exchange quoted by the Bank between those currencies on a day before
    the day on which the post time of the first race of the racing card
    is scheduled;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the foreign currency is not one for which the
    Bank of Canada quotes a rate of exchange with Canadian currency, the
    rate of exchange quoted between those currencies on the Internet at
    [www.xe.com](http://www.xe.com) at 16:00 Coordinated Universal Time
    (UTC) on the day before the day on which the post time of the first
    race of the racing card is scheduled or, if that rate is not
    available, the midpoint rate of exchange quoted between those
    currencies on the Internet at [www.oanda.com](http://www.oanda.com)
    at 22:00 Eastern Time on the day before the day on which the post
    time of the first race of the racing card is scheduled.

### [Calculation of Pay-out Price of Place Pools]{.HTitleText2} {#h-935479 .Subheading}

**[[123]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-123 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 124
to 126, an association shall calculate the pay-out price of a place pool
by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deducting the value of the place bets made on the
  horses that finish first and second in the official result from the
  net pool to determine the calculating pool;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} dividing the calculating pool equally between the
  horses that finish first and second in the official result;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} dividing each horse's portion of the calculating pool
  by the value of the place bets made on that horse; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} adding \$1 to the quotients obtained pursuant to
  paragraph (c).

**[[124]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-124 .sectionLabel}** Where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} place bets have been made on all but one of the
  horses that finish first and second in the official result,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} only one horse finishes a race,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} no place bets have been made on any of the horses
  that finish first and second in the official result, or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} two or more horses of the same entry or mutuel field
  finish first and second or in a dead heat in first place in the
  official result,

an association shall calculate the pay-out price of a place pool in
accordance with subsection 120(1), except that the net pool shall be
divided by the value of the place bets made on

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the horses referred to in paragraph (a) on which bets
  have been made,

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the horse referred to in paragraph (b) that finishes
  the race,

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the next horse in the official result after the
  horses referred to in paragraph (c), on which place bets have been
  made, or

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the entry or mutuel field referred to in paragraph
  (d),

as the case may be.

**[[125]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-125 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 126,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where two horses finish in a dead heat in first place
  in the official result, an association shall calculate the pay-out
  price of a place pool in accordance with section 123;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where three horses finish in a dead heat in first
  place in the official result, an association shall calculate the
  pay-out price of a place pool in accordance with section 127; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where two or more horses finish in a dead heat in
  second place in the official result, an association shall calculate
  the pay-out price of a place pool in accordance with section 123,
  except that the calculating pool shall be divided in the following
  manner, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} one half to the horse that finishes first, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} one half divided equally among the horses that
    finish in the dead heat in second place.

**[[126]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-126 .sectionLabel}** Where one horse of an
entry or a mutuel field finishes first in the official result and
another horse of the same entry or mutuel field finishes in a dead heat
in second place in the official result with one or more other horses, an
association shall calculate the pay-out price of a place pool in
accordance with section 123, except that the calculating pool shall be
divided in the following manner, namely,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} one half to the horse that finishes first, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} one half divided equally among the horses that finish
  in the dead heat in second place,

and the portions allocated in paragraphs (a) and (b) to the two horses
of the entry or mutuel field shall be combined.

### [Calculation of Pay-out Price of Show Pools]{.HTitleText2} {#h-935506 .Subheading}

### [Calculation of Pay-out Price of Daily Double Pools and Consolation Doubles]{.HTitleText2} {#h-935562 .Subheading}

**[[133]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-133 .sectionLabel}** Subject to subsection
134(2), section 135, subsection 136(2) and section 137, an association
shall calculate the pay-out price of a daily double pool by dividing the
net pool by the value of the bets made that combine the winning horses
in each of the two races of the daily double.

- **[[134]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-134
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where no daily double bets have been
  made on the winning horse of the first race of a daily double, the
  next horse in the official result on which daily double bets have been
  made shall be deemed to be the winning horse in that race, and for the
  purposes of the daily double pool any daily double bets on that horse
  shall be deemed to be winning bets on the first race of the daily
  double.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, in respect of the second race of a daily
  double, no daily double bets have been made that combine the winning
  horse in the first race of a daily double with the winning horse in
  the second race of the daily double, an association shall calculate
  the pay-out price of a daily double pool by dividing the net pool by
  the value of the bets made that combine the winning horse in the first
  race with the next horse in the official result, on which bets have
  been made in the second race of the daily double.

**[[135]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-135 .sectionLabel}** Where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an association has posted the official result of the
  first race of a daily double and the second race of a daily double

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is cancelled,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is declared "no contest" pursuant to the
    applicable rules of racing,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is postponed beyond the racing card for which it
    is scheduled, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} cannot properly be judged pursuant to the
    applicable rules of racing,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} no daily double bets have been made that combine the
  winning horse in the first race of a daily double with any horse that
  finishes the second race of the daily double, or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} no horses finish the second race of a daily double,

an association shall calculate the pay-out price of a daily double pool
in accordance with subsection 120(1), except that the net pool shall be
divided by the value of the daily double bets made on the horse that
finished first in the official result of the first race of the daily
double.

- **[[136]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-136 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  association shall calculate a consolation double by dividing the net
  pool by the value of the daily double bets made on the winning horse
  in the first race of the daily double.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, after betting has been closed for the first race
  of a daily double, a horse, entry or mutuel field in the second race
  of the daily double is scratched, the association shall calculate the
  pay-out price of the daily double pool by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} multiplying the quotient obtained in subsection (1)
    by the value of the bets made that combine the winning horse in the
    first race with the horse, entry or mutuel field that was scratched
    from the second race;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} deducting the product obtained pursuant to
    paragraph (a) from the net pool; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} dividing the second net pool obtained pursuant to
    paragraph (b) by the value of the bets made on the winning
    combination.

### [Calculation of Pay-out Prices of Exactor, Quinella and Triactor Pools]{.HTitleText2} {#h-935604 .Subheading}

**[[138]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-138 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 139 to 142,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the order of positions in which horses finish a race
  is based on the official result;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of a position that is not preceded by any
  other position is one;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number of any position that immediately follows
  another position is the number of that other position plus one;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the first position is considered to be the highest of
  all positions;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} where horses finish a race in a dead heat in any
  position, all of them are considered to finish the race in that
  position;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} where two or more horses from the same entry or
  mutuel field finish a race, each of those horses is considered to
  finish the race in a dead heat in the highest position in which any
  horse from that entry or mutuel field finishes the race;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the situation described in paragraph (f), no
  position may be recognized for the purposes of determining the order
  of positions if no horse is considered to finish a race in that
  position; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of determining the number of horses
  that finish a race in a dead heat in any position and of determining
  any winning bet, horses from the same entry or mutuel field are
  considered to be one horse.

**[[139]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-139 .sectionLabel}** An association shall
calculate the pay-out price in respect of winning quinella, exactor and
triactor bets determined under sections 140 to 142 as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where there is one combination of horses, by dividing
  the net pool by the value of the winning quinella, exactor or triactor

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where there are two or more combinations of horses,

    among those combinations,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} dividing each combination's portion of the
    calculating pool by the sum of the winning quinella, exactor or
    triactor bets in which the combination is selected, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} adding \$1 to each quotient obtained under
    subparagraph (iii).

#### [EIPH Program]{.HTitleText3} {#h-935762 .Subheading}

- **[[170.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-170.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an exercise-induced pulmonary
  hemorrhage (EIPH) program for horses, established by the appropriate
  Commission, is implemented at a race-course of an association, the
  Executive Director shall, subject to the other requirements of these
  Regulations, approve pari-mutuel betting at that race-course where the
  Commission, at that race-course,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establishes and maintains an up-to-date EIPH list
    and provides the test inspector with a copy of it at the beginning
    of every racing card;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensures that any horse on the EIPH list remains on
    that list for a period of at least 100 days;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} maintains an accurate medication record in respect
    of every horse on the EIPH list;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prohibits any horse on the EIPH list from racing
    after a recurrence of bleeding during or after a race that is
    confirmed by a veterinarian designated by the appropriate
    Commission, for a minimum period of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 14 consecutive days after that recurrence,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 90 consecutive days after the second recurrence,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 365 consecutive days after the third
      recurrence;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} verifies that any horse on the EIPH list receives
    the drug furosemide, in an amount not less than 150 mg and not more
    than 250 mg, intravenously, four hours plus or minus fifteen minutes
    before the post time of the race in which the horse is entered;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} ensures that any horse on the EIPH list that did
    not receive the drug furosemide in accordance with paragraph (e) is
    scratched;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} where any horse on the EIPH list did not receive
    the drug furosemide, as evidenced by the absence of that drug in an
    analysis of an official sample of urine collected from that horse,
    prohibits the horse from racing for a minimum period of 14
    consecutive days; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} ensures that an officer is given, on request,
    access to records maintained for the EIPH list.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(a), the
  Commission shall add a horse to the list if the following conditions
  are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the owner or trainer of the horse and the
    consulting veterinarian licensed by the appropriate Commission have
    determined that it would be in the horse's best interest to be
    placed on the list; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the veterinarian designated by the appropriate
    Commission has endorsed that determination.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a horse on an EIPH list is chosen to undergo a
  test pursuant to subsection 161(1), the owner or trainer of the horse
  shall provide a test inspector, immediately after the race, with an
  official sample of blood collected in accordance with section 162.

### [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-935783 .Subheading}

**[[171]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-171 .sectionLabel}** No person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} administer or permit the administration of a drug to
  a horse that is entered in a race in such a manner that a certificate
  of positive analysis would be issued under section 165 with respect to
  that horse;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} tamper with a horse before, during or after a race in
  such a manner as to interfere with the collection or analysis of an
  official sample;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} unless otherwise permitted by a test inspector or a
  veterinarian designated by the appropriate Commission, administer,
  after a race, anything except drinking water to a horse that has been
  chosen to undergo a test under subsection 161(1), until the horse is
  discharged;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} interfere with the work of any person who is
  undertaking activities relating to an equine drug control program;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} interfere with the collection or analysis of an
  official sample;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} substitute another horse for any horse that has been
  chosen to undergo a test pursuant to subsection 161(1); or

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} substitute or misrepresent the contents of an
  official sample container.
