Order Designating the President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada

P.C. 2016-656 2016-06-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}

the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (c.1)[[Footnote

the definition [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2

  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_4360_hq_18544){#fn_4360_hq_18544-ID0EBBBAA}
