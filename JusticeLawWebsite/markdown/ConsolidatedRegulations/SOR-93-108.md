## Quebec Beef Cattle Producers' Levies or Charges (Interprovincial and Export Trade) Order {#quebec-beef-cattle-producers-levies-or-charges-interprovincial-and-export-trade-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-108
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1993-03-08

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Levies or
Charges From Producers in the Province of Quebec Who Are Engaged in the
Production or Marketing of Beef Cattle in Interprovincial and Export
Trade
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Fédération des producteurs de bovins du Québec, pursuant to sections
3 and 4 of the Quebec Beef Cattle Order, made by Order in Council P.C.
1992-1067 of May 21, 1992[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBBBA}, hereby makes
the annexed Order providing for the fixing, imposing and collecting of
levies or charges from producers in the Province of Quebec who are
engaged in the production or marketing of beef cattle in interprovincial
and export trade.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EBBBA){#footnote1_e}SOR/92-293,
  1992 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2158
  :::

Longueuil, Quebec, March 5, 1993
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949859 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Quebec Beef Cattle Producers' Levies or Charges (Interprovincial
and Export Trade) Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-108).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949861 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[beef cattle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[beef cattle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any beef cattle produced
    or marketed in the Province of Quebec; ([bovin]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who acquires or
    receives any beef cattle from a producer, directly or through any
    type of auction, for the buyer's own purposes;
    ([acheteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Fédération des
    producteurs de bovins du Québec; ([Fédération]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who breeds beef
    cattle in Quebec for the person's use or for others, produces them
    or has them produced, or markets them in any way.
    ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies or Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949867 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Each producer shall pay to
the Commodity Board, for all beef cattle produced or marketed by that
producer in interprovincial and export trade, the levies or charges that
are set out in the schedule.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-15, s. 1
- SOR/2012-56, s. 1

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949871 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5, the
producer shall pay every levy or charge payable pursuant to section 3 to
the Commodity Board at its headquarters in Longueuil, Quebec, within the
time limits set out in the by-laws referred to in that section.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A buyer in Quebec shall
deduct from the moneys payable to a producer of beef cattle all levies
or charges payable by the producer to the Commodity Board under section
3 and shall remit the levies or charges directly to the Commodity Board
at its headquarters in Longueuil, Quebec.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2012-56, s. 2
- SOR/2014-1, s. 2
