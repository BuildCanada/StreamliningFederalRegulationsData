[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Fisheries
    Act](/eng/acts/F-14). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[aquatic research project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aquatic research project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a project
    that involves the deposit of a deleterious substance in water
    frequented by fish and any monitoring related to that
    project. ([projet de recherche aquatique]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[deleterious substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deleterious substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in subsection 34(1) of the Act. ([substance
    nocive]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Director, Forest Products
    and Fisheries Act Division, Industrial Sectors Directorate,
    Environmental Stewardship Branch, Department of the
    Environment. ([directeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Experimental Lakes Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Experimental Lakes Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area
    located in Ontario and comprising the lakes set out in Schedule 1
    and the streams set out in Schedule 2. ([Région des lacs
    expérimentaux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person who manages the
    Experimental Lakes Area. ([exploitant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of the Experimental Lakes Area.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to deposit deleterious
substance

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A deleterious substance may
be deposited in the Experimental Lakes Area during the course of an
aquatic research project if the purpose of the deposit is solely for
scientific research and the conditions set out in sections 4 to 7 are
met.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition --- annual report

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The operator must, not
later than March 31 of each year, submit to the Director a report for
the preceding calendar year containing the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the operator;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the operator's contact information, including their
  address and telephone number, and email address, if any;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and resumé, including a list of
  publications, of each person responsible for the supervision of every
  aquatic research project conducted in the Experimental Lakes Area;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} contact information for each person referred to in
  paragraph (c), including their address and telephone number, and email
  address, if any;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the reporting period;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the date of the report;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the number of aquatic research projects conducted
  during the reporting period; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the following information in respect of each aquatic
  research project conducted during the reporting period:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the title,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the starting date and the actual or projected
    ending date,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the name and affiliation of the lead researcher,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the project, including how it has
    contributed or will contribute to the development of knowledge for
    the purpose of managing, conserving, protecting or restoring fish
    populations or water frequented by fish,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} for each deposit, if any, made in the course of the
    project, the date and duration of the deposit, the location of the
    deposit, consisting of the lake or stream where the deposit is made
    and its latitude and longitude, the substance deposited and the
    amount deposited or the concentration of the substance in the
    deposit along with the flow rate of the deposit,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the findings up to the end of the
    reporting period,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} a list of publications related to the project, if
    any, and

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} a summary of any measures put in place to limit
    the harmful effects of any deposit on fish populations and water
    frequented by fish.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition --- emergency response plan

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator must prepare an emergency response plan, update it annually
  and make it readily available on site to the persons who are to
  implement it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elements of the plan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The emergency response plan must include the
  following elements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identification of the risks for potential
    unanticipated deposits that could reasonably be expected to have
    harmful effects on fish, fish habitat or the use of fish by man;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the processes put in place to
    prevent, prepare for and respond to those risks;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a list of individuals who are to be responsible for
    implementing the plan and a description of their roles and
    responsibilities;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the identification of the training required for
    those individuals; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} alerting and notification procedures, including the
    measures to be taken to notify members of the public who may be
    adversely affected by the unanticipated deposits referred to in
    paragraph (a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition --- requirement to notify

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The operator must notify a
fishery officer or an inspector in writing as soon as feasible in the
event of unanticipated morbidity and mortality in fish that occurs as a
result of any aquatic research project conducted in the Experimental
Lakes Area.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for notification

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The operator must notify
the Director in writing if the processes referred to in section 3 of the
[Regulations Establishing Conditions for Making Regulations under
Subsection 36(5.2) of the Fisheries Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-91)
change in a way that could result in a failure to meet one or more of
the conditions set out in that section, and must do so not later than 10
business days after that change occurs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
