## Ontario Bean Marketing (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#ontario-bean-marketing-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 177
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Regulations Respecting the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade
of Beans Produced in Ontario
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536010 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Ontario Bean Marketing (Interprovincial and Export)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._177).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536012 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[beans]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[beans]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means white pea beans and yellow-eye
    beans produced in Ontario; ([haricots]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Bean
    Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to The Farm Products
    Marketing Act of Ontario; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is authorized by
    the Commodity Board as its agent to purchase beans from producers on
    its behalf; ([négociant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production of beans. ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536018 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of beans in interprovincial and export trade and
to persons and property situated in the Province of Ontario.

## [Purchase and Sale]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536020 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  producer shall sell or offer to sell beans to any person other than a
  dealer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person other than a dealer shall purchase beans
  from a producer.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No dealer shall purchase beans from a producer except
  on behalf of the Commodity Board.

## [Transporting and Packing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536025 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  producer shall transport or ship beans to any place other than the
  premises of a dealer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall process, pack or package any beans
  that have not been sold by or on behalf of the Board.

## [Pools]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536029 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commodity Board shall, for the purpose of distributing the proceeds
  from sales by it of Canada Eastern beans delivered to it by producers
  pursuant to these Regulations, conduct the following pools:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a pool for the proceeds from sales of Canada
    Eastern pea beans;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a pool for the proceeds from sales of Canada
    Eastern yellow-eye beans that are marketed by producers within the
    quotas fixed and allotted to the producers by the Commodity Board;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a pool for the proceeds from sales of Canada
    Eastern yellow-eye beans that are marketed by producers in excess of
    the quotas fixed and allotted to the producers by the Commodity
    Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commodity Board shall distribute the proceeds of
  the sales in each pool conducted pursuant to subsection (1), after
  deducting therefrom all the necessary and proper disbursements and
  expenses in respect of the sales, in such a manner that each producer
  receives his share of the total proceeds in the pool in proportion to
  the quantity and taking into account the class, grade and size of the
  beans delivered by him to the Commodity Board.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each producer who sells beans to the Commodity Board
  pursuant to these Regulations is entitled to be paid

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on delivery to a dealer an initial payment in an
    amount determined by the Commodity Board; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} payments, in addition to the payment referred to in
    paragraph (a), in such amounts and at such times as the Commodity
    Board determines, until all of the proceeds referred to in
    subsection (2) received by the Commodity Board from the sale of
    beans have been paid to the producers entitled thereto.

## [Dealers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536039 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board may
appoint any person as a dealer and shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribe the duties and the terms and conditions of
  employment of every dealer; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide for the remuneration of every dealer.

## [Quality Control]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536043 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** All beans sold by a
producer to a dealer shall be inspected and sold on the basis of the
grades established by subsection 15(1) of the [Canada Grain
Act](/eng/acts/G-10) and Schedule I to that Act.

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  dealer receives beans from a producer, the dealer or the producer may
  require a sample of the beans to be taken for the purpose of checking
  the grade.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every sample of beans taken pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} weigh not less than 2 pounds;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be accepted by the dealer and the producer as a
    fair sample of the beans from which it was taken;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be placed by the dealer in a sealed, moisture-proof
    container with a label thereon containing the name and address of
    the producer and the name of the dealer; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if required by an inspector of the Canadian Grain
    Commission for examination and testing, be delivered to the
    inspector at Chatham, Ontario.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of accepting a sample of beans as a
  fair sample pursuant to paragraph (2)(b), any person delivering a
  shipment of beans to a dealer on behalf of a producer shall be deemed
  to be the authorized agent of the producer.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Where a dispute arises
between a dealer and a producer in respect of the grade, pick, moisture
content or condition of any shipment of beans, the matters in dispute
shall be referred to an inspector of the Canadian Grain Commission at
Chatham, Ontario, and the decision of the inspector shall be final and
binding on the dealer and the producer.
