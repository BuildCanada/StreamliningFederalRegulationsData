## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1130376 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this By-law.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Deposit Insurance
    Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bank group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bank group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a domestic systemically
    important bank and its affiliates, any entity in which that bank has
    a [[substantial investment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
    section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), and any business
    enterprise that that bank operates with other persons, including a
    joint venture or a special purpose vehicle. ([groupe
    bancaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[critical function]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[critical function]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an activity that is
    performed by an entity within a bank group for persons not
    affiliated with the bank group and whose absence or failure would
    have an adverse effect on the stability of the financial system or
    on the functioning of the economy in Canada. ([fonction
    essentielle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[critical shared service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[critical shared service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an activity
    that is performed by an entity within a bank group or an external
    provider for one or more business units or legal entities of the
    bank group and whose absence or failure would lead to the cessation
    of, or present a material risk to the continuity of, critical
    functions. ([service partagé essentiel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[material change]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[material change]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a change in a bank
    group's legal structure, business, operations, critical functions,
    critical shared services or material legal entities, or in the laws
    and regulations applicable to the bank group, that would require one
    or more amendments to the bank's resolution plan. ([changement
    important]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[material legal entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[material legal entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a legal entity
    or branch within a bank group that meets at least one of the
    following criteria:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it performs a critical function or a critical
      shared service;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cessation or disruption of its operations
      might prevent the implementation of the bank's resolution
      strategy; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is a member institution. ([entité juridique
      importante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[resolution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[resolution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the exercise by the
    Corporation of its powers under paragraph 7(d) of the Act, or the
    exercise of similar powers by an authority in another jurisdiction
    within or outside Canada. ([règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[resolution plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[resolution plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a domestic
    systemically important bank's plan for the bank group's
    resolution. ([plan de règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Submission and Maintenance of a Resolution Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1130390 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  resolution plan submitted to the Corporation by a bank under section
  39.01 of the Act must first be approved by the bank's board of
  directors or by a delegated committee of the bank's board of
  directors, unless otherwise specified by the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Partial plan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may request that a bank submit only a
  partial resolution plan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Policies and procedures for updating
resolution plan

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The bank
  must establish policies and procedures that ensure that the resolution
  plan is maintained, tested, regularly reviewed against the current
  legal, financial, regulatory and operational characteristics of the
  bank group and is updated to reflect any material change.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy provided to Corporation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The bank must provide, on the Corporation's request,
  a copy of the policies and procedures referred to in paragraph (1) and
  a summary of the maintenance and testing activities undertaken by the
  bank in respect of the resolution plan.

## [Contents of a Resolution Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1130401 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A resolution plan must
contain

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of each critical function;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of each critical shared service;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of each material legal entity;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a resolution strategy that meets the requirements set
  out in section 5;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the bank's assessment of how the bank group's legal
  structure, operations and financial arrangements will support the
  implementation of the resolution strategy;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the bank's assessment of how the resolution strategy
  will minimize reliance on public sector financial assistance and
  ensure that material legal entitie have access to sufficient
  loss-absorbing capacity, liquidity and funding during the resolution;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a list of the bank group's regulators and a summary
  of the regulatory requirements applicable to the resolution strategy
  and a description of how those requirements will be satisfied in a
  manner that supports implementation of the resolution strategy;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a list of the bank group's material contractual
  arrangements and a description of how the contractual requirements
  will be met in a manner that supports the implementation of the
  resolution strategy;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the bank's analysis of how the bank group's
  governance practices, operational capabilities and information systems
  will support the implementation of the resolution strategy;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the bank's assessment of impediments to the
  implementation of the resolution strategy, including a summary of the
  impact of those impediments on the Corporation's ability to implement
  the resolution strategy and on the continuity of critical functions;
  and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} a work plan that describes the planned activities for
  the maintenance, validation and testing of the resolution plan,
  including activities to account for any material change and proposes
  remedial actions and time frames to address deficiencies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resolution strategy

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The resolution strategy
must describe

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} how the Corporation will carry out the resolution of
  each member institution in a manner that supports the continuity of
  critical functions;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} how each material legal entity other than a member
  institution

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} will continue to operate in a manner that supports
    the continuity of critical functions;

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} can be disposed of or wound down without impeding
    the continuity of critical functions; or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} can be placed in an orderly insolvency proceeding
    or resolved without impeding the continuity of critical functions;
    and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} how the profile and operations of the bank group can
  be reduced through divestitures or wound down, with minimal adverse
  effect on the stability of the financial system in Canada.

## [Material Change]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1130423 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of material change

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The bank must notify the
Corporation as soon as feasible after the occurrence of a material
change.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Material change report

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of the notification required under section 6, the Corporation
  may request that the bank provide a material change report, which must
  include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a brief description of the material change;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a reference to the sections of the resolution plan
    affected by the material change; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the bank's plan and schedule for updating the
    resolution plan to account for the material change.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for providing report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The bank must provide the material change report
  within 30 days after the day on which the Corporation makes the
  request, unless the Corporation has specified a longer period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Work plan

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The bank must include, upon
request by the Corporation, the plan referred to in paragraph 7(1)(c) in
its work plan for the next submission of its resolution plan.

## [Notice of Compliance and Request for Revised Work Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1130436 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of compliance

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Following its assessment of
the resolution plan, the Corporation must notify the bank in writing
whether the resolution plan complies with this By-law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Particulars of non-compliance

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** If the resolution plan
does not comply with this By-law, the Corporation must advise the bank
in writing of the deficiencies that resulted in the non-compliance and
request that the bank submit a revised work plan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revised work plan

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  revised work plan must describe the remedial actions to be taken by
  the bank to address the relevant deficiencies and the schedule
  required to complete those actions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of revised work plan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The revised work plan submitted to the Corporation
  must first be approved by the bank's board of directors or by a
  delegated committee of the bank's board of directors, unless otherwise
  specified by the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The revised work plan must be submitted in writing to
  the Corporation within 30 days after the day on which the Corporation
  makes the request, unless the Corporation has specified a longer
  period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of the revised work plan, the Corporation
  must determine whether the resolution plan

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} complies with this By-law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is partially non-compliant, meaning that it
    contains one or more deficiencies that would prevent the
    implementation of the bank's resolution strategy but for which an
    acceptable work plan is being substantially complied with; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is materially non-compliant, meaning that it
    contains one or more deficiencies for which an acceptable work plan
    is either not in place or not being substantially complied with.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation must notify the bank in writing of
  its decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent revision to work plan

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Any subsequent revision to the work plan must be
  agreed to by the Corporation and, unless otherwise specified by the
  Corporation, must first be approved by the bank's board of directors
  or by a delegated committee of the bank's board of directors.
