## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726720 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cooperative Credit Associations
Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01).

## [Insider Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726722 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraph 263(a) of the Act, an insider means an insider as
  it is defined, for the purpose of insider reports, under any
  legislation set out in column 2 of the schedule.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph 263(b) of the Act, the
  required form and content of an insider report are to be the form and
  content required for insider reports under any legislation set out in
  column 2 of the schedule.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph 263(c) of the Act, the
  procedure for the submission and publication of insider reports is to
  be the procedure required for the submission and publication of
  insider reports under any legislation set out in column 2 of the
  schedule.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An insider required to
submit an insider report under any legislation set out in column 2 of
the schedule is to comply with the requirements set out in that
legislation.

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726728 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  that is subject to an exemption under any legislation set out in
  column 2 of the schedule, or an order of the relevant provincial
  securities regulator, that provides that the person is not an insider
  for the purposes of the applicable legislation, is not an insider for
  the purpose of subsection 2(1).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person that is subject to an exemption from any or
  all insider reporting requirements under any legislation set out in
  column 2 of the schedule, or an order of the relevant provincial
  securities regulator, is also exempt from those requirements for the
  purposes of these Regulations.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726732 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
