## Order Designating the President of the Federal Economic Development Agency for Northern Ontario as Deputy Head in Respect of that Agency {#order-designating-the-president-of-the-federal-economic-development-agency-for-northern-ontario-as-deputy-head-in-respect-of-that-agency .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2021-50
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2021-08-18

Order Designating the President of the Federal Economic Development
Agency for Northern Ontario as Deputy Head in Respect of that Agency

P.C. 2021-843 2021-08-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 11(2)(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_608651-E_hq_152){#fn_608651-E_hq_152-ID0EECAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_610261-E_hq_4314){#fn_610261-E_hq_4314-ID0EBCAA},
designates the President of the Federal Economic Development Agency for
Northern Ontario as deputy head in respect of that Agency, effective
August 12, 2021.
