## [Provision of Information to the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-984760 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Where the Department of
Transport has entered into an agreement with Statistics Canada for the
sharing of information collected under the [Statistics
Act](/eng/acts/S-19) and a carrier submits information required by these
Regulations to Statistics Canada, the information is considered to be
submitted to the Minister.

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided in these Regulations, the information required by these
Regulations to be provided to the Minister must be provided

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by electronic means; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the information is being collected directly by
  Statistics Canada under the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19), by the
  means by which the information is provided under that Act.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Air Carriers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-984767 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this section apply in this Part.

  [[air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canadian air carrier
      or foreign air carrier. ([transporteur aérien]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the holder of
      an air operator certificate issued under Subpart 3, 4 or 5 of
      Part VII of the [Canadian Aviation
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433). ([transporteur aérien
      canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[charter service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[charter service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the transportation
      of passengers or cargo by aircraft pursuant to a contract under
      which a person, other than the air carrier that operates the air
      service, reserves a block of seats or part of the cargo space of
      an aircraft for the person's use or for resale to the
      public. ([service d'affrètement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[foreign air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[foreign air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the holder of an
      air operator certificate issued under Subpart 1 of Part VII of the
      [Canadian Aviation
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433). ([transporteur aérien
      étranger]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[general aviation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[general aviation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the operation of an
      aircraft for purposes other than publicly available passenger or
      cargo transport for remuneration, and includes business aviation,
      aerial work, instructional flying and air ambulance
      services. ([aviation générale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[general aviation operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[general aviation operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any entity
      that engages in general aviation, and includes a corporation, a
      government entity as defined in subsection 211(1) of the [Excise
      Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1) and a foreign
      government. ([exploitant de l'aviation générale]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[large air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[large air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an air carrier
      that operates a charter service or a scheduled service that has
      transported a worldwide total of two million passengers or more
      during each of the two preceding calendar years. ([grand
      transporteur aérien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[level I air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[level I air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canadian air
      carrier that, in the calendar year before the year in which
      information is provided under this Part, transported at least
      2,000,000 revenue passengers or at least 400 000 tonnes of
      cargo. ([transporteur aérien de niveau I]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[level II air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[level II air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canadian air
      carrier that, in the calendar year before the year in which
      information is provided under this Part, transported

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least 100,000 but fewer than 2,000,000
        revenue passengers; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at least 50 000 but less than 400 000 tonnes of
        cargo. ([transporteur aérien de niveau II]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[level III air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[level III air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canadian air
      carrier that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not a level I air carrier or level II air
        carrier; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the calendar year before the year in which
        information is provided under this Part, realized gross revenues
        of at least \$2,000,000 for the provision of air services for
        which the air carrier held a licence. ([transporteur aérien de
        niveau III]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[level IV air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[level IV air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canadian air
      carrier that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not a level I air carrier, level II air
        carrier or level III air carrier; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the calendar year before the year in which
        information is provided under this Part, realized gross revenues
        of less than \$2,000,000 for the provision of air services for
        which the air carrier held a licence. ([transporteur aérien de
        niveau IV]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[level V air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[level V air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2013-196, s. 5\]]{.Repealed}

  [[level VI air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[level VI air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2013-196, s. 5\]]{.Repealed}

  [[local air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[local air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2013-196, s. 5\]]{.Repealed}

  [[non-revenue passenger]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[non-revenue passenger]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a passenger travelling for free or at a fare
        available only to persons who are employees or agents of an air
        carrier or are travelling on the business of an air carrier; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person, such as an infant, who does not
        occupy a seat. ([passager non payant]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[regional air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[regional air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2013-196, s. 5\]]{.Repealed}

  [[revenue passenger]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[revenue passenger]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a passenger who is
      not a non-revenue passenger and for whose transportation an air
      carrier receives remuneration, and includes a passenger travelling
      with a ticket

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} purchased under a publicly available
        promotional offer;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} purchased through a loyalty program or through
        the redemption of loyalty points or miles;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} purchased with a corporate discount or at a
        preferential fare; or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} obtained as compensation for denied
        boarding. ([passager payant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[scheduled service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[scheduled service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the transportation
      of passengers or cargo by an aircraft provided by an air carrier
      that operates the air service and that, directly or indirectly,
      sells some or all of its seats or part or all of its cargo space
      to the public on a price per seat, price per unit of mass or price
      per volume of cargo basis. ([service régulier]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[small air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[small air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an air carrier
      other than a large air carrier that operates a charter service or
      a scheduled service with at least one aircraft equipped for the
      carriage of passengers with a certificated maximum carrying
      capacity of 70 passengers or more. ([petit transporteur
      aérien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[unit toll service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[unit toll service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2013-196, s. 5\]]{.Repealed}

  carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[level III air
  carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[level IV air
  carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection (1), a Canadian air
  carrier that is in its first year of operation is considered to have

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it is created as the result of the

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the definition [[large air

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the definition [[small air
  carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, aircraft that are used solely for
  charter services are not included if the block of seats of the
  aircraft that is reserved is not resold to the public.

### [Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-984808 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  level I air carrier, level II air carrier, level III air carrier or
  level IV air carrier must provide to the Minister financial
  information, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a summary of its land, buildings, ground equipment
    and flight equipment accounts that sets out, in respect of an annual
    reporting period,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the gross investments at the beginning and at the
      end of the calendar year,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the additions to and retirement of assets, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the accumulated depreciation at the beginning
      and at the end of the calendar year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement of changes in its financial position
    that sets out, in respect of a quarterly reporting period, the
    increases and decreases in

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} working capital,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} operating activities, including net income,
      items not affecting cash, net change in non-cash working capital
      and cash provided by operating activities,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} financing activities, including long-term debt,
      subordinated debt, dividends paid and cash provided by financing
      activities, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} investing activities, including fixed assets and
      cash flow related to investing activities.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A level I air carrier that operates scheduled
  services, and a level II air carrier, level III air carrier, level IV
  air carrier or foreign air carrier that operates a scheduled service
  with at least one aircraft whose maximum certificated take-off weight
  is more than 25 000 kg, must provide to the Minister operational
  information about each take-off or landing in Canada or abroad of a
  scheduled service flight that originates in, is destined for or makes
  a stopover at an aerodrome in Canada, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the take-off or landing and
    the aerodrome where it took place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the flight number, if there is one;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the flight's origin and destination;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a take-off, the next aerodrome on
    the flight;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a landing, the previous aerodrome on
    the flight;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the passenger capacity and the cargo capacity;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the aircraft's model and registration mark;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the number of revenue passengers and the number of
    non-revenue passengers enplaning, deplaning, arriving, departing and
    transiting;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the mass of freight and the mass of mail enplaning,
    deplaning, arriving, departing and transiting;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the number of pieces and the mass of checked
    luggage presented for security screening; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} information as to whether the aircraft is a
    passenger aircraft or a freighter.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A level II air carrier, level III air carrier,
  level IV air carrier or foreign air carrier that operates a scheduled
  service only on aircraft whose maximum certificated take-off weight is
  not more than 25 000 kg must provide to the Minister information
  aggregated on a quarterly basis about

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of revenue passengers transported
    between each city-pair, in each direction, on a coupon origin and
    destination basis;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the mass of freight and mail transported between
    each city-pair, in each direction, on an on-board origin and
    destination basis; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the destinations of its non-stop flights from each
    aerodrome that it serves, and the number of those flights to each
    destination.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A level I air carrier, level II air carrier,
  level III air carrier, level IV air carrier or foreign air carrier
  that operates a charter service, whether or not available to the
  public, on an aircraft whose maximum certificated take-off weight is
  5 670 kg or more must provide to the Minister operational information
  about each charter flight that it operates with the aircraft and that
  originates in, is destined for or makes a stopover at an aerodrome in
  Canada, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of each take-off and landing and
    the aerodrome where it took place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the flight number, if there is one;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the passenger capacity and the cargo capacity;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the aircraft's model and registration mark;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the number of revenue passengers and the number of
    non-revenue passengers transported between each city-pair, including
    city-pairs that are not Canadian cities, on an on-board origin and
    destination basis or an enplaned and deplaned basis;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the mass of freight and the mass of mail
    transported, on an on-board origin and destination basis;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the number of pieces and the mass of checked
    luggage presented for security screening;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} information as to whether the flight terminates at
    the same aerodrome where it originated; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the code that identifies the type of charter permit
    issued by the Canadian Transportation Agency.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A level I air carrier or level II air carrier that
  transported more than 600,000 revenue passengers by scheduled service
  in each of the two previous calendar years must provide to the
  Minister information about each revenue passenger's complete
  itinerary, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the passenger's origin and destination;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the connecting points;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the operating air carrier, the advertised air
    carrier and the fare basis code, for each leg; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total fare paid.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A level I air carrier, level II air carrier,
  level III air carrier or level IV air carrier must provide to the
  Minister information about each aircraft in its fleet, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its make, model and year of construction;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the make, model and year of construction of its
    engine;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount and type of fuel it consumed on domestic
    flights; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount and type of fuel it consumed on
    international flights.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} An air carrier that transported dangerous goods
  between Canada and another country must provide to the Minister the UN
  number assigned to the goods by the United Nations Committee of
  Experts on the Transport of Dangerous Goods.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} An air carrier referred to in subsection (2), (3)
  or (4) must provide the information required by that subsection
  electronically using Transport Canada's Electronic Collection of Air
  Transportation Statistics program.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A level I air carrier,
level II air carrier or level III air carrier that transported more than
10 000 tonnes of cargo in the previous calendar year, and a foreign air

Canada in the previous calendar year, must provide to the Minister
information about the supply chain, including

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the air carrier is a participant in the
  International Air Transport Association's Cargo 2000 program, the date
  and time and the location of each step within the supply chain, as
  determined by the Cargo 2000 program; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the air carrier is not a participant in the
  International Air Transport Association's Cargo 2000 program,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date and time when, and the location where,
    each shipment of the cargo was delivered to the carrier, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date and time when, and the location where,
    each shipment of the cargo was ready to be picked up from the
    carrier.

**[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** An air carrier that
sells the entire capacity of an aircraft for a flight to another air
carrier must, in respect of that capacity, provide to the Minister the

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Rail Carriers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-984896 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-984897 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Part.

[[class I rail carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[class I rail carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a railway company
    that realized gross revenues of at least \$250,000,000 for the
    provision of rail services in each of the two calendar years before
    the year in which information is provided pursuant to this
    Part. ([transporteur ferroviaire de catégorie I]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[class II rail carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[class II rail carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a railway
    company that realized gross revenues of less than \$250,000,000 for
    the provision of rail services in each of the two calendar years
    before the year in which information is provided pursuant to this
    Part. ([transporteur ferroviaire de catégorie II]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[class III rail carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[class III rail carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a railway
    company, other than a class I rail carrier or a class II rail
    carrier, that is engaged in the operation of bridges, tunnels and
    stations. ([transporteur ferroviaire de catégorie
    III]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[gross ton miles per mile of track]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gross ton miles per mile of track]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    number of tons of freight transported by a train and the number of
    tons of locomotives and cars of the train multiplied by the distance
    travelled and divided by the length of track
    operated. ([tonnes-milles brutes par mille de voie]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[infant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[infant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is less than two
    years of age. ([enfant en bas âge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[movement event]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[movement event]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the placement of a railway car at a location by a
      class I rail carrier for the loading or unloading of grain;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the release of a railway car by a shipper,
      consignee or terminal for transportation by a class I rail
      carrier;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transportation of a railway car by a class I
      rail carrier; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the constructive or actual placement of a railway
      car at a location by a class I rail carrier. ([événement de
      mouvement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[passholder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[passholder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is an employee
    of a rail carrier or is otherwise entitled to be transported by a
    rail carrier without charge or at a reduced fare. ([titulaire de
    laissez-passer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[railway company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[railway company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned to
    that term in section 87 of the Act. ([compagnie de chemin de
    fer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[revenue passenger]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[revenue passenger]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a rail
    carrier, means a passenger the charge for whose transportation is
    remunerated to the rail carrier based on a tariff for the
    transportation. ([passager payant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Western Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Western Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 147 of the Act. ([région de l'Ouest]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

### [Report to Minister --- Traffic Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1394404 .Subheading}

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  class 1 rail carrier that is also a class II rail carrier and earns
  revenue in Canada for the reporting period must provide to the
  Minister a report by electronic means that sets out the following
  information with respect to any traffic that is moved by railway car:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the code that identifies the rail carrier;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the year and the month during which the rail
    carrier reports having earned revenue from the movement;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the SPLC or FSAC of the location in Canada where
    the movement originated;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the originating province of Canada or state of
    Mexico or the United States of the movement;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the SPLC or FSAC of the location where the movement
    terminated in Canada;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the destination province of Canada or state of
    Mexico or the United States of the movement;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} for traffic that did not originate on the reporting
    rail carrier's Canadian network, the SPLC or FSAC of the location
    where the traffic entered the rail carrier's Canadian network, as
    well as the name and identification code of the rail carrier that
    handed the traffic to the reporting rail carrier;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} for traffic that did not terminate on the reporting
    rail carrier's Canadian network, the SPLC or FSAC of the location
    where the traffic left the rail carrier's Canadian network, as well
    as the name and identification code of the rail carrier that
    received the traffic from the reporting rail carrier;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the SPLC or FSAC of the location where the traffic
    crossed the border from the United States to Canada and, in the case
    of an FSAC, the name of the rail carrier that made the crossing, if
    applicable;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the SPLC or FSAC of the location where the traffic
    crossed the border from Canada to the United States and, in the case
    of an FSAC, the name of the rail carrier that made the crossing, if
    applicable;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the code that identifies the rail carrier that
    originated the traffic, if it originated in Canada;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the code that identifies the rail carrier that
    terminated the traffic if the destination is in Canada;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} the identifier for a movement that is subject to
    the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286);

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} the STCC of the commodities;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} the equipment type code, taken from the Association
    of American Railroads codes, indicating the details of the equipment
    used in the movement;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} the number of railway cars involved in the
    movement;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} the number of containers, if any, involved in the
    movement;

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} the amount in tons of goods carried in the
    movement;

  - [(s)]{.lawlabel} the intermodal flag indicating that the traffic is
    containerized;

  - [(t)]{.lawlabel} the Hazardous Materials Response Code assigned to
    the goods by the United States Bureau of Explosives and defined in
    the Hazardous Materials Shipping Descriptions Data Base of the
    Association of American Railroads, if applicable; and

  - [(u)]{.lawlabel} the distance in miles of the movement by the rail
    carrier on its Canadian network.

  Minister on a monthly basis, no later than the last day of the month
  following the month to which the information relates.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The first report is to be provided for the month in
  which this section comes into force.

## [SCHEDULE XII]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 55)]{.OriginatingRef}[Rail Service and Performance Indicator Methodologies and Requirements]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-1394492 .scheduleLabel}
:::

### [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#part-1interpretation .SchedHeadL1}

- **1** The following definitions apply in this Schedule.

  [[bad order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[bad order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the status or designation
      assigned to railway rolling stock, whether railway cars or
      locomotives, that indicates that the asset is unfit for
      service. ([mauvais état]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[carload traffic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[carload traffic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means traffic, whether
      empty or loaded, that is not intermodal traffic. ([trafic de
      wagons complets]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[fertilizer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fertilizer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means traffic classified under
      STCC 2812534, 2818142, 2818146, 2818170, 2818426, 2819173,
      2819454, 2819815, 2871235, 2871236, 2871238, 2871244, 2871313,
      2871315 and 2871451. ([engrais]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[final railway facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[final railway facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the railway
      facility from which a railway car is delivered to
      destination. ([installation ferroviaire finale]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[intermodal traffic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[intermodal traffic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means intermodal
      containers, whether empty or loaded, regardless of the type of
      good that is being transported in the containers. ([trafic
      intermodal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[railway facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[railway facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means railway-owned
      infrastructure such as a terminal or yard. ([installation
      ferroviaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[reporting week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[reporting week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period from
      Monday to Sunday. ([semaine de déclaration]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[terminal area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[terminal area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a top 10 railway
      facility and the stations, interchanges, and other railway
      facilities that the rail carrier serves, either directly or
      indirectly, from that top 10 railway facility. ([zone
      terminale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[through train]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[through train]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a train that has the
      same railway cars when it enters and exits a railway
      facility. ([train direct]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[top 10 railway facilities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[top 10 railway facilities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the top 10
      of the rail carrier's railway facilities with the highest volume
      throughput, as calculated in accordance with section 31 of this
      Schedule. ([dix installations ferroviaires
      principales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[western grain]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[western grain]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
      paragraph (a) of the definition [[grain ]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}in
      section 147 of the Act. ([grain de l'Ouest]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- **2** The following are the types of commodities that are used in the
  methodologies:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} western grain;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} agricultural products and food;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} forest products;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} chemicals and plastics;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} fertilizers;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} petroleum products, including liquefied petroleum
    gases;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} coal and petroleum coke;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} metals and minerals;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} motor vehicles and equipment;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} other products.

- **3** The following are the detailed types of commodities that are
  used in the methodologies:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all western grain;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} western grain limited to canola meal;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} western grain limited to canola oil;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} agricultural products and food;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} all forest products;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} forest products limited to lumber, logs and wood
    products;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} forest products limited to pulp and paper;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} chemicals and plastics;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} fertilizers;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} all petroleum products, including liquefied
    petroleum gases;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} petroleum products limited to propane;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} coal and petroleum coke;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} all metals and minerals;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} metals and minerals limited to metallic ores;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} metals and minerals limited to non-metallic ores,
    stone, clay and glass;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} metals and minerals limited to metal products,
    scrap metal, waste and other scrap;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} motor vehicles and equipment;

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} other products.

- **4** The following are the equipment types that are used in the
  methodologies:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} intermodal containers;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} covered hopper cars;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} tank cars;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} gondola and open hopper cars;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} automotive flat cars;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} boxcars;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} centrebeam and bulkhead cars;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} other cars, excluding cars for carrying intermodal
    containers;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all equipment types listed in paragraphs (a) to
    (h).

- **5** The following are the geographic regions that are used in the
  methodologies:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} British Columbia;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Alberta and Northwest Territories;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Saskatchewan;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Manitoba;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Ontario;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} Quebec;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} New Brunswick and Nova Scotia.

- **6** The following are the periods of dwell time that are used in the
  methodologies:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} more than 24 hours but not more than 48 hours;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} more than 48 hours but not more than 96 hours;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} more than 96 hours but not more than 168 hours;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} more than 168 hours;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} 24 hours or more.

- **7** When calculating the indicators set out in Part 2 of this
  Schedule, the following cars are to be included:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} owned and leased cars, private cars and foreign
    cars that the rail carrier is operating on its network;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} loaded cars that have been released by the shipper
    to the rail carrier for pickup at origin;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} empty cars that have been released to the rail
    carrier.

- **8** When calculating the indicators set out in Part 2 of this
  Schedule, the following cars are to be excluded:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cars in bad order;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} stored cars;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} cars or intermodal containers in chargeable
    storage, except those that are accruing demurrage charges;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} loaded cars that have been placed by the rail
    carrier at destination;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} empty cars that have been placed at a customer
    facility for loading;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} cars in passenger, work and company service.

### [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Indicators]{.HTitleText1} {#part-2indicators .SchedHeadL1}

#### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#general .SchedHeadL2}

- **9** All indicators described in this Part are to be reported to one
  decimal place.

- - **10** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The first report must be accompanied by a
    list of the events that are used in the calculations of indicators
    set out in sections 15 to 18, 20, 21, 23, 25 and 26 of this
    Schedule.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The first report must be accompanied by a list of
    the STCC of the commodities that fall into each type listed in
    sections 2 and 3 of this Schedule.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The list of events must be updated following any
    subsequent change and submitted with the next report.

#### [Cars Online]{.HTitleText2} {#cars-online .SchedHeadL2}

- - **11** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The report must set out the following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of carload traffic, the daily average
      number of loaded cars online, broken down for each geographic
      region set out in section 5 of this Schedule, further broken down
      by each type of commodity set out in section 2 of this Schedule,
      as well as the Canada-wide total for all loaded cars online,
      broken down by each detailed type of commodity set out in section
      3 of this Schedule;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of intermodal traffic, the daily
      average number of loaded intermodal containers online, broken down
      for each geographic region set out in section 5 of this Schedule,
      as well as the Canada-wide total for all loaded intermodal
      containers;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of carload traffic and intermodal
      traffic,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the daily average number of loaded cars and
        loaded intermodal containers online, broken down for each
        geographic region set out in section 5 of this Schedule and
        Canada-wide, further broken down by each equipment type set out
        in section 4 of this Schedule;

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the daily average number of empty cars and
        empty intermodal containers online, broken down for each
        geographic region set out in section 5 of this Schedule and
        Canada-wide, further broken down by each equipment type set out
        in section 4 of this Schedule; and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the daily average number of all cars and
        intermodal containers online, broken down for each geographic
        region set out in section 5 of this Schedule and Canada-wide,
        further broken down by each equipment type set out in section 4
        of this Schedule.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Instead of the information required by subsection
    (1), the report for the BNSF Railway Company must set out, for all
    carload traffic and intermodal traffic, the Canada-wide daily
    average number of cars and intermodal containers online for the
    following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all loaded cars and intermodal containers
      combined;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all empty cars and intermodal containers
      combined; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all cars and intermodal containers combined.

- **12** The number of cars and intermodal containers referred to in
  section 11 of this Schedule includes cars and containers that the
  reporting rail carrier has placed for transfer at an interchange to
  another rail carrier until they are picked up by that other carrier
  and excludes cars and containers placed for interchange to the rail
  carrier by another rail carrier until they are picked up by the
  reporting rail carrier.

- **13** The calculation of the daily averages is to be based on a daily
  snapshot taken at the same time each day for all indicators that use a
  daily snapshot.

- **14** The first report must set out the time at which the snapshot is
  taken.

#### [Cars Not Moving]{.HTitleText2} {#cars-not-moving .SchedHeadL2}

- - **15** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The report must set out, in the case of
    carload traffic, the daily average number of loaded cars not moving
    at origin, except for cars containing fertilizer, broken down by
    each of the periods of dwell time set out in section 6 of this
    Schedule, broken down for each geographic region set out in section
    5 of this Schedule, and further broken down by each type of
    commodity, excluding fertilizer, set out in section 2 of this
    Schedule. Rail carriers are also to report the Canada-wide daily
    average number of loaded cars not moving at origin for each period
    of dwell time set out in section 6 of this Schedule, and further
    broken down by each detailed type of commodity set out in section 3
    of this Schedule.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must set out, in the case of intermodal
    traffic, the daily average number of loaded intermodal containers
    not moving at origin, broken down by each of the periods of dwell
    time set out in section 6 of this Schedule, further broken down by
    each geographic region set out in section 5 of this Schedule. Rail
    carriers are also to report the Canada-wide daily average number of
    loaded intermodal containers not moving at origin broken down for
    each period of dwell time set out in section 6 of this Schedule.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The daily average number of loaded cars and loaded
    intermodal containers not moving at origin is to be calculated as
    follows, based on a daily snapshot:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for carload traffic, the calculation is to
      include all loaded cars that have been released at origin by the
      customer for pickup by the rail carrier but have not yet departed
      the origin, with the time during which a car is not moving
      calculated as the time elapsed between the first customer release
      event and the time of the daily snapshot;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for intermodal traffic originating at a railway
      facility, the calculation is to include loaded intermodal
      containers that have arrived at the railway facility from which
      the rail movement begins but have not yet departed that facility
      by rail, with the time during which a container is not moving
      calculated as the time elapsed between the time at which the
      container arrives at the railway facility, or the last movement
      event after that, and the time of the daily snapshot;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for intermodal traffic originating at a facility
      other than a railway facility, the calculation is to include
      loaded intermodal containers that have been placed onto a railcar
      at origin and released by the customer for pickup by the rail
      carrier but have not yet departed the origin by rail, with the
      time during which a container is not moving calculated as the time
      elapsed between the first customer release event and the time of
      the daily snapshot.

- - **16** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The report must set out, in the case of
    carload traffic, the daily average number of loaded cars not moving
    en route for each of the periods of dwell time set out in section 6
    of this Schedule, broken down by each geographic region set out in
    section 5 of this Schedule, and further broken down by each type of
    commodity set out in section 2 of this Schedule. Rail carriers are
    also to report the Canada-wide average for each period of dwell time
    set out in section 6 broken down by each detailed type of commodity
    set out in section 3 of this Schedule.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must set out, in the case of intermodal
    traffic, the daily average number of loaded intermodal containers
    not moving en route, broken down by each of the periods of dwell
    time set out in section 6 of this Schedule, further broken down by
    each geographic region set out in section 5 of this Schedule. Rail
    carriers are also to report the Canada-wide daily average number of
    loaded intermodal containers not moving en route for each period of
    dwell time set out in section 6 of this Schedule.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The daily average number of loaded cars and loaded
    intermodal containers not moving en route is to be calculated on the
    basis of a daily snapshot and is to include all loaded traffic that
    has departed origin but has not yet arrived at the final railway
    facility or been placed at destination. The time during which a car
    or intermodal container is not moving is to be calculated as the
    time that has elapsed between its last recorded movement event and
    the time of the daily snapshot.

- - **17** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The report must set out, in the case of
    carload traffic, the daily average number of loaded cars not moving
    at destination for each of the periods of dwell time set out in
    section 6 of this Schedule, broken down by each geographic region
    set out in section 5 of this Schedule, and further broken down by
    each type of commodity set out in section 2 of this Schedule. Rail
    carriers are also to report the Canada-wide average for each period
    of dwell time set out in section 6 of this Schedule broken down by
    each detailed type of commodity set out in section 3 of this
    Schedule.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must set out, in the case of intermodal
    traffic, the daily average number of loaded intermodal containers
    not moving at destination, broken down by each of the periods of
    dwell time set out in section 6 of this Schedule, further broken
    down by each geographic region set out in section 5 of this
    Schedule. Rail carriers are also to report the Canada-wide daily
    average number of loaded intermodal containers not moving at
    destination for each period of dwell time set out in section 6 of
    this Schedule.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The daily average number of loaded cars and loaded
    intermodal containers not moving at destination is to be calculated
    as follows, based on a daily snapshot:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for carload traffic, the calculation is to
      include all loaded cars that have arrived at the final railway
      facility but have not yet been placed at destination, with the
      time during which a car is not moving calculated as the time that
      has elapsed between the arrival of the car at the final railway
      facility, or the last movement event after that, and the time of
      the daily snapshot, except if paragraph (d) applies;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for intermodal traffic that will depart the final
      railway facility by motor carrier, the calculation is to include
      all loaded intermodal containers that have arrived at the final
      railway facility but have not yet departed by motor carrier, with
      the time during which a container is not moving calculated as the
      time that has elapsed between the arrival of the car at the final
      railway facility, or the last movement event within that facility,
      and the time of the daily snapshot, except if paragraph (d)
      applies;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for intermodal traffic destined for a facility
      other than a railway facility, the calculation is to include all
      loaded intermodal containers that have arrived at the final
      railway facility but have not yet been placed at destination, with
      the time during which a container is not moving calculated as the
      time that has elapsed between the arrival of the car at the final
      railway facility, or the last movement event after that, and the
      time of the daily snapshot, except if paragraph (d) applies;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for traffic for which the final railway facility
      is part of the terminal area for one of the rail carrier's top 10
      railway facilities, the time during which a car or intermodal
      container is not moving is calculated as the time that has elapsed
      between the arrival of the car or intermodal container at the
      first railway facility within the terminal area, or the last
      movement event within the terminal area, and the time of the daily
      snapshot.

- - **18** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The report must set out the daily average
    number of loaded cars not moving and the daily average number of
    loaded intermodal containers not moving that are either destined for
    one of the rail carrier's top five major destination areas referred
    to in paragraph (3)(a), or transiting through one of the rail
    carrier's top five major destination areas referred to in paragraph
    (3)(b), for each of the periods of dwell time set out in section 6
    of this Schedule.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} For each major destination area described in
    subsection (1), the report must identify

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of carload traffic, the daily average
      number of loaded cars not moving, broken down by each type of
      commodity set out in section 2 of this Schedule; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of intermodal traffic, the daily
      average number of loaded intermodal containers not moving, broken
      down by whether they are at origin, en route, or at destination.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The top five major destination areas are determined
    on the basis of the highest number of loaded cars and loaded
    intermodal containers for the preceding three years that were

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} destined for one of its terminal areas; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} transiting through the last railway station on
      the line in Canada before it crosses the Canada-United States
      border.

- - **19** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The first report must be accompanied by a
    list of the top five major destination areas determined in
    accordance with subsection 18(3) of this Schedule.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The list must be updated three years after the day
    on which this Schedule comes into force and every three years after
    that in accordance with subsection 18(3) of this Schedule and
    submitted with the next report following any change to the list.

- - **20** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The report must set out the daily average
    number of empty cars not moving and, in the case of intermodal
    traffic, the number of empty intermodal containers not moving for
    each of the periods of dwell time set out in section 6 of this
    Schedule, broken down by each geographic region set out in section 5
    of this Schedule and Canada-wide, and further broken down by each
    equipment type set out in section 4 of this Schedule.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The daily average number of empty cars and
    intermodal containers not moving is to be calculated based on a
    daily snapshot , with the time during which an empty car or, for
    intermodal traffic, the time that an empty intermodal container is
    not moving, calculated as the time that has elapsed between the last
    recorded movement event and the time of the daily snapshot.

#### [Origin Dwell Time]{.HTitleText2} {#origin-dwell-time .SchedHeadL2}

- - **21** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The report must set out in hours the average
    dwell time at origin for all loaded traffic that departs an origin
    station during the reporting week,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of carload traffic, broken down by
      each geographic region set out in section 5 of this Schedule, and
      further broken down by each type of commodity, excluding
      fertilizer, set out in section 2 of this Schedule as well as a
      Canada-wide average broken down by each detailed type of commodity
      set out in section 3 of this Schedule; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of intermodal traffic, broken down by
      each geographic region set out in section 5 of this Schedule and
      Canada-wide.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The origin dwell time is to be calculated as
    follows:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for carload traffic, the origin dwell time is to
      be calculated as the time that has elapsed between the first
      customer release event at origin and the car's departure from
      origin;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for intermodal traffic originating at a railway
      facility, the origin dwell time is to be calculated as the time
      that has elapsed between the arrival of the container at the
      railway facility and its departure from that facility by rail;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for intermodal traffic originating at a facility
      other than a railway facility, the origin dwell time is to be
      calculated as the time that has elapsed between the first release
      of the loaded container by the customer and its departure from
      that facility by rail.

- **22** The number of cars and intermodal containers included in the
  calculation of the average dwell time at origin must be reported as
  follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the geographic regions set out in section 5 of
    this Schedule, the report must set out the total number of cars
    included in the calculation of the average dwell time at origin, not
    broken down by commodity, and, in the case of intermodal traffic,
    the total number of intermodal containers;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for Canada-wide, the report must set out the total
    number of cars and the total number of intermodal containers
    included in the calculation of the average dwell time at origin,
    broken down, in the case of carload traffic, by each detailed type
    of commodity set out in section 3 of this Schedule.

#### [Destination Dwell Time]{.HTitleText2} {#destination-dwell-time .SchedHeadL2}

- - **23** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The report must set out in hours the average
    dwell time at destination for all traffic that the rail carrier
    placed at destination during the reporting week, as follows:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of carload traffic, the average dwell
      time at destination broken down by each geographic region set out
      in section 5 of this Schedule, further broken down by each type of
      commodity, excluding fertilizer, set out in section 2 of this
      Schedule, as well as a Canada-wide average, broken down by each
      detailed type of commodity set out in section 3 of this Schedule;
      and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of intermodal traffic, the average
      dwell time at destination broken down by each geographic region
      set out in section 5 of this Schedule, as well as a Canada-wide
      average.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The average dwell time at destination is to be
    calculated as follows:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for carload traffic, the destination dwell time
      is to be calculated as the time that has elapsed between the
      arrival of the car at the final railway facility and its placement
      at destination and, in the case of a car that arrives at a final
      railway facility that is part of the terminal area for one of the
      rail carrier's top 10 railway facilities, the dwell time is to be
      calculated as the time that has elapsed between the car's first
      arrival at a railway facility that is part of the terminal area
      and its placement at destination;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for intermodal traffic that arrives at a final
      railway facility and departs the facility by motor carrier, the
      destination dwell time is to be calculated as the time that has
      elapsed between the arrival of the intermodal container at the
      final railway facility and its departure from that facility by
      motor carrier and, in the case of a container that arrives at a
      final railway facility that is part of the terminal area for one
      of the rail carrier's top 10 railway facilities, the dwell time is
      to be calculated as the time that has elapsed between the
      container's first arrival at a railway facility that is part of
      the terminal area and its departure from the final railway
      facility by motor carrier;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for intermodal traffic destined for a facility
      other than a railway facility, the destination dwell time is to be
      calculated as the time that has elapsed between the arrival of the
      intermodal container to the final railway facility and its
      placement at destination and, in the case of a container that
      arrives at a final railway facility that is part of the terminal
      area for one of the rail carrier's top 10 railway facilities, the
      dwell time is to be calculated as the time that has elapsed
      between the container's first arrival at a railway facility within
      the terminal area and its placement at destination.

- **24** The number of cars and intermodal containers included in the
  calculation of the average dwell time at destination must be reported
  as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the geographic regions set out in section 5 of
    this Schedule, the report must set out the total number of cars
    included in the calculation of the average dwell time at
    destination, not broken down by commodity, and, in the case of
    intermodal traffic, the total number of intermodal containers; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for Canada-wide, the report must set out the total
    number of cars and the total number of intermodal containers
    included in the calculation of the average dwell time at
    destination, broken down, in the case of carload traffic, by each
    detailed type of commodity set out in section 3 of this Schedule.

#### [Terminal Dwell Time]{.HTitleText2} {#terminal-dwell-time .SchedHeadL2}

- **25** The report must set out the average terminal dwell time in
  hours for the reporting week for the terminal areas served by each of
  the top 10 railway facilities of the rail carrier, for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of carload traffic, loaded cars, broken
    down by each type of commodity set out in section 2 of this
    Schedule;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of intermodal traffic, loaded
    intermodal containers; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of carload traffic and intermodal
    traffic, empty cars and intermodal containers, broken down by each
    equipment type set out in section 4 of this Schedule.

- **26** The report must set out the average terminal dwell time in
  hours for the reporting week for all the terminal areas served by each
  of the top 10 railway facilities of the rail carrier combined,
  calculated at the car level, for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of carload traffic, loaded cars, broken
    down by each type of commodity set out in section 2 of this
    Schedule;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of intermodal traffic, loaded
    intermodal containers; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of carload traffic and intermodal
    traffic, empty cars and intermodal containers, broken down by each
    equipment type set out in section 4 of this Schedule.

- **27** Cars on through trains are excluded from the calculations
  referred to in sections 25 and 26 of this Schedule.

- **28** Intermodal containers that have remained at a railway facility
  for more than 30 days are excluded from the calculations in sections
  25 and 26 of this Schedule.

- **29** For carload traffic, the following rules apply to the
  calculations in sections 25 and 26 of this Schedule:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the measurement of the terminal dwell time begins
    with the first of the following events to occur within a terminal
    area:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the release of a car by a customer,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the arrival of a car at a railway facility,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the receipt of a car at an interchange;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the measurement of the terminal dwell time ends
    with the first of the following events to occur within a terminal
    area:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the placement of a car at destination,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the placement of a car for transfer at an
      interchange,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the last recorded movement event of a car
      within the terminal area.

- **30** For intermodal traffic, the following rules apply to the
  calculations in sections 25 and 26 of this Schedule:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the measurement of the terminal dwell time begins
    with the first of the following events to occur within a terminal
    area:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the release of an intermodal container by a
      customer,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the arrival of an intermodal container at a
      railway facility,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the receipt of an intermodal container at an
      interchange,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the placement of an intermodal container onto a
      car;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the measurement of terminal dwell time ends with
    any of the following events occurs within a terminal area:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the placement of an intermodal container at
      destination,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the placement of an intermodal container for
      transfer at an interchange,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the departure of an intermodal container from a
      railway facility by a motor carrier,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} for a container that leaves the terminal area by
      rail under the control of the rail carrier, the last recorded
      movement event of the container in the terminal area.

- - **31** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The top 10 railway facilities of a rail
    carrier are to be determined according to throughput, calculated as
    the average annual number of loaded and empty cars, excluding
    through trains, processed during the previous three calendar years.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} When accounting for intermodal traffic in the
    calculation of throughput, intermodal railcars are to be counted,
    but not intermodal containers, and articulated cars and multi-packs
    are to be counted as one car regardless of the number of platforms.

- **32** If one of a rail carrier's top 10 railway facilities is part of
  the terminal area of another of the rail carrier's top 10 railway
  facilities, only the railway facility with the greatest volume
  throughput is to have a terminal area designated for reporting
  terminal dwell time, in which case the facility with the smaller
  throughput is to be replaced by the next largest railway facility in
  terms of throughput that does not already appear in the top 10 railway
  facilities.

- **33** A rail carrier must update the list of its top 10 railway
  facilities three years after the day on which this Schedule comes into
  force and every three years after that using the calculation set out
  in section 31 of this Schedule.

- - **34** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The first report must be accompanied by the
    following information for each terminal area:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and station code (the SPLC or, if not
      available, the FSAC) of the top 10 railway facility that serves
      the terminal area;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the names and station codes (the SPLC or, if not
      available, the FSAC) for any railway facilities served by the top
      10 railway facility;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the names and station codes (the SPLC or, if not
      available, the FSAC) of any station served by a railway facility
      referred to in paragraphs (a) or (b).

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in subsection (1) must
    be updated following any change to the list of top 10 railway
    facilities and submitted with the next report.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The information in subsection (1) with respect to a
    terminal area must be updated each time a change in the rail
    carrier's operations results in a change to the list of facilities
    and stations served by the facility, and submitted with the next
    report.

- **35** The report must include the following information for each
  terminal area and for the top 10 terminal areas combined:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the terminal dwell time reported in accordance
    with paragraphs 25(a) and 26(a) of this Schedule, the total number
    of loaded cars, not broken down by type of commodity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the terminal dwell time reported in accordance
    with paragraphs 25(b) and 26(b) of this Schedule, the total number
    of loaded intermodal containers;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the terminal dwell time reported in accordance
    with paragraphs 25(c) and 26(c) of this Schedule, the total number
    of empty cars and intermodal containers, broken down by each
    equipment type set out in section 4 of this Schedule.

- **36** Despite sections 25, 26 and 35 of this Schedule, the report for
  the BNSF Railway Company must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the average terminal dwell time in hours for the
    reporting week for the terminal areas served by each of the top 10
    railway facilities of the rail carrier, for

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all loaded cars and intermodal containers
      combined, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all empty cars and intermodal containers
      combined;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the average terminal dwell time in hours for the
    reporting week for all the terminal areas served by each of the top
    10 railway facilities of the rail carrier combined, calculated at
    the car level, for

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all loaded cars and intermodal containers
      combined, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all empty cars and intermodal containers
      combined; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the combined total number of cars and intermodal
    containers included in the calculation of each of the average
    terminal dwell times reported under paragraphs (a) and (b).

#### [Segment Transit Time]{.HTitleText2} {#segment-transit-time .SchedHeadL2}

- - **37** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The report must set out the average segment
    transit time in hours for cars, in the case of carload traffic, and
    for intermodal containers, in the case of intermodal traffic, that
    fully transit a segment that connects any of the rail carrier's top
    10 railway facilities with the next of the rail carrier's top 10
    railway facilities on the car or container's route.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} If any of the top 10 railway facilities of a rail
    carrier is within 40 km of another of the carrier's top 10 railway
    facilities, the facility with the smaller throughput is to be
    replaced by the next largest railway facility in terms of throughput
    that does not appear in the carrier's list of top 10 railway
    facilities, for the purposes of calculating the segment transit time
    indicator only.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The report must also set out the average segment
    transit time for cars and intermodal containers that fully transit a
    segment that connects any of the rail carrier's top 10 railway
    facilities with the largest railway facility in a province in which
    none of the top 10 railway facilities is located.

- **38** The measurement of segment transit time begins with the last
  event related to the car's or intermodal container's passing by or
  departing from the railway facility that begins the segment and ends
  with the first event related to the passing or arrival of the car or
  intermodal container at the railway facility that completes the
  segment.

- - **39** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Segment transit time is to be reported for

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} carload traffic, broken down by loaded and empty
      cars, and further broken down by direction of travel; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} intermodal traffic, broken down by loaded and
      empty containers, and further broken down by direction of travel.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} For each segment transit time reported, the report
    must set out the number of cars and of intermodal containers
    included in each calculation.

- **40** The segments reported under segment transit time must be
  updated each time a rail carrier changes its list of top 10 railway
  facilities.

- - **41** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The first report must be accompanied by a
    list of the length in kilometres of each segment.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The list of segment lengths must be updated
    following any change and submitted with the next report.

#### [Velocity]{.HTitleText2} {#velocity .SchedHeadL2}

- - **42** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The report must set out the average velocity
    --- daily average car kilometres --- for each equipment type set out
    in section 4 of this Schedule, for all cars and intermodal
    containers, whether or not they have moved.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The average velocity is calculated by dividing the
    total daily car-kilometres by the sum of daily cars online for the
    reporting week.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The report must set out the daily average number of
    cars and intermodal containers included in the calculation of each
    velocity reported.

#### [Locomotive Fleet Status]{.HTitleText2} {#locomotive-fleet-status .SchedHeadL2}

- - **43** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The report must set out for each geographic
    region set out in section 5 of this Schedule and on a Canada-wide
    basis the following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the daily average number of locomotives that are
      online;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the daily average number of locomotives that are
      in storage;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the daily average number of locomotives that are
      in bad order; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total of paragraphs (a) to (c).

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in subsection (1)
    includes the locomotives, whether owned or leased, within the
    network of the rail carrier and the foreign locomotives that are
    operated by the rail carrier on its network but does not include
    locomotives in passenger, work and company service.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The calculation of the daily averages referred to
    in subsection (1) is to be based on a daily snapshot.

#### [Number of Employees]{.HTitleText2} {#number-of-employees .SchedHeadL2}

- - **44** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The report must set out, for each geographic
    region set out in section 5 of this Schedule and on a Canada-wide
    basis, the number of railway operating employees, other than
    trainees, who are employed by the rail carrier, who were available
    to move traffic during the reporting week and whose primary
    occupation is one of the following positions:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} locomotive engineer;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} conductor.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The number of employees is to be based on a weekly
    snapshot taken on the same day of each reporting week, with the
    first report setting the day on which the snapshot is taken.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a carrier uses human resources systems that are
    not updated every week, the weekly snapshot for a reporting week may
    be used for any following reporting week for which the car1rier is
    unable to produce updated information.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} When determining the number of employees, employees
    who are available to move traffic in more than one of the geographic
    regions set out in section 5 of this Schedule are to be included in
    the count for only one of those geographic regions.

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} An employee may be considered to be available if
    they are on short-term leave, such as sick leave, personal leave,
    vacation leave or an elective or mandatory rest day, at the time of
    the snapshot.

  - [(6)]{.lawlabel} An employee is not considered to be available if
    they are suspended or on long-term leave, such as disability leave,
    parental leave or leave without pay, at the time of the snapshot.
