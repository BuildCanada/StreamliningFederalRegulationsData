:::

[BANK ACT](/eng/acts/B-1.01)

Registration 1992-05-21

Regulations Specifying the Circumstances Under Which a Natural Person is
Affiliated With a Bank

P.C. 1992-1100 1992-05-21
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 162 of the [Bank
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Regulations specifying the circumstances
under which a natural person is affiliated with a
bank](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-325), effective June 1, 1992.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944368 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Affiliated Persons (Banks)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-325).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944370 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01);
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[indebtedness]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[indebtedness]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes indebtedness in
    respect of commercial paper, acceptances, lines of credit to the
    extent drawn on and margin loans made to a director or officer of a
    bank; ([dette]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[not in good standing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[not in good standing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a loan,
    means a loan in respect of which

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any payment of principal or interest is 90 days
      or more overdue,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} interest is not being accrued on the books of the
      lender because it is doubtful whether the principal or interest
      will be paid or recovered, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the rate of interest is reduced by the lender
      because the borrower is financially weak; ([en
      souffrance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[significant borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[significant borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a bank,
    means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who has indebtedness for money
      borrowed from the bank or from an affiliate of the bank, other
      than a loan secured by a mortgage on the principal residence of
      that person, the total principal of which exceeds the greater of

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} \$200,000, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} one fiftieth of one per cent of the regulatory
        capital of the bank, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity that has indebtedness for money
      borrowed from the bank or from an affiliate of the bank the total
      principal of which exceeds the greatest of

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} \$500,000,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} one twentieth of one per cent of the
        regulatory capital of the bank, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} twenty-five per cent of the value of the
        assets of the entity. ([emprunteur important]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

## [Circumstances Under Which a Natural Person Is Affiliated]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944386 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
162 of the Act, a natural person is affiliated with a bank where the
person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is an officer or employee of the bank or of an
  affiliate of the bank;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a significant interest in a class of shares or in
  membership shares of the bank;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} has a substantial investment in an affiliate of the
  bank;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} is a significant borrower in respect of the bank;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} is an officer or employee of an entity that is a
  significant borrower in respect of the bank;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} controls one or more entities of which the total
  indebtedness to the bank or to an affiliate of the bank would cause
  those entities, if treated as a single entity, to be a significant
  borrower of the bank;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} provides goods or services to the bank, or is a
  partner or an employee in a partnership that provides goods or
  services to the bank or an officer or employee of, or a person who has
  a substantial investment in, a body corporate that provides goods or
  services to the bank, if the total annual billings to the bank in
  respect of the goods and services provided exceeds ten per cent of the
  total annual billings of the person, partnership or body corporate, as
  the case may be;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} has a loan that is not in good standing from the bank
  or from an affiliate of the bank or is a director, an officer or an
  employee of, or a person who controls, an entity that has a loan that
  is not in good standing from the bank or from an affiliate of the
  bank; or

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} is the spouse or common-law partner of a person
  described in any of paragraphs (a) to (h).
