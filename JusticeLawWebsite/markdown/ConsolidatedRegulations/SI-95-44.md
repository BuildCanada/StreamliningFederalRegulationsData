## Order Transferring from the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food to the Minister of National Health and Welfare, the Powers, Duties and Functions under Certain Acts {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-agriculture-and-agri-food-to-the-minister-of-national-health-and-welfare-the-powers-duties-and-functions-under-certain-acts .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/95-44
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1995-04-19

Order Transferring from the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food to the
Minister of National Health and Welfare, the Powers, Duties and
Functions under Certain Acts

P.C. 1995-525  1995-03-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), is pleased
hereby to transfer to the Minister of National Health and Welfare the
powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Agriculture and
Agri-Food under the [Pest Control Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01) and
the [Pesticide Residue Compensation Act](/eng/acts/P-10), effective
April 1, 1995.
