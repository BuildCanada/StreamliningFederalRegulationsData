## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-657798 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657799 .Subheading}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Nuclear Safety and Control
    Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bulk quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bulk quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of the terms
    [[exemption quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[unconditional
    clearance level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when referred to in section 5, a quantity of
      material greater than one tonne; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when referred to in section 5.1, a quantity of
      material greater than one tonne per year per nuclear
      facility. ([quantité en vrac]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a document issued by the
    Commission or by a designated officer authorized under paragraph
    37(2)(a) or (b) of the Act, indicating that prescribed equipment or
    a person is certified. ([attestation ou
    homologation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[certified]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certified]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means certified by the Commission
    under paragraph 21(1)(h) or (i) of the Act or by a designated
    officer authorized under paragraph 37(2)(a) or (b) of the
    Act. ([homologué]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Class II prescribed equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class II prescribed equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the
    meaning assigned to that term by section 1 of the [Class II Nuclear
    Facilities and Prescribed Equipment
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-205). ([équipement réglementé
    de catégorie II]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[conditional clearance level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[conditional clearance level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    activity concentration that does not result in an effective dose

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} greater than 1 mSv in a year due to a low
      probability event referred to in the IAEA Safety Standard
      RS-G-1.7; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} greater than 10 µSv in a year. ([niveau de
      libération conditionnelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[depleted uranium]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[depleted uranium]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means uranium that contains
    uranium 235 in a concentration that is less than that normally found
    in nature. ([uranium appauvri]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dosimeter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dosimeter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device for measuring a
    dose of radiation that is worn or carried by an
    individual. ([dosimètre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[effective dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[effective dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned to
    that term by subsection 1(1) of the [Radiation Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-203). ([dose
    efficace]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[exemption quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exemption quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the
    following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a radioactive nuclear substance set
      out in column 1 of Schedule 1,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the radioactive nuclear substance is
        uniformly distributed in material and not in bulk quantity, the
        corresponding activity concentration set out in column 2, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the corresponding activity set out in column
        3;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a radioactive nuclear substance
      that is not set out in column 1 of Schedule 1,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the atomic number of the substance is equal
        to or less than 81,

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} 10 Bq/g if the radioactive nuclear substance
          is uniformly distributed in material and not in bulk quantity,
          or

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} 10,000 Bq,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the atomic number of the substance is
        greater than 81 and the substance, or its short-lived
        radioactive progeny, does not emit alpha radiation,

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} 10 Bq/g if the radioactive nuclear substance
          is uniformly distributed in material and not in bulk quantity,
          or

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} 10,000 Bq, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the atomic number of the substance is
        greater than 81 and the substance, or its short-lived
        radioactive progeny, emits alpha radiation,

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} 1 Bq/g if the radioactive nuclear substance
          is uniformly distributed in material and not in bulk quantity,
          or

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} 1,000 Bq; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of more than one radioactive nuclear
      substance,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the radioactive nuclear substances are
        uniformly distributed in material and not in bulk quantity, the
        quotient obtained by dividing the total activity concentration
        by the sum of quotients obtained by dividing the activity
        concentration of each radioactive nuclear substance by its
        corresponding exemption quantity as referred to in paragraph (a)
        or (b), or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quotient obtained by dividing the total
        activity by the corresponding sum of quotients obtained by
        dividing the activity of each radioactive nuclear substance by
        its corresponding exemption quantity as referred to in
        paragraph (a) or (b). ([quantité d'exemption]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

[[exposure device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exposure device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a radiation device
    that is designed for carrying out gamma radiography, and includes
    any accessory to the device such as a sealed source assembly, a
    drive mechanism, a sealed source assembly guide tube and an exposure
    head. ([appareil d'exposition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[IAEA Safety Standard RS-G-1.7]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[IAEA Safety Standard RS-G-1.7]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the IAEA
    Safety Standards, Safety Guide No. RS-G-1.7 --- Application of the
    Concepts of Exclusion, Exemption and Clearance, 2004 Edition
    published by the International Atomic Energy Agency. ([Norme de
    sûreté RS-G-1.7 de l'AIEA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensed activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensed activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an activity
    described in any of paragraphs 26(a) to (c) of the Act that a
    licence authorizes the licensee to carry on in relation to a nuclear
    substance or a radiation device. ([activité
    autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is licensed to
    carry on an activity described in any of paragraphs 26(a) to (c) of
    the Act in relation to a nuclear substance or a radiation
    device. ([titulaire de permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[natural uranium]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[natural uranium]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means uranium that contains
    uranium 235 in a concentration that is normally found in
    nature. ([uranium naturel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nuclear criticality]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear criticality]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a self-sustaining
    chain reaction of nuclear fission. ([criticité
    nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[operate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes, in respect of an exposure
    device, coupling the drive mechanism to the exposure device,
    uncoupling the drive mechanism from the exposure device, locking or
    unlocking the exposure device, and all activities involving the
    device that take place while the sealed source assembly is not
    locked inside the device in the fully shielded position. ([faire
    fonctionner]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the equipment
    prescribed by section 20 of the [General Nuclear Safety and Control
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202). ([équipement
    réglementé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[radiation device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radiation device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a device that contains more than the exemption
      quantity of a nuclear substance and that enables the nuclear
      substance to be used for its radiation properties; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a device that contains a radium luminous
      compound. ([appareil à rayonnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[radiation survey meter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radiation survey meter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an instrument
    that is capable of measuring radiation dose
    rates. ([radiamètre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sealed source]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sealed source]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a radioactive nuclear
    substance in a sealed capsule or in a cover to which the substance
    is bonded, where the capsule or cover is strong enough to prevent
    contact with or the dispersion of the substance under the conditions
    for which the capsule or cover is designed. ([source
    scellée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sealed source assembly]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sealed source assembly]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a sealed source
    that is designed to be used in an exposure device, and includes the
    components that are permanently attached to the sealed
    source. ([assemblage de source scellée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[servicing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[servicing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in respect of radiation devices,
    means any maintenance of a device, including installation, repair,
    or dismantling, other than maintenance that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} constitutes routine operating procedures as
      indicated in the manufacturer's operating manual for the device;
      or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is authorized in the licence issued in respect of
      the possession or use of the device. ([entretien]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[specific activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[specific activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the activity per
    unit mass. ([activité spécifique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unconditional clearance level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unconditional clearance level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of
    a bulk quantity of material, except for surface contaminated
    material, in which the radioactive nuclear substance is uniformly
    distributed, means an activity concentration that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a radioactive nuclear substance set
      out in column 1 of Schedule 2, is the corresponding activity
      concentration set out in column 2;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a radioactive nuclear substance
      that is not set out in column 1,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is 1 Bq/g, if the atomic number of the
        substance is equal to or less than 81,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is 1 Bq/g, if the atomic number of the
        substance is greater than 81 and the substance, or its
        short-lived radioactive progeny, does not emit alpha radiation,
        and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is 0.1 Bq/g, if the atomic number of the
        substance is greater than 81 and the substance, or its
        short-lived radioactive progeny, emits alpha radiation; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of more than one radioactive nuclear
      substance --- except for Thorium 232, Uranium 235, Uranium 238 and
      their radioactive progeny mentioned in paragraph 4.3 of the IAEA
      Safety Standard RS-G-1.7 --- is the quotient obtained by dividing
      the total activity concentration by the sum of quotients obtained
      by dividing the activity concentration of each radioactive nuclear
      substance by its corresponding activity concentration as referred
      to in paragraph (a) or (b). ([niveau de libération
      inconditionnelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unsealed source]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unsealed source]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a source other than a
    sealed source. ([source non scellée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who performs work that
    is referred to in a licence. ([travailleur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657860 .Subheading}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to all nuclear substances and sealed sources, as
  well as to all radiation devices that are not included in Class II
  prescribed equipment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of the
  packaging or transport of nuclear substances, sealed sources or
  radiation devices.

- SOR/2008-119, s. 18

## [Licence Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-657866 .Part}

### [General Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657867 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for a licence in respect of a nuclear substance or a
  radiation device, other than a licence to service a radiation device,
  shall contain the following information in addition to the information
  required by section 3 of the [General Nuclear Safety and Control
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the methods, procedures and equipment that will be
    used to carry on the activity to be licensed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the methods, procedures and equipment that will be
    used while carrying on the activity to be licensed, or during and
    following an accident, to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} monitor the release of any radioactive nuclear
      substance from the site of the activity to be licensed,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} detect the presence of and record the radiation
      dose rate and quantity in becquerels of radioactive nuclear
      substances at the site of the activity to be licensed,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} limit the spread of radioactive contamination
      within and from the site of the activity to be licensed, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} decontaminate any person, site or equipment
      contaminated as a result of the activity to be licensed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the circumstances in which the
    decontamination referred to in subparagraph (b)(iv) will be carried
    out;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed location of the activity to be
    licensed, including a description of the site;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the roles, responsibilities, duties, qualifications
    and experience of workers;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the proposed training program for workers;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the proposed instructions for dealing with
    accidents, including fires and spills, in which the nuclear
    substance may be involved;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the proposed inspection program for the equipment
    and systems that will be used to carry on the activity to be
    licensed;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the methods, procedures and equipment that will be
    used to calibrate radiation survey meters in accordance with these
    Regulations;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the methods, procedures and equipment that will be
    used to calibrate and verify the calibration of dosimeters referred
    to in paragraphs 30(3)(d) and (e);

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the methods, procedures and equipment that will be
    used to conduct the leak tests and surveys required by these
    Regulations;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} where the application is in respect of a nuclear
    substance that is an unsealed source and that is to be used in a
    room, the proposed design of the room;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} if the application is in respect of a nuclear
    substance that is contained in a radiation device, the brand name
    and model number of the radiation device, and the quantity of the
    devices;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} where the application is in respect of Category I,
    II or III nuclear material, as defined in section 1 of the [Nuclear
    Security Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-209),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the measures that will be taken to prevent
      nuclear criticality, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the information required by section 3 or 4 of
      the [Nuclear Security Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-209),
      as applicable;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant will be manufacturing or
    distributing radiation devices referred to in paragraph 5(1)(c) or
    section 6 or 7, or check sources mentioned in section 8.1, the
    proposed procedure for the disposal of each radiation device or
    check source or for its return to the manufacturer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of an
  application for a licence to import or export for which the
  information requirements are prescribed by the Nuclear
  Non-proliferation Import and Export Control Regulations.

- SOR/2008-119, s. 19

### [Licence to Service Radiation Device]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657894 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence to service a radiation device shall contain the following
information in addition to the information required by section 3 of the
[General Nuclear Safety and Control
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the brand name and model number of the device or the
  number of the certificate relating to the device;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the nature of the servicing proposed
  to be carried on;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proposed methods, procedures and equipment for
  carrying on the servicing;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed qualification requirements and training
  program for workers; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the proposed procedures to be followed after
  completion of the servicing to confirm that the device is safe to use.

## [Exemptions from Licence Requirement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-657903 .Part}

### [General Exempted Activities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657904 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  may carry on any of the following activities without a licence:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} possess, transfer, import, export, use, mine,
    produce, refine, convert, enrich, process, reprocess, manage or
    store a radioactive nuclear substance if the activity or the
    activity concentration of the substance does not, at any one time,
    exceed

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its exemption quantity,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} its conditional clearance level, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} its unconditional clearance level;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} possess, transfer, import, export, store, use,
    abandon, produce or service a sealed source that contains less than
    the exemption quantity of a radioactive nuclear substance;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} possess, transfer, import, export, store, use or
    abandon a radiation device, other than an exposure device, if the
    quantity of the nuclear substance or substances contained in the
    device is less than 10 times the exemption quantity;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} possess, transfer, store, use, abandon, produce,
    refine, convert, enrich, process, reprocess, manage or dispose of
    deuterium or a compound containing deuterium, if the quantity of
    deuterium is less than 10 kg in any calendar year;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} possess, transfer, store, use or manage depleted
    uranium, in any quantity, that is used as counterweights in aircraft
    if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} each counterweight manufactured after the coming
      into force of this subparagraph is durably and legibly impressed
      with the words "DEPLETED URANIUM APPAUVRI" and the words are
      visible through any plating or other covering,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} each counterweight manufactured after the coming
      into force of this subparagraph is durably and legibly labelled or
      impressed with the name of the manufacturer and its unique
      identification number and the statement "UNAUTHORIZED ALTERATIONS
      PROHIBITED / MODIFICATIONS INTERDITES SANS AUTORISATION",

    - [(ii.1)]{.lawlabel} no alteration is made to the counterweights
      except in accordance with the requirements set out in subsection
      571.02(1) of the [Canadian Aviation
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433), and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} no chemical, physical or metallurgical
      treatment or processing of the counterweights is done other than
      for the repair or restoration of any plating or other covering; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} possess, transfer, use or abandon material that
    contains not more than 10 kg of depleted uranium, natural uranium or
    natural thorium in any calendar year and that is not used for its
    radiation properties.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of Category
  I, II or III nuclear material, as defined in section 1 of the [Nuclear
  Security Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-209).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Paragraphs (1)(a) to (c) do not apply in respect of
  the import or export of a nuclear substance, sealed source or
  radiation device to which the Nuclear Non-proliferation Import and
  Export Control Regulations apply.

### [Abandonment or Disposal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657925 .Subheading}

- **[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may, without a licence, abandon or dispose of a radioactive
  nuclear substance if the activity or the activity concentration of the
  substance does not exceed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its exemption quantity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its conditional clearance level; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its unconditional clearance level.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Category I nuclear material, Category II nuclear
    material or Category III nuclear material, as those terms are
    defined in section 1 of the [Nuclear Security
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-209); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} discharges of effluents from

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Class I nuclear facilities, as defined in section
      1 of the [Class I Nuclear Facilities
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-204), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} mines or mills, as those terms are defined in
      section 1 of the [Uranium Mines and Mills
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-206).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-119, s. 22

### [Smoke Detectors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657938 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A person may, without a
licence to carry on that activity, possess, transfer, use or abandon a
smoke detector that contains a nuclear substance, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the smoke detector does not contain more than 185 kBq
  of americium 241 or, where it is in a commercial or industrial
  facility, more than 740 kBq of americium 241;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the radiation dose rate does not exceed 1 µSv per
  hour at 0.1 m from any of the accessible surfaces of the smoke
  detector;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the design and construction of the smoke detector
  prevent persons from making direct contact with the nuclear substance
  that it contains under normal conditions of use;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} all markings and labels on the smoke detector are
  legible;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the radioactive nuclear substance contained in the
  smoke detector is a sealed source that, when it is mounted in its
  holder, conforms to International Standard 2919, Radiation Protection
  --- Sealed radioactive sources --- General requirements and
  classification (1999), of the International Organization for
  Standardization; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the smoke detector meets the tests specified in the
  annex entitled Prototype Tests of the Recommendations for ionization
  chamber smoke detectors in implementation of radiation protection
  standards (1977) of the Nuclear Energy Agency of the Organisation for
  Economic Co-operation and Development.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-119, s. 23

  quantity in becquerels of the nuclear substance, the manufacturer's
  recommended expiry date for the sign and the date of manufacture of

## [General Obligations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-658023 .Part}

### [Medical Supervision]{.HTitleText2} {#h-658024 .Subheading}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** No licensee shall use a
radioactive nuclear substance or a radiation device on a person except
as directed by a medical practitioner who is qualified to give such
direction under the applicable provincial legislation.

### [Radiation Safety Instructions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-658026 .Subheading}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Every licensee shall make
available to all workers, at the site of the licensed activity, copies
of any instructions referred to in the licence concerning radiation
safety and accidents, including fires and spills, in which a nuclear
substance may be involved.

### [Leak Tests]{.HTitleText2} {#h-658028 .Subheading}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee who possesses, uses or produces either a sealed source
  containing 50 MBq or more of a nuclear substance or a nuclear
  substance as shielding shall, at the following times, conduct leak
  tests on the sealed source or shielding using instruments and
  procedures that enable the licensee to detect a leakage of 200 Bq or
  less of the nuclear substance:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the sealed source or shielding is used after
    being stored for 12 or more consecutive months, immediately before
    using it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the sealed source or shielding is being
    stored, every 24 months;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where an event that may have damaged the sealed
    source or shielding has occurred, immediately after the event; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the sealed source or shielding is located
      in a radiation device, every 12 months, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the sealed source or shielding is not
      located in a radiation device, every six months.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a sealed
  source that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} gaseous;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contained in a static eliminator that has been
    retained by the licensee for less than 15 months;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} exempted under section 5, 6, 8 or 8.1; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} used or stored underwater in a nuclear facility
    that is equipped with a device capable of detecting water-borne
    contamination of 200 Bq or less of a nuclear substance.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a licensee, in the course of conducting a leak
  test on a sealed source or on shielding, detects the leakage of 200 Bq
  or more of a nuclear substance, the licensee shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} discontinue using the sealed source or shielding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} discontinue using the radiation device in which the
    sealed source or shielding is located or may have been located;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} take measures to limit the spread of radioactive
    contamination from the sealed source or shielding; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} immediately after complying with paragraphs (a) to
    (c), notify the Commission that the leakage has been detected.

### [Obligations of Licensees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-658092 .Subheading}

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee who possesses, uses or produces an exposure device shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure that there is affixed securely to the

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} lock the exposure device and keep it locked when it
    is not being used; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} return a dosimeter referred to in paragraph (3)(c)

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee who becomes aware of any of the
  following situations shall notify the Commission immediately of the
  location and circumstances of the situation and of any action that the
  licensee has taken or proposes to take with respect to it:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the exposure device or the sealed source assembly
    is lost, stolen or damaged to an extent that could impair its normal
    use;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exposure device has a radiation dose rate of
    more than 2 mSv per hour on any part of its surface when the sealed
    source assembly is in the shielded position;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the sealed source assembly is separated from the
    exposure device when the latter is not being serviced; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the sealed source assembly fails to return to the
    shielded position inside the exposure device.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee who authorizes a person to operate an
  exposure device shall provide the person with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a radiation survey meter that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is capable of measuring a dose rate of gamma

      hour, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} indicates that the power level of its batteries
      is sufficient for its operation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if an external sealed source assembly guide tube is
    to be used,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} material that can be used to attenuate, by a
      factor of at least 100, the radiation from the sealed source,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} tools that can sever from the exposure device
      the tube and cable to which the sealed source assembly is
      attached, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} tongs with a shaft at least 1.5 m long that can
      handle the sealed source assembly;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a dosimeter that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has been issued by a licensed dosimetry service,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has not been used by another person since its
      last reading, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is designed so that it can be worn on the trunk
      of the body;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a dosimeter that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has direct-reading display capability,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is of a type suitable for recording any dose of
      radiation that the person is likely to receive as a result of the
      operation of the exposure device,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has been calibrated or had its calibration
      verified, within the 12-month period prior to being provided, to
      an accuracy within 20 per cent of the true dose of radiation, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} is designed so that it can be worn on the trunk
      of the body;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a dosimeter that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is of a type suitable for recording any dose of
      radiation that the person is likely to receive as a result of the
      operation of the exposure device,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} emits an audible warning signal when the
      radiation dose rate reaches or exceeds 5 mSv per hour or when the
      total dose of radiation reaches or exceeds 2 mSv, or emits an
      audible warning signal that increases proportionally to the
      radiation dose rate,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is designed to prevent an unintentional change
      in the radiation dose rate or total dose of radiation at which the
      dosimeter will emit an audible warning signal,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} has been calibrated or had its calibration
      verified, within the 12-month period prior to being provided, to
      an accuracy within 20 per cent of the true dose of radiation, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} is designed so that it can be worn on the trunk
      of the body;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a sufficient number of durable and legible signs
    that bear the radiation warning symbol set out in Schedule 3 to the
    [Radiation Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-203)
    and the words "RAYONNEMENT --- DANGER --- RADIATION" to enable the
    person to comply with paragraph 31(1)(k); and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a sufficient number of forms to enable the person
    to keep the records referred to in paragraph 31(1)(e) and section
    37.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No licensee shall authorize a person to operate an
  exposure device that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} does not appear to be functioning normally; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a radiation dose rate of more than 2 mSv per
    hour on any part of its surface.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee who authorizes a person to remove a
  sealed source from or insert a sealed source into an exposure device
  shall provide the person with a written authorization signed by the
  licensee.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall limit the dose of radiation
  received by a person, other than a nuclear energy worker, as a result

  training in the safety, regulatory and technical requirements for

  person who has received the specialized training:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the exposure device or the sealed source assembly
    is damaged to an extent that could impair its normal use;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exposure device has a radiation dose rate of
    more than 2 mSv per hour on any part of its surface when the sealed
    source assembly is in the shielded position;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the sealed source assembly is separated from the
    exposure device when the latter is not being serviced; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the sealed source assembly fails to return to the
    shielded position inside the exposure device.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when an external sealed source assembly guide tube
    is being used, have immediately available the following items:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} material that can be used to attenuate, by a
      factor of at least 100, the radiation from the sealed source,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} tools that can sever from the exposure device
      the tube and cable to which the sealed source assembly is
      attached, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} tongs with a shaft at least 1.5 m long that can
      handle the sealed source assembly;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} wear on the trunk of the body a dosimeter that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has been issued by a licensed dosimetry service,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has not been used by another person since its
      last reading;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} wear on the trunk of the body a dosimeter that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has direct-reading display capability,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is of a type suitable for recording any dose of
      radiation that the person is likely to receive as a result of the
      operation of the exposure device, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has been calibrated or had its calibration
      verified within the 12-month period prior to wearing, to an
      accuracy within 20 per cent of the true dose of radiation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} keep a record of the dose of radiation received by
    the person for each day the person operates the exposure device, as
    indicated by the dosimeter referred to in paragraph (d);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} wear on the trunk of the body a dosimeter that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is of a type suitable for recording any dose of
      radiation that the person is likely to receive as a result of the
      operation of the exposure device,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} emits an audible warning signal when the
      radiation dose rate reaches or exceeds 5 mSv per hour or when the
      total dose of radiation reaches or exceeds 2 mSv, or emits an
      audible warning signal that increases proportionally to the
      radiation dose rate,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is designed to prevent an unintentional change
      in the radiation dose rate or total dose of radiation at which the
      dosimeter will emit an audible warning signal, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} has been calibrated or had its calibration
      verified within the 12-month period prior to wearing, to an
      accuracy within 20 per cent of the true dose of radiation;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} examine the sealed source assembly coupling and
    guide tube, the locking mechanism, the cranking device, the drive
    cable and the pneumatic pump of the exposure device immediately
    before operating the device to ensure that the device is functioning
    within the manufacturer's specifications;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} after each attempt to move the sealed source
    assembly to the shielded position inside the exposure device, use a
    radiation survey meter to determine that the sealed source assembly
    is in the shielded position;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} limit the dose of radiation received by any person,
    other than a nuclear energy worker, as a result of the possession or
    use of the exposure device to 0.1 mSv per week and 0.5 mSv per year;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} place persons or erect barriers to prevent entry
    into any area within which the radiation dose rate is greater than
    0.1 mSv per hour as a result of the possession or use of the
    exposure device;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} post a sufficient number of durable and legible
    signs that bear the radiation warning symbol set out in Schedule 3
    to the [Radiation Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-203) and the words
    "RAYONNEMENT --- DANGER --- RADIATION", to prevent entry into any
    area within which the radiation dose rate is greater than 0.1 mSv
    per hour as a result of the possession or use of the exposure
    device;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} lock the exposure device when it is not being
    operated; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} where the person becomes aware of any of the
    following situations, immediately report to the licensee the
    location and circumstances of the situation and any action that the
    person has taken or proposes to take with respect to it:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the exposure device or the sealed source assembly
      is lost, stolen or damaged to an extent that could impair its
      normal use,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the exposure device has a radiation dose rate of
      more than 2 mSv per hour on any part of its surface when the
      sealed source assembly is in the shielded position,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the sealed source assembly is separated from
      the exposure device when the latter is not being serviced, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a failure to return the sealed source assembly
      to the shielded position inside the exposure device.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who has been provided with a dosimeter
  referred to in paragraph 30(3)(c) by a licensee shall return the
  dosimeter to the licensee at the end of the 15-day period beginning on
  the first day that the person wore the dosimeter.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who keeps a record referred to in
  paragraph (1)(e) shall submit the record to the licensee at the end of
  each 15-day period, the first of which begins on the first day that
  the person operated the exposure device.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall operate an exposure device that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} does not appear to be functioning normally; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a radiation dose rate of more than 2 mSv per
    hour on any part of its surface.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every person operating an exposure device shall,
  during each work shift, ensure that the dose of radiation accumulated
  does not exceed 2 mSv, and if the dose exceeds 2 mSv, the person shall
  stop work immediately and notify the licensee at the earliest
  opportunity.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} No person shall respond to any of the following
  situations unless the person has received specialized training in the
  safety, regulatory and technical requirements for dealing with those
  situations or has received training in the safety, regulatory and
  technical requirements for dealing with those situations and is acting
  under the guidance of a person who has received the specialized
  training:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the exposure device or the sealed source assembly
    is damaged to an extent that could impair its normal use;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exposure device has a radiation dose rate of
    more than 2 mSv per hour on any part of its surface when the sealed
    source assembly is in the shielded position;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the sealed source assembly is separated from the
    exposure device when the latter is not being serviced; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the sealed source assembly fails to return to the
    shielded position inside the exposure device.

### [Appointment of Supervisors of Trainees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-658187 .Subheading}

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee may appoint a certified exposure device operator to supervise
  a trainee in the operation of an exposure device, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the certified exposure device operator has the
    qualifications, training and experience necessary to supervise a
    trainee in the safe operation of the exposure device;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licensee requests the certified exposure device
    operator, in accordance with subsection (2), to accept the
    appointment; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the certified exposure device operator accepts the
    appointment in writing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request referred to in paragraph (1)(b) shall be
  made in writing and shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state the name of the trainee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} state the brand name and model number of the
    exposure device;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} direct the attention of the certified exposure
    device operator to this section and to section 33; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} include a copy of the licence to use the exposure
    device.

### [Obligations of Supervisors of Trainees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-658200 .Subheading}

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  certified exposure device operator who is supervising a trainee in the
  operation of an exposure device shall permit the trainee to operate
  the device unless the trainee has sufficient knowledge to safely
  operate it.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every certified exposure device operator who is
  supervising a trainee in the operation of an exposure device shall
  directly supervise and continuously observe the trainee while the
  trainee is operating the device.

### [Replacement of Sealed Source]{.HTitleText2} {#h-658204 .Subheading}

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who removes a sealed source from or inserts a sealed source
  into an exposure device shall possess a written authorization to do
  so, signed by the licensee who possesses, uses, produces or services
  the exposure device.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Immediately after a person removes a sealed source
  from or inserts a sealed source into an exposure device, the person
  shall measure

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the radiation dose rate on each accessible surface
    of the exposure device; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the dose of radiation received by persons who were
    exposed to radiation during the removal or insertion, using a
    dosimeter referred to in paragraph 30(3)(d).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who removes a sealed source from or
  inserts a sealed source into an exposure device shall record the
  radiation dose rates and doses of radiation referred to in
  subsection (2) and report them to the licensee who possesses or uses
  the exposure device.

## [Tracer Studies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-658211 .Part}

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee who uses more than 2 GBq of a nuclear substance that is not a
  sealed source for the purpose of conducting a tracer or subsurface
  tracer study shall notify the Commission before conducting the study.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall, within 60 days after using a
  nuclear substance referred to in subsection (1) for the purpose of
  conducting a tracer or subsurface tracer study, file with the
  Commission a report that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date and location of the study;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, quantity in becquerels and form of the
    nuclear substance used in the study;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person for whom the study was
    conducted;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the names of all workers who handled the nuclear
    substance and the dosimeter readings and bioassay results for those
    workers;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of any unusual occurrence;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the disposition of any unused
    nuclear substance; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the specific activity of the nuclear substance upon
    entering and leaving the system studied and a description of the
    disposition of the nuclear substance.

## [Records to Be Kept and Retained]{.HTitleText1} {#h-658222 .Part}

### [Nuclear Substances]{.HTitleText2} {#h-658223 .Subheading}

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee shall keep the following records:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a record of the following information in respect of
    any nuclear substance in the licensee's possession that is referred
    to in the licence:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name, quantity, form and location of the
      nuclear substance,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the nuclear substance is a sealed source,
      the model and serial number of the source,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where the nuclear substance is contained in a
      radiation device, the model and serial number of the device,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the nuclear substance used, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which the nuclear substance was
      used;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a record of the name of each worker who uses or
    handles a nuclear substance;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a record of any transfer, receipt, disposal or
    abandonment of a nuclear substance, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date of the transfer, receipt, disposal or
      abandonment,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the supplier or the
      recipient,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the number of the licence of the recipient,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the name, quantity and form of the nuclear
      substance transferred, received, disposed of or abandoned,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} where the nuclear substance is a sealed source,
      the model and serial number of the source, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} where the nuclear substance is contained in a
      radiation device, the model and serial number of the device;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a record of the training received by each worker;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a record of every inspection, measurement, test or
    servicing performed by the licensee in accordance with the Act, the
    regulations made under the Act or the licence in respect of any
    radiation device containing a nuclear substance that the licensee is
    authorized by the licence to possess.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee who holds a licence to service
  radiation devices shall keep a record of the following information in
  respect of each servicing performed on any radiation device containing
  a nuclear substance that another licensee, by their licence, is
  authorized to possess, including:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the client for whom the
    servicing was performed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licence number of the client for whom the
    servicing was performed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the brand name, model number and serial number of
    the radiation device;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, quantity and date of measurement of the
    nuclear substance contained in the radiation device; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the work and the date on which the
    servicing was performed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall retain a record referred to in
  paragraph (1)(d) for the period ending three years after the
  termination of employment of the worker.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is required to keep a record
  referred to in paragraph (1)(e) or subsection (1.1) shall retain the
  record for the period ending three years after the earlier of the
  expiry date and the date of revocation, if any, of the licence.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall keep a record of each leak test
  conducted on a sealed source or on shielding in accordance with
  section 18 and that person shall retain the record for the period
  ending three years after the date on which it is conducted.

### [Exposure Devices]{.HTitleText2} {#h-658253 .Subheading}

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** Every licensee who
possesses an exposure device shall keep a record of the following
information in respect of the device:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the brand name, model number and serial number of the
  exposure device;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity in becquerels of any nuclear substance
  contained in the exposure device;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the dates on which and the locations where the
  exposure device is operated;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date of acquisition and, where applicable, the
  date of disposal of the exposure device and any sealed source
  assembly;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the names of all persons whom the licensee has
  authorized to possess or use the exposure device or any sealed source
  assembly;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} all written authorizations provided by the licensee
  in accordance with subsection 30(5);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} all requests made by the licensee in accordance with
  paragraph 32(1)(b) and all appointments accepted in response to those
  requests;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} every inspection, measurement, test, servicing or
  calibration performed in accordance with these Regulations; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the measurements submitted to the licensee in
  accordance with these Regulations by a person who has operated the
  exposure device.

## [Reports to Be Made by Licensees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-658266 .Part}

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee who possesses or uses a nuclear substance or a radiation
  device and becomes aware of any of the following situations shall
  notify the Commission immediately of the location and circumstances of
  the situation and of any action that the licensee has taken or
  proposes to take with respect to it:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nuclear substance or the radiation device is
    lost or stolen;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the radiation device is damaged to an extent that
    could impair its normal use;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the sealed source is separated from the radiation
    device when the latter is not being serviced;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the sealed source fails to return to the shielded
    position inside the radiation device; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} there is a spill of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an unsealed radioactive nuclear substance that is
      set out in column 1 of Schedule 1, that has produced in excess of
      100 times the activity set out in column 3, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an unsealed radioactive nuclear substance that
      is not set out in column 1.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee referred to in subsection (1) or
  subsection 30(2) who becomes aware of a situation referred to in one
  of those subsections shall file a full report of the situation with
  the Commission within 21 days after the day on which the licensee
  becomes aware of it or within the period specified in the licence, and
  the report shall contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the situation, the circumstances
    and the problem, if any, with the radiation device;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the probable cause of the situation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the nuclear substance, and if applicable, the brand
    name, model number and serial number of the radiation device
    involved;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date, time and location where the situation
    occurred or, if unknown, the approximate date, time and location,
    and the date and time of becoming aware of the situation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the actions that the licensee has taken to
    re-establish normal operations;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the actions that the licensee has taken or proposes
    to take to prevent a recurrence of the situation;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the situation involved an exposure device, the
    qualifications of the workers, including any trainee, who were
    involved;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the effective dose and equivalent dose --- as those
    terms are defined in subsection 1(1) of the [Radiation Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-203) --- received by any
    person as a result of the situation; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the effects on the environment, the health and
    safety of persons and the maintenance of security that have resulted
    or may result from the situation.
