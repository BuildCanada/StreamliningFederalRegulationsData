## Tobacco (Seizure and Restoration) Regulations {#tobacco-seizure-and-restoration-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/99-94
:::

[TOBACCO AND VAPING PRODUCTS ACT](/eng/acts/T-11.5)

TOBACCO ACT

Registration 1999-02-11

Tobacco (Seizure and Restoration) Regulations

P.C. 1999-198 1999-02-11
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 42.1 of the Tobacco Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA}, the Minister
of Health laid a copy of the proposed [Tobacco (Seizure and Restoration)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-94), in the annexed form, before
the House of Commons on June 3, 1998 and the House of Commons did not
concur in any report from a committee respecting the proposed
regulations within the following thirty sitting days;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to section 42 of the
Tobacco Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Tobacco (Seizure and Restoration)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-94).

  13

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032978 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Tobacco and Vaping Products
Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2018, c. 9, s. 77

## [Seizure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032982 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** When an inspector seizes a
tobacco product or other thing pursuant to subsection 39(1) of the Act,
the inspector shall provide its owner or the person in charge of the
place from which it was seized with a copy of these Regulations and of
section 40 of the Act.

## [Application for Restoration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032984 .Part}

### [Service of Notice]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1032985 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A notice referred to in
subsection 40(1) of the Act shall be served by registered mail on the
Minister at least 15 clear days before the day on which the application
for an order of restoration is to be made to the provincial court judge.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-104, s. 1
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

### [Contents of Notice]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1032989 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A notice referred to in
subsection 40(1) of the Act must specify

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the provincial court judge to whom the application
  for an order of restoration is to be made;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the time when and the place where the application is
  to be heard;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the tobacco product or other thing seized in respect
  of which the application is to be made; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the evidence on which the applicant intends to rely
  to establish that the applicant is entitled to possession of the
  product or other thing seized in respect of which the application is
  to be made.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032995 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
