## Cedar Point-Christian Island Ferry Fees Regulations {#cedar-point-christian-island-ferry-fees-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 681
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Regulations Respecting the Fees Payable for Ferry Service on the Ferry
Operating Between Cedar Point and Christian Island, Ontario
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560410 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Cedar Point-Christian Island Ferry Fees
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._681).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560412 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[barge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[barge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any barge owned and operated by
    Her Majesty in right of Canada in conjunction with the ferry;
    ([chaland]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[ferry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ferry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any vessel owned and operated by
    Her Majesty in right of Canada and normally operated as the ferry
    between Cedar Point and Christian Island, both in the Province of
    Ontario; ([traversier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an Indian as defined in the
    [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([Indien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Fees Payable]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560417 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every person who is carried
on the ferry or who charters the ferry or barge, or both, except

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an Indian,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who is employed by Her Majesty in right of
  Canada as a teacher and who resides on Christian Island, and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who is required to use the ferry in order to
  transact business with an Indian on Christian Island,

shall pay the fee in respect of such carriage or charter set out in the
schedule.
