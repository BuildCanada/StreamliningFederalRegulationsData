## [Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785196 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition of Board

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Rules, the members assigned by the Chairperson under subsection 93(2) of
the Act to deal with a matter constitute the Board.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785199 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Rules apply in
respect of any proceeding before the Board.

## [Quorum]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785202 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** In any proceeding, a quorum
of the Board consists of two members.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785205 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defect in form or procedure

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  proceeding or any part of a proceeding may not be defeated by reason
  only of a defect in form or procedure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unanticipated procedural matters

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any procedural matter or question that is not
  provided for in the Act, in these Rules or in any regulations made
  pursuant to the Act that arises in the course of any proceeding may be
  dealt with in any manner that the Board directs in order to ensure the
  fair and expeditious conduct of any proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board discretion

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of ensuring the fair and expeditious
  conduct of any proceeding, the Board may vary, supplement or dispense
  with any requirement set out in these Rules.

## [Procedure and Evidence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785213 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board powers

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  relation to any proceeding, the Board may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} receive any evidence that it considers appropriate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} take notice of facts that may be judicially noticed
    and of any generally recognized scientific or technical facts,
    information or opinions concerning patented or protected medicines;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} add parties at any stage of the proceeding;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} permit the amendment of any document filed with the
    Secretary; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} decide any question of procedure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions --- information, documents
  and facts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may, at any time, direct

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that a party provide any information or documents,
    in paper or electronic format, that the Board considers concerned to
    any proceeding; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that a particular fact be established by affidavit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affidavits

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} All affidavits must be filed with the Secretary.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witnesses appearing before Board

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Witnesses appearing before
the Board must testify under oath or solemn affirmation and may be
examined by the party calling the witness and cross-examined by parties
adverse in interest.

## [Expert Witnesses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785230 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Admissibility of expert evidence

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Expert
  witness evidence is not admissible in a proceeding before the Board in
  respect of any issue unless the issue has been raised in the pleadings
  or in a pre-hearing conference order or the expert witness evidence is
  called for the purpose of rebutting the evidence of an expert witness
  introduced by another party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum number

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} More than two expert witnesses may not be called by a
  party, per issue, without leave of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report, declaration and availability

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every party who, in a proceeding before the Board,
  intends to introduce evidence given by an expert witness must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} file with the Secretary and serve on each of the
    parties in accordance with the Board's schedule of events, an expert
    witness report that is supported by an affidavit and that must
    include

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the issues addressed in the
      report,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the qualifications of the
      expert with respect to those issues,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the expert's curriculum vitae attached to the
      report as a schedule,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the facts and assumptions on which the opinions
      in the report are based,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the opinions expressed,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a report provided in response to
      another expert's report, an indication of the points of agreement
      and of disagreement with the other expert's opinions,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the reasons supporting each opinion expressed,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} any literature or other documents specifically
      relied on in support of the opinions expressed,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the methodology on which the expert
      has relied;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} file with the Secretary and serve on each of the
    parties a signed Expert Witness Declaration in Form 1 set out in the
    schedule; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the expert witness is available for
    examination and cross-examination at the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of examination in chief

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Examination in chief of any expert witness may not,
  without the Board's consent, exceed 90 minutes.

## [Time Limits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785252 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Computation of time

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  time limit set out in these Rules expires on a Saturday, Sunday or a
  holiday, the period is extended to the next business day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any document that is served after 5:00 p.m. at the
  local time for the recipient is deemed to be served at 9:00 a.m. on
  the next business day.

## [Notice and Service]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785258 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of service

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  otherwise provided in these Rules, the giving of notice or the service
  of any document under these Rules must be effected

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by personal service in accordance with Rule 11;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by courier, in which case service is deemed to have
    been made on the day of delivery of the document;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by electronic transmission of a copy of the
    document to the address for service, in which case service is deemed
    to have been made on the day of transmission; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel}  in any other manner that the Board may direct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of filed documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a document is required to be filed with the Board
  pursuant to these Rules or a direction of the Board, except in
  connection with [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} motions, the person
  who files the document must, concurrently with the filing, serve a
  copy of the document on every person who is, at the time of service, a
  party to the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service to be filed

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Proof of service of any document that is required to
  be served must, at the request of the Board, be filed with the
  Secretary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service on respondent residing outside
  Canada

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If these Rules require or permit giving notice to or
  the service of any document on a respondent who does not reside or
  carry on business at a specified address in Canada, the giving of
  notice or the service may be effected by giving notice to or serving
  the representative of the respondent who is appointed pursuant to
  section 29 of the Act.

## [Personal Service]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785272 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal service

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Personal service of a
document must be effected by leaving a copy of the document

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service on an individual, with that
  individual;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service on a partnership, with one of
  the partners;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service on a corporation, with an
  officer or director of the corporation;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service on a concerned minister, with
  the concerned minister or the person apparently in charge of the
  office; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} if counsel has filed a notice of appearance on behalf
  of a person referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to (d), with counsel.

## [Address for Service]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785280 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Address for service

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The address for service
for a party is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if there is counsel of record for the party, the
  business address of counsel as shown on the last document filed by
  counsel;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a party is unrepresented,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a concerned minister, the address of
    the concerned minister's office, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other person, the address of
    the person as shown on the notice of hearing; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if a notice of change of address for service has been
  filed with the Board, the new address.

## [Counsel]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785288 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of appearance

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Counsel must file a notice of
  appearance with the Secretary before representing a party before the
  Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Beginning of representation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party is represented by counsel before the Board
  beginning on the date of filing of the notice of appearance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of change of counsel

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person may change counsel by filing a notice of
  change of counsel with the Board, together with proof of service of
  that notice on counsel and on all other parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of withdrawal of counsel

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Counsel may withdraw from a proceeding by filing with
  the Board a notice of withdrawal, together with proof of service of
  that notice on all parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Any party that is not an individual

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any party that is not an individual must be
  represented by counsel in all proceedings.

## [Filing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785300 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of filing

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  filing of a document with the Board must be effected

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by personal service of the document on the
    Secretary;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by delivery of the document by courier or by
    registered or certified mail to the Secretary; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by transmitting the document electronically to the
    Secretary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic filing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any documents that are filed with the Board
  electronically must meet the following requirements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the electronic and paper versions of documents must
    have the same cover page, table of contents and page numbers;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the filename of an electronic document must include
    the name of either the patented or protected medicine or the company
    if the hearing does not relate to a specific patented or protected
    medicine, the name of the party filing the document, the title of
    the document and the date which appears on the document or must meet
    alternative requirements, if any, set out by the Secretary; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} documents must be filed as PDF (Portable Document
    Format) documents or in any format authorized by the Secretary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed filing date

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The date of receipt marked by the Secretary on a
  document is deemed to be the date on which the document was filed with
  the Secretary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Three paper copies of any document that is served by
  a party must be filed with the Secretary. If the document is filed
  electronically, the three copies must be filed with the Secretary
  within 48 hours of electronic filing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confirmation of filing

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} On the request of a party or any interested person
  filing a document with the Secretary, the Secretary must provide the
  party or interested person with confirmation of that filing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidential documents

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a party files a document with the Board that
  contains confidential information, it must ensure that
  "CONFIDENTIAL/CONFIDENTIEL" appears prominently on the cover and pages
  of the document which contain confidential information.

## [Notice of Hearing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785320 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signed notice of hearing

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Proceedings are initiated by
  issuance of a notice of hearing signed by the Secretary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of hearing must be served personally on the
  respondent, all concerned ministers and on any other person that the
  Board may direct, in accordance with Rule 11.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement of allegation or notice of
  application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice of hearing must be accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an allegation of a patented or
    protected medicine sold at an excessive price, a statement of
    allegation set out in consecutively numbered paragraphs containing
    the material facts, the allegations and the order sought by Board
    Staff in the proceeding; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an allegation of a failure to
    provide information and documents related to a patented or protected
    medicine, a notice of application set out in consecutively numbered
    paragraphs containing a statement of the relief sought by Board
    Staff, the grounds for the relief and the material facts on which
    Board Staff intends to rely.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice of hearing

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  notice of hearing issued by the Board must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} specify the date and time of the teleconference
    referred to in Rule 22;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contain a statement of the purpose of the hearing;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state that the hearing will be held in public
    unless, on representations made by a respondent, the Board is
    satisfied that specific, direct and substantial harm would be caused
    to the respondent by the disclosure of information or documents
    relating to the hearing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by a schedule of events; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} contain any other information that the Board
    considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Secretary must give public notice of each hearing
  in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) and in any other
  manner that the Chairperson may direct.

## [Record of Proceeding]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785340 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 87 and
subsection 88(4) of the Act, if the Board issues a notice of hearing,
the Secretary must establish a record of the proceeding that includes

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the notice of hearing;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any document filed with the Secretary by a party in
  relation to the proceeding;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any direction, decision, notice or order of the Board
  in relation to the proceeding;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the transcript of any pre-hearing conference;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the transcript of the hearing in relation to the
  proceeding; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other document or information that is directed by
  the Board to be placed on the record of the proceeding.

## [Response]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785349 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of response

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  respondent who wishes to oppose the making of an order sought by Board
  Staff that is set out in a statement of allegation or in a notice of
  application must, within 20 days after being served with the notice of
  hearing, file with the Secretary and serve on all other parties a
  response that is dated and signed by the respondent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of response

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A response must be set out in consecutively numbered
  paragraphs and must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an admission or denial of each ground or material
    fact set out in the statement of allegation or notice of
    application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the grounds on which the proposed order is opposed
    and the material facts on which the respondent is relying;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the person on whom service
    of any document in relation to the proceeding may be effected.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No response filed

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a respondent has not filed a response within the
  period set out in subsection (1), the Board may, where the Board is
  satisfied that a copy of the notice of hearing was served on the
  respondent and where the Board has received any evidence that it
  required the respondent to provide, make any finding and issue any
  order that the Board considers appropriate under section 83 of the
  Act.

## [Reply]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785360 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of reply

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  Board Staff wishes to reply to the response it must, within 20 days
  after being served with the response, file with the Board and serve on
  all other parties a reply that is dated and signed by Board Staff.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of reply

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A reply must be set out in consecutively numbered
  paragraphs and must set out an admission or denial of each ground or
  material fact that was set out in the response.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No reply filed

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If Board Staff does not file a reply, it is deemed to
  have denied each ground and each material fact alleged in the
  response.

## [Intervention]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785368 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motion for leave to intervene

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who claims an interest in the subject-matter of a proceeding
  may, within any period and under any conditions that the Board may
  specify, bring a motion to the Board for leave to intervene in the
  proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of motion for leave to
  intervene

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A motion for leave to intervene must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the proposed intervener and
    of any counsel representing the intervener;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the nature of the proposed
    intervener's interest in the hearing and the reasons the
    intervention is necessary;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the facts upon which the
    motion is based; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the issues that the proposed intervener intends to
    address.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of motion

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A motion for leave to intervene must be filed with
  the Board and served on the parties in accordance with Rule 10.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of representations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The parties who are served with a motion for leave to
  intervene may make submissions with respect to the motion by filing
  their submissions with the Board and serving a copy of the submissions
  on the person seeking leave to intervene.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors considered by the Board

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 87 of the Act, if a person has
  moved to intervene in a proceeding, the Board may grant or deny the
  intervention and impose any conditions or restrictions on the
  intervention that it determines to be appropriate after considering
  relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the person has an interest in the
    proceeding that is sufficient to warrant the intervention;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the intervention will prejudice any party
    to the proceeding; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the intervention will interfere with the
    fair and expeditious conduct of the proceeding.

## [Appearance by Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785387 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of notice of appearance

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  concerned minister who intends to appear and make representations with
  respect to a matter that is before the Board must, within 20 days
  after being served with the notice of hearing, file with the Board and
  serve on all parties a notice of appearance that is dated and signed
  by the concerned minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice of appearance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of appearance must set out the name and
  address of the individual on whom service of any document intended for
  the concerned minister may be effected and must be accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the representations that the
    concerned minister intends to make and the material facts on which
    the concerned minister is relying, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a list of the documents that may be used in
    evidence to support the material facts on which the concerned
    minister is relying.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No notice of appearance filed

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A concerned minister who has not filed a notice of
  appearance within the period set out in subsection (1) need no longer
  be served by the parties.

## [Case Management Conference]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785397 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Teleconference

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The Secretary must
convene a teleconference with counsel to be held no later than 45 days
following the issuance of the notice of hearing for the purpose of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fixing the hearing schedule;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} establishing the official language the parties wish
  to use during the proceeding;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} discussing the filing of evidence by the parties;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} considering the procedure to be followed and means of
  expediting the hearing, including determining whether written
  submissions will be submitted;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} determining the expected duration of the hearing;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} facilitating the exchange among the parties of
  information and documents to be submitted at the hearing; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} identifying other issues to be resolved.

## [Pre-hearing Conference]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785407 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  parties must appear before the Board at the date, time and place fixed
  by the Board for a pre-hearing conference for the purpose of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} identifying or circumscribing the issues;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} allowing the Board to receive and consider
    representations referred to in subsection 86(1) of the Act and
    determine whether the hearing will be held in private; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} resolving any other issue that may facilitate the
    conduct of the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Teleconference or written submissions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may direct that issues referred to in
  subsection (1) be addressed during a teleconference with the parties
  or in written submissions to be filed with the Secretary in any manner
  that the Board may determine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing and content of written
  submissions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board may require any party who participates in
  the pre-hearing conference to file and serve, at any time before or
  during the pre-hearing conference and in any manner that the Board may
  determine, a written submission setting out any information that the
  Board considers necessary to expedite the proceeding, including a
  concise statement of any procedural issues or questions on the merits
  that the party intends to raise at the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Postponement, suspension or adjournment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board may, if necessary, postpone, suspend or
  adjourn and may reconvene a pre-hearing conference on any conditions
  that the Board considers appropriate.

## [Subpoenas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785420 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of subpoena

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In any
  proceeding, the Board may, on its own motion or on motion by a party,
  issue a subpoena for the attendance of witnesses and for the
  production or inspection of documents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons for subpoena

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a party applies to the Board for a subpoena, the
  party must provide reasons in support of the issuance of the subpoena
  and must identify the person and documents or information to be named
  in the subpoena and their relevance to the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subpoena signed and issued

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A subpoena that is issued by the Board must be signed
  by the Secretary and served by the Board or the party who applied for
  the subpoena, as the case may be, by personal service in accordance
  with Rule 11 on the person summoned to appear before the Board, at
  least five days before the day fixed for that person's appearance.

## [Motion for Interlocutory Relief]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785428 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of motion

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Interlocutory matters must be
  commenced by notice of motion.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice of motion

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of motion must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out the relief sought, the grounds for the
    motion and the official language or languages that the moving party
    wishes to use at the hearing of the motion;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by an affidavit that sets out the
    facts on which the motion is based that do not appear on the record
    of the proceeding;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by written submissions that set out
    a summary of the points at issue, a concise statement of
    submissions, a concise statement of the order sought and a list of
    authorities to be referred to; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be signed and dated by the moving party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing requirements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The moving party must file the notice of motion and
  accompanying memorandum and affidavit with the Board and must serve a
  copy of each of them on each party in accordance with Rule 10.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing date

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The date fixed for the hearing of a motion must not
  be earlier than two days after the date of service of the documents
  referred to in subsection (3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Testimony by affidavit

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  testimony before the Board on a motion in an interlocutory matter must
  be by affidavit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral testimony and cross-examination

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board, before or during the hearing of a motion
  on an interlocutory matter, may grant leave for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a witness to give testimony orally in relation to
    any points at issues raised in the motion; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cross-examination of any person making an
    affidavit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-hearing conference

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  pre-hearing conference is held under Rule 23, any motion for
  interlocutory relief must be made at that pre-hearing conference.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motion to be heard by Board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If no pre-hearing conference is held, or the
  pre-hearing conference has ended before the filing of a notice of
  motion, any motion on an interlocutory matter must be heard by the
  Board at a date, time and place that is fixed by the Board.

## [Hearings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785454 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Location of hearings

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Hearings must be held at
the principal office of the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Postponement, suspension or adjournment

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may, if necessary, postpone, suspend or adjourn and reconvene a
  hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of postponement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Board postpones a hearing, it must give notice
  of the postponement to the parties as soon as possible.

## [Submissions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785462 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written submissions

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** The Board may direct that
written submissions be presented by the parties in addition to or
instead of oral representations and may give any directions with respect
to time limits relating to those submissions that it considers
appropriate.

## [Directions, Decisions and Orders]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785465 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New hearing

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** The Board may reconvene a
hearing in order to receive further evidence relating to the
subject-matter of the original hearing or may rehear any matter in whole
or in part before giving any direction or making any decision or order
relating to the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of directions, decisions and orders

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The Board may give
directions, issue decisions and make orders in any form that it
considers appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written reasons

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Board makes an order under the Act, it must issue written reasons for
  the order, if possible within 180 days after the completion of the
  hearing on the merits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every order of the Board under the Act must be
  published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) and may
  also be published in any other publication that the Chairperson
  designates.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The effective date of any
direction, decision or order of the Board is the date on which it is
given, issued or made, unless the Board specifies a later date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Technical irregularities and procedural
directions, decisions and orders

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** The Board may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} vary or rescind any procedural direction, decision or
  order given, issued or made under these Rules; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} correct a technical irregularity in any direction,
  decision or order given, issued or made by the Board.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785481 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** These Rules come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
