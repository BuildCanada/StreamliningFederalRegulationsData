## Proclamation Proclaiming that His Royal Highness Prince Charles Philip Arthur George Is Now, by the Death of Our Late Sovereign, King Charles the Third {#proclamation-proclaiming-that-his-royal-highness-prince-charles-philip-arthur-george-is-now-by-the-death-of-our-late-sovereign-king-charles-the-third .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2022-42
:::

Registration 2022-09-10

Proclamation Proclaiming that His Royal Highness Prince Charles Philip
Arthur George Is Now, by the Death of Our Late Sovereign, King Charles
the Third
::::

:::::::::: section
::::::::: {.section .order}
\[L.S.\]

Privy Seal

Mary May Simon

TO ALL TO WHOM these presents shall come or whom the same may in any way
concern,

GREETING:

Whereas our late Sovereign, Queen Elizabeth the Second, passed away on
September 8, 2022, by whose death the Crown of Canada vests in His Royal
Highness Prince Charles Philip Arthur George;

We, the Right Honourable Mary May Simon, Governor General of Canada,
assisted by His Majesty's Privy Council for Canada, proclaim that His
Royal Highness Prince Charles Philip Arthur George is now, by the death
of our late Sovereign, Charles the Third, by the Grace of God of the
United Kingdom, Canada and His other Realms and Territories King, Head
of the Commonwealth, Defender of the Faith, to whom we acknowledge faith
and allegiance.

Given under my Hand and Seal of Office at Ottawa, this tenth day of
September, two thousand and twenty-two.

Long Live the King

::: FlushLeft
François-Philippe Champagne
:::

::: FlushLeft
Registrar General of Canada

David Lametti

::: FlushLeft
