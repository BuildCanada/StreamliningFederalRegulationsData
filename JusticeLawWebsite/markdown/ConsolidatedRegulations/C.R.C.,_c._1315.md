## International Joint Commission Immunity Order {#international-joint-commission-immunity-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1315
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Order Respecting the Immunities of the International Joint Commission
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503305 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [International Joint Commission Immunity
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1315).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503307 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations;
    ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Joint
    Commission created pursuant to the 1909 Boundary Water Treaty.
    ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-63, s. 1
:::

## [Immunity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503313 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Organization shall have
in Canada, to such extent as may be required for the performance of its
functions, immunity from every form of legal process except in so far as
in any particular case it has expressly waived its immunity.

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioners and
members of the staff of the Organization shall have immunity in Canada
from every form of legal process in respect of words spoken or written
and all acts performed by them in their official capacity, as set out in
Article V of the Convention, except in so far as in any particular case
they have expressly waived their immunity.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-63, s. 2
:::

**[[3.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.2 .sectionLabel}** The experts performing
missions for the Organization shall, to such extent as may be necessary
for the independant exercise of their functions, have immunity in Canada
from every form of legal process in respect of words spoken or written
and all acts done by them in the course of performing their missions, as
set out in Article VI of the Convention, except in so far as in any
particular case they have expressly waived their immunity.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-63, s. 2
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Order
extends immunity to the Organization in respect of judicial proceeding
commenced before the making of this Order.
