## Diplomatic (Excise Taxes) Remission Order {#diplomatic-excise-taxes-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 757
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Excise Taxes to Diplomatic and Other
Representatives of Another Country
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561593 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Diplomatic (Excise Taxes) Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._757).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561595 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Revenue; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the head of a diplomatic mission or the High
      Commissioner representing one of Her Majesty's Governments,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a counsellor, secretary, attaché or officer of
      equal rank at an embassy, legation or office of a High
      Commissioner in Canada, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a consul-general, consul, vice-consul, trade
      commissioner or assistant trade commissioner, who is a native or
      citizen of the country he represents and is not engaged in any
      other business or profession. ([représentant]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561602 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 4 and
5, remission is hereby granted of the excise taxes payable, under Parts
III and IV of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), on

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} cigars, cigarettes, manufactured tobaccos and wines,
  manufactured or produced in Canada and purchased by the representative
  for his personal or official use; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods imported into Canada by the representative for
  his personal or official use.

## [Resale]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561606 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted under
this Order in respect of goods purchased or imported by a representative
on condition that they are not sold or otherwise disposed of by him
before they have been in his use or possession for one year.

## [Reciprocity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561608 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No remission will be
granted under this Order to a representative of a government where the
Under-Secretary of State for External Affairs has advised the Minister
that full reciprocity is not being accorded by the government to
offices, officials and employees of the Government of Canada.

## [Application for Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561610 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An application for
remission shall be made on a form acceptable to the Minister and
approved in the name of the head of the mission, consul post or trade
commissioner's office, as the case may be.
