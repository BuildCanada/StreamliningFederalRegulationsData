## Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Application for Deposit Insurance By-law {#canada-deposit-insurance-corporation-application-for-deposit-insurance-by-law .Title-of-Act}

Registration 2006-09-28

Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Application for Deposit Insurance
By-law
::::

::::::::: section

The Board of Directors of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation,
pursuant to paragraphs 11(2)(g)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EHADA} and (i) and

Insurance By-law](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-515).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-724907 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this By-law.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Deposit Insurance
      Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a provincial institution
      that makes an application to the Corporation for deposit
      insurance. ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[associate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[associate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, when used to indicate a
      relationship with a person, means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a corporation of which that person beneficially
        owns, directly or indirectly, voting securities that carry more
        than 10 per cent of the voting rights attached to all of the
        outstanding voting securities of the corporation;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a partner of that person; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a trust or an estate or succession in which
        that person has a substantial beneficial interest or in respect
        of which that person serves as a trustee or in a similar
        capacity. ([associé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[beneficial ownership]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[beneficial ownership]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes ownership
      through one or more trustees, legal representatives, agents or
      mandataries or other intermediaries. ([véritable
      propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[common-law partnership]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[common-law partnership]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      relationship between two individuals who are cohabiting in a
      conjugal relationship, having so cohabited for a period of at
      least one year. ([union de fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an incorporated body
      wherever or however incorporated. ([personne
      morale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[direction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[direction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of the voting
      securities of an entity, means the power, alone or in combination
      with one or more persons, directly or indirectly, to exercise or
      direct the exercise of the voting rights attached to those
      securities. ([haute main]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation, a trust, a
      partnership, a fund, an unincorporated association or
      organization, Her Majesty in right of Canada or of a province, an
      agency of Her Majesty in either of those rights, the government of
      a foreign country or any political subdivision of that government
      or any agency of that government or of that political
      subdivision. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[financial statements]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[financial statements]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a balance
      sheet, a statement of retained earnings, an income statement and a
      statement of changes in financial position. ([états
      financiers]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[forecast]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[forecast]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means information in respect of
      prospective results of the operations and financial position or
      changes in the financial position of an entity, based on
      assumptions made in respect of future economic conditions and
      planned courses of action for a specified period, given
      management's judgment as to the most probable set of economic
      conditions. ([prévisions]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[influence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[influence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an entity, means
      the power to exercise an influence, directly or indirectly and
      alone or in combination with one or more persons, over the
      management and policies of the entity, whether through the
      beneficial ownership of voting securities or
      otherwise. ([influence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of any matter, means
      a matter that has or may reasonably be expected to have a bearing
      on the decision of the Corporation as to whether to approve an
      applicant for deposit insurance. ([important]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[misrepresentation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[misrepresentation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an untrue
      statement made in respect of a material fact or a statement that
      is misleading in the context in which it is made due to the
      omission of a material fact. ([fausse
      déclaration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a corporation, a chief executive
        officer, president, vice-president, secretary, controller,
        treasurer and any other individual designated as an officer of
        the corporation by by-law or by a resolution of its directors;
        and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any other entity, any individual
        designated as an officer of the
        entity. ([dirigeant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual or an entity
      and includes a legal representative. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[promoter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[promoter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} acting alone or in conjunction with one or more
        other persons, participates directly or indirectly in the
        founding, organizing or substantial reorganizing of the business
        of an applicant; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in connection with the founding, organizing or
        substantial reorganizing of the business of an applicant,
        directly or indirectly receives, in consideration of services or
        property or both services and property, five per cent or more of
        any class of securities of the applicant or five per cent or
        more of the proceeds from the sale of any class of securities of
        a particular issue, other than a person who receives such
        securities or proceeds either solely as underwriting commissions
        or solely in consideration of property but does not otherwise
        take part in the founding, organizing or substantial
        reorganizing of the business of the
        applicant. ([promoteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[regulatory authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[regulatory authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a supervisory
      or regulatory authority of financial institutions, capital
      markets, securities transactions or commodity futures that has
      jurisdiction over an applicant. ([organisme de
      réglementation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[relative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[relative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a person who is an
      individual, means an individual related to that person by birth,
      adoption, marriage or common-law
      partnership. ([parent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[subsidiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[subsidiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, when used to indicate a
      relationship between one corporation and another, means a
      corporation that is controlled by that other
      corporation. ([filiale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this By-law,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person controls a corporation if securities of
    the corporation, to which are attached more than 50 per cent of the
    votes that may be cast to elect directors of the corporation, are
    beneficially owned by the person and the votes attached to those
    securities are sufficient, if exercised, to elect a majority of the
    directors of the corporation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person controls an unincorporated entity, other
    than a limited partnership, if more than 50 per cent of the
    ownership interest in the entity, however designated, is
    beneficially owned by the person and the person is able to direct
    the business and affairs of the entity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the general partner of a limited partnership
    controls the limited partnership; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a person controls an entity if the person has any
    direct or indirect influence that, if exercised, would result in
    control in fact of the entity.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this By-law, a person who
  controls an entity is considered to control any other entity that is
  controlled, or is considered to be controlled, by the entity.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this By-law, a person is
  considered to control an entity where the aggregate of any securities
  of the entity that are beneficially owned by the person and any
  securities of the entity that are beneficially owned by any entity
  controlled by that person is such that, if that person and all of the
  entities that beneficially own securities of the entity were one
  person, that person would control the entity.

## [Application for Deposit Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-724943 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for deposit insurance must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information and documentation referred to in
    sections 3 to 17, in one of Canada's official languages; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an affidavit as set out in Form 1 of the schedule
    and, if applicable, an affidavit as set out in Form 2 of the
    schedule, duly executed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information and documentation included in an
  application must be current as of not more than 12 months before the
  day on which the final information or documentation relating to the
  application is submitted to the Corporation by the applicant in order
  to complete the application.

## [Applicant's General Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-724949 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The application must
include the following information in respect of the applicant:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} its legal name, including the form of that name in
  the other official language of Canada, if any;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the address, telephone number, e-mail address and
  facsimile number of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its head office,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} its principal place of business, if that place of
    business is located somewhere other than at the head office, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} each of its current branches, if any, and each of
    its proposed branches, if any;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date of its incorporation, the jurisdiction of
  its incorporation and the date on which it began to carry on business
  or proposes to do so;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, title or office, and address of each of its
  current and proposed directors and officers and a copy of their
  curriculum vitae or resumé;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} whether any director or officer referred to in
  paragraph (d) is an associate or a relative of any other director or
  officer referred to in that paragraph or of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a director or officer referred to in paragraph
    13(e), or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an individual referred to in section 14;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} detailed information, for each of the directors and
  officers referred to in paragraph (d), in respect of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any conviction, under the laws of any jurisdiction
    within or outside Canada, for a material criminal offence,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any conviction, under the laws of such a
    jurisdiction, for a statutory or regulatory violation in relation to
    any of their businesses or the applicant's business, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any proceedings that have been commenced against
    them in such a jurisdiction in respect of such an offence or
    violation;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} detailed information, for each of the directors and
  officers referred to in paragraph (d), in respect of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the suspension or revocation, under the laws of any
    jurisdiction within or outside Canada, of any licence held by them
    in relation to the business or affairs of the applicant,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the suspension or cancellation, under the laws of
    such a jurisdiction, of any registration in their name in relation
    to the business or affairs of the applicant, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any investigation that has been or is being
    conducted in such a jurisdiction in relation to such a licence or
    registration;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the legal name, address, telephone number, e-mail
  address and facsimile number of its current or proposed external
  auditor, as applicable;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number, e-mail address
  and facsimile number of any person who is, or who, within the 24-month
  period preceding the day referred to in subsection 2(2) has been, a
  promoter of the applicant; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the name, title or office, address, telephone number,
  e-mail address and facsimile number of the individual who is
  authorized to represent the applicant with respect to the application.

## [Applicant's Financial Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-724972 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  application must include the following documents in respect of the
  financial affairs of the applicant:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant has been incorporated for less
    than one financial year, an audited opening balance sheet and
    unaudited interim financial statements, if any, for the financial
    period beginning on the day on which it began to carry on its
    business; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant has been incorporated for one or
    more financial years

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} unaudited interim financial statements, if any,
      for the financial period following the period covered by the
      annual audited financial statements for the most recently
      completed financial year,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} audited financial statements for

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} each completed financial year from the date of
        incorporation, if it has been incorporated for one or more, but
        less than three, financial years, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} each of the last three financial years, if it
        has been incorporated for three or more financial years, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} its latest annual report, if any.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), all financial statements
  included in the application must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prepared in accordance with the accounting
    principles and auditing standards accepted under or required by the
    laws of the jurisdiction of incorporation of the applicant or
    prescribed by the regulatory authority primarily responsible for
    regulating the applicant; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prepared in accordance with the accounting
    standards and guidelines established by the Canadian Institute of
    Chartered Accountants ("CICA") --- also referred to as "Canadian
    Generally Accepted Accounting Principles" ("Canadian GAAP") --- and
    audited in accordance with the auditing standards and guidelines
    established by the CICA.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If financial statements that are included in an
  application are provided by a foreign entity that controls the
  applicant, they must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prepared in accordance with the generally accepted
    or applicable accounting principles and auditing procedures in the
    jurisdiction where the foreign entity was incorporated or
    established and accompanied by a comparison between the accounting
    standards used to complete the applicant's financial statements and
    Canadian GAAP; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prepared in accordance with Canadian GAAP and
    audited in accordance with the auditing standards and guidelines
    established by the CICA.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  application must include a business plan that covers the three-year
  period beginning on the day on which the applicant proposes to begin
  carrying on its deposit-taking business and that contains the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reasons why it wants to establish a
    deposit-taking business;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an analysis of target markets together with its
    business objectives for those markets, the short- and long-term
    strategy it will employ to achieve those objectives and the reasons
    why it believes that it will be successful in achieving those
    objectives;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an overview of each line of business to be
    conducted and a description of the products and services it will
    offer in each target market, together with the expected levels of
    business for those products and services;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an analysis of the competitive threats it will face
    in each target market, together with a description of how it plans
    to respond to those threats and the reasons why it believes that the
    response will be successful;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a detailed forecast that includes pro forma
    financial statements covering that three-year period and identifying
    the assumptions made in the preparation of the forecast;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a list of the assumptions that it made for the
    purposes of paragraphs (b) to (e) and the reasons why it considers
    those assumptions to be reasonable;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of the off-balance sheet activities
    that it proposes to carry on;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a description of its proposed outsourcing
    arrangements and its contingency plans respecting those
    arrangements;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a sensitivity analysis of its projected financial
    results under various assumptions or scenarios;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} its projected staff complement during that
    three-year period; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of the correlation between the
    business background and expertise of each director and officer and
    the applicant's business objectives.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must also include any other business
  plan that was prepared by, or for, the applicant within the 24-month
  period preceding the date of the business plan referred to in
  subsection (1) and an explanation of any significant differences
  between those business plans.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The application must
include the following information in respect of the applicant's capital:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} its authorized capital level;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sources of its initial and future capital;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount of its issued and outstanding capital;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount of its paid-in or stated capital; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of any other forms of its capital that
  are accepted as, or considered to be, capital in the jurisdiction in
  which the applicant was incorporated.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The application must
include detailed information in respect of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any material pending litigation to which the
  applicant is a party;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any material contingent liabilities, including
  guarantees, suretyships and indemnity obligations issued by the
  applicant to its shareholders, directors, officers, affiliates or
  associates; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any material transaction, in which the applicant has
  an interest, that is pending or is scheduled to occur within the
  24-month period preceding the day referred to in subsection 2(2) and
  that confers or may confer a benefit on any of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its directors or officers,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} its affiliates, associates or promoter, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the directors or officers of an affiliate,
    associate or promoter referred to in subparagraph (ii).

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  application must include evidence that the applicant has established
  appropriate, effective and prudent practices with respect to corporate
  governance, risk management and liquidity and capital management, as

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any procedures that are or will be integrated with
    those of the entity that controls the applicant, if any, or any of
    the applicant's subsidiaries, associates or affiliates, if any.

## [Information About Promoters, Affiliates, Associates and Controlling Entities of the Applicant]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725039 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The application must
include an organization chart that shows

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the entity that controls the applicant, if any, and
  the extent of the ownership interest in the applicant held by that
  entity; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all existing or proposed affiliates and associates of
  the applicant and the extent of the ownership interest held or
  proposed to be held by each of them in the applicant or in the entity
  that controls the applicant, as applicable, including the class of
  securities owned or controlled, the number and percentage of each
  issued and outstanding class of securities held and the nature of the
  ownership interest (that is, whether the interest is direct or
  indirect and whether it is registered, beneficial or exercised through
  direction).

<!-- -->

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  entity that controls the applicant is not a member institution, the
  application must include the information and documentation set out in
  sections 3, 4 and 9 in respect of that entity and the name, address,
  telephone number, e-mail address and facsimile number of each person
  who is the ultimate beneficial owner of, directly or indirectly, or
  who exercises direction over, 20 per cent or more of any class of
  voting securities of that entity.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the entity that controls the applicant carries on
  business in a foreign jurisdiction, the application must also indicate
  whether that entity is subject to supervision or regulation in the
  foreign jurisdiction and, if so, the nature and extent of the
  supervision or regulation.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The application must
include the following information in respect of any promoter of the
applicant and each of the applicant's existing and proposed affiliates
and associates that is not an individual:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} their legal name, including the form of that name in
  the other official language of Canada, if any;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the address, telephone number, e-mail address and
  facsimile number of their head office and their principal place of
  business, if that place of business is located somewhere other than at
  the head office;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date of their incorporation, the jurisdiction of
  their incorporation and the date on which they began to carry on
  business or propose to do so;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the business activities carried on or
  proposed to be carried on by them, including a list of the
  jurisdictions where those activities are carried on or are proposed to
  be carried on;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name, title or office, and address of each of
  their current and proposed directors and officers and a copy of their
  curriculum vitae or resumé;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} whether any director or officer referred to in
  paragraph (e) is an associate or a relative of any other director or
  officer referred to in that paragraph;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the information referred to in paragraphs 3(f)
  and (g) in respect of each of their current and proposed directors and
  officers;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the information described in section 9 in respect of
  that promoter or those affiliates and associates; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number, e-mail address
  and facsimile number of each person who is the ultimate beneficial
  owner of, directly or indirectly, or who exercises direction over, 10
  per cent or more of any class of voting securities of that promoter or
  those affiliates and associates.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The application must
include the following information in respect of any promoter or
associate of the applicant that is an individual:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} their name, address, telephone number, e-mail address
  and occupation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of their curriculum vitae or resumé;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether that promoter is an associate or a relative
  of any associate of the applicant or of any director or officer
  referred to in paragraph 13(e);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the information described in section 9 in respect of
  that promoter or associate; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a detailed summary of their assets and liabilities.

## [Other Required Documentation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725062 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The application must
include the following documents:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a certified copy of the incorporating instrument of
  the applicant and any amendments made to it;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a certified copy of all of the by-laws of the
  applicant and any amendments made to them;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a legal opinion attesting to the due incorporation,
  organization and capacity of the applicant;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any application, made by the applicant or
  any promoter of the applicant during the 12-month period preceding the
  day referred to in subsection 2(2), for incorporation, registration or
  licensing under any federal or provincial legislation that regulates
  financial institutions;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a copy of an agreement or understanding referred to
  in paragraph 9(a) if that agreement or understanding is in writing;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a detailed organization chart that lists all of the
  current and proposed officers of the applicant and all of the
  applicant's current and proposed employees;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the following bonds or insurance policies,
  if any, that relate to the business of the applicant, or of any
  suretyships similar to those bonds, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a fidelity bond or a fidelity insurance policy,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a banker's blanket bond or a banker's blanket
    insurance policy,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an extortion bond or an extortion insurance
    policy,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a safe depository bond or a safe depository
    insurance policy,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a director's or officer's insurance policy, or

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} any other bonds or insurance policies similar to
    those referred to in subparagraphs (i) to (v);

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a certified copy of the resolution of the board of
  directors of the applicant consenting to the making of the application
  and approving its contents;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a certified copy of the resolution of the board of
  directors of the entity that controls the applicant, if applicable,
  consenting to the making of the application and approving its
  contents; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} a certificate or other evidence confirming that the
  applicant is authorized by the province of its incorporation to apply
  for deposit insurance.

## [Consents and Undertakings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725080 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The application must
include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's consent to the release by any
  regulatory authority or government department or by the Bank of Canada
  to the Corporation and its agents or mandataries of any information
  provided by the applicant to that authority, department or bank; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an undertaking to provide, or cause to be provided,
  any other consents, releases or waivers requested by the Corporation
  in connection with its review of the application.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The application must
include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's consent to the release by the
  Corporation to any regulatory authority or government department or to
  the Bank of Canada of any information provided in the application; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an undertaking by the applicant to provide, or cause
  to be provided, any other consents, releases or waivers requested by
  that authority, department or bank in connection with the
  Corporation's review of the application.
