## Order Authorizing the Minister of the Environment to Prescribe Charges {#order-authorizing-the-minister-of-the-environment-to-prescribe-charges .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/98-89
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1998-09-16

Order Authorizing the Minister of the Environment to Prescribe Charges

P.C. 1998-1495 1998-08-26
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Treasury Board,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 19(1)(b)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_392_hq_6143){#fn_392_hq_6143-ID0EFBBAA} of
  the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby authorizes
  the Minister of the Environment to prescribe by order the charges to
  be paid to the Canadian Environmental Assessment Agency (the Agency)
  by the proponent of a project for the services that are provided by
  the Agency on behalf of Her Majesty in right of Canada in the course
  of an environmental assessment conducted by a review panel under the
  [Canadian Environmental Assessment Act](/eng/acts/C-15.2); and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_392_hq_6143-ID0EFBBAA){#fn_392_hq_6143}S.C.
    1991, c. 24, s. 6
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_392_hq_6144){#fn_392_hq_6144-ID0EDBAAA} of
  the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), if a service is
  not rendered to the proponent and the charges have already been paid
  in whole or in part, considering that it is in the public interest to
  remit the charges, hereby grants remission of the charges paid so that
  the Agency reimburses the charges to the person who paid them.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_392_hq_6144-ID0EDBAAA){#fn_392_hq_6144}S.C.
    1991, c. 24, s. 7(2)
    :::
