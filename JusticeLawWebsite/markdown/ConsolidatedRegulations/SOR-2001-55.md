## [Prescribed Supervisory Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678836 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 503.1 of the [Trust and Loan Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), the following information or any component of
  that information, is prescribed supervisory information in relation to
  a company:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any rating assigned by the Superintendent to the
    company to assess its financial condition and any other such rating
    that is substantially based on information obtained from the
    Superintendent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any stage of intervention assigned to the company
    under the Guide to Intervention for Federal Financial Institutions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any order made in respect of the company under
    subsection 473(3) of the [Trust and Loan Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), any prudential agreement entered into by the
    company under section 506.1 of that Act or any direction issued to
    it under section 507 of that Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any report prepared by or at the request of the
    Superintendent or any recommendation made by the Superintendent as a
    result of an annual or special examination or other supervisory
    review of the company, including any related correspondence to or
    from the directors or officers of the company; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2011-196, s. 33\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any category in which the company is classified
    under the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Differential Premiums
    By-law and any premium rate assigned to it and any annual premium
    determined for it under that By-law.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 503.1 of the [Trust and
  Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), the information prescribed by
  the following regulations is prescribed supervisory information in
  relation to an affiliate of a company:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Banks)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-59), if the affiliate is a
    bank to which the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) applies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Authorized Foreign
    Banks) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-58), if the affiliate
    is an authorized foreign bank to which the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) applies;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Cooperative Credit
    Associations) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-57), if the
    affiliate is an association to which the [Cooperative Credit
    Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01) applies;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Insurance Companies)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-56), if the affiliate is a
    company, society, foreign company or provincial company to which the
    [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Bank Holding
    Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-480), if the
    affiliate is a bank holding company to which the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) applies; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Insurance Holding
    Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-484), if the
    affiliate is an insurance holding company to which the [Insurance
    Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies.

## [Prohibited Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678854 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 and
4, a company shall not, directly or indirectly, disclose information
referred to in section 1.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-196, s. 34
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 2"){.PITLink}
:::

## [Limited Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678858 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A company may disclose
information referred to in section 1 to its affiliates or to its
directors, officers, employees, auditors, securities underwriters or
legal advisors, or to those of its affiliates, if the company ensures
that the information remains confidential.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-196, s. 34
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A company or any of its
affiliates may disclose information referred to in paragraph 1(1)(c) if
the company or affiliate considers the information to contain a material
fact or material change that is required by the securities laws of the
relevant jurisdiction to be disclosed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-196, s. 35(E)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-4-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678865 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
