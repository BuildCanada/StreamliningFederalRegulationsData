## Akwesasne Residents Remission Order {#akwesasne-residents-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-412
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1991-06-13

Order Respecting the Remission of Duties Paid or Payable on Goods
Acquired in the United States and Imported into Canada by Akwesasne
Residents, by Owners or Operators of Duly Authorized Community Stores
and by the Mohawk Council of Akwesasne or an Entity Authorized by that
Council

P.C. 1991-1129  1991-06-13
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue and the Minister of Indian Affairs and
Northern Development, pursuant to section 101 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-91-412_e_hq_5230){#fn_SOR-91-412_e_hq_5230-ID0EDBAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of
duties paid or payable on goods acquired in the United States and
imported into Canada by Akwesasne residents, by owners or operators of
duly authorized community stores and by the Mohawk Council of Akwesasne
or an entity authorized by that Council](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-412).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936625 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Akwesasne Residents Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-412).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936627 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Akwesasne Reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Akwesasne Reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the St. Regis
    Reserve No. 15 (Quebec) and the Cornwall Island Reserve No. 59
    (Ontario); ([réserve d'Akwesasne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Akwesasne resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Akwesasne resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any individual who
    has established permanent residence on the Akwesasne Reserve;
    ([résident d'Akwesasne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[authorized identification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorized identification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    identification document issued to an Akwesasne resident by the
    Mohawk Council of Akwesasne; ([pièce d'identité
    autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Cornwall customs office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Cornwall customs office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the customs
    office located at the highway border crossing on Cornwall Island;
    ([bureau de douane de Cornwall]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[duly authorized community store]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[duly authorized community store]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    store on the Akwesasne Reserve that is established primarily for the
    sale of goods to Akwesasne residents and that is authorized by the
    Mohawk Council of Akwesasne in a manner acceptable to the Minister
    of National Revenue and the Mohawk Council of Akwesasne; ([magasin
    communautaire dûment autorisé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[duties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[duties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 66
    of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011);
    ([droits]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[personal use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[personal use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any use, other than any
    income-producing use, by an Akwesasne resident or by a member of an
    Akwesasne resident's household. ([usage personnel]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936636 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies in
respect of goods referred to in sections 7, 8 and 9 that are acquired in
the United States and imported into Canada on or after the day this
Order comes into force.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936638 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 7,
remission is hereby granted of the duties paid or payable in respect of
goods for personal use that are imported into Canada by an Akwesasne
resident.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 8,
remission is hereby granted of the duties paid or payable in respect of
goods, other than alcoholic beverages, cigars, cigarettes and
manufactured tobacco, set out in the schedule that are imported into
Canada by the owner or operator of a duly authorized community store, or
by a person on behalf of that owner or operator, for the purpose of sale
to Akwesasne residents for their personal use.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 9,
remission is hereby granted of the duties paid or payable in respect of
goods, other than alcoholic beverages, cigars, cigarettes and
manufactured tobacco, that are imported into Canada, by the Mohawk
Council of Akwesasne or an entity authorized by that Council to import
the goods, for use in connection with educational, hospital or social
services provided to residents of the Akwesasne Reserve.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936642 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The remission referred to
in section 4 is granted to an Akwesasne resident on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods are reported under section 12 of the
  [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) at the Cornwall customs office at the
  time of importation and accounted for at the Cornwall customs office
  under section 32 of that Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an authorized identification is presented by the
  Akwesasne resident to the customs officer at the Cornwall customs
  office at the time of importation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the goods are for the personal use of that Akwesasne
  resident or are imported for the personal use of another Akwesesne
  resident;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the goods that are imported for personal use are not
  sold or otherwise transferred within 12 months after the day of
  importation, other than to an Akwesasne resident for the personal use
  of that Akwesasne resident; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} where duties have been paid, a claim for remission is
  made to the Minister of National Revenue within two years after the
  day the goods are accounted for under section 32 of the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The remission referred to
in section 5 is granted to an owner or operator of a duly authorized
community store on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods set out in the schedule are reported under
  section 12 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) at the Cornwall
  customs office at the time of importation and accounted for at the
  Cornwall customs office under section 32 of that Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} evidence of the authorization to operate the duly
  authorized community store is presented to the customs officer at the
  Cornwall customs office at the time of importation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the goods set out in the schedule are sold only to
  Akwesasne residents for their personal use or the personal use of
  another Akwesasne resident; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where duties have been paid, a claim for remission is
  made to the Minister of National Revenue within two years after the
  day the goods are accounted for under section 32 of the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The remission referred to
in section 6 is granted to the Mohawk Council of Akwesasne and to any
entity authorized by the Mohawk Council of Akwesasne to import goods
referred to in section 6 on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods are reported under section 12 of the
  [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) at the Cornwall customs office at the
  time of importation and accounted for at the Cornwall customs office
  under section 32 of that Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the goods are imported by an entity authorized
  by the Mohawk Council of Akwesasne to import the goods, evidence of
  the authorization is presented to the customs officer at the Cornwall
  customs office at the time of importation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the goods are not sold or otherwise transferred
  within 12 months after the day of importation, other than to an
  Akwesasne resident for the personal use of that Akwesasne resident;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where duties have been paid, a claim for remission is
  made to the Minister of National Revenue within two years after the
  day the goods are accounted for under section 32 of the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).
