## British Columbia Turkey Marketing Levies Order {#british-columbia-turkey-marketing-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 149
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Levies from
Certain Turkey Producers in British Columbia
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-523183 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [British Columbia Turkey Marketing Levies
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._149).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-523185 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Natural Products Marketing
    (British Columbia) Act; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the British Columbia
    Turkey Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[market allotment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[market allotment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the total quantity of
    turkey meat that a producer is entitled to market in
    intraprovincial, interprovincial and export trade pursuant to the
    Canadian Turkey Marketing Quota Regulations and the orders made by
    the Commodity Board pursuant to the Plan; ([allocation de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the British Columbia Turkey
    Marketing Scheme, established and amended from time to time pursuant
    to the Act and any regulations made thereunder to give effect to the
    Plan; ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person engaged in the
    production of turkey in the Province of British Columbia;
    ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a turkey of any variety, grade
    or class that is raised or kept for slaughter within the Province of
    British Columbia and sold for slaughter. ([dindon]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-523193 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay to
the Commodity Board, in addition to the licence fees set out under the
Plan, levies at the rate of \$0.05 per pound live weight on every turkey
sold by him that is in excess of his market allotment.

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-523195 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who buys turkeys from a producer shall deduct from the moneys
  payable to the producer for the turkeys all levies payable by the
  producer to the Commodity Board under section 3 and shall pay such
  levies to the Commodity Board at its office in Surrey, British
  Columbia, not later than the 15th day of the month next following the
  month in which the levies were deducted.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every producer shall pay to the Commodity Board at
  its office in Surrey, British Columbia, all levies payable by him
  under section 3 that were not deducted and paid to the Commodity Board
  in the manner prescribed in subsection (1), not later than the 15th
  day of the month next following the month in which the turkeys in
  respect of which the levies are payable were sold.
