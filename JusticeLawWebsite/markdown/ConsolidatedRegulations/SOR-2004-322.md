## Newfoundland and Labrador Sex Offender Information Registration Regulations {#newfoundland-and-labrador-sex-offender-information-registration-regulations .Title-of-Act}

Regulations

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713716 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Sex Offender Information
    Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[NLSOIRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[NLSOIRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Newfoundland and Labrador
    Sex Offender Information Registration Centre administered by RCMP
    "B" Division headquarters. ([BIRDSTNL]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police. ([GRC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Report and Notification by Telephone]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713721 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A sex offender whose main
residence is located in the Province of Newfoundland and Labrador may
make any report required under paragraph 4.1(a) or (b) or section 4.3 of
the Act, or provide any notification required under section 6 of the
Act, by telephone to the NLSOIRC.

## [Persons Authorized to Collect Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713723 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
authorized in the Province of Newfoundland and Labrador to collect
information for the purposes of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the RCMP;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a police officer within the meaning of paragraph 2(l)
  of the Royal Newfoundland Constabulary Act, 1992, Statutes of
  Newfoundland and Labrador, c. R-17, as amended from time to time; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person employed at the NLSOIRC whose duties include
  collection of information under the Act.

## [Persons Authorized to Register Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713728 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Persons employed at the
NLSOIRC whose duties include the registration of information are
authorized in the Province of Newfoundland and Labrador to register
information for the purposes of the Act.

## [Registration Centres]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713730 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  places set out in the schedule are designated as registration centres
  in the Province of Newfoundland and Labrador.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designated area of service for each registration
  centre is the entire Province of Newfoundland and Labrador.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713734 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2004-322_e_hq_6728){#fn_SOR-2004-322_e_hq_6728-ID0EBCA}[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the later of the
day on which the [Sex Offender Information Registration
Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7), chapter 10 of the Statutes of Canada, 2004, comes
into force and the day on which these Regulations are registered.
