## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664590 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Canadian Coast Guard ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a ship that is
entrusted for management or operation to the Department of Fisheries and
Oceans. (*navire de la Garde côtière canadienne*)

## [Exclusion from the Application of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664592 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following provisions of
the [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9) do not apply to Canadian Coast
Guard ships:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} section 110 (regulations respecting certification of
  masters and seamen);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 130 (production of certificates);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} section 231 (regulations respecting crew
  accommodation);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} section 232 (regulations giving effect to
  international labour conventions);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} section 274 (leaving port in default); and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} sections 389 and 390 (regulations respecting
  dangerous goods), in so far as they relate to the carriage of aviation
  fuel.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664600 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on February 25, 2000.
