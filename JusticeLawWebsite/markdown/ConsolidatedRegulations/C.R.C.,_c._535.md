## Marking of Imported Goods Order {#marking-of-imported-goods-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 535
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Order Respecting the Marking of Goods Imported into Canada
::::

::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557668 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Marking of Imported Goods Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._535).

## [Marking of Goods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557670 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 3, goods of a description or class specified in an item of
  the schedule and imported into Canada shall be marked, stamped,
  branded or labelled in legible English or French words, in a
  conspicuous place that shall not be covered or obscured by any
  subsequent attachments or arrangements, so as to indicate the country
  of origin.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The marking, stamping, branding or labelling
  prescribed by subsection (1) shall be as nearly indelible and
  permanent as the nature of the goods will permit.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-858, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order does not apply
to goods that are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} donations for charitable purposes and not for the
  purpose of sale;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} gifts or bequests;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} antiques;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} used goods, with the exception of iron or steel pipes
  and tubes;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} for the exclusive use of the importer or his
  employees and not for resale to the general public, with the exception
  of iron or steel pipes and tubes;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} imported under tariff item No. 9808.00.00, 9809.00.00
  or 9810.00.00 of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011); or

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} imported for subsequent exportation from Canada with
  the exception of iron or steel pipes and tubes.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-858, s. 1
- SOR/88-84
:::

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-557687 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- [1]{.lawlabel} *Goods for Personal or Household Use*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} Bakeware and cookware made of aluminum

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Bakeware and cookware made of cast iron

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Bath mats, towels and wash cloths, knitted or woven

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Batteries, dry cell

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} Blankets

  - [(6)]{.lawlabel} Brushes, including toothbrushes and handles
    therefor

  - [(7)]{.lawlabel} Candles

  - [(8)]{.lawlabel} Cards, the following; credit and identification,
    made of any material having a diameter or side exceeding 1/2 inch in
    width and imported in sheet form or otherwise

  - [(9)]{.lawlabel} Chrome plated ware and utensils for use in serving
    food and beverages

  - [(10)]{.lawlabel} Cigar and cigarette lighters, except lighters for
    incorporation into motor vehicles

  - [(11)]{.lawlabel} Clocks and movements, except clocks and movements
    for use as original equipment by motor vehicle manufacturers

  - [(12)]{.lawlabel} Containers, thermostatic, the following: carafes,
    flasks, jars, jugs and vacuum bottles, and refills or inserts
    therefor

  - [(13)]{.lawlabel} Cutlery, chrome plated or stainless steel

  - [(14)]{.lawlabel} Dishes and ornaments made of china, earthenware,
    ironstone, porcelain, semi-porcelain, stoneware or white granite

  - [(15)]{.lawlabel} Electronic equipment, the following: phonographs,
    radio receiving sets, radio-phonograph sets,
    radio-phonograph-television sets, record players, tape recorders,
    television receiving sets

  - [(16)]{.lawlabel} Ironing board covers and pads

  - [(17)]{.lawlabel} Kitchenware made of metal or plastic, coated,
    lithographed, painted or otherwise, the following: bread boxes, cake
    humidors, canisters, foil and paper dispensers, range sets, serving
    ovens and step-on waste cans

  - [(18)]{.lawlabel} Knives, the following: jack, pen and pocket;
    scissors and shears

  - [(19)]{.lawlabel} Lawn mowers (powered)

  - [(20)]{.lawlabel} Matches in books, boxes or folders

  - [(21)]{.lawlabel} Pencils

  - [(22)]{.lawlabel} Pens, the following: ball point and fountain; and
    nib penholders

  - [(23)]{.lawlabel} Pillowslips and sheets made of cotton

  - [(24)]{.lawlabel} Razor blades (safety type)

  - [(25)]{.lawlabel} Thermometers

  - [(26)]{.lawlabel} Tiles, glazed, unglazed and ceramic mosaic, the
    following: hearth, floor and wall

  - [(27)]{.lawlabel} Umbrellas

  - [(28)]{.lawlabel} Utensils, kitchen type, chrome plated or stainless
    steel

  - [(29)]{.lawlabel} Watch bracelets (expansion type)

- [2]{.lawlabel} *Hardware*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} Caps, made of metal, lithographed or printed, for
    containers, the following: lug, screw and vacuum

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Copper tubing

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Drapery I-beam rail, made of aluminum, brass, steel
    or other metals or plastic and component parts therefor

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Electrical measuring devices for panel mounting
    designed to indicate alternating or direct current microamperes,
    milliamperes or amperes, millivolts, volts or kilovolts and such
    other variables as pressure, resistance and temperature that may be
    translated into alternating or direct current or voltage

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} Glass in panes or sheets, the following: common or
    colourless window, laminated, plate and sheet

  - [(6)]{.lawlabel} Goods made of porcelain for electrical use

  - [(7)]{.lawlabel} Piles and rasps

  - [(8)]{.lawlabel} Sink strainers (basket type)

  - [(9)]{.lawlabel} Tubes, electronic

  - [(10)]{.lawlabel} Twines, the following: baler and binder

  - [(11)]{.lawlabel} Wire insect screening

  - [(12)]{.lawlabel} Iron or steel pipes and tubes

- [3]{.lawlabel} *Novelties and Sporting Goods*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} Articles in the style of Indian handicrafts

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Athletic gloves and mitts, including baseball and
    hockey gloves and mitts

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Bicycles

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Decorations, novelties and ornaments

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} Enamelled emblems and silver plated or sterling
    silver bracelets, brooches, pins and spoons, all designed as
    souvenirs of Canada, its provinces, territories, cities, towns or
    other geographical locations

  - [(6)]{.lawlabel} Gift wrappings, the following: bindings, braids,
    ribbons, tapes, ties and trimmings, made chiefly or wholly of
    textile fibres

  - [(7)]{.lawlabel} Toys, games and athletic and sporting goods

- [4]{.lawlabel} *Paper Products*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} Boxes and cartons, empty folding or set-up, made of
    paper, paper board, plain or corrugated fibre or fibre board, for
    use as shipping containers

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paper matter and products, lithographed or printed

- [5]{.lawlabel} *Wearing Apparel*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} Boots, shoes and slippers

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Brassieres, corselettes, garter belts, girdles and
    lacing corsets

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Fabrics, braided or woven, containing rubber yarns,
    not exceeding 12 inches in width; boot and shoe laces

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Gloves made partially or wholly of leather

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} Hair pieces, the following: wigs, half wigs,
    switches, postiches, pony tails, toupees and other types of hair
    pieces designed to be worn on the head of a person.

  - [(6)]{.lawlabel} Handbags and purses, except handbags and purses
    made of beads, metal mesh or similar material

  - [(7)]{.lawlabel} Hats, including berets, bonnets, caps and hats,
    hoods and shapes made of fur felt, wool felt and wool-and-fur felt

  - [(8)]{.lawlabel} Knitted garments

  - [(9)]{.lawlabel} Raincoats and rainwear made of plastic

  - [(10)]{.lawlabel} Wearing apparel made wholly or substantially of
    natural or synthetic textile fibres
