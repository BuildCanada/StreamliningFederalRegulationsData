## Baie Comeau Airport Zoning Regulations {#baie-comeau-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 75
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Baie Comeau Airport
::::

::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561324 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Baie Comeau Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._75).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561326 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Baie Comeau Airport, in the
    Township of Manicouagan, County of Saguenay, in the Province of
    Quebec; ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
    described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
    l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary inclined
    plane extending upward and outward from the end of a strip along and
    at right angles to the projected centre line thereof, which approach
    surface is more particularly described in Part III of the schedule;
    ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/93-401,
    s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

[[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
    located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
    outer surface is more particularly described in Part IV of the
    schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a rectangular portion of the
    landing area of the airport including the runway prepared for the
    take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction, which
    strip is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule;
    ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
    inclined plane extending upward and outward from the lateral limits
    of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional surface is
    more particularly described in Part VI of the schedule. ([surface de
    transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-401, s. 2
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, the airport reference point is deemed to be 67 feet above
sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561338 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands and lands under water, including public road allowances,
adjacent to or in the vicinity of the airport, the outer limits of which
are described in Part II of the schedule, other than such lands as from
time to time form part of the airport.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561340 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct, on any land or any land under water to which these
Regulations apply, any building, structure or object or any addition to
any existing building, structure or object, the highest point of which
will exceed in elevation at the location of the highest point any of the
surfaces hereinafter set out that project immediately over and above the
surface of the land at that location, namely,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iairport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

Premising that the bearings hereinafter mentioned are astronomical and
are referred to the centre line of strip 10-28, Baie Comeau Airport,
Baie Comeau, Province of Quebec, as North, seventy-nine degrees,
forty-eight minutes, twenty seconds East (N. 79°48′20″ E.).

BEGINNING at the intersection of the centre line of strip 10-28 with the
centre line of the proposed strip 16-34; THENCE, North, 79°48′20″ East
along the centre line of strip 10-28, a distance of two hundred
eighty-one feet and one tenth (281.1) to a point; THENCE, at right
angles in a general Northwesterly direction, a distance of five hundred
(500) feet to the airport reference point; said airport reference point
being also at two thousand five hundred forty-six feet and one tenth
(2,546.1) measured along a line having bearing of South 27°05′ East from
a point being the Northwesterly corner of Lot 16-1, Manicouagan Range,
where there is an iron boundary post.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Approach Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-each-approach-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Being a surface abutting each end of each strip associated with
runways 10-28 and 16-34, consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
of one (1) foot measured vertically to fifty (50) feet measured
horizontally, rising to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right
angles to the projected centre line of each strip two hundred (200) feet
measured vertically above the elevation at the end of each strip and ten
thousand (10,000) feet measured horizontally from the end of each strip,
the outer ends of each imaginary horizontal line being two thousand
(2,000) feet from each projected centre line, which surface is shown on
Department of Transport Plan No. M-3908, dated March 15, 1973.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Being an imaginary surface consisting of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a common plane established at a constant elevation of
  one hundred and fifty (150) feet above the assigned elevation of the
  airport reference point, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the common plane described in paragraph (a) is
  less than thirty (30) feet above the surface of the ground, an
  imaginary surface located thirty (30) feet above the surface of the
  ground,

which outer surface is shown on Department of Transport Plan No. M-3908,
dated March 15, 1973.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-strip .SchedHeadL1}

The strips associated with existing runway 10-28 and proposed
runway 16-39 are one thousand (1,000) feet in width, five hundred (500)
feet being on each side of the centre line of the runway, and eight
thousand four hundred (8,400) feet in length, and are shown on
Department of Transport Plan No. M-3908, dated March 15, 1973.

### [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-videscription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Being a surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at a ratio of one
(1) foot measured vertically to seven (7) feet measured horizontally at
right angles to the centre line and centre line production of each
strip, and extending upward and outward from the lateral limits of each
strip and its approach surfaces to an intersection with the outer
surface or another transitional surface of an adjoining strip, all as
shown on Department of Transport Plan No. M-3908, dated March 15, 1973.
