## Ontario Tender Fruit Service Charge (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations, 1986 {#ontario-tender-fruit-service-charge-interprovincial-and-export-regulations-1986 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-669
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1986-06-17

Regulations Respecting the Service Charge Imposed on Certain Persons
Engaged in the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade of Tender
Fruit Produced in Ontario
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Ontario Tender Fruit Producers' Marketing Board, pursuant to
sections 3[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EJCBA} and
4^\*^ of the [Ontario Tender Fruit Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-678)
made by Order in Council P.C. 1979-2538 of September 20, 1979[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2star_e){#footnote2star_e-ID0EDCBA},
hereby makes the annexed Regulations respecting the service charge
imposed on certain persons engaged in the marketing in interprovincial
and export trade of fresh fruit produced in Ontario.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^ \*^](#footnote1star_e-ID0EJCBA){#footnote1star_e}SOR/86-100,
  1986 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 449
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2star_e-ID0EDCBA){#footnote2star_e}SOR/79-678,
  1979 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3378
  :::

St. Catharines, Ontario, June 12, 1986

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897668 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Ontario Tender Fruit Service Charge (Interprovincial and
Export) Regulations, 1986](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-669).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-582, s. 2
- SOR/93-86, s. 2(F)
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897672 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Tender Fruit
    Producers' Marketing Board; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[dealer-shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dealer-shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who buys,
    sells or offers for sale, receives, assembles, pre-cools, stores,
    packs, ships or transports tender fruit but does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person employed by and driving a vehicle owned
      by a producer, shipper, dealer-shipper or producer-shipper,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a railway company, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who transports tender fruit by motor
      vehicle as the agent of the producer;
      ([négociant-expéditeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fresh fruit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fresh fruit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/92-582, s.
    3\]]{.Repealed}

[[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manufacture of tender fruit products or of
      juice, beverages, spirits or wine from tender fruit, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the canning, bottling, distilling, fermenting,
      dehydrating, pitting, drying or freezing of tender fruit;
      ([transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person engaged in the
    business of processing tender fruit;
    ([transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production of tender fruit. ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-582, ss. 3, 5
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897687 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of tender fruit in interprovincial and export
trade and to persons and property situated within the Province of
Ontario.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-582, s. 5
:::

## [Imposition of Service Charge]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897691 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/93-86, s. 2(F)

\]
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  producer shall pay to the Board a service charge in the amount of
  \$0.0035 per pound of fresh plums, prunes or pears marketed by the
  producer or on behalf of the producer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every producer shall pay to the Board a service
  charge in the amount of \$0.0078 per pound of fresh peaches marketed
  by the producer or on behalf of the producer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-283, s. 1
- SOR/90-693, s. 1
- SOR/92-582, ss. 4, 6(F)
- SOR/93-86, s. 1
:::

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897699 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  tender fruit is sold to the Board, the Board shall deduct the service
  charge imposed pursuant to section 4 from the moneys payable by the
  Board to the producer for the sale of the tender fruit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where tender fruit is sold to a dealer-shipper, the
  dealer-shipper shall deduct the service charge imposed pursuant to
  section 4 from the moneys payable by him to the producer and remit the
  amount deducted to the Board within 21 days after the receipt by the
  dealer-shipper of the tender fruit.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every producer shall pay to the Board at its office
  all service charges payable by the producer under section 4 that were
  not deducted and paid to the Board in the manner prescribed by
  subsection (1) or (2) in respect of tender fruit sold by the producer
  in any month not later than the fifteenth day of the following month.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-582, ss. 5, 6(F)
- SOR/93-86, s. 2(F)
:::
