## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-795540 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-181, s. 118\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-181, s. 118\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a retail association, as defined in section 2 of
      the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a company, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
      [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8);

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a foreign company, as defined in subsection 2(1)
      of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8); or

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a company, as defined in section 2 of the [Trust
      and Loan Companies
      Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8). ([institution]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[maintenance fee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[maintenance fee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a fee in relation to a
    prepaid payment product that is charged after the product has been
    purchased, other than a fee associated with the holder's use of the
    product or of any service related to it. ([frais de tenue de
    compte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prepaid payment product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prepaid payment product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a payment
    card, whether physical or electronic, that is --- or can be ---
    loaded with funds and that can be used by the card holder to make
    withdrawals or purchase goods or services.  ([produit de paiement
    prépayé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[promotional product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[promotional product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prepaid payment
    product that is purchased by an entity and distributed as part of a
    promotional, loyalty or award program. ([produit
    promotionnel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-795553 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issued in Canada by institution

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
prepaid payment products that are issued in Canada by an institution.

## [Manner of Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-795556 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clear and simple language

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Any disclosure referred to
in these Regulations must be made in language, and presented in a
manner, that is clear, simple and not misleading.

## [Initial Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-795559 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner and content

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 385.18(2) of the [Cooperative Credit
  Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01), subsections 482(1.1) and 601(2)
  of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) and subsection
  438(1.1) of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), the
  following information must, before a prepaid payment product is
  issued, be provided in any document that the issuing institution
  prepares for the issuance of the product, including on the product's
  exterior packaging, if any, and be provided in writing to any person
  applying to the institution for the product:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the issuing institution;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a toll-free telephone number that can be used to
    make inquiries about the product's terms and conditions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the following restrictions on the use of the
    product, if imposed by the issuing institution:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the fact that the product is not reloadable,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the fact that the product cannot be used to make
      withdrawals, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any other restriction that could reasonably be
      expected to affect a person's decision to acquire the product;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all fees that may be imposed on the product holder
    by the issuing institution in respect of the product;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the funds that are loaded on the product are not
    insured by the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation, a statement to
    that effect; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a promotional product, either that
      the product holder's right to use the funds that are loaded on the
      product will not expire or the day on which that right will
      expire, as the case may be, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other prepaid payment
      product, that the product holder's right to use the funds that are
      loaded on the product will not expire.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presentation of fee information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in paragraph (1)(d) must
  be presented in an information box and appear prominently on any
  exterior packaging or other document referred to in subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- application by telephone

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 4, if a
person applies for a prepaid payment product by telephone, the
information referred to in paragraphs 4(1)(a) to (f) must, before the
product is issued, be disclosed to them orally instead of in writing.

## [Additional Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-795578 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}On issuance

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 385.18(3) of the [Cooperative Credit
  Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01), subsections 482(2) and 601(3) of
  the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) and subsection 438(2)
  of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), any charges
  for which a natural person to whom a prepaid payment product is issued
  becomes responsible by accepting or using the product, as well as the
  following information, must be disclosed in writing to that person on
  issuance of the product:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the product's terms and conditions, including the
    product holder's rights and responsibilities with respect to a lost
    or stolen product;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the product holder can verify
    the balance of the funds loaded on the product;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the product holder may, in
    certain circumstances, use funds loaded on the product towards
    partial payment of a purchase; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the information referred to in paragraphs 4(1)(a)
    to (f) unless the product is issued in person and that information
    was disclosed under subsection 4(1) immediately before the issuance
    of the product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other than natural person

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An institution must, on issuance of a prepaid payment
  product to a person other than a natural person, disclose to them any
  charges for which they become responsible by accepting or using the
  product as well as the information referred to in paragraphs (1)(a) to
  (d).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}On product

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 385.18(3) of the [Cooperative Credit Associations
Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01), subsections 482(2) and 601(3) of the [Insurance
Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) and subsection 438(2) of the [Trust and
Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), an institution must disclose the
following information by setting it out directly on the prepaid payment
product or, if the product is electronic, by disclosing it
electronically on the product holder's request:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the issuing institution;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the prepaid payment product
  expires, if any;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if it is a promotional product, the date, if any, on
  which the person's right to use the funds that are loaded on the
  product will expire;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a toll-free telephone number that can be used to make
  inquiries about the prepaid payment product, including balance
  inquiries and complaints; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a website address where the information referred to
  in paragraphs 4(1)(a) to (e) and section 6 can be obtained.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-795595 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee changes

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An institution must not
increase any fee, or impose a new fee, associated with a prepaid payment
product that is issued to a natural person unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person has, in their capacity as holder of the
  product, provided the institution with their name and mailing or email
  address;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the product holder has been given the opportunity to
  modify the information referred to in paragraph (a); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the institution discloses the new or increased fee by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} sending a notice to the most recent address
    provided for the product holder at least 30 days before the
    effective date of the new or increased fee, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} displaying a notice on the institution's website
    for a period of at least 60 days immediately before the effective
    date of the new or increased fee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of funds

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An institution must not
impose an expiry date on a prepaid payment product holder's right to use
the funds that are loaded on a prepaid payment product unless it is a
promotional product.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintenance fee

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** An institution must not
impose a maintenance fee on a prepaid payment product holder for a
period of 12 months after the day on which the product is activated
unless it is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a promotional product; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a reloadable product and the product holder has given
  their express consent to the imposition of the fee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Overdraft

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An institution must not
charge overdraft fees or interest in respect of a prepaid payment
product without the express consent of the product holder.
