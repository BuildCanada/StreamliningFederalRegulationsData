## Ontario Egg Order {#ontario-egg-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 181
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Eggs Produced in Ontario
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536115 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Egg Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._181).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536117 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing
    Board of Ontario established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Régie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Egg and
    Fowl Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eggs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eggs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any eggs of a domestic hen
    produced in the Province of Ontario; ([oeufs]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    eggs established and amended from time to time pursuant to the Act.
    ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536124 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Board and the Commodity
Board are each authorized to regulate the marketing of eggs in
interprovincial and export trade and for such purposes may, with respect
to persons and property situated within the Province of Ontario,
exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable by each of them
in relation to the marketing of eggs locally within that Province under
the Act and the Plan.

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536126 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/80-90\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the powers granted to it by section 3
  with respect to the marketing of eggs in interprovincial and export
  trade,

to make orders fixing, imposing and collecting levies or charges from
persons described in section 3 who are engaged in the marketing of eggs,
and for such purposes classifying such persons into groups and fixing
the levies or charges payable by the members of the different groups in
different amounts, and the Commodity Board may use such levies or
charges for its purposes, including the creation of reserves, and the
payment of expenses and losses resulting from the sale or disposal of
eggs, and the equalization or adjustment among producers of eggs of
moneys realized from the sale thereof during such period or periods of
time as it may determine.
