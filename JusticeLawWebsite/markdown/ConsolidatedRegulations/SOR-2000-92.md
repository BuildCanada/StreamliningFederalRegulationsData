## Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg Marketing Levies Order {#canadian-broiler-hatching-egg-marketing-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2000-92
:::

[FARM PRODUCTS AGENCIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-4)

Registration 2000-03-09

Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg Marketing Levies Order
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council has, by the Canadian Broiler Hatching
Egg Marketing Agency Proclamation[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EGDFA}, established
the Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg Marketing Agency pursuant to
subsection 16(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EEDFA} of the [Farm
Products Agencies Act](/eng/acts/F-4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#footnotec_e){#footnotec_e-ID0EBDFA};

Whereas the Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg Marketing Agency has been
empowered to implement a marketing plan pursuant to the Canadian Broiler
Hatching Egg Marketing Agency Proclamation[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBAEA};

Whereas the proposed annexed [Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg Marketing
Levies Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-92) is an order of a class to
which paragraph 7(1)(d)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#footnoted_e){#footnoted_e-ID0EECDA} of that Act
applies by reason of section 2 of the [Agencies' Orders and Regulations
Approval Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._648)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#footnotee_e){#footnotee_e-ID0EBCDA} and has been
submitted to the National Farm Products Council pursuant to paragraph
22(1)(f) of that Act;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#footnoted_e-ID0EECDA){#footnoted_e}S.C. 1993, c.
  3, s. 7(2)
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#footnotee_e-ID0EBCDA){#footnotee_e}C.R.C., c.
  648
  :::

And whereas, pursuant to paragraph 7(1)(d)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#footnoted_e){#footnoted_e-ID0EBACA} of that Act,
the National Farm Products Council is satisfied that the proposed Order
is necessary for the implementation of the marketing plan that the
Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg Marketing Agency is authorized to
implement, and has approved the proposed Order;

Therefore, the Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg Marketing Agency, pursuant
to paragraph 22(1)(f) of the [Farm Products Agencies
Act](/eng/acts/F-4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#footnotec_e){#footnotec_e-ID0EGABA} and section 8
of the schedule to the Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg Marketing Agency
Proclamation[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg Marketing Levies
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-92).

Ottawa, Ontario, March 2, 2000

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664898 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A levy
  is hereby imposed on a producer in a signatory province, per broiler
  hatching egg produced in the province and marketed by the producer in
  interprovincial or export trade, as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Ontario, \$0.009000;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Quebec, \$0.012450;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Manitoba, \$0.014960;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of British Columbia, \$0.019400;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Saskatchewan, \$0.021400; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Alberta, \$0.021400.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A levy is imposed on a producer, dealer or hatchery
  operator in a non-signatory province of \$0.016435 per broiler
  hatching egg produced in any non-signatory province and marketed by
  that producer, dealer or hatchery operator in interprovincial trade
  into a signatory province.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) cease to have effect on June
  23, 2026.

## [Additional Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664911 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  additional charge is hereby imposed on a producer in any signatory
  province of \$0.175 per broiler hatching egg produced in that province
  and marketed by that producer in interprovincial or export trade in
  excess of that producer's interprovincial quota or export quota.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An additional charge is hereby imposed on a producer,
  dealer or hatchery operator in a non-signatory province of \$0.175 per
  broiler hatching egg produced in any non-signatory province and
  marketed by that producer, dealer or hatchery operator in
  interprovincial trade into a signatory province in excess of their
  orderly marketing quota or authorization under subsection 3(3) of the
  [Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg and Chick Orderly Marketing
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-283).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) apply in addition to any
  measures or sanctions imposed under the [Farm Products Agencies
  Act](/eng/acts/F-4) or any order or regulation of the Agency.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-19, s. 2

## [Collection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664918 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Agency hereby appoints
the Commodity Board of each signatory province to collect on behalf of
the Agency the levy imposed by subsection 2(1) and the additional
charges imposed by subsection 3(1) on producers in that province.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  producer in a signatory province markets broiler hatching eggs in
  interprovincial trade to a dealer or hatchery operator, the dealer or
  hatchery operator shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} collect the levies imposed on the producer by
    subsection 2(1) by deducting the amount of the levies from the money
    payable by the dealer or hatchery operator to the producer; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} remit the levies to the Commodity Board of that
    province not later than 15 days after the end of the month in which
    the broiler hatching eggs are marketed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the levies imposed on a producer in a signatory
  province by subsection 2(1) are not collected and remitted in
  accordance with subsection (1), the producer shall remit the levy to
  the Commodity Board of that province not later than 15 days after the
  end of the month in which the broiler hatching eggs are marketed.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A producer in a signatory
province shall remit to the Commodity Board of that province the
additional charges imposed on the producer by subsection 3(1) not later
than seven days after the broiler hatching eggs are marketed.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A producer, dealer or
hatchery operator in a non-signatory province shall remit to the Agency
the levies imposed by subsection 2(2) and the additional charges imposed
by subsection 3(2) on the producer, dealer or hatchery operator not
later than seven days after the broiler hatching eggs are marketed.

## [Statement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664927 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** When a producer, dealer or
hatchery operator remits levies or additional charges to the Agency or
to a Commodity Board under any of sections 5 to 7, the producer, dealer
or hatchery operator shall complete, certify and forward to the Agency
or the Commodity Board, as a statement that sets out the following
information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the producer, dealer or
  hatchery operator on whom the levies or additional charges were
  imposed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of broiler hatching eggs marketed by the
  producer, dealer or hatchery operator;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount of levies or additional charges remitted;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other information that relates to the levies or
  additional charges that the Agency or the Commodity Board may direct.

## [Commodity Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664933 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A Commodity Board shall
send to the Agency a copy of the statement required by section 8 and all
the levies and additional charges received by the Commodity Board during
a week, not later than the Wednesday of the following week.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664935 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into
force on the day on which it is registered.
