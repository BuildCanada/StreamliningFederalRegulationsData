[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for brokering permit

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
brokering permit referred to in subsection 7.1(1) of the [Export and
Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19) must be made in the form provided by
the Minister, be signed by the applicant and contain the following
information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name, address, telephone number,
  email address and any facsimile number and, if the applicant is an
  organization, the name of a contact person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is a natural person, their
  citizenship and, if they are not a citizen of Canada, whether they are
  a [[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the country from which the goods or technology to be
  brokered are to be exported;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number, email address
  and any facsimile number of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the person that is selling the goods or technology,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the person that is purchasing the goods or
    technology, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any other brokers or agents or mandataries
    involved in the transaction;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the country of final destination;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number and email address
  of each consignee and, if a consignee is an organization, the name, if
  known, of a contact person;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} for each type of good or technology

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the provision of the [Brokering Control
    List](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-220) that refers to the good or
    technology and the item number assigned to it by the Guide referred
    to in the [Export Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the good or technology, including
    its purpose and technical specifications, with sufficient detail to
    disclose its true identity and in terms that avoid the use of trade
    names, technical names or general terms that do not adequately
    describe the good or technology, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if available, the quantity and the total value
    and unit value in Canadian dollars;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any available end-use certificate or
  end-use statement and a statement indicating whether the good or
  technology is destined for commercial resale or for end-use by a
  government; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} whether the good or technology is included under
  paragraph 1(b) or (c) of the [Brokering Control
  List](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-220).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2019-221_e_transform_hq_25374){#fn_SOR-2019-221_e_transform_hq_25374-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 8 of the An Act to amend the Export and Import Permits Act and
the Criminal Code (amendments permitting the accession to the Arms Trade
Treaty and other amendments), chapter 26 of the Statutes of Canada 2018,
comes into force, but if they are registered after that day, they come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
