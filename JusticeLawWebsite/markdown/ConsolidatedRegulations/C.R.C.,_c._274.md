## Saskatchewan Hog Service Charge (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#saskatchewan-hog-service-charge-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 274
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Regulations Respecting the Service Charge Imposed on Certain Persons
Engaged in the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade of Hogs
Produced in Saskatchewan
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537687 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Saskatchewan Hog Service Charge (Interprovincial and
Export) Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._274).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537689 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Natural Products Marketing
    Act, 1972 of Saskatchewan; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[assembly yard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assembly yard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means premises to which
    producers may deliver their hogs; ([parc de
    groupage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[assembly yard operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assembly yard operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the owner or
    the operator of an assembly yard; ([exploitant d'un parc de
    groupage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan Hog
    Marketing Commission established pursuant to the Act and the Plan;
    ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any member of the species [Sus
    Scrofa L.]{.otherLang lang="la"} (domestic pig) produced in
    Saskatchewan; ([porc]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan Hog Marketing
    Plan established and amended from time to time pursuant to the Act;
    ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person engaged in the
    business of processing hogs or using hogs in manufacturing;
    ([transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person engaged in producing or feeding hogs
      and making them available for slaughter and includes the employer
      of any such person,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person who, under any lease or agreement, is
      entitled to a share of the hogs produced or fed by a person
      referred to in paragraph (a), and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person who takes possession of any hogs from
      any person under any form of security for a debt;
      ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[small processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[small processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a processor who, in
    the previous calendar year, processed less than two per cent of all
    hogs slaughtered in Saskatchewan according to the reports published
    by the Department of Agriculture of Canada. ([petit
    transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537703 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of hogs in interprovincial and export trade and to
persons and property situated in the Province of Saskatchewan.

## [Rate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537705 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 6, a
service charge of \$0.50 per hog marketed for slaughter is payable to
the Commission by every producer.

## [Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537707 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  service charge payable by a producer shall be deducted from the moneys
  payable to the producer by the Commission.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every small processor who purchases hogs directly
  from a producer shall deduct the service charge payable in respect of
  those hogs from the moneys payable to the producer and remit it on a
  monthly basis to the Commission.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every assembly yard operator who conducts an auction
  of sows shall deduct the service charge payable in respect of those
  sows from the proceeds payable to the producer and remit it on a
  monthly basis to the Commission.

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537712 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to a producer who

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} holds a dispensation certificate issued pursuant to
  section 8 of the [Saskatchewan Hog Marketing (Interprovincial and
  Export) Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._272);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a hog slaughtered for his own consumption;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} sells a hog to a consumer for consumption by the
  consumer;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} sells a hog to a processor designated by the
  Commission to provide specific research data; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} markets any of the following classes of hogs:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} breeding stock,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} weanling pigs,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} feeder pigs, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} boars.
