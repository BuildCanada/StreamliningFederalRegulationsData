## [Definitions and Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1129722 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions --- Part II of Act

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in Part II of the Act.

  [[mechanical malfunction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mechanical malfunction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mechanical
      problem that reduces the safety of passengers but does not include
      a problem that is identified further to scheduled maintenance
      undertaken in compliance with legal requirements. ([défaillance
      mécanique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[required for safety purposes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[required for safety purposes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      required by law in order to reduce risk to passenger safety and
      includes required by safety decisions made within the authority of
      the pilot of the aircraft or any decision made in accordance with
      a [[safety management system]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
      subsection 101.01(1) of the [Canadian Aviation
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433) but does not include
      scheduled maintenance in compliance with legal
      requirements. ([nécessaire par souci de sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions --- Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Transportation
      Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[arrival]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[arrival]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the time when one of the
      doors of an aircraft is opened after it lands to allow passengers
      to leave the aircraft. ([arrivée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[large carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[large carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a carrier that has
      transported a worldwide total of two million passengers or more
      during each of the two preceding calendar years. ([gros
      transporteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[small carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[small carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any carrier that is
      not a large carrier. ([petit transporteur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Denial of boarding

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of these Regulations, there is a
  denial of boarding when a passenger is not permitted to occupy a seat
  on board a flight because the number of seats that may be occupied on
  the flight is less than the number of passengers who have checked in
  by the required time, hold a confirmed reservation and valid travel
  documentation and are present at the boarding gate at the required
  boarding time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of small carriers

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of these Regulations, a small carrier
  has the same obligations as a large carrier towards a passenger that
  it carries on behalf of a large carrier under a commercial agreement
  with that carrier.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1129738 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Carrier liability

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  carrier operating a flight is liable to passengers with respect to the
  obligations set out in sections 7 to 22 and 24, or, if they are more
  favourable to those passengers, the obligations on the same matter
  that are set out in the applicable tariff.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint liability

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, if one carrier carries passengers on behalf
  of another carrier under a commercial agreement, the carriers are
  jointly and severally, or solidarily, liable to those passengers with
  respect to the obligations set out in sections 7, 22 and 24, or, if
  they are more favourable to those passengers, the obligations on the
  same matter that are set out in the applicable tariff.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tickets

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The issuing carrier of a ticket is liable to
  passengers for the obligations set out in sections 5 and 6, or, if
  they are more favourable to those passengers, the obligations on the
  same matter that are set out in the applicable tariff.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons with disabilities

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations do not limit a carrier's legal obligations with respect to
  persons with disabilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Carrier's right of action

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, these Regulations do not
  remove a carrier's right of action against any other person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other regimes

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 86.11(3) of the Act, a
  passenger must not be refused compensation in accordance with these
  Regulations for an event on the grounds that they are also eligible
  for compensation for the same event under a different passenger rights
  regime.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to charter flights

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  case of a charter flight, sections 2 to 24 apply

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to a charter flight within Canada if one or more
    seats on that flight are purchased for resale to the public; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to a charter flight to or from Canada if one or
    more passengers began their itinerary in Canada and one or more
    seats on that flight are purchased for resale to the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licensee obligations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensee must include the obligation to comply with
  these Regulations in its contracts with a charterer with respect to
  flights referred to in subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Simple, clear and concise communication

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  carrier must make its terms and conditions of carriage that apply in
  the following circumstances available in simple, clear and concise
  language:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} flight delay, flight cancellation and denial of
    boarding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} lost or damaged baggage; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the assignment of seats to children who are under
    the age of 14 years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of communication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The terms and conditions referred to in
  subsection (1) must be made available on all digital platforms that
  the carrier uses to sell tickets and on all documents on which the
  passenger's itinerary appears.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information on treatment, compensation
  and recourse

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A carrier must provide information on the treatment
  of passengers and minimum compensation owed by the carrier and the
  recourse against the carrier available to passengers, including their
  recourse to the Agency, in simple, clear and concise language on all
  digital platforms that it uses to sell tickets and on all documents on
  which the passenger's itinerary appears.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hyperlink

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsections (2) and (3), a digital
  platform or a document that contains a hyperlink is considered to
  contain the information that is contained on the web page to which
  that hyperlink leads.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The following notice must be made available on all
  digital platforms that the carrier uses to sell tickets and on all
  documents on which the passenger's itinerary appears:

  "If you are denied boarding, your flight is cancelled or delayed for
  at least two hours, or your baggage is lost or damaged, you may be
  entitled to certain standards of treatment and compensation under the
  [Air Passenger Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-150).
  For more information about your passenger rights please contact your
  air carrier or visit the Canadian Transportation Agency's website.

## [Delay, Cancellation and Denial of Boarding]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1129787 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tarmac delay obligations

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  flight is delayed on the tarmac after the doors of the aircraft are
  closed for take-off or after the flight has landed, the carrier must
  provide passengers with the following, free of charge:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the aircraft is equipped with lavatories, access
    to those lavatories in working order;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} proper ventilation and cooling or heating of the
    aircraft;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if it is feasible to communicate with people
    outside of the aircraft, the means to do so; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} food and drink, in reasonable quantities, taking
    into account the length of the delay, the time of day and the
    location of the airport.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Urgent medical assistance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a passenger requires urgent medical assistance
  while the flight is delayed on the tarmac after the doors of the
  aircraft are closed for take-off or after the flight has landed, the
  carrier must facilitate access to that assistance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Passenger disembarkation

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  flight is delayed on the tarmac at an airport in Canada, the carrier
  must provide an opportunity for passengers to disembark

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} three hours after the aircraft doors have been
    closed for take-off; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} three hours after the flight has landed, or at any
    earlier time if it is feasible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Take-off imminent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, a carrier is not required to provide an
  opportunity for passengers to disembark if it is likely that take-off
  will occur less than three hours and 45 minutes after the doors of the
  aircraft are closed for take-off or after the flight has landed and
  the carrier is able to continue to provide the standard of treatment
  referred to in section 8.\

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Priority disembarkation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A carrier that allows passengers to disembark must,
  if it is feasible, give passengers with disabilities and their support
  person, service animal or emotional support animal, if any, the
  opportunity to disembark first.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply if providing an
  opportunity for passengers to disembark is not possible, including if
  it is not possible for reasons related to safety and security or to
  air traffic or customs control.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations --- situations outside
carrier's control

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  section applies to a carrier when there is delay, cancellation or
  denial of boarding due to situations outside the carrier's control,
  including but not limited to the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} war or political instability;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} illegal acts or sabotage;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} meteorological conditions or natural disasters that
    make the safe operation of the aircraft impossible;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} instructions from air traffic control;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a [[NOTAM]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
    subsection 101.01(1) of the [Canadian Aviation
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a security threat;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} airport operation issues;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a medical emergency;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a collision with wildlife;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a labour disruption within the carrier or within an
    essential service provider such as an airport or an air navigation
    service provider;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a manufacturing defect in an aircraft that reduces
    the safety of passengers and that was identified by the manufacturer
    of the aircraft concerned, or by a competent authority; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} an order or instruction from an official of a state
    or a law enforcement agency or from a person responsible for airport
    security.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Earlier flight disruption

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A delay, cancellation or denial of boarding that is
  directly attributable to an earlier delay or cancellation that is due
  to situations outside the carrier's control, is considered to also be
  due to situations outside that carrier's control if that carrier took
  all reasonable measures to mitigate the impact of the earlier flight
  delay or cancellation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When there is delay, cancellation or denial of
  boarding due to situations outside the carrier's control, it must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide passengers with the information set out in
    section 13;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a delay of three hours or more,
    provide alternate travel arrangements or a refund, in the manner set
    out in section 18, to a passenger who desires such arrangements;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a cancellation, provide alternate
    travel arrangements or a refund, in the manner set out in section
    18; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a denial of boarding, provide
    alternate travel arrangements in the manner set out in section 18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations when required for safety
purposes

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 10(2), this
  section applies to a carrier when there is delay, cancellation or
  denial of boarding that is within the carrier's control but is
  required for safety purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Earlier flight disruption

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A delay, cancellation or denial of boarding that is
  directly attributable to an earlier delay or cancellation that is
  within that carrier's control but is required for safety purposes, is
  considered to also be within that carrier's control but required for
  safety purposes if that carrier took all reasonable measures to
  mitigate the impact of the earlier flight delay or cancellation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delay

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a delay, the carrier must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide passengers with the information set out in
    section 13;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a passenger is informed of the delay less than
    12 hours before the departure time that is indicated on their
    original ticket, provide the standard of treatment set out in
    section 14; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the delay is a delay of three hours or more,
    provide alternate travel arrangements or a refund, in the manner set
    out in section 17, to a passenger who desires such arrangements.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a cancellation, the carrier must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide passengers with the information set out in
    section 13;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a passenger is informed of the cancellation less
    than 12 hours before the departure time that is indicated on their
    original ticket, provide the standard of treatment set out in
    section 14; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide alternate travel arrangements or a refund,
    in the manner set out in section 17.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Denial of boarding

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a denial of boarding, the carrier must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide passengers affected by the denial of
    boarding with the information set out in section 13;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} deny boarding in accordance with section 15 and
    provide the standard of treatment set out in section 16 to

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide alternate travel arrangements or a refund,

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a delay, the carrier must communicate

  time for the flight is set or alternate travel arrangements have been
  made for the affected passenger.\

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The carrier must communicate to passengers any new
  information as soon as feasible.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in subsection (1) must be
  provided by means of audible announcements and, upon request, by means

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standards of treatment

## [Assignment of Seats to Children under the Age of 14 Years]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1129995 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assigning seats

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  order to facilitate the assignment of a seat to a child who is under
  the age of 14 years in close proximity to a parent, guardian or tutor
  in accordance with subsection (2), a carrier must, at no additional
  charge

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assign a seat before check-in to the child that is
    in close proximity to their parent, guardian or tutor; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the carrier does not assign seats in accordance
    with paragraph (a), do the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} advise passengers before check-in that the
      carrier will facilitate seat assignment of children in close
      proximity to a parent, guardian or tutor at no additional charge
      at the time of check-in or at the boarding gate,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} assign seats at the time of check-in, if
      possible,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if it is not possible to assign seats at the
      time of check-in, ask for volunteers to change seats at the time
      of boarding, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if it is not possible to assign seats at the
      time of check-in and no passenger has volunteered to change seats
      at the time of boarding, ask again for volunteers to change seats
      before take-off.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proximity to adult's seat

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The carrier must facilitate the assignment of a seat
  to a child who is under the age of 14 years by offering, at no
  additional charge,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a child who is four years of age or
    younger, a seat that is adjacent to their parent, guardian or
    tutor's seat;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a child who is 5 to 11 years of age,
    a seat that is in the same row as their parent, guardian or tutor's
    seat, and that is separated from that parent, guardian or tutor's
    seat by no more than one seat; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a child who is 12 or 13 years of
    age, a seat that is in a row that is separated from the row of their
    parent, guardian or tutor's seat by no more than one row.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Difference in price

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a passenger is assigned a seat in accordance with
  subsection (2) that is in a lower class of service than their ticket
  provides, the carrier must reimburse the price difference between the
  classes of service, but if the passenger chooses a seat that is in a
  higher class of service than their ticket provides, the carrier may
  request supplementary payment representing the price difference
  between the classes of service.

## [Baggage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1130012 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lost or damaged baggage

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in cases where the [Carriage by Air

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in cases where the [Carriage by Air

    payable by the carrier in accordance with the Convention for the
    Unification of Certain Rules for International Carriage by Air set
    out in Schedule VI to that Act, if the carrier were conducting
    international carriage of baggage within the meaning of paragraph 1
    of Article 1 of that Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary loss

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If baggage is lost for 21 days or less, the carrier
  must provide compensation equal to or greater than the sum of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fees paid for that baggage,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in cases where the [Carriage by Air
    Act](/eng/acts/C-26) applies, the compensation payable in accordance
    with that Act, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in cases where the [Carriage by Air
    Act](/eng/acts/C-26) does not apply, the amount that would be
    payable by the carrier for delay in the carriage of baggage in
    accordance with the Convention for the Unification of Certain Rules
    for International Carriage by Air set out in Schedule VI to that
    Act, if the carrier were conducting international carriage of
    baggage within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Article 1 of that
    Convention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Musical instruments

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  carrier must establish terms and conditions with regard to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} musical instruments that may be carried in the
    cabin or that must be checked, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} restrictions with respect to size and weight,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} restrictions with respect to quantity, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the use of stowage space in the cabin;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fees for transporting instruments; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} passenger options if, because a flight will occur
    on a different aircraft than expected, there is insufficient stowage
    space in the cabin.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to carry

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A carrier must accept musical instruments as checked
  or carry-on baggage, unless accepting an instrument is contrary to
  general terms and conditions in the carrier's tariff with respect to
  the weight or dimension of baggage or to safety.

## [Advertising]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1130035 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this section and sections 27 to 31.

[[air transportation charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[air transportation charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation
    to an air service, every fee or charge that must be paid upon the
    purchase of the air service, including the charge for the costs to
    the carrier of providing the service, but excluding any third party
    charge. ([frais du transport aérien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[third party charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[third party charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to an
    air service or an optional incidental service, any tax or prescribed
    fee or charge established by a government, public or airport
    authority or agent or mandatary of a government or public or airport
    authority, that upon the purchase of the service is collected by the
    carrier or other seller of the service on behalf of the government,
    public or airport authority or the agent or mandatary for remittance
    to it. ([somme perçue pour un tiers]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[total price]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[total price]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to an air service, the total of the
      air transportation charges and third party charges that must be
      paid to obtain the service; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to an optional incidental service,
      the total of the amount that must be paid to obtain the service,
      including all third party charges. ([prix total]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 86.1(2) of the Act

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 86.1(2) of the Act and sections 25 to 31, a prescribed fee or
charge is one that is fixed on a per person or [ad valorem]{.otherLang
lang="la"} basis.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), sections
  28 to 31 apply to advertising in all media of prices for air services
  within, or originating in, Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 28 to 31 do not apply to an advertisement
  that relates to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an air cargo service;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a package travel service that includes an air
    service and any accommodation, surface transportation or
    entertainment activity that is not incidental to the air service; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a price that is not offered to the general public
    and is fixed through negotiation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Medium to advertise

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 28 to 31 do not apply to a person who
  provides another person with a medium to advertise the price of an air
  service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information in advertisement

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who advertises the price of an air service must include the
  following information in the advertisement:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total price that must be paid to the advertiser
    to obtain the air service, expressed in Canadian dollars and, if it
    is also expressed in another currency, the name of that currency;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the point of origin and point of destination of the
    service and whether the service is one-way or round-trip;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any limitation on the period during which the
    advertised price will be offered and any limitation on the period
    for which the service will be provided at that price;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and amount of each tax, fee or charge
    relating to the air service that is a third party charge;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} each optional incidental service offered for which
    a fee or charge is payable and its total price or range of total
    prices; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any published tax, fee or charge that is not
    collected by the advertiser but must be paid at the point of origin
    or departure by the person to whom the service is provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Third party charges

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who advertises the price of an air service
  must set out all third party charges under the heading "Taxes, Fees
  and Charges" unless that information is only provided orally.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Air transportation charges

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who makes a reference to an air
  transportation charge in an advertisement must set the charge out
  under the heading "Air Transportation Charges" unless that information
  is only provided orally.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}One direction of round-trip service

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person who advertises the price of one direction of
  a round-trip air service is exempt from the application of paragraph
  (1)(a) if the following conditions are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the advertised price is equal to 50% of the total
    price that must be paid to the advertiser to obtain the service;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is clearly indicated that the advertised price
    relates to only one direction of the service and applies only if
    both directions are purchased; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the advertised price is expressed in Canadian
    dollars and, if it is also expressed in another currency, the name
    of that other currency is specified.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Readily obtainable information

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person is exempt from the requirement to provide
  the information referred to in paragraphs (1)(d) to (f) in their
  advertisement if the following conditions are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the advertisement is not interactive; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the advertisement mentions a location that is
    readily accessible where all the information referred to in
    subsection (1) can be readily obtained.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Total price readily determinable

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** A person must not provide
information in an advertisement in a manner that could interfere with
the ability of anyone to readily determine the total price that must be
paid for an air service or for any optional incidental service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distinction --- tax and charges

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** A person must not set out
an air transportation charge in an advertisement as if it were a third
party charge or use the term "tax" in an advertisement to describe an
air transportation charge.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Name of third party charge

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** A person must not refer
to a third party charge in an advertisement by a name other than the
name under which it was established.
