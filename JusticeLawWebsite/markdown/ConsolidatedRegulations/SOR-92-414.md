## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945488 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Fees in Respect of Mail
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-414).

## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945490 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2014-272, s. 1

\]
:::

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[commercial goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods imported into
Canada for sale or for any commercial, industrial, occupational,
institutional or other similar use.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-272, s. 2
:::

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945496 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4, the
fee charged for goods imported as mail is \$5 for each item of mail.

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945498 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The fee referred to in
section 3 does not apply to goods imported as mail where all the goods
in each item of mail are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} not charged with duties;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods in respect of which all duties are remitted;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} commercial goods that have an estimated value for
  duty exceeding \$3,300; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} sent by an Express Mail Service (EMS) to Canada from
  a country that is a member of the Universal Postal Union.
