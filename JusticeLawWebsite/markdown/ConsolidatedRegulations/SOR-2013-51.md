## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803220 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[casual goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods other than goods
imported for sale or for an industrial, occupational, commercial or
institutional or other like use.

## [Casual Goods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803222 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Casual goods that are
acquired in Panama are considered to originate in that country and are
entitled to the benefit of the Panama Tariff if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the marking of the goods is in accordance with the
  marking laws of Panama and indicates that the goods are the product of
  Panama or Canada; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods do not bear a mark and nothing indicates
  that the goods are not the product of Panama or Canada.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803226 .Part}

**[[Footnote

section 38 of the [Canada--Panama Economic Growth and Prosperity
Act](/eng/acts/C-7.95), chapter 26 of the Statutes of Canada, 2012,
comes into force, but if they are registered after that day, they come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
