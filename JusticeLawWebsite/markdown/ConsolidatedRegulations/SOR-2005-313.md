## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719441 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Motor Vehicle Transport
    Act](/eng/acts/M-12.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[adverse driving conditions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[adverse driving conditions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means snow,
    sleet, fog or other adverse weather or road conditions that were not
    known to a driver or a motor carrier dispatching a driver
    immediately before the driver began driving or could not reasonably
    have been known to them. ([mauvaises conditions de
    circulation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[co-driver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[co-driver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is present in a
    commercial vehicle because of having been, or being about to be, its
    driver. ([coconducteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vehicle that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is operated by a motor carrier and propelled
      otherwise than by muscular power; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is a truck, tractor, trailer or any combination
      of them that has a registered gross vehicle weight in excess of 4
      500 kg or a bus that is designed and constructed to have a
      designated seating capacity of more than 10 persons, including the
      driver. ([véhicule utilitaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cycle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cycle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cycle 1, under which on-duty time is accumulated
      over a period of 7 days; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cycle 2, under which on-duty time is accumulated
      over a period of 14 days. ([cycle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[daily log]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[daily log]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2019-165, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a driver, means a 24-hour
    period that begins at the hour designated by the motor carrier for
    the duration of the driver's cycle. ([jour ou
    journée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a federal director or a
    provincial director. ([directeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[driver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[driver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} means a person who drives a commercial vehicle;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} means, in respect of a motor carrier, a person
      employed or otherwise engaged by the motor carrier to drive a
      commercial vehicle, including a self-employed driver; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of section 98, includes a
      co-driver. ([conducteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[duty status]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[duty status]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following
    periods:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} off-duty time, other than time spent in a sleeper
      berth;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} off-duty time spent in a sleeper berth;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} driving time; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} on-duty time, other than driving
      time. ([activité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[electronic logging device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[electronic logging device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
    [[ELD]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device or technology that
    automatically records a driver's driving time and facilitates the
    recording of the driver's record of duty status, and that is
    certified by an accredited certification body under section
    79.1. ([dispositif de consignation électronique]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"} ou [DCE]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[electronic recording device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[electronic recording device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2019-165, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[emergency vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[emergency vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a fire-fighting
    vehicle, ambulance, police vehicle or other vehicle that is used for
    emergency purposes. ([véhicule de secours]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[federal director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chief of the
    Motor Carriers Division of the Department of Transport. ([directeur
    fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[home terminal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[home terminal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the place of business of
    a motor carrier at which a driver ordinarily reports for work and,
    for the purposes of recording information related to the driver's
    record of duty status, includes a temporary work site designated by
    the motor carrier. ([gare d'attache]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person designated under subsection 3(2); or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a peace officer within the meaning of section 2
      of the Criminal Code. ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[motor carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[motor carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is engaged
    in the operation of an extra-provincial bus undertaking or an
    extra-provincial truck undertaking. ([transporteur
    routier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[off-duty time]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[off-duty time]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any period other than
    on-duty time. ([heures de repos]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[oil well service vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[oil well service vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commercial
    vehicle that is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} specially constructed, altered or equipped to
      accommodate a specific service requirement associated with the oil
      or natural gas industry; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} used exclusively in the oil or natural gas
      industry for transporting equipment or materials to and from oil
      or natural gas well facilities or for servicing and repairing
      those facilities. ([véhicule de service de puits de
      pétrole]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[on-duty time]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[on-duty time]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period that begins
    when a driver begins work or is required by the motor carrier to be
    available to work, except if the driver is waiting to be assigned to
    work, and that ends when the driver stops work or is relieved of
    responsibility by the motor carrier, and

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} includes driving time and time spent by the
      driver

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} inspecting, servicing, repairing, conditioning,
        fuelling or starting a commercial vehicle,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} travelling in a commercial vehicle as a
        co-driver, when the time is not spent in the sleeper berth,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} participating in the loading or unloading of
        a commercial vehicle,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} inspecting or checking the load of a
        commercial vehicle,

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} waiting before and while a commercial vehicle
        is serviced, loaded, unloaded or dispatched,

      - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} waiting before and while a commercial vehicle
        or its load is inspected and the driver's requirements are
        assessed, and, if relevant, the time spent taking the required
        remedial actions,

      - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} waiting at an en-route point because of an
        accident or other unplanned occurrence or situation,

      - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} resting in or occupying a commercial vehicle
        for any other purpose, except

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} time counted as off-duty time in accordance
          with section 10,

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} time spent in a sleeper berth,

        - [(C)]{.lawlabel} time spent in a stationary commercial vehicle
          to satisfy the requirements of subsection 14(3), and

        - [(D)]{.lawlabel} time spent in a stationary commercial vehicle
          that is in addition to the off-duty requirements of subsection
          14(3),

      - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} performing any work for any motor carrier, and

      - [(x)]{.lawlabel} performing yard moves of a commercial vehicle
        within a terminal, depot or port and that is not on a public
        road; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not include driving time for the driver's
      personal use, if

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle is not used in the course of the
        business of the motor carrier,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle has been unloaded,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any trailers have been unhitched,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the distance travelled does not exceed 75 km
        in a day,

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the driver had recorded in the record of duty
        status the odometer reading at the beginning and at the end of
        the personal use, and

      - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the driver is not the subject of an
        out-of-service declaration under section 91. ([heures de
        service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[out-of-service declaration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[out-of-service declaration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    declaration issued by a director or inspector under section
    91. ([déclaration de mise hors service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[principal place of business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[principal place of business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the place
    or places designated by the motor carrier where records of duty
    status and supporting documents are stored. ([établissement
    principal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[record of duty status]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[record of duty status]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a record in
    which a driver records the information required under section 77 or
    82, as the case may be, for each day. ([rapport
    d'activités]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sleeper berth]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sleeper berth]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2019-165, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[supporting document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[supporting document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any one of the
    following documents or information received or prepared by a driver
    in the course of their duties or received or prepared by the motor
    carrier:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any electronic mobile communication record
      reflecting communications between a driver and a motor carrier
      transmitted through a driver call-in or fleet management system;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any payroll record, settlement sheet or
      equivalent document that indicates payments to the driver;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any government-issued document indicating the
      location of the commercial vehicle;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any reports, receipts, records or other
      documentation relating to the load of the commercial vehicle,
      including any bill of lading, itinerary, schedule or equivalent
      document that indicates the origin and destination of each trip;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any reports, receipts, records or other
      documentation relating to the servicing, repairing, conditioning,
      fuelling, inspection or rental of the commercial vehicle; and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any reports, dispatch or trip records, receipts,
      or other documentation indicating the date, time, or location of
      the commercial vehicle during a trip, including arrival and
      departure times. ([document justificatif]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Technical Standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Technical Standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Technical
    Standard for Electronic Logging Devices, April 11, 2019, published
    by the Canadian Council of Motor Transport Administrators, as
    amended from time to time, other than provision 4.5.1.11(b)(9) of
    that standard. ([norme technique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719493 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to all commercial vehicles other than the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a two or three-axle commercial vehicle being used
    for

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} transporting the primary products of a farm,
      forest, sea or lake, if the driver or the motor carrier is the
      producer of the products, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a return trip after transporting the primary
      products of a farm, forest, sea or lake, if the vehicle is empty
      or is transporting products used in the principal operation of a
      farm, forest, sea or lake;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an emergency vehicle; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a vehicle engaged in providing relief in the case
    of a public welfare emergency, as that expression is defined in
    section 5 of the [Emergencies Act](/eng/acts/E-4.5).

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2009-157, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

## [Responsibilities of Motor Carriers, Shippers, Consignees and Drivers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719515 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No motor carrier, shipper,
consignee or other person shall request, require or allow a driver to
drive and no driver shall drive if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2019-165, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} driving would be likely to jeopardize the safety or
  health of the public, the driver or the employees of the motor
  carrier;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the driver is the subject of an out-of-service
  declaration; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the driver, in doing so, would not be in compliance
  with these Regulations.

## [Travelling as a Passenger --- Off-duty Time]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719522 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** If a driver who has, at
the request of the motor carrier by whom the driver is employed or
otherwise engaged, spent time travelling as a passenger, by any mode of
transportation, to the destination at which the driver will begin
driving takes 8 consecutive hours of off-duty time before beginning to
drive, the time spent as a passenger shall be counted as off-duty time.

## [Scheduling --- Driving South of Latitude 60°N]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719524 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-719525 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Sections 12 to 29 apply
in respect of driving south of latitude 60°N.

### [Daily Driving and On-duty Time]{.HTitleText2} {#h-719527 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  motor carrier shall request, require or allow a driver to drive and no
  driver shall drive after the driver has accumulated 13 hours of
  driving time in a day.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No motor carrier shall request, require or allow a
  driver to drive and no driver shall drive after the driver has
  accumulated 14 hours of on-duty time in a day.

### [Mandatory Off-duty Time]{.HTitleText2} {#h-719531 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  motor carrier shall request, require or allow a driver to drive and no
  driver shall drive after the driver has accumulated 13 hours of
  driving time unless the driver takes at least 8 consecutive hours of
  off-duty time before driving again.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No motor carrier shall request, require or allow a
  driver to drive and no driver shall drive after the driver has
  accumulated 14 hours of on-duty time unless the driver takes at least
  8 consecutive hours of off-duty time before driving again.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No motor carrier shall request, require or allow a
  driver to drive and no driver shall drive after 16 hours of time have
  elapsed between the conclusion of the most recent period of 8 or more
  consecutive hours of off-duty time and the beginning of the next
  period of 8 or more consecutive hours of off-duty time.

### [Daily Off-duty Time]{.HTitleText2} {#h-719536 .Subheading}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  motor carrier shall ensure that a driver takes and the driver shall
  take at least 10 hours of off-duty time in a day.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Off-duty time other than the mandatory 8 consecutive
  hours may be distributed throughout the day in blocks of no less than
  30 minutes each.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The total amount of off-duty time taken by a driver
  in a day shall include at least 2 hours of off-duty time that does not
  form part of a period of 8 consecutive hours of off-duty time required
  by section 13.

**\[15 reserved\]**

### [Deferral of Daily Off-duty Time]{.HTitleText2} {#h-719542 .Subheading}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Despite sections 12 and
14, a driver who is not splitting off-duty time in accordance with
section 18 or 19 may defer a maximum of 2 hours of the daily off-duty
time to the following day if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the off-duty time deferred is not part of the
  mandatory 8 consecutive hours of off-duty time;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total off-duty time taken in the 2 days is at
  least 20 hours;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the off-duty time deferred is added to the 8
  consecutive hours of off-duty time taken in the second day;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total driving time in the 2 days does not exceed
  26 hours; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} there is a declaration in the record of duty status
  that states that the driver is deferring off-duty time under this
  section and that clearly indicates whether the driver is driving under
  day one or day two of that time.

### [Ferries]{.HTitleText2} {#h-719549 .Subheading}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Despite sections 13 and
14, a driver travelling by a ferry crossing that takes more than 5 hours
is not required to take the mandatory 8 consecutive hours of off-duty
time if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the time spent resting in a sleeper berth while
  waiting at the terminal to board the ferry, in rest accommodations on
  the ferry and at a rest stop that is no more than 25 km from the point
  of disembarkation from the ferry combine to total a minimum of 8
  hours;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the hours are recorded in the record of duty status
  as off-duty time spent in a sleeper berth;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the driver retains, as a supporting document, the
  receipt for the crossing and rest accommodation fees; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the supporting document coincides with the record of
  duty status entries.

### [Splitting of Daily Off-duty Time --- Single Driver]{.HTitleText2} {#h-719555 .Subheading}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  driver who is driving a commercial vehicle fitted with a sleeper berth
  may meet the mandatory off-duty time and daily off-duty time
  requirements of sections 13 and 14 by accumulating off-duty time in no
  more than 2 periods if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} neither period of off-duty time is shorter than 2
    hours;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total of the 2 periods of off-duty time is at
    least 10 hours;

- **[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  while driving the vehicle during a trip south of latitude 60°N may
  extend the permitted 13 hours of driving time specified in sections 12

  subsection 14(3) by the amount of time needed to complete the trip if

    driving conditions without the reduction.

  extend the permitted 15 hours of driving time specified in section 39

    hours;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the driver still takes the required 8 consecutive
    hours of off-duty time; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the trip could have been completed under normal
    driving conditions without the extension.

  elapsed time because of an emergency or adverse driving conditions

    otherwise became aware of the code;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a concise description of the actions taken by the
    motor carrier to repair or replace the ELD.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total number of hours of each period of duty
    status, which total must equal 24 hours.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, nothing in subsection (2)
  prevents the driver from changing the hours at the top of the grid in
  order to reflect a different start time.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The driver shall record in the record of duty status,
  as this information becomes known, the names and addresses of any
  other motor carriers by which they have been employed or otherwise
  engaged during the day.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a driver is engaged in making deliveries in a
  municipality that results in a number of periods of driving time being
  interrupted by a number of short periods of other on-duty time, the
  periods of driving time may be combined and the periods of other
  on-duty time may be combined on the grid.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A motor carrier shall require that the driver records
  at the end of each day, and the driver shall record at the end of each
  day, on the grid, the total hours for each duty status and the total
  distance driven by the driver that day, excluding the distance driven
  in respect of the driver's personal use of the vehicle, as well as the
  odometer reading at the end of the day and the driver shall sign the
  record of duty status certifying the accuracy of the information
  recorded in it.

## [Out-of-service Declarations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719836 .Part}

- **[[91]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  director or an inspector may issue an out-of-service declaration in
  respect of a driver if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the driver contravenes paragraph 4(b);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the driver fails to comply with any of the driving
    time or off-duty time requirements of sections 12 to 29 and 39 to 54
    or of a term or condition of a permit;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the driver is unable or refuses to produce their
    records of duty status in accordance with section 98;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} there is evidence that shows that the driver has
    completed more than one record of duty status for a day, has entered
    inaccurate information in a record of duty status or has falsified
    information in a record of duty status;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the driver has falsified, mutilated, obscured,
    altered, deleted, destroyed or defaced a record of duty status or a
    supporting document in such a way that the director or inspector
    cannot determine whether the driver has complied with the driving
    time and off-duty time requirements of sections 12 to 29 and 39 to
    54 or of a term or condition of a permit; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the driver uses an ELD that has a disabled,
    deactivated, disengaged, jammed or otherwise blocked or degraded
    signal transmission or reception, or uses an ELD that has been
    re-engineered, reprogrammed or otherwise tampered with so that it
    does not accurately record and retain the data that is required to
    be recorded and retained, in such a way that the director or
    inspector is unable to determine whether the driver has complied
    with the driving time and off-duty time requirements of sections 12
    to 29 and 39 to 54 or of a term or condition of a permit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The director or inspector shall notify the driver and
  the motor carrier in writing of the reason that the driver has been
  made the subject of an out-of-service declaration and the period
  during which it applies.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An out-of-service declaration applies

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for 10 consecutive hours, if the driver contravenes
    paragraph 4(b);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for 10 consecutive hours, if the driver contravenes
    section 12;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for 8 consecutive hours, if the driver contravenes
    sections 13 or 39;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for 72 consecutive hours, if the driver contravenes
    section 86; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} for the number of hours needed to correct the
    failure, if the driver fails to comply with the off-duty time
    requirements of any of sections 14 to 29 and 41 to 54 or of a term
    or condition of a permit or with the requirements of section 98.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The out-of-service declaration in respect of a driver
  who contravenes section 86 continues to apply beyond the 72 hours
  until the driver rectifies the record of duty status, if applicable,
  and provides it to the director or inspector so that the director or
  inspector is able to determine whether the driver has complied with
  these Regulations.

## [SCHEDULE 1]{.scheduleLabel}[(Paragraphs 18(1)(c.1), 19(1)(c), 41(1)(c.1) and 42(1)(c) and subsection 97(3))]{.OriginatingRef}[Sleeper Berths]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-719877 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** An area of a commercial vehicle is a sleeper berth if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is designed to be used as sleeping
    accommodation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is located in the cab of the commercial vehicle
    or immediately adjacent to the cab and is securely fixed to it;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is not located in or on a semi-trailer or a full
    trailer;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if it is located in the cargo space, it is securely
    compartmentalized from the remainder of the cargo space;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a bus,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is located in the passenger compartment,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it is at least 1.9 m in length, 60 cm in width
      and 60 cm in height,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} it is separated from the passenger area by a
      solid physical barrier that is equipped with a door that can be
      locked,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} it provides privacy for the occupant, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} it is equipped with a means to significantly
      limit the amount of light entering the area;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a commercial vehicle other than a
    bus, it is rectangular in shape with at least the following
    dimensions:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 1.9 m in length, measured on the centre line of
      the longitudinal axis,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 60 cm in width, measured on the centre line of
      the transverse axis, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 60 cm in height, measured from the sleeping
      mattress to the highest point of the area;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} it is constructed so that there are no impediments
    to ready entrance to or exit from the area;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} there is a direct and readily accessible means of
    passing from it into the driver's seat or compartment;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is protected against leaks and overheating from
    the vehicle's exhaust system;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} it is equipped to provide heating, cooling and
    ventilation within the range of household temperatures;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} it is sealed against dust and rain;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} it is equipped with a mattress that is at least 10
    cm thick; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} it is equipped with a means of preventing ejection
    of the occupant during deceleration of the commercial vehicle, the
    means being designed, installed and maintained to withstand a total
    force of 2 700 kg applied toward the front of the vehicle and
    parallel to the longitudinal axis of the vehicle.
