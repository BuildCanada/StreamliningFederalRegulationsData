Whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that the projects
referred to in section 4 of the annexed Regulations belong to classes of
projects that will have insignificant environmental effects;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development and the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to subsection
63(1) and paragraph 63(2)(b) of the [First Nations Oil and Gas and
Moneys Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.9)[[Footnote

Assessment Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-272).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737142 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [First Nations Oil and Gas
      and Moneys Management
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11.9). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[assessment by a review panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[assessment by a review panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      environmental assessment conducted in accordance with sections 40
      to 42. ([examen par une commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[comprehensive study]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[comprehensive study]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an environmental
      assessment conducted in accordance with sections 23 to 26. ([étude
      approfondie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[follow-up program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[follow-up program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
      project, means a program for

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} verifying the accuracy of the project's
        environmental assessment; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determining the effectiveness of any measures
        taken to mitigate the adverse environmental effects of the
        project. ([programme de suivi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[mediation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mediation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an environmental
      assessment conducted with the assistance of a mediator in
      accordance with sections 35 to 38. ([médiation]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[mitigation measures]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mitigation measures]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
      project, means the elimination, reduction or control of the
      adverse environmental effects of the project, and includes
      restitution for any damage to the environment caused by those
      effects through replacement, restoration, compensation or any
      other means. ([mesures d'atténuation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[proponent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[proponent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a project, means
      the person, body or government that proposes the
      project. ([promoteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any documentary material,
      regardless of medium or form. ([document]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[registry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[registry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a first nation,
      means a public registry established by the first nation under
      section 49. ([registre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[screening]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[screening]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an environmental
      assessment conducted in accordance with sections 13 and
      14. ([examen préalable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[test drilling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[test drilling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an evaluation well or
      test hole drilled for the primary purpose of determining
      subsurface lithology and obtaining geological or geophysical
      information. ([sondage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Precautionary principle

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the administration of a first nation's oil and gas
  law made in relation to environmental assessment, the decision-making
  authority and the council of the first nation shall exercise their
  powers in a manner that protects the environment and human health and
  applies the precautionary principle.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to consult if council not
  decision-making authority

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the council of the first nation is not the
  decision-making authority under the first nation's oil and gas law,
  the council of the first nation shall, before exercising any power
  referred to in paragraph 8(b), section 9 or 10, paragraph 11(1)(e),
  section 31, paragraph 34(1)(a), subsection 35(2), paragraph 39(1)(a)
  or subsection 40(2), consult with the decision-making authority, in
  addition to any other persons or bodies with whom it has a duty to
  consult under those provisions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of oil and gas laws

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 63(1) of the Act, a first nation's oil and gas law made in
relation to environmental assessment shall include the rules set out in
sections 1, 3 and 5 to 54, with any adaptations of form and reference
that will facilitate the incorporation of those rules into the first
nation's oil and gas law.

## [Exploration Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737165 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exploration activities

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph (b) of the definition "project" in subsection 2(1) of the Act,
any proposed geophysical examination or test drilling is a project for
the purposes of environmental assessments.

## [Exempted Projects]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737168 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exempted projects

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A first nation may, in its
oil and gas law, exempt from environmental assessment any class of
project that is set out in the [Exclusion List Regulations,
2007](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-108), other than those listed in
Schedules 2 and 3 to those Regulations, that relates to installations
used for oil or gas exploration or exploitation.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737171 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing of assessment

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** If an environmental
assessment of a project is required, the decision-making authority shall
ensure that it is conducted as early as is practicable in the planning
stages of the project and before irrevocable decisions are made by the
decision-making authority.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No power, etc., to be exercised until
assessment is complete

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A decision-making authority
shall not permit a project, for which an environmental assessment is
required, to proceed unless it makes a decision under paragraph
16(1)(a), 28(1)(a) or (b) or 44(1)(a) or (b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Environmental assessment process

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The environmental
assessment process shall include, if applicable,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a screening or comprehensive study and the
  preparation of a screening report or comprehensive study report;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a mediation or an assessment by a review panel and
  the preparation of a mediation report or a review panel report; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the design and implementation of a follow-up program.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scope of project

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The scope of the project
for which an environmental assessment is to be conducted shall be
determined

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by the decision-making authority; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the project is referred to a mediator or a review
  panel, by the council of the first nation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Same assessment for related projects

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
conducting an environmental assessment in respect of two or more
projects, the decision-making authority or, if at least one of the
projects is referred to a mediator or a review panel, the council of the
first nation may determine that the projects are so closely related that
they can be considered to form a single project.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}All proposed undertakings to be
considered

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** If a project concerns an
installation used for oil or gas exploration or exploitation, the
environmental assessment shall be conducted for any construction,
operation, modification, decommissioning, abandonment or other
undertaking during the life-cycle of that installation that is proposed
by the proponent as well as any construction, operation, modification,
decommissioning, abandonment or other undertaking during the life-cycle
of that installation that, in the opinion of the decision-making
authority or, if the project is referred to a mediator or a review
panel, of the council of the first nation, is likely to be carried out
in relation to that installation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors that must be considered

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  environmental assessment of a project shall consider the following
  factors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the environmental effects of the project, including
    the environmental effects of malfunctions or accidents that may
    occur in connection with the project, and any cumulative
    environmental effects that are likely to result from the project in
    combination with other projects or activities that have been or will
    be undertaken;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the significance of those environmental effects;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any comments from the public that are received in
    accordance with the first nation's oil and gas law;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} technically and economically feasible measures that
    would mitigate any significant adverse environmental effects of the
    project; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other matter relevant to a screening,
    comprehensive study, mediation or assessment by a review panel, such
    as the need for the project and alternatives to the project, whose
    consideration may be required by the decision-making authority or,
    except in the case of a screening, the council of the first nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors that may be considered

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The environmental assessment may also consider
  community knowledge and aboriginal traditional knowledge.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Source of information

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Any available information
may be used in conducting the screening or comprehensive study of a
project but, if a decision-making authority is of the opinion that the
information available is not adequate to enable it to make a decision,
it shall ensure that any studies and information that it considers
necessary for that purpose are undertaken or collected.

## [Screening Process]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737201 .Part}

### [Screening]{.HTitleText2} {#h-737202 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Screening

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  decision-making authority shall ensure that a screening of every
  project is conducted and a screening report is prepared.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a project referred
  to in section 4 that the first nation has exempted under its oil and
  gas law or to a project set out in the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scope of factors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In respect of a screening, the scope of the factors
  to be considered under paragraphs 11(1)(a), (b) and (d) and subsection
  11(2) shall be determined by the decision-making authority.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public participation

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** If there are
circumstances surrounding a project that would make the project of
interest to the public, the decision-making authority

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall, before providing the public with an
  opportunity to examine and comment on the screening report, post on
  the Internet site referred to in section 49 a description of the scope
  of the project and either a description of the factors to be
  considered in the screening and their scope, or an indication of how a
  copy of the description of those factors and their scope may be
  obtained;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall, before making a decision under section 16,
  give the public an opportunity to examine and comment on the screening
  report and any record relating to the project that has been included
  in the registry and shall give adequate notice of that opportunity;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} may, at any stage of the screening, give the public
  any other opportunity to participate in and comment on the screening.

### [Decision of the Decision-making Authority After Screening]{.HTitleText2} {#h-737215 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time for decision

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A decision-making
authority shall not make any decision under subsection 16(1) before the
15th day after the day on which the following are posted on the Internet
site referred to in section 49:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the notice of the commencement of the environmental
  assessment process;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the description of the scope of the project; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the decision-making authority gives the public an
  opportunity to examine and comment on the screening report and any
  record relating to the project that has been included in the registry,
  the description of the factors to be considered in the environmental
  assessment and their scope, or an indication of how a copy of the
  description may be obtained.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision-making authority decision ---
screening

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  considering the screening report and any comments from the public and
  taking into account the implementation of any technically and
  economically feasible mitigation measures, the decision-making
  authority shall make one of the following decisions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the project is not likely to cause significant
    adverse environmental effects;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the project is likely to cause significant adverse
    environmental effects that may be justified in the circumstances;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the project is likely to cause significant adverse
    environmental effects that cannot be justified in the circumstances;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} it is uncertain that the project is likely to cause
    significant adverse environmental effects; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} public concerns warrant a referral to a mediator or
    a review panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mitigation measures --- extent of
  authority

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The mitigation measures that may be taken into
  account are those measures

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whose implementation the decision-making authority
    can ensure; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the decision-making authority is satisfied
    will be implemented by another person or body.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Undertaking project, implementing
mitigation measures

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** If a decision-making
authority makes a decision under paragraph 16(1)(a), it may exercise any
power or perform any duty or function that would permit the project to
be undertaken in whole or in part, and it shall ensure that any
mitigation measures that it has taken into account and that are referred
to in paragraph 16(2)(a) are implemented and that a follow-up program,
if appropriate for the project, is designed and implemented.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Referral to mediator or to a review panel

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** If the decision-making
authority makes a decision under paragraph 16(1)(b), (d) or (e), it
shall refer the project to the council of the first nation, which shall
refer it to either a mediator or a review panel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** If a decision-making
authority makes a decision under paragraph 16(1)(c), it shall not
exercise any power or perform any duty or function that would permit the
project to be undertaken in whole or in part.

## [Comprehensive Study Process]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737239 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-737240 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specific projects

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Sections 21 to 30 apply
to a project set out in the schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public consultation

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  decision-making authority shall ensure that there is public
  consultation in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proposed scope of the project for the purposes
    of the environmental assessment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the factors proposed to be considered in the
    environmental assessment and their proposed scope; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the ability of a comprehensive study to address
    issues relating to the project.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report and recommendation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} After the public consultation, as soon as the
  decision-making authority has sufficient information to do so, it
  shall, if the council of the first nation is not the decision-making
  authority,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} report to the council of the first nation in
    respect of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the scope of the project,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the factors to be considered in the
      environmental assessment and their scope,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} public concerns in relation to the project,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the potential of the project to cause adverse
      environmental effects, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the ability of a comprehensive study to address
      issues relating to the project; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} recommend to the council of the first nation that
    it refer the project to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the decision-making authority to ensure that a
      comprehensive study is conducted and that a comprehensive study
      report is prepared and provided to the council of the first
      nation, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a mediator or a review panel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Referral of project

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The council of the first
nation --- taking into account the report and recommendation of the
decision-making authority if the council of the first nation is not the
decision-making authority, or taking into account the matters referred
to in subparagraphs 21(2)(a)(i) to (v) if the council of the first
nation is the decision-making authority and has sufficient information
to do so --- shall decide

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the council of the first nation is not the
  decision-making authority, to refer the project to the decision-making
  authority so that a comprehensive study is conducted and that a
  comprehensive study report is prepared and provided to the council of
  the first nation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the council of the first nation is the
  decision-making authority, to ensure that a comprehensive study is
  conducted and that a comprehensive study report is prepared; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to refer the project to a mediator or a review panel.

### [Comprehensive Study]{.HTitleText2} {#h-737265 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional factors

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** In addition to the
factors that shall be considered under subsection 11(1) and may be
considered under subsection 11(2), each comprehensive study shall
consider the following factors:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the project;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} alternative means of carrying out the project that
  are technically and economically feasible and the environmental
  effects of those alternative means;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the requirements of the follow-up program for the
  project; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the capacity of renewable resources that are likely
  to be significantly affected by the project to meet the needs of the
  present and those of the future.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public participation

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** If the council of the
first nation has decided under paragraph 22(a) or (b) that a
comprehensive study is to be conducted with respect to a project, the
decision-making authority shall ensure that the public is provided with
an opportunity, in addition to that provided under section 26, to
participate in the comprehensive study.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scope of factors

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** In respect of a
comprehensive study, the scope of the factors to be considered under
paragraphs 11(1)(a), (b) and (d), subsection 11(2) and paragraphs 23(b)
to (d) shall be determined by the decision-making authority.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public notice

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of a comprehensive study report, the decision-making authority
  shall publish, in an appropriate manner, a notice setting out the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the comprehensive study report
    will be available to the public;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the place at which copies of the report may be
    obtained; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the deadline and address for filing comments on the
    conclusions and recommendations and any other aspect of the report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public concerns

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before the deadline any person may file, at the
  address set out in the notice, comments relating to the conclusions
  and recommendations and any other aspect of the comprehensive study
  report.

### [Decision of the Decision-making Authority After Comprehensive Study]{.HTitleText2} {#h-737284 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time for decision

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** A decision-making
authority shall not make a decision under section 28 before the 30th day
after the day on which the following are posted on the Internet site
referred to in section 49:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the notice of the commencement of the environmental
  assessment process;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the description of the scope of the project;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the notice of the decision of the council of the
  first nation under paragraph 22(a) or (b) to refer the project to the
  decision-making authority to ensure that a comprehensive study is
  conducted;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the description of the factors to be considered in
  the environmental assessment and their scope, or an indication of how
  a copy of the description may be obtained; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the comprehensive study report that is to be
  considered by the decision-making authority or a description of how a
  copy of it may be obtained.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision by decision-making authority ---
comprehensive study

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  council of the first nation has decided under paragraph 22(a) or (b)
  that a comprehensive study is to be conducted with respect to a
  project, the decision-making authority shall, after considering the
  comprehensive study report and any comments filed in accordance with
  subsection 26(2) and taking into account the implementation of any
  technically and economically feasible mitigation measures, make one of
  the following decisions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the project is not likely to cause significant
    adverse environmental effects;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the project is likely to cause significant adverse
    environmental effects that can be justified in the circumstances; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the project is likely to cause significant adverse
    environmental effects that cannot be justified in the circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mitigation measures --- extent of
  authority

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The mitigation measures that may be taken into
  account are those measures

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whose implementation the decision-making authority
    can ensure; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the decision-making authority is satisfied
    will be implemented by another person or body.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Undertaking project, implementing
mitigation measures

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** If a decision-making
authority makes a decision under paragraph 28(1)(a) or (b), it may
exercise any power or perform any duty or function that would permit the
project to be undertaken in whole or in part, and it shall ensure that
any mitigation measures that it has taken into account and that are
referred to in paragraph 28(2)(a) are implemented and that a follow-up
program is designed and implemented.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** If a decision-making
authority makes a decision under paragraph 28(1)(c), it shall not
exercise any power or perform any duty or function that would permit the
project to be undertaken in whole or in part.

## [Referral Power]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737306 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Council referral to mediation or review
panel

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** The council of the first
nation may, at any time before a decision under section 16 or 22 is
made, refer a project to a mediator or a review panel if the council of
the first nation determines that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} taking into account the implementation of any
  technically and economically feasible mitigation measures, the project
  may cause significant adverse environmental effects; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} public concerns warrant a referral to a mediator or a
  review panel.

## [Mediation and Panel Review Processes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737311 .Part}

### [Referral]{.HTitleText2} {#h-737312 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial referral to mediator or review
panel

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), if a
  project is to be referred to a mediator or a review panel, the council
  of the first nation shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} refer the environmental assessment relating to the
    project to a mediator or a review panel; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} refer part of that assessment to a mediator and
    part of it to a review panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent referral to mediator

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The council of the first nation may, at any time,
  refer any issue relating to an environmental assessment by a review
  panel to a mediator if the council of the first nation is of the
  opinion, after consulting with the review panel, that mediation is
  appropriate in respect of the issue.

### [Mediation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-737320 .Subheading}

Definition of [[interested party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section and sections 34 to 38, the term [[interested
  party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person or body having an
  interest in the outcome of an environmental assessment for a purpose
  that is neither frivolous nor vexatious.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition on referral to mediator

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An environmental assessment or a part of one shall
  not be referred to a mediator unless the interested parties have been
  identified and are willing to participate in the mediation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of mediator

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  project is referred to a mediator, the council of the first nation or
  any other person or body specified in an agreement for the joint
  assessment of the project referred to in paragraph 37(3)(a) of the Act
  shall, after consulting with all parties who are to participate in the
  mediation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint a mediator; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fix the terms of reference of the mediation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications of mediator

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person appointed as a mediator shall be unbiased
  and free from any conflict of interest relative to the project and
  have knowledge or experience in acting as a mediator.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to be considered

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  addition to the factors that shall be considered under subsection
  11(1) and that may be considered under subsection 11(2), each
  mediation shall consider the factors set out in section 23.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scope of factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In respect of a mediation, the scope of the factors
  set out in paragraphs 11(1)(a), (b) and (d), subsection 11(2) and
  paragraphs 23(b) to (d) shall be determined by the council of the
  first nation or any other person or body specified in an agreement for
  the joint assessment of the project referred to in paragraph 37(3)(a)
  of the Act when fixing the terms of reference of the mediation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional participants

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** The mediator may, at any
time, allow an additional interested party to participate in a
mediation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When mediation fails

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** If, at any time after an
environmental assessment or part of an environmental assessment of a
project has been referred to a mediator, the council of the first nation
or the mediator determines that the mediation is not likely to produce a
result that is satisfactory to all the participants, the council of the
first nation shall order the conclusion of the mediation and shall refer
all outstanding issues to a review panel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  mediator shall, at the conclusion of the mediation, prepare a report
  and submit it to the council of the first nation, and to the
  decision-making authority if the council of the first nation is not
  the decision-making authority.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privilege

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No evidence of or relating to a statement made by a
  mediator or a participant to the mediation during the course of and
  for the purposes of the mediation is admissible without the consent of
  the mediator or participant, in any proceeding before a review panel,
  court, tribunal, body or person with jurisdiction to compel the
  production of evidence.

### [Assessment by a Review Panel]{.HTitleText2} {#h-737347 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of members of review panel

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  project is referred to a review panel, the council of the first nation
  or any other person or body specified in an agreement for the joint
  assessment of the project referred to in paragraph 37(3)(a) of the Act
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint members of the panel, including the
    chairperson; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fix the terms of reference of the panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualification of members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person appointed as a member of the panel shall
  be unbiased and free from any conflict of interest relative to the
  project and have knowledge or experience relevant to the anticipated
  environmental effects of the project.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to be considered

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  addition to the factors that shall be considered under subsection
  11(1) and that may be considered under subsection 11(2), each
  assessment by a review panel shall consider the factors set out in
  section 23.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scope of factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In respect of an assessment by a review panel, the
  scope of the factors set out in paragraphs 11(1)(a), (b) and (d),
  subsection 11(2) and paragraphs 23(b) to (d) shall be determined by
  the council of the first nation or any other person or body specified
  in an agreement for the joint assessment of the project referred to in
  paragraph 37(3)(a) of the Act when fixing the terms of reference of
  the review panel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment by review panel

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** A review panel shall,
within its terms of reference,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the information required for an
  assessment is obtained and made available to the public;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} hold hearings in a manner that offers the public an
  opportunity to participate in the assessment;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prepare a report setting out the rationale,
  conclusions and recommendations of the panel relating to the
  environmental assessment of the project, including any mitigation
  measures and follow-up program, and a summary of any comments received
  from the public; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} submit the report to the council of the first nation,
  and to the decision-making authority if the council of the first
  nation is not the decision-making authority.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of review panel

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  review panel has the power to summon any person to appear as a witness
  before the panel and to order the witness to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} give evidence, orally or in writing; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} produce any record that the panel considers
    necessary for conducting its assessment of the project.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A review panel has the same power to enforce the
  attendance of witnesses and to compel them to give evidence and
  produce records as is vested in a court of record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement of summonses and orders

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any summons issued or order made by a review panel
  under subsection (1) shall, for the purposes of enforcement, be made a
  summons or order of the court of competent jurisdiction by following
  the usual practice and procedure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearings to be public

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A hearing by a review panel shall be public unless
  the panel is satisfied, after representations made by a witness, that
  specific, direct and substantial harm to the witness or specific harm
  to the environment would be caused by the disclosure of the evidence
  or record that the witness is ordered to give or produce under
  subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-disclosure save with consent

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Evidence and records presented to a review panel are
  privileged and shall not, without the authorization of the person,
  body or organization to whom they relate, knowingly be disclosed or
  made available by any person who has obtained the evidence or record
  if the review panel is satisfied that the evidence or record contains

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} trade secrets;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} financial, commercial, scientific or technical
    information that is confidential;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} information whose disclosure could reasonably be
    expected to result in material financial loss or gain to, or could
    reasonably be expected to prejudice the competitive position of, any
    person, body or organization;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} information whose disclosure could reasonably be
    expected to interfere with contractual or other negotiations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} information whose disclosure could reasonably be
    expected to threaten the safety of individuals;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} information where the benefit of non-disclosure
    clearly outweighs the public interest in disclosure; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} information whose disclosure would cause specific,
    direct and substantial harm to an individual.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-disclosure save with panel consent

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Evidence and records presented to a review panel are
  privileged and shall not, without the authorization of the review
  panel, knowingly be disclosed or made available by any person who has
  obtained the evidence or record if the review panel is satisfied that
  the evidence or record contains

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} information whose disclosure would cause
    substantial harm to the environment; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information whose disclosure would result in the
    public becoming aware of aboriginal traditional knowledge that a
    first nation has always treated in a confidential manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} No action or other proceeding lies or shall be
  commenced against a member of a review panel for or in respect of
  anything done or omitted to be done during the course of and for the
  purposes of the assessment by a review panel.

### [Decision of the Decision-making Authority After Mediation or Assessment by a Review Panel]{.HTitleText2} {#h-737392 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time for decision

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** A decision-making
authority shall not make any decision under subsection 44(1) before the
30th day after the day on which the report submitted by a mediator or a
review panel or a summary of it has been posted on the Internet site
referred to in section 49.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision of the decision-making authority
--- mediation or panel review

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  decision-making authority shall, after considering the report
  submitted by a mediator or a review panel and taking into account the
  implementation of any technically and economically feasible mitigation
  measures, make one of the following decisions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the project is not likely to cause significant
    adverse environmental effects;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the project is likely to cause significant adverse
    environmental effects that can be justified in the circumstances; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the project is likely to cause significant adverse
    environmental effects that cannot be justified in the circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mitigation measures --- extent of
  authority

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The mitigation measures that may be taken into
  account are those measures

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whose implementation the decision-making authority
    can ensure; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the decision-making authority is satisfied
    will be implemented by another person or body.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision-making authority to ensure
implementation of mitigation measures

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** If a decision-making
authority makes a decision under paragraph 44(1)(a) or (b), it may
exercise any power or perform any duty or function that would permit the
project to be undertaken in whole or in part, and it shall ensure that
any mitigation measures that it has taken into account and that are
referred to in paragraph 44(2)(a) are implemented and that a follow-up
program is designed and implemented.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition: proceeding with project

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** If a decision-making
authority makes a decision under paragraph 44(1)(c), it shall not
exercise any power or perform any duty or function that would permit the
project to be undertaken in whole or in part.

## [Previous Assessment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737409 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of previously conducted environmental
assessment

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  proponent of a project proposes to undertake, in whole or in part, a
  project for which an environmental assessment was previously
  conducted, the decision-making authority shall use that assessment and
  the resulting report to the extent that their use is appropriate for
  the purpose of ensuring that a screening or comprehensive study is
  conducted and a screening report or comprehensive study report is
  prepared if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the project did not proceed after the assessment
    was completed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a project that is in relation to an
    installation, the proponent proposes an undertaking in relation to
    that installation that is different from that proposed when the
    assessment was conducted; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which the project is to be undertaken
    has subsequently changed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Necessary adjustments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The decision-making authority shall ensure that any
  adjustments are made to the report that are necessary to take into
  account any significant changes in the environment and in the
  circumstances of the project and any significant new information
  relating to the environmental effects of the project.

## [Termination of Assessment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737418 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination by decision-making authority

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If at
  any time a decision-making authority decides not to make any decision
  that would enable a project that has not been referred to mediation or
  assessment by a review panel to be undertaken, it may terminate the
  environmental assessment of the project.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination by council

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If at any time a decision-making authority decides
  not to make any decision that would enable a project that has been
  referred to a mediation or an assessment by a review panel to be
  undertaken, the council of the first nation may terminate the
  environmental assessment of the project.

## [Environmental Assessment Registry]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737424 .Part}

### [Establishment of Registry]{.HTitleText2} {#h-737425 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First nation's registry

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of facilitating convenient public access to records
  relating to environmental assessments and providing notice to the
  public in a timely manner, there shall be a public registry consisting
  of an Internet site and project files, which registry shall be
  established and maintained by the first nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public notification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The first nation shall ensure that the public has
  been notified concerning the existence of the Internet site as soon as
  it is established.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The first nation shall ensure that a copy of any
  record included in the registry is provided in a timely manner on
  request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of Internet site

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** Subject to subsection
52(1), the decision-making authority or the council of the first nation,

- [(q)]{.lawlabel} any other information that the decision-making
  authority or the council of the first nation, as the case may be,
  considers appropriate, including information in the form of a list of

### [Project Files]{.HTitleText2} {#h-737453 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment and maintenance

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  respect of every project for which an environmental assessment is
  conducted, the first nation shall establish and maintain a project
  file beginning on the day on which the environmental assessment
  process commences and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ending on the day on which any follow-up program in
    respect of the project is completed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if there is no follow-up program, ending on the day
    on which the decision-making authority makes a decision under
    section 16.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of project file

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 52(1), a project file shall
  contain all records produced, collected or submitted with respect to
  the environmental assessment of the project, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all records posted on the Internet site referred to
    in section 49;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any report relating to the environmental
    assessment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any comments filed by the public in relation to the
    environmental assessment;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any records relating to the need for, design of or
    implementation of any follow-up program; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any documents requiring mitigation measures to be
    implemented.

### [Disclosure of Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-737466 .Subheading}

#### [Registry Restrictions]{.HTitleText3} {#h-737467 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Categories of information that shall not
be made available

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) to (5),
  the first nation shall not deposit any record into the registry that
  contains

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} trade secrets;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} financial, commercial, scientific or technical
    information that is confidential;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} information whose disclosure could reasonably be
    expected to result in material financial loss or gain to, or could
    reasonably be expected to prejudice the competitive position of, any
    person, body or organization;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} information whose disclosure could reasonably be
    expected to interfere with contractual or other negotiations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} information whose disclosure could reasonably be
    expected to threaten the safety of individuals;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} information that is subject to solicitor-client
    privilege;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} information whose disclosure would cause specific
    harm to the environment;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} information where the benefit of non-disclosure
    clearly outweighs the public interest in disclosure;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} information whose disclosure would cause specific,
    direct and substantial harm to an individual;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} information whose disclosure could reasonably be
    expected to result in the public becoming aware of aboriginal
    traditional knowledge that a first nation has always treated in a
    confidential manner; or

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} information that was determined to be privileged by
    a review panel in accordance with section 42.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Severing information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The first nation shall deposit any part of a record
  that does not contain, and can reasonably be severed from any part
  that contains, information set out in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Available record

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A record or part of a record otherwise available to
  the public shall be included in the registry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deposit on consent

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The first nation may deposit into the registry a
  record that contains information set out in any of paragraphs (1)(a)
  to (d) and (f) if the person, body or organization to whom that
  information relates consents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public interest override

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The first nation may deposit into the registry a
  record or part of a record that contains information set out in
  paragraph (1)(b), (c) or (d) if disclosure of the information it
  contains would be in the public interest as it relates to public
  health, public safety or protection of the environment, and the public
  interest clearly outweighs any financial loss, gain or prejudice to
  the competitive position of or interference with contractual or other
  negotiations of any person, body or organization.

#### [Notice]{.HTitleText3} {#h-737490 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of intention to deposit

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  the first nation intends to deposit in the registry a record or part
  of a record that contains or that the first nation has reason to
  believe might contain information set out in any of paragraphs
  52(1)(a) to (d) and (f), the first nation shall, subject to subsection
  (2), if the person, body or organization to whom the information
  relates can reasonably be located, before the deposit of the record or
  part of a record, give written notice to the person, body or
  organization of the intention to deposit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waiver of notice requirement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person, body or organization to whom a notice is
  required to be given under subsection (1) in respect of an intention
  to deposit a record or part of a record may waive the requirement and,
  if the person, body or organization has consented to the deposit,
  shall be deemed to have waived the requirement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the first nation giving the notice
    intends to deposit a record or part of a record that might contain
    information set out in any of paragraphs 52(1)(a) to (d) and (f);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the contents of the record or part
    of the record that, as the case may be, belong to, were supplied by
    or relate to the person, body or organization to whom the notice is
    given; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the person, body or organization
    may, within 20 days after the day on which the notice is given, make
    representations to the first nation as to why the record or part of
    the record should not be deposited.

#### [Representations of Persons, Bodies or Organizations and Decision]{.HTitleText3} {#h-737501 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time for representations, decision

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  notice is given under section 53, the first nation shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 20 days after the day on which the notice is
    given, give the person, body or organization to whom the notice was
    given the opportunity to make representations as to why the record
    or part of the record should not be deposited into the registry; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 30 days after the day on which the notice is
    given, if the person, body or organization has been given an
    opportunity to make representations under paragraph (a), make a
    decision as to whether to deposit the record or part of the record
    and give written notice of the decision to the person, body or
    organization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of representations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Representations made by a person, body or
  organization under paragraph (1)(a) shall be made in writing unless
  the first nation waives that requirement, in which case they may be
  made orally.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a facility for the liquefaction, storage or

    gas processing capacity of more than 3 000 t/d or a liquefied
    natural gas storage capacity of more than 50 000 t;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a petroleum storage facility with a capacity of
    more than 500 000 m^3^; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a liquefied petroleum gas storage facility with a
    capacity of more than 100 000 m^3^.
