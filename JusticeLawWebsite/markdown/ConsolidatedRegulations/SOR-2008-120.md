## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743004 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[electrical propulsion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[electrical propulsion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mode of
    propulsion provided by a main propelling machinery that is an
    electric motor powered by electric cells. ([propulsion
    électrique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[engine power]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[engine power]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the engine power, in
    kilowatts, calculated in accordance with International Standard ISO
    8665, Small Craft --- Marine Propulsion Engines and Systems ---
    Power Measurements and Declarations, second edition, August 1,
    1994. ([puissance motrice]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[local authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[local authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a government of a
    municipality, township, parish, county or regional district, any
    other government constituted under the laws of a province or
    territory, or a department of a provincial or territorial government
    or of the federal government. ([administration
    locale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Transport. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[personal watercraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[personal watercraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a water-jet driven
    vessel with an enclosed hull and no cockpit that is designed to be
    used by one or more persons who are straddling, sitting, standing or
    kneeling. ([motomarine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[power-driven]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[power-driven]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a vessel, means
    that the vessel is propelled by a mode of propulsion provided by a
    main propelling machinery that is an internal combustion engine or a
    steam engine. ([propulsion mécanique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[provincial authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2025-158, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[rivers and lakes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[rivers and lakes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes all those waters
    of any river upstream of a line between the most seaward extremities
    of the shore of the river at high tide but does not include the
    waters of the St. Lawrence River east of 70°53′ W
    longitude. ([fleuves, rivières et lacs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[TP 15587]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[TP 15587]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means TP 15587E, Schedules of the
    Vessel Operation Restriction Regulations, published by the
    Department of Transport, as amended from time to time. ([TP
    15587]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743014 .Part}

### [Operation of Vessels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-743015 .Subheading}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not operate a vessel in the waters described in Schedule 1 in TP
  15587, except as indicated in that Schedule.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person must not operate a vessel driven by
  electrical propulsion or a power-driven vessel in the waters described
  in Schedule 2 in TP 15587, except as indicated in that Schedule.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person must not operate a vessel driven by
  electrical propulsion that has an aggregate maximum power greater than
  7.5 kW or a power-driven vessel in the waters described in Schedule 3
  in TP 15587, except as indicated in that Schedule.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a vessel driven by electrical propulsion or a
  power-driven vessel has an engine power greater than the maximum
  engine power set out in column 4 of Schedule 4 in TP 15587, a person
  must not operate that vessel in the waters described in columns 1 to 3
  of that Schedule, except as indicated in that Schedule.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person must not operate a vessel driven by
  electrical propulsion or a power-driven vessel in the waters described
  in columns 1 to 3 of Schedule 5 in TP 15587 at a speed in excess of
  the maximum speed set out in column 4 of that Schedule, except as
  indicated in that Schedule.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A person must not operate a vessel driven by
  electrical propulsion or a power-driven vessel in the waters described
  in Schedule 6 in TP 15587 for the purpose of towing a person on water
  skis or on any other sporting or recreational equipment, except during
  any permitted hours set out in that Schedule.

- [(6.1)]{.lawlabel} A person must not operate a vessel driven by
  electrical propulsion or a power-driven vessel in the waters described
  in Schedule 7 in TP 15587 for the purpose of allowing a person to wake
  surf, except during any permitted hours set out in that Schedule.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} A person must not operate a vessel driven by
  electrical propulsion or a power-driven vessel at a speed in excess of
  10 km/h within 30 m of the shore in the following waters:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the waters of Ontario, Manitoba, Saskatchewan and
    Alberta;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the rivers and lakes in British Columbia;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Nitinat River and Nitinat Lake, upstream of Nitinat
    Bar, in British Columbia;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the rivers and lakes in Nova Scotia; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Bras d'Or Lake in Nova Scotia, inland of a line
    drawn between Coffin Point and Red Head in Great Bras d'Or Channel
    and the inland end of St. Peters Canal.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (7) does not apply

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a vessel that is operated for the
    purpose of towing a person on water skis or on any other sporting or
    recreational equipment, if the vessel follows a course away from and
    perpendicular to the shore; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a vessel that is operated

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in rivers that are less than 100 m in width or in
      canals or buoyed channels, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any waters described in Schedule 5 in TP
      15587.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (5) and (7) do not apply to a vessel that
  is required to comply with another speed limit established under the
  [Canada Shipping Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) or the [Canada Marine
  Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7).

### [Signs]{.HTitleText2} {#h-743055 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall place a
sign anywhere for the purpose of restricting the operation of any vessel
in Canadian waters unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the placement has been authorized by the Minister
  under subsection 6(1) and, except in the case of a sign that provides
  information with respect to a restriction referred to in subsection
  2(7) or section 14, the sign meets the requirements set out in the
  standard entitled Signage Requirements and Recommendations --- Vessel
  Operation Restriction Regulations, published by the Department of
  Transport, as amended from time to time; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the placement is authorized under an Act of
  Parliament other than the [Canada Shipping Act,
  2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15).

### [Operation of a Vessel for Certain Purposes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-743111 .Subheading}

- **[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, after considering
  the factors listed in subsection (1.1), issue a permit, for a period
  specified in the permit, that authorizes the person in whose name the
  permit is issued to operate a vessel in the waters referred to in any
  of subsections 2(1) to (5) and (7) in a manner that is contrary to
  those subsections for any of the following purposes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} developing aquaculture;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} conducting scientific research;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} accessing a historical, cultural, ecological or
    geological site;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} educating the public on the marine environment;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} protecting the environment;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} supporting the construction and maintenance of
    infrastructure; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} ensuring safety during activities and events other
    than those referred to in subsections 11(2) and (3).

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The factors referred to in subsection (1) are the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the protection of the public interest;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the protection of the environment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the safe and efficient navigation of vessels;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the type and size of the vessel to which the permit
    relates;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the intended use of the vessel; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the characteristics of the body of water.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall set out in the permit any
  conditions that are necessary to protect the public interest and the
  environment and to minimize risk to the safety of persons and
  interference with the safe and efficient navigation of vessels.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person in whose name the permit is issued shall
  comply with the conditions set out in the permit.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cancel the permit in either of the
  following circumstances and notify the permit holder of the
  cancellation:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the permit holder has knowingly provided false or
    misleading information to obtain the permit; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the permit holder does not comply with the
    conditions set out in the permit and the non-compliance interferes
    with the navigation of vessels or endangers the safety of persons or
    the environment.

### [Special Events or Activities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-743121 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person must not hold a sporting, recreational or public event or
  activity in waters, other than those described in Schedule 8 in TP

  event or activity during which vessels would be operated in a manner
  that is contrary to those subsections, unless the person is authorized
  to do so by a permit issued under subsection 12(1).

### [Anchorage in False Creek]{.HTitleText2} {#h-743138 .Subheading}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** In section 14,
[[anchor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes securing a vessel to a mooring
buoy or attaching it to another vessel that is anchored.

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall, unless the person holds a permit issued by the Minister,
  anchor a vessel within the waters of False Creek in the City of
  Vancouver that are east of a line drawn 45° true from Kitsilano Point
  to the north shore of False Creek

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a cumulative period of more than eight hours
    between 9:00 a.m. and 11:00 p.m.; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any time after 11:00 p.m. of one day and before
    9:00 a.m. of the following day.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall issue a permit for a maximum
  period set out in subsection (3) or (4) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the owner or operator of a vessel submits to the
    Minister a signed application providing the following information:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of days that the vessel would be
      anchored,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name and permanent address of the applicant,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the telephone number or the place where the
      applicant may be contacted during the period of the permit, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the vessel's identification;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vessel owner or operator ensures that the
    vessel is seaworthy; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} there is space available to anchor the vessel.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A permit is valid for a maximum period of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 14 days for a permit issued during the summer,
    being the period beginning on April 1 and ending on September 30;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 21 days for a permit issued during the winter,
    being the period beginning on October 1 and ending on March 31.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The owner or operator of a vessel may apply for a
  permit to anchor the vessel for a maximum period, calculated as
  follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for permits issued during the summer, 14 days less
    the number of full or partial days that the vessel was anchored
    during the 30 days before the day of the application; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for permits issued during the winter, 21 days less
    the number of full or partial days that the vessel was anchored
    during the 40 days before the day of the application.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply to the following persons
  acting within the scope of their duties:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an enforcement officer; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an employee or agent or mandatary of Her Majesty in
    right of Canada or a province, or of a county, municipality or
    regional district.

## [Safe Operation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743163 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2008-120, err., Vol. 142, No. 10

\]
:::

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who operates a vessel shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} take into account any circumstances that could pose
    a danger to the vessel or to other vessels; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} avoid endangering the safety of persons involved in
    any activity in any waters.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who operates a vessel during a sporting,
  recreational or public event or activity for which a permit has been
  issued shall do so in a manner that does not interfere with the event
  or activity.

## [Age Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743191 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Sections 19 to 21 apply
in respect of a pleasure craft that is operated in Canadian waters,
other than in the waters of the Northwest Territories and Nunavut.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-124, s. 7
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-18-20080417.html "Link to previous version of section 18"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** No person who is under 12
years of age shall operate, and no person shall allow a person who is
under 12 years of age to operate, a pleasure craft, other than a
personal watercraft, propelled by a motor with an engine power greater
than 7.5 kW, unless the person is accompanied and directly supervised in
the pleasure craft by a person who is 16 years of age or older.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-123, s. 6(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-19-20080417.html "Link to previous version of section 19"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** No person who is under 16
years of age shall operate, and no person shall allow a person who is
under 16 years of age to operate, a pleasure craft, other than a
personal watercraft, propelled by a motor with an engine power greater
than 30 kW, unless the person is accompanied and directly supervised in
the pleasure craft by a person who is 16 years of age or older.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-123, s. 6(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-20-20080417.html "Link to previous version of section 20"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** No person who is under 16
years of age shall operate, and no person shall allow a person who is
under 16 years of age to operate, a personal watercraft.
