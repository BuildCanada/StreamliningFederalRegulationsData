## Order Transferring to the Federal Economic Development Agency for Southern Ontario from the Department of Industry the Control and Supervision of that Portion of the Federal Public Administration in the Department of Industry Known as Canada Business Ontario {#order-transferring-to-the-federal-economic-development-agency-for-southern-ontario-from-the-department-of-industry-the-control-and-supervision-of-that-portion-of-the-federal-public-administration-in-the-department-of-industry-known-as-canada-business-ontario .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2013-64
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2013-06-19

Order Transferring to the Federal Economic Development Agency for
Southern Ontario from the Department of Industry the Control and
Supervision of that Portion of the Federal Public Administration in the
Department of Industry Known as Canada Business Ontario

P.C. 2013-639 2013-06-05
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBCAA},
transfers to the Federal Economic Development Agency for Southern
Ontario from the Department of Industry the control and supervision of
that portion of the federal public administration in the Department of
Industry known as Canada Business Ontario, effective June 6, 2013.
