## China Direct Shipment Condition Exemption Order {#china-direct-shipment-condition-exemption-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/85-156
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1985-02-08

Order Respecting the Exemption of Goods that Originate in the People's
Republic of China from the Direct Shipment Condition

P.C. 1985-360 1985-02-07
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 3.1(1.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFBAA} of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), is pleased hereby to make the annexed Order
respecting the exemption of goods that are the product of the People's
Republic of China from the direct shipment condition, effective March 1,
1985.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EFBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1983-84, c. 22, s.
  1
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886124 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [China Direct Shipment Condition Exemption
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-156).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-76
:::

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886128 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3, an
exemption is hereby granted from the condition of direct shipment in
paragraph 40(b) of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) with respect
to goods that originate in the People's Republic of China.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-76
:::

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886132 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Exemption pursuant to
section 2 is granted on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods are imported into Canada and released and
  accounted for under the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) after
  January 1, 1988;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods were shipped through Hong Kong on a through
  bill of lading to a consignee in Canada; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the importer submits to the Minister of National
  Revenue any documentation requested by the Minister relating to the
  exemption.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-76
- SOR/92-685, s. 2
