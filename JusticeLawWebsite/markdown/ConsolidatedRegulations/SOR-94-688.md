Registration 1994-11-07

Patented Medicines Regulations

P.C. 1994-1819  1994-11-01
::::

::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Health and Welfare, pursuant to section
101[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-94-688_e_hq_4151){#fn_SOR-94-688_e_hq_4151-ID0EJCAA}
of the [Patent Act](/eng/acts/P-4), is pleased hereby to revoke the
[Patented Medicines Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-688), made by
Order in Council P.C. 1988-2013 of September 15, 1988[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-94-688_e_hq_4152){#fn_SOR-94-688_e_hq_4152-ID0EDCAA},
and to make the annexed Regulations specifying the information to be
provided relating to patented medicines and patentees' revenues and
research and development expenditures, in substitution therefor.

## [Information Respecting the Identity and Price of Medicines]{.HTitleText1} {#h-966035 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraphs 80(1)(a) and (2)(a) of the Act, information
  identifying the medicine shall be accompanied by the product monograph
  for the medicine or, if a notice of compliance has not been issued in
  respect of the medicine, by information similar to that contained in a
  product monograph, and shall indicate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the rights holder or former
    rights holder and their address for correspondence in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the reporting rights holder referred to in
    paragraph (a) is a patent holder, a holder of a certificate of
    supplementary protection, a person holding a licence other than a
    licence continued by subsection 11(1) of the Patent Act Amendment
    Act, 1992, or any other person referred to in the definition
    [[rights holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 79(1) of the
    Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the generic name and brand name of the medicine;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether the medicine is for human or veterinary
    use;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the therapeutic use of the medicine approved by the
    Minister of Health;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the first notice of compliance
    was issued to the rights holder or former rights holder in respect
    of the medicine;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the drug identification number assigned to each
    strength and dosage form of the medicine under the [Food and Drug
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the patent number of each invention of the patentee
    or former patentee pertaining to the medicine, the date on which
    each patent was granted and the date on which each patent will
    expire; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for each certificate of supplementary protection
    that has been issued in respect of each invention pertaining to the
    medicine of the rights holder or former rights holder:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of the certificate of supplementary
      protection,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the certificate takes effect
      and the date on which it expires, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the number of the patent set out in the
      application for the certificate of supplementary protection.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information required under subsection (1) shall
  be provided if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a notice of compliance has been issued in respect
    of the medicine; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the medicine is being offered for sale in Canada.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in subsection (1) shall
  be provided no later than the earlier of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} seven days after the day on which the first notice
    of compliance is issued in respect of the medicine; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} seven days after the day on which the medicine is
    first offered for sale in Canada.

- [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (3), in each of the following
  cases, the information referred to in subsection (1) shall be provided
  within 30 days after the day on which the Board sends a request for
  the rights holder to provide that information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the medicine is not a [[prescription
    drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section A.01.010 of the
    [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) and is
    not a drug described in Schedule D to the [Food and Drugs
    Act](/eng/acts/F-27);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the medicine contains a [[controlled
    substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of
    the [Controlled Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8), the
    sale or provision of which does not require a prescription under
    that Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a notice of compliance has been issued in respect
    of the medicine on the basis of information and material contained
    in a submission filed under section C.08.002.1 of the [Food and Drug
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the medicine is for veterinary use.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in subsection (1) shall
  be up to date and any change to that information shall be reported
  within 30 days after the day on which the change is made, including by
  providing the date on which any additional term granted for the patent
  -- as set out in the corresponding certificate of additional term that
  is made available on the website of the Canadian Intellectual Property
  Office under subsection 117.04(3) of the [Patent
  Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-423) or set out in an order made under
  subsection 46.4(2) of the Act -- will expire.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraphs 80(1)(b) and (2)(b) of the Act, information
  identifying the medicine and concerning the price of the medicine
  shall indicate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the rights holder or former rights
    holder;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the generic name and brand name of the medicine;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the medicine is first sold in
    Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the day or period, referred to in subsection (2) or
    (3), to which the information pertains;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the drug identification number assigned under the
    [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) in
    respect of the medicine or, if no drug identification number has
    been assigned, any other identification number assigned in respect
    of each dosage form and strength of the medicine of the rights
    holder or former rights holder; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the day or period referred to in
    paragraph (d),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the medicine sold in final dosage
      form and either the average price per package or the net revenue
      from sales in respect of each dosage form, strength and package
      size in which the medicine was sold by the rights holder or former
      rights holder to each class of customer in each province and
      territory,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the publicly available ex-factory price for each
      dosage form, strength and package size in which the medicine was
      sold by the rights holder or former rights holder to each class of
      customer in each province and territory, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the medicine is being sold in one or more of
      the countries set out in the schedule, the publicly available
      ex-factory price for each dosage form, strength and package size
      in which the medicine was sold to each class of customer in each
      of those countries.

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2008-70, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in subsection (1) shall
  be provided to the Board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the day on which the medicine is first sold in
    Canada, within 30 days after that day; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each six-month period beginning on January 1
    and July 1 in a year, within 30 days after the end of the period.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), in each of the following
  cases, the information referred to in subsection (1), for each
  six-month period beginning on January 1 and July 1 of each year, shall
  be provided within 30 days after the day on which the Board sends a
  request for the rights holder to provide that information and, during
  the two years following the request, within 30 days after the end of
  each six-month period:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the medicine is not a [[prescription
    drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section A.01.010 of the
    [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) and is
    not a drug described in Schedule D to the [Food and Drugs
    Act](/eng/acts/F-27);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the medicine contains a [[controlled
    substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of
    the [Controlled Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8), the
    sale or provision of which does not require a prescription under
    that Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a notice of compliance has been issued in respect
    of the medicine on the basis of information and material contained
    in a submission filed under section C.08.002.1 of the [Food and Drug
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the medicine is for veterinary use.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subparagraph (1)(f)(i),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in calculating the average price per package of
    medicine, the actual price after any reduction given as a promotion
    or in the form of rebates, discounts, refunds, free goods, free
    services, gifts or any other benefit of a like nature and after the
    deduction of the federal sales tax shall be used; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in calculating the net revenue from sales of each
    dosage form, strength and package size in which the medicine was
    sold in final dosage form, the actual revenue after any reduction in
    the form of rebates, discounts, refunds, free goods, free services,
    gifts or any other benefit of a like nature and after the deduction
    of federal sales taxes shall be used.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (6), this section does not
  apply to medicine sold by a rights holder or former rights holder to a
  person with whom they do not deal at arm's length or to another rights
  holder or former rights holder.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the rights holder or former rights holder sells
  the medicine to a person with whom they do not deal at arm's length
  and who is not required to provide information under paragraph
  80(1)(a) or (2)(a) of the Act, the rights holder or former rights
  holder shall provide the information required under paragraph (1)(f)
  in respect of any resale of the medicine by the person.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subparagraph (1)(f)(iii), the
  price at which a medicine was sold in a country other than Canada
  shall be expressed in the currency of that country.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), as that Act read on December 1, 1987, applies,
  with any modifications that the circumstances require, in determining
  whether a rights holder or former rights holder is dealing at arm's
  length with another person.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, [[publicly
  available ex-factory price]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any price of
  a patented medicine or medicine protected by a certificate of
  supplementary protection that is agreed on by the rights holder or
  former rights holder and the appropriate regulatory authority of the
  country in which the medicine is sold by the rights holder.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2008-70, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

## [Revenues and Research and Development Expenditures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-966082 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 88(1) of the Act, information concerning the
  identity of any licensee in Canada of the rights holder and the
  revenues and research and development expenditures of the rights
  holder shall indicate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the rights holder and their
    address for correspondence in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of all licensees in Canada of
    the rights holder;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total gross revenues from all sales in Canada
    during the year by the rights holder of medicine for human and
    veterinary use and the total revenues received from all licensees
    from the sale in Canada of medicine for human and veterinary use;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a summary of all expenditures made during the year
    by the rights holder towards the cost of research and development
    relating to medicine for human or veterinary use carried out in
    Canada by or on behalf of the rights holder, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the type of research and
      development and the name of the person or entity that carried out
      the research and development,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the expenditures of the rights holder or the
      person or entity that carried out the research and development, in
      respect of each type of research and development, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the province in which the research
      and development was carried out and the expenditures in that
      province by the rights holder or the person or entity.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in subsection (1) shall
  be provided for each calendar year and shall be submitted within 60
  days after the end of each calendar year.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The total gross revenues referred to in paragraph
  (1)(c) shall comprise revenues from sales of medicine

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for which a drug identification number has been
    issued under the [Food and Drug
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) or which has been
    approved for sale to qualified investigators under those
    Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is used in the diagnosis, treatment,
    mitigation or prevention of a disease, disorder or abnormal physical
    state or the symptoms thereof or in the modification of organic
    functions in humans or animals; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the sale of which is promoted by any means to
    physicians, dentists, veterinarians, hospitals, drug retailers or
    wholesalers or manufacturers of ethical pharmaceutical products.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(d), the rights
  holder shall specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total capital expenditures on buildings and the
    annual depreciation of the buildings which depreciation shall be
    calculated at an annual rate of four per cent for a maximum of 25
    years;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total capital expenditures on equipment; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the source and amount of the funds for expenditures
    made by the rights holder towards the cost of research and
    development.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 88(1) of the Act, the expression [[research and
development]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means those activities for which
expenditures qualify, or would qualify if the expenditures were made by
a taxpayer in Canada, for an investment tax credit in respect of
scientific research and experimental development under the [Income Tax
Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) as that Act read on December 1, 1987.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-966104 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person required by these Regulations to provide information to the
  Board shall do so by using the appropriate electronic document made
  available on the Board's website and by sending the completed
  electronic document, in its original format and file type, to the
  email address specified by the Board on its website.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The electronic document shall bear the electronic
  signature of an authorized individual, certifying that the information
  set out in the document is true and complete.
