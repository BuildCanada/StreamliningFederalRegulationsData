## Smithers Airport Zoning Regulations {#smithers-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-916
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1981-11-09

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Smithers Airport

P.C. 1981-3181 1981-11-05
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 6 of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to approve the annexed
[Regulations respecting zoning at Smithers
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-916) made by the Minister of Transport.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872661 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Smithers Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-916).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872663 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Smithers Airport, near
      the town of Smithers, in the Province of British Columbia;
      ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part III of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport including the runway prepared for the
      take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction, which
      strip is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule;
      ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part VI of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the assigned
  elevation of the airport reference point is 519 m above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872674 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, that consist of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lands within, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lands directly under that portion of an approach
  surface that extends beyond

the outer limits described in Part II of the schedule.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872679 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point any

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} approach surface;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} transitional surface.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872684 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in section 4, the Minister may make a
direction that the owner or occupier of the land on which that object is
growing remove the excessive growth.

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872686 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit that land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste edible by or attractive
to birds.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point is a punch mark on the down bolt at the
North East Corner of the Rotating Beacon, and having U.T.M. co-ordinates
of 6,076,470.969 North, 616,611.411 East.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Department of Transport Smithers Airport
Zoning Plan No. B.C. 1384, are surfaces abutting each end of the strip
associated with the runway designated 14-32 and are described as
follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 14 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  intersection with the outer surface; THENCE such approach surface
  shall be horizontal and contiguous to said outer surface to an
  intersection with an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles
  to the projected centre line of the strip at its intersection with the
  northerly boundary of the South East 1/4 of Section 26, Township 1A,
  Range 5, Coast District; THENCE said approach surface shall slope
  upward at the above specified slope of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m
  measured horizontally to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right
  angles to the projected centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m
  measured horizontally from the end of the strip, the outer ends of the
  imaginary horizontal line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line
  and 804.3 m, measured vertically, above the assigned elevation of the
  airport reference point; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 32 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  intersection with the outer surface; THENCE such approach surface
  shall be horizontal and contiguous to said outer surface to an
  intersection with an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles
  to the projected centre line of the strip at its intersection with the
  southerly boundary of Lot U, Plan 6127 of District Lot 865, Range 5,
  Coast District; THENCE said approach surface shall slope upward at the
  above specified slope of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured
  horizontally to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to
  the projected centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured
  horizontally from the end of the strip, the outer ends of the
  imaginary horizontal line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line
  and 799.7 m, measured vertically, above the assigned elevation of the
  airport reference point.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Department of Transport Smithers Airport
Zoning Plan dated January 31, 1980, is an imaginary surface located at a
common plane established at a constant elevation of 45 m above the
assigned elevation of the airport reference point except where that
common plane is less than 9 m above the surface of the ground, the
imaginary surface is located 9 m above the surface of the ground.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-the-strip .SchedHeadL1}

The strip, shown on Department of Transport Smithers Airport Zoning Plan
dated January 31, 1980, is described as a strip associated with
runway 14-32 being 300 m in width, 150 m being on each side of the
centre line of the runway, and 1 948.8 m in length.

### [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-videscription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Department of Transport Smithers
Airport Zoning Plan dated January 31, 1980, is a surface consisting of
an inclined plane having a ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 7 m
measured horizontally at right angles to the centre line and centre line
produced of the strip and extending upward and outward from the lateral
limits of the strip and its approach surfaces to an intersection with
the outer surface.
