## Employment Equity Programs Regulations {#employment-equity-programs-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-30b
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33)

Registration 1988-12-27

Regulations Respecting the Appointment of Target Group Members to
Positions Within the Employment Equity Programs During the Period
Beginning on January 1, 1989 and Ending on March 31, 1993

P.C. 1988-2798 1988-12-22
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Public Service Commission has decided that it is not
practicable nor in the best interests of the Public Service

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to apply section 10 and subsection 12(3) of the
  [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) to positions
  identified pursuant to the employment equity programs to which target
  group members coming from outside the Public Service are to be
  appointed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to apply subsection 12(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) in relation to selection standards
  other than language skills to training positions identified pursuant
  to the employment equity programs to which members of a target group
  coming from outside the Public Service are to be appointed;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to apply section 10 and subsection 12(3) of the
  [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) to positions other
  than those identified pursuant to the employment equity programs to
  which target group members, who were appointed to the Public Service
  for a specified period to a training position identified pursuant to
  the employment equity programs, are to be appointed; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to apply subsection 21(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) to persons who would otherwise have
  a right to appeal under that subsection against the appointment of
  target group members, who were appointed to the Public Service for a
  specified period to a training position identified pursuant to the
  employment equity programs, to positions other than those identified
  pursuant to the employment equity programs;

during the period beginning on January 1, 1989 and ending on March 31,
1993.

Whereas the Public Service Commission is of the opinion that it is
desirable to make the annexed Regulations respecting the appointment of
target group members to positions within the employment equity programs
during the period beginning on January 1, 1989 and ending on March 31,
1993.

Whereas the Public Service Commission recommends that, pursuant to
subsection 41(2) of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), the Governor in Council approve the revocation
by the Public Service Commission of the Northern Careers Exclusion of
Native Persons Approval Order, C.R.C., c. 1347;

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 37(1) of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), the Public Service Commission recommends that
the Governor in Council revoke the Northern Careers Appointments
Regulations, C.R.C., c. 1346;

Whereas the Public Service Commission recommends that, pursuant to
subsection 41(2) of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), the Governor in Council approve the revocation
by the Public Service Commission of the exclusion of certain provisions
of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) for the
purposes of the Visible Minority Employment Program during the period
beginning on December 22, 1986 and ending on March 31, 1989 approved by
Order in Council P.C. 1986-2859 of December 18, 1986[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EBBEA} and in consequence
thereof to revoke the said Order in Council;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EBBEA){#fn_1e}SOR/87-22, 1987 [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 229
  :::

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 37(1) of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), the Public Service Commission recommends that
the Governor in Council revoke the Visible Minority Employment Program
Regulations, P.C. 1986-2859 of December 18, 1986[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EBADA};

Whereas the Public Service Commission recommends that, pursuant to
subsection 41(1) of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), the Governor in Council make in substitution
therefor the annexed [Order approving the exclusion by the Public
Service Commission of certain persons and positions from the operation
of certain provisions of the Public Service Employment Act for the
purposes of the employment equity programs during the period beginning
on January 1, 1989 and ending on March 31,
1993](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-30a);

And whereas, pursuant to subsection 37(1) of the [Public Service
Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), the Public Service Commission
recommends that the Governor in Council make the annexed [Regulations
respecting the appointment of target group members to positions within
the employment equity programs during the period beginning on January 1,
1989 and ending on March 31, 1993](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-30b).

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Secretary of State, is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to subsection 41(2) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), to approve the revocation by the
  Public Service Commission of Northern Careers Exclusion of Native
  Persons Approval Order, C.R.C., c. 1347;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to subsection 37(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), to revoke the Northern Careers
  Appointment Regulations, C.R.C., c. 1346;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to subsection 41(2) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), to approve the revocation by the
  Public Service Commission of the exclusion of certain provisions of
  the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) for the
  purposes of the Visible Minority Employment Program during the period
  beginning on December 22, 1986 and ending on March 31, 1989 approved
  by Order in Council P.C. 1986-2859 of December 18, 1986[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EBADAA} and in consequence
  thereof to revoke the said Order in Council;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to subsection 37(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), to revoke the Visible Minority
  Employment Program Regulatoins, P.C. 1986-2859 of December 18,
  1986[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EBACAA};

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to subsection 41(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), to make in substitution therefor
  the annexed Order approving the exclusion by the Public Service
  Commission of certain persons and positions from the operation of
  certain provisions of the Public Service Employment Act for the
  purposes of the employment equity programs during the period beginning
  on January 1, 1989 and ending on March 31, 1993; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to subsection 37(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), to make the annexed [Regulations
  respecting the appointment of target group members to positions within
  the employment equity programs during the period beginning on
  January 1, 1989 and ending on March 31,
  1993](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-30b).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919389 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Employment Equity Programs
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-30b).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919391 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[aboriginal peoples]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[aboriginal peoples]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in the Order; ([autochtones]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[disabled persons]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[disabled persons]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in the Order; ([personnes handicapées]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[employment equity programs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employment equity programs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in the Order; ([programmes d'équité en matière
      d'emploi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[members of visible minority groups]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[members of visible minority groups]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the
      same meaning as in the Order; ([membres des minorités
      visibles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Employment Equity
      Programs Exclusion Approval Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-30a);
      ([décret]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[target group members]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[target group members]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
      as in the Order. ([membres des groupes-cibles]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} All words and expressions used in these Regulations
  and not defined in subsection (1) have the same meaning as in the
  [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) and the [Public
  Service Employment Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-334).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919401 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
persons and positions to which the Order applies.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919403 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may appoint a target group member coming from outside the
  Public Service to a position identified pursuant to the employment
  equity programs for which, in the opinion of the Commission, that
  person is qualified.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may appoint a target group member
  coming from outside the Public Service to a training position
  identified pursuant to the employment equity programs that, in the
  opinion of the Commission, is commensurate with that person's
  capacities.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may appoint for an indeterminate
  period a target group member who was appointed to the Public Service
  for a specified period to a training position identified pursuant to
  the employment equity programs, to a position other than one
  identified pursuant to the employment equity programs for which, in
  the opinion of the Commission, that person is qualified.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where there is any
inconsistency between these Regulations and the [Public Service
Employment Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-334), these
Regulations prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.
