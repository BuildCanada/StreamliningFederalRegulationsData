## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1470408 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Digital Services Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/D-1.65). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[quarter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[quarter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any period of three
    consecutive months beginning on January 1, April 1, July 1 or
    October 1. ([trimestre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prescribed Rates of Interest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1470413 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest to be paid to the Receiver
General

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of every provision of the Act that requires interest at a
  prescribed rate to be paid to the Receiver General for Canada, the
  prescribed rate in effect during any particular quarter is the total
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the rate that is the simple arithmetic mean,
    expressed as a percentage per year and rounded to the next higher
    whole percentage if the mean is not a whole percentage, of all
    amounts each of which is the average equivalent yield, expressed as
    a percentage per year, of Government of Canada Treasury Bills that
    mature approximately three months after their date of issue and that
    are sold at auctions of Government of Canada Treasury Bills during
    the first month of the quarter preceding the particular quarter, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 4%.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest to be paid by the Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of every provision of the Act that
  requires interest at a prescribed rate to be paid or applied on an
  amount payable by the Minister to a person, the prescribed rate in
  effect during any particular quarter is the rate determined under
  paragraph (1)(a) in respect of the particular quarter.

## [Prescribed Thresholds]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1470421 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Global revenue threshold

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
Act, the amount of the "global revenue threshold" is €750,000,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}In-scope revenue threshold

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
Act, the amount of the "in-scope revenue threshold" is \$20,000,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration threshold

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of Part 6
of the Act, the amount of the "registration threshold" is \$10,000,000.

## [Prescribed Rate of Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1470428 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rate

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the
description of B in subsection 10(2) of the Act, the rate prescribed in
respect of a taxpayer is 3%.

## [Prescribed Deduction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1470431 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction amount

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of Part 4
of the Act, the "deduction amount" is \$20,000,000.
