## [Surtax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1517842 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}25% surtax

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 2, the following goods that originate in the United States
  are subject to a surtax in the amount of 25% of the value for duty
  determined in accordance with sections 47 to 55 of the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods that are classified under any of the tariff
    items set out in Schedule 1 or 2;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods that are classified under any of the tariff
    items set out in Schedule 3 that are otherwise classifiable under a
    tariff item set out in Schedule 1; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} goods that are classified under any of the tariff
    items set out in Schedule 4 that are otherwise classifiable under a
    tariff item set out in Schedule 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Goods that originate in the United
  States

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), goods originate
  in the United States if they are eligible to be marked as goods of the
  United States in accordance with the [Determination of Country of
  Origin for the Purpose of Marking Goods (CUSMA Countries)
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-23).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following goods are not
subject to the surtax:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods that are classified under a tariff item of
  Chapter 98 or 99 of the List of Tariff Provisions that is not set out
  in Schedule 3, even if the goods are otherwise classifiable under a
  tariff item set out in Schedule 1;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} goods that are classified under a tariff item of
  Chapter 98 or 99 of the List of Tariff Provisions that is not set out
  in Schedule 4, even if the goods are otherwise classifiable under a
  tariff item set out in Schedule 2;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods that are in transit to Canada on the day on
  which this Order comes into force; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} goods that are imported from the United States at a
  port of entry on Campobello Island, New Brunswick, if the goods

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are imported by a person who ordinarily resides on
    Campobello Island and who is returning after an absence from Canada
    of less than 24 hours,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are in the person's possession or form part of
    their baggage, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} are for personal or household use.
