## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1250683 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[United Nations Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[United Nations Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Convention on the Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations
    set out in Schedule III to the [Foreign Missions and International
    Organizations Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention des Nations
    Unies]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Vienna Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Vienna Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Vienna
    Convention on Diplomatic Relations set out in Schedule I to the
    [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention de Vienne]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1250688 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Senior officials

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson of the African Union, the Chairperson of the African Union
  Commission, the Deputy Chairperson of the African Union Commission and
  the African Union Permanent Representative to the United States of
  America have, to the extent required for the exercise of their
  functions,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the privileges and immunities comparable to the
    privileges and immunities accorded to diplomatic agents under the
    Vienna Convention, other than duty and tax relief privileges; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the same immunities and facilities in respect of
    their personal baggage as are accorded to diplomatic agents under
    paragraph 2 of Article 36 of the Vienna Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other officials

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Other officials of the African Union have, to the
  extent required for the exercise of their functions, the privileges
  and immunities set out in paragraphs 18(a) and (c) to (f) of Article V
  of the United Nations Convention, subject to paragraph 20 of that
  Article.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Experts

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Experts who perform missions for the African Union
  have, to the extent required for the exercise of their functions, the
  privileges and immunities set out in Article VI of the United Nations
  Convention.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1250698 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
