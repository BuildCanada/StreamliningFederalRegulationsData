## Order Raising the Limit on the Amount that the Canadian Broadcasting Corporation may Expend Under a Lease or Other Rental Agreement {#order-raising-the-limit-on-the-amount-that-the-canadian-broadcasting-corporation-may-expend-under-a-lease-or-other-rental-agreement .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2002-351
:::

[BROADCASTING ACT](/eng/acts/B-9.01)

Registration 2002-09-24

Order Raising the Limit on the Amount that the Canadian Broadcasting
Corporation may Expend Under a Lease or Other Rental Agreement

P.C. 2002-1582 2002-09-24
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Canadian Heritage and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
paragraph 48(2)(b) of the [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBBAA}, hereby
prescribes \$15,000,000 as the amount of the expenditure referred to in
that paragraph in excess of which the Canadian Broadcasting Corporation
shall not, without the approval of the Governor in Council, enter into a
lease or other agreement for the use or occupation of real property.
