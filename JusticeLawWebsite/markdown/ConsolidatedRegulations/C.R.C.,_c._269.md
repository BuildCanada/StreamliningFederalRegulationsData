## Saskatchewan Egg Order {#saskatchewan-egg-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 269
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Eggs Produced in Saskatchewan
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537501 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Saskatchewan Egg Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._269).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537503 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Natural Products Marketing Act
    of Saskatchewan; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Saskatchewan
    Commercial Egg Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to
    the Act; ([Office de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eggs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eggs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any shell eggs of the domestic
    hen offered for sale for human consumption and produced in
    Saskatchewan; ([oeufs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Saskatchewan Commercial Egg
    Producers' Marketing Plan established and amended from time to time
    pursuant to the Act. ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537509 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized to regulate the marketing of eggs in interprovincial and
export trade and for such purposes may, with respect to persons and
property situated within the Province of Saskatchewan, exercise all or
any powers like the powers exercisable by it in relation to the
marketing of eggs locally within that Province under the Act and the
Plan.

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537511 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/80-90\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the powers granted to it under section
  3 with respect to the marketing of eggs in interprovincial and export
  trade,

to fix, impose and collect levies or charges from persons referred to in
section 3 who are engaged in the marketing of eggs, and for such purpose
to classify such persons into groups and fix the levies or charges
payable by the members of the different groups in different amounts, to
use such levies or charges for its purposes, including the creation of
reserves, the payment of expenses and losses resulting from the sale or
disposal of eggs, and the equalization or adjustment among producers of
eggs of moneys realized from the sale thereof during such period or
periods of time as it may determine.
