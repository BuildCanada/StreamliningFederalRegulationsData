## Designated Public Office Holder Regulations {#designated-public-office-holder-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2008-117
:::

[LOBBYING ACT](/eng/acts/L-12.4)

Registration 2008-04-17

Designated Public Office Holder Regulations

P.C. 2008-770 2008-04-17
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the President of the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection
2(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_610542-e_hq_2915){#fn_610542-e_hq_2915-ID0EIDAA}
and paragraph 12(c.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_610542-e_hq_2916){#fn_610542-e_hq_2916-ID0EGDAA}
of the [Lobbying Act](/eng/acts/L-12.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_610542-e_hq_2917){#fn_610542-e_hq_2917-ID0EDDAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Designated Public Office Holder
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2008-117).

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph (c) of the definition [[designated public office
holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the [Lobbying
Act](/eng/acts/L-12.4), the positions and classes of positions set out
in the schedule are designated as positions occupied by a designated
public office holder.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on July 2, 2008.
