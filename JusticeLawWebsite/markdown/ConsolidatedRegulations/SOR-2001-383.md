## [Exemption from Restrictions on Investments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673645 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed circumstances --- banks and
bank holding companies

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraphs 468(3)(d)(ii) and 930(3)(d)(ii) of the [Bank
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), subsections 468(4) to (6) of the Act do not
apply in determining whether a bank would be permitted to acquire a
substantial investment in an entity under subsection 466(2),
paragraph 466(3)(b) or (c) or subsection 466(4) or 468(1) or (2) of the
Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed circumstances --- foreign
banks and entities associated with foreign banks

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subparagraph 522.08(2)(d)(ii) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01),
subsection 522.22(1) of the Act does not apply in determining whether a
foreign bank or an entity associated with a foreign bank would be
permitted to acquire or hold control of, or a substantial investment in,
a Canadian entity under section 522.07, 522.08, any of
paragraphs 522.1(a) or (c) to (e) or Division 8 of Part XII of the Act.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673652 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBCA}[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
paragraphs 474(a) and (b) and 522.23(b) of the [Bank
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), as enacted by sections 127 and 132 of the
[Financial Consumer Agency of Canada Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1), chapter 9
of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, come into force.
