Order Declaring that the Regulations Respecting Reduction in the Release

## [Declaration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1501564 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Except with respect to a
federal work or undertaking, the [Regulations Respecting Reduction in
the Release of Methane and Certain Volatile Organic Compounds (Upstream
Oil and Gas Sector)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-66) do not apply in
Saskatchewan.

## [Cessation of Effect]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1501567 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of agreement

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order ceases to have
effect on the day on which the agreement between the Minister of the
Environment and the Government of Saskatchewan entitled "Agreement on
the Esouquivalency of Federal and Saskatchewan Regulations Respecting
the Release of Methane from the Oil and Gas Sector in Saskatchewan,
2025" terminates or is terminated under subsection 10(8) of the
[Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31).

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1501570 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The [Order Declaring that
the Provisions of the Regulations Respecting Reduction in the Release of
Methane and Certain Volatile Organic Compounds (Upstream Oil and Gas
Sector) Do Not Apply in
Saskatchewan](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-234)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_81000-2-5466_hq_1711501295){#fn_81000-2-5466_hq_1711501295-ID0EBBA}
is repealed.
