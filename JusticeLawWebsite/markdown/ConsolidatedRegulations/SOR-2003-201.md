[EXCISE ACT, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1)

Registration 2003-06-05

Regulations Respecting the Information to be Displayed on Alcohol
Containers and their Packaging

P.C. 2003-855 2003-06-05
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 304(1)(f) of the
[Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Regulations Respecting the Information to be Displayed on
Alcohol Containers and their Packaging](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-201).

  :::

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702334 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of section
87 of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), the prescribed
information to be displayed is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the alcohol licensee who
  packaged the alcohol; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} their licence number.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702338 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations take
effect on July 1, 2003.
