## Saskatchewan Pulse Crop Marketing Levies (Interprovincial and Export) Order {#saskatchewan-pulse-crop-marketing-levies-interprovincial-and-export-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-701
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1987-12-07

Order Providing for the Fixing and Imposing of Levies on Pulse Crops
Produced in Saskatchewan and Marketed in Interprovincial and Export
Trade
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Saskatchewan Pulse Crop Development Board, pursuant to section 4 of
the [Saskatchewan Pulse Crop Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-403), made
by Order in Council P.C. 1987-1360 of June 30, 1987[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBBA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Order providing for the fixing and imposing of levies on pulse crops
produced in Saskatchewan and marketed in interprovincial and export
trade](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-701).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBBA){#fn_1e}SOR/87-403, 1987 [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2765
  :::

Regina, Saskatchewan, December 1, 1987
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910431 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Saskatchewan Pulse Crop Marketing Levies (Interprovincial and
Export) Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-701).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910433 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[assembler]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assembler]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who is engaged in
    the business of assembling pulse crops;
    ([ramasseur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[assembling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assembling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the acquiring of pulse
    crops from a producer or processor or other person for purposes of
    shipment; ([ramassage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[buy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[buy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the purchase of pulse crops for
    any consideration including barter or the promise of future delivery
    of pulse crops; ([achat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who buys or contracts
    to buy pulse crops produced in Saskatchewan;
    ([acheteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes buying, selling, offering
    for sale, advertising, financing, assembling, shipping, grading,
    insuring, packing, processing or transporting;
    ([placement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person operating a farm in Saskatchewan who is
      engaged in the production of pulse crops and includes the employer
      of any such person,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who under any lease or agreement is
      entitled to a share of the pulse crops raised or the proceeds from
      their sale, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who takes possession of any pulse crops
      under any form of security or legal proceeding for a debt.
      ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910444 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  buyer, assembler or processor who buys, assembles or processes pulse
  crops shall deduct a levy of one per cent of the gross value of pulse
  crops that are marketed in interprovincial and export trade from any
  payments made to producers.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Pulse crops produced at or under the direction of any
  research institution shall be exempt from any levy under this Order.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-232, s. 1
:::

## [Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910450 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  buyer, assembler or processor, as the case may be, shall forward the
  levy deducted in accordance with subsection 3(1) to the Board not
  later than the 20th day of the month following the month in which the
  pulse crops are purchased.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each monthly remittance of levy forwarded to the
  Board in accordance with subsection (1) shall be accompanied by a list
  of the names of all producers from whom pulse crops were purchased and
  the amount of levy deducted from payments made to each producer,
  complete with the mailing addresses of those producers.
