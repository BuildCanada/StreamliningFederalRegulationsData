## Import and Export Returns Order {#import-and-export-returns-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1504
:::

[STATISTICS ACT](/eng/acts/S-19)

Order Prescribing the Time at Which and Manner in Which Import and
Export Returns Shall Be Sent
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-524525 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Import and Export Returns Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1504).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-524527 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Returns of all imports and
exports into and from Canada and details of the means of transportation
used therefor shall be sent to the Chief Statistician by mail at the
time of customs clearance of such imports and exports.
