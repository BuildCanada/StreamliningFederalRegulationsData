## Defence Production and Development Sharing Remission Order {#defence-production-and-development-sharing-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 755
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission and Refund of Taxes Imposed under the
Excise Tax Act, Other than the Tax under Part IX, in Respect of the
Canada-United States Defence Production and Development Sharing Program
::::

::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561549 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Defence Production and Development Sharing Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._755).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561551 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 and
4, remission is hereby granted of the taxes paid or payable under the
[Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), other than the tax under Part IX, on
articles and materials that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} are imported into or purchased in Canada by persons
  carrying on business in Canada who have been awarded contracts under
  defence production and development sharing arrangements between the
  Government of Canada and the Government of the United States; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have been or are to be used solely and exclusively in
  the development and production of goods or incorporated into goods
  that have been or are to be delivered to an agency of the Government
  of the United States in connection with a defence production and
  development sharing contract.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2(E)
- SI/98-11, s. 2
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A person claiming a
remission under this Order shall produce, together with the customs
accounting document or refund claim for the articles or materials
referred to in section 2,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} verification by the Canadian Commercial Corporation
  that he has been awarded a contract referred to in paragraph 2(a); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a certificate by the importer of the articles and
  materials, in such form as the Deputy Minister of National Revenue may
  prescribe, certifying that the articles and materials have been or are
  to be used for the purposes described in paragraph 2(b).

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
- SI/98-11, s. 3
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of National
Revenue may give such instructions and directions as he deems necessary
for the purpose of carrying out the provisions of this Order.
