## In-Transit Steel Goods Remission Order {#in-transit-steel-goods-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2018-288
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 2018-12-17

In-Transit Steel Goods Remission Order

P.C. 2018-1610 2018-12-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 115[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EGCAA} of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EDCAA}, makes the annexed
In-Transit Steel Goods Remission Order.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EGCAA){#fn_a}S.C.
  2005, c. 38, par. 145(2)(j)
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b-ID0EDCAA){#fn_b}S.C.
  1997, c. 36
  :::
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to subsection (2),
remission is granted of the surtax paid or payable under the [Order
Imposing a Surtax on the Importation of Certain Steel
Goods](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-206)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_1){#fn_1-ID0EBBA} in respect of goods that were
in transit to Canada before October 25, 2018.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_1-ID0EBBA){#fn_1}SOR/2018-206
  :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the good is imported into Canada on or after October
  25, 2018; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years after the
  date of importation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
