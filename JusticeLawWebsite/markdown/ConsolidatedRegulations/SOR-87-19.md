## Pension Benefits Standards Regulations, 1985 {#pension-benefits-standards-regulations-1985 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-19
:::

[PENSION BENEFITS STANDARDS ACT, 1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01)

Registration 1986-12-18

Regulations Respecting Pension Benefits Standards

P.C. 1986-2856 1986-12-18
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsections 4(6) and 9(6) and
section 39 of the [Pension Benefits Standards Act,
1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations respecting pension benefits
standards](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), effective January 1, 1987.

  :::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-902883 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-902885 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[accepted actuarial practice]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[accepted actuarial practice]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      standards of practice described in paragraph 9(2)(b) of the Act,
      taking into account any specification made by the Superintendent
      under that paragraph; ([normes actuarielles
      reconnues]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[accountant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[accountant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person authorized to
      act as an accountant under the laws of a
      province; ([comptable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Pension Benefits Standards
      Act, 1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[actuarial gain]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[actuarial gain]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2010-149, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[actuarial report]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[actuarial report]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an actuarial report
      filed with the Superintendent under subsection 9.01(5) or 12(2) of
      the Act or a copy of the report that is provided under subsection
      9.01(6) of the Act; ([rapport actuariel]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[actuary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[actuary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2011-85, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[adjusted solvency asset amount]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[adjusted solvency asset amount]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      amount determined by multiplying the average solvency ratio by the
      amount of the solvency liabilities; ([montant rajusté de l'actif
      de solvabilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[average solvency ratio]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[average solvency ratio]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the solvency
      ratio determined in accordance with subsections 9(8) to
      (11); ([ratio de solvabilité moyen]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[book value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[book value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an asset, means
      the cost of acquisition to the person acquiring the asset,
      including all direct costs associated with the
      acquisition; ([valeur comptable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[bridging benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[bridging benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a periodic payment
      that is provided under a plan to a former member of the plan for a
      temporary period of time after the former member's retirement for
      the purpose of supplementing the former member's pension benefit
      until the former member is eligible to receive benefits under the
      [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9) or is eligible for or
      commences to receive retirement benefits under the [Canada Pension
      Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or An Act respecting the Quebec Pension
      Plan; ([prestation de raccordement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian resource property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian resource property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in paragraph 66(15)(c) of the [Income Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); ([avoirs miniers
      canadiens]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[deferred life annuity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[deferred life annuity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a life annuity
      that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} commences periodic payments no earlier than one
        year after its purchase,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provides for equal periodic payments or
        periodic payments that have been varied by reference to

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any pension payable under the
          [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9),

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any pension payable under
          either the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or a
          provincial pension plan as defined in section 3 of the [Canada
          Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8),

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Consumer Price Index for Canada as
          published by Statistics Canada under the authority of the
          [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19), or

        - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the value of the assets held in a segregated
          fund, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is issued by a person authorized to carry on a
        life insurance business in Canada; ([prestation viagère
        différée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[experience gain]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[experience gain]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2010-149, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[experience loss]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[experience loss]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2010-149, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} except in section 11.1,

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a bank or an authorized foreign bank within
          the meaning of section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01),

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a body corporate to which the [Trust and
          Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) applies,

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a cooperative credit society to which the
          [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01)
          applies,

        - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an insurance company to which the [Insurance
          Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies,

        - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a trust, loan or insurance corporation
          incorporated by or under an Act of the legislature of a
          province,

        - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a cooperative credit society incorporated
          and regulated by or under an Act of the legislature of a
          province,

        - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} an entity that is incorporated or formed by
          or under an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a
          province and that is primarily engaged in dealing in
          securities, including portfolio management and investment
          counselling, or

        - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} a foreign institution; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of section 11.1, those
        entities referred to in subparagraphs (a)(i) to (vi) or a
        foreign institution for which an order of the Superintendent has
        been made under section 574 of the [Insurance Companies
        Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8); ([institution
        financière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[foreign institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[foreign institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity that
      is

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} engaged in the business of banking, the trust,
        loan or insurance business, the business of a cooperative credit
        society or the business of dealing in securities or is otherwise
        engaged primarily in the business of providing financial
        services, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} incorporated or formed otherwise than by or
        under an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a
        province; ([institution étrangère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[going concern assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[going concern assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the value of
      the assets of a plan, including income due and accrued, determined
      on the basis of a going concern valuation; ([actif évalué en
      continuité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[going concern deficit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[going concern deficit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount by
      which the going concern liabilities exceed the going concern
      assets; ([déficit évalué en continuité]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[going concern excess]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[going concern excess]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount by
      which the going concern assets exceed the going concern
      liabilities; ([excédent évalué en continuité]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[going concern liabilities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[going concern liabilities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      present value of the accrued benefits of a plan as determined on
      the basis of a going concern valuation, including

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} amounts due and unpaid;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a negotiated contribution plan,
        a provision for adverse deviations; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other plan, a provision for
        adverse deviations, if any; ([passif évalué en
        continuité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[going concern ratio]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[going concern ratio]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the ratio of the
      going concern assets to the going concern liabilities, on the
      basis of the most recent actuarial report, excluding those going
      concern assets and going concern liabilities that are attributable
      to benefits paid by means of an insurance contract or an annuity,
      other than a revocable annuity; ([ratio de
      continuité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[going concern special payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[going concern special payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      special payment made in respect of an unfunded liability under
      subsection 9(3); ([paiement spécial de
      continuité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[going concern valuation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[going concern valuation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a valuation
      of the assets and liabilities of a plan using actuarial
      assumptions and methods that are in accordance with accepted
      actuarial practice for the valuation of a plan that is not
      expected to be terminated or wound up; ([évalué en
      continuité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[immediate life annuity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[immediate life annuity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a life
      annuity that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} commences periodic payments within one year
        after its purchase,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provides for equal periodic payments or
        periodic payments that have been varied by reference to

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any pension payable under the
          [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9),

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any pension payable under
          either the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or a
          provincial pension plan as defined in section 3 of the [Canada
          Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8),

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Consumer Price Index for Canada as
          published by Statistics Canada under the authority of the
          [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19), or

        - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the value of the assets held in a segregated
          fund, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is issued by a person authorized to carry on a
        life insurance business in Canada; ([prestation viagère
        immédiate]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[insured plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[insured plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a plan in which all
      benefits are paid by means of an annuity or insurance contract
      issued by a person authorized to carry on a life insurance
      business in Canada and under which the person is obligated to pay
      all the benefits set out in the plan; ([régime
      assuré]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[investment fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[investment fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a fund ---
      established by a corporation, limited partnership or trust --- the
      purpose of which is to invest the moneys of two or more investors
      and the shares or units of which are allocated to each investor in
      proportion to the interest of the investor in the assets of the
      fund; ([fonds de placement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[letter of credit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[letter of credit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a letter of credit
      that meets the requirements of subsection 9.1(5); ([lettre de
      crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[life income fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[life income fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registered
      retirement income fund, as defined in subsection 146.3(1) of the
      [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), that meets the requirements set
      out in section 20.1; ([fonds de revenu viager]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[locked-in registered retirement savings plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[locked-in registered retirement savings
      plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registered retirement savings
      plan, as defined in subsection 146(1) of the [Income Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), that meets the requirements set out in
      section 20; ([régime enregistré d'épargne-retraite
      immobilisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[marketplace]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[marketplace]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an exchange;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a quotation and trade-reporting system;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other entity that

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} constitutes, maintains or provides a market
          or facility for bringing together buyers and sellers of
          securities or derivatives,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} brings together the orders for securities or
          derivatives of multiple buyers and sellers, and

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} uses established, non-discretionary methods
          under which the orders interact and with which the buyers and
          sellers entering the orders agree to the terms of a
          trade; ([marché]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[market value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[market value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an asset,
      means the price that would be obtained in the purchase or sale of
      the asset in an open market under conditions requisite to a fair
      transaction between parties who are at arm's length and acting
      prudently, knowledgeably and willingly; ([valeur
      marchande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[member choice account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[member choice account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an account in
      relation to which a member, former member, survivor or former
      spouse or former common-law partner of the member or former member
      is permitted to make investment choices under a plan referred to
      in subsection 8(4.2) of the Act; ([compte accompagné de
      choix]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[mutual fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mutual fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[pooled
      fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2015-60, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[normal cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[normal cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the cost of benefits
      that are to accrue during a plan year, as determined on the basis
      of a going concern valuation, excluding special payments and
      including

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a negotiated contribution plan,
        a provision for adverse deviations of at least 5%; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other plan, a provision for
        adverse deviations, if any; ([coûts normaux]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pension
      plan; ([régime]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[plan year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[plan year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2011-85, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[prior second valuation date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prior second valuation date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in relation to
      a valuation date, means the day two years prior to that valuation
      date; ([deuxième date d'évaluation antérieure]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[prior valuation date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prior valuation date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in relation to a
      valuation date, means the day one year prior to that valuation
      date; ([date d'évaluation antérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[PRPP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[PRPP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a plan registered under section
      12 of the [Pooled Registered Pension Plans
      Act](/eng/acts/P-15.3); ([RPAC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[restricted life income fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[restricted life income fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      registered retirement income fund, as defined in subsection
      146.3(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), that meets the
      requirements set out in section 20.3; ([fonds de revenu viager
      restreint]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[restricted locked-in savings plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[restricted locked-in savings plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      registered retirement savings plan, as defined in subsection
      146(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), that meets the
      requirements set out in section 20.2; ([régime d'épargne
      immobilisée restreint]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[segregated fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[segregated fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a fund established
      by a corporation that is duly authorized to operate a fund in
      which contributions to a pension plan are deposited and the assets
      of which are held exclusively for the purposes of that plan alone
      or that plan and one or more other pension plans; ([caisse
      séparée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[simplified pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[simplified pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2015-60, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[solvency assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[solvency assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount
      determined by the formula

      ::: {#902964 .Definition}
      A + B - C

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is the market value of the assets that relate to the defined
          benefit provisions of a plan as determined at the valuation
          date,

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the face value of all letters of credit in effect on the
          valuation date, other than those being used to fund a plan
          under Part 3 of the [Solvency Funding Relief
          Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-275) or Part 3 of the
          [Solvency Funding Relief Regulations,
          2009](/eng/regulations/SOR-2009-182), up to a maximum of 15%
          of the solvency liabilities of the plan as determined at the
          valuation date, and

      [C]{.dfn}
      :   is the estimated expense of the winding-up of the plan as
          certified by an actuary; ([actif de
          solvabilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})
      :::

  [[solvency deficiency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[solvency deficiency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount by
      which the solvency liabilities exceed the adjusted solvency asset
      amount; ([déficit de solvabilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[solvency excess]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[solvency excess]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount by which
      the adjusted solvency asset amount exceeds the solvency
      liabilities; ([excédent de solvabilité]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[solvency liabilities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[solvency liabilities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the liabilities
      of a plan that relate to defined benefit provisions and are
      determined on the basis that the plan is terminated; ([passif de
      solvabilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[solvency ratio]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[solvency ratio]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a plan that is a defined contribution plan
        that does not have defined benefit provisions, and an insured
        plan, one; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for any other plan, the ratio of the solvency
        assets to the solvency liabilities, excluding those solvency
        assets and solvency liabilities that are attributable to
        benefits that are paid by means of an annuity, other than a
        revocable annuity, or an insurance contract, based on the most
        recent actuarial report; ([ratio de
        solvabilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[solvency special payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[solvency special payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a special
      payment made under paragraph 9(4)(c) or (d); ([paiement spécial de
      solvabilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[solvency valuation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[solvency valuation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a valuation of
      the assets and liabilities of a plan using actuarial assumptions
      and methods that are in accordance with accepted actuarial
      practice for the valuation of a plan, determined on the basis that
      the plan is terminated; ([évaluation de la
      solvabilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[special payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[special payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a payment or one of
      a series of payments determined in accordance with section 9 and
      made for the purpose of liquidating an unfunded liability or a
      solvency deficiency; ([paiement spécial]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[valuation date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[valuation date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the date on which the
      actuarial report values the liabilities of a plan; ([date
      d'évaluation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [[Disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of paragraph 18(2)(b) of the Act, a
    mental or physical condition that a physician has certified as being
    likely to shorten considerably the life expectancy of a member; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of determining pensionable age, a
    mental or physical condition that a physician has certified as
    rendering a member unable to perform the member's duties as an
    employee. ([invalidité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Designated Provinces]{.HTitleText1} {#h-902985 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
definition [[designated province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
subsection 2(1) of the Act, Ontario, Quebec, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick,
Manitoba, British Columbia, Saskatchewan, Alberta and Newfoundland and
Labrador are prescribed as provinces in which there is in force pension
legislation applicable to private superannuation plans.

## [Funding]{.HTitleText1} {#h-903088 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The funding of a plan shall
be considered to meet the standards for solvency if the funding is in
accordance with section 9.

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  section [[unfunded liability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the going concern deficit of a plan as determined
    on the date that the plan was established;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which an increase in the going
    concern liabilities of a plan resulting from an amendment to the
    plan exceeds the going concern excess of the plan as determined on
    the day before the effective date of the amendment; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the going concern deficit of a
    plan determined at the valuation date exceeds the present value of
    going concern special payments of the plan established in respect of
    periods after the valuation date.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of emergence of an unfunded liability in
    respect of an occurrence described in

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} paragraph (1)(a) is the effective date of the
      plan,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} paragraph (1)(b) is the effective date of the
      amendment, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} paragraph (1)(c) is the valuation date;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date of emergence of a solvency deficiency is
    the date of the valuation that identified the deficiency; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the interest rate used to determine the present
    value of going concern special payments referred to in paragraph
    (1)(c) is the same as the interest rate used to determine the going
    concern liabilities of the plan at the valuation date.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An unfunded liability of a plan shall be funded by
  going concern special payments sufficient to liquidate the unfunded
  liability by equal annual payments over a period of 15 years from the
  date on which the unfunded liability emerged.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A plan shall be funded in each plan year as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by a contribution equal to the normal cost of the
    plan,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by going concern special payments;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the plan is not a negotiated contribution plan
    and there is a solvency deficiency, by annual solvency special
    payments equal to the amount by which the solvency deficiency
    divided by 5 exceeds the amount of going concern special payments
    that are payable during the plan year;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the plan is not a negotiated contribution plan
    and there is an additional solvency deficiency referred to in
    subsection (12), by additional annual solvency special payments
    payable from the effective date of the amendment and equal to the
    amount by which the additional solvency deficiency divided by 5
    exceeds the going concern special payment in respect of the unfunded
    liability emerging from the amendment to the plan; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} by an amount required to be paid by an employer
    under a defined contribution provision.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The amount required under paragraph (4)(a) or (e) may
  be reduced by all or a portion of the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the going concern excess, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the solvency assets exceed the
    solvency liabilities multiplied by 1.05.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If an unfunded liability or solvency deficiency is
  liquidated at a rate greater than the sum of the special payments
  required under paragraph (4)(b),(c) or (d) by the making of an
  additional payment, the amount of a special payment for a subsequent
  plan year may be reduced if the outstanding balance of an unfunded
  liability will at no time be greater than it would have been had the
  going concern special payments referred to in paragraph (4)(b) been
  made.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} If the aggregate of the present value of going
  concern special payments referred to in paragraph (1)(c) exceeds the
  going concern deficit, this excess shall be applied to reduce the
  outstanding balance of any unfunded liability and the going concern
  special payments remaining to be made in respect of the unfunded
  liability shall be reduced [pro rata]{.otherLang lang="la"}.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The average solvency ratio for a valuation date is
  the arithmetic average of the solvency ratios at the valuation date,
  the prior valuation date and the prior second valuation date adjusted
  as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the solvency ratio at the valuation date shall be
    adjusted to remove the effect of any amendment made after the prior
    second valuation date that retroactively increases or decreases the
    plan benefits;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the solvency ratio at the prior valuation date
    shall be adjusted to remove the effect of any amendment made after
    the prior second valuation date and before the prior valuation date
    that retroactively increases or decreases the plan benefits;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the solvency ratios at the prior valuation date and
    the prior second valuation date may be adjusted to increase the
    solvency assets by an amount not in excess of the present value of
    any special payment made in respect of the period between the prior
    valuation date and the valuation date, or in respect of the period
    between the prior second valuation date and the valuation date, as
    the case may be, but not including an additional payment referred to
    in subsection (6) that will be applied to reduce special payments in
    respect of periods after the valuation date;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the solvency ratio at the valuation date shall be
    adjusted by reducing the solvency assets at the valuation date by
    the amount of an additional payment referred to in subsection (6)
    that will be applied to reduce special payments in respect of
    periods after the valuation date;

  - [(d.1)]{.lawlabel} the solvency ratios at the prior valuation date
    and the prior second valuation date shall be adjusted to increase
    the solvency assets by the face value of all letters of credit
    included in the solvency assets on the valuation date and to reduce
    the solvency assets by the face value of all letters of credit
    included in the solvency assets on the prior valuation date or prior
    second valuation date, as the case may be;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the solvency ratios at the prior valuation date and
    the prior second valuation date shall be adjusted to reduce the
    solvency assets by the present value of any reduction made under
    subsection (5) or under subsection (7.1) as that subsection read
    immediately before this section comes into force, between the prior
    valuation date and the valuation date or between the prior second
    valuation date and the valuation date, as the case may be; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the solvency ratios at the prior valuation date and
    the prior second valuation date shall be adjusted to reflect the
    transfer into the plan of all of the assets of another plan between
    the prior valuation date and the valuation date or between the prior
    second valuation date and the valuation date, as the case may be, by
    including the assets of the transferring plan as solvency assets and
    the liabilities of the transferring plan as solvency liabilities.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} The average solvency ratio shall be adjusted to
  include the effect at the valuation date of any amendments referred to
  in paragraph (8)(a) or (b).

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} The interest rate used to determine the present
  value of the special payments referred to in paragraphs (8)(c) and the
  present value of the reductions referred to in paragraph (8)(e) shall
  be the same interest rate that was used to determine the solvency
  liabilities of the plan on the prior valuation date or the prior
  second valuation date, as the case may be.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} The solvency ratio at the valuation date, without
  the adjustments made under subsection (8) or (9), may be used as the
  solvency ratio for a prior valuation date or prior second valuation
  date in respect of which no actuarial report was filed or provided to
  the Superintendent.

- [(12)]{.lawlabel} An additional solvency deficiency resulting from an
  amendment to the plan is equal to the amount by which the increase in
  solvency liabilities determined in accordance with subsection (13)
  exceeds the solvency excess at the day before the effective date of
  the amendment.

- [(13)]{.lawlabel} If an amendment to the plan increases the solvency
  liabilities, the increase in solvency liabilities shall be valued
  using the actuarial assumptions and methods used in the solvency
  valuation of the actuarial report for the most recently completed plan
  year before the effective date of the amendment.

- [(13.1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (13.2), an employer, other
  than a participating employer under a multi-employer pension plan, may
  reduce the amount of any solvency special payment by the face value of
  a letter of credit that has been provided to a trustee or transferred
  to a trust under section 9.11 of the Act.

- [(13.2)]{.lawlabel} An employer may not act under section 9.11 of the
  Act if the face value of all letters of credit provided to a trustee
  or transferred to a trust exceeds, or would exceed, 15% of the
  solvency liabilities of the plan as determined at the valuation date.

- [(13.3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 9.16 of the Act, a
  payment that is required to be made under subsection 9(1.1) of the Act
  may be reduced if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the payment is a solvency special payment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Crown corporation meets the requirements of
    section 9.2;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate amount of all reductions does not
    exceed or would not exceed 15% of the solvency liabilities of the
    plan as determined at the valuation date.

- [(13.4)]{.lawlabel} The aggregate amount of all reductions made under
  section 9.16 of the Act may be adjusted in a plan year by subtracting
  the difference between

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the solvency special payment that
    would be payable for the plan year following the valuation date if
    no reductions were made under section 9.16 of the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the solvency special payment that
    would have been required for the plan year following the valuation
    date if the solvency assets at the valuation date were increased by
    the aggregate amount of all reductions made under section 9.16 of
    the Act at the valuation date.

- [(13.5)]{.lawlabel} The aggregate amount of all reductions made under
  section 9.16 of the Act may be adjusted to zero if, based on the most
  recent actuarial report,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the solvency ratio of the plan is no less than
    1.05; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the average solvency ratio of the plan is no less
    than 1.0.

- [(14)]{.lawlabel} Payments to a plan shall be made as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the normal cost of the plan shall be paid in equal
    instalments or as a percentage of the anticipated remuneration to be
    paid to the members during the plan year and shall be paid not less
    frequently than monthly and not later than 30 days after the end of
    the period in respect of which the instalment is paid;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any special payment to be made during the plan year
    shall be paid not less frequently than monthly and not later than 30
    days after the end of the period in respect of which the instalment
    is paid;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the contributions of plan members shall be remitted
    to the administrator not later than 30 days after the end of the
    period in respect of which such contributions were deducted;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the administrator shall immediately pay into the
    fund any amount remitted to the administrator; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an amount required to be paid by an employer under
    a defined contribution provision shall be paid not less frequently
    than monthly and not later than 30 days after the end of the period
    in respect of which the amount is required to be paid.

  :   [[issuer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bank, a cooperative credit
      society or an ATB, that has an acceptable rating by two credit
      rating agencies, is not an employer or affiliated with an employer
      within the meaning of subsection 2(2) of the [Canada Business
      Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44) and is a member of the Canadian
      Payment Association. ([émetteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the aggregate face value of the letters of credit
  held for the benefit of the plan exceeds 15% of the solvency
  liabilities of the plan as determined at the valuation date and if,
  based on the most recent actuarial report,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the average solvency ratio and the solvency ratio
    of the plan are 1.0 or more, the aggregate face value of the letters
    of credit may be reduced by the lesser of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the aggregate face value of
      the letters of credit exceeds 15% of the solvency liabilities of
      the plan as determined at the valuation date, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the lesser of the solvency excess and the excess
      of the solvency assets over the solvency liabilities; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} either the average solvency ratio or the solvency
    ratio of the plan is less than 1.0, the aggregate face value of the
    letters of credit may be reduced by the amount of the excess
    referred to in subparagraph (a)(i) to the extent of the difference
    between

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the solvency special payment for
      the plan year following the valuation date, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the solvency special payment that
      does not take into consideration the maximum referred to in the
      description of B of the definition [[solvency
      assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) for the plan
      year following the valuation date.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An employer may reduce the face value of a letter of
  credit after making a payment to the pension fund of the amount of the
  reduction.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The face value of a letter of credit may be reduced
  if, based on the most recent actuarial report,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the solvency ratio of the plan would have been no
    less than 1.05 had the reduced face value been in effect at the
    valuation date; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the average solvency ratio of the plan would have
    been no less than 1.0 had the reduced face value been in effect at
    the valuation date.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A letter of credit shall be an irrevocable and
  unconditional standby letter of credit that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is in accordance with the rules of the
    International Standby Practices ISP98, International Chamber of
    Commerce Publication No. 590, as amended from time to time;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specifies the date that it becomes effective, which
    can be no later than the date on which the instalment of the special
    payment that is being replaced is due, and the date that it expires,
    which shall be the day on which the plan year ends;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provides that the issuer will pay the face value of
    the letter of credit on demand from the trustee without inquiring
    whether the trustee has a right to make the demand;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is payable in Canadian currency;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} provides that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the insolvency, liquidation or bankruptcy of the
      employer is to have no effect on the rights or the obligations of
      the issuer of the letter of credit or the trustee,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it will, in accordance with these Regulations,
      be renewed, replaced or allowed to expire without renewal or
      replacement, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} it may not be

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} assigned except by the issuer to another
        issuer, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} amended except

        - [(I)]{.lawlabel} on a renewal, to increase the face value or
          to decrease the face value,

        - [(II)]{.lawlabel} if a successor issuer has taken over the
          rights and obligations under it from a predecessor issuer, to
          change the name of that predecessor to the name of that
          successor,

        - [(III)]{.lawlabel} following an assignment, to reflect the
          change in issuer, and

        - [(IV)]{.lawlabel} to decrease the face value in accordance
          with these Regulations;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} provides that if the issuer assigns the letter of
    credit without the agreement of the employer or, after the issuance
    of the letter of credit, fails to meet the definition of an issuer,
    the issuer is still required to pay the face value of the letter of
    credit on demand from the trustee; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} provides that any amendments to the letter of
    credit be provided to the employer within five days after the
    amendment is made.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The employer or, if the employer is not the
  administrator of the plan, the administrator shall enter into a trust
  agreement or may amend any existing trust agreement that they may have
  with the trustee regarding the letters of credit.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} If the employer is not the administrator, the
  administrator shall give a copy of the trust agreement to the employer
  within 10 business days after entering into or amending a trust
  agreement.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The trust agreement shall provide that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the trustee shall hold letters of credit in Canada
    in trust for the plan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the definition [[default]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
    subsection (1) applies to the agreement;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the employer shall immediately notify, in writing,

    administrator of the plan, the administrator of a default;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if not otherwise notified under paragraph (c), the
    administrator shall notify, in writing, the trustee and the
    Superintendent of a default immediately after becoming aware of it;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} on receipt of the notice referred to in
    paragraph (c) or (d), the trustee shall immediately make a demand
    for payment of the face value of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all of the letters of credit held for the benefit
      of the plan, if the default is one that is described in any of
      paragraphs (a) to (d) and (f) of the definition
      [[default]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection (1), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the letter of credit that has not been renewed,
      if the default is one that is described in paragraph (e) of that
      definition;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} on receipt of a written notice of default from any
    person other than the employer or the administrator, the trustee
    shall

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} immediately notify, in writing, the employer, the
      administrator and the Superintendent of the notice, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} make a demand for payment of the face value of
      all of the letters of credit held for the benefit of the plan
      unless the administrator provides a written notice to the trustee
      within 30 days after receipt of the notice that the default has
      not occurred;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} when a trustee makes a demand for payment of the
    face value of a letter of credit held for the benefit of the plan,
    it shall notify, in writing, the employer, the administrator and the
    Superintendent that it has made the demand;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the trustee shall immediately notify, in writing,
    the employer, the administrator and the Superintendent if the issuer
    does not pay the face value of a letter of credit after a demand for
    payment has been made;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the trustee shall not make a demand for payment if
    a letter of credit expires without being renewed or if the face
    value is being reduced, in accordance with these Regulations; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the administrator shall notify the trustee of any
    circumstance in which a letter of credit may expire or when the face
    value of a letter of credit may be reduced, under these Regulations.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall provide to the trustee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the letter of credit, on its initial issuance, at

  letter of credit provides.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} An issuer who assigns a letter of credit to another

### [Minimum Requirements for Funding Schedule]{.HTitleText2} {#h-903344 .Subheading}

**[[10.97]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.97 .sectionLabel}** The funding
schedule shall meet the following requirements:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the funding schedule shall only address the funding
  and liquidation of a solvency deficiency and an unfunded liability as
  determined at the latest valuation date minus special payments and
  other payments due to the plan before the start of the negotiation
  period;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the funding schedule shall specify the amounts of the
  going concern payments and solvency payments payable in each plan year
  that are used to fund the solvency deficiency and unfunded liability
  referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the payments shall be made to the plan in equal
  monthly instalments;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate present value, as at the end of the
  most recent plan year preceding the establishment of the funding
  schedule, of the going concern payments included in the funding
  schedule and the going concern special payments due to the plan before
  the start of the negotiation period shall be at least equal to the
  going concern deficit of the plan at the end of that year;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate present value, as at the end of the
  most recent plan year preceding the establishment of the funding
  schedule, of the solvency payments and the going concern payments
  included in the funding schedule and the special payments due to the
  plan before the start of the negotiation period shall be at least
  equal to the solvency deficiency of the plan as at the end of the plan
  year preceding the plan year in which the payment is to be made;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any annual going concern payment included in the
  funding schedule shall be no less than the annual amount of interest
  on the outstanding balance of the going concern deficit of the plan as
  at the end of the plan year preceding the plan year in which the
  payment is to be made;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} any annual solvency payment included in the funding
  schedule shall be no less than the annual amount of interest on the
  outstanding balance of the solvency deficiency as at the end of the
  plan year preceding the plan year in which the payment is to be made;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate going concern payments to be made in
  the first half of the funding schedule shall be no less than 40% of
  the aggregate going concern payments for the entire duration of the
  funding schedule;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate solvency payments to be made in the
  first five plan years of the funding schedule shall be no less than

### [Optional Requirements for Funding Schedule]{.HTitleText2} {#h-903359 .Subheading}

**[[10.98]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.98 .sectionLabel}** A funding schedule
may provide that if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the funding schedule includes the funding of an
  unfunded liability or solvency deficiency and the unfunded liability
  or solvency deficiency is liquidated at a rate greater than the sum of
  payments set out in the funding schedule by the making of additional
  payments, the amount of a payment set out in a funding schedule for a
  subsequent year may be reduced if the outstanding balance of the
  unfunded liability that is being liquidated by the remaining payments
  set out in the funding schedule or the solvency deficiency that is
  being liquidated by those payments will at no time be greater than it
  would have been had the payments that were required to be made under
  the funding schedule in relation to the unfunded liability or solvency
  deficiency, whichever is applicable, been made;

  unfunded liability and the aggregate of the present value of payments
  set out in the funding schedule and of going concern special payments,
  established in respect of a period after the valuation date, exceeds
  the going concern deficit, that excess may be applied to reduce the
  going concern payments that will become due at the latest dates in the
  approved funding schedule in such a way that the present value of
  those payments is reduced by the amount of reduction applied to the
  outstanding balance of the unfunded liability; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} there is a solvency excess as described in
  subsection 10.991(2), the payments established to liquidate a solvency
  deficiency that will become due at the latest dates in the approved
  funding schedule may be eliminated or reduced in such a way that the
  present value of the remaining payments set out in the funding
  schedule to liquidate the solvency deficiency is reduced by the

    solvency deficit as at the date of termination of the plan either in
    a lump sum or by equal annual payments sufficient to liquidate the
    solvency deficit over a period of five years from the date of
    termination;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the interest rate used to determine the annual
    payments is the same as the interest rate used to determine the
    solvency liabilities of the plan at the date of termination; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the annual payments shall be paid by equal monthly
    instalments no later than 30 days after the end of each month.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The annual payment determined under paragraph (2)(a)
  that is to be paid in the plan year in which the plan is terminated
  may be reduced by the amounts required to be paid under
  subsection 29(6) of the Act.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} An actuarial report, filed after termination of the
  plan but before it is wound up, shall set out the remaining solvency
  assets, solvency liabilities, solvency deficit and remaining payments
  required to liquidate the solvency deficit as at the valuation date.
  The solvency assets and solvency deficit shall not include the face
  value of any letters of credit.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the present value of remaining payments determined
  in accordance with paragraph (2)(a) exceeds the remaining solvency
  deficit established as at the valuation date in accordance with the
  actuarial report referred to in subsection (4), the payments remaining
  to be made in respect of the solvency deficit are reduced [pro
  rata]{.otherLang lang="la"}.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the remaining solvency deficit established as at
  the valuation date in accordance with the actuarial report referred to
  in subsection (4) exceeds the present value of remaining payments
  determined in accordance with paragraph (2)(a), the remaining payments
  are increased [pro rata]{.otherLang lang="la"} such that the remaining
  payments will liquidate the remaining solvency deficit over the
  remainder of the five-year period beginning on the date of
  termination.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Any solvency deficit that arises five or more years
  after the date of termination of the plan shall be immediately paid
  down.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 29(6.3) of the Act,
  the portion of the remaining amount that is attributable to the
  payments made under subsection 29(6.1) of the Act is equal to the
  lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount remaining in the pension fund at the
    date of winding-up, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the accumulated value at the date of winding-up,
    with interest at the rates earned by the pension fund, of the
    payments made under subsection 29(6.1) of the Act.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- SOR/2008-144, s. 9

  - - [9.]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite sections 3 and 4, a
      contract or arrangement for a locked-in registered retirement
      savings plan or a life income fund that is entered into within six
      months after the day on which these Regulations come into force
      may be made under sections 20 and 20.1 of the [Pension Benefits
      Standards Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19)
      respectively, as those sections read on the day before the day on
      which these Regulations come into force.

    - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Form 3 of Schedule II to the [Pension Benefits
      Standards Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), as it
      read before the day on which these Regulations come into force,
      may continue to be used for the purpose referred to in subsection
      18(3) of those Regulations for six months after the day on which
      these Regulations come into force.

- --- SOR/2010-149, s. 27

  - [27.]{.lawlabel} A plan may continue to be funded under section 9 of
    the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
    1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), as they read immediately before
    this section comes into force, until the day on which the first
    actuarial report is filed after this section comes into force.

- --- SOR/2010-149, s. 28

  - [28.]{.lawlabel} A reference to an "unfunded liability" in these
    Regulations includes a reference to an "initial unfunded liability"
    as defined in subsection 9(1) of the [Pension Benefits Standards
    Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), as it read
    immediately before this section comes into force.

- --- SOR/2010-149, s. 29

  - - [29.]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of determining
      the average solvency ratio for the first actuarial report required
      to be filed after this section comes into force, the solvency
      ratio that is determined on the valuation date, without the
      adjustments made under subsections 9(8) and 9(9), may be used as
      the solvency ratio for

      - [(*a*)]{.lawlabel} the prior valuation date and the prior second
        valuation date; or

      - [(*b*)]{.lawlabel} the prior second valuation date.

    - [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the average solvency ratio is determined under
      paragraph (1)(*a*), the [[solvency assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
      means the value of the assets of the plan, determined on the basis
      of market value or of a value related to the market value by means
      of a method using market values over a period of not more than
      five years to stabilize short-term fluctuations.

    - [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of determining the average
      solvency ratio for the second actuarial report required to be
      filed after this section comes into force, if the average solvency
      ratio for the first actuarial report was determined under
      paragraph (1)(*a*), the solvency ratio that is determined on the
      valuation date, without the adjustments made under subsections
      9(8) and 9(9), may be used as the solvency ratio for the prior
      second valuation date.

- --- SOR/2010-149, s. 30

  - [30.]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to a plan to which
    the [Canadian Press Pension Plan Solvency Deficiency Funding
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2009-183) apply.
