## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028798 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In the French version of
these Regulations, [société]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} has the meaning
assigned to it by section 663 of the [Insurance Companies
Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8).

## [Reduction of Assessments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028800 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Money in an amount equal to
or less than \$1,000,000 that is paid to or recovered by Her Majesty in
any period beginning on April 1 in any year and ending on March 31 in
the next year under section 691 of the [Insurance Companies
Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) or paragraph 161(1)(d) or (6)(c) or subparagraph
161(8)(b)(iv) of the [Winding-up and Restructuring Act](/eng/acts/W-11)
in respect of a company, society, foreign company or provincial company
shall be applied on a pro rata basis to reduce the assessments under
section 23 of the [Office of the Superintendent of Financial
Institutions Act](/eng/acts/O-2.7) against companies, societies, foreign
companies and provincial companies that have been assessed under
subsection 687(1) of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) in
respect of that company, society, foreign company or provincial company.

## [Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028802 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), money in an amount greater than
  \$1,000,000 that is paid to or recovered by Her Majesty in any period
  beginning on April 1 in any year and ending on March 31 in the next
  year under section 691 of the [Insurance Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) or paragraph 161(1)(d) or (6)(c) or
  subparagraph 161(8)(b)(iv) of the [Winding-up and Restructuring
  Act](/eng/acts/W-11) in respect of a company, society, foreign company
  or provincial company shall be paid on a pro rata basis to the
  companies, societies, foreign companies and provincial companies that
  have been assessed under subsection 687(1) of the [Insurance Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) in respect of that company, society, foreign
  company or provincial company.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Expenses incurred to find a company, society, foreign
  company or provincial company in respect of which a payment is to be
  made, or to find its successors or beneficiaries, shall be deducted
  from the amount payable to that company, society, foreign company or
  provincial company.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No payment shall be made if the amount of the payment
  would be less than \$10.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If a company, society,
foreign company or provincial company in respect of which a payment is
to be made, or its successors or beneficiaries, cannot be found after
reasonable attempts have been made to do so, the amount of the payment
determined under section 3 shall be applied on a pro rata basis to
reduce the assessments under section 23 of the [Office of the
Superintendent of Financial Institutions Act](/eng/acts/O-2.7) against
companies, societies, foreign companies and provincial companies.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028808 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
