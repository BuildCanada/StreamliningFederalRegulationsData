## Order Designating the Minister of Public Works and Government Services, a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-public-works-and-government-services-a-member-of-the-queens-privy-council-for-canada-as-the-minister-for-the-purposes-of-that-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2019-60
:::

[FEDERAL PROMPT PAYMENT FOR CONSTRUCTION WORK ACT](/eng/acts/F-7.7)

Registration 2019-07-24

Order Designating the Minister of Public Works and Government Services,
a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for
the purposes of that Act

P.C. 2019-1048 2019-07-09
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the [Federal Prompt Payment
for Construction Work Act](/eng/acts/F-7.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1811_hq_24851){#fn_81000-3-1811_hq_24851-ID0EBBAA},
designates the Minister of Public Works and Government Services, who is
a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for
the purpose of that Act.
