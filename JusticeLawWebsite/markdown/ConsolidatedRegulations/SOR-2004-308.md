## Electronic Alternatives Regulations for the Purposes of the Federal Real Property and Federal Immovables Act {#electronic-alternatives-regulations-for-the-purposes-of-the-federal-real-property-and-federal-immovables-act .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-308
:::

[PERSONAL INFORMATION PROTECTION AND ELECTRONIC DOCUMENTS
ACT](/eng/acts/P-8.6)

Registration 2004-12-13

Electronic Alternatives Regulations for the Purposes of the Federal Real
Property and Federal Immovables Act
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The President of the Treasury Board, as the responsible authority for
the [Federal Real Property and Federal Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4)
in accordance with the definition \"responsible authority\" in
subsection 31(1) of the [Personal Information Protection and Electronic
Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea){#footnotea-ID0EDBDA}, pursuant to
section 50 of that Act, hereby makes the annexed [Electronic
Alternatives Regulations for the Purposes of the Federal Real Property
and Federal Immovables Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-308).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea-ID0EDBDA){#footnotea}S.C. 2000, c. 5
  :::

Ottawa, December 6, 2004

Reginald B. Alcock

The President of the Treasury Board
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713573 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Personal Information
    Protection and Electronic Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6) unless the
    context otherwise requires. ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Crown grant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Crown grant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the instruments or
    acts referred to in section 5 of the [Federal Real Property and
    Federal Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4), a plan referred to in
    section 7 of that Act or any other instrument or act by which
    federal real property may be granted. ([concession de
    l\'État]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[federal real property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal real property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
    assigned to that term in section 2 of the [Federal Real Property and
    Federal Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4). ([bien réel
    fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Ontario law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Ontario law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the applicable law of the
    Province of Ontario that requires or authorizes the use of
    electronic means in respect of documents to be registered. ([règles
    de droit de l\'Ontario]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registered]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registered]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned to that
    term in section 17 of the Land Registration Reform Act of Ontario.
    ([enregistré]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713580 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only in respect of federal real property situated in the Province of
Ontario.

## [Electronic Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713582 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
application of section 41 of the Act, an electronic document must be in
the form required or authorized by the Ontario law in order for that
electronic document to satisfy any requirement for a written document
under

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sections 5 and 7 of the [Federal Real Property and
  Federal Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4) in respect of a Crown grant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 6 of the [Federal Real Property and Federal
  Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4) in respect of a licence; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} section 11 of the [Federal Real Property and Federal
  Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4) in respect of an instrument
  transferring administration and control.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-408, s. 1
:::

## [Electronic Signature]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713589 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of the application of section 43 of the Act, the electronic
  signature of a Minister must be in the form required or authorized by
  the Ontario law in order for that electronic signature to satisfy any
  requirement for signature or countersignature by that Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} under subsection 5(5) of the [Federal Real Property
    and Federal Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4) in respect of any
    instrument or act referred to in paragraph 5(1)(b) or subsection
    5(2) or (4) of that Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} under subsection 5(6) of the [Federal Real Property
    and Federal Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4) in respect of any
    instrument or act referred to in paragraph 5(1)(b) or subsection
    5(2) of that Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} under section 6 of the [Federal Real Property and
    Federal Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4) in respect of a licence;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} under subsection 7(2) of the [Federal Real Property
    and Federal Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4) in respect of a plan
    referred to in subsection 7(1) of that Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} under subsection 11(1) of the [Federal Real
    Property and Federal Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4) in respect of
    an instrument transferring administration and control; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2005-408, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the application of section 43 of
  the Act, the electronic signature of an officer authorized under
  section 3 of the [Federal Real Property and Federal Immovables
  Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4) must be in the form required or authorized by
  Ontario law in order for that electronic signature to satisfy any
  requirement for signature or countersignature that is referred to in
  subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-408, s. 2
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713601 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
