## Canada--Newfoundland and Labrador Offshore Area Registration Regulations {#canadanewfoundland-and-labrador-offshore-area-registration-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-263
:::

[CANADA--NEWFOUNDLAND AND LABRADOR ATLANTIC ACCORD IMPLEMENTATION
ACT](/eng/acts/C-7.5)

CANADA-NEWFOUNDLAND ATLANTIC ACCORD IMPLEMENTATION ACT

Registration 1988-05-05

Regulations Respecting the Administration and Registration of Interests
and Instruments in Relation to the Newfoundland Offshore Area and
Prescribing Fees to Be Paid in Respect of Such Interests and Instruments

P.C. 1988-828 1988-05-05
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 150(1) of the Canada-Newfoundland
Atlantic Accord Implementation Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Document1_e_hq_8161){#fn_Document1_e_hq_8161-ID0EFBCA},
a copy of proposed [Regulations respecting the administration and
registration of interests and instruments in relation to the
Newfoundland offshore area and prescribing fees to be paid in respect of
such interests and instruments](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-263),
substantially in the form annexed hereto, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on January 23, 1988 and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations to the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources with
respect thereto;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Document1_e_hq_8161-ID0EFBCA){#fn_Document1_e_hq_8161}S.C.
  1987, c. 3
  :::

And Whereas, pursuant to section 7 of the Canada-Newfoundland Atlantic
Accord Implementation Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Document1_e_hq_8161){#fn_Document1_e_hq_8161-ID0EBABA},
the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources has consulted with the
Minister of Energy for the Province of Newfoundland with respect to the
proposed Regulations and the Minister of Energy for the Province of
Newfoundland has given his approval to the making of the said
Regulations.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources, pursuant
to subsections 53(1) and 113(1) and sections 118 and 125 of the
Canada-Newfoundland Atlantic Accord Implementation Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Document1_e_hq_8161){#fn_Document1_e_hq_8161-ID0EDAAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the
administration and registration of interests and instruments in relation
to the Newfoundland offshore area and prescribing fees to be paid in
respect of such interests and instruments](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-263),
effective May 20, 1988.
:::

## [Registrar]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911586 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar shall
supervise the operation of the system of registration established under
Division VIII of Part II of the Act in accordance with that Division and
these Regulations.

## [Deputy Registrar]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911588 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  absence of the Registrar, the Deputy Registrar shall have the powers
  and perform the duties and functions of the Registrar.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, at any time, no person is designated by the
  Board to be the Registrar, the Deputy Registrar shall have the powers
  and perform the duties and functions of the Registrar.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-227, s. 2(F)

## [Interests]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911594 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  registering an interest, the Registrar shall prepare an abstract of
  the interest and without delay enter the abstract in a book to be
  maintained as the register.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The abstract shall contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the type, effective date and term of the interest;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the interest owner or, if the interest
    owner consists of two or more interest holders, the name of each
    holder and the name of the representative appointed or designated
    under section 53 of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the particulars of the shares in the interest; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of those portions of the offshore
    area, geological formations and substances to which the interest
    applies.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2007-227, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where an interest referred to in section 109 of the
  Act is registered, the abstract for that interest shall contain a
  notation of the registration of each instrument that applies to the
  interest by virtue of that section.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall record in the abstract of the
  relevant interest

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} changes to the information recorded in the
    abstract, other than the corrections referred to in subsection 9(2);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} every order, decision or action of the Board made
    in relation to that interest that is expressly stated in the Act to
    be subject to section 124 of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} every order made pursuant to section 56 of the Act
    in relation to that interest;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} every notice given by the Board pursuant to
    subsection 79(1) or 123(1) of the Act in relation to that interest;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the extension of the term of that interest under
    any provision of the Act.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where an interest expires or is cancelled, the
  Registrar shall endorse a memorandum to that effect on the original
  copy of that interest maintained pursuant to paragraph 7(2)(a) and
  shall make a notation of the expiration or cancellation in the
  abstract of that interest.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Where two or more interests are consolidated pursuant
  to subsection 68(3) or section 82 of the Act, the Registrar shall
  prepare an abstract of the consolidated interest and forthwith enter
  that abstract in the register.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Each abstract entered in the register pursuant to
  subsection (7) shall contain the notations made in the abstracts of
  the interests that have been consolidated.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-227, s. 3

## [Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911615 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2007-227, s. 4

\]
:::

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Registrar shall stamp each document submitted for registration under
  Division VIII of Part II of the Act with the date and time of its
  receipt at the office of the Registrar.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall keep a record to be called "the
  daybook" and, forthwith on receipt of a document submitted for
  registration, shall record therein in chronological order

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the type and date of the document;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person submitting the document; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of receipt of the document at the
    office of the Registrar.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Registrar refuses to register a document,
  the Registrar shall forthwith record in the daybook with respect to
  that document the fact of the refusal.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Registrar accepts a document for
  registration as an instrument, the Registrar shall forthwith

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} register the instrument;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make a notation of the registration of the
    instrument in the abstract of each interest to which the instrument
    relates; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} record in the daybook with respect to that
    instrument the fact of the registration.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the registration of an instrument is cancelled
  pursuant to any provision of the Act or these Regulations, the
  Registrar shall make a notation of the cancellation in each abstract
  in which a notation of the registration of the instrument appears.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-227, s. 5(F)
:::

## [Office]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911632 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  office of the Registrar shall be located at St. John's and shall be
  kept open from 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Monday to Friday, with the
  exception of statutory holidays on which days the offices of the Board
  in St. John's are closed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall maintain at the office of the
  Registrar

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the original copies of every interest and
    instrument registered under Division VIII of Part II of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the register; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the daybook.

## [Searches]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911639 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person may, at the office of the Registrar, inspect the daybook, the
  register and any copies of any interest or instrument registered under
  Division VIII of Part II of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall furnish to the person making the
  request a certified exact copy of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any interest or instrument registered under
    Division VIII of Part II of the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the abstract of any interest.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar may, for the purposes of subsection
  (2), certify that an enlarged print made from a microphotographic film
  or an electronic or magnetic recording of an abstract, interest or
  instrument is an exact copy of the abstract, interest or instrument.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-21, s. 17

## [Corrections]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911648 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Registrar shall cancel the registration of any interest registered in
  error and shall forthwith notify the interest owner, the
  representative appointed or designated under section 53 of the Act, if
  any, and any other person that is directly affected by the
  cancellation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the particulars of any interest, instrument or
  other matter required by these Regulations to be recorded or noted in
  an abstract have not been recorded or noted or have been recorded or
  noted incorrectly, the Registrar shall forthwith correct the omission
  or error when the omission or error becomes known to the Registrar.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall send a certified exact copy of
  each abstract corrected pursuant to subsection (2) to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the interest owner of the interest to which the
    corrected abstract relates or, where a representative has been
    appointed or designated pursuant to section 53 of the Act, to the
    representative; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the secured party under each security notice
    registered in respect of the interest to which the corrected
    abstract relates that has not been fully discharged as of the time
    of the correction.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-227, s. 6

## [Demand for Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911657 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Those persons that have
written authorization from a person referred to in paragraph 113(1)(a),
(b) or (c) of the Act to serve a demand for information are members of a
prescribed class for the purposes of subsection 113(1) of the Act.

## [Notice]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911659 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise
specified in the Act, a notice that is required to be given to a person
by the Board or the Registrar under Part II of the Act shall be given by
personal service or by registered mail sent to the latest address for
that person that is filed with the Registrar.

- SOR/2007-227, s. 7

## [Surrender of Interests]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911663 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  interest may be surrendered with respect to all or any portion of the
  offshore area that is subject to the interest by sending a notice of
  surrender to the Registrar describing the offshore area in question.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of surrender of an interest in respect of
  all the offshore area subject to the interest shall be executed by the
  interest owner or, if the interest owner consists of more than one
  interest holder, by each holder.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice of surrender of an interest in respect of a
  portion of the offshore area that is subject to the interest shall be
  executed by the interest owner or by the holder of each share in the
  interest held in respect of that portion.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-227, s. 8
:::

Version](section-12-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 12"){.PITLink}

**[[12.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.1 .sectionLabel}** If an interest is
surrendered, the Registrar shall endorse a memorandum to that effect on
the original copy of that interest maintained under paragraph 7(2)(a)
and shall make a notation of the surrender in the abstract of that
interest.

## [Appointment of a Representative]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911673 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Interest holders shall appoint a
  representative referred to in subsection 53(1) of the Act by sending a
  notice of appointment executed by each interest holder to the
  Registrar.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where there is a change in the name or address of a
  representative referred to in subsection (1), the representative shall
  notify the Registrar forthwith of the change.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the interest holders terminate the appointment of
  a representative referred to in subsection 53(1) of the Act, the
  interest holders shall, without delay, provide the Registrar with a
  notice of termination, and the termination shall be effective when the
  notice is received by the Registrar.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-227, s. 9
