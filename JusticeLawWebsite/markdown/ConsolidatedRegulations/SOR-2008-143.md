## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-744713 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of Public Works and
    Government Services Act. ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contracting department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contracting department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the department
    that awards the contract to which a complaint relates. ([ministère
    contractant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day that is not a
    Saturday or a holiday. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-744718 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to sections 7 to 14, to a contract for
  the acquisition of materiel or services that is awarded after the
  coming into force of these Regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to sections 15 to 22, to a contract for
  the acquisition of materiel or services if the facts or grounds on
  which the complaint is based arise after the coming into force of
  these Regulations; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to section 23, to a contract for the
  acquisition of materiel or services if a dispute referred to in that
  section arises after the coming into force of these Regulations.

## [Performance of the Duties and Functions of the Procurement Ombudsman]{.HTitleText1} {#h-744723 .Part}

### [Exception]{.HTitleText2} {#h-744724 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Procurement Ombudsman
shall not perform the duties and functions referred to in subsection
22.1(3) of the Act in respect of the following departments:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Security Intelligence Service; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the staff of the Senate and the House of Commons.

### [Review of Departmental Practices for Acquiring Materiel and Services]{.HTitleText2} {#h-744728 .Subheading}

#### [Review]{.HTitleText3} {#h-744729 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Procurement Ombudsman shall review the practices of a department for
  acquiring materiel and services under paragraph 22.1(3)(a) of the Act
  if there are reasonable grounds to do so and shall take into
  consideration the following factors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} consistency with relevant Cabinet policies and
    procedures, with relevant multi-departmental policies and procedures
    and with policies and procedures related to a national security
    exemption;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} consistency with the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11) and regulations made under it;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the resources that would be required of that
    department to respond to the review;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the observations or findings of any previous audits
    or assessments; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the time that has elapsed since his or her previous
    review of the practices of that department.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Procurement Ombudsman reviews the practices of
  a department under subsection (1), he or she may request that it
  provide any document or information necessary for the review.

#### [Recommendations]{.HTitleText3} {#h-744738 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Before making any
recommendations under paragraph 22.1(3)(a) of the Act, the Procurement
Ombudsman shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} take into consideration the following factors:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} consistency with relevant cabinet policies and
    procedures, with relevant multi-departmental policies and procedures
    and with policies and procedures related to a national security
    exemption,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} consistency with the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11) and regulations made under it,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the seriousness of any inconsistency with the
    principles of fairness, openness and transparency, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the potential impact that the recommendations
    could have on the government's operations or resources; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide an opportunity to the department whose
  practices have been subject to the review to make comments on the
  proposed recommendations and the reasons for them within 30 working
  days after their receipt.

<!-- -->

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Procurement Ombudsman makes recommendations under paragraph 22.1(3)(a)
  of the Act, he or she shall provide them, together with reasons,
  within one year after the commencement of a review and shall also
  provide a copy of them to the Minister without delay.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Procurement Ombudsman cannot provide the
  recommendations within the period set out in subsection (1), before
  the expiry of that period, he or she shall provide interim
  recommendations, together with the reasons for the delay, and shall
  provide the final recommendations within 120 working days after the
  provision of the interim recommendations.

### [Review of a Complaint Under Paragraph 22.1(3)(b) of the Act]{.HTitleText2} {#h-744749 .Subheading}

#### [Filing of a Complaint Under Paragraph 22.1(3)(b) of the Act]{.HTitleText3} {#h-744750 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  complaint under paragraph 22.1(3)(b) of the Act shall be filed in
  writing with the Procurement Ombudsman

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 30 working days after public notice of the
    award of contract to which the complaint relates; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if there was no public notice, within 30 working
    days after the day on which the award of contract became known or
    reasonably should have become known to the complainant.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, for reasons beyond the control of the
  complainant, the complainant is not able to file a complaint with the
  Procurement Ombudsman within the period set out in paragraph (1)(a) or
  (b), as the case may be, the Procurement Ombudsman may, at the request
  of the complainant, extend those periods to a maximum of 90 working
  days.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a complainant has previously contacted the
  contracting department to object to the award of a contract within the
  period set out in paragraph (1)(a) or (b), as the case may be, a
  complaint under subsection (1) shall be filed in writing with the
  Procurement Ombudsman within 30 working days after day on which the
  contracting department denied the objection.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The complaint is filed when the following information
  and documents are submitted to the Procurement Ombudsman:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the complainant;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information identifying the contract to which the
    complaint relates, including the date of public notice of the award
    of contract or, if there was no public notice, the date of award of
    that contract, if known;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the contracting department and, if
    applicable, of the department for which the materiel or services are
    intended under the contract to which the complaint relates;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} information and documents establishing that the
    complainant meets the requirement set out in subsection 22.2(1) of
    the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a clear and detailed statement setting out the
    facts and grounds on which the complaint is based;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a declaration by the complainant stating that the
    facts and grounds on which the complaint is based are not and have
    not been the subject of an inquiry before the Canadian International
    Trade Tribunal or a proceeding in a court of competent jurisdiction;
    and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} upon request of the Procurement Ombudsman for the
    purpose of section 13,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a calculation, together with supporting
      documents, of the net profit that the complainant would have
      realized, had it been awarded that contract, at the lower of the
      amount it bid, if any, and the amount at which the contract was
      awarded, without including the value of any options or any
      extensions of the contract, less the cost of submitting the bid,
      if any, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the complainant's costs of submitting the bid
      and supporting documentation.

#### [Review]{.HTitleText3} {#h-744767 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Procurement Ombudsman
shall determine whether to review a complaint referred to in subsection
7(1) within 10 working days after the filing of the complaint, and
shall, without delay, notify the complainant and the contracting
department of his or her decision and, at that time, provide that
department with a copy of the complaint.

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Procurement Ombudsman shall review a complaint referred to in
  subsection 7(1), if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the contract, the award of which is the subject of
    the complaint, is not covered by any of the exceptions in the
    [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the
    [Canadian Free Trade Agreement Implementation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-17.15), including those made under articles 800,
    801, 802, 809 and 1205.1 of that Agreement, and is one to which that
    Agreement would apply if its value, as determined under article 505
    of that Agreement, were not less than the amount set out in article
    504 of that Agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the requirements set out in subsection 22.2(1) of
    the Act and section 7 have been met;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the facts and grounds on which the complaint is
    based are not and have not been the subject of an inquiry before the
    Canadian International Trade Tribunal or a proceeding in a court of
    competent jurisdiction; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} there are reasonable grounds to believe that the
    contract was not awarded in accordance with regulations made under
    the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Procurement Ombudsman reviews the complaint
  under subsection (1), he or she may request that the complainant or
  the contracting department provide any document or information
  necessary for the review.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Procurement Ombudsman
shall end the review and notify the complainant and the contracting
department of that fact and the reasons for it, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the complainant withdraws the complaint;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} one or more of the requirements set out in section 7
  or 9 have not been met;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the award of contract has been cancelled; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Procurement Ombudsman determines that the
  complaint is frivolous or vexatious.

#### [Procedures]{.HTitleText3} {#h-744783 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  Procurement Ombudsman shall review complaints under a simple process
  and allow the contracting department 15 working days after the receipt
  of the notice referred to in section 8 to provide its comments on the
  complaint.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Procurement Ombudsman shall review a complaint
  under an expanded process on the request of the complainant or the
  contracting department, or if the Procurement Ombudsman determines
  that the circumstances warrant an expanded process, taking into
  consideration any relevant factors, including the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the complaint;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the value of the contract;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the complexity of the bid requirements or the
    procurement process; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the potential impact that the findings and any
    recommendations could have on the government's operations or
    resources.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Procurement Ombudsman shall without delay notify
  the complainant and the contracting department that a complaint is to
  be reviewed under an expanded process.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Under an expanded process, the Procurement Ombudsman
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide the contracting department with a period of
    at least 25 working days after the receipt of the notice referred to
    in subsection (3) to provide comments with respect to the complaint
    and may, on the request of that department, allow it to provide its
    comments after that period, having regard to the complexity of the
    bid requirements or the procurement process;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the complainant with a copy of the
    department's comments and an opportunity to respond within 10
    working days after the receipt of the copy and may, on the request
    of the complainant, allow it to provide its response after that
    period, having regard to the complexity of the bid requirements or
    the procurement process; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide the department with a copy of the
    complainant's response and an opportunity to respond within 10
    working days after the receipt of the copy if the complainant raises
    new arguments or evidence in its response and may, on the request of
    the department, allow the department to provide its response after
    that period, having regard to the complexity of the bid requirements
    or the procurement process.

#### [Findings]{.HTitleText3} {#h-744796 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of his or her review, the Procurement Ombudsman shall take
  into consideration any relevant factors, including the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the complainant would have had a reasonable
    prospect of being awarded the contract, but for the actions of the
    contracting department;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the seriousness of any deficiency in complying with
    the regulations made under the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the failure or refusal of the complainant to
    provide information about its goods and services at the request of
    the contracting department;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the degree to which the complainant was prejudiced;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the degree to which the fairness, openness or
    transparency of the procurement process was prejudiced; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} whether any of the parties acted in bad faith.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Procurement Ombudsman shall not substitute his or
  her opinion for the judgment of the persons involved in the
  acquisition process for the contract in relation to the assessment of
  any bid, unless there is insufficient written evidence to support that
  assessment or the assessment is unreasonable.

#### [Recommendations]{.HTitleText3} {#h-744806 .Subheading}

##### [Compensation]{.HTitleText4} {#h-744807 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  Procurement Ombudsman may recommend payment of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} compensation in respect of the profit that the
    complainant would have realized if, but for the actions of the
    contracting department, it had been awarded the contract, which
    compensation may not exceed the lesser of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the net profit that the complainant would have
      realized, had it been awarded the contract, at the lower of the
      amount that it bid, if any, and the amount at which the contract
      was awarded, without including the value of any options or any
      extensions of the contract, less the cost of submitting the bid,
      if any; and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 10% of the value of the contract awarded without
      including the value of any options or any extensions of the
      contract, less the cost of submitting the bid, if any; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} compensation equal to the costs of submitting the
    bid but that compensation may not exceed 10% of the value of the
    contract awarded, without including the value of any options or any
    extensions of the contract.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a competitive process was held, the complainant
  must have submitted a bid in respect of the contract to which the
  complaint relates, unless it was prevented from doing so because of
  the actions of the contracting department.

##### [Findings and Recommendations]{.HTitleText4} {#h-744815 .Subheading}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Procurement Ombudsman
shall provide his or her findings or any recommendations under
subsection 22.2(3) of the Act within 120 working days after the day on
which the complaint is filed.

### [Review of a Complaint Under Paragraph 22.1(3)(c) of the Act]{.HTitleText2} {#h-744817 .Subheading}

#### [Filing of a Complaint Under Paragraph 22.1(3)(c) of the Act]{.HTitleText3} {#h-744818 .Subheading}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A person may file a
complaint under paragraph 22.1(3)(c) of the Act only if, in addition to
meeting the requirement set out in subsection 22.2(1) of the Act, the
person has been awarded the contract to which the complaint relates.

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  complaint under paragraph 22.1(3)(c) of the Act shall be filed in
  writing with the Procurement Ombudsman within 30 working days after
  the day on which the complainant became aware, or reasonably should
  have become aware, of the grounds on which the complaint is based.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, for reasons beyond the control of the
  complainant, the complainant is not able to file a complaint with the
  Procurement Ombudsman within the period set out in subsection (1), the
  Procurement Ombudsman may, at the request of the complainant, extend
  that period to a maximum of 90 working days.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a complainant has previously contacted the
  contracting department to object to the administration of a contract
  within the period set out in subsection (1), a complaint under that
  subsection shall be filed in writing with the Procurement Ombudsman
  within 30 working days after day on which the contracting department
  denied the objection.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The complaint is filed when the following information
  and documents are submitted to the Procurement Ombudsman:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the complainant;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information identifying the contract to which the
    complaint relates and, on the request of the Procurement Ombudsman,
    a copy of that contract;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the contracting department and, if
    applicable, the department for which the materiel or services are
    intended under the contract to which the complaint relates;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} information and documents establishing that the
    complainant meets the requirements set out in subsection 22.2(1) of
    the Act and in section 15; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the complaint, setting out the facts
    and grounds on which the complaint is based.

#### [Review]{.HTitleText3} {#h-744830 .Subheading}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Procurement Ombudsman
shall determine whether to review a complaint referred to in subsection
16(1) within 10 working days after the filing of the complaint, and
shall, without delay, notify the complainant and the contracting
department of his or her decision, and provide that department with a
copy of the complaint.

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Procurement Ombudsman shall review a complaint referred to in
  subsection 16(1), if the requirements set out in subsection 22.2(1) of
  the Act and in sections 15 and 16 have been met and the interpretation
  and application of the terms and conditions or the scope of the work
  of the contract are not in dispute between the parties.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Procurement Ombudsman reviews the complaint
  under subsection (1), he or she may request that the complainant or
  the contracting department provide any document or information
  necessary for the review.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Procurement Ombudsman
shall end the review and notify the complainant and the contracting
department of that fact and the reasons for it, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the complainant withdraws the complaint;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} one or more of the requirements set out in subsection
  22.2(1) of the Act, section 15 or 16 have not been met; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Procurement Ombudsman determines that the
  complaint is frivolous or vexatious.

#### [Findings and Recommendations]{.HTitleText3} {#h-744839 .Subheading}

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the
review, the Procurement Ombudsman shall take into consideration any
relevant factors, including the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the administration of the relevant contract
  was conducted in a reasonable manner in the circumstances; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether any of the parties acted in bad faith.

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The Procurement Ombudsman
may not make recommendations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} altering the terms and conditions of a contract; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} providing a remedy other than as specified in the
  contract.

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The Procurement Ombudsman
shall provide, in accordance with subsection 22.2(3) of the Act, his or
her findings and any recommendations within 120 working days after the
day on which the complaint is filed.

### [Alternative Dispute Resolution Process]{.HTitleText2} {#h-744847 .Subheading}

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  party to a contract requests an alternative dispute resolution process
  under paragraph 22.1(3)(d) of the Act with respect to a dispute on the
  interpretation or application of a contract's terms and conditions,
  the Procurement Ombudsman shall ask the other parties if they agree to
  participate in such a process and to bear the costs of that process,
  within 10 working days after the request, and permit those parties 10
  working days to respond to his or her request.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Procurement Ombudsman shall receive a request
  that is in writing and includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the names of the parties to the contract;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the contract; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a brief summary of the facts in issue.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Within 10 working days after receiving the last of
  the other parties' responses,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if all parties agree, the Procurement Ombudsman
    shall submit to the parties a proposal for an alternative dispute
    resolution process; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the parties do not agree, the Procurement
    Ombudsman shall notify the party who made the request that the
    request cannot be granted.
