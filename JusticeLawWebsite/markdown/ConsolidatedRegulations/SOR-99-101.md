## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025981 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Marine
    Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[auditor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[auditor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a partnership of auditors.
    ([vérificateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[beneficial ownership]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[beneficial ownership]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes ownership of a
    share or borrowing through a trustee, legal representative, agent or
    other intermediary. ([véritable propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[body corporate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[body corporate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a company or other
    body corporate wherever or however incorporated. ([personne
    morale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[controlled]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[controlled]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a body corporate,
    has the meaning assigned in subsection 2(3) of the [Canada Business
    Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44). ([contrôle]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Ontario Court (General Division);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Superior Court of Quebec;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the trial division or branch of the Supreme Court
      of Prince Edward Island and Newfoundland;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Court of Queen's Bench for New Brunswick,
      Manitoba, Saskatchewan and Alberta;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Supreme Court of Nova Scotia and British
      Columbia;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the Supreme Court of the Yukon Territory and the
      Northwest Territories; and

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Federal Court. ([tribunal]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[long-term]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[long-term]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an investment,
    means that the term to maturity is one year or more but less than
    10.5 years. ([à long terme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a port authority,
    means a person appointed by the board of directors of the port
    authority under subsection 21(1) of the Act and the chairperson of
    the board of directors elected under section 17 of the Act.
    ([dirigeant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[short-term]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[short-term]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an investment,
    means that the term to maturity is less than one year. ([à court
    terme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Non-Application of Certain Acts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1026001 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The provisions of the
[Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44), the [Canada
Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8) and the [Winding-up and Restructuring
Act](/eng/acts/W-11) do not apply to a port authority unless in these
Regulations they are expressly stated to apply.

## [Binding on Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1026003 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
binding on Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General Administrative Matters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1026005 .Part}

### [Corporate Name]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026006 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A port authority shall set
out its corporate name as set out in its letters patent, in legible
characters, in all contracts, invoices, negotiable instruments and
orders for goods or services issued or made by or on behalf of it.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and except in the circumstances referred to in
  section 4, a port authority may carry on business under or identify
  itself by a name other than its corporate name if that other name does
  not contain, except in a figurative or descriptive sense, any of the
  following words or expressions or the corresponding abbreviations:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in its English version, "Limited", "Incorporated"
    or "Corporation"; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in its French version, "[Limitée]{.otherLang
    lang="fr"}", "[Incorporée]{.otherLang lang="fr"}" or "[Société par
    actions de régime fédéral]{.otherLang lang="fr"}".

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A port authority shall not carry on business under or
  identify itself by a name that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prohibited or deceptively misdescriptive within the
    meaning of paragraph 12(1)(a) of the [Canada Business Corporations
    Act](/eng/acts/C-44); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reserved for another corporation under section 11
    of the [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to the extent that the
  use of some other name is authorized under the letters patent.

### [Registered Office]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026016 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** If the street address of
the registered office of a port authority changes, the port authority
shall submit to the Minister a request for supplementary letters patent
that set out the new street address.

### [Corporate Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026018 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A port
  authority shall maintain, at its registered office, records that
  contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its letters patent, its by-laws and all amendments
    to the by-laws;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the annual financial statements, for a
    period of six years after the end of the fiscal year to which they
    relate;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of every document received by it in the last
    six years notifying it of the appointment or cessation of office of
    a director;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the constating instruments of each of its
    wholly-owned subsidiaries, as amended from time to time;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the financial statements of each of its
    wholly-owned subsidiaries and of each body corporate the accounts of
    which are consolidated in the latest financial statements of the
    port authority; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a copy of every document that a wholly-owned
    subsidiary of the port authority is required to make available to
    the public under the laws of the jurisdiction in which the
    subsidiary is incorporated.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A port authority shall prepare and maintain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at its registered office, a record of what
    transpired at the last six annual meetings held under the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at its registered office or at such other place in
    Canada as the board of directors thinks fit, a record of the minutes
    of meetings and resolutions of the board of directors and committees
    of directors.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a harbour commission, or a local port
  corporation within the meaning assigned in section 2 of the Canada
  Ports Corporation Act, that is continued, deemed to be incorporated or
  incorporated as a port authority under the Act, the requirement to
  maintain records that contain minutes of meetings and resolutions of
  the board of directors and committees of directors includes
  maintaining similar records that the harbour commission or local port
  corporation had in its possession before it was continued, deemed to
  be incorporated or incorporated.

<!-- -->

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to any other Act of Parliament, or any applicable Act of the
  legislature of a province, that provides for a longer retention
  period, a port authority shall maintain accounting records at its
  registered office or at such other place in Canada as the board of
  directors thinks fit for a period of six years after the end of the
  fiscal year to which they relate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a harbour commission, or a local port
  corporation or non-corporate port within the meaning assigned in
  section 2 of the Canada Ports Corporation Act, that is continued,
  deemed to be incorporated or incorporated as a port authority under
  the Act, the requirement to maintain accounting records includes
  maintaining similar records that the harbour commission, the local
  port corporation or the Canada Ports Corporation had in its possession
  before it was continued, deemed to be incorporated or incorporated.

### [Records --- Form and Precautions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026034 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  record required by the Act or these Regulations shall be prepared and
  maintained in a form that allows for the reproduction of any required
  information in intelligible written form within a reasonable time,
  including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a bound or loose-leaf form;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a photographic form;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a system of mechanical or electronic data
    processing; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other information storage device.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every port authority and every wholly-owned
  subsidiary of a port authority shall ensure that reasonable
  precautions are taken in respect of records required by the Act or
  these Regulations to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prevent their loss or destruction;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prevent the falsification of entries in them; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} facilitate the detection and correction of
    inaccuracies in them.

### [Access to Corporate Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026045 .Subheading}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), any
  person may examine records referred to in subsection 7(1) and
  paragraph 7(2)(a) during the normal business hours of the port
  authority.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21), any
  person may examine records referred to in paragraph 7(1)(c) during the
  normal business hours of the port authority.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} To the extent that examination of records is
  authorized under subsection (1) or (2), extracts from the records may
  be taken

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} free of charge by the Minister and creditors of the
    port authority or the agents or legal representatives of the
    creditors; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on payment of a fee that is reasonable and does not
    exceed the fees prescribed under the [Access to Information
    Act](/eng/acts/A-1), by any other person.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Records referred to in paragraph 7(2)(b) and section
  8 shall at all reasonable times be open to inspection by the
  directors.

### [Notice and Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026053 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  notice or document that is required to be sent to or served on a port
  authority may be sent by registered mail to the registered office of
  the port authority.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice or document required by the Act, these
  Regulations, the letters patent or the by-laws to be sent to or served
  on a director of a port authority may be sent by prepaid mail
  addressed, or may be delivered personally, to the director at the
  latest address shown for the director in the records of the port
  authority.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice or document sent by mail in accordance with
  this section is deemed to be received or served within the seven days
  after it was sent.

### [Corporate Seal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026058 .Subheading}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** An instrument or
agreement executed on behalf of a port authority is not invalid merely
because a corporate seal is not affixed to it.

### [Certificates]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026060 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  certificate issued on behalf of a port authority stating any fact that
  is set out in the letters patent, by-laws, minutes of a meeting of the
  board of directors or a committee of directors, or in a trust
  indenture or other contract to which the port authority is a party,
  may be signed by a director or officer of the port authority.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When introduced as evidence in a civil, criminal,
  administrative, investigative or other proceeding, the following
  things are, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, proof of the
  facts so certified without proof of the signature or official
  character of the person appearing to have signed the certificate:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a fact stated in a certificate referred to in
    subsection (1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a certified copy of minutes or a certified extract
    from minutes of a meeting of the board of directors or a committee
    of directors.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Directors and Officers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1026066 .Part}

### [Meetings of Board of Directors and Committees of Directors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026067 .Subheading}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the letters patent and
  the by-laws, the board of directors or a committee of directors of a
  port authority may meet at any place if reasonable notice is given.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of a meeting of directors shall specify
  any of the following matters that are to be dealt with at the meeting,
  but, unless the by-laws provide otherwise, need not specify any other
  business to be transacted at the meeting:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} filling a vacancy in the office of chairperson or
    auditor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} approving the annual financial statements;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} approving a borrowing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} adopting a land use plan or business plan;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} adopting, amending or repealing by-laws; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} authorizing or ratifying any activity carried on or
    to be carried on, or any power exercised or to be exercised, by a
    subsidiary of the port authority.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A director may in any manner waive notice of a
  meeting of directors, and the attendance of a director at a meeting is
  a waiver of notice of the meeting, except if a director attends a
  meeting for the express purpose of objecting to the transaction of any
  business on the grounds that the meeting was not lawfully called.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notice of an adjourned meeting is not required to be
  given if the time and place of the adjourned meeting are announced at
  the original meeting.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the by-laws, a director may attend a
  meeting by means of any telephonic, electronic or other communications
  facility that permits all persons participating in the meeting to
  communicate adequately with each other during the meeting, and a
  director participating in the meeting by those means is present at the
  meeting for the purposes of these Regulations.

### [Resolution in lieu of Meeting]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026080 .Subheading}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  resolution in writing signed by all the directors entitled to vote on
  the resolution is as valid as if it had been passed at a meeting of
  the board of directors or of a committee of directors, as the case may
  be.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of every resolution shall be kept with the
  minutes of the proceedings of the board or committee.

### [Dissent of Directors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026084 .Subheading}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  director of a port authority who is present at a meeting of the board
  of directors or of a committee of directors, whether the director
  abstains from voting on a resolution or an action to be taken at the
  meeting or remains silent, is deemed to have consented to any
  resolution passed or action taken at the meeting, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the director requests that a dissent be entered in
    the minutes of the meeting or a dissent is so entered;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the director sends a written dissent to the
    secretary of the meeting before the meeting is adjourned; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the director sends a dissent by registered mail, or
    delivers it, to the registered office of the port authority
    immediately after the meeting is adjourned.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A director who votes for or expressly consents to a
  resolution is not entitled to dissent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A director who is not present at a meeting at which a
  resolution is passed or an action taken is deemed to have consented to
  the resolution or action unless, within seven days after becoming
  aware of the resolution or action, the director

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} requests that a dissent be kept with the minutes of
    the meeting; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sends a dissent by registered mail, or delivers it,
    to the registered office of the port authority.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (3) does not apply to directors who absent
  themselves from a meeting in conformity with the code of conduct set
  out in the letters patent.

### [Validity of Acts of Directors and Officers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026095 .Subheading}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** An act of a director or
officer of a port authority is valid despite any irregularity in their
appointment or election or any defect in their qualification.

### [Failure to Disclose a Material Contract]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026097 .Subheading}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** If a director or officer
fails to disclose an interest in a material contract in accordance with
the code of conduct set out in the letters patent, a court may, on the
application of the Minister or the port authority, set aside the
contract on such terms as it thinks fit.

### [Liability of Directors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026099 .Subheading}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The directors of a port
authority are jointly and severally, or solidarily, liable to the
employees of the port authority for all debts not exceeding six months
wages payable for services performed by the employees for the port
authority during the period in which the directors held office.

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  director is not liable under section 19 for any amount in respect of
  statutory or contractual termination of employment, for severance pay
  or for any punitive damages related to termination of employment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A director is not liable under section 19 unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the port authority is sued for the debt within six
    months after it becomes due and execution is returned unsatisfied in
    whole or in part;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the debt is proved within six months after the
    earlier of the day of issue of the notice of intention to dissolve
    and the day of issue of the certificate of dissolution; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the port authority makes an assignment or is made
    subject to a receiving order under the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency
    Act](/eng/acts/B-3) and a claim for the debt is proved no later than
    six months after the date of the assignment or receiving order.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A director is not liable under section 19 unless the
  director is sued for the debt while in office or within two years
  after ceasing to be in office.

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** If execution has issued,
the amount recoverable from a director is the amount remaining
unsatisfied after execution.

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  director satisfies a debt referred to in section 19 that is proved in
  liquidation and dissolution or bankruptcy proceedings, the director is
  entitled to any preference to which the employee would have been
  entitled and, if a judgment has been obtained, the director is
  entitled to an assignment of the judgment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A director who satisfies a debt referred to in
  section 19 is entitled to contribution from the other directors who
  were liable for the debt.

<!-- -->

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Directors who vote for or consent to
  resolutions authorizing any of the following matters are jointly and
  severally, or solidarily, liable to restore to the port authority any
  amounts or property so distributed or paid and not otherwise recovered
  by the port authority:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} financial assistance contrary to section 32; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} payment of an indemnity or advance contrary to
    section 25 or 28.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A director who satisfies a judgment for a debt owed
  under this section is entitled to contribution from the other
  directors who were liable for the debt.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A director who is liable under subsection (1) may
  apply to a court for an order to recover money or property mentioned
  in that subsection.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A court may, on application under subsection (3), if
  it is satisfied that it is equitable to do so,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} order any person to pay or deliver to the director
    the money or property mentioned in subsection (1); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make any order that it sees fit.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} An action to enforce a liability imposed by this
  section may not be commenced more than two years after the date of the
  resolution authorizing the action complained of.

### [Defence of Due Diligence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026122 .Subheading}

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A director is not liable
under any of sections 19 or 21 to 23 if the director exercised the care,
diligence and skill that a reasonably prudent person would have
exercised in comparable circumstances to prevent a failure to fulfil
their duties, including reliance in good faith on financial statements
of the port authority, on the reports of experts and on information
presented by officers or professionals.

### [Indemnities and Advances]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026124 .Subheading}

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A port
  authority may indemnify a person who is a director or officer of the
  port authority, or a former director or officer, against all the
  costs, charges and expenses, including an amount paid to settle an
  action or satisfy a claim, reasonably incurred by the person in
  respect of any civil, criminal, administrative, investigative or other
  proceeding in which the person is involved by reason of being or
  having been a director or officer of the port authority.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A port authority may not indemnify a person under
  subsection (1) unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person acted honestly and in good faith with a
    view to the best interests of the port authority; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a criminal or administrative
    proceeding, the person had reasonable grounds for believing that the
    conduct was lawful.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A port authority may not indemnify a person under
  subsection (1) in respect of an action by or on behalf of the port
  authority against the person unless a court so orders.

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsection 25(1),
a person referred to in subsection 25(1) is entitled to indemnity for
the costs, charges and expenses referred to in that subsection if the
person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} was not judged by a court to have committed any fault
  or omitted to do anything that the person ought to have done; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} fulfils the conditions prescribed in subsection
  25(2).

<!-- -->

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  court may, on application by a port authority or a person referred to
  in section 26, approve an indemnity referred to in that section and
  make any further order that it sees fit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The court may, in respect of the application, order
  notice to be given to any interested person and the person is entitled
  to appear and be heard in person or by counsel.

<!-- -->

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A port
  authority may advance moneys to a person who is a director or officer
  of the port authority, or a former director or officer, in respect of
  all the costs, charges and expenses that the person may reasonably
  incur in respect of any civil, criminal, administrative, investigative
  or other proceeding in which the person is involved by reason of being
  or having been a director or officer of the port authority, including
  an amount to settle an action or satisfy a claim.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A port authority may not advance moneys to a person
  under subsection (1) in respect of an action by or on behalf of the
  port authority against the person unless a court so orders.

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** If an advance is made
under subsection 28(1), the person shall repay the moneys if a court
judges that the person did not fulfil the conditions prescribed in
subsection 25(2).

### [Remuneration of Officers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026141 .Subheading}

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Unless the letters patent
or by-laws provide otherwise, the board of directors of a port authority
may fix the remuneration of the officers or employees of the port
authority.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Financial Matters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1026143 .Part}

### [Categories of Investments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026144 .Subheading}

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 32(b) of the Act, a port authority may invest in the following
categories of investments:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an investment in a subsidiary that the port authority
  is authorized to incorporate under its letters patent;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any debt that, on the day on which the debt is
  acquired, is of a category of investments that meets the requirements
  set out in the schedule; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an investment made in accordance with the [Pension
  Benefits Standards Act, 1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01) and the regulations
  made under that Act if the moneys invested are moneys of a pension
  fund for a pension plan that is administered by the port authority and
  registered in accordance with that Act.

### [Annual Financial Statements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026155 .Subheading}

#### [Content of Annual Financial Statements]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1026156 .Subheading}

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** The annual financial
statements required by section 36 of the Act need not be designated by
the names set out in subsection 37(2) of the Act.

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** Every port authority and
every wholly-owned subsidiary of a port authority shall, in its annual
financial statements, set out comparative information from the previous
fiscal year's annual financial statements.

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A port
  authority and a wholly-owned subsidiary of a port authority shall each
  ensure that its annual financial statements include at least the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} wages, salaries and employee benefits;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} professional fees and fees for consulting services;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} maintenance and repair costs;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} depreciation of fixed assets; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} real property taxes and grants in lieu of taxes.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A port authority shall ensure that its annual
  financial statements include the name, title and total remuneration
  --- incorporating fees, allowances and other benefits --- of each of
  the four persons who, in that fiscal year, held office with or were
  employed by one or more members of the corporate group made up of the
  port authority and its wholly-owned subsidiaries, and who received the
  highest remuneration from the group overall.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) applies only if the remuneration in
  the fiscal year exceeds

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a fiscal year that ends on or before
    December 31, 1999, \$100,000; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a fiscal year that ends on or before
    December 31 of each subsequent year, the amount established in the
    previous year, adjusted on January 1 of that subsequent year by the
    change in the consumer price index between the two previous years.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The consumer price index for any year is the annual
  average all-items consumer price index for Canada (not seasonally
  adjusted) published by Statistics Canada in respect of that year.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The chief executive officer and the directors of the
  port authority or a wholly-owned subsidiary shall not be counted for
  the purpose of subsection (2).

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If more than four persons qualify as receiving the
  highest remuneration because some of them receive the same
  remuneration, the prescribed information on all of them shall be
  included in the financial statements.

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** A port authority and a
wholly-owned subsidiary of a port authority shall each ensure that its
annual financial statements include the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} total estimated capital expenditures, including those
  authorized or committed at year end, and, in respect of each capital
  project for which the combined capital expenditures are estimated to
  exceed \$1 million, a brief description of the project and the amount
  of the expenditures; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} total estimated proceeds from capital dispositions,
  including dispositions authorized, committed or receivable at year
  end, and, in respect of each capital disposition for which they exceed
  or are estimated to exceed \$1 million, the amount of the proceeds and
  a brief description.

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** Information required by
sections 35 and 36 may be presented in any one of the categories of
income or expenses that is appropriate, broken down among them or
presented in a footnote or endnote to the statements.

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 37(3) of the Act, a port authority and a
  wholly-owned subsidiary of a port authority shall each specify in its
  annual financial statements

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, title and total remuneration of every
    director and every chief executive officer who held office with it
    in that fiscal year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, title and total remuneration of every
    officer and every employee of it who held office or was employed in
    that fiscal year if the remuneration exceeded the threshold
    prescribed in section 39.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The port authority and the wholly-owned subsidiary
  shall each show in its annual financial statements a breakdown into
  the following amounts of remuneration in respect of each person
  referred to in subsection (1):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} salaries and fees;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} allowances; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} other benefits.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Information required by this section may be presented
  in any one of the categories of expenses that is appropriate or in a
  footnote or endnote to the statements.

#### [Remuneration Threshold]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1026186 .Subheading}

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of paragraph 37(3)(c) of the Act, the remuneration
  threshold applicable to officers and employees of a port authority or
  of a wholly-owned subsidiary of a port authority is \$150,000.
  Beginning on January 1, 2000, the remuneration threshold is adjusted
  on January 1 of each year by the change in the consumer price index
  between the previous two years.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The consumer price index for any year is the annual
  average all-items consumer price index for Canada (not seasonally
  adjusted) published by Statistics Canada in respect of that year.

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  board of directors of a port authority shall approve the annual

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A port authority shall not issue, publish or

### [Audits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026196 .Subheading}

#### [Qualification of Auditor]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1026197 .Subheading}

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), an
  auditor of a port authority shall be independent of the port
  authority, any body corporate controlled by the port authority and
  their directors and officers.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} independence is a question of fact; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person is not independent if the person or the
    person's business partner

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is a business partner, director, officer or
      employee of the port authority or a body corporate controlled by
      the port authority, or a business partner of a director, officer
      or employee of any of them,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has the beneficial ownership of, or directly or
      indirectly controls, a material interest in a borrowing of the
      port authority or a share or borrowing of a body corporate
      controlled by the port authority, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has been a receiver, receiver-manager,
      liquidator or trustee in bankruptcy of the port authority or a
      body corporate controlled by the port authority within two years
      before the day on which the appointment as auditor would take
      effect.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An auditor who becomes disqualified under this
  section shall, subject to subsection (5), resign without delay on
  becoming aware of the disqualification.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} An interested person may apply to a court for an
  order declaring an auditor to be disqualified under this section and
  the office of auditor to be vacant.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} An interested person may apply to a court for an
  order exempting an auditor from disqualification under this section
  and the court may, if it is satisfied that an exemption would not
  unfairly prejudice the port authority, make an exemption order on any
  terms that it thinks fit, which order may have retroactive effect.

#### [Appointment of Auditor]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1026209 .Subheading}

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  board of directors of a port authority shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at its first meeting, appoint an auditor to hold
    office until the close of the first annual meeting; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} before the first and each subsequent annual
    meeting, appoint an auditor to hold office from the close of that
    annual meeting until the close of the next following annual meeting.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(b), if an auditor is not
  appointed before an annual meeting, the incumbent auditor continues in
  office until a successor is appointed.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The port authority shall announce at the annual
  meeting the name of the auditor appointed to hold office beginning at

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a vacancy occurs in the office of auditor and the
  board of directors does not appoint an auditor, a court may, on the
  application of an interested person, appoint and fix the remuneration

  board of directors.

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Risk Assessment and Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1026285 .Part}

### [Risk Assessments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026286 .Subheading}

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  port authority and every wholly-owned subsidiary of a port authority
  shall ensure that, in respect of itself, a risk assessment is
  conducted by a competent risk assessor and contains an assessment
  relating to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} risk, risk management, property value, risk
    transfer and probable maximum loss;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} insurance coverage, limits and deductibles; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} underwriters.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The risk assessment shall be completed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} not later than six months after the day on which
    the port authority is continued, deemed to be incorporated or
    incorporated under the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at least every five years after the most recent
    risk assessment.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The board of directors of every port authority and of
  each of the wholly-owned subsidiaries of a port authority shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} review each risk assessment report within 30 days
    after it is received by the port authority or the subsidiary; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} approve the insurance coverage annually.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} In conducting a risk assessment, a risk assessor may,
  if acting reasonably and in good faith, rely on risk assessments
  conducted in respect of the property, liabilities and activities
  referred to in subsection 56(1).

### [Property and Liability Insurance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026299 .Subheading}

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  port authority and every wholly-owned subsidiary of a port authority
  shall ensure that insurance is maintained in accordance with
  subsection (2) in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the property that the port authority or subsidiary
    manages, holds, owns or occupies; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} liability for the activities in which the port
    authority or subsidiary engages and for the activities that take
    place in the port.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The insurance shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name the port authority or the wholly-owned
    subsidiary as the insured party or an additional insured;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} name Her Majesty in right of Canada as an
    additional insured;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} cover the risks and have limits and deductibles
    that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a prudent person in the position of owner of the
      property or a prudent person engaging in the activities referred
      to in paragraph (1)(b) would obtain or would require to be
      obtained, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} take into account the most recent risk
      assessment conducted under section 55; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be maintained with an underwriter that a prudent
    person in the position of owner of the property or a prudent person
    engaging in the activities referred to in paragraph (1)(b) would
    choose or require to be chosen, taking into account the most recent
    risk assessment conducted under section 55.

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a former or current director or officer of the

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who, at the request of the subsidiary, has

    acted or is acting in a capacity similar to that of a director or

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cover the risks and have limits and deductibles

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} would be obtained by a prudent person in the

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} take into account the most recent risk
      assessment conducted under section 55; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be maintained with an underwriter that a prudent

    choose, taking into account the most recent risk assessment
    conducted under section 55.

## [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Crown Indemnity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1026328 .Part}

- **[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A port
  authority shall indemnify Her Majesty in right of Canada for any
  action, claim or demand brought against, discharged or satisfied by
  Her Majesty, or any liability, obligation, loss, damage, cost or
  expense incurred, discharged or satisfied by Her Majesty because the
  port authority acted, or in consequence of the port authority acting,
  as agent of Her Majesty, including, without limiting the generality of
  the foregoing, an action, claim, demand, liability, obligation, loss,
  damage, cost or expense arising

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by or through the acts or omissions of the
    employees, agents, contractors, tenants or invitees of the port
    authority or those of any other person; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a judgment or decision that Her
    Majesty satisfies, in whole or in part, under subsection 23(1) of
    the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) applies if the action, claim, demand,
  liability, obligation, loss, damage, cost or expense arises

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} from the port authority's exercise of, or its
    failure to exercise, any power or right under the Act, the
    regulations under the Act or its letters patent; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by operation of law or otherwise, in respect of the
    operation of the port, including the management, using, occupying,
    leasing or licensing of federal real property.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if the action, claim,
  demand, liability, obligation, loss, damage, cost or expense is in
  respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the administration, by the Minister, of the Act or
    the letters patent; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an agreement or contract between Her Majesty and
    the port authority, except as provided in the agreement or contract.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall provide to the port authority
  notice of any action, claim, demand, liability, obligation, loss,
  damage, cost or expense for which the port authority is to indemnify
  Her Majesty and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of federal real property, the port
    authority shall undertake and defend any legal proceedings with
    respect to that property, as required by paragraph 45(1)(c) of the
    Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the Minister shall provide to
    the port authority an opportunity, in consultation with the Minister
    and subject to the terms and conditions that the Minister may
    specify, to assume control of any negotiations, settlement or
    defence and to discharge or satisfy the action, claim, demand,
    liability, obligation, loss, damage, cost or expense.

## [PART 5.1]{.HLabel1}[Amalgamation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1026342 .Part}

- **[[59.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by
  issuing a certificate of intent to amalgamate, require two or more
  port authorities to amalgamate and continue as one port authority in
  accordance with the certificate and may, at any time after the period
  set out in subsection (2), amalgamate the port authorities by issuing
  a certificate of amalgamation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of the certificate of intent to amalgamate
  shall be published in at least one major newspaper that is published
  or distributed in the municipalities where the ports managed by a port
  authority specified in the certificate are situated. The notice shall
  state that interested persons may, in accordance with the notice, make
  written representations to the Minister within 30 days after the day
  on which the notice is published.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-103, s. 1
:::

**[[59.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59.2 .sectionLabel}** A port authority
specified in a certificate of intent to amalgamate may, for the purposes
of the amalgamation, disclose personal information about its employees
to any other port authority specified in the certificate.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-103, s. 1
:::

- **[[59.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59.3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may revoke a
  certificate of intent to amalgamate by issuing a certificate of
  revocation of intent to amalgamate at any time before the certificate
  of amalgamation is issued.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of the certificate of revocation of intent
  to amalgamate shall be published in at least one major newspaper that
  is published or distributed in the municipalities where the ports
  managed by a port authority specified in the certificate of intent to
  amalgamate are situated. If possible, the notice shall be published in
  the same newspapers as the notice of the certificate of intent to
  amalgamate.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The revocation takes effect on the date set out in
  the certificate of revocation of intent to amalgamate.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-103, s. 1
:::

**[[59.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59.4 .sectionLabel}** A certificate of
intent to amalgamate and, if applicable, a certificate of revocation of
intent to amalgamate shall be sent to each port authority specified in
the certificate.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-103, s. 1
:::

**[[59.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59.5 .sectionLabel}** A certificate of
amalgamation shall specify the day on which the amalgamation takes
effect and contain the letters patent of the amalgamated port authority,
which include the information set out in subsection 8(2) of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-103, s. 1
:::

**[[59.6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59.6 .sectionLabel}** Any certificate
issued under this Part shall be published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-103, s. 1
:::

**[[59.7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59.7 .sectionLabel}** On the day on which
an amalgamation takes effect

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} every director of an amalgamating port authority who
  remains in office continues as a director of the amalgamated port
  authority for the balance of their term or until they cease to hold
  office in accordance with subsection 19(1) of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the navigable waters within the jurisdiction of each
  amalgamating port authority continue to be within the jurisdiction of
  the amalgamated port authority;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the property, rights and interests of each
  amalgamating port authority continue to be the property, rights and
  interests of the amalgamated port authority;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the management of any federal real property or
  federal immovable that the Minister has given to an amalgamating port
  authority under subsection 44(2) of the Act continues to be given to
  the amalgamated port authority;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the real property or immovables occupied by each
  amalgamating port authority continue to be occupied by the amalgamated
  port authority;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the amalgamated port authority continues to be liable
  for the obligations of each amalgamating port authority;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} an existing cause of action, claim or liability to
  prosecution is unaffected;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a civil, criminal or administrative action or
  proceeding pending by or against an amalgamating port authority may be
  continued to be prosecuted by or against the amalgamated port
  authority;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a conviction against, or ruling, order or judgment in
  favour of or against, an amalgamating port authority may be enforced
  by or against the amalgamated port authority;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} every fee fixed by an amalgamating port authority
  continues in force until the expiry date specified in the provision
  that fixes the fee or until the amalgamated port authority repeals
  that provision or replaces the fee; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the letters patent contained in the certificate of
  amalgamation are the letters patent of the amalgamated port authority.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-103, s. 1
:::

**[[59.8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59.8 .sectionLabel}** An amalgamation does
not constitute a disposition of the property, rights and interests of an
amalgamating port authority to the amalgamated port authority.

- SOR/2007-103, s. 1

## [PART 6]{.HLabel1}[Liquidation and Dissolution]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1026383 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026384 .Subheading}

**[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** In this Part, the court
having jurisdiction is the court in the place where the port authority
has its registered office.

### [Certificate of Intent]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026386 .Subheading}

- **[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  issuance of a certificate of intent to dissolve under subsection 55(1)
  of the Act and subject to the provisions of the certificate and
  section 62 of these Regulations, the port authority shall cease to
  carry on business except the business that is required for an orderly
  liquidation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 62, after the issuance of a
  certificate of intent to dissolve, the port authority shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} without delay have notice of the certificate sent
    to each of its known creditors;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} without delay take reasonable steps to give notice
    of the certificate in each province where the port authority was
    carrying on business at the time the certificate of intent to
    dissolve was issued;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} proceed to collect its property, dispose of
    properties that are not to be distributed in kind, discharge all its
    obligations and do all the other acts required to liquidate its
    business; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} after complying with paragraphs (a) and (b) and
    adequately providing for the payment or discharge of all its
    obligations, remit the proceeds of liquidation to Her Majesty in
    right of Canada in accordance with subsection 55(1) of the Act.

### [Cessation of Business and Powers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026394 .Subheading}

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** If a liquidator is
appointed to carry out a liquidation of the assets of a port authority,
the powers of the directors cease and vest in the liquidator, unless the
liquidator or a court authorizes otherwise.

### [Application for Supervision of Liquidation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026396 .Subheading}

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** The Minister or any
interested person may, at any time during the liquidation of a port
authority, apply to a court for an order that the liquidation be
continued under the supervision of the court as provided in this Part,
and on that application the court may so order and make any further
order it thinks fit.

### [Powers of Court]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026398 .Subheading}

**[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** In connection with the
dissolution or the liquidation and dissolution of a port authority,
subject to the certificate of intent, a court may, if it is satisfied
that the port authority is able to pay or adequately provide for the
discharge of all its obligations, make any order it thinks fit
including, without limiting the generality of the foregoing,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an order appointing or replacing a liquidator, with
  or without security, and fixing the remuneration of the liquidator,
  who, if the court so orders, may be a director or officer of the port
  authority;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an order appointing or replacing inspectors or
  referees, specifying their powers and fixing their remuneration;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an order determining that notice be given to any
  interested person or dispensing with giving notice to any person;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an order determining the validity of any claims made
  against the port authority;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an order, at any stage of the proceedings,
  restraining the directors or officers from

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} exercising any of their powers, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} collecting or receiving any debt or other property
    of the port authority, or from paying out or transferring any
    property of the port authority, except as permitted by the court;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} an order determining and enforcing the duty or
  liability of any present or former director or officer to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the port authority, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} third parties, for an obligation of the port
    authority;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} an order approving the payment, satisfaction or
  compromise of claims against the port authority and the retention of
  assets for that purpose, and determining the adequacy of provisions
  for the payment or discharge of obligations of the port authority,
  whether the obligations are liquidated, unliquidated, future or
  contingent;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} an order disposing of or destroying the documents and
  records of the port authority;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} on the application of a creditor, the inspectors or
  the liquidator, an order giving directions on any matter arising
  during the liquidation;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} after notice has been given to all interested
  persons, an order relieving the liquidator from any omission or
  default on any terms that the court thinks fit and confirming any act
  of the liquidator;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} subject to any restriction applicable to the port
  authority, an order approving any proposed interim or final
  distribution in money or in property; and

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} on the application of a director, an officer, a
  creditor or the liquidator,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an order staying the liquidation on any terms and
    conditions that the court thinks fit,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an order continuing or discontinuing the
    liquidation proceedings, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an order to the liquidator to restore to the port
    authority all its remaining property.

### [Duties of Liquidator]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026419 .Subheading}

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** A liquidator shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} without delay after being appointed, give notice of
  the appointment to the Minister and to each claimant and creditor
  known to the liquidator;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} without delay after being appointed, publish notice
  in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) and once a week for
  two consecutive weeks in a newspaper published in the place where the
  port authority has its registered office and take reasonable steps to
  give that notice in each province where the port authority carried on
  business, requiring any person who

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is indebted to the port authority to render an
    account and pay to the liquidator any amount owing at the day, time
    and place specified,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} possesses property of the port authority to
    deliver it to the liquidator at the day, time and place specified,
    and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has a claim against the port authority, whether
    liquidated, unliquidated, future or contingent, to present
    particulars of the claim in writing to the liquidator not later than
    two months after the date of first publication of the notice;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} without delay after being appointed, take into
  custody and control the property of the port authority;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} without delay after being appointed, open and
  maintain a trust account for money received by the liquidator in the
  course of the liquidation;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} keep accounts of the moneys of the port authority
  received and paid out in the course of the liquidation;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} maintain separate lists of the creditors and other
  persons who have claims against the port authority;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} if at any time the liquidator determines that the
  port authority is unable to pay or adequately provide for the
  discharge of its obligations, without delay apply to the court for
  directions;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} deliver to the Minister and the court, if applicable,
  at least once in every 12-month period after appointment and, in
  addition, more often as the Minister or the court may require, the
  information required by section 36 of the Act and sections 34 to 38 of
  these Regulations; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} after the final accounts are approved under section
  67, without delay remit the proceeds of liquidation to Her Majesty in
  right of Canada in accordance with subsection 55(1) of the Act.

### [Powers of Liquidator]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026433 .Subheading}

- **[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  liquidator may, subject to the certificate of intent,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} retain lawyers, notaries, accountants, engineers,
    appraisers and other professional advisers;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} bring, defend or take part in any civil, criminal
    or administrative action or proceeding in the name and on behalf of
    the port authority;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} carry on the business of the port authority that is
    required for an orderly liquidation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} subject to any restriction applicable to the port
    authority, sell any property of the port authority by public auction
    or private sale;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} do all acts and execute any documents in the name
    and on behalf of the port authority;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} subject to any restriction applicable to the port
    authority, borrow money;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} settle or compromise any claims by or against the
    port authority; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} do all other things necessary for the liquidation
    of the port authority and the distribution of its property.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A liquidator is not liable under these Regulations if
  the liquidator exercised the care, diligence and skill that a
  reasonably prudent person would have exercised in comparable
  circumstances to prevent a failure to fulfil their duties, including
  reliance in good faith on financial statements of the port authority,
  on the reports of experts and on information presented by officers or
  professionals.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a liquidator has reason to believe that a person
  possesses or controls or has concealed, withheld or misappropriated
  property of the port authority, the liquidator may apply to a court
  for an order requiring the person to appear before the court on the
  day and at the time and place specified in the order and to be
  examined.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the examination discloses that the person has
  concealed, withheld or misappropriated property of the port authority,
  the court may order the person to restore it to the liquidator or pay
  compensation for it.

### [Costs of Liquidation and Final Accounting]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026447 .Subheading}

- **[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any restriction
  applicable to the port authority, a liquidator shall pay the costs of
  liquidation out of the property of the port authority and shall pay or
  make adequate provision for all the claims against the port authority.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within one year after being appointed and after
  paying or making adequate provision for all the claims against the
  port authority, the liquidator shall apply to the court for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an approval of the final accounts of the liquidator
    and for an order permitting the remittance of the proceeds of
    liquidation to Her Majesty in right of Canada in accordance with
    subsection 55(1) of the Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an extension of time, setting out the reasons for
    the extension.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The liquidator shall give notice of intention to make
  an application under subsection (2) to the Minister, to any inspector
  appointed by the court and to any person who provided a security or
  fidelity bond for the liquidation, and the liquidator shall publish
  the notice in a major newspaper published in the place where the port
  authority has its registered office or as otherwise directed by the
  court.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the liquidator fails to make the application, the
  Minister may apply to the court for an order requiring the liquidator
  to show cause why a final accounting and distribution should not be
  made.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the court approves the final accounts rendered by
  the liquidator, the court shall make

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an order directing the custody or disposal of the
    documents and records of the port authority; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an order discharging the liquidator that is
    contingent on the liquidator, forthwith on the issuance of the
    order, sending a certified copy of it to the Minister.

### [Continuation of Actions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026458 .Subheading}

- **[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite the dissolution of a port
  authority,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a civil, criminal, administrative, investigative or
    other proceeding by or against the port authority before its
    dissolution may be continued as if the port authority had not been
    dissolved;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a civil, criminal, administrative, investigative or
    other proceeding may be brought against the port authority within
    two years after its dissolution as if the port authority had not
    been dissolved; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any property that would have been available to
    satisfy a judgment or order if the port authority had not been
    dissolved remains available for that purpose.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Service of a document on a port authority after its
  dissolution may be effected by serving it on the Minister.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite the dissolution of a port authority, a person
  to whom any of the port authority's property is distributed is liable
  to any other person claiming under subsection (1) to the extent of the
  amount received by that person on the distribution, and an action to
  enforce that liability may be brought within two years after the date
  of the dissolution of the port authority.

### [Unknown Claimants]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1026466 .Subheading}

- **[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  dissolution of a port authority, the portion of the property
  distributable to a creditor who cannot be found shall be converted
  into money and paid to Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The payment constitutes satisfaction of the debt or
  claim of the creditor.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If at any time a person establishes entitlement to
  any moneys paid to Her Majesty in right of Canada under these
  Regulations, the Receiver General shall pay an equivalent amount to
  the person out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund.

## [PART 7]{.HLabel1}[Offences and Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1026471 .Part}

**[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** A person who knowingly
makes or assists in making a financial statement referred to in section
36 of the Act or paragraph 53(3)(a) or 65(h) of these Regulations is
guilty of an offence if the financial statement

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} contains an untrue statement of a material fact; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} omits to state a material fact that is required or
  necessary in order to prevent a statement contained in the financial
  statement from being misleading in the light of the circumstances in
  which it was made.

**[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** A person referred to in
section 70 is liable to a fine of not more than \$5,000 in the case of
an individual, and of not more than \$50,000 in the case of a
corporation.

**[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on March 1, 1999.
