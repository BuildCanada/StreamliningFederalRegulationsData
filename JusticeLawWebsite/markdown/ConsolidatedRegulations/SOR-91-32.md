## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934418 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15).
(*Loi*)

## [Prescribed Goods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934420 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
7 of Schedule VII to the Act, the following imported goods are
prescribed goods:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} excisable goods and wine;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods that are prescribed property for the purposes
  of section 143.1 of the Act, if the supplier of the goods is required
  to register under Subdivision d of Division V of Part IX of the Act
  and is not so registered;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} goods for which the value for duty is reduced by the
  application of section 85 of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} goods that are purchased from a retailer in Canada
  and mailed or transported from outside Canada directly to the
  purchaser; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} goods that are purchased or ordered through or from a
  person in Canada acting for or on behalf of a person outside Canada
  who is selling the goods.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-63, s. 2
