## [Prohibited Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-675805 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited activities

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Paragraphs 510(1)(a) and
(b) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) apply in respect of the holding,
managing and other dealing with real property in Canada by a foreign
bank or an entity associated with a foreign bank if that holding,
managing or other dealing involves the making or acquiring of loans or
advances on security of real property in Canada.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-675808 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 514 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), as enacted by
section 132 of the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1), chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, comes
into force.
