## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550201 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Shipbuilding Industry Assistance
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._348).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550203 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an application for
    assistance made pursuant to section 4; ([demande]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[application date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[application date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, subject to section 9,
    means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a shipbuilder building a ship to
      be owned by that shipbuilder, the day on which a completed
      application form, together with a copy of the plans and
      specifications of the ship, is received by the Minister, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the day on which a completed
      application form, together with a copy of the contract, is
      received by the Minister; ([date de la demande]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[approved cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approved cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the approved cost of an
    eligible ship determined by the Minister pursuant to section 14;
    ([frais approuvés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a grant made pursuant to
    section 3 to a shipbuilder in respect of an eligible ship;
    ([aide]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[building]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[building]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
    [[construction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a ship, includes
    the conversion of a ship; ([construction]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[completed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[completed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with reference to an eligible
    ship, means that the ship has been built in Canada and delivered to
    a shipowner and that a certificate of registry has been issued for
    that ship under the [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9) or that
    such other form of documentation has been issued for the ship as may
    be specified in a subsidy agreement; ([achèvement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a contract in writing entered
    into by a shipbuilder for the construction in Canada by the
    shipbuilder of one or more ships; ([contrat]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[conversion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[conversion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a ship, means the
    work or alterations done to a ship that, in the opinion of the
    Minister, substantially change its size, function or other
    characteristics; ([transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a ship that is declared
    by the Minister, pursuant to section 6, to be an eligible ship for
    the purposes of these Regulations; ([navire
    admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fishing vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fishing vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that is
    intended for use in the catching or trapping of fish for sale;
    ([bateau de pêche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[improvement agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[improvement agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement in
    writing entered into between Her Majesty and a shipbuilder pursuant
    to section 11; ([accord d'amélioration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[improvement plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[improvement plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a plan for the
    improvement of shipyard performance submitted by a shipbuilder
    pursuant to subsection 10(1); ([plan
    d'amélioration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[improvement project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[improvement project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a project that
    forms a specific element of an improvement plan and whose
    description, estimated cost and relationship to the strategy of the
    improvement plan are identified therein; ([projet
    d'amélioration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[maximum approved cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[maximum approved cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the maximum
    approved cost of an eligible ship as determined pursuant to
    subsection 13(1); ([coût maximal approuvé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Industry,
    Trade and Commerce; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shipbuilder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shipbuilder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a company incorporated in
    Canada that is engaged in the business of building ships in Canada;
    ([constructeur de navires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[subsidy agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[subsidy agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement between
    the Crown and a shipbuilder pursuant to section 7. ([accord de
    subvention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Payment of Assistance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550226 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  accordance with these Regulations, the Minister may, in respect of
  each eligible ship or each series of eligible ships that, in the
  opinion of the Minister, are similar, authorize the following
  assistance:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the grant of a subsidy pursuant to section 8;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the grant of a contribution pursuant to section 12;
    or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} both of the grants referred to in paragraphs (a)
    and (b).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a ship is intended to be owned by Her Majesty
  in right of Canada, no subsidy shall be payable in respect of the
  construction thereof.

## [Application for Assistance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550233 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application for
assistance shall be submitted to the Minister in the form set out in the
schedule together with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} evidence that the shipbuilder has, in all respects,
  sufficient resources to complete the ship or ships in respect of which
  the application is made;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} evidence that the shipbuilder is able to carry out
  the construction of the ship without having to over-expand his
  facilities in relation to anticipated long-term demand;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} evidence that the construction of the ship will not
  have a detrimental effect on the long-term competitive status of the
  shipbuilder or of the Canadian shipbuilding and repair industry;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a ship to be built for a foreign
  owner, evidence that the construction of the ship will not have a
  detrimental effect on the continuing availability of facilities to
  satisfy domestic requirements for ship construction and repair;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} where the ship is being built by a shipbuilder for a
  person other than the shipbuilder, a copy of the contract;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} where the ship is being built by a shipbuilder to be
  owned by that shipbuilder, a copy of the plans and specifications of
  the ship; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the information required by the application form.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), an application shall be made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the ship is being built by a shipbuilder for
    a person other than the shipbuilder, within 60 days after

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the execution of a contract for the construction
      of the ship in respect of which the application is made, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the commencement of the construction of the ship
      whichever is the earlier; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the ship is being built by a shipbuilder for
    that shipbuilder, within 60 days after the commencement of the
    construction of the ship in respect of which the application is
    made.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may extend the time set out in
  subsection (1) for making an application where, in his opinion, the
  circumstances of the case require an extension.

## [Eligible Ship]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550252 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may declare a
ship to be an eligible ship if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ship, after completion, will be at least

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 100 tons gross tonnage if self-propelled,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 200 tons gross tonnage if not self-propelled,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 50 tons gross tonnage if a tug, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} 23 m in overall length if a fishing vessel;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the ship is not intended to be used by its owner
  primarily for personal recreation; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} construction of the ship commenced, or a contract for
  the construction of the ship was executed, prior to July 1, 1985.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-126, s. 3
- SOR/86-48, s. 1
:::

## [Subsidy Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550263 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an
  application submitted pursuant to section 4 by a shipbuilder is
  approved by the Minister, a subsidy agreement shall be entered into
  between Her Majesty and the shipbuilder in respect of each eligible
  ship or each series of eligible ships that, in the opinion of the
  Minister, are similar.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every subsidy agreement shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribe the maximum amount of the subsidy that
    may be granted under section 8 in respect of the eligible ship;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribe the maximum amount of the contribution
    that may be granted under section 12 in respect of the eligible
    ship;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide for the payment of any subsidy to the
    shipbuilder of an eligible ship by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a lump sum payment following completion of the
      construction of the ship, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (d), progress payments not
      exceeding in the aggregate 85 per cent of the estimated subsidy
      payable before the ship is completed and the balance of the
      subsidy in one or more payments after completion of the ship;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide that the lump sum payment of any subsidy
    under subparagraph (c)(i) or the final payment of any subsidy under
    subparagraph (c)(ii) shall not be made until, in the opinion of the
    Minister, all the terms in the subsidy agreement have been complied
    with;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide that the grant of a contribution payable
    pursuant to section 12 shall be in respect of moneys expended in
    accordance with an improvement agreement;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} provide that the shipbuilder shall use Canadian
    materials, components, equipment and services

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the construction of the eligible ship, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for the improvement of shipyard performance

    if such materials, components, equipment and services are, in the
    opinion of the Minister, available and competitive;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} provide that the shipbuilder shall repay that
    portion of any contribution granted that exceeds the contribution
    payable pursuant to section 12; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} set out the construction schedule of the ship or
    ships.

## [Subsidy Payable]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550280 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a grant of a subsidy may be paid by Her Majesty to
  a shipbuilder in respect of a ship in an amount not exceeding

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 14 per cent of the approved cost of the ship when
    the application date is before January 1, 1976;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 13 per cent of the approved cost of the ship when
    the application date is after December 31, 1975 and before January
    1, 1977;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 12 per cent of the approved cost of the ship when
    the application date is after December 31, 1976 and before January
    1, 1978;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} 11 per cent of the approved cost of the ship when
    the application date is after December 31, 1977 and before January
    1, 1979;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} 10 per cent of the approved cost of the ship when
    the application date is after December 31, 1978 and before January
    1, 1980;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} 20 per cent of the approved cost of the ship when
    the application date is not earlier than March 1, 1977 and not later
    than June 30, 1980;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} nine per cent of the approved cost of the ship when

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the application date is after June 30, 1980 and
      the application is supported by a contract made before January 7,
      1983, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the ship is being built by a shipbuilder for
      that shipbuilder and construction of the ship commenced before
      January 7, 1983; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} nine per cent of the approved cost of the ship when
    the ship is completed before July 1, 1985 and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the ship is being built by a shipbuilder
      for a person other than the shipbuilder, the contract was made
      after January 6, 1983, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the ship is being built by a shipbuilder
      for that shipbuilder, construction commenced after January 6,
      1983.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a shipbuilder or shipowner has received or will
  receive assistance under the Industrial and Regional Development
  Program or the Defence Industry Productivity Program in respect of the
  construction of an eligible ship, the amount of assistance shall be
  deducted from the approved cost before the calculation of the subsidy
  payable pursuant to subsection (1).

## [Deferred Application Date]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550299 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  construction schedule does not provide for expeditious commencement of
  the construction of the eligible ship, commensurate with the
  shipbuilder's building program, or if the construction schedule does
  not provide for completion of the eligible ship within a time that
  appears reasonable to the Minister, then, for the purposes of section
  8, the application date shall be a date as determined by the Minister
  but the date so determined shall not be earlier than the application
  date.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the construction schedule of a subsidy agreement
  made pursuant to these Regulations in respect of an eligible ship is
  not followed by the shipbuilder and the ship is completed at a date
  later than that provided in the schedule, for causes not beyond the
  control of the shipbuilder and the shipowner, the Minister may, in his
  discretion, for the purposes of calculating the subsidy under section
  8, declare the application to have been received at a date that is
  correspondingly later.

## [Improvement Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550303 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  shipbuilder who applies for the grant of a contribution under these
  Regulations shall submit to the Minister, in a form acceptable to the
  Minister, a proposed strategic plan for the improvement of his
  shipyard including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} market and product assumptions and strategies, and
    the specific projects that relate to them;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on-going improvements relating to safety and
    technological conditions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement as to the expected improvements in
    capacity, productivity or safety relating to each project;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} financial statements for the last three fiscal
    years of the shipbuilder; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a projected balance sheet that reflects the
    implementation of individual elements of the plan.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The projects and improvements referred to in
  paragraphs (1)(a) and (b) may be undertaken or made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by the shipbuilder; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with the approval of the Minister, by a
    wholly-owned subsidiary of the shipbuilder or, if the shipbuilder
    itself is a wholly-owned subsidiary, by its parent company.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-833, s. 1
- SOR/85-126, s. 5
- SOR/86-48, s. 2
:::

## [Improvement Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550316 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an improvement plan is approved by the Minister, an improvement
  agreement shall be entered into between Her Majesty and the
  shipbuilder.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every improvement agreement shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} identify expenditures proposed and their
    relationship to the improvement plan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} include the terms and conditions in respect of the
    payment of any grant of a contribution pursuant to section 12;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide that the shipbuilder shall repay that
    portion of the contribution payable pursuant to section 12 that is
    in respect of equipment obtained under the improvement agreement and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} diverted to uses other than those provided in the
      approved plan, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} disposed of by the shipbuilder

    within five years of its acquisition or such shorter period as may
    be determined by the Minister; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide for an audit to be made by the external
    auditors of the shipbuilder of the cost of the improvement project
    and for the cost of the improvement project to be verified, at the
    discretion of the Minister, by a person authorized by the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-126, s. 6
- SOR/86-48, s. 3
:::

**[[11.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.1 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
declare an improvement project to be eligible for the grant of a
contribution if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the improvement project is identified in a current
  improvement agreement as set out in section 11;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any conditions placed on the implementation of the
  improvement project as specified in the improvement agreement have
  been met;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an improvement project to commence
  after the coming into force of this section, the plans for the
  appropriate improvement project are presented for consideration by the
  Minister before work on the improvement project begins;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the improvement project provides for the use of
  Canadian materials, components and services when, in the opinion of
  the Minister, they are available and competitive; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the shipbuilder provides a detailed list of all
  expenditures necessary for the project, to the satisfaction of the
  Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-126, s. 7
:::

## [Contribution Payable]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550337 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a grant
  of a contribution may be paid by Her Majesty to a shipbuilder in
  respect of a ship in an amount equal to the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} three per cent of the approved cost of the eligible
    ship; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 50 per cent of the cost of expenditure made by the
    shipbuilder in accordance with an improvement agreement made
    pursuant to section 11.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The maximum amount of any contribution that may be
  granted under this section is an amount equal to three per cent of the
  maximum approved cost.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a shipbuilder or shipowner has received or will
  receive financial support from provincial or municipal governments in
  respect of an expenditure made in accordance with an improvement
  agreement, the value of such financial support, as determined by the
  Minister, may be deducted from the cost of the expenditure for the
  purposes of paragraph 12(1)(b).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-126, s. 8
:::

## [Maximum Approved Cost]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550346 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  maximum approved cost of an eligible ship to be used in calculating
  the maximum subsidy and maximum contribution for the purposes of
  paragraphs 7(2)(a) and (b) shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the eligible ship is being built for Her
    Majesty in right of Canada, the contract price that may be paid to
    the shipbuilder for that ship; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, the quotient obtained by
    dividing the maximum price set out in the contract by the applicable
    figure set out in subsection 14(2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} From the amounts referred to in paragraphs (1)(a)
  and (b) there shall be deducted

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cost of features that are specified in the
    subsidy agreement as being, in the opinion of the Minister, not
    eligible for subsidy; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cost of any material, components, equipment and
    services to be obtained outside Canada where, in the opinion of the
    Minister, the material, components, equipment and services could
    have been obtained at a competitive cost in Canada.

## [Approved Cost]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550354 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 15, for the
  purpose of calculating the amount of any assistance payable under
  these Regulations, the approved cost shall be the lesser of the
  maximum approved cost and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the eligible ship was built for Her Majesty
    in right of Canada, the amount paid to the shipbuilder for the
    construction of the ship;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the eligible ship was built by a shipbuilder
    to be owned by him, the quotient obtained by dividing the capital
    cost of the ship as entered on the books of the company for income
    tax purposes by the applicable figure set out in subsection (2); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the eligible ship is built by a shipbuilder
    for a person other than a person described in paragraphs (a) or (b),
    the quotient obtained by dividing the amount paid to the shipbuilder
    in respect of construction of the ship, including whatever value the
    Minister may allow for owner-supplied equipment, by the applicable
    figure set out in subsection (2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the applicable
  figure set out in this subsection is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 0.86, where payment of assistance is to be made
    under paragraph 8(1)(a);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 0.87, where payment of assistance is to be made
    under paragraph 8(1)(b);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 0.88, where payment of assistance is to be made
    under paragraph 8(1)(c);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} 0.89, where payment of assistance is to be made
    under paragraph 8(1)(d);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} 0.90, where payment of assistance is to be made
    under paragraph 8(1)(e);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} 0.80 where payment of assistance is to be made
    under paragraph 8(1)(f);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} 0.91 where payment of assistance is to be made
    under paragraph 8(1)(g); and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} 0.91 where payment of assistance is to be made
    under paragraph 8(1)(h).

- SOR/85-126, s. 9

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  subsidy is to be granted to a shipbuilder in respect of a ship, the
  approved cost for that ship shall be the lesser of the maximum
  approved cost calculated pursuant to section 13 and the cost of
  construction of the ship as certified by an audit made by the Audit
  Services Bureau, Department of Supply and Services.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Where a subsidy is not to be granted to a
  shipbuilder in respect of a ship other than a ship owned by Her

  the lesser of the maximum approved cost calculated pursuant to section
  13 and the cost of construction of the ship as certified by an audit
  made by the external auditors of the shipbuilder.

- [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} The cost of construction of the ship certified
  pursuant to subsection (1.1) may be verified, at the discretion of the

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The cost of construction of the ship certified
  pursuant to subsection (1) shall not include the shipbuilder's profit.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
calculating the amount of any assistance payable under these
Regulations, there shall be deducted from the approved cost in respect
of any ship

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cost, as set out in the subsidy agreement, of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any component or equipment set out in the agreement
    that was not fitted or installed in the ship, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} unless the Minister otherwise directs, any
    material, component or equipment obtained outside Canada where the
    agreement specified that the material, component or equipment be
    made in Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any increase in the costs attributable to any change
  made to the plans or specifications relating to the construction of
  the ship that are made subsequent to the making of an application in
  respect of the ship;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the cost of any spare parts provided in respect of
  the ship where, in the opinion of the Minister, the provision of the
  parts is in excess of those required;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the cost of any material, component or equipment that
  was provided in respect of the construction of the ship that, when
  provided, was not new;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the cost of any supervision by a representative of or
  on behalf of the shipowner in respect of the construction of the ship;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the amounts, specified in the subsidy agreement, that
  relate to the cost of features of the ship that, in the opinion of the
  Minister, should by their nature be excluded from the approved cost;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the cost of any designs or plans, in respect of the
  construction of the ship, prepared by a person outside Canada where,
  in the opinion of the Minister, the designs or plans could have been
  prepared in Canada at a competitive cost; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the cost of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any insurance relating to the ship or its
    construction, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any interest or other pecuniary charge relating to
    the financing of the construction of the ship

  that is paid to a company outside Canada where, in the opinion of the
  Minister, the insurance or the financing could have been obtained from
  a company incorporated in Canada at a competitive cost.

## [Expiry Dates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550392 .Part}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding any other
provision of these Regulations,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} no contribution may be granted pursuant to these
  Regulations if the application date is after March 31, 1990;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} every improvement agreement entered into after the
  coming into force of this section shall provide that the improvement
  project shall be completed no later than December 31, 1990 and that no
  costs incurred after December 31, 1990 shall be eligible whether or
  not the project has been completed;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} no contribution may be paid in respect of costs
  incurred for an improvement project where those costs are claimed
  after March 31, 1991.
