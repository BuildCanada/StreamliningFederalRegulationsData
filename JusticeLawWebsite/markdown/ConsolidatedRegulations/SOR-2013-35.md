## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-802990 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Section 32 of the [Species
at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) does not apply to the following licences,
authorizations and documents for a period of one year from the date that
the Westslope Cutthroat Trout (Alberta population) is added to the List
of Wildlife Species at Risk set out in Schedule 1 to that Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the [Alberta Fishery Regulations,
  1998](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-246) to the extent that they authorize
  sportfishing;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licences issued under subsection 13(3) of the
  [Alberta Fishery Regulations, 1998](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-246); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the [National Parks of Canada Fishing
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1120) to the extent that they
  authorize fishing.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-802995 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
