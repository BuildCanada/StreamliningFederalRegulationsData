## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676400 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank
      Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[balance sheet value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[balance sheet value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of the
      shares and ownership interests held by an entity, means the value
      reported on the entity's balance sheet on an unconsolidated
      basis. ([valeur au bilan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[book value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[book value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[ \[Repealed, SOR/2008-164, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[specialized financing entity of an entity associated with a foreign bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[specialized financing entity of an entity associated with a
      foreign bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a specialized financing
      entity that is controlled by an entity associated with a foreign
      bank or in which an entity associated with a foreign bank has a
      substantial investment. ([entité s'occupant de financement spécial
      d'une entité liée à une banque étrangère]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[specialized financing entity of a foreign bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[specialized financing entity of a foreign
      bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a specialized financing entity
      that is controlled by a foreign bank or in which a foreign bank
      has a substantial investment. ([entité d'une banque étrangère
      s'occupant de financement spécial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- Meaning of [[specialized financing entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations and for the purpose of the
  definition [[specialized financing entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
  subsection 507(1) of the Act, [[specialized financing
  entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canadian entity that acquires or
  holds shares of, or ownership interests in, entities that a bank may
  acquire control of, or hold, acquire or increase a substantial
  investment in, under subsection 466(4) of the Act.

- SOR/2008-164, s. 1

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676413 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
specialized financing activities under paragraph 522.1(e) of the Act and
to the ownership by a foreign bank or an entity associated with a
foreign bank of shares of, or ownership interests in, a specialized
financing entity under paragraph 522.08(1)(b) of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-164, s. 2
:::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676418 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limits on investments

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** A foreign bank or an
entity associated with a foreign bank shall not, by way of specialized
financing activities, acquire or hold control of, or a substantial
investment in, a Canadian entity

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} referred to in any of paragraphs 468(1)(a) to (j) of
  the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is primarily engaged in the leasing of motor
  vehicles in Canada for the purpose of extending credit to a customer
  or financing a customer's acquisition of a motor vehicle;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} that is primarily engaged in providing temporary
  possession of personal property, including motor vehicles, to
  customers in Canada for a purpose other than to finance the customer's
  acquisition of the property; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} that acts as an insurance broker or agent in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-164, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Length of investment

**[[2.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.2 .sectionLabel}** A foreign bank or an
entity associated with a foreign bank shall not, by way of specialized
financing activities, hold control of, or hold a substantial investment
in, a Canadian entity for more than 13 consecutive years.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-164, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum authorized investment

**[[2.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.3 .sectionLabel}** A foreign bank shall
not, by way of specialized financing activities, acquire or hold control
of, or a substantial investment in, a Canadian entity if the aggregate
balance sheet value of the shares and ownership interests that the
following entities hold or would hold in the entity is more than \$250
million:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the foreign bank;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the entities associated with the foreign bank;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the specialized financing entities of the foreign
  bank; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the specialized financing entities of the entities
  associated with the foreign bank.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-164, s. 2
- SOR/2010-71, s. 4(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2.3-20080519.html "Link to previous version of section 2.3"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum authorized investment

**[[2.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.4 .sectionLabel}** An entity associated
with a foreign bank shall not, by way of specialized financing
activities, acquire or hold control of, or a substantial investment in,
a Canadian entity if the aggregate balance sheet value of the shares and
ownership interests that the following entities hold or would hold in
the entity is more than \$250 million:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the entity associated with a foreign bank;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the foreign bank;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other entities associated with the foreign bank;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the specialized financing entities of the foreign
  bank;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the specialized financing entities of the entity
  associated with the foreign bank; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the specialized financing entities of any other
  entities associated with the foreign bank.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-164, s. 2
- SOR/2010-71, s. 5(F)

## [Acquisition of Specialized Financing Entities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676449 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2008-164, s. 2

\]
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ownership restrictions --- foreign banks

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  foreign bank shall not acquire or hold control of, or a substantial
  investment in, a specialized financing entity if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate balance sheet value of the shares and
    the ownership interests, other than shares or ownership interests
    held by the specialized financing entity in a specialized financing
    entity of the foreign bank that it controls, that the following
    entities hold or would hold in an entity that the specialized
    financing entity holds control of, or a substantial investment in,
    is more than \$250 million, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the specialized financing entity,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the specialized financing entities of the
      foreign bank,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the specialized financing entities of the
      entities associated with the foreign bank,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the foreign bank, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the entities associated with the foreign bank; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the specialized financing entity controls or holds
    shares of, or ownership interests in,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an entity referred to in any of paragraphs
      468(1)(a) to (j) of the Act,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an entity that is primarily engaged in the
      leasing of motor vehicles in Canada for the purpose of extending
      credit to a customer or financing a customer's acquisition of a
      motor vehicle,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an entity that is primarily engaged in
      providing temporary possession of personal property, including
      motor vehicles, to customers in Canada for a purpose other than to
      finance the customer's acquisition of the property, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an entity that acts as an insurance broker or
      agent in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}13 year limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A foreign bank shall not hold control of, or a
  substantial investment in, a specialized financing entity of the
  foreign bank if either the specialized financing entity of the foreign
  bank or the specialized financing entity of the foreign bank and one
  or more of the following entities, one after another, have held
  control of, or a substantial investment in, an entity, other than an
  entity described in subsection (3), for more than 13 consecutive
  years:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the foreign bank;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity associated with the foreign bank;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other specialized financing entity of the
    foreign bank; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a specialized financing entity of an entity
    associated with the foreign bank.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A specialized financing entity of the foreign bank
  controlled by the specialized financing entity of the foreign bank
  shall not be considered for the purpose of subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior periods not included

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a specialized financing entity of the foreign bank
  or an entity referred to in any of paragraphs (2)(a) to (d) held
  control of, or a substantial investment in, an entity for a period
  before becoming that entity, that period shall not be considered for
  the purpose of subsection (2).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-164, s. 3
:::

Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ownership restrictions --- entities
associated with foreign banks

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  entity associated with a foreign bank shall not acquire or hold
  control of, or a substantial investment in, a specialized financing
  entity if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate balance sheet value of the shares and
    the ownership interests, other than shares or ownership interests
    held by the specialized financing entity in a specialized financing
    entity of the entity associated with the foreign bank that it
    controls, that the following entities hold or would hold in an
    entity that the specialized financing entity holds control of, or a
    substantial investment in, is more than \$250 million, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the specialized financing entity,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the specialized financing entities of the
      foreign bank,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the specialized financing entities of the
      entity associated with the foreign bank,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the specialized financing entities of any other
      entities associated with the foreign bank,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the foreign bank,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the entity associated with the foreign bank, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} any other entities associated with the foreign
      bank; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the specialized financing entity controls or holds
    shares of, or ownership interests in, any of the entities referred
    to in subparagraphs 3(1)(b)(i) to (iv).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}13 year limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An entity associated with a foreign bank shall not
  hold control of, or a substantial investment in, a specialized
  financing entity of the entity associated with the foreign bank if
  either the specialized financing entity of the entity associated with
  the foreign bank or the specialized financing entity of the entity
  associated with the foreign bank and one or more of the following
  entities, one after another, have held control of, or a substantial
  investment in, an entity, other than an entity described in subsection
  (3), for more than 13 consecutive years:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the foreign bank;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the entity associated with the foreign bank;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other entity associated with the foreign bank;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any specialized financing entity of the foreign
    bank;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other specialized financing entity of the
    entity associated with the foreign bank; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a specialized financing entity of any other entity
    associated with the foreign bank.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A specialized financing entity of the entity
  associated with the foreign bank controlled by the specialized
  financing entity of the entity associated with the foreign bank shall
  not be considered for the purpose of subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior periods not included

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a specialized financing entity of the entity
  associated with the foreign bank or an entity referred to in any of
  paragraphs (2)(a) to (f) held control of, or a substantial investment
  in, an entity for a period before becoming that entity, that period
  shall not be considered for the purpose of subsection (2).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-164, s. 4
