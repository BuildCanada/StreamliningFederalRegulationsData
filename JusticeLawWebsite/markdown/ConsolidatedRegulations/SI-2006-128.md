## Order Designating the Minister of Health as Appropriate Minister with Respect to the Assisted Human Reproduction Agency of Canada for Purposes of the Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-health-as-appropriate-minister-with-respect-to-the-assisted-human-reproduction-agency-of-canada-for-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-128
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2006-11-15

Order Designating the Minister of Health as Appropriate Minister with
Respect to the Assisted Human Reproduction Agency of Canada for Purposes
of the Act

P.C. 2006-1138 2006-10-26
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (c.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EHBAA} of the
definition [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of
the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby designates
the Minister of Health as the appropriate Minister with respect to the
Assisted Human Reproduction Agency of Canada for the purposes of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).
