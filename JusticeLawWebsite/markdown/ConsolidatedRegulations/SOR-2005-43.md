## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721975 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Sex Offender Information
    Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police. ([GRC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[YSOIRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[YSOIRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Yukon Sex Offender
    Information Registration Centre located in Whitehorse and
    administered by RCMP "M" Division. ([BIRDSYUK]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Notification by Telephone]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721980 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A sex offender whose main
residence is located in Yukon may provide any notification required
under section 6 of the Act by telephone to the YSOIRC.

## [Persons Authorized to Collect Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721982 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A member of the RCMP is
authorized in Yukon to collect information for the purposes of the Act.

## [Persons Authorized to Register Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721984 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
authorized in Yukon to register information for the purposes of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the RCMP; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person employed by the RCMP whose duties include
  the registration of information collected under the Act.

## [Registration Centres]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721988 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  RCMP detachment in Yukon is designated as a registration centre and
  the designated area of service of each registration centre is the area
  served by the detachment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The YSOIRC is designated as a registration centre in
  Yukon and its designated area of service is all of Yukon.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721992 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
