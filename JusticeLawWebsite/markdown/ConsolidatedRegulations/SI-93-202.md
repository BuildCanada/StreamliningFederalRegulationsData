## Order Transferring to the Department of Communications the Control and Supervision of Certain Portions of the Public Service and to the Minister of Communications Certain Powers, Duties and Functions of the Secretary of State of Canada and of the Minister of Multiculturalism and Citizenship and Amalgamating and Combining the Departments of Communications and Multiculturalism and Citizenship under the Minister of Communications {#order-transferring-to-the-department-of-communications-the-control-and-supervision-of-certain-portions-of-the-public-service-and-to-the-minister-of-communications-certain-powers-duties-and-functions-of-the-secretary-of-state-of-canada-and-of-the-minister-of-multiculturalism-and-citizenship-and-amalgamating-and-combining-the-departments-of-communications-and-multiculturalism-and-citizenship-under-the-minister-of-communications .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-202
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1993-11-17

Order Transferring to the Department of Communications the Control and
Supervision of Certain Portions of the Public Service and to the
Minister of Communications Certain Powers, Duties and Functions of the
Secretary of State of Canada and of the Minister of Multiculturalism and
Citizenship and Amalgamating and Combining the Departments of
Communications and Multiculturalism and Citizenship under the Minister
of Communications

P.C. 1993-1902  1993-11-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
  Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), to transfer

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the Department of Communications the control and
    supervision of

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} those portions of the Department of the Secretary
      of State referred to in clauses (a)(i)(A) to (C) of Order in
      Council P.C. 1993-1489 of June 25, 1993[[Footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-202_e_hq_6534){#fn_SI-93-202_e_hq_6534-ID0EBBBBBAA},
      and

      - ::: Footnote
        [[Return to footnote
        ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-202_e_hq_6534-ID0EBBBBBAA){#fn_SI-93-202_e_hq_6534}SI/93-143,
        1993 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p.
        3235
        :::

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} all portions of the Department of the Secretary
      of State, other than the portions referred to in paragraph (b) of
      Order in Council P.C. 1993-1901 of November 4, 1993[[Footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-202_e_hq_6535){#fn_SI-93-202_e_hq_6535-ID0EBBABBAA},
      and

      - ::: Footnote
        [[Return to footnote
        ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-202_e_hq_6535-ID0EBBABBAA){#fn_SI-93-202_e_hq_6535}SI/93-201,
        1993 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p.
        3996
        :::

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to the Minister of Communications

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} all the powers, duties and functions of the
      Secretary of State of Canada under any Act of Parliament, other
      than the powers, duties and functions referred to in paragraph (a)
      of Order in Council P.C. 1993-1901 of November 4, 1993[[Footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-202_e_hq_6535){#fn_SI-93-202_e_hq_6535-ID0EBABABAA},
      and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} all the powers, duties and functions of the
      Minister of Multiculturalism and Citizenship under any Act of
      Parliament, other than the powers, duties and functions relating
      to citizenship transferred to the Secretary of State of Canada by
      subparagraph (a)(iv) of Order in Council P.C. 1993-1901 of
      November 4, 1993[[Footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-202_e_hq_6535){#fn_SI-93-202_e_hq_6535-ID0EBAAABAA};
      and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(b) of that Act, to amalgamate
  and combine the Department of Communications and the Department of
  Multiculturalism and Citizenship under the Minister of Communications
  and under the Deputy Minister of Communications.
