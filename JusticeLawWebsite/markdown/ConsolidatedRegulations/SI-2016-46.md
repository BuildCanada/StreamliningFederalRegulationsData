## Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure, Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs as the appropriate Minister for PPP Canada Inc. {#order-designating-the-minister-of-infrastructure-communities-and-intergovernmental-affairs-as-the-appropriate-minister-for-ppp-canada-inc. .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2016-46
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2016-07-27

Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure, Communities and
Intergovernmental Affairs as the appropriate Minister for PPP Canada
Inc.

P.C. 2016-679 2016-07-08
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (d) of the definition
[[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDBAA} and subparagraph (a)(ii) of
the definition [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
subsection 83(1) of that Act, designates the Minister of Infrastructure,
Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs, a member of the Queen's Privy
Council for Canada, as the appropriate Minister for PPP Canada Inc.
