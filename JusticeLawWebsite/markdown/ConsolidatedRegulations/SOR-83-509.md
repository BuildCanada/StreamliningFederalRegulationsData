## Cheticamp/Grand Etang Fishermen's Co-operative Society Limited Regulations {#cheticampgrand-etang-fishermens-co-operative-society-limited-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/83-509
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 1, 1980-81

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 4, 1981-82

Registration 1983-06-03

Regulations Respectingt the Insurance of Loans Made to Cheticamp/Grand
Etang Fishermen's Co-Operative Society Limited

P.C. 1983-1669 1983-06-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Industry, Trade and Commerce and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to Industry, Trade and Commerce Vote 1a of Appropriation Act
No. 1, 1980-81, as extended by Industry, Trade and Commerce Vote 1e of
Appropriation Act No. 4, 1981-82, is pleased hereby to make the annexed
Regulations respecting the insurance of loans made to Cheticamp/Grand
Etang Fishermen's Co-operative Society Limited.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878751 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Cheticamp/Grand Etang Fishermen's Co-operative Society
Limited Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-509).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878753 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Cheticamp/Grand Etang Fishermen's Co-operative Society Limited]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Cheticamp/Grand Etang Fishermen's Co-operative Society
    Limited]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a co-operative incorporated
    under the laws of the Province of Nova Scotia and has its business
    office in the town of Cheticamp, Nova Scotia; ([Cheticamp/Grand
    Etang Fishermen's Co-operative Society Limited]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Industry,
    Trade and Commerce; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any lender approved by
    the Minister, other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Government of Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the government of any province,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an agency of any government referred to in
      paragraph (a) or (b) or any company that, in the opinion of the
      Minister, is effectively controlled by any such government or
      agency thereof, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any municipal corporation. ([prêteur
      privé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878762 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 4 and
5, where a private lender makes a loan to Cheticamp/Grand Etang
Fishermen's Co-operative Society Limited for the purpose of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} promoting the growth, efficiency or international
  competitiveness of Cheticamp/Grand Etang Fishermen's Co-operative
  Society Limited, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} fostering the expansion of Canadian trade by
  Cheticamp/Grand Etang Fishermen's Co-operative Society Limited,

the Minister may, under Industry, Trade and Commerce Vote 1a of
Appropriation Act No. 1, 1980-81, as extended by Industry, Trade and
Commerce Vote 1e of Appropriation Act No. 4, 1981-82, provide, with the
approval of Treasury Board, insurance in an amount that does not exceed
the lesser of

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$360,000, and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} 90 per cent of the amount of the loan.

## [Application for Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878769 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A private lender who wishes
the Minister to provide insurance referred to in section 3 shall make
application therefor to the Minister and shall provide the Minister with
such information and documents relating to the application as he may
require.

## [Payment of Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878771 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where a private lender
demands repayment of a loan referred to in section 3 in respect of which
insurance has been provided by the Minister pursuant to that section,
the amount of such insurance payable to the private lender shall not
exceed the amount of insurance in effect on the date of such demand.
