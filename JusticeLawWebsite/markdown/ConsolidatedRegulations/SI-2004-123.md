## Order Transferring from the Department of Health to the Public Health Agency of Canada the Control and Supervision of the Population and Public Health Branch and Ordering the Minister of Health to Preside Over the Agency {#order-transferring-from-the-department-of-health-to-the-public-health-agency-of-canada-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-population-and-public-health-branch-and-ordering-the-minister-of-health-to-preside-over-the-agency .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2004-123
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2004-10-06

Order Transferring from the Department of Health to the Public Health
Agency of Canada the Control and Supervision of the Population and
Public Health Branch and Ordering the Minister of Health to Preside Over
the Agency

P.C. 2004-1069 2004-09-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
  Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), transfers
  from the Department of Health to the Public Health Agency of Canada
  the control and supervision of the portion of the public service in
  the Department of Health known as the Population and Public Health
  Branch, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} orders that the Minister of Health shall preside over
  the Public Health Agency of Canada,

effective September 24, 2004.
