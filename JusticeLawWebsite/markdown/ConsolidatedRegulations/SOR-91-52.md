## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939024 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Federal Sales Tax Inventory Rebate
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-52).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939026 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[computer carrier media]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[computer carrier media]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means media on which
    is stored instructions or data to be processed by data processing
    equipment; ([support de transmission de données]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[software products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[software products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means computer carrier
    media including the instructions or data stored thereon, but not
    including sound or image recordings, integrated circuits,
    semi-conductors and similar devices or articles incorporating such
    recordings, circuits, semi-conductors or devices. ([produit
    logiciel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prescribed Tax Factors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939031 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 120(5) of the Act, the prescribed tax factors in respect of
the following classes of goods are:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods included in Schedule IV to the
  Act, 5.6%;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of gasoline, the rate of tax under Part
  VI of the Act applicable on December 31, 1990 in respect of unleaded
  gasoline;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of diesel fuel, the rate of tax under
  Part VI of the Act applicable on December 31, 1990 in respect of
  diesel fuel;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of propane, 1.4%;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of mobile homes and modular building
  units, 2.8%;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of motor vehicles designed for highway
  use, 11.1%;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of software products, 8.1%; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, 8.1%.

## [Prescribed Method of Determining Rebate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939041 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 120(5) of the Act, the rebate in respect of a person's
inventory is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total of all amounts each of which is determined,
  for a class of goods, by the formula

  ::: {#939044 .Paragraph}
  A × B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the prescribed tax factor in respect of the class of goods; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is
      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the class of goods is gasoline or diesel
        fuel, the number of litres of gasoline or diesel fuel, as the
        case may be, that form part of the inventory,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the class of goods is software products,
        the total value of the computer carrier media, excluding the
        value of instructions or data stored thereon, that form part of
        the inventory or the product obtained when \$5 is multiplied by
        the number of those computer carrier media, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the total value of goods
        in the class (other than used goods) that form part of that
        inventory, as that total value would be required to be
        determined at the beginning of January 1, 1991 for the purpose
        of computing the person's income from a business for the
        purposes of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); or
  :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the person elects to use the following formula
  and where

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} goods included in Part V of Schedule III to the Act
    are held at the beginning of January 1, 1991 by the person for
    taxable supply (within the meaning assigned by subsection 123(1) of
    the Act) by way of sale to others in the ordinary course of the
    person's business, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the total value of the person's inventory (other
      than gasoline, diesel fuel and used goods) at that time, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the total value of goods (other than gasoline,
      diesel fuel and used goods) that would be inventory of the person
      at that time if the goods were tax-paid goods,

    each of which is determined as of that time in accordance with the
    method that the person would be required to use for purposes of
    computing the person's income from a business for purposes of the
    [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), does not exceed \$70,000,

  the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#939058 .Paragraph}
  A + B + C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the product obtained by multiplying the number of litres of
      gasoline that form part of the person's inventory at that time by
      the prescribed tax factor in respect of gasoline;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the product obtained by multiplying the number of litres of
      diesel fuel that form part of the person's inventory at that time
      by the prescribed tax factor in respect of diesel fuel; and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is 2.5% of the aggregate determined under subparagraph (ii).
  :::
