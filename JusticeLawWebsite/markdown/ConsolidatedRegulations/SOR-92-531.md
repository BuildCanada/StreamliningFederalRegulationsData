## Regulatory Capital (Banks) Regulations {#regulatory-capital-banks-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-531
:::

[BANK ACT](/eng/acts/B-1.01)

Registration 1992-08-31

Regulations Defining the Regulatory Capital of a Bank

P.C. 1992-1850 1992-08-27
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to the definition [[regulatory
capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 and section 559 of the [Bank
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed Regulations defining the regulatory capital
of a bank.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-946759 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01);
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an insurance company,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a securities dealer, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an entity controlled by an insurance company or a
      securities dealer; ([entité désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an insurance company
    that is incorporated by or under the laws of Canada or of a
    province; ([société d'assurances]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[minority interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minority interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an equity interest,
    in an entity that is controlled by a bank, that is held by a person
    other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the bank, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity controlled by the bank; ([participation
      minoritaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[securities dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[securities dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity described
    in paragraph (g) of the definition [[financial
    institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the Act.
    ([négociant en valeurs mobilières]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Regulatory Capital]{.HTitleText1} {#h-946771 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and section 4, the regulatory capital of a bank, at
  a particular time, is the aggregate of the amounts of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shareholders equity,

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} members' equity, if the bank is a federal credit
    union,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} minority interests, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subordinated indebtedness

  that would be reported in the financial statements of the bank
  prepared as at that time in accordance with the accounting principles
  and specifications of the Superintendent referred to in subsection
  308(4) of the Act, less the amount of goodwill that would be included
  in those financial statements.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In calculating the amount of regulatory capital under
  subsection (1), an amount may be included in respect of a security or
  membership share only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the security or membership share is, by its terms,
    subordinate in right of payment to all deposit liabilities and all
    other liabilities of the entity that issued the security or
    membership share other than liabilities that, by their terms, rank
    equally with, or are subordinate to, that security or membership
    share;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the security or membership share is issued and
    fully paid-up; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of subordinated indebtedness or a
    preferred share, the security

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has an initial minimum term of five years or more
      or has no term, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} cannot be redeemed or purchased for cancellation
      in the first five years after it is issued.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of sections 476 to 478 of the Act, the regulatory capital of
  a bank is the amount calculated under section 3, less the aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amounts of shareholders' equity and
    subordinated indebtedness of an insurance company or securities
    dealer controlled by the bank, or an entity controlled by such an
    insurance company or securities dealer, that are included in the
    amount of regulatory capital of the bank calculated pursuant to
    section 3,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of investments, other than shareholders'
    equity or subordinated indebtedness referred to in paragraph (a), of
    the bank or a designated entity controlled by the bank in an
    insurance company or securities dealer controlled by the bank, or in
    an entity controlled by such an insurance company or securities
    dealer, where the investments are part of the capital of the
    insurance company or securities dealer, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount of loans, other than subordinated
    indebtedness referred to in paragraph (a), made by the bank or by a
    designated entity controlled by the bank to an insurance company or
    securities dealer controlled by the bank, or to an entity controlled
    by such an insurance company or securities dealer, where the loans
    are part of the capital of the insurance company or securities
    dealer, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount of a minority interest in an insurance
    company or securities dealer controlled by the bank, or in an entity
    controlled by such an insurance company or securities dealer, that
    is included in the amount of regulatory capital of the bank
    calculated pursuant to section 3.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraphs (1)(b) and (c),
  [[capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an insurance company to which the
    [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies, regulatory
    capital within the meaning of the [Regulatory Capital (Insurance
    Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-529); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any other insurance company or a
    securities dealer, an amount that would be treated as capital by a
    regulatory body that regulates that insurance company or securities
    dealer.
