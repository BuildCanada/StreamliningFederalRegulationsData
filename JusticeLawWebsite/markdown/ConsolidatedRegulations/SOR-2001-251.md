## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-670395 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 2,
remission is hereby granted in the amount of customs duties paid or
payable under the General Tariff that exceed the customs duties
applicable under the Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on goods imported from Albania and the
Sultanate of Oman.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-670397 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to goods that originate from Albania,
  the goods were imported into Canada during the period commencing on
  September 8, 2000 and ending on March 14, 2001;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to goods that originate in the Sultanate
  of Oman, the goods were imported into Canada during the period
  commencing on November 9, 2000 and ending on March 14, 2001;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within two years of the date on which the Order comes
  into force, and,

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} no claim for refund or drawback under the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) or [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) has
  been granted with respect to the goods on which remission is claimed.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-670403 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
