## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-675626 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an entity referred to in any of paragraphs
      971(1)(a) to (j) of the Act;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity whose business includes one or more of
      the activities referred to in paragraph 971(2)(a) of the Act and
      that engages, as part of its business, in any financial
      intermediary activity that exposes the entity to material market
      or credit risk, including a factoring entity, a finance entity or
      a financial leasing entity; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an entity whose business includes an activity
      referred to in paragraph 971(2)(b) of the Act, including a
      specialized financing entity, other than an entity in which an
      insurance holding company is permitted to acquire or increase a
      substantial investment under subparagraph 971(4)(c)(iii) of the
      Act. ([entité désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[regulatory capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regulatory capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in section 2 of the [Regulatory Capital (Insurance Holding
    Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-424). ([capital
    réglementaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a share, ownership interest or loan
      held by an insurance holding company at a particular time, the
      book value of the share, ownership interest or loan that would be
      reported on the balance sheet of the insurance holding company
      prepared as at that time in accordance with the accounting
      principles and specifications of the Superintendent referred to in
      subsection 887(4) of the Act; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a guarantee, the face value of the
      guarantee. ([valeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- SOR/2011-196, s. 26

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-675640 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permitted substantial investments

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subparagraphs 971(4)(a)(ii),
  (b)(ii) and (c)(ii) of the Act, an insurance holding company may
  acquire or increase a substantial investment in a designated entity;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of paragraph 971(10)(a) of the Act,
  if an insurance holding company controls a designated entity, the
  insurance holding company may give up control of the designated entity
  while keeping a substantial investment in it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction concerning investments

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 5 and
6, an insurance holding company must not acquire or increase a
substantial investment in a designated entity under paragraph 2(a) or
give up control of the designated entity while keeping a substantial
investment in it under paragraph 2(b) if, after the acquisition,
increase or giving up of control, the total value of the following would
exceed 50% of the insurance holding company's regulatory capital:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all shares and ownership interests beneficially owned
  by the insurance holding company, and all shares and ownership
  interests beneficially owned by entities controlled by the insurance
  holding company, in designated entities in which the insurance holding
  company has a substantial investment but over which it does not
  exercise control,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all loans held by the insurance holding company, and
  all loans held by entities controlled by the insurance holding
  company, that were made to designated entities in which the insurance
  holding company has a substantial investment but over which it does
  not exercise control, and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} all outstanding guarantees given by the insurance
  holding company, and all outstanding guarantees given by entities
  controlled by the insurance holding company, on behalf of designated
  entities in which the insurance holding company has a substantial
  investment but over which it does not exercise control.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-196, s. 27(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction concerning loans

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 5 and
6, an insurance holding company that has a substantial investment in a
designated entity over which it does not exercise control must not make
a loan to, or give a guarantee on behalf of, the designated entity, or
permit entities controlled by it to do so, if, after the making of the
loan or the giving of the guarantee, the total value of the shares,
ownership interests, loans and guarantees referred to in paragraphs 3(a)
to (c) would exceed 50% of the insurance holding company's regulatory
capital.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In paragraphs 3(a) to (c)
and section 4, any reference to a substantial investment that an
insurance holding company has does not include a substantial investment
acquired by the insurance holding company under regulations made under
paragraph 977(a) of the Act other than these Regulations.

Calculation of [[total value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 3 or 4

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
calculating the total value referred to in section 3 or 4 in respect of
an insurance holding company, no amount may be included in respect of
shares or ownership interests acquired under sections 974 to 976 of the
Act or acquired by a bank controlled by the insurance holding company
under subsection 193(12) or (13) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01),
chapter B-1 of the Revised Statutes of Canada, 1985.
