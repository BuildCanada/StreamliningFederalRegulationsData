Whereas, pursuant to subsection 332(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EIAFA} of the [Canadian
Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EFAFA}, the Minister of the
Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on December 14, 2013, a
copy of the proposed [Disposal at Sea Permit Application
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-177), and persons were given an
opportunity to file comments with respect to the proposed Regulations or
to file a notice of objection requesting that a board of review be
established and stating the reasons for the objection;

Therefore, the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to
subsection 135(3)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_c){#fn_c-ID0EGAEA}
of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EDAEA}, makes the annexed [Disposal
at Sea Permit Application Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-177).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805803 .Part}

Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Environmental Protection
Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31).

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805806 .Part}

### [Permits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805807 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permit application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** An application for a permit
referred to in subsection 127(1) or 128(2) of the Act must be submitted
in the form set out in Schedule 1 and must contain the information
relevant to the application that is provided for in that Schedule.

### [Renewal of Permits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805810 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Renewal application

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
renewal of a permit referred to in subsection 127(1) of the Act must be
submitted in the form set out in Schedule 2 and must contain the
information relevant to the application that is provided for in that
Schedule.
