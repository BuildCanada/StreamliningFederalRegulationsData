## Non-Profit Grazing Associations (GST) Remission Order {#non-profit-grazing-associations-gst-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-10
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1993-02-10

Order Respecting the Remission of Tax under Part IX of the Excise Tax
Act Paid or Payable by a Non-Profit Organization in Respect of the
Acquisition of Property or Services for Use in Respect of a Business of
Supplying Grazing Land

P.C. 1993-117  1993-01-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of Finance, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-10_e_hq_6511){#fn_SI-93-10_e_hq_6511-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed Order respecting the remission of tax under Part IX
of the Excise Tax Act paid or payable by a non-profit organization in
respect of the acquisition of property or services for use in respect of
a business of supplying grazing land.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-646178 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Non-Profit Grazing Associations (GST) Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-93-10).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-646180 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15).
(*Loi*)

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-646182 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted to a non-profit organization of the tax
under Part IX of the Act that became payable by it before November 6,
1991, or that was paid by it before that date without having become
payable, in respect of property or services acquired or imported by the
non-profit organization for consumption, use or supply in the course of
a business of the non-profit organization of making supplies of real
property for grazing purposes.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-646184 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The remission of tax under
section 3 is on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the non-profit organization is not entitled to an
  input tax credit under Part IX of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the tax has not otherwise been rebated, refunded or
  remitted;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the non-profit organization is registered under Part
  IX of the Act before January 1, 1994 and files an election under
  section 211 of the Act, to take effect before January 1, 1994, in
  respect of the real property supplied by that organization for grazing
  purposes; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the non-profit organization applies in writing to the
  Minister of National Revenue for the remission within four years after

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the tax became payable, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the tax was paid without having become
    payable, the day on which the tax was paid.
