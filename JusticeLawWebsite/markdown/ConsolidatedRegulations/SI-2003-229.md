## Order Transferring from the Department of National Defence to the Department of the Solicitor General the Control and Supervision of the Office of Critical Infrastructure Protection and Emergency Preparedness {#order-transferring-from-the-department-of-national-defence-to-the-department-of-the-solicitor-general-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-office-of-critical-infrastructure-protection-and-emergency-preparedness .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2003-229
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2003-12-31

Order Transferring from the Department of National Defence to the
Department of the Solicitor General the Control and Supervision of the
Office of Critical Infrastructure Protection and Emergency Preparedness

P.C. 2003-2086  2003-12-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers from the Department of National Defence to the Department of
the Solicitor General the control and supervision of the portion of the
public service in the Department of National Defence known as the Office
of Critical Infrastructure Protection and Emergency Preparedness,
effective December 12, 2003.
