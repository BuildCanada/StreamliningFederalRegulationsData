Registration 1983-06-03

Privacy Regulations

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection
77(1) of the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21), is pleased hereby to make
the annexed Regulations with respect to privacy.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878560 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Access to Personal Information Request Form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Access to Personal Information Request Form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means the form prescribed by the designated Minister pursuant to
    paragraph 71(1)(c) of the Act for the purpose of requesting access
    to personal information under the control of a government
    institution; ([formule de demande d'accès à des renseignements
    personnels]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21);
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[appropriate officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appropriate officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the officer of the
    government institution whose title and address is published pursuant
    to subparagraphs 11(1)(a)(iii) and 11(1)(b)(ii) of the Act;
    ([fonctionnaire compétent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

:   [[Correction Request Form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the form
    prescribed by the designated Minister pursuant to paragraph 71(1)(c)
    of the Act for the purpose of requesting, under subsection 12(2) of
    the Act, a correction of personal information under the control of a
    government institution. ([formule de demande de

## [Government Institutions or Parts of Government Institutions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878569 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The government institutions
or parts of government institutions for the purpose of paragraph (e) of
the definition [[personal information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 3
of the Act are those set out in Schedule I to these Regulations.

## [Retention of Personal Information that Has Been Used by a Government Institution for an Administrative Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878571 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Personal
  information concerning an individual that has been used by a
  government institution for an administrative purpose shall be retained
  by the institution

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for at least two years following the last time the
    personal information was used for an administrative purpose unless
    the individual consents to its disposal; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where a request for access to the information has
    been received, until such time as the individual has had the
    opportunity to exercise all his rights under the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1) where personal
  information is under the control of a government institution at a post
  abroad, the head of the post or the senior officer in charge thereof
  may order the destruction of the information in an emergency in order
  to prevent the removal of the information from the control of the
  institution.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/88-123, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-1092, s. 2
- SOR/88-123, s. 1
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The investigative bodies
for the purposes of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 8(2)(e) of the Act are those listed in
  Schedule II;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 22(1)(a) of the Act are those listed in
  Schedule III; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} section 23 of the Act are those listed in Schedule
  IV.

## [Personal Information Under the Control of the Archives]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878584 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Personal information that
has been transferred to the control of the Library and Archives of
Canada by a government institution for archival or historical purposes
may be disclosed to any person or body for research or statistical
purposes where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information is of such a nature that disclosure
  would not constitute an unwarranted invasion of the privacy of the
  individual to whom the information relates;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure is in accordance with paragraph
  8(2)(j) or (k) of the Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} 110 years have elapsed following the birth of the
  individual to whom the information relates; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in cases where the information was obtained through
  the taking of a census or survey, 92 years have elapsed following the
  census or survey containing the information.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 1 (3rd Supp.), s. 12
- 2004, c. 11, subpar. 52(5) and (6)
:::

## [Retention for Two Years]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878592 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The head of a government
institution shall retain for a period of at least two years following
the date on which a request for access to personal information is
received by the institution under paragraph 8(2)(e) of the Act

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of every request received; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a record of any information disclosed pursuant to
  such a request.

## [Procedures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878596 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  request for access to personal information under paragraph 12(1)(a)
  or (b) of the Act shall be made by completing an Access to Personal
  Information Request Form in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each personal information bank that is the subject
    of the request, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} each class of personal information that is not
    contained in a personal information bank

  and forwarding the form to the appropriate officer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An individual who makes a request for access to
  personal information pursuant to subsection (1) shall provide adequate
  identification to the government institution before access to the
  information is provided and may be required to present himself in
  person.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Where access to personal
information is given to an individual by means of an opportunity to
examine the information, the head of the institution shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide reasonable facilities for the examination;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} set a time for the examination that is convenient for
  the institution and the individual.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-1092, s. 3(E)
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The rights or actions
provided for under the Act and these Regulations may be exercised or
performed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on behalf of a minor or an incompetent person by a
  person authorized by or pursuant to the law of Canada or a province to
  administer the affairs or estate of that person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on behalf of a deceased person by a person authorized
  by or pursuant to the law of Canada or a province to administer the
  estate of that person, but only for the purpose of such
  administration; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} on behalf of any other individual by any person
  authorized in writing to do so by the individual.

<!-- -->

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an individual who is given access under paragraph 12(1)(a) of the Act
  to personal information that has been used, is being used or is
  available for use for an administrative purpose believes there is an
  error or omission in the personal information, the individual may
  forward to the appropriate officer a Correction Request Form in
  respect of each personal information bank containing the information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} requesting correction of the personal information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} requesting that any person or body to whom the
    personal information has been disclosed within the two years
    immediately preceding the date that the request for correction is
    received be notified of any correction made pursuant to paragraph
    (a); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} requesting that any government institution to which
    the personal information has been disclosed within the two years
    immediately preceding the date that the request for correction is
    received be notified of any correction made pursuant to
    paragraph (a) and to make the correction on every copy of the
    personal information under its control.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a correction to personal information is made
  pursuant to a request by an individual under paragraph (1)(a), the
  head of the government institution that has control of the personal
  information shall, within thirty days after the receipt by the
  appropriate officer of the Correction Request Form forwarded by the
  individual,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the individual that the correction has been
    made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify any person or body referred to in paragraph
    (1)(b) that the correction has been made; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} notify the appropriate officer of any government
    institution referred to in paragraph (1)(c) that the correction has
    been made and that the officer is required to make the correction on
    every copy of the personal information under the control of the
    institution.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the appropriate officer referred to in
  paragraph (2)(c) receives a notice referred to in that paragraph, the
  officer shall make the correction required by the notice.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a request by an individual under paragraph
  (1)(a) to correct personal information is refused in whole or in part,
  the head of the government institution that has control of the
  personal information shall, within thirty days after the receipt by
  the appropriate officer of the Correction Request Form forwarded by
  the individual,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} attach a notation to the personal information
    reflecting that a correction was requested but was refused in whole
    or in part;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify the individual that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the request for correction has been refused in
      whole or in part and set out the reasons for the refusal,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the notation under paragraph (a) has been
      attached to the personal information, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the individual has the right under the Act to
      make a complaint to the Privacy Commissioner;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} notify any person or body referred to in paragraph
    (1)(b) that the notation under paragraph (a) has been attached to
    the personal information; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} notify the appropriate officer of any government
    institution referred to in paragraph (1)(c) that the notation under
    paragraph (a) has been attached to the personal information and that
    the officer is required to attach such a notation to every copy of
    the personal information under the control of the institution.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the appropriate officer referred to in
  paragraph (4)(d) receives a notice referred to in that paragraph, the
  officer shall comply with that notice.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-1092, s. 4
:::

## [Classes of Investigations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878633 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The classes of
investigations for the purpose of paragraph 22(3)(c) of the Act are the
classes of investigations set out in Schedule V.

## [Disclosure of Information Relating to Physical or Mental Health]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878635 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an individual has requested personal information relating to the
  physical or mental health of the individual, the head of the
  government institution that has control of the information may
  authorize the disclosure of the information to a duly qualified
  medical practitioner or psychologist in order that the practitioner or
  psychologist may provide an opinion as to whether disclosure of the
  information would be contrary to the best interests of the individual.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Personal information disclosed to a duly qualified
  medical practitioner or psychologist under subsection (1) shall not be
  disclosed by the duly qualified medical practitioner or psychologist
  to any other person, except to a duly qualified medical practitioner
  or psychologist for the purposes of the opinion referred to in
  subsection (1), without the consent of the individual to whom the
  information relates.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-157, s. 1
:::

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** An individual who is
given access to personal information relating to his physical or mental
health may be required by the head of the government institution that
has control of the information to examine the information in person and
in the presence of a duly qualified medical practitioner or psychologist
so that the practitioner or psychologist may explain or clarify the
information to the individual.

## [SCHEDULE V]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 12)]{.OriginatingRef}[Classes of Investigations]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-878725 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** Investigations by a Fact Finding Board established by the
  Department of Transport to investigate air traffic control where it
  has been alleged that due to a system deficiency

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} flight safety may have been jeopardized; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} less than the minimum required separation between
    aircraft may have existed.

- **2** Investigations by a Flight Service Station Review Committee
  established by the Department of Transport to investigate reported
  occurrences relating to aviation safety where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} procedures or action or a lack thereof,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} systems failure, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} other causes

  have brought the reliability of a Flight Service Station or the Flight
  Service Station System into question.

- **3** Canadian Forces flight safety accident investigations other than
  those conducted in the form of a board of inquiry or summary
  investigation under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5).

- **4** Investigations by or under the authority of the Canadian Forces
  Fire Marshall for the purpose of determining the cause of a fire,
  other than those conducted in the form of a board of inquiry or
  summary investigation under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5).

- **5** Investigations by an investigative body set out in Schedule IV
  for the purpose of reviewing or updating a security clearance referred
  to in paragraph 23(a) or (b) of the Act that was granted to an
  individual.

- **6** Investigation by an investigative body set out in Schedule IV
  for the purpose of granting, reviewing or updating a security
  clearance required by an international organization established by the
  governments of states or any institution of such an organization.

- **7** Investigations by the Special Inquiries Unit of the Inspector
  General's Branch of the Correctional Service of Canada.
