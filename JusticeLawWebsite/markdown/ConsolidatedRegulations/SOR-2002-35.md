## Canadian Chicken Marketing Levies Order {#canadian-chicken-marketing-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2002-35
:::

[FARM PRODUCTS AGENCIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-4)

Registration 2001-12-24

Canadian Chicken Marketing Levies Order
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council has, by the [Chicken Farmers of Canada
Proclamation](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-158)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EGAEA}, established
Chicken Farmers of Canada pursuant to subsection 16(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EEAEA} of the [Farm
Products Agencies Act](/eng/acts/F-4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#footnotec_e){#footnotec_e-ID0EBAEA};

Whereas Chicken Farmers of Canada has been empowered to implement a
marketing plan pursuant to the [Chicken Farmers of Canada
Proclamation](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-158)^a^;

Whereas the proposed annexed [Canadian Chicken Marketing Levies
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-35) is an order of a class to which
paragraph 7(1)(d)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#footnoted_e){#footnoted_e-ID0EHAADA} of the [Farm
Products Agencies Act](/eng/acts/F-4)^c^ applies by reason of section 2
of the [Agencies' Orders and Regulations Approval
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._648)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#footnotee_e){#footnotee_e-ID0EBAADA}, and has been
submitted to the National Farm Products Council pursuant to paragraph
22(1)(f) of that Act;

And whereas, pursuant to paragraph 7(1)(d)^d^ of the [Farm Products
Agencies Act](/eng/acts/F-4)^c^, the National Farm Products Council is
satisfied that the proposed order is necessary for the implementation of
the marketing plan that Chicken Farmers of Canada is authorized to
implement, and has approved the proposed order;

Therefore, Chicken Farmers of Canada, pursuant to paragraph 22(1)(f) of
the [Farm Products Agencies Act](/eng/acts/F-4)^c^ and section
12[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#footnotef_e){#footnotef_e-ID0EGGBA}
of the schedule to the [Chicken Farmers of Canada
Proclamation](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-158)^a^, hereby makes the annexed
[Canadian Chicken Marketing Levies Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-35).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-694831 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Farm Products Agencies
    Act](/eng/acts/F-4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[audit period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[audit period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means two consecutive periods
    during which chicken produced in a province is authorized to be
    marketed under the [Canadian Chicken Marketing Quota
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-36). ([période de
    vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[CFC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CFC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2015-228, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[chicken]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chicken]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2015-228, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[communal group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[communal group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Saskatchewan, has the same meaning as in Order
      No. 61/11 -- Communal Group Production Exemption made by the
      Chicken Farmers of Saskatchewan Board; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Alberta, has the same meaning as in paragraph
      1(g) of the Alberta Chicken Producers Marketing Regulation, Alta.
      Reg. 3/2000. ([ferme collective]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[communal group production quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[communal group production quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    quota for the production or marketing of chicken granted to a
    communal group under a law of the Province in which it is situated.
    ([contingent de production de ferme collective]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person, other than a producer
    or processor, who purchases live chicken in interprovincial or
    export trade. ([négociant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[federal market development quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal market development quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    number of kilograms of chicken, expressed in live weight, that a
    producer is entitled, under the [Canadian Chicken Marketing Quota
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-36), to market in
    interprovincial or export trade to market development licensees
    during the period referred to in the schedule to those Regulations.
    ([contingent fédéral d'expansion du marché]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[federal quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the number of kilograms
    of chicken, expressed in live weight, not including a federal market
    development quota or federal specialty chicken quota, that a
    producer is entitled, under the [Canadian Chicken Marketing Quota
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-36), to market in
    interprovincial or export trade during the period referred to in the
    schedule to those Regulations. ([contingent
    fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[federal specialty chicken quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal specialty chicken quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    number of kilograms of chicken, expressed in live weight, that a
    producer is entitled under the [Canadian Chicken Marketing Quota
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-36), to market in
    interprovincial or export trade, during the period referred to in
    the schedule to those Regulations, to any holder of a specialty
    chicken licence. ([contingent fédéral de poulet de
    spécialité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[market development commitment period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[market development commitment period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    the period that spans the following three periods:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the period referred to in section 1 of the market
      development commitment form set out in Schedule 1 to the [Canadian
      Chicken Licensing Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-22);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the period referred to in the schedule to the
      [Canadian Chicken Marketing Quota
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-36) that immediately
      precedes the period referred to in paragraph (a); and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the period referred to in the schedule to the
      [Canadian Chicken Marketing Quota
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-36) that immediately
      follows the period referred to in paragraph (a). ([période
      d'engagement pour l'expansion du marché]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[market development licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[market development licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a market
    development licence issued by CFC under the [Canadian Chicken
    Licensing Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-22). ([permis
    d'expansion du marché]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2015-228, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[primary processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[primary processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a processor who
    slaughters chicken that was produced and marketed under a federal
    market development quota or federal specialty chicken quota.
    ([transformateur primaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the slaughtering of chicken
    or the changing of the nature, size, quality or condition of
    slaughtered chicken. ([transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is engaged in
    the business of processing and otherwise marketing chicken.
    ([transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2015-228, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[Provincial Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Provincial Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2015-228, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[registered production facilities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registered production facilities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    production facilities that are registered under the laws of the
    province in which they are situated. ([installations de production
    agréées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[specialty chicken licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[specialty chicken licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a specialty
    chicken licence that is issued by CFC under the [Canadian Chicken
    Licensing Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-22). ([permis de
    poulet de spécialité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-694860 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Order applies to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a producer who sells, in interprovincial or export
    trade, live chicken produced under a federal quota;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a dealer who purchases, in interprovincial or
    export trade, live chicken produced under a federal quota;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a processor who purchases, for interprovincial or
    export trade, live chicken produced under a federal quota;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a producer who sells, in interprovincial or export
    trade, live chicken in excess of a federal quota;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a primary processor who markets chicken produced
    under a federal market development quota

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to a buyer, or for an end-use, not specified in
      section 3 of Schedule 2 to the [Canadian Chicken Licensing
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-22),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} outside the market development commitment
      period, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} otherwise than as one of the product categories
      set out in column 1 of section 1 of Schedule 2 to the [Canadian
      Chicken Licensing Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-22);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a primary processor or producer who markets, in
    interprovincial or export trade, chicken produced under a federal
    specialty chicken quota; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a primary processor or producer who markets, in
    interprovincial or export trade, chicken in excess of a federal
    specialty chicken quota.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Order does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a producer in the Province of Ontario who produces
    no more than 300 chickens per year at their premises and is
    permitted to produce chicken without being allotted a quota under
    regulations made by the Chicken Farmers of Ontario;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a producer in the Province of Quebec who produces
    and markets no more than 100 chickens per year, unless specifically
    authorized by [Les Éleveurs de volailles du Québec]{.otherLang
    lang="fr"} to market a greater quantity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a producer in the Province of Nova Scotia who
    produces no more than 200 chickens per year at their premises for
    personal consumption;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a producer in the Province of New Brunswick who
    produces and markets no more than 200 chickens per year;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a producer in the Province of Manitoba who produces
    and markets no more than 999 chickens per year;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a producer in the Province of British Columbia who
    produces and markets no more than 200 chickens per year;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a producer in the Province of Prince Edward Island
    who produces and markets no more than 500 chickens per year;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a producer in the Province of Saskatchewan who
    produces and markets no more than 999 chickens per year or a
    communal group within that Province that produces and markets
    chicken under a communal group production quota;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a producer in the Province of Alberta who produces
    and markets no more than 2,000 chickens per year or a communal group
    within that Province that produces and markets chicken under a
    communal group production quota;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a producer in the Province of Newfoundland and
    Labrador who produces and markets no more than 100 chickens per
    year.

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-694886 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A levy
  is hereby imposed on a producer per kilogram of live weight of chicken
  marketed by the producer in interprovincial or export trade, by
  reference to the province of production as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the province of Ontario, 2.71 cents;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the province of Quebec, 2.27 cents;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the province of Nova Scotia, 1.67 cents;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the province of New Brunswick, 1.20 cents;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the province of Manitoba, 2.46 cents;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the province of British Columbia 2.06 cents;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the province of Prince Edward Island, 2.11
    cents;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in the province of Saskatchewan, 1.87 cents;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the province of Alberta, 1.95 cents; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} in the province of Newfoundland and Labrador, 1.67
    cents.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) ceases to have effect on March 31,
  2026.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  addition to the levy payable under section 3, each producer must pay a
  levy of 44.0 cents per kilogram of live weight of chicken marketed in
  an audit period in interprovincial or export trade by the producer in
  excess of the federal quota or federal specialty chicken quota
  allotted to the producer, on behalf of CFC, by the Commodity Board of
  the province in which the producer's registered facilities are
  located.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) applies in addition to any other
  measures that may be taken under the Act or under any order or
  regulations of CFC.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of determining marketings in excess
  of federal quota under subsection (1), there must be no reduction on
  account of marketings below federal market development quota for the
  same audit period.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  primary processor who holds a market development licence issued under
  the [Canadian Chicken Licensing
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-22) and who markets chicken
  produced under a federal market development quota must pay the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of chicken that is not marketed during
    the market development commitment period, a levy of \$1.00 per
    kilogram on the live weight equivalent of that chicken;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of chicken that is not marketed before
    the end of the period referred to in the schedule to the [Canadian
    Chicken Marketing Quota Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-36)
    that immediately follows the market development commitment period,
    an additional levy of \$0.60 per kilogram on the live weight
    equivalent of that chicken;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of chicken, other than chicken referred
    to in paragraph (a) or (b), that is marketed by the primary
    processor to a buyer, or for an end-use, not referred to in section
    3 of Schedule 2 to the [Canadian Chicken Licensing
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-22), a levy of \$1.60 per
    kilogram on the live weight equivalent of that chicken;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of chicken, other than chicken referred
    to in paragraph (a) or (b), that is not marketed as one of the
    product categories set out in column 1 of the table to section 1 of
    Schedule 2 to the [Canadian Chicken Licensing
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-22), a levy of \$1.60 per
    kilogram on the live weight equivalent of that chicken; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of chicken, other than chicken referred
    to in paragraph (a) or (b), in respect of which the primary
    processor has not, within 21 days after each period (in this
    paragraph referred to as the "reporting period") that is described
    by any of paragraphs (a) to (c) of the definition [[market
    development commitment period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 1 and
    that is included in the market development commitment period during
    which chicken produced under the federal market development quota
    was marketed, provided sufficient information to CFC, or to any
    Commodity Board or person authorized by CFC to receive the
    information, to allow CFC to determine that the chicken was marketed
    during that reporting period to a buyer, and for an end-use,
    referred to in section 3 of Schedule 2 to the [Canadian Chicken
    Licensing Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-22) as one of the
    product categories set out in column 1 of the table to section 1 of
    Schedule 2 to those Regulations, a levy of \$1.60 per kilogram on
    the live weight equivalent of that chicken.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), the live weight
  equivalent of chicken is to be calculated using the coefficients set
  out in the schedule.

## [Levy Collection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-694921 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If live
  chicken is purchased for interprovincial or export trade by a
  processor from a producer, the levy imposed under section 3 must be
  collected by the processor by deducting the amount of the levy from
  the money payable by the processor to the producer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If live chicken is purchased for interprovincial or
  export trade by a dealer from a producer, the levy imposed under
  section 3 must be collected by the dealer by deducting the amount of
  the levy from the money payable by the dealer to the producer.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If a levy imposed under
section 3 is to be collected by a processor or dealer under section 6,
the processor or dealer must remit the levy to CFC, or to the Commodity
Board or person authorized by CFC, not later than the Monday following
the week in which the levy was collected under that section and, in any
other case, the producer must remit the levy to CFC, or to the Commodity
Board or person authorized by CFC, not later than two weeks after the
sale of the live chicken in interprovincial or export trade.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The levy to be remitted
under section 7 must be accompanied by a statement, on a form approved
by CFC, that sets out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the levy remitted by a processor or
  dealer,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of each producer from whom the
    levy was collected,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the number of head and the number of kilograms of
    live chicken purchased from the producer, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of levy remitted in respect of the

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the levy remitted by a producer,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the producer,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the number of head and the number of kilograms of
    live chicken marketed by the producer, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of levy remitted.
