## Inspection and Search Defence Regulations {#inspection-and-search-defence-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-958
:::

[NATIONAL DEFENCE ACT](/eng/acts/N-5)

Registration 1986-09-11

Regulations Authorizing Inspection in Accordance with the Custom or
Practice of the Service and Respecting the Safety and Conduct of Persons
in, on or about any Defence Establishment, Work for Defence or Materiel

P.C. 1986-2079 1986-09-11
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Defence, pursuant to section 231.1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations authorizing inspection in accordance with the
custom or practice of the service and respecting the safety and conduct
of persons in, on or about any defence establishment, work for defence
or materiel](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-958).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899354 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Inspection and Search Defence
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-958).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899356 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[controlled area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[controlled area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any defence
    establishment, work for defence or materiel; ([secteur
    contrôlé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the officer in
    command or person in charge of a controlled area or any restricted
    area within the controlled area and includes a person acting under
    the authority of that officer or person in charge. ([autorité
    compétente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Inspection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899360 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An officer or a
non-commissioned member may conduct an inspection, including an
inspection for the purpose of maintaining military standards of health,
hygiene, safety, security, efficiency, dress and kit, of any other
officer or non-commissioned member or any thing in, on or about

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any controlled area, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any quarters under the control of the Canadian Forces
  or the Department,

in accordance with the custom or practice of the service.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Search]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899365 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to all
persons who are subject to the Code of Service Discipline.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** As a condition of being
given access to a controlled area, a person shall, on demand of a
designated authority, submit to a search without warrant of his person
or personal property while entering or exiting the controlled area or
any restricted area within the controlled area.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  exiting as described in section 5 who refuses to submit to a search of
  his person or personal property when required to do so pursuant to
  that section may have his person or personal property searched by a
  designated authority.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of any search by a designated
  authority pursuant to subsection (1), the designated authority shall
  use only such force as is necessary.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Except where there are
reasonable grounds to believe that an immediate search is necessary to
maintain security or to prevent danger to the safety of any person, a
search of a person pursuant to this Part shall be carried out only by a
person of the same sex.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A designated authority may
search without warrant any personal property about a controlled area or
any restricted area within the controlled area where the designated
authority has reasonable grounds to believe that the personal property
is or may contain anything that is likely to endanger the safety of any
person within the controlled area.
