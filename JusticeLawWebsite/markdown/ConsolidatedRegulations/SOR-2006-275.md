## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725824 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[acceptable rating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[acceptable rating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rating given
      by a credit rating agency to an issuer at the time of the issuance
      or renewal of a letter of credit that is at least equal to one of
      the following ratings:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} A, from Dominion Bond Rating Service Limited;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} A, from Fitch Ratings;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} A2, from Moody\'s Investors Service; or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} A, from Standard & Poor\'s Ratings Services.
        ([note acceptable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Pension Benefits Standards
      Act, 1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bank or authorized foreign
      bank within the meaning of section 2 of the [Bank
      Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([banque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[beneficiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[beneficiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member or a former
      member of a plan or any person who is entitled to pension benefits
      under the plan except

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a former member who has transferred or has
        chosen to transfer their pension benefit credit under section 26
        of the Act; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a former member for whom the administrator has
        purchased an immediate or deferred life annuity.
        ([bénéficiaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[beneficiary representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[beneficiary representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a union
      representative or court-appointed representative of a beneficiary.
      ([représentant des bénéficiaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[cooperative credit society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cooperative credit society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      cooperative credit society to which the [Cooperative Credit
      Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01) applies or a cooperative
      credit society incorporated and regulated by or under an Act of
      the legislature of a province. ([coopérative de
      crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Crown Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Crown Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Crown
      corporation that is an agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada in
      respect of which employment has not been excepted from included
      employment by a regulation made under subsection 4(6) of the Act.
      ([société d\'État]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[default]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[default]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the occurrence of one of the
      following:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the written notification to the Superintendent
        that the administrator intends to terminate or wind up the whole
        plan under subsection 29(5) of the Act;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amendment of the plan, resolution by the
        employer or coming into force of any other measure that effects
        the termination of the whole plan;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Superintendent's declaration under
        subsection 29(2) or (2.1) of the Act that terminates the whole
        plan;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the filing of any application or petition by
        the employer, or against the employer, under the [Companies\'
        Creditors Arrangement Act](/eng/acts/C-36), the [Bankruptcy and
        Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3) or the [Winding-up and
        Restructuring Act](/eng/acts/W-11);

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the termination of the whole plan;

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the non-renewal of a letter of credit referred
        to in Part 3 for its full face amount unless

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it has been replaced by another letter of
          credit for the same face amount at least 30 days before the
          beginning of the following plan year,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to the face amount of the
          letter of credit has been remitted to the pension fund at
          least 30 days before the beginning of the following plan year,
          or

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the face amount has been reduced in
          accordance with section 26; or

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the failure by an employer to comply with a
        direction issued by the Superintendent pursuant to section 11 of
        the Act with respect to the face amount of the letters of credit
        required by subsection 19(2). ([défaut]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a trust company that is
      licensed to carry on business in Canada and that has entered into
      a trust agreement with the employer or, if the employer is not the
      administrator, with the employer and the administrator.
      ([détenteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[initial solvency deficiency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[initial solvency deficiency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      solvency deficiency of a plan that emerged on the date on which
      the valuation that identified the deficiency was performed, as
      reported in the first actuarial report filed after the coming into
      force of these Regulations, and that values the plan as of a date
      that is later than December 30, 2005 and before January 2, 2008.
      ([déficit initial de solvabilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[issuer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[issuer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bank or cooperative credit
      society that has an acceptable rating and that is not the employer
      or affiliated with the employer within the meaning of subsection
      2(2) of the [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44).
      ([émetteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[special payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[special payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a payment or one of
      a series of payments that is determined in accordance with section
      9 of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
      1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) or section 5, 6, 7 or 19 of
      these Regulations. ([paiement spécial]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided, expressions used in
  these Regulations have the same meaning as in the [Pension Benefits
  Standards Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725858 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to the funding of a defined benefit plan and, except
  as otherwise provided, the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) also apply to the funding of a plan
  under these Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, an initial
  solvency deficiency shall be calculated in accordance with the
  definition [[solvency deficiency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection
  9(1) of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) except that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the present value of any special payment referred
    to in paragraph (d) of that definition calculated in respect of the
    funding of a solvency deficiency that emerged before the emergence
    of the initial solvency deficiency shall be zero; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of Parts 2 and 3, that definition
    shall be interpreted as including the present value of the special
    payments calculated with respect to an initial unfunded liability
    that are due in the next 10 years.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, any special
  payment that would have been required to be made under subsection 9(4)
  of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) with respect to the funding of a
  solvency deficiency that emerged before the emergence of the initial
  solvency deficiency is not required to be made.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an inconsistency between these
  Regulations and the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), these Regulations shall prevail.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a plan that is established after December 31, 2005
  unless the plan is formed as a result of a merger of plans one or more
  of which was established before December 31, 2005 or is formed as a
  result of a splitting of a plan that was established before December
  31, 2005; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a plan to which the [Air Canada Pension Plan Solvency
  Deficiency Funding Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-174) apply.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Plans
  may only be funded under these Regulations if all of the payments that
  are owed to the pension fund before the day on which the initial
  solvency deficiency emerges, as required by subsection 9(14) of the
  [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), have been made as of the filing
  date of the actuarial report that shows the emergence of that initial
  solvency deficiency.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite section 8 of the [Pension Benefits Standards
  Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), the funding of a plan
  shall be considered to meet the standards for solvency if the funding
  is in accordance with Part 1, 2 or 3 of these Regulations.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[New Five-year Funding]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725872 .Part}

### [General Funding Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#h-725873 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite
  subsection 9(4) of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), an initial solvency deficiency of a
  plan may be funded by special payments sufficient to liquidate the
  initial solvency deficiency by equal annual payments over a period not
  exceeding five years from the day on which the initial solvency
  deficiency emerged.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the initial solvency deficiency is funded in
  accordance with this Part, the administrator of the plan shall notify
  the Superintendent in writing at the time of filing of the first
  actuarial report after the coming into force of these Regulations.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When a solvency deficiency emerges after the day on
  which the initial solvency deficiency emerged, the new solvency
  deficiency shall be calculated, for the purposes of subsection 9(4) of
  the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), in accordance with the definition
  [[solvency deficiency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 9(1) of
  those Regulations and that definition shall be interpreted as
  including the present value of the special payments referred to in
  subsection (1).

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[New 10-year Funding]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725878 .Part}

### [General Funding Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#h-725879 .Subheading}

**[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
this Part,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} despite paragraph 9(4)(c) of the Pension Benefits
  Standards Regulations, 1985, if there is a solvency deficiency, a plan
  shall be funded in each plan year by annual solvency special payments
  equal to the amount by which the solvency deficiency divided by 5
  exceeds the amount of going concern special payments --- other than
  those referred to in paragraph 12(1)(c) --- that are payable during
  the plan year; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [[unfunded liability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the going concern deficit of a plan as determined
    on the date that the plan was established;

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which an increase in the going
    concern liabilities of a plan resulting from an amendment to the
    plan exceeds the going concern excess of the plan as determined on
    the day before the effective date of the amendment; or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the going concern deficit of
    a plan determined at the valuation date exceeds the sum of

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the present value of going concern special
      payments established in respect of periods after the valuation
      date, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the present value of special payments referred to
      in paragraph 12(1)(b).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-149, s. 7
:::

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite
  subsection 9(4) of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), an initial solvency deficiency of a
  plan may be funded in accordance with Part 1, but the remittance to
  the pension fund of a portion of the special payments determined under
  that Part may be deferred as if the initial solvency deficiency were
  funded by special payments sufficient to liquidate the initial
  solvency deficiency by equal annual payments over a period not
  exceeding 10 years from the day on which the initial solvency
  deficiency emerged.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The initial solvency deficiency may be funded in
  accordance with this Part only if less than one third of the members
  and less than one third of the beneficiaries excluding members object
  before the date indicated in the statement referred to in paragraph
  8(1)(j).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any objection expressed by a beneficiary
  representative on behalf of the persons that they represent shall be
  counted as a separate objection for each person that they represent.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite the fact that the special payments referred
  to in subsection (1) may be made over a period that exceeds the period
  applicable under Part 1, for the purposes of subsection 8(1) of the
  Act, the amount by which the aggregate amount of special payments that
  would have been remitted to the pension fund in accordance with that
  Part from the day on which the initial solvency deficiency emerged, as
  adjusted to take into account the reductions in special payments
  resulting from the application of the [Pension Benefits Standards
  Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), plus interest, exceeds
  the aggregate amount of special payments made to the pension fund in
  accordance with this Part, plus interest, shall be considered to be an
  amount accrued to the pension fund.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Interest shall be calculated by using the interest
  rate that was assumed in valuing the liabilities of the plan for the
  purpose of calculating the initial solvency deficiency.

- SOR/2010-149, s. 8
- SOR/2015-60, s. 33

### [Multi-employer Pension Plan]{.HTitleText2} {#h-725898 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite
  subsection 9(4) of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) and section 6 of these Regulations,
  and subject to subsection (2), an initial solvency deficiency of a
  multi-employer pension plan may be funded by special payments
  sufficient to liquidate the initial solvency deficiency by equal
  annual payments over a period not exceeding 10 years from the day on
  which the initial solvency deficiency emerged.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the funding is for an initial solvency deficiency
  of a multi-employer pension plan and if the annual amount of payments
  required to be made to the pension fund under subsection (1) is less
  than the aggregate amount of payments that are required to be made to
  the pension fund, excluding the normal cost and the special payments
  required to liquidate an unfunded liability, under all applicable
  collective agreements, the amount of payments required to be made to
  the pension fund in accordance with this Part shall be the aggregate
  amount of payments required to be made to the pension fund pursuant to
  all applicable collective agreements.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The initial solvency deficiency may be funded in
  accordance with this Part only if less than one third of the members
  and less than one third of the beneficiaries excluding members object
  before the date indicated in the statement referred to in paragraph
  8(1)(j).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any objection expressed by a beneficiary
  representative on behalf of the persons that they represent shall be
  counted as a separate objection for each person that they represent.

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the solvency ratio of the plan as of the day on

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the initial solvency deficiency;

    less than the plan liabilities;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the special payments that would have been made
    during the first plan year covered by the actuarial report referred
    to in paragraph 10(b) if the initial solvency deficiency were to be
    funded in accordance with Part 1;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the special payments that are to be made during the
    first plan year covered by the actuarial report referred to in
    paragraph 10(b) if the initial solvency deficiency is funded in
    accordance with this Part;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2015-60, s. 34\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that the plan may be funded
    in accordance with this Part only if less than one third of the
    members object and less than one third of the beneficiaries

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that the Superintendent\'s
    approval is not required to fund the initial solvency deficiency in
    accordance with this Part;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that the beneficiaries may
    object to the proposal to fund the plan in accordance with this Part

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that if the plan is funded

    the pension benefits will be restricted for the first five plan
    years of funding in accordance with this Part; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a statement setting out the right of access to the
    documents described in paragraph 28(1)(c) of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a beneficiary is represented by a beneficiary

  out in subsection (1) to the beneficiary representative.

### [Documents and Information To Be Filed with Superintendent]{.HTitleText2} {#h-725925 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The administrator shall
file the following documents and information with the Superintendent:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} written notification that the initial solvency
  deficiency is to be funded in accordance with this Part;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the actuarial report valuing the plan as of the day
  on which the initial solvency deficiency emerged;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} other than in the case of a multi-employer pension
  plan, a written statement confirming that a resolution of the board of
  directors of the employer has been passed, if the employer is a
  corporation, or, if the employer is not a corporation, an approval of
  the persons who have the authority to direct or authorize the actions
  of that body, has been given, authorizing the special payment schedule
  calculated in accordance with this Part; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a written statement confirming that the information
  set out in section 8 has been provided to the beneficiaries or to the
  beneficiary representatives and that less than one third of the
  members have objected and less than one third of the beneficiaries
  excluding members have objected.

report.

### [New Solvency Deficiency]{.HTitleText2} {#h-725935 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite section 9 of the [Pension
  Benefits Standards Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), a
  solvency deficiency that emerges after the day on which the initial
  solvency deficiency emerged shall be calculated as the amount by which
  the solvency liabilities exceed the sum of the following amounts:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the adjusted solvency asset amount,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the present value of special payments made under
    section 6 or 7 if at least one of those payments is due more than
    five years after the valuation date, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the present value of the going concern special
    payments that were used to fund the initial solvency deficiency that
    are due during the period beginning on the valuation date and ending
    on the 10th anniversary of the date of emergence of the initial
    solvency deficiency if at least one of those payments is due more
    than five years after the valuation date.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The interest rate used to determine the present value
  of the special payments referred to in subsection (1) is the same as
  the interest rate used to determine the solvency liabilities.

### [Termination of Plan]{.HTitleText2} {#h-725944 .Subheading}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** If a plan is fully

plan exceed its assets, the lesser of the amount determined in
subsection 6(4) and the amount by which the liabilities of the plan
exceed its assets shall immediately be remitted to the pension fund.

### [Ceasing 10-year Funding]{.HTitleText2} {#h-725948 .Subheading}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A plan
  may cease to be funded under this Part, beginning on the first day of

  than six months after the beginning of that plan year.

terminated.

### [Plan with Surplus]{.HTitleText2} {#h-725955 .Subheading}

funded in accordance with this Part and the plan has a surplus as of the
first day of the plan year, this Part ceases to apply to the plan on the
first day of that plan year.

### [Plan Without Surplus]{.HTitleText2} {#h-725957 .Subheading}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a

  9 of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) applies except as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when funding ceases before the sixth plan year,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the administrator shall have an actuarial report
      prepared --- in which the present value of the special payments

      as of the first day of the plan year in which funding ceases,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the aggregate amount of
      special payments that would have been made to the pension fund in
      accordance with Part 1 from the day on which the initial solvency
      deficiency emerged to the day on which funding ceases, as adjusted
      to take into account the reductions in special payments resulting
      from the application of the [Pension Benefits Standards
      Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), plus interest,
      exceeds the aggregate amount of special payments made to the
      pension fund in accordance with this Part, plus interest, shall
      immediately be remitted to the pension fund, and

    - [(iii) and (iv)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2010-149, s.
      11\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the special payments referred to in section 6 or
      7 shall continue to be made until the first special payment
      required to fund the remaining initial solvency deficiency
      referred to in subparagraph (iii) is made to the pension fund; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when funding ceases after the fifth plan year,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the administrator shall have an actuarial report
      prepared as of the first day of the plan year in which funding
      ceases, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the aggregate amount of
      special payments that would have been made to the pension fund in
      accordance with Part 1 from the day on which the initial solvency
      deficiency emerged to the day on which funding ceases, as adjusted
      to take into account the reductions in special payments resulting
      from the application of the [Pension Benefits Standards
      Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), plus interest,
      exceeds the aggregate amount of special payments made to the
      pension fund in accordance with this Part, plus interest, shall
      immediately be remitted to the pension fund.

### [Crown Corporations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-725971 .Subheading}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  administrator of a plan of a Crown Corporation with an initial

  not have to comply with subsection 6(2) and sections 8 and 10 if the
  administrator files the following documents and information with the
  Superintendent:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the actuarial report valuing the plan as of the day

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written statement confirming that a resolution of
    the board of directors of the Crown Corporation has been passed
    authorizing the special payment schedule calculated in accordance
    with this Part;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a written statement confirming that the board of

    intends to fund the initial solvency deficiency in accordance with
    this Part.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[10-year Funding with Letters of Credit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725980 .Part}

### [General Funding Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#h-725981 .Subheading}

**[[18.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
this Part,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} despite paragraph 9(4)(c) of the Pension Benefits
  Standards Regulations, 1985, if there is a solvency deficiency, a plan
  shall be funded in each plan year by annual solvency special payments
  equal to the amount by which the solvency deficiency divided by 5
  exceeds the amount of going concern special payments --- other than
  those referred to in paragraph 27(1)(c) --- that are payable during
  the plan year; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [[unfunded liability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the going concern deficit of a plan as determined
    on the date that the plan was established;

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which an increase in the going
    concern liabilities of a plan resulting from an amendment to the
    plan exceeds the going concern excess of the plan as determined on
    the day before the effective date of the amendment; or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the going concern deficit of
    a plan determined at the valuation date exceeds the sum of

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the present value of going concern special
      payments established in respect of periods after the valuation
      date, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the present value of special payments referred to
      in paragraph 27(1)(b).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-149, s. 12
:::

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 9(4) of the
  [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), an initial solvency deficiency of a
  plan may be funded by special payments sufficient to liquidate the
  initial solvency deficiency by equal annual payments over a period not
  exceeding 10 years from the day on which the initial solvency
  deficiency emerged.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The initial solvency deficiency may be funded in
  accordance with this Part if the employer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtains letters of credit for each of the first
    five plan years of funding under this Part, for the amount
    representing the difference between the present value, at the end of
    each plan year, of the remaining special payments under this Part
    and the present value of the remaining special payments that would
    have been required to be made to liquidate the initial solvency
    deficiency as if it had been funded under Part 1; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintains letters of credit for the sixth plan year
    of funding and for each plan year after that year, representing the
    present value at the beginning of each plan year of the remaining
    special payments under this Part.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The present value of the remaining special payments
  shall be determined by using the interest rate that was assumed in
  valuing the liabilities of the plan for the purpose of calculating the
  initial solvency deficiency.

### [Letter of Credit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-725998 .Subheading}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  letter of credit required by this Part shall be an irrevocable,
  unconditional standby letter of credit that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is in accordance with the rules of International
    Standby Practices 1998 (publication No. 590 of the International
    Chamber of Commerce), as amended from time to time;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is payable only in Canadian currency;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is issued or confirmed by an issuer who is a member
    of the Canadian Payments Association that has been assigned an
    acceptable rating; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provides that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the letter of credit is made out to the holder\'s
      benefit,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the issuer will pay the face amount of the
      letter of credit on demand from the holder without inquiring
      whether the holder has a right to make the demand,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the bankruptcy of the employer shall have no
      effect on the rights and obligations of the issuer and the holder
      set out in the letter of credit,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the letter of credit will expire on the day on
      which the plan\'s year ends,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the letter of credit will automatically be
      renewed for the full face amount for further one-year periods on
      the expiry date referred to in subparagraph (iv) unless the issuer
      notifies the holder, in writing, of the non-renewal not less than
      90 days before the expiry date, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the letter of credit may not be amended during
      the term of the letter of credit and may not be assigned except to
      another holder.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A letter of credit shall be obtained not later than
  the day on which the actuarial report is filed with or provided to the
  Superintendent for the first plan year of funding, and at least
  30 days before the beginning of each subsequent plan year that is
  covered by it.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The letter of credit shall immediately be provided to
  the holder.

- SOR/2011-85, s. 19

### [Trust Agreement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-726019 .Subheading}

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  employer and, if the employer is not the administrator of the plan,
  the administrator shall enter into a trust agreement or shall amend
  any existing trust agreement it may have with the holder regarding the
  letters of credit referred to in this Part.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The trust agreement shall provide that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder shall hold the letters of credit in
    Canada in trust for the plan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the definition [[default]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
    subsection 1(1) applies to the agreement;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the employer shall immediately notify, in writing,
    the holder and the Superintendent and, if the employer is not the
    administrator of the plan, the administrator of a default;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if not otherwise notified under paragraph (c), the
    administrator shall notify, in writing, the holder and the
    Superintendent of a default immediately after becoming aware of it;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} on receipt of the notice referred to in
    paragraph (c) or (d), the holder shall immediately make a demand for
    payment of the face amount of all of the letters of credit held in
    respect of the plan;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} on receipt of a written notice of default from any
    person other than the employer or the administrator, the holder
    shall

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} immediately notify, in writing, the employer, the
      administrator and the Superintendent of the notice; and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} make a demand for payment of the face amount of
      all of the letters of credit held in respect of the plan unless
      the administrator provides a written notice to the holder within
      30 days after receipt of the notice that the default has not
      occurred;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} when a holder makes a demand for payment of a
    letter of credit held for the plan, it shall notify, in writing, the
    employer, the administrator and the Superintendent that it has made
    the demand;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the holder shall immediately notify, in writing,
    the employer, the administrator and the Superintendent if the issuer
    does not pay the face amount of a letter of credit after a demand
    for payment has been made,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the holder shall not make a demand for payment if a
    letter of credit expires without being renewed, or the face amount
    is being reduced, in accordance with this Part;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the administrator shall notify the holder of any
    circumstance when a letter of credit may expire, or when the face
    amount of a letter of credit may be reduced, in accordance with this
    Part; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the administrator shall provide the holder with a
    copy of the statements referred to in paragraph 24(1)(e) and
    subsection 24(2) and with a copy of the written notice referred to
    in paragraph 30(a).

### [Reduction of the Face Amount of a Letter of Credit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-726044 .Subheading}

  beginning of a plan year, by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the aggregate amount of

    payments and the normal cost of the plan for that year as shown in

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the aggregate face amount of
    all of the letters of credit that are held by the holder in respect
    of the plan exceeds the amount set out in paragraph 19(2)(a) or (b),
    as the case may be.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The face amount of the letter of credit shall not be
  reduced following a default.

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite section 9 of the [Pension
  Benefits Standards Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), a
  solvency deficiency that emerges after the day on which the initial
  solvency deficiency emerged shall be calculated as the amount by which

    than five years after the valuation date.

### [Failure to Pay Letter of Credit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-726061 .Subheading}

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** On receipt of the notice

### [Occurrence of Default]{.HTitleText2} {#h-726063 .Subheading}

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  default occurs, the amount by which the aggregate amount of special
  payments that would have been remitted to the pension fund in
  accordance with Part 1 from the day on which the initial solvency
  deficiency emerged, as adjusted to take into account the reductions in
  special payments resulting from the application of the [Pension
  Benefits Standards Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19),
  plus interest, exceeds the aggregate amount of special payments made
  to the pension fund in accordance with this Part, plus interest, shall
  immediately be remitted to the pension fund.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except if a plan is fully terminated, the
  administrator shall have an actuarial report prepared --- in which the
  present value of the special payments referred to in subsection 19(1)
  shall be zero --- valuing the plan as at the last day of the plan year
  in which the default occurs.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any remaining initial solvency deficiency disclosed
  by the actuarial report prepared in accordance with subsection (2)
  shall be calculated by including as an asset any amount remitted to
  the pension fund in accordance with subsection (1) and the remaining
  initial solvency deficiency shall be considered to have emerged as of
  the day on which the initial solvency deficiency emerged.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The remaining initial solvency deficiency calculated
  under subsection (3) shall be funded by special payments sufficient to
  liquidate that initial solvency deficiency by equal annual payments
  over a period not exceeding five years minus the number of years that
  the plan was funded in accordance with this Part.

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A plan
  may cease to be funded in accordance with this Part, beginning on the
  first day of a plan year, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the administrator gives written notice to the
    Superintendent not later than six months after the beginning of that
    plan year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the aggregate amount of special

    as if a default occurred, except that the actuarial report shall be
    prepared valuing the plan as of the first day of the plan year in
    which funding ceases.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraphs (1)(b) and (c) do not apply if the face
  amount of the letters of credit obtained to fund the plan under this
  Part is included as a solvency asset as defined in subsection 2(1) of

  - [28]{.lawlabel} A reference to an "unfunded liability" in these
    Regulations includes a reference to an "initial unfunded liability"
    as defined in subsection 9(1) of the [Pension Benefits Standards
    Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), as it read
    immediately before this section comes into force.

- --- SOR/2010-149, s. 30

  - [30]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to a plan to which
    the [Canadian Press Pension Plan Solvency Deficiency Funding
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2009-183) apply.
