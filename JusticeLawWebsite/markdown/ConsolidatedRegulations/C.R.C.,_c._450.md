[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 450

[CURRENCY ACT](/eng/acts/C-52)

Regulations Respecting the Redemption of Subsidiary Coin
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557337 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Redemption of Subsidiary Coin
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._450).

## [Redemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557339 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subsidiary coin of Canada
that has been called in or that is unfit for circulation is redeemable
at face value if it is identifiable as coin authorized for circulation
in Canada as described in subsection 6(1) of the [Currency
Act](/eng/acts/C-52), but the Minister of Supply and Services, or such
other person as he may designate, may refuse to redeem any such coin if
it appears to have been wilfully or maliciously mutilated or defaced.
