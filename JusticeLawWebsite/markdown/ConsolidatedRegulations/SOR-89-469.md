## Petro-Canada --- Inter-City Gas Corporation Transactions Authorization Order {#petro-canada-inter-city-gas-corporation-transactions-authorization-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-469
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1989-09-25

Order Authorizing Petro-Canada Limited and its Wholly-Owned Subsidiaries
to Undertake Certain Transactions in Relation to the Completion of the
Acquisition of the Assets and Undertaking Comprising the Propane
Business of Inter-City Gas Corporation

P.C. 1989-1913 1989-09-25
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas Petro-Canada proposes to acquire the assets and undertaking
comprising the propane business of Inter-City Gas Corporation;

Whereas the transactions necessary for the completion of the acquisition
do not fall within the scope of the Petro-Canada Transactions
Authorization Order, 1989[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EBBCA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EBBCA){#fn_1e}SOR/89-33, 1989 [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 249
  :::

And Whereas the Governor in Council, pursuant to subsection 91(6) of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is satisfied that
Petro-Canada and its wholly-owned subsidiaries are otherwise empowered
to undertake the transactions authorized by this Order.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources, pursuant
to subsections 91(1) and (3) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to make the annexed Order
authorizing Petro-Canada and its wholly-owned subsidiaries to undertake
certain transactions in relation to the completion of the acquisition of
the assets and undertaking comprising the propane business of Inter-City
Gas Corporation.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920417 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the Petro-Canada Limited --- Inter-City Gas Corporation Transactions
Authorization Order.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1991, c. 10, s. 19
:::

## [Authorized Transactions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920421 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Petro-Canada Limited and
its wholly-owned subsidiaries are hereby authorized to undertake the
following transactions in relation to the completion of the acquisition
of the assets and undertaking comprising the propane business of
Inter-City Gas Corporation:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the incorporation of corporations any shares of
  which, on incorporation, would be held by, on behalf of or in trust
  for Petro-Canada Limited or any of its wholly-owned subsidiaries;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the acquisition of shares of corporations that, on
  acquisition, would be held by, on behalf of or in trust for
  Petro-Canada Limited or any of its wholly-owned subsidiaries;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the acquisition of all or substantially all of the
  assets of corporations; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the dissolution or amalgamation of wholly-owned
  subsidiaries of Petro-Canada Limited.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1991, c. 10, s. 19
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every wholly-owned
subsidiary of Petro-Canada Limited is hereby authorized, in relation to
the completion of the acquisition and undertaking comprising the propane
business of Inter-City Gas Corporation,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to sell or otherwise dispose of any of the shares of
  the subsidiary; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to sell or otherwise dispose of all or substantially
  all of the assets of the subsidiary.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1991, c. 10, s. 19
:::
