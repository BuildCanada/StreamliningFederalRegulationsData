## Program Registration Fees Order {#program-registration-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/84-155
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1984-02-07

Order Prescribing the Fees To Be Paid for Registration under a Program
Established Pursuant to the Urea Formaldehyde Insulation Act
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs, pursuant to
paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)
and Order in Council P.C. 1984-398 of 2 February 1984[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EECCA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Order prescribing the fees to be paid for registration under a program
established pursuant to the Urea Formaldehyde Insulation
Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-155)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_2e){#fn_2e-ID0EBCCA}.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-882086 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Program Registration Fees Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-155).

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-882088 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The fees payable by a
person who applies

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to register in a Field Sampling Program, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for registration in an Analytical Program

established pursuant to the Urea Formaldehyde Insulation Act shall be as
follows:

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Field Sampling Program ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot}\$250, and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Analytical Program, per laboratory ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot}250.
