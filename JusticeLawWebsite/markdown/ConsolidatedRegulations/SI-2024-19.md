[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a [[passport]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
  section 2 of the [Canadian Passport Order](/eng/regulations/SI-81-86);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a [[certificate of
  citizenship]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of
  the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a travel document issued to a permanent resident
  under subsection 31(3) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a permanent resident card provided or issued under
  subsection 53(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an emergency travel document.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted to any
person who meets the conditions set out in section 3 of the fees paid or
payable under any of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} paragraphs 7(c), 9(a) and (b) and items 10 and 14 of
  the schedule to the [Passport and Other Travel Document Services Fees
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-253);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 4 of the [Consular Services Fees
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-538);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} item 6 of the schedule to the [Citizenship
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-246);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} section 315 of the [Immigration and Refugee
  Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on the
condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} at some time during the period beginning on August
  13, 2020 and ending on January 31, 2021, the person was in, or had
  their residence in, an area affected by the Port of Beirut explosions;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a document that was issued to that person was ---
  while it was valid --- lost, damaged, destroyed or rendered
  inaccessible as a result of the Port of Beirut explosions;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} during the period beginning on August 13, 2020 and
  ending on January 31, 2021, that person, or a person acting on their
  behalf, made an application in accordance with applicable regulations
  for the issuance of a document bearing, if applicable, the same expiry
  date as the document referred to in paragraph (b);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} that person, or the person acting on their behalf,
  included with the application a declaration made by that person, or on
  their behalf, stating that the document to be replaced was lost,
  damaged, destroyed or rendered inaccessible as a result of the Port of
  Beirut explosions; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the fees in question have not been remitted under
  section 11 of the [Passport and Other Travel Document Services Fees
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-253).
