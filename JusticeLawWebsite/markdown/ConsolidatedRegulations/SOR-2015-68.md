## Low-value Amounts Regulations {#low-value-amounts-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2015-68
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2015-03-25

Low-value Amounts Regulations
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to paragraphs 155.2(2)(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EECBA} and (c) of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBCBA}, the Treasury Board is of
the opinion that circumstances justify doing so;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EECBA){#fn_a}S.C.,
  2014, c. 39, s. 304
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b-ID0EBCBA){#fn_b}R.S., c. F-11
  :::

Therefore, the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 155.2(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EGAAA} of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EDAAA}, makes the annexed Low-value
Amounts Regulations.
:::

Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts deemed nil

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 155.2(1) of the Act, the amount is fixed at:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 99 cents for all amounts payable by Her Majesty in
  right of Canada under the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), the
  [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6) and the [Old Age Security
  Act](/eng/acts/O-9), as well as any regulations made under those Acts;
  or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} two dollars in any other case.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- accumulated amounts

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite
  section 2, the following amounts payable by Her Majesty in right of
  Canada accumulate for the period referred to in paragraph (2)(a) or
  (b):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} amounts payable under the [Canada Pension
    Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), the [Veterans Well-being
    Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8), the Department of Veteran Affairs Act, the
    [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9), the [Pension
    Act](/eng/acts/P-6) and the [War Veterans Allowance
    Act](/eng/acts/W-3), as well as any regulations made under those
    Acts;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} amounts payable under the authority conferred on
    the Minister of Public Works and Government Services under sections
    12 and 13 of the [Department of Public Works and Government Services
    Act](/eng/acts/P-38.2);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} amounts payable to officers and non-commissioned
    members as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5) and to members as defined in subsection 2(1) of
    the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10), on account
    of salary, wages, pay and pay and allowances; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} amounts payable under the [Canadian Forces
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17), as well as any regulations made
    under that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of accumulation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amounts payable accumulate during the following
  periods:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of amounts referred to in paragraph
    (1)(a), for a period of up to 12 months after the day on which they
    become payable;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of amounts referred to in paragraphs
    (1)(b) to (d), for a period of up to 12 months after the day on
    which they become payable or until the end of the calendar year in
    which they became payable, whichever comes first.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If at any time during the period referred to in
  paragraph (2)(a) or (b) the accumulated amount exceeds the applicable
  threshold, payment will be made within 30 days after that time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No payment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to paragraph 4(d), if the accumulated amount
  does not exceed the applicable threshold at the end of the period
  referred to in paragraph (2)(a) or (b), it is deemed nil under
  subsection 155.2(1) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Circumstance for accumulation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), only amounts
  payable to the same recipient that are of the same type or payable
  under the same program, legislation or regulation may accumulate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 155.2(1) of the
Act does not apply to the following amounts:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} amounts payable by or to Her Majesty in right of
  Canada in currencies other than Canadian dollars;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} user fees as defined in section 2 the [User Fees
  Act](/eng/acts/U-3.7);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} immediate payments in exchange for goods and
  services; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} amounts that are requested in writing by the
  recipient, if the request is sent to the appropriate Minister within
  12 months after the day on which the amount becomes payable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}April 1, 2015

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on April 1, 2015.
