Order Designating the Minister of Industry as the Designated Minister
for the purposes of the Business Development Bank of Canada Act and as

P.C. 2025-429 2025-03-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under the definition [[Designated
Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the [Business
Development Bank of Canada Act](/eng/acts/B-9.9)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2851_hq_1741740274){#fn_81000-3-2851_hq_1741740274-ID0ELFAA},
paragraph (d) of the definition [[appropriate
Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2851_hq_1741740307){#fn_81000-3-2851_hq_1741740307-ID0EGFAA}
and subparagraph (a)(ii) of the definition [[appropriate
Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 83(1) of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2851_hq_1741740307){#fn_81000-3-2851_hq_1741740307-ID0EBFAA},
