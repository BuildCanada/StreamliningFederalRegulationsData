## Polar Bear Pass Withdrawal Order {#polar-bear-pass-withdrawal-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/84-409
:::

[CANADA OIL AND GAS OPERATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/O-7)

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 1984-05-25

Order Respecting the Withdrawal of Certain Lands in and Between the
Bracebridge-Goodsir Inlets at Bathurst Island in the Northwest
Territories From Disposal

P.C. 1984-1768 1984-05-24
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas exploratory permits covering the lands described in Schedule I
hereto (hereafter referred to as the "lands") were issued by the
Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development under the [Canada
Oil and Gas Land Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1518) and
extensive exploration for oil and gas has been conducted pursuant to
such permits (hereafter referred to as the "permits");

Whereas Aberford Resources Ltd., Bankeno Mines Limited, Canterra Energy
Ltd., Dome Petroleum Limited, Esso Resources Canada Limited, Francana
Oil and Gas Limited, Global Arctic Islands Limited, Gulf Canada
Resources Incorporated, Hudsons Bay Oil and Gas Company Limited,
Panarctic Oils Limited, Phoenix Resources Company and Suncor
Incorporated and Mildred H. and Ethel A. Hembdt are the interest owners
in respect of the permits;

Whereas under subsection 63(1) of the Canada Oil and Gas Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-84-409_e_hq_7921){#fn_SOR-84-409_e_hq_7921-ID0EBBDA},
the interest owners of such permits are required to negotiate an
exploration agreement within a specified period of time in order to
maintain the interests;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-84-409_e_hq_7921-ID0EBBDA){#fn_SOR-84-409_e_hq_7921}S.C.
  1980-81-82-83, c. 81
  :::

Whereas it is intended to create a National Wildlife Area under the
provisions of the [Canada Wildlife Act](/eng/acts/W-9), to be located on
the lands described in Schedules II and III hereto;

And Whereas in support of that intent the aforesaid interest owners have
surrendered all interest in the lands described in Schedule I hereto.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development, is pleased hereby,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 19(a) of the [Territorial Lands
  Act](/eng/acts/T-7), to revoke the Withdrawal of Certain Lands
  (Bathurst Island N.W.T.) from Disposal Order, made by Order in Council
  P.C. 1983-706 of 10th March, 1983[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-84-409_e_hq_7922){#fn_SOR-84-409_e_hq_7922-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-84-409_e_hq_7922-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_SOR-84-409_e_hq_7922}SI/83-65,
    1983 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 1173
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 19(a) of the [Territorial Lands
  Act](/eng/acts/T-7) and subsection 5(2) of the Canada Oil and Gas Act,
  to make the annexed Order respecting the withdrawal of certain lands
  in and between the Bracebridge-Goodsir Inlets at Bathurst Island in
  the Northwest Territories from disposal, in substitution therefor.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-882771 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Polar Bear Pass Withdrawal Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-409).

## [Territorial Lands Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-882773 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The lands described in
Schedule II, being required for the protection of wildlife, wetlands and
associated habitat, are hereby withdrawn from disposal under the
[Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7).

## [Canada Oil and Gas Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-882775 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The lands described in
Schedule III, being required for the protection of wildlife, wetlands,
and associated habitat, which lands are not in Schedule II, are hereby
withdrawn from the application of the Canada Oil and Gas Act.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The lands described in
Schedule I, that are not described in Schedules II and III, are hereby
withdrawn from the application of the Canada Oil and Gas Act in order to
facilitate the use of the lands described in Schedules II and III for
the protection of wildlife, wetlands and associated habitat.
