## Transfer of Duties Order {#transfer-of-duties-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2018-72
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2018-08-08

Transfer of Duties Order

P.C. 2018-1017 2018-07-18
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1662-E_hq_22381){#fn_81000-3-1662-E_hq_22381-ID0EHDAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-1662-E_hq_22382){#fn_81000-3-1662-E_hq_22382-ID0EEDAA},
transfers from the Minister of Canadian Heritage to the Minister of La
Francophonie the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of
Canadian Heritage under the [Official Languages
Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-1662-E_hq_22383){#fn_81000-3-1662-E_hq_22383-ID0EBDAA}.
