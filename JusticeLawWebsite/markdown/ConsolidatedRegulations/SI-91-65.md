## Exclusion Approval Order for certain persons and certain positions (Air Traffic Control Group), 1991 {#exclusion-approval-order-for-certain-persons-and-certain-positions-air-traffic-control-group-1991 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/91-65
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 1991-05-08

Order Approving the Exclusion by the Public Service Commission from the
Operation of Section 10 and Subsection 21(1) of the Public Service
Employment Act of Certain Persons and Positions

P.C. 1991-771  1991-04-25
::::

:::::::::: section
:::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Public Service Commission has decided that it is not
practicable nor in the best interests of the Public Service

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to qualifications other than language
  skills, to apply section 10 of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) to training positions in the Department of
  Transport to which are to be or are appointed air traffic controllers
  who participate in the Selection by Seniority Program for operational
  training or in the Transfer Down Program,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to qualifications other than language
  skills, to apply section 10 of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) to operational positions in the Department of
  Transport to which are to be or are appointed air traffic controllers
  who have successfully completed their training in the Selection by
  Seniority Program for operational training or in the Transfer Down
  Program,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to apply subsection 21(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) to all persons who, but for this
  Order, would have a right of appeal under that subsection against the
  appointment or proposed appointment to training positions in the
  Department of Transport of certain air traffic controllers who
  participate in the Selection by Seniority Program for operational
  training or in the Transfer Down Program,

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to apply subsection 21(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) to all persons who, but for this
  Order, would have a right of appeal under that subsection against the
  appointment or proposed appointment to operational positions in the
  Department of Transport of air traffic controllers who have
  successfully completed their training in the Selection by Seniority
  Program for operational training or in the Transfer Down Program,

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} to apply subsection 21(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) to all persons who, but for this
  Order, would have a right of appeal under that subsection against the
  appointment or proposed appointment to positions in the Department of
  Transport of air traffic controllers who have not successfully
  completed or have terminated without completing their training in the
  Selection by Seniority Program for operational training or in the
  Transfer Down Program, during the period beginning on May 1, 1991 and
  ending on December 31, 1996.

And Whereas the Public Service Commission, pursuant to subsection 41(1)
of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), has decided
that certain persons and positions should be excluded from the operation
of section 10 and subsection 21(1) of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01);

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Secretary of State, pursuant to subsection 41(1)
of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Order approving the exclusion by the Public
Service Commission from the operation of section 10 and subsection 21(1)
of the Public Service Employment Act of certain persons and
positions](/eng/regulations/SI-91-65).

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/96-37, s. 1
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-644051 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Exclusion Approval Order for certain persons and certain positions
(Air Traffic Control Group), 1991](/eng/regulations/SI-91-65).

## [Approval]{.HTitleText1} {#h-644053 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The exclusion by the Public
Service Commission, from the operation of section 10 of the [Public
Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), in relation to
qualifications other than language skills, of training positions in the
Department of Transport to which are to be or are appointed air traffic
controllers who participate in the Selection by Seniority Program for
operational training or in the Transfer Down Program, during the period
beginning on May 1, 1991 and ending on December 31, 1996, is hereby
approved.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/96-37, s. 2
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The exclusion by the Public
Service Commission, from the operation of section 10 of the [Public
Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), in relation to
qualifications other than language skills, of operational positions in
the Department of Transport to which are to be or are appointed air
traffic controllers who have successfully completed their training in
the Selection by Seniority Program for operational training or in the
Transfer Down Program, during the period beginning on May 1, 1991 and
ending on December 31, 1996, is hereby approved.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/96-37, s. 2
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The exclusion by the Public
Service Commission, from the operation of subsection 21(1) of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), of all persons who,
but for this Order, would have a right of appeal under that subsection
against the appointment or proposed appointment to training positions in
the Department of Transport of certain air traffic controllers who
participate in the Selection by Seniority Program for operational
training or in the Transfer Down Program, during the period beginning on
May 1, 1991 and ending on December 31, 1996, is hereby approved.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/96-37, s. 2
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The exclusion by the Public
Service Commission, from the operation of subsection 21(1) of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), of all persons who,
but for this Order, would have a right of appeal under that subsection
against the appointment or proposed appointment to operational positions
in the Department of Transport of air traffic controllers who have
successfully completed their training in the Selection by Seniority
Program for operational training or in the Transfer Down Program, during
the period beginning on May 1, 1991 and ending on December 31, 1996, is
hereby approved.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/96-37, s. 2
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The exclusion by the Public
Service Commission, from the operation of subsection 21(1) of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), of all persons who,
but for this Order, would have a right of appeal under that subsection
against the appointment or proposed appointment to positions in the
Department of Transport of air traffic controllers who have not
successfully completed or have terminated without completing their
training in the Selection by Seniority Program for operational training
or in the Transfer Down Program during the period beginning on May 1,
1991 and ending on December 31, 1996, is hereby approved.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/96-37, s. 2
