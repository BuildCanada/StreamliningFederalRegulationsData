## Shipping Casualties Reporting Regulations {#shipping-casualties-reporting-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/85-514
:::

[CANADA SHIPPING ACT, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15)

Registration 1985-05-31

Regulations Respecting the Reporting of Shipping Casualties, Accidents,
Dangerous Occurrences, Deaths and Disappearances of Persons on Ships

P.C. 1985-1804 1985-05-30
::::

:::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 543 of the [Canada
Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9), is pleased hereby to revoke the [Shipping
Casualties Reporting Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-514), C.R.C.,
c. 1478 and to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the reporting of
shipping casualties, accidents, dangerous occurrences, deaths and
disappearances of persons on ships](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-514), in
substitution therefor.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886955 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Shipping Casualties Reporting
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-514).

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886957 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[accident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[accident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an accident occurring on
    board a ship as a result of which a person or several persons

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} lose their lives,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are believed to be missing overboard from a ship,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sustain bodily injuries serious enough to prevent
      the resumption of normal duties within 24 hours after medical
      treatment,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} suffer temporary asphyxiation, or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} suffer electrocution that results in temporary
      loss of consciousness; ([accident]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Shipping
    Act](/eng/acts/S-9); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canadian radio ship reporting station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian radio ship reporting station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    a Canadian Coast Guard radio station or a Canadian Vessel Traffic
    Service Centre of the Department of Transport, a Canadian marine
    radio station operated by the St. Lawrence Seaway Authority or a
    Canadian harbour radio station; ([station de radiocommunications
    maritime canadienne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dangerous occurrence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dangerous occurrence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an event
    involving

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the breakage or malfunction of any rigging,
      structure or machinery on a ship or belonging to a ship that could
      have caused serious injury or loss of life, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a situation which could have resulted in a
      casualty or accident; ([événement dangereux]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[shipping casualty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shipping casualty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a shipping casualty
    described in section 541 of the Act. ([sinistre
    maritime]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-1088, s. 1
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886973 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all ships in Canadian waters and to all Canadian ships in any waters.

## [Preliminary Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886975 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  ship, or a vessel being towed by a ship, is involved in a shipping
  casualty, an accident or a dangerous occurrence, the master, any
  certificated officer, operator, member of the crew, pilot or person
  responsible for the ship, or the vessel being towed, shall report the
  incident without delay in accordance with subsections (2) and (3).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the report referred to in
  subsection (1) shall be made without delay

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by radio communication to a Canadian radio ship
    reporting station, where the shipping casualty, accident or
    dangerous occurrence takes place in Canadian waters or on a voyage
    to or from a Canadian port or alongside a port facility in a
    Canadian port; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the shipping casualty, accident or dangerous
    occurrence takes place outside the range of a Canadian radio ship
    reporting station, to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any other radio ship reporting station,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a chief officer of customs or the superintendent
      of a mercantile marine office, if the location is in a
      Commonwealth country other than Canada, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian consular officer, if the location is
      a place other than a place referred to in subparagraph (ii).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the report referred to in subsection (1) cannot
  be communicated by radio from a ship while it is in a Canadian port,
  the shipping casualty, accident or dangerous occurrence shall be
  reported by telephone to the local Canadian radio ship reporting
  station, or to the Canadian Coast Guard Operations Centre of the
  Department of Transport in Ottawa.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The report referred to in subsection (1) shall
  indicate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the ship from which the report is
    being made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the incident;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the ship or ships involved in the
    incident;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date, time and location of the incident;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the number of persons killed, missing or injured as
    a result of the incident;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} whether a pilot is on board the ship or ships
    involved in the incident; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} whether the incident has caused or is likely to
    cause

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an obstruction to navigation or any other serious
      hazard, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} pollution of any waters.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-1088, s.2
:::

## [Written Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886997 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  ship is involved in a shipping casualty, an accident or a dangerous
  occurrence, the master, any certificated officer, operator, member of
  the crew or person responsible for the ship shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 24 hours of the occurrence of the incident
    or as soon as possible thereafter, make a written report on the
    incident, including a statement as to its probable cause; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} forward the report by air mail without delay to the
    Chief, Marine Casualty Investigations, Department of Transport,
    Ottawa.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report referred to in subsection (1) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be made on the form set out in the schedule; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where possible, be completed

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in Canada, in the presence of a chief officer of
      customs, a Department of Transport marine casualty investigations
      officer, a Canadian Coast Guard ship safety officer or any other
      Department of Transport Coast Guard officer, or, where none of
      these persons is available, a Royal Canadian Mounted Police
      officer.

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in another Commonwealth country, in the presence
      of a chief officer of customs or the superintendent of a
      mercantile marine office, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in a country other than a country referred to
      in subparagraph (i) or (ii), in the presence of a Canadian
      consular officer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-1088, s.3
:::

## [Other Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887010 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where a report is made
pursant to subsection 642(1) of the Act contains all the information
required by section 5 of these Regulations, no report is required to be
field under section 5 of these Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-1088, s. 4
:::
