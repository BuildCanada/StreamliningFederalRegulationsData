## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-618603 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[dividend]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dividend]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means funds to be distributed to
    creditors in accordance with sections 148 and 151 of the [Bankruptcy
    and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3). ([dividende]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Groupe Norbourg]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Groupe Norbourg]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the five companies set
    out below that were controlled by Vincent Lacroix, that managed
    securities and investments including Norbourg and Evolution funds,
    and that made assignments in bankruptcy which were filed under
    section 49 of the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3):

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Norbourg Groupe Financier inc;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Ascencia Capital inc;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Norbourg Gestion d'actif inc;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Fonds Évolution inc;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Gestion d'actifs Perfolio inc. ([Groupe
      Norbourg]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Groupe Norbourg trustee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Groupe Norbourg trustee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means RSM Richter
    Inc. in its capacity as trustee in bankruptcy of Groupe Norbourg
    under subsection 49(4) of the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency
    Act](/eng/acts/B-3). ([syndic du Groupe Norbourg]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[investor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[investor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a unit holder who, on August
    25, 2005, held units in one or more of the Norbourg or Evolution
    funds set out in the schedule, and who made a claim against any of
    these funds. ([investisseur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Lacroix trustee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Lacroix trustee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Ernst & Young Inc. in
    its capacity as trustee in bankruptcy of Vincent Lacroix under
    subsection 43(9) of the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency
    Act](/eng/acts/B-3). ([syndic Lacroix]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[liquidator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[liquidator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Martin Daigneault of Ernst
    & Young Inc. in his capacity as liquidator under the Securities Act,
    R.S.Q., c. V-11, of the mutual funds managed by Groupe Norbourg.
    ([liquidateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-618616 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is granted to the Lacroix trustee of an amount, not exceeding
\$256,000, that is equal to the estimated bankruptcy dividend payment in
respect of tax payable by Vincent Lacroix, a bankrupt, under Part I of
the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) for the 2005 taxation year.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-618618 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted
subject to the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the liquidator or the Groupe Norbourg trustee files a
  claim in the Vincent Lacroix bankruptcy for funds misappropriated from
  investments and securities managed by the Groupe Norbourg;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Lacroix trustee accepts the validity of the claim
  by the liquidator or the Groupe Norbourg trustee in the Vincent
  Lacroix bankruptcy or accepts that the claim be recognized as valid by
  a tribunal;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the assets in the bankruptcy estate of Vincent
  Lacroix do not permit settlement of the claim of the liquidator or of
  the Groupe Norbourg trustee at the time of distribution;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with instructions from the Crown, the
  amount of dividend described in section 2 will be remitted by the
  Lacroix trustee to the liquidator;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the liquidator accepts the amount of dividend and
  accepts to distribute it to the investors in accordance with the
  directives of the tribunal having jurisdiction;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} once the claims of all investors have been fully met,
  any remaining part of the amount described in section 2 is returned by
  the liquidator to the Crown as soon as possible;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} upon request from the Crown, the liquidator
  undertakes to provide a final report to the Crown of the distribution
  to investors of the amount described in section 2;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the Crown is not liable for any fees related to the
  determination and the distribution of the amounts distributed to the
  investors; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Crown has the right to require an investor to
  repay any amount received by the investor under this Remission Order
  to the extent that the total compensation paid to satisfy the
  investor's claim exceeds the amount of that claim.
