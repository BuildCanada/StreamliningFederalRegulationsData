## Antarctic Environmental Protection Regulations {#antarctic-environmental-protection-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-363
:::

[ANTARCTIC ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT](/eng/acts/A-11.44)

Registration 2003-11-10

Antarctic Environmental Protection Regulations

P.C. 2003-1806 2003-11-10
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the annexed proposed Regulations entitled [Antarctic
Environmental Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-363) are
respecting matters that the Governor in Council considers necessary for
carrying out the purposes of the [Antarctic Environmental Protection
Act](/eng/acts/A-11.44)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBBBA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EBBBA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2003, c.
  20
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to
subsection 26(1) of the [Antarctic Environmental Protection
Act](/eng/acts/A-11.44)^a^, hereby makes the annexed [Antarctic
Environmental Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-363).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706588 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Antarctic Environmental
Protection Act](/eng/acts/A-11.44).

## [Application for Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706591 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Who may apply

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any person may apply for a
permit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application form

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An application for a permit
must be in the form set out in Schedule 1 and must contain the
information required by the form.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of application

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application for a permit
must be signed by the applicant and must include, in addition to the
information required by the application form,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a preliminary environmental evaluation that meets the
  requirements of section 14;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the procedures to be put into place
  by the applicant, including any monitoring, to assess and verify the
  environmental impact of the activities to which the permit is to
  relate;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Management Plan, if any, for any specially
  protected area to which the permit relates;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a waste management plan that meets the requirements
  of sections 34 and 35;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an emergency plan that meets the requirements of
  section 48; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of anything that the applicant intends
  to remove from the Antarctic.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment of application

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Within 30 days after the
Minister receives an application for a permit, the Minister must send
the applicant an acknowledgment that the application has been received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's decision

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must provide
to the applicant a written notice of the Minister's decision concerning
the application within 90 days after the application is received if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has provided the Minister with all of
  the information that is required by these Regulations or requested by
  the Minister under subsection 21(3) of the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister, after considering the preliminary
  environmental evaluation in relation to the activities to which the
  permit is to relate, is of the opinion that the activities will likely
  have less than a minor or transitory impact on the environment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further evaluation

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If the Minister is of the
opinion that the activities are likely to have at least a minor or
transitory impact on the environment, the Minister must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 90 days after receiving the application,
  inform the applicant in writing that an initial environmental
  evaluation or a comprehensive environmental evaluation, as the case
  may be, must be conducted by the applicant; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 90 days after the completion of all the
  environmental evaluations that are required under section 23 of the
  Act, provide to the applicant a written notice of the Minister's
  decision concerning the application.

## [Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706614 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When security to be provided

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister requires security to be provided by an applicant for a
  permit, the security must be provided to the Minister at the time the
  permit is issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of security

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of security that may be required is
  \$2,000,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time security to be maintained

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The time during which the security must be maintained
  may not be longer than two years after the expiry date of the permit.

## [Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706622 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of permit

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Each permit must set out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the permit holder;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the persons authorized to conduct
  activities in the Antarctic;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the names of the vessels authorized to conduct
  activities in the Antarctic;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the authorized activities, including
  where those activities are to be conducted;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of anything that the permit holder is
  allowed to remove from the Antarctic;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the date of issuance of the permit;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the date of expiry of the permit; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the conditions of the permit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may include
in a permit the condition that the permit is not valid if the
application for the permit contained any false or misleading information
or failed to include any relevant information that might reasonably have
affected the Minister's decision to issue the permit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permit not transferable

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A permit is not
transferable.

## [Obligations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706637 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  permit holder must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure that all persons to whom the permit applies
    receive training designed to limit the impact of their activities on
    the Antarctic environment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inform all persons to whom the permit applies of
    the requirements of the permit and these Regulations, including the
    requirements relating to the waste management plan and the emergency
    plan;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} within the time required in the permit, provide the
    Minister with a written summary report on the activities conducted
    within the Antarctic;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} unless subsection (2) applies, declare in the
    written summary report that all of the conditions of the permit have
    been fully adhered to;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide the Minister with an itinerary for the
    activities, including the route to be followed;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} notify the Minister in advance about any
    significant changes to the itinerary, including changes to the route
    to be followed; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} provide any additional information that is
    requested by the Minister, within 30 days after the day on which the
    request is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to comply with condition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the permit holder or any other person to whom the
  permit applies fails to comply with a condition of the permit, the
  permit holder must give the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notice of that failure without delay and in any
    event within 30 days after the day on which the failure to comply
    occurs; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written report, within 60 days after the day on
    which the failure to comply occurs, that identifies the condition
    and explains the failure to comply.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Possession and production of permits

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every permit holder and every other person to whom
  the permit applies must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} either be in possession of the permit, or a
    numbered copy of the permit, or have ready access to it at all times
    while in the Antarctic; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} produce the permit, or a numbered copy of the
    permit, on request to the Minister, an inspector designated by the
    Minister under subsection 45(1) of the Act or an observer designated
    under Article VII of the Treaty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permit on vessel

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A permit issued for a vessel must be kept on board
  the vessel.

## [Suspension or Cancellation of Permit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706658 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surrender of permit

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Any person having
possession or custody of a permit that has been suspended or cancelled
by the Minister must surrender the permit, and any numbered copies of
it, to the Minister by the date specified in the notice of suspension or
cancellation.

## [Environmental Impact Assessment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706661 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment of impacts of activities

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  preliminary environmental evaluation that must be included with an
  application for a permit must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicate whether, in the opinion of the applicant,
    the activities to which the permit is to relate will have more than
    a minor or transitory impact on the Antarctic environment; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contain enough details of the impacts of those
    activities on that environment to allow the Minister to assess
    whether the activities will likely have more than a minor or
    transitory impact on that environment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}What must be included

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The preliminary environmental evaluation must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of each proposed activity that
    includes the purpose, nature, location, duration and frequency of
    the activity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a consideration of alternatives to the proposed
    activities, including the alternative of not proceeding, and the
    consequences of those alternatives;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of any environmental impacts that the
    proposed activities may have, including a description of predicted
    cumulative impacts in the light of existing and planned activities;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the initial environmental
    reference state with which predicted changes are to be compared and
    a prediction of the future environmental reference state in the
    absence of the proposed activities;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the methods and data used to
    predict the environmental impacts of the proposed activities;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an estimation of the nature, extent, duration and
    intensity of the likely direct environmental impacts of the proposed
    activities;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a consideration of possible indirect or secondary
    environmental impacts of the proposed activities;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} an identification of measures, including monitoring
    programs, that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} could be taken to minimize or mitigate the
      environmental impacts of the proposed activities and to detect
      unforeseen impacts, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} could provide an early warning of any adverse
      effects of the proposed activities and could deal promptly and
      effectively with accidents;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an identification of the unavoidable environmental
    impacts of the proposed activities;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a consideration of the effects of the proposed
    activities on the conduct of scientific research and on other
    existing uses and values;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} an identification of gaps in knowledge and
    uncertainties encountered in compiling the information relating to
    the evaluation;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a non-technical summary of the information
    provided;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the person or organization
    that prepared the evaluation; and

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} any other information relevant to the proposed
    activities.

- SOR/2010-196, s. 1(F)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The historic sites and

## [Native and Non-native Species]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706690 .Part}

### [Native Species]{.HTitleText2} {#h-706691 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation on authority to issue permit

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may issue a
permit for the purpose of an activity to which section 12 of the Act
applies only if that purpose is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to provide specimens for scientific study or
  scientific information;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to provide specimens for museums, herbaria,
  zoological or botanical gardens or other educational or cultural
  institutions or uses; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to provide for unavoidable consequences of other
  scientific activities or the construction and operation of scientific
  support facilities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of taking

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may include
in a permit for the taking of native mammals or native birds the
condition that the taking of the mammals or birds must be done in the
manner that involves the least degree of pain and suffering practicable
being inflicted on them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to be considered

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** In considering whether to
issue a permit referred to in section 12 of the Act, the Minister may
take into account the permits issued by all other Parties to the
Protocol to ensure that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} only small numbers of native mammals or native birds
  are killed and the number of native mammals or native birds that are
  killed during a season from a local population, considered along with
  the number of native mammals or native birds from that local
  population that are taken or to be taken under other permits, is not
  so great that the total number of native mammals or native birds taken
  from the local population could not be replaced by normal natural
  reproduction during the following season; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the diversity of species, as well as the habitats
  essential to their existence, and the balance of the ecological
  systems existing within the Antarctic are maintained.

- SOR/2010-196, s. 2(E)

### [Specially Protected Species]{.HTitleText2} {#h-706705 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation on issuance of permit

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may issue a
permit for the purpose of section 12 of the Act for the taking of a
specially protected species set out in Appendix A of Annex II to the
Protocol only if the taking

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is for a compelling scientific purpose;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} will not jeopardize the survival or recovery of that
  species or the local population of that species; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is done using non-lethal techniques, if practicable.

### [Non-native Species]{.HTitleText2} {#h-706711 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No dogs to be introduced

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** A permit may not be
issued for the purpose of subsection 13(1) of the Act to allow any dog
to be introduced into the Antarctic.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Species that may be introduced

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Permits may be issued for
the purpose of subsection 13(1) of the Act to allow the introduction
into the Antarctic of animals or plants of a species listed in Appendix
B to Annex II to the Protocol.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of permits

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Any permit issued for the
purpose of subsection 13(1) of the Act must specify

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the species that may be introduced into the
  Antarctic;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of members of that species that may be
  introduced;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if appropriate, the age and sex of the members that
  may be introduced;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the precautions to be taken to prevent

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any escape of the members,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any contact of the members with native fauna and
    flora, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any interference with an ecosystem of the
    Antarctic; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the obligations set out in section 23.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal or disposal

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  the expiry of a permit, the permit holder must remove from the
  Antarctic or dispose of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any plant or animal in respect of which the permit
    is issued and any progeny of the plant or animal; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other plant or animal that the permit holder
    has introduced and that is not indigenous to the Antarctic,
    including the progeny of any such plant or animal, unless the plant
    or animal poses no risk to native flora or fauna or to an ecosystem
    of the Antarctic.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of disposal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The disposal must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a case to which paragraph (1)(a) applies, by
    incineration or an equally effective means that eliminates risk to
    native fauna and flora and to the ecosystems of the Antarctic; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a case to which paragraph (1)(b) applies, by
    incineration or an equally effective means that renders the plants
    or animals sterile.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Plant and animal parts and products

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A permit holder must keep
all parts and products of plants and animals that are introduced into
the Antarctic under carefully controlled conditions, and before the
expiry of the permit must dispose of them in a manner that eliminates
any risk to native fauna and flora and to the ecosystems of the
Antarctic.

## [Specified Substances and Products]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706737 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specification

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The substances and

## [Specially Protected Areas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706740 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of areas

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The Antarctic specially
protected areas that are designated for the purpose of section 15 of the
Act are the ones set out in Schedule 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Management Plan

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** In considering whether to
issue a permit for the purpose of section 15 of the Act in relation to a
specially protected area, the Minister must take into account the
Management Plan for the specially protected area.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of permits

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Any permit issued for the
purpose of section 15 of the Act in relation to a specially protected
area

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} must be accompanied by the sections of the Management
  Plan for the specially protected area that are relevant to the
  activity for which the permit was sought; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} must specify

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the extent and location of the specially protected
    area,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the activities that are authorized in relation to
    the specially protected area,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} when, where and by whom the activities may be
    conducted, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any other conditions that are imposed by the
    Management Plan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Area without Management Plan

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** In the case of a
specially protected area that does not have a Management Plan, any
activities authorized by a permit issued for the purpose of section 15
of the Act must be for a compelling scientific purpose that cannot be
served elsewhere and must not jeopardize the natural ecological system
in the area.

## [Waste Management]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706757 .Part}

### [General Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-706758 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** If wastes are mixed with
another substance and the substances in the mixture are subject to
different requirements under sections 31 to 33 and 37 to 46, the most
stringent of the requirements under those sections that apply to any of
the substances apply to the mixture.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-196, s. 4(F)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** Each permit holder who
must remove wastes from the Antarctic or otherwise dispose of them must
store them, until they are removed or disposed of, in such a way as to
prevent their dispersal into the environment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal of wastes

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The permit holder must
return wastes that are removed from the Antarctic to the country from
which the activities generating the waste were organized or to any other
country in which arrangements have been made for the disposal of the
wastes in accordance with relevant international agreements.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Field camp wastes

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** The permit holder must
take all reasonable steps to move the wastes generated at a field camp
to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the station or vessel from which the field camp is
  supported; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} another station or vessel if the permit holder has
  arranged for the wastes to be managed there in accordance with Annex
  III to the Protocol.

### [Waste Management Plan]{.HTitleText2} {#h-706771 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of plan

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** Each waste management
plan must apply to all wastes generated by the activities to which the
permit is to relate, including wastes generated by vessels, stations and
field camps, and must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} include measures for the reduction, recycling, safe
  storage, removal and disposal of wastes from the activities to which
  the permit is to relate, as well as an undertaking by the permit
  holder to respect the measures described in sections 37 to 44;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide that all reasonable steps must be taken to
  minimize the amount of waste generated or disposed of in the
  Antarctic;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} specify current and planned waste management
  measures, including measures for final disposal;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} specify current and planned measures for analysing
  the effects of waste and waste management on the Antarctic
  environment;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} specify other measures to minimize any negative
  effects of waste and waste management on the Antarctic environment;
  and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} specify the method used for recording wastes.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-196, s. 5
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-34-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 34"){.PITLink}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classification of wastes

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** In the waste management
plan, wastes produced must be classified as

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sewage and domestic liquid wastes (Group 1);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} other liquid wastes and chemicals, including fuel and
  lubricants (Group 2);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} solids to be combusted (Group 3);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} other solid wastes (Group 4); and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} radioactive material (Group 5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual review

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** Within 30 days after each
anniversary of the date of issuance of a permit, the permit holder must
review and update his or her waste management plan and provide a copy of
the updated plan to the Minister.

### [Removal of Wastes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-706791 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Wastes to be removed

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** Before the expiry of a
permit, the permit holder must remove from the Antarctic the following
wastes if they are generated by the permit holder:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} radioactive materials;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} used or spent electrical batteries;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} fuel, both solid and liquid;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} wastes containing heavy metals or toxic persistent
  organic compounds;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} polyvinyl chloride (PVC), polyurethane foam,
  polystyrene foam, rubber, electronic scrap, lubricating oils, treated
  wood or wood products and other products that contain additives that
  could produce harmful emissions if incinerated;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} all other plastic wastes, except low density
  polyethylene containers (such as bags for storing wastes) if those
  containers are incinerated in accordance with section 40;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} fuel drums, except if their removal would result in a
  greater adverse environmental impact than leaving them in their
  existing locations;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} other solid, non-combustible wastes, except if their
  removal would result in a greater adverse environmental impact than
  leaving them in their existing locations; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the solid residue of combustible wastes burned in an
  incinerator referred to in section 40.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liquid wastes

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** Before the expiry of a
permit, the permit holder must also remove from the Antarctic all liquid
wastes to which section 37 does not apply, including sewage and domestic
liquid wastes.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other wastes

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** Before the expiry of a
permit, the permit holder must also remove the following wastes from the
Antarctic, unless they are incinerated, autoclaved or otherwise treated
to be made sterile:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} residues of carcasses of imported animals;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} laboratory cultures of micro-organisms and plant
  pathogens; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} introduced avian products.

### [Incineration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-706810 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Combustible wastes

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** Before the expiry of a
permit, the permit holder must burn all combustible wastes, other than
those that must be removed from the Antarctic, in incinerators that meet
any emission standards and equipment guidelines that may be recommended
by the Committee for Environmental Protection established in accordance
with Article 11 of the Protocol and the Scientific Committee on
Antarctic Research.

### [Other Waste Disposal on Land]{.HTitleText2} {#h-706815 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited disposal

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a permit
  holder must not dispose of any liquid wastes to which section 38
  applies onto sea ice, iceshelves or the grounded ice sheet.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal in ice pits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The permit holder may dispose of wastes referred to
  in subsection (1) in deep ice pits if the wastes are generated by
  stations located inland on iceshelves or on the grounded ice sheet and
  that disposal is the only practicable option.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Location of pits

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The deep ice pits must not be located on known
  ice-flow lines that terminate at ice-free areas or in areas of high
  ablation.

### [Disposal of Waste into the Sea]{.HTitleText2} {#h-706823 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal from vessel

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a permit
  holder may not dispose of wastes into the sea from a vessel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the disposal of wastes in cases permitted under
    Annex V to MARPOL 73/78, the International Convention for the
    Prevention of Pollution from Ships, 1973, as amended by the Protocol
    of 1978 relating to it and by any more recent amendment that is in
    force; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the disposal of wastes under a permit issued under
    Division 3 of Part 7 of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
    1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sewage and domestic liquid waste

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** Sewage or domestic liquid
waste, other than sewage or domestic liquid waste from a vessel, may be
disposed of directly into the sea if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sewage or waste is generated at a station with an
  average weekly occupancy over the austral summer of at least 30
  individuals;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sewage or waste is macerated before it is
  disposed of; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} all reasonable steps are taken to discharge the
  sewage or waste into the sea at a place where conditions exist for
  dilution and dispersal of the sewage or waste.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Treated sewage

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** The by-product of sewage,
other than sewage from a vessel, treated by the Rotary Biological
Contactor process or a similar process may be disposed of into the sea
if the disposal

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} does not adversely affect the local environment; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is in accordance with Annex IV to the Protocol.

### [Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-706840 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of locations

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** Within 30 days after each
anniversary of the date of issuance of a permit for a term of more than
one year (including the term of any renewal of the permit), and within
30 days after the day on which a permit expires, the permit holder must
provide the Minister with a list of all locations where wastes have been
incinerated or otherwise disposed of in the Antarctic.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** A permit holder must
record each disposal of wastes, including sewage discharges from
vessels, and retain the record for five years.

## [Emergencies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706845 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reporting

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** The permit holder must,
without delay, advise the Minister about all measures undertaken in
response to an emergency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergency plan

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** Each emergency plan must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} identify the potential emergencies, including the
  potential impact on human health and the environment, that could
  result from the activities to which the permit is to relate;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} include an assessment of the relative risk of
  occurrence of the potential emergencies;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} set out the names and telephone numbers of personnel
  and describe their roles and responsibilities during an environmental
  emergency;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} identify the training required for emergency response
  personnel;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} list the equipment that will be available to deal
  with potential emergencies and indicate where it is located;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} identify the procedures by which emergency resources,
  including personnel, equipment, facilities and financial resources,
  can be obtained; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} describe the procedures for implementing, reviewing
  and updating the plan.
