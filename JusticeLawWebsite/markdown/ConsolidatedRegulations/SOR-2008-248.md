## Special Economic Measures (Zimbabwe) Regulations {#special-economic-measures-zimbabwe-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2008-248
:::

[SPECIAL ECONOMIC MEASURES ACT](/eng/acts/S-14.5)

Registration 2008-09-04

Special Economic Measures (Zimbabwe) Regulations

P.C. 2008-1588 2008-09-04
::::

::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that the situation in
Zimbabwe constitutes a grave breach of international peace and security
that has resulted or is likely to result in a serious international
crisis;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to
subsections 4(1) to (3) of the [Special Economic Measures
Act](/eng/acts/S-14.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612089-E_hq_5077){#fn_612089-E_hq_5077-ID0EDBAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Special Economic Measures (Zimbabwe)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2008-248).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746348 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[arms and related material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[arms and related material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any type of
    weapons, ammunition, military vehicle or military or paramilitary
    equipment and their spare parts. ([armes et matériel
    connexe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person whose name
    is listed in the schedule in accordance with section 2. ([personne
    désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Foreign
    Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[technical assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[technical assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any form of
    assistance and includes instruction, training, consulting services,
    technical advice and the transferring or communicating of know-how
    or technical data. ([aide technique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Zimbabwe]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Zimbabwe]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any political subdivision of Zimbabwe;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the government, and any department, of Zimbabwe
      or of a political subdivision of Zimbabwe; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any agency of Zimbabwe or of a political
      subdivision of Zimbabwe. ([Zimbabwe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [List]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746359 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated person

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A person whose name is
listed in the schedule is a person in respect of whom the Governor in
Council, on the recommendation of the Minister, is satisfied that there
are reasonable grounds to believe is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a former or current senior official of the Government
  of Zimbabwe, the Zimbabwe African National Union --- Patriotic Front
  or a successor to either of the foregoing;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an associate or family member of a person set out in
  paragraph (*a*);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an entity owned or controlled by, or acting on behalf
  of a person set out in paragraph (a) or (b);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an entity owned or controlled by, or acting on behalf
  of the Government of Zimbabwe, the Zimbabwe African Nation Union ---
  Patriotic Front or a successor to either of the foregoing;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a person engaged in activities that seriously
  undermine democracy, respect for human rights and the rule of law in
  Zimbabwe; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a senior official of an entity referred to paragraph
  (e).

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746368 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Export

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 13, no
person in Canada and no Canadian outside Canada shall export, sell,
supply or ship arms and related material, wherever situated, to Zimbabwe
or to any person in Zimbabwe.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Carriage of arms and related material

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 13, no
owner or master of a [[Canadian vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, within
the meaning of section 2 of the [Canada Shipping Act,
2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15), and no operator of an aircraft registered in
Canada shall carry, cause to be carried or permit to be carried, arms
and related materials, wherever situated, destined for Zimbabwe or any
person in Zimbabwe.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Services

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 13, no
person in Canada and no Canadian outside Canada shall provide any person
in Zimbabwe with technical or financial assistance, technical or
financial services or brokerage or other services related to the supply,
sale, transfer, manufacture or use of arms and related materials.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assets freeze

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No person in Canada and no
Canadian outside Canada shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deal, directly or indirectly, in any property of a
  designated person, including funds derived or generated from property
  that is owned or controlled, directly or indirectly, by that person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into or facilitate, directly or indirectly, any
  financial transaction related to a dealing referred to in paragraph
  (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial or other related service in
  respect of the property referred to in paragraph (a); or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} make any property or any financial or other related
  service available, directly or indirectly, to or for the benefit of a
  designated person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Landing in Canada

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No person shall land in
Canada, or fly over Canada, an aircraft that is registered in Zimbabwe,
except if landing in Canada is necessary to safeguard human life.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assisting in a prohibited activity

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada to knowingly do anything
that causes, facilitates or assists in, or is intended to cause,
facilitate or assist in, any activity prohibited by sections 3 to 7.

## [Duty to Determine]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746385 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Each of the following
entities must determine on a continuing basis whether it is in
possession or control of property owned or controlled by or on behalf of
a designated person:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[authorized foreign banks]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
  as defined in section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), in
  respect of their business in Canada or banks to which that Act
  applies;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cooperative credit societies, savings and credit
  unions and caisses populaires regulated by a provincial Act and
  associations regulated by the [Cooperative Credit Associations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[foreign companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
  defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), in respect of their insurance business in
  Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[provincial
  companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
  [[societies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms are defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the Insurance Companies Act;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fraternal benefit societies regulated by a provincial
  Act in respect of their insurance activities and insurance companies
  and other entities engaged in the business of insuring risks that are
  regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} companies to which the [Trust and Loan Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) applies;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} trust companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} loan companies regulated by a provincial Act; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} entities authorized under provincial legislation to
  engage in the business of dealing in securities or to provide
  portfolio management or investment counselling services.

## [Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746397 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person in Canada and every Canadian outside Canada shall disclose
  without delay to the Commissioner of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the existence of property in their possession or
    control that they have reason to believe is owned or controlled,
    directly or indirectly, by a designated person or by an entity owned
    or controlled by a designated person; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information about a transaction or proposed
    transaction in respect of property referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No proceedings under the [Special Economic Measures
  Act](/eng/acts/S-14.5) and no civil proceedings lie against a person
  for a disclosure made in good faith under subsection (1).

## [Application to No Longer Be a Designated Person]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746405 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  designated person may apply in writing to the Minister to request to
  have their name removed from the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a written application, the Minister
  shall decide whether there are reasonable grounds to recommend to the
  Governor in Council that the applicant's name be removed from the
  schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister does not make a decision on the
  application within 60 days after the day on which the application is
  received, the Minister is deemed to have decided to recommend that the
  applicant remain a designated person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall give notice without delay to the
  applicant of any decision made or deemed to have been made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If there has been a material change in circumstances
  since the last application was submitted, a person may make another
  application under subsection (1).

## [Application for a Certificate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746417 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mistaken identity

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person claiming not to be a designated person may apply to the
  Minister in writing for a certificate stating that they are not the
  person who has been designated under section 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate --- ​time frame

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If it is established that the person is not a
  designated person, the Minister shall issue a certificate to the
  applicant within 15 days after the day on which the application is
  received.

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746423 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections 3 to 5 do not apply with
  respect to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} supplies of non-lethal military equipment intended
    solely for humanitarian or protective use, and related technical
    assistance and training;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} supplies of protective clothing and equipment,
    including flak jackets and military helmets, for the personal use of
    United Nations or African Union personnel, representatives of the
    media and humanitarian and development workers and associated
    personnel;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} firearms and related ammunition and accessories in
    respect of which a temporary export permit has been granted under
    the Export and Import Permit Act prior to the coming into force of
    this paragraph; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Canadian Forces who is in or
    travels to Zimbabwe in the performance of official duties, including
    providing security to Canadian embassy staff, providing humanitarian
    assistance, or engaging in other activities authorized by the Chief
    of the Defence Staff.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial support

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Section 6 does not apply with respect to financial or
  other support provided by the Government of Canada in connection with
  internationally or regionally mandated or sanctioned negotiations for
  a political settlement in Zimbabwe.
