## General Import Permit No. 2 --- Chickens and Chicken Products for Personal Use {#general-import-permit-no.-2-chickens-and-chicken-products-for-personal-use .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-39
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

Registration 1994-12-30

General Import Permit No. 2 --- Chickens and Chicken Products for
Personal Use
::::

::::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
Whereas chickens and chicken products have been included on the [Import
Control list](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._604);

And Whereas the Secretary of State for External Affairs has determined
an import access quantity for chickens and chicken products pursuant to
subsection 6.2(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBDA} of the
[Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19);

  [[Return to footnote

  1994, c. 47, s. 106

Therefore, the Secretary of State for External Affairs, pursuant to
section 10[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0EQECA} of the
[Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), hereby cancels the
[Import of Chickens Permit](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-72)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*^](#footnote3_e){#footnote3_e-ID0ELECA}, issued
on January 5, 1979[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*\*^](#footnote4_e){#footnote4_e-ID0EJECA}, and,
pursuant to subsection 8.3(3)[[Footnote

the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), hereby issues the
annexed General permit authorizing the importation of chickens and
chicken products for personal use, effective on the latest of the days
on which sections 74, 106, 109 and 111 of [An Act to implement the
Agreement Establishing the World Trade Organization](/eng/acts/W-11.8),
being chapter 47 of the Statutes of Canada, 1994, come into force.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975348 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Permit, [[chickens
and chicken products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the goods set out in
the schedule. (*volaille et produits de volaille*)

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975350 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Any resident of Canada may,
under the authority of this Permit, import into Canada for the personal
use of the importer and the importer's household, chickens and chicken
products in a quantity not exceeding 10 kg in net weight for each
importation.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where any goods imported
under the authority of this Permit are required to be reported in the
prescribed form under the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), that form
shall contain the statement "Imported under the authority of [General
Import Permit No. 2 --- Chickens and Chicken Products for Personal
Use](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-39)" or "[Importé en vertu de la [Licence
générale d'importation n^o^ 2 --- Volaille et produits de volaille pour
usage personnel](/eng/regulations/DORS-95-39)]{.otherLang lang="fr"}".

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-975354 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** Live fowls of the species [Gallus domesticus]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} (other than those for breeding purposes and other than
  started pullets and spent fowls), weighing more than 185 g, that are
  classified under tariff item No. 0105.94.91, in the List of Tariff
  Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- **2** Meat and edible offal of fowls of the species [Gallus
  Domesticus]{.otherLang lang="la"}, fresh, chilled or frozen, not cut
  in pieces (other than spent fowls), that are classified under tariff
  item No. 0207.11.91 or 0207.12.91 in the List of Tariff Provisions set
  out in the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- **3** Cuts of meat and edible offal, including livers, of fowls of the
  species [Gallus Domesticus]{.otherLang lang="la"}, fresh, chilled or
  frozen (other than spent fowls), that are classified under tariff item
  No. 0207.13.91, 0207.14.21 or 0207.14.91 in the List of Tariff
  Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- **4** Fat of fowls (not rendered or otherwise extracted) of the
  species [Gallus Domesticus]{.otherLang lang="la"}, fresh, chilled or
  frozen, salted, in brine, dried or smoked, that is classified under
  tariff item No. 0209.90.10 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in
  the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- **5** Meat of fowls of the species [Gallus domesticus]{.otherLang
  lang="la"}, salted, in brine, dried or smoked, that is classified
  under tariff item No. 0210.99.11 in the List of Tariff Provisions set
  out in the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- **6** Sausages and similar products, made from meat, meat offal or
  blood of fowls of the species [Gallus Domesticus]{.otherLang
  lang="la"}, and food preparations based on those products (other than
  in cans or glass jars or made from spent fowls), that are classified
  under tariff item No. 1601.00.21 in the List of Tariff Provisions set
  out in the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- **7** Prepared or preserved liver paste, made from fowls of the
  species [Gallus Domesticus]{.otherLang lang="la"} (other than in cans
  or glass jars), that is classified under tariff item No. 1602.20.21 in
  the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- **8** Prepared meals, made from fowls of the species [Gallus
  Domesticus]{.otherLang lang="la"} (other than specially defined
  mixtures and meals made from spent fowls), that are classified under
  tariff item No. 1602.32.12 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in
  the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- **9** Prepared or preserved meat or meat offal of fowls of the species
  [Gallus Domesticus]{.otherLang lang="la"} (other than prepared meals,
  fowls of the species [Gallus Domesticus]{.otherLang lang="la"} in cans
  or glass jars, specially defined mixtures and products made from spent
  fowls) that is classified under tariff item No. 1602.32.93 in the List
  of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- **10** Goods that are classified under heading No. 98.04 or 98.26 in
  the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and that are otherwise classifiable under
  a tariff item referred to in any of items 1 to 9 of this schedule.
