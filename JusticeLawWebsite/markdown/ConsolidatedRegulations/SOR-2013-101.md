## Administrative Monetary Penalties (Consumer Products) Regulations {#administrative-monetary-penalties-consumer-products-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2013-101
:::

[CANADA CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY ACT](/eng/acts/C-1.68)

Registration 2013-05-24

Administrative Monetary Penalties (Consumer Products) Regulations

P.C. 2013-568 2013-05-23
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Health, pursuant to paragraphs 37(1)(i) and (p) and
subsection 50(1) of the [Canada Consumer Product Safety
Act](/eng/acts/C-1.68)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna){#fna-ID0EDBAA}, makes the annexed
[Administrative Monetary Penalties (Consumer Products)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2013-101).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna-ID0EDBAA){#fna}S.C.
  2010, c. 21
  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-792914 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Consumer Product
    Safety Act](/eng/acts/C-1.68). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[compliance agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[compliance agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement
    referred to in paragraph 53(2)(a) of the
    Act. ([transaction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[violation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[violation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a violation committed under
    section 49 of the Act. ([violation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Classification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-792920 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violation --- minor, serious, and very
serious

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A violation is classified
as

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} minor, if the total gravity factor for the violation
  is two;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} serious, if the total gravity factor for the
  violation is three or four; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} very serious, if the total gravity factor for the
  violation is five.

## [Payment Referred to in Subsections 52(1) and 53(1) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-792956 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reduced penalty

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 52(1)(e) of the Act, the lesser amount that may
  be paid as complete satisfaction of the penalty is one half of the
  amount of the penalty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time and manner of payment --- reduced
  penalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person that is provided with a notice of violation
  and wishes to pay the lesser amount must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deliver the amount in person no later than 15 days
    after the day on which the notice is provided;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} send the amount by regular mail that is postmarked
    no later than 15 days after the day on which the notice is provided;
    or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} send the amount, by a mail or courier service that
    provides the recipient with a document specifying the date of
    sending, no later than 15 days after the day on which the notice is
    provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time and manner of payment --- full
  penalty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person that is provided with a notice of violation
  and wishes to pay the amount of the penalty set out in the notice must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deliver the amount in person no later than 30 days
    after the day on which the notice is provided;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} send the amount by regular mail that is postmarked
    no later than 30 days after the day on which the notice is provided;
    or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} send the amount, by a mail or courier service that
    provides the recipient with a document specifying the date of
    sending, no later than 30 days after the day on which the notice is
    provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of payment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Payments must be made by means of a certified cheque
  or bank draft made payable to the Receiver General for Canada and must
  be delivered or sent to the address set out in the notice of
  violation.

## [Compliance Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-792972 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 53(2)(a) of the Act, a request to enter into a
  compliance agreement must be submitted in writing to the address set
  out in the notice of violation by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} delivering it in person no later than 15 days after
    the day on which the notice is provided;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sending it by regular mail that is postmarked no
    later than 15 days after the day on which the notice is provided; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sending it, by a mail or courier service that
    provides the recipient with a document specifying the date of
    sending, no later than 15 days after the day on which the notice is
    provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment under paragraph 54(4)(a) of Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph 54(4)(a) of the Act, if
  the person is liable to pay twice the amount of the penalty, that
  amount must be paid in a manner set out in subsection 5(2) except that
  the relevant time is 15 days after the day on which the notice of
  default is provided. The amount must also be paid in accordance with
  subsection 5(4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment under subsection 55(1) of Act

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 55(1) of the Act, the amount of the penalty must be paid in a
manner set out in subsection 5(2) except that the relevant time is 15
days after the day on which written notice of the Minister's refusal is
provided. The amount must also be paid in accordance with
subsection 5(4).

## [Review by Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-792983 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time and manner of request

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 53(2)(b) of the Act, a request for a review by
  the Minister must be submitted in writing to the address set out in
  the notice of violation by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} delivering it in person no later than 30 days after
    the day on which the notice is provided;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sending it by regular mail that is postmarked no
    later than 30 days after the day on which the notice is provided; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sending it, by a mail or courier service that
    provides the recipient with a document specifying the date of
    sending, no later than 30 days after the day on which the notice is
    provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time and manner of payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 56(3) of the Act, the
  amount of the penalty that is confirmed or corrected in the Minister's
  decision must be paid in a manner set out in subsection 5(2) except
  that the relevant time is 30 days after the day on which the notice of
  the decision is provided. The amount must also be paid in accordance
  with subsection 5(4).

## [Providing Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-792992 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Individuals

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  document referred to in these Regulations, except an order made under
  section 31 or 32 of the Act, that is to be provided to an individual,
  other than the Minister, who is named in the document must be provided
  by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} delivering it to the individual at any place; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if delivery is attempted under paragraph (a) and
    for any reason is not successful, sending it, by a mail or courier
    service that provides the sender and the recipient with a document
    specifying the date of delivery, to the individual's last known
    address or usual place of residence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons other than individuals

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any document referred to in these Regulations, except
  an order made under section 31 or 32 of the Act, that is to be
  provided to a person, other than an individual, that is named in the
  document must be provided by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sending it, by a mail or courier service that
    provides the sender and the recipient with a document specifying the
    date of delivery, to the person at the head office or place of
    business of the person or of the person's agent or mandatary; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} delivering it, at the head office or place of
    business of the person or of the person's agent or mandatary, to an
    officer or any other individual who appears to be in control of or
    managing the head office or place of business.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of compliance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The requirements in this section also apply to
  notices provided under subsection 54(3) of the Act.
