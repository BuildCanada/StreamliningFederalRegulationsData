## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673863 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[information processing activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[information processing activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in
    relation to a bank, an activity referred to in any of
    subparagraphs 410(1)(c)(i) to (iv) of the Act, and in relation to an
    authorized foreign bank, an activity referred to in any of
    subparagraphs 539(1)(b.1)(i) to (iv) of the Act. ([activité de
    traitement de l'information]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[specified circumstances]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[specified circumstances]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means circumstances
    where the information processing activity

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is related to generating, recording or reporting
      on postings to accounts;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} was first carried on by the bank or authorized
      foreign bank for its own internal purposes and is an integral part
      of its banking operations; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is related to payments from and deposits to
      accounts and was carried on by the bank before December 1,
      1980. ([circonstances déterminées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Exemption from the Requirement to Obtain the Minister's Approval]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673872 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Circumstances where Minister's approval
not required

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A bank or authorized
foreign bank is exempted from the requirement to obtain the prior
written approval of the Minister

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in specified circumstances for carrying on the
  following information processing activities, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the classification and reporting of payments from
    and deposits to an account,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} payment item account reconciliation, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the institution and processing of pre-authorized
    debits and credits;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the information processing activity is carried
  on in respect of a financial institution or any other entity that is
  primarily engaged in the business of providing financial services, for
  any information processing activity that was first carried on by the
  bank or authorized foreign bank for its own internal purposes and is
  an integral part of its banking operations, including any information
  processing activity related to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} clearing and settlement,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} deposit and loan accounting,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} mortgage or hypotheque accounting,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} securities accounting,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} general ledger accounting,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} credit or payment cards, or

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} foreign exchange trading accounting;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the information processing activity is carried
  on in respect of an employer, for the preparation of the employer's
  payroll by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the calculation of gross salary or wages of the
    employees on the basis of the number of hours worked, items
    produced, data on rates of pay or commissions and other relevant
    information,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the calculation of net salary or wages of the
    employees and the preparation of cheques and cheque stubs or other
    evidence of payment, pay slips or similar statements that present
    the details of the gross to net salary or wage calculation,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the preparation of summary reports for the
    employer to permit an adequate audit and control of the payroll
    operation,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the preparation of an individual employee's
    earnings records,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the production of forms or statements in respect of
    the earnings of an individual employee or group of employees and the
    amounts withheld from those earnings, if those forms or statements
    are required under any federal or provincial law, and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the provision of summary reports and the
    compilation of lists of disbursements relating to amounts withheld
    from the gross earnings of employees;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the information processing activity is carried
  on in respect of an employer, for the compilation of any report or
  list from any of the data obtained by the bank or authorized foreign
  bank in order to carry on an information processing activity described
  in paragraph (c);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} where the information processing activity is carried
  on in respect of a farmer, for general ledger accounting;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} where the information processing activity is carried
  on in respect of a subsidiary of the bank or authorized foreign bank,
  for any information processing activity that is directly related to
  the business activity of the subsidiary and that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is general ledger accounting or an accounts
    receivable service,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is related to generating, recording or reporting
    on postings to accounts,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} was first carried on by the bank or authorized
    foreign bank for its own internal purposes and is an integral part
    of its banking operations, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} is related to payments from and deposits to
    accounts and was carried on by the bank before December 1, 1980; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} where the information processing activity is carried
  on in respect of an entity that does not carry on information
  processing activities for other persons and of which the bank or the
  authorized foreign bank holds more than 10% of the voting shares, for
  any information processing activity that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is related to generating, recording or reporting on
    postings to accounts,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} was first carried on by the bank or authorized
    foreign bank for its own internal purposes and is an integral part
    of its banking operations, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is related to payments from and deposits to
    accounts and was carried on by the bank before December 1, 1980.

## [Requirement to Provide Information to a Client]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673905 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information about client's business

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** If a bank or authorized
foreign bank directly or indirectly carries on in Canada an information
processing activity with respect to which it is exempted under
section 2, it must, at the request of a client and on the payment of a
reasonable fee, make available to the client within a reasonable time
after the request, in machine-readable or printed form, at the option of
the client, any information concerning the business of the client that
is acquired in the course of carrying on the information processing.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673908 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, come into force.
