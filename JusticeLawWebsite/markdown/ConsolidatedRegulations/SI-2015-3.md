## Order Designating the Minister of Transport as the appropriate Minister for The Federal Bridge Corporation Limited for purposes of the Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-transport-as-the-appropriate-minister-for-the-federal-bridge-corporation-limited-for-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2015-3
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2015-02-11

Order Designating the Minister of Transport as the appropriate Minister
for The Federal Bridge Corporation Limited for purposes of the Act

P.C. 2015-32 2015-01-26
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (d) of the definition
[[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-968-E_hq_15110){#fn_81000-3-968-E_hq_15110-ID0EDEAA}
and subparagraph (a)(ii) of the definition [[appropriate
Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 83(1) of that Act,

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-968-E_hq_15110-ID0EDEAA){#fn_81000-3-968-E_hq_15110}R.S., c.
  F-11
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2000-409 of March 23,
  2000[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-968-E_hq_15111){#fn_81000-3-968-E_hq_15111-ID0EBBCAA},
  that repeal taking effect on the day of the amalgamation referred to
  in paragraph (b);

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-968-E_hq_15111-ID0EBBCAA){#fn_81000-3-968-E_hq_15111}SI/2000-20
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of Transport as the
  appropriate Minister for The Federal Bridge Corporation Limited, an
  amalgamated corporation resulting from the amalgamation of The Federal
  Bridge Corporation Limited with the St. Mary's River Bridge Company,
  that designation taking effect on the day of that amalgamation and
  ceasing to have effect on the day before the day on which the
  designation in paragraph (c) takes effect; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of Transport as the
  appropriate Minister for The Federal Bridge Corporation Limited, an
  amalgamated corporation resulting from the amalgamation of The Federal
  Bridge Corporation Limited --- an amalgamated corporation resulting
  from the amalgamation referred to in paragraph (b) --- with the Blue
  Water Bridge Authority, that designation taking effect on the day of
  that amalgamation with the Blue Water Bridge Authority.
