## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765871 .Part}

### [Definition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-765872 .Subheading}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cooperative Credit Associations
Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01).

### [Non-application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-765874 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
section 487.02 of the Act, the prescribed notices, documents or other
information is that referred to in sections 89 to 142 of the Act.

## [Electronic Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765876 .Part}

### [Documents Provided by the Originator]{.HTitleText2} {#h-765877 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Notices, documents or other
information provided by the originator or the person acting for the
originator under these Regulations must be made in a language and
presented in a manner that is clear, simple and not misleading.

### [Electronic Documents Through Website]{.HTitleText2} {#h-765879 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraph 487.04(1)(b) of the Act, an electronic document
  need not be provided to the designated information system if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the electronic document is posted on or made
    available through a generally accessible electronic source, such as
    a website; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the addressee is provided in writing with a notice,
    in paper or electronic form, of the availability and location of the
    electronic document.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a notice, document
  or other information referred to in section 8.

### [Consent to Provision of an Electronic Document]{.HTitleText2} {#h-765885 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraph 487.04(1)(c) of the Act, the addressee may
  consent in writing, in paper or electronic form, or orally.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before an addressee consents in writing, the
  originator or the person acting for the originator must notify the
  addressee in writing, in paper or electronic form,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the addressee may revoke their consent at any
    time;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the addressee is responsible for informing the
    originator of any changes the addressee makes to the designated
    information system including any changes made to the contact
    information related to the designated information system;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that any electronic documents will be retained for
    the period specified in the notice and made available to the
    addressee and that the addressee is responsible for retaining a copy
    of the document; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} of when the consent takes effect.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the notification referred to in subsection (2) or
  the consent referred to in subsection (4) is provided in an electronic
  document, the electronic document must be accessible to the addressee
  and capable of being retained by them so as to be usable for
  subsequent reference.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the addressee's consent is provided in writing, in
  paper or electronic form, it must include the name of the information
  system designated by the addressee for the receipt of the electronic
  document and a list, in paper or electronic form, of the notices,
  documents or other information that is covered by the consent.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the addressee's consent is provided orally, the
  originator or the person acting for the originator must, without
  delay, provide the addressee in writing, in paper or electronic form,
  with the information referred to in subsection (2) and confirm the
  information referred to in subsection (4).

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the addressee is providing consent for the
  provision of an electronic document on a one-time basis,
  subsection (2) does not apply.

### [Revocation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-765897 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  addressee may revoke their consent in writing, in paper or electronic
  form, or orally.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the addressee revokes their consent, the
  originator or the person acting for the originator must, without
  delay, provide the addressee with confirmation, in writing, in paper
  or electronic form, of the revocation specifying when revocation of
  consent takes effect.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the originator or the person acting for the
  originator provides confirmation in an electronic document, the
  electronic document must be accessible to the addressee and capable of
  being retained by them so as to be usable for subsequent reference.

## [Provision and Receipt]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765902 .Part}

### [Several Addressees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-765903 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraphs 487.05(b) and 487.07(b) of the Act, if several addressees are
to be provided with a notice, document or other information, they are to
be provided with it concurrently.

### [Specific Address]{.HTitleText2} {#h-765905 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** If a notice, document or
other information is required under the Act to be sent to a specific
place, an electronic document may be sent instead to an information
system designated by the addressee for the receipt of the electronic
document.

### [Document Considered Provided]{.HTitleText2} {#h-765907 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An electronic document is
considered to have been provided to an addressee

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} when it leaves an information system within the
  control of the originator or the person acting for the originator; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} when it is posted on or made available through the
  secure website of the originator.

### [Believed Not To Be Received]{.HTitleText2} {#h-765911 .Subheading}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  case of a document provided under section 9, the originator or the
  person acting for the originator must mail a paper copy of the
  electronic document to the addressee's recorded address if they have
  reason to believe that the addressee may not have received it.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not affect when the electronic
  document is considered to have been provided under section 9.

### [Document Considered Received]{.HTitleText2} {#h-765915 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An electronic document is
considered to have been received

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} when it enters the information system designated by
  the addressee;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} when it is posted on or made available through the
  secure website of the originator; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} when the notice referred to in paragraph 4(1)(b) is
  received by the addressee.

## [Electronic Signature]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765920 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A signature referred to
in section 487.11 of the Act may be an electronic signature and must
consist of one or more letters, characters, numbers or other symbols in
digital form incorporated in, attached to or associated with an
electronic document.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765922 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on June 1, 2011.
