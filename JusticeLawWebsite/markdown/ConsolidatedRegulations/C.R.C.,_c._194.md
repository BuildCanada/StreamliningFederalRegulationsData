## Ontario Fresh Grape Order {#ontario-fresh-grape-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 194
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Fresh Grapes Produced in Ontario
::::

::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536247 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Fresh Grape Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._194).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536249 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing
    Board of Ontario, established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Régie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Fresh
    Grape Growers\' Marketing Board, established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fresh grapes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fresh grapes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means grapes produced in
    Ontario other than grapes that are used by a processor for
    processing; ([raisin frais]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    fresh grapes, established and amended from time to time pursuant to
    the Act, and any regulations made pursuant to the Act to give effect
    to the Plan; ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the manufacture of grape
    products or juice, beverage, spirits or wine from grapes and
    includes bottling, distilling or fermenting with sugar or sulphur
    dioxide or any other chemical. ([transformation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536257 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Board and the Commodity
Board are each authorized to regulate the marketing of fresh grapes in
interprovincial and export trade and for such purposes may, with respect
to persons and property situated within the Province of Ontario,
exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable by each of them
in relation to the marketing of fresh grapes locally within that
Province under the Act and the Plan.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-326, s. 1
:::

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536261 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/80-90\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the powers granted to it by section 3
  with respect to the marketing of fresh grapes in interprovincial and
  export trade,

to make orders fixing, imposing and collecting levies or charges from
persons situated in the Province of Ontario who are engaged in the
production or marketing of fresh grapes, and for such purpose to
classify such persons into groups and fix the levies or charges payable
by the members of the different groups in different amounts, and the
Commodity Board may use such levies or charges for its purposes,
including the creation of reserves, and the payment of expenses and
losses resulting from the sale or disposal of fresh grapes and the
equalization or adjustment among producers of fresh grapes of moneys
realized from the sale thereof during such period or periods of time as
it may determine.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/80-90
:::
