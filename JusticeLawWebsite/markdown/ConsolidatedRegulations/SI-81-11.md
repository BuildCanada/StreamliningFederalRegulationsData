## Canada Federal Court Reports Distribution Order {#canada-federal-court-reports-distribution-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/81-11
:::

[FEDERAL COURTS ACT](/eng/acts/F-7)

Registration 1981-01-28

Order Providing for Distribution without Charge of the Canada Federal
Court Reports to Certain Persons, Institutions and Departments of the
Government of Canada

P.C. 1981-57 1981-01-08
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 58(3) of the Federal
Court Act, is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order providing for
distribution without charge of the Canada Federal Court Reports to
certain persons, institutions and departments of the Government of
Canada](/eng/regulations/SI-81-11).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-636057 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Canada Federal Court Reports Distribution
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-81-11).

## [Distribution]{.HTitleText1} {#h-636059 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Canada Federal Court
Reports may be distributed without charge to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} judges of the Supreme Court of Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} judges, deputy judges, supernumerary judges and
  retired judges of the Federal Court of Canada;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} judges of the Tax Court of Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} judges who are members of the Canadian Judicial
  Council;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the library of the Supreme Court of Canada;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the library of the Federal Court of Canada;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the library of the Department of Justice of the
  Government of Canada;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the Translation Bureau of the Department of the
  Secretary of State of Canada; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the office of the Commissioner for Federal Judicial
  Affairs of Canada.
