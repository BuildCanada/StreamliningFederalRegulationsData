## Civilian Dental Treatment Regulations {#civilian-dental-treatment-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 682
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

[NATIONAL DEFENCE ACT](/eng/acts/N-5)

Regulations Respecting Dental Treatment for Civilians by the Canadian
Forces Dental Services
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560437 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Civilian Dental Treatment
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._682).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560439 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[isolated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area in which a unit
designated as an isolated unit by the Minister of National Defence is
located and includes such surrounding area as may be determined by the
commanding officer of that unit.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560441 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Except where the dental
treatment of members of the Canadian Forces would be interfered with,
dental officers of the Canadian Forces Dental Services may provide
dental treatment to civilians

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in an emergency, for the relief of pain or for the
  repair of broken dentures, where civilian dental facilities are not
  available;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in isolated areas where no civilian dental facilities
  exist; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} at the request of a provincial dental licensing body
  where that body considers it necessary to supplement civilian
  services.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of National
Defence may prescribe the procedure to be followed and the form to be
used in arranging and making payment for dental treatment provided
pursuant to these Regulations.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 6, fees
payable by patients for dental treatment provided pursuant to these
Regulations after June 30, 1972 shall be the fees set out for that
treatment in the schedule of fees of the provincial dental association
in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the province in which the treatment is provided,
  where the treatment is provided in a province other than the Yukon
  Territory or the Northwest Territories;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Province of British Columbia, where the treatment
  is provided in the Yukon Territory;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Province of Alberta, where the treatment is
  provided in the Northwest Territories; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Province of Ontario, where the treatment is
  provided outside Canada.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where, in respect of any
dental treatment provided pursuant to these Regulations, the applicable
schedule of fees referred to in section 5

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} does not set out any fee for that treatment,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not set out a fixed fee, or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} authorizes discretionary increases or decreases in
  the fees set out therein,

the Minister of National Defence may, on the recommendation of the
commanding officer of the dental unit that provided the treatment,
determine the fee to be charged therefor having regard to that
recommendation and to prevailing charges for that type of treatment in
the location where the treatment is provided.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Fees paid pursuant to these
Regulations shall be deposited to the credit of the Receiver General.
