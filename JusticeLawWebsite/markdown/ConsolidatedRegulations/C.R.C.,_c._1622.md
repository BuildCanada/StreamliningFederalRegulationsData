## Memorial Cross Order (World War I) {#memorial-cross-order-world-war-i .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1622
:::

ORDER RESPECTING THE AWARD OF THE MEMORIAL CROSS TO MOTHERS AND WIDOWS
OF SAILORS AND SOLDIERS---WORLD WAR I
::::

::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-535734 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the *Memorial Cross Order (World War I)*.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-535736 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Cross]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Cross]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Memorial Cross, which shall
    be a cross patonce in silver, suspended by a purple ribbon; at the
    end of the upright a crown; at the foot, and at the end of each arm,
    a maple leaf; in the centre, within a wreath of laurel, the Royal
    Cypher of the reigning monarch; engraved with the number, rank and
    name of the sailor or soldier commemorated;
    ([Croix]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Veterans
    Affairs; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mother]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mother]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the natural mother or, where
    the sailor or soldier was not raised from childhood by his natural
    mother, the woman who, in the opinion of the Minister, raised him
    from childhood; ([mère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sailor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sailor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who served during
    World War I in

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any branch of the Royal Canadian Naval Service,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other of the Naval Forces of His Majesty, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Naval Forces of one of the countries allied
      with His Majesty during World War I

    if, in the case of a person who served in other than a branch of the
    Royal Canadian Naval Service, that person was born or domiciled in
    Canada or Newfoundland or was an ordinary resident in Canada or
    Newfoundland at any time during the period between August 3, 1904
    and the date on which he commenced to so serve;
    ([marin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[soldier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[soldier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who served during
    World War I in

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Expeditionary Force,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any of the other military forces of His Majesty,
      or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the military forces of one of the countries
      allied with His Majesty during World War I

    if, in the case of a person who served in other than the Canadian
    Expeditionary Force, that person was born or domiciled in Canada or
    Newfoundland or was an ordinary resident in Canada or Newfoundland
    at anytime during the period between August 3, 1904 and the date on
    which he commenced to so serve; ([soldat]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[widow]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[widow]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the legal widow or widower of
    a sailor or soldier and any other person who is pensionable as a
    widow under the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) by reason of the death
    of the sailor or soldier or who, if the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6)
    had applied to her, could have been deemed to be the widow of the
    sailor or soldier, but does not include any person who was, at the
    time of the death of the sailor or soldier, divorced, judicially
    separated or separated pursuant to a written or other agreement from
    him; ([veuve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[World War I]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[World War I]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 3(1) of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6). ([Première
    Guerre mondiale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    ::: HistoricalNote
    - SI/91-68, s. 1
    :::

## [Award of the Cross]{.HTitleText1} {#h-535753 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5, a
Cross may be issued as a memento of personnal loss and sacrifice on the
part of the mother and widow of a sailor or soldier of Canada or
Newfoundland who laid down his life for his country during World War I
or who dies or died from causes attributable to service during World War
1.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A Cross referred to in
section 3 shall be issued in respect of a sailor or soldier described in
that section to only the following persons, namely,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the mother, if alive,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the widow, if alive,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where there is no person referred to in paragraph (a)
  or (b), to the eldest living child,

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where there is no person referred to in
  paragraphs (a) to (c), to the father, if alive, and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} where there is no person referred to in
  paragraphs (a) to (d), to the brother or sister, if alive, whichever
  is the eldest

of the sailor or soldier.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/81-30, s. 1
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A Cross or Crosses may be
issued in respect of each sailor or soldier who:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} was killed in action during World War I,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} died while on active service during World War I, or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} died or dies from causes attributable to service
  during World War I in the naval or military forces, whether his death
  occurred or occurs while serving on active service or subsequent
  thereto,

except that where death occurred or occurs subsequent to his discharge
from the naval or military forces, a Cross may be issued to the widow or
her next of kin only if she was married to the sailor or soldier at the
time of his discharge.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A Cross shall not be issued
to a person referred to in section 4 unless that person furnishes proof
of the death and service of the sailor or soldier in respect of whom the
cross may be issued and the eligibility of the person to be issued the
Cross.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/81-30, s. 2
:::
