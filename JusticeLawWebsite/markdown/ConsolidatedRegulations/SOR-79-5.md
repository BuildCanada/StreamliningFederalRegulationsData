## Import Permits Regulations {#import-permits-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/79-5
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

Registration 1978-12-18

Regulations Respecting Import Permits

P.C. 1978-3738 1978-12-14
::::

:::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Industry, Trade and Commerce, pursuant to section 12 of
the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), is pleased hereby
to revoke the [Import Permits Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-5)
made and established by Order in Council P.C. 1954-789 of 27th May,
1954[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, and to
make the annexed [Regulations respecting Import
Permits](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-5) in substitution therefor.

  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, Vol. 88, No. 12, June 23,
  1954, p. 475 and 1955 Consolidation, Vol. 2, p. 1225 and C.R.C.,
  c. 605
  :::

## [Application for Permit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865936 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A resident of Canada may
apply for a permit to the Minister by furnishing the following
information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name and address;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} information as to whether or not the applicant is a
  resident of Canada;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the importer's name and address if different from the
  applicant;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the supplier of the goods to
  be imported;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the country of origin of the goods;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the country from which the goods are imported;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the customs office where the goods will enter Canada;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the date of entry of the goods in Canada;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the goods;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the number of units of the goods to be imported and
  their value in Canadian currency; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} any information requested by the Minister in any case
  where, in his opinion, the information furnished by the applicant
  requires clarification or the description of the goods to be imported
  is not in sufficient detail.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-33, s. 2
:::

## [Issue of Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865951 .Part}

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/95-33, s.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a permit is issued, the Minister shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} affix his signature to each copy of the permit; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cause two copies of the permit to be transmitted to

## [Shipping Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865960 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every person to whom a
permit has been issued shall, prior to importing the goods described in
the permit,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} certify

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the information furnished in the application
    in respect of the permit is correct, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} their status as a resident of Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} affix their signature to the copy of the permit
  stamped "For Customs Clearance Only" or "Pour dédouanement seulement"
  and transmit the copy to the collector of customs at a customs office.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-33, s. 5
- SOR/96-49, s. 2
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/96-49, s.
3\]]{.Repealed}

## [Lost Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865969 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where a permit has been
lost or destroyed, the person to whom it was issued may request from the
Minister a permit to replace it and shall, when making the request,
submit a statutory declaration containing

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the permit has been lost or
  destroyed and an explanation of the loss or destruction; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a permit that has been lost, an
  undertaking to return the original permit to the Minister without
  delay, if it is found.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-33, s. 6
:::
