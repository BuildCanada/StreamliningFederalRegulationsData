## Canadian Egg Licensing Regulations, 1987 {#canadian-egg-licensing-regulations-1987 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-242
:::

[FARM PRODUCTS AGENCIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-4)

Registration 1987-04-24

Regulations Respecting the Licensing of Persons Who Are Engaged in the
Marketing of Eggs in Interprovincial or Export Trade
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council has by the [Canadian Egg Marketing
Agency Proclamation](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._646), C.R.C., c. 646
established the Canadian Egg Marketing Agency pursuant to subsection
17(1) of the Farm Products Marketing Agencies Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-87-242_e_hq_7347){#fn_SOR-87-242_e_hq_7347-ID0EBBFA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-87-242_e_hq_7347-ID0EBBFA){#fn_SOR-87-242_e_hq_7347}S.C.
  1970-71-72, c. 65
  :::

And Whereas the Canadian Egg Marketing Agency has been authorized to
implement a marketing plan pursuant to the [Canadian Egg Marketing
Agency Proclamation](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._646);

Therefore, the Canadian Egg Marketing Agency, pursuant to paragraph
23(1)(f) of the Farm Products Marketing Agencies Act and section 9 of
Part II of the schedule to the [Canadian Egg Marketing Agency
Proclamation](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._646), hereby revokes the
Canadian Egg Licensing Regulations, C.R.C., c. 655 and makes the annexed
[Regulations respecting the licensing of persons who are engaged in the
marketing of eggs in interprovincial or export
trade](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-242), in substitution therefor.

Ottawa, March 26th, 1987

The National Farm Products Marketing Council, being satisfied that the
making of the annexed Regulations is necessary for the administration of
the marketing plan that the Canadian Egg Marketing Agency is authorized
to implement, pursuant to paragraph 7(1)(e) of the Farm Products
Marketing Agencies Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-87-242_e_hq_7347){#fn_SOR-87-242_e_hq_7347-ID0EFABA},
hereby approves the revocation of the Canadian Egg Licensing
Regulations, C.R.C., c. 655 and the making of the annexed [Regulations
respecting the licensing of persons who are engaged in the marketing of
eggs in interprovincial or export trade](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-242),
made by the Canadian Egg Marketing Agency on March 26th, 1987, in
substitution therefor.

Ottawa, April 21, 1987
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905007 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Canadian Egg Licensing Regulations,
1987](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-242).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905009 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Egg Marketing
    Agency; ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who buys eggs for
    resale and includes a retailer, grading station operator, dealer and
    processor; ([acheteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[egg]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[egg]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the egg of a hen of any class of
    the domestic chicken belonging to the species [Gallus
    Domesticus]{.otherLang lang="la"}; ([oeuf]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a buyer's licence or a
    seller's licence issued pursuant to section 5;
    ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to eggs,
    selling and offering for sale, buying, pricing, assembling, packing,
    processing, transporting, storing and reselling, whether in whole or
    in processed form; ([commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production of eggs in Canada; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[seller]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[seller]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who sells eggs and
    includes a retailer, grading station operator, dealer and producer.
    ([vendeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905018 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
persons who are engaged in the marketing of eggs in interprovincial or
export trade in any province, including the Northwest Territories and
the Yukon Territory, except persons who are buyers of less than 300
dozen eggs per month.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-488, s. 1
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905022 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), no person shall engage in the marketing of
  eggs in interprovincial or export trade unless the person holds an
  appropriate licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who engages in the marketing of eggs in
  interprovincial or export trade, other than a producer, shall hold a
  separate licence in respect of each building used in the person's
  marketing operation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who engages in the marketing of eggs in
  interprovincial or export trade as a buyer and as a seller shall hold
  a buyer's licence and a seller's licence.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 8, the
Agency shall, on receipt of an application for a licence and payment of
the fee set out in Column II of an item of Schedule I, issue the
licence.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A licence

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} expires one year after the date of issue; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not transferable.

<!-- -->

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licence shall be subject to the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), the holder of the
    licence shall submit to the Agency for the periods set out in the
    licence a report containing the information set out in Schedule II;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the licence shall comply with all
    orders and regulations of the Agency and all orders of the National
    Farm Products Council;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the licence shall keep complete and
    accurate books containing, for each transaction of the holder of the
    licence in relation to the marketing of eggs in interprovincial and
    export trade,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date of the transaction,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and licence number of the
      other holder of a licence, from whom the eggs were bought or to
      whom the eggs were sold,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity and price per dozen of the eggs
      bought or sold, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if the eggs bought or sold are graded, the grade
      of the eggs;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the licence shall not knowingly
    engage in the marketing of eggs in interprovincial trade except with
    a person who holds a licence;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the licence shall not knowingly
    engage in the marketing of eggs in interprovincial or export trade
    except in eggs that have been produced by a producer authorized to
    do so under a federal quota allotted to the producer pursuant to the
    [Canadian Egg Marketing Agency Quota Regulations,
    1986](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-8); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the licence shall retain the books
    referred to in paragraph (c) for six years after the date of the
    last entry made in the books.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(a) does not apply to the holder of a
  licence who is a retailer who sells fewer than 300 dozen eggs per
  month.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-488, s. 2
- SOR/94-164, s. 1
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Agency may refuse to
issue or renew a licence or may suspend or revoke a licence where the
applicant or holder of the licence

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} has failed to observe any condition of the licence;
  or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is in contravention of any order, regulation or
  directive made pursuant to a provincial egg marketing plan established
  under the "Federal-Provincial Agreement in respect of the revision and
  consolidation of the Comprehensive Marketing Program for the purpose
  of regulating the marketing of Eggs in Canada", authorized by Order in
  Council P.C. 1976-1979.

<!-- -->

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Agency intends to refuse to issue or renew a licence or intends to
  suspend or revoke a licence, the Agency shall give notice of its
  intention to the applicant or holder of the licence by personal
  service or by registered mail.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice referred to in subsection (1) shall set
  out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reasons on which the Agency bases its
    intention; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a time and date at which the applicant or holder of
    the licence may show cause why the licence should be issued or
    renewed or should not be suspended or revoked.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The date referred to in paragraph (2)(b) shall be not
  less than 30 days after the date of the service or mailing of the
  notice.
