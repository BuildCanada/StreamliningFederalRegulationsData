## Diplomatic Motor Vehicle Diversion Remission Order {#diplomatic-motor-vehicle-diversion-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/78-162
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1978-10-11

Order Respecting the Remission of Sales and Excise Tax on Motor Vehicles
Purchased by Diplomatic and Other Representatives of Foreign Countries
and Diverted to Taxable Use

P.C. 1978-2963 1978-09-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
section 17 of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is
pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of
sales and excise tax on motor vehicles purchased by diplomatic and other
representatives of foreign countries and diverted to taxable
use](/eng/regulations/SI-78-162).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634103 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Diplomatic Motor Vehicle Diversion Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-78-162).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634105 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Revenue; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the head of a diplomatic mission or the High
      Commissioner representing other of Her Majesty's Governments,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a counsellor, a secretary or an attaché at an
      embassy, a legation or an office of a High Commissioner in Canada,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a trade commissioner or an assistant trade
      commissioner representing other of Her Majesty's Governments, and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a consul general, consul or vice-consul of a
      foreign nation,

    and who is a native or citizen of the country he represents and is
    not engaged in any other business or profession.
    ([représentant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission is hereby granted of the
  sales and excise tax payable under subsection 27(4) of the [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on a motor vehicle manufactured or produced in
    Canada that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has been purchased by a representative during the
      period commencing April 1, 1977 and ending December 31, 1978 for
      his personal or official use without payment of sales tax under
      the provisions of Part II of Schedule III to the [Excise Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/E-15) and without payment of excise tax under the
      provisions of section 13 of Schedule I to the [Excise Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/E-15), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), has been in Canada
      and used by or in the possession of the representative for at
      least one year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on a motor vehicle imported into Canada that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has been purchased by a representative during the
      period commencing April 1, 1977 and ending December 31, 1978 for
      his personal or official use without payment of sales tax under
      the provisions of Part II of Schedule III to the [Excise Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/E-15) and without payment of excise tax under the
      provisions of section 13 of Schedule I to the [Excise Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/E-15), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), has been in Canada
      and used by or in the possession of the representative for at
      least two years.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister is advised by the Secretary of
  State for External Affairs that a representative has been transferred
  out of Canada, that the transfer had not been anticipated and that the
  representative could not reasonably have been expected to take a motor
  vehicle referred to in subsection (1) with him or that the
  representative has died, remission may be granted pursuant to
  subsection (1) on the recommendation of the Minister, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a motor vehicle manufactured or
    produced in Canada, the motor vehicle has been in Canada and used by
    or been in the possession of the representative for less than one
    year; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a motor vehicle imported into
    Canada, the motor vehicle has been in Canada and used by or been in
    the possession of the representative for less than two years.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 5, remission is hereby granted of the sales tax and excise
  tax payable under subsection 27(4) of the [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on a motor vehicle that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has been purchased in Canada or imported into
    Canada on or after January 1, 1979 by a representative for his
    personal or official use without payment of sales tax under the
    provisions of Part II of Schedule III to the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15) and without payment of excise tax under the
    provisions of section 13 of Schedule I to the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), has been in Canada and
    used by or in the possession of the representative for at least two
    years.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister is advised by the Secretary of
  State for External Affairs that a representative has been transferred
  out of Canada, that the transfer had not been anticipated and that the
  representative could not reasonably have been expected to take the
  motor vehicle referred to in subsection (1) with him or that the
  representative has died, remission may be granted pursuant to
  subsection (1) on the recommendation of the Minister if the motor
  vehicle has been in Canada and used by or been in the possession of
  the representative for less than two years.

## [Reciprocity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634130 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No remission pursuant to
section 4 shall be granted under this Order to a representative of a
government where the Secretary of State for External Affairs has advised
the Minister that full reciprocity is not being accorded by the
government to offices, officials and employees of the Government of
Canada.
