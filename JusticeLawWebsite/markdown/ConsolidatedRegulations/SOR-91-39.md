## Agriculture and Fishing Property (GST/HST) Regulations {#agriculture-and-fishing-property-gsthst-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-39
:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Registration 1990-12-18

Agriculture and Fishing Property (GST/HST) Regulations

P.C. 1990-2749  1990-12-18
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 277(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the [Excise
Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), is pleased hereby to make the annexed
Regulations prescribing property related to agriculture and fishing.

## [Prescribed Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936451 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Property that is listed in
the schedule, when supplied by way of sale, is prescribed property for
the purposes of section 10 of Part IV of Schedule VI to the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15).

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-936454 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- - **1** []{.lawlabel} Property related to agriculture

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} Equipment

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Tractors designed for farm use that have a rating
      of at least 44.74 kW at power takeoff (60 PTO h.p.)

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Harvesting equipment

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} self-propelled or pull-type combines

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} self-propelled or pull-type swathers

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} self-propelled or pull-type wind-rowers

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} headers for combines, forage harvesters,
        swathers or wind-rowers

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} combine or forage harvester pickups

      - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} forage harvesters

      - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} self-propelled, tractor-mounted or pull-type
        mechanical fruit or vegetable pickers or harvesters

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Tillage equipment

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} mouldboard plows with three furrows or more

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} disc plows with three furrows or more

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} chisel plows with an operational width of at
        least 2.44 m (8 feet)

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} subsoil chisels with an operational width of
        at least 2.44 m (8 feet)

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} discers with an operational width of at least
        2.44 m (8 feet)

      - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} rod weeders with an operational width of at
        least 2.44 m (8 feet)

      - [(vi.1)]{.lawlabel} bean rods with an operational width of at
        least 2.44 m (8 feet)

      - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} field cultivators with an operational width
        of at least 2.44 m (8 feet)

      - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} row crop cultivators with an operational
        width of at least 2.44 m (8 feet)

      - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} combination discer-cultivators with an
        operational width of at least 2.44 m (8 feet)

      - [(x)]{.lawlabel} rototillers with an operational width of at
        least 1.83 m (6 feet)

      - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} rotovators with an operational width of at
        least 1.83 m (6 feet)

      - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} harrows with an operational width of at least
        2.44 m (8 feet), when sold in complete units

      - [(xiii)]{.lawlabel} pulverizers with an operational width of at
        least 2.44 m (8 feet)

      - [(xiii.1)]{.lawlabel} farm-type land packers with an operational
        width of at least 2.44 m (8 feet)

      - [(xiv)]{.lawlabel} mulchers with an operational width of at
        least 2.44 m (8 feet)

      - [(xv)]{.lawlabel} rotary hoes with an operational width of at
        least 2.44 m (8 feet)

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Seeders and planters

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} airflow seeders

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} grain or seed drills with an operational width
        of at least 2.44 m (8 feet)

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} farm-type row-crop or toolbar seeders or
        planters designed to seed two or more rows at a time

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Haying equipment

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} mower-conditioners

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} hay balers

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} hay cubers

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} bale throwers, elevators or conveyors

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} hay rakes

      - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} hay tedders

      - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} hay conditioners, crushers or crimpers

      - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} swath turners and wind-row turners

      - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} silage baggers and round-bale wrapping
        machines

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} Grain handling or processing equipment

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} grain bins or tanks with a capacity of not more
        than 181 m^3^ (5000 bushels)

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} transportable conveyors with belts less than
        76.2 cm (30 inches) wide and 0.48 cm (3/16 inch) thick,
        transportable farm grain augers, transportable utility augers
        and transportable elevators

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} bin sweeps or cleaners designed for
        attachment to portable farm grain augers

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} farm tractor-powered pneumatic grain conveyors

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} farm-type feed mills, including roller mills
        and hammer mills

      - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} farm-type feed mixers

      - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} farm-type feed grinder mixers

      - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} farm-type tub grinders and other farm-type
        feed grinders

      - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} ensilage mixers

      - [(x)]{.lawlabel} farm-type grain toasters for use in livestock
        feed production

      - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} self-propelled feed or ensilage carts

      - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} grain dryers

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} Miscellaneous property

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} farm-type refrigerated bulk milk coolers

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} mechanical rock or stone pickers

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} forage blowers

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} silo unloaders

      - [(iv.1)]{.lawlabel} self-propelled, tractor-mounted or pull-type
        agricultural wagons or trailers designed for

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} off-road handling and transporting of grain,
          forage, livestock feed or fertilizer, and

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} use at speeds not exceeding 40 km per hour

      - [(iv.2)]{.lawlabel} shredders with an operational width of at
        least 3.66 m (12 feet)

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} assembled, fully operational milking systems
        consisting of a receiver group, vacuum supplier, pulsators and
        related equipment

      - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} individual components of a milking system that
        consists of a receiver group, vacuum supplier, pulsators and
        related equipment, when the components are supplied together
        unassembled and, once assembled, constitute the fully
        operational milking system

      - [(vi.1)]{.lawlabel} assembled and fully operational automated
        and computerized farm livestock or poultry feeding systems

      - [(vi.2)]{.lawlabel} individual components of an automated and
        computerized farm livestock or poultry feeding system, when the
        components are supplied together unassembled and, once
        assembled, constitute the fully operational feeding system

      - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} rock or root rakes and rock or root
        wind-rowers

      - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} self-propelled, tractor-mounted,
        cultivator-mounted or pull-type field sprayers with a tank
        capacity of at least 300 L (66 gallons)

      - [(viii.1)]{.lawlabel} farm-type granular fertilizer or pesticide
        applicators with an operational capacity of at least 0.2265 m^3^
        (8 cubic feet)

      - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} liquid, box, tank or flail manure spreaders

      - [(x)]{.lawlabel} injection systems for liquid manure spreaders

      - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} leafcutter bees

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Feed, when sold in bulk quantities of at least 20
    kg (44 lbs) or in bags that contain at least 20 kg (44 lbs), that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a complete feed, supplement, macro-premix,
      micro-premix, or mineral feed (other than a trace mineral salt
      feed), all as defined in the [Feeds Regulations,
      1983](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-593),

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is labelled in accordance with those Regulations,
      and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is designed for

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a single species or class of farm livestock,
        fish or poultry ordinarily raised or kept to produce, or to be
        used as, food for human consumption or to produce wool, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} rabbits.

  - [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} Feed, when sold in bulk quantities of at least 20
    kg (44 lbs) or in bags that contain at least 20 kg (44 lbs), that is
    designed for ostriches, rheas, emus or bees.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} By-products of the food processing industry and
    plant or animal products, when sold in bulk quantities of at least
    20 kg (44 lbs) or in bags that contain at least 20 kg (44 lbs), that
    are ordinarily used as feed, or as ingredients in feed, for farm
    livestock, fish or poultry described in subparagraph (2)(c)(i) or
    for rabbits, ostriches, rheas, emus or bees.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Pesticides labelled in accordance with the [Pest
    Control Products Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1253) as
    having a purpose that includes agricultural use and a product class
    designation other than "Domestic".

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} Quota authorized by a government agency or
    marketing board in respect of an agricultural product the supply of
    which is included in section 1 of Part III or in section 1, 2, 3, 4
    or 7 of Part IV of Schedule VI to the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15).

- - **2** []{.lawlabel} Property related to fishing

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any fishing vessel that is supplied to a person by
    way of sale in Canada, or by way of sale outside Canada and imported
    by the person, for use by the person in commercial fishing, where
    the following is provided, in the case of a supply in Canada, to the
    supplier at the time of sale, or, in the case of an importation, to
    the customs office from which the vessel is released at the time of
    accounting for the vessel in accordance with section 32 of the
    [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6):

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the registration number assigned to the person
      under section 241 of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15),

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a declaration signed by the person that the
      person intends to use the vessel in commercial fishing,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number of a commercial fishing licence of the
      person, namely,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of commercial fishing on the
        Pacific coast of Canada, a licence that was issued to the
        person, or that was issued in respect of the vessel, by the
        Department of Fisheries and Oceans and that is not a Category D,
        P or Z licence,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of commercial fishing on the
        Atlantic coast of Canada, a licence that was issued to the
        person by the Department of Fisheries and Oceans and that is not
        a licence to harvest marine plants or to fish for mussels,
        oysters, shark, smelt or squid,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an inland fishery, a
        commercial fishing licence that was issued to the person by the
        government of a province, and

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in the case of commercial fishing in Yukon,
        the Northwest Territories or Nunavut, a commercial fishing
        licence that was issued to the person by the Department of
        Fisheries and Oceans, and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of commercial fishing on the Atlantic
      coast of Canada, the number set out in the registration card
      issued in respect of the vessel by the Department of Fisheries and
      Oceans.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Fishing nets and related items

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Gill-nets, webbing for gill-nets, leadline for
      gill-nets, corkline (top rope) for gill-nets and floats for
      gill-nets

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Seines, webbing for seines, leadlines for seines,
      corkline (top rope) for seines and floats for seines

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Trawl-nets, webbing for trawl-nets, leadlines for
      trawl-nets and corkline (top rope) for trawl-nets

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Gill-net drums, seine drums, trawl-net drums and
      long-line drums

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Entrapment webbing and predator webbing

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} Trawl-net doors

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Other equipment

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Automatic baiters

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Automatic jiggers

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Mechanical net washers

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Automatic netpen feeders

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Manufactured netpens for use in aquaculture

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} Pescalators.
