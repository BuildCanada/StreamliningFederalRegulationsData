## Banc-des-Américains Marine Protected Area Regulations {#banc-des-américains-marine-protected-area-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2019-50
:::

[OCEANS ACT](/eng/acts/O-2.4)

Registration 2019-02-25

Banc-des-Américains Marine Protected Area Regulations

P.C. 2019-95 2019-02-23
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to subsection 35(3) of
the [Oceans Act](/eng/acts/O-2.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-3372-E_hq_21683){#fn_81000-2-3372-E_hq_21683-ID0EDBAA},

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1112502 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Marine Protected Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Marine Protected Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area of
      the sea that is designated by section 2. ([zone de protection
      marine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
      2 of the [Canada Shipping Act,
      2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) ([bâtiment]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Geographical coordinates

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the schedule, all geographical coordinates
  (latitude and longitude) are expressed in the North American Datum
  1983 (NAD83) reference system.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Geographical coordinates for points

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The geographical coordinates of the points referred
  to in sections 2 and 3 are set out in the schedule.

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1112512 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marine Protected Area

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The area
  of the sea depicted in the schedule that is bounded by a series of
  rhumb lines drawn from points 1 to 4 and then back to point 1 is
  designated as the Banc-des-Américains Marine Protected Area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seabed, subsoil and water column

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Marine Protected Area consists of the seabed, the
  subsoil to a depth of five metres and the water column above the
  seabed.

## [Management Zones]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1112518 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Boundaries

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Marine Protected Area
consists of the following management zones, each of which is depicted in
the schedule:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Zone 1, which is bounded by a series of rhumb lines
  drawn from point 1 to point 5, then to points 6 to 16 and then back to
  point 1;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Zone 2a, which is bounded by a series of rhumb lines
  drawn from point 5 to point 2, then to point 3, then to point 11, then
  to point 10, then to point 9, then to point 8, then to point 7, then
  to point 6 and then back to point 5; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Zone 2b, which is bounded by a series of rhumb lines
  drawn from point 16 to point 15, then to point 14, then to point 13,
  then to point 12, then to point 4 and then back to point 16.

## [Prohibited Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1112524 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 5 to 8,
it is prohibited to carry out any activity in the Marine Protected Area
that disturbs, damages, destroys or removes from the Marine Protected
Area any living marine organism or any part of its habitat or that is
likely to do so.

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1112527 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fishing

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The following activities
may be carried out in the Marine Protected Area if they are carried out
in accordance with the provisions of the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14)
and the [Coastal Fisheries Protection Act](/eng/acts/C-33), as well as
their regulations:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fishing, other than commercial fishing, that is
  authorized under the [Aboriginal Communal Fishing Licences
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-332);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in Zones 2a and 2b, commercial fishing --- for any
  species other than capelin, herring, mackerel, sand lace, krill or
  copepods --- by means of a trap, longline or handline or by angling;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in Zones 2a and 2b, recreational fishing by means of
  a handline or by angling.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Navigation

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Navigation may be carried
out in the Marine Protected Area subject to the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a vessel must not anchor in Zone 1; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel that is of 400 gross tonnage
  or more or certified to carry 15 persons or more, it must not
  discharge [[sewage]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection
  1(1) of the [Vessel Pollution and Dangerous Chemicals
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-69) or release
  [[greywater]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 131.1(1)
  of those Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safety or emergency

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Any activity may be carried
out in the Marine Protected Area if it is carried out for the purpose of
public safety, national defence, national security, law enforcement or
to respond to an emergency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activity plan

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Any activity that is part
of an activity plan that has been approved by the Minister may be
carried out in the Marine Protected Area.

## [Activity Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1112541 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission to Minister

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person may submit to the Minister an activity plan for the carrying
  out of any scientific research or monitoring, habitat restoration,
  educational or commercial marine tourism activity in the Marine
  Protected Area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of plan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The activity plan must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person's name, address, telephone number and
    email address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the activity plan is submitted by an institution
    or organization, the name of the individual who will be responsible
    for the proposed activity and their title, address, telephone number
    and email address;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of each vessel that the person proposes to
    use to carry out the activity, its state of registration and
    registration number, its radio call sign and the name, address,
    telephone number and email address of its owner, master and any
    operator;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the proposed activity and
    its purpose, the methods or techniques that are to be used to carry
    out the activity and the data to be collected;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the geographical coordinates of the site of the
    proposed activity and a map that shows the location of the activity
    within the boundaries of the Marine Protected Area;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the proposed dates and alternative dates on which
    the activity is to be carried out;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a list of the equipment that is to be used, the
    means by which it will be deployed and retrieved and the methods by
    which it is to be anchored or moored;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a list of the type and quantity of samples that are
    to be collected;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a list of any substances that may be deposited
    during the proposed activity in the Marine Protected Area --- other
    than substances that are authorized under the [Canada Shipping Act,
    2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) to be deposited in the navigation of a
    vessel --- and the quantity and concentration of each substance;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a description of the adverse environmental effects
    that are likely to result from carrying out the proposed activity
    and of any measures that are to be taken to monitor, avoid, minimize
    or mitigate those effects;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a description of any scientific research or
    monitoring, habitat restoration, educational or commercial marine
    tourism activity that the person has carried out or anticipates
    carrying out in the Marine Protected Area; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a general description of any study, report or other
    work that is anticipated to result from the proposed activity and
    its anticipated date of completion.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of activity plan

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must approve an activity plan if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the scientific research, monitoring or habitat
    restoration activities that are set out in the plan are not likely
    to destroy the habitat of any living marine organism in the Marine
    Protected Area and serve to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} increase knowledge of the biodiversity or
      biological productivity of the Marine Protected Area,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} increase knowledge of the habitat of any living
      marine organism in the Marine Protected Area or of the ecosystem
      structure and function of the Marine Protected Area, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} assist in the management of the Marine
      Protected Area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the educational or commercial marine tourism
    activities that are set out in the plan

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are not likely to damage, destroy or remove from
      the Marine Protected Area any living marine organism or any part
      of its habitat, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} serve to increase public awareness of the Marine
      Protected Area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval prohibited

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the activity plan must not be
  approved if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any substance that may be deposited during the
    proposed activity is a [[deleterious
    substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 34(1) of
    the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14), unless the deposit of the
    substance is authorized under subsection 36(4) of that Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cumulative environmental effects of the
    proposed activity, in combination with those of any other past and
    current activities carried out in the Marine Protected Area, are
    such that the activity is likely to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} destroy the habitat of any living marine organism
      in the Marine Protected Area,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} adversely affect the biodiversity or biological
      productivity of the Marine Protected Area,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} adversely affect the ecosystem structure and
      function of the Marine Protected Area, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} adversely affect whales or wolffish.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timeline for approval

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister's decision in respect of an activity
  plan must be made within

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 60 days after the day on which the plan is
    received; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if amendments to the plan are made, 60 days after
    the day on which the amended plan is received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activity report

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister approves an activity plan, the person who submitted it must
  provide the Minister with an activity report within 90 days after the
  last day of the activity. The report must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the data collected during the activity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a list of the type and quantity of samples that
    were collected, the date of their collection and the geographic
    coordinates of the sampling sites;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an evaluation of the effectiveness of any measures
    taken to monitor, avoid, minimize or mitigate the adverse
    environmental effects of the activity; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of any event that occurred during the
    activity and that was not anticipated in the activity plan, if the
    event could result in the disturbance, damage, destruction or
    removal from the Marine Protected Area of any living marine organism
    or any part of its habitat.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Studies, reports or other works

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person must also provide the Minister with a copy
  of any study, report or other work that results from the activity and
  is related to the conservation and protection of the Marine Protected
  Area. The study, report or other work must be provided within 90 days
  after the day on which it is completed.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1112590 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
