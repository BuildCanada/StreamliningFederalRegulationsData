## Critical Habitat of the Fawnsfoot (Truncilla donaciformis) Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-fawnsfoot-truncilla-donaciformis-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2022-216
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2022-10-20

Critical Habitat of the Fawnsfoot (Truncilla donaciformis) Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Fawnsfoot ([Truncilla donaciformis]{.otherLang lang="la"})
is a wildlife species that is listed as an endangered species in Part 2
of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4877_hq_31137){#fn_81000-2-4877_hq_31137-ID0EBBGA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4877_hq_31137-ID0EBBGA){#fn_81000-2-4877_hq_31137}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

Whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is
specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection
58(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4877_hq_31138){#fn_81000-2-4877_hq_31138-ID0EBBEA}
of that Act;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4877_hq_31138-ID0EBBEA){#fn_81000-2-4877_hq_31138}S.C.
  2015, c. 10, s. 60
  :::

And whereas the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans is of the opinion that
the annexed Order would affect a reserve or other lands that are set
apart for the use and benefit of a band and, under subsection
58(7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-4877_hq_1657539797){#fn_81000-2-4877_hq_1657539797-ID0EBBDA}
of that Act, has consulted with the Minister of Indigenous Services and
the band in question with respect to the Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-4877_hq_1657539797-ID0EBBDA){#fn_81000-2-4877_hq_1657539797}S.C.
  2019, c. 29, subpar. 375(1)(f)(ii)
  :::

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans makes the annexed
[Critical Habitat of the Fawnsfoot ([Truncilla donaciformis]{.otherLang
lang="la"}) Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-216) under subsections
58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4877_hq_31137){#fn_81000-2-4877_hq_31137-ID0EBACA}.

Ottawa, October 17, 2022

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Fawnsfoot ([Truncilla donaciformis]{.otherLang lang="la"}), which
is identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is included
in the Species at Risk Public Registry.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
