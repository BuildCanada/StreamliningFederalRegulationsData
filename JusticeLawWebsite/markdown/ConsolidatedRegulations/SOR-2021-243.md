## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1331506 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Accessible Canada
      Act](/eng/acts/A-0.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Transportation
      Agency. ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[average number of employees]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[average number of employees]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the sum
      of the highest number of employees employed by a regulated entity
      in each month of a calendar year divided by 12 and rounded to the
      nearest whole number or, if the value is equidistant between two
      whole numbers, rounded up to the nearest whole number. ([nombre
      moyen d'employés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person employed by a
      regulated entity referred to in paragraph 7(1)(e) or (f) of the
      Act and includes a [[dependent contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
      as defined in subsection 3(1) of the [Canada Labour
      Code](/eng/acts/L-2), but does not include

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person employed under a program designated by
        the employer as a student employment program; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a student employed solely during their vacation
        periods. ([employé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[regulated transport entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[regulated transport entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      transport entity in respect of which a day has been fixed under
      section 3. ([entité de transport réglementée]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[transport entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transport entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity or person
      that is a member of one of the classes established under
      subsection 2(1). ([entité de transport]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[WCAG]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[WCAG]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Web Content Accessibility
      Guidelines, published by the World Wide Web Consortium, as amended
      from time to time. ([WCAG]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation of document incorporated
  by reference

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, if a document that is available
  in both official languages is incorporated by reference as amended
  from time to time, any amendment to that document is incorporated only
  when the amendment is available in both official languages.

## [Classes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1331521 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classes

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of these Regulations, the following classes are established:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Class 1 is a class that consists of every regulated
    entity referred to in any of paragraphs 7(1)(a) to (c) of the Act
    that is required to comply with any provision of regulations made
    under subsection 170(1) of the [Canada Transportation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Class 2 is a class that consists of every regulated
    entity referred to in paragraph 7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act that is
    required to comply with any provision of regulations made under
    subsection 170(1) of the [Canada Transportation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4) and whose average number of employees in the
    current calendar year is 100 or more;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Class 3 is a class that consists of every regulated
    entity referred to in paragraph 7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act that is
    required to comply with any provision of regulations made under
    subsection 170(1) of the [Canada Transportation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4) and whose average number of employees in the
    current calendar year is 10 or more but fewer than 100; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Class 4 is a class that consists of every regulated
    entity referred to in paragraph 7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act that is
    required to comply with any provision of regulations made under
    subsection 170(1) of the [Canada Transportation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4) and whose average number of employees in the
    current calendar year is fewer than 10.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed class

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the average number of employees of a regulated
  transport entity belonging to a certain class decreases such that the
  entity qualifies as a member of a class for entities having a lower
  average number of employees, that entity is deemed to have always been
  a member of that latter class and has the obligations of entities in
  that latter class.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations before deemed change

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), if a regulated transport
  entity published an accessibility plan as a member of a particular
  class, it must fulfill its obligations relating to the feedback
  process and progress reports with respect to that plan as though it
  were still a member of that class.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Day fixed

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), for the purposes of subsection 60(1) of the Act,
  the day fixed is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a Class 1 regulated entity, December
    31, 2021;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a Class 2 regulated entity, June 1,
    2022; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a Class 3 regulated entity, June 1,
    2023.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New transport entity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an entity or person qualifies as a Class 1, Class
  2 or Class 3 transport entity on or after January 1, 2022,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an entity or person that qualified
    as a Class 4 transport entity during the preceding year, the day
    fixed for the purposes of subsection 60(1) of the Act in respect of
    that entity or person is June 1 of the calendar year after the
    calendar year in which the entity or person qualified as a Class 4
    transport entity; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an entity or person that did not
    qualify as a Class 4 transport entity during the preceding year, the
    day fixed for the purposes of subsection 60(1) of the Act in respect
    of that entity or person is June 1 of the calendar year after the
    calendar year in which the entity or person qualified as a Class 1,
    Class 2 or Class 3 transport entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Updated accessibility plan

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An updated accessibility plan that is required by
  subsection 60(2) of the Act must be prepared and published no later
  than the third anniversary of the day on which the preceding
  accessibility plan was required to have been published.

## [Accessibility Plans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1331545 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  regulated transport entity must include the following headings in its
  accessibility plan:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} "General";

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a heading in respect of each element referred to in
    subsection 60(1) of the Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} "Consultations".

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}"General" heading

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information that is included under the heading
  "General" must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person designated to receive
    feedback on behalf of the entity or the position whose incumbent is
    designated to do so; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the means by which the public can provide feedback
    and request an alternate format of the accessibility plan or an
    alternate format of the description of the feedback process,
    including a mailing address, telephone number and email address.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}"Consultations" heading

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The information that is included under the heading
  "Consultations" must be drafted in accordance with subsection 60(5) of
  the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of accessibility plans

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a regulated transport entity must electronically
  publish its most recent accessibility plan in clear, simple and
  concise language

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the main digital platform that the entity owns,
    operates or controls and that it uses to communicate information to
    the public;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a manner that makes the plan accessible on the
    digital platform either directly on the homepage or home screen or
    by way of a hyperlink from that homepage or home screen; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in a format that meets the requirements of Level AA
    conformance that are set out in the WCAG.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No public digital platform

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the regulated transport entity does not use a
  digital platform to communicate information to the public, it must
  publish a print copy of its most recent accessibility plan in clear,
  simple and concise language and display it in a conspicuous location
  in the main reception area of each of its publicly accessible business
  locations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Agency

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A regulated transport
entity must, by electronic means, notify the Agency of the publication
of each version of its accessibility plan within 48 hours of publication
and include in the notice a hyperlink to the URL of the plan or the
addresses of the publicly accessible business locations where a print
copy of the plan is available.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternate format

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  may make a request that a regulated transport entity make its
  accessibility plan available to them in print, large print, braille or
  audio format or in an electronic format that is compatible with
  adaptive technology that is intended to assist persons with
  disabilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and manner of request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person may make the request through any means by
  which the regulated transport entity communicates with the public,
  including by mail, telephone or electronic means.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response time

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The regulated transport entity must make its
  accessibility plan available to the person in the requested format as
  soon as feasible after the request is received, but at the latest,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for a plan in braille or
    audio format, on the 45th day after the day on which the request is
    received; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for a plan in any other
    format,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for Class 1 and Class 2 regulated entities, on
      the 15th day after the day on which the request is received, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for Class 3 regulated entities, on the 20th day
      after the day on which the request is received.

## [Feedback]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1331579 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Feedback process

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 61(1) of the Act, the feedback process
  established by a regulated transport entity must allow feedback to be
  provided by any means by which the regulated transport entity
  communicates with the public, including by mail, telephone or
  electronic means.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Anonymous feedback

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The feedback process must allow feedback to be
  provided anonymously.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgement of feedback

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The regulated transport entity must acknowledge
  receipt of feedback, other than anonymous feedback, in the same manner
  in which it was received.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated position or person

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The regulated transport entity must designate and
  publicly identify a person responsible for receiving feedback on
  behalf of the entity or the position whose incumbent is designated to
  do so.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of feedback process

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a regulated transport entity must, for the purposes
  of subsection 61(2) of the Act, electronically publish a description
  of its process for receiving feedback in clear, simple and concise
  language

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the main digital platform that the entity owns,
    operates or controls and that it uses to communicate information to
    the public;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a manner that makes the description accessible
    on the digital platform either directly on the homepage or home
    screen or by way of a hyperlink from that homepage or home screen;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} together with its accessibility plan or progress
    report; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in a format that meets the requirements of Level AA
    conformance that are set out in the WCAG.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No public digital platform

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the regulated transport entity does not use a
  digital platform to communicate information to the public, it must
  publish a print copy of the most recent description of its process for
  receiving feedback, in clear, simple and concise language and,
  together with its accessibility plan or progress report, display it
  and in a conspicuous location in the main reception area of each of
  its publicly accessible business locations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of publication

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The regulated transport entity must publish the
  description of its feedback process on the same day that it publishes
  its accessibility plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Updated feedback process

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the regulated transport entity updates its
  feedback process, it must publish an updated description of the
  process in the manner set out in subsection (1) or (2), as applicable,
  as soon as feasible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternate formats

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may make a request that a regulated transport entity make the
  description of its feedback process available to them in print, large
  print, braille or audio format, or in an electronic format that is
  compatible with adaptive technology that is intended to assist persons
  with disabilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and manner of request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person may make the request through any means by
  which the regulated transport entity communicates with the public,
  including by mail, telephone or electronic means.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response time

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The regulated transport entity must make the
  description of its feedback process available to the person in the
  requested format as soon as feasible after the request is received,
  but at the latest,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for a description in
    braille or an audio format, on the 45th day after the day on which
    the request is received; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for a description in any
    other format,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for Class 1 and Class 2 regulated entities, on
      the 15th day after the day on which the request is received, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for Class 3 regulated entities, on the 20th day
      after the day on which the request is received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Agency

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A regulated transport
entity must, by electronic means, notify the Agency of the publication
of the description of its feedback process or of an updated description
of its feedback process within 48 hours of publication and include in
the notice a hyperlink to the URL of the description or updated
description or the addresses of the publicly accessible business
locations where a print copy of the description or updated description
is available.

## [Progress Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1331615 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  regulated transport entity must include the following headings in its
  progress report:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} "General";

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a heading in respect of each element referred to in
    subsections 60(1) and 62(5) of the Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} "Consultations".

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}"General" heading

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information that is included under the heading
  "General" must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person designated to receive
    feedback on behalf of the entity or the position whose incumbent is
    designated to do so; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the means by which the public can provide feedback
    and request an alternate format of the accessibility plan, an
    alternate format of the description of the feedback process or an
    alternate format of the progress report, including a mailing
    address, telephone number and email address.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}"Consultations" heading

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The information that is included under the heading
  "Consultations" must be drafted in accordance with subsection 62(4) of
  the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of progress reports

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a
  regulated transport entity must electronically publish its most recent
  progress report in clear, simple and concise language

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the main digital platform that the entity owns,
    operates or controls and that it uses to communicate information to
    the public;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a manner that makes the progress report
    accessible on the digital platform either directly on the homepage
    or home screen or by way of a hyperlink from that homepage or home
    screen; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in a format that meets the requirements of Level AA
    conformance that are set out in the WCAG.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No public digital platform

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the regulated transport entity does not use a
  digital platform to communicate information to the public, it must
  publish a print copy of its most recent progress report in clear,
  simple and concise language and display it in a conspicuous location
  in the main reception area of each of its publicly accessible business
  locations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for publication --- Class 1

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  Class 1 regulated transport entity must publish a progress report, no
  later than the anniversary of the day fixed under section 3 in respect
  of that entity, in each calendar year in which it is not required to
  publish an accessibility plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for publication --- Class 2
  or Class 3

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Class 2 or Class 3 regulated transport entity must
  publish a progress report by June 1 in each calendar year in which it
  is not required to publish an accessibility plan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Agency

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A regulated transport
entity must, by electronic means, notify the Agency of the publication
of its progress report within 48 hours of publication and include in the
notice a hyperlink to the URL of the progress report or the addresses of
the publicly accessible business locations where a print copy of the
progress report is available.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternate formats

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may make a request that a regulated transport entity make its
  progress report available to them in print, large print, braille or
  audio format, or in an electronic format that is compatible with
  adaptive technology that is intended to assist persons with
  disabilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and manner of request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person may make the request through any means by
  which the regulated transport entity communicates with the public,
  including by mail, telephone or electronic means.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response time

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The regulated transport entity must make its progress
  report available to the person in the requested format as soon as
  feasible after the request is received, but at the latest,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for a progress report in
    braille or audio format, on the 45th day after the day on which the
    request is received; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for a progress report in
    any other format,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for Class 1 and Class 2 regulated entities, on
      the 15th day after the day on which the request is received, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for Class 3 regulated entities, on the 20th day
      after the day on which the request is received.
