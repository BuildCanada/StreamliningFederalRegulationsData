## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1401334 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Family Orders and Agreements
Enforcement Assistance Act](/eng/acts/F-1.4).

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1401369 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for the release of information under Part I of the Act
  must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an indication of whether the applicant is an
    official of a court, a peace officer, a provincial enforcement
    service, a provincial child support service or a designated
    authority, and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is an official of a court, the
      name of the court,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is a peace officer, the name of
      the police force to which the officer belongs,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is a provincial enforcement
      service, the name of that service and, if it is acting on behalf
      of another entity under section 6.2 of the Act, an indication of
      that fact and the name of the other entity, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is a provincial child support
      service or a designated authority, the name of that service or
      authority;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the individual completing the
    application;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reference number of the file, if applicable;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the following information concerning the person in
    respect of whom information is requested:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their name,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} their date of birth,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} their gender,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} their phone number and email address, if known,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} their Social Insurance Number, if known, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the surnames at birth of their parents, if
      known;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the application;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an indication of the information being requested;
    and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is a provincial enforcement
    service, a provincial child support service or a designated
    authority, a declaration by the applicant stating that the
    information in the application is correct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation controlled indirectly

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If information referred to in paragraph 5(1)(d) is
  being requested in respect of a corporation that is controlled
  indirectly by the person named in the application, the application
  must also contain an explanation of the basis on which indirect
  control is established.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Making of application

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application for the
release of information under Part I of the Act is to be sent by mail to
the Department of Justice, Family Law Assistance Services, Ottawa,
Ontario K1A 0H8 or by the means of electronic communication set out in
the agreement, entered into under section 3 of the Act, that applies to
the applicant.

## [Release of Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1401394 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court official

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 12(1) of the Act, in the case of an application
  under section 7 of the Act in relation to the establishment or
  variation of a support provision, the information that is released to
  the official of the court is the information, from among the
  following, that is requested:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the address of the person named in the application
    for the release of information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the employer of the person
    named in the application for the release of information;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the information related to the person named in the
    application for the release of information --- other than their
    Social Insurance Number --- that is set out in their

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Income Tax and Benefit Return (T1), including the
      schedules,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Notice of Assessment and Notice of Reassessment,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Trust Income Allocations and
      Designations (T3),

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Remuneration Paid (T4),

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Pension, Retirement, Annuity, and
      Other Income (T4A),

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Old Age Security (T4A(OAS)),

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Canada Pension Plan Benefits
      (T4A(P)),

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Distributions from a Retirement
      Compensation Arrangement (RCA) (T4A-RCA),

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Employment Insurance and Other
      Benefits (T4E) or Statement of Employment Insurance and Other
      Benefits (Quebec) (T4EQ),

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Employee Profit-Sharing Plan
      Allocations and Payments (T4PS),

    - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Income from a Registered Retirement
      Income Fund (T4RIF),

    - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} Statement of RRSP Income (T4RSP),

    - [(xiii)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Investment Income (T5),

    - [(xiv)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Real Estate Rentals (T776),

    - [(xv)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Employment Expenses (T777),

    - [(xvi)]{.lawlabel} Government Service Contract Payments (T1204),

    - [(xvii)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Farming Activities (T2042) and
      documents related to the AgriStability and AgriInvest programs,

    - [(xviii)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Fishing Activities (T2121),

    - [(xix)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Business or Professional
      Activities (T2125),

    - [(xx)]{.lawlabel} Declaration of Conditions of Employment (T2200),

    - [(xxi)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Benefits (T5007),

    - [(xxii)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Securities Transactions (T5008),

    - [(xxiii)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Partnership Income (T5013), and

    - [(xxiv)]{.lawlabel} Statement of Contract Payments (T5018); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the person named in the application for the
    release of information controls a corporation, directly or
    indirectly in any manner whatever, within the meaning of subsection
    256(5.1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), the information
    set out in that corporation's Corporation Income Tax Return (T2),
    including the schedules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement of support provision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 12(1) of the Act, in
  the case of an application under section 7 of the Act in relation to
  the enforcement of a support provision, the information that is
  released to the official of the court is the information, from among
  the following, that is requested:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the address of the person named in the application
    for the release of information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the employer of the person
    named in the application for the release of information; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the taxation year preceding the taxation year
    in which the application for the release of information is made,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the information related to the person named in
      the application for the release of information that is set out in
      their Income Tax and Benefit Return (T1), other than their Social
      Insurance Number and information set out only in the schedules,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the information set out in their Notice of
      Assessment and Notice of Reassessment, other than their Social
      Insurance Number, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of each person and entity
      from which they received income and the amount of income received
      from each.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior taxation year

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the information referred to in any of
  subparagraphs (2)(c)(i) to (iii) is not found in an information bank
  for the taxation year referred to in paragraph (2)(c), the information
  that is released under that subparagraph is the information for the
  year preceding the taxation year referred to in that paragraph.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement of other family provisions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 12(1) of the Act, in
  the case of an application under section 7 of the Act in relation to
  the enforcement of a parenting provision, contact provision, custody
  provision or access provision, the information that is released to the
  official of the court is the information, from among the following,
  that is requested:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the address of the person named in the application
    for the release of information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the employer of the person
    named in the application for the release of information;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the child or children
    referred to in clause 9(1)(a)(iii)(B) of the Act; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the employer of the child
    or children referred to in clause 9(1)(a)(iii)(B) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to inform --- time and manner

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
12.1 of the Act, the Minister must send the copy of the order and the
notice by mail to the last known address of the person referred to in
paragraph 8(2)(a) or 9(2)(a) of the Act within 10 business days after
the day on which the Minister receives the application by the official
of the court for the release of information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Peace officer

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 14(1) of the Act, the information that is released to the
peace officer is the information, from among the following, that is
requested:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the address of the person named in the application
  for the release of information;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the employer of the person
  named in the application for the release of information;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the child or children who is
  or are alleged to have been abducted; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the employer of the child or
  children who is or are alleged to have been abducted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial enforcement service

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 15(1) of the Act, in the case of an application
  for the release of information that is made for the purpose set out in
  paragraph 15(2)(a) of the Act, the information that is released to the
  provincial enforcement service is the information, from among the
  following, that is requested:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the address of the person named in the application
    for the release of information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the employer of the person
    named in the application for the release of information;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Social Insurance Number of the person named in
    the application for the release of information; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for the taxation year preceding the taxation year
    in which the application for the release of information is made,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the information related to the person named in
      the application that is set out in their Income Tax and Benefit
      Return (T1), other than information set out only in the schedules,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the information set out in the Notice of
      Assessment and Notice of Reassessment of the person named in the
      application, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of each person and entity
      from which the person named in the application received income and
      the amount of income received from each.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior taxation year

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the information referred to in any of
  subparagraphs (1)(d)(i) to (iii) is not found in an information bank
  for the taxation year referred to in paragraph (1)(d), the information
  that is released under that subparagraph is the information for the
  year preceding the taxation year referred to in that paragraph.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parenting, contact, custody or access
  provision

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 15(1) of the Act, in
  the case of an application for the release of information that is made
  for the purpose set out in paragraph 15(2)(b) of the Act, the
  information that is released to the provincial enforcement service is
  the information, from among the following, that is requested:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the address of the person named in the application
    for the release of information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the employer of the person
    named in the application for the release of information;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the child or children
    referred to in paragraph 15(2)(b) of the Act; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the employer of the child
    or children referred to in paragraph 15(2)(b) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Creditor or debtor

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 15(1) of the Act, in
  the case of an application for the release of information that is made
  for the purpose set out in paragraph 15(2)(c) of the Act, the
  information that is released to the provincial enforcement service is
  the address of the person named in the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- prior agreements

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Paragraphs (1)(c) and (d) and subsection (4) apply
  only in respect of a provincial enforcement service of a province that
  has, under section 3 of the Act, entered into an agreement that has an
  effective date that is no earlier than the day on which this
  subsection comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application on behalf of another entity

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (1) to (5), if the provincial
  enforcement service is acting on behalf of another entity under
  section 6.2 of the Act, the information that is released to the
  provincial enforcement service is the information that would be
  released to the other entity under section 9 or 10 of these
  Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated authority

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 16(1) of the Act, the information that is released to the
designated authority is the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for the release of
  information that is made for a purpose described in paragraph 16(2)(a)
  of the Act, the address of the person named in the application for the
  release of information; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for the release of
  information that is made for a purpose described in paragraph 16(2)(b)
  of the Act, the province, territory, country or political subdivision
  of a country where the person named in the application for the release
  of information resides.
