## Organizations in the Province of Quebec Exemption Order {#organizations-in-the-province-of-quebec-exemption-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-374
:::

[PERSONAL INFORMATION PROTECTION AND ELECTRONIC DOCUMENTS
ACT](/eng/acts/P-8.6)

Registration 2003-11-19

Organizations in the Province of Quebec Exemption Order

P.C. 2003-1842 2003-11-19
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that An Act respecting the
protection of personal information in the private sector, R.S.Q., c.
P-39.1, of the Province of Quebec which is substantially similar to Part
1 of the [Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents
Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBABA}, applies to
the organizations referred to in the annexed Order;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Industry, pursuant to paragraph
26(2)(b) of the [Personal Information Protection and Electronic
Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6)^a^, hereby makes the annexed
[Organizations in the Province of Quebec Exemption
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-374).

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EBABA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2000, c.
  5
  :::

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707323 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Any organization, other
than a federal work, undertaking or business, that carries on an
enterprise within the meaning of section 1525 of the Civil Code of
Québec and to which An Act respecting the protection of personal
information in the private sector, R.S.Q., c. P-39.1, applies is exempt
from the application of Part 1 of the [Personal Information Protection
and Electronic Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6) in respect of the
collection, use and disclosure of personal information that occurs
within the Province of Quebec.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707325 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
