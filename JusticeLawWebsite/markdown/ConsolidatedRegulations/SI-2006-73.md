## Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Department of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness the Control and Supervision of the Borders Task Force {#order-transferring-from-the-privy-council-office-to-the-department-of-public-safety-and-emergency-preparedness-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-borders-task-force .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-73
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2006-05-31

Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Department of
Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness the Control and Supervision of
the Borders Task Force

P.C. 2006-360 2006-05-10
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA} of the [Public
Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34),
hereby transfers from the Privy Council Office to the Department of
Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness the control and supervision of
the portion of the federal public administration within the Privy
Council Office known as the Borders Task Force, effective June 1, 2006.
