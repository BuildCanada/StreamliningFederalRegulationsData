P.C. 1993-2195 1993-12-29

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 63.1(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1){#fn_1-ID0EIACA} of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_2){#fn_2-ID0EFACA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed Regulations for determining the country of origin of
goods imported from a NAFTA country for the purpose of specifying that
certain goods be marked, effective on the day on which section 133 of
[An Act to implement the North American Free Trade
Agreement](/eng/acts/N-23.8), being chapter 44 of the Statutes of
Canada, 1993, comes into force.

## [Marking]{.HTitleText1} {#h-962632 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Goods
  set out in Schedule I that are imported from a CUSMA country shall be

## [Determination of Country of Origin]{.HTitleText1} {#h-962639 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  country of origin of goods is the country in which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods are wholly obtained or produced;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods are produced exclusively from domestic
    materials;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} each of the foreign materials incorporated into the
    goods undergoes an applicable change in tariff classification and
    satisfies any other applicable requirements of these Regulations; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a good is considered to originate under a Chapter
    Note set out in Schedule III.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(a), goods are wholly
  obtained or produced in a country if the goods are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} mineral goods extracted in that country;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} vegetables or other goods harvested in that
    country;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} live animals born and raised in that country;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} goods obtained from hunting, trapping or fishing in
    that country;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} fish, shellfish or other marine life taken from the
    sea by vessels registered or recorded with that country and flying
    its flag;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} goods produced on board factory ships from goods
    referred to in paragraph (e), where the factory ship is registered
    or recorded with that country and is flying its flag;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} goods taken by that country or by a person of that
    country, from or beneath the seabed outside territorial waters,
    where that country has the right to exploit that seabed;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} goods taken from outer space, where the goods are
    obtained by that country or by a person of that country;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} waste and scrap derived from

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} production in that country, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} used goods collected in that country, where
      those used goods are fit only for the recovery of raw materials;
      or

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} goods produced in that country exclusively from
    goods referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to (i), or from
    derivatives of such goods, at any stage of production.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-447, s. 1
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  in the case of goods that are described in the schedule to the Act as
  a set or are classified as a set pursuant to Rule 3 of the General
  Rules, where the country of origin of goods cannot be determined under
  section 4, the country or countries of origin of the goods shall be
  the country or countries of origin of the single material that imparts
  the essential character of the goods.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the single material that imparts the essential
  character of the goods is a fungible material and has been commingled
  so that direct physical identification of the country or countries of
  origin of each fungible material is not practical, the country or
  countries of origin of that material shall be determined, at the
  choice of the importer of the goods, under subsection (1) or on the
  basis of an inventory management method set out in Part 1 of Schedule
  8 to the [CUSMA Rules of Origin
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-155).

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where the country or
countries of origin of goods cannot be determined under section 4 or 5
and the goods are described in the schedule to the Act as a set or
mixture, or are classified as a set or mixture or as composite goods
pursuant to Rule 3 of the General Rules, the country or countries of
origin of the goods shall be the country or countries of origin of all
the materials that merit equal consideration as imparting the essential
character of the goods.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where the country or
countries of origin of goods cannot be determined under any of sections
4 to 6, the country or countries of origin of the goods shall be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the goods are produced by only minor processing,
  the country or countries of origin of all the materials that merit
  equal consideration as imparting the essential character of the goods;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the production of the goods is by simple assembly
  and the parts that merit equal consideration as imparting the
  essential character of the goods have the same country of origin, the
  country of origin of those parts; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the last country in which the
  goods underwent production.

## [Tariff Preference Override]{.HTitleText1} {#h-962676 .Part}

origin of the goods is not determined to be a single CUSMA country under

CUSMA country in which the goods underwent production, other than minor
processing, if a Certificate of Origin under the [Proof of Origin of

## [Production Outside Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-962680 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Where the country of origin

7 and the goods have undergone production, other than minor processing,

underwent that production.

## [Fungible Goods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-962684 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), where two
  or more fungible goods have different countries of origin and have
  been commingled, all of the countries of origin of those fungible
  goods shall be the countries of origin of those commingled goods.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where fungible goods have been commingled so that
  direct physical identification of the countries of origin of the goods
  is not practical, the country or countries of origin of each of the
  fungible goods shall be determined, at the choice of the importer of
  the goods, under subsection (1) or on the basis of an inventory
  management method set out in Part 2 of Schedule 8 to the [CUSMA Rules

## [De Minimis]{.HTitleText1} {#h-962690 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Foreign materials that are
  incorporated into goods and do not undergo an applicable change in
  tariff classification or satisfy any other applicable requirements of
  these Regulations shall be disregarded in determining the country or
  countries of origin of the goods

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods classified under any of
    Chapters 50 to 63 of the List of Tariff Provisions, if the combined
    weight of the foreign materials does not exceed 7% of the total
    weight of the goods; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods classified under any other
    chapter of the List of Tariff Provisions, other than under any of
    Chapters 1 to 4, 6 to 8, 11, 12, 15, 17 and 20, if the value of the
    foreign materials is not more than 7% of the value of the goods, or
    10% where the goods are classified under Chapter 22 of that List.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of this section, the value of
  materials is, at the choice of the importer of the goods,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their [[value for duty]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
    defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6),
    except that for the purpose of determining that value, the reference
    in section 55 of that Act to "in accordance with regulations made
    under the [Currency Act](/eng/acts/C-52)" shall be read as a
    reference to "in accordance with subsection 2(1) of the [CUSMA Rules
    of Origin Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-155)"; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} their value determined in accordance with Schedule
    6 to the [CUSMA Rules of Origin
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-155), with any modifications
    that the circumstances require.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of this section, the value of any
  goods is,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the importer of the goods has chosen that the
    value of the materials incorporated into the goods be determined
    under paragraph (2)(a), their [[value for
    duty]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
    [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), except that for the purpose of
    determining that value, the reference in section 55 of that Act to
    "in accordance with regulations made under the [Currency
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52)" shall be read as a reference to "in accordance
    with subsection 2(1) of the [CUSMA Rules of Origin
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-155)"; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the importer of the goods has chosen that the
    value of the materials incorporated into the goods be determined
    under paragraph (2)(b), their value determined in accordance with
    Schedule 6 to the [CUSMA Rules of Origin
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-155), with any modifications
    that the circumstances require, as though they were a material.

## [Change in Tariff Classification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-962703 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following materials shall be disregarded in determining whether goods
  undergo an applicable change in tariff classification or satisfy any
  other applicable requirements of these Regulations:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2013-100, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} accessories, spare parts or tools that are
    delivered, classified and shipped with the goods;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} packing materials and containers in which the goods
    are packed for shipment; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} indirect materials.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, an applicable change in tariff
  classification includes a change to goods from other goods that are
  classified under the same tariff provision, where such a change is
  specified in a rule set out in Schedule III for the tariff provision

## [Non-qualifying Operations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-962713 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A foreign material
incorporated into goods shall not be considered to have undergone an
applicable change in tariff classification or to satisfy any other
applicable requirements of these Regulations by reason of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the change from one tariff classification to any
  other merely as the result of a change in the end use of the goods;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the change from one tariff classification to any
  other merely as the result of the dismantling or disassembly of the
  goods;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2013-100, s. 5\]]{.Repealed}

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the mere dilution with water or any other substance
  that does not materially alter the characteristics of the material; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the mere collection of parts so that the collection
  of parts is classified as if it were an assembled good pursuant to
  Rule 2(a) of the General Rules.

- SOR/95-447, s. 1
- SOR/2013-100, s. 5
