## Order Transferring to the Department of Citizenship and Immigration the Control and Supervision of Certain Portions within the Canada Border Services Agency and Transferring from the Deputy Minister and Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration Certain Powers, Duties and Functions {#order-transferring-to-the-department-of-citizenship-and-immigration-the-control-and-supervision-of-certain-portions-within-the-canada-border-services-agency-and-transferring-from-the-deputy-minister-and-minister-of-public-safety-and-emergency-preparedness-to-the-minister-of-citizenship-and-immigration-certain-powers-duties-and-functions .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2004-135
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2004-10-20

Order Transferring to the Department of Citizenship and Immigration the
Control and Supervision of Certain Portions within the Canada Border
Services Agency and Transferring from the Deputy Minister and Minister
of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to the Minister of
Citizenship and Immigration Certain Powers, Duties and Functions

P.C. 2004-1154 2004-10-08
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} transfers to the Department of Citizenship and
  Immigration the control and supervision of the portions of the public
  service within the Canada Border Services Agency that carry out
  pre-removal risk assessments and that were transferred pursuant to
  Order in Council P.C. 2003-2063 of December 12, 2003[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea){#footnotea-ID0EBBBAA}; and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea-ID0EBBBAA){#footnotea}SI/2003-215
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} transfers from the Solicitor General of Canada,
  styled Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Public Safety and
  Emergency Preparedness to the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration
  the powers, duties and functions provided for in subsection 77(1) of
  the [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) that
  were transferred pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 2003-2063 of
  December 12, 2003[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea){#footnotea-ID0EBAAAA}.
