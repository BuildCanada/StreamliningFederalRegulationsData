## Order Designating the President of Shared Services Canada as Deputy Head in Respect of that Entity {#order-designating-the-president-of-shared-services-canada-as-deputy-head-in-respect-of-that-entity .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2011-68
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2011-08-17

Order Designating the President of Shared Services Canada as Deputy Head
in Respect of that Entity

P.C. 2011-880 2011-08-03
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 11(2)(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EECAA} of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBCAA}, hereby designates the
President of Shared Services Canada as deputy head in respect of that
entity, effective August 4, 2011.
