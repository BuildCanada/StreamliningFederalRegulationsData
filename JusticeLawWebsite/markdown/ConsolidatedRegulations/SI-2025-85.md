## Order Designating the Minister of Foreign Affairs as the Minister for the Purposes of the Export and Import Permits Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-foreign-affairs-as-the-minister-for-the-purposes-of-the-export-and-import-permits-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-85
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

Registration 2025-06-04

Order Designating the Minister of Foreign Affairs as the Minister for
the Purposes of the Export and Import Permits Act

P.C. 2025-496 2025-05-13
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under the definition of
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) of the [Export and
Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2925_hq_1741884081){#fn_81000-3-2925_hq_1741884081-ID0EBDAA},

  E-19
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2025-430 of March 14,
  2025[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2925_hq_1741884029){#fn_81000-3-2925_hq_1741884029-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2925_hq_1741884029-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_81000-3-2925_hq_1741884029}SI/2025-49

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of Foreign Affairs, a member
  of the King's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the
  purposes of the [Export and Import Permits
  Act](/eng/acts/E-19)[[Footnote
