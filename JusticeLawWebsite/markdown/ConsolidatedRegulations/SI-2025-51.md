## Order Designating the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons To Be the Minister Referred to in the Federal Public Sector Labour Relations and Employment Board Act and as the Minister for the Purposes of the Federal Public Sector Labour Relations Act {#order-designating-the-leader-of-the-government-in-the-house-of-commons-to-be-the-minister-referred-to-in-the-federal-public-sector-labour-relations-and-employment-board-act-and-as-the-minister-for-the-purposes-of-the-federal-public-sector-labour-relations-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-51
:::

[FEDERAL PUBLIC SECTOR LABOUR RELATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.3)

[FEDERAL PUBLIC SECTOR LABOUR RELATIONS AND EMPLOYMENT BOARD
ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.35)

Registration 2025-03-26

Order Designating the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons
To Be the Minister Referred to in the Federal Public Sector Labour
Relations and Employment Board Act and as the Minister for the Purposes
of the Federal Public Sector Labour Relations Act

P.C. 2025-432 2025-03-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2025-87 of February 12,
  2025[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2614_hq_1741743279){#fn_81000-3-2614_hq_1741743279-ID0EBBCAA};

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2614_hq_1741743279-ID0EBBCAA){#fn_81000-3-2614_hq_1741743279}SI/2025-17
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designates, under section 3 of the [Federal Public
  Sector Labour Relations and Employment Board
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.35)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2614_hq_1741743327){#fn_81000-3-2614_hq_1741743327-ID0EBBBAA},
  the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons, who is not a
  member of the Treasury Board, to be the Minister referred to in that
  Act; and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2614_hq_1741743327-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_81000-3-2614_hq_1741743327}S.C.
    2013, c. 40, s. 365; S.C. 2017, c. 9, s. 36
    :::

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} designates, under the definition
  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2‍(1) of the [Federal
  Public Sector Labour Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-33.3)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2614_hq_1741743361){#fn_81000-3-2614_hq_1741743361-ID0EBBAAA},
  the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons, who is a member
  of the King's Privy Council for Canada and is not a member of the
  Treasury Board, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2614_hq_1741743361-ID0EBBAAA){#fn_81000-3-2614_hq_1741743361}S.C.
    2003, c. 22, s. 2; S.C. 2017, c. 9, s. 2
    :::
:::
