## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1029026 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Canadian government film agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian government film agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    federal or provincial  agency engaged in the development and
    production of cinematographic works. ([organisme cinématographique
    gouvernemental canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canadian program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canadian program as
    defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Pay Television Regulations,
    1990](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-105), in section 2 of the [Specialty
    Services Regulations, 1990](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-106), and in
    section 2 of the [Television Broadcasting Regulations,
    1987](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-49). ([émission
    canadienne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prescribed Cinematographic Works]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1029030 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following are
prescribed cinematographic works for the purposes of section 17 of the
[Copyright Act](/eng/acts/C-42), namely, a cinematographic work in which
a performer's performance has been embodied as a result of an agreement
entered into by the performer on or after April 22, 1999:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which the Minister of Canadian Heritage
  has issued a Canadian film or video production certificate under the
  [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is recognized as a Canadian program by the
  Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} that has received production funding from Telefilm
  Canada, or other Canadian government film agency.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2002\. c. 17, s. 15
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1029037 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on April 22, 1999.
