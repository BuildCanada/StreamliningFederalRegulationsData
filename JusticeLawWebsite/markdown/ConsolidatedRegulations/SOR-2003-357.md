## Fruit and Vegetable Remission Order, 2003 {#fruit-and-vegetable-remission-order-2003 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-357
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 2003-11-06

Fruit and Vegetable Remission Order, 2003

P.C. 2003-1777 2003-11-06
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 115 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Fruit and Vegetable Remission Order,
2003](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-357).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1997, c.
  36
  :::
:::

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706566 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 2,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) by or on behalf of the company
listed in Column I of an item of the attached schedule up to the amount
listed in Column IV, in respect of the product listed in Column II, if
the product was imported for processing during the year listed in Column
III.

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706568 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
the condition that a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
National Revenue no later than December 31, 2005.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706570 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
