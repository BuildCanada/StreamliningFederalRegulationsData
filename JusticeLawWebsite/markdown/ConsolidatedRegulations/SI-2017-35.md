## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-631194 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted to any
foreign national who meets the conditions set out in section 2 of the
fees paid under subsection 298(1), 299(1) or 306(1) of the [Immigration
and Refugee Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227) for
the processing of an application for, respectively,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a temporary resident permit issued under subsection
  24(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a work permit referred to in paragraph 208(b) of
  those Regulations; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the restoration of their temporary resident status
  under section 182 of those Regulations.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-631199 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The conditions are that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the foreign national applied for a work permit
  referred to in subparagraph 205(c)(ii) of those Regulations that was,
  between September 1, 2014, and March 15, 2016, refused on the basis of
  a determination that most of their coursework was completed via
  distance learning, which refusal was made without taking into account
  the entirety of their program of study, including transfer credits;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the foreign national applied for a temporary resident
  permit that was refused in the period that began on the day on which
  the application for their work permit referred to in paragraph (a) was
  refused and ended on September 27, 2016; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the foreign national made an application for a
  work permit referred to in paragraph (1)(b) or to have their temporary
  resident status referred to in paragraph (1)(c) restored, the
  application was made in conjunction with the application for the
  temporary resident permit referred to in paragraph (b) and was refused
  on or before September 27, 2016.
