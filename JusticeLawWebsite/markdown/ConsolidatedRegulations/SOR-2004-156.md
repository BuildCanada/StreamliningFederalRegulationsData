## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-711972 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the International Criminal Court
    adopted under the Rome Statute by the Assembly.
    ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Assembly]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Assembly]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Assembly of States
    Parties established under Article 112 of the Rome Statute.
    ([Assemblée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in Schedule
    III to the [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[International Criminal Court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[International Criminal Court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    International Criminal Court established by the Rome Statute. ([Cour
    pénale internationale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Rome Statute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Rome Statute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned to
    that term in subsection 2(1) of the [Crimes Against Humanity and War
    Crimes Act](/eng/acts/C-45.9). ([Statut de Rome]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-711979 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  International Criminal Court shall have in Canada the legal capacities
  of a body corporate and, to the extent specified in the Agreement, the
  privileges and immunities set out in Articles II and III of the
  Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), representatives of a
  foreign state that is a member of or that participates in the Assembly
  shall have in Canada, to the extent specified in the Agreement, the
  privileges and immunities set out in Article IV of the Convention.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), judges, officials and
  staff of the International Criminal Court and counsel, experts,
  witnesses and other persons required to be present at the seat of that
  Court shall have the privileges and immunities set out in Article 48
  of the Rome Statute and in the Agreement.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} This Order does not exempt permanent residents of
  Canada and Canadian citizens from liability for taxes or duties
  imposed by any law in Canada.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-711985 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
