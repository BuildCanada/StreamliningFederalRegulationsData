## Order Specifying the National Capital Region as the Place in Which Shall be Located the Head Office of Certain Museums {#order-specifying-the-national-capital-region-as-the-place-in-which-shall-be-located-the-head-office-of-certain-museums .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/90-89
:::

[MUSEUMS ACT](/eng/acts/M-13.4)

Registration 1990-07-18

Order Specifying the National Capital Region as the Place in Which Shall
be Located the Head Office of Certain Museums

P.C. 1990-1353  1990-06-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 25 of the [Museums
Act](/eng/acts/M-13.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-90-89_e_hq_6468){#fn_SI-90-89_e_hq_6468-ID0EDGAA},
is pleased hereby to specify the National Capital Region, as described
in the schedule to the [National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4), as the
place in which shall be located the head office of each of the following
museums, namely,

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-90-89_e_hq_6468-ID0EDGAA){#fn_SI-90-89_e_hq_6468}S.C.
  1990, c. 3
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the National Gallery of Canada,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Museum of Civilization,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Museum of Nature, and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the National Museum of Science and Technology,

effective July 1, 1990.
:::
