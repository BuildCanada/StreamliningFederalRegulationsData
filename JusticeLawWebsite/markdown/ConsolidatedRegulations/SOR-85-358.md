Regulations respecting zoning at Oshawa Airport

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886264 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Oshawa Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-358).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886266 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Oshawa Airport in the
      vicinity of Oshawa in the Regional Municipality of Durham, in the
      Province of Ontario; ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the imaginary
      inclined plane more particularly described in Part II of the
      schedule that extends upward and outward from each end of each
      strip associated with runways designated 12-30 and 04-22 of the
      airport; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport including the runway prepared for the
      take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction, which
      strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the schedule;
      ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the assigned
  elevation of the airport reference point is 135 m above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886277 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, which lands are more particularly described in
Part VI of the schedule.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886279 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct, on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surface of any strip;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surface.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886284 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in paragraphs 4(a) to (c), the Minister
may make a direction that the owner or occupier of the land on which
that object is growing remove the excessive growth.

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-886286 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit that land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste that is edible by or
attractive to birds.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point, shown on Oshawa Airport Zoning Plan
No. 18-013-80-58, Sheet 5, dated June 28, 1984, is a point distant
49.13 m measured southerly at right angles to the centre line of runway
12-30 from a point distant 518.62 m measured westerly along the said
centre line from the easterly end of said runway.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Oshawa Airport Zoning Plan
No. 18-013-80-58, Sheets 1 to 13, dated June 28, 1984, are surfaces
abutting each end of the strips associated with the runways designated
12-30 and 04-22 and are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 12 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 30 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 04 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 2 500 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 325 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 50 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 22 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip distant 2 500 m measured horizontally from
  the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal line
  being 325 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary horizontal
  line being 50 m measured vertically above the elevation at the end of
  the strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Oshawa Airport Zoning Plan No. 18-013-80-58,
Sheets 1 to 9 inclusive, dated June 28, 1984, is an imaginary surface
located at a common plane established at a constant elevation of 45 m
above the assigned elevation of the airport reference point except where
that common plane is less than 9 m above the surface of the ground, the
imaginary surface is located at 9 m above the surface of the ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strips]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strips .SchedHeadL1}

The strips, as shown on Oshawa Airport Zoning Plan No. 18-013-80-58,
Sheet 5, dated June 28, 1984, are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 12-30 being 300 m in
  width, 150 m on each side of the centre line of the runway and 1 416 m
  in length; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 04-22 being 150 m in
  width, 75 m each side of the centre line of the runway, and 1 050 m in
  length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Oshawa Airport Zoning Plan
No. 18-013-80-58, Sheets 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 and 8, dated June 28, 1984, is a
surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at a ratio of 1 m
measured vertically to 7 m measured horizontally at right angles to the
centre line and the centre line produced of each strip extending upward
and outward from the lateral limits of each strip and its approach
surfaces to an intersection with the outer surface or another
transitional surface of an adjoining strip.
