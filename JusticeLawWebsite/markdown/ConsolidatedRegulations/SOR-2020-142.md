Income Support Payment (Number of Weeks) Regulations

## [Maximum Number of Weeks]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1251887 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}28 weeks

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 8(1) of the [Canada Emergency Response Benefit
Act](/eng/acts/C-3.7), the maximum number of weeks for which income
support payments may be made to a worker is 28 weeks.
