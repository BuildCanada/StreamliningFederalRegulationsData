## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765314 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission of customs duties
paid under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) is granted to the
following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Algoma Central Corporation, St. Catharines, Ontario,
  in respect of the tankers *Algonova* and *AlgoCanada;* and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} British Columbia Ferry Services Inc., Victoria,
  British Columbia, in respect of the vessels *Coastal Renaissance*,
  *Coastal Inspiration*, *Coastal Celebration* and *Northern
  Expedition*.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765318 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the importer files all evidence that is required by
  the Canada Border Services Agency to determine eligibility for
  remission; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made by the importer to the
  Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years
  after the day on which this Order comes into force.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765322 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
