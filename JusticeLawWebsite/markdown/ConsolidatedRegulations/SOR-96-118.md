## Canada Oil and Gas Installations Regulations {#canada-oil-and-gas-installations-regulations .Title-of-Act}

:::

[CANADA OIL AND GAS OPERATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/O-7)

Registration 1996-02-13

Regulations Respecting Oil and Gas Installations Used in Areas of Canada
Under the Canada Oil and Gas Operations Act

P.C. 1996-167  1996-02-13
::::

:::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 15(1) of the [Canada Oil and Gas
Operations Act](/eng/acts/O-7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-96-118_e_hq_4491){#fn_SOR-96-118_e_hq_4491-ID0EFBBA},
a copy of proposed [Regulations respecting oil and gas installations
used in areas of Canada under the Canada Oil and Gas Operations
Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-118), substantially in the form annexed
hereto, was published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/)
Part I on May 14, 1994 and interested persons were afforded an
opportunity to make representations with respect thereto;

  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Natural Resources and the Minister of
Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to section
14[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-96-118_e_hq_4492){#fn_SOR-96-118_e_hq_4492-ID0EGBAA}
of the [Canada Oil and Gas Operations Act](/eng/acts/O-7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-96-118_e_hq_4491){#fn_SOR-96-118_e_hq_4491-ID0EDBAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting oil and
gas installations used in areas of Canada under the Canada Oil and Gas
Operations Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-118).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977812 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Canada Oil and Gas Installations
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-118).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977814 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[accidental event]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[accidental event]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an unplanned or
      unexpected event or circumstance or series of events or
      circumstances that may lead to loss of life or damage to the
      environment; ([événement accidentel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[accommodation area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[accommodation area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means dependent
      personnel accommodation or an accommodation installation ([secteur
      d'habitation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[accommodation installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[accommodation installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      installation that is used to accommodate persons at a production
      site or drill site and that functions independently of a
      production installation, drilling installation or diving
      installation, and includes any associated dependent diving system;
      ([installation d'habitation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Oil and Gas
      Operations Act](/eng/acts/O-7); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[certificate of fitness]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[certificate of fitness]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a certificate
      issued by a certifying authority in accordance with section 4 of
      the [Canada Oil and Gas Certificate of Fitness
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-114); ([certificat de
      conformité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[certifying authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[certifying authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
      as in section 2 of the [Canada Oil and Gas Certificate of Fitness
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-114);
      ([autorité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Chief]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Chief]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chief Safety Officer;
      ([délégué]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[classification society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[classification society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      independent organization whose purpose is to supervise the
      construction, ongoing maintenance and any modifications of an
      offshore platform in accordance with the society's rules for
      classing offshore platforms and includes the American Bureau of
      Shipping, Lloyd's Register of Shipping, Det norske Veritas
      Classification A/S and Bureau Veritas; ([société de
      classification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[contingency plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[contingency plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a plan that
      addresses abnormal conditions or emergencies that can reasonably
      be anticipated; ([plan d'urgence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[control point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[control point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a work area other than
      a control station from which systems and equipment critical to the
      safety of the installation can be monitored and controlled;
      ([poste de commande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[control station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[control station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a continuously
      manned work area from which process and export equipment, wellhead
      manifold and christmas trees, main and emergency power, fire and
      gas detection, fire control, communications equipment, emergency
      shutdown systems, ballast control system, dynamic positioning
      systems and other systems and equipment critical to the safety of
      the installation are remotely controlled or monitored; ([salle de
      commande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[damaged condition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[damaged condition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, with respect to a
      floating platform, the condition of the platform after it has
      suffered damage to the extent described in the Code referred to in
      subsection 57(9); ([condition avariée]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[dependent diving system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[dependent diving system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a diving
      system that is associated with an installation other than a diving
      installation and that does not function independently of the
      installation; ([système de plongée non autonome]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[dependent personnel accommodation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[dependent personnel accommodation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      personnel accommodation that is associated with an installation
      other than an accommodation installation and that does not
      function independently of the installation; ([logement du
      personnel connexe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[development plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[development plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a development plan
      relating to the development of a pool or field that is referred to
      in section 5.1 of the Act; ([plan de mise en
      valeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[development plan approval]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[development plan approval]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      approval of a development plan pursuant to section 5.1 of the Act;
      ([approbation de plan de mise en valeur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[diving installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[diving installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a diving system
      and any associated vessel that function independently of an
      accommodation installation, production installation or drilling
      installation; ([installation de plongée]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Diving Program Authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Diving Program Authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      authorization to conduct a diving program that is issued to an
      operator pursuant to paragraph 5(1)(b) of the Act; ([autorisation
      de programme de plongée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[diving system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[diving system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the plant or equipment
      used in or in connection with a diving operation, and includes the
      plant and equipment that are essential to a diver or to a pilot of
      a manned submersible; ([système de plongée]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[drilling base]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[drilling base]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the stable foundation
      on which a drilling rig is installed, and includes the ground
      surface, an artificial island, an ice platform, a platform fixed
      to the ground or seafloor and any other foundation specially
      constructed for drilling operations; ([base de
      forage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[drilling installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[drilling installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a drilling
      unit or a drilling rig and its associated drilling base, and
      includes any associated dependent diving system; ([installation de
      forage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Drilling Program Authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Drilling Program Authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      authorization to conduct a drilling program that is issued to a
      person pursuant to paragraph 5(l)(b) of the Act; ([autorisation de
      programme de forage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[drilling rig]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[drilling rig]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the plant used to make
      a well by boring or other means, and includes a derrick,
      draw-works, rotary table, mud pump, blowout preventer,
      accumulator, choke manifold, dependent personnel accommodation and
      other associated equipment, including power, control and
      monitoring systems; ([appareil de forage]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[drilling unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[drilling unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a drillship,
      submersible, semi-submersible, barge, jack-up or other vessel that
      is used in a drilling program and is fitted with a drilling rig,
      and includes other facilities related to drilling and marine
      activities that are installed on a vessel or platform; ([unité de
      forage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[drill site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[drill site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a location where a
      drilling rig is or is proposed to be installed; ([emplacement de
      forage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[environmental load]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[environmental load]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a load imposed by

      those phenomena; ([charge environnementale]{.DefinedTermLink

  :   [[floating platform]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a

  :   [[flowline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pipeline that is used to

  :   [[hazardous area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area classified as

  :   [[installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a diving installation,

  [[offshore production site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[General Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977881 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-977882 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of ensuring
the safety of an installation, no operator shall use the installation
unless the equipment on the installation is arranged in accordance with
these Regulations to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide for the safety of personnel;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} minimize damage to the environment; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} enable easy access to the equipment.

### [Quality Assurance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-977887 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  new installation shall be designed, constructed, installed and
  commissioned in accordance with a quality assurance program that
  complies with subsection (2) and that is selected in accordance with
  Canadian Standards Association CAN3-Z299.0-86, Guide for Selecting and
  Implementing the CAN3-Z299-85 Quality Program Standards.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A quality assurance program shall be developed in
  accordance with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Canadian Standards Association CAN3-Z299.l-85,
    Quality Assurance Program --- Category 1;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Canadian Standards Association CAN3-Z299.2-85,
    Quality Control Program --- Category 2;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Canadian Standards Association CAN3-Z299.3-85,
    Quality Verification Program --- Category 3; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Canadian Standards Association CAN3-Z299.4-85,
    Inspection Program --- Category 4.

### [Helicopter Deck]{.HTitleText2} {#h-977895 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  helicopter deck or facility that forms part of an offshore
  installation shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} conform to Transport Canada TP 4414, Guidelines
    Respecting Helicopter Facilities on Ships; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be equipped so that any fuel stored on or adjacent
    to the helicopter deck or to the accommodation areas

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} can be jettisoned by action taken at another
      location on the installation, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is protected against damage or impact.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every helicopter deck that forms part of an offshore
  installation shall be in a location that is readily accessible to and
  from the dependent personnel accommodation of the installation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every helicopter facility that forms part of an
  onshore installation shall conform to Transport Canada TP2586E,
  Heliport and Helideck Standards and Recommended Practices.

### [Facilities for Inspection and Maintenance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-977904 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An offshore installation
shall be designed and equipped in such a manner as to allow for the
monitoring, maintenance and periodic inspection of the installation,
including

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} clear marking and identification of the areas to be
  inspected;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provision for safe access to and adequate inspection
  space for the areas to be inspected;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} space for the storage and operation of diving
  equipment;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} means to facilitate the work of divers, where
  inspection by divers is required;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} means to assist maintenance personnel, including
  those doing underwater maintenance, to perform their work safely and
  effectively; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a mobile offshore platform that is not
  intended to be periodically drydocked, means to facilitate on-
  location inspection of the hull.

### [Secondary Structures and Fittings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-977912 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** All decks, deckhouses,
skids, modules and other structures located or installed on an offshore
installation shall be capable of withstanding all the loads and forces
to which they will be subjected, as determined in accordance with
section 45.

### [Arrangements of Materials and Equipment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-977914 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  section

  [[flame-type equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[flame-type equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any electric or
      fired heating equipment that uses an open flame, electric arc or
      element, and includes a space heater, a torch, a heated process
      vessel, a boiler, an electric arc or an open flame welder, or an
      open element electric heater or appliance; ([équipement de type à
      flamme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[process vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[process vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a heater, dehydrator,
      separator, treater or vessel used in the processing or treatment
      of produced gas or oil. ([récipient de
      fabrication]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall create or cause to be created any
  unprotected flame or source of ignition within 50 m of a well, an oil
  storage tank or other source of ignitable vapour.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No oil storage tank shall be placed or remain within
  50 m of an onshore well.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No flame-type equipment shall be placed or operated
  within 25 m of a well, an oil storage tank or other source of
  ignitable vapour, except

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the well is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a water supply well, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a water injection well equipped with a suitable
      packer and with the surface casing annulus vented outside any
      building; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where emergency work requires the use of flame-type
    equipment and the wellhead valves and blow-out preventer, if any,
    are closed.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No flame-type equipment shall be placed or operated
  within 25 m of a process vessel, unless the flame type equipment is
  fitted with an adequate flame arrester.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} No flame-type equipment shall be located in the same
  building as a process vessel or other source of ignitable vapour,
  unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the air intakes and flues of all burners are
    located outside the building;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} relief valves, safety heads and other sources of
    ignitable vapours are vented outside the building and discharged
    above roof level; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the building is adequately cross-ventilated.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} All process vessels and equipment from which
  ignitable vapour may issue shall be vented to the atmosphere, and all
  vent lines from every storage tank that is vented to flare pits or
  flare stacks shall be provided with flame arresters or other
  equivalent safety devices.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} An exhaust pipe from an internal combustion engine
  located within 25 m of a well, a process vessel, an oil storage tank
  or other source of ignitable vapour shall be constructed so that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any emergence of flame along its length or at its
    end is prevented; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the end is at least 6 m from the vertical centre
    line of the well projected upward and shall be directed away from
    the well.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} All equipment at or near a well, a process vessel, an
  oil storage tank or other source of ignitable vapour shall be
  constructed in accordance with Part I of the Canadian Electrical Code
  Part I and the Oil and Gas Occupational Health and Safety Regulations.

### [Access to Hazardous Areas]{.HTitleText2} {#h-977936 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), there shall not be direct access or any opening in
  an installation between

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a non-hazardous area and a hazardous area; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a Class I, Division 2, hazardous area and a Class
    I, Division 1, hazardous area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (3) to (5), an enclosed area
  that has direct access to, and that is classified as less hazardous
  than, a Class I, Division 1, hazardous area or a Class I, Division 2,
  hazardous area shall be considered to have the same classification as
  the area to which it has direct access.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An enclosed area that has direct access to a Class I,
  Division 1, area shall be considered to be a Class I, Division 2,
  hazardous area if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the access is fitted with a self-closing gastight
    door that opens into the enclosed area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the door is open, the air flows from the
    enclosed area into the Class I, Division 1, hazardous area.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} An enclosed area shall not be considered to be a
  hazardous area because of its direct access to a Class I, Division 2,
  hazardous area if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the access is fitted with a self-closing gastight
    door that opens into the enclosed area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the door is open, the air flows from the
    enclosed area into the Class I, Division 2, hazardous area.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} An enclosed area shall not be considered to be a
  hazardous area because of its direct access to a Class I, Division 1,
  hazardous area if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the access is fitted with self-closing gastight
    doors forming an air-lock; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the enclosed area is maintained at a pressure that
    is higher than the pressure maintained in the Class I, Division 1,
    hazardous area.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Piping systems on an installation shall be designed
  to preclude direct communications between hazardous areas of different
  classifications and between hazardous and non-hazardous areas.

### [Ventilation of Hazardous Areas]{.HTitleText2} {#h-977952 .Subheading}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  enclosed hazardous area on an installation shall be ventilated.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The ventilation systems fitted on an offshore
  installation for the purpose of subsection (1) shall be capable of
  replacing the air in the hazardous area at the rate of once every five
  minutes.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a mechanical ventilation system is used for the
  purpose of subsection (1), the air in the enclosed hazardous area
  shall be maintained at a pressure that is lower than the pressure of
  each adjacent hazardous area that is classified as less hazardous.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} All air let into an enclosed hazardous area shall be
  taken from a non-hazardous area, and where the inlet duct passes
  through a hazardous area classified as more hazardous than the one to
  which the duct leads, the air in the inlet duct shall be maintained at
  a pressure that is higher than the pressure of the air in the
  hazardous area through which it passes.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} All air let out of an enclosed hazardous area shall
  be let into an outdoor area that would be classified as the same as or
  less hazardous than the enclosed hazardous area if it did not receive
  the air from the enclosed hazardous area.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The ventilation system for every non-hazardous area
  shall be separate from the ventilation system for every hazardous
  area, and the ventilation fan inlets and outlets shall be arranged to
  prevent the air from a hazardous area from moving, as a result of the
  operation of any fan or the wind, into an area classified as less
  hazardous.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Every ventilation outlet duct leading from a
  non-hazardous area where drilling or production operations are
  conducted to a Class I, Division 2, hazardous area shall be equipped
  with self-closing shutters and a gas detector.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} A differential pressure gauge shall be installed to
  monitor any loss of ventilation pressure differential required by
  subsection (3) or (4) or maintained under section 9, and to activate
  audible and visual alarms at the appropriate control point after a
  suitable period of delay not exceeding 30 seconds if a loss occurs.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} The control station and all accommodation areas on an
  installation shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be maintained at a positive overpressure relative
    to atmospheric pressure; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have airlock arrangements on all external doors.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} The power for a mechanical ventilation system
  provided in accommodation areas, working areas, flammable liquid
  storage areas and other hazardous locations of an installation shall
  be capable of being shut off from the control station and from a
  position that is outside the area being served by the ventilation
  system and that will remain accessible during any fire that may occur
  within the area being ventilated.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} The main inlets and outlets of all ventilation
  systems shall be capable of being closed from a position that is
  outside the area being served by the ventilation system and that will
  remain accessible during any fire that may occur within the area being
  ventilated.

### [Mechanical Equipment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978037 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  internal combustion engine on an installation shall be installed,
  maintained and operated in accordance with American Petroleum
  Institute RP 7C-llF, Recommended Practice for Installation,
  Maintenance and Operation of Internal-Combustion Engines.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Combustion air for every internal combustion engine
  and fired vessel shall be taken from non-hazardous areas.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Exhaust gas from every internal combustion engine and
  fired vessel shall be discharged to non-hazardous areas.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The air induction system of every diesel engine
  operating in a hazardous area shall be equipped with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a flame arrester in the induction system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a shut-off valve that is located between the engine
    air inlet filter and the induction system flame arrester and that is
    capable of being closed automatically by the engine overspeeding
    device and manually;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a flame arrester in the exhaust system; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a spark arrester in the exhaust system, downstream
    of the flame arrester.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (13), the fuel supply system
  for every diesel engine shall be equipped with a manual shut-off
  device and, except for the emergency source of electrical power
  required by section 12, with a device that will automatically shut off
  the fuel supply if any of the following occur:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} overspeeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} high exhaust temperature;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} high cooling water temperature; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} low lubricating oil pressure.

  engine shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be equipped with a flame arrester; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an engine in an enclosed Class I,
    Division 2, hazardous area, lead to the atmosphere outside the
    installation.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Basic operating instructions for every diesel engine
  shall give details of stop, start and emergency procedures and be
  permanently attached to the engine.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The layout of every gas turbine, including the
  location of the control points, shall take into account the ability of
  the control point closest to the turbine to withstand pressure waves
  in the event of an explosion in the gas turbine exhaust duct or gas

  where the fragments cannot be contained.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Every gas turbine shall have, in addition to the

  be exceeded by more than 15 per cent.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} The air intakes and exhaust for every gas turbine
  shall be arranged to prevent, to the extent practicable, reingestion
  of combustion gases.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} Every multi-engine gas turbine shall have a separate
  air intake and exhaust, arranged so as to prevent induced circulation
  through a stopped turbine.

- [(12)]{.lawlabel} All machinery, components and systems essential to
  the operation of a floating platform shall be designed to function at
  full rated power at any inclination, up to a maximum angle of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a surface mobile offshore platform,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 15 degrees in any direction under static
      conditions,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 22½ degrees in any direction under rolling
      dynamic conditions, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 7½ degrees by bow or stern under rolling
      dynamic conditions;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a column-stabilized mobile offshore platform,
    15 degrees in any direction; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a self-elevating platform, 10 degrees in any
    direction.

- [(13)]{.lawlabel} The automatic shut-off device referred to in
  subsection (5) shall shut off the fuel supply to engines associated
  with fire pump systems only where overspeeding occurs.

- [(14)]{.lawlabel} Jacking mechanisms for self-elevating platforms
  shall, where possible, be arranged with redundancy so that a single
  failure of any component does not cause an uncontrolled descent of the
  platform.

### [Winterization]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978069 .Subheading}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  installation shall be designed, constructed, equipped and insulated to
  ensure that, at the minimum air temperature that may occur at the

  probability of exceedance of 10^-2^,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the emergency shutdown system referred to in

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} drilling safety systems and associated equipment
    will operate safely and in accordance with the manufacturer's
    specifications;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the fluids in the following systems and components

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} fresh water tanks and the associated piping,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} vent pipes,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} components of the drainage system,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the hydraulic system and its components,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the firefighting system, including pump drives
      and fuel supply lines, fire pumps and associated piping, fire
      hydrants, fire hoses and nozzles;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} every pneumatic control system will remain fully
    operational at all times;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the lifesaving appliances and associated devices
    will remain operational; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a mobile offshore platform,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the fluid in an operating ballast system,
      including the pumps, control systems and associated piping and
      valves, is protected against freezing,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the proper functioning of any thrusters is not
      impaired and the hydraulic fluid and lubricants for the thrusters

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the mooring winches and, where the platform is

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every installation shall be equipped with

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every installation that is equipped with
  steam-generating equipment, or an equivalent means, required by
  subsection (2) shall include outlets, hoses and hose clamps capable of
  being used in the following locations:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} work areas;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} walkways; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an offshore installation, the
    helicopter deck and the lifeboat embarkation stations.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where temperatures below -20°C may occur, based on an
  annual probability of exceedance of 10^-2^, at the drill site or
  production site more than one day per year and where the installation
  is equipped with steam-generating equipment, or an equivalent means,
  required by subsection (2),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that equipment shall meet the requirements set out
    in subsection (2) when operating at 75 per cent capacity; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the installation shall be equipped with a second
    set of steam-generating equipment or another means of providing
    equivalent protection against ice, snow and freezing.

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  structural elements that are part of an offshore installation and the
  failure of which as a result of corrosion would cause a safety hazard
  shall be protected or constructed with extra material so as to prevent
  the degree of corrosion that may cause that structural element to fail
  and shall be protected against corrosion in accordance with section
  4.15 of Canadian Standards Association CAN/CSA-S471-92, General
  Requirements, Design Criteria, the Environment, and Loads.

  installations shall be designed, installed and maintained in
  accordance with

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sections 4.9.5, 5.1.1, 5.3, 5.4.2, 5.6, 5.10 and
    11.19 of Canadian Standards Association Preliminary Standard
    S474-M1989, Concrete Structures, in the case of concrete platforms.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} All corrosion protection systems on an offshore
  installation shall be designed so that adjustment, repair or
  replacement can be done on site, except where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dry dock surveys are possible and are scheduled at
    a frequency of five years or less; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the corrosion protection system is a cathodic
    protection system that has a design life exceeding that of the
    installation.

### [Gas Release System]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978110 .Subheading}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[gas release system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
  system for releasing gas and combustible liquid from an installation,
  and includes a flare system, a pressure relief system, a
  depressurizing system and a cold vent system.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every gas release system shall be designed and
  located, taking into account the amount of combustibles to be
  released, the prevailing winds, the location of other equipment and
  facilities, including rigs, the dependent personnel accommodation, the
  air intake system, embarkation points, muster areas, the helicopter
  approaches and other factors affecting the safe, normal flaring or
  emergency release of the combustible liquid, gases or vapours, so that
  when the system is operating it will not damage the installation,
  other installations, the land or other platforms in the vicinity used
  for the exploration or exploitation of resources, or injure any
  person.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every gas release system shall be designed and
  installed in accordance with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} American Petroleum Institute RP 520, Recommended
    Practice for the Design and Installation of Pressure-Relieving
    Systems in Refineries;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} American Petroleum Institute RP 521, Guide for
    Pressure-Relieving and Depressuring Systems;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} American Petroleum Institute Standard 526, Flanged
    Steel Safety-Relief Valves;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} American Petroleum Institute Standard 527, Seat
    Tightness of Pressure Relief Valves; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} American Petroleum Institute Standard 2000, Venting
    Atmospheric and Low-Pressure Storage Tanks.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every gas release system shall be designed and
  constructed to ensure that oxygen cannot enter the system during
  normal operation.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any flare boom and its associated equipment shall be
  designed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to ensure a continuous flame using an automatic
    igniter system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to withstand the radiated heat at the maximum
    venting rate;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to prevent flashback; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to withstand all loads to which they may be
    subjected.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every gas release system shall be designed to limit
  to the acceptable levels permitted by the [Oil and Gas Occupational
  Safety and Health Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-612) the noise
  that may occur as the gas expands.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} With the exception of water, any liquid that cannot
  be safely and reliably burned at the flare tip of a gas release system
  shall be removed from the gas before it enters the flare.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Any vent that is used to release gas to the
  atmosphere without combustion shall be located and designed to
  minimize the risk of accidental ignition of the gas.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Every gas release system shall be designed and
  installed so that, taking into account the prevailing wind conditions,
  the maximum radiation on areas where personnel may be located, from
  the automatically ignited flame of a flare or vent, will be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 6.3 kW/m^2^, where the period of exposure will not
    be greater than one minute;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 4.72 kW/m^2^, where the period of exposure will be
    greater than one minute but not greater than one hour; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} l.9 kW/m^2^, where the period of exposure will be
    greater than one hour.

### [Emergency Shutdown System]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978133 .Subheading}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  installation shall have an emergency shutdown system that is capable
  of shutting down and isolating all potential sources of ignition and
  sources of flammable liquids or gases.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An emergency shutdown system shall be designed and
  installed so that when activated it causes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an audible and visual signal that indicates the
    cause of its activation and the identity of the equipment that has
    been shut down and isolated to be given in the appropriate control
    point; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an audible alarm to be sounded through the general
    alarm system required by section 34 unless the alarm is overridden
    by the control point operator.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a production installation, an
  emergency shutdown system shall be designed to ensure

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that there are at least two levels of shutdown; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (13), that the following will
    occur within the time and in the sequence set out in the operations
    manual:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the shutdown of all production facilities and
      associated test facilities,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the closure of all surface inlet manifold safety
      valves and production riser safety valves,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the closure of all Christmas tree safety valves
      and all downhole safety valves, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the shutdown of all utilities except the
      equipment listed in subsection 12(1).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a production installation, manual
  operation of an emergency shutdown system shall be in accordance with
  American Petroleum Institute RP 14C, Recommended Practice for
  Analysis, Design, Installation and Testing of Basic Surface Safety
  Systems for Offshore Production Platforms.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a drilling installation, an emergency
  shutdown system shall be designed to ensure

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the shutdown within the time and in the sequence
    set out in the operations manual of all utilities, except the
    equipment listed in subsection 12(1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that shutdown is possible from at least two
    strategic locations.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The emergency shutdown system shall be designed to
  permit the selective shutdown of the ventilation systems required by
  section 10, except the fans necessary for supplying combustion air to
  prime movers for the production of electrical power.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} At least one of the controls of the emergency
  shutdown system shall be located outside hazardous areas.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} After an emergency shutdown, the emergency shutdown
  system shall stay in a locked-out condition until it is manually
  reset.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} The emergency shutdown system shall be connected to a
  source of power in such a way that, in the event of a failure of the
  primary source of power, there is automatic changeover to an emergency
  source of power and audible and visual alarms indicating that failure
  are given at the appropriate control point.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} Where a hydraulic or pneumatic accumulator is used
  to operate any part of the emergency system, the accumulator shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be located as close as is practicable to the part
    that it is designed to operate, except where that part is part of a
    subsea production system; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have capacity for at least three operations.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} In the event of a failure of the accumulator
  referred to in subsection (10), the shutdown valves shall revert to a
  fail-safe mode.

- [(12)]{.lawlabel} All cables and pneumatic and hydraulic power lines
  that are part of the emergency shutdown system shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of cables and power lines that are
    exposed to the risk of mechanical or fire damage, be protected

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by metal channels or casings, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by being enclosed in a steel conduit or an
      equivalent covering; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as far as practicable, be segregated or routed away
    from the process and utility control systems so that any damage to
    those systems does not affect the shutdown system.

- [(13)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a production installation, on
  activation of the emergency shutdown system, the surface-controlled
  subsurface safety valve shall close in not more than two minutes after
  the Christmas tree safety valve has closed, except where a longer
  delay is justified by the mechanical or production characteristics of
  the well.

### [Escape Routes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978164 .Subheading}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  every onshore installation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} every work area shall have at least two well-marked
    separate escape routes that are situated as far apart as is
    practicable and that lead to an area away from the drill site or
    production site; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all corridors that are more than 5 m long, all
    accommodation areas and, where practicable, all work areas shall
    have at least two exits, located as far apart as is practicable,
    that lead to escape routes.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On every manned offshore installation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} every work area shall have at least two well-marked
    separate escape routes that are situated as far apart as is
    practicable;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all escape routes shall lead to the open deck and
    from there to an evacuation station;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in addition to the escape routes required by
    paragraph (a), clear passage shall be provided, where practicable,
    to the helicopter deck and sea level and other embarkation
    locations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all corridors that are more than 5 m long, all
    accommodation areas and, where practicable, all work areas shall
    have at least two exits, located as far apart as is practicable,
    that lead to escape routes;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} every escape route and embarkation station shall be
    free of all obstructions, and each exit door along the route shall
    be a sliding door or designed to open outwards;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} every escape route leading to an upper level shall,
    where practicable, be provided in the form of ramps or stairways;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} every escape route leading to a lower level shall,
    where practicable, be provided in the form of ramps, stairways or
    chutes of sufficient width to accommodate stretcher bearers with
    stretchers;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} suitable means shall be provided, where
    practicable, for persons to descend from the installation to the
    water;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} materials used for escape routes shall have a level
    of fire durability equivalent to steel;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the survival craft evacuation stations located
    adjacent to the accommodation areas and the associated escape routes
    from the accommodation areas shall provide fire protection for a
    period of at least two hours; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} all escape routes and associated stairwells shall
    be appropriately sheltered from the effects of fire and explosion.

### [Protection Against Impact Offshore]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978181 .Subheading}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), every
  offshore platform shall be designed to withstand accidental impacts
  with a vessel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where practicable, every offshore platform shall have
  a fender system, buoyage system or similar arrangement that will
  permit the transfer of goods to and from the production installation
  and a vessel without endangering that production installation or
  vessel or any person or goods.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), every offshore platform,
  including any fender system, shall be capable of absorbing the impact
  energy of not less than 4 MJ from a vessel without endangering any
  person or the environment.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (3) do not apply to an unmanned
  offshore platform if any impact described by those subsections will
  not cause major damage.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every offshore platform shall be designed so that the
  impact energy referred to in subsection (3)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} can be totally absorbed in the permanent
    deformation of the structural element impacted and by the elastic
    deflection of the platform; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} will not be absorbed in the permanent deformation
    of the vessel.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where a fender system is used to comply with
  subsection (2), its size and arrangement shall be such that a vessel
  cannot be trapped under it at low tide.

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} every opening in every bulkhead and deck in the
    accommodation areas shall have permanently attached to it a means of
    closing that will maintain the fire integrity of the bulkheads and
    decks;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} where a class A-0 division, class A-60 division,
    class B-15 division or class H-120 division is pierced for the
    passage of electric cables, pipes, trunks or structural elements or
    for other purposes, arrangements shall be made so that the fire
    resistance of the division is not impaired;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} air spaces enclosed behind ceilings, panelling or
    linings shall be divided by close-fitting draught stops that are
    spaced not more than 14 m apart and that are fitted transversely if
    the length of the space exceeds 14 m and lengthwise if the width
    exceeds 14 m;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} every internal stairwell, ladderwell and crew
    elevator trunk within the accommodation areas shall be constructed
    of steel or equivalent material;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} every stairwell in the accommodation areas shall be
    enclosed within a trunk constructed of class A-60 divisions and
    shall have self-closing doors, except that a stairwell connecting
    only two decks need only be fitted at one deck level with a division
    that has the same fire integrity and structural stability as the
    deck and self-closing doors;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} for compartments that contain or are affected by
    oil and oil vapour, the surface of insulating materials fitted to
    the inside of bulkheads and decks and forming the casings and crowns
    shall be impervious to oil and oil vapour;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} every door and shutter in a bulkhead opening to
    hatches in the galley and pantry shall be constructed so that the
    fire integrity of the bulkhead is maintained, and shall be capable
    of being readily closed from a position outside the galley or
    pantry;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} primary deck coverings shall be of a type that will
    not readily ignite;

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} paints, veneers and other finishes used on surfaces
    on concealed or inaccessible spaces and on exposed surfaces, except
    furniture, furnishings and floor coverings, shall be such that the
    surfaces are of a low flame spread type;

  - [(s)]{.lawlabel} overboard scuppers, sanitary discharges or other
    outlets close to the water shall be of material unlikely to fail in
    the event of fire;

  - [(t)]{.lawlabel} every load-bearing steel structural element shall
    be fire protected; and

  - [(u)]{.lawlabel} blast-resistant panels and explosion venting
    systems shall be provided in locations that are susceptible to an
    explosion.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply to an unmanned offshore
  installation, if the passive fire and blast structural protection
  provided will prevent major damage in the case of a fire or explosion.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Galleys on an offshore installation shall be provided
  with fire blankets.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsections (2) to (4), every
  offshore installation shall be arranged in such a way that a fire in
  one area on the installation will be prevented from spreading to other
  areas and the consequences of an explosion on the installation are
  minimized, taking into account the fire or explosion hazard of any
  particular area.

### [Fire Hydrant Systems]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978305 .Subheading}

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  manned offshore installation shall be provided with a fire hydrant
  system in accordance with this section.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fire hydrant system shall be connected to a
  continuously pressurized wet pipe water main that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is connected to at least two pump systems that are
    situated as far apart as possible; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when any one of the pump systems required by
    paragraph (a) is out of operation,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is capable of delivering at least one jet
      simultaneously from each of any two fire hydrants through the
      hoses and nozzles, at a pressure at the hydrants of at least 350
      kPa,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is capable of delivering water at a sufficient
      pressure and quantity so that the aggregate capacity of the pump
      systems that are still operating is not less than 120 m^3^/hour
      when the pump systems are delivering water to the fire hydrants,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is capable of maintaining a pressure of at
      least 700 kPa to any foam system protecting the helicopter deck.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The number and position of the fire hydrants in the
  fire hydrant system shall be such that water from any two hydrants,
  one of which is fitted with only a single length of fire hose and the
  other of which is fitted with one or two lengths of fire hose, can
  reach every part of the installation where a fire may occur.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Each fire hydrant in the fire hydrant system shall be
  provided with a hose that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is of not more than 18 m in length;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is equipped with a 19 mm dual purpose nozzle
    capable of spray or jet action and with the necessary couplings; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} meets the requirements of National Fire Protection
    Association 1961, Standard on Fire Hose.

  25 shall be located in a part of the installation remote from spaces

### [Sprinkler System in Accommodation Areas]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978356 .Subheading}

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  accommodation areas in every manned offshore installation shall be
  equipped with a sprinkler system that is supplied with water from

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} two dedicated pump systems connected to the water
    main referred to in section 24 or 25 by way of a lockable screwdown
    non-return valve that will prevent backflow from the sprinkler
    system to the water main; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one dedicated sprinkler pump connected to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the water main referred to in section 24 or 25 by
      way of a lockable screwdown non-return valve that will prevent
      backflow from the sprinkler system to the water main, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a pressurized fresh water tank having a volume
      equal to at least twice the volume of water required pursuant to
      subsection (2) to be supplied for a period of one minute.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The volume of water supplied by the pump systems or
  pump and fresh water tank described in subsection (1) shall be at a
  pressure sufficient to ensure, at the level of the highest sprinkler,
  continuous coverage of at least 280 m^2^ at the rate of at least 6
  L/minute/m^2^.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The sprinkler pump required by paragraph (1)(b) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serve only the sprinkler system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be activated automatically by a pressure drop in
    the system before the water in the fresh water tank is depleted; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be powered from at least two sources.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} When the sprinkler system is activated, an audible
  and visual alarm that shows the location of the activated sprinklers
  shall be given automatically at the fire and gas indicator panel in
  the control station.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The sprinkler system shall be designed to prevent the
  passage of sea water into the fresh water tank.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The sprinkler system shall be provided with at least
  one stop valve for every 200 sprinklers that can prevent the flow of
  water into those sprinklers without affecting the rest of the system.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Each of the stop valves provided pursuant to
  subsection (6) shall be protected from accidental operation.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} A gauge indicating the pressure in the sprinkler
  system shall be fitted at each stop valve and at the control station.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} The sprinkler heads shall be placed in positions and
  spaced in a pattern that will ensure an average application rate of 6
  L/minute/m^2^ throughout each space in the accommodation areas.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} The pump system or pump and fresh water tank
  required by subsection (1) shall be situated outside the accommodation
  areas and as far as possible from the main machinery space.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} The sprinkler system shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} installed in accordance with National Fire
    Protection Association 13, Standard for the Installation of
    Sprinkler Systems; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} tested and maintained in accordance with National
    Fire Protection Association 13A, Recommended Practice for the
    Inspection, Testing and Maintenance of Sprinkler Systems.

### [Fire-extinguishing Systems in Machinery and Flammable Liquid Storage Spaces]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978378 .Subheading}

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  every offshore installation, a fixed fire- extinguishing system
  utilizing carbon dioxide, pressure water spray or, where a fire will
  not involve any gases, liquefied gases with a boiling point below
  ambient temperature or cryogenic liquids, high expansion foam shall be
  installed in every space containing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} internal combustion machinery having an aggregate
    power of at least 750 kW;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an oil- or gas-fired boiler or any other fired
    process vessel having a thermal rating of at least 75 kW;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} paint or other flammable liquids as defined by
    National Fire Protection Association 321, Standard on Basic
    Classification of Flammable and Combustible Liquids; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} mud pits or equipment used for removing drill
    solids where oil-based mud is used.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On every offshore installation, a fixed
  fire-extinguishing system utilizing carbon dioxide or pressure water
  spray shall be provided in every compartment containing a pump for the
  transfer of oil.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A fire-extinguishing system utilizing carbon dioxide
  referred to in subsection (1) or (2) shall meet the requirements of
  National Fire Protection Association 12, Standard on Carbon Dioxide
  Extinguishing Systems.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A fire-extinguishing system utilizing pressure water
  spray referred to in subsection (1) or (2) shall meet the requirements
  of National Fire Protection Association 15, Standard for Water Spray
  Fixed Systems for Fire Protection.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A fire-extinguishing system utilizing high expansion
  foam referred to in subsection (1) shall meet the requirements of
  National Fire Protection Association 16, Standard on Deluge Foam-Water
  Sprinkler and Foam-Water Spray Systems.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} When a fire-extinguishing system utilizing carbon
  dioxide is provided pursuant to subsection (1) or (2), means shall be
  provided to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} stop all ventilation fans serving the space
    protected by that system automatically before the system is
    activated; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} close all dampers in the ventilation system serving
    the space protected by that system manually from a position that is
    outside that space and that will not be made inaccessible by a fire
    within that space.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Every fire-extinguishing system referred to in
  subsection (1) or (2) shall be capable of being activated manually

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} from a location close to but outside each space
    served by it; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the location where the extinguishing medium is
    stored.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} At each access to every space served by a
  fire-extinguishing system referred to in subsection (1) or (2) there
  shall be a notice indicating that the space contains such a system and
  stating which fire-extinguishing medium is used.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Visual indication of the operational status of the
  fire-extinguishing system referred to in subsection (1) or (2) shall
  be provided at each access to every space served by that system and at
  the control station.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} Every offshore installation provided with a
  fire-extinguishing system referred to in subsection (1) or (2) shall
  be provided with an automatic system that will give

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an audible warning, in every space served by that
    system to which personnel have access, before the fire-extinguishing
    medium is released from the fire-extinguishing system; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an audible and visual signal, outside the access to
    every space served by that system and at the fire and gas indicator
    panel in the control station, when the fire-extinguishing system is
    in operation.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} Every offshore installation provided with a
  fire-extinguishing system referred to in subsection (1) or (2) shall
  be provided with means to close all openings that may admit air to or
  allow gas to escape from a space served by that system.

### [Fire Extinguishers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978401 .Subheading}

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  every offshore installation a portable fire extinguisher shall be
  provided

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 10 m of any position accessible by personnel
    in every machinery, drilling and production and process space;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 15 m of any position accessible by personnel
    in every space other than a space described in paragraph (a); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} near the entrance to each space referred to in
    paragraphs (a) and (b).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the extinguishing medium
  employed by an extinguisher provided pursuant to subsection (1) shall
  be suitable for extinguishing fires in spaces in which it is intended
  to be used.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each extinguisher provided pursuant to subsection (1)
  for use in machinery spaces where oil is used as fuel shall be of a
  type discharging foam, carbon dioxide gas or dry powder.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The capacity of each portable extinguisher provided
  pursuant to this section shall be at least

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 9 L, for an extinguisher that discharges foam;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 4.5 kg, for an extinguisher that discharges dry
    powder;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 6 kg, for an extinguisher that discharges carbon
    dioxide gas; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} 9 L, for an extinguisher that discharges water.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A spare charge shall be provided for each portable
  extinguisher provided pursuant to this section for which a duplicate
  extinguisher has not been provided.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} On an offshore installation, every space containing
  internal combustion machinery that has an aggregate power of at least
  750 kW shall be provided with one portable foam applicator unit and
  the following fire extinguishers:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one foam type fire extinguisher of not less than 45
    L capacity in every engine space;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} two portable foam extinguishers, where the
    aggregate power of the machinery is at least 750 kW but not more
    than 1500 kW;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} three portable foam extinguishers, where the
    aggregate power of the machinery is more than 1500 kW but not more
    than 2250 kW;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} four portable foam extinguishers, where the
    aggregate power of the machinery is more than 2250 kW but not more
    than 3000 kW;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} five portable foam extinguishers, where the
    aggregate power of the machinery is more than 3000 kW but not more
    than 3750 kW; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} six portable foam extinguishers, where the
    aggregate power of the machinery is more than 3750 kW.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} On an offshore installation, every space containing
  an oil- or gas-fired boiler or any other fired process vessel that has
  a thermal rating of at least 75 kW shall be provided with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} two portable foam fire extinguishers plus an
    additional portable foam fire extinguisher for each burner up to a
    total capacity of 45 L;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one portable dry powder fire extinguisher; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one portable foam applicator unit.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Every portable foam applicator unit provided pursuant
  to this section shall be provided with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an air-foam nozzle of an inductor type capable of
    being connected to the water main described in subsection 24(2) and
    of producing foam effective for extinguishing an oil fire at the
    rate of at least 1.5 m^3^/minute; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at least two tanks, each containing at least 20 L
    of foam-making liquid.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Every portable fire extinguisher on an offshore
  installation shall be inspected, maintained and recharged in
  accordance with National Fire Protection Association 10, Standard for
  Portable Fire Extinguishers.

### [Gas Detection Systems]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978470 .Subheading}

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an audible and visual signal on the fire and gas

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an audible alarm that has a tone different from any

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least two portable gas detectors capable of

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a means of testing the portable gas detectors

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at every ventilation inlet duct leading to a

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Appendix C of American Petroleum Institute RP 14C,

    of Basic Surface Safety Systems for Offshore Production Platforms;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 9.2 of American Petroleum Institute RP 14F,
    Recommended Practice for Design and Installation of Electrical
    Systems for Offshore Production Platforms.

### [Alarm Panels and Signals]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978488 .Subheading}

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  manned offshore installation shall be equipped with a fire or gas
  detection system that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one or more fire and gas detector indicator panels
    located at the control station, that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} indicate the source of fire and gas by means of a
      visual signal,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are capable of being functionally tested, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} are fitted with equipment for resetting the
      fire and gas detection systems; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an audible fire and gas alarm that has a
    characteristic tone that distinguishes it from the alarms associated
    with machinery, safety and control system faults or any other alarm
    system and that is audible on all parts of the installation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A fire or gas detection system referred to in
  subsection (1) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be capable of being manually activated from each of
    the following locations, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the space adjacent to each entrance to each
      machinery and process space,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} each accommodation area,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the office of the manager of the installation,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} every control point in each machinery and
      process space, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the control station;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be designed so that, on activation of a detection
    device in one space, any signals received at the same time from a
    detection device in another space will register at the fire and gas
    indicator panel at that same time;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be installed and maintained in accordance with
    National Fire Prevention Association 72, Standard for the
    Installation, Maintenance, and Use of Protective Signaling Systems;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be arranged so that there will be an automatic
    changeover to an emergency source of electrical power in accordance
    with subsection 12(9) in the event of a failure of the primary
    source of electrical power and so that failure of the primary source
    of electrical power will be indicated both visually and audibly as a
    separate fault alarm.

### [General Alarm System]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978506 .Subheading}

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  installation shall be equipped with a general alarm system that is
  capable of alerting personnel to any hazardous conditions other than
  fire or gas that might

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} endanger the personnel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} endanger the installation; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be harmful to the environment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every general alarm system referred to in
  subsection (1) shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} operational and in operation at all times other
    than when the system is being inspected, maintained or repaired;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where applicable, flagged as being subject to
    inspection, maintenance or repair; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} designed in such a manner as to prevent tampering.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a general alarm system for an installation is
  being inspected, maintained or repaired, the operator of the
  installation shall ensure that the functions that the system performs
  are performed manually.

### [Piping Systems]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978517 .Subheading}

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  piping system and associated equipment of every installation shall be
  designed and installed in accordance with American Petroleum Institute
  RP 14E, Recommended Practice for Design and Installation of Offshore
  Production Platform Piping Systems.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every pressure vessel or fired vessel on a production
  installation shall be designed and constructed in accordance with the
  following standards:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} American Petroleum Institute Spec 12J,
    Specification for Oil and Gas Separators;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sections I, II, IV, V, VII, VIII and IX of American
    Society of Mechanical Engineers ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Canadian Standards Association B51-M1991, Boiler,
    Pressure Vessel, and Pressure Piping Code.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every compressor in hydrocarbon service at a
  production installation shall be designed in accordance with the
  following standards:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Canadian Standards Association CAN/CSA-Z184-M92,
    Gas Pipeline Systems;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} American Petroleum Institute STD 617, Centrifugal
    Compressors for General Refinery Service;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} American Petroleum Institute STD 618, Reciprocating
    Compressors for General Refinery Services; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} American Petroleum Institute STD 619, Rotary-Type
    Positive Displacement Compressors for General Refinery Services.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} All materials and procedures used in a production
  installation used to produce and process sour gas shall conform to
  National Association of Corrosion Engineers (U.S.) MR-01-75, Sulfide
  Stress Cracking Resistant Metallic Materials for Oil Field Equipment.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where an operator handles, treats or processes oil,
  gas or water that contains hydrogen sulphide, the operator shall do so
  in accordance with good industry practice to minimize the discharge of
  hydrogen sulphide into the environment and to ensure that the
  operation is carried out in a safe and efficient manner.

### [Communication Systems]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978531 .Subheading}

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall operate a manned installation unless the installation is
  equipped with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a radio or telephone communication system; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an emergency communications system.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The communication systems referred to in
  subsection (1) shall be operational at all times.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall operate a manned offshore
  installation unless the offshore installation is equipped with a
  two-way radio communication system that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enables effective communication by radio to be
    maintained between the installation and helicopters, the shore base,
    support vessels, standby vessels, search and rescue aircraft, and
    other nearby offshore installations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} enables effective communication with marine traffic
    in the vicinity.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The operator of a manned offshore installation shall
  ensure that the radio communication systems comply with the
  [Navigation Safety Regulations, 2020](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-216)
  as if the installation were a ship to which those Regulations apply.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Each installation shall comply with the [Navigation
  Safety Regulations, 2020](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-216) as if the
  installation were a ship to which those Regulations apply.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} No person shall operate a manned installation unless
  the installation is equipped with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an internal telephone system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a public address system with loudspeakers located
    so that a voice transmission can be heard throughout the
    installation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a means of transmitting written data to the shore
    base of the installation.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} No person shall operate an installation that is
  usually unmanned unless the installation is equipped with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an operational two-way radio communication system
    during any period when the installation is manned; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a system capable of detecting under ambient
    conditions any hazardous conditions that could endanger the safety
    of the installation or damage the environment and of alerting the
    control station about the hazardous conditions.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Analysis and Design]{.HTitleText1} {#h-978549 .Part}

### [General Design Considerations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978550 .Subheading}

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  installation and every component of an installation shall be designed
  in accordance with good engineering practice, taking into account

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the activities on and around the
    installation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the type and magnitude of functional loads,
    environmental loads, and foreseeable accidental loads;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} operating and ambient temperatures;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} corrosion conditions that may be encountered during
    the construction, operation and maintenance of the installation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the avoidance of damage to any part of the
    installation that may lead to the progressive collapse of the whole
    installation; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} soil conditions.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The design of an installation shall be based on such
  analyses or model tests of the installation, including simulations to
  the extent practicable, as are necessary to permit the determination
  of the behaviour of the installation and of the soils that support the
  installation or anchoring systems, under all foreseeable
  transportation, installation and operating conditions.

### [Design of Offshore Installations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978560 .Subheading}

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** Every offshore
installation shall be designed in accordance with the

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} section 4 of Canadian Standards Association
  CAN/CSA-S471-92, General Requirements, Design Criteria, the
  Environment, and Loads;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the foundation, section 5 of Canadian Standards
  Association CAN/CSA-S472-92, Foundations, Offshore Structures;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a steel platform, section 7 of Canadian Standards
  Association CAN/CSA-S473-92, Steel Structures, Offshore Structures;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for a concrete platform, sections 3 and 7 of Canadian
  Standards Association Preliminary Standard S474-M1989, Concrete
  Structures; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in respect of its transportation and installation,
  sections 5, 6 and 7 of Canadian Standards Association Preliminary
  Standard S475-M1989, Sea Operations.

### [Design of Offshore Platforms]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978567 .Subheading}

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** Every offshore platform
shall be designed in accordance with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for composite ice-resisting walls, section 13 of
  Canadian Standards Association CAN/CSA-S473-92, Steel Structures,
  Offshore Structures;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the foundation, section 5 of Canadian Standards
  Association CAN/CSA-S472-92, Foundations, Offshore Structures;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a steel platform, sections 9, 10, 11, 12 and 16
  of Canadian Standards Association CAN/CSA-S473-92, Steel Structures,
  Offshore Structures;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for a concrete platform, sections 8, 9, 10 and 12 of
  Canadian Standards Association Preliminary Standard S474-M1989,
  Concrete Structures; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} for a gravity base, fill, fill-retention or piled
  platform, section 6, 7, 8 or 9 of Canadian Standards Association
  CAN/CSA-S472-92, Foundations, Offshore Structures, as applicable.

### [Design of Onshore Installations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978574 .Subheading}

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** Onshore installations
shall be designed in accordance with the National Building Code of
Canada and the National Fire Code, issued by the National Research
Council of Canada.

### [Offshore Analyses]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978576 .Subheading}

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Analyses undertaken in respect of an
  offshore installation for the purposes of subsection 37(2) shall cover
  all relevant structural elements of the offshore installation, shall
  be based on good engineering practice and shall include a

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} structural analysis;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fatigue analysis;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} structural element stability analysis;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} overall installation stability analysis;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} for a mobile offshore platform, intact and damage
    stability analysis; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} hydrodynamic analysis.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Analyses undertaken in respect of an offshore
  platform for the purposes of subsection 37(2) shall be carried out in
  accordance with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} section 4.6.7 of Canadian Standards Association
    CAN/CSA-S471-92, General Requirements, Design Criteria, the
    Environment, and Loads;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a steel platform, section 8 of Canadian
    Standards Association CAN/CSA-S473-92, Steel Structures, Offshore
    Structures; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a concrete platform, sections 8 and 9 of
    Canadian Standards Association Preliminary Standard S474-M1989,
    Concrete Structures.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A fatigue analysis undertaken in respect of an
  offshore platform for the purpose of subsection 37(2) shall be carried
  out in accordance with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a steel platform, section 14 of Canadian
    Standards Association CAN/CSA-S473-92, Steel Structures, Offshore
    Structures; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a concrete platform, section 8.5 of Canadian
    Standards Association Preliminary Standard S474-M1989, Concrete
    Structures.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The foundation of major machinery and equipment on an
  offshore installation shall be analysed to determine if the resulting
  deflection, stresses and vibration are within the limits of the
  structural design criteria for the equipment.

### [Innovations for Offshore Installations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978593 .Subheading}

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** The design of an offshore
installation shall not involve the use of any design method, material,
joining technique, or construction technique that has not previously
been used in comparable situations, unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} there have been engineering studies or prototype or
  model tests that demonstrate the adequacy of the method, material or
  technique; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the operator implements a performance monitoring and
  inspection program that is designed to permit the determination of the
  correctness of the method, material or technique.

### [Removal and Abandonment of Fixed Offshore Production Installations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978597 .Subheading}

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** Where the removal of a
fixed offshore production installation is a condition of a development
plan approval, the operator shall incorporate in the design of the
installation such measures as are necessary to facilitate its removal
from the site without causing a significant effect on navigation or the
marine environment.

### [Concept Safety Analysis for Offshore Production Installations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978599 .Subheading}

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  operator shall, at the time the operator applies for a development
  plan approval in respect of an offshore production installation,
  submit to the Chief a concept safety analysis of the offshore
  production installation in accordance with subsection (5), that
  considers all components and all activities associated with each phase
  in the life of the production installation, including the
  construction, installation, operation and removal phases.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The concept safety analysis referred to in
  subsection (1) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be planned and conducted in such a manner that the
    results form part of the basis for decisions that affect the level
    of safety for all activities associated with each phase in the life
    of the production installation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} take into consideration the quality assurance
    program selected in accordance with section 4.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Target levels of safety for the risk to life and the
  risk of damage to the environment associated with all activities
  within each phase of the life of the production installation shall be
  defined and shall be submitted to the Chief at the time the operator
  applies for a development plan approval.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The target levels of safety referred to in
  subsection (3) shall be based on assessments that are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} quantitative, where it can be demonstrated that
    input data are available in the quantity and of the quality
    necessary to demonstrate the reliability of the results; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} qualitative, where quantitative assessment methods
    are inappropriate or not suitable.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The concept safety analysis referred to in
  subsection (1) shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each potential accident, a determination of the
    probability or susceptibility of its occurrence and its potential
    consequences without taking into account the plans and measures
    described in paragraphs (b) to (d);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each potential accident, contingency plans
    designed to avoid the occurrence of, mitigate or withstand the
    accident;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for each potential accident, personnel safety
    measures designed to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} protect, from risk to life, all personnel outside
      the immediate vicinity of the accident site,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} provide for the safe and organized evacuation of
      all personnel from the production installation, where the accident
      could lead to an uncontrollable situation,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} provide for a safe location for personnel until
      evacuation procedures can be implemented, where the accident could
      lead to an uncontrollable situation, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the control station, communications
      facilities or alarm facilities directly involved in the response
      to the accident remain operational throughout the time that
      personnel are at risk;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for each potential accident, appropriate measures
    designed to minimize the risk of damage to the environment;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} for each potential accident, an assessment of the
    determination referred to in paragraph (a) and of the implementation
    of the plans and measures described in paragraphs (b) to (d);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a determination of the effects of any potential
    additional risks resulting from the implementation of the plans and
    measures described in paragraphs (b) to (d); and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a definition of the situations and conditions and
    of the changes in operating procedures and practices that would
    necessitate an update of the concept safety analysis.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The determinations and assessments required by
  paragraphs (5)(a) and (e), respectively, shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} quantitative, where it can be demonstrated that
    input data is available in the quantity and of the quality necessary
    to demonstrate reliability of the results; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} qualitative, where quantitative assessment methods
    are inappropriate or not suitable.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The plans and measures identified in paragraphs
  (5)(b) to (d) shall be designed to ensure that the target levels of
  safety as defined pursuant to subsection (3) are met.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The operator shall maintain and update the concept
  safety analysis referred to in subsection (1) in accordance with the
  definition of situations, conditions and changes referred to in
  paragraph (5)(g) to reflect operational experience, changes in
  activity or advances in technology.

### [Offshore Environmental Criteria and Loads]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978626 .Subheading}

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of undertaking the analyses referred
  to in section 41, for offshore installations, load combinations shall

  the Environment, and Loads.

### [Offshore Site Investigations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978631 .Subheading}

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Offshore site investigations shall
  be carried out, for the purpose of undertaking the analyses referred

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of

### [Soil Deformation Offshore]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978638 .Subheading}

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of

### [Erosion Offshore]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978640 .Subheading}

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of undertaking the analyses referred to in section 41,
  the analysis of the erosion offshore shall be made in accordance with
  sections 6.2.3, 7.2.2 and 9.3.5 of Canadian Standards Association
  CAN/CSA-S472-92, Foundations, Offshore Structures.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where there is a potential for erosion around an
  offshore platform to a degree that would affect the stability of the
  platform, the platform shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provided with means of erosion protection that
    eliminate or prevent that degree of erosion; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} designed assuming all materials that are not
    resistant to erosion, determined from physical or numerical
    modelling, are removed.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The operator of an offshore platform or drilling unit
  that is placed so close to an existing offshore platform as to cause
  erosion that affects the stability of the existing platform shall take
  measures to prevent that erosion.

### [Materials for Offshore Installations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978647 .Subheading}

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding compliance with the
  standards referred to in this Part, all materials used in an offshore
  installation shall be suitable for the service in which, and for the
  conditions under which, they are used.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No materials other than non-combustible materials
  shall be used in an offshore installation except where a special
  property is required that cannot be obtained by using a
  non-combustible material.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), materials, such as organic
  foam insulation, that may give off toxic fumes or smoke when ignited
  shall not be used in an offshore installation.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Combustible foam insulation may be used for cold
  storage or refrigerated spaces on an offshore installation if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the foam is of a fire retardant type;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the foam is totally enclosed in stainless steel or
    another corrosion-resistant material that has all joints sealed; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the insulation and its casing does not form any
    part of the accommodation deck or bulkhead.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} All structural concrete used in an offshore
  installation shall be in accordance with sections 4, 5 and 6 of
  Canadian Standards Association Preliminary Standard S474-M1989,
  Concrete Structures.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} All structural steel used in an offshore installation
  shall be in accordance with sections 5, 6 and 17 of Canadian Standards
  Association CAN/CSA-S473-92, Steel Structures, Offshore Structures.

### [Air Gap and Freeboard]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978658 .Subheading}

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the air
  gap for an offshore installation, except for a surface platform, shall
  be determined in accordance with section 4.8 of Canadian Standards
  Association CAN/CSA-S471-92, General Requirements, Design Criteria,
  the Environment, and Loads.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The air gap for a column-stabilized mobile offshore
  platform may be calculated assuming the platform is at survival draft
  and at its lowest position relative to sea level, as determined from
  its motion characteristics.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every surface mobile offshore platform shall have
  sufficient freeboard, taking into consideration the environmental
  criteria and loads at the drill or production site determined under
  section 45.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every fixed offshore production platform shall have
  sufficient freeboard to prevent ice rubble or waves from flowing over
  the side of the platform unless it is designed to withstand the loads
  due to water and ice without major damage, under the most severe
  conditions as determined pursuant to section 45.

### [Offshore Load Measuring System]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978664 .Subheading}

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** Each leg on every
self-elevating mobile offshore platform shall have a load measuring
system that will permit

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} registration of the load on the leg at any time
  during jacking operations; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} measurement of the load on the leg periodically.

### [Gravity-Base, Fill, Fill-Retention and Self-elevating Platforms in the Offshore]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978668 .Subheading}

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** Every gravity-base, fill,
fill-retention and self-elevating platform shall be designed in
accordance with sections 5, 6, 7 and 8, respectively, of Canadian
Standards Association CAN/CSA-S472-92, Foundations, Offshore Structures,

S472.1-1992, Commentary to CSA Standard CAN/CSA-S472-92, Foundations.

### [Pile Foundations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978670 .Subheading}

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** Pile foundations of every
fixed offshore platform and, where applicable, subsea production system
shall be designed in accordance with section 9 of Canadian Standards
Association CAN/CSA-S472-92, Foundations, Offshore Structures, and
section 5.2.2 of Canadian Standards Association Special Publication
S472.1-1992, Commentary to CSA Standard CAN/CSA-S472-92, Foundations.

### [Structural Strength of Mobile Offshore Platforms]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978672 .Subheading}

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  floating platform that is intended to be used in areas in which sea
  ice is present shall be able to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} withstand, without major damage, the ice loads to
    which it may be subjected when it is operating in accordance with
    the operations manual;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} stay on location in the ice concentration and under
    the ice forces to which it may be subjected, as stated in the
    operations manual; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be moved from the production site or drill site in
    the ice concentration to which it may be subjected, as stated in the
    operations manual.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In an analysis undertaken in accordance with
  subsection 37(2) for the purpose of determining the resistance to
  overturning and the resistance to sliding of a self-elevating mobile
  offshore platform,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a lattice type leg may be analysed using equivalent
    single- beam hydrodynamic coefficients as determined in accordance
    with Det norske Veritas Classification Notes, Note No. 31.5 ---
    Strength Analysis of Main Structures of Self-Elevating Units, when
    determining

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the hydrodynamic loads to be used in calculating
      overturning forces and sliding forces, if a vertical load equal to
      5 per cent of the horizontal load is applied at the centre of the
      leg, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the hydrodynamic forces to be used in any
      detailed finite element analysis of the upper legs and hull;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the overturning moments and sliding forces shall be
    assessed assuming no spudcan fixity and using the most critical
    combination and direction of environmental and functional loads;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reaction point for an independent leg platform
    shall be taken as the point located at a distance above the spudcan
    tip that is equal to the lesser of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} half the height of the spudcan, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} half the total penetration; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the reaction point for a mat-supported platform
    shall be determined considering the soil characteristics determined
    in the site investigation undertaken pursuant to section 46.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where any wave frequency or seismic ground motion
  predicted for the production site of a self-elevating mobile offshore
  production platform is close to the frequency of oscillation of the
  platform, a dynamic response calculation shall be performed as part of
  the analyses required by section 37 and the dynamic loads determined
  thereby shall be included in the relevant stress and fatigue analyses.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The connection between each spudcan and each leg of a
  self-elevating mobile offshore platform shall be designed to withstand
  without failure the loads occurring at the full spudcan fixity
  condition.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Each spudcan and each connection between each spudcan
  and each leg of a self-elevating mobile offshore platform shall be
  designed for all possible penetrations or conditions ranging from tip
  penetration to full spudcan penetration as determined by the site
  investigation undertaken pursuant to section 46, and the shape of the
  spudcan.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every spudcan of a self-elevating mobile offshore
  platform shall have sufficient strength to withstand storm-induced
  horizontal loads, vertical loads, and one half of the lower guide
  bending moment that is calculated assuming the leg is pinned.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The secondary bending effects of the legs of a
  self-elevating mobile offshore platform shall be taken into account in
  the performance of an analysis pursuant to section 37.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The analysis required pursuant to section 37 to
  verify whether a self-elevating mobile offshore platform is capable of
  withstanding the loads imposed during transportation shall be
  performed in accordance with Part 3, Chapter 2, Sections 3 C-100 and
  D-300 of Det norske Veritas Rules for Classification of Mobile
  Offshore Units.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} A self-elevating mobile offshore platform with
  independent footing support shall be designed to withstand the loads
  that may be imposed during preload operations, including where there
  is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} loss of foundation support for one leg for a
    distance of at least 4 m; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} offset support of 1.5 m from the centre of the
    spudcan tip.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} The legs, spudcans and mats of every self-elevating
  mobile offshore platform shall be designed for any impact load that
  might occur on setdown, in accordance with Part 3, Chapter 2, Section
  3 E-400 of Det norske Veritas Rules for Classification of Mobile
  Offshore Units, using the maximum environmental and functional loading
  conditions for setdown operations, as specified in the operations
  manual.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} The legs of every self-elevating mobile offshore
  platform shall be preloaded to at least l.l times the reaction
  expected at the footing or mat at the extreme loading condition.

### [Stability of Mobile Offshore Platforms]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978700 .Subheading}

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of this section, [[lightship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in
  relation to a mobile offshore platform, means a platform with all its
  permanently installed machinery, equipment and outfit, including
  permanent ballast, spare parts normally retained on board and liquids
  in machinery and piping at their normal working levels, but not
  including liquids in storage or in reserve supply tanks, items of
  consumable or variable loads, stores and crew and their effects.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), an inclining test shall be
  carried out to determine the lightship weight and the location of the
  centre of gravity on every mobile offshore platform.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Detailed weight calculations showing the differences
  of weight and centres of gravity may be used in lieu of the inclining
  test required by subsection (2), in respect of a surface or
  self-elevating mobile offshore platform of a design that is identical
  with regard to hull form and arrangement to that of a platform for
  which an inclining test has been carried out, if the accuracy of the
  calculations is confirmed by a deadweight survey.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (6), during each five-year
  survey that is required and carried out by a classification society of
  a surface or self-elevating mobile offshore platform, a deadweight
  survey shall be carried out and, where there is a significant
  discrepancy between the measurement obtained from that survey and the
  weight change as calculated from weight records,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a surface platform, an inclining
    test shall be carried out; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a self-elevating platform, the
    allowable variable load in the elevated condition shall be adjusted
    in accordance with the deadweight survey and the stability in the
    floating mode shall be calculated.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (6), an inclining test shall be
  carried out during each five-year survey that is required and carried
  out by a classification society for every column-stabilized mobile
  offshore platform, except that after the second inclining test, the
  subsequent tests need only be carried out during every alternate
  five-year survey if there was no significant discrepancy between the
  weight records and the results of the second test.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} An inclining test is not required pursuant to
  subsection (4) or (5) where the platform is equipped with
  instrumentation that is capable of accurately measuring or providing
  data that permit an accurate calculation of the centre of gravity.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} A comprehensive and up-to-date record shall be kept
  of every change to a mobile offshore platform that involves a change
  in weight or position of weight.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Where the weight of a mobile offshore platform
  changes by more than 1 per cent of the lightship weight, a deadweight
  survey shall be carried out at the earliest opportunity and an
  up-to-date value of the lightship centre of gravity shall be recorded
  in the operations manual.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (10) to (13), the analysis of
  intact and damage stability of every mobile offshore platform
  undertaken for the purpose of paragraph 41(1)(e) shall include a
  verification as to whether the platform complies with Chapter 3 of
  International Maritime Organization Code for the Construction and
  Equipment of Mobile Offshore Drilling Units, 1989.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} Every mobile offshore platform shall be designed so
  that, in the intact condition, when subjected to the wind heeling
  moments described in the Code referred to in subsection (9), it has a
  static angle of heel of not more than 15 degrees in any direction.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} Every column-stabilized mobile offshore platform
  shall be designed so that, in the intact condition, it has a
  metacentric height of at least 1 m when it is in the operating and
  transit draft and a metacentric height of at least 0.3 m in all other
  draft conditions.

- [(12)]{.lawlabel} Every surface and self-elevating mobile offshore
  platform shall be designed so that, in the intact condition, it has a
  metacentric height of at least 0.5 m.

- [(13)]{.lawlabel} Every mobile offshore platform shall be designed so
  that, in the damaged condition or where any compartment is flooded,
  the final angle of heel does not exceed 15 degrees in any direction
  and the area under the righting moment curve is at least equal to the
  area under the heeling moment curve.

### [Ballast and Bilge Systems]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978717 .Subheading}

- **[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  mobile offshore platform shall be equipped with ballast tanks the
  number, location and degree of subdivision of which, together with the
  associated equipment, are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} capable of ballasting and trimming the platform
    efficiently under all reasonably anticipated environmental
    conditions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} designed to be fail safe.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every floating platform shall have a ballast system
  so arranged that any ballast tank can be filled or emptied by any one
  of at least two ballast pumps or by controlled free flow.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each lower hull on a floating platform with two lower
  hulls shall be provided with at least two ballast pumps each with the
  capacity to fill or empty any ballast tank in that hull.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The ballast system for a column-stabilized mobile
  offshore platform shall be designed to prevent uncontrolled transfer
  of water between tanks or through sea-connected inlets or discharges
  in any one of the following situations:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the failure of any valve or valve actuator for the
    system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the failure in the means of control or indication
    for the system; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the flooding of any space that contains equipment
    associated with the system.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Ballast piping in a ballast system for a floating
  platform that leads from a pump to more than one tank shall be led
  from readily accessible manifolds.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} All power-operated sea inlets, discharge valves and
  ballast tank isolating valves on a floating platform shall be designed
  to close automatically on loss of control power and remain closed when
  power is re-established until specific action is taken to open them.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Where crude oil is to be stored in a floating
  platform, the platform shall have enough ballast capacity, segregated
  from the crude storage, to be able to float at minimum operating draft
  with no crude oil on board.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Every floating platform shall be provided with a main
  ballast control station equipped with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an effective means of communication with other
    spaces that contain equipment relating to the operation of the
    ballast system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a ballast pump control and status system;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a ballast valve control and status system;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a tank level indicating system;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a draft indicating system;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} emergency lighting;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} heel and trim indicators;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} bilge and flood alarms; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} remote control indicators for watertight closing
    appliances.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Every column-stabilized mobile offshore platform
  shall be equipped with a secondary ballast control station equipped
  with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an effective means of communication with other
    spaces that contain equipment relating to the operation of the
    ballast system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a ballast pump control and status system;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a ballast valve control and status system;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a tank level indicating system;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} emergency lighting;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} heel and trim indicators; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a permanently mounted ballast schematic diagram.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} The main and secondary ballast control stations
  required by subsections (8) and (9) shall be located above the
  waterline in the final condition of equilibrium after flooding when
  the platform is in a damaged condition.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} Every column-stabilized mobile offshore platform
  shall have a ballast system that is capable, with any ballast pump out
  of operation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} of providing safe handling and operation of the
    platform under normal operating and transit conditions;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} of restoring the platform to a normal operating or
    transit draft and level trim from an inclination of 15 degrees in
    any direction; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} of raising the platform from the deepest operating
    draft to severe storm draft within three hours.

- [(12)]{.lawlabel} Every floating platform shall be equipped with a
  bilge system that has at least two bilge pumps connected to the bilge
  main and that is capable, under all conditions from upright to 15
  degrees in any direction, of pumping or draining any watertight
  compartment except for those spaces permanently designated for the
  storage of fresh water, water ballast, fuel oil or liquid cargo and
  for which other effective means of pumping are provided.

- [(13)]{.lawlabel} No floating platform shall be considered to comply
  with this section until the ballast and bilge system has been assessed
  through a failure modes and effects analysis.

### [Watertight Integrity of Floating Platforms]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978756 .Subheading}

- **[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  boundaries of watertight compartments in a floating platform shall
  contain no more openings than necessary for the operation of the
  platform and, where penetration of those boundaries is necessary for
  access, ventilation, piping and cables or any other similar purpose,
  arrangements shall be made to maintain the strength and watertight
  integrity of the boundaries.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every watertight boundary and associated closing
  appliance on a floating platform shall be of sufficient strength and
  tightness of closure to withstand without failure the pressure and
  other loads likely to occur in service.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} All watertight doors and hatches on a floating
  platform shall be operable locally from both sides of the associated
  bulkhead or deck.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Side scuttles and windows on a floating platform
  shall be of the non-opening type, fitted with internal hinged
  deadlight covers.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} All pipes and ducts on a floating platform shall,
  where possible, be routed clear of those compartments that are
  vulnerable to penetration damage or, where such routing is not
  possible, positive means of closure of those pipes and ducts shall be
  provided for each watertight boundary.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every valve required at a watertight boundary on a
  floating platform shall be remotely operable from the ballast control
  station or by a mechanical means from another readily accessible
  position that is above the waterline in the final condition of
  equilibrium that could result when the platform is in a damaged
  condition.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Every inlet or discharge port on a floating platform
  that is submerged at maximum operating draught shall be fitted with a
  valve that is remotely controlled from the ballast control station,
  and each such valve shall close automatically when the source of power
  fails, unless safety considerations require that it remain open.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Every discharge port on a floating platform that
  penetrates a boundary to a compartment intended to be watertight shall
  be fitted with an automatic non-return valve and with a second such
  valve or with a device whereby the port may be closed from a position
  outside and above the compartment.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Every door or hatch on a floating platform used to
  ensure the watertight integrity of internal access openings during the
  operation of the platform shall, while the platform is afloat, be
  equipped with an indicator at the ballast control station to indicate
  whether the door or hatch is open or closed.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} Every door or hatch on a floating platform that is
  normally closed while the platform is afloat shall be equipped

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with an alarm system that is triggered in a manned
    control station when the door or hatch is open; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with a notice affixed to that door or hatch cover
    to the effect that the door or hatch cover is not to be left open
    while the platform is afloat.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} All external openings on a floating platform, except
  manholes that are fitted with close-bolted watertight covers and that
  are kept permanently closed while the platform is afloat, shall, in
  the intact condition or in a damaged condition, be completely above
  any waterline associated with the platform being heeled because of
  wind forces.

- [(12)]{.lawlabel} Every external opening on a floating platform that
  becomes wholly or partially submerged when the platform is at the
  maximum angle of heel that could occur while the platform meets the
  area ratio requirement for intact and damage stability set out in the
  Code referred to in subsection 57(9) or that may become intermittently
  submerged because of wave action when the platform is in a damaged
  condition shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be designed and constructed to withstand any sea
    condition without penetration;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an opening that may become
    intermittently submerged when the platform is in a damaged
    condition,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} close automatically when submerged,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} be readily and quickly closable at any time, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} be assumed to be open in the damage stability
      calculations made pursuant to section 57;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an external opening that cannot be
    quickly closed,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} be assumed to be open in damage stability
      calculations made pursuant to section 57, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} be permanently closed when the platform is
      afloat;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a door or hatch that may be used
    during operation of the platform, be equipped with closing
    appliances that are operable locally from both sides of the bulkhead
    or deck;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a door or hatch that may become
    intermittently submerged when the platform is in a damaged
    condition,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} be equipped with an indication system to show in
      the ballast control station the status of the closing appliances,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} be self-closing on being submerged, or readily
      and quickly closable, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} bear a notice to the effect that the door or
      hatch is not to be left open during operation of the platform;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a ventilator or ventilation intake
    or outlet that may be used during operation of the platform,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} be equipped with a self-activating anti-flooding
      device, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if it is an intake or outlet not subject to
      intermittent submergence when the platform is in a damaged
      condition, be fitted with a manually operated means of closure
      that is readily accessible;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a closing appliance that is not to
    be opened during operation of the platform, bear a notice to that
    effect;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in the case of air pipes to a ballast tank, be
    equipped with an anti-flooding device; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a chain locker opening on a
    column-stabilized platform, except where the chain locker is kept
    full of water or is designed to be free-flooding, be fitted with a
    device that will prevent significant ingress of water in the event
    of submergence and with a suitable means for pumping out, both of
    which are remotely operable from a ballast control station.

- [(13)]{.lawlabel} All of the closing appliances required by
  subsection (12) shall be able to withstand without failure any wave
  impact load to which they may be subjected.

- [(14)]{.lawlabel} Every compartment on a floating platform required to
  remain watertight to comply with the intact and damage stability
  criteria described in section 57 shall incorporate a device suitably
  positioned to detect flooding and, where flooding occurs, trigger an
  alarm in a ballast control station.

### [Mooring]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978793 .Subheading}

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  mooring system for a floating platform shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide an anchor pattern that keeps all anchor
    lines, anchor chains and anchors a safe distance from existing
    pipelines, flow lines and other platforms;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide an anchor pattern that gives clear access
    to any support vessel intended to be used in operations and that
    clears lifeboat launching areas;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be sufficiently stiff so that the excursions of the
    platform are within the limits established for the risers in
    accordance with section 62 under all operating conditions; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be sufficiently strong so that the failure of any
    anchor line during operations will not lead to major damage.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The load factor for tension in the mooring lines of
  every floating platform, based on a quasi-static analysis, shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the operating condition with all lines intact,
    3.0;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the operating condition with one line failed,
    2.0;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the survival condition with all lines intact,
    2.0;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the survival condition with one line failed, if
    the platform will not threaten another platform used for the
    exploration or exploitation of subsea resources, l.4; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the survival condition with one line failed, if
    the platform may threaten another platform used for the exploration
    or exploitation of subsea resources, 2.0.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The fatigue life of the mooring system of every
  floating platform shall be equal to at least 15 years.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The mooring system of every floating platform that is
  to remain at the production site or drill site for longer than five
  years shall be designed so that its components can be inspected and
  replaced.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The design of the mooring system of every floating
  platform that is intended to remain moored in the survival condition
  shall be based on an appropriate model test or numerical analysis.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where there is an annual probability of 10^-2^ of ice
  or icebergs being present at the site of a floating platform, the
  mooring system of the platform shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} incorporate a primary quick release system with a
    remote triggering device and at least one back-up system; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have been demonstrated to be capable of permitting
    the quick release of the platform from its moorings and risers.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Except where the floating platform may threaten
  another platform used for the exploration or exploitation of subsea
  resources, the following factors may be taken into account in
  determining whether a thruster-assisted mooring system using a remote
  control thruster system complies with subsection (2):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the remote control is manual,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} zero thrust, for the operating condition,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 70 per cent of the net thrust effect from all
      except one thruster, for the survival condition,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} zero thrust, for one mooring line failed in the
      operating condition, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} 70 per cent of the net thrust effect from all
      thrusters, for one mooring line failed in the survival condition;
      and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the remote control is automatic,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the net thrust effect from all except one
      thruster, for the operating condition,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the net thrust effect from all except one
      thruster, for the survival condition,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the net thrust from all thrusters, for one
      mooring line failed in the operating condition, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the net thrust from all thrusters, for one
      mooring line failed in the survival condition.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Mooring system components on a floating platform that
  interface with the mooring chain or rope, except the attachment in the
  chain locker for anchor chain and the steel rope attachment on the
  drum, shall be designed to withstand the forces due to tension
  required to break the chain or rope.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} The mooring system for a floating platform shall be
  designed to keep the platform on location, under any ice loads to
  which it may be subjected as determined pursuant to section 45, and
  the chain or rope shall be able to withstand, without significant
  damage, the abrasion forces imposed by such loads.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} The load factors between the estimated anchor
  holding power in the mooring system of a floating platform and maximum
  mooring line tension at the anchor shall be at least

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the operating condition with all lines intact,
    2.1;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the operating condition with one line failed,
    1.4;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the survival condition with all lines intact,
    1.4;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the survival condition with one line failed, if
    the platform will not threaten another platform used for the
    exploration or exploitation of subsea resources, 1.0; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the survival condition with one line failed, if
    the platform may threaten another platform used for the exploration
    or exploitation of subsea resources, 1.4.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraphs (2)(d) and (e),
  subsection (7) and paragraphs (10)(d) and (e), one platform shall be
  considered to threaten another platform if the platform may drift or
  be pushed, by environmental conditions, into the other platform when
  all lines fail, taking into account any action likely to be taken to
  bring the platform under control.

- [(12)]{.lawlabel} All anchor winches and their stoppers, brakes,
  fairleads and sheaves, their attachments to the hull, and associated
  load- bearing structural elements for a floating platform shall be
  designed to withstand, without risk of permanent deformation or
  failure or of loss of ability to operate, the application of the
  breaking load of the associated anchor line with the anchor line in
  the most unfavourable direction.

- [(13)]{.lawlabel} The catenary mooring system on every floating
  platform shall be inspected in accordance with the requirements of
  American Petroleum Institute RP 21, Recommended Practice for
  In-Service Inspection of Mooring Hardware for Floating Drilling Units.

### [Dynamic Positioning]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978834 .Subheading}

- **[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  dynamic positioning system used to hold a floating platform in
  position at the production site or drill site shall be designed,
  constructed and operated so that the failure of any main component
  with an annual failure rate of greater than 0.1, as determined from a
  detailed reliability analysis, cannot result in major damage to the
  platform, as determined from a failure modes and effects analysis of
  the main components, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} operational procedures for the dynamic positioning
    system avoid or take into account the effect of the failure of the
    single component; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} every such component is routinely replaced so that
    the failure rate, as determined from the detailed reliability
    analysis, is no greater than 0.1 for the period between
    replacements.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every floating platform with a dynamic positioning
  system shall be equipped with an alert and response display system
  that demonstrates

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the position of the platform relative to the
    production site or drill site; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the percentage of the available power that is
    necessary to maintain the platform in a position relative to the
    site and that will permit the installation to continue to operate.

### [Subsea Production Systems]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978842 .Subheading}

- **[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  subsea production system shall be designed to withstand major damage
  under the loads listed in Part B, Section 4, of Det norske Veritas
  Guideline No. 1-85, Safety and Reliability of Subsea Production
  Systems.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the concept safety analysis required by section
  44 indicates a risk of damage to the subsea production system
  components from ice, dropped objects, trawl board nets or anchors, the
  design of the system shall include measures to minimize such damage.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The rigid risers in the subsea production system of a
  fixed offshore platform and the steel flowlines and flowline
  connectors in every subsea production system shall comply with
  National Standard of Canada CAN/CSA-Z187-M87, Offshore Pipelines.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every subsea production system and its components
  shall be subjected to equipment integration tests in accordance with
  section 7.2 of American Petroleum Institute RP 17A, Recommended
  Practice for Design and Operation of Subsea Production Systems.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every subsea production system shall be installed in
  accordance with section 7.3 of American Petroleum Institute RP 17A,
  Recommended Practice for Design and Operation of Subsea Production
  Systems.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every subsea wellhead system and subsea tree located
  in a caisson, silo, or glory hole shall be designed and installed in
  such a manner that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the effect of silting is minimized; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where practicable, inspection and maintenance
    during its production or injection life is possible.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Every subsea production riser shall be designed and
  operated in accordance with section 6 of American Petroleum Institute
  RP 17A, Recommended Practice for Design and Operation of Subsea
  Production Systems.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Every subsea production riser shall be designed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to withstand the maximum pressure to which the
    riser may be subjected during its service life;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} so that every component that is used to transport
    oil or gas from the seafloor to the production installation can
    withstand without failure the wellhead shut-in pressure, except
    where the component is equipped with an isolation valve at the
    seafloor and a pressure relief system at the platform to relieve the
    internal pressure of the component; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to withstand any ice loads to which it may be
    subject as determined pursuant to section 45, except where failure
    of the riser will not lead to uncontrolled pollution.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Flexible flowlines and risers in a subsea production
  system shall be designed in accordance with Det norske Veritas
  Technical Note TNA 503, Flexible Pipes and Hoses for Submarine
  Pipeline Systems.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} The end fittings of flexible flowlines or risers in
  a subsea production system shall have pressure integrity and
  load-bearing capacities greater than that of the pipe.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} The fatigue life of risers in a subsea production
  system shall be at least three times the service life of the
  production riser.

- [(12)]{.lawlabel} Adequate provision shall be made in the design of
  the risers in a subsea production system and in the configuration of
  their individual components, including production, injection, control
  and instrumentation lines and their attachment assemblies, for the
  safe and efficient maintenance and inspection of the risers and their
  components during their service life.

- [(13)]{.lawlabel} The analysis required by section 41 of the risers in
  a subsea production system in relation to fatigue and stress of the
  riser components and risk to the personnel and equipment as a result
  of failure or malfunction of individual components of the risers shall
  be performed using the methodology specified in section 6.5 of
  American Petroleum Institute RP 17A, Recommended Practice for Design
  and Operation of Subsea Production Systems.

- [(14)]{.lawlabel} Every riser in a subsea production system shall be
  equipped so that it can be disconnected

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before heave or excursion limits specified in the
    operations manual are exceeded; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when ice conditions pose a threat of major damage
    to the production platform.

- [(15)]{.lawlabel} Every riser on a subsea production system shall be
  equipped so that after it has been disconnected and reconnected it can
  be pressure tested in accordance with the procedures stipulated in the
  operations manual.

- [(16)]{.lawlabel} Every component of the riser in a subsea production
  system that is used to convey the pool fluids to the surface, inject
  fluids or chemicals into the pool, or transport processed or treated
  fluids to or from the production installation shall be designed and
  equipped so that when the fluids pose a threat to the environment, the
  component can be displaced with water or securely isolated before the
  riser is disconnected.

- [(17)]{.lawlabel} The templates and manifolds in a subsea production
  system shall be designed and operated in accordance with section 5 of
  American Petroleum Institute RP 17A, Recommended Practice for Design
  and Operation of Subsea Production Systems.

- [(18)]{.lawlabel} The control systems, including control lines and
  pressurized control fluids, of every subsea production system shall be
  designed and operated in accordance with section 4 of American
  Petroleum Institute RP 17A, Recommended Practice for Design and
  Operation of Subsea Production Systems.

- [(19)]{.lawlabel} Every subsea production system intended for manned
  intervention in an atmospheric chamber shall be designed in accordance
  with the requirements of Part B, Section 11, of Det norske Veritas
  Guideline No. 1-85, Safety and Reliability of Subsea Production
  Systems.

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Construction and Installation Offshore]{.HTitleText1} {#h-978870 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978871 .Subheading}

- **[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  offshore platform shall be fabricated and constructed in accordance
  with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a steel platform, sections 17, 18, 19, 20 and
    21 of Canadian Standards Association CAN/CSA-S473-92, Steel
    Structures, Offshore Structures;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a concrete platform, section 11 of Canadian
    Standards Association Preliminary Standard S474-M1989, Concrete
    Structures;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a gravity-base, fill, fill-retention or piled
    platform, sections 6.3, 7.3, 8.3 or 9.4, respectively, of Canadian
    Standards Association CAN/CSA-S472-92, Foundations, Offshore
    Structures; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the foundation, section 5.4 of
    Canadian Standards Association CAN/CSA-S472-92, Foundations,
    Offshore Structures.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every vessel or barge used for the construction,
  transportation, up-ending or positioning of an offshore installation
  or a component of an offshore installation shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be classified by a classification society or have
    documentation to prove that a similar verification process has taken
    place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if manned, be equipped with lifesaving appliances
    in accordance with the [Life Saving Equipment
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1436), as if it were in
    waters to which those Regulations apply; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be certified by the owner as being capable of
    performing the assigned task or tasks safely and as being otherwise
    fit for the services it is expected to provide.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} All slings, wire cables, shackles and any other
  component used for lifting and for securing loads during the
  construction, transportation, up-ending or positioning of an offshore
  installation or a component of an offshore installation shall have a
  minimum load factor of 3.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where loads developed during movement of an offshore
  platform from the construction site to the production site or drill
  site or during installation operations are in excess of those that
  will be encountered after installation, the platform shall be provided
  with load- and strain-measuring devices during the movement or
  installation of the platform.

## [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Operations and Maintenance Offshore]{.HTitleText1} {#h-978884 .Part}

### [Manual, Plans and Programs for Offshore Installations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978885 .Subheading}

- **[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), every
  operator shall prepare, adhere to and maintain, in respect of every
  offshore installation, an operations manual that contains the
  following data:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} limitations on the operation of the installation
    and its equipment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information as to environmental conditions at the
    site where the installation will be installed and the effect of
    those conditions on the installation, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} environmental conditions for which an offshore
      installation will be evacuated and the meteorological forecast
      following which such evacuation will be initiated,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of snow and ice that may be allowed
      to accumulate on the installation,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of marine growth that may be allowed
      to accumulate on the installation, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} for a mobile offshore platform, any operating
      limits imposed by environmental conditions and the effect of wind,
      sea, snow, ice and marine growth on the strength, stability and
      seaworthiness of the platform while in transit, in the operating
      condition or in the survival condition;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a fixed offshore platform, the characteristics
    of the platform foundation, bottom penetration and the maximum
    permitted amount of scour or other changing seabed conditions;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for a mobile offshore platform that is supported by
    the seabed,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} information concerning the different seabed
      conditions acceptable for the installation, including the varying
      capacity of the seabed, limiting values of seabed slope, and
      maximum and minimum penetrations of footings, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a program for inspecting for scour at regular
      intervals and after storms of a specified intensity;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} for a floating mobile offshore platform,
    information concerning stability, including all data and
    instructions necessary to determine whether any intended
    configuration of, or change to, the loading or ballasting will
    satisfy the stability requirement for the platform;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} information concerning permissible deck loads,
    variable load limits and preloading;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} details of any colour coding system used on the
    installation for the safety of personnel;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} information on corrosion protection systems used
    and any requirements for the safety and maintenance of the systems;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} details of openings and means of closure in
    watertight compartments;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} drawings that show

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the general arrangement of the deck structure,
      accommodation areas, helideck and equipment contained on the
      topside facilities,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a fixed steel platform, the jacket, piling,
      risers and conductors,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for a gravity-base platform and a
      fill-retention platform, the lower concrete or steel platform
      including any skirt arrangements or piling, the deck structure
      connection to the lower structure, the risers and the conductors,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} for a self-elevating mobile offshore platform,
      the main and supporting platforms, the equipment for the elevating
      and lowering of the deck structure and any arrangements for
      towing,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} for a column-stabilized mobile offshore platform,
      the main and support structure, the method for maintaining the
      station and arrangement for towing,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} for a surface mobile offshore platform and any
      similar- shaped platform, the hull structure and the positioning
      equipment,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} for a fill platform, the erosion protection and
      a cross-section of the platform including the locations of the
      conductors,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the locations of escape routes, fixed fire-
      extinguishing systems and life-saving appliances,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the fire divisions and the location of
      associated equipment, such as fire dampers,

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} the location of the hazardous areas on the
      installation, and

    - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} for a floating mobile offshore platform, the
      ballast and bilge systems and all openings and means of closure
      that could affect the stability of the platform;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the operating and maintenance requirements for all
    the life-saving appliances on the installation;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the maximum helicopter weight and wheel centres,
    and maximum size of the helicopter for which the helicopter deck on
    the installation has been designed, including the extent of the
    obstacle-free approach zone for the helicopter;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} special arrangements or facilities for the
    inspection and maintenance of the installation, any equipment or
    plant, and any crude oil storage facilities on or in the
    installation;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} special precautions or instructions to be followed
    when repairs or alterations to the installation are to be carried
    out;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} any special operational or emergency requirements
    covering essential features of the installation, including the
    shutdown systems;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} a description of any equipment for elevating and
    lowering the installation and of any special types of joints,
    including details of their purpose, proper operation and
    maintenance;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} for a fixed offshore platform, details of the air
    gap or freeboard;

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} for a mobile offshore platform, the means of
    ensuring that the air gap requirements determined in accordance with
    subsection 51(1) are met;

  - [(s)]{.lawlabel} the environmental loads the anchors can sustain to
    keep the installation moored in place, including the estimated
    holding power of the anchors in relation to the soil at the drill
    site or production site;

  - [(t)]{.lawlabel} for a floating platform,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} procedures for dealing with the excursion of the
      platform because of the failure of any anchor line, as determined
      by analysis,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where there is a thruster-assisted mooring
      system, procedures to control operations when thruster power is
      lost, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where there is a dynamic positioning system, a
      description of the capability of that system in all operational
      and survival conditions within stated tolerances, when any single
      source of thrust has failed and full power is being supplied for
      all foreseeable operations and emergency services;

  - [(u)]{.lawlabel} details of the number of persons to be accommodated
    during normal operations;

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} brief particulars of all the equipment on the
    installation, including flow sheets and instructions for the
    installation, operation and maintenance of the equipment;

  - [(w)]{.lawlabel} the procedure for preparing, and the description
    and format for, periodic reports concerning the integrity of the
    installation; and

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} a procedure for notifying the Chief of any
    situation or event described in section 68.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The part of the operations manual relating to the
  subsea production system shall comply with the requirements of
  sections 7.4 and 7.5 of American Petroleum Institute RP 17A,
  Recommended Practice for Design and Operation of Subsea Production
  Systems.

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** Every mobile offshore
platform shall be certified in accordance with the International
Convention on Load Lines, 1966.

**[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** Every operator of an
offshore installation shall at all times operate the installation in
accordance with limitations imposed by the certificate of fitness and by
these Regulations and in accordance with the operations manual.

**[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** Every operator of an
offshore installation shall develop and implement an inspection and
monitoring, a maintenance and a weight control program.

### [Repair, Replacement and Modification of Offshore Installations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978938 .Subheading}

- **[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), no holder
  of a certificate of fitness in respect of an offshore installation
  shall make any repair, replacement or modification to the
  installation, or bring on board any equipment, that could affect the
  strength, stability, integrity, operability or safety of the
  installation, without the approval of the Chief and the certifying
  authority.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In an emergency, the operator of an offshore
  installation may repair or modify the installation when the manager of
  the installation considers that the delay required to comply with
  subsection (1) would endanger personnel or the environment.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an operator makes a repair or modification to
  an installation pursuant to subsection (2), the operator shall
  immediately notify the Chief and the certifying authority.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The operator of an offshore installation shall notify
  the certifying authority and the Chief immediately if the operator
  notices any deterioration of the installation that could impair the
  safety of the installation or damage the environment.

**[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** Where an inspection

## [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Records and Reporting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-978950 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978951 .Subheading}

**[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** The international system
of units (SI) shall be used for recording the data and preparing the
reports required by these Regulations.

### [Report of Loss, Emergency or Accident]{.HTitleText2} {#h-978953 .Subheading}

- **[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  operator shall inform the Chief, by the most rapid and practical
  means, of any situation or event involving any danger or accident to a
  person or property, including loss of life, a missing person, serious
  injury to a person, an imminent threat to safety of personnel or the
  public, fire, explosion, loss of well control, hydrocarbon or toxic
  fluid spills, or significant damage to a pipeline, equipment or an
  installation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every operator shall submit a full written report to
  the Chief of any situation or event referred to in subsection (l) as
  soon as practicable.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every operator shall inform the Chief by the most
  rapid and practical means, at least twenty-four hours before any of
  the following events is scheduled to take place, of the time and place
  and the nature of the event:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} start of [[tow out]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of an
    installation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any lift at a production site in excess of 500
    tonnes; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the up-ending or setting on bottom of an
    installation.

## [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-978961 .Part}

**[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** The contravention of any
of the provisions of Parts IV and V is an offence under the Act.
