## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-792125 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in Schedule
    III to the [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International
    Organization for Migration. ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-792129 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Organization is to have in Canada the legal capacities of a body
  corporate and, to the extent necessary for the exercise of its
  functions and the fulfilment of its purposes in Canada, the privileges
  and immunities set out in sections 2 to 5 of Article II and in Article
  III of the Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The representatives of foreign states that are
  members of the Organization are to have in Canada, to the extent
  necessary for the independent exercise of their functions in
  connection with the Organization, the privileges and immunities set
  out in paragraphs 11(a) to (f) and sections 12 and 14 to 16 of Article
  IV of the Convention.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Director General of the Organization, the Deputy
  Director General of the Organization and officials of the Organization
  are to have in Canada, to the extent necessary for the independent
  exercise of their functions in connection with the Organization, the
  privileges and immunities set out in paragraphs 18(a) and (c) to (f)
  of Article V of the Convention.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Experts performing missions for the Organization are
  to have in Canada, to the extent necessary for the independent
  exercise of their functions in connection with the Organization, the
  privileges and immunities set out in Article VI of the Convention.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-792135 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
