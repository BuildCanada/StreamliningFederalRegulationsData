## Safety or Security Positions Bargaining Direction {#safety-or-security-positions-bargaining-direction .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1354
:::

[FEDERAL PUBLIC SECTOR LABOUR RELATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.3)

PUBLIC SERVICE LABOUR RELATIONS ACT

Direction Exempting from Bargaining Certain Positions Related to Safety
or Security
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503982 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Direction may be cited
as the [Safety or Security Positions Bargaining
Direction](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1354).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503984 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** It is hereby directed that,
in the interest of the safety or security of Canada and states allied or
associated with Canada, the Treasury Board shall not negotiate with or
enter into a collective agreement with any bargaining agent in respect
of any of those persons in the Department of External Affairs from time
to time holding any of the positions in that Department numbered as set
out in the schedule.
