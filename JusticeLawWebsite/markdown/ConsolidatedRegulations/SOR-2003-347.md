Ottawa, June 25, 2003

Whereas the annexed Canadian Payments Association By-law No. 6 ---
Compliance, made by the Board of Directors of the Canadian Payments
Association, has been approved, in accordance with subsection 18(3) of
the [Canadian Payments Act](/eng/acts/C-21)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SOR-2003-347_e_hq_7555){#fn_SOR-2003-347_e_hq_7555-ID0EBABA},
by the members of the Association at a meeting of members;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 18(2)
of the [Canadian Payments Act](/eng/acts/C-21)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SOR-2003-347_e_hq_7555){#fn_SOR-2003-347_e_hq_7555-ID0EDAAA},
hereby approves the annexed Canadian Payments Association By-law No. 6
--- Compliance, made by the Board of Directors of the Canadian Payments
Association.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-705814 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this By-law.

  [[By-law No. 3]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[By-law No. 3]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Canadian Payments
      Association By-law No. 3 --- Payment Items and Automated Clearing
      Settlement System. ([Règlement administratif n°
      3]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[clearing member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[clearing member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member that, on
      behalf of a non-member, exchanges payment items and effects
      clearing or makes entries into the Automated Clearing Settlement
      System within the meaning of By-law No. 3. ([membre de
      compensation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a committee established by
      the Board or a subcommittee of that
      committee. ([comité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes data stored by
      electronic means. ([document]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[item in dispute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[item in dispute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an item in dispute
      as defined in Canadian Payments Association Rule A6. ([effet
      contesté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[non-member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[non-member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a local referred to in
      subparagraph 6(1)(a)(ii) of Bylaw No.
      3. ([non-membre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a party to an investigation
      under section 10. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[secretary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[secretary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the secretary of the
      Association appointed by the Board. ([secrétaire]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention by member

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this By-law, a member commits a
  contravention when the member fails to comply with a provision of the
  by-laws or rules that imposes a duty on the member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention by non-member

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this By-law, a non-member commits
  a contravention when the non-member fails to comply with a provision
  of the by-laws or rules in respect of which a clearing member is
  responsible for ensuring compliance by the non-member under By-law No.
  3.

## [Investigation Initiated by President]{.HTitleText1} {#h-705832 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2010-43, s. 71

\]
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initiated by President

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President may, at any time, if the President reasonably believes that
  a member or a non-member has committed a contravention, refer the
  matter to a committee that has relevant expertise for the purposes of
  an investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The President shall provide the following when
  referring the matter to a committee:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the member or non-member involved;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the alleged contravention and the
    relevant facts;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any documentary evidence supporting the allegation;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other relevant information or arguments.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Immediately after the President refers a matter to a
  committee, the President shall give written notice to the parties that
  the President has referred the matter to a committee for the purposes
  of an investigation. The notice shall contain a description of the
  alleged contravention.

- SOR/2010-43, s. 72

## [Investigation Initiated by Complaint]{.HTitleText1} {#h-705848 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaint

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member
  may file with the President a complaint alleging that another member
  or non-member has committed a contravention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A complaint shall be in writing and signed by an
  authorized representative of the complainant. The complaint shall
  contain the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the member or non-member involved;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the alleged contravention and the
    relevant facts;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any documentary evidence supporting the complaint;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if appropriate, the relief sought; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other relevant information or arguments.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-43, s. 73
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Item in dispute

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  filing a complaint that involves an item in dispute, the member shall
  give written notice to the member alleged to be in contravention or,
  in the case of a non-member alleged to be in contravention, to the
  non-member and its clearing member

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} of the reason for the dispute, within 60 days after
    receipt of the returned item by the branch that originally received
    the item on negotiation or deposit; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} of the member's intention to file a complaint, at
    least 30 days before the day on which the complaint is filed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No complaint involving an item in dispute may be
  filed more than 180 days after the notice referred to in paragraph
  (1)(b) has been given.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgement of receipt

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The President shall
acknowledge, in writing, receipt of the complaint within 10 days after
the day on which it is filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of President

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President shall, if the President believes that the complaint is not
  frivolous, vexatious or made in bad faith,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} investigate the complaint and appoint one or more
    persons as necessary to assist in the investigation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} refer the complaint to a committee that has
    relevant expertise for the purposes of an investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the President decides to investigate a complaint
  or to refer a complaint to a committee, the President shall, within 30
  days after the day on which the complaint is filed, give written
  notice to the parties of the President's decision. The notice shall
  contain the details of the complaint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Referral

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the President refers a complaint to a committee,
  the referral shall be made within 40 days after the day on which the
  complaint is filed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-43, s. 75

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Frivolous complaints

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  President decides that the complaint is frivolous, vexatious or made
  in bad faith, the President shall give written notice of that decision
  with reasons to the complainant within 30 days after the day on which
  the complaint is filed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A complainant may appeal the President's decision to
  the Board within 30 days after the day on which the notice of the
  decision is given to the complainant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The appeal shall be made in writing, signed by an
  authorized representative of the appellant and given to the secretary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgement of receipt

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The secretary shall acknowledge, in writing, receipt
  of the appeal within 10 days after the day on which it is received.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution and scheduling

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The secretary shall distribute the appeal to the
  members of the Board, and the hearing of the appeal shall be placed on
  the agenda for the next regular meeting of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of the hearing

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The secretary shall give written notice of the
  hearing of the appeal to the complainant and the President at least 14
  days before the day of the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If a director has, or appears to have, a conflict of
  interest, that director shall withdraw from the appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submissions

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The complainant and the President shall be given the
  opportunity to make oral and written submissions to the Board without
  introducing new evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision of Board

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall either confirm the President's
  decision, if the Board believes the complaint is frivolous, vexatious
  or made in bad faith, or order that an investigation be conducted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for decisions

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} The decision of the Board shall be in writing with
  reasons and the Board shall give the decision to the complainant and
  the President within 30 days after the day of the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} If the Board orders that an investigation be held,
  the President shall, within 10 days after the day on which the order
  is received,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} refer the investigation to a committee that has
    relevant expertise; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give to the parties a written notice setting out
    the details of the complaint and that the complaint has been
    referred to a committee for the purposes of an investigation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-43, s. 76

## [Compliance Panel]{.HTitleText1} {#h-705910 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  complaint has been referred to a committee, the committee shall, at
  its next regular meeting,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish a compliance panel of at least three
    representatives of members other than the complainant and the member
    alleged to be in contravention or, in the case of a non-member
    alleged to be in contravention, the non-member's clearing member;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} appoint the chair of the compliance panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A decision of the compliance panel is by majority
  vote.

## [Conduct of Investigations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-705918 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fairness and impartiality

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  investigation shall be conducted in a fair and impartial manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the President or a member of a compliance panel
  has, or appears to have, a conflict of interest, the President or the
  panel member, as the case may be, shall withdraw from the
  investigation and be replaced by a person designated by the Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-43, s. 77
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parties to investigation

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member alleged to be in contravention and, in the case of a non-member
  alleged to be in contravention, the non-member and its clearing member
  are parties to an investigation of the contravention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Association

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the Association is a party
  to every investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complainant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an investigation involving an item in
  dispute that has been initiated by the complaint of a member involved
  in the dispute, the complainant, rather than the Association, is a
  party to the investigation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation by lawyer

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The parties to an
investigation are entitled to be represented by a lawyer during the
investigation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President or compliance panel, as the case may be, shall assemble
  information and documents that are relevant to the investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written summary

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The President or compliance panel, as the case may
  be, shall ensure that a written summary is prepared of any information
  that is assembled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The written summaries and documents are part of the
  record of the investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information and documents

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The President or compliance panel, as the case may
  be, may request any member to provide information or documents that
  are relevant to the investigation and may make copies of them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A member shall provide, or arrange to provide, the
  information or documents requested, unless the member satisfies the
  President or compliance panel, as the case may be, that there is a
  good reason as to why it should not provide or arrange to provide
  them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on use

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The President or compliance panel, as the case may
  be, shall use the information or documents that are assembled for the
  sole purpose of the investigation and for making a decision in respect
  of the alleged contravention.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-43, s. 78

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination by the parties

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The parties may, for the

investigation and make copies of it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Experts

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President or compliance panel, as the case may be, may engage legal
  and other experts. Their reasonable fees and disbursements shall be
  included in the expenses of the investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any information or documents provided to the
  President or compliance panel, as the case may be, by their experts,
  other than a legal expert, forms part of the record of the
  investigation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President or compliance panel, as the case may be, shall convene a
  hearing unless the parties agree that a hearing is not necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of hearing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The President or compliance panel, as the case may
  be, shall select a date for a hearing in consultation with the
  parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The President or compliance panel, as the case may
  be, shall give to the parties written notice of the day of a hearing
  at least 20 days before that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights of parties

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In a hearing, the parties have the right to be heard,
  to produce and cross-examine witnesses, to receive copies of any
  documentary evidence produced against them, to produce evidence and to
  make oral and written submissions.

## [Sanctions and Expenses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-705970 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sanctions

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If,
  after an investigation, the President or compliance panel, as the case
  may be, decides that a member is in contravention or, in the case of a
  non-member alleged to be in contravention, that the non-member is in
  contravention and that its clearing member failed to ensure compliance
  by the non-member, the President or compliance panel, as the case may
  be, may take one or more of the following actions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} reprimand the member;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} order the member to do anything that a member is
    required to do, or to stop doing anything that a member is not
    allowed to do, under the by-laws or rules;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} order the member to make restitution to any member
    that has suffered a loss as a result of the contravention;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} suspend one or more of the rights of the member,
    specifying the day on which the rights are automatically reinstated
    or the conditions that must be met before the rights can be
    reinstated on application under section 25;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} order the member to comply with certain conditions
    when the member exercises one or more of its rights;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} order the member to pay a penalty not to exceed
    \$250,000 for each contravention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendation to remove

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the sanctions referred to in
  subsection (1), the President or compliance panel, as the case may be,
  may notify the Board or any committee on which an officer or employee
  of the member serves that the member has committed a contravention and
  recommend that the officer or employee be removed from the Board or
  committee.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-43, s. 81

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The President or
compliance panel, as the case may be, may determine the amount of the
expenses of the investigation and order one or both of the following
persons to pay all or a part of those expenses:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the member who is the subject of the investigation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a complainant who is a party to the investigation
  under subsection 10(3).

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Every member who is
subject to an order under subsection 16(1) or section 17 shall comply
with the order.

## [Compliance Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-705992 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation by President

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  an investigation is conducted by the President, the President shall
  prepare a compliance report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation by compliance panel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When an investigation is conducted by a compliance
  panel, the panel shall prepare a compliance report.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-43, s. 83
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-19-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 19"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** A compliance report shall
contain the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the facts and analysis with respect to the
  investigation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the conclusions of the investigation, including a
  determination of whether a contravention has occurred;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any sanctions that are being imposed; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} who is required to pay all or a part of the expenses,
  if any, of the investigation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The President or
compliance panel, as the case may be, shall give the compliance report
to the parties within 30 days after the day on which the investigation
has concluded.

## [Enforcement and Collection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706010 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  amount to be paid by a member under subsection 16(1) or section 17 is
  due and payable within 30 days after the day on which the compliance
  report is given to the member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a member does not pay the amount within the 30-day
  period, the member shall pay interest calculated in accordance with
  subsection (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The interest is equal to an amount calculated in
  accordance with the formula

  ::: {#706018 .Subsection}
  A × B × C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount to be paid under subsection (1);

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the weighted average of the minimum interest rate that the Bank
      of Canada is prepared to charge in respect of an advance, as made
      public in accordance with the [Bank of Canada Act](/eng/acts/B-2),
      converted to a daily rate that is based on 365 days, for the
      period beginning on the day on which the compliance report is
      given to the member and ending on the day on which the amount
      referred to in the description of A is paid (in this subsection
      referred to as the interest period); and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of days in the interest period.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount owing added to membership dues

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any amount to be paid by the member that is not paid
  within the 30-day period, together with the interest calculated under
  subsection (3), shall be added to the following year's annual dues of
  that member.

## [Notices and Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706041 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Method of giving notices

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  notice to be given under this by-law is sufficiently given if it is
  given by personal delivery, transmitted by facsimile or electronic
  mail, or sent by registered mail (except during periods of actual or
  threatened interruption of normal postal service because of labour
  disturbance) addressed to the intended recipient at the recipient's
  address as recorded on the membership list or in the records of the
  Association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delivered notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice given by personal delivery is considered to
  have been given on the day it is delivered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mailed notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice sent by registered mail is considered to
  have been given on the fifth day after it was mailed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notices sent by other means

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A notice transmitted by facsimile or electronic mail
  is considered to have been given on the day it is transmitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of address

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The secretary may change or cause to be changed in
  the records of the Association the address of any member, director,
  officer, auditor or member of a committee of the Board in accordance
  with any reliable information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of change of address

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Members shall inform the secretary of any change in
  their address.

- SOR/2015-185, s. 27

## [Exclusion of Liability]{.HTitleText1} {#h-706059 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of liability

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Neither the Association,
nor a director, the President or a member of a compliance panel is
liable for any loss or damage suffered by a member for anything done or
omitted to be done honestly and in good faith in the administration or
discharge of any powers or duties that under this By-law are intended or
authorized to be executed or performed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-43, s. 88
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-28-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 28"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnity

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The Association shall
indemnify a member of a compliance panel or former member of a
compliance panel and his or her heirs and legal representatives, against
all costs, charges and expenses --- including an amount paid to settle
an action or to satisfy a judgment --- reasonably incurred by him or her
in respect of any civil, criminal or administrative action or proceeding
to which he or she was made a party by reason of being or having been a
member of a compliance panel, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the member or former member acted honestly and in
  good faith; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a criminal or administrative action or
  proceeding that is enforced by a monetary penalty, the member or
  former member had reasonable grounds for believing that his or her
  conduct was lawful.
