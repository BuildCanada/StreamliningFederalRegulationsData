## [Voters List]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599564 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At least
  49 days before the day on which a referendum is to be held

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the band holding the referendum has assumed
    control of its own membership under section 10 of the Act, the band
    shall provide the electoral officer with a list of the names of all
    electors; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the Band List of the band holding the
    referendum is maintained in the Department under section 11 of the
    Act, the Registrar shall provide the electoral officer with a list
    of the names of all electors.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A voters list shall set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the names of all electors, in alphabetical order;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the band membership or registry number of each
    elector or, if the elector does not have a band membership or
    registry number, the date of birth of the elector.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} On request, the electoral officer or deputy electoral
  officer shall confirm whether the name of a person is on the voters
  list.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer shall revise the voters list
  where it is demonstrated that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of an elector has been omitted from the
    list;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of an elector is incorrectly set out in
    the list; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of a person not qualified to vote is
    included in the list.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (4), a person may
  demonstrate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the name of an elector has been omitted from,
    or incorrectly set out in, the voters list by presenting to the
    electoral officer evidence from the Registrar or from the band that
    the elector is on the Band List, is at least 18 years of age and is
    qualified to vote at band elections; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the name of a person not qualified to vote has
    been included in the voters list by presenting to the electoral
    officer evidence that that person is not on the Band List, is not at
    least 18 years of age or is not qualified to vote at band elections.

- SOR/2000-392, s. 4

## [Notification of Referendum]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599586 .Part}

- **[[4.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At
  least 14 days before the day on which an information meeting for a
  referendum is to be held and at least 42 days before the day of the
  referendum, the electoral officer or deputy electoral officer shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} post a notice of the referendum and a list of the
    names of electors in at least one conspicuous place on the reserve;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} mail or deliver to every elector of the band who
    does not reside on the reserve and for whom an address has been
    provided

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a notice of the referendum,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a mail-in ballot, initialled on the back by the
      electoral officer,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an outer, postage-paid return envelope,
      pre-addressed to the electoral officer,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a second, inner envelope marked "Ballot" for
      insertion of the completed ballot,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a voter declaration form,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a letter of instruction regarding voting by
      mail-in ballot, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} an information package regarding the
      designation or surrender that is the subject of the referendum.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of a referendum shall state

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the question to be submitted to the electors;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the referendum will be held;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the location of each polling station and the hours
    that it will be open for voting;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that electors may vote either in person at a
    polling station in accordance with subsection 9(3) or by mail-in
    ballot;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name and telephone number of the electoral
    officer; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the date, time and location of the information
    meeting.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} At the request of an elector who resides on the
  reserve, the electoral officer shall provide the elector with the
  material referred to in subsection (1)(b).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer shall indicate on the voters
  list that a ballot has been provided to each elector to whom a mail-in
  ballot was mailed, delivered or otherwise provided, and keep a record
  of the date on which, and the addresses to which, each mail-in ballot
  was mailed or delivered.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} An elector to whom a mail-in ballot was mailed,
  delivered or provided under subsection (1) or (3) is not entitled to
  vote in person at a polling station other than in accordance with
  subsection 9(3).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-392, s. 4
:::

## [Voting by Mail-in Ballot]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599626 .Part}

- **[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  elector may vote by mail-in ballot by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} marking the ballot by placing a check mark or other
    mark, clearly indicating the elector's response to the question
    stated on the ballot;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} folding the ballot in a manner that conceals the
    question and any marks, but exposes the initials on the back;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} placing the ballot in the inner envelope and
    sealing that envelope;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} completing and signing the voter declaration form
    in the presence of a witness who is at least 18 years of age;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} placing the inner envelope and the completed,
    signed and witnessed voter declaration form in the outer envelope;
    and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} delivering or, subject to subsection (6), mailing
    the outer envelope to the electoral officer before the time at which
    the polls close on the day of the referendum.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an elector is unable to vote in the manner set
  out in subsection (1), the elector may enlist the assistance of
  another person to mark the ballot and complete and sign the voter
  declaration form in accordance with subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A witness referred to in paragraph (1)(d) shall
  attest to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fact that the person completing and signing the
    voter declaration form is the person whose name is set out in the
    form; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the elector enlisted the assistance of
    another person under subsection (2), the fact that the elector is
    the person whose name is set out in the form and that the ballot was
    marked according to the directions of the elector.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} An elector who inadvertently spoils a mail-in ballot
  may obtain another ballot by returning the spoiled ballot to the
  electoral officer.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} An elector who loses a mail-in ballot may obtain
  another ballot by delivering to the electoral officer a written
  affirmation that the elector has lost the mail-in ballot, signed by
  the elector in the presence of the electoral officer, a justice of the
  peace, a notary public or a commissioner for oaths.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Mail-in ballots that are not received by the
  electoral officer before the time at which the polls close on the day
  of the referendum are void and shall not be counted as a vote cast.

## [Voting at Polling Stations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599644 .Part}

**[[5.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.2 .sectionLabel}** The electoral officer
shall establish at least one polling station on the reserve.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-392, s. 6
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The electoral officer or
the deputy electoral officer shall provide a compartment at each polling
place where the elector can mark his ballot paper free from observation.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The electoral officer or
the deputy electoral officer shall, immediately before the opening of
the poll, open the ballot box and call upon such persons who may be
present to witness that it is empty and shall then lock and properly
seal the box and place it in view for the reception of the ballots.

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Polling
  stations shall be kept open from 9:00 a.m., local time, until 8:00
  p.m., local time, on the day of the referendum.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An elector who is inside a polling station at the
  time that the polling station is to close is entitled to vote.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-392, s. 7
:::

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection 4.2(5), where a person attends at a polling station for
  the purpose of voting, the electoral officer or deputy electoral
  officer shall, if the person's name is set out in the voters list,
  provide the person with a ballot.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer or the deputy electoral officer
  shall place on the list of electors a mark opposite the name of every
  elector receiving a ballot paper.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An elector to whom a mail-in ballot was mailed,
  delivered or provided under subsection 4.2(1) or (3) may obtain a
  ballot and vote in person at a polling station if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the elector returns the mail-in ballot to the
    electoral officer or deputy electoral officer; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the elector has lost the mail-in ballot, the
    elector provides the electoral officer or deputy electoral officer
    with a written affirmation that the elector has lost the mail-in
    ballot, signed by the elector in the presence of the electoral
    officer, deputy electoral officer, a justice of the peace, a notary
    public or a commissioner for oaths.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-392, s. 8
:::

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  electoral officer or the deputy electoral officer shall explain the
  mode of voting to an elector when requested to do so by such elector.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the application of an elector who is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} not able to read, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} incapacitated by blindness or other physical cause,

  the electoral officer or the deputy electoral officer shall assist
  that elector by marking his ballot paper in the manner directed by the
  elector and shall place such ballot paper in the ballot box.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer or the deputy electoral officer
  shall make an entry in the list of electors opposite the name of the
  elector that the ballot paper was marked by him at the request of the
  elector and the reasons therefor.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Except as provided in
subsection 10(2), every elector receiving a ballot paper shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} proceed immediately to the compartment provided for
  marking the ballot paper;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} mark the ballot by placing a check mark or other
  mark, clearly indicating the elector's response to the question stated
  on the ballot;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} fold the ballot in a manner that conceals the
  question and any marks, but exposes the initials on the back;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} forthwith deliver it to the electoral officer or the
  deputy electoral officer for deposit in the ballot box.

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  elector who receives a soiled or improperly printed ballot paper, or
  inadvertently spoils his ballot paper in marking it shall, upon
  returning the ballot paper to the electoral officer or the deputy
  electoral officer, be entitled to another ballot paper.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An elector who has received a ballot paper and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} leaves the compartment for marking ballot papers
    without delivering the same to the electoral officer or the deputy
    electoral officer in the manner provided, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} refuses to vote,

  shall forfeit his right to vote on the referendum and the electoral
  officer or the deputy electoral officer shall make an entry on the
  list of electors opposite the name of the elector that the elector did
  not return the ballot paper or refused to vote as the case may be.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The electoral officer or
the deputy electoral officer shall allow only one elector in the
compartment for marking ballot papers at any one time.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** No person shall interfere
or attempt to interfere with an elector when marking his ballot paper or
obtain or attempt to obtain at the polling place information as to how
an elector is about to vote or has voted.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The electoral officer and
deputy electoral officer shall maintain peace and good order during the
voting.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Whenever the electoral
officer or the deputy electoral officer does not understand the language
spoken by an elector, he or she shall enlist the aid of an interpreter
to communicate with respect to all matters required to enable that
elector to vote.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-392, s. 11

## [Counting of Votes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599692 .Part}

**[[17.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17.1 .sectionLabel}** As soon as is
practicable after the close of the polls, the electoral officer shall,
in the presence of the deputy electoral officer and any members of the
council of the band who are present, open each envelope containing a
mail-in ballot that was received before the close of the polls and,
without unfolding the ballot,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} set aside the ballot if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it was not accompanied by a voter declaration form,
    or the voter declaration form is not signed or witnessed,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the elector set out in the voter
    declaration form is not on the voters list, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the voters list shows that the elector has
    already voted; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} place a mark on the voters list opposite the name of
  the elector set out in the voter declaration form and deposit the
  ballot in a ballot box.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-392, s. 11
:::

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As
  soon as is practicable after the mail-in ballots have been deposited
  under section 17.1, the electoral officer shall, in the presence of
  the deputy electoral officer and any members of the council of the
  band who are present, open all ballot boxes and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine the ballot papers;

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} set aside any ballot that does not have the
    initials of the electoral officer or deputy electoral officer on the
    back;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reject all ballot papers

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2000-392, s. 12\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that have been marked incorrectly, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} upon which anything appears by which an elector
      can be identified;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} count the votes given in favour of and against the
    question submitted in the referendum; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prepare a statement in writing of the number of
    votes so given and of the number of ballot papers rejected.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The statement referred to in paragraph (1)(d) shall
  be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} signed by the electoral officer and by the chief or
    a member of the council of the band; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} filed in the local office of the Department.

**[[18.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18.1 .sectionLabel}** A ballot set aside
under paragraph 17.1(a) or 18(1)(a.1) is void and shall not be counted
as a vote cast.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-392, s. 13
:::

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** As soon as is practicable
after the results of the voting are known, the electoral officer shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prepare a statement in triplicate, signed by the
  electoral officer and by the chief or a councillor of the band, that
  indicates

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of electors who were entitled to vote,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the number of electors who voted,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the number of votes cast in favour of and against
    the question submitted in the referendum, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the number of rejected ballots; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} deliver a copy of the statement to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Assistant Deputy Minister,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the person in charge of the regional office of the
    Department, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the chief of the band.

## [Second Referenda]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599736 .Part}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding sections
4.2 and 4.3, in respect of a referendum held under subsection 39(2) of
the Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the notice of the referendum shall be posted, and the
  material referred to in paragraph 4.2(1)(b) shall be mailed or
  delivered, in the manner set out in subsection 4.2(1), at least 35
  days before the day on which the referendum is to be held;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the notice of the referendum is not required to set
  out the date, time or location of an information meeting;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in lieu of an information package referred to in
  subparagraph 4.2(1)(b)(vii), any elector to whom an information
  package was sent in respect of the previous referendum shall be sent

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a statement referring him or her to that
    information package, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} instructions on how to obtain an additional
    information package, if he or she requires one; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an information meeting is not required to be held.

## [Review Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599746 .Part}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  elector may, in the manner set out in subsection (2), request a review
  of the referendum by the Minister where the elector believes that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there was a contravention of these Regulations that
    may affect the results of the referendum; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there was corrupt practice in connection with the
    referendum.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request for a review of a referendum shall be made
  by forwarding the request to the Minister, by registered mail
  addressed to the Assistant Deputy Minister, within seven days after
  the day of the referendum, accompanied by a declaration, containing
  the grounds for requesting the review and any other relevant
  information, signed in the presence of a witness who is at least 18
  years of age.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Within 21 days after the receipt of a request for a
  review of a referendum, the Minister shall mail a copy of the request
  to the electoral officer who conducted the referendum.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Within 10 days after the receipt of a request under
  subsection (3), the electoral officer shall forward to the Minister,
  by registered mail addressed to the Assistant Deputy Minister, a
  declaration responding to the grounds stated in the request, signed in
  the presence of a witness who is at least 18 years of age.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-392, s. 15
:::

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  material referred to in section 22 or any other information in the
  Minister's possession is sufficient to call into question the validity
  of a referendum held under subparagraph 39(1)(b)(iii) or
  subsection 39(2) of the Act, the Minister shall advise the Governor in
  Council accordingly.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall take into account any material
  provided under section 22 in relation to a referendum held under
  section 39.1 of the Act when deciding whether to accept the
  designation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-392, s. 15
- SOR/2013-21, s. 3
