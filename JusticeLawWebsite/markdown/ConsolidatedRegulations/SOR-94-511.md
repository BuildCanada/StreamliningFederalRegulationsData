## British Columbia Milk Order {#british-columbia-milk-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-511
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1994-07-19

British Columbia Milk Order

P.C. 1994-1215 1994-07-19
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to subsections 2(1) and (2) of the
[Agricultural Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), is pleased hereby
to repeal the [British Columbia Milk
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-511), C.R.C., c. 143, and to make the
annexed Order granting authority to the British Columbia Milk Marketing
Board to regulate the marketing in interprovincial and export trade of
milk produced in British Columbia, in substitution therefor.
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/2001-16,
s. 14\]]{.Repealed}

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964299 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Acts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Acts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Milk Industry Act and the
    Natural Products Marketing (BC) Act of British Columbia;
    ([Lois]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the British Columbia Milk
    Marketing Board established pursuant to the Natural Products
    Marketing (BC) Act of British Columbia; ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[milk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[milk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means milk or cream from cows, produced
    in the Province of British Columbia for consumer use in liquid form
    in Canada; ([lait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    milk, established and amended from time to time pursuant to the
    Acts. ([plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-16, s. 15
:::

## [Interprovincial Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964307 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Board is authorized to regulate the marketing
  of milk in interprovincial trade and for that purpose may, with
  respect to persons and property situated within the Province of
  British Columbia, exercise all or any powers like the powers
  exercisable by the Board in relation to the marketing of milk locally
  within the province under the Acts and a Plan.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The authority to regulate the marketing of milk in
  interprovincial trade does not include any power that is exercised by
  the Canadian Dairy Commission under the [Canadian Dairy Commission
  Act](/eng/acts/C-15) in relation to the marketing of milk in
  interprovincial trade.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-16, s. 16
:::

## [Levies or Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964313 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Board may, in relation
to the powers granted to it under section 3,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix and impose levies or charges on milk or any
  component of milk and collect those levies or charges from persons
  described in subsection 3(1) who are engaged in the production or
  marketing of milk and for that purpose may classify those persons into
  groups and fix the levies or charges payable by the members of the
  different groups in different amounts; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the levies or charges for the purposes of the
  Board, including the creation of reserves, the payment of expenses and
  losses resulting from the sale or disposal of milk and the
  equalization or adjustment among producers of milk of moneys realized
  from the sale thereof during such period or periods of time as the
  Board may determine.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-16, s. 17
