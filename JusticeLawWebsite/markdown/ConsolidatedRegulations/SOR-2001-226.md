## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-670129 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 2,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of refractory
bricks, blocks, tiles, and similar refractory constructional products,
of heading No. 68.10 or 68.15, employed in the production of
metallurgical coke, iron and steel.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-670131 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the refractory products in question were imported
  into Canada during the period commencing on February 1, 1997, and
  ending on the day that this Order comes into force;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within two years after the date on which this Order
  comes into force; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the importer files such evidence as may be required
  by the Canada Customs and Revenue Agency to determine eligibility for
  remission.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-670136 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
