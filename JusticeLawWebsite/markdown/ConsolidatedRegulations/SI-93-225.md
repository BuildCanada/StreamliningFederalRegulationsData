## Order Designating the Commission of Inquiry on the Blood System in Canada as a Department and the Prime Minister as Appropriate Minister {#order-designating-the-commission-of-inquiry-on-the-blood-system-in-canada-as-a-department-and-the-prime-minister-as-appropriate-minister .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-225
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1993-12-15

Order Designating the Commission of Inquiry on the Blood System in
Canada as a Department and the Prime Minister as Appropriate Minister

P.C. 1993-1977  1993-12-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General In Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition
  [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to designate the Commission of
  Inquiry on the Blood System in Canada as a department for the purposes
  of that Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition
  [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to designate the Prime
  Minister as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Commission of
  Inquiry on the Blood System in Canada.
