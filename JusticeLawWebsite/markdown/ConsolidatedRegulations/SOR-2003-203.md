## Regulations Respecting the Possession of Tobacco, Cannabis or Vaping Products That Are Not Stamped {#regulations-respecting-the-possession-of-tobacco-cannabis-or-vaping-products-that-are-not-stamped .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-203
:::

[EXCISE ACT, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1)

Registration 2003-06-05

Regulations Respecting the Possession of Tobacco, Cannabis or Vaping
Products That Are Not Stamped

P.C. 2003-857 2003-06-05
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 304(1)(o) of the
[Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed Regulations Respecting the Possession of Tobacco Products
That Are Not Stamped.

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2002, c.
  22
  :::

## [Authorized Possession]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702688 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 32(2)(d) of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), a person
may possess tobacco products that are not stamped if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is authorized by an officer under section
  19 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) to transport tobacco
  products that have been reported under section 12 of that Act and is
  acting in accordance with that authorization; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person has in their possession documentation that
  provides evidence that the person is transporting the tobacco products
  on behalf of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a tobacco licensee,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an excise warehouse licensee,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a special excise warehouse licensee,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a duty free shop licensee,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any person, if the tobacco products are designated
    as ships' stores under the [Ships' Stores
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-40), or

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} an accredited representative.

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraph 158.11(3)(a)(i) of the [Excise Act,
2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), a person may possess a cannabis product that is
not stamped if the person has in their possession documentation that
provides evidence that the person is transporting the cannabis product
on behalf of a cannabis licensee or, in the case of an industrial hemp
by-product, an industrial hemp grower.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2018, c. 12, s. 107
:::

**[[1.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraph 158.11(3)(a)(ii) of the [Excise Act,
2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), a person may possess a cannabis product that is
not stamped if the person has in their possession documentation that
provides evidence that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is sterilizing the cannabis product on
  behalf of a cannabis licensee;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cannabis licensee owns the cannabis product
  throughout the period during which the person is in possession of it;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the cannabis product is to be returned to the
  premises of the cannabis licensee as soon as possible after the
  sterilization of the cannabis product.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2018, c. 12, s. 107
:::

**[[1.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 158.11(6)(a) of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), a
person may possess cannabis products in a particular specified province
that are not stamped to indicate that additional cannabis duty in
respect of the particular specified province has been paid if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person holds a licence for sale for medical
  purposes issued under section 62 of the [Cannabis
  Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person is authorized by another province to sell
  cannabis products in the other province and, if the other province is
  a specified province, the cannabis products are stamped to indicate
  that additional cannabis duty in respect of the other province has
  been paid; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person is an individual who possesses the
  cannabis products for their personal use and the total amount of the
  cannabis products is equivalent to no more than 30 g of dried
  cannabis, as determined in accordance with the [Cannabis
  Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5).

::: HistoricalNote
- [SOR/2019-77, s. 1](#1118561-1119470){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1118561-1119470.html"
  role="button"}
:::

**[[1.31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.31 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 158.44(2) of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), a
person may possess vaping products in a particular specified vaping
province that are not stamped to indicate that additional vaping duty in
respect of the particular specified vaping province has been paid if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vaping products are to be sold or offered for
  sale by the person, or by another person, to consumers in another
  province and, if the other province is a specified vaping province,
  the vaping products are stamped to indicate that additional vaping
  duty in respect of the other province has been paid; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person is an individual who possesses the vaping
  products for their personal use.

::: HistoricalNote
- [SOR/2024-70, s. 8](#1451123-1486145){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1451123-1486145.html"
  role="button"}
:::

**[[1.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 158.44(3)(b) of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), a
person may possess a vaping product that is not stamped if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is authorized by an officer under section
  19 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) to transport vaping products
  that have been reported under section 12 of that Act and is acting in
  accordance with that authorization; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person has in their possession documentation that
  provides evidence that the person is transporting the vaping product
  on behalf of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a vaping product licensee,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an excise warehouse licensee, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an accredited representative.

::: HistoricalNote
- [2022, c. 10, s. 115](#1356598-1376974){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1356598-1376974.html"
  role="button"}
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702707 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations take
effect on July 1, 2003.
