## Canada Industrial Relations Regulations {#canada-industrial-relations-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2002-54
:::

[CANADA LABOUR CODE](/eng/acts/L-2)

Registration 2002-01-31

Canada Industrial Relations Regulations

P.C. 2002-88  2002-01-31
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Labour, pursuant to section 111[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFABA} of the [Canada
Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2), hereby makes the annexed [Canada Industrial
Relations Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-54).

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698898 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Part I of the [Canada Labour
    Code](/eng/acts/L-2). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Director General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Director General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Director General
    of the FMCS. ([directeur général]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[FMCS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[FMCS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Federal Mediation and
    Conciliation Service referred to in section 70.1 of the Act.
    ([Service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Head of FMCS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Head of FMCS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the senior labour program
    official designated as Head of the FMCS. ([directeur du
    Service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation or an
    unincorporated association, and includes a trade union.
    ([organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Notice, Request or Report to the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698905 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), any notice, request or report that may be given or
  made to, or received by, the Minister under the Act may be given or
  made to, or received by, either the Head of FMCS or the Director
  General on behalf of the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any request that may be made to the Minister under
  subsection 57(2), (3) or (4) of the Act may be made to the Director
  General on behalf of the Minister.

## [Notice or Request by the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698909 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), any notice or request authorized or required under
  the Act to be given or sent by the Minister may be given or sent on
  behalf of the Minister by either the Head of FMCS or the Director
  General.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any notice that may be given or sent by the Minister
  in respect of a request under subsection 57(2), (3) or (4) of the Act
  may be given or sent by the Director General on behalf of the
  Minister.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  notice or report required or authorized under the Act to be given by
  the Minister, a conciliation commissioner, a conciliation board or an
  Industrial Inquiry Commission to any individual or organization is
  sufficiently served on the individual or organization to whom it is
  given or sent if it is given or sent

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by registered mail or courier addressed to that
    individual or organization at their latest known or usual address or
    at their place of business;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by personal service of a copy of it,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, upon that
      individual or, in their absence, upon some other individual who
      appears to be at least 16 years of age, at the individual's latest
      known or usual address or at that individual's place of business,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an organization, upon an officer
      or employee of that organization;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by facsimile; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by electronic mail.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a notice that may be given or
  sent by or on behalf of the Minister in respect of a request under
  subsection 57(2), (3) or (4) of the Act may be sent by ordinary mail.

## [Notice to Commence Collective Bargaining]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698922 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A notice
  to commence collective bargaining given under the Act shall be given
  in writing and be dated and signed by or on behalf of the party giving
  the notice.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice referred to in subsection (1) may specify
  the section of the Act under which the notice is being given and
  designate a convenient time and place for the collective bargaining to
  commence.

## [Notice of Dispute and Request for Conciliation Assistance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698926 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A notice
  of dispute to the Minister to be given under section 71 of the Act
  shall be given in writing and state

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the party giving the
    notice;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the other party to the
    dispute;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which notice to commence collective
    bargaining was given;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the steps that have been taken, the progress that
    has been made and the difficulties that have been encountered in
    collective bargaining following the giving of the notice;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the issues remaining in dispute;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the date and the results of any strike vote or
    lockout vote that has been conducted in accordance with section 87.3
    of the Act; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} whether the parties have reached an agreement on
    the maintenance of activities in accordance with section 87.4 of the
    Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  signed by or on behalf of the party giving the notice and be
  accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the notice to commence collective
    bargaining;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the most recent collective agreement
    between the parties; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any maintenance of activities agreement
    concluded under section 87.4 of the Act.

## [Strike or Lockout Notice]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698940 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A notice
  of strike or lockout given under section 87.2 of the Act shall be
  given in writing, be dated and signed by or on behalf of the party
  giving the notice, be addressed to the other party to the dispute and
  state

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the party giving the notice
    of a strike or lockout;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of employees in the bargaining unit that
    will be affected by the strike or lockout;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and time when the strike or lockout is to
    commence; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether it is a first notice under
    subsection 87.2(1) or (2) of the Act or a new notice under
    subsection 87.2(3) of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the notice referred to in subsection (1)
  shall be given to the Minister at the same time and in the same manner
  as referred to in subsection (1).

## [Request for the Appointment of an Arbitrator]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698948 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  request to the Minister for the appointment of an arbitrator under
  subsection 57(2), (3) or (4) of the Act or an arbitration board
  chairperson under subsection 57(4) of the Act shall state

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the party making the
    request and their representative;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the other party to the
    difference and their representative;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if an arbitration board is to be established, the
    names and addresses of the parties' nominees; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the nature and date of the occurrence of the
    difference.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  signed by or on behalf of the party making the request and be
  accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the grievance form, if any, required by
    the collective agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the applicable collective agreement
    between the parties; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of all the pertinent correspondence
    exchanged between the parties up to the time the request is made.

## [Arbitration Order or Decision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698959 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A copy of every order or
decision referred to in section 59 of the Act shall be filed with the
Minister, within 15 days after the order or decision has been given or
made, by mailing a copy to the Director General.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698961 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** \[Repeal\]

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698963 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
