## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831165 .Part}

Definition of [[carbonated beverage glass
container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations, a
[[carbonated beverage glass container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
glass container of a capacity of 1.5 L or more containing a
non-alcoholic carbonated beverage.

## [Specifications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831168 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Perforation

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A carbonated beverage glass
container must not, when tested in accordance with Schedule 1, shatter
in such a way as to cause any piece or part of the container to
perforate the plastic sheets described in that Schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distance of projection

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** When a carbonated beverage
glass container is tested in accordance with Schedule 2, at least 95% of
the dry weight of the container must remain inside the cylinder.

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831173 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warning

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A carbonated beverage glass
container must have printed on it, or affixed to it, in such a manner
that the text is clearly legible, the following warning prominently
displayed in boldface letters that are at least 3 mm in height:

**CONTENTS UNDER PRESSURE. HANDLE WITH CARE.**

**[CONTENU SOUS PRESSION. MANIPULER AVEC SOIN.]{.otherLang lang="fr"}**

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Code --- date of manufacture

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A carbonated beverage glass
container must be coded in such a manner that its date of manufacture is
identifiable.

## [Quality Control]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831180 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}System of control and documents

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who manufactures or imports a carbonated beverage glass
  container must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish and maintain a system of control and
    inspection to ensure that the carbonated beverage glass container
    meets the requirements set out in sections 2 and 3; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prepare and maintain documents describing tests
    carried out on the carbonated beverage glass container and the
    results of such tests.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period and examination by
  inspector

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The manufacturer or importer must keep the documents
  for a period of two years, and have the documents available during
  that period for examination by an inspector at all reasonable times.

## [SCHEDULE 1]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Drill Test --- Perforation]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-831194 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The following method is to be used to test whether a carbonated
  beverage glass container shatters in such a way as to cause
  perforation:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as illustrated in the figure of the test apparatus,
    provide

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a flat and horizontal support surface that is 10
      cm in diameter,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on a supporting structure of four corner posts
      16 mm in diameter, four straight rods 16 mm in diameter, forming a
      square frame with outside dimensions of 80 cm for each side
      centred on the vertical centreline through the support surface and
      parallel to, and with the upper edges of the rods 80 cm above, the
      support surface,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a drill probe of adjustable height, with a
      horizontal axial centreline in a vertical plane that passes
      equidistant between two opposite sides of the square frame,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a sharp "MM Glazemaster Glass Drill, 4.5 mm,
      Type A", affixed to and axially centred on the drill probe,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a drill probe drive mechanism capable of rotating
      the probe 0.2 revolutions per second while advancing the drill
      0.254 mm per second towards the vertical centreline through the
      support surface, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} two support points for the container of
      adjustable height, located and oriented as shown in the figure;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cover the top and the four vertical sides of the
    supporting structure using clear polyethylene plastic sheets of a
    thickness of 0.035 ± 0.005 mm, and using supplementary means of
    attachment as required, so that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a single sheet of plastic, 80 cm wide, is
      suspended across the side facing the drill probe,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} two sheets of plastic, each 33 cm wide, are
      suspended on the side where the drill probe is located with a gap
      of 14 cm between the two sheets, symmetrical with the drill probe
      centreline,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a single sheet of plastic, 80 cm wide, is
      suspended across the top and the other two sides,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the lower edges of the plastic sheets extend
      below the plane of the support surface, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the lower edges of the plastic sheets are
      subjected to a uniformly distributed downward force of 39 N/m of
      width by the use of weight rods;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} remove from the container any packaging and label
    that does not remain on it after its sale and during its use;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the unopened container is equilibrated
    to a test temperature by storing it in a water bath at 22°C for four
    hours and is equilibrated as to pressure at that temperature by
    ultrasonic agitation immediately before the test;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the plastic sheets are free of any
    perforation;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} adjust the drill probe and the support points to
    the mid-height of the container;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} place the container upright and unopened on the
    support surface and against the support points;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} adjust the probe drive mechanism to a linear
    velocity of 0.254 mm per second and a rotational velocity of
    0.2 revolutions per second, and advance the drill probe until it
    pierces the container; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the container shatters, note whether any piece
    or part of the container has perforated the plastic sheets above the
    horizontal plane of the support surface.

## [SCHEDULE 2]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 3)]{.OriginatingRef}[Drop Test --- Distance of Projection]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-831220 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The following method is to be used to test the distance of
  projection of a carbonated beverage glass container that has
  shattered:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as illustrated in the figure of the test apparatus,
    provide

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a block of concrete, 50 cm long, 40 cm wide and
      20 cm high, having a smooth, flat and horizontal impact surface,
      positioned on a flat and horizontal concrete base,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a rigid cylinder, with an inside diameter of 2 m
      and a height of 20 cm, placed vertically on the concrete base so
      that it is concentric with the concrete block, and the upper edge
      of the cylinder is in the plane of the impact surface, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a drop mechanism, consisting of two horizontal
      support bars of 6 mm diameter with their centrelines 4 cm apart
      positioned symmetrically over the longitudinal centreline of the
      impact surface and with their upper edges 75 cm above the impact
      surface, capable of permitting a free fall of the container from
      its horizontal support position by a snap action withdrawal of the
      two support bars in symmetrical paths downward and away from the
      container while maintaining the bar centrelines horizontal;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} remove from the container any packaging and label
    that does not remain on it after its sale and during its use;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the unopened container is equilibrated
    to a test temperature by storing it in a water bath at 25°C for four
    hours and is equilibrated as to pressure at that temperature by
    ultrasonic agitation immediately before the test;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} clear and clean the area inside and outside the
    cylinder;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} place the container horizontal and unopened,
    centred between and on the support bars, with the vertical plane
    through the mid-height of the container passing through the
    transverse centreline of the impact surface;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} activate the drop mechanism allowing the container
    to fall freely on the impact surface;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the container shatters,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} collect, clean, dry and weigh all pieces and
      parts of the container, including the top and label, that have
      travelled beyond the outside diameter of the cylinder, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} collect, clean, dry and weigh all pieces and
      parts of the container, including the top and label, that have
      remained within the inside diameter of the cylinder; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} calculate the dry weight of the container by adding
    the weights obtained in subparagraphs (g)(i) and (ii).
