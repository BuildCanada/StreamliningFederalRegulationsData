## Canada -- European Union Tariff Withdrawal Order (United Kingdom) {#canada-european-union-tariff-withdrawal-order-united-kingdom .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2020-286
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 2020-12-21

Canada -- European Union Tariff Withdrawal Order (United Kingdom)

P.C. 2020-1131 2020-12-20
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas Her Excellency the Governor General in Council considers that
goods originating from the United Kingdom are not entitled to the Canada
-- European Union Tariff under the Canada -- European Union
Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph
49.91(1)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-3743_hq_24307){#fn_81000-2-3743_hq_24307-ID0EGCAA}
of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-3743_hq_24308){#fn_81000-2-3743_hq_24308-ID0EDCAA},
makes the annexed [Canada -- European Union Tariff Withdrawal Order
(United Kingdom)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-286).

## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1281043 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order, [[United
Kingdom]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the Channel Islands, Gibraltar
and the Isle of Man, but does not include Anguilla, the British
Antarctic Territory, the British Indian Ocean Territory, Bermuda, the
British Virgin Islands, the Cayman Islands, the Falkland Islands,
Montserrat, Pitcairn, Saint Helena and Dependencies (Ascension Island
and Tristan Da Cunha), South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands and
the Turks and Caicos Islands.

## [Withdrawal of Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1281045 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Entitlement to the benefit
of the Canada -- European Union Tariff is withdrawn in respect of all
goods that originate in the United Kingdom.

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1281047 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Section 2 does not apply to
goods that were in transit to Canada on or before January 1, 2021.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1281051 .Part}
