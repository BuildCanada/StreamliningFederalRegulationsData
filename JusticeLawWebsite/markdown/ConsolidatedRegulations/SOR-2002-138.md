## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-680151 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Moose Jaw Airport,
    situated in the Municipality of Moose Jaw, in the Province of
    Saskatchewan. ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[airport zoning reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport zoning reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a point
    at the airport 573.02 m above sea level, more particularly described
    in Part 1 of the schedule. ([point de repère du zonage de
    l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[approach surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approach surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the imaginary
    inclined planes that extend upward and outward from each end of a
    strip, more particularly described in Part 3 of the schedule.
    ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary plane
    located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, more
    particularly described in Part 5 of the schedule, the outer limits
    of which are described in Part 6 of the schedule. ([surface
    extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a rectangular portion of the
    landing area of the airport, including the runway, that is prepared
    for the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
    more particularly described in Part 2 of the schedule.
    ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
    inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
    limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, more particularly
    described in Part 4 of the schedule. ([surface de
    transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-680159 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of all lands, other than lands that form part of the airport but
including public road allowances, that are adjacent to, or in the
vicinity of, the airport, the outer limits of which lands are described
in Part 7 of the schedule.

## [Building Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-680161 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No person shall place,
erect or construct or permit to be placed, erected or constructed on any
land in respect of which these Regulations apply any building, structure
or object, or an addition to any existing building, structure or object,
the highest point of which would exceed in elevation at the location of
the building, structure or object

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an approach surface;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a transitional surface.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-680166 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No owner or lessee of any
lands in respect of which these Regulations apply shall permit an object
of natural growth to exceed in elevation any of the surfaces referred to
in section 3 that project immediately over and above the surface of the
land at the location of the object.

## [Electronic Zoning]{.HTitleText1} {#h-680168 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No owner or lessee of any
lands in respect of which these Regulations apply shall permit those
lands or any part of them to be used in a manner that may cause
interference with aeronautical communications.

## [Bird Hazards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-680170 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), in order to reduce bird hazards to aviation, no
  owner or lessee of lands in respect of which these Regulations apply
  shall permit those lands or any part of them to be used as a site for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a sanitary land fill;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a food garbage disposal site;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a sewage lagoon; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an open water storage reservoir.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An owner or lessee of lands to which these
  Regulations apply, other than those lands described in Part 8 of the
  schedule, may permit those lands or any part of them to be used as a
  site for an open water storage reservoir in either of the following
  cases:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total surface area of the reservoir does not
    exceed one hectare; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of lands that are dry ponds,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} measures are taken to reduce bird hazards to
      aviation, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the approval of the Minister is obtained if the
      lands are used for open water storage for a period in excess of
      48 hours more than once per calendar year.

### [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Zoning Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-1description-of-the-airport-zoning-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport zoning reference point is a point on the centre line of
runway 10L-28R at the beginning of that runway for approach 28R.

### [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-2description-of-each-strip .SchedHeadL1}

Each strip, shown on Department of Public Works and Government Services
Canada Moose Jaw Airport Zoning Plan No. E.3054, dated August 1997, is
described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 10L-28R is 300 m in
  width, 150 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway, and
  2,656.25 m in length;

### [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-4description-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Department of Public Works and
Government Services Canada Moose Jaw Airport Zoning Plan No. E.3054,
dated August 1997, being a surface consisting of an inclined plane
rising at a ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 7 m measured
horizontally at right angles to the centre line and centre line produced
of each strip and extending upward and outward from the lateral limits
of each strip and its approach surfaces to an intersection with the
outer surface or another transitional surface of an adjoining strip.

### [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-5description-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Department of Public Works and Government
Services Canada Moose Jaw Airport Zoning Plan No. E.3054, dated
August 1997, is an imaginary surface that consists of a common plane
established at a constant elevation of 45 m above the assigned elevation
of the airport zoning reference point, except that, where the common
plane is less than 9 m above the ground at any point, the outer surface
is an imaginary plane located at 9 m above the ground.
