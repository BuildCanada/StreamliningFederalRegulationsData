## Canadian Films and Video Tapes Certification Fees Order {#canadian-films-and-video-tapes-certification-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/82-550
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1982-05-28

Order Prescribing the Fees to be Paid by Persons in the Private Sector
for Services Provided in Relation to the Certification of Canadian Films
and Video Tapes
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Communications, pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) and Order in Council
P.C. 1982-1394 of 6 May, 1982[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBCA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Order prescribing the fees to be paid by persons in the private sector
for services provided in relation to the certification of Canadian Films
and video tapes](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-550).

Dated at Ottawa, May 28, 1982

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874211 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Canadian Films and Video Tapes Certification Fees
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-550).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874213 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[certification service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certification service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a service
    provided with respect to an advance ruling, a provisional approval
    or a certificate given or issued with respect to the certification
    of films or tapes; ([service d'émission de visas]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[feature production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[feature production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a motion picture
    film or tape of not less than 75 minutes running time; ([production
    long métrage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[film or tape]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[film or tape]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a film or tape referred
    to in subsection 1104(2) of the [Income Tax
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._945); ([film ou bande
    magnétoscopique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[film or tape production budget]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[film or tape production budget]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where a film or tape is produced by a person in
      the private sector, the total financial contribution of that
      person to the production,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where a film or tape is co-produced under an
      agreement entered into between Canada and another country, the
      total financial contribution of Canadian producers to the
      co-production covered by the agreement, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where a film or tape is co-produced by persons in
      the private sector and by the public sector, the total financial
      contribution of the persons in the private sector to the
      co-production; ([budget de production du film ou de la bande
      magnétoscopique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Communications; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[review of a certification service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[review of a certification service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    review of a certification service undertaken by the Minister where
    the Minister has indicated in writing his intention to deny the
    certification; ([révision d'un service d'émission de
    visas]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[short production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[short production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a motion picture film
    or tape of less than 75 minutes running time. ([production court
    métrage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874225 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the fee payable by a person in the private sector
  who applies for a certification service shall be equal to one
  thousandth of the film production budget.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fee payable pursuant to subsection (1) shall not
  be less than \$25 or more than \$500.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The fee payable by a person
in the private sector who applies for a review of a certification
service shall be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$500 with respect to a short production; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$1,500 with respect to a feature production.
