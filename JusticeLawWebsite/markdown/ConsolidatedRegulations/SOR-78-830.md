## Wood Buffalo National Park Game Regulations {#wood-buffalo-national-park-game-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/78-830
:::

[CANADA NATIONAL PARKS ACT](/eng/acts/N-14.01)

Registration 1978-11-03

Regulations Respecting Game in Wood Buffalo National Park

P.C. 1978-3324 1978-11-02
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
subsection 7(1) of the National Parks Act, is pleased hereby to revoke
the Wood Buffalo Park Game Regulations made by Order in Council P.C.
1954-1739 of 18th November, 1954[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFCAA}, as amended[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^2^](#fn_2e){#fn_2e-ID0EDCAA}, and to make the annexed
[Regulations respecting game in Wood Buffalo National
Park](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-830).

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-864275 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only in Wood Buffalo National Park.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to the superintendent or a Park Warden while he is engaged in the
performance of his duties.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-420, s. 8
:::

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[General Prohibitions and Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-864280 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} handle, disturb, destroy, remove or have in his
  possession any egg or nest of a wild bird, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} handle game, feed game or entice game to approach a
  person by setting out foodstuffs,

unless he does so under and in accordance with a permit issued pursuant
to these Regulations.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cut, spear, break, destroy or interfere with a
    beaver house or beaver dam;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} kill a female bear with cubs under one year of age
    except in defence of human life; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} kill a bear cub under one year of age.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who kills a bear referred to in
  paragraph (1)(b) in defence of human life shall report the killing to
  a park warden within 24 hours after the killing or, if circumstances
  do not permit a report to be made within that time period, as soon as
  possible thereafter.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall hunt migratory birds unless the
  person hunts in accordance with these Regulations and the [Migratory
  Birds Regulations, 2022](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-105).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-657, s. 2
- [SOR/2022-105, s. 87](#1348311-1373130){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1348311-1373130.html"
  role="button"}
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-6-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 6"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No person shall hunt, trap
or discharge a firearm within eight hundred metres of the shoreline of
Pine Lake from April 1 to October 31 in any year.

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), no person shall, for any purpose, enter the
  whooping crane nesting areas, described in Schedule I, during the
  period from April 15 to October 31 in each year.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may grant permission to any person
  to enter an area referred to in subsection (1), where it is necessary
  for the protection of wildlife.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-657, s. 3
- SOR/91-420, s. 8
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} hunt game except during an open season for that game
  as set out in Schedule II or in a notice made pursuant to subsection
  56(2) or (3);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} take or kill game in excess of the bag limits for
  that game as set out in Schedule II; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have in his possession any live game or any game
  taken or killed in violation of these Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-657, s. 4
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** No person shall hunt or
be in possession of any game specified in Schedule III.

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall implement an agreement entered into with another person
  for assistance to trap game in a trapping area unless that agreement
  has been approved by the superintendent pursuant to subsection (2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall approve an agreement
  referred to in subsection (1) where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} after discussion with the group leader for the
    trapping area and having regard to all the circumstances in that
    area, the superintendent believes on reasonable grounds that such an
    agreement for assistance will not seriously and adversely affect
    wildlife or the interests of other trappers in respect of that
    trapping area;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person requiring assistance holds a trapping
    permit;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person with whom the agreement is made holds a
    general hunting permit; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person requiring assistance has demonstrated
    that an adequate livelihood from the trapping of game cannot be
    obtained without such assistance.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may, before approving an agreement
  pursuant to subsection (2), establish conditions of approval to ensure
  that the agreement will not adversely affect wildlife or the interests
  of other trappers in respect of a trapping area.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where an agreement is approved pursuant to subsection
  (2), the superintendent shall endorse the general hunting permit of
  the person with whom the agreement is made and shall inscribe on that
  general hunting permit the conditions of approval, if any, as
  described in subsection (3).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-657, s. 5
- SOR/91-420, ss. 2(F), 8
:::

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[General Hunting Permits, Trapping Permits and Certificates of Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-864318 .Part}

### [Issue of Permits and Certificates]{.HTitleText2} {#h-864319 .Subheading}

  other than fur bearing animals may be issued by the superintendent to
  a resident who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} was eligible to hold a general hunting licence
    pursuant to the former Regulations; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is the spouse, or child over 16 years of age of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a person who holds a general hunting permit, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a person who held a general hunting permit or a
      general hunting licence prior to his death.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A trapping permit
authorizing the holder thereof to hunt fur bearing animals may be issued
by the superintendent to any person who

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is the holder of a general hunting permit; and

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

  two or more persons, if each person is the holder of a general hunting
  permit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a certificate of registration is issued

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the certificate, where the

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the certificate and the second group

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the certificate and the majority of

superintendent may remove the name of a person from a certificate of
registration if he has

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a written request from the holder of the certificate;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} written approval from a majority of the members of
  the group.

### [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-864349 .Subheading}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** No person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} hunt game unless he is the holder of a general
  hunting permit; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} assist in hunting game unless he is the spouse, or
  child under 18 years of age, of the holder of a general hunting permit
  and is accompanied by the permit holder.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** No person shall, within a
trapping area, hunt fur bearing animals, set traps, skin or prepare or
assist in the skinning or preparation of pelts unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} he holds a trapping permit and is named in a
  certificate of registration for that area or is the spouse, or child
  under 18 years of age, of the holder of a trapping permit for that
  area;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} he is an assistant, under an agreement entered into
  pursuant to section 11, of a person who holds a trapping permit and is
  named in a certificate of registration for that area; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} he is authorized to take or kill fur bearing animals
  for scientific purposes pursuant to paragraph 56(1)(a) and has
  notified the holder of the certificate of registration for that area.

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-864357 .Subheading}

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
general hunting permit, trapping permit or certificate of registration
shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be made on a form supplied by the superintendent; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} contain the information required by the form and any
  additional information requested by the superintendent.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/81-357, s. 1
- SOR/91-420, s. 8
:::

### [Signature]{.HTitleText2} {#h-864363 .Subheading}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** No general hunting
permit, trapping permit or certificate of registration is valid unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is signed by the person to whom it is issued; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person signing by a mark, the mark
  is witnessed and the signature of the witness is endorsed thereon.

### [Expiry Date]{.HTitleText2} {#h-864367 .Subheading}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  general hunting permit or trapping permit expires on the expiry date
  mentioned in the permit, or if no expiry date is mentioned, on the
  30th day of June next following its date of issue.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A certificate of registration expires at the end of
  the fifth permit year following the date of its issue.

### [Renewal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-864371 .Subheading}

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/91-420,
s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may refuse to renew a trapping permit and may,
  notwithstanding section 15, remove the name of the holder of the
  trapping permit from a certificate of registration if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder did not, without reasonable excuse,
    engage in hunting and trapping fur bearing animals in any year in
    which the certificate of registration was in effect; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the superintendent has obtained the written
    agreement of the group leader or a majority of the group members of
    that trapping area in respect of which the certificate of
    registration was issued, as the case may be, for the refusal and
    removal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a person whose name is removed from a
  certificate of registration pursuant to subsection (1) was the holder
  of the certificate, the superintendent shall, if the certificate is
  issued on behalf of two or more persons, designate one of the other
  persons named in the certificate to be the holder thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-657, s. 6
- SOR/91-420, s. 8
:::

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a certificate of registration has expired, the holder of the
  certificate shall be given priority over other applicants for the
  renewal of the certificate if he applies for renewal within 60 days
  following the date of expiry.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a certificate of registration has expired and
  has not been renewed, trapping permits shall not be issued for that

returns and information respecting game and trapping and hunting
operations of any person named in the certificate as the superintendent
may reasonably require.

- SOR/87-657, s. 7

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** The holder of a general

when requested to do so by a Park Warden, forthwith submit the permit or
certificate to the Park Warden for inspection.

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Any person having

taking or killing of the game.

### [Suspension and Cancellation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-864391 .Subheading}

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the superintendent has reasonable grounds to believe that the holder
  of a general hunting permit, trapping permit or certificate of
  registration has violated these Regulations, he may suspend and take
  possession of the permit or certificate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A permit or certificate suspended pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall be reinstated and returned to the holder if

## [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[General Rules of the Park]{.HTitleText1} {#h-864421 .Part}

### [Dogs]{.HTitleText2} {#h-864422 .Subheading}

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  owner of a dog shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} permit the dog to run at large or to chase or
    molest game; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the dog for the purpose of hunting game.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a dog is running at large or chasing or
  molesting game, a Park Warden may impound the dog or destroy the dog,
  if he cannot seize it.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a Park Warden impounds or destroys a dog
  pursuant to subsection (2) he shall, if the name of the owner can
  readily be ascertained, forthwith notify the owner of the dog.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} An impounded dog shall not be released until the
  person claiming the dog has paid an impounding fee of \$10 and a
  boarding fee of \$5 for each day or part thereof that the dog has been
  impounded.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the owner of an impounded dog is unknown or
  where the owner of an impounded dog does not claim the dog within five
  days from the date of notification pursuant to subsection (3), the dog
  may be disposed of in a manner satisfactory to the superintendent.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-420, s. 8

### [Firearms]{.HTitleText2} {#h-864433 .Subheading}

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), no person
  shall be in possession of a firearm unless the firearm is unloaded and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the firearm is of a kind or model capable of
    being dismantled by taking apart and separating the barrel and
    stock, it is carried in a dismantled condition; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the firearm is not of a kind or model capable of
    being dismantled, it is carried in a closed case or wrapped and tied
    securely in such manner as not to expose any part of the firearm.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder of a general hunting permit; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person authorized to take or kill game for
    scientific purposes pursuant to paragraph 56(1)(a).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a loaded firearm in or on a motor vehicle; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} discharge a firearm from a motor vehicle.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall discharge a firearm or cause a shot,
  bullet or other missile to pass within two hundred metres of any
  building unless he is the owner or occupier of that building.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No person shall discharge a firearm or cause a shot,
  bullet or other missile to pass along, across or within one hundred
  metres of the center line of any highway.

<!-- -->

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} use a firearm of less than .23 calibre for the
    purpose of hunting big game;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be in possession of ammunition that contains
    non-expanding or steel jacketed bullets; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} use an automatic loading or recoil loading rifle of
    more than .22 calibre that has a capacity of more than five
    cartridges in the magazine.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall, for purposes of hunting or trapping,
  be in possession of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a firearm capable of firing more than one bullet
    during one pressure of the trigger;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a firearm that can be altered to operate as an
    automatic firearm; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a shotgun that is capable of holding more than
    three shells in the magazine and chamber combined unless the
    magazine has been permanently plugged or altered so as to reduce the
    capacity of the firearm to not more than three shells in the
    magazine and chamber combined.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 56(1), no person shall shoot
  fur bearing animals with a shotgun except a shotgun loaded with a
  single ball or bullet.
