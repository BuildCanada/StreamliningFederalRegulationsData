## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-782003 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted to
Husky Oil Operations Ltd., Calgary, Alberta, of the customs duties paid
or payable under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of
the "SeaRose FPSO" that was returned to Canada after having been
exported in 2012 for repair or alteration.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-782005 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the importer files, as requested, all evidence that
  is required by the Canada Border Services Agency to determine
  eligibility for remission; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made by the importer to the
  Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years
  after the day on which this Order comes into force.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-782009 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
