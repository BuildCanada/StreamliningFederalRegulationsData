  shares under section 206 of the Act, that results in the interest of a

  without the consent of the holder and without the substitution of an

  or winding up of the body corporate, a right to share in its assets.

### [[[Resident Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} Class of Persons Prescribed]{.HTitleText2} {#h-677309 .Subheading}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph (b) of the definition [[resident
Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the Act, the
following classes of persons are prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} persons who are full-time employees of the Government
  of Canada or of a province, of an agency of any of those governments
  or of a federal or provincial Crown corporation, if the principal
  reason for their residence outside Canada is to act as employees;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} persons who are full-time employees, if the principal
  reason for their residence outside Canada is to act as employees, of a
  body corporate

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} of which more than 50% of the voting shares is
    beneficially owned, or over which control or direction is exercised,
    by resident Canadians,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a majority of the directors of which are resident
    Canadians, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that is a subsidiary of a body corporate
    described in subparagraph (i) or (ii);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} persons who are full-time students at a university or
  other educational institution recognized by the educational
  authorities of a majority of the provinces of Canada and who have been
  resident outside Canada for fewer than 10 consecutive years;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} persons who are full-time employees of an
  international association or organization of which Canada is a member;
  and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} persons who were, at the time of reaching their
  60th birthday, ordinarily resident in Canada and who have been
  resident outside Canada for fewer than 10 consecutive years.

      ([distinctive]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  :   [[official mark]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an official mark

  :   [[use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the actual use by a person that
      carries on business in Canada or elsewhere.

### [Confusing Names]{.HTitleText2} {#h-677344 .Subheading}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A corporate name is
confusing with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a trademark or an official mark if it is the same as
  that trademark or official mark or if the use of both the corporate
  name and either the trademark or the official mark, as the case may
  be, is likely to lead to the inference that the business carried on or
  intended to be carried on under the corporate name and the business
  connected with the trademark or official mark, as the case may be, are
  one business, whether or not the nature of the business of each is
  generally the same; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a trade-name if it is the same as that trade-name or
  if the use of both names is likely to lead to the inference that the
  business carried on or intended to be carried on under the corporate
  name and the business carried on under the trade-name are one
  business, whether or not the nature of the business of each is
  generally the same.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 12(1) of the Act, a corporate name is prohibited if its use
causes confusion with a trademark, official mark or trade-name, having
regard to the circumstances, including

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the inherent distinctiveness of the whole or any
  element of the trademark, official mark or trade-name and the extent
  to which it has become known;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the length of time that the trademark, official mark
  or trade-name has been in use;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the goods, services or business with
  which the trademark, official mark or trade-name is associated;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the trade with which the trademark,
  official mark or trade-name is associated;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the degree of resemblance between the proposed
  corporate name and the trademark, official mark or trade-name in
  appearance or sound or in the ideas suggested by them; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the geographical area in Canada in which the trade
  name or proposed corporate name is likely to be used.

:   [[delivery shares]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means shares issued by a

[[particular subsidiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[particular subsidiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a subsidiary

### [Prescribed Conditions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-677433 .Subheading}

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 31(4) of the Act, the prescribed conditions are that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the consideration received by the corporation for the
  delivery shares is equal to the fair market value of those shares at
  the time of their issuance;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the class of shares of which the delivery shares are
  a part is widely held and shares of that class are actively traded on
  any of the following stock exchanges in Canada, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Venture Exchange,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} The Montreal Exchange, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Toronto Stock Exchange;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the sole purpose of effecting the acquisition by the
  particular subsidiary of delivery shares is to transfer them, as set
  out in paragraph 37(b), to the shareholders of another body corporate;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} immediately before the acquisition of the delivery
  shares by the particular subsidiary, the other body corporate and its
  shareholders deal at arm's length, to be determined in accordance with
  the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), with the corporation and the
  particular subsidiary; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} immediately before the acquisition of the delivery
  shares by the particular subsidiary, the particular subsidiary and the
  other body corporate are not resident in Canada, for the purposes of
  the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 31(5) of the Act, the prescribed conditions are that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the particular subsidiary does not acquire a
  beneficial interest in the delivery shares as a result of its
  acquisition of those shares and the beneficial interest is acquired by
  the shareholders of the other body corporate;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the acquisition by the particular subsidiary of the
  delivery shares is followed immediately by a transfer of the delivery
  shares by the particular subsidiary to shareholders of the other body
  corporate;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} immediately after the transfer of the delivery shares
  to the shareholders of the other body corporate, the particular
  subsidiary and the other body corporate are not resident in Canada,
  for the purposes of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} after the transfer of the delivery shares to the
  shareholders of the other body corporate, the other body corporate is
  a subsidiary body corporate of the particular subsidiary.

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 31(6) of the Act, the prescribed consequences are that within
30 days after one of the conditions described in section 36 or 37 is not
met or ceases to be met, the corporation shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} cancel the delivery shares on condition that, if the
  articles of the corporation limit the number of authorized shares, the
  delivery shares may be restored to the status of authorized but
  unissued shares;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} return the consideration received by the corporation
  for the delivery shares to the particular subsidiary; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} cancel the entry for the consideration in the
  corporation's stated capital account.

      shareholder or shareholders receiving the advice, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the proxy voting advice is not given on behalf
      of any person soliciting proxies or on behalf of a nominee for

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by a person who does not seek directly or

## [PART 9]{.HLabel1}[Constrained Share Corporations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-677630 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-677631 .Subheading}

**[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Part.

[[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a resident Canadian;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a partnership of which a majority of the members
      are resident Canadians and in which interests representing more
      than 50% of the total value of the partnership property are owned
      by resident Canadians;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a trust established by a resident Canadian

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a majority of the trustees of which are
        resident Canadians, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in which beneficial interests representing
        more than 50% of the total value of the trust property are owned
        by resident Canadians;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Canada or of a province
      or territory of Canada or a municipal corporation or public board
      or commission in Canada; or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a body corporate

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} incorporated under the laws of Canada or a
        province,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} of which a majority of the directors are
        resident Canadians, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} over which persons described in any of
        paragraphs (a) to (d) or in this paragraph exercise control or
        direction or of which the persons beneficially own shares or
        securities currently convertible into shares carrying more than
        50% of the voting rights under all circumstances or by reason of
        the occurrence of an event that has occurred and that is
        continuing, including currently exercisable options or rights to
        acquire the shares or convertible securities.
        ([Canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[constrained class]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[constrained class]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the class of persons
    specified in the articles of a constrained share corporation as
    being ineligible to hold, as a class, more than the maximum
    aggregate holdings. ([catégorie restreinte]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[constrained share corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[constrained share corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    corporation that has provisions in its articles imposing a
    constraint. ([société par actions à participation
    restreinte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[constraint]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[constraint]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a restriction on

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the issue or transfer of shares of any class or
      series to persons who are not resident Canadians;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the issue or transfer of shares of any class or
      series to enable a corporation or any of its affiliates or
      associates to qualify under a law referred to in
      paragraph 87(1)(a)

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to obtain a licence to carry on any business,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to become a publisher of a Canadian newspaper
        or periodical, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to acquire shares of a financial intermediary
        as defined in paragraph 87(1)(b); or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the issue, transfer or ownership of shares of any
      class or series in order to assist a corporation or any of its
      affiliates or associates to qualify under a law referred to in
      subsection 87(2) to receive licences, permits, grants, payments or
      other benefits by reason of attaining or maintaining a specified
      level of Canadian ownership or control.
      ([restriction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[control]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[control]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means control in any manner that
    results in control in fact, whether directly through the ownership
    of shares or indirectly through a trust, a contract, the ownership
    of shares of any other body corporate or otherwise.
    ([contrôle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[maximum aggregate holdings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[maximum aggregate holdings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the total
    number of voting shares of a constrained share corporation that may
    be held by or on behalf of persons in the constrained class and
    their associates in accordance with the articles of the corporation.
    ([avoir maximum total]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[maximum individual holdings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[maximum individual holdings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the total
    number of voting shares of a constrained share corporation that may
    be held by or on behalf of any one person in the constrained class
    and their associates in accordance with the articles of the
    corporation. ([avoir maximum individuel]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[voting share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[voting share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a share that is subject
    to a constraint referred to in paragraph (a) or (b) of the
    definition [[constraint]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and that carries
    voting rights under all circumstances or by reason of the occurrence
    of an event that has occurred and that is continuing, and includes a
    security currently convertible into such a share and a currently
    exercisable option or right to acquire such a share or convertible
    security. ([action avec droit de vote]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Limitation on Voting Rights]{.HTitleText2} {#h-677683 .Subheading}

**[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77 .sectionLabel}** Sections 78 and 79 apply
to a constrained share corporation that has provisions in its articles
imposing a constraint referred to in paragraph (a) or (b) of the
definition [[constraint]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 73.

- **[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, on
  the day on which a corporation becomes a constrained share
  corporation, the total number of voting shares of the corporation held
  by or on behalf of a person in the constrained class exceeds the
  maximum individual holdings, the person or their nominee may only, in
  person or by proxy, exercise the voting rights attached to the maximum
  individual holdings so held on that day or on any later day.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} After the total number of shares held by or on behalf
  of the person referred to in subsection (1) is reduced below the
  maximum individual holdings, they or their nominee may, in person or
  by proxy, exercise the voting rights attached to shares held.

<!-- -->

- **[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as provided in subsection 78(1), if the total number of voting shares
  of a constrained share corporation held by or on behalf of a person in
  the constrained class exceeds the maximum individual holdings, no
  person shall, in person or by proxy, exercise the voting rights
  attached to those shares.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If it appears from the securities register of a
  constrained share corporation that the total number of voting shares
  held by a shareholder is less than the maximum individual holdings, a
  proxyholder for the shareholder may vote those shares unless the
  proxyholder has knowledge that the shares beneficially owned by the
  shareholder exceed the maximum individual holdings.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, after the day on which a corporation becomes a
  constrained share corporation, a corporation or trust that was not a
  person in the constrained class becomes a person in the constrained
  class, the corporation or trust shall not exercise the voting rights
  attached to any shares it holds in the constrained share corporation
  while it is a person in the constrained class.

## [PART 10]{.HLabel1}[Rules of Procedure for Applications for Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-677806 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-677807 .Subheading}

**[[88]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-88 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to
every application for an exemption under subsection 2(6), 10(2), 82(3)
or 151(1), section 156 or subsection 171(2) or 187(11) of the Act.

### [Time of Filing Applications]{.HTitleText2} {#h-677809 .Subheading}

- **[[89]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-89 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for an exemption under

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subsection 2(6) of the Act may be made at any time;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subsection 10(2) or 187(11) of the Act shall be
    made before the date of issue of the certificate of continuance
    referred to in subsection 187(4) of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subsection 82(3) of the Act shall be made at least
    30 days before the corporation is required to comply with Part VIII
    of the Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} subsection 151(1) of the Act shall be made before
    the date of the notice referred to in subsection 149(1) of the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} section 156 of the Act shall be made at least
    60 days before the documents in respect of which the exemption is
    requested are to be sent to the Director; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} subsection 171(2) of the Act may be made at any
    time.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Director shall extend the
  time for making an application for an exemption if the applicant
  establishes that no prejudice will result from the extension.

### [Notice by Director of Decision]{.HTitleText2} {#h-677819 .Subheading}

**[[90]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-90 .sectionLabel}** The Director shall,
within 30 days after receipt of an application for an exemption, grant
the exemption requested or send to the applicant written notice of the
Director's refusal, together with reasons for the refusal.

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-677821 .Subheading}

**[[91]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91 .sectionLabel}** The Director may request
that an applicant for an exemption provide the Director with further
information or that any other person provide the Director with
information in writing that is relevant to the application.

**[[92]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-92 .sectionLabel}** The Director shall give
the applicant for an exemption a copy of any information received from
any other person under section 91 and shall allow the applicant a
reasonable opportunity to respond in writing.

**[[93]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-93 .sectionLabel}** If an applicant for an
exemption or a person from whom the Director has requested information
under section 91 does not provide the information within the time
specified by the Director, the Director may deal with the application
without regard to the information.

**[[94]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-94 .sectionLabel}** If the Director does not
grant an exemption or send written notice of the Director's refusal
within the time specified in section 90, the applicant may exercise
their rights under section 246 of the Act as if the Director had refused
the exemption.

## [PART 11]{.HLabel1}[Value of Total Financial Interest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-677826 .Part}

**[[95]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-95 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 237.5(1)(b) of the Act, the prescribed amount of the value of
the plaintiff's total financial interest is \$20,000.

## [PART 12]{.HLabel1}[Cancellation of Articles and Certificates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-677828 .Part}

- **[[96]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-96 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of subsection 265.1(1) of the Act, the prescribed
  circumstances are that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is an obvious error in the articles or in the
    related certificate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is an error in the articles or in the related
    certificate that was made by the Director;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the cancellation of the articles and related
    certificate is ordered by a court; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Director lacked the authority to issue the
    articles and related certificate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection 265.1(3) of the Act,
  the prescribed circumstances are that there is no dispute among the
  directors or shareholders as to the circumstances of the request for
  cancellation and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the corporation has not used the articles and
    related certificate; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it has used them, anyone dealing with the
    corporation on the basis of the articles and related certificate has
    consented to the cancellation.
