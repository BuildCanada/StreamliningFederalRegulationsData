## Mobile Offshore Drilling Units Remissions Order, 2004 {#mobile-offshore-drilling-units-remissions-order-2004 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-107
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 2004-05-03

Mobile Offshore Drilling Units Remissions Order, 2004

P.C. 2004-535 2004-05-03
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 115 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnote1){#footnote1-ID0EDBAA} hereby makes the
annexed Mobile Offshore Drilling Units Remission Order, 2004.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea-){#footnotea}S.C.
  1997, c. 36
  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708783 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order

[[mobile offshore drilling units]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mobile offshore drilling units]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    drilling platforms, classified under tariff item No. 8905.20.10 of
    the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote1){#footnote1-ID0EGBBA} , and
    jack-ups, drill ships and semi-submersibles, classified under tariff
    item No. 8905.90.10 of the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote1){#footnote1-ID0EDBBA}. ([unités
    mobiles de forage au large]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote1-ID0EGBBA){#footnote1}S.C. 1997, c.
      36
      :::

[[delineation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[delineation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means drilling a conduit or well
    subsequent to the drilling of an exploration well for the purpose of
    defining the size (volume) and extent of the hydrocarbon reservoir.
    ([délimitation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708787 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote1){#footnote1-ID0EDBA}, as reduced by the
[Vessel Duties Reduction or Removal
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-304), on the temporary importation
of mobile offshore drilling units.

- ::: Footnote
  ^1^S.C. 1997, c. 36
  :::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708789 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the mobile offshore drilling units are used only in
  drilling activity for exploration, delineation or development of the
  offshore project;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the mobile offshore drilling units are temporarily
  imported into Canada during the period beginning on May 4, 2004 and
  ending on May 4, 2014 for exploration, delineation or development
  activities;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the importers file such evidence as may be required
  by the Canada Border Services Agency to determine eligibility for
  remission; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness before May 4, 2016.
