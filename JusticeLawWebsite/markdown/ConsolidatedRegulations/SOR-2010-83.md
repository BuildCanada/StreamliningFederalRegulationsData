## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767883 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[certificated person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certificated person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in subsection 1(1) of the [Marine Personnel
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-115). ([personne
    brevetée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fire alarm signal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fire alarm signal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the continuous
    sounding of a vessel's fire alarm. ([signal
    d'alarme-incendie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fishing vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fishing vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 1(1) of the [Marine Personnel
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-115). ([bâtiment de
    pêche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[general emergency alarm signal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[general emergency alarm signal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    succession of seven or more short blasts followed by one long blast
    on the whistle or siren of a vessel. ([signal d'alarme générale en
    cas d'urgence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[marine evacuation system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marine evacuation system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an appliance
    for the rapid transfer of persons from the embarkation deck of a
    vessel to a floating survival craft. ([dispositif d'évacuation en
    mer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[near coastal voyage, Class 1]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[near coastal voyage, Class 1]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
    meaning as in section 1 of the [Vessel Safety Certificates
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135). ([voyage à proximité du
    littoral, classe 1]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[rescue boat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[rescue boat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a boat designed to be used
    for rescuing persons in distress and marshalling survival craft and,
    for greater certainty, includes an emergency boat that performs the
    same functions. ([canot de secours]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sheltered waters voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sheltered waters voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in section 1 of the [Vessel Safety Certificates
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135). ([voyage en eaux
    abritées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[survival craft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[survival craft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a lifeboat, rescue
    boat, emergency boat, suitable boat, buoyant apparatus, life raft,
    inflatable rescue platform or any other vessel capable of sustaining
    the lives of persons in distress from the time of abandoning the
    vessel on which those persons were carried. ([bateau de
    sauvetage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unlimited voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unlimited voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 1 of the [Vessel Safety Certificates
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135). ([voyage
    illimité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767896 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of self-propelled Canadian vessels that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are Safety Convention vessels; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are required to hold a safety certificate under
    section 12 of the [Vessel Safety Certificates
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fishing vessels of 150 gross tonnage or less;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cable ferries; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} vessels of 15 gross tonnage or less that carry 12
    or fewer passengers.

## [Emergency Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767907 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767908 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Muster lists

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 4, the master of a vessel shall ensure that, at all times,
  there is a muster list and, in the case of a vessel that is equipped
  with rescue boats, a rescue boat muster list.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Posting

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The master of the vessel shall ensure that the lists
  required under subsection (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are drawn up in either or both official languages,
    according to the needs of the crew; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are conspicuously posted throughout the vessel,
    including the navigation bridge, the engine room and the crew
    accommodations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Separate document

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The rescue boat muster list may be a document
  separate from the muster list or an addendum to it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The master of a vessel who
has a means of informing crew members of the essential actions to be
taken during an emergency is not required to ensure that there is a
muster list or a rescue boat muster list if the vessel

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} has a crew of fewer than three members and carries
  passengers; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a crew of fewer than five members and does not
  carry passengers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Illustrations and instructions

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a vessel shall, before the vessel embarks on a voyage,
  prepare and post illustrations and instructions that inform passengers
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the alarm signals used to indicate emergencies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the essential actions to be taken during an
    emergency;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the location of their designated muster stations;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the method of correctly donning a lifejacket.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Posting

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The master of the vessel shall ensure that the
  illustrations and instructions required under subsection (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are drawn up in both official languages; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are conspicuously posted in passenger staterooms,
    at muster stations and in other passenger spaces.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Updated information

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The master of a vessel
shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure that any information that is posted on board
  the vessel in accordance with these Regulations is kept up to date and
  is legible; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} revise or replace the muster list or rescue boat
  muster list if there is a change in the crew of the vessel.

### [Muster List]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767937 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a vessel shall ensure that a muster list contains the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the general emergency alarm
    signal, the fire alarm signal and, if the vessel has one, the public
    address system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the order to abandon ship is
    given;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an indication of the station at which each crew
    member is to report when the general emergency alarm signal or the
    fire alarm signal is sounded;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the specific duties assigned to
    each crew member and to be performed by the crew member when the
    general emergency alarm signal or the fire alarm signal is sounded,
    including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} closing the watertight doors, fire doors, valves,
      scuppers, side scuttles, skylights, portholes and other similar
      openings in the vessel,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} equipping the survival craft and the other life
      saving appliances,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} ensuring that the radio life saving equipment
      is placed on board the appropriate survival craft,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} preparing and launching the survival craft,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} generally preparing the other life saving
      appliances,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} mustering the passengers,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} using the radiocommunication equipment,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} performing the duties of fire parties, and

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} performing any special duties assigned in
      respect of the use of the fire fighting equipment and
      installations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the substitutes for key persons who may become
    disabled;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the crew member responsible for each survival craft
    and, if applicable, that member's second-in-command;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the crew member assigned as principal communicator
    in accordance with section 267 of the [Marine Personnel
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-115); and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the crew members who are assigned to ensure that
    the life saving appliances and fire fighting equipment and
    installations are maintained in good condition and are ready for
    immediate use.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The master of a vessel that carries passengers shall
  ensure that, in addition to the information set out in subsection (1),
  the muster list contains the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a list of the actions to be taken by the passengers
    when the general emergency alarm signal or the fire alarm signal is
    sounded; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the duties assigned to crew
    members and to be performed by them in relation to the passengers
    during an emergency, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} warning the passengers of the emergency,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} ensuring that the passengers are adequately
      dressed for protection against exposure and have donned their
      lifejackets correctly,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} assembling the passengers at their designated
      muster stations,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} locating and rescuing passengers who are trapped
      in their staterooms or who are otherwise unaccounted for during an
      emergency,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} keeping order in the passageways and on the
      stairways and generally controlling the movements of passengers,
      and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} ensuring that a supply of blankets is taken to
      the survival craft.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional requirements

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** In preparing a muster list,
the master of a vessel shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the number of certificated persons
  required to be on board and employed for each survival craft under
  Division 2 of Part 2 of the [Marine Personnel
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-115) are assigned to that
  survival craft;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensure that each available certificated person is
  assigned duties in connection with the preparation and launching of
  survival craft;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in assigning members of the vessel's crew to the
  crews of the survival craft, ensure an equitable distribution of
  certificated persons, deck officers and persons trained to assist
  others;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel carries motorized survival craft,
  ensure that a person capable of operating the motor and carrying out
  minor adjustments to it is assigned to each motorized survival craft;
  and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in selecting substitutes for key persons in case
  those persons become disabled, take into account that different
  emergencies may call for different actions.

### [Rescue Boat Muster List]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767977 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a vessel shall ensure that a rescue boat muster list
  contains the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the signal that will be sounded to
    muster the rescue boat crew to its designated position;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the members of the rescue boat crew who are to
    report to the designated position; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the duties to be performed by each member of the
    rescue boat crew when that signal is sounded.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificated persons

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In preparing a rescue boat muster list, the master of
  the vessel shall ensure that the number of certificated persons
  required to be on board and employed for each rescue boat under
  section 209 of the [Marine Personnel
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-115) are assigned to that
  rescue boat.

## [Measures Respecting Vessels that Carry Passengers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767986 .Part}

### [Passenger Count and Details]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767987 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Passenger count

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The master of a vessel
that carries passengers shall, before the vessel embarks on a voyage,
ensure that the following information is both communicated to him or her
and recorded:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of persons on board; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} details respecting all persons who have declared a
  need for special care or assistance during an emergency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Passenger details

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a vessel that carries passengers shall, before the vessel
  embarks on one of the following voyages, ensure that the name and
  gender of each person on board the vessel are recorded in a way that
  distinguishes between adults, children and infants:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an unlimited voyage or a near coastal voyage,
    Class 1;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a voyage longer than 12 hours; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a voyage on which there is at least one assigned
    passenger berth.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The master of the vessel shall ensure that
  information recorded under this section is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} kept on shore in a manner that makes it readily
    available to search and rescue services; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} updated if any passengers embark or disembark
    during the voyage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information privacy

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Personal information recorded under this section may
  be used or disclosed only for search and rescue purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in subsection (1).

  [[adult]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[adult]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is 12 years of
      age or older. ([adulte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is five years of
      age or older but under 12 years of age. ([enfant]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[infant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[infant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is under five
      years of age. ([enfant en bas âge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Missing passengers

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The master of a vessel
that carries passengers shall ensure that procedures are in place for
locating and rescuing passengers who are trapped in their staterooms or
who are otherwise unaccounted for during an emergency.

### [Practice Musters and Safety Briefings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-768011 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Practice muster

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  passengers are scheduled to be on board a vessel for more than 24
  hours, the master of the vessel shall ensure that a practice muster of
  the passengers and crew is held as soon as practicable but not later
  than 24 hours after the passengers embark.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safety briefing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If passengers are scheduled to be on board a vessel
  for 24 hours or less and a practice muster is not held, the master of
  the vessel shall ensure that, immediately before or after the vessel
  embarks on a voyage, a safety briefing is given to the passengers
  informing them of the safety and emergency procedures that are
  relevant to the type and size of the vessel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The master of a vessel referred to in subsection (2)
  shall ensure that the safety briefing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} informs the passengers of the essential actions
    they must take during an emergency;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specifies the location of lifejackets, survival
    craft and muster stations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} informs the passengers in each area of the vessel
    of the location of the lifejackets and survival craft that are
    closest to them;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} instructs the passengers in the donning and use of
    their lifejackets;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} is given in either or both official languages,
    according to the needs of the passengers;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} is given on the vessel's public address system if
    the vessel has one; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} is given in a way that is likely to be understood
    by the passengers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New passengers

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If new passengers embark after a practice muster has
  been held on a vessel, the master of the vessel is not required to
  hold another practice muster if a safety briefing that meets the
  requirements of subsection (3) is given to the new passengers before
  the vessel continues on its voyage.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of crew

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** During a practice muster
of passengers and crew, the crew members shall perform the duties
assigned to them, including

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} summoning the passengers to their designated muster
  stations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} keeping order in passageways and on stairways and
  generally controlling the movements of the passengers as they proceed
  to their designated muster stations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} assembling the passengers at their designated muster
  stations and ensuring that they are all accounted for;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} locating and rescuing passengers who are trapped in
  their staterooms or who are otherwise unaccounted for;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} instructing the passengers in dressing adequately for
  protection against exposure and in the donning and use of their
  lifejackets;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} ensuring that the passengers have donned their
  lifejackets correctly;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} ensuring that the passengers are made aware of how
  the order to abandon ship is given; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} instructing the passengers in the actions that they
  must take during an emergency, including how to enter marine
  evacuation systems at the embarkation stations and board survival
  craft.

### [Means of Exit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-768038 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of exit

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a vessel that carries passengers and is made fast at a dock
  for a purpose other than embarking or disembarking passengers shall,
  if there are passengers on board, ensure that the vessel is provided
  with more than one means of exit from the vessel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The means of exit shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} gangways with means of access from the various
    decks in the vessel; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} other means of escape that allow the passengers to
    reach places of safety during an emergency if the vessel's
    conditions of operation do not permit the use of more than one
    gangway.

## [Drills]{.HTitleText1} {#h-768046 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-768047 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Before sounding a signal
for the commencement of a drill, the master of a vessel shall ensure
that all passengers are notified, in either or both official languages,
according to their needs, that a drill will be held and that there is no
actual emergency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of carrying out

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The master of a vessel
shall ensure that drills, in so far as is feasible, are carried out as
if there were an actual emergency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equipment and installations

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The master of a vessel
shall ensure that any equipment or installations used during a drill are
immediately returned to their full operational condition and are ready
for immediate reuse, and that any faults or defects discovered in
equipment or installations during a drill are remedied as soon as
practicable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reporting to stations

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise
instructed in a notification referred to in section 16, if the general
emergency alarm signal or the fire alarm signal is sounded, the
passengers, if any, shall proceed to their designated muster stations
and the crew members shall report to their designated muster stations
and prepare to perform their assigned duties as described in the muster
list.

### [Obligation to Hold and to Participate in Drills]{.HTitleText2} {#h-768056 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intervals

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a vessel described in column 1 of the schedule that is on a
  voyage described in column 2 shall ensure that a fire drill and a
  survival craft drill are held on board the vessel at least once during
  each period set out in column 3.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fire drills

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The master of the vessel shall ensure that a fire
  drill for the crew of the vessel is held within 24 hours after the
  vessel embarks on a voyage if more than 25% of the crew did not
  participate in a fire drill on board the vessel during the month
  before the vessel embarks.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Survival craft drills

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The master of the vessel shall ensure that a survival
  craft drill for the crew of the vessel is held within 24 hours after
  the vessel embarks on a voyage if more than 25% of the crew did not
  participate in a survival craft drill on board the vessel during the
  month before the vessel embarks.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crew participation

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Every member of the crew
of a vessel shall participate in at least one fire drill and one
survival craft drill every month.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional drills

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** In addition to the drills
required under section 20, the master of a vessel shall ensure that
enough fire drills and survival craft drills for the crew of the vessel
are held to ensure that the entire crew is at all times competent and
operationally ready to respond to the emergencies addressed by the
drills.

### [Fire Drills]{.HTitleText2} {#h-768068 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Considerations

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The master of a vessel
shall ensure that fire drills are planned in such a way that due
consideration is given to the practice followed in the various
emergencies that could occur, depending on the type of vessel and its
cargo.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crew duties

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** During a fire drill, the
master of a vessel shall ensure that the crew members perform the duties
assigned to them in connection with the fire drill, including

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} mustering the passengers, if any;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} locating and rescuing passengers, if any, who are
  trapped in their staterooms or who are otherwise unaccounted for;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} locating and rescuing crew members who are trapped in
  their accommodations or who are otherwise unaccounted for;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} checking the operation of the fire doors, fire
  dampers and main inlets and outlets of the ventilation systems;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} closing the fire doors, valves, scuppers, side
  scuttles, skylights, portholes and other similar openings in the
  vessel;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} inspecting and operating the fire pump or, if the
  vessel has one, the emergency fire pump using at least two jets of
  water in order to ensure that the system is in proper working order;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} inspecting the fire fighting equipment that is fitted
  or carried on the vessel, other than the fire fighting equipment
  referred to in paragraph (f), including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} fire fighters' outfits and other personal rescue
    equipment,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the sprinkler systems,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the fire alarm systems,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the fire detection system, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the fire hoses and hydrants;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} inspecting and testing the relevant communications
  equipment, including the public address systems, alarm systems and
  klaxons;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} inspecting and testing the emergency lighting and
  power systems;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} preparing the survival craft and their equipment; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} checking the necessary arrangements for a subsequent
  abandonment of the vessel.

### [Survival Craft Drills]{.HTitleText2} {#h-768089 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crew lists and duties

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  a survival craft drill is held, the person in charge of a survival
  craft and his or her second-in-command shall each have a list of the
  survival craft crew members, and the person in charge shall ensure
  that the crew members know what their duties are.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crew duties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} During a survival craft drill, the master of a vessel
  shall ensure that the crew members perform the duties assigned to them
  in connection with the survival craft drill, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} mustering the passengers, if any;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} locating and rescuing passengers, if any, who are
    trapped in their staterooms or who are otherwise unaccounted for;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} locating and rescuing crew members who are trapped
    in their accommodations or who are otherwise unaccounted for;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} preparing for the launching of the survival craft
    and ensuring that the equipment and supplies, including a supply of
    blankets, that are required to be carried in the survival craft are
    in place and properly stowed;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} inspecting and, if practicable, testing the radio
    life saving equipment that is required to be carried in the survival
    craft;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} operating the davits used for launching life rafts;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel carries motor lifeboats, starting and
    operating the lifeboat motors and verifying that the fuel tanks are
    filled to capacity;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel carries survival craft other than
    lifeboats, participating in instruction in the operation and
    deployment of those survival craft;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel is fitted with a marine evacuation
    system, carrying out the procedures required for the deployment of
    the system up to the point immediately preceding its actual
    deployment;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} testing the emergency lighting for the mustering of
    passengers and crew and for the abandonment of the vessel; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} inspecting and testing the life saving appliances
    that are fitted or carried on the vessel, other than those referred
    to in paragraph (e) or (j) or in section 30.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Donning of suits

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The master of a vessel
shall ensure that the crew members are capable of donning the immersion
suits or marine anti-exposure suits that are carried on the vessel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lifeboats

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a vessel that carries more than one lifeboat shall ensure
  that a different lifeboat is launched by the assigned crew during each
  survival craft drill. However, subject to subsection (2) and
  sections 28 and 29, the master shall also ensure that each lifeboat on
  the vessel --- including a lifeboat that is a rescue boat --- is
  launched and manoeuvred in the water, by the assigned crew, at least
  once every three months during a survival craft drill.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vessel at sea

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When the vessel is at sea, the launching and
  manoeuvring of a lifeboat during a drill referred to in subsection (1)
  may be replaced by the clearing and swinging out of one or more
  lifeboats if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each lifeboat is cleared and swung out at least
    once every month; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} each lifeboat is launched and manoeuvred in the
    water, by the assigned crew, at least once every three months.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vessel under way

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When the vessel is under way, the master of the
  vessel shall ensure that the launching and manoeuvring referred to in
  paragraph (2)(b) are carried out in sheltered waters and under the
  supervision of an officer experienced in launchings and manoeuvrings
  while a vessel is under way.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Free-fall lifeboats --- every three
months

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a vessel that carries free-fall lifeboats shall ensure that
  at least once every three months, during a survival craft drill, the
  crew members

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} board the lifeboats;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} secure themselves properly in their seats; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} carry out the launch procedure up to but not
    including the actual release of the lifeboats.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional steps

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Once the crew members have commenced the launch
  procedure referred to in paragraph (1)(c), the master of the vessel
  shall ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lifeboats are either

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} free-fall launched with only the operating crew
      on board, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} lowered into the water by means of the secondary
      means of launching with or without the operating crew on board;
      and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lifeboats are manoeuvred in the water by the
    operating crew.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Free-fall lifeboats --- every six months

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsection 28(2),
the master of a vessel that carries free-fall lifeboats shall ensure
that at least once every six months, during a survival craft drill,
either

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lifeboats are free-fall launched with only the
  operating crew on board; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a simulated launching of the lifeboats is carried out
  in accordance with the Guidelines for Simulated Launching of Free-Fall
  Lifeboats, the Appendix to Annex 2 of MSC.1/Circ. 1206, Measures to
  Prevent Accidents with Lifeboats, published by the International
  Maritime Organization on May 26, 2006, as amended from time to time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fire-protected lifeboats

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** The master of a vessel
that carries fire-protected lifeboats shall ensure that the water spray
system and the self-contained air supply for those lifeboats are tested
at least once every six months during a survival craft drill.

### [Rescue Boat Drills]{.HTitleText2} {#h-768135 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intervals

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a vessel that is equipped with rescue boats that are not
  lifeboats shall ensure that a rescue boat drill is held, separately
  from any other drill, at least once every month.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crew on board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} During a rescue boat drill, the members of the crew
  of each rescue boat shall launch and manoeuvre the rescue boat in the
  water.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vessel under way

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When the vessel is under way, the master of the
  vessel shall ensure that, during a rescue boat drill, the launching
  and manoeuvring of a rescue boat are carried out in sheltered waters
  and under the supervision of an officer experienced in launchings and
  manoeuvrings while a vessel is under way.

### [Watertight Doors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-768143 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intervals

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The master of a vessel
shall ensure that a drill for the operation of watertight doors is held
at the same time as each fire drill and each survival craft drill.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspections

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** The master of a vessel
shall ensure that the following, if fitted, are inspected at least once
a week:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the watertight doors and all of the mechanisms and
  indicators of those doors; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all of the valves

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the closing of which is necessary to make a
    compartment watertight, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the operation of which is necessary for damage
    control cross-connections.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voyage that exceeds one week

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The master of a vessel
referred to in item 1, 2 or 3 of the schedule that is to embark on a
voyage of more than one week in duration shall ensure that a drill
referred to in section 32 is also held before the vessel embarks on the
voyage.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Daily operation

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** The master of a vessel
referred to in item 1, 2 or 3 of the schedule shall ensure that all of
the watertight doors, whether hinged or power-operated, in the main
transverse bulkheads, in use at sea, are operated daily.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other requirements

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in sections 32 to
35 authorizes the opening of a watertight door or any other appliance
that is required by any regulation to be kept closed.

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-768158 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a vessel shall record and keep the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of each muster of passengers and crew;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a detailed report of each fire drill, including the
    inspection and testing of any fire-fighting equipment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a detailed report of each survival craft drill,
    including the inspection and testing of any life saving appliances;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a detailed report of each rescue boat drill;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the time of the opening or closing of any
    watertight door that may be required to be opened at sea for the
    working of the vessel;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a detailed report of each drill for the operation
    of watertight doors, of each inspection of watertight doors or any
    other appliances referred to in section 33, and of any defects
    discovered;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if a muster or drill required by these Regulations
    is not held, or is held only in part, a report of the circumstances
    and the extent of the muster or drill, and the reason why the muster
    or drill was not held or was held only in part; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} an evaluation of the competency and operational
    readiness of the entire crew of the vessel in responding to the
    emergency addressed by each drill.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entering records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in subsection (1) shall
  be entered in writing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the official log book, if an official log book
    is required by Division 7 of Part 3 of the [Marine Personnel
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-115); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a deck log book or another document, in any
    other case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other documents

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the information referred to in subsection (1) is
  entered in a document referred to in paragraph (2)(b), the master of
  the vessel shall ensure that the document

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is kept on board the vessel for a period of five
    years after the day on which the last entry was made; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is made available to the Minister for inspection
    during that period at the request of the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
