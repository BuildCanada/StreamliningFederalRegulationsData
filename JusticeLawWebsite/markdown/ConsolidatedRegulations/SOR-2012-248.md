## Allocation Method Order (2013) --- Softwood Lumber Products {#allocation-method-order-2013-softwood-lumber-products .Title-of-Act}

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785509 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Export and Import Permits
    Act](/eng/acts/E-19). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[exported]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exported]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by section
    6.4 of the Act. ([exporté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Manitoba quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Manitoba quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the quantity of
    softwood lumber products that may be exported from Manitoba to the
    United States during a month under subsection 6.3(2) of the
    Act. ([quantité pour le Manitoba]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Ontario quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Ontario quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the quantity of
    softwood lumber products that may be exported from Ontario to the
    United States during a month under subsection 6.3(2) of the
    Act. ([quantité pour l'Ontario]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[primary producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[primary producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who produces
    softwood lumber products from softwood sawlogs and, in the case of
    Quebec, includes a person who produces Quebec softwood lumber
    products from softwood sawlogs. ([entreprise de première
    transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[primary producer's products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[primary producer's products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    softwood lumber products that are produced by a primary producer and
    that are not remanufactured in Canada. ([produits d'une entreprise
    de première transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Quebec quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Quebec quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the quantity of
    softwood lumber products that may be exported from Quebec to the
    United States during a month under subsection 6.3(2) of the
    Act. ([quantité pour le Québec]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Quebec softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Quebec softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    softwood lumber products produced in Quebec that were reported to
    the Minister of Natural Resources and Wildlife under the Forest Act,
    R.S.Q., c. F-4.1. ([produits de bois d'œuvre du
    Québec]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reference period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reference period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning
    on November 1, 2009 and ending on October 31, 2012. ([période de
    référence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[remanufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[remanufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
    remanufactures --- within the meaning of subsection 13(1) of the
    [Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act,
    2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55) --- softwood lumber products. ([entreprise
    de seconde transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[remanufacturer's products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[remanufacturer's products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the softwood
    lumber products that are remanufactured by a remanufacturer and that
    are not further remanufactured in Canada. ([produits d'une
    entreprise de seconde transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Saskatchewan quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Saskatchewan quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the quantity of
    softwood lumber products that may be exported from Saskatchewan to
    the United States during a month under subsection 6.3(2) of the
    Act. ([quantité pour la Saskatchewan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the products
    referred to in item 5104 of Group 5 of the schedule to the [Export
    Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202). ([produits de bois
    d'oeuvre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785525 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order establishes the
method for allocating the quantity of softwood lumber products that may
be exported during a month from each of Ontario, Quebec, Manitoba and
Saskatchewan in 2013 for the purpose of paragraph 6.3(3)(a) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Relinquishing export allocation

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Order, a primary producer or remanufacturer relinquishes its export
allocation for 2013 by so informing the Minister in writing by December
10, 2012.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of export allocation

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** When a primary producer or
remanufacturer transfers, with the consent of the Minister under
subsection 6.3(4) of the Act, a portion of the export allocation issued
to it by the Minister for a month, that portion is deemed to be included
in the volume of the primary producer's or remanufacturer's products
that were exported in that month to the United States under export
permits and not in the volume of the transferee, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the portion transferred includes a corresponding
  volume of products; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total of the portion that is transferred during
  the month and that does not include products does not exceed 15% of
  the volume of the primary producer's or remanufacturer's export
  allocation for that month.

## [Ontario]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785534 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of allocation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The allocation of the
Ontario quantity to a primary producer or remanufacturer who applies for
an export allocation is determined by the formula

::: {#785537 .Section}
OQ x \[(EO/TEO) × 97%\]

where

[OQ]{.dfn}
:   is the Ontario quantity;

[EO]{.dfn}
:   is the volume of the primary producer's or remanufacturer's products
    that were exported from Ontario to the United States under export
    permits during the reference period; and

[TEO]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of primary producers' and remanufacturers'
    products that were exported from Ontario to the United States under
    export permits during the reference period, excluding the volume of
    products of primary producers and remanufacturers who relinquish
    their export allocations for 2013.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation of remainder

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the allocation of the remainder of the Ontario
  quantity is based on the order of receipt of applications for an
  export permit for a share of the remainder, with each applicant
  receiving the volume applied for until the remainder of the Ontario
  quantity is fully allocated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exhaustion of monthly allocation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An applicant who has received an allocation of the
  Ontario quantity for a month must exhaust that allocation before
  applying for an export permit for a share of the remainder, which is
  to be dealt with in accordance with subsection (1).

## [Quebec]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785547 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remanufacturers

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If some
  or all of a remanufacturer's products were exported from Quebec to the
  United States under export permits during the reference period and the
  remanufacturer applies for an export allocation, the remanufacturer
  may only receive the allocation of the Quebec quantity determined by
  the formula in section 8 (which is based on its historic export
  volume).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Primary producers without export
  history

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If none of a primary producer's products were
  exported from Quebec to the United States under export permits during
  the reference period and if the primary producer produced Quebec
  softwood lumber products during the period beginning on January 1,
  2009 and ending on December 31, 2011 and it applies for an export
  allocation, the primary producer may only receive the allocation of
  the Quebec quantity determined by the formula in subsection 10(1)
  (which is based on its historic production volume).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Primary producers with export history

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If some or all of a primary producer's products were
  exported from Quebec to the United States under export permits during
  the reference period and the primary producer applies for an export
  allocation, the primary producer must choose to receive either an
  allocation of the Quebec quantity determined by the formula in section
  12 (which is based on its historic export volume) or the formula in
  subsection 10(1) (which is based on its historic production volume).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation to remanufacturers

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the allocation of the Quebec quantity to a
  remanufacturer that is based on its historic export volume is
  determined by the formula

  ::: {#785558 .Subsection}
  QQ × (RE/TEQ)

  where

  [QQ]{.dfn}
  :   is the Quebec quantity;

  [RE]{.dfn}
  :   is the volume of the remanufacturer's products that were exported
      from Quebec to the United States under export permits during the
      reference period; and

  [TEQ]{.dfn}
  :   is the total volume of primary producers' and remanufacturers'
      products that were exported from Quebec to the United States under
      export permits during the reference period, excluding the volume
      of products of primary producers and remanufacturers who
      relinquish their export allocations for 2013.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum of total allocations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The sum of the allocations of the Quebec quantity to
  remanufacturers must be equal to or greater than 7.5% of the Quebec
  quantity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reserve pool

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The reserve pool is
determined by the formula

::: {#785567 .Section}
(QQ × TTPE/TEQ -- RMQ) × 4%

where

[QQ]{.dfn}
:   is the Quebec quantity;

[TTPE]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of primary producers' products that were
    exported from Quebec to the United States under export permits
    during the reference period, excluding the volume of products of
    primary producers who relinquish their export allocations for 2013;

[TEQ]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of primary producers' and remanufacturers'
    products that were exported from Quebec to the United States under
    export permits during the reference period, excluding the volume of
    products of primary producers and remanufacturers who relinquish
    their export allocations for 2013; and

[RMQ]{.dfn}
:   is the amount of additional allocations required to meet the minimum
    7.5% threshold referred to in subsection 8(2) if the sum of the
    allocations to all remanufacturers is less than 7.5% of the Quebec
    quantity.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation to primary producers ---
historic production volume

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  allocation of the Quebec quantity to a primary producer that is based
  on its historic production volume is determined by the formula

  ::: {#785576 .Subsection}
  RP × (PPPV/TPV)

  where

  [RP]{.dfn}
  :   is the reserve pool determined under section 9;

  [PPPV]{.dfn}
  :   is the volume of Quebec softwood lumber products that the primary
      producer produced during the period beginning on January 1, 2009
      and ending on December 31, 2011; and

  [TPV]{.dfn}
  :   is the total volume of Quebec softwood lumber products produced
      during that period by primary producers whose allocations are
      based on their historic production volume.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum allocation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The allocation referred to in subsection (1) must not
  exceed 40% of the primary producer's average monthly volume of Quebec
  softwood lumber products produced during the period beginning on
  January 1, 2009 and ending on December 31, 2011.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records of production volume

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The volume of Quebec softwood lumber products
  produced by a primary producer is based on records provided, with the
  consent of the primary producer, by the Government of Quebec to the
  Government of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Residual non-allocated quantity

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The residual
non-allocated quantity of the reserve pool is determined by the formula

::: {#785587 .Section}
RP -- AP

where

[RP]{.dfn}
:   is the reserve pool determined under section 9; and

[AP]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of softwood lumber products allocated to primary
    producers determined under section 10.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation to primary producers ---
historic export volume

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The allocation of the
Quebec quantity to a primary producer that is based on its historic
export volume is determined by the formula

::: {#785593 .Section}
(PPE/TPPEH) × {\[(QQ × TPPE/TEQ -- RMQ) × 96%\] + RNA}

where

[PPE]{.dfn}
:   is the volume of the primary producer's products that were exported
    from Quebec to the United States under export permits during the
    reference period;

[TPPEH]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of primary producers' products that were
    exported from Quebec to the United States under export permits
    during the reference period in respect of export allocations based
    on historic export volume, excluding the volume of products of
    primary producers who relinquish their export allocations for 2013;

[QQ]{.dfn}
:   is the Quebec quantity;

[TPPE]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of primary producers' products that were
    exported from Quebec to the United States under export permits
    during the reference period, excluding the volume of products of
    primary producers who relinquish their export allocations for 2013;

[TEQ]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of primary producers' and remanufacturers'
    products that were exported from Quebec to the United States under
    export permits during the reference period, excluding the volume of
    products of primary producers and remanufacturers who relinquish
    their export allocations for 2013;

[RMQ]{.dfn}
:   is the amount of additional allocations required to meet the minimum
    7.5% threshold referred to in subsection 8(2) if the sum of the
    allocations to all remanufacturers is less than 7.5% of the Quebec
    quantity; and

[RNA]{.dfn}
:   is the residual non-allocated quantity of the reserve pool
    determined under section 11.
:::

## [Manitoba]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785602 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of allocation

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The allocation of the
Manitoba quantity to a primary producer or remanufacturer who applies
for an export allocation is determined by the formula

::: {#785605 .Section}
MQ x \[(EM/TEM) × 30%\]

where

[MQ]{.dfn}
:   is the Manitoba quantity;

[EM]{.dfn}
:   is the volume of the primary producer's or remanufacturer's products
    that were exported from Manitoba to the United States under export
    permits during the reference period; and

[TEM]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of primary producers' and remanufacturers'
    products that were exported from Manitoba to the United States under
    export permits during the reference period, excluding the volume of
    products of primary producers and remanufacturers who relinquish
    their export allocations for 2013.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation of remainder

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  allocation of the remainder of the Manitoba quantity is based on the
  order of receipt of applications for an export permit for a share of
  the remainder, with each applicant receiving the volume applied for
  until the remainder of the Manitoba quantity is fully allocated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exhaustion of monthly allocation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An applicant who has received an allocation of the
  Manitoba quantity for a month must exhaust that allocation before
  applying for an export permit for a share of the remainder, which is
  to be dealt with in accordance with subsection (1).

Saskatchewan quantity to a primary producer or remanufacturer who
