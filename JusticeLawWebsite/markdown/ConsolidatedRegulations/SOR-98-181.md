## Ontario Hydro Nuclear Facilities Exclusion from Part III of the Canada Labour Code Regulations (Labour Standards) {#ontario-hydro-nuclear-facilities-exclusion-from-part-iii-of-the-canada-labour-code-regulations-labour-standards .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-181
:::

[CANADA LABOUR CODE](/eng/acts/L-2)

Registration 1998-03-19

Ontario Hydro Nuclear Facilities Exclusion from Part III of the Canada
Labour Code Regulations (Labour Standards)

P.C. 1998-409 1998-03-19
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Labour, pursuant to section 266[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the [Canada
Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2), hereby makes the annexed [Ontario Hydro
Nuclear Facilities Exclusion from Part III of the Canada Labour Code
Regulations (Labour Standards)](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-181).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018749 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Employment Standards Act,
    2000, S.O. 2000, c. 41, except subsection 3(2) and Part XIII.
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means employment with Ontario
    Hydro or a person referred to in paragraph (b) of the definition
    [[nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}. ([emploi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a nuclear facility in
    Ontario that is subject to the [Nuclear Safety and Control
    Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3) or any regulations made under that Act and
    that is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} owned and operated by Ontario Hydro; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} owned or operated by a person other than Ontario
      Hydro if, on the day on which these Regulations come into force or
      on a day after that day, it is owned and operated by Ontario
      Hydro. ([installation nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-113, s. 9
:::

## [Exclusion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018758 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Employment on or in
connection with a nuclear facility is hereby excluded from the
application of Part III of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2),
except sections 265 to 267.

## [Application of Ontario Act and Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018760 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Act and any regulations
or rules made under it, as amended from time to time, apply, to the
extent that they are relevant, in relation to employment on or in
connection with a nuclear facility.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-113, s. 10
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/2002-113,
s. 10\]]{.Repealed}

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018765 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on April 1, 1998.
