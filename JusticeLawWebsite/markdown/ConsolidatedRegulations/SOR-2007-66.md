## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741081 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Remote Sensing Space
      Systems Act](/eng/acts/R-5.4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[command protection plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[command protection plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a plan
      designed to protect the commands given to a remote sensing
      satellite of a remote sensing space system and the sales orders
      that give rise to those commands. ([plan de protection des
      commandes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[contact information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[contact information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person's
      street and mail address, telephone and fax number and electronic
      mail address. ([coordonnées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[data protection plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[data protection plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a plan designed
      to protect raw data and the remote sensing products produced from
      that data. ([plan de protection des données]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation, a partnership,
      a government, a government agency and an unincorporated
      organization. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[identifying information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[identifying information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an individual, their date and
        place of birth and citizenship;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a corporation, its jurisdiction
        of incorporation or continuation; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a partnership or unincorporated
        entity, its jurisdiction of registration. ([renseignements
        identificatoires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[sales order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[sales order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an order for raw data or
      a remote sensing product, including an internal order within a
      licensee or system participant for raw data or a remote sensing
      product. ([commande client]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An entity is controlled by a person if the person has
  control in fact, whether directly through the ownership of securities
  or indirectly through a trust, an agreement, an arrangement, the
  ownership of any body corporate or other means.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affiliation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} One entity is affiliated with another entity if one
  of them is controlled by the other or both are controlled by the same
  person.

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741099 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of licence

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for the issuance of a licence must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information and documents set out in Schedule
    1; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a declaration, signed and dated by the applicant or
    their authorized representative, that the information contained in
    the application is true, complete and correct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}System participant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the application includes an application to have a
  person designated as a system participant, the application must
  include a declaration signed and dated by the proposed system
  participant or its authorized representative attesting that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proposed system participant is authorized to
    enter into the agreement referred to in paragraph 32(c) of Schedule
    1 by the laws of the jurisdiction in which it proposes to operate
    and, in the case of a corporation, the laws of the jurisdiction in
    which it is incorporated or continued; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information contained in the application
    relating to the proposed system participant is true, complete and
    correct.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of licence

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for the amendment of a licence must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name and contact information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amendment requested; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the requested effective date of the amendment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}System participant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the application includes a request to have a
  person designated as a system participant, the application must
  include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the declaration described in subsection 2(2); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information and agreement or proposed agreement
    referred to in section 32 of Schedule 1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Renewal of licence

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application for the
renewal of a licence must contain the applicant's name and contact
information and the requested term of the renewal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General requirements

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for the issuance, amendment or renewal of a licence must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be in writing; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by a copy of the application on
    electronic media.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changes

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If any of the information or documents contained in
  an application for the issuance, amendment or renewal of a licence
  changes before the application is approved or refused, the applicant
  shall, without delay, inform the Minister of the change.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of agreement

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** If an application contains
a copy of a proposed agreement referred to in paragraph 32(c) of
Schedule 1, the licensee must provide the Minister with the final copy
of the agreement as soon as it comes into effect.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's notification

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If an application to issue
a licence has not been approved or refused within 180 days after the
applicant has provided the required information and documents, the
Minister shall, as soon as feasible, notify the applicant of any issues
to be resolved and any action required to resolve them. In the case of
an application to amend or renew a licence, the period is 90 days after
the applicant has provided the required information and documents.

## [Prescribed Factors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741132 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}All applications

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 8(1) of the Act, the prescribed factors are the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ability of the applicant to comply with the Act
  and these Regulations; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the enhancement of the competitiveness, at the
  national and international levels, of the Canadian remote sensing
  space industry.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Renewal of licence

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** In addition to the factors
prescribed in section 8, the following factors are prescribed in respect
of an application for the renewal of a licence:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any unpaid fee, fine, penalty or other amount due
  under the Act that is owing by the licensee;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the danger to the environment, public health or the
  safety of persons and property arising from not disposing of the
  licensed system in accordance with the timing of the system disposal
  plan; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the data continuity for recipients of raw data or
  remote sensing products.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of licence conditions

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 10(1) of the Act, the prescribed factors are the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the failure of the licensee to comply with the Act,
  these Regulations or the conditions of the licence; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the failure of a system participant to comply with
  the provisions of the agreement referred to in paragraph 32(c) of
  Schedule 1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of licence

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 12 of the Act, the prescribed factors are the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the failure or inability of the licensee to comply
  with the Act, these Regulations or the conditions of the licence; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the failure of the licensee to pay any fee, fine,
  penalty or other amount due under the Act.

## [Licence Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741150 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed conditions

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The following conditions
are prescribed as conditions of a licence:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensee must have a contact person for the
  purpose of communication with the Government of Canada who is an
  individual who meets the requirements of the appropriate level of
  security clearance --- under the Government Security Policy, as
  amended from time to time, published by the Treasury Board Secretariat
  --- for the nature of the commands given to and the raw data acquired
  by the remote sensing satellites of the licensed system;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licensee must, without delay, notify the Minister
  in writing of any change in control of the licensee or an affiliate of
  the licensee that is involved in the operation of the licensed system
  and provide the name, identifying information and contact information
  of each person who exercises control;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the licensee must, without delay, notify the Minister
  in writing of any change in the name, identifying information or
  contact information of the licensee or any system participant;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the licensee must on a regular basis evaluate the
  system disposal plan for the licensed system and, if amendments are
  needed, without delay, apply to the Minister for an amendment to the
  plan in accordance with paragraph 9(3)(a) of the Act;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the licensee must evaluate its command protection
  plan and data protection plan on a regular basis and ensure that its
  system participants evaluate their data protection plans and, if any,
  their command protection plans and notify the Minister in writing,
  without delay, of any proposed changes to any of those plans; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the licensee must, without delay, notify the Minister
  in writing of its decision to discontinue operation of the licensed
  system, or of the financial insolvency, dissolution or termination of
  operations of the licensee or one of its system participants.

## [Transformation of Raw Data]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741159 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Process not considered to transform raw
data

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  process that retains the phase information of raw data, or that
  produces an output from which measurements can be taken to determine
  the phase response of a remotely sensed surface, including the process
  to produce the synthetic aperture radar output known as Single Look
  Complex, is not considered to transform the raw data.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Process considered to transform raw
  data

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any process or series of processes operating on raw
  data that rectifies errors, distortions and other artifacts of the
  system by pixel aggregation, averaging or resampling are considered to
  transform the raw data if the process or series of processes also

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} radiometrically calibrates the data; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} geocodes the data with respect to features of the
    Earth by resampling.

## [Priority Access Orders]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741167 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount paid by a minister

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** An amount paid by a
minister under subsection 22(2) of the Act for a service must not exceed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an amount that is in accordance with any agreement in
  effect between the minister and the licensee at the time the service
  was provided; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if there is no agreement, an amount that is
  proportionate to an amount received by the licensee for a comparable
  service provided on a priority basis to any person during the 12
  months prior to the providing of the service.

## [Notification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741172 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to notify Minister

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee must, as soon as feasible, notify the Minister in writing if
  the licensee has reasonable grounds to believe that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensed system poses a danger to the
    environment, public health or the safety of persons or property;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licensee has lost or is in danger of losing
    control of a remote sensing satellite;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the cryptography used in communications with the
    remote sensing satellite or the information assurance measures for
    the licensed system are malfunctioning;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an unauthorized communication of raw data has
    occurred;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the provision of a remote sensing product has been
    provided in breach of a condition imposed under subsection 8(7) of
    the Act; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} there has been a breach in the security of the
    licensed system.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within 21 days after notifying the Minister, the
  licensee must provide a written report to the Minister that describes
  the situation, its probable cause and the corrective measures taken or
  proposed to be taken.

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741184 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintenance of records

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee must maintain the following records for a period of one year:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a record of every sales order placed with it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a record of every command given to each remote
    sensing satellite of the remote sensing space system, including the
    date and time of the command;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a record of all raw data received from each remote
    sensing satellite, including the date and time of receipt;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a record of raw data being entered into the
    archives of the licensee and the disposal of raw data, including the
    date of each entry and disposal;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a catalogue that lists the raw data that is
    accessible to the public, including the date of each entry into the
    catalogue;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a record of every use of raw data by the licensee
    or of a system participant to make Single Look Complex data or a
    remote sensing product, including the date and time it was used;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a record of every communication of raw data or
    provision of a remote sensing product to any person, including the
    date and time it was communicated or provided; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a record of any amount paid by a minister under
    subsection 22(2) of the Act for the provision of a service on a
    priority basis, including the date the service was provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sales orders

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The licensee must keep the records in a manner that
  enables the ready determination of the following in respect of every
  sales order:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the sales order;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the person who
    placed the sales order;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the type of raw data or remote sensing product
    ordered;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the location sensed in order to fill the sales
    order; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the recipient
    of the raw data or remote sensing product and the conditions under
    which the recipient may make use of the data or product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination and communi­cation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The licensee must keep the records in a form that
  allows them to be readily examined and communicated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notifying Minister

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee who becomes aware of an inaccuracy or
  incompleteness in a record that has been examined by an inspector or
  communicated to the Minister must, without delay, notify the Minister
  in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}System participant

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee must ensure that every system
  participant maintains -- for one year and in accordance with
  subsections (2) and (3) -- the records identified in paragraphs (1)(a)
  to (g) that are in respect of the participant's activities in the
  operation of the licensed system.

## [Archiving and Access to Raw Data]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741209 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Archiving raw data

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee must archive raw data from the remote sensing satellite in a
  readily retrievable format for a minimum period of 15 months from

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which an entry for the raw data was
    first made in a catalogue accessible to the public; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the raw data was not entered in a catalogue
    accessible to the public, the day on which the raw data was received
    by a ground station.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of proposed disposal to Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensee must, before disposing of the raw data,
  notify the Minister and provide the following information about each
  scene of raw data:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a unique identifier;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and time when the raw data was acquired by
    the remote sensing satellite;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the geographic boundaries of the scene;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the position of the satellite when the raw data was
    acquired;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the sensor modes used to acquire the raw data;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the ground station that received the raw data;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the date and time when the ground station received
    the raw data;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the date on or after which the raw data will be
    disposed of;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the cost to communicate the raw data; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of a contact
    person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The licensee may not send the notice until 12 months
  of the 15-month period referred to in subsection (1) have expired.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal of raw data

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The licensee may not dispose of the raw data until
  three months after the day on which the notice was sent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of proposed disposal to others

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** On receipt of a
notification under subsection 17(2), the Minister may, and the licensee
must on the request of the Minister, inform any person to whom the
licensee is authorized to communicate raw data under subsection 8(6) of
the Act about the proposed disposal of the raw data.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for raw data

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister or a person to whom the licensee is authorized to communicate
  raw data under subsection 8(6) of the Act may, at any time before the
  raw data is disposed of, request the communication of the raw data
  from the contact person referred to in paragraph 17(2)(j), and the
  licensee must provide the raw data as soon as feasible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister or the person that receives the raw data
  must pay the licensee an amount equal to the reasonable costs of
  communicating the data.

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741238 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Design review

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee must provide to the Minister reports of the preliminary
  design review and critical design review for the following elements of
  the licensed system:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the remote sensing space system as a whole;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} each type of remote sensing sensor of each type of
    remote sensing satellite;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} each type of satellite platform of each type of
    remote sensing satellite;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} each class of telemetry, tracking and control
    station of the system, including its command generation and
    information assurance subsystems; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} each class of ground station of the system,
    including its reception, storage, processing, delivery and
    information assurance subsystems.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each report must contain a copy in written or
  electronic format of all information, documents and records prepared
  by or for the licensee for the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline to provide reports

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The licensee must provide each report within 45 days
  after the completion of each review.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Launch and flight sensors

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A licensee must, as soon
as feasible, provide a written report to the Minister

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} of the entry into effect of a contract for the launch
  of the first remote sensing satellite of the licensed system; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} of the licensee's taking delivery of the flight
  sensors of the first remote sensing satellite of the licensed system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proposed launch

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee must, at least 45 days prior to the planned launch of a
  remote sensing satellite, provide a written report to the Minister
  that contains

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the start date and time, expressed in Coordinated
    Universal Time, of the planned launch window and its duration
    expressed in days;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and location of the launch site expressed
    as a latitude and longitude;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the azimuth of the proposed launch trajectory
    expressed in degrees measured positive clockwise from True North in
    a local frame of reference centred on the launch site;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the type of the launch vehicle; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the geographic boundaries of the area that could be
    subject to falling debris from a normal launch and from a launch
    failure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change in information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The licensee shall, without delay, inform the
  Minister of any change in any of the information provided under
  subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Satellite becoming operational

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee must, within 21 days after a remote sensing satellite becomes
  operational, provide a written report to the Minister that contains

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of the launch and the name and location of
    the launch site expressed as a latitude and longitude;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the basic orbital parameters of the satellite,
    including nodal period, inclination, apogee and perigee; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any difference between the satellite performance
    specifications set out in the conditions of the licence and the
    actual performance of the satellite.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Satellite failure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a remote sensing satellite fails to become
  operational or becomes inoperable, the licensee must, within 21 days
  after determining that fact, provide a written report to the Minister
  that contains the information described in subsection (1) as is
  appropriate in the circumstances.

## [Representations and Notice]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741273 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representations

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  representation made to a minister or an enforcement officer under the
  Act must be in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notices

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice given by a minister under the Act must be in
  writing.

## [Violations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741279 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated provisions and conditions

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of carrying out section 23 of the Act, the following are
  designated as the provisions and conditions whose contravention may be
  proceeded with as a violation:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the provisions of the Act set out in column 1 of
    Part 1 of Schedule 2;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the provisions of these Regulations set out in
    column 1 of Part 2 of Schedule 2;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the provisions of orders made under subsections
    13(1), 14(1) and (2) and 15(1) to (3) of the Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the conditions of a licence set out in column 1 of
    Part 3 of Schedule 2 imposed by the Act or these Regulations; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the conditions of a licence imposed under
    subsections 8(5) to (7) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The maximum administrative monetary penalty for a
  violation of a designated provision or condition set out in column 1
  of Parts 1 to 3 of Schedule 2 is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of individuals, the amount set out in
    column 2; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the amount set out in column 3.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The maximum administrative monetary penalty for a
  violation of a designated provision referred to in paragraph (1)(c) or
  a designated condition referred to in paragraph (1)(e) is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of individuals, \$5,000; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, \$25,000.

penalty, an enforcement officer must take into account any notification
given by the licensee under subsection 15(1) or 16(4).

    of raw data by the satellite and the communication of the data or
    the provision of remote sensing products to a recipient.
