## [Exclusion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698973 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The indictable offences
under the following Acts are excluded from the definition [[designated
offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 462.3(1) of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2010, c. 12, s. 36\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2018-108, s. 391\]]{.Repealed}

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2010-74, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(d) and (e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2010, c. 12, s. 36\]]{.Repealed}

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} [Feeds Act](/eng/acts/F-9);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} [Fertilizers Act](/eng/acts/F-10);

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} [Foreign Publishers Advertising Services
  Act](/eng/acts/F-29.6);

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} [Health of Animals Act](/eng/acts/H-3.3);

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2010, c. 12, s. 36\]]{.Repealed}

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2018-108, s. 391\]]{.Repealed}

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} [Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3),
  except for the indictable offence under section 50;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} [Plant Protection Act](/eng/acts/P-14.8);

- [(m.1)]{.lawlabel} [Safe Food for Canadians Act](/eng/acts/S-1.1); and

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} [Seeds Act](/eng/acts/S-8).
