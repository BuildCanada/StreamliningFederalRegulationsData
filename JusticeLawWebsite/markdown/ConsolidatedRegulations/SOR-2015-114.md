## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-816490 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Marketing Authorization.

[[gluten]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gluten]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any gluten protein from the grain of any of the
      following cereals or from the grain of a hybridized strain that is
      created from at least one of the following cereals:

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} barley,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} oats,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} rye,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} triticale,

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} wheat; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any modified gluten protein, including any gluten
      protein fraction, that is derived from the grain of any of the
      cereals referred to in paragraph (a) or from the grain of a
      hybridized strain referred to in that paragraph.
      ([gluten]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[p.p.m.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[p.p.m.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means parts per million by weight.
    ([p.p.m.]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-816502 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation or advertisement ---
gluten-free oats

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any representation about
gluten on the label of a food that is oats or that contains oats, and an
advertisement for the food is exempt from the application of section
B.24.018 of the [Food and Drug
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the oats --- as a food or as an ingredient or
  component of a food --- are

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} specially produced and processed in a manner
    designed to avoid their contamination by gluten from barley, rye,
    triticale or wheat or from a hybridized strain created from at least
    one of those cereals, and do not contain more than 20 p.p.m. of
    gluten from those cereals and hybridized strains created from at
    least one of those cereals, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} specially processed in a manner designed to limit
    or reduce the amount of gluten from barley, rye, triticale or wheat
    or from a hybridized strain created from at least one of those
    cereals, and do not contain more than 20 p.p.m. of gluten from those
    cereals and hybridized strains created from at least one of those
    cereals;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the food contains no oats other than oats referred to
  in paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the food does not contain more than 20 p.p.m. of
  gluten from barley, rye, triticale or wheat or from a hybridized
  strain created from at least one of those cereals;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the food does not contain any added gluten from
  barley, rye, triticale or wheat or from a hybridized strain created
  from at least one of those cereals; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} all representations with respect to oats on the label
  of the food and in an advertisement for the food, clearly refer to the
  oats as gluten-free.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Source of gluten

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A food that is oats or that
contains oats and that satisfies the conditions set out in section 2 is
exempt from the application of section B.01.010.1 and subparagraph
B.01.010.3(1)(b)(ii) of the [Food and Drug
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) in respect of the
requirement to show the source of gluten from oats.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Name for oats

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A food that is oats or that
contains oats and that satisfies the conditions set out in section 2 is
exempt from the application of subsection B.01.010(2) of the [Food and
Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) in respect of the
name by which oats are shown as an ingredient or component in the list
of ingredients for the food.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-816516 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Marketing
Authorization comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
