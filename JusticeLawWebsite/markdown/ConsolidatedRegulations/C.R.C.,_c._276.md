## Saskatchewan Hog Information (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#saskatchewan-hog-information-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 276
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Regulations Respecting the Information Required from Persons Engaged in
the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade of Hogs Produced in
Saskatchewan
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537755 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Saskatchewan Hog Information (Interprovincial and Export)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._276).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537757 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Natural Products Marketing
    Act, 1972 of Saskatchewan; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[assembly yard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assembly yard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means premises to which
    producers may deliver their hogs; ([parc de
    groupage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[assembly yard operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assembly yard operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the owner or
    the operator of an assembly yard; ([exploitant d'un parc de
    groupage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan Hog
    Marketing Commission established pursuant to the Act and the Plan;
    ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any member of the species [Sus
    Scrofa L.]{.otherLang lang="la"} (domestic pig) produced in
    Saskatchewan; ([porc]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan Hog Marketing
    Plan established and amended from time to time pursuant to the Act;
    ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person engaged in the
    business of processing hogs or using hogs in manufacturing;
    ([transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person engaged in producing or feeding hogs
      and making them available for slaughter and includes the employer
      of any such person,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person who, under any lease or agreement, is
      entitled to a share of the hogs produced or fed by a person
      referred to in paragraph (a), and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person who takes possession of any hogs from
      any person under any form of security for a debt;
      ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[small processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[small processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a processor who, in
    the previous calendar year, processed less than two per cent of all
    hogs slaughtered in Saskatchewan according to the reports published
    by the Department of Agriculture of Canada. ([petit
    transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537771 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of hogs in interprovincial and export trade and to
persons and property situated in the Province of Saskatchewan.

## [Books and Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537773 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every person engaged in the
marketing of hogs shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep complete and accurate books and records of all
  matters relating to such marketing and retain those books and records
  for at least three years;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} upon request of any member or authorized
  representative of the Commission, produce the books and records
  referred to in paragraph (a).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-486, s. 1
:::

## [Inspection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537779 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  member or authorized representative of the Commission may, at any
  reasonable time, inspect any place or premises used for the marketing
  of hogs.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person in possession or control of any place or
  premises referred to in subsection (1) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} permit any member or authorized representative of
    the Commission to inspect such place or premises; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} furnish any member or authorized representative of
    the Commission with such information in respect of the marketing of
    hogs as he may reasonably require.

## [Weekly Statements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537785 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  small processor shall submit to the Commission a weekly statement
  indicating

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and farm registration number of
    every producer from whom it purchased hogs;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and farm registration number,
    where applicable, of every producer or consumer on behalf of whom it
    custom processed a hog;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the quantity, variety, grade, class or size of hogs
    so purchased or processed; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the price paid for such hogs.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every operator of an assembly yard who holds auctions
  of sows shall submit to the Commission a weekly statement indicating
  the number of sows sold at each auction.
