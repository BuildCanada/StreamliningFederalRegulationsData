## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-727144 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Library and Archives of
    Canada Act](/eng/acts/L-7.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[publisher]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[publisher]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who makes a
    publication available in Canada that the person is authorized to
    reproduce or over which the person controls the content. It does not
    include a person who only distributes a publication.
    ([éditeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Deposit --- Non-paper Publications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-727148 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In order to make a
publication and its contents that uses a medium other than paper
accessible to the Librarian and Archivist, the publisher shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} before providing a copy of the publication to the
  Librarian and Archivist,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} decrypt encrypted data contained in the
    publication, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} remove or disable security systems or devices that
    are designed to restrict or limit access to the publication; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} when providing a copy of the publication to the
  Librarian and Archivist,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} provide a copy of software specifically created by
    the publisher that is necessary to access the publication,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} provide a copy of technical or other information
    necessary to access the publication, including a copy of manuals
    that accompany the publication, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} provide any available descriptive data about the
    publication including its title, creator, language, date of
    publication, format, subject and copyright information.

## [Deposit --- One Copy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-727157 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following classes of
publications are those in respect of which, except where a publication
is also in a class described in section 4, only one copy is required to
be provided under section 10 of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} musical sound recordings;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} multi-media publications consisting of two or more
  physical parts in different formats;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} publications of which fewer than 100 copies have been
  produced;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} publications produced on demand from a master copy of
  which fewer than 100 copies have been produced; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} online publications.

## [Deposit on Request]{.HTitleText1} {#h-727164 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The following classes of
publications are those in respect of which the obligation to provide two
copies under subsection 10(1) of the Act applies only on a written
request from the Librarian and Archivist:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} publications, other than online publications, of
  which fewer than four copies have been produced;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} printed publications that are manufactured or
  distributed in Canada and that are not published under the imprint of
  a Canadian publisher;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} sound recordings that are manufactured or distributed
  in Canada and that have no Canadian content or major Canadian
  contributor such as a composer, artist, narrator, conductor,
  orchestra, performer, writer, lyricist or producer;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} reissues or portions of publications that are not
  substantially different from copies already deposited;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} newspapers printed on paper;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} programs of activities and events;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} publications, including *livres d'artiste* or book
  objects, produced by the author, the artist or the publisher using a
  creative process that significantly distinguishes each copy from the
  others;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} trade catalogues, advertisements, promotional
  material, prospectuses and price lists;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} timetables of transport services;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} blank books or blank forms without accompanying text;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} calendars and agendas without accompanying text;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} galley copies, works-in-progress, preprints and
  drafts;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} student reports, theses and other works produced to
  meet course requirements;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} drawing and colouring books without text and books of
  cut-outs for children;

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} press releases and circulars;

- [(p)]{.lawlabel} newsletters of local interest, in particular those
  that are published by associations, parishes, employee groups, union
  locals or schools;

- [(q)]{.lawlabel} minutes of meetings and by-laws;

- [(r)]{.lawlabel} posters and banners;

- [(s)]{.lawlabel} patterns, models, plans and blueprints;

- [(t)]{.lawlabel} bookmarks;

- [(u)]{.lawlabel} postcards;

- [(v)]{.lawlabel} elementary and secondary school yearbooks;

- [(w)]{.lawlabel} games;

- [(x)]{.lawlabel} deliberations of electronic discussion groups,
  listservs, bulletin boards and e-mails;

- [(y)]{.lawlabel} websites, including portals, personal websites,
  service sites, intranets and websites consisting primarily of links to
  other sites; and

- [(z)]{.lawlabel} dynamic databases and raw data.
