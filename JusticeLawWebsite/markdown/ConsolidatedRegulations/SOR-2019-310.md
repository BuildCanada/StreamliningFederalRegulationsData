## Critical Habitat of the Rainbow Smelt (Osmerus mordax) Lake Utopia Small-Bodied Population Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-rainbow-smelt-osmerus-mordax-lake-utopia-small-bodied-population-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2019-310
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2019-08-20

Critical Habitat of the Rainbow Smelt (Osmerus mordax) Lake Utopia
Small-Bodied Population Order
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Rainbow Smelt ([Osmerus mordax]{.otherLang lang="la"}) Lake
Utopia small-bodied population is a wildlife species that is listed as a
threatened species in Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBAGA};

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

And whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is
specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection
58(2)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBAEA} of that Act;

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to subsections
58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDADA}, makes the annexed Critical
Habitat of the Rainbow Smelt (Osmerus mordax) Lake Utopia Small-Bodied
Population Order.

Ottawa, August 12, 2019

::: FlushLeft
Jonathan Wilkinson
:::

::: FlushLeft
Minister of Fisheries and Oceans

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1197128 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Rainbow Smelt ([Osmerus mordax]{.otherLang lang="la"}) Lake
Utopia small-bodied population which is identified in the recovery
strategy for that species that is included in the Species at Risk Public
Registry.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1197130 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
