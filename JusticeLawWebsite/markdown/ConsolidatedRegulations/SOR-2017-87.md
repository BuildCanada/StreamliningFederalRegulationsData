## Order Declaring an Amnesty Period (2017) {#order-declaring-an-amnesty-period-2017 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2017-87
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 2017-05-12

Order Declaring an Amnesty Period (2017)

P.C. 2017-506 2017-05-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 117.14(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EGACA} of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EDACA}, makes the annexed [Order
Declaring an Amnesty Period (2017)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2017-87).

  1995, c. 39, s. 139

Definition [[non-restricted firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[non-restricted firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a firearm other
than a prohibited firearm or a restricted firearm.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amnesty

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amnesty period set out in subsection (3) is declared under section
  117.14 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) for an individual who,
  at any time during the amnesty period, is in possession of a
  non-restricted firearm and who holds or held a licence to possess
  firearms or a licence to possess and acquire firearms

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that expired during the period beginning on January
    1, 2004 and ending on May 16, 2017; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that expires during the period beginning on May 17,
    2017 and ending on December 31, 2017.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of the amnesty period is to permit the
  individual to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtain the licence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} deactivate the firearm so that it is no longer a
    firearm;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} export the firearm in accordance with all
    applicable legal requirements, including the legal requirements of
    the country to which the firearm is exported;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} turn in the firearm to a police officer or a
    firearms officer for destruction or other disposal;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} sell or give the firearm to a [[public service
    agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 1 of the
    [Public Agents Firearms Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-203),
    to a business, including a museum, that holds a licence authorizing
    the acquisition of firearms or to an individual who holds a licence
    to possess and acquire firearms; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} possess the firearm before doing one of the things
    described in paragraphs (a) to (e).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amnesty period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amnesty period begins on May 17, 2017 and ends on
  December 31, 2017.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
