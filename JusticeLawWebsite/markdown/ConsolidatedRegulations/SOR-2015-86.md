First Nations Elections Regulations

P.C. 2015-436 2015-04-02

the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
section 41 of the [First Nations Elections
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.65)[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826208 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [First Nations Elections
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11.65). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[quorum]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[quorum]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to the council of a
      First Nation, means a majority of the members of the council, or
      five members if the council consists of nine or more
      members. ([quorum]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Register number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Register number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the number assigned
      to a person registered under section 5 of the [Indian
      Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([numéro de registre]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context otherwise requires, other words
  and expressions used in these Regulations have the same meaning as in
  the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

## [Electoral Officer and Deputy Electoral Officers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826217 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of electoral officer

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  council of the First Nation must, by resolution, appoint an electoral
  officer or, if it is not possible for the council to form a quorum,
  the Minister must appoint an electoral officer, who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has not been found guilty of an offence under the
    Act within the last two years before the appointment; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is certified in accordance with subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person is certified if they successfully complete a
  training program that is approved by the Minister on the
  responsibilities of the electoral officer under the Act and these
  Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of certification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The certification is revoked if the electoral officer
  is found guilty of an offence under the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of deputy electoral officer

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer may appoint one or more deputy
  electoral officers.

## [Voters List]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826229 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At least
  65 days before the day on which an election is to be held

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the First Nation must provide the electoral officer
    with the information set out in subsection (2), if the First Nation
    holding the election has assumed control of its own membership under
    section 10 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Registrar must provide the electoral officer
    with the information set out in subsection (2), if the Band List of
    the First Nation holding the election is maintained in the
    Department under section 11 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compilation of list

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer must compile a voters list that
  contains the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the names of all electors, in alphabetical order;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} each elector's band membership or Register number
    or, if the elector does not have a band membership or Register
    number, their date of birth.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revision of list

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer must revise the voters list if
  it is demonstrated that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an elector's name has been omitted from the list;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an elector's name is incorrectly set out in the
    list; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of a person not entitled to vote is
    included in the list.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Demonstration of omission and inclusion

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (3),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person may demonstrate that an elector's name has
    been omitted from, or incorrectly set out in, the voters list by
    presenting to the electoral officer written evidence from the
    Registrar or from the First Nation that the elector is in the Band
    List and will be at least 18 years of age on the day of the
    election; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person may demonstrate that the name of a person
    not entitled to vote has been included in the voters list by
    presenting to the electoral officer written evidence that that
    person is not in the Band List or will not be a least 18 years of
    age on the day of the election.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Addresses

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At least
  65 days before the day on which an election is to be held, the First
  Nation must provide the electoral officer with a list setting out the
  last known postal address and email address of each elector who does
  not reside on the reserve.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of electors' names and
  addresses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the request of a candidate for election as chief
  or councillor, the electoral officer must provide the candidate with a
  list of the names of electors and the address of any elector who has
  consented to have their address released to the candidates.

## [Nomination Meeting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826253 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of nomination meeting

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At least
  25 days before the day on which a nomination meeting is to be held,
  the electoral officer must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} post a notice of the nomination meeting and a list
    of the names of electors in one conspicuous place on the reserve;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} send by mail and email a notice of the nomination
    meeting, a voter declaration form and a form on which the elector
    may request a mail-in ballot to the addresses provided under
    subsection 4(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of a nomination meeting must contain the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date, time, duration and location of the
    nomination meeting;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of positions to be filled;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the manner in which an elector can
    nominate a candidate or second the nomination of a candidate;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement that an elector must not nominate more
    than one candidate for each position to be filled in accordance with
    subsection 9(4) of the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the election is to be held and
    the location and hours of operation of each polling station;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the date on which any advance poll will be held and
    the location and hours of operation of each advance polling station;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the electoral officer's name, phone number, fax
    number, postal address and email address;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the elector may permit the
    electoral officer to release their address to the candidates;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a statement that, if the elector wants to receive a
    mail-in ballot, they must make a written request to the electoral
    officer;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} if the council of the First Nation has, by
    resolution, imposed a candidacy fee under section 8, a statement
    that sets out the fee to be paid by each candidate; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a statement that a nominee who wishes to become a
    candidate must remit to the electoral officer or deputy electoral
    officer, by 6 p.m. on the third day following the day on which the
    nomination meeting closes,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a signed declaration accepting the nomination and
      attesting to their eligibility to be a candidate under the Act,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any applicable candidacy fee, in the form of
      cash, certified cheque, money order or electronic transfer payable
      to the electoral officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record of names

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer must record the names of
  electors to whom a notice of the nomination meeting was sent or
  delivered, the postal address and email address of those electors and
  the date on which the notice was sent or delivered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voter declaration form

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A voter declaration form must be signed by the
  elector and attest to the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the elector's name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the elector's band and the elector's
    Register or band membership number; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the elector's date of birth.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witness

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A voter declaration form must contain the name,
  address, telephone number and signature of a witness who is at least
  18 years of age and who attests to the fact that the person completing
  and signing the voter declaration form is the person whose name is set
  out in the form.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witness

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The voter declaration form of the elector who
  enlisted the assistance of another person under subsection 17(2) must
  be signed by a witness that attests to the fact that the elector is
  the person whose name is set out in the form and that the ballot was
  marked in the manner directed by the elector.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nomination of candidates

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  elector may nominate a candidate and second the nomination of a
  candidate,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by delivering or sending by mail, email or fax to
    the electoral officer a nomination and a voter declaration form; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by orally nominating the candidate or seconding the
    nomination of the candidate at the nomination meeting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witness

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The witness to the elector's signature appearing on
  the voter declaration form is not to be considered a seconder to the
  nomination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mailed nominations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Mailed nominations that are not received by the
  electoral officer before the beginning of the nomination meeting are
  void.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nomination meeting

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  nomination meeting for an election must be held at least 35 days
  before the date of the election.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reading nominations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the beginning of the nomination meeting, the
  electoral officer must read aloud the nominations that have been
  received.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Two nominations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the same person receives two written nominations
  for the same position, the second nomination is considered to second
  the first nomination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A nomination meeting must remain open for at least
  three hours.

## [Candidates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826303 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Candidacy fee

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The council of a First
Nation may by resolution impose a candidacy fee of up to \$250 on every
candidate for election as chief or councillor.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration and fee

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To
  become a candidate, a nominee must remit to the electoral officer or
  deputy electoral officer, by 6 p.m. on the third day following the day
  on which the nomination meeting closes,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a signed declaration accepting the nomination for
    no more than one of the positions for which they have been nominated
    and attesting to their eligibility to be a candidate under the Act;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the council of a First Nation has passed a
    resolution referred to in section 8, the candidacy fee, in the form
    of cash, certified cheque, money order or electronic transfer
    payable to the electoral officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}In trust

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer must hold the candidacy fees in
  trust.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of candidacy

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  candidate may withdraw their candidacy at any time prior to the close
  of the polls by submitting to the electoral officer a written
  declaration of withdrawal, signed by the candidate in the presence of
  the electoral officer, a justice of the peace, a notary public or a
  commissioner for oaths.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Death of candidate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A candidate who dies before the close of the polls is
  considered to have withdrawn their candidacy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Candidacy fee

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A candidate who withdraws forfeits the candidacy fee
  and is considered to have received no votes.

## [Elections]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826320 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Close of nomination meeting

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** As soon as feasible after
the deadline set out in subsection 9(1) expires, the electoral officer
must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if there is only one candidate for chief, declare
  that person to be elected by acclamation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the number of candidates for councillor does not
  exceed the number of positions to be filled, declare those persons to
  be elected by acclamation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if there are more candidates than the number of
  positions to be filled, announce that an election will be held on the
  date set out in the notice referred to in paragraph 5(2)(e); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if, after candidates have been declared elected in
  accordance with paragraph (a) or (b), the number of positions filled
  is less than the number of positions that are required to be filled
  for the council of the First Nation to have quorum, post and send a
  notice of another nomination meeting in the manner described in
  subsection 5(1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of acclamations

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If,
  after candidates have been elected by acclamation, the number of
  positions filled is greater than or equal to the number of positions
  that are required to be filled for the council of the First Nation to
  have quorum, the electoral officer must post in at least one
  conspicuous place on the reserve, and mail to every elector who does
  not reside on the reserve for whom an address was provided under
  subsection 4(1), a notice that sets out the names of the persons who
  have been elected by acclamation and states that an election will not
  be held.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refund of candidacy fee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Candidates who are elected to the position of chief
  or the position of councillor by acclamation are, for the purposes of
  section 11 of the Act, considered to have received more than five
  percent of the total votes cast and are entitled to be refunded the
  candidacy fee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of candidacy fee

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer must return the candidacy fee
  to candidates elected by acclamation as soon as feasible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of ballots

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As
  soon as feasible after the deadline set out in subsection 9(1)
  expires, the electoral officer must prepare ballots setting out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the names of the candidates for chief in
    alphabetical order by surname; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the names of the candidates for councillor in
    alphabetical order by surname.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional distinguishing information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If two or more candidates have the same name, the
  electoral officer must add to the ballots any additional information
  that is necessary to distinguish between those candidates.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of election

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** No later than 30 days
before the day on which the election is to be held, the electoral
officer must post in at least one conspicuous place on the reserve a
notice that sets out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the election is to be held and the
  location and hours of operation of each polling station;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which any advance poll is to be held and
  the location and hours of operation of each advance polling station;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which and the time and place at which the
  counting of the votes is to take place;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number of positions to be filled;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement that, if the elector wants to receive a
  mail-in ballot, they must make a written request to the electoral
  officer and provide the electoral officer with proof of identity; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the electoral officer's name, phone number, fax
  number, postal address and email address.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mail-in ballot

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** An elector who wants to
receive a mail-in ballot must make a written request to the electoral
officer that includes a copy of their proof of identity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mail-in ballot package

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  later than 30 days before the day on which the election is to be held,
  the electoral officer must mail to every elector who has made a
  written request a mail-in ballot package consisting of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a ballot, initialed on the back by the electoral
    officer or deputy electoral officer;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an outer return envelope that is pre-addressed to
    the electoral officer and, if the elector's address is in Canada, is
    postage-paid;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an inner envelope marked "Ballot" for insertion of
    the completed ballot;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a voter declaration form;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} instructions regarding voting by mail-in ballot;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the notice set out in section 14;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the elector may vote in person at
    a polling station on the day of the election, or at an advance
    polling station if applicable, in lieu of voting by mail-in ballot,
    if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} they return the unused mail-in ballot to the
      electoral officer or deputy electoral officer, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} they provide the electoral officer or deputy
      electoral officer with a sworn affidavit stating that they have
      lost their mail-in ballot; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a list of the names of any candidates who were
    elected by acclamation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Six or more days before election

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an elector makes a written request for a mail-in
  ballot six or more days before the day on which the election is to be
  held, the electoral officer must mail, or deliver at an agreed time
  and place, a mail-in ballot package to the elector as soon as feasible
  after receipt of the request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voters list

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer must indicate on the voters
  list, next to the name of each elector to whom a mail-in ballot
  package was mailed or delivered, that a package has been provided to
  that elector and keep a record of the date on which, and the address
  to which, each package was mailed or delivered.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mail-in ballot

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  elector may vote by mail-in ballot by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} marking the ballot with a cross, check mark or
    other mark that clearly indicates the elector's choice, but does not
    identify the elector, next to the name of the candidates for whom
    they intend to vote;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} folding the ballot in a manner that conceals the
    candidates' names and any marks on the ballot without hiding the
    initials on the back;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} placing the ballot in the inner envelope and
    sealing that envelope;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} completing and signing the voter declaration form;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} placing the inner envelope and the completed voter
    declaration form in the outer envelope; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} delivering or mailing the mail-in ballot package to
    the electoral officer or deputy electoral officer before the time at
    which the polls close.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance of another person

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an elector is unable to vote in the manner set out
  in subsection (1), the elector may enlist the assistance of another
  person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voided mail-in ballot

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A mail-in ballot is void if the mail-in ballot
  package is not received by the electoral officer or deputy electoral
  officer before the time at which the polls close.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safekeeping of mail-in ballot

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer or deputy electoral officer
  must ensure the safekeeping of the mail-in ballot packages until they
  are opened in accordance with section 22.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advance poll

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  electoral officer may establish an advance polling station at any
  location that the electoral officer considers suitable and hold an
  advance poll for the period beginning on the tenth day and ending on
  the fifth day before the day on which the election is to be held.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedures

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The procedures set out in sections 20 and 21 apply to
  advance polling stations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}On close of advance polling station

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} As soon as the advance polling station closes, the
  electoral officer must seal the ballot box in a manner that prevents
  it from being opened without breaking the seal, place their initials
  on the seal, invite two witnesses to initial the seal, and ensure the
  safekeeping of the ballot box until the counting of the votes
  following the close of the polling stations on the day of the
  election.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Polling stations

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The electoral officer
must establish at least one polling station on the reserve on the day of
the election unless the electoral officer is unable to do so, in which
case the electoral officer must establish a polling station as close to
the reserve as possible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Polling station materials

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  electoral officer must, before the poll is open, ensure that each
  polling station is equipped with ballot boxes, ballots, materials for
  marking the ballots and any other necessary materials for the conduct
  of the vote.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compartments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer or deputy electoral officer
  must provide a compartment at each polling station where the electors
  can mark their ballots without being observed by any other person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer or deputy electoral officer may
  designate a person to maintain order at a polling station.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hours

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Polling stations must be open from 9:00 a.m. to
  8:00 p.m. on the day of the election.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Candidate's representatives

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A candidate is entitled to two representatives in a
  polling station.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sealed ballot box

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer or deputy electoral officer
  must, before the polling station is opened, open the ballot box, call
  all persons present to witness that it is empty, seal the box in a
  manner that prevents it from being opened without breaking the seal
  and place it in a location that is visible to the voters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seal

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The seal of a ballot box must not be broken and the
  ballot box must not be opened during the time that the polling station
  is open.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repeat of procedure

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If another ballot box is required during the time
  that the polling station is open, the electoral officer or deputy
  electoral officer must repeat the procedure set out in subsection (6).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ballot

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the
  electoral officer or deputy electoral officer must provide a ballot on
  which their initials have been placed to any person who has not voted
  at an advance poll, who attends at a polling station and whose name is
  set out in the voters list.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marked voters list

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer or deputy electoral officer
  must place a mark on the voters list next to the name of each elector
  who was given a ballot.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mail-in ballot

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An elector who received a mail-in ballot package
  under section 16 may obtain a ballot and vote in person at a polling
  station if the elector

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} returns the unused mail-in ballot to the electoral
    officer or deputy electoral officer; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provides the electoral officer or deputy electoral
    officer with a sworn affidavit stating that they have lost their
    mail-in ballot.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} After receiving a ballot, an elector must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immediately proceed to the compartment provided for
    marking ballots;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} mark the ballot with a cross, check mark or other
    mark that clearly indicates the elector's choice, but does not
    identify the elector, next to the name of the candidates for whom
    they intend to vote;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fold the ballot in a manner that conceals the
    candidates' names and any marks on the ballot without hiding the
    initials on the back; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} give the ballot to the electoral officer or deputy
    electoral officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ballot box

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer or deputy electoral officer
  must, without unfolding the ballot, verify the initials placed on it
  and return the ballot to the elector to deposit in the ballot box or,
  at the elector's request, deposit it in the ballot box.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privacy

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (7), while an elector is in the
  compartment provided for marking ballots, no other person is allowed
  to be in the compartment or be in a position to see the manner in
  which the elector marks their ballot.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} At the request of any elector who is unable to vote
  in the manner set out in subsection (4), the electoral officer or
  deputy electoral deputy must, in the presence of a witness that the
  elector has selected, assist that elector by marking their ballot in
  the manner directed by the elector and return the ballot to the
  elector to deposit in the ballot box or, at the elector's request,
  deposit it in the ballot box.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Note on voters list

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer or deputy electoral officer
  must make a note on the voters list next to the elector's name to
  indicate that the ballot was marked by the electoral officer or deputy
  electoral officer at the elector's request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Spoiled ballot

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} An elector who has inadvertently made their ballot
  unusable may return it to the electoral officer or deputy electoral
  officer and is, on one occasion, entitled to obtain another ballot,
  and the electoral officer or deputy electoral officer must write the
  word "cancelled" on the spoiled ballot and preserve it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} Any person who has received a ballot and who
  declines to vote or who leaves the polling station without voting
  forfeits their right to vote at the election.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voters list

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a person who forfeits their right to
  vote, the electoral officer or deputy electoral officer must make a
  note on the voters list next to that person's name to indicate that
  the person received a ballot and declined to vote and, if possible,
  write the word "declined" on the back of the ballot and preserve it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entitlement to vote before closure

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} Every elector who is inside the polling station at
  the time fixed for closing the poll is entitled to vote before the
  poll is to be closed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rejection or acceptance of mail-in ballot

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** At the time and on the
date set for the counting of the votes in the notice referred to in
section 14, the electoral officer or deputy electoral officer must, in
the presence of everyone present, open each envelope containing a
mail-in ballot that was received before the close of the polls and,
without unfolding the ballot,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} reject the ballot if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is not accompanied by a voter declaration form
    or the voter declaration form is not signed or witnessed,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the elector set out in the voter
    declaration form is not on the voters list, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the voters list shows that the elector has
    already voted; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} place a mark on the voters list next to the elector's
  name that is set out in the voter declaration form and deposit the
  ballot in a ballot box.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ballots

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** After all mail-in ballots
have been deposited in a ballot box, the electoral officer or deputy
electoral officer must, in the presence of everyone present, open all
ballot boxes and

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine the ballots and reject any ballots

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that do not contain the electoral officer's or
    deputy electoral officer's initials, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on which any marks appear that would identify the
    voter;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} declare any portion of a ballot on which votes have
  been given for more candidates than are to be elected to an office as
  void with respect to the candidates for that office;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} take note of any objection made by any candidate or
  their representative to any ballot found in the ballot box and decide
  any question arising out of the objection;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} number any objection and place a corresponding number
  on the back of the ballot with their initials and the word "allowed"
  or "disallowed", as the case may be;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} from the ballots that are not rejected and from the
  portions of ballots that are not void, count the votes given for each
  candidate who has not withdrawn before the close of the polls; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} prepare and sign a statement of the number of votes
  for each candidate, the number of ballots that are rejected and the
  number of ballots that have a portion declared void.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), after the
  completion of the counting of the votes, the electoral officer must,
  in the presence of everyone present, declare to be elected the
  candidates having the highest number of votes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Five or fewer votes

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the difference between the number of votes of a
  candidate with the highest number of votes --- who would otherwise be
  declared elected --- and another candidate for the same position is
  five or fewer, the electoral officer must establish a date, time and
  place for a recount of the votes cast for those candidates and
  announce that date, time and place in the presence of everyone
  present.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of recount

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A recount must commence within 24 hours after the
  announcement by the electoral officer that a recount is necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Handling of ballots

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the recount is not to be conducted immediately
  after the counting of the votes, the electoral officer must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deposit all ballots in envelopes and seal them in a
    manner that prevents them from being opened without breaking the
    seal;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} place their initials on the seal and have any two
    people present do the same;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} deposit the sealed envelopes into a ballot box and
    seal that box in a manner that prevents it from being opened without
    breaking the seal; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} ensure the safekeeping of the sealed ballot box
    until the time established for a recount.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recount

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} On the date and at the time and place established for
  a recount, the electoral officer must open the sealed ballot box and
  the sealed envelopes in the presence of everyone present and conduct a
  recount.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} After the completion of the recount, the electoral
  officer must, in the presence of everyone present, declare to be
  elected the candidates having the highest number of votes and, in the
  case where a draw has been held in accordance with section 24 of the
  Act, any candidate who has won the draw.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Within four days after completion of the counting of
  the votes or, if there has been a recount, within four days after the
  recount, the electoral officer must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sign and post, in a conspicuous place on the
    reserve, a statement indicating the number of votes cast for each
    candidate and the names of the elected candidates; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} send a copy of the statement to the Department.

## [Disposal of Ballots and Election Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826478 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of documents

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  electoral officer must deposit all ballots in envelopes, seal them and
  ensure their safekeeping along with other election documents, for a
  period of 120 days following the election.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Destruction of documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the end of the period set out in subsection (1),
  the electoral officer must destroy the ballots and election documents,
  unless they are served, in accordance with section 34 of the Act, with
  an application to contest the election.

## [Candidacy Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826484 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of candidacy fee

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Within 30 days of the day
on which the electoral officer declared the results of the election, the
electoral officer must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} refund the candidacy fee to every candidate who
  received more than five percent of the total votes cast; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} remit to the First Nation the candidacy fees of all
  candidates who received five percent or less of the total votes cast.
