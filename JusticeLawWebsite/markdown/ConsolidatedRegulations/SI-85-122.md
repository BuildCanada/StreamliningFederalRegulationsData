## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639612 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order

[[alcoholic beverage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[alcoholic beverage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means beer, spirits or
    wine; ([boisson alcoolique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[beer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[beer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    [[beer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[malt liquor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    in section 4 of the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14);
    ([bière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[brewer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[brewer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 4
    of the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14); ([brasseur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[liquor board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[liquor board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any board, commission or
    other agency of a government of a province that under the laws of
    the province is empowered to sell and distribute or to authorize the
    sale and distribution of alcoholic beverages to visiting forces and
    visiting forces personnel; ([régie des alcools]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[spirits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[spirits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 2
    of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1);
    ([spiritueux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[visiting forces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[visiting forces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the armed
    forces of a country that is a party to the North Atlantic Treaty, a
    member of the Commonwealth or a designated state within the meaning
    of the [Visiting Forces Act](/eng/acts/V-2) and that is present in
    Canada on official duty; ([forces étrangères présentes au
    Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[visiting forces personnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[visiting forces personnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any member
    of visiting forces who is the holder of an authorized identification
    card issued by the Minister of National Defence and who is present
    in Canada on official duty but does not include any member on duty
    at a diplomatic mission. ([personnel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[wine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 2 of
    the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1). ([vin]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639624 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 4, remission is granted of the customs duties and
  additional duties equal to excise duties imposed under the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) that are paid or payable by a liquor board
  and of the taxes imposed under Part IX of the [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15) in respect of spirits, wine or imported beer sold
  to visiting forces or visiting forces personnel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 4, remission is granted of the
  excise duties imposed under the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1)
  that are paid or payable on or after July 1, 2003 by a liquor board in
  respect of spirits or wine sold to visiting forces or visiting forces
  personnel.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 4, remission is granted of the
  excise duties imposed under the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14) that are
  paid or payable by a brewer licensed under that Act and of the taxes
  imposed under Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) in
  respect of beer brewed in Canada and sold to visiting forces or
  visiting forces personnel.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639631 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on
condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the purchase price that is paid or payable by
  visiting forces or visiting forces personnel for alcoholic beverages
  does not include any amount for the duties or taxes referred to in
  section 3, or, where the purchase price paid or payable does include
  an amount for those duties or taxes, that amount is reimbursed or
  credited to the visiting forces or visiting forces personnel; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made by a liquor board or a
  brewer licensed under the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14) to the Minister
  of National Revenue or to the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
  Preparedness, as appropriate, and is supported by documentary evidence
  or other information as may be required for the administration of this
  Order, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of visiting forces, a written
    statement, signed by the unit commander of the visiting forces or a
    person authorized by him, stating that the alcoholic beverages
    purchased are for the sole use of the visiting forces or for resale
    to visiting forces personnel, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of visiting forces personnel, a
    written statement, signed by the member of the visiting forces
    making the purchase, stating that the alcoholic beverages purchased
    are for consumption by visiting forces personnel and will not be
    resold, given as a gift or otherwise distributed.
