## Order Respecting the Remission of Interest Payments Owing to the Government of Canada on Loans Made to the Republic of Cameroon, the Republic of Madagascar and the Republic of Zambia {#order-respecting-the-remission-of-interest-payments-owing-to-the-government-of-canada-on-loans-made-to-the-republic-of-cameroon-the-republic-of-madagascar-and-the-republic-of-zambia .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2001-23
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2001-02-14

Order Respecting the Remission of Interest Payments Owing to the
Government of Canada on Loans Made to the Republic of Cameroon, the
Republic of Madagascar and the Republic of Zambia

P.C. 2001-146 2001-01-30
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that the
remission of accrued interest payments owing to the Government of Canada
on loans made to the Republic of Cameroon, the Republic of Madagascar
and the Republic of Zambia through the Canada Account is in the public
interest, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, the Minister
of Foreign Affairs and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection
23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed
[Order Respecting the Remission of Interest Payments Owing to the
Government of Canada on Loans Made to the Republic of Cameroon, the
Republic of Madagascar and the Republic of
Zambia](/eng/regulations/SI-2001-23).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1991, c.
  24, s. 7(2)
  :::
:::

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605108 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to a country set out in column 1 of the schedule in the amount set out
in column 2, which amount shall not be repaid and represents the amount
of accrued interest owing to the Government of Canada on loans made
through the Canada Account.
