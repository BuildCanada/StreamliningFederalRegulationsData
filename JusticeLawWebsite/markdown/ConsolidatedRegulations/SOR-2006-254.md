## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725300 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vote under s. 17 or 18 of the Act

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
a vote held under section 17 or 18 of the Act.

## [Duties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725303 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of council

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  council of a First Nation that intends to conduct a vote must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} designate a person to be the electoral officer for
    the purpose of the vote; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} forward a written resolution to the Minister
    notifying the Minister of its intention to hold the vote and
    identifying the electoral officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of deputy electoral officer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer must designate one or more
  persons to be deputy electoral officers for the purpose of the vote.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of officers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer and the deputy electoral
  officers must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} supervise the conduct of the vote in accordance
    with these Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} act with fairness and impartiality in the
    administration of these Regulations; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} perform the duties and functions that are necessary
    for the administration of these Regulations.

## [The Question for the Vote]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725316 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of the question

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The question to be put to
the eligible voters is set out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in Part 1 of the schedule, in the case of the
  ratification of an oil and gas code and a financial code referred to
  in section 10 of the Act and the approval of the transfer to a First
  Nation of the management and regulation of oil and gas exploration and
  exploitation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in Part 2 of the schedule, in the case of the
  ratification of a financial code referred to in section 11 of the Act
  and the approval of the payment of moneys to a First Nation in
  accordance with the code; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in Part 3 of the schedule, in the case of a single
  vote on the ratification of an oil and gas code and a financial code
  referred to in section 10 of the Act, the approval of the transfer to
  a First Nation of the management and regulation of oil and gas
  exploration and exploitation, the ratification of a financial code
  referred to in section 11 of the Act and the approval of the payment
  of moneys to the First Nation in accordance with the code.

## [Voters List]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725322 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to provide names

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At least
  49 days before the day on which a vote is to be held, a list of all
  eligible voters must be provided to the electoral officer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by the First Nation, if the First Nation has
    assumed control of its own membership under section 10 of the
    [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by the Registrar, if the Band List of the First
    Nation is maintained in the Department of Indigenous Services under
    section 11 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of list

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A voters list must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the names of all eligible voters, in alphabetical
    order; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the First Nation membership number or registry
    number of each eligible voter or, if the eligible voter does not
    have a First Nation membership number or registry number, the date
    of birth of the eligible voter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confirmation of name on list

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On request, the electoral officer or a deputy
  electoral officer must confirm whether the name of a person is on the
  voters list.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revision of list

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer must revise the voters list if
  it is demonstrated that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of an eligible voter has been omitted from
    the list;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of an eligible voter is incorrectly set
    out on the list; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of a person not qualified to vote is
    included on the list.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof required for revision

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (4), a person may
  demonstrate that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of an eligible voter has been omitted
    from, or incorrectly set out on, the voters list by presenting to
    the electoral officer evidence from the Registrar or from the First
    Nation that the eligible voter is on the Band List and has reached
    the eligible age for participation in the vote; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of a person not qualified to vote has been
    included on the voters list by presenting to the electoral officer
    evidence that the person is not on the Band List or has not reached
    the eligible age for participation in the vote.

## [Addresses of Eligible Voters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725343 .Part}

## [Notification of Vote]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725348 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of officers

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  earlier of 14 days before the day on which the information meeting for
  a vote is to be held and 42 days before the day of the vote, the
  electoral officer or a deputy electoral officer must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} post a notice of the vote and a list of the
    eligible voters in at least one conspicuous place on the reserve;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} mail or deliver to every eligible voter of the
    First Nation who does not reside on the reserve and for whom an
    address has been provided

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a notice of the vote,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a mail-in ballot, initialled on the back by the
      electoral officer or a deputy electoral officer,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an outer, postage-paid return envelope,
      pre-addressed to the electoral officer,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a second, inner envelope marked "Ballot" for
      insertion of the completed ballot,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a voter declaration form,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a letter of instruction regarding voting by
      mail-in ballot, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} an information package regarding the vote.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of a vote must state

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the question to be submitted to the eligible
    voters;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the vote will be held;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the location of each polling station and the hours
    that it will be open for voting;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the fact that eligible voters may vote either in
    person at a polling station in accordance with subsection 17(3) or
    by mail-in ballot;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name and telephone number of the electoral
    officer; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the date, time and location of the information
    meeting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of material on request

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an eligible voter makes a request to the electoral
  officer, at least seven days before the vote, for the material
  referred to in paragraph (1)(b), the electoral officer must provide
  the material to the eligible voter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records about mail-in ballots

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer must indicate on the voters
  list that a ballot has been provided to each eligible voter to whom a
  mail-in ballot was mailed, delivered or otherwise provided, and keep a
  record of the date on which, and the address, if any, to which, each
  mail-in ballot was mailed or delivered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on voting in person

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An eligible voter to whom a mail-in ballot was
  mailed, delivered or provided under subsection (1) or (3) is not
  entitled to vote in person at a polling station other than in
  accordance with subsection 17(3).

## [Voter Declaration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725375 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of declaration

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An eligible voter's
completed voter declaration form must set out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the eligible voter;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the First Nation membership number or registry number
  of the eligible voter or, if the eligible voter does not have a First
  Nation membership number or registry number, the date of birth of the
  eligible voter;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the eligible voter has read the
  information in the information package regarding the question that is
  the subject of the vote, or that the information was read to the
  eligible voter, and that the eligible voter understood the
  information;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the eligible voter has voted freely
  and without compulsion; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of a witness
  to the signature of the eligible voter.

## [Information Meeting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725383 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to hold meeting

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  the day on which a vote is to be held, the council of the First Nation
  must ensure that an information meeting is held to provide the
  eligible voters with information regarding the vote.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons to be present

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A legal adviser and a financial adviser, designated
  by the council of the First Nation, must be present at the information
  meeting and an interpreter must also be present to interpret between
  those advisers and the eligible voters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be provided by the
  advisers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The legal adviser and the financial adviser who are
  present at the information meeting must advise the eligible voters
  about the following subjects, according to their respective expertise:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the processes for the implementation of the Act,
    including the voting process;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vote on the ratification of an oil
    and gas code and a financial code referred to in section 10 of the
    Act and the approval of the transfer to the First Nation of the
    management and regulation of oil and gas exploration and
    exploitation, the possible legal and financial consequences of the
    ratification of the oil and gas code, the financial code and the
    transfer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vote on the ratification of a
    financial code referred to in section 11 of the Act and the approval
    of the payment of moneys to the First Nation in accordance with the
    code, the possible legal and financial consequences of the
    ratification of the financial code and the payment; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the content of the question that is the subject of
    the vote and the possible legal and financial consequences of an
    affirmative vote or a negative vote.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Questions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The legal adviser and the financial adviser who are
  present at the information meeting must, according to their respective
  expertise, answer the questions of the eligible voters concerning the
  vote, including any questions about the subjects referred to in
  subsection (3).

## [Preparation for Vote]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725403 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of electoral officer

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The electoral officer or
the deputy electoral officers must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prepare sufficient ballots stating the question to be
  submitted to the eligible voters and ensure that the initials of the
  electoral officer or a deputy electoral officer are placed on the back
  of each ballot;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} procure a sufficient number of ballot boxes; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} before the polls are opened, cause the ballots and a
  sufficient number of lead pencils for marking the ballots to be
  delivered to the polls.

## [Voting by Mail-in Ballot]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725409 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How to vote

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  eligible voter may vote by mail-in ballot by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} marking the ballot by placing a cross, check mark
    or other mark, clearly indicating the eligible voter's response to
    the question that is the subject of the vote;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} folding the ballot in a manner that conceals the
    question and any marks but exposes the initials on the back;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} placing the ballot in the inner envelope and
    sealing that envelope;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} completing the voter declaration form and signing
    the completed form in the presence of a witness who is at least 18
    years of age;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} placing the inner envelope and the completed,
    signed and witnessed voter declaration form in the outer envelope;
    and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} delivering or, subject to subsection (6), mailing
    the outer envelope to the electoral officer before the time at which
    the polls close on the day of the vote.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance if required

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An eligible voter who is unable to vote in the manner
  set out in subsection (1) may enlist the assistance of another person
  to mark the ballot and complete and sign the voter declaration form in
  accordance with subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matters to be witnessed

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A witness referred to in paragraph (1)(d) must attest
  to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fact that the person signing the voter
    declaration form is the person whose name is set out on the form; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the eligible voter enlisted the assistance of
    another person under subsection (2), the fact that the eligible
    voter is the person whose name is set out on the form and that the
    ballot was marked according to the directions of the eligible voter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacing spoiled ballot

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An eligible voter who inadvertently spoils a mail-in
  ballot may obtain another ballot by returning the spoiled ballot to
  the electoral officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacing ballot lost or not received

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An eligible voter who loses or has not received a
  mail-in ballot may obtain another ballot by delivering to the
  electoral officer a written affirmation that the eligible voter has
  lost or has not received the mail-in ballot, signed by the eligible
  voter in the presence of the electoral officer, a justice of the
  peace, a notary public or a commissioner for oaths.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Mail-in ballots that are not received by the
  electoral officer before the time at which the polls close on the day
  of the vote are void and must not be counted as votes cast.

## [Voting at Polling Stations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725431 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement for polling station

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The electoral officer
must establish at least one polling station on the reserve.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privacy for voting

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The electoral officer or
a deputy electoral officer must provide a compartment at each polling
place where the eligible voter can mark his or her ballot free from
observation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ballot box

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The electoral officer or
a deputy electoral officer must, immediately before the opening of the
poll, open the ballot box and call on those persons who are present to
witness that it is empty and must then lock the ballot box and seal it
--- so as to ensure that any opening of the ballot box will be made
obvious by a disturbance to the seal --- using one of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a mechanical device;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} adhesive tape or seal; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other method that achieves the same result.

- SOR/2009-150, s. 2

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hours for polling stations

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Polling stations must be kept open
  from 9:00 a.m., local time, until 8:00 p.m., local time, on the day of
  the vote.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presence at closing time

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An eligible voter who is inside a polling station at
  the time that the polling station is to close is entitled to vote.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of ballot

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 7(5), if a
  person whose name is set out on the voters list attends at a polling
  station for the purpose of voting, the electoral officer or a deputy
  electoral officer must provide the person with a ballot.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marking voters list

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer or a deputy electoral officer
  must place on the voters list a mark opposite the name of every
  eligible voter receiving a ballot.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change in voting method

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An eligible voter to whom a mail-in ballot was
  mailed, delivered or provided under subsection 7(1) or (3) may obtain
  a ballot and vote in person at a polling station if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the eligible voter returns the mail-in ballot to
    the electoral officer or a deputy electoral officer; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the eligible voter has lost or has not received
    the mail-in ballot, the eligible voter provides the electoral
    officer or a deputy electoral officer with a written affirmation
    that the eligible voter has lost or has not received the mail-in
    ballot, signed by the eligible voter in the presence of the
    electoral officer, a deputy electoral officer, a justice of the
    peace, a notary public or a commissioner for oaths.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Explanation of mode of voting

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  electoral officer or a deputy electoral officer must explain the mode
  of voting to an eligible voter when requested to do so by the eligible
  voter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance in voting

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the request of an eligible voter who is not able
  to read or who is incapacitated by blindness or other physical cause,
  the electoral officer or a deputy electoral officer must assist the
  eligible voter by marking the eligible voter's ballot in the manner
  directed by the eligible voter and must place the ballot in the ballot
  box.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record of assistance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer or a deputy electoral officer
  must make an entry on the voters list opposite the name of the
  eligible voter indicating that the ballot was marked by him or her at
  the request of the eligible voter and the reasons for the request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voting procedure

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Except as provided in
subsection 18(2), every eligible voter receiving a ballot must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} proceed immediately to the compartment provided for
  marking the ballot;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} mark the ballot by placing a cross, check mark or
  other mark, clearly indicating the eligible voter's response to the
  question that is the subject of the vote;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} fold the ballot in a manner that conceals the
  question and any marks but exposes the initials on the back; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} deliver it without delay to the electoral officer or
  a deputy electoral officer for deposit in the ballot box.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacement ballot

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  eligible voter who receives a soiled or improperly printed ballot, or
  who inadvertently spoils his or her ballot in marking it, and who
  returns the ballot to the electoral officer or a deputy electoral
  officer is entitled to another ballot.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiting right to vote

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An eligible voter who receives a ballot forfeits his
  or her right to vote if he or she

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} leaves the compartment for marking ballots without
    delivering the ballot to the electoral officer or a deputy electoral
    officer in the manner provided; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} refuses to vote.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recording the event

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In a case described in subsection (2), the electoral
  officer or a deputy electoral officer must make an entry on the voters
  list opposite the name of the eligible voter indicating that the
  eligible voter did not return the ballot or refused to vote, as the
  case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voting compartment

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The electoral officer or
a deputy electoral officer must allow only one eligible voter in the
compartment for marking ballots at any one time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition on interference

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person may interfere or attempt to interfere with an eligible voter
  when the eligible voter is marking his or her ballot.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition on obtaining information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person may obtain or attempt to obtain at the
  polling place information as to how an eligible voter is about to vote
  or has voted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpreter

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** If the electoral officer
or a deputy electoral officer does not understand the language spoken by
an eligible voter, the electoral officer or the deputy electoral officer
must enlist the aid of an interpreter to communicate with respect to all
matters required to enable that eligible voter to vote.

## [Counting Votes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725488 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mail-in ballots

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** After the close of the
polls, the electoral officer must, in the presence of a deputy electoral
officer and any members of the council of the First Nation who are
present, open each envelope containing a mail-in ballot that was
received before the close of the polls and, without unfolding the
ballot,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} set aside the ballot if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is not accompanied by a voter declaration form
    or the voter declaration form is not signed or witnessed,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the eligible voter set out in the
    voter declaration form is not on the voters list, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the voters list shows that the eligible voter has
    already voted; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} place a mark on the voters list opposite the name of
  the eligible voter set out on the voter declaration form and deposit
  the ballot in a ballot box.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure on opening ballot boxes

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  the mail-in ballots have been deposited under section 24, the
  electoral officer must, in the presence of a deputy electoral officer
  and any members of the council of the First Nation who are present,
  open all ballot boxes and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine the ballots;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set aside any ballot that does not have the
    initials of the electoral officer or a deputy electoral officer on
    the back;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} reject all ballots

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that have been marked incorrectly, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on which anything appears by which an eligible
      voter can be identified;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} count the votes cast in favour of and against the
    question submitted in the vote; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} prepare a statement in writing of the number of
    votes so cast and of the number of ballots rejected.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signing and filing statement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The statement referred to in paragraph (1)(e) must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} signed by the electoral officer and by the chief or
    a member of the council of the First Nation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} filed in the regional office of the Department of
    Indigenous Services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Void ballots

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** A ballot set aside under
paragraph 24(a) or 25(1)(b) or rejected under paragraph 25(1)(c) is void
and must not be counted as a vote cast.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement of results

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** When the results of the
voting are known, the electoral officer must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prepare a statement in triplicate, signed by the
  electoral officer and by the chief or a member of the council of the
  First Nation, that confirms that the vote was conducted in accordance
  with these Regulations and indicates the number of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} eligible voters who were entitled to vote,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} eligible voters who voted,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} votes cast in favour of and against the question
    submitted in the vote, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} rejected ballots; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} deliver a copy of the statement to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Assistant Deputy Minister, in the case of a
    vote held under section 17 of the Act, or the Senior Assistant
    Deputy Minister, in the case of a vote held under section 18 of the
    Act,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the person in charge of the regional office of the
    Department of Indigenous Services, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the chief of the First Nation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of ballots

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  electoral officer must deposit the ballots used in the voting,
  together with all supporting documents, in a sealed envelope and
  retain them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Destruction of ballots

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If no review has been requested within 60 days after
  the day of the vote, the electoral officer must destroy the ballots
  used in the voting.

## [Review Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725528 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for review

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  eligible voter may, in the manner set out in subsection (2), request a
  review of the vote by the Minister if the eligible voter believes that
  there was

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a contravention of these Regulations that may
    affect the results of the vote; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corrupt practice in connection with the vote.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure for request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request for a review of a vote must be forwarded to
  the Minister, by registered mail addressed to the Assistant Deputy
  Minister, in the case of a vote held under section 17 of the Act, or
  the Senior Assistant Deputy Minister, in the case of a vote held under
  section 18 of the Act, within 30 days after the day of the vote. The
  request must include a declaration signed in the presence of a witness
  who is at least 18 years of age that contains the grounds for
  requesting the review and any other relevant information.
