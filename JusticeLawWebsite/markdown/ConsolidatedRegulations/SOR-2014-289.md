[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Standing Orders.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
    Act](/eng/acts/R-10). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
    40.1 of the Act. ([document]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[informal resolution process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[informal resolution process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a process
    used by the parties to resolve a dispute informally and includes the
    informal conflict management system established under section 30.2
    of the Act. ([processus de règlement informel]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[OCGA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[OCGA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the office responsible for the
    coordination of administrative matters relating to grievances and
    appeals in the Force. ([BCGA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Royal Canadian
    Mounted Police Regulations,
    2014](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-281). ([Règlement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[standardized
test]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 31(4.2) of the Act, [[standardized test]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
means a test that is used by the Force to assess an individual's
behaviour, knowledge and experience in order to evaluate job-relevant
competencies, and includes the marking scheme, the answer key and any
materials prepared for the purpose of developing the test. The content
of the test must be equivalent for all test-takers, be administered
according to standard instructions and procedures and be scored
according to a set protocol.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Grievances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-808877 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808878 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Part.

[[adjudicator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[adjudicator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person constituting the
    initial level or the final level. ([arbitre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[final level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[final level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the final level in the
    grievance process. ([dernier niveau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[grievance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[grievance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a grievance presented under
    section 31 of the Act. ([grief]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[grievor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[grievor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member who presents a
    grievance. ([plaignant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[initial level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[initial level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the initial level in the
    grievance process. ([premier niveau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[parties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[parties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the grievor and the
    respondent. ([parties]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a grievance that has not been
      consolidated with any other grievance, the person who made the
      decision, did the act or made the omission that is the subject of
      the grievance or, if that person is absent or unable to act, the
      person who replaces them or, in the absence of a replacement, the
      person designated by the adjudicator who is considering the
      grievance; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a grievance that has been
      consolidated with another grievance, the person designated under
      paragraph 10(c) or (d). ([intimé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial level

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  persons who constitute the initial level are the persons who perform
  the functions of a level 1 in the Force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial level --- three persons

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection 6(1), all combinations
  of any three persons who perform the functions of a level 1 in the
  Force constitute the initial level.

### [Access to Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808897 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitations under subsection 31(4) of Act

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the
limitations referred to in subsection 31(4) of the Act, a member is not
entitled to be granted access to written or documentary information

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure of which could reasonably be expected
  to be injurious to the defence of Canada or any state allied or
  associated with Canada, as defined in subsection 15(2) of the [Access
  to Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1), or to the detection, prevention or
  suppression of subversive or hostile activities, as defined in that
  subsection;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure of which could reasonably be expected
  to be injurious to law enforcement;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure of which is contrary to a provision of
  any contract entered into by the Crown;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} about a person's financial or personal affairs if the
  person's interest or security outweighs the member's interest in the
  information; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure of which is prohibited by law.

### [Panel for Grievances]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808905 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial level panel

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner may designate a person to select any three-person
  combination described in subsection 3(2) as a panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Panel selection

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designated person may select the panel for the
  purpose of hearing a grievance in the following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the decision, act or omission giving rise to the
    grievance is likely to be the subject of numerous grievances;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the relevant law or the relevant Treasury Board or
    Force policy is vague or ambiguous; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the relevant law is contradictory, or the relevant
    Treasury Board or Force policy is contradictory or internally
    inconsistent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The designated person must designate one of the three
  persons to be the chairperson of the panel. The chairperson is
  responsible for its day-to-day management, including the management of
  communications between the panel and the OCGA and the panel and the
  parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Panel decision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Matters before a panel are decided by a majority of
  the members of the panel. The chairperson has a second vote in the
  case of a tie.

### [Presentation of Grievance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808918 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial level grievance

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  grievor presents a grievance at the initial level by filing a
  grievance form with the OCGA or the grievor's supervisor that includes
  the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the grievor's name and employee number;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} particulars concerning the decision, act or
    omission that is the subject of the grievance and the name of the
    person the grievor believes made the decision, did the act or made
    the omission;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the grievor knew of the decision,
    act or omission;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the grounds on which the
    grievance is based;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} particulars concerning the prejudice the grievor
    claims to have suffered as a result of the decision, act or
    omission; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} particulars concerning the redress requested.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final level grievance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A grievor presents a grievance at the final level by
  filing with the OCGA or the grievor's supervisor

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a grievance form that includes the following
    information:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the reasons why the grievor is presenting the
      grievance at the final level and, in particular, the reasons why
      the decision rendered by the adjudicator at the initial level
      contravenes the principles of procedural fairness, is based on an
      error of law or is clearly unreasonable, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} particulars concerning the redress requested;
      and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the decision rendered by the adjudicator
    at the initial level as to the disposition of the grievance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transmission of grievance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A supervisor presented with a grievance form must
  forward it, together with any request submitted under subsection 8(1),
  to the OCGA as soon as feasible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transmission of copy of grievance

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The OCGA must forward a copy of any grievance form
  that it receives to the respondent and to the person who holds the
  first position of officer or its equivalent in the respondent's chain
  of command, or to the person named in the decision rendered under
  subsection 8(3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request to not forward

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  grievor who wishes to object to a copy of the grievance form referred
  to in subsection 7(1) being forwarded to the person who holds the
  first position of officer or its equivalent in the respondent's chain
  of command must, when they present their grievance, submit to the OCGA
  a written request not to forward it that includes supporting reasons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transmission of request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The OCGA must forward the request and a copy of the
  grievance form to the adjudicator who is considering the grievance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision on request

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The adjudicator must render a decision on the
  request, including reasons for the decision, as soon as feasible, and
  send the decision to the OCGA. If the adjudicator allows the request,
  they must name in the decision an appropriate person for the purpose
  of receiving a copy of the grievance form.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of decision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The OCGA must cause a copy of the adjudicator's
  decision to be served on the parties.

### [Informal Resolution]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808947 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informal resolution

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As soon
  as feasible after receiving a copy of a grievance form, the respondent
  must contact the grievor to discuss an informal resolution of the
  grievance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to discuss grievance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the grievor refuses to discuss the grievance, or
  any aspect of it, with a view to resolving it informally, the
  respondent must provide written notice of that fact to the OCGA and
  cause a copy of it to be served on the grievor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing resolution

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The parties may, until the adjudicator disposes of
  the grievance in writing, continue to attempt to resolve the matters
  in dispute by way of an informal resolution process.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements for informal resolution

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} On request by a party, the adjudicator who is
  considering a grievance may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} review a proposed agreement for the informal
    resolution of the grievance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} approve an agreement by the parties for the
    informal resolution of the grievance; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} resolve any issues or concerns relating to the
    implementation of the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communications confidential and without
  prejudice

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any communications that pass among persons as a part
  of an informal resolution process are confidential, without prejudice
  to the parties and must not be disclosed unless the disclosure is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} agreed to by the parties;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} required by law;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} required for the purpose of reaching or
    implementing an agreement made as a part of the process; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} required to protect the health or safety of any
    person.

### [Adjudicator]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808966 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjudicator's powers

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** An adjudicator, when
considering a grievance, has the power to decide all matters related to
the grievance, including the power

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to dismiss it if it is moot, frivolous, vexatious or
  an abuse of process, or has already been adjudicated;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to render a single decision in respect of the
  grievance and any other substantially similar grievance that is being
  considered by the adjudicator;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to consolidate the grievance of a grievor with the
  grievance of any other grievor that is being considered by the
  adjudicator, with the consent of the grievors, and to designate the
  respondent for the consolidated grievance;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to consolidate the grievance with any other grievance
  of the grievor that is being considered by the adjudicator and to
  designate the respondent for the consolidated grievance;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} to sever the grievance into two or more distinct
  grievances; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} to decide whether any written or documentary
  information to which the grievor has requested access is a
  standardized test or the information referred to in section 5.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct of grievance

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  adjudicator must render their decision in respect of a grievance or
  any matter arising in the context of the grievance as informally and
  expeditiously as the principles of procedural fairness permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matters not provided for

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If any matter arises in the context of a grievance
  that is not otherwise provided for in the provisions of the Act, the
  Regulations or this Part, the adjudicator may give any direction that
  is appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to remedy --- non-compliance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The adjudicator may remedy any failure by any party
  to comply with any provision of this Part.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Case conference

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  adjudicator who is considering a grievance or any matter arising in
  the context of a grievance may, on their own initiative or at the
  request of a party, hold a case conference to discuss the grievance or
  matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communications confidential and without
  prejudice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any communications that pass among persons as a part
  of a case conference are confidential, without prejudice to the
  parties and must not be disclosed unless the disclosure is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} agreed to by the parties;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} required by law;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} required for the purpose of reaching or
    implementing an agreement made as a part of the conference; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} required to protect the health or safety of any
    person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjudicator not barred

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An adjudicator who holds a case conference is not
  prevented from exercising any of their other powers.

### [Procedure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808993 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submissions

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  parties may make submissions on any matter in dispute in respect of
  the grievance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of submissions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An adjudicator who is considering the grievance or
  any matter arising in the context of the grievance is responsible for
  deciding how and when the submissions may be made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision in absence of submissions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The adjudicator may render a decision on a matter in
  dispute in respect of the grievance in the absence of submissions from
  one or both of the parties if reasonable notice has been provided to
  them as to how and when submissions may be made in respect of the
  matter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of grievance

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A grievor may withdraw
their grievance by written notice to the OCGA before it is disposed of
in writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** An adjudicator, when
considering a grievance or any matter arising in the context of a
grievance, may accept any evidence submitted by a party.

### [Decision]{.HTitleText2} {#h-809005 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision at initial level

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  adjudicator may dispose of a grievance at the initial level by
  rendering a decision

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dismissing the grievance and confirming the
    decision, act or omission that is the subject of the grievance; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} allowing the grievance and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} remitting the matter, with directions for
      reconsidering the decision, act or omission, to the respondent or
      to the person who is responsible for the reconsideration, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} directing any appropriate redress.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Considerations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An adjudicator, when rendering the decision, must
  consider whether the decision, act or omission that is the subject of
  the grievance is consistent with the relevant law, or the relevant
  Treasury Board or Force policy and, if it is not, whether it has
  caused a prejudice to the grievor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-compliance with direction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), the adjudicator may, subject
  to the principles of procedural fairness, dispose of a grievance
  against the interests of a party that has failed to comply with any of
  their directions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rescission or amendment of decision

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  adjudicator who has rendered a decision that disposes of a grievance
  at the initial level may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} amend the decision to correct clerical or
    typographical errors or any errors of a similar nature or to clarify
    unclear wording; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the grievance has not been presented at the
    final level, rescind or amend their decision on the presentation of
    new facts or on determining that an error of fact or law was made in
    reaching the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjudicator's powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), the adjudicator
  has the powers set out in section 10.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opportunity to make submissions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before rescinding or amending the decision under
  paragraph (1)(b), the adjudicator must give the parties an opportunity
  to make submissions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the adjudicator rescinds or amends the decision,
  they must cause a copy of the notice of rescission or a copy of the
  amended decision to be served on the parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision at final level

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  adjudicator may dispose of a grievance at the final level by rendering
  a decision

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dismissing the grievance and confirming the
    decision rendered at the initial level; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} allowing the grievance and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} remitting the matter, with directions for
      reconsidering the decision, act or omission, to the respondent or
      to the person who is responsible for the reconsideration,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} remitting the matter, with directions for
      rendering a new decision to the adjudicator at the initial level
      or to another adjudicator, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} directing any appropriate redress.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Considerations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An adjudicator, when rendering the decision, must
  consider whether the decision at the initial level contravenes the
  principles of procedural fairness, is based on an error of law or is
  clearly unreasonable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-compliance with direction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), the adjudicator may, subject
  to the principles of procedural fairness, dispose of a grievance
  against the interests of a party that has failed to comply with any of
  their directions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opportunity to make submissions

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  adjudicator at the final level must, before rescinding or amending a
  decision under subsection 32(3) of the Act, give the parties an
  opportunity to make submissions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the adjudicator rescinds or amends the decision,
  they must cause a copy of the notice of rescission or a copy of the
  amended decision to be served on the parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of evidence

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** After the disposition of
a grievance, the OCGA must cause to be returned to a party anything that
the party tendered as evidence.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Appeals (Part IV of the Act)]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809047 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-809048 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Part.

[[appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an appeal made under section
    45.11 of the Act. ([appel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[appellant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appellant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the member or conduct
    authority who makes the appeal. ([appelant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[parties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[parties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the appellant and the
    respondent. ([parties]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an appeal of a conduct board's
      decision,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the appellant is the member who is the
        subject of the conduct board's decision, the conduct authority
        who initiated the hearing by the conduct board or, if that
        conduct authority is absent or unable to act, the conduct
        authority who replaces them, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the appellant is the conduct authority who
        initiated the hearing by the conduct board, the member who is
        the subject of the conduct board's decision;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an appeal of a conduct authority's
      decision, the conduct authority or, if that conduct authority is
      absent or unable to act, the conduct authority who replaces them;
      or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an appeal that has been
      consolidated with another appeal, the person designated under
      paragraph 29(c) or (d). ([intimé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Presentation of Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-809060 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement of appeal

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** An appeal to the
Commissioner must be made by filing a statement of appeal with the OCGA
within 14 days after the day on which copy of the decision giving rise
to the appeal is served on the member who is the subject of that
decision. The statement must be accompanied by a copy of the decision
that is being appealed and include the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the appellant's name and, if any, their employee
  number;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the grounds on which the
  appeal is based; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the particulars concerning the redress requested.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requests under subsection 45.15(3) of Act

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  request made by a member to the Commissioner not to refer a case to
  the Committee under subsection 45.15(3) of the Act must be filed in
  writing, with supporting reasons, with the OCGA within 14 days after
  the day on which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the member is served with a copy of the decision
    giving rise to the appeal, if the member is the appellant; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the member is served with a copy of the statement
    of appeal, if the member is the respondent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner must cause a copy of his or her
  decision to be served on the member.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to file material

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** In the case of an appeal
by a member of a decision rendered by a conduct authority, the
respondent must, as soon as feasible after being served with the
statement of appeal, file with the OCGA the material that was before the
conduct authority when the decision was rendered.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supporting documents

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  OCGA must provide the appellant with an opportunity to file written
  submissions and other documents in support of their appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appellant is not entitled to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} file any document that was not provided to the
    person who rendered the decision that is the subject of the appeal
    if it was available to the appellant when the decision was rendered;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} include in their written submissions any new
    information that was known or could reasonably have been known by
    the appellant when the decision was rendered.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of documents

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** After receiving the
statement of appeal and any written submission or other document filed
by a party, the OCGA must cause a copy of each one to be served on the
other party.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response to submissions --- appeal of
conduct authority decision

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** In the case of an appeal
of a decision rendered by a conduct authority, the respondent may file
with the OCGA, if the respondent is permitted to do so by the
Commissioner due to exceptional circumstances, a written response to the
appellant's submissions and the appellant may file with the OCGA a
written reply to that response.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response to submissions --- appeal of
conduct board decision

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** In the case of an appeal
of a decision rendered by a conduct board, the respondent may file with
the OCGA a written response to the appellant's submissions and the
appellant may file with the OCGA a written rebuttal to that response.

### [Commissioner]{.HTitleText2} {#h-809088 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissioner's powers

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner, when
considering an appeal, has the power to decide all matters related to
the appeal, including the power

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to render a single decision in respect of the appeal
  and any other substantially similar appeal that is being considered by
  the Commissioner;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to consolidate the appeal with any other appeal of
  the same decision;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to consolidate the appeal of an appellant with the
  appeal of any other appellant that is being considered by the
  Commissioner, with the consent of the appellants, and to designate the
  respondent for the consolidated appeal;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to consolidate the appeal with any other appeal of
  the appellant that is being considered by the Commissioner and to
  designate the respondent for the consolidated appeal; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} to extend the time limit referred to in section 22
  and subsection 23(1) in exceptional circumstances.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct of appeal

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner must render his or her decision in respect of an appeal
  or any matter arising in the context of the appeal as informally and
  expeditiously as the principles of procedural fairness permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matters not provided for

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If any matter arises in the context of an appeal that
  is not otherwise provided for in the provisions of the Act, the
  Regulations or this Part, the Commissioner may give any direction that
  is appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to remedy --- non-compliance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may remedy any failure by any party
  to comply with any provision of this Part.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of appeal

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** An appellant may withdraw
their appeal by a written notice to the OCGA before it is disposed of in
writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner, when
considering an appeal or any matter arising in the context of an appeal,
may accept any evidence submitted by a party.

### [Decision]{.HTitleText2} {#h-809107 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision of Commissioner

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner, when rendering a decision as to the disposition of the
  appeal, must consider whether the decision that is the subject of the
  appeal contravenes the principles of procedural fairness, is based on
  an error of law or is clearly unreasonable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-compliance with direction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Commissioner may, subject
  to the principles of procedural fairness, dispose of an appeal against
  the interests of a party that has failed to comply with any of his or
  her directions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of decision

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner must
cause a copy of the decision referred to in subsection 45.16(7) of the
Act to be served on the parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opportunity to make submissions

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  rescinding or amending a decision under subsection 45.16(10) of the
  Act, the Commissioner must give the parties an opportunity to make
  submissions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Commissioner rescinds or amends the decision,
  he or she must cause a copy of the notice of rescission or a copy of
  the amended decision to be served on the parties.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Appeals (Other than Part IV of the Act)]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809120 .Part}

### [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-809121 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Part.

[[adjudicator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[adjudicator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated as an
    adjudicator by the Commissioner. ([arbitre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an appeal referred to in
    section 37. ([appel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[appellant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appellant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the member who makes an
    appeal or the former member who makes an appeal of their discharge
    from the Force. ([appelant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[parties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[parties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the appellant and the
    respondent. ([parties]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an appeal that has not been
      consolidated with any other appeal, the person who rendered the
      written decision that is the subject of the appeal or, if that
      person is absent or unable to act, the person who replaces them;
      or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an appeal that has been
      consolidated with another appeal, the person designated under
      paragraph 43(b) or (c). ([intimé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** This Part provides the
process for appeals

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} of a written decision that is made in the course of a
  selection process for the promotion of a member to a higher rank or
  that is in respect of a job requirement established for a position,
  other than an official languages requirement;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} of the written decision referred to in subsection
  9(2) of the [Commissioner's Standing Orders (General
  Administration)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-293);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} of the written decisions referred to in subsection
  20(1) of the [Commissioner's Standing Orders (Employment
  Requirements)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-292);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} of the directive referred to in subsection 20(2) of
  the [Commissioner's Standing Orders (Employment
  Requirements)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-292); and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} of the written decisions referred to in subsection
  32(1) of the [Commissioner's Standing Orders
  (Conduct)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-291).

### [Presentation of Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-809138 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement of appeal

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the
provisions referred to in section 37, an appeal must be made by filing a
statement of appeal with the OCGA within 14 days after the day on which
a copy of the decision giving rise to the appeal is served on the member
who is the subject of that decision. The statement must be accompanied
by a copy of the decision that is being appealed and include the
following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the appellant's name and employee number;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the reasons why the appellant
  is of the opinion that the decision that is the subject of the appeal
  contravenes the principles of procedural fairness, is based on an
  error of law or is clearly unreasonable; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} particulars concerning the redress requested.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to file material

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** The respondent must, as
soon as feasible after being served with the statement of appeal, file
with the OCGA the material that was before the person who rendered the
written decision that is the subject of the appeal when that decision
was rendered.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supporting documents

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  OCGA must provide the appellant with an opportunity to file written
  submissions and other documents in support of their appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appellant is not entitled to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} file any document that was not provided to the
    person who rendered the decision that is the subject of the appeal
    if it was available to the appellant when the decision was rendered;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} include in their written submissions any new
    information that was known or could reasonably have been known by
    the appellant when the decision was rendered.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of documents

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** After receiving the
statement of appeal and any written submission or other document filed
by a party, the OCGA must cause a copy of each one to be served on the
other party.

### [Informal Resolution]{.HTitleText2} {#h-809155 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informal resolution

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  parties may, until the adjudicator disposes of an appeal in writing,
  attempt to resolve the matters in dispute by way of an informal
  resolution process.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements for informal resolution

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On request by a party, the adjudicator who is
  considering an appeal may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} review a proposed agreement for the informal
    resolution of the appeal;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} approve an agreement by the parties for the
    informal resolution of the appeal; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} resolve any issues or concerns relating to the
    implementation of the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communications confidential and without
  prejudice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any communications that pass among persons as a part
  of an informal resolution process are confidential, without prejudice
  to the parties and must not be disclosed unless the disclosure is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} agreed to by the parties;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} required by law;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} required for the purpose of reaching or
    implementing an agreement made as a part of the process; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} required to protect the health or safety of any
    person.

### [Adjudicator]{.HTitleText2} {#h-809170 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjudicator's powers

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** An adjudicator, when
considering an appeal, has the power to decide all matters related to
the appeal, including the power

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to render a single decision in respect of the appeal
  and any other substantially similar appeal that is being considered by
  the adjudicator;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to consolidate the appeal of an appellant with the
  appeal of any other appellant that is being considered by the
  adjudicator, with the consent of the appellants, and to designate the
  respondent for the consolidated appeal;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to consolidate the appeal with any other appeal of
  the appellant that is being considered by the adjudicator and to
  designate the respondent for the consolidated appeal; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to extend the time limit referred to in section 38 in
  exceptional circumstances.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct of appeal

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  adjudicator must render their decision in respect of an appeal or any
  matter arising in the context of the appeal as informally and
  expeditiously as the principles of procedural fairness permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matters not provided for

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If any matter arises in the context of an appeal that
  is not otherwise provided for in the provisions of the Act, the
  Regulations or this Part, the adjudicator may give any direction that
  is appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to remedy --- non-compliance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The adjudicator may remedy any failure by any party
  to comply with any provision of this Part.

### [Procedure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-809184 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of appeal

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** An appellant may withdraw
their appeal by a written notice to the OCGA before it is disposed of in
writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** The adjudicator, when
considering an appeal or any matter arising in the context of an appeal,
may accept any evidence submitted by a party.

### [Decision]{.HTitleText2} {#h-809189 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjudicator's decision

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  adjudicator may dispose of an appeal by rendering a decision

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dismissing the appeal and confirming the decision
    being appealed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} allowing the appeal and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} remitting the matter, with directions for
      rendering a new decision to the decision maker who rendered the
      decision being appealed or to another decision maker, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} directing any appropriate redress.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision in writing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An adjudicator considering an appeal must, as soon as
  feasible, render a decision in writing that disposes of the appeal and
  includes reasons for the decision. The decision is final and binding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Considerations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An adjudicator, when rendering the decision, must
  consider whether the decision that is the subject of the appeal
  contravenes the principles of procedural fairness, is based on an
  error of law or is clearly unreasonable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-compliance with direction

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (3), the adjudicator may, subject
  to the principles of procedural fairness, dispose of an appeal against
  the interests of a party that has failed to comply with any of their
  directions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision rendered in absence of
  Committee recommendations

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If an appeal has been referred to the Committee under
  section 17 of the Regulations, the adjudicator may render a decision
  on the appeal in the absence of any findings or recommendations made
  by the Committee if the Committee Chairperson indicates to the OCGA
  that the Committee will not be making any or if the Committee does not
  make any within a reasonable time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjudicator not bound

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The adjudicator is not bound to act on any findings
  or recommendations made by the Committee but, if the adjudicator does
  not so act, the adjudicator must include in the decision on the appeal
  the reasons for not so acting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of decision on parties

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The adjudicator must cause a copy of the decision to
  be served on the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of decision on Committee

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If the appeal has been referred to the Committee
  under section 17 of the Regulations, the adjudicator must cause a copy
  of the decision to be served on the Committee Chairperson.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rescission or amendment of decision

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  adjudicator may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} amend their decision to correct clerical or
    typographical errors or any errors of a similar nature or to clarify
    unclear wording; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} rescind or amend their decision on the presentation
    of new facts or on determining that an error of fact or law was made
    in reaching the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjudicator's powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(b), the adjudicator
  has the powers set out in section 43 and subsection 47(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opportunity to make submissions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before rescinding or amending the decision under
  paragraph (1)(b), the adjudicator must give the parties an opportunity
  to make submissions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the adjudicator rescinds or amends the decision,
  they must cause a copy of the notice of rescission or a copy of the
  amended decision to be served on the parties and, if the appeal has
  been referred to the Committee under section 17 of the Regulations, on
  the Committee Chairperson.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of evidence

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** After the disposition of
an appeal, the OCGA must cause to be returned to a party anything that
the party tendered as evidence.

### [Reference to Committee]{.HTitleText2} {#h-809224 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request not to refer

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appellant requesting that their appeal not be referred to the
  Committee under section 17 of the Regulations must, when they make
  their appeal, submit to the OCGA a written request that includes
  supporting reasons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transmission of request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The OCGA must forward the request and a copy of the
  statement of appeal to the adjudicator who is considering the appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjudicator's decision

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The adjudicator may allow the request or, if the
  adjudicator considers that a reference to the Committee is
  appropriate, reject it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transmission of decision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The adjudicator must render a decision in writing on
  the request, including reasons for the decision, as soon as feasible,
  and send a copy of the decision to the OCGA.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of decision

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The OCGA must cause a copy of the adjudicator's
  decision to be served on the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Material to be provided to Committee

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the adjudicator refers an appeal to the Committee
  in accordance with section 17 of the Regulations, the OCGA must
  provide the Committee Chairperson with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an appeal of a decision referred to
    in paragraph 17(a) of the Regulations, the material that was before
    the decision maker when the decision was rendered; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an appeal of a decision referred to
    in any of paragraphs 17(b) to (e) of the Regulations, the material
    that was before the Commissioner when the decision was rendered.

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** These Standing Orders
come into force on the day on which they are registered.
