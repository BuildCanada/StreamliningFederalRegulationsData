## Pay and Allowances Regulations (DSPCA) {#pay-and-allowances-regulations-dspca .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/67-619
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 7, 1967

Regulations Made Pursuant to Vote 49a of the Appropriation Act No. 7,
1967
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Treasury Board, pursuant to vote 49a of the Appropriation Act Number
7, 1967, hereby makes the annexed [Pay and Allowances Regulations
(DSPCA)](/eng/regulations/SOR-67-619).
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Pay and Allowances Regulations
(DSPCA)](/eng/regulations/SOR-67-619).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[authorized pay]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorized pay]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means pay at the rates
    prescribed by Regulations made under the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5) for the rank held by the person in respect of
    whom the expression is being applied; and ([solde
    autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[allowances]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[allowances]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the subsistence allowance
    and the marriage allowance authorized to be paid to a man pursuant
    to Regulations made under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5)
    at the rates in effect on September 30, 1966.
    ([indemnités]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every man to whom the
Defence Service Pension Continuation Act applies and who after September
30, 1966, served in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the regular forces of the Canadian Army or the Royal
  Canadian Air Force or on Continuous or Special Duty in the reserve
  forces of those Services and held a rank below the rank of warrant
  officer, class 2; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the regular force of the Canadian Forces or on
  Continuous or Special Duty in the reserve force of the Canadian Forces
  and held a rank below the rank of master warrant officer;

shall, for the purposes of sections 13 and 14 of the said Act, be deemed
to have been during that service in receipt of

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the allowances applicable to him for the rank held by
  him; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} pay in an amount equal to the amount by which his
  authorized pay exceed his allowances.
