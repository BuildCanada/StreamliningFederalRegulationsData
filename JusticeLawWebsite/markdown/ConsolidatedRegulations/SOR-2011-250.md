Transfer of Portions of the Parks Canada Agency Regulations

P.C. 2011-1295 2011-11-15

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to subsection 123(1) of the [Public Service
Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote

## [Block Transfer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-774959 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 132(1) of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) applies to all
persons employed or engaged in the portions of the federal public
administration in the Parks Canada Agency known as the Email, Data
Centre and Network Services Unit and the Email, Data Centre and Network
Services Support Unit.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-774961 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on November 15, 2011.
