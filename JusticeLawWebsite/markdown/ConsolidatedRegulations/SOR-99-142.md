## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028188 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Lands Surveyors
    Act](/eng/acts/L-5.8). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[incompetence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[incompetence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a lack of knowledge,
    skill or judgment or a disregard for the public interest of such a
    nature or to such an extent as to demonstrate that the member of the
    Association, Canada Lands Surveyor or permit holder is unable to
    meet the requirements of the profession or to conduct surveys in
    accordance with the Act or any other Act respecting surveying or any
    regulations made thereunder. ([incompétence]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[member of the Association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[member of the Association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of
    the Association of Canada Lands Surveyors. ([membre de
    l'Association]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[professional misconduct]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[professional misconduct]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
    member of the Association, Canada Lands Surveyor or permit holder,
    as the case may be, means any of the following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} receiving a conviction in a court of competent
      jurisdiction of an offence relating to surveying;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contravening the Act, these Regulations or the
      by-laws, or any other Act or regulations relating to surveying;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} failing to comply with the code of ethics of the
      Association;

    - [(d) and (e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2011-291,
      s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} failing to engage employees that are competent by
      virtue of education or training, or both, to perform the duties
      assigned to them;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} knowingly allowing or encouraging employees who
      are not Canada Lands Surveyors to engage in activities that
      reasonably would be regarded as those of a Canada Lands Surveyor;

    - [(h) and (i)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2011-291,
      s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} charging a fee for services not performed or
      knowingly submitting a false or misleading estimate, account or
      charge for services;

    - [(k)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2011-291, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(l)]{.lawlabel} making a false or malicious statement or
      publication that injures the professional reputation, the
      prospects or surveying business of another member of the
      Association, Canada Lands Surveyor or permit holder;

    - [(m)]{.lawlabel} soliciting or accepting any work when the member
      of the Association, Canada Lands Surveyor or permit holder knows
      or has reason to believe that another member of the Association,
      Canada Lands Surveyor or permit holder is engaged for the same
      purpose by the same client;

    - [(n)]{.lawlabel} offering a remuneration or an inducement to
      secure employment or a contract for services;

    - [(o)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2011-291, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(p)]{.lawlabel} failing to cooperate with the Association with
      respect to a claim made under a professional liability insurance
      policy;

    - [(q)]{.lawlabel} failing to cooperate with the Association in a
      review of the surveying activities of a member of the Association;

    - [(q.1)]{.lawlabel} failing to attend or produce documents in
      accordance with a notice issued under subsection 49(3) or (5);

    - [(r)]{.lawlabel} authorizing a non-member to act in a manner that
      would lead the public to believe that the non-member was licensed
      in accordance with the Act and these Regulations;

    - [(s)]{.lawlabel} engaging in conduct relevant to surveying that,
      having regard to all the circumstances, would reasonably be
      regarded by members of the Association as dishonourable or
      unprofessional;

    - [(t)]{.lawlabel} failing to remedy poor, incorrect or incomplete
      work after acknowledging that a deficiency exists;

    - [(u)]{.lawlabel} failing, in respect of a permit holder, to
      immediately notify the Registrar when there is no longer a member
      of the Association available to supervise services offered by the
      permit holder which must be supervised by such a member;

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} failing, in respect of a permit holder, to ensure
      that work is carried out under the supervision of a member of the
      Association; and

    - [(w)]{.lawlabel} authorizing, permitting, counselling, abetting,
      assisting, aiding or acquiescing in any act or omission referred
      to in paragraphs (b) to (v). ([manquement
      professionnel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- SOR/2011-291, s. 1

## [Association of Canada Lands Surveyors Code of Ethics]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028218 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2003-1, s. 1(E)

\]
:::

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
code of ethics set out in section 3, [[members]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
means members of the Association, Canada Lands Surveyors, licence
holders and permit holders.

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Members
  shall abide by the code of ethics set out in subsections (2) to (7).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Members shall serve the public to the best of their
  knowledge and ability and with accuracy and efficiency for the
  development and peaceful enjoyment of Canada's lands and natural
  resources.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Members shall be honest and trustworthy and shall, in
  particular,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain confidentiality with respect to client or
    employer affairs during the term of their contract or employment and
    after their contract or employment has been terminated;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} certify only work that was performed by the member
    or under the member's supervision; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} enter into fee-splitting arrangements only with the
    knowledge and consent of their client.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Members shall be vigilant upholders of the law
  relating to their profession and shall refrain from, in particular,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} entering into an arrangement that would allow the
    unauthorized practice of their profession; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} knowingly or willingly becoming accessories to a
    failure to report any illegal practice of their profession to the
    Council of the Association.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Members shall avoid any appearance of professional
  impropriety and shall, in particular,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} declare to their clients or employers any conflict
    of interest that impairs the quality of their services;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensure, to the best of their ability that their
    names are not used in association with persons or enterprises of
    dubious or doubtful ethics; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} accept no compensation from more than one source
    for the same service without the consent of all parties involved.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Members shall charge and accept only fair and
  reasonable compensation for their services and shall, in particular,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make their claims for compensation commensurate
    with the professional and technical complexity of their services,
    their level of responsibility and their professional liability; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make details relevant to their claims for
    compensation available to their client on request.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Members shall maintain their competence, integrity
  and respect for their profession in their relations with colleagues,
  clients, employers or employees and the public and shall, in
  particular,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assume professional responsibility for all phases
    of survey work carried out under their supervision;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cultivate into their employees the utmost integrity
    and a clear understanding of the professional obligations of
    surveyors to the public;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2011-291, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} continually advance their skills and knowledge
    through study and educational programs;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} refrain from public criticism of the conduct or
    practice of colleagues;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} report any perceived professional misconduct or
    incompetence of members to the Registrar;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} keep adequate records of their work such that its
    quality can be judged by their peers;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} refrain from accepting assignments beyond their
    competence or beyond the resources available to them; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} avoid misleading and self-laudatory language in
    their advertising.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-291, s. 2

## [Committees of Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028251 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1028252 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise stated, a
quorum of a committee of the Association shall be a majority of its
members.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Members of committees shall
be appointed by the Council.

### [Executive Committee]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1028255 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Executive Committee of the Association shall be composed of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the President of the Association;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Vice-President of the Association, who shall
    preside in the President's absence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the past President of the Association, as defined
    in the by-laws; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} one or more members of the Council.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A quorum of the Executive Committee shall be three
  members of the committee, at least one of whom is the President or the
  Vice-President.

### [Board of Examiners]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1028263 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board of Examiners shall be composed of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a chairperson; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} four members of the Association.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A quorum of the Board of Examiners shall be three
  members of the Board.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the chairperson or a member is unable to perform
  the duties of the Board of Examiners, the Council shall appoint a
  replacement.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The chairperson and every member of the Board of
  Examiners shall, before entering office, take, before any person duly
  authorized to take oaths or affidavits, an oath (or solemn
  affirmation) in the following form:

  I, \...\...\...\...\...\....., do solemnly swear (or affirm) that I
  will faithfully discharge the duty of an examiner of candidates for a
  commission as a Canada Lands Surveyor according to law and without
  favour, affection or partiality. (*Add, in the case of an oath*: So
  help me God.)

### [Complaints Committee]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1028271 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Complaints Committee shall be composed of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least one elected member of the Council;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one member of the Council appointed to the Council
    by the Minister; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at least three members of the Association.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person who is a member of the Discipline Committee
  shall be a member of the Complaints Committee.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Council shall appoint one member of the
  Complaints Committee to be the chairperson.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A quorum of the Complaints Committee consists of
  three members of the Committee, one of whom is an elected member of
  the Council.

### [Discipline Committee]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1028282 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Discipline Committee shall be composed of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least one elected member of the Council;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one member of the Council appointed to the Council
    by the Minister; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at least three members of the Association who each
    have no less than ten years experience in surveying.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Council shall appoint one member of the
  Discipline Committee to be the chairperson.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A quorum of the Discipline Committee shall be the
  member who is appointed to the Council by the Minister and two other
  members of the Committee.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Discipline Committee commences a hearing and a
  member of the Committee becomes unable to act, the remaining members
  may complete the hearing in the absence of the member who is unable to
  act, provided a quorum is maintained.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a proceeding is commenced before the Discipline
  Committee and the term of office of a member of the Committee expires
  or is terminated, other than for professional misconduct or
  incompetence, before the proceeding is disposed of but after evidence
  has been heard, the member shall remain a member of the Committee for
  the purpose of completing the disposition of the proceeding in the
  same manner as if the term of office had not expired or been
  terminated.

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} proof of liability insurance coverage or proof of

## [Membership]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028325 .Part}

### [Applications]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1028326 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A person may apply to
become a member of the Association by submitting to the Registrar an
application on the form provided by the Association.

### [Annual Renewal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1028328 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for a renewal of membership shall be filed, on the form
  provided by the Association, with the Registrar before the end of the
  fiscal year of the Association.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall renew the membership if the
  application complies with the requirements of the Act and these
  Regulations.

### [Reinstatement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1028332 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person whose membership has lapsed or has been cancelled may apply for
  a new membership in accordance with section 11.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall grant the new membership if the
  application complies with the requirements of the Act and these
  Regulations.

## [Commissions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028336 .Part}

### [Application for Admission as a Candidate for a Commission]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1028337 .Subheading}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who wishes to be a candidate for a commission as a Canada Lands
  Surveyor and who is entitled to survey lands in a province under the
  laws of that province shall submit to the Board of Examiners

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application for admission as a candidate on the
    form provided by the Association;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any commission or licence authorizing the
    person to survey lands in the province; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} certification of the status of the commission or
    licence by the agency that issued it.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who wishes to be a candidate for a
  commission as a Canada Lands Surveyor and who is registered as a
  candidate to become entitled to survey lands in a province shall
  submit to the Board of Examiners

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application for admission as a candidate on the
    form provided by the Association;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a certification from the applicable provincial
    organization that the person is registered as a candidate to become
    commissioned or licensed to survey lands in the province; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} as soon as the person has obtained a provincial
    licence or commission, certification from the issuing agency that
    the licence or commission has been issued.

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every

  certified translator.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall be admitted as a candidate unless the

### [Application for Examination]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1028358 .Subheading}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  candidate for a commission shall submit notice of the candidate's
  intention to be examined to the Registrar at least two months before
  the date of commencement of the examinations as announced in
  accordance with section 20.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a candidate who submits
  notice of the candidate's intention to be examined after the deadline
  referred to in subsection (1) shall only be admitted on a first come,
  first served basis in accordance with the availability of places and
  copies of the examinations.

### [Examination]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1028401 .Subheading}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A candidate is required,
within a period of seven years after the date on which the candidate's
application is accepted, to pass all the examinations prescribed by the
Board of Examiners after an evaluation of the candidate's
qualifications, or to demonstrate to the Board of Examiners that the
candidate has received equivalent training.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-1, s. 5
:::

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 18, a
candidate may rewrite any examination that the candidate fails.

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Examinations shall be held at least
  once a year at times and places determined by the Board of Examiners.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The times and places referred to in subsection (1)
  shall be announced by the Board of Examiners at least six months
  before the date of commencement of the examinations.

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** If a candidate who has
been admitted to an examination is found to be ineligible to be
examined, the examination of that candidate is void.

### [Special Examiners]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1028410 .Subheading}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Council shall, on the recommendation of the Board of Examiners,
  appoint as special examiners persons who are particularly conversant
  with the specific subjects set out in section 17 for which they will
  prepare examinations and appraise responses of candidates thereto.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every special examiner shall, before entering office,
  take the oath or affirmation required of members of the Board of
  Examiners under subsection 7(4).

### [Granting of Commissions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1028414 .Subheading}

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  candidate shall, before the Board of Examiners recommends the granting
  of a commission,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} take, before any person duly authorized to take
    oaths or affidavits, an oath (or solemn affirmation) in the
    following form:

    I, \...\...\...\...\...\....., do solemnly swear (or affirm) that I
    will faithfully discharge the duties of a Canada Lands Surveyor
    according to law and without favour, affection or partiality. (*Add,
    in the case of an oath*: So help me God); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pay to the Registrar the fee, as set out in the
    by-laws, for a commission.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The oath or affirmation referred to in paragraph 1(a)
  shall be in writing and signed by the candidate.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every commission shall be registered in the office of
  the Association by the Registrar, and a copy of the commission shall
  be retained by the Registrar.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The oath or affirmation referred to in
  paragraph (1)(a) shall be sent by the candidate to the office of the
  Association and be filed by the Registrar.

## [Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028434 .Part}

### [Applications]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1028435 .Subheading}

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Submission of the following to the

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} proof of professional liability insurance coverage

  by-laws or whose licence has lapsed, is not required, in order to
  obtain a new licence, to submit the affidavit or affidavits referred
  to in paragraph (1)(b) if the revocation or lapse occurred less than
  five years before the day on which the new licence is applied for.

  professional misconduct or incompetence, their application for a new
  licence shall be made in the manner set out in paragraph (1)(a) and
  shall include the affidavit or affidavits, fees, levies and proof of
  insurance coverage or exemption set out in paragraphs (1)(b) to (e).

## [Review of Surveying Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028531 .Part}

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** A review of the surveying
activities of members of the Association to ensure the maintenance of
minimum standards of surveying shall consist of a review of survey
plans, documents and drawings pertaining to surveys made by those
members, and may include any of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a field inspection of the work depicted in the survey
  plans, documents and drawings;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an examination of files and other documents
  pertaining to the production of survey plans, documents and drawings
  of the members of the Association and permit holders; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a written report of each plan review, field
  inspection and examination conducted under this section.

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** The Council shall appoint
one or more Canada Lands Surveyors to review the surveying activities of
members of the Association.

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Reports of the results of reviews of
  the surveying activities of members of the Association shall be based
  on compliance with the requirements of these Regulations and the Act
  and any other Act governing surveying standards.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Reports of the results of reviews of the surveying
  activities of members of the Association shall be submitted to the
  Registrar.

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar shall
maintain a file for each member of the Association and shall keep in
that file all reports of reviews of the surveying activities pertaining
to the member, and notify the member of any change to the member's file.

## [Complaint and Discipline Processes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028541 .Part}

### [Complaint Process]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1028542 .Subheading}

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  request to the Association under paragraph 25(5)(a) of the Act shall
  be made to the Registrar.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When the Registrar receives a request under
  paragraph 25(5)(a) of the Act, the Registrar shall notify the Council
  and, if the Council agrees with the request, it shall appoint a person
  to swear a charge under oath or affirmation in the name of the
  Association and to file the charge with the Registrar.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a complainant personally swears a charge under
  oath or affirmation under paragraph 25(5)(b) of the Act, the charge
  shall be filed by the complainant with the Registrar.

### [Discipline Process and Conduct of Hearings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1028547 .Subheading}

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  the holding of a hearing by the Discipline Committee, the Registrar
  shall serve on the member of the Association, Canada Lands Surveyor or
  permit holder whose conduct is the subject of the hearing, and on the
  complainant, a notice of hearing, signed by the chairperson of the
  Committee, stating the date, time and place at which the Committee
  will hold the hearing together with a detailed description of the
  allegations in respect of which the hearing will be held and a
  statement of material facts in respect of each allegation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of hearing shall be served at least 30 days
  before the date of the hearing.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Discipline Committee, on receipt of proof of
  service of the notice of hearing on the member of the Association,
  Canada Lands Surveyor or permit holder, and on the complainant, may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} proceed with the hearing in the absence of any of
    those persons; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} act on the matter being heard in the same way that
    it would if any of those persons were present.

- SOR/2011-291, s. 12

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At a
  hearing before the Discipline Committee, the member of the
  Association, Canada Lands Surveyor or permit holder whose conduct is
  the subject of the hearing, the Association and the complainant are
  parties to the proceedings.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any party has the right to be represented by counsel.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A member of the Association, Canada Lands Surveyor or
  permit holder whose conduct is the subject of a hearing by the
  Discipline Committee shall be afforded an opportunity before the
  hearing to examine any written or documentary material filed with the
  Committee.

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** All hearings held by the
Discipline Committee shall be in private.

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member of the Discipline Committee holding a hearing shall not have
  taken part before the hearing in any investigation of the
  subject-matter of the hearing other than as a member of the Council
  considering the referral of the matter to the Committee.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member of the Discipline Committee shall not
  communicate directly or indirectly in relation to the subject-matter
  of the hearing with a party, a party's counsel or any other person who
  is not a member of the Committee, unless all parties are notified and
  given an opportunity to participate.

<!-- -->

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of a hearing, the Discipline Committee may seek the
  advice of persons having expertise in the field of surveying relevant
  to the subject-matter at issue in the hearing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Discipline Committee may seek legal advice from a
  qualified professional who is not counsel in the hearing and in such
  case the nature of the advice shall be made known to all parties in
  order that they may make submissions as to the law.

<!-- -->

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member of the Association, Canada Lands Surveyor or permit holder,
  whose conduct is the subject of a hearing, and any other person who,
  in the opinion of the Discipline Committee, has knowledge bearing on
  the subject matter, may be requested to attend as witnesses in the
  hearing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} At a hearing, a witness may be examined under oath or
  affirmation on all matters relevant to the hearing.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The attendance of a witness before the Discipline
  Committee is to be requested by a notice issued by the Registrar, or
  by the chairperson of the Committee, that states the date, time and
  place at which the witness is to attend.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A witness --- other than the member of the
  Association, Canada Lands Surveyor or permit holder whose conduct is
  the subject of the hearing --- who has been served with a notice is
  entitled to be paid the same fees that are payable to witnesses in an
  action before the Federal Court.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The production of documents is to be requested by a
  notice issued by the Registrar, or by the chairperson of the
  Committee, that identifies the documents that are to be produced and
  the date, time and place at which they are to be produced.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Discipline Committee may apply to a court of
  competent jurisdiction

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to compel the attendance of any witness who is
    requested to attend but fails to do so; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to compel the production of any documents that are
    requested and not produced.

### [Powers of the Discipline Committee]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1028586 .Subheading}

**[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 31(1)(e) of the Act, if the Discipline Committee finds a
member of the Association or permit holder guilty of professional
misconduct or incompetence, the Committee may impose any of the
following terms and conditions on the member's licence or permit
holder's permit:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} monitoring of the surveying activities of the member
  or permit holder;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} restriction of activities of the member or permit
  holder to a specified office, number of survey crews or area of
  expertise;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} requirement to have or acquire employees with
  specified expertise;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} requirement to have a qualified person to manage the
  financial aspects of the member's or permit holder's business; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the completion of specified education or training.
