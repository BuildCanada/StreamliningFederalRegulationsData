## [Prescribed Group of Consumers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-744959 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 489.3 and 607.2 of the [Insurance Companies
Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), the following group of consumers is prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any individual; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any entity, other than a financial institution, that
  is engaged in a business, that employs fewer than 500 employees and
  that has a gross annual revenue that does not exceed \$ 50 million.

the day on which it is registered.
