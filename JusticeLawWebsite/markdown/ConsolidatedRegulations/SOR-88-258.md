## Fish Toxicant Regulations {#fish-toxicant-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-258
:::

[FISHERIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-14)

Registration 1988-04-29

Regulations Respecting the Deposit of Deleterious Substances that are
Fish Toxicants in the Waters of Ontario, Quebec, Manitoba, Saskatchewan
and Alberta

P.C. 1988-818 1988-04-29
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to subsection 33(13) of
the Fisheries Act, is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations
respecting the deposit of deleterious substances that are fish toxicants
in the waters of Ontario, Quebec, Manitoba, Saskatchewan and
Alberta](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-258).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911516 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Fish Toxicant Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-258).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911518 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Fisheries
    Act](/eng/acts/F-14); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fish toxicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fish toxicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pest control product
    that conforms to the requirements of the [Pest Control Products
    Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01) and the [Pest Control Products
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1253) and that is used for
    the purpose of destroying any fish that is a pest as defined in
    section 2 of that Act. ([produit ichtyotoxique]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-137, s. 14(F)
:::

## [Substances Prescribed as Deleterious Substances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911524 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 36(4)(b) of the Act, fish toxicants are prescribed as
deleterious substances that are authorized to be deposited.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-137, s. 15
:::

## [Waters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911528 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 36(4)(b) of the Act, the waters where fish toxicants are
authorized to be deposited are the waters of the provinces of
Saskatchewan and Alberta and the non-tidal waters of the provinces of
Ontario, Quebec and Manitoba.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-137, s. 16
:::

## [Persons Authorized]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911532 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 36(4)(b) of the Act and subject to section 6, the following
persons are prescribed as persons who may, in the absence of any other
authority, authorize the deposit of any fish toxicant:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the Province of Ontario, the Minister
  of Natural Resources for that Province;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to Quebec, the Minister responsible in
  the Province of Quebec for the application of An Act respecting the
  conservation and development of wildlife, R.S.Q., c. C-61.1,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the Province of Manitoba, the Minister
  of Natural Resources for that Province;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the Province of Saskatchewan, the
  Minister of Parks, Recreation and Culture for that Province; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the Province of Alberta, the chief
  fishery officer as defined in the Alberta Fishery Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-137, s. 17
- SOR/2005-269, s. 53
- SOR/2008-99, s. 5
- SOR/2008-322, s. 35
:::

## [Conditions for Granting the Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911541 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 36(4)(b) of the Act, the prescribed conditions for granting
the authorization referred to in section 5 are the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister or the chief fishery officer is
  satisfied that the eradication of any fish that is a pest by the use
  of fish toxicants in any waters set out in section 4 and the
  subsequent restocking of those waters will enhance fishing in those
  waters; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the authorization is given in writing.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-137, s. 18
:::

## [Condition for Authorizing the Deposit of Fish Toxicants]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911547 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 36(4)(b) of the Act, the condition under which fish toxicants
are authorized to be deposited in the waters referred to in section 4 is
that the deposit does not adversely affect fish in the waters adjacent
to the waters where the deposit is made.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-137, s. 19
:::
