## Order Accepting the Recommendation of the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness Concerning the Two-year Review of the List of Entities {#order-accepting-the-recommendation-of-the-minister-of-public-safety-and-emergency-preparedness-concerning-the-two-year-review-of-the-list-of-entities .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2012-103
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 2012-12-20

Order Accepting the Recommendation of the Minister of Public Safety and
Emergency Preparedness Concerning the Two-year Review of the List of
Entities

P.C. 2012-1767 2012-12-17
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, on July 23, 2012, ten years had elapsed since the establishment
of a list by the [Regulations Establishing a List of
Entities](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-284)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_Draft_Two-YearReviewEn_hq_9904){#fn_Draft_Two-YearReviewEn_hq_9904-ID0EGACA}
pursuant to subsection 83.05(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_Draft_Two-YearReviewEn_hq_9905){#fn_Draft_Two-YearReviewEn_hq_9905-ID0EEACA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_Draft_Two-YearReviewEn_hq_9906){#fn_Draft_Two-YearReviewEn_hq_9906-ID0EBACA};

And whereas, pursuant to subsection 83.05(9)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_Draft_Two-YearReviewEn_hq_9907){#fn_Draft_Two-YearReviewEn_hq_9907-ID0EEABA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_Draft_Two-YearReviewEn_hq_9906){#fn_Draft_Two-YearReviewEn_hq_9906-ID0EBABA},
the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness has reviewed
that list, as it existed on July 23, 2012, and has determined that there
are still reasonable grounds to believe that each entity listed in the
attached schedule has knowingly carried out, attempted to carry out,
participated in or facilitated a terrorist activity or is knowingly
acting on behalf of, at the direction of or in association with such an
entity;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
Preparedness, accepts the recommendation of the Minister of Public
Safety and Emergency Preparedness made pursuant to subsection
83.05(9)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_Draft_Two-YearReviewEn_hq_9907){#fn_Draft_Two-YearReviewEn_hq_9907-ID0EEEAA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_Draft_Two-YearReviewEn_hq_9906){#fn_Draft_Two-YearReviewEn_hq_9906-ID0EBEAA}
that each entity listed in that schedule remain a listed entity.
