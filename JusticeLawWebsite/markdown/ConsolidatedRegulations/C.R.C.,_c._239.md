## P.E.I. Vegetable Seizure (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#p.e.i.-vegetable-seizure-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 239
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Regulations Respecting Seizures of Certain Vegetables Produced in Prince
Edward Island and Marketed in Interprovincial and Export Trade
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537328 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [P.E.I. Vegetable Seizure (Interprovincial and Export)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._239).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537330 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Prince Edward Island
    Vegetable Growers Cooperative Association and includes any
    sub-agents thereof; ([Agence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Prince Edward
    Island Vegetable Commodity Marketing Board; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any bag, box or other
    receptacle in which vegetable is or can be packed, transported or
    marketed; ([contenant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any individual, firm,
    partnership, association or corporation that grows or markets
    vegetable and includes any servants or agents thereof;
    ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vegetable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vegetable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any turnip produced in the
    Province of Prince Edward Island. ([légumes]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537337 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to vegetable when marketed in interprovincial and export trade and
to persons and property situated in the Province of Prince Edward
Island.

## [Stopping Vehicles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537339 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where it
  appears to a member or an authorized representative of the Commodity
  Board or a police constable that a vehicle is transporting vegetable
  in violation of any regulation or order of the Commodity Board, he may
  order the person in control of the vehicle to stop the vehicle.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an order is given pursuant to subsection (1),
  the person in control of the vehicle shall forthwith stop the vehicle
  and permit it to be searched by the person who gave the order.

## [Seizure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537343 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where, in the opinion of
any member or authorized representative of the Commodity Board, any
vegetable is being or has been marketed in violation of any regulation
or order of the Commodity Board, he may seize the vegetable and
container and remove them from the motor vehicle or the premises in
which they were seized and deliver them to the Commodity Board.

## [Detention]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537345 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  vegetable that is seized pursuant to section 5 may be detained by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} attaching a detention tag on the premises where the
    vegetable was seized; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} delivering a notice in writing of the seizure to
    any person who appears to be in control of the motor vehicle or in
    charge of the premises in which the vegetable was seized.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person other than a member or an authorized
  representative of the Commodity Board shall, without written authority
  from the Commodity Board, move, destroy, sell or offer for sale any
  vegetable in respect of which a detention tag has been attached or a
  notice of detention has been given pursuant to subsection (1).

## [Hearing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537351 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  vegetable that is seized pursuant to section 5 may be disposed of by
  the Commodity Board after the expiration of 10 days from the day of
  seizure unless before that time the owner of the vegetable or the
  person from whom it was seized applies in writing to the Commodity
  Board for a hearing to show cause why the vegetable should not be
  disposed of.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, at the conclusion of the hearing referred to
  in subsection (1), the Commodity Board determines that the vegetable
  seized was not marketed in violation of these Regulations or, if so
  marketed was marketed inadvertently, the Commodity Board may return
  the vegetable to its owner or the person from whom it was seized or
  market it through the Agency on behalf of the owner.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where, at the conclusion of the hearing referred to
  in subsection (1), the Commodity Board determines that the vegetable
  seized was being marketed in violation of these Regulations, the
  Commodity Board may dispose of the vegetable in the manner described
  in section 8.

## [Disposal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537356 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where any vegetable is
seized pursuant to section 5, the Commodity Board may order it to be
sold through the Agency on behalf of the owner and the Agency may deduct
from the proceeds of sale an amount, for the handling and marketing of
the vegetable, equal to \$1 per 100 pounds plus the cost of grading,
washing and cartage and any expenses of the Commodity Board arising out
of or relating to the seizure and sale of the vegetable, and the
balance, if any, shall be paid by the Agency to the owner or the person
from whom the vegetable was seized.

## [Appeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537358 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Any person aggrieved by any
act or decision of a member or authorized representative of the
Commodity Board or the Agency may appeal to the Commodity Board and, if
not satisfied with the decision of the Commodity Board, may appeal to
the Prince Edward Island Marketing Board.
