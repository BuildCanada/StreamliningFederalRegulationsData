## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-512322 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Board of Steamship Inspection Scale of
Fees](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1405).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-512324 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Shipping
    Act](/eng/acts/S-9); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Administration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Administration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a ship's
    equipment, means the Government of the State whose flag the ship is
    entitled to fly; ([Administration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any certificate issued
    pursuant to section 318 or 319 of the
    Act; ([certificat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[first inspection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[first inspection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes all inspections of
    a ship made by an inspector prior to the issuance of the first
    certificate in respect of that ship; ([première
    inspection]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a steamship inspector
    appointed under the Act; ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a ship that is registered under
      the Act or required by the Act to be registered,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the distance from the fore part of the
        uppermost end of the stem to the aft side of the head of the
        stern post, except that if a stern post is not fitted to the
        ship the measurement shall be taken to the foreside of the head
        of the rudder stock,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the ship has no rudder stock or has a
        rudder stock situated outside of the hull at the stern, the
        distance from the foreside of the foremost permanent structure
        to the aft side of the aftermost permanent structure of the ship
        not including guards or rubbing strakes, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the ship is double-ended, the distance
        from the aft side of the forward rudder stock to the foreside of
        the after rudder stock, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a ship other than a ship described
      in paragraph (a), the horizontal distance measured between
      perpendiculars erected at the extreme ends of the outside of the
      hull; ([longueur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-Canadian ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-Canadian ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a ship registered
    elsewhere than in Canada; ([navire non canadien]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Safety Convention Certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Safety Convention Certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    certificate issued under the provisions of the International
    Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea, 1974. ([certificat selon
    la Convention de sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-267, s. 11
- SOR/95-372, s. 9
- SOR/2000-341, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} apply in respect of non-Canadian ships.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-512349 .Part}

### [Payment of Fees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-512350 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The fees set out in these
Regulations in respect of services performed by the Marine Safety Branch
of the Department of Transport under Part V or Part XV of the Act or
regulations made pursuant to those Parts shall be paid to any officer of
that Branch.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-267, s. 11
- SOR/95-372, s. 9
- SOR/97-486, s. 2
:::

### [Proportionate Fees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-512354 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** When a ship is inspected at
any time within a period of 12 months after the date of the last annual
inspection, a new certificate valid for 12 months may be issued upon the
payment by the owner of the ship of one-twelfth of the appropriate
annual fee for that ship, as specified in section 11 or 12, for each
month or part thereof that has elapsed since the date of the last annual
inspection.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/88-630, s.
1\]]{.Repealed}

### [Extension]{.HTitleText2} {#h-512357 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A fee of \$100 is payable
for the extension of a short-term certificate or a letter of compliance,
other than a letter of compliance referred to in column I of item 7 or 8
of the table to section 30, in relation to an inspection referred to in
any of sections 10 to 14, 24, 30, 31 and 34.

- SI/82-62, s. 1

- SOR/85-1024, s. 1
- SOR/94-338, s. 1
- SOR/95-267, s. 2
- SOR/97-486, s. 3
:::

### [Inspections Outside Canada]{.HTitleText2} {#h-512361 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** In addition to any expenses
payable under section 313 of the Act, the fee payable for inspection of
a ship outside Canada is an amount equal to the total of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the appropriate fee for each survey, inspection or
  service referred to in sections 10 to 14, 24, 25, 28 to 31 and 34; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$400 for each day, or part thereof, that an
  inspector is outside Canada to make the inspection.

### [First Inspection]{.HTitleText2} {#h-512370 .Subheading}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  fee payable for the first inspection of a ship of less than 25 tons,
  gross tonnage, is an amount equal to \$286 plus:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a passenger ship having a gross tonnage set out
    in column I of an item of Table I to section 11, twice the fee set
    out in column II of that item;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a non-passenger ship, other than a ship
    referred to in paragraph (c), having a gross tonnage set out in
    column I of an item of Table II to section 11, twice the fee set out
    in column II of the item; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for an unmanned, non-self-propelled ship, including
    deck cargo barges and barges that carry oil in bulk, having a gross
    tonnage set out in column I of an item of Table II to section 11,
    the fee set out in column II of the item.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fee payable for the first inspection of a ship of
  25 tons, gross tonnage, or more, is an amount equal to \$1,152 plus:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a passenger ship having a gross tonnage set out
    in column I of an item of Table I to section 11, twice the fee set
    out in column II of that item;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a non-passenger ship, other than a ship
    referred to in paragraph (c), having a gross tonnage set out in
    column I of an item of Table II to section 11, twice the fee set out
    in column II of the item; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for an unmanned, non-self-propelled ship, including
    deck cargo barges and barges that carry oil in bulk, having a gross
    tonnage set out in column I of an item of Table II to section 11,
    the fee set out in column II of the item.

### [Re-inspection]{.HTitleText2} {#h-512382 .Subheading}

- **[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The fee payable for the
  re-inspection of a ship of less than 25 tons gross tonnage that was
  registered in Canada, then registered elsewhere than in Canada and is
  then again registered in Canada, is an amount equal to \$286 plus:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a passenger ship having a gross tonnage set out
    in column I of an item of Table I to section 11, one and one-half
    times the fee set out in column II of the item;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a non-passenger ship, other than a ship
    referred to in paragraph (c), having a gross tonnage set out in
    column I of an item of Table II to section 11, one and one-half
    times the fee set out in column II of the item; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for an unmanned, non-self-propelled ship, including
    deck cargo barges and barges that carry oil in bulk, having a gross
    tonnage set out in column I of an item of Table II to section 11,
    three quarters of the fee set out in column II of the item.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fee payable for the re-inspection of a ship of 25
  tons gross tonnage or more that was registered in Canada, then
  registered elsewhere than in Canada and is then again registered in
  Canada, is an amount equal to \$1,152 plus:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a passenger ship having a gross tonnage set out
    in column I of an item of Table I to section 11, one and one-half
    times the fee set out in column II of that item;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a non-passenger ship, other than a ship
    referred to in paragraph (c), having a gross tonnage set out in
    column I of an item of Table II to section 11, one and one-half
    times the fee set out in column II of the item; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for an unmanned, non-self-propelled ship, including
    deck cargo barges and barges that carry oil in bulk, having a gross
    tonnage set out in column I of an item of Table II to section 11,
    three quarters of the fee set out in column II of the item.

- SOR/97-486, s. 7
