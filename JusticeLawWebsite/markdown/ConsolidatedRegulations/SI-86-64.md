## Designating the Civil Aviation Tribunal as a Department; the Minister of Transport as Appropriate Minister for Purposes of the Financial Administration Act and as Minister for Purposes of Section 31 of the Aeronautics Act {#designating-the-civil-aviation-tribunal-as-a-department-the-minister-of-transport-as-appropriate-minister-for-purposes-of-the-financial-administration-act-and-as-minister-for-purposes-of-section-31-of-the-aeronautics-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/86-64
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1986-05-14

Designating the Civil Aviation Tribunal as a Department; the Minister of
Transport as Appropriate Minister for Purposes of the Financial
Administration Act and as Minister for Purposes of Section 31 of the
Aeronautics Act

P.C. 1986-1068 1986-05-01
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition of
  [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to designate the Civil Aviation
  Tribunal as a department for the purposes of that Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition of
  [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to designate the
  Minister of Transport as the appropriate Minister with respect to the
  Civil Aviation Tribunal for the purposes of that Act; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to section 31[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EDBAA} of
  the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), to designate the Minister of
  Transport, a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the
  Minister for the purposes of that section.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote

    1985, c. 28, s. 5
