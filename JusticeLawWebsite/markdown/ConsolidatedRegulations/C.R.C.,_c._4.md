## Air Cushion Vehicle Regulations {#air-cushion-vehicle-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 4
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Regulations Respecting Air Cushion Vehicles
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-555298 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Air Cushion Vehicle
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._4).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-555300 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations, [[air
cushion vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a machine designed to derive
support in the atmosphere primarily from reactions against the earth's
surface of air expelled from the machine.

## [Exclusion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-555302 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Commercial air services
provided by means of air cushion vehicles are excluded from the
operation of Part II of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).
