## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-742886 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Lobbying
    Act](/eng/acts/L-12.4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commissioner of
    Lobbying appointed under subsection 4.1(1) of the Act.
    ([commissaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[consultant lobbyist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[consultant lobbyist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who
    is required to file a return under subsection 5(1) of the Act.
    ([lobbyiste-conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[in-house lobbyist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[in-house lobbyist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee of a
    corporation or organization who is named in the return filed under
    subsection 7(1) of the Act. ([lobbyiste salarié]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[member of a transition team]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[member of a transition team]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person
    referred to in subsection 2(3) of the Act. ([membre d'une équipe de
    transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Filing of Returns]{.HTitleText1} {#h-742893 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a return shall be filed electronically.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A return may be filed in paper format if the person
  filing the return

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has difficulty filing the return electronically
    because of a disability; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not have access to a computer system that
    allows them to file it electronically.

<!-- -->

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A return
  referred to in section 5 of the Act that is filed in paper format
  shall be in Form 1 of the schedule.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A return referred to in section 7 of the Act that is
  filed in paper format on behalf of a corporation shall be in Form 2 of
  the schedule.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A return referred to in section 7 of the Act that is
  filed in paper format on behalf of an organization shall be in Form 3
  of the schedule.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A return
  that is filed in paper format must be delivered to the Commissioner's
  office by mail, by facsimile transmission or by hand.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A return is deemed to have been received

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the date of receipt stamped on the document by
    the Commissioner's office, if the return is delivered by mail or by
    hand;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the date indicated by the receiving apparatus,
    if the return is sent by facsimile transmission; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the date that it enters the registry of the
    Commissioner's office, if the return is filed electronically.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A facsimile copy of the return is deemed to be the
  original.

## [Registration of Consultant Lobbyists]{.HTitleText1} {#h-742912 .Part}

### [Initial Return]{.HTitleText2} {#h-742913 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A consultant lobbyist
shall, with respect to a return filed under subsection 5(1) of the Act,
include, in addition to the information required under subsection 5(2)
of the Act, the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the client is a corporation, whether the
  consultant lobbyist is a member of the board of directors of the
  corporation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the client is an organization, whether the
  consultant lobbyist is a member of, or a member of the board of
  directors of, the organization;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the consultant lobbyist ceases to hold a
  designated public office on or after July 2, 2008 and they file the
  return less than five years after ceasing to hold the designated
  public office, whether they are subject to the five-year prohibition
  on lobbying set out in section 10.11 of the Act and, if not, the
  reason that they are not subject to it; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the consultant lobbyist ceased to be a transition
  team member on or after January 24, 2006 and they file the return less
  than five years after ceasing to carry out those functions, whether
  they are subject to the five-year prohibition on lobbying set out in
  section 10.11 of the Act and, if not, the reason that they are not
  subject to it; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name and title of the client's principal
  representative with whom the consultant lobbyist deals.

### [Monthly Return]{.HTitleText2} {#h-742920 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 5(3)(a) of the Act, the following types of communication are
prescribed if made orally and arranged in advance of the communication:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a communication referred to in any of subparagraphs
  5(1)(a)(i) to (vi) of the Act that is initiated by a person other than
  a public office holder; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a communication referred to in either subparagraph
  5(1)(a)(v) or (vi) that is initiated by a public office holder.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The consultant lobbyist
shall, with respect to a return filed under subsection 5(3) of the Act,
include, in addition to the information required under paragraph 5(3)(a)
of the Act, the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the position title of the designated public office
  holder; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the branch or unit and the name of the
  department or other governmental institution in which the designated
  public office holder is employed or serves at the time of the
  communication.

## [Registration of In-house Lobbyists (Corporations and Organizations)]{.HTitleText1} {#h-742927 .Part}

### [Initial Return]{.HTitleText2} {#h-742928 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The officer responsible
shall, with respect to a return filed under subsection 7(1) of the Act,
include, in addition to the information required under subsection 7(3)
of the Act, the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if an in-house lobbyist ceases to hold a designated
  public office on or after July 2, 2008 but less than five years before
  the return is filed, whether they are subject to the five-year
  prohibition on lobbying under section 10.11 of the Act and, if not,
  the reason why they are not subject to it; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if an in-house lobbyist ceases to be a transition
  team member on or after January 24, 2006 but less than five years
  before the return is filed, whether they are subject to the five-year
  prohibition on lobbying under section 10.11 of the Act and, if not,
  the reason why they are not subject to it.

### [Monthly Return]{.HTitleText2} {#h-742932 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 7(4) of the Act, the following types of communication are
prescribed if made orally and arranged in advance of the communication:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a communication referred to in any of subparagraphs
  7(1)(a)(i) to (v) of the Act that is initiated by a person other than
  a public office holder; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a communication referred to in subparagraph
  7(1)(a)(v) of the Act that is initiated by a public office holder.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The officer responsible
for filing shall, with respect to a return filed under subsection 7(4)
of the Act, include, in addition to the information required under
paragraph 7(4)(a) of the Act, the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the position title of the designated public office
  holder; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the branch or unit and the name of the
  department or other governmental institution in which the designated
  public office holder is employed or serves at the time of the
  communication.

## [Clarification or Correction of Returns]{.HTitleText1} {#h-742939 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  clarification to a return requested by the Commissioner under
  subsection 9(3.1) of the Act shall be provided by the individual who
  submitted the return, not later than 30 days after the day on which
  the request is made.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any correction to a return requested by the
  Commissioner under subsection 9(3.1) of the Act shall be provided by
  the individual who submitted the return, not later than 10 days after
  the day on which the request is made.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A request made by the Commissioner is deemed to have
  been made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the date that it is post marked, if the request
    is mailed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the date that it is delivered, if the request is
    delivered by hand;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the date indicated by the receiving apparatus,
    if the request is sent by facsimile transmission; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} on the date of transmission, if the request is sent
    by electronic mail.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A clarification or correction to a return is deemed
  to have been received

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the date of receipt stamped on the document by
    the Commissioner's office, if the clarification or correction to the
    return is delivered by mail or by hand;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the date indicated by the receiving apparatus,
    if the clarification or correction to the return is sent by
    facsimile transmission; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the date of transmission, if the clarification
    or correction to the return is sent by electronic mail.
