the Minister of State styled Minister of Human Resources and Skills

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716609 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Education Savings
    Act](/eng/acts/C-3.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[assisted contribution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assisted contribution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a contribution
    made to an RESP in respect of which a CES grant has been paid.
    ([cotisation subventionnée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[brother]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[brother]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in relation to a beneficiary,
    includes a son of the common-law partner or spouse of a parent of
    the beneficiary. ([frère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[CLB]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CLB]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Canada Learning Bond. ([Version
    anglaise seulement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[CLB account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CLB account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an account that holds the
    CLB paid into an RESP in respect of a beneficiary. ([compte du bon
    d'études]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in subsection 248(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).
    ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[EAP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[EAP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an educational assistance payment.
    ([PAE]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[grant account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[grant account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an account that holds
    all CES grants paid into an RESP. ([compte de
    subvention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[RESP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[RESP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registered education savings
    plan. ([REEE]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in relation to a beneficiary,
    includes a daughter of the common-law partner or spouse of a parent
    of the beneficiary. ([sœur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[trustee agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[trustee agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement entered
    into by the Minister and a trustee under an RESP that relates to the
    payment of a CES grant or CLB to the RESP. ([convention de
    fiducie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Ordering of Withdrawals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716622 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** If both assisted and
unassisted contributions have been made to an RESP, withdrawals of
contributions from the RESP are considered to be made in the order
specified below:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} assisted contributions are considered to be withdrawn
  before unassisted contributions; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} unassisted contributions made after 1997 are
  considered to be withdrawn before unassisted contributions made before
  1998.

## [Manner of Determining Amount of CES Grants]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716626 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** When a contribution has
been made in a particular year and a CES grant in respect of the
contribution would otherwise be payable at a time in a year following
the particular year, the CES grant shall be reduced by the total of all
amounts each of which is, in respect of another CES grant paid after the
particular year and before that time, the amount by which the other CES
grant would have been reduced had the CES grant been paid in the
particular year.

## [Conditions for Payment of CES Grants]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716628 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may pay a CES grant in respect of a contribution that has
  been made to an RESP and has not been withdrawn if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the trustee enters into a trustee agreement with
    the Minister that applies to the RESP and includes the terms and
    conditions set out in section 8;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the trustee submits, at the request of a subscriber
    under the RESP, an application for the CES grant

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a contribution made after 2004, within three
      years after the date of the contribution, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a contribution made before 2005, no later
      than December 31, 2007;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the beneficiary, in the year in
    which the contribution is made,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} attains 16 or 17 years of age, a minimum of
      \$2,000 of contributions has been made to, and not withdrawn from,
      RESPs in respect of the beneficiary before the year in which the
      beneficiary attains 16 years of age,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} attains 16 or 17 years of age, a minimum of
      \$100 of annual contributions has been made to, and not withdrawn
      from, RESPs in respect of the beneficiary in at least any four
      years before the year in which the beneficiary attains 16 years of
      age,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} attains 16 or 17 years of age and the year is
      1998, the beneficiary was a beneficiary under an RESP in at least
      four years before 1998, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} attains 17 years of age and the year is 1999,
      the beneficiary was a beneficiary under an RESP in at least four
      years before 1998;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total of the contribution and all other
    contributions to RESPs made, or deemed to have been made for the
    purpose of Part X.4 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), in
    respect of the beneficiary does not exceed the RESP lifetime limit
    (as defined in subsection 204.9(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3)) for the year in which the contribution is
    made;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the contribution is made after
    1999 to an RESP that was entered into before 1999, the RESP complies
    at the time of the contribution with the conditions for registration
    set out in subsection 146.1(2) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) that apply in respect of education savings
    plans entered into on January 1, 1999;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the beneficiary is not an ineligible beneficiary;
    and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the trustee complies with the terms and conditions
    of these Regulations and the trustee agreement that applies to the
    RESP.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the Minister may pay an
  amount under subsection 5(4) of the Act if the RESP has only one
  beneficiary or, if there is more than one, every beneficiary is a
  brother or sister of every other beneficiary.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If assisted contributions are withdrawn from an RESP
  after March 22, 2004, the Minister shall not pay the amount under
  subsection 5(4) of the Act in respect of an individual who was a
  beneficiary under the RESP at the time of the withdrawal throughout
  the period that begins on the day of the withdrawal and ends on the
  last day of the second year following the year in which the withdrawal
  is made unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the withdrawal is made at a time when at least one
    beneficiary under the RESP is eligible to receive an EAP under the
    RESP;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the withdrawal is an eligible transfer; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the withdrawal is all or part of an excess amount
    of contributions to reduce the amount of tax, otherwise payable
    under Part X.4 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), and, at the
    time of the withdrawal, the excess amount for the year is not
    greater than \$4,000.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} At the time a CES grant is paid to an RESP, the
  trustee shall credit the grant account of the RESP with the amount of
  the CES grant paid.

## [Ineligible Beneficiaries (CES Grants)]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716652 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 5(3)(b) of the Act and paragraph 4(1)(f) of
  these Regulations, if contributions that were made to an RESP before
  1998 are withdrawn from the RESP after February 23, 1998, any
  individual who is a beneficiary of the RESP at any time during the
  period beginning on February 24, 1998 and ending on the day of the
  withdrawal is an ineligible beneficiary throughout the period that
  begins on the day of the withdrawal and ends on the last day of the
  second year following the year in which the withdrawal is made.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total of all withdrawals made under the RESP
    for the year does not exceed \$200;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the withdrawal is made at a time when at least one
    beneficiary under the RESP is eligible to receive an EAP under the
    RESP;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the withdrawal is an eligible transfer; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the withdrawal is all or part of an excess amount
    of contributions to reduce the amount of tax, otherwise payable
    under Part X.4 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), and, at the
    time of the withdrawal, the excess amount for the year is not
    greater than \$4,000.

## [Conditions for Payment of CLB]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716660 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may pay a CLB if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the trustee enters into a trustee agreement with
    the Minister that applies to the RESP and includes the terms and
    conditions set out in section 8;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application for the CLB is made by the trustee,
    at the request of a subscriber under the RESP;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the RESP complies at the time of the CLB payment
    with the conditions for registration set out in subsection 146.1(2)
    of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) that apply in respect of
    education savings plans entered into on January 1, 1999;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the RESP has only one beneficiary or, if there is
    more than one, every beneficiary is a brother or sister of every
    other beneficiary;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the RESP has a CLB account for each beneficiary in
    respect of whom a CLB is paid; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the trustee complies with the terms and conditions
    of these Regulations and the trustee agreement that applies to the
    RESP.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the time a CLB is paid to an RESP, the trustee
  shall credit the beneficiary's CLB account within the RESP with the
  amount of the CLB paid.

## [Waiver --- Undue Hardship]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716672 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, for the
purposes of section 9.1 of the Act, waive the requirement

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to designate a trust under subsection 5(7) of the
  Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to apply for a CLB before the beneficiary reaches 21
  years of age under subsection 6(1) of the Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to designate a trust under subsection 6(4) of the
  Act;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to provide a Social Insurance Number or business
  number under paragraph 7(b) of the Act; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} to apply for CES grants within the time limits set
  out in paragraph 4(1)(b).

## [Terms and Conditions of Trustee Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716679 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Every trustee agreement
shall include the following terms and conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the trustee shall provide the Minister with any
  information that the Minister requires for the purposes of the Act and
  these Regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the trustee shall maintain records and books of
  account that relate to the amounts paid under the Act in such form and
  containing such information as the Minister requires to enable the
  Minister to determine whether the amounts will be paid or are required
  to be repaid;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the trustee shall allow the Minister access to all
  documents and other information that the Minister requires for
  auditing payments or repayments made under the Act or these
  Regulations;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the trustee shall report to the Minister

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all contributions and transfers to, and all
    withdrawals and transfers from, an RESP that are made after 1997,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the portion of EAPs made from the RESP that is
    attributable to amounts paid under the Act, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any other information related to the RESP that is
    specified in the trustee agreement;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the reporting referred to in paragraph (d) shall be
  done annually or within any shorter period that is set out in the
  trustee agreement;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the trustee shall submit all information to the
  Minister in a format and manner that is acceptable to the Minister;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the trustee may make a distribution from an RESP only
  if, at the time immediately after the distribution, the fair market
  value of the property held in connection with the RESP would not be
  less than the total of the balance in the grant account and all CLB
  accounts of the RESP, unless the distribution is an EAP made to a
  beneficiary of the RESP and all of the EAP is attributable to CES
  grants and to a CLB;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the trustee shall repay any amount required to be
  repaid to the Minister under these Regulations, other than the amounts
  payable by a beneficiary under subsection 12(2) and section 13; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the trustee shall not charge fees related to the RESP
  against the balance of the grant account or the CLB account of any
  beneficiary of the RESP.

## [Terms and Conditions of Agreements with Promoters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716693 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Every agreement between the
Minister and the promoter of an RESP shall include the following terms
and conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the promoter shall provide the trustee with any
  information that the Minister requires for the purposes of the Act and
  these Regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the promoter shall report to the Minister any
  information related to the RESP that is specified in the agreement;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the promoter shall submit all information to the
  Minister in a format and manner that is acceptable to the Minister;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the promoter shall allow the Minister access to all
  documents and other information related to RESPs that the Minister
  requires for audit purposes; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the promoter shall not charge fees related to the
  RESP against the balance of the grant account or the CLB account of
  any beneficiary in the RESP.

## [Apportionment of EAP]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716700 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the
  portion of an EAP made to a beneficiary under an RESP that is
  attributable to a CLB is the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#1201200 .Subsection}
  A × B/C

  where:

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the EAP,

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the balance in the beneficiary's CLB account immediately before
      the payment of the EAP, and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the total amount available to the beneficiary for an EAP.
  :::

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (3) and (4), the portion of an
  EAP made to a beneficiary under an RESP that is attributable to CES
  grants is the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

    ::: {#1201207 .Paragraph}
    A × D/C

    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount of the EAP,

    [D]{.dfn}
    :   is the balance in the grant account of the RESP immediately
        before the payment of the EAP, and

    [C]{.dfn}
    :   is the total amount available to the beneficiary for an EAP, and
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which \$7,200 is greater than the
    aggregate of all amounts determined under this subsection in respect
    of an EAP previously made by the promoter to the beneficiary.

- [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} The portion of an EAP made to a beneficiary under
  an RESP that is attributable to accumulated income is the amount
  determined by the formula

  ::: {#1201214 .Subsection}
  A × E/C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the EAP,

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the accumulated income in the RESP immediately
      before the payment of the EAP, and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the total amount available to the beneficiary for an EAP.
  :::

- [(2.2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (1) to (2.1), the
  total amount available to the beneficiary for the EAP is the sum of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the accumulated income,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amounts in the RESP paid by a designated
    provincial program and eligible to be paid in the EAP under the laws
    applicable to the designated provincial program,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the beneficiary is resident in Canada at the
    time the EAP is made, the balance in the beneficiary's CLB account,
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the aggregate of all amounts determined under
    subsection (2) in respect of an EAP previously made by the promoter
    to the beneficiary is less than \$7,200 and, if the beneficiary is
    resident in Canada at the time the EAP is made, the balance in the
    grant account of the RESP.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The portion of an EAP that is attributable to CES
  grants or a CLB is nil where the beneficiary is not resident in Canada
  at the time the EAP is made.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The portion of an EAP made to a beneficiary under an
  RESP that allows more than one beneficiary at any one time that is
  attributable to CES grants is nil where the beneficiary became a
  beneficiary under the RESP after attaining 21 years of age, unless,
  before attaining 21 years of age, the beneficiary had been a
  beneficiary under another RESP that allows more than one beneficiary
  at any one time.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a CES grant or a CLB is paid as a portion of an
  EAP to a beneficiary under an RESP, the trustee shall debit the grant
  account or the CLB account of the RESP, as the case may be, at the
  time of the payment with the amount of CES grant or CLB paid.

## [Repayments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716743 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), if
  assisted contributions are withdrawn from an RESP, other than by way
  of transfer to another RESP, when no beneficiary under the RESP is
  eligible to receive an EAP, the trustee under the RESP shall, within
  the period set out in the trustee agreement that applies to the RESP,
  repay to the Minister an amount equal to the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

    ::: {#716747 .Paragraph}
    A/B × C

    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is the balance in the grant account of the RESP immediately
        before the withdrawal,

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is the balance of the total assisted contributions in the RESP
        immediately before the withdrawal, and

    [C]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount of assisted contributions withdrawn, and
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the balance in the grant account of the RESP
    immediately before the withdrawal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A trustee under an RESP is not required to repay any
  amount of a CES grant paid in respect of a beneficiary if there is a
  withdrawal of contributions and the withdrawal is all or part of an
  excess amount of contributions to reduce the amount of tax payable
  under Part X.4 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), and, at the
  time of the withdrawal, the excess amount for the year is not greater
  than \$4,000.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A trustee under an RESP shall repay to the Minister,
  within the period set out in the trustee agreement that applies to the
  RESP, an amount referred to in subsection (4), if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the RESP is terminated;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the registration of the RESP is revoked;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a payment described in paragraph (b) or (d) of the
    definition [[trust]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 146.1(1) of
    the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) is made under the RESP;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an EAP is made under the RESP to an individual who
    is not a beneficiary under the RESP;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} property is transferred from the RESP to another
    RESP except where the transfer is an eligible transfer; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an individual becomes a beneficiary under the RESP
    in place of another beneficiary, except where paragraph 204.9(4)(b)
    of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) applies in respect of the
    replacement.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The amount that must be repaid as the result of an
  occurrence of an event described in subsection (3) is the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total of the balance in the grant account and
    all of the CLB accounts of the RESP immediately before the time of
    the occurrence, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

    ::: {#1201226 .Paragraph}
    (C × Y)/(Y + G)

    where

    [C]{.dfn}
    :   is the fair market value of the property held in the RESP,
        determined immediately before the time of the occurrence,

    [Y]{.dfn}
    :   is the total balance in the grant account and all of the CLB
        accounts of the RESP immediately before the time of the
        occurrence, and

    [G]{.dfn}
    :   is the total balance in the RESP immediately before the time of
        the occurrence, of the amounts that were paid into the RESP
        under a designated provincial program.
    :::

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If an amount has been paid into an RESP under
  subsection 5(4) of the Act and an individual, who is not a brother or
  sister of all of the other beneficiaries under the RESP, becomes a
  beneficiary under the RESP, the trustee shall repay to the Minister,
  within the period set out in the trustee agreement that applies to the
  RESP, the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the balance of the grant account of the RESP
    immediately before the individual becomes a beneficiary, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fair market value of the property held in
    connection with the RESP immediately before the individual becomes a
    beneficiary.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a beneficiary in respect of whom a CLB was paid
  into an RESP ceases to be a beneficiary under the RESP, the trustee
  shall repay to the Minister, within the period set out in the trustee
  agreement that applies to the RESP, the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the balance of the beneficiary's CLB account
    immediately before the beneficiary ceases to be a beneficiary, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fair market value, immediately before the
    beneficiary ceases to be a beneficiary, of the property held in
    connection with the RESP less the total of the balances of the CLB
    accounts in the RESP for all other beneficiaries.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} If a CLB has been paid into an RESP and an
  individual, who is not a brother or sister of every other beneficiary
  under the RESP, becomes a beneficiary under the RESP, the trustee
  shall repay to the Minister, within the period set out in the trustee
  agreement that applies to the RESP the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total of the balances of the CLB accounts in
    the RESP immediately before the individual becomes a beneficiary,
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fair market value, immediately before the
    individual becomes a beneficiary, of the property held in connection
    with the RESP.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} If an amount of a CLB or CES grant is repaid to the
  Minister, the trustee shall debit that amount from the CLB account or
  grant account, as the case may be, at the time of the repayment.

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  trustee of an RESP shall, within the period set out in the trustee
  agreement that applies to the RESP, repay to the Minister any portion
  of an amount paid as a CES grant or CLB to which the trustee was not
  entitled under the Act or these Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A beneficiary of an RESP shall repay to the Minister
  any portion of an EAP attributable to a CES grant or CLB to which the
  beneficiary was not entitled under the Act or these Regulations.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** If the aggregate of all
amounts that a beneficiary has received as an EAP that is attributable
to CES grants exceeds \$7,200, the beneficiary shall repay the excess to
the Minister.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Any amount of CLB repaid
to the Minister under section 11 may be paid into an RESP in respect of
the same beneficiary if the conditions of payment of a CLB are otherwise
met.

## [Additional Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716779 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 6(5) of the Act, the Minister may pay \$25 into the trust
when the Minister pays the amount under paragraph 6(2)(a) of the Act.

## [Eligible Transfers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716781 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of these Regulations, the transfer of an amount, other
  than an amount in a CLB account, from an RESP to another RESP is an
  eligible transfer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any beneficiary under the receiving RESP is,
      immediately before the transfer, a beneficiary under the
      transferring RESP, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a parent of a beneficiary under the receiving
      RESP was a parent of an individual who was, immediately before the
      transfer, a beneficiary under the transferring RESP and

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the receiving RESP is an RESP that allows more
        than one beneficiary at any one time, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the beneficiary under the
        receiving RESP had not attained 21 years of age at the time the
        receiving RESP was entered into;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if, at the time of the transfer,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the receiving RESP has only one beneficiary or,
      if there is more than one, every beneficiary is a brother or
      sister of every other beneficiary, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} no payments have been made into the transferring
      RESP under subsection 5(4) of the Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the receiving RESP complies with the conditions
    for registration set out in subsection 146.1(2) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) that apply in respect of education savings
    plans entered into on January 1, 1999.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If less than all of the property, other than the
  property in a CLB account or any amount paid under a designated
  provincial program, held in connection with an RESP is transferred to
  another RESP, the assisted contributions, unassisted contributions,
  CES grants and accumulated income are considered to be transferred in
  the same proportion of their total balances as the value of the
  property transferred is to the total value of the property, other than
  the value of the property in a CLB account or any amount paid under a
  designated provincial program, in the RESP at the time of the
  transfer.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If property held in connection with an RESP, other
  than the property in a CLB account, is transferred to another RESP,
  the amount of CES grant that is transferred or considered to be
  transferred under subsection (2) is, at the time of the transfer,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} debited from the grant account of the transferring
    RESP; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} credited to the grant account of the receiving
    RESP.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the CES grant that is transferred or is
  considered to be transferred under subsection (2) is considered to
  have been paid to the trustee under the receiving RESP.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The assisted contributions or unassisted
  contributions that are transferred or are considered to be transferred
  under subsection (2) are considered to have been made to the receiving
  RESP.

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  transfer of an amount in a CLB account of an RESP to the CLB account
  of another RESP is an eligible transfer if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} both CLB accounts are in respect of the same
    beneficiary;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the receiving RESP complies with the conditions for
    registration set out in subsection 146.1(2) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) that apply in respect of education savings
    plans entered into on January 1, 1999; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at the time of the transfer, the receiving RESP has
    only one beneficiary or, where there is more than one, every
    beneficiary is a brother or sister of every other beneficiary.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an amount held in connection with a CLB account is
  transferred to another RESP, the amount that has been transferred is,
  at the time of the transfer,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} debited from the CLB account of the transferring
    RESP; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} credited to the CLB account of the receiving RESP.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amount of a CLB that is transferred from an RESP
  is considered to have been paid to the trustee under the receiving
  RESP.

## [Sharing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716812 .Part}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} CES
  grants and the earnings generated on them may only be shared among the
  beneficiaries of the RESP.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A CLB shall not be shared among beneficiaries of the
  RESP. However, the earnings generated on a CLB may be shared among the
  beneficiaries of the RESP.
