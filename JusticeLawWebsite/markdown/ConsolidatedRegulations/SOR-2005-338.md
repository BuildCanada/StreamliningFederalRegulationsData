## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-720746 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 2,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on certain woven fabrics of
tariff item No. 5906.99.22, imported into Canada by Fenner Dunlop
(Bracebridge) Inc. during the period commencing on January 14, 1999 and
ending on May 19, 2004.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-720748 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the woven fabrics were or will be used in the
  manufacture of conveyor belts by Fenner Dunlop (Bracebridge) Inc.;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within twelve months after
  the date on which this Order comes into force;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the importer provides the Canada Border Services
  Agency with any evidence or information that demonstrates that the
  importer is entitled to remission under this Order; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} no claim for refund or drawback under the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) or the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) has
  been made with respect to the goods on which remission is claimed.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-720754 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
