## Quebec Hog Marketing Levies (Interprovincial and Export Trade) Order {#quebec-hog-marketing-levies-interprovincial-and-export-trade-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2018-27
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 2018-02-21

Quebec Hog Marketing Levies (Interprovincial and Export Trade) Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
[Les Éleveurs de porcs du Québec]{.otherLang lang="fr"}, formerly known
as the [Fédération des producteurs de porc du Québec]{.otherLang
lang="fr"}, pursuant to sections 3 and 4 of the [Quebec Hog Marketing
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-727)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-3040-E_hq_19716){#fn_81000-2-3040-E_hq_19716-ID0EDACA},
makes the annexed [Quebec Hog Marketing Levies (Interprovincial and
Export Trade) Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-549).

Longueuil, February 19, 2018

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858019 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order:

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production of hogs, sows or boars in
    Quebec. ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sow or boar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sow or boar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a sow or boar produced in
    Quebec. ([truie ou verrat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858023 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay
the following levies to the Commodity Board for each hog, sow or boar
sold or marketed by the producer in interprovincial or export trade:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} per hog whose net weight is at least 65 kg, a levy of
  \$0.0123 per kilogram of [[net weight of the hot
  carcass]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in the [Règlement sur la
  production et la mise en marché des porcs]{.otherLang lang="fr"},
  RLRQ, c. M-35.1, r. 281; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} per sow or boar, a levy of \$8.97.

## [Remittance of Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858027 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The producer shall remit
the levies referred to in section 2 to the Commodity Board on or before
the 15th day of each month in respect of hogs, sows and boars sold or
marketed during the preceding month.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858029 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The [Quebec Hog Marketing
Levies (Interprovincial and Export Trade)
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-549)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_81000-2-3040-E_hq_19911){#fn_81000-2-3040-E_hq_19911-ID0EBBA}
is repealed.
