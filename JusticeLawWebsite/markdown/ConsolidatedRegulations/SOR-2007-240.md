## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736330 .Part}

Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [First Nations Fiscal Management
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.67).

- SOR/2016-29, s. 6

## [Referral to Panel]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736335 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation to panel

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may delegate to a panel consisting of three or more
  commissioners its powers of review and approval under section 31 of
  the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of commissioners

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The commissioners of that panel must be designated by
  the Chief Commissioner.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-29, s. 7
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2-20071101.html "Link to previous version of section 2"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Referral to Commission

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** If a panel constituted
under section 2 determines that a law submitted for review and approval
does not comply with the Act or any regulations or standards made under
the Act, the panel must not approve the law and is to refer it back to
the Commission for review and approval.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-29, s. 7

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736347 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
