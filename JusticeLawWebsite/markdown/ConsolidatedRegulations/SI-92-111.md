## Withdrawal of Certain Lands (Fry Inlet on Contwoyto Lake, N.W.T.) from Disposal Order {#withdrawal-of-certain-lands-fry-inlet-on-contwoyto-lake-n.w.t.-from-disposal-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/92-111
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 1992-07-01

Withdrawal of Certain Lands (Fry Inlet on Contwoyto Lake, N.W.T.) from
Disposal Order

P.C. 1992-1264  1992-06-11
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, and for the
reason that the lands are required to facilitate the settlement of the
land claims by the Inuit of the Nunavut Settlement Area, is pleased
hereby, pursuant to paragraph 23(a) of the [Territorial Lands
Act](/eng/acts/T-7), to order the withdrawal from disposal of the lands
described in the schedule hereto, including mines and minerals, whether
solid, liquid or gaseous, and construction material that may be disposed
of pursuant to the [Territorial Quarrying
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1527), but excluding timber
that may be disposed of pursuant to the Forest Management Act (Northwest
Territories), for the period terminating on May 31, 1995, or on the date
of coming into force of the legislation ratifying the land claims
Agreement between the Inuit of the Nunavut Settlement Area and Her
Majesty in Right of Canada, whichever comes first, without prejudice to
the following interests, including any renewals, replacements,
extensions or transfers thereof:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} located or recorded mineral claims or prospecting
  permits in good standing under the Canada Mining Regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the right to obtain a surface lease on located or
  recorded mineral claims pursuant to the [Territorial Lands
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1525);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} rights in good standing created pursuant to section 8
  of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7) or the [Territorial
  Lands Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1525);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} permits, special renewal permits and leases in good
  standing under the [Canada Oil and Gas Land
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1518);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} permits in good standing under the [Territorial
  Quarrying Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1527);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} existing rights and interests granted pursuant to the
  [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} existing rights issued pursuant to the [Territorial
  Coal Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1522) and the
  [Territorial Dredging Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1523);
  and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} interests in lands described in "Exhibit 2" to the
  Acquisition Agreement dated May 5, 1988, among Her Majesty in right of
  Canada, the Northern Canada Power Commission, the Government of the
  Northwest Territories and the Northwest Territories Power Corporation.
:::

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-644753 .scheduleLabel}
:::

Those parcels of lands in the Northwest Territories at Fry Inlet on
Contwoyto Lake shown as 01/76E and 02/76E on the National Topographic
Series Map 76E labelled "Lands Identified for Final Withdrawal According
to the Agreement of July 31, 1991 Between Tungavik Federation of Nunavut
and Canada" which map is on file at the office of the Regional Manager,
Lands Resources Division at Yellowknife, Northwest Territories.
