## Order Designating Provinces Under Subsection 6(1) of the Federal Prompt Payment for Construction Work Act {#order-designating-provinces-under-subsection-61-of-the-federal-prompt-payment-for-construction-work-act .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2023-270
:::

[FEDERAL PROMPT PAYMENT FOR CONSTRUCTION WORK ACT](/eng/acts/F-7.7)

Registration 2023-12-08

Order Designating Provinces Under Subsection 6(1) of the Federal Prompt
Payment for Construction Work Act

P.C. 2023-1218 2023-12-08
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, under subsection 6(1) of the [Federal Prompt Payment for
Construction Work Act](/eng/acts/F-7.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_810000-2-4553_hq_1658421213){#fn_810000-2-4553_hq_1658421213-ID0EDBDA},
the Governor in Council is of the opinion, taking into account the
criteria set out in section 2 of the [Federal Prompt Payment for
Construction Work Regulations (Criteria, Time Limits, Interest and
Circumstances)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-269), that the designated
provinces have, by or under their laws, enacted.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_810000-2-4553_hq_1658421213-ID0EDBDA){#fn_810000-2-4553_hq_1658421213}S.C.
  2019, c. 29, s. 387
  :::

<!-- -->

- [a)]{.lawlabel} a regime for the payment of contractors and
  subcontractors that is reasonably similar to the one set out in that
  Act; and

- [b)]{.lawlabel} in case of non-payment of contractors or
  subcontractors, an adjudication regime that is reasonably similar to
  the one set out in that Act;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Public Works and Government Services,
under subsection 6(1) of the [Federal Prompt Payment for Construction
Work Act](/eng/acts/F-7.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_810000-2-4553_hq_31163){#fn_810000-2-4553_hq_31163-ID0EDBAA},
makes the annexed [Order Designating Provinces Under Subsection 6(1) of
the Federal Prompt Payment for Construction Work
Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-270).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_810000-2-4553_hq_31163-ID0EDBAA){#fn_810000-2-4553_hq_31163}S.C.
  2019, c. 29, s. 387
  :::
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated provinces

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following provinces are
the designated provinces under subsection 6(1) of the [Federal Prompt
Payment for Construction Work Act](/eng/acts/F-7.7):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Ontario;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Saskatchewan;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Alberta.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2023-270_e_hq_1703188228){#fn_SOR-2023-270_e_hq_1703188228-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force on the day on which section
387 of the [Budget Implementation Act, 2019, No. 1](/eng/acts/B-9.8593),
chapter 29 of the Statutes of Canada, 2019, comes into force, but if it
is registered after that day, it comes into force on the day on which it
is registered.
