## Ontario Tender Fruit Order {#ontario-tender-fruit-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/79-678
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1979-09-24

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing of Tender Fruit in
Ontario

P.C. 1979-2538 1979-09-20
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to section 2 of the [Agricultural
Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), is pleased hereby to revoke the
Ontario Fresh Fruit Order, C.R.C., c. 187 and the Ontario Tender
Fruit-for-Processing Order, C.R.C., c. 219, and to make the annexed
[Order granting authority to regulate the marketing of tender fruit in
Ontario](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-678) in substitution therefor.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-866371 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Tender Fruit Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-678).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-866373 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing
    Board of Ontario, established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Régie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Tender
    Fruit Producers' Marketing Board, established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    tender fruit, established and amended from time to time pursuant to
    the Act, and any regulations made pursuant to the Act to give effect
    to any such plan; ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manufacture of tender fruit products or of
      juice, beverages, spirits or wine from tender fruit, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the canning, bottling, distilling, fermenting,
      dehydrating, pitting, drying or freezing of tender fruit;
      ([transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tender fruit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tender fruit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means peaches, pears, plums,
    sour cherries and sweet cherries produced in Ontario. ([fruits
    tendres]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-291, s. 1
:::

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-866385 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board and the
Board are each authorized to regulate the marketing of tender fruit in
interprovincial and export trade and for such purposes may, with respect
to persons and property situated within the Province of Ontario,
exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable by each of them
in relation to the marketing of tender fruit locally within the Province
under the Act and the Plan.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-100, s. 1
:::

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-866389 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board may, in
relation to the powers granted to it under section 3 with respect to the
marketing of tender fruit in interprovincial and export trade,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix and impose by order and collect levies or charges
  on persons situated in the Province of Ontario who are engaged in the
  production or marketing of tender fruit and for that purpose classify
  such persons into groups and, by order, fix the levies or charges
  payable by the members of different groups in different amounts; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the levies or charges for the purposes of the
  Commodity Board and the Board, including the creation of reserves and
  payment of expenses and losses resulting from the sale or disposal of
  tender fruit and the equalization or adjustment among tender fruit
  producers of moneys realized from the sale of tender fruit during such
  period or periods of time as the Commodity Board and the Board may
  determine.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-100, s. 2
