## Hyperlinks to provincial and territorial courts\' websites {#wb-cont}

### Rules of provincial and territorial courts made under sections 482, 482.1 and 745.64 of the Criminal Code

These rules of court are current to September 18, 2019. As of September
19, 2019, the Department of Justice Canada no longer publishes on its
Justice Laws Website the rules made, by provincial and territorial
courts, under sections 482 and 482.1 of the Criminal Code. In addition,
the Statutory Instruments Act no longer applies to rules established for
the purposes of sections 745.6 and 745.63 of the Criminal Code. For more
information, please consult [hyperlinks to legal information
websites](/eng/Court/).

If you wish to consult previous versions of the rules published on the
Justice Laws Website, please see [Previous Versions](PITIndex.html).
