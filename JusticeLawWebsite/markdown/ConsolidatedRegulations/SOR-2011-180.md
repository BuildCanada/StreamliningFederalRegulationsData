## Qalipu Mi'kmaq First Nation Band Order {#qalipu-mikmaq-first-nation-band-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2011-180
:::

[INDIAN ACT](/eng/acts/I-5)

Registration 2011-09-22

Qalipu Mi'kmaq First Nation Band Order

P.C. 2011-928 2011-09-22
::::

:::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph (c) of the definition [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612964-E_hq_9019){#fn_612964-E_hq_9019-ID0EDBAA}
and subsection 73(3) of that Act, hereby makes the annexed [Qalipu
Mi'kmaq First Nation Band Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-180).

## [Declaration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-773870 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The body of Indians
described in section 2 is declared to be a band for the purposes of the
[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), to be known as the Qalipu Mi'kmaq First
Nation.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Qalipu Mi'kmaq First
Nation is the body of Indians consisting of the persons identified in
the Schedule.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-1402216 .scheduleLabel}
:::

The Qalipu Mi'kmaq First Nation is the body of Indians consisting of the
persons identified on the list of founding members provided by the
Enrolment Committee to the Government of Canada and to the Federation of
Newfoundland Indians on February 14, 2023.
