::::::: section

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 15(1) of the [Canada Oil and Gas
Operations Act](/eng/acts/O-7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_602187-E_hq_5493){#fn_602187-E_hq_5493-ID0EFBBA},

Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1517), substantially in the

Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I on April 18, 2009 and
interested persons were given an opportunity to make representations to
the Minister of Natural Resources and the Minister of Indian Affairs and
Northern Development with respect to the proposed Regulations;

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-751045 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[abandoned]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[abandoned]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a well, means a
      well or part of a well that has been permanently plugged.
      ([abandonné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Oil and Gas
      Operations Act](/eng/acts/O-7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[artificial island]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[artificial island]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a humanly
      constructed island to provide a site for the exploration and
      drilling, or the production, storage, transportation,
      distribution, measurement, processing or handling, of oil or gas.
      ([île artificielle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an authorization
      issued by the Board under paragraph 5(1)(b) of the Act.
      ([autorisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[barrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[barrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any fluid, plug or seal that
      prevents gas or oil or any other fluid from flowing
      unintentionally from a well or from a formation into another
      formation. ([barrière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the National Energy Board
      established by section 3 of the [National Energy Board
      Act](/eng/acts/N-7). ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[casing liner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[casing liner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a casing that is
      suspended from a string of casing previously installed in a well
      and does not extend to the wellhead. ([tubage
      partiel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[commingled production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[commingled production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means production of
      oil and gas from more than one pool or zone through a common
      well-bore or flow line without separate measurement of the
      production from each pool or zone. ([production
      mélangée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[completed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[completed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a well, means a
      well that is prepared for production or injection operations.
      ([complété]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[completion interval]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[completion interval]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a section within
      a well that is prepared to permit the

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} production of fluids from the well;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} observation of the performance of a reservoir;
        or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} injection of fluids into the well. ([intervalle
        de complétion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[conductor casing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[conductor casing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the casing that is
      installed in a well to facilitate drilling of the hole for the
      surface casing. ([tubage initial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[development plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[development plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the development
      plan that is approved by the Board in accordance with section 5.1
      of the Act. ([plan de mise en valeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[drilling program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[drilling program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the program for the
      drilling of one or more wells within a specified area and time
      using one or more drilling installations and includes any work or
      activity related to the program. ([programme de
      forage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[environmental protection plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[environmental protection plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      environmental protection plan submitted to the Board under
      section 6. ([plan de protection de
      l'environnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[flow allocation procedure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[flow allocation procedure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      procedure to

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} allocate total measured quantities of oil, gas
        and water produced from or injected into a pool or zone back to
        individual wells in a pool or zone where individual well
        production or injection is not measured separately; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} allocate production to fields that are using a
        common storage or processing facility. ([méthode de répartition
        du débit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[flow calculation procedure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[flow calculation procedure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      procedure to be used to convert raw meter output to a measured
      quantity of oil, gas or water. ([méthode de calcul du
      débit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[flow system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[flow system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the flow meters,
      auxiliary equipment attached to the flow meters, fluid sampling
      devices, production test equipment, the master meter and meter
      prover used to measure and record the rate and volumes at which
      fluids are

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} produced from or injected into a pool;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} used as a fuel;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} used for artificial lift; or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} flared or transferred from a production
        installation. ([système d'écoulement]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[fluid]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fluid]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means gas, liquid or a combination
      of the two. ([fluide]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[formation flow test]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[formation flow test]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an operation

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to induce the flow of formation fluids to the
        surface of a well to procure reservoir fluid samples and
        determine reservoir flow characteristics; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to inject fluids into a formation to evaluate
        injectivity. ([essai d'écoulement de formation]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any event that causes

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a lost or restricted workday injury,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} death,

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} fire or explosion,

        - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a loss of containment of any fluid from a
          well,

        - [(v)]{.lawlabel} an imminent threat to the safety of a person,
          installation or support craft, or

        - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} pollution;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any event that results in a missing person; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any event that causes

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the impairment of any structure, facility,
          equipment or system critical to the safety of persons, an
          installation or support craft, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the impairment of any structure, facility,
          equipment or system critical to environmental protection.
          ([incident]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[lost or restricted workday injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[lost or restricted workday injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      injury that prevents an employee from reporting for work or from
      effectively performing all the duties connected with the
      employee's regular work on any day subsequent to the day on which
      the injury occurred whether or not that subsequent day is a
      working day for that employee. ([blessure entraînant une perte de
      temps de travail]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[minor injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[minor injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employment injury
      for which medical treatment or first aid is provided and excludes
      a lost or restricted workday injury. ([blessure sans
      gravité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[multi-pool well]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[multi-pool well]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a well that is
      completed in more than one pool. ([puits à gisements
      multiples]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[natural environment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[natural environment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the physical and
      biological environment. ([milieu naturel]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[near-miss]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[near-miss]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an event that would likely
      cause an event set out in paragraph (a) of the definition of
      [[incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, but does not due to particular

  [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person that holds an

      authorization. ([exploitant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[permafrost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[permafrost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the thermal condition of

  [[physical environmental conditions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[physical environmental conditions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  [[pollution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[pollution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the introduction into the

  [[production control system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Board's Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-751127 .Part}

### [Spacing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751128 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Board is authorized to
make orders respecting the allocation of areas, including the
determination of the size of spacing units and the well production rates
for the purpose of drilling for or producing oil and gas and to exercise
any powers and perform any duties that may be necessary for the
management and control of oil or gas production.

### [Names and Designations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751130 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Board may give a name,
classification or status to any well and may change that name,
classification or status.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Board may also

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} designate a zone for the purposes of these
  Regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} give a name to a pool or field; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} define the boundaries of a pool, zone or field for
  the purpose of identifying it.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Management System, Application for Authorization and Well Approvals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-751136 .Part}

### [Management System]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751137 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  applicant for an authorization shall develop an effective management
  system that integrates operations and technical systems with the
  management of financial and human resources to ensure compliance with
  the Act and these Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The management system shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the policies on which the system is based;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the processes for setting goals for the improvement
    of safety, environmental protection and waste prevention;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the processes for identifying hazards and for
    evaluating and managing the associated risks;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the processes for ensuring that personnel are
    trained and competent to perform their duties;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the processes for ensuring and maintaining the
    integrity of all facilities, structures, installations, support
    craft and equipment necessary to ensure safety, environmental
    protection and waste prevention;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the processes for the internal reporting and
    analysis of hazards, minor injuries, incidents and near-misses and
    for taking corrective actions to prevent their recurrence;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the documents describing all management system
    processes and the processes for making personnel aware of their
    roles and responsibilities with respect to them;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the processes for ensuring that all documents
    associated with the system are current, valid and have been approved
    by the appropriate level of authority;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the processes for conducting periodic reviews or
    audits of the system and for taking corrective actions if reviews or
    audits identify areas of non-conformance with the system and
    opportunities for improvement;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the arrangements for coordinating the management
    and operations of the proposed work or activity among the owner of
    the installation, the contractors, the operator and others, as
    applicable; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the name and position of the person accountable for
    the establishment and maintenance of the system and of the person
    responsible for implementing it.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The management system documentation shall be
  controlled and set out in a logical and systematic fashion to allow
  for ease of understanding and efficient implementation.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The management system shall correspond to the size,
  nature and complexity of the operations and activities, hazards and
  risks associated with the operations.

### [Application for Authorization]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751154 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The application for
authorization shall be accompanied by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the scope of the proposed
  activities;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an execution plan and schedule for undertaking those
  activities;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a safety plan that meets the requirements of
  section 8;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an environmental protection plan that meets the
  requirements of section 9;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} information on any proposed flaring or venting of
  gas, including the rationale and the estimated rate, quantity and
  period of the flaring or venting;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} information on any proposed burning of oil, including
  the rationale and the estimated quantity of oil proposed to be burned;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a drilling installation, a description
  of the drilling and well control equipment;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a production installation, a
  description of the processing facilities and control system;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a production project, a field data
  acquisition program that allows sufficient pool pressure measurements,
  fluid samples, cased hole logs and formation flow tests for a
  comprehensive assessment of the performance of development wells, pool
  depletion schemes and the field;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} contingency plans, including emergency response
  procedures, to mitigate the effects of any reasonably foreseeable
  event that might compromise safety or environmental protection, which
  shall

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} provide for coordination measures with any relevant
    municipal, provincial, territorial or federal emergency response
    plan, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in an offshore area where oil is reasonably
    expected to be encountered, identify the scope and frequency of the
    field practice exercise of oil spill countermeasures; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} a description of the decommissioning and abandonment
  of the site, including methods for restoration of the site after its
  abandonment.

<!-- -->

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  application for authorization covers a production installation, the
  applicant shall also submit to the Board for its approval the flow
  system, the flow calculation procedure and the flow allocation
  procedure that will be used to conduct the measurements referred to in
  Part 7.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall approve the flow system, the flow
  calculation procedure and the flow allocation procedure if the
  applicant demonstrates that the system and procedures facilitate
  reasonably accurate measurements and allocate, on a pool or zone

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their relationship to each other, and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the possibility of pack sea ice, drifting icebergs

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a description of the arrangements for monitoring
  compliance with the plan and for measuring performance in relation to
  its objectives.

### [Well Approval]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751197 .Subheading}

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), an

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A well approval is not necessary to conduct a wire

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the work does not alter the completion interval or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the equipment, operating procedures and qualified

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** If the well approval

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a comprehensive description of the drilling program;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a well data acquisition program that allows for the

  cores, sidewall cores, pressure measurements and formation flow tests,

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The application shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the well approval sought is to re-enter, work

  well or part of it will be abandoned or completed.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall grant the

### [Suspension and Revocation of a Well Approval]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751211 .Subheading}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may suspend the well approval if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the operator fails to comply with the approval and
    the work or activity cannot be conducted safely, without waste or
    without pollution;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the safety of the work or activity becomes
    uncertain because

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the level of performance of the installation or
      service equipment, any ancillary equipment or any support craft is
      demonstrably less than the level of performance indicated in the
      application, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the physical environmental conditions
      encountered in the area of the activity for which the well
      approval was granted are more severe than the equipment's
      operating limits as specified by the manufacturer; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the operator fails to comply with the approvals
    issued under subsection 7(2), 52(4) or 66(2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may revoke the well approval if the
  operator fails to remedy the situation causing the suspension within
  120 days after the date of that suspension.

### [Safety and Environmental Protection]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751230 .Subheading}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall take
all reasonable precautions to ensure safety and environmental
protection, including ensuring that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any operation necessary for the safety of persons at
  an installation or on a support craft has priority, at all times, over
  any work or activity at that installation or on that support craft;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} safe work methods are followed during all drilling,
  well or production operations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} there is a shift handover system to effectively
  communicate any conditions, mechanical or procedural deficiencies or
  other problems that might have an impact on safety or environmental
  protection;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} differences in language or other barriers to
  effective communication do not jeopardize safety or environmental
  protection;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} all persons at an installation, or in transit to or
  from an installation, receive instruction in and are familiar with
  safety and evacuation procedures and with their roles and
  responsibilities in the contingency plans, including emergency
  response procedures;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any drilling or well operation is conducted in a
  manner that maintains full control of the well at all times;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} if there is loss of control of a well at an
  installation, all other wells at that installation are shut in until
  the well that is out of control is secured;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} plans are in place to deal with potential hazards;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} all equipment required for safety and environmental
  protection is available and in an operable condition;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the inventory of all equipment identified in the
  safety plan and the environmental protection plan is updated after the
  completion of any significant modification or repair to any major
  component of the equipment;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the administrative and logistical support that is
  provided for drilling, well or production operations includes
  accommodation, transportation, first aid and storage, repair
  facilities and communication systems suitable for the area of
  operations;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} a sufficient number of trained and competent
  individuals are available to complete the authorized work or
  activities and to carry out any work or activity safely and without
  pollution; and

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} any operational procedure that is a hazard to safety
  or the environment is corrected and all affected persons are informed
  of the alteration.

### [Cessation of a Work or Activity]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751257 .Subheading}

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator shall ensure that any work or activity ceases without delay
  if that work or activity

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} endangers or is likely to endanger the safety of

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} endangers or is likely to endanger the safety or
    integrity of the well or the installation; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} causes or is likely to cause pollution.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the work or activity ceases, the operator shall
  ensure that it does not resume until it can do so safely and without
  pollution.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Equipment and Operations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-751264 .Part}

### [Wells, Installations, Equipment, Facilities and Support Craft]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751265 .Subheading}

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all wells, installations, equipment and facilities

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a comprehensive inspection that includes a

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} records of maintenance, tests and inspections are

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the components of an installation and well tubulars,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any part of an installation that may be exposed to a
  sour environment is designed, constructed and maintained to operate
  safely in that environment.

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the indicators and alarms associated with the

### [Marine Riser]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751280 .Subheading}

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} furnishing access to the well;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} isolating the well-bore from the sea;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} withstanding the differential pressure of the

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} withstanding the physical forces anticipated in the

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} permitting the drilling fluid to be returned to the

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator shall ensure that every marine riser is

  caused by the motion of the installation.

### [Drilling Practices]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751289 .Subheading}

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that adequate equipment, procedures and personnel are in place to
recognize and control normal and abnormal pressures, to allow for safe,
controlled drilling operations and to prevent pollution.

### [Reference for Well Depths]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751291 .Subheading}

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that any depth in a well is measured from a single reference point,
which is either the kelly bushing or the rotary table of the drilling
rig.

### [Directional and Deviation Surveys]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751293 .Subheading}

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} directional and deviation surveys are taken at
  intervals that allow the position of the well-bore to be determined
  accurately; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} except in the case of a relief well, a well is
  drilled in a manner that does not intersect an existing well.

### [Formation Leak-Off Test]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751297 .Subheading}

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a formation leak-off test or a formation integrity
  test is conducted before drilling more than 10 m below the shoe of any
  casing other than the conductor casing;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the formation leak-off test or the formation
  integrity test is conducted to a pressure that allows for safe
  drilling to the next planned casing depth; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a record is retained of each formation leak-off test
  and the results included in the daily drilling report referred to in
  paragraph 83(a) and in the well history report referred to in
  section 88.

### [Formation Flow and Well Testing Equipment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751302 .Subheading}

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator shall ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the equipment used in a formation flow test is
    designed to safely control well pressure, properly evaluate the
    formation and prevent pollution;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the rated working pressure of formation flow test
    equipment upstream of and including the well testing manifold
    exceeds the maximum anticipated shut-in pressure; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the equipment downstream of the well testing
    manifold is sufficiently protected against overpressure.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator of an offshore well or a well in a sour
  environment shall ensure that the formation flow test equipment
  includes a down-hole safety valve that permits closure of the test
  string above the packer.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The operator shall ensure that any formation flow
  test equipment used in testing an offshore well that is drilled with a
  floating drilling unit has a subsea test tree that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a valve that may be operated from the surface and
    automatically closes when required to prevent uncontrolled well
    flow; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a release system that permits the test string to be
    hydraulically or mechanically disconnected within or below the
    blow-out preventers.

### [Well Control]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751312 .Subheading}

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that adequate procedures, materials and equipment are in place and
utilized to minimize the risk of loss of well control in the event of
lost circulation.

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator shall ensure that, during all well operations, reliably
  operating well control equipment is installed to control kicks,
  prevent blow-outs and safely carry out all well activities and
  operations, including drilling, completion and workover operations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} After setting the surface casing, the operator shall
  ensure that at least two independent and tested well barriers are in
  place during all well operations.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a barrier fails, the operator shall ensure that no
  other activities, other than those intended to restore or replace the
  barrier, take place in the well.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The operator shall ensure that, during drilling,
  except when drilling under-balanced, one of the two barriers to be
  maintained is the drilling fluid column.

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that pressure control equipment associated with drilling, coil tubing,
slick line and wire line operations is pressure-tested on installation
and as often as necessary to ensure its continued safe operation.

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** If the well control is
lost or if safety, environmental protection or resource conservation is
at risk, the operator shall ensure that any action necessary to rectify
the situation is taken without delay, despite any condition to the
contrary in the well approval.

### [Casing and Cementing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751321 .Subheading}

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that the well and casing are designed so that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the well can be drilled safely, the targeted
  formations evaluated and waste prevented;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated conditions, forces and stresses that
  may be placed upon them are withstood; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the integrity of gas hydrate and permafrost zones ---
  and, in the case of an onshore well, potable water zones --- is
  protected.

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that the well and casing are installed at a depth that provides for
adequate kick tolerances and well control operations that provide for
safe, constant bottom hole pressure.

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that cement slurry is designed and installed so that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the movement of formation fluids in the casing annuli
  is prevented and, where required for safety, resource evaluation or
  prevention of waste, the isolation of the oil, gas and water zones is
  ensured;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} support for the casing is provided;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} corrosion of the casing over the cemented interval is
  retarded; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the integrity of gas hydrate and permafrost zones ---
  and, in the case of an onshore well, potable water zones --- is
  protected.

### [Waiting on Cement Time]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751332 .Subheading}

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** After the cementing of
any casing or casing liner and before drilling out the casing shoe, the
operator shall ensure that the cement has reached the minimum
compressive strength sufficient to support the casing and provide zonal
isolation.

### [Casing Pressure Testing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751334 .Subheading}

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** After installing and
cementing the casing and before drilling out the casing shoe, the
operator shall ensure that the casing is pressure-tested to the value
required to confirm its integrity for maximum anticipated operating
pressure.

### [Production Tubing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751336 .Subheading}

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that the production tubing used in a well is designed to withstand the
maximum conditions, forces and stresses that may be placed on it and to
maximize recovery from the pool.

### [Monitoring and Control of Process Operations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751338 .Subheading}

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} operations such as processing, transportation,
  storage, re-injection and handling of oil and gas on the installation
  are effectively monitored to prevent incidents and waste;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all alarm, safety, monitoring, warning and control
  systems associated with those operations are managed to prevent
  incidents and waste; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} all appropriate persons are informed of the
  applicable alarm, safety, monitoring, warning or control systems
  associated with those operations that are taken out of service, and
  when those systems are returned to service.

### [Well Completion]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751343 .Subheading}

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  operator that completes a well shall ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is completed in a safe manner and allows for
    maximum recovery;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} except in the case of commingled production, each
    completion interval is isolated from any other porous or permeable
    interval penetrated by the well;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the testing and production of any completion
    interval are conducted safely and do not cause waste or pollution;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, sand production is controlled and
    does not create a safety hazard or cause waste;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} each packer is set as close as practical to the top
    of the completion interval and that the pressure testing of the
    packer to a differential pressure is greater than the maximum
    differential pressure anticipated under the production or injection
    conditions;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if practical, any mechanical well condition that
    may have an adverse effect on production of oil and gas from, or the
    injection of fluids into, the well is corrected;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the injection or production profile of the well is
    improved, or the completion interval of the well is changed, if it
    is necessary to do so to prevent waste;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if different pressure and inflow characteristics of
    two or more pools might adversely affect the recovery from any of
    those pools, the well is operated as a single pool well or as a
    segregated multi-pool well;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} after initial completion, all barriers are tested
    to the maximum pressure to which they are likely to be subjected;
    and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} following any workover, any affected barriers are
    pressure-tested.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator of a segregated multi-pool well shall
  ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} after the well is completed, segregation has been
    established within and outside the well casing and is confirmed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if there is reason to doubt that segregation is
    being maintained, a segregation test is conducted within a
    reasonable time frame.

### [Subsurface Safety Valve]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751359 .Subheading}

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of an offshore development well capable of flow shall ensure
  that the well is equipped with a fail-safe subsurface safety valve
  that is designed, installed, operated and tested to prevent
  uncontrolled well flow when it is activated.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a development well is located in a zone where
  permafrost is present in unconsolidated sediments, the operator shall
  ensure that a subsurface safety valve is installed in the tubing below
  the base of the permafrost.

### [Wellhead and Christmas Tree Equipment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751363 .Subheading}

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that the wellhead and Christmas tree equipment, including valves, are
designed to operate safely and efficiently under the maximum load
conditions anticipated during the life of the well.

## [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Evaluation of Wells, Pools and Fields]{.HTitleText1} {#h-751365 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751366 .Subheading}

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that the well data acquisition program and the field data acquisition
program are implemented in accordance with good oilfield practices.

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  part of the well or field data acquisition program cannot be
  implemented, the operator shall ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a conservation officer is notified as soon as the
    circumstances permit; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the procedures to otherwise achieve the goals of
    the program are submitted to the Board for approval.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the operator can demonstrate that those procedures
  can achieve the goals of the well or field data acquisition program or
  are all that can be reasonably expected in the circumstances, the
  Board shall approve them.

### [Testing and Sampling of Formations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751373 .Subheading}

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that every formation in a well is tested and sampled to obtain reservoir
pressure data and fluid samples from the formation, if there is an
indication that the data or samples would contribute substantially to
the geological and reservoir evaluation.

### [Formation Flow Testing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751375 .Subheading}

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator shall ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no development well is put into production unless
    the Board has approved a formation flow test in respect of the
    development well; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a development well is subjected to a well
    operation that might change its deliverability, productivity or
    injectivity, a formation flow test is conducted within a reasonable
    time frame after the well operation is ended to determine the
    effects of that operation on the well's deliverability, productivity
    or injectivity.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator may conduct a formation flow test on a
  well drilled on a geological feature if, before conducting that test,
  the operator

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} submits to the Board a detailed testing program;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} obtains the Board's approval to conduct the test.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board may require that the operator conduct a
  formation flow test on a well drilled on a geological feature, other
  than the first well, if there is an indication that the test would
  contribute substantially to the geological and reservoir evaluation.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall approve a formation flow test if the
  operator demonstrates that the test will be conducted safely, without
  pollution and in accordance with good oilfield practices and that the
  test will enable the operator to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtain data on the deliverability or productivity
    of the well;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish the characteristics of the reservoir; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} obtain representative samples of the formation
    fluids.

### [Submission of Samples and Data]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751388 .Subheading}

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that all cutting samples, fluid samples and cores collected as part of
the well and field data acquisition programs are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} transported and stored in a manner that prevents any
  loss or deterioration;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} delivered to the Board within 60 days after the rig
  release date unless analyses are ongoing, in which case those samples
  and cores, or the remaining parts, are to be delivered on completion
  of the analyses; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} stored in durable containers properly labelled for
  identification.

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that after any samples necessary for analysis or for research or
academic studies have been removed from a conventional core, the
remaining core, or a longitudinal slab that is not less than one half of
the cross-sectional area of that core, is submitted to the Board.

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** Before disposing of
cutting samples, fluid samples, cores or evaluation data under these
Regulations, the operator shall ensure that the Board is notified in
writing and is given an opportunity to request delivery of the samples,
cores or data.

## [PART 6]{.HLabel1}[Well Termination]{.HTitleText1} {#h-751395 .Part}

### [Suspension or Abandonment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751396 .Subheading}

**[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that every well that is suspended or abandoned can be readily located
and left in a condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provides for isolation of all oil or gas bearing
  zones and discrete pressure zones; and, in the case of an onshore
  well, potable water zones; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prevents any formation fluid from flowing through or
  escaping from the well-bore.

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** The operator of a
suspended well shall ensure that the well is monitored and inspected to
maintain its continued integrity and to prevent pollution.

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that, on the abandonment of any offshore well, the seafloor is cleared
of any material or equipment that might interfere with other commercial
uses of the sea.

### [Installation Removal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751402 .Subheading}

**[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** No operator shall remove
or cause to have removed a drilling installation from a well drilled
under these Regulations unless the well has been terminated in
accordance with these Regulations.

## [PART 7]{.HLabel1}[Measurements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-751404 .Part}

### [Flow and Volume]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751405 .Subheading}

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  otherwise included in the approval issued under subsection 7(2), the
  operator shall ensure that the rate of flow and the volume of the
  following are measured and recorded:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fluid that is produced from each well;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fluid that is injected into each well;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any produced fluid that enters, leaves, is used or
    is flared, vented, burned or otherwise disposed of on an
    installation, including any battery room, treatment facility or
    processing plant; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any air or materials injected for the purposes of
    disposal, storage or cycling, including drill cuttings and other
    useless material that is generated during drilling, well or
    production operations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator shall ensure that any measurements are
  conducted in accordance with the flow system, flow calculation
  procedure and flow allocation procedure, approved under
  subsection 7(2).

<!-- -->

- **[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator shall ensure that group production of oil and gas from wells
  and injection of a fluid into wells is allocated on a [pro
  rata]{.otherLang lang="la"} basis, in accordance with the flow system,
  flow calculation procedure and flow allocation procedure approved
  under subsection 7(2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a well is completed over multiple pools or zones,
  the operator shall ensure that production or injection volumes for the
  well are allocated on a [pro rata]{.otherLang lang="la"} basis to the
  pools or zones in accordance with the flow allocation procedure
  approved under subsection 7(2).

### [Testing, Maintenance and Notification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751416 .Subheading}

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that meters and associated equipment are calibrated
  and maintained to ensure their continued accuracy;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that equipment used to calibrate the flow system is
  calibrated in accordance with good measurement practices;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} that any component of the flow system that may have
  an impact on the accuracy or integrity of the flow system and that is
  not functioning in accordance with the manufacturer's specifications
  is repaired or replaced without delay, or, if it is not possible to do
  so without delay, corrective measures are taken to minimize the impact
  on the accuracy and integrity of the flow system while the repair or
  replacement is proceeding; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} that a conservation officer is notified, as soon as
  the circumstances permit, of any malfunction or failure of any flow
  system component that may have an impact on the accuracy of the flow
  system and of the corrective measures taken.

### [Transfer Meters]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751422 .Subheading}

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a conservation officer is notified at least 14 days
  before the day on which any transfer meter prover or master meter used
  in conjunction with a transfer meter is calibrated; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the calibration certificate is submitted to
  the Chief Conservation Officer as soon as the circumstances permit,
  following completion of the calibration.

### [Proration Testing Frequency]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751426 .Subheading}

**[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** The operator of a
development well that is producing oil or gas shall ensure that
sufficient proration tests are performed to permit reasonably accurate
determination of the allocation of oil, gas and water production on a
pool and zone basis.

## [PART 8]{.HLabel1}[Production Conservation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-751428 .Part}

### [Resource Management]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751429 .Subheading}

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} maximum recovery from a pool or zone is achieved in
  accordance with good oilfield practices;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} wells are located and operated to provide for maximum
  recovery from a pool; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if there is reason to believe that infill drilling or
  implementation of an enhanced recovery scheme might result in
  increased recovery from a pool or field, studies on these methods are
  carried out and submitted to the Board.

### [Commingled Production]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751434 .Subheading}

- **[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  operator shall engage in commingled production except in accordance
  with the approval granted under subsection (2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall approve the commingled production if
  the operator demonstrates that it would not reduce the recovery from
  the pools or zones.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The operator engaging in commingled production shall
  ensure that the total volume and the rate of production of each fluid
  produced is measured and the volume from each pool or zone is
  allocated in accordance with the requirements of Part 7.

### [Gas Flaring and Venting]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751439 .Subheading}

**[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** No operator shall flare
or vent gas unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is otherwise permitted in the approval issued
  under subsection 52(4) or in the authorization; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is necessary to do so because of an emergency
  situation and the Board is notified in the daily drilling report,
  daily production report or in any other written or electronic form, as
  soon as the circumstances permit, of the flaring or venting and of the
  amount flared or vented.

### [Oil Burning]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751443 .Subheading}

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** No operator shall burn
oil unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is otherwise permitted in the approval issued
  under subsection 52(4) or in the authorization; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is necessary to do so because of an emergency
  situation and the Board is notified in the daily drilling report,
  daily production report or in any other written or electronic form, as
  soon as the circumstances permit, of the burning and the amount
  burned.

## [PART 9]{.HLabel1}[Support Operations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-751447 .Part}

### [Support Craft]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751448 .Subheading}

**[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that all support craft are designed, constructed and maintained to
supply the necessary support functions and operate safely in the
foreseeable physical environmental conditions prevailing in the area in
which they operate.

- **[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of a manned installation shall ensure that at least one
  support craft is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} available at a distance that is not greater than
    that required for a return time of twenty minutes; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} suitably equipped to supply the necessary emergency
    services including rescue and first aid treatment for all personnel
    on the installation in the event of an emergency.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the support craft exceeds the distance referred to
  in paragraph (1)(a), both the installation manager and the person in
  charge of the support craft shall log this fact and the reason why the
  distance or time was exceeded.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Under the direction of the installation manager, the
  support craft crew shall keep the craft in close proximity to the
  installation, maintain open communication channels with the
  installation and be prepared to conduct rescue operations during any
  activity or condition that presents an increased level of risk to the
  safety of personnel or the installation.

### [Safety Zone]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751456 .Subheading}

- **[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of this section, the safety zone around an offshore
  installation consists of the area within a line enclosing and drawn at
  a distance of 500 m from the outer edge of the installation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A support craft shall not enter the safety zone
  without the consent of the installation manager.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The operator shall take all reasonable measures to
  warn persons who are in charge of vessels and aircraft of the safety
  zone boundaries, of the facilities within the safety zone and of any
  related potential hazards.

## [PART 10]{.HLabel1}[Training and Competency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-751461 .Part}

**[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all personnel have, before assuming their duties, the
  necessary experience, training and qualifications and are able to
  conduct their duties safely, competently and in compliance with these
  Regulations; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} records of the experience, training and
  qualifications of all personnel are kept and made available to the
  Board upon request.

## [PART 11]{.HLabel1}[Submissions, Notifications, Records and Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-751465 .Part}

### [Reference to Names and Designations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751466 .Subheading}

**[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** When submitting any
information for the purposes of these Regulations, the operator shall
refer to each well, pool and field by the name given to it under
sections 3 and 4, or if a zone, by its designation by the Board under
section 4.

### [Surveys]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751468 .Subheading}

- **[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator shall ensure that a survey is used to confirm

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for an onshore well, the surface location; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for an offshore well, the location on the seafloor.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The survey shall be certified by a person licensed
  under the [Canada Lands Surveyors Act](/eng/acts/L-5.8).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The operator shall ensure that a copy of the survey
  plan filed with the Canada Lands Surveys Records is submitted to the
  Board.

### [Incidents and Near-misses]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751475 .Subheading}

- **[[75]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-75 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator shall ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Board is notified of any incident or near-miss
    as soon as the circumstances permit; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Board is notified at least 24 hours in advance
    of any press release or press conference held by the operator
    concerning any incident or near-miss during any activity to which
    these Regulations apply, except in an emergency situation, in which
    case it shall be notified without delay before the press release or
    press conference.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator shall ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any incident or near-miss is investigated, its root
    cause and causal factors identified and corrective action taken; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for any of the following incidents or near-misses,
    a copy of an investigation report identifying the root cause, causal
    factors and corrective action taken is submitted to the Board no
    later than 21 days after the day on which the incident or near-miss
    occurred:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a lost or restricted workday injury,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} death,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} fire or explosion,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a loss of containment of any fluid from a well,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} an imminent threat to the safety of a person,
      installation or support craft, or

    - [vi)]{.lawlabel} a significant pollution event.

### [Submission of Data and Analysis]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751489 .Subheading}

- **[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator shall ensure that a final copy of the results, data, analyses
  and schematics obtained from the following sources is submitted to the
  Board:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} testing, sampling and pressure surveys carried out
    as part of the well and field data acquisition programs referred to
    in section 49 and testing and sampling of formations referred to in
    section 51; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any segregation test or well operation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise indicated in these Regulations, the
  operator shall ensure that the results, data, analyses and schematics
  are submitted within 60 days after the day on which any activity
  referred to in paragraphs (1)(a) and (b) is completed.

### [Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751495 .Subheading}

**[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that records are kept of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all persons arriving, leaving or present on the
  installation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the location and movement of support craft, the
  emergency drills and exercises, incidents, near-misses, the quantities
  of consumable substances that are required to ensure the safety of
  operations and other observations and information critical to the
  safety of persons on the installation or the protection of the
  environment;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} daily maintenance and operating activities, including
  any activity that may be critical to the safety of persons on the
  installation, the protection of the environment or the prevention of
  waste;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a production installation,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the inspection of the installation and related
    equipment for corrosion and erosion and any resulting maintenance
    carried out,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the pressure, temperature and flow rate data for
    compressors and treating and processing facilities,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the calibration of meters and instruments,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the testing of surface and subsurface safety
    valves,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the status of each well and the status of well
    operations, and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the status of the equipment and systems critical
    to safety and protection of the environment including any
    unsuccessful test result or equipment failure leading to an
    impairment of the systems; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a floating installation, all
  installation movements, data, observations, measurements and
  calculations related to the stability and station-keeping capability
  of the installation.

### [Meteorological Observations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751508 .Subheading}

**[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78 .sectionLabel}** The operator of an
offshore installation shall ensure

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the installation is equipped with facilities and
  equipment for observing, measuring and recording physical
  environmental conditions and that a comprehensive record of
  observations of physical environmental conditions is maintained
  onboard the installation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that forecasts of meteorological conditions, sea
  states and ice movements are obtained and recorded each day and each
  time during the day that they change substantially from those
  forecasted.

### [Daily Production Record]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751512 .Subheading}

**[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that a daily production record, which includes the metering records and
other information relating to the production of oil and gas and other
fluids in respect of a pool or well, is retained and readily accessible
to the Board until the field or well in which the pool is located is
abandoned and at that time shall offer the record to the Board before
destroying it.

### [Management of Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751514 .Subheading}

**[[80]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the records are readily accessible for inspection by
  the Board.

### [Formation Flow Test Reports]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751518 .Subheading}

**[[81]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-81 .sectionLabel}** The operator shall ensure
that

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of all wells, a formation flow test report

### [Pilot Scheme]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751522 .Subheading}

### [Annual Production Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751538 .Subheading}

reserve revision, reasons for significant deviations in well performance
from predictions in previous annual production reports, gas conservation
resources, efforts to maximize recovery and reduce costs, and any other
information required to demonstrate how the operator manages and intends

### [Annual Safety Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-751548 .Subheading}
