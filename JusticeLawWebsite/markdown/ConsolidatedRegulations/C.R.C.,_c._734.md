## Specification of Statutory Provisions (Treasury Board) Order {#specification-of-statutory-provisions-treasury-board-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 734
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Specifying the Provisions of Acts Respecting a Matter in Relation
to which the Treasury Board is Authorized to Exercise the Powers of the
Governor in Council
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561235 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Specification of Statutory Provisions (Treasury Board)
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._734).

## [Specification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561237 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Treasury Board is
authorized to exercise the powers of the Governor in Council under the
provisions specified in the schedule.

::::: section
:::: {#561239 .Schedule}
::: {}
## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-561240 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** Subsection 8(1) and subsection 9(1) of the [Canadian Wheat Board
  Act](/eng/acts/C-24).

- **2** Subsection 11(3) of the Central Mortgage and Housing Corporation
  Act in relation to the establishment and operation of a pension fund
  under subsection 14(3) of that Act.

- **3** Subsection 6(5) and section 7 of the [Diplomatic Service
  (Special) Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/D-2) and section 16 of that
  Act in relation to the powers of the Governor in Council under
  subsection 6(5) and section 7 of that Act.

- **4** Subsection 5(1) of the Government Companies Operation Act.

- **5** Section 44 of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

- **6** Finance Vote 462 of the Appropriation Act, No. 5, 1943.

- **7** Finance Vote 124 of the Appropriation Act, No. 6, 1960.

- **8** Finance Vote 686 of the Appropriation Act, No. 2, 1961.

- **9** Finance vote 632 of the Appropriation Act, No. 2, 1962.

- **10** Finance Vote 59a of the Appropriation Act, No. 5, 1963.

- **11** Finance Vote 63a of the Appropriation Act, No. 5, 1963.

- **12** Finance Vote 71a of the Appropriation Act, No. 5, 1963.

- **13** Finance Vote 20b of the Appropriation Act, No. 10, 1964.

- **14** Finance Vote 20a of the Appropriation Act, No. 9, 1966.
