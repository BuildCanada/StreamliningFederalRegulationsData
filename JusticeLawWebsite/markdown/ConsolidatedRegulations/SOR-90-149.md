## Exempt Personal Information Bank Order, No. 13 (RCMP) {#exempt-personal-information-bank-order-no.-13-rcmp .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-149
:::

[PRIVACY ACT](/eng/acts/P-21)

Registration 1990-02-22

Order Respecting the Designation of the Criminal Operational
Intelligence Records, No. CMP/P-PU-015, as an Exempt Personal
Information Bank

P.C. 1990-322  1990-02-22
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Solicitor General of Canada and the President of the Treasury Board,
is pleased hereby, pursuant to subsection 18(1) of the [Privacy
Act](/eng/acts/P-21), to revoke the [Exempt Personal Information Bank
Order, No. 13 (RCMP)](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-149), made by Order in
Council P.C. 1985-864 of March 14, 1985[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-90-149_e_hq_6118){#fn_SOR-90-149_e_hq_6118-ID0EDBAA},
and to make the annexed Order respecting the designation of the Criminal
Operational Intelligence Records, No. CMP/P-PU-015, as an exempt
personal information bank, in substitution therefor.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921639 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Exempt Personal Information Bank Order, No. 13
(RCMP)](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-149).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921641 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Criminal Operational
Intelligence Records, No. CMP/P-PU-015, a personal information bank
under the control of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, is hereby
designated as an exempt bank on the basis of section 22 of the [Privacy
Act](/eng/acts/P-21).

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 18(3)(b) of the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21), the laws
concerned, in relation to those files that are contained in the exempt
bank referred to in section 2 that consist predominantly of personal
information described in subparagraph 22(1)(a)(ii) of that Act, are the
[Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6),
the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14), the [Food and Drugs
Act](/eng/acts/F-27), the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), the
[Controlled Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8) and the [Foreign
Interference and Security of Information Act](/eng/acts/O-5).
