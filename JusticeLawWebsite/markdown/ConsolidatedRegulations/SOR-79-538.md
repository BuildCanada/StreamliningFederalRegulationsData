## Consolidated Regulations Delivery Regulations {#consolidated-regulations-delivery-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/79-538
:::

[STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS ACT](/eng/acts/S-22)

Registration 1979-07-24

Regulations Prescribing the Persons to Whom Copies of the Consolidated
Regulations of Canada 1978 May Be Delivered without Charge

P.C. 1979-1935 1979-07-19
::::

::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to paragraph 27(k) of the [Statutory
Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22), is pleased hereby to make the annexed
Regulations prescribing the persons to whom copies of the Consolidated
Regulations of Canada, 1978 may be delivered without charge.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865987 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Consolidated Regulations Delivery
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-538).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865989 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Consolidated Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Consolidated Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Consolidated Regulations of Canada 1978; ([Codification des
    règlements]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hard cover copy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hard cover copy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a series of volumes
    containing the Consolidated Regulations, the covers of which are

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} composed of solid binders board of 0.120 inch
      thickness,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} bound with Tanotex of bright finish quality
      Number 8524, colour Number 490 dark blue of Morocco grain finish
      and manufactured by Columbia Finishing Mills Limited, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} lettered in a silver colour; ([copie à couverture
      rigide]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[soft cover copy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[soft cover copy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a series of volumes
    containing the Consolidated Regulations, the covers of which are

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} composed of paper No. 1 coated cover, S.C.,
      C~1~S, coated one side, basis 301M, caliber 0.012, manufactured by
      Domtar Inc., and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} bound using a wraparound process known as
      "perfect binding". ([copies à couverture molle]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

## [Delivery]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865999 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A hard cover copy shall be
delivered without charge to those persons for the time being holding the
offices specified in Schedule I.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A soft cover copy shall be
delivered without charge to the persons for the time being holding the
offices specified in Schedule II for the use of those persons while
holding those offices.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A soft cover copy shall be
delivered without charge, on request, to the persons for the time being
holding the offices or performing the functions specified in Schedule
III for the use of those persons while holding those offices or
performing those functions.
