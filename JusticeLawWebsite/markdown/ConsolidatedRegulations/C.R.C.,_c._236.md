## Prince Edward Island Vegetable Order {#prince-edward-island-vegetable-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 236
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Vegetables Produced in Prince Edward Island
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537201 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Prince Edward Island Vegetable
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._236).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537203 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Prince Edward Island Natural
    Products Marketing Act; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Prince Edward
    Island Vegetable Commodity Marketing Board established pursuant to
    the Act; ([Office de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    vegetables established and amended from time to time pursuant to the
    Act; ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vegetable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vegetable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any turnip produced in the
    Province of Prince Edward Island. ([légumes]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537209 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized to regulate the marketing of vegetables in interprovincial
and export trade and for such purposes may, with respect to persons and
property situated within the Province of Prince Edward Island, exercise
all or any powers like the powers exercisable by it in relation to the
marketing of vegetables locally within that Province under the Act and
the Plan.

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537211 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/80-90\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the powers granted to it by section 3
  with respect to the marketing of vegetables in interprovincial and
  export trade,

to make orders fixing, imposing and collecting levies or charges from
persons situated within the Province of Prince Edward Island engaged in
the production or marketing of vegetables and for such purpose
classifying such persons into groups and fixing the levies or charges
payable by the members of the different groups in different amounts, and
to use such levies or charges for its purposes, including the creation
of reserves, and the payment of expenses and losses resulting from the
sale or disposal of vegetables, and the equalization or adjustment among
producers of vegetables of moneys realized from the sale thereof during
such period or periods of time as the Commodity Board may determine.
