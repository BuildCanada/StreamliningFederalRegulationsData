the Minister of Transport, Infrastructure and Communities, pursuant to
sections 14 and 15 of the [International Bridges and Tunnels
Act](/eng/acts/I-17.05)[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-748757 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Bridge Inspection Manual]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Bridge Inspection Manual]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Bridge
      Inspection Manual --- Bridge Engineering Highways and Bridges,
      published by the Department of Public Works and Government
      Services, the English version of which is dated March 2001 and the
      French version of which is dated October 2005. ([Manuel
      d'inspection des structures]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian Highway Bridge Design Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian Highway Bridge Design Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      standard CAN/CSA-S6-06, Canadian Highway Bridge Design Code,
      November 2006, published by the Canadian Standards Association.
      ([Code canadien sur le calcul des ponts routiers]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[detailed visual inspection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[detailed visual inspection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      element-by-element visual assessment (including hands-on
      inspection of fracture critical members) of the material defects,
      performance deficiencies and maintenance needs of an international
      bridge or tunnel. ([inspection visuelle
      détaillée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[engineer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[engineer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the holder of a licence
      from the [Ordre des ingénieurs du Québec]{.otherLang lang="fr"} or
      an association of professional engineers of a province of Canada
      or state of the United States. ([ingénieur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Highway and Rail Transit Tunnel Inspection Manual]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Highway and Rail Transit Tunnel Inspection
      Manual]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Highway and Rail Transit
      Tunnel Inspection Manual (2005), published by the Federal Highway
      Administration and Federal Transit Administration of the United
      States Department of Transportation. ([Highway and Rail Transit
      Tunnel Inspection Manual]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Manual for Condition Evaluation of Bridges]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Manual for Condition Evaluation of Bridges]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
      means the Manual for Condition Evaluation of Bridges, second
      edition, published by the American Association of State Highway
      and Transportation Officials, including its 2001 and 2003
      revisions. ([Manual for Condition Evaluation of
      Bridges]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Manuel d'évaluation de la capacité portante des structures]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Manuel d'évaluation de la capacité portante des
      structures]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Manuel d'évaluation de
      la capacité portante des structures]{.otherLang lang="fr"},
      December 2005, published by the [ministère des Transports du
      Québec]{.otherLang lang="fr"}. ([Manuel d'évaluation de la
      capacité portante des structures]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Manuel d'inspection des structures --- Évaluation des dommages]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Manuel d'inspection des structures --- Évaluation des
      dommages]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Manuel d'inspection des
      structures --- Évaluation des dommages]{.otherLang lang="fr"},
      December 2004, published by the [ministère des Transports du
      Québec]{.otherLang lang="fr"}, including its November 2005 update.
      ([Manuel d'inspection des structures --- Évaluation des
      dommages]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2012-33, s.
      3\]]{.Repealed}

  [[New York State Bridge Inspection Manual]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[New York State Bridge Inspection Manual]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
      means the Bridge Inspection Manual --- 1997, published by the
      Department of Transportation of the State of New York, including
      Addendum 1 dated April 1999 and Addendum 2 dated November 2001.
      ([Bridge Inspection Manual de l'État de New York]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Ontario Structure Inspection Manual]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Ontario Structure Inspection Manual]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      the Ontario Structure Inspection Manual (OSIM), October 2000,
      published by the Ministry of Transportation of Ontario, including
      its November 2003 and April 2008 revisions. ([Ontario Structure
      Inspection Manual]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[underwater inspection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[underwater inspection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a visual or
      tactual inspection of the submerged structural elements of an
      international bridge, including a scouring inspection of the
      bridge. ([inspection sous-marine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of an inconsistency between a provision

  prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of interpreting a document
  incorporated by reference into these Regulations, "should" shall be
  read to mean "shall".

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-748776 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of the international bridges and tunnels
  listed in the schedule.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of railway
  bridges and tunnels.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Maintenance and Repair]{.HTitleText1} {#h-748780 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-748781 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Part.

[[international bridge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international bridge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bridge, or any
    part of it, that connects any place in Canada to any place outside
    Canada, and includes approaches to the bridge but does not include
    facilities related to the bridge. ([pont
    international]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[international tunnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international tunnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a tunnel, or any
    part of it, that connects any place in Canada to any place outside
    Canada, and includes approaches to the tunnel but does not include
    facilities related to the tunnel. ([tunnel
    international]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[team leader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[team leader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of the
    inspection of an international bridge or international tunnel, an
    engineer who conducts the inspection or who exercises supervisory
    functions over a person who conducts the inspection, and who has

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a bridge, a minimum of five years
      of experience in the design, construction, repair, maintenance and
      inspection of bridges; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a tunnel, a minimum of five years
      of experience in the design, construction, repair, maintenance and
      inspection of tunnels, including their installed electrical,
      communication, mechanical and plumbing systems, and a knowledge of
      the standards and codes applicable to the construction and
      operation of tunnels. ([chef d'équipe]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

### [Inspection]{.HTitleText2} {#h-748788 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An owner
  of an international bridge shall ensure that a detailed visual
  inspection or an underwater inspection of the bridge is conducted in
  the manner set out in the

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Bridge Inspection Manual;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Ontario Structure Inspection Manual;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [Manuel d'inspection des structures --- Évaluation
    des dommages]{.otherLang lang="fr"};

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Manual for Condition Evaluation of Bridges; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} New York State Bridge Inspection Manual.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An owner of an international tunnel shall ensure that
  a detailed visual inspection or an inspection of the electrical,
  communication, mechanical and plumbing systems of the tunnel is
  conducted in the manner set out in the Highway and Rail Transit Tunnel
  Inspection Manual.

### [Frequency of Inspections --- International Bridges]{.HTitleText2} {#h-748797 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An owner
  of an international bridge shall ensure that a detailed visual
  inspection of the bridge is conducted at least once every two years.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An owner of an international bridge shall ensure that
  an underwater inspection of the bridge is conducted at least once
  every five years.

### [Frequency of Inspections --- International Tunnels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-748801 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An owner
  of an international tunnel shall ensure that a detailed visual
  inspection of the tunnel is conducted at least once every year.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An owner of an international tunnel shall ensure that
  an inspection of the electrical, communication, mechanical and
  plumbing systems of the tunnel is conducted at least once every year.

### [Inspection by the Minister]{.HTitleText2} {#h-748805 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may conduct an inspection of an international bridge or
  international tunnel after giving reasonable notice to the owner.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An owner of an international bridge or international
  tunnel shall allow the Minister access to the bridge or tunnel for
  inspection in accordance with the notice.

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-748809 .Part}

### [Obligation to Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-748810 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An owner of an
international bridge or international tunnel shall submit to the
Minister, for each inspection conducted under section 5 or 6, a report
on the condition of the bridge or tunnel.

### [Contents of Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-748812 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An owner
  of an international bridge or international tunnel shall ensure that
  the report referred to in section 8 contains at least the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of the inspection;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the names of the persons who conducted the
    inspection;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the elements of the international bridge or
    international tunnel that were inspected;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an international tunnel, a list of
    the electrical, communication, mechanical and plumbing systems that
    were inspected;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the utilities and their supports whose damage would
    have an impact on the safety of the international bridge or
    international tunnel;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} recommendations in respect of maintenance and
    repair projects, including recommendations as to when such projects
    must be completed and a list of maintenance work to be carried out;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a list of inspections to be conducted following the
    inspection in respect of which the report is prepared, and a
    recommendation as to when those inspections must be completed;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the maintenance work, major repairs and other work
    completed since the previous inspection report; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the special condition surveys, tests and other
    additional engineering investigations into the condition of the
    international bridge or international tunnel completed since the
    previous inspection report.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner shall ensure that the report is accompanied
  by a letter signed and sealed by two engineers, one of whom is the
  team leader, attesting to the correctness of the information in the
  report and providing a statement as to the overall condition of the
  structure.

### [Submission of Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-748827 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The owner of an
international bridge or international tunnel shall submit the report
referred to in section 8 within one month after the day on which it is
approved by the owner but not more than six months after the day on
which the inspection is completed.

### [Additional Engineering Investigations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-748829 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An owner of an
international bridge or international tunnel shall submit to the
Minister a final report signed and sealed by two engineers, relating to
a special condition survey, test or other additional engineering
investigation conducted in respect of the bridge or tunnel, other than
the evaluation referred to in section 12, within one month after the day
on which the report is approved by the owner but not more than six
months after the day on which the additional engineering investigation
is completed.

### [Evaluation of the Load Carrying Capacity of an International Bridge]{.HTitleText2} {#h-748831 .Subheading}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** An owner of an
international bridge shall ensure that an evaluation of the bridge's
capacity to carry traffic loads is conducted in accordance with the
requirements set out in the Canadian Highway Bridge Design Code, the
Manual for Condition Evaluation of Bridges or the [Manuel d'évaluation
de la capacité portante des structures]{.otherLang lang="fr"} when any
of the following occur:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} observed or suspected defects, deterioration or
  damage that may affect load capacity;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an anticipated increase in actual or permitted
  traffic loading or in loading effect;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a change in road classification;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a review of the load limit that is posted;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an alteration to the bridge that may affect its live
  load carrying capacity;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} an application for a permit to allow a vehicle not
  conforming to the load limit to cross the bridge; or

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} an unsatisfactory performance of the bridge in
  respect of serviceability or fatigue.

### [Load Carrying Capacity Evaluation Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-748840 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  owner of an international bridge shall submit to the Minister a report
  on an evaluation of the bridge's capacity to carry traffic loads
  within 60 days after the day on which the evaluation is completed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner of the international bridge shall ensure
  that the report is signed and sealed by two engineers.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Operations and Use]{.HTitleText1} {#h-748847 .Part}

### [Reports]{.HTitleText2} {#h-748848 .Subheading}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), an owner
  of an international bridge or tunnel shall submit to the Minister at
  least once every two years a report related to the operation and use
  of the bridge or tunnel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner of the international bridge or tunnel shall
  ensure

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the report covers a period starting at the end
    of the period covered by the previous report; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the report is submitted within 90 days after
    the end of the period covered by the report.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2012-33, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

### [Contents of Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-748857 .Subheading}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** An owner of an
international bridge or tunnel shall ensure that the report referred to
in section 14 contains at least the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of vehicles of the following types that
  pass over a point or segment of the international bridge or tunnel
  each month:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} passenger cars,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} trucks, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} buses and vehicles other than those referred to
    in subparagraphs (i) and (ii);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the types of vehicles that were permitted to use the
  international bridge or tunnel and the conditions or restrictions
  related to that use;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the tolls, fees or other charges applicable to users
  for the use of the international bridge or tunnel;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a brief description of all written complaints
  received in respect of the operation and use of the international
  bridge or tunnel; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the procedures that the owner has
  put in place to manage complaints from the public relating to the
  operation and use of the international bridge or tunnel.

### [Changes and Closures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-748869 .Subheading}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The owner of an
international bridge or tunnel shall inform the Minister, in writing, of
any of the following events within 30 days of its occurrence:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a change in tolls, fees or other charges;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a change in the types of vehicles permitted to use
  the international bridge or tunnel and, in the case of a newly
  permitted type of vehicle, the conditions or restrictions under which
  that type of vehicle is permitted to use the bridge or tunnel;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a change in the conditions or restrictions under
  which a type of vehicle referred to in the most recent report is
  permitted to cross the international bridge or traverse the
  international tunnel;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a change in normal operating hours; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the closure of the bridge or tunnel, or the closure
  of one of its lanes for a period of 48 hours or more.
