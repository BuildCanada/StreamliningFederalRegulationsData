## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-833000 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[bedding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bedding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means bedding that is composed of
    textile fibres. ([article de literie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[textile products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[textile products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means products that are
    composed of textile fibres, other than bedding. ([produit
    textile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-833005 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to the following products:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} children's sleepwear in sizes up to and including
  14X;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} dolls, plush toys, soft toys and toys intended to be
  entered by a child;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} cribs, cradles and bassinets;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} playpens;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} expansion gates and expandable enclosures for
  children;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} textile floor coverings;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} tents; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} mattresses.

## [Specifications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-833016 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Products without raised fibre surface

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  flame spread time for textile products without a raised fibre surface
  must be greater than 3.5 seconds.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Products with raised fibre surface

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The flame spread time for textile products with a
  raised fibre surface that exhibits ignition or fusion of its base
  fibres must be greater than 4 seconds.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bedding

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The flame spread time for
bedding without a raised fibre surface, or bedding with a raised fibre
surface that exhibits ignition or fusion of its base fibres, must be
greater than 7 seconds.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicable standard

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The flame spread time must
be determined according to the Canadian General Standards Board standard
CAN/CGSB-4.2 No. 27.5, entitled Textile Test Methods: Flame Resistance
--- 45° Angle Test --- One-Second Flame Impingement, as amended from
time to time.
