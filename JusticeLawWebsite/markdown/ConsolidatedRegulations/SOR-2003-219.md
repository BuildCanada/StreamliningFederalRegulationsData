[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the owner or
    operator of a commercial conveyance. ([transporteur
    commercial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any conveyance
    that is used for the commercial transportation of persons or goods
    by air, water or land. ([moyen de transport
    commercial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[crew member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[crew member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person assigned to duty
    on board a commercial conveyance. ([membre
    d'équipage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister\'s representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister\'s representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2016-35, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[reservation system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reservation system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any system, whether
    electronic or manual, that contains information about persons on
    board or expected to be on board a commercial conveyance. ([système
    de réservation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[time of departure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[time of departure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a commercial conveyance that
      carries persons or goods by air, the time of take-off from the
      last point of embarkation of persons before the conveyance arrives
      in Canada; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a commercial conveyance that
      carries persons or goods by water or land, the time of departure
      from the last point of embarkation of persons before the
      conveyance arrives in Canada. ([moment du départ]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of sections 3
to 7 is to prescribe the circumstances, conditions, classes of persons,
information and time and manner for the purposes of section 107.1 of the
Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-35, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 2"){.PITLink}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed circumstances and conditions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The prescribed
circumstances and conditions are the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conveyance must be a commercial conveyance; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the conveyance is expected to arrive in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-35, s. 2
:::

Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed classes

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The prescribed classes of
persons are commercial carriers and charterers that undertake to carry
persons or goods to Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2013-182, s. 2
- SOR/2016-35, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed information

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The prescribed information
about a person on board or expected to be on board a commercial
conveyance is the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} their surname, first name and any middle names, their
  date of birth, their citizenship or nationality and their gender;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the type and number of each passport or other travel
  document that identifies them and the name of the country or entity
  that issued it;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} their reservation record locator number, if any;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the unique passenger reference assigned to them, if
  any, by the person who is required to provide information or, in the
  case of a crew member who has not been assigned a unique passenger
  reference, notice of their status as a crew member;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any information about the person in a reservation
  system of the person who is required to provide information or in a
  reservation system of the representative of such a person; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the following information about their carriage on
  board the commercial conveyance:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the person is carried or is expected to be
    carried on board the commercial conveyance by air, the date and time
    of take-off from the last point of embarkation of persons before the
    commercial conveyance arrives in Canada or if the person is carried
    or is expected to be carried on board the commercial conveyance by
    water or land, the date and time of departure from the last point of
    embarkation of persons before the commercial conveyance arrives in
    Canada,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the last point of embarkation of persons before
    the commercial conveyance arrives in Canada,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of arrival of the commercial
    conveyance at the first point of disembarkation of persons in
    Canada,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the first point of disembarkation of persons in
    Canada, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a commercial conveyance that carries
    persons or goods by air, the flight code identifying the commercial
    carrier and the flight number.

- SOR/2016-35, s. 2

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed manner

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The information referred to
in section 5 must be provided by electronic means in accordance with the
technical requirements, specifications and procedures for electronic
data interchange set out in the document entitled CBSA Carrier Messaging
Requirements established by the Agency, as amended from time to time.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-35, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed time --- advance passenger
information

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  information referred to in paragraphs 5(a) to (d) must be provided not
  later than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one hour before the time of departure, if the
    information relates to a crew member; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the time of departure, if the information relates
    to any other person who is or is expected to be on board the
    commercial conveyance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed time --- information in a
  reservation system

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in paragraph 5(e) must be
  provided not later than at the time of departure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed time --- close-out
  information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in paragraph 5(d) must
  also be provided for each passenger on board the commercial conveyance
  at the time of departure not later than 30 minutes after the time of
  departure.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-35, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Missing or inaccurate information

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who becomes aware before or at the time of departure that information
  they have provided under paragraph 107.1 of the Act is incomplete or
  inaccurate must, in the manner described in section 6 and without
  delay, provide the Minister with the missing or accurate information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- paragraph 5(e)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of
  information referred to in paragraph 5(e).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-35, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of transmission --- paragraph 5(f)

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The information referred to
in paragraph 5(f) must be provided at the same time as the information
referred to in sections 7 and 8.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-35, s. 2
:::
