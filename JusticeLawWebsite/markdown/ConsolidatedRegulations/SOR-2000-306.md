Ottawa, July 4, 2000

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food, pursuant to subsection
8(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_SOR-2000-306_e_hq_5599){#fn_SOR-2000-306_e_hq_5599-ID0EGAAA}
of the [Canada Agricultural Products Act](/eng/acts/C-0.4)[[Footnote

hereby approves the making of the annexed Rules of the Board of
Arbitration (Agriculture and Agri-Food) by the Board of Arbitration.
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663455 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this section apply in these Rules.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Agricultural
      Products Act](/eng/acts/C-0.4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[complainant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[complainant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who files a
      complaint under subsection 9(1) of the
      Act. ([plaignant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[defendant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[defendant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person against whom a
      complaint is filed under subsection 9(1) of the
      Act. ([défendeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a complainant, a defendant or
      an intervenor. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Secretary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Secretary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Secretary of the
      Board. ([secrétaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Rules are to be liberally construed in order to
  permit the fairest, least expensive and most expeditious way of
  resolving complaints.

## [Rules of General Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663466 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedural matters not provided for

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** If any question of
procedure arises during a proceeding that is not covered, or not fully
covered, in these Rules, the Board must decide the question in a manner
that is consistent with these Rules.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unfairness

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** If the application of any
of these Rules would cause unfairness to a party during the complaint
process, the Board may waive compliance with that rule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Technical defects

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A defect in form or a
technical irregularity may be overlooked by the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculating periods

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In calculating periods
under these Rules, all days must be counted except that, if the time
ends on a Saturday, Sunday or other statutory holiday, the time must be
extended until the next business day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of time limits

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Board may extend the
time limits fixed in these Rules either before or after the end of the
time limits fixed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public nature of filed documents

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document filed with the Board by a party must be treated as a public
  document unless the party requests that the document be treated as
  confidential.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for confidential treatment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Reasons must be given for the request that a document
  be given confidential treatment and, if it is alleged that disclosure
  would cause harm to the party, the reasons must include details of the
  nature and extent of the harm.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents to be in duplicate

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  documents sent to the Board must be sent in duplicate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submitting documents to the Board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All documents required to be submitted to the Board
  must be submitted by hand or by mail, registered mail, courier or
  facsimile.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Faxes sent to the Board

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The original and a copy of any document sent by
  facsimile must be sent by mail without delay after the facsimile
  transmission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filed information to be sent to parties

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Board is responsible
for ensuring that copies of all documents submitted to it by a party are
sent to the other parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communications by the Board

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Written communications by
the Board may be delivered by hand or sent by registered mail, courier
or facsimile.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of address or fax number

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A party must without
delay notify the Board of a change of facsimile number or address.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Teleconference

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Board may meet by
teleconference for all purposes except for conducting hearings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation of parties

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A party may be
represented by counsel or by an agent authorized in writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[In camera]{.otherLang lang="la"}
hearings

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  hearing before the Board may, on the request of any complainant or
  defendant, be held [in camera]{.otherLang lang="la"} if that party
  establishes to the satisfaction of the Board that the circumstances of
  the case so require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of witnesses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may order a witness at a hearing to be
  excluded from the hearing until called to give evidence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taking notice

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Board may take notice
of any matter in order to expedite any proceeding.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Impartiality of Board member

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  party is of the opinion that a member of the Board is not in a
  position to act impartially, that party must without delay notify the
  Board in writing, stating the reason for that opinion.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of Board member

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Board is of the opinion that the reason given
  by that party is valid, the Board must exclude the member from
  consideration of the complaint.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** If the Board is of the
opinion that a member of the Board is in a conflict of interest in
relation to a complaint, the Board must exclude the member from
consideration of the complaint.

## [Complaint]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663513 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing complaint

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A complaint must be filed
with the Secretary together with copies of all relevant documents and a
statement as to whether or not the complainant requests that a hearing
be held.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaint to be signed

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The complaint must be
signed by the complainant or the complainant's counsel or agent. If an
agent signs the complaint, a copy of the complainant's written
authorization of the agent must be filed with the complaint.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of complaint

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The complaint must
contain the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a precise statement of the facts and the grounds on
  which the complaint is based and, if possible, stating the provision
  of the regulations made under the Act that the defendant allegedly
  failed to comply with;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone and facsimile numbers of
  the defendant and of any agent representing the defendant in the
  transaction;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the quantity, quality or grade of each kind of
  agricultural product shipped;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the dates of shipment and arrival;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the identification of any shipping vehicles;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the shipping and destination points; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the order requested and the amount of
  compensation requested.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Secretary, after reviewing the complaint and all supporting
  documentation, may require the complainant to file additional
  information within the period fixed by the Secretary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consequence of not providing
  information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the complainant does not provide the additional
  information requested by the Secretary within the period fixed by the
  Secretary and fails to provide a reasonable excuse for the
  non-compliance, the Board may proceed with the complaint based on the
  information already filed with it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inappropriate complaints

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** If the Board is of the
opinion that the complaint on its face is frivolous or vexatious or does
not disclose a valid basis, the Board must declare the complaint
inappropriate for consideration by the Board and so advise the
complainant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of defendant

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Unless the complaint is
declared inappropriate for consideration by the Board, the Secretary
must send to the defendant a copy of the complaint and copies of all
documents submitted with it.

## [Disputing the Complaint]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663536 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing a defence

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  30 days after the date on which the Secretary sends a copy of the
  complaint to the defendant, the defendant may file a defence with the
  Secretary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents to be filed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The defence must be accompanied by copies of all
  relevant documents not already filed by the complainant and a
  statement as to whether or not the defendant requests that a hearing
  be held.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of defence

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The defence must contain

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a precise statement of the facts that constitute the
  grounds of the defence; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating which facts in the complaint
  are admitted, denied, or of which the defendant has no knowledge.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing a counterclaim

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  complainant is a dealer licensed under the Act, the defendant may
  also, within 30 days after the date on which the Secretary sends a
  copy of the complaint to the defendant, file a counterclaim against
  the complainant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents to be filed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The counterclaim must be accompanied by copies of all
  relevant documents not already filed by the complainant or filed by
  the defendant in support of the defence and a statement as to whether
  or not the defendant requests that a hearing be held.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of counterclaim

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** A counterclaim must
contain the same information that is required for a complaint.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence and counterclaim to be signed

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The defence and any
counterclaim must be signed by the defendant or the defendant's counsel
or agent. If an agent signs the defence or counterclaim, a copy of the
defendant's written authorization of the agent must be filed with it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of complainant

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The Secretary must send
to the complainant a copy of the defence and of the counterclaim, if
any, and copies of all documents submitted with them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Secretary, after examining the defence, the counterclaim, if any, and
  any supporting documents, may require the defendant to file additional
  information within the period fixed by the Secretary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consequence of not providing
  information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the defendant does not provide the additional
  information requested by the Secretary within the period fixed by the
  Secretary and fails to provide a reasonable excuse for the
  non-compliance, the Board may proceed with the complaint based on the
  information already filed with it.

## [Intervention]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663562 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request to intervene

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** At any time after the
Secretary sends the complaint to the defendant, and before a hearing is
scheduled for the complaint, any person, other than the complainant or
the defendant, who has a direct financial interest in the proceedings
may request to intervene by sending the Secretary a request in writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of request

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  request to intervene must describe the person's financial interest in
  the proceedings, contain a precise statement of the facts and grounds
  that form the basis of the intervention and be accompanied by copies
  of any relevant documents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request to be signed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request must be signed by the person or the
  person's counsel or agent. If an agent signs the request, a copy of
  the person's written authorization of the agent must be filed with it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request accepted

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** If the Board is satisfied
that the person has a direct financial interest in the proceedings and
that the intervention would be useful to the proceedings, the Secretary
must authorize the intervention and send to the other parties copies of
the material submitted by the person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request denied

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** If the Board does not
authorize the intervention of a person, the Secretary must return the
material to that person.

## [Consideration of Complaint]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663574 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of hearing

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** If the complainant or
defendant has requested a hearing, the Secretary must set a date, time
and place for the hearing and must, in writing, notify the parties of
that date, time and place.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confirmation of presence

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  30 days after a party receives the notice of hearing, the party must
  notify the Board in writing as to whether they intend to attend the
  hearing or whether they intend to present evidence and make arguments
  in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement of evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party that indicates that they will attend the
  hearing, must also, within 30 days after receiving the notice of
  hearing, provide the Board with a written statement of the names of
  the party's witnesses and the general nature of their evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statements to be given to parties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Secretary must send a copy of the statement
  provided by a party under subsection (2) to the other parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for summons

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  30 days after a party receives the notice of hearing, the party may
  request that the Board issue a summons requiring any person to appear
  at the hearing to give evidence, or to produce any documents, relative
  to the subject-matter before the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of the request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request must indicate why the evidence or
  documents would be relative to the subject-matter before the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to issue summons

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** The Board may refuse to
issue a summons at the request of the party if the Board considers that
the evidence or documents would not be relative to the subject-matter
before the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-hearing conference

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  hearing is to be held and the Board is of the opinion that a
  pre-hearing conference might expedite the proceedings, the Board may
  request the Secretary to arrange a pre-hearing conference, to take
  place either by telephone or by the parties appearing before the
  Secretary, in order to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} simplify the issues;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} clarify the facts that are admitted and denied, or
    consider any amendment to the pleadings;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} determine the procedure to be followed at the
    hearing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} exchange any further documents proposed to be given
    in evidence at the hearing; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} attend to any other matters that may aid in the
    simplification of the evidence and the expeditious disposition of
    the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summary to be prepared

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} After any pre-hearing conference, the Secretary must
  summarize the matters that were agreed to by the parties and send a
  copy of the summary to them and to the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on communication

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No communication may be made to the Board with
  respect to any statement made at the pre-hearing conference except as
  disclosed in the summary.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to appear

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** If a party does not
appear at a hearing and the Board is satisfied that notice of the
hearing was sent to the party in accordance with section 35, the Board
may proceed in the party's absence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Postponements and adjournments

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** A hearing may be
postponed or adjourned by the Board from time to time on any terms that
the Board considers appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure if no agreement

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** Sections 43 to 46 apply
to govern the procedure at the hearing to the extent that the procedure
for the hearing has not been determined in a pre-hearing conference or
by agreement of the parties at the beginning of the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complainant's presentation

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** At the beginning of the
hearing, the complainant may present their case by first summarizing it
and then presenting their evidence and making their arguments.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defendant's presentation

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** After the complainant
presents their case, the defendant may present their case by first
summarizing it and then presenting their evidence and making their
arguments.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intervenor's presentation

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** After the complainant and
the defendant have presented their cases, any intervenor is entitled to
give evidence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summations

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** After all the evidence is
presented, the parties may make summations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination, cross-examination and
re-examination

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  complainant and the defendant are each entitled to examine their own
  witnesses, to cross-examine a witness of another party and to
  re-examine their own witness for clarification.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intervenor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An intervenor may be cross-examined by the
  complainant or the defendant but is not entitled to cross-examine them
  or their witnesses.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination of witnesses

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** Witnesses at the hearing
shall be examined orally on oath or affirmation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board in control of hearing

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** Any member of the Board
may intervene at any time and the Board may make any orders that it
considers necessary for the proper conduct of the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affidavit evidence

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** Affidavit evidence is not
admissible without the consent of the party against whom the affidavit
evidence is tendered.

## [The Decision of the Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663628 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision after a hearing

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  hearing is held, the Board may render its decision orally at the end
  of the hearing or it may render its decision at a later date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision without a hearing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a hearing is not held, the Board must render its
  decision after its deliberations based on the material submitted by
  the parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication of the decision

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  the Board renders its decision, the Secretary must send to each party

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the decision, if the Board rendered
    its decision orally; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the decision, if the Board rendered its
    decision in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signature required

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The summary or decision and the reasons, if any, must
  be signed by one of the Board members who took part in rendering the
  decision.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663641 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** These Rules come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
