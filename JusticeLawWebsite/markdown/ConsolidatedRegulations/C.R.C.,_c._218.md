## Ontario Onion Order {#ontario-onion-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 218
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Onions Produced in Ontario
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536853 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Onion Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._218).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536855 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing
    Board of Ontario established under the Act;
    ([Régie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Onion
    Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act; ([Office
    de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[onions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[onions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means onions produced in Ontario;
    ([oignons]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Provincial Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Provincial Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Ontario
    Regulation 129/66 as amended by Ontario Regulations 111/67, 238/67
    and 279/69, made by the Board pursuant to the Act. ([règlement
    provincial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536862 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Board and the Commodity
Board are each authorized to regulate the marketing of onions in
interprovincial and export trade and for such purposes may, with respect
to persons and property situated within the Province of Ontario,
exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable by the Board
that, pursuant to section 3 of the Provincial Regulations, have not been
delegated to the Commodity Board and exercisable by the Commodity Board
under sections 4, 5, 6 and 11 of the Provincial Regulations in relation
to the marketing of onions locally within that Province.
