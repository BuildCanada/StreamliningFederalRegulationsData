## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726473 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Trust and Loan Companies
Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8).

## [Civil Remedies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726475 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 276(1)(d) of the Act, the prescribed percentage of voting
rights attached to all of the trust and loan company's outstanding
shares is 10%.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-88, s. 1(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2-20061128.html "Link to previous version of section 2"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 276(3) of the Act, [[take-over bid]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
means a take-over bid under any legislation that is set out in column 2
of the schedule.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 276(6)(c) of the Act, the prescribed circumstances are that
the insider

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} entered into the purchase or sale of the security as
  an agent pursuant to a specific unsolicited order to purchase or sell;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} made the purchase or sale of the security pursuant to
  participation in an automatic dividend reinvestment plan, share
  purchase plan or other similar automatic plan that the insider entered
  into before the acquisition of the confidential information;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} made the purchase or sale of the security to fulfil a
  legally binding obligation that the insider entered into before the
  acquisition of the confidential information; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} purchased or sold the security as agent or trustee in
  the circumstances described in paragraph (b) or (c).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726485 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
