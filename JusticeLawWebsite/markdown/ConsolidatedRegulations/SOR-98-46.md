## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024269 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

## [Prescribed Classes of Persons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024271 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
hereby prescribed as classes of persons for the purposes of section 32.2
of the Act, where a declaration of tariff classification is rendered
incorrect by a failure referred to in subsection 32.2(6) of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} persons who purchase or otherwise acquire the
  imported goods after the goods are accounted for under subsection
  32(1), (3) or (5) of the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} persons who sell or otherwise dispose of the imported
  goods after the goods are accounted for under subsection 32(1), (3)
  or (5) of the Act.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024275 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on January 1, 1998.
