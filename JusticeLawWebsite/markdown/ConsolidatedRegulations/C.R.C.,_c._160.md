## Manitoba Turkey Order {#manitoba-turkey-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 160
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Turkeys Produced in Manitoba
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-531532 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Manitoba Turkey Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._160).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-531534 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Natural Products Marketing Act
    of Manitoba; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/82-669, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Manitoba Turkey
    Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act; ([Office
    de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    turkeys, established and amended from time to time pursuant to the
    Act; ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a turkey of any variety, grade
    or class raised or kept for slaughter within the Province of
    Manitoba. ([dindon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-669, s. 1
:::

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-531543 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized to regulate the marketing of turkeys in interprovincial and
export trade and for such purposes may, by order, with respect to
persons and property situated within the Province of Manitoba, exercise
all or any powers like the powers exercisable by it in relation to the
marketing of turkeys locally within that Province under the Act and
Plan, other than those powers that are exercised by the Canadian Turkey
Marketing Agency pursuant to the Farm Products Marketing Agencies Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-669, s. 2
:::

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-531547 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/80-90\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the powers granted to it by section 3
  with respect to the marketing of turkeys in interprovincial and export
  trade,

to make orders, fixing, imposing and collecting levies or charges from
persons situated within the Province of Manitoba who are engaged in the
production or marketing of turkeys and for such purpose classifying such
persons into groups and fixing the levies or charges payable by the
members of different groups in different amounts and the Commodity Board
may use such levies or charges for its purposes, including the creation
of reserves and the payment of expenses and losses resulting from the
sale or disposal of turkeys and the equalization or adjustment among the
producers of turkeys of moneys realized from the sale thereof during
such period or periods of time as it may determine.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/80-90
