## Imported Goods Records Regulations {#imported-goods-records-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-1011
:::

[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

Registration 1986-10-02

Imported Goods Records Regulations

P.C. 1986-2260 1986-10-02
::::

::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 164(3) of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), a copy of proposed Regulations, substantially in
the form annexed hereto, was published in Part I of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) on March 15, 1986 and a reasonable
opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to make
representations with respect thereto to the Minister of National
Revenue;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to
subsection 40(1) and paragraph 164(1)(i) of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EGBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed Regulations respecting the keeping of records by
persons who import goods or cause goods to be imported for sale or for
any industrial, occupational, commercial, institutional or other like
use, effective on the coming into force of subsection 40(1) and
paragraph 164(1)(i) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EBBAA}.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote

  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888286 .Part}

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods imported into
    Canada for sale or for any industrial, occupational, commercial,
    institutional or other like use. ([marchandises
    commerciales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[CSA importer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CSA importer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Accounting for Imported Goods and Payment of
    Duties Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1062). ([importateur
    PAD]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-554, s. 1
- SOR/2005-384, s. 3

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888293 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who is required by subsection 40(1) of the Act to keep records
  in respect of commercial goods shall keep, for the period of six years
  following the importation of the commercial goods, all records that
  relate to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the origin, marking, purchase, importation, costs
    and value of the commercial goods;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} payment for the commercial goods;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the sale or other disposal of the commercial goods
    in Canada; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any application for an advance ruling made under
    section 43.1 of the Act in respect of the commercial goods.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the requirements of subsection (1),
  every CSA importer shall keep, for the period of six years following
  the importation of the commercial goods,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} records that provide a description and the quantity
    of the commercial goods;

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} records relating to the reception of the
    commercial goods;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} records showing the credits and adjustments used to
    determine the amount paid for the commercial goods

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} records relating to the accounting for the
    commercial goods;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} records relating to any refund, drawback or
    re-determination of duties;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a list of vendors and consignees of the commercial
    goods; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} records relating to the information needed to
    complete the form entitled "CSA Revenue Summary" in respect of the
    commercial goods and to the payment of duties on those goods.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In addition to the records
referred to in section 2, a person who imports or causes to be imported
commercial goods that have been released free of duty or at a reduced
rate of duty because of their intended use or because they were intended
to be used by a specific person shall keep, for the same period of time
referred to in that section,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a certificate or other record signed by the user of
  the commercial goods that shows the user's name, address and
  occupation and indicates the actual use made of the commercial goods;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the commercial goods have been
  released free of duty under tariff item No. 9948.00.00 in the List of
  Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), an attestation signed by the person who
  imports the goods or causes them to be imported indicating the
  person's name, address and occupation and the intended use of the
  commercial goods, sufficient to confirm that the goods fall within the
  description of goods corresponding to that tariff item; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the commercial goods have been
  diverted to a use other than that on the basis of which they were
  released free of duty or at a reduced rate of duty or have been sold
  or otherwise disposed of to a person not entitled to have the
  commercial goods so released, records that contain information
  sufficient to confirm that the full applicable duties have been paid.

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 40(3) of the Act, commercial goods are the prescribed goods
and every person who is required to keep records under that subsection
shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the person has been issued a licence under
  section 24 of the Act for the operation of a place as a sufferance
  warehouse, keep all records that contain information concerning the
  receipt of commercial goods into it, and the removal of those
  commercial goods from it, for the period of six years after the
  receipt of the commercial goods into it;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the person has been issued a licence under
  section 24 of the Act for the operation of a place as a duty free
  shop, keep all records that relate to commercial goods received into
  it, for the period of six years after the sale or other disposal of
  those commercial goods, including all records that contain information
  concerning

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the accounting for the commercial goods,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the sale or disposal of the commercial goods from
    the duty free shop,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the actual selling price of the commercial goods
    in the duty free shop and the manufacturer's suggested retail price,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the amount that the person paid for the commercial
    goods,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the control of inventory in the duty free shop,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the composition of the inventory in the duty free
    shop,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the remittance of fees to the Department of
    National Revenue, or

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the size of the display area allocated in the
    duty free shop to the commercial goods;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the person has been issued a licence under
  subsection 91(1) of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) for the
  operation of a bonded warehouse, keep all records that relate to
  commercial goods received into it and commercial goods removed from
  it, for the period of six years after their removal, including all
  records that contain information

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} describing the commercial goods when they are
    received into or exported from the bonded warehouse,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} accounting for the commercial goods when they are
    removed from the bonded warehouse,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} concerning inventory and the transactions that
    occur while the commercial goods are in the bonded warehouse,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} concerning the transfer of ownership in respect of
    the commercial goods,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} concerning the transfer of the commercial goods to
    another bonded warehouse, to a sufferance warehouse or to a duty
    free shop, or

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} concerning the unpacking, packing, manipulation or
    alteration of the commercial goods, or any combining of those
    commercial goods with other goods; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the person has been issued a certificate under
  subsection 90(1) of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), keep all
  records that relate to commercial goods in respect of which relief
  from the payment of duties has been granted under section 89 of that
  Act, for the period of six years after the relief is granted,
  including all records that contain information concerning

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the importation of the commercial goods,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the processing in Canada of the commercial goods,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of relief granted on the commercial
    goods,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the sale or transfer, between persons to whom
    certificates have been issued, of the commercial goods,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the payment, under subsection 118(1) of the
    [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), of an amount equal to the
    amount of the duties on any of the commercial goods that have been
    transferred, sold or disposed of, or

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the inventory in Canada of the commercial goods.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The records referred to in
sections 2 to 3.1 shall be kept in such a manner as to enable an officer
to perform detailed audits of the records and to obtain or verify the
information on which a determination of the amount of the duties paid,
payable, deferred, refunded or relieved was made.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-67, s. 3(F)
- SOR/96-31, s. 3
- SOR/2006-153, s. 3
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The records referred to in
sections 2 to 3.1 may be copied by means of any photographic,
microphotographic or image processing process that is in accordance with
National Standard of Canada CAN/CGSB-72.11-93, Microfilm and Electronic
Imaging as Documentary Evidence, published by the Canadian General
Standards Board in November 1993, as amended from time to time, and kept
in that format for the period prescribed in those sections.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-67, s. 4
- SOR/96-31, s. 3
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The records referred to in
sections 2 to 3.1 may be kept electronically or on machine-sensible data
media if the electronic file or media can be related back to the source
documents and is supported by a system capable of producing accessible
and readable copy.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-554, s. 3

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888352 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where a person, other than
a person referred to in section 3.1, has not kept records in accordance
with these Regulations, preferential tariff treatment under NAFTA,
preferential tariff treatment under CCFTA or preferential tariff
treatment under CCRFTA, as the case may be, may be denied or withdrawn
in respect of the commercial goods that are the subject of those
records.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-554, s. 3
- SOR/97-329, s. 1
- SOR/2004-125, s. 1
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where a person, other than
a person referred to in section 3.1, who is required to produce records
in accordance with subsection 43(1) of the Act fails to do so,
preferential tariff treatment under NAFTA, preferential tariff treatment
under CCFTA or preferential tariff treatment under CCRFTA, as the case
may be, may be denied or withdrawn in respect of the commercial goods
that are the subject of those records.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-554, s. 3
- SOR/97-329, s. 1
- SOR/2004-125, s. 1
:::
