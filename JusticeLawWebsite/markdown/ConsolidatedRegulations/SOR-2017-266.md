## Critical Habitat of the Northern Abalone (Haliotis kamtschatkana) Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-northern-abalone-haliotis-kamtschatkana-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2017-266
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2017-12-04

Critical Habitat of the Northern Abalone (Haliotis kamtschatkana) Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Northern Abalone ([Haliotis kamtschatkana]{.otherLang
lang="la"}) is a wildlife species that is listed as an endangered
species in Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a1){#fn_a1-ID0EBBJA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a1-ID0EBBJA){#fn_a1}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans and the Minister
responsible for the Parks Canada Agency, namely the Minister of the
Environment, are the competent ministers in respect of the critical
habitat of that species for the purposes of the annexed Order and have
together prepared an action plan for that species;

Whereas the action plan that identified the critical habitat of that
species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

Whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is
specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection
58(2)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b2){#fn_b2-ID0EBBGA} of that
Act;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b2-ID0EBBGA){#fn_b2}S.C.
  2015, c. 10, s. 60
  :::

And whereas the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans and the Minister of the
Environment are of the opinion that the annexed Order would affect areas
in respect of which wildlife management boards are authorized by lands
claims agreements to perform functions in respect of wildlife species
and, pursuant to subsection 58(8) of that Act, have consulted the
wildlife management boards in question with respect to the Order;

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans and the Minister of the
Environment, pursuant to subsections 58(4) and (5) of the [Species at
Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a1){#fn_a1-ID0EDAEA}, make the annexed
[Critical Habitat of the Northern Abalone (Haliotis kamtschatkana)
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2017-266).

Ottawa, November 30, 2017

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Northern Abalone ([Haliotis kamtschatkana]{.otherLang
lang="la"}), which is identified in the action plan for that species
that is included in the Species at Risk Public Registry.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
