## Vessel Traffic Services Zones Regulations {#vessel-traffic-services-zones-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-98
:::

[CANADA SHIPPING ACT, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15)

Registration 1989-02-09

Regulations Respecting Vessel Traffic Services Zones

P.C. 1989-146 1989-02-09
::::

::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 635.2[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-89-98_e_hq_3767){#fn_SOR-89-98_e_hq_3767-ID0EHBBA}
of the [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9), a copy of the proposed
[Regulations respecting Vessel Traffic Services
Zones](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-98), substantially in the form annexed
hereto, was published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/)
Part I, on July 2, 1988 and a reasonable opportunity was thereby
afforded to ship owners, masters, seamen and other interested persons to
make representations to the Minister of Transport with respect thereto;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-89-98_e_hq_3767-ID0EHBBA){#fn_SOR-89-98_e_hq_3767}S.C.
  1987, c. 7, s. 75
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to
sections 635.15[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-89-98_e_hq_3767){#fn_SOR-89-98_e_hq_3767-ID0EJAAA}
and 635.16[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-89-98_e_hq_3767){#fn_SOR-89-98_e_hq_3767-ID0EHAAA}
of the [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9), is pleased hereby to make
the annexed [Regulations respecting Vessel Traffic Services
Zones](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-98), effective on the day on which
sections 73 to 76 of An Act to amend the Canada Shipping Act and to
amend the Arctic Waters Pollution Prevention Act, the Maritime Code Act
and the Oil and Gas Production and Conservation Act in consequence
thereof, being chapter 7 of the Statutes of Canada, 1987, come into
force.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921087 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Vessel Traffic Services Zones
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-98).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921089 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Shipping
    Act](/eng/acts/S-9); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[berth]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[berth]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a wharf, pier, lock,
    anchorage or mooring buoy; ([poste]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[departure manoeuvre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[departure manoeuvre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an operation
    during which a vessel leaves a berth and gets safely under way;
    ([manoeuvre de départ]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[ECAREG]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ECAREG]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Eastern Canada Vessel
    Traffic Services Zone as described in section 4 of the [Eastern
    Canada Vessel Traffic Services Zone
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-99); ([ECAREG]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[manoeuvre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[manoeuvre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any ship movement, and
    includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a compass adjustment,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the calibration and servicing of navigational
      aids,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a sea trial,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a dredging operation, and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the laying, picking up and servicing of submarine
      cables; ([manoeuvre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[marine traffic regulator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marine traffic regulator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
    designated by the Commissioner of the Canadian Coast Guard pursuant
    to subsection 562.18(2) of the Act; ([régulateur de trafic
    maritime]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pollutant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pollutant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 654 of the Act; ([polluant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[VTS OFFSHORE]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[VTS OFFSHORE]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Western Canada Vessel
    Traffic Services Zones as described in the Annual Edition of Notice
    to Mariners, TP 390, published by the Department of Transport. ([VTS
    OFFSHORE]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-214, s. 1
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921106 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), these Regulations apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} every ship 20 metres or more in length;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} every ship engaged in towing or pushing any vessel
    or object, other than fishing gear, where

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the combined length of the ship and any vessel or
      object towed or pushed by the ship is 45 metres or more in length,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the length of the vessel or object being towed
      or pushed by the ship is 20 metres or more in length.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a ship engaged in towing or pushing any vessel or
    object within a log booming ground;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a pleasure yacht that is less than 30 m in length;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a fishing vessel that is less than 24 m in length
    and not more than 150 tons gross tonnage.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-214, s. 2
:::

## [Vessel Traffic Services Zones]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921119 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, the Vessel Traffic Services Zones referred to in
section 562.16 of the Act are the zones set out in column I of the items
of the schedule.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-214, s. 3
:::

## [Communications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921123 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a ship shall ensure that,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the ship enters a Vessel Traffic Service
    Zone set out in column I of an item of the schedule, the ship's
    radio equipment is capable of receiving and transmitting radio
    communications on the channel set out in column 5 of an item of
    Table I of the Vessel Traffic Services Zones Regulatory
    Specifications, TP 8771, published by the Canadian Coast Guard, as
    amended from time to time, and on the radio frequency set out in
    column 6 of that item of that Table; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the ship is in a Zone referred to in
    paragraph (a), a continuous listening watch is maintained on the
    channel and on the radio frequency referred to in that paragraph on
    radio equipment located

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at any place on board the ship, where the ship is
      at anchor or moored to a buoy, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the vicinity of the ship's conning position,
      where the ship is under way.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The listening watch referred to in paragraph (1)(b)
  may be suspended if a marine traffic regulator directs the ship to
  communicate with coast stations and other ships on a different channel
  and radio frequency.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-214, s. 4
:::

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921133 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a ship shall ensure that a report is made to a marine
  traffic regulator

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least 15 minutes before the ship

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} enters a Vessel Traffic Services Zone, except
      where the ship has been given a traffic clearance in accordance
      with section 562.18 of the Act, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/96-214, s. 5\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} commences a manoeuvre in a Vessel Traffic
      Services Zone that may be detrimental to safe navigation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as soon as practicable after the ship arrives at a
    berth in a Vessel Traffic Services Zone;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} immediately before commencing a departure manoeuvre
    in a Vessel Traffic Services Zone;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} when the ship arrives at a calling-in point
    described in column 4 of an item of Table II of the Vessel Traffic
    Services Zones Regulatory Specifications, TP 8771, published by the
    Canadian Coast Guard, as amended from time to time;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} as soon as practicable after the manoeuvre referred
    to in subparagraph (a)(iii) is completed; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} immediately after completing the departure
    manoeuvre referred to in paragraph (c).

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The master of a ship shall ensure that a report is
  made to a marine traffic regulator at least 24 hours before the ship
  enters a Vessel Traffic Services Zone from seaward, or as soon as
  possible where the estimated time of arrival at that Vessel Traffic
  Services Zone is less than 24 hours after the ship departs from the
  last port of call, where the ship is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} of 500 tons gross tonnage or more;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} engaged in towing or pushing a vessel, where the
    combined tonnage of the ship and the vessel being towed or pushed is
    500 tons gross tonnage or more; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} carrying a pollutant or dangerous goods, or engaged
    in towing or pushing a vessel carrying a pollutant or dangerous
    goods.

- [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} A report required under subsection (1.1) shall
  specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the ship;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the radio call sign of the ship;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the master of the ship;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the position of the ship;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the time of arrival of the ship at the position;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the course of the ship, if any;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the speed of the ship, if any;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the prevailing weather conditions;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the estimated time that the ship will enter the
    Vessel Traffic Services Zone;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the name of the Vessel Traffic Services Zone the
    ship intends to enter;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the destination of the ship;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the estimated time of arrival of the ship at the
    destination;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} the intended route of the ship;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} the name of the last port of call of the ship;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} the draught of the ship;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} any dangerous goods, listed by class, or pollutant,
    that is carried on board the ship or the vessel being towed or
    pushed by the ship;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} any defect in the ship's hull, main propulsion
    systems or steering systems, radars, compasses, radio equipment,
    anchors or cables;

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} any discharge, or threat of discharge, into the
    water, of a pollutant from the ship or the vessel being towed or
    pushed by the ship, and any damage to the ship or vessel that may
    result in the discharge of a pollutant from the ship or vessel into
    the water;

  - [(s)]{.lawlabel} the name of the Canadian or United States agent of
    the ship; and

  - [(t)]{.lawlabel} the date of expiration of a certificate referred to
    in Article VII of the International Convention on Civil Liability
    for Oil Pollution Damage, 1969, the International Oil Pollution
    Prevention Certificate, the International Pollution Prevention
    Certificate for the Carriage of Noxious Liquid Substances in Bulk,
    the Certificate of Fitness and the Certificate of Compliance, if
    any, issued to the ship.

- [(1.3)]{.lawlabel} A report required under subsection (1.1) shall be
  addressed to ECAREG in Eastern Canada, and to VTS OFFSHORE in Western
  Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A report required in a case referred to in
  subparagraph (1)(a)(i) shall specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the ship;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the radio call sign of the ship;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the position of the ship;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the estimated time that the ship will enter the
    Vessel Traffic Services Zone;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the destination of the ship;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the estimated time that the ship will arrive at its
    destination; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} whether any pollutant or dangerous goods cargo is
    carried on board the ship or any vessel or object being towed or
    pushed by the ship.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A report required under paragraph (1)(c) shall
  specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the ship;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the radio call sign of the ship;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the position of the ship;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the estimated time that the ship will depart the
    berth;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the destination of the ship;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the estimated time that the ship will arrive at its
    destination; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} whether any pollutant or dangerous goods cargo is
    carried on board the ship or any vessel or object being towed or
    pushed by the ship.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A report required in a case referred to in
  subparagraph (1)(a)(iii) shall specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the ship;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the position of the ship; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manoeuvre that the ship is about to commence.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A report required in a case referred to in
  paragraph 1(b) shall specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the ship; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the position of the ship.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A report required under paragraph (1)(d) or (f) shall
  specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the ship;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the position of the ship; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the estimated time that the ship will arrive at the
    next location where a report is required by these Regulations to be
    made.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} A report required in the case referred to in
  paragraph (1)(e) shall describe the manoeuvre referred to in
  subparagraph (1)(a)(iii).

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the information referred to in
  subsection (6), when a vessel arrives at a calling-in point described
  in column 4 of an item of Table II of the Vessel Traffic Services
  Zones Regulatory Specifications, TP 8771, published by the Canadian
  Coast Guard, as amended from time to time, the master of the ship
  shall report the name of the calling-in point as set out in column 2
  of that item or the number of the calling-in point as set out in
  column 3 of that item.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-214, s. 5
:::

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a ship that is within or about to enter a Vessel Traffic
  Services Zone shall ensure that a report is made to a marine traffic
  regulator on any of the following matters as soon as the master
  becomes aware of them:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the occurrence on board the ship of any fire;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the involvement of the ship in any collision,
    grounding or striking;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any defect in the ship's hull, main propulsion
    systems or steering systems, radars, compasses, radio equipment,
    anchors or cables, where a report is not made under
    subsection 6(1.1);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any discharge or probable discharge of a pollutant
    from the ship into the water, where a report is not made under
    subsection 6(1.1);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/96-214, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} another ship in apparent difficulty;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any obstruction to navigation;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any aid to navigation that is functioning
    improperly, damaged, off-position or missing;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the presence of any pollutant in the water;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the presence of a ship that may impede the movement
    of other ships; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} any ice and weather conditions that are detrimental
    to safe navigation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A report made pursuant to subsection (1) shall
  include the name and position of the ship.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-214, s. 6
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The master of a ship that
is within or about to enter a Vessel Traffic Services Zone shall ensure
that a report is made to a marine traffic regulator describing any
significant change in the information contained in a report made
pursuant to these Regulations.

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), where any report made under these Regulations
  requires a time to be specified, the time shall be specified using the
  time zone in effect in the Vessel Traffic Services Zone that the ship
  is about to enter or is within, using the 24-hour clock system.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A report made pursuant to subsection 6(1.1) shall
  specify the time at which the report is made in Co-ordinated Universal
  Time using the 24-hour clock system.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-214, s. 7
:::
