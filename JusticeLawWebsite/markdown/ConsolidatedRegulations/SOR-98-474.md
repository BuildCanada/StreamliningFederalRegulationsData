## [Maximum Total Amount]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024503 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 73(2.1)(b) of the [Nunavut Act](/eng/acts/N-28.6), the
approval of the Governor in Council is not required if the maximum total
amount that the Government of Nunavut must pay under agreements
involving leases of residential property is less than \$15,000,000.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024505 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on September 24, 1998.
