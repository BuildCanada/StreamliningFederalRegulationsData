## Goods Imported and Exported Drawback Regulations {#goods-imported-and-exported-drawback-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 489
:::

[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Regulations Respecting the Payment of Drawback on Goods Imported into
Canada and Exported
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557517 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Goods Imported and Exported Drawback
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._489).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557519 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National Revenue.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557521 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to these
Regulations, the Minister shall authorize the payment to an exporter or
importer of goods of a drawback of 99 per cent of the customs duty and
excise taxes paid on imported goods that are exported and that have not

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} been used in Canada for any purpose other than
  exclusively in the development or production of goods that are to be
  exported;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} been used as plant equipment; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} been damaged prior to such export.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A claim for drawback shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be made in such form as the Minister may prescribe;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} waivers from any persons, other than the claimant,
    who, pursuant to these Regulations, could be entitled to claim a
    drawback, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} such other evidence of entitlement to the drawback
    as is satisfactory to the Minister; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be filed at a customs office within two years of the
  date of exportation shown on each export entry referred to in the
  claim.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No payment shall be made in
respect of any claim for drawback unless the customs duty and excise
taxes on the goods in respect of which the claim referred to in section
4 is made were paid within the three-year period immediately preceding
the date of exportation of the imported goods and have not been
refunded.
