## [Establishment of Land Management Zones]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526839 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2016-132, s. 2(F)

\]
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following lands are set
apart and appropriated as land management zones, and these Regulations
apply in those zones:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the areas of the Northwest Territories that are under
  the control, management and administration of the Minister and that
  are not part of the Mackenzie Valley unless otherwise provided for in
  the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the areas of Nunavut that are under the control,
  management and administration of the Minister.

- SOR/2003-126, s. 6
- SOR/2016-132, s. 3

## [Exemption from Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526855 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} anything done by a resident of the Northwest
  Territories or Nunavut in the normal course of hunting, fishing or
  trapping;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} anything done in the course of prospecting, staking
  or locating a mineral claim unless it requires a use of equipment or
  material that normally requires a permit;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} lands whose surface rights have all been disposed of
  by the Minister; and

- [(d) and (e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2003-126, s. 7\]]{.Repealed}

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} land-use operations in the Mackenzie Valley, as that
  area is defined in section 2 of the [Mackenzie Valley Resource
  Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2), other than

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a land-use operation authorized by a permit issued
    under these Regulations prior to the coming into force of Part 3 of
    that Act,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a land-use operation for which an application for
    a permit under these Regulations was pending on the coming into
    force of Part 3 of that Act, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a land-use operation in respect of which an
    application is made under section 156 of that Act.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526870 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No person shall, without a
Class A Permit, carry on any work or undertaking on territorial lands
that involves

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the use, in any 30-day period, of more than 150 kg of
  explosives;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the use, except on a public road or trail maintained
  wholly or in part by federal funds, of any vehicle that exceeds 10 t
  net vehicle weight;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the use of any power driven machinery for earth
  drilling purposes whose operating weight, excluding the weight of
  drill rods or stems, bits, pumps and other ancillary equipment,
  exceeds 2.5 t;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the establishment of any campsite that is to be used
  for more than 400 man-days;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the establishment of any petroleum fuel storage
  facility exceeding 80 000 l capacity or the use of a single container
  for the storage of petroleum fuel that has a capacity exceeding 4 000
  l;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the use of any self-propelled power driven machine
  for moving earth or clearing land of vegetation;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the use of any stationary power driven machine for
  hydraulic prospecting, moving earth or clearing land, other than a
  power saw; or

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the levelling, grading, clearing, cutting or
  snowploughing of any line, trail or right-of-way exceeding 1.5 m in
  width and exceeding 4 ha in area.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No person shall, without a
Class B Permit, carry on any work or undertaking on territorial lands
that involves

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the use, in any 30-day period, of more than 50 kg but
  less than 150 kg of explosives;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the use, except on a public road or trail maintained
  wholly or in part by federal funds, of any vehicle that is more than 5
  t but less than 10 t net vehicle weight, or the use of any vehicle of
  any weight that exerts pressure on the ground in excess of 35 k pa;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the use of any power driven machinery for earth
  drilling purposes whose operating weight, excluding the weight of
  drill rods or stems and bits, pumps and other ancillary equipment, is
  more than 500 kg but less than 2.5 t;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the establishment of any campsite that is to be used
  by more than two people for more than 100 but less than 400 man-days;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the establishment of any petroleum fuel storage
  facility that has a capacity of more than 4 000 l but less than 80 000
  l or the use of a single container for the storage of petroleum fuel
  that has a capacity of more than 2 000 l but less than 4 000 l; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the levelling, grading, clearing, cutting or
  snowploughing of any line, trail or right-of-way exceeding 1.5 m in
  width but not exceeding 4 ha in area.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** No permittee shall,
unless expressly authorized in his permit or expressly authorized in
writing by an inspector,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} conduct a land use operation within 30 m of a known
  monument or a known or suspected historic or archaeological site or
  burial site;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} when excavating territorial land within 100 m of any
  stream, excavate at a point that is below the normal high water mark
  of that stream;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} deposit on the bed of any stream any excavated
  material; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} when placing a fuel or supply cache within 100 m of
  any stream, place the fuel or supply cache below the normal high water
  mark of that stream.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-132, s. 6

## [Small Fuel Caches]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526894 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
establishes a fuel cache of more than 400 l and less than 4 000 l on
territorial land for which a permit is not required shall, within 30
days of the establishment thereof, notify the engineer in writing,
giving details of the cache including the amount and type of fuel, size
of containers and method of storage and proposed date of removal of the
cache.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-169, s. 6(E)
:::

## [Excavation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526898 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the terms and
conditions of his permit or the express written authority of an
inspector, every permittee shall replace all materials removed by him in
the course of excavating, other than rock trenching, and shall level and
compact the area of the excavation.

## [Water Crossings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526900 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the terms and conditions
  of his permit or the express written authority of an inspector, every
  permittee shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} remove any material or debris deposited in any
    stream in the course of a land use operation, whether for the
    purpose of constructing a crossing or otherwise, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} restore the channel and bed of the stream to their
    original alignment and cross-section,

  prior to the completion of the land use operation or prior to the
  commencement of spring break-up, whichever occurs first.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not permit any person to deposit
  any material or debris in a stream contrary to the [Fisheries
  Act](/eng/acts/F-14), the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management
  Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2), the [Nunavut Waters and Nunavut Surface Rights
  Tribunal Act](/eng/acts/N-28.8) or any regulations made under those
  Acts.

## [Clearing of Lines, Trails or Rights-of-way]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526909 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  expressly authorized in a permit, no permittee shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} clear a new line, trail or right-of-way where there
    is an existing line, trail or right-of-way that he can use;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} clear a line, trail or right-of-way wider than 10
    m; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} while clearing a line, trail or right-of-way, leave
    leaners or debris in standing timber.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, in the opinion of an inspector, serious
  erosion may result from a land use operation, the permittee shall
  adopt such measures to control erosion as may be required by the
  inspector.

## [Monuments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526916 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a boundary monument is damaged, destroyed, moved or altered in the
  course of a land use operation, the permittee shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} report the fact immediately to the Surveyor General
    and pay to the Surveyor General the costs of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} investigating such damage, destruction, movement
      or alteration, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} restoring or re-establishing the monument to its
      original condition or its original place; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with the prior written consent of the Surveyor
    General, cause the monument to be restored or re-established at his
    own expense.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a topographic or geodetic monument is damaged,
  destroyed, moved or altered in the course of a land use operation, the
  permittee shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} report the fact immediately to the Dominion
    Geodesist, and pay to the Dominion Geodesist the costs described in
    subparagraphs (1)(a)(i) and (ii); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with the prior written consent of the Dominion
    Geodesist, cause the monument to be restored or re-established at
    his own expense.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The restoration or re-establishment of a monument
  pursuant to subsection (1) or (2) shall be carried out in accordance
  with instructions from the Surveyor General or Dominion Geodesist, as
  the case may be.

## [Historic or Archaeological Sites and Burial Sites]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526929 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** If, in the course of a
land use operation, a suspected historic or archaeological site or
burial site is unearthed or otherwise discovered, the permittee shall
immediately

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend the land use operation on the site; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify the engineer or an inspector of the location
  of the site and the nature of any unearthed materials, structures or
  artifacts.

## [Campsites]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526935 .Part}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the terms and conditions
  of his permit, every permittee shall dispose of all garbage, waste and
  debris from any campsite used in connection with a land use operation
  by removal, burning or burial or by such other method as may be
  directed by an inspector.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sanitary sewage produced in connection with land use
  operations shall be disposed of in accordance with the Public Health
  Act, R.S.N.W.T. 1988, c. P-12, and any regulations and orders made
  under that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-126, s. 8

## [Restoration of Permit Area]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526941 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the terms and
conditions of his permit, every permittee shall, after completion of a
land use operation, restore the permit area as nearly as possible to the
same condition as it was prior to the commencement of the land use
operation.

## [Removal of Buildings and Equipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526943 .Part}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  every permittee shall, on completion of a land use operation, remove
  all buildings, machinery, equipment, materials and fuel drums or other
  storage containers used in connection with the land use operation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A permittee may, with the prior written approval of
  the engineer, leave on territorial lands such buildings, equipment
  machinery and materials as the permittee deems may be required for
  future land use operations or other operations in the area, but any
  equipment, machinery or materials so left shall be stored in a manner,
  at a location and for a duration approved by the engineer.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any applicable mining legislation, a
  permittee may, without the prior approval of the engineer, leave
  diamond drill cores at a drill site on territorial lands.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-169, s. 6(E)
:::

## [Emergencies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526950 .Part}

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Any person may, in an
emergency that threatens life, property or natural environment, carry
out such operation as he deems necessary to cope with the emergency,
whether or not the operation is carried out in accordance with these
Regulations or any permit that he may have and such person shall
immediately thereafter send a written report to the engineer describing
the duration, nature and extent of the operation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-169, s. 6(E)
:::

## [Eligibility for a Permit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526954 .Part}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** In order to be eligible
for a permit, a person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where a right to search for, win or exploit minerals
  or natural resources is to be exercised by the carrying out of the
  land use operation authorized by the permit, be

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the holder of that right,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the manager of operations, where there is more
    than one holder of that right and such holders have entered into an
    exploration or operating agreement designating one of them as
    manager of operations, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the person who contracts to have the land use
    operations carried out, where there is more than one holder of that
    right and they have not entered into an exploration or operating
    agreement designating one of them as manager of operations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where no right to search for, win or exploit minerals
  or natural resources is to be exercised by the carrying out of the
  land use operation authorized by the permit, be the person who
  contracts to have the land use operation carried out; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in any case not provided for in paragraph (a) or (b),
  be the person who is to carry out the land use operation.

## [Application for a Permit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526962 .Part}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who, in accordance with section 21, is eligible for a permit
  may submit to the engineer, in duplicate, an application for a permit
  in a form approved by the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every permit application submitted pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall be accompanied by the application fee set out in
  Schedule I, the land use fee set out in Schedule II and a preliminary
  plan showing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lands proposed to be used and an estimate of
    their area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the approximate location of all

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} existing lines, trails, rights-of-way and cleared
      areas proposed to be used in the land use operation,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} new lines, trails, rights-of-way and cleared
      areas proposed to be used in the land use operation,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} buildings, campsites, air landing strips, air
      navigation aids, fuel and supply storage sites, waste disposal
      sites, excavations and other works and places proposed to be
      constructed or used during the land use operation, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} bridges, dams, ditches, railroads, highways and
      roads, transmission lines, pipelines, survey lines and monuments,
      air landing strips, streams and all other features, structures or
      works that, in the opinion of the applicant, may be affected by
      the land use operation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of calculating the land use fee
  payable where territorial lands are proposed to be used for a line,

## [Terms and Conditions of Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527019 .Part}

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  engineer may include in any permit terms and conditions respecting

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the location and the area of territorial lands that
    may be used;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the times at which any work or undertaking may be
    carried on;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the type and size of equipment that may be used in
    the land use operation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the methods and techniques to be employed by the
    permittee in carrying out the land use operation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the type, location, capacity and operation of all
    facilities to be used by the permittee in the land use operation;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the methods of controlling or preventing ponding of
    water, flooding, erosion, slides and subsidences of land;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the use, storage, handling and ultimate disposal of
    any chemical or toxic material to be used in the land use operation;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the protection of wildlife and fisheries habitat;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the protection of objects and places of
    recreational, scenic and ecological value;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the deposit of security in accordance with section
    36;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the establishment of petroleum fuel storage
    facilities;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the methods and techniques for debris and brush
    disposal;

  - [(l.1)]{.lawlabel} the protection of historic or archaeological
    sites or burial sites; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} such other matters not inconsistent with these
    Regulations as the engineer thinks necessary for the protection of
    the biological or physical characteristics of the land management
    zone.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The engineer may modify any of the terms or
  conditions included in a permit on receipt of a written request from
  the permittee that sets out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the terms or conditions in the permit that the
    permittee wishes modified; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the modification proposed and the
    reasons therefor.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the engineer receives a written request from a
  permittee pursuant to subsection (2), he shall notify the permittee of
  his decision and the reasons therefor within 10 days of receipt of the
  request.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A permit expires at the end of the period of validity
  set out in the permit, which shall be based on the estimated dates of
  commencement and completion set out in the permit application and
  shall not exceed five years.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a written request from a permittee to
  extend the duration of a permit, the engineer may grant the extension,
  subject to any terms and conditions referred to in subsection (1), for
  a period not exceeding two years, as is necessary to enable the
  permittee to complete the land use operation authorized by the permit.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A permit's duration may be extended under
  subsection (5) only once.

## [Final Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527049 .Part}

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  permittee shall, within 60 days after the completion of a land use
  operation or the expiry of his permit, whichever occurs first, submit
  a final plan in duplicate to the engineer showing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lands actually subjected to the land use
    operation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the location of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} lines, trails, rights-of-way and cleared areas
      that were used by the permittee during the land use operation,
      specifying those that were cleared by the permittee and those that
      existed before the land use operation began,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} buildings, campsites, air landing strips, air
      navigation aids, fuel and supply storage sites, waste disposal
      sites, excavations and other works and places that were
      constructed or used by the permittee during the land use
      operation, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} bridges, dams, ditches, railroads, highways and
      roads, transmission lines, pipelines, survey lines and monuments,
      air landing strips, streams and all other features, structures or
      works that were affected by the land use operation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the calculations of the area of territorial lands
    used in the operation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The final plan must meet one of the following two
  requirements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is certified by or on behalf of the permittee as
    to the accuracy of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} locations, distances and areas, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the representation of the land use operation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is drawn from and accompanied by a detailed site
    survey, an aerial photograph or an image created using satellite or
    other imaging technology, showing the lands subjected to the land
    use operation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a written request from a permittee for
  an extension of the time for filing a final plan, the engineer may
  extend the time for filing the final plan by not more than 60 days.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The engineer shall reject the final plan if it does
  not comply with this section and section 35 and the permittee shall,
  within three weeks after receipt of written notice from the engineer
  of rejection of the plan, submit to the engineer another final plan
  that complies with this section and section 35.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding the expiry of a permit or the
  submission of a final plan, every permittee remains responsible for
  his obligations arising under the terms and conditions of the permit
  or under these Regulations until such time as the engineer issues a
  letter of clearance for the land use operation.

## [Security Deposit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527086 .Part}

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  order to ensure that a permittee complies with the terms and
  conditions of his permit and with these Regulations, the engineer may
  include in the permit a condition that the permittee deposit with the
  Minister a security deposit not exceeding \$100,000.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a permit includes a condition requiring a
  security deposit, the permittee shall not begin the land use operation
  until a security deposit has been deposited with the Minister.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A security deposit shall be in the form of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a promissory note guaranteed by a chartered bank
    and payable to the Receiver General;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a certified cheque drawn on a chartered bank in
    Canada and payable to the Receiver General;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} bearer bonds issued or guaranteed by the Government
    of Canada; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a combination of the securities described in
    paragraphs (a) to (c).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A security deposit shall be returned by the Minister
  when the engineer has issued a letter of clearance in respect of the
  land use operation.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a permittee has not complied with all the terms
  and conditions of his permit or with these Regulations and the land
  use operation of the permittee results in damage to the lands, the
  Minister may retain the whole of the security deposit or such portion
  of the security deposit as is required to restore the lands to their
  former condition.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister retains a portion of a security
  deposit pursuant to subsection (5), the Minister shall return the
  remainder of the security deposit to the permittee.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Where the whole of a security deposit retained by the
  Minister pursuant to subsection (5) is insufficient to cover the cost
  of restoring the lands to their former condition, the deficiency shall
  be collectable as a debt due to the Crown.

- SOR/88-169, ss. 5, 6(E)

## [Duties and Powers of Inspectors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527105 .Part}

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It
  shall be a condition of every permit that the permittee shall permit
  an inspector, at any reasonable time, to enter any place or premises
  on territorial lands under the permittee's ownership or occupation,
  other than a private dwelling, and make such inspections as he thinks
  necessary to determine whether the terms and conditions of the permit
  or the provisions of these Regulations are being complied with.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An inspector shall be furnished with a certificate of
  his appointment as an inspector and on entering any place or premises
  under subsection (1) shall, if so requested, produce the certificate.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person in any place or premises entered by an
  inspector under subsection (1) shall give the inspector such
  assistance and furnish him with such information as the inspector may,
  for the purpose of carrying out his duties under these Regulations,
  reasonably require.

obstruct or hinder an inspector in carrying out his duties under these
Regulations.

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** No person shall knowingly
make a false or misleading statement either orally or in writing to an
inspector engaged in carrying out his duties under these Regulations.

## [Suspension of a Land Use Operation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527112 .Part}

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an inspector is of the opinion that a permittee has failed to comply
  with any term or condition of his permit or any provision of these
  Regulations, he shall so inform the permittee and, if the default
  continues, the inspector may give notice to the permittee that if the
  default is not corrected within the time specified in the notice the
  inspector may order the suspension of the land use operation or any
  part thereof.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a permittee does not correct a default within the
  time specified in a notice given by an inspector under subsection (1),
  the inspector may order the permittee to suspend the land use
  operation or any part thereof and the permittee shall thereupon
  suspend the land use operation or part thereof until the inspector
  authorizes the permittee to resume the land use operation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An inspector shall authorize a permittee to resume a
  land use operation or part thereof suspended under subsection (2) when
  the inspector or the engineer is satisfied that the default has been
  corrected, unless the permit has in the meantime been cancelled
  pursuant to section 42.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a permittee has been informed of a default
  pursuant to subsection (1) or an order has been made in respect
  thereof pursuant to subsection (2), the engineer may, if the permittee
  fails to correct the default, take such action as he deems necessary
  to correct the default.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The costs of any action taken by the engineer
  pursuant to subsection (4) may be recovered from the permittee as a
  debt due to the Crown.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section relieves a permittee from
  prosecution for any violation of these Regulations.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} No order pursuant to subsection (2) shall be made in
  respect of an oil or gas drilling site between the time of spud-in and
  the rig release date without the concurrence of the district oil and
  gas conservation engineer.

- SOR/88-169, s. 6(E)
