## Indian Band Revenue Moneys Order {#indian-band-revenue-moneys-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-297
:::

[INDIAN ACT](/eng/acts/I-5)

Registration 1990-05-17

Order Respecting the Control, Management and Expenditure of Revenue
Moneys

P.C. 1990-899  1990-05-17
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
subsection 69(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed [Order respecting the control, management and
expenditure of revenue moneys](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-297).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925321 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Indian Band Revenue Moneys Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-297).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925323 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The bands listed in the
schedule are hereby permitted to control, manage and expend in whole
their revenue moneys.
