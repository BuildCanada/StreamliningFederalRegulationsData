## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1424572 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[beneficiary country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a country or territory
that is a beneficiary of the Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff, General
Preferential Tariff, General Preferential Tariff Plus, Least Developed
Country Tariff, Commonwealth Caribbean Countries Tariff, Australia
Tariff or New Zealand Tariff.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direct shipment

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 17(1) of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), goods are
shipped directly to Canada from a beneficiary country if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case where they are shipped without shipment
  through another country,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} they are shipped on a through bill of lading, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} they are shipped without a through bill of lading
    and the importer provides, when requested by an officer, documentary
    evidence that indicates the shipping route and all points of
    shipment and transhipment prior to the importation of the goods; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case where they are shipped through another
  country, the importer provides, when requested by an officer,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} documentary evidence that indicates the shipping
    route and all points of shipment and transhipment prior to the
    importation of the goods, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the customs control documents that
    establish that the goods remained under customs control while in
    that other country.
