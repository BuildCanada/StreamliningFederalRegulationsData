## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743564 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[class 3 or above waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[class 3 or above waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means waters that
    have

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} rapids with moderate and irregular waves; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} rapids that are stronger, have more obstructions
      or are otherwise more difficult to navigate than rapids with
      moderate and irregular waves. ([eaux de classe 3 ou
      plus]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a vessel, means its
    master. ([guide]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[helmet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[helmet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a helmet that has a fastening
    system and is designed to protect from injury the head of a person
    who wears it from the mid-line of the forehead to the back of the
    crown of the head. ([casque protecteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743571 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of Canadian vessels that are inflatable
  and carry persons on an excursion in Canadian waters for remuneration.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of
  motorized rigid-hull inflatable vessels.

## [Responsibilities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743575 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person in charge of an enterprise that engages in an excursion shall
  ensure that the requirements set out in sections 4 to 17 are met in
  respect of the excursion.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A vessel's guide shall ensure that the requirements
  set out in sections 12 to 17 are met in respect of the vessel.

## [Vessels and Equipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743579 .Part}

### [Vessels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-743580 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  vessel on an excursion shall be of sound construction and in good
  condition so as to be able to withstand the weather and water
  conditions that may reasonably be expected on the excursion.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every vessel shall have a line that is becketed to
  the gunwale around the outside of the vessel or safety straps that are
  suitable to use as handgrips.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every vessel shall be able
to maintain buoyancy if any one of its buoyancy chambers deflates.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  vessel shall have sufficient seating so that every person on board can
  be seated.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No vessel shall be loaded to exceed the
  manufacturer's capacity recommendations, if any.

### [Equipment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-743588 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person on an excursion, other than a guide, shall be provided with a
  properly sized small-vessel lifejacket or white-water vest.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before January 1, 2012, a person on an excursion in
  waters that are not class 3 or above waters may be provided with a
  properly sized personal flotation device instead of the equipment
  required by subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every guide on an excursion shall be provided with a
  properly sized small-vessel lifejacket or white-water vest or an
  inherently buoyant and properly sized personal flotation device that
  has a quick release harness.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every person on an excursion in class 3 or above
  waters, other than a person on an excursion on a motorized vessel that
  is over 6 m in length, shall be provided with a properly sized helmet.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (4) does not apply in respect of an
  excursion in waters where there are no rocks or other hard objects
  that could cause impact injuries to a person who falls overboard.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every person on an excursion in class 3 or above
  waters where the water temperature is less than 15° C shall, unless
  the excursion is on a motorized vessel that is over 6 m in length, be
  provided with properly sized gear designed to provide thermal
  protection to the body core when a person is immersed in water.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Every small-vessel lifejacket, white-water vest or
  personal flotation device provided under any of subsections (1) to (3)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall, in the case of a small-vessel lifejacket,
    bear in English and French its manufacturer's marking or labelling
    indicating that it meets the standards set out in CAN/CGSB-65.7-M88,
    Lifejackets, Inherently Buoyant Type or the standards for a class 2
    lifejacket set out in the CAN/CGSB-65.7-2007, Lifejackets;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall, in the case of a personal flotation device,
    bear in English and French its manufacturer's marking or labelling
    indicating that it meets the standards for type I personal flotation
    devices set out in CAN/CGSB-65.11-M88, Personal Flotation Devices;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} shall, in the case of a white-water vest, have been
    approved by the United States Coast Guard for use in commercial
    white-water activities;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} shall not be altered in a manner that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} compromises its original structural integrity, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} diminishes the integrity or readability of any
      marking or labelling required by paragraph (a) or (b);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} shall be in good condition; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} shall be maintained and replaced in accordance with
    the manufacturer's instructions or recommendations, if any, that
    come with the equipment.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} A person who provides their own equipment or gear
  that meets the applicable requirements of subsections (1) to (7) shall
  be considered to have been provided with that equipment or gear.

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A first
  aid kit that contains the following shall be carried in a watertight
  container by one vessel out of every five or fewer vessels travelling
  together:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 20 adhesive bandages;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} two sterile pads;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one 10-cm × 4.5-m gauze bandage;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} one 7.5-cm × 4.5-m roller bandage;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} one triangular bandage, with a minimum base length
    of 100 cm, and two pins;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} one 1.25-cm × 4.5-m roll of adhesive first-aid
    tape;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} one pair of safety scissors;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} 60 mL of antiseptic wound solution and 10
    disposable applicators, or 10 antiseptic swabs;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a first aid manual;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} two pairs of latex examination gloves; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} one resuscitation face shield.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every vessel shall carry a throw bag that has at
  least 15 m of buoyant line.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Every vessel shall carry,
travel with another vessel that carries or be at all times within 5 km
of a cache that contains

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a repair kit for inflatable vessels;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an air pump;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an engine repair kit and a spare motor if the vessel
  is propelled by a motor; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a spare oar with an oarlock or clip if the vessel is
  propelled by oars.

## [Operational Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743625 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  vessel's guide shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be at least 18 years of age;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} hold a standard first aid certificate as defined in
    section 16.1 of the [Canada Occupational Health and Safety
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-304); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the excursion is in class 3 or above waters,
    have completed five excursions in class 3 or above waters on an
    inflatable vessel, other than a motorized rigid-hull inflatable
    vessel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the excursion is in class 3 or above waters, every
  vessel's guide shall have completed one excursion on any type of
  vessel in those waters. However, if the excursion includes more than
  five nights, any guide who is not the excursion leader may instead
  have completed one excursion in other waters that are of equal or
  greater difficulty to navigate than the waters in which the excursion
  takes place.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before an excursion is started, every vessel's guide
  shall ensure that he or she is aware of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the current and expected conditions and the hazards
    of the waters in which the excursion will take place; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the contents of the rescue plan required by section
    17.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every overnight excursion
in class 3 or above waters shall include the participation of a person
who holds a swift-water rescue certificate that is evidence of their
successful completion of theoretical and practical training in the
skills and knowledge necessary for swift-water rescues, including
training in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} selecting and using swift-water rescue equipment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} executing swift-water rescues;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} ensuring the safety of rescue personnel; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} identifying and managing medical emergencies and
  carrying out evacuations.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Before an excursion is
started, every person on the excursion, other than the guides, shall
receive a safety briefing that includes

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} information about the excursion and a warning about
  the nature of the hazards of the waters in which it will take place;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the general safety precautions to
  take and the routine procedures to follow in the waters;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} details of the requirement under section 13 to wear
  equipment, as well as directions regarding its use; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the procedures to follow in the
  event of emergencies during the excursion, including casualties,
  persons overboard and the swamping and capsizing of vessels.

<!-- -->

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who is provided with a small-vessel lifejacket, white-water
  vest or personal flotation device under section 7 shall wear it when
  on board a vessel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is provided with a helmet under
  subsection 7(4) shall wear it when on board a vessel in class 3 or
  above waters.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is provided with gear under
  subsection 7(6) shall wear it when on board a vessel in class 3 or
  above waters where the water temperature is less than 15°C.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Any equipment or material
that is on board a vessel and that is not being used shall be stowed and
secured in place when the vessel is moving.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** No person may be allowed
on board a vessel if there are reasonable grounds to believe that their
faculties are impaired by alcohol or a drug to an extent that they might
present a hazard to the vessel or to persons on board it.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Except in an emergency,
no vessel shall be operated

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in class 3 or above waters unless it is accompanied
  by another vessel, whether inflatable or not; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in class 3 or above waters during the period
  beginning one-half hour after sunset and ending one-half hour before
  sunrise.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** On every excursion, a
rescue plan in respect of the excursion that contains the procedures to
be followed in the event of an emergency, including the following
procedures, shall be carried on a vessel:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the means of communication to be
  used in an emergency;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} emergency telephone numbers, such as the telephone
  numbers of local law enforcement and search and rescue groups;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of pull-out or extraction sites from
  which, in an emergency, persons can be transported by air or land; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the procedures to be followed in a medical emergency.

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743659 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Every person in charge of
an enterprise that engages in an excursion shall keep, for three years
after the excursion, records that include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of each guide on the excursion, the name
  of the guide, the date of the excursion, the number of passengers on
  the excursion and the class of waters and a geographical description
  of the waters in which the excursion took place;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} information in respect of the certificates required
  by paragraph 10(1)(b) and section 11, including the name of the holder
  of the certificate, its date of issuance and, if applicable, the name
  of the institution that issued it and the endorsed propulsion method
  as chosen by the enterprise or specified on the swift-water rescue
  certificate;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the safety briefing required by
  section 12; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the rescue plan required by section 17.
