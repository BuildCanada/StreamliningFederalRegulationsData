## Order Transferring the Internal E-staffing Unit from Human Resources and Skills Development to the Public Service Commission {#order-transferring-the-internal-e-staffing-unit-from-human-resources-and-skills-development-to-the-public-service-commission .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2011-59
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2011-07-20

Order Transferring the Internal E-staffing Unit from Human Resources and
Skills Development to the Public Service Commission

P.C. 2011-796 2011-06-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBCAA},
hereby transfers to the Public Service Commission from the Department of
Human Resources and Skills Development the control and supervision of
the portion of the federal public administration in the Department of
Human Resources and Skills Development known as the Internal E-staffing
Unit, effective July 1, 2011.
