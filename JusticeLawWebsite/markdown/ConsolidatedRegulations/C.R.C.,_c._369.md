## Orderly Payment of Debts Regulations {#orderly-payment-of-debts-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 369
:::

[BANKRUPTCY AND INSOLVENCY ACT](/eng/acts/B-3)

Orderly Payment of Debts Regulations
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/2005-168,
s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-551732 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Part X of the [Bankruptcy and
Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-578, s. 1
- SOR/2005-168, s. 3(F)
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-551736 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** On application by a debtor
for a consolidation order pursuant to subsection 219(1) of the Act, the
debtor shall pay the clerk an application fee of \$20 and the clerk
shall issue a receipt for that amount to the debtor.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-158, s. 1
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** All fees paid under the Act
shall be kept in an account entirely separate from moneys collected
under the statute establishing the court.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  affidavit of a debtor filed with the clerk in accordance with
  subsection 219(2) of the Act operates as an application for a
  consolidation order.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The affidavit of a debtor shall be filed in
  duplicate.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-168, s. 4
- SOR/2015-85, s. 1(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-5-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 5"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where a creditor is shown
to be related to the debtor, the clerk may, before entering the
creditor's name in the register, require an affidavit from the creditor
proving the alleged debt.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-168, s. 5
:::

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  clerk shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on filing the affidavit referred to in subsection
    219(2) of the Act and entering the particulars it contains in the
    register in accordance with paragraph 220(1)(a) of the Act, open a
    consolidation account in the name of the debtor; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} credit to the consolidation account all moneys paid
    into court under the consolidation order made in the name of the
    debtor.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The clerk may, before distributing any moneys
  credited to a consolidation account, first deduct the amount required
  for postage for the mailing of any notices and forms under the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-158, s. 2
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The original consolidation
order shall be filed with the clerk of the court in which it is made and
shall bear the number of the debtor's original affidavit.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-85, s. 2(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-8-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 8"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the clerk is satisfied that the affairs of a debtor may be better
  administered in a judicial district or centre in a province other than
  the judicial district or centre in which a consolidation order was
  originally issued, the clerk may, after giving notice to the debtor
  and the registered creditors, transfer the proceedings to that other
  judicial district or centre.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the clerk is satisfied that the affairs of a
  debtor may be better administered in a province where the Act is in
  force other than the province in which a consolidation order was
  originally issued, the clerk may, after giving notice to the debtor
  and the registered creditors and after distributing all of the moneys
  paid into court by or on behalf of the debtor, transfer the
  proceedings to that other province.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-158, s. 3
- SOR/92-578, s. 2
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Any application to the
court under the Act shall be to the next sitting thereof, unless the
sitting is to be held within seven days of the application, or unless
otherwise ordered by the court, or agreed to by the parties concerned.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The judge of the court
may hear any cause or matter in chambers.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** On any notice of motion
under the Act no affidavit need be filed unless the clerk deems one
necessary, and if he deems one necessary a copy of the affidavit shall
be served with the notice of motion.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A notice of motion under
the Act shall be served on the debtor and all the registered creditors,
unless it is otherwise provided in the Act.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Where, pursuant to
subsection 231(1) of the Act, the clerk gives notice to the debtor and
the creditor and to each registered creditor of a new claim against the
debtor, the clerk shall allow a period of 30 days for disputing the new
claim.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-158, s. 4
:::

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a consolidation order is varied pursuant to subsection 227(1), 231(3)
  or 234(4) of the Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Notice of Variation of Consolidation Order in
    Form 11 of Schedule I shall be given to the debtor and the
    registered creditors; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the notice shall be attached to the
    consolidation order in the court file.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The clerk shall endorse on the face of the
  consolidation order

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date and terms of any variation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and title of the authority ordering the
    variation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-158, s. 5
- SOR/92-578, s. 3
:::

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A consolidation order
shall be terminated

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where a debtor is in default and the clerk issues a
  Notice of Default in Form 18 of Schedule I; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where, under paragraph 233(3)(b) of the Act, the
  court makes an order permitting all registered creditors to proceed
  independently for the enforcement of their claims.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-168, s. 6
:::

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** An application by a
debtor under subsection 233(5) of the Act to continue under a
consolidation order on the grounds that the default of the debtor is due
to circumstances beyond their control shall be by notice of motion.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-168, s. 7
:::

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  registered creditor who elects to rely on their security pursuant to
  section 232 of the Act shall, before relying on that security, file
  with the clerk an affidavit as to its value and, if the security is
  repossessed and sold or seized and sold, disclose to the clerk whether
  the proceeds received from the disposal of the security are in excess
  of the creditor's claim.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where any registered creditor does not comply with
  the conditions set out in subsection (1), the court may direct that
  the security of that registered creditor be appraised by an appraiser
  appointed by the court.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-168, s. 8
- SOR/2015-85, s. 3(F)
:::

## [Sending of Notices]{.HTitleText1} {#h-551793 .Part}

**[[18.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18.1 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise
provided in these Regulations, every notice required by the Act or these
Regulations to be given shall be sent by ordinary mail.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-578, s. 4
- SOR/2005-168, s. 9(F)
:::

## [Duties of Clerk]{.HTitleText1} {#h-551797 .Part}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The clerk shall maintain

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a cash book in which, on each occasion when a payment
  is received from a debtor or disbursed by the court, he shall record

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date of the payment,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the number of the consolidation order in respect
    of which the payment is made,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the debtor,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the payment, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} particulars of the payment; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a separate consolidation account sheet and a separate
  register sheet for each debtor.

<!-- -->

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  clerk shall deposit all payments on consolidation orders within the
  jurisdiction of the court in an account with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a bank;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation other than a bank if deposits with
    that corporation

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are insured by the Canada Deposit Insurance
      Corporation, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are insured or guaranteed under a provincial
      enactment that provides depositors with protection against the
      loss of moneys on deposit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
  bank as defined in the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/84-356, s. 1
- SOR/86-101, s. 1(F)
:::

## [Dividends]{.HTitleText1} {#h-551817 .Part}

- **[[23.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The clerk shall deposit with the
  person designated by the province all unclaimed dividends and
  undistributed funds remaining in his hands and all interest, if any,
  on the money paid into court on account of the debts of the debtor and
  shall provide the person with a list of the names and the post office
  addresses, so far as known, of the creditors entitled to the unclaimed
  dividends, showing the amount payable to each creditor.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designated person referred to in subsection (1)
  shall, upon application by a creditor, pay to the creditor the
  appropriate dividend as shown on the list referred to in that
  subsection.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-737, s. 1
- SOR/86-101, s. 2(F)
:::

## [Costs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-551823 .Part}

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The costs of all
proceedings before the court shall be in the absolute discretion of the
court, but shall not exceed those taxable in a similar action in the
court.

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** There shall be no costs
in respect of a hearing before the clerk unless the court otherwise
orders.

## [Forms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-551826 .Part}

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  forms set out in Schedule I are the forms that shall be used in
  proceedings under the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The account forms set out in Schedule II are the
  forms that shall be used in maintaining accounts under the Act.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (2), the accounts that
  relate to proceedings under the Act may be maintained by an electronic
  accounting system if the accounts comprise the same information as is
  required by the applicable forms in Schedule II.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-158, s. 8
:::

## [Court]{.HTitleText1} {#h-551833 .Part}

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
Act and these Regulations, [[court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Saskatchewan, Her Majesty's Court
  of Queen's Bench for Saskatchewan;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Nova Scotia, the County Court;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of British Columbia, the Supreme
  Court of British Columbia and the County Courts of Nanaimo, Vancouver,
  Victoria, Westminster, Yale, Cariboo, Prince Rupert, East Kootenay and
  West Kootenay;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the Northwest Territories, the Supreme Court of
  the Northwest Territories; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Prince Edward Island, the Supreme
  Court of Prince Edward Island.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-386, s. 1
- SOR/83-539, s. 1
- SOR/2005-168, s. 10(F)
:::

## [Miscellaneous]{.HTitleText1} {#h-551842 .Part}

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** In a province in which
the Act is in force, the Act applies

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of debts of the classes referred to in
  subsection 218(1) of the Act without the monetary limits set out
  therein; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of debts of the classes that would
  otherwise be excluded by paragraphs 218(2)(a) and (b) of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/81-2, s. 1
- SOR/89-158, s. 9
:::

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding section
28, the Act does not apply to the following classes of debts:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Nova Scotia,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a claim for a lien or a judgment thereon under the
    Mechanics Lien Act, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a claim for a lien or a judgment thereon under the
    Woodman's Lien Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the Northwest Territories,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a claim for a lien or a judgment thereon under the
    Mechanics' Lien Ordinance, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a claim for a lien or a judgment thereon under the
    Miners' Lien Ordinance.

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
defraying administrative expenses, the clerk shall deduct 15 per cent
from each payment that is made to a registered creditor in respect of a
claim of that creditor under a consolidation order.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-158, s. 10
- SOR/92-578, s. 6
- SOR/98-239, s. 1
:::

## [Interest on Consolidation Orders]{.HTitleText1} {#h-551858 .Part}

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** Every consolidation order
shall bear interest at the rate of five per cent per annum.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-578, s. 7
:::
