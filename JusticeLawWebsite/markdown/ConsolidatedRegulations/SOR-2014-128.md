## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805176 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[Meeting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Summit on Maternal, Newborn
and Child Health, entitled "Saving Every Woman, Every Child", to be held
in Toronto, Ontario from May 28 to 30, 2014.

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805178 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the period beginning on
May 28, 2014 and ending on May 30, 2014, representatives of foreign
states that participate in the Meeting are to have in Canada, to the
extent required for the exercise of their functions in Canada in
relation to the Meeting, the privileges and immunities set out in
sections 11(b) and (e) and sections 14 to 16 of Article IV of the
Convention on the Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set
out in Schedule III to the [Foreign Missions and International
Organizations Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805180 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
