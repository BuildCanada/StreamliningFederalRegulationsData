## Regulations Concerning Information Required by Foreign States {#regulations-concerning-information-required-by-foreign-states .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2002-47
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 2002-01-16

Regulations Concerning Information Required by Foreign States

P.C. 2002-34 2002-01-16
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 4.83[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), hereby makes the annexed [Regulations
Concerning Information Required by Foreign
States](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-47).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2001, c.
  38, s. 1
  :::
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Landing in a foreign state

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 4.83 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), in the case of an
aircraft that is due to land in a foreign state, the information set out
in Schedules 1 and 2 relating to persons on board or expected to be on
board the aircraft may be provided to a competent authority in that
foreign state if the state and authority are set out in Schedule 3.
However, the information set out in Schedule 2 may be provided only on
request.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-209, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-1-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 1"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}United States overflight

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 4.83 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), in the case of an
aircraft that is due to fly over the United States, the information set
out in Schedule 4 relating to passengers on board or expected to be on
board the aircraft may be provided to the United States Department of
Homeland Security.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-209, s. 2
