## Ontario Turkey Order {#ontario-turkey-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 220
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Turkeys Produced in Ontario
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536871 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Turkey Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._220).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536873 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing
    Board of Ontario established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Régie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Turkey
    Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act; ([Office
    de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a turkey or any class or part
    thereof, produced or grown in the Province of Ontario.
    ([dindon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536879 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board and the
Board are each authorized to regulate the marketing of turkeys in
interprovincial and export trade and for such purposes may, by order,
with respect to persons and property situated within the Province of
Ontario, exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable by each
of them in relation to the marketing of turkeys locally within that
Province under section 15A of the Act.

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536881 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/80-90\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the powers granted to it by section 3
  with respect to the marketing of turkeys in interprovincial and export
  trade,

to make orders fixing, imposing and collecting levies or charges from
persons situated within the Province of Ontario who are engaged in the
production or marketing of turkeys and for such purpose classifying such
persons into groups and fixing the levies or charges payable by the
members of the different groups in different amounts, and the Commodity
Board may use such levies or charges for its purposes, including the
creation of reserves and the payment of expenses and losses resulting
from the sale or disposal of turkeys and the equalization or adjustment
among the producers of turkeys of moneys realized from the sale thereof
during such period or periods of time as it may determine.
