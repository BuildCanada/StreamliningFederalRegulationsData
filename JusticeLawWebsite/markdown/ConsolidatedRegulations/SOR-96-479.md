## NAV CANADA Divestiture Regulations {#nav-canada-divestiture-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/96-479
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

[PUBLIC SERVICE SUPERANNUATION ACT](/eng/acts/P-36)

Registration 1996-11-01

NAV CANADA Divestiture Regulations

T.B. 824634 1996-10-31
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Treasury Board, on the recommendation of the President of the
Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 42.1(1)(u)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-96-479_e_hq_4106){#fn_SOR-96-479_e_hq_4106-ID0EHBAA}
of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and paragraph
7(2)(a) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby

Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-479).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1001513 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[agreement to transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agreement to transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the agreement to
    transfer between Her Majesty in right of Canada and NAV CANADA,
    dated April 1, 1996. ([accord de transfert]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[civil air navigation services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[civil air navigation services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
    meaning as in subsection 2(1) of the [Civil Air Navigation Services
    Commercialization Act](/eng/acts/C-29.7). ([services de navigation
    aérienne civile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[NAV CANADA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[NAV CANADA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the corporation that was
    incorporated on May 26, 1995 under Part II of the [Canada
    Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8) as NAV CANADA. ([NAV
    CANADA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[NAV CANADA divestiture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[NAV CANADA divestiture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the transfer of
    the civil air navigation services from the public service to NAV
    CANADA in accordance with the agreement to transfer. ([cession à NAV
    CANADA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[NAV CANADA pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[NAV CANADA pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the pension
    plan referred to in the agreement to transfer. ([régime de pension
    de NAV CANADA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transfer date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transfer date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Civil Air Navigation Services
    Commercialization Act](/eng/acts/C-29.7). ([date de
    cession]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 48(E)

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1001524 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), these Regulations apply to persons who, on the
  transfer date, cease to be employed in the public service and become
  employed by NAV CANADA as a result of the NAV CANADA divestiture.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to persons who
  subsequently become re-employed by NAV CANADA.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 4 to 13 do not apply to a person who has
  exercised an option under subsection 3(2).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-233, s. 1
- SOR/2016-203, s. 48(E)

## [Applicable Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1001531 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections
  12, 13, 13.01 and 16 and subsections 17(1) and (3) of the Act only
  apply to a person on and after the date on which that person ceases to
  be employed by NAV CANADA.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), where, on or after April 1,
  1998 a person would be eligible, were it not for these Regulations, to
  exercise an option under subsection 12(3), paragraph 13(7)(a) or (b)
  or section 13.01 of the Act, the person may exercise such an option

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the cases referred to in paragraphs 13(7)(a)
    and (b) of the Act, no later than June 20, 1998; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the cases referred to in subsection 12(3) and
    section 13.01 of the Act, no later than April 1, 1999.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When a person exercises an option under subsection
  (2), for the purposes of subsection 12(3), paragraphs 13(7)(a) and (b)
  and section 13.01 of the Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the period of pensionable service is the period of
    pensionable service to that person's credit at the date on which the
    person ceases to be employed in the public service; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the age of the person is the age of that person on
    April 1, 1998.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} When a person exercises an option under subsection
  (2), for the purposes of section 13.01 of the Act and sections 83 to
  99 of the [Public Service Superannuation
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1358), [[valuation
  day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the date on which the person exercises
  an option for a transfer value.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person who has exercised an option under
  subsection (2) is not entitled to exercise an option under
  subsection (1) when the person ceases to be employed by NAV CANADA.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-233, s. 2
- SOR/2016-203, s. 48(E)

## [Surviving Spouse and Children]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1001544 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 12(8) of the Act, the surviving spouse and children of a
person who dies while employed by NAV CANADA are entitled to a death
benefit equal to a return of contributions.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 13(3) of the Act, the surviving spouse and children of a
person who dies while employed by NAV CANADA are entitled to an
allowance as described in paragraphs 12(4)(a) and (b) of the Act,
subject to the limitations set out in subsections 12(4) and (5) of the
Act.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 26(2) of the Act, a child who was born to or adopted by a
person or became the stepchild of the person during the period that
begins on the date on which the person ceases to be employed in the
public service and that ends on the date on which that person ceases to
be employed by NAV CANADA is entitled to an allowance under Part I of
the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 48(E)

## [Adaptation of Subsection 10(5) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1001550 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 10(5) of the Act, the one year period referred to in
paragraph 10(5)(a) of the Act shall begin on the date on which the
person ceases to be employed by NAV CANADA.

## [Adaptation of Sections 12 and 13 of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1001552 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 and 13 of the Act, pensionable service includes the period
of service with NAV CANADA that begins on the transfer date and that
ends on the date on which the person ceases to be employed by NAV
CANADA.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 and 13 of the Act, the age of a person when the person
ceases to be employed in the public service is the age of the person on
the day on which that person ceases to be employed by NAV CANADA.

## [Adaptation of Sections 16 and 17 of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1001557 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 17 of the Act, ceasing otherwise than voluntarily to be employed
in operational service

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} has the same meaning as in the NAV CANADA pension
  plan; but

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not include ceasing to be employed in the public
  service as a result of the NAV CANADA divestiture.

## [Adaptation of Paragraph 51(2)(b) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1001571 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of paragraph 51(2)(b) of the Act, a person to whom these
  Regulations apply, and to whom subsection 51(2) of the Act applied on
  November 1, 1996, and who has not made an election pursuant to
  paragraph 51(2)(b) of the Act, may, within thirty days after the
  coming into force of this section, elect to become a participant as
  defined in paragraph (c) of the definition
  [[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 47(1) of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An election under subsection (1) shall be made in the
  manner set out in section 25 of the [Supplementary Death Benefit
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1360) and shall be sent to
  the Minister within thirty days of the coming into force of this
  section.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who makes an election under subsection (1)
  shall pay contributions as determined in accordance with sections 11
  and 12 of the [Supplementary Death Benefit
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1360).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding sections 11 and 12 of the
  [Supplementary Death Benefit
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1360), the first contribution
  is payable on or before the thirtieth day after the day on which this
  section comes into force and, subsequently, such contributions are
  payable each year on or before the thirtieth day after the anniversary
  of the day on which this section comes into force.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a person does not pay an annual contribution in
  accordance with subsection (4), the Minister shall, where that failure
  to make such payment resulted from the fact that the person was
  misinformed as to that person's obligations under the Act, fix a
  subsequent day as the day on which that contribution is due, but the
  due date of each annual contribution thereafter payable by the person
  shall be the anniversary of the thirtieth day following the day on
  which this section comes into force.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/97-491, s. 1
- SOR/2000-166, s. 1(F)
:::
