## Indian Timber Regulations {#indian-timber-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 961
:::

[INDIAN ACT](/eng/acts/I-5)

Regulations in Respect of the Cutting of Timber on Indian Reserves and
Surrendered Lands
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599863 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Indian Timber
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._961).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-690, s. 3(F)
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599867 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5);
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Assistant Deputy Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Assistant Deputy Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked,
    SOR/93-244, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of Indian
    Affairs and Northern Development; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[dues]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dues]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any stumpage or royalty charged
    for the right or privilege of the cutting and removal of timber;
    ([droit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any written authority or
    contract granted by the Minister for the cutting of timber on
    surrendered lands or reserve lands; ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[limit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[limit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area included in a permit or
    licence; ([limite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Indian
    Affairs and Northern Development; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence granted to a band or
    to a member or group of members of a band for whose benefit the
    timber is being administered; ([licence]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes corporation, syndicate, firm
    and partnership. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-244, s. 2
- SOR/94-690, s. 3(F)
- SOR/95-531, s. 2
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599880 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to the cutting of timber on surrendered lands and on
  reserve lands.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of the
  cutting of timber on lands to which the [Indian Timber Harvesting
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-109) apply.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-244, s. 2
- SOR/95-531, s. 2
- SOR/2002-246, s. 1
:::

## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599886 .Part}

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** No person shall cut
timber on surrendered lands or on reserve lands without a licence.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-531, s. 2
:::

## [Permits for Indian Use]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599890 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)

\]
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Permits to cut timber free
of dues may be issued by the Minister to a band for band purposes, or to
a member or group of members of a band, to cut timber and fuel wood for
his or their individual use.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-244, s. 2
- SOR/94-690, s. 3(F)
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

## [Permits to Cut for Sale]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599896 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/93-244, s. 2
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)

\]
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} With the
  consent of the council of a band, permits to cut timber for sale may
  be issued by the Minister to a band or to a member or group of members
  of a band.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Dues shall be charged at prevailing rates for timber
  cut on band land, and for timber harvested from individual locations
  or holdings of Indians the rate of dues may be reduced to one-half of
  such prevailing rates, and the rate of the dues shall be stated in the
  permit.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/94-690, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-244, s. 2
- SOR/94-690, ss. 1, 3(F)
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** All timber cut under permit
shall be measured by a licensed scaler or by some competent person
appointed by the Minister,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the place of cutting or at a concentration point
  adjacent thereto; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of British Columbia, either at the
  place of cutting or at some point between such place and the mill.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-244, s. 2
- SOR/94-690, ss. 3(F), 4(F)
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Unless with the consent of
the Minister, timber cut under permit shall not be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} manufactured, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} except in the Province of British Columbia, removed
  from the place of cutting or concentration point adjacent thereto,

until it has been measured and dues paid thereon.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-244, s. 2
- SOR/94-690, s. 3(F), 4(F)
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** All timber permits expire
on April 30th in the year next following the year of issue.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-690, s. 3(F)
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

## [Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599919 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)

\]
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 10, the
Minister may grant licences for the right to cut timber

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on surrendered lands; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} with the consent of the council of a band, on reserve
  lands.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-244, s. 2
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Where it is estimated
that the dues payable pursuant to a licence will exceed \$2,500, the
Minister shall invite tenders for the licence by public advertisement.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-244, s. 2
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

## [Renewals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599930 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)

\]
:::

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Timber
  licences expire on April 30 in the year that follows the year in which
  the licence was granted, unless otherwise specified in the licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Application for renewal shall be made during the term
  of the licence and if the application is not made within 30 days
  following the date of expiration of the licence it shall thereupon
  determine, and in the discretion of the Minister any security given by
  the licensee may be declared forfeited.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a limit has not been worked during the licence
  year, the licensee shall, with his application for renewal, furnish a
  sworn statement of the reasons for his failure to operate and a
  renewal shall be granted only if the Minister is satisfied with such
  statement.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-244, s. 2
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

## [Ground Rent]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599939 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Ground rent shall be paid
for each licence year at the rate of \$10 per square mile, except in the
Province of British Columbia where the rate shall be \$0.20 per acre,
provided that in no case shall the rent for a licence year be less than
\$40.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [\[Revoked, SOR/93-244,
s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

## [Security Deposit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599944 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  a condition of every licence that the licensee shall deposit security
  in cash or bonds for the performance of the terms and conditions of
  the licence, in an amount equal to 15 per cent of the estimated dues
  payable under the licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may convert the security deposit and
  apply it against dues in arrears, and in such event the licence shall
  not be renewed until the security deposit has been restored to the
  full amount.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a licensee fails to comply with any condition of
  his contract or to complete the operation in a satisfactory manner,
  the Minister may declare the security deposit forfeited to the Crown
  for the benefit of the band.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-690, s. 2
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

## [Scaling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599951 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Without the consent of
the Minister, timber cut under licence shall not be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} manufactured, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} except in the Province of British Columbia, removed
  from the place of cutting or concentration point adjacent thereto,

until it has been measured and dues paid thereon.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-244, s. 2
- SOR/94-690, ss. 3(F), 4(F)
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Failing any other
provision in the licence, all timber cut from May 1st to November 30th
in any year shall be scaled and paid for by January 31st of the year
next following, and all timber cut from December 1st to April 30th in
any licence year shall be scaled and paid for by June 30th next
following the cutting.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-690, ss. 3(F), 4(F)
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A licensee shall at his
expense supply scaler\'s returns verified by affidavit.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

## [Fire Protection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599964 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The licensee shall pay
all costs of fire protection service and of the suppression of any fire
in the limit covered by his licence or occasioned by persons employed by
him.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [\[Revoked, SOR/93-244,
s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599969 .Part}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee shall maintain a record of timber cut each month and, when
  required, shall furnish a copy of the record to the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister, or anyone authorized by him, shall at
  all times have free access to and be permitted to examine the books
  and memoranda kept by any licensee showing the quantity of timber in
  board measure sawn from logs and of other timber products cut under
  the licence, and failure to produce such books and memoranda when
  required so to do shall subject such licensee to a forfeiture of his
  rights under the licence.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-244, s. 2
- SOR/94-690, s. 3(F)
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

## [Cancellation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599975 .Part}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** It is a condition of
every licence that, if the licensee fails to comply with the terms and
conditions of the licence or with these Regulations, the Minister may
cancel the licence.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-244, s. 2
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

## [Conservation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599979 .Part}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} With
  the consent of the licensee, the Minister may vary any licence in
  respect of one or more parts of a licensed area or in respect of any
  type, size or species of timber.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything contained in a licence, the
  Minister may, for the purpose of forest management, watershed
  protection, fire protection or the preservation of the beauty of the
  landscape, game or game shelters, order the marking of such trees as
  are to be left standing or cut in the licensed area and order the
  licensee to pay the cost of such marking.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-244, s. 2
- SOR/94-690, s. 3(F)
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[23 and 24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23_and_24 .sectionLabel}** [\[Revoked,
SOR/93-244, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

## [Compliance with Laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599986 .Part}

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Every licensee shall
exercise the rights conferred by the licence in accordance with the laws
of the province in which the licensee is operating under the licence
regarding disposal of slash, prevention of fire hazard and the conduct
of timber operations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-244, s. 2
- SOR/94-690, s. 4(F)
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

## [Seizure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599990 .Part}

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may seize and detain any timber and any product manufactured
  from timber, when he has reasonable grounds to believe that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} such timber or the timber from which such product
    was manufactured has not been measured or counted by a scaler as
    required by these Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any charges in respect of such timber or on the
    timber from which such product was manufactured or in respect of the
    lands on which such timber was cut are in default; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} such timber or the timber from which such product
    was manufactured was not cut under the authority of a licence or
    permit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any timber or product that is seized under
  subsection (1) may be removed to such place as the Minister may deem
  proper for the protection of the timber or product, and if it is
  seized when in possession of a carrier it shall be removed by the
  carrier on behalf of the Minister to such place as the superintendent
  may direct, provided that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Department may defray the costs of
    transportation and other charges incurred in consequence of the
    directions given by the superintendent, and all such costs shall be
    included in the costs of seizure; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} such seizure shall not prejudice or affect any lien
    to which the carrier may be entitled in respect of the timber or
    product to the time of such seizure.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where timber within the meaning of this section has
  been made up with other timber into a crib, dam or raft, or in any
  other manner has been so mixed at a mill or elsewhere as to render it
  impossible or difficult to distinguish such timber from other timber
  with which it is mixed, the whole of the timber so mixed may be seized
  and detained until separated by the person claiming to be the owner
  thereof to the satisfaction of the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-244, s. 2
- SOR/94-690, s. 3(F)
- SOR/95-531, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Seizure of timber or any
product therefrom may be made by posting beside the timber or product a
notice stating that the timber or product has been seized.

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Where timber or any
product manufactured therefrom has been seized and no claim to recover
it has been made within 30 days from the date of the seizure, the timber
or product is forfeited to the Crown.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-244, s. 2
:::

## [Proceedings Following Seizure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-600006 .Part}

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person claiming to be the owner of timber, or any product manufactured
  therefrom, that has been seized under section 26 may, on at least four
  days notice to the Minister, apply to a judge of a court of competent
  jurisdiction in the place in which the timber or product is held under
  seizure for an order for the release from seizure and delivery of the
  timber or product to that person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Upon receipt of a bond of the claimant, with two good
  and sufficient sureties in an amount not less than the market value of
  the timber or product and the costs of the seizure, to be forfeited to
  the Crown if the claimant is declared by the judge not to be the owner
  of the timber or product, the judge may order the timber or product to
  be released from seizure and to be delivered to the claimant.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Upon the application of the Minister or the claimant,
  and upon at least seven days notice, the judge shall determine the
  ownership of the timber or product whether or not it has been released
  and delivered to the claimant under subsection (2) and shall make an
  order

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} declaring the claimant to be the owner

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} free of any claim for charges, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} subject to payment of such dues, charges and
      expenses as he may find to be owing; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} declaring the claimant not to be the owner and the
    bond, if any, forfeited to the Crown.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The judge shall make such order as he may consider
  proper as to the costs of proceedings under this section and the costs
  of seizure.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the claimant is declared not to be the owner of
  the timber or product, it shall be disposed of in such manner as the
  Minister may determine.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-244, s. 2
:::

## [Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-600019 .Part}

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes a provision of these Regulations is guilty of an offence and
liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding one hundred dollars
or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months, or to both.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-690, s. 3(F)
- SOR/95-531, s. 2
:::
