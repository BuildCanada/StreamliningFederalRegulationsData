## Inuvik Airport Zoning Regulations {#inuvik-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-707
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1981-09-04

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Inuvik Airport

P.C. 1981-2432 1981-09-03
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 6 of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to approve the annexed
[Regulations respecting zoning at Inuvik
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-707) made by the Minister of Transport.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871964 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Inuvik Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-707).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871966 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Inuvik Airport, in the
      vicinity of Inuvik, in the Northwest Territories;
      ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part III of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport including the runway prepared for the
      take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction, which
      strip is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule;
      ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part VI of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the assigned
  elevation of the airport reference point is deemed to be 60.04 m above
  sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871977 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, that consist of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lands within, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lands directly under that portion of an approach
  surface that extends beyond

the outer limits described in Part II of the schedule.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871982 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point any

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} approach surface;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} transitional surface.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871987 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in paragraphs 4(a) to (c), the Minister
may make a direction that the owner or occupier of the land on which
that object is growing remove the excessive growth.

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871989 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit such land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste edible by or attractive
to birds.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(ss. 2 and 3)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-871992 .scheduleLabel}

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point is a point coincidental with coordinate
control survey monument No. 139302 (c.c.m. 32), located at latitude
68°18′18.7789″, longitude 133°29′05.2246″.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Limits of Land]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-outer-limits-of-land .SchedHeadL1}

The following described boundaries are the outer limits of lands:

COMMENCING at a point in the southern boundary of Lot 3, Group 1355,
Plan 43574 (C.L.S.R.) 218 (L.T.O.), where it is intersected by the
circumference of a circle having a radius of 4 000 m and centered at the
airport reference point as described in Part I of the schedule; THENCE,
in a southwesterly direction following the circumference of the said
circle clockwise to the point where it intersects the northern boundary
of Lot 3, Group 1355, Plan 43574 (C.L.S.R.) 218 (L.T.O.); as said outer
limits are shown on Department of Transport Inuvik Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.1552.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Department of Transport Inuvik Airport
Zoning Plan No. E.1552, are surfaces abutting each end of the strip
associated with the runway designated 04-22 and are described as
follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 04, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 22,

consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio of 1 m measured
vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an imaginary
horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected centre line of
the strip, 300 m measured vertically above the elevation at the end of
the strip, 15 000 m measured horizontally from the end of the strip, the
outer ends of the imaginary horizontal line being 2 400 m from the
projected centre line.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Department of Transport Inuvik Airport
Zoning Plan No. E.1552, is an imaginary surface located at a common
plane established at a constant elevation of 45 m above the assigned
elevation of the airport reference point, except where the common plane
is less than 9 m above the surface of the ground, the imaginary surface
is located 9 m above the surface of the ground.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-the-strip .SchedHeadL1}

The strip, shown on Department of Transport Inuvik Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.1552, is described as follows:

the strip associated with runway 04-22 is 300 m in width, 150 m being on
each side of the centre line of the runway and 1 948.8 m in length.

### [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-videscription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Department of Transport Inuvik
Airport Zoning Plan No. E.1552, is a surface consisting of an inclined
plane having a ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 7 m measured
horizontally at right angles to the centre line and centre line produced
of the strip and extending upward and outward from the lateral limits of
the strip and its approach surfaces to an intersection with the outer
surface.
