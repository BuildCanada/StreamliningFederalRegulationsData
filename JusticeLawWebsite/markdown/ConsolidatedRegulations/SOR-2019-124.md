## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1128580 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1128581 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Order.

  [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by the
      definition [[organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of
      the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of sections 16, 17, 25, 26,
        27.6, 27.7, 49, 53, 57, 58.2, 66, 67, 74 and 79, the fiscal year
        of a person that provides information under that section or of a
        person with which the person is affiliated; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of any other section, the
        period beginning on April 1 in one year and ending on March 31
        in the next year. ([exercice]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[performance standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[performance standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the document
      entitled Performance Standards for the Fees in Respect of Drugs
      and Medical Devices Order that is published by the Government of
      Canada on its website, dated February 16, 2024. ([norme de
      rendement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[small business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[small business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person in respect
      of which either of the following criteria applies:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total of the number of employees of the
        person and of the persons with which the person is affiliated is
        fewer than 100; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total of the gross revenue of the person
        and of the persons with which the person is affiliated is
        \$30,000 or more but less than \$5 million. ([petite
        entreprise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other words and expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context otherwise requires, other words
  and expressions used in this Order have the meanings assigned to them
  by the [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870),
  the [Medical Devices Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282) or the
  [Biocides Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-110), as the case may
  be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affiliation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Order,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one entity is affiliated with another entity if one
    of them is the subsidiary of the other or both are subsidiaries of
    the same entity or each of them is controlled by the same entity or
    individual;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if two entities are affiliated with the same entity
    at the same time, they are deemed to be affiliated with each other;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an individual is affiliated with an entity if the
    individual controls the entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsidiary entity

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Order, an entity is a
  subsidiary of another entity if it is controlled by that other entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Order,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a corporation is controlled by an entity or an
    individual if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} securities of the corporation to which are
      attached more than 50% of the votes that may be cast to elect
      directors of the corporation are held, directly or indirectly,
      whether through one or more subsidiaries or otherwise, other than
      by way of security only, by or for the benefit of that entity or
      individual, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the votes attached to those securities are
      sufficient, if exercised, to elect a majority of the directors of
      the corporation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity other than a corporation is controlled by
    an entity or individual if the entity or individual, directly or
    indirectly, whether through one or more subsidiaries or otherwise,
    holds an interest in the entity that is not a corporation that
    entitles them to receive more than 50% of the profits of that entity
    or more than 50% of its assets on dissolution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed affiliation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Order, if it may reasonably
  be considered that one of the main reasons for the separate existence
  of two or more corporations is so that one of them meets the
  applicable conditions for a remission of a fee fixed under this Order
  for which only small businesses are eligible, the two or more
  corporations are deemed to be affiliated with each other.

### [Purpose]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1128610 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose --- fees

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  purpose of this Order is to fix the fees for the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of drugs for human use and drugs for
    veterinary use only, the examination of a new drug submission, a
    supplement to a new drug submission, an abbreviated new drug
    submission or a supplement to an abbreviated new drug submission
    referred to in section C.08.002, C.08.002.1 or C.08.003 of the [Food
    and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), as the case
    may be, an application in respect of an establishment licence filed
    under those Regulations or an application for a drug identification
    number filed under section C.01.014.1 of those Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of drugs for veterinary use only, the
    examination of a notification for a veterinary health product filed
    under subsection C.01.615(1) of the [Food and Drug
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), a preclinical
    submission filed under subsection C.08.005(1) of those Regulations,
    information filed under section C.08.010 of those Regulations for
    the purpose of obtaining a letter of authorization, information and
    material filed under section C.08.014 of those Regulations for the
    purpose of obtaining an experimental studies certificate,
    information and material filed with the Minister in respect of a
    notifiable change or a protocol filed with the Minister;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} in respect of biocides, the examination of an
    application for a market authorization submitted under section 10 of
    the [Biocides Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-110), an
    application for a notice of acceptance in respect of a major change
    submitted under subsection 15(2) of those Regulations, the
    examination of a written description of a minor change provided to
    the Minister under subsection 17(1) of those Regulations, other than
    a change to contact information referred to in paragraph (a) of the
    definition of [[minor change]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection
    1(1) of those Regulations or a change referred to in paragraph (f)
    of that definition, or the examination of an application for a
    market authorization for a biocide that is based on a comparison to
    a biocide that is authorized for sale by a foreign regulatory
    authority submitted under section 26 of those Regulations;

  - [(b.2)]{.lawlabel} the right to sell a biocide under the [Biocides
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-110);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the right to sell a drug under the [Food and Drug
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the examination of an application in respect of a
    medical device licence, the right to sell a medical device or the
    examination of an application in respect of an establishment licence
    under the [Medical Devices
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the examination of an application to amend an
    authorization in respect of a Class II, III or IV medical device
    that is not a UPHN medical device or the right to sell such a device
    under the [Medical Devices
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose --- remission

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of this Order is also to remit, in whole
  or in part, certain of those fees.

### [Non-application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1128620 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Order does not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} publicly funded health care institutions;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} branches or agencies of the Government of Canada or
    of the government of a province; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} drugs that are the subject of an extraordinary use
    new drug submission filed under section C.08.002.01 of the [Food and
    Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) or of an
    abbreviated extraordinary use new drug submission filed under
    section C.08.002.1 of those Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[publicly funded health
  care institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), [[publicly funded
  health care institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an institution
  that is funded by the Government of Canada or the government of a
  province and that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} licensed, approved or designated by a province in
    accordance with the laws of the province to provide care or
    treatment to persons or animals suffering from any form of disease
    or illness; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} owned or operated by the Government of Canada or
    the government of a province and that provides health services.

### [Annual Adjustment of Fees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1128631 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustment of fees

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Beginning on April 1, 2021, every
  fee set out in this Order is to be adjusted in each fiscal year on
  April 1 by the percentage change over 12 months in the April All-items
  Consumer Price Index for Canada, as published by Statistics Canada
  under the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19), for the previous fiscal
  year and rounded up to the nearest dollar.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Formula

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a fee that is payable under any of
  Divisions 1 to 5 of Part 2 or Division 1 of Part 3 in a fiscal year
  that is not set out in the applicable schedule, the amount of the fee
  is to be calculated, on April 1 of the fiscal year, in accordance with
  the following formula and rounded up to the nearest dollar:

  ::: {#1128637 .Subsection}
  Fee = A + (A × B)

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the fee that was payable in the previous fiscal
      year; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the percentage change over 12 months in the April All-items
      Consumer Price Index for Canada, as published by Statistics Canada
      under the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19), for the previous
      fiscal year.
  :::

### [Performance Standard and Remission]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1128655 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission --- performance standard

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister determines that the performance standard has not been met in
  relation to a fee that is payable under this Order, remission is
  granted to the person that must pay the fee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} of an amount equal to 25% of the fee; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} of an amount equal to 25% of the amount that is
    payable, in the case where remission is granted of part of the fee
    under any other provision of this Order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a fee for the
  examination of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application or submission referred to in this
    Order in respect of which a joint or parallel review is conducted by
    the Minister and a foreign regulatory authority;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an application for a licence that is filed under
    section 32 of the [Medical Devices
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282) if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the medical device to which the application
      relates includes a component that is a drug, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has made a decision in respect of
      the application to issue or amend a medical device licence under
      section 36 of those Regulations, or to refuse to issue or amend
      such a licence under section 38 of those Regulations; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a new drug submission for a public health
      emergency drug if subsection C.08.002(2.3) of the [Food and Drug
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) applies to that
      submission, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a supplement to any new drug submission if
      subsection C.08.003(7) of those Regulations applies to that
      supplement.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Drugs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1128667 .Part}

### [DIVISION 1]{.HLabel2}[Fees for Examination of a Submission --- Drugs for Human Use]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1128668 .Subheading}

#### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1128669 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[submission]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** In this Division,
[[submission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application for a drug identification number that
  is filed under section C.01.014.1 of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a new drug submission that is filed under section
  C.08.002 of those Regulations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an abbreviated new drug submission that is filed
  under section C.08.002.1 of those Regulations; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a supplement to a new drug submission or abbreviated
  new drug submission that is filed under section C.08.003 of those
  Regulations.

#### [Non-application]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1128676 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** This Division does not
apply to drugs for veterinary use only.

#### [Fees and Remissions]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1128679 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee for examination

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to paragraph 10(b) and section 12, the fee for the examination of a
  submission is, in respect of the applicable submission class set out
  in column 1 of Schedule 1 and described in column 2, as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a fee that is payable in a fiscal
    year set out in any of columns 3 to 6 of Schedule 1, the fee set out
    in that column; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a fee that is payable in a fiscal
    year other than one set out in any of columns 3 to 6 of Schedule 1,
    the amount that is calculated in accordance with subsection 4(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee paid by person that files
  submission

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fee is payable by the person that files the
  submission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee and timing of payment --- preliminary
examination

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** If a preliminary
examination is conducted in respect of a submission,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the full fee is payable on the issuance by the
  Minister of a notice to the person referred to in subsection 9(2)
  stating that the submission has been found to be complete and has been
  accepted for further examination; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 10% of the fee is payable on the issuance by the
  Minister of a notice to the person referred to in subsection 9(2)
  stating that the submission has been found to be incomplete.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee and timing of payment --- no
preliminary examination

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** If a preliminary
examination is not conducted in respect of a submission, the fee is
payable on the issuance by the Minister of a notice to the person
referred to in subsection 9(2) stating that the submission has been
received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee --- filing in previous fiscal year

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 9(1), if the Minister issues a notice referred to in section
10 or 11 in the fiscal year that follows the fiscal year in which the
submission was filed, the fee that is payable is the fee that was
payable in the fiscal year in which the submission was filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deferred payment --- notice of compliance

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Despite sections 10 and
11, if the person referred to in subsection 9(2) files an application
for authorization under section C.07.003 of the [Food and Drug
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) at the same time that the
person files the submission, payment of the fee is deferred until the
issuance to the person of a notice of compliance under section C.08.004
of those Regulations or of a document setting out the drug
identification number assigned for the drug under subsection
C.01.014.2(1) of those Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission --- urgent public health need

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted to a
person referred to in subsection 9(2) that files a new drug submission
under section C.08.002 of the [Food and Drug
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) or an application for a
drug identification number under section C.01.014.1 of those Regulations
of the amount of the fee that is payable under subsection 9(1) if, as of
the day on which the person filed the new drug submission or
application,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the drug has the same medicinal ingredient, strength
  and route of administration and is in a comparable dosage form as a
  drug that may be imported under subsection C.10.001(2) of those
  Regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a drug identification number has not been assigned
  under subsection C.01.014.2(1) of those Regulations for the drug or
  for another drug that has the same medicinal ingredient, strength and
  route of administration and is in a comparable dosage form; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a notice of compliance has not been issued under
  section C.08.004 of those Regulations in respect of the drug or
  another drug that has the same medicinal ingredient, strength and
  route of administration and is in a comparable dosage form.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- SOR/2024-121, s. 14

  - [14]{.lawlabel} A person that submits an application, under
    subsection 10(1) of the [Biocides
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-110), for a market
    authorization for a [[biocide]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
    subsection 1(1) of those Regulations, is not required to pay the fee
    for the examination of the application referred to in subsection
    27.2(1) of the [Fees in Respect of Drugs and Medical Devices
    Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-124) if the biocide is the subject
    of one of the exceptions set out in section 67 or 69 of those
    Regulations.
