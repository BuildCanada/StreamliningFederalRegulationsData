[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[goods that originate in
China]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order, [[goods that
originate in China]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods that are eligible
to be marked as goods of China in accordance with the Determination of
Country of Origin for the Purpose of Marking Goods (Non-CUSMA Countries)
Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- goods in transit

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order does not apply
to goods that originate in China that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} are classified under any of the tariff items set out
  in Schedule 1 and are in transit to Canada on October 1, 2024; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} are classified under any of the tariff items set out
  in Schedule 2 and are in transit to Canada on the day on which section
  3.1 comes into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surtax --- tariff items set out in
Schedule 1

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Goods that originate in
China that are classified under any of the tariff items set out in
Schedule 1 are subject to a surtax in the amount of 100% of the value
for duty determined in accordance with sections 47 to 55 of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surtax --- tariff items set out in
Schedule 2

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** Goods that originate in
China that are classified under any of the tariff items set out in
Schedule 2 are subject to a surtax in the amount of 25% of the value for
duty determined in accordance with sections 47 to 55 of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

::: HistoricalNote
- [SOR/2024-202, s. 1](#1489834-1492767){.wb-lbx

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on October 1, 2024, but if it is registered after that day, it comes
into force on the day on which it is registered.
