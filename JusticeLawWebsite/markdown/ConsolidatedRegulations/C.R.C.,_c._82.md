## Fort McMurray Airport Zoning Regulations {#fort-mcmurray-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 82
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Fort McMurray Airport
::::

::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-565863 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Fort McMurray Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._82).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-565865 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Fort McMurray Airport, in the
    vicinity of Fort McMurray, in the Province of Alberta;
    ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
    described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
    l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary inclined
    plane extending upward and outward from each end of the strip along
    and at right angles to the projected centre line thereof, which
    approach surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
    schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/93-401,
    s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

[[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
    located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
    outer surface is more particularly described in Part IV of the
    schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
    landing area of the airport, including the runway prepared for the
    take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction, which
    strip is more particulary described in Part V of the schedule;
    ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
    inclined plane extending upward and outward from the lateral limits
    of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional surface is
    more particularly described in Part VI of the schedule. ([surface de
    transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-401, s. 2
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, the airport reference point is deemed to be 1,205 feet
above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-565877 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands and lands under water, including public road allowances,
adjacent to or in the vicinity of the airport, that consist of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lands the outer limits of which are described in
  Part II of the schedule; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lands directly beneath the approach surface,
  other than such lands as from time to time form part of the airport
  and such lands as form the Clearwater Indian Reserve No. 175.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-565881 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct, on any land or any land under water to which these
Regulations apply, any building, structure or object or any addition to
any existing building, structure or object, the highest point of which
will exceed in elevation at the location of that point any of the
surfaces hereinafter set out that project immediately over and above the
surface of the land at that location, namely,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Sections 2 and 4)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-565887 .scheduleLabel}

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iairport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

Being a point distant 500 feet measured northerly and perpendicular to
the centre line of runway 07-25 from a point distant 4,580.43 feet
measured westerly along the centre line from the intersection of the
said centre line with the easterly boundary of the Northeast quarter of
Section 21, Township 88, Range 8, West of the 4th Meridian, and which
said intersection is distant southerly 74.53 feet, more or less, from
the northeast corner of said quarter section.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of Outer Limits of Lands]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-outer-limits-of-lands .SchedHeadL1}

BOUNDED AS FOLLOWS: on the north by the northern boundaries of
Section 36, Township 88, Range 9, West of the 4th Meridian, and
Sections 31, 32, 33, 34 and 35, Township 88, Range 8, West of the
4th Meridian and their productions across the road allowances; on the
East by the eastern boundaries of Sections 14, 23, 26 and 35,
Township 88, Range 8, West of the 4th Meridian and their productions
across road allowances; on the South by the southern boundaries of
Sections 14, 15, 16, 17 and 18, Township 88, Range 8, West of the 4th
Meridian, and Section 13, Township 88, Range 9, West of the 4th Meridian
and their productions across road allowances; on the West by the western
boundaries of Sections 13, 24, 25 and 36, Township 88, Range 9, West of
the 4th Meridian and their productions across road allowances; which
outer limits are shown on Department of Public Works Plan No. E. 1017,
dated September 28, 1972.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Approach Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-each-approach-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Being a surface abutting each end of the strip associated with the
runway designated as 07-25 and more particularly described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 07 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of one (1) foot measured vertically to fifty (50) feet measured
  horizontally rising to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right
  angles to the projected centre line of the strip, one thousand (1,000)
  feet measured vertically above the elevation at the end of the strip
  and fifty thousand (50,000) feet measured horizontally from the end of
  the strip, the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal line being eight
  thousand (8,000) feet from the projected centre line, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 25 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of one (1) foot measured vertically to fifty (50) feet measured
  horizontally rising to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right
  angles to the projected centre line of the strip, one thousand (1,000)
  feet measured vertically above the elevation at the end of the strip
  and fifty thousand (50,000) feet measured horizontally from the end of
  the strip, the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal line being eight
  thousand (8,000) feet from the projected centre line, excepting
  thereout all that portion of the said surface contained within the
  vertical projections of the boundaries of the Clearwater Indian
  Reserve No. 175,

which approach surfaces are shown on Department of Public Works
Plan E. 1017, dated September 28, 1972.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Being an imaginary surface consisting of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a common plane established at a constant elevation of
  one hundred and fifty (150) feet above the assigned elevation of the
  airport reference point, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the common plane described in paragraph (a) is
  less than thirty (30) feet above the surface of the ground, an
  imaginary surface located thirty (30) feet above the surface of the
  ground,

which outer surface is shown on Department of Public Works Plan
No. E. 1017, dated September 28, 1972.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-strip .SchedHeadL1}

The strip associated with the runway designated as 07-25 is one thousand
(1,000) feet in width, five hundred (500) feet being on each side of the
centre line of the runway, and seven thousand four hundred (7,400) feet
in length, as shown on Department of Public Works Plan No. E. 1017,
dated September 28, 1972.

### [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-videscription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Being a surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at a ratio of one
(1) foot measured vertically to seven (7) feet measured horizontally at
right angles to the centre line and centre line produced of the strip
and extending upward and outward from the lateral limits of the strip
and its approach surfaces to an intersection with the outer surface, as
shown on Department of Public Works Plan No. E. 1017, dated
September 28, 1972.
