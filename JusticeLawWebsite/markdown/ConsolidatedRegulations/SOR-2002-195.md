## Regulations Clarifying the Application of Provisions of the Convention on the Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations {#regulations-clarifying-the-application-of-provisions-of-the-convention-on-the-privileges-and-immunities-of-the-united-nations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2002-195
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Registration 2002-05-23

Regulations Clarifying the Application of Provisions of the Convention
on the Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations

P.C. 2002-827  2002-05-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor General in Council considers it necessary to
clarify, in relation to section 5 of the [Foreign Missions and
International Organizations Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2002-195_e_hq_5854){#fn_SOR-2002-195_e_hq_5854-ID0EBBBA},
the application of the Convention on the Privileges and Immunities of
the United Nations.

  2000, c. 12, s. 119
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to section
13[[Footnote

of the [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4), hereby makes the annexed [Regulations Clarifying
the Application of Provisions of the Convention on the Privileges and
Immunities of the United Nations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-195).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-684168 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Foreign Missions and
    International Organizations Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4).
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to an
    individual, a person who is cohabiting with the individual in a
    conjugal relationship, having so cohabited for a period of at least
    one year. ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in Schedule
    III to the Act. ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Clarification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-684173 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of clarifying, in relation to section 5 of the Act, the
  application of provisions of the Convention, in particular paragraphs
  11(d) and 18(d) and (f) and section 19, the Convention shall be
  applied in Canada in a manner that extends to common-law partners the
  treatment afforded to spouses.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of clarifying, in relation to section
  5 of the Act, the application of provisions of the Convention, the
  [Organization of American States Privileges and Immunities in Canada
  Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-350), the [International
  Telecommunication Union Privileges and Immunities
  Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-230) and subsection 5(1) of the
  Agence de la Francophonie and the Institut de l'énergie et de
  l'environnement de la Francophonie Privileges and Immunities Order,
  the privileges and immunities accorded under paragraphs 11(d) and
  18(d) and (f) and section 19 of the Convention are extended to the
  spouses and common-law partners of the persons referred to in those
  provisions.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of clarifying, in relation to section
  5 of the Act, the application of provisions of the Convention, in
  particular section 19, the Convention shall be applied in Canada in a
  manner that extends, to the minor children of a spouse or common-law
  partner of the persons referred to in that section, the treatment
  afforded to the minor children of those persons.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-684178 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
