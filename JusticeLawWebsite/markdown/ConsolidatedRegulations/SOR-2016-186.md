## [Requirement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832305 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standard

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Ice hockey helmets must
meet the requirements of standard Z262.1, entitled Ice hockey helmets,
published by the Canadian Standards Association, as amended from time to
time.
