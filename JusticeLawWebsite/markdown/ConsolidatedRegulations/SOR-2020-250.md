[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Divorce Act](/eng/acts/D-3.4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed period --- subsection 25.01(4)
of Act

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 25.01(4) of the Act, a spouse becomes liable to pay the
amount of child support 31 days after the day on which they were
notified of the decision in accordance with the law of the province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed period --- subsection 25.01(5)
of Act

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 25.01(5) of the Act, the prescribed period for applying to a
court for an order is 30 days after the day on which both spouses were
notified of the decision in accordance with the law of the province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation for deeming income ---
subsection 25.1(1.2) of Act

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 25.1(1.2) of the Act, the deemed income is the income used to
determine the amount of the last child support order plus

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 10% of that income, if fewer than two years have
  elapsed since that order;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 15% of that income, if two years or more but fewer
  than five years have elapsed since that order;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} 20% of that income, if five years or more but fewer
  than 10 years have elapsed since that order; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} 30% of that income, if 10 years or more have elapsed
  since that order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed period --- subsection 25.1(3)
of Act

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 25.1(3) of the Act, a spouse becomes liable to pay the
recalculated amount 31 days after the day on which they were notified of
the recalculated amount in accordance with the law of the province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed period --- subsection 25.1(4)
of Act

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 25.1(4) of the Act, the prescribed period for applying to a
court for an order is 30 days after the day on which both spouses were
notified of the recalculated amount in accordance with the law of the
province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into Force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2020-250_e_transform_hq_28275){#fn_SOR-2020-250_e_transform_hq_28275-ID0EBCA}[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 24 of An Act to amend the Divorce Act, the Family Orders and
Agreements Enforcement Assistance Act and the Garnishment, Attachment
and Pension Diversion Act and to make consequential amendments to
another Act, chapter 16 of the Statutes of Canada, 2019, comes into
force, but if they are registered after that day, they come into force
on the day on which they are registered.
