## Order Respecting Ex-Gratia Payments to Persons who were in Conjugal Relationships with now Deceased Chinese Head Tax Payers or to Designated Beneficiaries {#order-respecting-ex-gratia-payments-to-persons-who-were-in-conjugal-relationships-with-now-deceased-chinese-head-tax-payers-or-to-designated-beneficiaries .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-137
:::

Registration 2006-12-13

Order Respecting Ex-Gratia Payments to Persons who were in Conjugal
Relationships with now Deceased Chinese Head Tax Payers or to Designated
Beneficiaries

P.C. 2006-1401 2006-11-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Canadian Heritage and the Treasury Board, hereby makes
the annexed Order Respecting Ex-Gratia Payments to Persons who were in
Conjugal Relationships with now Deceased Chinese Head Tax Payers or to
Designated Beneficiaries.
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612460 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Canadian
Heritage.

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612462 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister is hereby authorized, on application under subsection 3(1),
  to make an [ex-gratia]{.otherLang lang="la"} payment of \$20,000 to
  any person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establishes that they were in a conjugal
    relationship with a person who has died and who, at the time of
    their death, met the criteria set out in paragraphs 2(a) and (b) of
    the Order Respecting Ex-Gratia Payments to Chinese Head Tax
    Payers[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBBCCA};

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote1_e-ID0EBBCCA){#footnote1_e}SI/2006-109
      :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establishes that they are a Canadian citizen or a
    permanent resident of Canada or who, in the opinion of the Minister,
    has a long-standing connection to Canada; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} was alive on February 6, 2006.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is hereby authorized, on application
  under subsection 3(1), to make an [ex-gratia]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  payment of \$20,000 to the beneficiary identified in the application
  made under the Order Respecting Ex-Gratia Payments to Chinese Head Tax
  Payers[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBCBA} by a person
  who met the criteria set out in section 2 of that Order but died after
  February 6, 2006 if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no payment has been made as a result of the
    application made under that Order; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no application has been made by a person who meets
    the criteria set out in subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is hereby authorized to make an
  [ex-gratia]{.otherLang lang="la"} payment of \$20,000 to the
  beneficiary identified in the application made under subsection 3(1)
  by a person referred to in subsection (1) who died after February 6,
  2006.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612472 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), an application for an [ex-gratia]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} payment under this Order shall be made to the Minister on
  or before March 31, 2008, shall be in the form approved by the
  Minister and shall be supported by such evidence as he or she deems
  necessary.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an application is submitted after March 31,
  2008, the application may be accepted by the Minister if he or she is
  satisfied that the applicant was unable to submit the application
  before that date because of facts or circumstances beyond the control
  of the applicant.

## [Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612476 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Each payment shall be a
one-time lump sum payment.

## [No Crown Liability]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612478 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Payments made under this
Order shall not be construed as an admission of liability on the part of
the Crown.
