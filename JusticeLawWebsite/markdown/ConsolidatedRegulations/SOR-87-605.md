## Yorkton Airport Zoning Regulations {#yorkton-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-605
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1987-10-15

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Yorkton Airport

P.C. 1987-2150 1987-10-15
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 4.5 of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed Zoning Regulations
respecting Yorkton Airport, substantially in the form set out in the
schedule hereto, was published in two successive issues of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on May 16th and 23rd, 1987,
and in two successive issues of the Yorkton Enterprise on June 29th and
July 6th, 1987, and a reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to
interested persons to make representations to the Minister of Transport
with respect thereto.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 4.4 of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting zoning at Yorkton
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-605).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-907990 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Yorkton Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-605).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-907992 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Yorkton Airport, in the
      vicinity of Yorkton, in the Province of Saskatchewan;
      ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part II of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, prepared for
      the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
      which strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule;
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the airport reference point is 487 m above sea level.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-111, s. 1
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-908005 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, which lands are more particularly described as
follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lands within the outer limits of lands described
  in Part VI of the schedule; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lands directly under that portion of the approach
  surfaces that extend beyond the said outer limits.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-111, s. 2(F)
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-908011 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-111, s. 3(F)
:::

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-908018 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in paragraphs 4(a) to (c), the Minister
may require that the owner or occupier of the land on which that object
is growing remove the excessive growth.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-111, s. 4
:::

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-908022 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit that land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste that is edible by or
attractive to birds.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-111, s. 5(F)
:::

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point, shown on Public Works Canada Yorkton
Airport Zoning Plan No. E.1876 dated March 7, 1985, is a point lying
perpendicular distant 61.4 m southwesterly from a point on the centre
line of runway 12-30 and distant 270.1 m measured southeasterly along
the centre line of runway 12-30 from the intersection of the centre
lines of runways 03-21 and 12-30.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Public Works Canada Yorkton Airport
Zoning Plan No. E.1876 dated March 7, 1985, are surfaces abutting each
end of the strip associated with the runways designated 12-30 and 03-21
and are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 12, consisting of an inclined plane having a
  ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally, rising
  to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 2 500 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 295 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 62.5 m measured vertically above the elevation
  at the end of the strip;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 30, consisting of an inclined plane having a
  ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally, rising
  to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 2 500 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 295 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 62.5 m measured vertically above the elevation
  at the end of the strip;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 03, consisting of an inclined plane having a
  ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally, rising
  to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 3 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 375 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 60 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 21, consisting of an inclined plane having a
  ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally, rising
  to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 3 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 375 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 60 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Public Works Canada Yorkton Airport Zoning
Plan No. E.1876 dated March 7, 1985, is an imaginary surface located at
a common plane established at a constant elevation of 45 m above the
elevation of the airport reference point, except that, where that common
plane is less than 9 m above the surface of the ground, the outer
surface is an imaginary surface located at 9 m above the surface of the
ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strips]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strips .SchedHeadL1}

The strips associated with runways 12-30 and 03-21, as shown on Public
Works Canada Yorkton Airport Zoning Plan No. E.1876 dated March 7, 1985,
are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 12-30 is 90 m in
  width, 45 m on each side of the centre line of the runway and
  1 156.3 m in length; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 03-21 is 150 m in
  width, 75 m on each side of the centre line of the runway and 1 583 m
  in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-the-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The transitional surfaces, shown on Public Works Canada Yorkton Airport
Zoning Plan No. E.1876 dated March 7, 1985, are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surface associated with
  runway 12-30, is a surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at a
  ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 5 m measured horizontally at right
  angles to the centre line and projected centre line of the strip
  extending upward and outward from the lateral limits of the strip and
  its approach surfaces to an intersection with the outer surface or
  another transitional surface of an adjoining strip; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surface associated with
  runway 03-21, is a surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at a
  ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 7 m measured horizontally at right
  angles to the centre line and projected centre line of the strip
  extending upward and outward from the lateral limits of the strip and
  its approach surfaces to an intersection with the outer surface or
  another transitional surface of an adjoining strip.
