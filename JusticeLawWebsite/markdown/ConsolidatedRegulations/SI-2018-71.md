## Transfer of Duties Order {#transfer-of-duties-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2018-71
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2018-08-08

Transfer of Duties Order

P.C. 2018-1016 2018-07-18
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1661-E_hq_22384){#fn_81000-3-1661-E_hq_22384-ID0EJEAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-1661-E_hq_22385){#fn_81000-3-1661-E_hq_22385-ID0EGEAA},
transfers from the Minister of State styled the Minister of Small
Business and Tourism to the Minister of La Francophonie the powers,
duties and functions of the Minister of State styled the Minister of
Small Business and Tourism under the [Canadian Tourism Commission
Act](/eng/acts/C-23.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-1661-E_hq_22386){#fn_81000-3-1661-E_hq_22386-ID0EDEAA}
that were transferred to that Minister by Order in Council P.C. 2017-71
of February 1, 2017[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-3-1661-E_hq_22450){#fn_81000-3-1661-E_hq_22450-ID0EBEAA}.
