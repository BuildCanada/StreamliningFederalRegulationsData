## Order Transferring to the Canada Border Services Agency the Control and Supervision of Certain Portions in the Department of Citizenship and Immigration {#order-transferring-to-the-canada-border-services-agency-the-control-and-supervision-of-certain-portions-in-the-department-of-citizenship-and-immigration .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2004-136
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2004-10-20

Order Transferring to the Canada Border Services Agency the Control and
Supervision of Certain Portions in the Department of Citizenship and
Immigration

P.C. 2004-1155 2004-10-08
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers to the Canada Border Services Agency the control and
supervision of the following portions of the public service in the
Department of Citizenship and Immigration, namely

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Ports and Border Management Directorate within
  the Admissibility Branch; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} those portions located within Canada that are
  responsible for carrying out port of entry functions, including marine
  security.
