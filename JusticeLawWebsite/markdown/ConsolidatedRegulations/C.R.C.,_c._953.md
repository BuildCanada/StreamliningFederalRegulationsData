## Indian Bands Revenue Moneys Regulations {#indian-bands-revenue-moneys-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 953
:::

[INDIAN ACT](/eng/acts/I-5)

Regulations Respecting Revenue Moneys of Certain Bands of Indians
::::

::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599209 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Indian Bands Revenue Moneys
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._953).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599211 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the account of a Band
    established pursuant to section 5; ([compte]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a band listed in the schedule to the [Indian Band
      Revenue Moneys Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-297), or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other band permitted to control, manage or
      expend its revenue moneys in part pursuant to subsection 69(1) of
      the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-244, s. 2

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599220 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Any expenditure by a Band
of its revenue moneys shall be subject to the [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every Band shall establish
an account with a chartered bank, a trust or loan company or a credit
union or caisse populaire.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  Band shall authorize three persons, two of whom shall be members of
  the Band, to sign cheques or orders for payment of money drawn on its
  account.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every cheque or order for payment of money drawn on
  the account of a Band shall be signed by at least two of the persons
  authorized by the Band pursuant to subsection (1).

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where the Minister has,
pursuant to section 66 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), authorized or
directed the expenditure of revenue moneys of a Band, the amount of
moneys so authorized or directed to be expended shall be paid to the
Band\'s account out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund.

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  Band shall engage an auditor to audit its account and to render an
  annual report in respect thereof.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the auditor\'s annual report shall, within
  seven days of its completion,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be posted in conspicuous places on the Band Reserve
    for examination by members of the Band; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be supplied to the Minister of Indian Affairs and
    Northern Development.
