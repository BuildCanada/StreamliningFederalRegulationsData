## Order Designating the Minister of Industry, who is a Federal Minister, to be the Minister for the Purposes of that Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-industry-who-is-a-federal-minister-to-be-the-minister-for-the-purposes-of-that-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2020-25
:::

[COLLEGE OF PATENT AGENTS AND TRADEMARK AGENTS ACT](/eng/acts/C-33.7)

Registration 2020-04-01

Order Designating the Minister of Industry, who is a Federal Minister,
to be the Minister for the Purposes of that Act

P.C. 2020-119 2020-03-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 4 of the [College of Patent
Agents and Trade-mark Agents Act](/eng/acts/C-33.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_320_hq_10667){#fn_320_hq_10667-ID0EBBAA},
designates the Minister of Industry, who is a federal minister, to be
the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
