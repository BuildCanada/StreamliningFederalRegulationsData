## Special Status Order, 1983 {#special-status-order-1983 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/83-148
:::

[CANADIAN OWNERSHIP AND CONTROL DETERMINATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-20)

Registration 1983-02-04

Order Respecting Persons Declared To Have a Special Status

P.C. 1983-332 1983-02-03
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minsiter of Energy, Mines and Resources, pursuant to subsection
45(6) of the [Canadian Ownership and Control Determination
Act](/eng/acts/C-20)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EDBAA}, is
pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order respecting persons declared to
have a special status](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-956).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnote1star_e}S.C.
  1980-81-82, c. 107
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-875134 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Special Status Order, 1983](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-148).

declared to have a special status within the meaning of subsection 45(6)
of the [Canadian Ownership and Control Determination
