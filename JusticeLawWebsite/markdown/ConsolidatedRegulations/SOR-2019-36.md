[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 2,
remission is granted of the surtax paid under the [Order Imposing a
Surtax on the Importation of Certain Steel
Goods](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-206), as it read on October 25, 2018,
in respect of goods of classes 3 and 7 of the schedule to that Order
originating in Mexico.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods referred to in section 1 were imported into
  Canada on or after October 25, 2018 and before the day on which this
  Order comes into force;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an import permit was issued for the goods under the
  [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19) in respect of item 82
  of the [Import Control List](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._604) and is
  valid at the time at which the claim for remission is made;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the importer makes a claim for remission in writing
  to the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within a
  period of 200 days beginning on October 25, 2018; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the claim is supported by evidence that the import
  permit issued in respect of the goods is valid at the time at which
  the claim for remission is made and includes the permit number.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on February 2, 2019, but if it is registered after that day, it comes
into force on the day on which it is registered.
