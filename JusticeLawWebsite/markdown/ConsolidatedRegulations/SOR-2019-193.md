## Reimbursement Related to Assisted Human Reproduction Regulations {#reimbursement-related-to-assisted-human-reproduction-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2019-193
:::

[ASSISTED HUMAN REPRODUCTION ACT](/eng/acts/A-13.4)

Registration 2019-06-10

Reimbursement Related to Assisted Human Reproduction Regulations

P.C. 2019-751 2019-06-09
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 66(1) of the [Assisted Human
Reproduction Act](/eng/acts/A-13.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81200-2-406_hq_24787){#fn_81200-2-406_hq_24787-ID0EDADA},
the Minister of Health has laid a copy of the proposed[Reimbursement
Related to Assisted Human Reproduction
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-193) before each House of
Parliament, substantially in the annexed form;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to subsection
65(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81200-2-401_E_hq_20863){#fn_81200-2-401_E_hq_20863-ID0EGACA}
of the [Assisted Human Reproduction Act](/eng/acts/A-13.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81200-2-406_hq_24787){#fn_81200-2-406_hq_24787-ID0EDACA},
makes the annexed [Reimbursement Related to Assisted Human Reproduction
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-193).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1141410 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Assisted Human Reproduction
    Act](/eng/acts/A-13.4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dependant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dependant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a donor of
    sperm or ova or a surrogate mother, a person who resides with them
    and is dependent on them by reason of age or mental or physical
    incapacity. ([personne à charge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Reimbursement of Expenditures Under Subsection 12(1) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1141415 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenditures --- donating sperm or ova

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following expenditures
incurred by a donor in the course of donating sperm or ova may be
reimbursed under subsection 12(1) of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} travel expenditures, including expenditures for
  transportation, parking, meals and accommodation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for the care of dependants or pets;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for counselling services;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for legal services and disbursements;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for obtaining any
  [[drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or [[device]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as
  defined in section 2 of the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for obtaining products or services that
  are provided or recommended in writing by a person authorized under
  the laws of a province to practise medicine in that province;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for obtaining a written recommendation
  referred to in paragraph (f);

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for health, disability, travel or life
  insurance coverage; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for obtaining or confirming medical or
  other records.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenditures --- maintenance and
transport of [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following expenditures
incurred by any person may be reimbursed under subsection 12(1) of the
Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for the maintenance of an [in
  vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo, including storage; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for the transport of an [in
  vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo, including expenditures for
  preparing it for transport, for the shipping container and for
  preparing the container for transport.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenditures --- surrogacy

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The following expenditures
incurred by a surrogate mother in relation to her surrogacy may be
reimbursed under subsection 12(1) of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} travel expenditures, including expenditures for
  transportation, parking, meals and accommodation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for the care of dependants or pets;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for counselling services;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for legal services and disbursements;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for obtaining any
  [[drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or [[device]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as
  defined in section 2 of the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for obtaining products or services that
  are provided or recommended in writing by a person authorized under
  the laws of a province to assess, monitor and provide health care to a
  woman during her pregnancy, delivery or the postpartum period;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for obtaining a written recommendation
  referred to in paragraph (f);

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for the services of a midwife or doula;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for groceries, excluding non-food items;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for maternity clothes;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for telecommunications;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for prenatal exercise classes;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} expenditures related to the delivery;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for health, disability, travel or life
  insurance coverage; and

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} expenditures for obtaining or confirming medical or
  other records.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenditures --- use of an automobile

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In the case of expenditures
referred to in paragraphs 2(a), 3(b) and 4(a), the maximum amount that
may be reimbursed for the use of an automobile, other than an automobile
used by a transportation service that provides a receipt, is the amount
that is calculated on the basis of the distance driven in kilometres and
the applicable automobile allowance rate posted on the Canada Revenue
Agency's website for the year in which the transport or transportation
occurred.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preliminary requirements for
reimbursement

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A person may only reimburse
the applicable expenditure referred to in sections 2 to 4 to the person
requesting reimbursement if they have obtained the following documents:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a declaration dated and signed by the person who
  requests the reimbursement that sets out

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their name and address,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the nature of each expenditure incurred,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} with respect to each expenditure, the amount
    incurred and, if less than the amount incurred, the amount requested
    for reimbursement,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the date on which each expenditure was incurred,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a transport or transportation
    expenditure for the use of an automobile, other than an automobile
    used by a transportation service that provides a receipt, with
    respect to each trip,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the addresses of the points of departure and
      destination, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the total distance in kilometres driven between
      those two points,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that each expenditure was
    incurred in the course of donating sperm or ova, in the maintenance
    or transport of an [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo or in
    relation to a surrogacy, as applicable,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} a statement for each expenditure indicating that
    the amount has not been paid to them by any other source, in full or
    in part, and

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} a statement confirming that all of the
    information contained in the declaration is accurate and complete to
    the best of their knowledge;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, a copy of the written recommendation
  of a person authorized under the laws of a province to practise
  medicine in that province and obtained in relation to a product or
  service for which a request for reimbursement has been made;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, a copy of the written recommendation
  of a person authorized under the laws of a province to assess, monitor
  and provide health care to a woman during her pregnancy, delivery or
  the postpartum period and obtained in relation to a product or service
  for which a request for reimbursement has been made; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a receipt for each expenditure for which the
  reimbursement is requested that identifies the date on which the
  expenditure was incurred, except in the case of transport or
  transportation expenditures for the use of an automobile in accordance
  with section 5.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reimbursement --- sign and affirm

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A person who reimburses an
expenditure set out in a declaration must indicate on the declaration
the amount of each expenditure that they reimbursed and the date of the
reimbursement and must sign it to affirm that information.

## [Reimbursement of Surrogate Mother for Loss of Work-Related Income Under Subsection 12(3) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1141468 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preliminary requirements for
reimbursement

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A person may only reimburse
a surrogate mother for a loss of work-related income incurred during her
pregnancy under subsection 12(3) of the Act if they have obtained the
following documents:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a declaration dated and signed by the surrogate
  mother that sets out

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} her name and address,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the start and end dates of the period during her
    pregnancy when she did not work for a reason certified by a
    qualified medical practitioner under paragraph 12(3)(a) of the Act,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the amount requested for reimbursement,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that she has not received
    compensation from any other source, in full or in part, for the loss
    of income, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that all of the information
    submitted in the declaration is accurate and complete to the best of
    her knowledge;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} supporting evidence of the income that she would have
  earned had she not been absent from work for the period specified in
  subparagraph (a)(ii); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the certification provided by a qualified
  medical practitioner in paragraph 12(3)(a) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reimbursement --- sign and affirm

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A person who reimburses a
surrogate mother for a loss of work-related income must indicate on the
declaration the amount of the loss of income that they reimbursed and
the date of the reimbursement, and must sign it to affirm that
information.

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1141481 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption regarding receipt

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A person who reimburses
transport or transportation expenditures is exempt from the application
of subsection 12(2) of the Act if the expenditures were incurred for the
use of an automobile, other than an automobile used by a transportation
service that provides a receipt.

## [Maintenance of Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1141484 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintain records --- reimbursement for
expenditures

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who reimburses expenditures referred to in these Regulations
  must, for each reimbursement, maintain a record of all documents
  obtained for the purposes of that reimbursement for a period of six
  years after the date of the reimbursement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintain records --- reimbursement for
  loss of income

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who reimburses a surrogate mother for a loss
  of work-related income incurred during her pregnancy must, for each
  reimbursement, maintain a record of all documents obtained for the
  purposes of that reimbursement for a period of six years after the
  date of the reimbursement.

## [Provision of Records to Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1141490 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice from Minister

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by written notice, require a person who must maintain a
  record in relation to a reimbursement under section 11 to provide to
  the Minister any record or additional information related to the
  reimbursement by the date specified in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to provide records or
  information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person who has been notified under subsection (1)
  must provide to the Minister any required record or additional
  information related to the reimbursement by the date specified in the
  notice.
