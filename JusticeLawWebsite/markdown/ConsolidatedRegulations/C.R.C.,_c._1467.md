## Safe Working Practices Regulations {#safe-working-practices-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1467
:::

[CANADA SHIPPING ACT, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15)

Regulations Respecting Safe Working Practices for the Protection of
Persons Employed on Ships
::::

:::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521200 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Safe Working Practices
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1467).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521202 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Shipping
    Act](/eng/acts/S-9); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Board of Steamship
    Inspection established pursuant to section 369 of the Act;
    ([Bureau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hot work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hot work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means welding, burning, rivetting,
    drilling, grinding, chipping or any other work where flame is used
    or sparks are produced; ([travail à chaud]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a steamship inspector appointed under the Act, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person designated as an inspector pursuant to
      section 91; ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[marine chemist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marine chemist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has graduated from an educational institution
      approved by the Board and has completed

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} courses in chemical engineering, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a general course with a major in chemistry, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has obtained a fellowship in the Chemical
      Institute of Canada,

    and thereafter has had at least three years experience in chemical
    or engineering work, of which a minimum of 150 working hours has
    been gained under proper supervision in ship board work involving
    the testing and inspection of tank vessels and other vessels in the
    application of gas hazard control standards prescribed by the Board;
    ([chimiste de la marine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes, in respect of any working
    area, structure, machinery or equipment, the person who has the
    general management and control thereof;
    ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualified person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualified person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of work that is required by law to be
      performed by the holder of a licence, certificate or other
      authority, a person who is the holder of such licence, certificate
      or other authority, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of work that is not required by law to
      be performed by the holder of a licence, certificate or other
      authority, a person who, in the opinion of his employer, possesses
      the knowledge and experience necessary to perform the work safely
      and competently; ([personne compétente]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[scaffold]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[scaffold]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a working platform supported
    from below; ([échafaud]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[scaffolding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[scaffolding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the structure that
    supports a scaffold; ([échafaudage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[stage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[stage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a working platform supported
    from above; ([plate-forme volante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[working area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[working area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any area in which work is being performed on
      board a ship,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to persons employed in the
      maintenance or repair of a ship, any area immediately adjacent to
      the ship, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to persons employed in the loading
      or unloading of a ship, any area on shore that is within the reach
      of any derrick, crane or other hoisting equipment employed in
      loading or unloading the ship and the immediate approaches to such
      an area, but does not include any sheds, warehouses or any part of
      a wharf forward or aft of the ship's mooring lines. ([lieu de
      travail]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- 1987, c. 7, s. 84(F)
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521229 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
and in respect of the employment of persons in any working area
associated with any ship in Canada or on any Canadian ship outside
Canada.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521231 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every employer shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} arrange that work in a working area is carried out in
  a manner that does not endanger the safety or health of any person
  employed in the work or in connection therewith;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} adopt and carry out reasonable procedures and
  techniques designed or intended to prevent or reduce the risk of
  employment injury in the operation or carrying out of the work; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} without limiting the generality of paragraphs (a) and
  (b), arrange that the safe working practices set out in these
  Regulations will be complied with.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every owner of any working
area, structure, machinery or equipment used in the operation,
maintenance or repair of a ship, or in the loading or unloading of a
ship, shall ensure that the working area, structure, machinery or
equipment is maintained in a safe condition and complies with these
Regulations.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every employer or owner
shall ensure that a qualified person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is in charge in every working area; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} makes periodic inspections of every working area or
  structure and every item of machinery or equipment to ascertain that
  safe working conditions are maintained.

<!-- -->

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person engaged in any employment to which these Regulations apply
  shall, in the course of his employment,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} take all reasonable and necessary precautions to
    ensure his own safety and the safety of his fellow employees; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at all appropriate times use such devices and wear
    such articles of clothing or equipment as are intended for his
    protection and furnished to him by his employer, or required
    pursuant to these Regulations to be used or worn by him.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in subsection (1) relieves an employer from
  any duty imposed upon him by section 4.

<!-- -->

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall use any working area, structure, machinery or equipment
  for any purpose other than the purpose for which it is intended to be
  used.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall use any structure, machinery or
  equipment that has been re-assembled after being dismantled, in whole
  or in part, until it has been examined by a qualified person and found
  to be in a safe condition.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No person shall tamper
with, or make ineffective, any safeguard or safety device that is
intended to maintain the safe condition of any working area, structure,
machinery or equipment.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** No person shall cause an
emergency exit to be obstructed or otherwise made ineffective.

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who observes in a working area an unsafe condition or situation
  shall immediately report that condition or situation to the person in
  charge of the working area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The correction of an unsafe condition or situation
  referred to in subsection (1) shall be made only by a qualified
  person.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsections (1) and (2), in an
  emergency any person may take whatever measures are necessary to
  prevent loss of human life or injury to persons.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Where a person is
employed in an isolated working area, the employer of that person shall
make arrangements to verify at reasonable intervals that the person has
not been involved in an accident in that working area.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Any person who becomes
aware of an accident or an injury to a person in a working area shall
immediately report the accident or injury to the person in charge of the
working area.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** No person shall be
permitted in any working area whose ability to work is, in the opinion
of the person in charge of the area, impaired by alcohol or a drug.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** No person with a
disability shall be assigned to any work of any kind when, owing to the
nature or location of the work, the disability is likely to endanger
that person or any other person.

## [Lifting Appliances and other Equipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521258 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Any person operating any
lifting appliance or powered mobile equipment in a working area shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be a qualified person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} comply with the traffic laws applicable in the
  working area; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} comply with every notice and sign applicable to the
  operation of the appliance or equipment placed in the working area by
  the owner of the working area or by the employer of the operator of
  the appliance or equipment.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a lifting
appliance shall ensure that a notice is permanently affixed to the
appliance showing its safe working load.

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** No person shall be
permitted on any part of any lifting appliance or power operated
equipment while it is in operation except

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where safe accommodation is available thereon for
  that person; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where essential for the maintenance or repair of the
  appliance or equipment and safe access is provided for that person to
  perform such maintenance or repair.

<!-- -->

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Mobile
  equipment shall be fitted with guards to protect the operator of the
  equipment from falling objects or shifting loads, unless the nature of
  the operation of the equipment makes the fitting of such guards
  impracticable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall leave mobile equipment unattended
  unless the equipment has been properly secured to prevent its
  movement.

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The moving parts of the
machinery of power operated equipment shall be fitted with guards where
such parts constitute a hazard.

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Every deck of a ship,
every wharf and every other elevated working area on which mobile
equipment is used shall be fitted with guards that will prevent the
equipment from falling over the sides of the working area.

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  enclosed working area in which equipment powered by an internal
  combustion engine is used shall be ventilated to ensure that the
  carbon monoxide concentration in the atmosphere of the working area
  does not exceed 50 parts per million.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall operate equipment powered by an
  internal combustion engine in an enclosed working area unless there is
  at least one other person in attendance at the entrance to that
  working area.

<!-- -->

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), no person
  shall refuel equipment

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the hold of a ship;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when its engine is running; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} when there is any source of ignition in the
    vicinity of the equipment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Equipment in a hold or an enclosed space of a ship
  may be refuelled if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} only those persons engaged in the refuelling are in
    the hold or space;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one person in attendance has a suitable fire
    extinguisher ready for use;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} only the minimum quantity of fuel needed to operate
    each unit is taken into the hold or space at one time;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of liquefied gas, refuelling of the
    equipment is to be achieved only by the replacement of spent
    cylinders; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} fuel is not transferred into containers other than
    the fuel tank.

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Powered mobile equipment
shall be fitted with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an effective audible warning device; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sufficient lights to maintain safe working conditions
  when operated in darkness or a dimly lighted working area.

## [Holds, Tanks and other Compartments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521289 .Part}

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  not in use, every hatch or other opening that leads to a hold, tank or
  other compartment exceeding 1.5 m in depth shall be securely covered
  or fenced to a height of 900 mm unless the opening is fitted with a
  coaming of not less than 760 mm in height.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every opening in a deck or tank top that leads to a
  hold, tank or other compartment not exceeding 1.5 m in depth shall be
  covered, fenced or marked in such manner as necessary to prevent loss
  of life or injury to persons.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-632, s. 1
:::

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Hatch
  beams, covers and plugs shall be permanently marked to indicate the
  deck, hatch and hatch section to which they belong, unless the
  interchangeability of the beams, covers or plugs makes the application
  of such markings unnecessary.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a hatch beam, cover or plug is marked as
  required by subsection (1), it shall only be fitted in the deck, hatch
  and hatch section as indicated by the markings thereon.

<!-- -->

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  hatch beam, cover or plug is removed from a hatch it shall be placed
  clear of all working areas and securely stowed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any hatch beam, cover or plug that cannot be securely
  stowed between decks shall be taken to the upper deck, placed clear of
  all working areas and securely stowed.

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Any person who is between
decks shall stand clear of an overhead opening when a hatch beam, cover
or plug is being removed or replaced at the hatch.

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** A tarpaulin or similar
hatch covering shall not be spread over a hatch opening unless all hatch
beams, covers and plugs for the hatch are securely fitted in place.

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall enter a pump room, cofferdam, ballast tank, oil tank or
  similar compartment unless he is wearing a breathing apparatus.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where a compartment referred to in subsection (1)
    has not contained a substance that vaporizes to produce a hazardous
    or dangerous gas and the compartment has been ventilated to the
    satisfaction of a qualified person, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the compartment has contained a substance
    that vaporizes to produce a hazardous or dangerous gas and the
    compartment has been gas freed and has, subsequently, been tested

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by a marine chemist, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where a marine chemist is not available, by a
      qualified person, trained in the use of gas testing equipment, who
      has at least three years experience of which a minimum of 150
      working hours has been gained under proper supervision in the
      testing and inspection of tanks,

    and found safe for persons to enter and for the work to be performed
    therein.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A certificate or statement shall be completed for
  every compartment that has been ventilated or tested as required by
  subsection (2), and such certificate or statement shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be available for the information of any person
    entering the compartment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} indicate

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the ship in which the compartment is
      located,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any special precaution that is to be observed,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any subsequent tests that are, in the opinion
      of the marine chemist or qualified person, required to maintain
      the safe condition of the compartment; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be signed by the person responsible for the
    ventilation or testing of the compartment.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a compartment has been ventilated or gas freed
  as required by subsection (2), effective ventilation shall be
  continued in order to maintain the safe condition for the period that
  persons are to be inside the compartment.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1987, c. 7, s. 84(F)
:::

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall enter or remain in a cofferdam, ballast tank, oil tank or
  any similar compartment unless there is another person in attendance
  at the compartment entrance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is in attendance at an entrance to a
  compartment referred to in subsection (1) shall, except where an
  emergency occurs with respect to the person inside the compartment,
  remain in attendance until the person inside leaves the compartment.

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** Before a hold, tank or
other compartment is sealed, the person in charge of the working area
shall ascertain that no person is inside the compartment.

## [Fire Prevention and Protection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521325 .Part}

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In any
  working area where flammable gas, vapour or dust is present in the
  atmosphere, no person shall use

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any electrical equipment or fittings that are not
    gas-tight; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any equipment or materials that are
    spark-producing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall take spark-producing materials into a
  hold, tank or other compartment that contains flammable gas, vapour or
  dust.

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** Whenever hot work is to
be performed in a working area

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a qualified person shall be assigned to patrol the
  working area and adjoining areas and maintain therein a fire
  protection watch for the duration of the work and for a period of 30
  minutes thereafter; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sufficient fire extinguishers shall be provided in
  the working area and adjoining areas.

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** Oxygen shall not be used

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for ventilation purposes;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} as a pressure testing medium;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to blow out pipelines;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to operate pneumatic equipment;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} to start internal combustion engines; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} to clean working areas, equipment or other articles.

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** Every galley range and
the exhaust hood and duct thereof shall be kept free from accumulated
grease and other waste deposits.

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Space
  heaters shall not be fitted on board a ship without the permission of
  the owner of the ship.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When space heaters are fitted on board a ship, they
  shall be safely located and securely fastened in place.

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** When a ship's fire pumps
are inoperative and the ship is in dry dock or moored alongside a wharf,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sufficient fire hydrants and hoses shall be
  available, adjacent to the ship, for use in extinguishing a fire on
  board the ship; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a water supply, sufficient to fight a fire on board
  the ship, shall be connected to the ship's fire main.

<!-- -->

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Fire
  alarms, fire hydrants, sprinkler and smothering system control valves,
  fire extinguishers and other fire extinguishing equipment shall be
  accessible at all times.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Tools and fittings for use with fire hydrants and
  hoses or with sprinkler and smothering systems shall be located
  alongside the fire extinguishing equipment and secured by means of
  light chains or suitable fixtures.

<!-- -->

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Whenever a fire extinguisher is
  discharged or emptied, it shall be recharged as soon as possible and
  returned to its proper location.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a fire extinguisher is removed from a ship for
  service or overhaul, an equivalent means of fire protection shall be
  provided for the area from which the extinguisher was taken.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply in respect of a ship that
  is laid up when fire extinguishers have been emptied or removed and
  alternative arrangements have been made for fire protection.

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** No person shall overhaul
or repair a smothering gas system unless the gas bottles thereof have
been disconnected or otherwise rendered inoperative.

## [Hot Work Operations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521356 .Part}

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Hot
  work shall not be performed in a working area

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where inflammable gas, vapour or dust may be
    present in the atmosphere, unless the area has been freed of gas,
    tested by a marine chemist and found to be safe for that work to be
    performed therein;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where an explosive or inflammable substance may be
    present in the working area, unless a qualified person has ensured
    that adequate protection exists to permit that work to be safely
    performed therein; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the area is a tank that has previously
    contained petroleum or petroleum products, until the tank is found
    to be safe for the work to be performed therein by a qualified
    person who has had at least three years experience of which a
    minimum of 150 working hours has been gained under proper
    supervision in the testing and inspection of such tanks.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a marine chemist finds the atmosphere in a
  working area to be safe as described in paragraph (1)(a) or where a
  qualified person has ensured that adequate protection exists in a
  working area as described in paragraph (1)(b), he shall complete and
  sign a certificate or statement to that effect.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The certificate or statement referred to in
  subsection (2) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be available for the information of any person
    entering the working area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} indicate

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the location of the working area,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any special precaution that is to be observed,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any subsequent tests that are, in the opinion
      of the marine chemist or qualified person, required to maintain
      the safe condition.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1987, c. 7, s. 84(F)
:::

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** When harmful fumes, gas
or vapour are liable to be produced by hot work and to affect an
enclosed working area,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the working area shall be well ventilated; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person in the working area shall wear a breathing
  apparatus.

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** Cables of electric
welding equipment, and cylinders and pipes of gas welding or burning
equipment, shall be placed clear of areas used for vehicles unless
adequate protection for the cables, cylinders and pipes is provided.

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** Gas cylinders of welding
and burning equipment shall be placed securely in an upright position
when in use.

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** When equipment used for
hot work is left unattended, the person in charge of the working area
shall ensure that the equipment is in a safe condition.

## [Scaffolding, Scaffolds and Stages]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521377 .Part}

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** Scaffolding, scaffolds
and stages shall be constructed by qualified persons.

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** Scaffolding shall be
placed on firm footings and where it is built on an uneven surface,
substantial base plates shall be fitted to the scaffolding to maintain
its stability.

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), every
  scaffold shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be made with planks that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are not less than 50 mm thick by 250 mm wide,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are supported not less than 150 mm and not more
      than 300 mm from each end by plank supports spaced not more than 3
      m apart, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} are of the same thickness;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have no significant openings between adjoining
    planks;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} have a width of not less than 500 mm;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} have a flat and horizontal working surface; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be fitted with guard rails at a height of 900 mm
    above the scaffold except on the side thereof where the work to be
    performed would be hindered by the guard rails.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A safe means of access, secured to prevent its
  accidental movement, shall be provided to and from the working level
  of every scaffold.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A scaffold may be constructed of materials other than
  wood if the strength and safety features of the scaffold when so
  constructed are not less than those of a wooden scaffold.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-632, s. 2
:::

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 51,
every stage shall be made with planks that are, in the opinion of a
qualified person having regard to the distance between the plank
supports, of sufficient strength to carry the load the stage is intended
to support.

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  stage shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be made with planks that are not less than 50 mm
    thick by 250 mm wide;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be not more than 3.6 m in length where the stage is
    made of planks not more than 50 mm thick;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} have a flat and horizontal working surface;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be fitted with guard lines, where the stage is to
    be used at a height greater than 3 m; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be fitted with effective means for holding the
    stage away from the working area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a stage is more than one plank in width,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the planks shall be of the same thickness;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there shall be no significant openings between
    adjoining planks; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the planks shall be secured together on the
    underside with cleats not less than 25 mm thick and 150 mm wide that
    are securely nailed to the planks and spaced at intervals of not
    more than 1.2 m.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Stage supports shall be placed not less than 150 mm
  and not more than 300 mm from the plank ends.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-632, s. 3
:::

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** The supports and ropes or
tackle carrying a stage shall have a factor of safety of not less than
six.

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** Every person who is
required to work on a stage at a height greater than 3 m shall wear a
safety belt fitted with a safety line that is rigged to a life line so
as to limit the free fall of a person from the stage to not more than
1.2 m.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-632, s. 4
:::

## [Ladders and Gangways]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521413 .Part}

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  access ladder or gangway between a ship and the shore or between one
  ship and another ship shall provide a safe means of access between the
  ship and shore or between the ships, as the case may be.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every ship that is in dry dock or moored alongside a
  wharf or other ship shall be fitted with at least one access ladder or
  gangway that, in the opinion of a qualified person, complies with
  subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an access ladder or gangway leads to a location
  on board a ship at a height of more than 1 m above the deck, safe
  access to the deck shall be provided by means of a series of steps or
  some similar structure.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a bulwark ladder is used to provide the safe
  access to the deck of a ship required by subsection (3), it shall be
  firmly secured to the bulwark so as to prevent its shifting, slipping
  or pivoting and shall be equipped with two handhold stanchions

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each not less than 40 mm in diameter;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} each extending not less than 1.2 m above the top of
    the bulwark;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fitted at the point of boarding or leaving the ship
    not less than 0.7 m and not more than 0.8 m apart; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} firmly secured to the ship's structure at or near
    the base of the ladder and at a higher point.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-480, s. 1
- SOR/79-632, s. 5
:::

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  access ladder and gangway shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be maintained in a safe condition;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be secured to prevent its shifting and slipping;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be suitably rigged and maintained to compensate for
    the movement of the ship;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be adequately lighted; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} have a lifebuoy with line, strategically placed and
    ready for immediate use.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When an access ladder is being used by a person to
  board or disembark from a ship, at least one end of the ladder shall
  be fastened securely and, if necessary to ensure safety, a person,
  other than a person engaged in manoeuvring the ship, shall be
  stationed at the ladder to assist the person using it.

<!-- -->

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Safety
  nets shall be fitted under every part of an access ladder and gangway
  except where, in the opinion of a qualified person,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ladder or gangway and the approaches thereto
    are constructed in a manner that makes the fitting of a safety net
    unnecessary; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fitting of a safety net is impracticable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Safety nets shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} extend on both sides of an access ladder or a
    gangway for a distance of 1.8 m; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be kept taut at all times.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-632, s. 6
:::

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** Where a platform is
provided at the bottom of an access ladder or gangway, the platform
shall be flat and horizontal.

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** Every Jacob's ladder
shall be of sufficient length to reach the intended landing point and
the means of attachment thereof to the ship shall be effective and
maintained in a safe and good condition.

**[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** Every portable ladder
shall be maintained in a safe and good condition and shall be secured to
prevent its shifting and slipping when in use.

**[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** Every ladder, whether
portable or permanently secured, shall be positioned in such a manner
that it is not necessary for a person to use the underside of the
ladder.

## [Electrical]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521446 .Part}

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** No person other than a
qualified person shall work on the installation, maintenance and repair
of electrical equipment.

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** The person in charge of a
working area in which work is being performed on electrical equipment
that is isolated from its power supply shall, where the power supply
could be accidentally reconnected to the electrical equipment, ensure
that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} locking devices are fitted to the circuit breakers or
  control switches for the isolated electrical equipment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person is in attendance at the position where the
  electrical equipment is isolated;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} effective warning notices are placed at the position
  where the electrical equipment is isolated; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} when fuses are removed to isolate electrical
  equipment, the fuses are kept in the possession of a qualified person.

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** No person shall work on
an electrical circuit that has not been isolated from the power supply
unless he is protected by a rubber mat, rubber boots, rubber gloves or
insulated tools or any combination thereof.

**[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** Defective electrical
equipment shall be disconnected from its power supply by a means other
than the control switch, and notices shall be placed on the equipment
and at the control switch to indicate that the equipment is defective.

- **[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Electrical fuses shall be of the
  correct ampere rating and fault capacity rating for the circuit in
  which they are installed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall replace missing or burnt-out fuses
  without the permission of a qualified person.

**[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** Control switches for all
electrically operated machinery shall clearly indicate the "ON" and
"OFF" switch positions.

**[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** Where necessary,
electrical equipment shall be grounded and all electrical connections
shall be properly insulated or covered to protect persons from
electrical shock.

- **[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Grounded electrical equipment and
  appliances shall be used only when connected into a matching grounded
  electrical outlet fitting.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A three wire power supply cable on electrical
  equipment or appliances shall not be altered or changed for the
  purpose of using the equipment or appliances on a two wire power
  supply.

**[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** Power supply cables for
portable electrical equipment shall be placed clear of areas used for
vehicles unless adequate protection for the cables is provided.

**[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** Electrical appliances and
tools shall be constructed and used in accordance with the Canadian
Standards Association specifications or with any other specifications
acceptable to the Board.

## [Personal Protective Equipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521465 .Part}

**[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** The personal protective
equipment referred to in sections 72 to 74 shall comply with the
Canadian Standards Association specifications for that equipment or,
where a higher standard is required by a provincial authority having
jurisdiction over the use of that equipment, the higher standard
required by that authority.

**[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** Every person employed in
an area where there is danger of injury to the feet from falling or
moving objects shall wear safety shoes or boots, which shall be made
available by his employer.

**[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** Every person employed in
an area where there is danger of injury to the head from falling or
moving objects shall wear a safety hat or helmet fitted with a chin
strap.

**[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** Every person shall wear a
buoyancy aid that is not dependent upon manual control to produce its
buoyancy when

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} required to work over water; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} transferring between a ship and a tow.

**[[75]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-75 .sectionLabel}** Except as provided in
section 74, every person shall wear a life jacket

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} when crossing between a ship and the shore during the
  berthing or docking of the ship; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} when crossing between one ship and another ship,
  where one or both of the ships are not moored.

**[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76 .sectionLabel}** Every person who is
required to work on unmanned barges shall have two strips of reflective
tape, each of which is not less than 19 mm wide by 230 mm long, attached
to both front and back of his exterior jacket or coat when darkness or a
dimly lighted condition exists in the working area.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-632, s. 7
:::

- **[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person employed in welding or burning operations shall wear eye
  protection designed to protect his eyes from the glare of the
  operations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person employed in rivetting, drilling, grinding or
  chipping operations shall wear eye protection designed to protect his
  eyes from flying particles.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person exposed to a threat of danger to his
  eyes from flying grit, chips or excessive heat or light shall wear
  suitable eye protection.

**[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78 .sectionLabel}** Every employer shall
provide the appropriate protective clothing and respiratory equipment to
an employee required to work with a substance or in an atmosphere that
may adversely affect the employee's health.

**[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** Every person who is
required to work at a height greater than 3 m, on a mast or at the edge
of any other structure that is not provided with guard rails, shall wear
a safety belt fitted with a safety line that is rigged so as to limit
the free fall of a person to not more than 1.2 m.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-632, s. 8
:::

- **[[80]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Safety
  belts, safety lines and life lines shall be of sufficient strength to
  support a load of 1 134 kg

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a safety line or life line is subject to
  chafing, it shall be made of wire rope or wire core fibre rope.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-632, s. 9
:::

## [Pressure Vessels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521491 .Part}

- **[[81]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-81 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall enter or remain in a boiler unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a qualified person has ascertained that the boiler
    is isolated from every other steaming boiler; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is another person in attendance at a manhole
    opening of the boiler.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The qualified person referred to in paragraph (1)(a)
  shall ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} locking devices are fitted to the boiler isolating
    valves;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person is in attendance at the position where the
    boiler is isolated;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} effective warning notices are placed at the
    position where the boiler is isolated; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} blank flanges are fitted between the boiler and
    every other steaming boiler.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is in attendance at a manhole
  opening of a boiler shall, except where an emergency occurs with
  respect to the person inside the boiler, remain in attendance until
  the person inside leaves the boiler.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall enter the furnace side of any boiler
  that contains

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} hot water at a temperature in excess of 55°C; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} steam under pressure.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-632, s. 10
:::

**[[82]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-82 .sectionLabel}** Before a pressure vessel
is sealed, the person in charge of the working area in which the
pressure vessel is located shall ascertain that no person is inside the
pressure vessel.

**[[83]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-83 .sectionLabel}** When manhole covers are
to be removed from a boiler, the uppermost manhole cover shall be
removed first.

- **[[84]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-84
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), no
  person, other than a person appointed by an authority acceptable to
  the Board, shall alter or deal with a safety or relief valve of a
  boiler or other pressure vessel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A qualified person may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a case of emergency, adjust a valve referred to
    in subsection (1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} overhaul a valve referred to in subsection (1),
    prior to its inspection by a person appointed by an authority
    acceptable to the Board.

## [General Requirements and Working Practices]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521514 .Part}

**[[85]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-85 .sectionLabel}** Every person in charge of
a working area that is in darkness or in which a dimly lighted condition
exists shall ensure that portable lights are provided to any person
required to enter that working area.

**[[86]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-86 .sectionLabel}** Every person on board a
towed ship for the purpose of handling the mooring lines of that ship
shall maintain visible or audible contact with the operator of the
towing ship either directly or with the assistance of other persons.

- **[[87]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-87
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), no person
  shall use as a walkway the top of any rail car that is being carried
  on a barge.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person may ascend to the top of any rail car that
  is being carried on a barge and proceed along the top of that rail car
  for the purpose of placing or attending to navigation lights.

**[[88]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-88 .sectionLabel}** No person shall, while a
ship is underway, go over the side of the ship to carry out routine
maintenance thereof.

**[[89]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-89 .sectionLabel}** No person shall go
between a ship's side and a dock facing or between one ship and another
ship, unless there is an adequate fender secured to the ship's side or
the dock facing, or to the side of one of the ships, as the case may be.

- **[[90]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-90 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} manually lift or carry, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require any other person to manually lift or carry

  a weight that is likely to jeopardize the health or safety of any
  person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall handle, except by mechanical means,
  any material or object, the shape, size, toxicity or other
  characteristic of which is likely to jeopardize his health or safety.

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521528 .Part}

**[[91]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Transport
may designate as an inspector any person in the public service of Canada
who, in his opinion, is qualified to be so designated.

- **[[92]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-92 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector may at any reasonable time go on board any ship, visit any
  working area, or examine any structure, machinery or equipment, in or
  in respect of which he has reason to believe that any provision of
  these Regulations has been contravened.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the owner of any working area, structure, machinery
    or equipment,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person employed in the operation, maintenance
    or repair of a ship, or in the loading or unloading of a ship, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an officer of an organization that represents the
    owner or a person described in paragraph (b),

  complains that a contravention of these Regulations has taken place,
  an inspector shall investigate the circumstances giving rise to the
  complaint.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every complaint described in subsection (2), shall,
  where required by an inspector, be made in writing and signed by the
  person making the complaint.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where an investigation is made pursuant to this
  section, any person in possession of any records relating to a ship or
  any working area, structure, machinery or equipment, shall, when
  requested to do so by an inspector, produce such records and furnish a
  true copy thereof.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No person shall obstruct or hinder an inspector in
  the exercise of his powers or the carrying out of his duties under
  these Regulations.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Any person, when requested to do so by an inspector,
  shall give the inspector all reasonable assistance in his power to
  enable the inspector to carry out his duties under this section and
  shall furnish the inspector with such information as he may reasonably
  require.
