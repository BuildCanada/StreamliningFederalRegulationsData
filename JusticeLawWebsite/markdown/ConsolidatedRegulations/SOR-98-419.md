## Imports of Certain Textile and Apparel Goods from Chile Customs Duty Remission Order {#imports-of-certain-textile-and-apparel-goods-from-chile-customs-duty-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-419
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1998-08-26

Imports of Certain Textile and Apparel Goods from Chile Customs Duty
Remission Order

P.C. 1998-1455  1998-08-26
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 115 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_ae){#fn_ae-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Imports of Certain Textile and Apparel Goods from Chile Customs Duty
Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-419).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1023296 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[apparel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[apparel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods referred to in Chapters
    61 and 62 of the List of Tariff Provisions that are cut or knit to
    shape and are sewn or otherwise assembled in Chile from fabric or
    yarn produced or obtained outside the free trade area.
    ([vêtements]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fabric and made-up goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fabric and made-up goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cotton or man-made fibre fabric and cotton or
      man-made fibre made-up textile goods referred to in Chapters 52 to
      55 of the List of Tariff Provisions, other than goods containing
      36% or more by weight of wool or fine animal hair, and in Chapters
      58, 60 and 63 of the List of Tariff Provisions that are woven or
      knitted in Chile from yarn produced or obtained outside the free
      trade area or that are knitted in Chile from yarn spun in Chile
      from fibre produced or obtained outside the free trade area;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods of subheading No. 9404.90 that are finished
      and cut and sewn or otherwise assembled from fabrics referred to
      in any of subheading Nos. 5208.11 to 5208.29, 5209.11 to 5209.29,
      5210.11 to 5210.29, 5211.11 to 5211.29, 5212.11, 5212.12, 5212.21,
      5212.22, 5407.41, 5407.51, 5407.71, 5407.81, 5407.91, 5408.21,
      5408.31, 5512.11, 5512.21, 5512.91, 5513.11 to 5513.19, 5514.11 to
      5514.19, 5516.11, 5516.21, 5516.31, 5516.41 and 5516.91 that are
      produced or obtained outside the free trade area; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} wool fabric and wool made-up textile goods
      referred to in Chapters 51 to 55 of the List of Tariff Provisions,
      containing 36% or more by weight of wool or fine animal hair, and
      in Chapters 58, 60 and 63 of the List of Tariff Provisions that
      are woven or knitted in Chile from yarn produced or obtained
      outside the free trade area or that are knitted in Chile from yarn
      spun in Chile from fibre produced or obtained outside the free
      trade area. ([tissus et articles confectionnés]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[free trade area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[free trade area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area comprising
    Canada and Chile. ([zone de libre-échange]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[spun yarn]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[spun yarn]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means cotton or man-made fibre
    yarns referred to in any of heading Nos. 52.05 to 52.07 or 55.09 to
    55.11 that are spun in Chile from fibre referred to in any of
    heading Nos. 52.01 to 52.03 or 55.01 to 55.07 that is produced or
    obtained outside the free trade area. ([filés]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Remission of Customs Duty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1023305 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duty paid or payable at the
Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff rates of customs duty on apparel, fabric and
made-up goods and spun yarn imported from Chile on or after January 1,
1998, in an amount equal to the difference between the customs duty
payable at those rates and the customs duty that would be payable if the
imported goods were entitled to the applicable Chile Tariff rates of
customs duty.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1023307 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  remission granted under section 2 applies only to an amount not
  exceeding the annual quantitative limits for each of the goods
  referred to in that section that are set out in Schedules 5.B.1, 5.B.2
  and 5.B.3 to Appendix 5.1 of Annex C-00-B to Chapter C of the Canada
  --- Chile Free Trade Agreement.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Remission under section 2 is granted on the condition
  that the importer or owner of the goods provides to a customs officer,
  at the request of the officer,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the time that the goods are accounted for under
    subsection 32(1), (3) or (5) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)
    or are the subject of an application for a refund of the customs
    duty, a certificate issued pursuant to the [Export and Import
    Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19) and the [Import Certificate
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._603), identifying the
    quantity available for purposes of remission or refund pursuant to
    Chapter C of the Canada-Chile Free Trade Agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the time that the goods are accounted for under
    subsection 32(1), (3) or (5) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)
    or are the subject of an application for a refund of the customs
    duty, a written declaration, in English or French, indicating that
    the certification referred to in subparagraph (c)(i) is in the
    possession of the importer or owner; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at the time that the goods are released, or at any
    other time after the importer or owner provides the declaration
    referred to in paragraph (b),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a certification of the exporter, in English,
      French or Spanish, in the form set out in the schedule,
      accompanied by a copy of the applicable invoice or sales
      agreement, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the certification is in Spanish, an English
      or French translation of the certification.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1023316 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on August 26, 1998.
