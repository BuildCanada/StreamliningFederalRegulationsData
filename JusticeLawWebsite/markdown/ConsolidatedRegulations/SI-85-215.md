## Bottled Domestic Spirits Remission Order {#bottled-domestic-spirits-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/85-215
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1985-12-11

Order Respecting the Remission of Excise Duty Paid or Payable on Bottled
Domestic Spirits

P.C. 1985-3488 1985-11-28
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest, is pleased hereby, on the recommendation of the
Minister of National Revenue, the Minister of Finance and the Treasury
Board and pursuant to section 17[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the annexed
[Order respecting the remission of excise duty paid or payable on
bottled domestic spirits](/eng/regulations/SI-85-215) in accordance with
the schedule hereto.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-640012 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Bottled Domestic Spirits Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-85-215).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-640014 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any board, commission or other
    government agency that, by the law of a province, is empowered to
    sell or to authorize the sale and delivery of spirits;
    ([organisme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Revenue; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[spirits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[spirits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any material or substance,
    whether in liquid or in any other form, containing any proportion by
    mass or by volume of absolute ethyl alcohol (C~2~H~5~OH) other than
    beer or malt liquor. ([eau-de-vie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-640019 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted, effective September 1, 1984, of the excise
duty paid or payable by a licensed distiller under subsection 135(1) of
the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14) on domestic spirits which have been
bottled by any agency for a licensed distiller where the spirits are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} exported;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} returned to the licensed distiller's excise bonding
  warehouse under the supervision of an excise officer;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} sold to any agency and entered into the excise
  bonding warehouse of the agency under the supervision of an excise
  officer; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} delivered to a customs bonded warehouse and
  designated for delivery as ships stores.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/86-164, s. 1
:::

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-640027 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The remission granted under
this Order is on condition that a claim for remission is made to the
Minister in such form and manner as he may prescribe.
