## Order Placing Members of the Canadian Forces on Active Service (Yugoslavia) {#order-placing-members-of-the-canadian-forces-on-active-service-yugoslavia .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/92-42
:::

[NATIONAL DEFENCE ACT](/eng/acts/N-5)

Registration 1992-03-11

Order Placing Members of the Canadian Forces on Active Service
(Yugoslavia)

P.C. 1992-354  1992-02-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Security Council of the United Nations, by Resolution 743 of
February 21, 1992, established a United Nations Protection Force to
assist in the creation of the conditions of peace and security required
for the negotiation of an overall settlement of the Yugoslav crisis;

And Whereas it appears advisable that Canada participate in that Force;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Defence, pursuant to
paragraph 31(1)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-92-42_e_hq_6497){#fn_SI-92-42_e_hq_6497-ID0EDEAA}
of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), is pleased hereby to place
on active service beyond Canada those

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-92-42_e_hq_6497-ID0EDEAA){#fn_SI-92-42_e_hq_6497}R.S., c.
  31 (1st Supp.), s. 60 (Sch. I, item 14)
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} officers and non-commissioned members of the regular
  force of the Canadian Forces, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} officers and non-commissioned members of the reserve
  force of the Canadian Forces

who are part of, or serve in immediate support of, the United Nations
Protection Force.
