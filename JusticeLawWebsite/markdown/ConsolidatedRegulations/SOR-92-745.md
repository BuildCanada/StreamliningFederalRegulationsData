## Public Property Loan Regulations {#public-property-loan-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-745
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1992-12-14

Regulations Respecting the Loan of Public Property

P.C. 1992-2596  1992-12-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 61 of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to revoke the
Personal Property Loan Regulations, made by Order in Council P.C.
1983-506 of February 17, 1983[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, and to make the annexed
[Regulations respecting the loan of public
property](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-745) in substitution therefor.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949809 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Public Property Loan
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-745).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949811 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[lending authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an appropriate Minister within the meaning of
  paragraph (a), (a.1) or (b) of the definition [[appropriate
  Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a departmental corporation. (*autorité prêteuse*)

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949815 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of the lending of public property other than real property.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949817 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), every loan of public property shall be by way of a
  contract in writing, which shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the consideration for the loan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an undertaking that the borrower will indemnify Her
    Majesty for, and save Her Majesty harmless from, all losses and
    claims of any kind in respect of the borrower's use or possession of
    the public property;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the lending authority may, at any
    reasonable time, inspect the public property and view its state of
    repair;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the term of the loan; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} identification of any security that the lending
    authority requires.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, in the opinion of the lending authority, an
  emergency exists, a loan of public property may be made on obtaining a
  written acknowledgement of receipt thereof from the borrower.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a loan is made pursuant to subsection (2), the
  lending authority shall enter into a written contract for the loan, in
  the form described in subsection (1), as soon as practicable
  thereafter.
