## American Bases in Newfoundland Remission Order, 1990 {#american-bases-in-newfoundland-remission-order-1990 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/91-11
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1991-01-30

Order Respecting the Remission of Duties, Including the Tax Imposed
under Division III of Part IX of the Excise Tax Act, the Taxes Imposed
under any other Division of Part IX and under any other Part of that Act
and the Duties Imposed under the Excise Act, Paid or Payable on Goods,
Real Property or Services for Use at American Bases in Newfoundland

P.C. 1990-2850  1990-12-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest, on the recommendation of the Minister of
National Revenue and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 23 of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to
make the annexed Order respecting the remission of duties, including the
tax imposed under Division III of Part IX of the Excise Tax Act, the
taxes imposed under any other division of Part IX and under any other
Part of that Act and the duties imposed under the Excise Act, paid or
payable on goods, real property or services for use at American bases in
Newfoundland.
:::

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-643304 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted of the duties, including the tax imposed
under Division III of Part IX of the Act, the taxes imposed under any
other division of Part IX and under any other Part of the Act and the
duties imposed under the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14), paid or payable
on the supply, the importation into Canada or the purchase in Canada, of
the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods, real property or services for use in the
  construction, maintenance, operation or defence of American bases in
  Newfoundland;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods for use or consumption, or services for use,
  aboard American public vessels of the Army, Navy, Coast Guard or Coast
  and Geodetic Surveys assigned to American bases in Newfoundland;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} goods, real property or services for the use of
  establishments that are at American bases in Newfoundland and under
  the control of the Government of the United States, which
  establishments are known as Post Exchanges, Ships' Service Stores,
  Commissary Stores or Service Clubs;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} goods or services for supply at establishments
  referred to in paragraph (c) to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} members of the United States Forces,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} civilian employees of the United States who are
    nationals of the United States and employed in connection with
    American bases in Newfoundland, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} members of the family of a person referred to in
    subparagraph (i) or (ii) who reside with that person and who are not
    engaged in any business or occupation in Newfoundland; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the personal belongings and household effects of
  persons referred to in paragraph (d) and of contractors and their
  employees who are nationals of the United States engaged in the
  construction, maintenance or operation of American bases in
  Newfoundland and present in Newfoundland by reason only of that
  engagement.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-643314 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted
pursuant to section 3 on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Government of the United States provide such
  information to the Minister as may be required for the administration
  of this Order;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods described in paragraph 3(c), (d)
  or (e), administrative measures are taken by the American authorities
  to prevent the subsequent supply of those goods by establishments or
  persons mentioned in paragraph 3(c), (d) or (e) to persons who are not
  mentioned in paragraph 3(d) or (e);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission of the duties, including the
  tax imposed under Division III of Part IX of the Act, on imported
  goods is made to the Minister within two years after the final
  accounting for the goods under section 32 of the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission of any tax referred to in
  section 3, other than the duties referred to in paragraph (c), is made
  to the Minister within two years after the purchase or supply, as the
  case may be, is made.
