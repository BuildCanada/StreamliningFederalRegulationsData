## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679043 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[aircraft fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aircraft fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2012-191, s.
    20\]]{.Repealed}

[[children's car seat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[children's car seat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a restraint system
    or booster seat that conforms to the Canada Motor Vehicle Safety
    Standard 213, 213.1, 213.2 or 213.5 under the [Motor Vehicle
    Restraint Systems and Booster Seats Safety
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-90). ([siège
    d'auto]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[children's clothing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[children's clothing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means garments (other
    than garments of a class that is used exclusively in sports or
    recreational activities, costumes, children's diapers or children's
    footwear) that are

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} designed for babies, including baby bibs, bunting
      blankets and receiving blankets;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} children's garments

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} designed for girls and of a size not greater
        than the size that is girls' size 16 according to the national
        standard applicable to the garments,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} designed for boys and of a size not greater
        than the size that is boys' size 20 according to the national
        standard applicable to the garments, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if no national standard applies to the
        garments, designed for girls or boys and having a size
        designation of extra small, small, medium or large; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} hosiery or stretchy socks, hats, ties, scarves,
      belts, suspenders, mittens and gloves in sizes and styles designed
      for children or babies. ([vêtements pour enfants]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[children's diaper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[children's diaper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product that is
    designed for babies or children and that is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a diaper;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a diaper insert or liner;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a training pant; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a rubber pant designed for use in conjunction
      with any of the items referred to in paragraphs (a) to
      (c). ([couche pour enfants]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[children's footwear]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[children's footwear]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means footwear (other
    than stockings, socks or similar footwear or footwear of a class
    that is used exclusively in sports or recreational activities) that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is designed for babies; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is designed for girls or boys and has an insole
      length of 24.25 centimetres or less. ([chaussures pour
      enfants]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[composite property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[composite property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means property that is
    wrapped, packaged or otherwise prepared for sale as a single product
    the only components of which are a printed book and

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a read-only medium that contains material all or
      substantially all of the value of which is reasonably attributable
      to one or more of the following:

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a reproduction of the printed book, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} material that makes specific reference to the
        printed book and its content and that supplements, and is
        integrated with, that content; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the product is specially designed for use by
      students enrolled in a qualifying course, a read-only medium or a
      right to access a website, or both of them, that contains material
      that is related to the subject matter of the printed book. ([bien
      mixte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[diesel fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[diesel fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2012-191, s.
    20\]]{.Repealed}

[[feminine hygiene product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[feminine hygiene product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product
    that is marketed exclusively for feminine hygiene purposes and is a
    sanitary napkin, tampon, sanitary belt, menstrual cup or other
    similar product. ([produit d'hygiène féminine]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[gasoline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gasoline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2012-191, s.
    20\]]{.Repealed}

[[motor fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[motor fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2012-191, s.
    20\]]{.Repealed}

[[national standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[national standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a standard of the
    National Standards of Canada, as they read on January 1, 2010, in
    the subject area CAN/CGSB-49, Garment Sizes, published by the
    Canadian General Standards Board. ([norme
    nationale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[printed book]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[printed book]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
    subsection 259.1(1) of the Act. ([livre imprimé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[provincial schedule]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial schedule]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in respect of a
    participating province means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Ontario, Schedule 1;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Nova Scotia, Schedule 2;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of New Brunswick, Schedule 3;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2012-191, s. 20\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(d.1)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Prince Edward Island, Schedule
      4.1; and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Newfoundland and Labrador,
      Schedule 5. ([annexe provinciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualifying course]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualifying course]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a course the supply
    of the service of instructing in which,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is an exempt supply included in Part III of
      Schedule V to the Act; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} would be an exempt supply included in Part III of
      Schedule V to the Act but for the fact that the supplier of the
      service has made an election under that Part. ([cours
      admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualifying food and beverages]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualifying food and beverages]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means food or
    beverages (other than wine, spirits, beer, malt liquor or other
    alcoholic beverages) for human consumption that are, having regard
    to the nature of the product, the quantity sold or its packaging,
    sold in a form suitable for immediate consumption and that are

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} food or beverages heated for consumption;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} salads not canned or vacuum sealed;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sandwiches and similar products other than when
      frozen;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} platters of cheese, cold cuts, fruit or
      vegetables and other arrangements of prepared food;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} cakes, muffins, pies, pastries, tarts, cookies,
      doughnuts, brownies, croissants with sweetened filling or coating,
      or similar products if they are not prepackaged for sale to
      consumers and are sold as single servings in quantities of less
      than six;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} ice cream, ice milk, sherbet, frozen yoghurt,
      frozen pudding or non-dairy substitutes for any of the foregoing,
      or any product that contains any of the foregoing, sold in single
      servings and not prepackaged;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} food, the supply of which is a taxable supply
      (other than a zero-rated supply) that would be a zero-rated supply
      included in section 1 of Part III of Schedule VI to the Act if
      that section were read without reference to paragraph (q);

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} non-carbonated beverages dispensed at the place
      where they are sold;

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any of the following beverages, the supply of
      which is not a zero-rated supply:

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} milk (flavoured or unflavoured),

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} soy, rice or almond-based beverages or other
        similar non-dairy substitutes for milk, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} non-carbonated fruit juice beverages or fruit
        flavoured beverages, other than milk-based beverages, that
        contain 25% or more by volume of a natural fruit juice or
        combination of natural fruit juices or of a natural fruit juice
        or combination of natural fruit juices that have been
        reconstituted into the original state;

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} any of the following beverages sold to a person
      at the same time as food or beverages that are described in any of
      paragraphs (a) to (i) and the supply of which is not a zero-rated
      supply:

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} carbonated beverages dispensed at the place
        where they are sold, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} beverages (other than those described in any
        of paragraphs (a), (h) and (i)), provided that all of the
        following circumstances are satisfied:

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the cans, bottles or other primary containers
          in which they are sold contain a quantity not exceeding a
          single serving, and

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the beverages are not sold in multiples,
          prepackaged by the manufacturer or producer, of single
          servings; or

    - [(k)]{.lawlabel} any of the following food, sold to a person
      together with food or beverages that are described in any of
      paragraphs (a) to (i) for a single consideration:

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} cakes, muffins, pies, pastries, tarts, cookies,
        doughnuts, brownies, croissants with sweetened filling or
        coating, or similar products if they are prepackaged for sale to
        consumers in quantities of less than six items each of which is
        a single serving,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} ice cream, ice milk, sherbet, frozen yoghurt
        or frozen pudding, non-dairy substitutes for any of the
        foregoing, or any product that contains any of the foregoing,
        prepackaged and sold in single servings, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} food described in any of paragraphs 1(e)
        to (j) and (l) of Part III of Schedule VI to the Act. ([aliments
        et boissons admissibles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualifying heating oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualifying heating oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means fuel (other
    than heavy fuel oil or fuel sold as fuel for use in internal
    combustion engines) that is suitable for use as heating oil and is
    marketed or sold as fuel for use as heating oil for heating homes,
    buildings or similar structures. ([huile de chauffage
    admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualifying newspaper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualifying newspaper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a print newspaper
    (other than a flyer, insert, magazine, periodical or shopper) that
    contains news, editorials, feature stories or other information of
    interest to the general public and that is published at regular
    intervals. ([journal admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[read-only medium]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[read-only medium]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a tangible medium
    that is designed for the read-only storage of information and other
    material in digital format. ([support non
    inscriptible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prescribed Amount]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679111 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 234(3) of the Act, the prescribed amount for an item included
in the provincial schedule in respect of a participating province is the
amount that may be paid or credited under an Act of the legislature of
the participating province and that is equal to an amount of tax payable
under Part IX of the Act in respect of that item.

- SOR/2010-152, s. 10

## [Restrictions Related to Point-Of-Sale Rebates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679115 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coupons

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
new harmonized value-added tax system, section 181 of the Act is adapted
according to the following rules:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "a supply made in a participating
  province" in paragraph (a) of the definition [[tax
  fraction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 181(1) of the Act is to
  be read as "a supply made in a participating province (other than a
  supply in respect of which the supplier pays to, or credits in favour
  of, the recipient of the supply an amount prescribed for the purposes
  of subsection 234(3) or a supply in respect of which the supplier
  credits a qualifying amount (as defined in section 1 of the [Credit
  for Provincial Relief (HST)
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-57)) to a person)"; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of paragraphs 181(2)(b) and (c) of
  the Act, if the tax fraction referred to in those paragraphs is
  determined under paragraph (b) of the definition [[tax
  fraction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 181(1) of the Act, the
  references in paragraphs 181(2)(b) and (c) of the Act to "tax
  collectible" is to be read as "tax collectible under subsection
  165(1)" and the reference in paragraph 181(2)(c) of the Act to "tax
  payable" is to be read as "tax payable under subsection 165(1)".

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-152, s. 10
- SOR/2011-56, s. 22
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

**[[2.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.2 .sectionLabel}** Section 234 of the Act
is modified to adapt that section to the new harmonized value-added tax
system by adding the following subsection after subsection (4) of that
section:

> ::: ReadAsText
> - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional restriction
>
>   [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} No amount of an input tax credit, rebate, refund
>   or remission under this or any other Act of Parliament, and no
>   amount of a tax benefit (as defined in subsection 274(1)), shall be
>   credited, paid, granted or allowed to the extent that it can
>   reasonably be regarded that the amount is determined, directly or
>   indirectly, in relation to an amount that is prescribed for the
>   purposes of subsection (3).
> :::
