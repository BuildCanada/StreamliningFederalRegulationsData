Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._952).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[accelerated election]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[accelerated election]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an election
    referred to in subsection 11.1(1); ([élection
    accélérée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Definition of Residence for the Purpose of Determining the Eligibility of Voters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-598950 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following rules apply
to the interpretation of the words "ordinarily resident" in respect of
the residency of an elector on a reserve consisting of more than one
electoral section:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to the other provisions of this section, the
  question as to where a person is or was ordinarily resident at any
  material time or during any material period shall be determined by
  reference to all the facts of the case;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the place of ordinary residence of a person is,
  generally, that place which has always been, or which he has adopted
  as, the place of his habitation or home, whereto, when away therefrom,
  he intends to return and, specifically, where a person usually sleeps
  in one place and has his meals or is employed in another place, the
  place of his ordinary residence is where that person sleeps;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person can have one place of ordinary residence
  only, and he shall retain such place of ordinary residence until
  another is acquired;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} temporary absence from a place of ordinary residence
  does not cause a loss or change of place of ordinary residence.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-391, s. 2
:::

to all elections other than accelerated elections.

## [Voters List]{.HTitleText1} {#h-598962 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At least
  79 days before the day on which an election is to be held

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the band holding the election has assumed
    control of its own membership under section 10 of the Act, the band
    shall provide the electoral officer with a list of the names of all
    electors; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the Band List of the band holding the
    election is maintained in the Department under section 11 of the
    Act, the Registrar shall provide the electoral officer with a list
    of the names of all electors.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A voters list shall set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the reserve consists of more than one
    electoral section,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the names of band members eligible to vote for
      chief, in alphabetical order, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the names of band members eligible to vote for
      councillors, in alphabetical order;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the reserve consists of one electoral
    section, the names of all electors, in alphabetical order; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the band membership or registry number of each
    elector or, if the elector does not have a band membership or
    registry number, the date of birth of the elector.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} On request, the electoral officer or deputy electoral
  officer shall confirm whether the name of a person is on the voters
  list.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer shall revise the voters list
  where it is demonstrated that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of an elector has been omitted from the
    list;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of an elector is incorrectly set out in
    the list; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of a person not qualified to vote is
    included in the list.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (4),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person may demonstrate that the name of an
    elector has been omitted from, or incorrectly set out in, the voters
    list by presenting to the electoral officer evidence from the
    Registrar or from the band that the elector

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is on the Band List or is entitled to have his or
      her name entered on the Band List,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is at least 18 years of age, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is qualified to vote at band elections; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person may demonstrate that the name of a person
    not qualified to vote has been included in the voters list by
    presenting to the electoral officer evidence that that person

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is neither on the Band List nor entitled to have
      his or her name entered on the Band List,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is not at least 18 years of age, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is not qualified to vote at band elections.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-409, s. 1(E)
- SOR/2000-391, s. 3
:::

## [Addresses of Electors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-598989 .Part}

- **[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At
  least 79 days before the day on which an election is to be held, the
  band shall provide the electoral officer with the last known
  addresses, if any, of all electors who do not reside on the reserve.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A candidate for election as chief or councillor may
  obtain from the electoral officer a list of the names of electors and
  the addresses of any electors who have consented to have their
  addresses released to the candidates.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A document shall be considered to have been properly
  mailed under paragraphs 4.2(1)(b) and 4.7(a), subsection 5(4) and
  paragraphs 8(2)(b) and 11.1(2)(g) to electors who do not reside on the
  reserve if it was mailed or delivered to every elector who does not
  reside on the reserve and for whom an address was provided.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-391, s. 3
:::

## [Nomination Meeting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-598996 .Part}

- **[[4.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At
  least 30 days before the day on which a nomination meeting is to be
  held, the electoral officer shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} post a notice of the nomination meeting and a list
    of the names of electors in at least one conspicuous place on the
    reserve; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} mail a notice of the nomination meeting and a voter
    declaration form to every elector who does not reside on the
    reserve.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of a nomination meeting shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date, time, duration and location of the
    nomination meeting;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the election will be held and the
    location of each polling place;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and phone number of the electoral officer;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the statement that any voter may vote by mail-in
    ballot;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the manner in which an elector can
    nominate a candidate, or second the nomination of a candidate;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the statement that, if the elector wants to receive
    information from candidates, the elector can agree to have his or
    her address released to the candidates.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer shall record the names of
  electors to whom a notice of the nomination meeting was mailed, the
  addresses of those electors, and the date on which the notices were
  mailed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-391, s. 3
:::

- **[[4.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 75 of the Act, an
  elector may nominate a candidate, or second the nomination of a
  candidate,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by delivering or, subject to subsection (2), by
    mailing a written nomination and a completed, signed and witnessed
    voter declaration form to the electoral officer before the time set
    for the nomination meeting; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} orally, at the nomination meeting.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Mailed nominations that are not received by the
  electoral officer before the time set for the nomination meeting are
  void.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-391, s. 3
:::

- **[[4.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  nomination meeting for an election shall be held at least 42 days
  before the date of the election.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the start of the nomination meeting, the electoral
  officer shall read aloud all written nominations and secondments that
  have been received by mail or delivered.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the same person receives two written
  nominations for the same office, the second nomination shall
  constitute a secondment of the first nomination.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person present at a nomination meeting who is
  eligible to do so may second the nomination of any person nominated in
  writing.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A nomination meeting shall remain open for at least
  three hours.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} At the end of the nomination meeting, the electoral
  officer shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if only one person has been nominated for election
    as chief, declare that person to be elected;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the number of persons nominated to serve as
    councillors in an electoral section does not exceed the number to be
    elected, declare those persons to be elected; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where more than the required number of persons are
    nominated for election as chief or councillors, announce that an
    election will be held on the day set out in the notice referred to
    in subsection 4.2(1).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-391, s. 3
:::

**[[4.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.5 .sectionLabel}** As soon as is
practicable after the nomination meeting, the electoral officer shall
notify any nominated candidates who were not present at the meeting that
they have been nominated.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-391, s. 3
:::

## [Withdrawal of Candidates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599033 .Part}

- **[[4.6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.6
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a
  candidate who has been nominated may withdraw his or her candidature
  at any time prior to the close of the polls by submitting to the
  electoral officer a written withdrawal of nomination, signed by the
  candidate in the presence of the electoral officer, a justice of the
  peace, a notary public or a commissioner for oaths.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a candidate nominated to be a councillor to
  represent an electoral section in a reserve consisting of more than
  one electoral section withdraws his or her candidature less than 48
  hours before the time at which the polls open, or where any other
  candidate withdraws his or her candidature less than 37 days before
  the day on which the election is to be held, that candidate's name
  shall remain on the ballot.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A candidate who dies before the close of the polls
  shall be considered to have withdrawn his or her candidature.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-391, s. 3
:::

## [Acclamations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599040 .Part}

**[[4.7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.7 .sectionLabel}** Where the office of
chief and all offices of councillors are filled by acclamation,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the electoral officer shall post in at least one
  conspicuous place on the reserve, and mail to every elector who does
  not reside on the reserve, a notice that sets out the names of the
  persons who have been acclaimed and states that an election will not
  be held; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sections 5 to 11 do not apply.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-391, s. 3
:::

## [Manner in Which Voting Shall Be Carried Out]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599046 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (3), the electoral officer shall prepare ballots setting
  out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the names of the candidates nominated for election
    as chief, in alphabetical order; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the names of the candidates nominated for election
    as councillors, in alphabetical order.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where two or more candidates have the same name, the
  electoral officer shall add to the ballots such additional information
  as is necessary to distinguish between those candidates.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the reserve consists of more than one electoral
  section, the electoral officer shall prepare separate ballots for the
  candidates for election as chief and for the candidates for election
  as councillors.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), at least 35 days before
  the day on which an election is to be held, the electoral officer
  shall mail, to every elector who does not reside on the reserve, a
  package consisting of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a ballot, initialled on the back by the electoral
    officer;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an outer, postage-paid return envelope,
    pre-addressed to the electoral officer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a second, inner envelope marked "Ballot" for
    insertion of the completed ballot;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a voter declaration form;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a letter of instruction regarding voting by mail-in
    ballot;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statement

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} identifying the location of all polling places,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} advising the elector that he or she may vote in
      person at a polling place on the day of the election in accordance
      with subsection 6(3) in lieu of voting by mail-in ballot; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a list of the names of any candidates who were
    acclaimed.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the reserve consists of more than one electoral
  section, the package mailed to an elector who does not reside on the
  reserve shall contain a ballot for the candidates for chief only.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} At the request of an elector who resides on the
  reserve, the electoral officer shall provide a package referred to in
  subsection (4) to that elector.

- [(6.1)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer shall indicate on the voters
  list that a ballot has been provided to each elector to whom a mail-in
  ballot was mailed or otherwise provided and keep a record of the date
  on which, and the addresses to which, each mail-in ballot was mailed.

- [(6.2)]{.lawlabel} An elector may vote by mail-in ballot by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} marking the ballot by placing a cross, check mark
    or other mark, that clearly indicates the elector's choice but does
    not identify the elector, opposite the name of the candidate or
    candidates for whom he or she desires to vote;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} folding the ballot in a manner that conceals the
    names of the candidates and any marks but exposes the electoral
    officer's initials on the back;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} placing the ballot in the inner envelope and
    sealing that envelope;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} completing and signing the voter declaration form
    in the presence of a witness who is at least 18 years of age;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} placing the inner envelope and the completed voter
    declaration form in the outer envelope; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} delivering or, subject to subsection (6.7), mailing
    the mail-in ballot to the electoral officer before the time at which
    the polls close on the day of the election.

- [(6.3)]{.lawlabel} Where an elector is unable to vote in the manner
  set out in subsection (6.2), the elector may enlist the assistance of
  another person to mark the ballot and complete and sign the voter
  declaration form in accordance with that subsection.

- [(6.4)]{.lawlabel} A witness referred to in paragraph (6.2)(d) shall
  attest to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fact that the person completing and signing the
    voter declaration form is the person whose name is set out in the
    form; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the elector enlisted the assistance of
    another person under subsection (6.3), the fact that the elector is
    the person whose name is set out in the form and that the ballot was
    marked according to the directions of the elector.

- [(6.5)]{.lawlabel} An elector who inadvertently spoils a mail-in
  ballot may obtain another ballot by returning the spoiled ballot to
  the electoral officer.

- [(6.6)]{.lawlabel} An elector who loses a mail-in ballot may obtain
  another ballot by delivering to the electoral officer a written
  affirmation that the elector has lost the mail-in ballot, signed by
  the elector in the presence of the electoral officer, a justice of the
  peace, a notary public or a commissioner for oaths.

- [(6.7)]{.lawlabel} Mail-in ballots that are not received by the
  electoral officer before the time at which the polls close on the day
  of the election are void.

- [(6.8)]{.lawlabel} An elector to whom a mail-in ballot was mailed or
  provided under subsection (4) or (6) is not entitled to vote in person
  at a polling place other than in accordance with subsection 6(3).

- [(6.9)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer shall establish at least one
  polling place on the reserve.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer shall procure or cause to be
  procured as many ballot boxes as there are polling places, and shall
  cause to be prepared a sufficient number of ballot papers for the
  purpose of the election.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer shall, before the poll is open,
  cause to be delivered to his deputy the ballot papers, materials for
  marking the ballot papers, and a sufficient number of
  directions-for-voting as may be prescribed.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer or his deputy shall provide a
  compartment at each polling place where the electors can mark their
  ballot papers free from observation, and he may appoint a constable to
  maintain order at such polling place.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} Polling places shall be kept open from 9:00 a.m.,
  local time, until 8:00 p.m., local time, on the day of the election.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} A candidate shall be entitled to not more than two
  agents in a polling place at any one time.

- [(12)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2000-391, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

- [(13)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer or his deputy shall,
  immediately before the commencement of the poll, open the ballot box
  and call such persons as may be present to witness that it is empty;
  he shall then lock and properly seal the box to prevent it being
  opened without breaking the seal and shall place it in view for the
  reception of the ballots, and the seal shall not be broken nor the box
  unlocked during the time appointed for taking the poll.

- [(14)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2000-391, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-409, s. 2(F)
- SOR/2000-391, s. 4
:::

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection 5(6.8), where a person attends at a polling place for
  the purpose of voting, the electoral officer or deputy electoral
  officer shall, if the person's name is set out in the voters list,
  provide the person with a ballot.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer or his deputy shall cause to be
  placed in the proper column of the voters' list a mark opposite the
  name of every voter receiving a ballot paper.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An elector to whom a mail-in ballot was mailed or
  provided under subsection 5(4) or (6) may obtain a ballot and vote in
  person at a polling place if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the elector returns the mail-in ballot to the
    electoral officer or deputy electoral officer; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the elector has lost the mail-in ballot, the
    elector provides the electoral officer or deputy electoral officer
    with a written affirmation that the elector has lost the mail-in
    ballot, signed by the elector in the presence of the electoral
    officer, deputy electoral officer, a justice of the peace, a notary
    public or a commissioner for oaths.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer or his deputy may and when
  requested to do so shall explain the mode of voting to a voter.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} After receiving a ballot, an elector shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immediately proceed to the compartment provided for
    marking ballots;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} mark the ballot by placing a cross, check mark or
    other mark, that clearly indicates the elector's choice but does not
    identify the elector, opposite the name of the candidate or
    candidates for whom he or she desires to vote;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fold the ballot in a manner that conceals the names
    of the candidates and any marks, but exposes the initials on the
    back; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} deliver the ballot to the electoral officer or
    deputy electoral officer.

- [(5.1)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a completed ballot, the electoral
  officer or deputy electoral officer shall, without unfolding the
  ballot, verify the initials placed on it and deposit it in the ballot
  box in the presence of the voter and any other persons entitled to be
  present at the polling place.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} While any voter is in the compartment for the purpose
  of marking his ballot paper, no other person shall, except as provided
  in subsection (7), be allowed in the same compartment or be in any
  position from which he can see the manner in which such voter marks
  his ballot paper.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} At the request of any voter who is unable to vote in
  the manner set out in subsection (5), the electoral officer or deputy
  electoral deputy shall assist that voter by marking his or her ballot
  in the manner directed by the voter in the presence of another elector
  selected by the voter as a witness and place the ballot in the ballot
  box.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The electoral officer or his deputy shall state in
  the voters' list in the column for remarks opposite the name of such
  elector the fact that the ballot paper was marked by him at the
  request of the voter and the reasons therefor.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} A voter who has inadvertently dealt with his ballot
  paper in such a manner that it cannot be conveniently used shall, upon
  returning it to the electoral officer or his deputy, be entitled to
  obtain another ballot paper, and the electoral officer or his deputy
  shall thereupon write the word "cancelled" upon the spoiled ballot
  paper and preserve it.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} Any person who has received a ballot paper and who
  leaves the polling place without delivering the ballot paper to the
  electoral officer or his deputy, in the manner provided, or if, after
  receiving the ballot paper, refuses to vote, shall forfeit his right
  to vote at the election, and the electoral officer or his deputy shall
  make an entry in the voters' list in the column for remarks opposite
  the name of such person to show that such person received the ballot
  paper and declined to vote, and the electoral officer or his deputy
  shall mark upon the face of the ballot paper the word "declined", and
  all ballot papers so marked shall be preserved.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} An elector whose name does not appear on the voters'
  list may vote at an election if the electoral officer or his deputy is
  satisfied that such person is qualified to vote.

- [(12)]{.lawlabel} Every elector who is inside the polling place at the
  time fixed for closing the poll shall be entitled to vote before the
  poll is closed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-391, s. 6
:::

## [Counting of Votes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599114 .Part}

**[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** As soon as is
practicable after the close of the polls, the electoral officer or
deputy electoral officer shall, in the presence of any candidates or
their agents who are present, open each envelope containing a mail-in
ballot that was received before the close of the polls and, without
unfolding the ballot,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} reject the ballot if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it was not accompanied by a voter declaration form,
    or the voter declaration form is not signed or witnessed,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the elector set out in the voter
    declaration form is not on the voters list, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the voters list shows that the elector has
    already voted; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, place a mark on the voters list
  opposite the name of the elector set out in the voter declaration
  form, and deposit the ballot in a ballot box.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-391, s. 7
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Immediately after the
mail-in ballots have been deposited under section 6.1, the electoral
officer or deputy electoral officer shall, in the presence of any
candidates or their agents who are present, open all ballot boxes and

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine the ballot papers and reject all ballot
  papers

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that have not been supplied by him,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by which votes have been given for more candidates
    than are to be elected, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} on which anything appears by which the voter can
    be identified;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} declare a ballot paper containing the names of
  candidates for more than one office, on which votes are given for more
  candidates for any office than are to be elected, to be void as
  regards all the candidates for such office; but such ballot paper
  shall be good as regards the votes for any other offices in respect of
  which the voter has not voted for more candidates than are to be
  elected;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to review on recount or on an election
  appeal, take a note of any objection made by any candidate or his
  agent to any ballot paper found in the ballot box and decide any
  question arising out of the objection;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} number such objection and place a corresponding
  number on the back of the ballot paper with the word "allowed" or
  "disallowed", as the case may be, with his initials;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} from the ballots not rejected or declared void under
  paragraph (b), count the votes given for each candidate who has not
  withdrawn before the close of the polls; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} prepare and sign a statement of the number of votes
  for each candidate and the number of ballots rejected.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-391, s. 8
:::

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Immediately after the completion of
  the counting of the votes, the electoral officer shall publicly
  declare to be elected the candidate or candidates having the highest
  number of votes.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within four days after completion of the counting of
  the votes, the electoral officer shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sign and post, in at least one conspicuous place on
    the reserve, a statement indicating the number of votes cast for
    each candidate; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} mail a copy of the statement to every elector of
    the band who does not reside on the reserve.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-391, s. 9
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Where it appears that two
or more candidates have an equal number of votes, the electoral officer
shall give a casting vote for one or more of such candidates, but the
electoral officer shall not otherwise be entitled to vote.

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  electoral officer shall prepare a statement in triplicate showing the
  total number of votes cast for each candidate, the number of rejected
  ballots and the names of the candidates duly declared elected.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} One copy of such statement shall be forwarded to the
  Assistant Deputy Minister, one to the regional supervisor or the
  Indian commissioner for the Province of British Columbia, and one copy
  filed in the agency office.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The statement shall be signed by the electoral
  officer and such of the candidates or their agents as are present and
  desire to sign it.

## [Disposition of Ballot Papers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599147 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The electoral officer
shall deposit all ballot papers in sealed envelopes with the
superintendent, who shall retain them in his possession for eight weeks,
and unless otherwise directed by the Minister or by a person authorized
by him shall then destroy the ballot papers in the presence of two
witnesses who shall make a declaration that they witnessed the
destruction of those papers.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-409, s. 3(F)
:::

## [Accelerated Elections]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599151 .Part}

- **[[11.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This section applies to an election
  where, as a result of the office of chief or a councillor becoming
  vacant under subsection 78(2) of the Act or the election of a chief or
  councillor being set aside under section 79 of the Act, it is no
  longer possible for the council of a band to form a quorum.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An accelerated election shall be held in accordance
  with sections 4 to 11 for the election of chief of a band whose
  reserve consists of more than one electoral section, or for the
  election of chief or councillor of any other band, subject to the
  following changes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subsection 4.2(1), section 4.3 and subsection
    4.4(1) do not apply;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lists referred to in subsection 4(1) and
    addresses referred to in subsection 4.1(1) shall be provided to the
    electoral officer at least 30 days before the day of the election;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at least seven days before the day on which a
    nomination meeting is to be held, the electoral officer shall

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} post a notice of the nomination meeting and of
      the manner in which nominations can be made, and a list of names
      of the electors, in at least one conspicuous place on the reserve,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} give notice of the nomination meeting, and of
      the manner in which nominations may be made, by publishing an
      advertisement in the local newspaper with the largest circulation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an elector can nominate a candidate, or second the
    nomination of a candidate,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by communicating the nomination or secondment to
      the electoral officer at any time before the commencement of the
      nomination meeting, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} orally, at the nomination meeting;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the nomination meeting for the election shall be
    held at least 23 days before the date of the election;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} where a candidate withdraws his or her candidature
    less than 22 days before the day on which the election is to be
    held, that candidate's name shall remain on the ballot;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the electoral officer shall mail by priority post
    the package referred to in subsection 5(4) at least 21 days before
    the day on which the election is to be held;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the outer envelope referred to in paragraph 5(4)(b)
    shall be postage-paid for delivery by priority post; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the letter of instruction referred to in paragraph
    5(4)(e) shall advise the elector of the shorter time periods
    applicable and instruct the elector accordingly.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An accelerated election shall be held in accordance
  with sections 4 to 11 for the election of councillors of a band whose
  reserve consists of more than one electoral section, subject to the
  following changes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 4.3(1)(a), subsections 4.2(3), 4.3(2),
    4.4(2) to (4) and 5(3) to (5) do not apply;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the list referred to in subsection 4(1) shall be
    provided to the electoral officer at least 30 days before the day of
    the election;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at least six days before the day on which a
    nomination meeting is to be held, the electoral officer shall post a
    notice of the nomination meeting and the list of names of electors
    in at least one conspicuous place on the reserve; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the nomination meeting shall be held at least six
    days before the date of the election.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-391, s. 10
:::

## [Election Appeals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599175 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  45 days after an election, a candidate or elector who believes that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there was corrupt practice in connection with the
    election,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there was a violation of the Act or these
    Regulations that might have affected the result of the election, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person nominated to be a candidate in the
    election was ineligible to be a candidate,

  may lodge an appeal by forwarding by registered mail to the Assistant
  Deputy Minister particulars thereof duly verified by affidavit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an appeal is lodged under subsection (1), the
  Assistant Deputy Minister shall forward, by registered mail, a copy of
  the appeal and all supporting documents to the electoral officer and
  to each candidate in the electoral section in respect of which the
  appeal was lodged.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any candidate may, within 14 days of the receipt of
  the copy of the appeal, forward to the Assistant Deputy Minister by
  registered mail a written answer to the particulars set out in the
  appeal together with any supporting documents relating thereto duly
  verified by affidavit.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} All particulars and documents filed in accordance
  with the provisions of this section shall constitute and form the
  record.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-409, s. 4(E)
- SOR/2000-391, s. 11
:::

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, if the material that has been filed is not adequate for
  deciding the validity of the election complained of, conduct such
  further investigation into the matter as he deems necessary, in such
  manner as he deems expedient.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Such investigation may be held by the Minister or by
  any person designated by the Minister for the purpose.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister designates a person to hold such
  an investigation, that person shall submit a detailed report of the
  investigation to the Minister for his consideration.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall report
to the Governor in Council when the Minister is satisfied that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} there was corrupt practice in connection with an
  election;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} there was a contravention of the Act or these
  Regulations that might have affected the result of an election; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person nominated to be a candidate in an election
  was ineligible to be a candidate.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-285, s. 1
:::

## [Secrecy of Voting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599197 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person in attendance at a polling place or at the counting of the
  votes shall maintain and aid in maintaining the secrecy of the voting.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall interfere or attempt to interfere
  with a voter when marking his ballot paper or obtain or attempt to
  obtain at the polling place information as to how a voter is about to
  vote or has voted.
