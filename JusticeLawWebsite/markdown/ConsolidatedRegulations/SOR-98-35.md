## Australia Tariff and New Zealand Tariff Rules of Origin Regulations {#australia-tariff-and-new-zealand-tariff-rules-of-origin-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-35
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1997-12-29

Australia Tariff and New Zealand Tariff Rules of Origin Regulations

P.C. 1997-2006  1997-12-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 16(2) of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Australia Tariff and New Zealand Tariff Rules of Origin
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-35).

## [Origin of Goods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022649 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Goods
  originate in Australia if not less than 50 per cent of the cost of
  production of the goods is incurred by the industry of Australia or
  Canada or both and the goods were finished in Australia in the form in
  which they are imported into Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Goods originate in New Zealand if not less than 50
  per cent of the cost of production of the goods was produced by the
  industry of New Zealand or Canada or both and the goods were finished
  in New Zealand in the form in which they are imported into Canada.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In calculating the cost of
production for the purposes of section 1, the following shall not be
included:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cost of outside packing and expenses related to
  outside packing, required for the transportation of the goods, not
  including packing in which the goods are ordinarily sold for
  consumption;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} gross profit of the manufacturer or exporter and the
  profit or remuneration of any trader, broker or other person dealing
  in the goods in their finished manufactured condition;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} royalties;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} customs or excise duty or tax paid or payable on
  imported materials;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} carriage, insurance and other charges from the place
  of production or manufacture in the country of origin to the port of
  shipment; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other costs or charges incurred or to be incurred
  subsequent to the completion of the manufacture of the goods.

## [Direct Shipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022660 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Goods
  are entitled to the Australia Tariff only if the goods are shipped
  directly to Canada, with or without transhipment, from Australia.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Goods are entitled to the New Zealand Tariff only if
  the goods are shipped directly to Canada, with or without
  transhipment, from New Zealand.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022664 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** \[Repeal\]

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022666 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on January 1, 1998.
