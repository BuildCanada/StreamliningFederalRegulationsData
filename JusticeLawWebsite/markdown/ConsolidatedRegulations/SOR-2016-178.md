## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831847 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[charcoal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[charcoal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means charcoal for use in domestic
    cooking or heating. ([charbon de bois]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[principal display surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[principal display surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the surface
    of a container containing charcoal that is displayed or visible at
    the time the charcoal is sold to the consumer or when it is
    used. ([aire d'affichage principale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831852 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warning

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The container in which the
charcoal is sold to the consumer must, on each principal display
surface, carry a warning in English and in French that meets the
requirements of section 3 and clearly conveys the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} toxic fumes may occur and cause death if the charcoal
  is burned without adequate ventilation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the charcoal should not be used for cooking or
  heating indoors without a ventilation system that carries all the
  fumes outdoors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presentation

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  warning must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} begin with the words "Warning" in English and
    "[Avertissement]{.otherLang lang="fr"}" in French;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be easily legible and shown clearly and
    prominently;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be set apart from other information or visual
    images on the container; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be enclosed by a borderline.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Colour

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The warning and the borderline enclosing it must be
  of a colour that contrasts sharply with the background on which they
  are shown.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Typeface and letter size

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The warning must be shown in bold-face or medium-face
  type with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the words "Warning" and "[Avertissement]{.otherLang
    lang="fr"}" in letters of not less than 9 mm in height; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information set out in paragraph 2(b) in
    letters of not less than 4.5 mm in height.
