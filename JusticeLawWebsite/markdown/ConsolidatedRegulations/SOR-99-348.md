## [Network]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031844 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 30.8(9) of the [Copyright Act](/eng/acts/C-42), the following
are prescribed networks:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} networks that are networks within the meaning of the
  definition [[network]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the
  [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} networks that consist of two or more programming
  undertakings that are owned by the same person or group of persons and
  that have a practice of common programming or an arrangement by which
  they share programs, as the case may be.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031848 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on October 1, 1999.
