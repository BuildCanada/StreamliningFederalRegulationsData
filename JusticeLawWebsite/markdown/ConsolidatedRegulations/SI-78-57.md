## Cruiser Remission Order {#cruiser-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/78-57
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1978-04-12

Order Respecting Remission of Customs Duties and Sales Tax on certain
Pleasure Cruisers

P.C. 1978-842 1978-03-23
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 17
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed Order respecting the remission of Customs duty and
sales tax on certain pleasure cruisers.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634165 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Cruiser Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-78-57).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634167 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[base year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[base year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a class A manufacturer, the
      calendar year 1969, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a class B manufacturer, the
      calendar year immediately prior to the calendar year for which
      remission is first claimed under this Order; ([année de
      base]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canadian value added in boats]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian value added in boats]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    factory cost of production of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} water-borne pleasure craft, other than cruisers,
      and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} hulls and parts for water-borne pleasure craft,
      other than cruisers

    produced by a manufacturer in Canada for sale, minus

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the cost, other than any cost set out in
      paragraph (d), of any imported parts and materials used in the
      production of the craft, hulls and parts, and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the cost of duty, freight and insurance and any
      other cost incurred in transporting the imported parts and
      materials from the point of their shipment to the customs office
      where the goods are reported; ([valeur canadienne ajoutée dans le
      cas des bateaux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canadian value added in cruisers]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian value added in cruisers]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    factory cost of production of cruisers and of hulls and parts for
    cruisers produced by a manufacturer in Canada for sale minus

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cost, other than any cost set out in
      paragraph (b), of any imported parts and materials used in the
      production of cruisers or of hulls and parts for cruisers, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cost of duty, freight and insurance and any
      other cost incurred in transporting the imported parts and
      materials from the point of their shipment to the frontier port;
      ([valeur canadienne ajoutée dans le cas des yachts de
      croisière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[class A manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[class A manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a manufacturer
    who produced cruisers in Canada during the calendar year 1969;
    ([fabricant de la catégorie A]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[class B manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[class B manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a manufacturer
    who did not produce cruisers in Canada during the calendar year 1969
    and who produced water-borne pleasure craft in Canada subsequent to
    the calendar year 1976; ([fabricant de la catégorie
    B]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cruiser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cruiser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a water-borne pleasure craft,
    other than a houseboat, not equipped with or designed to be equipped
    with sails, that is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 25 feet or more in overall length, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} equipped with or designed to be equipped with one
      or more inboard motors; ([yachts de croisière]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[factory cost of production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[factory cost of production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    aggregate of the following costs to the manufacturer reasonably
    attributable to his production of water-borne pleasure craft and of
    hulls and parts for water-borne pleasure craft, namely,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cost of parts and materials,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the transportation costs, including insurance
      charges, incurred in transporting parts and materials from a
      supplier to the factory in Canada of the manufacturer to the
      extent that such costs are not included under paragraph (a),

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the wages paid for direct production labour,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} administrative and general expenses, and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer\'s overhead expenses for his
      factory in Canada; ([coût de production]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[frontier port]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[frontier port]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SI/88-17, s.
    2\]]{.Repealed}

[[initial production period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[initial production period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect
    of a class B manufacturer, the three consecutive calendar years
    immediately following that manufacturer\'s base year; ([période
    initiale de production]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Revenue; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[warehouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[warehouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in the
    [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([entrepôt]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-17, s. 2
:::

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Order \"manufacturer\'s overhead expenses for his factory in Canada\"
  means, subject to subsection (2), the aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} such part of the following costs to the
    manufacturer as is reasonably attributable to the production by the
    manufacturer of water-borne pleasure craft and of hulls and parts
    for water-borne pleasure craft, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} light, heat, power and water,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} materials used in the production operation but
      not incorporated in the final product,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} workmen\'s compensation, unemployment insurance
      and group insurance premiums, pension contributions and similar
      expenses,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} taxes on land and buildings in Canada,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} rent for factory premises,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} fire and other insurance premiums in respect of
      production inventories and the manufacturer\'s factory and its
      equipment,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} maintenance and repairs to buildings, machinery
      and equipment,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} tools, jigs, dies, fixtures and other similar
      plant equipment of a non-permanent character,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} engineering services, experimental work and
      product development work executed in Canada,

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} indirect and non-production labour, and

    - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} miscellaneous factory expenses; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a capital cost allowance of 10 per cent per annum
    on the cost of buildings, machinery and equipment used by the
    manufacturer and reasonably required by the manufacturer in the
    production of water-borne pleasure craft and of hulls and parts for
    water-borne pleasure craft.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A manufacturer\'s overhead expenses for his factory
  in Canada in respect of water-borne pleasure craft and hulls and parts
  for water-borne pleasure craft shall not include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the payment of any royalty or any payment analogous
    to a royalty;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the payment of any amount by a manufacturer as a
    result of a warranty given by the manufacturer; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any expenses incurred by the manufacturer in
    respect of the advertising for sale or the sale of water-borne
    pleasure craft or hulls and parts for the water-borne pleasure
    craft.

## [Remission of Customs Duties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634217 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 6 and
10, remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable
under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of cruisers
imported or taken out of warehouse by or on behalf of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a class A manufacturer in each calendar year
  subsequent to December 31, 1976, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a class B manufacturer, other than a class B
  manufacturer referred to in section 5, in each calendar year
  subsequent to his base year,

the aggregate of the value for duty of which does not exceed the amount
by which the sum of

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer\'s Canadian value added in cruisers
  in the calendar year for which the remission is claimed, and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the lesser of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer\'s Canadian value added in boats
    in that calendar year, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer\'s Canadian value added in boats
    in his base year

exceeds the sum of the manufacturer\'s

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} Canadian value added in boats, and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} Canadian value added in cruisers,

in his base year.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-17, s. 2
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and sections 7 and 10, on the election of a class B
  manufacturer pursuant to section 7, remission is hereby granted to
  that manufacturer of the customs duties paid or payable under the
  [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of cruisers imported
  or taken out of warehouse prior to January 1, 1998 by the manufacturer
  or on the manufacturer\'s behalf during the initial production period,
  the aggregate of the value for duty of which does not exceed the
  amount by which the sum of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer\'s Canadian value added in
    cruisers in the initial production period, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lesser of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer\'s Canadian value added in boats
      in the initial production period, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} three times the sum of the manufacturer\'s
      Canadian value added in boats in his base year

  exceeds three times the sum of the manufacturer\'s

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Canadian value added in boats, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Canadian value added in cruisers,

  in his base year.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where at any time in any year during the initial
  production period the aggregate value of cruisers imported or taken
  out of warehouse for which remission may be granted pursuant to
  subsection (1) exceeds one-third of the amount of the aggregate value
  for duty on which remission would, but for this subsection, be granted
  pursuant to subsection (1), no remission shall be granted in respect
  of such cruisers imported or taken out of warehouse during the balance
  of that year.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-17, s. 2
- SI/98-6, s. 7
:::

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  remission shall be granted to a class A manufacturer pursuant to
  section 4 unless a claim for remission is made by the manufacturer
  within three years following the calendar year for which remission is
  claimed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No remission shall be granted to a class B
  manufacturer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to section 4, unless a claim for remission
    is made by the manufacturer within three years following the
    calendar year for which remission is claimed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to section 5, unless the manufacturer
    makes an election pursuant to section 7 and a claim for remission is
    made by the manufacturer during the initial production period for
    that manufacturer.

## [Election of Initial Production Period]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634250 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  remission shall be granted pursuant to section 5 to a class B
  manufacturer unless, prior to the expiration of the manufacturer\'s
  initial production period, the manufacturer makes an election in
  writing addressed to the Minister electing to claim remission for the
  initial production period.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An election made pursuant to subsection (1) may be
  withdrawn by a class B manufacturer by a notice in writing to that
  effect, addressed to the Minister, at any time before the expiry of
  that manufacturer\'s initial production period.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an election is withdrawn by a class B
  manufacturer pursuant to subsection (2), remission shall be determined
  pursuant to section 4 for each calendar year of the initial production
  period.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-113, s. 1
:::

## [Remission of Sales Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634257 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the sales tax paid or payable under the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on each cruiser for which customs duties are
remitted by this Order in an amount equal to the difference between the
sales tax calculated on the duty paid value of the cruiser and the sales
tax calculated on the value for duty of the cruiser.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-17, s. 2(E)
:::

## [Bond]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634261 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As a
  condition precedent to any remission granted pursuant to sections 4
  and 8, where duty and sales tax

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have been paid and the remission is granted in the
    calendar year for which it is claimed, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have not been paid and the remission is applied for
    in the calendar year for which it is claimed,

  a class A manufacturer or a class B manufacturer shall, for each
  calendar year for which he claims remission and to ensure the
  performance by him of the conditions on which remission is granted
  under this Order, give to the Minister a guaranteed bond or other
  security in an amount estimated by the Minister, to be equal to the
  customs duties and sales tax payable on the imported goods referred to
  in sections 4 and 8, or \$250,000, whichever is the lesser.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} As a condition precedent to any remission granted
  pursuant to sections 5 and 8, a class B manufacturer shall, for the
  initial production period for which he claims remission and to ensure
  the performance by him of the conditions on which remission is granted
  under this Order, give to the Minister a guaranteed bond or other
  security in an amount estimated by the Minister to be equal to the
  customs duties and sales tax payable on the imported goods referred to
  in sections 5 and 8, or \$500,000, whichever is the lesser.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/82-33, s. 1
- SI/88-17, s. 2(E)
:::

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634270 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A class A manufacturer
and a class B manufacturer shall submit to the Minister as a condition
of any remission granted under this Order such reports and other
information as may be required by the Minister for the due
administration of this Order.
