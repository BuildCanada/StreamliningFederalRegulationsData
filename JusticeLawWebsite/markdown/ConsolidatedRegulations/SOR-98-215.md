## Importation and Exportation of Firearms Regulations (Individuals) {#importation-and-exportation-of-firearms-regulations-individuals .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-215
:::

[FIREARMS ACT](/eng/acts/F-11.6)

Registration 1998-03-24

Importation and Exportation of Firearms Regulations (Individuals)

P.C. 1998-490 1998-03-24
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 118 of the [Firearms
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBBA}, the Minister
of Justice had a copy of the proposed [Importation and Exportation of
Firearms Regulations (Individuals)](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-215),
substantially in the annexed form, laid before each House of Parliament
on October 30, 1997, which date is at least 30 sitting days before the
date of this Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBBA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1995, c.
  39
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to sections 35 and
37 to 40 and paragraph 117(w) of the [Firearms
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDAAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Importation and Exportation of Firearms Regulations
(Individuals)](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-215).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020582 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Firearms
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[in writing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[in writing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any communication made
    by electronic means capable of producing a paper record. ([par
    écrit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-restricted firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-restricted firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a firearm that
    is neither a prohibited firearm nor a restricted firearm. ([arme à
    feu sans restrictions]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[orally]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[orally]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any communication carried out
    in person, by telephone, or by any other means of oral
    communication. ([de vive voix]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Importation of Firearms --- Non-Residents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020588 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 35(1)(b) of the Act, a nonresident who imports a
  firearm shall declare it in writing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the declaration may be made
  orally if the non-resident satisfies the requirements of subparagraph
  35(1)(b)(iii) of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-270, s. 1
:::

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraph 35(1)(b)(i) of the Act, the information that a non-resident
who is importing firearms is to provide to the Registrar is the
following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} their name and address;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} their date of birth;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} their gender;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for importing the firearms;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the number of firearms being imported; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in respect of each firearm that the non-resident is
  importing,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the destination of the firearm in Canada,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} whether the firearm is a frame or receiver only,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the make,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the name of the manufacturer if different from the
    make,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the model,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the serial number, if any,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the type,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the action,

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the calibre or gauge,

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of ammunition that the cartridge
    magazine can contain, and

  - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} the barrel length.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-270, s. 1
:::

- **[[2.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  report from the Registrar referred to in subparagraph 35(1)(b)(i) of
  the Act expires six months after it is sent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report is sufficiently sent if it is addressed to
  the nonresident at their address that is set out in the report or, if
  the individual has advised the Registrar of a change of that address,
  at the new address, and if the report is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sent by mail; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} transmitted by electronic means that can produce a
    paper record.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-270, s. 1
:::

**[[2.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 35.1(1)(a) of the Act, a nonresident who imports a firearm
shall declare it orally.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-270, s. 1
:::

**[[2.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 35.1(2)(a) of the Act, a nonresident who imports a firearm
shall declare it in writing.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-270, s. 1
:::

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 35(1)(d) of the Act, a customs officer confirms
  a declaration and an authorization to transport by providing the
  non-resident with a confirmation number either in writing or orally.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a customs officer confirms a
  declaration and an authorization to transport if the conditions set
  out in subparagraph 35(1)(b)(iii) of the Act apply by stamping those
  documents with the date that the firearm was imported.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-270, s. 1
:::

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 35.1(2)(d) of the Act, a customs officer confirms a
declaration by providing the non-resident with a confirmation number in
writing.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-270, s. 1
:::

## [Exportation of Firearms --- Non-Residents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020635 .Part}

::::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  non-resident may export a firearm that the non-resident has imported
  in accordance with section 35 or 35.1 of the Act if, in addition to
  meeting the requirement of paragraph 37(1)(a) of the Act, at the time
  of importation the non-resident provided a customs officer with the
  date that the non-resident intended to export the firearm.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the non-resident knows that the firearm is going
  to be exported after the date that he or she provided, the
  non-resident shall advise the Registrar, on or before the date that
  the firearm was initially intended to be exported, of that fact and of
  the date that the firearm will be exported, if known. If the date is
  not known, the non-resident shall advise the Registrar of that date,
  without delay, after it becomes known.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-270, s. 2
:::
:::::

## [Deemed Authorizations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020641 .Part}

::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Permits to export firearms
that are issued to individuals under section 7 of the [Export and Import
Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19) are deemed to be authorizations to export
for the purposes of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-270, s. 2
:::
:::::

## [Disposal of Detained Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020645 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 35(2), 35.1(3) and 37(2) of the Act, a customs officer shall
dispose of a detained firearm in the same manner as a public service
agency disposes of a firearm under section 15 of the [Public Agents
Firearms Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-203).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-270, s. 3
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An individual who meets the
requirements of paragraph 38(1)(a) of the Act shall, before exporting a
firearm that he or she does not intend to reimport, provide a customs
officer with an authorization to export issued to the individual for the
firearm.

## [Exportation of Replica Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020658 .Part}

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of section 39 of the Act, an individual shall declare a
  replica firearm that they are exporting either in writing or orally.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The individual shall declare a replica firearm by
  providing the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the individual's name, address and telephone
    number; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of each type of replica firearm and
    the quantity being exported.

## [Parts and Components]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020677 .Part}

::::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

- **[[14.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  individual shall import a cylinder, slide, bolt, breech-block or
  barrel of a firearm unless the individual has been authorized in
  writing to do so by the Registrar.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the provisions
  of the Act and of these Regulations that apply to an authorization to
  import also apply, with any modifications that the circumstances
  require, to an authorization referred to in subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-270, s. 7
:::
