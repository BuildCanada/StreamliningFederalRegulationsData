## Report on the State of Canada's Forests Regulations {#report-on-the-state-of-canadas-forests-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-479
:::

[DEPARTMENT OF NATURAL RESOURCES ACT](/eng/acts/N-20.8)

Registration 1995-10-03

Regulations Respecting the Report on the State of Canada's Forests

P.C. 1995-1681 1995-10-03
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Natural Resources, pursuant to section 7 of the
Department of Natural Resources Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EBBAA}, is
pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations respecting the report on
the state of Canada's forests.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976046 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Report on the State of Canada's Forests
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-479).

## [Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976048 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** By June 30 of each year,
the Minister of Natural Resources shall prepare a report respecting the
state of the forests in Canada and their contribution to the economy and
environment of Canada and to the social well-being of Canadians.
