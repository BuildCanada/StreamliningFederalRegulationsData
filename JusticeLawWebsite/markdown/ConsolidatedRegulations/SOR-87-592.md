## Proclamation Designating Botswana as a Designated State for Purposes of the Act {#proclamation-designating-botswana-as-a-designated-state-for-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-592
:::

[VISITING FORCES ACT](/eng/acts/V-2)

Registration 1987-10-06

Proclamation Designating Botswana as a Designated State for Purposes of
the Act
::::

:::::::: section
::::::: {.section .order}
JEANNE SAUVÉ

\[L.S.\]

Canada

Elizabeth the Second, by the Grace of God of the United Kingdom, Canada
and Her other Realms and Territories QUEEN, Head of the Commonwealth,
Defender of the Faith.

To all to whom these Presents shall come or whom the same may in anyway
concern,

*Greeting:*

::: FlushLeft
FRANK IACOBUCCI
:::

::: FlushLeft
*Deputy Attorney General*
:::

A Proclamation

Whereas pursuant to section 4 of the [Visiting Forces
Act](/eng/acts/V-2), being chapter V-6 of the Revised Statutes of
Canada, 1970, it is provided that the Governor in Council may by
proclamation

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} designate any country as a designated state for the
  purposes of that Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} declare the extent to which that Act is applicable in
  respect of any designated state;

And Whereas it is expedient

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to designate the Republic of Botswana as a designated
  state for the purposes of the said Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to declare that the said Act is applicable, with the
  exception of Part VI thereof, in respect of that designated state;

Now Know You that We, by and with the advice of Our Privy Council for
Canada, do by this Our Proclamation,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} designate the Republic of Botswana as a designated
  state for the purposes of the [Visiting Forces Act](/eng/acts/V-2);
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} declare that the [Visiting Forces Act](/eng/acts/V-2)
  is applicable, with the exception of Part VI thereof, in respect of
  that designated state.

Of All Which Our Loving Subjects and all others whom these Presents may
concern are hereby required to take notice and to govern themselves
accordingly.

In Testimony Whereof, We have caused these Our Letters to be made Patent
and the Great Seal of Canada to be hereunto affixed. Witness: Our Right
Trusty and Well-beloved Jeanne Sauvé, a Member of Our Privy Council for
Canada, Chancellor and Principal Companion of Our Order of Canada,
Chancellor and Commander of Our Order of Military Merit upon whom We
have conferred Our Canadian Forces' Decoration, Governor General and
Commander-in-Chief of Canada.

At Our Government House, in Our City of Ottawa, this tenth day of
September in the year of Our Lord one thousand nine hundred and
eighty-seven and in the thirty-sixth year of Our Reign.

By Command,

::: FlushRight
I.D. CLARK
:::

::: FlushRight
*Deputy Registrar General of Canada*
:::
