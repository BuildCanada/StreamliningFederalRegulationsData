## [Definition and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-837669 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[fire
service]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[fire service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a federal, provincial or
municipal organization that is responsible for the prevention, detection
and control of fire.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fires

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of fires on line works regardless of who caused them, how they
were caused or where they started.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Railway Companies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-837674 .Part}

### [Definitions and Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-837675 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Part.

[[fire hazard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fire hazard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fuel or combustible material, including
      vegetation, that is found along a line work and that has a fast
      burning rate and could readily ignite; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} conditions, including topography, that are
      present along a line work and that increase the likelihood of a
      fire igniting on the line work or spreading from the line work
      into the surrounding environment. ([risque
      d'incendie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[high-risk work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[high-risk work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means work that involves the
    use of a rail-grinding train or the controlled burning of
    brush. ([activité à haut risque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fire danger level

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of this Part, the fire danger level for an area is the fire
  danger level shown for the area on the interactive map that, as part
  of the Canadian Wildland Fire Information System, is published on the
  Department of Natural Resources website or on any other Government of
  Canada website.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}More than one fire danger level

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If more than one fire danger level is shown for the
  area on the interactive map, the fire danger level for the area is the
  highest indicated level.

### [Fire Control]{.HTitleText2} {#h-837687 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fire on line work

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** When a railway company
becomes aware of a fire on a line work, the railway company must ensure
that steps are taken to extinguish or control the fire as soon as
practicable. The steps must include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the notification of the fire service that is
  responsible for the area where the fire is located if the fire cannot
  be extinguished or controlled without fire service assistance; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the notification, if applicable, of the railway
  company that operates or maintains the line work.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to fire service

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** If a fire service is
attempting to extinguish or control a fire on a line work, a railway
company must, at the request of the fire service and without delay,
provide the fire service with reasonable assistance. The assistance may,
depending on the circumstances, include the provision of transportation
to the fire.

### [Fire Preparedness Plan and Contact Information for Fire Services]{.HTitleText2} {#h-837694 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fire preparedness plan

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company must have a fire preparedness plan and must update it
  every five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of plan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fire preparedness plan must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} procedures for extinguishing or controlling a fire;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} internal fire notification procedures; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} procedures for notifying fire services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contact information for fire services

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A railway company must
keep, in a readily accessible location, up-to-date contact information
that sets out the name and telephone number of the fire service that is
responsible for each area where a line work that the company owns, or on
which the company conducts railway operations, is located.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A railway company must
communicate its fire preparedness plan and the contact information for
the fire services to employees who conduct railway operations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A railway company must
keep a record of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} each date on which, together with the manner in
  which, its fire preparedness plan was communicated; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} each date on which, together with the manner in
  which, the contact information for the fire services was communicated.

### [Fire Hazard Reduction Plan]{.HTitleText2} {#h-837711 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fire hazard reduction plan

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company must have a fire hazard reduction plan and must update
  it every five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of plan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fire hazard reduction plan must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a process for identifying fire hazards;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} measures for reducing or eliminating the fire
    hazards that are identified; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for each fire danger level used in the Canadian
    Wildland Fire Information System, the fire prevention measures that
    will be taken and the fire suppression equipment that will be used
    when the railway company conducts high-risk work.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A railway company must
communicate its fire hazard reduction plan to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} employees who conduct high-risk work;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} employees who supervise contractors who conduct
  high-risk work; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} contractors who conduct high-risk work.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A railway company must
keep a record of each date on which, together with the manner in which,
its fire hazard reduction plan is communicated.

### [High-risk Work]{.HTitleText2} {#h-837727 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of fire service

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  railway company proposes to conduct high-risk work in an area where
  the fire danger level is high to extreme, the railway company must
  notify the fire service that is responsible for the area at least 24
  hours in advance but not more than 48 hours in advance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fire danger level not available

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a fire danger level is not available for the area,
  the railway company must notify the fire service that is responsible
  for the area at least 24 hours in advance but not more than 48 hours
  in advance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A railway company that
notifies a fire service under subsection 14(1) or (2) must keep a record
of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the notification and the manner
  in which it was provided;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of each person who was contacted at the fire
  service; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any recommendations that were given by the fire
  service and, if a recommendation was not followed, the reasons for not
  following it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prevention measures

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company that is conducting high-risk work in an area must take
  the fire prevention measures that are set out in the railway company's
  fire hazard reduction plan for the fire danger level for that area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fire danger level not available

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a fire danger level is not available for the area,
  the railway company must take the fire prevention measures that are
  set out in the railway company's fire hazard reduction plan for at
  least a moderate fire danger level.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fire suppression equipment

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company that is conducting high-risk work in an area must
  ensure that employees and contractors who are conducting the high-risk
  work are equipped with the fire suppression equipment that is set out
  in the railway company's fire hazard reduction plan for the fire
  danger level for that area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fire danger level not available

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a fire danger level is not available for the area,
  the railway company must ensure that the employees and contractors who
  are conducting the high-risk work are equipped with the fire
  suppression equipment that is set out in the railway company's fire
  hazard reduction plan for at least a moderate fire danger level.

### [Training]{.HTitleText2} {#h-837748 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Critical positions

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraph 18(1)(c)(i) of the [Railway Safety Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2),
the positions of railway company employees who conduct high-risk work or
supervise contractors who conduct high-risk work are declared critical
to safe railway operations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}High-risk work

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A railway company must
not allow an employee to conduct high-risk work or to supervise
contractors who conduct high-risk work unless the employee has received
training on the prevention and control of fires.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** A railway company must
keep, for each employee that receives the training required under
section 19, a record that includes

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the employee's name;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date of the training; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the training provider's name.

### [Maintenance and Inspection of Fire Suppression Equipment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-837758 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintenance

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A railway company must
ensure that its fire suppression equipment is maintained in good working
order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual inspection

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The railway company must
conduct an annual inspection of its fire suppression equipment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintenance records

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company must keep a record that describes any maintenance work
  that is performed on the railway company's fire suppression equipment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspection records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A railway company must keep, for each inspection
  conducted under section 22, a record that includes the date of the
  inspection and the name of the person who conducted the inspection.

### [Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-837768 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A railway company must
keep each document referred to below for at least five years after the
day on which the document is created:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the railway company's fire preparedness plan and each
  updated version;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} each updated version of the contact information for
  the fire services;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the railway company's fire hazard reduction plan and
  each updated version; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any record that the railway company is required to
  keep under this Part.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision to Minister

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** A railway company must,
at the Minister's request, provide the Minister with a copy of any
document referred to in section 24.
