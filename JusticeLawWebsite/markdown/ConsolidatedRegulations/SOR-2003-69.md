## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708037 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}  means the [Trust and Loan Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[balance sheet value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[balance sheet value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of the
    shares and ownership interests held by an entity, means the value
    reported on its balance sheet on an unconsolidated basis. ([valeur
    au bilan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[book value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[book value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2008-168, s.
    29\]]{.Repealed}

:   [[member of a company's group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
    meaning as in subsection 449(2) of the Act. ( [membre du groupe
    d'une société]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prescribed Purpose or Circumstance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708046 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed purpose or circumstance

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraph 410(1)(c.1)(iii) of the Act and subject to the approval
required under paragraph 410(1)(c.1) of the Act, a company may develop,
design, hold, manage, manufacture, sell or otherwise deal with data
transmission systems, information sites, communication devices or
information platforms or portals that are used for a purpose or in a
circumstance that is materially related to the provision of financial
products or services by the company or a member of the company's group.

## [Investments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708049 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed activity

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 453(2)(f) of the Act and subject to
  subsections (2) and (3), a prescribed activity in relation to an
  entity is developing, designing, holding, managing, manufacturing,
  selling or otherwise dealing with any data transmission system,
  information site, communication device or information platform or
  portal that is used to provide information services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit on size of investment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company may not acquire control of, or hold,
  acquire or increase a substantial investment in, an entity engaging in
  an activity described in subsection (1) if the sum of the following
  exceeds 5% of the company's regulatory capital:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate balance sheet value of the shares and
    ownership interests that the company and its subsidiaries, whether
    individually or jointly, would acquire in the entity under paragraph
    453(2)(f) of the Act,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate balance sheet value of the shares and
    ownership interests held by the company and its subsidiaries,
    whether individually or jointly, in entities engaging in an activity
    described in subsection (1) that the company holds control of, or a
    substantial investment in, under paragraph 453(2)(f) of the Act, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate value of outstanding loans made by
    the company and its subsidiaries, whether individually or jointly,
    to entities engaging in an activity described in subsection (1) that
    the company holds control of, or a substantial investment in, under
    paragraph 453(2)(f) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restricted activities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company may not acquire control of, or hold,
  acquire or increase a substantial investment in, an entity engaging in
  an activity described in subsection (1) if the entity engages in the
  business of accepting deposit liabilities or if the activities of the
  entity include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} activities that a company is not permitted to
    engage in under any of sections 417 and 418 of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} dealing in securities, except as may be permitted
    under paragraph 453(2)(e) of the Act or as may be permitted to a
    company under paragraph 409(2)(c) of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} dealing in goods, wares or merchandise that a
    company is not permitted to deal in under subsection 410(2) of the
    Act, other than as permitted under subsection (1);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} acting as an executor, administrator or official
    guardian or as a guardian, tutor, curator, judicial adviser or
    committee of a mentally incompetent person;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} being a trustee for a trust;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} activities that a company is not permitted to
    engage in under section 416 of the Act if the entity engages in the
    activities of a finance entity or of any other entity prescribed
    under paragraph 453(3)(e) of the Act;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} acquiring control of or acquiring or holding a
    substantial investment in another entity unless

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an entity that is controlled by
      the company, the company itself would be permitted under Part IX
      of the Act to acquire a substantial investment in the other
      entity, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an entity that is not controlled
      by the company, the company itself would be permitted to acquire a
      substantial investment in the other entity under subsection
      451(2), paragraph 451(3)(b) or (c) or subsection 451(4) or 453(1)
      or (2) of the Act; or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any activity prescribed under paragraph 453(3)(g)
    of the Act.

## [Exemption from Restrictions on Investments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708072 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from restrictions

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraph 3(3)(g)(ii), subsections 453(4) to (6) of the Act do not
apply in determining whether a company would be permitted to acquire a
substantial investment in an entity under subsection 451(2), paragraph
451(3)(b) or (c) or subsection 451(4) or 453(1) or (2) of the Act.

## [Non-application of Subsection 453(5) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708075 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 453(5) of the
Act does not apply where, under paragraph 453(2)(f) of the Act, a
company acquires control of, or acquires or increases a substantial
investment in, an entity whose business is limited to activities
described in subsection 3(1). Nothing in this section limits the
operation of subsections 3(2) and (3).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708078 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
