## "SeaRose FPSO" Remission Order, 2004 {#searose-fpso-remission-order-2004 .Title-of-Act}

"SeaRose FPSO" Remission Order, 2004

P.C. 2004-493 2004-04-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 115 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed ["SeaRose FPSO" Remission Order,

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714296 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 2,
remission is hereby granted to Husky Oil Operations Limited, Calgary,
Alberta, and Petro Canada, Calgary, Alberta, in the amount representing
the customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of the hull and turret portion of
the "SeaRose FPSO" imported into Canada for the White Rose offshore oil
project.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714298 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
the conditions that:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the importers file such evidence as may be required
  by the Canada Border Services Agency to determine eligibility for
  remission; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue prior to April 1, 2007.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714302 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
