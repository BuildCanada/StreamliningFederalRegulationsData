## Farm Improvement Loans Regulations {#farm-improvement-loans-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[FARM IMPROVEMENT LOANS ACT](/eng/acts/F-3)

Regulations Respecting Loans Under the Farm Improvement Loans Act
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559154 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Farm Improvement Loans
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._645).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559156 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Farm Improvement Loans
    Act](/eng/acts/F-3); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[averaging period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[averaging period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/78-138, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[interest period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[interest period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/78-138, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who has either an
    equitable or legal interest in a farm or is a veteran having a
    subsisting contract under the [Veterans' Land Act](/eng/acts/V-1.5)
    in respect of a farm. ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-138, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the Act
and these Regulations,

[[addition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[addition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the addition to a building or
    equipment and includes the

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} addition of one or more rooms or storeys,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} construction of an attached garage or
      outbuilding,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} installation and construction of foundations and
      basements for buildings or equipment and the purchase of materials
      for any such purpose, and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} purchase of plumbing, heating, ventilating or air
      conditioning systems or parts thereof; ([rajout]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[alteration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[alteration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the structural alterations
    to the exterior or interior of any building or structure that are
    designed to improve or modernize or increase the usefulness of any
    building or structure on a farm and includes the

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} purchase of material for any such purpose,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} relocation of any equipment, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} alteration of plumbing, heating, ventilating,
      sewage disposal, water supply, farm electrical or air-conditioning
      systems or parts thereof; ([modification]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[major]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[major]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to repair and
    overhaul, a cost of not less than \$400 as estimated by the
    borrower; ([majeurs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[repair]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[repair]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the repair and the material
    required for the repair of any building, structure, implement or
    equipment for use in the business of farming and includes the
    painting of any farm building, the interior decorating of the farm
    home and repair to

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the family dwelling and outbuildings,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a separate house for farm help,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a summer cottage or tourist cabin, if erected on
      a farm upon which a dwelling is situated and operated as an
      enterprise incidental to the farmer's business of farming,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a barn, stable, silo or granary,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} plumbing, heating, ventilating, sewage disposal,
      water supply, farm electrical or air-conditioning systems or parts
      thereof, and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} wells, dug-outs or fencing;
      ([réparation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[responsible officer of the bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[responsible officer of the bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manager or assistant manager of the bank or a
      branch thereof,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person for the time being acting as the
      manager or assistant manager of the bank or a branch thereof,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the credit committee of the bank or a branch
      thereof, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any person duly authorized by the bank to
      supervise the granting of loans; ([fonctionnaire responsable de la
      banque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[works for drainage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[works for drainage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means ditches, tiling and
    drainage installations, pumping and diking installations and works
    for the conservation of soil against water erosion. ([ouvrages de
    drainage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559188 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
a guaranteed farm improvement loan authorized under the Act.

## [Designation of Works]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559190 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The following are
designated as works for the improvement or development of a farm for
which farm improvement loans may be made:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the construction, installation, repair, alteration or
  improvement of a sewage disposal system or any part thereof;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sinking, construction, installation, repair,
  alteration or improvement of wells, dug-outs and all types of water
  supply systems;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the clearing, breaking, irrigating and reclaiming of
  land;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the conservation of soil, prevention of erosion and
  the planting of trees and shelter belts;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the purchase, moving to and installing on the farm of
  complete or partially complete buildings or structures and, where
  necessary, the completing of the buildings or structures;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the repair, alteration or improvement of fencing;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the purchase and planting of fruit trees; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the construction of a road or driveway on a farm.

## [Application for Loan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559200 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every applicant for a loan
shall submit to the bank a signed application, in the form set out in
Schedule I, containing the required information.

## [Classes of Farm Improvement Loans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559202 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The following are the
classes of loans prescribed for the purposes of the Act and these
Regulations:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} agricultural implement loans, being loans made for
  the purpose of financing the purchase of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} agricultural implements, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} equipment for bee-keeping;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} livestock loans, being loans made for the purpose of
  financing the purchase of livestock or bee-stock but excluding
  livestock of the kind commonly known as short-term feeder steers;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} agricultural equipment loans, being loans made for
  the purpose of financing the purchase or installation of agricultural
  equipment or a farm electric system;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} building loans, being loans made for the purpose of
  financing the construction, renovation, alteration, repair or
  extension of any building or structure on a farm;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} additional land loans, being loans made for the
  purpose of financing the purchase by the owner of a farm of additional
  land;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} improvement or development loans, being loans made
  for the purpose of financing any alteration or improvement of a farm
  electric system, works for drainage, the erection or construction of
  fencing or any work designated in section 5 for the improvement or
  development of a farm; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} major repair or major overhaul loans, being loans
  made for the purpose of financing the major repair to or major
  overhaul of agricultural implements or equipment, equipment for
  bee-keeping or a farm electrical system.

## [Agricultural Implement Loans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559213 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the Act and
these Regulations, an agricultural implement loan or an equipment for
bee-keeping loan is a guaranteed farm improvement loan if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the loan is made to the owner or tenant of the farm;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the loan is made in an amount not greater than 75 per
  cent of the cost price of the agricultural implement or equipment for
  bee-keeping, as set out in the relevant contract or other written
  evidence of the sale; and

- [(c) and (d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/78-138, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the bank has required, in accordance with normal
  banking requirements, and the borrower has provided evidence that the
  funds advanced in respect of the loan were expended for the purpose
  set out in the application for the loan.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-138, s. 2
:::

## [Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559271 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A bank
  shall, at the time of making a loan of any prescribed class, take
  security for the repayment thereof

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} under section 88 of the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by way of chattel mortgage or commercial pledge;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by way of a mortgage or hypothec on real property;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by way of an assignment of any rights and interest
    of the borrower under an agreement for sale; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} by way of a written undertaking by the borrower to
    give security as required under paragraph (a), (b), (c) or (d) as
    the case may be.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A responsible officer of a bank may, whenever he
  deems it appropriate, release any security taken pursuant to
  subsection (1), but every bank shall, throughout the term of a loan,
  maintain adequate security in accordance with normal banking practice
  in a manner prescribed by subsection (1) for the repayment of the
  balance of the loan outstanding at any time.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where, in the opinion of a responsible officer of a
  bank, security additional to the security taken pursuant to
  subsection (1) or (2) is deemed necessary, the bank may take such
  additional security for the repayment of the loan as the responsible
  officer considers appropriate.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every bank shall, at the time of making a loan,
  require the borrower to give to the bank, in addition to any security
  taken pursuant to subsection (1) or (2), a written promise to repay
  the loan signed by the borrower and setting forth the principal amount
  of the loan, the basis for establishing the rate of interest and the
  repayment terms.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-138, s. 9
- SOR/79-149, s. 1
:::

## [Loan Terms and Revision of Loan Terms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559284 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Repayment of a loan shall be made in
  instalments that are payable at least annually but, at the option of
  the bank, repayments may be scheduled more frequently than annually.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to paragraph 3(1)(e) of the Act, the terms of
  repayment of a loan shall be set out in the agreement in connection
  with the loan and the repayment period and the amount and frequency of
  instalment payments shall conform to the probable ability of the
  borrower to pay, having regard to the type of farming carried on by
  him, the relevant marketing conditions, the repayment of other
  obligations and to any other relevant circumstances.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a borrower is in default in respect of the
  repayment of a loan or advises the bank that some of the terms of the
  agreement in connection with the loan are such that he may have to
  default, or where a borrower wishes to take out additional loans, and
  the bank is of the opinion that it would be appropriate in the light
  of the borrower's total repayment obligations to alter or revise the
  terms of the loan or any agreement in connection therewith, the bank
  may, with the approval of the borrower, alter or revise the terms of
  the loan or agreement by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} extending the term for repayment of the loan within
    the maximum terms specified in the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} changing the amount of the periodic instalments; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} changing the periods between instalments, but in no
    case shall instalments be due less frequently than annually.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/78-138, s. 10\]]{.Repealed}

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The terms of a loan or any agreement in connection
  therewith may not be altered or revised in a manner that would result
  in longer terms for repayment of the loan than the terms prescribed by
  paragraph 3(1)(e) of the Act or paragraph 8(d) of these Regulations
  unless the Minister approves the alteration or revision.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister approves the alteration or
  revision of the terms of a loan or any agreement in connection
  therewith in accordance with subsection (5), the alteration or
  revision shall not discharge the liability of the Minister to the bank
  under the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-138, s. 10
:::

## [Rate of Interest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559297 .Part}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), the
  maximum rate of interest per annum payable to a bank incorporated by
  or under the provisions of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) or
  established pursuant to the Treasury Branches Act of the Province of
  Alberta in respect of a loan is the aggregate of one per cent per
  annum and the prime lending rate in effect at that bank on the date
  the agreement in connection with the loan was signed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), the maximum rate of
  interest per annum payable to a bank other than a bank incorporated by
  or under the provisions of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) or
  established pursuant to the Treasury Branches Act of the Province of
  Alberta in respect of a loan shall be determined by the Minister for
  each month prior to the first day of that month and shall be the
  aggregate of one per cent per annum and the average of the prime
  lending rates in effect at the following chartered banks on the third
  Wednesday of the month preceding the month for which the rate is
  determined:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Bank of Montreal,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Bank of Nova Scotia,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Royal Bank of Canada,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Toronto Dominion Bank,

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Banque Canadienne Nationale,

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the Banque Provinciale, and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Imperial Bank of Commerce,

  rounded to the nearest one-eighth of one per cent or, if the result
  would be equidistant from two multiples of one-eighth of one per cent,
  to that multiple thereof that is the lower.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When the rate of interest determined by the Minister
  for a month pursuant to subsection (2) is a different rate from the
  rate in effect for the previous month, the Minister shall forthwith
  advise the banks to which subsection (2) applies of the new rate by
  such means as he sees fit.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The rate of interest payable to a bank in respect of
  a loan shall be revised

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a bank incorporated by or under the
    provisions of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) or established
    pursuant to the Treasury Branches Act of the Province of Alberta,
    whenever the prime lending rate of that bank changes; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a bank other than a bank
    incorporated by or under the provisions of the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) or established pursuant to the Treasury
    Branches Act of the Province of Alberta, on the first day of each
    month.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-138, s. 11
- SOR/79-149, s. 2
:::

## [When Entire Amount Becomes Due and Payable]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559315 .Part}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a borrower is in default in respect of any payment on a loan, the
  entire amount of the balance outstanding on the loan shall, at the
  option of the bank, become due and payable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a borrower is convicted of an offence under the
  Act, the entire amount of the balance outstanding on a loan shall
  become due and payable.

## [Misrepresentation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559319 .Part}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Where a bank discovers
that any statement in an application for a loan is false in any material
respect or that a borrower has used or is using the proceeds of a loan
otherwise than for a purpose specified in the application for the loan,
the bank may take any action it considers proper in the circumstances
and shall immediately make a full report in respect of the matter to the
Minister who may request the bank to take such action or further action
as he may require.

## [Procedure on Default]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559321 .Part}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a borrower is in default in respect of any payment on a loan and the
  entire amount of the balance outstanding on the loan becomes due and
  payable pursuant to section 18, the bank may take such action, whether
  by legal proceedings or otherwise, as it considers advisable in the
  circumstances to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} effect collection of the loan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} obtain additional security;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} realize upon any or all of the security it has
    taken; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} effect any compromise with or grant any concession
    to any person other than the borrower.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any action taken by the bank pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall not discharge the liability of the Minister to
  the bank under the Act.

## [Procedure for Claims]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559329 .Part}

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  claim for loss by a bank in respect of a loan shall not be made to the
  Minister until 90 days have elapsed after the entire amount of the
  loan becomes due and payable.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Where a loan has been in default for a period of 18
  months, a bank shall forthwith submit a claim for loss unless the bank
  has corresponded with the Minister in respect of the loan prior to the
  expiration of that period.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The claim for loss submitted by a bank in respect of
  a loan shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the unpaid principal amount of the loan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the uncollected earned interest outstanding at the
    time the claim is approved for payment by the Minister at the full
    rate of interest specified in the written promise to repay the loan
    for a maximum period of 180 days unless, in the opinion of the
    Minister, a longer period is justified for reasons beyond the
    control of the bank, and thereafter at half the rate of interest
    specified in the written promise to repay the loan;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any uncollected taxed costs for or incidental to
    legal proceedings in respect of the loan;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} legal fees, legal costs and legal disbursements,
    whether taxable or not, actually incurred by the bank, with or
    without litigation, in collecting or endeavouring to collect the
    loan or in protecting the interests of the Minister but only to the
    extent that the Deputy Minister of Justice taxes or allows; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other disbursements actually incurred by the
    bank in collecting or endeavouring to collect the loan or in
    protecting the interests of the Minister but only to the extent that
    the Minister allows.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A claim for loss by a bank in respect of a loan shall
  be submitted to the Minister by the bank together with a copy of the
  borrower's application form.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A claim for loss by a bank in respect of a loan shall
  be approved for payment by the Minister within 60 days after receipt
  by the Minister of the claim and shall thereupon be paid forthwith.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a claim for loss by a bank has been paid
  pursuant to subsection (4), the bank shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} execute a receipt in the form set out in Schedule
    II;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} send the receipt and the written promise to repay
    the loan by post to the Minister; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} deal with any security held by it for the loan in
    such manner as the Minister may direct.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Any expense incurred by the bank in dealing with any
  security pursuant to paragraph (5)(c) shall be paid by the Minister to
  the bank.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/78-138, s. 13\]]{.Repealed}

- SOR/79-149, ss. 3, 4

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  acting on behalf of the Minister, a bank shall, notwithstanding that
  its claim for loss in respect of a loan has been paid, take such
  reasonable steps as the Minister considers necessary to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} collect payments of principal and interest due by
    the borrower under the terms of the loan; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} realize upon any security taken under these
    Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any amount that is collected or realized pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall be remitted forthwith to the Minister.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any actual expenses of the bank incurred under
  subsection (1) shall be paid by the Minister to the bank.

## [Reports to the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559357 .Part}

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Any bank that has made a
loan under these Regulations shall furnish to the Minister such reports
or information as the Minister may from time to time require.

## [Registry]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559359 .Part}

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The registry established
by the Minister pursuant to the [Farm Improvement Loans
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._645), made by Order in Council
P.C. 1969-592 of 25th March, 1969, for the purpose of recording loans
made under the Act is hereby continued.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559361 .Part}

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  provision of these Regulations requiring security to be taken under
  section 88 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) shall be deemed to have
  been satisfied if a document purporting to give such security is
  signed and delivered, notwithstanding that the security is not
  effective in respect of some or all of the property to which the
  document relates by reason of any statute exempting property from
  seizure under writs of execution that was in force on September 1,
  1944.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a borrower

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has made a false material statement in any
    application that has been scrutinized and checked by a responsible
    officer of the bank with the care required of him by the bank in the
    conduct of its ordinary business, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has used the proceeds of any loan otherwise than
    for the purpose specified in the application for the loan,

  the liability of the Minister to the bank under the Act shall not for
  such reason be discharged to any extent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a bank, under the terms of a loan or any
  agreement in connection therewith, pays the premiums under a policy of
  insurance on property under which any amount is or may become payable
  to the bank, the bank may charge the amount of the premiums to the
  borrower.
