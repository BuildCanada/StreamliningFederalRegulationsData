## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-839333 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[microbeads]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[microbeads]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the plastic microbeads set
    out in item 133 of the List of Toxic Substances in Schedule 1 to the
    [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
    1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31). ([microbilles]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[toiletries]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[toiletries]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any personal hair, skin,
    teeth or mouth care products for cleansing or hygiene, including
    exfoliants. ([produit de toilette]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-839338 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to a [[prescription drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} within the
meaning of section A.01.010 of the [Food and Drug
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870).

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-839341 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manufacture and importation

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not manufacture or import any toiletries that contain microbeads,
  unless the toiletries are also natural health products or
  non-prescription drugs, in which case the prohibition applies on or
  after July 1, 2018.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person must not sell any toiletries that contain
  microbeads on or after July 1, 2018, unless the toiletries are also
  natural health products or non-prescription drugs, in which case the
  prohibition applies on or after July 1, 2019.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- toiletries in transit

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person contravenes
subsection 3(1) if the toiletries are in transit through Canada, from a
place outside Canada to another place outside Canada.

## [Presence of Microbeads]{.HTitleText1} {#h-839349 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accredited laboratory

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  analysis performed to determine the presence of microbeads for the
  purposes of these Regulations must be performed by a laboratory that
  meets the following conditions at the time of the analysis:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is accredited

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} under the International Organization for
      Standardization standard ISO/IEC 17025, entitled General
      requirements for the competence of testing and calibration
      laboratories, by an accrediting body that is a signatory to the
      International Laboratory Accreditation Cooperation Mutual
      Recognition Arrangement, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} under the Environment Quality Act, CQLR, c. Q-2;
      and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), the scope of its
    accreditation includes the analysis performed to determine the
    presence of microbeads.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standards of good practice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If no method has been recognized by a standards
  development organization in respect of the analysis performed to
  determine the presence of microbeads and the scope of the laboratory's
  accreditation does not therefore include that analysis, the analysis
  must be performed in accordance with standards of good scientific
  practice that are generally accepted at the time that it is performed.
