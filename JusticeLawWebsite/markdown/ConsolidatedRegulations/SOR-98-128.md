## [Record of Receipts and Deposits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018029 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Every person who collects
or receives public money shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} record the following information in a register kept
  for that purpose:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date of every receipt and deposit,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amounts received, deposited or withheld, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} all other information required for identification
    or audit purposes; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on request, or if directed to do so by the
  appropriate Minister, issue a receipt or acknowledgment for any public
  money collected or received.

## [Crediting the Receiver General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018036 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Public money must be paid
to the credit of the Receiver General by depositing it in an account
established under subsection 17(2) of the Act

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} without delay, if the money is collected or received
  electronically; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} once a day, if the money is received by other than
  electronic means, or, if it is not cost effective to do so,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} once a week, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in exceptional circumstances and if authorized by
    the Receiver General, once a month.

- **[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite section 3, the portion of
  the following money that is public money and is collected or received
  electronically by a service provider must be paid to the credit of the
  Receiver General by depositing it, within two business days after the
  day of collection or receipt, in an account established under
  subsection 17(2) of the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} money collected or received as repayment of
    financial assistance as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Canada
    Student Financial Assistance
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-329), as repayment of a
    guaranteed student loan as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Canada
    Student Loans Act](/eng/acts/S-23) or as payment of interest owing
    on that assistance or loan; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} interest received by the service provider on the
    money referred to in paragraph (a).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in this section:

  [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day other than a

## [Chargeback and Withholding of Public Money]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018054 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person referred to in paragraph 17(5)(b) of the Act may, if an
  instruction for payment has been dishonoured after having been
  credited to the Receiver General, charge the amount of the instruction
  for payment back to the Receiver General

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by debiting the account into which the instruction
    for payment was originally credited to the Receiver General with
    that amount; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} providing the Receiver General with confirmation of
    the amount that is charged back.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Receiver General shall inform the appropriate
  minister of any amounts charged back under subsection (1).

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every person who collects
or receives public money is authorized to withhold their fees or
commissions from payments of that money to the credit of the Receiver
General if the fees or commissions are determined in accordance with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an enactment, as defined in the [Interpretation
  Act](/eng/acts/I-21); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a contractual arrangement entered into by Her Majesty
  and that person and are in the form of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a lump sum,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a percentage of each amount collected or received,
    or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a flat-rate amount for each amount collected or
    received.
