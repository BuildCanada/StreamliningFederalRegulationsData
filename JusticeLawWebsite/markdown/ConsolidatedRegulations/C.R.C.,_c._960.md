## Indian Reserve Waste Disposal Regulations {#indian-reserve-waste-disposal-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 960
:::

[INDIAN ACT](/eng/acts/I-5)

Regulations Respecting Waste Disposal in Indian Reserves
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599814 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Indian Reserve Waste Disposal
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._960).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599816 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Indian
    Affairs and Northern Development; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit issued pursuant to
    section 5; ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a reserve as defined in the
    [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5); ([réserve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes garbage, liquid and
    semi-liquid substances, land-fill and scrap of all kinds and any
    combinations of any of the foregoing. ([déchets]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Prohibitions Respecting the Disposal or Storage of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599822 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} operate a garbage dump in a reserve, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use any land in a reserve for the disposal or storage
  of waste

except under the authority of a permit issued pursuant to paragraph 5(a)
or (b) and in the manner specified in the permit.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No Indian who is lawfully
in possession of any lands in a reserve and no person to whom reserve
lands have been leased or who lawfully occupies, uses, resides or
otherwise exercises rights on land in a reserve shall permit any person
to operate on that land a garbage dump or use any part of that land for
the disposal or storage of waste unless a permit to carry on that action
on that land has been issued pursuant to paragraph 5(a) or (b) and is
still valid.

## [Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599828 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister or the council
of a band, if authorized by the Minister pursuant to section 8, may
issue to any person a permit authorizing that person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to operate a garbage dump in a reserve;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to use land in a reserve for the disposal or storage
  of waste; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to burn waste on any land in a reserve.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A permit shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} specify the land in respect of which the permit is
  issued; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} specify the manner in which the activity authorized
  therein shall be exercised.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 11, a
permit shall expire on December 31st next following the date of issue
thereof.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, in
writing, authorize the council of any band to issue a permit in respect
of land in the reserve of that band and shall, in the authorization,
specify the manner in which the activity to be authorized in the permit
shall be exercised.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The revocation of an
authorization given by the Minister pursuant to section 8 does not
affect the validity of any permit issued under that authorization.

## [Burning of Waste Prohibited]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599839 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** No person shall burn any
waste on any land in a reserve except under the authority of a permit
issued pursuant to paragraph 5(c).

## [Orders and Cancellation of Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599841 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** If the holder of a permit
issued pursuant to section 5

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} operates a garbage dump in a reserve,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} uses land in a reserve for the disposal or storage of
  waste, or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} burns waste on any land in a reserve,

other than in the manner specified in the permit, the Minister or the
council of the band, whoever issued the permit, may cancel the permit
and order the holder of the permit to close and clean up the garbage
dump or to clean up the land in the reserve, as may be applicable, in a
manner satisfactory to the Minister or the council.

## [Violation of Sections 3 and 10]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599847 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Where a person is
convicted of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} operating a garbage dump in a reserve or using land
  in a reserve for the disposal or storage of waste except under the
  authority of a permit issued pursuant to paragraph 5(a) or (b), or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} burning waste on land in a reserve except under the
  authority of a permit issued pursuant to paragraph 5(c),

the Minister may order that person to close and clean up the garbage
dump or to clean up the land, as may be applicable, in a manner
satisfactory to the Minister.

## [Compliance with Orders]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599852 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Any person who has been
ordered by the Minister or the council of a band to do anything pursuant
to section 11 or section 12 shall comply with that order without delay.

## [Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599854 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Every person who violates
these Regulations is liable on summary conviction to a fine not
exceeding \$100 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three
months, or to both.
