## Billiard Cloth Remission Order, 1996 {#billiard-cloth-remission-order-1996 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/96-201
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1996-04-16

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties on Billiard Cloth for
the Manufacture or Repair of Billiard Tables

P.C. 1996-496 1996-04-16
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 101 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-96-201_e_hq_6200){#fn_SOR-96-201_e_hq_6200-ID0EDBAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of
customs duties on billiard cloth for the manufacture or repair of
billiard tables](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-201).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-980396 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Billiard Cloth Remission Order, 1996](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-201).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-980398 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
Schedule I to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on billiard
cloth, of tariff item Nos. 5111.19.00 or 5112.19.10, for the manufacture
or repair of billiard tables.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-980400 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted
pursuant to section 2 on the condition that:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the billiard cloth is imported into Canada during the
  period commencing on July 1, 1995 and ending on December 31, 1997; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within three years of the date of importation of the
  goods for which remission is claimed.
