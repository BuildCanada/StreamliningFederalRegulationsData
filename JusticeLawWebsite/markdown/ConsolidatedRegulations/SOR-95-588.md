## Unemployment Insurance Rate of Premium, 1996, Order {#unemployment-insurance-rate-of-premium-1996-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-588
:::

[EMPLOYMENT INSURANCE ACT](/eng/acts/E-5.6)

Registration 1995-12-13

Order Fixing the Rate of Premium that Persons Employed in Insurable
Employment are Required to Pay for the Year 1996

P.C. 1995-2097  1995-12-13
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Employment and Immigration and the Minister of Finance,
pursuant to section 48.1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-95-588_e_hq_6368){#fn_SOR-95-588_e_hq_6368-ID0EFBAA}
of the Unemployment Insurance Act, is pleased hereby to make the annexed
[Order fixing the rate of premium that persons employed in insurable
employment are required to pay for the year
1996](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-588).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote

  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977102 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Unemployment Insurance Rate of Premium, 1996,
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-588).

## [Rate of Premium]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977104 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The rate of premium that
persons employed in insurable employment are required to pay for the
year 1996 is 2.95 per cent of insurable earnings in that year.
