## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-807251 .Part}

Definition of [[rule]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[rule]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a rule that, under section 19 of the
[Railway Safety Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2), has been approved by the
Minister.

## [Submission of an Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-807254 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Email or registered mail

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** An application for the
issuance or variation of a railway operating certificate must be
submitted to the Minister by email or registered mail.

## [Application for the Issuance of a Railway Operating Certificate --- Railway Company]{.HTitleText1} {#h-807257 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of sections 4 and 5

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Sections 4 and 5 apply in
respect of an application for the issuance of a railway operating
certificate authorizing a railway company to operate and maintain a
railway.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of an application

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application must contain
all of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's legal name;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the applicant's railway by province
  and territory;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the applicant's operations;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a list of the rules that the applicant has in order
  to meet the conditions of issuance; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an attestation by the applicant's chief executive
  officer or most senior officer that the application is complete and
  accurate and that the applicant

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has the human and financial resources to operate
    and maintain its railway at the highest level of safety, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has or will have a safety management system that
    meets the requirements of the [Railway Safety Management System
    Regulations, 2015](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-26).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-26, s. 87
:::

::: PITLink

Version](section-4-20150101.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of issuance

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The conditions of issuance
are as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has rules that reproduce the Canadian
  Rail Operating Rules or that provide an equivalent level of safety to
  them;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has rules respecting the management of
  fatigue for operating employees;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has rules respecting the medical
  fitness for duty of employees who hold positions that are critical to
  safe railway operations;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has rules respecting employee
  qualifications;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant's operations involve the operation
  and maintenance of line works, the applicant has rules that set out
  minimum safety standards for track;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant's operations involve the transport
  of passengers, the applicant has rules respecting the safe handling of
  passengers and rules that set out minimum safety standards for

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} passenger cars,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} train brakes, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} locomotives;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant's operations involve the transport
  of freight, the applicant has rules that set out minimum safety
  standards for

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} freight cars,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} train brakes,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} locomotives, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} retroreflective materials and their application on
    railway equipment; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has the human and financial resources
  to operate and maintain its railway at the highest level of safety.

## [Application for the Issuance of a Railway Operating Certificate --- Local Railway Company]{.HTitleText1} {#h-807288 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of sections 7 and 8

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Sections 7 and 8 apply in
respect of an application for the issuance of a railway operating
certificate authorizing a local railway company to operate railway
equipment on a railway.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of an application

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application must contain all of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's legal name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the railways, by province and
    territory, on which the applicant will operate railway equipment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the applicant's operations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a list of the rules that the applicant has in order
    to meet the conditions of issuance; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an attestation by the applicant's chief executive
    officer or most senior officer that the application is complete and
    accurate and that the applicant

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has the human and financial resources to operate
      railway equipment on a railway at the highest level of safety, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has or will have a safety management system that
      meets the requirements of the [Railway Safety Management System
      Regulations, 2015](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-26).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of instruments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must also contain a list of all the
  instruments --- including agreements with railway companies,
  provincial laws and territorial laws --- that contain safety
  requirements that apply to the operation of railway equipment by the
  applicant on the railways described in accordance with subsection (1).
  The list must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be organized by instrument type; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} describe each instrument and any requirements
    respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the management of fatigue for operating
      employees,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the medical fitness for duty of employees who
      hold positions that are critical to the safe operation of railway
      equipment, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} employee qualifications.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-26, s. 88
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of issuance

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The conditions of issuance
are as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has rules that reproduce the Canadian
  Rail Operating Rules or that provide an equivalent level of safety to
  them;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant's operations involve the transport
  of passengers, the applicant has rules respecting the safe handling of
  passengers and rules that set out minimum safety standards for

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} passenger cars,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} train brakes, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} locomotives;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant's operations involve the transport
  of freight, the applicant has rules that set out minimum safety
  standards for

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} freight cars,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} train brakes,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} locomotives, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} retroreflective materials and their application on
    railway equipment; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has the human and financial resources
  to operate railway equipment on a railway at the highest level of
  safety.

## [Application for the Variation of a Railway Operating Certificate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-807323 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of an application

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An application for the
variation of a railway operating certificate must contain all of the
following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's legal name;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the certificate number;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the variation applied for and the
  reasons for it; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an attestation by the applicant's chief executive
  officer or most senior officer that the application is complete and
  accurate and that the conditions of issuance for the certificate
  continue to be met.
