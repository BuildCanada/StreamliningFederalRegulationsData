## Airport Vehicle Parking Charges Regulations {#airport-vehicle-parking-charges-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-543
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1987-09-08

Airport Vehicle Parking Charges Regulations
::::

:::::::::::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 3.4(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fnstar1_e){#fnstar1_e-ID0EKCCA} of the
[Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2) and the [Ministerial Regulations
Authorization Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-631)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fnstar2_e){#fnstar2_e-ID0EFCCA}, made by Order
in Council P.C. 1986-1348 of June 5, 1986, hereby revokes the Airport
Vehicle Parking Fees Regulations, C.R.C., c. 7, and makes the annexed
Regulations respecting the parking of vehicles at airports, in
substitution therefor.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-907578 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of parking services provided by the
  Minister of Transport in an area accessible to the public at an
  airport that is operated by the Minister and set out in the schedule.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to a member of the Senate for the parking of the
    member's vehicle at the airport serving the city, town or
    municipality where the member resides in areas identified by the
    Minister of Transport for such parking; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to a member of the House of Commons for the parking
    of the member's vehicle at the airport serving his or her electoral
    district in areas identified by the Minister of Transport for such
    parking.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-79, s. 1
- SOR/2012-102, s. 3
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 2"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The user of a parking
service set out in Column 1 of the schedule, at the airport set out in
Column 2, must pay the charge set out in Column 3.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-192, s. 1
- SOR/2012-102, s. 3
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The charge referred to in
section 3 must be paid

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where a metered parking space is used, at the
  commencement of the use of the parking service; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where other than a metered parking space is used, at
  the commencement or termination of use of the parking service as
  indicated by a public notice posted at the entrance to the parking lot
  or structure.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-192, s. 2
- SOR/2012-102, s. 4
