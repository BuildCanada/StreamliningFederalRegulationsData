## Order Transferring to the Secretary of State of Canada Certain Powers, Duties and Functions of the Ministers of Employment and Immigration, the Solicitor General of Canada and Multiculturalism and Citizenship Relating to Immigration and Citizenship and to the Department of the Secretary of State of Canada the Control and Supervision of Certain Portions of the Public Service Relating Thereto {#order-transferring-to-the-secretary-of-state-of-canada-certain-powers-duties-and-functions-of-the-ministers-of-employment-and-immigration-the-solicitor-general-of-canada-and-multiculturalism-and-citizenship-relating-to-immigration-and-citizenship-and-to-the-department-of-the-secretary-of-state-of-canada-the-control-and-supervision-of-certain-portions-of-the-public-service-relating-thereto .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-201
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1993-11-17

Order Transferring to the Secretary of State of Canada Certain Powers,
Duties and Functions of the Ministers of Employment and Immigration, the
Solicitor General of Canada and Multiculturalism and Citizenship
Relating to Immigration and Citizenship and to the Department of the
Secretary of State of Canada the Control and Supervision of Certain
Portions of the Public Service Relating Thereto

P.C. 1993-1901  1993-11-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), is pleased
hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to transfer to the Secretary of State of Canada

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of
    Employment and Immigration referred to in paragraphs (a) and (b) of
    Order in Council P.C. 1993-1134 of June 1, 1993[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6528){#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6528-ID0EBBDBAA},

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6528-ID0EBBDBAA){#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6528}SI/93-82,
      1993 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2697
      :::

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the powers, duties and functions of the Minister
    of Employment and Immigration referred to in subparagraphs (a)(i)
    and (ii) of Order in Council P.C. 1993-1669 of August 13,
    1993[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6529){#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6529-ID0EBBCBAA},

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6529-ID0EBBCBAA){#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6529}SI/93-168,
      1993 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3539
      :::

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the powers, duties and functions of the Solicitor
    General of Canada relating to immigration referred to in
    paragraph (a) of Order in Council P.C. 1993-1669 of August 13,
    1993[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6529){#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6529-ID0EBABBAA},
    and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} all the powers, duties and functions of the
    Minister of Multiculturalism and Citizenship relating to citizenship
    under any Act of Parliament, other than powers, duties or functions
    relating to voluntary action and human rights; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to transfer to the Department of the Secretary of
  State of Canada the control and supervision of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Immigration and Overseas Health Services
    Division of the Canada Employment and Immigration Commission
    referred to in Order in Council P.C. 1993-1135 of June 1,
    1993[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6531){#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6531-ID0EBBGAAA},

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6531-ID0EBBGAAA){#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6531}SI/93-83,
      1993 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2698
      :::

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Settlement Branch of the Department of
    Employment and Immigration,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that portion of Immigration in the Department of
    the Solicitor General referred to in paragraph (a) of Order in
    Council P.C. 1993-1485 of June 25, 1993[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6532){#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6532-ID0EBBEAAA},

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6532-ID0EBBEAAA){#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6532}SI/93-139,
      1993 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3231
      :::

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} those portions of the Department of the Solicitor
    General referred to in subparagraphs (b)(i) to (v) of Order in
    Council P.C. 1993-1669 of August 13, 1993[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6529){#fn_SI-93-201_e_hq_6529-ID0EBADAAA},

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} that portion of the Canada Employment and
    Immigration Commission relating to immigration settlement and the
    matters referred to in section 119 of the Immigration Act,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} those portions of the Canada Employment and
    Immigration Commission relating to the development of policy in
    respect of the matters referred to in subsections 6(1), (3), (5)
    and (8) and sections 7 and 108 of the Immigration Act, and

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} that portion of the Department of
    Multiculturalism and Citizenship relating to citizenship, other than
    the portions thereof relating to voluntary action and human rights.
