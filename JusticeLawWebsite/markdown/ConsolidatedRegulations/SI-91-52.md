## Order Giving Notice that a Tax Agreement Between Canada and the Polish People's Republic Came into Force on November 30, 1989 {#order-giving-notice-that-a-tax-agreement-between-canada-and-the-polish-peoples-republic-came-into-force-on-november-30-1989 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/91-52
:::

[AGREEMENTS AND CONVENTIONS](/eng/acts/Z-02)

CANADA-POLAND INCOME TAX CONVENTION ACT, 1989

Registration 1991-04-10

Order Giving Notice that a Tax Agreement Between Canada and the Polish
People's Republic Came into Force on November 30, 1989
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
Notice is hereby given, pursuant to section 12 of the Canada-Poland
Income Tax Convention Act, 1989[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-91-52_e_hq_6213){#fn_SI-91-52_e_hq_6213-ID0EBBCA},
that the Convention between the Government of Canada and the Government
of the Polish People's Republic for the Avoidance of Double Taxation
with respect to Taxes on Income and on Capital came into force on
November 30, 1989.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-91-52_e_hq_6213-ID0EBBCA){#fn_SI-91-52_e_hq_6213}S.C.
  1989, c. 20, Part II
  :::

Ottawa, February 2, 1991

::: FlushRight
MICHAEL WILSON
:::

::: FlushRight
*Minister of Finance*
