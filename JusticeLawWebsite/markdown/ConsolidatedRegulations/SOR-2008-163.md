## [Prescribed Percentage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-744941 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed percentage

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 508(2) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the percentage is
35%.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed percentage

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 508(3) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the percentage is
50%.
