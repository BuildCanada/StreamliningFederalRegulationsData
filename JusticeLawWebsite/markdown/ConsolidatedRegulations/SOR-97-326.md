## Regulations Exempting Goods of Chile from the Application of Anti-dumping Measures {#regulations-exempting-goods-of-chile-from-the-application-of-anti-dumping-measures .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-326
:::

[SPECIAL IMPORT MEASURES ACT](/eng/acts/S-15)

Registration 1997-07-05

Regulations Exempting Goods of Chile from the Application of
Anti-dumping Measures

P.C. 1997-957  1997-07-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 14(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-97-326_e_hq_5750){#fn_SOR-97-326_e_hq_5750-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15), hereby makes the
annexed Regulations Exempting Goods of Chile from the Application of
Anti-dumping Measures.

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006027 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Goods of Chile that qualify
as originating goods under the [CCFTA Rules of Origin
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-340) and that are entitled to the
benefit of the Chile Tariff under the [CCFTA Tariff Preference
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-322) are exempt from the
application of anti-dumping measures under the [Special Import Measures
Act](/eng/acts/S-15) as of the earlier of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the rate for all goods covered by
  the subheading that includes the tariff item under which the goods are
  classified is free under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) for
  goods of Chile that are originating goods and under the Arancel
  Aduanero for goods of Canada that are originating goods; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} January 1, 2003.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006031 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-97-326_e_hq_5751){#fn_SOR-97-326_e_hq_5751-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 89 of the [Canada-Chile Free Trade Agreement Implementation
Act](/eng/acts/C-1.6), chapter 14 of the Statutes of Canada, 1997, comes
into force.
