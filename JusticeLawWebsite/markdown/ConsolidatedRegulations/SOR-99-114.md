## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1026508 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The words and expressions
used in these Regulations have the same meaning as in Division 3 of Part
7 of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-79, s. 2

## [Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1026512 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The holder of a permit
granted under section 127 of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) shall pay to the Receiver General, for the
permit, a fee of \$470 for every 1 000 m³, or portion thereof, of
dredged material or inert, inorganic geological matter, that is
authorized by the permit to be disposed of at sea.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-79, s. 3
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 2"){.PITLink}
:::

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1026516 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The fee prescribed in
section 2 shall be paid

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in full prior to publication of the permit in the
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by payment of at least 50 per cent of the total fee,
  as calculated in accordance with section 2, prior to publication of
  the permit in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) and
  payment of the balance prior to the expiry of one half of the period
  for which the permit is valid.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1026520 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the date on which they are registered.
