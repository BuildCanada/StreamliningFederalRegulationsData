## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021283 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Permit.

[[CWC toxic chemicals and precursors]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CWC toxic chemicals and precursors]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    goods that are described in item 74 of the [Import Control
    List](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._604). ([produits chimiques
    toxiques et précurseurs CAC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Export Controls Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Export Controls Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Export
    Controls Division of the Department of Foreign Affairs and
    International Trade. ([Direction des contrôles à
    l'exportation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 1
    of the [Export Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202).
    ([Guide]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021288 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any resident of Canada may,
under the authority of and in accordance with this Permit, import CWC
Toxic Chemicals and Precursors from all sources.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021290 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** It is a condition of this
Permit that the importer

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep at the importer's place of business or residence
  the documents in respect of each import made under this Permit for a
  period of six years after the date of the import;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on request, make the documents referred to in
  paragraph (a) available to an officer of the Export Controls Division;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where any CWC toxic chemicals and precursors are
  required to be reported in a prescribed form under the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), insert the statement "GIP-108" or "LGI-108" in
  the appropriate field of the prescribed form.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021295 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Permit comes into
force on April 23, 1998.
