## Gatineau Region Wood Producers' Levy (Interprovincial and Export Trade) Order {#gatineau-region-wood-producers-levy-interprovincial-and-export-trade-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-407
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1988-07-25

Order Respecting the Fixing and Imposing of Levies on, and the
Collecting of Levies from, Producers of Wood in the Gatineau Region in
the Province of Quebec, With Respect to the Marketing of Wood in
Interprovincial and Export Trade
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Gatineau Region Wood Producers' Board, pursuant to section 4 of the
[Quebec Wood Order, 1983](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-713), made by Order in
Council P.C. 1983-2885 of September 22, 1983[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBBA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Order respecting the fixing and imposing of levies on, and the
collecting of levies from, producers of wood in the Gatineau Region in
the Province of Quebec, with respect to the marketing of wood in
interprovincial and export trade](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-407).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBBA){#fn_1e}SOR/83-713, 1983 [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3444
  :::

Maniwaki, Quebec, July 20, 1988
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-912751 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Gatineau Region Wood Producers' Levy (Interprovincial and Export
Trade) Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-407).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-912753 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Gatineau Region Wood
    Producers' Board; ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production or marketing of wood; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[wood]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wood]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means wood produced in the regional
    municipality of Gatineau Valley in the Province of Quebec, except
    for the township municipalities of Denholm, Dorion and Low and the
    municipality of Kazabazua. ([bois]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-912758 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A producer shall pay to the
Board in respect of the marketing of wood in interprovincial and export
trade a general administration levy calculated at one of the following
rates:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in tonnes, \$0.56 per
  tonne;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in apparent cubic metres,
  \$0.35 per apparent cubic metre;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in solid cubic metres,
  \$0.53 per solid cubic metre;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in cords of 128 apparent
  cubic feet (4 ft. ×  4 ft. ×  8 ft.), \$1.27 per cord;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in cords of 160 apparent
  cubic feet (5 ft. × 4 ft. × 8 ft.), \$1.59 per cord;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in cords of 192 apparent
  cubic feet (6 ft. × 4 ft. × 8 ft.), \$1.90 per cord;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in cords of 224 apparent
  cubic feet (7 ft. × 4 ft. × 8 ft.), \$2.22 per cord;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in cords of 256 apparent
  cubic feet (8 ft. × 4 ft. × 8 ft.), \$2.54 per cord;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in cubic feet, \$1.50 per
  100 cubic feet;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured by board measure, \$2.54
  per 1,000 board feet;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in short tons, \$0.50 per
  short ton;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in units of 1,000 pounds,
  \$0.25 per unit;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is measured in firewood units (4 ft. ×
  8 ft. × 16 in.), \$0.42 per unit; and

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} where the wood is sold by the piece, 4.6 per cent of
  the sale price to the mill.

## [Method of Collection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-912774 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), a producer shall pay any levy payable by
  the producer pursuant to section 3 to the Board at its headquarters in
  Maniwaki, Quebec, within 30 days after the marketing of the wood in
  respect of which the levy is payable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board markets wood in interprovincial or
  export trade on behalf of a producer, the Board may deduct from any
  money payable to the producer by the Board any levy payable by the
  producer, pursuant to section 3, in respect of the marketing of that
  wood.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a producer does not pay the levy set out in
  section 3 to the Board pursuant to subsection (1) and where the Board
  has not deducted the levy pursuant to subsection (2), every person who
  markets that producer's wood in interprovincial or export trade shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deduct from any money payable to the producer by
    that person any levy payable by the producer to the Board pursuant
    to section 3; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 30 days after the marketing of the wood in
    respect of which the levy is payable, pay the levy to the Board and
    provide the Board with an accounting in respect of the payment.
