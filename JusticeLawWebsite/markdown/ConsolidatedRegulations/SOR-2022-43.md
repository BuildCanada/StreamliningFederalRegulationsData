## Royal Canadian Mounted Police (Dependants) Pension Fund Increase in Benefits Order {#royal-canadian-mounted-police-dependants-pension-fund-increase-in-benefits-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2022-43
:::

[ROYAL CANADIAN MOUNTED POLICE PENSION CONTINUATION
ACT](/eng/acts/R-10.6)

Registration 2022-03-04

Royal Canadian Mounted Police (Dependants) Pension Fund Increase in
Benefits Order

P.C. 2022-198 2022-03-03
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas it appears from a report made under section 56 of the [Royal
Canadian Mounted Police Pension Continuation
Act](/eng/acts/R-10.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4549_hq_29127){#fn_81000-2-4549_hq_29127-ID0EBBBA}
that the Royal Canadian Mounted Police (Dependants) Pension Fund is
substantially in excess of the amount required to make adequate
provision for the prospective payments to be made out of it;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4549_hq_29127-ID0EBBBA){#fn_81000-2-4549_hq_29127}R.S.C.
  1970, c. R-10
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
Preparedness, pursuant to subsection 57(1) of the [Royal Canadian
Mounted Police Pension Continuation Act](/eng/acts/R-10.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4549_hq_29127){#fn_81000-2-4549_hq_29127-ID0EDAAA},
makes the annexed [Royal Canadian Mounted Police (Dependants) Pension
Fund Increase in Benefits Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-122).
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following pension
benefits, as provided in Part IV of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
Pension Continuation Act](/eng/acts/R-10.6), are to be increased in the
following manner:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pensions for current and prospective widows are to be
  increased by 2% on April 1 in each of the 2020, 2021 and 2022 plan
  years;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} lump sum benefits payable on the death of a former
  member who contributed to the plan and who is not survived by a widow
  are to be increased by 2% on April 1 in each of the 2020, 2021 and
  2022 plan years; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} residual amounts payable on the death of a former
  member's widow are to be calculated with a deemed increase in the
  former member's contributions of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 1,381% if the widow's death occurs in the 2021 plan
    year,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 1,411% if the widow's death occurs in the 2022
    plan year, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 1,441% if the widow's death occurs in the 2023
    plan year.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
