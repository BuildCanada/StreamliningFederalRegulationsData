## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765674 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).

## [Exemption Circumstances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765676 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
section 1005 of the Act, the prescribed circumstances are that the
exemption does not prejudice any of the shareholders or the public
interest.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765678 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on June 1, 2011.
