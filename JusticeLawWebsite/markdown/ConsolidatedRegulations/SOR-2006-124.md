## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-723682 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Pest Control Products
      Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[antimicrobial agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[antimicrobial agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      non-agricultural pest control product that is manufactured,
      represented, distributed or used as a means to directly or
      indirectly control or destroy the following on or in inanimate
      objects, industrial processes and systems, surfaces, water and
      air:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} micro-organisms; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} organisms that are not vascular plants and that
        cause fouling. ([agent antimicrobien]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[antimicrobial preservative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[antimicrobial preservative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      chemical substance, or a mixture of chemical substances, that is
      intentionally incorporated into, or applied to, an article for the
      purpose of preserving it from deterioration or degradation by
      preventing the growth of micro-organisms. ([agent de conservation
      antimicrobien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approved label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approved label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a label that meets
      the conditions of registration relating to the label as specified
      by the Minister and that is placed in the Register. ([étiquette
      approuvée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[CAS registry number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[CAS registry number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      identification number that is assigned to a chemical substance by
      the Chemical Abstracts Service Division of the American Chemical
      Society. ([numéro d'enregistrement CAS]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[certificate of equivalency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[certificate of equivalency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      certificate that is issued under subsection 39(1) with respect to
      a foreign product. ([certificat d'équivalence]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[common chemical name]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[common chemical name]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to an
      active ingredient of a pest control product, means the name set
      out in International Standard ISO 1750:1981 (E/F), entitled
      Pesticides and other agrochemicals --- Common names, published by
      the International Organization for Standardization, as amended

## [Prescribed Pest Control Products]{.HTitleText1} {#h-723725 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribing

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph (c) of the definition [[pest control
product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the Act, the
following are prescribed to be pest control products:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a device that is manufactured, represented,
  distributed or used to directly or indirectly control, destroy,
  attract or repel a pest or to mitigate or prevent the injurious,
  noxious or troublesome effects of a pest;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a compound or substance that is not an ingredient of
  a pest control product described in paragraph (a) of that definition
  but is added to or used with such a product to enhance or modify its
  physical or chemical characteristics or to modify an effect on host
  organisms in connection with which the product is intended to be used;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an active ingredient that is manufactured,
  represented, distributed or used to directly or indirectly control,
  destroy, attract or repel a pest or to mitigate or prevent the
  injurious, noxious or troublesome effects of a pest;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a treated article; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} treated seed.

## [Exemption of Certain Pest Control Products]{.HTitleText1} {#h-723730 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from application of Act

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following pest control products are exempt from the application of the
  Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a pest control product that is a device of a type
    not described in Schedule 1;

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} despite paragraph (a), an ultraviolet
    radiation-emitting device or ozone-generating device that is a
    [[device]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 2 of the
    [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27) and classified as a Class II,
    III or IV medical device under the [Medical Devices
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a pest control product that is subject to the [Food
    and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27) and that is used only

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to control arthropods on or in humans or animals,
      if the pest control product is to be administered directly and not
      by topical application, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} during the cooking or processing of food for
      humans to preserve the food;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a pest control product that is used to control
    viruses, bacteria or other micro-organisms in premises in which food
    is manufactured, prepared or kept for sale;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a pest control product that is a
    [[drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the [Food
    and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27), that is used to destroy or
    inactivate micro-organisms, or to reduce or control their number, on
    a non-living and non-liquid surface, other than a pest control
    product that is a [[biocide]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
    subsection 1(1) of the [Biocides
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-110), to which the exemption
    in subsection 66(1) or (3) of those Regulations applies;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2024-110, s. 85\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a pest control product --- other than an organism,
    a device of a type described in Schedule 1 or a treated article ---
    that is imported by a user for their personal use, that is in their
    possession at the time of the importation and that meets the
    following conditions:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the total quantity of the product does not exceed
      500 g or 500 mL,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by virtue of its active ingredient and
      concentration, the product would have the product class
      designation "DOMESTIC" if it were registered in Canada,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the product is registered or otherwise
      authorized in the country of origin as a product equivalent to a
      pest control product,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the product is in its original package with the
      original label intact,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the information on the package and label is in
      either English or French, is clear and legible, allows for the
      determination of the active ingredient, concentration and quantity
      of the product and includes the registration or authorization
      number assigned by the regulatory body in the country of origin;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a pest control product that is a treated article
    into which the only pest control product that is incorporated or to
    which the product is applied is an antimicrobial preservative, if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the product is imported by a user for their
      personal use,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the product is in their possession at the time
      of the importation,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the product is in its original package with the
      original label intact, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the information on the package and label is in
      English or French and is clear and legible; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a pest control product that is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an antimicrobial preservative when it is used in
      the manufacture of a treated article that is

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a feed that is regulated under the [Feeds
        Act](/eng/acts/F-9),

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a fertilizer or supplement that is regulated
        under the [Fertilizers Act](/eng/acts/F-10),

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} a drug or cosmetic that is regulated under the
        [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27), or

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} a medical device that is a
        [[device]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 2 of the
        [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27) and classified as a Class
        II, III or IV medical device under the [Medical Devices
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a treated article that is described in any of
      clauses (i)(A) to (D) into which the only pest control product
      that is incorporated or to which the product is applied is an
      antimicrobial preservative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption for named uses only

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A pest control product that is exempt under paragraph
  (1)(c) or (d) is exempt only in respect of any use described in that
  paragraph.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safety information

- **[[3.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  information set out in subsection (2) and the safety information set
  out in subsections (3) to (8) must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appear in documents that accompany the shipment of
    the pest control product during transport through Canada and during
    any short-term storage in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be readily available for use in emergency response
    to any accidents or incidents involving that product;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} appear in a manner that is clearly legible and
    indelible;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} appear in either English or French; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} appear in documents that accompany each load of a
    shipment, if the shipment is broken into loads that are sent to
    different destinations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The shipment of the pest control product must be
  accompanied by documents that contain all of the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the product, which may include a
    distinctive brand or trademark;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a declaration of net quantity of the product in the
    package;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the importer and the names of the
    exporter and owner of the product, if either is different from the
    importer;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal address, email address and
    telephone number of a contact person to whom public inquiries may be
    directed; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the physical form of the product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hazard identification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The shipment of the pest control product must be
  accompanied by documents that contain information that identifies the
  nature and degree of hazard inherent in the pest control product by
  appropriate precautionary symbols and signal words selected from
  either Schedule 3 or 4. For precautionary symbols and signal words
  selected from Schedule 4, a hazard statement that indicates the nature
  of the primary hazard to which the precautionary symbol relates must
  also be included.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Active ingredient

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The shipment of the pest control product must be
  accompanied by documents that contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the common chemical name of the active ingredient
    of the pest control product or, if it has no common chemical name,
    its chemical or other name; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of the active ingredient.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First aid measures

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The shipment of the pest control product must be
  accompanied by documents that contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} instructions that set out the practical measures to
    be taken in the event of poisoning, intoxication or injury caused by
    the pest control product and that include the statement "Take the
    documents accompanying the shipment or the container label, if it
    contains the same information, or the product name with you when
    seeking medical attention."; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information that is essential to the treatment of
    persons who are poisoned, intoxicated or injured by the pest control
    product, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} antidotes and remedial measures or, if no
      specific antidote or remedial measure exists, the statement "Treat
      symptomatically.",

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the injury or the symptoms of
      poisoning or intoxication, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a list of the components of the product that
      may affect the treatment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Firefighting, handling, transport and
  storage

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The shipment of the pest control product must be
  accompanied by documents that contain information that identifies the
  appropriate measures to be taken with respect to firefighting,
  handling, transportation and storage of the pest control product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accidental release, decontamination and
  safe disposal

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The shipment of the pest control product must be
  accompanied by documents that contain instructions on the procedure to
  be followed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in case of accidental release;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for decontamination; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the safe disposal of the pest control product
    and its packages.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Toxicological and ecological risk
  reduction

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The shipment of the pest control product must be
  accompanied by documents that contain information that identifies any
  significant risk to health and the environment, and instructions on
  procedures to reduce that risk.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Packaging

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The packaging of the pest control product must be
  constructed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to contain the product safely under normal
    conditions of storage and transportation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to minimize degradation or change of its contents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage and transport

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} The pest control product must be stored and
  transported in a separate compartment from any human food or animal
  feed or in such a way as to avoid any possible contamination of the
  food or feed if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the product bears the signal word "POISON"
    superimposed on the precautionary symbol for danger set out in item
    2 of Schedule 3; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the product bears the signal word DANGER and the
    hazard statements "Fatal if swallowed", "Fatal in contact with
    skin", "Toxic if swallowed", "Toxic in contact with skin", "Toxic if
    inhaled" or "Fatal if inhaled" set out in item 4 of Schedule 4.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a pest control product that is an ultraviolet

    component that emits ultraviolet radiation, if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the device is certified by a product
      certification body as meeting the applicable Canadian electrical
      safety requirements,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the certification mark of the product
      certification body appears on the label of the device,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any claim of efficacy that is made in respect

      sanitization,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} no express or implied claim in relation to the

      of the device,

      emits ultraviolet radiation, is fully shielded or enclosed in the

      radiation,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the device has a mechanism that

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} locks the device to prevent access to the

        radiation, during operation, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} automatically shuts off the device if it is
        opened during operation,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} a user of the device who follows the
      instructions referred to in paragraph 30.1(3)(c) for replacing the
      ultraviolet lamp or any other component that emits ultraviolet
      radiation is not exposed to the radiation,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the device does not produce or generate any
      other substance, including ozone or hydroxyl, that may be harmful
      to human health during operation, and

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the device is used in commercial or industrial
      premises or in educational institutions, health care facilities or
      similar institutions or facilities and, as applicable,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} it does not meet the requirement set out in
        subparagraph (v), is contained within the ventilation system in
        a manner that prevents exposure to ultraviolet radiation and is
        installed by an electrician who is the holder of a licence
        issued by the applicable provincial licensing authority, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} it does not meet one of the requirements set
        out in subparagraph (v) or (vi) and is certified by a product
        certification body either as being part of the Exempt group
        classification set out in the IEC 62471 standard developed by
        the International Electrotechnical Commission entitled
        Photobiological safety of lamps and lamp systems, as amended
        from time to time, or as meeting the applicable requirements of
        one of the standards set out in the List of Equivalent Standards
        for Ultraviolet Photobiological Hazard Assessment, published by
        the Government of Canada on its website, as amended from time to
        time;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an ultraviolet radiation-emitting device that is an
    ultraviolet lamp, or other component that emits ultraviolet
    radiation, if the component

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is manufactured, represented or distributed
      solely for use in a device referred to in paragraph (c), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} meets the conditions set out in subparagraphs
      (c)(iii) and (iv);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an ultraviolet radiation-emitting device that is an
    ultraviolet lamp, or other component that emits ultraviolet
    radiation, that is manufactured, represented or distributed for use
    as a component in a registered device, if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the component is evaluated as part of an
      application to register, amend or renew the registration of the
      device, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the label of the component contains any
      information specified by the Minister in the conditions of
      registration;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a pest control product that is a treated article
    into which the only pest control product that is incorporated or to
    which it is applied is an antimicrobial preservative, if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the sole purpose of the treatment is to protect
      or preserve the article,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the active ingredient of the preservative is
      registered or otherwise authorized under paragraph 21(5)(a) of the
      Act for incorporation into, or application to the article,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the article is treated in
      Canada, the preservative is registered or otherwise authorized
      under paragraph (b) or paragraph 21(5)(a) of the Act,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the treatment

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} includes the same active ingredient as that
        contained in a preservative registered in Canada or otherwise
        authorized under paragraph (b) or paragraph 21(5)(a) of the Act,
        and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} meets any conditions of registration or
        authorization, as the case may be, that relate to the method of
        application, the uses for which the preservative may be applied
        to or incorporated into the article and the range of application
        rates as specified by the Minister under the Act or as set out
        in Schedule 2; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a pest control product that is also a food additive
    set out in column 1 of the List of Permitted Preservatives,
    published by the Government of Canada on its website, as amended
    from time to time, if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the product is used as an antimicrobial
      preservative,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the sole purpose of the treatment is to protect
      or preserve the article, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of preservative protects or
      preserves the article, but does not exceed the quantity necessary
      to do so.

## [Product Classes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-723772 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The following are the
classes of pest control products:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} "DOMESTIC", if the pest control product is to be
  distributed primarily to the general public for personal use in or
  around their homes;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} "COMMERCIAL", if the pest control product is to be
  distributed for use in commercial activities that are specified on the
  label;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} "RESTRICTED", if the pest control product is one for
  which the Minister, out of concern for its health or environmental
  risks, has set out additional information to be shown on the label
  concerning essential conditions respecting the display, distribution
  or limitations on use of, or qualifications of persons who may use,
  the product; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} "MANUFACTURING", if the pest control product is to be
  used only in the manufacture of a pest control product or a product
  regulated under the [Feeds Act](/eng/acts/F-9) or the [Fertilizers
  Act](/eng/acts/F-10).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[17.01]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17.01 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in this section and sections 17.02 to 17.17.

[[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement referred to in
    subsection 66(1) of the Act. ([entente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[compensable data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[compensable data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means test data that the
    Minister considers for the first time in support of a registration,
    confirmation of the registration or amendment of the registration,
    but does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} test data that is referred to in the definition
      [[exclusive rights]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef};

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} test data that is included in a scientific study
      that has been published; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} test data that is generated by a scientific study
      that is fully funded by a government or one of its
      institutions. ([données assujetties à des droits à
      payer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[crop group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[crop group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a group of crops in which
    the residues at harvest are similar, based on similarities in
    appearance, harvestable commodity, edible portions and growth
    habits. ([groupe de cultures]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[data holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[data holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registrant to whom
    compensation may be payable in respect of test data. ([détenteur de
    données]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[exclusive rights]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exclusive rights]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the exclusive rights
    to use or rely on any of the following test data that the Minister
    considers for the first time in support of a registration:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} test data that the applicant provides in support
      of an application to register a new active ingredient;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} test data that the applicant provides in support
      of a concurrent application to register a new pest control product
      that contains the new active ingredient referred to in paragraph
      (a); or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} test data that the applicant provides in support
      of an application to register a new compound or substance referred
      to in paragraph 2(b), if the compound or substance has never been
      an ingredient in a registered pest control product. ([droits
      exclusifs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[minor use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minor use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a pest control
    product, means a use the demand for which originates with a grower
    or a group of growers and which product is intended to be used on a
    particular pest in connection with a particular host organism, in
    all of the following circumstances:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the use is for an agricultural purpose;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the use is supported by a federal or provincial
      agricultural authority;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the use is supported by crop residue data or
      dislodgeable foliar residue data. ([usage limité]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[representative crop]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[representative crop]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a crop in a crop
    group from which extrapolations of residue levels and maximum
    residue limits may be made to one or more crops in the
    group. ([culture répresentative]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[test data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[test data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means test data that is provided
    to the Minister

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in support of an application to register a pest
      control product or to amend a registration made under section 7 of
      the Act, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the context of a re-evaluation or special
      review, in response to a notice initiating the process under
      subsection 16(3) or 18(1) of the Act or a notice requiring
      additional information under paragraph 19(1)(a) of the
      Act. ([données d'essai]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  principal display panel of a registered pest control product must show
  the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the product name of the pest control product, which

    name of its active ingredient, if established;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the product type of the pest control product, which
    must be descriptive of its purpose;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the physical form of the pest control product;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the product class designation of the pest control
    product as set out in section 5;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} information concerning the nature and degree of
    hazard inherent in the pest control product, which must identify the
    nature and degree of hazard by appropriate precautionary symbols and
    signal words selected from Schedule 3, together with a statement
    that indicates the nature of the primary hazard to which the symbol
    relates;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the statement "READ THE LABEL BEFORE USING.";

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the pest control product has the product class
    designation "DOMESTIC", the statement "KEEP OUT OF THE REACH OF
    CHILDREN.";

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a statement, as follows:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the words "ACTIVE INGREDIENT:" or "ACTIVE
      INGREDIENTS:", as the case may be,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the common chemical name of the active
      ingredient of the pest control product or, if it has no common
      chemical name, its chemical or other name,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of the active ingredient,
      expressed, if the pest control product is

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a liquid, as a percentage by mass, or as mass
        per unit volume, or both, as specified by the Minister in the
        conditions of registration under paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a dust, wettable powder or other dry
        formulation, as a percentage by mass, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} neither a liquid nor a dry formulation, in
        terms specified by the Minister in the conditions of
        registration under paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the viscosity, specific gravity, particle size
      or any other property or characteristic that the Minister may
      specify in the conditions of registration under paragraph 8(1)(a)
      of the Act;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the registration number of the pest control
    product, as follows: "REGISTRATION NO. (assigned registration
    number) PEST CONTROL PRODUCTS ACT" or "REG. NO. (assigned
    registration number) P.C.P. Act";

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a declaration of net quantity of the product in the
    package, expressed

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by volume, if the product is a liquid or gas or
      is viscous,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by mass, if the product is a solid or
      pressure-packed, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in terms specified by the Minister in the
      conditions of registration under paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act, in
      any other case;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the registrant's name; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal address and telephone number of a
    contact person in Canada to which public inquiries may be directed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Secondary display panel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The secondary display panel of a registered pest
  control product must show the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} under the heading "DIRECTIONS FOR USE", the
    directions for the use of the pest control product, including
    application rates, timing and frequency of application, and any
    limitations on its use;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information that identifies any significant risk
    associated with the handling, storage, display, distribution and
    disposal of the pest control product, and instructions on procedures
    to reduce those risks and, if specified by the Minister in the
    conditions of registration under paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act,
    instructions on decontamination procedures and disposal of the pest
    control product and its empty packages;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} under the heading "PRECAUTIONS", information that
    identifies any significant risk to health, the environment or
    anything in connection with which the pest control product is to be
    used, and instructions on procedures to reduce that risk;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} under the heading "PRECAUTIONS", the following
    statement:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the pest control product does not have the
      product class designation "DOMESTIC": "KEEP OUT OF THE REACH OF
      CHILDREN.", or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the pest control product is to be used only
      in the manufacture of another pest control product: "PREVENT
      ACCESS BY UNAUTHORIZED PERSONNEL.";

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} under the heading "FIRST AID", instructions that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} set out the practical measures to be taken in the
      event of poisoning, intoxication or injury caused by the pest
      control product, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} include the statement "Take the container label
      or product name and Pest Control Product Registration Number with
      you when seeking medical attention.";

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} under the heading "TOXICOLOGICAL INFORMATION",
    information that is essential to the treatment of persons who are
    poisoned, intoxicated or injured by the pest control product that
    includes all of the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} antidotes and remedial measures or, if no
      specific antidote or remedial measure exists, the statement "Treat
      symptomatically.",

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the symptoms of poisoning or
      intoxication, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a list of the components of the product, not
      including the active ingredient, that may affect the treatment;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the following notice to users: "NOTICE TO USER:
    This pest control product is to be used only in accordance with the
    directions on the label. It is an offence under the [Pest Control
    Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01) to use this product in a way that is
    inconsistent with the directions on the label."

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a pest control product is contained in more than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information, as it appears on the approved
    label for the product, that is required under paragraphs (1)(a),
    (e), (h), (i), (k) and (l);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information, as it appears on the approved
    label for the product, that is required under paragraph (2)(b) and

    paragraph exists while the pest control product is contained in the

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the statement "For first aid instructions or the

#### [Product Equivalency]{.HTitleText3} {#h-724196 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), the
  Minister may determine that a foreign product is equivalent to a
  registered pest control product if all of the following conditions are
  met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a grower or group of growers requests the Minister
    to make the determination;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} four not-for-profit Canadian national grower
    associations support the request;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person who makes the request provides the
    Minister with all of the information listed in subsection (4);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the foreign product meets the requirements of
    subsection (2); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the registered pest control product meets the
    requirements of subsection (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility --- foreign products

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A foreign product must meet all of the following
  requirements to be considered when making a determination of
  equivalency:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is not an organism;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is not under review in the foreign country where
    it is registered with respect to its health and environmental risks
    and is being sold in that country;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it does not contain an active ingredient that is
    under special review in Canada; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} it is manufactured by a person who is related to
    the registrant of the registered pest control product, within the
    meaning of the definition [[related persons]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
    subsection 251(2) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), whether
    the related person is located in or outside Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility --- registered pest control
  products

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A registered pest control product must meet both of
  the following requirements to be considered when making a
  determination of equivalency:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it does not have the product class designation
    "RESTRICTED" described in paragraph 5(c); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the test data referred to in paragraphs (a) and (b)
    of the definition [[exclusive rights]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
    section 17.01 that were provided in support of its registration are
    no longer subject to exclusive rights within the meaning of that
    definition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- test data

  [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (3), [[test
  data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 17.01.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preliminary steps

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Before making the determination of equivalency, the
  Minister must first find that the foreign product and the registered
  pest control product are sufficiently similar so as to justify
  proceeding with the determination, based on the following preliminary
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and registration number of the registered
    pest control product, the name and product identifier of the foreign
    product and the name of the country where the foreign product is
    registered;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the registrant of the registered pest
    control product and the name of the holder of the registration of
    the foreign product;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the labels of both products;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the information described in subparagraphs
    26(1)(h)(ii) and (iii) with respect to both products;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} their type of formulation;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} their type of package;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the health and environmental risks that were
    evaluated by the regulatory body that registered the foreign
    product; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the standard of acceptability of risk that was
    applied by that regulatory body.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further information

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister finds that the foreign product is
  sufficiently similar to the registered pest control product, the
  Minister must so inform the registrant and request that they provide
  the Minister with the following information, in respect of both the
  registered pest control product and the foreign product:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the composition of all formulations of the end-use
    products and of the technical grade active ingredients used in their
    manufacture, which, in the case of the registered pest control
    product and its active ingredient, must be the composition specified
    by the Minister in the conditions of registration under paragraph
    8(1)(a) of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the manufacturer of the active
    ingredient that is used to manufacture each of the products and the
    address of the place where each active ingredient is manufactured;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the manufacturer of the formulation of
    each end-use product and the address of the place where each
    formulation is manufactured;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the information described in subparagraph
    26(1)(h)(iv); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} product safety information relevant to their use in
    the workplace, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the information provided under subsection (5) with
  respect to the registered pest control product or the foreign product
  is insufficient to enable the Minister to make the determination, the
  Minister may request additional relevant information from the
  registrant in order to make the determination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written consent of registrant

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Instead of providing the information required by
  subsection (5), the registrant may provide their written consent to
  the Minister to use or rely on any information that they had

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of no further action

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is unable to obtain the information

### [Research Notification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-724364 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** A research establishment
is exempt from the application of subsection 6(1) of the Act and section
48 if the Minister confirms under section 54 that the proposed research
meets all of the criteria set out in section 53.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice and contents

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** A research establishment
that wishes to obtain confirmation under section 54 must notify the
Minister by providing the information specified in subsections 6(1) and
(3) in addition to both of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an electronic copy of the proposed experimental
  label; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the research plan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** The following are the
criteria referred to in section 51:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the research does not involve the use of a
  semiochemical;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of research that involves the use of a
  chemical pest control product,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it does not involve the use of an antimicrobial
    agent,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it does not involve aerial application,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} it does not involve the application of a pest
    control product to water or to a place where runoff water may remove
    residues from the research site,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} it does not involve the use of a pest control
    product in any of the following areas:

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} greenhouses,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} residential areas, including lawns, gardens and
      parks,

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} industrial premises, and

    - [(D)]{.lawlabel} food-handling areas,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} it does not involve the use of a pest control
    product for either

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} structural pest control, or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} fumigation,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} it does not involve the use of a pest control
    product that contains a formulant that is on Part I of the List of
    Pest Control Product Formulants and Contaminants of Health or
    Environmental Concern or a contaminant that is on Part 3 of that
    List,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a pest control product that
    contains an unregistered active ingredient, the application of the
    product is carried out only by a researcher

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} on 5 to 50 ha of land owned or operated by a
      research establishment, or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} on 1 to 5 ha of land owned or operated by a
      cooperator, and

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a pest control product that
    contains a registered active ingredient,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the application of the pest control product is
      carried out by a researcher or cooperator on 10 to 50 ha of land
      owned or operated by a research establishment or a cooperator, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} there is a reasonable expectation that the
      research will not increase occupational exposure above the level
      that is expected when the pest control product is used in
      accordance with its conditions of registration; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of research that involves the use of a
  microbial agent,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it does not involve aerial application,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it does not involve the use of a pest control
    product that contains a formulant that is on Part 1 of the List of
    Pest Control Product Formulants and Contaminants of Health or
    Environmental Concern or a contaminant that is on Part 3 of that
    List,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the micro-organism is indigenous to the area
    where it is intended to be used, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the application of the pest control product is
    carried out on a maximum of 10 ha of land owned or operated by a
    research establishment or, in the case of an aquatic application, on
    a body of water that has a maximum of 1 ha of surface area and that
    is wholly contained on land owned or operated by a research
    establishment.

  that is used in research is accompanied by an experimental label.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An experimental label must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} show all of the information specified in section
    26, except paragraphs 26(1)(d), (g), (i) and (k) and (2)(d);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} show

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the following information on the principal
      display panel:

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the statement "EXPERIMENTAL USE ONLY",

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the research authorization certificate number
        or the research notification certificate number, when there is
        one, as follows:

        - [(I)]{.lawlabel} "RESEARCH AUTHORIZATION/NOTIFICATION NO.
          (assigned number) PEST CONTROL PRODUCTS ACT", or

        - [(II)]{.lawlabel} "RESEARCH AUTHORIZATION/NOTIFICATION NO.
          (assigned number) P.C.P. ACT",

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the statement "SALE PROHIBITED. NOT FOR
        DISTRIBUTION TO ANY PERSON OTHER THAN A RESEARCHER OR
        COOPERATOR.",

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} the statement "KEEP OUT OF THE REACH OF
        CHILDREN",

      - [(E)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer's name and address, and

      - [(F)]{.lawlabel} when the research involves the use of a pest
        control product that contains a formulant that is on Part 2 of
        the List of Pest Control Product Formulants and Contaminants of
        Health or Environmental Concern, the statement "Warning:
        contains the allergen (name of allergen)",

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on the secondary display panel, when the
      research involves the use of an unregistered pest control product,
      in the instructions on decontamination procedures and disposal
      referred to in paragraph 26(2)(b), the statement "Any unused
      product must be returned to the manufacturer.", and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} on the secondary display panel, despite
      paragraph (a), the statement set out in subparagraph 26(2)(e)(ii)
      amended to read as follows: "Take the experimental label with you
      when seeking medical attention."; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} represent the intended research as described in the
    research plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (2)(a), when the research involves
  the use of a registered pest control product, the following
  information need not be shown on the experimental label if that label
  is used in conjunction with the approved label:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the net quantity referred to in paragraph 26(1)(j);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} instructions on decontamination procedures and
    disposal referred to in paragraph 26(2)(b);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} information that identifies any significant risk to
    the environment, and instructions on procedures to reduce those
    risks, referred to in paragraph 26(2)(c); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the notice to users referred to in paragraph
    26(2)(g).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** A research establishment
must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} supply to every researcher and cooperator involved in
  the research a copy of the experimental label, which must be the
  approved experimental label if a research authorization certificate or
  research notification certificate has been issued; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} produce a copy of the experimental label to the
  Minister on request.

### [Unused Pest Control Products]{.HTitleText2} {#h-724516 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unregistered products

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** A research establishment

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registered products

### [Distribution]{.HTitleText2} {#h-724521 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Researchers and cooperators only

**[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** A research establishment
may not distribute a pest control product that is used in the conduct of
research to any person that is not a researcher or cooperator involved
in the research, except in accordance with section 65 or 66.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Treated food and feed crops

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** When a research
authorization certificate is issued, treated food and feed crops from
research sites and meat (including meat by-products and fat), milk and
eggs that may contain residues as a result of the research must not be
sold unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} written authorization to do so is set out on the
  certificate; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of research that involves the use of a
  chemical pest control product, residues do not exceed an amount that
  would result in the sale of the food being prohibited under section 4
  of the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Research --- section 53 criteria

**[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** In the case of research
that meets the criteria set out in section 53 and for which a research
notification certificate is issued,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of research that involves the use of a
  chemical pest control product, treated food and feed crops from
  research sites and meat (including meat by-products and fat), milk and
  eggs that may contain residues as a result of research must not be
  sold unless the residues do not exceed an amount that would result in
  the sale of the food being prohibited under section 4 of the [Food and
  Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of research that involves the use of a
  microbial agent, other than one that contains [Bacillus
  thuringiensis]{.otherLang lang="la"} registered for use on
  agricultural crops, treated food and feed crops from research sites
  and meat (including meat by-products and fat), milk and eggs that may
  contain residues as a result of research must not be sold.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- SOR/2010-119, s. 3

  - - [3]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations apply to an
      application to register a pest control product or to amend a
      registration made on or after August 1, 2007 and before the day on
      which these Regulations come into force, if the applicant wishes
      to use or rely on compensable data of a registrant and the
      Minister has requested in writing that the parties negotiate the
      amount of compensation payable for that use or reliance.

    - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), if the Minister's request
      is made more than 60 days before the coming into force of these
      Regulations and the applicant sends a copy of an agreement to the
      registrant under subsection 17.8(2) of the [Pest Control Products
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1253), as enacted by
      section 2, the 120-day period specified in subsection 17.9(2) of
      those Regulations, as enacted by section 2, is reduced to 60 days
      from the day after the agreement is sent.

- --- SOR/2010-119, s. 4

  - [4]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph 17.5(3)(b) of the [Pest Control
    Products Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1253), as enacted
    by section 2, the Minister must extend the exclusive use period
    established in accordance with section 17.5 of those Regulations, as
    enacted by section 2, if all of the following conditions are met:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there remain at least six months before the
      exclusive use period expires;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} minor uses are added to the registration on or
      after August 1, 2007 but before the day on which these Regulations
      come into force; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the registrant requests an extension of the
      exclusive use period within 30 days after the day on which these
      Regulations are published in the [Canada
      Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part II.

- --- SOR/2017-91, s. 11

  - - [11]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[former
      Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Pest Control Products
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1253) as they read
      immediately before the day on which these Regulations come into
      force.

    - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The validity period of a conditional registration
      that is in effect before the coming into force of these
      Regulations continues to be in effect until the end of that
      period.

    - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The validity period of a conditional registration
      that continues to be in effect after the coming into force of
      these Regulations may be extended under subsection 14(6) or (7) of
      the former Regulations.

- --- SOR/2017-91, s. 12

  - [12]{.lawlabel} The requirements in these Regulations respecting the
    information that must be shown on the label of a pest control
    product do not apply until the day on which the first of the
    following occurs:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the registration of the pest control product, if
      it occurs on or after the day on which these Regulations come into
      force,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reprinting of the label,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the modification of the information on the label,
      or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the manufacture of the pest control product, if
      it occurs on or after the tenth anniversary of the day on which
      these Regulations come into force.

- --- SOR/2023-104, s. 12

  - [12]{.lawlabel} In the context of a re-evaluation or special review
    of a pest control product, the former Regulations apply in respect

    registrant under subsection 7(1) of the Act to register another pest
    control product or to amend its registration if the following
    conditions are met:

      control product that is the subject of the re-evaluation or
      special review is not equivalent to the active ingredient
      contained in the other pest control product;

      or after June 28, 2006;

      2010; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has made public a decision statement
      in respect of the re-evaluation or special review under subsection
      28(5) of the Act on or after September 21, 2017 but before the
      commencement date.
