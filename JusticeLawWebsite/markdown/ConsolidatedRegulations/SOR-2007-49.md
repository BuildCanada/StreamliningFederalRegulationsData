## FIFA Men\'s U-20 World Cup Canada 2007 Remission Order {#fifa-mens-u-20-world-cup-canada-2007-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-740988 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[CSA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CSA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Soccer Association
    Incorporated. ([ACS]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[FIFA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[FIFA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Fédération Internationale de
    Football Association. ([FIFA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[foreign corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foreign corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means FIFA or any
    corporation the head office of which is located outside Canada, that
    does not have a branch office or a subsidiary corporation located in
    Canada and that, in respect of the U-20 World Cup, is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a media rights holder;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a sponsor; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a supplier. ([société étrangère]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[media rights holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[media rights holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation that
    has been granted broadcasting rights in respect of the U-20 World
    Cup by the CSA or FIFA. ([titulaire de droits de
    diffusion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sponsor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sponsor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any official sponsor of the
    U-20 World Cup that is designated as such by FIFA.
    ([commanditaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[supplier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[supplier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any official supplier to the
    U-20 World Cup that is designated as such by the CSA or FIFA.
    ([fournisseur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[U-20 World Cup]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[U-20 World Cup]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the FIFA Men\'s U-20
    World Cup Canada 2007, to be held in Canada, during the period
    beginning on June 30, 2007 and ending on July 22, 2007. ([Coupe du
    monde U-20]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[U-20 World Cup family member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[U-20 World Cup family member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    individual, other than an individual ordinarily resident in Canada,
    who is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an athlete participating in the U-20 World Cup or
      a coach, a team official, a support staff member or a technical
      official in the U-20 World Cup; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel}  a member of FIFA and the holder of an
      accreditation issued by the CSA and approved by FIFA. ([membre de
      la famille de la Coupe du monde U-20]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741005 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order does not apply
to alcoholic beverages or tobacco products.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741007 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the customs duties, the excise taxes and the goods and services tax
paid or payable on goods imported temporarily into Canada by a U-20
World Cup family member if the goods are for the exclusive use of that
member in connection with the U-20 World Cup.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission is hereby granted of the
  customs duties and a portion of the goods and services tax paid or
  payable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on goods for display, including apparatus and
    equipment to display those goods, imported temporarily into Canada
    by the CSA or a foreign corporation, or their agent or
    representative, for use exclusively in connection with the U-20
    World Cup; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on equipment, imported temporarily into Canada by
    the CSA or a foreign corporation, or their agent or representative,
    for use exclusively in connection with the U-20 World Cup.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The portion of the goods and services tax remitted
  under subsection (1) is equal to the difference between

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the goods and services tax paid or
    payable on the value of the goods; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the goods and services tax payable on
    1/60 of the value of the goods for each month or portion of a month
    that the goods remain in Canada.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the customs duties paid or payable on goods imported into Canada by a
media rights holder, a sponsor, a supplier, FIFA or the agent or
representative of any of them, if the goods have a unit value of \$60 or
less and are for free distribution at the U-20 World Cup.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the customs duties, the excise taxes and the goods and services tax
paid or payable on goods that are imported into Canada by a U-20 World
Cup family member if the goods have a unit value of \$60 or less and are
for distribution as gifts or awards to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} another U-20 World Cup family member;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an agent or representative of the CSA;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a resident of Canada participating in the U-20 World
  Cup; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a resident of Canada acting in an official capacity
  in connection with the U-20 World Cup.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the customs duties paid or payable on athletic equipment imported
into Canada by the CSA if the athletic equipment is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} certified by FIFA as complying with the international
  competition standards applicable to soccer and as being required by an
  athlete exclusively for the purpose of training or competing in the
  U-20 World Cup;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the conclusion of the U-20 World Cup, donated to a
  not-for-profit sport club, a sport federation, a registered charity,
  an educational or other public institution or a municipality, town or
  city; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} not sold or otherwise disposed of within two years
  after importation.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}**  Remission is hereby
granted of the customs duties paid or payable on clothing imported into
Canada where the clothing is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} donated to the CSA by a sponsor or supplier;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provided free of charge to CSA volunteers to be worn
  as uniforms when undertaking their official volunteer duties in
  connection with the U-20 World Cup; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} kept by the individual volunteer recipients following
  the U-20 World Cup.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741030 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted under
sections 3 and 4 on the condition that the goods, on or before October
1, 2007, are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} exported from Canada; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} destroyed in Canada under the supervision of an
  officer of the Canada Border Services Agency at the expense of the
  importer.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted
under this Order on the condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods are imported into Canada during the period
  beginning on February 1, 2007 and ending on July 22, 2007;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years after the
  day on which the goods were accounted for under section 32 of the
  [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the importer provides the Canada Border Services
  Agency with any evidence or information that demonstrates that the
  importer is entitled to remission under this Order.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741038 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into
force on the day on which it is registered.
