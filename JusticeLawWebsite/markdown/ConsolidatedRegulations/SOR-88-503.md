Expansion Vote L20 of Appropriation Act No. 2, 1988-89[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*^](#fn_3e){#fn_3e-ID0EDDAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations respecting the provision of loan insurance
in Northern Ontario](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-503).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913660 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Northern Ontario Loan Insurance
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-503).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913662 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual,
      partnership, cooperative or body corporate, or any trustee or
      legal representative thereof, who makes an application pursuant to
      subsection 3(1), but does not include a government, municipality
      or agency thereof or a legal entity owned or controlled by a
      government, municipality or agency or any subsidiary thereof, or
      any combination thereof; ([requérant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[capital costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[capital costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means capital costs that are
      determined in accordance with generally accepted accounting
      principles to be necessary to carry out a project, other than

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} goodwill,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} motor vehicles not used exclusively on a
        project site or between two or more project sites,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} aircraft, and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the portion of the cost of any asset which
        exceeds fair market value; ([coûts en
        immobilisations]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated region]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated region]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that area of
      Ontario north of and including the census divisions of Parry Sound
      and Nipissing as shown in the Statistics Canada publication 1981
      Census of Canada, Reference Maps, Census divisions and
      subdivisions; ([région désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[disbursement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[disbursement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any amount paid by a
      lender to an applicant under a loan
      agreement; ([versement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[eligible sector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[eligible sector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a sector set out in
      the schedule; ([secteur admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[formal demand]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[formal demand]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a demand in writing
      made by a lender to an applicant for the repayment of the
      outstanding amount under a loan agreement; ([demande
      officielle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bank, credit union, *caisse
      populaire* or any other cooperative society, a trust company, a
      loan company, an insurance company or a pension fund, or a
      non-bank affiliate of a foreign bank as defined in section 303 of
      the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) but does not include a
      government, municipality or agency thereof or an institution that
      is controlled by the Government of Canada, the government of a
      province, a municipality or by any agency thereof, or any
      combination thereof; ([prêteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any agreement entered
      into by a lender and an applicant whereby the lender agrees to
      grant to the applicant a loan to finance the applicant's project
      and for which a loan insurance agreement is made; ([convention de
      prêt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[loan insurance agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[loan insurance agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
      agreement entered into by the Minister and a lender whereby the
      Minister agrees to provide loan insurance to the lender in respect
      of one or more loans granted under one or more loan
      agreements; ([convention d'assurance-prêt]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Industry,
      Science and Technology; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the establishment, expansion, modernization or
        renovation of a commercial operation located in the designated
        region in eligible sector 11, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the establishment, expansion or modernization
        of a commercial operation located in the designated region in
        any other eligible sector. ([projet]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the definition
  [[project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection (1), the establishment
  of a commercial operation includes the purchase of the assets of a
  facility if, at the time an applicant makes an application under these
  Regulations,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the operation of the facility has ceased or is
    about to cease;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cessation or imminent cessation of the
    operation of the facility is due to circumstances beyond the control
    of its owner; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the purchase of the assets of the facility is an
    arm's length transaction by the applicant in good faith who is
    unrelated to the present owner or the immediate predecessor of that
    owner, and the purchase has not been undertaken for the sole purpose
    of an application under these Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-337, s. 1

## [Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913688 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant requiring that the Minister enter into a loan insurance
  agreement shall apply in writing to the Minister on or before March
  31, 1992 and shall provide a detailed description of the project and
  any other information that is necessary to effectively evaluate the
  application.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may enter into a loan insurance
  agreement where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the loan agreement is for an amount of not more
    than \$15,000,000 and not less than \$100,000 and the term of the
    loan does not exceed 15 years;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total amount of the loan is to be used to
    finance the applicant's project;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 4, the loan is to be used to
    finance only capital costs;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is unable to obtain a loan on
    commercially acceptable terms, without a loan insurance agreement;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the project would not likely be carried out within
    the proposed time or scope or in the proposed location, as described
    in an application made pursuant to subsection (1), without a loan
    insurance agreement;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an application made pursuant to subsection (1)
    includes [pro forma]{.otherLang lang="la"} financial statements for
    each year in which loan insurance is required by the lender;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the project and the applicant's commercial
    operations, as the case may be, are or are expected to be
    commercially viable, subject to reasonable commercial risk;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} an application made pursuant to subsection (1)
    indicates it is likely that the applicant will have equity
    representing a minimum of 20 per cent of the capital costs for the
    project;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the lender acquires an enforceable security for the
    purpose of securing the repayment of the loan under the loan
    agreement in a form consistent with standard banking practices;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the project would constitute a significant
    contribution to the economic benefit of the designated region and of
    Canada; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} all other requirements of these Regulations are
    met.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A loan insurance agreement
may be entered into in respect of a loan for working capital if, without
loan insurance, the project would not be viable.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 6, a
loan insurance agreement may be entered into in respect of a project in
eligible sector 11 if the project is of a quality and scope likely to
attract tourists from outside the designated region.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A loan insurance agreement
may be entered into in respect of a project in eligible sector 11 that
is a tourism facility designed to serve food or beverages if that
facility

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} constitutes part of or supports another tourism
  facility; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is of a sufficiently thematic or original nature as
  to constitute a tourism attraction in its own right.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-337, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No loan insurance agreement
shall be entered into in respect of a loan granted for a merger,
refinancing or an acquisition, except in respect of a project referred
to in subsection 2(2).

## [Coverage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913711 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A loan insurance agreement
shall insure 85 per cent or less of the amount of a loan disbursed
pursuant to a loan agreement.

## [Insurance Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913713 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  loan insurance agreement is entered into, an insurance fee is payable
  in advance by the lender to the Minister in accordance with the
  following formulae:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fee payable at the time of the first disbursement:

    ::: {#913717 .Paragraph}
    A × B × 0.01
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fee payable at the time of each subsequent
    disbursement:

    ::: {#913720 .Paragraph}
    A × B × C ÷ 365 × 0.01
    :::

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fee payable on each anniversary of the date of the
    first disbursement until the earlier of the date of formal demand
    and the date the loan is repaid:

    ::: {#913723 .Paragraph}
    A × D × 0.01

    where

    ["A"]{.dfn}
    :   is the percentage of the loan that is insured under the loan
        insurance agreement,

    ["B"]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount of the disbursement,

    ["C"]{.dfn}
    :   is the number of days remaining between the date of the
        disbursement and the next anniversary date of the first
        disbursement, and

    ["D"]{.dfn}
    :   is the outstanding amount of the loan under the loan agreement
        on the anniversary date.
    :::

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An insurance fee paid by the lender under
  subsection (1) is not refundable.

## [Payment of a Claim]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913730 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall not
pay a claim made by a lender under a loan insurance agreement unless the
lender

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} makes a formal demand; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} submits evidence to the Minister that establishes
  that the lender has

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} taken all reasonable steps to protect the lender's
    rights and realize on the security provided in respect of the loan,
    and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} suffered a loss as referred to in section 11.

<!-- -->

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  maximum amount payable to a lender pursuant to a loan insurance
  agreement shall be the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount representing the percentage of
    insurance, as stipulated in the loan insurance agreement, that is
    applied to the loss suffered; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the insured amount at the time of the formal
    demand.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1),
  [[loss]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the principal amount of the outstanding loan and
    the amount of unpaid interest on the loan at the date of the formal
    demand,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reasonable legal fees, costs and other expenses
    paid by the lender for the purpose of collecting any amount referred
    to in paragraph (a),

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} costs incurred and paid by the lender to protect
    any security provided in respect of the loan disbursed under the
    loan agreement, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} interest on the amounts referred to in paragraphs
    (a), (b) and (c), to be calculated from the date of the formal
    demand until the date of payment of the lender's claim by the
    Minister

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at the rate charged by the lender under the loan
      agreement at the date of the formal demand

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} for a period of up to 180 days after the date
        of the formal demand, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} for an additional period not exceeding 180 days
        where the Minister and the lender agree to realize on the
        security during that additional period, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at one-half the rate referred to in
      subparagraph (i) for any period after the relevant periods
      referred to in that subparagraph,

  less

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any amount

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} realized on the security provided in respect of
      the loan, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} recovered by the lender after the date of the
      formal demand in excess of the aggregate amount of all other loans
      granted by the lender to the applicant.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-337, s. 3
:::

## [Stock Options and Capital Shares]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913755 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, on behalf of Her Majesty in right of Canada, acquire a
  stock option as a condition of entering into a loan insurance
  agreement if the total amount of the loan provided for by the loan
  agreement is \$500,000 or more and the expected rate of return
  associated with the project justifies the acquisition of the stock
  option.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, pursuant to subsection (1), the Minister
  acquires a stock option, the Minister may exercise, assign or sell the
  stock option where the exercise, assignment or sale is of benefit to
  Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister on behalf of Her Majesty in right
  of Canada acquires capital stock under a stock option, the Minister
  shall, within two years after the day on which the capital stock is
  acquired, sell the capital stock to benefit Her Majesty in right of
  Canada.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister receives an offer to purchase a
  stock option or capital stock acquired under a stock option and the
  acceptance of the offer would be of benefit to Her Majesty in right of
  Canada, the Minister shall in writing notify the applicant of the
  offer.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the applicant intends to purchase the stock
  option or the capital stock at the price and on the terms and
  conditions specified in the offer referred to in subsection (4), the
  applicant shall so advise the Minister within seven business days
  following the day on which the applicant is notified of the offer.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where an applicant advises the Minister pursuant to
  subsection (5), the Minister shall sell the stock option or capital
  stock to the applicant.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-337, s. 4
- SOR/92-453, s. 1
