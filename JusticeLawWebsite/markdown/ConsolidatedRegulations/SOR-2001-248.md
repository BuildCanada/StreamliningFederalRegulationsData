## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-670314 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Standing Orders.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
    Act](/eng/acts/R-10). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[coordinator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[coordinator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person designated as
    such by the Commissioner. ([coordonnateur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[decision maker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[decision maker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Commissioner,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for review based on a
        decision, act or omission made in the course of the
        classification of a position at the Executive level, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for review when the
        corrective relief requested is reclassification of a position to
        the Executive level;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person designated as such by the
      Commissioner,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for review based on a
        decision, act or omission made in the course of the
        classification of a position at the Inspector or Superintendent
        rank, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for review when the
        corrective relief requested is reclassification of a position to
        the Inspector or Superintendent rank; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the coordinator, in the case of a request for
      review based on a decision, act or omission made in the course of
      the classification of a position at any level or rank other than
      the Executive level or the Inspector or Superintendent rank.
      ([décideur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-670326 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Standing Orders apply instead of Part III of the Act to the
  presentation and resolution of all grievances based on a decision, act
  or omission made in the course of the classification of a position,
  which decision, act or omission is in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the group to which the position has been allocated;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the rating, level or rank attributed to the
    position; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the effective date of the classification of the
    position.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Standing Orders apply only to the resolution of
  grievances initiated in accordance with these Standing Orders on or
  after the day on which these Standing Orders come into force.

## [Process for Review]{.HTitleText1} {#h-670333 .Part}

### [Request for Review]{.HTitleText2} {#h-670334 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A member may request the
review of a decision, act or omission made in the course of the
classification of the position to which the member is posted, by
submitting a request for classification review to the coordinator within
45 days after the day on which the member knew or reasonably ought to
have known of the decision, act or omission.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The request for review
shall be in writing and shall specify each of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the member and the title of the position
  to which the member is posted;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the decision, act or omission in question;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the corrective relief requested; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the member knew of the decision,
  act or omission.

### [Classification Review Committee]{.HTitleText2} {#h-670341 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As soon
  as possible after receiving a request for review, the coordinator
  shall appoint a chairperson of the classification review committee.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The chairperson of the committee shall be a person
  designated by the Commissioner as an Accredited Classification
  Analyst/Consultant.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The chairperson shall select two other persons to
  serve on the committee who are trained in the use of the
  classification standard for the position to which the request for
  review pertains.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall serve on a committee if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they took part in the decision, act or omission
    that is the subject of the request for review; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they will be placed in any other conflict of
    interest by being on the committee.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The chairperson shall
notify the member who requested the review of the names and titles of
the persons selected to serve on the classification review committee.

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  member who requested the review may, within seven days after the day
  on which the member is notified of the names of the persons selected
  to serve on the classification review committee, object to the
  selection of any person on the basis that the selection contravenes
  subsection 5(4), by submitting a written notice of objection to the
  coordinator.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of objection shall contain details of the
  alleged conflict of interest under subsection 5(4).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the coordinator finds that the objection is
  well-founded, the coordinator shall exclude the person from the
  committee and select a replacement.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The decision of the coordinator is final.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Each classification review
committee shall review the request for review of which it is seized.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** All submissions and reports
to the classification review committee shall be in writing.

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  classification review committee may obtain any additional
  documentation relevant to a request for review.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The coordinator shall provide the member who
  requested the review an opportunity to review any additional
  documentation obtained by the committee in respect of the request.

### [Report of the Classification Review Committee]{.HTitleText2} {#h-670360 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  classification review committee shall prepare a written report on its
  review and shall submit the report to the decision maker.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report shall contain the findings and
  recommendations, with reasons, of the persons on the committee,
  including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the group to which the position should be
    allocated, when the request for review is based on a decision, act
    or omission in respect of the group to which a position has been
    allocated;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the rating, level or rank that should be attributed
    to the position, when the request for review is based on a decision,
    act or omission in respect of the rating, level or rank attributed
    to a position; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the effective date that should be attributed to the
    classification, when the request for review is based on a decision,
    act or omission in respect of the effective date of the
    classification of a position.

### [Decision]{.HTitleText2} {#h-670367 .Subheading}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The decision maker shall
consider the request for review and the report of the classification
review committee and shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine the group to which the position should be
  allocated, when the request is based on a decision, act or omission in
  respect of the group to which a position has been allocated;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine the rating, level or rank of the position,
  when the request is based on a decision, act or omission in respect of
  the rating, level or rank attributed to a position; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} determine the effective date of the classification of
  the position, when the request is based on a decision, act or omission
  in respect of the effective date of the classification of a position.

<!-- -->

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  decision of the decision maker shall be in writing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The coordinator shall provide a copy of the decision
  of the decision maker and the report of the classification review
  committee, including reasons, to the member who requested the review.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The decision of the
decision maker is final.

## [Withdrawal of Request for Review]{.HTitleText1} {#h-670376 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A member may withdraw
their request for review, by notice in writing to the coordinator, at
any time before the decision relating to their request is rendered by
the decision maker.

## [Suspension of Time Limits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-670378 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  coordinator shall, on written request, in the situations described in
  subsection (2), suspend the time limit for compliance with section 3
  or subsection 7(1).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following situations justify a suspension of a
  time limit:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when the member is on sick leave;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the member is assigned to an operation that
    prevents the member from meeting the time requirement; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} when an informal review is ongoing in respect of
    the decision, act or omission that is the subject of the request for
    review.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-670385 .Part}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** These Standing Orders
come into force on the day on which they are registered.
