Registration 2003-06-05

Regulations Respecting the Fees for the Examination of Instruments and
the Provision of Tables

P.C. 2003-861 2003-06-05

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 304(1)(j) of the
[Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Regulations Respecting the Fees for the Examination of
Instruments and the Provision of Tables](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-207).

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702780 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The fee to be paid for the
examination of an instrument under subsection 148(2) of the [Excise Act,
2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1) is \$25.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The fee for each copy of
the tables set out in column 1 of the chart to this section is set out
in column 2 of the chart. No fee is payable for copies of tables
provided to an alcohol licensee or licensed user at the time their
licence is issued.
