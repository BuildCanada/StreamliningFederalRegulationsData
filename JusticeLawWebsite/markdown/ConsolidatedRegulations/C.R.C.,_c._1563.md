## Airport Personal Property Disposal Regulations {#airport-personal-property-disposal-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1563
:::

[DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORT ACT](/eng/acts/T-18)

Regulations Respecting the Disposal of Personal Property Left at
Airports
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-529005 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Airport Personal Property Disposal
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1563).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-529007 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[abandoned vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[abandoned vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vehicle, other
    than a derelict vehicle, that has been abandoned at an airport or
    otherwise remains unclaimed at an airport for a period of not less
    than 30 days; ([véhicule abandonné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an airport or aerodrome under
    the administration and control of the Minister of Transport;
    ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Airport Manager]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Airport Manager]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of
    Transport official in charge of the airport or his duly authorized
    representative; ([directeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of
    Transport; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[derelict vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[derelict vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vehicle, other than
    an abandoned vehicle, that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has been abandoned at an airport or otherwise
      remains unclaimed at an airport for a period of not less than 14
      days, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a market value less than \$200;
      ([épave]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to a motor vehicle,
    means a person who holds legal title to it or a person in whose name
    it is registered or is required to be registered by the laws of a
    province and includes a conditional purchaser, lessee or mortgagor
    who is entitled to or is in possession of the motor vehicle;
    ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[personal property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[personal property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all property, other
    than a vehicle, not owned by Her Majesty; ([biens
    personnels]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Regional Administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Regional Administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Regional
    Administrator, Canadian Air Transportation Administration, who has
    jurisdiction over the airport in question; ([administrateur
    régional]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a self-powered device in, on
    or by which a person or property is or may be transported or drawn
    upon a road, except a device used exclusively on stationary rails or
    tracks. ([véhicule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Personal Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-529020 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** All personal property that
has been lost or abandoned at an airport or that otherwise remains
unclaimed at an airport shall, subject to reclamation by the owner
thereof, be retained at the airport in the custody of the Airport
Manager for a period of not less than 30 days and at the end of such
period the Regional Administrator may dispose of that personal property,
at his discretion, by one or more of the following methods:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by return to the finder, if the finder is not an
  employee of the Department;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by private sale or by sale at public auction;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} by disposition, by gift or otherwise, to a charitable
  institution in Canada; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} by destruction where no other method of disposal is
  deemed appropriate.

## [Abandoned Vehicles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-529026 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  abandoned vehicle shall, subject to reclamation by the owner, be
  retained at the airport in the custody of the Airport Manager for a
  period of not less than 90 days.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Airport Manager shall, within the period referred
  to in subsection (1), send a notice by registered mail to the owner's
  latest known address or place of business advising that, if the
  vehicle is not claimed within 30 days of the date of such notice, the
  Regional Administrator may dispose of the vehicle by sale at public
  auction.

## [Derelict Vehicles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-529030 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  derelict vehicle shall, subject to reclamation by the owner, be
  retained at the airport in the custody of the Airport Manager for a
  period of not less than 30 days.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Airport Manager shall, within the period referred
  to in subsection (1), send a notice by registered mail to the owner's
  latest known address or place of business advising that, if the
  vehicle is not claimed within 20 days of the date of such notice, the
  Regional Administrator may dispose of the vehicle, at his discretion,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by private sale;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by sale at public auction; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by destruction.

## [Disposal of Vehicles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-529037 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where a notice has been
sent pursuant to subsection 4(2) or 5(2) and the vehicle is not claimed
within the time specified in the notice, the Regional Administrator may
dispose of the vehicle by a method specified in the notice.

## [Proceeds of Sale]{.HTitleText1} {#h-529039 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** All proceeds realized from
any sale pursuant to section 3, 4 or 5 shall be paid to the Receiver
General.
