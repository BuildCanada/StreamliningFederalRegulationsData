## Marine Spark-Ignition Engine, Vessel and Off-road Recreational Vehicle Emission Regulations {#marine-spark-ignition-engine-vessel-and-off-road-recreational-vehicle-emission-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2011-10
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 2011-02-04

Marine Spark-Ignition Engine, Vessel and Off-road Recreational Vehicle
Emission Regulations

P.C. 2011-45 2011-02-03
::::

::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 332(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EICBA} of the [Canadian
Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EFCBA}, the Minister of the
Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on December 30, 2006, a
copy of the proposed Marine Spark-Ignition Engine and Off-Road
Recreational Vehicle Emission Regulations, as they were then entitled,
and persons were given an opportunity to file comments with respect to
the proposed Regulations or a notice of objection requesting that a
board of review be established and stating the reasons for the
objection;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EICBA){#fn_a}S.C.
  2004, c. 15, s. 31
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b-ID0EFCBA){#fn_b}S.C.
  1999, c. 33
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to sections
160, 162 and 319 of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EDAAA}, hereby makes the annexed
Marine Spark-Ignition Engine, Vessel and Off-road Recreational Vehicle
Emission Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Environmental

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-770819 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of these
Regulations is to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} reduce emissions of hydrocarbons, oxides of nitrogen
  and carbon monoxide from engines, vessels and vehicles by establishing
  emission limits for those substances or combinations of those
  substances;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} reduce emissions of the toxic substances
  formaldehyde, 1,3-butadiene, acetaldehyde, acrolein and benzene by
  establishing emission limits for hydrocarbons from engines, vessels
  and vehicles; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} establish emission standards and test procedures for
  engines, vessels and vehicles that are aligned with those of the EPA.

## [Background]{.HTitleText1} {#h-770825 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Background

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations set out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribed classes of engines and vehicles for the
  purposes of section 149 of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} requirements respecting the conformity of engines,
  vessels and vehicles with emission-related standards for the purposes
  of sections 153 and 154 of the Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a system of credits for the purposes of section 162
  of the Act; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} other requirements for carrying out the purposes of
  Division 5 of Part 7 of the Act.

## [Model Year]{.HTitleText1} {#h-770832 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Model year

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A year
  that is used by a manufacturer as a model year is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the period of production of a model of engine,
    vessel or vehicle does not include January 1 of a calendar year, the
    calendar year during which the period of production falls or the
    calendar year immediately following the calendar year during which
    the period of production falls, at the manufacturer's choice; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the period of production of a model of engine,
    vessel or vehicle includes January 1 of a calendar year, that
    calendar year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The period of production may include only one January
  1.

## [Classes of Engines and Vehicles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-770840 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed engines

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Outboards, inboard engines and
  personal watercraft engines that operate under characteristics
  significantly similar to the theoretical Otto combustion cycle and use
  a spark plug or other sparking device are classes of engines that are
  prescribed for the purposes of the definition
  [[engine]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 149 of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed vehicles --- vessels

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Vessels --- other than amphibious vehicles --- that
  are designed to be propelled by a prescribed engine and in which a
  fuel line or fuel tank is installed are prescribed for the purposes of
  the definition [[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 149 of the
  Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed vehicles --- off-road
  recreational vehicles

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In addition, the following classes of off-road
  recreational vehicles are prescribed for the purposes of the
  definition [[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, in section 149 of the
  Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} snowmobiles;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} off-road motorcycles;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all-terrain vehicles; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} utility vehicles.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The classes of engines and vehicles referred to in
  subsections (1) to (3) do not include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an engine that is designed exclusively for
    competition, namely one that has the following characteristics, and
    bears a label that meets the requirements of subsections 7(3)
    and (4) and indicates that the engine is a competition engine:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has performance characteristics that are
      substantially superior to non-competitive engines, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is not displayed for sale in any public
      dealership or otherwise offered for sale to the general public;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vessel in which an engine referred to in
    paragraph (a) is installed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an off-road motorcycle that is designed exclusively
    for competition, namely one that meets at least four of the
    following characteristics, and bears either the label referred to in
    paragraph (a) of the definition [[competition
    vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) of the [Motor
    Vehicle Safety Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1038) or a
    label that meets the requirements of subsections 7(3) and (4) and
    indicates that it is a competition off-road motorcycle:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it has no headlight or other lights,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it has no spark arrestor,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} it has no manufacturer's warranty,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} it has suspension travel that is greater than
      25.4 cm,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} it has an engine displacement that is greater
      than 50 cm^3^, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} it has a seat surface that is less than
      195 cm^2^;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a snowmobile or all-terrain vehicle that is
    designed exclusively for competition, namely one that has
    performance characteristics that are substantially superior to a
    non-competitive model and is not covered by a manufacturer's
    warranty, and either bears the label referred to in paragraph (a) of
    the definition [[competition vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Motor Vehicle Safety
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1038) or bears a label that
    meets the requirements of subsections 7(3) and (4) and indicates
    that it is a competition snowmobile or all-terrain vehicle;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an engine or vehicle that is regulated by the
    On-Road Vehicle and Engine Emission Regulations;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a vehicle or vessel that is propelled by power
    generated solely by one or more electric motors;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a vehicle that has one or more compression-ignition
    engines for its propulsion, if the engines in question meet the
    requirements of the Off-road Compression-Ignition (Mobile and
    Stationary) and Large Spark-Ignition Engine Emission Regulations;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a vehicle, or a vessel, that is designed
    exclusively for use in combat or combat support during military
    activities, including reconnaissance missions, rescue missions and
    training missions, and the engine of the vessel;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an engine, vessel or vehicle that is being exported
    and is accompanied by written evidence that establishes that it will
    not be sold or used in Canada;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} an engine, of a vessel, that uses natural gas as
    fuel and is rated at 250 kW or more; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a vehicle that has a dry weight of less than 20 kg.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Section 152 of Act

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of section 152 of the Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the engines are those referred to in subsection (1)
    that are manufactured in Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vehicles are the vessels referred to in
    subsection (2), and the vehicles referred to in subsection (3),
    whose main assembly is completed in Canada.

  However, none of those engines, vessels or vehicles that is to be used
  in Canada solely for purposes of exhibition, demonstration, evaluation
  or testing is prescribed for the purpose of that section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Section 154 of Act

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the classes of engines and
  vehicles that are prescribed for the purposes of section 154 of the
  Act are those that are prescribed under subsections (1) to (3).

## [National Emissions Marks and Labels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-770880 .Part}

### [Authorization]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770881 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for authorization

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  company that intends to apply the national emissions mark, which is
  set out in the schedule, to an engine, vessel or vehicle must make an
  application to the Minister for an authorization to do so, signed by a
  person who is authorized to act on the company's behalf, that contains
  the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and street address of the company's head
    office and, if different, its mailing address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the class of engine, vessel or vehicle for which
    the authorization is requested;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the street address of the location at which the
    national emissions mark is to be applied; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} information to indicate that the company is capable
    of verifying compliance with the standards set out in these
    Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization number

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On granting an authorization, the Minister is to
  assign an authorization number to the company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Engines and vehicles --- before
  coming-into-force

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company that is authorized to apply the national
  emissions mark may apply it to an engine that is manufactured before
  the day on which this section comes into force or to a vehicle whose
  main assembly is completed before the day on which this section comes
  into force, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the engine or vehicle conforms to the standards set
    out in these Regulations for engines or vehicles for the 2012 model
    year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the company meets the requirements of these
    Regulations in respect of that engine or vehicle.

### [Display]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770895 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National emissions mark

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  national emissions mark must be displayed in a format of at least 7 mm
  in height and 10 mm in width.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization number

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The authorization number must be displayed
  immediately below or to the right of the national emissions mark, in
  figures that are at least 2 mm in height.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Location

### [Adjustable Parameters]{.HTitleText2} {#h-770932 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of adjustable parameter

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[adjustable parameter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
  device, system or element of design that is capable of being
  physically adjusted and can thereby affect emissions during emission
  testing or normal in-use operation, but does not include a device,
  system or element of design that is permanently sealed by the
  manufacturer or that is inaccessible with ordinary tools.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), an engine, vessel or
  vehicle that is equipped with adjustable parameters must conform to
  the applicable standards under these Regulations for any specification
  within the physically adjustable range.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vehicle

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a vehicle with an adjustable air-fuel
  ratio parameter, compliance with the applicable standards under these
  Regulations is required if the range of adjustable air-fuel ratios

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} other than any air-fuel ratio that does not occur
    during normal in-use operation, is between the lean limit and the
    rich limit; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is established by the manufacturer based on
    particular engine parts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lean limit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The lean limit is the air-fuel ratio that produces
  the highest engine power output averaged over the applicable test
  cycle referred to in section 501 of subpart F of CFR 1051.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rich limit

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The rich limit is the richest of the following
  air-fuel ratios:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the air-fuel ratio that results from operating the
    vehicle at applicable test conditions in accordance with the CFR,
    with the parameters adjusted to the settings that will be used at
    the time that the vehicle's main assembly is completed, excluding a
    vehicle manufactured without jets installed in its carburetted
    engine;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the air-fuel ratio of the engine during durability
    testing; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the richest air-fuel ratio specified by the
    manufacturer for the applicable ambient conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Carburettor jets and needles

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (3), if the range of adjustable
  air-fuel ratios of a vehicle is defined in terms of carburettor jets
  and needles, and the criteria in sections 115(d)(3)(ii) to (vi) of
  subpart B of CFR 1051 are met, the manufacturer's jetting chart that
  specifies the appropriate carburettor jet size and needle
  configuration as a function of the ambient conditions is the range of
  adjustable air-fuel ratios for which compliance with the applicable
  standards under these Regulations is required.

### [Fleet Average Emission Credits and Deficits for Engines]{.HTitleText2} {#h-771081 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fleet average emission credits or
deficits

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  fleet average emission credits or deficits, in respect of each
  emission type and for each fleet of engines, is the sum of the family
  emission credits or deficits for each emission family of that fleet
  determined in accordance with subsection (2). The sum is to be rounded
  to the nearest whole number of kilograms, and if the sum is
  equidistant between two whole numbers of kilograms, the sum is the
  higher of them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Family emission credits or deficits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The family emission credits or deficits in respect of
  each emission type and for each emission family of a fleet of engines
  for a given model year is determined in accordance with the following
  formula:

  ::: {#771104 .Subsection}
  (S-L) × N × P × U × 0.207 × 10^--3^

  where

  [S]{.dfn}
  :   is the applicable exhaust emission standard, expressed in g/kW-hr,
      for that emission type;

  [L]{.dfn}
  :   is the family emission limit for that emission family, expressed
      in g/kW-hr, for that emission type;

  [N]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of engines in that family;

  [P]{.dfn}
  :   is the maximum engine power for that emission family, expressed in
      kilowatts, determined in accordance with section 140 of subpart B
      of CFR 1045; and

  [U]{.dfn}
  :   is the useful life for engines of that emission family, as
      established under subsection 13(2) or 14(2) or (3), as the case
      may be, expressed in hours.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Engines manufactured before coming into
  force

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of determining the family emission
  credits or deficits in respect of each emission type and for each
  emission family of a fleet of engines for the 2012 model year, the
  company may include all engines of that model year, including those
  manufactured before this section comes into force.

#### [Offsetting Deficits and Use of Credits]{.HTitleText3} {#h-771113 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deficits

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  fleet average emission deficits for engines must be offset with an
  equivalent number of fleet average emission credits obtained by the
  company or transferred to it by another company. The deficits and
  credits must be in respect of the same type of fleet, the same
  emission type and the same standard, expressed in the same units.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remaining credits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company may bank any remaining credits to offset
  future deficits in accordance with subsection (1) or transfer them to
  another company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period for offsetting

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company must offset any fleet average emission
  deficits for a fleet for a given model year

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a fleet for the 2012 model year, no
    later than the day on which the company submits the end of model
    year report in respect of that fleet for the 2014 model year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a fleet for the 2013 and subsequent
    model years, no later than the day on which the company submits the
    end of model year report in respect of that fleet for that model
    year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- jet boat engines

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), during a given model year, a
  company that manufactures or imports a jet boat engine that is
  described in sections 660(a) and (c) of subpart G of CFR 1045 may
  offset any family emission deficits in respect of that jet boat engine
  with family emission credits, determined under subsection 26(2), in
  respect of a family of its fleet of outboards and personal watercraft
  engines that relate to the same model of engine as that jet boat
  engine. The credits so used cannot have been transferred to the
  company from another company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of credits --- CO exhaust
  emissions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), any fleet average emission
  credits in respect of CO exhaust emissions credits, other than those
  used under subsection (4), that are determined, in relation to a given
  model year, under subsection 26(1) in respect of a company's fleet of
  outboards and personal watercraft engines and are reported under
  section 33 are cancelled upon receipt of the report.

### [Fleet Average Emission Values for Vehicles]{.HTitleText2} {#h-771127 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicable standards --- sections 20 to
22

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 31 and for the
  purposes of paragraph 11(1)(d), the fleet average emission value in
  respect of each emission type for a fleet of vehicles for a given
  model year must not exceed the applicable standard referred to in any
  of sections 20 to 22, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emission types

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The emission types referred to in subsection (1) are
  as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a fleet of snowmobiles,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} HC exhaust emissions for the 2012 and subsequent
      model years,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} CO exhaust emissions for the 2012 and subsequent
      model years, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} fuel tank permeation emissions for the 2012 and
      subsequent model years;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a fleet of off-road motorcycles,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} HC + NO~x~ exhaust emissions for the 2012 and
      subsequent model years,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} CO exhaust emissions for the 2012 and subsequent
      model years, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} fuel tank permeation emissions for the 2012 and
      subsequent model years; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a fleet of all-terrain vehicles or of utility
    vehicles, or a fleet grouping both those classes,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} HC + NO~x~ exhaust emissions for the 2012 and
      subsequent model years, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} fuel tank permeation emissions for the 2012 and
      subsequent model years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election to not determine

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company may elect to not determine the fleet
  average emission value for any emission type for a given model year in
  respect of a fleet of vehicles if every vehicle in that fleet conforms
  to the family emission limit that is applicable to that emission type,
  which must not exceed the applicable standard, referred to in any of
  sections 20 to 22 as the case may be for vehicles of that model year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed average value

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a company makes an election under subsection (3),
  the fleet average emission value for the emission type for which the
  election was made in respect of that fleet of vehicles for the model
  year in question is deemed to be equal to that applicable standard.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of fleet average emission
values

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  fleet average emission value for a fleet of vehicles is determined, in
  respect of each emission type, in accordance with the following
  formula, expressed to one decimal place and in the same units as the
  applicable standard set out in any of sections 20 to 22, as the case
  may be:

  ::: {#771151 .Subsection}
  <figure>
  <div class="imageGroupcenter">
  <img src="/images/SOR-2011-10img/SOR-2011-10_e001.png"
  alt="The formula for the determination of the fleet average emission value for a fleet of vehicles is the quotient of the following two sums: the sum of the products resulting from the multiplication of Wi, Yi and Zi for each emission family, “i”, in the fleet and the sum of the products resulting from the multiplication of Yi and Zi for each emission family, “i”, in the fleet." />
  </div>
  </figure>

  where

  [TOT]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of emission families in the fleet;

  ["i"]{.dfn}
  :   is the i^th^ emission family in the fleet, where "i" goes from 1
      to TOT;

  [W~i~]{.dfn}
  :   is the family emission limit applicable to the emission family
      "i", expressed in the same units and to the same number of decimal
      places as the emission standard it replaces;

  [Y~i~]{.dfn}
  :   is, in the case of exhaust emissions, the number of vehicles in
      the fleet that are in the emission family "i" and, in the case of
      evaporative emissions, the number of vehicles in the fleet that
      are in the emission family "i" multiplied by the average internal
      surface area of the vehicles' fuel tanks expressed in metres
      squared; and

  [Z~i~]{.dfn}
  :   is determined as follows:
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a fleet of snowmobiles,

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of exhaust emissions, the useful
          life for the emission family "i" expressed in kilometres ---
          as established under section 103(c) of subpart B of CFR 1051
          --- multiplied by the maximum power output observed during the
          emissions test expressed in kilowatts divided by 30 km/h, and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of evaporative emissions, the
          useful life for the emission family "i" expressed in years ---
          as established under section 103(c) of subpart B of CFR 1051
          --- multiplied by 365.24 days/year;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a fleet of off-road motorcycles,

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of exhaust emissions, the useful
          life for the emission family "i" expressed in kilometres, as
          established under section 105(c) of subpart B of CFR 1051, and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of evaporative emissions, the
          useful life for the emission family "i", referred to in
          subparagraph (i), expressed in years multiplied by 365.24
          days/year; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a fleet of all-terrain vehicles or utility
        vehicles,

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of exhaust emissions, for those
          vehicles that must conform to a family emission limit
          expressed in g/km, the useful life for the emission family "i"
          expressed in kilometres --- as established under section
          107(c) of subpart B of CFR 1051 --- and for those vehicles
          that must conform to a family emission limit expressed in
          g/kW-h, Z is that useful life for the emission family "i"
          expressed in kilometres multiplied by the maximum power output
          observed during the emissions test expressed in kilowatts and
          divided by 30 km/h, and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of evaporative emissions, the
          useful life for the emission family "i", referred to in
          subparagraph (i), expressed in years multiplied by 365.24
          days/year.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emission limits expressed in g/kW-h

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of vehicles of a fleet that must conform
  to a family emission limit expressed in g/kW-h set out in section
  145(b) of subpart B of CFR 1051 or section 615(a) or (b) of subpart G
  of CFR 1051, the company must determine a separate fleet average
  emission value for those vehicles in respect of each emission type, as
  if those vehicles were a fleet.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vehicles --- before the coming into
  force date

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of determining the fleet average
  emission value under subsection (1) for a fleet for the 2012 model
  year, the company may include all vehicles for that model year,
  including those whose main assembly is completed before this section
  comes into force.

### [Fleet Average Emission Credits and Deficits for Vehicles]{.HTitleText2} {#h-771172 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obtaining credits

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subparagraph 162(1)(b)(i) of the Act, a company
  obtains fleet average emission credits in relation to a given emission
  type in respect of a fleet of vehicles referred to in subparagraph
  11(1)(d)(ii) for a given model year when

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fleet average emission value for the fleet for
    that emission type, determined under section 29, is less than the
    applicable standard in respect of the fleet for that emission type;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the company reports those credits in its end of
    model year report under section 33.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Result of excluding EPA certified
  vehicles

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company that excludes vehicles in accordance with
  subsection 24(4) from a fleet of vehicles for a given model year

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is ineligible to obtain fleet average emission
    credits in respect of that fleet; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} forfeits all fleet average emission credits,
    obtained in respect of previous model years, in respect of that
    fleet.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of fleet average emission
  credits

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The fleet average emission credits in respect of a
  fleet of vehicles in relation to a given emission type for a given
  model year, expressed in grams, are determined in accordance with the
  following formula:

  ::: {#771184 .Subsection}
  <figure>
  <div class="imageGroupcenter">
  <img src="/images/SOR-2011-10img/SOR-2011-10_e002.png"
  alt="The formula for the determination of the fleet average emission credits in respect of a fleet of vehicles in relation to a given emission type for a given model year is the difference between A and B multiplied by the sum of the products resulting from the multiplication of Yi and Zi for each emission family, “i”, in the fleet." />
  </div>
  </figure>

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the applicable standard for the fleet in relation to the given
      emission type for the given model year;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the fleet average emission value in relation to the given
      emission type, determined in accordance with section 29, for the
      given model year;

  [TOT]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of emission families in the fleet;

  ["i"]{.dfn}
  :   is the i^th^ emission family in the fleet, where "i" goes from 1
      to TOT;

  [Y~i~]{.dfn}
  :   is, in the case of exhaust emissions, the number of vehicles in
      the fleet that are in the emission family "i" and, in the case of
      evaporative emissions, the number of vehicles in the fleet that
      are in the emission family "i" multiplied by the average internal
      surface area of the vehicles' fuel tanks expressed in square
      metres; and

  [Z~i~]{.dfn}
  :   is the useful life applicable to the emission family "i" as
      described for Z~i~ in the formula set out in subsection 29(1).
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rounding

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the fleet average emission credits determined in
  accordance with subsection (3) results in a fraction, the fraction is
  to be expressed in decimal form and rounded to one decimal place, the
  digit at that first decimal place being increased by one if the digit
  at the second decimal place is 5 or more.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deficit

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  company's fleet average emission value in respect of an emission type
  for a fleet of vehicles exceeds the applicable standard in respect of
  the fleet for that emission type, the company's fleet average emission
  deficits is the negative number determined in accordance with the
  formula set out in subsection 30(3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offsetting of the deficit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any fleet average emission deficits must be offset
  with an equivalent number of fleet average emission credits obtained
  by the company or transferred to it by another company. The deficits
  and credits must be in respect of the same type of fleet, the same
  emission type and the same standard, expressed in the same units.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remaining credits

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company may bank any remaining credits to offset
  future deficits in accordance with subsection (2) or transfer them to
  another company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period for offsetting

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A company must offset any fleet average emission
  deficit for a fleet for a given model year

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a fleet for the 2012 model year and
    in relation to exhaust emissions, no later than the day on which the
    company submits the end of model year report in respect of that
    fleet for the 2014 model year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a fleet for the 2012 model year and
    in relation to fuel tank permeation emissions, no later than the day
    on which the company submits the end of model year report in respect
    of that fleet for the 2014 model year;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a fleet for the 2013 and subsequent
    model years and in relation to exhaust emissions, no later than the
    day on which the company submits the end of model year report in
    respect of that fleet for that model year; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a fleet for the 2013 and subsequent
    model years and in relation to fuel tank permeation emissions, no
    later than the day on which the company submits the end of model
    year report in respect of that fleet for that model year.

### [Corporate Changes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-771208 .Subheading}

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} for each model of vehicle in each of the

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the number determined in accordance with the
        formula set out in subsection 30(3);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number of engines or vehicles in the fleet;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number of fleet average emission credits

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records retained on company's behalf

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the records referred to in subsection (1) are
  retained on a company's behalf, the company must keep a record of the
  name and civic address and, if different, the mailing address of the
  person who retains those records.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline to submit records when
  requested

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister makes a written request for a record
  referred to in subsection (1) or (2), the company must submit it to
  the Minister in either official language

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 40 days after the day on which the request
    is made to the company; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 60 days after the day on which the request
    is made to the company, if the record must be translated from a
    language other than French or English.

## [Importation Requirements and Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771290 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Importer's declaration

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  for the purposes of paragraph 153(1)(b) of the Act, any person
  importing an engine, vessel or vehicle into Canada must submit, prior
  to importation, a declaration to the Minister, signed by that person
  or their duly authorized representative, that contains the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the importer's name, email address, telephone
    number, civic address and, if different, their mailing address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer's name, the number of engines,
    vessels or vehicles, as the case may be, to be imported and the
    make, model, model year and class of each of those engines, vessels
    and vehicles;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which they are expected to be imported;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the importer is a company,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the business number assigned to the company by
      the Minister of National Revenue, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a statement that each of those engines, vessels
      or vehicles bears the national emissions mark or that the company
      is able to produce the evidence of conformity referred to in
      subsection 35(1) or has produced the evidence of conformity in
      accordance with subsection 35(2), as the case may be; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the importer is not a company,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a statement from the importer that each of those
      engines, vessels or vehicles bears

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the national emissions mark,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the emission control information label referred
        to in paragraph 35(1)(d) that indicates, as the case may be,
        that

        - [(I)]{.lawlabel} the engine conformed to the emission
          standards of the EPA in effect when its manufacture was
          completed,

        - [(II)]{.lawlabel} the fuel lines and fuel tanks installed in
          the vessel or the outboard conformed to the emission standards
          of the EPA in effect at the time of the completion of its main
          assembly, or

        - [(III)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle conformed to the emission
          standards of the EPA in effect at the time of the completion
          of its main assembly, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} a label that indicates that the engine
        conformed to the emission standards of the California Air
        Resources Board in effect at the time of its manufacture or the
        vehicle conformed to the emission standards of the California
        Air Resources Board in effect at the time of the completion of
        its main assembly, as the case may be, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a statement from the manufacturer or their duly
      authorized representative that, as the case may be,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the engine conformed to the standards set out
        in these Regulations, or to the standards referred to in
        subclause (i)(B)(I) or clause (i)(C), when its manufacture was
        completed,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} each of the fuel lines and fuel tanks installed
        in the vessel or the outboard, as the case be, conformed to the
        standards set out in these Regulations, or to the standards
        referred to in subclause (i)(B)(II), at the time of the
        completion of the main assembly of the vessel or the manufacture
        of the outboard, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle conformed to the standards set out
        in these Regulations, or to the standards referred to in
        subclause (i)(B)(III) or clause (i)(C), at the time of the
        completion of its main assembly.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who is not a company who, in a calendar
  year, imports at most 10 of any combination of engines, vessels and
  vehicles is not required to make the declaration referred to in
  subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative declaration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph 153(1)(b) of the Act, a
  company that, in a calendar year, imports at least 500 of any
  combination of engines, vessels and vehicles into Canada may submit
  the information referred to in subsection (1) in a form and manner
  that is satisfactory to the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration --- paragraph 155(1)(a) of
Act

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  declaration referred to in paragraph 155(1)(a) of the Act must be
  signed by the importer, or their duly authorized representative, and
  must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information described in paragraphs 37(1)(a)
    to (c) and subparagraph 37(1)(d)(i);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the identification number, if any, of the engine,
    vessel or vehicle being imported;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the engine, vessel or vehicle is
    to be used in Canada solely for the purposes of exhibition,
    demonstration, evaluation or testing; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the engine, vessel or vehicle is
    to be removed from Canada or destroyed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When to file declaration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The declaration must be submitted to the Minister
  before the engine, vessel or vehicle is imported or, in the case of a
  company whose world production of engines, vessels and vehicles,
  combined, is at least 2,500 a year, quarterly, at the company's
  option.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 153(2) of Act

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** A company that imports an
engine, vessel or vehicle in reliance on subsection 153(2) of the Act
must, before the importation, submit a declaration to the Minister,
signed by its duly authorized representative, that contains the
information described in paragraphs 37(1)(a) to (c) and subparagraph
37(1)(d)(i) along with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement from the manufacturer of the engine,
  vessel or vehicle that when the engine, or the main assembly of the
  vessel or the vehicle, is completed in accordance with instructions
  provided by the manufacturer, the engine, vessel or vehicle will
  conform to the standards prescribed under these Regulations; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement from the company that the manufacture of
  the engine, or the completion of the main assembly of the vessel or
  vehicle, will be completed in accordance with the instructions
  referred to in paragraph (a).

## [Rental Rate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771329 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual --- 21%

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** The annual rental rate to
be paid to a company by the Minister under subsection 159(1) of the Act,
prorated on a daily basis for each day that an engine, vessel or vehicle
is made available, is 21% of the manufacturer's suggested retail price
of the engine, vessel or vehicle.

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771332 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** A company applying under
section 156 of the Act for an exemption from conformity with any
standard set out in these Regulations must, before manufacturing or
importing an engine, vessel or vehicle, submit the following
information, in writing, to the Minister

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} its name and civic address and, if different, its
  mailing address;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the province or country under the laws of which it is
  established;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the section number, title and text of the standards
  from which an exemption is sought;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the duration requested for the exemption;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the estimated number of engines, vessels or vehicles
  for which the exemption is sought and an estimate of the changes in
  the level of emissions if the exemption is granted;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the grounds, based on any of paragraphs 156(1)(a)
  to (c) for the application, including technical and financial
  information that supports, in detail, those grounds;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the grounds of the application is substantial
  financial hardship,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the world production of engines, vessels or
    vehicles manufactured by the company, or by the manufacturer of the
    model of the engine, vessel or vehicle that is the subject of the
    application, in the 12-month period that begins two years before the
    beginning of the exemption period that is requested, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the number of engines, vessels or vehicles
    manufactured for, or imported into, the Canadian market by the
    company in that 12-month period; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the company is requesting that information
  submitted be treated as confidential under section 313 of the Act, the
  reasons for the request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Label for exempt engines and vehicles

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Governor in Council has, by order, granted an exemption under section
  156 of the Act in respect of a model of an engine, vessel or vehicle,
  all engines, vessels or vehicles of that model must bear a label that
  meets the requirements set out in subsections 7(3) and (4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of label

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} That label must set out, in both official languages,
  the standard for which the exemption has been granted, as well as the
  title and date of the exemption order.

## [Defect Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771350 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice of defect

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  notice of defect referred to in subsections 157(1) and (4) of the Act
  must be given in writing and must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the company giving the notice;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the description of each engine, vessel or vehicle
    in respect of which the notice is given, including the make, model,
    identification number, model year, period of production and, if
    applicable, the EPA emission families;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the estimated percentage of the potentially
    affected engines, vessels or vehicles that contain the defect;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the defect;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an evaluation of the pollution risk arising from
    the defect;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the measures to be taken to correct
    the defect; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of the means available to the company
    to contact the current owner of each affected engine, vessel or
    vehicle.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of initial report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company must, within 60 days after the day on which
  a notice of defect is given, submit to the Minister the initial report
  referred to in subsection 157(7) of the Act that contains the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information required by subsection (1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of engines, vessels or vehicles in
    relation to which the notice of defect has been given;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a chronology of all principal events that led to
    the determination of the existence of the defect;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures taken to correct the
    defect; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} copies of all notices, bulletins and other
    circulars published by the company in respect of the defect,
    including a detailed description of the nature and physical location
    of the defect with diagrams and other illustrations if necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of follow-up reports

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The company that gave the notice of defect must
  submit subsequent regular reports respecting the defect and the
  measures taken to correct it to the Minister, each of which must
  contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number, title or other identification assigned
    by the company to the notice of defect;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of engines, vessels or vehicles in
    relation to which the notice of defect has been given;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date that notices of defect were given to the
    current owners of the affected engines, vessels or vehicles; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number or percentage of engines, vessels or
    vehicles repaired, and that required inspection only.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Frequency of follow-up reports

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Each subsequent regular report must be submitted

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for engines, within 12 months after the submission
    of the initial report or a prior subsequent regular report, as the
    case may be; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for vessels and vehicles, within six months after
    the submission of the initial report or a prior subsequent regular
    report, as the case may be.
