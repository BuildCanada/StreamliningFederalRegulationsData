## Minister of Fisheries and Oceans Authority to Prescribe Fees or Charges Orders {#minister-of-fisheries-and-oceans-authority-to-prescribe-fees-or-charges-orders .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/88-41
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1988-03-30

P.C. 1988-440  1988-03-10
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
paragraph 13(b) of the Financial Administration Act, is pleased hereby
to authorize the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans to prescribe, by
order, the fee or charge to be paid by a person to whom a service or the
use of a facility is provided by the Department of Fisheries and Oceans,
in accordance with the terms and conditions set out in the schedule
hereto.

#### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#interpretation .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} In this Schedule,

  [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of
      Fisheries and Oceans; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[direct costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[direct costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all personnel costs and
      all other operating costs and capital costs incurred by the
      Department that are directly attributable to the provision of a
      special service by the Department; ([coûts
      directs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Government of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Government of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
      as [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} as defined in section 2 of
      the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11); ([gouvernement
      du Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[indirect costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[indirect costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any costs to the
      Government of Canada that are not direct costs and that are
      attributable to the provision of a special service by the
      Department; ([coûts indirects]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Fisheries
      and Oceans; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any partnership and any
      corporation, any university and any government other than the
      Government of Canada; ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[special service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[special service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any service,
      information or the use of equipement or facilities provided by the
      Department to any equipement or facilities provided by the
      Department to any person with respect to fishing industry
      infrastructure, fisheries resource development, fisheries
      enhancement and protection, fisheries and aquaculture support,
      inspections, charts and navigation, oceanographic and marine
      science, laboratories, vessels and hatcheries; ([service
      spécial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[whole cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[whole cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the sum of direct costs
      and indirect costs. ([coût global]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

#### [Fees or Charges]{.HTitleText2} {#fees-or-charges .SchedHeadL2}

- [2]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 3 and 4, the Minister is authorized
  to prescribe for a special service provided to any person, a fee or
  charge that is equal to the whole cost of providing that service in
  respect of that person.

- [3]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 4, the Minister is authorized to
  prescribe for a special service provided to any person, a fee or
  charge that is equal to part of the whole cost of providing that
  service in respect of that person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the prescribing of a fee or charge at whole cost
    would have a serious detrimental effect on the income of the user of
    the service;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the prescribing of a fee or charge at whole cost
    would reduce the use of that service to an extent that the objective
    of providing the service would no longer be achieved; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if there is an equivalent or similar service
    provided commercially.

- [4]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister, pursuant to section 3 prescribes a
  fee or charge that is equal to a part of the whole cost of providing a
  special service, the Minister shall prescribe a fee or charge that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the circumstances set out in paragraphs 3(a) and
    3(b) achieves the highest level of partial cost recovery possible in
    those circumstances; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the circumstances set out in paragraph 3(c) is
    equal to the commercial rate for the equivalent or similar service.

- [5]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, for a special service provided to any
  registered charity as defined in the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), waive the prescribed or charge.
