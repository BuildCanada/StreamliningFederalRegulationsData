## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817106 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Payments
    Act](/eng/acts/C-21). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[affiliate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[affiliate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
    subsection 9(3) of the Act. ([appartenir à un
    groupe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nominating committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nominating committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the nominating
    committee referred to in section 20 of the Act. ([comité de
    nomination]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[senior officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[senior officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
    the definition [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
    [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([cadre dirigeant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Eligibility]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817113 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualified candidates

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  nominating committee must, in preparing a list of qualified candidates
  for the election as directors, ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} candidates for directors referred to in paragraphs
    8(1)(b) to (d) of the Act represent a sufficient range of skills,
    expertise and experience for the Board to carry out its
    responsibilities effectively;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} candidates for directors referred to in paragraphs
    8(1)(b) and (c) of the Act are at a level at least equivalent to the
    level of senior vice president or executive vice president within
    their organization; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} two of the three directors referred to in paragraph
    8(1)(b) of the Act are representatives of financial institutions
    listed in the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Particular case --- independent
  director

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In regard to the directors referred to in paragraph
  8(1)(d) of the Act, if the nominating committee is unable to prepare a
  list of qualified candidates in accordance with paragraph (1)(a)
  because of the time requirement referred to in paragraph 3(1)(a), (f),
  (g), (h) or (i), that time requirement is reduced to "within the last
  year".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Independent director

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 8(1)(d) of the Act,
  [[independent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person other than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who has been, within the last three years,
    a director, senior officer or employee of the Association, of a
    member or of an affiliate of a member;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who is involved in the day-to-day
    management or direction of the Association, of a member or of an
    affiliate of a member;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a director, senior officer or employee of a person
    referred to in subsection 4(2) of the Act, of a subsidiary of that
    person or of a corporation of which that person is its subsidiary;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a person who is a spouse or common-law-partner of a
    senior officer of the Association, of a senior officer of a member,
    of a senior officer of an affiliate of a member or anyone who
    resides with any such senior officer;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a person who is a family member in a financially
    dependent relationship, in a financially interdependent relationship
    or in a personal relationship with a senior officer of the
    Association, a senior officer of a member or a senior officer of an
    affiliate of a member, if that relationship could reasonably be
    expected to interfere with the exercise of a director's independent
    judgment;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a person who has had within the last three years a
    contractual or business relationship with the Association, with a
    member or with an affiliate of a member, if that relationship could
    reasonably be expected to interfere with the exercise of a
    director's independent judgment;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a partner, shareholder, director, senior officer or
    employee of an entity that has had a contractual or business
    relationship within the last three years with the Association, with
    a member or with an affiliate of a member, if that relationship
    could reasonably be expected to interfere with the exercise of a
    director's independent judgment;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a person who has been within the last three years
    an advisor or consultant to the Association, to a member or to an
    affiliate of a member, or a senior officer or director of that
    advisor or consultant, if that relationship could reasonably be
    expected to interfere with the exercise of a director's independent
    judgment; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a person who has received, in a calendar year any
    part of which falls within the last three years, from the
    Association, from a member or from an affiliate of a member any
    compensation that is greater than \$75,000, other than shares or
    share options or any compensation under a retirement plan, if the
    receipt of that compensation could reasonably be expected to
    interfere with the exercise of a director's independent judgment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Guidance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The nominating committee must provide guidance with
  respect to the documents or information that it considers relevant to
  verify the independence of a candidate or director in regard to any
  circumstance referred to in paragraphs (1)(e) to (i).

## [Election of Directors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817136 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Majority voting

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A candidate that receives
the greatest number of votes in an election of directors for which they
have been nominated is elected as a director. If only one candidate is
nominated as a director, they are elected as a director when they
receive a greater number of votes than the number of votes withheld.

## [Reporting of Director Independence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817139 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confirmation

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  nominating committee must confirm to the Board before the preparation
  of the report referred to in section 24 of the Act that each director
  referred to in paragraph 8(1)(d) of the Act is independent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board must indicate in the report

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the nominating committee has confirmed the
    independence of the directors; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the instances when the three-year period referred
    to in paragraph 3(1)(a), (f), (g), (h) or (i) has been reduced.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change in circumstance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A director referred to in paragraph 8(1)(d) of the
  Act must advise the Chairperson without delay of any change in their
  circumstance that is likely to affect their ability to meet the
  requirement to be independent.
