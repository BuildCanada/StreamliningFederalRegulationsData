## Visiting Forces (Part IX of the Excise Tax Act) Remission Order {#visiting-forces-part-ix-of-the-excise-tax-act-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/92-210
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1992-12-16

Order Respecting the Remission of the Tax Imposed under Part IX of the
Excise Tax Act, Paid or Payable on the Domestic Supply of Tangible
Personal Property, Real Property or Services for Use by Visiting Forces

P.C. 1992-2399  1992-11-19
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, is pleased hereby, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue and the Minister of
Finance, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-92-210_e_hq_6505){#fn_SI-92-210_e_hq_6505-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the
annexed [Order respecting the remission of the tax imposed under Part IX
of the Excise Tax Act, paid or payable on the domestic supply of
tangible personal property, real property or services for use by
visiting forces](/eng/regulations/SI-92-210).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-645278 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Visiting Forces (Part IX of the Excise Tax Act) Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-92-210).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-645280 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning on
    April 1 in one calendar year and ending on March 31 in the next
    calendar year; ([exercice]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[real property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[real property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 123(1) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15);
    ([immeuble]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 123(1) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15);
    ([service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[supply]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[supply]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    123(1) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15);
    ([fourniture]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the tax imposed under Division II
    of Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15);
    ([taxe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[visiting force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[visiting force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Visiting Forces Act](/eng/acts/V-2), but does not
    include any member of a visiting force or any civilian personnel
    designated as a civilian component of a visiting force. ([force
    étrangère présente au Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-645288 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted of the tax paid or payable on or after
January 1, 1991 in respect of a supply of tangible personal property,
real property or a service made to a visiting force for official use.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-645290 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on
condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a supply other than a supply made by
  the Department of National Defence or the Canadian Commercial
  Corporation, the visiting force submits a claim in writing to the
  Minister of National Revenue

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the claim is in respect of a supply made on
    or after the date of the coming into force of this subparagraph,
    within two years after the date on which the supply is made, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, within four years after the
    date on which the supply is made;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a claim submitted in accordance with
  paragraph (a), the visiting force provides, at the time the claim is
  submitted, the original or a certified true copy of an invoice
  substantiating the amount of tax paid in respect of the supply; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the visiting force provides the Minister of National
  Revenue with a complete report of the total amount of tax remitted to
  the force during the preceding fiscal year, not later than April 30
  following that fiscal year.
