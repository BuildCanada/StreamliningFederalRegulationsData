## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656416 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656417 .Subheading}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Nuclear Safety and Control
    Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[brachytherapy machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[brachytherapy machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2008-119, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[effective dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[effective dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned to
    that term by subsection 1(1) of the [Radiation Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-203). ([dose
    efficace]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[equivalent dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[equivalent dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned to
    that term by subsection 1(1) of the [Radiation Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-203). ([dose
    équivalente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hazardous substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hazardous substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[hazardous
    waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance or waste, other than a
    nuclear substance, that is used or produced in the course of
    carrying on a licensed activity and that may pose a risk to the
    environment or the health and safety of persons. ([substance
    dangereuse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}ou[déchet
    dangereux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[IAEA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[IAEA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Atomic Energy
    Agency. ([AIEA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[IAEA Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[IAEA Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement between
    the Government of Canada and the International Atomic Energy Agency
    for the Application of Safeguards in Connection with the Treaty on
    the Non-proliferation of Nuclear Weapons, effective on February 21,
    1972; INFCIRC/164; UNTS vol. 814, R. No. 11596. ([Accord avec
    l'AIEA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[irradiator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[irradiator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device that is designed
    to contain a nuclear substance and to deliver controlled doses of
    radiation to any target material except persons.
    ([irradiateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensed activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensed activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an activity
    described in any of paragraphs 26(a) to (f) of the Act that a
    licence authorizes the licensee to carry on. ([activité
    autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is licensed to
    carry on an activity described in any of paragraphs 26(a) to (f) of
    the Act. ([titulaire de permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the equipment
    prescribed by section 20. ([équipement réglementé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[prescribed information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the information
    prescribed by section 21. ([renseignements
    réglementés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[radioactive source teletherapy machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radioactive source teletherapy machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    a teletherapy machine that is designed to deliver doses of radiation
    produced by a nuclear substance. ([appareil de téléthérapie à source
    radioactive]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[safeguards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[safeguards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a verification system that
    is established in accordance with a safeguards agreement.
    ([garanties]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[safeguards agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[safeguards agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the IAEA Agreement and any arrangement between
      Canada and the IAEA made under that agreement; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any agreement to which Canada is a party for the
      establishment in Canada of a verification system in respect of
      nuclear substances, prescribed equipment or prescribed
      information, and any arrangements made under such an agreement.
      ([accord relatif aux garanties]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[safeguards equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[safeguards equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means equipment that is
    used in accordance with a safeguards agreement. ([équipement de
    garanties]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[teletherapy machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[teletherapy machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device that is
    designed to deliver controlled doses of radiation in a collimated
    beam for therapeutic purposes. ([appareil de
    téléthérapie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the process of being
    transported through Canada after being imported into and before
    being exported from Canada, in a situation where the place of
    initial loading and the final destination are outside Canada.
    ([transit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who performs work that
    is referred to in a licence. ([travailleur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656442 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
generally for the purposes of the Act.

## [Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656444 .Part}

### [General Application Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656445 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for a licence shall contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name and business address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the activity to be licensed and its purpose;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name, maximum quantity and form of any nuclear
    substance to be encompassed by the licence;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of any nuclear facility, prescribed
    equipment or prescribed information to be encompassed by the
    licence;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures to ensure compliance with the
    [Radiation Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-203),
    the [Nuclear Security Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-209)
    and the [Packaging and Transport of Nuclear Substances Regulations,
    2015](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-145);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any proposed action level for the purpose of
    section 6 of the [Radiation Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-203);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures to control access to the site
    of the activity to be licensed and the nuclear substance, prescribed
    equipment or prescribed information;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures to prevent loss or illegal
    use, possession or removal of the nuclear substance, prescribed
    equipment or prescribed information;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description and the results of any test, analysis
    or calculation performed to substantiate the information included in
    the application;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the name, quantity, form, origin and volume of any
    radioactive waste or hazardous waste that may result from the
    activity to be licensed, including waste that may be stored,
    managed, processed or disposed of at the site of the activity to be
    licensed, and the proposed method for managing and disposing of that
    waste;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's organizational management structure
    insofar as it may bear on the applicant's compliance with the Act
    and the regulations made under the Act, including the internal
    allocation of functions, responsibilities and authority;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a description of any proposed financial guarantee
    relating to the activity to be licensed; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} any other information required by the Act or the
    regulations made under the Act for the activity to be licensed and
    the nuclear substance, nuclear facility, prescribed equipment or
    prescribed information to be encompassed by the licence.

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2008-119, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The Commission or a designated officer authorized
  under paragraph 37(2)(c) of the Act, may require any other information
  that is necessary to enable the Commission or the designated officer
  to determine whether the applicant

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is qualified to carry on the activity to be
    licensed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} will, in carrying on that activity, make adequate
    provision for the protection of the environment, the health and
    safety of persons and the maintenance of national security and
    measures required to implement international obligations to which
    Canada has agreed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of an
  application for a licence to import or export for which the
  information requirements are prescribed by the Nuclear
  Non-Proliferation Import and Export Control Regulations, or in respect
  of an application for a licence to transport while in transit for
  which the information requirements are prescribed by the [Packaging
  and Transport of Nuclear Substances Regulations,
  2015](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-145).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-119, s. 2
- SOR/2015-145, s. 43

### [Application for Licence to Abandon]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656468 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence to abandon a nuclear substance, a nuclear facility, prescribed
equipment or prescribed information shall contain the following
information in addition to the information required by section 3:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and location of the land, buildings,
  structures, components and equipment that are to be abandoned;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proposed time and location of the abandonment;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proposed method of and procedure for abandonment;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the effects on the environment and the health and
  safety of persons that may result from the abandonment, and the
  measures that will be taken to prevent or mitigate those effects.

### [Application for Renewal of Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656474 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An application for the
renewal of a licence shall contain

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information required to be contained in an
  application for that licence by the applicable regulations made under
  the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement identifying the changes in the
  information that was previously submitted.

### [Application for Amendment, Revocation or Replacement of Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656478 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An application for the
amendment, revocation or replacement of a licence shall contain the
following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the amendment, revocation or
  replacement and of the measures that will be taken and the methods and
  procedures that will be used to implement it;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement identifying the changes in the
  information contained in the most recent application for the licence;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the nuclear substances, land, areas,
  buildings, structures, components, equipment and systems that will be
  affected by the amendment, revocation or replacement and of the manner
  in which they will be affected; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed starting date and the expected
  completion date of any modification encompassed by the application.

### [Incorporation of Material in Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656484 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence or for the renewal, suspension in whole or in part, amendment,
revocation or replacement of a licence may incorporate by reference any
information that is included in a valid, expired or revoked licence.

### [Renewal, Suspension, Amendment, Revocation or Replacement of Licence on Commission's Own Motion]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656486 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of section 25 of the Act, the Commission may renew a licence
  on its own motion if failure to renew the licence could pose an
  unreasonable risk to the environment, the health and safety of persons
  or national security.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of section 25 of the Act, the
  Commission may, on its own motion, suspend in whole or in part, amend,
  revoke or replace a licence under any of the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensee is not qualified to carry on the
    licensed activity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licensed activity poses an unreasonable risk to
    the environment, the health and safety of persons or the maintenance
    of national security;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the licensee has failed to comply with the Act, the
    regulations made under the Act or the licence;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the licensee has been convicted of an offence under
    the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a record referred to in the licence has been
    modified in a manner not permitted by the licence;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the licensee no longer carries on the licensed
    activity;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the licensee has not paid the licence fee
    prescribed by the Cost Recovery Fees Regulations; or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} failure to do so could pose an unreasonable risk to
    the environment, the health and safety of persons or national
    security.

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656500 .Part}

### [Exemptions from Licence Requirement for Inspectors, Designated Officers and Peace Officers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656501 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector, a designated officer or a peace officer may carry on any of
  the following activities without a licence to carry on that activity
  if the activity is carried on by that person to enforce the Act or the
  regulations made under the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} possess, transfer, transport or store a nuclear
    substance; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} possess or transfer prescribed equipment or
    prescribed information.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An inspector or a designated officer may service
  prescribed equipment without a licence to carry on that activity if
  the servicing is carried on by that person to enforce the Act or the
  regulations made under the Act.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the exemptions established in
  subsections (1) and (2) relate only to the activities specified in
  those subsections and do not derogate from the licence requirement
  imposed by section 26 of the Act in relation to other activities.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every person who carries on an activity without a
  licence in accordance with subsection (1) or (2) shall immediately
  notify the Commission of that fact.

### [Exemption of Naturally Occurring Nuclear Substances]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656509 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Naturally occurring
nuclear substances, other than those that are or have been associated
with the development, production or use of nuclear energy, are exempt
from the application of all provisions of the Act and the regulations
made under the Act except the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the provisions that govern the transport of nuclear
  substances;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a nuclear substance listed in the
  schedule to the Nuclear Non-proliferation Import and Export Control
  Regulations, the provisions that govern the import and export of
  nuclear substances.

### [Exemption by the Commission]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656515 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
section 7 of the Act, the Commission may grant an exemption if doing so
will not

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pose an unreasonable risk to the environment or the
  health and safety of persons;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pose an unreasonable risk to national security; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} result in a failure to achieve conformity with
  measures of control and international obligations to which Canada has
  agreed.

## [Obligations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656520 .Part}

### [Obligations of Licensees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656521 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure the presence of a sufficient number of
    qualified workers to carry on the licensed activity safely and in
    accordance with the Act, the regulations made under the Act and the
    licence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} train the workers to carry on the licensed activity
    in accordance with the Act, the regulations made under the Act and
    the licence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} take all reasonable precautions to protect the
    environment and the health and safety of persons and to maintain the
    security of nuclear facilities and of nuclear substances;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide the devices required by the Act, the
    regulations made under the Act and the licence and maintain them
    within the manufacturer's specifications;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} require that every person at the site of the
    licensed activity use equipment, devices, clothing and procedures in
    accordance with the Act, the regulations made under the Act and the
    licence;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} take all reasonable precautions to control the
    release of radioactive nuclear substances or hazardous substances
    within the site of the licensed activity and into the environment as
    a result of the licensed activity;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} implement measures for alerting the licensee to the
    illegal use or removal of a nuclear substance, prescribed equipment
    or prescribed information, or the illegal use of a nuclear facility;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} implement measures for alerting the licensee to
    acts of sabotage or attempted sabotage anywhere at the site of the
    licensed activity;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} take all necessary measures to facilitate Canada's
    compliance with any applicable safeguards agreement;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} instruct the workers on the physical security
    program at the site of the licensed activity and on their
    obligations under that program; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} keep a copy of the Act and the regulations made
    under the Act that apply to the licensed activity readily available
    for consultation by the workers.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee who receives a request from the
  Commission or a person who is authorized by the Commission for the
  purpose of this subsection, to conduct a test, analysis, inventory or
  inspection in respect of the licensed activity or to review or to
  modify a design, to modify equipment, to modify procedures or to
  install a new system or new equipment shall file, within the time
  specified in the request, a report with the Commission that contains
  the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} confirmation that the request will or will not be
    carried out or will be carried out in part;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any action that the licensee has taken to carry out
    the request or any part of it;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any reasons why the request or any part of it will
    not be carried out;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any proposed alternative means to achieve the
    objectives of the request; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any proposed alternative period within which the
    licensee proposes to carry out the request.

### [Transfers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656543 .Subheading}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** No licensee shall
transfer a nuclear substance, prescribed equipment or prescribed
information to a person who does not hold the licence, if any, that is
required to possess the nuclear substance, prescribed equipment or
prescribed information by the Act and the regulations made under the
Act.

### [Notice of Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656545 .Subheading}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee other than a licensee who is conducting field operations
  shall post, at the location specified in the licence or, if no
  location is specified in the licence, in a conspicuous place at the
  site of the licensed activity,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the licence, with or without the licence
    number, and a notice indicating the place where any record referred
    to in the licence may be consulted; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a notice containing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the licensee,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the licensed activity,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the nuclear substance, nuclear
      facility or prescribed equipment encompassed by the licence, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the location of the licence and
      any record referred to in it.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee who is conducting field operations
  shall keep a copy of the licence at the place where the field
  operations are being conducted.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply to a licensee in
  respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a licence to import or export a nuclear substance,
    prescribed equipment or prescribed information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a licence to transport a nuclear substance; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a licence to abandon a nuclear substance, a nuclear
    facility, prescribed equipment or prescribed information.

### [Representatives of Applicants and Licensees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656559 .Subheading}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Every applicant for a
licence and every licensee shall notify the Commission of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the persons who have authority to act for them in
  their dealings with the Commission;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the names and position titles of the persons who are
  responsible for the management and control of the licensed activity
  and the nuclear substance, nuclear facility, prescribed equipment or
  prescribed information encompassed by the licence; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any change in the information referred to in
  paragraphs (a) and (b), within 15 days after the change occurs.

### [Publication of Health and Safety Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656564 .Subheading}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee shall make available to all workers the health and safety
  information with respect to their workplace that has been collected by
  the licensee in accordance with the Act, the regulations made under
  the Act and the licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of personal
  dose records and prescribed information.

### [Obligations of Workers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656568 .Subheading}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Every worker shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} use equipment, devices, facilities and clothing for
  protecting the environment or the health and safety of persons, or for
  determining doses of radiation, dose rates or concentrations of
  radioactive nuclear substances, in a responsible and reasonable manner
  and in accordance with the Act, the regulations made under the Act and
  the licence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} comply with the measures established by the licensee
  to protect the environment and the health and safety of persons,
  maintain security, control the levels and doses of radiation, and
  control releases of radioactive nuclear substances and hazardous
  substances into the environment;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} promptly inform the licensee or the worker's
  supervisor of any situation in which the worker believes there may be

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a significant increase in the risk to the
    environment or the health and safety of persons,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a threat to the maintenance of the security of
    nuclear facilities and of nuclear substances or an incident with
    respect to such security,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a failure to comply with the Act, the regulations
    made under the Act or the licence,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an act of sabotage, theft, loss or illegal use or
    possession of a nuclear substance, prescribed equipment or
    prescribed information, or

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a release into the environment of a quantity of a
    radioactive nuclear substance or hazardous substance that has not
    been authorized by the licensee;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} observe and obey all notices and warning signs posted
  by the licensee in accordance with the [Radiation Protection
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-203); and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} take all reasonable precautions to ensure the
  worker's own safety, the safety of the other persons at the site of
  the licensed activity, the protection of the environment, the
  protection of the public and the maintenance of the security of
  nuclear facilities and of nuclear substances.

### [Presentation of Licence to Customs Officer]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656582 .Subheading}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** On importing or exporting
a nuclear substance, prescribed equipment or prescribed information, the
licensee shall present the required import or export licence to a
customs officer.

## [Prescribed Nuclear Facilities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656584 .Part}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The following facilities
are prescribed as nuclear facilities for the purpose of paragraph (i) of
the definition [[nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of
the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a facility for the management, storage or disposal of
  waste containing radioactive nuclear substances at which the resident
  inventory of radioactive nuclear substances contained in the waste is
  10^15^ Bq or more;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a plant for the production of deuterium or deuterium
  compounds using hydrogen sulphide; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a Class II nuclear facility, as defined in section 1
  of the [Class II Nuclear Facilities and Prescribed Equipment
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-205).

- SOR/2008-119, s. 3

## [Prescribed Equipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656591 .Part}

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Each of the following
items is prescribed equipment for the purposes of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a package, special form radioactive material, low
  dispersible radioactive material, fissile-excepted radioactive
  material, radioactive material that has a basic radionuclide value
  that is not listed in the IAEA Regulations and an instrument or
  article that has an alternative activity limit for an exempt
  consignment, as those terms are defined in subsection 1(1) of the
  [Packaging and Transport of Nuclear Substances Regulations,
  2015](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-145);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a radiation device and a sealed source, as defined in
  section 1 of the [Nuclear Substances and Radiation Devices
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-207);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Class II prescribed equipment, as defined in section
  1 of the [Class II Nuclear Facilities and Prescribed Equipment
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-205); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} equipment that is capable of being used in the
  design, production, operation or maintenance of a nuclear weapon or
  nuclear explosive device.

- SOR/2003-405, s. 2
- SOR/2015-145, s. 45

## [Prescribed Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656599 .Part}

### [Prescription]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656600 .Subheading}

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Information that concerns any of the
  following, including a record of that information, is prescribed
  information for the purposes of the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a nuclear substance that is required for the
    design, production, use, operation or maintenance of a nuclear
    weapon or nuclear explosive device, including the properties of the
    nuclear substance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the design, production, use, operation or
    maintenance of a nuclear weapon or nuclear explosive device;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the security arrangements, security equipment,
    security systems and security procedures established by a licensee
    in accordance with the Act, the regulations made under the Act or
    the licence, and any incident relating to security; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the route or schedule for the transport of
    Category I, II or III nuclear material, as defined in section 1 of
    the [Nuclear Security Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-209).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Information that is made public in accordance with
  the Act, the regulations made under the Act or a licence is not
  prescribed information for the purposes of the Act.

### [Exemptions from Licence Requirement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656610 .Subheading}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following persons may possess, transfer, import, export or use
  prescribed information without a licence to carry on that activity:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a minister, employee or other person acting on
    behalf of or under the direction of the Government of Canada, the
    government of a province or any of their agencies, for the purpose
    of assisting themselves in exercising a power or performing a duty
    or function lawfully conferred or imposed on them; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an official of a foreign government or an
    international agency, for the purpose of meeting obligations imposed
    by an arrangement made between the Government of Canada and the
    foreign government or international agency.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following persons may possess, transfer or use
  prescribed information without a licence to carry on that activity:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a worker, for the purpose of enabling the worker to
    perform duties assigned by the licensee; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who is legally required or legally
    authorized to obtain or receive the information.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the exemptions established in
  subsections (1) and (2) relate only to the activities specified in
  those subsections and do not derogate from the licence requirement
  imposed by section 26 of the Act in relation to other activities.

### [Transfer and Disclosure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656619 .Subheading}

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall transfer or disclose prescribed information unless the
  person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is legally required to do so; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} transfers or discloses it to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a minister, employee or other person acting on
      behalf or under the direction of the Government of Canada, the
      government of a province or any of their agencies, for the purpose
      of assisting themselves in exercising a power or performing a duty
      or function lawfully conferred or imposed on them,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an official of a foreign government or an
      international agency, for the purpose of meeting obligations
      imposed by an arrangement made between the Government of Canada
      and the foreign government or international agency,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a worker, for the purpose of enabling the
      worker to perform duties assigned by the licensee, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a person who is legally required or legally
      authorized to obtain or receive the information.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who possesses or has knowledge of prescribed
  information shall take all necessary precautions to prevent any
  transfer or disclosure of the prescribed information that is not
  authorized by the Act and the regulations made under the Act.

## [Contamination]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656629 .Part}

### [Prescribed Limits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656630 .Subheading}

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 45(a) and subsection 46(1) of the Act, the prescribed limit of
contamination for a place or vehicle where no licensed activity is being
carried on is any quantity of a radioactive nuclear substance that may,
based on the circumstances, increase a person's effective dose by 1 mSv
or more per year in excess of the background radiation for the place or
vehicle.

### [Prescribed Public Offices]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656632 .Subheading}

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 46(2) of the Act, a municipal office, a public library and a
public community centre are prescribed public offices.

### [Prescribed Measures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656634 .Subheading}

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 46(3) of the Act, the prescribed measures to reduce the level
of contamination are any measures to control access to or clean the
place, or to cover or remove the contamination, that are appropriate for
the substance and location and that will reduce the level of
contamination to below the limit prescribed by section 24.

## [Records and Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656636 .Part}

### [Record of Licence Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656637 .Subheading}

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Every licensee shall keep
a record of all information relating to the licence that is submitted by
the licensee to the Commission.

### [Retention and Disposal of Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656639 .Subheading}

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who is required to keep a record by the Act, the regulations
  made under the Act or a licence shall retain the record for the period
  specified in the applicable regulations made under the Act or, if no
  period is specified in the regulations, for the period ending one year
  after the expiry of the licence that authorizes the activity in
  respect of which the records are kept.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall dispose of a record referred to in
  the Act, the regulations made under the Act or a licence unless the
  person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is no longer required to keep the record by the
    Act, the regulations made under the Act or the licence; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has notified the Commission of the date of disposal
    and of the nature of the record at least 90 days before the date of
    disposal.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who notifies the Commission in accordance
  with subsection (2) shall file the record, or a copy of the record,
  with the Commission at its request.

### [General Reports]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656646 .Subheading}

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee who becomes aware of any of the following situations shall
  immediately make a preliminary report to the Commission of the
  location and circumstances of the situation and of any action that the
  licensee has taken or proposes to take with respect to it:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a situation referred to in paragraph 27(b) of the
    Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the occurrence of an event that is likely to result
    in the exposure of persons to radiation in excess of the applicable
    radiation dose limits prescribed by the [Radiation Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-203);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a release, not authorized by the licence, of a
    quantity of radioactive nuclear substance into the environment;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a situation or event that requires the
    implementation of a contingency plan in accordance with the licence;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an attempted or actual breach of security or an
    attempted or actual act of sabotage at the site of the licensed
    activity;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} information that reveals the incipient failure,
    abnormal degradation or weakening of any component or system at the
    site of the licensed activity, the failure of which could have a
    serious adverse effect on the environment or constitutes or is
    likely to constitute or contribute to a serious risk to the health
    and safety of persons or the maintenance of security;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} an actual, threatened or planned work disruption by
    workers;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a serious illness or injury incurred or possibly
    incurred as a result of the licensed activity;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the death of any person at a nuclear facility; or

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the occurrence of any of the following events:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the making of an assignment by or in respect of
      the licensee under the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency
      Act](/eng/acts/B-3),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the making of a proposal by or in respect of the
      licensee under the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3),

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the filing of a notice of intention by the
      licensee under the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3),

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the filing of a petition for a receiving order
      against the licensee under the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency
      Act](/eng/acts/B-3),

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the enforcement by a secured creditor of a
      security on all or substantially all of the inventory, accounts
      receivable or other property of the licensee that was acquired
      for, or used in relation to, a business carried on by the
      licensee,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the filing in court by the licensee of an
      application to propose a compromise or an arrangement with its
      unsecured creditors or any class of them under section 4 of the
      [Companies' Creditors Arrangement Act](/eng/acts/C-36),

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the filing in court by the licensee of an
      application to propose a compromise or an arrangement with its
      secured creditors or any class of them under section 5 of the
      [Companies' Creditors Arrangement Act](/eng/acts/C-36),

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the making of an application for a winding-up
      order by or in respect of the licensee under the [Winding-up and
      Restructuring Act](/eng/acts/W-11),

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the making of a liquidation, bankruptcy,
      insolvency, reorganization or like order in respect of the
      licensee under provincial or foreign legislation, or

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} the making of a liquidation, bankruptcy,
      insolvency, reorganization or like order in respect of a body
      corporate that controls the licensee under provincial or foreign
      legislation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee who becomes aware of a situation
  referred to in subsection (1) shall file a full report of the
  situation with the Commission within 21 days after becoming aware of
  it, unless some other period is specified in the licence, and the
  report shall contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date, time and location of becoming aware of
    the situation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the situation and the
    circumstances;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the probable cause of the situation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the effects on the environment, the health and
    safety of persons and the maintenance of security that have resulted
    or may result from the situation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the effective dose and equivalent dose of radiation
    received by any person as a result of the situation; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the actions that the licensee has taken or proposes
    to take with respect to the situation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) do not require a licensee to
  report a situation referred to in paragraphs (1)(a) to (j) if the
  licence contains a term or condition requiring the licensee to report
  that situation, or any situation of that nature, to the Commission.

### [Safeguards Reports]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656677 .Subheading}

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee who becomes aware of any of the following situations shall
  immediately make a preliminary report to the Commission of the
  situation and of any action that the licensee has taken or proposes to
  take with respect to it:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} interference with or an interruption in the
    operation of safeguards equipment or the alteration, defacement or
    breakage of a safeguards seal, other than in accordance with the
    safeguards agreement, the Act, the regulations made under the Act or
    the licence; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the theft, loss or sabotage of safeguards equipment
    or samples collected for the purpose of a safeguards inspection,
    damage to such equipment or samples, or the illegal use, possession,
    operation or removal of such equipment or samples.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee who becomes aware of a situation
  referred to in subsection (1) shall file a full report of the
  situation with the Commission within 21 days after becoming aware of
  it, unless some other period is specified in the licence, and the
  report shall contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date, time and location of becoming aware of
    the situation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the situation and the
    circumstances;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the probable cause of the situation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the adverse effects on the environment, the health
    and safety of persons and the maintenance of national and
    international security that have resulted or may result from the
    situation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the effective dose and equivalent dose of radiation
    received by any person as a result of the situation; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the actions that the licensee has taken or proposes
    to take with respect to the situation.

### [Report of Deficiency in Record]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656689 .Subheading}

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee who becomes aware of an inaccuracy or incompleteness in a
  record that the licensee is required to keep by the Act, the
  regulations made under the Act or the licence shall file a report of
  the inaccuracy or incompleteness with the Commission within 21 days
  after becoming aware of it, and the report shall contain the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the details of the inaccuracy or incompleteness;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any action that the licensee has taken or proposes
    to take with respect to the inaccuracy or incompleteness.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a licensee if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licence contains a term or condition that
    requires the licensee to report inaccuracies or incompleteness in a
    record to the Commission; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the inaccuracy or incompleteness in the record
    could not reasonably be expected to lead to a situation in which the
    environment, the health and safety of persons or national security
    is adversely affected.

### [Filing of Reports]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656697 .Subheading}

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  report shall include the name and address of its sender and the date
  on which it was completed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The date of filing of a report is the date on which
  it is received by the Commission.

## [Inspectors and Designated Officers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656701 .Part}

### [Certificate of Inspector]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656702 .Subheading}

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** An inspector's
certificate issued under section 29 of the Act shall be in the form set
out in the schedule and shall include, in addition to the information
required by subsection 29(2) of the Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and signature of the inspector;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a photograph showing the face of the inspector;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the employer of the inspector;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement of designation;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name, position and signature of the person who
  issued the certificate; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the expiry date of the certificate.

### [Certificate of Designated Officer]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656710 .Subheading}

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** A designated officer's
certificate issued under section 37 of the Act shall include, in
addition to the information required by subsection 37(1) of the Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and position or title of the designated
  officer;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the employer of the designated officer;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement of designation;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, position and signature of the person who
  issued the certificate; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the expiry date of the certificate.

### [Notification and Surrender of Certificate]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656717 .Subheading}

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector and a designated officer shall notify the Commission of any
  of the following situations:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the loss or theft of their certificate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any change in their employment that results in
    their no longer exercising a function that relates to the purpose of
    the certificate; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the suspension or termination of their employment
    with the employer named in the certificate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An inspector and a designated officer shall surrender
  their certificate to the Commission

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the information contained in the certificate is
    not accurate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the certificate expires; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on termination by the Commission of their
    designation as an inspector or a designated officer, as the case may
    be.
