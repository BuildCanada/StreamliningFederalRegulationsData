## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1392625 .Part}

Definition of [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Order, [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Medical Devices
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other words and expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context otherwise requires, other words
  and expressions used in this Order have the meanings assigned to them
  by the Regulations or the [Fees in Respect of Drugs and Medical
  Devices Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-124), as the case may be.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1392631 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment licence

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), remission is granted to a person referred to in
  subsection 71(2) of the [Fees in Respect of Drugs and Medical Devices
  Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-124) of the amount of the fee that is
  paid or payable under subsection 71(1) of that Order --- or, if
  applicable, that amount adjusted to account for the remission set out
  in subsection 6(1) or section 74 of that Order --- and any interest on
  it, for the examination of one of the following applications:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application for an establishment licence that is
    submitted under section 45 of the Regulations; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an application for the annual review of an
    establishment licence that is submitted under section 46.1 of the
    Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The remission is granted if the following conditions
  are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application was submitted to the Minister on or
    after April 1, 2020;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister completed the examination of the
    application on or before December 31, 2021;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the fee that is payable for the
    examination of the application was not paid to the Minister before
    the completion of the examination; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Minister issued the establishment licence or
    completed the annual review of the establishment licence, but
    cancelled it on or before January 31, 2022.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1392643 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
