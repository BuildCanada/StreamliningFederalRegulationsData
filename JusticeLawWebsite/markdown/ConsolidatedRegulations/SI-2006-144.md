## Order Designating the Internal Inquiry into the Actions of Canadian Officials in Relation to Abdullah Almalki, Ahmad Abou-Emaati and Muayyed Nureddin as a Department for Purposes of the Act and the Prime Minister as Appropriate Minister {#order-designating-the-internal-inquiry-into-the-actions-of-canadian-officials-in-relation-to-abdullah-almalki-ahmad-abou-emaati-and-muayyed-nureddin-as-a-department-for-purposes-of-the-act-and-the-prime-minister-as-appropriate-minister .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-144
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2006-12-27

Order Designating the Internal Inquiry into the Actions of Canadian
Officials in Relation to Abdullah Almalki, Ahmad Abou-Emaati and Muayyed
Nureddin as a Department for Purposes of the Act and the Prime Minister
as Appropriate Minister

P.C. 2006-1530 2006-12-11
::::

::::: section
:::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-2006-144_e_hq_1686){#fn_SI-2006-144_e_hq_1686-ID0EFADBA}
  of the definition [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of
  the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), designates the
  Internal Inquiry into the Actions of Canadian Officials in Relation to
  Abdullah Almalki, Ahmad Abou-Elmaati and Muayyed Nureddin as a
  department for the purposes of that Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SI-2006-144_e_hq_1687){#fn_SI-2006-144_e_hq_1687-ID0EDACBA}
  of the definition [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
  section 2 of that Act, designates the Prime Minister as the
  appropriate Minister with respect to the Inquiry referred to in
  paragraph (a).
