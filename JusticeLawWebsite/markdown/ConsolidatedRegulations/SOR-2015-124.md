## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-816799 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Citizenship
    Act](/eng/acts/C-29). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[ICCRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ICCRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Immigration Consultants of
    Canada Regulatory Council, incorporated under Part II of the [Canada
    Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8) on February 18,
    2011. ([CRCIC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[legal guardian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[legal guardian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in respect of a child who has
    not attained the age of 18 years, means a person who has custody of
    the child or who is empowered to act on the child's behalf by virtue
    of a court order or written agreement or by operation of
    law. ([tuteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[parent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[parent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 2
    of the Regulations. ([parent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5). ([résident permanent]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[prescribed form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the form prescribed
    under section 28 of the Act. ([formule prescrite]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Citizenship
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-246). ([règlement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Grant of Citizenship]{.HTitleText1} {#h-816809 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application under subsection 5(1) of Act

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application made under subsection 5(1) of the Act must be made in the
  prescribed form and filed in the manner determined by the Minister,
  together with the following information and materials:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes the applicant's date and
    place of birth;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any document created by the Canadian immigration
    authorities, or other evidence, that establishes the date on which
    the applicant became a permanent resident;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant meets
    the requirements set out in subparagraph 5(1)(c)(i) of the Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} whichever of the following numbers that was most
    recently issued to the applicant:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a social insurance number,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an individual tax number assigned to them by the
      Canada Revenue Agency, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a temporary tax number assigned to them by the
      Canada Revenue Agency;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant has one of the numbers referred to
    in subparagraphs (d)(i) to (iii), evidence that establishes that the
    applicant consents to the sharing of their tax information by the
    Canada Revenue Agency with the Minister;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant does not have one of the numbers
    referred to in subparagraphs (d)(i) to (iii), a declaration stating
    that they do not have such a number and were not required to file a
    return of income under the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) for
    each of the taxation years that are fully or partially within the
    five years before the day on which their application is made;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is at least 18 years of age but is
    under 55 years of age on the day on which their application is made,
    evidence that establishes that they have an adequate knowledge of
    one of the official languages of Canada, including language test
    results or other evidence that establishes that they meet the
    criteria set out in section 14 of the Regulations;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} two photographs of the applicant in the format
    specified on the prescribed form; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant has
    paid the fees required under sections 31 and 32 of the Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application under subsection 5(1) of
  Act --- Canadian Forces members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application made under subsection 5(1) of the Act
  by a permanent resident who is a member or a former member of the
  Canadian Forces must be made in the prescribed form and filed in the
  manner determined by the Minister, together with the following
  information and materials:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes the applicant's date and
    place of birth;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any document created by Canadian immigration
    authorities, or other evidence, that establishes the date on which
    the applicant became a permanent resident;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant has
    completed at least three years of service in the Canadian Forces
    during the six years before the day on which their application is
    made;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} whichever of the following numbers that was most
    recently issued to the applicant:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a social insurance number,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an individual tax number assigned to them by the
      Canada Revenue Agency, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a temporary tax number assigned to them by the
      Canada Revenue Agency;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant
    consents to the sharing of their tax information by the Canada
    Revenue Agency with the Minister;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant was released from the Canadian
    Forces, evidence that establishes that they were released
    honourably;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is at least 18 years of age but is
    under 55 years of age on the day on which their application is made,
    evidence that establishes that they have an adequate knowledge of
    one of the official languages of Canada, including language test
    results or other evidence that establishes that they meet the
    criteria set out in section 14 of the Regulations;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} two photographs of the applicant in the format
    specified on the prescribed form; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant has
    paid the fees required under sections 31 and 32 of the Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application under subsection 5(1) of
  Act --- Canadian Forces

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application made under subsection 5(1) of the Act
  by a person who is or was attached or seconded to the Canadian Forces

  by the Minister, together with the following information and

    made;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is at least 18 years of age but is
    under 55 years of age on the day on which their application is made,
    evidence that establishes that they have an adequate knowledge of
    one of the official languages of Canada, including language test
    results or other evidence that establishes that they meet the
    criteria set out in section 14 of the Regulations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} two photographs of the applicant in the format
    specified on the prescribed form; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant has
    paid the fees required under sections 31 and 32 of the Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application under subsection 5(2) of Act

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application made under
subsection 5(2) of the Act on behalf of a minor child must be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} made in the prescribed form by a parent or legal
  guardian;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} countersigned by the child, if the child has attained

    applicant has the custody of the child,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} two photographs of the child in the format

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant has

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An

  together with the following information and materials:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes the applicant's date and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant was a

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any document created by Canadian immigration
    authorities, or other evidence, that establishes the date on which
    the applicant became a permanent resident;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant meets
    the requirements set out in subparagraph 11(1)(d)(i) of the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} whichever of the following numbers that was most
    recently issued to the applicant:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a social insurance number,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an individual tax number assigned to them by the
      Canada Revenue Agency, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a temporary tax number assigned to them by the
      Canada Revenue Agency;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant has one of the numbers referred to
    in subparagraphs (e)(i) to (iii), evidence that establishes that the
    applicant consents to the sharing of their tax information by the
    Canada Revenue Agency with the Minister;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if an applicant does not have one of the numbers
    referred to in subparagraphs (e)(i) to (iii), a declaration stating
    that they do not have such a number and were not required to file a
    return of income under the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) for the
    taxation year before the day on which the application is made;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} two photographs of the applicant in the format
    specified on the prescribed form; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant has
    paid the fees required under sections 31 and 32 of the Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application under subsection 11(1) of
  Act --- Canadian Forces member

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application made under subsection 11(1) of the Act
  by a permanent resident who is a member or a former member of the
  Canadian Forces must be made in the prescribed form and filed in the
  manner determined by the Minister, together with the following
  information and materials:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes the applicant's date and
    place of birth;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant was a
    citizen but has ceased to be one;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any document created by Canadian immigration
    authorities, or other evidence, that establishes the date on which
    the applicant became a permanent resident;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant has
    completed at least six months of service in the Canadian Forces
    during the two years before the day on which the application is
    made;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} whichever of the following numbers that was most
    recently issued to the applicant:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a social insurance number,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an individual tax number assigned to them by the
      Canada Revenue Agency, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a temporary tax number assigned to them by the
      Canada Revenue Agency;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant
    consents to the sharing of their tax information by the Canada
    Revenue Agency with the Minister;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant was released from the Canadian
    Forces, evidence that establishes that the applicant was released
    honourably;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} two photographs of the applicant in the format
    specified on the prescribed form; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant has
    paid the fees required under sections 31 and 32 of the Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application under subsection 11(1) of
  Act --- Canadian Forces

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application made under subsection 11(1) of the Act
  by an applicant who is or was attached or seconded to the Canadian
  Forces must be made in the prescribed form and filed in the manner
  determined by the Minister, together with the following information
  and materials:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes the applicant's date and
    place of birth;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant was a
    citizen but has ceased to be one;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} two photographs of the applicant in the format
    specified on the prescribed form;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant has
    completed at least six months of service in the Canadian Forces
    during the two years before the day on which the application is
    made; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant has
    paid the fees required under sections 31 and 32 of the Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 11(2) of Act

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 11(2) of the Act, a woman may give notice
  in writing to the Minister in the manner determined by the Minister,
  together with the evidence that she has that relates to the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} her date and place of birth;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and place of her marriage;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} her nationality immediately before her marriage;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the nationality of her husband at the time of their
    marriage; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any nationality acquired by her husband since their
    marriage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inquiry

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister does not receive, together with the
  notice, evidence that establishes whether a woman meets the
  requirements of subsection 11(2) of the Act, the Minister must without
  delay contact the woman and make all reasonable inquiries in an
  endeavour to obtain such evidence.

## [Certificates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-816995 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application under subsection 12(1) of Act

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
certificate of citizenship by a citizen made under subsection 12(1) of
the Act must be made in the prescribed form and filed in the manner
determined by the Minister, together with the following information and
materials:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant is a
  citizen;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} two photographs of the applicant in the format
  specified on the prescribed form; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} evidence that establishes that the applicant has paid
  the fee required under section 31 of the Regulations.

## [Replacement of Certificates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817001 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 25(3) of Regulations

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 25(3) of the Regulations, the person
  referred to in that subsection must indicate in their application the
  reason why they desire another certificate of citizenship.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Details of loss or destruction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an application is made for reasons of loss or
  destruction in respect of any of the certificates referred to in
  subsection 25(3) of the Regulations, the applicant must provide the
  details of the loss or destruction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may require

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may require the applicant to provide
  evidence that establishes that the issuance of the certificate will
  not contravene subsection 25(1) of the Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 25(5) of Regulations

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 25(5) of the Regulations, the person
  referred to in that subsection must set out in the application the
  reasons why they desire another certificate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Details of loss or destruction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an application is made for reasons of loss or
  destruction of the previously issued certificate, the applicant must
  provide the details of the loss or destruction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may require

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may require the applicant to provide the
  Minister with evidence that establishes that the issuance of the
  certificate will not contravene subsection 25(1) of the Regulations.

## [Delivery of Revocation Notices]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817016 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delivery

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  notice referred in subsection 10(3) of the Act and the decision
  referred to in subsection 10(5) of the Act may be delivered by hand,
  by mail or by electronic means.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal delivery

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Delivery of a document to an individual by hand is
  effected by delivering it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the individual or to a person who has attained
    18 years of age and who is present at the individual's last known
    address; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the individual is a minor, to the individual's
    parent or to any other person having lawful custody of the minor or
    to a person who has attained 18 years of age who is present at the
    last known address of the parent or the person having lawful
    custody.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delivery by mail

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Delivery of a document to an individual by regular
  mail is effected by sending it by regular mail to the individual's
  last known address or, if the individual is a minor, to the last known
  address of the individual's parent or of any other person having
  lawful custody.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delivery by electronic means

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Delivery of a document to an individual by electronic
  means is effected by sending it to the individual's last known
  electronic address or, if the individual is a minor, by sending it to
  the last known electronic address of the minor's parent or the person
  having lawful custody of the minor.

## [Representatives and Advisors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817033 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purposes of subsection 21.1(5) of Act

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 21.1(5) of the Act, the ICCRC is designated as a body whose
members in good standing may represent or advise a person for
consideration --- or offer to do so --- in connection with a proceeding
or application under the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person not authorized under subsections
21.1 (2) to (4) of Act

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** If an applicant is
represented or advised for consideration in connection with a proceeding
or application under the Act by a person who is not referred to in
subsections 21.1 (2) to (4) of the Act the application will be returned
to the applicant because it is not accepted into processing.

## [Transitional Measure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817038 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representative or advisor

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A person who represents
or advises for consideration another person who has made an application
under the Act that is received by the Minister before the day on which
section 21.1 of the Act comes into force is deemed for the purposes of
only that application and that section 21.1 to be a member in good
standing of the ICCRC for a period of four years beginning on the day on
which that section 21.1 comes into force or until one of the following
occurs:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application is returned to the applicant because
  it was not accepted for processing;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application is approved, refused, withdrawn or
  treated as abandoned;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the applicant ceases to be represented by that
  person;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person becomes a person referred to in any of
  subsections 21.1(2) to (4) of the Act; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the applicant notifies the Minister in the prescribed
  form that they are represented by a person referred to in any of
  subsections 21.1(2) to (4) of the Act.
