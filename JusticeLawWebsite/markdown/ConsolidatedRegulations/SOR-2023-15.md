## Prohibition of the Manufacture and Importation of Wheel Weights Containing Lead Regulations {#prohibition-of-the-manufacture-and-importation-of-wheel-weights-containing-lead-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 2023-02-03

Prohibition of the Manufacture and Importation of Wheel Weights
Containing Lead Regulations

P.C. 2023-65 2023-02-03

## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1390483 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 2 of
the [Motor Vehicle Safety Act](/eng/acts/M-10.01).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1390486 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Wheel weights

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), these Regulations apply in respect of wheel weights
  containing lead that are designed to balance the wheels of a vehicle.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of wheel
  weights that are in transit through Canada, from a place outside
  Canada to another place outside Canada.

## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1390492 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited activities

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not manufacture or import wheel weights containing more than 0.1%
  lead by weight, except in accordance with a permit issued under
  section 5.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The prohibition in subsection (1) does not apply in
  respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manufacture for export of wheel weights; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the importation of wheel weights installed on
    vehicles.

## [Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1390500 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for permit

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for a permit must include the information specified in the
  schedule and be submitted to the Minister not later than the 90th day
  before the day on which the wheel weights containing lead are to be
  manufactured or imported.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be accompanied by a
  certification, dated and signed by the applicant or by their
  authorized representative, stating that the information contained in
  the application is accurate and complete.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of issuance

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Minister may issue a permit to manufacture or
  import wheel weights containing lead for the term requested in the
  application for a permit if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has established that, at the time of
    the application,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} there is no alternative to the manufacture or
      importation of wheel weights containing lead, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} there is an alternative but the alternative is
      not technically or economically feasible for the applicant; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the requested term of the permit does not exceed
    the period for which the permit is necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permit term

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The term of the permit must not exceed 24 months.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must refuse to issue a permit if the
  Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that the applicant has
  submitted false or misleading information in support of their
  application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Renewal of permit

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A permit
  may be renewed once.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for renewal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for the renewal of a permit must
  include the information specified in the schedule and be submitted to
  the Minister not later than the 90th day before the day on which the
  permit expires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The application must be accompanied by a
  certification, dated and signed by the applicant or by their
  authorized representative, stating that the information contained in
  the application is accurate and complete.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of renewal

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may renew the permit for a term that
  must not exceed 24 months if the conditions in subsection 5(1) are
  met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must refuse to renew the permit if the
  Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that the applicant has
  submitted false or misleading information in support of their
  application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of permit

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must revoke a permit if the Minister has reasonable grounds
  to believe that the permit holder has submitted false or misleading
  information to the Minister or that the permit holder has failed to
  comply with the permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for revocation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must not revoke a permit unless the
  Minister has

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notified the permit holder in writing of the
    reasons for the revocation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provided the permit holder with an opportunity to
    make written representations in respect of the revocation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of revocation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The revocation of a permit is not effective until the
  20th day after the day on which the Minister notifies the permit
  holder.

## [Documents and Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1390537 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic submission

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  document submitted to the Minister under these Regulations must be
  submitted electronically in the form specified by the Minister and
  must be accompanied by a certification, dated and signed by the person
  submitting the document or by their authorized representative, stating
  that the document is accurate and complete.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission on paper

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister has not specified an electronic form
  or if it is not feasible to submit the document electronically in
  accordance with subsection (1) because of circumstances beyond the
  person's control, the document must be submitted on paper in the form
  specified by the Minister and must be accompanied by a certification,
  dated and signed by the person submitting the document or by their
  authorized representative, stating that the document is accurate and
  complete. If no form has been specified, the information may be sent
  in any form.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission of information

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A person that manufactures
or imports wheel weights in respect of which these Regulations apply
must submit to the Minister, in English or French, any requested
information that relates to the lead in those wheel weights.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person that manufactures or imports wheel weights in respect of which
  these Regulations apply must maintain records that demonstrate that
  those wheel weights were manufactured or imported in accordance with
  these Regulations and that include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which those wheel weights were
    manufactured or imported, as the case may be;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which those wheel weights were exported
    or on which they are intended to be exported, as the case may be;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number of those wheel weights that were
    manufactured or imported.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retaining records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The records must be retained for a period of at least
  five years after the day on which they are made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Place retained

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The records must be retained at the person's
  principal place of business in Canada or, on notification to the
  Minister, at any other location in Canada where the records can be
  inspected.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records moved

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the records are moved, the person must notify the
  Minister in writing of the civic address in Canada of the new location
  within 30 days after the day of the move.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic record

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person that keeps records electronically must
  ensure that all equipment and software necessary to make the record
  intelligible are available during the retention period.

## [Accredited Laboratory]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1390559 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accredited laboratory

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Any analysis performed to
determine the concentration of lead for the purposes of these
Regulations must be performed by a laboratory that meets the following
conditions at the time of the analysis:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is accredited

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} under the International Organization for
    Standardization standard ISO/IEC 17025, entitled General
    requirements for the competence of testing and calibration
    laboratories, by an accrediting body that is a signatory to the
    International Laboratory Accreditation Cooperation Mutual
    Recognition Arrangement, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} under the Environmental Quality Act, CQLR, c. Q-2;
    and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the scope of its accreditation includes the analysis
  performed to determine the concentration of lead.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsections 4(1) and 6(2))]{.OriginatingRef}[Information to be Contained in an Application for a Permit or Renewal of a Permit]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-1390573 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** Information respecting the applicant:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their name, civic and postal addresses in Canada,
    telephone number and, if any, fax number and email address; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, title, civic and postal addresses in
    Canada, telephone number and, if any, fax number and email address
    of any authorized representative.

- **2** Information respecting the product:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for a permit,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the common or generic name of the product and its
      trade name, if any,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the estimated number of wheel weights to be
      imported or manufactured and the estimated total quantity of lead
      contained in the wheel weights, expressed in kilograms, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the type of vehicle for which the product is
      designed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for the renewal of a
    permit, the information specified in paragraph (a) as well as
    information specifying the number of wheel weights manufactured or
    imported under the terms of the initial permit and the total
    quantity of lead contained in the wheel weights, expressed in
    kilograms.

- **3** Information establishing that, at the time of the application,
  there is no alternative to the manufacture or importation of wheel
  weights containing lead or, if there is an alternative, that it is not
  technically or economically feasible for the applicant.

- **4** The requested term of the permit.

- **5** The civic and postal addresses of the location where the records
  referred to in section 10 of these Regulations are retained in Canada.
