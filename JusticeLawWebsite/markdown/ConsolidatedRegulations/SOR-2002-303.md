## Dakota Tipi Band Council Method of Election Regulations {#dakota-tipi-band-council-method-of-election-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2002-303
:::

[INDIAN ACT](/eng/acts/I-5)

Registration 2002-08-08

Dakota Tipi Band Council Method of Election Regulations

P.C. 2002-1396 2002-08-08
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
subsection 74(3) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), hereby makes the
annexed [Dakota Tipi Band Council Method of Election
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-303).
:::

## [Method of Election]{.HTitleText1} {#h-694532 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The chief and the
councillors of the Dakota Tipi band shall be elected by a majority of
the votes of the electors of the band.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-694534 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
