## Order Acknowledging Receipt of the Assessment Done Pursuant to Subsection 23(1) of the Act {#order-acknowledging-receipt-of-the-assessment-done-pursuant-to-subsection-231-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2021-32
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2021-07-07

Order Acknowledging Receipt of the Assessment Done Pursuant to
Subsection 23(1) of the Act

P.C. 2021-596 2021-06-17
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Administrator of the Government of Canada in Council,
on the recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, acknowledges
receipt, on the making of this Order, of the assessment done pursuant to
subsection 23(1) of the [Species at Risk
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4472-E_hq_26007){#fn_81000-2-4472-E_hq_26007-ID0EBBAA}
by the Committee on the Status of Endangered Wildlife in Canada
(COSEWIC) with respect to the species set out in the annexed schedule.
