## Letter Mail Regulations {#letter-mail-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-430
:::

[CANADA POST CORPORATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10)

Registration 1988-08-25

Regulations Respecting Letter Mail

P.C. 1988-1697 1988-08-25
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas a copy of proposed Regulations respecting first class mail,
substantially in the form annexed hereto, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on May 14, 1988 and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations to the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs
with respect thereto;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs,
pursuant to subsection 17(1) of the [Canada Post Corporation
Act](/eng/acts/C-10)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-88-430Eng_hq_7591){#fn_SOR-88-430Eng_hq_7591-ID0EFBAA},
is pleased hereby to approve the revocation of the Domestic First Class
Mail Regulations, C.R.C., c. 1278, and to approve the annexed
Regulations respecting first class mail, made by the Canada Post
Corporation, effective January 1, 1989.

## [General Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-912988 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  item of letter mail shall be securely wrapped with sufficient
  cushioning and reinforcing material as required to prevent loss of or
  damage to the item, damage to postal equipment or other mail and
  injury to persons handling the item.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every envelope or self-mailer shall be sealed except
  an item referred to in item 2 of the schedule.

## [Standard Mail]{.HTitleText1} {#h-912999 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following articles shall not be enclosed in an item of standard mail,
  namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} bottle caps;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} loose coins;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} foodstuffs;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} glass;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} jewellery;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} keys;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} liquids;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} pencils;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} pens;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} powders;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} seeds; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} any other articles that are fragile or perishable,
    can soil or damage other mail or postal equipment or can cause
    injury to persons handling them.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An item of mailable matter shall not be accepted for
  transmission by post as standard mail if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a staple is attached to the outer cover;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it has a pin-feed hole strip attached thereto when
    in the form of a self-mailer; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it consists of a punched or perforated card that is
    not enclosed in an envelope.

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  item of standard mail bearing a postage meter impression shall bear
  the address of the addressee, including the applicable postal code for
  that address.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the address of the
  addressee shall appear on the front of every item of standard mail and
  be located not less than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 35 mm from the top edge;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 19 mm from the bottom edge; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 15 mm from the left and right edges.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (2)(b) shall not apply in respect of an
  item of standard mail that bears a postage stamp other than a postage
  meter impression.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The postal code on an item referred to in
  subsection (1) shall be located on the last line of the address,
  either alone or separated from the preceding character by at least two
  spaces.

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the envelope of an item of standard mail has an address window, the
  address window shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} rectangular in shape with the greatest dimension of
    the window parallel to the length of the envelope;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} covered with transparent material that allows the
    address to be easily read by a person or an optical character
    reader; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2009-287, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} located not less than 15 mm from the top edge,
    19 mm from the bottom edge, 12 mm from the left edge and 15 mm from
    the right edge of the item.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The complete address of the addressee shall be
  clearly visible through the address window of an item referred to in
  subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An auxiliary window on an envelope referred to in
  subsection (1) shall be covered with transparent material and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where it is located on the front of the envelope,
    shall be located not less than 19 mm from the bottom edge, 12 mm
    from the left edge and 15 mm from the right edge and shall be
    outside the area prescribed in subsection 13(1) for the postage
    meter impression; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where it is located on the back of the envelope,
    shall be located not less than 15 mm from the top and bottom edges
    and 15 mm from the left and right edges.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No more than one auxiliary window may be located on
  the front, and no more than one auxiliary window may be located on the
  back, of an envelope referred to in subsection (1).

## [Size and Weight]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913068 .Part}

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  item of letter mail shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exceed 380 mm in length, 270 mm in width or 20 mm

    0.18 mm in thickness.

  length, 156 mm in width or 5 mm in thickness.
