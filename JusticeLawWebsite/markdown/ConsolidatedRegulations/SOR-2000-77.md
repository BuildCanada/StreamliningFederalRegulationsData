## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664610 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Sector Pension
    Investment Board Act](/eng/acts/P-31.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Canadian corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2001-463, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[Canadian resource property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian resource property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
    meaning as in paragraph (c) of the definition [[Canadian resource
    property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 66(15) of the [Income
    Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([avoir minier canadien]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the child of, or an individual
    adopted either legally or in fact by, a person or the person\'s
    spouse or common-law partner. ([enfant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an
    individual, means a person who is cohabiting in a relationship of a
    conjugal nature with the individual, having so cohabited for a
    period of at least one year at the relevant time. ([conjoint de
    fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[debt obligation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[debt obligation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bond, debenture,
    note or other evidence of indebtedness of an entity. ([titre de
    créance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[derivative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[derivative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a financial instrument or
    agreement whose value is derived from or is based on an underlying
    asset or the relationship between two underlying assets, other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a convertible security;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an asset-backed security;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a security of a mutual fund;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an index participation unit;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a security of a non-redeemable fund;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a government or corporate strip bond; or

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a listed dividend share of a subdivided equity or
      fixed income security. ([instrument dérivé]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a bank;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a body corporate to which the [Trust and Loan
      Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) applies;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a cooperative credit society to which the
      [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01) applies;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an insurance company to which the [Insurance
      Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a trust, loan or insurance corporation
      incorporated by or under an Act of the legislature of a province;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a cooperative credit society incorporated and
      regulated by or under an Act of the legislature of a province;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} an entity that is incorporated or formed by or
      under an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province and
      that is primarily engaged in dealing in securities, including
      portfolio management and investment counselling; or

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a foreign institution. ([institution
      financière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[foreign institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foreign institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity that is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} engaged in the business of banking, the trust,
      loan or insurance business, the business of a cooperative credit
      society or the business of dealing in securities or is engaged
      primarily in the business of providing financial services; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} incorporated or formed otherwise than by or under
      an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province.
      ([institution étrangère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[investment corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[investment corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation
    that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} holds at least 98 per cent of its assets in cash,
      investments and loans;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not issue debt obligations; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} obtains at least 98 per cent of its income from
      investments and loans. ([société de placement]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a deposit, financial lease,
    conditional sales contract, repurchase agreement and any other
    similar arrangement for obtaining money or credit, but does not
    include investments in securities or the making of an acceptance,
    endorsement or other guarantee. ([prêt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[market terms and conditions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[market terms and conditions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
    transaction, means terms and conditions, including those relating to
    price, rent or interest rate, that would apply to a similar
    transaction in an open market under conditions requisite to a fair
    transaction between parties who are at arm\'s length and acting
    prudently, knowledgeably and willingly. ([conditions du
    marché]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an entity.
    ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[public exchange]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public exchange]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a market for the
    trading of securities that is recognized by an appropriate
    governmental authority. ([bourse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[real estate corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[real estate corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation
    incorporated to acquire, hold, maintain, improve, lease or manage
    real or immovable property other than real property that yields
    petroleum or natural gas. ([société immobilière)]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[real or immovable property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[real or immovable property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a
    leasehold interest in real or immovable property. ([biens immeubles
    ou réels]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[real property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[real property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2005-416, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[related party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[related party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a director, officer or employee of the Board;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person responsible for holding or investing the
      assets of the Board, or any director, officer or employee of the
      person;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a contributor within the meaning of subsection
      2(1) of the [Canadian Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17),
      subsection 3(1) of the [Public Service Superannuation
      Act](/eng/acts/P-36) or subsection 3(1) of the [Royal Canadian
      Mounted Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11) or a
      participant or former participant in the Reserve Force Pension
      Plan established by the [Reserve Force Pension Plan
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-32);

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a survivor within the meaning of subsection 2(1)
      of the [Canadian Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17),
      paragraph 36(1)(b) of the [Reserve Force Pension Plan
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-32), subsection 3(1) of the
      [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) or subsection
      3(1) of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation
      Act](/eng/acts/R-11);

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an association or union representing a person
      referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to (d), or a director, an
      officer or an employee of that association or union;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the spouse, common-law partner or child of any
      person referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to (e) or the spouse
      or common-law partner of that child;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that is directly or indirectly
      controlled by a person referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to
      (f); or

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} an entity in which a person referred to in any of
      paragraphs (a) to (f) has a substantial investment.

    It does not include Her Majesty in right of Canada or of a province,
    or an agency of either one, or a trust company or financial
    institution that holds the assets of the Board.
    ([apparenté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[resource corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[resource corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation
    incorporated to acquire, hold, explore, develop, maintain, improve,
    manage, operate or dispose of Canadian resource properties.
    ([société minière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a corporation, a share of any class
      of shares of the corporation or a debt obligation of the
      corporation, and includes a warrant of the corporation, but does
      not include a deposit with a financial institution or an
      instrument evidencing such a deposit; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any other entity, any ownership
      interest in, or debt obligation of, the entity. ([titre ou valeur
      mobilière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a contract;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a guarantee;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an investment;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the taking of an assignment of, or otherwise
      acquiring, a loan made by a third party;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the taking of a security interest in securities;
      and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any modification, renewal or extension of a prior
      transaction. ([transaction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[underlying asset]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[underlying asset]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a financial
    instrument, security, commodity, currency, interest rate, foreign
    exchange rate, economic indicator, index, basket, agreement,
    benchmark or any other financial reference, interest or variable.
    ([actif sous-jacent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[voting share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[voting share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a share of any class of
    shares of a corporation that carries voting rights under all
    circumstances, by reason of an event that has occurred and is
    continuing or by reason of a condition that has been fulfilled.
    ([action avec droit de vote]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of these Regulations,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person controls a corporation if securities of
    the corporation to which are attached more than 50 per cent of the
    votes that may be cast to elect the directors of the corporation are
    beneficially owned by the person and the votes attached to those
    securities are sufficient, if exercised, to elect a majority of the
    directors of the corporation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person controls an unincorporated entity, other
    than a limited partnership, if more than 50 per cent of the
    ownership interests into which the unincorporated entity is divided
    are beneficially owned by the person and the person is able to
    direct the business and affairs of the unincorporated entity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the general partner of a limited partnership
    controls the limited partnership; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a trustee of a trust controls the trust.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a person who
  controls an entity is deemed to control any other entity that is
  controlled by the entity.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-243, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, the making, holding or acquiring of an investment
indirectly by the Board, or the holding, acquiring or owning of property
indirectly by the Board, includes the making, holding, acquiring or
owning of an investment or property, as the case may be, by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an investment corporation, a real estate corporation
  or a resource corporation the securities of which have been invested
  in by the Board under subsection 13(3), (4) or (5);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an investment corporation, a real estate corporation
  or a resource corporation the securities of which are held by a
  corporation referred in paragraph (a) and to which are attached more
  than 30 per cent of the votes that may be cast to elect the directors
  of the corporation; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a mutual or pooled fund or a trust fund in which the
  assets of the Board have been invested.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-243, s. 1
- SOR/2005-416, s. 2
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, an entity is affiliated with another entity if the entity
is controlled by the other entity or if both entities are controlled by
the same person.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-243, s. 1
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, a person has a substantial investment in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an unincorporated entity if the person or an entity
  controlled by the person beneficially owns more than 25 per cent of
  the ownership interests in the unincorporated entity; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the voting rights attached to voting shares of the
    corporation that are beneficially owned by the person, or by an
    entity controlled by the person, exceed 10 per cent of the voting
    rights attached to all of the outstanding voting shares of the
    corporation, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} shares of the corporation that are beneficially
    owned by the person, or by an entity controlled by the person,
    represent ownership of more than 25 per cent of the shareholders\'
    equity of the corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-243, s. 1
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, a person is associated with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that the person controls and every
  affiliate of every such corporation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who controls the person;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a partner who has a substantial investment in a
  partnership in which the person has a substantial investment;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a trust or estate in which the person has a
  substantial investment or for which the person serves as trustee or in
  a capacity similar to that of a trustee;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the spouse or common-law partner of the person; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a brother, sister or child or other descendant of the
  person, or the spouse or common-law partner of any of them.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-243, s. 1
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraphs 6(2)(g)(ii) and 10(1)(a)(ii) of the Act, the prescribed
kind of superannuation or pension benefit is one that is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} granted under the [Judges Act](/eng/acts/J-1);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} payable under the [Governor General\'s
  Act](/eng/acts/G-9) or the [Lieutenant Governors Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/L-8); or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} payable out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund, or out
  of a superannuation or pension fund or plan pursuant to which
  contributions have been paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund in
  respect of employees engaged locally outside of Canada, and that is

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} related in amount to the period of service that may
    be counted by the person to whom the superannuation or pension
    benefit is payable, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} payable in instalments during the lifetime of the
    recipient and after his or her lifetime if the plan under which the
    superannuation or pension benefit is payable so provides.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-243, s. 1

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Investments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664716 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-664717 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** In selecting investments,
the Board shall evaluate them having regard to the overall rate of
return and risk of loss of the entire portfolio of investments held by
the Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-243, s. 1
:::

### [Statement of Investment Policies, Standards and Procedures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-664721 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  written statement of the investment policies, standards and procedures
  in respect of the Board\'s portfolio of investments, established under
  section 32 of the Act, shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} categories of investments;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the use of options, futures and other derivatives;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} diversification of the investment portfolio;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} asset mix and rate of return expectations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} policies for the management of credit, market and
    other financial risks;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} liquidity of investments;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the lending of cash or securities;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the retention or delegation of voting rights
    acquired through investments;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2001-463, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the method of, and basis for, the valuation of
    investments that are not regularly traded at a public exchange; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} related party transactions permitted under section
    17 and the criteria used to establish whether the value of a
    transaction is nominal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The statement referred to in subsection (1) shall
  include a description of all factors that may affect the funding of
  the pension plans established under the Acts referred to in paragraph
  4(1)(a) of the Act and the ability of the pension plans to meet their
  financial obligations and the relationship of those factors to those
  policies, standards and procedures.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The board of directors shall review and confirm or
  amend the statement at least once each financial year.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-243, s. 1
- SOR/2001-463, s. 2
:::

### [Quantitative Limits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-664740 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board must not directly or indirectly invest more than 10% of the
  total market value of the Board's assets in the securities of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any one person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} two or more associated persons; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} two or more affiliated corporations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of
  investments in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary of the Board;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} securities issued or fully guaranteed by the
    Government of Canada, the government of a province, or an agency of
    either of them;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} securities issued or fully guaranteed by the
    Government of the United States or any of its agencies;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a fund composed of mortgage-backed securities that
    are fully guaranteed by the Government of Canada, the government of
    a province, or an agency of either of them;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a fund that substantially replicates the
    composition of a widely recognized index of a broad class of
    securities traded on a public exchange; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a segregated fund or mutual or pooled fund that
    complies with the requirements applicable to a plan under the
    [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
    1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19).

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall not directly or indirectly invest in the securities of a
  corporation to which are attached more than 30 per cent of the votes
  that may be cast to elect the directors of the corporation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of
  investments in securities issued by a subsidiary of the Board.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of
  investments in securities issued by an investment corporation if the
  corporation

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} does not enter into a transaction --- with a
    related party, or with a person who has ceased to be a related party
    within the 12 month period preceding the transaction --- that the
    Board would be prohibited from entering into under section 16;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} limits its investments to those that are authorized
    for the Board under these Regulations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not invest, or hold an investment, in
    securities of any other investment corporation to which are attached
    more than 30 per cent of the votes that may be cast to elect the
    directors of that corporation, unless the corporation first obtains,
    and provides to the Board, an undertaking by the other investment
    corporation

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} not to enter into a transaction --- with a
      related party, or with a person who has ceased to be a related
      party within the 12 month period preceding the transaction ---
      that the Board would be prohibited from entering into under
      section 16,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} not to invest, or hold an investment, in the
      securities of any other investment corporation, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to limit its investments to those that are
      authorized for the Board under these Regulations; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} has provided to the Board an undertaking described
    in subsection (6).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of
  investments in securities issued by a real estate corporation if the
  corporation

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} limits its activities to acquiring, holding,
    maintaining, improving, leasing or managing real or immovable
    property, that is owned by, or held on behalf of, or mortgaged or
    hypothecated to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Board,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the corporation,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any other real estate corporation in which
      securities to which are attached more than 30 per cent of the
      votes that may be cast to elect the directors of that corporation
      have been invested in by, or on behalf of, the Board pursuant to
      this subsection, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any other real estate corporation in which
      securities to which are attached more than 30 per cent of the
      votes that may be cast to elect the directors of that corporation
      are owned by the corporation or by a real estate corporation
      referred to in subparagraph (iii);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} restricts its investments and loans, other than
    investments in real or immovable property or in the securities of
    other real estate corporations, to those authorized for the Board;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not enter into a transaction --- with a
    related party, or with a person who has ceased to be a related party
    within the 12 month period preceding the transaction --- that the
    Board would be prohibited from entering into under section 16;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} does not invest, or hold an investment, in
    securities of any other real estate corporation to which are
    attached more than 30 per cent of the votes that may be cast to
    elect the directors of that corporation, unless the corporation
    first obtains and deposits with the Board an undertaking by the
    other real estate corporation

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} not to enter into a transaction --- with a
      related party, or with a person who has ceased to be a related
      party within the 12 month period preceding the transaction ---
      that the Board would be prohibited from entering into under
      section 16,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} not to invest, or hold an investment, in the
      securities of any other real estate corporation; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} has provided to the Board an undertaking described
    in subsection (6).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of
  investments in securities issued by a resource corporation if the
  corporation

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} limits its activities to acquiring, holding,
    exploring, developing, maintaining, improving, managing, operating
    or disposing of Canadian resource properties that are owned by, or
    held on behalf of,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Board,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the corporation,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any other resource corporation in which
      securities to which are attached more than 30 per cent of the
      votes that may be cast to elect the directors of that corporation
      have been invested in by, or on behalf of, the Board pursuant to
      this subsection, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any other resource corporation in which
      securities to which are attached more than 30 per cent of the
      votes that may be cast to elect the directors of that corporation
      are owned by the corporation or by a resource corporation referred
      to in subparagraph (iii);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} restricts its investments and loans --- other than
    investments in Canadian resource properties, investments in property
    to be used in connection with Canadian resource properties owned by
    the corporation, loans secured by Canadian resource properties to
    persons resident in Canada for the exploration or development of
    these properties, and investments in the securities of other
    resource corporations --- to those authorized for the Board;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not borrow money other than for the purpose of
    earning income from Canadian resource properties;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} does not enter into a transaction --- with a
    related party, or with a person who has ceased to be a related party
    within the 12 month period preceding the transaction --- that the
    Board would be prohibited from entering into under section 16;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} does not invest, or hold an investment, in
    securities of any other resource corporation to which are attached
    more than 30 per cent of the votes that may be cast to elect the
    directors of that corporation, unless the corporation first obtains,
    and provides to the Board, an undertaking by the other resource
    corporation

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} not to enter into a transaction --- with a
      related party, or with a person who has ceased to be a related
      party within the 12 month period preceding the transaction ---
      that the Board would be prohibited from entering into under
      section 16,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} not to invest, or hold an investment, in the
      securities of any other resource corporation; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} has provided to the Board an undertaking described
    in subsection (6).

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} An undertaking made by an investment corporation, a
  real estate corporation or a resource corporation to the Board must
  provide that for as long as the Board directly or indirectly holds
  securities to which are attached more than 30 per cent of the votes
  that may be cast to elect its directors, the corporation will

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} file with the Board, at such intervals or times as
    the Board directs,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} copies of its annual financial statements,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} copies of its audited financial statements in
      respect of the immediately preceding 5 fiscal years, or for each
      year since the corporation was incorporated, if less than 5 years,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a list clearly identifying the assets of the
      corporation and the market value of each asset,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a list of the names of its officers, directors
      and shareholders, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a certificate stating that the corporation is
      complying with its undertaking; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} permit the Board or a person authorized by the
    Board to visit its head office and to examine its books and records.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-243, s. 1
- SOR/2005-416, s. 4
:::

- **[[13.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the Board directly or indirectly invests in the securities of a real
  estate corporation or resource corporation to which are attached more
  than 30 per cent of the votes that may be cast to elect the directors
  of the corporation, the Board shall keep, in respect of each fund that
  holds common shares of the corporation, a record of the investment
  that shows the value of the common shares that is obtained by
  multiplying

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to the assets of the corporation
    less the sum of its liabilities and its preferred capital stock

  by

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of common shares of the corporation held
    by the fund, divided by the total number of the issued and
    outstanding common shares of the corporation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall keep the record with the record of
  investments referred in paragraph 35(1)(c) of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-416, s. 5
:::

### [Related Party Transactions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-664810 .Subheading}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 16 and 17

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a transaction is entered into by, or on behalf of,
  the Board with a person who the Board, or any person acting on the
  Board\'s behalf, knows will become a related party to the Board, the
  person shall be considered to be a related party of the Board in
  respect of the transaction; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fulfilment of an obligation under the terms of
  any transaction, including the payment of interest on a loan or
  deposit, is part of the transaction and not a separate transaction.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-243, s. 1
:::

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 17
and 18, the Board shall not directly or indirectly enter into a
transaction with a related party or with a person who has ceased to be a
related party within the 12-month period preceding the transaction.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-243, s. 1
:::

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may enter into a transaction with a related party if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the transaction is required for the operation or
    administration of the Board; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the terms and conditions of the transaction are not
    less favourable to the Board than market terms and conditions.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may invest the moneys of the Board in the
  securities of a related party if those securities are acquired at a
  public exchange.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board may enter into a transaction with a related
  party if the value of the transaction is nominal.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (3), in assessing
  whether the value of a transaction is nominal, two or more
  transactions with the same related party shall be considered as a
  single transaction.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-243, s. 1
:::

### [Miscellaneous]{.HTitleText2} {#h-664828 .Subheading}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Sections 11 to 16 do not
apply in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} investments in a corporation that are held as a
  result of an arrangement, within the meaning of subsection 192(1) of
  the [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44), for the
  reorganization or liquidation of the corporation or for the
  amalgamation of the corporation with another corporation, if the
  investments are to be exchanged for shares or debt obligations; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} assets that are acquired through the realization of a
  security interest held by the Board and that are held for a period not
  exceeding two years after the day on which the assets were acquired.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-243, s. 1
- SOR/2001-463, s. 4
:::

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  moneys of the Board are to be invested

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a name that clearly indicates that the
    investment is held in trust for the Board and, where the investment
    is capable of being registered, registered in that name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the name of a financial institution, or a
    nominee of one, in accordance with a custodial agreement or trust
    agreement, entered into with the financial institution, that clearly
    indicates that the investment is held for the Board; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the name of The Canadian Depository for
    Securities Limited, or a nominee of it, in accordance with a
    custodial agreement or trust agreement, entered into with a
    financial institution, that clearly indicates that the investment is
    held for the Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), \"custodial
  agreement\" means an agreement providing that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an investment made or held on behalf of the Board
    pursuant to the agreement

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} constitutes part of the Board\'s assets, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} shall not at any time constitute an asset of the
      custodian or nominee; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} records shall be maintained by the custodian that
    are sufficient to allow the ownership of any investment to be traced
    to the Board at any time.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-243, s. 1
:::

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall maintain
a current record that clearly identifies every investment held by the
Board in its name and every investment held in the name of a third party
on behalf of the Board, the name in which the investment is made and,
where appropriate, the name in which the investment is registered.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-243, s. 1

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Annual Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664849 .Part}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Part.

[[compensation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[compensation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes annual salary,
    bonuses, non-cash remuneration, termination arrangements, and
    deferred compensation, including pension benefits funded by the
    Board, for service rendered by an individual in all capacities to
    the Board and its subsidiaries. ([rémunération]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[executive officers]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[executive officers]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the five most
    highly compensated officers of the Board or its subsidiaries.
    ([principaux dirigeants]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-243, s. 1
:::

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  annual report must include a statement of the corporate governance
  practices of the board of directors that sets out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its duties, objectives and mandate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its committees, their composition, mandates and
    activities;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the decisions requiring its prior approval;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the procedures in place for the assessment of its
    performance; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} its expectations in respect of the Board\'s
    management.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall include, in its annual report,
  information disclosing the total compensation awarded to, earned by,
  or paid to the directors and executive officers during the financial
  year.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The annual report must include a summary of the
  procedures established under paragraph 7(2)(d) of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-243, s. 1
:::
