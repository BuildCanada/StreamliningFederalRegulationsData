Rainy River First Nations Settlement Agreement Remission Order

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613257 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Order.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/E-15). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[additional land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[additional land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      paragraph 1.01(b) of the settlement agreement. ([terre
      nouvelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Rainy River]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Rainy River]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Rainy River First
      Nations, which is a band as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
      [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([Rainy River]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[related supply]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[related supply]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, as the case may be,
      a supply or cost described in subparagraph 2(a)(ii), (iii) or (iv)
      or a corresponding supply or cost in relation to a supply of
      replacement land. ([fourniture connexe]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[replacement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[replacement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in paragraph 16.01(c) of the settlement agreement. ([terre de
      remplace- ment]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Indian
      Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([réserve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[settlement agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[settlement agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the settlement
      agreement that was entered into by Her Majesty the Queen in right
      of Canada, Her Majesty the Queen in right of Ontario and Rainy
      River and that became effective on May 6, 2005. ([accord de
      règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other words and expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any word or expression used in this Order and not
  defined in subsection (1) has the same meaning as in subsection 123(1)
  of the Act.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613270 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional land

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to Rainy River of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax paid or payable under Part IX of the Act by
  Rainy River or its agent in respect of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the supply to Rainy River or its agent of
    additional land so long as the cumulative total of additional land
    supplied to Rainy River does not exceed the quantity of additional
    land that Rainy River is entitled to request to be set apart as
    reserve in accordance with section 4.01 of the settlement agreement,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the supply by way of sale to Rainy River or its
    agent of tangible personal property that is collateral to a supply
    of additional land referred to in subparagraph (i),

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the supply to or cancellation in favour of Rainy
    River or its agent of a third party interest in additional land
    referred to in subparagraph (i) if the third party interest is one
    that is discharged, released, extinguished, cancelled or acquired in
    accordance with paragraph 4.40(a) of the settlement agreement, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any costs incurred by Rainy River or its agent in
    the context of a transaction described in any of subparagraphs (i)
    to (iii); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any interest and penalties paid or payable by Rainy
  River or its agent under Part IX of the Act in respect of any
  transaction described in any of subparagraphs (a)(i) to (iii) or a
  cost referred to in subparagraph (a)(iv).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacement land

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  additional land is replaced by replacement land, remission is hereby
  granted to Rainy River of the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#613282 .Subsection}
  A - B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the tax and any related interest or penalties
      paid or payable under Part IX of the Act by Rainy River or its
      agent in respect of the supply to Rainy River or its agent of the
      replacement land and any related supply; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the tax and any related interest or penalties
      that were remitted under section 2 in respect of the additional
      land and any related supply.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reduction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount that may be remitted under section 2 or
  subsection (1) in respect of any other additional land or replacement
  land for which there has been no remission shall be reduced by the
  absolute value of the amount determined under subsection (1) if that
  amount is less than zero.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of land

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** When additional land is
replaced by replacement land, the additional land that is replaced is
excluded, and the replacement land is included, for the purpose of
calculating the cumulative total of additional land referred to in
subparagraph 2(a)(i).

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613290 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission under this Order is
  granted on condition that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax, interest or penalties have not otherwise
    been rebated, credited, refunded or remitted to any person under the
    Act or the Financial Administration Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Department of Indian Affairs and Northern
    Development confirms in writing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the claim for remission is being made with
      respect to a quantity of additional land or of replacement land,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the claim is in respect of replacement land,
      the legal description of the additional land that is being
      replaced.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application deadlines

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A claim for remission is to be made in writing to the
  Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the claim is related to additional land,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an amount of tax, interest or
      penalty that was paid, or became due, before the date of the
      coming into force of this Order, within two years after that date,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an amount of tax, interest or
      penalty that was paid without having become due, or became due, on
      or after the date of the coming into force of this Order, within
      two years after the day on which the amount was paid or became
      due; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the claim is related to replacement land, within
    two years after the later of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the replacement land is set
      apart as reserve, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the amount of tax, penalty or
      interest that is the subject of the claim for remission becomes
      due or is paid without having become due.
