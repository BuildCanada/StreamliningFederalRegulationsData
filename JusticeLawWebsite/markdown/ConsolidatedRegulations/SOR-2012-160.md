## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-783227 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Permit.

  [[controlled]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[controlled]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means controlled in any manner
      that results in control in fact, whether directly through the
      ownership of securities or indirectly through a trust, an
      agreement or arrangement, the ownership of any corporation or
      otherwise. ([contrôlé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Argentina,
      Australia, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Czech Republic, Denmark,
      Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Japan,
      Luxembourg, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Portugal,
      Republic of Korea, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey, Ukraine,
      the United Kingdom or the United States. ([pays
      désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation, a trust, a
      partnership, a fund or an unincorporated association or
      organization. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Export Controls Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Export Controls Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Export
      Controls Division of the Department of Foreign Affairs and
      International Trade. ([Direction des contrôles à
      l'exportation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[government of a foreign country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[government of a foreign country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the government of any political subdivision of
        a foreign country;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any agent or agency of a government of a
        foreign country or its political subdivisions; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any entity controlled by a government of a
        foreign country or its political subdivisions. ([gouvernement
        étranger]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 1
      of the [Export Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202).
      ([guide]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[ineligible destination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[ineligible destination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Afghanistan,
      Belarus, Burma (Myanmar), Côte d'Ivoire, Cuba, Democratic People's
      Republic of Korea, Eritrea, Guinea, Iran, Iraq, Lebanon, Liberia,
      Libya, Pakistan, Sierra Leone, Syria, Sudan or Zimbabwe.
      ([destination inadmissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Permit, "development", "information
  security", "production", "software" and "use" have the same meaning as
  in the Guide under the heading "Definitions of Terms Used in Groups 1
  and 2".

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-783241 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 4 and 5
any resident of Canada may, under this Permit, export or transfer from
Canada to a designated country

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any good referred to in item 1-5.A.2. or 1-5.A.3. of
  the Guide, other than a good that is referred to in item

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 1-5.A.2.c., or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 1-5.A.3.b.;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any good referred to in item 1-5.B.2. of the Guide,
  other than a good that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is used in the development or production of a good
    referred to in any of subparagraphs (a)(i) to (iii) or (b)(ii), or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is specially designed to evaluate and validate the
    information security functions of a good referred to in any of
    subparagraphs (a)(i) to (iii) or (c)(i) to (iii);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any good referred to in item 1-5.D.2. of the Guide,
  other than software that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is used in the development, production or use of a
    good referred to in any of subparagraphs (a)(i) to (iii) or (c)(ii)
    or (iii),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has the characteristics of a good referred to in
    any of subparagraphs (a)(i) to (iii) or that performs or simulates
    the functions of any of those goods,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} certifies software referred to in subparagraph
    (ii); and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} is specially designed or modified to support
    technology used in the development, production or use of a good
    referred to in any of subparagraphs (a)(i) to (iii), (b)(i) or (ii)
    or (c)(i) to (iii).

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any technology referred to in item 1-5.E.2. of the
  Guide, other than a technology that is used in the development,
  production or use of a good referred to in any of subparagraphs (a)(i)
  to (iii), (b)(i) or (ii) or (c)(i) to (iii).

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 4 and
5, a good or technology that may be exported or transferred under
section 2 may be exported or transferred by a resident of Canada under
this Permit to any country, other than a country that is an ineligible
destination, if the export or transfer is made to a consignee that is
controlled by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a resident of Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an individual, other than an individual acting on
  behalf of a foreign country, who ordinarily resides in a designated
  country; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an entity, other than the government of a foreign
  country, that has its head office in a designated country.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Permit does not authorize export or transfer to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a country that is an ineligible destination;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a country that is listed in the [Area Control
    List](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-543);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a country in respect of which there is an order or
    regulation made under section 4 of the [Special Economic Measures
    Act](/eng/acts/S-14.5) or section 2 of the [United Nations
    Act](/eng/acts/U-2); or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a government of a foreign country.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Permit does not authorize the export or transfer
  of a good or technology that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is intended for any use other than the development
    or production of a product;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is used in the development or production of a
    product that is being developed for, or under contract with, the
    Government of Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is used in the development or production of a
    product that is intended for the exclusive use of a government of a
    foreign country; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is also referred to in any item of the Guide other
    than the items referred to in section 2, unless the export or
    transfer of the goods or technology is also authorized by another
    export permit issued under subsection 7(1.1) of the [Export and
    Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19).

## [Other Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-783271 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A person who exports or
transfers under this Permit must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide the following information in writing to the
  Export Controls Division before making their first export or transfer
  in a calendar year under this Permit:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their name, address, telephone number, facsimile
    number and email address,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the person that exports or transfers is a
    corporation, the business number assigned to the corporation by the
    Minister of National Revenue and the name of a contact person and
    their address, telephone number, facsimile number and email address,
    and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the product whose production or
    development will be facilitated by the export or transfer;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} insert the term "GEP-45" or "LGE-45" in the
  appropriate field of the form prescribed under the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) if the good exported is required to be reported
  under that Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide to the Export Controls Division, within 15
  days after receipt of its request, the records referred to in
  paragraph (e) in respect of any export or transfer made during the
  period specified in the request;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide to the Export Controls Division, before
  January 31, the records referred to in subparagraphs (e)(ii) and (iii)
  in respect of any export or transfer made during the previous calendar
  year; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} retain the following records for a period of six
  years after the year in which any export or transfer is made:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the item number of the Guide which describes the
    good or technology,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name and any version number used to identify
    the good or technology,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the product whose development or
    production is facilitated by the export or transfer, including the
    name and any version number used to identify the product,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the estimated value of the export or transfer and,
    in the case of a good or technology that can be quantified, the
    quantity of the export or transfer,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the following information in respect of the good or
    technology:

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} their cryptographic algorithms and the key sizes
      employed,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the function performed by the cryptographic
      algorithms, and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the keys are generated and
      managed,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the date of the export or transfer, if it is known
    to the person who exports or transfers, and

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the consignee's name, address, telephone number,
    facsimile number and email address.
