A Proclamation

Whereas, by Order in Council P.C. 2000-1351 of August 23, 2000, the
Governor in Council declared that, in accordance with Article XVIII of
the Interim Agreement on Social Security between the Government of
Canada and the Government of Israel, signed on April 9, 2000, the
Agreement shall enter into force on the first day of the fourth month
following the month in which each Party shall have received from the
other Party written notification that it has complied with all
requirements for the entry into force of the Agreement;

Whereas that Order in Council was laid before Parliament on October 16,
2000 and, as Parliament was prorogued before the Order was tabled for 30
sitting days, as required under the provisions of the [Old Age Security
Act](/eng/acts/O-9), the Order was again laid before Parliament on
February 12, 2001;

Whereas, before the twentieth sitting day after the Order had been laid
before Parliament, no motion for the consideration of either House to
the effect that the Order be revoked was filed with the Speaker of the
appropriate House;

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 42(2) of the [Old Age Security
Act](/eng/acts/O-9), the Order came into force on the thirtieth sitting
day after it had again been laid before Parliament, being April 2, 2001;

Whereas the exchange of written notification was completed on May 27,
2003;

Whereas the Agreement shall enter into force on the first day of the
fourth month following the month in which the Parties exchanged written
notices, being September 1, 2003;

And whereas, by Order in Council P.C. 2003-1226 of August 13, 2003, the
Governor in Council directed that a proclamation do issue giving notice
that the Interim Agreement on Social Security between the Government of
Canada and the Government of Israel, a copy of which is annexed to this
Order, shall enter into force on September 1, 2003;

Now Know You that We, by and with the advice of Our Privy Council for
Canada, do by this Our Proclamation give notice that the Interim
Agreement on Social Security between the Government of Canada and the
Government of Israel, signed on April 9, 2000, a copy of which is
annexed hereto, shall enter into force on September 1, 2003.

Of All Which Our Loving Subjects and all others whom these Presents may
concern are hereby required to take notice and to govern themselves
accordingly.

In Testimony Whereof, We have caused this Our Proclamation to be
published and the Great Seal of Canada to be hereunto affixed. Witness:
The Honourable J.E. Michel Bastarache, a Puisne Judge of the Supreme
Court of Canada and Deputy of Our Right Trusty and Well-beloved Adrienne
Clarkson, Chancellor and Principal Companion of Our Order of Canada,
Chancellor and Commander of Our Order of Military Merit, Chancellor and
Commander of Our Order of Merit of the Police Forces, Governor General
and Commander-in-Chief of Canada.

At Ottawa, this twenty-fifth day of August in the year of Our Lord two
thousand and three and in the fifty-second year of Our Reign.

By Command,

::: FlushRight
JACQUELINE GRAVELLE
:::

::: FlushRight
*Deputy Registrar General of Canada*

### [Interim Agreement on Social Security Between the Government of Canada and the Government of Israel]{.HTitleText1} {#interim-agreement-on-social-security-between-the-government-of-canada-and-the-government-of-israel .SchedHeadL1}

***THE GOVERNMENT OF CANADA***

***AND***

***THE GOVERNMENT OF ISRAEL,***

***hereinafter referred to as "the Parties",***

***RESOLVED to co-operate in the field of social security,***

***DESIRING, as a first stage in that co-operation, to regulate
questions regarding liability for contributions,***

***HAVE AGREED AS FOLLOWS:***

### [**PART I**]{.HLabel1}[General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#part-igeneral-provisions .SchedHeadL1}

#### [ARTICLE I]{.HLabel2}[**Definitions**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-idefinitions .SchedHeadL2}

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  For the purposes of this Agreement:
  :::
  :::::

  [[competent authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[competent authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, as regards
      Canada, the Minister or Ministers responsible for the application
      of the legislation of Canada; and, as regards Israel, the Minister
      of Labour and Social Affairs;

  [[competent institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[competent institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, as regards
      Canada, the competent authority; and, as regards Israel, the
      National Insurance Institute;

  [[legislation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[legislation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, as regards a Party, the
      laws and regulations specified in Article II with respect to that
      Party;

  [[liaison agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[liaison agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agency which is
      responsible for co-ordination and the exchange of information
      between the competent institutions of the Parties, and which is
      involved in the application of this Agreement and informing the
      persons concerned of the rights and obligations stemming from it.

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  Any term not defined in this Article has the meaning assigned to it in
  the applicable legislation.
  :::
  :::::

Subject to this Agreement,

  ::: listItemLabel
  [(a)]{.lawlabel}

  ::: listItemText2

### [**PART II**]{.HLabel1}[Provisions Concerning the Applicable Legislation]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiprovisions-concerning-the-applicable-legislation .SchedHeadL1}

#### [ARTICLE IV]{.HLabel2}[**General Rule**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-ivgeneral-rule .SchedHeadL2}

Subject to the provisions of Articles V to X:

- ::::: listItemBlock2
  ::: listItemLabel
  [(a)]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText2
  an employed person who works in the territory of a Party shall, in
  respect of that work, be subject only to the legislation of that
  Party;
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock2
  ::: listItemLabel
  [(b)]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText2
  a self-employed person who ordinarily resides in the territory of a
  Party and who works for his or her own account in the territory of the
  other Party or in the territories of both Parties shall, in respect of
  that work, be subject only to the legislation of the first Party;
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock2
  ::: listItemLabel
  [(c)]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText2
  a person who is neither an employed person nor a self-employed person
  and who resides in the territory of a Party shall be subject only to
  the legislation of that Party.
  :::
  :::::

#### [ARTICLE V]{.HLabel2}[**Detached Workers**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-vdetached-workers .SchedHeadL2}

An employed person who is subject to the legislation of a Party and who
is sent by his or her employer to work in the territory of the other
Party for the same or a related employer shall, in respect of that work,
be subject only to the legislation of the first Party as though that
work was performed in its territory. In the case of an assignment, this
coverage may not be maintained for more than 60 months without the prior
consent of the competent institutions of the Parties.

#### [ARTICLE VI]{.HLabel2}[**Crew of Aircrafts**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-vicrew-of-aircrafts .SchedHeadL2}

A person who is employed in the flight or cabin crew of an airline and
who works in the territories of both Parties shall, in respect of that
work, be subject only to the legislation of the Party in whose territory
the airline has its head office. If, however, the person resides in the
territory of the other Party, he or she shall be subject only to the
legislation of the other Party.

#### [ARTICLE VII]{.HLabel2}[**Persons in Government Employment and Other Public Employees**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-viipersons-in-government-employment-and-other-public-employees .SchedHeadL2}

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  This Agreement shall not affect the provisions of the Vienna
  Convention on Diplomatic Relations of 18 April 1961 or the general
  principles of customary international law regarding consular
  privileges and immunities.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  Article V shall apply without limitation in time to persons in
  government employment and other public employees of a Party, other
  than those to whom paragraph 1 applies, who are assigned in the course
  of that employment to work in the territory of the other Party.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [3]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  An employed person, other than a person to whom paragraph 1 or 2
  applies, shall, in respect of his or her duties performed in the
  territory of a Party in government employment for the other Party, be
  subject only to the legislation of the first Party if he or she is a
  citizen thereof or resides in its territory.
  :::
  :::::

#### [ARTICLE VIII]{.HLabel2}[**Exception Provision**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-viiiexception-provision .SchedHeadL2}

The competent authority of Canada and the competent institution of
Israel may agree on further exceptions to the provisions of Articles IV
to VII in the interest of certain persons or categories of persons.

#### [ARTICLE IX]{.HLabel2}[**Definition of Certain Periods of Residence with Respect to the Legislation of Canada**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-ixdefinition-of-certain-periods-of-residence-with-respect-to-the-legislation-of-canada .SchedHeadL2}

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  For the purpose of calculating the amount of benefits under the [Old
  Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9):
  :::
  :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock1
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(a)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText2
    if a person is subject to the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8)
    or to the comprehensive pension plan of a province of Canada during
    any period of presence or residence in Israel, that period shall be
    considered as a period of residence in Canada for that person as
    well as for that person's spouse and dependants who live with him or
    her and who are not subject to the legislation of Israel by reason
    of employment or self-employment.
    :::
    :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock1
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(b)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText2
    if a person is subject to the legislation of Israel during any
    period of presence or residence in Canada, that period shall not be
    considered as a period of residence in Canada for that person and
    for that person's spouse and dependants who live with him or her and
    who are not subject to the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or
    to the comprehensive pension plan of a province of Canada by reason
    of employment or self-employment.
    :::
    :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  In the application of paragraph 1:
  :::
  :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock1
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(a)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText2
    a person shall be considered to be subject to the [Canada Pension
    Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or to the comprehensive pension plan of a
    province of Canada during a period of presence or residence in
    Israel only if that person makes contributions pursuant to the plan
    concerned during that period by reason of employment or
    self-employment.
    :::
    :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock1
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(b)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText2
    a person shall be considered to be subject to the legislation of
    Israel during a period of presence or residence in Canada only if
    that person makes compulsory contributions pursuant to that
    legislation during that period by reason of employment or
    self-employment.
    :::
    :::::

#### [ARTICLE X]{.HLabel2}[**Definition of Certain Periods of Residence with Respect to the Legislation of Israel**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-xdefinition-of-certain-periods-of-residence-with-respect-to-the-legislation-of-israel .SchedHeadL2}

A person who is subject to the legislation of Israel as a result of the
application of Articles IV to VIII shall, as well as that person's
spouse who resides with him and who is not subject to the [Canada
Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or to the comprehensive pension plan of a
province of Canada by reason of employment or self-employment, be
considered to be resident in Israel.

  ::: listItemText1

  :::

  :::
  :::::

#### [ARTICLE XVI]{.HLabel2}[**Understandings with a Province of Canada**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-xviunderstandings-with-a-province-of-canada .SchedHeadL2}

understandings concerning any social security matter within provincial
jurisdiction in Canada in so far as those understandings are not
inconsistent with the provisions of this Agreement.

### [**PART IV**]{.HLabel1} {#part-iv .SchedHeadL1}

#### [ARTICLE XVII]{.HLabel2}[**Duration and Termination**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-xviiduration-and-termination .SchedHeadL2}

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  This Agreement shall remain in force without any limitation on its
  duration. It may be terminated at any time by either Party giving 12
  months' notice in writing to the other Party.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  In the event of the termination of this Agreement, any right acquired
  by a person in accordance with its provisions shall be maintained and
  negotiations shall take place for the settlement of any rights then in
  course of acquisition by virtue of those provisions.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [3]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  The Agreement between the Government of Canada and the Government of
  Israel relative to the Canada Pension Plan signed at Ottawa on 1
  December 1980 shall not apply in regard to periods after the date of
  entry into force of this Agreement.
  :::
  :::::

#### [ARTICLE XVIII]{.HLabel2}[**Entry into Force**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-xviiientry-into-force .SchedHeadL2}

This Agreement shall enter into force on the first day of the fourth
month following the month in which each Party shall have received from
the other Party written notification that it has complied with all
requirements for the entry into force of this Agreement.

***IN WITNESS WHEREOF,** the undersigned, being duly authorized thereto
by their respective Governments, have signed this Agreement.*

***DONE** in duplicate at Jerusalem, this 9th day of April, 2000, which
corresponds to the 4th day of Nissan 5760, in the English, French and
Hebrew languages, each text being equally authentic.*
