## Alberta Egg Marketing Levies Order {#alberta-egg-marketing-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 129
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Levies From
Certain Egg Producers in Alberta
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502319 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Alberta Egg Marketing Levies
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._129).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502321 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Marketing of Agricultural
    Products Act of Alberta; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Alberta Egg and
    Fowl Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[egg]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[egg]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the egg of a hen produced in
    Alberta and includes the egg product of a hen produced and prepared
    in Alberta; ([oeuf]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the hen of any class of the
    domestic chicken belonging to the species [Gallus
    Domesticus]{.otherLang lang="la"}; ([poule]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    eggs established and amended from time to time pursuant to the Act;
    ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who grades,
    packs, transforms and who markets, stores and transports eggs;
    ([transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person engaged in the
    production of eggs in Alberta; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[producer grader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer grader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any producer who
    grades, packs and markets his own eggs.
    ([producteur-classeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502331 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every producer or producer
grader shall pay to the Commodity Board, in addition to the service
charges set out under the Plan, levies at the rate of \$0.005 per dozen
eggs sold by him.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  processor who buys eggs from a producer shall deduct from the moneys
  payable to the producer for the eggs, the levies payable under section
  3, and shall pay and account to the Commodity Board for the said
  levies not later than Wednesday of the week following the week within
  which the eggs were bought.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a producer or a producer grader sells eggs to
  any person other than a processor, he shall pay to the Commodity Board
  the levies payable under section 3 not later than Wednesday of the
  week following the week in which the eggs were sold.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  accounting by the processor to the Commodity Board required by
  subsection 4(1) shall be in a form determined by the Commodity Board
  from time to time.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The levies, from the time of their deduction to the
  time of accounting and payment to the Commodity Board, shall be
  segregated as funds held in trust for the Commodity Board.
