## Order Transferring to the Department of Transport Certain Portions of the Public Service in the Department of Fisheries and Oceans and of the Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans under certain paragraphs of the Canada Shipping Act {#order-transferring-to-the-department-of-transport-certain-portions-of-the-public-service-in-the-department-of-fisheries-and-oceans-and-of-the-powers-duties-and-functions-of-the-minister-of-fisheries-and-oceans-under-certain-paragraphs-of-the-canada-shipping-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2004-35
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2004-04-07

Order Transferring to the Department of Transport Certain Portions of
the Public Service in the Department of Fisheries and Oceans and of the
Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans
under certain paragraphs of the Canada Shipping Act

P.C. 2004-322  2004-03-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} transfers to the Department of Transport the control
  and supervision of the following portions of the public service in the
  Department of Fisheries and Oceans:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Office of Boating Safety,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Canada Shipping Act 2001 Regulatory Reform
    Team,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Navigable Waters Protection Division, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the Environmental Response Division except for the
    portion of that group responsible for carrying out the powers,
    duties and functions of the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans under
    section 678 of the [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} transfers from the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans
  to the Minister of Transport the powers, duties and functions of the
  Minister of Fisheries and Oceans under paragraphs 519(a), (b) and (d)
  and sections 562.15 and 562.16 of the [Canada Shipping
  Act](/eng/acts/S-9).
