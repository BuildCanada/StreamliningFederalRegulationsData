## Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Department of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness the Control and Supervision of the Emergency Preparedness Secretariat {#order-transferring-from-the-privy-council-office-to-the-department-of-public-safety-and-emergency-preparedness-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-emergency-preparedness-secretariat .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2024-57
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2024-11-20

Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Department of
Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness the Control and Supervision of
the Emergency Preparedness Secretariat

P.C. 2024-1182 2024-11-08
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2784_hq_1729277451){#fn_81000-3-2784_hq_1729277451-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2784_hq_1729277469){#fn_81000-3-2784_hq_1729277469-ID0EBCAA},
transfers from the Privy Council Office to the Department of Public
Safety and Emergency Preparedness the control and supervision of that
portion of the federal public administration in the Privy Council Office
known as the Emergency Preparedness Secretariat, effective November 21,
2024.
