Act](/eng/acts/S-15.36)[[Footnote

for the making of rules governing the practice and procedures of the
Tribunal;

12(1) of the [Specific Claims Tribunal Act](/eng/acts/S-15.36) hereby

Procedure](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-119).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771934 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Rules.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Specific Claims Tribunal
    Act](/eng/acts/S-15.36). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the superior court judge
    who is appointed as the Chairperson of the Tribunal under subsection
    6(3) of the Act. ([président]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[file]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[file]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to file with the
    registrar. ([déposer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[legal counsel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[legal counsel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is a member
    of a provincial or territorial Bar. ([conseiller
    juridique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[letter size paper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[letter size paper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means paper that is
    21.5 cm by 28 cm (8½ in. by 11 in.). ([papier format
    lettre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the registrar referred to in
    subsection 10(2) of the Act. ([greffier]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a party,
    a First Nation or a person, their legal counsel or their
    representative permitted under subrule
    7(2). ([représentant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General Rules]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771943 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-771944 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General principle

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Rules must be
interpreted and applied so as to secure the just, timely and
cost-effective resolution of specific claims while taking the cultural
diversity and the distinctive character of specific claims into account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may make any
order that is necessary to secure the just, timely or cost-effective
resolution of the specific claim.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Varying , dispensing with and
supplementing Rules

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may vary a Rule, dispense with compliance with a Rule, or
  supplement a Rule, when the Tribunal considers it is necessary to do
  so in order to secure the just, timely or cost-effective resolution of
  the specific claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions --- technological problems

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a document is required by these Rules to be filed
  or served by email, and technological problems arise that prevent that
  from occurring, the Tribunal may provide directions, as necessary,
  regarding the filing or service of that document in the circumstances.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matters not provided for

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may provide
for any matter of practice or procedure not provided for in these Rules
by analogy to the [Federal Courts Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106).

### [Time limits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-771956 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation Act

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A time limit set out in
these Rules, or in an order of the Tribunal, must be determined in
accordance with sections 26 to 29 of the [Interpretation
Act](/eng/acts/I-21), except that Saturday is also considered to be a
holiday.

### [Representation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-771959 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal counsel required

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subrules (2) and (3), all parties, First Nations and persons must
  be represented in the proceedings by legal counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- First Nations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may, in special circumstances, grant
  leave for a claimant or other First Nation to be represented in the
  proceedings by someone who is not legal counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- individuals

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An individual is not required to be represented in
  the proceedings by legal counsel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ceasing representation

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A person ceases to be a
representative under these Rules when they file written notice to that
effect and serve it on each party.

### [Order and Stages of Hearings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-771969 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of hearings

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Chairperson may
determine the order in which specific claims are to be heard without
regard to the order in which they were filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing in stages

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** If validity of the
specific claim and any compensation arising from it are both at issue,
the Chairperson may order that the hearing of those matters proceed in
separate stages.

### [Adjournments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-771974 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ajournment

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A party who seeks to
adjourn all or part of the proceedings must make an application
requesting that relief.

### [Undertakings and Agreements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-771977 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}In writing

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** All undertakings given by
a party and all agreements entered into between parties, must be made in
writing.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Filing of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771980 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-771981 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registry file

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** For each specific claim,
the registrar must create and maintain a file that contains a paper copy
of the declaration of claim, the response and any application filed in
relation to the claim.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of documents

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Documents that are being
filed must be accompanied by a list that identifies each document and
provides a brief description for each of them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing after 17:00

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A document that is filed
by email or fax after 17:00 Ottawa local time is deemed to have been
filed on the next day that is not a holiday.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed original

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A document that is filed
by email or fax is deemed to be the original of that document.

### [Electronic Filing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-771990 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to Rule 18, parties and
  intervenors must file their documents by email.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document must be filed in PDF (Portable Document
  Format) or any other electronic format that allows the document to be
  converted for printing on letter size paper.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sworn Statements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a sworn statement is filed by email, the person
  filing it must retain the original paper copy of that sworn statement
  and, if requested, provide it to the Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time stamp

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The registrar must apply a stamp, indicating the date
  and time of filing, to each document that is filed.

### [Alternate Filing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772000 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Leave required

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may grant
leave for a document to be filed in paper or by fax if doing so would be
just, timely or cost-effective in the circumstances.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document that is filed in paper or by fax must be printed on letter
  size paper. However, a document that is being filed in paper may be
  filed in its existing format if it cannot be printed on letter size
  paper.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Page numbers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The pages of a document that is filed in paper or by
  fax must be consecutively numbered.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fax cover sheet

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** A document that is filed
by fax must have a cover sheet that sets out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the person
  filing the document;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the transmission;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total number of pages transmitted, including the
  cover page; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and telephone number of a person to be
  contacted in the event of a transmission problem.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Service of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772014 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772015 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A document must be served
personally or by email, fax or registered mail.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective on delivery

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document is served when it is delivered to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a party, the party's representative;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an intervenor, their representative,
    or if they are not represented, to them personally.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service after 17:00

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, a document that is served by email or fax
  after 17:00 at the recipient's local time is deemed to have been
  delivered on the next day that is not a holiday.

### [Service By Fax]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772025 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** A document that is served
by fax must be printed on letter size paper.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A document that is more
than 50 pages in length is not to be served by fax without the
recipient's prior consent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cover sheet

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** A document that is served
by fax must have a cover sheet that sets out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the person
  serving the document;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the transmission;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total number of pages transmitted, including the
  cover page; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and telephone number of a person to be
  contacted in the event of a transmission problem.

### [Service By Email]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772036 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** A document that is served
by email must be in PDF (Portable Document Format) or any other
electronic format that allows the document to be converted for printing
on letter size paper.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cover page

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** A document that is served
by email must be accompanied by an electronic message that sets out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of person
  serving the document;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the document that is being served;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the transmission; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, telephone number and email address of a
  person to be contacted in the event of a transmission problem.

### [Proof of Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772045 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Proof
  of the service of a document must be given by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an affidavit of service; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by the admission of the party served.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delivery receipt

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An affidavit of service for a document that was
  served by email, fax or registered mail must include a receipt
  confirming that the document was successfully delivered to the
  recipient.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772053 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772054 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to
applications referred to in the Act, these Rules and any other
application made by a party for the resolution of a procedural or
evidentiary issue by the Tribunal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Leave required

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Except for an application
referred to in the Act, subrule 60(2) or Part 11, leave of the Tribunal
is required before an application can be made to the Tribunal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** An application must be
made in writing unless the Tribunal grants leave for it to be made
orally.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presiding tribunal member

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  application and each request for leave to make an application must be
  heard by:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Tribunal member who presides over the case
    management conference, if the application or request is to be heard
    before the day on which the pre-hearing conference is to be held; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Tribunal member who presides over the hearing,
    if the application or request is to be heard on or after that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, if the Tribunal member who is required to
  hear the application or request is unavailable, the application may be
  heard by any other Tribunal member.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal member may
provide directions to the parties regarding the time and manner in which
he or she will hear their arguments and receive evidence in relation to
the application.

### [Written Applications]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772070 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of application

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** An application is made by
filing a notice of application that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sets out the relief sought by the party and the
  grounds for the application; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} indicates whether any parties have consented to the
  relief sought.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** Within 10 days after the
day on which a party is served with a notice of application, the party
may file a response that sets out their position regarding the relief
sought by the other party.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** A party who files a
notice of application or a response must serve every other party with a
copy of that document within 14 days after the day on which it is filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** A notice of application
and a response must be no more than 20 pages in length, be legible and
be printed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} single-sided on letter size paper; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in type that is 12 points or larger.

### [Ex Parte Applications]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772083 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ex parte applications

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** Rules 33 and 36 do not
apply to [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} applications.

## [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Pleadings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772086 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772087 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No evidence

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** A declaration of claim
and a response must not contain any evidence to support the facts set
out in that document.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** A party who files a
declaration of claim or a response must serve every other party with a
copy of that document within 30 days after the day on which it is filed.

### [Declaration of Claim]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772092 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** A specific claim begins
when a First Nation files a declaration of claim that sets out the
following information in consecutively numbered paragraphs:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the First Nation and identification
  of which paragraph of the definition [[First
  Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in section 2 of the Act, applies to that
  First Nation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the First
  Nation's representative, and his or her email address for the service
  of documents;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the conditions precedent to filing a claim with the
  Tribunal, set out in subsection 16(1) of the Act, that have been
  fulfilled;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the paragraphs in subsection 14(1) of the Act that
  provide the grounds for the specific claim;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a brief statement of the facts that form the basis
  for the specific claim; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statement that, for the purpose of the specific
  claim, the claimant does not seek compensation in excess of \$150
  million.

### [Response]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772101 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** Within 30 days after the
day on which the Crown is served with the declaration of claim, the
Crown must file a response that sets out the following information in
consecutively numbered paragraphs:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating whether the Minister decided
  not to negotiate the specific claim, in whole or in part and, if so,
  the date of that decision;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Crown's position with respect to the validity of
  the specific claim;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the Crown's position is that the specific claim is
  valid, which of paragraphs 20(1)(e) to (h) of the Act may provide the
  basis for the Tribunal to award compensation;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for each fact set out in the declaration of claim, a
  statement indicating whether the Crown admits, denies or has no
  knowledge of that fact;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a brief statement of the facts that are related to
  the specific claim;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the relief sought by the Crown; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} an email address for the service of documents.

## [PART 6]{.HLabel1}[Addition of Parties and Intervenors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772111 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** Notification made by the
Tribunal under subsection 22(1) of the Act must be in writing and be
served personally or by registered mail.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Late application

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** If a person makes an
application for party status, or for leave to intervene, more than 60
days after the day on which they were served with the notice under
subsection 22(1) of the Act, the Tribunal must, in deciding whether or
not to grant the application, consider whether the person took all
reasonable efforts to make the application in a timely manner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for leave to intervene

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** In addition to the
information required under Rule 34, the notice of application for an
application for leave to intervene must set out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the person
  and their representative, if any;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the manner in which they propose to
  participate in the proceedings and how their participation could
  assist the Tribunal in resolving the issues in relation to the
  specific claim;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the party, if any, whose position that
  person intends to support; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the language to be used by that person in the
  proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may provide
an intervenor with directions regarding their role in the proceedings
and the procedures to be followed, if doing so assists the just and
timely resolution of the specific claim. However, before providing
directions, the Tribunal must provide the parties with an opportunity to
make submissions regarding possible directions.

## [PART 7]{.HLabel1}[Case Management]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772124 .Part}

### [Case Management Conference]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772125 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** When a case management
conference is scheduled, the registrar must provide the parties with
notice of that conference.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attendance

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party's representative must attend each case management conference in
  person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, a party's representative may, with leave of
  the Tribunal, attend a case conference in another manner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First case management conference

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following matters must be discussed at the first case management
  conference:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the condition precedent set out in subsection 16(1)
    of the Act that gave rise to the filing of the specific claim;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Crown's position with respect to the validity
    of the specific claim;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the degree to which the parties have prepared their
    cases respecting compensation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether any of the parties are intending to enter
    oral history evidence;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} whether the interests of a province, First Nation
    or person might be significantly affected by a decision of the
    Tribunal described in subsection 22(1) of the Act;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} whether the parties are interested in pursuing a
    mediated settlement of all or part of the specific claim; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} how best to conduct the proceedings so that they
    remain proportionate to the amount in dispute and the importance and
    complexity of the issues involved.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}All case management conferences

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At every case management conference, the parties must
  be prepared to discuss any matter that may assist in the just, timely
  and cost-effective determination of an issue in relation to the
  specific claim, including:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} protocols for the entering of oral history evidence
    and expert evidence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the evidence that the parties intend
    to enter and whether any of that evidence will be oral history
    evidence, expert evidence or evidence that was not disclosed while
    the claim was filed with the Minister;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} procedural matters related to applications;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} cultural diversity matters that should be
    considered in applying these Rules;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} matters related to the disclosure of documents and
    any related issues of privilege or confidentiality;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} identification of issues in relation to the
    specific claim that have been resolved between the parties and those
    that remain to be decided by the Tribunal;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} identification and status of other claims that are
    based on similar facts or that involve the same lands or assets as
    the specific claim;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} matters related to the testimony of potential
    witnesses; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the procedural timeline.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Settlement discussions

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** Except with the written
consent of all parties, all statements made and all documents disclosed
at a case management conference in respect of the settlement of all or
part of an issue in relation to the specific claim are made and
disclosed without prejudice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic record

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** Each case management
conference must be recorded electronically. However, the content of that
record cannot be used in the proceedings unless leave is granted by the
Tribunal or the content is incorporated in an order of the Tribunal.

### [Mediation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772158 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Availability

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** Parties may, at any time
during the proceedings and on terms that are mutually agreeable to them,
enter into mediation of any of the issues in relation to the specific
claim.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mediator

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  mediator must be either a member of the Tribunal or a private mediator
  that is jointly selected by the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Mediation by a Tribunal member is subject to the
  availability of the member and the resources of the Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the mediator is a member of the Tribunal, that
  member cannot preside over the hearing unless the parties consent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Without prejudice

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** Except with the written
consent of the parties, all statements made and all documents disclosed
during the mediation are made and disclosed without prejudice. However,
documents disclosed during the mediation can be used at the hearing if
they are otherwise available to the parties or the Tribunal in
accordance with these Rules.

### [Pre-Hearing Conference]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772170 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-hearing conference

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** Before the day on which
the hearing is scheduled to begin, a pre-hearing conference must be held
between the parties' representatives and the Tribunal member who will
preside at the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matters to be discussed

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  pre-hearing conference, the parties must be prepared to discuss any
  matter that may facilitate the hearing, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether any directions or pre-hearing orders may be
    required;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the use of electronic technology at the hearing;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} procedural matters relating to oral history
    evidence and expert evidence;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the daily and weekly schedules of the hearing;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the parties' compliance with orders of the
    Tribunal;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} logistical matters related to the holding of the
    hearing;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the identity of witnesses who will be called at the
    hearing, and whether they have consented to testify or whether
    subponeas will be required; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} whether any of the parties intend to introduce
    demonstrative evidence at the hearing and whether any parties have
    consented to its introduction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral history evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party that intends to enter oral history evidence
  must be prepared to discuss the following matters at the pre-hearing
  conference:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the First Nation's practices with respect to
    safeguarding the integrity of its oral history;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the First Nation's protocols with respect to who is
    entitled to be custodian and speaker of its oral history; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the status of any pre-hearing disclosure ordered
    under Rule 84.

## [PART 8]{.HLabel1}[Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772189 .Part}

### [Application for Disclosure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772190 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** A party may make an
application for the disclosure of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any documents or information, or category of
  documents or information, that are relevant to the proceedings and
  that are in the possession, power or control of another party; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the identity of any witness that is expected to give
  evidence on behalf of another party at the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claim of confidentiality

- **[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  party claims confidentiality with respect to a document or
  information, or a category of documents or information, that is
  subject to an application for disclosure the party must, in addition
  to the information required under Rule 35, set out the following in
  its response:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the confidentiality claimed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the specific and direct harm that would result from
    the disclosure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspection by Tribunal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a party claims confidentiality with respect to a
  document or information, or a category of documents or information,
  that is subject to an application for disclosure, the Tribunal may
  inspect them in order to determine

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether they are relevant to the proceedings; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the claim of confidentiality is valid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Draft disclosure order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a party claims confidentiality with respect to
  documents or information, or a category of documents or information,
  but consents to their disclosure under certain conditions, the party
  must, together with its response, file a draft order that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} identifies the documents or information, or
    category of documents or information, for which there is consent to
    disclosure; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sets out the conditions for their disclosure,
    including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the parties that are to have access to them, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for each document for which there is consent to
      disclosure,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the number of copies that are to be provided to
        the parties,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the limitations imposed with respect to their
        reproduction, and

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} instructions for their return or destruction at
        the conclusion of the proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claim of privilege

- **[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  party claims privilege with respect to a document or information, or a
  category of documents or information, that is subject to an
  application for disclosure the party must, in addition to the
  information required under Rule 35, set out the nature of the
  privilege claimed in its response.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspection by Tribunal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a party claims privilege with respect to a
  document or information, or a category of documents or information,
  that is subject to an application for disclosure, the Tribunal may
  inspect them in order to determine whether the claim of privilege is
  valid.

## [PART 9]{.HLabel1}[Examinations Before the Hearing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772218 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772219 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may make an application to examine a person before the hearing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of discovery;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of cross-examining the person on
    their affidavit; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of taking the person's evidence for
    use at the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}On consent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Leave is not required to make the application if all
  parties consent to the examination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the information required under Rule
  34, the notice of application must set out the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the examination is proposed to be conducted
    orally, in writing or both;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the person or party proposed to be examined
    has consented to the examination; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether any other parties have consented to the
    examination.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Considerations

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** When deciding an
application made under paragraph 60(1)(c), the Tribunal must consider
the following factors in relation to the person proposed to be examined:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether they will be absent during the time of the
  hearing;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} their age or infirmity;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the distance they reside from the place of the
  hearing; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the expense of having them attend the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** If the Tribunal permits
the examination to be conducted, it may provide the parties with
directions regarding

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the format and location of the examination; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an oral examination, the means by
  which it is to be recorded.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated person

- **[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  the Crown or a First Nation is examined for discovery, it must
  designate a person to be examined on its behalf.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Substitute

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the application of the examining party, the
  Tribunal may order that a person other than the one designated be
  examined.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of witness

**[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** The witness must, to the
best of their knowledge, information and belief, answer every question
posed to them that is relevant to the specific claim.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oath

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** Examinations must be
conducted under oath.

### [Oral Examinations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772252 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recording

**[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** Oral examinations must be
recorded by a person who is qualified to record examinations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpreter

- **[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  witness understands neither French nor English, or is hearing or
  speech impaired, the examining party must arrange for the attendance
  of an independent and competent person to accurately interpret
  everything said during the examination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpreter's oath

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before providing services at the examination, the
  interpreter must take an oath that they will accurately interpret
  everything said during the examination.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Production of documents

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** At the examination, the
party whose witness is being examined must produce for inspection all
documents that are relevant to the examination and which have been
ordered to be disclosed under Part 8.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objections

- **[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party who objects to a question that is asked in an examination must
  briefly state the grounds for the objection for the record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Answer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The witness may answer a question for which an
  objection is made subject to the right to having the propriety of the
  question determined, by application to the Tribunal, before the answer
  is used at the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limiting an examination

**[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** On the application of a
party, the Tribunal may limit an examination that it considers to be
oppressive, vexatious, repetitive or otherwise unnecessary.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for directions

- **[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may suspend an examination and make an application for
  directions if they believe that their witness is being subjected to an
  excessive number of questions or to improper questions, or that the
  examination is being conducted in bad faith or in an abusive manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for directions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The examining party may suspend the examination and
  make an application for directions if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they believe that the answers being provided by the
    witness are evasive; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} documents have not been produced under Rule 68.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs of examination

**[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** The examining party must
pay the following costs:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all fees and disbursements related to the recording
  of the examination;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if an interpreter was required, the fees and
  disbursements of the interpreter; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reasonable travel expenses incurred by the
  witness.

### [Written examinations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772281 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written examinations

**[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** If an examination is to
be conducted in writing, the examining party must serve the party whose
witness is being examined with a list of concise, separately numbered
questions for the witness to answer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing and format for answers

**[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** The witness must, within
30 days after the day on which the questions are served, serve each
party with an affidavit setting out their answers to the questions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objections

**[[75]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-75 .sectionLabel}** If a party objects to a
question that is posed in a written examination, the examining party may
make an application to compel the witness to answer the question.

### [Sanctions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772288 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sanction

**[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76 .sectionLabel}** On the application of a
party, the Tribunal may award costs against any person or party whose
conduct necessitated the making of an application under Rule 70 or
subrule 71(1) or who unnecessarily suspended an examination under
subrule 71(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sanction

**[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77 .sectionLabel}** If a person fails to
attend an examination or refuses to take an oath, answer a proper
question, produce documents under Rule 68 or to comply with an order of
the Tribunal, the Tribunal may, on the application of a party,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} order the person to attend or re-attend the
  examination at their own expense;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} order the person to answer a question for which an
  improper objection was made and any proper question arising from that
  answer;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} strike all or part of the person's evidence,
  including any affidavit made by that person; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} order the party on whose behalf the person is being
  examined to pay the costs of the examination.

## [PART 10]{.HLabel1}[Evidence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772297 .Part}

### [Evidence Taken Before the Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772298 .Subheading}

#### [Discovery Evidence]{.HTitleText3} {#h-772299 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witness unavailable

**[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78 .sectionLabel}** With leave of the
Tribunal, a party may introduce all or part of an examination for
discovery of a person as evidence at the hearing if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person has died;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person is unable to testify at the hearing
  because they are ill or infirm; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person cannot be compelled to attend the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Introduction of evidence

**[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** A party may introduce as
its own evidence at the hearing any part of its examination for
discovery of a person, whether or not that person has already testified.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order to introduce

**[[80]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80 .sectionLabel}** If a party only
introduces part of the examination for discovery, the Tribunal may order
the party to introduce any other part of that examination that the
Tribunal considers relevant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use for impeachment

**[[81]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-81 .sectionLabel}** A party may use any part
of its examination for discovery of a person as evidence to impeach the
credibility of that person as a witness at the hearing. However, the
party must first ask the person the questions posed to them in that part
of the examination.

#### [Evidence Taken for Use at Hearing]{.HTitleText3} {#h-772311 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entering of evidence

**[[82]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-82 .sectionLabel}** Evidence taken in an
examination referred to in paragraph 60(1)(c) may be entered into
evidence by any party at the hearing.

### [Oral History Evidence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772314 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

**[[83]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-83 .sectionLabel}** A party that intends to
enter oral history evidence at the hearing must provide every other
party with written notice of that intention at least 90 days before the
day on which the hearing begins.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure order

**[[84]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-84 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may order a
party who intends to enter oral history evidence to make pre-hearing
disclosure of that evidence to every other party if it is necessary to
ensure a just and expeditious hearing.

### [Expert Evidence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772319 .Subheading}

#### [Expert Reports]{.HTitleText3} {#h-772320 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expert report

**[[85]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-85 .sectionLabel}** A party that intends to
have an expert witness give evidence at the hearing must, at least 120
days before the day on which the hearing begins, serve every other party
with a copy of the expert's report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responding expert report

**[[86]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-86 .sectionLabel}** A party that intends to
have an expert witness give evidence in response to an expert report
served under Rule 85 must, at least 60 days before the day on which the
hearing begins, serve every other party with a copy of their expert's
report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supplementary expert report

**[[87]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-87 .sectionLabel}** In reply to any report
served on them under Rule 86, the party who filed the report under Rule
85 may, at least 30 days before the day on which the hearing begins,
serve every other party with a copy of a supplementary expert report
prepared by their expert.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of expert report

**[[88]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-88 .sectionLabel}** An expert report referred
to in this Part must be signed and dated by the expert and include the
following information and documents:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, business address and email address of the
  expert;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the expert's [curriculum vitae]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  and a statement indicating how their training, education and
  experience qualify them to produce the report;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any matters addressed in the report that fall outside
  the expert's area of expertise;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an account of the nature of the request to prepare
  the report and of any directions the expert has received for its
  preparation;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the opinions expressed in the report;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the facts and assumptions on which the expert's
  opinions are based;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} any caveats or qualifications that are necessary to
  render the report complete and accurate;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} identification of any literature or other materials
  that the expert relied on in support of the opinions expressed in the
  report; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the report is prepared in response or in reply to
  another expert's report, identification of the points of agreement and
  disagreement with that other report.

#### [Tribunal-appointed Expert]{.HTitleText3} {#h-772338 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

- **[[89]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-89 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may appoint an independent expert to inquire into and report
  on any question of fact or opinion that is relevant to an issue in
  relation to the specific claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The order appointing the expert must contain the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the expert's name and qualifications;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the directions given to the expert regarding the
    preparation of the report;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the questions posed to the expert;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the expert's report is to be
    provided to the Tribunal;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the nature and extent of the expert's participation
    in the proceedings; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the remuneration to be paid to the expert.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service and filing

**[[90]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-90 .sectionLabel}** The expert's report must
be filed and the registrar must serve a copy of the report on each
party.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submissions

**[[91]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91 .sectionLabel}** Within 14 days after the
day on which they are served with the expert's report, a party may make
submissions to the Tribunal with respect to the admissibility of the
report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responding expert report

**[[92]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-92 .sectionLabel}** Within 60 days after the
day on which they are served with a report under Rule 90, a party that
intends to have an expert give evidence in response to that report must
file a responding expert report and serve a copy of it on every party.

#### [Admission into Evidence]{.HTitleText3} {#h-772356 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expert's testimony

- **[[93]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-93 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party who wishes to enter an expert's report into evidence at the
  hearing must call the expert as a witness unless the expert was
  examined before the hearing under Part 9.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The expert's testimony cannot consist of reading his
  or her report into evidence; however, the expert may provide a concise
  summary of the report's key points.

### [Affidavits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772362 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format

**[[94]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-94 .sectionLabel}** An affidavit must be
drawn in the first person and be sworn to be true by the affiant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certification

**[[95]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-95 .sectionLabel}** The person before whom
the affidavit is sworn must certify, on the affidavit, that the
affidavit was read to, and apparently understood by, the affiant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Translation

- **[[96]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-96 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  affidavit is written in a language that is not understood by the
  affiant, the affidavit must be translated orally into the affiant's
  language by an independent and competent person who will accurately
  translate the content of the affidavit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Translator's oath

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before providing services, the translator must take
  an oath that they will accurately translate the content of the
  affidavit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

**[[97]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-97 .sectionLabel}** Except for evidence
relating to oral history, the evidence in an affidavit must be confined
to facts that are within the affiant's personal knowledge.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exhibits

**[[98]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-98 .sectionLabel}** Each exhibit referred to
in an affidavit must be identified by an endorsement on the exhibit, or
on a certificate that is attached to the exhibit, that is signed by the
person before whom the affidavit is sworn.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cross-examination

**[[99]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-99 .sectionLabel}** A party who
cross-examines an affiant concerning their affidavit must order and pay
for a transcript of that cross-examination and must provide a copy of
the transcript to every other party.

### [Witness Testimony]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772378 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Under oath

**[[100]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-100 .sectionLabel}** Each witness called to
testify at the hearing must give their testimony under oath.

### [Documentary Evidence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-772381 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transmission to registrar

**[[101]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-101 .sectionLabel}** If a party enters a
document as evidence at the hearing and that document can be converted
into PDF (Portable Document Format) or any other electronic format that
allows the document to be converted for printing on letter size paper,
the party must transmit the document to the registrar in that format.

## [PART 11]{.HLabel1}[Summoning of Witnesses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772384 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of subpoena

**[[102]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-102 .sectionLabel}** A party may make an [ex
parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application to the Tribunal for the
issuance of a subpoena for the attendance of a witness or for the
production of documents at the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to set aside

**[[103]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-103 .sectionLabel}** A person who is subject
to a subpoena may, under Part 4, make an application to have it set
aside.

## [PART 12]{.HLabel1}[Hearing Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772389 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of presentation

**[[104]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-104 .sectionLabel}** The order of
presentation at the hearing is the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the claimant must make an opening address and then
  enter evidence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} after the claimant's evidence is concluded, the Crown
  must make an opening address and then enter its evidence; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} after the Crown's evidence is concluded, the claimant
  may enter evidence in reply.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exhibits

**[[105]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-105 .sectionLabel}** All exhibits entered in
evidence must be numbered and marked.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of argument

- **[[106]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-106
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After the parties have been given an
  opportunity to put in their respective cases, they must be heard in
  argument in the same order as that in which they entered evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party may reply to the arguments of an adverse
  party and, if the reply raises a new point of law, the adverse party
  may answer on that point.

## [PART 13]{.HLabel1}[Offers to Settle]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772402 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offer

**[[107]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-107 .sectionLabel}** A party may make an
offer to settle to another party at any time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

**[[108]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-108 .sectionLabel}** An offer to settle must
be made in writing and set out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of compensation offered, if any;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the period of time during which the offer will remain
  open for acceptance; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any conditions that apply to acceptance of the offer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure to Tribunal

**[[109]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-109 .sectionLabel}** The fact that an offer
to settle has been made, and any details of the offer, can only be
revealed to the Tribunal member presiding at the hearing

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} with the written consent of the parties to the offer;
  or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} during an application for costs.

## [PART 14]{.HLabel1}[Costs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772414 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs --- applications

- **[[110]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-110
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After the hearing of an application,
  the Tribunal may award costs in relation to that application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs --- proceedings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} After the hearing of the specific claim, the Tribunal
  may award costs in relation to the proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to be considered --- general

- **[[111]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-111 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  deciding whether award of costs under subrule 110(2), the Tribunal
  must consider the following factors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether a party has acted in bad faith;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether a party has failed to comply with an order
    of the Tribunal; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether a party has refused a reasonable offer to
    settle.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to be considered --- claimant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When deciding whether to award costs to the claimant
  under subrule 110(2), the Tribunal must also consider the following
  factors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the claimant's costs are reasonably
    incurred but are disproportionate to the amount of compensation
    awarded; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the issues in relation to the specific
    claim are complex or contain elements that are of general public
    importance.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772430 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[112]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-112 .sectionLabel}** These Rules come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
