## Royal Canadian Mounted Police, Criminal Record Verification for Civil Purposes Fee Regulations {#royal-canadian-mounted-police-criminal-record-verification-for-civil-purposes-fee-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-485
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1993-09-23

Regulations Prescribing the Fee To Be Paid for the Verification of a
Criminal Record for Civil Purposes

P.C. 1993-1822  1993-09-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Solicitor General of Canada and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
paragraph 19(1)(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-93-485_e_hq_6268){#fn_SOR-93-485_e_hq_6268-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed [Regulations prescribing the fee to be paid for the
verification of a criminal record for civil
purposes](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-485).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955122 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police, Criminal Record
Verification for Civil Purposes Fee
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-485).

## [Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955124 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The fee to be paid by an
individual for the verification by the Royal Canadian Mounted Police of
a criminal record for civil purposes is \$25.
