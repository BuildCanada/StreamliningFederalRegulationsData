## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614275 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Canadian mission worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian mission worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a citizen and
    resident of Afghanistan who directly supports the Canadian mission
    in Kandahar province, either

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as an employee or independent contractor of a
      department or agency of the federal public administration; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as an employee or independent contractor of a
      person or entity that has entered into a contract with a
      department or agency of the federal public administration.
      ([travailleur de la mission canadienne]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in subsection 1(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227). ([conjoint de
    fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[JRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[JRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Joint Referral Committee,
    established by the federal public administration and composed of
    senior officials of the federal public administration.
    ([CMR]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[spouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[spouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is considered to
    be a spouse under the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227).
    ([époux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614285 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted, to
persons who meet the conditions set out in section 3, of the fees paid
or payable under subsection 303(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee
Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227) for the
acquisition of permanent resident status.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614287 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
the condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is or was a Canadian mission worker who carried out
    their duties in Kandahar province for at least 12 months,
    consecutive or not, since October 9, 2007 and, due to that work,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} faces extraordinary and individualized risk, or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} has suffered a non-accidental or non-natural
      injury that resulted in risk to their life or that prevents them
      from working,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is a citizen and resident of Afghanistan and is
    the spouse or common-law partner of a person described in
    subparagraph (i), or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is a citizen and resident of Afghanistan and is
    the spouse or common-law partner of a Canadian mission worker who
    carried out their duties in Kandahar province for at least 12
    months, consecutive or not, since October 9, 2007 and who suffered a
    non-accidental or non-natural death due to that work;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an application is submitted to the JRC, using the
  form provided by the federal public administration, on or before
  December 31, 2011;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the JRC confirms the employment or contractual
  relationship of the Canadian mission worker named in the application
  and any claims of risk, injury and death;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person's immigration application is approved by
  the Department of Citizenship and Immigration; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} at the request of the JRC or the Department of
  Citizenship and Immigration, the person provides any additional
  evidence or information that is required to demonstrate that they meet
  the conditions of this Order.
