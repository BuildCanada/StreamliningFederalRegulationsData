## Minister of the Environment Authority to Prescribe Fees or Charges Order {#minister-of-the-environment-authority-to-prescribe-fees-or-charges-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/87-210
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1987-10-14

Minister of the Environment Authority to Prescribe Fees or Charges Order

P.C. 1987-1973 1987-09-24
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11),
is pleased hereby to authorize the Minister of the Environment to
prescribe, by order, the fee or charge to be paid by a person to whom a
service or the use of a facility is provided by the Department of the
Environment, in accordance with the terms and conditions set out in the
schedule hereto.
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641350 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Terms and
Conditions,

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of the
    Environment; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[direct costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[direct costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all personnel costs and
    all other operating costs and capital costs incurred by the
    Department that are directly attributable to the provision of a
    special service by the Department; ([coûts directs]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Government of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Government of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 2 of the
    [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11); ([gouvernement du
    Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[indirect costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[indirect costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any costs to the
    Government of Canada, that are not direct costs and that are
    attributable to the provision of a special service by the
    Department; ([coûts indirects]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of the
    Environment; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any partnership and any
    corporation, any university and any government other than the
    Government of Canada; ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[special service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[special service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any service,
    information or use of facilities provided by the Department to any
    person regarding weather, climate, water and ice conditions,
    wildlife, land resources, environmental protection and resource
    conservation; ([service spécial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[whole cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[whole cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the sum of direct costs and
    indirect costs. ([coût global]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Fees and Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641360 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 and
4, the Minister is authorized to prescribe for a special service
provided to any person, a fee or charge that is equal to the whole cost
of providing that service in respect of that person.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4, the
Minister is authorized to prescribe for a special service provided to
any person, a fee or charge that is equal to part of the whole cost of
providing that service in respect of that person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the prescribing of a fee or charge at whole cost
  would have a serious detrimental effect on the income of the user of
  the service;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the prescribing of a fee or charge at whole cost
  would reduce the use of that service to an extent that the objective
  of providing the service would no longer be achieved; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if there is an equivalent or similar service provided
  commercially.

## [Terms and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641366 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where the Minister,
pursuant to section 3 prescribes a fee or charge that is equal to a part
of the whole cost of providing a special service, the Minister shall
prescribe a fee or charge that,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the circumstances set out in paragraphs 3(a) and
  3(b) achieves the highest level of partial cost recovery possible in
  those circumstances; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the circumstances set out in paragraph 3(c) is
  equal to the commercial rate for the equivalent or similar service.

## [Waiver]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641370 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall, for a
special service provided to any registered charity as set out in the
[Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), other than a university, waive the
prescribed fee or charge.
