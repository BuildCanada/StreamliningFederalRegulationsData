## Order Designating the Minister of Communications as Appropriate Minister with Respect to the National Battlefields Commission {#order-designating-the-minister-of-communications-as-appropriate-minister-with-respect-to-the-national-battlefields-commission .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-227
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1993-12-15

Order Designating the Minister of Communications as Appropriate Minister
with Respect to the National Battlefields Commission

P.C. 1993-1979  1993-12-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (c.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-227_e_hq_6539){#fn_SI-93-227_e_hq_6539-ID0EFDAA}
of the definition [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is
pleased hereby

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-227_e_hq_6539-ID0EFDAA){#fn_SI-93-227_e_hq_6539}S.C.
  1992, c. 1, s. 69(2)
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1993-1452 of June 25,
  1993[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-227_e_hq_6540){#fn_SI-93-227_e_hq_6540-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-227_e_hq_6540-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_SI-93-227_e_hq_6540}SI/93-106,
    1993 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3198
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to designate the Minister of Communications, a member
  of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the appropriate Minister
  with respect to the National Battlefields Commission for the purposes
  of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).
:::
