2017 Toronto Invictus Games Remission Order

P.C. 2017-1194 2017-09-22
::::

::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Treasury
Board and the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, pursuant to
subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EGACA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote

Toronto Invictus Games Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2017-55).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any foreign national who is
seeking to enter Canada as

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a competitor, coach, judge or team official
  participating in the 2017 Invictus Games;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a medical staff member of a team participating in the
  2017 Invictus Games;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a member of a national or international sports
  organizing body participating in the 2017 Invictus Games; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a friend or family member of any foreign national
  listed in paragraphs (a) to (c) who is accredited in 2017 by the
  Invictus Games.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} of the fee of \$100, paid under subsection 296(1) of
  the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227), for the processing of an
  application for a temporary resident visa, on the condition that the
  applicant is a participant; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} of the fee of \$85, paid under subsection 315.1(1) of
  the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227), for the provision of
  services in relation to the collection of biometric information in
  respect of an application for a temporary resident visa, on the
  condition that the applicant is a participant.
