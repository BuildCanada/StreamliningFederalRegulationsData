## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1514175 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Disability Benefit
    Act](/eng/acts/C-3.45). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who has applied for
    a benefit or on whose behalf an application for a benefit has been
    made. ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[beneficiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[beneficiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person to whom payment
    of a benefit has been approved or on whose behalf a benefit has
    become payable. ([prestataire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canada disability benefit
    payable under the Act. ([prestation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cohabiting spouse or common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cohabiting spouse or common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has
    the same meaning as in section 122.6 of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([époux ou conjoint de fait
    visé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[payment period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payment period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning on
    July 1 of a calendar year and ending on June 30 of the following
    calendar year. ([période de paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[release]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[release]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a person who has
    been incarcerated, means release from custody on earned remission,
    at the expiry of a sentence, or on parole or statutory release that
    has not been terminated or revoked. ([libération]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Eligibility]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1514185 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility criteria

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  is eligible to receive a benefit for any month after May 2025 in which
  they meet the following criteria:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are at least 18 years of age but not more than
    65 years of age;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they are a [[DTC-eligible
    individual]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection
    146.4(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} they are resident in Canada for the purposes of the
    [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) and are

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian citizen,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a [[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
      as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee
      Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5),

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a temporary resident within the meaning of the
      [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) who was
      resident in Canada for the 18 months preceding that month,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a protected person within the meaning of the
      [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} an [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} they are not incarcerated as a result of a sentence
    of imprisonment of two years or more that is to be served in a
    penitentiary by virtue of any Act of Parliament; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} they have filed a return of income under the
    [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) for the last taxation year that
    ended before the beginning of the payment period in which that month
    falls.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- 65 years of age

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(a), a person is eligible for a
  benefit for the month in which they turn 65 years of age.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- incarceration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(d), a person is eligible for a
  benefit for the first month in which they are incarcerated and for the
  month in which they are released.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition --- cohabiting spouse or
  common-law partner

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (1) to (3), a person is eligible
  for a benefit for any month in a payment period only if their
  cohabiting spouse or common-law partner has filed a return of income
  under the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) for the last taxation year
  that ended before the beginning of that payment period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waiver

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may waive the condition set out in
  subsection (4) if the Minister is satisfied that the condition is
  unreasonable or impracticable in the circumstances, including if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cohabiting spouse or common-law partner is not
    resident in Canada for the purposes of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for reasons not attributable to the person or their
    cohabiting spouse or common-law partner, the person is not living
    with their cohabiting spouse or common-law partner; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the cohabiting spouse or common-law partner has
    engaged in [[family violence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Divorce Act](/eng/acts/D-3.4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- suspension of payments

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (1) to (3), a person is not
  eligible for a benefit for any month during which payment of a benefit
  has been suspended under section 11.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence of age and identity

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may determine
the age and identity of an applicant on the basis of any information
made available to the Minister by the Canada Employment Insurance
Commission under subsection 28.2(5) of the [Department of Employment and
Social Development Act](/eng/acts/H-5.7).

## [Application for Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1514214 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of application

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for benefits must be made in the form and manner specified
  by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application made

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application is deemed to have been made only when
  it is received by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application may be made before or after a person
  becomes eligible for a benefit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, for any given month, a beneficiary becomes
  ineligible, their benefit ceases as of that month and they must make a
  new application to resume benefits.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval date

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), an application is deemed to be approved by the
  Minister on the earliest day on which an applicant becomes eligible
  for a benefit in accordance with the Act and these Regulations and for
  which a benefit has not already been paid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The earliest day on which an application can be
  deemed to have been approved is the day that is 24 months before the
  day on which the application is made.

## [Amount of Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1514229 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of monthly benefit

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  benefit that is payable to a beneficiary for any month for which they
  are eligible to receive a benefit in a payment period is the amount
  --- rounded to the nearest cent or, if the amount is equidistant from
  two cents, to the higher of them --- determined by the formula

  ::: {#1514233 .Subsection}
  ((\$2,400 × A) − B) ÷ 12

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the indexing factor for the payment period, calculated in
      accordance with subsection (5); and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the reduction based on income, calculated in accordance with
      whichever of subsections (2) to (4) applies to the beneficiary on
      the last day of the month that precedes the applicable month.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reduction --- single beneficiary

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the beneficiary does not have a cohabiting spouse
  or common-law partner, the reduction based on income is the greater of
  zero and the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#1514239 .Subsection}
  20% × (C − D − (\$23,000 × A))

  where

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the beneficiary's adjusted income for the last taxation year
      that ended before the beginning of the payment period, without
      taking into account the income of the person who was their
      cohabiting spouse or common-law partner, if any, at the end of
      that year;

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the lesser of
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the beneficiary's working income for the last
        taxation year that ended before the beginning of the payment
        period, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the product of \$10,000 and the indexing factor
        for the payment period, calculated in accordance with subsection
        (5); and

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the indexing factor for the payment period, calculated in
      accordance with subsection (5).
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reduction --- non-beneficiary spouse or
  partner

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the beneficiary has a cohabiting spouse or
  common-law partner who is not a beneficiary, the reduction based on
  income is the greater of zero and the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#1514248 .Subsection}
  20% × (E − F − (\$32,500 × A))

  where

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is the beneficiary's adjusted income for the last taxation year
      that ended before the beginning of the payment period or, if their
      current cohabiting spouse or common-law partner was not their
      cohabiting spouse or common-law partner at the end of that year,
      the amount that would have been the beneficiary's adjusted income
      if their current cohabiting spouse or common-law partner had been
      their cohabiting spouse or common-law partner at the end of that
      year;

  [F]{.dfn}
  :   is the lesser of
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sum of the beneficiary's working income for
        the last taxation year that ended before the beginning of the
        payment period and that of their cohabiting spouse or common-law
        partner, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the product of \$14,000 and the indexing factor
        for the payment period, calculated in accordance with subsection
        (5); and

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the indexing factor for the payment period, calculated in
      accordance with subsection (5).
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reduction --- beneficiary spouse or
  partner

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the beneficiary has a cohabiting spouse or
  common-law partner who is also a beneficiary, the reduction based on
  income is the greater of zero and the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#1514257 .Subsection}
  10% × (E − F − (\$32,500 × A))

  where

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is the beneficiary's adjusted income for the last taxation year
      that ended before the beginning of the payment period or, if their
      current cohabiting spouse or common-law partner was not their
      cohabiting spouse or common-law partner at the end of that year,
      the amount that would have been the beneficiary's adjusted income
      if their current cohabiting spouse or common-law partner had been
      their cohabiting spouse or common-law partner at the end of that
      year;

  [F]{.dfn}
  :   is the lesser of
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sum of the beneficiary's working income for
        the last taxation year that ended before the beginning of the
        payment period and that of their cohabiting spouse or common-law
        partner, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the product of \$14,000 and the indexing factor
        for the payment period, calculated in accordance with subsection
        (5); and

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the indexing factor for the payment period, calculated in
      accordance with subsection (5).
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indexing factor

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The indexing factor for a payment period is the
  amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#1514266 .Subsection}
  CPI~max~ ÷ CPI~2024~

  where

  [CPI~max~]{.dfn}
  :   is the highest Consumer Price Index for any calendar year
      beginning with 2024 and ending with the last calendar year that
      ended before the beginning of the payment period; and

  [CPI~2024~]{.dfn}
  :   is the Consumer Price Index for 2024.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consumer Price Index

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (5), a reference to the
  Consumer Price Index for a calendar year is a reference to the
  all-items Consumer Price Index for Canada, annual average, not
  seasonally adjusted, for that year, as published by Statistics Canada
  under the authority of the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in this section.

  [[adjusted income]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[adjusted income]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the portion of a
      beneficiary's [[adjusted income]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
      defined in section 122.6 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3),
      that is not a benefit. ([revenu modifié]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[working income]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[working income]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, of an individual for a
      given taxation year, means the total of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amounts referred to in paragraphs (a)
        and (b) of the definition [[working
        income]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 122.7(1) of the
        [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total of the following types of business
        income:

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} net farming income,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} net fishing income,

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} net professional income,

        - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} net commission income, and

        - [(v)]{.lawlabel} other net business income. ([revenu de
          travail]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Payment of Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1514283 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When benefit begins

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A beneficiary's first
benefit is payable the month following the month in which their
application is approved by the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Monthly payments

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a benefit is payable each month during the payment
  period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of twenty dollars or less

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the monthly benefit that is payable to a
  beneficiary is twenty dollars or less, their entire benefit for a
  payment period, being an amount equal to their monthly benefit
  multiplied by the number of months remaining in the payment period, is
  payable as a lump sum on the day on which the first monthly benefit
  would have been paid.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First payment

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The first payment to a
beneficiary under section 7 must be an amount equal to the sum of the
monthly benefits that are payable to the beneficiary for the period
beginning with the month following the month in which their application
is approved under section 5 and ending with the month in which the first
payment is made.

## [Suspension of Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1514293 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Potential ineligibility

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that a beneficiary does not
  meet the eligibility criteria set out in section 2 or that an inquiry
  with respect to their eligibility is necessary, the Minister may
  suspend payment of the benefit to the beneficiary until the Minister
  is satisfied that they are eligible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasonable time

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must review a beneficiary's eligibility
  within a reasonable time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resumption of payments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a suspension is lifted, the Minister must resume
  payment of the benefit and make a lump sum payment that is equal to
  the sum of the monthly benefits to which the beneficiary was entitled
  to receive during the period of the suspension.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for suspension

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  beneficiary may request in writing that the Minister suspend payment
  of their benefit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of payments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The payment of a benefit is suspended on the later of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the last day of the month in which the request is
    processed by the Minister, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day indicated in the beneficiary's request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for resumption

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The beneficiary may request in writing that the
  Minister resume payment of their benefit within 24 months after the
  date of suspension. If no request to resume payment is received within
  that period, their benefit is deemed to have ceased and they must make
  a new application to resume benefits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a request, the Minister must resume
  payment of the benefit if the Minister is satisfied that the person
  meets the eligibility criteria set out in section 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resumption of payments

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must resume payment of the benefit on
  the later of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the month following the month in which the Minister
    received the request, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the month indicated in the beneficiary's request.

## [Death of a Beneficiary]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1514316 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If a beneficiary dies,
the executor or administrator of the estate, the liquidator of the
succession or the beneficiary's heirs must, as soon as feasible, notify
the Minister of the beneficiary's death.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment to estate or succession

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  beneficiary dies, the beneficiary's estate or succession or the
  beneficiary's heirs are eligible to receive a benefit payment for the
  month in which the beneficiary died if the beneficiary did not receive
  a benefit payment for that month.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount payable to deceased beneficiary

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any amount owing as a benefit on the death of the
  beneficiary, or any benefit paid to the beneficiary or on their behalf
  in accordance with the Act and these Regulations but returned to the
  Minister after the death of a beneficiary, must be paid to the
  beneficiary's estate or succession or the beneficiary's heirs. If
  there are no heirs, this amount must be paid to a person or agency
  designated by the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption of death

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  beneficiary has disappeared under circumstances that the Minister
  concludes raise, beyond a reasonable doubt, a presumption that the
  beneficiary is dead, the Minister may determine the date on which, for
  the purposes of the Act and these Regulations, that beneficiary's
  death is presumed to have occurred, and the beneficiary will be deemed
  for all purposes of the Act and these Regulations to have died on that
  date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of date

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, after having determined the date of the
  beneficiary's death, the Minister is satisfied on the basis of new
  information or evidence that the date of death is different, the
  Minister may determine a different date for the purposes of the Act
  and these Regulations and the Minister must, within a reasonable time,
  pay any benefit that would have been payable if the initial
  determination had not been made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Beneficiary is alive

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, after having determined the date of the
  beneficiary's death under this section, the Minister is satisfied on
  the basis of new information or evidence that the beneficiary is
  alive, the Minister must, within a reasonable time, pay any benefit
  that would have been payable to the beneficiary had that determination
  not been made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Death certificates

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the Minister is not
  bound by the issuance or revocation of a death certificate by any
  other authority.

## [Representatives]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1514333 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representative

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** If a person is incapable
of managing their own affairs, their representative may act on their
behalf, including by making any application, statement, notice, request
for reconsideration or appeal referred to in the Act or these
Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments to beneficiary's representative

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** If a beneficiary is
incapable of managing their own affairs, the Minister may direct that
the benefit be paid on their behalf to their representative, if the
representative is authorized by or under a law of Canada or of a
province to manage the beneficiary's affairs.

## [Amendment, Rescission or Reconsideration of Decisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1514338 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment or rescission

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, on the
basis of new facts, amend or rescind any decision made by the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reconsideration of benefit

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 19, a person who
  is dissatisfied with a decision made under the Act or these
  Regulations that no benefit be paid to them, or respecting the amount
  of a benefit that the person has received or will receive, may, within
  180 days after the day on which the person is notified in writing of
  the decision, make a request to the Minister for a reconsideration of
  that decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reconsideration of penalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 19, a person against whom a
  penalty has been imposed by the Minister under section 35 and who is
  dissatisfied with that decision or with the amount of the penalty may,
  within 180 days after the day on which the person is notified in
  writing of the decision, make a request to the Minister for a
  reconsideration of that decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of request

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A request for reconsideration must be made in writing
  to the Minister and contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the applicant or the
    beneficiary, as applicable, and their Social Insurance Number or the
    reference number assigned to the benefit application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person referred to in paragraph (a) is
    incapable of managing their own affairs, the name and address of
    their representative; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the decision for which a request for
    reconsideration is being made and a statement of the reasons and
    facts that form the basis of that request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's decision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must, within a reasonable time,
  reconsider the decision referred to in the request and may confirm,
  amend or rescind it, and may approve payment of a benefit, determine
  the amount of a benefit or determine that no benefit is payable. In
  all cases, the Minister must notify in writing the person who made the
  request of the Minister's decision and the reasons for it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for extension

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may request that the Minister extend the 180‑day period
  referred to in subsections 18(1) and (2) not later than 185 days after
  the end of that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Making of request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request for an extension of time must be made in
  the form and manner specified by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Situations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must allow an extension of time if the
  Minister is satisfied that at least one of the following situations
  has occurred and has significantly impacted the ability of the person
  referred to in subsection (1) to respect the time period:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an emergency, if a state of emergency has been
    declared by a federal or provincial minister under federal or
    provincial law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural or human‑made disaster, such as a flood
    or fire;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a civil disturbance, such as a riot;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a disruption in public services, such as a strike;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a serious accident, injury or illness suffered by
    the person; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an emotional or mental distress suffered by the
    person, such as from a death in their family.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's decision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must consider a request for an extension
  of time within a reasonable time and must notify, in writing, the
  person who made the request of the Minister's decision and the reason
  for it.

## [Appeals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1514368 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Social Security Tribunal

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of section 10.1 of the Act, the body to which a person
  may appeal is the Social Security Tribunal established under section
  44 of the [Department of Employment and Social Development
  Act](/eng/acts/H-5.7).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paragraphs 10.1(a) and (b) of Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person must follow the procedure set out in section
  18 of these Regulations in respect of any requests for reconsideration
  of decisions referred to in paragraphs 10.1(a) and (b) of the Act.
  Only a decision of the Minister made under subsection 18(4) of these
  Regulations in respect of these decisions may be appealed to the
  Social Security Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paragraph 10.1(c) of Act

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph 10.1(c) of the Act, a
  person may appeal to the Social Security Tribunal in respect of any
  decision of the Minister made under subsection 18(4) or 19(4) of these
  Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to Court

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  appeal brought under section 10.1 of the Act includes, as a ground of
  appeal, a claim or a submission that a decision or determination made
  by the Minister as to income under the Act was incorrectly made, the
  Social Security Tribunal must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the appellant and the Minister that the
    appeal on that ground has been referred to the Court for decision in
    accordance with subsection 66(2) of the [Department of Employment
    and Social Development Act](/eng/acts/H-5.7); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} transmit to the Registrar of the Court a copy of
    the documents filed in the appeal that are relevant to that ground
    of appeal.

- Definition of [[Court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section and sections 23 to
  29, [[Court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Tax Court of Canada.

Definition of [[judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 23 to 27, [[judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
in section 2 of the [Tax Court of Canada Act](/eng/acts/T-2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other persons affected

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  appeal is made in relation to a decision or determination as to income
  under the Act or these Regulations and the Minister is of the opinion
  that a person other than the appellant may be directly affected by the
  decision of the Court, the Minister must so notify the Social Security
  Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person added

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Social Security Tribunal must notify the
  Registrar of the Court and the Registrar must add that person as a
  party to the appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice --- receipt of documents

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar of the Court, on receipt of the
  documents transmitted by the Social Security Tribunal under paragraph
  21(1)(b), must notify the Chief Justice of the Court, the Social
  Security Tribunal, the Minister and any person added as a party to the
  appeal of that receipt.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of judge

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} When the Chief Justice of the Court is notified under
  subsection (3), the Chief Justice must appoint a judge to hear the
  reference.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time and place of reference

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Registrar of the Court, in consultation with the judge appointed to
  hear the reference, must set the date, time and place for the hearing
  of the reference.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where reference is held

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The hearing of a reference must be held in the city,
  town or village in which, or nearest to which, the appellant is
  ordinarily resident unless the appellant consents in writing to the
  hearing being held, in whole or in part, at some other place or by
  audioconference or videoconference or a combination of both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar of the Court must, by registered mail,
  notify the appellant, the Social Security Tribunal, the Minister and
  any person added as a party to the appeal of the date, place and time
  set for the hearing of the reference.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informal procedure

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Proceedings before the Court must be
  informal and conducted in a summary manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of procedure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The judge appointed to hear the reference must,
  subject to the right of all parties or their representatives to be
  heard, determine the procedure to be followed at the hearing of the
  reference.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written submissions

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The judge appointed to
hear the reference may, with the consent of the appellant, require that
written submissions be filed by the appellant, the Minister and any
person added as a party to the appeal in addition to or in lieu of an
oral hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  judge appointed to hear the reference must advise the Registrar of the
  Court of the judge's decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certified copy of decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} After being advised of the decision, the Registrar of
  the Court must forward a certified copy of the decision to the
  appellant, the Social Security Tribunal, the Minister and any person
  added as a party to the appeal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs and fees

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** No costs may be awarded
on the disposition of a reference and no fees may be charged to the
appellant by the Court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other grounds of appeal

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** If the appellant sets out
both a ground of appeal that has been referred to the Court under
subsection 66(2) of the [Department of Employment and Social Development
Act](/eng/acts/H-5.7) and a ground of appeal that has not been referred
to the Court under that subsection, the Social Security Tribunal, on
receipt of a certified copy of the decision of the Court, must proceed
in accordance with the Social Security Tribunal Rules of Procedure.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mailing address

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** All notices, requests and
other documents must be addressed to the person to whom they are
required to be sent, forwarded or mailed at their last known address.

## [Administrative Errors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1514419 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Denial of benefit due to administrative
error

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** If the Minister is
satisfied that, as a result of an administrative error in the
administration of the Act or these Regulations, any person has been
denied a benefit, or a portion of a benefit, to which that person would
have been entitled, the Minister must take any remedial action that the
Minister considers appropriate to place the person in the position that
they would be in had the administrative error not been made.

## [Compliance and Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1514422 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspections

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, for any purpose relating to the administration or
  enforcement of the Act or these Regulations, examine any document that
  relates or may relate to the entitlement of a person to a benefit or
  the amount of a benefit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to provide information or
  documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite any other provision of the Act or these
  Regulations, the Minister may, subject to subsection (3), by notice
  served personally or by confirmed delivery service, require that any
  person provide any information or document for any purpose relating to
  the administration or enforcement of the Act or these Regulations
  within the reasonable time specified in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other persons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must obtain the authorization of a judge
  under subsection (4) before requiring that a person --- other than an
  applicant, a beneficiary or the representative of an applicant or
  beneficiary who is incapable of managing their own affairs --- provide
  information or documents under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial authorization

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} On an *[ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}* application
  by the Minister, a judge may, subject to the conditions that the judge
  considers appropriate, authorize the Minister to require information
  or documents under subsection (2) from a person referred to in
  subsection (3) if the judge is satisfied by information on oath that
  the information or documents are required to verify compliance by the
  person with a duty or obligation under the Act or these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of authorization

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An authorization granted under subsection (4) must be
  served together with the notice referred to in subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of authorization

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A person on whom an authorization and a notice are
  served under subsection (5) may, not later than 15 days after the day
  on which the authorization and notice are served, apply to the judge
  who granted the authorization under subsection (4) or, if that judge
  is unable to act, to another judge of the same court for a review of
  the authorization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers on review

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} On hearing an application under subsection (6), a
  judge may cancel the authorization previously granted if the judge is
  not then satisfied that the conditions referred to in subsection (4)
  have been met, and the judge may confirm or vary the authorization if
  the judge is satisfied that those conditions have been met.

- Definition of [[judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section,
  [[judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a judge of a superior court having
  jurisdiction in the province where the matter arises or a judge of the
  Federal Court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies as evidence

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** When a document is
examined or provided under the Act or these Regulations, the person by
whom it is examined or to whom it is provided may make or cause to be
made one or more certified copies of it and any such copy is evidence of
the nature and content of the original document and has the same
probative force as the original document would have if it were proven in
the ordinary way.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to appear

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, for any
purpose related to verifying compliance or preventing non-compliance
with the Act or these Regulations, require an applicant, a beneficiary
or the representative of an applicant or beneficiary who is incapable of
managing their own affairs to be at a suitable place --- or to be
available by audioconference or videoconference or in any other suitable
manner --- at a suitable time in order to provide any information or
document related to their application that the Minister may require.

## [Administrative Monetary Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1514444 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violations

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person commits a violation if they

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} knowingly make, in relation to an application for a
    benefit under the Act, a representation that is false or misleading;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make an application for, and receive, a benefit
    under the Act knowing that they are not eligible to receive it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (7), the Minister may impose a
  penalty on a person if the Minister is of the opinion, based on
  documents or information collected or obtained by the Minister, that
  the person has committed a violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of penalty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The penalty that the Minister may issue is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a first violation, 15 per cent of the amount
    calculated by multiplying by 12 the monthly amount of the benefit
    payable to a beneficiary during the payment period in which the
    violation was committed, calculated in accordance with section 6
    without any reduction based on income; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each subsequent violation, 50 per cent of the
    amount calculated by multiplying by 12 the monthly amount of the
    benefit payable to a beneficiary during the payment period in which
    the violation was committed, calculated in accordance with section 6
    without any reduction based on income.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption --- first violation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (3), if no penalty was
  imposed on a person under this section in the 10 years preceding the
  day on which a violation occurred, the violation is deemed to be a
  first violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, no penalty may be imposed on a
  person if they mistakenly believe that a representation is true or
  that they, or the person on whose behalf they made an application for
  a benefit, were eligible to receive the benefit, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A penalty must not be imposed on a person in respect
  of an act referred to in subsection (1) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a prosecution for the act has been initiated
    against the person; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} five years have passed since the day on which the
    Minister became aware of the act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rescission of penalty

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may rescind a penalty imposed under
  subsection (2)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the presentation of new facts; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on being satisfied that the penalty was imposed
    without knowledge of, or on the basis of a mistake as to, some
    material fact.

## [Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1514468 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person is guilty of an offence punishable on summary conviction if
  they

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} knowingly use false identity information or another
    person's information for the purpose of obtaining a benefit for
    themselves;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} counsel a person to apply for a benefit with the
    intent to steal all or a substantial part of it; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} knowingly make false or misleading representations
    in relation to an application for a benefit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No proceeding may be commenced under this section or
  the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) for an act if a penalty for that
  act has been imposed under section 35.

## [Debts and Overpayments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1514477 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of benefit

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person or agency that has received a benefit payment to which the
  person or agency is not entitled, or that has received a benefit
  payment in excess of the amount of the benefit to which the person or
  agency is entitled, must return the amount of the erroneous payment or
  the overpayment, as the case may be, as soon as feasible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt due to His Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the erroneous payment or overpayment
  constitutes a debt due to His Majesty in right of Canada, as of the
  day on which it was paid, that may be recovered by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment in instalments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A debt recoverable from a person under this section
  may be paid as a single payment or, if the Minister agrees, in
  instalments in any amount that does not cause undue hardship to the
  person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No interest

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No interest is payable on any amount owing to His
  Majesty in right of Canada under the Act or these Regulations that
  results from an erroneous payment or overpayment unless the payment
  was made as a result of a violation for which a penalty was imposed
  under section 35 or as a result of an offence under subsection 36(1)
  for which a fine or a term of imprisonment was imposed under the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of penalties

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** A penalty imposed under
section 35 constitutes a debt due to His Majesty in right of Canada, as
of the day on which it was imposed, that may be recovered by the
Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period or prescription

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  no action or proceedings may be taken to recover money owing under the
  Act or these Regulations after the end of the six-year limitation
  period or prescription that begins on the day on which the money
  becomes due and payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period or prescription
  suspended

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The running of a limitation period or prescription is
  suspended during any period in which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is prohibited to commence or continue an action
    or other proceedings against the debtor to recover money owing under
    the Act or these Regulations; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a review of a decision establishing liability in
    respect of money owing under the Act or these Regulations is
    pending.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement proceedings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply in respect of an action
  or proceedings relating to the execution, renewal or enforcement of a
  judgment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificates

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All or
  part of the debt that has not been recovered may be certified by the
  Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} without delay, if, in the Minister's opinion, the
    debtor is attempting to avoid payment; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 30 days after the day on which the default
    occurred, in any other case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration of certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On production to the Federal Court, the certificate
  must be registered in the Court. When it is registered, it has the
  same force and effect, and all proceedings may be taken, as if the
  certificate were a judgment obtained in the Court for a debt of the
  amount specified in the certificate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judgment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A certificate registered under subsection (2) may
  also be registered in the superior court of a province as if it were a
  document evidencing a judgment of that court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} All reasonable costs and charges for the registration
  of the certificate are recoverable in the same way as if they had been
  certified and the certificate registered under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charge

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A document issued by the Federal Court or by a
  superior court of a province evidencing a certificate in respect of a
  debtor registered under subsection (2) or (3) may be recorded for the
  purpose of creating security, or a charge, lien or priority on, or a
  binding interest in, property in a province, or any interest in, or
  for civil law any right in, such property held by the debtor, in the
  same manner as a document evidencing a judgment of the superior court
  of the province against a person for a debt owing by the person may be
  recorded in accordance with the law of the province to create
  security, or a charge, lien or priority on, or a binding interest in,
  property in a province, or any interest in, or for civil law any right
  in, such property held by the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Garnishment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister knows or suspects that a person is or
  is about to become indebted or liable to make a payment to a person
  liable to make a payment to His Majesty in right of Canada under the
  Act or these Regulations, the Minister may, by notice served
  personally or by confirmed delivery service, require the first person
  to pay the money otherwise payable to the second person in whole or in
  part to the Receiver General on account of the second person's
  liability.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt due to His Majesty

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} An amount not paid as required by a notice under
  subsection (6) is a debt due to His Majesty in right of Canada.
