## Visitor Visa Fee (World Youth Day 2002) Remission Order {#visitor-visa-fee-world-youth-day-2002-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2001-109
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2001-11-07

Visitor Visa Fee (World Youth Day 2002) Remission Order

P.C. 2001-1913 2001-10-19
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of Citizenship and Immigration and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed [Visitor
Visa Fee (World Youth Day 2002) Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2001-109).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1991, c.
  24, s. 7(2)
  :::
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} of the fee of \$75, paid or payable under item 3 of
  the schedule to the Immigration Act Fees Regulations, for the
  processing of a duly completed application for a visitor visa to come
  into Canada once, on condition that the applicant is a participant in
  World Youth Day 2002; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} of the fee of \$150, paid or payable under item 4 of
  the schedule to the Immigration Act Fees Regulations, for the
  processing of a duly completed application for a visitor visa to come
  into Canada more than once, on condition that the applicant is a
  volunteer organizer of World Youth Day 2002 invited by the Canadian
  Conference of Catholic Bishops to facilitate and administer the
  activities of that event.
