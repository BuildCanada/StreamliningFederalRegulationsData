## United Nations Remission Order {#united-nations-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1320
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Order for the Remission of Certain Duties and Taxes on Goods Imported or
Purchased by the United Nations
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503440 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [United Nations Remission Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1320).

## [Free Entry, Refund or Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503442 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Authority is hereby
granted, effective January 2, 1952, for the refund or remission of the
excise stamp tax on official cheques and for free entry, refund or
remission of the following customs duties and the consumption or sales
tax imposed under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and the
[Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on the goods listed in the schedule,
when imported into Canada or purchased therein either for sale, use or
free distribution by the United Nations or its agents, subject to
compliance with the conditions and procedures set out in the schedule.
