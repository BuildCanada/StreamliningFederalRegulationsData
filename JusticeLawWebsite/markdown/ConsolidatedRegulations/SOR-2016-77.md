FAIR RAIL FOR GRAIN FARMERS ACT

Registration 2016-04-19

Order Establishing the Text of a Resolution Providing for the
Postponement of the Coming into Force of Subsections 5.1(2), 6(2), 7(2),

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to subsection 15(2) of the Fair Rail
for Grain Farmers Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2016-77_e_hq_17622){#fn_SOR-2016-77_e_hq_17622-ID0EDBAA},
makes the annexed [Order Establishing the Text of a Resolution Providing
for the Postponement of the Coming into Force of Subsections 5.1(2),
6(2), 7(2), 8(2), 9(2), 10(2), 11(2) and 12(2) of the Fair Rail for
Grain Farmers Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-77).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Text of the Resolution

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 15(1) of the Fair Rail for Grain Farmers Act, the text of the
resolution set out in the schedule is established.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.

::::: section
:::: {#838291 .Schedule}
::: {}
## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 1)]{.OriginatingRef}[Text of the Resolution]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-838292 .scheduleLabel}
:::

That, pursuant to subsection 15(1) of the Fair Rail for Grain Farmers
Act, the coming into force of subsections 5.1(2), 6(2), 7(2), 8(2),
9(2), 10(2), 11(2) and 12(2) of that Act on August 1, 2016 be postponed
for a period of one year.
