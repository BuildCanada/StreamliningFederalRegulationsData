## Metric Commission Order {#metric-commission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 972
:::

[DEPARTMENT OF INDUSTRY ACT](/eng/acts/I-9.2)

Order to Establish a Preparatory Commission for Conversion to the Metric
System
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-600683 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order maybe cited as
the [Metric Commission Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._972).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-600685 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Preparatory Commission
    for Metric Conversion established pursuant to section 3;
    ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Consumer and
    Corporate Affairs. ([Ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/82-25
:::

## [Establishment of Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-600691 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A committee is hereby
established to be known as the Preparatory Commission for Metric
Conversion, consisting of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a full-time member who shall be the Chairman of the
  Commission, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} not more than 20 part-time members.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may make
such rules as may be necessary for the conduct of its meetings and the
performance of its duties.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, upon the request of the Commission, provide
  the Commission with the services of such officers and employees
  employed by or in any department of the Government of Canada as are
  necessary for the proper conduct of the business of the Commission,
  and the Commission may obtain the advice and assistance of any
  department or agency of the Government of Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may engage such organizations or
  persons having a technical or specialized knowledge on any matter
  relating to the work of the Commission to advise and assist the
  Commission in the performance of its duties.

## [Purposes and Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-600699 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Commission is
established for the purpose of advising the Minister on plans for
conversion to the metric system, and for such purpose, the Commission
may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} initiate, coordinate and undertake investigations,
  surveys and studies relating to the implications of conversion to the
  metric system in different sectors of the Canadian economy;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prepare, in consultation and cooperation with any
  department or agency of the Government of Canada, any department or
  agency of the government of any province, the Standards Council of
  Canada, or other interested parties an overall program for conversion
  to the metric system which will ensure, as far as possible, that any
  programs for such conversion in the different sectors of the Canadian
  economy are phased and coordinated in order that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the benefits of conversion to the metric system are
    achieved at minimal cost, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} conversion to the metric system as a whole is
    effected to the best advantage to Canada; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} furnish, publish and disseminate information
  concerning conversion to the metric system.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Commission shall advise
the Minister on the need for legislation or any other action that may be
required to facilitate conversion to the metric system.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Commission shall make
such reports on its activities from time to time as the Minister may
require.

## [Oath]{.HTitleText1} {#h-600708 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Each member of the
Commission shall, before taking up his duties, take and subscribe an
oath or affirmation in the following form:

"I, ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}, solemnly and
sincerely swear (or affirm) that I will faithfully and honestly fulfil
the duties that devolve upon me by reason of my appointment as a member
of the Preparatory Commission for Metric Conversion and that I will not,
without due authority in that behalf, disclose or make known any matter
that comes to my knowledge by reason of such appointment.

(In the case where an oath is taken add, "So help me God")".
