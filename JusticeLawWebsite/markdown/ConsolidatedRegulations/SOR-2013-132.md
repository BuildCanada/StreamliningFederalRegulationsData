## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-793352 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Pension Benefits Standards
      Act, 1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[assets of the plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[assets of the plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} mean the assets of the
      plan that are attributable to a former member. ([éléments d'actif
      du régime]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[former member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[former member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a former member of the
      plan who is in receipt of an immediate pension benefit. ([ancien
      participant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[life income fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[life income fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registered
      retirement income fund, as defined in subsection 146.3(1) of the
      [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), that meets the requirements set
      out in paragraph 2(1)(b). ([fonds de revenu
      viager]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[locked-in registered retirement savings plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[locked-in registered retirement savings
      plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registered retirement savings
      plan, as defined in subsection 146(1) of the [Income Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), that meets the requirements set out in
      paragraph 2(1)(a). ([régime enregistré d'épargne-retraite
      immobilisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the pension plan in respect of
      which certificate of registration number 57573 has been issued by
      the Superintendent under the Act. ([régime]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided in these Regulations,
  words and expressions used in these Regulations have the same meaning
  as in the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer options

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite
  sections 18 and 36 of the Act, the administrator of the plan must, on
  request of a former member and with the consent of the spouse or
  common-law partner of the former member, if any, or on request of the
  survivor of the former member, transfer the assets of the plan to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a locked-in registered retirement savings plan that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} provides that the funds may only be

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} transferred to another locked-in registered
        retirement savings plan,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} transferred to a life income fund, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} used to purchase an immediate life annuity or a
        deferred life annuity,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} provides that, on the death of the holder of the
      locked-in registered retirement savings plan, the funds must be
      paid to the survivor of the holder by

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} transferring the funds to another locked-in
        registered retirement savings plan,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} transferring the funds to a life income fund,
        or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} using the funds to purchase an immediate life
        annuity or a deferred life annuity,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} provides that, subject to subsection 25(4) of
      the Act, the funds, and any interest or right in those funds, must
      not be transferred, charged, attached, anticipated or given as
      security and that any transaction appearing to do so is void or,
      in Quebec, null,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} provides that, if the assets of the plan that
      were transferred into the locked-in registered retirement savings
      plan were not varied according to the sex of the plan member, an
      immediate life annuity or a deferred life annuity purchased with
      the funds accumulated in the locked-in registered retirement
      savings plan must not differentiate as to sex,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} contains a statement as to whether the assets of
      the plan that were transferred were varied according to the sex of
      the plan member, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} sets out the method of determining the value of
      the locked-in registered retirement savings plan, including the
      valuation method used to establish its value on the date of death
      of the holder of the locked-in registered retirement savings plan
      or on the date of transfer of assets from the locked-in registered
      retirement savings plan; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a life income fund that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} provides that the funds may only be

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} transferred to a locked-in registered
        retirement savings plan,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} transferred to another life income fund, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} used to purchase an immediate life annuity or a
        deferred life annuity,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} provides that, on the death of the holder of the
      life income fund, the funds must be paid to the survivor of the
      holder by

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} transferring the funds to a locked-in
        registered retirement savings plan,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} transferring the funds to another life income
        fund, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} using the funds to purchase an immediate life
        annuity or a deferred life annuity,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} sets out the method of determining the value of
      the life income fund, including the valuation method used to
      establish its value on the date of death of the holder of the life
      income fund or on the date of transfer of assets from the life
      income fund,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} provides that the holder of the life income fund
      must, at the beginning of each calendar year or at any other time
      agreed to by the financial institution with whom the contract or
      arrangement was entered into, decide the amount to be paid out of
      the life income fund in that year,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} provides that in the event that the holder of the
      life income fund does not decide the amount to be paid out of the
      life income fund in a calendar year, the minimum amount determined
      in accordance with the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) must be
      paid out of the life income fund in that year,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} provides that, for any calendar year before the
      calendar year in which the holder of the life income fund reaches
      90 years of age, the amount of income paid out of the life income
      fund must not exceed the amount determined by the formula

      ::: {#793393 .Subparagraph}
      C/F

      where

      [C]{.dfn}
      :   is the balance in the life income fund
          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} at the beginning of the calendar year, or

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the amount determined under clause (A)
            is zero, at the date when the assets of the plan were
            transferred into the life income fund, and

      [F]{.dfn}
      :   is the value, as at the beginning of the calendar year, of a
          pension benefit of which the annual payment is \$1, payable on
          January 1 of each year between the beginning of that calendar
          year and December 31 of the year in which the holder reaches
          90 years of age, established using an interest rate that
          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} for the first 15 years after January 1 of
            the year in which the life income fund is valued, is less
            than or equal to the monthly average yield on Government of
            Canada marketable bonds of maturity over 10 years, as
            published by the Bank of Canada, for the second month before
            the beginning of the calendar year, and

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} for any subsequent year, is not more than
            6%,
      :::

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} provides that, for the calendar year in which
      the holder of the life income fund reaches 90 years of age and for
      all subsequent calendar years, the amount of income paid out of
      the life income fund must not exceed the value of the funds held
      in the fund immediately before the date of the payment,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} provides that, for the calendar year in which
      the contract or arrangement was entered into, the amount of income
      paid out of the life income fund, as referred to in
      subparagraph (vi) or (vii), as the case may be, must be multiplied
      by the number of months remaining in that year and then divided by
      12, with any part of an incomplete month counting as one month,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} provides that if, on the day on which the life
      income fund was established, part of the life income fund was
      composed of funds that had been held in another life income fund
      of the holder earlier in the calendar year in which the fund was
      established, the amount of income paid out of the life income
      fund, as referred to in subparagraph (vi) or (vii) as the case may
      be, is deemed to be zero in respect of that part of the life
      income fund for that calendar year;

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} provides that, subject to subsection 25(4) of the
      Act, the funds, and any interest or right in those funds, must not
      be transferred, charged, attached, anticipated or given as
      security and that any transaction appearing to do so is void or,
      in Quebec, null,

    - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} contains a statement as to whether the assets of
      the plan that were transferred were varied according to the sex of
      the plan member, and

    - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} provides that, if the assets of the plan that
      were transferred into a life income fund were not varied according
      to the sex of the plan member, an immediate life annuity or a
      deferred life annuity purchased with the funds accumulated in the
      life income fund must not differentiate as to sex.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request to transfer and the consent must be
  provided in Forms 1 and 2 respectively of the schedule within 90 days
  after receipt of the statement required under section 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The administrator of the
plan must, together with the statement required under subsection 28(2.1)
of the Act, provide a written statement to each former member and their
spouse or common-law partner, if any, or to the survivor of the former
member, that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicates the transfer options set out in section 2;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} describes the restrictions applicable to those
  transfer options;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} informs the former member or survivor that, if they
  do no make a request under section 2, the administrator must purchase
  a life annuity on their behalf;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} discloses the following risks associated with
  electing the transfer rather than having a life annuity purchased on
  their behalf by the administrator:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the amount of pension income could be
    significantly reduced if the future investment performance is lower
    than expected; and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that the income produced could be insufficient to
    provide the same retirement income that would otherwise have been
    received even if the future investment performance equals or exceeds
    the return that was expected; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} sets out the amount determined under section 4 and
  the amount that the former member or survivor would receive under a
  life annuity purchased on their behalf.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assets attributable to a former member

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The amount of the assets of
the plan is determined by the formula

::: {#793420 .Section}
A - B + C

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the commuted value of the former member's pension as of the
    effective date of the termination of the plan determined in
    accordance with section 3500 of the Standards of Practice of the
    Actuarial Standards Board, published by the Canadian Institute of
    Actuaries, as amended from time to time,

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the sum of any pension payments made from the effective date of
    the termination of the plan until the day on which the transfer is
    made, and

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the amount of any accumulated interest, calculated at the rate
    that is used in the termination report to calculate the commuted
    value of pension benefits, from the effective date of the
    termination of the plan to the beginning of the month in which the
    transfer is made.
:::
