## Prescribed Province Pension Regulations {#prescribed-province-pension-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 391
:::

[CANADA PENSION PLAN](/eng/acts/C-8)

Regulations Prescribing the Province of Quebec as a Province Providing a
Comprehensive Pension Plan
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-553727 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Prescribed Province Pension
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._391).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-553729 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Province of Quebec is
hereby prescribed, for the purposes of the [Canada Pension
Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), as a province the government of which has, before
the 30th day after April 3, 1965, signified the intention of such
province to provide for the establishment and operation in that
province, in lieu of the operation therein of the [Canada Pension
Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), of a plan of old age pensions and supplementary
benefits providing for the making of contributions thereunder,
commencing with the year 1966 and providing for the payment of benefits
thereunder comparable to those provided by the [Canada Pension
Plan](/eng/acts/C-8).
