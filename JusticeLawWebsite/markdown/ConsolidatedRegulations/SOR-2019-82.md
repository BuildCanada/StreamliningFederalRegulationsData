## Order Extending the Application of Section 14 of that Act for a Period of Five Years {#order-extending-the-application-of-section-14-of-that-act-for-a-period-of-five-years .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2019-82
:::

[CITIZENSHIP ACT](/eng/acts/C-29)

Registration 2019-03-28

Order Extending the Application of Section 14 of that Act for a Period
of Five Years
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, pursuant to subsection
28.1(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-3663_hq_23655){#fn_81000-2-3663_hq_23655-ID0EECCA}
of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-3663_hq_23656){#fn_81000-2-3663_hq_23656-ID0EBCCA},
extends the application of section 14 of that Act for a period of five
years, ending on July 31, 2024.

  [[Return to footnote

  2014, c. 22, s. 27
  :::

  :::
