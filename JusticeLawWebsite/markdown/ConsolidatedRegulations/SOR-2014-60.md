## Special Economic Measures (Ukraine) Regulations {#special-economic-measures-ukraine-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2014-60
:::

[SPECIAL ECONOMIC MEASURES ACT](/eng/acts/S-14.5)

Registration 2014-03-17

Special Economic Measures (Ukraine) Regulations

P.C. 2014-284 2014-03-17
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that the situation in
Ukraine constitutes a grave breach of international peace and security
that has resulted or is likely to result in a serious international
crisis;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to
subsections 4(1) to (3) of the [Special Economic Measures
Act](/eng/acts/S-14.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-1947-revised-E_hq_13243){#fn_81000-2-1947-revised-E_hq_13243-ID0EDBAA},
makes the annexed [Special Economic Measures (Ukraine)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-60).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-811190 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Vienna Convention on
    Diplomatic Relations, done at Vienna on April 18, 1961.
     ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Crimea region of Ukraine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Crimea region of Ukraine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Autonomous Republic of Crimea and the city of Sevastopol and
    includes their land areas and territorial sea. ([région de Crimée de
    l'Ukraine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is in
    Ukraine, or is or was a national of Ukraine who does not ordinarily
    reside in Canada, and whose name is listed in the schedule.
     ([personne désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[DNR region of Ukraine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[DNR region of Ukraine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2022-202, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[LNR region of Ukraine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[LNR region of Ukraine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2022-202, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Foreign
    Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[occupied region]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[occupied region]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the so-called Donetsk People's Republic and the
      territory it controls in the Donetsk oblast of Ukraine;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the so-called Luhansk People's Republic and the
      territory it controls in the Luhansk oblast of Ukraine;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the area of the Kherson oblast of Ukraine that is
      illegally occupied by the Russian Federation; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the area of the Zaporizhzhia oblast of Ukraine
      that is illegally occupied by the Russian Federation. ([région
      occupée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a benefit paid under the [Old
    Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9), the [Canada Pension
    Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or an Act respecting the Quebec Pension Plan,
    CQLR, c. R-9, any superannuation, pension or benefit paid under or
    in respect of any retirement savings plan or under any retirement
    plan, any amount paid under or in respect of the [Garnishment,
    Attachment and Pension Diversion Act](/eng/acts/G-2) or the [Pension
    Benefits Division Act](/eng/acts/P-6.7), and any other payment made
    in respect of disability.  ([pension]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[technical assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[technical assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any form of
    assistance, such as providing instruction, training, consulting
    services or technical advice or transferring know-how or technical
    data. ([aide technique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Ukraine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Ukraine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any political subdivision of Ukraine;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the government, and any department, of Ukraine or
      of a political subdivision of Ukraine; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any agency of Ukraine or of a political
      subdivision of Ukraine. ([Ukraine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [List]{.HTitleText1} {#h-811204 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Schedule

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A person whose name is
listed in the schedule is a person in respect of whom the Governor in
Council, on the recommendation of the Minister, is satisfied that there
are reasonable grounds to believe is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person engaged in activities that directly or
  indirectly facilitate, support, provide funding for or contribute to a
  violation or attempted violation of the sovereignty or territorial
  integrity of Ukraine or that obstruct the work of international
  organizations in Ukraine;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} a current or former member of the Government of the
  so-called Donetsk People's Republic or the so-called Luhansk People's
  Republic;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an associate of a person referred to in paragraph (a)
  or (a.1);

- [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} a family member of a person referred to in any of
  paragraphs (a) to (b) and (e);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an entity owned, held or controlled, directly or
  indirectly, by a person referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to (b.1),
  or acting on behalf of or at the direction of such a person;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an entity whose ownership or control was purported to
  be altered by a person who has violated or attempted to violate the
  sovereignty or territorial integrity of Ukraine; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a senior official of an entity engaged in activities
  described in paragraph (a) or of an entity referred to in
  paragraph (c) or (d).

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-811214 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited transactions and activities
--- designated persons

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada and any Canadian outside Canada to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deal in any property, wherever situated, held by or
  on behalf of a designated person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into or facilitate, directly or indirectly, any
  transaction related to a dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial or other related service in
  respect of a dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} make any goods, wherever situated, available to a
  designated person; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial or related service to or for
  the benefit of a designated person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Section 3 does not apply in
respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any payment made by or on behalf of a designated
  person that is due under a contract entered into before the person
  became a designated person, provided that the payment is not made to
  or for the benefit of a designated person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pension payments to any person in Canada or any
  Canadian outside Canada;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any transaction in respect of any account at a
  financial institution held by a diplomatic mission, if the transaction
  is required in order for the mission to fulfill its diplomatic
  functions as set out in Article 3 of the Convention or, if the
  diplomatic mission has been temporarily or permanently recalled, when
  the transaction is required in order to maintain the mission premises;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any transaction to international organizations with
  diplomatic status, a United Nations agency, the International Red
  Cross and Red Crescent Movement, or Canadian non-governmental
  organizations that have entered into a grant or contribution agreement
  with the Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any transactions necessary for a Canadian to transfer
  to a non-designated person any accounts, funds or investments of a
  Canadian held by a designated person on the day on which that person
  became a designated person;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} financial services required in order for a designated
  person to obtain legal services in Canada with respect to the
  application of any of the prohibitions set out in these Regulations;
  and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} loan repayments made to any person in Canada, or any
  Canadian outside Canada, in respect of loans entered into before the
  coming into force of these Regulations, enforcement of security in
  respect of those loans, or payments by guarantors guaranteeing those
  loans.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited activities --- Crimea region
of Ukraine

**[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** With respect to the
Crimea region of Ukraine, it is prohibited for any person in Canada and
any Canadian outside Canada to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} make an investment that involves a dealing in any
  property located in that region that is owned, held or controlled by
  the Crimea region of Ukraine or a person in the Crimea region of
  Ukraine, or a person acting on behalf of or at the direction of the
  Crimea region of Ukraine or a person in the Crimea region of Ukraine;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide or acquire financial or other related
  services with respect to an investment referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} import, purchase or acquire goods, wherever situated,
  from the Crimea region of Ukraine or any person in the Crimea region
  of Ukraine;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} export goods destined for the Crimea region of
  Ukraine or sell, supply or transfer goods, wherever situated, to any
  person in the Crimea region of Ukraine;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide technical assistance to the Crimea region in
  Ukraine or any person in the Crimea region of Ukraine;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} provide financial or other services related to
  tourism to, or acquire such services from, the Crimea region of
  Ukraine or any person in the Crimea region of Ukraine; or

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} dock a cruise ship in the Crimea region of Ukraine
  that is registered or licensed, or for which an identification number
  has been issued, under any Act of Parliament.

## [Obligations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-811259 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to determine

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Each of the following
entities must determine on a continuing basis whether it is in
possession or control of property owned or controlled by or on behalf of
a designated person:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[authorized foreign banks]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
  as defined in section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), in
  respect of their business in Canada or banks to which that Act
  applies;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cooperative credit societies, savings and credit
  unions and [caisses populaires]{.otherLang lang="fr"} regulated by a
  provincial Act and associations regulated by the [Cooperative Credit
  Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[foreign companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
  defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), in respect of their insurance business in
  Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[provincial
  companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
  [[societies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms are defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fraternal benefit societies regulated by a provincial
  Act in respect of their insurance activities and insurance companies
  and other entities engaged in the business of insuring risks that are
  regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} companies to which the [Trust and Loan Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) applies;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} trust companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} loan companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} entities that engage in any activity described in
  paragraph 5(h) of the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and
  Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501), if the activity involves
  the opening of an account for a client; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} entities authorized under provincial legislation to
  engage in the business of dealing in securities, or to provide
  portfolio management or investment counselling services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person in Canada and every Canadian outside Canada must disclose
  without delay to the Commissioner of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the existence of property in their possession or
    control that they have reason to believe is owned or controlled,
    directly or indirectly, by a designated person or by an entity owned
    or controlled by a designated person; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information about a transaction or proposed
    transaction in respect of property referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No proceedings under the [Special Economic Measures
  Act](/eng/acts/S-14.5) and no civil proceedings lie against a person
  for a disclosure made in good faith under subsection (1).

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-811279 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to no longer be a designated
person

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  designated person may apply in writing to the Minister to have their
  name removed from the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of the application, the Minister must
  decide whether there are reasonable grounds to recommend to the
  Governor in Council that the applicant's name be removed from the
  schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must make a decision on the application
  within 90 days after the day on which the application is received.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must give notice without delay to the
  applicant of the decision taken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If there has been a material change in circumstances
  since the last application was submitted, a person may submit another
  application under subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for a certificate

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  claiming not to be a designated person may apply to the Minister for a
  certificate stating that they are not the person who has been
  designated under section 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If it is established that the person is not a
  designated person, the Minister must issue a certificate to the
  applicant within 30 days after the day on which the application is
  received.
