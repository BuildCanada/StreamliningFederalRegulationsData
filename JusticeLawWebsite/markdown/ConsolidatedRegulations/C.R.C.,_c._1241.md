## Minister Designation Order (Nuclear Liability Act) {#minister-designation-order-nuclear-liability-act .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1241
:::

[NUCLEAR LIABILITY ACT](/eng/acts/N-28)

Order Designating the Minister for the Purposes of the Nuclear Liability
Act
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499836 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Minister Designation Order (Nuclear Liability
Act)](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1241).

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499838 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Energy,
Mines and Resources is hereby designated as the member of the Queen's
Privy Council for Canada to act as the Minister for the purposes of the
[Nuclear Liability Act](/eng/acts/N-28).
