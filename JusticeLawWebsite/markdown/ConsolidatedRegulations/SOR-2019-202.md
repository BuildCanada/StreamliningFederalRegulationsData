## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1139332 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Order.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cannabis
      Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[book value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[book value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the value of an asset
      according to its balance sheet account balance. ([valeur
      comptable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} )

  [[cannabis extract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cannabis extract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in subsection 1(1) of the Regulations. ([extrait de
      cannabis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[cannabis product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cannabis product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in subsection 1(2) of the Regulations. ([produit du
      cannabis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[edible cannabis]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[edible cannabis]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 1(1) of the Regulations. ([cannabis
      comestible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[licence for analytical testing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[licence for analytical testing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      licence referred to in paragraph 8(1)(c) of the
      Regulations. ([licences d'essais analytiques]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[licence for cultivation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[licence for cultivation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence
      referred to in paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Regulations. ([licence de
      culture]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[licence for processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[licence for processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence
      referred to in paragraph 8(1)(b) of the Regulations. ([licence de
      transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[licence for research]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[licence for research]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence
      referred to in paragraph 8(1)(e) of the Regulations. ([licence de
      recherche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[licence for sale for medical purposes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[licence for sale for medical
      purposes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence referred to in
      subsection 8(5) of the Regulations. ([licence de vente à des fins
      médicales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[public body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[public body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a Crown
      corporation. ([organisme public]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cannabis
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144). ([Règlement]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
      1(2) of the Regulations. ([lieu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[unpackaged cannabis]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[unpackaged cannabis]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means cannabis other
      than cannabis contained in a cannabis product. ([cannabis non
      emballé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Order,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cannabis contained in a cannabis product includes
    cannabis that is a cannabis product; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a reference to the sale or distribution of cannabis
    --- including cannabis products --- does not include

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the return of cannabis,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the sale or distribution of cannabis for the
      purposes of destruction, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the import or export of cannabis.

## [Federal Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1139357 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be provided

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A holder
  of a licence for cultivation, a licence for processing or a licence
  for sale for medical purposes that authorizes the possession of
  cannabis must, no later than the 15th day of each month, provide the
  Minister with the following information, as applicable, in respect of
  the site specified in the licence:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of cannabis products that formed part of
    the inventory on the first day of the previous month;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of unpackaged cannabis that formed
    part of the inventory on the first day of the previous month;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number of cannabis products that were added to
    the inventory during the previous month by virtue of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the sale or distribution of cannabis products to
      the holder,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the packaging and labelling of cannabis, or a
      cannabis accessory containing cannabis, by the holder for sale to
      consumers at the retail level,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the return of cannabis products to the holder,
      or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any other reason;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of unpackaged cannabis, other than
    cultivation waste, that was added to the inventory during the
    previous month by virtue of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} being sold or distributed to the holder,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} being produced from other unpackaged cannabis,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} being imported,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} being returned to the holder, or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any other reason;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the number of cannabis products that ceased to form
    part of the inventory during the previous month by virtue of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the sale or distribution of cannabis products,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the destruction of cannabis products,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} being lost or stolen in circumstances that
      require notice to be provided under paragraph 246(1)(b) of the
      Regulations,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the return of cannabis products by the holder,
      or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any other reason;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number and book value of cannabis products that
    ceased to form part of the inventory during the previous month by
    virtue of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the retail sale of cannabis products to
      consumers, other than as a result of a purchase order referred to
      in section 289 of the Regulations,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the sale of cannabis products as a result of a
      purchase order referred to in section 289 of the Regulations, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the sale or distribution of cannabis products
      to a person that is authorized under a provincial Act referred to
      in subsection 69(1) of the Act to sell cannabis, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the sale or distribution of cannabis products to
      another holder of a licence for cultivation, a licence for
      processing or a licence for sale for medical purposes;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of unpackaged cannabis, other than
    cultivation waste, that ceased to form part of the inventory during
    the previous month by virtue of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} being sold or distributed by the holder to
      another holder of a licence for cultivation, a licence for
      processing, a licence for sale for medical purposes, a licence for
      analytical testing or a licence for research,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} being used to produce other unpackaged cannabis,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} being packaged and labelled, either alone or
      within a cannabis accessory, by the holder for sale to consumers
      at the retail level,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} being destroyed,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} being lost due to drying or other normally
      accepted business activities,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} being lost or stolen in circumstances that
      require notice to be provided under paragraph 246(1)(b) of the
      Regulations,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} being returned by the holder, or

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} any other reason;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of unpackaged cannabis used in the
    production of cannabis of a class set out in any of items 8 to 14 of
    Schedule 2, in relation to each class;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the quantity and book value of unpackaged cannabis
    that ceased to form part of the inventory during the previous month
    by virtue of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} being sold or distributed to another holder of a
      licence for cultivation, a licence for processing or a licence for
      sale for medical purposes, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} being exported;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the number and book value of cannabis products, and
    the quantity of cannabis contained in those cannabis products, that
    formed part of the inventory on the last day of the previous month;
    and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the quantity and book value of unpackaged cannabis
    that formed part of the inventory on the last day of the previous
    month.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Number, quantity and book value by
  class

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In respect of cannabis products,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of cannabis products referred to in
    paragraphs (1)(a), (c), (e), (f) and (j) must be provided for each
    class specified in column 1 of Schedule 1;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of cannabis referred to in paragraph
    (1)(j) must be provided for each class specified in column 1 of
    Schedule 1 and expressed in the applicable unit of measurement
    specified in column 2; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the book value of cannabis products referred to in
    paragraph (1)(j) must be provided for each class specified in column
    1 of Schedule 1 and expressed in Canadian dollars, net of sales tax.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quantity and book value by class

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In respect of unpackaged cannabis,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quantities of unpackaged cannabis referred to
    in paragraphs (1)(b), (d), (g) and (k) must be provided for each
    class of unpackaged cannabis specified in column 1 of Schedule 2 and
    expressed in the applicable unit of measurement specified in column
    2;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of unpackaged cannabis referred to in
    paragraph (1)(i) must be provided for each class of unpackaged
    cannabis specified in items 1, 2, 4 and 5 and 8 to 14 of column 1 of
    Schedule 2 and expressed in the applicable unit of measurement
    specified in column 2; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the book value of unpackaged cannabis referred to
    in paragraphs (1)(i) and (k) must be provided for each applicable
    class of unpackaged cannabis and expressed in Canadian dollars, net
    of sales tax.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Number and book value by province and
  class

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In respect of the cannabis products referred to in
  paragraph (1)(f),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of cannabis products must be provided
    for each province in which the persons to which the cannabis
    products are sold or distributed are located and for each class
    specified in column 1 of Schedule 1; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the book value must be provided for each province
    in which the persons to which the cannabis products are sold or
    distributed are located and for each class specified in column 1 of
    Schedule 1 and expressed in Canadian dollars, net of sales tax.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quantity and book value by province and
  class

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In respect of the unpackaged cannabis referred to in
  paragraph (1)(i),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quantity must be provided for each province in
    which the persons to which the unpackaged cannabis is sold are
    located and for each class specified in column 1 of Schedule 2;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity must be expressed in the applicable
    unit of measurement specified in column 2 of Schedule 2; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the book value must be provided for each province
    in which the persons to which the unpackaged cannabis is sold are
    located and for each class specified in column 1 of Schedule 2 and
    expressed in Canadian dollars, net of sales tax.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of activities

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A holder of a licence for cultivation, a licence for
  processing or a licence for sale for medical purposes that ceases to
  conduct all the activities authorized by the licence must, within 15
  days after the day on which the activities cease, provide any
  information that has yet to be provided under subsection (1) in
  respect of the previous month, as well as information in respect of
  the month in which the activities cease.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[cultivation
  waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), [[cultivation
  waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection 1(2)
  of the Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  information that is provided under section 2 must be accompanied by
  the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licence holder's licence number;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the month and calendar year to which the
    information relates; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of information that is provided by a
    holder of a licence for cultivation or a licence for processing, the
    total surface area that is authorized by the licence, in square
    metres, of all buildings on the site;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of information that is provided by a
    holder of a licence for cultivation, the total surface area that is
    used for cultivating, propagating or harvesting cannabis,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of indoor cultivation, in square
      metres, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of outdoor cultivation, in hectares;
      and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of information that is provided by a
    holder of a licence for processing, the total surface area, in
    square metres, that is used to produce cannabis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surface area --- calculation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the surface area referred to in paragraph (1)(d)
  consists of multiple surfaces, such as surfaces arranged above one
  another, the area of each surface must be included in the calculation
  of the total surface area.

## [Sale Authorized Under Provincial Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1139433 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public body

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A public
  body that is authorized under an Act of a province to sell cannabis
  must, no later than the 15th day of each month, provide the following
  information to the Minister in respect of each location at which the
  body sells cannabis or from which it sends or delivers cannabis:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of cannabis products that formed part of
    the inventory on the first day of the previous month;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of cannabis products that were added to
    the inventory during the previous month by virtue of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the sale or distribution of cannabis products,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the return of cannabis products, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any other reason;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number of cannabis products that ceased to form
    part of the inventory during the previous month by virtue of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the sale or distribution of cannabis products,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the destruction of cannabis products,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the loss or theft of cannabis products,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the return of cannabis products, or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any other reason;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number and book value of cannabis products that
    ceased to form part of the inventory during the previous month by
    virtue of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the retail sale of cannabis products to consumers
      who were present at the location at the time of sale,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the retail sale of cannabis products to
      consumers who were not present at the location at the time of
      sale, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the sale of cannabis products to persons that
      are authorized to sell them or the distribution of cannabis
      products to other locations at which such products are sold or
      distributed;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the number and book value of cannabis products, and
    the quantity of cannabis contained in those cannabis products, that
    formed part of the inventory on the last day of the previous month;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an indication of whether, during the previous
    month, the primary activity was

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the sale of cannabis products to persons that are
      authorized to sell them or the distribution of cannabis products
      to other locations at which such products are sold or distributed,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the retail sale of cannabis products to
      consumers;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the business name of the person at that location
    that is selling, sending or delivering cannabis;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the unique identifier assigned to the location by
    the public body or, failing that, by the Minister;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the municipality in which the location
    is situated, if any, and the postal code of the location; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the month and calendar year to which the
    information referred to in paragraphs (a) to (f) relates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Number and book value --- not in
  inventory

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In respect of the cannabis products referred to in
  paragraph (1)(d),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of cannabis products must be provided
    for each province in which the persons to which the cannabis
    products are sold or distributed are located and for each class
    specified in column 1 of Schedule 1; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the book value must be provided for each province
    in which the persons to which the cannabis products are sold or
    distributed are located and for each class specified in column 1 of
    Schedule 1 and expressed in Canadian dollars, net of sales tax.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Number, quantity and book value ---
  cannabis products in inventory

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In respect of the cannabis products referred to in
  paragraph (1)(e),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of cannabis products must be provided
    for each class specified in column 1 of Schedule 1;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of cannabis contained in those
    cannabis products must be provided for each class specified in
    column 1 of Schedule 1 and expressed in the applicable unit of
    measurement specified in column 2; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the book value must be provided for each class
    specified in column 1 of Schedule 1 and expressed in Canadian
    dollars, net of sales tax.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of activities

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A public body referred to in subsection (1) that
  ceases to conduct all authorized activities at a location must, within
  15 days after the day on which the activities cease, provide any
  information that has yet to be provided under that subsection in
  respect of the previous month, as well as information in respect of
  the month in which the activities cease.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public body

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The public body to which the information must be
  provided is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the following cases, the public body that is
    responsible for authorizing the sale of cannabis in the province:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of cannabis products must be provided
    for each province in which the persons to which the cannabis
    products are sold or distributed are located and for each class

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the book value must be provided for each province
    in which the persons to which the cannabis products are sold or
    distributed are located and for each class specified in column 1 of
    Schedule 1 and expressed in Canadian dollars, net of sales tax.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Number, quantity and book value ---
  cannabis products in inventory

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In respect of the cannabis products referred to in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of cannabis products must be provided
    for each class specified in column 1 of Schedule 1;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of cannabis contained in those
    cannabis products must be provided for each class specified in
    column 1 of Schedule 1 and expressed in the applicable unit of
    measurement specified in column 2; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the book value must be provided for each class
    specified in column 1 of Schedule 1 and expressed in Canadian
    dollars, net of sales tax.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person is required to provide the information
  referred to in subsection (1) only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the public body to which the information is to be
    provided is required --- or the public body or the government of the
    province agrees --- to provide the information to the Minister; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a notice specifying the effective date of the
    requirement or agreement is published on the Government of Canada
    website or is provided to the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition no longer met

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the condition set out in paragraph (5)(a) ceases
  to be met, the Minister must ensure that a notice to that effect is
  published on the Government of Canada website or is provided to the
  affected persons as soon as feasible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of activities

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A person referred to in subsection (1) that ceases to
  conduct all authorized activities at a location must, within 15 days
  after the day on which the activities cease, provide any information
  that has yet to be provided under that subsection in respect of the
  previous month, as well as information in respect of the month in
  which the activities cease.

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1139520 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of providing information

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A holder of a licence, or a
public body, that is required to provide information under this Order
must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide the information through the dedicated website
  established by the Minister for that purpose; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify the Minister if an individual who was
  responsible for submitting the information on behalf of the holder or
  body is no longer responsible for doing so.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  that is required to provide information under this Order must ensure
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the records, reports, electronic data and other
    documents containing the information are retained for a period of at
    least two years beginning on the day on which the information is
    provided;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the records, reports, electronic data and other
    documents --- and any information on which the information contained
    in those documents is based --- are

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} retained in a manner that will enable an audit of
      the documents or information to be made in a timely manner,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the person holds a licence issued under
      subsection 62(1) of the Act, retained at the site specified in the
      licence, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the person does not hold a licence issued
      under subsection 62(1) of the Act, retained at their place of
      business in Canada or, if they do not have such a place of
      business, at a place of business in Canada; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the calculations, measurements and other data on
    which the information is based are documented in a manner that will
    enable them to be examined in a timely manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continued retention

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person is no longer required to provide
  information under this Order, they must ensure that the requirements
  set out in subsection (1) are complied with until the end of the
  applicable retention period.
