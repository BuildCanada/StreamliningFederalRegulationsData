## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Application and Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-680383 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-680384 .Subheading}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided by the Act or any other Act of Parliament, these Rules apply to
all proceedings in the Court.

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-680386 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Rules.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Supreme Court
    Act](/eng/acts/S-26). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[affidavit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[affidavit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an affirmation and a
    statutory declaration. ([affidavit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a lawyer practising in the
    National Capital Region within the meaning of the [National Capital
    Act](/eng/acts/N-4). ([correspondant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[application for leave to appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[application for leave to appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    application for leave to appeal referred to in Rule 25 and section
    40 of the Act. ([demande d'autorisation d'appel]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[attorney general]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[attorney general]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Attorney General
    of Canada, an attorney general of a province or a minister of
    Justice of a territory. ([procureur général]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[authorities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes legislative enactments,
    case law, articles and texts, treaties and excerpts from them.
    ([sources]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commissioner for oaths]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commissioner for oaths]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any person
    authorized under federal or provincial legislation or rules of
    practice, as the case may be, to conduct examinations. ([commissaire
    à l'assermentation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[counsel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[counsel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of the bar of a
    province. ([procureur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[electronic filing portal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[electronic filing portal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    electronic filing portal on the Court's website or on any other
    website specified in a direction given by the Court. ([portail de
    dépôt électronique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[holiday]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[holiday]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a holiday as defined in the
    [Interpretation Act](/eng/acts/I-21) and includes Saturday. ([jour
    férié]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[last known address]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[last known address]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the address that
    appears on the most recently filed document, whether it is a
    document filed in the Court or in the court appealed from, or, if
    applicable, on the notice of change that was most recently served
    and filed in accordance with Rule 17.1. ([dernière adresse
    connue]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[last known email address]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[last known email address]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the email
    address that appears on the most recently filed document, whether it
    is a document filed in the Court or in the court appealed from, or,
    if applicable, on the notice of change that was most recently served
    and filed in accordance with Rule 17.1. ([dernière adresse de
    courriel connue]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[last known fax number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[last known fax number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the fax number
    that appears on the most recently filed document, whether it is a
    document filed in the Court or in the court appealed from, or, if
    applicable, on the notice of change that was most recently served
    and filed in accordance with Rule 17.1. ([dernier numéro de
    télécopieur connu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person named in the style of
    cause in accordance with Rule 22 including any person added or
    substituted as a party under Rule 18, but where referring to the
    court appealed from, it means a person who was a party in that
    court. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a body politic or corporate,
    His Majesty the King and an attorney
    general. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[printing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[printing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes reproduction by any means
    but does not include hand writing. ([impression]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an appeal, application for
    leave to appeal, motion or reference before the Court, a judge or
    the Registrar. ([procédure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Where No Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-680406 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Whenever
  these Rules contain no provision for exercising a right or procedure,
  the Court, a judge or the Registrar may adopt any procedure that is
  not inconsistent with these Rules or the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party may, on motion to a judge or the Registrar,
  request directions as to the procedure referred to in subrule (1).

### [Computation of Time]{.HTitleText2} {#h-680418 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  computation of time under these Rules or under an order of the Court,
  a judge or the Registrar is governed by the [Interpretation
  Act](/eng/acts/I-21).

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} If the filing and serving are to be done within a
  specified number of weeks after a specified day or event, the period
  is calculated by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} excluding that day or the day of the event; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} including the last day of the last seven-day
    period.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A day that is a holiday shall not be included in
  computing a period of less than six days under these Rules.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The month of July shall not be included in the
  computation of time under these Rules except for the service and
  filing of a notice of intervention under subrule 33(4), a factum,
  record or book of authorities on an appeal or a cross-appeal under
  Rules 35 to 37 and a motion for intervention under paragraph 56(b),
  including any response or reply.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A day that is a holiday and the month of July shall
  be included in the computation of time under an order of the Court, a
  judge or the Registrar.

### [Extension or Abridgment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-680432 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Court, a judge or unless these Rules provide otherwise, the Registrar
  may, on motion or on their own initiative, extend or abridge a period
  provided for by these Rules.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The affidavit in support of a motion for an extension
  or abridgement of time shall set out the reason for the delay or
  urgency, as the case may be.

### [Adjournment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-680436 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Justice or, in his or her absence or at his or her request, any
  other judge may, on motion or on the judge's own initiative, adjourn
  the hearing of any proceeding.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The affidavit in support of a motion for an
  adjournment shall set out the facts or reasons for the adjournment.

### [Dispensing with Compliance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-680440 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Court, a judge or unless these Rules provide otherwise, the Registrar
  may, on motion or on their own initiative, excuse a party from
  complying with any of these Rules.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Court, a judge or the Registrar may refuse a
  document that does not comply with these Rules or that has not been
  served in accordance with these Rules or an order of the Court, a
  judge or the Registrar.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Documents that do not comply with these Rules may be
  excluded from costs upon the order of the Court, a judge or the
  Registrar.

### [Registrar to Keep Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-680450 .Subheading}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Registrar shall keep all records necessary for documenting the
  proceedings of the Court.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For each proceeding, the Registrar shall keep

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all documents and correspondence, including emails,
    that are filed in relation to a proceeding before the Court and all
    correspondence, judgments and orders of the Court that relate to
    that proceeding and that are delivered to the parties; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an electronic register in which all activities in
    the proceeding are recorded.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If only one version of a document is filed, whether
  printed or electronic, that version shall be the official version. If
  both printed and electronic versions are filed the printed version
  shall be the official version unless the Registrar declares otherwise.

### [Official Languages]{.HTitleText2} {#h-680459 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may use either English or French in any oral or written
  communication with the Court.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (3), the Registrar shall provide
  to the parties services for simultaneous interpretation in both
  official languages during the hearing of every proceeding.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a motion to be heard by a judge or the
  Registrar, the Registrar shall provide the services referred to in
  subrule (2) upon request of any party to the motion, made at least two
  days before the hearing of the motion.

### [Name of Party]{.HTitleText2} {#h-680472 .Subheading}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  case of an application for leave to appeal, an appeal referred to in
  paragraph 33(1)(b) or (c) or an originating motion, any party ---
  other than an individual --- must file with the Registrar a notice of

  Any changes that affect the notice of name must be submitted in

### [Representation of Parties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-680476 .Subheading}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to Rule 17, counsel for a
  party in the court appealed from is deemed to be counsel before the
  Court.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (3), a party may either act in
  person or be represented by counsel.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any party, other than an individual, shall be
  represented by counsel unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it was permitted to be represented by a person
    other than counsel in any of the lower courts and it chooses to
    continue to be represented by that person in the Court; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on motion, a judge grants it leave to be
    represented by a person other than counsel in accordance with the
    applicable federal or provincial legislative enactment.

### [Change of Representation or Withdrawal of Counsel or Agent]{.HTitleText2} {#h-680494 .Subheading}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may change their counsel or agent by identifying the new counsel
  or agent on the originating document, or during the course of the
  proceeding, by serving on all other parties and filing with the
  Registrar a notice of change setting out the name, address and
  telephone number, and the fax number and email address, if any, of the
  new counsel or agent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party who was represented by counsel in the court
  appealed from may elect to act in person by signing the originating
  document.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party who was not represented by counsel in the
  court appealed from may elect to be represented by counsel by serving
  on all other parties and filing with the Registrar a notice to that
  effect.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A counsel may choose to cease to represent a party
  before the Court by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serving on all other parties and filing with the
    Registrar a notice of withdrawal of counsel accompanied by the
    consent of the party; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the absence of the consent of the party, making
    a motion to a judge or the Registrar for an order that the counsel
    no longer represents the party, and serving on that party and all
    other parties the motion and, if the motion is granted, the order of
    withdrawal.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} An agent may cease to represent a party by serving on
  that party and all other parties and filing with the Registrar a
  notice of withdrawal.

### [Adding, Substituting and Removing Parties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-680509 .Subheading}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2013-175, s. 9

\]
:::

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may be added or substituted as a party on motion before a judge
  or the Registrar that sets out the reasons for the addition or
  substitution.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (5), no person shall be added or
  substituted as a party without the person's consent being filed with
  the Registrar.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The motion referred to in subrule (1) shall also be
  served on the proposed added or substituted party.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Parties added or substituted shall be served with all
  documents provided for in these Rules, and any time periods shall
  begin as provided for in the order unless a judge or the Registrar
  otherwise orders.

- [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} A party who is named in the style of cause can be
  removed on the consent of the applicant or the appellant, as the case
  may be, or on motion before a judge or the Registrar that sets out the
  reasons for the removal.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} In any proceeding, the Court or a judge may order
  that a party be added, substituted or removed if, in the opinion of
  the Court or the judge, the addition, substitution or removal is
  necessary to enable the Court to adjudicate the questions in issue.

- SOR/2013-175, s. 10

### [Filing of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-680521 .Subheading}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (1.1), a document
  --- including a document that these Rules require to be bound --- may
  be filed with the Registrar

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} through the electronic filing portal;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by fax transmission or email;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by hand delivery; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by mail or courier.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The documents referred to in subrules 19.1(1)
  and (2) must be filed with the Registrar by hand delivery, mail or
  courier.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a document, the Registrar may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} accept or reject the document for filing; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} accept the document for filing subject to the
    making of any corrections or the fulfilling of any conditions
    precedent.

- [(2.1) to (2.3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2011-74, s.
  7\]]{.Repealed}

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A document that is filed by hand delivery, mail or
  courier is deemed to have been filed on the date of the Court's filing
  stamp on that document, unless the Court, a judge or the Registrar

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A document that is filed by fax transmission or email

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} All documents filed, other than correspondence

### [Service of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-680551 .Subheading}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Service of any document on a party
  shall be made on the party's counsel or agent at that person's last
  known address, last known fax number or last known email address or,
  if the party is not represented by counsel, on the party or the
  party's agent, by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} personal service made on any day other than a
    holiday;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ordinary mail, except for originating documents or
    documents filed in support;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} registered or certified mail or by courier;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} fax transmission, except for

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} originating documents or documents filed in
      support, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} documents that are longer than 40 pages unless
      the party being served consents to service by fax transmission;

  - [(d.1)]{.lawlabel} email; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} leaving a copy with a party's counsel or agent or
    with an employee in the office of the counsel or agent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party served with an electronic version of a
  document that is required to be bound may also request a printed
  version that shall be sent no later than one week after receipt of the
  request.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every document served by fax transmission shall
  include a cover page that shows

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the title of the document being transmitted;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sender's name, address and telephone number;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the party being served and of the
    party's counsel, if any;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date and approximate time of the transmission;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the number of pages transmitted, including the
    cover page;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the fax number of the transmitting fax machine; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the name and telephone number of the person to
    contact if there are transmission problems.

- [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} If a document is served by email, the email shall
  contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the title of the document being transmitted;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sender's name, address and telephone number;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the party being served and of the
    party's counsel, if any;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date and approximate time of the transmission;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an indication of the number of attachments to the
    email or an indication of where the party being served can access
    the document electronically.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (5), a document is deemed to have
  been served on the day on which it is received or is admitted to have
  been received, unless it is received between 5:00 p.m. and 12:00 a.m.
  local time or on a day that is a holiday, in which case it is deemed
  to have been served on the following day that is not a holiday.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A document served by ordinary mail is deemed to have
  been served on the fifth business day after the document is mailed.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} When an attempt by an authorized person to serve a
  document in accordance with the rules of procedure applicable in the
  province or territory in which the document is served has failed and
  was recorded in that person's certificate of service, he or she may
  serve the document by leaving on the premises a copy of the document
  intended for the addressee.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Proof of service of the printed version or the
  electronic version is sufficient.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Proof of service shall be verified by filing, in
  accordance with Rule 19, one of the following documents within two
  days after service, unless a judge or the Registrar otherwise orders:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if service is made by ordinary mail, an affidavit
    of service in Form 20;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if service is made by registered or certified mail
    or by courier, an affidavit of service in Form 20 annexing a post
    office receipt, a receipt card bearing the signature of the person
    served or a copy of the tracking results of the courier service that
    indicates the status of the delivery of the document;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if service is made by fax transmission, an
    affidavit of service in Form 20 annexing a copy of the cover page
    referred to in subrule (3) and a transmission slip that confirms the
    date and time of transmission;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if service is made by email, an affidavit of
    service in Form 20 annexing a copy of the email referred to in
    subrule (3.1) and a copy of the read receipt, the delivery receipt
    or the confirmation by the party served that the service was
    effected electronically;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a certificate of service by any authorized person
    in accordance with the rules of procedure applicable in the province
    or territory in which the document is served; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an admission of service endorsed by the party or
    their counsel or agent.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} If service is made by registered or certified mail,
  courier, fax transmission or email, an affidavit of service in Form 20
  is not required for documents that are not originating documents,
  provided that the information that is required to be annexed under
  paragraph (8)(b), (c) or (d), as the case may be, is filed.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar may, on filing of an affidavit by the
  serving party, make any order for substitutional service that the
  circumstances require.

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Part I, a concise overview of the applicant's

      statement of facts,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Part II, a concise statement of the questions in
      issue and, if the proposed appeal raises an issue in respect of

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} Part III, a concise statement of argument,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} Part V, the order or orders sought, including the

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} Part VI, a table of authorities --- including

## [PART 6]{.HLabel1}[Leave to Cross-appeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-680695 .Part}

### [Application for Leave to Cross-Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-680696 .Subheading}

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  the deadline for filing a response under subrule 27(1) in the case of
  an appeal for which leave is required or, in all other cases, within
  30 days of service of a notice of appeal, a respondent who seeks to
  set aside or vary all or any part of the disposition of the judgment
  appealed from may apply for leave to cross-appeal by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serving a copy of the application for leave to
    cross-appeal on all parties who are named in the style of cause
    referred to in subrule 22(2) for the application for leave to
    cross-appeal;

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} filing with the Registrar the original and two
    copies of the printed version of the application for leave to
    cross-appeal and a copy of the electronic version of the notice of
    application for leave to cross-appeal, the memorandum of argument
    and any motion related to the application for leave to cross-appeal;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sending to the parties referred to in paragraph (a)
    a copy of the electronic version of the notice of application for
    leave to cross-appeal referred to in paragraph (2)(a), a memorandum
    of argument in accordance with paragraph 25(1)(c) and any motion
    related to the application for leave to cross-appeal by email, and
    filing with the Registrar a read or delivery receipt; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sending to any party in the court appealed from who
    is not named in the style of cause for the application for leave to
    cross-appeal a copy of the notice of application for leave to
    cross-appeal, and filing with the Registrar confirmation that the
    notice was sent.

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2020-281, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application for leave to cross-appeal

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} must be bound and consist of a notice of
    application for leave to cross-appeal in Form 29, and must be
    otherwise in accordance with Rule 25, with any modifications that
    the circumstances require; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may be joined with the response to the application
    for leave to appeal.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A respondent who seeks to uphold the judgment
  appealed from on a ground not relied on in the reasons for that
  judgment may do so in the respondent's factum without applying for
  leave to cross-appeal.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the circumstances set out in subrule (3), the
  appellant may serve and file, in accordance with subrule 35(3), a
  factum in response that is bound and does not exceed 20 pages.

#### [Factum on Appeal]{.HTitleText3} {#h-680849 .Subheading}

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2011-74, s.
  21\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The factum shall be bound and consist of the
  following parts:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Part I consisting of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the appellant's factum, a concise overview of
      their position and a concise statement of the facts,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the respondent's factum, a concise overview
      of their position and a concise statement of their position with
      respect to the appellant's statement of facts, including a concise
      statement of any other facts that the respondent considers
      relevant, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the intervener's factum, a concise overview
      of their position with respect to the questions on which they have
      intervened, including a concise statement of the facts relevant to
      the questions on which they have intervened;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Part II consisting of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the appellant's factum, a concise statement of
      the questions in issue in the appeal,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the respondent's factum, a concise overview
      of their position with respect to the appellant's questions, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the intervener's factum, a concise overview
      of their position with respect to the appellant's questions on
      which they have intervened;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Part III consisting of a statement of argument
    setting out concisely the questions of law or fact to be discussed,
    with reference to the page of the record and to the tab, page and
    paragraph number of the authorities being relied on;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Part IV consisting of submissions, if any, not
    exceeding one page in support of the order sought concerning costs;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Part V consisting of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the appellant's factum and the respondent's
      factum, a concise statement of the order or orders sought, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the intervener's factum, if not yet
      determined in the order granting the intervention, any request for
      permission to present oral argument at the hearing of the appeal;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} Part VI consisting of, in the appellant's factum
    and the respondent's factum, submissions on the impact that any
    sealing or confidentiality order, publication ban, classification of

### [Reference to the Court]{.HTitleText2} {#h-680905 .Subheading}

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  reference to the Court by the Governor in Council under section 53 of
  the Act shall be commenced by notice of reference in Form 46 to which
  shall be attached a copy of the order in council authorizing the
  reference.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} An electronic version of the notice of reference
  and the order in council authorizing the reference shall be filed with
  the Registrar.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a reference involves the giving of an opinion as
  to a case already disposed of by a court of appeal, the Court may, on
  its own initiative, require further evidence in respect of any
  question that the Court considers relevant.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Further evidence under subrule (2) shall be taken as
  authorized by the Act and in the manner directed by the Court.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council shall bring a motion to the
  Chief Justice or a judge to direct the Registrar to enter a reference
  on a list of cases to be heard by the Court and to determine any
  procedural issues.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Within one week after the filing of the notice of
  reference, the Governor in Council shall serve a copy of the printed
  and electronic version of the notice of reference and the order in
  council referred to in subrule (1) on the attorney general of each
  province and on the minister of Justice of each territory.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Within four weeks after being served with the notice
  of reference, an attorney general of a province, or a minister of
  Justice of a territory, with any special interest in the reference
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serve on the Governor in Council, and any counsel
    requested to argue a case under subsection 53(7) of the Act, a
    notice of intervention in Form 33C; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} file with the Registrar the original and two copies
    of the notice of intervention.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Within 12 weeks after the filing of the notice of
  reference, the Governor in Council shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serve on the attorney general of any province or on
    the minister of Justice of any territory, having served a notice of
    intervention under paragraph (6)(a), and any counsel requested to
    argue a case under subsection 53(7) of the Act, a printed and an
    electronic version of the Governor in Council's factum, record and
    book of authorities;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} file with the Registrar

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the electronic version of the factum, record and
      book of authorities,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the original version of the factum and 23 copies
      of its printed version, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 11 copies of the printed version of the record
      and book of authorities.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Any counsel requested to argue a case under
  subsection 53(7) of the Act shall, within eight weeks after the
  request or within eight weeks after the filing of the Governor in
  Council's factum, whichever is later,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serve electronic and printed versions of the
    counsel's factum, record and book of authorities on the Governor in
    Council;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} file with the Registrar

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the electronic version of the factum, record and
      book of authorities,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the original version of the factum and 23 copies
      of its printed version, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 11 copies of the printed version of the record
      and book of authorities.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} An attorney general of a province or a minister of
  Justice of a territory filing a notice of intervention under
  subrule (6) shall, within 20 weeks after filing the notice,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serve on the Governor in Council and any counsel
    requested to argue a case under subsection 53(7) of the Act a copy
    of the attorney general's factum, or the minister's factum, and book
    of authorities;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} file with the Registrar

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the electronic version of the factum, record and
      book of authorities,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the original version of the factum and 23 copies
      of its printed version, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 11 copies of the printed version of the record
      and book of authorities.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} Within four weeks after the filing of the factum of
  the Governor in Council, any person interested in intervening in the
  reference may make a motion for intervention to a judge in accordance
  with Rules 47 and 57 by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serving on each of the Governor in Council, and any
    counsel requested by the Court under subsection 53(7) of the Act, a
    copy of the person's motion; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} filing with the Registrar the original and two
    copies of the motion.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} The motion for intervention shall be dealt with in
  accordance with Rules 58 and 59, and the service and filing of the
  intervener's documents shall be in accordance with Rule 37.

- [(12)]{.lawlabel} A redacted electronic version of any factum in a
  reference shall be filed, if the factum contains any of the documents
  and information identified in the certificate filed in accordance with
  subrule 23(3).

### [Reconsideration of Application for Leave to Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-681152 .Subheading}

- **[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be no reconsideration of an application for leave to appeal
  unless there are exceedingly rare circumstances in the case that
  warrant consideration by the Court.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A motion for reconsideration must be served on all
  parties and the original and two copies of the printed version of the
  motion filed with the Registrar within 30 days after the judgment on
  the application for leave to appeal.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The motion for reconsideration must be bound and
  consist of the following, in the following order:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a notice of motion for reconsideration in Form 47,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an affidavit setting out the exceedingly rare
    circumstances in the case that warrant consideration by the Court
    and an explanation of why the issue was not previously raised;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any new documents that the party intends to rely
    on; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement of argument not exceeding 10 pages.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall refuse to accept a motion for
  reconsideration that includes an affidavit that does not set out
  exceedingly rare circumstances as required by paragraph (3)(b).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A respondent may respond to a motion for
  reconsideration within 10 days after its acceptance for filing by
  serving on all other parties and filing with the Registrar the
  original and two copies of the printed version of a memorandum of
  argument not exceeding 10 pages.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Within 10 days after the service of the response, the
  applicant may reply by serving on all parties and filing with the
  Registrar the original and two copies of the printed version of a
  memorandum of argument not exceeding five pages.

### [Objection to Taxation of Costs]{.HTitleText2} {#h-681221 .Subheading}

- **[[84]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-84 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  15 days after the date that the certificate of taxation bears, any
  party may object to the taxation of costs on the ground that the bill
  of costs contains a clerical or calculation error by serving on all
  other parties and filing with the Registrar a written objection
  specifying the errors alleged and the corrections sought to be made.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any party who objects to the taxation of costs on any
  other ground not specified in subrule (1) may, within 15 days after
  the date that the certificate of taxation bears, make a motion to a
  judge for a review of the taxation, and the judge may make any order
  with respect to the item in dispute that the judge considers
  appropriate.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An objection to the taxation shall be determined upon
  the evidence that had been brought before the Registrar, and no

## [PART 18]{.HLabel1}[Miscellaneous Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-681249 .Part}

### [Affidavit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-681250 .Subheading}

- **[[89]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-89 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  affidavit shall be filed to substantiate any fact that is not a matter
  of record in the Court.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An affidavit to be used in a proceeding shall be
  limited to the statement of facts within the knowledge of the
  deponent, but statements based on information or belief that state the
  source of the information or the grounds for the belief may be
  admitted by the Court, a judge or the Registrar.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When the record of the court appealed from or of the
  trial court is filed with the Registrar, that record is part of the
  record of the Court.

### [Examination on Affidavit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-681255 .Subheading}

- **[[90]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-90 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  party may, by leave of a judge or the Registrar on motion,
  cross-examine the deponent of an affidavit filed with the Registrar by
  serving on the party who filed the affidavit a notice requiring the
  production of the deponent and documents for cross-examination before
  a commissioner for oaths designated by the judge or the Registrar.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice required by subrule (1) shall be served
  within the time that the judge or Registrar may order.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any cross-examination referred to in subrule (1)
  shall take place before the proceeding is heard unless a judge or the
  Registrar otherwise orders.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The transcript of a cross-examination may be filed
  with the Registrar within 10 days after the cross-examination.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A judge or the Registrar may, on his or her own
  initiative, order the production of documents on a cross-examination.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where a deponent is not produced for
  cross-examination, the deponent's affidavit shall be struck out unless
  a judge or the Registrar otherwise orders.

### [Oaths and Witnesses]{.HTitleText2} {#h-681263 .Subheading}

###### [**Part 1 --- Counsel's fees**]{.HTitleText5}

- [1]{.lawlabel} Application for leave to appeal:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} preparation of an application for leave to appeal
    or application for leave to cross-appeal, or of a response to one of
    those applications,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if dealt with in writing ![blank
      line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$800

      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if oral hearing held ![blank
      line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$1,000

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if no response is filed, for the review of an
    application for leave or application for leave to cross-appeal, at
    the Registrar's discretion, up to ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$300

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} preparation of the first copy of an application for
    leave to appeal, an application for leave to cross-appeal, a
    response or a reply, per page ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$1.35

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} preparation of the electronic version of an
    application for leave to appeal, an application for leave to
    cross-appeal, a response or a reply, per page ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$0.30

- [2]{.lawlabel} Motion:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} preparation of a motion or response to a motion,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if dealt with in writing ![blank
      line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$300

      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if oral hearing held ![blank
      line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$800

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if no response is filed, for the review of a
    motion, at the Registrar's discretion, up to ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$200

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} preparation of the first copy of a motion book, a
    response or a reply, per page ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$1.35

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} preparation of the electronic version of a motion
    book, a response or a reply, per page ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$0.30

- [3]{.lawlabel} Appeal:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notice of appeal ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$75

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} preparation of factum ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$650

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} preparation of the first copy of factum, record,
    book of authorities and condensed book, per page ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$1.35

  - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} preparation of the electronic version of factum,
    record or book of authorities, per page ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$0.30

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} hearing of the appeal, preparation for and
    attendance at hearing,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} fee for the principal counsel ![blank

    as abandoned appeals ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$500

- [4]{.lawlabel} Miscellaneous:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fee to the duly entered agent,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on leave ![blank
      line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$200

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on appeal ![blank
      line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$500

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cross-examination of a deponent under Rule 90, at
    the Registrar's discretion, up to ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$150

All fees are subject to an increase or decrease in exceptional cases, at
the Registrar's discretion.

###### [**Part 2 --- Disbursements**]{.HTitleText5}

The following disbursements shall be taxed at the Registrar's
discretion:

- [1]{.lawlabel} Fees paid to the Registrar under Schedule A.

- [2]{.lawlabel} A reasonable amount for reproducing documents required
  to be filed with the Court in accordance with a receipt from the
  service provider that itemizes the services rendered. The amount must
  not include counsel's fees already claimed for the preparation of the
  printed or electronic version of any document referred to in Part 1.
  Documents reproduced in-house or not supported by an itemized receipt
  will be taxed at \$0.25 per page.

- [3]{.lawlabel} Subject to item 4, reasonable amounts incurred to
  attend proceedings before the Court for up to two counsel, including
  travel expenses, lodging expenses for a maximum of two nights for one
  hearing day plus an additional night of lodging for each additional
  hearing day, meals and any incidental expenses (for example, parking,
  taxi and telephone calls). Such expenses shall be supported by
  detailed and itemized receipts.

- [4]{.lawlabel} Amounts incurred for the use of privately owned
  vehicles driven to attend proceedings before the Court. Those amounts
  shall be calculated using the kilometric rates set out in Appendix B
  of the National Joint Council's Travel Directive; no receipts for
  those expenses are required.

- [5]{.lawlabel} All other itemized and detailed disbursements
  reasonably incurred, except disbursements incurred for electronic
  legal research. The disbursements must not include fees referred to in
  Part 1. Detailed and itemized receipts are required for amounts
  claimed over \$50.

- [6]{.lawlabel} Any service, sales, use or consumption taxes paid or
  payable on any disbursements set out in items 2, 3 and 5, unless those
  taxes have been reimbursed or are reimbursable.
