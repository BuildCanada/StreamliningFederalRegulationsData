## Ontario Flue-Cured Tobacco Licence Charges (Interprovincial and Export) Order {#ontario-flue-cured-tobacco-licence-charges-interprovincial-and-export-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-239
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1986-02-27

Order Provided for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Licence
Charges on Persons Engaged in the Marketing in Interprovincial and
Export Trade of Flue-Cured Tobacco Produced in Ontario
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Ontario Flue-Cured Tobacco Growers' Marketing Board, pursuant to
section 4[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-86-239_e_hq_10917){#fn_SOR-86-239_e_hq_10917-ID0EFBBA}
of the [Ontario Flue-Cured Tobacco
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._183), hereby makes the annexed Order
providing for the fixing, imposing and collecting of licence charges on
persons engaged in the marketing in interprovincial and export trade of
flue-cured tobacco produced in Ontario.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-892943 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Ontario Flue-Cured Tobacco Licence Charges
(Interprovincial and Export) Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-239).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-892945 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Flue-Cured
    Tobacco Growers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act and
    the Plan; ([Office de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[licensed producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensed producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a producer to whom a
    licence to produce tobacco has been issued by the Board, but does
    not include a producer whose licence has been suspended or revoked
    by the Board; ([producteur agréé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Flue-Cured Tobacco
    Growers' Marketing Plan established pursuant to the Act and any
    regulations made thereunder to give effect to the Plan;
    ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means unmanufactured flue-cured
    tobacco produced in Ontario. ([tabac]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-892952 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies only
with respect to the marketing of tobacco in interprovincial and export
trade and to persons and property situated in the Province of Ontario.

## [Licence Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-892954 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensed producer shall pay licence charges to the Commodity Board at
  the rate of 2.5¢ for each pound or fraction thereof of tobacco
  marketed by the producer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commodity Board may deduct the licence charges
  payable by a licensed producer from the moneys paid to the Commodity
  Board for the tobacco marketed by the producer.
