## Foreign Claims (Hungary) Settlement Regulations {#foreign-claims-hungary-settlement-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/70-527
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 9, 1966

Registration 1970-12-08

Regulations Respecting the Determination and Payment out of the Foreign
Claims Fund of Certain Claims Against the Government of the Hungarian
People's Republic and its Citizens

P.C. 1970-2078 1970-12-08
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Secretary of State for External Affairs and the Minister of Finance,
pursuant to Vote Item 22a in Schedule B of Appropriation Act No. 9,
1966, is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the
determination and payment out of the Foreign Claims Fund of certain
claims against the Government of the Hungarian People's Republic and its
citizens](/eng/regulations/SOR-70-527).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860738 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Foreign Claims (Hungary) Settlement
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-70-527).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860740 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement between the
    Government of Canada and the Government of the Hungarian People's
    Republic relating to the Settlement of Financial Matters, signed at
    Budapest, June 1, 1970; ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canadian citizen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian citizen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    Article III of the Agreement; ([citoyen canadien]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Chief Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chief Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chief
    Commissioner of the Commission; ([commissaire en
    chef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[claim]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[claim]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a claim by a Canadian citizen or
    the Canadian Government against the Government of the Hungarian
    People's Republic or against a Hungarian citizen in respect of any
    of the following matters:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} property, rights and interests, whether legally
      or beneficially owned, including debts, other than bonded debts,
      affected directly or indirectly by Hungarian measures of
      nationalization, expropriation, state administration of other
      similar measures arising out of structural changes in the
      Hungarian economy and regulations made or administrative action
      taken thereunder that have taken in effect before the date of the
      Agreement;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} debts arising out of contracts of insurance
      concluded on or before December 7, 1941, that are due from
      Hungarian citizens;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} shares in Hungarian banking companies affected by
      the Hungarian Law No. XXX of December 1, 1947, being shares that,
      from the date of that Law to the date of the Agreement, have been
      continuously owned by Canadian citizens;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} obligations arising out of Articles 24 and 26 of
      the Treaty of Peace with Hungary of February 10, 1947; and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} obligations arising out of Articles 231 and 232
      of the Treaty of Trianon of June 4, 1920;
      ([réclamation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Foreign Claims
    Commission established by Order in Council P.C. 1970-2077 of
    8th December, 1970; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the special account known as the
    Foreign Claims Fund established in the Consolidated Revenue Fund by
    the Minister of Finance pursuant to Vote 22a of Appropriation Act
    No. 9, 1966; ([Caisse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Hungarian citizen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Hungarian citizen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    Article III of the Agreement; and ([citoyen
    hongrois]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Secretary of State for
    External Affairs. ([Ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Notice of Claims]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860755 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No claim
  of which notice has not been given on or before March 31, 1971 shall
  be considered by the Commission.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every notice of a claim given to the Government of
  Canada on or before March 31, 1971 shall, for the purposes of
  subsection (1), be deemed to have been given to the Commission and
  shall be referred by the Minister to the Commission.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a claimant who has given notice of his claim dies
    before his claim has been determined by the Commission, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the claimant is survived by a person who, in the
    opinion of the Commission, may be lawfully entitled to an award in
    respect of that claimant's claim,

  the Commission may proceed with and determine that claimant's claim in
  the same manner as if he had not died.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} All documentary evidence and written representations
  on which a claimant intends to rely in establishing his claim shall be
  submitted to the Commission on or before March 31, 1975.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/75-61, s. 1
:::

## [Eligibility of Claimants to Awards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860766 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No award
  shall be paid to or in respect of any claimant who was not a Canadian
  citizen at the time notice of his claim was given.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a claimant who gave notice of his claim on or
  before June 1, 1970, dies, an award may be paid to any person lawfully
  entitled thereto irrespective of his nationality.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a claimant who gave notice of his claim after
  June 1, 1970 dies, no award shall be paid in respect of that
  claimant's claim to an person who was not a Canadian citizen at the
  time notice of the claim was given.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  claimant has been paid or, in the opinion of the Commission, is likely
  to be paid any compensation in respect of his claim from any source
  other than the Fund, the Commission shall deduct the amount of that
  compensation from the amount of an award it recommends be paid to that
  claimant out of the Fund.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a claimant by reason of his own neglect or
  default has failed to receive, has forfeited his right to receive or
  has lost his eligibility to receive, any compensation in respect of
  his claim from a source other than the Fund, he shall for the purposes
  of these Regulations, be deemed to have been paid that compensation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The failure of a claimant who knew or ought to have
  known that if he did not apply on or before a fixed date, he would
  lose his right or eligibility to receive compensation from a source
  other than the Fund shall be deemed, for the purposes of
  subsection (2), to be neglect by that claimant.

## [Additional Amounts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860775 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Chief Commissioner may,
in addition to the amount of an award recommended by him, recommend
payment of an amount equal to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} one-third of such award in lieu of accrued interest
  on the award; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} fifty dollars, or ten per cent of such award,
  whichever is the lesser, as reimbursement for expenses necessarily
  incurred in establishing the claim.

## [Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860779 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Commissioner shall report to the Minister and the Minister of
  Finance on each claim considered by the Commission stating

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the claimant is eligible to receive an
    award;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any award that, in the opinion of the
    Commission, should be made to a claimant; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the particular paragraph in the definition of
    [[claim]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 under which the claim
    arises.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Commissioner may make a report on any claim
  on the basis of the information then available to the Commission
  where, in his opinion, postponement of his report in respect of the
  claim until such time as the Commission is able to assess, with
  reasonable certainty, the possibility of recovery of compensation from
  a source other than the Fund would result in undue delay.

## [Payment from the Fund]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860786 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of a report from the Chief Commissioner, the Minister and the
  Minister of Finance shall determine

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any award recommended by the Chief
    Commissioner that is to be paid out of the Fund in respect of a
    claim; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the claimant is dead, the person, if any, to
    whom payment of the award is to be made.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No right to an award is conferred by any
  recommendation made by the Chief Commissioner to the Minister and the
  Minister of Finance.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Awards in respect of claims
arising under

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} paragraphs (a), (b), (c) and (e) of the definition of
  [[claim]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 shall be paid out of that
  part of the Fund consisting of moneys credited to the Fund pursuant to
  paragraph (b) of Vote 22a of Appropriation Act No. 9, 1966 and any
  interest accredited to the Fund in respect of those moneys; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} paragraph (d) of the definition of
  [[claim]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 shall be paid out of that
  part of the Fund consisting of moneys credited to the Fund pursuant to
  paragraph (a) of Vote 22a of Appropriation Act No. 9, 1966 and any
  interest credited to other Fund in respect of those moneys.

<!-- -->

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the moneys in any part of the Fund described in section 9 are
  insufficient to pay in full all awards that the Minister and the
  Minister of Finance determine may to paid out of that part,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any award payable to a claimant who
    has been paid or in the opinion of the Commission is likely to be
    paid compensation from any source other than the Fund, or who is
    deemed pursuant to subsection 5(2) to have been so paid, shall be
    reduced by the amount of that compensation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} equal payments shall be made in respect of all
    awards up to the full amount or of one thousand dollars, whichever
    is the lesser; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the balances of awards not paid in full shall be
    paid on a [pro rata]{.otherLang lang="la"} basis from any moneys
    remaining in that part of the Fund.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), no payment of an
  amount recommended by the Chief Commissioner pursuant to section 6
  shall be paid out of a part of the Fund described in section 9 until
  the awards that may be paid out of that part of the Fund have been
  paid in full.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Awards may be paid in one
or more instalments at such times as in the opinion of the Minister and
the Minister of Finance may be warranted having regard to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amounts available in the part of the Fund out of
  which awards of a similar kind may be paid; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the possible amounts that may be awarded in respect
  of claims still before the Commission.

<!-- -->

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  making payment of an award, the Minister of Finance shall obtain a
  release in such form as he considers satisfactory in respect of the
  claim for which payment is to be made.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In consideration of making an award the Minister of
  Finance shall obtain all documents of title which are available in
  respect of the claim for which payment is to be made.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of finance may require that any claimant
  assign to Her Majesty in right of Canada his entitlement to
  compensation from a source other than the Fund, or if such entitlement
  cannot be validly assigned, an undertaking by the claimant to do all
  things necessary to vest such amounts and his rights thereto in Her
  Majesty.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860808 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** In the event of the
absence or incapacity of the Chief Commissioner or if the office of the
Chief Commissioner is vacant, the Deputy Chief Commission may perform
all the duties and functions of the Chief Commissioner under these
Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/75-61, s. 2
:::

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** In the event of the
absence, incapacity or disqualification of any two Commissioners, the
remaining Commissioner may perform all the duties and functions of the
Commission under these Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/75-61, s. 2
:::
