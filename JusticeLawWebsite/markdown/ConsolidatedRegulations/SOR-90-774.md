## Hibernia Development Project Offshore Application Regulations {#hibernia-development-project-offshore-application-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-774
:::

[HIBERNIA DEVELOPMENT PROJECT ACT](/eng/acts/H-3.7)

Registration 1990-11-09

Regulations Respecting the Application of Federal Laws and Laws of
Newfoundland in the Newfoundland Offshore Area for the Purposes of the
Hibernia Development Project Act

P.C. 1990-2419  1990-11-09
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council considers that certain modifications or
additions to the laws of Newfoundland that apply in the offshore area
pursuant to section 8 of the [Hibernia Development Project
Act](/eng/acts/H-3.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-90-774_e_hq_5211){#fn_SOR-90-774_e_hq_5211-ID0EBBBA}
are necessary;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-90-774_e_hq_5211-ID0EBBBA){#fn_SOR-90-774_e_hq_5211}S.C.
  1990, c. 41
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources, pursuant
to sections 7 and 8 of the [Hibernia Development Project
Act](/eng/acts/H-3.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-90-774_e_hq_5211){#fn_SOR-90-774_e_hq_5211-ID0EDAAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the
application of federal laws and laws of Newfoundland in the Newfoundland
offshore area for the purposes of the Hibernia Development Project
Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-774).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930880 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Hibernia Development Project Offshore Application
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-774).

## [Federal Laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930882 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The prescribed federal laws
in relation to the subjects referred to in subsection 7(1) of the
[Hibernia Development Project Act](/eng/acts/H-3.7) are the Acts of
Parliament or the provisions thereof set out in Schedule I, any
regulations as defined in section 2 of the [Interpretation
Act](/eng/acts/I-21) that are issued, made or established under the
authority of those Acts and any rules of law within the jurisdiction of
Parliament in relation to those subjects.

## [Laws of Newfoundland]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930884 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of creating, continuing and enforcing, under the laws of
  Newfoundland that apply in the offshore area by virtue of subsection
  8(1) of the [Hibernia Development Project Act](/eng/acts/H-3.7), a
  security interest in any personal property that is affixed to, rests
  on or is otherwise in contact with the seabed or subsoil of the
  submarine areas of the offshore area, those laws shall be read as if
  that personal property were not a fixture of that seabed or subsoil.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 8(1) of the [Hibernia
  Development Project Act](/eng/acts/H-3.7), the Acts of Newfoundland
  set out in column I of an item of Schedule II shall be read as if they
  were modified or added to in the manner and to the extent set out in
  column II of that item.

## [Application of the Interpretation Act (Newfoundland)]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930888 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Interpretation Act,
R.S.N. 1970, c. 182, applies in respect of all laws of Newfoundland,
including the Acts referred to in section 3 as modified or added to
pursuant to that section, that, by virtue of subsection 8(1) of the
[Hibernia Development Project Act](/eng/acts/H-3.7), apply in the
offshore area.
