## [Blood Alcohol Concentration and Blood Drug Concentration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-851486 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summary offence

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 320.14(4) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the
prescribed blood drug concentration for tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) is
2 ng of THC per mL of blood.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-149, s. 1
:::

paragraph 320.14(1)(c) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the

the table to this section is set out in column 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hybrid offence --- combination of drugs
and alcohol

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 320.14(1)(d) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the
prescribed blood alcohol concentration is 50 mg of alcohol per 100 mL of
blood and the prescribed blood drug concentration for
tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) is 2.5 ng of THC per mL of blood.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-149, s. 3
