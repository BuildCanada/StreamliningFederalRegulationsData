## Certain Fees in Respect of the Issuance of Replacement Documents (2024 Wildfires) Remission Order {#certain-fees-in-respect-of-the-issuance-of-replacement-documents-2024-wildfires-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

Certain Fees in Respect of the Issuance of Replacement Documents (2024
Wildfires) Remission Order

P.C. 2024-882 2024-07-18

:::: section
::: {.section .order}

in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Treasury
Board, the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration and the Minister of
Foreign Affairs, makes the annexed [Certain Fees in Respect of the
Issuance of Replacement Documents (2024 Wildfires) Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2024-36) under subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted to any person
  who meets the conditions set out in subsection (2) of fees paid or
  payable set out in the following provisions, in respect of the
  replacement of a document:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} items 3 to 8, 14 and 15 of the schedule to the
    [Passport and Other Travel Document Services Fees
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-253);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} item 6 of the schedule to the [Citizenship
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-246);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subsection 308(2) of the [Immigration and Refugee
    Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} section 4 of the [Consular Services Fees
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-538).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The conditions are the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at some time during the period beginning on April
    1, 2024 and ending on November 30, 2024, the person was in, or had
    their residence in, an area in Canada affected by a wildfire;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the document was, while it was valid, lost,
    damaged, destroyed or rendered inaccessible as a result of the
    wildfire;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person, or a person acting on their behalf,
    makes an application to replace that document on or before November
    30, 2024;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the application includes a declaration made by that
    person or the person acting on their behalf stating that the
    document was lost, damaged, destroyed or rendered inaccessible as a
    result of the wildfire and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} proof that, at the time that the document was
      lost, damaged, destroyed or rendered inaccessible, the applicant
      was a resident of an area referred to in paragraph (a), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a declaration made by the applicant or the
      person acting on their behalf stating that, at the time that the
      document was lost, damaged, destroyed or rendered inaccessible,
      the applicant was in the area referred to in paragraph (a); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the fees in question have not been remitted under
    section 11 of the [Passport and Other Travel Document Services Fees
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-253).
