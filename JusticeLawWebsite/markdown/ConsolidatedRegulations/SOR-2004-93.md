## General Preferential Tariff Withdrawal Order (Certain Countries Acceding to the European Union) {#general-preferential-tariff-withdrawal-order-certain-countries-acceding-to-the-european-union .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-93
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 2004-04-27

General Preferential Tariff Withdrawal Order (Certain Countries Acceding
to the European Union)

P.C. 2004-490 2004-04-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 34(1)(b) of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea){#footnotea-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the
annexed [General Preferential Tariff Withdrawal Order (Certain Countries
Acceding to the European Union)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-93).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea-ID0EDBAA){#footnotea}S.C. 1997, c. 36
  :::
:::

## [Withdrawal of Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714280 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The benefit of the General
Preferential Tariff is hereby withdrawn in respect of all goods that
originate in Cyprus, the Czech Republic, Republic of Estonia, Hungary,
Republic of Latvia, Republic of Lithuania, Malta, Poland, Slovak
Republic and the Republic of Slovenia.

## [Exception]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714282 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Section 1 does not apply to
goods that were in transit to Canada before May 1, 2004.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714286 .Part}

on May 1, 2004.
