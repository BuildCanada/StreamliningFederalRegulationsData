## Administrative Monetary Penalties Regulations (International Bridges and Tunnels) {#administrative-monetary-penalties-regulations-international-bridges-and-tunnels .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2012-149
:::

[INTERNATIONAL BRIDGES AND TUNNELS ACT](/eng/acts/I-17.05)

Registration 2012-07-09

Administrative Monetary Penalties Regulations (International Bridges and
Tunnels)
::::

::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 43 of the [International
Bridges and Tunnels Act](/eng/acts/I-17.05)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDACA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Administrative Monetary Penalties Regulations (International Bridges
and Tunnels)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-149).

Ottawa, July 6, 2012

DENIS LEBEL

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-783142 .Part}

Meaning of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [International Bridges and Tunnels
Act](/eng/acts/I-17.05).

## [Designated Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-783145 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions of the Act and Regulations

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  provisions of the Act set out in column 1 of Part 1 of the schedule
  and the provisions of the [International Bridges and Tunnels
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2009-17) set out in column 1 of Part
  2 of the schedule are designated as provisions the contravention of
  which is a violation that may be proceeded with in accordance with
  sections 45 to 55 of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An amount set out in column 2 of the schedule is
  prescribed as the maximum penalty payable by a corporation in respect
  of a contravention of the designated provision set out in column 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Individual

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An amount set out in column 3 of the schedule is
  prescribed as the maximum penalty payable by an individual in respect
  of a contravention of the designated provision set out in column 1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and directives

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  provisions of an order made under section 9, 13, 15.1 or 26 of the Act
  and the provisions of a directive made under section 17 or 18 of the
  Act are designated as provisions the contravention of which may be
  proceeded with as a violation in accordance with sections 45 to 55 of
  the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum penalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The maximum amount of a penalty payable in respect of
  a contravention of a designated provision referred to in
  subsection (1) is \$25,000 in the case of a corporation and \$5,000 in
  the case of an individual.
