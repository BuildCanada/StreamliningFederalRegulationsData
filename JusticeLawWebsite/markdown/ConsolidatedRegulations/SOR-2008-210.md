## Prosecution of Provincial Offences Regulations (Donkin Coal Block) {#prosecution-of-provincial-offences-regulations-donkin-coal-block .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2008-210
:::

[DONKIN COAL BLOCK DEVELOPMENT OPPORTUNITY ACT](/eng/acts/D-3.9)

Registration 2008-06-12

Prosecution of Provincial Offences Regulations (Donkin Coal Block)

P.C. 2008-1063 2008-06-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Natural Resources and the Minister of Labour, pursuant
to subsection 13(4) of the [Donkin Coal Block Development Opportunity
Act](/eng/acts/D-3.9)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_610min-E_hq_2782){#fn_610min-E_hq_2782-ID0EDBAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Prosecution of Provincial Offences Regulations
(Donkin Coal Block)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2008-210).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Nova Scotia laws]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Summary Proceedings
Act (R.S.N.S. 1989, c. 450) and its regulations, as amended from time to
time and, for greater certainty, includes regulations made under that
Act after the coming into force of these Regulations, unless otherwise
provided.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nova Scotia [Interpretation
Act](/eng/acts/I-21)

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Nova Scotia
[Interpretation Act](/eng/acts/I-21) (R.S.N.S. 1989, c. 235), as amended
from time to time, applies to the interpretation of the Nova Scotia
laws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation of Nova Scotia laws

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Nova Scotia laws are
incorporated by reference and apply to prosecutions of offences under
the [Donkin Coal Block Development Opportunity Act](/eng/acts/D-3.9).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
