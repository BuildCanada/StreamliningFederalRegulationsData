## [Supervisory Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-677077 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 956 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the
  following information or any component of that information is
  prescribed supervisory information in relation to a bank holding
  company:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any rating assigned by the Superintendent to the
    bank holding company to assess its financial condition and any other
    such rating that is substantially based on information obtained from
    the Superintendent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any stage of intervention assigned to the bank
    holding company on the basis of the principles set out in the Guide
    to Intervention for Federal Financial Institutions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any order made in respect of the bank holding
    company under subsection 949(3) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01),
    any prudential agreement entered into by the bank holding company
    under section 959 of that Act or any direction issued to it under
    section 960 of that Act; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any report prepared by or at the request of the
    Superintendent or any recommendation made by the Superintendent as a
    result of an annual or special examination or other supervisory
    review of the bank holding company, including any related
    correspondence to or from the directors or officers of the bank
    holding company.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 956 of the [Bank
  Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the information prescribed by the following
  regulations is prescribed supervisory information in relation to an
  affiliate of a bank holding company:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Banks)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-59), if the affiliate is a
    bank to which the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) applies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Authorized Foreign
    Banks) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-58), if the affiliate
    is an authorized foreign bank to which the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) applies;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Cooperative Credit
    Associations) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-57), if the
    affiliate is an association to which the [Cooperative Credit
    Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01) applies;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Insurance Companies)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-56), if the affiliate is a
    company, society, foreign company or provincial company to which the
    [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Insurance Holding
    Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-484), if the
    affiliate is an insurance holding company to which the [Insurance
    Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Trust and Loan
    Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-55), if the
    affiliate is a company to which the [Trust and Loan Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) applies.

- SOR/2011-196, s. 9

## [Prohibited Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-677093 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 and
4, a bank holding company shall not, directly or indirectly, disclose
information referred to in section 1.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-196, s. 10
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 2"){.PITLink}
:::

## [Limited Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-677097 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A bank holding company may
disclose information referred to in section 1 to its affiliates or to
its directors, officers, employees, auditors, securities underwriters or
legal advisors, or to those of its affiliates, if the bank holding
company ensures that the information remains confidential.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-196, s. 10
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A bank holding company or
any of its affiliates may disclose information referred to in paragraph
1(1)(c) if the bank holding company or affiliate considers the
information to contain a material fact or material change that is
required by the securities laws of the relevant jurisdiction to be
disclosed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-196, s. 11(E)
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-677104 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
