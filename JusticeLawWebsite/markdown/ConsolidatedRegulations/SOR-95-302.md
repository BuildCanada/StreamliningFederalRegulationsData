## Government Employees Land Acquisition Order, 1995, No. 2 {#government-employees-land-acquisition-order-1995-no.-2 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-302
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 1995-06-23

Order Authorizing a Certain Employee of the Government of Canada to
Acquire an Interest in Territorial Lands in the Northwest Territories

P.C. 1995-1027 1995-06-23
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 29(1)(a) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), is
pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order authorizing a certain employee
of the Government of Canada to acquire an interest in territorial lands
in the Northwest Territories](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-478).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973520 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Government Employees Land Acquisition Order, 1995, No.
2](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-302).

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973522 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The employee of the
Government of Canada named in the schedule is hereby authorized to
acquire an interest in territorial lands in the Northwest Territories as
described in the schedule.

::::: section
:::: {#973524 .Schedule}
::: {}
## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-973525 .scheduleLabel}
:::

George Douglas Lennie, of the Town of Inuvik in the Northwest
Territories, Hydrometric Survey Technician, Water Survey of Canada, an
employee of the Department of the Environment, to lease for a trapper's
cabin the whole of a parcel of land situated 10 air kilometres west of
Inuvik on an unnamed channel 20 river kilometres upstream from Oniak
Channel, in the Mackenzie Delta, at approximately 68°20′ North Latitude
and 133°58′ West Longitude in Quad numbered 107 B/7 in the Northwest
Territories, as said parcel is shown outlined in red on a sketch plan on
file 33-1-12 in the Land Management Division of the Department of Indian
Affairs and Northern Development at Ottawa, saving and excepting
therefrom and reserving thereout all mines and minerals, including
hydrocarbons, whether solid, liquid or gaseous and the right to work the
same.
