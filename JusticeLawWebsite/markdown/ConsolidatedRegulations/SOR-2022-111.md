## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1346668 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations establish
an offset credit system for projects that reduce GHGs either by
preventing GHGs from being emitted or by removing GHGs from the
atmosphere and to which a protocol applies.

## [Overview]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1346671 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elements of the System

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations provide
for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conditions for registering a project in the
  offset credit system, as well as the circumstances under which a
  registration can be cancelled;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the requirements for proponents;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the issuance of offset credits; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the requirements for keeping and retaining records.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1346678 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Project characteristics

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to a proponent of a project

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} which is of a type for which a protocol has been
    included in the Compendium of Federal Offset Protocols;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that aims to generate GHG reductions by preventing
    GHG emissions or removing GHGs from the atmosphere; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to which the reductions are real,
    additional, quantified, verified, unique and permanent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reductions --- additional

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(c), reductions are
  additional if those reductions

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} would not have been generated in the absence of the
    project;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} were not required by law or the result of a legal
    requirement; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} are not from sources, sinks and reservoirs that are
    subject to federal or provincial pricing mechanisms for GHG
    emissions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reductions --- unique

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(c), reductions are
  unique if those reductions are not credited under another offset
  program or another GHG reduction mechanism.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to projects that have
  a start date before January 1, 2017.

## [Crediting Period]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1346694 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crediting period

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  otherwise provided in the applicable protocol, the period in respect
  of which a project can generate offset credits is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a sequestration project related to
    forestry, 30 years;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a sequestration project other than
    one related to forestry, 20 years; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other project, 10 years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Beginning of period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), the crediting period
  begins on the day on which the project is registered or on the project
  start date, whichever is later.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- beginning of period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), in the case of a project that
  is of an agricultural nature, if required in the protocol, the
  crediting period begins on the date of the start of the growing
  season. However, that date cannot be earlier than the day on which the
  project is registered or the project start date, whichever is later.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- length of period

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a project that was registered in a GHG
  offset credit system other than the one set out in these Regulations,
  the crediting period is decreased by subtracting the period that
  begins on the registration date in the other system and ends on the
  day on which the project is no longer registered in that other system.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aggregation of projects

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (6), the crediting period for
  an aggregation of projects, registered in accordance with subsection
  9(1), begins on the day on which the aggregation is registered or on
  the earliest project start date of any of the projects in the
  aggregation at the time of registration, whichever is later. However,
  if one of the projects in the aggregation starts after that day, the
  project cannot generate credits until that project's start date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Addition of projects

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a project is added to an aggregation under
  subsection 9(4) or if a project that is part of an aggregation is
  transferred to another aggregation under subsection 9(5), its
  crediting period begins on the day on which it is added or
  transferred, as the case may be, or on its project start date,
  whichever is later, and ends on the day on which the crediting period
  for the aggregation ends.

## [Protocol]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1346711 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration of a project

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  project is registered, the protocol or, if several versions of a
  protocol are included in the Compendium of Federal Offset Protocols,
  the most recent version of the protocol, is the one that applies for
  the duration of the crediting period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Renewal of crediting period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, at the time of a renewal of a crediting period,
  several versions of a protocol are included in the Compendium of
  Federal Offset Protocols, the most recent version of the protocol is
  the one that applies to the project for the duration of the crediting
  period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proponent's choice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (1) and (2), if a new version of
  a protocol is issued during a project's crediting period, a proponent
  may opt to have the new version apply to their project. However, only
  one version of a protocol may apply during the period covered by a
  project report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (1) and (2), if, at the time the
  application for registration of a project or the request for renewal
  of the crediting period is submitted, the protocol for the given
  project type is withdrawn, the application or request must be refused.

## [Process for Offset Credit Issuance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1346721 .Part}

### [Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1346722 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General requirements

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** To be issued offset credits
by the Minister, the proponent of a project must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have registered the project in accordance with
  section 8 or 9;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be the holder of a GHG Offset Credit System account
  in the tracking system;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} implement the project in accordance with the
  applicable protocol;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} generate GHG reductions that are real, additional,
  quantified, verified, unique and permanent;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} prepare a project report in accordance with section
  20 and have it verified by a verification body in accordance with
  sections 24 to 27;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} submit to the Minister the project reports, in
  accordance with subsection 20(7), accompanied by a verification report
  prepared in accordance with section 28;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, submit to the Minister a corrected
  project report in accordance with section 32;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, meet the obligations with respect to
  implementing a reversal risk management plan in accordance with
  subsection 21(2) and monitor the project in accordance with section
  22, specifically submitting monitoring reports; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the proponent was required to remit compliance
  units under section 181 of the Act, have met that obligation.

### [Project Registration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1346734 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of registration

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 9, a project, of a type for which a protocol has been
  included in the Compendium of Federal Offset Protocols, may be
  registered in the offset credit system if its proponent submits an
  application for registration to the Minister that includes the
  information set out in Schedule 1 and the following conditions are
  met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proponent is an individual who resides in
    Canada or, if the proponent is not an individual, the proponent has
    a place of business in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proponent has exclusive entitlement to claim
    the credits issued for the GHG reductions generated by the project;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proponent has the necessary authorizations to
    carry out the project activities;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prior to the project start date, the baseline
    conditions set out in the protocol to be eligible under that
    protocol are met;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the activities undertaken as part of the project to
    prevent GHGs from being emitted or to remove GHGs from the
    atmosphere are set out in the applicable protocol;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the reductions that the project could generate
    would be additional;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the project is not registered in any other offset
    credit system;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} no credits will be attributed under another GHG
    reduction mechanism for the GHG reductions generated by the project;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} no provincial offset credit protocol in the
    province in which the project is situated, established under an
    offset credit program for that province, covers the same activities
    as those set out in the applicable federal protocol;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a sequestration project,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the proponent has specified whether the
      tonne-tonne, tonne-year or hybrid tonne-year quantification
      method, as set out in the protocol, will be used for the duration
      of the project, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a proponent opting for the
      tonne-tonne or hybrid tonne-year quantification method, the
      proponent has established a reversal risk management plan in
      accordance with section 21;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a previous registration by the proponent of the
    project under these Regulations has not been cancelled as a result
    of a voluntary reversal;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} neither the proponent nor their authorized official
    has been found guilty of an offence under section 380 of the
    [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), within five years prior to
    submitting the application for registration; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} the application for registration is submitted
    within the time limits set out in section 10.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- provincial protocol

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(i), in the case where a
  provincial offset credit protocol, established under an offset credit
  program for that province, applies to the activities undertaken as
  part of a project, the project may be registered if:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the provincial protocol is published after the
    federal protocol is included in the Compendium of Federal Offset
    Protocols; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application for registration is submitted no
    more than six months after the day on which the project could have
    been registered in the provincial offset credit program under that
    protocol.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The application for registration must be accompanied
  by a declaration, dated and signed by the proponent or their
  authorized official, stating that the information contained in the
  application is accurate and complete.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Project in more than one province

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a project is situated in more than one province,
  the proponent must register, as distinct projects, the portions of the
  project situated in each province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request to open account

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The proponent, in accordance with subsection 186(1)
  of the Act, must submit a request to open a GHG Offset Credit System
  account in the tracking system if they do not already have such an
  account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aggregation of projects

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  proponent may register an aggregation of projects, made up of a group
  of projects of a type for which a protocol has been included in the
  Compendium of Federal Offset Protocols and that have not previously
  been registered under these Regulations or a group of projects that
  are already registered as part of an aggregation under these
  Regulations, if the following conditions are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proponent is the proponent for each of the
    projects in the aggregation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all of the projects in the aggregation are situated
    in the same province;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} each project in the aggregation meets the
    conditions of registration set out in subsection 8(1);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the same protocol, and version of that protocol,
    applies to each project in the aggregation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} with respect to sequestration projects, if the
    protocol provides for a choice between the tonne-tonne, hybrid
    tonne-year or tonne-year quantification method, the same method is
    used for all projects in the aggregation;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an application for registration of the aggregation
    was submitted in accordance with subsection (2); and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the number of projects in the aggregation does not
    exceed the maximum number of projects for the given project type set
    out in the protocol.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application for registration of an aggregation of
  projects must be submitted to the Minister and include the information
  referred to in Schedule 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The application for registration must be accompanied
  by a declaration, dated and signed by the proponent or their
  authorized official, stating that the information contained in the
  application is accurate and complete.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Addition of a project

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A proponent may add a project, that has not
  previously been registered under these Regulations, to an aggregation
  of projects if the conditions set out in paragraphs (1)(a) to (e)
  and (g) are met and if they submit an application to the Minister,
  including the declaration referred to in subsection (3), containing
  the information referred to in subsection (2) for the project, with
  the information specified in paragraphs 4(c) and (d) of Schedule 2
  updated to account for the addition of the new project.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of a project

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A project in an aggregation may only be transferred
  to another aggregation if the date on which the project's crediting
  period began is on or after the date on which the crediting period of
  the aggregation to which the project is being added began.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of a project

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a project is withdrawn from an aggregation of
  projects, the proponent must notify the Minister in writing and
  specify the date the project was withdrawn from the aggregation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application deadline

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  application for registration must be submitted

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the project start date is before the date the
    applicable protocol is included in the Compendium of Federal Offset
    Protocols, within 18 months after the date the protocol is included
    in the Compendium of Federal Offset Protocols and no more than 10
    years after the project start date; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the project start date is on or after the date
    on which the applicable protocol is included in the Compendium of
    Federal Offset Protocols, within 18 months after the project start
    date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- project registered in
  another system

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(a), if the project is
  registered in a GHG offset credit system, other than a system
  administered by the federal or a provincial government, at the time
  the applicable protocol is included in the Compendium of Federal
  Offset Protocols, the application may be submitted more than 18 months
  after the protocol is included in the Compendium of Federal Offset
  Protocols but no later than 10 years after the project start date.

### [General Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1346850 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information management system

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A proponent must
implement a data and information management system to collect, manage
and store data and information related to their project in a way that
ensures the integrity, completeness, accuracy and validity of the data
and information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measuring devices

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A proponent must ensure
that any measuring device used to determine a quantity that is related
to the preparation of a project report is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} installed, operated, maintained and calibrated in
  accordance with the manufacturer's specifications or the applicable
  protocol; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintained to be accurate within ± 5%.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quantification

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  proponent must quantify the GHGs emitted and the GHGs removed from the
  atmosphere for the sources, sinks and reservoirs that must be taken
  into account for the baseline scenario and the project scenario, in
  accordance with the applicable protocol. For the purposes of the
  quantification, the applicable global warming potentials set out in
  Schedule 3 to the Act and the applicable emission factors and
  reference values set out in the document Emission Factors and
  Reference Values, published in 2022 by the Department of the
  Environment, are to be used.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), GHGs emitted or removed from
  the atmosphere are not included in the quantification if the GHGs were
  from sources, sinks and reservoirs that were subject to provincial or
  federal pricing mechanisms or the emissions or removals from sources,
  sinks and reservoirs that were required by law or the result of a
  legal requirement unless, with respect to a source, the quantity of
  GHGs emitted increased due to the project.

### [Reversal Risk Management Plan]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1346918 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of plan

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  proponent of a sequestration project for which the tonne-tonne or
  hybrid tonne-year quantification method is used must establish, in
  accordance with the protocol, a reversal risk management plan that
  identifies and assesses reversal risks associated with the project and
  describes any measures and monitoring activities to mitigate those
  risks.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation of plan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The proponent of a sequestration project referred to
  in subsection (1) must implement the reversal risk management plan for
  the duration of the project's crediting period and, in the case of a
  sequestration project for which the tonne-tonne quantification method
  is used, continue to implement that plan for a period of 100 years
  following the end of the crediting period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Updating the plan

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A proponent may update their reversal risk management
  plan but must not cease implementing existing reversal risk mitigation
  measures. The proponent may only add new reversal risk mitigation
  measures or increase the stringency of existing measures.

  the Minister a monitoring report, accompanied by a verification

### [Verification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1346938 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Verification body

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the following accreditation requirements:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is accredited as a verification body, under ISO
    Standard 14065:2020 entitled General principles and requirements for
    bodies validating and verifying environmental information, by the
    Standards Council of Canada, the ANSI National Accreditation Board
    or any other accreditation organization that has a memorandum of
    understanding with the Department of Environment and that is a
    member of the International Accreditation Forum,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it has a scope of accreditation that includes
    technical activities at the project level with respect to GHG
    project verifications, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} it is not suspended by an accreditation
    organization that issued its accreditation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use a verification team that have the necessary
  competencies to verify a project report, corrected project report,
  reversal report or monitoring report, including at least one
  individual with training relevant to the project type, as specified in
  the applicable protocol.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Verification standard

  Standard 14064-3 by applying methods that allow it to make a

  Standard as to whether, in its opinion,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a project report or corrected
    project report

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of GHG reductions reported in the

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the report was prepared in accordance with these

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the project was implemented in accordance with

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the conditions of registration for the project,

      preparation of the verification report,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the proponent meets the requirements for the
      issuance of offset credits, set out in section 7, at the time of
      the preparation of the verification report, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} no material discrepancy, referred to in
      subsection (2) or (3), as the case may be, exists with respect to
      the quantity of GHG reductions reported in the report;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a reversal report

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} reversal risk mitigation measures and monitoring
      activities were implemented in accordance with the reversal risk
      management plan prior to the reversal,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the report was prepared in accordance with these
      Regulations, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the errors or omissions identified during the
      verification and referred to in paragraph 3(b) of Schedule 5 do

      (4); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a monitoring report

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the report was prepared in accordance with these

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the reversal risk mitigation measures and

      reversal risk management plan, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the reversal risk mitigation measures and
      monitoring activities that were implemented were those set out in
      the report.

      during the verification and that may be quantified, the amount,

### [Issuance of Offset Credits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1347025 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria for issuance of offset credits

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must issue offset credits to a proponent for the period
  covered by a project report, in the amount determined in accordance
  with subsection (2), if the requirements of section 7 are met and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the project report and any corrected project report
    is free of material discrepancies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of GHG reductions referred to in any
    of subsections 20(1) to (3) reported in the report were quantified
    in accordance with these Regulations; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the GHG reductions for which the credits are issued
    were generated during the period covered by the report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of offset credits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The number of offset credits that the Minister must
  issue in respect of a project for each calendar year covered by the
  report is determined by the following formula:

  ::: {#1347034 .Subsection}
  A~i~ -- B~i~ -- C~i~ -- D~i~

  where

  [A~i~]{.dfn}
  :   is the total reported quantity of GHG reductions determined in
      accordance with subsection 20(1) or (2) or paragraph 20(3)(a) or
      (b), as the case may be, for calendar year "i", expressed in
      CO~2~e tonnes;

  [B~i~]{.dfn}
  :   is the product of the description of A and percentage of credits
      the proponent agrees to forgo, for calendar year "i", with respect
      to the project, pertaining to direct financial incentives in
      accordance with an agreement;

  [C~i~]{.dfn}
  :   is for calendar year "i", equal to the product of the result of
      the following formula and either, for a sequestration project for
      which the tonne-tonne or hybrid tonne-year quantification method
      is used, the sum of 3% and the percentage set out in the protocol
      that corresponds to the reversal risk mitigation measures and
      monitoring activities implemented for the project for that
      calendar year, or, for any other project, 3%:
      ::: {#1347039 .NestedFormula}
      A~i~ -- (B~i~ + D~i~)
      :::

  [D~i~]{.dfn}
  :   is the net increase in GHGs that was carried forward in accordance
      with subsection 20(5) for calendar year "i"; and

  [i]{.dfn}
  :   is the i^th^ calendar year, where "i" goes from 1 to n and where n
      is the number of calendar years covered by the report.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rounding

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), any result
  calculated under that subsection is to be rounded to the nearest whole
  number and, if the number is equidistant between two whole consecutive
  numbers, to the higher number.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must notify
the proponent of the number of offset credits being issued for each
calendar year in the period covered by the report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Environmental integrity account

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must deposit, for a given calendar year, the number of offset
  credits equal to the amount determined for C in subsection 29(2) into
  the environmental integrity account for that calendar year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Offset credits in the environmental integrity account
  may not be sold or transferred. The credits may only be revoked in
  accordance with these Regulations.

### [Errors and Omissions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1347052 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Errors or omissions --- identified by
proponent

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If,
  within eight years after submitting a project report, a proponent
  becomes aware of an error or omission in the report, the proponent
  must notify the Minister in writing as soon as feasible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice from proponent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the project registration number or, in the case of
    an aggregation of projects, the registration number of the
    aggregation along with the project registration number for the
    projects in which an error or omission occurred;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating whether the error or
    omission had an impact on the quantity of GHG reductions reported in
    the project report; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating whether the error or
    omission led to an over-issuance or under-issuance of offset
    credits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corrected project report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The proponent must submit to the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a corrected project report within 60 days after the
    day on which the notice is submitted, if the notice indicates that
    the error or omission does not impact the quantity of GHG reductions
    provided in the project report; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corrected project report along with a
    verification report, prepared in accordance with section 28, within
    90 days after the day on which the notice is submitted, if the
    notice indicates that the error or omission impacts the quantity of
    GHG reductions provided in the project report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of corrected report

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The corrected project report must include the
  information referred to in subsection 20(1), (2) or (3) with respect
  to the period covered by the report and, under a heading, the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information provided in the original project
    report that required correction along with a description of the
    corrections made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the circumstances that led to the
    error or omission and why the error or omission was not previously
    detected;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures that have been and
    will be implemented to avoid future errors or omissions of the same
    type;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the difference between the number of
    offset credits that were calculated in accordance with section 29
    and the number of offset credits that is calculated based on the
    corrected project report; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating whether the aggregate of the
    errors and omissions constitute a material discrepancy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The corrected project report must be accompanied by a
  declaration, dated and signed by the proponent or their authorized
  official, stating that the information contained in the report is
  accurate and complete.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Errors or omissions --- identified by
Minister

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** If, within eight years
after a project report has been submitted under these Regulations, the
Minister is of the opinion that there is an error or omission that
requires a corrected report to be submitted, the Minister must require
the proponent to submit

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the error or omission did not have an impact on
  the quantity of GHG reductions reported in the project report, a
  corrected project report within 60 days after the day on which the
  Minister required it; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the error or omission had an impact on the
  quantity of GHG reductions reported in the project report, a corrected
  project report, accompanied by a verification report prepared in
  accordance with section 28, within 90 days after the day on which the
  Minister required it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Under-issuance

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** If a corrected project
report indicates that an under-issuance of offset credits was issued for
a calendar year, the Minister must issue to the proponent the number of
offset credits for that calendar year that is equal to the difference
referred to in paragraph 32(4)(d) for that calendar year and deposit a
corresponding number of credits into the environmental integrity
account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Over-issuance

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  corrected project report indicates that there was an over-issuance of
  offset credits, the proponent must remit to the Minister, with the
  corrected report, the number of offset credits that is equal to the
  difference referred to in paragraph 32(4)(d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the proponent does not remit the number of offset
  credits required under subsection (1), the Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of section 180 of the Act, revoke
    a number of offset credits and that are in the proponent's GHG
    Offset Credit System account, issued with respect to the project,
    equal to the number of offset credits necessary to make up the
    difference; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the number of offset credits remitted by the
    proponent under subsection (1) or revoked under paragraph (a) is
    less than the number of offset credits necessary to make up the
    difference,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsection 181(1) of the Act,
      require the proponent to remit compliance units by notifying the
      them of the number of compliance units to be remitted and the
      deadline by which the remittance is to be made, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the proponent does not remit compliance
      units in accordance with subparagraph (i) or make a payment in
      lieu of remitting compliance units thereof under subsection 181(3)
      of the Act, revoke, for the purposes of section 180 of the Act,
      offset credits in the environmental integrity account to make up
      the difference.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Environmental integrity account

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, for the purposes of section 180 of
  the Act, revoke a number of offset credits in the environmental
  integrity account that is equal to the number of offset credits
  deposited into that account as a result of the error or omission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of remittance

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), the compliance units
  remitted to the Minister for the purposes of subparagraph (2)(b)(i),
  in accordance with subsection 181(2) of the Act, must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of offset credits or units or credits
    recognized as compliance units under a regulation made under the
    Act, have been issued for GHG reductions that occurred within the
    eight calendar years before the deadline indicated in the notice
    provided under that subparagraph; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of surplus credits, have been issued
    within five calendar years before the deadline indicated in the
    notice provided under that subparagraph.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surplus credits

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a proponent is required to remit compliance units,
  the proponent may remit surplus credits if those surplus credits were
  issued to a covered facility located in a province listed in Part 2 of
  Schedule 1 to the Act in the calendar year during which the Minister
  notified the proponent under subparagraph (2)(b)(i) of the requirement
  to remit compliance units.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension --- error or omission

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 180(1) of the Act, if the Minister is of
  the opinion, based on a notice referred to in subsection 32(2) or in
  accordance with section 33, that an error or omission resulted in the
  over-issuance of offset credits, the Minister may suspend offset
  credits for the applicable project in the proponent's GHG Offset
  Credit System account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of suspension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must, without delay, notify the
  proponent of the suspension of the credits, the reasons for the
  suspension and the date on which it takes effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lifting of suspension

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must lift the suspension if the number
  of compliance units remitted or revoked, or payments made under
  section 35, compensate for the amount of the over-issuance.

## [Reversals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1347104 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proponent's notice of reversal

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  proponent of a sequestration project --- other than a sequestration
  project for which the tonne-year quantification method is used ---
  becomes aware of a reversal, they must, without delay, notify the
  Minister in writing, and include the following information with the
  notice:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the project registration number, or in the case of
    an aggregation of projects, the registration number of the
    aggregation along with the project registration numbers for the
    projects in respect of which a reversal occurred;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the reversal started, the date on
    which the GHGs stopped being released, if applicable, and a
    description of the circumstances and causes of the reversal; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the steps taken to contain the reversal or prevent
    the release of additional GHGs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reversal report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The proponent must, within 18 months after the date
  of the notice, submit to the Minister a reversal report, together with
  a verification report, that includes the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the registration number of the project;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the title of the applicable protocol and, if more
    than one version of the protocol is included in the Compendium of
    Federal Offset Protocols, the date of the applicable version;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the circumstances and causes of the reversal;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the location within the geographic boundaries of
    the project where the reversal occurred;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the reversal started and, if
    applicable, the date on which the GHGs stopped being released;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the list of reversal risk mitigation measures and
    monitoring activities implemented since the last monitoring report
    but prior to the reversal, along with the date of their
    implementation;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of GHGs released into the atmosphere
    during the reversal up to the date the report is prepared,
    determined in accordance with the protocol, expressed in CO~2~e
    tonnes; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the data, values and calculations used to quantify
    the GHGs released into the atmosphere, that are specified in
    paragraph (g).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aggregation of projects

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of an aggregation of projects, the
  reversal report referred to in subsection (2) must contain the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the registration number of the aggregation of
    projects along with the project registration numbers for the
    projects in respect of which a reversal occurred;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the title of the applicable protocol and, if more
    than one version of the protocol is included in the Compendium of
    Federal Offset Protocols, the date of the applicable version; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the information referred to in paragraphs (2)(c)
    to (h) for the projects within the aggregation in respect of which a
    reversal occurred.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The reversal report must be accompanied by a
  declaration, dated and signed by the proponent or their authorized
  official, stating that the information contained in the report is
  accurate and complete.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of offset credits

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** If, for the purposes of
subsection 180(1) of the Act, the Minister suspends offset credits in
the proponent's GHG Offset Credit System account --- either because the
Minister has received notice from a proponent that a reversal has
occurred or because he or she has reasonable grounds to suspect that a
reversal has occurred ---, the Minister must notify the proponent of the
suspension and its effective date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to the Minister

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister suspends offset credits in the proponent's GHG Offset Credit
  System account because the Minister has reasonable grounds to suspect
  that a reversal has occurred, the proponent must, within 60 days after
  the date the notice referred to in section 38 is received, submit to
  the Minister the information referred to in subsection 37(1) and, if
  the proponent contests that a reversal has occurred, submit the list
  of reversal risk mitigation measures and monitoring activities that
  were implemented since the submission of the last monitoring report
  and a declaration by the proponent that the reversal risk management
  plan was implemented and that no reversal occurred.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must notify the proponent of the
  Minister's decision with respect to whether or not a reversal has
  occurred.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reversal report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines that a reversal has
  occurred, the proponent must, within 18 months after the date of the
  notice referred to in subsection (2) is received, submit to the
  Minister the reversal report set out in subsection 37(2) and the
  declaration specified in subsection 37(4), together with a
  verification report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evaluation of reversal

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Following receipt of the reversal
  report, the Minister must determine whether the cause of the reversal
  was within the control of the proponent and whether there was a
  failure to implement a reversal risk management plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voluntary reversal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines that the cause of the
  reversal was within the control of the proponent or there was a
  failure to implement a reversal risk management plan, the Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of section 180 of the Act, revoke
    any offset credits in the proponent's GHG Offset Credit System
    account for the project in respect of which the reversal occurred
    and a number of offset credits in the environmental integrity
    account equal to the number of offset credits deposited into that
    account in respect of the project;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the number of offset credits revoked from the
    proponent's GHG Offset Credit System account for the project under
    paragraph (a) is less than the number of offset credits issued to
    the proponent in respect of the project,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsection 181(1) of the Act,
      require the proponent to remit compliance units by giving them
      notice indicating the number of compliance units to be remitted
      and the deadline by which the remittance is to be made, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the proponent does not remit compliance
      units in accordance with subparagraph (i) or make a payment in
      lieu under subsection 181(3) of the Act, revoke, for the purposes
      of section 180 of the Act, offset credits in the environmental
      integrity account to make up the difference; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} cancel the registration of the project in
    accordance with paragraph 14(1)(e).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of remittance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), the compliance units
  remitted to the Minister under subparagraph (2)(b)(i), in accordance
  with subsection 181(2) of the Act, must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of offset credits or units or credits
    recognized by a regulation made under the Act, have been issued for
    GHG reductions that occurred within the eight calendar years before
    the deadline indicated in the notice provided under that
    subparagraph; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of surplus credits, have been issued
    within five calendar years before the deadline indicated in the
    notice provided to the proponent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surplus credits

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the case where a proponent is required to remit
  compliance units, the proponent may remit surplus credits if those
  surplus credits were issued to a covered facility located in a
  province listed in Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the Act in the calendar
  year the Minister notified the proponent under subparagraph (2)(b)(i)
  of the requirement to remit compliance units.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Involuntary reversal

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines that the cause of the
  reversal was not within the proponent's control and there was no
  failure to implement the reversal risk management plan, the Minister
  may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} lift any suspension;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} revoke, for the purposes of section 180 of the Act,
    the number of offset credits in the environmental integrity account
    that corresponds to the lesser of the quantity of the GHGs released
    into the atmosphere during the reversal and the quantity of offset
    credits that have been issued in respect of the project; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, cancel the registration of the
    project in accordance with paragraph 14(1)(f).

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the proponent has exclusive
    entitlement to the credits issued for the GHG reductions generated
    by the project and that they have documents that demonstrate that
    entitlement;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the proponent has the necessary
    authorizations to carry out the project activities and that they
    have documents that demonstrate those authorizations, including, in
    the case of a project that is located, in part or in whole, on
    private property that is not owned by the proponent or that uses
    equipment that is not owned by the proponent, a declaration signed
    by the owner stating that they authorize the use of their land or
    their equipment to implement, as the case may be;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a statement the proponent is in compliance with the
    legislative and regulatory requirements applicable to the project;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a statement that neither the proponent nor their
    authorized official has been found guilty of an offence under
    section 380 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) since submitting
    the application for registration; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a statement that no credits were attributed under
    another GHG reduction mechanism for the GHG reductions generated by
    the project.

- **2** Information with respect to the project:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the project registration number;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reporting period covered by the project report;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the title of the protocol that is applicable to the
    project and, if more than one version of the protocol is included in
    the Compendium of Federal Offset Protocols, the date of the
    applicable version;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of any changes to the latest
    information provided in either the project registration application,
    the latest application for renewal of the crediting period or the
    latest project report;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the project, including the
    activities set out in the protocol undertaken to prevent GHGs from
    being emitted or to remove GHGs from the atmosphere, as applicable,
    and the equipment set out in the protocol, used for those
    activities;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a list of the sources, sinks and reservoirs that,
    in accordance with the protocol, must be included in the
    quantification of aggregate of all of the GHGs emitted and the GHGs
    removed from the atmosphere based on the project activities
    undertaken, for each calendar year, specifying, in accordance with
    the protocol, if those sources, sinks and reservoirs

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are included in the baseline scenario, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are included in the project scenario;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the value that corresponds to the leakage discount
    factor that was applied when determining the quantity of GHG
    reductions, in accordance with the protocol, if applicable;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of all of the GHGs emitted and the
    GHGs removed from the atmosphere from the sources, sinks and
    reservoirs as specified in the protocol for the baseline scenario,
    quantified in accordance with the protocol, expressed in CO~2~e
    tonnes, by calendar year;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of all of the GHGs emitted and the
    GHGs removed from the atmosphere from the sources, sinks and
    reservoirs included in the protocol for the project scenario,
    quantified in accordance with the protocol, expressed in CO~2~e
    tonnes, by calendar year;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} if, for the first project report, the difference
    between the quantity determined for A under subsection 20(1) --- or
    the total quantity referred to in paragraph (h) --- and that
    determined for B under subsection 20(1) --- or that referred to in
    paragraph (i) --- indicates a net increase in GHG emissions, the
    quantity of GHG emissions that must be carried forward to the next
    project report in accordance with subsection 20(5) of these
    Regulations;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the applicable percentage determined for element C
    in the formula set out in subsection 29(2) of these Regulations;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} any data, values, and calculation methods used to
    quantify the GHG reductions referred to in subsections 20(1) or (2),
    including any applicable GHG leakage reductions;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} any information regarding GHG reductions that the
    protocol requires to be included in the project report; and

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} any data and calculations used to estimate missing
    data.

- **3** A statement that environmental and social safeguards, as set out
  in the protocol, have been implemented to minimize the potential
  negative outcomes of the project and a description of the implemented
  measures.

- **4** If the proponent is choosing not to have a project report
  verified in accordance with subsection 20(9), a statement that they
  have chosen not to have the project report verified and they have
  agree to not have offset credits issued for the period covered by that
  report.

## [SCHEDULE 4]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsection 20(3))]{.OriginatingRef}[Content of the Report for an Aggregation of Projects]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-1347358 .scheduleLabel}

- **1** Information with respect to the proponent

    used by them) and civic address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, title, civic and postal addresses,
    telephone number and, if any, email address of their authorized
    official.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name, title, civic and postal addresses,
    telephone number and, if any, email address of a contact person, if

    proponent by the Canada Revenue Agency, or, if the proponent is a
    charity that does not have a federal Business Number, the registered
    charity number assigned to them by the Canada Revenue Agency;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the proponent has exclusive
    entitlement to claim the credits issued for the GHG reductions
    generated by the projects in the aggregation and that they have
    documents that demonstrate that entitlement;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statement that neither the proponent nor their
    authorized official has been found guilty of an offence under
    section 380 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) since submitting
    the application for registration; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a statement that no credits were attributed under
    another GHG reduction mechanism for the GHG reductions generated by
    the projects in the aggregation.

- **2** Information with respect to the person carrying out the project
  activities for each project in the aggregation:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement that they have the necessary
    authorizations to carry out the project activities and that they
    have documents that demonstrate those authorizations, including, in
    the case of a project that is located, in part or in whole, on
    private property that is not owned by person carrying out the
    project activities or that uses equipment that is not owned by
    person carrying out the project activities, a declaration signed by
    the owner stating that they authorize the use of their land or their
    equipment to implement, as the case may be; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement that they have complied with the
    legislative and regulatory requirements applicable to the project.

- **3** Information with respect to each project in the aggregation:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the project registration number;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of any changes to the latest
    information provided in either the registration application, the
    latest application for renewal of the crediting period or in the
    latest project report;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the project, including its name,
    the activities set out in the protocol that are undertaken to
    prevent GHGs from being emitted or to remove GHGs from the
    atmosphere, as applicable, and the equipment, as set out in the
    protocol, used for those activities;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a list of the sources, sinks and reservoirs that,
    in accordance with the protocol, must be included in the
    quantification of the aggregate of all of the GHG emitted and the
    GHGs removed from the atmosphere based on the project activities
    undertaken, for each calendar year, specifying, in accordance with
    the protocol, if those sources, sinks and reservoirs

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are included in the baseline scenario, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are included in the project scenario;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the value that corresponds to the leakage discount
    factor that was applied when determining the quantity of GHG
    reductions, in accordance with the protocol, if applicable;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of all of the GHGs emitted and the
    GHGs removed from the atmosphere from the sources, sinks and
    reservoirs as specified in the protocol for the baseline scenario,
    quantified in accordance with the protocol, expressed in CO~2~e
    tonnes, by calendar year;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of all of the GHG emitted and the
    GHGs removed from the atmosphere from the sources, sinks and
    reservoirs as specified in the protocol for the project scenario,
    quantified in accordance with the protocol, expressed in CO~2~e
    tonnes, by calendar year;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if, in the first project report, the difference
    between the quantities referred to in element A of the formula set
    out in subsection 20(3) --- or in paragraph (f) --- and element C of
    the formula set out in subsection 20(3) --- or in paragraph (g) ---
    indicate a net increase in GHG emissions, the quantity of GHG
    emissions that must be carried over in accordance with subsection
    20(5) of these Regulations;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the applicable percentage determined for C in the
    formula set out in subsection 29(2) of these Regulations;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} any data, values, and calculation methods used to
    quantify the GHG reductions referred to in subsection 20(3) of these
    Regulations, including any applicable GHG leakage deductions;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} any information regarding GHG reductions that the
    protocol requires to be included in the project report; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} any data and calculations used to estimate missing
    data.

- **4** With respect to each project, a statement that environmental and
  social safeguards, as set out in the protocol, have been implemented
  to minimize the potential negative outcomes of the project and a
  description of the implemented measures.

- **5** If the proponent is choosing not to have a project report
  verified in accordance with subsection 20(9) of the Regulations, a

  verified and that they agree to not have offset credits issued for the
  period covered by the report.

- **6** Information with respect to the aggregation:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the period covered by the project report; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the title of the applicable protocol and, if more
    than one version of the protocol is included in the Compendium of
    Federal Offset Protocols, the date of the applicable version.

## [SCHEDULE 5]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subparagraph 24(1)(b)(iii) and section 28)]{.OriginatingRef}[Content of the Verification Report]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-1347392 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The following information for the verification of all reports:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the proponent:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their name (including any trade name or other
      name used by them) and civic address,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name, title, civic and postal addresses,
      telephone number and, if any, email address of the proponent's
      authorized official,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the name, title, civic and postal addresses,
      telephone number and, if any, email address of a contact person,
      if different from the authorized official, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the federal Business Number assigned to them by
      the Canada Revenue Agency, or, with respect to a charity that does
      not have a federal Business Number, the registered charity number
      assigned to them by the Canada Revenue Agency;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the project:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the project registration number,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the municipal address of the physical site, if
      applicable,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the coordinates (latitude and longitude), in
      decimal places to five decimal places, of the site on which the
      project activities are undertaken,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the geographic boundaries of the site where the
      project activities are undertaken and the site plan, established
      in accordance with the protocol, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the title of the protocol that is applicable to
      the project and, if more than one version is included to the
      Compendium of Federal Offset Protocols, the date of the applicable
      version; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the verification:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name and civic address of the verification
      body, as well as the name, telephone number and email address of
      the lead verifier for the team that conducted the verification,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the
      accreditation organization that accredited the verification body
      and the date of the accreditation,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the names and functions of each member of the
      verification team,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the version of the ISO standard 14064-3
      according to which the verification was conducted and a
      description of the objectives and scope of the verification and
      the verification criteria,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the verification procedures
      conducted on the data and information supporting the report that
      is being verified including

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} any assessments, data sampling, tests and
        reviews that were conducted during the verification,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} any tests of the GHG information system and
        controls,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the date and location of each visit conducted
        for the purposes of section 27 of these Regulations, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a declaration, dated and signed by the lead
      verifier, stating that the requirements of section 25 of these
      Regulations have been complied with and that any real or potential
      conflicts of interest have been effectively managed,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} a declaration signed and dated by a reviewer
      who is not a member of the verification team, stating their
      approval of the verification report, including the name, civic
      address, telephone number and email address of that reviewer,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the period covered by the report that is being
      verified, and

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the date and location of each visit that has
      been conducted for the purposes of the verification.

- **2** In the case of a verification of a project report or corrected
  project report, the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the first project report, an
    indication of whether the project start date is correct;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the GHG reductions generated by the project --- the
    quantity of tonnes of GHG prevented from being emitted or removed
    from the atmosphere --- expressed in CO~2~e tonnes, for each
    calendar year covered by the report;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a record of errors or omissions, if they may be
    quantified, capable of influencing the quantity of GHG reductions
    generated by the project, identified during the verification, of the
    data, information or methods used in the preparation of the report
    that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} with respect to each error or omission, the
      number of CO~2~e tonnes to which the error or omission
      corresponds, the related percentage calculated in accordance with
      subparagraph 24(2) or (3), as the case may be, of these
      Regulations and a statement indicating whether the error or
      omission results in an understatement or overstatement,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the aggregate of errors or
      omissions, if it may be quantified, the sum of the absolute value
      of the overstatements and understatements, expressed in CO~2~e
      tonnes, the related percentage calculated in accordance with
      subparagraph 24(3) of these Regulations and a statement indicating
      whether the net result is an understatement or an overstatement,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} includes any corrections made by the proponent
      as a result of the errors or omissions;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an opinion from the verification body in accordance
    with Standard ISO 14064-3 as to whether

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of GHG reductions reported in the
      report was calculated in accordance with these Regulations,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the report was prepared in accordance with these
      Regulations,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the project was implemented in accordance with
      the applicable protocol,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the conditions of registration for the project,
      set out in section 8 or 9, are still being met at the time of the
      preparation of the verification report,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the proponent meets the requirements for the
      issuance of credits, set out in section 7, at the time of the
      preparation of the verification report, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} no material discrepancy exists with respect to
      the quantity of GHG reductions reported in the report; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any modifications or limitations, related to a
    modified or adverse opinion, that prevented the verification body
    from providing an unmodified opinion with respect to the elements
    set out in paragraph (d), if applicable.

- **3** In the case of a verification of a reversal report, the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of GHGs released into the atmosphere
    during the reversal up to the date the reversal report is prepared,
    expressed in CO~2~e tonnes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a record of errors or omissions capable of
    influencing the assessment of the quantity of the GHGs released into
    the atmosphere during the reversal, identified during the
    verification, of data, information or methods used in the
    preparation of the reversal report that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} includes any corrections made by the proponent as
      a result of the errors or omissions, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} sets out the calculations made for the purpose
      of the opinion referred to in subparagraph 3(c)(iii);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an opinion from the verification body as to whether

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} reversal risk mitigation measures and monitoring
      activities were implemented in accordance with the reversal risk
      management plan,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the reversal report was prepared in accordance
      with these Regulations,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the errors or omissions identified during the
      verification and referred to in paragraph (b) constitute a
      material discrepancy; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any modifications or limitations, related to a
    modified or adverse opinion, that prevented the verification body
    from providing an unmodified opinion with respect to the elements
    set out in paragraph (c).

- **4** In the case of a verification of a monitoring report, the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an opinion as to whether

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the monitoring report was prepared in accordance
      with these Regulations,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the reversal risk mitigation measures and
      monitoring activities were implemented in accordance with the
      reversal risk management plan, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the mitigation measures and monitoring
      activities that were implemented were those set out in the
      monitoring report; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any modifications or limitations, related to a
    modified or adverse opinion, that prevented the verification body
    from providing an unmodified opinion with respect to the elements
    set out in paragraph (a).
