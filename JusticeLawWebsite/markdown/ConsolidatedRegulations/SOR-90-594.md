## Veterans Health Care Regulations {#veterans-health-care-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-594
:::

[DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS ACT](/eng/acts/V-1)

Registration 1990-08-28

Regulations Respecting Health Care For Veterans And Other Persons

P.C. 1990-1825 1990-08-28
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Veterans Affairs and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
subsection 5(1) of the [Department of Veterans Affairs
Act](/eng/acts/V-1), is pleased hereby to approve the revocation by the
Minister of Veterans Affairs of the Veterans Care Regulations, approved
by Order in Council P.C. 1984-2971 of August 31, 1984[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnoteastrx_e){#footnoteastrx_e-ID0EDBAA}, and
to approve the annexed [Regulations respecting health care for veterans
and other persons](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-594), made by the Minister of
Veterans Affairs, in substitution therefor, effective September 1, 1990.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-926578 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Veterans Health Care
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-594).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-926580 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[adult residential care]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[adult residential care]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a service
    provided in a health care facility to meet a type I health
    need; ([soins institutionnels pour adultes]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Canada service veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canada service veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a veteran who

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} served

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on full-time active service, other than service
        in a theatre of actual war as that expression is defined in
        subsection 37(8) of the [War Veterans Allowance
        Act](/eng/acts/W-3), as a member of the naval, army or air
        forces of Canada or of similar forces raised in Newfoundland,
        for a minimum of 365 days during any of the following periods,
        namely,

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the period beginning on August 4, 1914 and
          ending on August 31, 1921, and

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the period beginning on September 1, 1939 and
          ending on August 15, 1945, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} as a Canadian merchant mariner of World War I
        or World War II, as described in section 21.1 of the [Pension
        Act](/eng/acts/P-6), other than as a merchant navy veteran of
        World War I or World War II within the meaning of subsection
        37(7.3) of the [War Veterans Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3), for
        a minimum of 365 days during any of the following periods,
        namely,

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the period beginning on August 4, 1914 and
          ending on August 31, 1921, and

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the period beginning on September 1, 1939 and
          ending on August 15, 1945,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is 65 years of age or more, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} would qualify as a recipient under the [War
      Veterans Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3) but for the fact

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the veteran does not meet the requirements
        of subsection 37(3) or (5) of that Act, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the veteran or the veteran's spouse or
          common-law partner is in receipt of payments made under the
          [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9) or under similar
          legislation of another country, and

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the veteran does not meet the requirements of
          subsection 37(3) or (5) of the [War Veterans Allowance
          Act](/eng/acts/W-3); ([ancien combattant ayant servi au
          Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[chronic care]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chronic care]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a service provided in a
    health care facility to meet a type III health need; ([soins
    prolongés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[civilian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[civilian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 56(1) of the [Civilian War-related Benefits
    Act](/eng/acts/C-31); ([civil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[civilian pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[civilian pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is
    entitled to a pension under

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Parts I to III or VI to X of the [Civilian
      War-related Benefits Act](/eng/acts/C-31), or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Civilian Government Employees (War)
      Compensation Order; ([pensionné civil]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[client]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[client]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} veteran pensioner, income-qualified veteran,
      overseas service veteran or veteran to whom paragraphs (a) and (b)
      of the definition [[Canada service veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
      apply,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} civilian pensioner, income-qualified civilian or
      civilian,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Newfoundland Special Award pensioner,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} military service pensioner,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Red Cross pensioner,

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} flying accident pensioner,

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} special duty service pensioner,

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} prisoner of war entitled to basic compensation
      under subsection 71.2(1) of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} veteran described in paragraph (h) of the
      definition [[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} former member or reserve force member who is
      entitled to a disability award or entitled to pain and suffering
      compensation, or

    - [(k)]{.lawlabel} former member or reserve force member who has
      received a detention benefit under Part 3 of the [Veterans
      Well-being Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8); ([client]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an
    individual, means a person who is cohabiting with the individual at
    the relevant time in a conjugal relationship, having so cohabited
    for a period of at least one year. For greater certainty,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual's death, the
      [[relevant time]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the time of that
      death, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} common-law partners cease to be common-law
      partners when they cease to cohabit except if it is by reason only
      of the placement of one of the partners in a health care
      facility; ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[community facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[community facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a health care
    facility in Canada that is approved by the Minister and provides
    accommodation and meals and adult residential care, intermediate
    care or chronic care; ([établissement
    communautaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contract bed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contract bed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bed that is set aside
    in a community facility pursuant to a contractual arrangement
    entered into by the Minister for the adult residential care,
    intermediate care or chronic care of certain veterans; ([lit
    réservé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[departmental facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[departmental facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2016-31, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[dual service veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dual service veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2022-69, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[entitled to a disability award]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entitled to a disability award]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in
    respect of a member or former member, that the member or former
    member

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has received a [[disability
      award]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
      [Veterans Well-being Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8),

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has not received a [[disability
      award]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of
      that Act because one of the conditions set out in section 53 of
      that Act as it read immediately before April 1, 2019 had not been
      met as of that day, unless a decision regarding the application
      for pain and suffering compensation referred to in subsection
      174(1) of the [Budget Implementation Act, 2018, No.
      1](/eng/acts/B-9.8592) has been made, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} but for subsection 54(1) of that Act as it read
      immediately before April 1, 2019, would have received a
      [[disability award]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
      subsection 2(1) of that Act; ([droit à une indemnité
      d'invalidité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entitled to a disability award in respect of special duty service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entitled to a disability award in respect of special duty
    service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2018-177, s.
    21\]]{.Repealed}

[[entitled to a disability award or pain and suffering compensation in respect of special duty service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entitled to a disability award or pain and suffering compensation
    in respect of special duty service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in
    respect of a member or former member, that the injury or disease, or
    its aggravation, for which the member or former member is entitled
    to a disability award or entitled to pain and suffering compensation
    is attributable to or was incurred during [[special duty
    service]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
    [Veterans Well-being Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8); ([droit à une indemnité
    d'invalidité ou à une indemnité pour douleur et souffrance en raison
    du service spécial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entitled to a pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entitled to a pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
    person, that the person receives a pension, has received a final
    payment of a pension or has been declared eligible for a
    pension; ([droit à une pension]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entitled to pain and suffering compensation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entitled to pain and suffering
    compensation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a member or
    former member, that the member or former member

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has received pain and suffering compensation
      under subsection 45(1) or 47(1) of the [Veterans Well-being
      Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8),

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is referred to in subsection 45(1) of that Act
      but whose extent of disability has not yet been assessed, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} but for subsection 56.4(1) of that Act, would
      have received pain and suffering compensation under subsection
      45(1) or 47(1) of that Act; ([droit à une indemnité pour douleur
      et souffrance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[flying accident pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[flying accident pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
    entitled to a pension that is compensation under the [Flying
    Accidents Compensation Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._10)
    for an injury sustained by the person; ([pensionné à la suite d'un
    accident d'aviation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[full-time active service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[full-time active service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that part of
    active service that does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} periods of absence without leave or leave of
      absence without pay,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} periods in respect of which pay is forfeited, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} time served in prison or other
      detention; ([service actif à temps plein]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[health]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[health]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a state of physical, mental and
    social well-being; ([santé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[health professional]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[health professional]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a physician,
    dentist, nurse or other health care practitioner approved by the
    Minister; ([professionnel de la santé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[income-qualified civilian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[income-qualified civilian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a civilian
    who is in receipt of an allowance under subsection 57(1) of the
    [Civilian War-related Benefits Act](/eng/acts/C-31) or in respect of
    whom a determination has been made that the civilian would be
    eligible for such an allowance if the civilian or their spouse or
    common-law partner were not in receipt of, or eligible to receive,
    payments under the [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9) or similar
    legislation of another country; ([civil au revenu
    admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[income-qualified overseas service civilian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[income-qualified overseas service
    civilian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an overseas service civilian
    who is an income-qualified civilian; ([civil au revenu admissible
    ayant servi outre-mer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[income-qualified veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[income-qualified veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a veteran
    referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to (g) of the definition
    [[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} who is in receipt of an allowance
    under the [War Veterans Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3) or in respect
    of whom a determination has been made that the veteran would be
    eligible for such an allowance if the veteran or their spouse or
    common-law partner were not in receipt of, or eligible to receive,
    payments under the [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9) or similar
    legislation of another country; ([ancien combattant au revenu
    admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[intermediate care]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[intermediate care]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a service provided
    in a health care facility to meet a type II health need; ([soins
    intermédiaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[merchant navy veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[merchant navy veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2001-157, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[military service pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[military service pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a former
    member or reserve force member who is entitled to a pension under
    the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) for a disability related to
    military service that was not

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} active service in World War I or World War II,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} service in a theatre of operations, as that
      expression is defined in section 2 of the [Veterans Benefit
      Act](/eng/acts/V-1.3), or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} special duty service, as defined in subsection
      3(1) of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6); ([pensionné du service
      militaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Newfoundland Special Award pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Newfoundland Special Award pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    person who was not entitled to a pension in accordance with the
    terms of the union of Canada and Newfoundland but was recognized by
    the Canadian government as entitled to the continuation of an award
    paid before that union in respect of a disability incurred during
    service in World War I or World War II; ([pensionné titulaire d'une
    attribution spéciale (Terre-Neuve)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[overseas service civilian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[overseas service civilian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a civilian
    described in paragraph (e), (f), (g), (h) or (i) of the definition
    [[civilian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 56(1) of the
    [Civilian War-related Benefits Act](/eng/acts/C-31); ([civil ayant
    servi outre-mer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[overseas service veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[overseas service veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of Parts I and III, a veteran referred
      to in any of paragraphs (a) to (g) of the definition
      [[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} who, before April 1, 1946,
      served during World War I or World War II,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in a theatre of actual war, as that expression
        is defined in subsection 37(8) of the [War Veterans Allowance
        Act](/eng/acts/W-3), or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} as a merchant navy veteran of World War I or
        World War II within the meaning of subsection 37(7.3) of that
        Act,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of Part II, a veteran referred to in
      any of paragraphs (a) to (g) of the definition
      [[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} who served during World War I or
      World War II as defined in subsection 37(10) of that Act

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in a theatre of actual war, as that expression
        is defined in subsection 37(8) of that Act, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} as a merchant navy veteran of World War I or
        World War II within the meaning of subsection 37(7.3) of that
        Act,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a veteran who

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} was on service in a theatre of operations, as
        that expression is defined in section 2 of the [Veterans Benefit
        Act](/eng/acts/V-1.3), as a member of the Canadian Forces,
        including the special force, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} served as a Canadian merchant navy veteran of
        the Korean War, within the meaning of subsection 37(7.4) of the
        [War Veterans Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3), or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an allied veteran described in paragraphs
      37(4)(c.1) and (d.1) or subsection 37(4.2) of the [War Veterans
      Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3); ([ancien combattant ayant servi
      outre-mer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a pension that is awarded under the [Pension
      Act](/eng/acts/P-6), other than a pension that is awarded under
      subsection 22(2) of that Act,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a pension that is awarded under any of Parts I to
      III or VI to X of the [Civilian War-related Benefits
      Act](/eng/acts/C-31),

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a pension to which a Red Cross pensioner is
      entitled,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} compensation that is payable under the [Flying
      Accidents Compensation
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._10), or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a pension that is awarded under the Civilian
      Government Employees (War) Compensation Order,

    but, for greater certainty, does not include a compassionate award
    under section 34 of the [Veterans Review and Appeal Board
    Act](/eng/acts/V-1.6); ([pension]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pensioned condition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pensioned condition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a disability in
    respect of which a person is entitled to a pension; ([état
    indemnisé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Red Cross pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Red Cross pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Red Cross worker
    who is entitled to a pension pursuant to the Memorandum of Agreement
    between Her Majesty The Queen and The Canadian Red Cross Society
    made on October 17, 1952; ([pensionné de la
    Croix-Rouge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reserve force member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reserve force member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} of the Supplementary Reserve Force,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on a period of Class A Reserve Service, as
      described in article 9.06 of the Queen's Regulations and Orders
      for the Canadian Forces, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on a period of 180 days or less of Class B
      Reserve Service, as described in article 9.07 of the Queen's
      Regulations and Orders for the Canadian Forces; ([membre de la
      force de réserve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[seriously disabled]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[seriously disabled]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a client,
    means that the client's extent of disability, in respect of the
    aggregate of all of the client's disability assessments under the
    [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) and the [Veterans Well-being
    Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8), is equal to or greater than
    78%; ([déficience grave]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[special duty area pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[special duty area pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2003-362, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[special duty service pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[special duty service pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    former member or reserve force member who is entitled to a pension
    under the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) for a disability attributable
    to or incurred during special duty service, as defined in subsection
    3(1) of that Act; ([pensionné du service spécial]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[spouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[spouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an individual, means
    a person who is legally married to the individual and who

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} resides with the individual or in a health care
      facility, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintains or is being maintained by the
      individual; ([époux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[supplementary benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[supplementary benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the benefits
    referred to in section 6; ([avantages
    supplémentaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[treatment benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[treatment benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the benefits
    referred to in section 4; ([avantages médicaux]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[type I health need]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[type I health need]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the need of a
    person for personal care and supervision on a continuing basis,
    where the person is ambulant or independently mobile but has
    decreased physical or mental faculties; ([besoins de santé de type
    I]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[type II health need]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[type II health need]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the need of a
    person for personal care on a continuing basis under the supervision
    of a health professional, where the person has a functional
    disability, has reached the apparent limit of recovery and has
    little need for diagnostic or therapeutic services; ([besoins de
    santé de type II]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[type III health need]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[type III health need]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the need of a
    person for personal care and for diagnostic, nursing and therapeutic
    services provided by a health professional on a continuing basis,
    where the person is chronically ill or has a functional disability
    and the acute phase of the illness or disability has ended, whether
    or not the status of the illness or disability is
    unstable; ([besoins de santé de type III]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who was on active service during World
      War I or World War II as a member of the naval, army or air forces
      of Canada or of similar forces raised in Newfoundland,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who was on service in a theatre of
      operations, as that expression is defined in section 2 of the
      [Veterans Benefit Act](/eng/acts/V-1.3), as a member of the
      Canadian Forces, including the special force,

    - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} a person who was on active service during the
      Korean War as a member of the special force, as defined in
      subsection 3(1) of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6),

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a special operator who is deemed to be a veteran
      pursuant to section 3 of the Special Operators War Service
      Benefits Act,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a supervisor who is deemed to be a veteran
      pursuant to section 3 of the Supervisors War Service Benefits Act,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an allied veteran described in subsection 37(4),
      (4.1) or (4.2) of the [War Veterans Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3),

    - [(e.1)]{.lawlabel} a person referred to in paragraph 64(1)(a) or

### [Treatment Benefits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-926714 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following clients are eligible to receive treatment benefits in Canada
  or elsewhere in respect of a pensioned condition:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a veteran pensioner;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a civilian pensioner;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a Red Cross pensioner; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a flying accident pensioner.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Newfoundland Special Award pensioner is eligible to
  receive treatment benefits in Canada or elsewhere in respect of the
  disability for which they receive the award.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The following clients are eligible to receive
  treatment benefits in Canada or elsewhere in respect of a pensioned
  condition or a disability for which they are entitled to a disability
  award or entitled to pain and suffering compensation to the extent
  that those benefits are not available to them as a member or former
  member of the Canadian Forces:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a special duty service pensioner;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a military service pensioner; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a former member or a reserve force member.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The following clients are eligible to receive
  treatment benefits in Canada, for any health condition, to the extent
  that those benefits are not available to them as a member or former
  member of the Canadian Forces nor available as an insured service
  under a provincial health care system:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a veteran pensioner or a civilian pensioner if the
    extent of their disability in respect of the aggregate of all of
    their disability assessments under the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6)
    and the [Veterans Well-being Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8) is equal to or
    greater than 48%;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a client referred to in subsection (1) or (2) who
    is seriously disabled; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a special duty service pensioner or a former member
    or reserve force member who is entitled to a disability award or
    pain and suffering compensation in respect of special duty service,
    if they are eligible to receive any of the veterans independence
    program services referred to in paragraphs 19(a), (b) and (e).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The following clients are eligible to receive
  treatment benefits in Canada, for any health condition, to the extent
  that those benefits are not available as an insured service under a
  provincial health care system:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an income-qualified veteran;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an income-qualified civilian;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a client who is in receipt of adult residential
    care, intermediate care or chronic care in a contract bed;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a client who is in receipt of the cost to them of
    intermediate care or chronic care under section 21.1 or 21.2;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a Canada service veteran who is in receipt of
    chronic care in a community facility under subsection 22(2); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a client who is in receipt of chronic care in a
    community facility under section 22.1.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The following clients are eligible to receive
  treatment benefits in Canada, for any health condition, to the extent
  that those benefits are not available as an insured service under a
  provincial health care system:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if they are eligible under section 15, 17 or 17.1
    to receive any of the veterans independence program services
    referred to in paragraphs 19(a), (b) and (e) or are in receipt of
    those services under section 18:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a veteran pensioner,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an overseas service veteran, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2022-69, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an overseas service civilian; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if they are eligible to receive any of the veterans
    independence program services referred to in paragraphs 19(a), (b)

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a civilian pensioner,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a prisoner of war who is entitled to basic
      compensation under subsection 71.2(1) of the [Pension

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a Canada service veteran, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a former member or a reserve force member who

      Well-being Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8).

  benefits are required is the client's pensioned condition.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The veterans independence
program consists of the following services:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} home care that is provided to or on behalf of a
  client at the client's principal residence, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} health and support services by a health
    professional, such as nursing care, therapy and personal care,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} personal care services by a person other than a
    health professional,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} housekeeping services, namely, those tasks or
    domestic chores to assist with daily living, which services normally
    include only routine housekeeping unless the health and safety of
    the client is at risk,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} access to nutrition services, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} maintenance of the grounds necessary to permit
    independent living at the principal residence, such as the mowing of
    lawns and the removal of ice, snow and leaves from the walkways,
    driveways and eavestroughs, providing:

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the maintenance is the responsibility of the
      client and would normally be performed by the client were the
      client's ability to do so not impaired as a result of the health
      condition the assessment of which indicated the client's
      eligibility for the service, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} there are no relatives of the client living at
      the residence who are capable of performing such maintenance;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} ambulatory health care, namely, health services such
  as health assessments, diagnostic services and social and recreational
  services that are provided by a health professional, and
  transportation between the client's residence and the health centre or
  other similar facility where the health services are provided;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} transportation to foster independence and to enable a
  client to participate in social activities, if an assessment referred
  to in paragraph 15(2)(b), subparagraph 17(c)(ii) or paragraph 18(2)(d)
  indicates that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the client requires home care or ambulatory health
    care, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} social isolation is harmful to the client's
    health;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} home adaptations to a client's principal residence to
  enable the client to carry out the activities of daily living; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} intermediate care in a community facility, other than
  in a contract bed.

      that is effective on July 1 of the same year

    bears to

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the same income factor that was effective on
      July 1 of the preceding year.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The client's income for a month is the total of all
  amounts that are subtracted from the income factor in computing the
  client's allowance payable for that month under subsection 4(3), (6),
  (6.1) or (8), as the case may be, of the [War Veterans Allowance
  Act](/eng/acts/W-3), or that would be so subtracted if the client were
  a recipient under that Act.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The client's personal and family exemption amount for
  any of the 12 months following September 30 of a year is the sum of
  the following amounts:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount for personal comforts, which is equal to
    the amount for personal comforts that applies on September 30 of the
    same year, multiplied by the ratio referred to in paragraph (4)(b);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the client has a spouse or common-law
    partner, an amount equal to the income factor set out in paragraph
    2(a) of the schedule to the [War Veterans Allowance
    Act](/eng/acts/W-3) that is effective on July 1 of the same year;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the client has a spouse or common-law partner
    and one or more dependent children within the meaning of the [War
    Veterans Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3), an amount for each such
    child equal to the income factor set out in item 4 of the schedule
    to that Act that is effective on July 1 of the same year; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the client does not have a spouse or
    common-law partner but has one or more dependent children within the
    meaning of the [War Veterans Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to the income factor set out in
      paragraph 3(a) of the schedule to that Act that is effective on
      July 1 of the same year, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for each such child in addition to one, an
      amount equal to the income factor set out in item 4 of the
      schedule to that Act that is effective on July 1 of the same year.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The cost of accommodation and meals shall be paid by
  a client referred to in subsection (1) as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the client pays the cost directly to the community
    facility; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cost is recovered by the Minister if the cost
    has been paid to the community facility by the Minister, in
    accordance with an arrangement with that facility.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} For the period beginning on October 1, 2021 and
  ending on September 30, 2022,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the maximum monthly accommodation and meal charge
    is \$1,091.43 per month; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount for personal comforts is \$263.62 per
    month.

### [Exceeding Rates]{.HTitleText2} {#h-927125 .Subheading}

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall authorize the payment of costs at a rate that is higher
  than a rate set out in section 20 or 23 if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the higher rate is necessary in order to provide an
    appropriate standard of service or care;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the higher rate is, in the opinion of the Minister,
    necessary on humanitarian grounds;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} no bed is available for less than the higher rate
    in any health care facility that is within a reasonable distance of
    the community in which the client is normally resident;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the client is not accepted for the care the client
    requires by any health care facility that is within a reasonable
    distance of the community in which the client is normally resident
    and the rate at the nearest health care facility that will accept
    the client is higher than the rate set out in that section for that
    care; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an agreement between the Minister and the health
    care facility or the province in which the health care facility is
    situated provides for a higher rate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may authorize that no deduction under
  subsection 7(2) be made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the client's mobility or cognition is severely
    impaired;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the deduction would seriously impede the
    client's ability to access treatment benefits; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a transfer referred to in section
    29.

- SOR/95-440, s. 10
- SOR/98-386, s. 16

### [Limitation on Reimbursements and Payments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-927139 .Subheading}

- **[[34.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a person incurs an expenditure
  to meet a health need, no reimbursement or payment shall be made under
  these Regulations to or in respect of the person except in accordance
  with this section.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (4) to (6), reimbursement or
  payment shall be made if the person was eligible to receive benefits,
  services or care under these Regulations for that health need at the
  time that the expenditure was incurred.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (4) to (6), reimbursement or
  payment shall be made if the person, within 90 days after incurring
  the expenditure, applies for a pension and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is awarded the pension for the condition
    in respect of which the expenditure was incurred and would have been
    eligible to receive benefits, services or care in respect of that
    condition if the person had been entitled to the pension at the time
    the expenditure was incurred;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person is awarded the pension and as a result
    qualifies as a seriously disabled veteran or seriously disabled
    civilian; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person is awarded the pension and, as a result,
    qualifies as a veteran pensioner or civilian pensioner, if the
    aggregate of all of their disability assessments under the [Pension
    Act](/eng/acts/P-6) and the [Veterans Well-being
    Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8) is equal to or greater than 48%.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A claim for reimbursement or payment must be made by
  or on behalf of the person within 18 months of the day on which the
  expenditure was incurred.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Proof of the expenditure must be provided by the
  person or on the person's behalf.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 34(1), no reimbursement or
  payment shall exceed the maximum amount payable under these
  Regulations at the time the expenditure was incurred.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply to a claim in respect of
  the services referred to in subparagraph 19(a)(iii) or (v). Payment
  shall be made for those services only in respect of periods during
  which a person is eligible to receive those services.

### [Payment Arrangements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-927153 .Subheading}

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** Where a client is
eligible under these Regulations to receive a payment in respect of a
benefit, service or care provided or paid for by a third party, the
payment may be made

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} directly to the client;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the third party on behalf of the client; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to the client and the third party jointly.

### [Notice of Decisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-927158 .Subheading}

**[[35.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35.1 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall
notify a client or the client's representative of any decision relating
to the award, increase, decrease, suspension or cancellation of any
benefit under these Regulations concerning or affecting the client.

- SOR/98-386, s. 17
:::

### [Review of Decisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-927162 .Subheading}

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is dissatisfied with any decision made under these
  Regulations may, within 60 days after receiving notice of the decision
  or, where circumstances beyond the control of the person necessitate a
  longer period, within that longer period, apply in writing to the
  Minister for a review of that decision by an official of the
  Department of Veterans Affairs other than the official who made the
  original decision.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a person is dissatisfied with the results of a
  review referred to in subsection (1), the person may, within 60 days
  after receiving notice of the decision on the review unless
  circumstances beyond the control of the applicant necessitate a longer
  period, apply in writing to the Minister for a final decision to be
  rendered by an official of the Department of Veterans Affairs other
  than the official who made the original decision or who reviewed it.

### [Reciprocal and Other Arrangements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-927168 .Subheading}

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** Benefits, services or
care under these Regulations may be accorded to residents of a country
other than Canada in accordance with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a reciprocal or other arrangement made by the
  Minister with the government of that country, with such modifications
  as are necessary to comply with the terms of that arrangement; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} special arrangements under which the benefits,
  services or care are provided at the expense of that country, with
  such modifications as are necessary to comply with the terms of those
  arrangements.
