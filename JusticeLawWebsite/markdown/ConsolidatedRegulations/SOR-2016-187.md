## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832321 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[CFR]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CFR]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Title 16 of the Code of Federal
    Regulations of the United States, revised as of January 1,
    2004. ([CFR]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[gas lighter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gas lighter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a lighter that uses as
    fuel liquid hydrocarbons, such as *n*-butane and isobutane, that
    have a gauge vapour pressure at 24°C greater than 100 kPa. ([briquet
    à gaz]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[lighter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lighter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device that has a fuel
    reservoir, whether detachable or not, and an integral means of
    ignition and that is used for domestic purposes or to ignite tobacco
    products. ([briquet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[luxury lighter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[luxury lighter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a lighter, other than a
    utility lighter, that is refillable and that has

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it is manufactured in Canada, a value of more
      than \$2.50 at the time of sale by the manufacturer; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is imported into Canada, a value for duty
      of more than \$2.50 as determined in accordance with sections 47
      to 55 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([briquet de
      luxe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[production lot]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[production lot]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a group of
    substantially identical lighters that are manufactured by the same
    manufacturer under substantially identical conditions. ([lot de
    production]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shield]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shield]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a structure that totally or
    partially surrounds the fuel orifice of a lighter and projects
    beyond it. ([écran de protection]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[surrogate lighter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[surrogate lighter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device used for
    testing purposes that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} approximates the appearance, size and weight of
      the lighter that it represents;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is, within reasonable manufacturing tolerances,
      identical to the lighter that it represents in all factors that
      affect child resistance, including the operation and force
      required for operation;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} contains no fuel; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} produces a clearly discernible audible or visual
      signal instead of a flame when operated. ([briquet de
      substitution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[utility lighter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[utility lighter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a lighter that is 100
    mm or greater in length when it is in the fully extended
    position. ([briquet à usages multiples]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[wick lighter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wick lighter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a lighter that uses as
    fuel liquid hydrocarbons, such as hexane, that have a gauge vapour
    pressure at 24°C not greater than 34 kPa. ([briquet à
    essence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Specifications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832339 .Part}

### [Application of Force]{.HTitleText2} {#h-832340 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Luxury lighters

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** At least one of the
following manual operations must be performed to achieve ignition of a
luxury lighter:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a deliberate action to produce and maintain a flame;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} at least two separate and distinct actions; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the application of a force equal to or greater than
  15 N.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flame height adjustment

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** If a lighter has a device
to adjust the flame height, the device must require the deliberate
application by the user of a reasonable force.

### [Child Resistance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-832348 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  lighters, other than luxury lighters, must be child resistant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standard

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), a lighter is child
  resistant if its surrogate lighter has been tested in accordance with
  one of the following test protocols and at least 85% of the children
  who participated in the test were unable to cause the surrogate
  lighter to operate:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a utility lighter, the test protocol
    described in section 1212.4, without regard to sections 1212.4(a)(3)
    and (b)(1), of the CFR; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of all other lighters, the test
    protocol described in section 1210.4, without regard to sections
    1210.4(a)(3) and (b)(1), of the CFR.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mechanism

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The mechanism or system
that makes a lighter child resistant must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} reset itself automatically after each operation of
  the lighter's ignition mechanism;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a gas lighter, continue to function
  effectively after each test set out in sections 5 to 9 of the
  schedule; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a wick lighter, continue to function
  effectively after each test set out in sections 11 and 12 of the
  schedule.

### [Gas Lighters]{.HTitleText2} {#h-832361 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flame height and duration

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A gas
  lighter, when tested in accordance with subsection (2), must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the lighter has neither a shield nor a device to
    adjust the flame height, have a vertical flame height of not more
    than 50 mm;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the lighter has a shield but no device to adjust
    the flame height, have a vertical flame height of not more than 100
    mm;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the lighter has a device to adjust the flame
    height, have a vertical flame height of not more than

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 120 mm, when the device is adjusted to produce
      maximum flame height, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 50 mm, when the device is adjusted to produce
      minimum flame height; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} not exhibit a flame above the fuel orifice for more
    than two seconds after the normal action to extinguish the flame has
    been taken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Test procedures

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A gas lighter must be tested in accordance with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the procedures described in sections 5, 6 and 8 of
    the schedule, followed by a repetition of the procedures described
    in sections 5 and 6 of the schedule; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the procedures described in sections 5, 6 and 9 of
    the schedule, followed by a repetition of the procedures described
    in sections 5 and 6 of the schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Abnormal burning

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A gas lighter, when tested
in accordance with the procedures described in sections 5, 6 and 8 to 10
of the schedule, must not exhibit

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a sudden increase in flame height of 50 mm or more;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vertical flame height in excess of the maximum
  values, as the case may be, specified in subsection 6(1);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an explosion;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} combustion at any place other than at the fuel
  orifice of the lighter or in the main flame;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an escape of liquid fuel that produces a shower of
  burning droplets that separate from the main flame; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other abnormal or unsafe burning characteristics.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Structural integrity

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A gas lighter, when tested
in accordance with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the procedures described in sections 5 to 10 of the
  schedule, must not exhibit damage that would make its operation
  unsafe;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the procedures described in section 8 of the
  schedule, must not exhibit an escape of fuel of more than 15 mg/min;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the procedures described in section 9 of the
  schedule, must not exhibit leakage that results in total exhaustion of
  fuel after the cooling period described in paragraph 9(2)(c) of the
  schedule; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the procedures described in section 10 of the
  schedule, must not exhibit a drop in internal pressure of more than
  250 kPa/min.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extended operation

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A gas lighter, when tested
in accordance with the procedures described in section 7 of the
schedule, must not exhibit, during a continuous burning time of two
minutes,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} ignition of its component parts;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} expulsion of the valve mechanism; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} rupture of the fuel reservoir, with or without flame.

### [Wick Lighters]{.HTitleText2} {#h-832394 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Abnormal burning

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A wick lighter, when
tested in accordance with the procedures described in sections 11 and 12
of the schedule, followed by a repetition of the procedures described in
section 11 of the schedule, must not exhibit

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a sudden increase in flame height of 50 mm or more;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an explosion;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} combustion at any place other than at the wick; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other abnormal or unsafe burning characteristics.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Structural integrity

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A wick lighter, when
tested in accordance with the procedures described in section 12 of the
schedule, must not exhibit rupture of its fuel reservoir or any other
damage that could make its operation unsafe.

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832403 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Name of manufacturer or trademark

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  lighter must have permanently marked on it, in such a manner that the
  mark remains clear and visible throughout the lighter's useful life,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the manufacturer, in Roman letters,
    Arabic numerals or a combination of both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a trademark registered in Canada or for which an
    application for registration in Canada is pending.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Production lots

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When more than one production lot is offered for
  sale, every lighter must have marked on it, in a clear and visible
  manner, an identification of its production lot.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principal place of business

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), every lighter must have
  marked on it, in a clear and visible manner,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the lighter is manufactured in Canada, the
    manufacturer's principal place of business or the distributor's name
    and principal place of business; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the lighter is not manufactured in Canada, the
    importer's or distributor's name and principal place of business in
    Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warning

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), every lighter must have
  marked on it, in a clear and visible manner, the warning "KEEP OUT OF
  REACH OF CHILDREN / [TENIR HORS DE LA PORTÉE DES ENFANTS]{.otherLang
  lang="fr"}" or any other warning that conveys the same meaning.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Placement of markings

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The information required by subsections (3) and (4)
  may be marked

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the package, if the lighter is enclosed in a
    sealed package; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the package or on the instructions within the
    package, if the lighter is individually enclosed in an unsealed
    package.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flame height symbol

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Every lighter that has a
device to adjust the flame height must have permanently marked on it, in
such a manner that the mark remains clear and visible throughout the
lighter's useful life, an easily understood symbol that indicates the
direction in which force is to be applied to increase and decrease the
flame height.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refillable lighters --- warning

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  refillable lighter must be accompanied by a clear and legible warning,
  in English and in French, to keep lighters and their fuels out of the
  reach of children.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refillable lighters --- instructions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every refillable lighter must be accompanied by clear
  and legible instructions that set out, in English and in French, the
  safe method of refuelling the lighter, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} information about the type of fuel to be used and a
    warning to use only that type of fuel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a gas lighter, a warning to ensure
    proper mating between the refill container and the fuel reservoir of
    the lighter; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a wick lighter, a warning to fill
    the lighter slowly, to avoid overfilling and to wipe dry the lighter
    and the user's hands before activating the lighter.

## [Compliance Certificate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832431 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents and retention

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A manufacturer or an
importer of a lighter, other than a luxury lighter, must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} possess a certificate that states in English or
  French that its surrogate lighter has been tested and complies with
  the child resistance requirements of subsection 4(2) and that
  specifies

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the person who issued the
    certificate,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the manufacturer of the
    lighter, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the name and model of the lighter;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} keep a copy of the certificate for a period of three
  years after the date of manufacture or importation; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} within 15 days after the day on which an inspector's
  request is received, provide to the inspector, in English or French,
  the test data on which the statement referred to in paragraph (a) is
  based, including the information set out in sections 1210.4(g)(1)
  to (10) or 1212.4(g)(1) to (10), as applicable, of the CFR, and the
  name and model of the lighter to which the test data relates.

## [Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832440 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Luxury lighters

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A manufacturer or an
importer of a luxury lighter must prepare and maintain documents that
identify the lighter and that show the following information, and must
keep those documents for a period of six years after the date of sale or
the date of importation:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a lighter manufactured in Canada, the
  value at the time of sale by the manufacturer; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an imported lighter, the value for
  duty as determined in accordance with sections 47 to 55 of the
  [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Paragraphs 5(b) and (c), subsection 6(2) and sections 7 to 11)]{.OriginatingRef}[Test Procedures for Lighters]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-832451 .scheduleLabel}
:::

### [General]{.HTitleText1} {#general .SchedHeadL1}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fuelling before testing

  **1** If a lighter is not fuelled at the point of sale, it must be
  fuelled in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions before
  testing in accordance with sections 5 to 9 and 11 and 12, using the
  fuel recommended by the manufacturer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lighter temperature before testing

  - **2** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The lighter must be maintained at a
    temperature of 23 ± 2°C for at least 10 hours immediately before
    testing in accordance with sections 5 to 7 and 9 to 12.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Area temperature during testing

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} The area in which tests are conducted must be
    maintained at a temperature of 23 ± 2°C during testing in accordance
    with sections 5 to 12.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flame

  **3** Testing in accordance with sections 5 to 9 and 11 must meet the
  following requirements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the testing must be conducted inside a draft-free
    chamber that is constructed from a suitable non-flammable material
    and, in the case of flame tests, must be conducted in subdued
    lighting conditions;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lighter must be positioned to produce a
    vertically upward flame; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the flame height must be measured to the nearest 5
    mm.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record of damage

  **4** During testing in accordance with sections 5 to 12, any damage
  sustained by a lighter or any abnormal or unsafe functioning of a
  lighter must be recorded.

### [Gas Lighters]{.HTitleText1} {#gas-lighters .SchedHeadL1}

#### [Flame Tests]{.HTitleText2} {#flame-tests .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flame height test

  - **5** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the following
    procedures are to be used in conducting a gas lighter flame height
    test:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} activate the lighter to produce a flame for a
      continuous five-second period;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the lighter has no shield, measure and record
      the maximum linear distance observed between the tip of the
      visible flame and the top of the fuel orifice;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the lighter has a shield, measure and record
      the maximum linear distance observed between the tip of the
      visible flame and the top of the shield or, if the shield is
      retractable, the top of the shield in its fully withdrawn
      position;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} take the normal action to extinguish the flame;
      and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} measure and record the duration of any burning
      that occurs immediately after the normal action to extinguish the
      flame has been taken.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum and minimum flame heights

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a lighter has a device to adjust the flame
    height, the procedures described in subsection (1) must be first
    carried out at the maximum flame height and then once more at the
    minimum flame height.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inversion test

  **6** The following procedures are to be used in conducting a gas
  lighter inversion test:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the lighter has a device to adjust the flame
    height, adjust it to produce a flame height of 50 mm;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} proceed to one of the following procedures:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a lighter other than a utility
      lighter, activate the lighter and turn it to an inverted hand-held
      position 45° below the horizontal for a continuous 10-second
      period, and then return it to the original vertical position, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a utility lighter, turn the
      lighter to an inverted hand-held position 45° below the horizontal
      and then activate it for a continuous 10-second period;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} take the normal action to extinguish the flame; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} measure and record the duration of any burning that
    occurs immediately after the normal action to extinguish the flame
    has been taken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extended operation test

  **7** The following procedures are to be used in conducting a gas
  lighter extended operation test:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the lighter has a device to adjust the flame
    height, adjust it to produce a flame height of 50 mm;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} proceed to one of the following procedures:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a lighter other than a utility
      lighter, activate the lighter to produce a vertical flame for a
      continuous two-minute period, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a utility lighter, turn the
      lighter to an inverted hand-held position 45° below the
      horizontal, activate it and let it burn for a continuous
      two-minute period;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} take the normal action to extinguish the flame; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} observe and record any ignition of the component
    parts of the lighter, expulsion of the valve mechanism or rupture of
    the fuel reservoir, with or without flame.

#### [Structural Integrity Tests]{.HTitleText2} {#structural-integrity-tests .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Drop test --- apparatus

  - **8** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The test apparatus required for a gas lighter
    drop test is a concrete slab with a minimum mass of 55 kg and
    dimensions of at least 60 cm x 60 cm x 6 cm.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Drop test --- procedures

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following procedures are to be used in
    conducting a gas lighter drop test:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the lighter has a device to adjust the flame
      height, adjust it to produce a flame height of 50 mm;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintain the lighter at a temperature of -10 ±
      2°C for at least 10 hours, and then maintain it at a temperature
      of 23 ± 2°C for at least 10 hours;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} immediately after the procedure described in
      paragraph (b), cause the lighter to fall three times onto the
      horizontally positioned concrete slab from a height of 1.5 m

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} first, from an upright vertical position,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} second, from an inverted vertical position,
        and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} third, from a horizontal position;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} inspect the lighter immediately after each fall
      for escape of fuel and record any damage; and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} measure and record any escape of fuel to the
      nearest milligram per minute.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temperature test --- apparatus

  - **9** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The test apparatus required for a gas lighter
    temperature test is an explosion-resistant oven that is capable of
    maintaining a temperature of 65 ± 2°C.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temperature test --- procedures

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following procedures are to be used in
    conducting a gas lighter temperature test:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the lighter has a device to adjust the flame
      height, adjust it to produce a flame height of 50 mm;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} place the lighter in the oven and maintain a
      temperature in the oven of 65 ± 2°C for four hours;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} remove the lighter from the oven and maintain it
      at a temperature of 23 ± 2°C for at least 10 hours; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} inspect the lighter and record any damage and any
      instance of total loss of fuel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pressure test --- apparatus

  - **10** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The test apparatus required for a gas
    lighter pressure test is a device that is capable of producing a
    gauge pressure of 2 MPa.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pressure test --- procedures

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following procedures are to be used in
    conducting a gas lighter pressure test:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} empty the lighter's fuel reservoir;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject the fuel reservoir to an internal
      pressure of 1.5 MPa, but do not permit the internal pressure to
      rise faster than 150 kPa/s; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} observe the lighter for 60 seconds and record any
      drop in internal pressure.

### [Wick Lighters]{.HTitleText1} {#wick-lighters .SchedHeadL1}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Burn test

  **11** The following procedures are to be used in conducting a wick
  lighter burn test:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} activate the lighter to produce a flame for a
    continuous 10-second period and then take the normal action to
    extinguish the flame;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reactivate the lighter and turn it to an inverted
    hand-held position 45° below the horizontal for a continuous
    10-second period, and then turn it to the original vertical
    position;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} take the normal action to extinguish the flame; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} observe and record any abnormal or unsafe burning
    characteristics.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Drop test --- apparatus

  - **12** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The test apparatus required for a wick
    lighter drop test is a concrete slab as described in subsection
    8(1).

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Drop test --- procedures

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following procedures are to be used in
    conducting a wick lighter drop test:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cause the lighter to fall three times onto the
      horizontally positioned concrete slab from a height of 1.5 m

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} first, from an upright vertical position,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} second, from an inverted vertical position,
        and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} third, from a horizontal position; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inspect the lighter after each fall and record
      any damage.
