## Order Transferring to Shared Services Canada the Control and Supervision of the Portion of the Federal Public Administration in the Acquisitions Branch of the Department of Public Works and Government Services known as the Information Technology Shared Services Procurement Directorate {#order-transferring-to-shared-services-canada-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-portion-of-the-federal-public-administration-in-the-acquisitions-branch-of-the-department-of-public-works-and-government-services-known-as-the-information-technology-shared-services-procurement-directorate .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2012-59
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2012-08-01

Order Transferring to Shared Services Canada the Control and Supervision
of the Portion of the Federal Public Administration in the Acquisitions
Branch of the Department of Public Works and Government Services known
as the Information Technology Shared Services Procurement Directorate

P.C. 2012-960 2012-06-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12091){#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12091-ID0EECBA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12092){#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12092-ID0EBCBA},
transfers to Shared Services Canada the control and supervision of the
portion of the federal public administration in the Acquisitions Branch
of the Department of Public Works and Government Services known as the
Information Technology Shared Services Procurement Directorate.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12091-ID0EECBA){#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12091}S.C.
  2003, c. 22, s. 207
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12092-ID0EBCBA){#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12092}R.S., c.
  P-34
  :::

This Order comes into force on the day on which section 711 of the
[Jobs, Growth and Long-term Prosperity Act](/eng/acts/J-0.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-510-E_hq_12583){#fn_81000-3-510-E_hq_12583-ID0EBBAA}
comes into force.

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-510-E_hq_12583-ID0EBBAA){#fn_81000-3-510-E_hq_12583}S.C.
