## Designating the Office of the Co-ordinator, Status of Women as a Department, the Minister of National Health and Welfare as Appropriate Minister and the Co-ordinator as Deputy Head {#designating-the-office-of-the-co-ordinator-status-of-women-as-a-department-the-minister-of-national-health-and-welfare-as-appropriate-minister-and-the-co-ordinator-as-deputy-head .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/76-44
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1976-04-14

Designating the Office of the Co-ordinator, Status of Women as a
Department, the Minister of National Health and Welfare as Appropriate
Minister and the Co-ordinator as Deputy Head

P.C. 1976-779 1976-04-01
::::

::::: section
:::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition
  [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to designate the Office of the
  Co-ordinator, Status of Women as a department for the purposes of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to the definition
  [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) of the [Public
  Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), to designate the Office of
  the Co-ordinator, Status of Women as a department for the purposes of
  the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to subsection 3(1.1)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnotea){#footnotea-ID0EDBCBA} of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to amend Schedule I.1
  to that Act by striking out the reference to the Minister of National
  Health and Welfare in column II thereof opposite the name of the
  Office of the Co-ordinator, Status of Women in column I thereof and by
  substituting for that reference a reference to Minister of
  Communications;

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnotea-ID0EDBCBA){#footnotea}S.C.
    1992, c. 1, s. 70(2)
    :::

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to the definition [[deputy
  head]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), to designate the Co-ordinator of
  the Office of the Co-ordinator, Status of Women as the deputy head for
  the purposes of the Public Sevice Employment Act.

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SI/95-113\]]{.Repealed}
