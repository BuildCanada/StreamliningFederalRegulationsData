## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1398833 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[ASTM F833]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ASTM F833]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the ASTM F833 standard
    entitled Standard Consumer Safety Performance Specification for
    Carriages and Strollers, published by ASTM International, as amended
    from time to time. ([norme ASTM F833]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[carriage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[carriage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a wheeled vehicle that is
    designed to transport a child in a reclined or horizontal position,
    and includes a wheeled vehicle that is designed to be converted to
    function as a carriage and has been so
    converted. ([landau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[good laboratory practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[good laboratory practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means practices
    that are in accordance with the principles set out in the
    Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development document
    entitled OECD Principles of Good Laboratory Practice, Number 1 of
    the OECD Series on Principles of Good Laboratory Practice and
    Compliance Monitoring, ENV/MC/CHEM(98)17, the English version of
    which is dated January 21, 1998 and the French version of which is
    dated March 6, 1998. ([bonnes pratiques de
    laboratoire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[good scientific practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[good scientific practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of test data, conditions and
      procedures that are in accordance with or equivalent to those set
      out in the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development
      document entitled OECD Guidelines for the Testing of Chemicals, as
      amended from time to time; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of laboratory practices, good
      laboratory practices. ([bonnes pratiques
      scientifiques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[ISO 31110]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ISO 31110]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the ISO 31110 standard
    entitled *Wheeled child conveyances --- Pushchairs and prams ---
    Requirements and test methods,* published by the International
    Organization for Standardization, as amended from time to
    time. ([norme ISO 31110]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[LC~50~]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[LC~50~]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the concentration of a
    substance in air that, when administered by means of inhalation over
    a specified length of time in an animal assay, is expected to cause
    the death of at least 50% of a defined population of
    animals. ([CL~50~]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[LD~50~]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[LD~50~]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the single dose of a substance
    that, when administered by a defined route in an animal assay, is
    expected to cause the death of at least 50% of a defined population
    of animals. ([DL~50~]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[stroller]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[stroller]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a wheeled vehicle that is
    designed to transport a child in a seated position and includes a
    wheeled vehicle that is designed to be converted to function as a
    stroller and has been so converted. ([poussette]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Technical Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1398846 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements --- standards

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Carriages and strollers must meet
  either

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the requirements set out in ASTM F833, except
    sections 5.3, 5.12, 8 and 9 of that standard, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the requirements set out in sections 6.8 and 6.10
    of ASTM F833 and those set out in ISO 31110, except sections 6, 7
    and 10 of ISO 31110.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a carriage or stroller that
  meets the requirements set out in either paragraph (1)(a) or (b) as
  the requirements read on the day before the day on which a new version
  of ASTM F833 or ISO 31110, as the case may be, is published may
  continue to meet those requirements for the following activities:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the manufacture or import of a
    carriage or stroller, for the period of 180 days that begins on the
    day on which the new version of the applicable standard is
    published; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the advertising or sale of a
    carriage or stroller, for the period of 365 days that begins on the
    day on which the new version of the applicable standard is
    published.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited substances

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A carriage or stroller must
not contain any of the following substances if the substance could,
under reasonably foreseeable circumstances, become accessible to a child
or, if the substance is used as a filling, could be released on breakage
or leakage:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} carbon tetrachloride or any substance that contains
  it;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any substance that contains more than 10 mg of methyl
  alcohol per gram;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any substance that contains more than 100 mg of
  petroleum distillates per gram;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} benzene;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any substance that contains more than 100 mg of
  turpentine per gram;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} boric acid or salts of boric acid;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} ethyl ether.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Toxic substances

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If a carriage or stroller
contains a toxic substance, at least one of the following requirements
must be met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the substance must not be capable of being ingested,
  inhaled or absorbed through the skin because of the nature, physical
  form, size or any other characteristic of the carriage or stroller;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total quantity of the substance for a child who
  has a body weight of 10 kg must not exceed the lesser of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 1% of the oral LD~50~ as determined in accordance
    with good scientific practices, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 1% of the dermal LD~50~ as determined in
    accordance with good scientific practices;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the toxicity of the substance must not exceed the
  limits set out in Schedule 1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specific substances in surface coatings

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  sticker, film or other similar material that can be removed, or a
  surface coating material, that is applied to an accessible part of a
  carriage or stroller must not contain, when it is tested in accordance
  with a method that conforms to good laboratory practices,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} more than 90 mg/kg total lead;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any compound of antimony, arsenic, cadmium,
    selenium or barium if more than 1000 mg/kg of the compound migrates
    from the material; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} more than 10 mg/kg total mercury.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[accessible
  part]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[surface coating
  material]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), [[accessible
  part]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[surface coating
  material]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} have the same meanings as in section
  1 of the [Surface Coating Materials
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-193).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Phthalates --- DEHP, DBP and BBP

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The vinyl in a carriage or
stroller must not contain more than 1000 mg/kg of di(2-ethylhexyl)
phthalate (DEHP), dibutyl phthalate (DBP) or benzyl butyl phthalate
(BBP) when tested in accordance with a method that conforms to good
laboratory practices.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Phthalates --- DINP, DIDP and DNOP

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  vinyl in any part of a carriage or stroller that can, in a reasonably
  foreseeable manner, be placed in the mouth of a child under four years
  of age must not contain more than 1000 mg/kg of diisononyl phthalate
  (DINP), diisodecyl phthalate (DIDP) or di-n-octyl phthalate (DNOP)
  when tested in accordance with a method that conforms to good
  laboratory practices.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), a part of a
  carriage or stroller can be placed in the mouth of a child under four
  years of age if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it can be brought to the child's mouth and kept
    there so that it can be sucked or chewed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one of its dimensions is less than 5 cm.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dimensions --- deflated state

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (2)(b), if a part of a
  carriage or stroller is inflatable, its dimensions must be determined
  in its deflated state.

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1398891 .Part}

### [Presentation of Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1398892 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Languages and legibility

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The information required by
these Regulations must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be displayed in both English and French; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be legible and clearly displayed, including being
  printed in characters whose colour contrasts sharply with the
  background.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Print

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  information required by these Regulations must be printed in a
  sans-serif type that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not compressed, expanded or decorative;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a large x-height relative to the ascender or
    descender of the type, as illustrated in Schedule 2; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has a minimum height of 2.5 mm.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Height of type

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The height of the type is determined by measuring an
  upper-case letter or a lower-case letter that has an ascender or a
  descender, such as "b" or "p".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signal words

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The signal words
"WARNING" and "MISE EN GARDE" must be displayed in upper-case type.

### [Required Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1398907 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to [Canada Consumer Product
Safety Act](/eng/acts/C-1.68) or these Regulations

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Information that appears
on a carriage or stroller, that accompanies a carriage or stroller or
that is in any advertisement for a carriage or stroller must not make
any direct or indirect reference to the [Canada Consumer Product Safety
Act](/eng/acts/C-1.68) or these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information --- general

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following information must be permanently printed on every carriage or
  stroller or on a label that is permanently affixed to it:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a carriage or stroller that is
    manufactured in Canada, the name and principal place of business of
    the manufacturer or the person for which the carriage or stroller
    was manufactured;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a carriage or stroller that is
    imported for commercial purposes, the name and principal place of
    business of the manufacturer, the person for whom the carriage or
    stroller was manufactured or the importer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the model name or model number of the carriage or
    stroller; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the year and month of manufacture of the carriage
    or stroller.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every carriage or stroller must display or be
  accompanied by the following information, with line drawings or
  photographs illustrating the sequence of steps if needed:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it is sold not fully assembled, how it is to be
    assembled;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is capable of being folded, how it is to be
    folded and unfolded;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer's recommended maximum weight of
    the child;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer's recommended use positions;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the operation of the braking device;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} how the child restraint system is to be used;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of carriages or strollers with an
    integral or added part that is intended to carry a load, the maximum
    permissible load;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the maintenance and cleaning instructions; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any other information for safe usage.

## [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1398973 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}180 days

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A carriage or stroller,
as those terms are defined in section 1 of the [Carriages and Strollers
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-379) as it reads as of the day on
which these Regulations come into force, may satisfy the requirements of
the [Carriages and Strollers Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-379),
as they read immediately before that day, for the period of 180 days
that begins on that day.

## [SCHEDULE 1]{.scheduleLabel}[(Paragraph 4(c))]{.OriginatingRef}[Permissible Limits of Toxicity]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-1398981 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- - **1** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a
    substance or stuffing material is toxic for humans in all of the
    following cases:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its acute oral LD~50~ value for rat is 5 g or
      less per kilogram of body weight;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its acute dermal LD~50~ value for rabbit is 2 g
      or less per kilogram of body weight;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its LC~50~ value for a one-hour exposure,
      determined using acute toxicity test results for rats, is 20,000
      ppm by volume of gas or vapour or less, or 200 mg/L by volume of
      mist or dust or less, if gas, vapour, mist or dust is likely to be
      encountered when the substance or stuffing material is used in a
      reasonably foreseeable manner.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} LD~50~ and LC~50~ values are to be determined in
    accordance with good scientific practices.
