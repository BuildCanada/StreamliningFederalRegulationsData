## Order Establishing the Text of a Resolution Providing for the Extension of the Application of Sections 83.28, 83.29 and 83.3 of the Criminal Code {#order-establishing-the-text-of-a-resolution-providing-for-the-extension-of-the-application-of-sections-83.28-83.29-and-83.3-of-the-criminal-code .Title-of-Act}

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 2007-02-06

Order Establishing the Text of a Resolution Providing for the Extension
of the Application of Sections 83.28, 83.29 and 83.3 of the Criminal
Code

P.C. 2007-140 2007-02-06
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 83.32(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_609189-E_hq_976){#fn_609189-E_hq_976-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), hereby makes the annexed [Order
Establishing the Text of a Resolution Providing for the Extension of the
Application of Sections 83.28, 83.29 and 83.3 of the Criminal
Code](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-25).

## [Resolution]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736892 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 83.32(2) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the text of
the resolution set out in the schedule is established.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736894 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.

::::: section
:::: {#736896 .Schedule}
::: {}
## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 1)]{.OriginatingRef}[Resolution by Both Houses of Parliament Extending the Application of Sections 83.28, 83.29 and 83.3 of the Criminal Code]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-736897 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- [1]{.lawlabel} That pursuant to subsection 83.32(1) of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the application of sections 83.28, 83.29 and
  83.3 of that Act be extended for a period of three years from the
  first day on which this resolution is passed by both Houses of
  Parliament.

- [2]{.lawlabel} That this Resolution come into force on the day on
  which it has been passed by both Houses of Parliament.
