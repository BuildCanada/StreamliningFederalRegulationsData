## Prince Edward Island Sex Offender Information Registration Regulations {#prince-edward-island-sex-offender-information-registration-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-307
:::

[SEX OFFENDER INFORMATION REGISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/S-8.7)

Registration 2004-12-13

Prince Edward Island Sex Offender Information Registration Regulations
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Lieutenant Governor in Council of Prince Edward Island, pursuant to
subsection 18(1) of the [Sex Offender Information Registration
Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBDA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Prince Edward Island Sex Offender Information Registration
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-307).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713536 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Sex Offender Information
    Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police. ([GRC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Report and Notification by Telephone]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713540 .Part}

- **[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  sex offender whose main residence is located in the Province of Prince
  Edward Island may make any report required under paragraph 4.1(1)(a)
  or (b) or section 4.3 of the Act, or provide any notification required
  under section 6 of the Act, by telephone.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsection (1) does not remove
  the requirement for the sex offender to report in person under
  paragraph 4.1(1)(c) of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-4, s. 1
:::

## [Persons Authorized to Collect Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713546 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
authorized in the Province of Prince Edward Island to collect
information for the purposes of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the RCMP; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a [[member of a police
  department]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in paragraph 1(n) of the
  Police Act, R.S.P.E.I. 1988, c P-11.1, as amended from time to time.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-4, s. 2
:::

## [Persons Authorized to Register Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713552 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
authorized in the Province of Prince Edward Island to register
information for the purposes of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the RCMP; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person employed by the RCMP whose duties include
  the registration of information collected under the Act.

## [Registration Centre]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713556 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The RCMP "L" Division
headquarters located at 450 University Avenue, Charlottetown, Queens
County, is designated as a registration centre in the Province of Prince
Edward Island, and its designated area of service is the entire
province.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-4, s. 3
