Whereas, pursuant to subsection 332(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EICBA} of the
[Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote

of the Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on October 30, 2004, a copy
of the proposed [New Substances Notification Regulations
(Organisms)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-248), substantially in the
annexed form, and persons were given an opportunity to file comments
with respect to the proposed Regulations or to file a notice of
objection requesting that a board of review be established and stating
the reasons for the objection;

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-718384 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Environmental
      Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian Biosafety Standards and Guidelines]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian Biosafety Standards and
      Guidelines]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the documents entitled
      Canadian Biosafety Standard, 2nd Edition, published in 2015, and
      the Canadian Biosafety Guideline: Containment Level 1: Physical
      Design and Operational Practices, published in 2017, both
      developed by the Public Health Agency of Canada and the Canadian
      Food Inspection Agency, as amended from time to time. ([Normes et
      lignes directrices canadiennes sur la
      biosécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[confinement procedures]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[confinement procedures]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any physical,
      chemical, biological or operational control, or combination of
      those controls, to restrict the exit or dispersal of a
      micro-organism. ([méthodes de confinement]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[contained facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[contained facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an enclosed
      building with walls, floor and ceiling, or an area within such a
      building, where the containment is in accordance with the physical
      and operational requirements of a level set out in either the
      Canadian Biosafety Standards and Guidelines or Appendix K of the
      NIH Guidelines. ([installation étanche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[ecozone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[ecozone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means one of the ecozones shown on
      the map entitled Terrestrial Ecozones and Ecoregions of Canada
      1995, dated May 6, 1999, Catalogue No. cas016e, whose boundaries
      are more particularly described in the National Soil Data Base
      (NSDB) of the Canada Soil Information System (CanSIS), developed
      by the Department of Agriculture and Agri-Food and the Department
      of the Environment, as amended from time to
      time. ([écozone]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[experimental field study]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[experimental field study]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a study of
      a research and development organism that is a micro-organism,
      which study uses the minimum area, consisting of one or more sites
      whose total area does not exceed one hundred hectares, and the
      minimum quantity of the micro-organism as are required to meet the
      objectives of the study. ([étude expérimentale sur le
      terrain]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[indigenous]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[indigenous]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
      micro-organism, means occurring naturally in the ecozone into
      which the micro-organism is intended to be
      introduced. ([indigène]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Laboratory Biosafety Guidelines]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Laboratory Biosafety
      Guidelines]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2018-11, s.
      33\]]{.Repealed}

  [[micro-organism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[micro-organism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a microscopic
      organism that is

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} classified in the Bacteria, the Archaea, the
        Protista, which includes protozoa and algae, or the Fungi, which
        includes yeasts;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a virus, virus-like particle or sub-viral
        particle;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a cultured cell of an organism not referred to
        in paragraph (a) or (b), other than a cell used to propagate the
        organism; or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any culture other than a pure
        culture. ([micro-organisme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[NIH Guidelines]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[NIH Guidelines]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Guidelines for
      Research Involving Recombinant DNA Molecules (NIH Guidelines) June
      1994, published in the Federal Register by the U.S. Department of
      Health and Human Services, 59 FR 34472 (July 5, 1994), as amended
      from time to time. ([NIH Guidelines]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[organism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[organism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a living organism as
      defined in section 104 of the Act, except in the following
      provisions:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} paragraph (c) of the definition
        [[micro-organism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm};

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} paragraphs 2(4)(b) and (c);

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 1(d) and subparagraphs 1(e)(vi)
        and (viii) of Schedule 5;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 2(d) of Schedule 5;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} subparagraph 3(d)(iii) and paragraph 3(f) of
        Schedule 5; and

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} items 5 to 7 in Schedule
        5. ([organisme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[research and development organism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[research and development organism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      organism that is undergoing systematic investigation or research,
      by means of experimentation or analysis other than test marketing,
      whose primary objective is any of the following:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to create or improve a product or process;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to determine the technical viability or
        performance characteristics of a product or process; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to evaluate the organism prior to its
        commercialization, by pilot plant trials, production trials,
        including scale-up, or customer plant trials so that technical
        specifications can be modified in response to the performance
        requirements of potential customers. ([destiné à la recherche et
        au développement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[test marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[test marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a product,
      means the exploration of its market capability in a competitive
      situation in which the creation or improvement of the product is
      not the primary objective. ([test de marché]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Government agencies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The definition of
  [[government]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 3(1) of the Act
  does not apply to the expression "government agencies" in these
  Regulations.

## [Scope]{.HTitleText1} {#h-718418 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Avoiding regulatory duplication

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  greater certainty, these Regulations do not apply in respect of an
  organism that is manufactured or imported for a use that is regulated
  under any other Act or regulations listed in Schedule 4 to the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of an
  organism that is loaded on a carrier outside Canada and moved through
  Canada to a location outside Canada, whether or not there is a change
  of carrier during transit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Research and development
  micro-organisms

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of a
  micro-organism that is a research and development organism not for
  introduction outside a contained facility, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the containment of the micro-organism is in
    accordance with the Canadian Biosafety Standards and Guidelines or
    Appendix K of the NIH Guidelines; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the micro-organism is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} imported to a contained facility in a quantity
      that, at the time of the import, is less than 50 mL or 50 g, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} manufactured in a contained facility under one
      of the following conditions:

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the micro-organism does not require containment
        level 2, 3 or 4 as identified in the Canadian Biosafety
        Standards and Guidelines and at any given moment there is less
        than 1 000 L of the micro-organism present at that facility, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the micro-organism requires containment level
        2, 3 or 4 as identified in the Canadian Biosafety Standards and
        Guidelines, its manufacture is a controlled activity authorized
        under a licence issued under section 18 of the [Human Pathogens
        and Toxins Act](/eng/acts/H-5.67) whose conditions of licence
        are complied with and at any given moment there is less than
        250 L of the micro-organism present at that facility.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Research and development organism other
  than a micro-organism

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of an
  organism, other than a micro-organism, that is a research and
  development organism and is imported to or manufactured in a facility
  from which there is no release into the environment of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the organism;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the genetic material of the organism; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} material from the organism involved in toxicity.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-11, s. 34
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agricultural research study ---
micro-organisms

- **[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 3(5), if the
  following conditions are met, these Regulations do not apply in
  respect of a micro-organism that is a research and development
  organism for introduction in an agricultural research study conducted
  on plants:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the study is conducted and supervised by an
    agronomist, a plant pathologist or a researcher who is trained in
    conducting agricultural research;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the micro-organism

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is not modified from its natural form and occurs
      naturally in, and has been isolated from, an ecozone that is the
      same as the one in which the study is located, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is identified at a taxonomic level that enables
      the persons referred to in paragraph (a) to

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} consider its overall characteristics and the
        potential hazards to the environment and human health that are
        associated with its release in the study, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the study is conducted as set out
        in paragraphs (c) to (e);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manufacture of the micro-organism for the
    purpose of the study employs laboratory and operation practices to
    minimize contamination of the formulation and minimize the
    unintentional release of the micro-organism from the manufacturing
    facility;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the study is conducted in a manner that follows
    field and sanitation practices to minimize the likelihood of the
    micro-organism's dispersal outside the study area where it may be a
    hazard to the environment and human health; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer has operational procedures in
    place to ensure that physical possession or control of the
    micro-organism is transferred only to individuals who are made aware
    of the conditions of this subsection and the manufacturer has reason
    to believe that they are capable of meeting those conditions when
    conducting the study;

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agricultural research study

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), [[agricultural
  research study]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an experimental field study
  that is conducted

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on land that is cultivated with annual agricultural
    crops, such as corn and soybeans;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on land that is cultivated with perennials or woody
    plants that do not have to be replanted for several years such as
    asparagus, grapevines, fruit trees, Christmas trees, ornamental
    trees and shrubs destined for nurseries and seedling trees destined
    for replanting in reforestation, conservation and restoration
    projects, excluding lands for forestry uses;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on land that is used for five or more consecutive
    years to grow herbaceous forage crops that are either cultivated or
    growing wild; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} inside a greenhouse that is not a contained
    facility.

## [Micro-organisms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-718459 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information: Schedule 1

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) to (6), a person who manufactures or imports a
  micro-organism must provide the information specified in Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions: Information in Schedule 1

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who manufactures or imports a micro-organism
  for introduction

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} into an ecozone where it is not indigenous is not
    required to provide the information specified in paragraph 5(a) of
    Schedule 1, but must, in a separate notification for each ecozone,
    provide the other information specified in that Schedule as well as
    the identification of the ecozone of intended introduction and the
    data from tests conducted to determine the effects of the
    micro-organism on plant, invertebrate and vertebrate species likely
    to be exposed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with confinement procedures is not
    required to provide the information specified in paragraphs 5(a) and
    6(c) and (d) of Schedule 1, but must provide the other information
    specified in that Schedule and a description of those confinement
    procedures and their effectiveness in restricting the exit or
    dispersal of the micro-organism from the locations of introduction;
    or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} into an ecozone to which the micro-organism is
    indigenous is not required to provide the information specified in
    subparagraphs 1(f)(i), (iii) and (iv) and paragraphs 1(i) and 5(a)
    of Schedule 1, but must, in a separate notification for each
    ecozone, provide the other information specified in that Schedule as
    well as the identification of the ecozone of intended introduction
    and the data that demonstrates that the micro-organism is indigenous
    to that ecozone.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Introduction within 10 km of ecozone
  boundary

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who manufactures or imports a micro-organism
  for introduction into an ecozone at a point within 10 km of the
  boundary of an ecozone referred to in paragraph (2)(a) or (c), as the
  case may be, may elect to have the introduction of the micro-organism
  considered to be in that ecozone and not into the actual ecozone of
  introduction, in which case the person must provide a notice in
  writing of the election and the information required by that
  paragraph.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information: Schedule 2

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person who manufactures in a contained facility or
  imports to a contained facility a micro-organism that is not for
  introduction outside the contained facility, or is for export only, is
  not required to provide the information specified in Schedule 1, but
  must provide the information specified in Schedule 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information: Schedule 3

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person who manufactures or imports a micro-organism
  for introduction in an experimental field study is not required to
  provide the information specified in Schedule 1, but must provide the
  information specified in Schedule 3.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information: Schedule 4

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A person who manufactures a micro-organism at the
  site from which it was isolated, for introduction into the same site,
  is not required to provide the information specified in Schedule 1,
  but must provide the information specified in Schedule 4.

## [Organisms Other than Micro-organisms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-718476 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information: Schedule 5

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A person who manufactures
or imports an organism other than a micro-organism must provide the
information specified in Schedule 5.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Addition to Domestic Substances List ---
information

**[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 112(1)(b) of the Act, a person who provides the information
referred to in sections 3 and 4 must also provide --- within 30 days
after the day on which they manufactured or imported the organism --- a
notice confirming that manufacture or importation.

## [Information Provision Time Limits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-718483 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limits

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The information required
under sections 3 and 4 must be provided as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the information required under

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} subsection 3(1), at least 120 days before the day
    on which the person manufactures or imports the micro-organism, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} subsections 3(2) or (3), at least 120 days before
    the day on which the person manufactures or imports the
    micro-organism under conditions that trigger the requirement to
    provide the information;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the information required under subsection 3(4) or
  (6), at least 30 days before the day on which the person manufactures
  or imports the micro-organism under conditions that trigger the
  requirement to provide the information;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the information required under subsection 3(5),
  at least 90 days before the day on which the person manufactures or
  imports the micro-organism under conditions that trigger the
  requirement to provide the information; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for the information required under section 4, at
  least 120 days before the day on which the person manufactures or
  imports the organism.

## [Assessment Periods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-718492 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limits

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 108(1) of the Act, the periods within which the Ministers
must assess the information provided under sections 3 and 4 are as
follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 120 days after receiving the information referred to
  in Schedule 1 or the other information required under subsection 3(2);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 30 days after receiving the information referred to
  in Schedule 2 or 4;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} 90 days after receiving the information referred to
  in Schedule 3; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} 120 days after receiving the information referred to
  in Schedule 5.

## [Retention of Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-718499 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Five years

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A person who is required to
provide information to the Minister under these Regulations must keep a
copy of that information and any supporting data at the person's
principal place of business in Canada or at the principal place of
business in Canada of a representative of that person. The information
and the supporting data must be kept for a period of five years after
the year in which the information is provided.

## [Administrative Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-718502 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information and certification

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  information to be provided to the Minister under these Regulations
  must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, civic and postal addresses and telephone
    number, as well as the fax number and e-mail address, if any, of the
    manufacturer or importer of the organism;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, title, civic and postal addresses and

    any, of the person authorized to act on behalf of the manufacturer
    or importer of the organism, if any;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name, civic and postal addresses and telephone
    number, as well as the fax number and e-mail address, if any, of the
    head of the quality assurance unit of every laboratory that
    developed test data included in the information;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an indication of whether the organism will be
    manufactured in or imported into Canada and the civic address of the
    site of manufacture in Canada of the organism or, if known, the port
    of entry into Canada of the organism, as the case may be; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a certification that the information is accurate
    and complete, dated and signed by the manufacturer or importer if
    they are resident in Canada or, if not, the person authorized to act
    on their behalf.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recipient

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Two copies of any information provided under these
  Regulations must be sent in English or French to the Minister, care of
  the Substances Management Coordinator, Department of the Environment,
  Ottawa, Ontario K1A 0H3.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agent

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person that provides the information under these
  Regulations is not resident in Canada, they must identify, under
  paragraph (1)(b), a person resident in Canada that is authorized to
  act on their behalf to whom any notice or correspondence may be sent
  and that is required to keep the information and any supporting data
  under section 7.

## [SCHEDULE 1]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsections 3(1) and (2) and paragraph 6(a))]{.OriginatingRef}[Information Required in Respect of Micro-organisms]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-718521 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The following information in respect of the micro-organism:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its identification and the information
    substantiating its identification;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its common and superseded names and any synonyms;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its strain history;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of any modifications to the
    micro-organism, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the modifications,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the methods and steps taken to make the
      modifications,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the phenotypic and genotypic changes that
      resulted from the steps referred to in subparagraph (ii),

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the stability of the changes referred to in
      subparagraph (iii), and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the nature, source and function of any inserted
      genetic material;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the methods that can be used to
    distinguish and detect the micro-organism;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the biological and ecological
    characteristics of the micro-organism, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its life cycle,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} its infectivity, pathogenicity to non-human
      species, toxicity and toxigenicity,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} its resistance to antibiotics and tolerance to
      metals and pesticides,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} its involvement in biogeochemical cycling,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the conditions required for, and conditions that
      limit, its survival, growth and replication, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the mechanisms of its dispersal and the modes of
      interaction with any dispersal agents;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of the mode of action in relation to
    the intended use;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the identification of any patent or any application
    for a patent, as the case may be;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the dispersal by gene transfer of traits of
    pathogenicity to non-human species, toxigenicity and resistance to
    antibiotics, including a description of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the genetic basis for pathogenicity to non-human
      species, toxigenicity and resistance to antibiotics,

  health effects, and the most likely route of human exposure to the
  organism.

- **7** All other information and test data in respect of the organism
  that permit the identification of hazards to the environment and human
  health and that are in the person's possession or to which the person
  may reasonably be expected to have access.

- **8** The identification of other government agencies, either outside
  or within Canada, that the person has notified of the manufacture or
  importation of the organism, and the purpose of that notification.

- **9** A description or specification of the test procedures followed
  in developing the test data, including the test methods, reference
  substances and quality control and quality assurance procedures.
