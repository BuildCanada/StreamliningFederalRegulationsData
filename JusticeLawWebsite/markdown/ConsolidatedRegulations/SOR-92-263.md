## Security Certificate Transfer Fee (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations {#security-certificate-transfer-fee-cooperative-credit-associations-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-263
:::

[COOPERATIVE CREDIT ASSOCIATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/C-41.01)

Registration 1992-05-07

Regulations Prescribing the Maximum Fee that an Assocition May Charge
for a Security Certificate Issued in Respect of a Transfer

P.C. 1992-912 1992-05-07
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 92(2) and section 463 of
the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed Regulations prescribing the maximum fee that an
association may charge for a security certificate issued in respect of a
transfer, effective June 1, 1992.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1991, c. 48
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-942191 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Security Certificate Transfer Fee (Cooperative Credit
Associations) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-263).

## [Maximum Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-942193 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** An association may charge a
fee not exceeding five dollars for a security certificate issued in
respect of a transfer.
