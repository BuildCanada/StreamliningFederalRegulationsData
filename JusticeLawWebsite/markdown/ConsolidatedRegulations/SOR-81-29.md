## Payments in Lieu of Taxes Regulations {#payments-in-lieu-of-taxes-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-29
:::

[PAYMENTS IN LIEU OF TAXES ACT](/eng/acts/M-13)

Registration 1980-12-22

Payments in Lieu of Taxes Regulations

P.C. 1980-3501 1980-12-18
::::

:::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas a copy of the proposed regulation was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on December 5, 1980 and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations with respect thereto.

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Public Works, pursuant to subsection
8(1) of the Municipal Grants Act, 1980, is pleased hereby to make the
annexed Regulations respecting Grants in lieu of a real property tax or
a frontage or area tax made by the Minister to a taxing authority.
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** \[Repealed, SOR/2001-494,
s. 2\]

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870636 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Payments in Lieu of Taxes
    Act](/eng/acts/M-13); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[international organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    organization as defined in section 3 of the Privileges and
    Immunities (International Organizations) Act; ([organisation
    internationale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[visiting force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[visiting force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a visiting force as
    defined in the [Visiting Forces Act](/eng/acts/V-2). ([force
    étrangère présente au Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-494, s. 3
:::

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph (b) of the definition [[other attribute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
in subsection 2(1) of the Act, the following criteria apply in relation
to a tax levy for the purpose of financing all or part of the operating
cost of a service to real property or immovables:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the actual or estimated cost of constructing a new
  building;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of rooms, living units or beds in a
  building;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number of persons in occupancy of a building; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any lot, parcel, improvement or any property
  component or feature that would be established by an assessment
  authority in respect of federal property as the basis for computing
  the amount of any frontage or area tax that would be applicable to
  that property and payable by the owners of that property if it were
  taxable property.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-20, s. 1
- SOR/2001-494, s. 4
:::

## [Real Property and Immovables Leased to or Occupied by Non-departmental Bodies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870650 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2001-494, s. 5

\]
:::

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following classes of real property and immovables owned by Her Majesty
  in right of Canada and leased to or occupied by a person or a body,
  whether incorporated or not, that is not a department, are to be
  included in the definition [[federal property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
  subsection 2(1) of the Act, for the purposes of the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any real property or immovable that is leased to or
    occupied by an employee of Her Majesty or a member of the Canadian
    Forces and used by that person as a domestic establishment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any real property or immovable that is under the
    management, charge and direction of a minister of the Crown and that
    is occupied by a corporation included in Schedule III or IV to the
    Act and in respect of which the corporation is exempt from real
    property tax;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any real property that is leased to or occupied by
    any person or body where that property forms part of the real
    property acquired by Her Majesty as a site for the proposed
    Pickering Airport;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any real property or immovable that, immediately
    prior to its acquisition by Her Majesty, was leased to or occupied
    by a person or body from whom, by reason of that person's or body's
    interest in or occupation of the property or immovable, a taxing
    authority may collect a real property tax or a frontage or area tax,
    so long as the term of the lease or occupation provided for at the
    time of the acquisition continues;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} notwithstanding paragraph (d), any real property or
    immovable that, immediately prior to its acquisition by Her Majesty,
    was specially assessed for a frontage or area tax in respect of the
    cost of a local improvement, without reference to the term of the
    lease or occupation in existence at the time of the acquisition, if
    any;

  - [(e.1)]{.lawlabel} any real property or immovable where the term of
    the lease or occupation, together with any right of renewal or
    extension, is for a period of one year or less;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any real property or immovable that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is held by Her Majesty for the purpose of
      providing occupancy to or permitting use by a person who is an
      Indian as defined in the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) or an Inuit
      and from whom, by reason of his interest in or occupation of that
      real property, a taxing authority may collect a real property tax,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} when leased to or occupied by an Indian or Inuit
      referred to in subparagraph (i), is used primarily by that person
      for residential or farming purposes, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} does not form part of an Indian Reserve;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any real property or immovable in Canada leased to
    or occupied by a member of a visiting force and used for residential
    or military purposes;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any real property or immovable in Canada leased to
    or occupied by a government other than the Government of Canada or a
    province or the head of a diplomatic mission of such a government
    and used as

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the chancery or offices of a diplomatic mission,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the official residence of the head of a
      diplomatic mission,

    of that government;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any real property or immovable in Canada leased to
    or occupied by a government other than the Government of Canada or a
    province and used as

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the chancery or offices of a consular post or
      trade commission, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the official residence of the head of a consular
      post or trade commission,

    of that government;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} any real property or immovable in Canada leased to
    or occupied by an international organization and used as

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the chancery or offices, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the official residence of the head of a mission,

    of that international organization;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} real property situated within the City of Ottawa
    and leased to or occupied by the National Arts Centre Corporation;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} any real property or immovable in Canada that is
    leased or occupied for residential purposes and that is the subject
    of an agreement under section 12 of the Act; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} any real property or immovable owned by Her Majesty
    and leased to a designated airport authority within the meaning of
    the [Airport Transfer (Miscellaneous Matters)
    Act](/eng/acts/A-10.4),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} which is not sublet to or occupied by any person
      other than the designated airport authority or a receiver-manager
      in possession of the assets of the designated airport authority,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} which is sublet to or occupied by Her Majesty.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Real property and immovables owned by Her Majesty in
  right of Canada that, by virtue of a lease to a corporation included
  in Schedule III or IV to the Act, are under the management, charge and
  direction of the corporation but are occupied by a department are to
  be included in the definition [[federal property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  in subsection 2(1) of the Act for the purposes of the Act.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2001-494, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

## [Additions to Schedule I to the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870687 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Schedule I to the Act is
amended by adding thereto, in alphabetical order within the Schedule,
the following:

"Canada Museums Construction Corporation Inc., with respect to the real
property that consists of buildings owned by Her Majesty in right of
Canada in which the National Gallery of Canada in the City of Ottawa and
the Canadian Museum of Civilization in the City of Hull are contained.

Cape Breton Development Corporation, with respect to real property owned
by Her Majesty in right of Canada and occupied by the Department of
Transport for purposes of the Canadian Coast Guard College.

National Battlefields Commission, with respect to real property owned by
Her Majesty in right of Canada the administration and control of which
is held by the Commission, whether title to the said real property is
vested in the name of Her Majesty or in the name of the Commission.

National Capital Commission, with respect to the real property that
consists of lands owned by Her Majesty in right of Canada on which the
National Gallery of Canada in the City of Ottawa and the Canadian Museum
of Civilization in the City of Hull are situated.

Queens Quay West Land Corporation, with respect to real property owned
by Her Majesty in right of Canada, the administration and control of
which is held by the Corporation, whether title to the said real
property is vested in the name of Her Majesty or in the name of the
Corporation."
