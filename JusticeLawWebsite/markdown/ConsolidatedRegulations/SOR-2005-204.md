## Gros Morne National Park of Canada Timber Harvesting Regulations {#gros-morne-national-park-of-canada-timber-harvesting-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2005-204
:::

[CANADA NATIONAL PARKS ACT](/eng/acts/N-14.01)

Registration 2005-06-28

Gros Morne National Park of Canada Timber Harvesting Regulations

P.C. 2005-1270 2005-06-28
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to section 17 of the [Canada
National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Gros Morne National Park of Canada Timber Harvesting
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-204).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717186 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada National Parks
    Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[authorized person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorized person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person specified
    in a timber harvesting permit in accordance with paragraph 9(1)(b).
    ([personne autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Chief Executive Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chief Executive Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in subsection 2(1) of the Parks Canada Agency Act. ([directeur
    général]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a son or daughter of a person or
    an individual adopted in fact by a person.
    ([enfant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[community]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[community]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following
    communities in the Province of Newfoundland and Labrador, namely,
    Birchy Head, Cow Head, Curzon Village, Glenburnie, Neddy Harbour,
    Norris Point, Rocky Harbour, St. Pauls, Sally\'s Cove, Shoal Brook,
    Trout River, Winter House Brook and Woody Point.
    ([collectivité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area or areas of
    land specified in a timber harvesting permit in accordance with
    subparagraph 9(1)(f)(iii). ([zone désignée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[harvest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[harvest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any or all of the
    following, namely, the cutting, sawing, chopping, splitting and
    de-limbing of timber in the park. ([récolte]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[harvesting season]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[harvesting season]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning
    on October 15 of one year and ending on April 30 of the following
    year. ([saison de récolte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Gros Morne National Park of
    Canada. ([parc]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[possession]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[possession]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 4(3) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).
    ([possession]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[special timber]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[special timber]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means timber that is
    especially suited for the construction of boats. ([bois
    spécial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the superintendent of
    the park. ([directeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[temporary shelter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[temporary shelter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a structure that is
    constructed in such a manner and with such materials that it may
    readily be dismantled and removed, the sole purpose of which is to
    provide daytime temporary shelter from the elements during
    harvesting. ([abri temporaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[timber]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[timber]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all trees --- living, dying,
    dead, standing or fallen --- that have grown in the park, and
    includes parts of those trees. ([bois]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[timber harvesting permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[timber harvesting permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit
    issued under section 8. ([permis de récolte de
    bois]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[waterbody]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[waterbody]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any permanent pond, lake or
    other body of standing water that is represented on a National
    Topographic Series (NTS) 1:50,000 map. ([étendue
    d\'eau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[watercourse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[watercourse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any permanent river,
    stream, brook, creek or other flowing water that is represented on a
    National Topographic Series (NTS) 1:50,000 map. ([cours
    d\'eau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717205 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to Gros Morne National Park of Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of an inconsistency between these
  Regulations and any other regulations made under the Act, these
  Regulations prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Sections 5 and 11 do not
apply to the superintendent, a park warden, an enforcement officer or a
peace officer while they are performing functions related to the
management of timber harvesting or to any other person authorized by the
superintendent while they are conducting scientific research in the park
in respect of the timber or the harvesting activity.

## [Traditional Renewable Resource Harvesting Activity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717210 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Harvesting of timber is
hereby specified to be a traditional renewable resource harvesting
activity.

## [General Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717212 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall harvest timber or be in possession of harvested timber
  unless the person is the holder of a timber harvesting permit or is an
  authorized person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall harvest white pine ([Pinus
  strobus]{.otherLang lang="la"}), yellow birch ([Betula
  alleghaniensis]{.otherLang lang="la"}), black ash ([Fraxinus
  nigra]{.otherLang lang="la"}) or red maple ([Acer rubrum]{.otherLang
  lang="la"}).

## [Timber Harvesting Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717216 .Part}

### [Purposes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717217 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Timber harvested under a
timber harvesting permit may be used for one or more of the following
purposes:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a permit to harvest timber other than
  special timber, for

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} heating, repairing or maintaining the dwelling of
    the permit holder,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} constructing a dwelling, barn, garage or shed
    appurtenant to an existing dwelling of the permit holder, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} constructing a shed to be used for the commercial
    fishing operations of the permit holder; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a permit to harvest special timber,
  for commercial boat building in Rocky Harbour.

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717224 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An application for a timber
harvesting permit shall be submitted to the superintendent on the form
provided by the superintendent and must include the name of the
applicant, the name of any person whom the applicant intends to
authorize to harvest the timber with them or on their behalf, the
purpose for which the timber to be harvested under the permit will be
used, and

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an application for a permit to harvest
  timber other than special timber, written evidence that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is a permanent resident of a
    community and was, on or before August 13, 1973, at least 19 years
    of age and a permanent resident of a community or of the lands that
    constitute the park, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is the child of a person described
    in subparagraph (i) and a permanent resident of a community; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an application for a permit to harvest
  special timber, written evidence that the applicant is a permanent
  resident of a community and that, on August 13, 1973, the applicant
  was

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at least 19 years of age and a permanent resident
    of a community or of the lands that constitute the park, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a commercial boat builder in Rocky Harbour.

### [Issuance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717232 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to any other provisions of these Regulations and any determinations
  made under sections 17 to 19, the superintendent shall issue a timber
  harvesting permit to an applicant who submits an application in
  accordance with section 7.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In determining whether to issue a timber harvesting
  permit and the conditions to be set out in the permit, the
  superintendent shall have regard to the protection of timber and the
  preservation, control and management of the park generally, including
  the maintenance or restoration of its ecological integrity.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person is entitled to obtain more than one timber
  harvesting permit

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at any time, for a purpose set out in subparagraph
    6(a)(i); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during their lifetime, for a purpose set out in
    subparagraph 6(a)(ii) or (iii).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person is entitled to obtain a timber harvesting
  permit if they are already the holder of a timber harvesting permit
  that has been suspended or if, during the 12 months preceding the
  submission of the application,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a timber harvesting permit issued to them was
    revoked; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the authorized person identified in their permit
    application contravened a condition of a timber harvesting permit or
    of these Regulations resulting in the revocation of a timber
    harvesting permit.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The permit holder shall sign the timber harvesting
  permit.

### [Contents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717245 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  timber harvesting permit shall contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the permit holder;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the authorized person, if any;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement that transfer or assignment of the
    permit is prohibited;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the duties of the permit holder
    and the authorized person, if any, as set out in sections 12 to 16;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement that contravention of a condition of
    the permit by the permit holder or the authorized person, if any,
    constitutes an offence under subsection 24(3) of the Act; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any standard conditions that are specified by the
    superintendent, in accordance with sections 17 to 19, in respect of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the duration of the permit,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the purpose or purposes for which the timber is
      to be harvested,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the designated areas in the park in which the
      timber may be harvested,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the quantity and species of timber that may be
      harvested,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the minimum diameter of timber that may be
      harvested,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2009-322, s. 25\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the harvesting methods and types of equipment
      that may be used in the harvesting,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the date when, and location where, harvested
      timber shall be stacked for purposes of scaling and inspection,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the method to be used to mark and stack
      harvested timber, and

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} the number, location and type of temporary
      shelters that may be constructed in any designated area.

    - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2009-322, s. 25\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may, when necessary for the
  protection of timber and the preservation, control and management of
  the park, including the maintenance or restoration of its ecological
  integrity, include in timber harvesting permits to be issued in any
  harvesting season any supplementary conditions that may be required.

### [Expiry]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717268 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A timber harvesting
permit expires on the earliest of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the expiry date stated on the permit,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the permit is revoked, and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the permit holder ceases to be a
  permanent resident of a community.

## [Prohibitions --- Holders of Timber Harvesting Permits and Authorized Persons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717273 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** No holder of a timber
harvesting permit or authorized person, if any, shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} transfer or assign the permit;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} harvest timber or, after May 15 and before October 15
  in any year, leave harvested timber within 30 m of the high-water mark
  of the shoreline of a watercourse;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} harvest timber or, after May 15 and before October 15
  in any year, leave harvested timber within 100 m of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the centre of a road,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the high-water mark of the shoreline of a
    waterbody, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a public structure, facility or trail;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} construct

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an access route for the purpose of removing
    harvested timber except in a designated area, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a building or other structure other than a
    temporary shelter;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} allow harvested timber to enter a waterbody or
  watercourse;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} transport harvested timber using any means other than
  a horse or a snowmobile;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} during harvesting or along access routes to and from
  a designated area, damage

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} young timber, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} white pine ([Pinus strobus]{.otherLang
    lang="la"}), yellow birch ([Betula alleghaniensis]{.otherLang
    lang="la"}), black ash ([Fraxinus nigra]{.otherLang lang="la"}) or
    red maple ([Acer rubrum]{.otherLang lang="la"});

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} occupy a temporary shelter at night;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} leave, in a designated area after the expiry of the
  permit, stumps that exceed 15 cm in height;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} leave timber having a diameter of 8 cm or more that
  they cut during a harvesting season

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in a designated area after May 3 following the
    harvesting season, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} anywhere else in the park after May 15 following
    that harvesting season; or

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} use harvested timber for any purpose other than that
  for which it was harvested as set out in the permit.

## [Duties of Permit Holder and Authorized Person]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717295 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder of a timber harvesting permit and the authorized person, if
  any, shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} carry the permit or a copy of the permit at all
    times when engaged in harvesting; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when requested to do so by the superintendent, a
    park warden, a peace officer or an enforcement officer, immediately
    produce the permit or a copy of the permit for examination.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The permit holder shall notify the superintendent in
  writing, as soon as possible, of any change in the information
  provided as part of the application for the permit.

<!-- -->

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  timber harvested under a timber harvesting permit remains the property
  of Her Majesty in right of Canada until it is scaled and inspected by
  the superintendent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The permit holder shall have the timber that was
  harvested under their timber harvesting permit scaled and inspected by
  the superintendent, at the stacking location specified in the permit,
  no later than May 15 after the harvesting season in which the timber
  was harvested.

<!-- -->

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent shall remove and dispose of any timber that has been
  left in the park by the holder of a timber harvesting permit or the
  authorized person, if any, in contravention of these Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The permit holder shall be responsible for all
  expenses incurred under subsection (1).

<!-- -->

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder of a timber harvesting permit or the authorized person, as the
  case may be, shall, before the expiry of the permit, remove from the
  park any temporary shelters that they erected and all equipment that
  they brought into the park.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall remove and dispose of any
  temporary shelter or equipment that has been left in the park in
  contravention of subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The permit holder shall be responsible for all
  expenses incurred under subsection (2).

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The holder of a timber
harvesting permit shall submit to the superintendent, within 30 days
after the expiry of the permit, a written report setting out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of timber harvested under the permit
  from each designated area;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the purpose for which the timber has been or will be
  used; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other information that must be recorded under a
  condition of the permit.

## [Powers of Superintendent]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717315 .Part}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent shall, taking into account the necessity of protecting
  timber and of preserving, conserving and managing the park generally,
  including the maintenance or restoration of its ecological integrity,
  determine in respect of each harvesting season those areas in the park
  in which harvesting will be permitted or restricted.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall post notices containing a
  description of the areas and their status under subsection (1) at park
  warden offices and information centres in the park.

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent may,
at any time, for the purposes of park management, public safety or the
conservation of natural resources, close areas of the park to
harvesting.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Based on the
determinations made under sections 17 and 18, the superintendent shall,
in respect of each harvesting season, determine the maximum quantity of
any type of timber that may be harvested under timber harvesting permits
in any area of the park.

## [Suspension and Revocation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717321 .Part}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may suspend a timber harvesting permit if the permit
  holder or authorized person, if any, contravenes any provision of
  these Regulations or any condition of the permit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall reinstate a suspended timber
  harvesting permit

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the contravention that led to the suspension has
    been corrected;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} after the expiry of a period of 30 days following
    the day on which the permit was suspended, unless proceedings have
    been instituted before the end of that period in respect of the
    alleged contravention; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if proceedings are instituted, after the person
    charged --- the permit holder or the authorized person --- has been
    found not guilty of an offence in respect of the alleged
    contravention or after the proceedings against them have been
    discontinued.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall revoke a timber harvesting
  permit if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the permit holder or authorized person, if any, is
    convicted of an offence in respect of a contravention of any
    provision of these Regulations or any condition of the permit; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the permit has been suspended twice during the
    period for which it was issued and was not reinstated under
    paragraph (2)(b) or (c).

## [Decisions of Superintendent]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717331 .Part}

### [Notice]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717332 .Subheading}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** If the superintendent
refuses to issue, or suspends or revokes, a timber harvesting permit,
the superintendent shall, as soon as possible after making their
decision, provide written notice of their decision, including reasons,
to the applicant or permit holder, as applicable.

### [Review]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717334 .Subheading}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person to whom the superintendent has refused to issue a timber
  harvesting permit, or whose timber harvesting permit has been
  suspended or revoked by the superintendent, may request a review of
  the superintendent\'s decision by submitting a request in writing to
  the Chief Executive Officer within 30 days after receipt by that
  person of the notice referred to in section 21.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a written request for review, the Chief
  Executive Officer shall require that the superintendent issue or
  reinstate the timber harvesting permit if the Chief Executive Officer
  reaches a decision that differs from the superintendent\'s decision
  with respect

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a refusal, to the requirements and
    matters to be considered under sections 7 and 8; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a suspension or revocation, to the
    reasons set out in section 20.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Executive Officer shall provide written
  notice of their decision, including reasons, to the person who
  requested the review.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717341 .Part}

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on October 1, 2005.
