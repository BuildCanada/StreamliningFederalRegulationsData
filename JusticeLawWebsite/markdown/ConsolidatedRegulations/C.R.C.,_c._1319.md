## UNESCO Remission Order {#unesco-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1319
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Order for the Remission of Certain Duties and Taxes on Goods Imported or
Purchased by UNESCO
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503422 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [UNESCO Remission Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1319).

## [Free Entry, Refund or Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503424 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Authority is hereby
granted, effective January 22, 1948, for free entry, refund or remission
of the following customs duties and the consumption or sales tax imposed
under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and the [Excise
Act](/eng/acts/E-14), respectively, on printed matter and official
publications and articles and materials used in the production thereof,
when imported into Canada or purchased in Canada, either for sale or
free distribution by the United Nations Educational, Scientific and
Cultural Organization, or its duly authorized agents, subject to
compliance with the conditions and procedures set out in the schedule.
