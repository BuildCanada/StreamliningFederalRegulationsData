## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1456248 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Food and Drugs
      Act](/eng/acts/F-27). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[active ingredient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[active ingredient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a component of a
      biocide that is directly responsible for any of the biocide's
      intended effects. ([ingrédient actif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[biocide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[biocide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a drug that is manufactured,
      sold or represented for use in destroying or inactivating
      micro-organisms, or in reducing or controlling their number, on a
      non-living and non-liquid surface, but it does not include

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} such a drug that is manufactured, sold or
        represented for use exclusively on the surface of food;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} such a drug that is manufactured, sold or
        represented for use on the surface of a contact lens that is a
        [[medical device]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section
        1 of the [Medical Devices
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282); or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} such a drug that

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is manufactured, sold or represented for use
          on the surface of

          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} an [[invasive
            device]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 1 of
            the [Medical Devices
            Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282), or

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a [[medical device]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
            as defined in section 1 of the [Medical Devices
            Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282), that is not an
            [[invasive device]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
            that section, but is intended to channel or store gases,
            liquids, tissues or body fluids, for the purpose of being
            introduced into the body by infusion or other means of
            administration, and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is capable of destroying or irreversibly
          inactivating either

          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} all types of pathogenic micro-organisms,
            but not necessarily large numbers of pathogenic bacterial
            spores, or

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} all types of
            micro-organisms. ([biocide]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[CAS registry number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[CAS registry number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      identification number that is assigned to a chemical substance by
      the Chemical Abstracts Service division of the American Chemical
      Society. ([numéro d'enregistrement CAS]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[conditions of use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[conditions of use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
      biocide, means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its intended uses or purposes;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the settings in which it is intended to be
        used;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its risk information;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} its directions for use; and

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} its directions for storage. ([conditions
        d'utilisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[contact information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[contact information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes civic
      address, except in paragraphs 32(1)(e), 49(2)(d) and
      50(1)(a). ([coordonnées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[expiry date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[expiry date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a biocide,
      means the year and month in which its shelf life ends. ([date
      limite d'utilisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[foreign regulatory authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[foreign regulatory authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      government agency or other entity outside Canada that controls the
      manufacture, sale or use of biocides within its jurisdiction and
      that may take enforcement action to ensure that biocides marketed
      within its jurisdiction comply with the applicable legal
      requirements. ([autorité réglementaire étrangère]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[formulant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[formulant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any component of a biocide
      other than an active ingredient or
      contaminant. ([formulant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[import]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[import]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means import for the purpose of
      sale. ([importer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[List of Foreign Regulatory Authorities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[List of Foreign Regulatory
      Authorities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the document entitled List
      of Foreign Regulatory Authorities for Biocides that is published
      by the Government of Canada on its website, as amended from time
      to time. ([Liste des autorités réglementaires
      étrangères]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[lot number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[lot number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any combination of
      letters, figures or both by which a biocide can be traced in
      manufacture and identified in distribution. ([numéro de
      lot]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[major change]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[major change]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a change, other than a
      change referred to in section 14, that relates to the information
      or material provided to the Minister in connection with a biocide
      and that may reasonably be expected to have a major impact on the
      quality of the biocide, the benefits or risks associated with the
      biocide or the uncertainties related to the benefits and risks,
      including

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a change to a method of application, unless the
        changed method is similar to an approved method of application
        for the biocide;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the addition of a method of application, unless
        the new method is similar to an approved method of application
        for the biocide;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the addition of a formulant, unless the
        formulant is replacing a similar formulant in a similar quantity
        in a given quantity of the biocide;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the removal of a formulant, unless the
        formulant is being replaced by a similar formulant in a similar
        quantity in a given quantity of the biocide;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a change to the quantity of a formulant that a
        given quantity of the biocide contains; and

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a change to the conditions of use of the
        biocide, other than

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the removal of an intended use or purpose,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the removal of a setting in which the
          biocide is intended to be used,

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the addition of risk information,

        - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a change to or the addition or removal of a
          direction for use, if the change, addition or removal does not
          require an update to the information on the biocide's safety
          or effectiveness that was provided to the Minister, or

        - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the removal of a direction for use that is
          due to the removal of a method of application or a change to
          the biocide's intended use or purpose. ([changement
          majeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[master formula]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[master formula]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a biocide,
      means a document that sets out

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ingredients that are used in the
        manufacture of the biocide and the quantities of those
        ingredients that are required to manufacture a given quantity of
        the biocide;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the specifications for the biocide;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the procedures
        required to manufacture, package, label and store the biocide,
        including safety precautions and in-process controls;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the methods used to
        test and examine the packaging material; and

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement of tolerances for the properties
        and qualities of the packaging material. ([formule
        type]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[minor change]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[minor change]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a change that relates
      to the information or material provided to the Minister in
      connection with a biocide and that may reasonably be expected to
      have a minor impact on the quality of the biocide, the benefits or
      risks associated with the biocide or the uncertainties related to
      the benefits and risks, including

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a change to the name or contact information of
        the holder of the market authorization for the biocide;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a change to the biocide's brand names;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the removal of a method of application;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the replacement of a formulant with a similar
        formulant in a similar quantity in a given quantity of the
        biocide;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a change referred to in any of subparagraphs
        (f)(i) to (v) of the definition [[major
        change]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef};

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the addition, replacement or removal of an
        importer of the biocide or a person that manufactures the
        biocide or a change to the name or contact information of such
        an importer or person; and

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a change in the location where the biocide is
        manufactured. ([changement mineur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[pest control product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[pest control product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
      as in subsection 2(1) of the [Pest Control Products
      Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01). ([produit
      antiparasitaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[shelf life]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[shelf life]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period, beginning on
      the day on which a biocide is packaged for sale to consumers,
      during which the biocide will, when stored appropriately, retain
      without any appreciable deterioration its stability and any other
      qualities claimed for it by the holder of the market authorization
      for it. ([durée de conservation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[specifications]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[specifications]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a biocide,
      means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement of its properties and qualities,
        and of the properties and qualities of its active ingredients
        and formulants, including the identity, potency and purity of
        the biocide and those ingredients and formulants;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the net quantity of the biocide in the package,
        the type of its package and a statement of the properties and
        qualities of the packaging material;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the methods used to
        test and examine the biocide and its active ingredients and
        formulants; and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement of tolerances for the properties
        and qualities of the biocide and for the properties and
        qualities of its active ingredients and
        formulants. ([spécifications]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[serious adverse drug
  reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 21.8 of the Act as it
  relates to biocides, [[serious adverse drug
  reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a response to a biocide that, in
  respect of human health,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} results in in-patient hospitalization, prolongation
    of existing hospitalization, congenital malformation or chronic or
    significant disability or incapacity; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is life-threatening or results in death.

## [Non-application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1456315 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Food and Drug
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870)

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided in these Regulations, the [Food and Drug
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) do not apply to a biocide.

## [Import]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1456318 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- import of non-compliant
biocides

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), it is prohibited to import a biocide if its sale in
  Canada would contravene the Act or these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The prohibition does not apply if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sale of the biocide in Canada would not
    contravene the Act or these Regulations if the biocide was
    relabelled or modified; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the importer provides an inspector with advance
    notice of the import.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- importer information not
published

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), it is prohibited to import a biocide unless the
  name and civic address of the importer of the biocide are published
  under subsection 12(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- testing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not prohibit the import of a
  biocide for the purpose of testing to support an application for a
  market authorization for that biocide submitted under section 10 or
  26.

## [Export]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1456331 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Export certificates

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 37(1)(c) of the Act, an export certificate in
  respect of a biocide must be in the form set out in Appendix III to
  the [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to retain copy

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The exporter must retain a copy of the export
  certificate for at least five years after the day on which the biocide
  is exported.

## [Transhipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1456337 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transhipment in bond

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 38(c) of the Act, all biocides must be in bond.

## [Exemptions --- Section 3 of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1456340 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advertising as preventative only

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A biocide is exempt from
the application of subsection 3(1) of the Act if the biocide is not
advertised to the general public as a treatment or cure for any of the
diseases, disorders or abnormal physical states referred to in Schedule
A.1 to the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale as preventative only

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A biocide is exempt from
the application of subsection 3(2) of the Act with respect to its sale
by a person if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the biocide is not represented by label as a
  treatment or cure for any of the diseases, disorders or abnormal
  physical states referred to in Schedule A.1 to the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person does not advertise the biocide to the
  general public as a treatment or cure for any of those diseases,
  disorders or abnormal physical states.

## [Market Authorizations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1456347 .Part}

### [Prohibition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1456348 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Import, sale or advertising

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), it is prohibited to import, sell or advertise a
  biocide unless the biocide is the subject of a market authorization
  issued under section 11.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- testing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not prohibit the import or sale
  of a biocide for the purpose of testing to support an application for
  a market authorization for that biocide submitted under section 10 or
  26.

### [Issuance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1456354 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application requirements

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for a market authorization for a biocide must be submitted
  to the Minister and contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name and contact information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the brand names under which the biocide is proposed
    to be sold or advertised;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the physical form of the biocide and the methods of
    application;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a list of the biocide's active ingredients and
    their CAS registry numbers, if they have one;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a list of the formulants that the biocide contains,
    including formulants that only a particular version of the biocide
    contains, and their CAS registry numbers, if they have one;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of each active ingredient and
    formulant that a given quantity of the biocide contains;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the conditions of use of the biocide;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} information regarding the benefits and risks
    associated with the biocide and any uncertainties relating to those
    benefits and risks, including reports of any tests and studies
    conducted by or on behalf of the applicant to identify and assess
    those benefits, risks and uncertainties;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the information referred to in
    paragraph (h);

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the names and contact information of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the persons that will manufacture the biocide,
      excluding individuals who are employees or agents of such persons,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any persons that will import the biocide, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if known, the persons that will package or
      label the biocide, excluding individuals who are employees or
      agents of such persons;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the civic addresses of the locations where the
    biocide will be manufactured and, if known, packaged and labelled;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the specifications for the biocide;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} the shelf life of the biocide;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} the text of every label to be used in connection
    with the biocide, including any supplementary information on the use
    of the biocide that is to be provided to users on request;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} if the biocide is a pest control product that is
    registered under the [Pest Control Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the registration number assigned to it under that
      Act, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the biocide's registration was
      based on a comparison with another biocide that is a pest control
      product, a statement to that effect and the registration number
      assigned to the other biocide under that Act; and

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} if a drug identification number has been assigned
    to the biocide under the [Food and Drug
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) and has not been
    cancelled,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the drug identification number assigned to it
      under those Regulations, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the biocide's drug
      identification number was assigned on the basis of a comparison
      with another biocide, a statement to that effect and the drug
      identification number assigned to the other biocide under those
      Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application based on comparison

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in paragraphs (1)(h), (i)
  and (l) --- other than information relating to the net quantity of the
  biocide in the package, the type of its package and a statement of the
  properties and qualities of the packaging material --- may be omitted
  from the application if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application is based on a comparison between
    the biocide and another biocide that is the subject of a market
    authorization;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application contains the other biocide's
    identification number;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the application contains information that
    demonstrates that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a given quantity of biocide, the biocide
      contains the same active ingredients in the same quantities as the
      other biocide,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the formulants that the biocide contains are
      among the formulants that the other biocide may contain under its
      market authorization,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for a given quantity of biocide, the quantity
      of each formulant that the biocide contains is the same as the
      quantity of that formulant that the other biocide may contain
      under its market authorization,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} subject to subparagraph (v), the conditions of
      use of the biocide fall within the conditions of use of the other
      biocide, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the biocide has the same risk information and
      directions for storage as the other biocide;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the application contains, if the applicant is not
    the holder of the market authorization for the other biocide, an
    attestation, by an individual who has authority to bind the holder,
    that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} confirms that the holder has provided the
      applicant with the master formula for the other biocide, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} mentions the other biocide's brand names and
      identification number;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the application contains an attestation, by an
    individual who has authority to bind the applicant, that confirms
    that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the biocide will be manufactured in accordance
      with the master formula for the other biocide, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the specifications for the biocide --- other
      than in respect of the net quantity of the biocide in the package,
      the type of its package and the properties and qualities of the
      packaging material --- are the same as those for the other
      biocide;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the following conditions are met in respect of the
    other biocide's market authorization:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (3), the other biocide's
      authorization was not issued on the basis of a comparison to a
      third biocide, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the other biocide's authorization is not
      suspended;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the other biocide was exempted
    from the application of these Regulations under subsection 66(1),
    the other biocide was not registered under the [Pest Control
    Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01) on the basis of a comparison to a
    third biocide that is a pest control product; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the other biocide was exempted
    from the application of these Regulations under subsection 68(1),
    the drug identification number assigned to the other biocide under
    the [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) was
    not assigned on the basis of a comparison to a third biocide.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- foreign biocide

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subparagraph (2)(f)(i) does not apply if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application for the other biocide's market
    authorization was submitted under section 26; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the market authorization for the
    other biocide is also the holder of the authorization to sell the
    foreign biocide.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information and material

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may request the applicant to provide any
  additional information or any material, including samples, that is
  necessary to enable the Minister to determine whether the market
  authorization must be issued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must issue a
market authorization for a biocide to an applicant if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application meets the applicable requirements set
  out in section 10 or 26, as the case may be;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has provided the Minister with any
  additional information and material that is requested under subsection
  10(4) or 26(5);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has sufficient evidence to support the
  conclusion that the benefits associated with the biocide outweigh the
  risks associated with it, taking into account any uncertainties
  relating to the benefits and risks; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
  the biocide will be packaged and labelled in accordance with the Act
  and these Regulations.

### [Terms and Conditions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1456455 .Subheading}

### [Direction to Stop Sale]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1456587 .Subheading}

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lot or batch is not manufactured, packaged or

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must lift a direction if the Minister is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a direction made for the reason set
    out in paragraph (1)(a), the lot or batch is manufactured, packaged

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a direction made for the reason set

### [Suspension]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1456599 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- sale or advertising

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), it is
  prohibited for a person to sell or advertise a biocide if the market
  authorization for the biocide is suspended under section 21 and the
  person has been notified of the suspension.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Partial suspension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case where the market authorization is
  suspended in part, a person does not contravene subsection (1) if they
  sell or advertise a version of the biocide other than the version
  described in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the notice referred to in subsection 21(1) or
    paragraph 22(1)(c); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information provided to them under subparagraph
    22(1)(a)(i) or paragraph 22(1)(b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to suspend

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by written notice provided to the holder of the market
  authorization for a biocide, suspend the authorization in whole or in
  part if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the holder or an importer has, in respect of the biocide,
    contravened any provision of the Act or these Regulations or any
    order made under the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the risks associated with the biocide --- or, in the case of a
    partial suspension, the risks associated with certain conditions of
    use, formulants or other aspects of the biocide --- outweigh the
    benefits associated with the biocide, taking into account any
    uncertainties relating to the benefits and risks; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the market authorization was
    issued on the basis of an application submitted under section 26,
    the Minister becomes aware that the authorization to sell the
    foreign biocide has been revoked or suspended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Precondition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before suspending a market authorization in whole,
  the Minister must consider if a partial suspension would be sufficient
  to address the situation giving rise to the proposed suspension.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice referred to in subsection (1) must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for the suspension;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement of whether the market authorization is
    suspended in whole or in part and, if it is suspended in part, a
    description of the version of the biocide to which the suspension
    relates; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the effective date of the suspension.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Serious and imminent risk

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may make the suspension effective
  immediately if the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that the
  suspension is necessary to prevent a serious and imminent risk of
  injury to human health.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to third parties

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  market authorization for a biocide is suspended,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the authorization must

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} without delay after receiving the notice referred
      to in subsection 21(1), provide the information that is set out in
      the notice under paragraphs 21(3)(b) and (c) as well as the
      biocide's identification number --- and, in the case of a partial
      suspension, the lot numbers of the version of the biocide to which
      the suspension relates --- to any importer of the biocide and any
      person, other than a user, to whom the holder has sold the biocide
      or the affected version, as the case may be, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the holder is required to
      provide information to importers or other persons under
      subparagraph (i), provide to the Minister, within two business
      days after receiving the notice referred to in subsection 21(1) or
      within any longer period specified by the Minister, written
      confirmation that the information has been provided to those
      persons;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person that receives the information referred
    to in subparagraph (a)(i) from the holder or from any person that
    has sold them the biocide must provide the information without delay
    to any person, other than a user, to whom the recipient of the
    information has sold the biocide or the affected version, as the
    case may be; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister must publish on the Government of
    Canada website a notice of the suspension that sets out

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the reasons for the suspension, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the information referred to in paragraphs
      21(3)(b) and (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[business
  day]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (1), [[business
  day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day other than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Saturday; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a Sunday or other holiday.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reinstatement of market authorization

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must reinstate a suspended market authorization if the
  situation that gave rise to the suspension has been corrected or if
  the reasons for the suspension were unfounded.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of reinstatement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a market authorization is reinstated, the Minister
  must publish on the Government of Canada website a notice to that
  effect.

### [Revocation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1456641 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Automatic revocation

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  market authorization, or a part of a market authorization, that is
  suspended for a reason set out in paragraph 21(1)(a) or (c) is revoked
  if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a suspension under paragraph
    21(1)(a), the situation that gave rise to the suspension is not
    corrected within the six-month period that begins on the effective
    date of the suspension; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a suspension under paragraph
    21(1)(c), the situation that gave rise to the foreign revocation or
    suspension is not corrected within the six-month period that begins
    on the effective date of the suspension of the market authorization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to revoke --- suspension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may revoke a market authorization, or a
  part of a market authorization, that is suspended for the reason set
  out in paragraph 21(1)(b) if the Minister has reasonable grounds to
  believe that the holder has failed, within the six-month period that
  begins on the effective date of the suspension, to establish that the
  benefits associated with the biocide outweigh the risks associated
  with it --- or, in the case of a partial suspension, the risks that
  led to the suspension --- taking into account any uncertainties
  relating to the benefits and risks.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Minister is not authorized
  under subsection (2) to revoke a larger part of the market
  authorization than the part that is suspended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to revoke --- permanent
  discontinuance of sale

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must revoke a market authorization if
  the holder of the market authorization informs the Minister under
  section 47 that they have permanently discontinued the sale of the
  biocide in Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public notice

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** If a market authorization
for a biocide is revoked under section 24, the Minister must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} publish on the Government of Canada website a notice
  of revocation that sets out

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the reasons for the revocation,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a statement of whether the authorization is
    revoked in whole or in part and, if it is revoked in part, a
    description of the version of the biocide to which the revocation
    relates, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the effective date of the revocation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, amend the information published under
  subsection 12(2) accordingly.

  :   [[flame projection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the flame resulting

      container when tested in accordance with official method DO-30,
      Determination of Flame Projection, dated October 15, 1981 and
      published by the Government of Canada on its website. ([projection
      de la flamme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[flashback]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[flashback]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the part of a flame
      projection that extends from the point of ignition back to the
      designated container when tested in accordance with official
      method DO-30, Determination of Flame Projection, dated October 15,
      1981 and published by the Government of Canada on its
      website. ([retour de flamme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions --- small quantities

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  primary hazard statements set out in paragraph 34(1)(c) and
  subparagraphs 35(1)(a)(iii), (b)(iii) and (c)(iii) may be omitted from
  the inner label of a biocide if the net quantity shown on the inner
  label does not exceed 60 mL or 60 g.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inner label --- 120 mL or g

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The statements set out in subsections 34(2) and 35(2)
  may be omitted from the inner label of a biocide if the net quantity
  shown on the inner label does not exceed 120 mL or 120 g.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Outer label --- 120 mL or g

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in subsections 34(1) and
  35(1) may be omitted from the outer label of a biocide if the net
  quantity shown on the inner label does not exceed 120 mL or 120 g.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Packaging standards

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** The immediate container
of a biocide must be constructed to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} contain the biocide safely under normal conditions of
  storage, display and distribution;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} permit the user to withdraw the biocide in a safe
  manner and to close the container in a manner that will contain the
  biocide safely under normal storage conditions; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} minimize changes to, including degradation of, its
  contents.

## [Information Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1456810 .Part}

### [Notable and Serious Incidents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1456811 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this section and sections 39 and 40.

[[notable incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[notable incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a response to a biocide that adversely affects
      human health; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a failure in the effectiveness of a biocide that,
      in respect of human health,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} could have resulted in in-patient
        hospitalization, prolongation of existing hospitalization,
        congenital malformation or chronic or significant disability or
        incapacity, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} could have been life-threatening or resulted
        in death. ([événement notable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[serious incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[serious incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a response to or a
    failure in the effectiveness of a biocide that, in respect of human
    health,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} results in in-patient hospitalization,
      prolongation of existing hospitalization, congenital malformation
      or chronic or significant disability or incapacity; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is life-threatening or results in
      death. ([événement grave]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[serious unexpected incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[serious unexpected incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a serious
    incident that is not identified in nature, severity or frequency in
    the risk information that is set out on the label of the
    biocide. ([événement grave et imprévu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Serious incident reporting

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** The holder of the market
authorization for a biocide must provide the Minister with the
information in their control about the following serious incidents
within 15 days after becoming aware of the incident:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any serious incident involving the biocide that
  occurs in Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any serious unexpected incident involving the biocide
  that occurs outside Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information relating to incidents

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder of the market authorization for a biocide must compile and
  review, in a manner that ensures the effective and timely detection of
  significant safety issues associated with the biocide, information
  that they become aware of relating to notable incidents and serious
  incidents involving the biocide that occur in or outside Canada,
  including information about measures that have been taken outside
  Canada to address such issues.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Significant safety issue

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, in reviewing the information, the holder
  concludes that there is a significant safety issue related to the
  benefits or risks associated with the biocide, they must notify the
  Minister in writing without delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), the holder must retain the
  compiled information for at least 10 years after becoming aware of it,
  even if they cease to hold the market authorization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New holder

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, during the 10-year period, another person becomes
  the holder of the market authorization, the former holder must
  transfer the compiled information to the new holder and the new holder
  must retain it for the remainder of the period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for information

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister becomes aware of new information
  relating to the benefits or risks associated with the biocide, the
  Minister may request in writing that the holder provide the Minister
  with any of the compiled information that is relevant to those
  benefits or risks.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must specify a period within which the
  information is to be provided that is reasonable in the circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to provide information

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The holder must provide the Minister with the
  requested information within the specified period.

## [Recalls]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1456909 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recall system

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person that has sold a biocide must maintain a system of control,
  including records, that permits the complete and rapid recall of the
  biocide from the persons to whom they have sold it, other than
  consumers that purchased the biocide at the retail level.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person must retain each record

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case where an expiry date is shown on the
    label of the biocide to which the record relates, for a period that
    ends no earlier than the last day of the month that is shown as the
    expiry date; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, for at least six years after the
    day on which the person sold the biocide to which the record
    relates.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voluntary recall

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If any
  of the following persons decides to recall a biocide that is the
  subject of a market authorization without being ordered to do so by
  the Minister, the person must, within 24 hours after making the
  decision, provide the Minister with the information set out in
  subsection (2), in writing:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the market authorization;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an importer of the biocide; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person that manufactures the biocide and sells
    it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be provided

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information to be provided is as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the biocide's brand names;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the biocide's identification number;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the names and contact information of the persons
    that manufactured the biocide, excluding individuals who are
    employees or agents of such persons, and, in the case of a recall by
    the holder, the names and contact information of any importers;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the individual
    who is responsible for the recall;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the lot numbers, dates of manufacture and expiry
    dates of the biocide that is being recalled;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the biocide that was manufactured
    in Canada;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the biocide that was imported;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the biocide that the person sold to
    persons in Canada;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the period during which the person sold the biocide
    in Canada;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the biocide that the person
    exported from Canada, as well as the quantity exported, by country;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the biocide that is in Canada and
    that remains in the possession or control of the person;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the names of the persons in Canada, other than
    consumers that purchased the biocide at the retail level, to whom
    the biocide was sold by the person and the quantity sold to each of
    the named persons;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} the classes of persons from whom the biocide is
    being recalled;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} the expected dates for the start and completion of
    the recall;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} the reason for the recall and the date on which,
    and the manner in which, the situation that prompted the recall was
    discovered;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} an assessment of the risk of injury to human health
    posed by the biocide that is being recalled, including because of a
    failure of its effectiveness; and

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} a description of any other measures that the person
    is taking in respect of the recall.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communications

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before starting the recall, provide the Minister
    with a copy of the communications that they intend to use in
    connection with starting the recall; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} after starting the recall, provide the Minister, on
    request and within the time specified by the Minister, with a copy
    of any additional communications that they use, or intend to use, in
    connection with the recall.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures that are intended to

## [Quality Control]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1456980 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- failure to follow master
formula

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for the holder of the market authorization for a biocide or
  the importer of a biocide to sell the biocide unless the biocide has
  been manufactured, packaged, labelled and stored in accordance with
  the master formula for the biocide.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- manufacturer, packager
  or labeller

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for a person that manufactures,
  packages or labels a biocide that is the subject of a market
  authorization to sell the biocide if they have failed to manufacture,
  package or label it, as the case may be, in accordance with the master
  formula for the biocide or have failed to store it in accordance with
  the master formula.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records --- quality control

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder of the market authorization for a biocide and the importer of a
  biocide must maintain records that demonstrate that each lot or batch
  of the biocide that they sell has been manufactured, packaged,
  labelled and stored in accordance with the master formula for the
  biocide.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records --- manufacturer, packager or
  labeller

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person that manufactures, packages or labels a
  biocide that is the subject of a market authorization must, for each
  lot or batch of the biocide that they sell, maintain records
  demonstrating that they have manufactured, packaged or labelled, as
  the case may be, the lot or batch in accordance with the master
  formula for the biocide and have stored it in accordance with the
  master formula.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The holder, importer or person must --- even if they
  cease to hold the market authorization or no longer import or sell the
  biocide, as the case may be --- retain each record for at least one
  year after the last day of the month in which the shelf life of the
  biocide ends or, if that day cannot be determined, for at least six
  years after the day on which they sold the biocide.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to provide information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Anyone that is required to retain a record under
  subsection (3) must provide the information in the record to the
  Minister, on request, within five days after the request is made or,
  if the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that there is a
  serious and imminent risk of injury to human health, within any
  shorter period specified by the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- sterile biocides

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited to
manufacture or package a biocide that is intended to be sterile unless
the manufacturing or packaging is carried out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a separate and enclosed area;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} under the supervision of an individual who is trained
  in microbiology; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} using a method that is scientifically proven to
  ensure the sterility of the biocide.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation of complaints

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If any
  of the following persons receives a complaint in respect of the
  quality of a biocide that is the subject of a market authorization,
  the person must investigate it and, if necessary, take corrective
  action:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the market authorization;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an importer of the biocide; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person that manufactures, packages or labels the
    biocide and sells it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person must maintain a record of each complaint
  and investigation and any corrective action taken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person must --- even if they cease to hold the
  market authorization or no longer import or sell the biocide, as the
  case may be --- retain each record for at least one year after the
  latest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the last day of the month in which the shelf life
    of the biocide to which the complaint relates ends, if that day can
    be determined,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if no corrective action is taken, the day on which
    the investigation is completed, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if corrective action is taken, the day on which the
    action is completed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to provide information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Anyone that is required to retain a record under
  subsection (3) must provide the information in the record to the

  shorter period specified by the Minister.
