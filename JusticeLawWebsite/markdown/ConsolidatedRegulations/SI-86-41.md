## United States Diplomatic and Consular Staff Remission Order {#united-states-diplomatic-and-consular-staff-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/86-41
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1986-04-02

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties Imposed Under the
Customs Tariff and Taxes Imposed Under Division III of Part IX and Under
any Other Part of the Excise Tax Act on Articles Imported by Members of
the United States Diplomatic and Consular Administrative and Technical
Staff in Canada for Their Personal Use and the Use of Their Families

P.C. 1986-684 1986-03-20
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to make the annexed remission order, is pleased
hereby, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance and the
Treasury Board and pursuant to section 17[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the annexed
Order respecting the remission of customs duty and sales and excise
taxes on articles imported by members of the United States diplomatic
and consular administrative and technical staff in Canada for their
personal use and the use of their families.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA){#footnote_e}S.C.
  1980-81-82-83, c. 170, s. 4
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641129 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [United States Diplomatic and Consular Staff Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-86-41).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641131 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[importer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of the United States
diplomatic and consular administrative and technical staff in Canada who
imports one or more articles into Canada. (*importateur*)

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641133 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and the taxes paid or payable
under Division III of Part IX and under any other Part of the [Excise
Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on articles imported by an importer for the
importer's personal use or the use of the importer's family.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-17, s. 2
- SI/91-12, s. 2
:::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641137 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The remission granted under
section 3 is on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the article was imported on or after September 1,
  1985;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the appropriate application in respect of the article
  is made by the importer under section 5 or 6 of the [Customs
  Diplomatic Privileges Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._522)
  and the application is approved by or in the name of the Ambassador of
  the United States to Canada; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made by the importer to the
  Minister of National Revenue within two years after the date of
  importation of the article into Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-17, s. 2(E)
- SI/92-203, s. 2
:::

## [Disposition of Articles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641144 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  customs duties imposed under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)
  and no taxes imposed under Division III of Part IX or under any other
  Part of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) are payable on the
  disposition of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an article, other than a motor vehicle, in respect
    of which remission has been granted under section 3, where the
    article has been in the possession of and used by an importer in
    Canada for the one-year period following its importation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a motor vehicle in respect of which remission has
    been granted under section 3, where the vehicle has been in the
    possession of and used by an importer in Canada for the two-year
    period following its importation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(b) applies in respect of the
  disposition of a motor vehicle the importer of which dies or is
  transferred from Canada before the expiration of the two-year period
  referred to therein.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-17, s. 2(E)
- SI/91-12, s. 2
