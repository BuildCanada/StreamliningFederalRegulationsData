## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024910 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a medical emergency, fire,
    flood or any disaster that threatens life, property or the
    environment. ([urgence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Exemption for Emergency Use]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024914 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Imported goods that, in
response to an emergency, are temporarily diverted to a use other than
that for which they were released are exempt from the operation of
subsection 32.2(6) of the Act for the period during which the goods are
used in response to the emergency.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Imported goods that are
consumed in response to an emergency are exempt from the operation of
subsection 32.2(6) of the Act.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024917 .Part}

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024919 .Part}

force on January 1, 1998.
