## Alpine Joe Sportswear Remission Order, 1998 {#alpine-joe-sportswear-remission-order-1998 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-345
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1998-06-18

Alpine Joe Sportswear Remission Order, 1998

P.C. 1998-1118  1998-06-18
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 115 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Alpine Joe Sportswear Remission Order,
1998](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-345).

  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e-ID0EDBAA){#fna_e}S.C.
  1997, c. 36
  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022577 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order, [[specified
fabrics]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means 4-thread twill fabrics, 109.2 cm (43
in.) width, containing 60% or more by weight of polyester staple fibres,
30% or more by weight of cotton and 5% or more by weight of elastomeric
monofilaments, having in the warp 423 yarns or more but not exceeding
433 yarns per 10 cm (172 decitex per single yarn) and in the weft 252
yarns or more but not exceeding 262 yarns per 10 cm (316 decitex per
single yarn), weighing less than 300 g/m^2^, of subheading No. 5514.22.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022579 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted to Alpine Joe Sportswear Ltd., Vancouver, of
the customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on specified fabrics up to an annual
quantity limit of 13,716 metres (15,000 linear yards).

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022581 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted
pursuant to section 2 on condition that:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the specified fabrics are used by Alpine Joe
  Sportswear in the manufacture of uniform or hiking pants and shorts or
  bicycle touring shorts;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the specified fabrics are imported during the period
  beginning on January 1, 1997 and ending on December 31, 2002; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within two years of the date of importation.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022586 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on June 18, 1998.
