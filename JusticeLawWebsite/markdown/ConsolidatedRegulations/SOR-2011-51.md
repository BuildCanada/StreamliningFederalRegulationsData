## Regulations Implementing the United Nations Resolutions and Imposing Special Economic Measures on Libya {#regulations-implementing-the-united-nations-resolutions-and-imposing-special-economic-measures-on-libya .Title-of-Act}

[UNITED NATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/U-2)

Registration 2011-02-27

Regulations Implementing the United Nations Resolutions and Imposing
Special Economic Measures on Libya

P.C. 2011-253 2011-02-27
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Security Council of the United Nations, acting under Article
41 of the Charter of the United Nations, adopted Resolution 1970 (2011)
on February 26, 2011;

Whereas that resolution calls for the taking of economic measures
against Libya;

Whereas it appears to the Governor in Council to be necessary to make
regulations for enabling the measures set out in that resolution to be
effectively applied;

And whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that the situation
in Libya constitutes a grave breach of international peace and security
that has resulted or is likely to result in a serious international
crisis;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, hereby makes the
annexed Regulations Implementing the United Nations Resolution on Libya
and Taking Special Economic Measures pursuant to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} subsections 4(1) to (3) of the [Special Economic
  Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-14.5)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_ae){#fn_ae-ID0EBABAA}; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 2 of the [United Nations
  Act](/eng/acts/U-2)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_be){#fn_be-ID0EBCAAA}.

  - ::: Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-779324 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Libya]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Libya]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Libyan state and includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any of its political subdivisions;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its government and any of its departments or a
      government or department of its political subdivisions; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any of its agencies or any agency of its
      political subdivisions. ([Libye]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Foreign
    Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is or was employed in the service of Her Majesty
      in right of Canada or of a province;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} occupies or occupied a position of responsibility
      in the service of Her Majesty in right of Canada or of a province;
      or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is or was engaged by or on behalf of Her Majesty
      in right of Canada or of a
      province. ([fonctionnaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day that is not Saturday
    or a holiday. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[United Nations Resolutions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-779339 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-779340 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Part.

  [[armed mercenary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[armed mercenary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is specially recruited to use arms and related
        material in Libya;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} uses arms and related material in Libya and is
        motivated essentially by the desire for private gain;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is not a member of the Libyan Armed Forces; and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} has not been sent to Libya by a state on
        official duty as a member of that state's armed
        forces. ([mercenaire armé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[arms and related material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[arms and related material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any type
      of weapon, ammunition, military vehicle, military equipment or
      paramilitary equipment, and their spare parts. ([armes et matériel
      connexe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Committee of the Security Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Committee of the Security Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      the Committee of the Security Council established under paragraph
      24 of Security Council Resolution 1970. ([Comité du Conseil de
      sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person that is
      designated by the Security Council under paragraph 17 of Security
      Council Resolution 1970 or by the Committee of the Security
      Council under paragraph 24(c) of that resolution or whose name
      appears in Annex II to that resolution or in Annex II to Security
      Council Resolution 1973. ([personne désignée]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[designated vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that is
      designated by the Committee of the Security Council or by the
      Security Council under paragraph 11 of Security Council Resolution
      2146. ([bâtiment désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[military activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[military activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any activities
      conducted by state armed forces, non-state armed forces or armed
      mercenaries and any activities that support the operational
      capabilities of an armed group in Libya. ([activités
      militaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Security Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Security Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Security
      Council of the United Nations. ([Conseil de
      sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Security Council Resolution 1970]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Security Council Resolution 1970]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      Resolution 1970 (2011) of February 26, 2011, adopted by the
      Security Council. ([résolution 1970 du Conseil de
      sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Security Council Resolution 1973]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Security Council Resolution 1973]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      Resolution 1973 (2011) of March 17, 2011, adopted by the Security
      Council. ([résolution 1973 du Conseil de
      sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Security Council Resolution 2146]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Security Council Resolution 2146]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      Resolution 2146 (2014) of March 19, 2014, adopted by the Security
      Council. ([résolution 2146 du Conseil de
      sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[technical assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[technical assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any form of
      assistance, such as providing instruction, training, consulting
      services or technical advice or transferring know-how or technical
      data. ([aide technique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian, entity, person and property

### [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-779363 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited activities

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada to knowingly

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deal in any property in Canada that is owned, held or
  controlled, directly or indirectly, by a designated person or by a
  person acting on behalf of or at the direction of a designated person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into or facilitate any transaction related to a
  dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial or related services in respect
  of a dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} make available any property or provide any financial
  or related services to a designated person, to a person acting on
  behalf of or at the direction of a designated person or to an entity
  that is owned, held or controlled, directly or indirectly, by a
  designated person; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} make available any property or provide any financial
  or related services for the benefit of any person or any entity
  referred to in paragraph (d).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-101, s. 2

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- humanitarian assistance

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** Section 3 does not
prohibit the provision, processing or payment of funds, other financial
assets or economic resources or the provision of goods and services that
are necessary to ensure the timely delivery of humanitarian assistance
or to support other activities that support basic human needs in Libya
by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the United Nations, including its programs, funds and
  other entities and bodies, as well as its specialized agencies and
  related organizations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} international organizations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} humanitarian organizations that have been granted
  observer status by the United Nations General Assembly and members of
  those humanitarian organizations;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} bilaterally or multilaterally funded non-governmental
  organizations that are participating in any United Nations
  humanitarian response plan, refugee response plan or other United
  Nations appeals for assistance or in groups of humanitarian
  organizations coordinated by the United Nations Office for the
  Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs, including the employees,
  grantees, subsidiaries and implementing partners of those
  non-governmental organizations while acting in those capacities;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other individual or entity authorized for that
  purpose by a United Nations committee established by the Security
  Council acting within its mandate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arms and related material --- exporting

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for any person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada to
  knowingly export, sell, supply or transfer, directly or indirectly,
  arms and related material, wherever situated, destined for Libya or
  for any person in Libya.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arms and related material --- importing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for any person in Canada or any
  Canadian outside Canada to knowingly import, purchase or acquire arms
  and related material, wherever situated, from Libya or from any person
  in Libya.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arms and related material --- technical
  assistance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for any person in Canada or any
  Canadian outside Canada to knowingly provide technical assistance
  related to the sale, supply, transfer, manufacture, maintenance or use
  of arms and related material to Libya, to any person in Libya or to
  any person acting on behalf of Libya.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arms and related material --- property
  and financial services

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for any person in Canada or any
  Canadian outside Canada to knowingly make available any property or
  provide any financial or related services to Libya, to any person in
  Libya or to any person acting on behalf of Libya if the property or
  services are related to the sale, supply, transfer, manufacture,
  maintenance or use of any arms and related material.

### [Applications]{.HTitleText2} {#h-779429 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person that wishes to engage in any activity that is prohibited under
  this Part may, before doing so, apply to the Minister in writing for a
  certificate to exempt the activity from the application of these
  Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must issue the certificate if the
  Security Council did not intend that the activity be prohibited or if
  the Security Council or the Committee of the Security Council has
  approved the activity in advance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption for property

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person whose property is affected by the application of section 3 may
  apply to the Minister in writing for a certificate to exempt the
  property from the application of that section if the property is
  necessary for basic or extraordinary expenses or is subject to a lien,
  mortgage or security interest, to a hypothec or prior claim, to a
  charge or to a judicial, administrative or arbitral decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If it is established in accordance with Security
  Council Resolution 1970 that the property is necessary for basic or
  extraordinary expenses or is subject to a lien, mortgage or security
  interest, to a hypothec or prior claim, to a charge or to a judicial,
  administrative or arbitral decision, the Minister must issue a
  certificate within the following time periods:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 15 working days after receiving the
    application, in the case of property that is necessary for basic
    expenses, if the Security Council does not oppose the application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 30 working days after receiving the
    application, in the case of property that is necessary for
    extraordinary expenses, if the Security Council approves the
    application; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} within 90 working days after receiving the
    application, in the case of property that is subject to a lien,
    mortgage or security interest, to a hypothec or prior claim, to a
    charge or to a judicial, administrative or arbitral decision that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} was created or issued before the person became a
      designated person,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is not for the benefit of a designated person,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has been brought to the attention of the
      Committee of the Security Council by the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mistaken identity

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person whose name is the same as or similar to the name of a
  designated person and that claims not to be that person may apply to
  the Minister in writing for a certificate stating that they are not
  that designated person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination by Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within 45 working days after receiving the
  application, the Minister must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it is established that the applicant is not the
    designated person, issue the certificate; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is not so established, provide notice to the

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Special Economic Measures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-779482 .Part}

### [List]{.HTitleText2} {#h-779483 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Listed person

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A person whose name is
listed under this Part is a person in respect of whom the Governor in
Council, on the recommendation of the Minister, is satisfied that there
are reasonable grounds to believe is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person engaged in activities that directly or
  indirectly facilitate, support, provide funding for or contribute to a
  violation or attempted violation of the security, stability or
  territorial integrity of Libya;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an associate or family member of a person referred to
  in paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an entity owned, held or controlled, directly or
  indirectly, by a person referred to in paragraph (a) or by a person
  acting on behalf of or at the direction of a person referred to in
  paragraph (a); or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a senior official of an entity referred to in
  paragraph (c).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2013-160, s. 1
- SOR/2018-101, s. 2

### [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-779494 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited dealings and activities

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deal in any property, wherever situated, that is
  owned, held or controlled by a listed person or by a person acting on
  behalf of a listed person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into or facilitate any transaction related to a
  dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial or related services in respect
  of a dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} make available any goods, wherever situated, to a
  listed person or to a person acting on behalf of a listed person; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial or related services to or for
  the benefit of a listed person.

- SOR/2018-101, s. 2

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-779525 .Part}

### [Prohibited Involvement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-779526 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assisting a prohibited activity

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada to knowingly do anything
that causes, facilitates or assists in, or is intended to cause,
facilitate or assist in, any activity prohibited by sections 3, 5 to 9
and 18.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-101, s. 2
:::

### [Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-779531 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to determine

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following entities must determine on a continuing basis whether they
  are in possession or control of property that is owned, held or
  controlled by or on behalf of a designated person:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[authorized foreign banks]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
    as defined in section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), in
    respect of their business in Canada or banks regulated by that Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cooperative credit societies, savings and credit
    unions and caisses populaires regulated by a provincial Act and
    associations regulated by the [Cooperative Credit Associations
    Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[foreign companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
    defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), in respect of their insurance activities in
    Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[provincial
    companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
    [[societies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms are defined in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} fraternal benefit societies regulated by a
    provincial Act in respect of their insurance activities and
    insurance companies and other entities regulated by a provincial Act
    that are engaged in the business of insuring risks;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} companies to which the [Trust and Loan Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) applies;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} trust companies regulated by a provincial Act;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} loan companies regulated by a provincial Act;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} entities that engage in any activity referred to in
    paragraph 5(h) of the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and
    Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501) if the activity
    involves the opening of an account for a client; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} entities authorized under provincial legislation to
    engage in the business of dealing in securities or to provide
    portfolio management or investment counselling services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Listed person

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every entity referred to in subsection (1) must also
  determine on a continuing basis whether they are in possession or
  control of property that is owned, held or controlled by or on behalf
  of a listed person.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-101, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to disclose --- RCMP or CSIS

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person in Canada, every Canadian outside Canada and every entity
  referred to in subsection 23(1) must disclose without delay to the
  Commissioner of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police or to the Director
  of the Canadian Security Intelligence Service

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the existence of property in their possession or
    control that they have reason to believe is owned, held or
    controlled by or on behalf of a designated person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the existence of property in their possession or
    control that they have reason to believe is owned, held or
    controlled by or on behalf of a listed person; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any information about a transaction or proposed
    transaction in respect of property referred to in paragraph (a) or
    (b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No proceedings under the [United Nations
  Act](/eng/acts/U-2) or the [Special Economic Measures
  Act](/eng/acts/S-14.5) and no civil proceedings lie against a person
  for a disclosure made in good faith under subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-101, s. 2
