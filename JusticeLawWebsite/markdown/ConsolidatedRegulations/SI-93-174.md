## Order Designating the Minister of Finance as Minister for Purposes of the Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-finance-as-minister-for-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-174
:::

[PETRO-CANADA PUBLIC PARTICIPATION ACT](/eng/acts/P-11.1)

Registration 1993-09-08

Order Designating the Minister of Finance as Minister for Purposes of
the Act

P.C. 1993-1674  1993-08-18
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the [Petro-Canada
Public Participation Act](/eng/acts/P-11.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-174_e_hq_6523){#fn_SI-93-174_e_hq_6523-ID0EDCAA},
is pleased hereby to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1991-414 of March 1,
1991[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-174_e_hq_6524){#fn_SI-93-174_e_hq_6524-ID0EBCAA}
and to designate the Minister of Finance, a member of the Queen's Privy
Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
