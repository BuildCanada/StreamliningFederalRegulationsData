## Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials (Ukraine) Regulations {#freezing-assets-of-corrupt-foreign-officials-ukraine-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2014-44
:::

[FREEZING ASSETS OF CORRUPT FOREIGN OFFICIALS ACT](/eng/acts/F-31.6)

Registration 2014-03-05

Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials (Ukraine) Regulations

P.C. 2014-198 2014-03-05
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas Ukraine has asserted to the Government of Canada, in writing,
that each of the persons listed in the annexed [Freezing Assets of
Corrupt Foreign Officials (Ukraine)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-44) has misappropriated property
of Ukraine or has acquired property inappropriately by virtue of their
office or a personal or business relationship and Ukraine has asked the
Government of Canada to freeze the property of those persons;

Whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that each of those persons
is a politically exposed foreign person in relation to Ukraine;

Whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that there is internal
turmoil or an uncertain political situation in Ukraine;

And whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that the making of the
annexed [Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials (Ukraine)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-44) is in the interest of
international relations;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to
subsections 4(1) to (3) of the [Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign
Officials Act](/eng/acts/F-31.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-870-E_hq_10504){#fn_81000-2-870-E_hq_10504-ID0EDABA},
makes the annexed [Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials
(Ukraine) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-44).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-810730 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Section 2 applies in
respect of the property of the politically exposed foreign persons
listed in the schedule.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-810733 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Asset freeze

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A person in Canada or a
Canadian outside Canada must not

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deal, directly or indirectly, in any property,
  wherever situated, of any politically exposed foreign person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into or facilitate, directly or indirectly, any
  financial transaction related to a dealing referred to in
  paragraph (a); or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide financial services or other related services
  in respect of any property of any politically exposed foreign person.

## [Application Before Publication]{.HTitleText1} {#h-810739 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application before publication

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 11(2)(a) of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22),
these Regulations apply before they are published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-810742 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
