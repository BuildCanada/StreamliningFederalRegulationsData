## [Total Assets]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676563 .Part}

Definition of [[total assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 380(3) of the [Trust and Loan Companies
Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), [[total assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect
of a company, at a particular time, means the total value of the assets
that would be reported on its balance sheet, prepared as at that time in
accordance with the accounting principles and specifications of the
Superintendent referred to in subsection 313(4) of that Act.
