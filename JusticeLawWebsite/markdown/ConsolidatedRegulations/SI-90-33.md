## Order Designating the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology as Minister for Purposes of the Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-industry-science-and-technology-as-minister-for-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/90-33
:::

[AGRICULTURAL AND RURAL DEVELOPMENT ACT (ARDA)](/eng/acts/A-3)

Registration 1990-03-14

Order Designating the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology as
Minister for Purposes of the Act

P.C. 1990-392 1990-02-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 2 of the [Agricultural and Rural
Development Act (ARDA)](/eng/acts/A-3), is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1984-2290 of June 28,
  1984[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBABA}; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to designate the Minister of Industry, Science and
  Technology, a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the
  Minister for the purposes of that Act.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EBABA){#footnote1_e}SI/84-104,
    1984 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2988
    :::
