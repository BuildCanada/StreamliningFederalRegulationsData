## Solicitor General of Canada Authority to Prescribe Fees or Charges Order (RCMP Training Academy-Regina) {#solicitor-general-of-canada-authority-to-prescribe-fees-or-charges-order-rcmp-training-academy-regina .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/89-214
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1989-10-11

Solicitor General of Canada Authority to Prescribe Fees or Charges Order
(RCMP Training Academy-Regina)

P.C. 1989-1898  1989-09-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that the cost of
providing services or the use of facilities to persons by the Royal
Canadian Mounted Police should be borne by the persons to whom those
services or facilities are provided.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Solicitor General of Canada and the Treasury
Board, pursuant to paragraph 19(b) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1985-137 of January
  17, 1985[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-89-214_e_hq_6458){#fn_SI-89-214_e_hq_6458-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-89-214_e_hq_6458-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_SI-89-214_e_hq_6458}SI/85-12,
    1985 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 1037
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to authorize the Solicitor General of Canada to
  prescribe, by order, the fee or charge to be paid by persons to whom
  services or the use of facilities is provided by the Royal Canadian
  Mounted Police at its Training Academy (Depot Division) in Regina,
  Saskatchewan.
