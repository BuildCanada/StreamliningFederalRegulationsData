## Order Transferring Certain Portions of the Department of Human Resources Development to the Department of Human Resources and Skills Development {#order-transferring-certain-portions-of-the-department-of-human-resources-development-to-the-department-of-human-resources-and-skills-development .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2003-203
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2003-12-31

Order Transferring Certain Portions of the Department of Human Resources
Development to the Department of Human Resources and Skills Development

P.C. 2003-2038  2003-12-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} from the Department of Human Resources Development to
  the Department of Human Resources and Skills Development the control
  and supervision of the following portions of the Department of Human
  Resources Development:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Human Investment Programs Branch, except

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} those portions associated with the Social
      Development Partnership Program,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the Office for Disability Issues, and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} those portions involving the Canada Student Loans
      Program call centres and web-services,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Employment Programs Branch, except those
    portions that administer employment programs for persons with
    disabilities,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Insurance Programs Branch, except those
    portions related to

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} activities related to the streamlining and
      automation of services and benefits processing, including
      Modernizing Services for Canadians and Government on Line,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the Social Insurance Number and Social Insurance
      Register operations, and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} channel management activities related to call
      centres and web-services,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} those portions of the Strategic Policy Branch
    related to labour market policy and learning policy,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the National Secretariat for Homelessness, and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} all in-person services functions related to the
    programs and services of the Department of Human Resources
    Development, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} from the Minister of Human Resources Development to
  be styled Minister of Social Development to the Minister of State to
  be styled Minister of Human Resources and Skills Development the
  powers, duties and functions relating to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the employment insurance boards of referees
    established under Part VI of the [Employment Insurance
    Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6)[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EBBBBAA}, conferred upon the
    Minister of Human Resources under that Act, and

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e-ID0EBBBBAA){#fna_e}S.C. 1996, c. 23
      :::

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Canada Employment Insurance Commission
    conferred upon the Minister of Human Resources Development under the
    [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6)[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EEBABAA} and the [Department
    of Human Resources Development Act](/eng/acts/H-5.8)[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_e){#fnb_e-ID0EBBABAA},

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_e-ID0EBBABAA){#fnb_e}S.C. 1996, c. 11
      :::

effective December 12, 2003.
