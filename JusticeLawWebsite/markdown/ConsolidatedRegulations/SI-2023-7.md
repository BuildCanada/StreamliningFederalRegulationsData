## Interest on Debts for Services Rendered by the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Remission Order {#interest-on-debts-for-services-rendered-by-the-royal-canadian-mounted-police-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2023-7
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2023-04-12

Interest on Debts for Services Rendered by the Royal Canadian Mounted
Police Remission Order

P.C. 2023-289 2023-03-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Treasury
Board and the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness,
under subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-25043_hq_1674656598){#fn_81000-25043_hq_1674656598-ID0EECAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-25043_hq_1674656699){#fn_81000-25043_hq_1674656699-ID0EBCAA},
remits the interest paid or payable on debt owed by a municipality for
the retroactive compensation increases for the period beginning on April
1, 2017 and ending on March 31, 2021, under its Municipal Police Service
Agreement.
