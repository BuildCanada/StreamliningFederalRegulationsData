## Order Accepting the Recommendation of the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness Concerning the Two-Year Review of the List set out in the Regulations Establishing a List of Entities {#order-accepting-the-recommendation-of-the-minister-of-public-safety-and-emergency-preparedness-concerning-the-two-year-review-of-the-list-set-out-in-the-regulations-establishing-a-list-of-entities .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2015-24
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 2015-03-25

Order Accepting the Recommendation of the Minister of Public Safety and
Emergency Preparedness Concerning the Two-Year Review of the List set
out in the Regulations Establishing a List of Entities

P.C. 2015-331 2015-03-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, on July 23, 2014, 12 years had elapsed since the establishment
of a list by the [Regulations Establishing a List of
Entities](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-284)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13196){#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13196-ID0EGDCA}
pursuant to subsection 83.05(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13197){#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13197-ID0EEDCA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13198){#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13198-ID0EBDCA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13196-ID0EGDCA){#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13196}SOR/2002-284
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13197-ID0EEDCA){#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13197}S.C.
  2005, c. 10, subpar. 34(1)(f)(iii)
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13198-ID0EBDCA){#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13198}R.S., c.
  C-46
  :::

And whereas, pursuant to subsection 83.05(9)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13199){#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13199-ID0EEBBA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13198){#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13198-ID0EBBBA},
the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness has reviewed
that list, as it existed on July 23, 2014, and has determined that there
are still reasonable grounds to believe that each entity listed on that
date has knowingly carried out, attempted to carry out, participated in
or facilitated a terrorist activity or is knowingly acting on behalf of,
at the direction of or in association with such an entity;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13199-ID0EEBBA){#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13199}S.C.
  2005, c. 10, s. 18(3)
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
Preparedness made pursuant to subsection 83.05(9)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13199){#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13199-ID0EEAAA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13198){#fn_81000-2-1514-E_hq_13198-ID0EBAAA},
accepts that each entity listed as of July 23, 2014 remain a listed
entity.
