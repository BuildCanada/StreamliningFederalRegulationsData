## [Non-duty-paid Packaged Alcohol]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702729 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraphs 88(2)(a)(iv), (c)(ii) and (d)(ii) of the [Excise Act,
2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), a person may possess non-duty-paid packaged
alcohol if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is authorized by an officer under section
  19 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) to transport non-duty-paid
  packaged alcohol that has been reported under section 12 of that Act
  and is acting in accordance with that authorization; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person has in their possession documentation that
  provides evidence that the person is transporting the alcohol on
  behalf of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an excise warehouse licensee,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a licensed user,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a registered user,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a duty free shop licensee,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any person, if the alcohol is designated as ships'
    stores under the [Ships' Stores
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-40), or

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} an accredited representative.

## [Non-duty-paid, Marked Special Container of Alcohol]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702739 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 88(3)(b) of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), a person
may possess a non-duty-paid marked special container of alcohol if the
person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is authorized by an officer under section 19 of the
  [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) to transport a non-duty-paid marked
  special container of alcohol reported under section 12 of that Act and
  is acting in accordance with that authorization; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} has in their possession documentation that provides
  evidence that the person is transporting the special container on
  behalf of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an excise warehouse licensee, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a registered user.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702745 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations take
effect on July 1, 2003.
