## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979184 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Fisheries
      Act](/eng/acts/F-14). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[angling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[angling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means fishing with a hook and
      line, with or without a rod. It does not include fishing with a
      set line. ([pêche à la ligne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 1
      of the [Pacific Fishery Management Area Regulations,
      2007](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-77). ([secteur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[artificial fly]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[artificial fly]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in non-tidal waters, a single-pointed hook that
        is dressed only with fur, feathers, hair, textiles, tinsel, wire
        or any combination of those materials and to which no external
        weight or external attracting device is attached; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in tidal waters, a hook, or two hooks tied in
        tandem, dressed only with fur, feathers, hair, textiles, tinsel,
        wire or any combination of those materials. ([mouche
        artificielle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a calendar
      day. ([jour]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou
      [journée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[fin fish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fin fish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all fish other than
      crustaceans, echinoderms, molluscs, shellfish and marine
      mammals. ([poisson à nageoires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[fly fishing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fly fishing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means angling with a line to
      which only an artificial fly is attached. ([pêche à la
      mouche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[foul hook]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[foul hook]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to hook a fish in any part
      of its body other than the mouth. ([casaquer]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[gill net]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[gill net]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 2 of the [Pacific Fishery Regulations,
      1993](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-54). ([filet
      maillant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[hatchery trout]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[hatchery trout]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a trout of any kind
      that has a healed scar in place of the adipose fin. ([truite
      d'élevage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[hatchery chinook salmon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[hatchery chinook salmon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a chinook
      salmon that has a healed scar in place of the adipose, pelvic or
      pectoral fin, or in place of a maxillary. ([saumon quinnat
      d'élevage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[hatchery coho salmon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[hatchery coho salmon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a coho salmon
      that has a healed scar in place of the adipose, pelvic or pectoral
      fin, or in place of a maxillary. ([saumon coho
      d'élevage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[kokanee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[kokanee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a land-locked sockeye
      salmon. ([kokani]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[management unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[management unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
      [[M.U.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area designated as a
      management unit in section 2 of the Management Unit Regulation
      made pursuant to the Province's Wildlife Act. ([zone
      d'aménagement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou
      [Z.A.]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[mesh size]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mesh size]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 2 of the [Pacific Fishery Regulations,
      1993](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-54). ([maillage]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[natural bait]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[natural bait]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any foodstuff or
      natural substance other than wood, cotton, wool, hair, fur or
      feathers. It does not include fin fish other than roe. ([appât
      naturel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[non-tidal waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[non-tidal waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} eans the waters of the
      Province other than tidal waters. ([eaux sans
      marée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[overall length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[overall length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} with respect to a fish,
      means the distance measured from the tip of the nose to the fork
      of the tail or, where there is no fork, to the tip of the
      tail. ([longueur totale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Province of British
      Columbia. ([province]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Region]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Region]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a region as described in
      section 3 of the Hunting Regulation made pursuant to the
      Province's Wildlife Act. ([Région]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[ring net]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[ring net]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 2 of the [Pacific Fishery Regulations,
      1993](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-54). ([bolinche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[salmon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[salmon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a chinook salmon, a chum
      salmon, a coho salmon, a pink salmon or a sockeye salmon other
      than a kokanee. ([saumon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[set line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[set line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a fishing line that is left
      unattended in the water. ([ligne fixe]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[spear fishing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[spear fishing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means fishing with a spear
      or an arrow that is propelled by a spring, an elastic band,
      compressed air or a bow or by hand. ([pêche au
      harpon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[sport fishing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[sport fishing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means fishing for
      recreational purposes. ([pêche sportive]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[steelhead]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[steelhead]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in waters where anadromous
      rainbow trout are found, means a rainbow trout that is greater
      than 50 cm in overall length. ([truite arc-en-ciel
      anadrome]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[stream]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[stream]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a watercourse formed by the
      perennial or intermittent flow of water between continuous,
      definable banks and includes a river. ([ruisseau]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Subarea]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Subarea]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
      1 of the [Pacific Fishery Management Area Regulations,
      2007](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-77). ([sous-secteur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[surfline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[surfline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 1 of the [Pacific Fishery Management Area Regulations,
      2007](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-77). ([ligne de
      démarcation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[tidal portion of the Fraser River]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[tidal portion of the Fraser River]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      the following Subareas or portions of Subareas:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Subarea 29-11 and Subareas 29-13 to 29-17;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the portion of Subarea 29-9 bounded on the west
        by a line from a fishing boundary sign near the entrance to
        Canoe Passage, thence to a fishing boundary sign on Westham
        Island, thence following the westerly shoreline of Westham
        Island and Reifel Island to Pelly Point on Reifel Island, thence
        to Garry Point on Lulu Island; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the portion of Subarea 29-12 bounded on the
        west by a line from the most southwesterly point of Sea Island,
        thence following the westerly shoreline of that island to the
        Iona Island Road Causeway, thence following that causeway and
        continuing in a straight line in a northerly direction to a
        fishing boundary sign on the north shore of Iona Island, thence
        in a straight line in a northerly direction to a fishing
        boundary sign on the mainland. ([partie à marée du fleuve
        Fraser]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[tidal waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[tidal waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the waters of the Areas
      set out in Schedule 2 to the [Pacific Fishery Management Area
      Regulations, 2007](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-77). ([eaux à
      marée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[trout]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[trout]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means brook trout, brown trout,
      cutthroat trout, Dolly Varden, lake trout, rainbow trout or
      steelhead. ([truite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[wild chinook salmon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[wild chinook salmon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a chinook salmon
      that is not a hatchery chinook salmon. ([saumon quinnat
      sauvage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[wild coho salmon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[wild coho salmon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a coho salmon that
      is not a hatchery coho salmon. ([saumon coho
      sauvage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[wild trout]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[wild trout]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a trout that is not a
      hatchery trout. ([truite sauvage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a period beginning on April 1
      and ending on March 31. ([année]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A reference in these Regulations to a species of fish

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A reference in these Regulations to a measurement of
  mesh size, depth, length or any other dimension of a net shall be
  construed as a reference to a measurement taken when the net is wet.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979232 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of sport fishing in the Canadian
  fisheries waters of the Pacific Ocean and of the Province.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of fishing
  in waters to which the [National Parks of Canada Fishing
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1120) apply.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of fishing
  in the Chilkoot Trail National Historic Site.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to fishing and related
  activities carried out under the authority of a licence issued under
  the [Aboriginal Communal Fishing Licences
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-332).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-225, s. 15
- SOR/2002-380, s. 2
:::

## [Gear Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979250 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), no person shall angle with more than one fishing
  line

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in any lake or stream; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for white sturgeon.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who is alone in a boat may angle with two
  fishing lines in a lake.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-545, s. 2
- SOR/2001-156, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No person shall angle with
a fishing line to which is attached a weight that is greater than one
kilogram.

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) to (4), no person shall angle with a fishing line
  to which more than one hook, artificial lure or artificial fly is
  attached.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person may angle in the tidal portion of the Fraser
  River with two hooks, artificial lures or artificial flies, attached
  to a fishing line.

- [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} No person shall angle in the tidal portion of the
  Fraser River with a fishing line to which a barbed hook is attached.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), a person may angle in
  tidal waters with any number of hooks attached to a fishing line if
  the hooks are used in combination to hold a single piece of bait.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person who is angling in tidal waters for herring,
  northern anchovy, Pacific sand lance or squid may use any number of
  hooks on a fishing line.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-156, s. 3(F)

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No person shall fish with a
dip net unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is hung on a frame that measures no more than
  90 cm in its greatest dimension;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the frame is attached to a handle; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the bottom of the dip net is closed to form a bag
  that is no longer than 1.5 times the greatest dimension of the frame.

## [Prohibited Methods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979270 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} use a light in any manner to attract fish, unless
    the light is submerged and attached to a fishing line within 1 m of
    the fish hook;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} catch or attempt to catch fish with snares; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} wilfully foul hook or attempt to foul hook any fish
    other than herring, northern anchovy, Pacific sand lance and squid.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person who accidentally foul hooks a fish in a
  lake or stream shall retain the fish.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-156, s. 4(F)
:::

## [Angling from a Vessel]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979279 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** No person shall angle
from a vessel that is equipped with a motor in the waters set out in
column I of an item of Schedule II situated in the M.U. set out in
column II of that item during the period set out in column III of that
item.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** No person shall angle
from a vessel in the waters set out in column I of an item of Schedule
III situated in the M.U. set out in column II of that item during the
period set out in column III of that item.

## [Possession]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979282 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall possess more fish of a species, other than halibut,
  except at the person's ordinary residence, than twice the daily quota
  for fish of that species prescribed by these Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall possess more than 3 halibut, except
  at the person's ordinary residence.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** No person on board a
vessel shall possess any halibut taken by sport fishing if there are any
other fish on board the vessel that are destined for sale, trade or
barter.

**[[14.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14.1 .sectionLabel}** No person shall
possess female Dungeness, red rock or king crab.

## [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Fishing for Fin Fish Other than Salmon in Non-Tidal Waters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979460 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-979461 .Subheading}

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to
fishing for fin fish, other than salmon, in non-tidal waters.

### [Gear]{.HTitleText2} {#h-979463 .Subheading}

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 53, no
person shall fish other than by angling or with a set line.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-156, s. 12
:::

### [Spears]{.HTitleText2} {#h-979467 .Subheading}

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** A person may fish with a
spear for burbot or fish of any species not listed in the table to
section 58 in Regions 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8.

### [Set Lines]{.HTitleText2} {#h-979469 .Subheading}

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** A person who is fishing
with a set line shall not

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} use more than one set line;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use a hook that is less than 3 cm measured from the
  point to the shank; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} retain fish of any species listed in the table to
  section 58 other than burbot.

### [Close Times]{.HTitleText2} {#h-979474 .Subheading}

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall fish for, or catch and retain, fish of any species, in a
  lake or stream, from 23:00 hrs to 24:00 hrs on December 31.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The close time established by subsection (1) is
  considered to be fixed separately and individually for each species of
  fish.

### [Daily Quotas]{.HTitleText2} {#h-979478 .Subheading}

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall catch and retain in a day more fish of a species set out
  in column I of Schedule VII, of an overall length set out in column
  II, than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the daily quota set out in column III or the daily
    catch limit under section 7 of the [Yukon Territory Fishery
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._854), in respect of fish
    caught in Atlin Lake, Bennett Lake, Laidlaw Lake, Morley Lake,
    Tagish Lake, Teslin Lake, or in Rancheria River, Swift River or
    their tributaries; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the daily quota set out in column III, in any other
    case.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who, in a day, has caught and retained the
  daily quota for steelhead in a lake or stream shall cease fishing in
  that lake or stream for the rest of the day.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-156, s. 13
:::

### [Aggregate Daily Quotas]{.HTitleText2} {#h-979486 .Subheading}

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** No person shall catch and
retain in a day from any lake or stream, more than

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} six wild rainbow trout and hatchery rainbow trout, in
  the aggregate;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} one wild steelhead and hatchery steelhead, in the
  aggregate;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} six wild cutthroat trout and hatchery cutthroat
  trout, in the aggregate;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} six wild brown trout and hatchery brown trout, in the
  aggregate;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} six wild brook trout and hatchery brook trout, in the
  aggregate;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} six wild Dolly Varden and hatchery Dolly Varden, in
  the aggregate;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} six wild lake trout and hatchery lake trout, in the
  aggregate;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} four smallmouth and largemouth bass, in the
  aggregate; or

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} 25 wild kokanee and hatchery kokanee, in the
  aggregate.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-380, s. 9(E)
:::

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** No person shall catch and
retain in a day a quantity of fish of a species set out in column I of
an item of the table to this section, from all non-tidal waters, that
exceeds the aggregate daily quota set out in column II of that item.

### [Monthly Quota for Steelhead]{.HTitleText2} {#h-979506 .Subheading}

**[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** No person shall catch and
retain in a month more than two steelhead in any lake or stream.

### [Annual Quotas]{.HTitleText2} {#h-979510 .Subheading}

**[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** No person shall catch and
retain in a year more than

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 10 steelhead;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} one white sturgeon;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} five rainbow trout greater than 50 cm in overall
  length from the part of Kootenay Lake east of a line between signs
  posted at Balfour Point and Proctor Lighthouse;

  length from Shuswap Lake, including Seymour Arm, Anstey Arm and Salmon
  Arm, Little Shuswap Lake, and the portion of South Thompson River
  (Little River) between those lakes and Mara Lake; or

  in overall length, in the aggregate, from Shuswap Lake, including
  Seymour Arm, Anstey Arm and Salmon Arm, Little Shuswap Lake, and the
  portion of South Thompson River (Little River) between those lakes and
  Mara Lake.
