## Safety of Sperm and Ova Regulations {#safety-of-sperm-and-ova-regulations .Title-of-Act}

:::

Registration 2019-06-10

Safety of Sperm and Ova Regulations

P.C. 2019-750 2019-06-09
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 66(1) of the [Assisted Human
Reproduction Act](/eng/acts/A-13.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81200-2-406_hq_24787){#fn_81200-2-406_hq_24787-ID0EDADA},
the Minister of Health has laid a copy of the proposed [Safety of Sperm
and Ova Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-192) before each House of
Parliament, substantially in the annexed form;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to section
65[[Footnote

of the [Assisted Human Reproduction Act](/eng/acts/A-13.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81200-2-406_hq_24787){#fn_81200-2-406_hq_24787-ID0EDACA},
makes the annexed [Safety of Sperm and Ova
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-192).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1142295 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[accident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[accident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an unexpected event that is
      not attributable to a deviation from the standard operating
      procedures or applicable laws, including these Regulations and
      that could compromise human health and safety or the safety of
      sperm or ova. ([accident]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of sperm or ova,
      means any of the following activities:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} processing, which means

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} performing the donor suitability assessment,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} obtaining the sperm or ova from a donor,

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} preparing,

        - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} identifying,

        - [(v)]{.lawlabel} testing,

        - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} preserving,

        - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} assessing quality,

        - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} labelling,

        - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} quarantining, or

        - [(x)]{.lawlabel} storing;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} distributing; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} importing. ([activité]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[adverse reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[adverse reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the unexpected
      presence of an infectious disease agent or the unexpected
      occurrence of an infectious disease in a recipient of sperm or ova
      or a child created from that sperm or those ova. ([effet
      indésirable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Directive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Directive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the document entitled
      Technical Requirements for Conducting the Suitability Assessment
      of Sperm and Ova Donors, published by the Department of Health, as
      amended from time to time. ([directive]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[donation code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[donation code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the unique group of
      numbers, letters, symbols or a combination of any of them that
      identifies the sperm or ova donation. ([code d'identification du
      don]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[donor identification code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[donor identification code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the unique
      group of numbers, letters, symbols or a combination of any of them
      that is assigned to a donor. ([code d'identification du
      donneur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[donor suitability assessment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[donor suitability assessment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      assessment of a donor that is based on the following:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} donor screening;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} physical examination of the donor; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} donor testing. ([évaluation de l'admissibilité
        du donneur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}) 

  [[error]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[error]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a deviation from the standard
      operating procedures or applicable laws, including these
      Regulations, that could compromise human health and safety or the
      safety of sperm or ova. ([manquement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person, partnership,
      unincorporated entity or a part of any of them that conducts an
      activity but only includes a health professional if the health
      professional conducts an activity that is not referred to in the
      definition [[health
      professional]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}. ([établissement]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[health professional]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[health professional]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is
      authorized under the laws of a province to make use of sperm or
      ova in that province and who

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} makes use of sperm or ova or distributes sperm
        to a recipient for their personal use;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prepares, quarantines, labels or stores sperm
        or ova for the purpose of their use by that person; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prepares, quarantines, labels or stores sperm
        for the purpose of its distribution by that person to a
        recipient for their personal use. ([professionnel de la
        santé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[human health and safety]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[human health and safety]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the health
      and safety of a recipient of sperm or ova or a child created from
      that sperm or those ova to the extent that their health and safety
      relate to the safety of the sperm or ova. ([santé et sécurité
      humaines]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[medical director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[medical director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a primary
      establishment, means a person who is authorized under the laws of
      the jurisdiction in which the primary establishment is situated to
      practise the profession of medicine and who is responsible for all
      medical and technical procedures carried out during the processing
      of sperm or ova. ([directeur médical]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[primary establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[primary establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      establishment that conducts all processing activities in respect
      of sperm or ova, whether it conducts them itself or another
      establishment conducts any of the activities on its
      behalf. ([établissement principal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[quarantine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[quarantine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to sperm and ova,
      means the quarantine described in subsection 28(2) conducted by an
      establishment or a health professional. ([mise en
      quarantaine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[standard operating procedures]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[standard operating procedures]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      component of a quality management system that comprises
      instructions that set out the processes applicable to the
      components of the system and to the activities carried out by an
      establishment. ([procédures opérationnelles
      normalisées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments to Directive

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The document referred to in the definition of
  [[Directive]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} is deemed to be amended for the
  purposes of these Regulations if the amendment is not inconsistent
  with the purpose of reducing risks to human health and safety.

## [General Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1142335 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Primary establishment --- conformity of
processing

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  primary establishment must ensure that sperm or ova are processed in
  accordance with these Regulations before distributing or making use of
  them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Primary establishment --- activities on
  its behalf

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The primary establishment must ensure that every
  establishment that conducts any processing on its behalf meets the
  requirements of these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment that imports

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
imports sperm or ova must ensure that the sperm or ova are processed by
a primary establishment that is registered in accordance with these
Regulations.

## [Registration and Notification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1142343 .Part}

### [Registration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1142344 .Subheading}

#### [Application, Issuance and Refusal]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1142345 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to register --- primary
establishment

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A primary establishment
that processes sperm or ova must be registered and may process sperm or
ova, subject to any change under paragraph 11(1)(a), only in accordance
with its registration.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  primary establishment must submit an application for registration to
  the Minister, in the form established by the Minister, that contains
  the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name, telephone number, email
    address, postal address and, if different from the postal address,
    civic address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an applicant that previously
    conducted its activities under another name, either under these
    Regulations or the [Processing and Distribution of Semen for
    Assisted Conception Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-254), that
    other name;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the first name, last name, telephone number and
    email address of a person to contact for further information
    concerning the application and, if different, a person to contact in
    case of emergency;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating whether the applicant
    proposes to process sperm or ova;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a list of the processing activities that the
    applicant proposes to conduct in each building and, if not already
    provided, the civic address of the respective buildings;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating whether the applicant
    proposes to have another establishment process sperm or ova on its
    behalf; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the name and civic address of any other
    establishment that the applicant proposes to have conduct any of the
    processing activities on its behalf, a list of the processing
    activities that are proposed to be conducted in each building and,
    if not already provided, the civic address of the respective
    buildings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signature and attestation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be signed and dated by a senior executive officer;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} include an attestation from that senior executive
    officer of the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the applicant has evidence demonstrating
      that it is able to meet the requirements of these Regulations,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that any other establishment that is proposed to
      process sperm or ova on its behalf is able to meet the
      requirements of these Regulations,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that all information submitted in support of
      the application is accurate and complete, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} that the senior executive officer has the
      authority to bind the applicant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional documents and information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The applicant must provide to the Minister, on or
  before the date specified in the Minister's written request to that
  effect, any documents or information that the Minister considers
  necessary to complete the Minister's review of the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration number

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** If the Minister determines,
after reviewing an application for registration, that the information
provided in the application is complete, the Minister must register the
primary establishment and issue a registration number.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may refuse to
register an applicant if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
  the applicant has submitted, in the application for registration,
  false, misleading, inaccurate or incomplete information;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has not complied with subsection 5(3)
  or the documents and information that the applicant has provided under
  subsection 5(3) are not sufficient to complete the review of the
  application; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
  registering the primary establishment could compromise human health
  and safety or the safety of sperm or ova.

#### [Amendments]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1142375 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments --- application

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  primary establishment that processes only one of sperm or ova and
  proposes to begin processing the other must, before doing so, submit
  an application to the Minister to amend its registration, in the form
  established by the Minister, that contains a description of the
  proposed amendment, as well as the information referred to in section
  5 that is relevant to the proposed amendment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signature and attestation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be signed and dated by a senior executive officer;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} include an attestation from that senior executive
    officer of the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the applicant has evidence demonstrating
      that it is able to meet the requirements of these Regulations,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that any other establishment that is proposed to
      process sperm or ova on its behalf is able to meet the
      requirements of these Regulations,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that all information submitted in support of
      the application is accurate and complete, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} that the senior executive officer has the
      authority to bind the applicant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional documents and information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The primary establishment must provide to the
  Minister, on or before the date specified in the Minister's written
  request to that effect, any documents or information that the Minister
  considers necessary to complete the Minister's review of the
  application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If the Minister determines,
after reviewing the application for the amendment to the registration,
that the information provided in that application is complete, the
Minister must amend the registration.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may refuse
to amend the registration of the primary establishment if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
  the primary establishment has submitted, in the application for
  amendment, false, misleading, inaccurate or incomplete information;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the primary establishment has not complied with
  subsection 8(3) or the documents and information that it has provided
  under subsection 8(3) are not sufficient to complete the review of the
  application; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
  the amendment of the registration could compromise human health and
  safety or the safety of sperm or ova.

#### [Changes or Cessation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1142396 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  primary establishment must notify the Minister in writing, in the form
  established by the Minister, within 30 days after the day on which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is any change to the information provided in
    the application for registration --- other than a change that is the
    subject of an application for an amendment to the registration ---
    including the cessation of all activities with respect to either
    sperm or ova if the registration is for both sperm and ova; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the primary establishment has ceased all of its
    activities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the primary establishment's name, telephone number,
    email address, postal address and, if different from the postal
    address, civic address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the primary establishment's registration number;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the change or cessation became
    effective; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of cessation, details of the
    disposition of the sperm or ova that are in the possession or
    control of the primary establishment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signature and attestation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be signed and dated by a senior executive officer;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} include an attestation from that senior executive
    officer of the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that if the primary establishment has not ceased
      all of its activities, it has evidence demonstrating that it meets
      the requirements of these Regulations,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that if the primary establishment has not ceased
      all of its activities, any other establishment that processes
      sperm or ova or is proposed to process sperm or ova on its behalf
      meets the requirements of these Regulations,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that all information submitted in support of
      the notice is accurate and complete, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} that the senior executive officer has the
      authority to bind the primary establishment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Update to registration

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must update
the registration to reflect the notice.

#### [Suspension]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1142418 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension without notice

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may suspend, in whole or in part, without prior notice, a
  primary establishment's registration, if the Minister has reasonable
  grounds to believe that human health and safety or the safety of the
  sperm or ova has been or could be compromised.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister suspends a registration, the Minister
  must send a notice to the primary establishment that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} gives the reasons for the suspension and its
    effective date;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} indicates that the primary establishment has an
    opportunity to be heard; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, indicates that corrective action
    must be taken by the primary establishment and the date by which it
    must do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action following suspension of
  registration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On the suspension of its registration, the primary
  establishment must immediately notify every establishment, health
  professional or recipient to which it has distributed the implicated
  sperm or ova during the period specified in the notice of the reasons
  for its suspension, the effective date of the suspension and the parts
  of the registration that are the subject of the suspension.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action upon notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An establishment that has been notified under
  subsection (3) or under this subsection must immediately notify to the
  same effect any establishment, health professional or recipient to
  which it distributed the implicated sperm or ova.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written notice

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister or the establishment gives a notice
  verbally under this section, that notice must be confirmed in writing
  as soon as feasible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reinstatement of registration

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must reinstate a registration, in whole or in part, if the
  primary establishment makes a request to the Minister, in the form
  established by the Minister, and provides evidence that demonstrates
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the primary establishment has corrected the
    situation that gave rise to the suspension; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the situation that gave rise to the suspension did
    not exist.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The reinstatement takes effect immediately after the
  Minister sends to the primary establishment a notice to that effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- compliance history

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may refuse to reinstate a primary
  establishment's registration if its compliance history demonstrates an
  inability to consistently conduct its activities in accordance with
  these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Partial reinstatement

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister does not reinstate any part of a
  registration that was suspended, the Minister must remove that part of
  the registration.

#### [Cancellation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1142444 .Subheading}

##### [Cancellation Initiated by Primary Establishment]{.HTitleText4} {#h-1142445 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of activities

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must cancel
a registration if the Minister receives a notice under section 11 that
the primary establishment has ceased carrying out all of the activities
that are the subject of its registration.

##### [Cancellation Initiated by Minister]{.HTitleText4} {#h-1142448 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Circumstances

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may cancel a registration in any of the following
  circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the primary establishment has not provided the
    annual attestation that is required under section 20;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the primary establishment has not complied with the
    requirements set out in section 21 to provide additional documents
    or information;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any information provided by the primary
    establishment to the Minister in accordance with these Regulations
    proves to be false or misleading;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the primary establishment fails to take corrective
    action, within the required period, in accordance with
    subsection (2) or paragraph 13(2)(c);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the corrective action that was taken by the primary
    establishment in accordance with subsection (2) or paragraph
    13(2)(c) has not corrected the situation that gave rise to a notice
    of suspension or cancellation of the registration;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the registration has been suspended for a period of
    more than 12 months;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the primary establishment does not meet the requirements of these
    Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before cancelling a registration, the Minister must
  send to the primary establishment a notice that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} gives the reasons for the proposed cancellation and
    its effective date;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} indicates that the primary establishment has an
    opportunity to be heard; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, indicates that corrective action
    must be taken by the primary establishment and the date by which it
    must do so.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action following cancellation of
registration

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  registration is cancelled under section 16, the primary establishment
  must immediately take the following action:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cease carrying out all of the activities that are
    the subject of its registration; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify any establishment, health professional or
    recipient to which it has distributed the implicated sperm or ova
    during the period specified in the notice of the cancellation and
    the effective date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action upon notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An establishment that has been notified under
  paragraph (1)(b) or under this subsection must, in turn, immediately
  notify to the same effect any establishment, health professional or
  recipient to which it distributed the implicated sperm or ova.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an establishment gives a notice verbally under
  this section, that notice must be confirmed in writing within 24 hours
  after it is given.

### [Notification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1142473 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice before distribution or importation

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  distributing or importing sperm or ova, an establishment must send to
  the Minister a notice, in the form established by the Minister, that
  contains the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the establishment's name, telephone number, email
    address, postal address and, if different from the postal address,
    civic address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an establishment that previously
    conducted its activities under another name, either under these
    Regulations or the [Processing and Distribution of Semen for
    Assisted Conception Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-254), that
    other name;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the first name, last name, telephone number and
    email address of a person to contact for further information
    concerning the notice and, if different, a person to contact in case

## [Donor Suitability]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1142519 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Donor testing

set out in the Directive under the heading "Donor Testing".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Donor reassessment

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** In order to determine

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of donor suitability

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Donor unsuitability

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A primary establishment must ensure that its medical
  director determines a donor to be unsuitable if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the donor meets any criteria set out in the
    Directive under the heading "Donor Exclusion"; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the donor suitability assessment is not complete.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summary document

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a donor has been determined to be suitable, the
  primary establishment must ensure that its medical director creates
  and signs a summary document that confirms this determination and that
  contains

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the age of the donor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the donor suitability assessment
    and, if applicable, donor reassessment have been conducted in
    accordance with these Regulations; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the dates and results of the donor testing and the
    assessment of the risk of genetic disease transmission.

#### [Quarantine]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1142544 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment must quarantine all sperm and ova that it processes in
  the manner set out in subsection (2) until the medical director of the
  primary establishment that is responsible for the quarantine of that
  sperm and those ova has

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determined the donor to be suitable; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determined and documented that the sperm and ova
    can be released from quarantine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Segregation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The establishment must quarantine the sperm and ova
  by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} clearly indicating that they are quarantined;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} segregating them from sperm and ova that are not
    quarantined; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ensuring that they are not distributed or used.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Release from quarantine --- exceptional
access

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph 28(1)(a), an
  establishment may release sperm or ova from quarantine if the primary
  establishment that is responsible for their quarantine receives a
  request from a health professional for exceptional access to that
  sperm or those ova and if one of the following conditions is met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the recipient has previously been exposed to sperm
    or ova from that donor and the risk profile of the requested sperm
    or ova, based on the results of any part of the donor suitability
    assessment, is at least equivalent to the risk profile of the sperm
    or ova to which the recipient has previously been exposed, based on
    the results of any of the donor suitability assessment that was
    conducted at that time; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sperm or ova from that donor have previously been
    used to create a child for an individual or a couple and the
    requested sperm or ova are to be used for the purpose of creating
    another child for that individual or couple.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summary document

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before the sperm or ova are released from quarantine,
  the primary establishment must ensure that its medical director
  creates and signs a summary document that contains the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the age of the donor, if known;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the conditions that have been met;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the dates and results of any donor screening,
    physical examination or donor testing; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the reasons the donor was determined to be
    unsuitable and a detailed explanation for each reason.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An establishment and a health professional must
  ensure that sperm or ova that are in their possession or control and
  are intended for exceptional access are segregated from sperm and ova
  that are not intended for exceptional access.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication of risk

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A health professional must meet the following
  requirements before making use of the sperm or ova or distributing the
  sperm to a recipient for their personal use:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} create a document that states that, based on the
    summary document and any risk mitigating measures with respect to
    that sperm or those ova, in their medical opinion, the use of the
    sperm or ova would not pose a serious risk to human health and
    safety; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} create a document that states that the health
    professional has informed the recipient of the risks that the use of
    the sperm or ova could pose to human health and safety and that the
    health professional has obtained written consent from the recipient.

## [Quality Management]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1142626 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Risk reduction

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
conducts an activity must do so in such a way as to reduce the risks to
human health and safety and the safety of sperm or ova by having
appropriate quality management measures, including the taking of
measures

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to prevent contamination or cross-contamination;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to prevent the transmission of an infectious disease;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to maintain the quality of the sperm or ova.

### [Quality Management System]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1142632 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Organizational structure

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** An establishment must
have an organizational structure that sets out the responsibility of
management for all activities that it conducts and all measures that it
takes in order to meet the requirements related to quality management.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Components of system

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** An establishment must,
with respect to the activities that it conducts and the measures that it
takes in order to meet the requirements related to quality management,
establish and maintain a quality management system that includes the
following components and must name an individual to be responsible for
that system:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} standard operating procedures;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a process control program that includes a system for
  verifying and validating any change to a process;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a system that allows for process improvement and that
  includes complaint monitoring and the implementation of corrective and
  preventative actions including recalls; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a document control and records management system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standard operating procedures

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  standard operating procedures must meet the following requirements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are in a standardized format;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they are approved by the individual responsible for
    the quality management system;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} they are easily accessible at each location where
    the relevant activities are conducted;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all changes to them are approved by the individual
    responsible for the quality management system before they are
    implemented; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} they are kept up-to-date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of procedures

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An establishment must review its standard operating
  procedures every two years or after either of the following events and
  every two years after that event:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} following any amendment to these Regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when a deficiency in the standard operating
    procedures is revealed as a result of an investigation into an
    error, accident or adverse reaction or as a result of an internal
    audit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Internal audit

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** An establishment must
establish and maintain an internal audit system for quality management
purposes and must carry out an internal audit every two years of the
activities that it conducts to ensure that those activities comply with
these Regulations and with its standard operating procedures, to be
carried out by a person who is qualified to do so and who does not have
direct responsibility for the activities being audited.

  occurred during the processing, distributing or importing of sperm or

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine the donor identification codes and
    donation codes of the implicated sperm or ova;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} quarantine any implicated sperm or ova that are in
    their possession or control;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} notify the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the establishment from which they received the
      implicated sperm or ova, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an establishment, every
      establishment, health professional or recipient to which it
      distributed the implicated sperm or ova; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a primary establishment that has
    reasonable grounds to believe that the error or accident occurred
    during the processing of sperm and ova conducted on its behalf,
    initiate an investigation into the suspected error or accident.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the donor identification code and the donation code
    associated with the implicated sperm or ova; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reason for the belief that an error or accident
    has occurred.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action upon notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An establishment or health professional that is
  notified under subparagraph (1)(c)(ii) or under this subsection must
  immediately

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} quarantine all implicated sperm or ova in its
    possession or control; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an establishment, notify to the same
    effect every establishment, health professional and recipient to
    which it distributed the implicated sperm or ova.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If an establishment or a health professional gives a
  notice verbally under this section, that notice must be confirmed in
  writing within 24 hours after it is given.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} quarantine any implicated sperm or ova that are in

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), initiate an

safety of the implicated sperm or ova is not compromised.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Release from quarantine --- exceptional

- **[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite section 65, an establishment

  the establishment or health professional that is responsible for their
  quarantine receives a request from a health professional for
  exceptional access to that sperm or those ova and if one of the
  following conditions is met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the recipient has previously been exposed to sperm
    or ova from that donor and the risk profile of the requested sperm
    or ova, based on the results of any part of the donor suitability
    assessment is at least equivalent to the risk profile of the sperm
    or ova to which the recipient has previously been exposed, based on
    the results of any part of the donor suitability assessment that was
    conducted at that time; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sperm or ova from that donor have previously been
    used to create a child for an individual or a couple and the
    requested sperm or ova are to be used for the purpose of creating
    another child for that individual or couple.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before the sperm or ova are released from quarantine,
  the establishment or health professional that is responsible for the
  quarantine must create and sign a summary document that contains the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the age of the donor, if known;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the condition that has been met;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the dates and results of any donor screening,
    physical examination or donor testing; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the suspected error or accident
    and an explanation of how human health and safety or the safety of
    the sperm or ova might have been compromised.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An establishment and a health professional must
  ensure that sperm or ova that are in their possession or control and
  are intended for exceptional access are segregated from sperm and ova
  that are not intended for exceptional access.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication of risk

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A health professional must meet the following
  requirements before making use of the sperm or ova or distributing the
  sperm to a recipient for their personal use:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} create a document that states that, based on the
    summary document and any risk mitigating measures with respect to
    that sperm or those ova, in their medical opinion, the use of the
    sperm or ova would not pose a serious risk to human health and
    safety; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} create a document that states that the health
    professional has informed the recipient of the risks that the use of
    the sperm or ova could pose to human health and safety and that the
    health professional has obtained written consent from the recipient.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preliminary and interim reports

**[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** The establishment or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 15 days after the start of the investigation

  interim report that contains

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any new information with respect to the suspected
    error or accident,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the progress of the investigation, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any measures taken during those 15 days to
    mitigate further risk.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final report

error or accident that could lead to an adverse reaction must send,

to the Minister, in the form established by the Minister, that contains
the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the results of the investigation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any corrective action taken; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} details concerning the disposition of the implicated
  sperm or ova.

### [Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1142866 .Subheading}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An establishment must keep records containing records

  the establishment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period --- standard operating
  procedures

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An establishment must keep a copy of every version of

  the purpose for which the authorization is granted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immediate container

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before distributing or making use of the sperm, an
  establishment and a health professional must ensure that the
  identification code, within the meaning of the [Processing and
  Distribution of Semen for Assisted Conception
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-254), appears in a clear and
  indelible manner on the label of the immediate container.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documentation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Before distributing or making use of the sperm, an
  establishment and a health professional must ensure that the immediate
  container of sperm is accompanied by documentation that contains the
  following information in English or French:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the donation code;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and business address of the processor
    within the meaning of the [Processing and Distribution of Semen for
    Assisted Conception Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-254);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date of the donation, the tests performed in
    respect of the donor, the dates and results of the tests and, if
    necessary, an interpretation of the results; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the special access authorization, if any.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

**[[91]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91 .sectionLabel}** An establishment and a
health professional must keep records of all documents and information
as required under the [Processing and Distribution of Semen for Assisted
Conception Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-254), in respect of each
immediate container of sperm, unless otherwise required by these
Regulations, for a period of 10 years after the day on which they
distribute, make use of or effect the disposition of the sperm.
