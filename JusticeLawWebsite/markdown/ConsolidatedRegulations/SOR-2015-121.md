## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-816546 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Fisheries
    Act](/eng/acts/F-14). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[aquatic invasive species]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aquatic invasive species]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a species set
    out in Part 2 or 3 of the schedule. ([espèce aquatique
    envahissante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[indigenous]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[indigenous]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an aquatic
    species, means that the species originated naturally in a particular
    region or body of water. ([indigène]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-816552 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of aquatic invasive species

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 43(3) of the Act, the list of aquatic invasive species is the
list set out in Parts 2 and 3 of the schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Estimate of costs

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Before publishing proposed
regulations made under subsection 43(3) of the Act, the Minister must
provide the President of the Treasury Board with an estimate of the
costs of their implementation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of species

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A reference in these
Regulations to an aquatic invasive species or family of aquatic invasive
species by its common name as set out in column 1 in Part 2 or 3 of the
schedule is to be read as a reference to the scientific name of that
species or family of species that is set out in column 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency with other regulations

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In the event of an
inconsistency between these Regulations and any other regulations made
under the Act, these Regulations prevail to the extent of the
inconsistency.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-816561 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition against importation

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person to import members of a species set out in Part 2 of the schedule,
including any genetic material capable of propagating the species, into
the applicable area set out in column 4 of that Part, unless the member
is in the condition, if any, set out in column 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition against possession

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person to possess members of a species set out in Part 2 of the
schedule, including any genetic material capable of propagating the
species, within the applicable area set out in column 5 of that Part,
unless the member is in the condition, if any, set out in column 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition against transportation

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person to transport members of a species set out in Part 2 of the
schedule, including any genetic material capable of propagating the
species, within the applicable area set out in column 6 of that Part,
unless the member is in the condition, if any, set out in column 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition against release

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person to release, or engage in any activity that may lead to the
release of, members of a species set out in Part 2 of the schedule,
including any genetic material capable of propagating the species, into
a body of water frequented by fish within the applicable area set out in
column 7 of that Part, unless the member is in the condition, if any,
set out in column 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition against introduction of
non-indigenous species

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person to introduce an aquatic species into a particular region or body
of water frequented by fish where it is not indigenous unless authorized
to do so under federal or provincial law.

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-816572 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption for certain persons

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Sections 6 to 9 do not
apply to fishery officers or fishery guardians carrying out their duties
under these Regulations or to any other person acting under their
direction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption for emergencies

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Sections 6 to 10 do not
apply to persons operating any vehicles, vessels or aircraft engaged in
emergency, search and rescue or firefighting operations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption for authorized purposes

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  purpose of the importation, possession, transportation or release of
  members of a species set out in Part 2 of the schedule is for
  scientific, educational or aquatic invasive species control purposes,
  sections 6 to 9 do not apply

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to employees or any other person acting under the
    direction of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an educational institution,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a research facility,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a zoo or aquarium, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a federal or provincial department with a
      mandate to manage or control aquatic invasive species; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to persons engaged in the activities authorized
    under subsection 19(3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permit, licence or authorization
  required

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The employees or persons exempted under subparagraphs
  (1)(a)(i) to (iii) must hold the applicable permits, licences and
  authorizations issued under the following provisions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} section 6.1 of the [Manitoba Fishery Regulations,
    1987](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-509);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 19 of the [Quebec Fishery Regulations,
    1990](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-214);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sections 52 and 56 of the [Fishery (General)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-53);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} subsection 9(3) of the [Saskatchewan Fishery
    Regulations, 1995](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-233);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} subsection 6(2) of the [Ontario Fishery
    Regulations, 2007](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-237);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} sections 34.1 and 34.2 of Fish Licensing of
    Ontario, O. Reg. 664/98;

    of Manitoba, Man. Reg. 124/97;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} subsection 12(1) of the Fisheries (Alberta) Act of
    Alberta, R.S.A. 2000, c. F-16;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} section 86 of the Wild Life Regulations of
    Newfoundland and Labrador, C.N.L.R. 1156/96;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} all applicable provisions in regulations made under
    the [Canada National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption for licence holder

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Section 7 does not apply
to a person holding a fishing licence who has caught a member of a
species set out in Part 2 of the schedule if that person takes immediate
measures to destroy the member in a manner that ensures that it and any
genetic material capable of propagating the species cannot survive.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- certain diploid grass carp

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Section 7 does not apply, in respect
  of the possession of diploid grass carp, to persons employed by the
  Aquaculture Centre of Excellence at Lethbridge College and Alberta
  Agriculture and Rural Development, or persons under their direction,
  if they hold a fish research licence issued under subsection 12(1) of
  the Fisheries (Alberta) Act of Alberta and if the diploid grass carp
  are possessed for the purpose of culturing triploid grass carp.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- triploid grass carp

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 7 and 8 do not apply, in respect of the
  possession and transportation of triploid grass carp, to persons
  holding a cultured fish licence issued under subsection 12(1) of the
  Fisheries (Alberta) Act of Alberta.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption for holder of cultured fish
licence

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Section 9 does not apply
to persons holding a cultured fish licence issued under subsection 12(1)
of the Fisheries (Alberta) Act of Alberta in respect of the release of
grass carp --- that have been confirmed as triploid grass carp --- for
the purpose of vegetation control, in a body of water on privately owned
land that is isolated from other bodies of water in a manner that
ensures that the grass carp will not adversely affect --- in any other
body of water --- other fish, fish habitat or the use of fish.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption if otherwise regulated

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections 6 to 10 and directions
  given under subsections 22(2), 26(1) and 27(1), do not apply

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of ballast water and sediments that are
    carried on board a vessel designed or constructed to carry ballast
    water, to the persons referred to in subsection 4(1) of the [Ballast
    Water Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-120); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the biofouling of a vessel that is
    over 24 m in length, to the person in charge of the vessel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption for some vessels

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A fishery officer or fishery guardian must not take
  the measures referred to in subsection 25(1) in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ballast water and sediments that are carried on
    board a vessel designed or constructed to carry ballast water; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the biofouling of a vessel that is over 24 m in
    length.

- Definition of [[biofouling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsections (1) and (2),
  [[biofouling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the accumulation of aquatic
  organisms such as micro-organisms, plants and animals on surfaces and
  structures that are immersed in or exposed to the aquatic environment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions of [[ballast
  water]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[sediments]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsections (1) and (2), [[ballast
  water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[sediments]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have
  the same meaning as in subsection 1(1) of the [Ballast Water
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-120).

## [Compliance and Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-816632 .Part}

### [Control and Eradication]{.HTitleText2} {#h-816633 .Subheading}

Definition of [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of this section and sections 27 and 28,
  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the persons listed in
  section 18.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purposes

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may take a measure referred to in
  subsection (3)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to prevent the introduction or spread of, or to
    control or eradicate,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any species set out in Part 2 of the schedule in
      the areas in which it is prohibited,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any species set out in Part 2 or 3 of the
      schedule in a particular region or body of water frequented by
      fish where the species is not indigenous and may harm fish, fish
      habitat or the use of fish, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any aquatic species, other than a species set
      out in Part 2 or 3 of the schedule, in a particular region or body
      of water frequented by fish where the aquatic species is not
      indigenous and may harm fish, fish habitat or the use of fish; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to treat or destroy any member of a species
    described in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control activities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite any other regulations made under the Act, the
  Minister may authorize the deposit of deleterious substances belonging
  to one of the classes of deleterious substances authorized under
  section 21 and provide directions for their deposit in any water or
  place referred to in subsection 36(3) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption for aquaculture activities

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (3) does not apply in respect of
  aquaculture activities if the deposit of a deleterious substance is
  otherwise authorized under the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licensed fishing for aquatic invasive
species

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Despite any other
regulations made under the Act, the Minister and any provincial minister
having jurisdiction over fisheries may license fishing for any species
referred to in subsection 19(2) in the places referred to in that
subsection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized deleterious substances

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The following classes of
deleterious substances are authorized for the purpose of paragraph
36(4)(b) of the Act and subsections 19(3) and 27(1):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} drugs whose sale is permitted or otherwise
  authorized, or whose importation is not prohibited, under the [Food
  and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pest control products that are registered, or whose
  use is authorized, under the [Pest Control Products
  Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01).

### [Measures and Directions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-816654 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification regarding non-indigenous
species

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  fishery officer or fishery guardian may notify a person, directly or
  through a public notice, that an aquatic species is not indigenous in
  a particular region or body of water frequented by fish.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions to stop introduction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event that the unauthorized introduction of an
  aquatic species into a particular region or body of water frequented
  by fish where it is not indigenous is imminent or in the process of
  occurring, a fishery officer may give a direction to a person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prohibiting that person from engaging in any
    activity that may lead to that introduction; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} directing that person to cease engaging in any
    activity that may lead to that introduction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in sections 25 to 27 and 29.

[[carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means anything, other than a
    conveyance or structure, that is a host to, or that facilitates the
    movement of, a species set out in Part 2 or 3 of the
    schedule. ([porteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[conveyance or structure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[conveyance or structure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a conveyance
    or structure that is a host to, or that facilitates the movement of,
    a species set out in Part 2 or 3 of the schedule. ([moyen de
    transport ou structure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitations

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A fishery officer or
fishery guardian may take a measure set out in section 25 or give a
direction set out in section 26 only

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to determine whether the species is an aquatic
  invasive species;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to prevent the introduction or spread of, or to
  control or eradicate,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any species set out in Part 2 of the schedule in
    the areas in which it is prohibited, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any species set out in Part 2 or 3 of the schedule
    in a particular region or body of water frequented by fish where the
    species is not indigenous and may harm fish, fish habitat or the use
    of fish; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to treat or destroy any member of a species described
  in paragraph (b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  fishery officer or fishery guardian or a person acting under their
  direction may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} treat or destroy a member of an aquatic invasive
    species or a carrier or treat a conveyance or structure;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish a temporary barrier around the member of
    an aquatic invasive species or the carrier, conveyance or structure;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} post signs or markers that prohibit access around
    the member of an aquatic invasive species or the carrier, conveyance
    or structure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasonable assistance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person must give all reasonable assistance
  requested by the fishery officer or the fishery guardian to enable
  them to carry out the measures set out in subsection (1) and must
  provide any information requested by them that is relevant to the
  measures if the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is in possession of a member of a species set out
    in Part 2 or 3 of the schedule;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is in possession or in charge of a carrier,
    conveyance or structure or owns or occupies the land, building or
    place where the member of the species is found; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is engaged in any activity that may lead or has led
    to the introduction or spread of the species.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a fishery
  officer may give a written direction requiring a person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to restrict any activity that may lead to the
    introduction or spread of an aquatic invasive species;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to restrict access to a place where a member of the
    species is found;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to engage in any activity to prevent the
    introduction or spread of the species; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to engage in any activity to treat or destroy the
    member of the species or a carrier or to treat a conveyance or
    structure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons subject to direction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The direction may be given only to a person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is in possession of a member of the species or of a
    carrier, conveyance or structure where a member of the species is
    found;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is in charge of a carrier, conveyance or structure
    where a member of the species is found;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} owns or occupies the land, building or place where
    a member of the species is found; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is engaged in any activity that may lead or has led
    to the introduction or spread of the species.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from fishing licence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a direction given under paragraph (1)(d) requires
  the use of fishing as a means to destroy members of a species set out
  in Part 2 or 3 of the schedule, the person to whom the direction is
  given is exempted from the requirement to hold a licence to fish for
  that species.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption while under direction

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a direction given under subsection (1) requires a
  person to be in possession of or to transport a member of a species
  set out in Part 2 of the schedule, that person is exempted from
  sections 7 and 8 to the extent necessary to fulfill the requirements
  of the direction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of deleterious substance

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may give a written direction requiring the deposit of a
  deleterious substance belonging to one of the classes of deleterious
  substances authorized under section 21

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to prevent the introduction or spread of, or to
    control or eradicate,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any species set out in Part 2 of the schedule in
      the areas in which it is prohibited, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any species set out in Part 2 or 3 of the
      schedule in a particular region or body of water frequented by
      fish where the species is not indigenous and may harm fish, fish
      habitat or the use of fish; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to treat or destroy any member of a species
    described in paragraph (a) or a carrier or to treat a conveyance or
    structure where a member of the species is found.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons subject to direction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The direction may be given only to a person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is in possession of a member of the species or of a
    carrier, conveyance or structure where a member of the species is
    found;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is in charge of a carrier, conveyance or structure
    where a member of the species is found;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} owns or occupies the land, building or place where
    a member of the species is found; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is engaged in any activity that may lead or has led
    to the introduction or spread of the species.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption while under direction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a direction given under subsection (1) requires a
  person to be in possession of or to transport a member of a species
  set out in Part 2 of the schedule, that person is exempted from
  sections 7 and 8 to the extent necessary to fulfill the requirements
  of the direction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  measures set out in subsections 19(2) and (3) and 25(1) and the
  directions given in accordance with subsections 22(2), 26(1) and 27(1)
  must not be taken or given unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the measure or direction does not compromise public
    safety;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a measure or direction affecting a
    vessel, the measure or direction is taken or given only to the
    extent that it does not compromise the safety of the vessel or the
    persons on board and, in the case of vessels over 24 m in length, if
    the Minister, the fishery officer or the fishery guardian has
    consulted the Minister of Transport or a marine safety inspector
    before taking the measure or giving the direction; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a non-emergency situation in which
    the measure or direction may have a significant impact on activities
    under the jurisdiction of a department or agency of the Government
    of Canada or the government of any province or municipality, the
    Minister, the fishery officer or the fishery guardian has notified
    that department, agency or government.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deleterious substance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a measure or direction referred to in
  subsection (1) involves the deposit of a deleterious substance,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} alternative measures and the impact of the deposit
    on fish, fish habitat or the use of fish must be taken into account;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the deposit must be authorized by a person
    prescribed under section 18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of directions

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Directions given under subsection
  26(1) or 27(1) must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} describe the member of the aquatic invasive species
    and the carrier, conveyance or structure, if there is one, that is
    the subject of the direction;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} briefly describe the reasons for the direction;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set out the time period in respect of which the
    direction applies, up to a maximum of 15 days with an extension of
    up to a maximum of 90 days;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} describe the requirements set out in subsection
    26(1) or 27(1); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be delivered in person or, if that is not possible,
    be posted in a conspicuous place at or near the location of the
    member of the species or of the carrier, conveyance or structure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Directions given under subsection 27(1) must also

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out the place and manner in which the member of
    the species or the carrier, conveyance or structure, if there is
    one, must be treated or destroyed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out the date by which the requirement must be
    satisfied; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state the terms and conditions applicable to the
    deposit of the deleterious substance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person to whom a direction is given under subsection 22(2), 26(1) or
  27(1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} must follow the requirements specified in the
    direction; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is prohibited from engaging in any activity
    contrary to the requirements specified in the direction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for any person to enter an area

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} around which the temporary barrier referred to in
    paragraph 25(1)(b) has been established; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} around which the signs or markers referred to in
    paragraph 25(1)(c) have been posted.
