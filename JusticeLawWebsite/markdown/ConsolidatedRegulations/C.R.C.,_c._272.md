## Saskatchewan Hog Marketing (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#saskatchewan-hog-marketing-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 272
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Regulations Respecting the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade
of Hogs Produced in Saskatchewan
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537580 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Saskatchewan Hog Marketing (Interprovincial and Export)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._272).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537582 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Natural Products Marketing
    Act, 1972 of Saskatchewan; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[assembly yard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assembly yard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means premises to which
    producers may deliver their hogs; ([parc de
    groupage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan Hog
    Marketing Commission established pursuant to the Act and the Plan;
    ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any member of the species [Sus
    Scrofa L.]{.otherLang lang="la"} (domestic pig) produced in
    Saskatchewan; ([porc]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[order buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[order buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person buying sows at
    a public auction in an assembly yard for the purpose of reselling to
    or on behalf of another person; ([courtier en
    truies]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan Hog Marketing
    Plan established and amended from time to time pursuant to the Act;
    ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person engaged in the
    business of processing hogs or using hogs in manufacturing;
    ([transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person engaged in producing or feeding hogs
      and making them available for slaughter and includes the employer
      of any such person,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person who, under any lease or agreement, is
      entitled to a share of the hogs produced or fed by a person
      referred to in paragraph (a), and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person who takes possession of any hogs from
      any person under any form of security for a debt;
      ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[small processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[small processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a processor who, in
    the previous calendar year, processed less than two per cent of all
    hogs slaughtered in Saskatchewan according to the reports published
    by the Department of Agriculture of Canada. ([petit
    transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537596 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of hogs in interprovincial and export trade and to
persons and property situated in the Province of Saskatchewan.

## [Purchase and Sale]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537598 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to sections 5 to 8, no producer shall sell or offer to sell hogs to
  any person other than the Commission.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 5 to 8, no person other than the
  Commission shall purchase hogs from a producer.

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537602 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  producer may sell hogs directly to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a small processor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a consumer for consumption by the consumer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} another producer; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a processor designated by the Commission to provide
    specific research data.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The persons referred to in paragraphs (1)(a) to (d)
  may purchase hogs directly from a producer.

<!-- -->

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  producer may sell sows to any order buyer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An order buyer may purchase sows from a producer.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The following classes of
hogs shall be exempt from these Regulations:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} breeding stock;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} weanling pigs;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} feeder pigs; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} boars.

<!-- -->

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  producer may market hogs in the interprovincial trade, otherwise than
  through the Commission, if he

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} holds a dispensation certificate; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} does so through the hog marketing system, if any,
    in the province where the hogs are marketed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A dispensation certificate may be issued by the
  Commission to any producer who applies therefor

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the producer has obtained prior approval
    therefor from the hog marketing authority, if any, in the province
    where the hogs are to be marketed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a period expiring on December 31st next
    following the day of issue; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} without charge.

## [Payment to Producers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537626 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A producer selling hogs to
the Commission is entitled to receive, immediately after the slaughter
of the hogs,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the Commission conducts a pool for the
  distribution of the proceeds of the sale of the hogs,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an initial payment in an amount determined by the
    Commission, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} payments, in addition to the payment referred to
    in subparagraph (i), in such amounts and at such times as the
    Commission determines, until all the proceeds of the sale of the
    hogs have been paid to the producers entitled thereto, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the Commission does not conduct a pool referred
  to in paragraph (a), a payment not exceeding the price obtained by the
  Commission for the sale of the hogs,

according to the quantity and taking into account the quality, variety,
grade, class or size of hogs delivered by him, less the disbursements
and expenses incurred by the Commission in respect of the sales.
