## Order Designating the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology as Minister for Purposes of the National Research Council Act and as Appropriate Minister with Respect to the National Research Council of Canada for Purposes of the Financial Administration Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-industry-science-and-technology-as-minister-for-purposes-of-the-national-research-council-act-and-as-appropriate-minister-with-respect-to-the-national-research-council-of-canada-for-purposes-of-the-financial-administration-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-235
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

[NATIONAL RESEARCH COUNCIL ACT](/eng/acts/N-15)

Registration 1993-12-15

Order Designating the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology as
Minister for Purposes of the National Research Council Act and as
Appropriate Minister with Respect to the National Research Council of
Canada for Purposes of the Financial Administration Act

P.C. 1993-1989  1993-12-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [National Research
Council Act](/eng/acts/N-15) and paragraph (c.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-235_e_hq_6549){#fn_SI-93-235_e_hq_6549-ID0EFEAA}
of the definition [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is
pleased hereby

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-235_e_hq_6549-ID0EFEAA){#fn_SI-93-235_e_hq_6549}S.C.
  1992, c. 1, s. 69(2)
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1993-1468 of June 25,
  1993[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-235_e_hq_6550){#fn_SI-93-235_e_hq_6550-ID0EBBCAA};

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-235_e_hq_6550-ID0EBBCAA){#fn_SI-93-235_e_hq_6550}SI/93-122,
    1993 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3214
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to designate the Minister of Industry, Science and
  Technology, a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, to act
  as the Minister for the purposes of the [National Research Council
  Act](/eng/acts/N-15); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to designate the Minister of Industry, Science and
  Technology as the appropriate Minister with respect to the National
  Research Council of Canada for the purposes of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).
:::
