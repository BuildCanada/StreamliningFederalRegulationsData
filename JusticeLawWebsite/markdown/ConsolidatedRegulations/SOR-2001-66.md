## [Prescribed Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679201 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following property is
prescribed for the purposes of subsection 177(1.3) of the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} cut flowers and foliage, bedding plants, nursery
  stock, potted plants and plant bulbs and tubers;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} horses;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} motor vehicles designed for highway use;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} machinery and equipment (other than office equipment)
  designed for use in

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the exploration for, or the development or
    production of, petroleum, natural gas, minerals or water,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} mining, quarrying or logging,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the construction or demolition of capital works,
    buildings, structures, roads, bridges, tunnels or other projects,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the manufacture or production of tangible personal
    property, the development of manufacturing or production processes
    or the development of tangible personal property for manufacture or
    production,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the treatment or processing of toxic waste or the
    detection, measurement, prevention, treatment, reduction or removal
    of pollutants,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} carrying refuse or waste from, or exhausting dust
    and noxious fumes produced by, manufacturing or producing
    operations, or

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the prevention of accidents in the workplace or
    the mitigation of their effects;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} attachments for tangible personal property included
  in paragraph (d); and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} repair or replacement parts for tangible personal
  property included in paragraph (d) or (e).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679216 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
deemed to have come into force on April 1, 1997.
