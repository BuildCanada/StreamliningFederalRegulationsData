## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838458 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[nuclear fuel conversion facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear fuel conversion facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    facility for the storage or processing of unirradiated enriched
    uranium. ([installation de conversion de combustible
    nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nuclear fuel production facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear fuel production facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    facility for the production of nuclear fuel containing unirradiated
    enriched uranium. ([installation de production de combustible
    nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nuclear fuel waste management facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear fuel waste management facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    a facility for the storage or management of nuclear fuel that has
    been removed from a nuclear reactor and that has a quantity of
    fissionable materials that meets any of the following conditions:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contains any quantity of natural or depleted
      uranium that was irradiated only in a thermal-neutron reactor, if
      the facility does not contain any other fissionable materials nor
      significant quantities of graphite, heavy water, beryllium, or
      other moderators more effective than light water;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not exceed 500 g of contained ^233^U, 700 g
      of ^235^U, 450 g of ^239^Pu, or 450 g of any combination of those
      three isotopes, if the facility does not contain any other
      fissionable materials nor significant quantities of graphite,
      heavy water, beryllium, or other moderators more effective than
      light water; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not exceed 80% of the appropriate smallest
      critical mass. ([installation de gestion des déchets de
      combustible nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nuclear fuel waste processing facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear fuel waste processing facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    a facility for the

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} processing of nuclear fuel that has been removed
      from a nuclear reactor; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} storage or management of nuclear fuel that has
      been removed from a nuclear reactor and that is not eligible to be
      stored or managed at a nuclear fuel waste management
      facility. ([installation de traitement des déchets de combustible
      nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[power reactor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[power reactor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a nuclear reactor with
    the capacity to produce electricity for commercial
    purposes. ([réacteur de puissance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[radioactive waste management facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radioactive waste management facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    a facility for the storage, management or processing of material
    that has been made radioactive by exposure to radiation
    consequential on or incidental to the production or use of nuclear
    fuel, other than natural uranium or depleted uranium and excluding
    nuclear fuel that has been removed from a nuclear
    reactor. ([installation de gestion des déchets
    radioactifs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reactor of less than 1 MW]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reactor of less than 1 MW]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a nuclear
    reactor with the capacity to produce less than 1 MW of thermal
    output. ([réacteur de moins de 1 MW]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reactor of 1 MW to 7 MW]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reactor of 1 MW to 7 MW]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a nuclear
    reactor with the capacity to produce from 1 MW to 7 MW of thermal
    output, inclusive. ([réacteur de 1 MW à 7 MW]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[reactor of over 7 MW]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reactor of over 7 MW]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a nuclear
    reactor, other than a power reactor, with the capacity to produce
    more than 7 MW of thermal output. ([réacteur de plus de 7
    MW]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Designation of Nuclear Installations and Operators]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838475 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of nuclear installations

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  sites set out in column 1 of the schedule are designated as nuclear
  installations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Class

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The class set out in column 2 of the schedule
  indicates the class of the nuclear installation set out in column 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Description of site

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The sites are described in column 3 of the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of facilities

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The facilities at each site that are authorized to
  contain nuclear material are listed in column 4 of the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of operator

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The holder of the licence set out in column 5 of the
  schedule is designated as the operator of the nuclear installation set
  out in column 1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 2(2) and column 2 of the
schedule

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 2(2) and column
2 of the schedule do not form part of these Regulations but are included
for convenience only.

## [Risk and Classes of Nuclear Installations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838489 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Risk -- facilities

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection (2), facilities are ranked from the highest to
  lowest risk in the following order:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} power reactor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reactor of over 7 MW;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} nuclear fuel waste processing facility;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} nuclear fuel waste management facility;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} nuclear fuel conversion facility;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} nuclear fuel production facility;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} reactor of 1 MW to 7 MW;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} radioactive waste management facility; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} reactor of less than 1 MW.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classes of nuclear installations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph 24(2)(b) of the Act and
  section 5 of these Regulations, the following classes of nuclear
  installations are prescribed:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Power Reactor Class is a class of nuclear
    installation at which the facility with the highest risk is a power
    reactor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Reactor of over 7 MW Class is a class of
    nuclear installation at which the facility with the highest risk is
    a reactor of over 7 MW;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Nuclear Fuel Waste Processing Facility Class is
    a class of nuclear installation at which the facility with the
    highest risk is a nuclear fuel waste processing facility;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Nuclear Fuel Waste Management Facility Class is
    a class of nuclear installation at which the facility with the
    highest risk is a nuclear fuel waste management facility;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Nuclear Fuel Conversion Facility Class is a
    class of nuclear installation at which the facility with the highest
    risk is a nuclear fuel conversion facility;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the Nuclear Fuel Production Facility Class is a
    class of nuclear installation at which the facility with the highest
    risk is a nuclear fuel production facility;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Reactor of 1 MW to 7 MW Class is a class of
    nuclear installation at which the facility with the highest risk is
    a reactor of 1 MW to 7 MW;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the Radioactive Waste Management Facility Class is
    a class of nuclear installation at which the facility with the
    highest risk is a radioactive waste management facility; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Reactor of less than 1 MW Class is a class of
    nuclear installation at which the facility with the highest risk is
    a reactor of less than 1 MW.

## [Limit of Liability]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838513 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operator's liability

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The liability of an
operator of a class of nuclear installations for damage resulting from a
nuclear incident is limited to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$180 million for the Reactor of over 7 MW Class;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$40 million for the Nuclear Fuel Waste Processing
  Facility Class;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$13 million for the Nuclear Fuel Waste Management
  Facility Class;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} \$3.3 million for the Nuclear Fuel Conversion
  Facility Class;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} \$2.3 million for the Nuclear Fuel Production
  Facility Class;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} \$1.3 million for the Reactor of 1 MW to 7 MW Class;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} \$1 million for the Radioactive Waste Management
  Facility Class; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} \$0.5 million for the Reactor of less than 1 MW
  Class.
