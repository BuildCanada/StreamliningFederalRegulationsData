## Certain Foreign Marine Carriers Remission Order {#certain-foreign-marine-carriers-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2008-23
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2008-03-05

Certain Foreign Marine Carriers Remission Order

P.C. 2008-274 2008-02-14
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of Fisheries and Oceans and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection
23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_610646-e_hq_3230){#fn_610646-e_hq_3230-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the
annexed [Certain Foreign Marine Carriers Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2008-23).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_610646-e_hq_3230-ID0EFBAA){#fn_610646-e_hq_3230}S.C.
  1991, c. 24, s. 7(2)
  :::
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to the foreign marine carriers set out in column 1 of the schedule of
the amounts set out in column 2, which are fees paid or payable by those
carriers between 1997 and 2005 under subsection 47(1) of the [Oceans
Act](/eng/acts/O-2.4).
