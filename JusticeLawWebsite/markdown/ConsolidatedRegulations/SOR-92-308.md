## Rules Governing Proceedings at Public Inquiries into Objections (Banks) {#rules-governing-proceedings-at-public-inquiries-into-objections-banks .Title-of-Act}

Rules Governing Proceedings at Public Inquiries into Objections (Banks)

P.C. 1992-1083 1992-05-21

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 26(5) of the [Bank
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDCAA}, is pleased
hereby to approve the revocation by the Superintendent of Financial
Institutions of the Bank Act Public Inquiry Rules of Procedure, approved
by Order in Council P.C. 1982-60 of January 14, 1982[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0EBCAA}, and to
approve the annexed Rules governing proceedings at public inquiries into
objections to proposed incorporations, continuances and amalgamations,
made by the Superintendent of Financial Institutions, in substitution
therefor.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944033 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Rules,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01);
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an application submitted
    pursuant to section 25, 33 or 228 of the Act;
    ([demande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inquiry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inquiry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a public inquiry held pursuant
    to subsection 26(3), 34(1) or 228(3) of the Act;
    ([enquête]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[objection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[objection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an objection to a proposed
    incorporation, a proposed continuance or a proposed amalgamation, as
    the case may be; ([opposition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an objector or an applicant;
    ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[presiding officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[presiding officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Superintendent
    or, where a person has been appointed pursuant to section 7, that
    person. ([président]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-532, s. 3
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944043 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Rules apply in
respect of public inquiries held pursuant to subsection 26(3), 34(1) or
228(3) of the Act.

## [Notice of the Inquiry]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944045 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Superintendent is satisfied that it is necessary and in the public
  interest that an inquiry be held into an objection, the Superintendent
  shall, at least 14 days before the inquiry,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} publish a notice of the inquiry in the [Canada
    Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) and in a newspaper in general
    circulation at or near the place where the head office of the bank
    is to be situated; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} serve the parties with a notice of the inquiry, a
    copy of the objection and an invitation to attend the inquiry.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice referred to in subsection (1) shall set
  out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date, time and place of the inquiry;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the inquiry and the substance of the
    objection;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the presiding officer; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} such other information as the Superintendent
    considers appropriate.

## [Withdrawal of Application or Objection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944055 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Superintendent shall cancel an inquiry where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application is withdrawn; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the objection is withdrawn or, if there is more
    than one objection, all of the objections are withdrawn.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an inquiry is cancelled, the Superintendent
  shall serve the parties with, and publish in accordance with paragraph
  4(1)(a), a notice of the cancellation.

## [Service of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944061 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document required to be served under these Rules shall be served on a
  person by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} delivering the document to the person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} posting the document by registered or certified
    mail

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the person's address for service, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where a person has no address for service at the
      time of service, the usual or last known address of the person; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} such other method as the presiding officer may
    specify.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The address for service of documents is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/98-532, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an applicant, the address set out in
    the application; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an objector, the address set out in
    the objection.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-532, s. 4
:::

## [General Conduct of the Inquiry]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944075 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Superintendent may
appoint a person to preside as presiding officer over an inquiry.

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A party
  may, in respect of an objection, present written submissions and make
  oral submissions to the presiding officer at an inquiry.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party may attend an inquiry in person or be
  represented by legal counsel.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A presiding officer may allow a party to call
  witnesses.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} At an inquiry, the objector shall make any
  submissions in support of the objection first, after which the
  applicant may make submissions in response.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A presiding officer may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} receive any information that is relevant to the
  inquiry; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} refuse to receive any information that is not
  relevant to the inquiry.

## [Public Access]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944085 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every inquiry shall be
open to attendance by the public.

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may examine and obtain a copy of the objection and any document
  submitted in support of, or in opposition to, the objection during the
  period beginning on the day of publication of the notice of the
  inquiry in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) and ending
  on the day that the Minister issues, or renders a decision not to
  issue, the letters patent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An examination or a request for a copy referred to in
  subsection (1) shall be made at the Office of the Superintendent of
  Financial Institutions in Ottawa or, during the conduct of the
  inquiry, at the place where the inquiry is held.

## [Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944090 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Superintendent shall
send a copy of the report of the findings of an inquiry to the parties
immediately after the Minister makes the report available to the public
pursuant to subsection 26(4) of the Act.
