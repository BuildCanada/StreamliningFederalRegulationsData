## Order Authorizing the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development to Act as Federal Interlocutor for Métis and Non-status Indians {#order-authorizing-the-minister-of-indian-affairs-and-northern-development-to-act-as-federal-interlocutor-for-métis-and-non-status-indians .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2004-87
:::

Registration 2004-08-11

Order Authorizing the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development to Act as Federal Interlocutor for Métis and Non-status
Indians

P.C. 2004-850 2004-07-20
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} terminates the authorization given by Order in
  Council P.C. 2003-2092 of December 12, 2003, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} authorizes the Minister of Indian Affairs and
  Northern Development to act as Federal Interlocutor for Métis and
  Non-status Indians and to enter into contribution agreements with
  organizations representing Métis and off-reserve Aboriginal peoples,

effective July 20, 2004.
