Remission Order in Respect of Non-residential Leases and Licences of

P.C. 2020-414 2020-05-30
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}

in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of the Environment and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection
23(2.1)[[Footnote

of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4205_hq_27304){#fn_81000-2-4205_hq_27304-ID0EDCAA},
makes the annexed Remission Order in Respect of Non-residential Leases
and Licences of Occupation Under the Administration of the Parks Canada
Agency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted to the persons
  referred to in subsection (3) of 75% of the following amounts paid or
  payable by them in respect of the period beginning on April 1, 2020
  and ending on September 30, 2020:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} rent in respect of any lease referred to in
    subsection 11(1) of the [National Parks of Canada Lease and Licence
    of Occupation Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-25);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} rent in respect of any lease or lease amending
    agreement referred to in subsection 15.1(2) of the [National Parks
    of Canada Lease and Licence of Occupation
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-25);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fees in respect of any licence of occupation of
    public lands

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} granted under section 18 of the [National Parks
      of Canada Lease and Licence of Occupation
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-25), if the licence permits
      those lands to be used for commercial purposes, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} referred to in subsection 18(23) of those
      Regulations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} rent under Article 9.1 of the Town of Banff
    Incorporation Agreement, dated December 12, 1989;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} amounts in respect of any lease entered into under
    subsection 4(1) of the [Federal Real Property
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-502) or any licence given under
    paragraph 4(2)(a) of those Regulations, in relation to real property
    under the administration of the Parks Canada Agency, if the lease or
    licence permits the property to be used for commercial or municipal
    purposes; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} amounts in respect of any lease or licence of
    occupation in relation to public lands in the Rouge National Urban
    Park, if the lease or licence permits those lands to be used for
    commercial purposes and was entered into or issued

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} under subsection 13(1) of the [Rouge National
      Urban Park Act](/eng/acts/R-8.55), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by the province of Ontario or a municipality and
      subsequently assumed by the Minister of the Environment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual payments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an amount referred to in any of paragraphs (1)(a)
  to (f) is calculated on an annual basis, the amount paid or payable in
  respect of the period referred to in subsection (1) is to be
  determined on a pro rata basis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligible persons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The remission is granted to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} would have been eligible for the Canada Emergency
      Commercial Rent Assistance program for small businesses, had the
      lessor or licensor not been the Government of Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} provides to the Parks Canada Agency, no later
      than December 31, 2020, a signed attestation supporting their
      eligibility;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Corporation of the Town of Banff; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Municipality of Jasper.
