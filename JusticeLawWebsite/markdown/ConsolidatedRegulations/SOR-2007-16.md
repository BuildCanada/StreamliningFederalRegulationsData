[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Export and Import Permits
    Act](/eng/acts/E-19). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[region]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[region]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    6.3(1) of the Act. ([région]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods
    included in item 5104 of Group 5 of the schedule to the [Export
    Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202). ([produits de bois
    d'oeuvre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  exporter or their agent or mandatary who applies for an export
  allocation must submit to the Minister the following information,
  together with a statement that the information is true, correct and
  complete:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the exporter and, if
    applicable, of the agent or mandatary;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the official language selected for communications
    with the exporter;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether or not the exporter and, if applicable, the
    agent or mandatary are residents of Canada; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} information necessary to enable the Minister to
    take into account the considerations referred to in paragraphs 4(b)
    and (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarifications

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The exporter, agent or mandatary must provide to the
  Minister, on request, any additional information that is necessary to
  clarify information furnished in the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resident of Canada

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** If the exporter is not a
resident of Canada, the application must be made by an agent or
mandatary who is a resident of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Considerations to be taken into account

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must take the
following considerations into account when deciding whether to issue an
export allocation:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the exporter or, if applicable, the agent or
  mandatary has furnished the information required by these Regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the exporter has complied with the provisions
  of the Act, or the regulations made under the Act, or any condition of
  an export allocation or export permit during the 12-month period
  preceding the month for which the export allocation is to apply; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the exporter has furnished false or
  misleading information in connection with any reports required by the
  Act, or the regulations made under the Act, or by any condition of an
  export allocation or export permit during the 12-month period
  preceding the month for which the export allocation is to apply.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be supplied by allocation
holders

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An export allocation holder
must provide the Minister with a detailed report of the circumstances of
the holder or of any other person who has failed or will likely fail to
comply with the requirements of these Regulations in respect of the
softwood lumber products to which the allocation relates or with the
terms of the allocation as soon as the holder becomes aware of those
circumstances.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
deemed to have come into force on October 12, 2006.
