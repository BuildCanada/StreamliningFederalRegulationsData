Public Ports and Public Port Facilities Regulations

P.C. 2001-701 2001-04-26

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to subsections 65(1), (4) and (6) of
the [Canada Marine Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7)[[Footnote

Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-154).

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Public Ports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-666678 .Part}

### [Designation of Public Ports]{.HTitleText2} {#h-666679 .Subheading}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The ports set out in
Schedule 1 are designated as public ports and have the limits set out in
that Schedule.

### [Repeal of Designation of Certain Public Ports]{.HTitleText2} {#h-666681 .Subheading}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  designation under section 1 of a public port set out in Schedule 2 is
  repealed, effective on the day on which the entire public port
  facility at the port or the last part of it is transferred to a person
  or body by Her Majesty in Right of Canada as represented by the
  Minister of Transport.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2002-358, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-358, s. 1
:::

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  designation under section 1 of a public port set out in Schedule 3 is
  repealed effective on the day on which the bed of the navigable waters
  at the port that is owned by Her Majesty in Right of Canada, or the
  last part of it, or, if applicable, the day on which the entire public
  port facility at the port, or the last part of it, is transferred to a
  person or body by Her Majesty in Right of Canada as represented by the
  Minister of Transport, whichever is later.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2002-358, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-358, s. 2
:::

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** The designation of
Esquimalt as a public port under section 1 is repealed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-74, s. 1
:::

**[[3.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.2 .sectionLabel}** The designation of
Nanoose Bay as a public port under section 1 is repealed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-74, s. 1
:::

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Operations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-666700 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-666701 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Part.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Marine
    Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dangerous goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dangerous goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned in
    section 2 of the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods Act,
    1992](/eng/acts/T-19.01). ([marchandises
    dangereuses]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area designated by
    a port official in respect of an activity set out in column 1 of
    Schedule 4. ([endroit désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a fee fixed under subsection 67(1)
    of the [Canada Marine Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7) or accepted by the
    Minister under section 68 of that Act. ([droit]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[floating structure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[floating structure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any type of
    floathome, houseboat, ship, work or other type of floating craft
    that is used for residential or commercial purposes. ([construction
    flottante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hot work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hot work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any work that uses flame or
    that can produce a source of ignition, such as heating, cutting or
    welding. ([travail à chaud]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[port official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[port official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, with respect to a
    public port or a public port facility, a representative of the
    Department of Transport, or a wharfinger or harbour master appointed
    under section 69 of the Act. ([responsable de port]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
- SOR/2004-254, s. 1
:::

### [Safety and Order in Public Ports and Public Port Facilities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-666720 .Subheading}

#### [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666721 .Subheading}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise
authorized under these Regulations, no person shall, by act or omission,
do anything or permit anything to be done in a public port or at a
public port facility that has or is likely to have any of the following
results:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to jeopardize the safety or health of persons in the
  public port or at the public port facility;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to interfere with navigation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to obstruct or threaten any part of the public port
  or the public port facility;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to interfere with an authorized activity in the
  public port or at the public port facility;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} to divert the flow of a river or stream, cause or
  affect currents, cause silting or the accumulation of material or
  otherwise reduce the depth of the waters of the public port;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} to cause a nuisance;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} to cause damage to ships or other property;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} to adversely affect sediment, soil, air or water
  quality; or

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} to adversely affect public port or public port
  facility operations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
- SOR/2004-254, s. 2(F)
:::

#### [Access to Public Port Facilities]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666734 .Subheading}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** No person shall access
any area of a public port facility unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person accesses the area to conduct legitimate
  business in the area;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person is authorized by a port official to access
  the area; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} access is not restricted by a sign or in some other
  way such as by a fence.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
:::

#### [Signs]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666741 .Subheading}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A port official may have
signs posted or devices placed for the purpose of ensuring the safety of
persons and property in a public port or at a public port facility, the
environmental protection of the public port or the public port facility,
or the management and development of the marine infrastructure and
services in the public port or at the public port facility.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
:::

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person in a public port or at a public port facility must comply with
  the instructions on signs posted and with devices placed under the
  authority of a port official unless the person is authorized by the
  port official to do otherwise.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall remove, mark or deface any sign or
  device within a public port or at a public port facility.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
:::

#### [Operation of Vehicles]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666750 .Subheading}

#### [Registration and Permits]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666751 .Subheading}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** No person shall operate a
vehicle at a public port facility unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person holds all licences and permits required
  under the laws of the province and municipality in which the public
  port facility is situated to operate the vehicle in that province and
  municipality; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle is registered and equipped as required
  under the laws of the province and municipality in which the public
  port facility is situated.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
:::

#### [Compliance with Provincial and Municipal Laws]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666757 .Subheading}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** No person shall operate a
vehicle at a public port facility except in accordance with the laws of
the province and municipality in which the public port facility is
situated.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
:::

#### [Traffic Control]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666761 .Subheading}

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Every person who operates
a vehicle at a public port facility shall do so in a safe manner and at
a speed that does not exceed the lowest of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 20 km/h;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the speed limit posted on signs at the public port
  facility; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the speed that is warranted by prevailing weather
  conditions or by the movement or storage of equipment, trains or
  goods.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
- SOR/2004-254, s. 3
:::

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A port
  official may have signs posted and devices placed at the public port
  facility respecting

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the safe operation of vehicles;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the parking or stopping of vehicles, including
    signs and devices restricting or prohibiting parking or stopping;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} weight and dimension restrictions of vehicles.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who operates a vehicle at a public port
  facility must comply

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with the instructions on any sign posted or with
    any device placed by a port official and applicable to the person,
    vehicle or facility; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with the traffic directions given by a port
    official.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
- SOR/2004-254, s. 4(F)
:::

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** A port official may have
a vehicle moved or stored at the time at which the vehicle is found to
be parked or stopped contrary to this section, if the vehicle is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} apparently abandoned;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} parked or stopped

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in a place or manner that creates a hazard or
    obstruction, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at a time that is not within the posted parking or
    stopping hours; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} parked or stopped in an area

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} not posted as a parking or stopping area, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} posted as a no-parking or no-stopping area.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
- SOR/2004-254, s. 5
:::

#### [Removal --- Property or Waters]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666788 .Subheading}

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  otherwise authorized under these Regulations, a person who, in a
  public port or at a public port facility, drops, deposits, discharges
  or spills refuse, a substance that pollutes, cargo, ship's gear or any
  other thing that interferes with navigation must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immediately make every technically feasible and
    reasonable effort to remove it; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify a port official of the incident without
    delay and provide a description of what was dropped, deposited,
    discharged or spilled and its approximate location, and the efforts
    made by the person, if any, to remove it.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the person does not remove the refuse, substance,
  cargo, ship's gear or thing immediately, the port official may conduct
  the removal and, if the thing removed interfered with navigation, the
  removal may be done at the expense of the person.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
- SOR/2004-254, s. 6
:::

#### [Fire Protection]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666796 .Subheading}

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Every person in a public
port or at a public port facility must follow the fire protection and
prevention measures established for the safety of persons and property
in the public port or at the public port facility.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
- SOR/2004-254, s. 7
:::

#### [Dangerous Situations]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666800 .Subheading}

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Every person who by act
or omission causes a dangerous situation in a public port or at a public
port facility must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} immediately take one of the following precautions:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} post the notices, deploy the lights and erect the
    fences, barricades or other devices that are necessary to prevent
    accidents and to protect persons and property, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} station a person at the site of the dangerous
    situation to warn persons of the danger;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} take appropriate measures to prevent injury to
  persons or damage to property; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} notify a port official without delay as to the nature
  of the dangerous situation and the precautions that have been taken
  and their location.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
- SOR/2004-254, s. 8
:::

#### [Emergencies]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666809 .Subheading}

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Despite any other
provision of these Regulations, a person may, in a public port or at a
public port facility, conduct an activity set out in column 1 of
Schedule 4 without having a contract or lease with the Minister, or a
licence granted by the Minister or authorization from a port official or
without complying with the conditions of an authorization for the
duration of an emergency situation if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the activity is required as a result of an emergency
  situation that jeopardizes the safety of persons or threatens to cause
  damage to property or the environment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person conducting the activity makes every effort
  to notify a port official of the activity and the emergency situation,
  if practicable; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person conducting the activity submits a report
  to the port official without delay describing the activity and
  explaining why the situation was regarded as an emergency.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
:::

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** If a situation causes or
is likely to cause death, bodily injury or any other emergency situation
in a public port or at a public port facility, or damages or is likely
to damage property or the environment, a person directly involved in the
situation and, in the case of an activity conducted under a contract,
lease, licence or authorization, the person authorized to conduct the
activity, must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify a port official without delay of the emergency
  situation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} submit a detailed report of the emergency situation
  to the port official as soon as possible after the situation ceases to
  exist; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} at the request of the port official, submit with the
  report to the port official a copy of each report of the emergency
  situation that the person makes to municipal, provincial and federal
  authorities.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
:::

#### [Accidents and Incidents]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666822 .Subheading}

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** A person who does
anything in a public port or at a public port facility that results in
an incident involving injury to a person, damage to the environment,
material loss or damage, or an explosion, fire, accident, grounding, or
stranding must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify a port official of the incident without delay;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} submit a detailed report of the incident to the port
  official as soon as possible after the incident occurs.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
:::

#### [Precautionary Measures]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666828 .Subheading}

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, in
  a public port or at a public port facility, a person conducts an
  activity, other than an activity set out in Schedule 4, that is likely
  to have any of the results prohibited under section 14, a port
  official may instruct the person conducting the activity to cease the
  activity or to take the precautions reasonably necessary to mitigate
  or prevent the result.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person must immediately comply with the
  instructions of the port official.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
- SOR/2004-254, s. 9
:::

#### [Activities of Port Officials]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666834 .Subheading}

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** If a port official is the
proponent of an activity that is set out in column 1 of Schedule 4 which
is likely to have any of the results prohibited under section 14, the
port official must take appropriate measures designed to mitigate or
prevent the result, if technically feasible and reasonable, taking into
account

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the safety of persons and property in the public port
  or at the public port facility;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the environmental protection of the public port or
  the public port facility; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the management and the development of the marine
  infrastructure and services in the public port or at the public port
  facility.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
:::

### [Authorizations and Instructions for Activities in a Public Port and at a Public Port Facility]{.HTitleText2} {#h-666841 .Subheading}

#### [Activities under Contracts, Leases and Licences]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666842 .Subheading}

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** A person may, in a public
port or at a public port facility, conduct an activity set out in column
1 of Schedule 4 if authorized to do so in writing expressly or by
necessary implication under a contract or lease entered into with, or a
licence granted by, the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
:::

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** If, by entering into a
contract or lease or granting a licence, the Minister authorizes an
activity set out in column 1 of Schedule 4 that has or is likely to have
any of the results prohibited under section 14, the Minister may
stipulate as a condition of the contract, lease or licence that the
person with whom the contract or lease is made or to whom the licence is
granted must take measures designed to mitigate or prevent the result,
if technically feasible and reasonable.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
:::

#### [Authorizations by Posted Signs or Forms]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666849 .Subheading}

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  a port official may, by such means as posted signs, posted notices,
  maps or forms, give an authorization under this section to conduct, in
  a public port or at a public port facility, an activity set out in
  column 1 of Schedule 4 if an "X" is set out in column 2.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the conduct of the activity is not likely to have
  a result prohibited under section 14, the port official may give the
  authorization without conditions, as long as the authorization is
  posted in a prominent place clearly visible to persons wanting to
  conduct the activity.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the conduct of the activity is likely to have a
  result prohibited under section 14, the port official may give the
  authorization for the activity only if the port official

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establishes conditions designed to mitigate or
    prevent the result; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} communicates the conditions by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} posting them in a prominent place clearly visible
      to persons wanting to conduct the activity, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} setting them out on forms that are readily
      available to persons wanting to conduct the activity.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
:::

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall conduct an activity set out in column 1 of Schedule 4 if
  an "X" is set out in column 2 unless the person complies with the
  conditions, if any, for conducting the activity that are posted or set
  out on forms and pays the applicable fee, if any.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a condition for conducting the activity is to
  complete a checklist, the person conducting the activity must keep the
  checklist readily available for inspection.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
:::

#### [Authorization to a Specific Person]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666865 .Subheading}

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A port
  official may give an authorization under this section to a person to
  conduct, in a public port or at a public port facility, an activity
  set out in column 1 of Schedule 4 if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an "X" is set out in column 3; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an "X" is set out in column 2 and the person or any
    person who would be covered by the authorization is unable to comply
    with the conditions posted or set out on forms for the conduct of
    the activity under section 33.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a request for an authorization, along
  with payment of the applicable fee, if any, and the information
  required under subsection 36(2), the port official must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} give their authorization;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the results of the conduct of the activity are
    uncertain or if the conduct of the activity is likely to have any of
    the results prohibited under section 14,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} refuse to give their authorization, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} give their authorization subject to conditions
      designed to mitigate or prevent the results; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the port official required that the person
    obtain insurance coverage, performance security or damage security
    in respect of the conduct of the activity and none is obtained or
    that which is obtained is inadequate, refuse to give their
    authorization.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
- SOR/2004-254, s. 10
:::

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall, in a public port or at a public port facility, conduct
  an activity set out in column 1 of Schedule 4 if an "X" is set out in
  column 3 unless the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtains an authorization under section 35 or is
    covered by an authorization given to a partnership, an association
    or a body corporate under that section; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} complies with the conditions, if any, of the
    authorization.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person that seeks an authorization from a port
  official to conduct an activity in a public port or at a public port
  facility must provide to the port official

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicable fee, if any;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} information relevant to the proposed activity and
    required by the port official to assess the likelihood of the
    occurrence of any of the results prohibited under section 14;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if requested by the port official, proof that the
    applicant has an insurance policy that provides adequate coverage
    for the activity, names Her Majesty in right of Canada as an
    additional insured and provides for the insurer to notify the port
    official in the event that the policy is amended or cancelled; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if required by the port official, performance
    security and damage security in respect of the conduct of the
    activity.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
:::

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** A port official may
cancel an authorization given under section 35 or change the conditions
to which an authorization is subject if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conduct of the activity has a result prohibited
  under section 14 or, as a result of a change in circumstances, becomes
  likely to have such a result;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the authorization was based on incorrect or
  misleading information; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person to whom the authorization is given or any
  person covered by the authorization does not comply with a condition
  of the authorization.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
:::

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  authorization given under section 35 is cancelled, the port official
  must give notice of the cancellation to the person to whom the
  authorization was given.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The cancellation is effective at the earliest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the end of the fifth business day after the notice
    of cancellation is sent by registered mail to the address provided
    in the application for the authorization;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} two hours after a facsimile or electronic
    transmission of the notice of cancellation is sent to the address
    provided in the application for the authorization; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} immediately on service of the notice of
    cancellation at the address provided in the application for the
    authorization.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
:::

#### [Instructions to Cease, Remove, Return and Restore]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666904 .Subheading}

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A port
  official may instruct a person to take any of the actions referred to
  in subsection (2) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person conducts an activity for which an
    authorization is required under section 35 without first obtaining
    the authorization or without being covered by one;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person, or any person covered by the
    authorization, fails to comply with a condition of the
    authorization;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the authorization to conduct the activity is
    cancelled under section 37; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an activity for which no
    authorization is required under these Regulations, the conduct of
    the activity has a result prohibited under section 14.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The actions are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to cease the activity or comply with the conditions
    for conducting the activity; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person is instructed to cease the activity,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to remove anything brought into the public port
      or to the public port facility in connection with the activity,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to return to the public port or the public port
      facility anything that was removed from it in connection with the
      activity, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to restore the property affected by the
      activity to its former state.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person must comply immediately with the
  instructions of the port official.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the person fails to remove the thing or to restore
  the property immediately, the port official may conduct the removal or
  restoration and may store the thing.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the thing removed or stored by the port official
  interfered with navigation, the removal and, if applicable, the
  storage may be done at the expense of the person.

### [Ships and Cargoes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-666922 .Subheading}

#### [Information in Respect of Ships and Cargoes]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666923 .Subheading}

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  section applies in respect of ships for which fees are fixed under
  subsection 67(1) of the Act or accepted under section 68 of that Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} At least 24 hours before a ship enters a public port
  or arrives at a public port facility, the owner or the person in
  charge of the ship must provide the following information to a port
  official if the information has not already been provided for the
  purpose of obtaining permission to access the public port or public
  port facility:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the ship, its port of registry and its
    identification number as it appears in the Lloyd's Register of
    Shipping or on the ship's certificate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the owner, the master and the agent of
    the ship;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the gross tonnage of the ship and its overall
    length;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the port from which and the date on which the ship
    began its voyage;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the estimated time of arrival of the ship in the
    public port or at the public port facility;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the estimated draught of the ship on arrival in the
    public port or at the public port facility and on departure;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the description, quantity and tonnage of dangerous
    goods that are in transit on the ship or that will be loaded,
    unloaded or transferred in the public port or at the public port
    facility, in the form provided by the port official;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the description, quantity and tonnage of goods to
    be loaded, unloaded or transferred in the public port or at the
    public port facility;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of passengers in transit on the ship,
    embarking on the ship or disembarking from the ship;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} if the ship is not engaged in cargo or passenger
    operations, the purpose of the ship's visit to the public port;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the last port of call of the ship and its next
    intended port of call; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} any other information requested by the port
    official and relevant to assessing the likelihood of the occurrence
    of any of the results prohibited under section 14 or to the
    management of the marine infrastructure and services of the public
    port or the public port facility.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The owner or the person in charge of the ship must
  provide the port official with the description, quantity and tonnage
  of the cargo loaded, unloaded or transferred at each berth or
  anchorage within 24 hours after the loading, unloading or transferral,
  but before the ship's departure.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The information must be provided by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} delivering it by hand in the form of a certificate
    signed by the owner or person in charge of the ship; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sending it by facsimile or electronic transmission.

#### [Cargo Operations]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666945 .Subheading}

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  ship is waiting for another ship to load, unload or transfer cargo to
  get a berth or anchorage, the owner or the person in charge of the
  other ship must ensure that, regardless of whether working around the
  clock is necessary or overtime charges are incurred,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the loading, unloading or transfer operations are
    conducted with dispatch; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cargo is moved with dispatch from the immediate
    vicinity of the berth or anchorage to enable the waiting ship to
    load, unload or transfer its cargo.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the loading, unloading or transfer operations of
  the ship, or the movement of its cargo, are not conducted with
  dispatch, a port official may instruct the owner or the person in
  charge of the ship

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to move the ship from the berth or anchorage to
    allow the waiting ship to berth or to anchor and begin its loading,
    unloading or transfer operations; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to move the cargo from the immediate vicinity of
    the berth or anchorage.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, to allow a waiting ship to berth or to anchor
  promptly, the loading, unloading or transfer operations of a ship, or
  the movement of its cargo, are conducted around the clock or the ship
  or cargo is moved on the instructions of the port official, the owner
  or person in charge of the waiting ship must ensure that, regardless
  of whether working around the clock is necessary or overtime charges
  are incurred,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the loading, unloading or transfer operations of
    the waiting ship are conducted with dispatch; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cargo of the waiting ship is moved with
    dispatch from the immediate vicinity of the berth or anchorage.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
:::

#### [Crewing]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666958 .Subheading}

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  owner or the person in charge of a ship berthed, moored or anchored in
  a public port or at a public port facility must ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a port official is kept informed of the location
    and telephone number of a person who can take command of the ship
    immediately at the request of the port official if it is necessary
    to move it for the safety of persons or property in the public port
    or at the public port facility or the management of the port or
    facility; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), the ship is kept in a
    state of readiness so that it can be moved quickly.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(b) does not apply to a ship in respect
  of which an authorization to lay-up in the public port or at the
  public port facility has been obtained.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
:::

#### [Removal]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666966 .Subheading}

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  purposes of management, control and use of a public port, a port
  official may order the removal, within a reasonable time fixed by the
  port official, of any ship from an area to another area of the public
  port or the alteration of its position if that ship interferes with
  navigation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the person in charge of the ship fails to remove
  the ship or alter its position within the reasonable time, the port
  official may have the ship removed or its position altered at the
  expense of the person.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-121, s. 1
- SOR/2004-254, s. 12
:::

#### [Arrangements for Towing]{.HTitleText3} {#h-666972 .Subheading}

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** The owner or the person
in charge of a ship that is berthed, moored or anchored in a public port
or at a public port facility must ensure that the ship is fitted with
arrangements suitable for attaching a towing line so that the ship can
be towed from its berth, mooring or anchorage if moving the ship is
necessary for the safety of persons or property within the public port
or at the public port facility.
