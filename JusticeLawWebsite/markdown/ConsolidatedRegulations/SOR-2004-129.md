## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708965 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[customs administration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[customs administration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the competent
    authority that is responsible under the law of Canada or of Costa
    Rica, as the case may be, for the administration of customs laws and
    regulations. ([administration douanière]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Generally Accepted Accounting Principles]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Generally Accepted Accounting Principles]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has
    the meaning assigned to that expression by subsection 1(4) of the
    [CCRFTA Rules of Origin Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-395).
    ([principes de comptabilité généralement reconnus]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods for which preferential
    tariff treatment under the CCRFTA is claimed.
    ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a good that is used in the
    production of another good, and includes a part or ingredient.
    ([matière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification letter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification letter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a letter that
    requests information with respect to the origin of goods that are
    the subject of a verification of origin. ([lettre de
    vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification questionnaire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification questionnaire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    questionnaire that requests information with respect to the origin
    of goods that are the subject of a verification of origin.
    ([questionnaire de vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification visit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification visit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the entry into
    premises or a place for the purpose of conducting a verification of
    origin of goods under paragraph 42.1(1)(a) of the Act. ([visite de
    vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Manner of Conducting a Verification of Origin]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708975 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In addition to a
verification visit, a verification of origin of goods may be conducted
in any of the following manners:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2008-111, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by reviewing a verification questionnaire completed
  by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the exporter or producer of the goods, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a producer or supplier of a material;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} by reviewing a written response received from a
  person referred to in paragraph (b) to a verification letter; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} by reviewing any other information received from a
  person referred to in paragraph (b).

## [Prescribed Premises]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708985 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The premises in Costa Rica
of the following persons are prescribed premises for the purposes of a
verification visit:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an exporter or a producer of goods; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a producer or a supplier of a material.

## [Conditions for Conducting a Verification Visit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708989 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  officer may conduct a verification visit on condition that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the officer sends a written notice of the intention
    to conduct the verification visit to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the person whose premises are the subject of the
      verification visit,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the customs administration of Costa Rica at
      least five working days before the person referred to in
      subparagraph (i) is notified, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if requested by the customs administration of
      Costa Rica, the Embassy of Costa Rica located in Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person whose premises are the subject of the
    verification visit consents to the verification visit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice referred to in paragraph (1)(a) shall
  specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the customs administration of the
    officer sending the notice;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person whose premises are the
    subject of the verification visit;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and place of the verification visit;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the object and scope of the verification visit,
    including a description of the goods or materials that are the
    subject of the verification of origin;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name and title of the officer; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the legal authority for the verification visit.

## [Postponement of a Verification Visit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-709004 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When the
  customs administration of Costa Rica receives a notice referred to in
  paragraph 4(1)(a), it may postpone the verification visit by sending a
  written request to the officer who sent the notice within 15 days of
  receiving the notice.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A postponement of a proposed verification visit under
  subsection (1) shall be for a period not exceeding 60 days beginning
  on the date of receipt of the notice, or for a longer period, where
  the longer period is agreed to by the customs administration of Canada
  and the customs administration of Costa Rica.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When a person receives written notice pursuant to
  paragraph 4(1)(a), the person may, on a single occasion, and within 15
  days of receipt of the written notice, request the postponement, in
  writing, of the verification visit for a period that does not exceed
  60 days from the date of the receipt of the notice, or for such longer
  period that is agreed to by the customs administration of Canada.

## [Observers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-709009 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who receives a notice pursuant to subparagraph 4(1)(a)(i) may
  designate two observers to be present during the verification visit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An observer designated under subsection (1) may not
  participate in the verification visit in a manner other than as an
  observer.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who designates an observer under
  subsection (1) shall identify that observer to the officer conducting
  the verification visit.

## [Generally Accepted Accounting Principles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-709016 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If an officer determines,
during the course of a verification of origin, that a producer of goods
has failed to record costs in accordance with Generally Accepted
Accounting Principles applied in the territory of Costa Rica, the
officer shall send a written notice to the producer stating that the
producer must record those costs in accordance with those principles
within 60 days after receiving that written notice.

## [Verification Letters and Verification Questionnaires]{.HTitleText1} {#h-709018 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A verification letter or
verification questionnaire shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} identify the customs administration of Canada and the
  name and title of the officer sending the verification letter or
  verification questionnaire;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} describe the goods or materials that are the subject
  of the verification of origin; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} specify the period within which the verification
  letter must be answered or the verification questionnaire must be
  completed and returned, which period shall be not less than 30 days
  after the date on which the verification letter or the verification
  questionnaire was received.

## [Extension of Completion of a Verification Letter or Verification Questionnaire]{.HTitleText1} {#h-709023 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A person who receives a
verification letter or verification questionnaire may, on a single
occasion, make a written request, within 30 days of receipt of the
verification letter or verification questionnaire, to the customs
administration conducting the erification, asking for an extension to
the period referred to in paragraph 8(c). The extension shall be granted
for a further period of not more than 30 days.

## [Denial or Withdrawal of Preferential Tariff Treatment Under CCRFTA]{.HTitleText1} {#h-709025 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 42.1(2) of the Act, preferential tariff treatment under the
CCRFTA may be denied or withdrawn from the goods that are the subject of
a verification of origin where the exporter or producer of the goods

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} does not consent to a verification visit within 30
  days of receipt of a notice pursuant to paragraph 4(1)(a);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} who is required to maintain records with respect to
  goods in accordance with the applicable laws of the country in which
  the verification of origin is conducted

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} fails to maintain those records in accordance with
    those laws, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} denies the officer conducting the verification of
    origin access to those records; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not answer the verification letter or complete
  and return the verification questionnaire to the officer within the
  time period specified in paragraph 8(c) or, in the case of an
  extension, section 9.

<!-- -->

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  officer shall send a written notice of the intent to re-determine the
  origin of goods under subsection 59(1) of the Act with any statement
  that is provided to an exporter or producer of goods pursuant to
  subsection 42.2(1) of the Act and that states that the goods that are
  the subject of a verification of origin are not eligible for
  preferential tariff treatment under the CCRFTA.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice referred to in subsection (1) shall include
  the date after which preferential tariff treatment under the CCRFTA,
  may be denied or withdrawn in respect of the goods that are the
  subject of the verification of origin and the period during which the
  exporter or producer of the goods may provide written comments or
  additional information regarding the origin of those goods.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A re-determination of the
origin of goods shall not be made earlier than 10 days after the date on
which the notice referred to in subsection 11(1) is received.

## [Methods of Sending Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-709036 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A verification letter, a
verification questionnaire, the notices referred to in paragraph 4(1)(a)
and in sections 7 and 11 and the written request referred to in
subsection 5(1) shall be sent by any method that produces confirmation
of receipt.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-709038 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
deemed to have come into force on November 1, 2002.
