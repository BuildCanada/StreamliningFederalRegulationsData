## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-628300 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Order.

  [[qualified flight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[qualified flight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a flight that was
      provided by Air Canada on June 10, 2014 for no consideration
      solely to travellers who were attending a Royal Canadian Mounted
      Police regimental funeral in Moncton on that day if the flight was
      taking place either

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} from Ottawa (Macdonald-Cartier International)
        or Toronto (Lester B. Pearson International) to Moncton; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} from Moncton to Ottawa (Macdonald-Cartier
        International) or Toronto (Lester B. Pearson International).
        ([vol admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[traveller]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[traveller]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who, on June
      10, 2014,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} was a member of the Royal Canadian Mounted
        Police, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Royal Canadian
        Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10);

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} was a civilian employee of the Royal Canadian
        Mounted Police referred to in section 10 of the [Royal Canadian
        Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10); or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if neither paragraph (a) nor (b) applies, was
        otherwise working in the core public administration, as defined
        in subsection 11(1) of the [Financial Administration
        Act](/eng/acts/F-11). ([passager]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context otherwise requires, words and
  expressions used in this Order have the same meaning as in the [Air
  Travellers Security Charge Act](/eng/acts/A-10.5).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-628311 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
charge imposed under section 11 of the [Air Travellers Security Charge
Act](/eng/acts/A-10.5) in respect of an air transportation service that
included a chargeable emplanement of a traveller in a qualified flight.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of any
tax imposed under section 165 of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) in
respect of the charge referred to in section 2.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of
interest and penalties paid or payable on any amount for which remission
is granted under section 2 or 3.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-628315 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted only
to the extent to which the amount remitted has not otherwise been
rebated, remitted, credited or refunded to any person under the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) or any other Act of
Parliament.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** If the charge referred to
in section 2, or the tax referred to in section 3, was paid by the
person acquiring the air transportation service, or was remitted by Air
Canada to the Receiver General, remission is granted on the condition
that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made in writing to the
  Minister of National Revenue not later than one year after the day on
  which this Order is made; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person making the claim for remission provides
  the Minister of National Revenue with evidence or information that
  demonstrates that the charge or the tax has been paid or remitted by
  that person.
