## New Brunswick Potato Marketing Levies (Interprovincial and Export) Order --- No. 2 {#new-brunswick-potato-marketing-levies-interprovincial-and-export-order-no.-2 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-307
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1988-06-01

Order Respecting Fixing, Imposing and Collecting Levies on Potatoes
Produced in New Brunswick and Marketed in Interprovincial and Export
Trade
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The New Brunswick Potato Agency, pursuant to sections 3[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EHCBA} and 4
of the [New Brunswick Potato Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-726), made
by Order in Council P.C. 1980-2344 of September 5, 1980[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2star_e){#footnote2star_e-ID0EDCBA},
hereby makes the annexed Order respecting fixing, imposing and
collecting levies on potatoes produced within a designated area in New
Brunswick and marketed in interprovincial and export trade.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911743 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies to the
marketing of potatoes in interprovincial and export trade and to persons
and property situated in the Province of New Brunswick.

## [Imposition of Levy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911745 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay to
the Commodity Board a levy of \$16.50 for each acre used in the
production of potatoes by the producer or on their behalf.

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911747 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A producer shall remit the
levy referred to in section 4 to the Commodity Board within 30 days
after the producer has sold his entire crop of potatoes.
