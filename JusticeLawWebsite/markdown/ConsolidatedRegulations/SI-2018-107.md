[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2018-12-12

Order Transferring from the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development, to the Minister of State to assist the Minister of Indian
Affairs and Northern Development, the powers, duties and functions of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development under certain
Acts

P.C. 2018-1421 2018-11-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23447){#fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23447-ID0EECSA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23448){#fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23448-ID0EBCSA},
transfers from the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development,
to the Minister of State to assist the Minister of Indian Affairs and
Northern Development, the powers, duties and functions of the Minister
of Indian Affairs and Northern Development under the following Acts:
