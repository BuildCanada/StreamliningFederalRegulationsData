## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-747592 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Nova Scotia and Newfoundland
    and Labrador Additional Fiscal Equalization Offset Payments
    Act](/eng/acts/N-27.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include Yukon, the
    Northwest Territories or Nunavut. ([province]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[provincial and territorial general government sub-sector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial and territorial general government
    sub-sector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that component of the
    provincial and territorial government sector as defined by
    Statistics Canada for the purposes of the Financial Management
    System. ([sous-secteur des administrations publiques générales
    provinciales et territoriales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[provincial general government sub-sector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial general government
    sub-sector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2010-174, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-747600 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of additional fiscal equalization offset payments that may be
made to Nova Scotia or Newfoundland and Labrador under the Act in
respect of any fiscal year during the period beginning on April 1, 2009
and ending on March 31, 2020.

## [Per Capita Net Debt]{.HTitleText1} {#h-747602 .Part}

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 12(1)(b) of the Act, "per capita net debt" for a province as
of March 31, 2012 is the amount determined by the Minister in accordance
with the following formula:

::: {#747604 .Section}
(A -- B -- C -- D) × E

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the amount for the province, as of March 31, 2012, for the
    "liabilities" category of the provincial and territorial general
    government sub-sector of the Financial Management System, as
    determined by Statistics Canada;

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the amount for the province, as of March 31, 2012, for the "other
    liabilities" category of the provincial and territorial general
    government sub-sector of the Financial Management System, as
    determined by Statistics Canada;

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the amount for the province, as of March 31, 2012, for the
    "liabilities to pension plans" category of the provincial and
    territorial general government sub-sector of the Financial
    Management System, as determined by Statistics Canada;

[D]{.dfn}
:   is the amount for the province, as of March 31, 2012, for the
    "financial assets" category of the provincial and territorial
    general government sub-sector of the Financial Management System, as
    determined by Statistics Canada; and

[E]{.dfn}
:   is a fraction whose numerator is 1 and whose denominator is the last
    estimate of the population of the province on July 1, 2011 published
    by Statistics Canada prior to its determination of the value for A.
:::

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-174, s. 2
:::

## [Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-747613 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall pay Nova Scotia or Newfoundland and Labrador, as the
  case may be, the amount of any additional fiscal equalization offset
  payment payable to that province in respect of any fiscal year during
  the period beginning on April 1, 2009 and ending on March 31, 2020 --
  other than the fiscal year beginning on April 1, 2012 -- within 30
  days after the day on which the Minister determines the amount in
  accordance with the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the fiscal year beginning on April 1, 2012, the
  Minister shall pay the amount of the payment within 30 days after the
  later of:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the Minister determines the
    amount, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a payment payable to Nova Scotia,
      Statistics Canada determines the amounts for A, B, C and D in
      subsection 2.1(1); and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a payment payable to Newfoundland
      and Labrador, all provinces have released their Public Accounts
      for the fiscal year ending on March 31, 2012.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-174, s. 3
