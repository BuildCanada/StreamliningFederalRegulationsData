Minor Works Order

out in the annexed Order are likely to slightly interfere with

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1308613 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Order.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Navigable Waters
      Act](/eng/acts/N-22). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[aerial cables]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[aerial cables]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means cables that are in the
      air and includes the towers and poles from which they are
      suspended. ([câbles aériens]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[charted navigable water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[charted navigable water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a navigable
      water for which nautical charts are produced by the Canadian
      Hydrographic Service or the National Oceanic and Atmospheric
      Administration of the United States. ([eaux navigables
      cartographiées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[erosion-protection work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[erosion-protection work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any stones,
      rocks, concrete, logs or other common building materials or living
      plants placed to protect the bank of a navigable water from
      erosion. ([ouvrages de protection contre
      l'érosion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[navigation channel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[navigation channel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a channel
      represented on a nautical chart produced by the Canadian
      Hydrographic Service or the National Oceanic and Atmospheric
      Administration of the United States or a channel for which markers
      have been placed by a federal, provincial or municipal government
      or by a [[port authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Canada Marine
      Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7). ([chenal de navigation]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[navigation route]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[navigation route]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a channel, other
      than a navigation channel, formed by the connection of the deep
      sections of a navigable water that, based on local knowledge, is
      predominantly used by vessels for navigation purposes. ([voie de
      navigation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Order, [[submarine
  cables]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes concrete blocks, concrete
  mattresses and any other objects attached to, laid on or placed under
  submarine cables to hold them in place or to protect them from being
  damaged.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measurements --- length or width of minor
work

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  otherwise indicated, any length or width of a minor work referred to
  in this Order is measured from the ordinary high water mark at the
  site where the minor work is situated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measurements --- width of navigable
  water

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise indicated, any width of a navigable
  water referred to in this Order is measured from the ordinary high
  water mark on one side of the navigable water to the ordinary high
  water mark on the other side of the navigable water at the site where
  the minor work is situated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measurements --- depth or height

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise indicated, any depth or height
  referred to in this Order is measured from the ordinary high water
  level at the site where the minor work is situated.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1308632 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (2) and (3) apply to the
  following minor works:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} erosion-protection works referred to in subsection
    12(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} aerial cables referred to in subsection 16(1);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} submarine cables referred to in section 17;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} buried pipelines referred to in section 19;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} outfalls and water intakes referred to in section
    26;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} dredging referred to in section 28; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} watercourse crossings referred to in section 34.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deposit of information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before beginning the construction, placement,
  alteration, rebuilding, removal or decommissioning of a minor work,
  the owner must deposit information in the registry established under
  section 27.2 of the Act describing the proposed activity and the minor
  work's location.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before beginning the construction, placement,
  alteration, rebuilding, removal or decommissioning of a minor work,
  the owner must publish a notice in accordance with the requirements
  set out on the Internet site maintained by the Government of Canada
  entitled Navigation Protection Program External Submission Site under
  the heading "Create a Notification of a Minor Work", as amended from
  time to time, unless the proposed minor work has gone through a
  federal or provincial review process.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior notification of Canadian Coast
Guard

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** At least 48 hours before
beginning the construction, placement, alteration, rebuilding, removal
or decommissioning of a minor work in, on, over, under, through or
across a charted navigable water, the owner of the minor work must, in
writing, notify a Canadian Coast Guard Marine Communications and Traffic
Services Centre of the day on which the activity is expected to begin.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Buoys

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A buoy referred to in this
Order must meet the following criteria:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the part of the buoy that shows above the surface of
  the water is at least 15.25 cm wide and at least 30.5 cm high;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the buoy, including its anchor, remains in position
  after it has been anchored; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the buoy complies with the requirements set out in
  the section entitled "Floating Aids to Navigation (Buoys)" of TP 968,
  entitled Canadian Aids to Navigation System and published by the
  Canadian Coast Guard, as amended from time to time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** During the construction,
placement, alteration, rebuilding, removal, decommissioning, repair or
maintenance of a minor work, the owner of the minor work must ensure

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that vessels can navigate safely through or around
  the work site or, if navigation is interrupted by any activity related
  to the construction, placement, alteration, rebuilding, removal,
  decommissioning, repair or maintenance of the minor work, that a
  suitable means, such as a portage, exists to allow vessels to resume
  navigation upstream and downstream of the work site;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the perimeter of the work site is visible from
  sunset to sunrise and during periods of restricted visibility by the
  placement of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} yellow flashing lights,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} cautionary buoys with retro-reflective material,
    or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} cautionary buoys with yellow flashing lights; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} that any cable or pipe that is in, on, over, through
  or across the navigable water is not left unattended unless

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the cable or pipe is lying on the bed of the
    navigable water, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the cable meets the requirements referred to in
    paragraph 16(1)(e).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) applies to the
  following minor works:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} erosion-protection works referred to in subsection
    12(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} aerial cables referred to in subsection 16(1);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} buried pipelines referred to in section 19; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} watercourse crossings referred to in section 34.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} During the construction, placement, alteration,
  rebuilding, removal, decommissioning, repair or maintenance of a minor
  work, the owner of the minor work must ensure that navigators are
  notified of the work site both upstream and downstream of the work
  site by one of the following means:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} signs stating "Construction Ahead" and "Travaux de
    construction", legible from a distance of at least 50 m;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vessel tasked with notifying navigators; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} cautionary buoys.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification on completion

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** On completion of the
construction, placement, alteration, rebuilding, removal or
decommissioning of a minor work in, on, over, under, through or across a
charted navigable water, the owner of the minor work must, in writing,
notify:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian Coast Guard Marine Communications and
  Traffic Services Centre; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Hydrographic Service.

## [Designations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1308680 .Part}

### [Temporary Works]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1308681 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- temporary works

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A work that meets the
following criteria is designated as a minor work:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the work is installed exclusively for the
  construction, placement, alteration, rebuilding, removal,
  decommissioning, repair or maintenance of another minor work;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the work is not situated in, on, over, under, through
  or across a navigation channel or, if there is no navigation channel,
  a navigation route; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the work does not occupy more than one-third of the
  width of the navigable water.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a work that
is designated as a minor work under section 9 must ensure that it is
removed on completion of the construction, placement, alteration,
rebuilding, removal, decommissioning, repair or maintenance of the minor
work for which it was installed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bed contours of navigable water

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** If the construction,
placement, alteration, rebuilding, removal, decommissioning, repair or
maintenance of a work that is designated, or intended to be designated,
as a minor work under section 9 disturbs the bed contours of a navigable
water to the point of interfering or likely to interfere with
navigation, the owner of the work must ensure, on completion of the
removal of the work, that the bed contours of the navigable water do not
and are not likely to interfere with navigation.

### [Erosion-Protection Works]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1308691 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- erosion-protection works

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  erosion-protection work that meets the following criteria is
  designated as a minor work:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the erosion-protection work is integrated with and
    follows the bank of the navigable water;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the submerged part of the erosion-protection
    work extends horizontally into the navigable water further than the
    non-submerged part of the erosion-protection work, the submerged
    part is integrated with and follows the slope of the bed of the
    navigable water;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the erosion-protection work does not extend
    horizontally more than 5 m into the navigable water;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the erosion-protection work does not occupy more
    than one-third of the width of the navigable water;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the erosion-protection work is not associated with
    an existing or proposed work, unless the existing or proposed work
    is a minor work; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the erosion-protection work does not include
    groynes or other structures to deflect the current.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[groyne]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[groyne]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
  a structure built out from the bank of a navigable water in a
  direction transverse to the current in order to prevent erosion of the
  bank.

### [Docks and Boathouses]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1308703 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in section 14.

[[dock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a wharf, a pier and a
    jetty. ([quai]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[similar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[similar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means used for the same purpose ---
    commercial or recreational --- and under the same type of ownership
    --- public or private. ([similaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- docks and boathouses

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A dock or boathouse that
meets the following criteria is designated as a minor work:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dock or boathouse is situated 5 m or more from
  the adjacent property boundaries and property line extensions;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the dock or boathouse is situated more than 10 m from
  any work that is situated in, on, over, through or across the
  navigable water and is not owned by the owner of the dock or
  boathouse;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the dock or boathouse is situated 30 m or more from a
  navigation channel or, if there is no navigation channel, is not
  situated in, on, over, through or across a navigation route;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the dock or boathouse does not extend horizontally
  into, onto, over, through or across the navigable water

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} more than the length of a similar dock or boathouse
    situated 100 m or less, on the same bank, from the dock or
    boathouse, to a maximum length of 50 m, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} more than 30 m, in any other case;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the dock or boathouse does not occupy more than
  one-third of the width of the navigable water;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the dock or boathouse is not associated with an
  existing or proposed work, unless the existing or proposed work is a
  minor work; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the dock or boathouse is not used for float planes or
  other aircraft equipped with floats.

### [Slipways and Boat-Launching Ramps]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1308719 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- slipways and
boat-launching ramps

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A slipway or
boat-launching ramp that meets the following criteria is designated as a
minor work:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the slipway or boat-launching ramp does not include a
  marine railway;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the slipway or boat-launching ramp is situated 5 m or
  more from the adjacent property boundaries and property line
  extensions;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the portion of the slipway or boat-launching ramp
  that is situated below the ordinary high water mark lies on the bed of
  the navigable water; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the slipway or boat-launching ramp is not associated
  with an existing or proposed work, unless the existing or proposed
  work is a minor work.

### [Aerial Cables]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1308726 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- aerial cables

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  aerial cable that meets the following criteria is designated as a
  minor work:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cable is used exclusively for power or
    telecommunication purposes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cable is not over or across a charted navigable
    water or a navigation channel;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the cable is not over or across a Canadian heritage
    river or a river, harbour or [[historic
    canal]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the
    [Historic Canals Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-220), that is
    maintained by the Government of Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the towers and poles are not in the area between
    the ordinary high water marks on each side of the navigable water;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the cable meets the requirements of section 5.3.3
    of Overhead Systems, CAN/CSA C22.3 No. 1, as amended from time to
    time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments to the standard

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Amendments to one language version of the standard
  referred to in paragraph (1)(e) are not incorporated by reference
  until the corresponding amendments are published in the other language
  version.

### [Submarine Cables]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1308737 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- submarine cables

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A submarine cable that
meets the following criteria is designated as a minor work:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the submarine cable is used exclusively for power or
  telecommunication purposes;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the submarine cable lies on or is buried under the
  bed of the navigable water;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the submarine cable does not extend vertically above
  the bed of the navigable water more than

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the navigable water is less than 15 m in depth,
    5% of the depth of the water, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, 1 m;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the submarine cable is not situated at or under the
  entrance to any port, including a marina;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the submarine cable is not in an area where routine
  dredging occurs; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the submarine cable is not in an area that is
  identified as an anchorage area on a nautical chart produced by the
  Canadian Hydrographic Service or the National Oceanic and Atmospheric
  Administration of the United States.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-lay, rebury or remove cables

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** If a submarine cable that
was designated as a minor work under section 17 is no longer lying on or
buried under the bed of the navigable water, the owner of the submarine
cable must, as soon as feasible, re-lay the cable on the bed of the
navigable water, rebury the cable under the bed of the navigable water
or remove the cable.

### [Buried Pipelines]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1308750 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- buried pipelines

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A pipeline that meets the
following criteria is designated as a minor work:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the pipeline is buried under the bed of the navigable
  water;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the pipeline is not in an area where routine dredging
  occurs; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the pipeline is not in an area that is identified as
  an anchorage area on a nautical chart produced by the Canadian
  Hydrographic Service or the National Oceanic and Atmospheric
  Administration of the United States.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement --- water with width of more
than 50 m

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** During the construction
or placement of a pipeline that is intended to be designated as a minor
work under section 19 in navigable water the width of which is more than
50 m at the location of the burial, the owner of the pipeline must
ensure that the pipeline is buried using the horizontal directional
drilling method.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Horizontal directional drilling method
with guiding cables

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** If the owner of a
pipeline that is intended to be designated as a minor work under section
19 buries the pipeline using the horizontal directional drilling method
with guiding cables placed in the navigable water, the owner must ensure
that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} no equipment is placed in the navigable water, except
  the guiding cables and equipment required to use those cables; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the guiding cables and equipment required to use
  those cables do not extend vertically above the bed of the navigable
  water more than

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the navigable water is less than 15 m in depth,
    5% of the depth of the water, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, 1 m.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bed contours of navigable water

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** If the construction,
placement, alteration, rebuilding, removal, decommissioning, repair or
maintenance of a pipeline that is designated, or intended to be
designated, as a minor work under section 19 disturbs the bed contours
of a navigable water to the point of interfering or likely to interfere
with navigation, the owner of the pipeline must ensure, on completion of
the activity, that the bed contours of the navigable water do not and
are not likely to interfere with navigation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rebury or remove pipelines

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** If a pipeline that was
designated as a minor work under section 19 is no longer buried under
the bed of the navigable water, the owner of the pipeline must, as soon
as feasible, rebury the pipeline under the bed of the navigable water or
remove the pipeline.

### [Pipelines and Cables Used for Power or Telecommunication Purposes Attached to an Existing Work]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1308768 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- pipelines and certain
cables

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A pipeline or a cable
used exclusively for power or telecommunication purposes that meets the
following criteria is designated as a minor work:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the pipeline or cable is attached to an existing work
  that was approved, validly constructed or placed under the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the pipeline or cable does not increase the
  interference with navigation caused by the existing work.

### [Works Within an Area Bounded by a Boom]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1308773 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- certain works

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** A work that meets the
following criteria is designated as a minor work:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the work is within an area bounded by a boom that was
  approved, validly constructed or placed under the Act upstream or
  downstream of a water control structure that was approved, validly
  constructed or placed under the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the work does not adversely affect the efficacy of
  the boom;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the work does not alter either the level or the flow
  of the navigable water outside the area bounded by the boom;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the work is not related to rebuilding or altering the
  boom or the water control structure; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the work is owned by the owner of the boom or the
  water control structure.

### [Outfalls and Water Intakes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1308781 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- outfalls and water
intakes

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** An outfall or a water
intake that meets the following criteria is designated as a minor work:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the outfall or water intake does not extend
  vertically above the bed of the navigable water more than

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the navigable water is less than 15 m in depth,
    5% of the depth of the water, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, 1 m;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outfall or water intake does not alter either the
  level or the flow of the navigable water to the point of interfering
  with navigation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the outfall or water intake is more than 30 m from a
  navigation channel; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the outfall or water intake is not associated with an
  existing or proposed dam or weir or an existing or proposed reservoir
  of water created by the construction of a dam or weir.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reposition or remove

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** If an outfall or water
intake that was designated as a minor work under section 26 no longer
meets the criteria set out in paragraph 26(a), the owner of the outfall
or water intake must, as soon as feasible, reposition the outfall or
water intake to meet this criteria or remove the outfall or water
intake.

### [Dredging]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1308792 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- dredging

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Dredging to maintain the
charted width or depth of a charted navigable water or the width or
depth of any other navigable water is designated as a minor work.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  owner of dredging designated as a minor work under section 28 must
  ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the dredging uses floating pipes,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the pipes are not situated in, on, over or across
      a navigation channel, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the pipes are visible from sunset to sunrise and
      during periods of restricted visibility by the placement of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} cautionary buoys with yellow flashing lights,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} cautionary buoys with retro-reflective
        material, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} floats with retro-reflective material;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the dredging uses submerged pipes,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the pipes are not situated in, under, through or
      across a navigation channel,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the portion of the pipes not lying on the bed of
      the navigable water are marked by the placement of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} cautionary buoys with yellow flashing lights,
        or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} cautionary buoys with retro-reflective
        material, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the pipes do not extend vertically above the
      bed of the navigable water more than

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the navigable water is less than 15 m in
        depth, 5% of the depth of the water, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, 1 m;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the dredging does not use cables that are situated
    in, on, over, through or across the navigable water;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the dredging does not include blasting; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} all materials removed by the dredging are disposed
    of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on land above the ordinary high water mark, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in water where the disposal is authorized by or
      under an Act of Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- turbidity curtain

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subparagraphs (1)(a)(ii) and (b)(ii) do not apply if
  the owner of the dredging

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} uses a turbidity curtain with a yellow or orange
    flotation system marked with retro-reflective material; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensures that all dredging equipment is located
    within the area demarcated by the turbidity curtain.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bed contours of navigable water

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** If dredging designated as
a minor work under section 28 disturbs the bed contours of a navigable
water to the point of interfering, or likely to interfere, with
navigation, the owner of the dredging must ensure, on completion of the
activity, that the bed contours of the navigable water do not and are
not likely to interfere with navigation.

### [Mooring Systems]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1308823 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- mooring systems

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  mooring system that meets the following criteria is designated as a
  minor work:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on completion of its construction or placement, the
    mooring system consists of the following components:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an anchor that is set in or on the bed of a
      navigable water,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a single anchor line,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a single mooring buoy, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a mooring line that attaches to a vessel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when a vessel is moored to the mooring system, the
    swing area

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is more than 20 m from a work, other than an
      overhead cable, or from the swing area of another mooring system
      that is not owned by the owner of the mooring system,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is more than 50 m from a marina or public
      boat-launching ramp,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is more than 50 m from a navigation channel or,
      if there is no navigation channel, is not in, on, through or
      across a navigation route, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} does not exceed the maximum diameter set out in
      column 2 of the table to this section when

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the mooring system is situated in tidal
        waters, the location where the mooring system is situated has
        the depth set out in column 1 of the table according to nautical
        charts produced by the Canadian Hydrographic Service or the
        National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration of the United
        States, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the location where the
        mooring system is situated has the depth set out in column 1 of
        the table;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the mooring system is not associated with an
    existing or proposed marina;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the mooring system is situated in a location where
    the width of the navigable water is more than 100 m; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the anchor of the mooring system remains in the
    location where it was set in or on the bed of the navigable water.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vessels of more than 12 m

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a mooring
system that is designated as a minor work under subsection 31(1) must
not moor, or permit others to moor, to the mooring system a vessel that
is more than 12 m in length, as measured between the fore and aft
extremities of the vessel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal of mooring system

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a mooring
system that is designated as a minor work under subsection 31(1) must
remove the mooring system as soon as feasible if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any component of the mooring system has been removed;
  or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} during any two-year period, no vessel has moored to
  the mooring system.

### [Watercourse Crossings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1308856 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- watercourse crossings

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** A watercourse crossing,
including its abutments, footings and armouring, that meets the
following criteria is designated as a minor work:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the watercourse crossing is not a major work;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the watercourse crossing is not a zipline or other
  cable used for transportation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the watercourse crossing is situated over or across a
  navigable water and the width of the navigable water is 30 m or less;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the watercourse crossing is situated above the
  ordinary high water mark; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the watercourse crossing is designed to ensure that
  the clearance available below the watercourse crossing is at least 1 m
  more than the height of the tallest vessel that may navigate on the
  navigable water at the site where the watercourse crossing is
  situated.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Infilling

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** During the construction,
placement, alteration, rebuilding, removal, decommissioning, repair or
maintenance of a watercourse crossing that is designated, or intended to
be designated, as a minor work under section 34, the owner of the
watercourse crossing must ensure that there is no infilling of navigable
water.

### [Swim Areas]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1308866 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this section and in sections 37 and 38.

[[Indigenous governing body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indigenous governing body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a council,
    government or other entity that is authorized to act on behalf of an
    Indigenous group, community or people that holds rights recognized
    and affirmed by section 35 of the [Constitution Act,
    1982](/eng/Const/). ([corps dirigeant autochtone]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[local authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[local authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a government of a
    municipality, township, county or regional district, an Indigenous
    governing body, any other government constituted under the laws of a
    province or territory or a department of a provincial or territorial
    government or of the federal government. ([administration
    locale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[swim area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[swim area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area for swimming that
    has been demarcated with ropes by, or on behalf of, a local
    authority. ([zone de baignade]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- swim areas

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** A swim area that meets
the following criteria is designated as a minor work:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the swim area is situated 30 m or more from a
  navigation channel or, if there is no navigation channel, is not
  situated in, on, over, through or across a navigation route;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the swim area does not extend more than 30 m into or
  on the navigable water;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the swim area does not occupy more than one-third of
  the width of the navigable water;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the ropes used to demarcate the swim area are marked
  by line floats;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the perimeter of the swim area is marked by swimming
  buoys; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the swim area is situated 5 m or more from the
  adjacent property boundaries and property line extensions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- works used by swimmers

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** A work that is used by
swimmers in a swim area that is designated as a minor work under section
37 is designated as a minor work.

### [Scientific Equipment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1308882 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- scientific equipment

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Scientific equipment that meets the
  following criteria is designated as a minor work:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the scientific equipment is required to perform
    scientific research and is being used for this sole purpose;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the scientific equipment is not being used for
    geotechnical testing or geotechnical investigations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the scientific equipment is situated 30 m or more
    from a navigation channel or, if there is no navigation channel, is
    not situated in, on, over, through or across a navigation route;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the scientific equipment does not lie on the bed
    of the navigable water, it is marked by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a cautionary buoy with retro-reflective material,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an Ocean Data Acquisition System (ODAS) buoy
      with retro-reflective material;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the scientific equipment lies on the bed of the
    navigable water, it does not extend vertically above the bed of the
    navigable water more than

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the navigable water is less than 15 m in
      depth, 5% of the depth of the water, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, 1 m; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the scientific equipment is situated more than 20 m
    from any work that is situated in, on, over, under, through or
    across the navigable water and is not owned by the owner of the
    equipment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[scientific
  equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[scientific
  equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means monitoring and measurement
  devices, and any associated buoys, platforms or other similar
  structures, installed in, on, under, through or across a navigable
  water.
