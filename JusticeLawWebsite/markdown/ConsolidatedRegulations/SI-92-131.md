## Fingerprinting, Palmprinting and Photography Order {#fingerprinting-palmprinting-and-photography-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/92-131
:::

[IDENTIFICATION OF CRIMINALS ACT](/eng/acts/I-1)

Registration 1992-07-29

Fingerprinting, Palmprinting and Photography Order

P.C. 1992-1354  1992-06-18
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice and pursuant to paragraph 2(1)(b) of the
[Identification of Criminals Act](/eng/acts/I-1), is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1954-1109 of July 22,
  1954[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-92-131_e_hq_6502){#fn_SI-92-131_e_hq_6502-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-92-131_e_hq_6502-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_SI-92-131_e_hq_6502}C.R.C.
    1955, p. 2025
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of the [Identification of Criminals
  Act](/eng/acts/I-1), to sanction the measurements, processes and
  operations of fingerprinting, palmprinting and photography.
:::
