## Nova Scotia Turkey Marketing Levies Order {#nova-scotia-turkey-marketing-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 169
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Levies From
Certain Turkey Producers in Nova Scotia
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-535944 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Nova Scotia Turkey Marketing Levies
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._169).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-535946 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Natural Products Marketing Act
    of Nova Scotia; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Nova Scotia Turkey
    Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[market allotment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[market allotment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the total quantity of
    turkey meat that a producer is entitled to market in
    intraprovincial, interprovincial and export trade pursuant to the
    Canadian Turkey Marketing Quota Regulations and the orders made by
    the Commodity Board pursuant to the Plan; ([allocation de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Nova Scotia Turkey Marketing
    Plan, established and amended from time to time pursuant to the Act
    and any regulations made thereunder to give effect to the Plan;
    ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}means any person engaged in the
    production of turkeys in the Province of Nova Scotia;
    ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a turkey six months of age or
    less of any variety, grade or class that is raised or kept for
    slaughter within the Province of Nova Scotia and sold for slaughter.
    ([dindon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-535954 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay to
the Commodity Board, in addition to the licence fees set out under the
Plan, levies at the rate of \$0.05 per pound live weight on every turkey
sold by him that is in excess of his market allotment.

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-535956 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who buys turkeys from a producer shall deduct from the moneys
  payable to the producer for the turkeys all levies payable by the
  producer to the Commodity Board under section 3 and shall pay such
  levies to the Commodity Board at its office in Port Williams, Nova
  Scotia, not later than the 10th day of the month following the month
  in which the levies were deducted.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every producer shall pay to the Commodity Board at
  its office in Port Williams, Nova Scotia, all levies payable by him
  under section 3 that were not deducted and paid to the Commodity Board
  in the manner prescribed by subsection (1), not later than the 10th
  day of the month following the month in which the turkeys in respect
  of which the levies are payable were sold.
