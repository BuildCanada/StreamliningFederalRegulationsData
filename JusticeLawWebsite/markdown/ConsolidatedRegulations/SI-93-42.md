## Quebec Family Allowances Income Tax Remission Order, 1992 {#quebec-family-allowances-income-tax-remission-order-1992 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-42
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1993-04-07

Order Respecting the Remission of Income Tax Payable in Respect of
Quebec Family Allowances for 1992

P.C. 1993-515  1993-03-16
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of Finance, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote

of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of income tax
payable in respect of Quebec family allowances for
1992](/eng/regulations/SI-93-42).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-647203 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Quebec Family Allowances Income Tax Remission Order,
1992](/eng/regulations/SI-93-42).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-647205 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).
(*Loi*)

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-647207 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission of income tax is
hereby granted, to every individual who received family allowances in
1992 under An Act Respecting Family Assistance Allowances of the
Province of Quebec, of an amount equal to the aggregate of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the total amount of taxes, interest and penalties
      payable by the individual under Parts I, I.1 and I.2 of the Act
      for the 1992 taxation year

    exceeds

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the amount, if any, deemed under subsection
      120(2) of the Act to have been paid by the individual on account
      of the individual's tax under Part I of the Act for the 1992
      taxation year

  exceeds

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the excess amount that would be determined under
    subparagraph (i) for the 1992 taxation year if no amount were
    included in computing the individual's income for that year with
    respect to family allowances received under An Act Respecting Family
    Assistance Allowances of the Province of Quebec in that year, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the total of all amounts, each of which is an
    amount that would be deemed under subsections 122.2(1) or 122.5(3)
    of the Act to have been paid by the individual on account of the
    individual's tax under Part I of the Act for the 1992 taxation year,
    if no amount were included in computing the individual's income for
    that year with respect to family allowances received under An Act
    Respecting Family Assistance Allowances of the Province of Quebec in
    that year

  exceeds

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the total of all amounts, each of which is an
    amount deemed under subsections 122.2(1) or 122.5(3) of the Act to
    have been paid by the individual on account of the individual's tax
    under Part I of the Act for the 1992 taxation year.
