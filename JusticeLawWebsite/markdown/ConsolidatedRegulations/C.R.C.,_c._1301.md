## Prairie Grain Advance Payments Regulations {#prairie-grain-advance-payments-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1301
:::

[PRAIRIE GRAIN ADVANCE PAYMENTS ACT](/eng/acts/P-18)

Regulations Under the Prairie Grain Advance Payments Act
::::

:::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502818 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Prairie Grain Advance Payments
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1301).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502820 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Prairie Grain Advance
    Payments Act](/eng/acts/P-18); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Canadian Wheat Board.
    ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Applications and Endorsements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502824 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for an advance payment made by an applicant who has
  discharged his undertakings, if any, given in relation to all advance
  payments made to him in prior years, and has not received an advance
  payment in the current crop year, shall be made in accordance with
  Form AR-A of the schedule.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for an advance payment made by an
  applicant other than an applicant described in subsection (1), shall
  be made in accordance with Form AR-B of the schedule.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  producer who applies for an advance payment shall submit an
  application, completed in prescribed form in ink or indelible pencil,
  to a manager or operator of an elevator or other person authorized by
  the Board to make advance payments on its behalf.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Upon receipt of an application for an advance
  payment, the manager or operator of an elevator or other person
  authorized by the Board to make advance payments on its behalf shall
  examine the application with care and, if he is satisfied that the
  statements set forth in the application are true, shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for an advance
    payment made in accordance with Form AR-A of the schedule, approve
    the application on behalf of the Board; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for an advance
    payment made in accordance with Form AR-B of the schedule, forward
    the application to the Board for verification of the amount of the
    first advance payment made to the applicant, the deliveries made in
    respect thereof and the balance of grain remaining to be delivered
    in respect thereof, and, upon receiving verification from the Board,
    shall approve the application on behalf of the Board.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A producer shall, at the
time of making his application for an advance payment, produce to the
person to whom the application is made the permit book described in the
application.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Upon
  approval of an application in Form AR-A of the schedule, the person
  who approved the application shall place in the permit book described
  in the application the endorsement required by section 8 of the Act,
  which shall be in accordance with Form AR-C of the schedule, and shall
  sign the endorsement.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Upon approval of an application for an advance
  payment made in accordance with Form AR-B of the schedule, the person
  who approved the application shall amend the endorsement in the permit
  book by adding to it the amount of the new advance payment.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Upon the approval of an
application for an advance payment, the person who approved the
application shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} write on the outside front cover of the permit book
  the word "endorsed" and the date of the endorsement, and shall sign
  the endorsement; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} make the advance payment sought in the application.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** At the time of making the
advance payment, the person making it shall sign in ink or indelible
pencil the certificate of approval contained in the application form,
certifying that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} he has examined the application with care and is
  satisfied that the statements made therein are true;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the persons whose names appear as signatories in the
  application are the producers and all of the producers named in the
  permit book described in the application;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the permit book described in the application was
  produced to him and he has endorsed therein the endorsement required
  by the Act and these Regulations; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} he has approved of the application on behalf of the
  Board and has made the advance payment sought therein.

<!-- -->

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the recipient of an advance payment in a prior crop year has failed to
  discharge his undertaking given in respect of such advance payment and
  applies to the Board for a permit book, the Board shall place or cause
  to be placed in the permit book a transfer of endorsement form which
  shall be in accordance with Form AR-C of the schedule.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} After a transfer of endorsement has been placed in a
  permit book, the person who placed the transfer of endorsement in the
  permit book shall write on the outside front cover of the permit book
  the word "endorsed" and the date of the transfer of endorsement, and
  shall sign the endorsement.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Where the recipient of an
advance payment has discharged his undertaking, the Board shall cancel
the endorsement in his permit book and shall cause the cancellation in
Form AR-E of the schedule.

## [Prescribed Quota Limitation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502849 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), for the
  purpose of subsection 7(2) of the Act, the number of tonnes prescribed
  for the purpose of making advances under the Act shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 0.270 tonnes per quota acre, in respect of wheat;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 0.230 tonnes per quota acre, in respect of oats;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 0.435 tonnes per quota acre, in respect of barley.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The maximum quantity of any grain deliverable under
  an applicant's permit book in respect of any application made after
  the commencement of a crop year shall be reduced by a quantity equal
  to the difference between

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of that grain that the applicant was
    authorized to deliver to the Board under the permit book during that
    crop year under the same quotas; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of that grain delivered to the Board
    by the applicant under the permit book during that crop year under
    general quotas that have terminated prior to his application.

## [Prescribed Grade and Advance Payment Rate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502860 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsections 7(4) and (5) of the Act and for the crop
  year commencing on August 1, 1986,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed grade of wheat is No. 1 Canada
    Western Red Spring;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed grade of oats is No. 1 Feed; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed grade of barley is No. 1 Feed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The advance payment rate for the crop year commencing
  on August 1, 1986 shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$87 per tonne for wheat;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$50 per tonne for oats; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$53 per tonne for barley.

## [Interest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502872 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Act requires interest to be paid on advance payments, the annual
  rate of interest payable on the advance payments shall be equal to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the period beginning on August 1 in a year
    and ending on January 31 of the following year, the lowest of the
    annual lending rates offered by the banks listed in subsection (2)
    to their best commercial customers on the first banking day within
    that period, plus 1½ per cent; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during the period beginning on February 1 in a year
    and ending on July 31 of that year, the lowest of the annual lending
    rates offered by the banks listed in subsection (2) to their best
    commercial customers on the first banking day within that period,
    plus 1½ per cent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the banks are:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} The Royal Bank of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Canadian Imperial Bank of Commerce;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Bank of Montreal;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} The Bank of Nova Scotia; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} The Toronto-Dominion Bank.

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502885 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall make such
reports to the Minister in respect of advance payments as the Minister
may require.

## [Collections]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502887 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Where a recipient is in
default in respect of his undertaking, the Board or Her Majesty may
effect collection of the amount in default and any interest thereon by
instituting proceedings in the Federal Court of Canada.

## [Emergency Advance Payments for Unthreshed Grain]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502889 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Board may, in the
1984-85 crop year, upon application therefor made before June 15, 1985,
make advance payments to producers for unthreshed grain in respect of
the 1984-85 crop year.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/84-952, s. 1
:::

**[[16.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16.1 .sectionLabel}** The Board may, in the
1985-86 crop year, upon an application therefor made before June 1,
1986, make advance payments to producers for unthreshed grain in respect
of the 1985-86 crop year.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-1112, s. 1
:::

## [Emergency Advance Payments for the Drying of Damp or Tough Grain]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502896 .Part}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Board may, in the
1985-86 crop year, upon application therefor made before June 1, 1986,
make advance payments in respect of the 1985-86 crop year to enable
producers of grain to better finance the drying of damp or tough grain.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-1013, s. 1
:::
