## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676515 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} )

[[distribution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[distribution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a trade by or on behalf of a bank holding company
      in securities of the bank holding company that have not previously
      been issued; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a trade in previously issued securities of a bank
      holding company from the holdings of any person or group of
      persons who act in concert and who hold in excess of 10% of the
      shares of any class of voting shares of the bank holding
      company. ([mise en circulation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[regulated securities entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regulated securities entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    financial institution whose primary business is dealing in
    securities and that is regulated in its securities dealings by an
    Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province or by the laws
    of a foreign country. ([entité de valeurs mobilières
    réglementée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Holding of Bank Holding Company's Shares by Subsidiary]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676523 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where subsidiary may hold shares

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 714(c) of the Act, a bank holding company may, subject to
section 3, permit a subsidiary of the bank holding company that is a
regulated securities entity to hold shares of the bank holding company
if the aggregate value of shares of the bank holding company held by all
subsidiaries of the bank holding company that are regulated securities
entities, other than shares referred to in section 716 of the Act, does
not exceed 1% of the regulatory capital of the bank holding company.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception for securities underwriter

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** If a subsidiary of a bank
holding company is a regulated securities entity and is acting as a
securities underwriter in connection with a distribution of shares of
the bank holding company, the bank holding company may permit the
subsidiary to hold shares of the bank holding company in excess of the
limit set out in section 2 until the distribution is ended.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676528 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

section 183 of the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada

into force.
