## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1354416 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Code.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [College of Immigration and
      Citizenship Consultants
      Act](/eng/acts/C-33.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[client]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[client]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person or entity that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has entered into a consultation agreement or
        service agreement with a licensee;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} consults with a licensee who provides or agrees
        to provide immigration or citizenship consulting services to
        them; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} having consulted with a licensee, reasonably
        concludes that the licensee has agreed to provide immigration or
        citizenship consulting services to
        them. ([client]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Code, a conflict of interest
  exists if there is a substantial risk that a licensee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} may improperly further their private interests or
    another person's interests, resulting in a material compromise to
    the licensee's ability to fulfill their professional obligations to
    a client; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cannot fulfill their professional obligations to a
    client without materially compromising the licensee's ability to
    fulfill their professional obligations to another client or a former
    client.

## [Purpose and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1354429 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Code sets out the
standards of professional conduct and competence that must be met by
licensees of the College.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Code applies to
licensees, including in respect of any [pro bono]{.otherLang lang="la"}
immigration or citizenship consulting services offered or provided by
them.

## [General Standards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1354434 .Part}

### [Professional Conduct]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1354435 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standards of profession

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee must uphold the standards of the profession and perform their
  professional obligations honourably and with integrity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct unbecoming

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensee must not engage in conduct that is likely
  to discredit the profession or jeopardize the public's confidence and
  trust in the profession.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of loyalty

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A licensee must be loyal to
their clients, including by avoiding conflicts of interest and by being
committed to their clients' cause.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of honesty and candour

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A licensee must be honest
and candid when advising their clients.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of civility

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A licensee must be
courteous and civil in all of their professional dealings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Relationship of trust

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A licensee must foster a
relationship of trust with their clients, including by not taking
advantage of their clients' vulnerabilities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with applicable legislation

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A licensee must demonstrate
law-abidance through compliance with all applicable legislation,
including the Act and any regulations and by-laws made under the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discrimination

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A licensee must not, in
any of their professional dealings, engage in a discriminatory practice
within the meaning of the [Canadian Human Rights Act](/eng/acts/H-6).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intimidation and coercion

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A licensee must not, in
any of their professional dealings, intimidate or coerce any person,
including by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} applying undue pressure, directly or indirectly;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} making physical or verbal threats;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} engaging in any form of harassment, including sexual
  harassment;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} using their knowledge about social and cultural norms
  to exploit a situation;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} untruthfully asserting possible sanctions or
  administrative consequences; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} threatening, without reasonable grounds, to file a
  complaint with law enforcement authorities alleging a contravention of
  a federal or provincial law, initiate a criminal proceeding or make a
  complaint to an administrative or regulatory body.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dishonesty, fraud or illegal conduct

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A licensee must not, in
any of their professional dealings, knowingly assist in or encourage
dishonesty, fraud or illegal conduct.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inducement

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee must not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} offer an inducement to any organization or person
    for recommending the licensee to a client or referring a client to
    the licensee; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} solicit or accept an inducement from any
    organization or person for recommending the organization or person
    to a client or referring a client to the organization or person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(a) does not apply in respect of an
  inducement offered to an agent who solicits clients for a licensee if
  the licensee registers the agent's name with the College in advance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A fee referred to in paragraph 17(3)(d) or 18(3)(d)
  is not an inducement for the purposes of paragraph (1)(b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Original documents

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee must not take possession of any of a client's original
  documents unless the possession is for one of the following purposes
  and the documents are returned to the client as soon as that purpose
  has been achieved:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} making copies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} complying with a legal requirement or a requirement
    of a government authority; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a purpose to which the client has consented in
    writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Client documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensee must, at the request of the client,
  deliver to the client any documents or information in the licensee's
  possession that pertain to the client's application or expression of
  interest or to a proceeding in which the client is a party.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflicts of interest

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 16 to 18, a
  licensee must not provide immigration or citizenship consulting
  services to a client if doing so would or could result in a conflict
  of interest unless the licensee has disclosed the nature and extent of
  the conflict to the client in writing and the client provides free and
  informed consent in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to avoid conflict

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a licensee must not provide
  immigration or citizenship consulting services to a client, even with
  their consent, if doing so would result in a conflict of interest,
  unless the licensee has reasonable grounds to believe that they are
  able to advise and represent that client without compromising

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their objectivity or the relationship of trust with
    any of their clients; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} their duty of confidentiality towards any of their
    clients or former clients.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unauthorized behaviours

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following behaviours constitute a conflict of interest to which a
  client cannot consent:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a licensee directly or indirectly lending money to,
    or borrowing money from, a client;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a licensee directly or indirectly undertaking any
    transactions with a client that are not in relation to the provision
    of immigration or citizenship consulting services; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a licensee having an intimate personal relationship
    with a client or a former client within one year after the day on
    which the service agreement is completed or terminated before its
    completion, unless the client is the licensee's spouse at the time
    that the immigration or citizenship consulting services are provided
    or has cohabited with the licensee in a conjugal relationship for a
    period of at least one year before the services are provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- paragraph (1)(b)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A client may consent to the behaviour referred to in
  paragraph (1)(b) if the transaction is fair and reasonable and the
  client has confirmed to the licensee in writing that they have
  received independent advice in respect of the transaction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[employment recruitment
services]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of this section, [[employment recruitment
  services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following services:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} seeking or obtaining employment for a client;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} assisting or advising any person with respect to
    seeking or obtaining employment for a client;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} assisting or advising an employer or another person
    with respect to hiring a client; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} referring a client to another person who offers the
    services referred to in any of paragraphs (a), (b) or (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest --- employment
  recruitment services

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensee is in a conflict of interest if they
  provide both immigration or citizenship consulting services and
  employment recruitment services to a client who is a [[foreign
  national]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
  [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} However, a licensee may provide both immigration or
  citizenship consulting services and employment recruitment services to
  a client who is a foreign national if the licensee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before providing those services, advises the client
    that they are not obligated to receive both services from the same
    individual and obtains the client's free and informed consent in
    writing to proceed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensures that the service agreement clearly
    differentiates between the immigration or citizenship consulting
    services and the employment recruitment services that will be
    provided to the client;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not directly or indirectly charge a fee or
    disbursement to the client for any employment recruitment services;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} discloses to the client the fees that the licensee
    is receiving from an employer for recruiting the client to work for
    the employer;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} complies with all applicable legislation governing
    the provision of employment recruitment services; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} demonstrates honesty and candour towards the client
    and commitment to the client's cause, including by providing the
    client, before they begin working in Canada, with a copy of their
    employment contract and accurate information regarding the work that
    they will be doing and their wages, benefits and working conditions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[student recruitment
services]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of this section, [[student recruitment
  services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following services:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} seeking or obtaining enrollment for a client at an
    institution that provides education or training to international
    students;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} assisting or advising any person with respect to
    seeking or obtaining enrollment for a client at an institution that
    provides education or training to international students;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} assisting or advising a representative of an
    institution that provides education or training to international
    students or any person with respect to admitting a client to such an
    institution; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} referring a client to another person who offers the
    services referred to in any of paragraphs (a), (b) or (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest --- student
  recruitment services

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensee is in a conflict of interest if they
  provide both immigration or citizenship consulting services and
  student recruitment services to a client who is a [[foreign
  national]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
  [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), unless the
  licensee is providing those services in their capacity as a salaried
  employee of an institution that provides education or training to
  international students.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} However, a licensee may provide both immigration or
  citizenship consulting services and student recruitment services to a
  client who is a foreign national if the licensee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before providing those services, advises the client
    that they are not obligated to receive both services from the same
    individual and obtains the client's free and informed consent in
    writing to proceed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensures that the service agreement clearly
    differentiates between the immigration or citizenship consulting
    services and the student recruitment services that will be provided
    to the client;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not directly or indirectly charge a fee or
    disbursement to the client for any student recruitment services;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} discloses to the client the fees that the licensee
    is receiving from an institution that provides education or training
    to international students for recruiting the client to study at that
    institution;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} complies with all applicable legislation governing
    the provision of student recruitment services; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} demonstrates honesty and candour towards the client
    and commitment to the client's cause, including by providing the
    client with accurate information regarding

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the institution they will be enrolled in, which
      includes providing a copy of their enrollment agreement, if any,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} their program of study,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the tuition fees and the institution's refund
      policy, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the services, support and benefits that the
      institution will be providing.

### [Competence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1354533 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Competence and diligence

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee must fulfill their professional obligations competently and
  diligently and must refrain from providing any immigration or
  citizenship consulting services that they are not competent to provide
  or that are beyond the scope of their licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nature of competence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} To fulfill their professional obligations
  competently, a licensee must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have the knowledge and experience necessary to
    provide the required immigration or citizenship consulting services
    and offer sound and comprehensive advice to a client, including
    in-depth knowledge of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) and the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29) and
    any related programs and policies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have the oral and written communication skills
    necessary to protect a client's interests and present a client's
    case firmly and persuasively and within the limits of the law,
    including the ability to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} make clear and cogent oral and written
      representations in legal proceedings,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} identify the salient points in an argument and
      respond to them effectively in the course of a hearing, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} know when it is appropriate to apply for an
      adjournment of a hearing and argue effectively for it;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} meet any applicable provincial competency
    requirements in relation to the provision of immigration or
    citizenship consulting services;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be able to provide the services to the client in at
    least one of the official languages of Canada;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be able to deliver the services to the client using
    technology that is appropriate and effective; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} maintain a good working knowledge of the
    regulations and by-laws made under the Act that relate to the
    professional conduct and competence of licensees and of any related
    policies, procedures and guidelines of the College.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation if not competent

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee who lacks the competence to provide the required immigration
  or citizenship consulting services must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} decline to act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with the client's consent, obtain assistance from
    another individual who is authorized to provide representation or
    advice under section 91 of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) or section 21.1 of the [Citizenship
    Act](/eng/acts/C-29) and who is competent to provide those services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements --- services of another

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a licensee obtains assistance from another
  individual to provide the required immigration or citizenship
  consulting services,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the terms of the arrangement, as well as the name
    of the other individual who will be providing the services and the
    scope of the services, must be disclosed to the client in writing;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any fees or disbursements in relation to the
    services provided by the other individual are subject to subsection
    31(3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintaining competence

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A licensee must maintain
the level of knowledge and skills required for the class of licence that
they hold.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delivering quality services

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  providing immigration or citizenship consulting services to a client,
  a licensee must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} comply with the applicable deadlines and timelines
    for an application, expression of interest or proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} conduct the client's affairs in an efficient and
    cost-effective manner;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} communicate with the client in a timely and
    effective manner;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} demonstrate cultural sensitivity;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} obtain assistance, when necessary, including by
    retaining the services of an interpreter or translator; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, provide instructions and guidance on
    how the client may access online information regarding their
    application, expression of interest or proceeding, including any
    associated processing information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Actions taken when representing client

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When representing a client in respect of an
  application, expression of interest or proceeding, the licensee must
  ensure that all the necessary documents and information are, as
  applicable, properly prepared, signed and submitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Keeping client informed

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The licensee must provide timely information to the
  client in writing concerning the status of their case, including by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notifying the client when a document or information
    has been submitted or received on the client's behalf; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on request, providing a copy of the documents that
    were submitted or received.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpreter or translator

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A licensee who retains the services of an interpreter
  or a translator must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} instruct the interpreter or translator to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} accurately translate what is said, with no
      additions or modifications, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} keep the information confidential; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} take reasonable measures to ensure that the
    interpreter or translator

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is certified by an organization that is legally
      authorized to certify interpreters or translators, as the case may
      be, for the languages in question, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if no certification process is available, is
      fluent in reading, writing or speaking the client's language,
      depending on the client's needs.

## [Relationship to Clients]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1354582 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial consultation

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  a licensee has an initial consultation with a potential client with
  respect to the provision of immigration or citizenship consulting
  services, the licensee must enter into a written consultation
  agreement with that potential client.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of consultation agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The consultation agreement must include the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensee's name, registration number, address,
    telephone number and email address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the potential client's name and contact
    information, including their address, telephone number and email
    address, if any;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fee for the consultation or, if the
    consultation is provided [pro bono]{.otherLang lang="la"}, a
    statement to that effect;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a brief description of the College's role as the
    licensee's regulator; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the purpose and scope of the
    consultation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of agreement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The licensee must keep a copy of the signed
  consultation agreement for their records and provide a copy to the
  client.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service agreement

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee must enter into a written service agreement with a client
  before any immigration or citizenship consulting services are provided
  or, if there was an initial consultation, before any additional
  immigration or citizenship consulting services are provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preconditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before entering into a service agreement with a
  client, a licensee must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} confirm the following information in respect of the
    client, seeking corroboration of the information if possible:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their full name,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} their home address and their personal telephone
      number and email address, if any, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} their business address, telephone number and
      email address, if applicable;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the client with a draft of the service
    agreement; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} verify whether the client has entered into a
    service agreement with another individual who is authorized to
    provide representation or advice under section 91 of the
    [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) or section
    21.1 of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29) and, if so,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} confirm that the service agreement with the other
      individual has been completed or has been terminated in writing
      before completion and, if applicable, confirm the outcome of the
      completed service agreement, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} obtain clear instructions from the client on the
      scope of the service agreement, if the client wishes to engage the
      services of both the licensee and the other individual.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of service agreement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The service agreement must include the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensee's name, registration number, address,
    telephone number and email address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information referred to in subparagraphs
    (2)(a)(i) to (iii);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a summary of any preliminary advice given to the
    client by the licensee;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the licensee endeavours to provide
    quality immigration or citizenship consulting services and to
    adequately supervise any person who assists in the provision of
    those services;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the names of the people who are likely to assist
    the licensee in the provision of immigration or citizenship
    consulting services;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the client's instructions;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} an itemized list of the services to be provided,
    tailored to the needs of the client, that describes the nature of
    the services and their scope;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} estimated time frames for the delivery of the
    services;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an estimate of fees, including the hourly rate and
    the anticipated number of hours, or an agreed fixed fee or, if the
    services are provided [pro bono]{.otherLang lang="la"}, a statement
    to that effect;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} an estimate of expected disbursements;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} any goods and services tax, harmonized sales tax or
    other tax or levy to be charged to the client;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the terms of payment for fees and disbursements,
    including any interest payable on unpaid amounts;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} any advance payments to be made by the client and
    the licensee's refund policy;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of any additional costs that the
    client may be required to pay;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, a description of any conflict of
    interest or potential conflict of interest relating to the client;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} a statement that any original documents provided by
    the client to the licensee will be returned to the client as soon as
    the purpose for which the licensee took possession of the documents
    has been achieved;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the licensee has an obligation of
    confidentiality under this Code and a description of the manner in
    which the licensee will maintain the confidentiality of the client's
    information and documents;

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} the licensee's complaint-handling procedure;

  - [(s)]{.lawlabel} the official language of Canada in which the
    services will be provided;

  - [(t)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the licensee will provide timely
    information related to the status of the client's case;

  - [(u)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the licensee will obtain
    assistance, when necessary, including by retaining the services of
    an interpreter or translator;

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a description of the College's role as the
    licensee's regulator and an explanation of the College's complaints
    process;

  - [(w)]{.lawlabel} an explanation that the College may require the
    production of documents in accordance with the Act and any
    regulations or by-laws made under the Act;

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the licensee has provided a copy
    of this Code to the client;

  - [(y)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of what will happen to the client's
    file if the licensee becomes incapacitated or is otherwise unable to
    continue providing services under the agreement; and

  - [(z)]{.lawlabel} any other terms agreed to.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of agreement

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The licensee must keep a copy of the signed service
  agreement for their records and provide a copy to the client.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments to agreement

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any amendments to the service agreement must be
  agreed to by the client and the licensee in writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of sections 23 and 24

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Sections 23 and 24 do not
apply to a licensee who is a salaried employee of an institution that
provides education or training to international students or an
organization that represents such an institution if the following
conditions are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensee, in their capacity as an employee of the
  institution or organization, provides immigration or citizenship
  consulting services to the institution or organization, employees of
  the institution or organization or current or prospective students of
  the institution;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} no fees are payable to the licensee by the employees
  or current or prospective students of the institution or organization
  in respect of the services;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the licensee only provides advice --- not
  representation --- in connection with the submission of an expression
  of interest under subsection 10.1(3) of the [Immigration and Refugee
  Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) or a proceeding or application under
  that Act or under the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the licensee discloses to the institution or
  organization and the employees or current or prospective students to
  whom the services are provided that the licensee is regulated by the
  College and is subject to this Code, but that the obligations set out
  in sections 23 and 24 do not apply;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the licensee provides information about the College's
  complaints process to the institution or organization and the
  employees or current or prospective students to whom the services are
  provided; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the licensee provides information about the
  licensee's or their employer's complaints-handling procedure to the
  current or prospective students to whom the services are provided.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from application of sections 23
and 24

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The College may exempt a
licensee from the application of sections 23 and 24 if the College is
satisfied that the following conditions are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensee is a salaried employee of an
  organization other than an institution or organization referred to in
  section 25 and, in that capacity, provides immigration or citizenship
  consulting services to the organization or its employees;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the primary business of the organization is not the
  provision of immigration or citizenship consulting services;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} no fees are payable to the licensee by employees of
  the organization in respect of the services;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the licensee discloses to the organization and the
  employees to whom the services are provided that the licensee is
  regulated by the College and is subject to this Code, but that the
  obligations set out in sections 23 and 24 do not apply;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the licensee provides information about the College's
  complaints process to the organization and the employees to whom the
  services are provided; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the licensee is not providing employment recruitment
  services to the organization or the employees.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opinion to be provided

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** If a licensee is of the
opinion that a client's proposed application, expression of interest or
proceeding is futile, unfounded or has little or no hope of success, the
licensee must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide the client with a written opinion that sets
  out the licensee's reasoning; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if, despite the opinion, the client wishes to pursue
  the matter, obtain written acknowledgement from the client of the
  risks of doing so.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation of confidentiality

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee must keep confidential all information in relation to a
  client or former client, or a client's or former client's business
  affairs, that was acquired in the course of their professional
  relationship with that client and take the measures that are necessary
  to maintain the confidentiality of that information indefinitely.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of confidential information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensee must not disclose any information referred
  to in subsection (1), or allow such information to be disclosed,
  unless the disclosure is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} authorized by the client;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} required or authorized by law;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} required to comply with a subpoena or warrant
    issued or an order made by a court, person or body with jurisdiction
    to compel the production of information, or to comply with rules of
    court relating to the production of records;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} required by the College in the exercise of its
    powers under the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} made to a person who is assisting the licensee in
    the provision of immigration or citizenship consulting services to
    the client;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} necessary to collect an outstanding account; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} required in the context of judicial or
    administrative proceedings to defend the licensee or a person who is
    assisting the licensee in the provision of immigration or
    citizenship consulting services against allegations that the
    licensee or the person has

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} committed an offence involving a client's
      affairs,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} committed a violation involving a client's
      affairs in respect of which administrative penalties and
      consequences may be imposed,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} engaged their civil liability for an act or
      omission in relation to a client's affairs, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} engaged in conduct that is subject to
      investigation by the College or a law society of a province or the
      Chambre des notaires du Québec.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scope of disclosure

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a licensee discloses confidential information
  under subsection (2), the licensee must not disclose more information
  than necessary to respond to the specific basis for the disclosure.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaints

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** A licensee must promptly
respond to any complaints made to the licensee by a client in respect of
the immigration or citizenship consulting services provided or in
respect of any person assisting the licensee in the provision of those
services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Errors or omissions

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  licensee is responsible for an error or omission, in respect of a
  client's case, that results or may result in prejudice to the client
  and that cannot be readily corrected, the licensee must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} promptly and fully inform the client, the
    licensee's professional liability insurer and the Registrar of the
    error or omission;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} promptly recommend that the client obtain legal
    advice concerning any rights that the client may have arising from
    the error or omission;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} promptly provide confirmation to the Registrar that
    the licensee's professional liability insurer has been informed of
    the error or omission; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} determine if it is appropriate to continue
    providing immigration or citizenship consulting services to the
    client.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- liability insurance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a licensee is covered by an employer's liability
  insurance in respect of the error or omission, the reference in
  paragraph (1)(a) and (c) to the licensee's professional liability
  insurer is to be read as a reference to the employer's liability
  insurer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  fees charged to a client by a licensee for immigration or citizenship
  consulting services must be fair and reasonable in the circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disbursements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any disbursements charged to a client by a licensee
  for immigration or citizenship consulting services must not exceed the
  actual amount of the disbursement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional fees or disbursements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If any fees or disbursements will exceed the
  estimates or fixed amounts agreed to in the service agreement, or if
  any new disbursements arise, a licensee must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} inform the client of the additional fees or
    disbursements; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} obtain the client's agreement in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unnecessary increases --- fees or
  disbursements

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A licensee must not undertake work that unnecessarily
  increases fees or disbursements.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advance payments

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** A licensee who receives
an advance payment from a client for immigration or citizenship
consulting services must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} hold the funds in trust in a client account, opened
  by the licensee, at a financial institution that is approved by the
  College;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on receipt of an advance payment, provide the client
  with a receipt that clearly indicates the amount of the advance
  payment and deposit the funds into the client account;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} refrain from depositing any funds not belonging to a
  client into the client account;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} maintain separate records of deposits and withdrawals
  for each client;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} use the funds held in the client account only for the
  purpose for which they were paid to the licensee;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} before withdrawing funds from the client account,
  invoice the client in accordance with subsection 33(1); and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} not later than 30 days after the day on which the
  client is invoiced under paragraph (f), withdraw the invoiced amount
  from the client account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Invoice

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee may invoice a client only once they have provided immigration
  or citizenship consulting services to the client or have made
  disbursements on the client's behalf.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Description of services and
  disbursements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every invoice issued by the licensee must contain a
  full description of the services and disbursements to which the
  invoice relates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When a payment is received from a client, the
  licensee must provide the client with a receipt that clearly indicates
  the invoice to which the payment relates.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of service agreement

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 35, a
licensee may terminate a service agreement before its completion only if
reasonable notice is provided to the client and the termination

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is done for good reason, such as the client having

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} deceived the licensee,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} failed to give adequate instructions to the
    licensee,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} failed to follow the licensee's advice on a
    significant point, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} failed to pay the licensee's fees or disbursements
    as agreed; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} will not result in serious prejudice to the client.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory termination of service
agreement

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee must terminate a service agreement before its completion if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the client no longer wishes to receive the services
    from the licensee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the client, despite advice provided in accordance
    with subsection (2), asks the licensee to act in a manner that is
    dishonest, fraudulent or illegal or that would not meet the
    standards of professional conduct and competence that are
    established by this Code or would contravene a provision of a
    regulation or a by-law made under the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the continued provision of the services would place
    the licensee in a conflict of interest, unless the licensee obtains
    the client's consent in accordance with section 15; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the licensee lacks the competence to continue
    serving the client and fulfilling their professional obligations and
    does not obtain assistance from another individual in accordance
    with paragraph 20(1)(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dishonest or illegal conduct

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a client asks the licensee to act in a manner that
  is dishonest, fraudulent or illegal or that would not meet the
  standards of professional conduct and competence that are established
  by this Code or would contravene a provision of a regulation or a
  by-law made under the Act, the licensee must advise the client of that
  fact and that the conduct should not be pursued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Referral to other licensee

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a service agreement is terminated in accordance
  with paragraph (1)(c) or (d), the licensee must, if possible, refer
  the client to another individual who is authorized to provide
  representation or advice under section 91 of the [Immigration and
  Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) or section 21.1 of the
  [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29) and who is competent to provide the
  immigration or citizenship consulting services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Actions required --- completion or
termination

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  service agreement is completed or terminated before its completion a
  licensee must, not later than 30 days after the day on which the
  service agreement is completed or terminated,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the licensee is in possession of any of the
    client's documents or anything else that belongs to the client,
    return them to the client;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide an accounting of all funds received from
    the client;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} issue a final invoice to the client for any amounts
    owed by the client for services rendered and disbursements made; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} refund to the client any funds held in trust on the
    client's behalf that are in excess of the invoiced amount.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Actions required --- termination

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the service agreement is terminated before its
  completion, the licensee must also

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide the client with all information in the
    licensee's possession that may be required in connection with the
    client's file;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cooperate with a successor representative, if any,
    to minimize expense and avoid prejudice to the client; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the licensee is listed as the client's
    representative with any department or agency of the government of
    Canada or of a province, provide notice that they are no longer
    representing the client to that department or agency

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with the department or agency's
      procedures, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if no procedures exist, in writing within 10
      days after the day on which the service agreement is terminated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of file

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a client requests that their file be transferred
  to another representative, the licensee must, even if there are
  payments outstanding, deliver all documents relating to the client's
  file to that representative not later than 10 business days after the
  day on which the transfer request is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delay

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If it is, for reasons beyond the licensee's control,
  not feasible to deliver the documents within the time frame referred
  to in subsection (3), the licensee must advise the client and the
  other representative of the delay and deliver the documents not later
  than 30 days after the day on which the transfer request is made.

## [Office Administration and Management]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1354753 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** A licensee must maintain
a reliable system of office administration in relation to the
immigration or citizenship consulting services that they provide and
keep and preserve records in accordance with the by-laws made under the
Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with Code

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee must ensure that a person who assists in the provision of
  immigration or citizenship consulting services

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is of good character;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is provided with a copy of this Code and is
    familiar with its contents; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not carry out any acts that, if carried out by
    the licensee, would contravene this Code.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Professional responsibility

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensee must supervise and assume professional
  responsibility for any work done by a person who assists in the
  provision of immigration or citizenship consulting services and ensure
  that the level of supervision is adequate for the type of work in
  question.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a licensee may delegate
  certain aspects of their work to a person who is not a licensee but
  must ensure that the person does not provide representation or advice
  in contravention of section 91 of the [Immigration and Refugee
  Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) or section 21.1 of the [Citizenship
  Act](/eng/acts/C-29).

## [Relationship to the College and Other Persons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1354766 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory reporting to College

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** If any of the following
circumstances arise, a licensee must report the circumstances to the
Registrar not later than 30 days after the day on which they arise:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensee becomes bankrupt or insolvent;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licensee is suffering from a physical or mental
  health problem or an addiction, verified by a health care
  professional, that limits the licensee's capacity to practise;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the licensee is charged with or found guilty of an
  offence under an Act of Parliament;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the licensee is subject to disciplinary or remedial
  measures --- imposed by a tribunal, a regulatory body, an employment
  or human rights board or a similar body --- in relation to any aspect
  of their professional affairs;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the licensee is named as a defendant in a civil
  action arising from or relating to the licensee's professional
  affairs;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the licensee has discovered that an error was made
  with respect to the use or attribution of funds held in trust in a
  client account; or

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the licensee is subject to a formal complaint or a
  disciplinary or remedial measure by an employer in relation to the
  provision of immigration or citizenship consulting services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response to College

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** A licensee must respond
fully to any communication from the College in which a response is
specifically requested and must do so promptly or, if a time for
responding is specified in the communication from the College, within
that time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communications with complainant

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee must not communicate directly or indirectly with a person who
  has made a complaint to the College about the licensee, or with the
  superior of such a person, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the College has consented to the communication in
    writing; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licensee complies with any terms stipulated by
    the College.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Current clients

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the complainant is a current client and steps have
  not been taken to terminate the service agreement with that client,
  the licensee must continue to fulfill their obligations to the client
  but must only communicate with the client as permitted under
  subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct of fellow licensee

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  licensee suspects on reasonable grounds that a fellow licensee has
  engaged in conduct that is more than trivially inconsistent with this
  Code or obtains any information that raises a material concern as to
  the competence, integrity or capacity to practise of a fellow
  licensee, the licensee must promptly report the conduct or information
  to the College.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reportable conduct

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Without limiting the generality of subsection (1),
  information regarding any of the following occurrences must be
  reported to the College:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a fellow licensee misappropriates or misapplies
    funds belonging to a client;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a fellow licensee fails to comply with an order of
    a court or a decision made under subsection 68(1) or 69(3) of the
    Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a client files a civil claim against a fellow
    licensee in relation to conduct that is likely to discredit the
    profession or jeopardize the public's confidence and trust in the
    profession;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a fellow licensee abandons their immigration or
    citizenship consulting services practice;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a fellow licensee participates in serious criminal
    activity related to their practice; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any situation in which a fellow licensee's clients
    are likely to be severely prejudiced.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A licensee is not required to report conduct or
  information to the College under subsection (1) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} doing so would result in the breach of a duty of
    confidentiality that is imposed by or under an Act of Parliament or
    of the legislature of a province; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licensee knows that the matter has already been
    reported to the College.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False, misleading or inaccurate public
statement

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** A licensee must not make
a false, misleading or inaccurate public statement about another
licensee, the College or any staff or person whose services are retained
by the College.

## [Marketing of Immigration or Citizenship Consulting Services]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1354802 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marketing of services

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee who markets immigration or citizenship consulting services
  must ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their name, as registered with the College, is
    prominently displayed or announced at or near the beginning of any
    advertisement for the services, in the language used in the
    advertisement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any written advertisement for the services includes
    the Internet address of the College's online public register of
    licensees; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the marketing is in the best interests of the
    public and is consistent with a high standard of professionalism.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False, misleading or inaccurate
  representations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In marketing immigration or citizenship consulting
  services, a licensee must not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make false, misleading or inaccurate
    representations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} guarantee the success of an application, expression
    of interest or proceeding; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} imply that the licensee has a relationship with the
    Government of Canada or a provincial government.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Client endorsement

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** A licensee may use an
endorsement by a client or former client in the marketing of their
immigration or citizenship consulting services only if the endorsement

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} was actually given by a client or former client;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is true and accurate; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} has been reviewed and approved for public use by the
  client or former client in writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identifying marks of College

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** A licensee may use the
College's name, logo or other identifying mark only as permitted by the
College.

## [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1354821 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency with Council by-laws and
regulations

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** In the event of an
inconsistency or conflict between this Code and any by-laws of the
Immigration Consultants of Canada Regulatory Council, or any regulations
made by that Council's board of directors, that remain in effect by
virtue of paragraph 85(7)(o) of the Act, this Code prevails to the
extent of the inconsistency or conflict.

## [Establishment and Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1354824 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** This Code is established
and comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
