## War Service Grants Regulations {#war-service-grants-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1601
:::

[WAR SERVICE GRANTS ACT](/eng/acts/W-2.5)

Regulations Made Pursuant to the War Service Grants Act
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-531581 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [War Service Grants
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1601).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-531583 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [War Service Grants
      Act](/eng/acts/W-2.5); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Advisory Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Advisory Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      Re-establishment Credit Advisory Committee consisting of not less
      than two nor more than three employees of the Department appointed
      by the Minister in and for any district; ([Comité
      consultatif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of
      Veterans Affairs. ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Other words and expressions in these Regulations have
  the same meaning as in the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-307, s. 1(F)
:::

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-531592 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall cause
such records to be kept as he may consider necessary for the proper
administration of the Act.

## [War Service Gratuity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-531594 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  favourable decision in any case by two members of the Committee of
  Review shall be final, and an unfavourable decision can be made only
  by three members.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of every decision of the Committee of Review
  shall be placed on the file of the member of the forces concerned held
  at the head office of the Department.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The gratuity or any portion
thereof payable to a person who qualifies therefor in respect of a
member of the forces who dies on service or after discharge, but before
such member has been paid in full, shall be paid in one lump sum.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Any overpayment of pay and
allowances as described in subsection 6(1) of the Act shall be deducted
from the gratuity, but such overpayment shall not be deemed to include
overpayment of pay and allowances made in respect of service in the
forces of Her Majesty other than those raised in Canada to a person
claiming benefits by virtue of section 26 of the Act.

## [Re-establishment Credit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-531600 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may refer any application to an Advisory Committee for its
  advice and recommendation respecting the qualifications of the
  applicant for re-establishment credit in relation to the objects for
  which the credit is proposed to be used and the soundness of the
  investment proposed by the applicant.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person applying for a credit may request that his
  application be referred to an Advisory Committee.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No credit shall be made available to a member for the
  purchase of a business unless the application therefor is first
  referred to the Advisory Committee established for the district in
  which the member resides or the district in which he proposes to use
  the credit, for the purpose of securing its advice as to the soundness
  of the investment in the case of such member.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** All or any part of the
credit established in favour of any member may be made available to him
or on his behalf upon his application therefor in writing, which
application shall include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an election not to take benefits under the [Veterans'
  Land Act](/eng/acts/V-1.5) or any educational, vocational or technical
  training benefits that are provided under the Veterans Rehabilitation
  Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} full particulars of any of the said benefits applied
  for or already received by him;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement verified by his affidavit as to the
  purpose for which he intends to use the credit; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} such further information as the Minister may require.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A member who makes
application under section 8 shall submit to the Minister, in addition to
his application, such other information and material as the Minister may
require.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Where the Minister makes
any payment under the Act or these Regulations to a person other than a
member, the person receiving payment shall furnish a receipt for such
payment to the member on whose behalf the payment is made.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** In addition to the
purposes under section 11 of the Act, re-establishment credit may be
made available to a member resident outside Canada, for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} payment of premiums pursuant to any contract of
  insurance to which the member is a party under the [Civil Service
  Insurance Act](/eng/acts/C-31.6);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} payment under subsection 16(2) of the [Royal Canadian
  Mounted Police Pension Continuation Act](/eng/acts/R-10.6) of a
  deficiency in deduction from his pay as an officer of the Royal
  Canadian Mounted Police;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} payment of contribution in respect of his service as
  a constable of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police under section 36, 47,
  50 or 51 of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Pension Continuation
  Act](/eng/acts/R-10.6);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} payment of contributions under section 7 of the
  [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36); and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} payment under subsection 9(2) of the [Defence
  Services Pension Continuation Act](/eng/acts/D-1.3) of a deficiency in
  deduction from his pay as an officer as defined in that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-307, s. 2
:::

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** In addition to the
purposes under section 14 of the Act, re-establishment credit may be
made available to the member for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} payment of contributions under section 7 of the
  [Canadian Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} acquisition of a unit of living accommodation in a
  housing project receiving financial assistance under the [National
  Housing Act](/eng/acts/N-11);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} payment of the purchase price or any part thereof of
  an annuity already purchased, being purchased, or to be purchased, on
  his behalf and for his benefit under any pension plan established by
  his employer and based upon the [Government Annuities
  Act](/eng/acts/G-3.7), except for payment of current contributions
  ordinarily payable from the renumeration of the member under any such
  pension plan;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} purchase of clothing by him for his personal use or
  for the use of his dependants where, at the time of application, there
  is need for such clothing;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} payment of contributions under section 7 of the
  [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36); and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} closing his re-establishment credit account by
  payment to the member where the amount available is \$50 or less.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-307, s. 2
:::

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may allow a member, to whom has been made available all or
  part of his re-establishment credit, to make the compensating
  adjustment referred to in section 16 of the Act through deductions in
  such amounts and at such times as will enable such adjustment to be
  completed contemporaneously with termination of his training under the
  Veterans Rehabilitation Act, and these deductions may be made from
  appropriate allowances under the Veterans Rehabilitation Act which
  might otherwise be available to the member in full.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The method of effecting the compensating adjustment
  set forth in subsection (1) shall not apply to a member if, in the
  opinion of the Minister, training would not result in reasonable
  re-establishment owing to the advanced age of the member or for any
  other reason, but shall apply to a member who, in the opinion of the
  Minister, is unable to obtain suitable employment but who possesses a
  potential physical and mental capacity for work.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The compensating adjustment shall be considered to be
  either the amount of re-establishment credit made available to the
  member, or the anticipated costs of training, whichever is the lesser.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where there is an amount of credit still available to
  the member which is greater than the anticipated costs of training, no
  compensating adjustment need be made.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where due to factors or a combination of factors it
  would be impossible to make total compensating adjustment by deduction
  from training allowances within the duration of the course of
  training, the member shall be required to pay or provide additional
  compensating adjustment in such amount and manner as the Minister
  deems just, if the additional adjustment may be reasonably expected to
  effect total compensating adjustment contemporaneously with the
  duration of training.
