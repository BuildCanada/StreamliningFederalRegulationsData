## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-794057 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[accident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[accident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an unexpected event that is
    not attributable to a deviation from the operating procedures or
    applicable laws and that could compromise human safety or the safety
    of blood. ([accident]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Food and Drugs
    Act](/eng/acts/F-27). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[adverse reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[adverse reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an undesirable
    response that is associated with

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a donor, the collection of blood;
      and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a recipient, the safety of the
      transfused blood. ([effet indésirable]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[allogeneic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[allogeneic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of blood or a blood
    donation, means that the blood is collected from an individual
    either for transfusion into another individual or for use in the
    manufacture of a drug for human use. ([allogénique]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of any blood or
    process, means an authorization that is issued under
    section 7. ([homologation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[autologous]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[autologous]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of blood or a blood
    donation, means that the blood is collected from an individual for
    transfusion into the same individual at a later
    time. ([autologue]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[blood]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[blood]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means human blood that is collected
    either for transfusion or for use in the manufacture of a drug for
    human use, and for greater certainty, it includes whole blood and
    blood components. ([sang]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[circular of information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[circular of information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a document
    that describes all of the following in relation to blood:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the composition and properties of the blood;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} directions for storage and for use; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} indications for use, contraindications, warnings
      and a list of possible adverse reactions. ([document
      d'information]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[critical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[critical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of equipment, supplies
    and services, means that the equipment, supply or service could, if
    it does not meet its specifications, compromise human safety or the
    safety of blood. ([essentiel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated donation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated donation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a blood donation
    that is made by a donor who is selected for medical reasons to make
    the donation for a specific recipient. ([don
    désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[directed donation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[directed donation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a blood donation
    that is made by a donor who is known by the recipient and selected
    for medical reasons by the recipient's physician. ([don
    dirigé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[distribute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[distribute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include to
    transfuse. ([distribution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[donation code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[donation code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the unique group of
    numbers, letters or symbols, or combination of any of them, that an
    establishment assigns to a unit of blood at the time of
    collection. ([code d'identification du don]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[donor identification code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[donor identification code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the unique
    group of numbers, letters or symbols, or combination of any of them,
    that an establishment assigns to a donor. ([code d'identification du
    donneur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[donor suitability assessment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[donor suitability assessment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    evaluation of a donor that is based on all of the following
    criteria:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the donor's medical history;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the results of any donor tests and physical
      examination; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the donor's social history, to the extent that it
      is relevant in determining the presence of risk factors for
      diseases transmissible by blood. ([évaluation de l'admissibilité
      du donneur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[error]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[error]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a deviation from the operating
    procedures or applicable laws that could compromise human safety or
    the safety of blood. ([manquement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person that conducts
    any of the following activities in respect of blood:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} importation;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} processing;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} distribution;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} transformation; or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} transfusion. ([établissement]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[human safety]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[human safety]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the safety of donors and
    recipients of blood, in so far as it relates to the safety of the
    blood. ([sécurité humaine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[medical director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[medical director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
    establishment, means a physician who is entitled under the laws of a
    province to practise the profession of medicine and who is
    responsible for all medical procedures carried out by the
    establishment and for the application of the operating procedures
    that relate to them. ([directeur médical]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[operating procedures]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operating procedures]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
    establishment, means the component of the establishment's quality
    management system that is composed of instructions that set out the
    processes to follow in conducting its activities. ([procédures
    opérationnelles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pre-assessed donor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pre-assessed donor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a donor who has
    been accepted into a pre-assessed donor program described in
    sections 86 to 91 from whom blood is taken in an emergency to be
    transfused before completion of the testing. ([donneur
    pré-évalué]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following
    activities:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} donor suitability assessment;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} collection;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} testing; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} blood component
      preparation. ([traitement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[safety]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[safety]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of blood, means that the
    blood has been determined safe for distribution or for autologous
    transfusion, as the case may be, in accordance with section 73, and
    includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of blood for transfusion, its quality
      and efficacy; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of blood for use in the manufacture
      of a drug for human use, its quality. ([sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[serious adverse reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[serious adverse reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an adverse
    reaction that results in any of the following consequences for the
    donor or recipient:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their in-patient hospitalization or its
      prolongation;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} persistent or significant disability or
      incapacity;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} medical or surgical intervention to preclude a
      persistent or significant disability or incapacity;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a life-threatening condition; or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} death. ([effet indésirable
      grave]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means National Standard of Canada
    CAN/CSA-Z902 published by the Canadian Standards Association and
    entitled Blood and blood components, as amended from time to
    time. ([norme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transformation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transformation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of blood
    components, means washing, pooling and irradiation that are
    performed after blood has been determined safe for
    transfusion. ([transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unexpected adverse reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unexpected adverse reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an adverse
    reaction that is not identified among the possible adverse reactions
    either in the circular of information or in any other information
    provided to the recipient. ([effet indésirable
    imprévu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-794111 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scope of Regulations

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
blood that is collected for transfusion or for use in the manufacture of
a drug for human use.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- various therapeutic
products

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations do not apply to any of the following therapeutic products:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cord blood and peripheral blood that are for use in
    lymphohematopoietic cell transplantation and that are regulated
    under the [Safety of Human Cells, Tissues and Organs for
    Transplantation Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-118);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} blood that is the subject of clinical trials under
    Division 5 of Part C of the [Food and Drug
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} blood that is imported for use in the manufacture
    of a drug for human use.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except for section A.01.045 of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), no other regulation
  made under the Act applies to blood that is the subject of these
  Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- imported rare
  phenotypes

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 4 to 124 do not apply to blood that is of a
  rare phenotype if it is imported pursuant to a prescription.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-794124 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allogeneic blood

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), an establishment must not import,
  distribute or transfuse allogeneic blood unless it is processed by an
  establishment in accordance with an authorization and determined safe
  for distribution under subsection 73(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- pre-assessed donor
  programs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if the processing is
  conducted as part of a pre-assessed donor program.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- urgent circumstances

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An establishment may, in urgent circumstances,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} import, in accordance with section 92, allogeneic
    blood that has not been processed in accordance with an
    authorization; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} distribute or transfuse such blood if the importer
    imported it in accordance with section 92.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-assessed donors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An establishment must not transfuse allogeneic blood
  that is collected from a pre-assessed donor unless the establishment
  has complied with the requirements of sections 86 to 91.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transformations

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An establishment must not distribute or transfuse
  blood that has been transformed unless the transformation is conducted
  by a registered establishment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Autologous blood

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} An establishment must not distribute or transfuse
  autologous blood unless it has been processed by a registered
  establishment and determined safe for autologous transfusion under
  subsection 73(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigations

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} An establishment must not distribute or transfuse
  blood in either of the following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} while the blood is in quarantine; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the results of an investigation into a
    suspected error or accident or an unexpected adverse reaction or
    serious adverse reaction are inconclusive or indicate that there has
    been a compromise to the safety of the blood.

## [Authorizations, Establishment Licences and Registrations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-794144 .Part}

### [Authorizations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-794145 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization --- processing

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  for an establishment that only tests blood, an establishment that
  processes allogeneic blood must have an authorization to do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- pre-assessed donor
  programs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if the processing is
  conducted as part of a pre-assessed donor program.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization --- importation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 92, an establishment that imports
  blood must have an authorization to do so, unless the blood is already
  the subject of another establishment's authorization.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for authorization

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment must file with the Minister an application for an
  authorization in the form established by the Minister. The application
  must be dated and signed by a senior executive officer and contain all
  of the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name and civic address, and its
    postal address if different, and the civic address of each building
    in which it proposes to conduct its activities;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and telephone number, fax number, email
    address or other means of communication of a person to contact for
    further information concerning the application;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and telephone number of a person to
    contact in an emergency, if different from the person mentioned in
    paragraph (b);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement of whether the establishment proposes
    to import whole blood or blood components;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a list of the whole blood and blood components that
    the establishment proposes to process or import;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a list of the processing activities that are
    proposed to be conducted in each building;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of the establishment's facilities,
    including its buildings and all critical equipment, supplies and
    services that it proposes to use in the conduct of its activities;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a description of the processes that the
    establishment proposes to use or to have used on its behalf in
    respect of blood and each blood component in the conduct of its
    activities;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a draft of each proposed label and circular of
    information;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} evidence that any foreign establishment that it
    proposes to have conduct any of its processing activities is
    licensed in the foreign jurisdiction; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} sufficient evidence to demonstrate that the
    proposed processes will not compromise human safety and will result
    in blood that can be determined safe for distribution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Site inspection

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} During the review of an application, the Minister may
  inspect the establishment's facilities to evaluate on site the
  information provided in the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information on request

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An establishment must provide the Minister, on
  written request, with any information that the Minister determines is
  necessary to complete the Minister's review of the application, by the
  date specified in the request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** On completion of the review
of an application, the Minister must issue an authorization, with or
without terms or conditions, if she or he determines that the
establishment has provided sufficient evidence to demonstrate that
issuance of the authorization will not compromise human safety or the
safety of blood.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may refuse to
issue an authorization if she or he determines that the information
provided by the establishment in its application is inaccurate or
incomplete.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Significant changes

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  making a significant change, an establishment must file with the
  Minister an application to amend its authorization and include with it
  all relevant information to enable the Minister to determine whether
  the change or the way in which it is implemented could compromise
  human safety or the safety of blood.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications to amend

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 6 to 8 apply to an application to amend an
  authorization, with any necessary modifications.

- Meaning of [[significant change]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section and sections 10 and 12, [[significant
  change]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following changes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the addition of blood or a blood component to the
    list required by paragraph 6(1)(e);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the deletion of or a change to any authorized
    process;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the addition of a process described in
    paragraph 6(1)(h); or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a change to the description of the establishment's
    facilities referred to in paragraph 6(1)(g).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergency changes

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In an
  emergency, if it becomes necessary for an establishment to implement a
  significant change before filing an application to amend its
  authorization, the establishment may do so if the change is necessary
  to prevent a compromise to human safety or the safety of blood.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice and application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The establishment must notify the Minister in writing
  of any significant change that it implements under subsection (1) no
  later than the day after implementing it and file an application to
  amend its authorization within 15 days after the day on which that
  notice is given.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrative changes --- notice

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An establishment must
notify the Minister in writing of any change to the information provided
under paragraphs 6(1)(a) to (c) as soon as possible after the change is
made, and the Minister must amend the authorization accordingly.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other changes --- annual report

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment must file with the Minister an annual report that
  describes any changes made in the year that are not described in
  section 9 or 11 and that could compromise human safety or the safety
  of blood.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment by Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of the report, the Minister must amend the
  establishment's authorization accordingly.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When changes determined significant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines that a change that was
  included in a report under subsection (1) is a significant change, the
  Minister must notify the establishment in writing to that effect and
  may require the establishment to cease or reverse the implementation
  of the change.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to amend authorization

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of the notice, the establishment must file
  an application to amend its authorization

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New or amended terms and conditions

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may add terms and conditions to an establishment's
  authorization or amend its terms and conditions in either of the
  following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    it is necessary to do so to prevent a compromise to human safety or
    the safety of blood; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the establishment fails to provide the Minister, on
    written request, with sufficient evidence to demonstrate that its
    processes will not compromise human safety and will result in blood
    that can be determined safe for distribution, by the date specified
    in the request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before adding terms or conditions to an authorization
  or amending its terms or conditions, the Minister must send the
  establishment a notice at least 15 days before the proposed terms and
  conditions are to take effect that sets out the Minister's reasons and
  that gives the establishment a reasonable opportunity to be heard
  concerning them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Urgent circumstances

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), the Minister may immediately
  add terms and conditions to an authorization or amend its terms and
  conditions if she or he has reasonable grounds to believe that it is
  necessary to do so to prevent a compromise to human safety or the
  safety of blood.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Urgent circumstances --- notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} When the Minister adds or amends terms or conditions
  under subsection (3), the Minister must send the establishment a
  notice that sets out the reasons for the new or amended terms and
  conditions and that gives the establishment a reasonable opportunity
  to be heard concerning them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal of terms and conditions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by notice in writing, remove a term
  or condition from an authorization if she or he determines that the
  term or condition is no longer necessary to prevent a compromise to
  human safety or the safety of blood.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may suspend all or part of an authorization in either of the
  following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} information provided by the establishment under
    section 6 or 9 proves to be inaccurate or incomplete; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the establishment fails to provide the Minister, on
    written request, with sufficient evidence to demonstrate that its
    processes will not compromise human safety and will result in blood
    that can be determined safe for distribution, by the date specified
    in the request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before suspending an authorization, the Minister must
  send the establishment a notice that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sets out the reasons for the proposed suspension
    and the effective date;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, indicates that the establishment
    must take corrective action and specifies the date by which it must
    be taken; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} gives the establishment a reasonable opportunity to
    be heard concerning the suspension.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Urgent circumstances

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), the Minister may immediately
  suspend all or part of an authorization if she or he has reasonable
  grounds to believe that it is necessary to do so to prevent a
  compromise to human safety or the safety of blood.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Urgent circumstances --- notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} When the Minister suspends an authorization under
  subsection (3), the Minister must send the establishment a notice that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sets out the reasons for the suspension; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} gives the establishment a reasonable opportunity to
    be heard concerning the suspension.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reinstatement

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  Minister must reinstate an authorization if the establishment provides
  the Minister with sufficient evidence to demonstrate that its
  processes will not compromise human safety and will result in blood
  that can be determined safe for distribution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Partial reinstatement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister does not reinstate any part of an
  authorization that was suspended, the Minister must amend the
  authorization to remove that part.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must cancel an authorization in either of the following
  circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the establishment fails to provide the Minister
    with the evidence described in paragraph 14(1)(b) within a
    reasonable period after the authorization was suspended; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the establishment's licence is cancelled under
    section 29.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When the Minister cancels an authorization, she or he
  must send the establishment a notice that sets out the reasons for the

## [Processing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-794398 .Part}

### [Donor Suitability Assessment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-794399 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- autologous donations

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** Sections 39 to 44 do not
apply to an autologous donation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licensed establishments

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** A licensed establishment
that collects allogeneic blood must, before the collection, assess the
donor's suitability to donate against the establishment's authorized
criteria.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Past unsuitability

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** In conducting a donor
suitability assessment, an establishment must verify whether the donor
has been previously determined unsuitable, and the reason why and the
duration, if applicable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Donor screening

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** In conducting a donor
suitability assessment, an establishment must take both of the following
steps:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtain information from the donor by use of a
  questionnaire or other similar means about their identity and medical
  history, and their social history to the extent that it is relevant in
  determining the presence of risk factors for diseases transmissible by
  blood; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the donor with information about the risks
  associated with donating blood and the risks to the recipient of
  contracting a transmissible disease.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion criteria

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** An establishment must
determine that a donor is unsuitable to donate if any of the information
obtained under sections 39 to 41 indicates that human safety or the
safety of blood could be compromised.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When donor determined unsuitable

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** If a donor is determined
unsuitable to donate, the establishment must not collect blood from that
donor and must inform the donor of the reasons why they are not suitable
to donate and indicate the date, if any, when the donor will again be
suitable to donate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When donor determined suitable

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  donor is determined suitable to donate, the establishment must take
  both of the following steps:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assign a donor identification code to the donor, if
    the donor does not already have one; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} instruct the donor to inform the establishment in
    either of the following situations:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the donor develops, within the periods set out in
      the establishment's operating procedures, an illness or condition
      that may potentially compromise the safety of donated blood, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} after the donation the donor has any reason to
      believe that their blood should not be used.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reassessment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of any post-donation information under
  paragraph (1)(b), the establishment must evaluate the information to
  reassess the safety of the current and any other donation made by that
  donor and the donor's suitability for future donations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the reassessment shows that the safety of the
  blood may have been compromised and the establishment has already
  distributed the blood, it must notify every person to which it
  distributed the blood to that effect, and if the person is an
  establishment, specify in the notice that the blood must not be
  distributed or transfused.

### [Collection]{.HTitleText2} {#h-794425 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licensed establishments

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** A licensed establishment
that collects allogeneic blood must do so in accordance with its
authorization.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Donor identification code

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
collects autologous blood must assign a donor identification code to the
donor.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Donation code

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
collects blood must assign a donation code to every unit of blood that
it collects and link the code in its records to the donor identification
code.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Labelling of containers

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 59, an
establishment that collects blood must ensure that every container is
labelled in accordance with section 63 at the time of the collection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collection procedures

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment that collects blood must conduct the collection in the
  following way:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} use aseptic methods;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} use collection equipment that is licensed under the
    [Medical Devices Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} use containers that are licensed under the [Medical
    Devices Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282) and free from
    defects or damage; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} record the container lot number in the records and
    link it to the donation code.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reuse of containers prohibited

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An establishment must ensure that the containers that
  it uses are used only once.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Samples

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
collects blood must obtain samples of blood for testing at the same time
as the collection in a way that avoids contamination of the donated
blood and the samples.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Autologous donations

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
collects autologous blood must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} comply with the criteria set out in section 12.2.1 of
  the standard; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} when appropriate, adjust the volume of the blood
  collected and the volume of anticoagulant based on the donor's weight.

### [Testing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-794449 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** A licensed establishment
that tests allogeneic blood --- except blood from a pre-assessed donor
--- must do so in accordance with an authorization.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Autologous donations --- transmissible
disease testing

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
collects autologous blood must test a sample of the blood using
appropriate and effective tests for transmissible diseases and disease
agents in accordance with section 12.3.1.2 of the standard.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Autologous donations --- ABO and Rh

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment that collects autologous blood must test a sample of the
  blood at the time of each donation to identify both of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ABO group; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Rh factor, including weak D testing when
    appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Comparison of results

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The establishment must compare the results of the
  tests conducted under paragraphs (1)(a) and (b) with the last
  available results, if any, for that donor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discrepancies

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the comparison indicates a discrepancy, the
  establishment must repeat the tests and must not transfuse the blood
  until the discrepancy is resolved.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Medical devices

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** When testing autologous
blood or blood that is collected from a pre-assessed donor, an
establishment must use medical devices that are licensed under the
[Medical Devices Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282) for the
following purposes:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} either for diagnosis or for screening donors, in the
  case of autologous blood; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for screening donors, in the case of blood that is
  collected from a pre-assessed donor.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Test results --- allogeneic blood

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment that collects allogeneic blood must immediately take all
  of the following actions if a donor's blood is positive or repeat
  reactive for a transmissible disease agent or marker listed in its
  authorization as a contraindication to use:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} quarantine any blood that was collected from that
    donor at that donation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identify and quarantine any other implicated blood
    from the same donor in the establishment's possession; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} notify every person to which it distributed any of
    the implicated blood from the same donor of the test results and, if
    the person is an establishment, specify in the notice that the blood
    must not be distributed or transfused.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An establishment that collects autologous blood must
  inform the donor's physician of any of the test results described in
  section 12.3.1.6 of the standard.

## [Labelling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-794480 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- pre-assessed donors

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Language requirement

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General requirements

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** A label must meet all of
the following requirements:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all information on the label must be accurate and
  must be presented clearly and legibly;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it must be made using only adhesives and inks that
  will not permeate the container;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} it must be permanently affixed to the container; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a tag, it must be firmly attached to
  the container.

  person who requests a copy of it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if the blood is
  transfused in the same establishment where it is collected.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Donation code

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of label --- blood for

- **[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment that collects blood for transfusion must ensure that all

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the establishment's licence number, if it has one,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the donation code;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement of whether the donation is whole blood
    or a blood component, and if it is a component, its name;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} when appropriate, the ABO group and Rh factor of

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} except in the case of apheresis, the approximate

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the recommended storage temperature;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the expiry date and, if applicable, the time;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} in the case of blood for transfusion, a warning

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} in the case of allogeneic blood for transfusion, a

    indications, contraindications, warnings and a list of possible
    adverse reactions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Autologous blood

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the blood has not been tested for the

## [Distribution]{.HTitleText1} {#h-794556 .Part}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of safety --- autologous

  damage and maintaining the safety of the blood.

## [Exceptional Distribution, Pre-Assessed Donor Programs and Importation in Urgent Circumstances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-794596 .Part}

### [Exceptional Distribution]{.HTitleText2} {#h-794597 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

**[[81]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-81 .sectionLabel}** An establishment may
distribute or transfuse allogeneic blood for transfusion for which the
test results for ABO group, Rh factor and transmissible diseases or
disease agents are not yet available if both of the following conditions
are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} blood that has been determined safe for distribution
  is not immediately available; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the recipient's physician requests the blood for use
  in the emergency treatment of their patient.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of exceptional distribution

- **[[82]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-82 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment that distributes blood under section 81 must complete a
  notice of exceptional distribution that contains all of the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the establishment and the signature of
    the medical director;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the donation code;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement of whether the blood was whole blood or
    a blood component, and if it was a component, its name;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a list of the test results that were not available
    at the time of the distribution;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name and signature of the recipient's
    physician;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the justification for the distribution;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the name of the establishment to which it
    distributed the blood; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the distribution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice in establishments' records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The establishment must keep the notice in its records
  and send a copy of it to the establishment to which it distributed the
  blood.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to be forwarded

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the establishment to which the blood is
  distributed does not perform the transfusion, it must send a copy of
  the notice to the establishment where the transfusion is performed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice in recipient's file

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The establishment where the transfusion is performed
  must keep the notice in the recipient's file.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Labelling

**[[83]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-83 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
distributes blood under section 81 must label it to indicate that the
testing required by these Regulations is incomplete or that all of the
test results are not yet available, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Follow-up

- **[[84]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-84 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment that distributes blood under section 81 either before
  the testing is complete or before the test results are all available
  must, after the distribution, conduct any remaining testing and
  provide the establishment to which it distributed the blood with all
  of the relevant test results as soon as they become available.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Results to be forwarded

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the establishment to which the blood was
  distributed did not perform the transfusion, it must send a copy of
  the test results to the establishment where the transfusion was
  performed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When blood not transfused

**[[85]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-85 .sectionLabel}** If blood that is the
subject of an exceptional distribution is not transfused into the
intended recipient in the emergency, the establishment that was to
perform the transfusion must not store the blood or transfuse it into
another recipient.

### [Pre-assessed Donor Programs]{.HTitleText2} {#h-794628 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Program characteristics

**[[86]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-86 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that has
a pre-assessed donor program must ensure that the program has both of
the following characteristics:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is carried out under the supervision of a medical
  director; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is used only when

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} no other alternative source of blood appropriate
    for the recipient is available, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the recipient's physician requests the blood for
    use in the emergency treatment of their patient.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Donor identification code

**[[87]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-87 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that has
a pre-assessed donor program must assign a donor identification code at
the time of the donor's acceptance into the program.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regular donor assessment and testing

- **[[88]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-88 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment that has a pre-assessed donor program must take both of
  the following steps every three months:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assess the suitability of every donor in the
    program in accordance with sections 40 to 44; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} take blood samples from every donor and test them
    for all of the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the transmissible diseases and disease agents
      listed in sections 8.4.1 and 8.4.2 of the standard,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the ABO group,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Rh factor, including weak D testing when
      appropriate, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} clinically significant antibodies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Comparison of results

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The establishment must compare the results of the
  tests conducted under subparagraphs (1)(b)(ii) and (iii) with the last
  available results, if any, for that donor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discrepancies

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the comparison indicates a discrepancy, the
  establishment must repeat the tests and must not collect any blood
  from that donor until the discrepancy is resolved.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}At each collection

**[[89]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-89 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
collects blood from a pre-assessed donor must take all of the following
steps at each collection:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} assess the suitability of the donor;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} assign a donation code to the blood collected and
  link the code in its records to the donor identification code; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} take a sample of blood from the donor and test it
  within 72 hours for all of the following:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the transmissible diseases and disease agents
    listed in sections 8.4.1 and 8.4.2 of the standard,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the ABO group,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Rh factor, including weak D testing when
    appropriate, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} clinically significant antibodies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Labelling

**[[90]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-90 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
collects blood from a pre-assessed donor must ensure that at least the
donation code and the ABO group and, when appropriate, the Rh factor
appear on the label of the blood.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When blood not transfused

**[[91]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91 .sectionLabel}** If blood that is
collected from a pre-assessed donor is not transfused into an intended
recipient in the emergency, the establishment that was to perform the
transfusion must comply with the requirements of section 16.2.5 of the
standard.

### [Importation in Urgent Circumstances]{.HTitleText2} {#h-794663 .Subheading}

### [Personnel, Facilities, Equipment and Supplies]{.HTitleText2} {#h-794722 .Subheading}

  orientation and training, both initial and ongoing, of personnel and
  for the evaluation of their competency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Facilities

**[[99]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-99 .sectionLabel}** A licensed or registered
establishment must have facilities that permit all of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conduct of all of its activities;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the performance by personnel of their respective
  tasks using proper hygiene;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the cleaning of the facilities in a way that
  maintains sanitary conditions;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} environmental controls that are appropriate to all
  areas where its activities are conducted;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} controlled access to all areas where its activities
  are conducted; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} donor screening to be conducted in privacy.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equipment

- **[[100]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-100 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensed or registered establishment must ensure that the critical
  equipment that it uses is cleaned and maintained and, as appropriate,
  validated for its intended purpose and calibrated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repair or change

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The establishment must, whenever necessary after it
  repairs or makes any change to critical equipment, revalidate and
  recalibrate the equipment, as appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage equipment

**[[101]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-101 .sectionLabel}** An establishment must
use equipment to store blood that enables the establishment to meet the
requirements of sections 69 to 72.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supplies

**[[102]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-102 .sectionLabel}** A licensed or
registered establishment must ensure that the critical supplies that it
uses are validated or qualified, as applicable, for their intended use
and must store them under appropriate environmental conditions.

### [Error and Accident Investigation and Reporting]{.HTitleText2} {#h-794745 .Subheading}

#### [Errors and Accidents]{.HTitleText3} {#h-794746 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Error or accident of another
establishment

- **[[103]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-103 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment that has reasonable grounds to believe that the safety
  of blood may have been compromised by the occurrence of an error or
  accident during an activity conducted by another establishment must
  immediately take all of the following actions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine the donation codes of the implicated
    blood;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identify and quarantine any implicated blood in its
    possession; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} notify all of the following establishments:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the establishment that collected the implicated
      blood,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the establishment from which it received the
      implicated blood, if different from the establishment mentioned in
      subparagraph (i), and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any establishment to which it distributed
      implicated blood.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must include all of the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the donation codes of the implicated blood;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement of whether the implicated blood is
    whole blood or blood components, and the names of the implicated
    blood components; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reason for the establishment's belief that the
    safety of the blood may have been compromised.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action on receipt of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An establishment that is notified under
  subparagraph (1)(c)(iii) or under this subsection must immediately
  notify to the same effect every establishment to which it distributed
  implicated blood and quarantine all implicated blood in its
  possession.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a notice under this section is given verbally, a
  confirmatory written notice must be sent as soon as possible
  afterwards.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment's own error or accident

- **[[104]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-104 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment that receives a notice under subparagraph 103(1)(c)(i)
  or (ii) or suspects that an error or accident that occurred during an
  activity it conducted may have compromised the safety of blood must
  immediately take all of the following actions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine the donation codes of the implicated
    blood;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identify and quarantine any implicated blood in its
    possession; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} determine whether there is sufficient evidence to
    warrant proceeding to an investigation into the suspected error or
    accident.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When no investigation --- notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the establishment determines that an investigation
  is not warranted, it must notify the establishment that sent it the
  notice under subparagraph 103(1)(c)(i) or (ii) that it will not be
  conducting an investigation and provide its reasons for that decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action on receipt of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An establishment that is notified under
  subsection (2) or under this subsection must immediately notify to the
  same effect every establishment to which it distributed implicated
  blood.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of investigation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the establishment determines that an investigation
  is warranted, it must begin the investigation, notify every
  establishment and other person to which it distributed implicated
  blood, and include the following information in the notice:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the donation codes of all implicated blood; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the suspected error or accident
    and an explanation of how the safety of the implicated blood may
    have been compromised.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action on receipt of notice

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An establishment that is notified under
  subsection (4) or under this subsection must immediately notify to the
  same effect every establishment to which it distributed implicated
  blood and quarantine all implicated blood in its possession.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written notice

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a notice under this section is given verbally, a
  confirmatory written notice must be sent as soon as possible
  afterwards.

#### [Investigation and Reporting]{.HTitleText3} {#h-794783 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to cooperate

- **[[105]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-105 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment must, on request, provide any establishment that is
  conducting an investigation with any relevant information in its
  possession in respect of blood that it distributed or transfused.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When more than one establishment is affected by an
  error or accident or the investigation of one, each establishment must
  ensure that every other establishment that is so affected is kept
  informed of all relevant information and of all developments and
  issues that arise during the investigation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation results

- **[[106]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-106 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment that is conducting an investigation must notify in
  writing every establishment and other person to which it distributed
  implicated blood of the results of the investigation and of any action
  that is required to be taken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to be forwarded

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An establishment that is notified under
  subsection (1) or under this subsection must send a copy of the notice
  to every establishment to which it distributed implicated blood.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports to Minister

- **[[107]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-107 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment that is conducting an investigation into a suspected
  error or accident that is thought to have occurred during an activity
  that it conducted and that is identified after the blood is
  distributed or transfused must file the reports described in
  subsection (2) with the Minister if there is a reasonable probability
  that the error or accident could lead to a serious adverse reaction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents and timing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The reports must include the following information
  and be filed at the following times:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a preliminary report that includes all relevant
    information that is available, within 24 hours after the start of
    the investigation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written update on any new information about the
    suspected error or accident, on the progress made in the
    investigation since the last report and on the steps taken to
    mitigate further risks,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} within 15 days after the start of the
      investigation, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on request of the Minister at any time after the
      preliminary report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the report under paragraph (2)(a) is given
  verbally, a written report must be filed as soon as possible
  afterwards.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final report to Minister

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} On completion of an investigation, the establishment
  must file a final report with the Minister that contains all of the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the results of the investigation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the final disposition of the blood that was the
    subject of the investigation and the reasons for that disposition;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any corrective actions taken and any other changes
    that are recommended to be made to relevant processes.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[108]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-108 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment must prepare an annual report that summarizes all of the
  error and accident investigations that it conducted in the previous
  12 months, including a concise critical analysis of those
  investigations, and must file it with the Minister on request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When to notify Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the analysis reveals a previously unidentified
  risk to the safety of blood, the establishment must notify the
  Minister immediately.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional reports

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An establishment must, on the Minister's request,
  file additional reports described in subsection (1) in respect of the
  period specified in the request.

### [Adverse Reaction Investigation and Reporting]{.HTitleText2} {#h-794817 .Subheading}

#### [Adverse Donor Reactions]{.HTitleText3} {#h-794818 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister

- **[[109]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-109 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment that has reasonable grounds to believe that a donor has
  experienced a serious adverse reaction during a donation or within
  72 hours after a donation must notify the Minister of the adverse
  reaction within 24 hours after it learns of the death of the donor or
  within 15 days after it learns of the adverse reaction in any other
  case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must contain all of the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the adverse reaction;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any actions that were taken to address it; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the outcome.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a notice under this section is given verbally, a
  confirmatory written notice must be sent as soon as possible
  afterwards.

#### [Adverse Recipient Reactions]{.HTitleText3} {#h-794829 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required action

- **[[110]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-110
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 111, an
  establishment that has reasonable grounds to believe that a recipient
  has experienced an unexpected adverse reaction or a serious adverse
  reaction must immediately take all of the following actions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine the donation codes of all implicated
    blood;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identify and quarantine any implicated blood in its
    possession;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if a preliminary inquiry indicates that the root
    cause of the adverse reaction is attributable to an activity that it
    carried out, conduct an investigation into the adverse reaction and
    notify any establishment to which it distributed implicated blood;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if a preliminary inquiry indicates that the root
    cause of the adverse reaction is attributable to an activity carried
    out by another establishment, notify all of the following
    establishments:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the establishment that collected the implicated
      blood,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the establishment from which it received the
      implicated blood, if different from the establishment mentioned in
      subparagraph (i), and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any establishment to which it distributed
      implicated blood.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice required by paragraphs (1)(c) and (d) must
  contain all of the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the adverse reaction;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of how the safety of the implicated
    blood may have been compromised, if known;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the donation codes of all implicated blood;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement of whether the implicated blood is
    whole blood or blood components, and the names of the implicated
    blood components; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name of any suspected transmissible disease or
    disease agent, if known.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quarantine

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An establishment that is notified under
  subsection (1) or under this subsection must immediately notify to the
  same effect every establishment and other person to which it
  distributed implicated blood and quarantine any implicated blood in
  its possession.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An establishment that is notified under
  subparagraph (1)(d)(i) or (ii) must, if a preliminary inquiry
  indicates that the root cause of the adverse reaction is attributable
  to an activity that it carried out, conduct an investigation into the
  adverse reaction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written notice

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a notice under this section is given verbally, a
  confirmatory written notice must be sent as soon as possible
  afterwards.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Autologous donations

**[[111]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-111 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
both collects and transfuses the same autologous blood must, if it has
reasonable grounds to believe that a recipient has experienced an
unexpected adverse reaction or a serious adverse reaction, immediately
quarantine any other blood from that donor in its possession and conduct
an investigation into the adverse reaction and the implicated blood.

#### [Investigation and Reporting of Adverse Recipient Reactions]{.HTitleText3} {#h-794855 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to cooperate

**[[112]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-112 .sectionLabel}** An establishment must,

of blood that it distributed or transfused.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister

- **[[113]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-113 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An

  other unexpected adverse reaction or serious adverse reaction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a notice under this section is given verbally, a
  confirmatory written notice must be sent as soon as possible
  afterwards.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Results of investigation

- **[[114]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-114 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  establishment that is conducting an investigation must notify in
  writing every establishment and other person to which it distributed
  implicated blood of the results of the investigation and of any action
  that is required to be taken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to be forwarded

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An establishment that is notified under
  subsection (1) or under this subsection must send a copy of the notice
  to every establishment to which it distributed implicated blood.

  that are recommended to be made to relevant processes.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

**[[116]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-116 .sectionLabel}** At the end of each
year, an establishment must prepare an annual report that summarizes all
of the final reports that it filed in the year, including a concise
critical analysis of the investigations that were the subjects of those
reports, and must file it with the Minister on request.

## [Powers of Inspectors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-794930 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Making visual recordings

**[[124]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-124 .sectionLabel}** An inspector may, in
the administration of these Regulations, take photographs and make
recordings of any of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any article that is referred to in subsection 23(2)
  of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any place where the inspector believes on reasonable
  grounds any article referred to in paragraph (a) is processed,
  transformed or stored; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} anything that the inspector believes on reasonable
  grounds is used or is capable of being used in the conduct of an
  establishment's activities.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-794938 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed authorization

**[[126]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-126 .sectionLabel}** The information that is
required by section 6 to be included in an application for an
authorization and that was filed with and accepted by the Minister under
sections C.01A.005 to C.01A.007 and C.01A.014 of the [Food and Drug
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) before the day on which
these Regulations come into force is deemed to be an authorization
issued by the Minister under section 7 of these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence continued

**[[127]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-127 .sectionLabel}** If an establishment
files an application for a licence under section 18 --- without regard
to paragraphs (1)(j) and (k) --- within three months after the day on
which these Regulations come into force, any licence that was issued to
the establishment under section C.01A.008 of the [Food and Drug
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) before that day is
continued until a licence is either issued under section 20 or refused
under section 21 of these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delayed registration

- **[[128]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-128 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment that, before the day on which these Regulations come
  into force, conducts any of the activities mentioned in section 30 may
  continue to do so without a registration if it files an application
  for registration under section 31 within three months after that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) applies until the determination of the
  application under section 32.
