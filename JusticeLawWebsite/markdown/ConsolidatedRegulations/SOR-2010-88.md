## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-768305 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order, "renewable
fuel" includes fuel that is produced from biomass, waste material or
feedstock of biological origin, whether or not the fuel is blended with
a petroleum product.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-768307 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of renewable fuels that are
imported into Canada during the period beginning on April 1, 2006 and
ending on June 17, 2009 and that are laden on board a conveyance for use
as ships' stores.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-768309 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the renewable fuels are either released and
  subsequently exported in the same condition in which they were
  imported or released, processed in Canada and subsequently exported;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the conveyance on board of which the renewable fuels
  are laden for use is one set out in column I of the schedule to the
  [Ships' Stores Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-40), as it read on
  June 17, 2009 for the purposes of the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), and any limits, set out in the notes to
  that schedule, that are applicable to petroleum products with respect
  to that conveyance are met or not exceeded, as the case may be;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a written claim for remission is made by the importer
  to the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two
  years after the day on which this Order comes into force;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the importer provides the Canada Border Services
  Agency with evidence or information that demonstrates that they are
  entitled to remission under this Order; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} no claim for refund or drawback under the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) or [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) has
  been made with respect to the customs duties paid or payable in
  respect of the renewable fuels in question.
