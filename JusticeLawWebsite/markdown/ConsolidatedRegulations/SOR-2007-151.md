## "MV Sonia" Remission Order, 2007 {#mv-sonia-remission-order-2007 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2007-151
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 2007-06-14

"MV Sonia" Remission Order, 2007

P.C. 2007-1002 2007-06-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 115 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the annexed
["MV Sonia" Remission Order, 2007](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-151).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734637 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted to
British Columbia Ferry Services Inc., Victoria, British Columbia, in the
amount representing the customs duties paid or payable under the
[Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of the "MV Sonia"
imported into Canada for ferry service in coastal British Columbia.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734639 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
the conditions that:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the importer files such evidence as may be required
  by the Canada Border Services Agency to determine eligibility for
  remission; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made by the importer to the
  Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years
  after the day on which this Order comes into force.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734643 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
