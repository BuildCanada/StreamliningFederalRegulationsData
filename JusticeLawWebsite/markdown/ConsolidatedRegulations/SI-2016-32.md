## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-629150 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[licence of occupation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence of occupation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence of
    occupation granted under section 18 of the Regulations for lands
    located adjacent to the Castle Mountain Chalets site or the Storm
    Mountain Lodge site. ([permis d'occupation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [National Parks of
    Canada Lease and Licence of Occupation
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-25). ([Règlement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Settlement Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Settlement Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Siksika
    Nation Castle Mountain Land and Timber Claim Settlement Agreement
    between Canada and the Siksika Nation. ([entente de
    règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Siksika entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Siksika entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation, limited
    partnership or other entity whose ownership and controlling interest
    is 100% controlled by, or beneficially held in trust for, the
    members of the Siksika Nation. ([entité siksika]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[special lease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[special lease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a lease granted under
    paragraph 3(1)(e) of the Regulations for

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Castle Mountain Chalets site;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Storm Mountain Lodge site;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Castle Mountain Campground site; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Castle Mountain Hostel site. ([bail
      spécial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-629162 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special leases

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted to a
Siksika entity lessee of any special lease of the monies payable for
rent under subsection 6(1) of the Regulations with the exception of
\$1.00 per year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licences of occupation

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted to a
Siksika entity licensee of any licence of occupation of the monies
payable for the fee under section 18 of the Regulations with the
exception of \$1.00 per year.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-629167 .Part}
