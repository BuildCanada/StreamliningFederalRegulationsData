## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734581 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Export and Import Permits
    Act](/eng/acts/E-19). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[board foot]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[board foot]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act,
    2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55). ([pied-planche]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[date of shipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[date of shipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a softwood
    lumber product, means the time at which the product is considered to
    be exported under section 5 of the Softwood Lumber Products Export
    Charge Act, 2006. ([date d'expédition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an export permit issued under
    the Act. ([licence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[region]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[region]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Nova Scotia, New Brunswick,
    Prince Edward Island, Newfoundland and Labrador, Yukon , Northwest
    Territories or Nunavut, and includes a region as defined in
    subsection 6.3(1) of the Act. ([région]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[region of first primary processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[region of first primary processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in
    respect of a softwood lumber product, means the region where the
    product undergoes its first "primary processing", as defined in
    section 2 of the [Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act,
    2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55). ([région de première
    transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods
    included in item 5104 of Group 5 of the schedule to the [Export
    Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202). ([produits de bois
    d'œuvre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- SOR/2015-221, s. 2

## [Application for a Permit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734593 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  exporter or their agent or mandatary who applies for a permit to
  export softwood lumber products to the United States must submit to
  the Minister the following information, together with a statement that
  the information is true, correct and complete:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the exporter and, if
    applicable, of the agent or mandatary;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exporter's identifier number assigned by the
    Minister;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the official language selected for communications
    with the exporter;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether or not the exporter and, if applicable, the
    agent or mandatary are residents of Canada;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the softwood lumber
    products;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the tariff classification of the softwood lumber
    products set out in the List of Tariff Provisions under the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) ;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the softwood lumber products,
    expressed in board feet;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the name of the region of first primary processing;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name and business number assigned by the
    Minister of National Revenue of the mill that carried out the first
    primary processing of the softwood lumber products;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the export price in Canadian or United States
    dollars of the softwood lumber products;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the date of shipment of the softwood lumber
    products;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the mode of transportation of the softwood lumber
    products;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} the port of entry of the softwood lumber products
    into the United States;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} the date of entry of the softwood lumber products
    into the United States; and

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the importer or consignee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of origin

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the softwood lumber products originate from Nova
  Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island or Newfoundland and
  Labrador, the application must include the Maritime Lumber Bureau
  Certificate of Origin number for those products.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resident of Canada

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** If the exporter is not a
resident of Canada, the application must be made by an agent or
mandatary who is a resident of Canada.

## [Information in Respect of Certain Products that Do Not Require a Permit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734616 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Single family home package or kit

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A person who exports to the
United States a single family home package or kit that is expressly
excluded from the [Export Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202)
under item 5104 of that list must retain the following documents and
provide them to U.S. Customs and Border Protection on request:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the appropriate home design plan, or of a
  blueprint matching the customs entry in the United States;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a purchase contract from a retailer of home packages
  or kits, signed by a customer not affiliated with the person who
  imports the packages or kits into the United States;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a listing of inventory of all parts of the package or
  kit entering the United States that conforms to the home design
  package; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of multiple shipments on the same
  contract, a listing of inventory described in paragraph (c) that
  indicates the items that are included in the shipment at issue.
