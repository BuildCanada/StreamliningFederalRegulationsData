## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024031 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[demand for funds]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[demand for funds]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a request for funds
    made by the Minister in accordance with an agreement entered into
    under subsection 40.2(2) of the Act concerning the payment of
    amounts calculated in accordance with section 6 of the Regulations.
    ([demande de fonds]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee of an employer in
    respect of whom the Minister has entered into an agreement under
    subsection 40.2(2) of the Act who has ceased to be employed by that
    employer and is or becomes employed by the public service.
    ([employé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an eligible employer with
    whom the Minister has entered into an agreement under subsection
    40.2(2) of the Act. ([employeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pensionable service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pensionable service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any service that
    an employee is entitled to count for the purposes of any plan
    referred to in subsection 40.2(1) of the Act and that may be counted
    by the employee in accordance with an agreement for the purposes of
    subsection 6(1) of the Act, subject to the conditions set out in
    subsection 40.2(9) of the Act. ([période de service ouvrant droit à
    pension]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registered pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registered pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pension plan
    registered under section 147.1 of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([régime de pension agréé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Class of Pension Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024041 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 40.2(1) of the Act, a pension plan is a registered pension
plan:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} with at least 10 active participants at the time the
  employer enters into a transfer agreement under subsection 40.2(2) of
  the Act; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} established as a result of a divestiture of service
  under section 40.1 of the Act.

## [Pensionable Service]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024045 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Pensionable service
includes periods of past service that an employee is entitled to count
under paragraph 147.1(10)(a) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The demand for funds made
to an employer in respect of an employee shall be determined in
accordance with a transfer agreement and shall equal the total of:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an amount not exceeding the value, actuarially
  calculated in accordance with the transfer agreement, of all benefits
  that would have been payable under Parts I and III of the Act in
  respect of the pensionable service of the employee; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an amount that represents interest on the amount
  determined in accordance with paragraph (a) as of the date of payment,
  which interest shall be determined by the Minister under subparagraph
  40.2(3)(a)(ii) of the Act.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Any period of part-time
service credited to an employee by an employer shall be deemed to be a
period of part-time service within the meaning of the Act.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  period of service of an employee that, according to the employer, is
  subject to credit-splitting under a federal or provincial Act shall be
  credited on a pro-rata basis.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The total period referred to in subsection (1) shall
  be retained in determining eligibility for a benefit.

<!-- -->

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  period of service of an employee in respect of which the amounts
  referred to in a demand for funds have been paid in total by an
  employer into the Superannuation Account is deemed to be pensionable
  service in accordance with subsection 6(1) of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The period of service of an employee in respect of
  which the amounts referred to in a demand for funds have not been paid
  in total by an employer is calculated as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the employer has specified that the period
    credited is exclusively a period of full-time service, the period of
    service is pro-rated on the basis of the amounts paid by the
    employer and is deemed to be the most recent period; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the employer has credited a period of service
    consisting of both part-time service and full-time service, the
    period is converted to a period of full-time service, costed as a
    period of full-time service and deemed to be the most recent period.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024059 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on August 28, 1998.
