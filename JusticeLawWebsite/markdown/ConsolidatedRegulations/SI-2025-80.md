## Order Transferring from the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to the Minister of Emergency Management and Community Resilience the Powers, Duties and Functions Under Section 7 of the Emergency Management Act {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-public-safety-and-emergency-preparedness-to-the-minister-of-emergency-management-and-community-resilience-the-powers-duties-and-functions-under-section-7-of-the-emergency-management-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-80
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2025-06-04

Order Transferring from the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
Preparedness to the Minister of Emergency Management and Community
Resilience the Powers, Duties and Functions Under Section 7 of the
Emergency Management Act

P.C. 2025-491 2025-05-13
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2‍(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2909_hq_1746839215){#fn_81000-3-2909_hq_1746839215-ID0EJEAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2909_hq_1746839239){#fn_81000-3-2909_hq_1746839239-ID0EGEAA},
transfers from the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness
to the Minister of Emergency Management and Community Resilience the
powers, duties and functions under section 7 of the [Emergency
Management Act](/eng/acts/E-4.56)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2909_hq_1746839271){#fn_81000-3-2909_hq_1746839271-ID0EDEAA}
that were transferred to that Minister by Order in Council P.C. 2025-418
of March 14, 2025[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-3-2909_hq_1746839825){#fn_81000-3-2909_hq_1746839825-ID0EBEAA}.
