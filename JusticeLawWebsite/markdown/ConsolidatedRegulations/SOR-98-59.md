## Time Limit for the Application of Subsection 118(1) of the Customs Tariff Regulations {#time-limit-for-the-application-of-subsection-1181-of-the-customs-tariff-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-59
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1997-12-29

Time Limit for the Application of Subsection 118(1) of the Customs
Tariff Regulations

P.C. 1997-2031  1997-12-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 118(4)(a) of the
[Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Time Limit for the Application of Subsection 118(1) of the Customs
Tariff Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-59).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e-ID0EDBAA){#fna_e}S.C.
  1997, c. 36
  :::
:::

## [Time Limit for Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025450 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 118(1) of the
[Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) applies in respect of any imported
goods for a period of six years after the day on which the goods are
released.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025452 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on January 1, 1998.
