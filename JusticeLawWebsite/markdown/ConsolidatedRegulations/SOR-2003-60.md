## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707601 .Part}

Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).

## [Prescribed Purpose or Circumstance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707604 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed purpose or circumstance

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraph 539(1)(b.2)(iii) of the Act and subject to the approval
required under paragraph 539(1)(b.2) of the Act, an authorized foreign
bank may develop, design, hold, manage, manufacture, sell or otherwise
deal with data transmission systems, information sites, communication
devices or information platforms or portals that are used for a purpose
or in a circumstance that is materially related to the provision of
financial products or services by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the authorized foreign bank;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity associated, within the meaning of section
  507 of the Act, with the authorized foreign bank; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an entity in which the authorized foreign bank or an
  entity associated, within the meaning of section 507 of the Act, with
  the authorized foreign bank holds a substantial investment.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707610 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
