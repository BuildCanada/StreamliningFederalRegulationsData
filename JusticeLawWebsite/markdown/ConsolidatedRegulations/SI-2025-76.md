## Order Transferring from the Minister of Industry to the Minister of Employment and Social Development the Powers, Duties and Functions in Relation to the Federal Economic Development Agency for Northern Ontario {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-industry-to-the-minister-of-employment-and-social-development-the-powers-duties-and-functions-in-relation-to-the-federal-economic-development-agency-for-northern-ontario .Title-of-Act}

:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2025-06-04

Order Transferring from the Minister of Industry to the Minister of
Employment and Social Development the Powers, Duties and Functions in
Relation to the Federal Economic Development Agency for Northern Ontario

P.C. 2025-482 2025-05-13
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBCAA},
transfers from the Minister of Industry to the Minister of Employment
and Social Development the powers, duties and functions of the Minister
of Industry in relation to the Federal Economic Development Agency for
Northern Ontario.
