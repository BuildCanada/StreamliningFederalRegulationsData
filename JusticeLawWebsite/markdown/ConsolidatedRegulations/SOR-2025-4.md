## Critical Habitat of the Redside Dace (Clinostomus elongatus) Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-redside-dace-clinostomus-elongatus-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2025-4
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2025-01-27

Critical Habitat of the Redside Dace (Clinostomus elongatus) Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Redside Dace ([Clinostomus elongatus]{.otherLang lang="la"})
is a wildlife species that is listed as an endangered species in Part 2
of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5629_hq_1727360755){#fn_81000-2-5629_hq_1727360755-ID0EBBHA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5629_hq_1727360755-ID0EBBHA){#fn_81000-2-5629_hq_1727360755}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the Minister responsible for the Parks Canada Agency, namely the
Minister of the Environment, and the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans
have together prepared a recovery strategy that identified the critical
habitat of that species and that recovery strategy has been included in
the Species at Risk Public Registry;

And whereas a portion of the critical habitat of that species is in a
place referred to in subsection 58(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-5629_hq_1727360861){#fn_81000-2-5629_hq_1727360861-ID0EBBFA}
of that Act and, under subsection 58(5) of that Act, that portion must
be excluded from the annexed Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-5629_hq_1727360861-ID0EBBFA){#fn_81000-2-5629_hq_1727360861}S.C.
  2015, c. 10, s. 60
  :::

Therefore, the Minister of the Environment and the Minister of Fisheries
and Oceans make the annexed [Critical Habitat of the Redside Dace
(Clinostomus elongatus) Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2025-4) under
subsections 58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)^a^

Ottawa, January 6, 2025

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Redside Dace ([Clinostomus elongatus]{.otherLang lang="la"}) ---
which is identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is
included in the Species at Risk Public Registry --- other than the
portion of that critical habitat that is in a place referred to in
subsection 58(2) of that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
