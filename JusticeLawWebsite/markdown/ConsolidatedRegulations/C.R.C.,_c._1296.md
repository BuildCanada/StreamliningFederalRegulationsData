## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Registered Mail]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502423 .Part}

### [Registration of Mail]{.HTitleText2} {#h-502424 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following mailable matter shall be accepted for registration at the
  applicable rate set out in column II of item 1 of Schedule VII:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} letter mail as defined in the [Letter Mail
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-430); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} letters referred to in the [International
    Letter-post Items Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-807).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/91-635, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2000-199, s. 10\]]{.Repealed}

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} When mailable matter is accepted for registration,
  the Corporation shall give to the sender a receipt in a form approved
  by the Corporation.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/88-441, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

- [(6) and (7)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/91-628, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The signature of the addressee or his representative
  shall be obtained before registered mail is delivered.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Mailable matter on which the addressee is described
  only by his initials or his surname, in a manner that does not permit
  the clear identification of the addressee, shall not be accepted for
  registration.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} If adhesive tape is used to close an item of
  mailable matter referred to in subsection (1), the sender must apply
  their name, mark, stamp or signature on the tape.

## [PART VII]{.HLabel1}[\[Repealed, SOR/88-441, s. 12\]]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502479 .Part}

## [PART VIII]{.HLabel1}[Payment of Indemnity for Registered Mail Posted in Canada for Delivery in or Outside Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502480 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2003-378, s. 7
- SOR/2010-287, s. 2

\]
:::

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to
registered mail that is posted in Canada for delivery in or outside
Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-287, s. 3
:::

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of indemnity that the Corporation is authorized to pay in
  respect of any claim under these Regulations regarding registered mail
  shall not exceed the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the value of the mailable matter less any
    compensation received from any other source; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of indemnity coverage obtained pursuant
    to these Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), payment may be made in
  respect of loss of or damage to registered mailable matter for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} postage paid in respect of the registered mailable
    matter; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provincial sales tax paid in respect of the
    registered mailable matter if proof of payment of the tax is
    available and the Corporation is satisfied that the tax cannot be
    recovered.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation shall not make payments in respect of
  loss of or damage to registered mailable matter for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} postage paid for special services provided pursuant
    to these Regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any expense incurred by the sender or the addressee
    in submitting his claim for a payment referred to in subsections (1)
    and (2).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), the Corporation may
  replace the contents of registered mailable matter instead of paying
  an indemnity.

- [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} Where the Corporation returns to the sender, as a
  result of a suspension of postal service, a registered mail item
  posted to a country outside Canada, the postage paid for it shall be
  returned to the sender.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No indemnity shall be paid pursuant to these
  Regulations in respect of registered mailable matter where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the mail was a commercial shipment that was not
    ordered or requested by the addressee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the injury or damage was not a direct result of the
    loss, damage, delay, non-delivery or misdelivery of any mail;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the mail was delivered without any complaint or
    evidence of loss, damage or rifling or no evidence of loss, damage
    or rifling was produced;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the loss or damage was wholly or partially the
    fault of the sender;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the damage was to mail that contained a glass or
    ceramic article or was of a perishable nature;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2010-287, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a common carrier would have been exempt from legal
    liability;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the mail that was lost, damaged or rifled contained
    intoxicating liquor; or

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the mail was lawfully seized by the country of
    destination.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} No indemnity shall be paid pursuant to these
  Regulations in respect of registered mailable matter unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicable receipt issued to the sender by the
    Corporation is available;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (9), the person claiming the
    indemnity submits a claim to the Corporation within the following
    claim periods, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} within 90 days after the date of mailing in the
      case of items mailed in Canada for delivery within Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} within 6 months after the date of mailing in the
      case of items mailed in Canada for delivery outside of Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} proof is submitted by the person claiming the
    indemnity of the

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} value of the registered mailable matter, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} loss, damage or rifling of the registered mail.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/98-559, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (9), where indemnity is paid in
  respect of registered mailable matter pursuant to these Regulations,
  it shall be paid to the sender or, at the request of the sender, to
  the addressee.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Where the addressee, or the sender in a case where an
  item is returned to the sender, accepts delivery of a registered mail
  item posted to a country outside Canada without complaint or evidence
  of loss or damage and immediately after that informs the Corporation
  of the loss of or the damage to the item and provides proof that the
  loss or damage did not occur after delivery, indemnity may be paid to
  the addressee or the sender, as the case may be.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} Where indemnity is paid for the loss of a registered
  mail item posted to a country outside Canada and the registered mail
  item is subsequently found, the sender or addressee, as the case may
  be, shall be informed that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the registered mail item has been found; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sender or addressee, as the case may be, may
    take delivery of the registered mail item within three months after
    being so informed on repayment of the indemnity to the Corporation.

## [PART IX]{.HLabel1}[Payment of Indemnity for Registered Mailable Matter Posted Outside Canada for Delivery in Canada Under the Universal Postal Convention]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502526 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2010-287, s. 6

\]
:::

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) to (6),
  when registered mailable matter that is posted outside Canada for
  delivery in Canada is lost, the Corporation must pay an indemnity
  equal to the amount, in Canadian currency, set out in the Universal
  Postal Convention for the loss of such mailable matter.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No indemnity shall be paid pursuant to subsection (1)
  unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2010-287, s. 7\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person or postal administration claiming the
    indemnity submits a claim to the Corporation within six months after
    the date of mailing; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} proof of the loss of the registered mailable matter
    is submitted to the Canada Post Corporation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No indemnity shall be paid pursuant to subsection (1)
  where,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a loss is due to force majeure;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the registered mailable matter cannot be accounted
    for owing to destruction by force majeure of the records of the
    postal administration where the loss occurred unless proof of the
    liability of that postal administration is submitted by the person
    claiming the indemnity to the Canada Post Corporation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the registered item contains non-mailable matter as
    described in the schedule to the [Non-mailable Matter
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-10);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2010-287, s. 7\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2003-378, s. 11\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the registered mailable matter was lawfully seized
    by Canadian authorities; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} damage that has occurred to the registered mailable
    matter is due to the nature of the contents of the mailable matter.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where indemnity is paid pursuant to subsection (1),
  it shall be paid to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sender;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the addressee or a third party at the request of
    the sender;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the addressee, or the sender or a third party at
    the request of the addressee, where the addressee has taken delivery
    of mailable matter of which the whole of the contents has been lost
    or damaged; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the postal administration.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where indemnity is paid pursuant to subsection (1)
  and the mailable matter referred to in that subsection is subsequently
  found,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sender and the addressee shall be informed that
    the mailable matter has been found; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sender, or the addressee, if the sender
    requested that the indemnity be paid to the addressee pursuant to
    subsection (4), shall be informed that he may take delivery of the
    mailable matter

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} within a period of three months, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on repayment to the Canada Post Corporation of
      the amount of indemnity so paid.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsections (1) to (5), registered
  mailable matter is lost where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the mailable matter cannot be found by the Canada
    Post Corporation,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the whole of the contents of the mailable matter
    has been stolen, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the contents of the mailable matter has been
    damaged and the cost of repair or replacement of the contents would
    exceed the depreciated value of the mailable matter when posted,

  if

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the loss, theft or damage is discovered before or
    at the time of delivery of the mailable matter to the addressee or,
    where it is returned to the sender, to the sender; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of mailable matter referred to in
    paragraphs (b) and (c), the packing of the mailable matter was
    recognized by the Canada Post Corporation as reasonably sufficient
    to protect the mailable matter against risk of theft or damage.
