## Equivalent Courses (GST/HST) Regulations {#equivalent-courses-gsthst-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-27
:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Registration 1990-12-18

Equivalent Courses (GST/HST) Regulations

P.C. 1990-2736  1990-12-18
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 277(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-91-27_e_hq_6136){#fn_SOR-91-27_e_hq_6136-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed Regulations prescribing equivalents of courses that follow a
curriculum designated by a school authority.

  1990, c. 45, s. 12
  :::
:::

s. 5\]]{.Repealed}

## [Prescribed Courses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-933658 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of section
9 of Part III of Schedule V to the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15),
music lessons are prescribed equivalents of music courses that follow a
curriculum designated by a school authority.

- SOR/94-368, s. 4
