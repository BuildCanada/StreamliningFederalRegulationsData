## Order Designating the Prime Minister as the Minister responsible for that Act {#order-designating-the-prime-minister-as-the-minister-responsible-for-that-act .Title-of-Act}

:::

[NATIONAL SECURITY AND INTELLIGENCE REVIEW AGENCY
ACT](/eng/acts/N-16.62)

Registration 2019-07-24

Order Designating the Prime Minister as the Minister responsible for
that Act

P.C. 2019-1089 2019-07-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}

the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 55(a) of the [National
Security and Intelligence Review Agency
Act](/eng/acts/N-16.62)[[Footnote
