## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1484833 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dissident's proxy circular]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dissident's proxy circular]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    dissident's proxy circular referred to in paragraph 156.05(1)(b) of
    the Act. ([circulaire de procuration d'opposant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[management proxy circular]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[management proxy circular]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    management proxy circular referred to in paragraph 156.05(1)(a) of
    the Act. ([circulaire de la direction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of National Instrument 51-102

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations, [[NI
51-102]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the version of National Instrument
51-102 that applies within a province set out in column 1 of the table
to this section in accordance with the instrument set out in column 2.

## [Proxies and Proxy Solicitation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1484847 .Part}

### [Form of Proxy]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1484848 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}NI 51-102

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection 156.02(4) of the Act, a form of proxy must be in a form
  that complies with the requirements set out in section 9.4 of NI
  51-102.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of certain words

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), in section 9.4 of
  NI 51-102

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a reference to "form of proxy" is to be read as a
    reference to [[form of proxy]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
    section 2 of the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a reference to "securityholder" is to be read as a
    reference to a shareholder within the meaning of section 7 of the
    Act.

### [Proxy Solicitation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1484856 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public announcement

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subparagraph (b)(v) of the definition
[[solicitation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 156.01 of the Act, a
solicitation does not include a public announcement that is made by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a speech in a public forum; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a press release, opinion, statement or advertisement
  that is provided through a broadcast medium or by a telephonic,
  electronic or other communication facility or that appears in a
  newspaper, magazine or other publication that is generally available
  to the public.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed circumstances

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of subparagraph (b)(vii) of the definition
  [[solicitation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 156.01 of the Act,
  the prescribed circumstances are circumstances in which the
  communication is made to shareholders

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by one or more shareholders and concerns the
    business and affairs of a bank --- including its management or
    proposals set out in or attached to a management proxy circular ---
    and no form of proxy is sent to the shareholders to whom the
    communication is made by the shareholder or shareholders making the
    communication or by a person acting on their behalf;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by one or more shareholders and concerns the
    organization of a dissident's proxy solicitation, and no form of
    proxy is sent to the shareholders to whom the communication is made
    by the shareholder or shareholders making the communication or by a
    person acting on their behalf;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} as clients, by a person who gives financial,
    corporate governance or proxy voting advice in the ordinary course
    of business and concerns proxy voting advice if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the person discloses to the shareholder any
      significant relationship with the bank and any of its affiliates
      or with a shareholder who has submitted a proposal under
      subsection 143(1) of the Act and any material interests the person
      has in relation to a matter on which advice is given,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the person receives any special commission or
      remuneration for giving the proxy voting advice only from the
      shareholder or shareholders receiving the advice, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the proxy voting advice is not given on behalf
      of any person soliciting proxies or on behalf of a nominee for
      election as a director; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by a person who does not seek directly or
    indirectly, the power to act as proxy for a shareholder.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The circumstances described in paragraph (1)(a) are
  not prescribed circumstances if the communication is made by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a shareholder who is an officer or director of the
    bank, or who serves in a similar capacity, if the communication is
    financed directly or indirectly by the bank;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a shareholder who is a nominee or who proposes a
    nominee for election as a director, if the communication relates to
    the election of directors;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a shareholder whose communication is in opposition
    to an amalgamation, arrangement, reorganization or other transaction
    recommended or approved by the board of directors of the bank and
    who is proposing or intends to propose an alternative transaction to
    which the shareholder or an affiliate or associate of the
    shareholder is a party;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a shareholder who, because of a material interest
    in the subject-matter to be voted on at a shareholders meeting, is
    likely to receive a benefit from its approval or non-approval, which
    benefit would not be shared [pro rata]{.otherLang lang="la"} by all
    other holders of the same class of shares, unless the benefit arises
    from the shareholder's employment with the bank; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any person acting on behalf of a shareholder
    described in any of paragraphs (a) to (d).

## [Proxy Circulars]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1484878 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required form

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and sections 7 and 8, a management proxy circular
  and a dissident's proxy circular must be in the form provided for in
  Form NI 51-102F5 (Information Circular) of NI 51-102.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The circulars referred to in subsection (1) are not
  required to include the information referred to in Items 8 to 10 and
  Item 16 of Form NI 51-102F5 (Information Circular) if they are in
  respect of a bank or bank holding company that, as the case may be,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not a distributing bank or distributing bank
    holding company described in section 2 of the [Distributing Bank and
    Distributing Bank Holding Company
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-303); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is subject to an exemption under subsection 2.4(2)
    or (3) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of certain words

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), in Form NI
  51-102F5 (Information Circular)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a reference to "affiliate" is to be read as a
    reference to [[affiliate]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
    section 2 of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a reference to "subsidiary" is to be read as a
    reference to [[subsidiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
    section 2 of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a reference to "beneficial ownership" is to be read
    as a reference to [[beneficial ownership]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as
    defined in section 2 of the Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a reference to "company" is to be read as a
    reference to [[bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or [[bank holding
    company]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as the case may be, as defined in
    section 2 of the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a reference to "security" is to be read as a
    reference to [[security]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
    section 2 of the Act;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a reference to "control" is to be read as a
    reference to "control" within the meaning of section 3 of the Act;
    and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a reference to "securityholder" is to be read as a
    reference to a shareholder within the meaning of section 7 of the
    Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Management proxy circular --- additional
information

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A management proxy circular
must also contain the following information and documents:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the percentage of votes required for the approval of
  any matter that is submitted to a vote of shareholders at the meeting;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement, signed by a director or an officer of
  the bank or bank holding company, indicating that the directors have
  approved the content and sending of the management proxy circular;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a proposal made to the bank or bank
  holding company under section 143 or 732 of the Act, the day by which
  it must be received by the bank or bank holding company, as the case
  may be;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for every purchase of insurance under section 213 of
  the Act,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the insurance purchased by the bank
    in respect of the directors and officers, expressed as the total
    amount purchased or as the amount purchased for directors and
    officers, respectively,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the insurance premium or, if the
    insurance purchased was a comprehensive liability policy, the
    approximate amount of the insurance premium paid in respect of the
    directors and officers, expressed either as the total amount paid or
    as the amount paid for directors and officers, respectively,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the insurance premium paid by the
    directors and officers, expressed either as the total amount paid or
    as the amount paid by directors and officers, respectively, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a summary of each clause in the insurance policy,
    such as a co-insurance or deductible clause, that exposes the bank
    to a liability other than the payment of the insurance premium;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} for every indemnification paid or payable to a person
  under subsection 212(1) of the Act during the financial year,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name and job title of the person indemnified or
    to be indemnified,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount paid or payable to the person, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the circumstances that gave rise to the
    indemnification;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of the right to dissent that is
  provided to a shareholder under section 277 of the Act;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the circular includes a comparative annual
  financial statement referred to in paragraph 308(1)(a) or 840(1)(a) of
  the Act that has been audited by the auditor of the bank or of the
  bank holding company, a statement indicating that the financial
  statement has been audited and that it has been prepared in accordance
  with the accounting principles referred to in subsection 308(4) or
  840(4) of the Act, as the case may be; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the circular includes a comparative annual
  financial statement referred to in paragraph 308(1)(a) or 840(1)(a) of
  the Act but that financial statement has not been audited as described
  in paragraph (g), a report that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is signed by the chief financial officer of the
    bank or the directors of the bank holding company, as the case may
    be, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} indicates that the financial statements have not
    been audited but have been prepared in accordance with the
    accounting principles referred to in subsection 308(4) or 840(4) of
    the Act, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dissident's proxy circular --- additional
statement

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  dissident's proxy circular must also contain a statement, signed by
  the dissident or a person they have authorized, indicating that the
  dissident has approved the content and sending of the circular.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the dissident does not have any of the information
  that is required to be included in a dissident's proxy circular and
  the information cannot be readily obtained by them, the circular must
  contain an explanation of why the information cannot be readily
  obtained.
