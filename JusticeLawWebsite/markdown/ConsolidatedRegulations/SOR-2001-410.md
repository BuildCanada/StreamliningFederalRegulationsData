## [Permitted Use of Name]{.HTitleText1} {#h-675788 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of name of company or insurance
holding company

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** A person may use the name
of a company or of an insurance holding company in a prospectus,
offering memorandum, takeover bid circular, advertisement for a
transaction related to securities or in any other document in connection
with a transaction related to securities if the use is required by law
or if the company or insurance holding company, as the case may be, has
given its written permission for the use.
