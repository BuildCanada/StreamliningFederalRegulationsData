## Excise Duty Indexing Ratio Regulations {#excise-duty-indexing-ratio-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/83-680
:::

[EXCISE ACT](/eng/acts/E-14)

Registration 1983-08-24

Regulations Respecting the Ratio Used in the Indexing of Excise Duties
on Spirits, Beer, Tobacco, Cigars and Cigarettes

P.C. 1983-2590 1983-08-24
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance and the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant
to clauses 1(b)(i)(B)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EHBAA} and 2(b)(i)(B)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFBAA} of Part V of the schedule
to the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting the ratio used in the indexing of excise
duties on spirits, beer, tobacco, cigars and
cigarettes](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-680).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EHBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1980-81-82-83, c.
  68, s. 77
  :::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-880972 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Excise Duty Indexing Ratio
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-680).

## [Alteration of Ratio]{.HTitleText1} {#h-880974 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
adjusting the duties of excise enumerated in Parts I and III of the
schedule to the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14), other than
subsections 1(2) and (3) and section 2 of Part I thereof, the ratio
determined according to clause 1(b)(i)(B) of Part V of that schedule is
altered by multiplying the ratio

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by 1.35559 for the adjustment on the first day of
  September, 1983; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by 1.99924 for the adjustment on the first day of
  September, 1984.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/84-730, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
adjusting the duties of excise enumerated in Part IV of the schedule to
the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14), the ratio determined according to
clause 2(b)(i)(B) of Part V of that schedule is altered by multiplying
the ratio

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by 1.37078 for the adjustment on the first day of
  September, 1983; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by 1.88464 for the adjustment on the first day of
  September, 1984.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/84-730 s. 2
:::
