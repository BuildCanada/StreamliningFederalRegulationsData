## [Oath of Allegiance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-535661 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The oath of allegiance
prescribed by the [Oaths of Allegiance Act](/eng/acts/O-1) is the oath
of allegiance to be taken and subscribed by the Commissioner of Yukon.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-116, s. 24
:::

## [Oath of Office]{.HTitleText1} {#h-535665 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The oath of office to be
taken and subscribed by the Commissioner of Yukon is set out in Schedule
I.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-116, s. 28
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/2003-116,
s. 25\]]{.Repealed}

## [Swearing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-535670 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The oath of allegiance and
oath of office of the Commissioner of Yukon shall be sworn and
subscribed before a judge of the Supreme Court of the Yukon Territory
or, in his absence, a police magistrate.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-116, s. 28
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/2003-116,
s. 26\]]{.Repealed}

## [SCHEDULE I]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 3)]{.OriginatingRef}[Oath of Office for Commissioner of Yukon]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-535676 .scheduleLabel}
:::

I, ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}, do solemnly and
sincerely promise and swear that I will duly and faithfully and to the
best of my skill and knowledge execute the powers and trust reposed in
me as the Commissioner of Yukon. So help me God.

![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}![blank
line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-116, s. 28
