Definition of [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods that, on the date of their
importation, were classified under tariff item No. 2404.11.00 in the
List of Tariff Provisions as it read on January 1, 2022, but that would
have been classified under tariff item No. 2403.99.90 had they been
imported on December 31, 2021.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of goods that were imported
during the period beginning on January 1, 2022 and ending on the day
before the day on which this Order comes into force, in an amount equal
to the difference between

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the customs duties paid or payable at the rate of
  customs duty applicable to the goods on the date of importation, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the customs duties that would be payable at the rate
  of customs duty that would have applied to the goods had the goods
  been imported on December 31, 2021.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on
condition that a claim for remission is made to the Minister of Public
Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years after the date of
importation of the goods.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
