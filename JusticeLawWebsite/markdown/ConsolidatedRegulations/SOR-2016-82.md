## Critical Habitat of the Nooksack Dace (Rhinichthys cataractae ssp.) Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-nooksack-dace-rhinichthys-cataractae-ssp.-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2016-82
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2016-05-02

Critical Habitat of the Nooksack Dace (Rhinichthys cataractae ssp.)
Order
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Nooksack dace ([Rhinichthys cataractae]{.otherLang
lang="la"} ssp.) is a wildlife species that is listed as an endangered
species in Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBBFA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_b-ID0EBBFA){#fn_b}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

And whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is
specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection
58(2) of that Act;

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to subsections
58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EDACA}, makes the annexed [Critical
Habitat of the Nooksack Dace ([Rhinichthys cataractae]{.otherLang
lang="la"} ssp.) Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-82).

Ottawa, April 21, 2016

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838333 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Nooksack dace ([Rhinichthys cataractae]{.otherLang lang="la"}
ssp.), which is identified in the recovery strategy for that species
that is included in the Species at Risk Public Registry.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/2020-171,
s. 1\]]{.Repealed}
