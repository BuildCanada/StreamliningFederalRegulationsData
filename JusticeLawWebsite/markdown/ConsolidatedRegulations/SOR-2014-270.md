## 2015 Pan American and Parapan American Games Remission Order {#pan-american-and-parapan-american-games-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2014-270
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 2014-11-21

2015 Pan American and Parapan American Games Remission Order

P.C. 2014-1273 2014-11-20
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 115[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EGCAA} of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EDCAA}, makes the annexed [2015 Pan
American and Parapan American Games Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-270).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-807530 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[APC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[APC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Americas Paralympic Committee.
    ([CPA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Games]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Games]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Pan American Games or the
    Parapan American Games, as the case may be. ([Jeux]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Games family member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Games family member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual,
    other than an individual ordinarily resident in Canada, who is the
    holder of an accreditation issued by TO2015 and is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an athlete participating in the Games or a coach,
      judge, team official, support staff member or technical official
      in the Games; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a member of PASO, IPC, APC or of an NOC, an NPC
      or an international sports federation. ([membre de la famille des
      Jeux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[IPC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[IPC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Paralympic
    Committee. ([CIP]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[media accreditation holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[media accreditation holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    individual, other than an individual ordinarily resident in Canada,
    who holds a media accreditation issued by TO2015 for the purpose of
    conducting media coverage of the Games. ([titulaire d'une
    accréditation de média]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[media rights holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[media rights holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person that has
    been granted broadcasting rights in respect of the Games by TO2015,
    PASO, IPC or APC. ([titulaire de droits de
    diffusion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[NOC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[NOC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a National Olympic Committee of a
    country that is participating in the Pan American Games.
    ([CNO]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[NPC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[NPC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a National Paralympic Committee of
    a country that is participating in the Parapan American Games.
    ([CNP]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Pan American Games]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Pan American Games]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the 2015 Pan
    American Games to be held in and around Toronto, Ontario, during the
    period beginning on July 10, 2015 and ending on July 26, 2015.
    ([Jeux panaméricains]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Parapan American Games]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Parapan American Games]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the 2015
    Parapan American Games to be held in and around Toronto, Ontario,
    during the period beginning on August 7, 2015 and ending on
    August 15, 2015. ([Jeux parapanaméricains]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[PASO]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[PASO]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Pan American Sports
    Organization. ([OSP]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual, a partnership, a
    corporation, a trust, the estate or succession of a deceased
    individual, or a body that is a society, a union, a club, an
    association, a commission or another organization of any kind.
    ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sponsor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sponsor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person that has been
    designated by TO2015, PASO, IPC or APC as an official sponsor of the
    Games. ([commanditaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[supplier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[supplier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person that has been
    designated by TO2015, PASO, IPC or APC as an official supplier to
    the Games. ([fournisseur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[TO2015]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[TO2015]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Toronto Organizing
    Committee for the 2015 Pan American and Parapan American Games.
    ([TO2015]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-807549 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order does not apply
to alcoholic beverages or tobacco products.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-807551 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
tax paid or payable under Part IX of the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15), and of the customs duties paid or payable, on
goods imported temporarily into Canada by a Games family member or a
media accreditation holder, if the goods are imported for the exclusive
use of that member or holder in connection with the Games.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted of a portion of
  the tax paid or payable under Part IX of the [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15), and of the customs duties paid or payable, on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods for display, including apparatus to display
    those goods, imported temporarily into Canada by TO2015, PASO, IPC,
    APC, an NOC, an NPC, a media rights holder, a sponsor or a supplier,
    or the agent or representative of any of them, for use exclusively
    in connection with the Games;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} equipment imported temporarily into Canada by
    TO2015, PASO, IPC, APC, an NOC, an NPC, a media rights holder, a
    sponsor or a supplier, or the agent or representative of any of
    them, for use exclusively in connection with the Games; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} vehicles of heading No. 87.02 in the List of Tariff
    Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) that are imported temporarily into
    Canada, by TO2015 or a person who acts on its behalf, for use
    exclusively in connection with the Games.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the tax remitted under subsection (1)
  is equal to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods, equipment or vehicles that
    are imported by a person who is not resident in Canada and is not a
    registrant, as defined in subsection 123(1) of the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the total of the tax paid or payable under
    Part IX of that Act in respect of the importation of the goods,
    equipment or vehicles; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the difference between

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the total of the tax paid or payable under
      Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) in respect of the
      importation of the goods, equipment or vehicles, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the tax paid or payable under
      Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) in respect of the
      importation on 1/60 of the value of the goods, equipment or
      vehicles for each month, or portion of a month, that the goods,
      equipment or vehicles remain in Canada.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
customs duties paid or payable on goods imported into Canada by TO2015,
PASO, IPC, APC, an NOC, an NPC, a media rights holder, a sponsor or a
supplier, or the agent or representative of any of them, if the goods
have a unit value of \$60 or less and are intended for free distribution
in connection with the Games.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
tax paid or payable under Part IX of the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15), and of the customs duties paid or payable, on
goods that are imported into Canada by TO2015 or a Games family member,
if the goods have a unit value of \$60 or less and are intended for
distribution in connection with the Games as gifts or awards to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Games family member;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a member, agent or representative of TO2015;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an individual who is resident in Canada and is
  participating in the Games; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an individual who is resident in Canada and is acting
  in an official capacity in connection with the Games.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
customs duties paid or payable on goods imported into Canada by TO2015,
if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods are

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} ancillary to the hosting or staging of the events
    of the Games, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} athletic equipment that is certified by the
    relevant international sports federation as complying with the
    international competition standards applicable to the sport for
    which it is designed and that is required by an athlete exclusively
    for the purpose of training or competing in the Games;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods are donated at the conclusion of the Games
  to a not-for-profit sports club, a sports federation, a registered
  charity, an educational or other public institution or a municipality,
  town or city; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the goods are not sold or otherwise disposed of
  within two years after the day on which they are imported.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
customs duties paid or payable on clothing imported into Canada, if the
clothing

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is donated to TO2015 by a sponsor or supplier;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is provided free of charge to TO2015 or Games
  volunteers to be worn as uniforms when undertaking their official
  volunteer duties in connection with the Games; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is kept by the individual volunteer recipients
  following the Games.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-807579 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted under
sections 3 and 4 on the condition that, on or before December 31, 2015,
the goods, equipment or vehicles are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} exported from Canada; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} destroyed in Canada under the supervision of an
  officer of the Canada Border Services Agency at the expense of the
  importer.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted
under this Order only to the extent to which the amount remitted has not
otherwise been rebated, remitted, credited or refunded to any person
under the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) or any other
Act of Parliament.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted
under this Order on the condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods, equipment or vehicles are imported into
  Canada during the period beginning on August 1, 2014 and ending on
  August 15, 2015;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years after the
  day on which the goods, equipment or vehicles were accounted for under
  section 32 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the importer provides the Canada Border Services
  Agency with evidence or information that demonstrates that the
  importer is entitled to remission under this Order.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-807588 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into
force on the day on which it is registered.
