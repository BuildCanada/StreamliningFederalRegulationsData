## Bolster and Sideframe Remission Order {#bolster-and-sideframe-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-124
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1993-03-09

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties on Specified Bolsters
and Sideframes for Railcars

P.C. 1993-419  1993-03-09
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 101 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-93-124_e_hq_6247){#fn_SOR-93-124_e_hq_6247-ID0EDBAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of
customs duties on specified bolsters and sideframes for
railcars](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-124).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949963 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Bolster and Sideframe Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-124).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949965 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
Schedule I to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on railcar
bolsters and sideframes imported by Ronsco Inc., Montreal, Quebec.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949967 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted
pursuant to section 2 on the condition that:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the bolsters and sideframes are for use by CN North
  America, Montreal, in the conversion of 525 boxcars from 70 ton
  capacity to 100 ton capacity at its Transcona shop in Winnipeg or its
  shop in Moncton, New Brunswick; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within three years of the date of importation of the
  goods for which remission is claimed.
