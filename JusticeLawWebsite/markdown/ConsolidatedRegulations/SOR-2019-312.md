## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1197326 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this By-law.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Deposit Insurance
      Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[determination date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[determination date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
      member institution,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a winding-up order is made in respect of the
        institution before the day on which the Corporation makes a
        payment in respect of the majority --- in number or value --- of
        the deposits held by the institution that are insured by deposit
        insurance, the day on which the petition or other originating
        process is filed in respect of the winding-up; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a winding-up order is not made in respect of
        the institution before the day on which the Corporation makes a
        payment in respect of the majority --- in number or value --- of
        the deposits held by the institution that are insured by deposit
        insurance, the day on which any of the circumstances described
        in subsection 14(2.1) of the Act first occurs in respect of the
        institution. ([date-repère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[senior officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[senior officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
      nominee broker or professional trustee that is not an individual,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its chief executive officer or a member of its
        board of directors, or an individual who performs functions
        similar to those normally performed by someone occupying one of
        those positions; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an officer who reports directly to a person
        referred to in paragraph (a) or to the board of
        directors. ([cadre dirigeant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[special income arrangement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[special income arrangement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a registered retirement savings plan referred
        to in subsection 5(1) of the schedule to the Act;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a registered retirement income fund referred to
        in subsection 5(2) of the schedule to the Act;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a tax-free savings account referred to in
        subsection 5(3) of the schedule to the Act;

      - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} a Tax-Free First Home Savings Account
        referred to in subsection 5(3.1) of the schedule to the Act;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a registered education savings plan referred to
        in subsection 5(4) of the schedule to the Act; or

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a registered disability savings plan referred
        to in subsection 5(5) of the schedule to the Act. ([arrangement
        spécial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of special income arrangement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this By-law, each paragraph in
  the definition [[special income arrangement]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
  describes a type of special income arrangement.

## [Co-owned Deposits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1197345 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 4(1) of the schedule to the Act, a co-ownership must be
disclosed on the records of a member institution before the
determination date by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicating that the deposit is co-owned; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} setting out each co-owner's name.

## [Trust Deposits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1197350 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1197351 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records of member institution

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of subsection 6(1) of the schedule to the Act, if the deposit
  is neither a nominee broker deposit nor a deposit held in an account
  identified as a professional trustee account, the trusteeship must be
  disclosed on the records of the member institution before the
  determination date by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicating that the deposit is held in trust; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} setting out each trustee's name and one trustee's
    address.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest or right of beneficiary

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection 6(2) of the schedule to
  the Act, if the deposit is neither a nominee broker deposit nor a
  deposit held in an account identified as a professional trustee
  account, the trusteeship must be disclosed on the records of the
  member institution before the determination date by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicating that the deposit is held in trust;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} setting out each trustee's name and one trustee's
    address; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} setting out the beneficiary's name and address.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Multiple beneficiaries

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection 6(3) of the schedule to
  the Act, if the deposit is neither a nominee broker deposit nor a
  deposit held in an account identified as a professional trustee
  account, a beneficiary's interest or right in the deposit must be
  disclosed on the records of the member institution before the
  determination date by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicating that the deposit is held in trust;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} setting out each trustee's name and one trustee's
    address;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} setting out the beneficiary's name and address; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} setting out the amount or percentage of the
    beneficiary's interest or right in the deposit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special income arrangement

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If a deposit is received
under a special income arrangement from a depositor who is acting as a
trustee for another person and it is neither a nominee broker deposit
nor a deposit held in an account identified as a professional trustee
account, the type of special income arrangement and the name and address
of the individual for whose benefit the arrangement is established are
to be disclosed on the records of the member institution before the
determination date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to depositor

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of subsection 6(5) of the schedule to the Act, the information
  that the Corporation may require a member institution to provide to a
  depositor who indicates that they are acting as a trustee for another
  person includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fact that the depositor is responsible for
    providing the member institution with, and updating, the information
    referred to in section 3 for disclosure on the member institution's
    records;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which the depositor may provide and
    update that information;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fact that failure to provide that information
    may result in the deposit not receiving full deposit insurance
    protection; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the fact that the Corporation will rely on the
    latest information disclosed on the member institution's records,
    which could affect deposit insurance coverage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time and manner

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information must be provided in writing at the
  time of opening an account for the depositor and during the month of
  March in each year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a deposit

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for which the depositor is a nominee broker;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is held in an account identified as a
    professional trustee account; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for which the depositor is the member institution.

### [Nominee Broker Deposits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1197384 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to member institution

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of subparagraph 7(1)(a)(ii) of the schedule to the Act,
  alphanumeric codes must be assigned in accordance with the following
  rules:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a nominee broker must not assign the same code to
    more than one beneficiary;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a nominee broker must assign the same code in
    respect of all of a beneficiary's deposits made by the nominee
    broker at the same member institution that are held under the same
    type of special income arrangement;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a nominee broker must assign the same code in
    respect of all of a beneficiary's deposits made by the nominee
    broker at the same member institution that are not held under a
    special income arrangement;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a nominee broker may assign the same code in
    respect of the beneficiary's deposits that are held under different
    types of special income arrangement or under no such arrangement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subparagraph 7(1)(a)(iv) of the
  schedule to the Act, the nominee broker must provide the following
  information to the member institution:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nominee broker's legal name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the type of special income arrangement under which
    the deposit is held or an indication that the deposit is not held
    under any special income arrangement;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the deposit is held under a special income
    arrangement, the alphanumeric code, assigned in accordance with the
    following rules, for each individual for whose benefit the
    arrangement is established, along with an indication that the code
    is assigned for the purposes of this paragraph:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a nominee broker must not assign the same code to
      more than one individual,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a nominee broker must assign the same code to an
      individual in respect of all deposits made by the nominee broker
      at the same member institution that are held for the individual's
      benefit under the same type of special income arrangement,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a nominee broker may assign the same code to an
      individual in respect of deposits that are held for the
      individual's benefit under different types of special income
      arrangement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Same code as beneficiary and individual

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A nominee broker must assign the same alphanumeric
  code under subsection (1) and paragraph (2)(c) to a person who is both
  a beneficiary of a deposit that is held under a special income
  arrangement and an individual for whose benefit a deposit is held
  under the same type of special income arrangement at the same member
  institution.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records of member institution

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of subsection 6(1) of the schedule to the Act, if the deposit
  is a nominee broker deposit, the trusteeship must be disclosed on the
  records of the member institution before the determination date by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicating that the deposit is held in trust by a
    nominee broker; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} setting out the nominee broker's legal name and
    address.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest or right of beneficiary

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 6(2) of the schedule
  to the Act, if the deposit is a nominee broker deposit, the
  trusteeship must be disclosed on the records of the member institution
  before the determination date by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicating that the deposit is held in trust by a
    nominee broker;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} setting out the nominee broker's legal name and
    address; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} setting out the alphanumeric code assigned to the
    beneficiary in respect of the deposit in accordance with subsection
    6(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest or right of beneficiary

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 6(3) of the schedule
  to the Act, if the deposit is a nominee broker deposit, a
  beneficiary's interest or right in the deposit must be disclosed on
  the records of the member institution before the determination date by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicating that the deposit is held in trust by a
    nominee broker;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} setting out the nominee broker's legal name and
    address;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} setting out the alphanumeric code assigned to the
    beneficiary in respect of the deposit in accordance with subsection
    6(1); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} setting out the amount or percentage of the
    interest or right in the deposit associated with that code.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special income arrangement

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** If a nominee broker deposit
is held under a special income arrangement, the type of special income
arrangement and each alphanumeric code assigned in relation to it in
accordance with paragraph 6(2)(c) must be disclosed on the records of
the member institution before the determination date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to Corporation

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subparagraph 7(1)(b)(iii) of the schedule to the Act, the nominee broker
must provide the following information to the Corporation:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the type of special income arrangement, if any,
  associated with each alphanumeric code assigned in accordance with
  subsection 6(1);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} each alphanumeric code assigned in accordance with
  paragraph 6(2)(c), as well as the name of the individual and the type
  of special income arrangement associated with it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and manner of providing information

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The information referred
to in paragraph 7(1)(b) of the schedule to the Act must be provided
electronically in a format that permits data extraction and
manipulation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attestation

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  initial attestation referred to in paragraph 8(1)(b) of the schedule
  to the Act must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out the member institution's name and the
    nominee broker's legal name and mailing address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} include, if the nominee broker states that they are
    capable of fulfilling the obligations set out in paragraph 7(1)(b)
    of the schedule to the Act, a description of the nominee broker's
    policies and procedures for ensuring that they are capable of
    fulfilling those obligations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} include, if the nominee broker states that they are
    not capable of fulfilling the obligations set out in paragraph
    7(1)(b) of the schedule to the Act, an explanation of why they are
    not capable and their proposed actions and time frame for remedying
    the situation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be signed by the nominee broker or a senior officer
    of the nominee broker;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} set out the mailing address, email address and
    telephone number of the person who signed the attestation and, if
    the nominee broker is not an individual, of another senior officer
    of the nominee broker; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} be sent electronically not later than the 30th day
    after the day on which the nominee broker becomes a party to the
    agreement or arrangement that requires the making of the
    attestation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Multiple agreements or arrangements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, if an initial attestation is
  required under more than one agreement or arrangement entered into by
  a nominee broker within a 30-day period, the nominee broker may make
  and send to the Corporation, not later than the 30th day after the day
  on which the first agreement or arrangement is entered into, a single
  attestation containing the names of all of the member institutions
  with which the agreements or arrangements are entered into.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Update

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An updated attestation referred to in paragraph
  8(1)(b) of the schedule to the Act must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out the nominee broker's legal name and mailing
    address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out the name of each member institution with
    which the nominee broker has entered into an agreement or
    arrangement, highlighting any changes since the most recent updated
    attestation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} include, if the nominee broker states that they are
    capable of fulfilling the obligations set out in paragraph 7(1)(b)
    of the schedule to the Act, a description of the nominee broker's
    policies and procedures for ensuring that they are capable of
    fulfilling those obligations, unless they are unchanged since the
    nominee broker's last updated attestation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} include, if the nominee broker states that they are
    not capable of fulfilling the obligations set out in paragraph
    7(1)(b) of the schedule to the Act, an explanation of why they are
    not capable and their proposed actions and time frame for remedying
    the situation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be signed by the nominee broker or a senior officer
    of the nominee broker;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} set out the mailing address, email address and
    telephone number of the person who signed the attestation and, if
    the nominee broker is not an individual, of another senior officer
    of the nominee broker; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} be sent electronically no later than May 31 of each
    year, starting in 2022, and within 10 days after any change to the
    nominee broker's legal name or to their capability to fulfill the
    obligations set out in paragraph 7(1)(b) of the schedule to the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contact information

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of paragraph 8(1)(c) of the schedule to the Act, the
  contact information that must be provided to the member institution
  consists of the name, mailing address, email address and telephone
  number of the nominee broker, if they are an individual, or of two
  senior officers of the nominee broker.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Update

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The contact information must be updated within 10
  days after any change to it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Corporation

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of subsection 8(3) of the schedule to the Act, the member
  institution must notify the Corporation electronically, not later than
  the 15th day after the day on which it enters into an agreement or
  arrangement, by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} providing the nominee broker's legal name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specifying the day on which the member institution
    entered into the agreement or arrangement; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} including a confirmation that the agreement or
    arrangement includes the provisions required under subsection 8(1)
    of the schedule to the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}End of agreement or arrangement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection 8(4) of the schedule to
  the Act, the member institution must notify the Corporation
  electronically, not later than the 15th day after the day on which the
  member institution ceases to be a party to an agreement or
  arrangement, by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} providing the nominee broker's legal name; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specifying the day on which the member institution
    ceased to be a party to the agreement or arrangement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to nominee broker

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 8(5) of the schedule to the Act, the information that the
member institution must provide consists of an indication that the
nominee broker is not in compliance with paragraph 7(1)(a) of the
schedule to the Act and an indication of what information they have
failed to provide.

### [Professional Trustee Accounts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1197463 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records of member institution

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 6(1) and (2) of the schedule to the Act, if the deposit is
held in an account identified as a professional trustee account, the
trusteeship must be disclosed on the records of the member institution
before the determination date by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicating that the deposit is held in trust;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} indicating that the account in which the deposit is
  held is a professional trustee account; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} setting out the name and address of the professional
  trustee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of making attestation

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 9(a) of the schedule to the Act, the attestation must be made
in writing and be signed by the professional trustee or a senior officer
of the professional trustee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contact information

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 9(b) and subparagraph 11(c)(iii) of the schedule to the Act,
the contact information must be provided to the member institution in
writing and must include the name, mailing address, email address and
telephone number of the professional trustee, if they are an individual,
or of a senior officer of the professional trustee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information on request

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of paragraph 11(b) of the schedule to the Act, the
  following information must be provided, as it exists as of the date
  specified in the Corporation's request:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information referred to in paragraph 11(a) of
    the schedule to the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the deposit is held under a special income
    arrangement, the type of arrangement and the name and address of the
    individual for whose benefit the arrangement is established.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and manner of providing
  information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information must be provided electronically in a
  format that permits data extraction and manipulation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of providing attestation

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The attestation referred
to in subparagraph 11(c)(i) of the schedule to the Act must be provided
in writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information on removal of designation

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  member institution removes, in accordance with paragraph 12(3)(b) of
  the schedule to the Act, the designation of an account identified as a
  professional trustee account, the information that the Corporation
  may, for the purpose of subsection 6(5) of the schedule to the Act,
  require the member institution to provide to the depositor includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fact that the designation has been removed in
    accordance with that paragraph; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fact that the designation will be reinstated if
    the depositor complies with section 9 of the schedule to the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time and manner

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information must be provided in writing within
  five days after the day on which the designation is removed.
