## Inspection of Defence Materiel Order {#inspection-of-defence-materiel-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 707
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Authorizing the Minister of National Defence to Provide for the
Inspection of Defence Materiel Produced by Canadian Industry
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560872 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Inspection of Defence Materiel
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._707).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560874 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of National
    Defence; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Defence. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560878 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4, the
Minister is authorized to provide for the inspection of defence materiel
being produced by Canadian industry in Canada on behalf of allied,
foreign and Commonwealth governments.

## [Terms and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560880 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The authority granted to
the Minister under section 3 is subject to the following terms and
conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the inspection resources of the Department shall be
  provided without charge if reciprocal no-charge arrangements have been
  entered into between Canada and the country concerned;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the facilities of inspection services shall be
  provided under the following circumstances:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} without charge in those cases where no reciprocal
    no-charge arrangement exists and the inspection services can be
    provided without additional cost to the Crown and within the
    existing inspection resources of inspection services of the
    Department,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} when no reciprocal no-charge arrangement exists
    and the inspection services cannot be provided without additional
    significant cost to the Crown, such additional costs incurred in the
    carrying out of the inspection shall be recovered for the Department
    by the Department of Supply and Services from the foreign government
    concerned;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the facilities of the Canadian Forces be provided,
  subject to Treasury Board approval, under the following circumstances:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} without charge in those cases where no reciprocal
    no-charge arrangement exists and the inspection services can be
    provided without additional cost to the Crown and within the
    existing inspection resources of the Canadian Forces,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} when no reciprocal no-charge arrangement exists
    and the inspection services cannot be provided without additional
    significant cost to the Crown, such additional costs incurred in the
    carrying out of the inspection shall be recovered for the Department
    by the Department of Supply and Services from the foreign government
    concerned; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} no liability is assumed by the Crown in respect of or
  resulting from any such inspection.
