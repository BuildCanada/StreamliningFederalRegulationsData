## Personal Health Information Custodians in New Brunswick Exemption Order {#personal-health-information-custodians-in-new-brunswick-exemption-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2011-265
:::

[PERSONAL INFORMATION PROTECTION AND ELECTRONIC DOCUMENTS
ACT](/eng/acts/P-8.6)

Registration 2011-11-17

Personal Health Information Custodians in New Brunswick Exemption Order

P.C. 2011-1319 2011-11-17
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that the Personal Health
Information Privacy and Access Act, S.N.B. 2009, c. P-7.05, of New
Brunswick, which is substantially similar to Part 1 of the [Personal
Information Protection and Electronic Documents
Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-738-E_hq_9981){#fn_81000-2-738-E_hq_9981-ID0EBBBA},
applies to the personal health information custodians referred to in the
annexed Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-738-E_hq_9981-ID0EBBBA){#fn_81000-2-738-E_hq_9981}S.C.
  2000, c. 5
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Industry, pursuant to paragraph
26(2)(b) of the [Personal Information Protection and Electronic
Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-738-E_hq_9981){#fn_81000-2-738-E_hq_9981-ID0EDAAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Personal Health Information Custodians in New
Brunswick Exemption Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-265).
:::

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-775010 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Any personal health
information custodian to which the Personal Health Information Privacy
and Access Act, S.N.B. 2009, c. P-7.05, applies is exempt from the
application of Part 1 of the [Personal Information Protection and
Electronic Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6) in respect of the collection,
use and disclosure of personal health information that occurs in New
Brunswick.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-775012 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
