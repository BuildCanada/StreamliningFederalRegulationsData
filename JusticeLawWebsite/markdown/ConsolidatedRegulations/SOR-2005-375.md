## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721234 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of organizations named in Schedule I or IV to the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

## [Probationary Period]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721236 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  probationary period referred to in paragraph 61(1)(a) of the [Public
  Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), for the class of employees
  described in column 1 of an item of the schedule, is the period set
  out in column 2 of the item.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The probationary period does not include any period

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} of leave without pay;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} of full-time language training;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} of leave with pay of more than 30 consecutive days;
    or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} during which a seasonal employee is not required to
    perform the duties of the position because of the seasonal nature of
    the duties.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The probationary period for an employee who is
  disabled and requires job accommodation begins on the day on which the
  necessary accommodation is made.

## [Notice Period]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721245 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  notice period referred to in paragraph 62(1)(a) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), for the class of employees
  described in column 1 of an item of the schedule, is the period set
  out in column 3 of the item.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice period begins on the day the deputy head
  gives notice to an employee.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721249 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR_2005-375_EN_hq_4639){#fn_SOR_2005-375_EN_hq_4639-ID0EBCA}[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 12 of the [Public Service Modernization Act](/eng/acts/P-33.4),
being chapter 22 of the Statutes of Canada, 2003, comes into force.
