## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1194692 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[originating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[originating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means qualifying as originating
    in the territory of a Party under the rules of origin set out in the
    [CIFTA Rules of Origin
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-276). ([originaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[non-Party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-Party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Member States of the European Free Trade
      Association;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Member States of the European Union;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Mexico; or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the United States of America. ([partie non
      signataire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1194701 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 24(1)(b) of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011),
originating goods are entitled to the benefit of the Canada-Israel
Agreement Tariff if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods are shipped to Canada from Israel or
  another CIFTA beneficiary, without shipment through another country,
  either

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on a through bill of lading, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} without a through bill of lading and the importer
    provides, when requested by an officer, documentary evidence that
    indicates the shipping route and all points of shipment and
    transhipment prior to the importation of the goods;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods, except for goods listed in any
  of Chapters 50 through 63 of the List of Tariff Provisions, that are
  shipped to Canada from Israel or another CIFTA beneficiary through the
  territory of a non-Party, the importer provides, if requested by an
  officer, documentary evidence that indicates that the goods have not
  undergone

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} further production in the territory of a non-Party
    other than minor processing, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any processing in the territory of a non-Party
    that increased the transaction value of the goods by greater than
    10%;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in case of goods listed in any of Chapters 50 through
  63 of the List of Tariff Provisions that are shipped to Canada from
  Israel or another CIFTA beneficiary through the territory of a
  non-Party, the importer provides, if requested by an officer,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} documentary evidence that indicates the shipping
    route and all points of shipment and transhipment prior to the
    importation of the goods, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the customs control documents that
    demonstrate that the goods remained under customs control while in
    the territory of a non-Party; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the goods are shipped to Canada through another
  country, other than a non-Party, and the importer provides, when
  requested by an officer,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} documentary evidence that indicates the shipping
    route and all points of shipments and transhipment prior to the
    importation of the goods, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the customs control documents that
    establish that the goods remained under customs control while in
    that other country.
