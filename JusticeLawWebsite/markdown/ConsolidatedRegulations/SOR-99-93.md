## Tobacco (Access) Regulations {#tobacco-access-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/99-93
:::

[TOBACCO AND VAPING PRODUCTS ACT](/eng/acts/T-11.5)

TOBACCO ACT

Registration 1999-02-11

Tobacco (Access) Regulations

P.C. 1999-197 1999-02-11
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 42.1 of the Tobacco Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA}, the Minister
of Health laid a copy of the proposed [Tobacco (Access)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-93), in the annexed form, before
the House of Commons on June 3, 1998 and the House of Commons did not
concur in any report from a committee respecting the proposed
regulations within the following thirty sitting days;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to section 14 of the
Tobacco Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Tobacco (Access) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-93).

## [Proof of Age]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032932 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Only the following
documentation may be used to verify the age of a person for the purposes
of subsection 8(2) of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a driver's licence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a passport;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a certificate of Canadian citizenship that contains
  the person's photograph;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian permanent resident document;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian Armed Forces identification card; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other documentation that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is issued by a federal or provincial authority or a
    foreign government, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} contains the person's photograph, date of birth
    and signature.

## [Self-Service]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032942 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
exempt from the application of section 11 of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a manufacturer or wholesaler who sells tobacco
  products to persons other than consumers at a location to which
  consumers do not have access; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a retailer who sells tobacco products at a duty free
  shop as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

## [Signs Prohibiting Sales to Minors Posted At Retail]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032946 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), every sign to be posted by a retailer at a retail
  establishment pursuant to section 9 of the Act must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a total surface area of not less than
    600 cm^2^ and minimum dimensions of 20 cm by 30 cm;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} display the following message:

    "It is prohibited by federal law to provide tobacco products to
    persons under 18 years of age. [Il est interdit par la loi fédérale
    de fournir des produits du tabac aux personnes âgées de moins de 18
    ans.]{.otherLang lang="fr"}";

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} display the message set out in paragraph (b) in
    such a manner that the message is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} legible,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} centred on the sign,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in black Helvetica Bold type on a white
      background,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in type of such size that the message occupies
      not less than 30 per cent and not more than 40 per cent of the
      total surface area of the sign,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} displayed using upper-case lettering for the
      first letter of the message in each official language and
      lower-case lettering for the remainder of the message, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} surrounded by a red border along the edges of
      the sign that has a thickness of not less than 1 cm and not more
      than 1.5 cm; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be posted

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in such a manner that the sign is conspicuous and
      not obstructed from view, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at every location in the retail establishment
      where tobacco products are furnished.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A retailer is exempt from the application of section
  9 of the Act if the retailer carries on business in a province in
  which provincial legislation

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prohibits the sale of tobacco products to persons
    under a specified age that is eighteen years of age or greater; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} requires the posting of notices to that effect in
    retail establishments that sell tobacco products.
