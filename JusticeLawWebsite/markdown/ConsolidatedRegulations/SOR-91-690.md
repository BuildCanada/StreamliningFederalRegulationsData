the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs and the Minister of
Communications, pursuant to subsection 70.63(4)[[Footnote

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939524 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Retransmission Royalties Criteria
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-690).

## [Criteria]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939526 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The criteria to which the
Board must have regard in establishing under paragraph 70.63(1)(a) of
the [Copyright Act](/eng/acts/C-42) a manner of determining royalties
that are fair and equitable are the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} royalties paid for the retransmission of distant
  signals in the United States under the retransmission regime in the
  United States;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the effects on the retransmission of distant signals
  in Canada of the application of the [Broadcasting
  Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01) and regulations made thereunder; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} royalties and related terms and conditions stipulated
  in written agreements in respect of royalties for the retransmission
  of distant signals in Canada that have been reached between collecting
  bodies and retransmitters and that are submitted to the Board in their
  entirety.
