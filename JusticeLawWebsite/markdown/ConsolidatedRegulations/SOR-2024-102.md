## Order Exempting Kapisikama Lake, Located in Quebec, from the Application of Section 23 of the Canadian Navigable Waters Act {#order-exempting-kapisikama-lake-located-in-quebec-from-the-application-of-section-23-of-the-canadian-navigable-waters-act .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2024-102
:::

[CANADIAN NAVIGABLE WATERS ACT](/eng/acts/N-22)

Registration 2024-05-27

Order Exempting Kapisikama Lake, Located in Quebec, from the Application
of Section 23 of the Canadian Navigable Waters Act

P.C. 2024-578 2024-05-24
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Minister of Transport has received an application to exempt
Kapisikama Lake, located in Quebec, from the application of section
23[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81100-2-391_hq_1689276193){#fn_81100-2-391_hq_1689276193-ID0EECCA}
of the [Canadian Navigable Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-22)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81100-2-391_hq_1689276236){#fn_81100-2-391_hq_1689276236-ID0EBCCA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81100-2-391_hq_1689276193-ID0EECCA){#fn_81100-2-391_hq_1689276193}S.C.
  2019, c. 28, s. 57
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81100-2-391_hq_1689276236-ID0EBCCA){#fn_81100-2-391_hq_1689276236}R.S., c.
  N-22; S.C. 2012, c. 31, s. 316; S.C. 2019, c. 28, s. 46
  :::

And whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that this exemption
would be in the public interest;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, makes the annexed [Order
Exempting Kapisikama Lake, Located in Quebec, from the Application of
Section 23 of the Canadian Navigable Waters
Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-102) under subsection 24(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81100-2-391_hq_1689276193){#fn_81100-2-391_hq_1689276193-ID0EEAAA}
of the [Canadian Navigable Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-22)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81100-2-391_hq_1689276236){#fn_81100-2-391_hq_1689276236-ID0EBAAA}.
:::

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1453226 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Kapisikama Lake

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Kapisikama Lake, which is
located in Quebec and whose location and description are set out in the
table to this section, is exempt from the application of section 23 of
the [Canadian Navigable Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-22).
