## Goods for Ships and Aircraft (Excise) Drawback Regulations {#goods-for-ships-and-aircraft-excise-drawback-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-948
:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Registration 1986-09-11

Regulations Respecting the Drawback of Taxes Paid on Goods Manufactured
or Produced in Canada that are Supplied as Ships' Stores, Used for the
Equipment, Repair or Reconstruction of Ships or Aircraft or Delivered to
Telegraph Cable Ships Proceeding on an Ocean Voyage for Use in Laying or
Repairing Oceanic Telegraph Cables Outside Canadian Territorial Waters

P.C. 1986-2069 1986-09-11
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 46(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_ea){#footnote_ea-ID0EICAA} of the
[Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), is pleased hereby to make the annexed
[Regulations respecting the drawback of taxes paid on goods manufactured
or produced in Canada that are supplied as ships' stores, used for the
equipment, repair or reconstruction of ships or aircraft or delivered to
telegraph cable ships proceeding on an ocean voyage for use in laying or
repairing oceanic telegraph cables outside Canadian territorial
waters](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-948), effective on the day the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote_eb){#footnote_eb-ID0EBCAA}, other than
paragraph 99(1)(b), subsections 99(2) to (4) and sections 192 to 194
thereof, comes into force.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899220 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Goods for Ships and Aircraft (Excise) Drawback
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-948).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899222 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[ships' stores]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ships' stores]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods that have been
    designated as ships stores by the [Ships' Stores
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-40). ([approvisionnements de
    navire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899226 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
the grant, under subsection 46(1) of the Act and in the circumstances
and under the conditions prescribed in section 6, of a drawback of taxes
paid on goods manufactured or produced in Canada that are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} supplied as ships' stores;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} used for the equipment, repair or reconstruction of
  ships or aircraft set out in the schedule; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} delivered to any telegraph cable ship proceeding on
  an ocean voyage for use in laying or repairing oceanic telegraph
  cables outside Canadian territorial waters.

## [Person Who May Claim]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899231 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A drawback may only be
claimed by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person who supplied the goods referred to in
  paragraphs 3(a) and (c); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person who used the goods for the equipment,
  repair or reconstruction of ships or aircraft referred to in
  paragraph 3(b).

## [Application for Drawback]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899235 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
drawback must be filed in the prescribed form at the excise office
nearest the head office or a branch office of the claimant within four
years after the taxes were paid.

## [Circumstances and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899237 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A drawback may only be
granted if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods referred to in section 3 were supplied,
  used or delivered as described in paragraph 3(a), (b) or (c), as the
  case may be, before an application for the drawback is filed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods referred to in section 3 were not used in
  Canada for any purpose before being supplied, used or delivered as
  described in paragraph 3(a), (b) or (c), as the case may be;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods used for the equipment, repair
  or reconstruction of a ship or aircraft referred to in paragraph 3(b),
  the goods become part of the ship or aircraft or part of the equipment
  of that ship or aircraft referred to in that paragraph;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods delivered to a telegraph cable
  ship proceeding on an ocean voyage for use in laying or repairing
  oceanic telegraph cables outside Canadian territorial waters, the ship
  is registered and is used exclusively for laying or repairing oceanic
  telegraph cables; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the applicant provides a waiver, in the prescribed
  form, from all other persons entitled to claim a drawback, refund or
  remission of the taxes, waiving their right to do so.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 3)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-899245 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** Ocean-going ships operating or being repaired or reconstructed
  to operate exclusively in international trade

- **2** Foreign warships, within the meaning of the Ships Stores
  Regulations

- **3** Telegraph cable ships, within the meaning of the Ships Stores
  Regulations

- **4** Aircraft operating exclusively in international flights

- **5** Ships registered in a country other than Canada that are used
  exclusively for pleasure purposes

- **6** International aircraft, within the meaning of the Ships Stores
  Regulations
