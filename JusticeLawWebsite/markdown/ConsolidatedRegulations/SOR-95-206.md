## Air Transportation Tax Order, 1995 {#air-transportation-tax-order-1995 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-206
:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Registration 1995-04-26

Order Prescribing Amounts for the Purposes of Subparagraph 11(1)(a)(ii)
and Paragraphs 11(1)(b) and (2)(b) of the Excise Tax Act

P.C. 1995-667  1995-04-26
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 11[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-95-206_e_hq_6339){#fn_SOR-95-206_e_hq_6339-ID0EJCAA}
of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), is pleased hereby to revoke the
Air Transportation Tax Order, 1994, made by Order in Council P.C.
1994-666 of April 28, 1994[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-95-206_e_hq_6340){#fn_SOR-95-206_e_hq_6340-ID0EDCAA},
and to make the annexed [Order prescribing amounts for the purposes of
subparagraph 11(1)(a)(ii) and paragraphs 11(1)(b) and (2)(b) of the
Excise Tax Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-206), in substitution therefor,
effective May 1, 1995.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-971908 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Air Transportation Tax Order, 1995](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-206).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-971910 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15).
(*Loi*)

## [Amounts Prescribed]{.HTitleText1} {#h-971912 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of air transportation tax imposed, levied and collected under
  subsection 10(1) of the Act, if the amount paid or payable for the
  transportation of a person by air that begins after February 28, 1998,
  is paid or payable after December 31, 1997, is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subparagraph 11(1)(a)(ii) of
    the Act,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for one segment of air travel without either
      intermediate stops or return to the point of departure, \$1.50;
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in all other circumstances, \$3; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of paragraph 11(1)(b) of the Act,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the person completes his or her own ticket and
      that ticket is restricted by the ticketing system of the air
      carrier to one segment of air travel without either intermediate
      stops or return to the point of departure, \$15, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in all other circumstances, \$30.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of air transportation tax imposed, levied
  and collected under subsection 10(2) of the Act, if the amount paid or
  payable for the transportation of a person by air is paid or payable
  after December 31, 1997 and the emplanement occurs after February 28,
  1998, is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subparagraph 11(1)(a)(ii) of
    the Act,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the person completes his or her own ticket and
      that ticket is restricted by the ticketing system of the air
      carrier to one segment of air travel without either intermediate
      stops or return to the point of departure, \$1.50, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in all other circumstances, \$3;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of paragraph 11(1)(b) of the Act,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the person completes his or her own ticket and
      that ticket is restricted by the ticketing system of the air
      carrier to one segment of air travel without either intermediate
      stops or return to the point of departure, \$15, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in all other circumstances, \$30.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amount of air transportation tax imposed, levied
  and collected under subsection 10(1) of the Act, for the
  transportation of a person by air that begins on or before February
  28, 1998, is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subparagraph 11(1)(a)(ii) of
    the Act, if the person completes his or her own ticket and that
    ticket is restricted by the ticketing system of the air carrier to
    one segment of air travel without either intermediate stops or
    return to the point of departure, \$3;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of paragraph 11(1)(b) of the Act,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the person completes his or her own ticket and
      that ticket is restricted by the ticketing system of the air
      carrier to one segment of air travel without either intermediate
      stops or return to the point of departure, \$27.50, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in all other circumstances, \$55.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The amount of air transportation tax imposed, levied
  and collected under subsection 10(2) of the Act, if the emplanement
  occurs on or before February 28, 1998, is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subparagraph 11(1)(a)(ii) of
    the Act, if the person completes his or her own ticket and that
    ticket is restricted by the ticketing system of the air carrier to
    one segment of air travel without either intermediate stops or
    return to the point of departure, \$3;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of paragraph 11(1)(b) of the Act,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the person completes his or her own ticket and
      that ticket is restricted by the ticketing system of the air
      carrier to one segment of air travel without either intermediate
      stops or return to the point of departure, \$27.50, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in all other circumstances, \$55.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-11, s. 1
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  relation to an amount paid or payable after December 31, 1997 for
  transportation by air that begins after February 28, 1998, for the
  purposes of paragraph 11(2)(b) of the Act, the amount of the air
  transportation tax imposed, levied and collected in respect of each
  emplanement pursuant to the charter agreement is \$15.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In relation to an amount paid or payable for
  transportation by air that begins on or before February 28, 1998, for
  the purposes of paragraph 11(2)(b) of the Act, the amount of the air
  transportation tax imposed, levied and collected in respect of each
  emplanement pursuant to the charter agreement is \$27.50.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-11, s. 1
