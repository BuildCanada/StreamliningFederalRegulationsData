## Manitoba Chicken Marketing Levies Order {#manitoba-chicken-marketing-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/85-533
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1985-06-07

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Levies From
Chicken Producers in Manitoba
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Manitoba Chicken Broiler Producers' Marketing Board, pursuant to
section 4 of the [Manitoba Chicken
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._152), C.R.C., c. 152, hereby makes
the annexed [Order providing for the fixing, imposing and collecting of
levies from chicken producers in Manitoba](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-533).

Winnipeg, Manitoba, May 31, 1985
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887099 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Manitoba Chicken Marketing Levies
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-533).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887101 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Manitoba Chicken
    Broiler Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to The
    Natural Products Marketing Act of Manitoba; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[chicken]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chicken]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a chicken of any variety,
    grade or class that is not more than five months of age and that is
    raised or kept within the Province of Manitoba for a purpose other
    than egg production, and includes broiler, fryer and roasting
    chickens; ([poulet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who raises, keeps,
    prepares for slaughter or slaughters chickens within the Province of
    Manitoba and includes the employer of any such person, a person who
    under any lease or agreement is entitled to a share of the chickens
    raised or kept by such a person and a person who takes possession of
    any chickens from any such person under any form of security for a
    debt. ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Exception]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887106 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order does not apply
to the marketing of chickens less than 10 days old.

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887108 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay to
the Commodity Board levies at the rate of \$0.0076 per kilogram live
weight on all chicken marketed by the producer in interprovincial or
export trade.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-48, s. 1
- SOR/88-50, s. 1
:::

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887112 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), every producer shall pay the levies referred to in
  section 4 to the Commodity Board at its office, 1200 King Edward
  Street, Winnipeg, Manitoba, not later than seven days after the last
  day of the week in which the chickens were marketed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The levies referred to in section 4 may be deducted
  from the amount owed to the producer by the person who purchases the
  chicken and where a purchaser deducts such levies, the purchaser shall
  remit the levies so deducted to the Commodity Board at its office,
  1200 King Edward Street, Winnipeg, Manitoba, not later than seven days
  after the last day of the week in which the chickens were purchased.
