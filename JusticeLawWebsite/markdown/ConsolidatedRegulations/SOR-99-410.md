Canadian Peacekeeping Service Medal Award Regulations

P.C. 1999-1858 1999-10-21

the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 8 of the [Canadian Peacekeeping
Service Medal Act](/eng/acts/C-21.1)[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032108 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definition in this
section applies in these Regulations.

[[Medal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Medal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Peacekeeping
    Service Medal referred to in section 3 of the [Canadian Peacekeeping
    Service Medal Act](/eng/acts/C-21.1). ([médaille]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Qualifications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032111 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A Canadian citizen who has
completed a cumulative total of at least 30 days of peacekeeping
service, as referred to in subsection 4(1) of the [Canadian Peacekeeping
Service Medal Act](/eng/acts/C-21.1), since 1948 is qualified to be
awarded the Medal.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Persons of the following
classes are excluded from entitlement to a Medal:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} veterans of the Korean War;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} veterans of the Gulf and Kuwait War; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} veterans of future wars in which Canada participates.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032117 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
