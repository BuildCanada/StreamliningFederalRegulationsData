## Ontario Cream Producers' Marketing Levies Order {#ontario-cream-producers-marketing-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 216
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Levies from
Certain Cream Producers in Ontario
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536813 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Cream Producers' Marketing Levies
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._216).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536815 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Cream
    Producers' Marketing Board established by Order of the Lieutenant
    Governor in Council of Ontario pursuant to The Milk Act of Ontario;
    ([Office de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cream]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cream]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means cream delivered to a plant for
    manufacture into creamery butter; ([crème]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a cheese factory, concentrated
    milk plant, cream receiving station, creamery, dairy or milk
    receiving station; ([fabrique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a producer engaged in the
    production of cream; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a quota in pounds of milk-fat
    fixed and allotted to a producer by the Commodity Board in relation
    to the volume of sales of cream by all producers.
    ([contingent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536822 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  producer shall pay to the Commodity Board, in addition to the licence
  fees set out under the Ontario Cream Producers'
  Marketing-for-Processing Plan, levies at the rate of \$1 for each
  pound of butterfat sold or delivered by him to a plant that is in
  excess of the quota fixed and allotted to him.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The plant operator shall deduct from the moneys
  payable to a producer all levies payable by the producer to the
  Commodity Board under subsection (1) and shall pay such levies to the
  Commodity Board not later than the 15th day of the month following the
  month in which the levies were deducted.
