## Order Designating Prince Edward Island for the Purposes of the Criminal Interest Rate Provisions of the Criminal Code {#order-designating-prince-edward-island-for-the-purposes-of-the-criminal-interest-rate-provisions-of-the-criminal-code .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2014-277
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 2014-11-28

Order Designating Prince Edward Island for the Purposes of the Criminal
Interest Rate Provisions of the Criminal Code

P.C. 2014-1298 2014-11-27
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas Prince Edward Island has legislative measures that protect
recipients of payday loans and that provide for limits on the total cost
of borrowing under a payday loan agreement;

And whereas the Lieutenant Governor in Council of Prince Edward Island
has requested that the Governor in Council designate that province for
the purposes of section 347.1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#nbp_a){#nbp_a-ID0EECBA} of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#nbp_b){#nbp_b-ID0EBCBA},

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#nbp_a-ID0EECBA){#nbp_a}S.C.
  2007, c. 9, s. 2
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#nbp_b-ID0EBCBA){#nbp_b}R.S., c. C-46
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice and the Minister of Industry,
pursuant to subsection 347.1(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#nbp_a){#nbp_a-ID0EGAAA} of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#nbp_b){#nbp_b-ID0EDAAA}, makes the annexed [Order
Designating Prince Edward Island for the Purposes of the Criminal
Interest Rate Provisions of the](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-277)
[Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).
:::
