[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 4, 1981-82

Registration 1987-02-18

Regulations Respecting Insurance of Loans Made to Versatile Pacific
Shipyards Inc.

P.C. 1987-277 1987-02-18

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Regional Industrial Expansion and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to Industry, Trade and Commerce Vote 1a of Appropriation Act
No. 1, 1980-81[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EGCAA}, as

No. 4, 1981-82[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2star_e){#footnote2star_e-ID0EDCAA}, is
pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations respecting insurance of
loans made to Versatile Pacific Shipyards Inc.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910767 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Versatile Pacific Shipyards Inc.
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-95).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910769 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Regional
    Industrial Expansion; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any lender, or its
    assignee, other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Government of Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the government of any province of Canada,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an agency of any government referred to in
      paragraph (a) or (b) or any company that is effectively controlled
      by any such government or any agency thereof, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any municipal corporation; ([prêteur
      privé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Versatile Pacific Shipyards Inc.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Versatile Pacific Shipyards Inc.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    company incorporated with that name under the laws of the province
    of British Columbia and having its head office in Vancouver, British
    Columbia. ([Versatile Pacific Shipyards Inc.]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910778 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Insurance provided by the
Minister in respect of loans made by a private lender to Versatile
Pacific Shipyards Inc. to permit Versatile Pacific Shipyards Inc. to
continue operations until the completion of the construction of the
Henry Larson 1200 Series Icebreaker shall be subject to the terms and
conditions set out in sections 4 to 7.

## [Terms and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910780 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The insurance referred to
in section 3 shall be for an amount up to 100 per cent of any loss up to
13 million dollars that may result in respect of loans referred to in
that section.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A private lender shall pay
to the Minister, by semi-annual payments in advance, a fee of one half
of one per cent per annum of the amount of insurance in force.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where a private lender has
demanded repayment of a loan insured under these Regulations, the amount
payable by the Minister shall not exceed an amount equal to the lesser
of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of insurance in force on the date of
  demand, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 100 per cent of the loss incurred by the private
  lender.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The amount of insurance
provided by the Minister to a private lender may be reduced at the
request of the private lender.
