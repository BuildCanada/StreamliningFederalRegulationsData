## Critical Habitat of the Lilliput (Toxolasma parvum) Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-lilliput-toxolasma-parvum-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2022-215
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2022-10-20

Critical Habitat of the Lilliput (Toxolasma parvum) Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Lilliput ([Toxolasma parvum]{.otherLang lang="la"}) is a
wildlife species that is listed as an endangered species in Part 2 of
Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_91000-2-4879_hq_31146){#fn_91000-2-4879_hq_31146-ID0EBBFA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_91000-2-4879_hq_31146-ID0EBBFA){#fn_91000-2-4879_hq_31146}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

And whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is
specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection
58(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4879_hq_31143){#fn_81000-2-4879_hq_31143-ID0EBBDA}
of that Act;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4879_hq_31143-ID0EBBDA){#fn_81000-2-4879_hq_31143}S.C.
  2015, c. 10, s. 60
  :::

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans makes the annexed
[Critical Habitat of the Lilliput (Toxolasma parvum)
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-215) under subsections 58(4) and (5) of
the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_91000-2-4879_hq_31146){#fn_91000-2-4879_hq_31146-ID0EBACA}.

Ottawa, October 17, 2022

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Lilliput ([Toxolasma parvum]{.otherLang lang="la"}), which is
identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is included in
the Species at Risk Public Registry.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
