Scott Islands Protected Marine Area Establishment Order

P.C. 2018-712 2018-06-11

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}

the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to subsection 4.1(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-2358-E_hq_18963){#fn_81000-2-2358-E_hq_18963-ID0EGCAA}
of the [Canada Wildlife Act](/eng/acts/W-9)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-2358-E_hq_18964){#fn_81000-2-2358-E_hq_18964-ID0EDCAA},
makes the annexed [Scott Islands Protected Marine Area Establishment
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2018-44).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** There is established a
protected marine area to be known as the Scott Islands Protected Marine
Area in the area of the sea that forms part of the internal waters of
Canada, the territorial sea of Canada and the exclusive economic zone of
Canada and that is described in Schedule 1 and depicted in Schedule 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_IndF266_hq_22220){#fn_IndF266_hq_22220-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force on the day on which the
[Scott Islands Protected Marine Area
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-119) come into force.
