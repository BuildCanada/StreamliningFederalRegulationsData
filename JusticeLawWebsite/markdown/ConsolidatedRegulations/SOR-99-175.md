## [Specified Crown Agents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028720 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
definition [[specified Crown agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection
123(1) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the following are
prescribed agents of Her Majesty in right of Canada:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Broadcasting Corporation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Telefilm Canada;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} The Canadian Wheat Board;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Bank of Canada;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} every agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada that is
  included in Schedule III to the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11); and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} every agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada that is
  a subsidiary of a corporation referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to
  (e).
