## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-947904 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Corrections and Conditional
    Release Act](/eng/acts/C-44.6); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the committee established
    under subsection 37.31(3) of the Act; ([comité]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[CORCAN]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CORCAN]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the part of the Service that is
    responsible for penitentiary industry; ([CORCAN]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[correctional plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[correctional plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a correctional plan
    developed in accordance with section 102; ([plan
    correctionnel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[independent chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[independent chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
    appointed pursuant to section 24 to conduct hearings of serious
    disciplinary offences; ([président indépendant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[inmate grievance committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inmate grievance committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a committee
    that is established in a penitentiary for the purpose of reviewing
    inmates' grievances and making recommendations with respect thereto
    to the institutional head and that consists of an equal number of
    inmates and staff members; ([comité d'examen des griefs des
    détenus]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[net approved earnings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[net approved earnings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
    inmate, means the inmate's income per pay period from pensions,
    institutional work, programs referred to in paragraph 78(1)(a) of
    the Act, authorized employment in the community and sales of hobby
    crafts, less any deductions made for the purposes of reimbursement
    pursuant to subsection 104(4); ([gains nets
    approuvés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[offender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[offender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in Part I, an offender as defined in section 2 of
      the Act, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in Part II, an offender as defined in section 99
      of the Act; ([délinquant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[outside review board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[outside review board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a committee of
    members of the community, other than staff members or inmates, that
    is established for the purpose of reviewing inmates' grievances and
    making recommendations with respect thereto to the person who is
    reviewing the inmate's grievance; ([comité externe d'examen des
    griefs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[parole supervisor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[parole supervisor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 134(2) of the Act; ([surveillant de liberté
    conditionnelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[region]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[region]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means one of the following regions,
    namely, the Atlantic region, Quebec, Ontario, the Prairie region and
    the Pacific region; ([région]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registered health care professional]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registered health care professional]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    registered health care professional employed or engaged by the
    Service; ([professionnel de la santé agréé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[releasing authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[releasing authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in subsection 133(1) of the Act; ([autorité
    compétente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[secure area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[secure area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area within a
    penitentiary that is designated by the institutional head by means
    of institutional standing orders for that purpose. ([secteur de
    sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unauthorized item]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unauthorized item]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an item that is not
    authorized by a Commissioner's Directives or by a written order of
    the institutional head and that an inmate possesses without prior
    authorization; ([objet non autorisé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 93(5) of the Act. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-947925 .Subheading}

#### [Duties]{.HTitleText3} {#h-947926 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every staff member shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be familiar with the Act, these Regulations and every
  written policy directive that relates to the staff member's duties;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} perform the staff member's duties impartially and
  diligently and in accordance with the principles set out in the Act
  and in the Mission of the Correctional Service of Canada, published by
  the Service, as amended from time to time; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} encourage and assist offenders to become law-abiding
  citizens.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An institutional head is
responsible, under the direction of the Commissioner, for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the care, custody and control of all inmates in the
  penitentiary;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the management, organization and security of the
  penitentiary; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the direction and work environment of staff members.

#### [Authorization]{.HTitleText3} {#h-947935 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A staff
  member who is designated by name or position for that purpose in
  Commissioner's Directives may exercise the powers, perform the duties
  or carry out the functions that are assigned to the Commissioner by
  any of the following provisions of the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subsection 27(3);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 29;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} section 37.4; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subsection 81(3).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A staff member who is assigned responsibility for
  liaison with victims in Commissioner's Directives may exercise the
  powers, perform the duties or carry out the functions that are
  assigned to the Commissioner by section 26 of the Act.

#### [Citizen Advisory Committees]{.HTitleText3} {#h-947951 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  institutional head or a person responsible for a parole office may, in
  accordance with this section, set up a Citizen Advisory Committee that
  consists of members of the community in which the penitentiary or
  parole office is situated to promote and facilitate the involvement of
  members of the community in the operation of the Service.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An institutional head or a person responsible for a
  parole office shall ensure that the Citizen Advisory Committee is
  representative of the community in which the penitentiary or parole
  office, as the case may be, is situated.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No staff member or offender may be appointed to a
  Citizen Advisory Committee.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A Citizen Advisory Committee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} may advise an institutional head or a person
    responsible for a parole office on any matter within the
    institutional head's or person's jurisdiction; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall make itself available for discussions and
    consultations with the public, offenders, staff members and Service
    management.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The institutional head or a person responsible for a
  parole office shall ensure that the members of the Citizen Advisory
  Committee that relates to the penitentiary or parole office have
  reasonable access, for the purpose of carrying out the functions of
  the Committee, to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} every part of the penitentiary or parole office;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} every staff member of the penitentiary or parole
    office;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any offender in the penitentiary or under the
    supervision of the parole office; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any hearing, conducted under this Part or Part I of
    the Act, respecting an offender in the penitentiary or under the
    supervision of the parole office, if the offender consents to the
    access.

### [Inmates' Attendance at Judicial Proceedings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-947964 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an
  inmate is an applicant for a reduction in the inmate's number of years
  of imprisonment without eligibility for parole, pursuant to section
  745 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the Commissioner shall
  ensure that the inmate is produced in court for the purpose of
  attending the hearing of the application, where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the court requires that the inmate be present at
    the hearing; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the inmate requests to be present at the hearing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner or a staff member designated by the
  Commissioner may authorize the transfer of an inmate to another
  penitentiary or to a provincial correctional facility where the
  transfer is necessary to facilitate the inmate's attendance at a
  judicial proceeding.

### [Escorted Temporary Absences and Work Releases]{.HTitleText2} {#h-947970 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 17(1)(b) of the Act, the institutional head may authorize an
escorted temporary absence of an inmate

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for medical reasons to allow the inmate to undergo
  medical examination or treatment that cannot reasonably be provided in
  the penitentiary;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for administrative reasons to allow the inmate to
  attend to essential personal affairs or legal matters or to matters
  related to the administration of the sentence that the inmate is
  serving;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for community service purposes to allow the inmate to
  undertake voluntary activity with a non-profit community institution,
  organization or agency, or for the benefit of the community as a
  whole;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for family contact purposes to assist the inmate in
  maintaining and strengthening family ties as a support to the inmate
  while in custody and as a potential community resource on the inmate's
  release;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} for parental responsibility reasons to allow the
  inmate to attend to matters related to the maintenance of a
  parent-child relationship, including care, nurture, schooling and
  medical treatment, where such a relationship exists between the inmate
  and the child;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} for personal development for rehabilitative purposes
  to allow the inmate to participate in specific treatment activities
  with the goal of reducing the risk of the inmate re-offending or to
  allow the inmate to participate in activities of a rehabilitative
  nature, including cultural and spiritual ceremonies unique to
  Indigenous persons, with the goal of assisting the reintegration of
  the inmate into the community as a law-abiding citizen; or

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} for compassionate reasons to allow the inmate to
  attend to urgent matters affecting the members of the inmate's
  immediate family or other persons with whom the inmate has a close
  personal relationship.

### [Placement and Transfers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-947982 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An institutional head
shall ensure that an inmate is informed in writing of the reasons for
the placement of the inmate in a particular penitentiary and that the
inmate is given an opportunity to make representations with respect
thereto,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the penitentiary placement process takes place
  in a provincial correctional facility, within two weeks after the
  initial placement of the inmate in a penitentiary; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the penitentiary placement process takes place
  in a penitentiary, before the transfer of the inmate to the assigned
  penitentiary but after the initial reception process.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Before the transfer of an
inmate pursuant to section 29 of the Act, other than a transfer at the
request of the inmate, an institutional head or a staff member
designated by the institutional head shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} give the inmate written notice of the proposed
  transfer, including the reasons for the proposed transfer and the
  proposed destination;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} after giving the inmate a reasonable opportunity to
  prepare representations with respect to the proposed transfer, meet
  with the inmate to explain the reasons for the proposed transfer and
  give the inmate an opportunity to make representations with respect to
  the proposed transfer in person or, if the inmate prefers, in writing;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} forward the inmate's representations to the
  Commissioner or to a staff member designated in accordance with
  paragraph 5(1)(b); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} give the inmate written notice of the final decision
  respecting the transfer, and the reasons for the decision,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at least two days before the transfer if the final
    decision is to transfer the inmate, unless the inmate consents to a
    shorter period; and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} within five working days after the decision if the
    final decision is not to transfer the inmate.

### [Security Classification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-948006 .Subheading}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 30 of the Act, the Service shall consider the following factors
in assigning a security classification to each inmate:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the seriousness of the offence committed by the
  inmate;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any outstanding charges against the inmate;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the inmate's performance and behaviour while under
  sentence;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the inmate's social, criminal and, if available,
  young-offender history and any dangerous offender designation under
  the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any physical or mental illness or disorder suffered
  by the inmate;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the inmate's potential for violent behaviour; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the inmate's continued involvement in criminal
  activities.

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 30 of the Act, an inmate shall be classified as

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} maximum security where the inmate is assessed by the

    risk to the safety of the public in the event of escape, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} requiring a high degree of supervision and control

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} medium security where the inmate is assessed by the
  Service as

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} presenting a low to moderate probability of escape

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} minimum security where the inmate is assessed by the
  Service as

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} presenting a low probability of escape and a low
    risk to the safety of the public in the event of escape, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} requiring a low degree of supervision and control
    within the penitentiary.

### [Search and Seizure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-948140 .Subheading}

#### [Manner of Carrying out Searches]{.HTitleText3} {#h-948141 .Subheading}

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** A non-intrusive search
shall be carried out by means of a hand-held scanner, a walk-through
scanner whereby the person being searched is required to walk through a
metal detector scanner, or any similar non-intrusive device.

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** A frisk search shall be
carried out from head to foot, down the front and rear of the body,
around the legs and inside clothing folds, pockets and footwear.

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** A strip search shall
consist of a visual inspection of the person by a staff member, in the
course of which inspection the person being searched shall undress
completely in front of the staff member and may be required to open the
person's mouth, display the soles of their feet, run their fingers
through their hair, present open hands and arms, bend over or otherwise
enable the staff member to perform the visual inspection.

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** A strip search and a body
cavity search shall be carried out in a private area that is out of
sight of every other person except for one staff member of the same sex
as the person being searched, which staff member is required to be
present as a witness unless, in the case of a strip search, the search
is an emergency as described in subsection 49(4) of the Act.

- **[[46.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in
  this Part.

  [[body scanner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[body scanner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, for the purposes of section
      46 of the Act and subsection 48(2) of the Act, means a security
      screening device that is capable of detecting contraband that is
      in or on a clothed person and includes a device that can produce
      detailed images of the insides of a person's body. ([détecteur à
      balayage corporel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[detailed body scan search]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[detailed body scan search]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a body
      scan search conducted by a body scanner in a manner that does
      produce detailed images of the insides of a person's body that is
      the subject of the search for a staff member to review. ([fouille
      par balayage corporel détaillée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[dry cell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[dry cell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a cell without plumbing
      fixtures. ([cellule nue]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[non-detailed body scan search]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[non-detailed body scan search]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a body
      scan search conducted by a body scanner in a manner that does not
      produce detailed images of the insides of a person's body that is
      the subject of the search. ([fouille par balayage corporel
      non-détaillée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A body scan search --- detailed or non-detailed ---
  conducted in order to detect the presence of contraband shall be
  carried out in accordance with the Commissioner's Directives by a
  staff member trained in the use of the body scanner.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Prior to performing a body scan search, the Service
  shall make available to any person who is to be the subject of the
  search all relevant health and safety information about the body
  scanner.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The results of a detailed body scan search on an
  inmate who is detained in a dry cell under section 51 of the Act shall
  be provided to the institutional head as soon as practicable.

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} possession of the item would be lawful; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an owner who is an inmate,
    possession of the item by the inmate would not constitute possession
    of contraband or an unauthorized item.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to paragraph (5)(e), the institutional head
  or a staff member designated by the institutional head may order that
  the inmate be given a reasonable opportunity to make arrangements for

  be lawful.

  forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Service does not know who the owner is and 30
    days have passed since the seizure;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the owner does not apply for the return of the item
    within 30 days after being notified of the seizure;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} possession of the item would be unlawful;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an owner who is an inmate,
    possession of the item by the inmate would constitute possession of
    contraband or an unauthorized item and the inmate has not arranged
    for the disposal or safe-keeping of the item outside the

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the item is contraband or an unauthorized item and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the institutional head or staff member determines

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} possession of the item by the owner would be
    lawful.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (8), the institutional head or

    forfeiture of items that are seized;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the institutional head or staff member determines
    that the forfeiture would cause undue hardship to the inmate; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} possession of the item by the inmate would be

**[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this

[[approved procedure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    lang="fr"})

[[collector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[collector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a staff member or any other

    lang="fr"})

[[confirmation test]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[confirmation test]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a test of a sample

    confirmation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    lang="fr"})

#### [Collection of Samples]{.HTitleText3} {#h-948301 .Subheading}

- **[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  sample shall be collected in the following manner:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a collector shall be of the same sex as the donor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the collector shall ensure that the donor washes
    the donor's hands before providing a sample;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the collector shall provide the donor with a
    container for the sample and shall supervise as the donor provides
    the sample;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the collector shall give the donor up to two hours
    to provide a sample, from the time of a demand;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the collector shall ensure that the donor is kept
    separate from any other person except the collector and is
    supervised during the two hour period referred to in paragraph (d);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} once the sample has been provided, the collector
    shall, in the presence of the donor,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} seal the container with a pre-numbered seal,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} affix a label identifying the sample in such a
      manner that the identity of the donor is not disclosed to the
      laboratory,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} initial the label to certify that the container
      contains the sample provided by that donor,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} request the donor to initial the label and to
      certify in writing that the sample in the container was provided
      by that person, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} where the person is unable or refuses to comply
      with a request referred to in subparagraph (iv), initial the label
      in the place of the donor and certify in writing, in the presence
      of another person, that the person who provided the sample was
      unable or refused to comply with the request; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the collector shall maintain a record that
    indicates the number on the container that corresponds to the name
    of the donor.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a person fails to provide a sample in
  accordance with subsection (1), the person shall be considered to have
  refused to provide the sample.

#### [Testing of a Sample]{.HTitleText3} {#h-948317 .Subheading}

**[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** The testing of a sample
shall be conducted in the following manner:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the urinalysis program co-ordinator shall, before

  over-the-counter or prescription medication was being taken by the
  donor at the time the sample was provided, and shall inform the
  laboratory of any such medication;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sample shall be tested by the laboratory, using
  an approved procedure; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the result of the initial screening test is
  positive, a confirmation test shall be conducted on the sample.

### [Unescorted Temporary Absences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-948796 .Subheading}

**[[155]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-155 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of

  attend to essential personal affairs or legal matters or to matters

  serving;

  medical treatment, where such a relationship exists between the
  offender and the child;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} for personal development for rehabilitative purposes
  to allow the offender to participate in specific treatment activities
  with the goal of reducing the risk of the offender re-offending, or to
  allow the offender to participate in activities of a rehabilitative
  nature, including cultural and spiritual ceremonies unique to
  Indigenous persons, with the goal of assisting the reintegration of
  the offender into the community as a law-abiding citizen; or

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} for compassionate reasons to allow the offender to
  attend to urgent matters affecting the members of the offender's
  immediate family or other persons with whom the offender has a close
  personal relationship.

### [Conditions of Release]{.HTitleText2} {#h-948844 .Subheading}

- **[[161]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-161 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 133(2) of the Act, every offender who is
  released on parole or statutory release is subject to the following
  conditions, namely, that the offender

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on release, travel directly to the offender's place
    of residence, as set out in the release certificate respecting the
    offender, and report to the offender's parole supervisor immediately
    and thereafter as instructed by the parole supervisor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} remain at all times in Canada within the
    territorial boundaries fixed by the parole supervisor;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} obey the law and keep the peace;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} inform the parole supervisor immediately on arrest
    or on being questioned by the police;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} at all times carry the release certificate and the
    identity card provided by the releasing authority and produce them
    on request for identification to any peace officer or parole
    supervisor;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} report to the police if and as instructed by the
    parole supervisor;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} advise the parole supervisor of the offender's
    address of residence on release and thereafter report immediately

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any change in the offender's address of
      residence,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any change in the offender's normal occupation,
      including employment, vocational or educational training and
      volunteer work,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any change in the domestic or financial
      situation of the offender and, on request of the parole
      supervisor, any change that the offender has knowledge of in the
      family situation of the offender, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any change that may reasonably be expected to
      affect the offender's ability to comply with the conditions of
      parole or statutory release;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} not own, possess or have the control of any weapon,
    as defined in section 2 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46),
    except as authorized by the parole supervisor; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an offender released on day parole,
    on completion of the day parole, return to the penitentiary from
    which the offender was released on the date and at the time provided
    for in the release certificate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 133(2) of the Act,
  every offender who is released on unescorted temporary absence is
  subject to the following conditions, namely, that the offender

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on release, travel directly to the destination set
    out in the absence permit respecting the offender, report to a
    parole supervisor as directed by the releasing authority and follow
    the release plan approved by the releasing authority;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} remain in Canada within the territorial boundaries
    fixed by the parole supervisor for the duration of the absence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} obey the law and keep the peace;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} inform the parole supervisor immediately on arrest
    or on being questioned by the police;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} at all times carry the absence permit and the
    identity card provided by the releasing authority and produce them
    on request for identification to any peace officer or parole
    supervisor;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} report to the police if and as instructed by the
    releasing authority;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} return to the penitentiary from which the offender

### [Cancellation, Suspension, Termination and Revocation of Release]{.HTitleText2} {#h-948887 .Subheading}

- **[[163]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-163
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board cancels parole under
  subsection 124(3) of the Act, the Board shall review its decision
  within 90 days after the day on which the Board cancels the parole.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board terminates parole under subsection
  124(3) of the Act, the Board shall review its decision within 90 days
  after the day on which the Board is notified of the offender's
  recommitment to custody in a penitentiary.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the case of an offender has been referred to
  the Board pursuant to subsection 135(4) or (5) of the Act, and unless
  an adjournment of the review is granted by the Board at the offender's
  request, the Board shall render its decision within 90 days after the
  date of the referral, or the date of admission of the offender to a
  penitentiary or to a provincial correctional facility where the
  sentence is to be served in such a facility, whichever date is the
  later.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board acts pursuant to subsection 135(7) of
  the Act, the Board shall review its decision within 90 days after the
  day on which the Board is notified of the offender's recommitment to
  custody in a penitentiary.

- SOR/96-108, s. 4

### [Review by Way of Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-948895 .Subheading}

  of death, and who applies for an escorted temporary absence for
  community service, family contact, personal development for
  rehabilitative purposes or parental responsibilities, shall be by way
  of hearing until a first escorted temporary absence is approved by the
  Board.

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board conducts a review of

### [Access to the Registry of Decisions for Research Purposes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-948912 .Subheading}

- **[[167]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-167 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is requesting, pursuant to subsection 144(3) of the Act,
  access to the registry of decisions of the Board for research purposes
  shall apply in writing to the Board and provide a written description
  of the nature of the information and the classes of decisions in
  respect of which access is sought.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), where the conditions set
  in subsection (1) have been complied with, the Board shall allow the
  person to have access to the registry within one month after receiving
  the application.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson of the Board may extend the time
  limit set out in subsection (2) where, having regard to all of the
  circumstances,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} access is requested to such a large number of
    decisions or necessitates a search in such a large number of
    decisions that the time set out in subsection (2) is unreasonable;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} consultations are necessary and require a longer
    period of time before the Board may adequately respond to the
    request.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a longer period of time is required by the
  Board pursuant to subsection (3), it shall inform the person who is
  requesting access of the reasons for the extension within one month
  after receiving the application.
