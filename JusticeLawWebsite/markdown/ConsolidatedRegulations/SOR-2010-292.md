## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767094 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this By-law.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Deposit Insurance
      Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a member
      institution, means a weekday that is not a holiday at the location
      of its head office. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Data Requirements]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Data Requirements]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2019-187, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[determination date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[determination date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a member
      institution, means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a winding-up order is made in respect of the
        institution before the day on which the Corporation makes a
        payment under section 14 of the Act, the day on which the
        petition or other originating process is filed in respect of the
        winding-up; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a winding-up order is not made in respect of
        the institution, the day on which any of the circumstances
        described in subsection 14(2.1) of the Act first occurred in
        respect of the institution. ([date-repère]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[determination time]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[determination time]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the deposit liabilities of a
        member institution that are located in Canada

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the determination date is a business day,
          the time by which all of the transactions made on that
          business day are processed and posted to the deposit records
          of the depositors of the member institution, and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the determination date is not a business
          day, the time by which all of the transactions made on the
          business day immediately preceding the determination date are
          processed and posted to the deposit records of the depositors
          of the member institution; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the deposit liabilities of a
        member institution that are located in a foreign branch of the
        member institution or posted in the records of that foreign
        branch

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the determination date is a business day,
          the time by which all of the transactions made on that
          business day are processed and posted to the deposit records
          of the depositors of the foreign branch of the member
          institution, and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the determination date is not a business
          day, the time by which all of the transactions made on the
          business day immediately preceding the determination date are
          processed and posted to the deposit records of the depositors
          of the foreign branch of the member
          institution. ([heure-repère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[foreign branch]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[foreign branch]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a branch of a member
      institution that is located outside Canada. ([succursale
      étrangère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[standardized data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[standardized data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2019-187, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to day or time

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this By-law,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a reference to a day, in respect of a member
    institution, is a reference to a period of 24 hours beginning
    immediately after midnight at the location of its head office; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a reference to a particular time, in respect of a
    member institution, is a reference to that time at the location of
    its head office.

## [Obligations of Member Institutions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767119 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capabilities

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of facilitating the Corporation's exercise of its functions
  under section 14 of the Act or in the event that an order is made
  under any of paragraphs 39.13(1)(a) to (c) of the Act, every member
  institution must be capable of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} producing the following data, as of the
    determination time, in relation to its deposit liabilities --- other
    than those posted in the records of a foreign branch of the member
    institution --- and providing that data or making it available to
    the Corporation in a usable format no later than the time referred
    to in subsection (2):

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} data that enables the Corporation to identify and
      contact each depositor and ascertain their preferred official
      language and their province of residence,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} data that enables the Corporation to identify
      and group those deposit liabilities by

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} unique depositor,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} eligibility to be insured by the Corporation,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} insurance category, and

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} account type, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the interest accrued and payable in relation to
      each deposit liability as of the determination date; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} temporarily preventing withdrawals of deposit
    liabilities or any portion of them according to account type within
    six hours after receiving instructions from the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit --- provision of data

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The member institution must be capable of providing
  or making available to the Corporation the data referred to in
  paragraph (1)(a) no later than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the data referred to in
    subparagraphs (1)(a)(i) and (ii),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the determination time occurs on or after the
      determination date, the earlier of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} six hours after the determination time, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} 4:00 p.m. on the day after the determination
        date, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the determination time occurs before the
      determination date, 4:00 p.m. on the day after the determination
      date; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the data referred to in subparagraph
    (1)(a)(iii),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the determination time occurs on or after the
      determination date, the earlier of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} 30 hours after the determination time, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} 4:00 p.m. on the second day after the
        determination date, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the determination time occurs before the
      determination date, 4:00 p.m. on the second day after the
      determination date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Policies and procedures

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The member institution must develop and implement
  policies and procedures to ensure that it has the capabilities
  referred to in subsections (1) and (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information and evidence

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every member institution
must, on request of the Corporation and within the period set out in the
request,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide to the Corporation a copy of the policies and
  procedures referred to in subsection 2(3);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} inform the Corporation of the time by which all of
  the transactions made on a given day are processed and posted to the
  deposit records of the depositors of the member institution;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide or make available to the Corporation the data
  referred to in paragraph 2(1)(a), including as of a time or date
  specified by the Corporation if there is no determination time or
  determination date;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} demonstrate that any data provided or made available
  to the Corporation is consistent with the records of the member
  institution and explain any variances; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide or make available to the Corporation any
  other evidence that the member institution has the capabilities
  referred to in subsections 2(1) and (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certification

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every member institution
must, within 30 days after the day on which a request to that effect is
sent by the Corporation, certify whether it has, in all material
respects, the capabilities referred to in subsections 2(1) and (2) and
whether it adheres to the policies and procedures referred to in
subsection 2(3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing of compliance

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), an institution must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide to the Corporation, within 30 days after
    the day on which it becomes a member institution, a plan to become
    compliant with this By-law; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} comply with this By-law as soon as feasible but
    within 18 months after the day on which it becomes a member
    institution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amalgamated member institutions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member institution that results from an
  amalgamation of two or more institutions must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the deposit liabilities of any
    amalgamating institution that became a member institution during the
    18 months before the amalgamation date,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} provide to the Corporation, within 30 days after
      that date, a plan to become compliant with this By-law, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} comply with this By-law as soon as feasible but
      within 18 months after the day on which the amalgamating
      institution became a member institution;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the deposit liabilities of any
    amalgamating institution that was not a member institution before
    the amalgamation date,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} provide to the Corporation, within 30 days after
      that date, a plan to become compliant with this By-law, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} comply with this By-law as soon as feasible but
      within 18 months after the amalgamation date; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of its other deposit liabilities, comply
    with this By-law without delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assumed deposit liabilities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A member institution that assumes deposit liabilities
  of another institution must, in respect of the assumed liabilities,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide to the Corporation, within 30 days after
    the day on which it assumes the deposit liabilities, a plan to
    become compliant with this By-law; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} comply with this By-law as soon as feasible but
    within 18 months after the day on which it assumes the deposit
    liabilities.
