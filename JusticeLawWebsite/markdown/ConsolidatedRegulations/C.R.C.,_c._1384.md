## Victoria Jubilee Bridge Traffic By-law {#victoria-jubilee-bridge-traffic-by-law .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1384
:::

[CANADA TRANSPORTATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10.4)

By-Law Regulating Vehicular Traffic upon the Victoria Jubilee Bridge,
Montreal, and the Approaches Thereto (C.N.R. By-Law Number 27)
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508221 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This By-law may be cited as
the [Victoria Jubilee Bridge Traffic
By-law](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1384).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508223 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this By-law,
[[company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian National Railway
Company.

## [Traffic Allowed]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508225 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the weight,
height and other restrictions hereinafter set out, the use of the bridge
roadways is limited to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} motor vehicles having not more than two axles, nor
  more than six tires;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} trailers or other vehicles in tow, having not more
  than two axles and not more than four tires; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} tractor-trailer combination units if the operator is
  in possession of a weight slip indicating gross vehicle weight not in
  excess 13 1/2 tons (27,000 lb.) of which not more than nine tons
  (18,000 lb.) may be on one axle.

## [Traffic Prohibited]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508230 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The use of a bridge roadway
by any motor vehicle is prohibited if such vehicle, together with
trailer or other vehicle in tow, if any,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not equipped with pneumatic tires;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is carrying dynamite or other high explosive or any
  substance on fire;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} has a gross weight (including load) in excess of 13
  1/2 tons (27,000 lb.); the operator of any vehicle shall supply a
  weight certificate if requested to do so by a bridge attendant;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} has a height (including load) in excess of 12 feet;
  and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} has a width (including load) in excess of 8 feet.

## [Speed Limit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508237 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No motor vehicle shall be
driven over a bridge roadway at a speed that is unsafe or unreasonable,
nor shall such speed in any case exceed 30 miles per hour.

## [Passage Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508239 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No motor vehicle shall
overtake or pass a moving autobus on a bridge roadway.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Autobuses or motor trucks
equipped with more than four tires shall not under any condition attempt
to pass an autobus or such a truck, whether in motion or stopped, on the
bridge roadways at any time.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Motor vehicles, other than
autobuses, having more than four tires are not permitted to use the
traffic lanes (St. Lambert to Montreal) between 7 a.m. and 9.30 a.m. nor
traffic lanes (Montreal to St. Lambert) between 4 p.m. and 6.30 p.m.

## [Stalled Vehicles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508243 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Any motor vehicle that
becomes stalled on the bridge will be immediately towed to a convenient
location beyond the exit of the bridge, and if such towing service can
be performed by a company vehicle, a fee of \$2 will be assessed, but if
towing service is performed by other than a company vehicle, the actual
expense as assessed by the party performing the towing service will be
paid by the owner of the vehicle.

## [Closing of Bridge]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508245 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The bridge roadways may
be closed to traffic by means of notices, gates, or otherwise, either
during a period of emergency or for work or maintenance to be done on
the roadways, for such period of time as the operating officers of the
company may deem necessary, and during such period, no vehicle shall
pass upon the roadways unless the operator holds a special permit issued
for such purpose by the company.

## [Penalty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508247 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every person who violates
this By-law shall be liable on summary conviction to a penalty not
exceeding \$40.
