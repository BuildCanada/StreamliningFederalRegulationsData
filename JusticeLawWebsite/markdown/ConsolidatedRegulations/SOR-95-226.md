## Crown Corporation General Regulations, 1995 {#crown-corporation-general-regulations-1995 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-226
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1995-05-02

Crown Corporation General Regulations, 1995

P.C. 1995-711  1995-05-02
::::

:::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsections
93(2), 99(4), 108(3), 114(4) and 127(4) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to repeal the Crown Corporation
Borrowing Regulations, made by Order in Council P.C. 1984-3093 of August
31, 1984[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-95-226_e_hq_6341){#fn_SOR-95-226_e_hq_6341-ID0EHCAA},
and the Crown Corporation General Regulations, made by Order in Council
P.C. 1984-3094 of August 31, 1984[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-95-226_e_hq_6342){#fn_SOR-95-226_e_hq_6342-ID0EDCAA},
and to make the annexed Regulations respecting restricted transactions,
disposal of property, remuneration, by-laws, deemed transactions to
borrow money and exemptions from a provision of the Financial
Administration Act in respect of borrowing transactions for Crown
corporations, in substitution therefor.

## [Notification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973003 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 93(1) of the Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed persons to be notified are the
  appropriate Minister for the parent Crown corporation and the
  President of the Treasury Board; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed manner in which those persons are to
  be notified is by the subsequent corporate plan or corporate plan
  amendment to be submitted by the parent Crown corporation.

## [Disposal of Property by Agent Corporations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973007 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 99(2) of the Act, an agent corporation that is a parent Crown
corporation or that is a wholly-owned subsidiary of a parent Crown
corporation may sell or otherwise dispose of personal property on the
condition that the property is sold or otherwise disposed of at fair
market value in the ordinary course of business in a transaction that is
consistent with the most recently approved corporate plan or amended
corporate plan of the parent Crown corporation.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 99(2) of the Act, an agent corporation that is a parent Crown
corporation named in Schedule III to the Act or that is a wholly-owned
subsidiary of such a parent Crown corporation may sell or otherwise
dispose of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} real property that has a fair market value of
  \$200,000 or less; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} real property that has a fair market value of more
  than \$200,000, on the condition that the property is sold or
  otherwise disposed of at not less than fair market value in the
  ordinary course of business in a transaction that is consistent with
  the most recently approved corporate plan or amended corporate plan of
  the parent Crown corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-103, s. 1
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 99(2) of the Act, an agent corporation that is a parent Crown
corporation or that is a wholly-owned subsidiary of a parent Crown
corporation may retain and use all or any part of the proceeds of any
disposition of property in a manner that is consistent with the most
recently approved corporate plan or amended corporate plan of the parent
Crown corporation.

## [Remuneration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973015 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
108 of the Act, [[remuneration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a salary, a
fee, an allowance or any other form of monetary compensation, except
monetary compensation payable under the provisions of a registered
pension plan as defined in the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3),
whether payable on or during the course of the appointment or on or by
reason of termination of the appointment.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-103, s. 2
:::

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the aggregate value of any benefits fixed by a
  board of directors of a parent Crown Corporation in respect of a
  director, chairman or chief executive officer for services in respect
  of that office and, in the case of a chairman or chief executive
  officer, any other office of the corporation or an affiliate thereof,
  pursuant to subsection 108(2) of the Act shall not exceed the
  aggregate value of the standard benefits usually provided by other
  corporations in the public and private sectors

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that carry on businesses or activities similar to
    the businesses or activities of the parent Crown corporation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where there are no corporations carrying on
    businesses or activities similar to those of the parent Crown
    corporation, that provide such benefits to persons having
    responsibilities similar to the responsibilities of the director,
    chairman or chief executive officer of the parent Crown corporation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No benefit that is a low interest loan or interest
  free loan shall be provided to a director, chairman or chief executive
  officer of a parent Crown corporation for services in respect of that
  office and, in the case of a chairman or chief executive officer, any
  other office of the corporation or an affiliate thereof, unless the
  loan is provided for purposes of aiding in meeting costs due to
  relocation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[low interest loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    loan or mortgage at a rate of interest below that commercially
    available for a loan or mortgage for similar purposes; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [[costs due to relocation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means costs attributable to relocation where the relocation is
    solely for the purposes of the parent Crown corporation.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a board of directors of a parent Crown
  corporation fixes any benefits or modifies them pursuant to subsection
  108(2) of the Act, the board of directors shall report to the
  appropriate Minister and to the Clerk of the Privy Council

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of the resolution or other
    instrument so fixing or modifying the benefits, within 15 working
    days after fixing the benefits; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any appointment as chief executive
    officer, by sending a statement detailing the full benefits accorded
    to the chief executive officer, including the registered pension
    plan provisions, no later than six months after the effective date
    of the appointment.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/99-103, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-103, s. 3
:::

## [By-laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973033 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A copy of a by-law of a
parent Crown Corporation is to be sent to the appropriate Minister and
the President of the Treasury Board within 15 workings days after the
adoption of the resolution by the board of directors of the parent Crown
corporation approving the making, amending or repealing of the by-law.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-103, s. 4
:::

## [Transactions to Borrow Money]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973037 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The following classes of
transactions are deemed, for the purposes of Part X of the Act, to be
transactions to borrow money:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the issuance and sale of a term preferred share of a
  Crown corporation to a person, partnership or association;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a lease, as described in The Handbook of the
  Chartered Professional Accountants of Canada, as amended from time to
  time, to a Crown corporation by a person, partnership or association,
  if the lease exceeds the lesser of 5% of the total assets of the Crown
  corporation and \$10,000,000;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the entering into a supplier credit arrangement that
  matures after a period of more than 12 consecutive months by a Crown
  corporation with a person, partnership or association, where the
  person, partnership or association is extending credit to the Crown
  corporation and the supplier credit arrangement exceeds the lesser of
  1 per cent of the total assets of the Crown corporation and \$100,000;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a guarantee by a Crown corporation, other than the
  Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation, in respect of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a note, bond or debenture,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} term preferred shares,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a loan,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a banker's acceptance,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a capital lease, or

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a supplier credit arrangement.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-220, s. 1
:::

## [Borrowing Approvals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973051 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An approval under
subsection 127(3) of the Act shall be granted by an instrument in
writing addressed to the Crown corporation proposing to enter into the
transaction to borrow money.

## [Borrowing Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973053 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  parent Crown corporation is exempt from the application of subsection
  127(3) of the Act in respect of the borrowing of money where the
  lender is a wholly-owned subsidiary of the parent Crown corporation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A wholly-owned subsidiary of a parent Crown
  corporation is exempt from the application of subsection 127(3) of the
  Act in respect of the borrowing of money where the lender is the
  parent Crown corporation or another wholly-owned subsidiary of the
  parent Crown corporation.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The following Crown
corporations are exempt from the application of subsection 127(3) of the
Act in respect of any borrowing of money of the class set out in
paragraph 10(d):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Export Development Canada;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Farm Credit Canada; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Federal Business Development Bank.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 22, s. 22, c. 33, s. 30
:::

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[local port corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
  corporation established pursuant to section 25 of the Canada Ports
  Corporation Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Canada Ports Corporation is exempt from the
  application of subsection 127(3) of the Act in respect of the
  borrowing of money from a local port corporation pursuant to
  subsection 57(2) of the Canada Ports Corporation Act.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A local port corporation is exempt from the
  application of subsection 127(3) of the Act in respect of the
  borrowing of money from the Canada Ports Corporation pursuant to
  section 27 of Schedule I to the Canada Ports Corporation Act.
