## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-793757 .Part}

Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Species at Risk
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3).

## [Permit Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-793760 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person applying for a permit under section 73 of the Act in relation
  to an activity affecting a listed wildlife species, any part of its
  critical habitat or the residences of its individuals must submit an
  application to the competent minister in a form and manner that is
  satisfactory to that minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content --- activity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must include information
  demonstrating that any of paragraphs 73(2)(a) to (c) of the Act
  applies to the activity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content --- additional information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The application must also include information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} demonstrating that all reasonable alternatives to
    the activity that would reduce the impact on the species have been
    considered and the best solution has been adopted;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} demonstrating that all feasible measures will be
    taken to minimize the impact of the activity on the species, its
    critical habitat or the residences of its individuals; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} describing any changes that the activity may cause
    to the listed wildlife species, its critical habitat or the
    residences of its individuals, the possible effects of those changes
    and the significance of those effects.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of receipt

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The competent minister must notify the applicant in
  writing once the application has been received.

## [Issuance of Permit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-793773 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}90-day time limit

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), the competent minister must either issue a
  permit or notify the applicant of the refusal to issue a permit within
  90 days after the date of the notice indicating that the application
  has been received.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application incomplete

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The time limit set out in subsection (1) is suspended
  if the application is incomplete. The suspension begins on the day on
  which the competent minister notifies the applicant in writing that
  the information provided is insufficient to allow the competent
  minister to issue or refuse to issue a permit and ends on the day on
  which the competent minister receives all of the missing information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of time limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The time limit set out in subsection (1) is not
  applicable in the following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} additional consultations are necessary, including
    consultations held under subsections 73(4) and (5) of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an Act of Parliament other than the Act or a land
    claims agreement requires that a decision be made before the
    competent minister issues or refuses to issue a permit under
    section 73 of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the terms and conditions of a permit previously
    issued to the applicant under section 73 of the Act have not been
    met;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the applicant requests or agrees that the time
    limit is not to apply; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the activity described in the permit application is
    modified before the competent minister issues or refuses to issue a
    permit under section 73 of the Act.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-793786 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
