## Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs Authority to Prescribe Fees Order {#minister-of-consumer-and-corporate-affairs-authority-to-prescribe-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/88-46
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1988-03-30

Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs Authority to Prescribe Fees
Order

P.C. 1988-495 1988-03-17
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1987-1092 of May 28,
  1987[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EBABA};
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to authorize the Minister of Consumer and Corporate
  Affairs to prescribe, by order, the fee to be paid by persons for
  services in respect of registration under the [Industrial Design
  Act](/eng/acts/I-9), notwithstanding section 26 of that Act.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e-ID0EBABA){#footnote1star_e}SI/87-108,
    1987 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2211
    :::
