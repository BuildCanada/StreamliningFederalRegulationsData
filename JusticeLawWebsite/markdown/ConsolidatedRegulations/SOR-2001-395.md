## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-674463 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cooperative Credit
    Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a joint venture;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an insurance company;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a securities dealer; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary of an insurance company or
      securities dealer. ([entité désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an insurance company
    incorporated by or under the laws of Canada or a province. ([société
    d'assurances]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[joint venture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[joint venture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a real property entity

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that was formed by an association, or by a
      designated entity controlled by an association, and one or more
      other persons for the purpose of a specific business undertaking;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in which the association or designated entity has
      a substantial investment; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which the persons who formed it
      have agreed on joint control, regardless of the distribution of
      their equity. ([coentreprise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[real property entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[real property entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity that is
    primarily engaged in holding, managing or otherwise dealing with

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} real property;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shares of a body corporate that is primarily
      engaged in holding, managing or otherwise dealing with real
      property, including shares of a body corporate that is another
      real property entity; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ownership interests in an unincorporated entity
      that is primarily engaged in holding, managing or otherwise
      dealing with real property, including ownership interests in an
      unincorporated entity that is another real property
      entity. ([entité immobilière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[related real property entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[related real property entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of
    an association, means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity, other than a designated
      entity controlled by the association, in which the association or
      a designated entity controlled by the association beneficially
      owns sufficient shares or ownership interests to cause the
      association or designated entity to have a substantial investment
      in the real property entity; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity that is controlled by a
      real property entity described in paragraph (a). ([entité
      immobilière apparentée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[securities dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[securities dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity described
    in paragraph (g) of the definition [[financial
    institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
    Act. ([négociant en valeurs mobilières]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[third party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[third party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an association,
    means a person other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the association;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a designated entity controlled by the
      association; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the
      association. ([tierce partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Book value of interest in real property

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 8 to 11, the book value of an asset that is an interest of an
association in real property, at a particular time, is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the asset is real property, the gross book value
  of the real property, less any accumulated depreciation on the real
  property, that would be reported on a balance sheet of the association
  prepared as at that time in accordance with the accounting principles
  and specifications of the Superintendent referred to in
  subsection 292(4) of the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the asset is a security or loan, the book value of
  the security or loan that would be reported on a balance sheet of the
  association prepared as at that time in accordance with the accounting
  principles and specifications of the Superintendent referred to in
  subsection 292(4) of the Act.

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-674493 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exempt associations

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Sections 401 and 402 of the
Act do not apply to an association if the sum of the equity of the
association and the minority interests in entities controlled by the
association, as they appear in the consolidated financial statements of
the association, is five billion dollars or more.

## [Prescribed Percentage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-674506 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this section.

  [[real property percentage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[real property percentage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of an association
      means the amount determined by the formula

      ::: {#674513 .Definition}
      (A/B) × 100%

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is the total value of all interests of the association in real
          property, other than interests that, because of section 397 of
          the Act, are not to be included in computing the value of
          loans, investments and interests of the association, and of
          its subsidiaries prescribed by section 4, under sections 398
          to 402 of the Act; and

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the regulatory capital of the association. ([pourcentage de
          biens immeubles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})
      :::

  [[equity percentage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[equity percentage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of an association means
      the amount determined by the formula

      ::: {#674518 .Definition}
      ((A + B)/C) × 100%

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is the total value of all participating shares that are
          beneficially owned by the association and its subsidiaries
          prescribed by section 4, other than
          - [(a)]{.lawlabel} participating shares of permitted entities
            in which the association has a substantial investment, and

          - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shares that, because of section 397 of the
            Act, are not to be included in computing the value of loans,
            investments and interests of the association, and of its
            subsidiaries prescribed by section 4, under sections 398 to
            402 of the Act;

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the total value of all ownership interests in
          unincorporated entities that are beneficially owned by the
          association and its subsidiaries prescribed by section 4,
          other than
          - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ownership interests in permitted entities
            in which the association has a substantial investment, and

          - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ownership interests that, because of
            section 397 of the Act, are not to be included in computing
            the value of loans, investments and interests of the
            association, and of its subsidiaries prescribed by
            section 4, under sections 398 to 402 of the Act; and

      [C]{.dfn}
      :   is the regulatory capital of the association. ([pourcentage de
          capitaux propres]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})
      :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed percentage --- real property

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of section 401 of the Act, the
  prescribed percentage of the regulatory capital of an association is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 35%, if the association is primarily engaged in the
    business of managing liquidity or providing treasury, clearing,
    settlement or payment services;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 70%, if the association is not an association
    referred to in paragraph (a) and its equity percentage does not
    exceed 30%; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 100% minus its equity percentage, if the
    association is not an association referred to in paragraph (a) and
    its equity percentage exceeds 30%.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed percentage --- equity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of section 402 of the Act, the
  prescribed percentage of the regulatory capital of an association is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 35%, if the association is primarily engaged in the
    business of managing liquidity or providing treasury, clearing,
    settlement or payment services;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 70%, if the association is not an association
    referred to in paragraph (a) and its real property percentage does
    not exceed 30%; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 100% minus its real property percentage, if the
    association is not an association referred to in paragraph (a) and
    its real property percentage exceeds 30%.

## [Valuation of Equity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-674539 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value equals book value

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
section 402 of the Act, the value of the participating shares and
ownership interests referred to in that section that are beneficially
owned by an association and its subsidiaries prescribed by section 4 is
equal to their book value as reported on the consolidated balance sheet
of the association.

## [Interests of an Association in Real Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-674542 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-674543 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of interest in real property

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of Part X of the Act, other than subsection 397(3) of the
  Act, sections 8 to 12 define the interests of an association in real
  property and set out how those interests are to be valued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection 397(3) of the Act, the
  interests of an association in real property means real property
  referred to in paragraph 11(1)(a) and shares and ownership interests
  referred to in paragraph 11(1)(b) that become beneficially owned by
  the association, or by a designated entity controlled by the
  association, as a result of the realization of a security interest on
  a loan or debt obligation referred to in subsection 11(1).

### [Direct Interests --- Real Property and Debt Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-674549 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direct interests

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following are interests of an association in real property:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} real property beneficially owned by the association
    or by a designated entity controlled by the association; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a debt obligation, for which the association or a
    designated entity controlled by the association is liable, that was
    issued for the purpose of acquiring or improving real property
    referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of direct interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of an association in real
  property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of real property referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a), the book value of the real property; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a debt obligation referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b), any amount by which the book value of the debt
    obligation exceeds the book value of the real property referred to
    in that paragraph.

### [Indirect Interests --- Real Property, Shares and Ownership Interests]{.HTitleText2} {#h-674559 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain indirect interests

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following are interests of an association in real property:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at a particular time, real property

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that is beneficially owned by

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the
        association that is a joint venture, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} an entity in which an entity described in
        clause (A) has a substantial investment, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that would be reported on a balance sheet of the
      association prepared as at that time in accordance with the
      accounting principles and specifications of the Superintendent
      referred to in subsection 292(4) of the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shares and ownership interests beneficially owned
    by the association, or by a designated entity controlled by the
    association, in any related real property entity of the association
    other than a related real property entity referred to in
    paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of those interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of an association in real
  property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of real property referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a), the book value of the real property; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of shares and ownership interests
    referred to in paragraph (1)(b), the book value of the shares and
    ownership interests.

### [Indirect Interests --- Loans and Debt Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-674573 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain indirect interests

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following are interests of an association in real property:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a debt obligation issued by a related real property
    entity of the association and beneficially owned by the association
    or by a designated entity controlled by the association;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a loan made by the association, or by a designated
    entity controlled by the association, to a related real property
    entity of the association;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a loan made by the association, or by a designated
    entity controlled by the association, to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity in which a securities
      dealer or insurance company controlled by the association has a
      substantial investment, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity that is controlled by a
      real property entity described in subparagraph (i);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a debt obligation issued by a real property entity
    described in subparagraph (c)(i) or (ii) and beneficially owned by
    the association or by a designated entity controlled by the
    association;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a debt obligation that is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} issued by a related real property entity of the
      association,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} beneficially owned by a third party, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} guaranteed by the association or by a
      designated entity controlled by the association; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a loan made by a third party to a related real
    property entity of the association and guaranteed by the association
    or by a designated entity controlled by the association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of those interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of an association in real
  property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a debt obligation referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a) or (d), the book value of the debt obligation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a loan referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b) or (c), the book value of the loan; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a guaranteed debt obligation referred
    to in paragraph (1)(e) or a guaranteed loan referred to in
    paragraph (1)(f),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligation is issued by, or the loan
      is made to, an entity that is a related real property entity of
      the association and the entity beneficially owns real property
      that is an interest of the association in real property under
      paragraph 9(1)(a), any amount by which the face value of the
      guarantee exceeds the value of the real property determined in
      accordance with paragraph 9(2)(a), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the face value of the
      guarantee.

### [Indirect Interests --- Secured Loans and Debt Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-674595 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain indirect interests

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  association or a designated entity controlled by an association makes
  a loan to, or beneficially owns or guarantees the debt obligation of,
  a third party, the loan or debt obligation is an interest of the
  association in real property if it is secured by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} real property beneficially owned by a third party
    in conjunction with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the association,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the designated entity,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the
      association,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a securities dealer or insurance company
      controlled by the association,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} an entity controlled by a securities dealer or
      insurance company referred to in subparagraph (iv), or

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity described in
      subparagraph 10(1)(c)(i) or (ii); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shares or ownership interests beneficially owned by
    a third party in

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an entity that beneficially owns real property in
      conjunction with the association, a related real property entity
      of the association or a designated entity controlled by the
      association, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the
      association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of those interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of an association in real
  property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a loan or debt obligation that is
    secured by real property referred to in paragraph (1)(a), the lesser
    of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the net realizable value of the third party's
      interest in the real property at the time that the security
      interest was given, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

      ::: {#674614 .Subparagraph}
      A - B

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is
          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the loan was made by the association or
            a designated entity controlled by the association or the
            debt obligation is beneficially owned by the association or
            a designated entity controlled by the association, the book
            value of the loan or debt obligation, or

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligation is guaranteed by the
            association or a designated entity controlled by the
            association, the face value of the guarantee, and

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the total net realizable value of any other security
          interests that were given for the loan or debt obligation;
      :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a loan or debt obligation that is
    secured by shares or ownership interests beneficially owned by a
    third party in an entity referred to in subparagraph (1)(b)(i), the
    lesser of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the net realizable value of the third party's
      interest in those shares or ownership interests at the time that
      the security interest was given, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

      ::: {#674623 .Subparagraph}
      A - (B - (C × D/E))

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is
          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the loan was made by the association or
            a designated entity controlled by the association or the
            debt obligation is beneficially owned by the association or
            a designated entity controlled by the association, the book
            value of the loan or debt obligation, or

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligation is guaranteed by the
            association or a designated entity controlled by the
            association, the face value of the guarantee,

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the total net realizable value of all security interests
          that were given for the loan or debt obligation,

      [C]{.dfn}
      :   is the net realizable value of the entity's interest in the
          real property referred to in subparagraph (1)(b)(i),

      [D]{.dfn}
      :   is the value of the shares or ownership interests that are
          given as the security interest, and

      [E]{.dfn}
      :   is the total value of the outstanding shares or ownership
          interests in the entity; and
      :::

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a loan or debt obligation that is
    secured by shares or ownership interests beneficially owned by a
    third party in a related real property entity referred to in
    subparagraph (1)(b)(ii), the lesser of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the net realizable value of the third party's
      interest in those shares or ownership interests at the time that
      the security interest was given, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

      ::: {#674635 .Subparagraph}
      A - B

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is
          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the loan was made by the association or
            a designated entity controlled by the association or the
            debt obligation is beneficially owned by the association or
            a designated entity controlled by the association, the book
            value of the loan or debt obligation, or

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligation is guaranteed by the
            association or a designated entity controlled by the
            association, the face value of the guarantee, and

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the total net realizable value of any other security
          interests that were given for the loan or debt obligation.
      :::

### [Other Interests]{.HTitleText2} {#h-674641 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other interests

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following are interests of an association in real property:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a guarantee given by the association, or by a
    designated entity controlled by the association, to an entity other
    than the association or designated entity for the purpose of
    completing the development of real property that is beneficially
    owned by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the association,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a designated entity controlled by the
      association, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the
      association; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an agreement made by the association, or by a
    designated entity controlled by the association, to support a third
    party's cost of operating or financing real property that is
    beneficially owned by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the association,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a designated entity controlled by the
      association, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the
      association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of those interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of an association in real
  property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a guarantee referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a), the estimated cost of completing the development
    of the real property; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an agreement referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b), the amount of any funds advanced by the
    association, or by a designated entity controlled by the
    association, under the agreement.

## [Repeals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-674657 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repeal
