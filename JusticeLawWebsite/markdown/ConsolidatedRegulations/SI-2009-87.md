#### [ARTICLE 3]{.HLabel2}[Personal Scope]{.HTitleText2} {#article-3personal-scope .SchedHeadL2}

This Agreement shall apply to any person who is or who has been subject
to the legislation of one or both Parties, as well as to other persons
who derive rights from such a person.

#### [ARTICLE 4]{.HLabel2}[Equality of Treatment]{.HTitleText2} {#article-4equality-of-treatment .SchedHeadL2}

Any person described in Article 3 shall be subject to the obligations of
the legislation of the other Party and shall be eligible for the
benefits of that legislation and under the same conditions as citizens
of the latter Party.

#### [ARTICLE 5]{.HLabel2}[Export of Benefits]{.HTitleText2} {#article-5export-of-benefits .SchedHeadL2}

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel}

  the legislation of a Party to any person described in Article 3,
  including benefits acquired by virtue of this Agreement, shall not be
  subject to any reduction, modification, suspension or cancellation by
  reason only of the fact that the person resides in the territory of
  the other Party.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  With respect to the Republic of Poland, paragraph 1 shall not apply to

  :::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel

  ::: listItemText1
  Benefits payable under this Agreement to a person who is or who has

  derives rights from such a person, shall be paid when that person, or
  a person who derives rights from such a person, resides in the
  territory of a third State, under the same conditions as its own
  citizens residing in the territory of a third State.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Provisions Determining the Legislation Applicable]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiprovisions-determining-the-legislation-applicable .SchedHeadL1}

#### [ARTICLE 6]{.HLabel2}[General Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#article-6general-rules .SchedHeadL2}

Subject to Articles 7 to 9:

- ::::: listItemBlock1
  ::: listItemLabel
  [(a)]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText2
  An employed person who works in the territory of a Party shall, in
  respect of that work, be subject only to the legislation of that
  Party;
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock1
  ::: listItemLabel
  [(b)]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText2
  A self-employed person who resides in the territory of a Party and who
  works for his or her own account in the territory of the other Party
  or in the territories of both Parties shall, in respect of that work,
  be subject only to the legislation of the first Party.
  :::
  :::::

#### [ARTICLE 7]{.HLabel2}[Detachments]{.HTitleText2} {#article-7detachments .SchedHeadL2}

An employed person who is subject to the legislation of a Party and who
works in the territory of the other Party for the same or related
employer shall, in respect of that work, be subject only to the
legislation of the first Party as though that work was performed in its
territory. This period of detachment may not be maintained for more than
60 months without the consent of the competent authorities of both
Parties.

categories of persons.

##### [ARTICLE 12]{.HLabel3}[Periods Under the Legislation of a Third State]{.HTitleText3} {#article-12periods-under-the-legislation-of-a-third-state .SchedHeadL3}

If a person is not eligible for a benefit on the basis of the creditable
periods under the legislation of the Parties, totalized as provided in
Article 11, the eligibility of that person for that benefit shall be
determined by totalizing these periods and creditable periods completed
under the legislation of a third State with which both Parties are bound
by social security instruments which provide for the totalizing of
periods.
