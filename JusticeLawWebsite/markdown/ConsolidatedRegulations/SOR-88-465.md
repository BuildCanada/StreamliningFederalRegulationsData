## Manitoba Chicken Marketing (Interprovincial and Export) Order {#manitoba-chicken-marketing-interprovincial-and-export-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-465
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1988-08-30

Order Respecting the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade of
Chicken Produced in Manitoba
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Manitoba Chicken Broiler Producers' Marketing Board, pursuant to
section 3[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFBBA} of
the [Manitoba Chicken Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._152), C.R.C.,
c. 152, hereby makes the annexed [Order respecting the marketing in
interprovincial and export trade of chicken produced in
Manitoba](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-465).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EFBBA){#fn_1e}SOR/82-668, 1982 [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2550
  :::

Dated at Winnipeg, Manitoba this 23rd day of August, 1988
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913526 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Manitoba Chicken Marketing (Interprovincial and Export)
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-465).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913528 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[chicken]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chicken]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a chicken of any variety,
    grade or class that is not more than five months of age and that is
    raised or kept within the Province of Manitoba for a purpose other
    than egg production, and includes broiler, fryer and roasting
    chickens; ([poulet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means selling or offering for
    sale, processing, buying, purchasing or offering to buy or purchase
    and includes advertising, packing, storing, shipping and
    transporting but does not include packing and storing by a producer
    on the producer's premises; ([vente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who raises, keeps,
    prepares for slaughter, or slaughters chicken within the Province of
    Manitoba and includes the employer of any such person, a person who
    under any lease or agreement is entitled to a share of the chicken
    raised or kept by any such person, and a person who takes possession
    of any chicken from any such person under any form of security for a
    debt. ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913533 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies in
respect of the marketing of chicken in interprovincial and export trade
and, in relation to such marketing, to persons and property situated in
the Province of Manitoba.

## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913535 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No producer shall market
chicken in interprovincial or export trade, unless exempted from this
section by order of the Manitoba Chicken Broiler Producers' Marketing
Board.
