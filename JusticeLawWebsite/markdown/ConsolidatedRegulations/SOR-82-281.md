## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873363 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Broadcast Technical Data Services Fees
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-281).

## [Payment of Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873365 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Every person who applies to
the Minister of Communications for the provision of a service set out in
Column I of an item of the schedule shall pay to the Minister prior to
the provision of that service,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where that service involves an initial subscription,
  the fee set out in Column II of that item;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where that service involves a renewal or update, the
  fee set out in Column III of that item; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where that service involves a special request, the
  fee set out in Column IV of that item.
