## Emergency Economic Measures Order {#emergency-economic-measures-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2022-22
:::

[EMERGENCIES ACT](/eng/acts/E-4.5)

Registration 2022-02-15

Emergency Economic Measures Order

P.C. 2022-108 2022-02-15
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council has, by a proclamation made pursuant to
subsection 17(1) of the [Emergencies Act](/eng/acts/E-4.5), declared
that a public order emergency exists;

And whereas the Governor in Council has reasonable grounds to believe
that the measures with respect to property referred to in this Order are
necessary;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
Preparedness, pursuant to subsection 19(1) of the [Emergencies
Act](/eng/acts/E-4.5), makes the annexed [Emergency Economic Measures
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-22).
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply to this Order:

[[designated person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any individual or
    entity that is engaged, directly or indirectly, in an activity
    prohibited by sections 2 to 5 of the [Emergency Measures
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-21). ([personne
    désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a corporation, trust,
    partnership, fund, unincorporated association or organization or
    foreign state. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to cease dealings

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  entity set out in section 3 must, upon the coming into force of this
  Order, cease

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dealing in any property, wherever situated, that is
    owned, held or controlled, directly or indirectly, by a designated
    person or by a person acting on behalf of or at the direction of
    that designated person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} facilitating any transaction related to a dealing
    referred to in paragraph (a);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} making available any property, including funds or
    virtual currency, to or for the benefit of a designated person or to
    a person acting on behalf of or at the direction of a designated
    person; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} providing any financial or related services to or
    for the benefit of any designated person or acquire any such
    services from or for the benefit of any such person or entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insurance policy

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph 2(1)(d) does not apply in respect of any
  insurance policy which was valid prior to the coming in force of this
  Order other than an insurance policy for any vehicle being used in a
  public assembly referred to in subsection 2(1) of the [Emergency
  Measures Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-21).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to determine

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following entities must
determine on a continuing basis whether they are in possession or
control of property that is owned, held or controlled by or on behalf of
a designated person:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[authorized foreign banks]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
  as defined in section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), in
  respect of their business in Canada, and banks regulated by that Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cooperative credit societies, savings and credit
  unions and caisses populaires regulated by a provincial Act and
  associations regulated by the [Cooperative Credit Associations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[foreign companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
  defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), in respect of their insurance business in
  Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[provincial
  companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
  [[societies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms are defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fraternal benefit societies regulated by a provincial
  Act in respect of their insurance activities and insurance companies
  and other entities regulated by a provincial Act that are engaged in
  the business of insuring risks;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} companies regulated by the [Trust and Loan Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} trust companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} loan companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} entities that engage in any activity described in
  paragraphs 5(h) and (h.1) of the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering)
  and Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501);

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} entities authorized under provincial legislation to
  engage in the business of dealing in securities or to provide
  portfolio management or investment counselling services;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} entities that provide a platform to raise funds or
  virtual currency through donations; and

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} entities that perform any of the following payment
  functions:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the provision or maintenance of an account that, in
    relation to an electronic funds transfer, is held on behalf of one
    or more end users,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the holding of funds on behalf of an end user
    until they are withdrawn by the end user or transferred to another
    individual or entity,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the initiation of an electronic funds transfer at
    the request of an end user,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the authorization of an electronic funds transfer
    or the transmission, reception or facilitation of an instruction in
    relation to an electronic funds transfer, or

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the provision of clearing or settlement services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration requirement --- FINTRAC

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  entities referred to in paragraphs 3(k) and (l) must register with the
  Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada
  established by section 41 of the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering)
  and Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501) if they are in
  possession or control of property that is owned, held or controlled by
  or on behalf of a designated person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reporting obligation --- suspicious
  transactions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Those entities must also report to the Centre every
  financial transaction that occurs or that is attempted in the course
  of their activities and in respect of which there are reasonable
  grounds to suspect that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the transaction is related to the commission or the
    attempted commission of a money laundering offence by a designated
    person; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the transaction is related to the commission or the
    attempted commission of a terrorist activity financing offence by a
    designated person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reporting obligation --- other
  transactions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Those entities must also report to the Centre the
  transactions and information set out in subsections 30(1) and 33(1) of
  the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-184).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to disclose --- RCMP or CSIS

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every entity set out in
section 3 must disclose without delay to the Commissioner of the Royal
Canadian Mounted Police or to the Director of the Canadian Security
Intelligence Service

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the existence of property in their possession or
  control that they have reason to believe is owned, held or controlled
  by or on behalf of a designated person; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any information about a transaction or proposed
  transaction in respect of property referred to in paragraph (a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of information

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A Government of Canada,
provincial or territorial institution may disclose information to any
entity set out in section 3, if the disclosing institution is satisfied
that the disclosure will contribute to the application of this Order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No proceedings under the
[Emergencies Act](/eng/acts/E-4.5) and no civil proceedings lie against
an entity for complying with this Order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
