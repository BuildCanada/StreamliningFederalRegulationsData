## Statutes of Canada Distribution Direction, No. 2 {#statutes-of-canada-distribution-direction-no.-2 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/89-140
:::

[PUBLICATION OF STATUTES ACT](/eng/acts/S-21)

Registration 1989-05-24

Direction Respecting the Distribution of the Acts of the Parliament of
Canada

P.C. 1989-851 1989-05-11
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Supply and Services, pursuant to subsection 10(2) of the
[Publication of Statutes Act](/eng/acts/S-21), is pleased hereby to make
the annexed [Direction respecting the distribution of the Acts of the
Parliament of Canada](/eng/regulations/SI-89-140).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642095 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Direction may be cited
as the [Statutes of Canada Distribution Direction,
No. 2](/eng/regulations/SI-89-140).

## [Distribution]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642097 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Four copies of the Acts of
the Parliament of Canada shall be delivered or sent without charge to
the chairperson or to one of the joint chairpersons of the Committee of
the House of Commons, of the Senate or of both Houses of Parliament
established for the purpose of reviewing and scrutinizing statutory
instruments.
