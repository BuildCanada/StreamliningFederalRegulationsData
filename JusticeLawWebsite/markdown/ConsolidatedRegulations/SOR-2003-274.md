## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703255 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order, the
expressions [[CFC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
[[chicken]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[federal market development
quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[federal quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
[[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
[[Provincial Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[provincial market
development quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[provincial
quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[registered production
facilities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have the same meaning as in section 1
of the [Canadian Chicken Marketing Quota
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-36).

## [Authority to Allot and Administer Federal Quotas and Federal Market Development Quotas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703257 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), CFC grants authority to each Provincial Commodity
  Board to perform on its behalf the function of allotting and
  administering, in accordance with the [Canadian Chicken Marketing
  Quota Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-36), federal quotas and
  federal market development quotas in the province and, for the purpose
  of performing that function, to exercise on behalf of CFC the powers
  that it would be entitled to exercise in the performance of that
  function.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The authority granted to each Provincial Commodity
  Board under subsection (1) does not include the authority to allot

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quotas referred to in subsection (1) to
    producers who are not in good standing with CFC; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} federal market development quotas to producers to
    authorize the marketing of chicken to primary processors who do not
    hold a valid market development licence that has been issued by CFC
    under the [Canadian Chicken Licensing
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-22) and is in effect.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In performing the function
referred to in section 2, a Provincial Commodity Board shall also apply
the orders, regulations and rules of the province, if any, in respect of
the allotment and administration of provincial quotas and provincial
market development quotas, to the extent that they are not inconsistent
with the [Canadian Chicken Marketing Quota
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-36), in relation to the following
matters:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of federal quotas, quota banks; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of federal quotas and federal market
  development quotas,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the entitlement to a quota,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the basis on which the amount of a quota is
    determined,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an increase in or a reduction of a quota,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the allotment of quotas to producers,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the period during which a quota is valid,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the maximum and minimum quota sizes,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the determination of who is a producer and who
    are the producer's partners, affiliates, associates and
    subsidiaries,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the cancellation, suspension or variation of
    quotas for breach of the orders, regulations and rules in relation
    to quotas or for non-payment of levies imposed by the Provincial
    Commodity Board or CFC,

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the reduction or loss of a quota for failure to
    utilize the quota,

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} the ownership, leasing and transfer of quotas,

  - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} the determination of what constitutes registered
    production facilities and the application of the determination to
    and its effect on quotas,

  - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} the utilization of quotas,

  - [(xiii)]{.lawlabel} the marketing arrangements with processors, and

  - [(xiv)]{.lawlabel} the information, documents and reports to be
    submitted by producers.

## [Authority to Receive Information from Market Development Licensees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703280 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} CFC
  grants authority to each Provincial Commodity Board to receive on its
  behalf from market development licensees the information, documents
  and reports required by subsection 5(3) of the [Canadian Chicken
  Licensing Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-22).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Provincial Commodity Board must forward all
  information, documents and reports referred to in subsection (1) to
  CFC.

## [Revocation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703284 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Canadian Chicken
Marketing Agency Quota Grant of Administrative Authority, approved by
Order in Council P.C. 1991-1090 of June 13, 1991[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_SOR-2003-274_e_hq_5816){#fn_SOR-2003-274_e_hq_5816-ID0EBBA},
is revoked.
