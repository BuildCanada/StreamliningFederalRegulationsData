## Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff Rules of Origin Regulations {#most-favoured-nation-tariff-rules-of-origin-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-33
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1997-12-29

Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff Rules of Origin Regulations

P.C. 1997-2004 1997-12-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 16(2) of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote

## [Origin of Goods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022490 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Goods originate in a
country that is a beneficiary of the Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} not less than 50 per cent of the cost of production
  of the goods is incurred by the industry of one or more countries that
  are beneficiaries of the Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff, or by the
  industry of Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods were finished in a country that is a
  beneficiary of the Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff in the form in which
  they are imported into Canada.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In calculating the cost of
production for the purposes of paragraph 1(a), the following shall not
be included:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cost of outside packing and expenses related to
  outside packing, required for the transportation of the goods, not
  including packing in which the goods are ordinarily sold for
  consumption;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} gross profit of the manufacturer or exporter and the
  profit or remuneration of any trader, broker or other person dealing
  in the goods in their finished manufactured condition;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} royalties;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} customs or excise duty or tax paid or payable on
  imported materials;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} carriage, insurance and other charges from the place
  of production or manufacture in the country of origin to the port of
  shipment; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other costs or charges incurred or to be incurred
  subsequent to the completion of the manufacture of the goods.

## [Direct Shipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022501 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Goods are entitled to the
Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff only if the goods are shipped directly to
Canada, with or without transhipment, from a country that is a
beneficiary of the Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022503 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** \[Repeal\]

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022505 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on January 1, 1998.
