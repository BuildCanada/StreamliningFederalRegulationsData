## Cooperative Vereniging International Post Corporation U.A. Acquisition of Shares Order {#cooperative-vereniging-international-post-corporation-u.a.-acquisition-of-shares-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-31
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1988-12-27

P.C. 1988-2799 1988-12-22
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council, pursuant to subsection 91(6) of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is satisfied that the
Canada Post Corporation is empowered under subsection 16(2) of the
[Canada Post Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-10) to acquire any real or
personal property.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs and the
Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 91(1)(b) of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to authorize the
Canada Post Corporation to acquire shares in the Cooperative Vereniging
International Post Corporation U.A., a corporation incorporated under
the laws of the Netherlands and having its head office located in
Amsterdam, the Netherlands, effective December 31, 1988.
