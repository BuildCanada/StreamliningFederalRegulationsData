## Order Transferring to the Public Health Agency of Canada the Control and Supervision of the Portion of the Federal Public Administration in the Department of Health known as the Travelling Public Program Unit {#order-transferring-to-the-public-health-agency-of-canada-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-portion-of-the-federal-public-administration-in-the-department-of-health-known-as-the-travelling-public-program-unit .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2013-40
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2013-04-10

Order Transferring to the Public Health Agency of Canada the Control and
Supervision of the Portion of the Federal Public Administration in the
Department of Health known as the Travelling Public Program Unit

P.C. 2013-341 2013-03-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12091){#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12091-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12092){#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12092-ID0EBCAA},
transfers from the Department of Health to the Public Health Agency of
Canada the control and supervision of the portion of the federal public
administration in the Department of Health known as the Travelling
Public Program Unit, effective April 1, 2013.
