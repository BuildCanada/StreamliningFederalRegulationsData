## Non-Taxable Imported Goods (GST/HST) Regulations {#non-taxable-imported-goods-gsthst-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-31
:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Registration 1990-12-18

Non-Taxable Imported Goods (GST/HST) Regulations

P.C. 1990-2740  1990-12-18
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 277(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-91-31_e_hq_11132){#fn_SOR-91-31_e_hq_11132-ID0EDBAA}
of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed Regulations prescribing goods that are non-taxable where
imported in prescribed circumstances.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934260 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an officer within the meaning
    of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([agent]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[qualifying vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualifying vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vehicle (other
    than a racing car described in heading No. 87.03 of the List of
    Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)) registered under the laws of a foreign
    jurisdiction relating to the registration of motor vehicles that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is described in any of heading No. 87.02,
      subheading Nos. 8703.21 to 8703.90, 8704.21, 8704.31, 8704.90 and
      8711.20 to 8711.90 and tariff item Nos. 8716.39.30 and 8716.39.90
      of that List,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is described in subheading No. 8704.22 or 8704.32
      of that List and has a gross vehicle weight rating (as defined in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Motor Vehicle Safety
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1038)) not exceeding
      10 tonnes, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is described in tariff item No. 8716.10.00 of
      that List and is a vehicle for camping. ([véhicule
      admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tax-relieved supply]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tax-relieved supply]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a supply of goods

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} included in Part V of Schedule VI to the Act;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which the recipient was entitled to
      claim a rebate under subsection 252(1) or 260(1) of the Act; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} made outside Canada, except if the recipient
      subsequently imported the goods and it is the case that all of the
      following do not apply in respect of that importation of the
      goods:

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} tax under section 212 of the Act was payable
        and was calculated on a value determined under the [Value of
        Imported Goods (GST/HST)
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-30) (other than under
        sections 7, 8, 12 and 13 of those Regulations),

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} tax under section 212 of the Act was payable
        by a person that was entitled to obtain a rebate, refund or
        remission of that tax under any Act of Parliament only because
        the goods were subsequently exported, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} tax under section 212 of the Act was not
        payable as a consequence of section 213 of the Act only because
        the goods were subsequently exported. ([fourniture
        dégrevée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prescribed Goods and Circumstances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934277 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 8 of Schedule VII to the Act, the following goods and
circumstances are prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} precious metals imported under any circumstances;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} unwrought silver, gold or platinum, waste and scrap
  of precious metal or of metal clad with precious metal, and
  concentrates of silver, gold or platinum, where imported for the
  purpose of being refined into precious metals;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} goods imported for the sole purpose of public exhibit
  by a public sector body, where, while the goods are in Canada,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} title to the goods is not intended to pass and does
    not pass to a person in Canada, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} beneficial use of the goods is not intended to
    pass and does not pass to a person in Canada that is not a public
    sector body;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} goods imported for the sole purpose of maintenance,
  overhaul or repair of those goods in Canada, where

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} neither title to nor beneficial use of the goods is
    intended to pass, or passes, to a person in Canada while the goods
    are in Canada, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the goods are exported as soon after the
    maintenance, overhaul or repair is completed as is reasonable having
    regard to the circumstances surrounding the importation and, where
    applicable, to the normal business practice of the importer;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} crude oil where

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} imported for the sole purpose of being refined in
    Canada,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} title to the crude oil is not held by a person in
    Canada at the time of importation,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} title to the crude oil is not intended to pass
    and does not pass to a person in Canada while the crude oil is in
    Canada,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} title to all refined products produced from the
    crude oil is not intended to pass and does not pass to a person in
    Canada while the refined products are in Canada, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the refined product is exported as soon after the
    refining is completed as is reasonable having regard to the
    circumstances surrounding the importation and, where applicable, to
    the normal business practice of the importer;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} foreign-based conveyances where

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the conveyance is non-taxable by reason of the
    reference to heading No. 98.01 of Schedule I to the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in section 1 of Schedule VII to the Act,
    and the conveyance is diverted for maintenance, overhaul or repair
    in Canada,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} neither title to nor beneficial use of the
    conveyance is intended to pass, or passes, to a person in Canada
    while the conveyance is in Canada, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the conveyance is exported as soon after the
    maintenance, overhaul or repair is completed as is reasonable having
    regard to the circumstances surrounding the importation and, where
    applicable, to the normal business practice of the importer;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a print, an etching, a drawing, a painting, a
  sculpture or other similar work of art where

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the work is part of a shipment of imported art on
    consignment and the total value of the shipment, determined in
    accordance with section 215 of the Act, is at least \$250,000,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at the time of importation, having regard to
    previous experience, if any, of the importer in importing works of
    art, it is reasonable to expect that at least 75%, in value, of the
    shipment will be exported within one year after the importation,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the work is imported for the purpose of supply by
    the importer in the ordinary course of the importer's business, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the importer provides a declaration pursuant to
    section 4;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} locomotives, railway rolling stock and vessels
  imported in circumstances where customs duties have been remitted or
  removed under

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Railway Rolling Stock (International Service)
    Remission Order No. 3,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} code 2338 of Schedule II to the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011),

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} Railway Rolling Stock (International Service)
    Remission Order No. 4, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2014-248, s. 7\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} section 5, 6, 7, 15, 16 or 17 of the [Vessel Duties
    Reduction or Removal Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-304);

- [(h.1)]{.lawlabel} railway passenger, baggage or freight cars (in this
  paragraph referred to as "imported cars") if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the imported cars are imported temporarily for use
    in the transportation of passengers, baggage or freight from a place
    in Canada to another place in Canada,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} railway cars of the same kind and number as the
    imported cars could not have been acquired from Canadian production
    or other Canadian sources at a reasonable cost or could not have
    been delivered or made available in Canada when needed, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the imported cars are exported on or before the
    earlier of

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the day that is one year after the day on which
      the imported cars are imported, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the day on or before which railway cars of the
      same kind and number as the imported cars could be delivered or
      made available in Canada after having been acquired from Canadian
      production or other Canadian sources at a reasonable cost;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} goods described in the following items of the
  schedule to the Temporary Importation Regulations imported in
  circumstances where the terms and conditions of those Regulations are
  met, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} items 3, 16 to 18, 27, 32, 33, 36, 39 to 44, 49, 52
    to 54 and 57, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} items 38 and 47, when the goods are imported by a
    non-resident person;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} goods imported after having been exported for
  warranty repair work;

- [(j.1)]{.lawlabel} a representational gift that is an article

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that is presented by a donor acting in the capacity
    as a Head of State, Head of Government or representative of a
    government, a public body of a foreign country or a political
    subdivision of a foreign country, to a donee acting in the capacity
    of the Governor General, the Prime Minister of Canada, a minister of
    the Government of Canada, a member of the Senate or House of
    Commons, a provincial premier or a municipal mayor, in the course of
    an official visit by the donee outside Canada, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is to be presented by a donor described in
    subparagraph (i) in the course of an official visit by the donor to
    Canada and that is subsequently so presented;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} medals, trophies, plaques or other similar articles
  to be presented by the importer at awards ceremonies;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} goods enumerated in code 1910 of Schedule II to the
  [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) where the goods are imported in
  circumstances that meet the requirements of that code;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} a qualifying vehicle that is imported temporarily by
  an individual resident in Canada and not accounted for as a commercial
  good (as defined in subsection 212.1(1) of the Act) under section 32
  of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the qualifying vehicle was last supplied in the
    course of a vehicle rental business to the individual by way of
    lease, licence or similar arrangement under which continuous
    possession or use of the qualifying vehicle is provided for a period
    of less than 180 days,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} immediately before the importation, the individual
    was outside Canada for an uninterrupted period of at least 48 hours,
    and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the qualifying vehicle is exported within 30 days
    after the importation; and

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} goods that are classified under tariff item No.
  9813.00.00 or 9814.00.00 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in
  the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), or that
  would be so classified in the absence of paragraphs (a) and (b) of
  those tariff items, if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the goods are being imported for
    the first time since the goods were last supplied and delivered or
    made available to the recipient of that last supply,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the supply of the goods was not a tax-relieved
      supply

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the supply of the goods was made by way of sale
      by a supplier that did not acquire the goods by way of a
      tax-relieved supply and the recipient is returning the goods to
      the supplier because

      - [(I)]{.lawlabel} under the agreement for the supply, or as a
        consequence of the termination of that agreement, ownership of
        the goods is never transferred to the recipient or is
        transferred back to the supplier,

      - [(II)]{.lawlabel} the goods are defective or not as ordered by
        the recipient, or

      - [(III)]{.lawlabel} the recipient exported the goods for sale and
        did not sell the goods, or

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the supply was made outside Canada by way of
      lease, licence or similar arrangement by the importer of the goods
      and the importer

      - [(I)]{.lawlabel} did not acquire the goods by way of a
        tax-relieved supply,

      - [(II)]{.lawlabel} exported the goods at a time when the importer
        was the owner of the goods for the sole purpose of making the
        supply, and

      - [(II)]{.lawlabel} is importing the goods after the termination
        of the lease, licence or similar arrangement, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the goods were either not
    imported previously or it is the case that all of the following do
    not apply in respect of the last importation of the goods:

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} tax under section 212 of the Act was payable and
      was calculated on a value determined under the [Value of Imported
      Goods (GST/HST) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-30) (other
      than under sections 7, 8, 12 and 13 of those Regulations),

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} tax under section 212 of the Act was payable by a
      person that was entitled to obtain a rebate, refund or remission
      of that tax under any Act of Parliament only because the goods
      were subsequently exported, and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} tax under section 212 of the Act was not payable
      as a consequence of section 213 of the Act only because the goods
      were subsequently exported.

## [Documentation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934344 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An importer of goods
prescribed by paragraph 3(g) shall, at the time of accounting for the
goods in accordance with section 32 of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), attach to or endorse on the accounting document
the following declaration, signed and dated:

"I hereby declare that it is my expectation that at least 75%, in value,
of the works of art in this shipment will be exported within one year
after this date.

(Signed)

(Dated)![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}"

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  importer of goods prescribed by paragraph 3(j) shall, at the time of
  accounting for the goods under section 32 of the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), attach to the accounting document

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} except where subsection (2) applies, a copy of the
    export report in respect of the goods; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an invoice or written statement from the supplier
    of the goods showing that, excluding shipping charges, communication
    expenses and other non-repair expenses, the cost of the warranty
    repair to the goods was borne by the supplier under the terms of a
    warranty.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the export report referred to in
  paragraph (1)(a) is unavailable due to circumstances beyond the
  control of the importer, the importer shall provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Canada customs document proving the exportation
    of the goods in accordance with the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a transportation company document respecting the
    exportation of the goods;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a customs accounting document respecting the
    importation of the goods into the country where the warranty repair
    work was performed;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a declaration made by the foreign exporter that the
    goods exported to Canada are the goods that were exported from
    Canada for warranty repair; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} other evidence satisfactory to the Minister showing
    that the goods had been exported from Canada.

## [Notification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934359 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where an importer imports
works of art prescribed by paragraph 3(g), and less than 75%, in value,
of the shipment is exported within one year after the importation, the
importer shall notify in writing an officer at a customs office
indicating the percentage, in value, of the works of art in the shipment
that was exported.
