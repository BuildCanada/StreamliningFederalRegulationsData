## Diplomatic Service (Special) Superannuation Regulations, 1988 {#diplomatic-service-special-superannuation-regulations-1988 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-485
:::

[DIPLOMATIC SERVICE (SPECIAL) SUPERANNUATION ACT](/eng/acts/D-2)

Registration 1988-09-15

Regulations Respecting the Carrying into Effect of the Diplomatic
Service (Special) Superannuation Act

P.C. 1988-2024 1988-09-15
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 6(5) and section 16 of the
[Diplomatic Service (Special) Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/D-2), is
pleased hereby to revoke the Diplomatic Service (Special) Superannuation
Regulations, C.R.C., c. 555, and to make the annexed [Regulations
respecting the carrying into effect of the Diplomatic Service (Special)
Superannuation Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-485), in substitution
therefor.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913583 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Diplomatic Service (Special) Superannuation Regulations,
1988](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-485).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913585 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Diplomatic Service (Special)
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/D-2); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the President of the Treasury
    Board. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Election]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913589 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An election made by a
Public Official pursuant to subsection 6(1) of the Act shall be in
writing, dated and signed by the Public Official and a witness, and
forwarded to the Minister within the period set out in that subsection
for the making of the election.

## [Basis of Computation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913591 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An instalment provided for
in subsection 6(5) of the Act shall be computed on the basis of the
Canadian Life Table No. 2 (1941), Males or Females, as the case may be,
in the Life Tables for Canada and Regions, 1941 and 1931, published in
1947 by the Dominion Bureau of Statistics of the Department of Trade and
Commerce, with interest at the rate of four per cent per annum.

## [Increased Instalments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913593 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where a Public Official
makes a contribution by instalments pursuant to subsection 6(5) of the
Act, the Public Official may, at any time, have the amount of the
instalments increased.

## [Documentation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913595 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where a Public Official is
entitled to payment of a pension under the Act and either the Public
Official has not made an election pursuant to section 8 of the Act or
the spouse of the Public Official is deceased, the Public Official
shall, on request by the Minister, submit to the Minister

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the birth certificate of the Public Official; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Public Official retired by reason of permanent
  infirmity, the certificate of infirmity issued by a qualified medical
  practitioner.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where a Public Official and
the spouse of the Public Official are entitled to the payment of a
pension under the Act, the Public Official shall, on request by the
Minister, submit to the Minister, in addition to the certificates
referred to in paragraphs 6(a) and (b), the marriage certificate of the
Public Official and the spouse.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where the surviving spouse
of a deceased Public Official is entitled to the payment of a pension
under the Act, the spouse shall, on request by the Minister, submit to
the Minister

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the marriage certificate of the Public Official and
  the spouse; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} proof of the death of the Public Official.
