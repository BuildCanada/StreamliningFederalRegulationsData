## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834706 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Inuvialuit Final
    Agreement as approved, given effect and declared valid by the
    [Western Arctic (Inuvialuit) Claims Settlement
    Act](/eng/acts/W-6.7). ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Marine Protected Areas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Marine Protected Areas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the areas of
    the sea that are designated by section 2. ([zones de protection
    marine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834711 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marine Protected Areas

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following areas of the sea are designated as the Anguniaqvia niqiqyuam
  Marine Protected Areas:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the area of the sea in Darnley Bay and Amundsen
    Gulf in the Beaufort Sea consisting of the waters within the
    boundaries of Zone 1 as described in plan number FB40812, certified
    on February 10, 2015 and depicted in plan number CLSR 104411, which
    plans are deposited in the Canada Lands Surveys Records; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the area of the sea in Kendall Inlet in Amundsen
    Gulf in the Beaufort Sea consisting of the waters within the
    boundaries of Zone 2 as described in plan number FB40812, certified
    on February 10, 2015 and depicted in plan number CLSR 104411, which
    plans are deposited in the Canada Lands Surveys Records.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Plan number FB40812

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of plan number FB40812 is set out in
  Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seabed, subsoil and water column

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each Marine Protected Area consists of the seabed,
  the subsoil to a depth of five metres and the water column, including
  the sea ice, each of which is below the low-water line.

## [Prohibited Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834721 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited in the
Marine Protected Areas to carry out any activity that disturbs, damages,
destroys or removes from the Marine Protected Areas any living marine
organism or any part of its habitat or is likely to do so.

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834724 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fishing

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 3, the
following activities may be carried out in the Marine Protected Areas if
no fishing gear listed in Schedule 2 is used:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fishing in accordance with the Agreement;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} fishing in accordance with subsection 22(1) of the
  [Northwest Territories Fishery
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._847);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} fishing for subsistence usage in accordance with
  subsection 22.1(1) of the [Northwest Territories Fishery
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._847);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} fishing in accordance with a domestic fishing licence
  issued under section 23 of the [Northwest Territories Fishery
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._847); and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} sport fishing in accordance with subsection 27(1) of
  the [Northwest Territories Fishery
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._847).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Navigation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 3,
navigation may be carried out in the Marine Protected Areas if it is
carried out in accordance with the [Canada Shipping Act,
2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) and the [Arctic Waters Pollution Prevention
Act](/eng/acts/A-12).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dredging

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 3, dredging
may be carried out in the Marine Protected Areas if the following
conditions are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dredging is required to enable navigation for the
  purpose of supplying goods to the community of Paulatuk, Northwest
  Territories; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a recommendation with respect to the dredging has
  been made in accordance with the Agreement and the dredging has been
  authorized by a competent government authority.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safety or emergency

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 3, an
activity may be carried out in the Marine Protected Areas if it is
carried out for the purpose of public safety, national defence, national
security, law enforcement or to respond to an emergency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activity plan

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 3, a
scientific research or monitoring activity, educational activity or
commercial marine tourism activity may be carried out in the Marine
Protected Areas if it is part of an activity plan that has been approved
by the Minister.

## [Activity Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834742 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission and contents

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Any person who proposes to
carry out a scientific research or monitoring activity, educational
activity or commercial marine tourism activity in the Marine Protected
Areas must submit to the Minister an activity plan that contains

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person's name, address, telephone number,
  facsimile number and email address;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the activity plan is submitted by an institution
  or organization, the name of the individual who will be responsible
  for the proposed activity and their title, address, telephone number,
  facsimile number and email address;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of each vessel that the person proposes to
  use to carry out the activity, its state of registration and
  registration number, its radio call sign and the name and address of
  its owner, master and any operator;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed dates of the vessel's first entry into
  and final exit from the Marine Protected Areas, and any proposed
  alternative dates;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the geographic coordinates of the site of the
  proposed activity and a map that shows the location of the activity
  within the boundaries of the Marine Protected Areas;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a list of any substances that may be deposited during
  the proposed activity in the Marine Protected Areas --- other than
  substances that are authorized by the [Canada Shipping Act,
  2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) and its regulations to be deposited in the
  navigation of a vessel --- and the quantity and concentration of each
  substance;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the proposed activity and
  its purpose;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a general description of any study, report or other
  work that is anticipated to result from the proposed activity, and its
  anticipated date of completion;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of any scientific research or
  monitoring activity, educational activity or commercial marine tourism
  activity that the person has previously carried out in the Marine
  Protected Areas or anticipates carrying out in those areas in the
  future;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} a description of any measures to be taken to monitor,
  avoid, minimize or mitigate any adverse environmental effects of the
  proposed activity; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} a description of any measures to be taken to not
  interfere with fishing carried out in accordance with the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of activity plan

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must approve an activity plan if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the scientific research or monitoring activities
    set out in the plan are not likely to destroy the habitat of any
    living marine organism in the Marine Protected Areas, will not
    interfere with fishing carried out in accordance with the Agreement,
    and will serve to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} increase knowledge of the biodiversity, the
      habitat of any living marine organism or the ecosystem function of
      the Marine Protected Areas,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} assist in the management of the Marine Protected
      Areas, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} assist in the evaluation of the effectiveness
      of any measures taken to conserve and protect the Marine Protected
      Areas; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the educational activities or commercial marine
    tourism activities set out in the plan

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are not likely to result in the damage,
      destruction or removal of any part of the habitat of any living
      marine organism within the Marine Protected Areas,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} will serve to increase public awareness of the
      Marine Protected Areas, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} will not interfere with fishing carried out in
      accordance with the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval prohibited

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Minister must not approve
  an activity plan if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any substance that may be deposited during the
    proposed activity is a [[deleterious
    substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 34(1) of
    the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14), unless the deposit of the
    substance is authorized under subsection 36(4) of that Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cumulative environmental effects of the
    proposed activity, in combination with any other past and current
    activities carried out in the Marine Protected Areas, are likely to
    adversely impact the biological, chemical or oceanographic processes
    that maintain or enhance the biodiversity, habitat or ecosystem
    function in the Marine Protected Areas.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timeline for approval

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister's decision in respect of an activity
  plan must be made within

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 60 days after the day on which the plan is
    received; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if amendments to the plan are made, 60 days after
    the day on which the amended plan is received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Studies, reports or other work

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  activity plan has been approved by the Minister, the person who
  submitted the plan must provide the Minister with a copy of any study,
  report or other work that results from the activity and that is
  related to the conservation and protection of the Marine Protected
  Areas.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Data

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The study, report or other work must be accompanied
  by the data that was obtained during the activity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The study, report or other work, together with the
  data, must be provided to the Minister within 90 days after the day on
  which the study, report or other work is completed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission of data to Minister

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the study, report or other work is not completed
  within a period of three years after the last day of the activity, the
  person must submit the data that was obtained during the activity to
  the Minister within 90 days after that period.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834784 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
