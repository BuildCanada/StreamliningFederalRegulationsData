## Assessable Activities, Exceptions and Executive Committee Projects Regulations {#assessable-activities-exceptions-and-executive-committee-projects-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2005-379
:::

[YUKON ENVIRONMENTAL AND SOCIO-ECONOMIC ASSESSMENT ACT](/eng/acts/Y-2.2)

Registration 2005-11-28

Assessable Activities, Exceptions and Executive Committee Projects
Regulations

P.C. 2005-2199 2005-11-28
::::

::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development has
consulted with the member of the Executive Council of Yukon designated
by the Commissioner of Yukon as the territorial minister for the
purposes of the [Yukon Environmental and Socio-economic Assessment
Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBBBA} and with the
first nations, within the meaning of that Act;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EBBBA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2003, c.
  7
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development, pursuant to subsection 47(1) and paragraph 122(c) of the
[Yukon Environmental and Socio-economic Assessment
Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDAAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Assessable Activities, Exceptions and Executive Committee
Projects Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-379).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721307 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[aboveground storage tank system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[aboveground storage tank system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      storage tank system in which each container has more than 90% of
      its volume above surface grade and the system operates at
      atmospheric pressure plus or minus 10 kPa. ([système de réservoirs
      de stockage hors terre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Yukon Environmental and
      Socio-economic Assessment
      Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.2). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[aerodrome]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[aerodrome]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any area of land, any
      water body or any other supporting surface used, designed,
      prepared, equipped or set apart for use either in whole or in part
      for the arrival, departure, movement or servicing of aircraft and
      includes any associated structure, facility or
      installation. ([aérodrome]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an aerodrome in respect of
      which a certificate issued under Subpart 2 of Part III of the
      [Canadian Aviation Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433) is in
      force. ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Class II nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Class II nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in section 1 of the Class II Nuclear Facilities and
      Prescribed Equipment Regulations. ([installation nucléaire de
      catégorie II]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Class II prescribed equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Class II prescribed equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in section 1 of the Class II Nuclear Facilities and
      Prescribed Equipment Regulations. ([équipement réglementé de
      catégorie II]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[community undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[community undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an undertaking
      that is located

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a municipality or settlement and that uses
        only a municipal water and sewage system; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a camp or lodge. ([entreprise
        communautaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[contaminant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[contaminant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a contaminant mentioned
      in Schedule 1 or 3, or a substance for which a table has been set
      out in Schedule 2, to the Contaminated Sites Regulations, Yukon
      O.I.C. 2002/171. ([polluant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[contaminated site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[contaminated site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area of land in
      which the soil, including any groundwater lying beneath it, or the
      water, including the sediment and bed below it, contains a
      contaminant the amount, concentration or level of which is equal
      to or greater than that prescribed by the Contaminated Sites
      Regulations, Yukon O.I.C. 2002/171. ([site
      pollué]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[control product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[control product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any product, device,
      organism, substance or thing that is manufactured, represented,
      sold or used as a means for directly or indirectly controlling,
      preventing, destroying, mitigating, attracting or repelling any
      injurious, noxious or troublesome animal, plant or other organism,
      including any injurious, noxious or troublesome organic function
      of an animal, plant or other organism, and includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any compound or substance that enhances or
        modifies or is intended to enhance or modify the physical or
        chemical characteristics of a control product to which it is
        added; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any active ingredient used for the manufacture
        of a control product. ([produit
        antiparasitaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[corridor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[corridor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a path from which trees and
      brush have been cut to accommodate a water line, fuel line or
      power line. ([corridor]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Crown land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Crown land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means land vested in Her
      Majesty in right of Canada, whether the administration and control
      of the land is appropriated to the Commissioner of Yukon or
      not. ([terre domaniale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[effective dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[effective dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 1(1) of the [Radiation Protection
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-203). ([dose
      efficace]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[endangered species]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[endangered species]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means those species
      listed in Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk
      Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3). ([espèce en voie de
      disparition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[exemption quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[exemption quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in section 1 of the [Nuclear Substances and Radiation Devices
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-207). ([quantité
      d'exemption]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[explosive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[explosive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any thing that is made,
      manufactured or used to produce an explosion, a detonation or a
      pyrotechnic effect, and includes any thing prescribed to be an
      explosive by the regulations under the [Explosives
      Act](/eng/acts/E-17), but does not include gases, organic
      peroxides and any thing prescribed not to be an explosive by those
      regulations. ([explosif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[fish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes shellfish, crustaceans,
      marine animals and the eggs, sperm, spawn, larvae, spat and
      juvenile stages of fish, shellfish, crustaceans and marine
      animals. ([poissons]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[fish habitat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fish habitat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means spawning grounds and
      nursery, rearing, food supply and migration areas on which fish
      depend directly or indirectly in order to carry out their life
      processes. ([habitat du poisson]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[food or beverage processing facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[food or beverage processing facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      a facility established for the processing of food or beverages for
      commercial purposes and includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an alcohol distilling plant,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a brewery,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a facility for

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} beverage production,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the bottling or canning of food,

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} meat or meat products processing,

        - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} poultry or poultry products processing,

        - [(v)]{.lawlabel} fish or seafood processing,

        - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} milk processing, or

        - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturing of pet, stock or
          aquaculture food,

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a rendering operation, and

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} smoking, drying or curing works,

      but does not include a facility, such as a restaurant or bar,
      whose primary purpose is to prepare and serve food or beverages.
      ([établissement de transformation d'aliments ou de fabrication de
      boissons]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[foundation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[foundation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the part of a structure
      that penetrates the ground and supports the
      structure. ([fondation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Indian reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Indian reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the [Indian
      Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([réserve indienne]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[industrial development]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[industrial development]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} mining and the development of an energy
        resource or of agricultural land;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for commercial purposes, cutting standing or
        fallen trees or removing fallen or cut trees;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the development of a townsite; and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any land use or the construction, operation,
        modification, decommissioning or abandonment of a structure,
        facility or installation associated with any activity referred
        to in paragraphs (a) to (c). ([développement
        industriel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[irradiator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[irradiator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 1 of the Class II Nuclear Facilities and Prescribed
      Equipment Regulations. ([irradiateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[land treatment facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[land treatment facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a facility
      designed and operated for the purpose of removing, destroying or
      containing a contaminant found in soil, sediment, snow or other
      similar material, or for the purpose of carrying out any other
      activity intended to reduce the exposure of human beings, animals
      and plants to the contaminant. ([installation de traitement des
      sols]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[local advisory area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[local advisory area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
      as in section 1 of the Municipal Act, R.S.Y. 2002,
      c. 154. ([collectivité locale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[mapped community]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mapped community]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a community in
      respect of which the boundaries are, for the purpose of this
      definition, shown on a map deposited with the Board by the
      territorial minister or the Regional Director General, Yukon
      Region of the Department of Indian Affairs and Northern
      Development, on November 28, 2005. ([collectivité
      désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[migratory birds]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[migratory birds]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      the Convention set out in the schedule to the [Migratory Birds
      Convention Act, 1994](/eng/acts/M-7.01), as that Convention is
      amended from time to time. ([oiseaux migrateurs]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[municipality]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[municipality]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any part of Yukon
      established as a city or town under the Municipal Act, R.S.Y.
      2002, c. 154, and any municipality continued under that
      Act. ([municipalité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[national historic site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[national historic site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a place of
      national historic interest or significance that is commemorated
      under section 3 of the [Historic Sites and Monuments
      Act](/eng/acts/H-4) and is under the administration of the Parks
      Canada Agency. ([lieu historique national]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[national park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[national park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a national park of
      Canada named and described in Part 11 of Schedule 1 to the [Canada
      National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01). ([parc
      national]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[national park reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[national park reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a national
      park reserve of Canada named and described in Schedule 2 to the
      [Canada National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01) that is in
      Yukon. ([réserve foncière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[natural gas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[natural gas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes all substances that
      are produced in association with natural gas, including solution
      gas, and any substance designated as a gas product by regulations
      made under section 130 of the [National Energy Board
      Act](/eng/acts/N-7). ([gaz naturel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[natural gas processing plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[natural gas processing plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not
      include a well-head separator, treater or dehydrator. ([usine de
      traitement de gaz naturel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in section 2 of the [Nuclear Safety and Control
      Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3). ([installation nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[nuclear substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[nuclear substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in section 2 of the [Nuclear Safety and Control
      Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3). ([substance nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[petroleum]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
      means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} crude petroleum, regardless of gravity,
        produced at a well-head in liquid form; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other hydrocarbons, except coal and natural
        gas, including hydrocarbons that may be extracted or recovered
        from surface or subsurface deposits, including deposits of oil
        sand, bitumen, bituminous sand, oil shale and other types of
        deposits. ([pétrole]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[oil or natural gas field facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[oil or natural gas field facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      battery, a satellite, a pumping station, a compressor station, a
      well-head separator, treater or dehydrator, a natural gas
      injection station, a line heater, an oilfield waste storage,
      treatment, processing or disposal facility, a liquefied natural
      gas handling facility, a water injection, storage, treatment or
      disposal station, a truck terminal, a power generating station, a
      flare system or any system of vessels and equipment designed to
      accommodate the production, injection, storage, treatment or
      disposal of well effluent products and by-products. ([installation
      de champ de pétrole ou de gaz naturel]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[oil or natural gas pipeline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[oil or natural gas pipeline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      pipeline that is used, or is intended to be used, for the
      transmission of oil or natural gas alone or with any other
      commodity, and includes all property used in connection with or
      incidental to the operation of the pipeline. ([pipeline
      d'hydrocarbures]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[oil or natural gas pipeline component]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[oil or natural gas pipeline
      component]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a connection;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} piping;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a cathodic protection system, including a
        rectifier;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a valve, including a valve vault and pressure
        transmitter;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a compressor and pump station component,
        including a compressor, pump, motor, silencer, scrubber, natural
        gas seal, system boiler, scraper trap, switch gear, transformer
        and uninterruptible power supply;

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a storage tank component, including a mixer and
        ladder;

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a metering and regulating facility;

      - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a quality measurement system, including an
        analyzer for water or basic sediments, a densitometer,
        calorimeter, in-line viscometer, gas chromatograph and
        automatic/composite sampler; or

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a mechanical or electrical system of a
        facility, including a plumbing, air conditioning, heating or
        ventilation system not involving the use or disposal of
        chlorofluorocarbons. ([composant d'un pipeline
        d'hydrocarbures]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[paper product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[paper product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes paper, coated
      paper, paperboard, hardboard, boxboard, linerboard, insulating
      board, building board, corrugating medium, tissue, molded
      cellulose product and any other product directly derived from
      pulp, but does not include viscose, rayon, cellophane and any
      other cellulose derivative. ([produit de papier]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[petroleum product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[petroleum product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in section 1 of the Storage Tank Regulations, Yukon O.I.C.
      1996/194. ([produit pétrolier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[placer grant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[placer grant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a parcel of land
      described in a grant by a federal agency, the Governor in Council
      or a territorial agency for the exclusive right to conduct placer
      mining or, in the case of category A settlement land, by a first
      nation for the right to conduct placer mining. ([concession de
      placer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[placer mining]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[placer mining]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prospecting for or
      mining gold or other precious minerals found within any natural
      stratum or bed of earth, gravel or cement. ([exploitation d'un
      placer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[placer mining operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[placer mining operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means placer
      mining, and any other activity in relation to placer mining,
      undertaken by the same operator on either a placer grant that is
      not part of a group of claims referred to in a grouping
      certificate within the meaning of the Placer Mining Act, S.Y.
      2003, c. 13, or on any placer grant in the same group if the
      grants are part of a group of claims referred to in a grouping
      certificate within the meaning of that Act. ([programme
      d'exploitation d'un placer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[polluting substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[polluting substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance
      that, if added to a water body, is likely to degrade or alter or
      form part of a process of degradation or alteration of the
      physical, chemical or biological conditions of the water body to
      an extent that is detrimental to its use by human beings, animals
      or plants. ([substance polluante dans un plan
      d'eau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[power line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[power line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means facilities constructed or
      operated for the purpose of transmitting electricity and includes
      electrical transmission lines, distribution lines, switching
      stations and transformers. ([ligne d'énergie
      électrique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[public road]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[public road]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a public road within a
      municipality and a public road outside of a municipality that the
      Minister responsible for the Highways Act, R.S.Y. 2002, c. 108,
      has a duty to maintain. ([route publique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[pulp]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[pulp]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means processed cellulose fibres that
      are derived from wood, other plant material or recycled paper
      products. ([pâte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[quartz exploration program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[quartz exploration program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      exploration for the purpose of quartz mining, and those activities
      conducted in relation to the exploration, undertaken within a
      12-month period by the same operator on one or more quartz grants
      within an area circumscribed by a circle with a diameter of
      20 km. ([programme d'exploration du quartz]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[quartz grant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[quartz grant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a parcel of land
      described in a grant by a federal agency, the Governor in Council
      or a territorial agency for the exclusive right to conduct quartz
      mining or exploration for the purpose of quartz mining or, in the
      case of category A settlement land, by a first nation for the
      right to conduct quartz mining or exploration for the purpose of
      quartz mining. ([concession de quartz]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[quartz mining]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[quartz mining]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the mining of minerals
      contained within a vein, lode or rock in place, other than coal,
      oil, natural gas, limestone, marble, gypsum, shale, slate, clay,
      sand, gravel, construction stone, volcanic ash, earth, marl or
      peat. ([exploitation du quartz]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[radiation device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[radiation device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in section 1 of the [Nuclear Substances and Radiation Devices
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-207). ([appareil à
      rayonnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[railway line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[railway line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any structure that
      supports or protects a line of railway or provides drainage for
      it, a system of switches, signals or other like devices and that
      facilitates railway operations, or any other structure built
      across, beside, under or over a line of railway that facilitates
      railway operations, but does not include a utility crossing or a
      road crossing. ([ligne de chemin de fer]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[road crossing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[road crossing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the part of a road
      that passes across, over or under a railway track, and includes a
      structure supporting or protecting that part of the road or
      facilitating the crossing. ([franchissement
      routier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[sealed source]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[sealed source]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 1 of the [Nuclear Substances and Radiation Devices
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-207). ([source
      scellée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[solid waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[solid waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include special
      waste, untreated forest debris and untreated wood products that
      are not mixed with other materials. ([déchets
      solides]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[solid waste disposal facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[solid waste disposal facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a site
      designed for the handling or disposal by the public of solid waste
      and includes any machinery, equipment, devices, tanks or other
      installations used on the site for that purpose and any land,
      transfer stations or buildings associated with the handling or
      disposal of solid waste on the site. ([installation d'élimination
      des déchets solides]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[special waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[special waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 1 of the Special Waste Regulations, Yukon O.I.C.
      1995/047. ([déchets spéciaux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[storage tank system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[storage tank system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means one or more
      closed containers with a capacity of more than 230 L each that are
      designed to be installed in a fixed location and includes all
      connections, piping, pumps, dispensers, diking, overfill
      protection equipment and associated spill containment and
      collection apparatus. ([système de réservoirs de
      stockage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[subcritical nuclear assembly]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[subcritical nuclear assembly]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      assembly of fissile material, moderators and reflectors that is
      not designed to achieve a self-sustaining nuclear chain
      reaction. ([assemblage nucléaire non divergent]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[temporary trail]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[temporary trail]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a trail that is
      established or used as part of a quartz exploration program and
      that is required under a territorial or first nation law to be
      reclaimed and for which access is required, under that law, to be
      blocked at the end of the program. ([sentier
      temporaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[threatened species]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[threatened species]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means those species
      listed in Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk
      Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3). ([espèce menacée]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[trenching]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[trenching]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means excavation of a trench
      that extends below the vegetative mat. ([creusement de
      tranchées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[unsealed source]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[unsealed source]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 1 of the [Nuclear Substances and Radiation Devices
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-207). ([source non
      scellée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[utility crossing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[utility crossing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the part of a line
      of wire, cable or pipeline, or other like means of enabling the
      transmission of goods or energy or the provision of services, that
      passes over or under a railway track, and includes a structure
      supporting or protecting that part of the utility line or
      facilitating the crossing. ([franchissement par
      desserte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[vegetative mat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vegetative mat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the organic surface
      of soil, characterized by the accumulation of organic matter, or
      partly decomposed organic matter, derived mainly from leaves,
      twigs and woody materials, and includes the root mass of living
      vegetation. ([couvert végétal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, for the purposes of Part 9 of
      Schedule 1 and section 49 of Schedule 3, means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any substance that, if added to water, would
        degrade or alter, or form part of a process of degradation or
        alteration of, the quality of the water to an extent that is
        detrimental to its use by human beings, animals or plants; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} water that contains a substance in a quantity
        or concentration, or that has been treated, processed or changed
        by heat or other means, such that if added to other water, would
        degrade or alter, or form part of a process of degradation or
        alteration of, the quality of that other water to the extent
        described in paragraph (a). ([déchet]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[waste oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[waste oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means oil that has become
      unsuitable for its intended purpose owing to the presence of
      impurities or the loss of original properties, and includes oil
      used as lubrication oil, hydraulic fluid, metal-working fluid,
      insulating fluid or coolant fluid. ([huile usée]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[water body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[water body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an inland water body, up
      to its ordinary high-water mark, in a liquid or frozen state,
      including a swamp, marsh, bog, fen, reservoir and any other land
      that is covered by water during at least three consecutive months
      of the year, but does not include a sewage or waste treatment
      lagoon, a dugout to hold water for livestock and a mine tailings
      pond. ([plan d'eau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[watercourse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[watercourse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a natural watercourse,
      water body or water supply, including one that contains water
      intermittently, and includes groundwater, springs, swamps and
      gulches. ([cours d'eau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[wildlife]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[wildlife]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vertebrate animal of any
      species or type that is wild by nature, and includes wildlife in
      captivity, but does not include fish nor does it include a species
      of animal prescribed not to be wildlife by a regulation made under
      the Wildlife Act, R.S.Y. 2002, c. 229. ([animal
      sauvage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[wildlife area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[wildlife area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area of land set
      out in Part X of Schedule I to the [Wildlife Area
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1609). ([réserve
      d'espèces sauvages]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[winter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[winter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period of the year during
      which the ground is frozen sufficiently to support a vehicle that
      applies more than 35 kPa of pressure to the ground without rutting
      or gouging of the surface and during which there is a sufficient
      amount of snow on the ground to produce a packed base of at least
      10 cm. ([hiver]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of Part 13 of Schedule 1, none of

## [Activities and Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721419 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 47(1) of the Act, activities that may, subject to section 3,
be made subject to assessment are listed in column 1 of Parts 1 to 13 of
Schedule 1.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Exceptions to activities
referred to in section 2 are set out in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} column 2 of Parts 1 to 13 of Schedule 1;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Part 1 of Schedule 2 if the activity is conducted in
  an area other than a national park, national park reserve and national
  historic site; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Part 2 of Schedule 2 if the activity is conducted in
  a national park, national park reserve or national historic site.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  proposed activity of a proponent is an activity listed in column 1 of
  an item of Schedule 1 if the proposed activity is mentioned or
  included, in whole or in part, in column 1 of that item.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The proposed activity is excepted from assessment if,
  for column 1 of each item of Schedule 1 in which the activity is
  listed in whole or in part, an exception applicable to that activity,
  or to that part, is set out in column 2 of the respective item, or in
  Schedule 2.

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an explosives factory for the manufacture of
    chemical explosives involving chemical processes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a facility for the manufacture of lead-acid
    batteries;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a metal smelter;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a facility for the commercial production of
    non-ferrous metals or light metals by pyrometallurgy or high
    temperature electrometallurgy;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a facility for the production of primary steel with
    a production capacity of 5000 t/day or more;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a facility for the manufacture of chemical products
    with a production capacity of 250 000 t/year or more;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a facility for the production of pharmaceutical
    products with a production capacity of 200 t/year or more;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a facility for the manufacture of wood products
    that are pressure-treated with chemical products with a production
    capacity of 50 000 m^3^/year or more;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a facility for the manufacture of plywood or
    particle board with a production capacity of 100 000 m^3^/year or
    more;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a tannery with a production capacity of
    500 000 m^2^/year or more;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a facility for the manufacture of primary textiles
    with a production capacity of 50 000 t/year or more; or

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a facility for the production of respirable natural
    mineral fibres.

- **11** Construction, decommissioning or abandonment of a metal mill
  with an ore input capacity of 2000 t/day or more.

- **12** Expansion of a metal mill that increases ore input capacity by
  50% or more, or by 1000 t/day or more, and increases the ore input
  capacity to 2000 t/day or more.

#### [Oil and Natural Gas]{.HTitleText2} {#oil-and-natural-gas .SchedHeadL2}

- **13** Construction, decommissioning or abandonment, or an expansion
  that increases inlet capacity by more than 35%, of a sour natural gas
  processing plant with sulphur inlet capacity of more than 2000 t/day.

- **14** Construction, decommissioning or abandonment, or an expansion
  that increases processing capacity by more than 35%, of a sweet
  natural gas processing plant with processing capacity of
  2 000 000 m^3^/day or more.

- **15** Construction, decommissioning or abandonment, or an expansion
  that increases processing capacity by more than 35%, of a plant for
  the liquefaction of natural gas or re-gasification of liquefied
  natural gas with processing capacity of more than 3000 t/day.

- **16** Construction, decommissioning or abandonment, or an expansion
  that increases storage capacity by more than 35%, of a plant for the
  storage of natural gas with storage capacity of more than 50 000 t.

- **17** Construction of an oil or natural gas pipeline 25 km or more in
  length if the pipeline is not on a right of way developed for a power
  line, pipeline, railway line or road nor on a right of way contiguous
  to, for its whole length, a right of way developed for a power line,
  pipeline, railway line or road.

- **18** Construction, decommissioning or abandonment of an oil or
  natural gas pipeline 75 km or more in length if the pipeline is on a
  right of way developed for a power line, pipeline, railway line or
  road.

- **19** Construction, decommissioning or abandonment of an offshore oil
  or natural gas pipeline in fresh water.

- **20** Construction, decommissioning or abandonment, or an expansion
  that increases input capacity by more than 35%, of an oil refinery
  with an input capacity of more than 5000 m^3^/day.

- **21** Construction, decommissioning or abandonment, or an expansion
  that increases production capacity by more than 35%, of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a facility for the production of liquid hydrocarbon
    products from coal with a production capacity of more than
    2000 m^3^/day;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a heavy oil or oil sands processing facility with
    an oil production capacity of more than 5000 m^3^/day; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an oil sands mine with a bitumen production
    capacity of more than 5000 m^3^/day.

#### [Energy and Telecommunications]{.HTitleText2} {#energy-and-telecommunications .SchedHeadL2}

- **22** Construction or installation of a power line with a voltage of
  138 kV or more or of a length of more than 50 km if the power line is
  not on a right of way developed for a power line, pipeline, railway
  line or road nor on a right of way contiguous to, for its whole
  length, a right of way developed for a power line, pipeline, railway
  line or road.

- **23** Expansion of a power line that results in an increase, for a
  length of more than 100 km, of the width of the right of way on which
  it is located.

- **24** Expansion of the length of a power line by 25 km or more if the
  expanded portion of the power line is not on a right of way developed
  for a power line, pipeline, railway line or road nor on a right of way
  contiguous to, for its whole length, a right of way developed for a
  power line, pipeline, railway line or road.

- **25** Construction, decommissioning or abandonment of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a hydroelectric generating station with a
    production capacity of 5 MW or more;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a fossil fuel-fired electrical generating station
    with a production capacity of 5 MW or more; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a wood-fired electrical generating station with a
    production capacity of 5 MW or more.

- **26** Expansion of a hydroelectric generating station, or a fossil
  fuel-fired electrical generating station, that increases production
  capacity by 5 MW or more.

#### [Wildlife]{.HTitleText2} {#wildlife .SchedHeadL2}

- **27** In a wildlife area, construction, decommissioning or
  abandonment of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an electrical generating station or electrical
    transmission line;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a dam, dike, reservoir or other structure for the
    diversion of water;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an oil or natural gas facility or oil or natural
    gas pipeline;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a mine or mill;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a nuclear facility;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an industrial undertaking;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a marine terminal;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a railway line or public road;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an aerodrome; or

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a waste management facility.

- **28** Importing wildlife from outside Canada that are non-indigenous
  to Yukon and that are to be introduced into the natural environment.

- **29** Transporting from Yukon live wildlife, other than wood bison,
  that are indigenous to Yukon and that are an endangered species or a
  threatened species.

#### [Transportation]{.HTitleText2} {#transportation .SchedHeadL2}

- **30** Construction or decommissioning of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an aerodrome located in the developed area of a
    community; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an airport.

- **31** Flying of military fixed-wing jet aircraft as part of a
  training program at an altitude below 330 m above ground level if the
  number of flying hours planned is more than 25 hours in a calendar
  year.

- **32** Construction of a railway line.

- **33** Construction of an all-season public road more than 50 km in
  length if the road

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not on a right of way developed for a power
    line, pipeline, railway line or road nor on a right of way
    contiguous to, for its whole length, a right of way developed for a
    power line, pipeline, railway line or road; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} will lead to a community that lacks all-season
    public road access.

- **34** Construction of a bridge on the Yukon, Pelly, Stewart, White,
  Teslin, Liard, Peel, Takhini or Porcupine River.

#### [Nuclear Facilities and Nuclear Substances]{.HTitleText2} {#nuclear-facilities-and-nuclear-substances .SchedHeadL2}

- **35** Preparation of a site for, or construction, operation,
  modification, decommissioning or abandonment of, a nuclear facility.

- **36** Abandonment, disposal or release into the environment of a
  nuclear substance, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nuclear substance is readily removable from and
    is not distributed throughout a substance, material, a device or
    equipment, and the quantity of the nuclear substance exceeds its
    exemption quantity; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the nuclear substance is distributed throughout,
    and is not readily removable from, the substance, material, device
    or equipment, and the concentration of the nuclear substance
    delivers an effective dose that exceeds 1% of the effective dose
    limit set out in column 3 of item 3 of the table to subsection 13(1)
    of the Radiation Protection Regulations.

- **37** Possession or use of, or other activity in relation to the
  possession or use of, a nuclear substance that is an unsealed source
  in a quantity exceeding 1 PBq (10^15^ Bq).

- **38** Abandonment or disposal of a substance, material, a device or
  equipment the surface of which is contaminated with a nuclear
  substance that is not readily removable from the surface, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the contamination exceeds 3 Bq/cm^2^ averaged over
    a surface of not more than 100 cm^2^; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the dose rate at the surface of the substance,
    material, device or equipment exceeds 1 µSv/h.

#### [Contaminants and Waste]{.HTitleText2} {#contaminants-and-waste .SchedHeadL2}

- **39** Construction, decommissioning or abandonment of a facility used
  exclusively for the treatment, incineration, disposal, recycling or
  storage of special waste if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its treatment, incineration, disposal or recycling
    capacity is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 20 t/year or more of waste oil,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 10 t/year or more of special waste, other than
      waste oil, generated as a result of the repair and maintenance of
      motor vehicles, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 5 t/year or more of special waste not described
      in subparagraphs (i) and (ii); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its storage capacity is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 20 t or more of waste oil,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 10 t or more of special waste, other than waste
      oil, generated as a result of the repair and maintenance of motor
      vehicles, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 5 t or more of special waste not described in
      subparagraphs (i) and (ii).

- **40** Expansion of a facility used exclusively for the treatment,
  incineration, disposal or recycling of special waste that increases
  its capacity by more than 35% if its treatment, incineration, disposal
  or recycling capacity is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 20 t/year or more of waste oil;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 10 t/year or more of special waste, other than
    waste oil, generated as a result of the repair and maintenance of
    motor vehicles; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 5 t/year or more of special waste not described in
    paragraphs (a) and (b).

#### [Water]{.HTitleText2} {#water .SchedHeadL2}

- **41** Drilling, decommissioning or abandonment of a well that
  extracts 200 000 m^3^/year or more of groundwater.

- **42** Expansion of a well for the extraction of groundwater that
  increases its extraction capacity by more than 35% and increases the
  extraction capacity to 200 000 m^3^/year or more.

- **43** Construction, decommissioning or abandonment of a facility or
  installation, other than a well, that extracts 200 000 m^3^/year or
  more of groundwater.

- **44** Expansion of a facility or installation, other than a well, for
  the extraction of groundwater that increases its extraction capacity
  by more than 35% and increases the extraction capacity to
  200 000 m^3^/year or more.

- **45** Diverting 10 000 000 m^3^/year or more of water from one
  natural water body to another.

- **46** Increasing, by more than 35%, the capacity of a diversion that
  diverts 10 000 000 m^3^/year or more of water from one natural water
  body to another.

- **47** Construction, decommissioning or abandonment of a dam or dike
  having a reservoir with a surface area that exceeds the annual mean
  surface area of the natural water body by 100 ha or more.

- **48** Expansion of a dam or dike that results in the surface area of
  a reservoir increasing by more than 35% and exceeding the annual mean
  surface area of the natural water body by more than 100 ha.

- **49** The deposit of waste into surface water, or in any other place
  under conditions in which the waste, or any other waste that results
  from the deposit, can enter surface water if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the deposit is related to placer mining in which
    chemicals are used during the mineral recovery process; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the deposit is related to a community undertaking
    and the waste is deposited by means of a disposal system that serves
    500 persons or more and is installed in accordance with the Sewage
    Disposal Systems Regulations, Yukon O.I.C. 1999/82.

#### [National Parks, National Park Reserves and National Historic Sites]{.HTitleText2} {#national-parks-national-park-reserves-and-national-historic-sites .SchedHeadL2}

- **50** In a national park, national park reserve or national historic
  site, the construction, operation, modification, decommissioning or
  abandonment of, or other activity in relation to, a structure,
  facility or installation if the activity is contrary to a management
  plan tabled in each House of Parliament in accordance with section 32
  of the [Parks Canada Agency Act](/eng/acts/P-0.4) or section 11 of the
  [Canada National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01).

- **51** In a national park or national park reserve, the expansion of
  the size of an area that is used for golfing or an expansion of the
  number of holes that are used for golfing in that area.

#### [Miscellaneous]{.HTitleText2} {#miscellaneous .SchedHeadL2}

- **52** On other than Crown land and settlement land, the construction,
  operation, modification, decommissioning or abandonment of a storage
  tank system for petroleum products that has a capacity that is, and in
  the case of a modification remains, more than 250 000 000 L.

- **53** On Crown land or settlement land, the establishment of a
  petroleum fuel storage facility, or the storage of petroleum fuel in a
  container, that has a capacity of more than 250 000 000 L.

- **54** Operation, decommissioning or abandonment, or an expansion that
  increases the production capacity by more than 35%, of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a gravel or sand pit with a production capacity of
    500 000 m^3^/year or more; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a stone quarry with a production capacity of
    1 000 000 t/year or more.

- **55** Cutting 20 000 m^3^ or more of standing or fallen trees or
  removing that amount of fallen or cut trees.

- **56** Other than in a training area, test establishment or range
  established on January 18, 1995 for the testing of weapons by the
  Minister of National Defence under section 4 of the [National Defence
  Act](/eng/acts/N-5), testing of weapons for military purposes.

- **57** Construction, outside of a military base, of a training area,
  test establishment or range for the testing of weapons or for military
  training.

- **58** Construction of a military base or of a station outside of a
  military base.

- **59** Decommissioning of a military base or station.

- **60** Expansion of a military base or station that increases its area
  by more than 25% or increases the cumulative footprint of its existing
  buildings by more than 25%.
