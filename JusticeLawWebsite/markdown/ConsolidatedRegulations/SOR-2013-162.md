## [Withdrawal of Entitlement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-793931 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Entitlement to the benefit
of the Least Developed Country Tariff is withdrawn in respect of all
goods that originate in Equatorial Guinea and Maldives.

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-793933 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Section 1 does not apply to
goods that were in transit to Canada before January 1, 2015.

## [Amendments to the Customs Tariff Schedule]{.HTitleText1} {#h-793935 .Part}

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-793937 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on January 1, 2015.
