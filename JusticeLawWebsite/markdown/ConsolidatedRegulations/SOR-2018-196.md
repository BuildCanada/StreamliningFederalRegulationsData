## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-853046 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[military equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[military equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an aircraft, ship,
    submarine or land vehicle designed to be used in combat or in a
    combat support role. ([équipement militaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Nuclear Safety and Control
    Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3). ([installation nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Non-application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-853051 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}In transit

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations do not apply to asbestos or a product containing asbestos
  that is in transit through Canada, from a place outside Canada to
  another place outside Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mining residues and pest control
  products

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 6, to mining residues; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to a [[pest control product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
    as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Pest Control Products
    Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Integrated asbestos

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations, other than sections 5 and 8, do not apply to asbestos
  that is integrated into a structure or infrastructure if the
  integration occurred before the day on which these Regulations come
  into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Asbestos product in use

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to a product
  containing asbestos and used before the day on which these Regulations
  come into force but section 5 applies to the asbestos contained in
  that product.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-853064 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Import, sale or use

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 7 to
21, a person must not import, sell or use

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} processed asbestos fibres;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a product containing processed asbestos fibres unless
  those fibres are the result of the degradation of asbestos integrated
  into a product, a structure or infrastructure; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a consumer product containing asbestos in greater
  than trace amounts.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manufacture

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A person must not
manufacture

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 8, a product containing processed
  asbestos fibres unless those fibres are the result of the degradation
  of asbestos integrated into a product, a structure or infrastructure
  before the coming into force of these Regulations; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a consumer product containing asbestos in greater
  than trace amounts.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mining residues --- landscaping or
construction

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not sell, for use in construction or landscaping, asbestos mining
  residues that are located at an asbestos mining site or accumulation
  area unless the use is authorized by the province in which the
  construction or landscaping is to occur.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mining residues --- products containing
  asbestos

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person must not use asbestos mining residues to
  manufacture a product containing asbestos.

## [Exclusions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-853079 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The physical possession or
control of the following may be transferred to allow its disposal:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} processed asbestos fibres;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a product containing processed asbestos fibres; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a consumer product containing asbestos in greater
  than trace amounts.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Roads

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Asbestos may be reused in
the restoration of asbestos mining sites or in road infrastructure,
including mounds and noise barriers, if that asbestos was integrated
into road infrastructure before the day on which these Regulations came
into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Importing military equipment

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  may import, sell or use military equipment that was serviced with a
  product containing processed asbestos fibres if the product was used
  to service the military equipment while it was outside Canada for the
  purposes of a military operation and there was no technically or
  economically feasible asbestos-free alternative available at that time
  in that place.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who imports military equipment in accordance
  with subsection (1) must submit to the Minister, before March 31 of
  the calendar year following the calendar year of the importation, a
  report that includes the following elements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the importer's name, civic address in Canada and
    postal address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the individual who is authorized to act
    for that importer and their title or rank, civic address in Canada,
    postal address, telephone number and email address;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and a description of the military
    equipment that was imported in the calendar year to which the report
    relates;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and a description of each type of product
    containing processed asbestos fibres that was used to service the
    military equipment that was imported in the calendar year to which
    the report relates;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the estimated concentration and mass of asbestos in
    each product containing processed asbestos fibres that was used to
    service the military equipment that was imported in the calendar
    year to which the report relates and the unit of measurement used to
    describe that concentration and mass;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number of products containing processed
    asbestos fibres that were used to service the military equipment
    that was imported in the calendar year to which the report relates,
    by type of product;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that there was no
    technically or economically feasible asbestos-free alternative
    available at the time and place of the servicing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[military
  operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), [[military
  operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any operation taken to protect
  national security, support humanitarian relief efforts, participate in
  multilateral military or peacekeeping activities under the auspices of
  international organizations or defend a member state of the North
  Atlantic Treaty Organization.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Servicing military equipment

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may import, sell or use products containing processed asbestos
  fibres to service military equipment before January 1, 2023, if there
  is no technically or economically feasible asbestos-free alternative
  available when the product is imported, sold or used, as the case may
  be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use or sale of military equipment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person may use or sell the military equipment
  referred to in subsection (1) if it was serviced using a product
  referred to in that subsection before January 1, 2023.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report and management plan

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who uses a product containing processed
  asbestos fibres to service military equipment in accordance with
  subsection (1) must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prepare and implement an asbestos management plan
    that meets the requirements set out in Schedule 1; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} submit to the Minister, before March 31 of the
    calendar year following the calendar year in which the product is
    used, a report that includes the elements set out in subsection (4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of report

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The report must include the following elements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, civic address in Canada and postal
    address of the person using the product containing processed
    asbestos fibres;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the individual who is authorized to act
    for that person and their title or rank, civic address in Canada,
    postal address, telephone number and email address;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and a description of the military
    equipment that was serviced with the products containing processed
    asbestos fibres in the calendar year to which the report relates;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and a description of each type of product
    containing processed asbestos fibres that was used to service the
    military equipment in the calendar year to which the report relates;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the estimated concentration and mass of asbestos in
    each product containing processed asbestos fibres that was used to
    service the military equipment in the calendar year to which the
    report relates and the unit of measurement used to describe that
    concentration and mass;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number of products containing processed
    asbestos fibres that were used to service the military equipment in
    the calendar year to which the report relates, by type of product;
    and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that there was no
    technically or economically feasible asbestos-free alternative
    available at the time the military equipment was serviced and that
    an asbestos management plan that meets the requirements set out in
    Schedule 1 has been implemented.

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person may use or sell the equipment referred to in

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Museum display

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may import, sell or use the following if it is intended for
  display in a museum:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} processed asbestos fibres;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a product containing processed asbestos fibres; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a consumer product containing asbestos in greater
    than trace amounts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report and management plan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who displays processed asbestos fibres
  referred to in paragraph (1)(a) in a museum must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prepare and implement an asbestos management plan

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} submit to the Minister, before March 31 of the

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of report

    meets the requirements set out in Schedule 1 has been implemented.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laboratory use

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may use the following in a laboratory in scientific research,
  for sample characterization or as an analytical standard and may
  import or sell it for such use:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} processed asbestos fibres;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a product containing processed asbestos fibres; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a consumer product containing asbestos in greater
    than trace amounts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Asbestos management plan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who uses processed asbestos fibres referred
  to in paragraph (1)(a) in a laboratory in scientific research or as an
  analytical standard must prepare and implement an asbestos management
  plan that meets the requirements set out in Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person must submit a report to the Minister that
  includes the elements referred to in subsection (5) before March 31 of
  the calendar year following the calendar year in which they

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} imported processed asbestos fibres referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a) or a product referred to in paragraph (1)(b) or (c)
    for use in a laboratory in scientific research or as an analytical
    standard if the import occurred after the coming into force of these
    Regulations; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} used processed asbestos fibres referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a) or a product referred to in paragraph (1)(b) or (c)
    in a laboratory in scientific research or as an analytical standard
    if those fibres or that product was imported after the coming into
    force of these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Previously reported asbestos

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (3) does not apply in respect of processed
  asbestos fibres referred to in paragraph 1(a) or products referred to
  in paragraph (1)(b) or (c) for which the elements set out in
  subsection (5) have been submitted to the Minister in a previous
  report by the same person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of report

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The report must include the following elements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, civic address in Canada and postal
    address of the laboratory or the importer, as the case may be;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the individual who is authorized to act
    for the laboratory or the importer and their title, civic address in
    Canada, postal address, telephone number and email address;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the person submitting the report is described in
    paragraph (3)(a)

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the forms and quantities of processed asbestos
      fibres that they imported in the calendar year to which the report
      relates and the unit of measurement used to describe those
      quantities, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name, description and number of the products
      referred to in paragraph (1)(b) or (c) that they imported in the
      calendar year to which the report relates (by type of product),
      the estimated concentration and mass of asbestos in each product
      and the unit of measurement used to describe that concentration
      and mass; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the person submitting the report is described in
    paragraph (3)(b)

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the forms and quantities of processed asbestos
      fibres that they used in the calendar year to which the report
      relates and the unit of measurement used to describe those
      quantities,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name, description and number of the products
      referred to in paragraph (1)(b) or (c) that were used in the
      calendar year to which the report relates(by type of product), the
      estimated concentration and mass of asbestos in each product and
      the unit of measurement used to describe that concentration and
      mass,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating whether the processed
      asbestos fibres or the products referred to in paragraph (1)(b)
      or (c) were used in the calendar year to which the report relates
      in scientific research, as an analytical standard or both, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if the person used processed asbestos fibres, a
      statement that an asbestos management plan that meets the
      requirements set out in Schedule 1 has been implemented.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chlor-alkali facilities

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Asbestos management plan

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who imports or uses processed asbestos
  fibres for use in diaphragms to be used in a chlor-alkali facility in
  accordance with subsection (1) must submit to the Minister, before
  March 31 of the calendar year following the calendar year in which the
  fibres are imported or used, a report that includes the following
  elements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the facility's name, civic address in Canada and
    postal address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the individual who is authorized to act
    for the facility and their title, civic address in Canada, postal
    address, telephone number and email address;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the forms and quantities of any processed asbestos
    fibres imported in the calendar year to which the report relates and
    the unit of measurement used to describe those quantities;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number and the percentage of cells in the
    facility that have been converted to asbestos-free diaphragms; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement that an asbestos management plan that
    meets the requirements set out in Schedule 1 has been implemented.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Labelling

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person who imports or uses processed asbestos
  fibres for use in diaphragms used in a chlor-alkali facility in
  accordance with subsection (1) must ensure that each container of the
  fibres is labelled in accordance with Schedule 2.

## [Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-853194 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Asbestos management plan

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who applies for a permit referred to in subsection 16(1),
  17(1), 18(1), 19(1), 20(1) or 21(1) must prepare an asbestos
  management plan that meets the requirements set out in Schedule 1 and
  must implement the plan before they import or use the processed
  asbestos fibres, the product containing processed asbestos fibres or
  the consumer product containing asbestos in greater than trace
  amounts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use or sale --- imported fibres and
  products

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person may use or sell fibres or products
  referred to in subsection 16(1) that were imported under the authority
  of a permit issued under subsection 16(3) if the use or sale is
  consistent with the purpose of the permit, unless that permit has been
  revoked under subsection 23(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale --- fibres and products

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any person may sell fibres or products referred to in
  subsection 17(1) whose use is authorized by a permit issued under
  subsection 17(3) if the sale is consistent with the purpose of the
  permit, unless that permit has been revoked under subsection 23(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use --- sold fibres and products

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person who purchases fibres or products that were
  sold in accordance with subsection (3) may use them if the use is
  consistent with the purpose of the permit issued under subsection
  17(3), unless that permit has been revoked under subsection 23(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale to permit holder

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person may sell a product referred to in subsection
  18(1), 19(1), 20(1) or 21(1) to the holder of a permit issued under
  subsection 18(3), 19(3), 20(3) or 21(3), as the case may be, if the
  sale is consistent with the purpose of the permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use and sale --- equipment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Any person may use or sell equipment that was
  serviced with a product containing processed asbestos fibres under the
  authority of a permit issued under subsection 18(3), 19(3), 20(3) or
  21(3), unless the permit that allowed the product to be imported or
  used, as the case may be, has been revoked under subsection 23(1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection of environment or human health
--- import

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  permit issued under subsection (3) authorizes its holder to import the
  following to protect the environment or human health if there was no
  technically or economically feasible asbestos-free alternative
  available at the time the permit application was submitted:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} processed asbestos fibres;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a product containing processed asbestos fibres; and
