## Minor Waters Order {#minor-waters-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1448
:::

[CANADA SHIPPING ACT, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15)

Order Specifying Certain Waters on the Sea Coasts of Canada as Minor
Waters
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521104 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Minor Waters Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1448).

## [Minor Waters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521106 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following sheltered
waters on the sea coasts of Canada are hereby specified as minor waters
of Canada:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in British Columbia,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Alberni Inlet and the eastern channel of Barclay
    Sound as far west as Bamfield Inlet,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Quatsino Sound and all waters connected therewith
    as far west as Koprino Harbour,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} False Creek, Vancouver, east of Burrard Bridge,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} Jervis Inlet inside a line drawn between Thunder
    Point and Ball Point and all waters connected therewith not seaward
    of Fox Island in Telescope passage, including the Agamemnon Channel
    and Pender Harbour inside a line drawn between Fearney Point and
    Moore Point, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} Prince Rupert Harbour as far south as Charles
    Point;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in New Brunswick,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Saint John Harbour inside the southern breakwater
    and inside a line drawn between the southern extremity of the
    northern breakwater and the most easterly point of Partridge Island,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Shediac Harbour westward of a line drawn between
    Pointe du Chêne and Caissie Point,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} Miramichi Bay westward of a line drawn from the
    eastern shore of Neguac Beach to the eastern shores of Portage and
    Fox Islands and thence to the western point of Preston Beach,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} Nepisiquit Bay inside a line drawn between Alston
    and Carron Points,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} Dalhousie Harbour and the Restigouche River
    westward of a line drawn from Maguacha Point in the Province of
    Quebec to the mouth of Charlo River,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} Shippigan Sound inside the breakwater at Shippigan
    Gulley and south of a line drawn between Grasse Point and Pokesudi
    Point,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} Miscou Harbour east of a line drawn between
    Herring Point and Mya Point, and

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} Passamoquoddy Bay as far as Campobello Island
    and inside a line drawn at the northern entrance between east Quoddy
    Head and Deadman Head;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in Newfoundland,

  Humber Arm, east of a line drawn from Frenchman's Head triangulation
  station to McIver's Point triangulation station;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in Nova Scotia,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Bras d'Or Lake, Great Bras d'Or and all waters
    connected therewith inside a line joining Carey and Noir Points, and
    northwards of the seaward end of St. Peters Canal,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Annapolis Basin and Digby Gut inside a line
    between Prim Point lighthouse and Victoria Beach at the entrance to
    Digby Gut, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} Halifax Harbour and the waters inside a line
    joining the triangulation station on Osborne Head and the eastern
    extremity of Chebucto Head;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the Northwest Territories,

  Kugmallit Bay, south of a line drawn from the northern tip of the
  peninsula adjoining Kidluit Bay to Toker Point; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in Prince Edward Island,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Charlottetown Harbour inside Canseaux and Battery
    Points,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Summerside Harbour inside a line between Phelan
    Point and Indian Head Breakwater, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} Cardigan Bay inside a line drawn between Panmure
    Head and Red Point.
