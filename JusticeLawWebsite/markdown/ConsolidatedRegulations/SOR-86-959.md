## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899389 .Part}

**[[RULE 2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-RULE_2 .sectionLabel}** In these Rules,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chief Administrator
    appointed under section 5 of the [Courts Administration Service
    Act](/eng/acts/C-45.5), or a person acting on their
    behalf; ([administrateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[appellant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appellant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person referred to in
    section 248.9 of the Act on whose behalf a decision or order is
    appealed from, a person on whose behalf a petition has been referred
    to the Court under subsection 249.16(2) of the Act or a person on
    whose behalf a Notice of Appeal is delivered;
    ([appelant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person, other than an
    appellant, on whose behalf an application under Division 3 or 10 of
    Part III of the Act is filed, or a person on whose behalf a Notice
    of Motion is filed; ([requérant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[counsel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[counsel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of the bar of a
    province; ([avocat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Court Martial Appeal Court
    of Canada; ([Cour]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Court Martial Administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Court Martial Administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person
    appointed under section 165.18 of the Act; ([administrateur de la
    cour martiale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[holiday]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[holiday]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Saturday or any other day
    defined as a [[holiday]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 35(1)
    of the [Interpretation Act](/eng/acts/I-21); ([jour
    férié]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Judicial Administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Judicial Administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the officer of
    the Registry so designated pursuant to Rule 39; ([administrateur
    judiciaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Minister of National Defence
    and includes any person instructed to exercise the right of appeal
    under section 230.1 of the Act; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Notice of Appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Notice of Appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Notice of Appeal
    that is required by section 232 of the Act, the form of which is
    prescribed by paragraph 115.08(2) of the Queen's Regulations and
    Orders for the Canadian Forces; ([avis d'appel]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the original minutes of the proceedings of the
      court martial or of the hearing under Division 3 or 10 of Part III
      of the Act,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all documents and records relevant to the appeal
      or application that were attached to the original minutes,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to Rule 6.1, all other records and
      exhibits filed at the court martial or hearing under Division 3 or
      10 of Part III of the Act, and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all documents and records relevant to any
      application under Division 3 or 10 of Part III of the Act;
      ([Dossier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Registry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Registry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2022-253, s.
    2\]]{.Repealed}

:   [[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a party to a proceeding

    a notice of intention to intervene pursuant to subsection (3) of

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899412 .Part}

**[[RULE 2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-RULE_2.1 .sectionLabel}** These Rules
apply to all proceedings before the Court.

::: HistoricalNote
- [SOR/2022-253, s. 3](#1382241-1388243){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1382241-1388243.html"
  role="button"}
:::

- **[[RULE 3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-RULE_3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These Rules are intended to render
  effective the substantive law and to ensure that it is carried out;
  they shall be liberally construed to secure the just, expeditious and
  economical resolution of each proceeding.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Court may, on motion or its own initiative,
  provide for any procedural matter that is not provided for in these
  Rules or in an Act of Parliament by analogy to these Rules or by
  reference to the practice of the superior court of the province to
  which the subject matter of the proceeding most closely relates.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A form that is required by these Rules may
  incorporate any variations that are necessary in the circumstances.

::: HistoricalNote
- [SOR/2022-253, s. 4](#1382243-1387901){.wb-lbx

among themselves according to the dates upon which they respectively
became judges of the Court.

## [Time and Place of Hearings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899418 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2022-253, s. 5

\]
:::

- **[[RULE 4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-RULE_4.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For each proceeding in which a
  hearing is to be held, the Chief Justice shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} designate each judge that will sit at the hearing;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make an order fixing the date, time, place and
    manner of the hearing.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} A hearing shall be conducted, in whole or in part,
  in person, by telephone or by videoconference.

- [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} The Court may give any directions that are
  necessary to facilitate the conduct of the hearing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registry shall send a copy of the order referred
  to in paragraph (1)(b) to the Court Martial Administrator and the
  parties.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A judge, prior to a hearing, or the Court, at the
  hearing, may postpone the hearing if it is considered just in the
  circumstances. The Registry shall notify the Court Martial
  Administrator and the parties of the postponement.

## [Form of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899425 .Part}

- **[[RULE 4.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-RULE_4.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This Rule applies in respect of a
  document, other than the Record, the appeal book or a petition
  referred to in subsection 249.16(2) of the Act, that is prepared for
  use in a proceeding.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The document shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be legible;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is in type, be in Times New Roman, Arial or
    Tahoma font that is at least 12 points in size or, in the case of
    footnotes, at least 10 points in size;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be dated;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} have a cover page that sets out the Court file
    number, the style of cause and the document's title;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if there is more than one component to the
    document, have a table of contents; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} set out the name, address, telephone and fax
    numbers and email address of the counsel who is filing the document
    or, if the party is not represented by counsel, of the party.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the document is on paper, each page of the
  document shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be printed on good quality white or off-white paper
    measuring 21.5 cm by 28 cm; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a document other than a book of
    authorities,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} have top and bottom margins of not less than
      2.5 cm and left and right margins of not less than 3.5 cm,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} be printed on one side of the paper only, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} have no more than 30 lines, exclusive of
      headings.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A memorandum of fact and law that is on paper shall
  have

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the appellant's memorandum, a beige
    cover;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the respondent's memorandum, a green
    cover; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an intervener's memorandum, a blue
    cover.

- [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} If the document is in electronic format, it shall
  be formatted in PDF (Portable Document Format), or in any other format
  approved by the Court, such that the document can be searched
  electronically and converted for printing on paper measuring 21.5 cm
  by 28 cm, with each page of the document meeting the requirements set
  out in paragraph (3)(b).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No Memorandum of Fact and Law shall exceed 30 pages
  in length, and no Memorandum in Reply shall exceed 10 pages in length,
  exclusive in each case of the list of authorities, unless the Court,
  if it is considered just in the circumstances, permits otherwise.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} All documents filed in the Registry shall be signed
  by the counsel for the party, or by the party, if the party is not
  represented by counsel.

## [Commencement of Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899444 .Part}

- **[[RULE 5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-RULE_5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A file shall be opened by the
  Registry without delay upon receipt by the Registry of the first of
  the following documents:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application for review under section 159.9 of
    the Act that sets out the date of the direction to be reviewed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a Notice of Appeal that sets out the date of the
    decision appealed from;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} an application under section 248.2 of the Act for
    release until the determination of the appeal;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an application for review under section 248.8 of
    the Act that sets out the date of the undertaking to be reviewed;

  - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} an application under section 248.81 of the Act to
    address a breach or likely breach of an undertaking; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a Notice of Motion commencing an appeal under
    section 248.9 of the Act that sets out the date of the decision or
    order appealed from.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within 10 days after a document referred to in
  subsection (1) is filed, the appellant or applicant shall serve a copy
  of the document on the respondent and the Court Martial Administrator
  and, within 10 days after serving the document, file proof of service
  with the Registry.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A respondent who intends to participate in an appeal
  or review shall, within 15 days after service of the document referred
  to in subsection (1), serve on the appellant or applicant and the
  Court Martial Administrator, and file with the Registry,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a notice of appearance in the form set out in
    Schedule 1; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the respondent seeks a different disposition
    of the order appealed from or under review, a notice of cross-appeal
    in the form set out in Schedule 2.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a document referred to in subsection (1) is
  filed in respect of a decision that is already the subject of an
  appeal or application, that document is deemed to be the notice
  required by subsection (3) and shall be placed on the file of the
  first proceeding.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} All proceedings before the Court under Divisions 3,
  9, 10 and 11 of Part III of the Act arising out of or relating to a
  decision of a court martial, or a direction of a military judge, in
  respect of the same charge or charges against a person shall be placed
  on the file opened under subsection (1).

## [Obligations of the Court Martial Administrator]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899459 .Part}

- **[[RULE 6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-RULE_6
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within 30 days after being served
  with a Notice of Appeal, the Court Martial Administrator shall cause a
  Memorandum of Particulars, prepared in accordance with Schedule 3, to
  be served on the parties and filed with the Registry.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (3) and (4), the Court Martial
  Administrator shall cause the Record to be forwarded to the Registry
  within 90 days after the day on which the Court Martial Administrator
  is served with a copy of a document under subsection 5(2) or a copy of
  documents and a petition under subsection 13.1(4).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} On the written request of the Court Martial
  Administrator made before the expiration of the time within which the
  Record must be forwarded, the Court may extend the time for doing so
  if it is considered just in the circumstances.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Court Martial Administrator shall not forward to
  the Registry any portion of the Record that the parties to the
  proceedings agree in writing is not relevant to any issue in a
  proceeding.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Court Martial Administrator shall, when causing
  the Record to be forwarded to the Registry, file with the Registry
  five paper copies of the appeal book, prepared and certified in
  accordance with Schedule 4, and serve a copy of the book on the
  parties and file proof of service with the Registry within 10 days
  after the day on which the book is served.

- **[[RULE 6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-RULE_6.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Court Martial Administrator
  shall include in the Record and in the appeal book a photograph or a
  written description of any exhibit entered at the court martial or
  hearing under Division 3 or 10 of Part III of the Act that is not a
  document or that is a document that is impracticable to be forwarded
  to the Registry with the Record.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Court Martial Administrator shall cause every
  exhibit referred to in subsection (1) to be produced at the hearing,
  unless excused from doing so by agreement of the parties or by an
  order that is applied for by a party at least 15 days before the date
  fixed for the hearing.

## [Appellant's Memorandum of Fact and Law]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899473 .Part}

- **[[RULE 7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-RULE_7
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within 30 days after the day on
  which the appellant is served with the appeal book, the appellant
  shall serve a copy of their Memorandum of Fact and Law on the
  respondent and file five paper copies of it with the Registry, unless
  it is filed by electronic transmission.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appellant's Memorandum of Fact and Law shall
  contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the facts of the case;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the arguments the appellant proposes to make to the
    Court;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} appropriate references to the appeal book; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a list of the statutes, regulations and authorities
    on which the appellant intends to rely in their argument.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The appellant may, in the Memorandum of Fact and Law,
  give notice of intention to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} argue grounds of appeal not contained in the Notice
    of Appeal; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} abandon any grounds of appeal contained in the
    Notice of Appeal.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any new grounds the appellant proposes to argue shall
  be clearly identified as such and shall be set out in the Memorandum
  of Fact and Law.

## [Quashing an Appeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899488 .Part}

- **[[RULE 8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-RULE_8
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A respondent may, within 15 days
  after being served with the appellant's Memorandum of Fact and Law,
  apply for an order quashing the appeal on the grounds that no
  substantial grounds of appeal have been shown.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A judge may at any time order an appellant to show
  cause why an appeal ought not be quashed on the grounds that no
  substantial grounds of appeal have been shown.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where on service of a notice of motion in respect of
  an order referred to in subsection (1) or on the making of a show
  cause order to quash referred to in subsection (2), unless a judge
  orders otherwise, no further proceedings in the appeal shall take
  place until the issue is disposed of, and the time for taking further
  proceedings shall be extended accordingly.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-152, s. 2

## [Respondent's Memorandum of Fact and Law]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899495 .Part}

- **[[RULE 9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-RULE_9
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within 30 days after the day on
  which the respondent is served with the appellant's Memorandum of Fact
  and Law, the respondent shall serve a copy of their Memorandum of Fact
  and Law on the appellant and file five paper copies of it with the
  Registry, unless it is filed by electronic transmission.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The respondent's Memorandum of Fact and Law shall
  contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement in respect of the appellant's statement
    of facts indicating those accepted as correct and those not so
    accepted and giving a concise statement of any additional facts the
    respondent considers material to the appeal;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the arguments the respondent proposes to make to
    the Court;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} appropriate references to the appeal book; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a list of the statutes, regulations and authorities
    on which the respondent intends to rely in their argument.

## [Memorandum in Reply]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899505 .Part}

- **[[RULE 10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-RULE_10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the respondent has served a
  notice of cross-appeal and has, in their Memorandum of Fact and Law,
  advanced arguments in support of varying the decision under appeal,
  the appellant shall, within 30 days after the day on which they are
  served with the respondent's Memorandum of Fact and Law, serve a copy
  of the appellant's Memorandum in Reply on the respondent and file five
  paper copies of it with the Registry, unless it is filed by electronic
  transmission.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The Memorandum in Reply shall, with any necessary
  modifications as the circumstances require, contain the information
  referred to in subsection 9(2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as provided by subsection (1), no Memorandum
  in Reply shall be filed without leave of a judge applied for within
  10 days after the date of service of the respondent's Memorandum of
  Fact and Law.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is served within Canada by registered mail,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if it is served outside of Canada by registered

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if it is served within Canada by courier, on the

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if it is served outside Canada by courier, on the
    fourth day after the day on which the document is provided to the
    courier;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if it is served by fax, on the day of transmission
    that is indicated on the transmission confirmation receipt; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if it is served by email, on the day on which it is
    sent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except for a document that is served by personal

  time at the recipient's location, service of the document is effected
  on the next day that is not a holiday.

## [Filing of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899691 .Part}

- **[[RULE 23.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-RULE_23.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  documents shall be filed, with the Registry's principal office in
  Ottawa or with any other Registry office that is established by the
  Administrator, in person or by registered mail, courier, fax or
  electronic transmission.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appeal book referred to in subsection 6(5) shall
  not be filed with the Registry by electronic transmission.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The following documents may not be filed by fax
  without the consent of the Administrator, which consent may be given
  where filing by fax is required in order to allow a case to proceed
  expeditiously:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Record, appeal book, Memorandum of Fact and Law
    and Memorandum in Reply; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other document that is longer than 20 pages.

  page that sets out the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number and email
    address of the sender;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of transmission;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total number of pages transmitted, including
    the cover page;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number of the fax to which documents may be
    sent; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name and telephone number of a person to
    contact in the event of a transmission problem.

- [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} A document that is filed by electronic transmission
  shall be formatted in PDF (Portable Document Format), or in any other
  format approved by the Court, such that the document can be searched
  electronically, and the document shall be accompanied by the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the title of the document being filed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number and email
    address of the sender.

- [(4.2)]{.lawlabel} A party who files a document by electronic
  transmission shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide a paper copy of the document in person, by
    registered mail or by courier to the Registry within five days after
    the day on which the document is filed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if required by the Court, provide the Registry with
    the same number of paper copies of the document as would have been

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A document is not considered to have been filed until

- [(5.1)]{.lawlabel} Before dating a document under subsection (5), the

- **[[RULE 24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-RULE_24

## [Special Dispositions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899749 .Part}

- **[[RULE 28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-RULE_28
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Justice or the Court may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the appellant is not ready to proceed with
    his appeal when called upon to do so, dismiss the appeal;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the respondent is not ready to proceed with
    an appeal when called upon to do so, order the appellant to proceed
    [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"};

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} upon cause shown, postpone the hearing of the
    appeal to another time;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} upon motion, or without motion on its own
    initiative, dismiss an appeal where the appellant has failed to
    observe, without dispensation, any requirement of the Act or these
    Rules;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} extend or abridge any time limited by these Rules
    before or after the expiration of the time prescribed; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} make any order that justice requires.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), no order or judgment
  the effect of which is to dispose finally of an appeal, including an
  appeal under section 248.9 of the Act, shall be made, except by the
  Court.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When, in his opinion, there is a significant issue of
  law involved in the disposition of any matter which, by the Rules, is
  required to be disposed of by him, the Chief Justice may refer the
  matter to the Court for disposition or may state a case for its
  opinion.

## [Practice and Procedures at Hearings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899762 .Part}

of Motion, the Notice of Appeal or in the Memorandum of Fact and Law may
be argued, except by leave of the Court, and, save in exceptional cases,
leave shall not be granted unless reasonable notice of the additional

## [Judgments and Orders]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899785 .Part}

- **[[RULE 35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-RULE_35
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every judgment and order shall be
  committed to writing, signed by the presiding judge, and filed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When reasons for judgment are given in open court or
  when reasons for an order are given, they shall be committed to
  writing, signed by the judge who gave them and filed.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When a judge gives reasons that are not the reasons
  of the Court, he shall commit them to writing, sign and file them.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} When there is a dissent to the Court's decision,
  written reasons shall be given by both the majority and the dissenting
  judges.

- [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} Where a dissent to the Court's decision is based in
  whole or in part on a question of law, the grounds for the dissent
  shall be specified in the judgment issued by the Court.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} When judgment is reserved, written reasons for
  judgment shall be given and, unless subsection (4) applies, all the
  judges shall indicate, in writing, their concurrence in whole or in
  part or in the result by an appropriate endorsement on the reasons or
  by separate reasons.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A copy of every document filed under this Rule shall
  be sent without delay to each party by the Registry.

## [Abandonment of Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899796 .Part}

- **[[RULE 36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-RULE_36
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A party may abandon a proceeding
  begun by the party at any time by notice filed with the Registry and
  served on the respondent and the Court Martial Administrator.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of abandonment under subsection (1) shall be
  signed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by the party and a witness to the party's signature
    and be supported by an affidavit or other evidence of the witness as
    to the signature of the party; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the appellant is represented by counsel, by
    such counsel, who shall state in the notice that he has consulted
    his client and is authorized by his client to abandon the
    proceeding.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-91, s. 32
:::

**[[RULE 37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-RULE_37 .sectionLabel}** An appeal may
be considered to have been abandoned for want of prosecution where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Notice of Appeal does not, in the opinion of the
  Court, contain sufficient particulars of the grounds upon which it is
  founded and the appellant has failed, within a reasonable time, to
  comply with an order requiring the furnishing of such particulars;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the appellant has failed to include an address for
  service in the Notice of Appeal or, if the Notice of Appeal is not
  signed by counsel, counsel named to accept service refuses to do so;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the respondent has reason to believe that the
  appellant intends to abandon the appeal and files an affidavit
  deposing to that reason in the Registry and the appellant fails,
  within reasonable time, to respond to a request by the Registry for an
  expression of intention as to the abandonment or otherwise of the
  appeal; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the appellant has failed to appear at the time and
  place fixed for the hearing.

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} receiving and filing all papers, documents and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entering in an appropriate book provided for that

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} transmitting to each judge by whom an appeal is to

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} attending with all relevant records, exhibits and
    papers at sittings of the Court;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} keeping a full and correct record of all
    proceedings before the Court, showing the names of the judges of the
    Court present, the date and place, the names of counsel, the result

    hearing;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} causing a transcript of all [viva voce]{.otherLang

    Martial Administrator the original minutes of the proceedings of the

    Act;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (h), retaining possession of
    all papers, documents and exhibits in respect of an appeal, unless
    otherwise required by the Chief Justice; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} where an appeal has been taken to the Supreme Court
    of Canada, transmitting to it all papers, documents and exhibits in
    his possession required by that Court.

## [SCHEDULE 4]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsection 6(5))]{.OriginatingRef}[Appeal Book]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-899914 .scheduleLabel}

- [1]{.lawlabel} Any appeal book shall be prepared on white paper of
  good quality in such manner that printed pages are to the left as the
  book is read.

- [2]{.lawlabel} The type used in an appeal book shall be 12 point
  unless accounts or tabular matter is being printed, in which case
  10 point type shall be used and shall be clearly legible.

- [3]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2001-91, s. 37\]]{.Repealed}

- [4]{.lawlabel} The size of an appeal book shall be 21.5 cm by 28.

- [5]{.lawlabel} There shall be approximately 47 lines of print on each
  page of an appeal book and every tenth line shall be numbered in the
  margin.

- [6]{.lawlabel} An appeal book shall be arranged in the following
  sequence and shall consist of a red-coloured cover, a title page, an
  index and the following parts:

    ---------- --- ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Part I     1   Notice of Appeal, Notice of Motion commencing an appeal or application or Petition for a New Trial sent to the Court by the Minister, and notice of cross-appeal, if any.
               2   Memorandum of Particulars in accordance with Schedule 3.
               3   Any agreement referred to in subsection 6(4) of these Rules.
               4   Charges, arraignments, pleas, admissions, entries and orders at the court martial or hearing in chronological order.
    Part II        The evidence.
    Part III       The closing addresses, finding and disposition by the Court Martial or military judge.
    Part IV        The exhibits.
    Part V         The certificate of the Court Martial Administrator, as follows:
                   I, ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-solid}, hereby certify that the foregoing record of the case on appeal to the Court Martial Appeal Court of Canada is a true and correct reproduction of the originals of which they purport to be copies.

  and facsimile reproduced exhibits shall have a heading that states the

- [8]{.lawlabel} Where evidence is printed in an appeal book, the
  questions shall be preceded by the letter "Q", and the answer, which
  shall continue on the line which the question concludes, shall be
  preceded by the letter "A", if in English, or the letter "R", if in
