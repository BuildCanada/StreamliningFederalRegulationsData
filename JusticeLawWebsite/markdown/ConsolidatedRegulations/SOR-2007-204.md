## Regulations Implementing the United Nations Resolutions on Lebanon {#regulations-implementing-the-united-nations-resolutions-on-lebanon .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2007-204
:::

[UNITED NATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/U-2)

Registration 2007-09-18

Regulations Implementing the United Nations Resolutions on Lebanon

P.C. 2007-1352 2007-09-18
::::

::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Security Council of the United Nations, acting under Article
41 of the Charter of the United Nations, adopted Resolution 1701 (2006)
on August 11, 2006;

And whereas it appears to the Governor in Council to be necessary to
make regulations for enabling the measures set out in that Resolution to
be effectively applied;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to section 2
of the [United Nations Act](/eng/acts/U-2), hereby makes the annexed
[Regulations Implementing the United Nations Resolution on
Lebanon](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-204).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-735023 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[arms and related material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[arms and related material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any type of
    weapon, ammunition, military vehicle or military or paramilitary
    equipment, and includes their spare parts. ([armes et matériel
    connexe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who is a
    citizen within the meaning of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29)
    or a body corporate incorporated by or continued under the laws of
    Canada or a province. ([Canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Lebanon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Lebanon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Lebanese Republic and
    includes its political subdivisions. ([Liban]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Foreign
    Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual, a body
    corporate, a trust, a partnership, a fund, an unincorporated
    association or organization or a foreign state.
    ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Security Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Security Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Security Council
    of the United Nations. ([Conseil de sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Security Council Resolution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Security Council Resolution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2024-138, s. 19\]]{.Repealed}

[[technical assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[technical assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any form of
    assistance, such as providing instruction, training, consulting
    services or technical advice or transferring know-how or technical
    data. ([aide technique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[technical data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[technical data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes blueprints,
    technical drawings, photographic imagery, computer software, models,
    formulas, engineering designs and specifications, technical and
    operating manuals and any technical information. ([données
    techniques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-735032 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
binding on Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-735034 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No person in Canada and no
Canadian outside Canada shall knowingly export, sell, supply or ship,
directly or indirectly, arms and related material, wherever situated, to
any person in Lebanon.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No owner or master of a
Canadian vessel, as defined in section 2 of the [Canada Shipping Act,
2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15), and no operator of an aircraft registered in
Canada shall knowingly carry, cause to be carried or permit to be
carried, arms and related material, wherever situated, destined for any
person in Lebanon.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-23, s. 7
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person in Canada and no
Canadian outside Canada shall knowingly provide, directly or indirectly,
to any person in Lebanon technical assistance related to the provision,
manufacture, maintenance or use of arms and related material.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-23, ss. 8(E), 9
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-5-20070918.html "Link to previous version of section 5"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Sections 3 to 5 do not
apply in respect of arms and related material and related technical
assistance authorized in advance in writing by the Government of Lebanon
or by the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada to knowingly do anything
that causes, facilitates or assists in, or is intended to cause,
facilitate or assist in, any activity prohibited by sections 3 to 5.

**[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in these
Regulations prohibits the provision, processing or payment of funds,
other financial assets or economic resources or the provision of goods
and services that are necessary to ensure the timely delivery of
humanitarian assistance or to support other activities that support
basic human needs in Lebanon by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the United Nations, including its programs, funds and
  other entities and bodies, as well as its specialized agencies and
  related organizations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} international organizations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} humanitarian organizations that have been granted
  observer status by the United Nations General Assembly and members of
  those humanitarian organizations;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} bilaterally or multilaterally funded non-governmental
  organizations that are participating in any United Nations
  humanitarian response plan, refugee response plan or other United
  Nations appeals for assistance or in groups of humanitarian
  organizations coordinated by the United Nations Office for the
  Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs, including the employees,
  grantees, subsidiaries and implementing partners of those
  non-governmental organizations while acting in those capacities;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other person authorized for that purpose by a
  United Nations committee established by the Security Council acting
  within its mandate.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-735044 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  that wishes to engage in any activity that is prohibited under these
  Regulations may, before doing so, apply to the Minister in writing for
  a certificate to exempt the activity from the application of the
  Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must issue the certificate if the
  Security Council did not intend that such an activity be prohibited or
  if the activity has been approved in advance by the Security Council
  or the Committee of the Security Council established under Resolution
  1636 (2005) of October 31, 2005, adopted by the Security Council.

## [Application Prior to Publication]{.HTitleText1} {#h-735048 .Part}

paragraph 11(2)(a) of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22),
these Regulations apply according to their terms before they are
