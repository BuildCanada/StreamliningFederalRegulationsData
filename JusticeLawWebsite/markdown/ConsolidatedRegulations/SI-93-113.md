## Order Designating the Minister of Supply and Services as Minister for Purposes of the Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-supply-and-services-as-minister-for-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-113
:::

[TRANSLATION BUREAU ACT](/eng/acts/T-16)

Registration 1993-07-14

Order Designating the Minister of Supply and Services as Minister for
Purposes of the Act

P.C. 1993-1459  1993-06-25
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 2 of the [Translation Bureau
Act](/eng/acts/T-16), is pleased hereby to designate the Minister of
Supply and Services, a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada,
to act as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
