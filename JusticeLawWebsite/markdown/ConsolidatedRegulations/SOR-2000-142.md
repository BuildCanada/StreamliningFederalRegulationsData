## [Amount and Interest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656082 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 4.1(4) of the [Expropriation Act](/eng/acts/E-21), subsection
6(4) of the [Dry Docks Subsidies Act](/eng/acts/D-4), subsection 31(3.1)
of the [Northwest Territories Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-27.3), subsection
7(1.3) of the [Dominion Water Power Act](/eng/acts/W-4) and subsection
31(3.1) of the [Yukon Waters Act](/eng/acts/Y-4.6), for every billable
hour that each employee of the Department of Public Works and Government
Services assigned to an expropriation works on the expropriation, fees
are prescribed in the amount of twice the hourly rate of each employee
prorated from the employee's salary per diem, based on a productivity
rate of 220 days per year, plus employee benefits (20% of total
chargeable salaries).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The rate of interest
payable in respect of overdue fees shall be calculated in accordance
with section 5 of the [Interest and Administrative Charges
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-188).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656085 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
