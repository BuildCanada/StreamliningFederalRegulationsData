## Regulations Establishing the Period Within Which Owners of Copyright not Represented by Collective Societies Can Claim Retransmission Royalties {#regulations-establishing-the-period-within-which-owners-of-copyright-not-represented-by-collective-societies-can-claim-retransmission-royalties .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-164
:::

[COPYRIGHT ACT](/eng/acts/C-42)

Registration 1997-03-19

Regulations Establishing the Period Within Which Owners of Copyright not
Represented by Collective Societies Can Claim Retransmission Royalties
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Copyright Board, pursuant to paragraph 70.66(3)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea){#footnotea-ID0EFABA} of the [Copyright
Act](/eng/acts/C-42), hereby makes the annexed Regulations Establishing
the Period for Royalty Entitlements of Non-members of Collecting Bodies.

Ottawa, March 18, 1997

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea-ID0EFABA){#footnotea}S.C. 1988, c.
  65, s. 65
  :::
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The owner of copyright who
does not authorize a collective society to collect, for that person\'s
benefit, the royalties referred to in paragraph 31(2)(d) of the
[Copyright Act](/eng/acts/C-42) and whose work is so retransmitted has a
period of two years following the end of the calendar year in which the
retransmission occurred to claim the royalties payable under subsection
76(1) of that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-152, s. 2
:::

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on March 19, 1997.
