## Members of Parliament Disability Allowance Regulations {#members-of-parliament-disability-allowance-regulations .Title-of-Act}

Members of Parliament Disability Allowance Regulations

P.C. 2002-1742  2002-10-10

the Minister of State and Leader of the Government in the House of
Commons, pursuant to subsection 71.1(4)[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696439 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Clerk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Clerk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a member of the Senate, the Clerk
      of the Senate; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a member of the House of Commons,
      the Clerk of the House of Commons. ([greffier]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[disability allowance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[disability allowance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the allowance
    referred to in section 71.1 of the [Parliament of Canada
    Act](/eng/acts/P-1). ([allocation d'invalidité]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of the Senate or the
    House of Commons and includes a former member who resigned from the
    Senate or the House of Commons because of disability.
    ([parlementaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada as is designated as the Minister for the
    purposes of the [Parliament of Canada Act](/eng/acts/P-1).
    ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Determination of Eligibility]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696447 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-696448 .Subheading}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member
  may, in order to have their eligibility for a disability allowance
  determined, submit an application for a determination to that effect
  to the Clerk.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application shall be in writing and shall be
  accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a report of a medical examination performed, within
    the six months immediately preceding the day on which the
    application is made, by a physician authorized to practise medicine
    in the jurisdiction in which the examination was performed, which
    report shall certify that the member is incapable by reason of
    disability of performing their duties as a member; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} written evidence of the age of the member.

<!-- -->

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  member's application for a determination of eligibility is incomplete
  or if the report of the medical examination is incomplete or
  imprecise, the Clerk may request in writing that the member, within
  the time set out in the request, supply the missing information or
  undergo one or more supplementary medical examinations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member who does not comply with the Clerk's request
  within the time set out in the request, or within the extended time if
  an extension is granted under subsection 9(2), shall be considered to
  have abandoned their application.

### [Determination]{.HTitleText2} {#h-696457 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Clerk shall, on the basis of the member's application and any other
  documentation supplied in support of the application, recommend to the
  Minister whether or not the member is eligible for a disability
  allowance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the recommendation of the Clerk, the Minister
  shall request that the Governor in Council make a determination of
  eligibility in respect of the member.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The determination of eligibility by the Governor in
  Council shall be final in respect of the application to which it
  pertains. The determination shall be in writing and a copy shall be
  provided to the member.

### [New Applications]{.HTitleText2} {#h-696462 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A member who abandons ---
or who is considered to have abandoned --- their application for a
determination of eligibility for a disability allowance, who has, in
respect of a prior application, been determined by the Governor in
Council to be ineligible for a disability allowance, or who has revoked
an election to receive a disability allowance, is not prevented from
subsequently submitting a new application to the Clerk.

## [Election]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696464 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A member in respect of whom
the Governor in Council has made a determination of eligibility for a
disability allowance may, within 90 days after the day on which the
determination is made, make an election to receive the disability
allowance by sending a notice to that effect to the Clerk, which notice
shall be accompanied by details of the member's resignation.

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member
  who makes an election to receive a disability allowance before the
  Governor in Council has made a determination of eligibility shall send
  a notice to that effect to the Clerk and shall submit, with their
  notice, the documents referred to in paragraphs 2(2)(a) and (b).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 3 and 4 apply to a determination of
  eligibility in respect of a member who makes an election under
  subsection (1).

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Notice of an election to
receive a disability allowance shall be signed by the member, witnessed
and dated.

## [Extensions of Time]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696470 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member
  who is unable, for reasons beyond the member's control, to comply with
  a request of the Clerk under subsection 3(1) or make an election under
  section 6 within the time set out for doing so, may in a timely manner
  make a request in writing to the Clerk --- with evidence in support of
  those reasons --- for an extension of time.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Clerk shall grant the extension if the request
  and evidence in support establish that it is required.

## [Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696474 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every disability
allowance shall be paid in accordance with standard payment procedures
of the Senate or the House of Commons, as the case may be.

## [Revocation of Election]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696476 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A member who makes an
election to receive a disability allowance may revoke that election at
any time on written notice to the Clerk.

## [Representatives]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696478 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If a member is unable to
make any application, election or revocation under these Regulations on
their own behalf, a person authorized in writing to do so may act for
them, subject to the authorization being provided to the Clerk.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696480 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
