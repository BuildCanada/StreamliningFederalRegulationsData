## First Nations Commercial and Industrial Development Act {#first-nations-commercial-and-industrial-development-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2005, c. 53
:::

Assented to 2005-11-25

An Act respecting the regulation of commercial and industrial
undertakings on reserve lands
::::

::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS first nations are planning commercial and industrial
undertakings on reserve lands;

WHEREAS effective regulatory regimes are required for the governance of
such undertakings;

WHEREAS first nations have requested the Government of Canada to provide
for the establishment of such regimes to facilitate economic development
on their lands;

WHEREAS existing Acts of Parliament do not provide sufficient authority
for Canada or first nations to establish such regimes;

AND WHEREAS regulations in relation to a first nation's reserve lands
will be made under this Act only at the request of the first nation;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224290 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [First Nations Commercial and Industrial Development
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.64).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224293 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[first nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[first nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a band as defined in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([première
      nation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Indigenous
      Services. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[provincial body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[provincial body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a body established
      by the laws of a province. ([organisme
      provincial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[provincial official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[provincial official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a minister of
      the Crown in right of a province, a person employed by a province
      or a person appointed to, or employed by, a provincial
      body. ([fonctionnaire provincial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[reserve lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[reserve lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means lands within a reserve
      as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Indian
      Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([terres de réserve]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References to province

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Act, references to a province, in relation to
  reserve lands, are to the province in which the reserve lands are
  situated.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224306 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulation-making power

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations governing commercial or
  industrial undertakings that are located on reserve lands described in
  the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Included powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Regulations made under subsection (1) may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} designate a particular undertaking or a class of
    undertakings to which the regulations apply;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} confer any legislative, administrative, judicial or
    other power on any person or body that the Governor in Council
    considers necessary to effectively regulate the undertakings;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} confer on any person or body the power, exercisable
    in circumstances and subject to conditions similar to those
    applicable to the exercise of that power outside reserve lands under
    laws of the province,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to make orders to cease any work, comply with any
      provision of the regulations or remedy the consequences of a
      failure to comply with the regulations, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to do any work that the person or body considers
      necessary and to recover the costs of that work;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} fix, or prescribe the manner of calculating, the
    royalties or other charges to be paid to Her Majesty for the use and
    benefit of a first nation in respect of the exploitation of natural
    resources;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} fix, or prescribe the manner of calculating, the
    fees to be paid in respect of the undertakings to any person or
    body, and the rents or other charges to be paid to Her Majesty for
    the use and benefit of a first nation in respect of the use of the
    reserve lands;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} fix the rate of interest to be charged on amounts
    owing under the regulations;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} establish offences punishable on summary conviction
    for contraventions of the regulations, where similar acts or
    omissions constitute an offence under laws of the province
    applicable outside reserve lands, and set fines or terms of
    imprisonment or both for such offences not exceeding those
    applicable under laws of the province;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} establish administrative monetary penalties for
    contraventions of the regulations, where similar acts or omissions
    are punishable by similar penalties under laws of the province
    applicable outside reserve lands, and set the amount of those
    penalties not exceeding that imposed under laws of the province;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} confer on any person the power to inspect and
    search places and things and to seize and detain property, for the
    purpose of verifying compliance with the regulations, in
    circumstances and subject to conditions similar to those applicable
    to the exercise of that power under laws of the province applicable
    outside reserve lands;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} require that security be given or that a trust or
    other fund be established to secure the performance of any
    obligation arising under the regulations;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} prescribe rules respecting the confidentiality, or
    the disclosure, of any information obtained under the regulations;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} prescribe, or confer on any person or body the
    power to prescribe, rules of procedure for hearings to be held in
    relation to the undertakings, including rules for the issuance of
    subpoenas to require the appearance of persons and the production of
    documents and rules requiring that evidence be given under oath;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} set limits on the liability of, and establish
    defences and immunities for, any person or body exercising a power
    or performing a duty under the regulations;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} require that an assessment of the environmental
    effects of the undertakings be undertaken in circumstances where the
    [Impact Assessment Act](/eng/acts/I-2.75) does not apply, and
    establish a procedure to be followed in such an assessment;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} with respect to reserve lands that have been
    designated for the purposes of an undertaking under subsection 38(2)
    of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), authorize --- to the extent
    permitted by the terms of the designation --- the disposition by any
    person or body of any right or interest in those lands for the
    purposes of the undertaking, and specify the terms and conditions of
    such dispositions;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} exclude the reserve lands or the undertakings from
    the application of the [Indian Oil and Gas Act](/eng/acts/I-7);

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} provide for the relationship between the
    regulations and aboriginal and treaty rights referred to in section
    35 of the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/), including limiting
    the extent to which the regulations may abrogate or derogate from
    those aboriginal and treaty rights;

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} provide for the arbitration of disputes arising
    under the regulations; and

  - [(s)]{.lawlabel} provide for the disposition or destruction of
    documents, regardless of medium, that are created or submitted under
    the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The regulations may incorporate by reference any laws
  of the province, as amended from time to time, with any adaptations
  that the Governor in Council considers necessary.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expanded meaning of undertaking

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations enlarging the meaning of the expression "commercial or
industrial undertaking" for the purposes of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations --- land registration

- **[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations respecting the establishment
  and operation of a system for the registration of interests and rights
  in reserve lands described in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Regulations made under subsection (1) may, despite
  any other Act of Parliament,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish priorities or, in Quebec, rankings among
    interests or rights that have been registered;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish a fund to compensate for losses in
    relation to interests or rights that have been registered or that
    should have been registered;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for purposes of registration, deem Her Majesty's or
    a first nation's interest or rights in the reserve lands to
    constitute fee simple title to or, in Quebec, ownership of the
    lands;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} deem valid any surrender or designation of the
    reserve lands made or purported to be made under the [Indian
    Act](/eng/acts/I-5) despite an assertion to the contrary by any
    person;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} confirm the legal capacity of a first nation to
    hold, transfer and register interests and rights in the reserve
    lands;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} authorize Her Majesty, without any new surrender or
    designation of reserve lands under the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)
    by a first nation, to grant fee simple title to those lands to
    Herself or to the first nation or, in Quebec, confer ownership of
    those lands on Herself or on the first nation, for a purpose
    authorized under a surrender or designation made under that Act
    prior to the grant or conferral;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} authorize the Minister, for the purposes of
    registration, to certify that Her Majesty or any other person has an
    interest or right in the reserve lands;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} entitle the holders of registrable interests and
    rights in the reserve lands to apply for registration;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} provide for the registration, at the request of the
    Minister or a first nation, of registrable interests and rights of
    any person that existed at the time of the initial registration of
    the fee simple title or ownership or other interests or rights of
    Her Majesty or the first nation in the reserve lands;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} provide for the substitution by the Minister or a
    first nation, as the case may be, of registrable interests and
    rights in the reserve lands for non-registrable ones;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} provide for the extinguishment of any interests and
    rights that are not registered;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} provide for the manner of calculating the
    compensation, if any, to be paid by a first nation for any
    substitution or extinguishment referred to in paragraphs (j) and
    (k), and limit the time for claiming such compensation; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} exclude the reserve lands from the application of
    sections 19, 21 and 55 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interests and rights not affected

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A grant or conferral under paragraph (2)(f) does not
  affect Her Majesty's title to the reserve lands, or a first nation's
  interest or rights in the reserve lands, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The regulations may incorporate by reference any laws
  of the province, as amended from time to time, with any adaptations
  that the Governor in Council considers necessary.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2010, c. 6, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for making regulations

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Regulations may not be made under
  section 3 or 4.1 in respect of reserve lands of a first nation unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has received a resolution of the
    council of the first nation requesting that the Minister recommend
    to the Governor in Council the making of those regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the regulations specify a provincial official by
    whom, or body by which, a power may be exercised or a duty must be
    performed, an agreement has been concluded between the Minister, the
    province and the council of the first nation for the administration
    and enforcement of the regulations by that official or body.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dispute resolution

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An agreement referred to in paragraph (1)(b) may
  provide for the arbitration, in accordance with the laws of the
  province, of disputes arising from the interpretation or application
  of that agreement, in which case the [Commercial Arbitration
  Act](/eng/acts/C-34.6) does not apply to the dispute.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to the amendment or
  repeal of regulations made under section 3 or 4.1.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 53, s. 5
- 2010, c. 6, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict with regulations under another
Act

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Regulations made under any
other Act of Parliament prevail over regulations made under section 3 to
the extent of any conflict or inconsistency between them, unless
otherwise provided in the regulations made under that section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict with first nation laws or
by-laws

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Regulations made under
section 3 or 4.1 prevail over any laws or by-laws made by a first nation
to the extent of any conflict or inconsistency between them, unless
those regulations provide otherwise.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 53, s. 7
- 2010, c. 6, s. 6

Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does not apply to any instruments made by a
provincial official or body under authority of a provincial law
incorporated by reference in regulations made under section 3 or 4.1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7)

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  provincial official or body that exercises a power or performs a duty
  under regulations made under section 3 or 4.1 is not a federal board,
  commission or other tribunal for the purposes of the [Federal Courts
  Act](/eng/acts/F-7).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review or appeal in provincial courts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise provided by regulations made under
  section 3 or 4.1, where a power or duty is conferred or imposed by a
  provincial law that is incorporated by reference in the regulations,
  its exercise or performance pursuant to the regulations is subject to
  review by, or appeal to, the courts of the province in the same manner
  and to the same extent as if the provincial law applied of its own
  force.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 53, s. 9
- 2010, c. 6, s. 6

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts received

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Fees, charges, fines or
other payments collected by a provincial official or body pursuant to
regulations made under section 3 are not Indian moneys for the purposes
of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) or public money for the purposes of
the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

## [Limits on Liability, Defences and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224394 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acts and omissions

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** In respect of any act or
omission occurring in the exercise of a power or the performance of a
duty under regulations made under section 3 or 4.1,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Canada is entitled to the
  same limits on liability, defences and immunities as apply to Her
  Majesty in right of the province under the laws of the province; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person or body exercising the power or performing
  the duty is entitled, unless otherwise provided by the regulations, to
  the same limits on liability, defences and immunities as those that
  would apply to a person or body exercising such a power or performing
  such a duty under the laws of the province.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 53, s. 11
- 2010, c. 6, s. 7
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-11-20060401.html "Link to previous version of section 11"){.PITLink}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right or interest in reserve lands

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** No civil proceeding may
be brought, no order may be made and no fine or monetary penalty may be
imposed in relation to reserve lands against Her Majesty in right of
Canada under regulations made under section 3 based on any right or
interest retained by Her Majesty in those lands.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration, substitution or
extinguishment

**[[12.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.1 .sectionLabel}** No civil proceeding
may be brought against Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province, a
federal or provincial official or body or a person acting on behalf of
any of them, in relation to the registration of the title or ownership
of Her Majesty or a first nation, or of any registrable interest or
right referred to in paragraph 4.1(2)(i), or in relation to the
substitution or extinguishment of any interest or right in reserve lands
pursuant to regulations made under section 4.1.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2010, c. 6, s. 8
:::
