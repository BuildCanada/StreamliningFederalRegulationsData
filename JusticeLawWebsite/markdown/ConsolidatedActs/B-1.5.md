## Bank for International Settlements (Immunity) Act {#bank-for-international-settlements-immunity-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2007, c. 35, s. 140
:::

Assented to 2007-12-14

An Act to provide immunity to the Bank for International Settlements
from government measures and from civil judicial process
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
\[Enacted by section 140 of chapter 35 of the Statutes of Canada, 2007,
in force on assent December 14, 2007.\]
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Bank for International Settlements (Immunity)
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity --- government measures

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Bank for International
Settlements, its property and any property entrusted to it are exempt
from the measures referred to in Article 1 of the Protocol regarding the
immunities of the Bank for International Settlements that was ratified
by Canada on January 20, 1938.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity --- judicial process

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Bank
  is immune from the juris-diction of any court in respect of a civil
  proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity --- property

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Bank's property and any property entrusted to it
  are immune, in respect of any civil proceeding, from attachment and
  execution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) are binding on Her Majesty in
  right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of sections 2 and 3

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For reasons of national
security or for the purposes of the conduct of Canada's international
affairs or the implementation of Canada's international obligations, the
Governor in Council may determine that, to the extent specified by the
Governor in Council,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Bank, its property and any property entrusted to
  it are not exempt under section 2;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Bank is not immune under subsection 3(1); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Bank's property and any property entrusted to it
  are not immune under subsection 3(2).
