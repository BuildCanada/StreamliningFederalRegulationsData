## Privacy Act {#privacy-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. P-21
:::

An Act to extend the present laws of Canada that protect the privacy of
individuals and that provide individuals with a right of access to
personal information about themselves
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-397172 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 111, Sch. II "1"
:::

## [Purpose of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-397177 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to extend the present laws of Canada that protect the privacy of
individuals with respect to personal information about themselves held
by a government institution and that provide individuals with a right of
access to that information.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 111, Sch. II "2"
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-397182 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[administrative purpose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[administrative purpose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to the
    use of personal information about an individual, means the use of
    that information in a decision making process that directly affects
    that individual; ([fins administratives]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[alternative format]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[alternative format]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to personal
    information, means a format that allows a person with a sensory
    disability to read or listen to the personal information; ([support
    de substitution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Federal
    Court; ([Cour]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is
    designated as the Minister under subsection 3.1(1); ([ministre
    désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[government institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[government institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any department or ministry of state of the
      Government of Canada, or any body or office, listed in the
      schedule, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any parent Crown corporation, and any
      wholly-owned subsidiary of such a corporation, within the meaning
      of section 83 of the [Financial Administration
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11); ([institution fédérale]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a government
    institution, means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a department or ministry of state,
      the member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada who presides
      over the department or ministry, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, either the person designated
      under subsection 3.1(2) to be the head of the institution for the
      purposes of this Act or, if no such person is designated, the
      chief executive officer of the institution, whatever their
      title; ([responsable d'institution fédérale]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[personal information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[personal information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means information about
    an identifiable individual that is recorded in any form including,
    without restricting the generality of the foregoing,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} information relating to the race, national or
      ethnic origin, colour, religion, age or marital status of the
      individual,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information relating to the education or the
      medical, criminal or employment history of the individual or
      information relating to financial transactions in which the
      individual has been involved,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any identifying number, symbol or other
      particular assigned to the individual,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the address, fingerprints or blood type of the
      individual,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the personal opinions or views of the individual
      except where they are about another individual or about a proposal
      for a grant, an award or a prize to be made to another individual
      by a government institution or a part of a government institution
      specified in the regulations,

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} correspondence sent to a government institution
      by the individual that is implicitly or explicitly of a private or
      confidential nature, and replies to such correspondence that would
      reveal the contents of the original correspondence,

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the views or opinions of another individual about
      the individual,

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the views or opinions of another individual about
      a proposal for a grant, an award or a prize to be made to the
      individual by an institution or a part of an institution referred
      to in paragraph (e), but excluding the name of the other
      individual where it appears with the views or opinions of the
      other individual, and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the individual where it appears with
      other personal information relating to the individual or where the
      disclosure of the name itself would reveal information about the
      individual,

    but, for the purposes of sections 7, 8 and 26 and section 19 of the
    [Access to Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1), does not include

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} information about an individual who is or was an
      officer or employee of a government institution that relates to
      the position or functions of the individual including,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the fact that the individual is or was an
        officer or employee of the government institution,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the title, business address and telephone
        number of the individual,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the classification, salary range and
        responsibilities of the position held by the individual,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the name of the individual on a document
        prepared by the individual in the course of employment, and

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the personal opinions or views of the
        individual given in the course of employment,

    - [(j.1)]{.lawlabel} the fact that an individual is or was a
      [[ministerial adviser]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or a member of a
      [[ministerial staff]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms are
      defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Conflict of Interest
      Act](/eng/acts/C-36.65), as well as the individual's name and
      title,

    - [(k)]{.lawlabel} information about an individual who is or was
      performing services under contract for a government institution
      that relates to the services performed, including the terms of the
      contract, the name of the individual and the opinions or views of
      the individual given in the course of the performance of those
      services,

    - [(l)]{.lawlabel} information relating to any discretionary benefit
      of a financial nature, including the granting of a licence or
      permit, conferred on an individual, including the name of the
      individual and the exact nature of the benefit, and

    - [(m)]{.lawlabel} information about an individual who has been dead
      for more than twenty years; ([renseignements
      personnels]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[personal information bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[personal information bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a collection
    or grouping of personal information described in section
    10; ([fichier de renseignements personnels]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Privacy Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Privacy Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commissioner
    appointed under section 53; ([Commissaire à la protection de la vie
    privée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sensory disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sensory disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a disability that
    relates to sight or hearing. ([déficience
    sensorielle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Collection, Retention and Disposal of Personal Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-397235 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collection of personal information

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No personal information
shall be collected by a government institution unless it relates
directly to an operating program or activity of the institution.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 111, Sch. II "4"
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal information to be collected
directly

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  government institution shall, wherever possible, collect personal
  information that is intended to be used for an administrative purpose
  directly from the individual to whom it relates except where the
  individual authorizes otherwise or where personal information may be
  disclosed to the institution under subsection 8(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Individual to be informed of purpose

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A government institution shall inform any individual
  from whom the institution collects personal information about the
  individual of the purpose for which the information is being
  collected.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply where compliance
  therewith might

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} result in the collection of inaccurate information;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} defeat the purpose or prejudice the use for which
    information is collected.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 111, Sch. II "5"
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of personal information used
for an administrative purpose

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Personal
  information that has been used by a government institution for an
  administrative purpose shall be retained by the institution for such
  period of time after it is so used as may be prescribed by regulation
  in order to ensure that the individual to whom it relates has a
  reasonable opportunity to obtain access to the information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accuracy of personal information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A government institution shall take all reasonable
  steps to ensure that personal information that is used for an
  administrative purpose by the institution is as accurate, up-to-date
  and complete as possible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal of personal information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A government institution shall dispose of personal
  information under the control of the institution in accordance with
  the regulations and in accordance with any directives or guidelines
  issued by the designated minister in relation to the disposal of that
  information.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 111, Sch. II "6"
:::

## [Protection of Personal Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-397260 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of personal information

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Personal information under
the control of a government institution shall not, without the consent
of the individual to whom it relates, be used by the institution except

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose for which the information was
  obtained or compiled by the institution or for a use consistent with
  that purpose; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a purpose for which the information may be
  disclosed to the institution under subsection 8(2).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 111, Sch. II "7"
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of personal information

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Personal
  information under the control of a government institution shall not,
  without the consent of the individual to whom it relates, be disclosed
  by the institution except in accordance with this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where personal information may be
  disclosed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any other Act of Parliament, personal
  information under the control of a government institution may be
  disclosed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose for which the information was
    obtained or compiled by the institution or for a use consistent with
    that purpose;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for any purpose in accordance with any Act of
    Parliament or any regulation made thereunder that authorizes its
    disclosure;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of complying with a subpoena or
    warrant issued or order made by a court, person or body with
    jurisdiction to compel the production of information or for the
    purpose of complying with rules of court relating to the production
    of information;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to the Attorney General of Canada for use in legal
    proceedings involving the Crown in right of Canada or the Government
    of Canada;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} to an investigative body specified in the
    regulations, on the written request of the body, for the purpose of
    enforcing any law of Canada or a province or carrying out a lawful
    investigation, if the request specifies the purpose and describes
    the information to be disclosed;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of administering or enforcing any
    law or carrying out a lawful investigation, under an agreement or
    arrangement between the Government of Canada or any of its
    institutions and any of the following entities or any of their
    institutions:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the government of a foreign state,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an international organization of states or an
      international organization established by the governments of
      states,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the government of a province,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the council of the Westbank First Nation,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the [[council of a participating First
      Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of
      the [First Nations Jurisdiction over Education in British Columbia
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11.75),

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the council of a [[participating First
      Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 2 of the
      [Anishinabek Nation Education Agreement Act](/eng/acts/A-11.3),

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} a [[First Nation
      Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or the [[Anishinabek Nation
      Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the
      [Anishinabek Nation Governance Agreement Act](/eng/acts/A-11.31),
      or an Anishinaabe Institution, within the meaning of section 1.1
      of the [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section
      2 of that Act,

    - [(vii.1)]{.lawlabel} the [[Whitecap Dakota
      Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the
      [Self-Government Treaty Recognizing the Whitecap Dakota Nation /
      Wapaha Ska Dakota Oyate Act](/eng/acts/S-8.36);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} to a member of Parliament for the purpose of
    assisting the individual to whom the information relates in
    resolving a problem;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} to officers or employees of the institution for
    internal audit purposes, or to the office of the Comptroller General
    or any other person or body specified in the regulations for audit
    purposes;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the Library and Archives of Canada for archival
    purposes;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} to any person or body for research or statistical
    purposes if the head of the government institution

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is satisfied that the purpose for which the
      information is disclosed cannot reasonably be accomplished unless
      the information is provided in a form that would identify the
      individual to whom it relates, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} obtains from the person or body a written
      undertaking that no subsequent disclosure of the information will
      be made in a form that could reasonably be expected to identify
      the individual to whom it relates;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} to any aboriginal government, association of
    aboriginal people, Indian band, government institution or part
    thereof, or to any person acting on behalf of such government,
    association, band, institution or part thereof, for the purpose of
    researching or validating the claims, disputes or grievances of any
    of the aboriginal peoples of Canada;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} to any government institution for the purpose of
    locating an individual in order to collect a debt owing to Her
    Majesty in right of Canada by that individual or make a payment
    owing to that individual by Her Majesty in right of Canada; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} for any purpose where, in the opinion of the head
    of the institution,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the public interest in disclosure clearly
      outweighs any invasion of privacy that could result from the
      disclosure, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} disclosure would clearly benefit the individual
      to whom the information relates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal information disclosed by
  Library and Archives of Canada

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any other Act of Parliament, personal
  information under the custody or control of the Library and Archives
  of Canada that has been transferred there by a government institution
  for historical or archival purposes may be disclosed in accordance
  with the regulations to any person or body for research or statistical
  purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies of requests under paragraph
  (2)(e) to be retained

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The head of a government institution shall retain a
  copy of every request received by the government institution under
  paragraph (2)(e) for such period of time as may be prescribed by
  regulation, shall keep a record of any information disclosed pursuant

  regulation and shall, on the request of the Privacy Commissioner, make
  those copies and records available to the Privacy Commissioner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of disclosure under paragraph
  (2)(m)

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The head of a government institution shall notify the

  information under paragraph (2)(m) prior to the disclosure where

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record of disclosures to be retained

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The head
  of a government institution shall retain a record of any use by the
  institution of personal information contained in a personal
  information bank or any use or purpose for which that information is
  disclosed by the institution where the use or purpose is not included

## [Access to Personal Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-397361 .Part}

### [Right of Access]{.HTitleText2} {#h-397362 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of access

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this Act, every
  individual who is a Canadian citizen or a permanent resident within
  the meaning of subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee
  Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) has a right to and shall, on request,
  be given access to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any personal information about the individual
    contained in a personal information bank; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other personal information about the individual
    under the control of a government institution with respect to which
    the individual is able to provide sufficiently specific information
    on the location of the information as to render it reasonably
    retrievable by the government institution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other rights relating to personal
  information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every individual who is given access under paragraph
  (1)(a) to personal information that has been used, is being used or is
  available for use for an administrative purpose is entitled to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} request correction of the personal information
    where the individual believes there is an error or omission therein;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require that a notation be attached to the
    information reflecting any correction requested but not made; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} require that any person or body to whom that
    information has been disclosed for use for an administrative purpose
    within two years prior to the time a correction is requested or a
    notation is required under this subsection in respect of that
    information

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} be notified of the correction or notation, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the disclosure is to a government
      institution, the institution make the correction or notation on
      any copy of the information under its control.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of right of access by order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by order, extend the
  right to be given access to personal information under subsection (1)
  to include individuals not referred to in that subsection and may set
  such conditions as the Governor in Council deems appropriate.

## [Complaints]{.HTitleText1} {#h-397544 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt and investigation of complaints

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this Act, the Privacy
  Commissioner shall receive and investigate complaints

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} from individuals who allege that personal
    information about themselves held by a government institution has
    been used or disclosed otherwise than in accordance with section 7
    or 8;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} from individuals who have been refused access to
    personal information requested under subsection 12(1);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} from individuals who allege that they are not being
    accorded the rights to which they are entitled under subsection
    12(2) or that corrections of personal information requested under
    paragraph 12(2)(a) are being refused without justification;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} from individuals who have requested access to
    personal information in respect of which a time limit has been
    extended pursuant to section 15 where they consider the extension
    unreasonable;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} from individuals who have not been given access to
    personal information in the official language requested by the
    individuals under subsection 17(2);

  - [(e.1)]{.lawlabel} from individuals who have not been given access
    to personal information in an alternative format pursuant to a
    request made under subsection 17(3);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} from individuals who have been required to pay a
    fee that they consider inappropriate;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the index referred to in subsection
    11(1); or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any other matter relating to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the collection, retention or disposal of personal
      information by a government institution,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the use or disclosure of personal information
      under the control of a government institution, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} requesting or obtaining access under subsection
      12(1) to personal information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaints submitted on behalf of
  complainants

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act precludes the Privacy
  Commissioner from receiving and investigating complaints of a nature
  described in subsection (1) that are submitted by a person authorized
  by the complainant to act on behalf of the complainant, and a
  reference to a complainant in any other section includes a reference
  to a person so authorized.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privacy Commissioner may initiate
  complaint

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Privacy Commissioner is satisfied that
  there are reasonable grounds to investigate a matter under this Act,
  the Commissioner may initiate a complaint in respect thereof.

- 1992, c. 21, s. 37

## [Investigations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-397570 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of intention to investigate

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** Before commencing an
investigation of a complaint under this Act, the Privacy Commissioner
shall notify the head of the government institution concerned of the
intention to carry out the investigation and shall inform the head of
the institution of the substance of the complaint.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 111, Sch. II "31"
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulation of procedure

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Act, the
Privacy Commissioner may determine the procedure to be followed in the
performance of any duty or function of the Commissioner under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 111, Sch. II "32"
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigations in private

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  investigation of a complaint under this Act by the Privacy
  Commissioner shall be conducted in private.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to make representation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the course of an investigation of a complaint
  under this Act by the Privacy Commissioner, the person who made the
  complaint and the head of the government institution concerned shall
  be given an opportunity to make representations to the Commissioner,
  but no one is entitled as of right to be present during, to have
  access to or to comment on representations made to the Commissioner by
  any other person.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 111, Sch. II "33"
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Privacy Commissioner in
carrying out investigations

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Privacy Commissioner has, in relation to the carrying out of the
  investigation of any complaint under this Act, power

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to summon and enforce the appearance of persons
    before the Privacy Commissioner and compel them to give oral or
    written evidence on oath and to produce such documents and things as
    the Commissioner deems requisite to the full investigation and
    consideration of the complaint, in the same manner and to the same
    extent as a superior court of record;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to administer oaths;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to receive and accept such evidence and other
    information, whether on oath or by affidavit or otherwise, as the
    Privacy Commissioner sees fit, whether or not the evidence or
    information is or would be admissible in a court of law;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to enter any premises occupied by any government
    institution on satisfying any security requirements of the
    institution relating to the premises;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} to converse in private with any person in any
    premises entered pursuant to paragraph (d) and otherwise carry out
    therein such inquiries within the authority of the Privacy
    Commissioner under this Act as the Commissioner sees fit; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} to examine or obtain copies of or extracts from
    books or other records found in any premises entered pursuant to
    paragraph (d) containing any matter relevant to the investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite any other Act of Parliament, any privilege
  under the law of evidence, solicitor-client privilege or the
  professional secrecy of advocates and notaries and litigation
  privilege, and subject to subsection (2.1), the Privacy Commissioner
  may, during the investigation of any complaint under this Act, examine
  any information recorded in any form under the control of a government
  institution, other than a confidence of the Queen's Privy Council for
  Canada to which subsection 70(1) applies, and no information that the
  Commissioner may examine under this subsection may be withheld from
  the Commissioner on any grounds.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protected information --- solicitors,
  advocates and notaries

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} The Privacy Commissioner may examine information
  that is subject to solicitor-client privilege or the professional
  secrecy of advocates and notaries or to litigation privilege only if
  the head of a government institution refuses to disclose the
  information under section 27.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(2.2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the disclosure by the head
  of a government institution to the Privacy Commissioner of information
  that is subject to solicitor-client privilege or the professional
  secrecy of advocates and notaries or to litigation privilege does not
  constitute a waiver of those privileges or that professional secrecy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence in other proceedings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Except in a prosecution of a person for an offence
  under section 131 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) (perjury) in
  respect of a statement made under this Act, in a prosecution for an
  offence under this Act or in a review before the Court under this Act
  or an appeal therefrom, evidence given by a person in proceedings
  under this Act and evidence of the existence of the proceedings is
  inadmissible against that person in a court or in any other
  proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witness fees

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any person summoned to appear before the Privacy
  Commissioner pursuant to this section is entitled in the discretion of
  the Commissioner to receive the like fees and allowances for so doing
  as if summoned to attend before the Federal Court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of documents, etc.

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any document or thing produced pursuant to this
  section by any person or government institution shall be returned by
  the Privacy Commissioner within ten days after a request is made to
  the Commissioner by that person or government institution, but nothing
  in this subsection precludes the Commissioner from again requiring its
  production in accordance with this section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Findings and recommendations of Privacy
Commissioner

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, on
  investigating a complaint under this Act in respect of personal
  information, the Privacy Commissioner finds that the complaint is
  well-founded, the Commissioner shall provide the head of the
  government institution that has control of the personal information
  with a report containing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the findings of the investigation and any
    recommendations that the Commissioner considers appropriate; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where appropriate, a request that, within a time
    specified therein, notice be given to the Commissioner of any action
    taken or proposed to be taken to implement the recommendations
    contained in the report or reasons why no such action has been or is
    proposed to be taken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to complainant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Privacy Commissioner shall, after investigating a
  complaint under this Act, report to the complainant the results of the
  investigation, but where a notice has been requested under paragraph
  (1)(b) no report shall be made under this subsection until the
  expiration of the time within which the notice is to be given to the
  Commissioner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matter to be included in report to
  complainant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a notice has been requested under paragraph
  (1)(b) but no such notice is received by the Commissioner within the
  time specified therefor or the action described in the notice is, in
  the opinion of the Commissioner, inadequate or inappropriate or will
  not be taken in a reasonable time, the Commissioner shall so advise
  the complainant in his report under subsection (2) and may include in
  the report such comments on the matter as he thinks fit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to be given

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where, pursuant to a request under paragraph (1)(b),
  the head of a government institution gives notice to the Privacy
  Commissioner that access to personal information will be given to a
  complainant, the head of the institution shall give the complainant
  access to the information forthwith on giving the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of review

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where, following the investigation of a complaint
  relating to a refusal to give access to personal information under
  this Act, access is not given to the complainant, the Privacy
  Commissioner shall inform the complainant that the complainant has the
  right to apply to the Court for a review of the matter investigated.

## [Review of Exempt Banks]{.HTitleText1} {#h-397620 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation of exempt banks

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Privacy Commissioner may, from time to time at the discretion of the
  Commissioner, carry out investigations of the files contained in
  personal information banks designated as exempt banks under section
  18.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sections 31 to 34 apply

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 31 to 34 apply, where appropriate and with
  such modifications as the circumstances require, in respect of
  investigations carried out under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report of findings and recommendations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, following an investigation under subsection (1),
  the Privacy Commissioner considers that any file contained in a
  personal information bank should not be contained therein within the
  terms of the order designating the bank as an exempt bank, the
  Commissioner shall provide the head of the government institution that
  has control of the bank with a report containing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the findings of the Commissioner and any
    recommendations that the Commissioner considers appropriate; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where appropriate, a request that, within a time
    specified therein, notice be given to the Commissioner of any action
    taken or proposed to be taken to implement the recommendations or
    reasons why no such action has been or is proposed to be taken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports to be included in annual or
  special reports to Parliament

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any report made by the Privacy Commissioner under
  subsection (3), together with any notice given to the Commissioner in
  response thereto, may be included in a report made pursuant to section
  38 or 39.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of exempt banks by Court

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the Privacy Commissioner requests a notice
  under paragraph (3)(b) in respect of any file contained in a personal
  information bank designated under section 18 as an exempt bank and no
  notice is received within the time specified therefor or the action
  described in the notice is, in the opinion of the Commissioner,
  inadequate or inappropriate or will not be taken in a reasonable time,
  the Privacy Commissioner may make an application to the Court under
  section 43.

- 1980-81-82-83, c. 111, Sch. II "36"

## [Review by the Federal Court]{.HTitleText1} {#h-397667 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review by Federal Court where access
refused

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** Any individual who has
been refused access to personal information requested under subsection
12(1) may, if a complaint has been made to the Privacy Commissioner in
respect of the refusal, apply to the Court for a review of the matter
within forty-five days after the time the results of an investigation of
the complaint by the Privacy Commissioner are reported to the
complainant under subsection 35(2) or within such further time as the
Court may, either before or after the expiration of those forty-five
days, fix or allow.

  investigation has been carried out by the Privacy Commissioner, if the

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} appear before the Court on behalf of any individual
  who has applied for a review under section 41; or

which to refuse to disclose the personal information, order the head of

  Council may appoint any qualified person to hold that office in the

  while holding office, be paid the salary or other remuneration and
  expenses that may be fixed by the Governor in Council.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-397922 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties and functions of designated
Minister

- **[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  designated Minister shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cause to be kept under review the manner in which
    personal information banks are maintained and managed to ensure
    compliance with the provisions of this Act and the regulations
    relating to access by individuals to personal information contained
    therein;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} assign or cause to be assigned a registration
    number to each personal information bank;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribe such forms as may be required for the
    operation of this Act and the regulations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} cause to be prepared and distributed to government
    institutions directives and guidelines concerning the operation of
    this Act and the regulations; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} prescribe the form of, and what information is to
    be included in, reports made to Parliament under section 72.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception for Bank of Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Anything that is required to be done by the
  designated Minister under paragraph (1)(a) or (d) shall be done in
  respect of the Bank of Canada by the Governor of the Bank of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of existing and proposed
  personal information banks

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), the designated Minister
  shall cause to be kept under review the utilization of existing
  personal information banks and proposals for the creation of new
  banks, and shall make such recommendations as he considers appropriate
  to the heads of the appropriate government institutions with regard to
  personal information banks that, in the opinion of the designated
  Minister, are under-utilized or the existence of which can be
  terminated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment and modification of
  personal information banks

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), no new personal
  information bank shall be established and no existing personal
  information banks shall be substantially modified without approval of
  the designated Minister or otherwise than in accordance with any term
  or condition on which such approval is given.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of subsections (3) and (4)

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (3) and (4) apply only in respect of
  personal information banks under the control of government
  institutions that are departments as defined in section 2 of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation to head of government
  institution

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The designated Minister may authorize the head of a
  government institution to exercise and perform, in such manner and
  subject to such terms and conditions as the designated Minister
  directs, any of the powers, functions and duties of the designated
  Minister under subsection (3) or (4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of services related to privacy

- **[[73.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  government institution may provide services related to any power, duty
  or function conferred or imposed on the head of a government
  institution under this Act to another government institution that is
  presided over by the same Minister or that is under the responsibility
  of the same Minister and may receive such services from any other such
  government institution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A government institution may provide services under
  subsection (1) to another government institution only if it enters
  into an agreement in writing with the other government institution in
  respect of those services before it provides the services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The head of a government institution that receives
  the services shall provide a copy of the agreement to the Privacy
  Commissioner and the designated Minister as soon as possible after the
  agreement is entered into. The head of the institution shall also
  notify the Commissioner and the designated Minister of any material
  change to that agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The head of a government institution that provides
  the services may charge a fee for those services. The fee is not to
  exceed the cost of providing the service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Spending authority

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The head of the institution that charges the fee may
  spend the revenues that are received from the provision of the
  services for any purpose of that institution. If the head of the
  institution spends the revenues, he or she must do so in the fiscal
  year in which they are received or, unless an appropriation Act
  provides otherwise, in the next fiscal year.
