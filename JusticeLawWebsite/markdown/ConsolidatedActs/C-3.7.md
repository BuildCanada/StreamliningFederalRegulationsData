## Canada Emergency Response Benefit Act {#canada-emergency-response-benefit-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2020, c. 5, s. 8
:::

Assented to 2020-03-25

An Act respecting income support payments for workers (coronavirus
disease 2019)

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 8 of chapter 5 of the Statutes of Canada, 2020, in
force on assent March 25, 2020.\]
:::
:::::

:::::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada Emergency Response Benefit Act](/eng/acts/C-3.7).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[COVID-19]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[COVID-19]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the coronavirus disease
    2019. ([COVID-19]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Employment
    and Social Development. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period of seven consecutive
    days beginning on and including Sunday. ([semaine]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is at least 15
    years of age, who is resident in Canada and who, for 2019 or in the
    12-month period preceding the day on which they make an application
    under section 5, has a total income of at least \$5,000 --- or, if
    another amount is fixed by regulation, of at least that amount ---
    from the following sources:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} employment;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} self-employment;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} benefits paid to the person under any of
      subsections 22(1), 23(1), 152.04(1) and 152.05(1) of the
      [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6); and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} allowances, money or other benefits paid to the
      person under a provincial plan because of pregnancy or in respect
      of the care by the person of one or more of their new-born
      children or one or more children placed with them for the purpose
      of adoption. ([travailleur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations --- definition of
[[worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** With the consent of the
Minister of Finance, the Minister may, by regulation, fix an amount for
the purposes of the definition [[worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
section 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must make an
income support payment to a worker who makes an application under
section 5 and who is eligible for the payment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A worker
  may, in the form and manner established by the Minister, apply for an
  income support payment for any four-week period falling within the
  period beginning on March 15, 2020 and ending on October 3, 2020.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No worker is permitted to file an application after
  December 2, 2020.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An applicant must provide the Minister with any
  information that the Minister may require in respect of the
  application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A worker
  is eligible for an income support payment if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the worker, whether employed or self-employed,
    ceases working for reasons related to COVID-19 for at least 14
    consecutive days within the four-week period in respect of which
    they apply for the payment; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they do not receive, in respect of the consecutive
    days on which they have ceased working,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} subject to the regulations, income from
      employment or self-employment,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} [[benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined
      in subsection 2(1) of the [Employment Insurance
      Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6), or an employment insurance emergency
      response benefit referred to in section 153.7 of that Act,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} allowances, money or other benefits paid to the
      worker under a provincial plan because of pregnancy or in respect
      of the care by the worker of one or more of their new-born
      children or one or more children placed with them for the purpose
      of adoption, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any other income that is prescribed by
      regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An employed worker does not cease work for the
  purpose of paragraph (1)(a) if they quit their employment voluntarily.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by regulation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exclude a class of income from the application of
    subparagraph (1)(b)(i); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribe any other income for the purposes of
    subparagraph (1)(b)(iv).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of payment

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of an income support payment for a week is the amount fixed by
  regulation for that week.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} With the consent of the Minister of Finance, the
  Minister may, by regulation, fix the amount of an income support
  payment for a week specified in the regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distinguishing --- classes

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Regulations made under subsection (2) may distinguish
  among different classes of workers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum number of weeks

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  maximum number of weeks for which income support payments may be made
  to a worker is 16 weeks or, if another number of weeks is fixed by
  regulation, that number of weeks.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} With the consent of the Minister of Finance, the
  Minister may, by regulation, fix a number of weeks for the purpose of
  subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Social Insurance Number

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is authorized
to collect and use, for the purposes of the administration and
enforcement of this Act, the Social Insurance Number of a person who
makes an application under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information and documents

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, for any
purpose related to verifying compliance or preventing non-compliance
with this Act, by notice served personally or by confirmed delivery
service, require that any person provide any information or document
within the reasonable time that is stated in the notice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments cannot be charged, etc.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An income support payment

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not subject to the operation of any law relating
  to bankruptcy or insolvency;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cannot be assigned, charged, attached or given as
  security;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} cannot be retained by way of deduction, set-off or
  compensation under any Act of Parliament other than this Act; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} is not garnishable moneys for the purposes of the
  [Family Orders and Agreements Enforcement Assistance
  Act](/eng/acts/F-1.4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of erroneous payment or
overpayment

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister determines that a person has received an income support
  payment to which the person is not entitled, or an amount in excess of
  the amount of such a payment to which the person is entitled, the
  person must repay the amount of the payment or the excess amount, as
  the case may be, as soon as is feasible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery as debt due to Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the erroneous payment or overpayment,
  as determined by the Minister, constitutes a debt due to Her Majesty
  in right of Canada, as of the day on which it was paid, that may be
  recovered by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of default

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amount of any debt referred to in subsection (2)
  may be certified by the Minister, and registration of the certificate
  in the Federal Court has the same effect as a judgment of that Court
  for the amount specified in the certificate and all related
  registration costs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation or prescription period

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) to (7),
  no action or proceedings are to be taken to recover money owing under
  this Act after the expiry of the six-year limitation or prescription
  period that begins on the day on which the money becomes due and
  payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction, set-off or compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Money owing by a person under this Act may be
  recovered at any time by way of deduction from, set-off against or
  compensation against any sum of money, including an income support
  payment under this Act, that may be due or payable by Her Majesty in
  right of Canada to the person, other than an amount payable under
  section 122.61 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment of liability

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person's liability for money owing under this
  Act is acknowledged in accordance with subsection (5), the time during
  which the limitation or prescription period has run before the
  acknowledgment does not count in the calculation of that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment after expiry of
  limitation or prescription period

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a person's liability for money owing under this
  Act is acknowledged in accordance with subsection (5) after the expiry
  of the limitation or prescription period, an action or proceedings to
  recover the money may, subject to subsections (3) and (6), be brought
  within six years after the date of the acknowledgment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of acknowledgment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An acknowledgment of liability means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a promise to pay the money owing, made by the
    person or their agent, mandatary or other representative;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgment of the money owing, made by the
    person or their agent, mandatary or other representative, whether or
    not a promise to pay can be implied from it and whether or not it
    contains a refusal to pay;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a part payment by the person or their agent,
    mandatary or other representative of any money owing; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgment of the money owing, made in the
    course of proceedings under the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency
    Act](/eng/acts/B-3) or any other legislation dealing with the
    payment of debts by the person, their agent, mandatary or other
    representative or the trustee or administrator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation or prescription period
  suspended

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The running of a limitation or prescription period in
  respect of money owing under this Act is suspended during any period
  in which it is prohibited to commence or continue an action or other
  proceedings against the person to recover money owing under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement proceedings

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply in respect of an action
  or proceedings relating to the execution, renewal or enforcement of a
  judgment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No interest payable

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** No interest is payable on
any amount owing to Her Majesty in right of Canada under this Act as a
result of an erroneous payment or overpayment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt of benefits, allowances or money

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If,
  for any four-week period, the Minister determines that a worker
  received an income support payment for which they were not eligible by
  reason only that they received one or more payments of benefits,
  allowances or money referred to in subparagraph 6(1)(b)(ii) or (iii),
  the Minister is deemed to have determined under subsection 12(2) that
  the amount that the worker must repay under subsection 12(1) is the
  amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#1368270 .Subsection}
  \$2,000 × (A ÷ 4)

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of weeks for which the worker received such
      benefits, allowances or money during that four-week period.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of an
  employment insurance emergency response benefit received by the worker
  if the Canada Employment Insurance Commission informs the Minister
  that subsection (1) should not apply in respect of that benefit and,
  if the Commission does so, the worker is, despite subparagraph
  6(1)(b)(ii), deemed to have been eligible to receive the income
  support payment.
