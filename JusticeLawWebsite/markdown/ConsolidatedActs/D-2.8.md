## Disability Tax Credit Promoters Restrictions Act {#disability-tax-credit-promoters-restrictions-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2014, c. 7
:::

Assented to 2014-05-29

An Act restricting the fees charged by promoters of the disability tax
credit and making consequential amendments to the Tax Court of Canada
Act
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-172649 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Disability Tax Credit Promoters Restrictions Act](/eng/acts/D-2.8).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-172652 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[claimant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[claimant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who is the
    subject of a disability tax credit request or who has a dependant on
    behalf of whom a disability tax credit request is
    made. ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[disability tax credit request]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[disability tax credit request]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    request, made in respect of a claimant,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for determination of disability tax credit
      eligibility under subsection 152(1.01) of the [Income Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a deduction under subsection
      118.3(1) or (2) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any deduction or overpayment of tax
      under the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) that is contingent
      upon the eligibility for a deduction under subsection 118.3(1)
      or (2) of that Act. ([demande de crédit d'impôt pour personnes
      handicapées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the fair market value of any
    consideration accepted or charged by a person, directly or
    indirectly, to prepare a disability tax credit
    request. ([frais]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[maximum fee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[maximum fee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the maximum fee set by
    regulations made under section 9. ([frais maximaux]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Revenue. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    248(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by
    regulation. ([Version anglaise seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[promoter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[promoter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who, directly or
    indirectly, accepts or charges a fee in respect of a disability tax
    credit request. ([promoteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Promoter's Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-172666 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for a promoter to accept or charge a fee that exceeds the
  maximum fee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty for fee exceeding maximum fee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every promoter who contravenes subsection (1) is
  liable to a penalty in respect of the fee equal to the total of
  \$1,000 and the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#172672 .Subsection}
  A -- (B + C)

  where
  :::

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the fee in respect of a disability tax credit request,

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the maximum fee, and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the fee in respect of the disability tax credit
      request that is repaid to the claimant within 120 days after
      notification is given to the Minister in accordance with section 4
      or any longer period that is acceptable to the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reporting requirement  --- promoter

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every promoter, other than
a prescribed exempt promoter, must notify the Minister, in a form and
manner authorized by the Minister, of the fee accepted or charged by the
promoter in respect of a disability tax credit request if it exceeds the
maximum fee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deceptive information

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for a
promoter to make, participate in, assent to or acquiesce in the making
of any false or deceptive entries in a notification required under
section 4.

## [Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-172681 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every promoter who fails to
notify the Minister under section 4 or who contravenes section 5 is
guilty of an offence and, in addition to any penalty otherwise provided,
is liable on summary conviction to a fine of not less than \$1,000 and
not more than \$25,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every promoter who
contravenes section 3 is guilty of an offence and, in addition to any
penalty otherwise provided, is liable on summary conviction to a fine of
not less than 100% and not more than 200% of the total of all amounts by
which the fee exceeds the maximum fee amount in respect of a disability
tax credit request.

## [Disclosure of Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-172686 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information may be disclosed

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Section 241 of the [Income
Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) does not apply to information or documents
that can reasonably be regarded as necessary for the administration or
enforcement of this Act and an official or authorized person, as defined
for the purposes of that section, may make that information or a copy of
any such documents available to any person for a purpose related to the
administration or enforcement of this Act.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-172689 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations for carrying out the purposes and provisions of this
Act including, without restricting the generality of the foregoing,
regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} setting the maximum fee;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} exempting certain promoters from the notifying
  requirements set out in section 4; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that by this Act is to be
  prescribed.

## [Application of Income Tax Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-172695 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions applicable

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Sections 152, 158 and
159, subsections 161(1) and (11), sections 162 to 167 and 257, Division
J of Part I and Part XV of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) are
applicable with respect to the circumstances related to a disability tax
credit request, with such modifications as the circumstances require.
