## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-434273 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Special Retirement Arrangements Act](/eng/acts/S-15.2).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-434276 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a refund of contributions
    and any interest payable on those
    contributions; ([prestations]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the President of the Treasury
    Board; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pension benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:     [[pension benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a periodic payment
    to which a member or former member of a special pension plan, or the
    beneficiary, estate or succession of that member or former member,
    is or may become entitled under that pension plan; ([prestation de
    retraite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pension benefit credit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pension benefit credit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the aggregate
    value at a particular time of the pension benefit and any other
    benefits that have accrued under a special pension plan to a member
    or former member of that pension plan, as calculated in the
    prescribed manner; ([droits à pension]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by
    regulation; ([Version anglaise seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[retirement compensation arrangement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[retirement compensation arrangement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, unless
    the context otherwise requires, means a plan or an arrangement that
    is established by or under the authority of an order made under
    paragraph 10(a) or subsection 11(1); ([régime
    compensatoire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[retirement compensation benefit credit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[retirement compensation benefit credit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    the aggregate value at a particular time of the benefits that have
    accrued to a person under a retirement compensation arrangement, as
    calculated in the prescribed manner; ([droits à pension
    compensatoires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[special pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[special pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pension plan
    established by or under the authority of an order made under
    paragraph 3(1)(b). ([régime spécial de pension]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Special Pension Plans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-434289 .Part}

### [Establishment of Plans]{.HTitleText2} {#h-434290 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Plans may be established

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of the Minister, by
  order,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exempt, subject to the regulations, any person or
    class of persons from the application of the [Diplomatic Service
    (Special) Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/D-2) or the [Lieutenant
    Governors Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/L-8);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish or authorize the establishment of a
    pension plan to provide pension benefits and other benefits to or in
    respect of any person or class of persons so exempted from the
    application of that Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} designate any person as a member of a special
    pension plan or designate any class of persons as members of that
    pension plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A special pension plan shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} comply with the provisions of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) and the regulations made under that Act
    respecting registered pension plans; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide for the payment of supplementary benefits
    in relation to any pension benefit that is payable under that
    pension plan, calculated at the same rates and in the same manner as
    is provided under the [Supplementary Retirement Benefits
    Act](/eng/acts/S-24) in respect of supplementary retirement benefits
    payable under that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of the Minister, by
  order, amend or authorize the amendment of any special pension plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retroactivity of amendments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An amendment to a special pension plan may, if the
  order by which the amendment is made or is authorized to be made so
  provides, be retroactive and be deemed to come into force on a day
  prior to the day on which the order is made, which prior day shall not
  be before the day on which that pension plan is established.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Void amendments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An amendment to a special pension plan is void if the
  amendment would reduce or have the effect of reducing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a pension benefit accrued under that pension plan
    before the day on which the amendment is made; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the pension benefit credit accrued under that
    pension plan in respect of that pension benefit.

### [Contributions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-434310 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons required to contribute

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every member of a special
pension plan that is designated by the regulations is required to
contribute to the Registered Pension Plans Account, by reservation from
that member's salary or otherwise, in the manner and under the
circumstances prescribed in respect of that pension plan, at the rate or
rates established by the regulations in respect of that pension plan.

### [Registered Pension Plans Account]{.HTitleText2} {#h-434313 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of Account

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** There shall be established
in the accounts of Canada an account to be known as the Registered
Pension Plans Account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts to be credited to Account

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** There shall be credited to
the Registered Pension Plans Account in each fiscal year

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of every month, an amount equal to the
  amount estimated by the Minister to be required to provide for the
  cost of the benefits that have accrued in respect of that month and
  that will become chargeable against the Account; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an amount representing interest on the balance from
  time to time to the credit of the Account, calculated at the same
  rates and credited in the same manner and at the same time as interest
  is calculated and credited in respect of the Superannuation Account
  continued under subsection 4(2) of the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Valuation and assets reports

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  valuation report and an assets report on the state of the Registered
  Pension Plans Account shall be prepared, filed with the Minister and
  laid before Parliament in accordance with the [Public Pensions
  Reporting Act](/eng/acts/P-31.4) as if the special pension plans
  established under this Act were pension plans established under an Act
  referred to in subsection 3(1) of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review dates

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the review date
  as of which an actuarial review of the Registered Pension Plans
  Account must be conducted for the purposes of the first valuation
  report is December 31 in the year that is four years after the day on
  which this subsection comes into force and, thereafter, the review
  dates must not be more than three years apart.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts to be credited on basis of
  valuation report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Following the laying before Parliament of any
  valuation report under subsection (1), there shall be credited to the
  Registered Pension Plans Account, at the time and in the manner
  determined by the Minister, such amounts as in the opinion of the
  Minister will, at the end of the prescribed period, together with the
  amount estimated by the Minister to be to the credit of the Account at
  that time, meet the cost of the benefits chargeable against the
  Account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment out of Consolidated Revenue Fund

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Benefits and other amounts
payable under a special pension plan shall be paid out of the
Consolidated Revenue Fund and charged to the Registered Pension Plans
Account.

## [Retirement Compensation Arrangements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-434329 .Part}

### [Establishment of Arrangements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-434330 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arrangements may be established

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, on the recommendation of the Minister, by order,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish or authorize the establishment of a plan or
  an arrangement for the payment of benefits, of a kind referred to in
  the definition "retirement compensation arrangement" in subsection
  248(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), to or in respect of
  any person

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} who is required to contribute to the Superannuation
    Account referred to in section 4 of the [Public Service
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) or to the Public Service Pension
    Fund as defined in subsection 3(1) of that Act,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} who is required to contribute to the Canadian
    Forces Superannuation Account referred to in section 4 of the
    [Canadian Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17), to the
    Canadian Forces Pension Fund as defined in subsection 2(1) of that
    Act or to a fund established under regulations made under section
    59.1 of that Act,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} who is required to contribute to the Royal
    Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Account referred to in
    section 4 of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/R-11) or to the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Pension
    Fund as defined in subsection 3(1) of that Act,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} to whom the [Diplomatic Service (Special)
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/D-2) or the [Lieutenant Governors
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/L-8) applies,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} who is a member of a special pension plan,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} who, but for the provisions of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) and the regulations made under that Act
    respecting the registration of pension plans, would be able to
    accrue benefits under a special pension plan or a pension plan
    provided by an Act referred to in subparagraph (i), (ii), (iii) or
    (iv), or

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} who is prevented by an Act of Parliament from
    becoming or remaining a member of a pension plan provided by an Act
    referred to in subparagraph (i), (ii), (iii) or (iv); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designate any person referred to in paragraph (a) or
  any class of such persons as being subject to a plan or an arrangement
  that is established by or under the authority of an order made under
  that paragraph.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arrangements shall be established

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall, on the recommendation of the Minister, by
  order, as soon as is practicable after the day on which this
  subsection comes into force, establish or authorize the establishment
  of a plan or an arrangement for the payment of benefits, of a kind
  referred to in the definition "retirement compensation arrangement" in
  subsection 248(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), to or in
  respect of any person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} who, on or after that day, is required to
    contribute to the Superannuation Account referred to in section 4 of
    the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) or to the
    Public Service Pension Fund as defined in subsection 3(1) of that
    Act and whose annual rate of salary is greater than the annual rate
    of salary that is fixed by any regulations made under paragraph
    42.1(1)(a) of that Act or is greater than the annual rate that may
    be determined in the manner prescribed by those regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} who, on or after that day, is required to
    contribute to the Canadian Forces Superannuation Account referred to
    in section 4 of the [Canadian Forces Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-17), to the Canadian Forces Pension Fund as defined
    in subsection 2(1) of that Act or to a fund established under
    regulations made under section 59.1 of that Act and whose annual
    rate of pay is greater than the annual rate of pay that is fixed by
    the regulations made under paragraph 50(1)(g) of that Act or is
    greater than the annual rate that may be determined in the manner
    prescribed by those regulations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} who, on or after that day, is required to
    contribute to the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation
    Account referred to in section 4 of the [Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11) or to the Royal Canadian
    Mounted Police Pension Fund as defined in subsection 3(1) of that
    Act and whose annual rate of pay is greater than the annual rate of
    pay that is fixed by the regulations made under paragraph 26.1(1)(a)
    of that Act or is greater than the annual rate that may be
    determined in the manner prescribed by those regulations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} who is or has been a deputy head, has to the
    person's credit under the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36) not less than ten years of pensionable service
    and elects, in accordance with the regulations, before the person
    ceases to be employed in the public service, to become subject to
    such a plan or arrangement; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} who, immediately before the coming into force of
    section 11 of [An Act to amend certain Acts in relation to pensions
    and to enact the Special Retirement Arrangements Act and the Pension
    Benefits Division Act](/eng/acts/P-31.8), introduced in the third
    session of the thirty-fourth Parliament, was deemed by section 14 of
    the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), as it read
    at that time, to be employed in the Public Service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council shall, when establishing or
  authorizing the establishment of a plan or an arrangement under
  subsection (1), designate the persons who shall be subject to that
  plan or arrangement and may designate those persons either
  individually or by class of persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A plan or an arrangement that is established by or
  under the authority of an order made under subsection (1) shall
  provide for the payment of benefits to or in respect of a person
  described in that subsection that are equivalent in value to the value
  of the benefits that the person would have been able to accrue but for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subsection 5(6) of the [Public Service
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subsection 5(5) of the [Canadian Forces
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subsection 5(5) of the [Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11); or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} section 11 of [An Act to amend certain Acts in
    relation to pensions and to enact the Special Retirement
    Arrangements Act and the Pension Benefits Division
    Act](/eng/acts/P-31.8), introduced in the third session of the
    thirty-fourth Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of "deputy head"

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[deputy head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means the deputy head of a department, a person having by law the
  status of a deputy head and the chairperson, president or other chief
  executive officer of a portion of the public service, as defined in
  section 3 of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36),
  other than a department.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supplementary benefits

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A retirement compensation
arrangement shall provide for the payment of supplementary benefits in
relation to any periodic payments to which a person is entitled under
that arrangement, calculated at the same rates and in the same manner as
is provided under the [Supplementary Retirement Benefits
Act](/eng/acts/S-24) in respect of supplementary retirement benefits
payable under that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of the Minister, by
  order, amend or authorize the amendment of any retirement compensation
  arrangement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retroactivity of amendments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An amendment to a retirement compensation arrangement
  may, if the order by which the amendment is made or is authorized to
  be made so provides, be retroactive and be deemed to come into force
  on a day prior to the day on which the order is made, which prior day
  shall not be before the day on which that arrangement is established.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Void amendments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An amendment to a retirement compensation arrangement
  is void if the amendment would reduce or have the effect of reducing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a benefit accrued under that arrangement before the
    day on which the amendment is made; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the retirement compensation benefit credit accrued
    under that arrangement in respect of that benefit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advisory committees

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may establish advisory committees to advise and
  assist the Minister on matters arising in connection with the
  operation of any retirement compensation arrangement or with any
  proposal to establish a retirement compensation arrangement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Membership

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An advisory committee shall consist of eight persons
  appointed by the Governor in Council on the recommendation of the
  Minister, four of whom shall be selected from among persons who are
  subject to a retirement compensation arrangement or may become subject
  to a retirement compensation arrangement that is proposed to be
  established, as appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A member of an advisory committee shall be appointed
  to hold office for a term not exceeding three years.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-434416 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefits not assignable, etc.

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Subject to Part II of the
[Garnishment, Attachment and Pension Diversion Act](/eng/acts/G-2) and
to the [Pension Benefits Division Act](/eng/acts/P-6.7),

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a benefit under a special pension plan or retirement
  compensation arrangement is not capable of being assigned, charged,
  anticipated or given as security, and any transaction that purports to
  assign, charge, anticipate or give as security any such benefit is
  void;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a benefit to which a person is entitled under a
  special pension plan or retirement compensation arrangement is not
  capable of being surrendered or commuted during the lifetime of that
  person, and any transaction that purports to so surrender or commute
  any such benefit is void; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a benefit under a special pension plan or retirement
  compensation arrangement is exempt from attachment, seizure and
  execution, either at law or in equity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remedial action

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Where the Minister is
satisfied that, as a result of erroneous advice or an administrative
error on the part of any person acting in an official capacity in the
administration of a special pension plan, a retirement compensation
arrangement or this Act, a person has been denied a benefit to which the
person was entitled or the opportunity to exercise a right, to elect a
benefit or to opt for a benefit, the Minister may take such remedial
action as the Minister considers appropriate to place that person in the
position that the person would have been in if that erroneous advice had
not been given or that administrative error had not been made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of amounts due

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a member or former member of a special pension plan or a person
  subject to a retirement compensation arrangement dies and an amount
  payable by that member, former member or person into the Registered
  Pension Plans Account or Retirement Compensation Arrangements Account
  remains due at the time of death, that amount, together with interest
  at the prescribed rate or determined in the prescribed manner, from
  the time that amount became due, may be recovered in the prescribed
  manner from any benefit payable under that pension plan or arrangement
  in respect of that member, former member or person, without prejudice
  to any other recourse available to Her Majesty in right of Canada with
  respect to the recovery thereof.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts to be credited to appropriate
  Account

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An amount recovered under subsection (1) shall be
  credited to the Registered Pension Plans Account or the Retirement
  Compensation Arrangements Account, as appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of amounts paid in error

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the
regulations, where an amount is paid in error under a special pension
plan or retirement compensation arrangement on account of any benefit
payable on a periodic basis, the Minister may authorize the retention,
by way of deduction from any subsequent payment of that benefit, of the
amount paid in error, together with interest at the prescribed rate or
determined in the prescribed manner, from the time the amount was paid
in error, and any charge prescribed in respect thereof, without
prejudice to any other recourse available to Her Majesty in right of
Canada with respect to the recovery thereof.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall in each fiscal year prepare a report on the
  administration of this Act during the preceding fiscal year, which
  report shall include a statement showing the amounts paid into and out
  of the Registered Pension Plans Account and the Retirement
  Compensation Arrangements Account during that year, by appropriate
  classifications, the number of persons contributing to each Account
  and the number of persons receiving benefits under the special pension
  plans and retirement compensation arrangements established under this
  Act, together with such additional information as the Governor in
  Council may require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to be laid before Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause every report prepared under
  subsection (1) to be laid before each House of Parliament on any of
  the first thirty days on which that House is sitting after it is
  prepared.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Governor in Council may exempt

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, on the recommendation of the Minister, by order, exempt, subject to
the regulations, any person or class of persons from the application of
the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the [Canadian
Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17) or the [Royal Canadian
Mounted Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11), if that person or
class of persons

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} would not be eligible to receive a pension benefit
  under that Act were that Act to apply to that person or class of
  persons;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} would not be eligible to receive any additional
  pension benefit were that Act to continue to apply to that person or
  class of persons; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is subject to any other pension plan that is
  registered under the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of the Minister, make
  regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the time or times at which a person or
    class of persons becomes exempt from the application of the
    [Diplomatic Service (Special) Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/D-2) or
    the [Lieutenant Governors Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/L-8) by an
    order made under paragraph 3(1)(a) and respecting the manner in
    which and extent to which any provision of that Act or the
    regulations made under that Act applies, notwithstanding the
    exemption, to that person or class of persons and to other persons
    affected by the exemption, and adapting any such provision to those
    persons or that class of persons, where necessary for the purpose of
    providing for the consequences of the exemption or of ensuring the
    practical operation of that Act or those regulations and a special
    pension plan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the time or times at which a person
    becomes or ceases to be a member of a special pension plan or is
    deemed to become or to cease to be a member for the purposes of that
    pension plan or this Act;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} specifying the persons who may be beneficiaries
    of a pension benefit;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of section 5, designating special
    pension plans, defining the word "salary", and establishing the rate
    or rates at which persons shall contribute to the Registered Pension
    Plans Account in respect of a special pension plan;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the crediting to the Registered Pension
    Plans Account and the charging to the Consolidated Revenue Fund of
    amounts representing the aggregate value at any particular time of
    any benefits that have accrued to a person under the [Diplomatic
    Service (Special) Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/D-2) or the
    [Lieutenant Governors Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/L-8), as
    calculated in the prescribed manner;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} respecting the conditions under which and the
    extent to which the employment or service that relates to the
    benefits referred to in paragraph (d) may be counted as pensionable
    employment or service for the purposes of a special pension plan;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the time or times at which a person
    becomes or ceases to be subject to a retirement compensation
    arrangement or is deemed to become or to cease to be subject to a
    retirement compensation arrangement for the purposes of that
    arrangement or this Act;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} respecting elections under paragraph 11(1)(d);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of section 15, designating
    retirement compensation arrangements, defining the word "salary" and
    establishing the rate or rates at which persons shall contribute to
    the Retirement Compensation Arrangements Account in respect of a
    retirement compensation arrangement;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} designating Public Service corporations for the
    purposes of subsection 16(1) and determining the amounts that Public
    Service corporations shall pay into the Retirement Compensation
    Arrangements Account under that subsection;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} respecting any overpayment made out of the
    Registered Pension Plans Account or the Retirement Compensation
    Arrangements Account and respecting the deletion, writing off and
    remission of amounts required to be paid into either Account;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} respecting the administration of any special
    pension plan or retirement compensation arrangement;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} respecting the termination or winding-up of any
    special pension plan or retirement compensation arrangement;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} respecting the retention of an amount referred to
    in section 25;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} respecting the time or times at which any person or
    class of persons becomes exempt from the application of the [Public
    Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the [Canadian Forces
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17) or the [Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11) by an order made under
    section 27 and respecting the manner in which and extent to which
    any provision of that Act or the regulations made under that Act
    applies, notwithstanding the exemption, to that person or class of
    persons and to other persons affected by the exemption, and adapting
    any such provision to those persons or that class of persons, where
    necessary for the purpose of providing for the consequences of the
    exemption;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} prescribing any matter or thing that by this Act is
    to be or may be prescribed; and

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} generally as the Governor in Council may consider
    necessary for carrying out the purposes and provisions of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retroactive application of regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Regulations made under paragraph (1)(a), (b), (c),
  (h), (i) or (n) may, if they so provide, be retroactive and have
  effect with respect to any period before they are made.
