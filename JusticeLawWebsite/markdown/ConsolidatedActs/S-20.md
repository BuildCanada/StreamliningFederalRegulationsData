## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-441272 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Statute Revision
    Commission established by section 3; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Justice and
    Attorney General of Canada; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:    [[regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} statutory orders and regulations published in the
      Consolidated Regulations of Canada, 1978,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} regulations, statutory instruments and other
      documents published in the [Canada
      Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part II, after the
      publication of the Consolidated Regulations of Canada, 1978, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other regulations, statutory instruments or
      documents that, in the opinion of the Minister, are of continuing
      effect or apply to more than one person or body and that are not
      exempted from publication pursuant to regulations made under
      paragraph 20(c) of the [Statutory Instruments
      Act](/eng/acts/S-22); ([règlements]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[revision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:    [[revision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of Part I, the arrangement,
      revision and consolidation of the public general statutes of
      Canada authorized under that Part; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of Part II, the arrangement,
      revision and consolidation of the regulations authorized under
      that Part. ([révision]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-20, s. 2
- 2000, c. 5, s. 61

## [Statute Revision Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-441286 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a Statute Revision Commission consisting of three
  employees of the Department of Justice appointed thereto by the
  Minister.

  the Commission to be Chairman thereof.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Public General Statutes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-441298 .Part}

### [Revision]{.HTitleText2} {#h-441299 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revision of statutes

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Commission shall, from
time to time, revise the public general statutes of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Commission

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** In preparing a revision,
the Commission may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} omit therefrom all Acts or parts thereof that have
  expired, have been repealed or suspended, or have had their effect;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} omit therefrom all Acts or parts thereof that,
  although enacted as or in public Acts, have reference only to a
  particular country, province, locality, place or body politic, or
  otherwise have no general application;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} include therein Acts or parts thereof that, although
  enacted as or in private Acts, or although deemed to be local Acts or
  enactments, are of such a character that they impose duties or
  obligations on, or limit the rights or privileges of, the public;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} alter the numbering and arrangement of the statutes
  and of the different Parts, sections and other divisions thereof;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} make such alterations in the language of the statutes
  as may be required to preserve a uniform mode of expression, without
  changing the substance of any enactment;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} make such minor improvements in the language of the
  statutes as may be required to bring out more clearly the intention of
  Parliament, or make the form of expression of the statute in one of
  the official languages more compatible with its expression in the
  other official language, without changing the substance of any
  enactment;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} make such changes in the statutes as are required to
  reconcile seemingly inconsistent enactments; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} correct editing, grammatical or typographical errors
  in the statutes.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 20, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parliamentary examination

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During
  the progress of the preparation of a revision or on the conclusion
  thereof, or both during the progress and on the conclusion thereof,
  the Minister shall cause drafts of the statutes so revised to be laid
  for examination and approval before such Committee of the House of
  Commons and such Committee of the Senate, or such Committee of both
  Houses of Parliament, as may be designated for the purpose of the
  examination and approval.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enactment of Revised Statutes

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When drafts of all the statutes included in a
  revision have been examined and approved by the Committee or
  Committees referred to in subsection (1), the Minister shall cause to
  be prepared and introduced in Parliament a bill substantially in
  accord with the model bill set out in the schedule, or to the like
  effect.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 20, s. 7

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deposit of revision

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of a written report from the Commission in respect of the
  completion of all or any part of the Revised Regulations, the Governor
  in Council may cause a printed Roll of the regulations, attested under
  the signature of the Minister and the President of the Privy Council,
  to be deposited in the office of the Clerk of the Privy Council, and
  the Roll shall be held to be the original of the regulations included
  in it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Schedule

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} There shall be appended to each Roll a schedule
  similar in form to the Schedule to Appendix I appended to the Revised
  Statutes of Canada, 1985, and the Commission may include in the
  schedule a list of all regulations and parts of regulations that,
  although not expressly repealed, are superseded by the regulations
  included in the Roll, or are inconsistent with them, and a list of all
  regulations and parts of regulations that were for a temporary purpose
  the force of which is spent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Old regulations not revived

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  repeal of the regulations and parts of regulations listed in the
  schedule appended to a Roll does not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} revive any regulation or part of any regulation so
    repealed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} affect any saving clause in the regulations or
    parts of regulations so repealed; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prevent the application of any of those regulations
    or parts of regulations, or of any regulation or any part of a
    regulation formerly in force, to any transaction, matter or thing
    before the repeal to which they would otherwise apply.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not new law

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A regulation included in the Revised Regulations
  shall not be held to operate as a new regulation, but shall be
  construed and have effect as a consolidation and as declaratory of the
  law as contained in the regulation and parts of regulations as
  revised, and for which the regulation included in the Revised
  Regulations is substituted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where revision differs

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where, on any point, the provisions of a regulation
  included in the Revised Regulations are not in effect the same as
  those of the repealed provisions for which they are substituted, in
  respect of all transactions, matters and things subsequent to the time
  when the regulation included in the Revised Regulations takes effect,
  the provisions contained in that regulation prevail, but in respect of
  all transactions, matters and things before that time, the repealed
  provisions prevail.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction of references

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A reference in any regulation remaining in force and
  not revised, or in any instrument or document, to any regulation or
  part of a regulation repealed under subsection 13(3) by inclusion in
  the Revised Regulations shall, after the regulation in the Revised
  Regulations takes effect, be deemed, in respect of any subsequent
  transaction, matter or thing, to be a reference to the regulation or
  part of a regulation in the Revised Regulations having the same effect
  as the repealed regulation or part of a regulation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-20, s. 18
- 2000, c. 5, s. 67

### [Consolidation of the Statutes and Regulations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-441427 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to maintain

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may maintain
a consolidation of the public statutes of Canada and a consolidation of
the regulations of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 5, s. 71
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** In maintaining a
consolidation of the statutes or regulations, the Minister may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} omit any Act or regulation, or any part of an Act or
  a regulation, that has expired, has been repealed or has had its
  effect;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} include historical references or other information
  that enhances the value of the consolidation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} correct grammatical and typographical errors without
  changing the substance of any enactment; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} set out as a separate Act or regulation any Act or
  regulation enacted by another Act or regulation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 5, s. 71
:::

### [Publication and Distribution]{.HTitleText2} {#h-441440 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to publish

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may cause the consolidated statutes or consolidated
  regulations to be published in printed or electronic form, and in any
  manner and frequency that the Minister considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Differences in form

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A publication in an electronic form may differ from a
  publication in another form to accommodate the needs of the electronic
  form if the differences do not change the substance of any enactment.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 5, s. 71
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Free distribution

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** Copies of the
consolidated statutes and consolidated regulations must be distributed
without charge to the persons or classes of persons, and in the form and
manner, that the Governor in Council, on the recommendation of the
Minister, directs.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 5, s. 71

### [Effect of Consolidation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-441452 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consolidation not new law

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** The consolidated statutes
and consolidated regulations do not operate as new law.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 5, s. 71
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Published consolidation is evidence

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  copy of a consolidated statute or consolidated regulation published by
  the Minister under this Act in either print or electronic form is
  evidence of that statute or regulation and of its contents and every
  copy purporting to be published by the Minister is deemed to be so
  published, unless the contrary is shown.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistencies in Acts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of an inconsistency between a
  consolidated statute published by the Minister under this Act and the
  original statute or a subsequent amendment as certified by the Clerk
  of the Parliaments under the [Publication of Statutes
  Act](/eng/acts/S-21), the original statute or amendment prevails to
  the extent of the inconsistency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistencies in regulations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the event of an inconsistency between a
  consolidated regulation published by the Minister under this Act and
  the original regulation or a subsequent amendment as registered by the
  Clerk of the Privy Council under the [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22), the original regulation or amendment prevails to
  the extent of the inconsistency.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 5, s. 71
:::

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may enter
into agreements for the production of the consolidated statutes or
consolidated regulations and for their publication, sale or
distribution.

- 2000, c. 5, s. 71

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 7)]{.OriginatingRef}[Model Bill]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-441472 .scheduleLabel}
:::

An Act to bring into force the Revised Statutes of Canada, ![blank
line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}

[Whereas]{.Smallcaps}, pursuant to the Statute Revision Act, the Statute
Revision Commission has, under the direction of the Minister of Justice,
prepared a revision of the public general statutes of Canada, which
revision has been examined and approved by (*here specify the Committees
of the House of Commons and of the Senate or the Joint Committee of the
House and Senate, as the case may be, designated for the purpose*),
designated for the purpose of examining and approving the work of the
Statute Revision Commission;

[And Whereas]{.Smallcaps} a Statute Roll, in the form of a printed copy
of the revision in ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}
volumes, has been prepared, attested at the beginning of each volume
under the signature of His Excellency, the Governor General,
countersigned by the Minister of Justice, and certified correct by the
Chairman of the Statute Revision Commission, and deposited in the office
of the Clerk of the Parliaments on the ![blank
line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot} day of ![blank
line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}, 19![blank
line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot};

[And Whereas]{.Smallcaps} the Statute Revision Commission has
recommended that the Acts and portions of Acts listed in the schedule
entitled Schedule I, attached to the Statute Roll, be repealed on the
coming into force of the Revised Statutes of Canada, ![blank
line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot};

[And Whereas]{.Smallcaps} it is expedient to confirm the Statute Roll
and to give the statutes set out therein the force and effect of law as
the Revised Statutes of Canada, ![blank
line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}, subject to the limitations
stated in this Act;

[Now]{.Smallcaps}, [Therefore]{.Smallcaps}, Her Majesty, by and with the
advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts
as follows:

### [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#short-title .SchedHeadL1}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

  **1** This Act may be cited as the *Revised Statutes of Canada*
  (*year*) *Act*.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confirmation of Statute Roll

  - **2** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section and to the exceptions
    or limitations mentioned in this Act, the Statute Roll deposited in
    the office of the Clerk of the Parliaments on the ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot} day of ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}, 19![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot} and the several parts and
    sections therein contained, being comprised within the chapters
    numbered from ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot} to
    ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot} as therein set out,
    are hereby declared to be, and are, law by the designation of the
    "Revised Statutes of Canada, ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}" in English and « *Lois
    révisées du Canada de*![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}
    » in French, to all intents and purposes as though the Statute Roll
    and those statutes therein contained, and the several chapters,
    parts and sections thereof, were expressly embodied in and enacted
    by this Act.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement of Revised Statutes

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this Act, the Revised Statutes of
    Canada, ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot} (in this Act
    called the "Revised Statutes") come into force, and have operation
    and effect as law, as provided in this Act, on the ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot} day of ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}, 19![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acts to be proclaimed

    [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an Act included in the Revised Statutes
    contains a provision stating that the Act or any portion thereof is
    to come into force on a day to be fixed by proclamation, that Act or
    that portion thereof shall not be deemed to be in force solely by
    reason of subsections (1) and (2), but the Act or portion thereof
    comes into force only on the day fixed by proclamation for the
    coming into force thereof, or of that portion thereof, as provided
    in that Act.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulation-making power

    [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where an Act included in the Revised Statutes, or
    any provision thereof, confers power to make regulations or to
    prescribe forms, that power, for the purpose of making the Act or
    the provision effective at the date of its coming into force, may be
    exercised at any time after this Act comes into force, but any
    regulation or form so made or prescribed does not come into force or
    have effect until the Act conferring the power comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enactments repealed

  **3** On the ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot} day of
  ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}, 19![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}, all the provisions in the
  several Acts and parts of Acts listed in Schedule I to the Statute
  Roll are repealed to the extent mentioned in the third column of that
  Schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dead law not revived

  **4** The repeal of the Acts and parts of Acts listed in Schedule I to
  the Statute Roll does not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} revive any Act or provision of law repealed by
    them;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} affect any saving clause in the Acts or parts of
    Acts so repealed; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prevent the application of any of those Acts or
    parts of Acts, or of any Act or provision of law formerly in force,
    to any transaction, matter or thing anterior to the repeal to which
    they would otherwise apply.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operation of Revised Statutes

  **5** The Revised Statutes shall not be held to operate as new law,
  but shall be construed and have effect as a consolidation and as
  declaratory of the law as contained in the Acts and parts of Acts
  repealed by section 3, and for which the Revised Statutes are
  substituted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References to repealed enactments

  **6** A reference in any Act enacted before the coming into force of
  the Revised Statutes and remaining in force after that time, or in any
  instrument or document, to any Act or enactment repealed under section
  3, shall, after the Revised Statutes take effect, be deemed, in
  respect of any subsequent transaction, matter or thing, to be a
  reference to the enactment in the Revised Statutes having the same
  effect as the repealed Act or enactment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of inclusion in Schedule I

  **7** The inclusion of any Act in Schedule I to the Statute Roll shall
  not be considered as a declaration that the Act or any part of it was
  or was not in force immediately before the coming into force of the
  Revised Statutes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Publication of Statutes
  Act](/eng/acts/S-21)

  - **8** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The [Publication of Statutes
    Act](/eng/acts/S-21) does not apply to the Revised Statutes.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Printing and distribution of Revised
    Statutes

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Revised Statutes shall be printed and bound in
    such style or form as the Statute Revision Commission deems best
    suited to the Revised Statutes and copies of the Revised Statutes
    shall be distributed without charge to such persons or classes of
    persons as the Governor in Council directs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Citation of Revised Statutes

  **9** A chapter of the Revised Statutes may be cited and referred to
  in any Act, regulation, proceeding, instrument or document whatever
  either by its short or long title as an Act or by using the expression
  "Revised Statutes, 19![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot},
  chapter ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}", or "Chapter
  ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot} of the Revised
  Statutes", or the abbreviation "R.S.C. 19![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}, c. ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}", or "R.S., c. ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}", adding in each case the
  number of the particular chapter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of this Act

  **10** A copy of this Act may be included in one of the volumes that
  contain the Revised Statutes in addition to being printed and
  published with the statutes enacted at the session of Parliament in
  which this Act is passed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of appendices, etc.

  - **11** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Statute Revision Commission may cause to
    be printed, and included in the volumes that contain the Revised
    Statutes,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appendices prepared by the Statute Revision
      Commission containing statutes of the Parliament of the United
      Kingdom and of the Parliament of Canada and other documents
      relating to the Constitution of Canada or of any of its provinces
      or territories;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} schedules prepared by the Statute Revision
      Commission of similar character to those published with the
      Revised Statutes of Canada, 1970, with any alterations or
      additions that seem proper; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an index to the Revised Statutes.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Constitutional statutes

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Statute Revision Commission may cause a public
    general statute of Canada that is of a constitutional or
    quasi-constitutional nature to be printed with the appendices
    referred to in subsection (1) and may thereupon omit any such
    statute from the volumes of the Revised Statutes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supplements

  - **12** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After the completion of the Revised
    Statutes, the Statute Revision Commission may, if the convenience of
    the public will be served thereby, prepare, under and in accordance
    with the Statute Revision Act, supplements to the Revised Statutes
    showing, as amendments or additions to the Revised Statutes,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the public general statutes of Canada passed
      after the completion of the Statute Roll but before the coming
      into force of the Revised Statutes,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other public general statutes of Canada not
      included in the Statute Roll that the Commission may consider
      advisable to add thereto, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any corrections required to be made to the
      Statute Roll by reason of any omission or error in its
      preparation, editing or printing,

    and the provisions of this Act applicable to or in respect of the
    Revised Statutes apply, with such modifications as the circumstances
    require, to and in respect of those supplements.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Part of Revised Statutes

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} A supplement to the Revised Statutes shall be
    deemed to be included in and to be part of the Revised Statutes, and
    the citation of any chapter of the Revised Statutes in accordance
    with section 9 shall be deemed to include any amendments thereto
    contained in a supplement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notes and tables

  - **13** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any explanatory notes and tables inserted by
    the Statute Revision Commission in the Revised Statutes form no part
    of those statutes and shall be held to have been inserted for
    convenience only.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Text of orders

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} The texts of orders in council inserted by the
    Statute Revision Commission in the Revised Statutes shall be held to
    have been inserted for convenience only and shall not be held to
    have affected the authority to revoke or replace the orders in
    council so inserted.
