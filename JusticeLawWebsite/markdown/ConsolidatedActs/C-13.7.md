## Canadian Commercial Bank Financial Assistance Act {#canadian-commercial-bank-financial-assistance-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1985, c. 9
:::

Assented to 1985-03-29

An Act respecting the provision of financial assistance to the Canadian
Commercial Bank
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61652 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canadian Commercial Bank Financial Assistance
Act](/eng/acts/C-13.7).

## [Financial Assistance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61655 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement to provide financial assistance

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of State (Finance) may, on behalf of Her Majesty, enter into
  one or more agreements to provide financial assistance to the Canadian
  Commercial Bank in furtherance of the points of agreement, and other
  matters agreed on only in principle, that are set out in the
  Memorandum of Intent tabled in the House of Commons by the Minister on
  March 28, 1985 and recorded as sessional paper number 331-7/11.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of State (Finance) may, for the purpose
  of carrying out the terms of an agreement entered into under
  subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} acquire, hold or dispose of participation
    certificates in a portfolio of assets of the Canadian Commercial
    Bank comprising loans and securities relating thereto;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} acquire, hold or dispose of transferable rights or
    warrants to purchase common shares of the Canadian Commercial Bank;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} acquire, hold or dispose of bank debentures issued
    by the Canadian Commercial Bank; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} enter into any agreement or arrangement necessary
    or incidental to any activity referred to in paragraph (a), (b)
    or (c) or enter into any agreement or arrangement in order to comply
    with conditions set out in the Memorandum of Intent referred to in
    subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Moneys

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby
appropriated, for the purposes of section 2, seventy-five million
dollars to be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund from time to
time as required.
