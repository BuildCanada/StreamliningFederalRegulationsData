## An Act to amend the Constitution Act, 1867 (electoral representation) {#an-act-to-amend-the-constitution-act-1867-electoral-representation .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2022, c. 6
:::

Assented to 2022-06-23

An Act to amend the Constitution Act, 1867 (electoral representation)
::::

:::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Alternative Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1362079 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Preserving Provincial Representation in the House of Commons
Act](/eng/acts/C-36.71).

## [[30-31 Vict., c. 3 (U.K.); 1982, c. 11 (U.K.)]{.HistoricalNote}]{.MarginalNoteInHeading}[Constitution Act, 1867]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1362082 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[2011, c. 26, s. 2]{.HistoricalNote}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Rule 2 of subsection 51(1)
of the [Constitution Act, 1867](/eng/Const/) is replaced by the
following:

:::: section
::: AmendedText
- ::::: listItemBlock1
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2.]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText2
  If the number of members assigned to a province by the application of
  rule 1 and section 51A is less than the total number assigned to that
  province during the 43rd Parliament, there shall be added to the
  number of members so assigned the number of members that will result
  in the province having the same number of members as were assigned
  during that Parliament.
  :::
  :::::
:::
::::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1362089 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed reference

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A reference to the
[Constitution Acts, 1867 to 1982](/eng/Const/) is deemed to include a
reference to section 2.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1362092 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this section and section 5.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Electoral Boundaries
      Readjustment Act](/eng/acts/E-3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[old calculation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[old calculation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the calculation of
      the number of members of the House of Commons to be assigned to
      each province done by the Chief Electoral Officer under subsection
      14(1) of the Act, the statement of the results of which was
      published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) on
      October 16, 2021. ([calcul antérieur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Words and expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context otherwise requires, words and
  expressions used in this section and section 5 have the same meaning
  as in the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New calculation

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As soon
  as feasible after the day on which this section comes into force, the
  Chief Electoral Officer must calculate the number of members of the
  House of Commons to be assigned to each province under subsection
  14(1) of the Act, subject and according to the provisions of section
  51 of the [Constitution Act, 1867](/eng/Const/) and the rules set out
  in that section as they are amended by section 2, and on completing
  that calculation must cause a statement to be published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) setting out the results.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Province with different number of
  members resulting from new calculation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the calculation referred to in subsection (1)
  results in a different number of members of the House of Commons for a
  province than was obtained under the old calculation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any report referred to in subsection 14(2) of the
    Act that is prepared in respect of the province on the basis of the
    old calculation, and anything done under sections 19 to 23 of the
    Act on the basis of the report, are nullities;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the commission established for the province under
    section 3 of the Act in respect of the most recent decennial census
    must prepare a report under subsection 14(2) of the Act in respect
    of the province on the basis of the calculation referred to in
    subsection (1); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the duties and functions set out in sections 19 to
    23 of the Act are to be performed in respect of the province
    following the preparation of the report under paragraph (b), and the
    10-month period referred to in subsection 20(1) of the Act is, in
    respect of the province, deemed to begin on the day on which the
    statement referred to in subsection (1) is published in the [Canada
    Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No representation order before coming
  into force

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, before the day on which this section comes into
  force, no representation order was prepared and transmitted under
  subsection 24(1) of the Act on the basis of the old calculation, the
  Chief Electoral Officer must --- without waiting for the report
  prepared under subsection 20(1) of the Act for a province referred to
  in subsection (2) --- prepare and transmit a representation order
  under subsection 24(1) of the Act that applies in respect of all
  provinces other than that province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation order before coming into
  force

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, before the day on which this section comes into
  force, a representation order was prepared and transmitted under
  subsection 24(1) of the Act on the basis of the old calculation, that
  representation order and any proclamation made under subsection 25(1)
  of the Act that declares it to be in force are nullities only in
  respect of a province referred to in subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation order and proclamation
  --- province referred to in subsection (2)

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If subsection (3) or (4) applies, a representation
  order is to be prepared under subsection 24(1) of the Act, and a
  proclamation is to be made under subsection 25(1) of the Act, in
  respect of a province referred to in subsection (2) and on the basis
  of the calculation referred to in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Previous representation order applies

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the representation order
  annexed to the [Proclamation Declaring the Representation Order to be
  in Force Effective on the First Dissolution of Parliament that Occurs
  after May 1, 2014](/eng/regulations/SI-2013-102), registered as
  SI/2013-102, continues to apply in respect of a province referred to
  in subsection (2) until the representation order referred to in
  subsection (5) becomes effective under subsection 25(1) or (2) of the
  Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrative consolidation

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} When the representation order and proclamation
  referred to in subsection (5) are published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) under section 26 of the Act, the
  Chief Electoral Officer must publish on their website an
  administrative consolidation consisting of that representation order
  and the portion of the representation order referred to in
  subsection (3) or (4), as the case may be, that applies in respect of
  all provinces other than a province referred to in subsection (2). The
  consolidation must set out, in respect of each province, the day on
  which the applicable representation order came into force.
