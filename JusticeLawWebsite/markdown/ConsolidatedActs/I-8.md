## Industrial and Regional Development Act {#industrial-and-regional-development-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. I-8
:::

An Act to provide for financial assistance for industrial development in
all regions of Canada
::::

::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-333261 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Industrial and Regional Development Act](/eng/acts/I-8).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 160, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-333266 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[approved lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approved lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an approved lender as
    defined by the regulations; ([prêteur agréé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[commercial operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commercial
    operation as defined by the regulations and includes such class of
    operations within the service industry as are designated by the
    Minister pursuant to subsection 7(2); ([affaire
    commerciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[development index]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[development index]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the index that may
    be established pursuant to regulations made under paragraph
    15(a); ([indice de développement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[district]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[district]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a census division established
    by Statistics Canada for the purpose of tabulating and publishing
    census data; ([district]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who carries
    on activities that support commercial operations and, without
    limiting the generality of the foregoing, includes an economic,
    business or technological institute or centre, a municipal
    corporation or a municipal industrial development
    corporation; ([personne admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to any class of
    matters to which this Act extends in any province or region of a
    province, means such member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada
    as is designated by the Governor in Council as the Minister for the
    purposes of this Act in relation to that class of matters in that
    province or region of a province; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by regulations
    of the Governor in Council; ([Version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include Yukon, the
    Northwest Territories or Nunavut. ([province]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-8, s. 2
- R.S., 1985, c. 11 (4th Supp.), s. 14
- 1993, c. 28, s. 78
- 2002, c. 7, s. 185(E)

## [Tier Groups]{.HTitleText1} {#h-333279 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arrangement of districts into Tier Groups

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On or
  before July 1 of each year, the Minister shall, in accordance with
  subsection (2), arrange all districts into four groups, in this Act
  called Tier Groups.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In arranging districts into Tier Groups, the Minister
  shall ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one Tier Group, called Tier Group IV, includes
    those districts, comprising not more than five per cent of the
    population of the provinces, that, as ranked under the development
    index, require the greatest assistance in creating opportunities for
    economic growth;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one Tier Group, called Tier Group III, includes

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} those districts, comprising not more than fifteen
      per cent of the population of the provinces, that, as ranked under
      the development index, require the greatest assistance, after
      those districts in Tier Group IV, in creating opportunities for
      economic growth, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Yukon, the Northwest Territories and Nunavut;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one Tier Group, called Tier Group II, includes
    those districts, comprising not more than thirty per cent of the
    population of the provinces, that, as ranked under the development
    index, require the greatest assistance, after those districts in
    Tier Groups III and IV, in creating opportunities for economic
    growth; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} one Tier Group, called Tier Group I, includes all
    districts in the provinces not included in Tier Group II, III or IV.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-8, s. 3
- 1993, c. 28, s. 78
- 2002, c. 7, s. 186(E)
:::

## [Grants, Contributions, Loans and Guarantees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-333293 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grants, contributions and loans in
respect of projects of commercial operations

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this Act, on application therefor to the Minister by a commercial
  operation, the Minister may, in accordance with the regulations, make
  a grant, contribution or loan to the commercial operation in respect
  of a project carried out or to be carried out by the commercial
  operation for the purpose of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establishing, expanding, modernizing or enhancing
    the productivity of the commercial operation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} promoting the marketing of one or more products or
    services of the commercial operation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} developing new or improved products or production
    processes of the commercial operation, or carrying on research in
    respect thereof; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} restructuring the commercial operation so as to
    enable it to continue on a commercially viable basis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Guarantees in respect of commercial
  operations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this Act, on application therefor to the
  Minister by a commercial operation, the Minister may, in accordance
  with the regulations, guarantee the repayment of any portion of the
  principal and interest owing on any loan made by an approved lender to
  the commercial operation in respect of a project carried out or to be
  carried out by the commercial operation for a purpose referred to in
  paragraphs (1)(a) to (d).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 160, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grants, contributions and loans to
eligible persons

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this Act, on application therefor to the Minister by an eligible
  person, the Minister may, in accordance with the regulations, make a
  grant, contribution or loan to the eligible person, if the eligible
  person supports, directly or indirectly, commercial operations by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} carrying on activities that provide a framework for
    the establishment, modernization or expansion of a commercial
    operation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} promoting the marketing of one or more products or
    services of commercial operations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} carrying on research in respect of the development
    of new or improved products or production processes; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} providing services, or engaging in other
    activities, of benefit to commercial operations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Guarantees in respect of eligible
  persons

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this Act, on application therefor to the
  Minister by an eligible person, the Minister may, in accordance with
  the regulations, guarantee the repayment of any portion of the
  principal and interest owing on any loan made by an approved lender to
  an eligible person if the eligible person supports, directly or
  indirectly, commercial operations in a manner described in paragraphs
  (1)(a) to (d).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 160, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms and conditions in respect of
projects of commercial operations

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  grant, contribution or loan made, or any guarantee given, in respect
  of a project carried out or to be carried out by a commercial
  operation under section 4 shall be made or given in accordance with
  the terms and conditions prescribed in respect of the Tier Group that
  includes the district in which the project is carried out or is to be
  carried out.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms and conditions in respect of
  eligible person

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any grant, contribution or loan made to, or any
  guarantee given in respect of, an eligible person by virtue of his
  carrying on activities described in paragraph 5(1)(a) in respect of a
  commercial operation shall be made or given in accordance with the
  terms and conditions prescribed in respect of the Tier Group that
  includes the district in which the commercial operation is located.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 160, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where commercial operation in more than
one district

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  project or a commercial operation is located in more than one
  district, it shall, for the purposes of this Act, be deemed to be
  located in such district as the Minister may determine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of operations within the
  service industry

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by order, designate any class of
  operations within the service industry as commercial operations for
  the purposes of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 160, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where no contribution, loan, grant or
guarantee permitted

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No contribution, loan or
grant may be made to, or guarantee given in respect of, a commercial
operation or an eligible person under this Act if, in the opinion of the
Minister,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is probable that the project carried out or to be
  carried out by the commercial operation or the activity of the
  eligible person by which the eligible person supports commercial
  operations would proceed or would continue in the actual or proposed
  location, within the actual or proposed scope and within the actual or
  proposed time, whether or not the grant, contribution or loan is made
  or the guarantee given; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the project or the activity will not or would not
  make a significant contribution to the economic or social benefit of
  Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 160, s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stock options

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the regulations,
the Minister may acquire, exercise, assign or sell a stock option
obtained as a condition under which a contribution or loan was made or
guarantee given under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 160, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation on loans guaranteed

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The aggregate amount of
guarantees given under this Act and insurance of loans provided for by
subparagraph (a)(i) of Industry, Trade and Commerce Vote 1a of
Appropriation Act No. 1, 1980-81, chapter 3 of the Statutes of Canada,
1980-81-82-83, as amended by paragraph (a) of Industry, Trade and
Commerce Vote 1e of Appropriation Act No. 4, 1981-82, chapter 90 of the
Statutes of Canada, 1980-81-82-83, that are outstanding at any one time
shall not exceed one billion two hundred million dollars.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 160, s. 10
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of contributions

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a contribution is made to a commercial operation or an eligible person
  under this Act and for any reason

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the commercial operation or eligible person is not
    entitled to the contribution,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the contribution exceeds the amount
    to which the commercial operation or eligible person is entitled, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a term or condition to which the contribution is
    subject has not been met or has been contravened,

  the amount of the contribution or excess, as the case may be, together
  with interest determined as prescribed and computed from the date the
  contribution is made, is a debt due to Her Majesty in right of Canada
  and may be recovered as such from the commercial operation or eligible
  person in any court of competent jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No recovery in certain cases

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a circumstance referred to in paragraph
  (1)(a), (b) or (c) occurs in respect of a contribution made to a
  commercial operation or an eligible person under this Act and the
  Minister has, under such conditions as are prescribed, advised the
  commercial operation or eligible person in writing that the amount of
  the contribution will not be recovered from the commercial operation
  or eligible person in that circumstance, subsection (1) shall,
  effective on the date the circumstance occurred, cease to apply in
  respect of the contribution.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 160, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Every one who, under this
Act, submits any information or documentation, makes any statement or
answers any question, whether in connection with an application for a
grant, contribution, loan or guarantee or otherwise, knowing that the
information, documentation, statement or answer is false or misleading
or misrepresents or fails to disclose a material fact, is guilty of an
offence and liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding ten
  thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year
  or to both; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine not exceeding
  one hundred thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not
  exceeding five years or to both.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 160, s. 12
:::

## [Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-333361 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Boards may be established

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may establish boards to advise him with regard to the
  administration of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and expenses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each member of a board established under
  subsection (1) is entitled to be paid, for each day that the member
  performs duties under this Act, such remuneration as is fixed by the
  Governor in Council and reasonable travel and other expenses incurred
  by him while absent from his ordinary place of residence in the course
  of those duties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A board established under subsection (1) may exercise
  such powers and perform such duties and functions as may be
  prescribed.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 160, s. 14
:::

## [Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-333371 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Parliament

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall cause to be laid before each House of Parliament, not
  later than June 1 next following the end of each fiscal year, or, if
  Parliament is not then sitting, on the first day thereafter that
  Parliament is sitting, a report respecting the administration of this
  Act for that fiscal year to the extent that information is available
  at the end of that fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference of report to Committee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every report laid before Parliament under this
  section stands permanently referred to any Committee of Parliament
  designated or established for the purpose of reviewing matters
  relating to regional and industrial expansion.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 160, s. 15
:::

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-333379 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} providing the method of establishing a development
  index, which would include a ranking for each district in a province
  having regard, with respect to each district, to the per capita level
  of income in the district, the level of employment in the district and
  the capacity of the province in which the district is located to raise
  revenue;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} defining, for the purposes of this Act, the
  expressions "commercial operation" and "approved lender";

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} determining the scope of any of the activities
  referred to in paragraphs 4(1)(a) to (d) or 5(1)(a) to (d);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} prescribing, generally or with respect to each Tier
  Group, terms on which and conditions under which grants, contributions
  and loans may be made or guarantees given pursuant to this Act;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} establishing the method of calculating the maximum
  amount, generally or with respect to each Tier Group, of a grant,
  contribution or loan that may be made or guarantee that may be given
  under this Act;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} prescribing, generally or with respect to each Tier
  Group, the type of costs and activities in respect of which a grant,
  contribution or loan may be made or guarantee given under this Act;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} specifying the circumstances in which and the manner
  in which the Minister may acquire, exercise, assign or sell a stock
  option obtained as a condition under which a contribution or loan was
  made or guarantee given under this Act;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that by this Act is to be
  prescribed; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} generally, for carrying out the purposes and
  provisions of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 160, s. 16
:::

## [Expiration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-333393 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expiration

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** No grant, contribution,
loan or guarantee may be made under this Act if the application therefor
is received after June 30, 1988.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 160, s. 17
:::
