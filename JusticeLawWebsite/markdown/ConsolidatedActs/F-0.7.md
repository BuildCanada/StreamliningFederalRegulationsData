### [DIVISION 1]{.HLabel2}[Canada Growth Fund]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1384049 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acquisition of shares

- **[[118]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-118 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Finance may acquire and hold on behalf of His Majesty in
  right of Canada non-voting shares of a corporation that is
  incorporated as a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Canada Development
  Investment Corporation and that is responsible for administering the
  Canada Growth Fund.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consolidated Revenue Fund

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the requisition of the Minister of Finance, there
  may be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund amounts not exceeding
  \$15,000,000,000 in the aggregate, or any greater amount that is
  specified in an appropriation Act, for the acquisition of shares under
  subsection (1).

  Act](/eng/acts/F-11.8), chapter 24 of the Statutes of Canada, 1999.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other definitions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In sections 123 and 124, [[First Nation
  law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[Framework
  Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[individual
  agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
  [[land code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and
  [[right]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have the same meaning as in subsection
  2(1) of the former Act, as it read immediately before the day on which
  section 143 of this Act comes into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents and decisions previously made

**[[123]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-123 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act
affects

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any land codes, individual agreements, First Nation
  laws or documents, including licences and other authorizations,
  contracts and other instruments or acts, that were issued, granted,
  entered into or made in accordance with the former Act or the
  Framework Agreement and that are in force on the day on which this Act
  comes into force;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any actions taken or decisions made, before the day
  on which this Act comes into force, in accordance with the former Act
  or the Framework Agreement, including the registration of an interest
  or right in the First Nation Land Register, as established under
  section 25 of the former Act and the establishment of priorities or,
  in Quebec, rankings among interests or rights that have been
  registered.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to former Act

**[[124]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-124 .sectionLabel}** Unless the context
otherwise requires, any reference to the former Act, or any provision of
that Act, in any land codes, individual agreements, First Nation laws or
documents referred to in paragraph 123(a) is to be read as a reference
to the [Framework Agreement on First Nation Land Management
Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46) or the Framework Agreement, or any relevant
provision of that Act or Agreement, as the case may be.

  [[apprentice loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[apprentice loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 1(1) of the [Apprentice Loans
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-255). ([prêt aux
      apprentis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 1(1) of the [Apprentice Loans
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-255). ([emprunteur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[transition period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transition period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period
      beginning on April 1, 2023 and ending on the day on which a
      regulation amending or repealing section 4 of the [Apprentice
      Loans Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-255) comes into
      force. ([période de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repayment of principal and interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} During the transition period, a borrower must begin
  to repay the principal amount of an apprentice loan made to them, and
  any interest, on the last day of the month in which interest would
  have started accruing under the [Apprentice Loans
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-255) but for subsection 8(1) of
  the [Apprentice Loans Act](/eng/acts/A-11.9), as enacted by section
  164.
