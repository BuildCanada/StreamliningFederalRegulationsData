## Manitoba Supplementary Provisions Act {#manitoba-supplementary-provisions-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"} 1927, c. 124
:::

An Act respecting the Province of Manitoba
::::

::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348510 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Manitoba Supplementary Provisions Act](/eng/acts/M-0.54).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 99, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348515 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act, unless the
context otherwise requires,

- [[Commissioners]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[Commissioners]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the
  commissioner in cases in which the commission is issued to one person
  only;

- [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(b)]{.lawlabel} [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister
  of the Interior;

- [[Province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[Province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the province
  of Manitoba.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 99. s. 2
:::

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348526 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allotment of 150,000 acres for a
university

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An allotment of land, not
exceeding one hundred and fifty thousand acres, of fair average quality,
shall be selected by the Dominion Government and granted as an endowment
to the University of Manitoba for its maintenance as a university
capable of giving proper training in the higher branches of education,
and to be held in trust for that purpose upon some basis or scheme to be
framed by the University and approved by the Dominion Government.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 99, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laws in force in Manitoba

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the provisions
of this Act, the laws of England relating to matters within the
jurisdiction of the Parliament of Canada, as the same existed on the
fifteenth day of July, one thousand eight hundred and seventy, were from
the said day and are in force in the Province, in so far as applicable
to the Province, and in so far as the said laws have not been or are not
hereafter repealed, altered, varied, modified or affected by any Act of
the Parliament of Great Britain applicable to the Province, or of the
Parliament of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 99, s. 6
:::
