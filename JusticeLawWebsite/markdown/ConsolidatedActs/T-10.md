## Textile Labelling Act {#textile-labelling-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. T-10
:::

An Act respecting the labelling, sale, importation and advertising of
consumer textile articles
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448588 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Textile Labelling Act](/eng/acts/T-10).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 46(1st. Supp.), s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448593 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[advertise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[advertise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means make any representation to
    the public by any means whatever, except a representation on a
    label, for the purpose of promoting directly or indirectly the sale
    of a textile fibre product; ([publicité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}
    ou [annonce]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[analyst]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[analyst]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated as an
    analyst by the Commissioner pursuant to section
    7; ([analyste]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[apply]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[apply]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a label, to
    attach to, imprint on, include in or cause to accompany in any other
    way a textile fibre product; ([Version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commissioner of
    Competition appointed under the [Competition
    Act](/eng/acts/C-34); ([commissaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[consumer textile article]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[consumer textile article]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any textile fibre, yarn or fabric, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any product made in whole or in part from a
      textile fibre, yarn or fabric

    that is in the form in which it is or is to be sold to any person
    for consumption or use, other than consumption or use in the
    manufacturing, processing or finishing of any product for
    sale; ([article textile de consommation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is a manufacturer,
    processor or finisher of a textile fibre product or a person who is
    engaged in the business of importing or selling any textile fibre
    product; ([fournisseur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fabric]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fabric]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any material woven, knitted,
    crocheted, knotted, braided, felted, bonded, laminated or otherwise
    produced from, or in combination with, a textile
    fibre; ([tissu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated as an
    inspector pursuant to the [Department of Industry
    Act](/eng/acts/I-9.2) for the purpose of the enforcement of this
    Act; ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any label, mark, sign, device,
    imprint, stamp, brand or ticket; ([Version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Industry; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by the
    regulations; ([Version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} offer for sale, expose for sale and have in
      possession for sale, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} display in such manner as to lead to a reasonable
      belief that the product so displayed is intended for
      sale; ([vendre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[textile fibre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[textile fibre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any natural or
    manufactured matter that is capable of being made into a yarn or
    fabric and, without limiting the generality of the foregoing,
    includes human hair, kapok, feathers and down and animal hair or fur
    that has been removed from an animal skin; ([fibre
    textile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[textile fibre product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[textile fibre product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any consumer textile article, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any textile fibre, yarn or fabric used or to be
      used in a consumer textile article. ([produit de fibres
      textiles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-10, s. 2
- 1992, c. 1, s. 145(F)
- 1995, c. 1, ss. 62, 63
- 1999, c. 2, ss. 52, 53
:::

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448619 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition respecting consumer textile
articles

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No dealer shall sell,
import into Canada or advertise

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a prescribed consumer textile article unless the
  article has applied to it a label containing a representation with
  respect to the textile fibre content of the article; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any consumer textile article that has applied to it a
  label containing a representation with respect to the textile fibre
  content of the article unless the label is applied to it in accordance
  with and complies with all applicable provisions of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 46(1st Supp.), s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition respecting advertising

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No dealer shall, in
advertising a consumer textile article, make any representation with
respect to the textile fibre content of the article except in accordance
with the regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 46(1st Supp.), s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representations relating to consumer
textile articles

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  dealer shall apply to a consumer textile article a label, or sell,
  import into Canada or advertise a consumer textile article that has
  applied to it a label containing any false or misleading
  representation that relates to or may reasonably be regarded as
  relating to the article.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representations relating to textile
  fibre products

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No dealer shall, by means of a label, advertising or
  otherwise, make any false or misleading representation that relates to
  or may reasonably be regarded as relating to a textile fibre product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of "false or misleading
  representation"

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, [[false or
  misleading representation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any representation in which expressions, words,
    figures, depictions or symbols are arranged or shown in a manner
    that may reasonably be regarded as likely to deceive any person with
    respect to textile fibre content;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any expression, word, figure, depiction or symbol
    that implies or may reasonably be regarded as implying that a
    textile fibre product contains any fibre, fur or hair not contained
    in the product; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any description of the type, quality, performance,
    origin or method of manufacture or production of a textile fibre
    product that may reasonably be regarded as likely to deceive any
    person with respect to the matter so described.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 46(1st Supp.), s. 5
:::

## [Labels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448642 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Label containing representation
respecting textile fibre content

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Each label containing a
representation with respect to the textile fibre content of the consumer
textile article to which it is applied shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be applied to the article in such form and manner as
  may be prescribed; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} show, in such form and manner as may be prescribed,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the generic name of each textile fibre comprising
    five per cent or more by mass of the total fibre mass of the
    article,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} subject to the regulations, such percentage by
    mass of the total fibre mass of the article as each textile fibre
    named pursuant to subparagraph (i) comprises,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the person by or for whom the
    consumer textile article was manufactured or made, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} such other information and representations as may
    be required by the regulations to be included in the label.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-10, s. 6
- 1993, c. 34, s. 119
:::

## [Analysts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448653 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of analysts by Commissioner

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner may
designate as an analyst for the purposes of this Act any person who, in
his opinion, is qualified to be so designated.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-10, s. 7
- 1999, c. 2, s. 53
:::

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448658 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of inspectors

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (1.1), an inspector may at any reasonable time enter any
  premises of a dealer or any other place in which the inspector
  believes on reasonable grounds there is any textile fibre product that
  is owned by a dealer and may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine any textile fibre product found therein;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} open and examine any package found therein that the
    inspector believes on reasonable grounds contains any textile fibre
    product; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} examine any books, reports, test data, records,
    shipping bills and bills of lading or other documents or papers that
    the inspector believes on reasonable grounds contain any information
    relevant to the enforcement of this Act and make copies thereof or
    extracts therefrom.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant required to enter
  dwelling-house

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Where any place or premises referred to in
  subsection (1) is a dwelling-house, an inspector may not enter that
  dwelling-house without the consent of the occupant except under the
  authority of a warrant issued under subsection (1.2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} Where on [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  application a justice of the peace is satisfied by information on oath

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the conditions for entry described in
    subsection (1) exist in relation to a dwelling-house,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that entry to the dwelling-house is necessary for
    any purpose relating to the administration or enforcement of this
    Act, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that entry to the dwelling-house has been refused
    or that there are reasonable grounds for believing that entry
    thereto will be refused,

  the justice of the peace may issue a warrant under his hand
  authorizing the inspector named therein to enter that dwelling-house
  subject to such conditions as may be specified in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

  [(1.3)]{.lawlabel} In executing a warrant issued under subsection
  (1.2), the inspector named therein shall not use force unless the
  inspector is accompanied by a peace officer and the use of force has
  been specifically authorized in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate to be produced

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An inspector shall be furnished with a certificate of
  his designation as an inspector and, on entering any place described
  in subsection (1), shall, if so required, produce the certificate to
  the person in charge of that place.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to inspectors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The owner or person in charge of a place referred to
  in subsection (1) and every person found in that place shall give the
  inspector all reasonable assistance to enable the inspector to carry
  out his duties and functions under this Act and shall furnish the
  inspector with any information he may reasonably require with respect
  to the administration of this Act and the regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-10, s. 8
- R.S., 1985, c. 31 (1st Supp.), s. 24
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall obstruct or hinder an inspector in the carrying out of
  his duties and functions under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False statements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall knowingly make any false or
  misleading statement, either orally or in writing, to an inspector
  engaged in carrying out his duties and functions under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interference

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Except with the authority of an inspector, no person
  shall remove, alter or interfere in any way with any textile fibre
  product or other thing detained by an inspector pursuant to the
  regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 46(1st Supp.), s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seizure

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an inspector believes on reasonable grounds that any provision of this
  Act or the regulations has been contravened, the inspector may seize
  and detain any textile fibre product or any labelling, packaging or
  advertising material by means of or in relation to which the inspector
  believes on reasonable grounds the contravention was committed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Detention

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A textile fibre product or other thing seized and
  detained pursuant to subsection (1) shall not be detained after

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the provisions of this Act or any regulations that
    are applicable to the textile fibre product or other thing have, in
    the opinion of the inspector, been complied with, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the expiration of ninety days after the day of
    seizure or such longer period as may be prescribed with respect to
    any textile fibre product,

  unless before that time proceedings have been instituted in respect of
  the contravention, in which event the textile fibre product or other
  thing may be detained until the proceedings are finally concluded.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 46(1st Supp.), s. 10
:::

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448700 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing consumer textile articles for the
    purposes of this Act and prescribing any other matter or thing that
    by this Act is to be prescribed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} exempting, conditionally or unconditionally, any
    textile fibre product or any portion of such a product from the
    application of any provision of this Act or the regulations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} exempting, conditionally or unconditionally, any
    type of transaction in relation to a prescribed consumer textile
    article from the prohibition set out in paragraph 3(a);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} requiring, authorizing or prohibiting the inclusion
    in a label of any information or representation in addition to the
    information required by section 6;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} requiring, authorizing or prohibiting the inclusion
    in any advertising of a consumer textile article of any
    representation in relation to the textile fibre content of the
    article;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} requiring or authorizing the statement on the
    container of a consumer textile article of any information or
    representation required or authorized to be shown in a label, in
    addition to or in place of the statement of that information or
    representation in a label;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the form and manner in which any
    information or representation required or authorized to be shown in
    any label, on any container or in any advertisement shall be shown;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} prescribing any expressions, words, figures,
    depictions or symbols the use of which, in relation to a textile
    fibre product, shall be deemed, unless the contrary is proven, to
    constitute a false or misleading representation;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} prescribing generic names for textile fibres;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} prescribing how a textile fibre for which no
    generic name is prescribed pursuant to this Act shall be described
    for the purposes of this Act;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} requiring the disclosure to the Minister of
    information in respect of any textile fibre in order to enable a
    generic name to be prescribed for the textile fibre and prescribing
    the time and manner in which the disclosure shall be made;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} establishing tolerances in relation to the
    percentage by mass of textile fibres shown in labels or otherwise;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} respecting the duties and functions of analysts and
    the taking of samples of textile fibre products;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} respecting the detention of textile fibre products
    and other things seized pursuant to subsection 10(1);

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} respecting the disposition of textile fibre
    products and other things forfeited under section 16; and

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} generally, for carrying out the purposes and
    provisions of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where statement on container deemed to
  be label

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where any information or representation is required
  or authorized by the regulations to be stated on the container of a
  consumer textile article in place of the statement of that information
  or representation in a label and is so stated, that statement on the
  container shall be deemed to be a label and to be applied to the
  article.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where no generic name for textile fibre

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a textile fibre comprising or contained in a
  consumer textile article is a textile fibre for which no generic name
  and description is set out in the regulations, a label applied to the
  article shall be deemed to show the generic name of the textile fibre
  if the textile fibre is described in the label in accordance with the
  regulations.

## [Offence and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448726 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of sections 3 to 5

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  dealer who contravenes section 3, 4 or 5 is guilty of an offence and
  liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding five
    thousand dollars; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine not
    exceeding ten thousand dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of other provisions, or
  regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes any provision of this
  Act, other than section 3, 4 or 5, or of the regulations is guilty of
  an offence and liable,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding one
    thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
    months or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine not
    exceeding three thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not
    exceeding one year or to both.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 46(1st Supp.), s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence by employee or agent or mandatary

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In any
  prosecution for an offence under this Act, it is sufficient proof of
  the offence to establish that it was committed by an employee or an
  agent or mandatary of the accused, whether or not the employee or
  agent or mandatary is identified or has been prosecuted for the
  offence, unless the accused establishes that the offence was committed
  without their knowledge or consent and that they exercised all due
  diligence to prevent its commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Proceedings by way of summary conviction under this
  Act may be instituted at any time within but not later than twelve
  months after the time when the subject-matter of the proceedings
  arose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Venue

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A complaint or an information in respect of an
  offence under this Act may be heard, tried or determined by a court if
  the accused is resident or carrying on business within the territorial
  jurisdiction of that court although the matter of the complaint or
  information did not arise in that territorial jurisdiction.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-10, s. 13
- 2011, c. 21, s. 157
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of analyst

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section, a
  certificate purporting to be signed by an analyst stating that the
  analyst has analyzed or examined an article or a product or substance
  and stating the result of the analysis or examination is, in any
  prosecution for an offence under this Act, evidence of the statements
  contained in the certificate without proof of the signature or
  official character of the person appearing to have signed the
  certificate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attendance of analyst

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party against whom a certificate of an analyst is
  produced pursuant to subsection (1) may, with leave of the court,
  require the attendance of the analyst for the purposes of
  cross-examination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No certificate shall be admitted in evidence pursuant
  to subsection (1) unless the party intending to produce it has given
  to the party against whom it is intended to be produced reasonable
  notice of the intention together with a copy of the certificate.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 46(1st Supp.), s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identification appearing in labels

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In any
  prosecution for an offence under this Act, evidence that a label
  applied to a textile fibre product bore identification purporting to
  identify the person by or for whom the product was manufactured or
  made is, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, proof that the
  person whose identification appeared in the label is the person by or
  for whom the product was manufactured and the person responsible for
  the information and representations in the label.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identification appearing on containers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In any prosecution for an offence under this Act in
  relation to a textile fibre product to which a label purporting to
  identify the person by or for whom the product was manufactured or
  made is not applied, evidence that a container of the product bore
  identification purporting to identify the person by or for whom the
  product was manufactured is, in the absence of evidence to the
  contrary, proof that the person whose identification appeared on the
  container is the person by or for whom the product was manufactured
  and the person responsible for the information and representations on
  the container.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 46(1st Supp.), s. 15
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a person has been convicted of an offence under this Act, any textile
  fibre product or any labelling, packaging or advertising material in
  relation to which or by means of which the offence was committed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is, on that conviction, in addition to any
    punishment imposed for the offence, forfeited to Her Majesty if the
    forfeiture is directed by the court; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may be restored by order of the court to the person
    from whom it was seized on such conditions relating to sale or
    advertising as are specified in the order and as, in the opinion of
    the court, are necessary to avoid the commission of any further
    offence under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection of persons claiming interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 74 to 76 of the [Fisheries
  Act](/eng/acts/F-14) apply, with such modifications as the
  circumstances require, to any textile fibre product or other thing
  forfeited under this section as though that textile fibre product or
  other thing were an article forfeited under subsection 72(1) of that
  Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 46(1st Supp.), s. 16
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Articles received or in transit before
contravened regulation in force

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall be convicted of an offence under this Act in relation to
  the sale, importation or advertising of a consumer textile article if
  the person establishes to the satisfaction of the court that the
  consumer textile article in relation to which the offence was
  committed was received by, or was in transit to, the person from a
  dealer before the coming into force of the regulation that created the
  requirement with which the person has failed to comply.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advertising before contravened
  regulation in force

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall be convicted of an offence under this
  Act in relation to the advertising of a consumer textile article if
  the person establishes to the satisfaction of the court that the
  advertisement

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} was published, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} was authorized in final form and dispatched for
    publication,

  before the coming into force of the regulation that created the
  requirement with which the person has failed to comply.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 46(1st Supp.), s. 17
:::
