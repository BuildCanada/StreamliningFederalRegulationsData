## Cape Breton Development Corporation Divestiture Authorization and Dissolution Act {#cape-breton-development-corporation-divestiture-authorization-and-dissolution-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2000, c. 23
:::

Assented to 2000-06-29

An Act to authorize the divestiture of the assets of, and to dissolve,
the Cape Breton Development Corporation, to amend the Cape Breton
Development Corporation Act and to make consequential amendments to
other Acts
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-78747 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Cape Breton Development Corporation Divestiture Authorization and
Dissolution Act](/eng/acts/C-25.2).

## [Disposal of Assets and Dissolution]{.HTitleText1} {#h-78750 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Cape
  Breton Development Corporation ("the Corporation"), established by the
  [Cape Breton Development Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-25), is
  authorized

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to sell or otherwise dispose of all or
    substantially all of its assets; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to do everything necessary for or incidental to
    closing out its affairs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions not applicable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 99(2) to (5) of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) do not apply to the authorization
  in subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement of legal proceedings

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  action, suit or other legal proceeding in respect of an obligation or
  liability incurred by the Corporation in closing out its affairs may
  be brought against Her Majesty in any court that would have had
  jurisdiction if the action, suit or other legal proceeding had been
  brought against the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of legal proceedings

  [[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#C-25.2_en_1){#C-25.2_en_1-ID0EBCAA}[(2)]{.lawlabel} Any
  action, suit or other legal proceeding to which the Corporation is
  party that is pending in any court on the coming into force of this
  section may be continued by or against Her Majesty in like manner and
  to the same extent as it could have been continued by or against the
  Corporation.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#C-25.2_en_1-ID0EBCAA){#C-25.2_en_1}\[Note:
    Section 3 in force on assent June 29, 2000.\]
    :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dissolution

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation is
dissolved on a day to be fixed by order of the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration of general advantage

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The works and undertakings
operated or carried on by the Corporation on or after June 15, 1967 are
declared to be works for the general advantage of Canada.
