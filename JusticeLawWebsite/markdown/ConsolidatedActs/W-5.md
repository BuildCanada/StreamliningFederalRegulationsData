## Weather Modification Information Act {#weather-modification-information-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. W-5
:::

An Act to provide for the obtaining of information respecting weather
modification activities
::::

::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467627 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Weather Modification Information Act](/eng/acts/W-5).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 59, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467632 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the
    public service as may be designated by the Governor in
    Council; ([directeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[weather modification activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[weather modification activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any
    action designed or intended to produce, by physical or chemical
    means, changes in the composition or dynamics of the atmosphere for
    the purpose of increasing, decreasing or redistributing
    precipitation, decreasing or suppressing hail or lightning or
    dissipating fog or cloud. ([essais de modification du
    temps]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. W-5, s. 2
- 2003, c. 22, s. 225(E)
:::

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467639 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrator to be informed

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  any person proposes to engage in weather modification activities in
  Canada, that person shall, in such form and manner as may be
  prescribed, submit to the Administrator such information as may be
  prescribed respecting that person and the weather modification
  activities in which it is proposed to engage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before commencing any weather modification activity,
  the person proposing to carry out the activity shall inform the
  Administrator in writing of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date and time when and the place where the
    activity is to be carried out;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the names and addresses of the persons by and for
    whom the activity is to be carried out;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the activity;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the equipment, materials and method to be used; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the geographic area that may be affected.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 59, s. 3
:::

## [Records and Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467652 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to maintain daily records and
submit report

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who carries out any weather modification activity shall, in
  such form and manner as may be prescribed,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain a daily record of the activity, in which
    shall be included detailed information relating to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the location and operation of any equipment used,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any meteorological observations made in the
      geographic area affected or intended to be affected by the
      activity, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the chemical nature, physical properties and
      quantities of any substances emitted into the atmosphere for the
      purposes of weather modification; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within fifteen days from the end of the month in
    which the activity was carried out, submit to the Administrator a
    report specifying

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date when the activity was carried out,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the nature and scope of the activity,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any meteorological observations that were made,
      and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} such other information and observations relevant
      to the activity as may be specified by the Administrator or an
      authorized representative of the Administrator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Availability of records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person who has custody of a record referred to in
  paragraph (1)(a) shall, at such reasonable times as may be specified
  by the Administrator or an authorized representative of the
  Administrator, make that record available for inspection by the
  Administrator or the representative.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 59, s. 4
:::

## [Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467669 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of information

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Any information obtained by
the Administrator or an authorized representative of the Administrator
pursuant to this Act may be made public or made available on request to
any member of the public.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 59, s. 5
:::

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467674 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations prescribing any matter or thing that by this Act may be
prescribed.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 59, s. 6
:::

## [Offence and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467679 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who fails to comply with any provision of this Act is guilty of
  an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding
  one thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
  months or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers, etc., of corporations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where any corporation commits an offence under this
  Act, any officer, director or agent of the corporation who directed,
  authorized, assented to or acquiesced in the commission of the offence
  is a party to and guilty of the offence and is liable on summary
  conviction to the punishment provided by subsection (1) whether or not
  the corporation has been prosecuted or convicted.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 59, s. 7
:::
