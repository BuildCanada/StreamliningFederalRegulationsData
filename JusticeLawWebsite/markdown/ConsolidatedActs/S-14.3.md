## An Act to grant access to records of the Special Committee on the Defence of Canada Regulations {#an-act-to-grant-access-to-records-of-the-special-committee-on-the-defence-of-canada-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1984, c. 36
:::

Assented to 1984-06-29

An Act to grant access to records of the Special Committee on the
Defence of Canada Regulations
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of access to records

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person has a right to and shall, on request, be given access to any
  records of the House of Commons Special Committee on the Defence of
  Canada Regulations, whith sat [in camera]{.otherLang lang="la"} from
  1939 to 1945.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who is granted access to a record under
  subsection (1) shall be given an opportunity to examine the record or
  shall be given a copy thereof.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who makes a request for access to a record
  may be required to pay, before any copies are made, a fee reflecting
  the cost of reproduction.

[[record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Act, [[record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all the minutes of the
Committee proceedings, correspondence, memoranda and any other
documentary material relevant to the Committee proceedings.
