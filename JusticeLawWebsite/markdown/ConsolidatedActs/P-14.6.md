## [Conditions for Protection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-393025 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligible plant varieties

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Plant
  breeder's rights may not be granted except in respect of a plant
  variety that belongs to a prescribed category and meets all of the
  conditions set out in subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Plant breeder's rights may be granted in respect of a
  plant variety if it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a new variety;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is, by reason of one or more identifiable
    characteristics, clearly distinguishable from all varieties whose
    existence is a matter of common knowledge at the filing date of the
    application for the grant of plant breeder's rights respecting that
    plant variety;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is stable in its essential characteristics in that
    after repeated propagation or, if the applicant has defined a
    particular cycle of propagation, at the end of each cycle it remains
    true to its description; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is, having regard to the particular features of its
    sexual reproduction or vegetative propagation, a sufficiently
    homogeneous variety.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New variety

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A plant variety is a new variety if the propagating
  or harvested material of that variety has not been sold by, or with
  the concurrence of, the breeder of that variety or the breeder's legal
  representative

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in Canada, before

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed period preceding the filing date
      of the application for the grant of plant breeder's rights, in the
      case of a variety belonging to a recently prescribed category, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the period of one year before the filing date of
      the application for the grant of plant breeder's rights, in the
      case of any other variety; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} outside Canada, before

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the period of six years before the filing date of
      the application for the grant of plant breeder's rights, in the
      case of a tree or vine, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the period of four years before the filing date
      of the application for the grant of plant breeder's rights, in any
      other case.

- Definition of [[sufficiently homogeneous variety]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (2)(d), [[sufficiently
  homogeneous variety]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a variety for which,
  in the event of its sexual reproduction or vegetative propagation in
  substantial quantity, any variations in characteristics of the plants
  so reproduced or propagated are predictable, capable of being
  described and commercially acceptable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make regulations
  prescribing classes of sales that are not to be considered sales for
  the purposes of subsection (3).

## [Plant Breeder's Rights]{.HTitleText1} {#h-393049 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nature of plant breeder's rights

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to the other provisions of this Act and the regulations, the holder of
  the plant breeder's rights respecting a plant variety has the
  exclusive right

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to produce and reproduce propagating material of
    the variety;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to condition propagating material of the variety
    for the purposes of propagating the variety;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to sell propagating material of the variety;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to export or import propagating material of the
    variety;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} to make repeated use of propagating material of the
    variety to produce commercially another plant variety if the
    repetition is necessary for that purpose;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a variety to which ornamental plants
    belong, if those plants are normally marketed for purposes other
    than propagation, to use any such plants or parts of those plants as
    propagating material for the production of ornamental plants or cut
    flowers;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} to stock propagating material of the variety for
    the purpose of doing any act described in any of paragraphs (a) to
    (f); and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} to authorize, conditionally or unconditionally, the
    doing of any act described in any of paragraphs (a) to (g).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Royalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Without prejudice to any rights or privileges of the
  Crown, an authorization conferred under paragraph 1(h) may be subject
  to a condition to pay royalty to the holder of the plant breeder's
  rights whether or not the holder is Her Majesty in right of Canada or
  a province.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1990, c. 20, s. 5
- 2015, c. 2, s. 5

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights respecting harvested materials

**[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the other
provisions of this Act and the regulations, the holder of the plant
breeder's rights respecting a plant variety has the exclusive right to
do any act described in any of paragraphs 5(1)(a) to (h) in respect of
any harvested material, including whole plants or parts of plants, that
is obtained through the unauthorized use of propagating material of the
plant variety, unless the holder had reasonable opportunity to exercise
his or her rights under section 5 in relation to that propagating
material and failed to do so before claiming rights under this section.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2015, c. 2, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights respecting certain other varieties

- **[[5.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the other provisions of
  this Act and the regulations, the holder of the plant breeder's rights
  respecting a plant variety has the exclusive right to do any act
  described in any of paragraphs 5(1)(a) to (h) in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any other plant variety that is essentially derived
    from the plant variety if the plant variety is not itself
    essentially derived from another plant variety;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other plant variety that is not clearly
    distinguishable from the plant variety; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other plant variety whose production requires
    the repeated use of the plant variety.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of essentially derived

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(a), a plant variety
  is essentially derived from another plant variety (in this subsection
  referred to as the "initial variety") if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is predominantly derived from the initial
    variety or from a plant variety that is itself predominantly derived
    from the initial variety and it retains the essential
    characteristics that result from the genotype or combination of
    genotypes of the initial variety;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is clearly distinguishable from the initial
    variety; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it conforms to the initial variety in the
    expression of the essential characteristics that result from the
    genotype or combination of genotypes of the initial variety, except
    for the differences that result from its derivation from the initial
    variety.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2015, c. 2, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of rights

- **[[5.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  rights referred to in sections 5 to 5.2 do not apply to any act done

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} privately and for non-commercial purposes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for experimental purposes; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of breeding other plant varieties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Farmers' privilege

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The rights referred to in paragraphs 5(1)(a) and (b)
  and --- for the purposes of exercising those rights and the right to
  store --- the right referred to in paragraph 5(1)(g) do not apply to
  harvested material of the plant variety that is grown by a farmer on
  the farmer's holdings and used by the farmer on those holdings for the
  sole purpose of propagation of the plant variety.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2015, c. 2, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of rights

- **[[5.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  rights referred to in sections 5 to 5.2 do not apply to any act done
  in relation to material of a plant variety after the material has been
  sold in Canada by the holder or with the holder's consent, unless that
  act involves

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the further propagation of the plant variety; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the export of material of the plant variety to a
    country that does not protect varieties of the plant genus or
    species to which the exported plant variety belongs if the exported
    material is not intended for consumption.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[material]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1),
  [[material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means propagating material and
  harvested material, including whole plants and parts of plants.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2015, c. 2, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of plant breeder's rights

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The term
  of the grant of plant breeder's rights, subject to earlier termination
  under this Act, shall be a period of 25 years in the case of a tree, a
  vine or any category specified by the regulations and 20 years in any
  other case. The period begins on the day on which the certificate of
  plant breeder's rights is issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of annual fee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A holder of plant breeder's rights shall, during the
  term of the grant of those rights, pay to the Commissioner, within the
  prescribed time, the prescribed annual fee.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1990, c. 20, s. 6
- 2015, c. 2, s. 5

## [Applications for Plant Breeder's Rights]{.HTitleText1} {#h-393108 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for plant breeder's rights

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  breeder or the breeder's legal representative may make an application
  to the Commissioner for the grant of plant breeder's rights respecting
  a plant variety if, in the case of an individual, the breeder or legal
  representative is a citizen of, or is resident in, Canada, a country
  of the Union or an agreement country or, in the case of a person that
  is not an individual, the breeder or legal representative has an
  establishment in Canada, a country of the Union or an agreement
  country.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If two or more breeders

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a plant variety is bred by two or more breeders
  otherwise than independently of each other, the persons entitled to
  make an application for the grant of plant breeder's rights respecting
  that variety may make an application jointly and, if any of those
  persons refuses to do so or information regarding their whereabouts
  cannot be obtained through diligent inquiry, the remainder of those
  persons may make an application for that grant.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1990, c. 20, s. 7
- 2015, c. 2, s. 6

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Priority when breeders breed
independently

**[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1 .sectionLabel}** In the case of two or
more applications respecting a plant variety whose breeders bred it
independently of each other, priority shall be given to the application
with the earliest filing date. If the filing dates of the applications
are the same, priority shall be given to the application pertaining to
the breeder that was first in a position to apply for the plant
breeder's rights respecting the variety or that would have been first in
the position to do so if the provisions under this Act for so doing had
always been in force.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2015, c. 2, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Priority when preceding application in
country of Union or agreement country

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  application made under section 7 is preceded by another application
  made in a country of the Union or an agreement country for protection
  in respect of the same plant variety and the same breeder, the filing
  date of the application made under section 7 is deemed to be the date
  on which the preceding application was made in that country of the
  Union or agreement country and, consequently, the applicant is
  entitled to priority in Canada despite any intervening use,
  publication or application respecting the variety if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application is made in the prescribed form
    within 12 months after the date on which the preceding application
    was made in that country of the Union or agreement country; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application is accompanied by a claim
    respecting the priority and by the prescribed fee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confirmation of claim to priority

  generality of the foregoing, may make an order

    the subject-matter of those rights as may constitute such an

    order for that restraint;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for and in respect of inspection or account; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the custody or disposition of any
    offending material, products, wares or articles.

## [Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-393485 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Secrecy

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person commits an offence who knowingly discloses any information with
  regard to any variety in respect of which an application for plant
  breeder's rights is made or with regard to the business affairs of the
  applicant that was acquired by that person in performing any functions
  under this Act except if the information is disclosed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the Minister, the advisory committee or the
    Commissioner or to any other person for the purposes of the
    performance by that other person of any functions pursuant to this
    Act or of any duties in an official capacity for enforcement of this
    Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in compliance with any requirements imposed by or
    under this Act or by virtue of any power lawfully exercised in the
    course or for the purposes of any judicial proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences respecting denominations and
  sales

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person commits an offence who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} knowingly contravenes section 15;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of selling any propagating
    material, knowingly designates the material by reference to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a denomination different from any denomination
      registered in respect of the plant variety of which the material
      is propagating material,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a denomination registered in respect of a plant
      variety of which the material is not propagating material, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a denomination corresponding so closely to a
      registered denomination as to mislead; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} knowingly, for the purpose of selling any
    propagating material, represents falsely that the material is
    propagating material of, or is derived from, a plant variety in
    respect of which plant breeder's rights are held or have been
    applied for.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Falsification in relation to
  administration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person commits an offence who, in relation to
  the administration of this Act, knowingly

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} makes any false representation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} makes or causes to be made any false entry in the
    register or any record;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} makes or causes to be made any false document or
    any alteration, false in a material respect, in the form of a copy
    of any document; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} produces or tenders any document containing false
    information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment of individuals

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An individual who commits an offence under subsection
  (1), (2) or (3)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is liable on summary conviction to a fine of not
    more than five thousand dollars; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is liable on conviction on indictment to a fine of
    not more than fifteen thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term
    not exceeding three years, in the case of an offence under
    subsection (1) or (2), or five years, in the case of an offence
    under subsection (3), or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment of corporations

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A corporation that commits an offence under
  subsection (1), (2) or (3)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is liable on summary conviction to a fine of not
    more than twenty-five thousand dollars; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is liable on conviction on indictment to a fine the
    amount of which is in the discretion of the court.

  [[representation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} In this section,
  [[representation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any manner of express

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of "seed"

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (1), [[seed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has
  the meaning assigned to that expression by section 2 of the [Seeds
  Act](/eng/acts/S-8).

## [Review of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-393725 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

- **[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As
  soon as practicable after the expiration of the period of ten years
  beginning on the day of the coming into force of this Act, the
  Minister shall prepare a report with respect to the administration of
  this Act during the period and shall cause a copy of the report to be
  laid before each House of Parliament on any of the first fifteen days
  on which that House is sitting after it is completed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report prepared pursuant to subsection (1) shall
  indicate whether the operation of this Act

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} results in

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the stimulation of investment in businesses
      involving the breeding of plant varieties in respect of which
      protection afforded by plant breeders' rights is applicable,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any improvement in facilities to obtain foreign
      varieties of plants in the interests of agriculture in Canada,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} protection abroad, for commercial purposes, of
      Canadian plant varieties,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} improvement of plant varieties to the public
      benefit, and particularly, to the benefit of farmers and
      nurserymen, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any other public advantage,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has some but not all of the results described in
    paragraph (a),

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has all or any of those results but is, in any
    respect, not in the public interest, or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is, in the total absence of those results, not in
    the public interest,

  as the case may be, and particulars of anything so indicated shall be
  furnished in the report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

**[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall each
year prepare a report with respect to the administration of this Act
during the preceding calendar year and shall lay it before Parliament on
any of the first fifteen days that either House of Parliament is sitting
after he completes it.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-393743 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Plant breeders' rights --- previously
granted

**[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** This Act, as it reads on
or after the day on which this section comes into force, does not apply
with respect to plant breeders' rights granted before that day, but this
Act, as it read immediately before that day, continues to apply with
respect to those rights.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1990, c. 20, s. 79
- 2015, c. 2, s. 51
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing applications

**[[80]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80 .sectionLabel}** An application for the
grant of plant breeder's rights that was made before the day on which
this section comes into force and that was not disposed of before that
day is to be dealt with and disposed of in accordance with this Act.
However, if plant breeder's rights are granted to the applicant,
sections 19 to 21, as they read immediately before the day on which this
section comes into force, continue to apply with respect to those
rights.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1990, c. 20, s. 80
- 2015, c. 2, s. 51
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing proceedings

**[[81]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-81 .sectionLabel}** A proceeding commenced
under this Act before the day on which this section comes into force
that, on that day, is pending before a court and in respect of which no
decision has been made shall be dealt with and disposed of in accordance
with this Act as it read immediately before that day.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1990, c. 20, s. 81
- 2015, c. 2, s. 51
:::
