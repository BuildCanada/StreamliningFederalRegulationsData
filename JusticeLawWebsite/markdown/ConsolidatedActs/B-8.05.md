and Detroit and other works

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33423 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Bridge To Strengthen Trade Act](/eng/acts/B-8.05).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33426 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[bridge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bridge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bridge that spans the Detroit
    River and connects Windsor, Ontario to Detroit, Michigan and whose
    piers, in Ontario, are located within the boundaries of the
    territory described in the schedule, as well as the approaches to
    the bridge. ([pont]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[construction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[construction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in relation to the bridge, the
    parkway or a related work, includes any work or activity related to
    its construction. ([construction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Transport. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in relation to the bridge or a
    related work, includes its maintenance and
    repair. ([exploitation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[parkway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[parkway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a road connecting Highway 401
    with any work referred to in paragraph (a) or (b) of the definition
    [[related work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} that is located within the
    boundaries of the territory described in the schedule and any works
    ancillary to that road. ([promenade]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual, corporation,
    partnership, trust, joint venture or unincorporated association or
    organization. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[related work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[related work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following
    works:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any border services facility related to the
      bridge that is located in Michigan or within the boundaries of the
      territory described in the schedule;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any work useful to the operation of the bridge or
      any border services facility referred to in paragraph (a),
      including toll booths, duty-free shops and parking lots, that is
      located in Michigan or within the boundaries of the territory
      described in the schedule;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any road or interchange connecting Interstate 75
      with any work referred to in paragraph (a) or (b) that is located
      in Michigan;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any work that is accessory to the bridge or to
      any work referred to in paragraphs (a) to (c); and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other work specified in the
      regulations. ([ouvrage connexe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Construction of the Bridge, Parkway and Related Works]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33441 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from certain Acts and
regulations

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The [Fisheries
Act](/eng/acts/F-14), the [Canadian Navigable Waters
Act](/eng/acts/N-22), the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3),
section 6 of the [International Bridges and Tunnels
Act](/eng/acts/I-17.05) and the [Port Authorities Operations
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-55) do not apply to the
construction of the bridge, parkway or any related work.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expansion, decommissioning, abandonment

  [[project]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 81 of the
  [Impact Assessment Act](/eng/acts/I-2.75) and is subject to sections
  82 to 91 of that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsible authority

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  responsible authority is exempt from any obligation accrued or
  accruing under subsections 20(2) and 38(1) of the [Canadian
  Environmental Assessment Act](/eng/acts/C-15.2), chapter 37 of the
  Statutes of Canada, 1992, in relation to the bridge, parkway or any
  related work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Windsor Port Authority

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Windsor Port Authority is exempt from any
  obligation accrued or accruing under subsection 15(2) of the Canada
  Port Authority Environmental Assessment Regulations in relation to the
  bridge, parkway or any related work.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other exemptions

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, by order, exempt any person, on any condition
  that the Governor in Council considers to be in the public interest,
  from any requirement under any federal Act to obtain a permit,
  licence, approval or other authorization in relation to the
  construction of the bridge, parkway or any related work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply to the order. However, the order must be published in the
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorizations deemed issued

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} After completion of the construction of the bridge,
  parkway or the related work, as the case may be, any authorization
  that would have been required in relation to its construction but for
  an exemption granted under subsection (1), is deemed to have been
  issued for the purpose of the application of the federal Act for which
  the exemption was granted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction of bridge

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before a
  person who proposes the construction of the bridge begins its
  construction, they must file with the Minister a plan that includes
  all measures to be taken to mitigate the bridge's impact on
  navigation, the plans for its design and construction, a description
  of its proposed site and the plans for its management and operation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to consult

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person must consult with the Minister before
  filing the plan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Death of fish or harmful alteration,
disruption or destruction of fish habitat

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before a
  person who proposes to carry on any work, undertaking or activity, for
  the purpose of the construction of the bridge, parkway or any related
  work and for which an authorization referred to in paragraph
  34.4(2)(b) or (c) or 35(2)(b) or (c) of the [Fisheries
  Act](/eng/acts/F-14) would have been required but for section 3,
  begins to carry on the work, undertaking or activity, they must file
  with the Minister a plan that includes all measures to be taken to
  mitigate the extent of death of fish or the harmful alteration,
  disruption or destruction of fish habitat, resulting from the carrying
  on of the work, undertaking or activity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to consult

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person must consult with the Minister of
  Fisheries and Oceans before filing the plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorizations under [Fisheries
  Act](/eng/acts/F-14)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any authorization referred to in paragraph 35(2)(b)
  or (c) of the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) that was issued before
  the coming into force of this section in relation to the carrying on
  of such a work, undertaking or activity is deemed to be a plan that
  has been filed in accordance with subsections (1) and (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transitional provision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a plan was filed with respect to a work,
  undertaking or activity under subsection (1) as it read before the
  coming into force of this subsection, then, as of that coming into
  force, that subsection (1) continues to apply with respect to that
  work, undertaking or activity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Impact on listed wildlife species, etc.

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before a
  person who proposes to engage in an activity affecting a listed
  wildlife species, any part of its critical habitat or the residences
  of its individuals, for the purpose of the construction of the bridge,
  parkway or any related work and for which an authorization under
  subsection 73(1) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) would
  have been required but for section 3, begins to engage in the
  activity, they must file with the Minister a plan that includes all
  measures to be taken to mitigate the impact of the activity on the
  species, its critical habitat or the residences of its individuals.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to consult

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person must consult with the Minister of the
  Environment before filing the plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorizations under [Species at Risk
  Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any authorization referred to in subsection 73(1) of
  the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) that was granted before
  the coming into force of this section in relation to the activity is
  deemed to be a plan that has been filed in accordance with
  subsections (1) and (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adverse environmental effects

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Before a person who
proposes the construction in Canada of the bridge or of any work
referred to in paragraph (a) or (b) of the definition [[related
work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 begins its construction, they
must file with the Minister a plan that includes all measures to be
taken to mitigate any adverse environmental effects caused by the
construction and sets out a process for consulting the public with
respect to the construction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Port Authorities Operations
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-55)

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  a person who proposes to do anything in the Port of Windsor --- in
  order to construct the bridge --- that will have or is likely to have
  any result that is listed in section 5 of the [Port Authorities
  Operations Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-55) begins to do any
  such thing, they must file with the Minister a plan that includes all
  measures to be taken to mitigate or prevent the result.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to consult

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person must consult with the Windsor Port
  Authority before filing the plan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Plan amendment

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who files a plan under any of sections 7 to 11 may amend that
  plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of sections 7 to 11 to
  amended plan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The requirements regarding the content of a plan
  filed under any of sections 7 to 11, as well as any obligation to
  consult with respect to that plan, also apply to the corresponding
  amended plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of amended plan

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person must file the amended plan with the
  Minister. Once filed, the amended plan replaces the plan previously
  filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation of and compliance with
plans

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Any person who files a
plan must ensure that it is implemented and complied with.

## [Operation of the Bridge and Related Works]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33502 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of initial operator

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  of the bridge and any related work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from [International Bridges
  and Tunnels Act](/eng/acts/I-17.05)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph 23(1)(b) of the [International Bridges and
  Tunnels Act](/eng/acts/I-17.05) does not apply to the initial
  operator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply to the designation.

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33510 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization --- persons

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person may, with the approval of the Governor in Council, do anything
  referred to in any of paragraphs 90(1)(a) to (e) of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) for any purpose relating to the
  construction or operation of the bridge or any related work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization --- parent Crown
  corporation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any parent Crown corporation as defined in subsection
  83(1) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) may, with
  the approval of the Governor in Council, sell or otherwise dispose of
  all or substantially all of the corporation's assets for any purpose
  relating to the construction or operation of the bridge or any related
  work.

Definition of [[corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 17 to 21, [[corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
corporation established under section 29 of the [International Bridges
and Tunnels Act](/eng/acts/I-17.05) for any purpose relating to the
construction or operation of the bridge or any related work.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization to construct and operate

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to its letters patent, a
  corporation is authorized to construct and operate the bridge or any
  related work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization by corporation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The corporation may authorize another person to
  construct or operate the bridge or related work.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming --- establishment of corporation

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  corporation that was established before the coming into force of
  section 180 of the Jobs and Growth Act, 2012 is deemed to have been
  established under section 29 of the [International Bridges and Tunnels
  Act](/eng/acts/I-17.05) as amended by that section 180 and its
  establishment is deemed to have been authorized for the purpose of
  paragraph 90(1)(a) of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming --- action taken by corporation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any action taken by the corporation between the date
  of its establishment and the date of the coming into force of this
  section is deemed to have been taken as if sections 16, 17 and 19 to
  21 were in force at the time that the action was taken.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not agent of Her Majesty

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A corporation is not an
agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public agency

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** A corporation is deemed
to be a public agency for the purposes of the Urban Cooperation Act of
1967, MCL 124.501 to 124.512, an Act of the state of Michigan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public body corporate and compact entity

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A corporation may enter
into an agreement with the government of the state of Michigan or of any
political subdivision of that state or with any of their agencies or
agents to establish an entity that is both a public body corporate and a
compact entity under the laws of the United States.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may enter into an agreement for any purpose relating to the
  construction or operation of the bridge, parkway or any related work
  with any person or with the government of the United States or of any
  political subdivision of the United States or any of their agencies or
  agents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The agreement may include undertakings to provide
  financial assistance by Canada, including the granting of guarantees.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to carry out agreement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may take any measures that he or she
  considers appropriate to carry out the agreement or to protect the
  interests or enforce the rights of Her Majesty in right of Canada
  under the agreement, including accepting and holding on behalf of Her
  Majesty any security granted under the agreement or releasing or
  realizing on that security.

## [Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33547 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A person who contravenes
any of sections 7 to 11 and 13 and subsection 23(2) is guilty of an
offence punishable on summary conviction and liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine of not more
  than \$25,000; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, to a fine of not more than
  \$500,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence committed by employee or agent or
mandatary of accused

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** In a prosecution for an
offence under this Act, it is sufficient proof of the offence to
establish that it was committed by an employee or agent or mandatary of
the accused, whether or not the employee, agent or mandatary is
identified or has been prosecuted for the offence, unless the accused
establishes that the offence was committed without the knowledge or
consent of the accused and that the accused exercised due diligence to
prevent its commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Due diligence defence

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 25, a
person is not to be found guilty of an offence under this Act if they
establish that they exercised due diligence to prevent its commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Proceedings in respect of
an offence under this Act may be instituted within two years after the
day on which the subject matter of the proceedings arose.
