## Civil Service Insurance Act {#civil-service-insurance-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"} 1952, c. 49
:::

An Act respecting Government Civil Service Insurance
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-85634 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Civil Service Insurance Act](/eng/acts/C-31.6).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1927, c. 23, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-85639 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[children]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[children]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes adopted
    children; ([enfants]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an
    insured, means a person who is cohabiting with the insured in a
    conjugal relationship, having so cohabited for a period of at least
    one year; ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[declaration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[declaration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an instrument signed by
    the insured in which he apportions the insurance money payable under
    his insurance contract, if

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the instrument is attached to the insurance
      contract,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an endorsement is made on the insurance contract
      with respect to the instrument, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the instrument identifies or describes the
      insurance contract; ([déclaration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[instrument]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[instrument]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a
    will; ([acte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insurance contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurance contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any contract
    whereby, under the authority of this Act, the Minister contracts
    with a person for the payment of a certain sum of money to be made
    upon the death of that person; ([contrat
    d'assurance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insurance money]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurance money]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount contracted
    to be paid by the Minister under an insurance contract; ([produit de
    l'assurance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[the insured]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[the insured]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person contracting
    with the Minister under this Act; ([l'assuré]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Finance; ([Ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[will]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[will]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a
    codicil. ([testament]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 49, s. 2
- 1974-75-76, c. 42, s. 1
- 2000, c. 12, s. 78
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insurance contract

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Where it is stated in an
insurance contract or in a declaration in respect thereof that the
insurance contract is for the benefit of the spouse and children or of
the children of the insured, without specifying their names, then the
word [[children]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all the children of the
insured living at the time of his death.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 49, s. 3
- 1974-75-76, c. 42, s. 2
:::

::: HistoricalNote

## [Insurance Contracts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-85665 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Who may insure

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may contract
with a person appointed to a permanent position in any branch of the
federal public administration, whether civil or military, for the
payment of a certain sum of money to be made on the death of the person.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 49, s. 5
- 1992, c. 1, s. 39
- 2003, c. 22, s. 224(E)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-5-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 5"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Apportionment of insurance if the insured
is a married man

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where the insured is a
married man or a widower with children, the insurance contract shall be
for the benefit of his wife, or of his wife and children, or of his wife
and some one or more of his children, or of his children only, or of
some one or more of them, and where the insurance contract is effected
for the benefit of more than one, the insured may apportion the
insurance money among them as he deems proper.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1927, c. 23, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If the insured is unmarried

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where the insured is an
unmarried man, the insurance contract shall be for the benefit of his
future wife, or of his future wife and children, and the insured may
apportion the insurance money among them in such manner as he sees fit,
but if, at the maturity of the contract, he is still unmarried, or is a
widower without children, the insurance money shall fall into and become
part of his estate.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1927, c. 23, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How evidenced

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An apportionment under
section 6 or 7 may be made in the insurance contract or by a
declaration.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 49, s. 8
- 1974-75-76, c. 42, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Share of person dying before the insured

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an
  apportionment has been made as provided in sections 6 to 8 or in this
  section, and one or more of the persons in whose favour the
  apportionment has been made dies in the lifetime of the insured, the
  insured may by a declaration provide that the shares formerly
  apportioned to the person or persons so dying shall be for the benefit
  of the wife and children of the insured, or of one or more of them, as
  he sees fit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If no declaration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In default of such declaration the shares of the
  persons so dying shall be for the benefit of the survivor, or
  survivors of the persons in whose favour the apportionment was made,
  in equal shares if more than one.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If all the beneficiaries die

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an apportionment has been made as provided in
  sections 6 to 8 or in this section and all the persons in whose favour
  the apportionment has been made die in the lifetime of the insured,
  the insured may by a declaration provide that the insurance money
  shall be for the benefit of the wife and children of the insured, or
  of one or more of them, in such proportions as he sees fit, and in
  default of such declaration, the insurance money shall be for the
  benefit of his wife and his children, in equal shares.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If insured survives wife, etc.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the insured survives his wife and all his children
  the insurance money shall fall into and become part of the estate of
  the insured.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 49, s. 9
- 1974-75-76, c. 42, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}In case of married woman or widow

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the insured is a married woman or a widow, the insurance contract
  shall be for the benefit of her husband, children, father, mother,
  brothers, sisters and any other person dependent on her, or one or
  more of them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}In case of unmarried woman

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the insured is an unmarried woman, the
  insurance contract shall be for the benefit of her future husband,
  future children, father, mother, brothers, sisters and any other
  person dependent on her, or one or more of them, but if, at the
  maturity of the insurance contract, she is still unmarried or is a
  widow without children, any insurance money that she has apportioned
  for the benefit of her future husband or future children shall be for
  the benefit of the other persons in whose favour an apportionment has
  been made, in equal shares, or where no such apportionment has been
  made, shall fall into and become part of the estate of the insured.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}In case of more than one beneficiary

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the insured is a woman and the insurance
  contract is for the benefit of more than one person, the insured may
  apportion the insurance money among them in such a manner as she sees
  fit, and such apportionment may be made in the insurance contract or
  by a declaration.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 49, s. 10
- 1974-75-76, c. 42, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When one or more beneficiaries die during
lifetime of insured

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an apportionment has been made as provided in section 10 or in this
  section and one or more of the persons in whose favour the
  apportionment has been made dies in the lifetime of the insured, the
  insured may by a declaration provide that the shares formerly
  apportioned to the person or persons so dying shall be for the benefit
  of the persons who may be her beneficiaries under this Act, or of one
  or more of them, in such proportions as she sees fit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}In default of declaration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In default of such declaration the shares of the
  persons so dying shall be for the benefit of the survivor or
  survivors, if any, of the persons in whose favour the apportionment
  was so made, in equal shares if more than one.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When all beneficiaries die during
  lifetime of insured

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an apportionment has been made as provided in
  section 10 or in this section and all the persons in whose favour the
  apportionment has been made die in the lifetime of the insured, the
  insured may by a declaration provide that the insurance money shall be
  for the benefit of the persons who may be her beneficiaries under this
  Act, or of one or more of them, in such proportions as she sees fit
  and, in default of such declaration, the insurance shall fall into and
  become part of the estate of the insured.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When insured survives beneficiaries

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the insured survives all the persons who may be
  her beneficiaries under this Act, the insurance money shall fall into
  and become part of the estate of the insured.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1974-75-76, c. 42, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 49, s. 11
- 1974-75-76, c. 42, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Invalid will

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  apportionment in an instrument purporting to be a will is not
  ineffective by reason only of the fact that the instrument is invalid
  as a will or that the apportionment is invalid as a bequest under the
  will.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Priorities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An apportionment in a will is of no effect against a
  declaration made later than the making of the will.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an apportionment is contained in a will and the
  will is subsequently revoked by operation of law or otherwise, the
  apportionment is thereby revoked.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where an apportionment is contained in an instrument
  that purports to be a will and the instrument, if valid as a will,
  would subsequently be revoked by operation of law or otherwise, the
  apportionment is thereby revoked.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 49, s. 12
- 1974-75-76, c. 42, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where no apportionment is made

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Where no apportionment is
made of the insurance money as hereinbefore provided, all persons
interested shall be held to share equally therein.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1927, c. 23, s. 13
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents affecting title

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  payment of insurance money made pursuant to this Act before the
  Superintendent of Financial Institutions receives in Ottawa an
  instrument or an order of a court affecting the payment of the
  insurance money or a notarial copy or a copy verified by statutory
  declaration of the instrument or order discharges Her Majesty from
  liability under the insurance contract to the extent of the amount
  paid as if there were no instrument or order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not affect the rights or
  interests of any person other than Her Majesty.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 49, s. 14
- 1974-75-76, c. 42, s. 8
- 1992, c. 1, s. 40
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tables to be constructed

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall cause tables to be constructed fixing the premiums to
  be paid by the insured to the Minister as the consideration for
  insurance contracts, and also all other tables necessary for the
  carrying out of the provisions of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Basis of same

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All such tables shall be based on the H.M. Mortality
  Table of the Institute of Actuaries of Great Britain, and on a rate of
  interest of six per cent per annum, and no allowance shall be made for
  expenses.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Premiums how payable

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The tables shall be framed so that the premium to
  obtain an insurance contract may be paid in one sum, or in annual,
  semi-annual, quarterly, or monthly instalments, and either during the
  life of the insured or during a limited period.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1927, c. 23, s. 15
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of insurance

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The minimum and maximum
amounts payable at death that may be contracted for under this Act are
one thousand dollars and ten thousand dollars, respectively.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1927, c. 23, s. 16
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Medical certificate

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Every applicant for
insurance shall furnish with his application a medical certificate in
such form as is prescribed by the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1927, c. 23, s. 17
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of beneficiaries

**[[17.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17.1 .sectionLabel}** Despite any other
provision of this Act, an insured may, in a declaration, an insurance
contract, a will or an instrument purporting to be a will, designate as
beneficiary the insured's child, spouse, common-law partner, father,
mother, brother, sister or any other person dependent on the insured,
apportioned among them as the insured sees fit.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 12, s. 79
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations by Governor in Council

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, for the purposes of this Act, from time to time make regulations
for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} regulating the mode and form of making contracts;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the mode of proving the age, identity,
  and the existence or death of persons;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the mode of paying money in connection
  with insurance contracts;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} providing for the payment of the insurance money as
  an annuity for a term of years certain or for the lifetime of the
  beneficiary or beneficiaries or otherwise, and prescribing from time
  to time the tables to be used in the calculations of such annuities;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} dispensing with the production of probate of a will
  or letters of administration, either generally or in any particular
  class of cases;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the accounts to be kept and their
  management;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} determining beforehand the cases or classes of cases
  in which an insurance contract may be surrendered and a cash surrender
  value paid therefor, or a free or paid-up insurance contract issued
  instead thereof, and for prescribing the manner in which such cash
  surrender value or amount of paid-up insurance shall be determined;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} determining the cases, not otherwise provided for in
  this Act, in which a person not originally named as, but who is
  eligible under this Act to be a beneficiary may be made a beneficiary;
  and in which an apportionment of the insurance money once made may be
  varied; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} any other purpose for which it is deemed expedient to
  make regulations in order to carry this Act into effect.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 49, s. 18
- 1974-75-76, c. 42, s. 9
:::

## [Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-85776 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual reports

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Superintendent of Financial Institutions shall, not later than June 30
  in each fiscal year, submit to the Minister a statement showing the
  amount received for premiums during the preceding fiscal year and the
  amount of all sums paid in connection with insurance contracts during
  that fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause each statement to be laid
  before each House of Parliament on any of the first fifteen days on
  which that House is sitting after the Minister receives it.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 49, s. 21
- 1992, c. 1, s. 42
