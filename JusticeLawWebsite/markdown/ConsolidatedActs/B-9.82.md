## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Authorization of Divestiture]{.HTitleText1} {#h-35725 .Part}

Meaning of [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,
[[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Development
Investment Corporation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to sell

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on any terms and conditions that the Governor
  in Council considers appropriate, by order, authorize

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Corporation to sell or otherwise dispose of any
    shares of any of its wholly-owned subsidiaries; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any wholly-owned subsidiary of the Corporation to
    sell or otherwise dispose of all or substantially all of the
    wholly-owned subsidiary's assets.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shares

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(b), "assets" of a
  wholly-owned subsidiary of the Corporation include shares of any other
  corporation held by the wholly-owned subsidiary or by any person on
  its behalf, or held in trust for it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of administration of property

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, by order, transfer or direct to be
  transferred the administration, management and control of any
  property, rights or interests held by any wholly-owned subsidiary of
  the Corporation from the wholly-owned subsidiary to any minister,
  department or agency of the Government of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of obligations and liabilities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by order, transfer or
  direct to be the transferred any obligation or liability incurred by
  any wholly-owned subsidiary of the Corporation from the wholly-owned
  subsidiary to any minister, department or agency of the Government of
  Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procurement of dissolution

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Finance is hereby authorized to procure the dissolution of
  the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the dissolution of the Corporation, every
  reference to the Canada Development Investment Corporation in any
  deed, contract or other document executed by that Corporation shall,
  unless the context otherwise requires, be read as a reference to Her
  Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Closing out affairs

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Finance may do all things and perform
  all acts necessary for or incidental to closing out the affairs of the
  Corporation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement of legal proceedings

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  action, suit or other legal proceeding in respect of an obligation or
  liability incurred by the Corporation, or by the Minister of Finance
  in closing out the affairs of that corporation, may be brought against
  Her Majesty in right of Canada in any court that would have had
  jurisdiction if the action, suit or other legal proceeding had been
  brought against the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of legal proceedings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal proceeding to which
  the Corporation is party pending in any court on the coming into force
  of this section may be continued by or against Her Majesty in right of
  Canada in like manner and to the same extent as it could have been
  continued by or against the Corporation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustment of accounts of Canada

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Finance,
after consultation with the President of the Treasury Board, shall cause
any adjustments to be made in the accounts of Canada that are required
as a result of any transaction authorized or required under this Part.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Early Retirement --- Suspension of Separation Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-35757 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of separation benefit

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person to whom a notice of surplus status is given between July 15,
  1995 and March 31, 1998 who subsequently elects to receive an annual
  benefit under section 4 of the [Retirement Compensation Arrangements
  Regulations, No. 2](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-169) and who ceases to be
  employed in the Public Service between June 23, 1998 and September 30,
  1998 is not entitled to a separation benefit under the Work Force
  Adjustment Directive.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), [[notice of
  surplus status]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[Public
  Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[Work Force Adjustment
  Directive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have the same meaning as in subsection
  2(1) of the [Retirement Compensation Arrangements Regulations, No.
  2](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-169).

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2008, c. 28, s. 94

  - ###### Liquidation {#liquidation .MarginalNote}

    - [94]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within six months after the day
      on which this subsection comes into force, or any longer time that
      the Governor in Council determines on the recommendation of the
      Minister of Human Resources and Skills Development, the Canada
      Millennium Scholarship Foundation, established by section 3 of the
      [Budget Implementation Act, 1998](/eng/acts/B-9.82) and referred
      to in this Part as "the Foundation", shall sell or otherwise
      dispose of all of its property other than property referred to in
      subsection (3) and discharge all of its liabilities, on terms and
      conditions that are approved by the Governor in Council.

    - ###### Donations of money {#donations-of-money .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), money that was donated to
      the Foundation under section 21 of the [Budget Implementation Act,
      1998](/eng/acts/B-9.82) --- including any income arising from the
      investment of the money --- and that was not used for carrying out
      the objects and purposes of the Foundation shall be paid to the
      donor.

    - ###### Transfer to department {#transfer-to-department .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} Within the period referred to in subsection (1),
      the Foundation shall transfer to the Department of Human Resources
      and Skills Development the following things, including any
      electronic versions of them:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the books of account and other records referred
        to in section 35 of the [Budget Implementation Act,
        1998](/eng/acts/B-9.82), as well as any information that the
        Foundation collected in order to produce them;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all other information that the Foundation has
        under its control concerning persons who have received
        scholarships or any other financial assistance from it;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any studies that the Foundation has under its
        control, and any other information that it has collected through
        research; and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any database containing information related to
        any of those books of account and other records, that other
        information and those studies, as well as any information
        necessary in order to use the database.

    - ###### Remaining money {#remaining-money .MarginalNote}

      [(4)]{.lawlabel} After satisfying the obligations set out in
      subsections (1) to (3), the Foundation shall deposit all of its
      remaining money in the Consolidated Revenue Fund to the credit of
      the Receiver General.

    - ###### Dissolution {#dissolution .MarginalNote}

      [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Foundation is dissolved.
