## Electoral Boundaries Readjustment Suspension Act, 1994 {#electoral-boundaries-readjustment-suspension-act-1994 .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1994, c. 19
:::

Assented to 1994-06-15

An Act to suspend the operation of the Electoral Boundaries Readjustment
Act
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Electoral Boundaries Readjustment Suspension Act,
1994](/eng/acts/E-3.1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of electoral boundaries
readjustment

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operation of subsection 20(2) and sections 21 to 28 of the [Electoral
  Boundaries Readjustment Act](/eng/acts/E-3) is suspended until such
  time as Parliament makes other provision in respect of those sections.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expiration of suspension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If no other provision is made in respect of
  subsection 20(2) and sections 21 to 28 of the [Electoral Boundaries
  Readjustment Act](/eng/acts/E-3) before June 22, 1995, that subsection
  and those sections cease to be suspended and are in force subject to
  this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of existing commissions

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#E-3.1_en_1){#E-3.1_en_1-ID0EBCA}[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
.sectionLabel}** An electoral boundaries commission established pursuant
to section 3 of the [Electoral Boundaries Readjustment
Act](/eng/acts/E-3) following the decennial census of Canada in 1991 is
temporarily discharged of its duties from the day on which it transmits
its completed report to the Chief Electoral Officer as required under
subsection 20(l) of the Electoral Boundaries Readjustment Act, or the
day on which this Act comes into force, whichever is the later, until
the day on which subsection 20(2) and sections 21 to 28 of the
[Electoral Boundaries Readjustment Act](/eng/acts/E-3) cease to be
suspended by virtue of subsection 2(2) of this Act.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#E-3.1_en_1-ID0EBCA){#E-3.1_en_1}\[Note: Act in
  force on assent June 15, 1994.\]
  :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parliamentary consideration of reports
and preparation of draft representation order

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** In the event that
subsection 20(2) and sections 21 to 28 of the [Electoral Boundaries
Readjustment Act](/eng/acts/E-3) cease to be suspended by virtue of
subsection 2(2) of this Act, sections 21 to 24 of that Act apply, with
such modifications as the circumstances require, in respect of the
reports of the electoral boundaries commissions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repeal

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Electoral Boundaries
Readjustment Suspension Act, chapter 25 of the Statutes of Canada, 1992,
is repealed.
