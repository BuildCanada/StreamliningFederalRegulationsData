## An Act to incorporate Continental Bank of Canada {#an-act-to-incorporate-continental-bank-of-canada .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1976-77, c. 58
:::

Assented to 1977-07-14

An Act to incorporate Continental Bank of Canada
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas IAC Limited, a corporation incorporated under the laws of
Canada, has by its petition prayed that it be enacted as hereinafter set
forth and it is expedient to grant the prayer of the said petition:
Therefore Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Incorporation and Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-93600 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby
incorporated a corporation under the name Continental Bank of Canada, in
English, and the name [Banque Continentale du Canada]{.otherLang
lang="fr"}, in French, hereinafter called the "Bank", consisting of such
persons as are from time to time shareholders of the Bank.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisional directors

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the persons who are the directors of IAC Limited on
  the coming into force of this Act are the provisional directors of the
  Bank, and this subsection constitutes a declaration of the names,
  addresses and occupations of those persons for purposes of subsection
  8(1) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A director of IAC Limited is not eligible to be a
  provisional director of the Bank unless he holds, as the absolute and
  sole owner thereof in his individual right and not as trustee or in
  the right of another, not less than one hundred common shares of the
  capital stock of IAC Limited, and the requirement in subsection 10(2)
  of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) that a provisional director of a
  bank be a subscriber for stock of that bank does not apply to a
  provisional director of the Bank.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized capital

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The authorized capital
stock of the Bank shall be one hundred million dollars, divided into
shares having a par value of ten dollars each.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  purposes of this Act, a corporation is a subsidiary of another
  corporation (in this subsection called the "parent corporation") only
  if it is controlled by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the parent corporation,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a group of two or more corporations each of which
    is either

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the parent corporation,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a corporation controlled by the parent
      corporation, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a corporation controlled by one or more
      corporations that are controlled by the parent corporation, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a corporation described in subpararaph (b)(ii) or
    (b)(iii).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For purposes of subsection (1), a corporation is
  controlled by another corporation or by two or more other corporations
  only if that other corporation owns, or those other corporations own,
  more than 50% of the issued shares having full voting rights in all
  circumstances of the capital stock of the first-mentioned corporation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the Bank
shall be in the Municipality of Metropolitan Toronto, in the Province of
Ontario.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of [Bank
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01)

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Act shall be construed
to confer upon the Bank all the powers, privileges and immunities, and
to subject it to all the liabilities and provisions set forth in the
[Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), except as provided in the [Bank
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) or in this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception to [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01)

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsections 11(1)
  and 12(1) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the provisional
  directors of the Bank are not required to give public notice of the
  opening of stock books of the Bank or of the meeting of subscribers
  referred to in subsection 12(1) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings of shareholders

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Until the day on which an amalgamation under
  subsection 10(1) takes effect, the annual general meeting or any other
  meeting of the shareholders of the Bank is validly constituted
  notwithstanding that only the holder of a proxy of IAC Limited is
  present and qualified to vote thereat, and, notwithstanding section 19
  of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), no public notice of any such
  meeting is required.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary exceptions to [Bank
  Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} During the period commencing on the day this Act
  comes into force and ending on the expiration of ten years next
  following that day or on the day on which an amalgamation under
  subsection 10(1) takes effect, whichever occurs first,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} IAC Limited may, notwithstanding sections 53 and 54
    of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} subscribe for shares of the capital stock of the
      Bank at not less than par value and cause to be registered in the
      name of IAC Limited the shares issued pursuant to such
      subscriptions, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} exercise, in person or by proxy, the voting
      rights pertaining to shares of the capital stock of the Bank
      registered in the name of IAC Limited;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person is not eligible to be a director of the
    Bank unless he holds, as the absolute and sole owner thereof in his
    individual right and not as trustee or in the right of another, not
    less that one hundred common shares of the capital stock of IAC
    Limited or such other number of common shares of that capital stock
    as the Minister of Finance may require, and, notwithstanding
    subsection 18(2) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), he is not
    required to hold stock of the Bank;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} notwithstanding subsection 18(7) of the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), a person is not ineligible to be elected or
    appointed a director of the Bank by reason of his being a director
    of IAC Limited or of a corporation that is a subsidiary of IAC
    Limited;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 75(2)(g) of the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) does not apply to the Bank; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the total principal amount outstanding, on the
    expiration of any financial year of the Bank, of all loans and
    advances that are made by the Bank on the security of real or
    immovable property of the kind described in subsection 75(4) of the
    [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), other than loans or advances made or
    guaranteed under any Act of the Parliament of Canada other than the
    [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) shall not exceed the amount referred to
    in paragraph 75(4)(a) of that Act but may, notwithstanding that
    subsection, exceed the amount referred to in paragraph 75(4)(b) of
    that Act.

## [Relationship Between the Bank and IAC Limited]{.HTitleText1} {#h-93639 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bank as wholly-owned subsidiary

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Until the day on which an
amalgamation under subsection 10(1) takes effect,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all of the voting shares in the capital stock of the
  Bank from time to time outstanding shall be held in the name or right
  of and for the use or benefit of IAC Limited;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} notwithstanding any provision of the [Bank
  Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), IAC Limited shall cause a majority of the
  persons who are directors of the Bank at any time to be persons who
  are also directors of IAC Limited.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1976-77, c. 58, s. 8
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 40, s. 91
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrictions on lending

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Bank shall not lend
money to IAC Limited and no loan by the Bank to any corporation that is
a subsidiary of IAC Limited but is not a subsidiary of the Bank shall be
made, or permitted to remain outstanding, at a time when such
corporation is carrying on any business, or owns any assets, that could
not be carried on or owned by a chartered bank in compliance with the
[Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), except as contemplated by Section 18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amalgamation

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} IAC
  Limited and the Bank shall, within ten years after the coming into
  force of this Act, amalgamate in accordance with the [Bank
  Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) and, subject to subsections (4) to (6), the
  Bank after the amalgamation is subject in all respects to the [Bank
  Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issue of shares

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding section 110 of the [Bank
  Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the Bank resulting from the amalgamation under
  subsection (1) may issue its shares to the persons who were the
  registered owners of shares of IAC Limited immediately prior to the
  amalgamation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of certain provisions of
  the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01)

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2.2), section 110.1 of the
  [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) applies to IAC Limited and in respect of
  the shares of IAC Limited.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2.2)]{.lawlabel} A person who immediately prior to the amalgamation
  under subsection (1) of this section was permitted by section 110.1 of
  the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) to continue to hold or beneficially
  own shares of IAC Limited that but for subsection (2) of that section
  would be held or beneficially owned in contravention of paragraph
  (1)(a) of that section, may hold or beneficially own the shares issued
  to him under subsection (2) of this section after the amalgamation
  during the same period of time as he would have been permitted by
  subsection 110.1(2) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) to continue to
  hold or beneficially own shares of IAC Limited had the amalgamation
  not occurred.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01)
  applies

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Prior to an amalgamation under subsection (1), if
  there are no parties to the amalgamation other than IAC Limited, the
  Bank or any wholly owned subsidiary of either of them, the Governor in
  Council may, by order, prescribe that, notwithstanding any provision
  of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the proposed amalgamation
  agreement need not be submitted to the shareholders of IAC Limited.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Outstanding indebtedness

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (6), if, when an amalgamation
  under subsection (1) takes effect, there is outstanding any
  indebtedness of IAC Limited, other that the debentures referred to in
  subsection (5), that is of a kind that the Bank is not permitted to
  incur under the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), then, notwithstanding
  the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), any such indebtedness incurred prior
  to October 28, 1975, remains outstanding after the amalgamation as
  indebtedness of the Bank and is binding upon and enforceable against
  the Bank in accordance with its terms, including any terms as to
  security.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exercise of rights of conversion

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (6), if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an amalgamation under subsection (1) takes effect
    prior to July 15, 1984, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the day when the amalgamation takes effect there
    are outstanding any debentures that carry rights of conversion into
    shares of IAC Limited to be issued on such conversion,

  then, notwithstanding the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), during the
  period from the day the amalgamation takes effect until July 15, 1984,
  the rights of conversion under any of those debentures that were
  issued prior to October 28, 1975 remain outstanding as rights of
  conversion into shares of the Bank and shares of the Bank may be
  validly issued during that period upon the exercise of the rights of
  conversion except that shares of the Bank may not be so issued to a
  person from whom a subscription for a share of the capital stock of
  the Bank could not, by reason of paragraphs 53(4)(a) or (b) or
  subsection 56(2) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), be accepted by
  the Bank.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of subsections (4) and (5)

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (4) and (5) apply to any indebtedness and
  any debentures referred to therein only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the terms thereof do not permit the debtor, at its
    option, to discharge the indebtedness or the debentures prior to the
    amalgamation, whether or not the discharge would require payment by
    the debtor of a premium or bonus; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of Finance consents to the application
    of those subsections to that indebtedness or those debentures upon
    submission to the Minister made by IAC Limited that it has attempted
    to arrive at alternative arrangements that would avoid the necessity
    of relying upon those subsections as to that indebtedness or those
    debentures.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Undertaking to discharge indebtedness

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The submission referred to in paragraph (6)(b) shall
  be accompanied by an undertaking to discharge the indebtedness at the
  first date upon which it may be discharged at the option of the
  debtor, whether or not upon payment of a premium or bonus.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discharge of indebtedness and
  debentures

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Any indebtedness referred to in subsection (4) and
  any debentures referred to in subsection (5) that have not met the
  conditions set out in subsection (6) shall be discharged prior to an
  amalgamation under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effects of amalgamation

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, all of the provisions of the
  [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) relating to the effects of an
  amalgamation apply to an amalgamation under subsection (1) except as
  provided in this section and section 11, but bonds, debentures and
  other evidences of indebtedness that the Bank is permitted to continue
  to have issued and outstanding pursuant to an order under paragraph
  269(1)(d) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) shall not be subject to
  this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confirming agreements

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} The Bank may enter into such agreements as may be
  reasonably necessary to confirm that any indebtedness to which
  subsection (4) applies remains outstanding after the amalgamation as
  indebtedness of the Bank, and that any debentures to which
  subsection (5) applies are convertible after the amalgamation into
  shares of the Bank to be issued on such conversion.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1976-77, c. 58, s. 10
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 40, s. 91
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing bank

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Bank shall be the
continuing corporation resulting from the amalgamation of the Bank and
IAC Limited referred to in subsection 10(1) so that, without limiting
the generality of the foregoing,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for purposes of clause 75(4)(b)(i)(B) of the [Bank
  Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the Bank after the amalgamation shall be
  considered to have been permitted under section 13 of the [Bank
  Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) to commence business when the Bank was
  originally permitted under that section to commence business;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for purposes of subsections 76(3), (4) and (6) of the
  [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the Bank after the amalgamation shall be
  considered to have acquired any shares owned by IAC Limited or the
  Bank prior to the amalgamation, on the date when those shares were
  acquired by IAC Limited or the Bank, as the case may be;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for purposes of paragraph 77(6)(b) of the [Bank
  Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), financial years of the Bank prior to the
  amalgamation shall be included after the amalgamation in determining
  the number of financial years of the Bank completed after the 31st day
  of October, 1965; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} except as provided in section 10, provisions of this
  Act applicable to IAC Limited or to the securities of IAC Limited are
  not applicable to the Bank or its securities after the amalgamation.

## [Application of the Bank Act to IAC Limited]{.HTitleText1} {#h-93686 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of [Bank
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01)

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 15 of this Act,
  the provisions of sections 18 to 24 and 38 to 56 and subsections
  158(1) and (2) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) apply to IAC
  Limited and sections 38 to 56 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01)
  apply to the shares of IAC Limited, and for the purpose of the
  application of those provisions,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} references to "a bank" or "the bank" shall be
    construed as references to IAC Limited;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the references in paragraph 18(2)(c) of the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) to shares on which five thousand dollars, or
    such greater amount as the by-laws require, have been paid up, shall
    be construed as a reference to one hundred paid-up common shares of
    the capital stock of IAC Limited or such other number of those
    shares as the Minister of Finance may require pursuant to paragraph
    7(3)(b); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the common shares without par value of the capital
    stock of IAC Limited shall be deemed to have a par value of twenty
    dollars or such other amount as the Minister of Finance may require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of other Acts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 39 to 43 and 86 to 92 of the [Canada
  Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8) and Part VI and sections 97, 100 to
  104 and 116 of the [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44)
  do not apply to IAC Limited, and sections 39 to 43 of the [Canada
  Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8) and Part VI of the [Canada Business
  Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44) to not apply to the shares of IAC
  Limited.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws of IAC Limited

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} By-laws of IAC Limited affecting the interpretation
  or application of the provisions of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01)
  made applicable to IAC Limited and its shares by subsection (1) are
  properly made if made in accordance with sections 17 and 27 of the
  [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investment Companies Act

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} To the extent that the requirements of sections 11 to
  13 of the Investment Companies Act differ from those of sections 53 to
  56 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), those sections of the
  Investment Companies Act do not apply to IAC Limited or to its shares
  but IAC Limited shall be considered for purposes of all other sections
  of the Investment Companies Act to be a sales finance company to or in
  respect of which sections 11 to 13 of the Act apply.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issue of shares

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** IAC Limited shall not
issue any additional shares of its capital stock unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} concurrently with the receipt by IAC Limited of the
  proceeds of the issue of shares, it applies an amount equal to the net
  cash proceeds to the purchase of shares in the capital stock of the
  Bank or as a contribution to the rest account of the Bank, or
  allocates that amount between the two applications; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the shares are issued in accordance with the
  procedures, and subject to the requirements, set out in sections 33 to
  35 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), and for that purpose
  references therein to "a bank" or "the bank" shall be construed as
  references to IAC Limited and subsection 33(1) shall be read without
  reference to the words "not less than par" therein;

and compliance with the procedures referred to in paragraph (b)
constitutes compliance with the requirements of the [Canada Corporations
Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8) and the [Canada Business Corporations
Act](/eng/acts/C-44) as to the issue of shares by IAC Limited and the
shares issued in compliance with those procedures constitute validly
issued shares.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain investments

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Subsections 76(6) and (7)
of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) apply to the shares owned by IAC
Limited in the capital stock of Sovereign Life Assurance Company of
Canada and of Sovereign General Insurance Company as if IAC Limited were
a bank that had acquired those shares on the day on which this Act comes
into force, and a transfer of those shares to any corporation that is a
subsidiary of IAC Limited does not constitute a sale or disposal for
purposes of those subsections, but the ownership by IAC Limited of those
shares in the capital stock of those corporations does not make section
16 applicable to those corporations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Securities of IAC Limited

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Neither the preferred shares of IAC
  Limited outstanding on October 28, 1975, nor the debentures of IAC
  Limited outstanding on that date and carrying a right of conversion
  into common shares of IAC Limited, shall be considered to have been
  issued or to be outstanding in contravention of the [Bank
  Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) or of this Act, and shares of IAC Limited may
  be validly issued upon the exercise of such conversion rights
  notwithstanding anything contained in the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01)
  or this Act, except that shares of IAC Limited may not be so issued to
  a person from whom a subscription for a share could not, by reason of
  paragraph 53(4)(a) or (b) or subsection 56(2) of the [Bank
  Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), be accepted by IAC Limited.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors and officers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The persons who are the directors and officers of IAC
  Limited on the coming into force of this Act shall be considered to
  have been duly elected or appointed in accordance with sections 19 and
  21 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).

## [Business of IAC Limited Pending Amalgamation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-93711 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited activities

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  the effective day as defined in subsection (2), IAC Limited shall not
  permit any corporation, other than the Bank or a subsidiary of the
  Bank, while that corporation is a subsidiary of IAC Limited (any such
  corporation being hereinafter in this section and in sections 17 to 19
  called a "restricted corporation"), to carry on any business or
  acquire any asset, nor shall IAC Limited carry on any business or
  acquire any asset, except as permitted by sections 17 and 18.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section and in sections 17 and 18,
  [[effective day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as to a business that could be carried on or an
    asset that could be acquired by the Bank without contravention of
    the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) or this Act, the day on which the
    Bank first carries on any of the activities referred to in
    subsection 75(1) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), or the day
    that is twelve months after the Bank obtains the approval of the
    Governor in Council to commence the business of banking, whichever
    occurs first; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as to any other business or asset, the day on which
    this Act comes into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permitted activities

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection 16(1),
  but subject to subsections (2) and (3), during the period commencing
  on the day this Act comes into force and ending on the expiration of
  ten years next following that day or on the day on which an
  amalgamation under subsection 10(1) takes effect, whichever occurs
  first,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} IAC Limited may acquire, and may permit any
    restricted corporation to acquire,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} assets from the Bank previously acquired by the
      Bank as permitted by the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) (such assets
      and other assets which the Bank is permitted to acquire under the
      [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) being hereinafter in this section
      called "eligible assets"), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} eligible assets from IAC Limited or any
      restricted corporation,

  but the prior consent of the Inspector General of Banks shall be
  required for the acquisition of any eligible assets that consist of
  shares in the capital stock of a corporation, other than a corporation
  that is a subsidiary of IAC Limited when this Act comes into force;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} IAC Limited may acquire, and may permit any
    restricted corporation to acquire, assets for the purpose of leasing
    such assets to its customers, and IAC Limited may enter into leases
    of any such assets and may permit any restricted corporation to
    enter into leases of any such assets; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} IAC Limited may lend money or make advances, and
    may permit any restricted corporation to lend money or make
    advances, upon the security of real or immovable property in Canada
    or of an equity of redemption therein or of an assignment of or
    mortgage on the interest of a lessee thereof where such loans or
    advances would not be permissible for the Bank by reason of the
    restrictions contained in subsections 75(3) or (4) of the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) (the said loans and advances, and leases of
    assets referred to in paragraph (b), being hereinafter in this
    section referred to as "non-eligible assets"); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} IAC Limited may acquire, and may permit any
    restricted corporation to acquire, non-eligible assets from any
    other of those corporations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception as to eligible assets

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At no time after the effective day defined in
  paragraph 16(2)(a) shall the aggregate value, determined in a manner
  acceptable to the Inspector General of Banks, of the eligible assets
  held by IAC Limited and every restricted corporation be in excess of
  the aggregate value, so determined, of the eligible assets held by
  them on the effective day defined in paragraph 16(2)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions as to non-eligible assets

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} At no time after the effective day defined in
  paragraph 16(2)(b) shall the aggregate value, determined in a manner
  acceptable to the Inspector General of Banks, of the non-eligible
  assets held by IAC Limited and every restricted corporation, excluding
  those non-eligible assets that are leases, loans or advances entered
  into subsequent to that date pursuant to commitments with the lessees
  or borrowers in effect on that date, be in excess of the aggregate
  value, so determined, of the non-eligible assets held by them on the
  effective day defined in paragraph 16(2)(b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** For purposes of
subsection 16(1), the following shall be considered not to constitute
carrying on business or the acquisition of assets:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the continued ownership of assets held prior to the
  effective day;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the taking of all actions and the doing or executing
  of all deeds or documents that may be reasonably necessary in
  connection with the assets referred to in paragraph (a) and any other
  assets acquired without contravention of the [Bank
  Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) or this Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the deposit or temporary investment of money derived
  from the recovery upon or sale of assets acquired without
  contravention of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) or this Act, if the
  deposit or investment is for the purpose of preserving the money for
  future investments not in contravention of the [Bank
  Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) or this Act;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the making of investments, whether by way of purchase
  of shares or indebtedness, by IAC Limited or any restricted
  corporation in any other of those corporations or, subject to
  paragraph 8(a), in the Bank;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 9, the borrowing of money; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} matters reasonably necessary to facilitate the
  effective performance of the activities referred to in paragraphs (a)
  to (e) or otherwise permitted under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection of customers

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A customer or other
person dealing with IAC Limited or any restricted corporation is under
no obligation to inquire into compliance by that corporation with
sections 16 and 17, and a contravention of those sections does not
affect any right or remedy otherwise available to that person.

## [Sanctions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-93743 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of exemptions

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** If the Bank or IAC
Limited or a director of the Bank or IAC Limited is, in the opinion of
the Minister of Finance, in contravention of any requirement of sections
8, 9, 12, 13, 15 or 16, then the Minister may, unless that contravention
is cured to his satisfaction not later than ninety days after written
notice describing the contravention has been sent by prepaid registered
mail by or on behalf of the Minister to the Bank, withdraw any or all of
the exceptions to the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) set out in subsection
7(3) of this Act or continue all or any of them on such terms and
conditions as he may require.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to amalgamate

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** If the Bank does not
amalgamate with IAC Limited within ten years after this Act comes into
force, the Bank shall not thereafter carry on the business of banking.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** A violation of any of the
provisions of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) made applicable to IAC
Limited by sections 12 to 14 of this Act constitutes an offence against
the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) and the person committing the offence
is liable to the penalties prescribed in the [Bank
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) for contravention of such provisions.

## [Other Matters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-93750 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No approval to commence business

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** If no approval permitting
the Bank to commence business is obtained by the Bank from the Governor
in Council within one year after the coming into force of this Act,
then, in addition to the consequences set out in subsection 15(1) of the
[Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the provisions of this Act that affect or
restrict IAC Limited, the subsidiaries of IAC Limited or the shares of
IAC Limited shall cease to have effect, except that subparagraph
7(3)(a)(ii) and paragraphs 7(3)(b) and (c) shall remain in effect for
purposes of giving effect to subsections 15(2) to (9) of the [Bank
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).
