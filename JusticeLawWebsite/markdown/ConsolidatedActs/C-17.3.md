## Department of Canadian Heritage Act {#department-of-canadian-heritage-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1995, c. 11
:::

Assented to 1995-06-15

An Act to establish the Department of Canadian Heritage and to amend and
repeal certain other Acts
::::

:::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69701 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Department of Canadian Heritage Act](/eng/acts/C-17.3).

## [Establishment of the Department]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69704 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Department established

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a department of the Government of Canada called the
  Department of Canadian Heritage over which the Minister of Canadian
  Heritage, appointed by commission under the Great Seal, shall preside.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister holds office during pleasure and has the
  management and direction of the Department.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy head

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
appoint an officer called the Deputy Minister of Canadian Heritage to
hold office during pleasure and to be the deputy head of the Department.

## [Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69712 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's powers, duties and functions

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  powers, duties and functions of the Minister extend to and include all
  matters over which Parliament has jurisdiction, not by law assigned to
  any other department, board or agency of the Government of Canada,
  relating to Canadian identity and values, cultural development and
  heritage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister's jurisdiction referred to in
  subsection (1) encompasses, but is not limited to, jurisdiction over

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the promotion of a greater understanding of human
    rights, fundamental freedoms and related values;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} multiculturalism;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the arts, including cultural aspects of the status
    of the artist;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} cultural heritage and industries, including
    performing arts, visual and audio-visual arts, publishing, sound
    recording, film, video and literature;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} national battlefields;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the encouragement, promotion and development of
    sport;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the advancement of the equality of status and use
    of English and French and the enhancement and development of the
    English and French linguistic minority communities in Canada;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} state ceremonial and Canadian symbols;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} broadcasting, except in respect of spectrum
    management and the technical aspects of broadcasting;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the formulation of cultural policy, including the
    formulation of cultural policy as it relates to foreign investment
    and copyright;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the conservation, exportation and importation of
    cultural property;

  - [(k.1)]{.lawlabel} the organization, sponsorship and promotion of
    public activities and events, in the National Capital Region as
    defined in section 2 of the [National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4),
    that will enrich the cultural and social fabric of Canada, taking
    into account the federal character of Canada, the equality of status
    of the official languages of Canada and the heritage of the people
    of Canada; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} national museums, archives and libraries.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General duties

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In exercising the powers
and performing the duties and functions assigned to the Minister by
section 4, the Minister shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} initiate, recommend, coordinate, implement and
  promote national policies, projects and programs with respect to
  Canadian identity and values, cultural development and heritage; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the Minister's jurisdiction referred
  to in paragraph 4(2)(k.1), coordinate the policies and programs of the
  Government of Canada respecting the organization, sponsorship or
  promotion by departments of public activities and events related to
  the National Capital Region as defined in section 2 of the [National
  Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 11, s. 5
- 2005, c. 2, s. 2
- 2013, c. 33, s. 215

## [Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69739 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other powers

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** In exercising the powers
and performing the duties and functions under this Act, the Minister may
enter into agreements with the government of any province or any agency
thereof.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69742 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial assistance and dealing with
property

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** To facilitate the
implementation of any program of the Minister under this Act, the
Minister may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide financial assistance in the form of grants,
  contributions and endowments to any person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to the [Federal Real Property and Federal
  Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4) and any direction made by the
  Treasury Board,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} acquire or seek to acquire any property by way of
    gift, bequest or other form of donation, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} subject to the [Surplus Crown Assets
    Act](/eng/acts/S-27) and to the terms and conditions on which the
    property was acquired, hold, administer, invest, expend, sell,
    exchange or otherwise dispose of that property; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} make gifts and issue prizes, awards, souvenirs and
  mementos on behalf of the Department of Canadian Heritage or of any
  other department.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 11, s. 7
- 2001, c. 4, s. 155
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Funding --- test cases

**[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** To promote a greater
understanding of human rights, fundamental freedoms and related values,
the Minister may take measures to provide funding to an organization,
independent of the Government of Canada, responsible for administering a
program whose purpose is to provide funding for test cases of national
significance to be brought before the courts to clarify and assert
constitutional human rights.

::: HistoricalNote
- [2023, c. 15, s. 52](#1402652-1405213){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1402652-1405213.html"
  role="button"}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees for services or use of facilities

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, subject to any regulations that the Treasury Board may
  make for the purposes of this section, fix the fees to be paid for a
  service or the use of a facility provided by the Minister, the
  Department or any board or agency of the Government of Canada for
  which the Minister has responsibility.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount not to exceed cost

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Fees for a service or the use of a facility that are
  fixed under subsection (1) may not exceed the cost to Her Majesty in
  right of Canada of providing the service or the use of the facility.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees for products, rights and privileges

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, subject
to any regulations that the Treasury Board may make for the purposes of
this section, fix fees in respect of products, rights and privileges
provided by the Minister, the Department or any board or agency of the
Government of Canada for which the Minister has responsibility.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees in respect of regulatory processes,
etc.

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, subject to any regulations that the Treasury Board may
  make for the purposes of this section, fix fees in respect of
  regulatory processes or approvals provided by the Minister, the
  Department or any board or agency of the Government of Canada for
  which the Minister has responsibility.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Fees that are fixed under subsection (1) shall in the
  aggregate not exceed an amount sufficient to compensate Her Majesty in
  right of Canada for any reasonable outlays incurred by Her Majesty for
  the purpose of providing the regulatory processes or approvals.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  fixing a fee under section 8, 9 or 10, the Minister shall consult with
  such persons or organizations as the Minister considers to be
  interested in the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fees that are fixed under section 8, 9 or 10
  shall be published once every calendar year in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) and by such appropriate electronic
  or other means that the Treasury Board may authorize by regulation and
  any change in those fees shall be published within thirty days after
  its effective date in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/)
  and by such appropriate electronic or other means that the Treasury
  Board may authorize by regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to Scrutiny Committee

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any fee fixed under section 8, 9 or 10 shall stand
  referred to the Committee referred to in section 19 of the [Statutory
  Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) to be reviewed and scrutinized as if
  it were a statutory instrument.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to make regulations

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Treasury Board may
make regulations for the purposes of sections 8, 9, 10 and 11.

## [Transitional]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69773 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Positions

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act shall be
  construed as affecting the status of an employee who, immediately
  before the coming into force of this subsection, occupied

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a position in that portion of the public service in
    the Department of the Environment referred to in clause (a)(i)(A) of
    Order in Council P.C. 1993-1489 of June 25, 1993,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a position in that portion of the public service in
    the Department of National Health and Welfare referred to in clause
    (a)(i)(B) of Order in Council P.C. 1993-1489 of June 25, 1993,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a position in the Department of the Secretary of
    State of Canada other than

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the portions referred to in clause (a)(i)(B)
      of Order in Council P.C. 1993-1484 of June 25, 1993, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the portions referred to in clause (a)(i)(A)
      of Order in Council P.C. 1993-1488 of June 25, 1993, known as the
      Student Assistance Branch and the Office of the Assistant Under
      Secretary of State (Social Development and Regional Operations),
      including the Education Support Branch, except for

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the Native Citizens Directorate,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Studies and Special Projects
        Directorate, and

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the Youth Participation Directorate,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a position in the Department of Communications
    other than in the portions referred to in clause (a)(i)(B) of Order
    in Council P.C. 1993-1487 of June 25, 1993, Order in Council P.C.
    1993-1670 of August 18, 1993 and clause (a)(i)(A) of Order in
    Council P.C. 1993-1484 of June 25, 1993, or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a position in the Department of Multiculturalism
    and Citizenship,

  except that the employee shall, on the coming into force of this
  subsection, occupy their position in the Department of Canadian
  Heritage under the authority of the Deputy Minister of Canadian
  Heritage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of "employee"

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has
  the same meaning as in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of appropriations

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Any amount appropriated,
for the fiscal year in which this section comes into force, by an
appropriation Act based on the Estimates for that year for defraying the
charges and expenses of the public service of Canada within the
Department of Communications or the Department of Multiculturalism and
Citizenship and that, on the day on which this section comes into force,
is unexpended, is deemed, on that day, to be an amount appropriated for
defraying the charges and expenses of the Department of Canadian
Heritage.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of powers, duties and functions

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Wherever under any Act of
  Parliament, any instrument made under an Act of Parliament or any
  order, contract, lease, licence or other document, any power, duty or
  function is vested in or exercisable by any of the persons referred to
  in subsection (2) in relation to any matter to which the powers,
  duties and functions of the Minister of Canadian Heritage extend by
  virtue of this Act, that power, duty or function is vested in or
  exercisable by the Minister of Canadian Heritage, the Deputy Minister
  of Canadian Heritage or the appropriate officer of the Department of
  Canadian Heritage, as the case may be, unless the Governor in Council
  by order designates another Minister, Deputy Minister or officer of
  the public service of Canada to exercise that power, duty or function.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the persons are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of the Environment, the Minister of
    National Health and Welfare, the Minister of State (Multiculturalism
    and Citizenship), the Minister of Multiculturalism and Citizenship,
    the Secretary of State of Canada and the Minister of Communications;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Deputy Minister of the Environment, the Deputy
    Minister of National Health and Welfare, the Deputy Minister of
    Multiculturalism and Citizenship, the Under-Secretary of State and
    the Deputy Minister of Communications; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any officer of the Department of the Environment,
    the Department of National Health and Welfare, the Department of
    Multiculturalism and Citizenship, the Department of the Secretary of
    State of Canada and the Department of Communications.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2013, c. 33, s. 216

  - ###### Definitions {#definitions .MarginalNote}

    [216]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in sections 217 to
    220.

    [[activity and event mandate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[activity and event mandate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
        organization, sponsoring or promotion of any public activities
        and events, in the National Capital Region as defined in section
        2 of the [National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4), that will enrich
        the cultural and social fabric of Canada. ([mandat en matière
        d'activités et de manifestations]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the National Capital
        Commission continued by section 3 of the [National Capital
        Act](/eng/acts/N-4). ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- --- 2013, c. 33, s. 217

  - ###### Appointment of employees {#appointment-of-employees .MarginalNote}

    [217]{.lawlabel} Every employee of the Commission whose functions
    relate to the activity and event mandate is deemed, on the day on
    which this section comes into force, to be a person appointed to a
    position in the Department of Canadian Heritage under the [Public
    Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) and to be an employee as
    defined in subsection 2(1) of that Act.

- --- 2013, c. 33, s. 218

  - ###### Credits transferred {#credits-transferred .MarginalNote}

    [218]{.lawlabel} Any money that is appropriated by an Act of
    Parliament for the fiscal year in which this section comes into
    force to defray any expenditures of the Commission related to the
    activity and event mandate and that is unexpended is deemed to have
    been appropriated to defray any operating expenditures of the
    Department of Canadian Heritage.

- --- 2013, c. 33, s. 219

  - ###### Assets, obligations and authorizations {#assets-obligations-and-authorizations .MarginalNote}

    [219]{.lawlabel} On the day on which this section comes into force,
    and to the extent that they relate to the activity and event
    mandate,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Commission's assets are transferred to Her
      Majesty in right of Canada as represented by the Minister of
      Canadian Heritage;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Commission's obligations are assumed by Her
      Majesty in right of Canada as represented by the Minister of
      Canadian Heritage;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} permits, licences and other authorizations issued
      to the Commission are transferred to Her Majesty in right of
      Canada as represented by the Minister of Canadian Heritage; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} permits, licences and other authorizations issued
      by the Commission are deemed to have been issued by Her Majesty in
      right of Canada as represented by the Minister of Canadian
      Heritage.

- --- 2013, c. 33, s. 220

  - ###### Continuation of legal proceedings {#continuation-of-legal-proceedings .MarginalNote}

    [220]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal proceeding to which
    the Commission is party that is pending in any court on the day on
    which this section comes into force and that relates to the activity
    and event mandate may be continued by or against Her Majesty in
    right of Canada in the same manner and to the same extent as it
    could have been continued by or against the Commission.
