## [Purpose of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1245287 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supervision and protection

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act
is to ensure that financial institutions, the external complaints body,
payment card network operators, participating entities and the technical
standards body are supervised by an agency of the Government of Canada
so as to contribute to the protection of consumers of financial products
and services and the public and to the safety and security of
consumer-driven banking, including by strengthening the financial
literacy of Canadians.

## [Establishment of Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-222474 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established an agency of the Government of Canada called the
  Financial Consumer Agency of Canada over which the Minister shall
  preside and for which the Minister shall be responsible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The objects of the Agency are to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} supervise financial institutions and the external
    complaints body to determine whether they are in compliance with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the consumer provisions applicable to them, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the terms and conditions or undertakings with
      respect to the protection of customers of financial institutions
      that the Minister imposes or requires, as the case may be, under
      an Act listed in Schedule 1 and the directions that the Minister
      imposes under this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} strive to protect the rights and interests of
    consumers of financial products and services and the public, taking
    into account the need of financial institutions to efficiently
    manage their business operations;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2018, c. 27, s. 338\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} promote the adoption by financial institutions of
    policies and procedures designed to implement provisions, terms and
    conditions, undertakings or directions referred to in paragraph (a)
    --- as well as voluntary codes of conduct that are adopted by
    financial institutions, and any public commitments made by them,
    that are designed to protect the rights and interests of their
    customers --- and monitor the implementation of those voluntary
    codes and public commitments;

  - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} promote the adoption by the external complaints
    body of policies and procedures designed to implement the
    provisions, terms and conditions, undertakings or directions
    referred to in paragraph (a);

  - [(c.2)]{.lawlabel} monitor and evaluate trends and emerging issues
    that may have an impact on consumers of financial products and
    services, and make information on those trends and issues public;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} strengthen the financial literacy of Canadians and
    promote consumer awareness about the obligations of financial
    institutions and the external complaints body under the consumer
    provisions applicable to them and about all matters connected with
    the protection of consumers of financial products and services; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} foster, in co-operation with any department, agency
    or agent corporation of the Government of Canada or of a province,
    financial institutions and consumer and other organizations, an
    understanding of financial services and issues relating to financial
    services.

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2018, c. 27, s. 338\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2018, c. 27, s. 338\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects --- payment card network
  operators

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The objects of the Agency are also to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} supervise payment card network operators to
    determine whether they are in compliance with the provisions of the
    [Payment Card Networks Act](/eng/acts/P-4.3) and its regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} promote the adoption by payment card network
    operators of policies and procedures designed to implement the
    provisions of the [Payment Card Networks Act](/eng/acts/P-4.3) and
    its regulations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} monitor the implementation of voluntary codes of
    conduct that have been adopted by payment card network operators and
    that are publicly available, and to monitor any public commitments
    made by them regarding their commercial practices in relation to
    payment card networks; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} promote public awareness about the obligations of
    payment card network operators under a voluntary code of conduct or
    under the [Payment Card Networks Act](/eng/acts/P-4.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects --- consumer-driven banking

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The objects of the Agency in relation to
  consumer-driven banking are to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} supervise the participating entities, the external
    complaints body and the technical standards body to determine
    whether they are in compliance with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the provisions of the [Consumer-Driven Banking
      Act](/eng/acts/C-36.75) that are applicable to them, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any terms and conditions or undertakings with
      respect to consumer-driven banking that the Minister imposes or
      requires, as the case may be, under an Act listed in Schedule 1
      and the directions that the Minister imposes under this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} monitor and evaluate trends and emerging issues
    that may have an impact on consumers of consumer-driven banking,
    including the trends and issues in respect of products, services and
    market developments, and make information on those trends and issues
    public;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} foster --- in co-operation with any department,
    agency or agent corporation of the Government of Canada or of a
    province, financial institutions and consumer and other
    organizations --- participation in consumer-driven banking; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} foster an understanding of consumer-driven banking
    and related issues among consumers.

## [Commissioner of Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-222500 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Commissioner

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall appoint an officer to be called the
  Commissioner of the Financial Consumer Agency of Canada. The
  Commissioner has the rank and all the powers of a deputy head of a
  department.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure of office and removal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner holds office during good behaviour
  for a term of not more than five years, but may be removed for cause
  by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further terms

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner, on the expiration of any term of
  office, is eligible to be re-appointed for a further term of office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  Commissioner, or if the office of Commissioner is vacant, the Minister
  may appoint a qualified person to exercise the powers and perform the
  duties and functions of the Commissioner, but no person may be so
  appointed for a term of more than 90 days without the approval of the
  Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall be paid the remuneration fixed
  by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner and any person appointed under
  subsection (4) are entitled to be paid reasonable travel and living
  expenses incurred in the course of performing their duties while
  absent from their ordinary place of work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed employment

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner and any person appointed under
  subsection (4) are deemed to be employed in the public service for the
  purposes of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36)
  and to be employed in the federal public administration for the
  purposes of the [Government Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5)
  and any regulations made under section 9 of the [Aeronautics
  Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

## [Powers, Duties and Functions of Commissioner]{.HTitleText1} {#h-222535 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions of the
Commissioner

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner has the powers, duties and functions assigned to the
  Commissioner by this Act and by the Acts listed in Schedule 1 and
  shall examine and inquire into, and report to the Minister from time
  to time on, all matters connected with the administration of this Act
  and of the consumer provisions of those other Acts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Functions --- [Payment Card Networks
  Act](/eng/acts/P-4.3)

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall also examine and inquire
  into, and report to the Minister from time to time on, all matters
  connected with the administration of the [Payment Card Networks
  Act](/eng/acts/P-4.3) and its regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may collect any personal information
  that he or she considers necessary in furtherance of the objects
  described in subsection 3(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal information

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may also collect any personal
  information that he or she considers necessary in furtherance of the
  objects described in subsection 3(3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review re voluntary codes of conduct

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner, if a financial institution has
  adopted a voluntary code of conduct referred to in paragraph 3(2)(c)
  or made a commitment designed to protect the interests of its
  customers, may make or cause to be made any review that he or she
  considers necessary to monitor compliance with the code or the
  commitment, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review

  [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} If a payment card network operator has adopted a
  voluntary code of conduct or made a public commitment referred to in
  paragraph 3(3)(c), the Commissioner may make or cause to be made any
  review that he or she considers necessary to monitor compliance with
  the code or the commitment, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Respect for other monitors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} When acting under subsection (3), the Commissioner
  shall have due regard for the role of any department, agency or agent
  corporation of the Government of Canada or of a province or any
  organization that has a role in monitoring compliance by financial
  institutions with voluntary codes of conduct or commitments.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activities

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may carry on any activity that he or
  she considers necessary in furtherance of the objects described in
  subsection 3(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activities

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may also carry on any activity that
  he or she considers necessary in furtherance of the objects described
  in subsection 3(3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's direction

- **[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may give a written direction to the Agency if the Minister is
  of the opinion that it can strengthen consumer protection and the
  public's confidence in that protection, foster safe and secure
  consumer-driven banking or enhance the financial literacy of
  Canadians.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of implementation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} After implementing the direction, the Commissioner
  shall notify the Minister without delay that the direction has been
  implemented.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Best interests

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Compliance by the Agency with a direction is deemed
  to be in its best interests.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply to a direction given under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause a notice to be published in
  the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) that a direction was
  given under subsection (1) as soon as is feasible after the direction
  is implemented.

## [Assessments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-222667 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissioner to ascertain expenses

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner shall, before December 31 in each year, ascertain the
  total amount of expenses incurred during the immediately preceding
  fiscal year for or in connection with the administration of this Act
  and the consumer provisions --- excluding the expenses incurred in
  connection with the objects described in subsection 3(3) --- and the
  amounts of any prescribed categories of those expenses in relation to
  any prescribed group of financial institutions and the external
  complaints body.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissioner to ascertain expenses ---
  direction

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} After the publication of a notice under subsection
  5.1(5), the Commissioner shall ascertain the amount of expenses that
  the Agency incurred in complying with the direction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount conclusive

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amounts ascertained under subsections (1) and
  (1.1) are final and conclusive for the purposes of this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} As soon as possible after ascertaining the amounts
  under subsections (1) and (1.1), the Commissioner shall assess a
  portion of the total amount of expenses against each financial
  institution and the external complaints body to the extent and in the
  manner that the Governor in Council may, by regulation, prescribe.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} No assessment is to be made in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a company to which the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies that was exempt from the application
    of paragraphs 165(2)(f) and (g) and sections 479 to 489.2 of that
    Act during the entire fiscal year to which the assessment relates;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a foreign company to which that Act applies that
    was exempt from the application of sections 598 to 607.1 of that Act
    during the entire fiscal year to which the assessment relates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interim assessment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may, during each fiscal year,
  prepare an interim assessment against any financial institution or the
  external complaints body.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment for financial literacy

  [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may, during a fiscal year, impose
  an assessment against any financial institution to cover some or all
  of the expenses related to initiatives to strengthen the financial
  literacy of Canadians.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment is binding

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every assessment and interim assessment is final and
  conclusive and binding on the financial institution against which it
  is made or the external complaints body, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissioner to ascertain expenses ---
  payment card network operators

  [(5.1)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall, before December 31 in each
  year, ascertain the total amount of expenses incurred during the
  immediately preceding fiscal year in connection with the objects
  described in paragraphs 3(3)(a), (b) and (d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount conclusive

  [(5.2)]{.lawlabel} The amount ascertained under subsection (5.1) is
  final and conclusive for the purposes of this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment

  [(5.3)]{.lawlabel} As soon as possible after ascertaining the amount
  under subsection (5.1), the Commissioner shall assess a portion of the
  total amount of expenses against each payment card network operator to
  the extent and in the manner that may be prescribed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interim assessment

  [(5.4)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may, during each fiscal year,
  prepare an interim assessment against any payment card network
  operator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment is binding

  [(5.5)]{.lawlabel} Every assessment and interim assessment is final
  and conclusive and binding on the payment card network operator
  against which it is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every assessment and interim assessment constitutes a
  debt due to Her Majesty, is immediately payable and may be recovered
  as a debt in any court of competent jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Interest may be charged on the unpaid amount of an
  assessment or interim assessment at a rate equal to the rate
  prescribed under the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) for amounts
  payable by the Minister of National Revenue as refunds of overpayments
  of tax under that Act in effect from time to time plus 2%.

### [Proceedings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-222730 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission of violation

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  contravention or non-compliance that is designated under paragraphs
  19(1)(a) to (a.2) constitutes a violation and the person that commits
  the violation is liable to a penalty determined in accordance with
  sections 19 and 20.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of violation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Commissioner believes on reasonable grounds
  that a person has committed a violation, he or she may issue, and
  shall cause to be served on the person, a notice of violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice of violation shall name the person believed
  to have committed a violation, identify the violation and set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the penalty that the Commissioner proposes to
    impose;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the right of the person, within 30 days after the
    notice is served, or within any longer period that the Commissioner
    specifies, to pay the penalty or to make representations to the
    Commissioner with respect to the violation and the proposed penalty,
    and the manner for doing so; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fact that, if the person does not pay the
    penalty or make representations in accordance with the notice, the
    person will be deemed to have committed the violation and the
    Commissioner may impose a penalty in respect of it.

### [Determination of Responsibility and Penalty]{.HTitleText2} {#h-222743 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of penalty

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  person pays the penalty proposed in the notice of violation, the
  person is deemed to have committed the violation and proceedings in
  respect of it are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representations to Commissioner

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the person makes representations in accordance
  with the notice, the Commissioner shall decide, on a balance of
  probabilities, whether the person committed the violation and, if so,
  may, subject to any regulations made under paragraph 19(1)(b), impose
  the penalty proposed, a lesser penalty or no penalty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to pay or make representations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who neither pays the penalty nor makes
  representations in accordance with the notice is deemed to have
  committed the violation and the Commissioner may, subject to any
  regulations made under paragraph 19(1)(b), impose the penalty
  proposed, a lesser penalty or no penalty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision and right of appeal

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall cause notice of any decision
  made under subsection (2) or (3) to be issued and served on the person
  together with notice of the right of appeal under section 24.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Court

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On an appeal, the Court may confirm, set aside or,
  subject to any regulations made under paragraph 19(1)(b), vary the
  decision of the Commissioner.

## AMENDMENTS NOT IN FORCE {#amendments-not-in-force .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2024, c. 17, s. 213

  - - [213]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 18(1) of the Act is
      replaced by the following:

      :::: section
      ::: AmendedText
      - ###### Commissioner to ascertain expenses {#commissioner-to-ascertain-expenses .MarginalNote}

        - [18]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner must, before
          December 31 in each year, ascertain the total amount of
          expenses incurred during the immediately preceding fiscal year
          for or in connection with the administration of this Act and
          the consumer provisions --- excluding the expenses incurred in
          connection with the objects described in subsections 3(3)
          and (4) --- and the amounts of any prescribed categories of
          those expenses in relation to any prescribed group of
          financial institutions and the external complaints body.
      :::
      ::::

    - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Section 18 of the Act is amended by adding the
      following after subsection (5.5):

      :::: section
      ::: AmendedText
      - ###### Commissioner to ascertain expenses --- participating entities {#commissioner-to-ascertain-expenses-participating-entities .MarginalNote}

        [(5.6)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner must, before December 31 in
        each year, ascertain the total amount of expenses incurred
        during the immediately preceding fiscal year in connection with
        the objects described in subsection 3(4).

      - ###### Amount conclusive {#amount-conclusive .MarginalNote}

        [(5.7)]{.lawlabel} The amount ascertained under subsection (5.6)
        is final and conclusive for the purposes of this section.

      - ###### Assessment {#assessment .MarginalNote}

        [(5.8)]{.lawlabel} As soon as possible after ascertaining the
        amount under subsection (5.6), the Commissioner must assess a
        portion of the total amount of expenses against each
        participating entity to the extent and in the manner that may be
        prescribed.

      - ###### Interim assessment {#interim-assessment .MarginalNote}

        [(5.9)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may, during each fiscal
        year, prepare an interim assessment against any participating
        entity.

      - ###### Assessment is binding {#assessment-is-binding .MarginalNote}

        [(5.91)]{.lawlabel} Every assessment and interim assessment is
        final and conclusive and binding on the participating entity
        against which it is made.
      :::
      ::::

- --- 2024, c. 17, s. 215

  - [215]{.lawlabel} Sections 20.1 and 21 of the Act are replaced by the
    following:

    :::: section
    ::: AmendedText
    - ###### Purpose of penalty {#purpose-of-penalty .MarginalNote}

      [20.1]{.lawlabel} The purpose of the penalty is not to punish but
      to promote compliance with the consumer provisions, compliance
      agreements that have been entered into under an Act listed in
      Schedule 1, any terms and conditions, undertakings or directions
      referred to in subparagraph 3(2)(a)(ii) or (4)(a)(ii), the
      provisions of the [Payment Card Networks Act](/eng/acts/P-4.3),
      the [Consumer-Driven Banking Act](/eng/acts/C-36.75) or their
      regulations, and agreements entered into under section 7.1.

    - ###### How act or omission may be proceeded with {#how-act-or-omission-may-be-proceeded-with .MarginalNote}

      [21]{.lawlabel} If a contravention or non-compliance that is
      designated under paragraph 19(1)(a), (a.1) or (a.3) can be
      proceeded with either as a violation or as an offence, proceeding
      in one manner precludes proceeding in the other.
    :::
    ::::

- --- 2024, c. 17, s. 216

  - [216]{.lawlabel} Section 22 of the Act is replaced by the following:

    :::: section
    ::: AmendedText
    - ###### Commission of violation {#commission-of-violation .MarginalNote}

      - [22]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every contravention or
        non-compliance that is designated under paragraphs 19(1)(a) to
        (a.3) constitutes a violation and the person that commits the
        violation is liable to a penalty determined in accordance with
        sections 19 and 20.

      - ###### Notice of violation --- Commissioner {#notice-of-violation-commissioner .MarginalNote}

        [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Commissioner believes on reasonable
        grounds that a person has committed a violation referred to in
        any of paragraphs 19(1)(a) to (a.2), they may issue and must
        cause to be served on the person a notice of violation.

      - ###### Notice of violation --- Senior Deputy Commissioner {#notice-of-violation-senior-deputy-commissioner .MarginalNote}

        [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} If the Senior Deputy Commissioner believes on
        reasonable grounds that a person has committed a violation
        referred to in paragraph 19(1)(a.3), they may issue and must
        cause to be served on the person a notice of violation.

      - ###### Contents of notice {#contents-of-notice .MarginalNote}

        [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice of violation must name the person
        believed to have committed a violation, identify the violation
        and set out

        - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the penalty that the Commissioner or Senior
          Deputy Commissioner, as the case may be, proposes to impose;

        - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the right of the person, within 30 days after
          the notice is served, or within any longer period that the
          Commissioner or Senior Deputy Commissioner, as the case may
          be, specifies, to pay the penalty or to make representations
          to the Commissioner or Senior Deputy Commissioner, as the case
          may be, with respect to the violation and the proposed
          penalty, and the manner for doing so; and

        - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fact that, if the person does not pay the
          penalty or make representations in accordance with the notice,
          the person will be deemed to have committed the violation and
          the Commissioner or Senior Deputy Commissioner, as the case
          may be, may impose a penalty in respect of it.
    :::
    ::::

- --- 2024, c. 17, s. 217

  - [217]{.lawlabel} Subsections 23(2) to (4) of the Act are replaced by
    the following:

    :::: section
    ::: AmendedText
    - ###### Representations to Commissioner {#representations-to-commissioner .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the person makes representations in accordance
      with the notice, the Commissioner or Senior Deputy Commissioner,
      as the case may be, must decide, on a balance of probabilities,
      whether the person committed the violation and, if so, may,
      subject to any regulations made under paragraph 19(1)(b), impose
      the penalty set out in the notice, a lesser penalty or no penalty.

    - ###### Failure to pay or make representations {#failure-to-pay-or-make-representations .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who neither pays the penalty nor makes
      representations in accordance with the notice is deemed to have
      committed the violation and the Commissioner or Senior Deputy
      Commissioner, as the case may be, may, subject to any regulations
      made under paragraph 19(1)(b), impose the penalty proposed, a
      lesser penalty or no penalty.

    - ###### Notice of decision and right of appeal {#notice-of-decision-and-right-of-appeal .MarginalNote}

      [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner or Senior Deputy Commissioner,
      as the case may be, must cause notice of any decision made under
      subsection (2) or (3) to be issued and served on the person
      together with notice of the right of appeal under section 24.
    :::

- --- 2024, c. 17, s. 221

  - [221]{.lawlabel} Sections 30 and 31 of the Act are replaced by the
    following:

    :::: section
    ::: AmendedText
    - ###### Time limit {#time-limit .MarginalNote}

      - [30]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} No proceedings in respect of a
        violation may be commenced later than two years after the
        subject-matter of the proceedings became known to the
        Commissioner or Senior Deputy Commissioner, as the case may be.

      - ###### Certificate {#certificate .MarginalNote}

        [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document appearing to have been issued by the
        Commissioner or Senior Deputy Commissioner, as the case may be,
        certifying the day on which the subject-matter of any
        proceedings became known to them, is admissible in evidence
        without proof of the signature or official character of the
        person appearing to have signed the document and is, in the
        absence of evidence to the contrary, proof of the matter
        asserted in it.

    - ###### Publication {#publication .MarginalNote}

      - [31]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any regulations, the
        Commissioner or Senior Deputy Commissioner, as the case may be,
        must make public the nature of a violation, the name of the
        person who committed it and the amount of the penalty imposed.

      - ###### Publication --- reasons {#publication-reasons .MarginalNote}

        [(2)]{.lawlabel} In making public the nature of a violation, the
        Commissioner or Senior Deputy Commissioner, as the case may be,
        may include the reasons for their decision, including the
        relevant facts, analysis and considerations that formed part of
        the decision.
