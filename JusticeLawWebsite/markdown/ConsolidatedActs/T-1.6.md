## Tax Conventions Implementation Act, 2004 {#tax-conventions-implementation-act-2004 .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2005, c. 8
:::

Assented to 2005-03-23

An Act to implement an agreement, conventions and protocols concluded
between Canada and Gabon, Ireland, Armenia, Oman and Azerbaijan for the
avoidance of double taxation and the prevention of fiscal evasion
::::

::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-447729 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Tax Conventions Implementation Act, 2004](/eng/acts/T-1.6).

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Canada--Gabon Tax Convention]{.HTitleText1} {#h-447732 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  [Canada--Gabon Tax Convention Act, 2004](/eng/acts/C-5.8) is enacted
  as follows:

  \[See [Canada--Gabon Tax Convention Act, 2004](/eng/acts/C-5.8)\]

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The schedule to the [Canada--Gabon Tax Convention
  Act, 2004](/eng/acts/C-5.8) is set out in Schedule 1 to this Act.
