## Haida Nation Recognition Act {#haida-nation-recognition-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2024, c. 29
:::

Assented to 2024-11-07

An Act respecting the recognition of the Haida Nation and the Council of
the Haida Nation
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the Haida Nation, His Majesty in right of British Columbia and
His Majesty in right of Canada, on August 13, 2021, entered into an
agreement entitled ["GayG̱ahlda • Kwah.hlahl.dáyaa "Changing Tide"
Framework for Reconciliation"]{style="font-family:Arial"} to advance
their objective of achieving reconciliation through negotiations;

Whereas an agreement entitled ["Nang K̲'uula • Nang K̲'úulaas Recognition
Agreement"]{style="font-family:Arial"} was entered into on July 18, 2023
on behalf of the Haida Nation, His Majesty in right of British Columbia
and His Majesty in right of Canada;

And whereas an Act of Parliament is required to implement sections 5 and
7 of that agreement;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Haida Nation Recognition Act](/eng/acts/H-0.3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Council of the Haida Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Council of the Haida Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    government of the Haida Nation recognized under paragraph
    3(b). ([Conseil de la Nation haïda]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Haida Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Haida Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the collectivity of Haida
    citizens referred to in Article 2 of the Constitution of the Haida
    Nation. ([Nation haïda]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Secretariat of the Haida Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Secretariat of the Haida Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    administrative body of the Council of the Haida Nation that is
    registered as a society incorporated under the Societies Act, S.B.C.
    2015, c. 18, immediately before the day on which section 4 of the
    Haida Nation Recognition Act, S.B.C. 2023, c. 24, comes into
    force. ([Secrétariat de la Nation haïda]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recognition

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
recognizes that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Haida Nation holds inherent rights of governance
  and self-determination; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Council of the Haida Nation is the government
  that is authorized to act on behalf of the Haida Nation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** In accordance with the
Constitution of the Haida Nation, the Haida Nation exercises its
governing powers through the Council of the Haida Nation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity, rights, powers and privileges

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Council of the Haida
Nation is a legal entity and has the capacity, rights, powers and
privileges of a natural person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity --- representatives and public
officials

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  action or other proceeding for damages lies or may be instituted
  against a representative of the Haida Nation or a public official for
  anything done or omitted to be done in the exercise or purported
  exercise of any power, or in the performance or purported performance
  of any duty or function unless it is established that the person acted
  in bad faith.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vicarious liability --- Council and
  Haida Nation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not absolve the Council of the
  Haida Nation or the Haida Nation from vicarious liability arising out
  of anything done or omitted to be done by a representative of the
  Haida Nation or a public official in the exercise or purported
  exercise of any power, or in the performance or purported performance
  of any duty or function, for which the Council of the Haida Nation or
  the Haida Nation would be liable if that subsection were not in force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vicarious liability --- public entities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not absolve a body, board,
  commission or corporate entity referred to in paragraph (a) of the
  definition of [[public official]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
  subsection (4) from vicarious liability arising out of anything done
  or omitted to be done by a public official in the exercise or
  purported exercise of any power, or in the performance or purported
  performance of any duty or function, for which that body, board,
  commission or corporate entity would be liable if that subsection were
  not in force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in this section.

  [[public official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[public official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a director, officer, commissioner, trustee or
        employee of the Council of the Haida Nation or an appointee of
        the Council of the Haida Nation or of a body, board, commission
        or corporate entity established by the Council of the Haida
        Nation that delivers public services and programs;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person, including a volunteer, who
        participates in the delivery of public services and programs
        under the supervision of a person referred to in paragraph (a)
        or a representative of the Haida Nation; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an election official appointed by the Council
        of the Haida Nation. ([titulaire de charge
        publique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[representative of the Haida Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[representative of the Haida Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      person who is identified as an elected representative and referred
      to in Article 5.S3 of the Constitution of the Haida Nation or who
      is identified as a hereditary chief and referred to in Article
      5.S7 of that constitution. ([représentant de la Nation
      haïda]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make any orders and regulations that are necessary for the purpose of
this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interests, obligations, etc. vested or
transferred

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** On the day on which section
4 of the Haida Nation Recognition Act, S.B.C. 2023, c. 24, comes into
force

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all interests, assets, properties and tenures of the
  Secretariat of the Haida Nation vest in the Council of the Haida
  Nation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all obligations and liabilities of the Secretariat of
  the Haida Nation are transferred to the Council of the Haida Nation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of agreements

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An agreement entered into
by the Secretariat of the Haida Nation and on behalf of His Majesty in
right of Canada before the day on which section 4 of the Haida Nation
Recognition Act, S.B.C. 2023, c. 24, comes into force continues in force
in accordance with its terms as if it were entered into by the Council
of the Haida Nation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of legal arrangements

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act
affects the legal arrangements between His Majesty in right of Canada
and the Skidegate Band Council and Old Massett Village Council or the
members of Skidegate and Old Massett Village Council bands.
