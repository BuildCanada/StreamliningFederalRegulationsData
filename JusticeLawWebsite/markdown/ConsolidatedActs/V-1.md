## Department of Veterans Affairs Act {#department-of-veterans-affairs-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. V-1
:::

An Act respecting the Department of Veterans Affairs
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-463827 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Department of Veterans Affairs Act](/eng/acts/V-1).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. V-1, s. 1
- 2000, c. 34, s. 95(F)
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-463832 .Part}

Definition of [[dependant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
this Act, [[dependant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the spouse or
common-law partner or child of any person referred to in subparagraph
4(a)(i), or any other person who provided or received care or support,
financial or otherwise, to or from that person.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2011, c. 24, s. 179
:::

## [Establishment of the Department]{.HTitleText1} {#h-463837 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Department established

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a department of the Government of Canada called the
  Department of Veterans Affairs over which the Minister of Veterans
  Affairs appointed by commission under the Great Seal shall preside.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister holds office during pleasure and has the
  management and direction of the Department.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. V-1, s. 2
- 2000, c. 34, s. 94(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy head

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
appoint an officer called the Deputy Minister of Veterans Affairs to
hold office during pleasure and to be the deputy head of the Department.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. V-1, s. 3
- 2000, c. 34, s. 94(F)
:::

## [Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-463849 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions of Minister

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The powers, duties and
functions of the Minister extend and apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the administration of such Acts of Parliament, and of
  such orders of the Governor in Council, as are not by law assigned to
  any other department of the Government of Canada or any Minister
  thereof, relating to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the care, treatment or re-establishment in civil
    life of any person who served in the Canadian Forces or merchant
    navy or in the naval, army or air forces or merchant navies of Her
    Majesty, of any person who has otherwise engaged in pursuits
    relating to war, and of any other person designated by the Governor
    in Council, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the care of the dependants or survivors of any
    person referred to in subparagraph (i); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all such other matters and such boards and other
  bodies, subjects, services and properties of the Crown as may be
  designated, or assigned to the Minister, by the Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. V-1, s. 4
- 2000, c. 34, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Gifts and bequests

**[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may accept
and administer any property and moneys that are conveyed to the Minister
for the benefit of a class or subclass of persons referred to in
subparagraph 4(a)(i) or (ii), on such terms and conditions as are set
out in the conveyance or, if none are set out, on such terms and
conditions as the Minister deems reasonable.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 34, s. 12
:::

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} with respect to reciprocal or other arrangements

      persons who have served in the naval, army or air forces or
      merchant navy of any such government,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the issue of payments, grants or allowances to

      survivors,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the assumption or authorization of guardianship

      necessitating an escort when travelling, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the treatment of persons classified as wholly
      incurable, or chronically recurrent cases needing institutional
      care;

  - [(g.1)]{.lawlabel} for providing, maintaining and replacing

    - [(i.1)]{.lawlabel} the person died of an injury or a disease for

  - [(g.6)]{.lawlabel} providing for the Minister to enter into

  - [(g.7)]{.lawlabel} respecting matters that must be included in
    agreements referred to in paragraph (g.6);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the provision of benefits, similar to those

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2000, c. 34, s. 13\]]{.Repealed}

## [Overpayments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-463912 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[overpayment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[5.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[overpayment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to any
  period, means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a payment that was paid to a person in respect of
    that period and to which the person had no entitlement under the
    regulations made under section 5; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a payment was paid to a person in respect of
    that period that was in excess of the amount of the payment to which
    the person was entitled under the regulations made under section 5,
    the amount of that excess.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of overpayments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, through any cause, an overpayment is paid to a
  person, the overpayment is a debt due to Her Majesty by that person or
  by that person's estate or succession, and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} may be recovered by deduction from any future
    payments made under the regulations made under section 5 to that
    person or to that person's estate or succession;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may be recovered in accordance with section 155 of
    the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} may be recovered in any court of competent
    jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission of overpayments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person has received or obtained an overpayment
  and the Minister is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the overpayment cannot be recovered within the
    reasonably foreseeable future,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the administrative costs of recovering the
    overpayment are likely to equal or exceed the amount to be
    recovered,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} repayment of the overpayment would cause undue
    hardship to the person, or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the overpayment is the result of an administrative
    error, delay or oversight on the part of a public servant,

  the Minister may, unless that person has been convicted of an offence
  under the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) in connection with the
  receiving or obtaining of the overpayment, remit all or any portion of
  the overpayment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery from survivor

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a survivor of a deceased person retains any
  amount paid to that person after the last day of the month in which
  that person died, that amount may be deducted from any amount payable
  to the survivor under the regulations made under section 5.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 34, s. 14
:::

## [Oaths and Affidavits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-463934 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taking oaths, etc.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  officer or employee of the Department authorized by the Minister may,
  in the course of their employment and subject to any other Act of
  Parliament or any Act of the legislature of a province, administer
  oaths and take and receive affidavits, declarations and solemn
  affirmations for the purpose of the administration of this Act or the
  regulations, and every person so authorized has, with respect to any
  such oath, affidavit, declaration or affirmation, all the powers of a
  commissioner for taking affidavits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acceptance of oaths, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may accept, for the purpose of the
  administration of this Act or the regulations, any oath administered
  or any affidavit, declaration or solemn affirmation taken or received
  by any officer or employee of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a department in, or other portion of, the federal
    public administration specified in Schedule I, IV or V to the
    [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a department of the government of a province

  who has all the powers of a commissioner for taking affidavits.

- 2000, c. 34, s. 14

## [Bureau of Pensions Advocates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-463945 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bureau continued

- **[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is hereby continued the Bureau
  of Pensions Advocates, consisting of a Chief Pensions Advocate and
  such other pensions advocates, officers and employees as may be
  required for the performance of the duties of the Bureau, and the
  Bureau shall form part of the Department.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications of pensions advocates

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Pensions Advocate and each pensions
  advocate shall be appointed from among persons who are members of the
  bar of a province.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 18, s. 101
- 2000, c. 34, s. 94(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bureau's duties

- **[[6.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It
  is the duty of the Bureau, on request,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to assist applicants and pensioners in the
    preparation of applications for review or of appeals under the
    [Veterans Review and Appeal Board Act](/eng/acts/V-1.6); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to arrange for applicants and pensioners to be
    represented by a pensions advocate at hearings on review or appeals
    under that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bureau to act as solicitor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The relationship between the Bureau and a person
  requesting its assistance is that of solicitor and client, and the
  Bureau shall not be required in any proceedings before the Veterans
  Review and Appeal Board to disclose any information or material in its
  possession relating to any such person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency with other provisions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If there is any inconsistency between subsection (2)
  and any other provision of this Act or any provision of any other
  enactment administered by the Minister, subsection (2) prevails to the
  extent of the inconsistency.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 18, s. 101
- 2000, c. 34, s. 15

## [Annual Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-464006 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall cause to
be laid before each House of Parliament, not later than the fifth
sitting day of that House after January 31 next following the end of
each fiscal year, a report showing the operations of the Department for
