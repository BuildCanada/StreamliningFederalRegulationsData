## First Nations Financial Transparency Act {#first-nations-financial-transparency-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2013, c. 7
:::

Assented to 2013-03-27

An Act to enhance the financial accountability and transparency of First
Nations
::::

:::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224746 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [First Nations Financial Transparency Act](/eng/acts/F-11.66).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224749 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[consolidated financial statements]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[consolidated financial statements]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    financial statements of a First Nation --- prepared in accordance
    with generally accepted accounting principles --- in which the
    assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses and cash flows of the
    First Nation and of those entities that are required by those
    principles to be included are presented as those of a single
    economic entity, as if the First Nation were a government reporting
    on its financial information. ([états financiers
    consolidés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as [[council of
    the band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the [Indian
    Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation or a partnership,
    a joint venture or any other unincorporated association or
    organization. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[expenses]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[expenses]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the costs of
    transportation, accommodation, meals, hospitality and incidental
    expenses. ([dépenses]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a band, as defined in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), but does not
    include a band that is party to a comprehensive self-government
    agreement given effect by an Act of Parliament. ([première
    nation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as [[member of a
    band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the [Indian
    Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([membre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Indigenous
    Services. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[remuneration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[remuneration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any salaries, wages,
    commissions, bonuses, fees, honoraria and dividends and any other
    monetary benefits --- other than the reimbursement of expenses ---
    and non-monetary benefits. ([rémunération]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224760 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of Act

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to enhance the financial accountability and transparency of First
Nations by requiring the preparation and public disclosure of their
audited consolidated financial statements and of the schedules of
remuneration paid and expenses reimbursed to a First Nation's chief and
each of its councillors --- acting in their capacity as such and in any
other capacity, including their personal capacity --- by the First
Nation and by any entity that, in accordance with generally accepted
accounting principles, is required to be consolidated with the First
Nation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act applies in respect
of every First Nation's financial year that begins after the day on
which this Act comes into force.

## [Financial Statements and Schedule of Remuneration and Expenses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224765 .Part}

### [Preparation and Independent Audit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-224766 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accounts and consolidated financial
statements

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A First
  Nation must maintain its accounts and prepare its consolidated
  financial statements annually in accordance with generally accepted
  accounting principles, the primary sources of which are the handbooks
  --- including the handbook respecting public sector accounting --- of
  the Canadian Institute of Chartered Accountants, or its successor, as
  they are amended from time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A First Nation's consolidated financial statements
  must be audited, in accordance with the generally accepted auditing
  standards of the Canadian Institute of Chartered Accountants, or its
  successor, by an independent auditor who is a member in good standing
  of a corporation, institute or association of accountants incorporated
  under an Act of the legislature of a province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Schedule

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A First
  Nation must annually prepare a document entitled "Schedule of
  Remuneration and Expenses" that sets out, separately, the remuneration
  paid and the expenses reimbursed to its chief and each of its
  councillors --- acting in their capacity as such and in any other
  capacity, including their personal capacity --- by the First Nation
  and by any entity that, in accordance with generally accepted
  accounting principles, is required to be consolidated with the First
  Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distinct document

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The schedule does not form part of the consolidated
  financial statements.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An auditor's report or a review engagement report, as
  the case may be, prepared by the auditor referred to in subsection
  5(2), must accompany the schedule.

### [Disclosure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-224779 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies --- members

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A First
  Nation must, on the request of any of its members, provide the member
  with copies of any of the following documents:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its audited consolidated financial statements;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Schedule of Remuneration and Expenses;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the auditor's written report respecting the
    consolidated financial statements; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the auditor's report or the review engagement
    report, as the case may be, respecting the Schedule of Remuneration
    and Expenses.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The First Nation must provide the copies without
  delay, but has until 120 days after the end of the financial year in
  question to provide them if the request is received within those 120
  days.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A First Nation may charge a fee for providing the
  copies, but the fee must not exceed the cost of the service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Internet site --- First Nation

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A First
  Nation must publish the documents referred to in paragraphs 7(1)(a)
  to (d) on its Internet site, or cause those documents to be published
  on an Internet site, within 120 days after the end of each financial
  year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents archived

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The documents referred to in subsection (1) must
  remain accessible to the public, on an Internet site, for at least 10
  years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discharging duty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Publishing any document on an Internet site is
  insufficient to discharge the First Nation's duty to make copies of it
  available to its members who request that document.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Internet site --- Minister

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must publish
the documents referred to in paragraphs 7(1)(a) to (d) on the Internet
site of the Department of Indigenous Services without delay after the
First Nation has provided him or her with those documents or they have
been published under subsection 8(1).

## [Court Remedies and Administrative Measures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224800 .Part}

### [Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-224801 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application by member of First Nation

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** If a First Nation fails
to provide copies of any document under section 7, any member of that
First Nation may apply to a superior court for an order requiring the
council to carry out the duties under that section within the period
specified by the court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application by any person

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** If a First Nation fails
to publish any document under section 8, any person, including the
Minister, may apply to a superior court for an order requiring the
council to carry out the duties under that section within the period
specified by the court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** An application for an
order in respect of documents referred to in paragraphs 7(1)(a) to (d)
for the most recent financial year may only be made after the expiry of
120 days after the end of that financial year.

### [Administrative Measures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-224808 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power --- Minister

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  First Nation is in breach of any duty imposed on it under sections 5
  to 8, the Minister may take one or more of the following measures:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} require the council to develop an appropriate
    action plan to remedy the breach;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} withhold moneys payable as a grant or contribution
    to the First Nation under an agreement that is in force on the day
    on which the breach occurs and that is entered into by the First
    Nation and Her Majesty in right of Canada as represented by the
    Minister, solely or in combination with other ministers of the
    Crown, until the First Nation has complied with its duty; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} terminate any agreement referred to in paragraph
    (b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An amount withheld under paragraph (1)(b) is deemed
  to be an amount that is due or owing for the purposes of section 37.1
  of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).
