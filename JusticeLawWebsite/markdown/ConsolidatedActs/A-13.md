## [Purpose of Foundation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6549 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of Foundation

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of the
Foundation is to develop closer ties between the peoples and
institutions of Canada and the peoples and institutions of the
Asia-Pacific region and to promote capacity development in persons and
entities that share an interest in the Asia-Pacific region and the
building of networks between them by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} promoting mutual awareness and understanding of the
  cultures, histories, religions, philosophies, languages, life styles
  and aspirations in the Asia-Pacific region and Canada and their
  effects on each other's societies;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} promoting dialogue on, and understanding of,
  foreign policy issues as they relate to Canada and the Asia-Pacific
  region;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} supporting development cooperation between
  organizations, institutions and associations in Canada and in the
  Asia-Pacific region;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} promoting collaboration among organizations,
  institutions and associations in private and public sectors in Canada
  and in the Asia-Pacific region;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} promoting closer economic and commercial ties between
  Canada and the Asia-Pacific region;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} promoting, in Canada, scholarship in and expertise on
  economic, cultural, social and other subjects relating to the
  Asia-Pacific region, and in the Asia-Pacific region, scholarship in
  and expertise on economic, cultural, social and other subjects
  relating to Canada; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} collecting information and ideas relating to Canada
  and the Asia-Pacific region and disseminating such information and
  ideas within Canada and the Asia-Pacific region.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. A-13, s. 3
- 2005, c. 30, s. 60

## [Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6561 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Foundation has the
capacity of a natural person and, without restricting the generality of
the foregoing, the Foundation may,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} initiate, finance and administer programs and
  activities relevant to its purpose;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} support, assist and implement programs and activities
  by government, public or private organizations and agencies or by
  individuals for the furtherance of its purpose;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} enter into contracts or agreements with governments,
  public or private organizations and agencies or with individuals;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} publish or otherwise disseminate information of all
  kinds related to its purpose;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} sponsor or support conferences, seminars and other
  meetings related to its purpose;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} establish, maintain and operate information and data
  centres and facilities for research and other activities related to
  its purpose;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} establish and award scholarships or fellowships for
  study related to its purpose;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} give recognition, by such means as it deems
  appropriate, for outstanding contributions to the development of
  Canada's relationship with countries of the Asia-Pacific region;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} acquire and hold real property or any interest
  therein and sell or otherwise dispose of the same;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} acquire any property, money or securities by gift,
  bequest or otherwise, and hold, expend, invest, administer or dispose
  of any such property, money or securities subject to the terms, if
  any, on which the property, money or securities is given, bequeathed
  or otherwise made available to the Foundation;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} expend, for the purpose of the Foundation, such
  moneys as may be appropriated by Parliament or by any other government
  for the activities of the Foundation or received by it through the
  conduct of its operations;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} expend, for the purpose of the Foundation, all
  amounts received by it as grants, contributions and donations of money
  for its activities; and

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} do such other things as are conducive to the
  fulfilment of its purpose and to the exercise of its powers.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. A-13, s. 4
- 2005, c. 30, s. 61

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Board consists of the

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Chairperson and up to four other directors

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation and experience

**[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1 .sectionLabel}** The appointment of
directors is to be made from persons whose background or experience
would assist the Foundation in the fulfilment of its purpose and having
regard to the following considerations:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the need to ensure, as far as possible, that at least
  one half of the membership has experience or expertise concerning
  relations between Canada and the Asia-Pacific region;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the need for a membership that has sufficient
  knowledge of corporate governance, investment management, auditing and
  evaluations; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the importance of having membership that is
  representative of Canadian society.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 30, s. 65

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors without voting rights

**[[16.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16.1 .sectionLabel}** A director who is
part of the federal public administration does not have the right to
vote on any matter before the Board or a committee of the Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 30, ss. 67, 81(E)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of care

**[[16.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16.2 .sectionLabel}** The Chairperson, the
President and every other director of the Foundation, in exercising any
of their powers and in discharging any of their duties, shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} act honestly and in good faith with a view to the
  best interests of the Foundation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} exercise the care, diligence and skill that a
  reasonably prudent person would exercise in comparable circumstances;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} comply with this Act and the by-laws of the
  Foundation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 30, s. 67
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnification

**[[16.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16.3 .sectionLabel}** Except in respect of
an action by or on behalf of the Foundation to procure a judgment in its
favour, the Foundation may indemnify a present or former Chairperson,
President or other director or officer of the Foundation against all
costs, charges and expenses, including amounts paid to settle an action
or to satisfy a judgment, that are reasonably incurred by him or her in
respect of any civil, criminal or administrative action or proceeding to
which he or she is a party by reason of being or having been a director
or officer of the Foundation, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} he or she acted honestly and in good faith with a
  view to the best interests of the Foundation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a criminal or administrative action or
  proceeding enforced by a monetary penalty, he or she had reasonable
  grounds for believing that the impugned conduct was lawful.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 30, s. 67
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

**[[16.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16.4 .sectionLabel}** The Board or a
committee of the Board may not, for the purpose of making a decision
other than a decision to appoint a director under paragraph 9(b), be
comprised of a majority of directors appointed under paragraph 9(a) who
have the right to vote.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 30, s. 67
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation by Board

- **[[16.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16.5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Board may delegate to the
  Chairperson, a committee of directors or an officer of the Foundation
  any of the powers or rights of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrictions on delegation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may not, however, delegate any power or
  right of the Board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to make, amend or repeal by-laws;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to appoint directors to, or fill vacancies on, a

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to appoint officers of the Foundation or fix their
    remuneration;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to accept grants, contributions and donations; or

- 2005, c. 30, s. 67

## [Registered Charity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6753 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foundation deemed registered charity

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 29,
the Foundation shall, for the purposes of the [Income Tax
Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), be deemed to be a registered charity within the
meaning of that expression for the purposes of that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. A-13, s. 28
- 1999, c. 31, s. 246(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ceases to be registered charity

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The Foundation shall, for
all purposes, cease to be deemed to be a registered charity within the
meaning of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) if the aggregate of
amounts for which it has issued receipts that contain prescribed
information pursuant to that Act are not used in accordance with the
provisions of that Act governing expenditures by charitable
organizations.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. A-13, s. 29
- 1999, c. 31, s. 246(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Profits

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Subject to any by-law of
the Foundation providing for the payment of any expenses to the members
of the Board or any remuneration to the officers, employees and agents
of the Foundation, any profits of the Foundation or accretions to the
value of the property of the Foundation shall be used to further the
activities of the Foundation and no part of the property or profits of
the Foundation may be distributed, directly or indirectly, to any member
of the Board or member of the Foundation.

::: HistoricalNote

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grants, contributions and donations

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the
  Foundation may accept grants, contributions and conditional or
  unconditional donations of money from any person, including the
  Government of Canada and the government of a province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of grants, contributions and
  donations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All grants, contributions and donations of money
  received by the Foundation, and any income arising from the investment
  of those grants, contributions and donations, shall be used by the
  Foundation in fulfilling its purpose and in accordance with the terms
  and conditions of any agreement for the provision of funding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditional grants, contributions and
  donations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Foundation may not accept a grant, contribution
  or donation of money that is made on the condition that the Foundation
  use the grant, contribution or donation, or any income arising from
  it, for any purpose that is not consistent with the purpose of the
  Foundation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investment policies

**[[33.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33.1 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall
establish investment policies, standards and procedures that a
reasonably prudent person would apply in respect of a portfolio of
investments to avoid undue risk of loss and obtain a reasonable return,
having regard to the Foundation's obligations and anticipated
obligations.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 30, s. 75
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investments

- **[[33.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any conditions of a
  grant, contribution or donation restricting the investment of money,
  the Foundation shall invest its funds, and reinvest any income from
  those funds, in accordance with the investment policies, standards and
  procedures established by the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation of other corporations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Foundation may not, without the prior written
  authorization of the Minister, cause any corporation to be
  incorporated or participate in the incorporation of a corporation or
  become a partner in a partnership.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 30, s. 75

## [Winding-up]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6789 .Part}

Meaning of [[eligible recipient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[eligible recipient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
  entity that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is established in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} meets the criteria of eligibility established by
    the Foundation in accordance with any agreement entered into between
    Her Majesty in right of Canada and the Foundation for the provision
    of funding by Her Majesty in right of Canada to the Foundation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has legal capacity or is composed of organizations
    each of which has legal capacity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property to be divided

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Foundation is wound up or dissolved

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its property remaining after its debts and
    obligations have been satisfied shall be liquidated;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the moneys arising from the liquidation shall be
    distributed among all the eligible recipients that have received
    funding from the Foundation and that are, as of the day the
    distribution begins, carrying on projects that are consistent with
    the purpose of the Foundation, to be used by them for those or any
    other projects that are so consistent; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} each of those eligible recipients shall receive an
    amount that is the same proportion of the moneys arising from the
    liquidation as the total funding received by that eligible recipient
    from the Foundation is of the total of all funding that has been
    provided by the Foundation to all of those eligible recipients.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repayment out of remaining property

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), the Minister may require the
  Foundation to repay, out of the moneys arising from the liquidation,
  to the Receiver General for credit to the Consolidated Revenue Fund
  any amount that is so repayable under the terms or conditions on which
  public moneys were provided to the Foundation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. A-13, s. 34
- 2005, c. 30, s. 76
:::

## [Audit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6805 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Auditor

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall appoint an auditor for the Foundation and fix the
  auditor's remuneration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications of auditor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In order to be appointed, the auditor must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is a member in good standing of an institute or
      association of accountants incorporated by or under an Act of the
      legislature of a province,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has at least five years experience at a senior
      level in carrying out audits,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is ordinarily resident in Canada, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} is independent of the Board, the directors, the
      officers and, if any, the members of the Foundation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a firm of accountants of which the member or
    employee, jointly designated by the Board and the firm to conduct
    the audit of the books and records of the Foundation on behalf of
    the firm, meets the qualifications set out in paragraph (a).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. A-13, s. 35
- 2005, c. 30, s. 77

## [Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6823 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall, within four months after the end of each fiscal year of
  the Foundation, submit to the Minister a report, in both official
  languages, of the activities of the Foundation for that year. The
  report shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the financial statements of the Foundation for that
    year, prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting
    principles, and the auditor's report on those financial statements;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a detailed statement of its investment activities
    during that year and its investment portfolio as at the end of that
    year;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a detailed statement of its funding activities;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement of its plans for fulfilling its purpose
    for the next fiscal year; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an evaluation of the overall results achieved by
    the Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling in Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause a copy of the report to be
  laid before each House of Parliament on any of the first 15 days on
  which that House is sitting after the Minister receives it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Making report available

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall, as soon as is practicable after it
  is tabled under subsection (2), make the report available for public
  scrutiny.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. A-13, s. 36
- 2005, c. 30, s. 78
