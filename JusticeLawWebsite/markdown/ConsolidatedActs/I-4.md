## Income Tax Conventions Interpretation Act {#income-tax-conventions-interpretation-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. I-4
:::

An Act respecting the interpretation of Canada's international
conventions relating to income tax and the Acts implementing those
conventions
::::

:::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-331548 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Income Tax Conventions Interpretation Act](/eng/acts/I-4).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1984, c. 48, s. 1
:::

## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-331553 .Part}

Definition of [[convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,
[[convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any convention or agreement
between Canada and another state relating to tax on income, and includes
any protocol or supplementary convention or agreement relating thereto.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1984, c. 48, s. 2
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-331558 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of undefined terms

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding the
provisions of a convention or the Act giving the convention the force of
law in Canada, it is hereby declared that the law of Canada is that, to
the extent that a term in the convention is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} not defined in the convention,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} not fully defined in the convention, or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to be defined by reference to the laws of Canada,

that term has, except to the extent that the context otherwise requires,
the meaning it has for the purposes of the [Income Tax
Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), as amended from time to time, and not the meaning
it had for the purposes of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) on the
date the convention was entered into or given the force of law in Canada
if, after that date, its meaning for the purposes of the [Income Tax
Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) has changed.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1984, c. 48, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permanent establishments in Canada

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding the
provisions of a convention or the Act giving the convention the force of
law in Canada, it is hereby declared that the law of Canada is that
where, for the purposes of the application of the convention, the
profits from a business activity, including an industrial or commercial
activity, attributable or allocable to a permanent establishment in
Canada are to be determined for any period,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} there shall, except where the convention expressly
  otherwise provides, be included in the determination of those profits
  all amounts with respect to that activity that are attributable or
  allocable to the permanent establishment and that would be required to
  be included under the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), as amended
  from time to time, by a person resident in Canada carrying on the
  activity in Canada in the computation of his income from a business
  for that period; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} there shall, except to the extent that an agreement
  between the competent authorities of the parties to the convention
  expressly otherwise provides, not be deducted in the determination of
  those profits any amount with respect to that activity that is
  attributable or allocable to the permanent establishment and that
  would not be deductible under the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3),
  as amended from time to time, by a person resident in Canada carrying
  on the activity in Canada in the computation of his income from a
  business for that period.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1984, c. 48, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of section 245 of the [Income
Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3)

**[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding the
provisions of a convention or the Act giving the convention the force of
law in Canada, it is hereby declared that the law of Canada is that
section 245 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) applies to any
benefit provided under the convention.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 19, s. 60
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stock exchanges

**[[4.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.2 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding the
provisions of a convention or the Act giving the convention the force of
law in Canada, each reference in a convention to a stock exchange that
is prescribed under, or for the purposes of, the [Income Tax
Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) shall be read as a reference to a designated stock
exchange, as defined in the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2007, c. 35, s. 70
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of section 94 of the [Income
Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3)

**[[4.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.3 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding the
provisions of a convention or the Act giving the convention the force of
law in Canada, if a trust is deemed by subsection 94(3) of the [Income
Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) to be resident in Canada for a taxation year
for the purposes of computing its income, the trust is deemed to be a
resident of Canada, and not a resident of the other contracting state,
for the purposes of applying the convention

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the trust for that taxation year; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any other person for any period that
  includes all or part of that taxation year.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 34, s. 26
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding the
provisions of a convention or the Act giving the convention the force of
law in Canada, in this section and in the convention,

[[annuity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[annuity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include any pension payment
    or any payment under a plan, arrangement or contract described in
    subparagraphs (a)(i) to (ix) of the definition
    [[pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}; ([rente]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the territory of Canada, and
    includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} every area beyond the territorial seas of Canada
      that, in accordance with international law and the laws of Canada,
      is an area in respect of which Canada may exercise rights with
      respect to the seabed and subsoil and their natural resources, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the seas and airspace above every area described
      in paragraph (a); ([Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[immovable property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[immovable property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[real
    property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to property in Canada,
    are hereby declared to include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any right to explore for or exploit mineral
      deposits and sources in Canada and other natural resources in
      Canada, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any right to an amount computed by reference to
      the production, including profit, from, or to the value of
      production from, mineral deposits and sources in Canada and other
      natural resources in Canada; ([biens immobiliers]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"} et [biens immeubles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of payments that
    arise in Canada,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the convention does not include a definition
      [[pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, a payment under any plan,
      arrangement or contract that is

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a registered pension plan,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a registered retirement savings plan,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a registered retirement income fund,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a retirement compensation arrangement,

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a deferred profit sharing plan,

      - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a plan that is deemed by subsection 147(15) of
        the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) not to be a deferred
        profit sharing plan,

      - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} an annuity contract purchased under a plan
        referred to in subparagraph (v) or (vi),

      - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} an annuity contract where the amount paid by
        or on behalf of an individual to acquire the contract was
        deductible under paragraph 60(l) of the [Income Tax
        Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) in computing the individual's income for
        any taxation year (or would have been so deductible if the
        individual had been resident in Canada), or

      - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} a superannuation, pension or retirement plan
        not otherwise referred to in this paragraph, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the convention includes a definition
      [[pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, a payment that is a pension for
      the purposes of the convention or a payment (other than a payment
      of social security benefits) that would be a periodic pension
      payment if the convention did not include a definition
      [[pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}; ([pension]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[periodic pension payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[periodic pension payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect
    of payments that arise in Canada, a pension payment other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a lump sum payment, or a payment that can
      reasonably be considered to be an instalment of a lump sum amount,
      under a registered pension plan,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a payment before maturity, or a payment in full
      or partial commutation of the retirement income, under a
      registered retirement savings plan,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a payment at any time in a calendar year under a
      registered retirement income fund, where the total of all payments
      (other than the specified portion of each such payment) made under
      the fund at or before that time and in the year exceeds the total
      of

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount that would be the greater of

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} twice the amount that, if the value of C in
          the definition [[minimum amount]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
          subsection 146.3(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3)
          were nil, would be the minimum amount under the fund for the
          year, and

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} 10% of the fair market value of the property
          (other than annuity contracts that, at the beginning of the
          year, are not described in paragraph (b.1) of the definition
          [[qualified investment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection
          146.3(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3)) held in
          connection with the fund at the beginning of the year

        if all property transferred in the year and before that time to
        the carrier of the fund as consideration for the carrier's
        undertaking to make payments under the fund had been so
        transferred immediately before the beginning of the year and if
        the definition [[minimum amount]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
        subsection 146.3(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3)
        applied with respect to all registered retirement income funds,
        and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the total of all amounts each of which is an
        annual or more frequent periodic payment under an annuity
        contract that is a qualified investment, as defined in
        subsection 146.3(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3),
        (other than an annuity contract the fair market value of which
        is taken into account under clause (i)(B)) held by a trust
        governed by the fund that was paid into the trust in the year
        and before that time, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a payment to a recipient at any time in a
      calendar year under an arrangement, other than a plan or fund
      referred to in paragraphs (a) to (c), where

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the payment is not

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} one of a series of annual or more frequent
          payments to be made over the lifetime of the recipient or over
          a period of at least 10 years,

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} one of a series of annual or more frequent
          payments each of which is contingent on the recipient
          continuing to suffer from a physical or mental impairment, or

        - [(C)]{.lawlabel} a payment to which the recipient is entitled
          as a consequence of the death of an individual who was in
          receipt of periodic pension payments under the arrangement,
          and that is made under a guarantee that a minimum number of
          payments will be made in respect of the individual, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at the time the payment is made, it can
        reasonably be concluded that

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the total amount of payments (other than
          excluded payments) under the arrangement to the recipient in
          the year will exceed twice the total amount of payments (other
          than excluded payments) made under the arrangement to the
          recipient in the immediately preceding year, otherwise than
          because of the fact that payments commenced to be made to the
          recipient in the preceding year and were made for a period of
          less than twelve months in that year, or

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the total amount of payments (other than
          excluded payments) under the arrangement to the recipient in
          the year will exceed twice the total amount of payments (other
          than excluded payments) to be made under the arrangement to
          the recipient in any subsequent year, otherwise than because
          of the termination of the series of payments or the reduction
          in the amount of payments to be made after the death of any
          individual,

        and, for the purposes of this subparagraph, [[excluded
        payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a payment that is neither a
        periodic payment nor a payment described in any of clauses
        (i)(A) to (C). ([paiement périodique de
        pension]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-4, s. 5
- 1993, c. 24, s. 147
- 1998, c. 19, s. 286
- 1999, c. 22, s. 84
:::

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1999, c. 22, s.
  85\]]{.Repealed}

- Definition of [[specified portion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of the definition [[periodic pension
  payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 5, the [[specified
  portion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of a payment means the total of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the portion of the payment that is not required by
    section 146.3 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) to be
    included in computing the income of any person and that is not
    included under paragraph 212(1)(q) of that Act in respect of any
    person; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the portion of the payment in respect of which a
    deduction is available under paragraph 60(l) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) in computing the income of any person.

Meaning of [[interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding section 3,
the meaning of the term [[interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in any
convention given the force of law in Canada before November 19, 1974
does not include any amount paid or credited, pursuant to an agreement
in writing entered into before June 23, 1983, as consideration for a
guarantee referred to in paragraph 214(15)(a) of the [Income Tax
Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1984, c. 48, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transitional

**[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** Where a taxation year
of a taxpayer includes June 23, 1983, the additional tax payable under
the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) (except Part XIII thereof) by the
taxpayer for the taxation year by virtue of this Act shall be calculated
in accordance with the following formula:

::: {#331642 .Section}
A = T × (B/C)

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the amount of additional taxes payable under the Income Tax Act
    (except Part XIII thereof) by the taxpayer for the taxation year by
    virtue of this Act,

[T]{.dfn}
:   is the amount of additional taxes payable under the Income Tax Act
    (except Part XIII thereof) by the taxpayer for the taxation year by
    virtue of this Act (except this section),

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the number of days in the taxation year after June 23, 1983, and

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the number of days in the taxation year.
:::

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 48 (1st Supp.), s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Partnerships

**[[6.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.2 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding the
provisions of a convention between Canada and another state or the Act
giving it the force of law in Canada, it is hereby declared that the law
of Canada is that, for the purposes of the application of the convention
and the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) to a person who is a resident
of Canada, a partnership of which that person is a member is neither a
resident nor an enterprise of that other state.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1991, c. 49, s. 220
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Gains arising in Canada

**[[6.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.3 .sectionLabel}** Except where a
convention expressly otherwise provides, any amount of income, gain or
loss in respect of the disposition of a property that is taxable
Canadian property within the meaning assigned by the [Income Tax
Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) is deemed to arise in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1999, c. 22, s. 86
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-331658 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** This Act applies

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of tax under Part XIII of the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), to amounts paid or credited after June 23,
  1983; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, to taxation years ending after
  June 23, 1983.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1984, c. 48, s. 7
:::
