## World Trade Organization Agreement Implementation Act {#world-trade-organization-agreement-implementation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1994, c. 47
:::

Assented to 1994-12-15

An Act to implement the Agreement Establishing the World Trade
Organization
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the Government of Canada together with the other governments and
the European Communities that participated in the Uruguay Round of
Multilateral Trade Negotiations under the General Agreement on Tariffs
and Trade (herein referred to as GATT) have entered into the Agreement
Establishing the World Trade Organization;

WHEREAS free, fair and open trade is essential for the future of the
Canadian economy and for securing the competitiveness and long-term
sustainable development of Canada;

WHEREAS trade expansion contributes to job creation, achieves higher
standards of living, offers greater choices for consumers and
strengthens the Canadian economic union;

WHEREAS the multilateral trading system of mutually agreed upon market
access conditions and non-discriminatory trade rules applicable to all,
is the cornerstone of Canadian trade policy;

WHEREAS the trade agreements achieved under the Uruguay Round of
Multilateral Trade Negotiations under the GATT will lead to a
significantly more open and stable international trading environment for
Canadian agriculture, resources, manufacturing, services, technology and
investment;

WHEREAS the World Trade Organization will provide for integrated
management of the new and strengthened multilateral trading system,
particularly for the resolution of trade disputes;

WHEREAS the World Trade Organization, as successor to the GATT, will
also provide the forum for future trade negotiations aimed at furthering
trade liberalization world-wide and the development of new global trade
rules;

AND WHEREAS it is necessary, in order to give effect to the Agreement,
to make related or consequential amendments to certain Acts;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-465237 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [World Trade Organization Agreement Implementation
Act](/eng/acts/W-11.8).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-465240 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement Establishing
      the World Trade Organization, including

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the agreements set out in Annexes 1A, 1B, 1C, 2
        and 3 to that Agreement, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the agreements set out in Annex 4 to that
        Agreement that have been accepted by Canada,

      all forming an integral part of the Final Act Embodying The
      Results Of The Uruguay Round Of Multilateral Trade Negotiations,
      signed at Marrakesh on April 15, 1994; ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the whole or any portion
      of any Act of Parliament or any regulation, order or other
      instrument issued, made or established in the exercise of a power
      conferred by or under an Act of Parliament; ([texte législatif
      fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of any provision of
      this Act, means the member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada
      designated as the Minister for the purposes of that provision
      under section 9; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[World Trade Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[World Trade Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the World
      Trade Organization established by Article I of the
      Agreement; ([Organisation mondiale du commerce]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[WTO Member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[WTO Member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Member of the World
      Trade Organization. ([membre de l'OMC]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Agreement shall be published in the Canada Treaty
  Series.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-465254 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to implement the Agreement.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-465257 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-465260 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition of private cause of action
under Part I

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person has any cause of
action and no proceedings of any kind shall be taken, without the
consent of the Attorney General of Canada, to enforce or determine any
right or obligation that is claimed or arises solely under or by virtue
of Part I or any order made under Part I.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition of private cause of action
under Agreement

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No person has any cause of
action and no proceedings of any kind shall be taken, without the
consent of the Attorney General of Canada, to enforce or determine any
right or obligation that is claimed or arises solely under or by virtue
of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of Agreement to water

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  greater certainty, nothing in this Act or the Agreement, except the
  Canadian Schedule to the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade 1994
  set out in Annex 1A to the Agreement, applies to water.

- Definition of [[water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
  natural surface and ground water in liquid, gaseous or solid state,
  but does not include water packaged as a beverage or in tanks.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Implementation of Agreement Generally]{.HTitleText1} {#h-465270 .Part}

### [Approval of Agreement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-465271 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement approved

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement is hereby
approved.

### [Designation of Minister]{.HTitleText2} {#h-465274 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order designating Minister

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate any member of the Queen's Privy Council for
Canada to be the Minister for the purposes of any provision of this Act.

### [World Trade Organization]{.HTitleText2} {#h-465277 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial Conference

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may appoint any member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada to be the
representative of Canada in respect of the Ministerial Conference
established under Article IV of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committees and bodies

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may appoint
any person to be the representative of Canada on any committee, council
or body, other than the Ministerial Conference referred to in section
10, that is or may be established under the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of expenditures

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
shall pay its appropriate share of the aggregate of any expenditures
incurred by or on behalf of the World Trade Organization.

### [Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-465284 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders re suspension of concessions

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, for the purpose of suspending in accordance
  with the Agreement the application to a WTO Member of concessions or
  obligations of equivalent effect pursuant to Article 22 of the
  Understanding on Rules and Procedures Governing the Settlement of
  Disputes set out in Annex 2 to the Agreement, by order, do any one or
  more of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend rights or privileges granted by Canada to
    that Member or to goods, service providers, suppliers, investors or
    investments of that Member under the Agreement or any federal law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} modify or suspend the application of any federal
    law with respect to that Member or to goods, service providers,
    suppliers, investors or investments of that Member;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} extend the application of any federal law to that
    Member or to goods, service providers, suppliers, investors or
    investments of that Member; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} take any other measure that the Governor in Council
    considers necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of concessions to non-WTO
  Members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, with respect to a
  country that is not a WTO Member, by order, do any one or more of the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend rights or privileges granted by Canada to
    that country or to goods, service providers, suppliers, investors or
    investments of that country under any federal law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} modify or suspend the application of any federal
    law with respect to that country or to goods, service providers,
    suppliers, investors or investments of that country;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} extend the application of any federal law to that
    country or to goods, service providers, suppliers, investors or
    investments of that country; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} take any other measure that the Governor in Council
    considers necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless repealed, an order made under subsection (1)
  or (2) shall have effect for such period as is specified in the order.

- Definition of [[country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  includes any state or separate customs territory that may, under the
  Agreement, become a WTO Member.
