## Civil Lunar Gateway Agreement Implementation Act {#civil-lunar-gateway-agreement-implementation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2022, c. 10, s. 294
:::

Assented to 2022-06-23

An Act to implement the Memorandum of Understanding between the
Government of Canada and the Government of the United States of America
concerning Cooperation on the Civil Lunar Gateway and to make related
amendments to other Acts

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 294 of chapter 10 of the Statutes of Canada, 2022,
in force October 26, 2022, see SI/2022-51.\]
:::
:::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1365587 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Civil Lunar Gateway Agreement Implementation
Act](/eng/acts/C-31.4).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1365590 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Memorandum of
    Understanding between the Government of Canada and the Government of
    the United States of America concerning Cooperation on the Civil
    Lunar Gateway, entered into on December 15, 2020, as amended from
    time to time under article 22 of the
    Agreement. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of any provision of
    this Act, means the member or members of the Queen's Privy Council
    for Canada designated as the Minister or Ministers for the purpose
    of that provision. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1365595 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to fulfil Canada's obligations under the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order designating Minister

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate one or more members of the Queen's Privy
Council for Canada as the Minister or Ministers for the purpose of any
provision of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation of powers

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may delegate
any powers, duties and functions conferred on the Minister by or under
this Act to one or more persons who are to exercise those powers and
perform those duties and functions, subject to any terms and conditions
that the Minister specifies.

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1365604 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to order production

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by order, require any person that the Minister believes,
  on reasonable grounds, has information or documents relevant to the
  administration or enforcement of this Act, to provide that information
  or those documents to the Minister or any person that the Minister
  designates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person to whom an order under subsection (1) is
  directed must provide the information or documents that are required
  by the order in the time and manner specified in it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of [Statutory
  Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply to an order issued under subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for a person who obtained information or a document under
  this Act or the Agreement that is subject to a claim that it is
  confidential to communicate it, allow its recommunication or allow any
  person to have access to it without the written consent of the person
  who provided it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a person may communicate or
  allow any person to have access to information or a document that has
  been provided under this Act or the Agreement and that is subject to a
  claim that it is confidential if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the public interest in the communication or access
    in relation to public health or public safety outweighs in
    importance any financial loss or prejudice to the competitive
    position of any person or any harm to the privacy interests,
    reputation or human dignity of any individual likely to be caused by
    that communication or access; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the communication or access is necessary for the
    purpose of the administration or enforcement of this Act or any
    other Act of Parliament or of giving effect to the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compelled production

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite any other Act or law, a person is not to be
  compelled to give or produce evidence relating to information or a
  document that has been provided under this Act or the Agreement and
  that is subject to a claim that it is confidential, unless the
  proceeding in which the evidence is sought to be compelled relates to
  the enforcement of this Act or another Act of Parliament.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Goods and data

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Despite any other Act or
law, any person who receives goods or data referred to in Article 19.4
of the Agreement must, upon completion of the activities to which they
relate, destroy or return them in accordance with the instructions of
the party that provided them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance order

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister believes on reasonable grounds that a person who has received
  information or documents under the Agreement is contravening, or is
  likely to contravene, section 8 or 9, the Minister may, by order,
  require the person to return the information or the documents in
  question to the person who provided them or to dispose of them in the
  manner the Minister deems appropriate in the circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person to whom an order under subsection (1) is
  directed must return the information or documents, or dispose of them,
  in the time and manner specified in the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of [Statutory
  Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply to an order issued under subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 7, 8 and 10, information and documents are deemed to include
goods or data referred to in Article 19.4 of the Agreement.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1365632 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations that the Governor in Council considers necessary
for carrying out the purposes of this Act or giving effect to the
Agreement, including the code of conduct, memorandums of understanding
and other implementing arrangements that the Agreement refers to.
