## An Act to give effect to the Westbank First Nation Self Government Agreement {#an-act-to-give-effect-to-the-westbank-first-nation-self-government-agreement .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2004, c. 17
:::

Assented to 2004-05-06

An Act to give effect to the Westbank First Nation Self Government
Agreement
::::

::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the Government of Canada has undertaken to recommend to
Parliament the enactment of legislation to give effect to the Westbank
First Nation Self Government Agreement;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467703 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Westbank First Nation Self-Government Act](/eng/acts/W-6.2).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467706 .Part}

Definition of [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act, [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Westbank First Nation
  Self-Government Agreement signed on behalf of the Westbank First
  Nation and Her Majesty in right of Canada on October 3, 2003 and laid
  before the Senate and the House of Commons on November 5, 2003, and
  includes any amendments made to it from time to time in accordance
  with its provisions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expressions used in Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Act, the expressions
  [[council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
  [[Westbank First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[Westbank
  lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[Westbank law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  have the same meaning as in the Agreement.

## [Effect of Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467712 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Force of law of Agreement

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement is approved and has the force of law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons and bodies subject to Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Persons and bodies have the powers, rights,
  privileges and benefits conferred on them by the Agreement and are
  subject to the obligations and liabilities imposed on them by the
  Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement binding

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement is binding on and may be relied on by all persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}International obligations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The provisions of the Agreement respecting Canada's
  international legal obligations may be invoked only by a party to the
  Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflicts with other federal laws

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In the event of a conflict
between this Act or the Agreement and any other federal law, this Act or
the Agreement prevails to the extent of the conflict.

## [Application of Other Acts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467725 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5) does not apply in respect of the Westbank First
Nation, its council, its members or Westbank lands except to the extent
provided by the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acts respecting reserve lands

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The [Framework Agreement on
First Nation Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46) and the [Indian Oil
and Gas Act](/eng/acts/I-7) do not apply in respect of the Westbank
First Nation, its council, its members or Westbank lands.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[First Nations Fiscal Management
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.67)

**[[8.1 ]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1_ .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding any

Westbank First Nation to benefit from the provisions of the [First
Nations Fiscal Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.67) or obtain the services
of any body established under that Act, the Governor in Council may make
any regulations that the Governor in Council considers necessary,
including regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} adapting any provision of that Act or of any

## [Legal Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467738 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Judicial notice shall be
taken of the Agreement and Westbank law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial review

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Application for judicial
review in accordance with the Agreement may only be made after all
applicable procedures for appeal or review provided by Westbank law have
been exhausted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7)

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Neither the council nor
any person or body appointed by the Westbank First Nation and having,
exercising or purporting to exercise jurisdiction or powers conferred by
or under Westbank law is a federal board, commission or other tribunal
within the meaning of the [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of issues arising

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party in any proceeding before a court or tribunal shall serve notice
  in writing on the Attorney General of Canada and the Westbank First
  Nation of any issue raised by that party in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the interpretation or validity of the Agreement; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the validity or applicability of this Act or of
    Westbank law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice must identify the proceeding in which the
  issue arises, state whether it arises in relation to paragraph (1)(a)
  or (b), give particulars of the point to be argued and, if a date has
  been fixed for argument, give the date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attachments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice shall be accompanied by copies of all
  pleadings and other documents pertaining to the issue that are in the
  possession of the court or tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of service

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A notice shall be served within seven days after the
  issue is first raised by a party to the proceeding, whether in the
  initial pleadings or otherwise, and the issue may not be argued sooner
  than fourteen days after service unless the court or tribunal allows a
  shorter period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation in proceedings

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In any proceeding to which subsection (1) applies,
  the Attorney General of Canada and the Westbank First Nation may
  appear and participate with the same rights as any other party.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467758 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations and orders

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, on the recommendation of the Minister of Crown-Indigenous Relations
made after consideration of any representations of the council, make any
regulations or orders that the Governor in Council considers necessary
or advisable for the purpose of carrying out the provisions of the
Agreement.

References to [[Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** References in the
Agreement to [[Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, other than as a place,
shall be read as references to Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Canada Lands Surveys Act](/eng/acts/L-6)

- **[[14.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 29(3)
  of the [Canada Lands Surveys Act](/eng/acts/L-6), in relation to
  Westbank lands, surveys and plans must be satisfactory to the council
  rather than to the Minister of Crown-Indigenous Relations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in relation to
  boundaries between Westbank lands and other lands.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deposit of Agreement and amendments

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Indian
Affairs and Northern Development shall have a copy of the Agreement and
of every amendment made to the Agreement, certified by the Minister to
be a true copy, deposited in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Library and Archives of Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the library of the Department of Indian Affairs and
  Northern Development situated in the National Capital Region; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the office of that Department situated nearest to
  Westbank lands.
