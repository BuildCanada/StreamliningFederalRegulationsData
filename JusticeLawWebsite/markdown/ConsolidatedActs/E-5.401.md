## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-215132 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Employment Equity Act](/eng/acts/E-5.401).

## [Purpose of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-215135 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of Act

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to achieve equality in the workplace so that no person shall be denied
employment opportunities or benefits for reasons unrelated to ability
and, in the fulfilment of that goal, to correct the conditions of
disadvantage in employment experienced by women, Aboriginal peoples,
persons with disabilities and members of visible minorities by giving
effect to the principle that employment equity means more than treating
persons in the same way but also requires special measures and the
accommodation of differences.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-215166 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This Act
  applies to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} private sector employers;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the portions of the federal public administration
    set out in Schedule I or IV to the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the portions of the federal public administration
    set out in of Schedule V to the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11) that employ one hundred or more employees; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} such other portion of the public sector employing
    one hundred or more employees, including the Canadian Forces and the
    Royal Canadian Mounted Police, as may be specified by order of the
    Governor in Council on the recommendation of the Treasury Board, in
    consultation with the minister responsible for the specified
    portion.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Royal Canadian Mounted Police

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Royal Canadian Mounted Police is deemed to
    consist only of its members within the meaning of subsection 2(1) of
    the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Royal Canadian Mounted Police is deemed not to
    be included in Schedule IV to the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} civilian employees appointed or employed in
    accordance with section 10 of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
    Act](/eng/acts/R-10) are deemed to be included in Schedule IV to the
    [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian Forces and Royal Canadian
  Mounted Police

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Members of the Canadian Forces and the Royal Canadian
  Mounted Police are deemed to be employees for the purposes of this
  Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibilities of Treasury Board and
  Public Service Commission

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Treasury Board and the Public Service Commission,
  each acting within the scope of its powers, duties and functions under
  the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) and the [Public
  Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), are responsible for
  carrying out the obligations of an employer under this Act in relation
  to employees employed in those portions of the federal public
  administration referred to in paragraph (1)(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed employer

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every portion of the public sector referred to in
  paragraphs (1)(c) and (d) is deemed to be an employer for the purposes
  of this Act in relation to employees employed in that portion except
  that, with respect to any of those portions for which the Public
  Service Commission exercises any power or performs any function under
  the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), the Public
  Service Commission and that portion are responsible for carrying out
  the obligations of an employer under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References to employer

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} In this Act, a reference to an employer is deemed, in
  relation to those portions of the public sector referred to in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} paragraph (1)(b), to be a reference to the Treasury
    Board and the Public Service Commission, each acting within the
    scope of its powers and functions under the [Financial
    Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) and the [Public Service
    Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} paragraphs (1)(c) and (d) for which the Public
    Service Commission exercises any power or performs any function
    under the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), to be
    a reference to the employer and the Public Service Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation by Treasury Board and Public
  Service Commission

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Treasury Board and the Public Service Commission
  may, for the purpose of carrying out their obligations under this Act
  in relation to a portion of the federal public administration or other
  portion of the public sector referred to in subsection (1), authorize
  the chief executive officer or deputy head concerned to exercise, in
  relation to that portion, any of the powers and perform any of the
  functions of the Treasury Board or the Public Service Commission, as
  the case may be, referred to in this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation by chief executive officer
  or deputy head

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Any chief executive officer or deputy head authorized
  under subsection (7) to exercise any of the powers and perform any of
  the duties and functions of the Treasury Board or Public Service
  Commission may, subject to and in accordance with the authorization
  given to that officer or deputy head, authorize one or more persons to
  exercise any of those powers and perform any of those duties and
  functions.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Employment Equity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-215195 .Part}

### [Employer Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-215196 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employer's duty

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every employer shall
implement employment equity by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} identifying and eliminating employment barriers
  against persons in designated groups that result from the employer's
  employment systems, policies and practices that are not authorized by
  law; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} instituting such positive policies and practices and
  making such reasonable accommodations as will ensure that persons in
  designated groups achieve a degree of representation in each
  occupational group in the employer's workforce that reflects their
  representation in

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian workforce, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} those segments of the Canadian workforce that are
    identifiable by qualification, eligibility or geography and from
    which the employer may reasonably be expected to draw employees.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employer not required to take certain
measures

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The obligation to implement
employment equity does not require an employer

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to take a particular measure to implement employment
  equity where the taking of that measure would cause undue hardship to
  the employer;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to hire or promote persons who do not meet the
  essential qualifications for the work to be performed;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the public sector, to hire or promote
  persons without basing the hiring or promotion on merit in cases where
  the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) requires that
  hiring or promotion be based on merit; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to create new positions in its workforce.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employment of Aboriginal peoples

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding any other
provision of this Act, where a private sector employer is engaged
primarily in promoting or serving the interests of Aboriginal peoples,
the employer may give preference in employment to Aboriginal peoples or
employ only Aboriginal peoples, unless that preference or employment
would constitute a discriminatory practice under the [Canadian Human
Rights Act](/eng/acts/H-6).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain rights not employment barriers

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Employee
  seniority rights with respect to a layoff or recall under a collective
  agreement or pursuant to the established practices of an employer are
  deemed not to be employment barriers within the meaning of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other seniority rights

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless they are found to constitute a discriminatory
  practice under the [Canadian Human Rights Act](/eng/acts/H-6),
  employee seniority rights other than those referred to in subsection
  (1), including rights acquired under workforce adjustment policies
  implemented when an employer is downsizing or restructuring, under a
  collective agreement or pursuant to an established practice, are
  deemed not to be employment barriers within the meaning of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adverse impact on employment
  opportunities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsections (1) and (2), where, after
  a review under paragraph 9(1)(b), it appears that a right referred to
  in either of those subsections that is provided for under a collective
  agreement may have an adverse impact on the employment opportunities
  of persons in designated groups, the employer and its employees'
  representatives shall consult with each other concerning measures that
  may be taken to minimize the adverse impact.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public sector

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The following are not, in relation to the public
  sector, employment barriers within the meaning of the Act, namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} priorities for appointment under the [Public
    Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) or regulations made by
    the Public Service Commission; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} workforce adjustment measures established by the
    Treasury Board, including measures set out in agreements relating to
    workforce adjustment, or by the Public Service Commission or any
    other portion of the public sector referred to in paragraphs 4(1)(c)
    and (d).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Analysis and review

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of implementing employment equity, every employer shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} collect information and conduct an analysis of the
    employer's workforce, in accordance with the regulations, in order
    to determine the degree of the underrepresentation of persons in
    designated groups in each occupational group in that workforce; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} conduct a review of the employer's employment
    systems, policies and practices, in accordance with the regulations,
    in order to identify employment barriers against persons in
    designated groups that result from those systems, policies and
    practices.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Self-identification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Only those employees who identify themselves to an
  employer, or agree to be identified by an employer, as Aboriginal
  peoples, members of visible minorities or persons with disabilities
  are to be counted as members of those designated groups for the
  purposes of implementing employment equity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality of information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Information collected by an employer under paragraph
  (1)(a) is confidential and shall be used only for the purpose of
  implementing the employer's obligations under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employment equity plan

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  employer shall prepare an employment equity plan that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} specifies the positive policies and practices that
    are to be instituted by the employer in the short term for the
    hiring, training, promotion and retention of persons in designated
    groups and for the making of reasonable accommodations for those
    persons, to correct the underrepresentation of those persons
    identified by the analysis under paragraph 9(1)(a);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specifies the measures to be taken by the employer
    in the short term for the elimination of any employment barriers
    identified by the review under paragraph 9(1)(b);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} establishes a timetable for the implementation of
    the matters referred to in paragraphs (a) and (b);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where underrepresentation has been identified by
    the analysis, establishes short term numerical goals for the hiring
    and promotion of persons in designated groups in order to increase
    their representation in each occupational group in the workforce in
    which underrepresentation has been identified and sets out measures
    to be taken in each year to meet those goals;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} sets out the employer's longer term goals for
    increasing the representation of persons in designated groups in the
    employer's workforce and the employer's strategy for achieving those
    goals; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} provides for any other matter that may be
    prescribed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of numerical goals

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In establishing the short term numerical goals
  referred to in paragraph (1)(d), every employer shall consider

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the degree of underrepresentation of persons in
    each designated group in each occupational group within the
    employer's workforce;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the availability of qualified persons in designated
    groups within the employer's workforce and in the Canadian
    workforce;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated growth or reduction of the
    employer's workforce during the period in respect of which the
    numerical goals apply;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated turnover of employees within the
    employer's workforce during the period in respect of which the
    numerical goals apply; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other factor that may be prescribed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section, "short term" means a period of not
  less than one year and not more than three years, and "longer term"
  means a period of more than three years.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasonable progress

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every employer shall
ensure that its employment equity plan would, if implemented, constitute
reasonable progress toward implementing employment equity as required by
this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation and monitoring of plan

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Every employer shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} make all reasonable efforts to implement its
  employment equity plan; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} monitor implementation of its plan on a regular basis
  to assess whether reasonable progress toward implementing employment
  equity is being made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Periodic review and revision of plan

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Every employer shall, at
least once during the period in respect of which the short term
numerical goals referred to in paragraph 10(1)(d) are established,
review its employment equity plan and revise it by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} updating the numerical goals, taking into account the
  factors referred to in subsection 10(2); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} making any other changes that are necessary as a
  result of an assessment made pursuant to paragraph 12(b) or as a
  result of changing circumstances.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information about employment equity

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Every employer shall
provide information to its employees explaining the purpose of
employment equity and shall keep its employees informed about measures
the employer has undertaken or is planning to undertake to implement
employment equity and the progress the employer has made in implementing
employment equity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation with employee
representatives

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  employer shall consult with its employees' representatives by inviting
  the representatives to provide their views concerning

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the assistance that the representatives could
    provide to the employer to facilitate the implementation of
    employment equity in its workplace and the communication to its
    employees of matters relating to employment equity; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the preparation, implementation and revision of the
    employer's employment equity plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where employees represented by
  bargaining agents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where employees are represented by a bargaining
  agent, the bargaining agent shall participate in a consultation under
  subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collaboration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every employer and its employees' representatives
  shall collaborate in the preparation, implementation and revision of
  the employer's employment equity plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rule of interpretation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Consultation under subsection (1) and collaboration
  under subsection (3) are not forms of co-management.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New employers

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who becomes an employer after the day on which this section
  comes into force shall, within eighteen months after becoming an
  employer, comply with sections 9 and 10.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance audit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may not conduct a compliance audit of
  the discharge of the obligations of a person referred to in
  subsection (1) within two years after the day on which that person
  becomes an employer.

### [Records and Reports]{.HTitleText2} {#h-215285 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employment equity records

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Every employer shall, in
accordance with the regulations, establish and maintain employment
equity records in respect of the employer's workforce, the employer's
employment equity plan and the implementation of employment equity by
the employer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports of private sector employers

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  private sector employer shall, on or before June 1 in each year, file
  with the Minister a report in respect of the immediately preceding
  calendar year containing information in accordance with prescribed
  instructions, indicating, in the prescribed manner and form,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the industrial sector in which its employees are
    employed, the location of the employer and its employees, the number
    of its employees and the number of those employees who are members
    of designated groups;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the occupational groups in which its employees are
    employed and the degree of representation of persons who are members
    of designated groups in each occupational group;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the salary ranges of its employees and the degree
    of representation of persons who are members of designated groups in
    each range and in each prescribed subdivision of the range and any
    other information in relation to the salary of its employees that
    may be prescribed; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number of its employees hired, promoted and
    terminated and the degree of representation in those numbers of
    persons who are members of designated groups.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), an employer is
  the person who or organization that was the employer on December 31 in
  the immediately preceding year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic filing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An employer may file a report using electronic media
  in a manner specified in writing by the Minister and, in such a case,
  the report is deemed to have been filed on the day that the Minister
  acknowledges receipt of it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Self-identification

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Only those employees who identify themselves to their
  employer, or agree to be identified by their employer, as Aboriginal
  peoples, members of visible minorities and persons with disabilities
  are to be counted as members of those designated groups for the
  purposes of the report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate required

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A report shall be certified, in the prescribed
  manner, as to the accuracy of the information contained in it and
  shall be signed by the employer or, where the employer is a
  corporation, by a prescribed person on behalf of the corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} An employer shall include in a report a description
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the measures taken by the employer during the
    reporting period to implement employment equity and the results
    achieved; and

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Compliance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-215365 .Part}

### [Compliance Audits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-215366 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance audits

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission is responsible for the enforcement of the obligations
  imposed on employers by sections 5, 9 to 15 and 17.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Guiding policy

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall, in discharging its
  responsibility under subsection (1), be guided by the policy that,
  wherever possible, cases of non-compliance be resolved through
  persuasion and the negotiation of written undertakings pursuant to
  subsection 25(1) and that directions be issued under subsection 25(2)
  or (3) and applications for orders be made under subsection 27(2) only
  as a last resort.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance officers designated

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may designate any person or category
  of persons as employment equity compliance review officers for the
  purposes of conducting compliance audits of employers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where compliance officer may not act

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person who has been designated as an investigator
  under section 43 of the [Canadian Human Rights Act](/eng/acts/H-6) to
  investigate a complaint under that Act in respect of an employer may,
  during the investigation, conduct a compliance audit of that employer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation by Commission

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may authorize any officer or employee
  of the Commission whom the Commission considers appropriate to
  exercise any power and perform any duty or function of the Commission
  under this Act and any power so exercised and any duty or function so
  performed shall be deemed to have been exercised or performed by the
  Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of compliance officers

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of ensuring compliance with the provisions referred to in
  subsection 22(1), a compliance officer may conduct a compliance audit
  of an employer and, for that purpose, may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at any reasonable time, enter any place in which
    the officer believes on reasonable grounds there is any thing
    relevant to the enforcement of any of those provisions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require any person to produce for examination or
    copying any record, book of account or other document that the
    officer believes on reasonable grounds contains information that is
    relevant to the enforcement of any of those provisions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Data processing systems and copying
  equipment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In conducting a compliance audit, a compliance
  officer may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} reproduce or cause to be reproduced any record from
    a data processing system in the form of a print-out or other
    intelligible output and remove the print-out or other output for
    examination and copying; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any copying equipment at
    the place to make copies of any record, book of account or other
    document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate to be produced

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Compliance officers shall be furnished with
  certificates in a form established by the Commission certifying their
  designation as compliance officers and, on entering a place under
  paragraph (1)(a), a compliance officer shall show the certificate to
  the person in charge of the place if the person requests proof of the
  officer's designation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to compliance officers

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The person in charge of a place entered pursuant to
  paragraph (1)(a) and every person found in the place shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} give the compliance officer all reasonable
    assistance to enable the officer to exercise the powers conferred on
    compliance officers by this section; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the officer with any information relevant
    to the enforcement of this Act that the officer may reasonably
    require.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security requirements

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Every compliance officer
or any other person acting on behalf of or under the direction of the
Commission who receives or obtains information relating to a compliance
audit under this Act shall, with respect to access to and use of that
information by that compliance officer or person, satisfy any security
requirements applicable to, and take any oath of secrecy required to be
taken by, persons who normally have access to and use of that
information.

### [Undertakings and Directions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-215395 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employer undertaking

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a compliance officer is of the opinion that an employer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has not collected information or conducted an
    analysis referred to in paragraph 9(1)(a) or conducted a review
    referred to in paragraph 9(1)(b),

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has not prepared an employment equity plan referred
    to in section 10,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has prepared an employment equity plan that does
    not meet the requirements of sections 10 and 11,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} has not made all reasonable efforts to implement
    its employment equity plan in accordance with section 12,

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} has failed to review and revise its employment
    equity plan in accordance with section 13,

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} has failed to provide information to its employees
    in accordance with section 14,

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} has failed to consult with its employees'
    representatives in accordance with section 15, or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} has failed to establish and maintain employment
    equity records as required by section 17,

  the compliance officer shall inform the employer of the non-compliance
  and shall attempt to negotiate a written undertaking from the employer
  to take specified measures to remedy the non-compliance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information re underrepresentation

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an employer has been informed of a non-compliance
    by a compliance officer under subsection (1) and the finding of
    non-compliance is based, in whole or in part, on the apparent
    underrepresentation of Aboriginal peoples, members of visible
    minorities or persons with disabilities in the employer's work
    force, as reflected in the employer's work force analysis conducted
    pursuant to paragraph 9(1)(a), and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the employer believes that the apparent
    underrepresentation is attributable to the decision of employees who
    may be members of the designated groups concerned not to identify
    themselves as such or not to agree to be identified by the employer
    as such under subsection 9(2),

  the employer may inform the compliance officer of such belief.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reason for underrepresentation to be
  considered

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} Where the employer satisfies the compliance officer
  that the finding of non-compliance is attributable, in whole or in
  part, to the reason described in paragraph (1.1)(b) and that the
  employer has made all reasonable efforts to implement employment
  equity, the compliance officer shall take the reason into account in
  exercising any powers under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No employer identification of
  individual employees

  [(1.3)]{.lawlabel} In satisfying the compliance officer under
  subsection (1.2) that the finding of non-compliance is attributable,
  in whole or in part, to the reason mentioned in paragraph (1.1)(b),
  the employer must do so by means other than the identification of
  individual employees in its work force that the employer believes are
  members of designated groups who have not identified themselves as
  such, or agreed to be identified by the employer as such, under
  subsection 9(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a compliance officer fails to obtain a written
  undertaking that, in the opinion of the compliance officer, would be
  sufficient to remedy the non-compliance, the compliance officer shall
  notify the Commission of the non-compliance and the Commission may
  issue and send, by registered mail, a direction to the employer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} setting out the facts on which the officer's
    finding of non-compliance is based; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} requiring the employer to take such actions as are
    specified in the direction to remedy the non-compliance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Breach of undertaking

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a compliance officer obtains a written
  undertaking and the compliance officer is of the opinion that the
  employer has breached the undertaking, the compliance officer shall
  notify the Commission of the non-compliance and the Commission may
  issue and send, by registered mail, a direction to the employer
  requiring the employer to take such actions as are specified in the
  direction to remedy the non-compliance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of direction

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may rescind or amend a direction
  issued by the Commission pursuant to subsection (2) or (3) on the
  presentation of new facts or on being satisfied that the direction was
  issued without knowledge of, or was based on a mistake as to, a
  material fact.

### [Limitations respecting Directions and Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-215505 .Subheading}

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the public sector, require an
    employer to hire or promote persons without basing the hiring or
    promotion on merit in cases where the [Public Service Employment
    Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) requires that hiring or promotion be based
    on merit, or impose on the Public Service Commission an obligation
    to exercise its discretion regarding exclusion orders or
    regulations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} require an employer to create new positions in its
    workforce;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} impose a quota on an employer; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a direction or order respecting the
    establishment of short term numerical goals, fail to take into
    account the factors set out in subsection 10(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of
  [[quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In paragraph (1)(e), [[quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means a requirement to hire or promote a fixed and arbitrary number of
  persons during a given period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public sector

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In making a direction or order that applies to the
  public sector, the Commission, in the case of a direction, and a
  Tribunal, in the case of an order, shall take into account the
  respective roles and responsibilities of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Public Service Commission and the Treasury
    Board under the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)
    and the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a portion of the public sector referred to in
    paragraph 4(1)(c) or (d) under any other Act of Parliament.

### [Privileged Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-215523 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privileged information

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Information obtained by the
  Commission under this Act is privileged and shall not knowingly be, or
  be permitted to be, communicated, disclosed or made available without
  the written consent of the person from whom it was obtained.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence and production of documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No member of the Commission or person employed by it
  who obtains information that is privileged under subsection (1) shall
  be required, in connection with any legal proceedings, other than
  proceedings relating to the administration or enforcement of this Act,
  to give evidence relating to that information or to produce any
  statement or other writing containing that information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication or disclosure of
  information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Information that is privileged under subsection (1)
  may, on any terms and conditions that the Commission considers
  appropriate, be communicated or disclosed to a minister of the Crown
  in right of Canada or to any officer or employee of Her Majesty in
  right of Canada for any purpose relating to the administration or
  enforcement of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section prohibits the communication
  or disclosure of information for the purposes of legal proceedings
  relating to the administration or enforcement of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employer's consent required

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} No information obtained by the Commission or a
  Tribunal under this Act may be used in any proceedings under any other
  Act without the consent of the employer concerned.

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Assessment of Monetary Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-215535 .Part}

### [Violations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-215536 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violation

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  private sector employer commits a violation of this Act who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} without reasonable excuse, fails to file an
    employment equity report as required by section 18;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} without reasonable excuse, fails to include in the
    employment equity report any information that is required, by
    section 18 and the regulations, to be included; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provides any information in the employment equity
    report that the employer knows to be false or misleading.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing violations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A violation that is committed or continued on more
  than one day constitutes a separate violation for each day on which it
  is committed or continued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violations not offences

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A violation is not an offence and accordingly the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) does not apply in respect of a
  violation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment of monetary penalty

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, within two years after the day on which the Minister
  becomes aware of a violation, issue a notice of assessment of a
  monetary penalty in respect of the violation and send it by registered
  mail to the private sector employer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of a monetary penalty shall not exceed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$10,000 for a single violation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$50,000 for repeated or continued violations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to be considered

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In assessing the amount of a monetary penalty, the
  Minister shall take into account

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature, circumstances, extent and gravity of
    the violation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the wilfulness or intent of the private sector
    employer and the employer's history of prior violations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of assessment of monetary penalty

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** A notice of the
assessment of a monetary penalty shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} identify the alleged violation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} specify the amount of the monetary penalty; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} specify the place where the employer may pay the
  monetary penalty.

### [Options]{.HTitleText2} {#h-215563 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employer's options

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  employer may, not later than thirty days after receiving a notice of
  assessment of a monetary penalty,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} comply with the notice; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contest the assessment of the monetary penalty by
    making a written application to the Minister for a review, by a
    Tribunal, of that assessment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister receives a written application, the
  Minister shall send a copy of it to the Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of notice of assessment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an employer who is issued a notice of assessment
  of a monetary penalty fails to exercise one of the options set out in
  subsection (1) within the period referred to in that subsection, the
  Minister shall send a copy of the notice to the Chairperson.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 44, s. 38
- 1998, c. 9, s. 40
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review by Tribunal

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of a copy of a written application or a copy of a notice of
  assessment, the Chairperson shall establish a Tribunal consisting of
  one member selected from the Canadian Human Rights Tribunal to review
  the assessment and shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} send, by registered mail, a request that the
    employer appear before the Tribunal at the time and place set out in
    the request to hear the allegations against the employer in respect
    of the alleged violation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in writing, advise the Minister who issued the
    notice of assessment of the time and place set out in the request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to appear before Tribunal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an employer to whom a request is sent fails to
  appear before a Tribunal at the time and place set out in the request,
  the Tribunal shall consider all the information that is presented to
  it by the Minister in relation to the alleged violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opportunity to make representations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In conducting its review, a Tribunal shall provide
  the Minister and the employer with a full opportunity consistent with
  procedural fairness and natural justice to present evidence and make
  representations to it with respect to the alleged violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of Tribunal

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where at the conclusion of its proceedings a Tribunal
  determines that the employer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has not committed the alleged violation, the
    Tribunal shall immediately inform the employer and the Minister of
    its determination and no further proceedings shall be taken against
    the employer in respect of the alleged violation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has committed the alleged violation, the Tribunal
    shall immediately

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} issue to the Minister a certificate, in the
      prescribed form, of its determination that sets out an amount, not
      exceeding the applicable amount set out in subsection 36(2),
      determined by the Tribunal to be payable by the employer in
      respect of the violation, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} send a copy of the certificate to the employer
      by registered mail.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to be considered

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In determining an amount under subparagraph
  (4)(b)(i), a Tribunal shall take into account the factors set out in
  subsection 36(3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Burden of proof

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} In proceedings under this section, the Minister has
  the burden of proving, on a balance of probabilities, that an employer
  has committed the alleged violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A certificate that purports to have been issued by a
  Tribunal under subparagraph (4)(b)(i) is evidence of the facts stated
  in the certificate, without proof of the signature or official
  character of the person appearing to have signed the certificate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determinations are final

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} A determination of a Tribunal under this section is
  final and, except for judicial review under the [Federal Courts
  Act](/eng/acts/F-7), is not subject to appeal or review by any court.

### [Enforcement of Monetary Penalties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-215600 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration of certificate

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

  the Federal Court and when registered has the same force and effect,
  and all proceedings may be taken on the certificate, as if the
  certificate were a judgment in that Court obtained by Her Majesty in

## [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-215606 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} defining, for the purposes of the Act, the
    expressions "employee", "hired", "occupational group", "promoted",
    "salary" and "terminated";

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the manner of calculating the number of
    employees employed by an employer for the purpose of determining
    when an employer is considered to employ one hundred or more
    employees;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} governing the collection of information and the
    conduct of analyses referred to in paragraph 9(1)(a) and the conduct
    of reviews referred to in paragraph 9(1)(b);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} governing the establishment and maintenance of
    employment equity records referred to in section 17;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that is to be prescribed by
    this Act; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} generally, for carrying out the purposes and
    provisions of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made pursuant to subsection (1) may be
  of general application or may apply to a particular employer or group
  of employers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where regulations apply to public
  sector

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No regulation may be made under subsection (1) that
  applies to the public sector without prior consultation with the
  Treasury Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistent meanings

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No expression defined pursuant to paragraph (1)(a)
  that applies to the public sector shall be given a meaning that is
  inconsistent with the meaning that that expression or any similar
  expression is given under the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptation of Act to certain portions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, taking into account the
  operational effectiveness of the appropriate portion of the public
  sector referred to in paragraph (a) or (b), make any regulation that
  the Governor in Council considers necessary to adapt this Act or the
  regulations or any provision of this Act or the regulations to
  accommodate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Security Intelligence Service; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where an order is made under paragraph 4(1)(d) in
    relation to the Canadian Forces or the Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police, the Canadian Forces or the Royal Canadian Mounted Police.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under subsection (5) shall be made
  on the recommendation of the Treasury Board after consultation with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a regulation respecting the Canadian
    Security Intelligence Service, or the Royal Canadian Mounted Police,
    the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a regulation respecting the Canadian
    Forces, the Minister of National Defence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements may differ

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The effect of a regulation made under subsection (5)
  with respect to any matter may differ from the effect of the Act or
  the regulations or of any provision of the Act or the regulations with
  respect to that matter.
