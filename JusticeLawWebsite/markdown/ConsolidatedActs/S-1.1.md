## Safe Food for Canadians Act {#safe-food-for-canadians-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2012, c. 24
:::

Assented to 2012-11-22

An Act respecting food commodities, including their inspection, their
safety, their labelling and advertising, their import, export and
interprovincial trade, the establishment of standards for them, the
registration or licensing of persons who perform certain activities
related to them, the establishment of standards governing establishments
where those activities are performed and the registration of
establishments where those activities are performed
::::

:::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-429385 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Safe Food for Canadians Act](/eng/acts/S-1.1).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-429388 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[advertisement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[advertisement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a representation by
    any means for the purpose of promoting directly or indirectly the
    sale of a food commodity. ([publicité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Food Inspection
    Agency established by section 3 of the [Canadian Food Inspection
    Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-16.5). ([Agence]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[analyst]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[analyst]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated as an
    analyst under subsection 13(3) of the [Canadian Food Inspection
    Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-16.5) for the purposes of this
    Act. ([analyste]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel, aircraft, train,
    motor vehicle, trailer or other means of transportation, including a
    cargo container. ([véhicule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means anything on which information
    that is capable of being understood by a person, or read by a
    computer or other device, is recorded or
    marked. ([document]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any place, including a
    conveyance, where a food commodity is manufactured, prepared,
    stored, packaged or labelled. ([établissement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[food commodity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[food commodity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any food as defined in section 2 of the [Food and
      Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any animal or plant, or any of its parts, from
      which food referred to in paragraph (a) may be derived; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} anything prescribed to be a food
      commodity. ([produit alimentaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[grade name]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[grade name]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prescribed name, mark or
    designation of a food commodity. ([nom de
    catégorie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inspection mark]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspection mark]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prescribed mark,
    stamp, seal, product legend, word or design or any combination of
    those things. ([sceau d'inspection]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated under
    subsection 13(3) of the [Canadian Food Inspection Agency
    Act](/eng/acts/C-16.5) or paragraph 9(2)(b) of the [Canada Border
    Services Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-1.4) as an inspector for the
    purposes of this Act. ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[item to which this Act applies]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[item to which this Act applies]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a food commodity;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} anything used in an activity regulated under this
      Act; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a document that is related to a food commodity or
      to any activity regulated under this Act. ([chose visée par la
      présente loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a legend, word or mark that
    is or is to be applied or attached to or included in, or that
    accompanies or is to accompany, a food commodity or a
    package. ([étiquette]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Agriculture
    and Agri-Food. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an inner or outer receptacle
    or covering used or to be used in connection with a food commodity
    and includes a wrapper or confining
    band. ([emballage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 2
    of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[personal information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[personal information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in section 3 of the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21). ([renseignements
    personnels]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prepare]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prepare]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a food commodity,
    includes to process, treat, preserve, handle, test, grade, code or
    slaughter it or to do any other activity in respect of it that is
    prescribed. ([conditionnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by the
    regulations. ([Version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes agree to sell, offer for sale,
    expose for sale or have in possession for sale --- or distribute to
    one or more persons whether or not the distribution is made for
    consideration. ([vente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Review Tribunal continued
    by subsection 27(1) of the [Agriculture and Agri-Food Administrative
    Monetary Penalties
    Act](/eng/acts/A-8.8). ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[violation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[violation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following that
    may be proceeded with in accordance with the [Agriculture and
    Agri-Food Administrative Monetary Penalties Act](/eng/acts/A-8.8):

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any contravention of any provision of this Act or
      of a regulation made under this Act; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any refusal or neglect to perform any duty
      imposed by or under this Act. ([violation]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-429420 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-429423 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Importing

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for a
person to import a food commodity the selling of which is prohibited
under section 4 of the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recall order --- [Canadian Food
Inspection Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-16.5)

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for a
person to sell a food commodity that is the subject of a recall order
referred to in subsection 19(1) of the [Canadian Food Inspection Agency
Act](/eng/acts/C-16.5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deception, erroneous impression, etc.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for a person to manufacture, prepare, package, label, sell,
  import or advertise a food commodity in a manner that is false,
  misleading or deceptive or is likely to create an erroneous impression
  regarding its character, quality, value, quantity, composition, merit,
  safety or origin or the method of its manufacture or preparation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Labelled or packaged contrary to
  regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A food commodity that is labelled or packaged in
  contravention of a provision of the regulations is considered, for the
  purposes of this section, to be labelled or packaged in contravention
  of subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advertised contrary to regulations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A food commodity that is advertised in contravention
  of a provision of the regulations is considered, for the purposes of
  this section, to be advertised in contravention of subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tampering

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for a
person to tamper with any food commodity, its label or its package with
intent to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} render the food commodity injurious to human health;
  or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cause a reasonable apprehension in others that the
  food commodity is injurious to human health.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Threats

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for a
person to threaten to render a food commodity injurious to human health.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communicating false or misleading
information

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for a
person to communicate information, knowing that information to be false
or misleading or being reckless as to whether it is false or misleading,
with intent to cause a reasonable apprehension in others that a food
commodity was tampered with in order to render it injurious to human
health.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sending, conveying, importing or
exporting in accordance with regulations

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for a person to send or convey from one province to another
  --- or to import or export --- a prescribed food commodity unless the
  person does so in accordance with the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sending, conveying, importing or
  exporting with licence or registration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for a person to send or convey from
  one province to another --- or to import or export --- a prescribed
  food commodity unless the person is authorized to do so by a
  registration made under paragraph 20(1)(a), by a licence issued under
  that paragraph or by both such a registration and licence, as provided
  for in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sending, conveying, importing or
  exporting of commodity that meets requirements of regulations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for a person to send or convey from
  one province to another --- or to import or export --- a prescribed
  food commodity unless the food commodity meets the requirements of the
  regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Selling, advertising or possessing

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for a
person to sell, advertise or have in their possession a food commodity
that has been sent or conveyed from one province to another, or
imported, in contravention of any provision of this Act or the
regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Possession of commodity that meets
requirements of regulations

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for a
person to have in their possession for the purpose of sending or
conveying from one province to another --- or for the purpose of
exporting --- a prescribed food commodity, unless it meets the
requirements of the regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct of prescribed activity in
accordance with the regulations

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for a person to conduct a prescribed activity in respect of
  a prescribed food commodity that has been imported --- or that is to
  be exported or to be sent or conveyed from one province to another ---
  unless the activity is conducted in accordance with the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct of prescribed activity with
  licence or registration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for a person to conduct a prescribed
  activity in respect of a prescribed food commodity that has been
  imported --- or that is to be exported or to be sent or conveyed from
  one province to another --- unless the person is authorized to conduct
  that activity by a registration made under paragraph 20(1)(b), a
  licence issued under that paragraph or by both such a registration and
  licence, as provided for in the regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of inspection mark or grade name

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  authorized by the regulations, it is prohibited for a person to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} apply or use an inspection mark or grade name; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} advertise or sell anything if the thing has an
    inspection mark or grade name on it, or an inspection mark or grade
    name is used in connection with the thing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of similar mark or name

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for a person to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} apply or use a thing that so resembles an
    inspection mark or grade name that it is likely to be mistaken for
    it; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} advertise or sell anything that has on it a thing
    referred to in paragraph (a) or that has a thing referred to in that
    paragraph used in connection with it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person found in possession of anything referred to
  in paragraph (1)(b) or (2)(b) is considered, in the absence of
  evidence to the contrary, to be in possession of it for the purpose of
  advertising or selling.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False or misleading information

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for a
person to make a false or misleading statement to any person who is
exercising powers or performing duties or functions under this Act ---
or to provide him or her with false or misleading information --- in
connection with any matter under any provision of this Act or the
regulations, including in respect of an application for a licence or
registration.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for a
person to obstruct or hinder a person who is exercising powers or
performing duties or functions under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Falsifying or altering, etc., required
documents

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for a person to alter, destroy or falsify a document that
  they are required under this Act to keep, maintain or provide.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Altering, possessing, etc., official
  documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for a person to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} alter a document issued or made --- or in any
    manner given --- under this Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have in their possession, or use, a document issued
    or made --- or in any manner given --- under this Act that has been
    altered.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Possessing or using documents that
resemble official documents

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for a
person to have in their possession, or use, any document that has not
been issued or made --- or in any manner given --- under this Act if the
document so resembles a document issued or made --- or in any manner
given --- under this Act that it is likely to be mistaken for such a
document.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal use

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the
regulations, a provision of this Act or the regulations that prohibits
an activity --- or that requires the doing of an activity --- does not
apply to a person who is carrying out the activity solely for personal
use.

## [Registrations and Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-429485 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, on application,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} register a person, or issue a licence to a person,
    authorizing them to send or convey from one province to another, or
    to import or export, a prescribed food commodity, or both register a
    person and issue them a licence; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} register a person, or issue a licence to a person,
    authorizing them to conduct a prescribed activity in respect of a
    prescribed food commodity that has been imported or that is to be
    exported or to be sent or conveyed from one province to another, or
    both register a person and issue them a licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions --- regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The registration and the licence are subject to the
  prescribed conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions --- Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may make a registration or licence
  subject to any additional conditions that the Minister considers
  appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to comply

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The holder of the registration or licence must comply
  with all the conditions to which the registration or licence is
  subject.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No transfer

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The registration or licence is not transferable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishments

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, on application, register an establishment as one where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an imported prescribed food commodity is to be sent
    or conveyed, in its imported condition, for the purposes of the
    exercise of an inspector's powers under this Act in respect of that
    food commodity; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a prescribed activity in respect of a prescribed
    food commodity that has been imported or is to be exported or to be
    sent or conveyed from one province to another may be exercised.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Holder

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The applicant in respect of an establishment is the
  holder of the registration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions --- regulations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The registration is subject to the prescribed
  conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions --- Minister

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may make a registration subject to any
  additional conditions that the Minister considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to comply

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The holder of the registration must comply with all
  the conditions to which the registration is subject.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No transfer

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The registration is not transferable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Act

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If an establishment is registered, it and all food
  commodities in it are subject to this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment, suspension, cancellation and
renewal

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the
regulations, the Minister may amend, suspend, cancel or renew a
registration made under subsection 20(1) or 21(1) or a licence issued
under subsection 20(1).

## [Administration and Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-429518 .Part}

### [Certificate]{.HTitleText2} {#h-429519 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate to be produced

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Each inspector is to be
given a certificate in a form established by the President of the Agency
or the President of the Canada Border Services Agency, as the case may
be, attesting to the inspector's designation and, on entering a place
under subsection 24(1), the inspector must, on request, produce the
certificate to the person in charge of that place.

### [Inspection]{.HTitleText2} {#h-429522 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to enter a place

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 26(1), an
  inspector may, for a purpose related to verifying compliance or
  preventing non-compliance with this Act, enter a place, including a
  conveyance, in which they have reasonable grounds to believe that an
  activity regulated under this Act is conducted or an item to which
  this Act applies is located.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The inspector may, for the purpose referred to in
  subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine or test, or take samples of, anything that
    is in the place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} open a package that is in the place;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} examine a document that is in the place, make
    copies of it or take extracts from it;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} order the owner or person having possession, care
    or control of an item to which this Act applies that is in the place
    to move it or, for any time that may be necessary, not to move it or
    to restrict its movement;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used a computer or other device
    that is in the place to examine data that is contained in or
    available to a computer system or reproduce it or cause it to be
    reproduced in the form of a printout or other intelligible output
    and remove the output for examination or copying;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used copying equipment that is
    in the place and remove the copies for examination;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} take photographs or make recordings or sketches;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} order any person in the place to establish their
    identity to the inspector's satisfaction;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} order any person who, in the place, conducts an
    activity regulated under this Act to stop or start the activity;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} prohibit or limit access to all or part of the
    place or to anything that is in the place; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} remove anything from the place for the purpose of
    examination, conducting tests or taking samples.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stopping or moving conveyance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of entering a conveyance, the
  inspector may order the owner or person having possession, care or
  control of the conveyance to stop it or move it to a place where the
  inspector can enter it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons accompanying inspector

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The inspector may be accompanied by any person that
  they believe is necessary to help them exercise their powers or
  perform their duties or functions under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entering private property

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An inspector and any person accompanying them may
  enter and pass through private property, other than a dwelling house
  on that property, in order to gain entry to a place referred to in
  subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to be given to inspector

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The owner of the place, the person in charge of it
  and every person in it must give all assistance to the inspector that
  is reasonably required to enable the inspector to exercise their
  powers or perform their duties or functions under this Act and provide
  the inspector with any document or information, or access to any data,
  that they may reasonably require.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seizure

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The inspector may seize
and detain anything that the inspector has reasonable grounds to believe

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} was used in the contravention of any provision of
  this Act or the regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is something in relation to which a provision of this
  Act or the regulations was contravened; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} was obtained by the contravention of a provision of
  this Act or the regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dwelling-house

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  place is a dwelling-house, the inspector is not authorized to enter it
  without the occupant's consent except under the authority of a warrant
  issued under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A justice of the peace may, on [ex parte]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} application, issue a warrant authorizing, subject to any
  conditions specified in the warrant, the inspector named in it to
  enter a dwelling-house if the justice of the peace is satisfied by
  information on oath that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dwelling-house is a place referred to in
    subsection 24(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entry to the dwelling-house is necessary for a
    purpose referred to in that subsection; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} entry to the dwelling-house was refused by the
    occupant or there are reasonable grounds to believe that it will be
    refused or to believe that consent to entry cannot be obtained from
    the occupant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In executing a warrant issued under subsection (2),
  the inspector is not entitled to use force unless the use of force has
  been specifically authorized in the warrant and they are accompanied
  by a peace officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of telecommunication

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An application for a warrant under subsection (2) may
  be submitted, and the warrant may be issued, by a means of
  telecommunication and section 487.1 of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46) applies for those purposes with any necessary
  modifications.

### [Dealing with Seized Things]{.HTitleText2} {#h-429566 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removing, altering and interfering

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Except with the
authorization of an inspector, it is prohibited for a person to remove,
alter or interfere with anything seized under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of inspector

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** An inspector may, in
respect of anything seized under this Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on notice to its owner or the person having
  possession, care or control of it at the time of its seizure, store it
  or move it at the expense of the person to whom the notice is given;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} order its owner or the person having possession, care
  or control of it at the time of its seizure to store it or move it at
  the expense of the person being so ordered; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} order its owner or the person having possession, care
  or control of it at the time of its seizure to dispose of it at the
  expense of the person being so ordered --- or, on notice to its owner
  or the person having possession, care or control of it at the time of
  its seizure, dispose of it at the expense of the person to whom the
  notice is given --- if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the thing is perishable, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the inspector is of the opinion that the thing
    presents a risk of injury to human health and that its disposal is
    necessary to respond to the risk.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Release of seized thing

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** If an inspector is
satisfied that the provisions of this Act and the regulations that apply
with respect to a thing seized under this Act have been complied with,
the thing must be released.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for return

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 35, if
  proceedings are instituted in relation to a thing seized under this
  Act, its owner or the person having possession, care or control of it
  at the time of its seizure may apply, in the case of a violation, to
  the Tribunal or, in the case of an offence, to the court before which
  the proceedings are being held, for an order that the thing be
  returned.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order for return

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Tribunal or court, as the case may be, is
  satisfied that sufficient evidence exists or may reasonably be
  obtained without the continued detention of the thing, the Tribunal or
  court may order it to be returned to the applicant, subject to any
  conditions the Tribunal or court may impose to ensure that it is
  preserved for any purpose for which it may subsequently be required.

### [Other Measures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-429583 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal or destruction of unlawful
imports

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector who has reasonable grounds to believe that an imported food
  commodity does not meet the requirements of the regulations or was
  imported in contravention of a provision of this Act or the
  regulations may, by notice, whether the food commodity is seized or
  not, order its owner or importer, or the person having possession,
  care or control of it, to remove it from Canada at their expense or,
  if removal is not possible, to destroy it at their expense.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must be either delivered personally to the
  owner or importer of the food commodity, or to the person having
  possession, care or control of it, or sent by registered mail, to the
  owner's, importer's or person's address in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the food commodity is not removed from Canada, or
  destroyed, within the period specified in the notice --- or, if no
  period was specified, within 90 days after the day on which the notice
  was delivered or sent, --- it is, despite section 30, forfeited to Her
  Majesty in right of Canada and may be disposed of, as the Minister may
  direct, at the expense of the person to whom the notice was delivered
  or sent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of application of subsection
  (3)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An inspector may, for a period specified by the
  inspector, suspend the application of subsection (3) if the inspector
  is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the food commodity does not present a risk of
    injury to human health;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the food commodity will not be sold within that
    period;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the measures that should have been taken for the
    food commodity not to have been imported in contravention of a
    provision of this Act or the regulations will be taken within that
    period; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the food commodity does not meet the
    requirements of the regulations, it will be brought into compliance
    with the regulations within that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An inspector may cancel the notice if the inspector
  is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the food commodity does not present a risk of
    injury to human health;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the food commodity has not been sold within the
    period referred to in subsection (6);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the measures referred to in paragraph (4)(c) were
    taken within that period; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the food commodity did not meet the requirements
    of the regulations when it was imported, it was brought into
    compliance with the regulations within that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The period for the purposes of subsection (5) is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the application of subsection (3) was suspended
    under subsection (4), the period of the suspension; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the application of subsection (3) was not
    suspended, the period specified in the notice or, if no period was
    specified, the period that ends 90 days after the day on which the
    notice was delivered or sent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Injunction

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, on
  the application of the Minister, it appears to a court of competent
  jurisdiction that a person has committed, is about to commit or is
  likely to commit an act or omission that constitutes or is directed
  toward the commission of an offence under this Act, the court may
  order the person named in the application to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} refrain from doing an act that it appears to the
    court may constitute or be directed toward the commission of an
    offence under this Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} do an act that it appears to the court may prevent
    the commission of an offence under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No order may be made unless 48 hours' notice is
  served on the person named in the application or the urgency of the
  situation is such that service of notice would not be in the public
  interest.

### [Forfeiture]{.HTitleText2} {#h-429614 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unclaimed seized things

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  thing seized under this Act is, at the Minister's election, forfeited
  to Her Majesty in right of Canada if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 60 days after the seizure, no person is
    identified as its owner or as the person entitled to possess it; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its owner or the person having possession, care or
    control of it at the time of its seizure does not claim it within 60
    days after the day on which they are notified that an inspector has
    released it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings instituted

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if proceedings are
  instituted for a violation or an offence that relates to the seized
  thing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A seized thing that is forfeited under subsection (1)
  may be disposed of, as the Minister may direct, at the expense of its
  owner or the person having possession, care or control of the thing at
  the time of its seizure.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent --- seized thing

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** If the owner of a thing
seized under this Act consents to its forfeiture, it is forfeited to Her
Majesty in right of Canada and may be disposed of, as the Minister may
direct, at the owner's expense.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violation or offence

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Tribunal decides that a person has committed a violation or a person
  is convicted of an offence under this Act, the Tribunal or the
  convicting court, as the case may be, may, in addition to any
  punishment imposed, order that a thing by means of or in respect of
  which the violation or offence was committed, regardless of whether it
  was seized under this Act or not, be forfeited to Her Majesty in right
  of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A thing forfeited under subsection (1) may be
  disposed of, as the Minister may direct, at the expense of,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the thing was not seized, its owner; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the thing was seized, its owner or the person
    having possession, care or control of the thing at the time of its
    seizure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of seized things if no
  forfeiture ordered

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Tribunal or court does not order the
  forfeiture of a thing that was seized, it must be returned to its
  owner or the person having possession, care or control of it at the
  time of its seizure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (3), if a penalty or fine was
  imposed,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the thing may continue to be detained until the
    penalty or fine is paid; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the thing may be sold under execution and any
    proceeds realized from the sale may be applied in payment of the
    penalty or fine.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture on application of inspector

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  judge of a superior court of the province in which anything is seized
  under this Act may, on the application of an inspector, order that the
  thing be forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice and inquiry

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The order may be made only if any notice to any
  persons that the judge directs was given and the judge finds, after
  making any inquiry that he or she considers necessary, that the thing
  is one by means of or in relation to which any of the provisions of
  this Act or the regulations have been contravened.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A thing that is forfeited under subsection (1) may be
  disposed of, as the Minister may direct, at the expense of its owner
  or the person having possession, care or control of it at the time of
  its seizure.

### [Offences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-429651 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who contravenes a provision of this Act, other than sections 7
  and 9, or a provision of the regulations --- or fails to do anything
  the person was ordered to do by, or does anything the person was
  ordered not to do by, the Minister or an inspector under this Act
  other than subsection 32(1) --- is guilty of an offence and is liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine of not more
    than \$5,000,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than two
    years or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, for a first offence, to a
    fine of not more than \$250,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not
    more than six months or to both and, for a subsequent offence, to a
    fine of not more than \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not
    more than 18 months or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Due diligence defence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person is not to be found guilty of an offence
  under subsection (1) if they establish that they exercised due
  diligence to prevent the commission of the offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences --- other provisions and
  orders

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who contravenes section 7 or 9 or who, in
  contravening any other provision of this Act or a provision of the
  regulations or in failing to do anything the person was ordered to do
  by, or in doing anything the person was ordered not to do by, the
  Minister or an inspector under this Act, other than subsection 32(1),
  knowingly or recklessly causes a risk of injury to human health is
  guilty of an offence and is liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine the amount
    of which is at the discretion of the court or to imprisonment for a
    term of not more than five years or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, for a first offence, to a
    fine of not more than \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not
    more than 18 months or to both and, for a subsequent offence, to a
    fine of not more than \$1,000,000 or to imprisonment for a term of
    not more than two years or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parties to offence

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a person other than an individual commits an
  offence under subsection (1), any of the person's directors or
  officers, or agents or mandataries, who directs, authorizes, assents
  to or acquiesces or participates in the commission of the offence is a
  party to the offence and is liable on conviction to the punishment
  provided for by this Act, even if the person is not prosecuted for the
  offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of offence

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In a prosecution for an offence under this Act, it is
  sufficient proof of the offence to establish that it was committed by
  an employee or an agent or mandatary of the accused, even if the
  employee or the agent or mandatary is not identified or prosecuted for
  the offence, unless the accused establishes that the offence was
  committed without the knowledge or consent of the accused and that the
  accused exercised all due diligence to prevent its commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offence

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** If an offence under this
Act is committed or continued on more than one day, it constitutes a
separate offence for each day on which it is committed or continued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** Summary conviction
proceedings for an offence under this Act may be instituted no later
than two years after the time when the subject matter of the proceedings
arose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Venue

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** A prosecution for an
offence under this Act may be instituted, heard or determined in the
place in which the offence was committed, the accused was apprehended or
the accused is carrying on business.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Admissibility of documents as evidence

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In an
  application under section 33 or any proceedings for a violation or for
  an offence under this Act, a certificate, report or other document
  purporting to be signed by the Minister, the President of the Agency
  or any person who is exercising powers or performing duties or
  functions under this Act is admissible in evidence without proof of
  the signature or official character of the person appearing to have
  signed it and, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, is proof of
  the statements contained in it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies and extracts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In an application under section 33 or any proceedings
  for a violation or for an offence under this Act, a copy or extract
  from a certificate, report or other document that is made by a person
  referred to in subsection (1) that appears to have been certified
  under the signature of that person as a true copy or extract is
  admissible in evidence without proof of the signature or official
  character of the person appearing to have signed it and, in the
  absence of evidence to the contrary, has the same probative force as
  the original would have if it were proved in the ordinary way.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of intention to produce

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A certificate, report or other document referred to
  in subsection (1), or a copy of or an extract from it, may be admitted
  in evidence under that subsection only if the person intending to
  produce it gives to the person against whom it is intended to be
  produced reasonable notice of that intention together with a duplicate
  of the certificate, report, document, copy or extract.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof --- person

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** In an application under
section 33 or any proceedings for a violation or for an offence under
this Act, proof that any food commodity, or its package or label, bore a
name or address purporting to be the name or address of --- or bore any
other information that permits the identification of --- the person by
whom the food commodity was manufactured, prepared, stored, packaged,
labelled or imported is, in the absence of evidence to the contrary,
proof that the food commodity was manufactured, prepared, stored,
packaged, labelled or imported by that person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof --- establishment

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** In an application under
section 33 or any proceedings for a violation or for an offence under
this Act, proof that any food commodity, or its package or label, bore a
name or address purporting to be the name or address of --- or bore any
other information that permits the identification of --- the
establishment where the food commodity was manufactured, prepared,
stored, packaged or labelled is proof, in the absence of evidence to the
contrary, that the food commodity was manufactured, prepared, stored,
packaged or labelled at that establishment.

    application of this Act or the regulations or a provision of this
    Act or the regulations;

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} respecting the measures to be taken in respect of
    items to which this Act applies that present a risk of injury to
    human health or that are in contravention of any provision of this
    Act or the regulations, or that are suspected on reasonable grounds
    of presenting such a risk or being in contravention of any such
    provision; and

  - [(y)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that by this Act is to be
    prescribed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paragraph (1)(e)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Regulations made under paragraph (1)(e) may, among
  other things, establish pre-clearance or in-transit requirements for
  any imported food commodity or anything imported with it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paragraphs (1)(d) to (e.1)

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} Regulations made under paragraph (1)(d), (e) or
  (e.1) in respect of any fresh fruit or vegetable may, among other
  things, require a person to be a member of an entity or organization
  specified in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paragraph (1)(m)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Regulations made under paragraph (1)(m) may, among
  other things, require the provision to the Minister or an inspector of

### [Incorporation by Reference]{.HTitleText2} {#h-429764 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** A regulation made under
subsection 51(1) may incorporate by reference any document, regardless
of its source, either as it exists on a particular date or as it is
amended from time to time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessibility

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must ensure
that any document that is incorporated by reference in a regulation made
under subsection 51(1), including any amendments to the document, is
accessible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** A person is not liable to
be found guilty of an offence or subjected to an administrative sanction
for any contravention in respect of which a document that is
incorporated by reference in a regulation made under subsection 51(1) is
relevant unless, at the time of the alleged contravention, the document
was accessible as required by section 53 or it was otherwise accessible
to the person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No registration or publication

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, a
document that is incorporated by reference in a regulation made under
subsection 51(1) is not required to be transmitted for registration or
published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) by reason
only that it is incorporated by reference.

### [Interim Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-429773 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interim orders

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may make an interim order that contains any provision that
  may be contained in a regulation made under subsection 51(1) if he or
  she believes that immediate action is required to deal with a
  significant risk, direct or indirect, to health, safety or the
  environment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of effect

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An interim order has effect from the time that it is
  made but ceases to have effect on the earliest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 14 days after the day on which it is made, unless
    it is approved by the Governor in Council,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which it is repealed,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which a regulation made under subsection
    51(1) that has the same effect as the interim order comes into
    force, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} one year after the interim order is made or any
    shorter period that may be specified in the interim order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of unpublished order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person is not to be determined to have committed a
  violation, or to be convicted of an offence, that consists of a
  contravention of an interim order that, at the time of the alleged
  contravention, had not been published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) unless it is proved that, at the
  time of the alleged contravention,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person had been notified of the interim order;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reasonable steps had been taken to bring the
    purport of the interim order to the notice of those persons likely
    to be affected by it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An interim order is exempt from the application of
  sections 3 and 9 of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of any provision of this Act other
  than this section, any reference to regulations made under subsection
  51(1) is considered to include interim orders, and any reference to a
  regulation made under a specified provision of that subsection is
  considered to include a reference to the portion of an interim order
  containing any provision that may be contained in a regulation made
  under the specified provision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of order

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A copy of each interim order must be tabled in each
  House of Parliament within 15 days after the day on which it is made.
  It is to be sent to the Clerk of the House if the House is not
  sitting.

  - ###### Impact of remote proceedings {#impact-of-remote-proceedings .MarginalNote}

    - [78.1]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Justice must,
      no later than three years after the day on which this Act receives
      royal assent, initiate one or more independent reviews on the use
      of remote proceedings in criminal justice matters that must
      include an assessment of whether remote proceedings

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enhance, preserve or adversely affect access to
        justice;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintain fundamental principles of the
        administration of justice; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} adequately address the rights and obligations
        of participants in the criminal justice system, including
        accused persons.

    - ###### Report {#report .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Justice must, no later than five
      years after the day on which a review is initiated, cause a report
      on the review --- including any findings or recommendations
      resulting from it --- to be laid before each House of Parliament.

- --- 2022, c. 17, s. 78.2

  - ###### Review by committee {#review-by-committee .MarginalNote}

    - [78.2]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the start of the fifth year
      after the day on which this Act receives royal assent, the
      provisions enacted or amended by this Act are to be referred to a
      committee of the Senate and a committee of the House of Commons
      that may be designated or established for the purpose of reviewing
      the provisions.

    - ###### Report {#report-1 .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The committees to which the provisions are
      referred are to review them and the use of remote proceedings in
      criminal justice matters and submit reports to the Houses of
      Parliament of which they are committees, including statements
      setting out any changes to the provisions that they recommend.
