## [PART 8]{.HLabel1}[Payments to Provinces and Territories]{.HTitleText1} {#h-37025 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of \$650,000,000

- **[[190]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-190 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} From
  and out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund, there may, on the
  requisition of the Minister of Human Resources and Skills Development,
  be paid to the provinces a payment in the amount of \$650,000,000 for
  the fiscal year beginning on April 1, 2006, in respect of early
  learning and child care.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial allocation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The payment referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  allocated to the provinces as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to Ontario, \$252,933,933.35;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to Quebec, \$152,740,663.90;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to Nova Scotia, \$18,743,350.65;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to New Brunswick, \$15,028,068.42;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} to Manitoba, \$23,683,368.44;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} to British Columbia, \$85,920,064.72;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} to Prince Edward Island, \$2,762,632.45;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} to Saskatchewan, \$19,863,918.85;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to Alberta, \$65,973,415.33;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} to Newfoundland and Labrador, \$10,266,477.67;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} to Yukon, \$619,370.20;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} to the Northwest Territories, \$862,067.75; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} to Nunavut, \$602,668.27.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment to territories

**[[191]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-191 .sectionLabel}** From and out of the
Consolidated Revenue Fund, there may, on the requisition of the Minister
of Finance, be paid to the territories the following payments for the
fiscal year beginning on April 1, 2006:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to Yukon, \$10,900,000;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the Northwest Territories, \$18,000,000; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to Nunavut, \$17,500,000.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- SI/2011-16

  - []{.lawlabel} His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
    recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to
    paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_ae){#fn_ae-ID0EEHAAAA} of the [Public
    Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
    Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_be){#fn_be-ID0EBHAAAA}, hereby

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} transfers to the Minister of Indian Affairs and
      Northern Development the powers, duties and functions of the
      Minister of the Environment

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} under the [Mackenzie Gas Project Impacts
        Act](/eng/acts/M-0.1)[[Footnote
        ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_ce){#fn_ce-ID0EBABGAAAA} in relation to
        the Mackenzie gas project, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} under section 209 of the [Budget
        Implementation Act, 2006](/eng/acts/B-9.855)[[Footnote
        ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_de){#fn_de-ID0EBAAGAAAA}; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} transfers from the Department of the Environment
      to the Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development the
      control and supervision of the portion of the federal public
      administration in the Department of the Environment known as the
      Mackenzie Gas Project Office,

    effective February 16, 2011.
