## Department of the Environment Act {#department-of-the-environment-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. E-10
:::

An Act respecting the Department of the Environment
::::

::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-177827 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Department of the Environment Act](/eng/acts/E-10).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 14(2nd Supp.), s. 2
:::

## [Establishment of the Department]{.HTitleText1} {#h-177832 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Department established

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a department of the Government of Canada called the
  Department of the Environment over which the Minister of the
  Environment appointed by commission under the Great Seal shall
  preside.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister holds office during pleasure and has the
  management and direction of the Department.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 14(2nd Supp.), s. 3
- 1978-79, c. 13, s. 13
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy head

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
appoint an officer called the Deputy Minister of the Environment to hold
office during pleasure and to be the deputy head of the Department.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 14(2nd Supp.), s. 4
:::

## [Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-177844 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions of Minister

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  powers, duties and functions of the Minister extend to and include all
  matters over which Parliament has jurisdiction, not by law assigned to
  any other department, board or agency of the Government of Canada,
  relating to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the preservation and enhancement of the quality of
    the natural environment, including water, air and soil quality;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} renewable resources, including migratory birds and
    other non-domestic flora and fauna;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} water;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} meteorology;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} notwithstanding paragraph 4(2)(g) of the
    [Department of Health Act](/eng/acts/H-3.2), the enforcement of any
    rules or regulations made by the International Joint Commission,
    promulgated pursuant to the treaty between the United States of
    America and His Majesty, King Edward VII, relating to boundary
    waters and questions arising between the United States and Canada,
    in so far as they relate to the preservation and enhancement of the
    quality of the natural environment; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the coordination of the policies and programs of
    the Government of Canada respecting the preservation and enhancement
    of the quality of the natural environment.

  - [(g) and (h)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1995, c. 11, s.
    18\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The powers, duties and functions of the Minister also
  extend to and include such other matters, relating to the environment
  and over which Parliament has jurisdiction, as are by law assigned to
  the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. E-10, s. 4
- 1995, c. 11, s. 18
- 1996, c. 8, s. 19
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem re preservation and enhancement of
environmental quality

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister, in exercising
his powers and carrying out his duties and functions under section 4,
shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} initiate, recommend and undertake programs, and
  coordinate programs of the Government of Canada that are designed

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to promote the establishment or adoption of
    objectives or standards relating to environmental quality, or to
    control pollution,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to ensure that new federal projects, programs and
    activities are assessed early in the planning process for potential
    adverse effects on the quality of the natural environment and that a
    further review is carried out of those projects, programs, and
    activities that are found to have probable significant adverse
    effects, and the results thereof taken into account, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to provide to Canadians environmental information
    in the public interest;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} promote and encourage the institution of practices
  and conduct leading to the better preservation and enhancement of
  environmental quality, and cooperate with provincial governments or
  agencies thereof, or any bodies, organizations or persons, in any
  programs having similar objects; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} advise the heads of departments, boards and agencies
  of the Government of Canada on all matters pertaining to the
  preservation and enhancement of the quality of the natural
  environment.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 14(2nd Supp.), s. 6
- 1978-79, c. 13, s. 14
:::

## [Guidelines by Order]{.HTitleText1} {#h-177869 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Guidelines related to environmental
quality

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
carrying out his duties and functions related to environmental quality,
the Minister may, by order, with the approval of the Governor in
Council, establish guidelines for use by departments, boards and
agencies of the Government of Canada and, where appropriate, by
corporations named in Schedule III to the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11) and regulatory bodies in the exercise of their
powers and the carrying out of their duties and functions.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 14(2nd Supp.), s. 6
- 1978-79, c. 13, s. 14
- 1984, c. 31, s. 14
:::

## [Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-177874 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, with the
approval of the Governor in Council, enter into agreements with the
government of any province or any agency thereof respecting the carrying
out of programs for which the Minister is responsible.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 14(2nd Supp.), s. 6
- 1978-79, c. 13, s. 14
:::

## [Annual Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-177879 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall, on or
before January 31 next following the end of each fiscal year or, if
Parliament is not then sitting, on any of the first five days next
thereafter that either House of Parliament is sitting, submit to
Parliament a report showing the operations of the Department for that
fiscal year.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 14(2nd Supp.), s. 7
- 1978-79, c. 13, s. 14
:::
