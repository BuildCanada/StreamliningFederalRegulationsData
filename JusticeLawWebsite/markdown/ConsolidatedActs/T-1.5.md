## Tax Conventions Implementation Act, 2002 {#tax-conventions-implementation-act-2002 .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2002, c. 24
:::

Assented to 2002-12-12

An Act to implement an agreement, conventions and protocols concluded
between Canada and Kuwait, Mongolia, the United Arab Emirates, Moldova,
Norway, Belgium and Italy for the avoidance of double taxation and the
prevention of fiscal evasion and to amend the enacted text of three tax
treaties
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::
