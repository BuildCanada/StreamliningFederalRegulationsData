## Marine Transportation Security Act {#marine-transportation-security-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1994, c. 40
:::

Assented to 1994-12-15

An Act to provide for the security of marine transportation
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-352594 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Marine Transportation Security Act](/eng/acts/M-0.8).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-352597 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[authorized screening]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[authorized screening]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means anything
      authorized or required to be done under any regulation, security
      measure or security rule for the control, observation, inspection
      and search of persons or goods to prevent the unauthorized
      possession or carriage of weapons, explosives and incendiaries on
      board vessels and at marine
      facilities; ([contrôle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :    [[Canadian ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel registered
      under the [Canada Shipping Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15)  or
      registered in Canada before August 1, 1936 under the Merchant
      Shipping Act, 1894  of the Parliament of the United Kingdom, 57-58
      Victoria, chapter 60, and all Acts adding to or amending that
      Act; ([navire canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means anything that may be taken or
      placed on board a vessel, including personal belongings, baggage
      and cargo; ([bien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[marine facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[marine facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an area of land, water, ice or other supporting
        surface used, designed, prepared, equipped or set apart for use,
        either in whole or in part, for the arrival, departure, movement
        or servicing of vessels,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} buildings, installations and equipment on the
        area, associated with it or used or set apart for handling or
        storing goods that have been or are destined to be transported
        on a vessel,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} equipment and facilities used to provide
        services relating to marine transportation, and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} marine installations and structures, as defined
        in section 2 of the Canadian Laws Offshore Application
        Act; ([installation maritime]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
      Transport; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when used in respect of a vessel,

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the actual owner of the vessel, if it is not
          registered, or the registered owner, if it is registered,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a person having a beneficial interest in the
          vessel, including an interest arising under contract and any
          other equitable interest, other than an interest by way of a
          mortgage,

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a lessee or charterer of the vessel who is
          responsible for its navigation, or

        - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a master or other person who has command or
          charge of the vessel, other than a pilot, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when used in respect of a marine facility, a
        person who has the charge, management and control of the
        facility, whether on that person's own account or as an agent of
        another person; ([exploitant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[proposed security rule]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[proposed security rule]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a rule
      submitted for the approval of the Minister under section
      10; ([règle de sûreté proposée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[restricted area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[restricted area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area established
      under any regulation, security measure or security rule to which
      access is restricted to authorized persons; ([zone
      réglementée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[screening officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[screening officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
      designated by the Minister under section 19.1 as a screening
      officer for the purposes of this Act; ([agent de
      contrôle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[security inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[security inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
      designated by the Minister under section 22 as a security
      inspector for the purposes of this
      Act; ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[security measure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[security measure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a measure
      formulated by the Minister under section 7; ([mesure de
      sûreté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[security rule]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[security rule]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a rule approved by the
      Minister under section 10; ([règle de sûreté]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :    [[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Transportation Appeal
      Tribunal of Canada established by subsection 2(1) of the
      [Transportation Appeal Tribunal of Canada Act](/eng/acts/T-18.5)
      ; ([Tribunal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a dredge, floating
      elevator, floating home, floating oil rig, seaplane, raft, log or
      lumber boom, air cushion vehicle and any type of ship, boat or
      craft used or capable of being used for marine navigation,
      regardless of how it is propelled. ([bâtiment]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References to the Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a provision of this Act authorizes or requires
  the Minister to do anything, the Minister may authorize it to be done
  by a person appointed to serve in the Department of Transport in an
  appropriate capacity and references to the Minister in the provision
  shall be interpreted as including that person.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-352629 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to vessels and marine
facilities

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), this Act applies in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} vessels and marine facilities in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Canadian ships outside Canada; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} marine installations and structures, as provided in
    sections 5 and 6 of the Canadian Laws Offshore Application Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Act does not apply so as to require or authorize
  any person outside Canada to contravene the law of a foreign country
  or any Canadian ship outside Canada to be operated in contravention of
  that law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation - military vessels, etc.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} This Act does not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} vessels and marine facilities operated under the
    authority of the Minister of National Defence; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} military vessels of a foreign country to the extent
    that the Minister of National Defence may exempt them from the
    application of this Act.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-352644 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations respecting security

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations respecting the security of
  marine transportation, including regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for preventing unlawful interference with marine
    transportation and ensuring that appropriate action is taken where
    that interference occurs or could occur;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} requiring or authorizing screening for the purpose
    of protecting persons, goods, vessels and marine facilities;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the establishment of restricted areas;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the keeping and preservation of records
    and documents relating to the security of marine operations,
    including copies of the regulations, security measures and security
    rules;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} for requiring the records and documents to be filed
    with the Minister or provided on request; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} respecting the protection, preservation and return
    of any evidence that has been seized under this Act without a
    warrant or any vessel that has been detained under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences relating to the regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes a regulation made under
  subsection (1) is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
  conviction and liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine not
    exceeding \$5,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
    months or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, to a fine not
    exceeding \$100,000.

## [Security Measures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-352666 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may formulate security measures

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may formulate measures respecting the security of marine
  transportation, including measures containing provisions that may be
  included in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operators to carry out security
  measures

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by notice in writing, require or
  authorize the operator of a vessel or marine facility to carry out the
  security measures, and the measures may apply instead of or in
  addition to any provision of the regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may carry out security measures

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may carry out security measures on any vessel or at any
  marine facility where the Minister considers that the security of
  persons or goods is not adequately protected or is not protected in
  accordance with this Act, the regulations and any applicable security
  measures and security rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability for costs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operators of the vessel or marine facility are
  jointly and severally liable to Her Majesty in right of Canada for the
  costs incurred by the Minister in carrying out the security measures.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences relating to security measures

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Every operator who does not
carry out security measures that the operator is required to carry out,
and every person who wilfully obstructs a person who is carrying out
security measures, is guilty of an offence and liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine not
    exceeding \$10,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one
    year or to both, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, to a fine not
    exceeding \$200,000; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine not
    exceeding \$5,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
    months or to both, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, to a fine not
    exceeding \$100,000.

## [Security Rules]{.HTitleText1} {#h-352685 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of section

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  purpose of this section is to allow operators of vessels and marine
  facilities to formulate and operate under security rules as an
  alternative to security measures required or authorized by the
  Minister, but this section does not limit the authority of the
  Minister to require or authorize security measures.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Formulation of rules

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator of a vessel or marine facility may
  formulate rules respecting any matter relating to the security of the
  vessel or facility and the operator may submit the rules to the
  Minister for approval.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may request rules

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by notice in writing, request the
  operator of a vessel or marine facility

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to formulate rules respecting any matter specified
    in the notice relating to the security of the vessel or facility;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to submit the rules to the Minister within any
    period specified in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Before submitting the rules, the operator shall
  consult persons who are likely to be directly affected by the rules,
  including any persons specified in the notice by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Results of consultation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} When submitting the rules, the operator shall also
  submit a statement identifying the persons consulted and describing
  any disagreement they may have expressed about the rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of rules

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Within one hundred and twenty days after the rules
  have been submitted, the Minister shall decide whether to approve them
  and shall notify the operator of the decision in writing and, if the
  Minister approves the rules,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister may make the approval subject to any
    conditions the Minister considers appropriate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the operator shall notify the persons who were
    consulted that the rules have been approved; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the operator shall carry out the rules and any
    conditions of their approval until the approval is revoked.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules not approved

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister decides not to approve the rules, the
  Minister shall give the operator reasons for the decision and the
  Minister may request the operator to revise and resubmit the rules
  within any period that the Minister may specify.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments to security rules

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Amendments to security rules may be submitted and
  approved in the same way as are the security rules they amend and,
  when approved, have the same effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of approval

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may revoke the approval of security
  rules, either at the request of the operator or otherwise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences relating to security rules

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every operator who does
not carry out security rules and conditions approved by the Minister in
relation to the operator, and every person who wilfully obstructs a
person who is carrying out security rules, is guilty of an offence and
liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine not
    exceeding \$10,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one
    year or to both, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, to a fine not
    exceeding \$200,000; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine not
    exceeding \$5,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
    months or to both, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, to a fine not
    exceeding \$100,000.

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-352719 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption by Minister

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, on any
terms and conditions the Minister considers necessary, exempt any
person, vessel or marine facility from the application of any
regulation, security measure or security rule if, in the opinion of the
Minister, the exemption is in the public interest and is not likely to
affect marine security.

## [Confidentiality of Security Measures and Security Rules]{.HTitleText1} {#h-352722 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition against disclosure

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall disclose to any other person the substance of a security
  measure, security rule or proposed security rule unless the disclosure
  is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} authorized by the Minister;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ordered by a court or other body under section 14;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} required by any law; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} necessary to give effect to the measure or rule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes subsection (1) is guilty
  of an offence punishable on summary conviction and liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine not
    exceeding \$5,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
    months or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, to a fine not
    exceeding \$100,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court to inform Minister

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a request is made for the production or discovery of a security
  measure, security rule or proposed security rule in any proceeding
  before a court or other body having jurisdiction to compel its
  production or discovery, the court or other body shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the Minister of the request, if the Minister
    is not a party to the proceeding; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} examine the measure or rule in a hearing closed to
    the public and give the Minister a reasonable opportunity to make
    representations with respect to it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order for production and discovery

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the court or other body concludes that the public
  interest in the proper administration of justice outweighs the
  interests that would be protected by non-disclosure, the court or
  other body

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall order the production and discovery of the
    measure or rule, subject to any restrictions or conditions that the
    court or other body considers appropriate; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may require any person to give evidence relating to
    the measure or rule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security measures and security rules not
statutory instruments

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Security measures and
security rules are not statutory instruments for the purposes of the
[Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

## [Directions to Vessels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-352745 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security threats

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that a vessel is a
  threat to the security of any person or thing, including any goods,
  vessel or marine facility, the Minister may direct the vessel

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to proceed to a place specified by the Minister in
    accordance with any instructions the Minister may give regarding the
    route and manner of proceeding and to remain at the place until the
    Minister is satisfied that the security threat no longer exists;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to proceed out of Canada in accordance with any
    instructions the Minister may give regarding the route and manner of
    proceeding; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to remain outside Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inadequate security measures

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may direct that a vessel registered
  outside Canada remain outside Canada, proceed out of Canada or not
  enter or dock at a marine facility if the Minister has reasonable
  grounds to believe that the vessel or any persons or goods on board it
  have not been subjected to screening or other measures equivalent to
  the authorized screening or other measures that would apply under this
  Act if the vessel were registered in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions not statutory instruments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a direction issued under this
  section is not a statutory instrument for the purposes of the
  [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22), but no operator shall be
  found to have contravened the direction unless it is proved that, at
  the date of the alleged contravention, reasonable steps had been taken
  to bring the substance of the direction to the attention of the
  operator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence of notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A certificate appearing to be signed by the Minister,
  stating that a notice containing the direction was given to the
  operator of the vessel, is evidence that reasonable steps were taken
  to bring its substance to the attention of the operator.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences relating to directions

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** An operator of a vessel
that contravenes a direction is guilty of an offence and liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine not
    exceeding \$10,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one
    year or to both, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, to a fine not
    exceeding \$200,000; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine not
    exceeding \$5,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
    months or to both, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, to a fine not
    exceeding \$100,000.

## [Notices to Operators]{.HTitleText1} {#h-352766 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of giving notice

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A notice given to an
operator of a vessel or marine facility or to an agent of the operator
is effective with respect to that operator and any other operator of the
vessel or marine facility.

## [Screening]{.HTitleText1} {#h-352769 .Part}

Definition of [[designation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 19.1 to 19.8, [[designation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any
privilege accorded by a designation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1994, c. 40, s. 19
- 2001, c. 29, s. 57
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-19-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 19"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of screening officers

**[[19.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19.1 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
designate persons or classes of persons to act as screening officers for
the purposes of this Act or any of its provisions.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 29, s. 57
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to designate, etc.

- **[[19.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may refuse to designate
  a person as a screening officer under section 19.1, or may suspend,
  cancel or refuse to renew the designation of a person as a screening
  officer, if the Minister is of the opinion that the person is
  incompetent, does not meet the qualifications or fulfil the conditions
  required for the designation or ceases to meet the qualifications or
  fulfil the conditions of the designation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension or cancellation of
  designation for contraventions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may suspend or cancel the designation of
  a person as a screening officer if the Minister is of the opinion that
  the person has contravened this Act or any regulation, security
  measure or security rule, or any condition referred to in subsection
  10(6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension for immediate threat

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may suspend the designation of a person
  as a screening officer if the Minister is of the opinion that the
  exercise by the person of the functions of a screening officer
  constitutes, or is likely to constitute, an immediate threat to marine
  transportation security.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - 2001, c. 29, s. 57
  :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

- **[[19.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19.3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the Minister decides to refuse to designate a person as a screening
  officer or decides to suspend, cancel or refuse to renew a person's
  designation as a screening officer, the Minister shall notify the
  person of that decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice under subsection (1) shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the grounds for the Minister's decision; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the address at which, and the date, being thirty
    days after the notice is given, on or before which, the person may
    file a request for a review of the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date of suspension or

  effective date of the decision shall not be earlier than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a decision made under subsection
    19.2(1) or (3), the day that the notice was received by the person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a decision made under subsection
    19.2(2), the thirtieth day after the notice is given.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of request

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person not compelled to testify

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a decision made under subsection

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a decision made under subsection
    19.2(2), confirm the decision or substitute his or her own

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Screening before boarding

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  screening officer may require a person or any goods to undergo
  authorized screening before the person or goods come on board a vessel
  or enter a restricted area and, where so required,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person shall not board the vessel or enter the
    restricted area unless the person has undergone the authorized
    screening; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no person shall bring the goods on board the vessel
    or into the restricted area unless the goods have undergone the
    authorized screening.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Screening after boarding or in
  restricted areas

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A screening officer may require a person on board a
  vessel or in a restricted area to undergo authorized screening and, if
  the person refuses,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the officer may order the person to leave the
    vessel or restricted area and to remove from it any goods that the
    person took or had placed there; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person shall leave the vessel or restricted
    area and remove or permit the removal of the goods immediately or,
    in the case of a vessel that is not docked, at the first reasonable
    opportunity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unaccompanied goods

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A screening officer may carry out authorized
  screening of any goods that are received at a marine facility for
  transport on a vessel and are not accompanied by a person who may give
  permission for the screening and the officer may use any force that is
  reasonably necessary to gain access to the goods.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False or misleading information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every person who knowingly makes any false or
  misleading statement to a screening officer, or knowingly provides
  false or misleading information to a screening officer, is guilty of
  an offence punishable on summary conviction and liable to a fine not
  exceeding \$5,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
  months or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Circumventing authorized screening

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes subsection (2) or who
  wilfully circumvents authorized screening in any manner is guilty of
  an offence and liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine not
    exceeding \$10,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one
    year or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding
    \$5,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to
    both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operators to post notices

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  authorized screening is required or authorized on a vessel or at a
  marine facility, the operator of the vessel or facility shall post
  notices stating that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} authorized screening is being carried out;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no person is obliged to undergo authorized
    screening of their person if they choose not to board a vessel or
    enter a restricted area; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} no person is obliged to permit authorized screening
    of their goods if they choose not to have the goods placed on board
    the vessel or in the restricted area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Placement and languages of notices

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notices must be posted in prominent places where
  authorized screening is carried out and they must be written in both
  of the official languages of Canada and may, in addition, be written
  in any other language.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes this section is guilty
  of an offence punishable on summary conviction and liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine not
    exceeding \$1,000; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, to a fine not
    exceeding \$10,000.

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-352875 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of security inspectors

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate persons or classes of persons whom the Minister
  considers qualified to act as security inspectors for the purposes of
  this Act or any of its provisions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall furnish every security inspector
  with a certificate of designation as a security inspector and, on
  entering any place or inspecting anything, a security inspector shall
  show the certificate to the person apparently in charge of the place
  or thing if the person requests proof of the security inspector's
  designation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspection of vessels and marine
facilities

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of ensuring compliance with this Act and any regulation,
  security measure or security rule, a security inspector may at any
  reasonable time, but subject to subsection (3), board and inspect any
  vessel or enter and inspect any marine facility if the inspector is
  designated to inspect the vessel or facility.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of inspector

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When conducting the inspection, the security
  inspector may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} require the attendance of and question any person
    who the inspector considers will be able to assist in the
    inspection;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require any person to produce for inspection and
    copying any document that the inspector believes, on reasonable
    grounds, contains any information relevant to the administration of
    this Act or the regulation, security measure or security rule;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} seize anything found in the course of the
    inspection that the inspector believes, on reasonable grounds, will
    afford evidence with respect to an offence under this Act; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} detain any vessel that the inspector believes, on
    reasonable grounds, is a threat to the security of any person,
    goods, vessel or marine facility, until the inspector is satisfied
    that the vessel is no longer such a threat.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant required to enter
  dwelling-place

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A security inspector may not enter a dwelling-place
  except under the authority of a warrant or with the consent of the
  occupant or person apparently in charge of it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where on [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application
  a justice, as defined in section 2 of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46), is satisfied by information on oath that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conditions for entry described in
    subsection (1) exist in relation to a dwelling-place,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entry is necessary for any purpose relating to the
    administration or enforcement of this Act, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} entry has been refused or there are reasonable
    grounds for believing that entry will be refused,

  the justice may at any time sign and issue a warrant authorizing the
  security inspector named in the warrant to enter the dwelling-place
  subject to any conditions that may be specified in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The security inspector who executes the warrant shall
  not use force unless the inspector is accompanied by a peace officer
  and the use of force has been specifically authorized in the warrant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Search and seizure

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
sections 487 to 492 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) apply in
relation to this Act, but a security inspector may exercise the powers
of search and seizure provided in section 487 without a warrant if the
conditions for obtaining a warrant exist but the circumstances are such
that the delay necessary to obtain a warrant would result in danger to
human life or safety.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to inspectors

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of any vessel or marine facility boarded or entered by a
  security inspector who is carrying out functions under this Act, and
  every person found there, shall give the inspector all reasonable
  assistance in that person's power to enable the inspector to carry out
  those functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction of inspectors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a security inspector is carrying out functions
  under this Act, no person shall fail to comply with any reasonable
  request of the inspector or otherwise wilfully obstruct the inspector.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other prohibitions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} knowingly make any false or misleading statement or
    knowingly provide false or misleading information to a security
    inspector or other person carrying out functions under this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} wilfully destroy any record or document required to
    be kept under this Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} make a false entry in a record required to be kept
    under this Act with intent to mislead, or wilfully omit to make any
    entry in such a record;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} remove, alter or interfere in any way with anything
    seized by a security inspector, except with the inspector's
    permission; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} wilfully operate a vessel that has been detained
    under this Act, unless authorized to do so under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes this section is guilty
  of an offence and liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine not
      exceeding \$10,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one
      year or to both, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, to a fine not
      exceeding \$200,000; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine not
      exceeding \$5,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
      months or to both, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, to a fine not
      exceeding \$100,000.

## [General Provisions Relating to Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-352922 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offence

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Where an offence is
committed or continued on more than one day, the person who committed it
is liable to be convicted of a separate offence for each day on which
the offence is committed or continued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period for summary conviction
offences

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Proceedings by way of
summary conviction may be instituted at any time within, but not later
than, two years after the day on which the subject-matter of the
proceedings arose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence by employee or agent

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person is liable to be convicted of an offence committed by their
  employee or agent, whether or not the employee or agent has been
  identified, prosecuted or convicted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of operator of a vessel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator of a vessel is liable to be convicted of
  an offence committed in relation to the vessel by another person,
  whether or not the other person has been identified, prosecuted or
  convicted, but no person shall be convicted under this subsection if
  it is established that, without the operator's consent, the vessel was
  in the possession of another person at the time of the offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of operator of marine
  facility

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The operator of a marine facility is liable to be
  convicted of an offence committed with their consent by another person
  in relation to the facility, whether or not the other person has been
  identified, prosecuted or convicted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers, etc., of corporation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An officer, director or agent of a corporation that
  commits an offence under this Act is liable to be convicted of the
  offence if he or she directed, authorized, assented to, acquiesced in
  or participated in the commission of the offence, whether or not the
  corporation has been prosecuted or convicted.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1994, c. 40, s. 28
- 2001, c. 29, s. 58(F)
:::

:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, no
person is liable to be convicted of an offence if the person exercised
all due diligence to prevent its commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Things seized or detained

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Where a proceeding is
commenced in respect of an offence involving anything seized or detained
under this Act and it is detained until the final conclusion of the
proceeding, it must be returned to its lawful owner or to the person
lawfully entitled to its possession, unless that person has been
convicted of an offence under this Act, in which case it may be detained
until any fine imposed on conviction has been paid, or it may be sold in
satisfaction of the fine or any part of it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of fines

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a fine imposed on a person convicted of an offence is not paid when
  required, the conviction may be registered in the superior court of
  the province in which the trial was held and, when registered, has the
  same effect as if the conviction were a judgment of that court
  obtained by Her Majesty in right of Canada against the convicted
  person for a debt in the amount of the fine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of costs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All reasonable costs involved in registering the
  conviction are recoverable in the same way as if they were part of the
  fine.

### [Assurances of Compliance and Notices of Violation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-352953 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If reasonable grounds to believe a
violation

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that a person has committed
  a violation, the Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter into an assurance of compliance with the
    person that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} identifies the violation and provides that the
      person will comply with the provision to which the violation
      relates within the period, and be subject to the terms and
      conditions, specified in the assurance,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} sets out the amount and form of any security
      that, pending compliance with the assurance, must be deposited
      with the Minister, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} sets out the penalty, fixed by or within the
      range fixed by the regulations, that the person would have been
      liable to pay for the violation if the assurance had not been
      entered into; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} issue, and cause to be served on the person, a
    notice of violation that names the person, identifies the violation
    and sets out

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the penalty, fixed by or within the range fixed
      by the regulations, that the person is liable to pay for the
      violation,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the period, being thirty days after notice is
      served, within which the penalty must be paid or a review must be
      requested, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} particulars of the manner in which, and the
      address at which, the penalty must be paid or a review requested.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may extend the period specified under
  subparagraph (1)(a)(i) if the Minister is satisfied that the person is
  unable to comply with the assurance of compliance for reasons beyond
  the person's control.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short-form descriptions in notices of
  violation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may establish, in respect of each
  violation, a short-form description to be used in notices of
  violation.

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

  33(1)(a) is, unless a review is requested under subsection (2), deemed
  to have committed the violation in respect of which the assurance was
  entered into.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for review

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who enters into an assurance of compliance

  notice of default is served within that period under subsection 36(1),
  file a request with the Tribunal for a review of the facts of the

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** If the Minister is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} no further proceedings may be taken against the

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is liable to pay double the amount of

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the security deposited under subparagraph

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice under subsection (1) shall include the

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No set-off

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On the service of a notice of default, the person

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for review

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

  before the date specified in the notice or within any further time
  that the Tribunal on application allows, file a written request for a
  review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time and place for review

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a request filed under subsection (1),
  the Tribunal shall appoint a time and place for the review and shall
  notify the Minister and the person who filed the request of the time
  and place in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review procedure

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The member of the Tribunal assigned to conduct the
  review shall provide the Minister and the person who filed the request
  with an opportunity consistent with procedural fairness and natural
  justice to present evidence and make representations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Burden of proof

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The burden is on the Minister to establish that the
  person did not comply with the assurance of compliance referred to in
  the notice. The person is not required, and shall not be compelled, to
  give any evidence or testimony in the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain defences not available

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person does not have a defence by reason that the
  person exercised all due diligence to comply with the assurance of
  compliance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The member may confirm the Minister's decision or
  determine that the person has complied with the assurance of
  compliance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of security

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** Any security deposited

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person pays double the amount of the penalty set
  out in the assurance of compliance in accordance with a notice under
  subsection 36(1); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a member determines under subsection 37(6), or an
  appeal panel decides under subsection 40(3), that the assurance has
  been complied with.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 29, s. 59
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of violation

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person served with a notice of violation under paragraph 33(1)(b) must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pay the amount of the penalty; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within thirty days after being served or any
    further time that the Tribunal on application allows, file with the
    Tribunal a written request for a review of the facts of the
    violation or the amount of the penalty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When review not requested

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a review of the facts of the violation is not
  requested, the person is deemed to have committed the violation in
  respect of which the notice was served.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time and place for review

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a request filed under paragraph (1)(b),
  the Tribunal shall appoint a time and place for the review and shall
  notify the Minister and the person who filed the request of the time
  and place in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review procedure

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The member of the Tribunal assigned to conduct the
  review shall provide the Minister and the person who filed the request
  with an opportunity consistent with procedural fairness and natural
  justice to present evidence and make representations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Burden of proof

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The burden is on the Minister to establish that the
  person committed the violation referred to in the notice. The person
  is not required, and shall not be compelled, to give any evidence or
  testimony in the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The member may confirm the Minister's decision or,
  subject to any regulations made under paragraph 51(c), substitute his
  or her own determination.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 29, s. 59
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of appeal

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister or the person who requested the review of the decision may
  appeal a determination made under subsection 37(6) or 39(6) to the
  Tribunal within thirty days after the determination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loss of right of appeal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party that does not appear at a review hearing is
  not entitled to appeal a determination, unless they establish that
  there was sufficient reason to justify their absence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition of appeal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The appeal panel of the Tribunal assigned to hear the
  appeal may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a determination made under
    subsection 37(6), dismiss the appeal or allow the appeal and
    substitute its own decision; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a determination made under
    subsection 39(6), dismiss the appeal, or allow the appeal and,
    subject to regulations made under paragraph 51(c), substitute its
    own decision.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 29, s. 59
:::

### [Choice of Proceedings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-353044 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How contravention may be proceeded with

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** If a contravention can be
proceeded with as a violation or as an offence, the Minister may
commence proceedings in respect of that contravention as a violation or
recommend that it be proceeded with as an offence, but it may be
proceeded with only as one or the other.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 29, s. 59
:::

### [Recovery of Debts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-353049 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debts due to Her Majesty

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** The following amounts
constitute debts due to Her Majesty in right of Canada that may be
recovered in a court of competent jurisdiction:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} unless a review of the amount of a penalty is
  requested under subsection 39(1), the amount of the penalty, from the
  time the notice of violation that sets out the penalty is served;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of a penalty set out in a notice of
  default referred to in subsection 36(1), from the time the notice
  under that subsection is served;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount of a penalty determined by a member under
  section 39, or decided by an appeal panel under section 40, from the
  time of the respective determination or decision; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any reasonable expenses incurred in
  attempting to recover an amount referred to in any of paragraphs (a)
  to (c).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 29, s. 59
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All or
  part of a debt referred to in section 42 in respect of which there is
  a default of payment may be certified by the Minister or the Tribunal,
  as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration of certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On production to the Federal Court, a certificate
  shall be registered. When it is registered, a certificate has the same
  force and effect, and proceedings may be taken in connection with it,
  as if it were a judgment obtained in that Court for a debt of the
  amount specified in it and all reasonable costs and charges attendant
  on its registration.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 29, s. 59
:::

### [Rules of Law about Violations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-353065 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violations are not offences

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, a
violation is not an offence and, accordingly, section 126 of the
[Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) does not apply.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 29, s. 59
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, no
person is liable for the commission of a violation if the person
exercised all due diligence to prevent the commission.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 29, s. 59
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violation by employee or agent

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person is liable for a violation committed by their employee or agent,
  whether or not the employee or agent has been identified or proceeded
  against under sections 33 to 43.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of operator of a vessel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator of a vessel is liable for a violation
  committed in relation to the vessel by another person, whether or not
  the other person has been identified or proceeded against under
  sections 33 to 43 unless, at the time of the violation, the vessel was
  in the possession of another person without the operator's consent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of operator of marine
  facility

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The operator of a marine facility is liable for a
  violation committed with their consent by another person in relation
  to the facility, whether or not the other person has been identified
  or proceeded against under sections 33 to 43.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers, etc., of corporation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An officer, director or agent of a corporation that
  commits a violation under this Act is a party to and liable for the
  violation if he or she directed, authorized, assented to, acquiesced
  in or participated in the commission of the violation, whether or not
  the corporation has been identified or proceeded against under
  sections 33 to 43.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 29, s. 59
:::

### [General Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-353085 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notations removed

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  the Minister is of the opinion that it is not in the public interest
  to do so, five years after the last day on which any of the following
  events occurs, the Minister is to remove every notation of violation
  or default, and every suspension or cancellation of the designation of
  or refusal to designate or to renew the designation of a person as a
  screening officer on prescribed grounds, from any records that the
  Minister may keep respecting the person:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} payment by the person of every penalty that the
    person is liable to pay after a notice of violation, a notice of
    default, a determination of a member of the Tribunal on review or a
    decision of an appeal panel of the Tribunal; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the suspension or cancellation of the designation
    of, or the refusal to designate or to renew the designation of, the
    person as a screening officer under section 19.2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to notify

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When the Minister is of the opinion that removal of a
  notation is not in the public interest, the Minister shall give notice
  of that fact to the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice under subsection (2) shall provide all
  relevant information concerning the grounds on which the Minister has
  refused to remove the notation and shall include the address at which,
  and the date, being thirty days after the notice is served, on or
  before which, a request for a review may be filed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 37(1) to (3) apply in respect of a
  refusal to remove a notation, with any modifications that the
  circumstances require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The member of the Tribunal assigned to conduct the
  review may confirm the Minister's decision or refer the matter back to
  the Minister for reconsideration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of appeal

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The person may, within thirty days after a
  determination is made under subsection (5), appeal the determination
  to the Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loss of right of appeal

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If the person does not appear at the review hearing,
  the person is not entitled to appeal a determination unless they
  establish that there was sufficient reason to justify their absence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition of appeal

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The appeal panel of the Tribunal assigned to hear the
  appeal may dismiss the appeal or refer the matter back to the Minister
  for reconsideration.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 29, s. 59
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public record

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall keep a
public record of notations of violations or default that appear on any
record that the Minister may keep respecting persons under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 29, s. 59
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** No notice of violation
may be issued more than two years after the Minister becomes aware of
the violation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 29, s. 59
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of Minister

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** A document that purports
to have been issued by the Minister and certifies the day on which the
Minister became aware of a violation is evidence, without proof of the
signature or official character of the person appearing to have signed
the document, that the Minister became aware of the violation on that
day.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 29, s. 59
:::

### [Regulations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-353119 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} designating, as a violation that may be proceeded
  with in accordance with sections 33 to 46, 49 and 50, the
  contravention of a provision that is an offence under this Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designating violations that, if continued on more
  than one day, constitute a separate violation for each day on which
  they are continued;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} designating violations that may be proceeded with by
  issuing notices of violation and fixing a penalty or a range of
  penalties in respect of each such violation, up to a maximum
  of \$25,000 but in any event not greater than the maximum fine that
  would be payable if the violation were proceeded with by way of
  summary conviction; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that by subsections 33 to 50 is
  to be prescribed.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 29, s. 59
:::
