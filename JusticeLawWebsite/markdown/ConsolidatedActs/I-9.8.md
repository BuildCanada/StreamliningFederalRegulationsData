## Injured Military Members Compensation Act {#injured-military-members-compensation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2003, c. 14
:::

Assented to 2003-06-19

An Act to compensate military members injured during service
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-333647 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Injured Military Members Compensation Act](/eng/acts/I-9.8).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-333650 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Class "A" Reserve Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class "A" Reserve Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
    meaning as in article 9.06 of the Queen's Regulations and Orders for
    the Canadian Forces. ([service de réserve de classe
    « A »]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Class "B" Reserve Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class "B" Reserve Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
    meaning as in article 9.07 of the Queen's Regulations and Orders for
    the Canadian Forces. ([service de réserve de classe
    « B »]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Class "C" Reserve Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class "C" Reserve Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
    meaning as in article 9.08 of the Queen's Regulations and Orders for
    the Canadian Forces. ([service de réserve de classe
    « C »]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Defence. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[personal information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[personal information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in section 3 of the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21). ([renseignements
    personnels]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-333658 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation by Minister

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Minister may designate any person to exercise
  any power or perform any duty or function that may be exercised or
  performed by the Minister under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister has designated a person to make a
  decision under this Act, the Minister may not designate that same
  person to review the decision under subsection 11(1).

## [Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-333664 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of compensation

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who, while serving as a member of the regular force, or a member of
  the reserve force performing Class "B" Reserve Service for more than
  180 days or Class "C" Reserve Service, suffered an injury that
  resulted in a loss set out in column 1 of the schedule during the
  corresponding period set out in column 2 is entitled, on application,
  to the corresponding benefit set out in column 3 if the Minister is
  satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the injury is attributable to the person's service
    with the Canadian Forces;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the loss resulted directly from the injury;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the loss was not caused directly or indirectly by a
    self-inflicted injury, even if caused by insanity, or by the
    person's improper conduct, including their wilful disobedience of an
    order and vicious or criminal conduct;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person survived for a period of at least 30
    days after the injury;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the loss occurred within 90 days after the injury;
    and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the person was not entitled to a lump-sum payment
    in respect of the injury under an insurance plan provided by the
    Government of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who, while serving as a member of the
  reserve force performing Class "B" Reserve Service for 180 days or
  less or Class "A" Reserve Service, suffered an injury that resulted in
  a loss set out in column 1 of the schedule during the corresponding
  period set out in column 2 is entitled, on application, to the
  corresponding benefit set out in column 4 if the Minister is satisfied
  that the conditions in paragraphs (1)(a) to (f) are met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (1) and (2) and the
  schedule, the loss of a hand or foot, or thumb and index finger,
  includes the loss of their use, and the loss of hearing or speech or
  of sight in one eye or both eyes means total and irrecoverable loss.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The total benefits payable
to a person in respect of an injury that may result in one or more
losses shall not exceed \$250,000 for a person referred to in subsection
4(1) or \$100,000 for a person referred to in subsection 4(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No benefit payable

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No benefit is payable for
any loss caused wholly or partly, or directly or indirectly, by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} disease, bodily or mental infirmity or the medical
  treatment of the disease or infirmity; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} ptomaines or bacterial infections, except an
  infection that is caused by the injury.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application made to Minister

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person referred to in section 4 may make an application to the
  Minister for a benefit in the manner required by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deceased member

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the person died before the coming into force of
  this Act, or dies after the coming into force of this Act without
  making an application, the person's estate or succession may apply for
  the benefit and has the same rights and obligations that the person
  would have had under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may require the applicant to provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any documentation necessary to substantiate their
    claim;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any relevant information; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an affidavit or statutory declaration attesting to
    the truth of the information provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration of application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall consider an application without
  delay after its receipt and, in considering the application, may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} investigate the facts stated in the application and
    any other matter related to the application; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} collect relevant material and information from the
    records of departments and agencies of the Government of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} After considering the application, the Minister shall
  approve the payment of the benefit if the Minister is satisfied that
  the applicant is entitled to a benefit, or refuse to approve the
  payment of the benefit in any other case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision and reasons

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall notify the applicant of any
  decision made under subsection (5) and of the reasons for any refusal
  to approve the payment of a benefit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefit of doubt

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** In making a decision under
this Act, the Minister shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} draw from the circumstances of the case, and the
  evidence presented to the Minister, every reasonable inference in
  favour of the applicant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} accept any uncontradicted evidence presented to the
  Minister by the applicant that the Minister considers to be credible
  in the circumstances; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} resolve in favour of the applicant any doubt, in the
  weighing of evidence, as to whether the applicant has established a
  case.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decisions shall be made expeditiously

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Decisions of the Minister
shall be made as informally and expeditiously as the circumstances and
considerations of fairness permit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Inquiries Act](/eng/acts/I-11)

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister has all the powers of a commissioner appointed under Part II
  of the [Inquiries Act](/eng/acts/I-11) for the purpose of carrying out
  the functions of the Minister under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taking oaths, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any officer or employee of the Department of National
  Defence or officer of the Canadian Forces, if designated by the
  Minister for the purpose, may, in the course of their employment or
  service, administer oaths and take and receive affidavits,
  declarations and solemn affirmations for the purpose of or incidental
  to the administration of this Act, and every person so designated has,
  with respect to any such oath, affidavit, declaration or affirmation,
  all the powers of a commissioner for administering oaths or taking
  affidavits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acceptance of oaths, etc.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may accept, for the purpose of the
  administration of this Act, any oath administered or any affidavit,
  declaration or solemn affirmation taken or received by any person who
  has the powers of a commissioner for taking affidavits and who is an
  officer or employee of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a department in, or other portion of, the federal
    public administration specified in Schedule I, IV or V to the
    [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a department of the government of a province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for review

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant who is dissatisfied with a decision made by the Minister
  under this Act may request a review of the decision by the Minister.
  After receiving the request, the Minister shall review the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review on Minister's motion

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, on the Minister's own motion,
  review a decision made under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confirmation, amendment or rescinding

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} After reviewing the decision, the Minister may either
  confirm the decision or amend or rescind the decision if the Minister
  determines that there was an error with respect to any finding of fact
  or the interpretation of any law, or if new evidence is presented to
  the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision is final

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A decision of the Minister respecting a review is
  final and binding and, except for judicial review under the [Federal
  Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7), is not subject to appeal or to review by
  any court. The decision shall not be the subject of a grievance under
  section 29 of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2003, c. 14, ss. 11, 21

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation of applicant

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** In all matters under this
Act, an applicant may, at their own expense, be represented by a
representative of their choice.

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-333733 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information that shall be made available
to Minister

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The following personal
information relating to the person who sustained the injury shall, if
requested by the Minister, be made available to the Minister to
determine the applicant's eligibility for a benefit:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} personal information collected or obtained by the
  Department of Veterans Affairs in the administration of the [Pension
  Act](/eng/acts/P-6) or Part 3 of the [Veterans Well-being
  Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} personal information collected or obtained by the
  Department of Transport in the administration of the [Aeronautics
  Act](/eng/acts/A-2), the [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9) or the
  [Canada Shipping Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) or any predecessor
  enactment relating to the same subject-matter; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} personal information collected or obtained by the
  Library and Archives of Canada in the administration of the [Library
  and Archives of Canada Act](/eng/acts/L-7.7), or any predecessor
  enactment relating to the same subject-matter.

- 2017, c. 20, s. 292

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information that Minister may disclose

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Personal information that
has been collected or obtained by the Minister in the administration of
this Act may be disclosed by the Minister

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to any person or body, to the extent that the
  disclosure is necessary in order for the Minister to obtain
  information required for the administration of this Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to any officer or employee of the Department of
  National Defence or any member of the Canadian Forces, to the extent
  that the disclosure is required for the administration of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Social Insurance Numbers

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** If a Social Insurance
Number has been used to identify the service or medical records of a
member of the Canadian Forces, the Minister or other authority having
custody of those records may use the Social Insurance Number for the
purpose of making those records available.

Definition of [[overpayment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[overpayment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a benefit, or
  part of a benefit, that was paid to a person and to which the person
  had no entitlement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of overpayment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, through any cause, an overpayment is paid to a
  person, the overpayment is a debt due to Her Majesty by that person,
  and the Minister may recover the debt

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with section 155 of the [Financial
    Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by proceedings in any court of competent
    jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission of overpayment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person has received or obtained an overpayment,
  the Minister may, unless that person has been convicted of an offence
  under the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) in connection with the
  receiving or obtaining of the overpayment, remit all or any portion of
  the overpayment if the Minister is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the overpayment cannot be recovered within the
    reasonably foreseeable future;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the administrative costs of recovering the
    overpayment are likely to equal or exceed the amount to be
    recovered;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} repayment of the overpayment would cause undue
    hardship to the person; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the overpayment is the result of an administrative
    error, delay or oversight on the part of a public servant or a
    member of the Canadian Forces.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** No action or other
proceeding lies against any person for anything done or said in good
faith in the administration of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 25 of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6), the payment of benefits
under this Act is deemed not to be a compensation plan established by
any other legislation of a similar nature.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefit not taxed

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  benefit paid under this Act is not subject to tax under any Act of
  Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from seizure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A benefit payable under this Act is exempt from
  seizure.
