## British Columbia Indian Cut-off Lands Settlement Act {#british-columbia-indian-cut-off-lands-settlement-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1984, c. 2
:::

Assented to 1984-02-23

An Act to provide for the settlement of claims by Indian bands in
British Columbia relating to certain lands cut off from their reserves
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33759 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [British Columbia Indian Cut-off Lands Settlement
Act](/eng/acts/B-8.3).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33762 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in the [Indian
    Act](/eng/acts/I-5); ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[council of the band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[council of the band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5); ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[cut-off lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cut-off lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means land referred to in
    section 2(a) of the McKenna-McBride Agreement that had before 1916
    been set aside for the use and benefit of Indians but that, pursuant
    to

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Indian Affairs Settlement Act, chapter 32 of
      the Statutes of British Columbia, 1919,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the British Columbia Indian Lands Settlement Act,
      chapter 51 of the Statutes of Canada, 1920, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the report of the Royal Commission on Indian
      Affairs for the Province of British Columbia of June 30, 1916, as
      approved by Canada Order in Council 1265 of July 19, 1924 and
      British Columbia Order in Council 911 of July 26, 1923,

    ceased to be so set aside, but does not include any lands in the
    Railway Belt or Peace River Block; ([terres
    retranchées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[McKenna-McBride Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[McKenna-McBride Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    agreement signed in Victoria, British Columbia on September 24, 1912
    and entitled "Memorandum of an Agreement arrived at between J.A.J.
    McKenna, Special Commissioner appointed by the Dominion Government
    to investigate the condition of Indian Affairs in British Columbia,
    and the Honourable Sir Richard McBride, as Premier of the Province
    of British Columbia". ([Convention McKenna-McBride]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33773 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements re cut-off lands settlements

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A band,
  or the council of the band, may enter into an agreement with Her
  Majesty in right of Canada and Her Majesty in right of British
  Columbia for the purposes of resolving and extinguishing claims in
  respect of cut-off lands asserted by the band and the council of the
  band

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} against Her Majesty in right of British Columbia;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} against Her Majesty in right of British Columbia
    and Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement binding on band members and
  council

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a band or the council of the band enters into
  an agreement described in subsection (1), that agreement binds all
  existing and future members of the band and the council of the band
  and its successors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Negotiations not an admission of legal
obligation

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Negotiations toward an
agreement described in subsection 3(1), whether or not those
negotiations result in such an agreement, do not constitute an admission
by Her Majesty in right of Canada of the existence of any legal
obligation of Her Majesty in right of Canada toward the band in
question.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing agreements approved

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Any agreement described in
subsection 3(1) executed prior to the day this Act comes into force is
hereby approved and declared to have effect as of the date of its
execution.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of future agreements

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where an agreement
described in subsection 3(1) is executed on or after the day this Act
comes into force, the Governor in Council may by order approve such
agreement and declare it to have effect as of the date of its execution.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33787 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other claims not affected

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act affects
any claims asserted by a band or the council of the band other than
claims in respect of cut-off lands.
