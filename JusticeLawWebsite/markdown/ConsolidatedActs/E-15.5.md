## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-185140 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Expenditure Restraint Act](/eng/acts/E-15.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-185143 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[additional remuneration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[additional remuneration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any allowance,
    bonus, differential or premium or any payment to employees that is
    similar to any of those payments. ([rémunération
    additionnelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[arbitral award]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[arbitral award]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an arbitral award
    governing employees to whom this Act applies. ([décision
    arbitrale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bargaining agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bargaining agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as in subsection 3(1) of the [Canada Labour
      Code](/eng/acts/L-2), in relation to employees to whom Part I of
      that Act applies;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as in section 3 of the [Parliamentary Employment
      and Staff Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-1.3), in relation to
      employees to whom that Act applies; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} as in subsection 2(1) of the Public Service
      Labour Relations Act, in relation to employees to whom that Act
      applies. ([agent négociateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[collective agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[collective agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a collective
    agreement governing employees to whom this Act applies. ([convention
    collective]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Her Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Her Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Her Majesty in right of
    Canada. ([Sa Majesté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[National Joint Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[National Joint Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in subsection 4(1) of the Public Service Labour Relations
    Act. ([Conseil national mixte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[rate of pay]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[rate of pay]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a base rate of pay,
    whether expressed as a single rate of pay or a range of rates of pay
    --- or, if no such rate or range exists, any fixed or ascertainable
    amount of base pay --- but does not include any additional
    remuneration. ([taux de salaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[restraint period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[restraint period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period that
    begins on April 1, 2006 and ends on March 31, 2011. ([période de
    contrôle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed bonus

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of this
Act, any lump sum that an employer is required by an arbitral award to
pay to employees is deemed to be a bonus.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National Joint Council recommendations

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Any reference in this Act
to additional remuneration does not include any additional remuneration
that is provided for by a directive, policy, regulation, agreement or
other instrument issued or made

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the recommendation of the National Joint Council
  and with the employer's approval; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} unilaterally by an employer in respect of a subject
  matter that, in the opinion of the Treasury Board, is the same as or
  is related to the subject matter of any instrument made in accordance
  with paragraph (a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When certain collective agreements are
deemed to have been entered into

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of this Act, a collective agreement is deemed to have been
  entered into before December 8, 2008 if it was actually entered into
  on or after that date but its parties had, before that date, agreed in
  writing to enter into it with effect on the expiry of a previous
  collective agreement and they entered into it without alteration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When provisions of certain terms and
  conditions of employment are deemed to have been established

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If subsection (1) applies in respect of a collective
  agreement and terms and conditions of employment were established on
  or after December 8, 2008, the provisions of those terms and
  conditions of employment that are identical in all material respects
  to those of the collective agreement and that are applicable to
  non-represented and excluded employees that normally have terms and
  conditions of employment that are similar to those of the employees
  governed by the collective agreement are, for the purposes of this
  Act, deemed to have been made before December 8, 2008.

## [Effects of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-185168 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to bargain collectively

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the other
provisions of this Act, the right to bargain collectively under the
[Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2), the [Parliamentary Employment and
Staff Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-1.3) and the Public Service Labour
Relations Act is continued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to strike

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act affects
the right to strike under the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2) or the
Public Service Labour Relations Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments permitted

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act
precludes the bargaining agent for employees governed by a collective
agreement or arbitral award and the employer of those employees from
amending, by agreement in writing, any provision of the collective
agreement or arbitral award, other than a provision relating to its
term, so long as the amendment is not contrary to any provision of this
Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Workplace improvements

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act
precludes the co-development of workplace improvements by bargaining
agents and employers under the auspices of the National Joint Council or
any other body that they may agree on.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incremental and merit increases

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act is to
be construed as precluding the entitlement of any employee to
incremental increases --- including any based on the attainment of
further qualifications or the acquisition of further skills --- or to
merit or performance increases, in-range increases, performance bonuses
or similar forms of compensation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflicts with other Acts

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** In the event of a
conflict between a provision of this Act and a provision of any other
Act of Parliament, including a provision in Part X of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the provision of this Act prevails
to the extent of the conflict, unless the other Act expressly declares
that it or any of its provisions apply despite this Act.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-185181 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members of Parliament

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** This Act applies to
members of the Senate and the House of Commons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employees

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Act applies to employees who are employed in or by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the departments and other portions of the federal
    public administration named in Schedules I and IV, respectively, to
    the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) and the separate
    agencies named in Schedule V to that Act, other than the Financial
    Consumer Agency of Canada and the Staff of the Non-Public Funds,
    Canadian Forces;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Crown corporations and public bodies named in
    Schedule 1; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Senate, the House of Commons, the Library of
    Parliament, the office of the Senate Ethics Officer, the office of
    the Conflict of Interest and Ethics Commissioner, the Parliamentary
    Protective Service and the office of the Parliamentary Budget
    Officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members of the Royal Canadian Mounted
  Police

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, members of the Royal Canadian
  Mounted Police are employees.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed employees

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} This Act applies to the following persons, who are
  deemed to be employees for the purposes of this Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the staff of members of the Senate and the House of
    Commons;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} directors of the Crown corporations and public
    bodies named in Schedule 1;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} officers and non-commissioned members of the
    Canadian Forces; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Chief Electoral Officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons appointed by Governor in
  Council

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} This Act applies to persons who are appointed by the
  Governor in Council, and those persons are deemed to be employees for
  the purposes of this Act. Despite this subsection, this Act does not
  apply to lieutenant governors, judges who are paid a salary under the
  [Judges Act](/eng/acts/J-1), military judges appointed under section
  165.21 of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) and associate
  judges appointed under section 12 of the [Federal Courts
  Act](/eng/acts/F-7).

## [Restraint Measures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-185206 .Part}

### [Increases to Rates of Pay]{.HTitleText2} {#h-185207 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Increases to rates of pay

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Despite any collective
agreement, arbitral award or terms and conditions of employment to the
contrary, but subject to the other provisions of this Act, the rates of
pay for employees are to be increased, or are deemed to have been
increased, as the case may be, by the following percentages for any
12-month period that begins during any of the following fiscal years:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the 2006--2007 fiscal year, 2.5%;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the 2007--2008 fiscal year, 2.3%;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the 2008--2009 fiscal year, 1.5%;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the 2009--2010 fiscal year, 1.5%; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the 2010--2011 fiscal year, 1.5%.

### [Employees Represented by a Bargaining Agent]{.HTitleText2} {#h-185215 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Increases to rates of pay --- collective
agreements or arbitral awards after coming into force

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  provisions of any collective agreement that is entered into, or
  arbitral award that is made, after the day on which this Act comes
  into force may not provide for increases to rates of pay that are
  greater than those set out in section 16, but they may provide for
  increases that are lower.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}12-month periods

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, any collective agreement that
  is entered into, or any arbitral award that is made, after the day on
  which this Act comes into force and that provides for increases to
  rates of pay for any period that begins during the restraint period
  must do so on the basis of a 12-month period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Increases to rates of pay --- collective
agreements and arbitral awards --- December 8, 2008 until coming into
force

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The provisions of any
collective agreement that is entered into, or any arbitral award that is
made, during the period that begins on December 8, 2008 and ends on the
day on which this Act comes into force that provide, for any particular
period, for increases to rates of pay that are greater than those
referred to in section 16 for that particular period are of no effect or
are deemed never to have had effect, as the case may be, and are deemed
to be provisions that provide for the increases referred to in section
16.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Increases to rates of pay --- collective
agreements and arbitral awards --- before December 8, 2008

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** With respect to a
collective agreement that is entered into, or an arbitral award that is
made, before December 8, 2008,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} section 16 does not apply in respect of any period
  that began during the 2006--2007 or 2007--2008 fiscal year; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for any 12-month period that begins during any of the
  2008--2009, 2009--2010 and 2010--2011 fiscal years, section 16 applies
  only in respect of periods that begin on or after December 8, 2008 and
  any provisions of those agreements or awards that provide, for any
  particular period, for increases to rates of pay that are greater than
  those referred to in section 16 for that particular period are of no
  effect or are deemed never to have had effect, as the case may be, and
  are deemed to be provisions that provide for the increases referred to
  in section 16.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other than 12-month periods --- section
18

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** If a collective agreement
or arbitral award to which section 18 applies provides for an increase
to rates of pay for a period of other than 12 months that begins during
any particular fiscal year in the restraint period, that increase is of
no effect or is deemed never to have had effect, as the case may be, and
is deemed to be an increase for that period of other than 12 months,
determined on an annualized basis to the nearest 1/100%, that provides
for the increase referred to in section 16 for a period that begins
during that particular fiscal year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other than 12-month periods --- section
19

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** If a collective agreement
or arbitral award to which section 19 applies provides for an increase
to rates of pay for a period of other than 12 months that begins during
any particular fiscal year that begins during the period that begins on
December 8, 2008 and ends on March 31, 2011, that increase is of no
effect or is deemed never to have had effect, as the case may be, and is
deemed to be an increase for that period of other than 12 months,
determined on an annualized basis to the nearest 1/100%, that provides
for the increase referred to in section 16 for a period that begins
during that particular fiscal year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lower percentages not affected

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** If a collective agreement
or arbitral award to which section 18 or 19 applies provides for an
increase to the rates of pay for any particular period that is lower
than the increase referred to in section 16 for that period, section 16
does not apply in respect of that increase.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restructuring prohibited

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 31 to
34,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} no provision of a collective agreement that is
  entered into, or of an arbitral award that is made, after the day on
  which this Act comes into force may provide for the restructuring of
  rates of pay during any period that begins during the restraint
  period;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any provision of a collective agreement that is
  entered into, or of an arbitral award that is made, during the period
  that begins on December 8, 2008 and ends on the day on which this Act
  comes into force that provides for the restructuring of rates of pay
  during any period that begins during the restraint period is of no
  effect or is deemed never to have had effect, as the case may be; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any provision of a collective agreement that is
  entered into, or of an arbitral award that is made, before December 8,
  2008 that provides for the restructuring of rates of pay during any
  period that begins during the period that begins on December 8, 2008
  and ends on March 31, 2011 is of no effect or is deemed never to have
  had effect, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No increases to additional remuneration
--- after coming into force

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** No collective agreement
that is entered into, or arbitral award that is made, after the day on
which this Act comes into force may provide, for any period that begins
during the restraint period, for any increase to the amount or rate of
any additional remuneration that applied to the employees governed by
the collective agreement or the arbitral award immediately before the
collective agreement, or the arbitral award, as the case may be, becomes
effective.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No increases to additional remuneration
--- December 8, 2008 until coming into force

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** If a collective agreement
that is entered into, or arbitral award that is made, at any time during
the period that begins on December 8, 2008 and ends on the day on which
this Act comes into force contains provisions that provide, for any
period that begins during the restraint period, for an increase to the
amount or rate of any additional remuneration that applied to the
employees governed by the collective agreement or the arbitral award
immediately before the collective agreement, or the arbitral award, as
the case may be, became effective, those provisions are of no effect or
are deemed never to have had effect, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No increases to additional remuneration
--- before December 8, 2008

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** If a collective agreement
that is entered into, or an arbitral award that is made, before December
8, 2008 contains provisions that, for any period that begins in the
period that begins on December 8, 2008 and ends on March 31, 2011,
provide for an increase to the amount or rate of any additional
remuneration that applied to the employees governed by the collective
agreement or the arbitral award immediately before the first period that
began on or after December 8, 2008, those provisions are of no effect or
are deemed never to have had effect, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No new additional remuneration --- after
coming into force

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** No collective agreement
that is entered into, or arbitral award that is made, after the day on
which this Act comes into force may provide, for any period that begins
during the restraint period, for any additional remuneration that is new
in relation to the additional remuneration that applied to the employees
governed by the collective agreement or the arbitral award immediately
before the collective agreement or the arbitral award, as the case may
be, becomes effective.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No new additional remuneration ---
December 8, 2008 to coming into force

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** If a collective agreement
that is entered into, or an arbitral award that is made, at any time
during the period that begins on December 8, 2008 and ends on the day on
which this Act comes into force contains a provision that provides, for
any period that begins during the restraint period, for any additional
remuneration to the employees governed by the collective agreement or
the arbitral award that is new in relation to the additional
remuneration that applied to the employees governed by the collective
agreement or the arbitral award, as the case may be, immediately before
it became effective, that provision is of no effect or is deemed never
to have had effect, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No new additional remuneration --- before
December 8, 2008

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** If a collective agreement
that is entered into, or an arbitral award that is made, before December
8, 2008 contains a provision that provides, for any period that begins
in the period that begins on December 8, 2008 and ends on March 31,
2011, for any additional remuneration to the employees governed by the
collective agreement or the arbitral award that is new in relation to
the additional remuneration that applied to the employees governed by
the collective agreement or arbitral award, as the case may be,
immediately before the first period that began on or after December 8,
2008, that provision is of no effect or is deemed never to have had
effect, as the case may be.

collective agreement that is entered into --- or of an arbitral award
that is made, or of terms and conditions of employment that are
established --- before December 8, 2008 changes, for any period that
begins in the period that begins on December 8, 2008 and ends on March
31, 2011, the performance pay plans, including the amounts or rates,
that apply to any employees governed by the agreement, award or terms
and conditions of employment, the change is of no effect or is deemed
never to have had effect, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Royal Canadian Mounted Police

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** Despite sections 44 to
49, the Treasury Board may change the amount or rate of any allowance,
or make any new allowance, applicable to members of the Royal Canadian
Mounted Police if the Treasury Board is of the opinion that the change
or the new allowance, as the case may be, is critical to support
transformation initiatives relating to the Royal Canadian Mounted
Police.

## [Administration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-185389 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers and duties of Treasury Board

- **[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Treasury Board may exercise the powers and shall perform the duties in
  relation to this Act that are necessary to enable it to determine
  whether an employer of employees, other than employees referred to in
  paragraph 13(1)(c) or (3)(a), is complying with this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information and documentation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Treasury Board may require from the employer any
  information and documentation that it considers necessary to enable it
  to determine whether the employer is complying with this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Treasury Board directive

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Treasury Board determines under this section
  that the employer is not complying with this Act, it may issue any
  directives that it considers appropriate to ensure the compliance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt due to Her Majesty

- **[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  amount paid --- including amounts paid before the day on which this
  Act comes into force --- to any person in excess of the amount that
  should have been paid as a result of this Act is a debt due to Her
  Majesty and may be recovered as such.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Overpayment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any amount that is a debt due to Her Majesty as a
  result of subsection (1) is deemed to be an overpayment to which
  subsection 155(3) of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11) applies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsection (1) applies to, but
  is not limited to, the following amounts:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} amounts paid under a provision that by the
    operation of this Act is of no effect or is deemed never to have had
    effect; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} amounts paid as a result of the payment of any
    amount referred to in paragraph (a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, on the recommendation of the Treasury Board, by order, amend
Schedule 1 by adding to or deleting from it the name of any Crown
corporation or public body.
