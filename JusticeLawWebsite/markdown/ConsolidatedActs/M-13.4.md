of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-353992 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Museums Act](/eng/acts/M-13.4).

## [Interpretation and Declaration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-353995 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to a museum, means the
    Board of Trustees of that museum; ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to a museum, means
    such member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada as may be
    designated by the Governor in Council as the Minister responsible
    for that museum for the purposes of this
    Act; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[museum]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[museum]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation established by
    Part I; ([musée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[museum material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[museum material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means objects and
    documentary material, regardless of medium or form, of a type
    normally kept by a museum for reference or exhibition
    purposes. ([matériel de musée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- 1990, c. 3, s. 2
- 2008, c. 9, s. 1

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** It is hereby declared that
the heritage of Canada and all its peoples is an important part of the
world heritage and must be preserved for present and future generations
and that each museum established by this Act

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} plays an essential role, individually and together
  with other museums and like institutions, in preserving and promoting
  the heritage of Canada and all its peoples throughout Canada and
  abroad and in contributing to the collective memory and sense of
  identity of all Canadians; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is a source of inspiration, research, learning and
  entertainment that belongs to all Canadians and provides, in both
  official languages, a service that is essential to Canadian culture
  and available to all.

### [Establishment of the National Gallery of Canada]{.HTitleText2} {#h-354009 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a corporation, to be called the National Gallery of
  Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affiliated museums

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The National Gallery of Canada shall include the
  Canadian Museum of Contemporary Photography and such other affiliated
  museums as the Board of the National Gallery of Canada may, by by-law,
  establish with the approval of the Governor in Council.

### [Purposes, Capacity and Powers of the National Gallery of Canada]{.HTitleText2} {#h-354015 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purposes

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The purposes of the
National Gallery of Canada are to develop, maintain and make known,
throughout Canada and internationally, a collection of works of art,
both historic and contemporary, with special but not exclusive reference
to Canada, and to further knowledge, understanding and enjoyment of art
in general among all Canadians.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity and powers

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  furtherance of its purposes, the National Gallery of Canada has the
  capacity and, subject to this Act, the rights, powers and privileges
  of a natural person and in particular, but without limiting the
  generality of the foregoing, the National Gallery of Canada may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} collect works of art and other museum material;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} document, record, preserve, conserve and restore
    works of art and other museum material;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sell, exchange, give away, destroy or otherwise
    dispose of works of art and other museum material in its collection
    and use any revenue obtained therefrom to further its collection;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} lend or borrow works of art and other museum
    material on long-term or short-term loan;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} organize, sponsor, arrange for and participate in
    travelling exhibitions, in Canada and internationally, of works of
    art and other museum material in its collection and from other
    sources;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} undertake and sponsor any research, including
    fundamental or basic research and theoretical and applied research,
    related to its purposes and to museology, and communicate the
    results of that research;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} provide facilities to permit qualified individuals
    to use and study its collection;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} promote knowledge of and disseminate information
    about works of art and other museum material in its collection and
    art in general, throughout Canada and internationally, by such means
    of education and communication as are appropriate;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} establish and foster liaison with other
    organizations with purposes similar to its purposes;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} share the expertise of its staff by undertaking and
    sponsoring programs for training and apprenticeship in the
    professional and technical skills involved in the operation of other
    organizations with purposes similar to its purposes;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} provide and arrange for professional and technical
    services to other organizations with purposes similar to its
    purposes;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} acquire property by gift, bequest or otherwise and
    hold in trust or otherwise, expend, invest, administer and dispose
    of that property;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} develop, operate and maintain branches or
    exhibition centres;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} operate restaurants, lounges, parking facilities,
    shops and other facilities for the use of the public;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} lease or otherwise make available any of its
    facilities to other persons; and

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} charge for goods, services and admission and use
    the revenue obtained therefrom for its own purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The National Gallery of Canada may not deal with
  property otherwise than in accordance with the terms, if any, on which
  it was acquired or is held.

### [Purpose, Capacity and Powers of the Canadian Museum of Nature]{.HTitleText2} {#h-354085 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of the
Canadian Museum of Nature is to increase, throughout Canada and
internationally, interest in, knowledge of and appreciation and respect
for the natural world by establishing, maintaining and developing for
research and posterity a collection of natural history objects, with
special but not exclusive reference to Canada, and by demonstrating the
natural world, the knowledge derived from it and the understanding it
represents.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity and powers

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  furtherance of its purpose, the Canadian Museum of Nature has the
  capacity and, subject to this Act, the rights, powers and privileges
  of a natural person and in particular, but without limiting the
  generality of the foregoing, the Canadian Museum of Nature may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} collect natural history objects and other museum
    material;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintain its collection by preservation,
    conservation and restoration and the establishment of records and
    documentation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sell, exchange, give away, destroy or otherwise
    dispose of natural history objects and other museum material, other
    than primary type specimens, in its collection and use any revenue
    obtained therefrom to further its collection;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} exchange with, lend to or borrow from any other
    organization or person any exhibits, museum material, knowledge or
    information;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} organize, sponsor, arrange for and participate in
    travelling exhibitions, in Canada and internationally, of natural
    history objects and other museum material in its collection and from
    other sources;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} undertake and sponsor any research, including
    fundamental or basic research and theoretical and applied research,
    in the natural sciences, with special but not exclusive emphasis on
    its collection, and in museology, and communicate the results of
    that research;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} provide facilities to permit qualified individuals
    to use and study its collection;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} create new knowledge and synthesize existing
    knowledge into new patterns and models and provide a taxonomic data
    base for biological and geological research;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} foster the coordination of collection-based
    research in natural history in Canada;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} promote scientific literacy and a better
    understanding of nature by demonstrating the interrelationships
    among all organisms, including human beings, and their environment;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} inform the academic, scientific and technical
    community and the public of its discoveries and understanding of
    nature by such means of education and communication as are
    appropriate;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} provide expertise in natural history and provide
    informed advice and opinion on issues dealing with the natural
    world;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} establish authoritative centres for the
    identification of natural history objects and specimens;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} create and foster, through programs, functions and
    facilities, active public involvement and interest in the Canadian
    Museum of Nature, both at the community level and throughout Canada;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} establish and foster liaison with other
    organizations with a purpose similar to its purpose;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} share the expertise of its staff by undertaking and
    sponsoring programs for training and apprenticeship in the
    professional and technical skills involved in the operation of other
    organizations with a purpose similar to its purpose;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} assist, cooperate with and enlist the aid of other
    organizations with a purpose similar to its purpose;

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} provide advice and expertise on museology,
    education and communication;

  - [(s)]{.lawlabel} acquire property by gift, bequest or otherwise and
    hold in trust or otherwise, expend, invest, administer and dispose
    of that property;

  - [(t)]{.lawlabel} develop, operate and maintain branches or
    exhibition centres;

  - [(u)]{.lawlabel} operate restaurants, lounges, parking facilities,
    shops and other facilities for the use of the public;

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} lease or otherwise make available any of its
    facilities to other persons; and

  - [(w)]{.lawlabel} charge for goods, services and admission and use
    the revenue obtained therefrom for its own purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Canadian Museum of Nature may not deal with
  property otherwise than in accordance with the terms, if any, on which
  it was acquired or is held.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sell, exchange, give away, destroy or otherwise
    dispose of museum material in its collection and use any revenue
    obtained from that disposal to further its collection;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} lend or borrow museum material on long- or
    short-term loan;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} organize, sponsor, arrange for or participate in
    travelling exhibitions, in Canada or internationally, of museum
    material in its collection or from other sources;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} undertake or sponsor any research related to its
    purpose or to museology and communicate the results of that
    research;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} provide facilities to permit qualified individuals
    to use or study its collection;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} promote knowledge and disseminate information
    related to its purpose, throughout Canada and internationally, by
    any appropriate means of education and communication;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} establish and foster links with other organizations
    that have a purpose similar to its own;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} share the expertise of its staff by undertaking or
    sponsoring training and apprenticeship programs that relate to its
    purpose;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} provide or arrange for professional and technical
    services to any other organization that has a purpose similar to its
    own;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} acquire property by gift, bequest or otherwise and
    hold in trust or otherwise, expend, invest, administer and dispose
    of that property;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} develop, operate and maintain branches or
    exhibition centres;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} operate restaurants, lounges, parking facilities,
    shops and other facilities for the use of the public;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} lease or otherwise make available any of its
    facilities to other persons; and

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} charge for goods, services and admission and use
    the revenue so obtained for its own purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Canadian Museum of Immigration at Pier 21 may not
  deal with property otherwise than in accordance with the terms, if
  any, on which it was acquired or is held.

### [Capacity in Canada and Elsewhere]{.HTitleText2} {#h-354219 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity in Canada

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  museum may carry on its activities throughout Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity elsewhere

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each museum has the capacity to carry on its
  activities, conduct its affairs and exercise its powers in any
  jurisdiction outside Canada to the extent that the laws of that
  jurisdiction permit.

### [Validity of Acts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-354225 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights preserved

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** No act of a museum,
including any transfer of property to or by a museum, is invalid by
reason only that the act is contrary to this Act or the by-laws of the
museum.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-354228 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board of Trustees

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be a Board of Trustees of each museum, consisting of a
  Chairperson, a Vice-Chairperson and not more than nine other trustees
  to be appointed as provided in section 19.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person is not eligible to be appointed as a member
  of the Board if that person is not a Canadian citizen.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibility of Board

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board is responsible for the fulfilment of the
  purposes and the management of the business, activities and affairs of
  the museum.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1990, c. 3, s. 18
- 1995, c. 29, s. 46
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Chairperson and
Vice-Chairperson

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson of a museum shall be appointed by the
  Minister, with the approval of the Governor in Council, to hold office
  during pleasure for a term not exceeding four years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of trustees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The trustees of a museum, other than the Chairperson
  and Vice-Chairperson, shall be appointed by the Minister, with the
  approval of the Governor in Council, to hold office during pleasure
  for a term, not exceeding four years, that will ensure, as far as
  possible, the expiration in any one year of the terms of office of not
  more than four of those trustees.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation in office

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsections (1) and (2), if a trustee
  is not appointed to take office on the expiration of the term of an
  incumbent trustee, the incumbent trustee continues in office until a
  successor is appointed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A trustee is eligible to be reappointed to the Board
  in the same or another capacity, except that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no person may be appointed as Chairperson, and no
    person may be appointed as Vice-Chairperson, for more than two
    consecutive terms; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a trustee, other than the Chairperson or
    Vice-Chairperson, who has served three consecutive terms in that
    capacity is not, during the year following the expiration of the
    third term, eligible to be reappointed in that capacity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or vacancy: Chairperson

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the Chairperson is absent or unable to act or if
  the office of the Chairperson is vacant, the Vice-Chairperson has all
  the powers, and may exercise and perform all the duties and functions,
  of the Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or vacancy: Vice-Chairperson

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the Vice-Chairperson is absent or unable to act or
  if the office of the Vice-Chairperson is vacant, the Minister may
  designate another trustee to act as Vice-Chairperson for the time
  being, but no person so designated shall act in that office for a
  period exceeding ninety days without the approval of the Governor in
  Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancy: other trustees

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Where the office of a trustee, other than the
  Chairperson or Vice-Chairperson, becomes vacant during the term of the
  person appointed thereto, the Minister, with the approval of the
  Governor in Council, may appoint a person to that office for the
  remainder of that term.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1990, c. 3, s. 19
- 2006, c. 9, s. 279
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  trustee of a museum, other than a trustee who is in receipt of a
  salary fixed by Parliament, the Governor in Council or the Treasury
  Board, is entitled to be paid by the museum such fees or other
  remuneration as the Governor in Council may fix.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A trustee of a museum is entitled to be paid by the
  museum such reasonable travel and living expenses incurred by the
  trustee in the course of duties as a trustee as may be fixed by or
  pursuant to the by-laws of the museum.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual meetings of Board

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The Board of a museum
shall meet at least once each year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The Board of a museum may
make by-laws for the administration, management and control of the
property, business, activities and affairs of the museum, including,
without limiting the generality of the foregoing, by-laws

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the conduct and management of activities and
  proceedings of the Board, including the establishment of committees of
  trustees, other persons or both, the time and place for the holding of
  any meeting of the Board or of any such committee and the quorum and
  procedure at any such meeting;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the powers, duties and functions of the
  Board, any committee established by or pursuant to a by-law of the
  museum and of the Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, Director, trustees,
  officers, employees and agents of the museum;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the delegation or sub-delegation of any
  power, duty or function of the Board to any committee established by
  or pursuant to a by-law of the museum or to the Chairperson,
  Vice-Chairperson or Director of the museum or any trustee, officer,
  employee or agent of the museum;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} prescribing conflict of interest rules for the
  trustees, committee members, officers, employees and agents of the
  museum;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fixing or providing for the fixing of the travel and
  living expenses to be paid pursuant to subsection 20(2); and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} respecting the performance review of the Director of
  the museum and the exclusion of the Director from meetings of the
  Board, or a committee established by or pursuant to a by-law of the
  museum, during consideration of the employment, performance
  objectives, performance review, remuneration and terms and conditions
  of employment of the Director.

### [Staff]{.HTitleText2} {#h-354272 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Director

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (1.1) and
  (1.2), there shall be a Director of each museum who shall be
  appointed, with the approval of the Governor in Council, by the
  museum's Board to hold office during pleasure for a term of not more
  than five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Director of the Canadian Museum for
  Human Rights

  Canadian Museum for Human Rights to hold office during pleasure for a
  term of not more than five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Director of the Canadian Museum of
  Immigration at Pier 21

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council shall, by order, on the
  recommendation of the Minister, appoint the first Director of the
  Canadian Museum of Immigration at Pier 21 to hold office during
  pleasure for a term of not more than five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Director of each museum is eligible to be
  reappointed, with the approval of the Governor in Council, by the
  museum's Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chief executive officer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Director is the chief executive officer of the
  museum and, under the direction of the Board, has control and
  supervision of the work and staff of the museum.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsible to Board

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Director is responsible to the Board for the
  exercise of the powers and the performance of the duties and functions
  of that office and, subject to the by-laws of the museum made pursuant
  to paragraph 22(f), may attend meetings of the Board or of any
  committee established by or pursuant to the by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (5.1) and (5.2), the Director
  shall be paid by the museum any remuneration that the Board, with the
  approval of the Governor in Council, may determine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration --- first Director of the
  Canadian Museum for Human Rights

  [(5.1)]{.lawlabel} The first Director of the Canadian Museum for Human
  Rights shall be paid by that museum for the duration of his or her
  first term of office any remuneration that the Governor in Council
  may, by order, on the recommendation of the Minister, determine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration --- first Director of the
  Canadian Museum of Immigration at Pier 21

  [(5.2)]{.lawlabel} The first Director of the Canadian Museum of
  Immigration at Pier 21 shall be paid by that museum for the duration
  of his or her first term of office any remuneration that the Governor
  in Council may, by order, on the recommendation of the Minister,
  determine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting Director

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the Director is absent or unable to act or if the
  office of the Director is vacant, the Board may designate another
  officer of the museum to act as Director for the time being, but no
  person so designated shall act in that office for a period exceeding
  ninety days without the approval of the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  museum may engage such officers, employees and agents and such
  technical and professional advisers as it considers necessary for the
  proper conduct of its activities and may fix the terms and conditions
  of their engagement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not part of public service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (3) to (5), the trustees,
  officers, employees and agents of a museum are not part of the federal
  public administration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Superannuation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The officers and employees of a museum shall be
  deemed to be employed in the public service for the purposes of the
  [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and the museum
  shall be deemed to be a Public Service corporation for the purposes of
  section 37 of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36) does not apply to a trustee, other than a trustee
  who is in receipt of a salary fixed by Parliament, the Governor in
  Council or the Treasury Board, unless, in the case of any one of them,
  the Governor in Council otherwise directs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The trustees, officers and employees of a museum
  shall be deemed to be employees for the purposes of the [Government
  Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and to be employed in the
  federal public administration for the purposes of any regulation made
  pursuant to section 9 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-354309 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-establishment contracts

- **[[24.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As
  of the date of its establishment under this Act, each museum shall be
  bound by any deed, contract or agreement entered into on its behalf by
  the Minister before its establishment and shall be entitled to the
  benefits of any such deed, contract or agreement and the Minister
  shall cease to be bound.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not retroactive

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to any museum already
  established by this Act on the day on which that subsection comes into
  force.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2008, c. 9, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The head office of each
museum shall be in such place in Canada as the Governor in Council may,
by order, specify.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agent of Her Majesty

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Each museum is, for all
purposes of this Act, an agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on directives

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  directive shall be given to a museum under section 89 or subsection
  114(3) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) with
  respect to cultural activities, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the acquisition, disposal, conservation or use of
    any museum material relevant to its activities;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its activities and programs for the public,
    including exhibitions, displays and publications; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} research with respect to the matters referred to in
    paragraphs (a) and (b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations not applicable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No regulation made pursuant to subsection 114(4) of
  the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) prescribing the
  content of by-laws applies to a museum with respect to cultural
  activities, including the matters referred to in paragraphs (1)(a) to
  (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 19, 23 and 28 apply in respect of each
  museum notwithstanding any inconsistency with Part X of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal of real property

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection 99(3) of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), subsection 99(2) of that Act
  applies to each museum with respect to the sale or other disposal of
  real property.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial year

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The financial year of
each museum is the period beginning on April 1 in one year and ending on
March 31 in the following year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** Interest, calculated at
the rate fixed by the Minister of Finance for the purposes of section
129 of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), shall be paid
monthly from the Consolidated Revenue Fund on moneys, other than moneys
appropriated by Parliament, on deposit to the credit of a museum in the
accounts of Canada and shall be credited to the account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Auditor

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** The Auditor General of
Canada is the auditor of each museum.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of real property

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, vest the administration and control of any real property
that is assigned to or vested in a Minister of Her Majesty in right of
Canada or a department or Crown corporation as defined in the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) in a museum, and from the date
specified in the order, the administration and control is vested in that
museum.

    [5]{.lawlabel} Section 2 does not affect the status of any person
    who was an officer, a trustee, an employee or an agent or mandatary
    of the former museum immediately before the day on which that
    section comes into force, except that, as of that day, the person is
    an officer, a trustee, an employee or an agent or mandatary of the
    new museum.

- --- 2013, c. 38, s. 6

  - ###### Transfer of rights and obligations {#transfer-of-rights-and-obligations .MarginalNote}

    [6]{.lawlabel} All rights and property held by or in the name of or
    in trust for the former museum and all obligations and liabilities
    of the former museum are deemed to be rights, property, obligations
    and liabilities of the new museum.

- --- 2013, c. 38, s. 7

  - ###### Transfer of powers and duties {#transfer-of-powers-and-duties .MarginalNote}

    - [7]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any power or duty that is vested
      in or is exercisable by the former museum under a contract, lease,
      licence, deed, agreement or other document is vested in or is
      exercisable by the new museum.

    - ###### References {#references .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every reference to the former museum in any
      contract, lease, licence, deed, agreement or other document
      executed or signed by the former museum in its own name shall,
      unless the context otherwise requires, be read as a reference to
      the new museum.

- --- 2013, c. 38, s. 8

  - ###### Transfer of appropriations {#transfer-of-appropriations .MarginalNote}

    [8]{.lawlabel} Any amount appropriated, for the fiscal year in which
    section 2 comes into force, by an appropriation Act based on the
    Estimates for that year for defraying the charges and expenses of
    the former museum and that, on the day on which section 2 comes into
    force, is unexpended is deemed, on that day, to be an amount
    appropriated for defraying the charges and expenses of the new
    museum.

- --- 2013, c. 38, s. 9

  - ###### Commencement of legal proceedings {#commencement-of-legal-proceedings .MarginalNote}

    [9]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal proceeding in respect
    of an obligation or liability incurred by the former museum may be
    brought against the new museum in any court that would have had
    jurisdiction if the action, suit or other legal proceeding had been
    brought against the former museum.

- --- 2013, c. 38, s. 10

  - ###### Continuation of legal proceedings {#continuation-of-legal-proceedings .MarginalNote}

    [10]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal proceeding to which
    the former museum is a party that is pending in any court
    immediately before the day on which section 2 comes into force may
    be continued by or against the new museum in the same manner and to
    the same extent as it could have been continued by or against the
    former museum.
