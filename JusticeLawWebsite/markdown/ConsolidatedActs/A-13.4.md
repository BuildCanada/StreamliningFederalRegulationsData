An Act respecting assisted human reproduction and related research

of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-5999 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Assisted Human Reproduction Act](/eng/acts/A-13.4).

## [Principles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6002 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Parliament of Canada
recognizes and declares that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the health and well-being of children born through
  the application of assisted human reproductive technologies must be
  given priority in all decisions respecting their use;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the benefits of assisted human reproductive
  technologies and related research for individuals, for families and
  for society in general can be most effectively secured by taking
  appropriate measures for the protection and promotion of human health,
  safety, dignity and rights in the use of these technologies and in
  related research;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} while all persons are affected by these technologies,
  women more than men are directly and significantly affected by their
  application and the health and well-being of women must be protected
  in the application of these technologies;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the principle of free and informed consent must be
  promoted and applied as a fundamental condition of the use of human
  reproductive technologies;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} persons who seek to undergo assisted reproduction
  procedures must not be discriminated against, including on the basis
  of their sexual orientation or marital status;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} trade in the reproductive capabilities of women and
  men and the exploitation of children, women and men for commercial
  ends raise health and ethical concerns that justify their prohibition;
  and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} human individuality and diversity, and the integrity
  of the human genome, must be preserved and protected.

## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6012 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

:   [[assisted reproduction procedure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,

[[chimera]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chimera]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an embryo into which a cell of any non-human life
      form has been introduced; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an embryo that consists of cells of more than one
      embryo, foetus or human being. ([chimère]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[consent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[consent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2012, c. 19, s.

:   [[donor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to an [in vitro]{.otherLang

[[embryo]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[embryo]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a human organism during the

    suspended, and includes any cell derived from such an organism that
    is used for the purpose of creating a human
    being. ([embryon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[foetus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foetus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a human organism during the
    period of its development beginning on the fifty-seventh day
    following fertilization or creation, excluding any time during which
    its development has been suspended, and ending at
    birth. ([foetus]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[gene]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gene]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a nucleotide sequence, and an
    artificially created gene or nucleotide
    sequence. ([gène]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[genome]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[genome]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the totality of the
    deoxyribonucleic acid sequence of a particular
    cell. ([génome]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[health reporting information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[health reporting information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    2012, c. 19, s. 713\]]{.Repealed}

[[human clone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[human clone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an embryo that, as a
    result of the manipulation of human reproductive material or an [in
    vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo, contains a diploid set of
    chromosomes obtained from a single --- living or deceased --- human
    being, foetus or embryo. ([clone humain]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[human reproductive material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[human reproductive material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a sperm,
    ovum or other human cell or a human gene, and includes a part of any
    of them. ([matériel reproductif humain]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hybrid]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hybrid]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a human ovum that has been fertilized by a sperm
      of a non-human life form;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an ovum of a non-human life form that has been
      fertilized by a human sperm;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a human ovum into which the nucleus of a cell of
      a non-human life form has been introduced;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an ovum of a non-human life form into which the
      nucleus of a human cell has been introduced; or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a human ovum or an ovum of a non-human life form
      that otherwise contains haploid sets of chromosomes from both a
      human being and a non-human life form. ([hybride]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[[in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[[in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"}
    embryo]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an embryo that exists outside the
    body of a human being. ([embryon [in vitro]{.otherLang
    lang="la"}]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2012, c. 19, s.
    713\]]{.Repealed}

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Health. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[ovum]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ovum]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a human ovum, whether mature or
    not. ([ovule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sperm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sperm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a human sperm, whether mature or
    not. ([spermatozoïde]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[surrogate mother]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[surrogate mother]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a female person who
    --- with the intention of surrendering the child at birth to a donor
    or another person --- carries an embryo or foetus that was conceived
    by means of an assisted reproduction procedure and derived from the
    genes of a donor or donors. ([mère porteuse]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

and Toxins Act](/eng/acts/H-5.67) does not apply in respect of sperm,
ova and [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryos to be used for the
purpose of assisted human reproduction.

## [Prohibited Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6052 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited procedures

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall knowingly

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} create a human clone by using any technique, or
    transplant a human clone into a human being or into any non-human
    life form or artificial device;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} create an [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo
    for any purpose other than creating a human being or improving or
    providing instruction in assisted reproduction procedures;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of creating a human being, create
    an embryo from a cell or part of a cell taken from an embryo or
    foetus or transplant an embryo so created into a human being;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} maintain an embryo outside the body of a female
    person after the fourteenth day of its development following
    fertilization or creation, excluding any time during which its
    development has been suspended;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of creating a human being, perform
    any procedure or provide, prescribe or administer any thing that
    would ensure or increase the probability that an embryo will be of a
    particular sex, or that would identify the sex of an [in
    vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo, except to prevent, diagnose or
    treat a sex-linked disorder or disease;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} alter the genome of a cell of a human being or [in
    vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo such that the alteration is
    capable of being transmitted to descendants;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} transplant a sperm, ovum, embryo or foetus of a
    non-human life form into a human being;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of creating a human being, make use
    of any human reproductive material or an [in vitro]{.otherLang
    lang="la"} embryo that is or was transplanted into a non-human life
    form;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} create a chimera, or transplant a chimera into
    either a human being or a non-human life form; or

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} create a hybrid for the purpose of reproduction, or
    transplant a hybrid into either a human being or a non-human life
    form.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall offer to do, or advertise the doing
  of, anything prohibited by this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment for prohibited act

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall pay or offer to pay consideration to
  any person for doing anything prohibited by this section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment for surrogacy

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall pay consideration to a female person to be a surrogate
  mother, offer to pay such consideration or advertise that it will be
  paid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting as intermediary

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall accept consideration for arranging
  for the services of a surrogate mother, offer to make such an
  arrangement for consideration or advertise the arranging of such
  services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment to intermediaries

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall pay consideration to another person
  to arrange for the services of a surrogate mother, offer to pay such
  consideration or advertise the payment of it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surrogate mother --- minimum age

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall counsel or induce a female person to
  become a surrogate mother, or perform any medical procedure to assist
  a female person to become a surrogate mother, knowing or having reason
  to believe that the female person is under 21 years of age.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity of agreement

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} This section does not affect the validity under
  provincial law of any agreement under which a person agrees to be a
  surrogate mother.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purchase of gametes

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall purchase, offer to purchase or advertise for the purchase
  of sperm or ova from a donor or a person acting on behalf of a donor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purchase or sale of embryos

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} purchase, offer to purchase or advertise for the
    purchase of an [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sell, offer for sale or advertise for sale an [in
    vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purchase of other reproductive material

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall purchase, offer to purchase or
  advertise for the purchase of a human cell or gene from a donor or a
  person acting on behalf of a donor, with the intention of using the
  gene or cell to create a human being or of making it available for
  that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exchanges included

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section, "purchase" or "sell" includes to
  acquire or dispose of in exchange for property or services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of reproductive material without
consent

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall make use of human reproductive material for the purpose
  of creating an embryo unless the donor of the material has given
  written consent, in accordance with the regulations, to its use for
  that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Posthumous use without consent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall remove human reproductive material
  from a donor's body after the donor's death for the purpose of
  creating an embryo unless the donor of the material has given written
  consent, in accordance with the regulations, to its removal for that
  purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  embryo without consent

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall make use of an [in vitro]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} embryo for any purpose unless the donor has given written
  consent, in accordance with the regulations, to its use for that
  purpose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Gametes obtained from minor

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No person shall obtain any
sperm or ovum from a donor under 18 years of age, or use any sperm or
ovum so obtained, except for the purpose of preserving the sperm or ovum
or for the purpose of creating a human being that the person reasonably
believes will be raised by the donor.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  purpose of this section is to reduce the risks to human health and
  safety arising from the use of sperm or ova for the purpose of
  assisted human reproduction, including the risk of the transmission of
  disease.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution, etc. of gametes

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), no person shall
  distribute, make use of or import any of the following for the purpose
  of assisted human reproduction:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sperm that has been obtained from a donor and that
    is meant for the use of a female person other than a spouse,
    common-law partner or sexual partner of the donor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an ovum that has been obtained from a donor and
    that is meant for the use of a female person other than the donor or
    the spouse, common-law partner or sexual partner of the donor; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an ovum that has been obtained from a donor and
    that is meant for the donor's use as a surrogate mother.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} tests have been conducted in respect of the sperm
    or ovum in accordance with the regulations, and the sperm or ovum
    has been obtained, prepared, preserved, quarantined, identified,
    labelled and stored and its quality assessed in accordance with the
    regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the donor of the sperm or ovum has been screened
    and tested, and the donor's suitability has been assessed, in
    accordance with the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Testing, etc. in respect of gametes

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall, except in accordance with the
  regulations, engage in any activity described in paragraph (3)(a)
  or (b) in respect of any of the following with the intention of
  distributing or making use of it for the purpose of assisted human
  reproduction:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sperm described in paragraph (2)(a);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an ovum described in paragraph (2)(b); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an ovum described in paragraph (2)(c).

- Definition of [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[common-law
  partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an individual, means a
  person who is cohabiting with the individual in a conjugal
  relationship at the relevant time, having so cohabited for a period of
  at least one year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reimbursement of expenditures

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall, except in accordance with the regulations,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} reimburse a donor for an expenditure incurred in
    the course of donating sperm or an ovum;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reimburse any person for an expenditure incurred in
    the maintenance or transport of an [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"}
    embryo; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} reimburse a surrogate mother for an expenditure
    incurred by her in relation to her surrogacy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall reimburse an expenditure referred to
  in subsection (1) unless a receipt is provided to that person for the
  expenditure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No reimbursement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall reimburse a surrogate mother for a
  loss of work-related income incurred during her pregnancy, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a qualified medical practitioner certifies, in
    writing, that continuing to work may pose a risk to her health or
    that of the embryo or foetus; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reimbursement is made in accordance with the
    regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taking measures

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that this Act has been, or
  is likely to be, contravened, the Minister may take, or order any
  person to take, all reasonable measures that the Minister considers
  necessary to mitigate the effects of the contravention or to prevent
  the contravention.

- [(2) and (3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2012, c. 19, s.
  725\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal liability

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person who takes measures under this section, or
  who takes measures specified in an order made under this section, is
  personally liable either civilly or criminally in respect of any act
  or omission in the course of taking those measures unless it is
  established that the person acted in bad faith.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (4) does not apply to a person who has
  committed a contravention of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, orders made under this section
  are not statutory instruments within the meaning of the [Statutory

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entry by inspectors

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 48, an inspector
  may, for a purpose related to verifying compliance or preventing
  non-compliance with any of sections 8, 10 and 12, enter any place or

  that there is any activity, material or information in respect of
  which any of those sections applies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspection

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An inspector entering a place or conveyance may, for
  a purpose set out in subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine any material or information that is
    relevant to that purpose;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require any person in the place or conveyance to
    produce, in the manner and form requested by the inspector, any such
    material or information;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} open and examine any receptacle or package that the
    inspector believes on reasonable grounds contains such material or
    information;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} take, or require any person in the place or
    conveyance to produce, a sample of such material; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} conduct any test or analysis or take any
    measurement of such material.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination of information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In carrying out an inspection, an inspector may, for
  a purpose set out in subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine and make copies of or extracts from any
    books, documents or other records that the inspector believes on
    reasonable grounds contain information that is relevant to that
    purpose;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require any person to produce such books, documents
    or other records for examination or copying;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any computer system to
    examine information relevant to that purpose that is contained in or
    available to the computer system;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} reproduce such information in the form of a
    printout or other intelligible output for examination or copying;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any copying equipment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance and information to inspector

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The owner or person in charge of a place entered by
  an inspector under subsection (1) and every person found in that place
  shall give the inspector all reasonable assistance and furnish them
  with any information that they may reasonably require.

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person from whom material or information is seized may, within 60 days

  restoration, if the person sends to the Minister notice of their
  intention to do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of restoration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The provincial court judge may order that seized
  material or information be restored immediately to the applicant if,
  on hearing the application, the judge is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is entitled to possession of it; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it will not be required as evidence in any
    proceedings under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of later restoration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, on hearing an application, a provincial court
  judge is satisfied that the applicant is entitled to possession of
  seized material or information but is not satisfied as regards
  paragraph (2)(b), the judge may order that the material or information
  be restored to the applicant

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the expiry of 180 days after the date of the
    seizure if no proceedings under this Act have been commenced before
    that time; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the final conclusion of proceedings under this
    Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A provincial court judge may not make an order for
  the restoration of material or information if it has been forfeited by

  - [(z.3)]{.lawlabel} exempting any person from the application of
    section 10, conditionally or unconditionally, in the circumstances
    provided for in the regulations; and

  - [(z.4)]{.lawlabel} exempting from the application of subsection
    12(2), conditionally or unconditionally, in the circumstances
    provided for in the regulations, any person who reimburses
    expenditures referred to in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The regulations may incorporate any document by
  reference, regardless of its source, either as it reads on a
  particular date or as it is amended from time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents in one language

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a document that is available in both official
  languages has been incorporated by reference as amended from time to
  time, an amendment to one language version of that document is not
  incorporated until the corresponding amendment is made to the other
  language version.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A document does not become a regulation within the
  meaning of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) merely
  because it is incorporated by reference.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proposed regulations to be laid before
Parliament

- **[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  a regulation is made under section 65, the Minister shall lay the
  proposed regulation before each House of Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report by committee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A proposed regulation that is laid before Parliament
  shall be referred to the appropriate committee of each House, as
  determined by the rules of that House, and the committee may review
  the proposed regulation and report its findings to the House.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standing Committee on Health

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} The committee of the House of Commons referred to
  in subsection (2) shall be the Standing Committee on Health or, in the
  event that there is not a Standing Committee on Health, the
  appropriate committee of the House.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Making of regulations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A regulation may not be made before the earliest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 30 sitting days after the proposed regulation is
    laid before Parliament,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 160 calendar days after the proposed regulation is
    laid before Parliament, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day after the appropriate committee of each
    House of Parliament has reported its findings with respect to the
    proposed regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Explanation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall take into account any report of
  the committee of either House. If a regulation does not incorporate a
  recommendation of the committee of either House, the Minister shall
  lay before that House a statement of the reasons for not incorporating
  it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alteration

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A proposed regulation that has been laid before
  Parliament need not again be so laid prior to the making of the
  regulation, whether it has been altered or not.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

- **[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  regulation may be made without being laid before either House of
  Parliament if the Minister is of the opinion that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the changes made by the regulation to an existing
    regulation are so immaterial or insubstantial that section 66 should
    not apply in the circumstances; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the regulation must be made immediately in order to
    protect the health or safety of any person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of opinion

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a regulation is made without being laid before
  Parliament, the Minister shall lay before each House of Parliament a
  statement of the Minister's reasons.

    [741]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in sections 742 to
    745.

    :   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Assisted Human
        Reproduction Agency of Canada established by subsection 21(1) of
        the [Assisted Human Reproduction
        Act](/eng/acts/A-13.4). ([Agence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    [[Her Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[Her Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Her Majesty in right
        of Canada. ([Sa Majesté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- --- 2012, c. 19, s. 742

  - ###### Appointments terminated {#appointments-terminated .MarginalNote}

    - [742]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} The members of the board of
      directors of the Agency cease to hold office on the coming into
      force of this subsection.

    - ###### No compensation {#no-compensation .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite the provisions of any contract, agreement
      or order, no person appointed to hold office as a member of the
      board of directors of the Agency, other than the President, has
      any right to claim or receive any compensation, damages, indemnity
      or other form of relief from Her Majesty in right of Canada or
      from any employee or agent of Her Majesty for ceasing to hold that
      office or for the abolition of that office by operation of this
      Division.

- --- 2012, c. 19, s. 743

  - ###### Employees of Agency {#employees-of-agency .MarginalNote}

    [743]{.lawlabel} Any employee of the Agency who has been advised
    that they will be laid off in accordance with subsection 64(1) of
    the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) is, for the
    purpose of any workforce adjustment measure taken with respect to
    them, transferred to the Department of Health on the day on which
    section 722 comes into force.

- --- 2012, c. 19, s. 744

  - ###### Rights and obligations transferred {#rights-and-obligations-transferred .MarginalNote}

    - [744]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} All rights and property held by
      or in the name of or in trust for the Agency and all obligations
      and liabilities of the Agency are deemed to be rights, property,
      obligations and liabilities of Her Majesty.

    - ###### References {#references .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every reference to the Agency in any deed,
      contract or other document executed by the Agency in its own name
      is, unless the context otherwise requires, to be read as a
      reference to Her Majesty.

    - ###### Closing out affairs {#closing-out-affairs .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Health may do and perform all
      acts and things necessary for and incidental to closing out the
      affairs of the Agency.

- --- 2012, c. 19, s. 745

  - ###### Commencement of legal proceedings {#commencement-of-legal-proceedings .MarginalNote}

    - [745]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal
      proceeding in respect of an obligation or liability incurred by
      the Agency, or by the Minister of Health in closing out the
      affairs of the Agency, may be brought against Her Majesty in any
      court that would have had jurisdiction if the action, suit or
      proceeding had been brought against the Agency.

    - ###### Continuation of legal proceedings {#continuation-of-legal-proceedings .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal proceeding to
      which the Agency is party pending in any court on the coming into
      force of this section may be continued by or against Her Majesty
      in the same manner and to the same extent as it could have been
      continued by or against the Agency.
