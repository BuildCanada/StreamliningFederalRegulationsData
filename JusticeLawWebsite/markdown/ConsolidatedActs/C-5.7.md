## Canada Fund for Africa Act {#canada-fund-for-africa-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2002, c. 9, s. 45
:::

Assented to 2002-04-12

An Act to establish a program to provide contributions for the economic
and social development of Africa in fulfilment of the objectives set out
in the New Partnership for Africa's Development

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 45 of chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2002;
sections 1 and 2 in force April 12, 2002, see SI/2002-71; sections 3 to
5 in force June 27, 2002, see SI/2002-101.\]
:::
:::::

::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada Fund for Africa Act](/eng/acts/C-5.7).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of "Minister"

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the member of the Queen's Privy
Council for Canada who is designated by the Governor in Council as the
Minister for the purposes of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of program

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a program, to be called the Canada Fund for Africa,
  the object of which is to provide for the payment of contributions to
  eligible recipients for the carrying out of eligible activities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligible activities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An eligible activity is an activity that is, or is to
  be, conducted by an eligible recipient and that, in the opinion of the
  Minister, significantly promotes the fulfilment of the objectives set
  out in the *New Partnership for Africa's Development*, adopted as the
  New Africa Initiative by the Organization of African Unity at Lusaka
  in July 2001 and, in particular, those objectives that were identified
  for support in the Africa Action Plan called for by the Group of Eight
  industrialized countries in Genoa in July 2001 and that are adopted by
  the Group of Eight at its summit scheduled at Kananaskis in June 2002.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligible recipients

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A foreign government, an agency of a foreign
  government, an international organization, a corporation, a
  partnership or a trust is an eligible recipient if it carries on or,
  in the opinion of the Minister, is capable of carrying on an eligible
  activity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contribution agreements

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may enter into
an agreement with an eligible recipient to provide for the payment of a
contribution for an eligible activity under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations generally for the carrying out of the purposes and
provisions of this Act, including regulations establishing additional
terms and conditions for the Fund.
