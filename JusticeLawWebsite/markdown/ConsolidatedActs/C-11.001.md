## Canada Water Agency Act {#canada-water-agency-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2024, c. 15, s. 209
:::

Assented to 2024-06-20

An Act respecting the Canada Water Agency

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 209 of chapter 15 of the Statutes of Canada, 2024,
in force October 15, 2024, see SI/2024-49.\]
:::
:::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the Government of Canada recognizes the importance of taking
action to respond to the growing challenges threatening the health and
sustainable management of freshwater ecosystems;

Whereas the Government of Canada wishes to foster collaboration with
respect to freshwater issues;

Whereas the Government of Canada wishes to contribute to the protection,
conservation and restoration of the quality of fresh water and the
health of freshwater ecosystems in Canada and to take other
collaborative measures, including the development of policy and the
promotion of sound governance with respect to fresh water, as well as
the improvement of the ease of access to and use of relevant data;

Whereas the Government of Canada recognizes the importance of relying on
scientific knowledge related to fresh water and of relying, through
cooperation with the Indigenous peoples of Canada, on Indigenous
knowledge related to fresh water;

Whereas the Government of Canada wishes to coordinate federal policies
and programs with respect to freshwater issues;

Whereas the Government of Canada is committed, in the course of
exercising and performing its powers, duties and functions with respect
to fresh water, to fostering reconciliation with the Indigenous peoples
of Canada and to ensuring respect for their rights recognized and
affirmed under section 35 of the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/);

Whereas the Government of Canada is committed to implementing the United
Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples;

Whereas the Government of Canada is committed to promoting cooperation
with respect to freshwater issues with provincial and territorial
governments and the Indigenous peoples of Canada;

Whereas the Government of Canada wishes to promote cooperation with
respect to freshwater issues with foreign governments, international
organizations and interested persons and organizations;

And whereas the Government of Canada considers that the creation of the
Canada Water Agency will contribute to the coordination of federal
efforts to promote sustainable freshwater management;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1470458 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada Water Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-11.001).

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1470461 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Water Agency
    established by section 3. ([Agence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of the
    Environment. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[President]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[President]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the President of the Agency
    appointed under section 7. ([président]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Canada Water Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1470467 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Canada Water Agency is
established for the purpose of assisting the Minister in exercising or
performing the Minister's powers, duties and functions in relation to
fresh water under any Act of Parliament, including the [Department of
the Environment Act](/eng/acts/E-10) and the [Canada Water
Act](/eng/acts/C-11).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the
Agency is to be at a place in Canada that is designated by the Governor
in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister to preside

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister presides over
the Agency and has the management and direction of it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation to Agency

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, subject to any terms and conditions that the Minister
  specifies, delegate to an officer or employee of the Agency any power,
  duty or function that the Minister is authorized to exercise or
  perform under any Act of Parliament in relation to fresh water.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, the Minister is not authorized to delegate a
  power to make regulations or a power to delegate under subsection (1).

## [President]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1470479 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The President of the Agency
is to be appointed by the Governor in Council to hold office during
pleasure for a renewable term of up to five years.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chief executive officer

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The President is the chief
executive officer of the Agency and has the rank and status of a deputy
head of a department.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The President is to be paid
the remuneration fixed by the Governor in Council.

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1470486 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers and employees

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The officers and
employees necessary for the proper conduct of the work of the Agency are
to be appointed in accordance with the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other government services and facilities

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  department, board or agency of the Government of Canada may provide to
  the Agency services and facilities that are necessary for carrying out
  the Agency's purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of services and facilities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In exercising its powers and performing its duties
  and functions, the Agency must, where appropriate, make use of those
  services and facilities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of services and facilities

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Agency may provide
services and facilities to departments, boards and agencies of the
Government of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committees

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may establish advisory committees in relation to fresh water
  and provide for their membership, duties, functions and operation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may fix the remuneration that members of
  a committee are to be paid for the performance of their duties and
  functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reimbursement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may determine whether members of a
  committee are to be reimbursed for the travel, living and other
  expenses incurred in the performance of their duties and functions
  while absent from their ordinary place of residence. Any such
  reimbursement is to be paid in accordance with Treasury Board
  directives.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1470503 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in sections 15 to 18.

[[former agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[former agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the portion of the
    federal public administration, within the Department of the
    Environment, known as the Canada Water Agency. ([ancienne
    agence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[new agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[new agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Water Agency
    established by section 3. ([nouvelle agence]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Position

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act is to be
  construed as affecting the status of an employee who, immediately
  before the coming into force of this section, occupied a position in
  the former agency, except that the employee, on that coming into
  force, is to occupy that position in the new agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (1), [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  has the same meaning as in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriations

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Any amount that is
appropriated by an Act of Parliament, for the fiscal year in which this
section comes into force, to defray the expenditures of the former
agency and that is unexpended on the day on which this section comes
into force is deemed to be an amount appropriated to defray the
expenditures of the new agency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of powers, duties and functions

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Any power, duty or
function that is exercisable by an officer or employee of the former
agency under any Act, order, rule or regulation or under any contract,
lease, licence or other document, is to be exercised by the appropriate
officer or employee of the new agency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
the powers, duties and functions referred to in section 17 include those
related to the administration, in whole or in part, of any contract,
lease, licence or other document that relates to the activities,
management or operation of the former agency.
