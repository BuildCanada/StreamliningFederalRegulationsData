## Canadair Limited Divestiture Authorization Act {#canadair-limited-divestiture-authorization-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1986, c. 57
:::

Assented to 1986-12-19

An Act to authorize the divestiture of Canadair Limited and to provide
for other matters in connection therewith
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-146875 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canadair Limited Divestiture Authorization Act](/eng/acts/C-6.2).

## [Authorizations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-146878 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale of Canadair shares

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Canada Development
Investment Corporation and Cartierville Financial Corporation Inc., its
wholly-owned subsidiary, are hereby authorized to sell, on such terms
and conditions as are approved by the Governor in Council, any or all
shares of Canadair Limited held by them to Bombardier-Canadair Inc. for
monetary consideration and Class A and Class B shares of
Bombardier-Canadair Inc.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal of Bombardier-Canadair Inc.
shares

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Canada Development
Investment Corporation and Cartierville Financial Corporation Inc. are
hereby authorized to sell or otherwise dispose of, on such terms and
conditions as are approved by the Governor in Council, any or all of the
Class A and Class B shares of Bombardier-Canadair Inc. acquired by them
in connection with the sale of shares authorized by section 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acquisition of Bombardier-Canadair Inc.
shares

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Regional Industrial Expansion is hereby authorized to
  acquire 500,000 Class B shares of Bombardier-Canadair Inc. from Canada
  Development Investment Corporation and Cartierville Financial
  Corporation Inc. on such terms and conditions as are approved by the
  Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shares held in trust for Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Shares acquired pursuant to subsection (1) shall be
  held by the Minister in trust for Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions not applicable to sale of
shares

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subsections 183(2) to (8)
of the [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44) and
subsections 108(2) to (5) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11) do not apply in respect of any sale or other
disposal of shares authorized by section 2 or 3.

## [Financial Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-146890 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustment to accounts of Canada

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of State
(Privatization), after consultation with the President of the Treasury
Board, shall cause such adjustments to be made in the accounts of Canada
as are required as a result of any sale or other disposal of shares
authorized by section 2 or 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnification

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of State
(Privatization) and the Minister of Regional Industrial Expansion may,
on behalf of Her Majesty in right of Canada and on such terms and
conditions as are approved by the Governor in Council, enter into an
agreement to become jointly and severally liable with Canada Development
Investment Corporation and Cartierville Financial Corporation Inc. with
respect to any obligation assumed as a result of any sale of shares
authorized by section 2.
