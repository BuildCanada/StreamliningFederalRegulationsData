## Government Services Act, 1999 {#government-services-act-1999 .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1999, c. 13
:::

Assented to 1999-03-25

An Act to provide for the resumption and continuation of government
services
::::

:::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-251881 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Government Services Act, 1999](/eng/acts/G-7.4).

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Operational Groups]{.HTitleText1} {#h-251884 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-251885 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in this Part.

  [[bargaining agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[bargaining agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Public Service
      Alliance of Canada. ([agent négociateur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person employed in the
      Public Service who is bound by a group specific
      agreement. ([fonctionnaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Her Majesty in right of
      Canada as represented by the Treasury
      Board. ([employeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[government services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[government services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the services
      provided by that part of the Public Service in which the employees
      are employed. ([services gouvernementaux]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[group specific agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[group specific agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
      agreement specified in Schedule 1. ([convention
      particulière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[master agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[master agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the collective
      agreement between the employer and the bargaining agent signed on
      May 17, 1989, as amended and extended after that
      date. ([convention cadre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Words and expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise provided, words and expressions used
  in this Part have the same meaning as in the [Public Service Staff
  Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-35).

### [Government Services]{.HTitleText2} {#h-251897 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resumption or continuation of government
services

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** On the coming into force of
this Part,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the employer shall resume without delay, or continue,
  as the case may be, government services; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} every employee shall, when so required, resume
  without delay, or continue, as the case may be, the duties of that
  employee's employment.

### [Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-251902 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of bargaining agent

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The bargaining agent and
each officer and representative of the bargaining agent shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} without delay on the coming into force of this Part,
  give notice to the employees that, by reason of the coming into force
  of this Part,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any declaration, authorization or direction to go
    on strike given to them before the coming into force of this Part is
    invalid, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} government services are to be resumed or
    continued, as the case may be, and that the employees, when so
    required, are to resume without delay, or continue, as the case may
    be, the duties of their employment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} take all reasonable steps to ensure that employees
  comply with paragraph 3(b); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} refrain from any conduct that may encourage employees
  not to comply with paragraph 3(b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of employer

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No officer or
representative of the employer shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in any manner impede any employee from complying with
  paragraph 3(b); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} discharge or in any other manner discipline, or
  authorize or direct the discharge or discipline of, any employee by
  reason only of that employee's having been lawfully on strike before
  the coming into force of this Part.

### [Collective Agreements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-251914 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resumption of expired agreements

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The master agreement and
each group specific agreement is deemed to have had effect from the date
it expired to the coming into force of this Part and shall continue to
have effect in respect of the employer, the bargaining agent and the
employees until the earlier of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day they become bound by a single collective
  agreement concluded by the employer and the bargaining agent, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day they become bound by the collective agreement
  referred to in subsection 7(3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to prescribe terms and
conditions

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of the Treasury Board,
  and taking into account collective agreements entered into by the
  employer in respect of bargaining units in the Public Service since
  the [Public Sector Compensation Act](/eng/acts/P-31.6) ceased to apply
  to compensation plans applicable to them, prescribe

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the terms and conditions of employment applicable
    to the employees; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the period during which those terms and conditions
    of employment are applicable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into effect of provisions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may provide that any of the
  terms and conditions of employment is effective and binding on a day
  before or after the beginning of the period prescribed under paragraph
  (1)(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New single collective agreement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The terms and conditions prescribed under paragraph
  (1)(a) constitute a single collective agreement binding on the
  bargaining units composed of the employees referred to in that
  paragraph.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Public Service Staff Relations
  Act](/eng/acts/P-35) applies

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The [Public Service Staff Relations
  Act](/eng/acts/P-35) applies to the collective agreement referred to
  in subsection (3) and that collective agreement is effective and
  binding on the employer, the bargaining agent and the employees for
  the duration of the period it is applicable, despite any provision of
  that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does not apply

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does not apply in respect of anything done under
  this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Spent provisions

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the employer, the bargaining agent and the
  employees become bound by a single collective agreement concluded by
  the employer and the bargaining agent before terms and conditions of
  employment applicable to the employees are prescribed under subsection
  (1), subsections (1) to (5) and section 9 are deemed to be spent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Strikes prohibited

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** During the period beginning
on the coming into force of this Part and ending on the expiration of
the period during which the collective agreement referred to in
paragraph 6(a) or the collective agreement referred to in subsection
7(3), whichever is applicable, has effect,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} no officer or representative of the bargaining agent
  shall declare, authorize or direct a strike by any employee bound by
  the collective agreement; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} no employee bound by the collective agreement shall
  participate in a strike against the employer.

### [Amendment of Collective Agreement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-251938 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Part shall
be construed so as to limit or restrict the rights of the parties to
agree to amend any provision of the collective agreement referred to in
subsection 7(3) and to give effect to the amendment.

### [Enforcement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-251941 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Individuals

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual who contravenes any provision of this Part is guilty of an
  offence punishable on summary conviction and is liable, for each day
  or part of a day during which the offence continues, to a fine

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} of not more than \$50,000, if the individual was
    acting in the capacity of an officer or representative of the
    employer or of the bargaining agent when the offence was committed;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} of not more than \$1,000, in any other case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bargaining agent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the bargaining agent contravenes any provision of
  this Part, it is guilty of an offence punishable on summary conviction
  and is liable, for each day or part of a day during which the offence
  continues, to a fine of not more than \$100,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No imprisonment

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsection 787(2)
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), a term of imprisonment may not
be imposed in default of payment of a fine that is imposed under section
10.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of fines

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A fine
  imposed under this Part constitutes a debt payable to Her Majesty in
  right of Canada and may be recovered as such in any court of competent
  jurisdiction or by any manner provided for in any Act of Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deductions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A fine imposed under this Part on a bargaining agent
  or one of its officers or representatives may also be recovered by Her
  Majesty by deducting the amount of the fine or any portion of the fine
  from the amount of the membership dues that the employer is or may be
  required, under any collective agreement between the employer and the
  bargaining agent, to deduct from the pay of persons employed in the
  Public Service who are bound by the collective agreement and to remit
  to the bargaining agent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed deposit in C.R.F.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An amount equal to every amount deducted under
  subsection (2) is deemed to be deposited to the credit of the Receiver
  General in the Consolidated Revenue Fund.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Part, the bargaining agent is deemed to be a person.

### [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText2} {#h-251960 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** This Part and Schedule 1
come into force on the expiration of the twelfth hour after the time
this Act is assented to.
