[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

the [Feeds Act](/eng/acts/F-9).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-242258 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[analyst]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[analyst]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated as an
    analyst pursuant to section 6; ([analyste]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel, aircraft, train,
    motor vehicle, trailer or other means of transportation, including a
    cargo container; ([véhicule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means anything on which information
    that is capable of being understood by a person, or read by a
    computer or other device, is recorded or
    marked; ([document]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[environment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[environment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the components of the
    Earth and includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} air, land and water,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all layers of the atmosphere,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all organic and inorganic matter and living
      organisms, and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the interacting natural systems that include
      components referred to in paragraphs (a) to
      (c); ([environnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any place, including a
    conveyance, where a feed is manufactured, stored, packaged or
    labelled; ([établissement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any substance or mixture of
    substances containing amino acids, anti-oxidants, carbohydrates,
    condiments, enzymes, fats, minerals, non-protein nitrogen products,
    proteins or vitamins, or pelletizing, colouring, foaming or
    flavouring agents and any other substance manufactured, sold or
    represented for use

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for consumption by livestock,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for providing the nutritional requirements of
      livestock, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of preventing or correcting
      nutritional disorders of livestock,

    or any substance for use in any such substance or mixture of
    substances; ([aliments]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inspection mark]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspection mark]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prescribed mark,
    stamp, seal, product legend, word or design or any combination of
    those things; ([sceau d'inspection]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated as an
    inspector pursuant to section 6; ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[item to which this Act applies]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[item to which this Act applies]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a feed,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} anything used in an activity regulated under this
      Act, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a document that is related to a feed or to any
      activity regulated under this Act; ([chose visée par la présente
      loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any legend, word, mark,
    symbol or design applied or attached to, included in, belonging to
    or accompanying any feed or package; ([étiquette]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[livestock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[livestock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any animals designated by
    regulation as livestock for the purposes of this Act; ([animaux de
    ferme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Agriculture
    and Agri-Food; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a sack, bag, barrel, case
    or any other container in which feeds are placed or
    packed; ([emballage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[penalty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[penalty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an administrative monetary
    penalty imposed under the [Agriculture and Agri-Food Administrative
    Monetary Penalties Act](/eng/acts/A-8.8) for a
    violation; ([sanction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by
    regulation; ([Version anglaise seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes agree to sell, offer for sale,
    expose for sale or have in possession for sale or distribute to one
    or more persons; ([vente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Review Tribunal continued
    by subsection 27(1) of the [Agriculture and Agri-Food Administrative
    Monetary Penalties
    Act](/eng/acts/A-8.8); ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[violation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[violation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any contravention of this
    Act or the regulations that may be proceeded with in accordance with
    the [Agriculture and Agri-Food Administrative Monetary Penalties
    Act](/eng/acts/A-8.8). ([violation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-242292 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence of registration, etc.

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall manufacture, sell or import into Canada any feed unless
  the feed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has, in accordance with the regulations, been
    approved by the Minister or registered;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} conforms to prescribed standards; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is packaged and labelled in accordance with the
    regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraphs (1)(a) and (b) do not apply to any feed
  consisting of whole seeds or grains of cultivated farm crops if it is
  free from prescribed deleterious substances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Feed presenting risk of harm

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall manufacture, sell, import or export
  in contravention of the regulations any feed that presents a risk of
  harm to human or animal health or the environment.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-9, s. 3
- 2015, c. 2, s. 54
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed activity with licence or
registration

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** No person shall conduct
a prescribed activity in respect of a prescribed feed that has been
imported for sale --- or that is to be exported or to be sent or
conveyed from one province to another --- unless the person is
authorized to do so by a registration made under subsection 5.2(1), by a
licence issued under that subsection or by both such a registration and
licence, as provided for in the regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2015, c. 2, s. 55
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed activity in registered
establishment

**[[3.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.2 .sectionLabel}** No person shall conduct
a prescribed activity in respect of a prescribed feed that has been
imported for sale --- or that is to be exported or to be sent or
conveyed from one province to another --- unless the activity is
conducted in an establishment registered under subsection 5.3(1) in
accordance with the regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2015, c. 2, s. 55
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of inspection mark

- **[[3.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless authorized by the
  regulations, no person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} apply or use an inspection mark; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} advertise or sell anything if the thing has an
    inspection mark on it or an inspection mark is used in connection
    with the thing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of similar mark

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} apply or use a thing that so resembles an
    inspection mark that it is likely to be mistaken for it; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} advertise or sell anything that has on it a thing
    referred to in paragraph (a) or that has a thing referred to in that
    paragraph used in connection with it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person found in possession of anything referred to
  in paragraph (1)(b) or (2)(b) is considered, in the absence of
  evidence to the contrary, to be in possession of it for the purpose of
  advertising or selling.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2015, c. 2, s. 55
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recall order --- [Canadian Food
Inspection Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-16.5)

**[[3.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall sell a
feed that is the subject of a recall order referred to in subsection
19(1) of the [Canadian Food Inspection Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-16.5).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2015, c. 2, s. 55
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act does not apply in
respect of a feed that is manufactured by a livestock producer if it is
not sold and has not had incorporated into it any drug or other
substance that presents a risk of harm to human or animal health or the
environment.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-9, s. 4
- 2015, c. 2, s. 55
:::

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-242334 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting applications for registration or for
    approval of feeds and the information to be furnished with the
    applications;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the registration of feeds and
    prescribing fees for registration;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} respecting the approval of feeds;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the duration and cancellation of the
    registration or approval of feeds;

  - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} respecting the manufacturing, sale, importation
    or exportation of any feed that presents a risk of harm to human or
    animal health or the environment;

  - [(c.2)]{.lawlabel} respecting the sending or conveying from one
    province to another or the importation or exportation of any feed;

  - [(c.3)]{.lawlabel} respecting the manufacturing or sale of any feed
    that is to be exported or to be sent or conveyed from one province
    to another;

  - [(c.4)]{.lawlabel} respecting the sale of any feed that has been
    imported;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} exempting, with or without conditions, any item to
    which this Act applies, or a person or activity in respect of a
    feed, from the application of this Act or the regulations or a
    provision of this Act or the regulations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the form, composition and other
    standards for feeds;

  - [(e.1)]{.lawlabel} prescribing standards for the manufacturing or
    the safety of feeds;

  - [(e.2)]{.lawlabel} prescribing inspection marks in respect of any
    feeds and regulating their application or use;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} respecting the packaging and labelling of feeds and
    packages thereof;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} respecting the taking of samples and the making of
    analyses for the purposes of this Act;

  - [(g.1)]{.lawlabel} requiring persons to take or keep samples of any
    feed, or its package or label, and to provide the Minister or an
    inspector with, or with access to, those samples, and respecting the
    manner in which those samples are to be taken or kept and the manner
    in which they are to be provided or access to them is to be
    provided;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} providing that feeds registered under this Act and
    containing a pest control product as defined in subsection 2(1) of
    the [Pest Control Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01) are, in prescribed
    circumstances and subject to prescribed conditions, deemed to be
    registered under that Act;

  - [(h.1)]{.lawlabel} respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the registration of persons or the issuing of
      licences to persons under section 5.2 or the registration of
      establishments under section 5.3,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the suspension, cancellation and renewal of
      those licences and registrations, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the amendment of those licences and
      registrations or of any of the conditions to which they are
      subject by reason of subsection 5.2(3) or 5.3(4);

  - [(h.2)]{.lawlabel} respecting quality management programs, quality
    control programs, safety programs, preventive control plans or any
    other similar programs or plans to be implemented by persons who
    conduct any activity regulated under this Act;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} designating specific animals, including birds, as
    livestock for the purposes of this Act;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} respecting the detention, preservation and
    safeguarding of anything seized under section 9;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} respecting the disposition of anything forfeited
    under section 9;

  - [(k.1)]{.lawlabel} respecting the evaluation of a feed, including
    regulations respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the provision of samples of the feed,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the provision of information in respect of the
      feed, including information that

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} permits the feed to be distinguished from other
        feeds, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} is required for evaluating the potential impact
        of the feed on, and the risk of harm posed by the feed to, human
        and animal health and the environment, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the evaluation of the potential impact of the
      feed on, and the risk of harm posed by the feed to, human and
      animal health and the environment;

  - [(k.2)]{.lawlabel} requiring persons to prepare, keep or maintain
    documents and to provide the Minister or an inspector with, or with
    access to, those documents, and respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the information in those documents,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which they are to be prepared,
      kept or maintained,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the place where they are to be kept or
      maintained, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which they are to be provided or
      access to them is to be provided;

  - [(k.3)]{.lawlabel} respecting the issuance of certificates or other
    documents for the purpose of section 5.5;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything else that by this Act is
    required to be prescribed; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} generally, for carrying out the purposes and
    provisions of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paragraphs (1)(c.1) and (c.2)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Regulations made under paragraph (1)(c.1) or (c.2)
  may, among other things, establish preclearance or in-transit
  requirements for any imported feed or anything imported with it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paragraph (1)(k.2)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Regulations made under paragraph (1)(k.2) may, among
  other things, require persons who conduct any activity regulated under
  this Act and who become aware that a feed presents a risk of harm to
  human or animal health or the environment or does not meet the
  requirements of the regulations to provide written notice to that
  effect to the Minister or an inspector.

## [Incorporation by Reference]{.HTitleText1} {#h-242382 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

- **[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  regulation made under subsection 5(1) may incorporate by reference any
  document, regardless of its source, either as it exists on a
  particular date or as it is amended from time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessibility

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must ensure that any document that is
  incorporated by reference in a regulation made under subsection 5(1),
  including any amendments to the document, is accessible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person is not liable to be found guilty of an
  offence or subjected to an administrative sanction for any
  contravention in respect of which a document that is incorporated by
  reference in a regulation made under subsection 5(1) is relevant
  unless, at the time of the alleged contravention, the document was
  accessible as required by subsection (2) or it was otherwise
  accessible to the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No registration or publication

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a document that is
  incorporated by reference in a regulation made under subsection 5(1)
  is not required to be transmitted for registration or published in the
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) by reason only that it is
  incorporated by reference.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2015, c. 2, s. 57
:::

## [Registrations and Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-242394 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons

- **[[5.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, on application, register a person, or issue a licence to
  a person, authorizing them to conduct a prescribed activity in respect
  of a prescribed feed that has been imported for sale --- or that is to
  be exported or to be sent or conveyed from one province to another ---
  or both register a person and issue them a licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions --- regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The registration and the licence are subject to the
  prescribed conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions --- Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may make a registration or licence
  subject to any additional conditions that he or she considers
  appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to comply

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The holder of the registration or licence must comply
  with all the conditions to which the registration or licence is
  subject.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No transfer

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The registration or licence is not transferable.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2015, c. 2, s. 57
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishments

- **[[5.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, on application, register an establishment as one where a
  prescribed activity may be conducted in respect of a prescribed feed
  that has been imported for sale or that is to be exported or to be
  sent or conveyed from one province to another.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Holder

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The applicant in respect of an establishment is the
  holder of the registration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions --- regulations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The registration is subject to the prescribed
  conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions --- Minister

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may make a registration subject to any
  additional conditions that he or she considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to comply

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The holder of the registration must comply with all
  the conditions to which the registration is subject.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No transfer

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The registration is not transferable.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2015, c. 2, s. 57
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment, suspension, cancellation and
renewal

**[[5.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the
regulations, the Minister may amend, suspend, cancel or renew a
registration made under subsection 5.2(1) or 5.3(1) or a licence issued
under subsection 5.2(1).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2015, c. 2, s. 57

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-242444 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of inspectors and analysts

  President of the Canadian Food Inspection Agency may designate under
  section 13 of the [Canadian Food Inspection Agency
  Act](/eng/acts/C-16.5) the inspectors and analysts necessary for the
  administration and enforcement of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The President of the Canada Border Services Agency
  may designate inspectors under paragraph 9(2)(b) of the [Canada Border
  Services Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-1.4) for the purposes of enforcing

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate to be produced

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Inspectors shall be given certificates in a form
  established by the President of the Canadian Food Inspection Agency or
  the President of the Canada Border Services Agency, as the case may
  be, attesting to their designation and, on entering any place under
  subsection 7(1), an inspector shall, if so required, produce the
  certificate to the person in charge of that place.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of inspectors

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (1.1), an inspector may, for a purpose related to
  verifying compliance or preventing non-compliance with this Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter any place in which the inspector believes on
    reasonable grounds there is any feed to which this Act applies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} open any package found in that place that the
    inspector believes on reasonable grounds contains any such feed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} examine the feed and take samples thereof;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} require any person to produce for inspection or for
    the purpose of obtaining copies thereof or extracts therefrom any
    books, shipping bills, bills of lading, documents containing mixing
    instructions, or other documents or papers with respect to the
    administration of this Act or the regulations; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} remove anything from that place for the purpose of
    examination, conducting tests or taking samples.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant required to enter
  dwelling-house

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Where any place referred to in paragraph (1)(a) is
  a dwelling-house, an inspector may not enter that dwelling-house
  without the consent of the occupant except under the authority of a
  warrant issued under subsection (1.2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} Where on [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  application a justice of the peace is satisfied by information on oath

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the conditions for entry described in
    paragraph (1)(a) exist in relation to a dwelling-house,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that entry to the dwelling-house is necessary for
    any purpose relating to the administration or enforcement of this
    Act, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that entry to the dwelling-house has been refused
    or that there are reasonable grounds for believing that entry
    thereto will be refused,

  the justice of the peace may issue a warrant under his hand
  authorizing the inspector named therein to enter that dwelling-house
  subject to such conditions as may be specified in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

  [(1.3)]{.lawlabel} In executing a warrant issued under subsection
  (1.2), the inspector named therein shall not use force unless the
  inspector is accompanied by a peace officer and the use of force has
  been specifically authorized in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to inspectors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner or person in charge of any place described
  in subsection (1) and every person found in that place shall give an
  inspector all reasonable assistance to enable the inspector to carry
  out his duties and functions under this Act and shall furnish the
  inspector with any information he may reasonably require with respect
  to the administration of this Act and the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of documents, information or
  samples

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An inspector may, for a purpose related to verifying
  compliance or preventing non-compliance with this Act, order a person
  to provide, on the date, at the time and place and in the manner
  specified by the inspector, any document, information or sample
  specified by the inspector.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction of inspectors

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall obstruct or hinder an inspector in the carrying out of
  his duties or functions under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False statements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall make a false or misleading statement
  either orally or in writing to an inspector or other officer engaged
  in carrying out his duties or functions under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-7, s. 9

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seizure

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an
  inspector believes on reasonable grounds that this Act or the
  regulations have been contravened, the inspector may seize any article
  by means of or in relation to which the inspector believes on
  reasonable grounds the contravention was committed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Release of seized article

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an inspector is satisfied that the provisions of
  this Act and the regulations that apply with respect to an article
  seized under this Act have been complied with, the article must be
  released.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Tribunal decides that a person has
  committed a violation, or a person is convicted of an offence under
  this Act, the Tribunal or the convicting court, as the case may be,
  may, in addition to any penalty or punishment imposed, order that any
  article by means of or in relation to which the violation or offence
  was committed, be forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- R.S., 1985, c. F-9, s. 9
- 1995, c. 40, s. 47

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal or destruction of unlawful
imports

- **[[9.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector who has reasonable grounds to believe that an imported feed
  does not meet the requirements of the regulations or was imported in
  contravention of a provision of this Act or the regulations may, by
  notice, whether the feed is seized or not, order its owner or importer
  or the person having possession, care or control of it to remove it
  from Canada or, if removal is not possible, to destroy it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must either be delivered personally to the
  owner or importer of the feed or to the person having possession, care
  or control of it or be sent by registered mail to the owner's,
  importer's or person's address in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the feed is not removed from Canada or destroyed
  within the period specified in the notice --- or, if no period was
  specified, within 90 days after the day on which the notice was
  delivered or sent --- it is, despite subsection 9(2), forfeited to Her
  Majesty in right of Canada and may be disposed of as the Minister may
  direct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of application of subsection
  (3)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An inspector may, for a period that he or she
  specifies, suspend the application of subsection (3) if he or she is
  satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} harm to human or animal health or the environment
    is unlikely to result;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the feed will not be sold within that period;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the measures that should have been taken for the
    feed not to have been imported in contravention of a provision of
    this Act or the regulations will be taken within that period; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the feed does not meet the requirements of the
    regulations, it will be brought into compliance with the regulations
    within that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An inspector may cancel the notice if he or she is
  satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} harm to human or animal health or the environment
    is unlikely to result;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the feed has not been sold within the period
    referred to in subsection (6);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the measures referred to in paragraph (4)(c) were
    taken within that period; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the feed did not meet the requirements of the
    regulations when it was imported, it was brought into compliance
    with the regulations within that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The period for the purposes of subsection (5) is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the application of subsection (3) was suspended
    under subsection (4), the period of the suspension; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the application of subsection (3) was not
    suspended, the period specified in the notice or, if no period was
    specified, the period that ends 90 days after the day on which the
    notice was delivered or sent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of [Statutory
  Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply in respect of the notice.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2015, c. 2, s. 60
:::

## [Limitation on Liability]{.HTitleText1} {#h-242528 .Part}

in right of Canada is not liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for any costs, loss or damage resulting from the
  compliance; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to pay any fee, including any rent or charge, for

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-242539 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who contravenes any provision of this Act or the regulations or
  fails to do anything that the person is ordered to do by an inspector
  under this Act is guilty of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an offence punishable on summary conviction and
    liable to a fine not exceeding \$50,000 or to imprisonment for a
    term not exceeding six months, or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an indictable offence and liable to a fine not
    exceeding \$250,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two
    years, or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parties to offence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person other than an individual commits an
  offence under subsection (1), any of the person's directors, officers
  or agents or mandataries who directs, authorizes, assents to or
  acquiesces or participates in the commission of the offence is a party
  to the offence and is liable on conviction to the punishment provided
  for by this Act, even if the person is not prosecuted for the offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of offence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In a prosecution for an offence under this Act, it is
  sufficient proof of the offence to establish that it was committed by
  an employee or an agent or mandatary of the accused, even if the
  employee or the agent or mandatary is not identified or prosecuted for
  the offence, unless the accused establishes that the offence was
  committed without the knowledge or consent of the accused and that the
  accused exercised all due diligence to prevent its commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Summary conviction proceedings for an offence under
  this Act may be instituted no later than two years after the day on
  which the subject matter of the proceedings arises.
