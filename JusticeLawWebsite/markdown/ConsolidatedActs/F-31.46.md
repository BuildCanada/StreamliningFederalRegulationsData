## Framework Agreement on First Nation Land Management Act {#framework-agreement-on-first-nation-land-management-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2022, c. 19, s. 121
:::

Assented to 2022-12-15

An Act respecting the Framework Agreement on First Nation Land
Management

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 121 of chapter 19 of the Statutes of Canada, 2022,
in force on assent December 15, 2022.\]
:::
:::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas Her Majesty in right of Canada and 13 First Nations signed the
Framework Agreement on First Nation Land Management on February 12, 1996
in relation to the management by those First Nations of their lands, and
other First Nations have signed the Framework Agreement after that date;

Whereas the [First Nations Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.8), the
purpose of which was to ratify and bring into effect the Framework
Agreement, received royal assent on June 17, 1999;

And whereas it is appropriate to enact a new implementation Act to
replace the [First Nations Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.8) and to
reflect the primacy of the Framework Agreement, while also ensuring the
continuity with the previous legal framework;
:::

::: Enacts
Now therefore, His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1385591 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Framework Agreement on First Nation Land Management
Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1385594 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[First Nation land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[First Nation land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means reserve land or
      lands set aside to which a land code applies. It includes all the
      interests or land rights in, and resources of, the land that are
      within the legislative authority of Parliament. ([terres de la
      première nation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[First Nation law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[First Nation law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a law that is
      enacted by a First Nation in accordance with the Framework
      Agreement and the land code of that First Nation. ([texte
      législatif de la première nation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Framework Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Framework Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Framework
      Agreement on First Nation Land Management, signed by Her Majesty
      in right of Canada and 13 First Nations on February 12, 1996 ---
      and signed by other First Nations after that date --- as amended
      from time to time in accordance with its
      provisions. ([accord-cadre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
      Crown-Indigenous Relations. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Indian
      Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([réserve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions in Framework Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Act, [[First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
  [[First Nation Lands Register]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
  [[interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[land code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
  [[land right]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[Lands Advisory
  Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have
  the same meaning as in subclause 1.1 of the Framework Agreement and
  [[lands set aside]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
  [[Lands Set Aside]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in that subclause 1.1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not a treaty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Framework Agreement is
not a treaty within the meaning of section 35 of the [Constitution Act,
1982](/eng/Const/).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on His Majesty

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on His
Majesty in right of Canada.

## [Framework Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1385609 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Force of law

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Framework Agreement continues to have effect and has the force of law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and obligations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a person or body has the
  powers, rights, privileges, immunities and benefits conferred on the
  person or body by the Framework Agreement and must perform the duties,
  and is subject to the liabilities, imposed on the person or body by
  the Framework Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Framework Agreement binding

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Framework Agreement is
  binding on, and may be relied on by, all persons and bodies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Framework Agreement prevails

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  event of any inconsistency or conflict between the Framework Agreement
  and this Act, the Framework Agreement prevails to the extent of the
  inconsistency or conflict.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act prevails

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of any inconsistency or conflict between
  this Act and any other federal law, this Act prevails to the extent of
  the inconsistency or conflict.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must make
available to the public

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Framework Agreement that was signed by Her
  Majesty in right of Canada and 13 First Nations on February 12, 1996
  and any amendments made to it; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the most recent consolidated version of the Framework
  Agreement that has been approved by the Minister and the Lands
  Advisory Board.

## [Rights and Obligations of His Majesty in Right of Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1385626 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** On the coming into force of
the land code of a First Nation, the rights and obligations of His
Majesty in right of Canada as grantor in respect of the interests or
land rights and the licences described in the First Nation's individual
agreement referred to in clause 6 of the Framework Agreement are
transferred to the First Nation in accordance with that individual
agreement.

## [First Nation Lands Register]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1385629 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Register continued

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The First Nation Land
Register established by the Minister under section 25 of the [First
Nations Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.8), chapter 24 of the
Statutes of Canada, 1999, is continued as the First Nation Lands
Register.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, on the recommendation of the Minister and in accordance with the
Framework Agreement, make regulations respecting the administration of
the First Nation Lands Register, the registration of interests or land
rights in it and the recording of any other matter in it, including but
not limited to regulations respecting

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the effects of registering interests or land rights,
  including priorities;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the payment of fees for the registration of interests
  or land rights and for any other service in relation to the Register;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the appointment, remuneration, powers, duties and
  functions of the officers and employees who administer the Register;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the keeping, by officers and employees, of documents
  that are not registrable; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the transfer of the administration of the Register to
  any person or body and the disclosure of [[personal
  information]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 3 of the
  [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21) for that purpose.

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1385639 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Judicial notice must be
taken of the Framework Agreement, a land code that is in force and any
First Nation law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  issue is raised by a party in any judicial or administrative
  proceeding in respect of the interpretation or validity of a provision
  of the Framework Agreement, this Act, a land code or a First Nation
  law, the issue must not be decided until the party has served notice

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the Attorney General of Canada and the Lands
    Advisory Board, in the case of a provision of the Framework
    Agreement or this Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the First Nation, in the case of a provision of
    a land code or a First Nation law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content and timing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} describe the proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} state the subject matter of the issue;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state the date on which the issue is to be argued;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} give the particulars that are necessary to show the
    point to be argued; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be served at least 30 days before the day on which
    the issue is to be argued, unless the court or tribunal authorizes a
    shorter period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation in proceedings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In any proceeding, the recipient of a notice may
  appear and participate as a party with the same rights as any other
  party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsections (2) and (3) do not
  require that an oral hearing be held if one is not otherwise required.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of [Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The [Statutory
Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does not apply in respect of a land
code, a First Nation law or an order made under subclause 14.2.1 of the
Framework Agreement.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2022, c. 19, s. 122

  - ###### Definition of [[former Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} {#definition-of-former-act .MarginalNote}

    - [122]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this section and sections 123
      and 124, [[former Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [First
      Nations Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.8), chapter 24 of the
      Statutes of Canada, 1999.

    - ###### Other definitions {#other-definitions .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} In sections 123 and 124, [[First Nation
      law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[Framework
      Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[individual
      agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
      [[land code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
      and [[right]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have the same meaning as in
      subsection 2(1) of the former Act, as it read immediately before
      the day on which section 143 of this Act comes into force.

- --- 2022, c. 19, s. 123

  - ###### Documents and decisions previously made {#documents-and-decisions-previously-made .MarginalNote}

    [123]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act affects

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any land codes, individual agreements, First
      Nation laws or documents, including licences and other
      authorizations, contracts and other instruments or acts, that were
      issued, granted, entered into or made in accordance with the
      former Act or the Framework Agreement and that are in force on the
      day on which this Act comes into force;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any actions taken or decisions made, before the
      day on which this Act comes into force, in accordance with the
      former Act or the Framework Agreement, including the registration
      of an interest or right in the First Nation Land Register, as
      established under section 25 of the former Act and the
      establishment of priorities or, in Quebec, rankings among
      interests or rights that have been registered.

- --- 2022, c. 19, s. 124

  - ###### Reference to former Act {#reference-to-former-act .MarginalNote}

    [124]{.lawlabel} Unless the context otherwise requires, any
    reference to the former Act, or any provision of that Act, in any
    land codes, individual agreements, First Nation laws or documents
    referred to in paragraph 123(a) is to be read as a reference to the
    [Framework Agreement on First Nation Land Management
    Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46) or the Framework Agreement, or any relevant
    provision of that Act or Agreement, as the case may be.
