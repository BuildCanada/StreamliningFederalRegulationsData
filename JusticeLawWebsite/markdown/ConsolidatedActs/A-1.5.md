## Administrative Tribunals Support Service of Canada Act {#administrative-tribunals-support-service-of-canada-act .Title-of-Act}

An Act to establish the Administrative Tribunals Support Service of
Canada

in force November 1, 2014, see SI/2014-83.\]

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-89 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Administrative Tribunals Support Service of Canada
Act](/eng/acts/A-1.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-92 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[administrative tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[administrative tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a body
    referred to in the schedule to this Act. ([tribunal
    administratif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Chief Administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chief Administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person
    appointed under subsection 5(1). ([administrateur en
    chef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Justice. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Administrative Tribunals
    Support Service of Canada established by section
    3. ([Service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Administrative Tribunals Support Service of Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-99 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of Service

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Administrative
Tribunals Support Service of Canada, consisting of the Chief
Administrator and employees of the Service, is established as a portion
of the federal public administration.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principal office

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  principal office of the Service is to be in the National Capital
  Region described in the schedule to the [National Capital
  Act](/eng/acts/N-4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other offices

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Administrator may establish other offices
  of the Service elsewhere in Canada.

## [Chief Administrator]{.HTitleText1} {#h-107 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Administrator is to be appointed by the Governor in Council to
  hold office during pleasure for a term of up to five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Administrator is eligible for
  re-appointment at the end of each term of office.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of Chief Administrator

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Chief Administrator has
the rank and status of a deputy head of a department.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Chief Administrator is absent or incapacitated or the office of Chief
  Administrator is vacant, the Minister must appoint another person to
  act as Chief Administrator, but a person must not be so appointed for
  a term of more than 90 days without the approval of the Governor in
  Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person acting as Chief Administrator has all of
  the powers, duties and functions of the Chief Administrator conferred
  under this Act or any other Act of Parliament.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salary and expenses

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Administrator is to be paid the remuneration that may be fixed
  by the Governor in Council and is entitled to be paid reasonable
  travel and living expenses incurred in the exercise of his or her
  powers or the performance of his or her duties and functions while
  absent from the Chief Administrator's ordinary place of work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Administrator is deemed to be a person
  employed in the public service for the purposes of the [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and to be employed in the federal
  public administration for the purposes of the [Government Employees
  Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and any regulations made under
  section 9 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chief executive officer

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Chief Administrator is
the chief executive officer of the Service and has the control and
management of the Service and all matters connected with it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibility

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Chief Administrator
is responsible for the provision of the support services and the
facilities that are needed by each of the administrative tribunals to
exercise its powers and perform its duties and functions in accordance
with the rules that apply to its work.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General powers

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Administrator has all the powers that are necessary to perform
  his or her duties and functions under this or any other Act of
  Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contracts, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Administrator may enter into contracts,
  memoranda of understanding or other arrangements, including contracts
  to engage the services of legal counsel or other persons having
  professional, technical or specialized knowledge to advise or assist
  an administrative tribunal or any of its members.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Chief Administrator's
powers, duties and functions do not extend to any of the powers, duties
and functions conferred by law on any administrative tribunal or on any
of its members.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Chief Administrator
may delegate to any employee of the Service any of the powers, duties,
and functions conferred on the Chief Administrator under this Act or any
other Act of Parliament.

## [Administrative Tribunal Chairpersons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-138 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
the chairperson of an administrative tribunal continues to have
supervision over and direction of the work of the tribunal.

## [Employees of the Service]{.HTitleText1} {#h-141 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of employees

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The employees who are
required for the purposes of the Service are to be appointed under the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-144 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming  --- filing documents and giving
notice

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Any provision of an Act
of Parliament, or of a regulation, that requires the filing of a
document with, or the giving of a notice to, an administrative tribunal
is deemed to require that document to be filed with, or the notice to be
given to, the Service, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming --- amounts payable

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Any provision of an Act
of Parliament, or of a regulation, that requires an amount to be paid to
an administrative tribunal is deemed to require that amount to be paid
to the Service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts for operation of administrative
tribunal

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Any amount to be paid for
the operation of any administrative tribunal may be paid out of moneys
appropriated by Parliament for the expenditures of the Service.
