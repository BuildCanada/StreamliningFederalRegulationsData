## Sports Pool and Loto Canada Winding-Up Act {#sports-pool-and-loto-canada-winding-up-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1985, c. 22
:::

Assented to 1985-06-20

An Act respecting the winding-up of the Canadian Sports Pool Corporation
and Loto Canada Inc.
::::

::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-437239 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Sports Pool and Loto Canada Winding-Up Act](/eng/acts/S-15.8).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-437242 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Sports Pool
    Corporation established by section 3 of the Athletic Contests and
    Events Pools Act; ([Société]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Her Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Her Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Her Majesty in right of
    Canada; ([Sa Majesté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Health. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- 1996, c. 8, s. 32
:::

## [Canadian Sports Pool Corporation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-437250 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation ceases to exist

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation hereby
ceases to exist.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of property and obligations of
the Corporation

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  rights and property held by or in the name of or in trust for the
  Corporation and all obligations and liabilities of the Corporation are
  deemed to be rights, property, obligations and liabilities of Her
  Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Closing out affairs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may do and perform all acts and things
  necessary for or incidental to closing out the affairs of the
  Corporation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repeal

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Athletic Contests and
Events Pools Act is repealed.

## [Loto Canada Inc.]{.HTitleText1} {#h-437260 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of property and obligations of
Loto Canada Inc.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  rights and property held by or in the name of or in trust for Loto
  Canada Inc. and all obligations and liabilities of Loto Canada Inc.
  are deemed to be rights, property, obligations and liabilities of Her
  Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dissolution and closing out affairs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may do and perform all acts and things
  necessary for or incidental to procuring the dissolution and closing
  out of the affairs of Loto Canada Inc.

## [Legal Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-437266 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal proceedings

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  action, suit or other legal proceeding in respect of an obligation or
  liability incurred by the Corporation or Loto Canada Inc., or incurred
  by the Minister in closing out the affairs of the Corporation or Loto
  Canada Inc., may be brought or taken against Her Majesty in any court
  that would have had jurisdiction if the action, suit or other legal
  proceeding had been brought or taken against the Corporation or Loto
  Canada Inc., as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal proceeding pending in
  any court against the Corporation or Loto Canada Inc. may be continued
  against Her Majesty to the same extent as it could, but for the coming
  into force of this Act, have been continued against the Corporation or
  Loto Canada Inc., as the case may be.
