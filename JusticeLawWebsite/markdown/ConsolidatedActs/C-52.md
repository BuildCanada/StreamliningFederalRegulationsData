## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Currency and Coinage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-142353 .Part}

### [Monetary Unit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-142354 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Monetary unit

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  monetary unit of Canada is the dollar.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Denominations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The denominations of money in the currency of Canada
  are dollars and cents, the cent being one hundredth of a dollar.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-52, s. 3
- 1999, c. 4, s. 10
:::

**[[4 to 6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4_to_6 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
R.S., 1985, c. 35 (3rd Supp.), s. 17\]]{.Repealed}

### [Current Coins and Notes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-142363 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Current coins

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A coin
  is current for the amount of its denomination in the currency of
  Canada if it was issued under the authority of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the [Royal Canadian Mint Act](/eng/acts/R-9); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Crown in any province of Canada before it
    became part of Canada and if the coin was, immediately before
    October 15, 1952, current and legal tender in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defaced coins not current

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No coin that is bent, mutilated or defaced, or that
  has been reduced in weight otherwise than by abrasion through ordinary
  use, shall pass current.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-52, s. 7
- 1999, c. 4, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Current notes

**[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** A note is current for
the amount of its denomination in the currency of Canada if it was
issued under the authority of the [Bank of Canada Act](/eng/acts/B-2).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2018, c. 12, s. 228
:::

### [Legal Tender]{.HTitleText2} {#h-142377 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal tender

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this section, a tender of payment of money is a legal tender if it
  is made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in coins that are current under section 7; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in notes that are current under section 7.1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A tender of payment in coins referred to in
  subsection (1) is a legal tender for no more than the following
  amounts for the following denominations of coins:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} forty dollars if the denomination is two dollars or
    greater but does not exceed ten dollars;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} twenty-five dollars if the denomination is one
    dollar;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ten dollars if the denomination is ten cents or
    greater but less than one dollar;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} five dollars if the denomination is five cents; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} twenty-five cents if the denomination is one cent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coins of denominations greater than ten
  dollars

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} In the case of coins of a denomination greater than
  ten dollars, a tender of payment referred to in subsection (1) may
  consist of not more than one coin, and the tender of payment is a
  legal tender for no more than the value of a single coin of that
  denomination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Different amounts payable on the same
  day

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (2) and (2.1), where
  more than one amount is payable by one person to another on the same
  day under one or more obligations, the total of those amounts is
  deemed to be one amount due and payable on that day.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2012, c. 19, s. 388\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote

- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (3rd Supp.), s. 18

- 2012, c. 19, s. 388
- 2018, c. 12, s. 229
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calling in of coins and notes

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, by order, call in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} coins of any date and denomination that are current
    under section 7; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any notes that are current under section 7.1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of call in

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite sections 7 and 7.1, a coin or note that has
  been called in is not current.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-52, s. 9
- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (3rd Supp.), s. 19
- 1999, c. 4, s. 13(F)
- 2012, c. 19, s. 389
- 2018, c. 12, s. 230
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Redemption of coins

- **[[9.01]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.01
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make
  regulations for the redemption by the Minister of coins of the
  currency of Canada that are or that have at any time been current in
  Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments for redemption of coins

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Payments for the redemption of coins, including
  related costs, shall be made out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund on
  the authorization of the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2012, c. 19, s. 389
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts received from issue of coins

**[[9.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.1 .sectionLabel}** All amounts received
from the issue of coins of the currency of Canada must be paid into the
Consolidated Revenue Fund.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (3rd Supp.), s. 20
- 1999, c. 4, s. 14
:::

### [Counterfeit Coins]{.HTitleText2} {#h-142417 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defacement of counterfeit coins

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every officer employed in
the collection of the revenue in Canada shall cause to be cut, broken or
defaced every counterfeit coin that is paid to the officer in payment of
an amount payable to Her Majesty, and shall forthwith forward the
counterfeit coin to the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-39, s. 9
:::

### [Melting Coins]{.HTitleText2} {#h-142422 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Melting down coins

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall, except in accordance with a licence granted by the
  Minister, melt down, break up or use otherwise than as currency any
  coin that is current and legal tender in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes subsection (1) or any
  condition attached to a licence referred to in that subsection is
  liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding two hundred and
  fifty dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding twelve
  months or to both, and, in addition to any fine or imprisonment
  imposed, the court may order that the articles by means of or in
  relation to which the offence was committed be forfeited to Her
  Majesty.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-39, s. 10
:::

### [Accounts, Contracts and other Matters Relating to Money]{.HTitleText2} {#h-142430 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public accounts and statements

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** All public accounts
established or maintained in Canada shall be in the currency of Canada,
and any reference to money or monetary value in any indictment or other
legal proceedings shall be stated in the currency of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-39, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contracts, etc.

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  contract, sale, payment, bill, note, instrument and security for money
  and every transaction, dealing, matter and thing relating to money or
  involving the payment of or the liability to pay money shall be made,
  executed, entered into, done or carried out in the currency of Canada,
  unless it is made, executed, entered into, done or carried out in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the currency of a country other than Canada; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a unit of account that is defined in terms of the
    currencies of two or more countries.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Previous contracts, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), every contract, sale,
  payment, bill, note, instrument and security for money and every
  transaction, dealing, matter and thing relating to money or involving
  the liability to pay money that was made, executed or entered into,
  done or carried out before October 15, 1952 shall, in so far as
  anything remained or remains to be or may be executed, done or carried
  out thereunder on or after that date, be construed and operate without
  reference to this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-39, s. 12
- 1976-77, c. 38, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sums mentioned in Constitution and Acts

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Any sum mentioned in
dollars and cents in the [Constitution Acts, 1867 to 1982](/eng/Const/)
and in any Act of Parliament shall, unless it is otherwise expressed, be
construed as being a sum in the currency of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-39, s. 13
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Valuation of gold, etc.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding any other
law, where any law of Canada or any treaty, convention, contract or
agreement to which Canada is a party makes reference to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a currency of a country other than Canada,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a unit of account that is defined in terms of
  currencies of two or more countries,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} gold, or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a combination of any of the things mentioned in
  paragraphs (a) to (c),

the Governor in Council may make regulations specifying, or specifying
the means or method of ascertaining, determining or calculating, the
equivalent dollar value of that currency, unit of account, gold or
combination thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1976-77, c. 38, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conversion of foreign currencies for
purposes of customs laws

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
administration, application and operation of the laws relating to the
customs, the Governor in Council may make regulations specifying, or
specifying the means or method of ascertaining, determining or
calculating, the equivalent dollar value of currencies of countries
other than Canada for a day or any other period or generally.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1984, c. 25, s. 100
:::

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Exchange Fund Account]{.HTitleText1} {#h-142461 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exchange Fund Account continued

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  special account in the name of the Minister known as the Exchange Fund
  Account is continued, and all assets acquired and held by or on behalf
  of the Minister in the Exchange Fund Account continue to be so held.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purposes of Account

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The purposes of the Exchange Fund Account are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to aid in the control and protection of the
    external value of the monetary unit of Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to provide a source of liquidity for the Government
    of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-52, s. 17
- 1993, c. 33, s. 1
- 2005, c. 30, s. 113
- 2018, c. 12, s. 223
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-17-20051230.html "Link to previous version of section 17"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Policy

- **[[17.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may establish a policy
  concerning the investment of the assets held in the Exchange Fund
  Account, including concerning the acquisition of assets to be held in
  the Exchange Fund Account, based on principles that a person of
  ordinary prudence would apply in dealing with the property of others.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-delegation of power

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may not delegate the power to establish
  the policy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply to the policy.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 30, s. 113
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to acquire, sell, etc.

- **[[17.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may acquire or borrow
  assets to be held in the Exchange Fund Account, and sell or lend those
  assets, in order to carry out the policy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial transactions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may carry out any transaction of a
  financial nature concerning assets held in the Exchange Fund Account
  that is in accordance with the policy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agents and mandataries

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may appoint agents and mandataries to
  perform services concerning the Exchange Fund Account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation in writing

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may delegate the powers set out in
  subsections (1) to (3) only in writing.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 30, s. 113
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crediting of Account

**[[17.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17.3 .sectionLabel}** All amounts received
by the Minister from transactions carried out under subsections 17.2(1)
and (2) shall be credited to the Exchange Fund Account.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 30, s. 113
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Expenses determined by
the Minister to have been incurred in respect of the operation of the
Exchange Fund Account shall be paid out of the Account.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-39, s. 20
- 1984, c. 9, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advances out of and payments into C.R.F.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, on any
terms and conditions that he or she considers appropriate, authorize

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} advances to the Exchange Fund Account out of the
  Consolidated Revenue Fund; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} payments out of the Exchange Fund Account into the
  Consolidated Revenue Fund.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-52, s. 19
- 2005, c. 30, s. 114
- 2018, c. 12, s. 224
:::

Version](section-19-20051230.html "Link to previous version of section 19"){.PITLink}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Net income credited to C.R.F.

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  three months after the end of each fiscal year, the amount of the net
  income of the Exchange Fund Account for the fiscal year, expressed in
  dollars, shall be paid into the Consolidated Revenue Fund if the net
  income for the year is a positive amount, and shall be charged to that
  Fund if the net income for the year is a negative amount.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consistency with Public Accounts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The net income of the Exchange Fund Account for a
  fiscal year shall be determined in accordance with accounting
  principles consistent with those applied in preparing the Public
  Accounts referred to in section 64 of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-52, s. 20
- R.S., 1985, c. 3 (4th Supp.), s. 1
- 2005, c. 30, s. 114
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Parliament

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall cause to be tabled before each House of Parliament a
  report on the operation of the Exchange Fund Account for each fiscal
  year within the first 60 days on which that House is sitting after the
  end of that fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report referred to in subsection (1) shall
  include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the policy established under
    subsection 17.1(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the objectives of the Exchange Fund Account for
    that fiscal year, and a statement of whether those objectives have
    been met;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the objectives of the Exchange Fund Account for the
    current fiscal year;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the financial statements of the Exchange Fund
    Account; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a list of the agents and mandataries appointed by
    the Minister under subsection 17.2(3).

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2012, c. 19, s. 172\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-52, s. 21
- 2005, c. 30, s. 114
- 2012, c. 19, s. 172

  - ###### First fiscal year --- payment into C.R.F. {#first-fiscal-year-payment-into-c.r.f. .MarginalNote}

    this Act, applies.

    Parliament under section 21 of the [Currency Act](/eng/acts/C-52),

    be the first fiscal year to which section 21 of that Act, as enacted
    by section 114 of this Act, applies.

  - ###### [Currency Act](/eng/acts/C-52) {#currency-act-1 .MarginalNote}

    of the [Currency Act](/eng/acts/C-52), as those subsections read
    immediately before the day on which this Act receives royal assent,
    continue to apply in respect of the fiscal year beginning on April
    1, 2012 but do not apply in respect of any subsequent fiscal year.
