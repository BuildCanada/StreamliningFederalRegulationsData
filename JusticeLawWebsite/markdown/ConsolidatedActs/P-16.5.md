## Postal Services Resumption and Continuation Act {#postal-services-resumption-and-continuation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2018, c. 25
:::

Assented to 2018-11-26

An Act to provide for the resumption and continuation of postal services
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the Canada Post Corporation and the Canadian Union of Postal
Workers were parties to two collective agreements, one that expired on
December 31, 2017 and one that expired on January 31, 2018;

Whereas the parties have engaged, since November 2017, in collective
bargaining to reach new collective agreements;

Whereas the Minister of Labour and the Minister of Public Works and
Government Services met with the parties on a number of occasions to
encourage them to reach new collective agreements;

Whereas the Minister of Labour appointed conciliation officers in July
2018 to assist the parties in their negotiations and the conciliation
period expired without the parties having entered into new collective
agreements;

Whereas the Minister of Labour appointed mediators in September 2018 to
further assist the parties in the negotiation of a settlement of their
differences for the purpose of renewing their collective agreements;

Whereas work stoppages commenced on October 22, 2018;

Whereas the Minister of Labour appointed a special mediator on October
24, 2018, and then re-appointed the special mediator on November 7,
2018, to assist the parties in negotiating new collective agreements and
in ending the work stoppages, and in each case the appointment ended
without the parties having entered into new collective agreements and
without the work stoppages having ended;

Whereas the work stoppages are disrupting the delivery of mail and
parcels across Canada;

Whereas the work stoppages are having a significant adverse impact on
Canadian workers, consumers and businesses as well as on those Canadians
who rely on postal services;

Whereas the Minister of Labour recognizes the importance of effective
collective bargaining practices and the need for stable industrial
relations for employees, unions and employers in the postal services
sector;

And whereas, having regard to the negative impact of the work stoppages,
the public interest requires an exceptional solution to address the
matters in dispute so that new collective agreements may be concluded;
:::

::: Enacts
Now therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-395694 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Postal Services Resumption and Continuation Act](/eng/acts/P-16.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-395697 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[collective agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[collective agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the collective
      agreement between the employer and the union that expired on
      December 31, 2017 or the collective agreement between the employer
      and the union that expired on January 31, 2018. ([convention
      collective]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is employed by
      the employer and bound by a collective
      agreement. ([employé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Post
      Corporation. ([employeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[mediator-arbitrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mediator-arbitrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      mediator-arbitrator appointed under subsection
      8(2). ([médiateur-arbitre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
      Labour. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Union of Postal
      Workers, representing the Urban Postal Operations Unit or the
      Rural and Suburban Mail Carriers
      Unit. ([syndicat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Words and expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise provided, words and expressions used
  in this Act have the same meaning as in Part I of the [Canada Labour
  Code](/eng/acts/L-2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, the union is deemed to
  be a person.

## [Postal Services]{.HTitleText1} {#h-395711 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resumption or continuation of postal
services

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** On the coming into force of
this Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the employer must resume without delay, or continue,
  as the case may be, postal services; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} every employee must, when so required, resume without
  delay, or continue, as the case may be, the duties of their
  employment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibitions

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for the
employer and for any of its officers and representatives to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in any manner impede any employee from complying with
  paragraph 3(b); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} discharge or in any other manner discipline, or
  authorize or direct the discharge or discipline of, any employee by
  reason of the employee having been on strike before the coming into
  force of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The union and each of its
officers and representatives must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} without delay on the coming into force of this Act,
  give notice to the employees that, by reason of that coming into
  force, postal services are to be resumed or continued, as the case may
  be, and that the employees, when so required, are to resume without
  delay, or continue, the duties of their employment; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} take all reasonable steps to ensure that employees
  comply with paragraph 3(b).

## [Extension of Collective Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-395724 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The term
  of the collective agreement that expired on December 31, 2017 is
  extended to include the period beginning on January 1, 2018 and ending
  immediately before a new collective agreement between the parties
  comes into effect, except for any period during which there is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a strike against the employer in respect of the
    bargaining unit to which the collective agreement applies; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a lockout against the union in respect of the
    bargaining unit to which the collective agreement applies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The term of the collective agreement that expired on
  January 31, 2018 is extended to include the period beginning on
  February 1, 2018 and ending immediately before a new collective
  agreement between the parties comes into effect, except for any period
  during which there is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a strike against the employer in respect of the
    bargaining unit to which the collective agreement applies; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a lockout against the union in respect of the
    bargaining unit to which the collective agreement applies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collective agreement binding for
  extended term

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A collective agreement, as extended by subsection (1)
  or (2), is effective and binding on the parties to it for the period
  for which it is extended, despite anything in the collective agreement
  or in Part I of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2). However, that
  Part applies in respect of the collective agreement, as extended, as
  if that period were the term of the collective agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arbitration of discipline or discharge

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any person employed by the employer who is
  disciplined or discharged during any period that is excluded under
  subsection (1) or (2), and who, but for the exclusion, would have been
  bound by the collective agreement during that period, may submit the
  matter, for final settlement,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to an arbitrator selected by the employer and the
    union; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if they are unable to agree on the selection of an
    arbitrator and either of them makes a written request to the
    Minister to appoint an arbitrator, to an arbitrator appointed by the
    Minister after any inquiry that the Minister considers necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions applicable

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Sections 58 to 61 and 63 to 66 of the [Canada Labour
  Code](/eng/acts/L-2) apply, with any modifications that the
  circumstances require, in respect of an arbitrator to whom a matter is
  submitted under subsection (4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Strikes and lockouts prohibited

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Until a collective
agreement, as extended by subsection 6(1) or (2), expires, it is
prohibited

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the employer and for any of its officers and
  representatives to declare or cause a lockout against the union in
  respect of the bargaining unit to which the collective agreement
  applies;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the union and for any of its officers and
  representatives to declare or authorize a strike against the employer
  in respect of that bargaining unit; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for an employee who is a member of that bargaining
  unit to participate in a strike against the employer.

## [Mediator-Arbitrator]{.HTitleText1} {#h-395747 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lists of candidates

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  employer and the union may, within two days after the day on which
  this Act comes into force, each provide to the Minister a list of the
  names of up to three individuals that the employer or union, as the
  case may be, considers qualified to act as mediator-arbitrator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of mediator-arbitrator

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the two lists have only one name in common, the
  Minister must appoint that individual as the mediator-arbitrator, and
  if they have more than one name in common, he or she must appoint one
  of those individuals. However, if the Minister does not receive both
  lists within the period referred to in subsection (1), or if the lists
  that are received within that period have no names in common, he or
  she must appoint the mediator-arbitrator after seeking advice from the
  Chairperson of the Canada Industrial Relations Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Referral of matters in dispute

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must refer to
the mediator-arbitrator all matters relating to the amendment or
revision of each collective agreement that are, at the time of the
appointment, in dispute between the parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers and duties of mediator-arbitrator

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The mediator-arbitrator
has, with any modifications that the circumstances require, all the
powers and duties referred to in paragraphs 60(1)(a) and (a.2) to (a.4)
and sections 61 and 84 of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of mediator-arbitrator

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  90 days after the day on which he or she is appointed or any longer
  period that the Minister may allow, the mediator-arbitrator must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} endeavour to mediate all the matters that were
    referred to him or her relating to the amendment or revision of each
    collective agreement and to bring about an agreement between the
    parties to it on those matters;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if he or she is unable to bring about an agreement
    between the parties in respect of any such matter,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} hear the parties on the matter, arbitrate the
      matter and render a decision in respect of the matter, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} ask each of the parties to submit, within the
      time and in the manner that he or she may specify, that party's
      final offer in respect of the matter and, subject to subsection
      (7), select, in order to resolve the matter, either the final
      offer of the employer or the final offer of the union; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} report to the Minister on the resolution of each of
    the matters that were referred to the mediator-arbitrator and
    provide the parties with a copy of the report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Precision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the choice of acting in
  accordance with subparagraph (1)(b)(i) or (ii) is at the discretion of
  the mediator-arbitrator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Guiding principles

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In rendering a decision or selecting a final offer
  under paragraph (1)(b), the mediator-arbitrator is to be guided by the
  need

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to ensure that the health and safety of the
    employees is protected;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to ensure that the employees receive equal pay for
    work of equal value;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to ensure the fair treatment of temporary or
    part-time employees, and other employees in non-standard employment,
    as compared to full-time, permanent employees;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to ensure the financial sustainability of the
    employer;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} to create a culture of collaborative
    labour-management relations; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} to have the employer provide high-quality service
    at a reasonable price to Canadians.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mediation period

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the mediator-arbitrator has a
  period of not more than seven days after the day on which he or she is
  appointed --- that may be extended for an additional period of not
  more than seven days if the parties agree --- within which to
  endeavour to mediate the matters referred to in paragraph (1)(a) and
  to bring about an agreement between the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contractual language --- decision

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every decision of the mediator-arbitrator under
  subparagraph (1)(b)(i) must be worded in appropriate contractual
  language so as to allow its incorporation into a new collective
  agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contractual language --- final offer

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The final offer of the employer and of the union
  referred to in subparagraph (1)(b)(ii) must be submitted with proposed
  contractual language that can be incorporated into a new collective
  agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If no final offer submitted

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If either party fails to submit to the
  mediator-arbitrator a final offer when requested to do so under
  subparagraph (1)(b)(ii), the mediator-arbitrator must select the final
  offer submitted by the other party.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New collective agreements not precluded

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act
precludes the parties to a collective agreement from entering into a new
collective agreement at any time before the mediator-arbitrator reports
to the Minister under paragraph 11(1)(c) and, if they do so, the
mediator-arbitrator's duties under this Act respecting the collective
agreement cease as of the day on which the new collective agreement is
entered into.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** All costs incurred by Her
Majesty in right of Canada relating to the appointment of the
mediator-arbitrator and the performance of their duties under this Act
are debts due to Her Majesty in right of Canada and may be recovered as
such, in equal parts from the employer and the union, in any court of
competent jurisdiction.

## [New Collective Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-395787 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New collective agreement

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite anything in Part I of the
  [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2), but subject to subsection (2),
  beginning on the day after the day on which the mediator-arbitrator
  reports to the Minister under paragraph 11(1)(c) in respect of the
  matters that were referred to them in respect of the amendment or
  revision of a collective agreement (in this subsection referred to as
  the "former collective agreement"), a new collective agreement
  consisting of the following is effective and binding on the parties:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} every agreement entered into by the parties, before
    the appointment of the mediator-arbitrator, in relation to the
    amendment or revision of the former collective agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} every agreement entered into by the parties, after
    the appointment of the mediator-arbitrator, in relation to the
    matters that were referred to the mediator-arbitrator relating to
    the amendment or revision of the former collective agreement; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} every decision made by, and every final offer
    selected by, the mediator-arbitrator under paragraph 11(1)(b) in
    relation to the matters referred to in paragraph (b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Part I of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2)
  applies in respect of the new collective agreement as if it had been
  entered into under that Part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into effect of provisions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The new collective agreement may provide that any of
  its provisions are effective and binding as of a day that is before or
  after the day on which the new collective agreement becomes effective
  and binding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act is to be construed so as to limit
  or restrict the rights of the parties to the new collective agreement
  to amend any of its provisions and to give effect to the amendment.

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-395800 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Individuals

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual who contravenes any provision of this Act is guilty of an
  offence punishable on summary conviction and is liable, for each day
  or part of a day during which the offence continues, to a fine of not
  more than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$50,000, if the individual was acting in the
    capacity of an officer or representative of the employer or the
    union when the offence was committed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$1,000, in any other case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employer or union

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the employer or the union contravenes any
  provision of this Act, it is guilty of an offence punishable on
  summary conviction and is liable, for each day or part of a day during
  which the offence continues, to a fine of not more than \$100,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No imprisonment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 787(2) of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46), no term of imprisonment is to be imposed in
  default of payment of a fine that is imposed under subsection (1) or
  (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of fines

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a person is convicted of an offence under
  subsection (1) or (2) and the fine that is imposed is not paid when
  required, the prosecutor may, by filing the conviction, enter as a
  judgment the amount of the fine and costs, if any, in a superior court
  of the province in which the trial was held, and the judgment is
  enforceable against the person in the same manner as if it were a
  judgment rendered against the person in that court in civil
  proceedings.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-395812 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Noon of day after royal assent

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** This Act comes into force
at noon Eastern Standard Time on the day after the day on which it
receives royal assent.
