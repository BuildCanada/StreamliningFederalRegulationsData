## Canada--EFTA Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act {#canadaefta-free-trade-agreement-implementation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2009, c. 6
:::

Assented to 2009-04-29

An Act to implement the Free Trade Agreement between Canada and the
States of the European Free Trade Association (Iceland, Liechtenstein,
Norway, Switzerland), the Agreement on Agriculture between Canada and
the Republic of Iceland, the Agreement on Agriculture between Canada and
the Kingdom of Norway and the Agreement on Agriculture between Canada
and the Swiss Confederation
::::

::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-83035 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada--EFTA Free Trade Agreement Implementation
Act](/eng/acts/C-3.65).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-83038 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in this Act.

  [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Free Trade Agreement
      between Canada and the States of the European Free Trade
      Association (Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland), signed
      on January 26, 2008. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[bilateral agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[bilateral agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Agreement on Agriculture between Canada and
        the Republic of Iceland, signed on January 26, 2008;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Agreement on Agriculture between Canada and
        the Kingdom of Norway, signed on January 26, 2008; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Agreement on Agriculture between Canada and
        the Swiss Confederation, signed on January 26, 2008. ([accord
        bilatéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[EFTA state]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[EFTA state]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member state of the
      European Free Trade Association, namely:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Republic of Iceland;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Principality of Liechtenstein;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Kingdom of Norway; or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Swiss Confederation. ([État de
        l'AELÉ]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the whole or any portion
      of an Act of Parliament or a regulation, order or other instrument
      issued, made or established in the exercise of a power conferred
      by or under an Act of Parliament. ([texte législatif
      fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Joint Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Joint Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the committee
      established pursuant to Article 26 of the Agreement. ([comité
      mixte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister for
      International Trade. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Agreement and the bilateral agreements shall be
  published in the Canada Treaty Series.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation consistent with agreements

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, this
Act, any provision of an Act enacted or amended by Part 2 and any other
federal law that implements a provision of the Agreement or a bilateral
agreement or fulfils an obligation of the Government of Canada under the
Agreement or a bilateral agreement shall be interpreted in a manner
consistent with the Agreement or bilateral agreement, as the case may
be.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-83059 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to implement the Agreement and the bilateral agreements, the objectives
of which, as elaborated more specifically through their provisions, are
to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish a free trade area in accordance with the
  Agreement and the bilateral agreements;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} promote, through the expansion of reciprocal trade,
  the harmonious development of the economic relations between Canada
  and the EFTA states in order to foster in Canada and in the EFTA
  states the advancement of economic activity;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide fair conditions of competition affecting
  trade between Canada and the EFTA states;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} establish a framework for further co-operation
  between Canada and the EFTA states in the light of developments in
  international economic relations, in particular with the aim of
  liberalizing trade in services and increasing investment
  opportunities; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} contribute, by the removal of barriers to trade, to
  the harmonious development and expansion of world trade.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-83067 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-83070 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Causes of action under Part 1

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person has any cause of action and no proceedings of any kind shall be
  taken, without the consent of the Attorney General of Canada, to
  enforce or determine any right or obligation that is claimed or arises
  solely under or by virtue of Part 1 or an order made under Part 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Causes of action under agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person has any cause of action and no proceedings
  of any kind shall be taken, without the consent of the Attorney
  General of Canada, to enforce or determine any right or obligation
  that is claimed or arises solely under or by virtue of the Agreement
  or a bilateral agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of agreements to water

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act, the Agreement or the bilateral agreements applies
to natural surface or ground water in liquid, gaseous or solid state.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act, by specific mention or omission, shall be construed
to affect in any manner the right of Parliament to enact legislation to
implement any provision of the Agreement or a bilateral agreement or
fulfil any of the obligations of the Government of Canada under the
Agreement or a bilateral agreement.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Implementation of Agreements Generally]{.HTitleText1} {#h-83080 .Part}

### [Approval of Agreements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-83081 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements approved

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement and the
bilateral agreements are approved.

### [Administrative and Institutional Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-83084 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representative on Joint Committee

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is the
principal representative of Canada on the Joint Committee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of expenditures

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
shall pay its appropriate share of the aggregate of any expenditures
incurred by or on behalf of the Joint Committee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrative support

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall
designate an agency, division or branch of the Government of Canada to
facilitate the operation of, and provide administrative assistance to
arbitral tribunals established under, Chapter VIII of the Agreement.

### [Subcommittees and Working Groups]{.HTitleText2} {#h-83091 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointments to subcommittees and working
groups

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may appoint
any person to be a representative of Canada on the subcommittee referred
to in Article 9 of the Agreement or on any subcommittee or working group
established under Article 26 of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
shall pay the costs of or its appropriate share of the costs of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the remuneration and expenses payable to members of
  arbitral tribunals, subcommittees and working groups; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the general expenses incurred by arbitral tribunals,
  subcommittees and working groups.

### [Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-83098 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders re Article 31

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, for the purpose of suspending benefits or
  obligations in accordance with Article 31 of the Agreement, by order,
  do any one or more of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend rights or privileges granted by Canada to
    an EFTA state or to goods of an EFTA state under the Agreement, a
    bilateral agreement or any federal law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} modify or suspend the application of any federal
    law enacted by Part 2, with respect to an EFTA state or to goods of
    an EFTA state;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} extend the application of any federal law to an
    EFTA state or to goods of an EFTA state; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} take any other measure that the Governor in Council
    considers necessary for that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless revoked, an order made under subsection (1)
  has effect for the period specified in the order.
