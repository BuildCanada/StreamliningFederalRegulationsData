## First Nations Jurisdiction over Education in British Columbia Act {#first-nations-jurisdiction-over-education-in-british-columbia-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2006, c. 10
:::

Assented to 2006-12-12

An Act to provide for jurisdiction over education on First Nation lands
in British Columbia
::::

::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-226742 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [First Nations Jurisdiction over Education in British Columbia
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.75).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-226745 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[Authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the First Nations
      Education Authority established by section 11. ([Autorité
      scolaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[council of a participating First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[council of a participating First
      Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as the
      expression [[council of the band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([conseil de
      la première nation participante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[education]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[education]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means education programs and
      services of a nature generally provided to students from
      kindergarten to grade 12. ([éducation]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[First Nation land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[First Nation land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1)
      of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), that is situated in British
      Columbia and that is set apart for a participating First Nation,
      and includes [[First Nation land]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
      defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Framework Agreement on First
      Nation Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46). ([terres
      autochtones]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[First Nation law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[First Nation law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a law made under
      subsection 9(1). ([loi autochtone]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[individual agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[individual agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement
      entered into between Her Majesty in right of Canada and a
      participating First Nation with respect to jurisdiction over
      education by the participating First Nation on First Nation land,
      and includes any amendments to the agreement made pursuant to its
      provisions. ([accord spécifique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
      Crown-Indigenous Relations. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[participating First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[participating First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a band
      named in the schedule. ([première nation
      participante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Words and expressions in [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context otherwise requires, words and
  expressions used in this Act have the same meaning as in the [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict with agreement

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  event of a conflict between an individual agreement and any Act of
  Parliament, including this Act, the individual agreement prevails to
  the extent of the conflict.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict with Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of a conflict between this Act and any
  other Act of Parliament, this Act prevails to the extent of the
  conflict.

## [Purpose and Effect of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-226764 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act and
of orders of the Governor in Council made under this Act is to bring
into effect individual agreements entered into with participating First
Nations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bringing agreement into effect

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Once an
  individual agreement has been entered into, the Governor in Council
  may, by order, bring it into effect and add the name of the band that
  is party to it to the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Force of law of agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An individual agreement is given the force of law by
  an order made under subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order in council --- deletions from the
schedule

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, delete the name of a participating First Nation from the
schedule if the Minister is satisfied that the participating First
Nation has entered into a comprehensive self-government agreement, a
treaty or a land claims agreement, providing for jurisdiction over
education.

## [Effect of Individual Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-226774 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons and bodies subject to agreement

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Persons and bodies have the
powers, rights, privileges and benefits conferred on them by an
individual agreement and are subject to any obligations and liabilities
imposed on them by an individual agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement binding

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An individual agreement is
binding on and may be relied on by all persons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legislative powers

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  participating First Nation has, to the extent provided by an
  individual agreement, the power

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to enact laws respecting education on First Nation
    land; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to delegate to the Authority its power to make laws
    under paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transferability

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A participating First Nation shall provide, or make
  provision for, education so as to allow students to transfer without
  academic penalty to an equivalent level in another school within the
  school system of British Columbia.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scope of individual agreement

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act renders ineffective any provision, or any amendment
made to a provision, of an indi-vidual agreement for which specific
provision is not made in this Act.

## [First Nations Education Authority]{.HTitleText1} {#h-226788 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  is hereby established in British Columbia the First Nations Education
  Authority, to be managed by a board of directors that consists of a
  minimum of six directors, including a President and a Vice-President.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity, rights, powers and privileges

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Authority has the capacity, rights, powers and
  privileges of a natural person, including the capacity

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to enter into contracts;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to acquire, hold and dispose of property or an
    interest in property;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to raise, invest or borrow money; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to sue and be sued in its own name.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not agent of Her Majesty

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Authority is not an
agent of Her Majesty.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of directors

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  participating First Nation has the right to appoint two directors to
  the board of directors, at least one of whom shall be a member of the
  participating First Nation, for an initial term of two years.
  Directors may be reappointed, for a term fixed by the board, for
  second or subsequent terms.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal from office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A director holds office at the pleasure of the
  participating First Nation that appointed them, but they may be
  removed by the board of directors at any time for cause or for a
  ground set out in the board's rules.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of President and
Vice-President

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The board of directors
shall appoint a President and a Vice-President, from among the
directors, to hold office at the pleasure of the board for an initial
term not exceeding two years, and they may be reappointed for terms of
appointment to be established by the board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules of procedure

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The board of directors
may make rules that are consistent with this Act for the purpose of
carrying out the Authority's work,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} specifying the grounds for the removal of directors
  in addition to those generally recognized by law;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} conducting and managing its internal administrative
  affairs; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} specifying the duties of its officers, directors and
  employees and of persons retained by it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Authority's head
office shall be in British Columbia.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Authority may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} hire any persons that are necessary to conduct its
    work; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine the duties of those persons and the
    conditions of their employment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salaries and benefits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Persons hired under subsection (1) shall be paid the
  salary and benefits fixed by the Authority.

## [Purpose and Powers of the Authority]{.HTitleText1} {#h-226821 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandate

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of the
Authority is to assist participating First Nations in developing the
capacity to provide education on First Nation land and to provide for
any other matters related to education that may be agreed to by the
Authority and a participating First Nation in accordance with an
individual agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Education co-management agreements

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  furthering its purpose, the Authority shall, if requested by a
  participating First Nation and in accordance with an individual
  agreement, enter into a co-management agreement in respect of
  education with that participating First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Education standards

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Authority shall, as provided for by a
  co-management agreement,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish standards that are applicable to
    education provided by a participating First Nation on First Nation
    land for curriculum and examinations for courses necessary to meet
    graduation requirements;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide a teacher certification process for
    teachers providing educational instruction in schools operated by a
    participating First Nation on First Nation land, other than teachers
    who teach only the language and culture of the participating First
    Nation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide, upon request by a participating First
    Nation, a teacher certification process for teachers who teach only
    the language and culture of the participating First Nation in
    schools operated by the participating First Nation on First Nation
    land;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide a process for certifying schools that are
    operated by a participating First Nation on First Nation land; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} perform any other duties that are consistent with
    the individual agreement and this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation with province

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Authority shall consult with the competent
  authorities in British Columbia regarding standards established under
  paragraph (2)(a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The Authority may
exercise the jurisdiction over education that is delegated to it by a
participating First Nation in accordance with an individual agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other First Nations

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  board of directors may authorize the Authority to enter into
  agreements in respect of education with any First Nation in British
  Columbia other than a participating First Nation if that First Nation
  has entered into a comprehensive self-government agreement, a treaty
  or a land claims agreement, providing for jurisdiction over education.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements with other First Nations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Authority has been authorized by the board of
  directors to enter into agreements under subsection (1), the Authority
  may, if requested by a First Nation described in subsection (1) and in
  accordance with a comprehensive self-government agreement, a treaty or
  a land claims agreement, providing for jurisdiction over education,
  enter into an agreement in respect of education with that First
  Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegated jurisdiction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Authority has entered into an agreement with a
  First Nation under subsection (2), the Authority shall exercise the
  jurisdiction over education that is delegated to it by that First
  Nation in British Columbia in a manner consistent with subsection
  19(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional directors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Authority has entered into an agreement with a
  First Nation under subsection (2), that First Nation has the right to
  appoint two directors to the board of directors, at least one of whom
  shall be a member of that First Nation. A director holds office at the
  pleasure of the First Nation that appointed them, but may be removed
  by the board for cause or for a ground set out in the board's rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms of appointment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A director appointed under subsection (4) holds
  office for an initial term of two years and may be reappointed for a
  second or subsequent term, for a term of appointment fixed by the
  board of directors.

## [Community Education Authority]{.HTitleText1} {#h-226849 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  participating First Nation may, on its own or jointly with other
  participating First Nations, establish a Community Education Authority
  to operate, administer and manage the education system of the
  participating First Nation on First Nation land in accordance with an
  individual agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, rights and duties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Community Education Authority has the powers,
  rights, privileges and benefits conferred on it by a participating
  First Nation and shall perform the duties --- and be subject to the
  liabilities --- imposed on it by a First Nation law in accordance with
  an individual agreement.

## [Other Acts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-226855 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** On the coming into force
of a First Nation law, sections 114 to 122 of the [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5) cease to apply to the participating First Nation and
its members.

## [Liability]{.HTitleText1} {#h-226858 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participating First Nation not liable

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  participating First Nation is not liable in respect of anything done
  or omitted to be done by Her Majesty, or any person or body authorized
  by Her Majesty to act, in the exercise of their powers, duties and
  functions in relation to education.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnification of participating First
  Nation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty shall indemnify a participating First
  Nation for any loss suffered by the participating First Nation as a
  result of an act or omission described in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Her Majesty not liable

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty is not liable in respect of anything done
  or omitted to be done by a participating First Nation, or any person
  or body authorized by the participating First Nation to act, in the
  exercise of their powers, duties and functions in relation to
  education.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnification of Her Majesty

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The participating First Nation shall indemnify Her
  Majesty for any loss suffered by Her Majesty as a result of an act or
  omission described in subsection (3).

## [Minister's Duties and Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-226868 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Schools

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, in accordance with this Act, establish and maintain
  schools on First Nation land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deposit of copies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause a copy of each individual
  agreement and of any amendment made to the individual agreement,
  certified by the Minister to be a true copy, to be deposited in the
  library of the Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development
  situated in the National Capital Region, as described in the schedule
  to the [National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4), and in any regional
  offices of that Department and other places that the Minister
  considers advisable.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-226874 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice of individual agreements

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Judicial notice shall be
taken of each individual agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice of First Nation laws

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice shall be taken of
  First Nation laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence of First Nation laws

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of a First Nation law purporting to be
  deposited in the public registry of First Nation laws referred to in
  an individual agreement is evidence of that law and of its contents,
  unless the contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
First Nation laws are not statutory instruments within the meaning of
the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7)

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, no
body established by this Act or by a First Nation law is a federal
board, commission or other tribunal as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
[Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of court or tribunal proceedings

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party in any proceeding before a court or tribunal shall serve notice
  in writing on the Attorney General of Canada and the participating
  First Nation of any issue raised by that party in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the interpretation or validity of an individual
    agreement; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the validity or applicability of this Act or of a
    First Nation law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice shall identify the proceeding in which the
  issue arises, state whether it arises in relation to paragraph (1)(a)
  or (b), give particulars of the point to be argued and, if a date has
  been fixed for argument, give the date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attachments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice shall be accompanied by copies of all
  pleadings and other documents pertaining to the issue that have been
  filed with the court or tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of service

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A notice shall be served within seven days after the
  issue is first raised by a party to the proceeding, whether in the
  initial pleadings or otherwise, and the issue may not be argued sooner
  than 14 days after service unless the court or tribunal allows a
  shorter period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation in proceedings

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In any proceeding to which subsection (1) applies,
  the Attorney General of Canada and the participating First Nation may
  appear and participate with the same rights as any other party.

## [Orders and Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-226899 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders in council

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make orders and regulations that are necessary
  to implement individual agreements entered into after the day on which
  this subsection comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulation- making authority

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make regulations to
  establish standards for buildings and equipment, and the inspection of
  buildings and equipment, used by schools administered under this Act.

      otherwise, every reference to the "Department of Indian Affairs
      and Northern Development" is, with any grammatical adaptations, to

      Relations and Northern Affairs" in the following provisions:
