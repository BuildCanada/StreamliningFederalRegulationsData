## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-331721 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a body of Indians

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for whose use and benefit in common, lands, the
        legal title to which is vested in Her Majesty, have been set
        apart before, on or after September 4, 1951,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for whose use and benefit in common, moneys are
        held by Her Majesty, or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} declared by the Governor in Council to be a
        band for the purposes of this Act; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[Band List]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Band List]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a list of persons that is
      maintained under section 8 by a band or in the Department; ([liste
      de bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a legally adopted child and
      a child adopted in accordance with Indian
      custom; ([enfant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an
      individual, means a person who is cohabiting with the individual
      in a conjugal relationship, having so cohabited for a period of at
      least one year; ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[council of the band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[council of the band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a band to which section 74
        applies, the council established pursuant to that section,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a band that is named in the
        schedule to the [First Nations Elections
        Act](/eng/acts/F-11.65), the council elected or in office in
        accordance with that Act,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a band whose name has been
        removed from the schedule to the [First Nations Elections
        Act](/eng/acts/F-11.65) in accordance with section 42 of that
        Act, the council elected or in office in accordance with the
        community election code referred to in that section, or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other band, the council
        chosen according to the custom of the band, or, if there is no
        council, the chief of the band chosen according to the custom of
        the band; ([conseil de la bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of
      Indigenous Services; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a tract of land or
      any interest therein the legal title to which remains vested in
      Her Majesty and in which the band for whose use and benefit it was
      set apart as a reserve has, otherwise than absolutely, released or
      surrendered its rights or interests, whether before or after the
      coming into force of this definition; ([terres
      désignées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[elector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[elector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is registered on a Band List,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is of the full age of eighteen years, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is not disqualified from voting at band
        elections; ([électeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[estate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[estate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes real and personal property
      and any interest in land; ([biens]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who pursuant to this
      Act is registered as an Indian or is entitled to be registered as
      an Indian; ([Indien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Indian moneys]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Indian moneys]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all moneys collected,
      received or held by Her Majesty for the use and benefit of Indians
      or bands; ([argent des Indiens]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Indian Register]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Indian Register]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the register of
      persons that is maintained under section 5; ([registre des
      Indiens]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[intoxicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[intoxicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes alcohol, alcoholic,
      spirituous, vinous, fermented malt or other intoxicating liquor or
      combination of liquors and mixed liquor a part of which is
      spirituous, vinous, fermented or otherwise intoxicating and all
      drinks, drinkable liquids, preparations or mixtures capable of
      human consumption that are intoxicating; ([boisson
      alcoolisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[member of a band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[member of a band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person whose name
      appears on a Band List or who is entitled to have his name appear
      on a Band List; ([membre d'une bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[mentally incompetent Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mentally incompetent Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      Indian who, pursuant to the laws of the province in which he
      resides, has been found to be mentally defective or incompetent
      for the purposes of any laws of that province providing for the
      administration of estates of mentally defective or incompetent
      persons; ([Indien mentalement incapable]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Indigenous
      Services; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[registered]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[registered]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means registered as an Indian
      in the Indian Register; ([inscrit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the officer in the
      Department who is in charge of the Indian Register and the Band
      Lists maintained in the
      Department; ([registraire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} means a tract of land, the legal title to which
        is vested in Her Majesty, that has been set apart by Her Majesty
        for the use and benefit of a band, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} except in subsection 18(2), sections 20 to 25,
        28, 37, 38, 42, 44, 46, 48 to 51 and 58 to 60 and the
        regulations made under any of those provisions, includes
        designated lands; ([réserve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a commissioner,
      regional supervisor, Indian superintendent, assistant Indian
      superintendent and any other person declared by the Minister to be
      a superintendent for the purposes of this Act, and with reference
      to a band or a reserve, means the superintendent for that band or
      reserve; ([surintendant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[surrendered lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[surrendered lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a reserve or part
      of a reserve or any interest therein, the legal title to which
      remains vested in Her Majesty, that has been released or
      surrendered by the band for whose use and benefit it was set
      apart; ([terres cédées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a deceased
      individual, means their surviving spouse or common-law
      partner. ([survivant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The expression [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with
  reference to a reserve or surrendered lands, means the band for whose
  use and benefit the reserve or the surrendered lands were set apart.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exercise of powers conferred on band or
  council

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context otherwise requires or this Act
  otherwise provides,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a power conferred on a band shall be deemed not to
    be exercised unless it is exercised pursuant to the consent of a
    majority of the electors of the band; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a power conferred on the council of a band shall be
    deemed not to be exercised unless it is exercised pursuant to the
    consent of a majority of the councillors of the band present at a
    meeting of the council duly convened.

## [Application of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-331775 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Act

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  reference in this Act to an Indian does not include any person of the
  race of aborigines commonly referred to as Inuit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act may be declared inapplicable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may by proclamation declare
  that this Act or any portion thereof, except sections 5 to 14.3 or
  sections 37 to 41, shall not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any Indians or any group or band of Indians, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any reserve or any surrendered lands or any part
    thereof,

  and may by proclamation revoke any such declaration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority confirmed for certain cases

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, and without restricting the
  generality of subsection (2), the Governor in Council shall be deemed
  to have had the authority to make any declaration under subsection (2)
  that the Governor in Council has made in respect of section 11, 12 or
  14, or any provision thereof, as each section or provision read
  immediately prior to April 17, 1985.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain sections inapplicable to
  Indians living off reserves

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 114 to 117 and, unless the Minister
  otherwise orders, sections 42 to 52 do not apply to or in respect of
  any Indian who does not ordinarily reside on a reserve or on lands
  belonging to Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-5, s. 4

- 2014, c. 38, s. 4

## [Definition and Registration of Indians]{.HTitleText1} {#h-331794 .Part}

### [Indian Register]{.HTitleText2} {#h-331795 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indian Register

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be maintained in the Department an Indian Register in which
  shall be recorded the name of every person who is entitled to be
  registered as an Indian under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing Indian Register

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The names in the Indian Register immediately prior to
  April 17, 1985 shall constitute the Indian Register on April 17, 1985.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deletions and additions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar may at any time add to or delete from
  the Indian Register the name of any person who, in accordance with
  this Act, is entitled or not entitled, as the case may be, to have his
  name included in the Indian Register.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of change

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Indian Register shall indicate the date on which
  each name was added thereto or deleted therefrom.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for registration

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The name of a person who is entitled to be registered
  is not required to be recorded in the Indian Register unless an
  application for registration is made to the Registrar.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unknown or unstated parentage

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a parent, grandparent or other ancestor of a
  person in respect of whom an application is made is unknown --- or is
  unstated on a birth certificate that, if the parent, grandparent or
  other ancestor were named on it, would help to establish the person's
  entitlement to be registered --- the Registrar shall, without being
  required to establish the identity of that parent, grandparent or
  other ancestor, determine, after considering all of the relevant
  evidence, whether that parent, grandparent or other ancestor is, was
  or would have been entitled to be registered. In making the
  determination, the Registrar shall rely on any credible evidence that
  is presented by the applicant in support of the application or that
  the Registrar otherwise has knowledge of and shall draw from it every
  reasonable inference in favour of the person in respect of whom the
  application is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No presumption

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, if the identity of a parent,
  grandparent or other ancestor of an applicant is unknown or unstated
  on a birth certificate, there is no presumption that this parent,
  grandparent or other ancestor is not, was not or would not have been
  entitled to be registered.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-5, s. 5
- R.S., 1985, c. 32 (1st Supp.), s. 4
- 2017, c. 25, s. 1

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} the name of that person was omitted or deleted

    1951, under subparagraph 12(1)(a)(iv), paragraph 12(1)(b) or
    subsection 12(2) or under subparagraph 12(1)(a)(iii) pursuant to an
    order made under subsection 109(2), as each provision read
    immediately before April 17, 1985, or under any former provision of
    this Act relating to the same subject matter as any of those
    provisions;

  - [(a.2)]{.lawlabel} that person meets the following conditions:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} they were born female during the period beginning
      on September 4, 1951 and ending on April 16, 1985 and their
      parents were not married to each other at the time of the birth,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} their father was at the time of that person's
      birth entitled to be registered or, if he was no longer living at
      that time, was at the time of death entitled to be registered, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} their mother was not at the time of that
      person's birth entitled to be registered;

  - [(a.3)]{.lawlabel} that person is a direct descendant of a person
    who is, was or would have been entitled to be registered under
    paragraph (a.1) or (a.2) and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} they were born before April 17, 1985, whether or
      not their parents were married to each other at the time of the
      birth, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} they were born after April 16, 1985 and their
      parents were married to each other at any time before April 17,
      1985;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that person is a member of a body of persons that
    has been declared by the Governor in Council on or after April 17,
    1985 to be a band for the purposes of this Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2017, c. 25, s. 2.1\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(c.01)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2017, c. 25, s. 2.1\]]{.Repealed}

    (a.3) and who was no longer living on the day on which that

    under that paragraph.

    deleted from the Indian Register under this Act; or

    it read immediately prior to April 17, 1985, or under any former

    be registered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(b) does not apply in respect of the

### [Band Lists]{.HTitleText2} {#h-331879 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Band Lists

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** There shall be maintained
in accordance with this Act for each band a Band List in which shall be
entered the name of every person who is a member of that band.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-5, s. 8
- R.S., 1985, c. 32 (1st Supp.), s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Band Lists maintained in Department

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Until
  such time as a band assumes control of its Band List, the Band List of
  that band shall be maintained in the Department by the Registrar.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing Band Lists

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The names in a Band List of a band immediately prior

  may direct.

## [Possession of Lands in Reserves]{.HTitleText1} {#h-332093 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Possession of lands in a reserve

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  Indian is lawfully in possession of land in a reserve unless, with the
  approval of the Minister, possession of the land has been allotted to
  him by the council of the band.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of Possession

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may issue to an Indian who is lawfully
  in possession of land in a reserve a certificate, to be called a
  Certificate of Possession, as evidence of his right to possession of
  the land described therein.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Location tickets issued under previous
  legislation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, any person who, on
  September 4, 1951, held a valid and subsisting Location Ticket issued
  under The Indian Act, 1880, or any statute relating to the same
  subject-matter, shall be deemed to be lawfully in possession of the
  land to which the location ticket relates and to hold a Certificate of
  Possession with respect thereto.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary possession

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where possession of land in a reserve has been
  allotted to an Indian by the council of the band, the Minister may, in
  his discretion, withhold his approval and may authorize the Indian to
  occupy the land temporarily and may prescribe the conditions as to use
  and settlement that are to be fulfilled by the Indian before the
  Minister approves of the allotment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of Occupation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister withholds approval pursuant to
  subsection (4), he shall issue a Certificate of Occupation to the
  Indian, and the Certificate entitles the Indian, or those claiming
  possession by devise or descent, to occupy the land in respect of
  which it is issued for a period of two years from the date thereof.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension and approval

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may extend the term of a Certificate of
  Occupation for a further period not exceeding two years, and may, at
  the expiration of any period during which a Certificate of Occupation
  is in force

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} approve the allotment by the council of the band
    and issue a Certificate of Possession if in his opinion the
    conditions as to use and settlement have been fulfilled; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} refuse approval of the allotment by the council of
    the band and declare the land in respect of which the Certificate of
    Occupation was issued to be available for re-allotment by the
    council of the band.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-6, s. 20
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Register

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** There shall be kept in
the Department a register, to be known as the Reserve Land Register, in
which shall be entered particulars relating to Certificates of

respecting lands in a reserve.

permanent improvements thereon before that time, he shall be deemed to
be in lawful possession of those lands at the time they are included.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-6, s. 22

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation for improvements

improvements may, if the Minister so directs, be paid compensation in
respect thereof in an amount to be determined by the Minister, either
from the person who goes into possession or from the funds of the band,
at the discretion of the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-6, s. 23
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of possession

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** An Indian who is lawfully
in possession of lands in a reserve may transfer to the band or another
member of the band the right to possession of the land, but no transfer
or agreement for the transfer of the right to possession of lands in a
reserve is effective until it is approved by the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-6, s. 24

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indian ceasing to reside on reserve

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  Indian who ceases to be entitled to reside on a reserve may, within
  six months or such further period as the Minister may direct, transfer
  to the band or another member of the band the right to possession of
  any lands in the reserve of which he was lawfully in possession.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When right of possession reverts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an Indian does not dispose of his right of
  possession in accordance with subsection (1), the right to possession
  of the land reverts to the band, subject to the payment to the Indian
  who was lawfully in possession of the land, from the funds of the
  band, of such compensation for permanent improvements as the Minister
  may determine.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-6, s. 25
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Correction of Certificate or Location

## [Wills]{.HTitleText1} {#h-332280 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indians may make wills

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act shall be
  construed to prevent or prohibit an Indian from devising or
  bequeathing his property by will.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of will

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may accept as a will any written
  instrument signed by an Indian in which he indicates his wishes or
  intention with respect to the disposition of his property on his
  death.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Probate

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No will executed by an Indian is of any legal force
  or effect as a disposition of property until the Minister has approved
  the will or a court has granted probate thereof pursuant to this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-6, s. 45
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may declare will void

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may declare the will of an Indian to be void in whole or in
  part if he is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the will was executed under duress or undue
    influence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the testator at the time of execution of the will
    lacked testamentary capacity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the terms of the will would impose hardship on
    persons for whom the testator had a responsibility to provide;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the will purports to dispose of land in a reserve
    in a manner contrary to the interest of the band or contrary to this
    Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the terms of the will are so vague, uncertain or
    capricious that proper administration and equitable distribution of
    the estate of the deceased would be difficult or impossible to carry
    out in accordance with this Act; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the terms of the will are against the public
    interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where will declared void

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a will of an Indian is declared by the Minister
  or by a court to be wholly void, the person executing the will shall
  be deemed to have died intestate, and where the will is so declared to
  be void in part only, any bequest or devise affected thereby, unless a
  contrary intention appears in the will, shall be deemed to have
  lapsed.

## [Distribution of Property on Intestacy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-332308 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surviving spouse's share

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the net value of the estate of an intestate does not, in the opinion
  of the Minister, exceed seventy-five thousand dollars or such other
  amount as may be fixed by order of the Governor in Council, the estate
  shall go to the survivor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the net value of the estate of an intestate, in
  the opinion of the Minister, exceeds seventy-five thousand dollars, or
  such other amount as may be fixed by order of the Governor in Council,
  seventy-five thousand dollars, or such other amount as may be fixed by
  order of the Governor in Council, shall go to the survivor, and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the intestate left no issue, the remainder shall
    go to the survivor,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the intestate left one child, one-half of the
    remainder shall go to the survivor, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the intestate left more than one child,
    one-third of the remainder shall go to the survivor,

  and where a child has died leaving issue and that issue is alive at
  the date of the intestate's death, the survivor shall take the same
  share of the estate as if the child had been living at that date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where children not provided for

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsections (1) and (2),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where in any particular case the Minister is
    satisfied that any children of the deceased will not be adequately
    provided for, he may direct that all or any part of the estate that
    would otherwise go to the survivor shall go to the children; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister may direct that the survivor shall
    have the right to occupy any lands in a reserve that were occupied
    by the deceased at the time of death.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution to issue

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where an intestate dies leaving issue, his estate
  shall be distributed, subject to the rights of the survivor, if any,
  [per stirpes]{.otherLang lang="la"} among such issue.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution to parents

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where an intestate dies leaving no survivor or issue,
  the estate shall go to the parents of the deceased in equal shares if
  both are living, but if either of them is dead the estate shall go to
  the surviving parent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution to brothers, sisters and
  their issue

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where an intestate dies leaving no survivor or issue
  or father or mother, his estate shall be distributed among his
  brothers and sisters in equal shares, and where any brother or sister
  is dead the children of the deceased brother or sister shall take the
  share their parent would have taken if living, but where the only
  persons entitled are children of deceased brothers and sisters, they
  shall take per capita.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Next-of-kin

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Where an intestate dies leaving no survivor, issue,
  father, mother, brother or sister, and no children of any deceased
  brother or sister, his estate shall go to his next-of-kin.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution among next-of-kin

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Where an estate goes to the next-of-kin, it shall be
  distributed equally among the next-of-kin of equal degree of
  consanguinity to the intestate and those who legally represent them,
  but in no case shall representation be admitted after brothers' and
  sisters' children, and any interest in land in a reserve shall vest in
  Her Majesty for the benefit of the band if the nearest of kin of the
  intestate is more remote than a brother or sister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Degrees of kindred

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, degrees of kindred
  shall be computed by counting upward from the intestate to the nearest
  common ancestor and then downward to the relative, and the kindred of
  the half-blood shall inherit equally with those of the whole-blood in
  the same degree.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Descendants and relatives born after
  intestate's death

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} Descendants and relatives of an intestate begotten
  before his death but born thereafter shall inherit as if they had been
  born in the lifetime of the intestate and had survived him.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Estate not disposed of by will

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} All such estate as is not disposed of by will shall
  be distributed as if the testator had died intestate and had left no
  other estate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No community of property

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} There is no community of real or personal property
  situated in a reserve.

- [(13) and (14)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, R.S., 1985, c. 32 (1st
  Supp.), s. 9\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equal application to men and women

  [(15)]{.lawlabel} This section applies in respect of an intestate
  woman as it applies in respect of an intestate man.

- [(16)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, R.S., 1985, c. 32 (1st Supp.), s.
  9\]]{.Repealed}

- 2000, c. 12, ss. 149, 151

descent shall be deemed not to be in lawful possession or occupation of

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-resident of reserve

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is not entitled to reside on a reserve does not by devise
  or descent acquire a right to possession or occupation of land in that
  reserve.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale by superintendent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a right to possession or occupation of land in
  a reserve passes by devise or descent to a person who is not entitled
  to reside on a reserve, that right shall be offered for sale by the
  superintendent to the highest bidder among persons who are entitled to
  reside on the reserve and the proceeds of the sale shall be paid to
  the devisee or descendant, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unsold lands revert to band

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where no tender is received within six months or such
  further period as the Minister may direct after the date when the
  right to possession or occupation of land is offered for sale under
  subsection (2), the right shall revert to the band free from any claim
  on the part of the devisee or descendant, subject to the payment, at
  the discretion of the Minister, to the devisee or descendant, from the
  funds of the band, of such compensation for permanent improvements as
  the Minister may determine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval required

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The purchaser of a right to possession or occupation
  of land under subsection (2) shall be deemed not to be in lawful
  possession or occupation of the land until the possession is approved
  by the Minister.

## [Money of Infant Children]{.HTitleText1} {#h-332387 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distributions of capital

- **[[52.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The council of a band may determine
  that the payment of not more than three thousand dollars, or such
  other amount as may be fixed by order of the Governor in Council, in a
  year of the share of a distribution under paragraph 64(1)(a) that
  belongs to an infant child who is a member of the band is necessary or
  proper for the maintenance, advancement or other benefit of the child.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before making a determination under subsection (1),
  the council of the band must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} post in a conspicuous place on the reserve fourteen
    days before the determination is made a notice that it proposes to
    make such a determination; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give the members of the band a reasonable
    opportunity to be heard at a general meeting of the band held before
    the determination is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's duty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the council of the band makes a determination
  under subsection (1) and notifies the Minister, at the time it gives
  its consent to the distribution pursuant to paragraph 64(1)(a), that
  it has made that determination and that, before making it, it complied
  with subsection (2), the Minister shall make a payment described in
  subsection (1) for the maintenance, advancement or other benefit of
  the child to a parent or person who is responsible for the care and
  custody of the child or, if so requested by the council on giving its
  consent to that distribution, to the council.

  the Minister under section 52 to which the child is entitled to that

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Uncultivated or unused lands

- **[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  land in a reserve is uncultivated or unused, the Minister may, with
  the consent of the council of the band,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} improve or cultivate that land and employ persons
    therefor, and authorize and direct the expenditure of such amount of
    the capital funds of the band as he considers necessary for that
    improvement or cultivation including the purchase of such stock,
    machinery or material or for the employment of such labour as the
    Minister considers necessary;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the land is in the lawful possession of any
    individual, grant a lease of that land for agricultural or grazing
    purposes or for any purpose that is for the benefit of the person in
    possession of the land; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the land is not in the lawful possession of
    any individual, grant for the benefit of the band a lease of that
    land for agricultural or grazing purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution of proceeds

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Out of the proceeds derived from the improvement or
  cultivation of lands pursuant to paragraph (1)(b), a reasonable rent
  shall be paid to the individual in lawful possession of the lands or
  any part thereof and the remainder of the proceeds shall be placed to
  the credit of the band, but if improvements are made on the lands
  occupied by an individual, the Minister may deduct the value of the
  improvements from the rent payable to the individual under this
  subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lease at request of occupant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may lease for the benefit of any Indian,
  on application of that Indian for that purpose, the land of which the
  Indian is lawfully in possession without the land being designated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition of grass, timber,
  non-metallic substances, etc.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything in this Act, the Minister

    or under lands in a reserve, or, where that consent cannot be
    obtained without undue difficulty or delay, may issue temporary
    permits for the taking of sand, gravel, clay and other non-metallic
    substances on or under lands in a reserve, renewable only with the
    consent of the council of the band.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceeds

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The proceeds of the transactions referred to in
  subsection (4) shall be credited to band funds or shall be divided
  between the band and the individual Indians in lawful possession of

## [Management of Indian Moneys]{.HTitleText1} {#h-332498 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indian moneys to be held for use and
benefit

- **[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Indian
  moneys shall be expended only for the benefit of the Indians or bands
  for whose use and benefit in common the moneys are received or held,
  and subject to this Act and to the terms of any treaty or surrender,
  the Governor in Council may determine whether any purpose for which
  Indian moneys are used or are to be used is for the use and benefit of
  the band.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Interest on Indian moneys held in the Consolidated
  Revenue Fund shall be allowed at a rate to be fixed from time to time
  by the Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-6, s. 61
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capital and revenue

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** All Indian moneys derived
from the sale of surrendered lands or the sale of capital assets of a
band shall be deemed to be capital moneys of the band and all Indian
moneys other than capital moneys shall be deemed to be revenue moneys of
the band.

- R.S., c. I-6, s. 62

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments to Indians

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), where moneys to which an
Indian is entitled are paid to a superintendent under any lease or
agreement made under this Act, the superintendent may pay the moneys to
the Indian.

- R.S., c. I-6, s. 63

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenditure of capital moneys with
consent

- **[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} With
  the consent of the council of a band, the Minister may authorize and
  direct the expenditure of capital moneys of the band

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to distribute per capita to the members of the band
    an amount not exceeding fifty per cent of the capital moneys of the
    band derived from the sale of surrendered lands;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to construct and maintain roads, bridges, ditches
    and watercourses on reserves or on surrendered lands;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to construct and maintain outer boundary fences on
    reserves;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to purchase land for use by the band as a reserve
    or as an addition to a reserve;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} to purchase for the band the interest of a member
    of the band in lands on a reserve;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} to purchase livestock and farm implements, farm
    equipment or machinery for the band;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} to construct and maintain on or in connection with
    a reserve such permanent improvements or works as in the opinion of
    the Minister will be of permanent value to the band or will
    constitute a capital investment;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} to make to members of the band, for the purpose of
    promoting the welfare of the band, loans not exceeding one-half of
    the total value of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the chattels owned by the borrower, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the land with respect to which he holds or is
      eligible to receive a Certificate of Possession,

    and may charge interest and take security therefor;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to meet expenses necessarily incidental to the
    management of lands on a reserve, surrendered lands and any band
    property;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} to construct houses for members of the band, to
    make loans to members of the band for building purposes with or
    without security and to provide for the guarantee of loans made to
    members of the band for building purposes; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} for any other purpose that in the opinion of the

## [Elections of Chiefs and Band Councils]{.HTitleText1} {#h-332641 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elected councils

- **[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Whenever he deems it advisable for
  the good government of a band, the Minister may declare by order that
  after a day to be named therein the council of the band, consisting of
  a chief and councillors, shall be selected by elections to be held in
  accordance with this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition of council

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise ordered by the Minister, the council
  of a band in respect of which an order has been made under
  subsection (1) shall consist of one chief, and one councillor for
  every one hundred members of the band, but the number of councillors
  shall not be less than two nor more than twelve and no band shall have
  more than one chief.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, for the purposes of
  giving effect to subsection (1), make orders or regulations to provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the chief of a band shall be elected by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a majority of the votes of the electors of the
      band, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a majority of the votes of the elected
      councillors of the band from among themselves,

    but the chief so elected shall remain a councillor; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the councillors of a band shall be elected by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a majority of the votes of the electors of the
      band, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a majority of the votes of the electors of the
      band in the electoral section in which the candidate resides and
      that he proposes to represent on the council of the band.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electoral sections

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A reserve shall for voting purposes consist of one
  electoral section, except that where the majority of the electors of a
  band who were present and voted at a referendum or a special meeting
  held and called for the purpose in accordance with the regulations
  have decided that the reserve should for voting purposes be divided
  into electoral sections and the Minister so recommends, the Governor
  in Council may make orders or regulations to provide for the division
  of the reserve for voting purposes into not more than six electoral
  sections containing as nearly as may be an equal number of Indians
  eligible to vote and to provide for the manner in which electoral
  sections so established are to be distinguished or identified.

- R.S., c. I-6, s. 74

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person who holds that office

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} the removal and punishment of persons trespassing

  - [(p.1)]{.lawlabel} the residence of band members and other persons

  - [(p.2)]{.lawlabel} to provide for the rights of spouses or

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} with respect to any matter arising out of or

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} the imposition on summary conviction of a fine not
