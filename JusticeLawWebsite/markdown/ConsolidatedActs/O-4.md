## Official Residences Act {#official-residences-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. O-4
:::

An Act to provide for the operation and maintenance of official
residences
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-384747 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Official Residences Act](/eng/acts/O-4).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-20, s. 1
- R.S., c. 20(2nd Supp.), s. 2
:::

## [Properties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-384752 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prime Minister's residence

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding anything in
the [Parliament of Canada Act](/eng/acts/P-1), the lands described in
Schedule I and the buildings thereon shall be maintained as a residence
for the Prime Minister of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-20, s. 2
- R.S., c. 20(2nd Supp.), s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Leader of the Opposition's residence

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding anything in
the [Parliament of Canada Act](/eng/acts/P-1), the lands described in
Schedule II and the buildings thereon shall be maintained as a residence
for the person holding the recognized position of Leader of the
Opposition in the House of Commons, in this Act called "the Leader of
the Opposition".

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-20, s. 2
- R.S., c. 20(2nd Supp.), s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Speaker of the House of Commons residence

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding anything in
the [Parliament of Canada Act](/eng/acts/P-1), the lands described in
Schedule III and the buildings thereon shall be maintained as a
residence for the Speaker of the House of Commons.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-20, s. 2
- R.S., c. 20(2nd Supp.), s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Leader of the Opposition's summer
residence

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, by order, allocate any lands in the National
  Capital Region, as described in the schedule to the [National Capital
  Act](/eng/acts/N-4), and the buildings thereon as a summer residence
  for the Leader of the Opposition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An order made by the Governor in Council under
  subsection (1) shall be laid before Parliament not later than the
  fifteenth sitting day of Parliament after it is made.

- Definition of [[sitting day of Parliament]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), [[sitting day of
  Parliament]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day on which either House of
  Parliament sits.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 75, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Furnishings, maintenance, etc.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Public
Works and Government Services shall furnish, maintain, heat and keep in
repair the buildings on the lands described in the schedules or
allocated pursuant to section 5, and the National Capital Commission
shall maintain and, from time to time as required, improve those lands.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. O-4, s. 6
- 1999, c. 31, s. 172
:::

## [Staff]{.HTitleText1} {#h-384778 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff of Prime Minister's residence

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may appoint a steward or housekeeper and such
  other employees as the Governor in Council deems necessary for the
  management of the Prime Minister's residence, and may fix their rate
  of remuneration and conditions of employment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Casual employees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The steward or housekeeper may, from time to time,
  with the approval of the Prime Minister, engage casual employees to
  assist the regular staff appointed under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chauffeur

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Prime Minister's chauffeur may be provided with
  lodging without charge.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-20, s. 4
- R.S., c. 20(2nd Supp.), s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff of Leader of the Opposition's
residence

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Leader of the
Opposition may appoint a steward or housekeeper and not more than three
other employees to manage the residence of the Leader of the Opposition.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-20, s. 5
- R.S., c. 20(2nd Supp.), s. 6
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 75, s. 3
:::

## [Moneys Authorized and Appropriated]{.HTitleText1} {#h-384792 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized benefits received as expense
allowance

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, it
is hereby declared that any benefit authorized by this Act and received
by the Prime Minister, the Leader of the Opposition or the Speaker of
the House of Commons is received as a living expense allowance expressly
fixed in this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 20(2nd Supp.), s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Uses of moneys appropriated

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Moneys appropriated by
Parliament for the operation of the Prime Minister's residence may be
used for the payment of the remuneration of persons employed under the
authority of section 7, for the purchase of food and other supplies
required for the serving of food, for cleaning, laundering, and the
ordinary maintenance of a residence, and for defraying other costs of
official hospitality provided by the Prime Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-20, s. 6
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 75, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other uses of moneys appropriated

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Moneys appropriated by
Parliament for the operation of the House of Commons may be used for the
payment of the remuneration of persons employed under the authority of
section 8 and for defraying other expenses related to their employment.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-20, s. 6
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 75, s. 4
:::

All and Singular that certain parcel or tract of land and premises
situate, lying and being in the City of Ottawa, in the County of
Carleton, in the Province of Ontario, being composed of part of lot 3 in
the Ottawa and Rideau Junction Gore in the Township of Gloucester
described as follows:

COMMENCING at a point on the westerly limit of Ottawa Street (now Sussex
Street) distant one hundred and seventy three feet, eight inches
(173′8″) more or less southerly from the intersection of the westerly
limit of Ottawa Street aforesaid with the westerly prolongation of the
northerly limit of MacKay Street in the Village of New Edinburgh, the
said point being the south-east angle of that part of the said lot
lastly described in Instrument registered in the Registry Office for the

whole containing an area of three acres and ninety-eight hundredths of
an acre (3.98 acs.) more or less.

the Township of Eardley, County of Gatineau, Province of Quebec,
