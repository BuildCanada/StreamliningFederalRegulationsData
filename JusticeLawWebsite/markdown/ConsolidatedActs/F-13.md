## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Freshwater Fish Marketing Corporation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-230907 .Part}

### [Constitution of the Corporation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-230908 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation established

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  corporation is hereby established, to be known as the Freshwater Fish
  Marketing Corporation, consisting of a Board of Directors composed of
  a Chairperson, a President, one director for each participating
  province and four other directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Chairperson and
  President

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson and the President shall each be
  appointed by the Governor in Council to hold office during pleasure
  for such term as the Governor in Council considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of directors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each director, other than the Chairperson and the
  President, shall be appointed by the Minister, with the approval of
  the Governor in Council, to hold office during pleasure for a term not
  exceeding four years that will ensure, as far as possible, the
  expiration in any one year of the terms of office of not more than one
  half of the directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A director of the Corporation for a participating
  province shall be appointed on the recommendation of the lieutenant
  governor in council of the participating province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person who has reached the age of seventy years is
  not eligible to be appointed a director of the Corporation and a
  director of the Corporation ceases to hold office on reaching the age
  of seventy years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A director of the Corporation on the expiration of
  that director's term of office is, if not disqualified by age,
  eligible for re-appointment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancies

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A vacancy on the Board does not impair the right of
  the remaining directors of the Corporation to act, but where the
  vacancy relates to the office of a director for a participating
  province, it shall be filled as soon as practicable in the manner
  provided in this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary substitute directors

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If any director of the Corporation, other than the
  Chairperson or the President, is absent or unable to act, the Governor
  in Council may appoint a temporary substitute director on such
  conditions as the Governor in Council prescribes.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-13, s. 3
- R.S., 1985, c. 1 (4th Supp.), s. 44(E)
- 2006, c. 9, ss. 277, 278(E)

### [Purpose and Powers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-230946 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose and powers

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation is
established for the purpose of marketing and trading in fish, fish
products and fish by-products in and outside Canada and, in addition to
the powers conferred by other provisions of this Act and by any other
Act, has for that purpose power to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} buy fish and dress, fillet, freeze, package or
  otherwise prepare fish for market;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} buy, manufacture or produce fish products and fish
  by-products and package or otherwise prepare fish products and fish
  by-products for market;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} store, ship, insure, import, export, market, sell or
  otherwise dispose of fish, fish products and fish by-products bought,
  prepared, manufactured or produced by it;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} purchase, lease or otherwise acquire and hold, sell
  or otherwise deal with any real property;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} establish branches or employ agents in Canada or
  elsewhere;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} invest any money in its possession or under its
  control that in its opinion is not immediately required for the
  purposes of its operations, in securities of or guaranteed by the
  Government of Canada and sell any securities so acquired by it and
  reinvest the proceeds or any part of the proceeds thereof in like
  manner;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} borrow money from any bank on the credit of the
  Corporation;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} make loans of working capital on a seasonal basis to
  persons engaged in fishing for commercial purposes in a participating
  province; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} do all such other things as are necessary or
  incidental to the exercise of any of its powers or the carrying out of
  any of its functions under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-13, s. 7
- 1984, c. 31, s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acceptance and exercise of additional
powers

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation may enter
into and carry out arrangements with any government or person that the
Corporation deems necessary or desirable in furtherance of the purpose
for which it is established, and may receive and exercise any grants,
rights, franchises, privileges and concessions that may be granted to or
conferred on it by any government or person.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-13, s. 8
:::

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Regulation of Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-231023 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-231024 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** In this Part, except
section 30,

[[fish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means round, dressed or filleted fish
    of any species enumerated in the schedule, whether fresh or frozen
    and whether packaged or unpackaged, that are fished for commercial
    purposes in a participating province, and includes parts of any such
    fish; ([poisson]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fisherman]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fisherman]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person licensed pursuant
    to the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) or the regulations thereunder
    to fish for commercial purposes in a participating province, and
    includes any person acting on behalf of and representing any two or
    more persons so licensed. ([pêcheur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-13, s. 20
:::

### [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText2} {#h-231031 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Trade in fish

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  in accordance with the terms and conditions set out in any licence
  that may be issued by the Corporation in that behalf, no person, other
  than the Corporation or an agent of the Corporation, shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} export fish from Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} send, convey or carry fish from a participating
    province to another participating province or to any other province;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in a participating province, receive fish for
    conveyance or carriage to a destination outside the province; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} sell or buy, or agree to sell or buy, fish situated
    in a participating province for delivery in another participating
    province or any other province, or outside Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws respecting licences

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may, for the purposes of this section, make
  by-laws providing for the issue of licences by the Corporation and
  prescribing the form of and the terms and conditions to be set out in
  those licences.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-13, s. 21
:::

### [Regulations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-231043 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may by regulation

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} add to the schedule any species of fish that are
  fished in a participating province or delete from the schedule any
  species of fish enumerated therein; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} exempt from the application of all or any of the
  provisions of this Part, either conditionally or unconditionally and
  either in general terms or for a specified period, any species of fish
  enumerated in the schedule, any area or region in a participating
  province or any transaction, person or class of transactions or
  persons.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-13, s. 22
:::

### [Duties and Powers of the Corporation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-231050 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation to undertake interprovincial
and export marketing of fish

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 20, the
  Corporation has the exclusive right to trade in and to market fish in
  interprovincial and export trade and shall exercise that right, either
  by itself or by its agents, with the object of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} marketing fish in an orderly manner;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} increasing returns to fishermen; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} promoting international markets for, and increasing
    interprovincial and export trade in, fish.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation to buy all fish offered

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All fish lawfully fished by a fisherman and offered
  by the fisherman for sale to the Corporation for disposal in
  interprovincial or export trade shall be bought by the Corporation
  from the fisherman on such terms and conditions and for such price as
  may be agreed on by the Corporation and the fisherman subject to any
  applicable scheme for payment established and operated by the
  Corporation pursuant to section 23.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marketing of fish

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation shall determine the form in which
  fish bought by it pursuant to this section shall be marketed and shall
  carry out or cause to be carried out any processing or preparation for
  market that it deems necessary, and the Corporation shall market the
  fish at such times and places and in such manner as in its opinion
  will best achieve the attainment of the objects mentioned in
  subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-13, s. 23
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Price systems and pooling authorized

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation may
develop, establish and operate schemes for payment for fish bought by it
pursuant to this Part that provide for either or both

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a system of initial and final prices or payments; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the pooling of receipts for fish, including the
  operation of pool accounts, either generally or in relation to the
  area or region where the fish were fished, or the species, volume or
  quality of the fish sold to the Corporation, or on such other basis as
  the Corporation deems appropriate.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-13, s. 24
:::

### [Agreements Respecting Participation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-231069 .Subheading}

Definition of [[province to which this section
applies]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[province to which this section
  applies]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following provinces or
  territories, namely, Ontario, Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Alberta, the
  Northwest Territories or Nunavut.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} With the approval of the Governor in Council, the
  Minister may, on behalf of the Government of Canada, enter into an
  agreement with the government of any province to which this section
  applies, providing for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sharing by the province with the Government of
    Canada of initial operating and establishment expenses of the
    Corporation and of any losses incurred as a result of the guarantee,
    under paragraph 16(1)(a), of repayments of loans, and interest
    thereon, made by any bank to the Corporation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the performance by the Corporation, on behalf of
    the province, of functions relating to intraprovincial trade in
    fish;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the undertaking by the province of arrangements for
    the payment, to the owner of any plant or equipment used in storing,
    processing or otherwise preparing fish for market, of compensation
    for any such plant or equipment that will or may be rendered
    redundant by reason of any operations authorized to be carried out
    by the Corporation under this Part; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} such other matters as may be agreed on by the
    Minister and the government of the province.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-13, s. 24
- 1993, c. 28, s. 78
:::

### [Enforcement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-231081 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspectors

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may designate any qualified person as an inspector
  for the purposes of this Part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate to be produced

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An inspector shall be furnished with a certificate of
  his designation as an inspector and on entering any place, premises or
  conveyance referred to in subsection 26(1) shall, if so required,
  produce the certificate to the person in charge thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-13, ss. 26, 27
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of inspectors

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (1.1), an
  inspector may at any reasonable time

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter any place or premises that the inspector
    believes on reasonable grounds is being used to store, pack, process
    or prepare fish for market or shipment or any vehicle, trailer,
    vessel, railway car or aircraft that the inspector believes on
    reasonable grounds is being used to ship or convey fish for market;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} open any container found therein or examine
    anything found therein that the inspector believes on reasonable
    grounds contains any such fish, and take samples thereof; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} require any person to produce for inspection or for
    the purpose of obtaining copies thereof or extracts therefrom, any
    books, shipping bills, bills of lading, invoices or other documents
    or papers concerning any matter relevant to the administration of
    this Part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant required to enter
  dwelling-house

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Where any place or premises referred to in
  paragraph (1)(a) is a dwelling-house, an inspector may not enter that
  dwelling-house without the consent of the occupant except under the
  authority of a warrant issued under subsection (1.2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} Where on [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  application a justice of the peace is satisfied by information on oath

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the conditions for entry described in
    paragraph (1)(a) exist in relation to a dwelling-house,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that entry to the dwelling-house is necessary for
    any purpose relating to the administration or enforcement of this
    Act, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that entry to the dwelling-house has been refused
    or that there are reasonable grounds for believing that entry
    thereto will be refused,

  the justice of the peace may issue a warrant under his hand
  authorizing the inspector named therein to enter that dwelling-house
  subject to such conditions as may be specified in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

  [(1.3)]{.lawlabel} In executing a warrant issued under subsection
  (1.2), the inspector named therein shall not use force unless the
  inspector is accompanied by a peace officer and the use of force has
  been specifically authorized in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to inspectors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner or person in charge of any place, premises
  or conveyance referred to in subsection (1) and every person found
  therein shall give an inspector all reasonable assistance to enable
  the inspector to carry out his duties and functions under this Part
  and shall furnish the inspector with any information he may reasonably
  require with respect to the administration of this Part.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-13, s. 26
- R.S., 1985, c. 31 (1st Supp.), s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seizure

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an inspector believes on reasonable grounds that any provision of this
  Part has been contravened, the inspector may seize and detain the fish
  by means of or in relation to which he believes on reasonable grounds
  the contravention was committed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Detention

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any fish seized and detained pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall not be detained after

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the opinion of an inspector, the provisions of
    this Part have been complied with, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the expiration of ninety days after the date of
    seizure,

  unless before that time proceedings have been instituted in respect of
  the contravention, in which event the fish may be detained until the
  proceedings are finally concluded.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a person has been convicted of a contravention
  of any provision of this Part, any fish by means of or in relation to
  which the offence was committed is, on the conviction, in addition to
  any punishment imposed, forfeited to Her Majesty if the forfeiture is
  directed by the court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the detention of fish seized under this
    section and for preserving or safeguarding the fish so detained; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the disposition of fish forfeited under
    this section.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-13, s. 28
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction and false statements

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** No person shall obstruct
or hinder, or make a false or misleading statement either orally or in
writing to, an inspector while the inspector is engaged in carrying out
his duties or functions under this Part.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-13, s. 29
:::

### [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-231129 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of this Part

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who, or whose employee or agent, contravenes any provision of
  this Part

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an indictable offence and liable to
    imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
    conviction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence by employee or agent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In any prosecution for an offence under this Part, it
  is sufficient proof of the offence to establish that it was committed
  by an employee or agent of the accused whether or not the employee or
  agent is identified or has been prosecuted for the offence, unless the
  accused establishes that the offence was committed without the
  knowledge or consent of the accused and that the accused exercised all
  due diligence to prevent its commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any proceedings by way of summary conviction in
  respect of an offence under this Part may be instituted at any time
  within but not later than one year after the time when the
  subject-matter of the proceedings arose.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-13, s. 30
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence with respect to geographical
origin

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** In any prosecution for an
offence under this Part, evidence that a container was marked in such a
manner as to indicate or give rise to a reasonable belief that the fish
contained therein were the product of or were fished in a particular
participating province or a particular area or region in the province
is, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, proof that the fish
contained therein were fished in that province.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-13, s. 31
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Venue

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** A complaint or
information in respect of an offence under this Part may be heard, tried
or determined by a provincial court judge or a justice if the accused is
resident or carrying on business within the territorial jurisdiction of
that provincial court judge or justice, although the matter of the
complaint or information did not arise in that territorial jurisdiction.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-13, s. 31
- R.S., 1985, c. 27 (1st Supp.), s. 203
:::

## [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Annual Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-231149 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission to lieutenant governors

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation shall
submit a copy of each annual report of the Corporation prepared in
accordance with section 150 of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11) to the lieutenant governor in council of each
participating province.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-13, s. 33
- 1984, c. 31, s. 14
