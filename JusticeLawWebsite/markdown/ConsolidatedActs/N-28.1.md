## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368216 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Nuclear Liability and Compensation Act](/eng/acts/N-28.1).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368219 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[approved insurer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approved insurer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an insurer or
    association of insurers that is designated under section 29 as an
    approved insurer. ([assureur agréé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Contracting State]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Contracting State]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a State that has
    ratified, accepted or approved the Convention in accordance with its
    Article XVIII or that has acceded to it in accordance with its
    Article XIX. ([État contractant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on
    Supplementary Compensation for Nuclear Damage, done at Vienna on
    September 12, 1997 and signed by Canada on December 3, 2013, as
    amended from time to time. ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Installation State]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Installation State]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Contracting State
    within whose territory is situated a nuclear installation as defined
    in Article 1.I(b) of the Annex to the Convention or, if the nuclear
    installation is not within the territory of a Contracting State, the
    Contracting State by which or under whose authority the nuclear
    installation is operated. ([État où se trouve
    l'installation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nuclear fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means material that is capable
    of a self-sustaining nuclear fission chain reaction. ([combustible
    nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nuclear incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an occurrence or a
    series of occurrences having the same origin that causes damage for
    which an operator is liable under this Act. ([accident
    nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nuclear installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, other than in
    the definition [[Installation State]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and
    subparagraphs 9(1)(b.1)(i) and (b.2)(i) and 9(4)(b)(i) and (c)(i) of
    the English version, any site or means of transport that is
    designated under section 7 as a nuclear
    installation. ([établissement nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[nuclear material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} nuclear fuel, other than natural uranium or
      depleted uranium, that can produce energy by a self-sustaining
      nuclear fission chain reaction outside a nuclear reactor, either
      alone or in combination with another material; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} radioactive products or waste, other than
      radioisotopes that have reached the final stage of fabrication so
      as to be usable for any scientific, medical, agricultural,
      commercial or industrial purpose. ([matière
      nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nuclear reactor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear reactor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a structure containing
    nuclear fuel arranged such that a self-sustaining nuclear fission
    chain reaction can occur in the structure without an additional
    source of neutrons. ([réacteur nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is designated by
    a regulation made under section 7 as an
    operator. ([exploitant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[public funds]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public funds]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an amount that
    Contracting States must contribute when a call for funds is made
    under Article VII.1 of the Convention. ([fonds
    publics]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[radioactive products or waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radioactive products or waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} radioactive material that is produced in the
      production or use of nuclear fuel other than natural uranium or
      depleted uranium; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} material that is made radioactive by exposure to
      radiation consequential on or incidental to the production or use
      of nuclear fuel other than natural uranium or depleted
      uranium. ([produit ou déchet radioactif]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a nuclear claims tribunal
    established under subsection 41(1). ([Tribunal]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Purpose of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368239 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Civil liability and compensation

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to govern civil liability and compensation for damage in case of a
nuclear incident.

## [Designation of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368242 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate a minister of the Crown to be the Minister
referred to in this Act.

## [Non-Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368245 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- war, etc.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This Act
  does not apply to a nuclear incident that results from an act of war,
  hostilities, civil war or insurrection, other than a terrorist
  activity as defined in subsection 83.01(1) of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- damage to nuclear
  installation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Act does not apply to damage to the nuclear
  installation of an operator who is responsible for that damage or to
  any property at the installation that is used in connection with the
  installation, including property under construction.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368251 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Designation of Nuclear Installations and Operators]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368254 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of nuclear installations

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the Minister's recommendation and after
  consultation with the Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission, designate by
  regulation any site at which is located a facility or facilities that
  are authorized by a licence issued under the [Nuclear Safety and
  Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3) and that contain nuclear material as a
  nuclear installation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Description of site and designation of
  operator

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The regulation must describe the site, list the
  facilities on it that are authorized to contain nuclear material and
  designate the holder of a licence described in subsection (1) as the
  operator of the nuclear installation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The regulation may be made before a licence has been
  issued, but it must not come into force before the day on which the
  licence is issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of means of transport

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, on the Minister's
  recommendation and after consultation with the Canadian Nuclear Safety
  Commission, designate by regulation any means of transport that is
  equipped with a nuclear reactor as a nuclear installation and
  designate by regulation the holder of a licence issued under the
  [Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3) respecting that
  means of transport as the operator of the nuclear installation.

## [Liability for Nuclear Incidents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368264 .Part}

### [Operator's Liability]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368265 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An operator is not liable
for damage that is caused by a nuclear incident except for any liability
that is provided for under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability --- Canada

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  operator --- and no person other than an operator --- is liable for
  damage that is caused within Canada or its exclusive economic zone by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ionizing radiation emitted from any source of
    radiation within, or released from, the operator's nuclear
    installation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ionizing radiation emitted from nuclear material
    being transported

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} from the operator's nuclear installation until it
      is placed in another nuclear installation or until liability is
      assumed by the operator of that other nuclear installation, under
      the terms of a written contract,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2015, c. 4, s. 121\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} from the operator's nuclear installation to a
      person who is within the territory of a State that is not a
      Contracting State until it is unloaded from the means of transport
      by which it arrived in that State, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} with the operator's written consent, from a
      person who is within the territory of a State that is not a
      Contracting State to the operator's installation, from the time
      that it is loaded on the means of transport by which it is to be
      carried from that State;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} ionizing radiation emitted from nuclear material
    being transported from the operator's nuclear installation

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} before liability is assumed under the terms of a
      written contract, by a person who is within the territory of a
      Contracting State other than Canada and who is designated or
      recognized under the laws of that State as operating a nuclear
      installation as defined in Article 1.I(b) of the Annex to the
      Convention, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the absence of a contract, before that person
      takes charge of the nuclear material;

  - [(b.2)]{.lawlabel} ionizing radiation emitted from nuclear material
    being transported to the operator's nuclear installation

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} after liability is assumed by the operator under
      the terms of a written contract, from a person who is within the
      territory of a Contracting State other than Canada and who is
      designated or recognized under the laws of that State as operating
      a nuclear installation as defined in Article 1.I(b) of the Annex
      to the Convention, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the absence of a contract, after the operator
      takes charge of the nuclear material; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a combination of the radioactive properties and
    toxic, explosive or other hazardous properties of a source referred
    to in paragraph (a) or nuclear material referred to in paragraph
    (b), (b.1) or (b.2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preventive measure --- liability in
  Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An operatorand no person other than an operator ---
  is liable for damage that is caused within Canada or its exclusive
  economic zone if the damage is caused by a preventive measure that is
  taken under subsection --- 20(1) in relation to that operator's
  nuclear installation or in relation to any transportation for which
  the operator is responsible.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2015, c. 4, s. 121\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional liability --- Contracting
  State other than Canada

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An operator --- and no person other than an operator
  --- is liable for damage that is caused within a Contracting State
  other than Canada or within that State's exclusive economic zone by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ionizing radiation emitted from any source of
    radiation within, or released from, the operator's nuclear
    installation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ionizing radiation emitted from nuclear material
    being transported from the operator's nuclear installation

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} before liability is assumed, under the terms of a
      written contract, by a person who is within the territory of the
      Contracting State other than Canada and who is designated or
      recognized under the laws of that State as operating a nuclear
      installation as defined in Article 1.I(b) of the Annex to the
      Convention, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the absence of a contract, before that person
      takes charge of the nuclear material;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ionizing radiation emitted from nuclear material
    being transported to the operator's nuclear installation

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} after liability is assumed by the operator, under
      the terms of a written contract, from a person who is within the
      territory of the Contracting State other than Canada and who is
      designated or recognized under the laws of that State as operating
      a nuclear installation as defined in Article 1.I(b) of the Annex
      to the Convention, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the absence of a contract, after the operator
      takes charge of the nuclear material; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a combination of the radioactive properties and
    toxic, explosive or other hazardous properties of a source referred
    to in paragraph (a) or nuclear material referred to in paragraph (b)
    or (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preventive measure --- liability in
  Contracting State other than Canada

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An operator --- and no person other than an operator
  ---  is liable for any damage that is caused within a Contracting
  State other than Canada or within that State's exclusive economic zone
  if the damage is caused by a preventive measure that is taken under
  subsection 21(1) in relation to that operator's nuclear installation
  or in relation to any transportation for which the operator is
  responsible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional liability --- transportation
  to or from non-contracting State

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} An operator --- and no person other than an operator
  --- is liable for damage that is caused within a Contracting State
  other than Canada or within that State's exclusive economic zone by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ionizing radiation emitted from nuclear material
    being transported

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} from the operator's nuclear installation to a
      person who is within the territory of a State that is not a
      Contracting State until it is unloaded from the means of transport
      by which it arrived in that State; or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} with the operator's written consent, from a
      person who is within the territory of a State that is not a
      Contracting State to the operator's nuclear installation, from the
      time it is loaded on the means of transport by which it is to be
      carried from that State; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a combination of the radioactive properties and
    toxic, explosive or other hazardous properties of nuclear material
    referred to in paragraph (a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absolute liability

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  liability of an operator for damage that is caused by a nuclear
  incident is absolute.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tort or fault

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), no proof of tort
  or of fault within the meaning of the Civil Code of Québec is
  required.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability --- jointly and severally, or
solidarily

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** If liability under this
Act is incurred by two or more operators, each is jointly and severally,
or solidarily, liable to the extent that it cannot reasonably be
determined what portion of the liability is attributable to each
operator.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person responsible for nuclear incident

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** An operator is not liable
for damage that is suffered by a person if that person intentionally
caused the nuclear incident wholly or partly by an act or omission or
under circumstances amounting to gross negligence or, in Quebec, gross
fault.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No recourse

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** In respect of damage that
is caused by a nuclear incident, an operator has no right of recourse
against any person other than an individual who intentionally caused the
nuclear incident by an act or omission.

### [Compensable Damage]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368318 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bodily injury or damage to property

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Bodily injury or death
and damage to property that are caused by a nuclear incident are
compensable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Psychological trauma

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Psychological trauma that
is suffered by a person is compensable if it results from bodily injury
to that person that was caused by a nuclear incident.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability for economic loss

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Economic loss that is
incurred by a person as a result of their bodily injury or damage to
their property and that is caused by a nuclear incident, or
psychological trauma that results from that bodily injury, is
compensable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs and wages

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  costs that are incurred by a person who loses the use of property as a
  result of a nuclear incident and the resulting wage loss by that
  person's employees are compensable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power failure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a nuclear incident occurs at a nuclear
  installation that generates electricity, the costs resulting from a
  failure of the installation to provide electricity are not compensable
  under subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Environmental damage --- Canada

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Reasonable costs of
remedial measures that are taken to repair, reduce or mitigate
environmental damage that is caused by a nuclear incident are
compensable if the measures are ordered by an authority acting under
federal or provincial legislation relating to environmental protection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Environmental damage --- Contracting
State other than Canada

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Unless the damage is
insignificant, reasonable costs of remedial measures that are taken to
repair, reduce or mitigate environmental damage that is caused by a
nuclear incident are compensable if the measures are ordered by an
authority of a Contracting State other than Canada acting under the laws
of that State relating to environmental protection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preventive measures --- Canada

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  authority --- acting under a nuclear emergency scheme established
  under federal or provincial legislation --- has recommended that
  measures be taken in a specified area to prevent damage, the following
  costs and losses of persons who live in, carry on business in, work in
  or are present in the area are compensable:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reasonable costs of the measures; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the costs and economic loss --- including lost
    wages --- arising from the loss of use of property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, any federal, provincial or
  municipal authority, or any of its agencies, that establishes or
  implements a nuclear emergency scheme is not to be compensated under
  subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preventive measures --- Contracting State
other than Canada

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  authority --- acting under an emergency scheme established under the
  laws of a Contracting State other than Canada --- has recommended
  that, because of grave and imminent danger of damage, measures be
  taken in a specified area to prevent such damage, the following costs
  and losses of persons who live in, carry on business in, work in or
  are present in the area are compensable:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reasonable costs of the measures; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the costs and economic loss --- including lost
    wages --- arising from the loss of use of property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, any authority, or any of its
  agencies, that establishes or implements a nuclear emergency scheme is
  not to be compensated under subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Damage attributable to concomitant
nuclear incidents

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Any damage resulting from
a nuclear incident and any concomitant non-nuclear incident is deemed to
be damage that is caused by the nuclear incident to the extent that it
cannot be identified as having been caused only by the non-nuclear
incident.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Damage to means of transport, structure
or site

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** If a nuclear incident
occurs during the transportation of nuclear material to or from a
nuclear installation, or any storage incidental to the transportation,
damage to the means of transport or the structure or site where the
nuclear material is stored is not compensable under this Act.

### [Financial Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368352 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit of operator's liability

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  liability of an operator under this Act for damage resulting from a
  nuclear incident is limited to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$650 million for a nuclear incident arising within
    one year after the day on which this paragraph comes into force;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$750 million for a nuclear incident arising within
    one year after the year referred to in paragraph (a);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$850 million for a nuclear incident arising within
    one year after the year referred to in paragraph (b); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} \$1 billion for a nuclear incident arising after
    the year referred to in paragraph (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment to amount of liability

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by regulation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} amend subsection (1) to increase any amount of
    liability; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reduce the amount of liability applicable to an
    operator of a nuclear installation, or operators of a class of
    nuclear installations, having regard to the nature of the
    installation and the nuclear material contained in it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not relieve an operator from
  payment of the costs of administering claims, court costs or interest
  on compensation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability --- transportation

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** If a nuclear incident
occurs during the transportation of nuclear material or storage
incidental to the transportation and more than one operator is liable
for the damage that is caused by that nuclear incident, the total
liability of those operators is limited to the amount referred to in
subsection 24(1) in relation to one operator.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review by Minister

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must review the limit of liability, referred to in subsection
  24(1), on a regular basis and at least once every five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In carrying out the review, the Minister must have
  regard to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} changes in the Consumer Price Index, as published
    by Statistics Canada under the authority of the [Statistics
    Act](/eng/acts/S-19);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} financial security requirements under international
    agreements respecting nuclear liability; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other considerations that the Minister
    considers relevant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operator's obligation

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  operator, other than a department listed in Schedule I to the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), must maintain, for
  each of the operator's nuclear installations, financial security to
  compensate persons who suffer damage that is caused by a nuclear
  incident in an amount that is equal to the amount referred to in
  subsection 24(1) or, if the operator is subject to a regulation made
  under paragraph 24(2)(b), the amount set out in that regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foreign operator's obligation ---
  transporting nuclear material within Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may require an operator, as defined in
  Article 1.I(d) of the Annex to the Convention but who is not an
  operator as defined in section 2 of this Act, and who is transporting
  nuclear material within Canada to maintain financial security in an
  amount prescribed by regulation but not more than the amount referred
  to in subsection 24(1) to compensate persons who suffer damage that is
  caused by a nuclear incident.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to transport by sea if, under international law,
    there is a right of entry into a Canadian port in a case of
    distress, or if there is a right of innocent passage through
    Canadian territory; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to transport by air if, under an agreement to which
    Canada is a party or under international law, there is a right to
    fly over or land on Canadian territory.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of financial security

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The financial security is not to be used by an
  operator referred to in subsection (1) to pay their costs of
  administering claims, court costs, legal fees or interest on
  compensation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insurance

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  financial security is to be in the form of insurance with an approved
  insurer, containing only the terms and conditions set out in a
  standard insurance policy that is approved by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternate financial security

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may enter into an agreement with the
  operator that authorizes that a portion of the financial security be
  an alternate financial security.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amount of financial security

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the alternate financial security must
  not, unless another percentage has been fixed by regulation, exceed
  50% of the operator's liability that is applicable under section 24.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms of agreement

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The agreement must identify the financial instrument
  being used as the alternate financial security, specify its dollar
  value and set out any conditions that the Minister considers
  appropriate, including a requirement that the operator submit reports
  or allow the Minister to undertake financial audits in respect of the
  security or that the operator pay a fee for the authorization of the
  security or for the audits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment or revocation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may amend the conditions of an agreement
  or revoke an agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approved insurer

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, subject
to any terms and conditions that he or she may impose, designate as an
approved insurer any insurer or association of insurers that, in his or
her opinion, is qualified to fulfill the obligations of an approved
insurer under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension or cancellation

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** An approved insurer or
any provider of an alternate financial security referred to in
subsection 28(2) may suspend or cancel an operator's insurance or
alternate financial security only if written notice is given to the
Minister at least two months before the suspension or cancellation, but,
if the insurance or security relates to the transportation of nuclear
material, the cancellation or suspension is not to take effect during
the period of transportation to which it relates.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnity agreements --- general rule

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may enter into an indemnity agreement with an operator under
  which Her Majesty in right of Canada covers any risks that, in the
  Minister's opinion, would not be assumed by an approved insurer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnity agreements --- operator
  subject to regulation made under paragraph 24(2)(b)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the nuclear damage is caused by an operator who is
  subject to a regulation made under paragraph 24(2)(b) and that damage
  exceeds that operator's liability under that regulation, the indemnity
  agreement may also provide that Her Majesty in right of Canada must
  cover that operator for the difference between the operator's
  liability under the regulation and the liability of any other operator
  under subsection 24(1). Despite the indemnity agreement, the operator
  remains liable for the damage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any indemnity agreement may provide for the payment
  of fees to Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of agreements

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must cause a copy of each indemnity
  agreement that is entered into under this section to be laid before
  each House of Parliament on any of the first 30 days on which that
  House is sitting after the agreement is entered into.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nuclear Liability Account

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Nuclear Liability Reinsurance Account, established in the accounts of
  Canada under the [Nuclear Liability Act](/eng/acts/N-28), is continued
  as the Nuclear Liability Account to which are to be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} credited all amounts received by Her Majesty in
    right of Canada as fees under an indemnity agreement; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} charged all amounts that are payable by Her Majesty
    in right of Canada under an indemnity agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advances to account out of C.R.F.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the amount standing to the credit of the Nuclear
  Liability Account is insufficient for the payment of the amounts that
  are required under the terms of an indemnity agreement, an amount that
  is sufficient to meet the deficit is, with the Minister of Finance's
  approval, to be paid from the Consolidated Revenue Fund and credited
  to the Nuclear Liability Account.

### [Judicial Proceedings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368424 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where action is to be brought

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  action involving damage that is caused by a nuclear incident is to be
  brought in the court in Canada that has jurisdiction in the place
  where the incident occurs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Federal Court jurisdiction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Federal Court has jurisdiction if the nuclear
  incident occurs

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in more than one province;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} partly within a province and partly within Canada's
    exclusive economic zone; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} within Canada's exclusive economic zone.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional jurisdiction of Federal
  Court

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the nuclear incident occurs outside the territory
  or the exclusive economic zone of any Contracting State, or the place
  where the nuclear incident occurred cannot be determined with
  certainty, the Federal Court has jurisdiction if the nuclear incident
  is caused by an operator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Concurrent jurisdiction

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a court of a Contracting State other than Canada
  has concurrent jurisdiction for a claim or action for damage under
  this Act, Canada and the other Contracting State must determine, by
  agreement, which court is to have exclusive jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recognition of foreign judgments

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A court of competent jurisdiction in Canada must, as
  soon as feasible on receipt of an application, recognize and enforce a
  judgment of a court of a Contracting State other than Canada that, in
  addition to meeting the criteria under Canadian law for being
  recognized in Canada, is rendered in accordance with the Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on jurisdiction

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Except as provided in this Act, no court in Canada
  and no tribunal has jurisdiction to entertain any application or grant
  any relief or remedy relating to damage that occurs outside Canada or
  its exclusive economic zone.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation on bringing actions and claims

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  action or claim must be brought within three years

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an action or claim for loss of life,
    after the day on which the person bringing the action or making the
    claim had knowledge or ought reasonably to have had knowledge of
    both the loss of life and the identity of the operator who is
    responsible for the loss of life;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case where conclusive evidence of the loss
    of life is not available, after the day on which both an order
    presuming the person to be dead is made by a court having
    jurisdiction and the person bringing the action or making the claim
    had knowledge or ought reasonably to have had knowledge of the
    identity of the operator who is responsible for the presumed loss of
    life; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, after the day on which the
    person bringing the action or making the claim had knowledge or
    ought reasonably to have had knowledge of both the damage and the
    identity of the operator who is responsible for the damage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absolute limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No action or claim is to be brought

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to bodily injury or death, 30 years
    after the day on which the nuclear incident to which the action or
    claim relates occurred; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, 10 years after the day on which
    the nuclear incident to which the action or claim relates occurred.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), if the damage is the result
  of a nuclear incident involving nuclear material that was, at the time
  of the nuclear incident, lost, stolen, jettisoned or abandoned, no
  action or claim is to be brought 20 years after the day on which the
  loss, theft, jettison or abandonment occurred.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of period

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by regulation, extend
  the period set out in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New jurisdiction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any claims that could have been made before the
  declaration is made are, after the day on which it is made, only to be

### [Establishment of a Nuclear Claims Tribunal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368480 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tribunal's establishment

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council must, as soon as feasible after a declaration is
  made under subsection 36(1), establish a nuclear claims Tribunal and
  designate the location of its head office in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal's purpose is to examine and adjudicate
  claims for damage arising from the nuclear incident as expeditiously
  as the circumstances and considerations of fairness permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claims treated equitably

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal must carry out its duties and functions
  with respect to claims for damage in an equitable manner, without
  discrimination on the basis of nationality or residence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public notice

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal must notify the public, in a manner that it considers
  appropriate, of the details of its purpose and how to obtain
  information on bringing a claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of the Tribunal's purpose and how to obtain
  information on bringing a claim must also be published, without delay,
  in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members of Tribunal

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council must appoint a minimum of five persons to the
  Tribunal, one of whom is to be designated as the chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members' qualifications

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A majority of the members of the Tribunal are to be
  appointed from among persons who are sitting or retired judges of a
  superior court or members of at least 10 years' standing at the bar of
  a province or the [Chambre des notaires du Québec]{.otherLang
  lang="fr"}.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The members are to be paid the remuneration and
  expenses fixed by the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** Each member of the
Tribunal is to be appointed to hold office during good behaviour for a
term that the Governor in Council considers appropriate and may be
removed for cause.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** No civil proceedings lie
against any member of the Tribunal for anything done or omitted to be
done by the member in good faith in the exercise or purported exercise
of a power or in the performance or purported performance of a duty or
function of the Tribunal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tribunal's staff

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may employ
the staff that it considers necessary for the proper conduct of its
duties or functions, prescribe their duties and, subject to any
regulations, their terms and conditions of employment and, with the
approval of the Treasury Board, fix and pay their remuneration.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Technical or specialized knowledge

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may engage,
on a temporary basis, the services of counsel and other persons having
technical or specialized knowledge to assist the Tribunal in its work,
establish the terms and conditions of their employment and, with the
approval of the Treasury Board, fix and pay their remuneration and
expenses.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** In the event of an
inconsistency between a provision of the [Judges Act](/eng/acts/J-1) and
any provision of this Act that is applicable to a sitting or retired
judge, the [Judges Act](/eng/acts/J-1) prevails to the extent of the
inconsistency.

### [Tribunal's Powers and Duties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368510 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearings

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal is to hold
its hearings in Canada at the times and locations that it considers
appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intervenor

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** The Attorney General of
Canada and the competent authority of any other Contracting State may
intervene in proceedings that are before the Tribunal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers --- witnesses and documents

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal has, with respect to the attendance, swearing and examination
  of witnesses, the production and inspection of documents, the
  enforcement of its orders and other matters that are necessary or
  proper for the due exercise of its jurisdiction, all of the powers,
  rights and privileges that are vested in a superior court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence at hearings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal is not, in the hearing of any claim,
  bound by the legal rules of evidence, but it must not receive as
  evidence anything that would be inadmissible in a court by reason of
  any privilege under the law of evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foreign evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may issue commissions to take evidence
  outside Canada and may make orders for that purpose and for the return
  and use of the evidence so obtained.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examinations

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may require
persons claiming compensation to undergo medical or other examinations
that are, in the Tribunal's opinion, reasonably necessary to enable it
to determine their claims.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Frivolous or vexatious claims

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may refuse
to hear any claim referred to it that it considers to be frivolous or
vexatious.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report on Tribunal's activities

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal must, at the
Minister's request, submit to him or her a report on its activities. The
Minister must cause the report to be laid before each House of
Parliament on any of the first 15 days on which that House is sitting
after he or she receives it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may make any
rules that it considers necessary for the exercise of its powers and the
performance of its duties and functions, including rules respecting

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} procedures for bringing claims;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the form and manner in which evidence is to be
  submitted;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a quorum;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} procedures that claims officers are to follow in
  dealing with claims;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fees and travel expenses that are to be paid to
  witnesses;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the allowance of costs; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} appeals and rehearings.

### [Claims]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368537 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Panels

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  chairperson may establish panels of the Tribunal consisting of one or
  more members to hear claims.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claims officer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may, in order to process claims
  expeditiously, establish classes of claims that may be determined by
  the claims officer without an oral hearing and designate as a claims
  officer anyone that it considers qualified.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers and duties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A panel or claims officer must exercise the powers
  and perform the duties and functions of the Tribunal with respect to
  claims that are before that panel or claims officer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** The chairperson must
assign a claim to a panel or claims officer and notify the claimant, the
operator and the Minister of the assignment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public hearings

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** Panel hearings are to be
held in public. However, a panel may hold all or part of a hearing in
private if, in its opinion, a person's privacy interest outweighs the
principle that hearings be open to the public.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interim award of compensation

- **[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may award interim compensation in respect of a claim that is
  heard by it before it makes a decision with respect to the claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal must inform the Minister of the amount
  of the interim compensation awarded, and the Minister must pay that
  amount to the claimant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice --- decision

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal must notify the claimant and the operator of its decision
  with respect to the claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Awards of compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Tribunal decides to award compensation in
  respect of a claim, the notification must also be sent to the Minister
  and must indicate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the award;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any reduction in that amount applicable under the
    regulations; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any amounts that have already been paid with
    respect to the claim in accordance with this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs and interest

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the award must not include any costs
  awarded to the claimant in any proceeding that is before the Tribunal
  or any interest payable on that award.

### [Rehearing and Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368564 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rehearing of claims officer's decision

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** A claimant or operator
who is dissatisfied with a claims officer's decision may, within 30 days
after receiving notification of the decision, apply to the Tribunal for
a rehearing by a panel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal

- **[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  claim has been heard by a panel that consists of fewer than three
  members, the claimant or operator may, within 30 days after receiving
  notification of the decision, apply in writing to the chairperson for
  leave to appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing of appeal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appeal is to be heard and decided by a panel
  consisting of three other members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The appeal is to be heard on the basis of the record
  of the panel whose decision is appealed and on the submissions of
  interested parties. The panel hearing the appeal may, in exceptional
  circumstances, if, in its opinion, it is essential in the interests of
  justice to do so, admit additional evidence or testimony.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial review

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 61
and 62, every decision of the Tribunal is final and conclusive and is
not to be questioned or reviewed in any court except in accordance with
the [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7) on the grounds referred to in
paragraph 18.1(4)(a), (b) or (e) of that Act.

### [Financial Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368576 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of awards

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of overpayment

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** Any overpayment is a debt

accordance with section 155 of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of Nuclear Liability Account

- **[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  payments made by the Minister are to be paid out of the Nuclear
  Liability Account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advances to account out of C.R.F.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the amount standing to the credit of the Nuclear
  Liability Account is insufficient for the payment of the amounts that
  are required, an amount that is sufficient to meet the deficit is,
  with the Minister of Finance's approval, to be paid from the
  Consolidated Revenue Fund and credited to the Nuclear Liability
  Account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operator's liability to Her Majesty

- **[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  declaration is made under subsection 36(1), the operator who is liable
  for the damage that is caused by a nuclear incident must pay to Her
  Majesty in right of Canada an amount that is equal to the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount set out in subsection 24(1) ---  or, if
    the operator is subject to a regulation made under paragraph
    24(2)(b), the amount set out in that regulation --- less the total
    amounts that were paid by the operator to compensate persons for
    damage arising from the nuclear incident before the declaration
    under subsection 36(1) is made, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total of all amounts that are paid by the
    Minister under section 64.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to pay

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the operator fails to pay any amount that is due,
  it must be paid to Her Majesty in right of Canada by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approved insurer, for the financial security
    that is in the form of insurance; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the issuer of the financial instrument, for
    alternate financial security.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount paid by operator, approved
  insurer or issuer of security

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The operator, the approved insurer or the issuer of
  the financial instrument, as the case may be, must pay to Her Majesty
  in right of Canada, at the Minister's request, any amount that is
  specified in the request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The total amount that is requested by the Minister
  under subsection (3) in respect of any year must not be more than the
  total amount that is paid by the Minister under section 39, subsection
  59(2) and section 64.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt due to Crown

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An amount that is not paid as required under
  subsection (3) is a debt that is due to Her Majesty in right of Canada
  and may be recovered in accordance with section 155 of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nuclear Liability Account

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Any amount received by Her Majesty in right of Canada
  under this section is to be credited to the Nuclear Liability Account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit of payments

- **[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal must not award, in respect of a nuclear incident, an amount
  that is more than the amount set out in subsection 24(1) less the
  total of all amounts that are paid by the operator to compensate
  persons for damage arising from the nuclear incident before the
  declaration is made under subsection 36(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional funds --- public funds

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), if the Minister makes a call
  for public funds under subsection 72(1), the Tribunal may award an
  additional amount of funds that are equal to the amount of public
  funds that are paid by Contracting States.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further funds --- Parliament

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If further funds are appropriated by Parliament to
  provide compensation for damage arising from the nuclear incident, the
  Tribunal may award those further funds for the damage.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changes to reductions

- **[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  regulation made under paragraph 80(b) is amended, the Tribunal must
  inform the Minister of any change to applicable reductions that is to
  the advantage of any claimant who was not fully compensated because of
  the previous regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must pay to the claimant the difference
  between the amount that has already been paid and the amount that
  would be paid under the amended regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration of new claims

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a regulation made under paragraph 80(c) is
  amended, the Tribunal may consider any new claim for which
  compensation could not be awarded because of the previous regulation.

## [Other International Obligations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368619 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional liability --- call for public
funds

- **[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  call for public funds is made under subsection 72(1), those funds are
  to be used to compensate the damage that is suffered, if it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} occurs in the territory of a Contracting State;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} occurs in or above the exclusive economic zone of a
    Contracting State or on the continental shelf of a Contracting
    State, and relates to the exploitation or exploration of the natural
    resources of that exclusive economic zone or continental shelf; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} occurs in or above the maritime areas beyond the
    territorial sea of a Contracting State --- on board or by a ship
    flying the flag of a Contracting State, on board or by an aircraft
    registered in a Contracting State, on or by an artificial island, on
    or by an installation or a structure under a Contracting State's
    jurisdiction or by a national of a Contracting State.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The public funds are not to be used to compensate the
  damage that is referred to in paragraph (1)(c) if the damage that is
  suffered occurs in the territorial sea of a non-Contracting State.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preventive measure --- liability

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The public funds may also be used to compensate the
  damage that is caused in one of the areas referred to in paragraph
  (1)(a) or (b) by a preventive measure that was taken under subsection
  20(1) or 21(1) in relation to the operator's nuclear installation or
  in relation to any transportation for which the operator is
  responsible.

- Meaning of [[national of a Contracting State]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (1), a [[national of a Contracting
  State]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any subdivision of the
  Contracting State and any entity that is established or incorporated
  in that State.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial contribution --- call for
public funds by Canada

- **[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If in
  the Minister's opinion a nuclear incident for which the Tribunal or
  any other Canadian court has jurisdiction will result, or is likely to
  result, in compensation for damage that exceeds the amount made
  available by Canada, under Article III.1(a) of the Convention, and
  public funds may be necessary to compensate the damages that are
  caused in one of the areas that are referred to in subsection 71(1),
  he or she must immediately give notice under Article VI of the
  Convention to all other Contracting States and, if in his or her
  opinion public funds are necessary to compensate the damage, he or she
  must make a call for public funds under Article VII.1 of the
  Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canada's contribution

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When the Minister makes a call for public funds, he
  or she must calculate the amount of public funds that are to be
  contributed by Canada, in accordance with the formula provided for by
  regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advances out of C.R.F.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the amount standing to the credit of the Nuclear
  Liability Account is insufficient for the purposes of subsection (2),
  an amount that is sufficient to meet the deficit is, with the Minister
  of Finance's approval, to be paid from the Consolidated Revenue Fund
  and credited to the Nuclear Liability Account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public funds credited to Nuclear
  Liability Account

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must have all public funds to be
  contributed by Canada and other Contracting States, as a result of a
  call for public funds, credited to the Nuclear Liability Account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment from Nuclear Liability Account

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} When an award is final or when a decision concerning
  an action for damage is final or not subject to an appeal, the public
  funds that are payable by the Minister to compensate the damages that
  are caused in one of the areas that are referred to in subsection
  71(1) are to be paid out of the Nuclear Liability Account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canada's financial contribution --- call
for public funds by other Contracting State

- **[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  Contracting State other than Canada makes a call for public funds
  under Article VII.1 of the Convention and if in the Minister's opinion
  the claims for compensation cannot be satisfied out of the amount that
  the Installation State has made available in accord­ance with Article
  III.1(a) of the Convention, the Minister must, without delay, cause
  public funds to be paid by Canada to that Contracting State that are
  calculated in accordance with the formula provided for by regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advances out of C.R.F.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the amount standing to the credit of the Nuclear
  Liability Account is insufficient for the purposes of subsection (1),
  an amount that is sufficient to meet the deficit is, with the Minister
  of Finance's approval, to be paid from the Consolidated Revenue Fund
  and credited to the Nuclear Liability Account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment from Nuclear Liability Account

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any public funds that are payable are to be paid by
  the Minister out of the Nuclear Liability Account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reimbursement

**[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** Members of the nuclear
industry who are prescribed by regulations must reimburse the Minister,
in the prescribed manner and by the prescribed proportion, for any
public funds that were contributed by Canada under section 72 or 73, in
accordance with the prescribed formula, within the fiscal year in which
the payments are made. The amounts received by the Minister are to be
credited to the Nuclear Liability Account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recognition of settlements ---
Contracting State other than Canada

**[[75]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-75 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must
recognize a settlement by a Contracting State other than Canada that is
made in accordance with the laws of that Contracting State and that is,
in respect of the payment out of public funds, for compensation for the
damage to which the Convention applies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subrogation --- contribution by Canada

- **[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  public funds that were contributed by Canada under section 72 have
  been paid by the Minister, the Attorney General of Canada may exercise
  an operator's right of recourse under section 13.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subrogation --- contribution by
  Contracting State other than Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If public funds were contributed by a Contracting
  State other than Canada under Article VII.2 of the Convention, that
  Contracting State may exercise an operator's right of recourse under
  section 13.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subrogation --- request of Contracting
  State other than Canada

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Attorney General of Canada may, at the request of
  a Contracting State other than Canada that contributed public funds
  under Article VII.2 of the Convention, exercise an operator's right of
  recourse under section 13 on that Contracting State's behalf.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Denial of request --- subrogation by
  Contracting State other than Canada

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, despite the request referred to in subsection
  (3), the Attorney General of Canada does not exercise an operator's
  right of recourse under section 13 within three months after that
  request, the Contracting State may exercise that right.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution of public funds

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must, within a reasonable time,
  distribute any public funds recovered under subsection (3) to the
  Contracting States in proportion to the public funds that they
  contributed.

## [Offence and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368665 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to maintain financial security

- **[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  operator who contravenes subsection 27(1) or who does not hold
  financial security in the form and manner required by section 28
  commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of
  not more than \$300,000 for each day on which the offence is committed
  or continued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Due diligence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No operator is to be found guilty of the offence if
  it is established that the operator exercised due diligence to prevent
  its commission.

### Undertaking {#undertaking .SchedHeadL2}

  specified to the Depositary at any time prior to the nuclear incident,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a Contracting Party may establish for the maximum

    Convention, a transitional amount of at least 150 million SDRs in
    respect of a nuclear incident occurring within that period.

### Article VI Notification of Nuclear Damage {#article-vi-notification-of-nuclear-damage .SchedHeadL2}

Without prejudice to obligations which Contracting Parties may have
under other international agreements, the Contracting Party whose courts
have jurisdiction shall inform the other Contracting Parties of a
nuclear incident as soon as it appears that the damage caused by such
incident exceeds, or is likely to exceed, the amount available under
Article 111.1 (a) and that contributions under Article 111.1 (b) may be
required. The Contracting Parties shall without delay make all the
necessary arrangements to settle the procedure for their relations in
this connection.

### Article VII Call for Funds {#article-vii-call-for-funds .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} Following the notification referred to in Article VI,
  and subject to Article X.3, the Contracting Party whose courts have
  jurisdiction shall request the other Contracting Parties to make
  available the public funds required under Article 111.1 (b) to the
  extent and when they are actually required and shall have exclusive
  competence to disburse such funds.

- [2]{.lawlabel} Independently of existing or future regulations
  concerning currency or transfers, Contracting Parties shall authorize
  the transfer and payment of any contribution provided pursuant to
  Article III.1 (b) without any restriction.

### Article VIII List of Nuclear Installations {#article-viii-list-of-nuclear-installations .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} Each Contracting State shall, at the time when it
  deposits its instrument of ratification, acceptance, approval or
  accession, communicate to the Depositary a complete listing of all
  nuclear installations referred to in Article IV.3. The listing shall
  contain the necessary particulars for the purpose of the calculation
  of contributions.

- [2]{.lawlabel} Each Contracting State shall promptly communicate to
  the Depositary all modifications to be made to the list. Where such
  modifications include the addition of a nuclear installation, the
  communication must be made at least three months before the expected
  date when nuclear material will be introduced into the installation.

- [3]{.lawlabel} If a Contracting Party is of the opinion that the
  particulars, or any modification to be made to the list communicated
  by a Contracting State pursuant to paragraphs 1 and 2, do not comply
  with the provisions, it may raise objections thereto by addressing
  them to the Depositary within three months from the date on which it
  has received notice pursuant to paragraph 5. The Depositary shall
  forthwith communicate this objection to the State to whose information
  the objection has been raised. Any unresolved differences shall be
  dealt with in accordance with the dispute settlement procedure laid
  down in Article XVI.

- [4]{.lawlabel} The Depositary shall maintain, update and annually
  circulate to all Contracting States the list of nuclear installations
  established in accordance with this Article. Such list shall consist
  of all the particulars and modifications referred to in this Article,
  it being understood that objections submitted under this Article shall
  have effect retrospective to the date on which they were raised, if
  they are sustained.

- [5]{.lawlabel} The Depositary shall give notice as soon as possible to
  each Contracting Party of the communications and objections which it
  has received pursuant to this Article.

\...

### Article XVIII Ratification, Acceptance, Approval {#article-xviii-ratification-acceptance-approval .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} This Convention shall be subject to ratification,
  acceptance or approval by the signatory States. An instrument of
  ratification, acceptance or approval shall be accepted only from a
  State which is a Party to either the Vienna Convention or the Paris
  Convention, or a State which declares that its national law complies
  with the provisions of the Annex to this Convention, provided that, in
  the case of a State having on its territory a nuclear installation as
  defined in the Convention on Nuclear Safety of 17 June 1994, it is a
  Contracting State to that Convention.

- [2]{.lawlabel} The instruments of ratification, acceptance or approval
  shall be deposited with the Director General of the International
  Atomic Energy Agency who shall act as the Depositary of this
  Convention.

- [3]{.lawlabel} A Contracting Party shall provide the Depositary with a
  copy, in one of the official languages of the United Nations, of the
  provisions of its national law referred to in Article II.1 and
  amendments thereto, including any specification made pursuant to
  Article III. I (a), Article XI.2, or a transitional amount pursuant to
  Article III.1 (a)(ii). Copies of such provisions shall be circulated
  by the Depositary to all other Contracting Parties.

### Article XIX Accession  {#article-xix-accession .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} After its entry into force, any State which has not
  signed this Convention may accede to it. An instrument of accession
  shall be accepted only from a State which is a Party to either the
  Vienna Convention or the Paris Convention, or a State which declares
  that its national law complies with the provisions of the Annex to
  this Convention, provided that, in the case of a State having on its
  territory a nuclear installation as defined in the Convention on
  Nuclear Safety of 17 June 1994, it is a Contracting State to that
  Convention.

- [2]{.lawlabel} The instruments of accession shall be deposited with
  the Director General of the International Atomic Energy Agency.

- [3]{.lawlabel} A Contracting Party shall provide the Depositary with a
  copy, in one of the official languages of the United Nations, of the
  provisions of its national law referred to in Article II.1 and
  amendments thereto, including any specification made pursuant to
  Article III.1 (a), Article XI.2, or a transitional amount pursuant to
  Article III.1 (a)(ii). Copies of such provisions shall be circulated
  by the Depositary to all other Contracting Parties.

- [1]{.lawlabel} In addition to the definitions in Article I of this

  Annex:
