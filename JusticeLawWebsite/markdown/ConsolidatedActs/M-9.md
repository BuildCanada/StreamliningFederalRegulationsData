## Motor Vehicle Fuel Consumption Standards Act {#motor-vehicle-fuel-consumption-standards-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. M-9
:::

An Act respecting motor vehicle fuel consumption standards
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358633 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Motor Vehicle Fuel Consumption Standards Act](/eng/acts/M-9).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358638 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} engaged in the business of manufacturing motor
      vehicles in Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} engaged in the business of importing motor
      vehicles into Canada, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} engaged in the business of selling to other
      persons, principally for the purpose of resale, motor vehicles
      obtained directly from a person described in paragraph (a) or his
      agent; ([compagnie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[company average fuel consumption]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[company average fuel consumption]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in
    relation to a given company, the average fuel consumption of all
    motor vehicles of a prescribed class, calculated in accordance with
    section 10; ([moyenne de consommation de carburant d'une
    compagnie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means gasoline, diesel oil or any other
    combustible matter and includes any other prescribed form of
    energy; ([carburant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fuel consumption]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fuel consumption]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the quantity of fuel
    used by a motor vehicle when driven a given distance; ([consommation
    de carburant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fuel consumption number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fuel consumption number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a number that
    represents the fuel consumption of a motor vehicle under controlled
    test conditions; ([cote de consommation de
    carburant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fuel consumption standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fuel consumption standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a standard
    prescribed pursuant to section 3; ([norme de consommation de
    carburant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated as an
    inspector pursuant to subsection
    23(1); ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Transport; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[motor vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[motor vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any vehicle designed to
    be driven or drawn on roads by any means other than exclusively by
    muscular power, and includes pedal cycles with auxiliary motors,
    minibikes and motorized snow vehicles, but does not include any
    vehicle designed for running exclusively on rails; ([véhicule
    automobile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by
    regulation; ([Version anglaise seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a calendar
    year. ([année]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 2
:::

## [Fuel Consumption Standards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358657 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fuel consumption standards

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, on the recommendation of the Minister and the Minister of Natural
Resources, make regulations prescribing, for the purposes of section 11,
a fuel consumption standard for any prescribed class of motor vehicle
for any year.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. M-9, s. 3
- 1994, c. 41, s. 37
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of proposed regulations

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a copy of each regulation that the Governor in
  Council proposes to make under section 3 or 37 shall be published in
  the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) at least ninety days
  before the proposed effective date thereof, and a reasonable
  opportunity within those ninety days shall be afforded to companies
  and other interested persons to make representations to the Minister
  with respect thereto.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a
  proposed regulation that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has previously been published pursuant to that
    subsection, whether or not it has been changed as a result of
    representations made pursuant to that subsection; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} makes no substantive change to an existing
    regulation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity of fuel consumption standard

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A fuel consumption standard
prescribed under section 3 for a particular year is not valid unless one
of the following conditions is met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fuel consumption standard has been published in
  the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) before the end of the
  third year preceding that year;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fuel consumption standard is no more stringent
  than the fuel consumption standard applicable to the preceding year;
  or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} no objections by a company were received by the
  Minister during the ninety day period following publication of the
  proposed fuel consumption standard under subsection 4(1).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 5
:::

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358678 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interprovincial trade and imports

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  company shall, for the purpose of sale,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ship a motor vehicle of a prescribed class from the
    province in which it was manufactured to another province, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} import into Canada a motor vehicle of a prescribed
    class

  unless

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a fuel consumption number in respect of that motor
    vehicle is registered pursuant to section 7,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a label setting out the prescribed information
    relating to fuel consumption is affixed to the motor vehicle in the
    prescribed manner, and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the motor vehicle is, according to its
    manufacturer's specifications and according to the prescribed rules,
    substantially similar to the motor vehicle or vehicles used to
    establish the registered fuel consumption number.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving provision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No contravention of subsection (1) shall be deemed to
  be committed by a company if the requirements of paragraphs (1)(c)
  to (e) are met before the motor vehicle leaves the possession of that
  company or its consignee.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 6
:::

## [Registration of Fuel Consumption Numbers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358692 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for registration

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  company has established a fuel consumption number in accordance with
  the prescribed procedure, it may submit an application to the
  Minister, in prescribed form and containing the prescribed
  information, requesting registration of that fuel consumption number
  in respect of the motor vehicles described in the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed procedure must be used

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company shall not submit an application under
  subsection (1) where the fuel consumption number was not established
  in accordance with the prescribed procedure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister to register fuel consumption
  number

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister receives a duly completed
  application under subsection (1), he shall forthwith register the fuel
  consumption number set out in the application.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition orders re advertising

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by order, prohibit the use by any company of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a registered fuel consumption number, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fuel consumption information related to that
    registered fuel consumption number

  in advertising or other representations to the public relating to a
  motor vehicle if the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that the motor vehicle is not, according to its
    manufacturer's specifications and according to the prescribed rules,
    substantially similar to the motor vehicle or vehicles used to
    establish the registered fuel consumption number,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that the application for registration of the fuel
    consumption number contained false or misleading information, or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} that the fuel consumption number was not
    established in accordance with the prescribed procedure,

  and that, as a result, the registered fuel consumption number does not
  correctly represent the fuel consumption of that motor vehicle.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When order takes effect

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An order under subsection (1) takes effect sixty days
  after the order is made or on such later date as is fixed by the
  order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revised fuel consumption number

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister has made an order under subsection
  (1), the company that had obtained that registered fuel consumption
  number under section 7 may apply to the Minister for registration of a
  revised fuel consumption number, and subsections 7(1) and (2) apply to
  such an application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's discretion

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a company applies under subsection (3) for
  registration of a revised fuel consumption number, the Minister is not
  required to register that number if he has reasonable grounds to
  believe that it does not correctly represent the fuel consumption of
  the motor vehicle.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where order deemed not to be
  contravened

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In any prosecution under paragraph 30(1)(b) for
  contravention of an order made under subsection (1), a company shall
  be deemed not to have contravened the order if it establishes that it
  took all reasonable measures

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to withdraw the offending advertising or
    representations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to substitute new advertising or representations,
    with similar distribution, using a registered revised fuel
    consumption number obtained pursuant to this section.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 8
:::

## [Calculation of Company Average Fuel Consumption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358724 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Company's annual report to Minister

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  company shall, before the prescribed time, submit to the Minister a
  report with respect to each year, in prescribed form, setting out, by
  registered fuel consumption number, with respect to all motor vehicles
  to which registered fuel consumption numbers apply, the aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of those motor vehicles that it has
    manufactured in Canada in that year for the purpose of sale in a
    province other than the province of manufacture,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of those motor vehicles that it has
    imported into Canada in that year for the purpose of sale in Canada,
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with subsection 19(2), the number of
    those motor vehicles that it has manufactured in Canada in that year
    for the purpose of sale in Canada, other than motor vehicles
    described in paragraph (a), and to which has been applied a national
    fuel consumption mark described in section 17.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Year of manufacture or importation of
  motor vehicle

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a motor vehicle
  shall be deemed to have been manufactured or imported either in the
  year in which it was in fact manufactured or imported, as the case may
  be, or in the following year, at the option of the company.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of company average fuel
consumption

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For each prescribed class
of motor vehicle, the Minister shall calculate for each year, in litres
per one hundred kilometres, according to the prescribed method of
calculation, the company average fuel consumption for each company,
based on

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information contained in the annual report
  submitted by the company pursuant to section 9; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribed equivalence factors, in the case of motor
  vehicles that use fuels other than the prescribed reference gasoline.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 10
:::

## [Enforcement of Fuel Consumption Standards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358741 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Company average fuel consumption not to
exceed fuel consumption standard

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ships motor vehicles of a prescribed class from the
    province in which they were manufactured to another province,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} imports into Canada motor vehicles of a prescribed
    class,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} applies to motor vehicles of a prescribed class any
    national fuel consumption mark, or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} sells, offers for sale, has in possession for sale
    or delivers for sale motor vehicles of a prescribed class to which
    has been applied any national fuel consumption mark

  shall ensure that, for that class of motor vehicle, its company
  average fuel consumption for any particular year does not exceed the
  fuel consumption standard for that year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty where company exceeds fuel
  consumption standard

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 16, where, for any year, for a
  particular prescribed class of motor vehicle, the company average fuel
  consumption of a company exceeds the fuel consumption standard, the
  Minister shall issue an assessment against that company imposing,
  subject to subsection (3), a penalty equal to the product obtained by
  multiplying

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one dollar for every one-hundredth of a litre per
    one hundred kilometres by which the company average fuel consumption
    exceeds the fuel consumption standard

    by

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate number of motor vehicles set out in
    the report submitted pursuant to section 9.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Credits will reduce penalty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The penalty calculated under subsection (2) shall be
  reduced by an amount equal to the product obtained by multiplying

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one dollar for every one-hundredth of a litre per
    one hundred kilometres by which the company average fuel consumption
    for the same prescribed class of motor vehicle is lower than the
    fuel consumption standard

    by

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate number of motor vehicles set out in
    the report submitted pursuant to section 9

  in respect of

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any or all of the three years preceding the year
    for which the penalty is imposed, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the year immediately following the year for which
    the penalty is imposed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}A credit usable only once

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An amount used under subsection (3) to reduce a
  penalty is not available to reduce a subsequent penalty.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When penalty payable

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A penalty imposed under
section 11 is payable to the Receiver General one year after the notice
of assessment has been served on the company under subsection 13(1).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of assessment

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Minister issues an assessment against a company under section 11,
  he shall forthwith serve a notice of the assessment on the company and
  cause notice of the assessment to be published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objection to assessment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company that objects to an assessment under section
  11 may, within thirty days from the day on which it is served with the
  notice of assessment, serve on the Minister a notice of objection, in
  prescribed form, setting out the reasons for objecting to the
  assessment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reconsideration of assessment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a notice of objection under subsection
  (2), the Minister shall forthwith

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} reconsider the assessment objected to;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} confirm, cancel or vary the assessment; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} serve a copy of his decision on the company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Considerations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister's decision under subsection (3) to
  confirm, cancel or vary an assessment shall be based solely on the
  following criteria:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the information on which the original
    assessment was based was correct; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether this Act and the regulations were correctly
    applied to that information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Methods of service

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Documents required to be served under this section
  may be served personally or by registered mail, and, if served by
  registered mail, shall be deemed to have been served on the day of
  actual receipt.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 13
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of appeal

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Minister confirms or varies an assessment, the company may, within
  thirty days of being served with the Minister's decision, appeal the
  Minister's decision to the Federal Court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Institution of appeal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An appeal to the Federal Court under subsection (1)
  shall be instituted in the manner set forth in section 48 of the
  [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal of appeal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Federal Court shall dispose of an appeal under
  this section by confirming, cancelling or varying the assessment, and
  shall base its decision solely on the criteria set out in paragraphs
  13(4)(a) and (b).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. M-9, s. 14
- 2002, c. 8, ss. 182, 183
:::

## [Exemption from Fuel Consumption Standard]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358802 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications for exemption from fuel
consumption standard

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  application by a company in the prescribed form and containing the
  prescribed information, the Governor in Council may, by order, in
  respect of up to three years,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exempt from a fuel consumption standard up to one
    thousand motor vehicles per year manufactured by a manufacturer
    whose world production was less than ten thousand motor vehicles in
    the second year preceding the year in respect of which the
    application for exemption is made, if the Governor in Council is of
    the opinion that it would not be desirable to have the fuel
    consumption standard apply to those motor vehicles; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} exempt from a fuel consumption standard any or all
    vehicles manufactured or imported by a company, where the Governor
    in Council is of the opinion that compliance with the fuel
    consumption standard would

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} create substantial financial hardship for the
      company, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} prevent the development of new kinds of motor
      vehicles.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New fuel consumption standard may be
  imposed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by order, in respect of
  a company that has been granted an exemption under subsection (1),
  impose a new fuel consumption standard applicable to the motor
  vehicles exempted under that subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of exemption

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where all the motor vehicles manufactured or imported
  by a company are exempted under paragraph (1)(a) or (b) and no new
  fuel consumption standard is imposed under subsection (2), sections 11
  to 15 do not apply in respect of that company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where all the motor vehicles manufactured or imported
  by a company are exempted under paragraph (1)(a) or (b) and a new fuel
  consumption standard is imposed under subsection (2), sections 11 to
  15 apply in respect of that company, but a reference in subsection
  11(1) or (2) to "fuel consumption standard" shall be read as a
  reference to the new fuel consumption standard imposed under
  subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where some but not all of the motor vehicles
  manufactured or imported by a company are exempted under paragraph
  (1)(a) or (b), then

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall calculate, in respect of that
    company, in the same manner as he would calculate the company
    average fuel consumption,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a partial company average fuel consumption, based
      on the motor vehicles other than those exempted, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where applicable, a second partial company
      average fuel consumption, based on the exempted vehicles for which
      a new fuel consumption standard has been imposed under subsection
      (2);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a penalty under subsection 11(2) shall be
    calculated on the basis of the partial company average fuel
    consumption described in subparagraph (a)(i); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where a new fuel consumption standard has been
    imposed under subsection (2) and the partial company average fuel
    consumption described in subparagraph (a)(ii) exceeds that new fuel
    consumption standard,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall issue an assessment against
      the company imposing a penalty, subject to subsection (6), equal
      to the product obtained by multiplying

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} one dollar for every one-hundredth of a litre
        per one hundred kilometres by which that partial company average
        fuel consumption exceeds the new fuel consumption standard

      by

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate number of motor vehicles to which
        the new fuel consumption standard applies, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} subsections 11(3) and (4) and sections 12 to 15
      apply, with such modifications as the circumstances require, in
      respect of a penalty imposed under subparagraph (i).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum penalty

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A penalty imposed under subparagraph (5)(c)(i) shall
  not exceed the penalty that would have been imposed had no exemption
  been granted under subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 16
:::

## [National Fuel Consumption Marks]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358832 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National fuel consumption marks

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The words "Canada Motor
Vehicle Fuel Consumption Standard" and "[Normes de consommation de
carburant des véhicules automobiles du Canada]{.otherLang lang="fr"}",
and any abbreviations thereof, shall be national trademarks and, except
as provided in this Act, the exclusive property in and right to the use
of those marks, referred to in sections 18, 19 and 20 as "national fuel
consumption marks", is hereby declared to be vested in Her Majesty in
right of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. M-9, s. 17
- [2014, c. 20, s. 366(E)](#1034903-1145729){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1034903-1145729.html"
  role="button"}
:::

Version](section-17-20030101.html "Link to previous version of section 17"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** No person shall use any
national fuel consumption mark except in accordance with section 19 and
the regulations thereunder.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 18
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for use of national fuel
consumption marks

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} apply to a motor vehicle of a prescribed class any
    national fuel consumption mark, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sell, offer for sale, have in possession for sale
    or deliver for sale a motor vehicle of a prescribed class to which
    has been applied any national fuel consumption mark

  unless

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a fuel consumption number in respect of that motor
    vehicle is registered pursuant to section 7,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a label setting out the prescribed information
    relating to fuel consumption is affixed to the motor vehicle in the
    prescribed manner,

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the motor vehicle is, according to its
    manufacturer's specifications and according to the prescribed rules,
    substantially similar to the motor vehicle or vehicles used to
    establish the registered fuel consumption number, and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the national fuel consumption mark is in the
    prescribed form and is applied to the motor vehicle in the
    prescribed manner and at the prescribed place on the vehicle.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is a further condition of the use of any national
  fuel consumption mark that, where a national fuel consumption mark has
  been applied to a motor vehicle of a prescribed class, other than a
  motor vehicle exported from Canada, that motor vehicle shall be
  included in the aggregate set out in the report under section 9 and
  shall be taken into account for purposes of the calculation of the
  company average fuel consumption under section 10 and any assessment
  of a penalty under section 11.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 19
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of similar marks prohibited

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** No person shall use a
mark or designation so closely resembling a national fuel consumption
mark as to be likely to be mistaken therefor.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 20
:::

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358859 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Company to keep records

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company shall maintain records in prescribed form containing the
  prescribed information relating to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the procedure by which it established each
    registered fuel consumption number;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} details of the manufacture of each motor vehicle to
    which a registered fuel consumption number applies;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} applications made to the Minister for registration
    of each fuel consumption number;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} applications made to the Governor in Council for
    exemptions under section 16;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} interim statistics and forecasts of the information
    to be contained in the annual reports to the Minister under section
    9; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the company's annual reports to the Minister under
    section 9.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may examine records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When the Minister so requests, a company shall
  provide him forthwith with any information contained in the records
  described in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records to be kept five years

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company shall keep the records described in
  subsection (1) for a period of five years from the end of the year to
  which such records relate.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 21
:::

## [Test Vehicles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358875 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may examine test vehicle or
component

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may request a company to make available, at such place as the
  Minister requests, a motor vehicle or any component thereof that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} was used in tests conducted to establish a
    registered fuel consumption number, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is representative of a motor vehicle or component
    that was used in tests conducted to establish a registered fuel
    consumption number,

  and the company shall forthwith comply with the Minister's request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may dismantle and examine
  motor vehicle or component

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a company makes available to the Minister a
  motor vehicle or a component thereof pursuant to subsection (1), the
  Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dismantle and examine the motor vehicle or the
    component; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} conduct all necessary tests to verify the accuracy
    of tests conducted by the company to establish the fuel consumption
    number.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister to return motor vehicle or
  component

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A motor vehicle or component made available to the
  Minister under this section shall not be detained by the Minister
  after the expiration of thirty days after the completion of the
  examination and tests referred to in subsection (2) unless, before
  that time, proceedings have been instituted in respect of an offence
  under this Act, in which case the motor vehicle or component may be
  detained until the proceedings are finally concluded.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 22
:::

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358890 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspectors

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate as an inspector for the purposes of this Act
  any person who, in his opinion, is qualified to be so designated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate to be produced

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall furnish every inspector with a
  certificate of his designation as an inspector and on entering any
  place described in subsection 24(1) an inspector shall, if so
  required, produce the certificate to the person in charge of that
  place.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 23
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of inspectors

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector may at any reasonable time enter any place in which he
  believes on reasonable grounds that there is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a motor vehicle of a class for which a fuel
    consumption standard has been prescribed and that is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} owned by, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} situated on the premises of

    a company or a consignee of imported motor vehicles,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a component of a motor vehicle of a class for which
    a fuel consumption standard has been prescribed, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any record described in section 21,

  and may

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} examine any motor vehicle, any component of a motor
    vehicle or any records found in that place,

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} open and examine any package found therein that he
    believes on reasonable grounds contains any component of a motor
    vehicle or any records, and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} require any person to produce for inspection any
    books, reports, test data, control records, shipping bills, bills of
    lading or other documents or papers that he believes on reasonable
    grounds contain any information relevant to the administration or
    enforcement of this Act, and make copies thereof or extracts
    therefrom.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to inspectors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner or person in charge of a place entered by
  an inspector pursuant to subsection (1) and every person found therein
  shall give the inspector all reasonable assistance to enable the
  inspector to carry out his duties under this Act, and shall furnish
  the inspector with any information he may reasonably require with
  respect to the administration or enforcement of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 24
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction of inspectors

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall obstruct or hinder an inspector engaged in carrying out
  his duties under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False statements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall knowingly make any false or
  misleading statement, either orally or in writing, to an inspector
  engaged in carrying out his duties under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 25
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358922 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure is not warranty

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** A disclosure relating to
fuel consumption that is required to be made by a company under this Act
in respect of tests conducted in accordance with this Act does not
create an express or implied warranty by anyone that the fuel
consumption established by those tests will be achieved under conditions
of actual use.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 26
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privileged information

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as provided in this section, information obtained by the Minister
  under this Act or by the Minister of Natural Resources under
  subsection (2) is privileged and shall not knowingly be or be
  permitted to be communicated, disclosed or made available without the
  written consent of the person from whom it was obtained.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Information obtained under this Act may be
  communicated, disclosed or made available for the purposes of the
  administration or enforcement of this Act, legal proceedings related
  thereto or criminal proceedings under an Act of Parliament, and may be
  communicated, disclosed or made available to the Minister of Natural
  Resources.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may disclose certain
  information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may disclose

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} registered fuel consumption numbers or fuel
    consumption numbers derived therefrom;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} descriptions of the motor vehicles to which any
    number referred to in paragraph (a) relates; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the company average fuel consumption of any
    company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of other information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the right to disclose information
  described in subsection (3), the Minister may, where in his opinion it
  is in the public interest and will not unduly impair a company's
  competitive position, disclose any information obtained under this
  Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification and opportunity to make
  representations

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister proposes to disclose information
  pursuant to subsection (4) in a form that identifies or permits the
  identification of the company to which the information relates, he
  shall so notify the company and afford it a reasonable opportunity to
  make representations respecting the effect that the disclosure of the
  information might have on the company's competitive position.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. M-9, s. 27
- 1994, c. 41, s. 37
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidentiary privilege

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding any other
Act or law, no person who obtains information under this Act shall be
required, in connection with any legal proceedings, other than
proceedings relating to the administration or enforcement of this Act or
criminal proceedings under an Act of Parliament, to give evidence
relating to any information that is privileged under this Act or to
produce any statement, document, writing or portion thereof containing
any such information.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 28
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Research and equipment

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} conduct such research, studies and evaluations as he
  deems necessary for the administration or enforcement of this Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} purchase or lease such equipment as he deems
  necessary in order to carry out tests under this Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have tests related to this Act carried out by any
  person; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} lease or loan equipment owned by Her Majesty to any
  person, for periods not exceeding twelve months, for testing or
  research related to this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. M-9, s. 29
- R.S., 1985, c. 1 (4th Supp.), s. 45(F)
:::

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358955 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences and punishment

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contravenes or fails to comply with any provision
    of this Act, other than subsection 11(1) or 27(1), or the
    regulations,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contravenes an order made by the Minister under
    section 8,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fails to comply with an order of the court under
    subsection 36(1), or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} knowingly makes a false or misleading statement in
    any report or application under this Act

  is guilty of an offence and liable

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding one
    hundred thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding
    one year or to both, or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine not
    exceeding one million dollars or to imprisonment for a term not
    exceeding five years or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offences

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an offence under subsection (1) is committed on
  more than one day or is continued for more than one day, it shall be
  deemed to be a separate offence for each day on which the offence is
  committed or continued.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 30
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence by employee or agent

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** In any prosecution for an
offence under subsection 30(1), it is sufficient proof of the offence to
establish that it was committed by an employee or agent of the accused
whether or not the employee or agent is identified or has been
prosecuted for the offence, unless the accused establishes that the
offence was committed without his knowledge or consent and that he
exercised all due diligence to prevent its commission.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 31
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers, etc., of corporations

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** Where a corporation
commits an offence under subsection 30(1), any officer, director or
agent of the corporation who directed, authorized, assented to,
acquiesced in or participated in the commission of the offence is a
party to and guilty of the offence and is liable on conviction to the
punishment provided for the offence whether or not the corporation has
been prosecuted or convicted.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 32
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of subsection 27(1)

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes subsection 27(1) is guilty of an offence punishable on
summary conviction.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 33
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** Any proceedings by way of
summary conviction in respect of an offence under this Act may be
instituted at any time within but not later than two years after the
time when the subject-matter of the proceedings arose.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 34
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Venue

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** A complaint or
information in respect of an offence under this Act may be heard, tried
or determined by a court if the accused is resident or carrying on
business within the territorial jurisdiction of that court, although the
matter of the complaint or information did not arise in that territorial
jurisdiction.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 113, s. 35
:::

## [Correction of Defects]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358990 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Company to give notice of defects
affecting fuel consumption

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a company has been convicted of an offence for contravening section 6
  or subsection 19(1) on the basis of paragraph 6(1)(e) or 19(1)(e), as
  the case may be, and the convicting court is of the opinion that the
  dissimilarity was in the nature of a defect the result of which is
  that the registered fuel consumption number does not correctly
  represent the fuel consumption of that motor vehicle, the court may
  order the company to give notice forthwith, in the prescribed manner,
  containing a statement of the means to be taken to correct the defect
  at the company's expense, to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person who has obtained that motor vehicle from
    the company for the purpose of sale or resale;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the current owner of that motor vehicle as
    determined

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} from any warranty by the manufacturer,
      distributor or importer of that motor vehicle with respect to the
      functioning of that motor vehicle that has, to the company's
      knowledge, been given, sold or transferred to the current owner,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} from provincial motor vehicle registration
      records; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Company to correct defects at its own
  expense

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the court makes an order against a company
  under subsection (1), the company shall, forthwith after giving notice
  pursuant to that order, correct the defect at its own expense in
  respect of motor vehicles described in the order

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with its usual warranty procedures;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in such manner as the Minister may direct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Two year limitation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company's obligation under subsection (2) does not
  extend to a motor vehicle presented to the company for correction of
  the defect more than two years after the company gave notice in
  respect of that motor vehicle pursuant to the court order made under
  subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice by publication in newspapers

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where it is made to appear to the satisfaction of the
  Minister that the name of the current owner of a motor vehicle cannot
  reasonably be determined in the manner provided under paragraph
  (1)(b),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister may order notice to be given by
    publication in the prescribed form for a period of five consecutive
    days in two major daily newspapers in each of the six regions of
    Canada, namely, the Atlantic provinces, Quebec, Ontario, the Prairie
    provinces, British Columbia, and the three territories, or by an
    alternative medium for any period that the Minister deems expedient,
    and the notice is deemed to be notice given in the manner prescribed
    for the purpose of subsection (1); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister may, in his discretion, order that the
    current owner need not be notified and that the obligation to notify
    the current owner of any defect under subsection (1) has been
    discharged.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quarterly reports to be submitted

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every company that gives a notice mentioned in
  subsection (1) to the Minister shall submit to the Minister, in the
  prescribed form and manner, quarterly reports containing prescribed
  information relating to the defect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Unless the Minister otherwise directs, the quarterly
  reports referred to in subsection (5) shall be submitted to the
  Minister for a period of two years from the date of the notice
  mentioned in subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. M-9, s. 36
- 1993, c. 28, s. 78
- 2002, c. 7, s. 208
:::
