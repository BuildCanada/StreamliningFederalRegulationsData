## Public Safety Act, 2002 {#public-safety-act-2002 .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2004, c. 15
:::

Assented to 2004-05-06

An Act to amend certain Acts of Canada, and to enact measures for
implementing the Biological and Toxin Weapons Convention, in order to
enhance public safety
::::

:::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::
