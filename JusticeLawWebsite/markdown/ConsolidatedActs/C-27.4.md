## Celebrating Canada's Seniors Act {#celebrating-canadas-seniors-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2010, c. 13
:::

Assented to 2010-11-18

An Act to establish National Seniors Day
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas Parliament wishes to acknowledge the contribution of seniors to
Canadian society and to the Canadian economy;

Whereas seniors contribute in many ways, including by sharing their
experiences, expertise and knowledge with other generations;

Whereas the contribution of seniors is invaluable and ongoing;

And whereas October 1 is recognized as the International Day of Older
Persons;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-79868 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Celebrating Canada's Seniors Act](/eng/acts/C-27.4).

## [National Seniors Day]{.HTitleText1} {#h-79871 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Throughout Canada, in each
year, October 1 is to be known as National Seniors Day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not holiday or non-juridical day

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
National Seniors Day is not a holiday or a non-juridical day.
