## Coasting Trade Act {#coasting-trade-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1992, c. 31
:::

Assented to 1992-06-23

An Act respecting the use of foreign ships and non-duty paid ships in
the coasting trade
::::

::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-86288 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Coasting Trade Act](/eng/acts/C-33.3).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-86291 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Transportation
      Agency; ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[British entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[British entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that is incorporated in the
        territory of the United Kingdom, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a trust, partnership, joint venture or other
        association that is formed in the territory of the United
        Kingdom; ([entité britannique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that is incorporated in Canada,
        or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a trust, partnership, joint venture or other
        association that is formed in Canada; ([entité
        canadienne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canadian vessel, as
      defined in section 2 of the [Canada Shipping Act,
      2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15), in respect of which all duties and taxes
      imposed under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and the
      [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) have been paid; ([navire
      canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the inland waters
      within the meaning of section 2 of the [Customs
      Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), the internal waters of Canada and the
      territorial sea of Canada; ([eaux canadiennes]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[CETA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[CETA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the
      [Canada--European Union Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement
      Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-4.8); ([AÉCG]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[coasting trade]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[coasting trade]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the carriage of goods by ship, or by ship and
        any other mode of transport, from one place in Canada or above
        the continental shelf of Canada to any other place in Canada or
        above the continental shelf of Canada, either directly or by way
        of a place outside Canada, but, with respect to waters above the
        continental shelf of Canada, includes the carriage of goods only
        in relation to the exploration, exploitation or transportation
        of the mineral or non-living natural resources of the
        continental shelf of Canada,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (c), the carriage of
        passengers by ship from any place in Canada situated on a lake
        or river to the same place, or to any other place in Canada,
        either directly or by way of a place outside Canada,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the carriage of passengers by ship from any
        place situated on the St. Lawrence River northeast of the Saint
        Lambert lock or on the Fraser River west of the Mission Bridge

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the same place, without any call at any
          port outside Canada, other than one or more technical or
          emergency calls, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to any other place in Canada, other than as
          an in-transit call, either directly or by way of a place
          outside Canada,

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the carriage of passengers by ship from any
        place in Canada other than from a place to which paragraph (b)
        or (c) applies

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the same place, without any call at any
          port outside Canada, other than one or more technical or
          emergency calls, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to any other place in Canada, other than as
          an in-transit call, either directly or by way of a place
          outside Canada,

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the carriage of passengers by ship

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} from any place in Canada to any place above
          the continental shelf of Canada,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} from any place above the continental shelf
          of Canada to any place in Canada, or

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} from any place above the continental shelf
          of Canada to the same place or to any other place above the
          continental shelf of Canada

        where the carriage of the passengers is in relation to the
        exploration, exploitation or transportation of the mineral or
        non-living natural resources of the continental shelf of Canada,
        and

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the engaging, by ship, in any other marine
        activity of a commercial nature in Canadian waters and, with
        respect to waters above the continental shelf of Canada, in such
        other marine activities of a commercial nature that are in
        relation to the exploration, exploitation or transportation of
        the mineral or non-living natural resources of the continental
        shelf of Canada; ([cabotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[foreign ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[foreign ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a ship other than a

  [[in-transit call]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[in-transit call]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any call, other than

  [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a document, issued pursuant

  [[master]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [[technical call]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[technical call]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any call, other than

      technique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[territory of the European Union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[territory of the European Union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the

      with the conditions specified in those treaties; ([territoire de
      l'Union européenne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[territory of the United Kingdom]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[territory of the United Kingdom]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a

## [Regulation of Foreign Ships and Non-duty Paid Ships]{.HTitleText1} {#h-86338 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  foreign ship or non-duty paid ship shall, except in accordance with a
  licence, engage in the coasting trade.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of any
  foreign ship or non-duty paid ship that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} used as a fishing vessel, as defined by the
    [Coastal Fisheries Protection Act](/eng/acts/C-33), in any activity
    governed by that Act and that does not carry any goods or passengers
    other than goods or passengers incidental to any activity governed
    by that Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} engaged in any ocean research activity commissioned
    by the Department of Fisheries and Oceans;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} operated or sponsored by a foreign government that
    has sought and received the consent of the Minister of Foreign
    Affairs to conduct marine scientific research;

  - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} engaged in seismic activities in waters above the
    continental shelf of Canada that are in relation to the exploration
    for mineral or non-living natural resources of the continental shelf
    of Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} engaged in salvage operations, except where such
    operations are performed in Canadian waters; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} engaged, with the approval of a person designated
    as a pollution response officer under section 174.1 of the [Canada
    Shipping Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15), in activities related to a
    marine pollution emergency, or to a risk of a marine pollution
    emergency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repositioning of empty containers

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of
  carriage between one place in Canada and another, without
  consideration, of empty containers that are owned or leased by the
  ship's owner and of any ancillary equipment that is necessary to
  ensure the safety, security, containment and preservation of the goods
  that may be carried in those containers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dredging activities

  [(2.11)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of
  dredging activities --- other than dredging activities that are
  provided under an agreement with Her Majesty in right of Canada or
  with an entity that is listed either in Annex 19-1, as amended from
  time to time, of Chapter Nineteen of CETA or in Annex 19-1 of Chapter
  Nineteen of CETA as incorporated by reference in CUKTCA --- carried
  out by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a non-duty paid ship whose owner is a Canadian
    entity, an EU entity or a British entity; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a foreign ship whose owner is a Canadian entity, an
    EU entity or a British entity and that is registered in a register
    other than

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Register of Vessels, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a register referred to in paragraph (2.2)(a)
      or (b) or (2.21)(a) or (b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dredging activities --- EU registers

  [(2.2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of
  dredging activities --- other than dredging activities that are
  provided under an agreement with Her Majesty in right of Canada or
  with an entity that is listed in Annex 19-1, as amended from time to
  time, of Chapter Nineteen of CETA --- carried out by a foreign ship
  whose owner is a Canadian entity, an EU entity or an entity that is
  under Canadian or European control and that is registered in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the first, or domestic, register of a member state
    of the European Union; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a second, or international, register of a member
    state of the European Union.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dredging activities --- UK registers

  [(2.21)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of
  dredging activities --- other than dredging activities that are
  provided under an agreement with Her Majesty in right of Canada or
  with an entity that is listed in Annex 19-1 of Chapter Nineteen of
  CETA as incorporated by reference in CUKTCA --- carried out by a
  foreign ship whose owner is a Canadian entity, a British entity or an
  entity that is under Canadian or British control and that is
  registered in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the first, or domestic, register of the United
    Kingdom; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the second, or international, register of the
    United Kingdom, or the Gibraltar register.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Feeder services --- continual or single
  trip

  [(2.3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the carriage of goods by a ship that is described
    in paragraph (2.2)(a) or (2.21)(a), from the port of Halifax ---
    where the goods are loaded --- to the port of Montreal, or vice
    versa, if that carriage is one leg of the importation of the goods
    into Canada; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the carriage of goods by the ship from the port of
    Montreal --- where the goods are loaded --- to the port of Halifax,
    or vice versa, if that carriage is one leg of the exportation of the
    goods from Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Feeder services --- single trip

  [(2.4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2.5), subsection (1) does
  not apply in respect of the carriage, by a ship that is described in
  paragraph (2.2)(b) or (2.21)(b), of goods in a container from the port
  of Montreal to the port of Halifax, or vice versa, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that carriage is one leg of the importation of the
    goods into or exportation from Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the container is at least 6.1 metres in length and
    has an internal volume of at least 14 cubic metres, is designed for
    carrying goods more than once and by one or more modes of
    transportation, and does not have wheels or is not otherwise built
    for being driven or drawn.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent coasting trade subject to
  subsection (1)

  [(2.5)]{.lawlabel} Once a ship has completed a carriage of goods as
  described in subsection (2.4), subsection (1) applies to the ship in
  respect of any such subsequent carriage of goods by the ship, until it
  departs from the exclusive economic zone of Canada or from the
  [[inland waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1)
  of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), at a location where the inland
  waters are contiguous to the United States.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information

  [(2.6)]{.lawlabel} Before a ship engages, without a licence, in any
  activities referred to in subsections (2.11) to (2.4) and for which
  the owner of the ship intends to rely on an exemption under any one of
  those subsections, the owner shall provide information to an
  enforcement officer, in the form and manner specified by the Minister,
  establishing that the ship meets the applicable conditions under any
  of subsections (2.11) to (2.21).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance in cases of distress

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section precludes a foreign ship or a
  non-duty paid ship from rendering assistance to persons, ships or
  aircraft in danger or distress in Canadian waters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[United States Wreckers
  Act](/eng/acts/U-3)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of
  operations permitted by the [United States Wreckers
  Act](/eng/acts/U-3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}DEW Sites

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to any ship that is
  owned by the Government of the United States and used for the sole
  purpose of transporting goods of Canadian or United States origin
  owned by the Government of the United States to supply Distant Early
  Warning Sites.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other laws apply

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, any law of Canada that imposes
  safety or pollution prevention requirements applies to a foreign ship
  that is exempted from the application of subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2.2), an entity is
  under Canadian or European control

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a third party entity that is a
    corporation, if securities of the corporation to which are attached
    more than 50% of the votes that may be cast to elect directors of
    the corporation are directly or indirectly held, otherwise than
    through a subsidiary or by way of security only, by or for the
    benefit of any of, or any combination of, the following individuals:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian citizen,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a [[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
      as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee
      Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a national of a member state of the European
      Union; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a third party entity that is a
    trust, partnership, joint venture or other association, if an
    individual, or any combination of individuals, described in any of
    subparagraphs (a)(i) to (iii) holds, directly or indirectly, but not
    through a subsidiary, an interest in the trust, partnership, joint
    venture or other association that entitles the individual or
    combination of individuals to receive more than 50% of its profits
    or more than 50% of its assets on dissolution. 

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Third party entity

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (7), [[third party
  entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a corporation, other than an entity referred to in
    paragraph (a) of the definition of either [[Canadian
    entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or [[EU
    entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, that is not incorporated under the
    law of the United States; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a trust, partnership, joint venture or other
    association, other than an entity referred to in paragraph (b) of
    the definition of either [[Canadian entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
    or [[EU entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, that is not formed under the
    law of the United States.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control --- CUKTCA

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2.21), an entity is
  under Canadian or British control

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a third party entity that is a
    corporation, if securities of the corporation to which are attached
    more than 50% of the votes that may be cast to elect directors of
    the corporation are directly or indirectly held, otherwise than
    through a subsidiary or by way of security only, by or for the

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian citizen,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a [[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
      as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee
      Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a national of the United Kingdom; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a third party entity that is a
    trust, partnership, joint venture or other association, if an

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Third party entity --- CUKTCA

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (9), [[third party

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a corporation, other than an entity referred to in

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of licence: foreign ship

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 7, on application therefor by a person resident in Canada
  acting on behalf of a foreign ship, the Minister of Public Safety and
  Emergency Preparedness shall issue a licence in respect of the foreign
  ship, where the Minister is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Agency has determined that no Canadian ship or
    non-duty paid ship is suitable and available to provide the service
    or perform the activity described in the application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the activity described in the application
    entails the carriage of passengers by ship, the Agency has
    determined that an identical or similar adequate marine service is
    not available from any person operating one or more Canadian ships;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} arrangements have been made for the payment of the
    duties and taxes under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and
    the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) applicable to the foreign ship
    in relation to its temporary use in Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all certificates and documents relating to the
    foreign ship issued pursuant to shipping conventions to which Canada
    is a party are valid and in force; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the foreign ship meets all safety and pollution
    prevention requirements imposed by any law of Canada applicable to
    that foreign ship.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other laws apply

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the issuance of a licence
  pursuant to subsection (1) does not affect the application of any law
  of Canada that imposes safety or pollution prevention requirements in
  respect of ships.

## [Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-86465 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a ship contravenes subsection 3(1), the ship is guilty of an offence
  and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding fifty
  thousand dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed separate offence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an offence is committed by a ship under
  subsection (1) on more than one day or is continued by the ship for
  more than one day, it shall be deemed to be a separate offence for
  each day on which the offence is committed or continued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Trial of offences

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a ship is charged with an offence under section 13, any court in
  Canada that would have had jurisdiction in respect of the offence if
  that offence had been committed within the limits of the court's
  ordinary jurisdiction has jurisdiction to try the offence as if it had
  been committed in the court's jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service on ship and appearance at trial

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a ship is charged with an offence under section
  13, the summons in relation thereto may be served by leaving it with
  the master or any officer of the ship or by posting the summons on a
  conspicuous part of the ship, and the ship may appear by counsel or
  agent but, if it does not appear, a court may, on proof of service of
  the summons, proceed [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} to hold the
  trial.

## [Searches]{.HTitleText1} {#h-86476 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of enforcement officer

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an enforcement officer believes on reasonable grounds that a ship has
  contravened this Act, the enforcement officer may stop and board the
  ship and, with a warrant, search the ship and seize anything found in
  or on the ship that the enforcement officer believes on reasonable
  grounds will afford evidence with respect to any contravention of this
  Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where warrant not necessary

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An enforcement officer may carry out the powers under
  subsection (1) without a warrant if by reason of exigent circumstances
  it would not be practicable to obtain a warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} While carrying out any of the powers under this
  section, an enforcement officer may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} require the owner, master or any other person who
    may have possession of the official log book of the ship, or any
    other document or paper that may provide evidence of the
    contravention, to produce, for inspection or for the purposes of
    obtaining copies thereof or extracts therefrom, the log book or
    other document or paper; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require the master or any other person found on
    board the ship to give all reasonable assistance in the power of the
    master or other person, as the case may be, to enable the
    enforcement officer to carry out the enforcement officer's duties
    and functions under this Act.

## [Detention of Ship]{.HTitleText1} {#h-86486 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Detention of ships

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an enforcement officer believes on reasonable grounds that an offence
  under this Act has been committed by or in respect of a ship, the
  enforcement officer may make a detention order in respect of the ship.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Geographical application of section

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The power to make a detention order under this
  section may be exercised in Canadian waters and in waters above the
  continental shelf of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order to be in writing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A detention order made under subsection (1) shall be
  in writing and addressed to all persons who, at the place where the
  ship to which the order relates is or will be, are empowered to give a
  clearance in respect of the ship.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of detention order to be served
  on master

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a detention order under this section is made in
  respect of a ship, notice thereof shall be served on the master of the
  ship

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by delivering a copy thereof personally to the
    master; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if service cannot reasonably be effected in the
    manner provided in paragraph (a),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by leaving a copy thereof for the master on board
      the ship with the person who is, or appears to be, in command or
      charge of the ship, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by leaving a copy thereof with the owner or
      agent of the owner of the ship residing in Canada or, where no
      such owner or agent is known or can be found, by fixing a copy
      thereof to a prominent part of the ship.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ship under detention not to depart

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where notice of a detention order in respect of a
  ship is served under subsection (4) and, during the term of the
  detention order, the master or owner of the ship gives an order for
  the ship to depart from Canadian waters or from waters above the
  continental shelf of Canada, the master or owner, as the case may be,
  is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not
  exceeding fifty thousand dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of persons empowered to give
  clearance

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (7), no person to whom a
  detention order made under subsection (1) is addressed shall, after
  notice of the order is received by that person, give clearance in
  respect of the ship to which the order relates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When clearance shall be given

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A person to whom a detention order made under
  subsection (1) is addressed and who has received notice of the order
  shall give clearance in respect of the ship to which the order relates
  where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} security satisfactory to the Minister of Transport
    in the amount of fifty thousand dollars is given to Her Majesty in
    right of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the ship has not been charged with an offence under
    this Act within thirty days after the making of the detention order;
    or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the ship has been charged with an offence under
    this Act within the period referred to in paragraph (b) and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} security satisfactory to the Minister of
      Transport for payment of the maximum fine that might be imposed as
      a result of a conviction of a ship charged with that offence, or
      in such lesser amount as may be approved by the Minister of
      Transport, is given to Her Majesty in right of Canada, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} proceedings in respect of the alleged offence
      that gave rise to the making of the detention order are
      discontinued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale of ship where no appearance and no
  security

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Where a ship has been charged with an offence under
  this Act within thirty days after the making of a detention order in
  respect thereof and, within thirty days after the day on which the
  ship was charged with the offence,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no one has appeared on behalf of the ship to answer
    to the charge, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} security referred to in paragraph (7)(c) has not
    been given,

  the Minister of Transport may apply to a court for an order
  authorizing the Minister of Transport to sell the ship.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale of ship where appearance but no
  security

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} Where a ship has been charged with an offence under
  this Act within thirty days after the making of a detention order in
  respect thereof and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within thirty days after the day on which the ship
    was charged with the offence, someone has appeared on behalf of the
    ship to answer to the charge but security referred to in paragraph
    (7)(c) has not been given, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the ship is convicted and a fine is imposed but not
    paid forthwith,

  the Minister of Transport may apply to a court for an order
  authorizing the Minister of Transport to sell the ship.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} Forthwith on making an application under
  subsection (8) or (9), the Minister of Transport shall, by registered
  mail, give notice of the application to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person responsible for maintaining any register
    in which the ship is registered, listed or recorded;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder of any mortgage against the ship that is
    registered on the register referred to in paragraph (a); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the holder of any maritime lien against the ship to
    which the application relates, and the holder of any like interest,
    known to the Minister of Transport at the time of the making of the
    application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Day notice deemed to be given

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} A notice sent by registered mail under
  subsection (10) shall be deemed to have been given to the person to
  whom it was sent on the day on which an acknowledgement of receipt in
  respect thereof is received by the Minister of Transport.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court may relieve Minister of giving
  notice

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} Where the court seized of an application under
  subsection (8) or (9) is satisfied that it is appropriate to do so,
  the court may relieve the Minister of Transport of the obligation to
  give the notice referred to in subsection (10), or authorize the
  Minister of Transport to give the notice in such other manner as the
  court deems fit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claiming interest

  [(13)]{.lawlabel} Where an application is made under subsection (8)
  or (9) in respect of a ship,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person referred to in paragraph (10)(b) or (c)
    may, within sixty days after the notice is given to that person, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other person who claims an interest in the ship
    as mortgagee, or as the holder of a maritime lien or as the holder
    of any like interest, may, within sixty days after the application
    is made,

  apply by notice in writing to the court seized of the application for
  an order referred to in subsection (14).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order

  [(14)]{.lawlabel} Where, on the hearing of an application made under
  subsection (13), it is made to appear to the satisfaction of the court

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the applicant acquired the interest in respect
    of which the applicant is applying in good faith prior to the
    commission of the offence in respect of which the ship was detained,
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the applicant is innocent of any complicity or
    collusion in respect of the offence in respect of which the ship was
    detained,

  the court shall grant to the applicant an order declaring the nature
  and extent of the applicant's interest at the time of the commission
  of the offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal

  [(15)]{.lawlabel} Any person who makes an application under subsection
  (13), and the Minister of Transport, may appeal to the court of appeal
  from an order referred to in subsection (14) and the appeal shall be
  asserted, heard and decided according to the ordinary procedure
  governing appeals to the court of appeal from orders or judgments of a
  court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications under subsection (13) to
  be heard first

  [(16)]{.lawlabel} A court shall not hear an application under
  subsection (8) or (9) until all applications under subsection (13) in
  relation thereto have been heard.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court may authorize sale

  [(17)]{.lawlabel} A court hearing an application under subsection (8)
  or (9) may authorize the Minister of Transport to sell the ship to
  which the application relates in such manner and subject to such terms
  and conditions as the court considers appropriate and, on application
  by the Minister of Transport, give directions to the Minister of
  Transport as to the rank of the interests of persons who have obtained
  an order referred to in subsection (14).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of proceeds

  [(18)]{.lawlabel} Where a ship is sold pursuant to this section, any
  surplus remaining from the proceeds of the sale after deducting

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the maximum fine that could have been imposed for
      the offence, where subsection (8) applies, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the fine actually imposed, where subsection (9)
      applies, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cost of the detention and the sale

  shall, to the extent of the surplus, be paid in accordance with the
  interests of persons who have obtained an order referred to in
  subsection (14), and any surplus remaining thereafter shall be paid to
  the person registered as the owner of the ship or, in the absence of
  registration, to the person who owns the ship.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clear title

  [(19)]{.lawlabel} On selling a ship pursuant to this section, the
  Minister of Transport may, by bill of sale, give the purchaser a valid
  title to the ship free from any mortgage or other claim on the ship
  that is in existence at the time of the sale.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registrability not implied

  [(20)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in subsection (19) shall be construed as
  meaning that the ship may be registered in the name of the purchaser.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not duty paid

  [(21)]{.lawlabel} No ship sold pursuant to this section shall be
  deemed to have been duty paid under the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) by reason only of that sale.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(22)]{.lawlabel} In this section,

## [Other Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-86572 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** No person shall obstruct
or hinder an enforcement officer in the carrying out of the enforcement
officer's duties and functions under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False statements

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** No person shall knowingly
make a false or misleading statement, either orally or in writing, as
the case may be,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in an application for a licence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the Agency pursuant to a request under section 9;
  or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to an enforcement officer while the enforcement
  officer is engaged in carrying out that enforcement officer's duties
  and functions under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summary conviction offence and punishment

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes section 17 or 18 is guilty of an offence punishable on
summary conviction and is liable to a fine of not more than fifteen
thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding twelve
months or to both.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-86582 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies or extracts

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a copy or
  extract of any document or paper made or taken pursuant to subsection

  Act without proof of the signature or official character of the person
  appearing to have signed the copy or extract and, in the absence of
  any evidence to the contrary, has the same probative force as the
  original document or paper would have if it had been proved in the
  ordinary way.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No copy or extract referred to in subsection (1)
  shall be received in evidence in any proceedings unless the party
  intending to produce it has given to the party against whom it is
  intended to be produced notice of that intention at least seven days
  prior to the proceedings together with a duplicate of the copy or
  extract.
