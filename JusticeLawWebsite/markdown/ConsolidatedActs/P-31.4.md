## Public Pensions Reporting Act {#public-pensions-reporting-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. 13 (2nd
Supp.)
:::

An Act to impose reporting requirements with respect to public pension
plans and to amend certain Acts in consequence thereof

::: ReaderNote
\[1986, c. 16, assented to 26th March, 1986\]
:::
:::::

::::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Public Pensions Reporting Act](/eng/acts/P-31.4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Chief Actuary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chief Actuary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chief Actuary of the
    Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions; ([actuaire
    en chef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to any provision,
    means such member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada as is
    designated by the Governor General in Council as the Minister for
    the purpose of that provision. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 13 (2nd Supp.), s. 2, c. 18 (3rd Supp.), s. 40
- 1992, c. 1, s. 143(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pension plan review

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall cause the Chief Actuary to conduct actuarial reviews,
  in accordance with this section, of the pension plans established
  under the following Acts:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the [Canadian Forces Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-17);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the [Members of Parliament Retiring Allowances
    Act](/eng/acts/M-5);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/R-11);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the [Judges Act](/eng/acts/J-1); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Supplementary Retirement Benefits
  Act](/eng/acts/S-24)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each pension plan referred to in paragraphs (1)(a)
  to (e) shall be deemed to include the related benefits payable under
  the [Supplementary Retirement Benefits Act](/eng/acts/S-24) and the
  related assets of the Supplementary Retirement Benefits Account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review dates

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The review dates as of which the actuarial reviews
  under subsection (1) must be conducted are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of each of the pension plans referred to
    in paragraphs (1)(a) to (e), a date not later than four years after
    the last review date and, thereafter, dates not more than three
    years apart;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the pension plan established under
    Part I of the [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9), a date not
    later than December 31, 1988 and, thereafter, dates not more than
    three years apart; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the pension plans established under
    Parts II and III of the [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9), a
    date fixed by the Governor in Council and, thereafter, dates not
    more than three years apart.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of [[last review
  date]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In paragraph (3)(a), the [[last review
  date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} shall be deemed to be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the [Canadian Forces Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-17), December 31, 1983;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the [Members of Parliament Retiring
    Allowances Act](/eng/acts/M-5), March 31, 1982;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36), December 31, 1983;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11), December 31, 1985; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the [Judges Act](/eng/acts/J-1),
    December 31, 1983.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where plan is amended

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where an amendment is made
to a pension plan referred to in subsection 3(1) and the amendment
affects the cost of benefits or creates an initial unfunded liability,
the Minister shall cause the Chief Actuary to conduct an actuarial
review of the plan as of the effective date of the amendment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cost certificate

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Actuary shall, within eighteen months after the review date of a
  pension plan reviewed in accordance with section 3, file with the
  Minister a cost certificate in respect of the plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of cost certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A cost certificate filed pursuant to subsection (1)
  in respect of a plan referred to in paragraphs 3(1)(a) to (e) shall
  set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the estimated cost of benefits under the pension
    plan (expressed as a percentage of pensionable payroll, showing
    separately the employer contributions and the employee
    contributions) in respect of service

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the three plan years following the review
      date, where that date falls on the last day of a plan year, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for the plan year in which the review date falls
      and the two subsequent plan years, where that date falls on any
      other day of a plan year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the actuarial method for computing the cost of
    benefits, including, where applicable, the rule for allocating the
    cost between the employer and the employees in respect of service
    for the next two plan years following the applicable plan year
    referred to in paragraph (a); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amounts of any new initial unfunded liability
    and any new experience deficiency or surplus on the review date and
    any special payment to be made in respect thereto.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A cost certificate filed pursuant to subsection (1)
  in respect of a plan referred to in paragraph 3(1)(f) shall show the
  estimated annual cost of monthly pensions, monthly guaranteed income
  supplements, allowances, and other benefits, if any, for each of the
  ten years following the review, and for each fifth year thereafter for
  a period not less than thirty years from the date of such a review.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 13 (2nd Supp.), s. 5
- 2000, c. 12, s. 271
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Valuation report

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Actuary shall, in respect of a review required pursuant to
  paragraphs 3(1)(a) to (e), file with the Minister a valuation report
  based on the review of the plan, within the time limit referred to in
  subsection 5(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of valuation report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A valuation report filed pursuant to this section
  shall be prepared in a manner consistent with the guides and
  Recommendations for the preparation of an actuarial report in
  connection with a pension plan, published by the Canadian Institute of
  Actuaries, and shall include such additional information as the
  Minister may from time to time require.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change in actuarial methods or
assumptions

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where, in the review of a
pension plan, actuarial assumptions or methods are used that differ from
those used for the immediately preceding review in respect of which a
cost certificate was filed pursuant to section 5 and such different
assumptions or methods result

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a decrease in the going concern unfunded actuarial
  liability but do not result in an excess of going concern assets over
  the going concern actuarial liabilities, the outstanding special
  payments shall be recalculated by multiplying each of the amounts
  thereof by a factor having, as numerator, the going concern unfunded
  actuarial liability and, as denominator, the sum of the present values
  of the previously determined special payments where the present values
  are calculated on the basis of the actuarial assumptions used at the
  current review; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in an excess of the going concern assets over the
  going concern actuarial liabilities, the valuation report referred to
  in section 6 shall include a statement as to the method, if any,
  proposed for the disposition of such excess.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assets report

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall cause a certification of the assets of a pension plan
  established under the [Canadian Forces Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-17), the [Members of Parliament Retiring Allowances
  Act](/eng/acts/M-5), the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11) and the [Supplementary Retirement
  Benefits Act](/eng/acts/S-24) to be made and a report thereof to be
  filed with the Minister at the same time as a cost certificate is
  filed pursuant to subsection 5(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assets report by Comptroller General

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The certification and the assets report referred to
  in subsection (1) shall be made by the Comptroller General of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports laid before Parliament

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall lay before Parliament any cost certificate, valuation
  report or assets report filed with the Minister pursuant to this Act,
  within thirty sitting days of their being filed if Parliament is then
  sitting, or if Parliament is not then sitting, on any of the first
  thirty days thereafter that Parliament is sitting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dissolution of Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, at the time any cost certificate, valuation
  report or assets report is received by the Minister, Parliament is
  dissolved, the Minister shall forthwith cause a copy of the
  certificate or report to be published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).
