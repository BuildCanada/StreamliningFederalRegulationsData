## Child Day Act {#child-day-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1993, c. 18
:::

Assented to 1993-05-06

An Act respecting a national child day
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS on November 20, 1989, the Convention on the Rights of the Child
was adopted in the United Nations General Assembly;

AND WHEREAS the United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child has
been ratified by Canada;

AND WHEREAS it is desirable to promote in Canada an awareness of the
United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80492 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Child Day Act](/eng/acts/C-27.7).

## [Child Day]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80495 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Child day

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Throughout Canada, in each
and every year, the 20th day of November, being the day in the year 1989
when the United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child was
adopted in the United Nations General Assembly, shall be known as
\"Child Day\".
