**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-93772 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Attorney General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Attorney General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Attorney General
    of Canada or the Attorney General of a province, and includes
    counsel or an agent exercising any of the powers or performing any
    of the duties and functions of the Attorney General for the purposes
    of the applicable laws of a province or this Act, as the case may
    be; ([procureur général]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contravention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contravention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an offence that is
    created by an enactment and is designated as a contravention by
    regulation of the Governor in
    Council; ([contravention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contraventions court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contraventions court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
    contravention alleged to have been committed in, or otherwise within
    the territorial jurisdiction of the courts of, a province, a court
    designated by order of the Governor in Council in respect of that
    province; ([tribunal des contraventions]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[enactment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[enactment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any Act of Parliament or any
    regulation, rule, order, by-law or ordinance made under an Act of
    Parliament; ([texte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[enforcement authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[enforcement authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
    contravention,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any police officer or constable, including a
      special or auxiliary constable,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the minister responsible for administering the
      enactment creating the contravention,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person, or member of a class of persons,
      designated by the minister responsible for administering the
      enactment creating the contravention, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the corporation or other body that made or is
      responsible for administering the enactment creating the
      contravention; ([agent de l'autorité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fees]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fees]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the amounts provided for
    under paragraph 8(1)(e); ([frais]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[forfeitable thing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[forfeitable thing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 1996, c. 7, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Justice; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by
    regulation; ([réglementaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[ticket]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ticket]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a ticket issued under this
    Act; ([procès-verbal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[young person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[young person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
    contravention, a person who, at the time of the contravention, is
    or, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, appears to be twelve
    years of age or more but under eighteen years of
    age; ([adolescent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[youth court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[youth court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2002, c. 1, s.
    167\]]{.Repealed}

[[youth justice court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[youth justice court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
    contravention alleged to have been committed by a young person in,
    or otherwise within the territorial jurisdiction of the courts of, a
    province, the court established or designated by or under an Act of
    the legislature of the province, or designated by the Governor in
    Council or lieutenant governor in council of the province, as the
    youth justice court for the purposes of the [Youth Criminal Justice
    Act](/eng/acts/Y-1.5). ([tribunal pour adolescents]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-93797 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The purposes of this Act
are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to provide a procedure for the prosecution of
  contraventions that reflects the distinction between criminal offences
  and regulatory offences and that is in addition to the procedures set
  out in the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) for the prosecution of
  contraventions and other offences; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to alter or abolish the consequences in law of being
  convicted of a contravention, in light of that distinction.

## [Relationship with Other Acts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-93802 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Relationship with [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46) and [Youth Criminal Justice Act](/eng/acts/Y-1.5)

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The provisions of the
[Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) relating to summary conviction offences
and the provisions of the [Youth Criminal Justice Act](/eng/acts/Y-1.5)
apply to proceedings in respect of contraventions that are commenced
under this Act, except to the extent that this Act, the regulations or
the rules of court provide otherwise.

## [Designation of Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-93810 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, for the purposes of this Act, make
  regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} designating as contraventions offences created by
    any enactment, other than offences for which an offender may be
    prosecuted only on indictment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establishing short-form descriptions of
    contraventions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} establishing, in respect of a contravention, an
    amount as the amount of the fine for the purposes of proceedings
    commenced by means of a ticket;

  - ::: hiddenMsg
    The following provision is not in force.
    :::

    ::: nif
    [(d)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the form of tickets and other forms
    that may be used for the purposes of this Act;
    :::

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} providing for the fees, costs, penalties and other
    amounts that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} shall be imposed in respect of a contravention,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} may be imposed in respect of a contravention,

    in the circumstances prescribed, at any stage of the proceedings;
    and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} prescribing classes of contraventions for the
    purposes of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Different fees may be provided for in respect of
  each province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Governor in Council may
  revoke the designation of an offence as a contravention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum fine

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An amount established under paragraph (1)(c) may not
  exceed the maximum amount established for the relevant offence by the
  enactment creating the offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum fine for contraventions by
  young persons

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An amount established under paragraph (1)(c) in
  respect of a contravention, other than a contravention relating to
  parking a vehicle, may not exceed one hundred dollars, if the
  contravention is committed by a young person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum fine

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An amount established under paragraph (1)(c) may not
  be less than any minimum amount prescribed for the relevant offence by
  the enactment creating the offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporations and individuals

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Different amounts may be established under paragraph
  (1)(c) in respect of corporations and individuals committing the same
  contravention if the enactment creating the offence differentiates
  between them in prescribing the punishment for the offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sufficiency of short-form description

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The use on a form prescribed under this Act of a
  short-form description established under paragraph (1)(b) or of a
  description that deviates from that description without affecting its
  substance is sufficient for all purposes to describe the
  contravention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of ticket

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A ticket must be in the
form prescribed under paragraph 8(1)(d) and

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} contain a statement signed by the enforcement
  authority certifying, in the case of a ticket served under section 9,
  that the enforcement authority has reasonable grounds to believe that
  a contravention has been committed and, in the case of a ticket served
  under section 12, that the enforcement authority believes from
  personal knowledge that a contravention has been committed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} include the short-form description of the
  contravention established under paragraph 8(1)(b);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} indicate with reasonable precision, having regard to
  all the circumstances, the time and place at which the contravention
  was committed;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} set out a total amount equal to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount established under paragraph 8(1)(c) as
    the amount of the fine, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the fees that are applicable on serving a ticket;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} set out the options the person served with the ticket
  has in responding to it, the time within which the person must respond
  and the consequences under section 44 of failing to respond;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} provide an opportunity for the person to indicate in
  which official language, being the person's language, the person
  wishes to be tried;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} provide an opportunity for the person to indicate
  whether the person is a young person;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} mention that the ticket may be used as the evidence
  of the enforcement authority; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} provide an opportunity for the person to indicate
  whether the attendance of the enforcement authority who completed the
  ticket is required for the purposes of cross-examination.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Plea of guilty with representations

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} plead guilty but make representations with respect

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The matters referred to in subsection (1) are the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fine, fees or other sentence that ought to be
    imposed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the person ought to be given time to pay
    the fine or fees.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1996, c. 7, s. 12\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The contraventions court or a justice of the peace or
  other officer or, in the case of a young person in a province the
  lieutenant governor in council of which has not made an order under
  subsection 17(2), the youth court shall consider the representations,
  along with the ticket, and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the court, justice or officer is satisfied that
    the representations should be accepted without holding a sentencing

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

  is liable to a fine in an amount not exceeding the amount established
  under section 8 or to any sentence prescribed by law other than a
  fine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No imprisonment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who is convicted in a proceeding commenced

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a defendant in a proceeding commenced by means
  of a ticket requests a trial but does not appear for it or a
  resumption of it and is convicted, a contraventions or youth court or
  a justice of the peace shall impose the fine in the amount established
  under section 8.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment on default

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a defendant in a proceeding commenced by means
  of a ticket does not respond to the ticket, a contraventions or youth
  court or a justice of the peace shall impose the fine in the amount
  established under section 8.

  shall be returned to the defendant.

the contravention within thirty days after the imposition or, if the
time for payment is extended, within thirty days after the expiration of
that time, the clerk of the contraventions or youth court shall cause a

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Civil enforcement

- **[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  offender, other than a young person, who is convicted in a proceeding
  does not pay a fine or any fees imposed for the contravention within
  thirty days after the imposition or, if the time for payment is
  extended, within thirty days after the expiration of that time, the
  Attorney General may, by filing the conviction, enter as a judgment
  the amount of the fine and fees, if any, in any civil court in Canada,
  other than the Federal Court, that has jurisdiction to enter a
  judgment for that amount.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of filing conviction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A conviction that is entered as a judgment under this
  section is enforceable against the convicted person in the same manner
  as if it were a judgment obtained against that person in that court in
  civil proceedings by Her Majesty in right of Canada or of a province
  or by the person to whom the proceeds of the fine belong.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A conviction may not be entered as a judgment under
  this section more than two years after the day on which the conviction
  was entered.

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** Except in respect of a

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who has been convicted of a contravention
  has not been convicted of a criminal offence; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a contravention does not constitute an offence for

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications for employment

**[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** Every one is guilty of an
offence who uses or authorizes the use of an application form for or
relating to any of the following matters that contains a question that
by its terms requires the applicant to disclose a conviction for a
contravention, other than a conviction entered after a trial on an
indictment:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} employment in any department, as defined in section 2
  of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} employment by any Crown corporation, as defined in
  subsection 83(1) of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} enrolment in the Canadian Forces; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} employment on or in connection with the operation of
  any work, undertaking or business that is within the legislative
  authority of Parliament.

## [Partial Abolition of Royal Prerogative]{.HTitleText1} {#h-94273 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance and revocation of passports

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** The prerogative right of
the Crown to refuse to issue a passport to a person, or to revoke the
passport of a person, by reason only that the person is charged with or
convicted of a contravention or of an offence committed outside Canada
that, if committed in Canada, would constitute a contravention is
abolished.
