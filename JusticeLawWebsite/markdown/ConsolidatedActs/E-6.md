## Energy Administration Act {#energy-administration-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. E-6
:::

An Act to provide for charges, compensation and pricing in respect of
certain energy sources and for the administration and control of other
matters respecting energy sources in Canada

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Oil Export Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-219368 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Part,

  [[export]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[export]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (b), where oil is
        transported by pipeline, to deliver it at its point of delivery
        outside Canada,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where oil is transported by pipeline from the
        offshore area as defined in section 20, to deliver it at its
        point of delivery outside that area and Canada, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where oil is transported by any other means, to
        send it

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} from Canada other than to export it within
          the meaning of subsection 16(1), or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to a place outside Canada from the offshore
          area as defined in section 20; ([exporter]{.DefinedTermLink
          lang="fr"})

  [[exporter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[exporter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person holding a
      licence; ([exportateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence or other
      authorization that is issued under Part 7 of the [Canadian Energy
      Regulator Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1) and that permits the export of
      oil under that Act. ([licence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation for pipeline traffic

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of calculating the number of cubic
  metres of oil exported at any place by pipeline during a period in
  which a specified charge applies to that exportation, the period shall
  be deemed to commence at seven o'clock local time in the forenoon of
  the day on which that charge is imposed and to end at seven o'clock
  local time in the forenoon of the day on which the charge is varied.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charge on oil

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be imposed, levied and collected on each cubic metre of oil
  exported in any month or part of a month a charge in such amount not
  exceeding three hundred and fifty dollars per cubic metre as may be
  prescribed in a tariff of charges for that month or part of a month
  made by order of the Governor in Council on the recommendation of the
  Minister and the Minister of Finance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tariff of charges

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A tariff of charges on oil may set out the charge
  applicable in respect of any or all of the various kinds or qualities
  of oil, oil from any source, any or all destinations for oil and such
  other factors or circumstances as are specified in the tariff.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tariff continues

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a tariff of charges on oil is prescribed in
  respect of any month or part of a month under subsection (1), that
  tariff of charges shall continue in respect of each subsequent month
  until changed pursuant to subsection (1) by order of the Governor in
  Council in respect of a subsequent month or part of a month.

::: HistoricalNote

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By whom charge payable

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A charge
  imposed under this Part on the exportation of oil is payable to the
  Minister by the exporter under whose licence the oil is purported to
  be exported.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who exports oil on which a charge is imposed
  under this Part in circumstances in which there is no exporter who is
  liable under this Part to pay that charge is liable to pay that
  charge.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption or reduction

  thousand dollars or more ordered pursuant to this section shall be

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Monthly return of export sales

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who is required by this Part to pay a charge shall make each
  month a true return of his exports of oil for the last preceding month
  in such form and containing such information as the regulations
  require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of filing and payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The return required by subsection (1) shall be filed
  with the Regulator and the charge payable shall be paid to it not

  the exports were made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty on default

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On default in payment of the charge or any portion
  thereof payable under this Part within the time prescribed by
  subsection (2), there shall be paid in addition to the amount of the
  default a penalty equal to the greater of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one per cent of the amount of default, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the percentage, if any, prescribed by regulations
    made under section 15, of the amount of default,

  in respect of each month or part of a month during which the default
  continues.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of time

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission of the Regulator may, before or after
  the day prescribed by subsection (2), specify in writing a later day

  later day

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no penalty shall accrue or shall be deemed to have
    accrued under subsection (3) prior to that later day in respect of
    default in payment of the charge or portion thereof for the payment

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} failure to pay, on or before the later day, the
    charge or portion thereof for the payment of which a later day was
    specified constitutes a default for the purposes of subsection (3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deductions and refunds

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

  granted

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where an overpayment has been made by the exporter;

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No deduction from, or refund of, the charges imposed

  refunded unless application therefor has been made in writing within
  two years after the moneys were paid or overpaid.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Transportation Fuel Compensation Recovery Charge]{.HTitleText1} {#h-219484 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this Part,

  [[export]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[export]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of transportation fuel,
      means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the delivery by an exporter of that fuel to a
        person other than an exporter in order that the person may take
        it from Canada by means of an aircraft or vessel for immediate
        use by that aircraft or vessel, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the taking by an exporter of that fuel from
        Canada by means of an aircraft or vessel for use by that
        aircraft or vessel; ([exporter]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[exporter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[exporter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and
      [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have the same meanings as in
      section 4; ([exportateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} et
      [licence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transportation fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transportation fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means fuel designated
      by regulations under section 19 as being fuel for use by an
      aircraft or vessel. ([carburant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation of
  [[export]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the definition
  [[export]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection (1), an export in respect
  of an aircraft or vessel referred to in that definition shall be
  deemed to occur at the time the aircraft or vessel is at its last port
  of landing in Canada preceding its journey outside Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 114, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charge

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be imposed, levied and collected on each cubic metre of
  transportation fuel acquired in Canada and exported from Canada in any
  month or part of a month, in respect of which no charge has been
  imposed, levied and collected under Part I, a transportation fuel
  compensation recovery charge in such amount not exceeding three
  hundred and fifty dollars per cubic metre as may be prescribed in a
  tariff of charges for that month or part of a month made by order of
  the Governor in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister and the
  Minister of Finance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tariff of charges

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A tariff of charges on transportation fuel may set
  out the charge applicable in respect of any or all of the various
  kinds or qualities of transportation fuel, transportation fuel from
  any source, any or all destinations for transportation fuel and such
  other factors or circumstances as are specified in the tariff.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tariff continues

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a tariff of charges on transportation fuel is
  prescribed in respect of any month or part of a month under subsection
  (1), that tariff of charges shall continue in respect of each
  subsequent month until changed pursuant to subsection (1) by order of
  the Governor in Council in respect of a subsequent month or part of a
  month.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 114, s. 12
:::

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Domestic Oil]{.HTitleText1} {#h-219517 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-219518 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

[[consumption]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[consumption]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to crude oil,
    the action of using it as a fuel or energy source or consuming it in

### [Purpose of Part]{.HTitleText2} {#h-219538 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Part
is to provide legislative authority for measures that will, so far as
may be practicable, enable the Government of Canada

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to achieve a uniform price, exclusive of
  transportation costs, for crude oil used in Canada outside its
  province of production;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to achieve a balance in Canada between the interests
  of consumers and producers in Canada;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to protect consumers in Canada from instability of
  prices for petroleum in the international markets; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to encourage the discovery, development and
  production of a supply of crude oil adequate to the self-sufficiency
  of Canada.

  or access will be refused, a judge of a superior, county or district
  court, on [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application by that person,
  may by order direct a peace officer to take such steps as to the judge
  seem necessary to give that person such admission or access.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grounds on which judge may act

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A judge mentioned in subsection (3) may act under
  that subsection where he is satisfied that there are reasonable
  grounds for entering the premises to which the application relates and
  that admission or access has been refused or that there are reasonable
  grounds for believing that admission or access will be refused, as the
  case may be.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 47, s. 90
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies of documents

**[[97]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-97 .sectionLabel}** A document purporting to
be certified by the Minister to be a copy of a record, book, paper or
other document made pursuant to paragraph 95(1)(b) or (d) is admissible
in evidence in any prosecution for an offence under this Act and is, in
the absence of evidence to the contrary, proof of the contents thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 47, s. 91
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication of privileged information

- **[[98]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-98
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section, all
  information with respect to a person or business obtained by any
  person in the course of the administration of this Act is privileged
  and no person shall knowingly, except as provided in this Act,
  communicate or allow to be communicated to any person not legally
  entitled thereto any such information or allow any person not legally
  entitled thereto to inspect or have access to any such information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception where request in writing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any information with respect to a person or business
  obtained by any person in the course of the administration of this Act
  may, on request in writing to the Minister by or on behalf of the
  person to which it relates or by or on behalf of the person or group
  of persons carrying on the business to which it relates, be
  communicated to any person or authority named in the request on such
  terms and conditions and under such circumstances as are approved by
  the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence and production of documents

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding any other Act or law, no person
  employed in the administration of this Act shall be required, in
  connection with any legal proceedings, to give evidence relating to
  any information that is privileged under subsection (1) or to produce
  any statement or other writing containing that information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of subsections (1) and (3)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (3) do not apply in respect of
  legal proceedings relating to the administration or enforcement of
  this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 47, s. 92
:::
