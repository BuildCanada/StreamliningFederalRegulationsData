## Cree Nation of Eeyou Istchee Governance Agreement Act {#cree-nation-of-eeyou-istchee-governance-agreement-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2018, c. 4, s. 1
:::

Assented to 2018-03-29

An Act to give effect to the Agreement on Cree Nation Governance between
the Crees of Eeyou Istchee and the Government of Canada

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 1 of chapter 4 of the Statutes of Canada, 2018, in
force on assent March 29, 2018.\]
:::
:::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the Grand Council of the Crees (Eeyou Istchee), the Cree Nation
Government and the Government of Canada have negotiated the Agreement on
Cree Nation Governance between the Crees of Eeyou Istchee and the
Government of Canada;

Whereas that agreement was signed by the parties on July 18, 2017;

And whereas ratification of that agreement requires the coming into
force of an Act of Parliament that gives effect to that agreement;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-114463 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Cree Nation of Eeyou Istchee Governance Agreement
Act](/eng/acts/C-45.75).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-114466 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement on Cree
      Nation Governance between the Crees of Eeyou Istchee and the
      Government of Canada, entered into on July 18, 2017, between the
      Government of Canada, the Grand Council of the Crees (Eeyou
      Istchee) and the Cree Nation Government, including any amendments
      made to it. ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Cree Constitution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Cree Constitution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the constitution
      developed under section 3.1 of the Agreement and ratified in
      accordance with sections 31.4, 31.6 and 31.7 of the
      Agreement. ([constitution crie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Cree Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Cree Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[Cree Nation of Eeyou
      Istchee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as [[Cree
      Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 1.1 of the
      Agreement. ([nation crie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou [nation
      crie d'Eeyou Istchee]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Cree Nation Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Cree Nation Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the legal
      person established under section 2 of An Act respecting the Cree
      Nation Government, CQLR, chapter G-1.031. ([Gouvernement de la
      nation crie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions used in Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Act, [[Category IA
  land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[Cree beneficiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
  [[Cree First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[Cree First Nation
  law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[Cree law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[Cree
  Nation Government law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[federal
  law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[Inuk of Chisasibi]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  (in the singular) or [[Inuit of Chisasibi]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} (in
  the plural) and the [[James Bay and Northern Quebec
  Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have the same meaning as in section
  1.1 of the Agreement.

## [Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-114476 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement given effect

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement is approved, given effect and declared valid and has the
  force of law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement binding

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Agreement is binding on,
  and may be relied on by, the parties and all other persons and bodies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}James Bay and Northern Quebec Agreement
prevails

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The James Bay and Northern
Quebec Agreement prevails over the Agreement to the extent of any
inconsistency or conflict between them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement prevails

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement prevails over
this Act to the extent of any inconsistency or conflict between them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act prevails

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), this Act prevails over any other federal law to the
  extent of any inconsistency or conflict between them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[James Bay and Northern Quebec Native
  Claims Settlement Act](/eng/acts/J-0.3)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The [James Bay and Northern Quebec Native Claims
  Settlement Act](/eng/acts/J-0.3) prevails over this Act to the extent
  of any inconsistency or conflict between them.

## [Constitution and Cree Laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-114491 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cree Constitution

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Cree
  Constitution is given effect and has the force of law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Constitution binding

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Cree Constitution is
  binding on, and may be relied on by, all persons and bodies. However,
  the Cree Constitution is not binding on Her Majesty in right of Canada
  or a province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cree law

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A Cree
  law made in accordance with the Agreement and the Cree Constitution
  has the force of law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Third parties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a Cree law is binding on, and
  may be relied on by, all persons and bodies. However, a Cree law is
  not binding on Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Cree First Nations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-114502 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of Cree bands

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Cree
  bands constituted as corporations under sections 12 and 12.1 of the
  Cree-Naskapi (of Quebec) Act, as they read immediately before the day
  on which section 3 of this Act comes into force, are continued as Cree
  First Nations and continue to be the same legal entities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New Cree First Nations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} New Cree First Nations may be constituted in
  accordance with Chapter 23 of the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal capacity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A Cree First Nation has, subject to the Agreement,
  the capacity, rights, powers and privileges of a natural person.

## [Application of Other Acts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-114510 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The [Statutory
Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does not apply to Cree laws or
resolutions of Cree First Nations or of the Cree Nation Government made
under the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Except for the purpose of
determining which of the Cree beneficiaries are
[[Indians]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} within the meaning of the [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5), the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) does not apply to
Cree First Nations, nor does it apply on or in respect of Category IA
land.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of certain Acts

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  [Canada Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8), chapter C-32 of the
  Revised Statutes of Canada, 1970, the [Canada Business Corporations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-44), the [Canada Not-for-profit Corporations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-7.75) and any other Acts of Parliament specifically
  applicable to corporations do not apply to Cree First Nations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, the Governor in Council may, by order, at
  the request of the Cree Nation Government, provide that an Act of
  Parliament referred to in subsection (1), or any provision of that
  Act, applies to any Cree First Nation.

## [Tax and Seizure Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-114520 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of exemptions

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 9 of
the James Bay and Northern Quebec Agreement, the tax and seizure
exemptions regimes set out in sections 187 to 193 of the Cree-Naskapi
(of Quebec) Act, as they read immediately before the day on which
section 3 comes into force, continue to apply as provided in sections 14
to 20.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section and section 15, [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in subsection (2), a Cree beneficiary who is an
    [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in the [Indian
    Act](/eng/acts/I-5); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in section 15, an [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
    as defined in the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section and section 15,
  personal property

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that became the property of a Cree First Nation by
    virtue of section 13 or 13.1 of the Cree-Naskapi (of Quebec) Act, as
    they read immediately before the day on which section 3 comes into
    force, and had been purchased by Canada with money appropriated by
    Parliament,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is purchased by Canada, after July 3, 1984,
    with money appropriated by Parliament for the use and benefit of
    Indians or Cree First Nations, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that is given, after July 3, 1984, to Indians or to
    a Cree First Nation under a treaty or agreement between a Cree First
    Nation and Canada

  shall be deemed always to be situated on Category IA land.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property exempt from taxation

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding any other Act of
  Parliament or of the legislature of any province, but subject to any
  Cree First Nation laws made pursuant to paragraph 6.2(1)(k) of the
  Agreement or any Cree Nation Government laws made pursuant to section
  8.15 of the Agreement, the following property is exempt from taxation:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the interest of an Indian or a Cree First Nation in
    Category IA land; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the personal property of an Indian or a Cree First
    Nation situated on Category IA land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ownership, occupation, etc., of exempt
  property

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding any other Act of Parliament or of the
  legislature of any province, but subject to any Cree First Nation laws
  made pursuant to paragraph 6.2(1)(k) of the Agreement or any Cree
  Nation Government laws made pursuant to section 8.15 of the Agreement,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no Indian or Cree First Nation is subject to
    taxation in respect of the ownership, occupation, possession or use
    of any property described in paragraph (1)(a) or (b) or is otherwise
    subject to taxation in respect of any such property; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no succession duty, inheritance tax or estate duty
    is payable on the death of any Indian in respect of any such
    property or the succession thereto if the property passes to an
    Indian.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** In sections 17 to 20,
[[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
as defined in the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property exempt from seizure, etc.

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section and sections
  18 to 20, movable and immovable property situated on Category IA land
  and belonging to a Cree beneficiary or an Indian ordinarily resident
  on Category IA land, and any right or interest of such a person in
  Category IA land, is not subject to privilege, hypothec or any other
  charge, or to attachment, levy, seizure or execution, in favour of or
  at the instance of any person other than a Cree beneficiary, a Cree
  First Nation, the Cree Nation Government or an Indian ordinarily
  resident on Category IA land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property exempt from seizure, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section and sections 18 to 20,
  movable and immovable property situated on Category IA land and
  belonging to a Cree First Nation is not subject to privilege, hypothec
  or any other charge, or to attachment, levy, seizure or execution, in
  favour of or at the instance of any person other than a Cree
  beneficiary, a Cree First Nation, the Cree Nation Government or an
  Indian ordinarily resident on Category IA land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The right or interest of a Cree First Nation in its
  Category IA land is not subject to privilege, hypothec or any other
  charge, or to attachment, levy, seizure or execution, in favour of or
  at the instance of any person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A right or interest in Category IA land of a person
  other than a Cree beneficiary or a Cree First Nation, or the immovable
  property of such person situated on Category IA land, is not subject
  to attachment, levy, seizure or execution in favour of or at the
  instance of any person other than a Cree beneficiary or a Cree First
  Nation, except where the Cree First Nation has authorized that person
  to pledge, charge or hypothecate that right or interest or immovable
  property, in which case creditors may exercise their normal remedies
  in relation to that pledge, charge or hypothecation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditional sales

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person who sells movable property to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Cree beneficiary,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an Indian ordinarily resident on Category IA land,
    or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a Cree First Nation

  under an agreement whereby the right of property or right of
  possession thereto remains wholly or in part in the seller may
  exercise his or her rights under that agreement notwithstanding that
  the movable property is situated on Category IA land.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property deemed situated on Category IA
land

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 17, movable property

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that became the property of a Cree First Nation by
  virtue of section 13 or 13.1 of the Cree-Naskapi (of Quebec) Act as it
  read immediately before the day on which section 3 comes into force,
  and had been purchased with money appropriated by Parliament,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is purchased after July 3, 1984 with money
  appropriated by Parliament or by the legislature of Quebec for the use
  and benefit of Indians, Cree beneficiaries, or Cree First Nations, or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} that is, after July 3, 1984, provided to Cree
  beneficiaries or a Cree First Nation under a treaty or agreement
  between a Cree First Nation and Canada

shall be deemed always to be situated on Category IA land.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property deemed to be property of a Cree
First Nation

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Where a Cree First Nation
has, pursuant to paragraph 11A.0.6 or 11.2.6, as the case may be, of the
James Bay and Northern Quebec Agreement, delegated to the Cree Nation
Government the power to coordinate and administer a program, movable
property that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is necessary for the coordination or administration
  of that program,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is owned by the Cree Nation Government, and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} was purchased with money appropriated by Parliament
  or by the legislature of Quebec for the use and benefit of Indians,
  Cree beneficiaries or Cree First Nations

shall, for the purposes of section 17, be deemed always to be the
property of the Cree First Nation for whose use and benefit it was
purchased.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waiver of exemption

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A Cree
  beneficiary or an Indian ordinarily resident on Category IA land may,
  in writing, waive the exemption conferred by subsection 17(1) in
  favour of any person, on such terms and conditions as are agreed to by
  the parties, but, in the case of a right or interest in Category IA
  land, the consent of the Cree First Nation must be obtained to the
  waiver and the terms and conditions thereof in accordance with the
  Cree Constitution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waiver by Cree First Nation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Cree First Nation may, in writing, waive the
  exemption conferred by subsection 17(2) in favour of any person, on
  such terms and conditions as are agreed to by the parties, subject to
  the approval of the waiver and the terms and conditions thereof in
  accordance with the Cree Constitution.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-114577 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice --- Agreement

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice must be taken of the
  Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Agreement must be published by the Queen's
  Printer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the Agreement published by the Queen's
  Printer is evidence of the Agreement and of its contents, and a copy
  purporting to be published by the Queen's Printer is presumed to be so
  published unless the contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice --- Cree law

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice must be taken of any
  Cree law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of any Cree law that is certified to be a true
  copy by a person authorized by the Cree First Nation that made the law
  or the Cree Nation Government, as the case may be, is evidence of that
  law and of its contents, without proof of the signature or official
  character of the person appearing to have signed it, unless the
  contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Cree First Nation

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  issue arises in any judicial or administrative proceeding in respect
  of the operability, constitutionality or validity of a provision of a
  Cree First Nation law, then the issue must not be decided until the
  party raising the issue has served notice on the Cree First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Cree Nation Government

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an issue arises in any judicial or administrative
  proceeding in respect of the operability, constitutionality or
  validity of the Agreement, the James Bay and Northern Quebec
  Agreement, this Act or a Cree Nation Government law, then the issue
  must not be decided until the party raising the issue has served
  notice on the Cree Nation Government.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content and timing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice must clearly state the contentions the
  person intends to assert and the grounds on which they are based and
  be served at least 30 days before the case is ready for trial. The
  notice must be accompanied by all pleadings already filed in the
  record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation in proceedings

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Cree First Nation or the Cree Nation Government,
  as the case may be, becomes a party to the proceeding without further
  formality and may submit conclusions to the court or tribunal, in
  which case the court or tribunal must rule on them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waiver

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Only the Cree First Nation or the Cree Nation
  Government, as the case may be, may waive the notice period referred
  to in subsection (3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inuit of Chisasibi

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  any period during which the council of the Cree Nation of Chisasibi
  does not include an Inuk of Chisasibi, the Inuit of Chisasibi are
  entitled to have one Inuk of Chisasibi present as an observer at
  meetings of that council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations -- Inuk observer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make regulations
  respecting the manner of selection and term and tenure of the Inuk
  observer referred to in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights of Inuk observer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Inuk observer referred to in subsection (1) shall
  be notified of, and has the right to attend, all council meetings, and
  has the right to participate in the deliberations of the council, as
  if he or she were a council member, but he or she does not have the
  right to vote.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make any orders and regulations that the Governor in Council
considers necessary for the purpose of carrying out any of the
provisions of the Agreement.
