[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2014, c. 39, s. 145
:::

Assented to 2014-12-16

in force June 1, 2015, see SI/2015-36.\]

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69887 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canadian High Arctic Research Station Act](/eng/acts/C-17.8).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69890 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Arctic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Arctic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, when used in relation to Canada,
    means those parts of Canada

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} north of sixty degrees north latitude; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} south of sixty degrees north latitude but north
      of the southern limit of the discontinuous permafrost
      zone. ([Arctique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Board of Directors of
    CHARS. ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[CHARS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CHARS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian High Arctic
    Research Station established by subsection
    4(1). ([SCREA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[immovable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[immovable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
    2 of the [Federal Real Property and Federal Immovables
    Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4). ([immeuble]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister designated under
    section 3. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[real property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[real property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Federal Real Property and Federal Immovables
    Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4). ([biens réels]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69903 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of Minister

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate any federal minister to be the Minister
referred to in this Act.

## [CHARS Established]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69906 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}CHARS established

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  corporation is established to be called the Canadian High Arctic
  Research Station.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agent of Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} CHARS is for all its purposes an agent of Her Majesty
  in right of Canada and it may exercise its powers only as an agent of
  Her Majesty.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69912 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of CHARS is to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} advance knowledge of the Canadian Arctic in order to
  improve economic opportunities, environmental stewardship and the
  quality of life of its residents and all other Canadians;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} promote the development and dissemination of
  knowledge of the other circumpolar regions, including the Antarctic;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} strengthen Canada's leadership on Arctic issues; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} establish a hub for scientific research in the
  Canadian Arctic.

## [Powers and Functions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69919 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}CHARS --- functions

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  carrying out its purpose, CHARS is to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} undertake scientific research and develop
    technology;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} plan, direct, manage and implement programs and
    projects relating to scientific research and the development of
    technology;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} promote the testing, application, transfer,
    diffusion and commercialization of technology;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} encourage integration across disciplines and
    activities;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} publish and disseminate studies, reports and other
    documents;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} complement national and international networks of
    expertise and of facilities; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} perform any other functions that the Minister
    assigns to it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}CHARS --- powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In carrying out its purpose, CHARS may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} construct, manage and operate research facilities
    and systems;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} spend any money that it receives through the
    conduct of its operations, in the fiscal year in which the money is
    received or in subsequent fiscal years;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide services and facilities to any department,
    board or agency of the Government of Canada, or to any other
    government or any organization or person;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} make recoverable expenditures on behalf of any
    department, board or agency of the Government of Canada, or on
    behalf of any other government or any organization or person;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} license, assign, sell or otherwise make available,
    and receive fees, royalties and payment for, any patent, copyright,
    industrial design, trademark, trade secret or other similar property
    right held, controlled or administered by CHARS;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} enter into contracts, agreements, memoranda of
    understanding or other arrangements in the name of Her Majesty in
    right of Canada or of CHARS;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} lease personal property or movables, acquire any
    money, securities or other personal property or movables, including
    by purchase, gift or bequest or legacy, and --- subject to any terms
    of the acquisition --- spend or invest the money or securities;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} acquire real property or immovables, have the
    administration of the real property or immovables or acquire a
    [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 2 of the
    [Federal Real Property and Federal Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4);

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} as lessor, lease any personal property or movables
    referred to in paragraph (g) or dispose by any means of property or
    movables acquired under that paragraph, subject to any terms on
    which the property or movables were acquired;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} subject to any terms on which the real property or
    immovables were acquired, dispose of any real property or immovables
    or give a licence as defined in section 2 of the [Federal Real
    Property and Federal Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4); and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} undertake any other activities that the Board
    considers conducive to the furtherance of CHARS's purpose and to the
    performance of its functions and exercise of its powers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of Governor in Council

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraphs (2)(h) and (j), CHARS may acquire
  or dispose of real property or immovables only with the approval of
  the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (3) does not apply to any

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} lease of real property or of an immovable; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} disposition to a public utility or municipality of
    an easement or any other interest in real property, other than a fee
    simple, or of a servitude or any other immovable real right, other
    than the ownership of an immovable, if the disposition is necessary
    for the utility or municipality to carry out its activities,
    including the construction or maintenance of a public work.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2018, c. 12, s. 248\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property of Her Majesty

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Property acquired by CHARS is property of Her Majesty
  in right of Canada and title to it may be held in the name of Her
  Majesty in right of Canada or of CHARS.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of administration

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph 16(1)(g) of the [Federal Real Property and
  Federal Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4) applies to CHARS as if it
  were an [[agent corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in that
  Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may refer matters relating to Arctic science and technology
  to CHARS for its consideration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} CHARS must advise the Minister in respect of any
  matter that is referred to it under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may require CHARS to provide him or her
  with reports on its activities and operations, and may make such a
  report available to the public.

## [Board of Directors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69963 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oversight of CHARS

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  organization and administration of CHARS are to be overseen by a Board
  of Directors consisting of nine members, including a Chairperson and a
  Vice-chairperson. The members of the Board are to be appointed by the
  Governor in Council to hold office on a part-time basis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of plans and budgets

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board's oversight function includes approving
  CHARS's science and technology plan as well as annual workplans and
  budgets referred to in subsection 17(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications of members

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Persons appointed to the Board must have knowledge or
  experience that will assist CHARS in carrying out its purpose, having
  regard to the ethnic, linguistic and regional diversity of Canada's
  Arctic.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms of office

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Members
  of the Board are appointed to hold office during pleasure for terms of
  up to five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member of the Board is eligible for reappointment
  for a second term of office in the same or another capacity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Members of the Board are
to be paid the remuneration that is fixed by the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Members of the Board are
entitled to be paid reasonable travel and living expenses while absent
from their ordinary place of residence in the course of their duties
under this Act.

## [Chairperson]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69980 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson's role

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Chairperson presides
over meetings of the Board and performs any other duties that are
assigned by the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting Chairperson

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** In the event of the
absence or incapacity of the Chairperson, or a vacancy in that office,
the Vice-chairperson acts as Chairperson.

## [President of CHARS]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69985 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of President

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President of CHARS is to be appointed by the Governor in Council to
  hold office during pleasure on a full-time basis for a term of up to
  five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The President is eligible for reappointment for one
  or more further terms of office.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The President is to be
paid the remuneration that is fixed by the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The President is entitled
to be paid reasonable travel and living expenses while absent from his
or her ordinary place of work in the course of his or her duties under
this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President's role

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President is the chief executive officer of CHARS and is responsible
  for its day-to-day management and direction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Plans and budgets

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The President must submit to the Board for approval

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} CHARS's science and technology plan, which must be
    submitted at intervals of five years or less; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} annual workplans and budgets.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting President

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  event of the absence or incapacity of the President, or a vacancy in
  that office, the Board must appoint an employee of CHARS to act as
  President.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum acting period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, the acting President may act as President
  for longer than 90 days only with the approval of the Governor in
  Council.

## [Human Resources]{.HTitleText1} {#h-70007 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} CHARS
  has authority over all matters relating to human resources management,
  including the determination of the terms and conditions of employment
  of its employees.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supervision of CHARS employees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The President is to exercise supervision over and
  direction of the activities of the employees of CHARS.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Human resources management

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** CHARS may, in the
exercise of its authority in relation to human resources management,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine its requirements with respect to human
  resources and provide for the allocation and effective utilization of
  human resources;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide for the classification of CHARS positions;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} determine and regulate the pay to which its employees
  are entitled for services rendered, the hours of work and leave of
  those employees and any related matters;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} determine and regulate the payments that may be made
  to its employees by way of reimbursement for travel or other expenses
  and by way of allowances in respect of expenses and conditions arising
  out of their employment; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} take any other measures that CHARS considers
  necessary for effective human resources management.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}CHARS's power to appoint

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** CHARS has the exclusive
right and authority to appoint any employees that it considers necessary
for carrying out its purpose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mobility to departments

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of deployments or appointments made, or advertised
  internal appointment processes, under the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), employees of CHARS must be treated as if they
  were employees within the meaning of that Act and had the rights of
  recourse provided by that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When deployments made subject to terms

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Public Service Commission may, in consultation
  with the President of the Treasury Board, set terms and conditions for
  the deployment of CHARS's employees to departments and agencies under
  the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) if, in the
  opinion of the Commission, the principles governing CHARS's staffing
  program are incompatible with those governing staffing under that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mobility to CHARS

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When CHARS considers employees within the meaning of
  the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) for employment
  within CHARS, it must treat them as if they were employees of CHARS
  and had the rights of recourse of CHARS's employees.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public Service Commission review

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Public Service
Commission may periodically review the compatibility of the principles
governing CHARS's staffing program with those governing staffing under
the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) and may report
its findings in its annual report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Political activities

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Part 7 of the [Public
Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) applies to the President and
employees of CHARS. For the purposes of that Part, the President is
deemed to be a deputy head as defined in subsection 2(1) of that Act and
the employees of CHARS are deemed to be employees as defined in that
subsection.

## [Offices and Meetings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-70033 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office and other offices

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** CHARS's head office is to
be in Cambridge Bay, Nunavut. The Board may establish other offices
elsewhere in Canada that it considers necessary for carrying out CHARS's
purpose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the Board
  must meet at least three times in each year at the times and places
  that the Chairperson selects.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Northern meetings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At least one of the Board's meetings in each year
  must be held at CHARS's head office.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committees

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** The Board may appoint
advisory or other committees. Each committee must include at least one
person who is a member of the Board.

## [Bylaws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-70043 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bylaws

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The Board may make bylaws
for the administration, management and control of the property, business
and activities of CHARS, including bylaws

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the constitution of any committees
  appointed under section 27 and the roles and duties of those
  committees;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} fixing reasonable allowances or expenses for those
  appointed to a committee under section 27 who are not members of the
  Board; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the procedure at meetings of the Board and
  its committees.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-70049 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inventions

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 9 of the
[Public Servants Inventions Act](/eng/acts/P-32), the administration and
control of any invention made by an officer or employee of CHARS and
vested in Her Majesty by that Act, and any patent issued with respect to
the invention, are vested in CHARS.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Actions, suits or other
legal proceedings in respect of any right or obligation acquired or
incurred by CHARS on behalf of Her Majesty in right of Canada, whether
in its name or in the name of Her Majesty, may be brought or taken by or
against CHARS in the name of CHARS in any court that has jurisdiction
with respect to the subject-matter of the claim.

    extent as it could have been continued by or against the Commission.
