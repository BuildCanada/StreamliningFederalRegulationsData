## Veterans Insurance Act {#veterans-insurance-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"} 1970, c. V-3
:::

An Act to provide for the insurance of veterans
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Veterans Insurance Act](/eng/acts/V-1.4).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 279, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act

  [[amount of insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[amount of insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount
      stated as such in the contract of insurance; ([montant de
      l'assurance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[brother]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[brother]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a half-brother and
      [[sister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a
      half-sister; ([frère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} et
      [soeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a legally adopted child,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a stepchild who is designated by the insured as
        a beneficiary and in such designation is described either by
        name or as a stepchild, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a child acknowledged or maintained by the
        insured or for whom the insured has been judicially ordered to
        provide support; ([enfant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an
      individual, means a person who is cohabiting with the individual
      in a conjugal relationship, having so cohabited for a period of at
      least one year; ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[discharge from service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[discharge from service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any

      lang="fr"})

  [[grandchild]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[grandchild]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a child as above
      defined of a child as above
      defined; ([petit-fils]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou
      [petite-fille]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[insured]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[insured]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person with whom the
      Minister enters into a contract of insurance under this
      Act; ([assuré]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou [personne
      assurée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Veterans
      Affairs or such other Minister as the Governor in Council may from
      time to time determine; ([Ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[parent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[parent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a father, mother,
      grandfather, grandmother, stepfather, stepmother, foster-father,

      partner of the insured; ([parent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} service in the naval, army or air forces of
        Canada by any person while in receipt of either active service
        rates of pay or of Permanent Force rates of pay, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} active service in the naval, army or air forces
        of His Majesty by any person domiciled in Canada at the
        commencement thereof; ([service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person, male or female,
      who was engaged in service during the war and who has been granted
      discharge from such service; ([ancien combattant]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[war]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[war]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the war that commenced in

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Newfoundland veterans

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (a) of the definition
  [[service]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection (1), service by a
  person in the naval or army forces of Newfoundland and service by a
  person recruited in Newfoundland in any naval, army or air forces
  raised in Newfoundland by or on behalf of the United Kingdom, shall be
  deemed to be service in the naval, army or air forces of Canada and,
  for the purposes of paragraph (b) of that definition, domicile in
  Newfoundland shall be deemed to be domicile in Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligible persons and amount of contract

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, without requiring medical examination or other evidence
  of insurability, enter into a contract of insurance that provides for
  the payment in the event of the death of the insured of five hundred
  dollars or any multiple thereof not exceeding ten thousand dollars,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with a veteran, on or before the 31st day of
    October 1968, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with any of the following persons, on or before the
    31st day of October 1968,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the widow or widower of a veteran, if the
      Minister has not entered into a contract of insurance with the
      veteran,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the widow or widower of a person who died in
      service during the war,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a member of the regular force who has not been
      released from such force and who was engaged in service during the
      war,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a merchant seaman who received or was eligible
      to receive a bonus pursuant to The Merchant Seamen Special Bonus
      Order, or a seaman who received or was eligible to receive a War
      Service Bonus pursuant to The Merchant Seamen War Service Bonus
      Order, 1944, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any other person who is, under the [Pension
      Act](/eng/acts/P-6), in receipt of a disability pension relating
      to the war.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a contract of insurance is entered into under
  this Act with a person whose life is insured under The Returned
  Soldiers' Insurance Act, the amount of insurance under such contract
  shall be limited so that the aggregate amount of insurance in force on
  his life under The Returned Soldiers' Insurance Act and this Act does
  not exceed ten thousand dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mode of payment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Payment under a contract of insurance shall be made
  on the death of the insured in an amount that, at the option of the
  insured, is the full amount of insurance or any lesser amount, and the
  remainder, if any, or the portion thereof to which any beneficiary is
  entitled shall, at the option of the insured, be payable as

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an annuity certain for five, ten, fifteen or twenty
    years;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a life annuity; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an annuity guaranteed for five, ten, fifteen or
    twenty years and payable thereafter as long as the beneficiary may
    live.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Variation by insured

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any option as to the mode of payment chosen by the
  insured in his application for insurance may subsequently be varied by
  declaration of the insured.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Variation by beneficiary

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The option as to mode of payment chosen by the
  insured may after the death of the insured be varied by the
  beneficiary with the consent of the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of annuity in a lump sum or
  otherwise

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything in this Act, where, at the
  death of the insured, any insurance money is being paid or is to be
  paid as an annuity to any beneficiary, such money shall, upon the
  request of the beneficiary, be paid in a lump sum or in any other
  manner provided for in subsection (3) as the beneficiary may request.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. V-3, s. 3
- 1974-75-76, c. 92, s. 2
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, 1974-75-76, c.
92, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disability, waiver of premiums

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  contract may provide that if, before attaining the age of sixty years,
  the insured becomes totally and permanently disabled so that he is
  thereby rendered incapable of pursuing continuously any substantially
  gainful occupation, and if such disability is not deemed to be
  attributable to his service to such an extent as to entitle him to
  pension on the grounds of total disability under the [Pension
  Act](/eng/acts/P-6), the premiums thereafter falling due under the
  contract, during the continuance of such disability, shall be waived.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption of disablement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The insured shall, for the purposes of this section,
  be deemed to be totally and permanently disabled where his total
  disability has existed continuously for a period of at least one year.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 279, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Who are the beneficiaries

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the insured has a spouse, common-law partner or children, the
  beneficiary is, subject to subsections (4) and (5), the spouse,
  common-law partner or children of the insured, or some one or more of
  such persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Who are the beneficiaries

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the insured has no spouse, common-law partner
  or children, the beneficiary is, subject to subsections (4) and (5)
  and section 7, the future spouse, future common-law partner or future
  children of the insured, or some one or more of such persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Apportionment of insurance money

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the insured designates more than one
  beneficiary, the insured may apportion, and may at any time
  re-apportion, the insurance money between or among them as he sees
  fit, and in default of any such apportionment the insurance money
  shall be paid to the designated beneficiaries surviving the insured in
  equal shares.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to new designation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a designated beneficiary dies in the lifetime
  of the insured, the insured may, subject to subsections (1) and (2),
  designate a beneficiary or beneficiaries to whom the share formerly
  apportioned to the deceased beneficiary shall be paid, and in default
  of any such designation, the said share shall be divided equally among
  the surviving designated beneficiaries, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If no designated beneficiary

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the insured does not designate a beneficiary,
  or where all of the beneficiaries designated by the insured die within
  the insured's lifetime, the insurance money shall be paid to the
  spouse, the common-law partner and the children of the insured in
  equal shares, and if the insured survives the spouse, the common-law
  partner and all the children of the insured, and there is no
  contingent beneficiary within the meaning of section 7 surviving the
  insured, the insurance money shall be paid, as it falls due or
  otherwise as the Minister may determine, to the estate or succession
  of the insured.

- [(6) and (7)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2000, c. 12, s.
  304\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. V-3, s. 6
- 1974-75-76, c. 92, s. 4
- 1990, c. 43, s. 57
- 2000, c. 12, s. 304
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of contingent beneficiaries

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  insured may designate as a contingent beneficiary a grandchild,
  parent, brother, or sister of the insured, or such other person as may
  by regulation be prescribed for the purposes of this section, to whom
  the insurance money or any portion thereof shall be paid in the event
  that the insured at the time of his death has no spouse, common-law
  partner or children.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment to contingent beneficiaries or
  to estate or succession

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the insured survives the spouse, the common-law
  partner and all the children of the insured, the insurance money shall
  be paid to the contingent beneficiary or beneficiaries, if any, but in
  default of the designation of a contingent beneficiary, or in the
  event of the death of all the contingent beneficiaries within the
  lifetime of the insured, the insurance money shall be paid, as it
  falls due or otherwise as the Minister may determine, to the estate or
  succession of the insured.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Apportionment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the insured designates more than one contingent
  beneficiary, the insured may apportion, and may at any time
  re-apportion, the insurance money among them as he sees fit, and in
  default of any such apportionment the insurance money shall be paid to
  the contingent beneficiaries surviving the insured in equal shares.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Death of designated beneficiary

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a contingent beneficiary dies in the lifetime
  of the insured, the insured may, subject to subsection (1), designate
  a contingent beneficiary or beneficiaries to whom the share formerly
  apportioned to the deceased contingent beneficiary shall be paid, and
  in default of any such designation, the said share shall be divided
  equally among the contingent beneficiaries, if any, surviving the
  insured.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. V-3, s. 7
- 2000, c. 12, ss. 306(E), 307(F), 309, 310(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of beneficiaries

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Act, the
insured may at any time change the beneficiary or beneficiaries, or the
contingent beneficiary or contingent beneficiaries, theretofore
designated by the insured.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 279, s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision by contract for change of
beneficiary

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Any designation of a
beneficiary or of a contingent beneficiary, or any variation in the
option as to the mode of payment or any apportionment of insurance
money, other than is made by the insured in the application for the
insurance, may be made in accordance with the provisions of the contract
of insurance in that behalf.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 279, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms for payment of premiums

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  contract of insurance may provide for payment of premiums during the
  lifetime of the insured for a period of ten, fifteen or twenty years
  or until the anniversary of the policy nearest the sixty-fifth or
  eighty-fifth birthday of the insured.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Premiums as provided in Schedule A

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The premiums payable under the various plans of
  contract shall be as provided for in Schedule A.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 279, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to insure

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may refuse
to enter into a contract of insurance in any case where there are in his
opinion sufficient grounds for so doing but, in the exercise of the
powers conferred upon him by this section, the Minister shall be
governed by Schedule B and he may require for this purpose that the
applicant shall submit himself to medical examination or shall furnish
such other information as the Minister may require.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 279, s. 13
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insurance money unassignable etc.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The insurance money
payable under the contract of insurance is unassignable and is not
subject to the claims of creditors of the insured or of the beneficiary.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 279, s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Death of applicant before completion of
contract

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Where an application for
insurance is made and the applicant dies before the contract of
insurance is entered into, the contract shall be deemed to have been
entered into if the initial premium is paid and the application is one
that would have been approved if the applicant had not died.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 279, s. 15
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment to estate or succession of
deceased beneficiary

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Where a beneficiary or
contingent beneficiary survives the insured but dies before receiving
all of the insurance money to which under the contract of insurance such
beneficiary or contingent beneficiary is entitled, the remaining unpaid
money shall be paid, as it falls due or otherwise as the Minister may
determine, to the estate or succession of the deceased beneficiary or
deceased contingent beneficiary.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. V-3, s. 14
- 2000, c. 12, s. 310(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When insured treated as if dead

- **[[14.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the death of the insured has not been proved in accordance with the
  regulations made under paragraph 16(b) but the Minister is satisfied,
  after efforts satisfactory to the Minister have been made, that the
  insured has died or cannot be located, the Minister shall, subject to
  this section, apply this Act as if the insured had died on a date
  specified by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[Ministerial
  payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (3), [[Ministerial
  payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an amount of insurance money that
  is payable by virtue of the operation of subsection (1) and would not
  be payable in the absence of its operation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement to repay Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not make a Ministerial payment
  unless the person to whom that payment is to be made agrees in
  writing, in such form as is prescribed by the Minister, to repay to
  the Minister the amount of that payment in the event that the insured
  referred to in subsection (1) is subsequently determined by the
  Minister to be alive.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 34, s. 58
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When beneficiary or contingent
beneficiary treated as if dead

- **[[14.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, after the insured's death, the
  death of a beneficiary or contingent beneficiary has not been proved
  in accordance with the regulations made under paragraph 16(b) but the
  Minister is satisfied, after efforts satisfactory to the Minister have
  been made, that that beneficiary or contingent beneficiary died within
  the insured's lifetime or cannot be located, the Minister shall,
  subject to this section, apply this Act as if that beneficiary or
  contingent beneficiary had died within the insured's lifetime.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[Ministerial
  payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (3), [[Ministerial
  payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an amount of insurance money that
  is payable by virtue of the operation of subsection (1) and would not
  be payable in the absence of its operation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement to repay Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not make a Ministerial payment
  unless the person to whom that payment is to be made agrees in
  writing, in such form as is prescribed by the Minister, to repay to
  the Minister the amount of that payment in the event that the
  beneficiary or contingent beneficiary referred to in subsection (1) is
  subsequently determined by the Minister to be alive.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 34, s. 58
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insured or beneficiary may be Member of
Parliament

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding the
Senate and House of Commons Act, or any other law, no person, by reason
only of his entering into a contract of insurance or receiving a benefit
under this Act, is liable to any forfeiture or penalty imposed by the
Senate and House of Commons Act or disqualified as a member of the House
of Commons or incapable of being elected to, or of sitting or voting in
the House of Commons.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 279, s. 17
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the form of contracts and such other
  forms as he may consider necessary under this Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the mode of proving the age, identity and
  existence or death of persons;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the mode of paying money under contracts
  of insurance;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for dispensing with the production of letters probate
  or letters of administration, either generally or in any particular
  case or class of cases;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the accounts to be kept and their
  management;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} respecting the cases or classes of cases in which a
  contract of insurance may be surrendered and a cash surrender value
  paid therefor, or a paid-up contract of insurance issued instead
  thereof, and for prescribing the manner in which such cash surrender
  value or amount of paid-up insurance shall be determined;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the cases, not otherwise provided for in
  this Act, in which a person not originally named as, but who is
  eligible under this Act to be a beneficiary, may be made a
  beneficiary;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the cases, not otherwise provided for in
  this Act, in which an apportionment of the insurance money may be made
  or varied;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the class or classes of persons other
  than those mentioned in sections 6 and 7 who are entitled to be
  beneficiaries;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the cases in which a dependant, other
  than the spouse, common-law partner or child of the insured, may be
  named as a beneficiary under the contract;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} prescribing, in cases not otherwise provided for by
  the contract of insurance or by declaration or by this Act, the person
  or persons entitled to whom the instalments, if any, of insurance
  money remaining unpaid at the death of a beneficiary shall be paid;
  and

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} for any other purpose for which it is deemed
  expedient to make regulations in order to carry this Act into effect.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. V-3, s. 16
- 2000, c. 12, s. 308
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}C.R.F.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The moneys received under
this Act form part of the Consolidated Revenue Fund, and the moneys
payable under this Act are payable out of the said Fund.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 279, s. 19
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual statement

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall cause a statement to be prepared within three months
  after the end of each fiscal year showing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the premiums received during the fiscal year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the insurance moneys paid during the fiscal year;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number and amount of contracts entered into
    during the fiscal year;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number and amount of contracts in force at the
    end of the fiscal year; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} such further information as the Minister deems
    advisable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}To be laid before Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every such statement shall be laid before Parliament
  as soon as may be after it is prepared.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1952, c. 279, s. 20
:::

## [SCHEDULE B]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-462708 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  CLASS I --- Applicants who are not seriously ill.
  :::
  :::::

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} An applicant with dependants, ill with a
    pensionable disability.

    Application is to be accepted.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} An applicant with dependants, ill with a disability
    that is not pensionable.

    Application is to be accepted.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} An applicant without dependants, ill with a
    pensionable disability.

    Application is to be accepted.

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} An applicant without dependants, ill with a
    disability that is not pensionable.

    Application is to be accepted.

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  CLASS II --- Applicants who are seriously ill.
  :::
  :::::

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} An applicant with dependants, seriously ill with a
    pensionable disability.

    Application is to be accepted.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} An applicant with dependants, seriously ill with a
    disability that is not pensionable.

    Application is to be refused.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} An applicant without dependants, seriously ill with
    a pensionable disability.

    Application is to be refused.

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} An applicant without dependants, seriously ill with
    a disability that is not pensionable.

    Application is to be refused.

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  CLASS III --- Applications from persons in so serious a condition of
  health that they have no reasonable expectation of life.
  :::
  :::::

  Applications are to be refused.

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  CLASS IV --- General.
  :::
  :::::

  In cases where an applicant with or without dependants whose health
  has become impaired as a result of immoral conduct prior to
  enlistment, or as a result of refusal of treatment for such condition
  during service or after discharge from service.

  Applications are to be refused.
