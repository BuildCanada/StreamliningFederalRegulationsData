## Indian Lands Agreement (1986) Act {#indian-lands-agreement-1986-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1988, c. 39
:::

Assented to 1988-07-28

An Act to provide for the implementation of an agreement respecting
Indian lands in Ontario
::::

::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Indian Lands Agreement (1986) Act](/eng/acts/I-6.7).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the agreement between the
    Government of Canada and the Government of Ontario set out in the
    schedule; ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[specific agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[specific agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a specific
    agreement referred to in the agreement, entered into by the
    Government of Canada, the Government of Ontario and a band of
    Indians or group of bands of Indians pursuant to the
    agreement. ([accord particulier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The agreement shall be
binding on Her Majesty in right of Canada and the Governor in Council is
hereby authorized to carry out the provisions of the agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) to apply

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5) shall continue to apply to reserve lands and
surrendered lands that are the subject of a specific agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations respecting the procedures to be adopted for the
confirmation of a specific agreement under paragraph 10(a) of the
agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specific agreements

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  band confirms a specific agreement pursuant to paragraph 10(a) or (b)
  of the agreement, the specific agreement and any notification from the
  council of the band referred to in paragraph 10(b) of the agreement
  bind all existing and future members of the band and the council of
  the band and its successors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of confirmation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, where a specific agreement is
  confirmed by order of the Governor in Council, by order of the
  Lieutenant Governor of Ontario in Council and by the band to which the
  specific agreement relates, the specific agreement shall be binding on
  Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#I-6.7_en_1){#I-6.7_en_1-ID0EBCA}[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7
.sectionLabel}** This Act or any provision thereof shall come into force
on a day or days to be fixed by proclamation.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[The 1986 Indian Lands Agreement]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-333009 .scheduleLabel}
:::

The Agreement witnesseth that the parties hereto have agreed as follows:

- [1]{.lawlabel} Definitions

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[Band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[Council of the
    Band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[Surrender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
    [[Custom]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, and [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    have the same meaning as those words in the [Indian
    Act](/eng/acts/I-5), R.S.C. 1970, c. I-6, as the same may be amended
    from time to time;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [[land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any interest
    in land;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[minerals]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes gold,
    silver and all other metals, precious and base, and coal, natural
    gas, oil, salt, sand and gravel;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[1924 Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    agreement between Canada and Ontario dated March 24, 1924, and the
    statutes confirming it, i.e., Statutes of Canada, 14-15 George V,
    Chapter 48, and Statutes of Ontario, 14 George V, Chapter 15.

- [2]{.lawlabel} Ontario, Canada, and any band or group of bands may
  enter into specific agreements. Any one or more Bands may enter into
  one or more specific agreements.

- [3]{.lawlabel} A specific agreement may be entered into with respect
  to any matter or question relating to lands or natural resources,
  including any of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any matter dealt with in the 1924 Agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} administration and control;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the exercise, allocation or transfer or disposal of
    any interests in lands or natural resources;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} minerals, mineral rights and royalties, and the
    disposition or taxation of any of them;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} hydro powers;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} disposition of lands or natural resources;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} consequences of extinction or enfranchisement of a
    band;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} disposition of any monies;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the non-applicability of any section or sections of
    the 1924 Agreement;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} any other provision required for the implementation
    of a specific agreement.

- [4]{.lawlabel} The provisions of any specific agreement shall have
  effect upon confirmation. In the event of any inconsistency with the
  1924 Agreement, the specific agreement shall supersede.

- [5]{.lawlabel} Neither this Agreement nor any specific agreement shall
  affect the validity of any treaty or surrender.

- [6]{.lawlabel} Canada and Ontario may enter into an agreement or
  agreements for the confirmation of patents issued or other
  dispositions of land by the other with respect to land, but no such
  agreement or confirmation shall in any way affect the rights of any
  band or the recourse which any band would, absent such agreement, have
  against any person or land, including the Crown and Crown lands.

- [7]{.lawlabel} If Canada has collected money or collects money on
  behalf of any band or bands pursuant to sales or other dispositions of
  land or interests in land, Ontario acknowledges that Canada may
  continue to administer that money for the use and benefit of the band
  or bands, but in no case shall money collected by Canada expressly on
  behalf of Ontario be deemed to be money collected by Canada on behalf
  of a band or bands.

- [8]{.lawlabel} This Agreement shall come into force when it is
  confirmed by the Parliament of Canada and the Legislature of Ontario
  and such confirmations come into force.

- [9]{.lawlabel} A specific agreement shall come into force when it is
  confirmed by Orders in Council of both Canada and Ontario and is
  confirmed by the band.

- [10]{.lawlabel} Confirmation by a band of a specific agreement shall
  take place

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by a Referendum conducted pursuant to regulations
    made by the Governor General in Council under the authority of the
    Act of Parliament implementing this agreement, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to the band's custom or constitution,
    provided that the Council of the band gives written notification to
    the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development and to the
    Minister of Natural Resources for Ontario that confirmation took
    place pursuant to the band's custom or constitution, as the case may
    be.

- [11]{.lawlabel} Where a specific agreement affects or deals with
  lands, lands affected shall be described in a schedule to the specific
  agreement.

- [12]{.lawlabel} No specific agreement entered into by any band shall
  be binding upon any other band or have any effect on any other band
  unless it has been confirmed by that other band.

- [13]{.lawlabel} A specific agreement may be amended by the parties or
  their successors in the same manner as it was originally made.
