## Apprentice Loans Act {#apprentice-loans-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2014, c. 20, s. 483
:::

Assented to 2014-06-19

An Act respecting the making of loans to apprentices

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 483 of chapter 20 of the Statutes of Canada, 2014,
in force January 2, 2015, see SI/2014-100.\]
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-5003 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Apprentice Loans Act](/eng/acts/A-11.9).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-5006 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[eligible trade]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[eligible trade]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a trade listed in the
      schedule to the regulations. ([métier admissible]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Employment
      and Social Development. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[technical training provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[technical training provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      institution that is approved by a province for the purpose of
      providing technical training. ([fournisseur de formation
      technique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other definitions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Act, the words and expressions
  [[apprentice]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[apprentice
  loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
  [[eligible apprentice]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[technical
  training]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[technical training
  period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have the meanings assigned by the
  regulations.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-5015 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to assist eligible apprentices by making loans available to them.

## [Apprentice Loans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-5018 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements with eligible apprentices

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Minister, or any person authorized by order of
  the Minister to act on the Minister's behalf, may enter into an
  agreement with any eligible apprentice who is registered in an
  eligible trade for the purpose of making an apprentice loan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial terms and conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any terms and conditions in the agreement that could
  have a financial impact on Her Majesty in right of Canada are subject
  to approval by the Governor in Council, on the recommendation of the
  Minister with the concurrence of the Minister of Finance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements or arrangements with service
providers

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may enter into an agreement or arrangement with any
  corporation incorporated under the laws of Canada or a province and
  carrying on business in Canada (in this section referred to as a
  "service provider"), respecting the administration of apprentice loans
  made by the Minister. An agreement may provide for, but is not limited
  to, any matter referred to in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Receipt and Deposit of Public Money
  Regulations, 1997](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-128)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite section 3 of the [Receipt and Deposit of
  Public Money Regulations, 1997](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-128), the
  portion of the following money that is public money and is collected
  or received electronically by a service provider that has entered into
  an agreement under subsection (1) must be paid to the credit of the
  Receiver General by depositing it, within two business days after the
  day on which it is collected or received, in an account established
  under subsection 17(2) of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} money collected or received as repayment of an
    apprentice loan or as payment of interest owing on that loan; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} interest received by the service provider on the
    money referred to in paragraph (a).

- Definition of [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means a day other than a Saturday or a holiday.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension or denial of apprentice loans

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may suspend or
deny the making of apprentice loans to all those who are eligible
apprentices participating in technical training provided by a technical
training provider if the Minister is satisfied that there are compelling
reasons to believe that the making of the apprentice loans would

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} facilitate the commission by the technical training
  provider of an offence under this Act or any other Act of Parliament;
  or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} expose the eligible apprentices or Her Majesty in
  right of Canada to significant financial risk.

## [Special Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-5037 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special payments

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may pay a
province the amount that is determined in accordance with the
regulations if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister determines that apprentices registered
  with the province are unable to enter into agreements for apprentice
  loans under section 4;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the province has in place a program providing for
  financial assistance to apprentices; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister considers that the purpose of the
  program is substantially similar to the purpose of this Act.

## [Interest and Periods of Deferral]{.HTitleText1} {#h-5043 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No accrual of interest

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Beginning on April 1, 2023, no
  interest is payable by a borrower on an apprentice loan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, nothing in this section
  relieves a borrower from liability for interest that accrued before
  April 1, 2023 on an apprentice loan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deferral period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No amount on account of principal or interest in
  respect of an apprentice loan is required to be paid by the borrower
  until the end of the prescribed period.

## [Period --- March 30, 2020 to September 30, 2020]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1241588 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of interest and payments

**[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** During the period that
begins on March 30, 2020 and ends on September 30, 2020,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} no interest is payable by a borrower on an apprentice
  loan; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} no amount on account of principal and interest in
  respect of an apprentice loan is required to be paid by a borrower.

## [Death or Disability of Borrower]{.HTitleText1} {#h-5049 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Death of borrower

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** All obligations of a
borrower in respect of an apprentice loan terminate if the borrower
dies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Severe permanent disability

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  obligations of a borrower in respect of an apprentice loan terminate
  if the Minister is satisfied, on the basis of information specified by
  the Minister and provided by or on behalf of the borrower, that the
  borrower, by reason of the borrower's severe permanent disability, is
  unable to repay the loan and will never be able to repay it.

- Definition of [[severe permanent disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, the expression [[severe permanent
  disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by the
  regulations.

## [Maximum Amount of Outstanding Apprentice Loans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-5057 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amount

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The aggregate amount of
apprentice loans made under this Act that are outstanding may not exceed
the prescribed amount.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-5060 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} defining the words and expressions referred to in
    subsections 2(2), 10(2) and 17(7);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establishing a schedule that sets out a list of
    eligible trades, including eligible trades by province;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the circumstances in which a borrower
    is or ceases to be an eligible apprentice;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} providing for the conditions to be met before a
    disbursement in respect of an apprentice loan may be made;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the manner of determining the amount
    that may be paid to a province under section 7;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2022, c. 19, s. 166\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the circumstances under which an
    apprentice loan may be denied to an eligible apprentice;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the maximum amount of an apprentice
    loan that may be made to an eligible apprentice for each technical
    training period;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the maximum period that may elapse
    after which, despite anything in this Act, the principal amount of
    an apprentice loan and interest on that amount begin to be payable
    by the borrower;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the maximum number of technical
    training periods for which an apprentice is eligible to be advanced
    an apprentice loan;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2022, c. 19, s. 166\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} providing for repayment of apprentice loans by
    borrowers or classes of borrowers on an income-contingent basis;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} prescribing information to be included in any form
    or document referred to in section 13 in addition to any information
    that is otherwise required under this Act to be included in the form
    or document;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} providing for the times referred to in paragraph
    15(a);

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} providing for the form and manner in which
    information referred to in paragraph 15(b) is to be provided;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} providing for the measures referred to in
    subsection 20(1);

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} providing for the period after which the Minister
    may no longer take a measure referred to in paragraph (p);

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that, by this Act, is to be
    prescribed by the regulations; and

  - [(s)]{.lawlabel} generally, for carrying into effect the purposes
    and provisions of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum aggregate amount of outstanding
  apprentice loans

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the Minister's recommendation with the Minister of
  Finance's concurrence, the Governor in Council may make regulations,
  for the purposes of section 11,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the aggregate amount of outstanding
    apprentice loans that may not be exceeded; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the apprentice loans that are to be
    considered for the purposes of determining, at a given time, the
    aggregate amount of outstanding apprentice loans.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligible trades

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by regulation, amend the schedule
  to the regulations by adding a trade to it or removing a trade from
  it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Externally produced material

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under this section may incorporate
  by reference documents produced by a person or body other than the
  Minister, including by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an organization established for the purpose of
    writing standards, including an organization accredited by the
    Standards Council of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an industrial or trade organization; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a government.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reproduced or translated material

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under this section may incorporate
  by reference documents that the Minister reproduces or translates from
  documents produced by a body or person other than the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with any adaptations of form and reference that
    will facilitate their incorporation into the regulation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a form that sets out only the parts of them that
    apply for the purposes of the regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jointly produced documents

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under this section may incorporate
  by reference documents that the Minister produces jointly with another
  government for the purpose of harmonizing the regulation with other
  laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Internally produced standards

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under this section may incorporate
  by reference technical or explanatory documents that the Minister
  produces, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} specifications, classifications or other
    information of a technical nature; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} test methods, procedures, operational standards,
    safety standards or performance standards of a technical nature.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation as amended from time to
  time

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Documents may be incorporated by reference as amended
  from time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (4) to (8) are for greater certainty and
  do not limit any authority to make regulations incorporating material
  by reference that exists apart from those subsections.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessibility

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must ensure that any document that is
  incorporated by reference in the regulation is accessible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} A person is not liable to be found guilty of an
  offence for any contravention in respect of which a document that is
  incorporated by reference in the regulation is relevant unless, at the
  time of the alleged contravention, the document was accessible as
  required by subsection (10) or it was otherwise accessible to the
  person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration and publication not
  required

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a document that is
  incorporated by reference in the regulation is not required to be
  transmitted for registration or published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) under the [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22) by reason only that it is incorporated by
  reference.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-5114 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forms and documents

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A form or other document
that is to be used in connection with making apprentice loans, or to be
otherwise used for the effective operation of this Act, must be either
determined by the Minister or approved by the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of recovery by Minister

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** An apprentice loan that
is made to a borrower who is not of full age and any interest on the
loan are recoverable by the Minister from the borrower as though the
borrower had been of full age at the time the agreement was entered
into.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waiver

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** On application by an
eligible apprentice or a borrower, the Minister may, to avoid undue
hardship to the apprentice or borrower, waive

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a requirement of the regulations with respect to the
  times within which information in respect of the eligible apprentice
  or borrower is to be provided; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a requirement of the regulations with respect to the
  form or manner in which information in respect of the eligible
  apprentice or borrower is to be provided, or a requirement determined
  or approved by the Minister with respect to a form or other document
  in which such information is to be provided.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Apprentice loan denied due to error

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** If the Minister is
satisfied that, as a result of an error made in the administration of
this Act or the regulations, a person was denied an apprentice loan to
which the person would have been entitled, the Minister may take
remedial action to place the person in the position that he or she would
have been in under this Act had the error not been made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation or prescription period

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section, no action
  or proceedings may be taken to recover money owing under this Act
  after the end of the six-year period that begins on the day on which
  the money becomes due and payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction and set-off

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Money owing by a person under this Act may be
  recovered at any time by way of deduction from, set-off against or, in
  Quebec, compensation against any sum of money that may be due or
  payable by Her Majesty in right of Canada to the person or their
  estate or succession.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment of liability

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person acknowledges liability for money owing
  under this Act, the time during which the limitation or prescription
  period has run before the acknowledgment of liability does not count
  in the calculation of that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment of liability after end
  of limitation or prescription period

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a person acknowledges liability for money owing
  under this Act after the end of the limitation or prescription period,
  an action or proceedings to recover the money may, subject to
  subsections (3) and (5), be brought within six years after the date of
  the acknowledgment of liability.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation or prescription period
  suspended

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The running of a limitation or prescription period in
  respect of an apprentice loan is suspended during any period in which
  it is prohibited to commence or continue an action or other
  proceedings against the borrower to recover money owing under the
  loan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement proceedings

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply in respect of an action
  or proceedings relating to the execution, renewal or enforcement of a
  judgment.

- Definition of [[acknowledgement of liability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In this section, the expression [[acknowledgement of
  liability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by the
  regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to provide information or
documents

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of verifying compliance or preventing non-compliance with
  this Act, the Minister may, by notice served personally or by
  confirmed delivery service, require any person to whom an apprentice
  loan has been made to provide the Minister, within the time and in the
  manner that are stipulated in the notice, with any information or
  document that is in their possession or to which they could reasonably
  be expected to have access.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies as evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a document is provided in accordance with
  subsection (1), the Minister may make, or cause to be made, one or
  more certified copies of it and any of those copies is evidence of the
  nature and content of the original document and has the same probative
  force as the original document would have if it were proven in the
  ordinary way.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False statement or information

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who, in respect of an apprentice loan, knowingly makes any
  false statement or misrepresentation, including by omission, in an
  application or other document or knowingly provides any false or
  misleading information, including by omission, is guilty of an offence
  and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding \$1,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A prosecution for an offence under this Act may not
  be instituted later than six years after the time when the subject
  matter of the complaint arose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrative measures

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person, in respect of an apprentice loan, knowingly makes any false
  statement or misrepresentation, including by omission, in an
  application or other document or knowingly provides any false or
  misleading information, including by omission, the Minister may take
  any measure provided for in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, the Minister may not take any measure under
  subsection (1) without having given the person 60 days' notice of the
  Minister's intention to take it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submissions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person may make submissions to the Minister in
  respect of the measure at any time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rescission or modification of measure

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may rescind or modify a measure taken
  under subsection (1) if new facts are presented or the Minister
  considers that the measure was taken without knowledge of a material
  fact or on the basis of a mistake concerning one.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to enter into agreements and
arrangements

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter into agreements or arrangements with any
  department, board or agency of the Government of Canada or any other
  public or private organization or agency to assist the Minister in
  carrying out the purposes and provisions of this Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} with the approval of the Governor in Council, enter
  into agreements or arrangements with the government of any province to
  facilitate the administration or enforcement of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment out of C.R.F.

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Any amount payable by the
Minister under this Act, the regulations or an agreement or arrangement
entered into under this Act, including any apprentice loans to be made
by the Minister, is to be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2022, c. 19, s. 167

  - ###### Definitions {#definitions .MarginalNote}

    - [167]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply
      in this section.

      [[apprentice loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

      :   [[apprentice loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
          as in subsection 1(1) of the [Apprentice Loans
          Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-255). ([prêt aux
          apprentis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

      [[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

      :   [[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
          subsection 1(1) of the [Apprentice Loans
          Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-255). ([emprunteur]{.DefinedTermLink
          lang="fr"})

      [[transition period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

      :   [[transition period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period
          beginning on April 1, 2023 and ending on the day on which a
          regulation amending or repealing section 4 of the [Apprentice
          Loans Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-255) comes into
          force. ([période de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    - ###### Repayment of principal and interest {#repayment-of-principal-and-interest .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} During the transition period, a borrower must
      begin to repay the principal amount of an apprentice loan made to
      them, and any interest, on the last day of the month in which
      interest would have started accruing under the [Apprentice Loans
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-255) but for subsection
      8(1) of the [Apprentice Loans Act](/eng/acts/A-11.9), as enacted
      by section 164.
