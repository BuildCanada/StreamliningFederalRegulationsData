## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Salaries]{.HTitleText1} {#h-443957 .Part}

### [Consolidated Revenue Fund]{.HTitleText2} {#h-443958 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salaries payable out of C.R.F.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The salaries mentioned in
this Act are payable yearly, and pro rata for any period less than a
year, out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. S-2, s. 2

### [Salary of Lieutenant Governors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-443963 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salaries of lieutenant governors from
April 1, 1985 to December 31, 1985

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  salary of the lieutenant governor of each province for the period from
  April 1, 1985 to December 31, 1985 is \$69,000 per annum.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Base for 1986 indexation

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (2) and (3), the
  salary annexed to the office of lieutenant governor for the 1985
  calendar year shall be deemed to be \$69,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual adjustment of salary

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the 1984 calendar year and for each calendar year
  thereafter, the salary of the lieutenant governor of each province
  shall be the amount obtained by multiplying the salary annexed to that
  office for the calendar year immediately preceding the calendar year
  in respect of which the salary is to be determined by the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the percentage that the Industrial Aggregate for
    the first adjustment year is of the Industrial Aggregate for the
    second adjustment year, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one hundred and seven per cent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of certain expressions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to any calendar year in respect of
    which the salary is to be determined,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the "first adjustment year" is the last twelve
      month period preceding the commencement of the calendar year in
      respect of which the salary is to be determined for which the
      Industrial Aggregate is available on the first day of the calendar
      year in respect of which the salary is to be determined, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the "second adjustment year" is the twelve month
      period immediately preceding the first adjustment year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the "Industrial Aggregate" for an adjustment year
    is the average weekly wages and salaries of the Industrial Aggregate
    in Canada for that year as published by Statistics Canada under the
    authority of the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rounding of amounts

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A salary determined for a calendar year pursuant to
  subsection (2) that is not a multiple of one hundred dollars shall be
  rounded to the closest multiple of one hundred dollars that is lower
  than the salary so determined.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salary for 1993, 1994, 1995 and 1996

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (2), for each of the 1993,
  1994, 1995 and 1996 calendar years, the salary of the lieutenant
  governor of each province shall be the same as the salary annexed to
  that office for the 1992 calendar year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Base for 1997 calculation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of calculating a salary under
  subsection (2) for the 1997 calendar year, the salary annexed to the
  office of lieutenant governor for the 1996 calendar year shall be
  deemed to be the salary payable under subsection (5).

::: HistoricalNote

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Support for Ministers Referred to in Paragraphs 4.1(3)(z.4) to (z.9)]{.HTitleText1} {#h-444083 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to designate department

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, by order, designate a department to provide
  support to a minister referred to in any of paragraphs 4.1(3)(z.4) to
  (z.9) in the carrying out of his or her responsibilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated department

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A minister in respect of whom a department is
  designated under subsection (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} may use the services and facilities of that
    department; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may delegate to officers or employees of that
    department any of the minister's powers, duties or functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Particular responsibilities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by order, designate a
  department to provide support to a minister referred to in any of
  paragraphs 4.1(3)(z.4) to (z.9) in the carrying out of the particular
  responsibilities that are specified in the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated department --- particular
  responsibilities

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A minister in respect of whom a department is
  designated under subsection (3)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} must use the services and facilities of that
    department when carrying out the particular responsibilities that
    are specified in the order; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may delegate to officers or employees of that
    department any of the minister's powers, duties or functions that
    are related to those particular responsibilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11)

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate Minister for a department that is
  designated under subsection (1) or (3) may delegate, to a minister in
  respect of whom that department is designated, any of the appropriate
  Minister's powers, duties or functions referred to in sections 33 and
  34, subsections 155(1) and (4) and sections 155.1 and 155.2 of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) or the power to write
  off debts in accordance with regulations made under subsection 25(1)
  of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Department of Public Works and
  Government Services Act](/eng/acts/P-38.2)

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister of Public Works and Government
  Services delegates, under subsection 8(1) of the [Department of Public
  Works and Government Services Act](/eng/acts/P-38.2), any of the
  Minister's powers, duties or functions under that Act to the
  appropriate Minister for a department that is designated under
  subsection (1) or (3), that appropriate Minister may subdelegate to a
  minister in respect of whom that department is designated any of the
  powers, duties or functions that were delegated to that appropriate
  Minister under that subsection 8(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in this section.

  [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
      as in section 2 of the [Financial Administration
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11). ([ministre compétent]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a department named in
      Schedule I to the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11),
      a division or branch of the federal public administration set out
      in column I of Schedule I.1 to that Act and a corporation named in
      Schedule II to that Act. ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

      paragraph (1)(a) is deemed to have elected immediately before the
      member's death to have the provisions referred to in
      subsection (1) apply to the member.

    - ###### Election irrevocable {#election-irrevocable .MarginalNote}

      [(4)]{.lawlabel} An election under paragraph (1)(a) is
      irrevocable.
