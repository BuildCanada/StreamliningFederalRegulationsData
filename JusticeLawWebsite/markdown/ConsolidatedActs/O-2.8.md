## Official Development Assistance Accountability Act {#official-development-assistance-accountability-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2008, c. 17
:::

Assented to 2008-05-29

An Act respecting the provision of official development assistance
abroad
::::

::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-384049 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Official Development Assistance Accountability
Act](/eng/acts/O-2.8).

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-384052 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  purpose of this Act is to ensure that all Canadian official
  development assistance abroad is provided with a central focus on
  poverty reduction and in a manner that is consistent with Canadian
  values, Canadian foreign policy, the principles of the Paris
  Declaration on Aid Effectiveness of March 2, 2005, sustainable
  development and democracy promotion and that promotes international
  human rights standards.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Official development assistance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Canadian official development assistance abroad shall
  be defined exclusively with regard to these values.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-384058 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Canadian values]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian values]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, amongst others,
    values of global citizenship, equity and environmental
    sustainability. ([valeurs canadiennes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[civil society organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[civil society organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    not-for-profit or charitable organization whose governing structure
    is independent of government direction, and includes, but is not
    limited to, registered charities, non-governmental development
    organizations, community groups, women's organizations, faith-based
    organizations, professional associations, trade unions, self-help
    groups, social movements, business associations, coalitions, human
    rights organizations and advocacy groups. ([organisme de la société
    civile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[competent minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[competent minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister for
    International Development, the Minister of Finance, the Minister of
    Foreign Affairs or any other minister who is providing official
    development assistance. ([ministre compétent]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[democracy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[democracy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes, but is not limited to,
    political and civil rights as defined by the International Covenant
    on Civil and Political Rights. ([démocratie]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[international agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any organization
    whose objectives include global poverty reduction or international
    human­itarian assistance. ([agence internationale]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[international assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means funding
    provided by government for international development, international
    financial institutions, global peace and security, crises overseas
    and international development research. ([aide
    internationale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[international human rights standards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international human rights standards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    standards that are based on international human rights conventions
    to which Canada is a party and on international customary
    law. ([normes internationales en matière de droits de la
    personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister for
    International Development or any other minister designated by the
    Governor in Council as the Minister for the purposes of this
    Act. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[official development assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[official development assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    2018, c. 27, s. 656\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
this Act, the Governor in Council may make regulations defining the
expression [[official development assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, in
which case, the Governor in Council must take into account, among other
things, the most recent definition of [[official development
assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} formulated by the Development
Assistance Committee of the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and
Development.

## [Official Development Assistance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-384074 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Official development assistance

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Official
  development assistance may be provided only if the competent minister
  is of the opinion that it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contributes to poverty reduction;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} takes into account the perspectives of the poor;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is consistent with international human rights
    standards.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disaster or other emergency occurring
  outside Canada

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), official
  development assistance may be provided for the purposes of alleviating
  the effects of a natural or artificial disaster or other emergency
  occurring outside Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The competent minister shall consult with
  governments, international agencies and Canadian civil society
  organizations at least once every two years, and shall take their
  views and recommendations into consideration when forming an opinion
  described in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of contribution

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In calculating Canada's official development
  assistance contribution in Government of Canada publications, the
  competent minister or the Governor in Council shall consider only
  official development assistance as defined by this Act that meets the
  criteria in subsections (1) and (1.1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No limit or restriction imposed

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act shall be construed so as to limit
  the funding or restrict the activities of the International
  Development Research Centre.

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-384089 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Parliament

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister or the competent minister shall cause to be laid before each
  House of Parliament within one year after the end of each fiscal year
  or, if either House is not sitting, on any of the first five days on
  which that House of Parliament is sitting, a report containing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total amount spent by the Government of Canada
    on official development assistance in the previous fiscal year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a summary of any activity or initiative taken under
    this Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a summary of Canada's activities under the [Bretton
    Woods and Related Agreements Act](/eng/acts/B-7) that have
    contributed to carrying out the purpose of this Act.

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2018, c. 27, s. 658\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2013, c. 33, s. 193\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statistical report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall issue a statistical report on the
  disbursement of official development assistance within one year after
  the end of each fiscal year.
