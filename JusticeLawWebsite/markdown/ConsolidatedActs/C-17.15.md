## Canadian Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act {#canadian-free-trade-agreement-implementation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2017, c. 33, s. 219
:::

Assented to 2017-12-14

An Act to implement the Canadian Free Trade Agreement

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 219 of chapter 33 of the Statutes of Canada, 2017,
deemed to have come into force on July 1, 2017.\]
:::
:::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the Government of Canada together with the Governments of
Ontario, Quebec, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Manitoba, British Columbia,
Prince Edward Island, Saskatchewan, Alberta, Newfoundland and Labrador,
Yukon, the Northwest Territories and Nunavut have entered into the
Canadian Free Trade Agreement;

And whereas the reduction or elimination of barriers to the free
movement of persons, goods, services and investments is essential for
the promotion of an open, efficient and stable domestic market to
enhance the competitiveness of Canadian business and to promote
sustainable and environmentally sound development;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69585 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canadian Free Trade Agreement Implementation
Act](/eng/acts/C-17.15).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69588 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Free Trade
    Agreement signed in 2017, as amended from time to
    time. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada who is designated as the Minister for the
    purposes of any provision of this Act under section
    8. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69593 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to implement the Agreement.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69596 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69599 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition of private cause of action
--- section 12 or 14

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  no cause of action and no proceedings of any kind are to be taken,
  without the consent of the Attorney General of Canada, to enforce or
  determine any right or obligation that is claimed or arises solely
  under or by virtue of section 12 or 14 or an order made under section
  12.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition of private cause of action
  --- Chapter Ten

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except to the extent provided in Chapter Ten of the
  Agreement, there is no cause of action and no proceedings of any kind
  are to be taken, without the consent of the Attorney General of
  Canada, to enforce or determine any right or obligation that is
  claimed or arises solely under or by virtue of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act, by specific mention or omission, limits in any
manner the right of Parliament to enact legislation to implement any
provision of the Agreement or fulfil any of the obligations of the
Government of Canada under the Agreement.

## [Implementation of Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69607 .Part}

### [Approval of Agreement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-69608 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement approved

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement is approved.

### [Designation of Minister]{.HTitleText2} {#h-69611 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order designating Minister

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate any member of the Queen's Privy Council for
Canada as the Minister for the purposes of any provision of this Act.

### [Orders Made Under Chapter Ten of Agreement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-69614 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders of Federal Court

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An order
  to pay a monetary penalty or tariff costs made under Chapter Ten of
  the Agreement may, for the purpose of its enforcement only, be made an
  order of the Federal Court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} To make the order an order of the Federal Court, the
  party to the Agreement or the person in favour of whom the order is
  made must file a certified copy of the order in the Registry of the
  Federal Court and, on filing, the order becomes an order of that
  Court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** An order that is made an
order of the Federal Court is enforceable in the same manner as any
other order of that Court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders final and binding

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An order that is made an
order of the Federal Court is final and binding and is not subject to
appeal to any court.

### [Orders of Governor in Council]{.HTitleText2} {#h-69624 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders: suspending benefits or imposing
retaliatory measures

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of suspending benefits of equivalent effect or imposing
  retaliatory measures of equivalent effect under Article 1013 of the
  Agreement, the Governor in Council may, by order, do any one or more
  of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend rights or privileges granted by the
    Government of Canada to a province under the Agreement or any
    federal law; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} modify or suspend the application of any federal
    law with respect to a province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[federal
  law]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means the whole or any portion of any Act of Parliament or any
  regulation, order or other instrument issued, made or established in
  the exercise of a power conferred by or under an Act of Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order subject to Chapter Ten

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the making of an order under
  subsection (1) must be in accordance with and subject to Chapter Ten
  of the Agreement and, in particular,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the requirements for standing set out in Article
    1004.8 of the Agreement; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the conditions and limitations set out in Articles
    1013.3, 1013.4 and 1013.10 of the Agreement.

### [Committee on Internal Trade]{.HTitleText2} {#h-69636 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of representative

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may appoint a Minister to be a representative on the Committee on
Internal Trade continued under Article 1100 of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual budget

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
must pay its portion of the Internal Trade Secretariat's annual
operating budget referred to in Article 1102.3 of the Agreement.

### [Panels, Committees and Working Groups]{.HTitleText2} {#h-69641 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rosters

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may appoint any person who meets the requirements set out in Annex
1005.2 of the Agreement to be on the rosters referred to in Article
1005.2 of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representatives on committees and working
groups

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may appoint
any person to be a representative of Canada on any committee or working
group referred to in the Agreement, other than the Committee on Internal
Trade continued under Article 1100 of the Agreement.

### [Appointments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-69646 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointments

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, by order, appoint any person to fill any
  position that may be necessary or advisable, in the opinion of the
  Governor in Council, for the carrying out of the purposes of the
  Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person appointed under subsection (1) may be paid
  the remuneration and expenses for their services that are fixed by the
  Governor in Council.
