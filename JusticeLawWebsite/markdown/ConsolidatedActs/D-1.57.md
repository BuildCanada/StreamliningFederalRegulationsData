## Dental Care Measures Act {#dental-care-measures-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2023, c. 26, s. 508
:::

Assented to 2023-06-22

An Act respecting certain matters in relation to the Canadian Dental
Care Plan

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 508 of chapter 26 of the Statutes of Canada, 2023,
in force on assent June 22, 2023.\]
:::
:::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1414149 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Dental Care Measures Act](/eng/acts/D-1.57).

## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1414152 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[Canadian Dental Care
Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act, [[Canadian
Dental Care Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the plan established under
the authority of the [Department of Health Act](/eng/acts/H-3.2) in
respect of dental service for individuals.

## [His Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1414155 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on His Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on His
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Reporting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1414158 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who is required to make an information return in prescribed
  form under subsection 200(1) of the [Income Tax
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._945) in respect of a payment
  referred to in paragraph 153(1)(a) or (b) of the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) must, in the information return for every person
  (in this section referred to as the "payee") in respect of whom the
  payment is made, indicate whether the payee or any of their family
  members were, on December 31 of the taxation year to which the
  information return relates, eligible in respect of the payee's
  employment or former employment, or that of the payee's spouse or
  common-law partner, to access any dental care insurance, or coverage
  of dental services of any kind, offered by the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) applies only in respect of the 2023
  and subsequent taxation years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[family
  member]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[family
  member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a payee, means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the payee's spouse or common-law partner;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a child of the payee (including a child of the
    payee's spouse or common-law partner) who is under the age of 18
    years; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a child of the payee (including a child of the
    payee's spouse or common-law partner) who is 18 years of age or
    older and dependent, by reason of mental or physical infirmity, on
    the payee for support.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information return

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
assisting the Minister of Health in the administration and enforcement
of the Canadian Dental Care Plan, the Minister of National Revenue may,
in the information return that a person is required to make under
subsection 200(1) of the [Income Tax
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._945), ask for the information
required under subsection 4(1) of this Act and, for those purposes, may
collect that information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of information obtained

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Any information obtained
under subsection 4(1) is information obtained for the purposes of the
administration and enforcement of the Canadian Dental Care Plan and not
for the purposes of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

## [Disclosure of Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1414173 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of information

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of National
Revenue or any person acting on behalf of that Minister may provide any
information collected under section 5

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the Minister of Health, for the purposes of the
  administration and the enforcement of the Canadian Dental Care Plan or
  the formulation or evaluation of policy for that plan; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to an official of the Department of Employment and
  Social Development, for the purposes of assisting the Minister of
  Health in the administration and the enforcement of the Canadian
  Dental Care Plan or the formulation or evaluation of policy for that
  plan.

## [Violations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1414178 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violations

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  commits a violation if they

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fail to comply with subsection 4(1) in respect of
    any person in respect of whom the income return referred to in that
    subsection is to be made; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} knowingly make, in the information return referred
    to in that subsection, a representation that is false or misleading
    in relation to the information required under that subsection in
    respect of any person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Health may impose a penalty of \$100
  on a person for each violation if that Minister is of the opinion that
  the person has committed a violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of penalty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of the penalty is to promote compliance
  with this Act and not to punish.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation on imposition of penalty

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A penalty must not be
imposed under section 8 if more than three years have passed since the
day on which the act that would constitute the violation occurred.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rescission or reduction of penalty

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Health
may rescind the imposition of a penalty under section 8, or reduce the
penalty, on the presentation of new facts or on being of the opinion
that the penalty was imposed without knowledge of, or on the basis of a
mistake as to, some material fact.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery as debt due to His Majesty

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A penalty imposed under
section 8 constitutes a debt due to His Majesty and the debt is payable
and may be recovered by the Minister of Health as of the day on which
the penalty is imposed.

## [Social Insurance Number]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1414194 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Social Insurance Number

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Health is
authorized to collect and use, for the purposes of the administration
and enforcement of the Canadian Dental Care Plan, the Social Insurance
Number of a person who makes an application under the plan.
