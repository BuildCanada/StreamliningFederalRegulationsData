## Lobbying Act {#lobbying-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. 44 (4th
Supp.)
:::

An Act respecting lobbying

::: ReaderNote
\[1988, c. 53, assented to 13th September, 1988\]
:::
:::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS free and open access to government is an important matter of
public interest;

AND WHEREAS lobbying public office holders is a legitimate activity;

AND WHEREAS it is desirable that public office holders and the public be
able to know who is engaged in lobbying activities;

AND WHEREAS a system for the registration of paid lobbyists should not
impede free and open access to government;
:::

:::: Enacts
NOW THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

- R.S., 1985, c. 44 (4th Supp.), preamble; 2003, c. 10, s. 1.
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-339035 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[designated public office holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated public office holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a minister of the Crown or a minister of state
        and any person employed in his or her office who is appointed
        under subsection 128(1) of the [Public Service Employment
        Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01),

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other public office holder who, in a
        department within the meaning of paragraph (a), (a.1) or (d) of
        the definition [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2
        of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11),

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} occupies the senior executive position,
          whether by the title of deputy minister, chief executive
          officer or by some other title, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is an associate deputy minister or an
          assistant deputy minister or occupies a position of comparable
          rank, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any individual who occupies a position that has
        been designated by regulation under paragraph
        12(c.1). ([titulaire d'une charge publique
        désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Ethics Counsellor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Ethics Counsellor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2004, c.
      7, s. 19\]]{.Repealed}

  [[organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a business, trade, industry, professional or
        voluntary organization,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a trade union or labour organization,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a chamber of commerce or board of trade,

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a partnership, trust, association, charitable
        society, coalition or interest group,

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a government, other than the Government of
        Canada, and

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a corporation without share capital
        incorporated to pursue, without financial gain to its members,
        objects of a national, provincial, patriotic, religious,
        philanthropic, charitable, scientific, artistic, social,
        professional or sporting character or other similar
        objects; ([organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means money or anything of value
      and includes a contract, promise or agreement to pay money or
      anything of value; ([paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by
      regulation; ([Version anglaise
      seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

  [[public office holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[public office holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any officer or
      employee of Her Majesty in right of Canada and includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Senate or the House of Commons
        and any person on the staff of such a member,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who is appointed to any office or body
        by or with the approval of the Governor in Council or a minister
        of the Crown, other than a judge receiving a salary under the
        [Judges Act](/eng/acts/J-1) or the lieutenant governor of a
        province,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an officer, director or employee of any federal
        board, commission or other tribunal as defined in the [Federal
        Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7),

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Canadian Armed Forces, and

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Royal Canadian Mounted
        Police; ([titulaire d'une charge publique]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2006, c. 9, s.
      67\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsidiary corporation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, a corporation is a
  subsidiary of another corporation if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} securities of the first-mentioned corporation to
    which are attached more than fifty per cent of the votes that may be
    cast to elect directors of the first-mentioned corporation are held,
    otherwise than by way of security only, directly or indirectly,
    whether through one or more subsidiaries or otherwise, by or for the
    benefit of the other corporation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the votes attached to those securities are
    sufficient, if exercised, to elect a majority of the directors of
    the first-mentioned corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transition team

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any person identified by the Prime Minister as having

  and his or her ministry is subject to this Act, except subsections
  10.11(2) to (4), as if the person were a designated public office
  holder during that period.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-339070 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on application

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This Act
  does not apply to any of the following persons when acting in their
  official capacity, namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} members of the legislature of a province or persons
    on the staff of such members;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} employees of the government of a province;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} members of a council or other statutory body
    charged with the administration of the civil or municipal affairs of
    a city, town, municipality or district, persons on the staff of such
    members or officers or employees of a city, town, municipality or
    district;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} members of the council of a band as defined in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) or of the council
    of an Indian band established by an Act of Parliament, persons on
    their staff or employees of such a council;

  - [(d.1)]{.lawlabel} members of an aboriginal government or
    institution that exercises jurisdiction or authority under a
    self-government agreement, or under self-government provisions
    contained in a land claims agreement, given effect by or under an
    Act of Parliament, persons on the staff of those members or
    employees of that government or institution;

  - [(d.2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2003, c. 10, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d.3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2004, c. 17, s. 20\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} diplomatic agents, consular officers or official
    representatives in Canada of a foreign government; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} officials of a specialized agency of the United
    Nations in Canada or officials of any other international
    organization to whom there are granted, by or under any Act of
    Parliament, privileges and immunities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Act does not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any oral or written submission made to a committee
    of the Senate or House of Commons or of both Houses of Parliament or
    to any body or person having jurisdiction or powers conferred by or
    under an Act of Parliament, in proceedings that are a matter of
    public record;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any oral or written communication made to a public
    office holder by an individual on behalf of any person or
    organization with respect to the enforcement, interpretation or
    application of any Act of Parliament or regulation by that public
    office holder with respect to that person or organization; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any oral or written communication made to a public
    office holder by an individual on behalf of any person or
    organization if the communication is restricted to a request for
    information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act shall be construed as requiring
  the disclosure of the name or identity of any individual where that
  disclosure could reasonably be expected to threaten the safety of that
  individual.

## [Office of the Commissioner of Lobbying]{.HTitleText1} {#h-339094 .Part}

### [Commissioner of Lobbying]{.HTitleText2} {#h-339095 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissioner of Lobbying

- **[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall, by commission under the Great Seal, appoint
  a Commissioner of Lobbying after consultation with the Leader of the
  Government in the Senate or Government Representative in the Senate,
  the Leader of the Opposition in the Senate, the Leader or Facilitator
  of every other recognized party or parliamentary group in the Senate
  and the leader of every recognized party in the House of Commons and
  approval of the appointment by resolution of the Senate and House of
  Commons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure of office and removal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section, the Commissioner holds
  office during good behaviour for a term of seven years, but may be
  removed for cause by the Governor in Council at any time on address of
  the Senate and House of Commons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further terms

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner, on the expiry of a first or any
  subsequent term of office, is eligible to be reappointed for a further
  term not exceeding seven years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interim appointment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  Commissioner, or if that office is vacant, the Governor in Council may
  appoint any qualified person to hold that office in the interim for a
  term not exceeding six months, and that person shall, while holding
  office, be paid the salary or other remuneration and expenses that may
  be fixed by the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rank and powers

- **[[4.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner has the rank and powers of a deputy head of a department,
  shall engage exclusively in the duties of the office of Commissioner
  under this Act or any other Act of Parliament and shall not hold any
  other office or employment for reward.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties and functions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner's duties and functions, in addition
  to those set out elsewhere in this Act, include developing and
  implementing educational programs to foster public awareness of the
  requirements of this Act, particularly on the part of lobbyists, their
  clients and public office holders.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and expenses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall be paid the remuneration and
  expenses set by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pension benefits

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The provisions of the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36), other than those relating to tenure of office,
  apply to the Commissioner, except that a person appointed as
  Commissioner from outside the public service, as defined in the
  [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), may, by notice in
  writing given to the President of the Treasury Board not more than 60
  days after the date of appointment, elect to participate in the
  pension plan provided in the [Diplomatic Service (Special)
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/D-2), in which case the provisions of
  that Act, other than those relating to tenure of office, apply to the
  Commissioner from the date of appointment and the provisions of the
  [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) do not apply.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other benefits

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner is deemed to be employed in the
  federal public administration for the purposes of the [Government
  Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and any regulations made
  under section 9 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

## [Registration of Lobbyists]{.HTitleText1} {#h-339135 .Part}

### [Consultant Lobbyists]{.HTitleText2} {#h-339136 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to file return

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual shall file with the Commissioner, in the prescribed form
  and manner, a return setting out the information referred to in
  subsection (2), if the individual, for payment, on behalf of any
  person or organization (in this section referred to as the "client"),
  undertakes to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} communicate with a public office holder in respect
    of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the development of any legislative proposal by
      the Government of Canada or by a member of the Senate or the House
      of Commons,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the introduction of any Bill or resolution in
      either House of Parliament or the passage, defeat or amendment of
      any Bill or resolution that is before either House of Parliament,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the making or amendment of any regulation as
      defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Statutory Instruments
      Act](/eng/acts/S-22),

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the development or amendment of any policy or
      program of the Government of Canada,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the awarding of any grant, contribution or other
      financial benefit by or on behalf of Her Majesty in right of
      Canada, or

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the awarding of any contract by or on behalf of
      Her Majesty in right of Canada; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} arrange a meeting between a public office holder
    and any other person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for filing return

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} An individual shall file the return referred to in
  subsection (1) not later than 10 days after entering into the
  undertaking.

- [(1.2) and (1.3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2006, c. 9, s.
  69\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of return

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The return shall set out the following information
  with respect to the undertaking:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and business address of the individual
    and, if applicable, the name and business address of the firm where
    the individual is engaged in business;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and business address of the client and the
    name and business address of any person or organization that, to the
    knowledge of the individual, controls or directs the activities of
    the client and has a direct interest in the outcome of the
    individual's activities on behalf of the client;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the client is a corporation, the name and
    business address of each subsidiary of the corporation that, to the
    knowledge of the individual, has a direct interest in the outcome of
    the individual's activities on behalf of the client;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the client is a corporation that is a
    subsidiary of any other corporation, the name and business address
    of that other corporation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} where the client is a coalition, the name and
    business address of each corporation or organization that is a
    member of the coalition;

  - [(e.1)]{.lawlabel} the client is funded in whole or in part by a
    government or government agency, the name of the government or
    agency, as the case may be, and the amount of funding received;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} particulars to identify the subject-matter in
    respect of which the individual undertakes to communicate with a
    public office holder or to arrange a meeting, and any other
    information respecting the subject-matter that is prescribed;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the fact that the undertaking does not provide for
    any payment that is in whole or in part contingent on the outcome of
    any matter described in subparagraphs (1)(a)(i) to (vi) or on the
    individual's success in arranging a meeting referred to in paragraph
    (1)(b);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} particulars to identify any relevant legislative
    proposal, Bill, resolution, regulation, policy, program, grant,
    contribution, financial benefit or contract;

  - [(h.1)]{.lawlabel} if the individual is a former public officer
    holder, a description of the offices held, which of those offices,
    if any, qualified the individual as a designated public office
    holder and the date on which the individual last ceased to hold such
    a designated public office;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of any department or other governmental
    institution in which any public office holder with whom the
    individual communicates or expects to communicate in respect of any
    matter described in subparagraphs (1)(a)(i) to (vi) or with whom a
    meeting is, or is to be, arranged, is employed or serves;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} if the individual undertakes to communicate with a
    public office holder in respect of any matter described in
    subparagraphs (1)(a)(i) to (vi), particulars to identify any
    communication technique that the individual uses or expects to use
    in connection with the communication with the public office holder,
    including any appeals to members of the public through the mass
    media or by direct communication that seek to persuade those members
    of the public to communicate directly with a public office holder in
    an attempt to place pressure on the public office holder to endorse
    a particular opinion (in this Act referred to as "grass-roots
    communication"); and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} such other information relating to the identity of
    the individual, the client, any person or organization referred to
    in paragraph (b), any subsidiary referred to in paragraph (c), the
    other corporation referred to in paragraph (d), any member of a
    coalition referred to in paragraph (e) or any department or
    institution referred to in paragraph (i) as is prescribed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to file monthly return

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The individual shall file a return, in the prescribed
  form and manner, not later than 15 days after the end of every month,
  beginning with the one in which the return is filed under subsection
  (1), that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sets out, with respect to every communication
    referred to in paragraph (1)(a) that is of a prescribed type and
    that was made in that month involving a designated public office
    holder and relating to the undertaking,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the designated public office holder
      who was the object of the communication,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date of the communication,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} particulars, including any prescribed
      particulars, to identify the subject-matter of the communication,
      and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any other information that is prescribed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if any information contained in the return filed
    under subsection (1) is no longer correct or additional information
    that the individual would have been required to provide under that
    subsection has come to the knowledge of the individual after the
    return was filed, provides the corrected or additional information;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the undertaking has been performed or
    terminated, advises the Commissioner of that fact.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First monthly return

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The first return filed under subsection (3) shall,
  despite paragraph (3)(a), set out the information required by that
  paragraph in respect of communications made between the day on which
  the undertaking referred to in subsection (1) was entered into and the
  end of the month immediately before the filing of the return.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4.2), no return is required
  under subsection (3) if no communication referred to in paragraph
  (3)(a) was made during the period with respect to which the return is
  to set out information, and if the circumstances referred to in
  paragraphs (3)(b) and (c) have not arisen.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return --- six-month period

  [(4.2)]{.lawlabel} In any case, no more than five months shall have
  elapsed since the end of the month in which a return was last filed
  without a return being filed by the individual under subsection (3),
  even if, since the last return, no communication was made as referred
  to in paragraph (3)(a) and the circumstances referred to in paragraphs
  (3)(b) and (c) have not arisen, in which case the report shall so
  state.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of reporting obligation

  [(4.3)]{.lawlabel} The obligation to file a return under
  subsection (3) terminates when the undertaking has been performed or
  is terminated and a report has been filed under that subsection
  advising of that fact in accordance with paragraph (3)(c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information requested by Commissioner

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An individual who files a return shall provide the
  Commissioner, in the prescribed form and manner, with such information
  as the Commissioner may request to clarify any information that the
  individual has provided to the Commissioner pursuant to this section,
  and shall do so not later than thirty days after the request is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on application

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply in respect of anything
  that an employee undertakes to do on the sole behalf of their employer
  or, where their employer is a corporation, in respect of anything that
  the employee, at the direction of the employer, undertakes to do on
  behalf of any subsidiary of the employer or any corporation of which
  the employer is a subsidiary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, an individual who undertakes
  to communicate with a public office holder as described in paragraph
  (1)(a) is not required to file more than one return under
  subsection (1) with respect to the undertaking, even though the
  individual, in connection with that undertaking, communicates with
  more than one public office holder or communicates with one or more
  public office holders on more than one occasion.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to file return

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  officer responsible for filing returns for a corporation or
  organization shall file with the Commissioner, in the prescribed form
  and manner, a return setting out the information referred to in
  subsection (3) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the corporation or organization employs one or more
    individuals any part of whose duties is to communicate with public
    office holders on behalf of the employer or, if the employer is a
    corporation, on behalf of any subsidiary of the employer or any
    corporation of which the employer is a subsidiary, in respect of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the development of any legislative proposal by
      the Government of Canada or by a member of the Senate or the House
      of Commons,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the introduction of any Bill or resolution in
      either House of Parliament or the passage, defeat or amendment of
      any Bill or resolution that is before either House of Parliament,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the making or amendment of any regulation as
      defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Statutory Instruments
      Act](/eng/acts/S-22),

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the development or amendment of any policy or
      program of the Government of Canada, or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the awarding of any grant, contribution or other
      financial benefit by or on behalf of Her Majesty in right of
      Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} those duties constitute a significant part of the
    duties of one employee or would constitute a significant part of the
    duties of one employee if they were performed by only one employee.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The officer responsible for filing returns shall file

## [Restriction on Lobbying Activity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-339311 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Five-year prohibition --- lobbying

- **[[10.11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No individual shall, during a period
  of five years after the day on which the individual ceases to be a
  designated public office holder,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} carry on any of the activities referred to in
    paragraph 5(1)(a) or (b) in the circumstances referred to in
    subsection 5(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the individual is employed by an organization,
    carry on any of the activities referred to in paragraph 7(1)(a) on
    behalf of that organization; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the individual is employed by a corporation,
    carry on any of the activities referred to in paragraph 7(1)(a) on
    behalf of that corporation if carrying on those activities would
    constitute a significant part of the individual's work on its
    behalf.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of any
  designated public office that was held only because the individual
  participated in an employment exchange program.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On application, the Commissioner of Lobbying may, on
  any conditions that the Commissioner specifies, exempt an individual
  from the application of subsection (1) if the Commissioner is of the
  opinion that the exemption would not be contrary to the purposes of
  this Act having regard to any circumstance or factor that the
  Commissioner considers relevant, including whether the individual

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} was a designated public office holder for a short
    period;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} was a designated public office holder on an acting
    basis;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} was employed under a program of student employment;
    or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} had administrative duties only.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall without delay cause every
  exemption and the Commissioner's reasons for it to be made available

## [Investigations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-339367 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation

- **[[10.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall conduct an
  investigation if he or she has reason to believe, including on the
  basis of information received from a member of the Senate or the House
  of Commons, that an investigation is necessary to ensure compliance
  with the Code or this Act, as applicable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may refuse to conduct or may cease
  an investigation with respect to any matter if he or she is of the
  opinion that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the matter is one that could more appropriately be
    dealt with according to a procedure provided for under another Act
    of Parliament;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the matter is not sufficiently important;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} dealing with the matter would serve no useful
    purpose because of the length of time that has elapsed since the
    matter arose; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} there is any other valid reason for not dealing
    with the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of investigation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of conducting the investigation, the
  Commissioner may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the same manner and to the same extent as a
    superior court of record,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} summon and enforce the attendance of persons
      before the Commissioner and compel them to give oral or written
      evidence on oath, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} compel persons to produce any documents or other
      things that the Commissioner considers relevant for the
      investigation;

    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} administer oaths and receive and accept
    information, whether or not it would be admissible as evidence in a
    court of law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation in private

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The investigation shall be conducted in private.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence in other proceedings

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Evidence given by a person in the investigation and
  evidence of the existence of the investigation are inadmissible
  against the person in a court or in any other proceeding, other than
  in a prosecution of a person for an offence under section 131 of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) (perjury) in respect of a statement
  made to the Commissioner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opportunity to present views

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Before finding that a person has breached the Code,
  the Commissioner shall give the person a reasonable opportunity to
  present their views to the Commissioner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner, and every person acting on behalf
  of or under the direction of the Commissioner, may not disclose any
  information that comes to their knowledge in the performance of their
  duties and functions under this section, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure is, in the opinion of the
    Commissioner, necessary for the purpose of conducting an
    investigation under this section or establishing the grounds for any
    findings or conclusions contained in a report under section 10.5;

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-339439 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  individual who fails to file a return as required under subsection
  5(1) or (3) or 7(1) or (4), or knowingly makes any false or misleading
  statement in any return or other document submitted to the
  Commissioner under this Act or in any response provided relative to
  information sent under subsection 9.1(1), whether in electronic or
  other form, is guilty of an offence and liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding
    \$50,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or
    to both; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on proceedings by way of indictment, to a fine not
    exceeding \$200,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two
    years, or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other contraventions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every individual who contravenes any provision of
  this Act --- other than subsections 5(1) and (3), 7(1) and (4) and
  10.3(1) --- or the regulations is guilty of an offence and liable on
  summary conviction to a fine not exceeding \$50,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Proceedings by way of summary conviction in respect
  of an offence under this section may be instituted at any time within
  but not later than five years after the day on which the Commissioner
  became aware of the subject-matter of the proceedings but, in any
  case, not later than ten years after the day on which the
  subject-matter of the proceedings arose.

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every individual who contravenes subsection (1)
      is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine
      not exceeding \$50,000.

    - ###### Limitation {#limitation .MarginalNote}

      [(4)]{.lawlabel} Proceedings by way of summary conviction in
      respect of an offence under this section may be instituted at any
      time within but not later than five years after the day on which
      the registrar referred to in section 8 of the other Act became
      aware of the subject-matter of the proceedings but, in any case,
      not later than ten years after the day on which the subject-matter
      of the proceedings arose.

    - ###### Definition {#definition .MarginalNote}

      [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, members of a
      transition team are those persons identified by the Prime Minister
      as having had the task of providing support and advice to him or
      her during the transition period leading up to the swearing in of
      the Prime Minister and his or her ministry.

- --- 2006, c. 9, s. 88.11

  - ###### Application for exemption {#application-for-exemption .MarginalNote}

    - [88.11]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any member of a transition
      team referred to in section 88.1 may apply to the Commissioner of
      Lobbying for an exemption from that section.

    - ###### Commissioner of Lobbying may exempt {#commissioner-of-lobbying-may-exempt .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner of Lobbying may, on any
      conditions that the Commissioner of Lobbying specifies, exempt the
      member from the application of section 88.1 having regard to any
      circumstance or factor that the Commissioner of Lobbying considers
      relevant, including the following:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the circumstances under which the member left
        the functions referred to in subsection 88.1(5);

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the nature, and significance to the Government
        of Canada, of information that the member possessed by virtue of
        the functions referred to in subsection 88.1(5);

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the degree to which the member's new employer
        might gain unfair commercial advantage by hiring the member;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the authority and influence that the member
        possessed while having the functions referred to in subsection
        88.1(5); and

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the disposition of other cases.

    - ###### Publication {#publication .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner of Lobbying shall without delay
      cause every exemption and the Commissioner of Lobbying's reasons
      for it to be made available to the public.

    - ###### Audit {#audit .MarginalNote}

      [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner of Lobbying may verify the
      information contained in any application under subsection (1).

- --- 2006, c. 9, s. 88.2

  - ###### Publication {#publication-1 .MarginalNote}

    [88.2]{.lawlabel} The registrar referred to in section 8 of the
    other Act may make public the nature of an offence committed under
    section 88.1, the name of the person who committed it and the
    punishment imposed.
