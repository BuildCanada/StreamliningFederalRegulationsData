## Canada Strategic Infrastructure Fund Act {#canada-strategic-infrastructure-fund-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2002, c. 9, s. 47
:::

Assented to 2002-03-27

An Act to establish a program to provide contributions for the carrying
out of strategic infrastructure projects

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 47 of chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2002, in
force on assent March 27, 2002.\]
:::
:::::

::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada Strategic Infrastructure Fund Act](/eng/acts/C-10.3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Act.

[[eligible project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a large-scale project
    for the construction, renewal or material enhancement of strategic
    infrastructure that is carried out, or to be carried out, by an
    eligible recipient. ([travaux admissibles]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada who is designated by the Governor in
    Council as the Minister for the purposes of this
    Act. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[strategic infrastructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[strategic infrastructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the
    following fixed capital assets that are used or operated for the
    benefit of the public:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} highway or rail infrastructure;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} local transportation infrastructure;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} tourism or urban development infrastructure;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} sewage treatment infrastructure;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} water infrastructure; or

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} infrastructure prescribed by
      regulation. ([infrastructure stratégique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of program

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a program, to be called the Canada Strategic
  Infrastructure Fund, the object of which is to provide for the payment
  of contributions to eligible recipients for the carrying out of
  large-scale strategic infrastructure projects that contribute to
  economic growth or quality of life in Canada and that advance Canada's
  objectives with respect to infrastructure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public-private partnerships

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Fund shall, where appropriate, promote the use of
  partnerships between public and private sector bodies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligible recipients

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The following are eligible recipients:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a province or a municipal or regional government
    established by or under provincial legislation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a public sector body that is established by or
    under provincial legislation or by regulation or is wholly owned by
    a province or a private sector body that is in partnership with a
    province or a government referred to in paragraph (a), if the body

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} carries out or, in the opinion of the Minister,
      is capable of carrying out an eligible project in Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has legal capacity, or is composed of
      organizations each of which has legal capacity; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a private sector body that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} carries out or, in the opinion of the Minister,
      is capable of carrying out an eligible project in Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has legal capacity, or is composed of
      organizations each of which has legal capacity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contribution agreements

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may enter into
an agreement with an eligible recipient to provide for the payment of a
contribution for an eligible project under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations generally for the carrying out of the purposes and
provisions of this Act, including regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing other fixed capital assets for the
  purpose of paragraph (f) of the definition "strategic infrastructure"
  in section 2;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} defining what constitutes a large-scale project; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} establishing additional terms and conditions for the
  Fund.
