## Public Servants Inventions Act {#public-servants-inventions-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. P-32
:::

An Act respecting inventions by public servants
::::

::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-403892 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Public Servants Inventions Act](/eng/acts/P-32).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-31, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-403897 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[appropriate minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appropriate minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a
    public servant, means the minister who under the [Financial
    Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) is the appropriate minister with
    respect to the department in which the public servant is
    employed; ([ministre compétent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a department as defined in
    the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), and includes an
    agent corporation, as defined in section 83 of that Act, that is
    named in Part I of Schedule III to that
    Act; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[invention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[invention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any new and useful art,
    process, machine, manufacture or composition of matter, or any new
    and useful improvement in any art, process, machine, manufacture or
    composition of matter; ([invention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[public servant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public servant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person employed in
    a department, and includes a member of the Canadian Forces or the
    Royal Canadian Mounted Police. ([fonctionnaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-31, s. 2
- 1984, c. 31, s. 14
:::

## [Inventions Vested in Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-403906 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inventions vested in Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following inventions,
and all rights with respect thereto in Canada or elsewhere, are vested
in Her Majesty in right of Canada, namely,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an invention made by a public servant while acting
  within the scope of his duties or employment, or made by a public
  servant with facilities, equipment or financial aid provided by or on
  behalf of Her Majesty; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an invention made by a public servant that resulted
  from or is connected with his duties or employment.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-31, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of inventor

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  public servant who makes an invention

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall inform the appropriate minister of the
    invention and shall provide the minister with such information and
    documents with respect thereto as the minister requires;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall not file outside Canada an application for a
    patent in respect of the invention without the written consent of
    the appropriate minister; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} shall, in any application in Canada for a patent in
    respect of the invention, disclose in his application that he is a
    public servant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of Commissioner of Patents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If it appears to the Commissioner of Patents that an
  application for a patent relates to an invention made by a public
  servant, the Commissioner shall inform the appropriate minister of the
  application and give to the minister such information with respect
  thereto as the minister requires.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-31, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of questions

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Whenever
  any question arises as to whether an invention is vested in Her
  Majesty by this Act, the appropriate minister shall determine the
  question within three months after the question is referred to the
  minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate minister, on determining a question
  under subsection (1) in respect of an invention, shall forthwith in
  writing notify the inventor of the determination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal to Federal Court

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the appropriate minister determines under
  subsection (1) that an invention is vested in Her Majesty by this Act,
  the inventor or any other person claiming an interest in the invention
  may appeal to the Federal Court within thirty days after the date the
  inventor or other person is notified of the determination or within
  such longer period as the appropriate minister may allow.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination by Federal Court

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If no determination is made by the appropriate
  minister within the time specified in subsection (1), the inventor or
  any other person claiming an interest in the invention may apply to
  the Federal Court, within thirty days after the expiration of the time
  specified, to have the question determined.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-31, s. 5
- R.S., c. 10(2nd Supp.), s. 64
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for patent

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything in the
  [Patent Act](/eng/acts/P-4), the appropriate minister may file an
  application, naming the inventor, for a patent for an invention vested
  in Her Majesty by this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Patent issued in the name of Her
  Majesty or as otherwise directed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any patent issued on an application under
  subsection (1) shall be issued in the name of Her Majesty or as
  otherwise directed by the appropriate minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-31, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inventor to execute documents

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A public servant who has
made an invention vested in Her Majesty by this Act shall execute all
documents required by the appropriate minister in connection with the
filing of an application for a patent for the invention in Canada or
elsewhere.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-31, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waiver of rights

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  appropriate minister may on behalf of Her Majesty waive, abandon or
  transfer all or any of the rights in respect of any invention vested
  in Her Majesty by this Act or in respect of any invention made or to
  be made by any public servant, and may execute any instrument to give
  effect thereto.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrictions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No interest in an invention coming within section 20
  or 21 of the [Patent Act](/eng/acts/P-4) shall be waived, abandoned or
  transferred under this section without the approval of the Minister of
  National Defence, and no interest in an invention coming within
  section 22 of that Act shall be waived, abandoned or transferred under
  this section without the approval of the Canadian Nuclear Safety
  Commission.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-32, s. 8
- 1997, c. 9, s. 116
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administration and control of inventions

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  administration and control of any invention vested in Her Majesty by
  this Act and any patent or certificate of supplementary protection
  issued with respect to the invention are vested in the appropriate
  minister, and the appropriate minister may transfer that
  administration and control to any other minister or to any corporate
  agency of Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Development and exploitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate minister or other minister or agency
  referred to in subsection (1) may develop and exploit any invention
  under the administration and control of such minister or agency, as
  the case may be, and may on behalf of Her Majesty enter into any
  agreement with any person for such purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority of Crown agencies

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite anything in its charter or Act of
  incorporation, an agency to which the administration and control of
  any invention, patent or certificate of supplementary protection is
  transferred under this section has the capacity and power to receive,
  hold, administer, control, develop and exploit the invention, patent
  or certificate and generally to carry out the provisions of this Act
  with respect to the invention, patent or certificate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administration of moneys

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the administration and control of any invention or
  patent has been transferred under this section to a corporate agency
  of Her Majesty, any money received by the corporate agency in the
  course of the administration and control of the invention, patent or
  certificate of supplementary protection may be retained by that
  corporate agency, and shall be used for the purposes of this Act and
  the objects and purposes for which the agency was established.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-32, s. 9
- 2017, c. 6, s. 129
:::

## [Awards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-403963 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Awards

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the
regulations, the appropriate minister may authorize the payment of an
award to a public servant who makes an invention that is vested in Her
Majesty by this Act, in such amount as the appropriate minister and the
public servant may agree on or as the appropriate minister determines.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-31, s. 10
:::

## [Offence and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-403968 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes subsection 4(1) or section 7 is guilty of an offence and
liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding five hundred
dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to
both.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-31, s. 11
:::

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-403973 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations for carrying out the purposes and provisions of
this Act and, without restricting the generality of the foregoing, may
make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing rules of practice and procedure
  respecting

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} applications for patents pursuant to this Act,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the determination of questions as to whether an
    invention is vested in Her Majesty by this Act, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any appeal or application under this Act to the
    Federal Court;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the information to be furnished in any
  application for a patent in respect of an invention made by a public
  servant; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the amount of and the method of
  calculating and determining the awards to be paid under this Act and
  the manner and time of payment.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-31, s. 12
- R.S., c. 10(2nd Supp.), s. 64
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-403984 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where two ministers concerned

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  there are two or more appropriate ministers with respect to any
  invention, any one of such appropriate ministers may in relation to
  that invention act as the appropriate minister under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint inventions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an invention is made jointly by a public
  servant and another person who is not a public servant, this Act
  applies to the interest of the public servant in the invention.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-31, s. 13
:::
