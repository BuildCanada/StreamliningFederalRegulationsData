## War Veterans Allowance Act {#war-veterans-allowance-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. W-3
:::

An Act respecting allowances for war veterans and their dependants
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-466770 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [War Veterans Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. W-5, s. 1
:::

## [Construction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-466775 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction

**[[1.01]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.01 .sectionLabel}** The provisions of
this Act shall be liberally construed and interpreted to the end that
the recognized obligation of the people and Government of Canada to
those who have served their country so well and to their dependants may
be fulfilled.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 20 (3rd Supp.), s. 29
:::

## [Allowances to Veterans, Survivors and Orphans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-466880 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Veterans, survivors and orphans

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this Act, an allowance is payable to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any male person who is a veteran or a survivor of a
    veteran and who has attained the age of sixty years,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any female person who is a veteran or a survivor of
    a veteran and who has attained the age of fifty-five years,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any veteran, or survivor of a veteran, who, in the
    opinion of the Minister,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is permanently unemployable because of physical
      or mental disability,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is, because of physical or mental disability or
      insufficiency combined with economic handicaps, incapable and
      unlikely to become capable of maintaining himself or herself, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is, because of the need to provide care for a
      dependent child residing at home, incapable of maintaining himself
      or herself, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an orphan,

  and who is resident in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Section 3 does not apply to subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of allowance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The monthly allowance payable under this section to a
  veteran, survivor or orphan in a current payment period shall be
  computed as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine the applicable monthly income factor
    specified for the veteran, survivor or orphan in column II of the
    schedule;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine the applicable monthly allowance ceiling
    for the veteran, survivor or orphan by subtracting from the
    applicable monthly income factor determined under paragraph (a)
    one-twelfth of the income for the base calendar year of the veteran
    and the veteran's spouse or common-law partner, if any, or the
    survivor or orphan, as the case may be; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} determine the monthly allowance payable to the
    veteran, survivor or orphan by subtracting from the applicable
    monthly allowance ceiling determined under paragraph (b) the current
    monthly benefits, if any,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} payable under the [Old Age Security
      Act](/eng/acts/O-9) or, if no such benefits are payable, such
      benefits as are deemed to be payable pursuant to regulations made
      under paragraph 25(p), to or in respect of the veteran and the
      veteran's spouse or common-law partner, if any, or the survivor or
      orphan, as the case may be, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} payable under section 34 of the [Veterans Review
      and Appeal Board Act](/eng/acts/V-1.6), any enactment prescribed
      by regulations made under section 25, or any similar or equivalent
      laws of the country in whose forces the veteran served.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment where recipient absent from
  Canada

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), the allowance payable
  under this section to a veteran, survivor or orphan may be paid to
  that veteran, survivor or orphan who absents himself or herself from
  Canada after July 31, 1960 if, on the day that he or she leaves
  Canada, he or she

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a recipient of an allowance under this section
    or section 5; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} had been resident in Canada for the twelve months
    immediately preceding that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment to survivors and orphans
  resident outside Canada

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything in this section, the
  allowance payable under this section to a survivor or orphan may be
  paid to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the survivor of a recipient if that survivor
    resides outside Canada and was living with, maintaining or being
    maintained by that recipient at the time of the recipient's death;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an orphan of a recipient if that orphan resides
    outside Canada and if that recipient, at the time of death,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} was receiving an additional allowance in respect
      of the child, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} would have been eligible to receive an
      additional allowance in respect of the child but for the level of
      the recipient's income.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- allied veterans

  [(5.1)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (4) and (5) do not apply to an allied
  veteran referred to in paragraph 37(4)(d.1) or subsection 37(4.1) or
  (4.2) or to the survivor or orphan of that veteran.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Veteran couples

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where spouses residing together, or common-law
  partners, are both veterans, each may be paid the allowance that would
  be payable under this section if each veteran were a veteran without a
  spouse or common-law partner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Veteran couples

  [(6.1)]{.lawlabel} Where one of the veterans referred to in
  subsection (6) is not entitled to any allowance under that subsection,
  each may be paid the allowance that would be payable under this
  section if each veteran

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} were a veteran without a spouse or common-law
    partner; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} had one half of the aggregate of the income and
    benefits of both veterans.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dependent child allowance exception

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (6), either veteran
  referred to in that subsection is entitled to an allowance in respect
  of a dependent child of either or both of the veterans.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If couple not residing together

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is satisfied that an applicant and
  their spouse or common-law partner, or a recipient and their spouse or
  common-law partner, are not residing together as a result of one or
  both of them having to reside in a treatment or care facility or as a
  result of any other circumstances that are prescribed by regulations
  made under section 25, the Minister may direct that the applicant and
  spouse or common-law partner, or the recipient and spouse or
  common-law partner, as the case may be, be treated in the same manner
  as if they were persons referred to in subsection (6) and each person
  had one half of the aggregate of the income and benefits of both
  persons, and in that case, if the Minister deems it appropriate, the
  Minister shall apportion the allowances payable to them having regard
  to the circumstances of each of them and any dependent children
  involved.

## [Special Awards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-466925 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Death of veteran

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  death of a veteran who, at the time of their death or at any time
  within the last twelve months of their life, was a recipient of an
  allowance under section 4, the Minister may, in the Minister's
  discretion and within twelve months from the date of the death, award
  a monthly allowance to the veteran's survivor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of allowance

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The monthly allowance payable to a survivor under
  this section shall be computed in the same manner as a monthly
  allowance under section 4, except that the applicable monthly income
  factor referred to in paragraph 4(3)(a) in respect of the survivor
  shall be a monthly income factor specified in column II of the

## [Recoveries and Underpayments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467095 .Part}

Definition of [[overpayment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[overpayment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to any
  period, means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an allowance payment that was paid to a person in
    respect of that period and to which the person had no entitlement;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if an allowance payment was paid to a person in
    respect of that period that was in excess of the amount of the
    allowance payment to which the person was entitled, the amount of
    that excess.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of overpayments

  [(1.01)]{.lawlabel} If, through any cause, an overpayment is paid to a
  person, the overpayment is a debt due to Her Majesty by that person or
  by that person's estate or succession, and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} may be recovered by deduction from any future
    payments made pursuant to this Act to that person or to that
    person's estate or succession;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may be recovered in accordance with section 155 of
    the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} may be recovered in any court of competent
    jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excessive payments and underpayments

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Where an allowance has been paid in respect of any
  payment period and it is subsequently determined that the income of
  the recipient and, if applicable, the recipient's spouse or common-law
  partner, for the base calendar year calculated as required by this
  Act, in this subsection referred to as the "actual income", is not the
  same as the income of the recipient, in this subsection referred to as
  the "shown income", calculated as required by this Act on the basis of
  a statement required or permitted by section 8.1 to be made or filed
  by the recipient, the following adjustment shall be made:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the actual income exceeds the shown income, any
    amount by which the allowance paid to the recipient for months in
    that payment period exceeds the allowance that would have been paid
    to the recipient for those months if the shown income had been equal
    to the actual income shall be considered an overpayment; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the shown income exceeds the actual income,

  least 10 years beginning on or after August 15, 1945, who has been
  honourably discharged or has been permitted honourably to resign or
  retire from one of those forces and who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} served in a theatre of actual war during that war;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is in receipt of a pension for an injury or disease
    incurred or aggravated during service in any such force during that
    war or is declared to have been eligible for, or awarded, such a
    pension subsequent to their death; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has accepted a commuted pension.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allied veteran --- Korean War

  [(4.2)]{.lawlabel} An allied veteran is also any former member of any
  of the forces that took part in the Korean War and who served during
  that war, who resided in Canada for a total period of at least 10
  years beginning on or after July 27, 1953, who has been honourably
  discharged or has been permitted honourably to resign or retire from
  one of those forces and who served in a theatre of operations during
  that war.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian dual service veteran

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A Canadian dual service veteran is a person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} served during World War I and World War II as a
    member of His Majesty's Canadian forces;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} was enlisted or obligated to serve in those forces
    without territorial limitation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has been honourably discharged or has been
    permitted honourably to resign or retire from those forces.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allied dual service veteran

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} An allied dual service veteran is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} served during World War I as a member of His
      Majesty's forces,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} was domiciled in Canada when he or she became a
      member of those forces,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} was a member of His Majesty's Canadian forces
      during World War II, enlisted or obligated to serve without
      territorial limitation, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} has been honourably discharged or has been
      permitted honourably to resign or retire from those forces.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1995, c. 17, s. 71\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian Forces veteran

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A Canadian Forces veteran is a person who,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as a member of the forces referred to in section 14
    of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), left Canada or the
    United States, including Alaska, at any time prior to July 27, 1953
    to participate in military operations undertaken by the United
    Nations to restore peace in the Republic of Korea; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is in receipt of a pension under the [Pension
    Act](/eng/acts/P-6), or is declared to have been eligible for, or
    awarded, a pension under the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) after the
    member's death, having become eligible for that pension by virtue of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} service as a member of the special force, as that
      force is defined in subsection 3(1) of that Act, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} service in the Korean War, as that service is
      defined in subsection 3(1) of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(7.1)]{.lawlabel} The definitions in this subsection apply in this
  section.

  [[allied ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[allied ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a ship other than a
      Canadian ship or a ship serving the enemy, and includes a ship
      that belonged to a government of an enemy-occupied country, or to
      a person resident in an enemy-occupied country, and that was
      placed under Canadian or allied control. ([navire
      allié]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian manning pool]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian manning pool]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a manning pool
      of the Canadian merchant navy established under Order in Council
      P.C. 14/3550 of May 19, 1941. ([dépôt d'équipages
      canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a ship registered or licensed in Canada or
        Newfoundland, except

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a ship chartered by bareboat charter to a
          charterer resident outside Canada or Newfoundland, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a ship taken over and operated by any allied
          country; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a ship, wherever registered or licensed, having
        a crew engaged under the laws of Canada or Newfoundland and

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} chartered by bareboat charter to a charterer
          resident in Canada or Newfoundland, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} taken over and operated by, under the
          control of, or on behalf of the Canadian Shipping Board or a
          Canadian naval authority. ([navire canadien]{.DefinedTermLink
          lang="fr"})

  [[distressed mariner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[distressed mariner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who was
      in distress in a place outside Canada or Newfoundland, having been
      shipwrecked, discharged or left behind from a ship on which the
      person was engaged, and who received or was entitled to receive
      relief under

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Distressed Seamen Regulations, made by
        Order in Council P.C. 609 of March 23, 1937, as those
        regulations read at the relevant time; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Order in Council P.C. 8592 of November 9,
        1943. ([en détresse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[foreign voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[foreign voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a voyage from or to
      any place outside the area of a home-trade voyage. ([voyage de
      long cours]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[home-trade voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[home-trade voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a voyage between
      places in Canada, Newfoundland, the United States or Saint Pierre
      and Miquelon, in the course of which the ship did not go south of
      36° North latitude or through the Bering Strait. ([voyage de
      cabotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[territorial waters of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[territorial waters of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all
      waters within three nautical miles of any of the coasts, bays,
      creeks or harbours of Canada and Newfoundland, not included within
      the limits specified and described in the first article of the
      convention between His Majesty King George III and the United
      States, signed at London on October 20, 1818, and includes all
      waters that, pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 3139 of December
      18, 1937, were delimited as Canadian territorial waters, but does
      not include

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the waters of the St. Lawrence River seaward
        from a line drawn due south across that river from the mouth of
        the Saguenay River; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the waters of the Strait of Juan de Fuca
        westward beyond 124° 30′ West longitude. ([eaux territoriales du
        Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[war risk bonus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[war risk bonus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the pay supplement
      that was paid to ships' crews serving in dangerous waters

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by shipowners or shipping companies under the
        authority of decisions, findings or directions of the National
        War Labour Board pursuant to

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Wartime Wages and Cost of Living Bonus
          Order, Order in Council P.C. 8253 of October 24, 1941,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Wartime Wages Control Order, Order in
          Council P.C. 5963 of July 10, 1942, or

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Wartime Wages Control Order, 1943,
          Order in Council P.C. 9384 of December 9, 1943,

        as those respective orders read at the relevant time; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by the Government of Canada pursuant to Order
        in Council P.C. 122/7359 of August 19, 1942, as it read at the
        relevant time. ([indemnité pour risques de
        guerre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicable rules

  [(7.2)]{.lawlabel} The following rules apply for the purposes of this
  section:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the status of a vessel and of the members of its
    crew, and the class of voyage in which a vessel was engaged, shall
    be determined according to the Canada Shipping Act, 1934 and the
    regulations made thereunder, as that Act and those regulations read
    at the relevant time;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the official records verifying the authenticity of
    any ship, other than a ship registered in Canada or Newfoundland,
    shall be those used under international practice or under the laws
    of the country of registration;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a ship does not qualify as a Canadian ship or an
    allied ship if, at the relevant time,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it was neither registered nor licensed,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it was a pleasure yacht not engaged in trade, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} it was engaged in the fishing industry;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where official records of a person's service as a
    merchant navy veteran of World War I or World War II or as a
    Canadian merchant navy veteran of the Korean War are not available,
    the Minister may accept a statutory declaration or like statement
    from anyone if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} information about the existence of any registered
      or licensed ship on which it is claimed that the person served is
      corroborated by official records,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} no information in the declaration or statement
      is contradicted by other evidence, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} after taking into consideration such
      corroboration as may be available, the Minister is satisfied, on
      the balance of probabilities, that the information in the
      declaration or statement is true,

    but this paragraph does not apply in relation to subparagraph
    (7.3)(a)(i) with respect to the availability of war risk bonus;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a person's domicile shall be understood as meaning
    the person's domicile at the commencement of the service, activity
    or status referred to in paragraph (7.3)(d); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the Minister may presume death in every case where,
    according to the evidence available as to the circumstances
    surrounding the disappearance of the person whose death is in
    question or the loss of the ship on which the person was serving,
    the Minister is satisfied beyond a reasonable doubt that the death
    has in fact occurred.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Merchant navy veteran of World War I or
  World War II

  [(7.3)]{.lawlabel} A merchant navy veteran of World War I or World War
  II is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person who, during World War I or World War II,
    served on board a Canadian ship while it was making

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a voyage for which war risk bonus was available,
      as verified by articles of agreement or other official
      documentation,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a foreign voyage,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a home-trade voyage any part of which went
      outside the territorial waters of Canada and the territorial
      waters of all other countries and was

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} between a place in Canada and a place in the
        United States, in either direction,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} between a place in one province and a place in
        another province,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} between a place in Newfoundland or Saint Pierre
        and Miquelon and a place outside Newfoundland and Saint Pierre
        and Miquelon, in either direction, or

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} a search and rescue, salvage, cable-laying or
        cable-repairing operation, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a voyage during the course of which

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the ship served on, or another Canadian or
        allied ship in the vicinity of the ship served on, was attacked
        by the enemy, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the ship served on was attacked or damaged

        - [(I)]{.lawlabel} by friendly forces engaging in action or
          counteraction against the enemy, or

        - [(II)]{.lawlabel} as a result of marine hazards due to the
          war;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person who, during World War I or World War II,
    made a trip by sea, land or air through or over a theatre of actual
    war for the purpose of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} proceeding to a Canadian ship in order to serve
      on a voyage referred to in subparagraph (a)(i), (ii) or (iii), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} returning to Canada, or to the country of which
      the person was a citizen or national, from having made a voyage
      referred to in subparagraph (a)(i), (ii), (iii) or (iv) or from
      service referred to in paragraph (c);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person who, as a member of a Canadian manning
    pool during World War I or World War II, served in a theatre of
    actual war;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any person domiciled in Canada who, during World
    War I or World War II,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} served on board an allied ship while it was
      making a voyage referred to in subparagraph (a)(i), (ii), (iii) or
      (iv),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} made a trip by sea, land or air through or over
      a theatre of actual war for the purpose of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} proceeding to an allied ship in order to serve
        on a voyage referred to in subparagraph (a)(i), (ii) or (iii),
        or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} returning to Canada from having made a voyage
        referred to in subparagraph (a)(i), (ii), (iii) or (iv) or from
        service referred to in subparagraph (iii),

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} as a member of an allied manning pool, served
      in a theatre of actual war, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} was a distressed mariner; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is in receipt of a pension,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has accepted a commuted pension, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is declared to have been eligible for, or is
      awarded, a pension subsequent to the person's death

    for an injury or disease incurred or aggravated during service as a
    Canadian merchant mariner of World War I or Canadian merchant
    mariner of World War II within the meaning of section 21.1 of the
    [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian merchant navy veteran of the
  Korean War

  [(7.4)]{.lawlabel} A Canadian merchant navy veteran of the Korean War
  is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person who, at any time during the period from
    June 25, 1950 to July 27, 1953, served on board a Canadian ship in
    any of the waters, including gulfs, bays and inlets, lying between

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the coasts of Siberia, Korea and China

    and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a line beginning at a point on the coast of
      Siberia at longitude 135° East; thence south to a point at
      latitude 38° 30′ North and longitude 135° East; thence
      southwesterly to a point at latitude 30° North and longitude 124°
      East; thence south to Shokoto Sho; thence westerly to Shichisei
      Seki; and thence westerly to a point on the coast of China at
      latitude 23° North; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is in receipt of a pension,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has accepted a commuted pension, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is declared to have been eligible for, or is
      awarded, a pension subsequent to the person's death

    for an injury or disease incurred or aggravated during service as a
    Canadian merchant mariner of the Korean War within the meaning of
    section 21.1 of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[theatre of actual
  war]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, [[theatre of actual
  war]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2015, c. 3, s. 168\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of World War I,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} as applied to the army or air forces, the zone of
      the allied armies of the continents of Europe, Asia or Africa, or
      wherever the veteran has sustained injury or contracted disease
      directly by a hostile act of the enemy, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} as applied to the naval forces or the merchant
      navy, the high seas or wherever contact has been made with hostile
      forces of the enemy, or wherever the veteran has sustained injury
      or contracted disease directly by a hostile act of the enemy; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of World War II,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a former member of His Majesty's
      Canadian forces or a merchant navy veteran of World War II, any
      place where the person has been on service involving duties
      performed outside the Western Hemisphere, including

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} service involving duties performed outside
        Canada, Newfoundland, the United States, Saint Pierre and
        Miquelon and the territorial waters thereof in aircraft, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} service anywhere in a ship or other vessel,
        which service is classed as "sea time" for the purpose of
        advancement of naval ratings, or which would be so classed were
        the ship or other vessel in the service of the naval forces of
        Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a former member of His Majesty's
      forces other than His Majesty's Canadian forces, or of any of the
      forces of His Majesty's Allies or powers associated with His
      Majesty in World War II, such places, zones or areas as the Board
      may prescribe.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of [[sea
  time]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(8.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of clause (8)(c)(i)(B), [[sea
  time]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} for the purpose of advancement of naval
  ratings includes time served in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a sea-going ship,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a defensively-equipped merchant ship, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any ship employed in harbour, other than a boom
    defence scow, gate vessel, depot ship or harbour craft,

  as well as time served in combined operations organizations, but does
  not include time served on any ship prior to its commissioning.

- Definition of [[commuted pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, [[commuted
  pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a final payment under the [Pension
  Act](/eng/acts/P-6) in lieu of annual pension in respect of a
  disability rated at five per cent or more of total disability, or a
  similar or analogous final payment under the laws relating to the
  forces with which the veteran served.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement and duration of wars

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of this section, except
  subparagraphs (3)(a)(ii) to (iv) and paragraphs (4)(e) to (g),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2015, c. 3, s. 168\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} World War I shall be deemed to have commenced on
    August 4, 1914 and to have concluded on August 31, 1921; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} World War II shall be deemed to have commenced on
    September 1, 1939 and to have terminated

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in respect of service in connection with
      operations in the European and Mediterranean theatres of war, on
      May 8, 1945, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of service in connection with
      operations in the Pacific theatre of war, on August 15, 1945.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to Newfoundland

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, [[Canadian
  forces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any forces raised in
  Newfoundland, and [[domicile in Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and
  [[residence in Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} include respectively
  domicile and residence in Newfoundland, whether before or after the
  union of Newfoundland with Canada.

- [(12)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, R.S., 1985, c. 12 (2nd Supp.), s.
  14\]]{.Repealed}

  - ###### Retroactive pension {#retroactive-pension .MarginalNote}

    [159]{.lawlabel} If, on the day on which this Division comes into
    force, a person is or has been in receipt of war veterans allowance
    referred to in subsection 32(2) of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6)
    as it read immediately before that day and, on or after that day, is
    awarded a retroactive pension or a retroactive increase of pension
    under that Act, then any pension payments made under that Act in
    respect of a month the whole of which is before that day are subject
    to that subsection 32(2) and to section 13 of the [War Veterans
    Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3) as they read immediately before that
    day.
