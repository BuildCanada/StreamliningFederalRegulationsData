## Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Act {#royal-canadian-mounted-police-superannuation-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. R-11
:::

An Act respecting the superannuation of members of the Royal Canadian
Mounted Police
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-422701 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. R-11, s. 1
:::

## [Equality of Status]{.HTitleText1} {#h-422706 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of males and females

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Male and female
contributors under this Act have equality of status and equal rights and
obligations under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 81, s. 54
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-422711 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[active service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[active service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any service that is
      specified in the regulations to be active service and is deemed
      for the purposes of this Act to have terminated on discharge or,
      in the case of a person who underwent treatment in a veterans'
      hospital, as defined in the regulations, immediately following
      their discharge, on their release from that hospital; ([activité
      de service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a child or stepchild of --- or
      an individual adopted either legally or in fact by --- a
      contributor, who at the time of the contributor's death was
      dependent on the contributor for
      support; ([enfant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[contributor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[contributor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is required
      by section 5 to contribute to the Royal Canadian Mounted Police
      Pension Fund, and includes, unless the context otherwise requires,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who has ceased to be required by this
        Act to contribute to the Superannuation Account or the Royal
        Canadian Mounted Police Pension Fund, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of sections 15 to 19 and 22, a
        contributor under Part V of the former Act who has been granted
        a pension or annual allowance under that Act or has
        died; ([contributeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[disabled]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[disabled]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, except in Part II, as applied to
      any member of the Force, means incapable, by reason of any
      condition, of performing their duties as a member of the Force,
      and, for the purposes of section 12 as applied to any person,
      means ordinarily incapable, by reason of any condition, of
      pursuing any substantially gainful
      occupation; ([invalide]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Royal Canadian Mounted
      Police; ([Gendarmerie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[former Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[former Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Royal Canadian
      Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10), chapter 241 of the Revised
      Statutes of Canada, 1952, as it read before April 1, 1960, and
      includes, unless the context otherwise requires, any other Act of
      Parliament providing for the payment of pensions to members of the
      Force based on length of service, other than this Act; ([ancienne
      loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[member of the Force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[member of the Force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of the
      Force, as those terms are defined in the [Royal Canadian Mounted
      Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10), holding a rank in the Force, and any
      other member of the Force, as those terms are defined in that Act,
      of a class designated in accordance with the regulations for the
      purposes of this Act; ([membre de la Gendarmerie]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Public
      Safety and Emergency Preparedness; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commissioned officer of
      the Force; ([officier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[pay]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[pay]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as applied to the Force, the pay of the
        substantive rank held by the person in respect of whom the
        expression is being applied, not including the pay of acting
        rank or extra pay for staff or similar temporary appointments,
        or, in the case of a person not holding a rank in the Force, the
        salary or other remuneration for the performance of the regular
        duties of that person as a member of the Force, together with
        such allowances by way of compensation or otherwise as are
        prescribed by the regulations, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as applied to the public service or the
        Canadian Forces, the salary or pay and allowances, as the case
        may be, applicable in the case of that person, as determined
        under the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) or
        the [Canadian Forces Superannuation
        Act](/eng/acts/C-17); ([solde]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[provincial pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[provincial pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8); ([régime
      provincial de pensions]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[public service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[public service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 3(1) of the [Public Service Superannuation
      Act](/eng/acts/P-36); ([fonction publique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[regular force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[regular force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the regular force of
      the Canadian Forces and includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the forces known before February 1, 1968 as the
        regular forces of the Canadian Forces, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the forces known before February 1, 1968 as the
        Royal Canadian Navy, the Canadian Army Active Force, the
        Permanent Active Militia, the Permanent Militia Corps, the
        permanent staff of the Militia, the Royal Canadian Air Force
        (Regular) and the Permanent Active Air Force; ([force
        régulière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[retirement age]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[retirement age]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, as applied to any rank or
      class of contributor, means such age as is fixed by the
      regulations as the retirement age applicable to that rank or
      class; ([âge de retraite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Royal Canadian Mounted Police Pension Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Royal Canadian Mounted Police Pension
      Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the fund established under
      section 29.2; ([Caisse de retraite de la Gendarmerie royale du
      Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Investment Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Investment
      Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the fund established under
      section 29.1; ([Fonds de placement du compte de pension de
      retraite de la Gendarmerie royale du Canada]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[service in the Force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[service in the Force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any period
      of service

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as a special constable of the Force before
        April 1, 1960,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as a police officer that is counted as
        pensionable service under subsection 24.1(9),

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} as a police officer that is described in clause
        6(b)(ii)(A) or (L) if the contributor elects to pay for that
        service, and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} as a police officer or as a member of the Force
        that forms part of any period of service described in any of
        clauses 6(b)(ii)(H), (I), (O) and (P) if the contributor elects
        to pay for that service; ([service dans la
        Gendarmerie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Superannuation Account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Superannuation Account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Royal
      Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Account referred to in
      section 4; ([compte de pension de retraite]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[supplementary benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[supplementary benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      supplementary benefit payable under Part III; ([prestation
      supplémentaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a contributor,
      means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who was married to the contributor at
        the time of the contributor's death, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person referred to in subsection
        18(1); ([survivant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[World War I]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[World War I]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the war that was
      declared on August 4, 1914 and that is deemed, for the purposes of
      this Act, to have terminated on August 31, 1921; ([Première Guerre
      mondiale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[World War II]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[World War II]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the war that was
      declared on September 10, 1939 and that is deemed, for the
      purposes of this Act, to have terminated on September 30,
      1947. ([Seconde Guerre mondiale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References to [Canadian Forces
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A reference in this Act to the [Canadian Forces
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17) includes a reference to any other
  Act of Parliament in force either before or after April 1, 1960
  providing for the payment of pensions to members of the Canadian
  Forces based on length of service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons employed under [Royal Canadian
  Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, the [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and the [Canadian Forces
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17), a person who is employed under
  the authority of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
  Act](/eng/acts/R-10) but who is not a member of the Force is deemed to
  be employed in the public service, and any period of service of a
  person during which they were employed under the authority of that Act
  but were not a member of the Force or during which they were a person
  to whom Part VII of the former Act applied is deemed to be a period of
  service during which they were employed in the public service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pensionable employment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Except as provided in the regulations, employment as
  a member of the Force is not excepted employment for the purposes of
  the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8).

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Superannuation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-422757 .Part}

### [Eligibility for Benefits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-422758 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this Part, an annuity or other benefit specified in this Act shall
  be paid to or in respect of every person who, being required to
  contribute to the Superannuation Account or the Royal Canadian Mounted
  Police Pension Fund in accordance with this Part, ceases to be a
  member of the Force or dies, which annuity or other benefit shall,
  subject to this Part, be based on the number of years of pensionable
  service to the credit of that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Superannuation Account

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Royal Canadian Mounted Police Pension Account
  established in the accounts of Canada pursuant to the former Act is
  hereby continued under the name of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police
  Superannuation Account.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-11, s. 4
- 1999, c. 34, s. 170
:::

### [Contributions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-422766 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contribution rates --- 2013 and later

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member
  of the Force is required to contribute to the Royal Canadian Mounted
  Police Pension Fund, in respect of every portion of the period
  beginning on January 1, 2013, by reservation from pay or otherwise, at
  the contribution rates determined by the Treasury Board in respect of
  that portion on the joint recommendation of the President of the
  Treasury Board and the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contribution rates --- 35 years of
  service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who has to his or her credit, on or after
  January 1, 2013, a period of pensionable service --- or a period of
  pensionable service and other pensionable service --- totalling at
  least 35 years is not required to contribute under subsection (1) but
  is required to contribute, by reservation from pay or otherwise, to
  the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Pension Fund, in respect of the
  period beginning on the later of January 1, 2013 and the day on which
  the person has to his or her credit those 35 years, in addition to any
  other amount required under this Act, at the rates determined by the
  Treasury Board on the joint recommendation of the President of the
  Treasury Board and the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- determination of
  contribution rate

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In determining the contribution rates for the
  purposes of subsections (1) and (2), the rates must not exceed the
  rates paid under section 5 of the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36) by Group 1 contributors described in subsection
  12(0.1) of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other pensionable service

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (2), [[other
  pensionable service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means years of service
  giving rise to a superannuation or pension benefit of a kind specified
  in the regulations that is payable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund, or out of any
    account in the accounts of Canada other than the Superannuation
    Account; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} out of the Canadian Forces Pension Fund within the
    meaning of the [Canadian Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17)
    or the Public Service Pension Fund within the meaning of the [Public
    Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contributions not required

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite anything in this Part, no person shall, in
  respect of any period of his or her service on or after December 15,
  1994, make a contribution under this Part in respect of any portion of
  his or her annual rate of pay that is in excess of the annual rate of
  pay that is fixed by or determined in the manner prescribed by the
  regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A member of the Force who is engaged to work on
  average fewer than a number of hours per week or days per year
  prescribed in the regulations shall not contribute under this Act.

### [Pensionable Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-422784 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pensionable service

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Part, the
following service may be counted by a contributor as pensionable service
for the purposes of this Part:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} non-elective service, comprising,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a contributor who, immediately
    before April 1, 1960, was a contributor under Part V of the former
    Act, any period of service that he would have been entitled to count
    for the purpose of computing any pension, allowance or gratuity
    under that Part had he, at that time, retired from the Force, except
    any such period for which he elected under that Part to pay, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any contributor,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} any period during which he or she was required by
      subsections 5(1) and (2), as they read on December 31, 2012, to
      contribute to the Superannuation Account or the Royal Canadian
      Mounted Police Pension Fund, and any period during which he or she
      is required by subsection 5(1) to contribute to the Royal Canadian
      Mounted Police Pension Fund,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} any period of service that may be counted by him
      as pensionable service pursuant to section 23, and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} any period of service that may be counted by that
      contributor as pensionable service under subsection 24.1(9); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} elective service, comprising,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a contributor who, immediately
    before April 1, 1960, was a contributor under Part V of the former
    Act,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} any period of service for which he elected under
      that Part to pay, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} any period of service for which he might have
      elected, under the provisions of that Part in force immediately
      before April 1, 1960, to pay, if he elects, within the time
      prescribed by those provisions, to pay for that service, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any contributor,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} any period of service as a member of a police
      force of a province or municipality with which the Minister has
      entered into an arrangement under section 20 of the [Royal
      Canadian Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10), that, in accordance
      with the regulations, may be counted by him as pensionable service
      for the purpose of this Part, if he elects, within one year of
      becoming a contributor under this Part, to pay for that service,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} any period of service on active service in the
      navy, army or air forces of His Majesty during World War I or
      World War II, if he elects, within one year of becoming a
      contributor under this Part, to pay for that service,

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} any period of service in the Canadian Army
      Special Force established by Order in Council P.C. 3860 of August
      7, 1950 made under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), if
      he elects, within one year of becoming a contributor under this
      Part, to pay for that service,

    - [(D)]{.lawlabel} any period of service in the regular force, if he
      elects, within one year of becoming a contributor under this Part,
      to pay for that service,

    - [(E)]{.lawlabel} any continuous period of full-time service of six
      months or more in the Canadian Forces other than the regular force
      or in the navy, army or air forces of Her Majesty raised by Canada
      other than the regular force, if he elects, within one year of
      becoming a contributor under this Part, to pay for that service,

    - [(F)]{.lawlabel} any period of service as a member of the Force
      for which he was not required to contribute under the former Act,
      if he elects, within one year of becoming a contributor under this
      Part, to pay for that service,

    - [(G)]{.lawlabel} any period of service during which he was
      employed in the public service on a full-time basis and was in
      receipt of salary, if he or she elects, within one year of
      becoming a contributor under this Part, to pay for that service,
      and any period of service with any board, commission, corporation
      or portion of the federal public administration that is added to
      Schedule I to the [Public Service Superannuation
      Act](/eng/acts/P-36) after April 1, 1960, during which he or she
      was employed on a full-time basis and was in receipt of salary, if
      he or she elects, within one year of such addition, to pay for
      that service,

    - [(H)]{.lawlabel} any period of service that may be counted by him
      as pensionable service pursuant to section 24 of this Act or
      section 18 of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation
      Act](/eng/acts/R-11), chapter R-11 of the Revised Statutes of
      Canada, 1970,

    - [(I)]{.lawlabel} any period of service in respect of which the
      contributor was entitled to be paid or was granted a return of
      contributions or other lump sum payment, other than a transfer
      value or a commuted value, under this Part or under Part V of the
      former Act, if the contributor elects, within one year after
      subsequently becoming a contributor under this Part, to pay for
      that service,

    - [(J)]{.lawlabel} any period of service described in this paragraph
      for which he might have elected, under this Part, Part V of the
      former Act, the Civil Service Superannuation Act, chapter 50 of
      the Revised Statutes of Canada, 1952, the [Public Service
      Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the [Canadian Forces
      Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17) or any order in council made
      under The Canadian Forces Act, 1950, as amended by the Canadian
      Forces Act, 1954, to pay, but for which he failed so to elect
      within the time prescribed therefor, if he elects, at any time
      before he ceases to be a member of the Force, to pay for that
      service,

    - [(K)]{.lawlabel} any period of service in respect of which the
      contributor makes an election under subsection 6.1(1), if the
      contributor elects, at any time before the contributor ceases to
      be a member of the Force, to pay for that service,

    - [(L)]{.lawlabel} any period of service of a kind described in the
      regulations if the contributor elects within the time specified,
      and in the manner specified, in the regulations to pay for that
      service,

    - [(M)]{.lawlabel} any period of service in the Public Service,
      after December 31, 1980 and before the day on which this clause
      comes into force, during which he or she was engaged to work on
      average at least a number of hours per week or days per year
      prescribed in the regulations, if the contributor was a
      contributor immediately before the day on which this clause comes
      into force and the contributor elects, within one year after that
      day, to pay for that service,

    - [(N)]{.lawlabel} any period of service in the Public Service,
      after December 31, 1980, during which he or she was engaged to
      work on average at least a number of hours per week or days per
      year prescribed in the regulations, if he or she elects, within
      one year of becoming a contributor under this Part, to pay for
      that service,

    - [(O)]{.lawlabel} subject to the regulations, any period of service
      in respect of which payment of a commuted value or a transfer
      value, as the case may be, to a contributor has been effected in
      accordance with section 12.1 of this Act, section 22 of the
      [Canadian Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17) or section
      13.01 of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36),
      if the contributor elects, in accordance with the regulations, to
      pay for that service, and

    - [(P)]{.lawlabel} subject to the regulations, any period of service
      in respect of which a payment has been made in respect of the
      contributor pursuant to an agreement entered into under subsection
      24.1(2), if the contributor elects, in accordance with the
      regulations, to pay for that service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election for leave of absence

- **[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), where a
  contributor is or has been absent from the Force on leave of absence
  without pay for a period that exceeds three months, the contributor
  may elect, at the time and in the manner prescribed by the
  regulations, not to count as pensionable service under clause
  6(a)(ii)(A) that portion of the period that is in excess of three
  months.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contributions not required

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding section 5, a contributor who makes an
  election under subsection (1) is not required to contribute to the
  Superannuation Account or the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Pension
  Fund under that section in respect of the portion of the period to
  which the election relates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A contributor is not entitled to make an election
  under subsection (1) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the period of leave of absence ended before the day
    on which that subsection comes into force; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the contributor has, before that day, made all of
    the contributions that are required to be made by the contributor to
    the Superannuation Account in respect of that period.

### [Transfer Agreements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-423288 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[eligible
employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[24.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[eligible employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
  employer for the benefit of whose employees there is a pension plan or
  retirement savings plan of a class prescribed by regulations made
  under paragraph 26.1(1)(h .3), and includes the administrator of any
  such pension plan or retirement savings plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to enter into agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, on terms approved by the Treasury
  Board, enter into an agreement with any eligible employer that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} requires the Minister to pay to the employer, for
    the purpose of any plan referred to in subsection (1), an amount
    determined in accordance with subsection (3) in respect of any
    contributor who has ceased or ceases to be a member of the Force and
    is or becomes employed by that employer; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may provide that any eligible employer pay into the
    Superannuation Account or the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Pension
    Fund an amount determined in accordance with the agreement in
    respect of any person who has ceased or ceases to be employed by
    that employer and is or becomes a member of the Force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to transfer contributions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When a contributor ceases to be a member of the Force
  and is or becomes employed by any eligible employer with whom the
  Minister has entered into an agreement pursuant to subsection (2),
  there may be paid, subject to the terms and conditions that the
  agreement provides and if the agreement so provides, to that employer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} out of the Superannuation Account

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} amounts equal in the aggregate to

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} an amount not exceeding the value, actuarially
        calculated in accordance with the agreement, of all benefits
        accrued under this Part and Part III in respect of the
        pensionable service to the credit of the contributor before
        April 1, 2000, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} an amount representing interest on the amount
        determined in accordance with clause (A) as of the date of
        payment to the eligible employer that the Minister determines,
        or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the benefits payable under this Part and Part
      III to or in respect of the contributor, as they become payable,
      in respect of the pensionable service to the credit of the
      contributor before April 1, 2000; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} out of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Pension
    Fund

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} amounts equal in the aggregate to

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} an amount not exceeding the value, actuarially
        calculated in accordance with the agreement, of all benefits
        accrued under this Part and Part III in respect of the
        pensionable service of the contributor on or after April 1, 2000
        or that comes to the credit of the contributor on or after that
        date, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} an amount representing interest on the amount
        determined in accordance with clause (A) as of the date of
        payment to the eligible employer that the Minister determines,
        or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the benefits payable under this Part and Part
      III to or in respect of the contributor, as they become payable,
      in respect of the pensionable service to the credit of the
      contributor on or after April 1, 2000 or that comes to the credit
      of the contributor on or after that date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent of contributor

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No payment shall be made pursuant to subsection (3)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of operations

**[[32.14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32.14 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraphs 32.12(1)(b) and 32.13(1)(a), the types of operations are as
follows:

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an operation that, in the opinion of the Minister of
  Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness, is a search and rescue
  operation;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an operation that, in the opinion of the Minister of
  Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness, is a disaster relief
  operation;

  Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness, is a counter-terrorism
  operation;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} an operation that, in the opinion of the Minister of
  Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness, is aimed at re-establishing
  social order or rebuilding social institutions following political or
  social unrest; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} an operation involving a level of risk that, in the
  opinion of the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness,
  is comparable to that normally associated with an operation referred
  to in paragraphs (a) and (b).

    by

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the ratio that the number of complete months that

    twelve.

    Force; and

    who is in receipt of a pension by reason of being a survivor or a
    child, is the retirement year or retirement month, as the case may
    be, of the person in respect of whom or in respect of whose service
    the pension is payable.
