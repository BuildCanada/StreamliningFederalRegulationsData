## Mackenzie Gas Project Impacts Fund Act {#mackenzie-gas-project-impacts-fund-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2013, c. 40, s. 282
:::

Assented to 2013-12-12

An Act to establish the Mackenzie Gas Project Impacts Fund

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 282 of chapter 40 of the Statutes of Canada, 2013,
in force on assent December 12, 2013.\]
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345694 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Mackenzie Gas Project Impacts Fund Act](/eng/acts/M-0.15).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345697 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Mackenzie gas project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Mackenzie gas project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the project
    proposed by a consortium led by Imperial Oil Resources Ventures
    Limited that includes the development of the natural gas fields of
    Niglintgak, Taglu and Parsons Lake, and the construction and
    operation of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a gathering system related to those fields;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural gas liquids pipeline;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the natural gas pipeline that is the subject of
      the application GH-12004 made to the National Energy Board on
      October 7, 2004; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} facilities related to the fields, the gathering
      system or the pipelines. ([projet gazier
      Mackenzie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[regional organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regional organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an organization
    referred to in the schedule. ([organisation
    régionale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Designation of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345706 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of Governor in Council

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
designate a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada to be the
Minister for the purpose of this Act.

## [Purpose of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345709 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to establish the Mackenzie Gas Project Impacts Fund in order to provide
for contributions to regional organizations with respect to projects
described in section 8.

## [Mackenzie Gas Project Impacts Fund]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345712 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Account established

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** There is established in the
accounts of Canada an account to be known as the Mackenzie Gas Project
Impacts Fund.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charges

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** On the requisition of the
Minister, there is to be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund and
charged to the Mackenzie Gas Project Impacts Fund all contributions that
are to be paid under section 8.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest to be credited to Fund

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Finance
must, from the Consolidated Revenue Fund, credit to the Mackenzie Gas
Project Impacts Fund interest --- calculated in the manner and at the
rate determined by the Governor in Council --- on the balance from time
to time of that Fund.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligible project

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may provide
contributions to regional organizations with respect to a project if the
project

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} mitigates the existing or anticipated socio-economic
  impacts on communities in the Northwest Territories arising from the
  Mackenzie gas project; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} meets the criteria established and made publicly
  available by the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement with regional organizations

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must, before
providing a contribution to a regional organization, enter into an
agreement with the organization respecting, among other things,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which advances will be made in respect
  of contributions and when those advances will be made;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the terms or conditions on which the contributions
  will be provided; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the evaluation of the regional organization's
  performance in achieving the objectives associated with the provision
  of contributions for eligible projects and the evaluation of the
  results of the projects that are funded.

## [Amendment of Schedule]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345728 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order in council

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order made on the recommendation of the Minister, add or delete
the name of any organization to or from the schedule.

## [Payment Out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345731 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of \$500,000,000

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} From
  the Consolidated Revenue Fund, there may, on the requisition of the
  Minister of Finance made on the recommendation of the Minister, be
  credited to the Mackenzie Gas Project Impacts Fund payments not
  greater than, in the aggregate, \$500,000,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition for recommendation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may only make the recommendation if the
  Mackenzie gas project has not been terminated and the Minister is of
  the opinion that progress is being made on the project.
