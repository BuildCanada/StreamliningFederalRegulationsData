## North American Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act {#north-american-free-trade-agreement-implementation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1993, c. 44
:::

Assented to 1993-06-23

An Act to implement the North American Free Trade Agreement
::::

:::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the Government of Canada, the Government of the United Mexican
States and the Government of the United States of America have entered
into the North American Free Trade Agreement having resolved to

strengthen the special bonds of friendship and cooperation among their
nations,

contribute to the harmonious development and expansion of world trade
and provide a catalyst to broader international cooperation,

create an expanded and secure market for the goods and services produced
in their territories,

reduce distortions to trade,

establish clear and mutually advantageous rules governing their trade,

ensure a predictable commercial framework for business planning and
investment,

build on their rights and obligations under the General Agreement on
Tariffs and Trade and other multilateral and bilateral instruments of
cooperation,

enhance the competitiveness of their firms in global markets,

foster creativity and innovation, and promote trade in goods and
services that are the subject of intellectual property rights,

create new employment opportunities and improve working conditions and
living standards in their respective territories,

undertake each of the preceding in a manner consistent with
environmental protection and conservation,

preserve their flexibility to safeguard the public welfare,

promote sustainable development,

strengthen the development and enforcement of environmental laws and
regulations, and

protect, enhance and enforce basic workers' rights;

WHEREAS the Government of Canada has entered into the Agreement having
further resolved to

strengthen Canada's national identity while at the same time protecting
vital national characteristics and qualities,

establish effective procedures for the review and resolution of disputes
in antidumping and countervailing duty cases involving Canada and other
NAFTA countries, and

strengthen the Canadian economy and Canada's competitiveness as a
trading nation;

WHEREAS the Agreement applies generally throughout Canada;

AND WHEREAS it is necessary, in order to give effect to the Agreement,
to make related or consequential amendments to certain Acts;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365704 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [North American Free Trade Agreement Implementation
Act](/eng/acts/N-23.8).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365707 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the North American Free
      Trade Agreement entered into between the Government of Canada, the
      Government of the United Mexican States and the Government of the
      United States of America and signed on December 17, 1992, and
      includes any rectifications thereto made prior to its ratification
      by Canada; ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the whole or any portion
      of any Act of Parliament or any regulation, order or other
      instrument issued, made or established in the exercise of a power
      conferred by or under an Act of Parliament; ([texte législatif
      fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Free Trade Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Free Trade Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Free Trade
      Commission established under Article 2001 of the
      Agreement; ([Commission du libre-échange]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of any provision of
      this Act, means the member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada
      designated as the Minister for the purposes of that provision
      under section 11; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[NAFTA country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[NAFTA country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a country that is a
      Party to the Agreement; ([pays ALÉNA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Secretariat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Secretariat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Secretariat
      established pursuant to paragraph 1 of Article 2002 of the
      Agreement; ([Secrétariat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[territory]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[territory]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to a NAFTA
      country, has the meaning set out with respect to that country in
      Annex 201.1 of the Agreement. ([territoire]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of Agreement and Tariff
  Schedules

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Agreement, including the Schedules of Canada, of
  Mexico and of the United States referred to in Annex 302.2 of the
  Agreement, shall be published in the Canada Treaty Series.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation consistent with Agreement

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, this
Act, any provision of an Act of Parliament enacted by Part II and any
other federal law that implements a provision of the Agreement or
fulfils an obligation of the Government of Canada under the Agreement
shall be interpreted in a manner consistent with the Agreement.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365722 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to implement the Agreement, the objectives of which, as elaborated more
specifically through its principles and rules, including national
treatment, most-favoured-nation treatment and transparency, are to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} eliminate barriers to trade in, and facilitate the
  cross-border movement of, goods and services between the territories
  of the NAFTA countries;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} promote conditions of fair competition in the
  free-trade area established by the Agreement;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} increase substantially investment opportunities in
  the territories of the NAFTA countries;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide adequate and effective protection and
  enforcement of intellectual property rights in the territory of each
  NAFTA country;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} create effective procedures for the implementation
  and application of the Agreement, for its joint administration and for
  the resolution of disputes; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} establish a framework for further trilateral,
  regional and multilateral cooperation to expand and enhance the
  benefits of the Agreement.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365731 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365734 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition of private cause of action

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person has any cause of action and no proceedings of any kind shall be
  taken, without the consent of the Attorney General of Canada, to
  enforce or determine any right or obligation that is claimed or arises
  solely under or by virtue of Part I or any order or regulation made
  under Part I.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to Section B of Chapter Eleven of the
  Agreement, no person has any cause of action and no proceedings of any
  kind shall be taken, without the consent of the Attorney General of
  Canada, to enforce or determine any right or obligation that is
  claimed or arises solely under or by virtue of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of Agreement to water

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  greater certainty, nothing in this Act or the Agreement, except
  Article 302 of the Agreement, applies to water.

- Definition of [[water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
  natural surface and ground water in liquid, gaseous or solid state,
  but does not include water packaged as a beverage or in tanks.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of [Interpretation
Act](/eng/acts/I-21)

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where the operation of a
provision of an Act of Parliament is suspended pursuant to a provision
of that Act enacted by Part II, section 43 of the [Interpretation
Act](/eng/acts/I-21) applies, with such modifications as the
circumstances require, as if the suspended provision had been repealed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act, by specific mention or omission, limits in any
manner the right of Parliament to enact legislation to implement any
provision of the Agreement or fulfil any of the obligations of the
Government of Canada under the Agreement.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Implementation of Agreement Generally]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365749 .Part}

### [Approval of Agreement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-365750 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement approved

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement is hereby
approved.

### [Designation of Minister]{.HTitleText2} {#h-365753 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order designating Minister

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate any member of the Queen's Privy Council for
Canada to be the Minister for the purposes of any provision of this Act.

### [Free Trade Commission]{.HTitleText2} {#h-365756 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Free Trade Commission

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may appoint any member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada to be a
representative of Canada on the Free Trade Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of expenditures

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
shall pay its appropriate share of the aggregate of any expenditures
incurred by or on behalf of the Free Trade Commission.

### [Canadian Section of Secretariat]{.HTitleText2} {#h-365761 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian Section of Secretariat

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Canadian Section of
the Secretariat is established within the Department of Foreign Affairs,
Trade and Development for the purpose of facilitating the operation of
the Agreement, including the work of panels, committees and scientific
review boards that may be established in accordance with the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Secretary

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be a Secretary of the Canadian Section of the Secretariat to be
  appointed in accordance with the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Secretary is responsible for fulfilling the
  mandate of the Section. In doing so, the Secretary shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide assistance to the Free Trade Commission;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide administrative assistance to panels and
    committees established under Chapter Nineteen of the Agreement and
    panels established under Chapter Twenty of the Agreement;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} support --- as the Free Trade Commission may direct
    --- the work of other committees and groups established under the
    Agreement;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} otherwise facilitate --- as the Free Trade
    Commission may direct --- the operation of the Agreement; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} supervise and direct the work of the Section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Such officers and
employees as are required for the proper conduct of the work of the
Canadian Section of the Secretariat shall be appointed in accordance
with the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

### [Panels, Committees and Scientific Review Boards]{.HTitleText2} {#h-365780 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointments to roster

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, in accordance with a consensus reached under Article 1124, 1414 or
2009 of the Agreement, appoint any person to be a member of the roster
established under that Article.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointments to certain committees

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may appoint
any person to be a representative of Canada on any committee referred to
in Section A of Annex 2001.2 of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs of panels, committees and
scientific review boards

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
shall, in accordance with Annex 2002.2 of the Agreement, pay the costs
of or its appropriate share of the costs of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the remuneration and expenses payable to panelists,
  members of committees, including persons appointed under section 18,
  and members of scientific review boards;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the remuneration and expenses payable to assistants;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the general expenses incurred by panels, committees
  and scientific review boards.

### [Orders and Regulations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-365790 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations re Articles 312 and 313

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, where the Governor in Council is of the
  opinion that, for the purpose of giving effect in a province to
  Articles 312 and 313 of the Agreement, regulations are necessary in
  relation to any matter dealt with by those Articles, make regulations
  for that purpose including, without limiting the generality of the
  foregoing, regulations requiring or prohibiting the doing of anything
  in relation to which a regulation may be made under this subsection
  and prescribing penalties for the contravention of any such
  regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicability of regulations in respect
  of a province

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under subsection (1) shall not come
  into force in respect of a province if the Governor in Council is of
  the opinion that the province has, by or under provincial law, enacted
  provisions or is carrying on practices that are in conformity with the
  portion of Articles 312 and 313 of the Agreement in respect of which
  the regulation is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation with provincial
  governments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall consult with the government of a
  province prior to the making of a regulation under subsection (1) in
  respect of that province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expiration

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under subsection (1) or any
  provision thereof shall cease to be in force in respect of a province
  on a day or days to be fixed, in respect of that province, by order of
  the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on province

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under subsection (1) in respect of
  a province is binding on Her Majesty in right of that province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders re Article 2019

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, for the purpose of suspending in accordance
  with the Agreement the application to a NAFTA country of benefits of
  equivalent effect pursuant to Article 2019 of the Agreement, by order,
  do any one or more of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend rights or privileges granted by Canada to
    that country or to goods, service providers, suppliers, investors or
    investments of that country under the Agreement or any federal law,
    except under Chapter Nineteen of the Agreement or under any
    provision of the [Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15)
    enacted by Part II;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} modify or suspend the application of any federal
    law, except any provision of the [Special Import Measures
    Act](/eng/acts/S-15) enacted by Part II, with respect to that
    country or to goods, service providers, suppliers, investors or
    investments of that country;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} extend the application of any federal law to that
    country or to goods, service providers, suppliers, investors or
    investments of that country; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} take any other measure that the Governor in Council
    considers necessary for that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless revoked, an order made under subsection (1)
  shall have effect for such period as is specified in the order.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2010, c. 12, s. 1778

  - ###### Definitions {#definitions .MarginalNote}

    - [1778]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply
      in this section.

      [[former Section]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

      :   [[former Section]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian
          Section of the Secretariat established by section 14 of the
          [North American Free Trade Agreement Implementation
          Act](/eng/acts/N-23.8) as it read immediately before the
          coming into force of section 1776. ([ancienne
          section]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

      [[new Section]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

      :   [[new Section]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Section
          of the Secretariat established by section 14 of the [North
          American Free Trade Agreement Implementation
          Act](/eng/acts/N-23.8) as enacted by section 1776. ([nouvelle
          section]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    - ###### Secretary {#secretary .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person occupying the position of Secretary of
      the former Section immediately before the day on which section
      1776 comes into force becomes on that day the Secretary of the new
      Section and is deemed to have been appointed under section 15 of
      the [North American Free Trade Agreement Implementation
      Act](/eng/acts/N-23.8) as enacted by section 1777.

    - ###### Staff {#staff .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in section 1776 shall be construed as
      affecting the status of an officer or employee who, immediately
      before the coming into force of that section, occupied a position
      in the former Section, except that the officer or employee shall,
      on the coming into force of that section, occupy their position in
      the new Section.

    - ###### Transfer of appropriations {#transfer-of-appropriations .MarginalNote}

      [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any amount appropriated, for the fiscal year in
      which section 1776 comes into force, by an appropriation Act based
      on the Estimates for that year for defraying the charges and
      expenses of the federal public administration in respect of the
      former Section that, on the day on which that section comes into
      force, is unexpended, is deemed, on that day, to be an amount
      appropriated for defraying the charges and expenses of the federal
      public administration in respect of the new Section.

    - ###### Transfer of powers, duties and functions --- Secretary {#transfer-of-powers-duties-and-functions-secretary .MarginalNote}

      [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any power, duty or function described in section
      15 of the [North American Free Trade Agreement Implementation
      Act](/eng/acts/N-23.8) as enacted by section 1777 that is vested
      in or exercisable by the Secretary of the former Section
      immediately before the day on which that section comes into force
      is, as of that day, vested in and exercisable by the Secretary of
      the new Section. Any other power, duty or function that is vested
      in or exercisable by the Secretary of the former Section
      immediately before that day is, as of that day, vested in and
      exercisable by the Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs.

    - ###### Transfer of powers, duties and functions --- officers and employees {#transfer-of-powers-duties-and-functions-officers-and-employees .MarginalNote}

      [(6)]{.lawlabel} Any power, duty or function exercisable by an
      officer or employee of the former Section, immediately before the
      day on which section 1776 comes into force is, as of that day,
      exercisable by that officer or employee as officer or employee of
      the new Section.
