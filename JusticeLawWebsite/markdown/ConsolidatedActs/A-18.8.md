## Avoiding Complicity in Mistreatment by Foreign Entities Act {#avoiding-complicity-in-mistreatment-by-foreign-entities-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2019, c. 13, s. 49.1
:::

Assented to 2019-06-21

An Act respecting the disclosure of and request for information that
would result in a substantial risk of mistreatment of an individual by a
foreign entity and the use of information that is likely to have been
obtained as the result of mistreatment of an individual by a foreign
entity

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 49.1 of chapter 13 of the Statutes of Canada, 2019,
in force July 13, 2019, see SI/2019-71.\]
:::
:::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas a fundamental responsibility of the Government of Canada is to
protect Canada's national security and the safety of Canadians;

Whereas Parliament recognizes that information needs to be disclosed,
requested or used in order to enable the Government to fulfill that
responsibility;

Whereas that responsibility must be fulfilled in accordance with the
rule of law and in a manner that safeguards the rights and freedoms of
Canadians and that respects the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms;

Whereas Canada is a party to a number of international agreements that
prohibit torture and other cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment or
punishment, including the Geneva Conventions, the International Covenant
on Civil and Political Rights and the Convention Against Torture and
Other Cruel, Inhuman or Degrading Treatment or Punishment;

Whereas torture is an offence under the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46),
which Act also prohibits aiding and abetting the commission of torture,
counselling the commission of torture, conspiring to commit torture,
attempting to commit torture and being an accessory after the fact to
torture;

And whereas torture and other cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment or
punishment of individuals are an affront to Canadian values and are
opposed by the Government of Canada in the strongest terms;
:::

::: Enacts
Now therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170109 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Avoiding Complicity in Mistreatment by Foreign Entities
Act](/eng/acts/A-18.8).

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170112 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a department named in Schedule I
      to the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the
      Minister presiding over the department;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a division or branch of the
      federal public administration set out in column I of Schedule I.1
      to the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the
      Minister set out in column II of that Schedule;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a corporation named in Schedule
      II to the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the
      Minister designated as the appropriate Minister by order of the
      Governor in Council made under that Act;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a [[parent Crown
      corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 83(1)
      of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the
      [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in that
      subsection; or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the Canadian Forces, the Minister
      of National Defence. ([ministre compétent]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a department named in
    Schedule I to the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), a
    division or branch of the federal public administration set out in
    column I of Schedule I.1 to that Act, a corporation named in
    Schedule II to that Act, a [[parent Crown
    corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 83(1)
    of that Act or the Canadian Forces. ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[deputy head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deputy head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a department named in Schedule I
      to the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the deputy
      minister of that department;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the Canadian Forces, the Chief of
      the Defence Staff;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the Royal Canadian Mounted
      Police, the Commissioner of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the Canadian Security
      Intelligence Service, the Director;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the Canada Border Services
      Agency, the President;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the Communications Security
      Establishment, the Chief; and

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} with respect to any other portion of the federal
      public administration, the person designated by order of the
      Governor in Council to be the deputy head of that portion of the
      federal public administration for the purposes of this
      Act. ([administrateur général]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mistreatment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mistreatment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means torture or other cruel,
    inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment, within the meaning of
    the Convention Against Torture and Other Cruel, Inhuman or Degrading
    Treatment or Punishment, signed at New York on December 10,
    1984. ([mauvais traitements]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Directions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170135 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of the appropriate
  Minister, issue written directions to any deputy head in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure of information to any foreign entity
    that would result in a substantial risk of mistreatment of an
    individual;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the making of requests to any foreign entity for
    information that would result in a substantial risk of mistreatment
    of an individual; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the use of information that is likely to have been
    obtained through the mistreatment of an individual by a foreign
    entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to issue

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council must issue written directions
  in respect of the matters referred to in subsection (1) to the
  following deputy heads:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Chief of the Defence Staff;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Deputy Minister of National Defence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Commissioner of the Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Director of the Canadian Security Intelligence
    Service;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the President of the Canada Border Services Agency;
    and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Chief of the Communications Security
    Establishment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions not statutory instruments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Directions are not statutory instruments within the
  meaning of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments to schedule

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, amend the schedule to add a reference to each deputy head
to whom directions have been issued under section 3 or to delete any
such reference if the directions that were issued to the deputy head
have been repealed or if the deputy head's position has ceased to exist
or has changed name.

## [Deputy Heads]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170155 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions to be made available to public

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every deputy head to whom
directions have been issued under section 3 must, as soon as feasible
after receiving them, make them available to the public.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy to be provided

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every deputy head to whom
directions have been issued under section 3 must, as soon as feasible
after receiving them, provide a copy of the directions to the National
Security and Intelligence Committee of Parliamentarians, the National
Security and Intelligence Review Agency and, if applicable, the Civilian
Review and Complaints Commission for the Royal Canadian Mounted Police.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  deputy head to whom directions have been issued under section 3 must,
  before March 1 of each year, submit to the appropriate Minister a
  report in respect of the implementation of those directions during the
  previous calendar year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Version available to the public

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every deputy head must, as soon as feasible after
  submitting a report under subsection (1), make a version of it
  available to the public that does not contain information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure of which would be injurious to
    national security, national defence or international relations or
    compromise an ongoing operation or investigation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is subject to solicitor-client privilege or
    the professional secrecy of advocates and notaries or to litigation
    privilege.

## [Appropriate Ministers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170167 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to provide copy

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As soon
  as feasible after receiving a report under section 7, the appropriate
  Minister must provide a copy of it to the National Security and
  Intelligence Committee of Parliamentarians, the National Security and
  Intelligence Review Agency and, if applicable, the Civilian Review and
  Complaints Commission for the Royal Canadian Mounted Police.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The copy must not contain any information that the
  Committee, the Agency or the Commission is not entitled to receive.
